## Compositional source-observability, never a flat stereotype state

A statement about a survey item may be `DOCUMENTED_DESIGN` (what the instrument says), `OBSERVED_INSTRUMENT_CONSEQUENCE` (what questionnaire/routing design demonstrably does), `DOCUMENTED_RESPONDENT_RESPONSE` (a named actual eligible asked released observed respondent code), `SCIENTIFIC_INTERPRETATION` (qualified researcher inference), `MODEL_OR_FICTION`, or `UNKNOWN_HELD`. Those are different kinds of claims, not equivalent input values. Orthogonal facets: asked, released, observation, eligibility, unit, attribution, source_status, translation; exact release/instrument/country/wave/question/source unit/target unit and evidence must accompany selected claims.

**Hard illegal promotion:** if original respondent-value claim, require `DOCUMENTED_RESPONDENT_RESPONSE`, `asked=YES`, `released=YES`, `observation=VALUE`, `eligibility=ELIGIBLE`, selected `record_value_ref`, `original_code_ref`, source→target same unit and self/interviewer/administrative attribution. No household→individual, interviewer→self, response from nonexistent released respondent, unasked→negative, source absence→absence-of-effect, unobserved Lebanese ancestry→“no ancestry”, modeled answer→original source, instrument metadata→respondent belief, or unqualified translation/wave comparability. Code checks this typed rule. Qualified scholarly uncertainty and legitimate claims *about* source nonobservation remain allowed without becoming affirmative respondent values.

**Fresh non-overfit challenge:** C must test at least one source semantics issue that A/B did not use to design their fixtures, e.g. household-resource attribution versus individual command, GSS religiosity label, source-specific sampled sect stratum versus respondent self-identified sect, retrospective memory of a survey question, or wave translation non-equivalence. The task is not to construct an exhaustive 35-source codebook court; it is to determine whether the chosen architecture can transmit novel semantic distinctions without unauthorized promotion.

## Distinct scientific and person-world domains

`OBSERVED`: original source-qualified observational data; `DESCRIPTIVE_MODELED`: statistical descriptive/conditionally estimated products with explicit original provenance; `EXTERNAL_OBSERVED`: independently qualified material/institutional evidence, not inferred from survey answers; `STRUCTURAL_QUALIFIED`: causal/institutional/property/control conditions under competent science; `FICTIONAL_WORLD`: created or simulated but historically constrained possibilities and committed world particulars; `ACTOR_STATE`: separately owned developing person with actual access, learning, motive, activity; `HISTORY_EVENT`: performed acts and independently occurring exogenous events, correctly clocked and qualified.

`contracts/policy.json` is the finite permitted tuple matrix for `from-domain → to-domain + transition-kind + authority`. Typed graph validation rejects unauthorized transitions, shadow competing authority strings, broken edge connectivity and wrong end domains. It also demands that the **named first-consumer node is the actual declared endpoint**, with `ACTUAL` vs `PLANNED_OWNER_HELD` expressly distinguished. A beautiful fake route `source → C11` cannot satisfy `WORLD_TO_ACTOR`; no observed/structural/fiction/actor rights flow merely because a node has a suggestive label.

Five mandatory route questions: observed→descriptive; independent external observation→structural/world; world→actor access; actor→performed history; exogenous world→history. Preserve real **independent Other** agency, institutional constraints, global/local conflicting claims and exogenous events; never make actor acts the only cause of world changes. Future route nonpreclusion is a *preservation and lawful possible construction* claim, **not** a claim a full actor/world engine is running. Source-static preservation can qualify a narrow C11 architecture only if the actual data and rights required for a later legitimate construction are not erased; unissued scientific behavior remains labeled held.
