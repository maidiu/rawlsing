## Constitutional rule

`NO_VALID_GROUNDING_RECEIPT = DECISION_AUTHORITY_DISABLED`.

Any VOZHD, SUB-VOZHD, HQ, architecture-synthesis, conceptual-synthesis, semantic-adjudication, authority-adjudication, campaign-acceptance, project-acceptance, succession, adoption, or cross-campaign interpretation agent must prove grounding in the current central project state before exercising project-level judgment.

Ungrounded cognition is not prohibited. Ungrounded **project authority** is.

## Mandatory start

A grounded project-decision agent begins at `../00_RAWLSING_GROUND.md`, then reads `../CENTRAL_PROJECT_STATE_R1/00_STATE_NOW.md`, the relevant `PROJECT_STATE.tsv` rows, relevant `OPEN_OBLIGATIONS.tsv` rows, and the deeper routes required by its declared scope.

The agent must emit a grounding receipt before adjudicative reasoning. The receipt declares:

- grounding receipt ID;
- agent role and canonical agent mode;
- exact canonical project head;
- ground version;
- task scope;
- relevant state IDs;
- relevant obligation, tension and unknown IDs;
- binding-authority surfaces read;
- must-factor surfaces read;
- anti-resurrection constraints acknowledged;
- authority ceiling;
- what the agent may decide;
- what it may not decide;
- dependencies;
- exact return destination;
- `CENTRAL_STATE_HEAD_MATCH = PASS`;
- `GROUNDING_COMPLETE_FOR_DECLARED_SCOPE = PASS`.

Grounding is scope-sensitive. It does not require loading the entire deep corpus. It requires knowing the central state and descending into the exact evidence that materially bears on the decision.

## Canonical modes

### GROUNDED_PROJECT_DECISION
Full grounding receipt required. May exercise only the authority explicitly granted by the commissioning layer.

### GROUNDED_BOUNDED_EXECUTION
A mechanical executor may inherit an exact grounded parent adjudication. It must record the canonical base, governing adjudication/ref, state/obligation IDs, authority ceiling, forbidden reinterpretations and return destination. It cannot invent project-level decisions.

### FORENSIC_READ_ONLY
May inspect history and produce evidence. No project decision, adoption or closure authority unless later consumed by a grounded competent agent.

### DELIBERATELY_UNGROUNDED_EXPLORATION
Intentional clean-room exploration. Must declare `NO_PROJECT_AUTHORITY`, `NO_ADOPTION_POWER`, and `NO_CLOSURE_POWER`. Requires later grounded reconciliation.

### HOSTILE_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT
May intentionally delay prior synthesis during a cold audit. Independence is explicit. The auditor may rule on the audit question but cannot self-adopt resulting architecture/project state.

## Fail-closed adoption law

A project-level result lacking a valid receipt may be preserved as evidence or exploration, but cannot be banked as project acceptance. Persuasiveness, passing tests, branch ancestry, worker PASS, or downstream use do not repair missing grounding retroactively.

## Staleness

If the canonical central project head moves after grounding and before adoption, the adopting authority must determine whether the touched state changed. Machine validation should fail closed on a head mismatch unless an explicit re-grounding or competent no-material-delta ruling is attached.
