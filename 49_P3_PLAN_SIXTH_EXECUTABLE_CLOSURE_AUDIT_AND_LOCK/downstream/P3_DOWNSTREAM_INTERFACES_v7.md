# P3 Downstream Interfaces v3

## P4 measurement compatibility
P4 receives canonical family revisions, variant revisions, and exact upstream response-contract references. P3 records topology and contract identity but authorizes no transformations.

## P5 structural authority
P5 receives family, variant, implementation, and context subjects. P3 preserves upstream strata/horizon references as provenance-only passthroughs; P5 authors structural meaning and direction separately.

## P6 shadow runtime
P6 installs one validated P3 authority release in a shadow namespace, rebuilds prior and proposed V550J/V551J/V552J outputs under identical inputs, classifies every difference, and promotes or rolls back transactionally.

## P7 persona and pressure computation
Every computation names its P3 family revision, P4 measurement release, P5 structural release, runtime builder, country/time inputs, and unresolved boundaries.

## Maintenance interfaces
New waves and P2 corrections traverse the dependency graph. Source licensing and provenance remain attached to every imported object and downstream family revision.

No visual or numerical pulsing-matrix object is required by this contract.


## V27 ownership and promotion boundary

Candidate variants and exclusions are proposals owned by candidate revisions. Canonical variants and exclusions are new immutable objects owned by canonical-family revisions. An accepted adjudication must preserve an explicit promotion mapping. Downstream P4/P5/P6 interfaces may reference only canonical-owned objects after P3 closure.
