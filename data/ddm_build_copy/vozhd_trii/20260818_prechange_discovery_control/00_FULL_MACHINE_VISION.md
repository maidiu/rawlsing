## 1. The machine's object is a becoming person

Rawlsing exists to generate a person who is causally intelligible without being inevitable. The person is neither an autonomous trait bundle later decorated with history nor the mechanical output of class, nation, family, religion, demography, or survey response. A life begins already embedded in historically organized relations of provision, dependence, coercion, recognition, labor, kinship, language, legitimacy, violence, memory, desire, attachment and meaning. Those relations alter what can happen, what costs what, what is available to be noticed, what can be believed, what can be endured, and what routes vitality can take. They do not contain a finished psyche.

The final machine therefore has to solve a more difficult problem than profile synthesis:

> Given a historically structured world, how can a living organism become a particular person through path-dependent interaction with that world while preserving contingency, contradiction, opacity, agency and surprise?

The unit of generative explanation is **history**, not classification.

## 2. The structural world: nine finite strata plus Absolute Horizon

They are relational pressure fields, not nine demographic columns and not nine independent scalar scores. Pressures can move downward, upward and laterally. Multiple fields can coexist, reinforce one another, contradict one another, or become mutually unintelligible. A person can be agent-of one structure while agent-against another. A local design that collapses this plurality into one net-pressure score starves the terminal person even if it makes storage or retrieval easier.

Absolute Horizon is separate. It is not stratum ten and not a religiosity variable. It concerns the evolving relation to what is experienced as ultimately binding: what may be sacrificed for what, which finite commitments become absolute, and which criteria can later judge the mediators through which they were learned.

## 3. Pressure is world-side; flow is life-side

Structure acts through pressure: scarcity, permission, expectation, opportunity, threat, prestige, shame, security, obligation, exclusion, support, debt, aspiration, legitimacy, protection and other historically governed constraints or affordances.

What moves through those pressures is living flow: need, desire, vitality, attachment, curiosity, fear, sexuality, imagination, rage, play, care, appetite, beauty, humor, prayer, craft, striving, belonging, autonomy, recognition, fairness and meaning-seeking.

The deepest causal question is therefore:

> **Where does living flow go under pressure?**

It can be blocked, captured, amplified, redirected, buffered, poisoned, sublimated, mis-aimed, metabolized or transformed. Repeated routing becomes history; history changes later routing possibilities. Personality-like regularities, where they emerge, are developmental attractors rather than essences assigned at birth.

## 6. Why conditional and joint empirical evidence can matter

A population average can erase exactly the differences Rawlsing is trying to preserve. The same answer can have different structural meaning in different positions, places, historical moments or cotravelling attitude configurations. Two position variables with weak marginal effects can have a strong joint effect. Simpson reversals can make an aggregate association point in the opposite direction from lawful subgroup associations. A small minority configuration can matter historically even when it contributes little to a scalar score.

The minimum semantic empirical object identified by the VT3 Conditional-Meaning Chamber is therefore distributional rather than deterministic:

```text
CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION (CED)
= P(response topology | exact governed empirical predicate, scope)
```

with exact predicate identity, raw and governed weighted support, denominators, response topology, time/geography, typed zero/absence/noncomparability, semantic authority and provenance.

Unary CEDs are not sufficient for all future lawful queries. Same-respondent incidence or an information-equivalent source substrate must remain reconstructible so separately authorized conjunctions can be computed without pretending that independent marginals determine joint reality.

The empirical discovery engine may calculate diagnostics such as MI, normalized MI, Cramér's V, entropy reduction, recursive splits, outlier cohorts or co-signature scores. Such diagnostics can be useful for discovery or authoring without becoming canonical world facts.

## 9. Actor knowledge is not analyst knowledge

A generated objective fact can exist while the actor does not know it, misremembers it, misrecognizes its cause, or lacks language to articulate it. Structural power often appears in consciousness without its causal label: as duty, normality, shame, ambition, piety, common sense, gratitude, failure or simply "how things are."

The machine must preserve that opacity. Generated history, actor-accessible history, memory, belief, claim permission and rendered expression are separate objects.

This is essential to the human vision: explanation must not turn the generated actor into an analyst of their own causal graph.

## 10. Identification, alienation and agency

The terminal person must be capable of dynamic relations to the structures that formed them.

Identification can become internalization, naturalization, invisibility and agency-through a structure. Alienation can make a previously transparent structure visible enough to become an object of judgment, resistance, mourning, mockery, reform or transformation. Neither sequence guarantees emancipation. People remain contradictory and can move in opposing directions across strata.

Agency is situated divergence whose consequences become later causes. It does not require an unconditioned ghost. A field of lawful possibilities plus contingent history is enough for later states to diverge from identical starting conditions.
