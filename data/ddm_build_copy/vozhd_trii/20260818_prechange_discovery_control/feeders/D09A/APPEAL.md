## 4. Primary theorem: different fact identity is the floor and the ceiling absent further authority

D08's accepted algebra gives a strong, precise result.

An empirical fact is not identified by its numerical probability alone. Its identity includes, as applicable:

- source/wave/canonical-wave/country scope;
- target question or component identity;
- response topology;
- exact condition predicate and predicate provenance;
- denominator/risk-set semantics;
- routing/applicability and typed missingness;
- raw versus governed weighted measure basis;
- branch/cohort provenance;
- authority, admissibility and replay provenance.

Therefore:

```text
P(Y=y | C1, R1) = 0.42
P(Y=y | C2, R2) = 0.42
```

with `C1 != C2` or `R1 != R2` can still be two different empirical facts.

That conclusion is exact and sufficient. It does not require a second semantic layer.

### 4.1 Predicate difference is not meaning difference

A predicate can differ for reasons that alter fact identity without altering any governed interpretation of the focal response. Examples include:

- a different exact risk set;
- a different routing population;
- a different source/wave/country scope;
- a differently governed empirical coordinate that happens to produce the same observed distribution;
- a different provenance path to an otherwise numerically equal result.

All of these may make the facts non-identical. None automatically changes what the response item means, what structural relation it evidences, or what any actor means by giving that response.

Thus the implication

`DIFFERENT_EMPIRICAL_FACT_IDENTITY -> DIFFERENT_MEANING_FIELD`

is not established.

### 4.2 Joint incidence is stronger, but still empirical

E2 gives a stronger counterexample to marginal collapse. Two populations can have identical focal marginals yet different same-respondent k-way configurations. XOR/parity fixtures can make higher-order structure invisible to pairwise summaries. This proves:

`DIFFERENT_E2_INCIDENCE -> DIFFERENT_EMPIRICAL_CONFIGURATION_FACT_IDENTITY`.

It does **not** by itself prove:

`DIFFERENT_E2_INCIDENCE -> DIFFERENT_STRUCTURAL_MEANING`.

A governed structural transformation may later care about the difference. Until that transformation exists, the difference remains a population empirical configuration fact.

This is not semantic impoverishment. It is jurisdictional precision.
