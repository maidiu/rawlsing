## 1. Starvation proof bar

D12B treats a terminal-starvation claim as demonstrated only when all of the following hold:

1. **Controlled worlds:** World A and World B differ only in the targeted empirical distinction. Independently governed structural inputs, actor-generation rules, and other source evidence are held fixed.
2. **Derived outputs are recomputed:** a downstream object derived from the removed distinction is not artificially held fixed. If its upstream cause disappears, the pipeline must lawfully recompute or fail closed.
3. **No equivalent reconstruction:** the removed distinction cannot be exactly reconstructed from other remaining lawful evidence. An old table may disappear without starvation when a deeper authoritative relation retains the same information.
4. **Terminal requirement:** an accepted terminal capability—historical pressure/change, contradiction/multiplicity, mediation prerequisites, developmental causality, actor opacity, genealogy, epistemic separation, etc.—becomes impossible, ambiguous, or dependent on invention.
5. **Jurisdiction remains intact:** the proof may not create terminal relevance by smuggling population statistics into generated position, biography, psyche, belief, memory, encounter, or action.

This deliberately distinguishes:

`current executable transport failure != terminal semantic starvation`

and:

`historical intended consumer != terminal necessity`.
