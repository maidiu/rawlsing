## 4. Adjudication of the seven candidate information targets

### 4.1 Unary conditionals only

**Ruling:** `UNARY_INSUFFICIENT` for the declared general target.

Unary conditionals are sufficient only when target arity is exactly one, or when exact **mutual conditional independence** of every target variable is separately established under the same conditioner/risk set/scope/measure/topology.

They cannot generally reconstruct:

- response-response cotravel;
- impossible/absent combinations;
- multimodality;
- contradictory supported configurations;
- XOR/parity structure;
- higher-order interactions.

### 4.2 Pairwise conditionals

**Ruling:** `PAIRWISE_INSUFFICIENT` for arbitrary `k > 2`.

A complete 2-way contingency is the full joint for a 2-variable bounded target and is sufficient for that target.

But a collection of all pairwise contingencies does not generally determine a 3-way or higher joint.

The four-bit parity fixture in `REVERSE_QUERY_CASEBOOK.md` proves the stronger theorem:

> two 4-way populations can have identical unary, pairwise, and every 3-way marginal while having disjoint 4-way support.

Therefore no fixed lower-order ceiling can substitute for arbitrary requested target arity.

### 4.3 Full joint distributions

**Ruling:** `TARGET_REQUIRED` at the **requested bounded arity**, not as a universal materialization command.

For a fixed authorized target `(Q1,...,Qk)` and fixed `C/R/S/μ`, the exact full conditional joint distribution/support geometry is information sufficient.

D12E does not require precomputing the full joint over every question and every empirical coordinate simultaneously.

### 4.4 Sparse joint pattern support

**Ruling:** `EXACT_FIXED_CONTINGENCY_SUFFICIENT_FOR_BOUNDED_QUERY` only when the sparse representation is exhaustive.

A sparse pattern representation is lossless for a fixed query if it preserves/proves:

### 4.5 Graphical/factorized representation

## 5. Independence attack

Assume:

```text
Qi ~ P(Qi | actor_properties)
```

sampled independently for every i.

D12E rules:

`UNARY_INSUFFICIENT` by default.

Independent sampling is lossless **only** under an exact proven factorization:

```text
P(Q1=q1,...,Qk=qk | C,R,S,μ)
=
Π_i P(Qi=qi | C,R,S,μ)
```

for every lawful configuration.

The factorization proof must bind:

- exact same conditioner C;
- exact same source/historical scope S;
- exact same risk-set semantics R, or a governed decomposition proving equivalence;
- exact same measure basis μ;
- response topology/routing compatibility;
- exact support/zero semantics;
- **mutual** conditional independence, not merely pairwise independence.

### 5.1 Why pairwise independence is inadequate

XOR/parity variables can be pairwise independent while jointly constrained.

Therefore:

```text
all pairwise associations = 0
```

is not authority for independent sampling.

### 5.2 Why separate risk sets are inadequate

Even true-looking factors such as:

```text
P(Q1|C,R1) × P(Q2|C,R2)
```

are not one respondent-level joint if `R1 != R2` and no exact missingness/risk-set law establishes equivalence.

### 5.3 Why statistical nonsignificance is inadequate

Failure to detect dependence is not an exact conditional-independence authority. Low support can mask dependence; a significance threshold cannot convert unproved independence into empirical ontology.

### 5.4 Final independence ruling

Independent unary sampling is permissible as a no-loss representation only when exact conditional-independence/factorization authority or proof exists for the bounded query. Otherwise it fabricates possibility geometry.

`UNPROVEN_UNLESS_EXACT_CONDITIONAL_INDEPENDENCE_AUTHORITY`
