## 4. Exact ontology ruling

### 4.1 Empirical respondent coordinate

An empirical respondent coordinate is an observed survey-side fact such as:

```text
respondent age = 35
respondent income_level = high
respondent region = Beirut
```

It belongs to the empirical evidence jurisdiction. D08 permits such coordinates to participate in exact predicates if their field/value semantics, risk set, scope and provenance are governed.

### 4.2 Generated actor demographic / biographical fact

This is an actor-specific generated objective fact/history owned by the relevant V3 semantic rule, for example conceptually:

```text
actor age = 35
actor resides at place P
actor is member of generated household H at time t
actor had exposure E at time t
actor self-identifies as S
```

The word “generated” is decisive: the value must be produced/admitted under exact authority. A Phase-A context field, prompt attribute, structural prior, demographic prevalence or plausible label is not automatically an actor fact.

### 4.3 Generated nine-stratum structural position

ADP-009 position facts are relational generated runtime facts. They are not the D12D actor-fact source.

Examples of forbidden substitutions:

```text
generated class position -> survey income_level
generated sect position -> survey religion_label
generated regional position -> survey region
generated occupation/relation position -> survey employment
```

CAN-006 generated-position binding cannot serve as a generic comparator registry.

### 4.4 Empirical possibility-field conditioner

This is a **derived query predicate**, not a copied actor value.

For actor fact `F_actor`, a comparator rule resolves something like:

```text
C_empirical(r) := survey_age(r) in [35,35]
```

or:

```text
C_empirical(r) := survey_age_group(r) == AGE_BAND_X
```

or:

```text
C_empirical(r) := respondent_region(r) in governed_historical_region_set(P,t,source,wave)
```

The predicate may then be used by the D08 algebra to estimate an E1/E2 possibility geometry if the later query is separately authorized.

The comparator does **not** assert that the historical respondents are the actor, structural equivalents of the actor, psychological peers of the actor, or members of the actor's generated position.

## 5. Current actor-fact authority audit

### 5.1 ADP-007 — initialization does not supply universal demographics

It owns actor creation and the `V3_INITIAL_FACT_RULE_REGISTRY` admission contract, but v1 has:

### 5.2 ADP-009 — position is explicitly not the bridge

```text

!= RESOLVED STRUCTURAL CONTEXT
!= POSITION CANDIDATE

```

It also rejects demographic category → position and raw prevalence → actor membership/probability.

D12D adds the reciprocal non-use rule: even a valid ADP-009 fact must not be repurposed as a demographic comparator merely because a survey field has a similar label.

### 5.3 ADP-010 — objective exposure history exists, but travel does not follow

ADP-010 owns exact actor-specific `AVAILABLE -> OPPORTUNITY -> ENCOUNTER -> EXPOSURE` records.

Its `EXPOSURE` is objective presentation/impingement through an encounter. It does not imply awareness, memory, belief, response, relationship—or physical travel/migration.

Therefore:

- a generated exposure fact can potentially be used in a source-specific comparator whose survey item measures the same objective exposure;
- an exposure fact cannot automatically produce a `traveled abroad`, `migrant`, `resident of X` or similar empirical predicate.

### 5.4 ADP-015 — household and relationship biography is real but survey categories are not copied

But ADP-015 deliberately separates:

```text

HOUSEHOLD_MEMBERSHIP != PARENTHOOD
```

and does not define a universal `marital_status` category or a source-survey household definition.

Hence `marital` and `household_size` comparability has partial current endpoint authority but still requires a target derivation/comparability rule.

### 5.5 ADP-016 — memory is not objective biography

ADP-016 establishes:

```text

!= GENERATED_MEMORY_STATE
!= CURRENT_MEMORY_ACCESSIBILITY
```

A remembered trip is not automatically evidence that the actor's objective travel-history comparator should be selected, and an objective trip need not be remembered.

Only a survey construct explicitly measuring remembered/self-reported experience could potentially compare to memory—and that would require a separate source-specific rule.

### 5.6 ADP-017 — belief is not affiliation/self-identification

ADP-017 separates objective fact, memory, epistemic stance and claim accessibility. It does not permit structural causation or objective fact to become actor belief automatically.

For D12D:

## 7. Authority test — mandatory ten questions

Every proposed mapping must pass all ten tests below.

### 7.1 Actor fact authority

The input must cite the exact generated fact/event owner, version, fact ID/hash, effective time and value semantics.

If the owner is missing, disposition:

`ACTOR_FACT_AUTHORITY_MISSING`

The comparator may not create the actor fact.

### 7.2 Empirical coordinate authority

The source/wave/country/native-or-normalized coordinate and value semantics must be governed.

If not:

`EMPIRICAL_COORDINATE_AUTHORITY_MISSING`

### 7.3 Comparability rule

There must be an explicit rule saying the actor construct and survey construct are comparable for the intended reverse-DDM purpose.

Endpoint authority alone is insufficient.

Absent rule:

`TARGET_COMPARABILITY_LAW_REQUIRED`

or, where the construct can only ever be resolved narrowly:

`COMPARABLE_ONLY_WITH_SOURCE_SPECIFIC_RULE`

### 7.4 Normalization

The rule must define exact units, intervals, category taxonomies or crosswalks.

No fuzzy matching, display-label equality, “obvious” age bands, current geographic hierarchy, likely religion equivalence or common occupational label is authority.

### 7.5 Time semantics

The rule must say whether the comparison is:

- contemporaneous;
- life-stage based;
- reference-age based;
- retrospective over a bounded window;
- historically reclassified at a reference time;
- noncomparable.

### 7.6 Source scope

A mapping may be valid for:

- one native survey item;
- one source family;
- one country;
- one wave;
- several sources under one versioned harmonization authority;
- no source automatically.

### 7.7 Missingness / unknown

Actor unknown/missing and respondent missing/special states must remain separate. Actor-side unknown does not authorize unconditioned empirical fallback. Respondent-side missingness belongs to the D08 risk set.

### 7.8 Multiple possible matches

One actor property may map to multiple empirical coordinates or categories only under explicit cardinality/composition authority:

- alternative;
- conjunctive;
- set-valued;
- ambiguous.

No first/nearest/modal fallback.

### 7.9 No match

No lawful comparator must yield a typed no-match/valid-empty/deferred/negative state. It must not broaden the query silently.

### 7.10 Provenance

The resolved empirical predicate must retain the exact actor fact, comparator rule, crosswalk/geography/time authority, survey coordinate and predicate AST/hash lineage.

## 9. Critical comparator-mode test

D12D rejects a global choice among A–G.

| Mode | Lawful role | Typical fields |
|---|---|---|
| A `EXACT VALUE EQUALITY` | Only identical governed value domain + time semantics | exact age in rare fully aligned case; exact canonical IDs under same taxonomy |
| B `VERSIONED NORMALIZATION/CROSSWALK` | Different taxonomies/units preserving same construct | education, age bands, income bands, occupation, religion, language |
| C `RANGE/BAND MATCH` | scalar actor value to empirical interval | age, income where absolute bands are governed |
| D `HIERARCHICAL GEOGRAPHIC MATCH` | place to historical survey geography | residence/locality/region/country |
| E `SOURCE-SPECIFIC SEMANTIC EQUIVALENCE` | source-native construct is narrower/different | sex/gender, employment, language, religion, ethnicity, marital, household, migration/travel |
| F `TEMPORALLY CONDITIONAL EQUIVALENCE` | value meaning depends on reference time/window | nearly every mutable biography; geography and age especially |
| G `NOT LAWFULLY COMPARABLE` | construct mismatch or negative authority | belief→religion affiliation; structural position→demographic coordinate; memory→objective history; response leakage |

A rule may require several modes simultaneously. For example residence may require `D+B+E+F`.

## 12. Multiple-match law

One actor fact may map to several empirical coordinates or values. D12D requires explicit cardinality semantics:

```text
ZERO_LAWFUL_COMPARATORS
ONE_EXACT_COMPARATOR
MULTIPLE_ALTERNATIVE_COMPARATORS
MULTIPLE_CONJUNCTIVE_COMPARATORS
MULTIPLE_COMPARATORS_AMBIGUOUS
```

D07 controls composition: individual empirical field permissions do not authorize arbitrary conjunctions.

Therefore:

- alternatives are not silently OR'ed;
- multiple properties are not silently AND'ed;
- one-to-many crosswalks do not choose first/modal/nearest;
- multilingual/multi-affiliation facts require explicit set semantics.

## 13. Time semantics theorem

A generated actor fact and an empirical coordinate can be semantically identical in value space and still be noncomparable because their reference times differ.

Examples:

- actor age 35 now vs respondent age 35 twenty years before the target historical period;
- actor current employment vs survey “worked last week”;
- current residence vs survey region at interview;
- adult self-identification vs survey question about childhood religion;
- travel event vs survey “ever traveled” or “past 12 months”;
- historical locality after administrative boundary changes.

Thus temporal alignment is part of comparator identity, not optional metadata.

## 14. Source-scope theorem

A normalized V550 field name is not sufficient cross-survey authority.

The comparator must know whether its rule applies to:

- one native variable;
- one source family;
- one wave;
- one country;
- a precisely versioned harmonized set.

If several sources happen to use `income_level = high`, `religion_label = X` or `region = Y`, D12D does not treat those as equivalent without a crosswalk/semantic authority.

## 15. Provenance theorem

A lawful empirical conditioner must preserve the chain:

```text
actor fact + owner + effective time
→ comparator rule/version/hash
→ normalization/crosswalk/geography/time authority
→ survey source/wave/country
→ native/canonical empirical coordinate authority
→ exact empirical predicate AST/hash
```

When the predicate later creates an E1/E2 empirical fact, D08's normal predicate/risk-set/topology/measure/provenance identity must also survive.

The actor fact remains comparator provenance. It is not copied into respondent incidence.
