## 4. What authority must specify before discovery

### A. Search-family identity

Authority must identify the semantic family: unary fork, response×coordinate association, conjunction, interaction, recursive subgroup, reversal, cotravel, branch-conditioned cotravel, k-way response configuration, temporal change or cross-source disagreement.

These families have different candidate roles, topology, denominator, depth and validation law.

### B. Candidate atom universe

Authority must supply a finite or finitely constructible set of eligible atoms:

- focal question/response terrain;
- eligible other governed response questions/events;
- eligible empirical social/demographic coordinates;
- lawful value/event atoms;
- branch/cohort predicates where relevant;
- exact exclusions/negative authority.

The universe may be generated from rule authority. It does **not** need to enumerate every relationship the data may form.

Current `INDICATOR_FIELDS` is an implementation inventory, not generic target authority.

### C. Source/respondent universe

Every candidate evaluation binds:

- source;
- raw wave;
- canonical wave;
- country/geography;
- survey/sample universe;
- respondent identity domain;
- comparability/harmonization authority where comparing times/sources.

Same-respondent discovery requires a genuine common respondent domain. Cross-source disagreement normally compares separately scoped empirical facts rather than row-joining respondents.

### D. Response topology/event semantics

Authority must bind scalar/nominal/ordinal/multiselect/repeated/ranked/routed/matrix semantics, including event multiplicity/order/set identity, duplicates, substantive response states and typed missing/refusal/IAP/not-asked states.

D04 and D13C prove these cannot safely be inferred from “nonblank row.”

### E. Empirical-coordinate authority

Coordinates such as income, education, religion, region, employment, etc. can be eligible **empirical conditioners** only under exact authority.

D10 remains binding:

```text
EMPIRICAL RESPONDENT COORDINATE
!=
GENERATED NINE-STRATUM STRUCTURAL POSITION
```

Discovery eligibility never authorizes demographic→actor psyche, belief, identity or generated structural-position inference.

### F. Predicate/composition grammar

Individual atom permission does not automatically authorize composition.

Authority must say which combinations may be generated:

```text
coordinate=value
response=value
A AND B
A AND B AND C
response X=x AND coordinate A
branch predicate B(r)
```

and what order/role semantics apply.

This is the authority seam D07 left open.

### G. Interaction depth / complexity frontier

The search frontier must be finite.

Authority must bind maximum arity/depth or another finite complexity budget, field reuse/recursion semantics where relevant, and the **claimed discoverability class**.

If XOR/parity or other zero-lower-order-signal interaction is in scope, positive marginal/lower-order gain cannot be a hidden prerequisite for reaching higher-order candidates.

### H. Risk-set, denominator and measure

D08 makes risk-set identity part of empirical fact identity.

Authority must bind:

- applicability/routing;
- missing/special handling;
- complete-case/anchor-selected/condition-selected or other denominator semantics;
- raw versus governed weighted measure;
- weight authority/status.

Equal displayed numbers under different risk sets are not the same fact.

### I. Support, sparse-cell, validation and multiplicity

Authority must define typed behavior for supported, underpowered, zero-support, zero-overlap, invalid topology, missing authority, noncomparable and valid-empty candidates.

Where many candidates are screened with inferential/statistical claims, multiplicity/validation law is required.

D12F does **not** invent a universal alpha, p-value, effect metric or threshold.

### J. Algorithm coverage/pruning contract

Algorithmic pruning may not silently become semantic absence.

Top-k, top-two, positive-gain, beam-width, screening or adaptive decisions that affect reachability are part of the coverage/replay contract.

The engine must state what portion of the authorized frontier it guarantees to discover and its lawful blind spots.

### K. Branch/cotravel identity

Branch-conditioned discovery requires:

- exact branch predicate and provenance sufficient to recreate membership, or immutable membership;
- source/scope identity;
- same-respondent join identity where applicable;
- candidate response topology;
- overlap/risk-set semantics.

A branch ID or scalar association score is not membership.

### L. Temporal/cross-source comparability

Temporal fork discovery requires governed cross-wave comparability.

Cross-source disagreement requires explicit source-universe identity and comparison authority. Shared field names cannot create equivalence, and unrelated respondent IDs cannot create a join.

### M. Negative authority

At minimum fail closed for:

- forbidden candidate atoms;
- ungoverned compositions;
- incompatible topologies;
- unsupported depth;
- missing risk-set law;
- false cross-source joins;
- missing comparability;
- silent missingness-as-attitude;
- demographic/social association→actor psyche/identity/belief/knowledge/behavior.

### N. Replay/provenance

A discovery result must bind enough identity to reproduce and audit it:

- authority/registry version/hash;
- candidate grammar;
- exact empirical source/scope;
- topology/missingness/risk-set/measure;
- algorithm/version/parameters;
- deterministic order/tie behavior;
- candidate/result identity;
- validation outcome;
- rejected/pruned candidate trace where required for a completeness claim, or exact deterministic reconstruction.

This does not require permanently storing every rejected candidate.

## 6. Predeclaration hostile court

### Named-query prison

Synthetic XOR:

| income | education | Y=1 |
|---|---|---:|
| low | low | 0% |
| low | high | 100% |
| high | low | 100% |
| high | high | 0% |

Every unary conditional is 50/50. The conjunction is deterministic.

If authority only names:

```text
P(Y | income=low)
P(Y | education=high)
```

and nobody names:

```text
P(Y | income=low AND education=high)
```

the system never evaluates the conjunction.

`PREDECLARED_QUERY_ONLY__TARGET_INSUFFICIENT`

The lawful repair is to authorize eligible atoms plus bounded conjunction grammar, not to guess and predeclare this particular answer.

### Search-everything failure

If arbitrary columns, recodings, source mixing, topologies and unbounded conjunction depth are permitted with no risk-set/support/multiplicity law, the miner can manufacture:

- administrative-column forks;
- sparse extremes;
- routing artifacts;
- false respondent joins;
- multiplicity-driven “discoveries”;
- unrepeatable candidate sets.

`UNBOUNDED_SEARCH__UNLAWFUL`

Autonomy means empirical selection inside authority, not absence of authority.

## 8. Algorithm-family court

D12F does not select among:

- exhaustive bounded search;
- greedy search;
- beam search;
- recursive search;
- adaptive search;
- statistical screening followed by exact replay;
- generic candidate-evaluation contract.

`ALGORITHM_FAMILY_OPEN`

Any algorithm must preserve the semantic guarantee it claims.

### Exhaustive bounded

Can claim completeness only over an explicitly bounded lawful frontier. Exhaustiveness does not waive topology, risk-set, support, validation or negative authority.

### Greedy

May be lawful for a declared limited diagnostic or if a separate proof shows pruning preserves the claimed discovery class.

D01 proves current V550 primary-star search can miss B×C when A is selected primary. D01/D02 show parity can be invisible under positive lower-order gain.

### Beam

Beam width/scoring affects reachability. It is therefore part of coverage/replay law when completeness is claimed.

### Recursive

Must govern branch predicate identity, child coverage, field reuse, depth, stop semantics, support and membership replay.

Historical V548 and current V550 are different recursive geometries; neither is target ontology.

### Adaptive

Adaptation must be deterministic/replayable or use separately governed randomness and cannot expand candidate semantics beyond authority.

### Screening → exact replay

Exact replay of survivors does not prove screened-out candidates irrelevant. Screening needs a governed false-negative/coverage relationship to the target claim.

### Generic candidate evaluation

A generic evaluator could preserve authority/algorithm separation if candidate identity includes family, scope, target, predicate, topology, risk set, measure, support/validation authority and provenance, while the search controller separately declares its coverage frontier.

D12F does not select this as architecture.

## 9. Mandatory guarantees

### Permutation/order independence

Required where candidate semantics are unordered.

D04 proves current cloud pair coverage is processing-order dependent. Directional A→B/B→A can be distinct, but direction must be explicit rather than inherited from processing order.

### Minority retention / non-top categories

A supported minority cannot become semantic absence because it is not top-k.

D02 shows current recursive V550 follows only two most frequent child values.

### Rejected-candidate traceability

A completeness claim needs proof of what was considered and why candidates were rejected/pruned, or exact deterministic reconstruction of that candidate frontier.

### XOR/parity discoverability

If interaction/higher-order discovery claims include zero-lower-order-signal structure, the algorithm must make it reachable.

### Arbitrary lawful conjunctions

“Arbitrary” means not individually named. It does not mean unlimited.

Every conjunction inside the authorized atom grammar/depth must be constructible according to the search family's claimed coverage.

### Exact denominator/risk set

Required.

D08 proves denominator/risk-set/topology/measure identity is part of the empirical fact.

### Reproducibility/provenance

Required. D04 order/resume failures show output rows alone cannot prove the intended search was replayed.

### Typed absence

No row is not a sufficient result state.

### No demographic→actor psyche

Required negative authority. Discovery is population empirical evidence only.
