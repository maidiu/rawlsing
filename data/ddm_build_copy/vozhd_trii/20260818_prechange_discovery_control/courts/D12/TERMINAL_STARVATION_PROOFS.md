1. the information distinction that must survive;
2. which declared terminal DDM service uses it;
3. a World-A / World-B distinction lost if removed;
4. why remaining evidence cannot reconstruct the distinction;
5. current authority/consumer state;
6. bounded-query versus future-query-frontier status;
7. whether respondent incidence is necessary or an exact information-equivalent contingency suffices.

Services:

- **A** — Autonomous Forward Social Discovery.
- **B** — Reverse Positioned-Subject Empirical Possibility Inference.
- **C** — Structural / Stratal Field Production.

```text
POPULATION EMPIRICAL FACT
!= POSITIONED-SUBJECT EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY FIELD
!= V3 DEVELOPED / REALIZED DISPOSITION
```

```text

!= EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY-FIELD CONDITIONER
```

## 2. Why C20–C23 and C32–C33 are not terminal starvation requirements

### C20 — E3 cloud composite summaries

No starvation occurs if E3 disappears while deeper exact E1/E2/E0B information remains. E3 is lossy and replayable as a diagnostic view where useful.

### C21 — E4 scalar association diagnostics

No starvation occurs if NMI/Cramer's V/lift/etc. disappear while their exact source contingency remains. They are noninvertible diagnostics.

### C22 — Historical recursive-tree materialization

The target requires recursive discovery and branch predicate/membership reconstructibility, not the historical/current stored tree shape. Tree artifacts may be regenerated under a selected future search authority/algorithm.

### C23 — Historical direct profile/persona assignment machinery

Removal does not starve the target because its valid empirical geometry is retained under C02–C18 while V3 owns developed disposition/belief. Direct survey covariance -> finished actor psyche is a forbidden shortcut.

### C32 — Generic cross-universe pooling/backfill statistical composition model

Source-indexed disagreement and explicit composition authority state are required; no universal pooling/backfill model is established as terminal law.

### C33 — Specific universal numeric uncertainty estimator

Uncertainty honesty and method provenance are required; one universal formula across all designs/topologies is not established.

