## 5. Terminal workload geometry — W01 through W17

`TERMINAL_WORKLOAD_COST_MATRIX.csv` is dispositive for per-workload geometry.

### W01 — unary empirical fact

Unavoidable logical work is exact predicate/risk-set evaluation plus target response aggregation and raw/governed measure handling.

Reference scan geometry without assuming a physical accelerator is proportional to the relevant coordinate incidence plus relevant target events. A matching fixed E1 can eliminate recomputation only for the exact fact identity it represents.

### W02 — multi-coordinate conjunction

The machine must perform an exact same-respondent conjunction. Cost increases with `K`, the coordinate incidence needed to evaluate its predicates, and the selected cohort. Independent marginals are not a lawful acceleration.

A fixed exact conjunction contingency can be reused for that fixed query, but cannot answer a new conjunct.

### W03 — response-response pair

Cost consists of common/declared risk-set resolution plus same-respondent binding of the two response event sets and exact cell accumulation.

A fixed pair E2 can avoid repeated joins. E4 association metrics are separate optional calculations over the exact contingency.

### W04 — J-way joint

The unavoidable semantic operation is exact same-respondent binding across all `J` targets under the fixed condition/risk set.

For a sparse exact representation:

`M_J <= min(N_joint, product_j C_j)`

The machine need not physically emit the full dense Cartesian product merely to preserve observed-zero semantics if response domains plus sparse occupied cells make those zeros exact. But it may not replace J-way incidence with lower-order marginals.

### W05 — minority discovery

The terminal cost is candidate coverage plus exact support/uncertainty evaluation for the governed minority/heterogeneity frontier.

Supported low-frequency configurations may not be suppressed solely because evaluating them is expensive.

### W06 — interaction / reversal discovery

The principal cost cliff is candidate count. If the authority claims exhaustive coordinate-subset coverage through arity `K`, the subset frontier alone can grow as:

`H_K = sum_{k=2..K} choose(I,k)`

before value-category and target-response expansion.

That is a semantic coverage cost when that exhaustive class is claimed. It is **not** a requirement to rescan the complete C11 corpus separately for every candidate; shared evaluation is an implementation optimization D15 may seek.

### W07 — recursive social fork

The target law is not a binary tree. Generic cost is:

`sum_{b in visited branches} [branch-membership/replay cost + sum_{f in F_b} exact candidate cost on R_b]`

Branch count and risk-set size are data- and authority-dependent. D14 does not canonize the current V550 top-two recursion.

### W08 — branch-conditioned cotravel

Pay once to replay/obtain exact branch membership, then pay the relevant pair/J-way cost within that member set.

Persisting exact branch membership can be an accelerator, but deterministic predicate replay is information-equivalent under D13.

### W09 — forward autonomous search

The general law is:

`T_search = T_frontier + sum_{f in F_eval} T_eval(f) + T_validation/replay`

For exhaustive exact coverage, `F_eval = F`. For heuristic search, `F_eval < F` may be lawful only with the correspondingly narrower/proven coverage claim.

### W10 — reverse unary field

Pipeline:

actor fact -> comparator -> exact empirical predicate -> risk set/respondents -> unary target field.

Comparator no-match/noncomparability can terminate before respondent work. Zero support can terminate after exact cohort/risk-set evaluation.

### W11 — reverse multi-property J-way field

Cost includes all comparator resolutions, exact `K`-property conjunction, then J-way joint construction independently for each admitted universe.

Sparse `M_J` is the principal target-state size. No independent-marginal shortcut is lawful.

### W12 — actor comparator resolution

This is a genuine governed operation over comparator/crosswalk/range/geographic/time/source authority. It does not intrinsically require a respondent scan.

A typed ambiguity result may terminate instead of expanding every possible comparator combination. Cartesian expansion is not a mandatory cost unless a separate authority specifically requests alternative fields.

### W13 — temporal empirical comparison

Replay each required time-slice fact, then apply exact comparability/harmonization/proxy authority. Cost is additive across admitted waves plus comparison overhead.

A current snapshot cannot replace historical replay.

### W14 — cross-universe disagreement

Replay each admitted universe independently and compare source-indexed facts. By default the cost is additive over universes.

There is no default respondent join or pooling operation. Such an operation exists only under separate positive authority.

### W15 — structural empirical admission

Cost is empirical fact replay plus exact structural-admission/transform authority. The empirical reconstruction may dominate, but D14 does not assume that without measurement.

### W16 — typed absence/routing/topology

The cost grain is response events and routing/eligibility facts, not scalar rows. Multiselect, repeated and ranked questions may legitimately carry multiple events per respondent/question.

Dropping event multiplicity would be a false saving.

### W17 — replay optional views

Replay cost inherits the underlying operation:

- E1 -> W01/W02;
- fixed E2 -> W03/W04;
- E3 -> pair replay plus diagnostic metrics;
- E4 -> metrics over exact contingency;
- branch/tree/search views -> W07/W09.

This workload measures the cost of *not* making every derived artifact permanent. It does not make those artifacts terminally required.

## 7. Autonomous discovery cost/coverage court

`AUTONOMOUS_DISCOVERY_COST_COVERAGE.csv` is dispositive.

### Bounded exhaustive evaluation

Strongest explicit coverage, potentially highest candidate count. It guarantees discovery only inside the exact declared finite/finitely constructible grammar.

### Greedy search

Can be much cheaper in evaluated candidates, but its coverage is path-local unless an independent theorem establishes more. It cannot be called XOR-complete, minority-complete or reversal-complete by default.

### Beam/adaptive search

Offers an intermediate cost/coverage frontier. Beam truncation is heuristic pruning unless a safe-pruning theorem exists.

### Screen then exact

Cost:

`T_screen(F) + sum_{f in F_pass} T_exact(f)`

Potentially attractive, but the screen's false-negative law determines what coverage survives. A unary-signal screen cannot claim exhaustive XOR discovery by default because XOR may have zero unary signal.

### Recursive search

Strong for structure that becomes visible inside reached branches. Cost follows visited branch/member frontiers, not one universal tree formula. It can still miss relations that require entering a branch the root criterion never exposes.

### Support/risk-set pruning

Lawful when the *authority* declares the feasibility/support boundary and the resulting typed state is preserved. A cost-driven threshold is not allowed to erase a supported minority merely because it is inconvenient.

### Binding conclusion

The combinatorial obligation is **coverage-class dependent**.

D12 requires autonomous discovery. It does not require exhaustive evaluation of every mathematically imaginable conjunction. Once D15 chooses or defines an authority-backed coverage class, D14's formulas determine the corresponding candidate-cost frontier.

## 13. Hostile cost questions — explicit answers

### 1. Which current V550 computations dominate asymptotically?

Recursive candidate evaluation and pair/joint/candidate-combination work have the steepest growth in their controlling axes; current eager repeated scans/checkpointing add substantial linear/write amplification. Actual wall-clock dominance is unknown without a populated run.

### 2. Which costs exist only because current artifacts are eagerly materialized?

Per-question/final serialization of many derived summaries, current checkpoint amplification, eager tree/signature/cloud/diagnostic table writes, and parts of current aggregate publication. Their underlying semantic computations are not necessarily avoidable, but universal persistence is.

### 3. Which terminal capabilities inherently require respondent-bound scans or equivalent indexed lookup work?

Future-selectable conjunctions, response pairs, J-way joints, branch-conditioned facts, recursive discovery, interaction discovery, reverse fields, flexible risk sets and topology-aware replay. An exact fixed contingency can substitute only for the exact bounded query it contains.

### 4. Which costs grow combinatorially with interaction/joint arity?

Candidate frontier count can grow as combinations of `I`/`Q` and their value/target expansions. Dense potential joint-state space grows as products of response cardinalities. Actual occupied J-way state is bounded by selected respondents.

### 5. Which combinatorial costs are semantic obligations and which are algorithm consequences?

Covering every candidate in an **explicit exhaustive declared class** is a semantic coverage obligation. Evaluating each candidate in a separate full scan, using binary recursion, testing a chosen-primary star, computing all diagnostics, or materializing every result are algorithm/implementation consequences.

### 6. Where can exact bounded contingencies lawfully avoid deeper rescans?

Whenever condition, target tuple/arity, risk set, source/scope, topology, measure and authority identity all match exactly. A fixed E2 can answer that bounded query directly.

### 7. Where does future-authorized flexibility force retention of deeper incidence?

Any new conditioner/conjunction, target subset/arity, branch, risk set, topology, source component or discovery frontier outside the cached contingency. This is why C11 survives even if most day-to-day queries are cheap.

### 8. What is the likely cost role of C11 relative to the current broad determinicity suite?

C11 moves the mandatory burden toward preserving one deep exact information generator and paying query/search costs when requested, while allowing many present summary artifacts to become optional replay/cache work. It may reduce eager repeated computation, but D14 cannot quantify or choose how because C11 has no certified implementation.

### 9. Which current computations have no terminal justification except as optional diagnostics/caches?

The diagnostic position/outlier/signature families, E3/E4, current primary-star interaction summary as a target discovery mechanism, current recursive-tree materialization as the terminal recursive capability, and current within-frame co-signature as branch cotravel. Bounded V550J exact joint work is different: its underlying joint capability is terminal, although the artifact remains derived.

### 10. What empirical/runtime measurements remain essential for hardware/runtime budgets?

At minimum:

- populated `E`, `G`, `Z`, `R`, `Q`, `I`, `C_q`, support/selectivity distributions;
- per-workload wall/CPU/RSS/I/O/bytes;
- query reuse/frequency and cache hit/invalidation rates;
- authorized discovery `F/P/H`, branch `B/D/R_b`, and pruning rates;
- reverse-query `K/J/U` and comparator no-match/ambiguity distributions;
- source snapshot/C11 physical byte sizes;
- concurrency/SLA/update cadence;
- current and concrete C11 implementation measurements on the **same semantic workload**.

These are implementation-sizing measurements, not discovery blockers.
