## 3. The nine strata are relational fields, not demographic buckets

Current governing law defines the finite nine-stratum field as:

1. Planetary
2. Imperial Block
3. Nation State
4. Sub-national Region
5. Sect / Ethno / Class
6. Party / Movement
7. Neighborhood / Household
8. Family
9. Self

The governing documents do not authorize these as nine mutually exclusive demographic labels. They are relational structural fields in which several simultaneous and contradictory relations may lawfully coexist.

Consequences for D10:

- a shared noun does not establish identity;
- a survey field called `region` is not therefore the `Sub-national Region` structural relation;
- a field called `class_level` is not therefore a generated class relation;
- a religion label is not therefore a sect position;
- a household count is not therefore a household role;
- no one coordinate may be forced to choose an actor's single total position.

## 8. No positive structural-evidence shortcut is certified

D10 distinguishes two propositions:

1. a raw coordinate may lawfully condition empirical survey distributions; and
2. a raw coordinate may lawfully constitute evidence admitted into an ADP-009 structural-position candidate rule.

The first is established for the current V550 coordinate set under its empirical use gates.

The second requires a registered ADP-009 rule or another separately accepted generated-position producer defining the transformation, target predicate, provenance, time, scope, and binding key.

No such rule was located for the inspected raw coordinates.

Accordingly `evidentiary_use_allowed=false` in the D10 matrix means:

> not authorized **for the empirical-coordinate → generated-position seam under current accepted authority**.

It does **not** mean a future accepted producer could never use empirical evidence. It means D10 may not invent that producer today.

ADP-009's generic admissible class `GOVERNED_POSITION_DISTRIBUTION` does not make every V550 demographic distribution a governed actor-position distribution. Object class and producer provenance must be proven, not inferred from vocabulary.
