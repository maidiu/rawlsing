## 6. Autonomous discovery architecture

D15 chooses two truthful execution modes over one authority contract.

### EXACT_BOUNDED

Complete for the exact finite/finitely constructible authority-declared frontier. Candidate enumeration/evaluation cannot require unary signal unless that restriction is itself part of the declared coverage; therefore XOR/zero-unary interactions can be discovered when the frontier includes them.

### ADAPTIVE_EXPLORATORY

Exact-validates every emitted result but claims no completeness for unevaluated candidates absent a proven safe-pruning theorem.

This architecture preserves autonomy because authority declares the search **space** and validation law while data select the discovered relationship.

It does not claim universal exhaustive discovery over every imaginable relationship.

## 15. Hostile architecture questions

### Why smaller than full current V550/V550J?

One deep C11 carrier replaces many duplicated analytical materializations as authority. Derived facts and diagnostics are replayed/cached only when useful.

### Why richer than sparse conditional facts?

C11 retains future-selectable respondent-bound event/coordinate incidence, enabling new conjunctions, branches, risk sets, joints and autonomous discovery.

### Why no historical persona shortcut?

Joint geometry becomes an empirical possibility field only. V3 still generates the actual life through history.

### Why no independent-marginal flattening?

Exact pair/J-way facts come from same-respondent incidence; parity/XOR is a mandatory fixture.

### Why no survey-demographic/structural-position collapse?

Actor comparator and structural admission are separate authorities; generated structural position retains CAN-006/ADP-009/V3 ownership.

### Why empirical possibility is not destiny?

The field lacks actor-state authority and must pass through V3 causal history. Same fields can produce divergent lives.

### Why no silent pooling?

EmpiricalUniverse is a canonical physical boundary; linkage/pooling/backfill requires positive authority.

### Why discovery is autonomous but governed?

Authority defines the lawful frontier; data select winners. Exact bounded and exploratory modes make coverage claims explicit.

### Why caches may be optional?

C11 exact replay preserves terminal meaning. Cache is acceleration, not storage authority.

### What expensive work is intrinsic?

Exact respondent/risk-set binding, requested joint geometry, topology/routing/weights, source identity, comparator resolution, structural admission provenance and whatever discovery coverage is explicitly claimed.

### What current expensive work may disappear?

Repeated scans, broad checkpoint/materialization amplification, current secondary/tree/cloud/signature outputs as canonical state and fragmented V551/V552 packet republishing—only after positive equivalence migration.

### What cannot be claimed yet?

Production savings %, runtime, RSS/I/O, storage bytes, hardware budget, cache hit rates, populated source equivalence, real frontier sizes/support/selectivity.
