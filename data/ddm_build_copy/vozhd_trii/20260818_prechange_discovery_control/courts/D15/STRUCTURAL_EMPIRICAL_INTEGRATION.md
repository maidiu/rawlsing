# D15 — STRUCTURAL EMPIRICAL INTEGRATION

## 1. Governing seam

The target structural path is:

```text
C11 empirical evidence
→ exact EmpiricalQuerySpec
→ exact EmpiricalFact
→ STRUCTURAL_EMPIRICAL_ADMISSION_AUTHORITY
→ StructuralEmpiricalAdmission
→ V553 temporal structural production
→ V554 affordance
→ V555 structural mediation possibility
→ V556 pressure-flow topology
→ V557 lossless publication
→ V558 structural repository/context
→ V3 actor-specific history
```

This lane is separate from reverse positioned-subject empirical possibility inference.

## 2. Noncollapse

```text
EmpiricalCoordinateFact
!= generated nine-stratum structural position
```

```text
EmpiricalFact
!= StructuralEmpiricalAdmission
```

```text
StructuralEmpiricalAdmission
!= ResolvedStructuralContext
!= actor encounter/exposure/belief
```

No structural rule may use shared strings such as `income=low`, `religion=X`, `employment=unemployed` or `region=Y` as proof that an empirical respondent coordinate already is a generated V3 structural position.

## 3. Why a new admission service is necessary

D10 found no generic current empirical-coordinate/configuration -> generated structural-position authority. D11 found narrow current V551/V552 and V551J/V552J structural paths plus stranded broader empirical meaning. D12 makes structural/stratal empirical evidence terminal while explicitly leaving broader promotions authority-bound.

The target therefore needs one canonical structural admission seam rather than separate implicit semantics hidden in V551, V551J, V552 and V552J packet shapes.

The new service does not grant all empirical facts structural meaning. It centralizes the requirement that every admitted fact have a positive rule.

## 4. Admission authority contract

Every rule must state:

- allowed EmpiricalFact type/query family;
- exact source/universe applicability;
- time/geography applicability and comparability requirements;
- empirical predicate/target semantics required;
- structural target identity: stratum, relation, pressure/affordance/other producer-specific target;
- transformation/interpretive meaning;
- whether relation is source→target, target→source or otherwise directional;
- conditions/limits;
- support/uncertainty admissibility requirements;
- whether source disagreement/noncomparability blocks admission or creates plural structural evidence;
- negative authority: what the fact cannot establish;
- version/hash/effective period;
- downstream structural producer/use.

## 5. Admission output

`StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` contains:

- admission ID;
- exact EmpiricalFact / EmpiricalQuerySpec reference;
- source EmpiricalUniverse identity;
- structural rule ID/version/hash;
- target structural object/relation/stratum;
- exact interpreted structural proposition or bounded producer input;
- temporal scope;
- support/uncertainty state;
- provenance/replay refs;
- negative/limit authority;
- typed state.

Respondent IDs and respondent events do not need to cross this boundary. The exact empirical fact remains replayable from C11.

## 6. Admissible fact families

D15 does not automatically authorize any family merely by naming it. The architecture supports rules for, where separately governed:

- unary distributions/prevalence;
- empirical-coordinate conditional distributions;
- bounded conjunction conditionals;
- exact response-response joint facts;
- branch/cohort-conditioned facts;
- conditional reversal/interactions;
- temporal empirical change;
- source-indexed disagreement.

Each use needs its own structural admission rule. A discovery result is not structurally important merely because it scored highly.

## 7. Existing V551/V552 semantics

Existing narrow V551/V552 and V551J/V552J semantics are deliberate precedent and must be migrated by explicit parity, not discarded because their packet shapes are replaced.

Migration adapter path:

```text
current lawful V550/V550J fact
→ compatibility EmpiricalQuerySpec/EmpiricalFact
→ compatibility StructuralEmpiricalAdmission rule matching existing V551/V552 meaning
→ corrected V553 intake
```

Once parity is proven, V551/V551J packet materialization and V552/V552J split unary/joint packaging may retire or collapse into the unified admission contract.

The producer-owned downstream structural meaning itself survives where lawful.

## 8. V553 revalidation

V553's jurisdiction remains correct: it temporalizes/harmonizes already-governed structural evidence and preserves producer meaning/provenance.

What changes is its upstream producer surface.

Old assumption:

`V552/V552J narrow structural facts = complete structurally admitted evidence frontier`.

Corrected target:

`any exact EmpiricalFact with positive StructuralEmpiricalAdmission authority may become V553 input`.

V553 therefore gains a typed intake/adapter for StructuralEmpiricalAdmission. It must not itself decide whether an arbitrary empirical fact deserves structural meaning.

## 9. V554–V558 revalidation

V554, V555 and V556 continue to consume structural objects, not survey objects. Their semantics remain valid with corrected V553 supply.

V557/V558 continue to publish/resolve structural priors losslessly. They may need schema/adapter updates to carry admission/provenance refs, but no semantic jurisdiction change follows.

`PositionedSubjectEmpiricalPossibilityField` is expressly excluded from V554–V558 structural prior publication.

## 10. Raw empirical coordinate vs generated structural position

Generated structural position remains CAN-006/ADP-009/V3 authority.

A structural admission may conclude that a population empirical fact bears on a structural relation or field. It may not silently assert that a generated actor occupies the corresponding structural position.

Example:

- `P(response | survey income band=low)` can be a lawful empirical fact.
- a separately governed rule may admit it as evidence relevant to a material-resource pressure relation.
- this does not mean a generated actor with some income fact automatically occupies a nine-stratum `class position` merely because labels resemble one another.

Actor generated position remains separately generated/authorized.

## 11. Generic association is not structural truth

E4 scores, predictor rankings, MI/NMI/Cramer's V, entropy reduction, outlier flags or discovery score alone may not be admitted as structural facts.

Where a structural rule cares about a relationship discovered using a diagnostic score, it references the exact underlying EmpiricalFact and the admission rule, not the diagnostic scalar as primary evidence.

## 12. Temporal / source plurality

Structural admission may be source-indexed and temporally plural.

If multiple sources disagree:

- preserve independent admissions/evidence where the structural authority permits both;
- or return a typed conflict/noncomparability state;
- do not average by default.

V553 temporalization then operates over these admitted producer-owned structural inputs under its own comparability law.

## 13. Negative states

At minimum:

- `MISSING_STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION_AUTHORITY`
- `ADMISSION_DENIED`
- `EMPIRICAL_FACT_NONCOMPARABLE`
- `SOURCE_NOT_APPLICABLE_TO_STRUCTURAL_USE`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_UNDER_RULE`
- `TEMPORAL_SCOPE_MISMATCH`
- `STRUCTURAL_TARGET_UNRESOLVED`
- `NEGATIVE_AUTHORITY_ONLY`

Absence of a positive rule does not become generic admission.

## 14. Replay

Structural replay binds:

- exact EmpiricalQuerySpec and EmpiricalFact identity;
- C11 content/source identity;
- StructuralEmpiricalAdmission authority version/hash;
- V553 producer version and temporal/comparability authority;
- downstream structural references where applicable.

A later revised structural interpretation rule does not rewrite the historical admission receipt.

## 15. Terminal benefit

This architecture lets Rawlsing use richer empirical conditionals, joints, branches and temporal/source differences where they genuinely bear on structural history, while preventing two illicit moves:

1. survey coordinate -> generated structural position by name;
2. empirical association -> actor psyche.

The structural world becomes empirically richer without becoming psychologically deterministic.
