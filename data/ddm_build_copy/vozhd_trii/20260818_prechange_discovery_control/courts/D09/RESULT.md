## 2. Core theorem

Two identical displayed answers can differ lawfully at several levels, but those levels are not interchangeable.

```text
same displayed answer

may coexist with

!= empirical fact identity
!= population response-configuration / meaning-field identity
!= structural interpretation identity, if separately governed
!= mediation/framework availability, if separately governed

but does not imply

!= actor belief probability
!= actor belief
!= actor psyche/persona
```

The strongest current statement is therefore:

> **Same answer under a different lawful empirical predicate, risk set, response topology, joint configuration or provenance is a different empirical fact even when the numerical answer distribution is identical. A sufficiently rich collection of such facts can describe a different population response-configuration field. It becomes a different structural interpretation or mediation field only through explicit separate authority. It never becomes an actor belief merely because respondents with some measured configuration tended to answer that way.**

This preserves the historical insight while removing the survey-to-psyche shortcut.

## 4. What “cloud” lawfully means after D03 + D08

D09 rejects the equation:

`cloud == current question_response_attitude_cloud row`

The current E3 attitude-cloud row is a particular derived view. It retains one selected modal joint cell plus derived conditional/baseline and association summaries over multiple risk-set constructions. It cannot stand in for the deeper empirical object.

For D09, the useful historical word **cloud** decomposes into typed empirical content:

- **E1 conditional response facts** — `P(target response topology | exact governed empirical predicate, exact risk set, exact scope)`;

- **E3 derived composite cloud views** — convenient lossy summaries over deeper facts;

The historical proposition survives only when these are not collapsed.

D09 accepts `POPULATION_MEANING_FIELD` only in this restricted sense:

> **A scoped empirical configuration of already-governed response meanings, represented by conditional and/or joint incidence facts sufficient to preserve which answer combinations, contradictions, conditional differences and minority configurations were historically observed or remained empirically possible in the governed respondent universe.**

Required identity includes, as applicable:

- source;

## 11. Mandatory human cases A–F

These are **analytic human fixtures**, not claims about populated survey data.

### A — same answer, different cloud

Two historical populations can give the same focal answer at the same marginal rate while one population's answer co-occurs predominantly with response configuration `{q2=a, q3=b, q4=c}` and the other's with `{q2=d, q3=e, q4=f}`.

**Lawful distinction:** different E2 configuration fact and potentially different E1 conditioned facts; therefore a different population meaning field.

**Not established:** that two individual respondents giving the focal answer meant different things psychologically.

**Terminal relevance:** a future governed structural/mediation rule could distinguish the two historical fields; without such authority the difference remains upstream empirical evidence, not actor state.

### B — same position, different answer

Two generated actors may occupy the same exact governed structural position and still develop opposite answers/beliefs because V3 histories, opportunities, encounters, exposures, mediations and responses can diverge.

**Lawful distinction:** generated developmental history.

**Forbidden shortcut:** structural position -> survey-derived belief probability -> actor belief.

**What this proves:** even a valid structural interpretation function cannot be a deterministic persona compiler.

### C — same cloud, different position

Two generated actors may inhabit the same population-level historical meaning field while occupying different exact generated positions.

**Lawful consequence:** a separately governed V554 applicability or V3 context rule may make different structural affordances available to them.

**Forbidden shortcut:** infer either actor's generated position from the empirical cloud or from raw demographic coordinates.

**What this proves:** population field identity and actor structural-position identity are separate dimensions.

### D — contradictory / multimodal configuration

A historically observed respondent universe may contain several substantial response configurations, including combinations that violate a single-axis ideological ordering.

A modal cloud cell or one scalar association score can erase this structure.

**Required empirical object:** E2 joint incidence at the arity necessary to preserve the contradiction, or an information-equivalent exact contingency.

**Lawful interpretation:** the population field is contradictory/multimodal.

**Forbidden interpretation:** each generated actor is internally contradictory in the same way.

### E — minority but real configuration

A low-frequency response combination may be empirically real and historically meaningful even when it is not the modal cell and would disappear under top-k/modal-only summaries.

**Required discipline:** preserve support/provenance and typed absence; do not convert low prevalence into impossibility.

**Workload implication:** D07 does not require universal pre-materialization, but a later-authorized query must not be impossible merely because the minority configuration was not precomputed.

**Actor implication:** minority population incidence is neither actor membership nor a rare-persona assignment.

### F — cross-field disagreement / local-versus-global field

The same focal answer can sit inside one local response configuration in a labor/economic field and another configuration in a religious, household, national or institutional field. A global average or one latent ideology score can erase those local differences.

**Lawful representation:** scoped E1/E2 facts with exact component and field provenance.

**Potential later use:** separate structural/mediation rules may admit some local fields and not others.

**Forbidden shortcut:** collapse all local configurations into a single actor worldview.
