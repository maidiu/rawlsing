# 1. Executive verdict

Rawlsing does **not** currently have one universal workload law of the form:

> every semantically permitted empirical relation must be computed and materialized.

Nor does current authority establish the opposite universal law:

> only a permanently fixed tiny subset may ever be evaluated.

The current law is **mixed and incomplete**.

Three different things are positively established and must not be collapsed:

1. **Semantic/use permission** — Final R1 plus the Question Use Contract (`QUC`) can establish that a question or feature is lawful for a role. These permissions answer questions such as whether a row may be a response distribution, demographic predictor, branch signature feature, relation co-signature feature, attitude-cloud anchor/feature, or V551 relation evidence. They do **not**, merely by existing, command that every permitted relation be computed.
2. **Reconstructibility / future-query law** — the binding full-machine vision expressly requires same-respondent incidence or an information-equivalent source substrate to remain reconstructible where future-authorized queries need it, so separately authorized conjunctions can be computed without pretending that independent marginals determine joint reality. This is a real workload-adjacent obligation, but it is an obligation to preserve **queryability/replay possibility**, not to precompute every possible conditional, conjunction, cotravel pair, or diagnostic today.
3. **Authority-selected bounded workload** — V550J has genuine bounded execution-selection authority. An executable joint-configuration registry plus accepted authority releases selects exact joint question configurations by source/wave/configuration. That is a real instance of `AUTHORITY_SELECTED_WORKLOAD`. The same registry explicitly requires `NO_PRODUCTION_COMPLETENESS_CLAIM`, so even this positive authority is not an assertion that every semantically possible joint relation must be computed.

Most base V550 work remains on the other side of that line. `answer_distribution`, `country_answer_distribution`, the fixed-`INDICATOR_FIELDS` determinicity families, recursive branching, and the cloud/signature family are demonstrably computed by current implementation paths, but D06 R2 and current-code reinspection do not establish family-specific workload authority commanding those computations merely because their inputs are selected or semantically permitted.

The consequence for the three hostile workload laws is therefore:

| Candidate law | D07 ruling |
|---|---|
| **A. `MATERIALIZE_ALL_PERMITTED`** | **NOT ESTABLISHED.** Semantic permission is not a workload command. Governing law expressly allows diagnostics to be recomputed on demand and says canonical storage need not mirror every exploratory calculation. |
| **B. `RECONSTRUCTIBLE_ON_DEMAND`** | **ESTABLISHED AT THE FUTURE-AUTHORIZED EMPIRICAL QUERY FRONTIER.** Same-respondent empirical incidence must remain reconstructible where future-authorized queries need it. This does not make every diagnostic artifact canonical or require current precomputation. |
| **C. `AUTHORITY_SELECTED_SUBSET_ONLY`** | **ESTABLISHED FOR BOUNDED V550J JOINT CONFIGURATIONS; NOT ESTABLISHED AS A GLOBAL LAW FOR BASE V550/CLOUD/ASSOCIATION WORK.** |

The missing authority is therefore not “data access” and not a statistical formula. It is an **execution-selection law** for the families that currently lack one: which exact permitted questions, predicates, conjunctions, pair directions, branches, or association candidates are required workload; which are merely permitted; and which must only remain reconstructible until a separately authorized query selects them.

D07 does **not** create that authority.

# 3. The authority taxonomy D07 applies

D07 uses the following meanings consistently:

### `GOVERNING_AUTHORITY`
A current accepted authority directly determines whether a semantic or executable act is lawful within its jurisdiction.

### `DERIVED_PERMISSION`
A current authority synthesis permits a use. Permission does not imply execution duty.

### `RECONSTRUCTIBILITY_REQUIREMENT`
The machine must preserve enough lawful empirical information that a separately authorized query can later be answered exactly or fail with typed unavailability. This does not require current materialization of every possible result.

### `MATERIALIZATION_REQUIREMENT`
Authority requires a concrete current artifact/result to be produced for the selected workload.

### `AUTHORITY_SELECTED_WORKLOAD`
Authority selects an exact bounded workload member/configuration to evaluate.

### `OPERATIONAL_RESOURCE_POLICY`
A runtime/cardinality/memory/acceptance-run decision controls execution but cannot create semantic authority.

### `IMPLEMENTATION_DEFAULT`
Current code does something automatically because the implementation is written that way, not because D07 located a governing command to do it.

### `NOT_LOCATED`
D07 did not locate the authority after repository/search and adjacent-surface inspection. This is deliberately weaker than `ABSENT`.

### `UNKNOWN`
Evidence cannot determine the proposition, including propositions dependent on D05's unavailable populated data.

### `NOT_APPLICABLE`
The authority dimension does not apply until another authority makes the query/workload live.

# 4. A critical distinction: permission, workload, admission, publication

A current computation can pass through several legally different gates:

```text
semantic classification
    ↓
DERIVED_PERMISSION
    ↓
workload selection / execution decision   <-- D07 jurisdiction
    ↓
empirical computation
    ↓
admissibility / support / semantic gates
    ↓
publication / downstream promotion
```

The existence of a gate below computation does not retroactively command the computation above it.

This is directly visible in current V550. `build_distribution_tables()` runs before a false `allowed_as_response_distribution` clears its outputs. The permission is therefore not a precompute workload command in that path.

The same noncollapse appears in V550J association work. Association-lane authority can govern whether a calculated candidate is publishable; that does not prove the candidate was required to be calculated in the first place.

# 10. Materialization versus reconstructibility

```text
SEMANTICALLY PERMITTED
    != REQUIRED TO COMPUTE NOW
    != REQUIRED TO MATERIALIZE NOW
    != REQUIRED TO PUBLISH

REQUIRED TO REMAIN RECONSTRUCTIBLE
    != REQUIRED TO PRECOMPUTE

PUBLISHABLE IF COMPUTED
    != REQUIRED CANDIDATE WORKLOAD
```

# 19. Final verdict

`MISSING_WORKLOAD_AUTHORITY`

The precise current law is:

1. **No universal `MATERIALIZE_ALL_PERMITTED` law is established.** Current semantic/use permissions do not command exhaustive materialization.
2. **A real `RECONSTRUCTIBILITY_REQUIREMENT` is established for the future-authorized empirical query frontier.** Same-respondent incidence must remain available in some lawful information-equivalent form where later separately authorized conjunctions/cotravel queries need it. This is not proof that current populated storage satisfies the requirement.
3. **A real `AUTHORITY_SELECTED_WORKLOAD` exists for bounded V550J joint configurations.** It is explicitly bounded and denies a production-completeness claim.
4. **Most base V550 family workloads remain implementation defaults unless a separate workload authority is located.** Fixed `INDICATOR_FIELDS`, current recursive search geometry, and compute-before-suppress behavior do not become authority by repetition.
5. **Cloud role permissions do not establish an exhaustive pair workload.** D03's ordered-pair set remains a conditional completeness oracle. Both lawful directions need not be precomputed solely from dual eligibility; lawful future direction queries must remain reconstructible when separately authorized.
6. **V550J association publication authority is stronger than its candidate-computation authority.** The lane registry governs classification/publication, while the authoritative derivation of `allowed_as_association_feature` and the exact candidate workload selector remain `NOT_LOCATED`.
7. **Arbitrary conjunctions are not automatically generated from independent permissions.** Their future evaluation requires separate authority, while governing law requires that the empirical information needed for such lawful queries not be prematurely destroyed.

D07 therefore closes the false equivalence:

```text
CAN COMPUTE
!= SEMANTICALLY PERMITTED
!= REQUIRED WORKLOAD

!= REQUIRED PUBLICATION

RECONSTRUCTIBLE WHEN AUTHORIZED
!= PRECOMPUTED TODAY
```

The machine presently possesses some workload authority, but **not enough to determine the complete lawful V550/V550J workload surface**. That missing authority must be supplied elsewhere; D07 does not invent it.
