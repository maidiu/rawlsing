# 01 — AUTHORITY HIERARCHY & GENERAL STAFF

## A. Authority hierarchy

The Campaign Vozhd is the supreme implementation commander but is not constitutionally supreme over Rawlsing law.

Binding order:

1. `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md` and equivalent accepted full-project spiritual/governance law.
2. `TERMINAL_DEMAND_ENVELOPE.md`.
3. `ARCHITECTURE_PASS_LAW.md`.
4. Accepted upstream discovery/architecture courts and parent adjudications, especially D12-D15.
5. Accepted downstream architecture authorities, corrected downstream rebase, R03 synthesis, stage close-reads, and final whole-machine closure.
6. This command constitution and its shared-contract authorities.
7. Domain Vozhd orders.
8. Bounded rabochii implementation choices.

Implementation evidence may reveal a contradiction but may never silently overrule higher authority.

## B. Campaign Vozhd

The Campaign Vozhd owns:

- the one authoritative whole-machine DAG;
- campaign activation and wave release;
- assignment and dismissal of sub-Vozhds;
- acceptance/rejection of General Staff contracts;
- global dependency graph;
- resolution of cross-domain authority conflicts;
- synchronization state;
- final decision to send a tranche to Integration Command;
- whole-machine reconciliation after every meaningful wave.

The Campaign Vozhd does not micro-author every module. It owns the fact that all modules constitute one lawful machine.

## E. No local legislation

A subordinate may discover a missing shared definition. It may propose one. It may not make its proposal real by committing a locally invented convention and forcing siblings to follow it.

Required response:

`BLOCKED_PENDING_SHARED_CONTRACT_ADJUDICATION`

with exact requested change, affected interfaces, and why current authority is insufficient.
