## 4. External-event admission and zero-mutation law

Two interaction modes remain distinct:

- `PRESENTATION_NONCAUSAL`: rendered output may be displayed/persisted noncausally and is not submitted as an in-world causal occurrence through the V2 interaction lane.
- `IN_WORLD_CAUSAL`: an exact rendered outbound artifact may become a **candidate** only. It remains `CANDIDATE_NONCAUSAL_PENDING_V3_ADMISSION` until V3 decides the exact proposal.

Packaging is not admission. Admission is not downstream causal effect.

For an in-world candidate:

```text
RenderedTurnArtifact
-> exact CAN-025 package/proposal
-> ADP-019 V3 external-event admission
-> ADMIT | REJECT | DEFER | INVALID
```

Only `ADMIT` creates exactly one V3 external-runtime occurrence and may advance the V3-owned event/session causal cursors as specified by ADP-019.

`REJECT`, `DEFER`, and `INVALID` produce:

- zero V3 causal state mutation;
- zero V3 event-ledger cursor advancement;
- zero V3 causal-session cursor advancement;
- no memory/knowledge/belief/relationship/attractor effect;
- no causal publication of the candidate artifact.

A `DEFER` may be reevaluated only as the same immutable proposal/package after its named blocker changes. A terminally rejected exact artifact may not be re-proposed under a different proposal identity to evade rejection.

Even `ADMIT` decides occurrence only. It does not itself imply memory, belief, knowledge, relationship, trust, position, or attractor effects; those remain owned by their separate V3 transition authorities.
