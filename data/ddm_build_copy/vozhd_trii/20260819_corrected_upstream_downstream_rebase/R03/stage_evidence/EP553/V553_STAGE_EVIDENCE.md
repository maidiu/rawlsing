## 1. CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_SURFACE_SUMMARY

### Accepted semantic jurisdiction

The accepted Vozhd Dva V553 ruling selects a **Direct-Passage / Thin-Service Compiler** jurisdiction. V553's surviving semantic work is temporal, not a generic mandatory stage wrapper. Its lawful work is to:

- assign canonical time meaning to admissible structural evidence;
- express time-scoped observations;
- perform governed cross-time/source-time comparability operations;
- derive temporal relations such as deltas, persistence, drift, reversal, rupture/revision where the evidence and authority permit them;
- emit explicit temporal limits when an operation is unsupported, noncomparable, ambiguous, out of jurisdiction, or otherwise not lawfully performable;
- produce replayable/content-addressed temporal artifacts and their verification/replay sidecars.

The accepted ruling simultaneously excludes identity/trust/quality adjudication, generic graph deduction, actor sampling, actor/world realization, and downstream geometric/frame selection from V553.

Current named helpers include legacy-artifact adaptation, weighted trend / change-point style logic, deflator handling, detectability, derived-metric and release-lineage support.

The executable defines `V553_SCHEMA_VERSION = "ddm.v553.temporal_pressure.v1"` and current row/fragment structures including `PopulationSlice`, `PeriodizedSlice`, and `TemporalPressureFragment`. `TemporalPressureFragment` carries, among other fields:

## 2. EXACT_INPUT_CONTRACT

### Accepted pre-R01 V553 input contract

The accepted Vozhd Dva ruling describes V553 as compiling admissible V552/V552J outputs into authoritative `TEMPORAL_*` objects. That establishes the historical completed-stage contract at the time of the ruling, but R01/R02 subsequently changed the upstream/downstream seam: generic empirical facts require `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` before lawful structural downstream use, and the direct DAG is typed rather than a universal straight line.

Therefore, **the direct V552/V552J wording is evidence of the old input shape, not authority for universal current routing.**

### Current canonicalizer input assumptions

`ddm_v553_canonical_harmonization.py` documents itself as canonicalizing admissible V552/V552J rows into deterministic V553 observation inputs. It hard-codes zero-configuration selectors:

- `V552_EXACT_COUNTS`
- `V552J_EXACT_COUNTS`
- fallback `V552J_EXACT_AGGREGATE_GROUP_FALLBACK`

The current V552 adapter assumes fields including:

- schema version;
- source population;
- `n`, interpreted as exact `count`;
- `aggregate_group`;
- `observed_group` for nonzero counts;
- row/source identity;
- query/query-key/source/axis information;
- period fields;
- source universe;
- provenance.

The current V552J adapter assumes, at minimum:

- schema version;
- coordinate;
- `answer_bucket`;
- `n`;
- population either directly or via coordinate;
- row/source identity;
- query/source/period/provenance fields.

Where a V552J row has `aggregate_group` other than `ALL`, the canonicalizer records use of the aggregate-group fallback selector.

### R02-corrected routing predicate

The exact lawful predicate for **whether an admitted structural fact needs V553 at all** is narrower than the adapter's accepted row classes. V553 is required where the target structural use requires new temporal/comparability semantics, including examples established by R02:

- cross-wave or cross-time comparability determination;
- governed harmonization across temporal representations;
- temporal relation/delta construction;
- persistence, drift, change, reversal, rupture/revision semantics;
- temporal limit or noncomparability semantics;
- derived effective period/historical interval;
- derived relation timing/sequence;
- source-time comparability determination.

V553 is not required merely because source provenance contains a timestamp/history field, or because an admitted fact has a governed period/interval already sufficient for its downstream target use.

### Input seam fact for R03

No exact-base V553 adapter inspected by EP553 establishes a native `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` input contract. The current adapter surface is instead coupled to V552/V552J exact-count row shapes. This is a concrete R03 seam: compatibility must be proved rather than inferred from shared fields such as `counts`, `aggregate_group`, period labels, or provenance.

## 3. EXACT_OUTPUT_CONTRACT

The accepted V553 output contract consists of four canonical semantic families plus governed replay/verification closure:

1. `TEMPORAL_KEY`
2. `TEMPORAL_OBSERVATION`
3. `TEMPORAL_RELATION`
4. `TEMPORAL_LIMIT`

`ddm_v553_schema.py` represents the same four families in `V553ObjectFamily` and packages them in `V553OutputSet` together with manifest/replay material.

The output-set surface includes identifiers/maps for the four object families and replay/governance fields including governed-manifest identity, replay-key/verifier material, authority digest/closure, and sidecars. Validation is designed to ensure deterministic IDs/hashes, manifest closure, replay closure, and absence of dangling/unpublished references.

The accepted V554 handoff vocabulary is ref-first and temporally typed:

- `TEMPORAL_OBSERVATION_REF`
- `TEMPORAL_RELATION_REF`
- `TEMPORAL_LIMIT_REF`
- optional `TEMPORAL_KEY_REF`

This is an important jurisdictional boundary: V553 publishes temporal meaning; V554 consumes it when relevant and owns its own next transformation rather than re-deriving V553's temporal semantics.

R02 adds a crucial routing qualification: a qualifying non-temporal `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` may pass directly to V554. Therefore, the existence of V553's temporal output set does **not** establish that every V554 basis must be wrapped in a temporal object.

## 4. TEMPORAL_KEY_CURRENT_SEMANTICS

`TEMPORAL_KEY` is the canonical identity of a time statement/scope/interval, not merely a copied timestamp.

`ddm_v553_temporal_identity.py` provides a first-class versioned temporal identity contract. Its `V553TemporalIdentity` includes canonicalized temporal fields such as:

- period label;
- period start/end;
- event start/end;
- system-validity information;
- explicitly asserted unknown temporal fields.

Validation is strict about date/time/ISO representations and timezone-bearing timestamp semantics where required.

`BuildTemporalKeyInput` includes periodicity/temporal fields, asserted unknowns, source-row IDs, upstream query keys, and authority reference. Metadata-derived temporal claims require an authority reference rather than being silently accepted as source truth.

The key material is domain/version separated (`V553_TEMPORAL_IDENTITY_V1`) and content-addressed. The resulting `TemporalKey` is deterministic and carries V553 provenance. The key binds the temporal identity to relevant source rows, upstream query keys, and authority token/material.

**Current semantic obligation:** canonical identity, fail-closed unknown handling, and authority-backed derivation are invariant-bearing. A no-op wrapper around an already sufficient admitted period is not established as a required V553 operation by R02.

## 5. TEMPORAL_OBSERVATION_CURRENT_SEMANTICS

A `TEMPORAL_OBSERVATION` is a normalized structural assertion/value/status over a canonical temporal scope, with uncertainty and source provenance. It is not the underlying empirical row and is not an actor disposition.

The current `TemporalObservation` schema includes, materially:

- observation ID;
- `key_id` linking the observation to canonical temporal identity;
- query / upstream query key information;
- source universe;
- source artifact identities;
- axes;
- metric kind;
- value/status;
- uncertainty;
- provenance;
- detected temporal-limit references.

The schema prohibits downstream V554+ provenance from being smuggled backward into a V553 observation. Its source is upstream structural evidence.

In the current canonicalizer, observations are especially coupled to exact-count V552/V552J rows (`n`/count semantics). That coupling is implementation evidence, not proof that the surviving semantic concept of temporal observation is limited to legacy exact-count row shapes.

The accepted ruling requires observation semantics to remain replayable and governed. Where an observation cannot lawfully be established because support/comparability/authority is missing, the stage should preserve the failure/limit rather than fabricate a normalized value.

## 7. TEMPORAL_LIMIT_CURRENT_SEMANTICS

`TEMPORAL_LIMIT` is an authoritative, explicit record that a temporal evidence operation was not performed or not accepted, together with the reason/scope/provenance. It is not an error to be erased to keep a pipeline moving.

Current `V553LimitReason` values include:

- `OUT_OF_JURISDICTION`
- `MISSINGNESS`
- `INSUFFICIENT_PERIODS`
- `INSUFFICIENT_COMPARABILITY`
- `CONFLICT`
- `AMBIGUOUS_SUPPORT`
- `BASELINE_UNAVAILABLE`
- `BACKEND_UNAVAILABLE`
- `UNSUPPORTED_QUERY_FAMILY`
- `MALFORMED_INPUT`
- `UNKNOWN`

Current confidence/status vocabulary includes `ASSERTED`, `SUPPORTED`, `ESTIMATED`, `TENTATIVE`, `UNRESOLVED`, and `BLOCKED`.

The `TemporalLimit` schema binds a limit to reason/scope, query/source-universe/source-artifact context, boundary/detail, optional temporal-key/relation identities, and provenance. As with the other V553 families, downstream V554+ artifacts are not lawful upstream provenance.

The accepted ruling makes limits semantically important for noncomparability, unknown support, temporal conflict, unsupported operations, missing baseline, and backend/authority inability. This is therefore invariant-bearing negative authority, not optional diagnostic logging.

## 8. V552_V552J_ADAPTER_AND_INPUT_COMPATIBILITY

### What the adapter currently proves

The current canonical harmonizer proves compatibility with specific **legacy/existing producer row shapes**, not with arbitrary future admitted empirical facts.

For V552, exact-count intake is selected by `V552_EXACT_COUNTS`; `n` is consumed as count, `aggregate_group` and `observed_group` participate in admissibility/normalization, and source/query/axis/period/provenance fields are repackaged for V553.

The normalized rows hard-code source-stage identity as V552 or V552J and retain producer-row/source-artifact identity.

### What the adapter does not prove

The adapter does not prove that:

- a corrected R01 `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` has exactly the same row schema;
- `counts` or `aggregate_group` have the same authority/grain semantics merely because similarly named fields exist;
- admission provenance can be flattened back into `source_stage = V552/V552J` without information loss;

- a direct Admission→V554 case can be safely forced through `V552_EXACT_COUNTS`/`V552J_EXACT_COUNTS` adapters.

### R03 evidence classification

The V552/V552J adapter is therefore a **compatibility seam**. R03 should test it against the corrected admission contract field-by-field and authority-by-authority. Shared strings are insufficient evidence of semantic equivalence.

## 9. PROVENANCE_AND_REFERENCE_SURFACE

### V553 provenance

`ddm_v553_schema.py` defines `AuthorityRef` with authority class/id/version and optional digest, plus `V553Provenance` carrying source-stage/source-artifact/source-universe/query/upstream-query material, authority references, plugin identity/detail, and asserted unknowns.

Metadata-derived claims require authority/plugin support. Plugin identity is versioned/digested so backend semantics cannot change silently while producing apparently equivalent artifacts.

### Replay and content addressing

The accepted V553 ruling requires deterministic IDs/hashes with no wall-clock/session/random semantic inputs. Exact replay is byte-identical; verify-only mode checks drift; bounded resealing may produce new objects only while preserving provenance/history under the governing replay rules.

`V553OutputSet` and replay sidecars carry enough material to validate object identities/hashes, set/manifest integrity, source-row/adaptor context, policy/backend identities/digests, and reference closure. Output validation rejects dangling/unpublished references.

### Temporal reference vocabulary

`validate_v553_consumer_reference` accepts the four V553 temporal reference kinds and requires the referenced target to exist in the V553 output set. That is strong evidence for temporal-object closure.

### Direct-DAG seam

R02's target closure is typed:

`V556 → V555 → V554 → [V553 iff required] → StructuralEmpiricalAdmission → EmpiricalFact → Query / Universe / measure / authority / replay`

For a lawful direct Admission→V554 case, V553's temporal-only consumer-reference validator is not itself a representation of the admission basis. R02 therefore requires V554-side basis/reference machinery capable of distinguishing at least a direct structural-admission basis from a V553-temporal-object basis; V553 must not manufacture a `TEMPORAL_*` object solely to satisfy reference closure.

This is a reference-seam fact, not an EP553 architecture selection.

### Historical-shape tests / fixtures that must not be mistaken for final authority

Tests or fixtures are historical-shape evidence, rather than dispositive semantics, if their correctness depends on:

- universal direct V552/V552J → V553 transit;
- exact `n`/count-only input;

- current fallback-engine portfolio or ensemble/hybrid orchestration;
- mandatory V553 wrapping merely because a row contains time provenance;
- the current executable `TemporalStructuralAuthority` enum being the final accepted policy-authority taxonomy;
- downstream reference closure requiring a V553 object even where R02 authorizes direct Admission→V554 passage.

### Evidence gap

## 11. V553_R01_SEAM_GAPS

The following are concrete evidence-backed hostile-review targets for R03. They are **gaps/seams, not proposed final designs**.

### GAP-553-01 — pre-R01 direct producer intake versus corrected admission intake

The accepted old V553 ruling and current harmonizer consume V552/V552J-shaped rows directly. R01/R02 require generic empirical facts to acquire lawful structural admission before structural downstream use. No native `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` adapter was established in the exact V553 intake surface inspected here.

**Attack:** prove how admission authority, empirical fact identity, query/universe/measure authority and replay lineage enter V553 without being reconstructed from weaker row fields.

### GAP-553-02 — exact-count / aggregate-group coupling

Current adapters select `V552_EXACT_COUNTS` / `V552J_EXACT_COUNTS` and depend on `n`, `aggregate_group`, `observed_group`, coordinate, and answer-bucket conventions.

**Attack:** prove whether corrected admission `counts` / grouping semantics are actually equivalent in grain and authority. Do not map on shared names alone.

### GAP-553-03 — source-stage/provenance flattening

Current canonicalization hard-codes V552/V552J source-stage identity. Corrected admission adds a lawful authority boundary between empirical fact and structural use.

**Attack:** detect any path that erases `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` identity/authority and leaves only its ancestral V552/V552J row identity.

### GAP-553-04 — temporal authority taxonomy mismatch

The accepted completion ruling names a policy-authority family including `WaveNamingAuthority`, `PeriodLabelPolicy`, `PeriodicityAuthority`, `TimestampSupportPolicy`, `WindowingAuthority`, `ReleaseLineageAuthority`, `RevisionAuthority`, `MissingnessPolicy`, `ImputationAuthority`, `DeflatorAuthority`, `UnitHarmonizationAuthority`, `ComparatorAuthority`, `BaselineAuthority`, `ReductionAuthority`, `EventOnsetAuthority`, and `TemporalConflictAuthority` under the governing temporal structural authority surface.

The current executable schema's `TemporalStructuralAuthority` enum instead contains implementation-oriented values such as `DEFAULT_POLICY`, `IDENTIFIED_SOURCE`, `SYSO`, `BOOTSTRAP_DECLARATION`, `COHERENT_PR`, `REGIME_SHIFT`, `RELEASE_LINEAGE`, `DERIVED_METRIC`, `DEFLATOR`, `TIER4_FI`, `COMORBIDITY`, and `PROXY_LINK`.

**Attack:** determine which current names are legacy mechanisms, which map to accepted policy authority, and whether any path silently substitutes implementation/backend identity for semantic authority.

### GAP-553-05 — legacy hybrid executable versus accepted thin-service jurisdiction

`ddm_v553_temporal_pressure.py` retains fallback engines/adapters and a broader hybrid computation surface. The accepted ruling selects Direct-Passage / Thin-Service and rejects retained ensemble semantics.

**Attack:** separate invariant temporal operations from obsolete orchestration and prevent historical implementation abundance from expanding V553 jurisdiction.

### GAP-553-06 — no-op temporal transit

R02 explicitly rejects V553 transit merely for timestamps/history provenance or an already-governed sufficient temporal coordinate.

**Attack:** locate any stage runner, adapter, consumer, test or manifest rule that still treats V553 as mandatory stage order rather than a typed semantic edge.

### GAP-553-07 — direct Admission→V554 reference closure

V553's native reference validator is temporal-family-specific. R02 requires direct Admission→V554 for qualifying non-temporal structural use and identifies separate V554 basis kinds for direct structural admission versus V553 temporal object.

**Attack:** prove that direct-admission reference closure is representable without synthesizing a V553 temporal object and without losing the transitive empirical/query/universe/measure/authority/replay chain.

### GAP-553-08 — over-bypass versus under-bypass

The direct DAG creates two symmetric risks:

- **under-bypass:** forcing an already complete admission through V553 and duplicating/normalizing away authority;
- **over-bypass:** sending evidence directly to V554 even though the target operation requires V553-owned comparability, harmonization, temporal relation, derived interval, or negative temporal authority.

**Attack:** route by semantic predicate, not source-stage name or presence/absence of a timestamp field.

### GAP-553-09 — source-universe and query identity conservation

Current V553 objects carry source universe, query and upstream-query material. Corrected upstream authority makes these fields part of a longer transitive chain rather than loose metadata.

**Attack:** ensure normalization, cross-wave pairing, relation construction, and direct bypass cannot enlarge source universe, merge incompatible universes, or detach a derived temporal object from the empirical query/measure/admission authority that licenses it.

### GAP-553-10 — negative authority preservation

`TEMPORAL_LIMIT` is a first-class semantic result. A migration that only preserves successful observations/relations would silently strengthen the evidence.

**Attack:** preserve limits, conflicts, insufficient support, unknown comparability and backend/authority inability through V554+ reference closure and replay.

### GAP-553-11 — actor/world boundary

Nothing in V553's temporal objects authorizes conversion of population structural evidence into actor belief, identity, psyche, or realized world fact.

**Attack:** ensure adapters/consumers do not use temporal relation confidence or repeated observations as actor-level determinism. Preserve the D15/R02 distinction between empirical population evidence, structural downstream semantics, positioned-subject possibility fields, and V3 realization.

## EP553 evidentiary conclusion

The evidence supports a narrow, still-live V553 semantic core: **governed creation of new temporal/comparability meaning and its replayable negative/positive evidence objects**. The evidence does not support universal V553 transit for every corrected structural admission.

The current V553 executable/schema surface is valuable evidence for temporal identity, object-family semantics, validation, provenance and replay obligations, but its direct V552/V552J exact-count intake, authority enum, broad fallback-engine surface and any implied mandatory stage ordering require hostile R03 review against the accepted Vozhd Dva ruling and R02 typed-DAG boundary.

EP553 makes no final architecture ruling.
