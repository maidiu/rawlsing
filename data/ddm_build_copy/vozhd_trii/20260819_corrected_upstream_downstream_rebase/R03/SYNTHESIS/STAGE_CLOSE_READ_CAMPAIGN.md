The campaign separates **launch dependencies** from **final adjudication dependencies**.

Every stage cell may begin immediately and in parallel on:

- authority close-read;
- schema/reference inventory;
- hostile fixture design;
- exact-base implementation-gap mapping;
- semantic-object and negative-state contract drafting;

This yields maximum parallelism without using guessed schemas as authority.
