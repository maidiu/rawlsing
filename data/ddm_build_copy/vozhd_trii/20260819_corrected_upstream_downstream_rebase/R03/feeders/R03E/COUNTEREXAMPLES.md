# R03E — COUNTEREXAMPLES

**Court:** R03E — V3 POSSIBILITY BOUNDARY / DIVERGENCE / V2 NONCONSUMPTION  
**Authority:** NON-DISPOSITIVE HOSTILE EVIDENCE ONLY  
**Base under attack:** R02 accepted direct-DAG holdings, especially R02-H08 and R02-H10

## CE-E01 — Eliminating the wrapper leaks ownership

Assume an immutable `PositionedSubjectEmpiricalPossibilityField` exists for actor objective-fact snapshot `A0` and source-indexed field set `F0`.

Attempt A removes `V3EmpiricalPossibilityInput` by adding to PSEPF:

- V3 decision/use identity;
- V3 decision effective time;
- V3 lifecycle/current/stale status;
- V3 metabolism-authority applicability;
- V3 causal-history parentage.

This fails because PSEPF is upstream population-empirical possibility geometry. Those fields make it own actor-runtime policy and V3 causal-use applicability.

Attempt B leaves PSEPF unchanged and lets V3 consume it directly. This fails because no typed owner remains for whether `F0` is lawfully usable by this V3 decision, at this V3 time, under this metabolism authority, or whether use must fail closed.

**Counterexample result:** wrapper elimination is not semantically neutral. The semantic boundary survives.

## CE-E02 — A self-contained wrapper becomes a second field authority

Construct canonical PSEPF `F0` and a V3 wrapper `I0` that copies all configuration cells, masses, support, uncertainty and comparator/query payload into itself.

Now replay or authority verification finds that the canonical immutable PSEPF hash pinned by `I0` does not match one copied cell or one copied authority-dependent field.

There are now two candidate empirical truths:

1. producer-owned PSEPF; and
2. wrapper-owned copied geometry.

No lawful rule lets V3 choose the wrapper copy over the producer object. The correct behavior is mismatch/fail-closed, proving the copy was never needed as semantic content.

**Counterexample result:** full geometry copying creates duplicate authority and stale-divergence risk. Canonical wrapper content must remain reference-and-lifecycle oriented.

## CE-E03 — Field validity and V3 intake applicability are different questions

Start with immutable PSEPF `F0` generated for objective actor-fact snapshot `A0`, exact comparator/source/query authorities, and V3 intake `I0` under metabolism authority `M0` and use class `U0`.

### Case 1 — objective comparator fact changes

Actor reaches a governed age boundary such that the comparator predicate for a later decision may differ.

`I0` cannot silently remain current. The later V3 decision requires a governed applicability decision and may require a new upstream comparator/query/field production request. `F0` remains an immutable historical object; it is not mutated.

### Case 2 — source-applicability time changes

The V3 decision time crosses a source-applicability boundary. Again, old intake cannot be silently reused. Whether a new field must be produced is an upstream applicability/requery question.

### Case 3 — only V3 use/metabolism authority changes

The empirical field can remain exactly the same population field while `I0` becomes inapplicable because use class `U0` or metabolism authority `M0` no longer governs the new V3 decision.

This requires a new V3-side intake binding or denial, but does **not** by itself prove that PSEPF `F0` is empirically stale or must be recomputed.

**Counterexample result:** one undifferentiated `stale/refresh` flag is too coarse if interpreted as empirical ownership. The close-read must distinguish upstream field/comparator/source validity from V3 decision-bound intake applicability and distinguish upstream requery from V3 re-binding.

## CE-E04 — Negative states cannot become priors or defaults

### Comparator-positive receipt as prior

A positive `ActorComparatorReceipt` establishes only that exact actor objective facts lawfully map to an empirical conditioner predicate. It contains no target response geometry. Feeding it directly into V3 as a developmental prior invents semantics the receipt does not own.

### `NO_COMPARATOR` / `AMBIGUOUS_COMPARATOR` / `TEMPORAL_NONCOMPARABLE`

These are comparator-owner failures. They cannot authorize nearest cohort, fuzzy mapping, structural-position substitution or population prevalence imputation.

### `ZERO_SUPPORT`

This means no empirical conditional basis in the governed risk set. It does not mean the actor has zero probability of a state, and it cannot be converted into a belief or response zero.

### `LOW_SUPPORT`

This means supported but weak/uncertain. Treating it as impossibility loses lawful supported minority geometry.

### `SOURCE_NOT_APPLICABLE` / source disagreement

These belong to source/field authority. They cannot be collapsed into one default global distribution or a persona choice.

**Counterexample result:** R02-H08 survives. R02-H10 requires an ownership clarification: V3 intake may preserve/expose upstream typed failures, but it must not re-author their empirical meanings. V3-owned intake negatives are only V3 applicability failures such as stale/refresh-required, metabolism authority unavailable, or use denied, with exact vocabulary deferred to close-read.

## CE-E05 — Equal priors do not determine equal lives

Construct two replay branches at a common branch point with:

- identical immutable PSEPF `F0`;
- identical `ResolvedStructuralContext` `S0`;
- identical relevant starting objective comparator facts;
- identical V3 intake authority at the branch point.

After the branch point vary only lawful V3-owned causal history:

- one actor encounters a relationship/event/exposure that the other does not;
- consequences, accessible knowledge, memory and later choices therefore differ under existing V3 causal law.

The architecture must permit later belief/action/attractor divergence and attribute it to the different V3 histories (and, where explicitly governed, randomness), without mutating `F0` into actor state.

If equal `F0 + S0` forced equal developed states, PSEPF would be a covert finished persona/prior rather than possibility geometry.

**Counterexample result:** same-prior/same-context determinism is forbidden. Divergent-life fixtures are mandatory close-read evidence.

## CE-E06 — V2 nonconsumption is semantic, not topical censorship

### Illicit direct personalization

Attempt to let V2/application choose voice, persona, belief or expression directly from:

- modal PSEPF cells;
- comparator receipt;
- source disagreement;
- `ResolvedStructuralContext`.

All fail because those objects are population/structural upstream authorities, not actor-accessible generated truth.

### Lawful control case

A V3 actor later lawfully encounters a report, survey result or structural fact, gains actor-accessible knowledge under V3 causality, and V2 renders that V3-generated knowledge.

This is lawful even if the subject matter is empirical or structural. The semantic authority comes from V3-generated actor-accessible truth, not direct dereference of PSEPF or structural context by V2/application.

**Counterexample result:** nonconsumption must block direct upstream-object-to-persona/voice authority while preserving ordinary rendering of lawfully generated actor-accessible knowledge.

## Counterexample closure

No E01-E06 case falsifies R02-H08 or the existence/minimality purpose of R02-H10.

The hostile sweep does expose two qualifications for synthesis and later close-read:

1. **negative-state ownership must remain at the earliest lawful owner; the V3 wrapper preserves upstream state but does not acquire comparator/field semantics;**
2. **V3 intake staleness/refresh must distinguish V3 applicability/re-binding from upstream empirical field requery/recomputation.**

No metabolism algorithm is selected here, and no direct PSEPF-to-actor-state mapping is authorized.
