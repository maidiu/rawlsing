## 2. E01 — wrapper elimination attack

### Hostile construction

Remove `V3EmpiricalPossibilityInput`.

Variant A moves into PSEPF:

- actor/V3-decision binding;
- V3 effective/use time;
- lifecycle/current/stale policy;
- metabolism-authority applicability;
- V3 causal-history parentage.

Variant B leaves PSEPF pure and feeds it directly to V3 without a typed decision/effective-time/use/metabolism applicability boundary.

### Result

Both variants fail.

Variant A makes an upstream empirical object own actor-runtime policy. Variant B leaves V3 with no governed owner for whether an exact immutable field is usable by a particular developmental decision at a particular effective time.

Therefore the semantic boundary survives.

### Qualification

R03E does **not** prove that this boundary must be one globally durable database table or transport-heavy object. R02's own persistence law is sound: persist the boundary when causal parentage, replay, audit or governed refresh requires it; otherwise an exact typed intake/receipt may be transient while the PSEPF remains canonical.

## 4. E03 — lifecycle/effective-time/stale/refresh attack

This attack finds a material qualification.

### 4.1 Old field immutability is not current-use validity

An immutable PSEPF remains a valid historical/replay object even after it ceases to be applicable to a later V3 decision. No lifecycle rule may mutate an old field into a newer one or infer `latest wins`.

### 4.2 Two different questions must not collapse

**Upstream empirical applicability/requery question:**

Does the actor objective-fact snapshot, comparator rule, source-applicability scope, query identity, source universe or other upstream authority still support this empirical field for the requested context?

This remains owned upstream by the comparator/source/query/field authorities.

**V3 intake applicability/re-binding question:**

Is this exact already-produced field lawfully available to this V3 developmental decision, at this V3 effective/use time, under this metabolism authority/use class?

This belongs to the V3 intake boundary.

A change in objective comparator facts or governed source/time applicability may require a fresh upstream request. A change only in V3 use class or metabolism-authority applicability may require a new V3 intake binding/denial without proving that the underlying population field must be recomputed.

### 4.3 Fail-closed consequence

Until the V3 close-read freezes the lifecycle state machine and trigger ownership, unresolved current applicability must not silently reuse a field.

The close-read must distinguish at least:

- immutable historical field;
- current V3 intake;
- intake stale/inapplicable;
- upstream requery required;
- V3 rebind required;
- denied/missing authority;
- exact replay of the historical intake.

R03E does not select the exact vocabulary or algorithm.

- `ACTOR_PROPERTY_MISSING`;
