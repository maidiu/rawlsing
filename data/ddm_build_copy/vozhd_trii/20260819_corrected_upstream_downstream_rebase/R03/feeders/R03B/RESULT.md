## 3. Hostile theorem: operation, logical boundary, and deployment are not the same claim

R02's unique-meaning test is necessary but needs one hostile refinement.

Three distinct propositions must not be conflated:

1. **The operation is necessary.** A downstream capability fails without the operation.
2. **The logical authority seam is necessary.** The operation requires a distinct owner/rule version/object identity/negative authority or permission decision that cannot lawfully be borrowed from an earlier or later owner.
3. **A separately deployed numbered stage is necessary.** The seam must be a separate executable, process, directory, job, or storage packet.

R03B finds strong support for propositions 1 and 2 for V554–V558, but not a general law requiring proposition 3.

The decisive anti-faux-merge rule is:

> If a proposed bypass or merge must recreate the removed stage's independently governed rule decision, canonical object identity, typed negative/valid-empty state, or permission boundary before the next consumer can act lawfully, then the proposal did not remove the logical stage. It only moved or co-packaged its implementation.

This matters most for V557/V558 because their binding completion ruling expressly freezes them as **logically distinct even if one library/deployment physically co-packages them**.

## 4. B01 — attack V554 necessity

### Hostile construction

Delete V554 conceptually and attempt:

for the direct non-temporal class, and:

`V553 temporal object -> V555`

for the temporal class.

Let V555's route rule inspect the earlier structural basis and directly choose mediator/channel/receiver routes.

### What disappears under a true bypass

The earlier objects do not own:

- positive bounded eligibility for `STRUCTURAL_MEDIATION_INPUT` use;
- `BOUNDED_STRUCTURAL_AFFORDANCE` identity;
- pre-route `STRUCTURAL_MEDIATION_APPLICABILITY_CONDITION` identity;

`StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` owns empirical-to-structural admission. V553 owns temporal structural meaning. Neither owns mediation-input affordance eligibility.

### Minimal substitute attack

Put a V554-equivalent affordance rule inside the V555 implementation and require it to emit a stable affordance identity and pre-route conditions before the route rule runs.

This works operationally, but it recreates the V554 seam:

`admitted/temporal basis -> V554-authority decision -> V554-equivalent F1/F2 -> V555 route decision`.

If the substitute does **not** retain that distinction, one V555 rule simultaneously owns both “may this structural basis be used as a mediation input?” and “what mediator/channel/receiver route exists?”, collapsing two independently governed questions and destroying the pre-route denial/requirement boundary.

### Disposition

`SURVIVES_WITH_QUALIFICATION`

Recommendation: `QUALIFY_RETAIN` V554 as a logical semantic authority boundary. Do not infer that it requires a separately deployed process. Preserve R02-H03: a true semantic `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission -> V555` shortcut remains forbidden.
