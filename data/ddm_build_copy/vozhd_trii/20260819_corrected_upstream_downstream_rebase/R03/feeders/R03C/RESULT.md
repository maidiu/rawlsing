## 6. Source-universe attack ruling

D13/D15 source-universe separation survives attack, but it becomes an explicit closure requirement for R02-H07.

The minimum durable identity for an empirical source component is not a source label. It is the exact `EmpiricalUniverse` identity plus source/instrument/time/geography/design/respondent namespace authority.

Cross-universe operations require the exact positive relation appropriate to the operation. These are not interchangeable:

```text
SOURCE_SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE
!= RESPONDENT_IDENTITY_LINKAGE
!= SAMPLE_POOLING_AUTHORITY
!= GENERATED_ACTOR_COMPARABILITY
```

Therefore a V558 index may index a source label for lookup convenience but may not use that label to merge universe identities. A structural admission derived from plural conflicting sources must preserve plural lineage or a typed governed conflict/noncomparability state.

## 7. Historical authority attack ruling
