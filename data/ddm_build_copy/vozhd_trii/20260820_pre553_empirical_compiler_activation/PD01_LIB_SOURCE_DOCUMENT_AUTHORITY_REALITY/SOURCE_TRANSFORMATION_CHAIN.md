## Court boundary

This is a documentary transformation-chain adjudication, not a populated-data receipt. The governing court explicitly forbids upgrading schema capability or documentary expectation into populated evidence. Citation: Library: Pasted text(20260820-183309).txt, lines 1-490 (especially 37-58, 143-171, 173-426, 442-490).

D13/C11 requires at least M01-M18 and holds the historical V550/V550J respondent substrate insufficient for the full future frontier. Citation: Library: Pasted text(20260819-162348).txt, D12/D13/C11 sections; M01-M18 and source-retention law. D13C specifically identifies the danger of scalar duplicate keys, missing first-class event/rank/routing identity, and defaults that can erase missing-state semantics. Citation: Library: Pasted text(20260819-153505).txt, hostile burden section: required replay inputs, scalar duplicate-key/event/routing deficits.

## Active information-loss hazards

- **Typed absence collapse — LOSSY if present.** W7 has multiple source-native nonresponse/applicability states; a single NULL/missing bucket destroys risk-set semantics. Evidence: Library: WVS Wave 7 (2017) Questionnaire.pdf, p.1 (global special codes); pp.11-12 (ranked first/second-choice batteries); Library: WVS Wave 7 (2017) Codebook.pdf, p.51 (Q154/Q155 special states including DK, no answer, not asked, no second answer/no first answer).
- **Repeated-event scalarization — NONPARITY in the general case.** The public response table primary key is `(respondent_id,var_code)` with no event ordinal. Evidence: Conversation upload: Rawlsing_WVS_public_schema(1).sql:L6486-L6494,L7377-L7382.
- **Routing erasure — LOSSY if branch state is not exactly reconstructible.** Generic raw responses have no route/risk-set incidence column. Evidence: Conversation upload: Rawlsing_WVS_public_schema(1).sql:L6450-L6512 and L7369-L7389; governing requirement Library: Pasted text(20260819-162348).txt, D12/D13/C11 sections; M01-M18 and source-retention law.
- **Release flattening — LOSSY if `dataset_id/source_file/source/wave` are missing or normalized without replay identity.** The schema can represent these, but population is unverified. Evidence: Conversation upload: Rawlsing_WVS_public_schema(1).sql:L6450-L6512 and L7369-L7389.
- **Weight generalization — NONPARITY/AUTHORITY_GAP if W_WEIGHT and S018 are treated as interchangeable or as universal estimand authority.** Evidence: Library: WVS Wave 7 (2017) Codebook.pdf, pp.8-9 (W_WEIGHT purpose; S018 equilibrated country weight; product guidance).
- **Canonical semantic mapping without authority — AUTHORITY_GAP.** Historical R14 semantic review does not become P05 certification by existence. Evidence: Library: R14_SOURCE_NATIVE_CANDIDATE_INTEGRITY_AUDIT_R3.csv, WVS W7 grouped source-native candidate rows and questionnaire locators; status SEMANTIC_REVIEW_READY where reported; court law Library: Pasted text(20260820-183309).txt, lines 1-490 (especially 37-58, 143-171, 173-426, 442-490).
