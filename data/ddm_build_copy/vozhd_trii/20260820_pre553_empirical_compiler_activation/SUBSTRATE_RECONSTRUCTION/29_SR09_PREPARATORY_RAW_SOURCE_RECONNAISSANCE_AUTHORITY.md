Do not blindly rescan the full 486M-cell corpus.

Start from the accepted SR03 physical census and source manifests. Use targeted raw-source reads/probes only where necessary to establish source-specific burden facts, hostile sentinel cells, identity behavior, encoding behavior, topology, weight/design candidates, or cross-wave drift.
