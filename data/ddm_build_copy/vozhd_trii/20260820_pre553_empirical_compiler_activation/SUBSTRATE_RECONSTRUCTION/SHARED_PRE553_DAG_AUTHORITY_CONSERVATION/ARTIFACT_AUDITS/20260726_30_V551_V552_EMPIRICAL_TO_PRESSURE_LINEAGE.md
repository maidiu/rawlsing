# Whole-lineage evaluation

This V551→V552 repair sequence embodies **strong conceptual discipline** and should be treated as a conservation target even if the physical V551/V552 artifact chain disappears in a new DAG.

The irreducible information laws are:

1. **Empirical observation is not structural inference.**
2. **Support, prevalence, response intensity and pressure are distinct measurements.**
3. **Observed zero, neutral, missing, special and ineligible are distinct states.**
4. **Authority to use evidence for a downstream computation is separate from existence of the evidence.**
5. **Stable empirical identity is separate from semantic/structural/measurement contract identity.**
6. **Source/response-scale authority must govern transformations; empirical ordering may not invent it.**
7. **Primary observations, structural rollups and contextual/audit products are distinct authority layers.**
8. **A numeric context field cannot become a pressure fallback.**
9. **Aggregation must preserve heterogeneity/identity status.**
10. **Local failure cannot globally block unrelated lawful atoms, but local lack of authority must remain visible.**
11. **Provenance, known absences and forbidden inference survive transformation.**
12. **Execution validation and whole-machine freeze are different closure types.**
