## Source, response, routing, and composition

Routing/applicability must be executable or explicitly unresolved. Column presence, co-occurrence, or source routing signals do not substitute for exact predicates/risk sets.

Member applicability and completion semantics are configuration-local authority. Family applicability does not prove every member was asked.

Same-respondent joint evidence is irreducible. Aggregate/source-structure evidence cannot manufacture respondent-level co-occurrence.

Measurement portability/value equivalence remains a separate authority problem. Proposition identity, common labels, source equivalence, or weights do not automatically authorize value interchangeability.

Survey weight/design/estimand/variance authority is purpose-specific. Raw weight columns or PSU/strata fragments are not generalized authority for every estimand or uncertainty claim.
