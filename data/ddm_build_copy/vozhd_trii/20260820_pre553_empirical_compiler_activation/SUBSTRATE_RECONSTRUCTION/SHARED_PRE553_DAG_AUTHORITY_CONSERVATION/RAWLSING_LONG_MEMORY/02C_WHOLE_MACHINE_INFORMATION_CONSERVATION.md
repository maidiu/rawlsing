`PHYSICAL_COPYING_IS_OPTIONAL__LAWFUL_INFORMATION_AVAILABILITY_IS_NOT`

A modern DAG may eliminate historical stage-to-stage payload duplication. It may not delete interpretation-critical information, authority state, provenance, or dependency reachability that a lawful downstream consumer requires.

10. **Lane separation** — certified, review/context, unlinked, and blocked material cannot share an ambiguous authority lane.
11. **Invalidation closure** — changed source/authority/contract/determinant invalidates every affected dependent before serving.
12. **Caches are nonauthoritative** — truth must remain recoverable with cache bytes absent.
13. **Stale-cache rejection** — root/dependency mismatch forces rejection/rebuild.

20. **Fail closed on canonical corruption** — no legacy/shadow/old-root rescue unless explicit authority precedence allows it.
21. **No fallback laundering** — correct output from the wrong authority path is failure.
22. **Exact coverage, not sampling** — canaries prove behavior/sensitivity, not global source closure.
23. **Execution green != information green** — query/count parity cannot override field/provenance/negative-state loss.
24. **Maintenance is conservation** — recompute, invalidate, reauthorize, replay, and atomically republish after upstream change.
25. **Verdicts are evidence-state-specific** — repairing an old defect does not retroactively turn an older DO_NOT_FREEZE verdict into PASS; a new court must bind the repaired root.
