## Strongest whole-history pattern already visible

Across otherwise different historical lineages, Rawlsing repeatedly learned the same meta-discipline:

1. preserve the semantic/empirical grain at which a fact is actually known;
2. do not let a convenient carrier grain silently become the authority grain;
3. separate source evidence, semantic authority, empirical observation, structural transformation, model state, and generated-person state;
4. preserve typed negative/unresolved/inapplicable/review states rather than collapsing them into null/zero/false;
5. make currentness and supersession explicit rather than inferred from filename/version/timestamp;
6. allow physical carriers to disappear only when exact authority/function/information access is losslessly preserved;
7. treat replay, invalidation, provenance, future maintenance, and atomic promotion as part of conservation rather than optional operations;
8. do not treat an exhaustive run over an accidentally narrow input universe as global completeness;
9. do not promote heuristic/model-rich generated constructs into empirical truth;
10. preserve enough history/dependency context that downstream interpretation does not have to guess or reopen archaeology.

This common spine does not erase lane-specific obligations. Person-world ontology, joint-source configuration, statistical design authority, structural-channel inference, component vectors, and future semantic-authority production remain distinct questions.
