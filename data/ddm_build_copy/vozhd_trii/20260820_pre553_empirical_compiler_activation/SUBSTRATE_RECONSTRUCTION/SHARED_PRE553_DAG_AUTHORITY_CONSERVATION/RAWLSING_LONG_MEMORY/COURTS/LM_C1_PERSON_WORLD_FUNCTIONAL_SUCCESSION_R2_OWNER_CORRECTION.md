## 1. Absolute Horizon / finite-strata relation

The Absolute Horizon is distinct from the nine finite strata. It must not become a tenth stratum, a religiosity score, a generic ideology variable, or a scalar summary of the nine.

Its relevant person-world function includes the persona's dynamic relation to finite structural determination.

For each finite stratum, preserve at minimum the possibility that the persona may:

- identify with it strongly enough that its categories and pressures remain phenomenologically transparent or naturalized;
- become alienated from it such that it becomes visible as an external, historical, contingent or contradictory relation;
- occupy mixed, contradictory or changing identification/alienation states rather than one permanent binary;
- acquire critical distance from a stratum without thereby escaping its material force;
- remain materially constrained by a stratum while becoming capable of naming, contesting, rerouting, resisting or strategically navigating it;
- re-identify, deepen alienation, or alter the relation through later experience, frameworks, relationships, conflict, memory, practice and material change.

Binding noncollapse:

`ALIENATION_FROM_STRATUM != ABSENCE_OF_STRATUM_PRESSURE`

`IDENTIFICATION_WITH_STRATUM != EXPLICIT_CONSCIOUS_AGREEMENT`

`STRATUM_VISIBILITY != AUTOMATIC_CRITICAL_CONSCIOUSNESS`

`CRITICAL_DISTANCE != AUTOMATIC_LIBERATION_OR_SUCCESSFUL_RESISTANCE`

`ABSOLUTE_HORIZON != TENTH_FINITE_STRATUM`

`MATERIAL_STRUCTURAL_POSITION != PERSONAS_RELATION_TO_THAT_POSITION`

## 2. Why alienation / identification change interpressuring

The nine-strata interpressure system cannot be treated as though every pressure reaches every persona through an identical phenomenological channel.

A stratum that is strongly identified-with may act through the persona as taken-for-granted reality, common sense, duty, identity, inevitability or self-description. Its pressure can therefore be powerful while remaining comparatively invisible as an external relation.

A stratum from which the persona becomes alienated may become objectified and visible. That visibility can create a causal opening for:

- critical distance;
- explicit contradiction recognition;
- comparison with alternative frameworks or social positions;
- deliberate compliance rather than transparent identification;
- resistance, deflection or rerouting;
- changed solidarity or antagonism;
- reflective agency.

This does not mean alienation automatically weakens structural pressure. A visible and hated structure can remain materially overwhelming. The function is to alter HOW the pressure is mediated through the persona and what forms of response become causally available.

The required conceptual chain is therefore approximately:

`FINITE_STRATUM_RELATION / PRESSURE`
`-> PERSONA-SPECIFIC IDENTIFICATION / ALIENATION RELATION`
`-> VISIBILITY / OBJECTIFICATION / NATURALIZATION STATE`
`-> FRAMEWORK + MEMORY + EXPERIENCE MEDIATION`
`-> CRITICAL-DISTANCE / AGENCY / RESPONSE POSSIBILITIES`
`-> LATER PERSONA ACTION / DEVELOPMENT`
`-> POSSIBLE FEEDBACK INTO FUTURE IDENTIFICATION / ALIENATION`

The exact state representation and equations remain unselected.

## 4. Developmental availability — strengthened successor target

R1 correctly rejected universal hard age bands as person essence, but it framed the successor question too narrowly.

Historical fixed driver-age introduction solved a legitimate problem:

`ADULT_SURVEY_CONSTRUCT_EXISTS != CONSTRUCT_CAUSALLY_AVAILABLE_FROM_INFANCY`

The stronger modern target should preserve developmental conditioning while allowing different generated lives to make the same construct available at different ages.

Desired capability:

`PERSON_HISTORY_RESPONSIVE_CONSTRUCT_AVAILABILITY`

A construct/driver may become causally available as a function of lawful generated history including, where relevant:

- chronological age/time as one input or guard, not a universal sufficient cause;
- developmental state;
- language/literacy/education and conceptual prerequisites;
- household and caregiving responsibilities;
- work/labor participation;
- migration/displacement;
- war/violence/state coercion;
- religious formation;
- political organization/social movement participation;
- institutional encounters;
- peer/family/relationship histories;
- media/information exposure;
- generated structural position and materially realized consequences where an owning rule permits their use;
- previous frameworks, memories, practices, conflicts and ruptures;
- other exact generated causal witnesses under governed rule.

The point is not to infer maturity or ideology from social category. It is to model when a problem-space or interpretive construct can lawfully enter this person's causal repertoire.

## 5. Four states must not collapse

A lawful successor should distinguish at least:

`CONSTRUCT_NOT_YET_CAUSALLY_AVAILABLE`

`CONSTRUCT_CAUSALLY_AVAILABLE_BUT_NOT_INTRODUCED`

`CONSTRUCT_INTRODUCED_BUT_DORMANT_OR_NON-SALIENT`

`CONSTRUCT_ACTIVELY_PARTICIPATING_IN_CURRENT_PROCESSING`

These must remain distinct from:

`ACTOR_BELIEF`

`ACTOR_IDENTITY`

`ACTOR_RESPONSE`

`PERSONALITY / ATTRACTOR TYPE`

A construct becoming available means only that lawful later experience can now recruit it. It does not determine what the persona thinks with it.

Do not create a universal age/driver table that becomes a hidden ontology of human development.

## 8. Interaction between developmental availability and Absolute Horizon

These two corrections are related but distinct.

Developmental availability determines whether a construct/problem-space can participate at all.

Identification/alienation determines, among other things, whether structural relations become transparent/naturalized or visible/objectified to the persona and therefore how the persona can process their pressures.

Thus a generated life may lawfully produce trajectories such as:

- early exposure makes a structural contradiction conceptually available;
- repeated lived contradiction produces alienation from a previously identified-with stratum;
- the stratum becomes visible as a contingent social relation;
- alternative frameworks become usable for interpretation;
- critical distance and new agency possibilities become available;
- later actions and relationships feed back into both structural position and identification/alienation state.

The machine must preserve the possibility of this developmental sequence without scripting its outcome.
