# RAWLSING HISTORICAL ARTIFACT AUDIT — METHOD & COMPLETENESS LAW

Status: `ACTIVE_SHARED_AUDIT__NOT_A_NEW_COMMAND__NOT_A_NEW_ARCHITECTURE`

## Purpose

Audit the historical Rawlsing workspace so that Trii Route A and Chetiri Routes B/C may simplify/replace the old V550–V552J physical architecture without losing any already-earned semantic, empirical, structural, replay, audit, maintenance, or downstream capability.

## Unit of audit

Every top-level object in the sealed historical workspace tree receives an identity/classification row. Every object plausibly bearing PRE553 authority, runtime contract, audit, repair, semantic capital, discovery capability, replay law, or downstream-consumption obligation receives a deep artifact audit where payload is available.

For each deep-audited artifact record:
1. exact name/path/hash;
2. what it is;
3. what it was for;
4. conceptual problem/failure mode it solved;
5. intended scope;
6. actual contents;
7. execution/test evidence;
8. execution quality;
9. coverage/completeness;
10. self-declared limitations or evidence of intended-but-unfinished scope;
11. authority/currentness status;
12. irreducible conservation obligations;
13. significant absent/external dependencies;
14. recommended Trii/Chetiri/shared disposition.

## Completeness states

- `DEEP`: governing docs plus relevant payload/results inspected.
- `STRUCTURE_AND_IDENTITY_VERIFIED`: payload identity/shape verified but semantic fields not exhaustively adjudicated.
- `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`: archive/file inventory examined; governing content still open.
- `METADATA_ONLY`: historical tree identity only.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: existence referenced but payload not yet recovered.

No metadata-only object may be treated as semantically retired merely because it looks old.

## Missing-significant-things law

The missing-resource ledger is evidence-driven. A row may enter when a present artifact explicitly or strongly indicates an absent resource/capability through e.g. `missing`, `unpackaged`, `not installed`, `not performed`, `blocked`, `unresolved`, `next pass`, dependency lists, failed audit returns, or a successor claim whose predecessor payload is absent.

Each row records source context, exact/likely name, search keywords, global-tree presence status and retrieval priority.

`NOT_FOUND_IN_CURRENT_AUDIT != NEVER_EXISTED`

## Anti-sprawl / embedding law

This audit is a shared correctness surface only. It cannot become Pyat or another architecture. Every conserved obligation must ultimately be:
- embedded in Trii Route A;
- embedded in Chetiri Route B/C;
- shared prerequisite/binding law common to both; or
- retired/superseded under explicit proof.

## Completion criterion

The archaeology pass is not complete until:
- all top-level objects in the sealed tree are classified;
- all high-priority PRE553-relevant objects are deep-audited or marked unavailable with a retrieval target;
- significant missing references are extracted and checked against the global tree;
- predecessor/successor lineages are reconciled enough that stale package front matter cannot masquerade as final authority;
- the resulting obligation set can be crossed hostilely against Trii/Chetiri.

The later Trii/Chetiri crosscheck will classify each obligation as `FULLY_COVERED`, `NEED_RECOGNIZED_RESOURCE_UNAWARE`, `PARTIALLY_COVERED`, `OBLIVIOUS_TO_NECESSITY`, or `EXPLICITLY_RETIRED_WITH_PROOF`.
