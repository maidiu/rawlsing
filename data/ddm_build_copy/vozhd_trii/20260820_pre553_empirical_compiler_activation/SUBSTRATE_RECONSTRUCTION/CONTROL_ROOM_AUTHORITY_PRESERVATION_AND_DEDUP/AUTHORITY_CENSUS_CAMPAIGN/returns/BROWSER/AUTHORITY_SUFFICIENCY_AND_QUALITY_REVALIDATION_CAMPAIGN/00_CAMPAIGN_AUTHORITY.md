Any later correction must preserve:

- the historical assertion;
- its provenance and prior acceptance state;
- the hostile-review evidence;
- the superseding assertion;
- the explicit same-grain supersession edge;
- any downstream replay consequences.

The object is epistemic improvement with conservation of history, not silent rewriting.
