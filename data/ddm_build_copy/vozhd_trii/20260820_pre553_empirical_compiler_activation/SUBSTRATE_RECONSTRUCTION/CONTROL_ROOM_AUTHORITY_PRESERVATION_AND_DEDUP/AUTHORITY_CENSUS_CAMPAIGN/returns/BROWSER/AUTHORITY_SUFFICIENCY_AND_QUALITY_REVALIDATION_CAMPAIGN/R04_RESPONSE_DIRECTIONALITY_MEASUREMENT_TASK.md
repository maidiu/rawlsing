# R04 — Response, Directionality & Measurement Quality Court

Status: `ISSUED__NONDISPOSITIVE__HIGH_REASONING`

## Mission

Determine whether Rawlsing's response-topology, response-option semantics, measurement-use, directionality/polarity and response-scale authority is sufficient for its actual consumers and semantically correct within its declared jurisdictions.

This court must explicitly distinguish a bounded directional authority from a universal directional map.

## Primary authority surfaces

- R10H O07 measurement/use classification over all 18,276 implementations;
- accepted QRA/manual directional authority for 236 certified rows;
- 145 governed response-scale contracts associated with that directional surface;
- V551/V552 response-scale/directionality/applicability inputs;
- source-native response options/value labels/codebooks;
- H001/H005 response authority and negative-state evidence;
- source-family response-option registries/overlays;
- any current R10F topology classifications bearing on response structure.

## Questions

1. For which consumers is directionality actually required?
2. Is QRA236 jurisdiction-complete for those consumers, or merely partial?
3. Does O07 correctly classify binary, signed scalar, unsigned ordered, nominal, continuous, count, ranking, tradeoff, open-text, mixed/conditional and unresolved structures?
4. Are response contracts source-faithful and correctly bound?
5. Are neutral, refusal, unknown, inapplicable and missing states preserved correctly?
6. Are bipolar semantic differentials being mistaken for scalar support/opposition or tradeoffs?
7. Are nominal/status variables receiving unjustified polarity?
8. Are reverse-keyed or asymmetric scales handled correctly?
9. Are 30 O07 unresolved rows truly source-limited, or did existing response evidence resolve them?
10. Are there material rows outside QRA236 that downstream consumers nevertheless require directionality for?

## High-risk strata

- QRA rows created or accepted in older agent stages;
- scale semantics with reversed direction;
- GSS/LiTS structural weirdness already found by R10F/H004;
- mixed modality batteries;
- binary responses whose semantic orientation is not obvious from code order;
- source families with translation/native-response differences;
- explicit O07 unresolved rows;
- response-option authority whose succession is not proved;
- questions used in high-fanout relations or empirical scoring.
