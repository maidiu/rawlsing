# R02 Semantic Expansion Recommendations

These recommendations are non-dispositive evidence proposals. R02 creates no new canonical semantic authority.

## 1. Expand the R10G normative-role class, not the whole semantic universe

The confirmed defect is concentrated in canonical role `NORMATIVE_OR_POLICY_STANCE`. Expand that class as a census against exact source evidence. The review should compare, for every candidate unit:

- exact respondent-facing question text;
- parent/stem wording where the child label alone is incomplete;
- response options/topology;
- actor, target, object, condition, modality, horizon and respondent universe where proposition-defining;
- current semantic summary and measured predicate;
- current canonical role.

Prioritize units whose own predicate/summary contains descriptive or perceptual signals such as `perceived`, `believe`, `guaranteed`, `extent`, `frequency`, `contribution`, `advancement`, `commitment`, `performance`, `observed`, `realized`, or causal attribution. Do not assume those lexical signals are themselves dispositive; use them only to prioritize exact source review.

The required correction grain is proposition class. A unit can discuss morally or politically charged content while still measuring a respondent's belief about whether another actor is committed to it. Object content must not be confused with respondent modality.

Applicable requirements: `TIR-015`, `TIR-016`, `TIR-017`, `TIR-025`.

## 2. Preserve actor and target throughout the role audit

The QGAZA controls show why actor preservation matters: Israeli government commitment, Israeli public commitment, and Palestinian public commitment are separate descriptive judgments even though all concern the same two-state-solution object. Any successor must preserve actor identity and must not collapse these because the object proposition is shared.

Likewise, P2's South Asia and GSS controls show that target/referent changes are durable semantic distinctions. Existing negative guards and target-specific successors should remain intact during expansion.

Applicable requirements: `TIR-004`, `TIR-005`, `TIR-015`, `TIR-017`, `TIR-057`.

## 3. Do not rewrite good semantic authority as collateral damage

R9D's Arab V/VIII clerics split, WVS evidence-ceiling discipline, R10G's GSS target correction, and the reviewed P2 negative-continuity controls all survive this bounded challenge. Expansion should be surgical: identify the defective role assignments and any directly dependent coordinate claims, not rebuild 3,324 semantic units from scratch.

## 4. Complete TIR-019 information succession before retirement

Build a field-class succession table for QSM negative semantic information. At minimum, prove exact conservation or intentional governed retirement for:

- `forbidden_inference`;
- `does_not_measure`;
- evidence ceilings;
- prohibited/negative semantic claims;
- explicit noncomparability / unresolved guards where they carry semantic meaning.

A newer positive semantic carrier is not a successor proof. The table must identify predecessor grain, successor carrier/field, transformation rule if any, negative-state preservation, consumer compatibility, and HQ supersession authority. Until then, QSM negative information remains non-retirable under R00.

Applicable requirements: `TIR-019`, `TIR-020`, `TIR-050`.

## 5. Residual continuity hostile revalidation

The 119-edge Afrobarometer carrier census is replayable and showed no direct contradiction at its recorded evidence-contract grain. If HQ requires stronger source-native revalidation, expand by exact deterministic selection over endpoints/edges and refetch questionnaire/codebook context. Preserve the existing distinction among proposition continuity, proposition+response continuity, wording-revision continuity, response-topology variation, and explicit portability ceilings.

Do not treat `semantic_role_equal = false` as a defect by itself: some accepted proposition-continuity edges intentionally restrict portability or separately version response/role differences. The hostile question is whether the proposition-continuity claim is actually supported by source evidence.

Applicable requirements: `TIR-004`, `TIR-005`, `TIR-017`, `TIR-057`.
