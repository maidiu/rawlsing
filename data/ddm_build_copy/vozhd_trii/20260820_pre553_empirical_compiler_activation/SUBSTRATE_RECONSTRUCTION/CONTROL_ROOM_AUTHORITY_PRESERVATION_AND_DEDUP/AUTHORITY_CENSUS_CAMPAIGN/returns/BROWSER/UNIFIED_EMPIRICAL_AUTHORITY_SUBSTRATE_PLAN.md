## Important correction: recovery is not global sufficiency/quality certification

The preceding courts proved that authority objects exist and can be bound at their recovered jurisdictions. They did **not** globally prove:

- that each required authority class covers the exact denominator demanded by recovered consumers;
- that partial scope is always intentional;
- that every legacy accepted semantic/manual judgment is semantically good enough;
- that old project acceptance constitutes current hostile revalidation;
- that all source-family/manual obligations have been exhaustively searched;
- that succession is fully governed for all authority lineages.

Therefore:

`NO_MISSING_PROVED_ROW`

means

`NO_NEW_AUTHORITY_YET_AUTHORIZED_BY_PROVED_ABSENCE`

and does **not** mean

`NO_FUTURE_AUTHORITY_WILL_BE_REQUIRED`.

For every required authority class:

- what exact consumer/grain requires it?

- what existing authority covers it?
- is partiality intentional or deficient?

For every material semantic/manual legacy lineage:

- what produced it and with what evidence?
- does it survive risk-stratified source-backed hostile review?
- are defects local or systematic?
- does lineage/provenance predict defect risk?
- is targeted expansion required?
