# R01 — Required-Authority Coverage Sufficiency Court

Status: `ISSUED__NONDISPOSITIVE`

## Mission

Determine, authority family by authority family, **what coverage Rawlsing actually requires** for the recovered current-35 compiler/runtime/empirical capabilities, and whether existing authority covers that required jurisdiction.

This court exists to stop mistakes such as calling a 236-row bounded directionality authority either obviously complete or obviously incomplete without first proving its lawful consumer denominator.

## Core inputs

- `AUTHORITY_EXPECTATION_UNIVERSE.csv`
- `CURRENT35_AUTHORITY_QUALITY_AND_GAP_LEDGER.csv`
- `NORMALIZED_AUTHORITY_BINDING_MATRIX.csv`
- `TRII_CHETIRI_AUTHORITY_REQUIREMENTS.md`
- accepted runtime/compiler authority and recovered capability obligations
- R10F/R10G/R10H/R10I current successor surfaces
- H001-H005 census/adjudication records

## Required work

### A. Reconstruct required denominators

For every material authority class, state:

- exact consumer(s);
- required grain;
- required scope/denominator;
- whether the authority is universal, conditional, bounded or not applicable;
- what failure mode occurs if authority is absent.

Do not infer required scope from the size of the existing authority file.

### B. Compare existing coverage to required coverage

For each class, classify uncovered units as one of:

- outside lawful jurisdiction;
- intentionally bounded / not required;
- explicit source-limited unresolved;
- blocked/review state;
- execution inheritance debt;
- unlocated/search incomplete;
- actual authority deficiency candidate.

### C. Resolve stale expectation-ledger statuses

Explicitly revisit at least:

- EXP-004 respondent source;
- EXP-005 respondent semantics;
- EXP-007 driver/facet current quality/coverage;
- EXP-008 response option;
- EXP-009 family overlays;
- EXP-011 position conditioning;
- EXP-012 manual exceptions;
- EXP-013 routing/applicability;
- EXP-015 QSM predecessor lineage where relevant to scope;
- EXP-018 V550 authority inputs;
- EXP-020 V552J;
- EXP-021 I04;
- EXP-023 through EXP-029 family authority classes;
- EXP-030 supersession registry as a required governance object.

Later R10 work may close some old search states. Prove this explicitly rather than assuming either old status or later closure.

### D. Bounded-authority tests

Give special attention to:

- QRA 236 rows / 145 response contracts;
- FQRA 18,250 P2-bound implementations with 26 outside target and 9 blocked joins;
- V525b partial historical current-35 projections;
- source-family-specific manual authority;
- R10H full carrier coverage containing large explicit unresolved surfaces.

## Forbidden

- no semantic-quality re-adjudication except where needed to identify jurisdiction;
- no conclusion that more rows are always better;
- no conclusion that an existing file's denominator defines required scope;
- no new authority creation;
- no physical schema design.
