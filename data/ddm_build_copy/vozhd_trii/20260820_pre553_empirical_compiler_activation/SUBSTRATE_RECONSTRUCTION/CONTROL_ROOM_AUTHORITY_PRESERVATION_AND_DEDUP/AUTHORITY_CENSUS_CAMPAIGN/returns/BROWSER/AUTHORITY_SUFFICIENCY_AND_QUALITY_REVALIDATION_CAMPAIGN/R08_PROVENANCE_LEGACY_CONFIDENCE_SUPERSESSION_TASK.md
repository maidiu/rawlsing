## Supersession law

A proposed supersession edge requires:

- same assertion grain;
- explicit or reconstructable predecessor/successor relationship;
- evidence that the later object intends to replace rather than augment;
- no loss of accepted information unless explicitly retired;
- preserved negative/review states;
- source/quality evidence supporting the successor.

Otherwise classify as coexistence, augmentation, projection or unresolved succession.
