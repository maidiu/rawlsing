Status: `ISSUED__NONDISPOSITIVE__HIGH_REASONING`

## Mission

Revalidate the semantic quality, coverage jurisdiction and provenance confidence of Rawlsing's driver/facet/domain/axis/target assignments without allowing d62's flat-row history to determine authority by accident.

## Questions

For audited assignments determine:

1. Does the named driver/facet actually follow from the source proposition?
2. Is the assignment at the correct grain: semantic driver, structural domain, relation axis, target, context-only, strata-only, etc.?
3. Did a later structural assignment lawfully add information without superseding an earlier semantic driver?
4. Are blanks intentional, unresolved or merely carrier-loss?
5. Are multiple drivers/facets legitimate many-to-many assertions or accidental duplicates?
6. Are context/admin/respondent-position variables being forced into substantive drivers?
7. Did legacy agents overuse broad drivers because finer distinctions were not yet available?
8. Are targets/axes stable across equivalent implementations where source meaning is stable?
