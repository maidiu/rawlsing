## 2. Central holding

P10-B08 cannot yet be converted into a positive preservation theorem.

The historical P3/P3A/P3C stack mixed two different things:

1. **implementation-specific mechanisms** that are not intrinsically sacred — particular PostgreSQL tables, strict-component construction machinery, ranking tables, one collision schema, one importer, one release number; and
2. **irreducible authority-maintenance functions** that Rawlsing still needs if a future source, corrected source interpretation, new semantic family, split family, blocked case, withdrawal, or contradictory evidence is ever to change current semantic authority without rewriting history.

Accepted post-G5 law has generalized a large part of (2): P1 separates claim identity from authority identity; P2 supplies typed lifecycle/current-selection semantics; P4 supplies operation-prerequisite change/invalidation law; P7 supplies the bounded forward dependency/replay architecture; P8 preserves shared-policy factoring without erasing object-local meaning.

- current I05 `DISCOVERY_FRONTIER` jurisdiction is a candidate/search/validation/coverage frontier and is explicitly not a T01–T15 semantic-truth producer;
- P7 can bind exact evidence and authority premises used by an admission, but `PREREQUISITE_VALIDATOR != SEMANTIC_TRUTH_GENERATOR` and replay is not semantic adjudication;
- P2 proves lifecycle meaning but explicitly leaves the exact live lifecycle/current-selection owner/API/carrier unproved;
- no accepted post-G5 contract positively owns the complete family split/merge/variant/exclusion genealogy and no-loss promotion function;
- historical R184 replay proves frozen authority/replay, not a general future-maintenance compiler; later successor-release work itself encountered a missing complete executable object-graph/import boundary.

Therefore frozen read access plus generalized lifecycle law is **not** yet equivalent to future authority-maintenance capability.

## 4. Hostile-rule results

### `H1 — GENERIC_IMMUTABILITY == SEMANTIC_LIFECYCLE`

**Rejected.**

Current exact refs/snapshots can preserve bytes and historical identity. They cannot decide whether a successor `RECERTIFIES`, `CORRECTS`, `WITHDRAWS`, `ONTOLOGY_EXPANDS`, or becomes current. P2 reserves that classification to semantic lifecycle authority.

### `H2 — I05 NOMINATION == SEMANTIC AUTHORITY PRODUCTION`

**Rejected.**

P4's accepted jurisdiction is candidate/search/validation/coverage frontier. It explicitly does not govern typed T01–T15 semantic truth. Candidate generation can nominate work; it cannot promote itself into canonical semantic authority.

### `H3 — P7 EXACT EVIDENCE BINDING == SEMANTIC ADJUDICATION`

**Rejected.**

P7's value is exact dependency binding and reverse reachability. It deliberately leaves semantic truth upstream. A perfect evidence/ref bundle still requires a semantic court to decide what, if anything, the evidence authorizes.

### `H4 — REOPEN == ACCEPT`

**Rejected.**

P2 freezes `REOPENS_BLOCKED = ADJUDICATION_ELIGIBLE` only. Original BLOCKED history remains replayable and no truth follows mechanically.

### `H5 — REVERSE DEPENDENCY == LIFECYCLE CLASSIFICATION`

**Rejected.**

P7 may identify descendants after an already-governed change. It may not infer the change type from hashes, chronology, equal member sets, or topology.

### `H6 — R184 FROZEN REPLAY == FUTURE MAINTENANCE`

**Rejected.**

R184 and Section 84 are strong evidence of frozen authority/replay. They do not prove a general current compiler for future semantic-family production. Historical successor-release work requiring new candidate families/revisions, adjudications, signatures, evidence snapshots, genealogy and release ledgers demonstrates that a later release can require authority-production work beyond replay; the old fixed-shape replay importer was not a complete successor compiler.

### `H7 — OLD P3 PHYSICAL FORM MUST SURVIVE`

**Rejected.**

The old table/layout/importer forms are not intrinsically authoritative. Chetiri may remove machinery after its functions have exact successors. This court finds several forms replaceable but several functions still unowned/unexecuted; therefore the whole mechanism cannot yet retire.
