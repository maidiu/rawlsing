# C07 — COMPLETE LEARNED-WORLD PRODUCT & SERVICE CONSTITUTION

Status: `NON_DISPOSITIVE__RETURNED_TO_HQ`

## Holding

Rawlsing's lawful learned-world destination is a provenance-bound DAG of empirical-social learned releases/services, structural learned releases, and shared replay/authority material, with optional regenerable serving representations at lifecycle-specific grains; THE PAST remains primary empirical evidence and THE LIFE remains V3-owned.

## 1. Absolute domain boundary

### THE PAST
Primary epistemic authority for empirical evidence remains exact source bytes/content identity plus source family/wave/proxy identity, decoder/replay determinants, documentary/source-semantic authority, question/variable/topology/routing/design bindings, and their exact authority roots. Learned products may cite and transform these under governance; they do not replace them as source truth.

### THE LEARNED WORLD
Historically bounded governed knowledge may contain two noncollapsed families plus shared lineage:

1. **Empirical-social learned world** — discovered candidates, validation/release state, accepted empirical relations, interactions and same-respondent cotravel, higher-order joints, multimodal/minority/contradictory modes, source-universe-specific and historically indexed empirical fields, positioned-subject empirical possibility services, and DDM analytical services.
2. **Structural learned world** — V553–V558 governed structural material, ContextResolver material, temporal/geographic/scope applicability, mediation and pressure-flow prerequisites, support/uncertainty/admission provenance, direct upstream structural references, and justified runtime indexes.
3. **Shared release/replay graph** — authority roots, source roots, product dependency fingerprints, release identities, explanation lineage, invalidation determinants, and replay receipts.

A learned object can simultaneously be a meaningful governed release, deterministically rebuildable, worth retaining operationally, and still subordinate to source evidence. Those predicates are independent.

### THE LIFE
V3 owns generated actor history: relationships; exposure; action; consequences; orientation; identification; alienation; memory; accessible knowledge; realized disposition; belief/response; attractor realization; migration/property history as biography; and all other developed life. No learned-world product may pre-generate these.

## 2. Noncollapse equations

`POPULATION_EMPIRICAL_FACT != POSITIONED_SUBJECT_EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY_FIELD != V3_DEVELOPED_OR_REALIZED_DISPOSITION != ACTOR_BELIEF_OR_RESPONSE`

`DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE != VALIDATED_EMPIRICAL_RELATION != STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION`

`EMPIRICAL_COMPARABILITY != GENERATED_STRUCTURAL_POSITION_BINDING`

Probability geometry is lawful learned knowledge where governed. It constrains possibility; it does not make demographic puppets. V3 realizes one life through history.

## 4. Empirical-social semantic products

The following capabilities must remain representable/servable after discovery when terminally governed: discovered social forks; candidate relation state; validation evidence and accepted/rejected state; accepted empirical relations; interactions; same-respondent cotravel; higher-order joint configurations; multimodal/minority/contradictory modes; source-universe-specific possibility fields; historical/wave-indexed fields; positioned-subject reverse inference; uncertainty/support/release state; explanation lineage; DDM analytical views.

A compiler may recompute some of these from source. Rebuildability alone does not erase the semantic product requirement when a governed learned release is cited downstream. Physical retention is determined separately by lineage cost, reproducibility, serving needs and publication contract.

## 5. Structural semantic products

Structural products may directly reference authoritative earlier V553–V557 objects rather than republish them when identity and availability are sufficient. V558 is the immutable structural repository boundary already accepted by upstream authority. Runtime racks/indexes are lawful only as semantics-preserving views over governed structural products; their deletion may increase latency but cannot change meaning.

## 6. Persistence law per object

Four independent questions apply to every object:

1. **Semantic product requirement** — must the governed knowledge/capability exist or remain addressable?
2. **Physical persistence requirement** — must bytes for this representation survive between sessions?
3. **Operational warm-retention value** — does retained material materially improve a named lifecycle?
4. **Primary epistemic authority** — what evidence ultimately authorizes the claim?

Primary classes used by this return are `PERSIST_AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE`, `PERSIST_AUTHORITATIVE_SEMANTIC_OR_LEARNED_PRODUCT`, `PERSIST_PROVENANCE_OR_REPLAY_RECEIPT`, `PERSIST_REGENERABLE_CACHE_OPTIONAL`, `TRANSIENT_COMPILER_STATE`, and `DERIVE_ON_DEMAND`.

## 7. Five lifecycle objectives

- **Compiler/discovery:** exactness and search completeness dominate; broad transient state is lawful; latency may be slow.
- **Generated-person runtime:** only justified structural/context lookups need low latency. Runtime convenience does not delete empirical-social capabilities or promote caches to authority.
- **Empirical-inference service:** historical, universe-specific, joint and multimodal geometry must remain queryable; positioned-subject outputs may be derived per request and optionally cached.
- **DDM analytical service:** may use slower governed views/materializations; must retain universe/time/provenance distinctions.
- **Audit/replay:** may be cold and slow; source/authority roots, release identity, operation/model versions, lineage and deterministic reconstruction paths must survive.

## 8. Selective invalidation

Invalidation follows dependency fingerprints, not global purge:

- a source-wave change invalidates descendants that cite that exact source/release root;
- a question/semantic binding change invalidates descendants of that binding;
- a discovery algorithm/rule change invalidates search receipts/candidates dependent on it and downstream accepted products only where the governing release treats the rule as a determinant;
- a validation rule change invalidates validation/accepted-relation descendants, not source facts;
- a possibility-model change invalidates the model release, derived positioned-subject fields and their caches;
- a structural admission/authority change invalidates structural descendants under its dependency edge;
- a cache-key determinant change invalidates the cache only.

## 9. Empirical universe and time law

Local, regional, global and source-family universes remain distinct identities. No composition/merger is implied by convenience. Wave, period, proxy, historical gap and noncomparability are first-class coordinates. `latest` is never an epistemic merge operator.

## 10. Repeated actor evaluation

Actor aging, migration, property/state changes can require repeated possibility-field evaluation because the query coordinate has changed. That does not authorize upstream actor history. V3 supplies only lawfully comparable query facts; learned-world services return evidence-conditioned possibilities. The authority that decides which generated actor facts are empirically comparable is currently missing and must fail closed where not already explicitly governed.

## 12. Strongest failure witnesses

**Starvation witness:** treating the destination as only a current-V3 structural runtime file would discard autonomous discovery outputs, candidate/validation lineage, higher-order/multimodal/minority geometry, historical/multi-universe empirical fields, positioned-subject inference and DDM serviceability. Current V3 non-consumption is not authority to delete these learned-world capabilities.

**Leakage witness:** persisting a positioned-subject possibility output as actor orientation, belief, memory, realized disposition, attractor or biography moves THE LIFE upstream and is forbidden.

## 13. C07 holding

The complete learned world is lawfully a DAG, not one giant compiled-everything artifact. Persistence is product-specific and lifecycle-specific; source evidence remains source authority; learned releases remain governed derivatives; optional indexes remain replaceable; V3 owns the life.
