### 3.2 Predicate-atom catalog construction

Classification: `NEW_RULE_AUTHORITY_DECISION_PROPOSED`, with concrete catalog `DERIVED_MECHANICALLY_FROM_EXACT_GOVERNED_OBJECTS_AFTER_RULE_CHOICE`.

For one exact production source-universe/release instance, construct the atom catalog by exhaustive deterministic closure over **all finite, exact, authority-resolved I03 predicate leaves that are lawful in that instance**:

1. every exact governed empirical-position/coordinate value predicate whose value/state domain is finitely and authoritatively declared;
2. every exact governed survey-response value predicate whose canonical value/state domain is finitely and authoritatively declared;
3. every exact governed typed response-state predicate;
4. every exact governed routing/applicability-state predicate;
5. exact governed baseline/risk-cohort predicates needed by the frozen I05 compiler;
6. exact derived branch-cohort predicates generated by prior discovery ancestry only when their parent ancestry ExactRefs are already frozen and the current candidate family calls for them.

Eligibility is determined solely by exact governed semantic objects and authority, never by observed prevalence, frequency, association, support, effect size, diagnostic score, or source-row presence. A value/state that is lawful but unobserved remains eligible when its finite domain is authoritatively declared.

Every atom must bind exact source-universe/release scope, risk-set refs, grammar/operator authority, semantic question/coordinate/value/state refs, same-respondent binding semantics, routing/applicability semantics where relevant, and exact topology authority where the leaf meaning depends on topology. Missing/conflicting authority fails closed; generic physical missingness never manufactures a semantic state.

A semantic object with no exact finite predicate domain and no already-governed finite predicate construction is outside this profile's exact-bounded atom frontier. That exclusion is an authority/domain limitation, not a data-derived pruning decision, and must be recorded in the instance manifest.

Canonical atom order is ascending exact predicate `ExactRef.key`, then canonical atom kind; duplicate exact predicate refs collapse. Atom IDs are deterministic semantic-hash-derived IDs over operation identity + exact predicate ref + exact scope/risk refs + atom kind + ancestry refs.

### 3.4 K bound

Classification: `NEW_RULE_AUTHORITY_DECISION_PROPOSED`, with per-instance integer `DERIVED_MECHANICALLY_FROM_EXACT_GOVERNED_OBJECTS_AFTER_RULE_CHOICE`.

Do not impose an arbitrary numeric production K. For each exact scope/risk partition, let `K_partition` equal the number of distinct eligible conditioner atom refs after only exact-scope incompatibility and exact-ref duplicate removal. The production authority sets:

`maximum_conditioner_arity_K = max(K_partition)`

across its admitted risk-set partitions, and each generated rule may combine only atoms from one compatible partition.

No atom is removed because it is rare, redundant in observed data, weak, univariate-null, inconvenient, or expensive. Semantically contradictory but individually lawful atoms may still generate valid-empty/zero-support candidates; exact execution decides the terminal state.

Capability justification: this closes every finite lawful conjunction, cartesian conditioner and XOR arity that the accepted compiler can represent. It therefore includes the `K>=2` terrain required by hostile XOR and Simpson constructions whenever at least two exact compatible atoms exist.

Outside frontier: only combinations crossing forbidden source/release/risk authority boundaries, duplicate exact atoms, or predicates not finitely authorizable under the adopted profile. Performance implication: K may be large; C05-R2 measures that cost after freeze rather than shrinking K semantically.

### 3.5 J bound

Classification: `NEW_RULE_AUTHORITY_DECISION_PROPOSED`, with per-instance integer `DERIVED_MECHANICALLY_FROM_EXACT_GOVERNED_OBJECTS_AFTER_RULE_CHOICE`.

Do not impose an arbitrary numeric production J. For each exact query partition, let `J_partition` equal the number of distinct exact target refs that can participate together under the same source/release/risk/measure scope with fully resolved I03 target-domain and topology authority. Set:

`maximum_target_arity_J = max(J_partition)`

and require the identical value in both grammar `limits.maximum_target_arity_J` and `DiscoveryAuthority.topology_scope.maximum_target_arity_J`.

Capability justification: all arities from unary through the largest exactly compatible target set remain reachable, including two-way, three-way, five-way and higher-order joint structures. Same-marginals/different-joint and higher-order hostile capability is therefore not amputated.

Outside frontier: only target tuples that are not jointly lawful under exact source/risk/measure/topology authority. Performance implication: query-template and joint-domain counts may be very large; C05-R2 measures this downstream.

### 3.6 Recursive-depth bound

Classification: `NEW_RULE_AUTHORITY_DECISION_PROPOSED`, with per-instance closure `DERIVED_MECHANICALLY_FROM_EXACT_GOVERNED_OBJECTS_AFTER_RULE_CHOICE`.

Use finite ancestry closure, not an arbitrary small integer. A recursive branch may extend only by adding at least one conditioner atom ExactRef not already present on that root-to-leaf ancestry path. It may never drop or rewrite parent ancestry, change source/release/risk scope, or reuse an ancestor atom as a new depth step.

Therefore for a partition:

`maximum_recursive_depth = K_partition`

and the production instance records the maximum across admitted partitions. Termination is guaranteed because each depth step consumes at least one previously unused finite atom ref.

Capability justification: every finite nested subgroup path representable from the admitted atom catalog is reachable; recursive hostile capability is preserved. Outside frontier: infinite/repeated-atom recursion and cross-scope ancestry mutation, both of which are semantically invalid rather than merely expensive. Performance is measured in C05-R2.

### 3.7 Source-universe scope

Classification: `NEW_RULE_AUTHORITY_DECISION_PROPOSED`.

Adopt **one production `DiscoveryAuthority` instance per exact source universe + exact sealed release**. A campaign may register many such independent authorities, but I05 executes each named universe independently.

Cross-universe comparison/composition is not implied by registry membership. Any pooled/composite candidate requires separate positive `SourceUniverseRelation` / nonduplication / estimand-composition authority already accepted by I03. Without it, universes stay distinct and noncomparability is preserved.

This choice prevents false respondent joins, duplicate counting and hidden harmonization while still permitting a registry of many exact waves/releases.

## 6. Freeze-before-data production construction sequence

After HQ adoption, a production instance is lawful only in this order:

1. resolve one exact sealed release/universe and all governing semantic/replay authority;
2. deterministically enumerate exact risk, measure and topology partitions;
3. deterministically construct complete atom and query-template catalogs;
4. derive K, J and recursive-depth closure from those catalogs;
5. deterministically expand all accepted family/form rules;
6. construct canonical grammar material and grammar hash/ref;
7. construct and positively authorize exact `DiscoveryAuthority`;
8. validate authority closure and catalog scope;
9. compile the full candidate frontier and seal candidate identities/frontier hash/count;
10. only then open/read observed response data for I03 candidate evaluation.

Any post-step-9 change to catalog, K/J/depth, rule set, release, risk, measure or topology scope creates a different authority/frontier and requires a new pre-data freeze. It cannot be post-selection tuning.

## 7. Hostile-capability preservation

The proposal preserves:
- XOR with zero unary signal: all lawful conditioner combinations remain reachable and K is not unary-screened;
- Simpson reversal: exact exposure/stratum atoms remain eligible independent of pooled results;
- minority/nonmodal structure: no prevalence pruning;
- recursive subgroup forks: exhaustive finite unused-atom ancestry closure;
- same-respondent cotravel: exact respondent identity/risk scope and target templates retained;
- higher-order joints: J is maximal exact compatible target closure, not a small fixture value;
- typed negative/routing states: finite authoritative states are atoms/results, not generic missing;
- routed/applicable denominators: risk authority precedes condition evaluation;
- weighted/design estimands: all authorized measures remain distinct;
- multi-universe noncollapse: one authority per exact universe/release and positive composition authority only;
- topology-general 0/1/many structure: no scalarization or order/rank loss.

Authority-61/70 concrete blind-fixture selector, four-workload universe, selected recursive fixture and `k=2` remain `CERTIFICATION_ONLY_NOT_PRODUCTION_LAW`.
