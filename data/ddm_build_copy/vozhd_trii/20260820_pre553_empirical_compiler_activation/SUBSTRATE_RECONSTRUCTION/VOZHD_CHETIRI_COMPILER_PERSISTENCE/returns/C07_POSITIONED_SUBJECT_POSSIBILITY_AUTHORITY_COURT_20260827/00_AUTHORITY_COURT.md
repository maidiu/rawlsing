## 3. Recovered domain law

The recovered domain partition remains:

`THE PAST`
= exact source evidence and source-semantic/provenance authority.

`THE LEARNED WORLD`
= governed empirical-social learned releases/services + governed structural learned releases + shared provenance/replay.

`THE LIFE`
= V3-generated actor history, interpretation, orientation, belief, memory, realized disposition, response, and development.

The positioned-subject empirical possibility capability belongs to THE LEARNED WORLD as an empirical-inference service. It may accept exact downstream-owned facts as query criteria only through a lawful empirical-comparability seam. It does not acquire ownership of those facts.

Binding noncollapse:

`POPULATION_EMPIRICAL_FACT != POSITIONED_SUBJECT_EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY_FIELD != V3_DEVELOPED_OR_REALIZED_DISPOSITION != ACTOR_BELIEF_OR_RESPONSE`

`EMPIRICAL_RESPONDENT_COORDINATE != GENERATED_ACTOR_FACT != GENERATED_STRUCTURAL_POSITION != EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY_FIELD_CONDITIONER`

`DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE != VALIDATED_EMPIRICAL_RELATION != STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION != ACTOR_STATE`

## 4. Empirical possibility is not prediction

A positioned-subject possibility result answers a bounded empirical question:

> Given these exact, separately authored subject facts that have been lawfully established as comparable to these exact empirical respondent coordinates, within this exact empirical universe/time/release, what joint empirical configurations/relations remain supported, plausible, available, absent, uncertain, or unresolved under the governing empirical model?

It does **not** answer:

- what this actor will believe;
- what this actor will do;
- which disposition the actor will acquire;
- which developmental path the actor will take;
- which candidate will be selected in V3;
- a calibrated individual forecast unless a future authority separately creates such a prediction operation.

The field may constrain or inform the empirical possibility-space surrounding a person. V3 history remains free to realize divergent lawful lives inside, across, or in tension with that empirical evidence, subject to its own authority.

Therefore:

`EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY != INDIVIDUAL_PREDICTION`

A model authority could later authorize predictive semantics as a distinct operation, but no such semantics may be smuggled into this capability.

## 5. Possibility is not normative recommendation

Nothing in the recovered authority gives this empirical service jurisdiction to decide what the actor **should** believe, choose, become, prefer, or pursue.

The service may return empirical support, uncertainty, conditional relevance, incompatibility, negative authority, or unresolved state as authorized by a future model rule. It may not convert empirical frequency/support into desirability, moral ranking, policy advice, or a preferred life path.

Therefore:

`EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY != NORMATIVE_RECOMMENDATION`

No normative ordering is recovered or created here.

## 6. Respondent-derived evidence is not actor ontology

The empirical side begins from governed respondent-derived/source-derived coordinates and learned empirical-social relations. The actor side begins from generated V3 facts and generated structural-position facts owned by their downstream authorities.

A shared label is not a bridge.

Examples of forbidden implicit bridges include:

- empirical respondent religion label -> actor identity;
- empirical income coordinate -> actor class position;
- regional prevalence -> actor membership;
- demographic frequency -> actor probability;
- generated structural position -> empirical conditioner merely because names resemble one another.

A generated actor fact becomes an `EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY_FIELD_CONDITIONER` only through separate positive empirical-comparability authority that binds exact actor fact semantics to exact empirical coordinate semantics, scope, source universe, time/wave/proxy treatment, and provenance.

The empirical service consumes that binding. It does not create it by inference convenience.

## 7. Upstream evidence is not downstream interpretation

PRE553/learned-world jurisdiction may own:

- source-backed empirical coordinates and relations;
- discovery/validation/release state where separately governed;
- joint, multimodal, minority, contradictory empirical geometry;
- historical and source-universe identity;
- support/uncertainty/negative/unresolved states;
- the governed possibility-model release and explanation lineage when authority exists.

V3/downstream jurisdiction owns:

- actor fact generation;
- structural-position establishment/transition;
- biography/history;
- exposure and encounter realization;
- interpretation and awareness;
- orientation and identity;
- memory;
- belief/response;
- developmental realization.

The query seam may carry downstream-owned refs/hashes into an empirical query. That is a query criterion, not ontology transfer and not semantic ownership transfer.

## 9. A/B/C parity does not select a permanent empirical carrier

The logical capability is mandatory; the physical carrier is not fixed here.

Recovered authority explicitly permits the service to be supplied through, for example:

- source-backed virtual incidence/query execution;
- a separately governed compiled learned-world empirical model;
- a hybrid/regenerable representation;
- lawful caches/indexes over any of the above.

Those are architectural/physical possibilities, not holdings selecting a route.

Binding distinction:

`LEARNED_WORLD_PRODUCT_CAPABILITY != PERMANENT_EMPIRICAL_CARRIER_REQUIREMENT`

A query result may be derived on demand and optionally cached. A cache is not authority. If a compiled learned empirical model later receives governing release authority, it may have persistent release identity even when rebuildable. Neither proposition proves that all transformed empirical incidence must persist.

Thus A/B/C parity is measured at the logical interface, semantics, provenance, replay, fail-closed behavior, and result equivalence ceiling—not by requiring identical storage.
