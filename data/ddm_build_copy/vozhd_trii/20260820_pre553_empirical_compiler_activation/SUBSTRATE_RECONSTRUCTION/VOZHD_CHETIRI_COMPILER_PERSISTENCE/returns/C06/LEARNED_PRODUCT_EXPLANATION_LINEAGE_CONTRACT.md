## Empirical learned-product minimum lineage

For every governed accepted empirical relation/model/product, retain or exact-reference, as applicable:

- product/release schema, object ID and content hash;
- source snapshot/release roots;
- empirical universe/source family/wave/time/geography/scope;
- canonical and native question identities;
- target and conditioner predicates;
- branch/factor/interaction ancestry;
- risk set and routing/applicability basis;
- weights, survey design and estimand authority;
- respondent-set hash or replay coordinates;
- raw and weighted support;
- uncertainty/evidence state;
- candidate discovery receipt and algorithm/search-law version;
- exact search frontier/completeness/stop status;
- validation evidence/state and validation/model-selection rule version;
- RNG/subsample/split identity where used;
- source disagreement/noncomparability state;
- authority snapshot roots;
- parent learned-product refs;
- producer replay receipt.

Do not replace source-universe/time identity with `latest`.

## Candidate and validation history

A published product must not erase whether it was:
- discovered but unevaluated;
- validation failed;
- supported but noncomparable;
- rejected/limited by authority;
- validated for a named empirical use;
- eligible/ineligible for positioned-subject inference;
- eligible/ineligible or admitted/rejected by I04.

C06 does not invent these substantive rules. Missing rule authority is itself lineage state.

## I04 and structural continuation

Where empirical material enters I04, lineage must continue through:
- exact empirical fact/query refs;
- I04 admission rule/authority;
- decision state;
- target structural proposition/use;
- source/time/scope/estimand/measure/weight refs;
- uncertainty and negative/noncomparability refs;
- transformation/replay ref.

Downstream structural producer objects retain their own producer identity/build/hash and exact dependencies. V557 publication and V558 repository identities are administrative envelopes: they may package/index producer meaning but may not replace it. Preserve role/cardinality/order closure.

## Audit question

For any published learned empirical relation or structural object, an auditor should be able to answer:

> Which exact source evidence, authorities, search trajectory/frontier state, validation evidence, admissions, producer rules and historical releases caused Rawlsing to publish this object, and can those determinants be replayed against the pinned expected identity?

If the answer requires trusting a deleted cache/carrier or reconstructing meaning from prose, lineage is insufficient.

## Invalidation

New identity/revalidation is required when a bound source release, question mapping, universe/time/scope, design/weight/estimand, search law, candidate ancestry, validation/model-selection rule, randomness/split, comparability authority, I04 admission rule, structural producer, publication policy, or interpretation-critical dependency changes.
