## 6. THE R5 CASE STUDY — WHAT CHETIRI SHOULD LEARN AND WHAT IT MUST NOT INFER

### 6.1 V1 failure

The first R5 LONG_SHARDED implementation used a verbose self-describing JSON object per physical event.

On real WVS7 it crossed the entire 4 GiB hard carrier budget after only about 5.33 million of 59.60 million physical cells.

That failure demonstrated:

`VERBOSE_LOGICAL_SERIALIZATION != INFORMATION_REQUIREMENT`

It did **not** demonstrate that LONG_SHARDED, C11 or exact event semantics were inherently too large.

### 6.2 Compact V2 repair

Compact V2 retained exact reconstructed canonical rows while physically omitting deterministic repetition such as reconstructable IDs/hashes/coordinates/constants.

Real WVS7 prefix validation returned approximately:

- 250k: `14.963 B/cell`;
- 1M: `14.400 B/cell`;
- 2M: `14.4385385 B/cell`;
- projected full WVS7 carrier: about `0.801 GiB`;
- 2M peak RSS: `141,787,136 bytes`;
- 2M build wall: `456.230361254 s`;
- 2M reopen/full-audit wall: `209.878318941 s`;
- relevant R5 suite: `68 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped`;
- logical/audit digests matched.

The lesson for Chetiri is two-sided:

1. Candidate A is much stronger than failed V1 and must be represented by current Compact V2 or its accepted successor in any fair bakeoff.
2. Compact V2 itself proves that much of what appears to be “event information” is actually derivable physical redundancy and need not persist as repeated bytes.

Forbidden inference:

`COMPACT_V2_IS_SMALL -> PERSISTENT_EVENT_STAGE_IS_ARCHITECTURALLY_NECESSARY`

Forbidden inverse inference:

`EVENT_ROWS_ARE_RECONSTRUCTABLE -> MATERIALIZATION_HAS_ZERO_OPERATIONAL_VALUE`

### 6.3 Parallel-12 work

Authority 65 explores bounded process-level compilation concurrency only.

Its result, whatever it is, may change build throughput for Candidate A.

It has no authority to prove or disprove the necessity of persistent materialization.

Chetiri should nevertheless use the newest accepted A implementation available at C09 so the bakeoff does not compare freshly optimized B/C against an obsolete A.

## 11. WHAT TRII EVIDENCE SHOULD MAKE CHETIRI ESPECIALLY SUSPICIOUS OF

### Suspicion A — treating “source-backed” as “no compiler state”

A direct-source architecture may still need enormous transient execution state.

That is lawful if bounded, exact, nonauthoritative and cleanly deletable.

Do not optimize for aesthetic purity.

### Suspicion B — treating “reconstructable” as “cheap enough to reconstruct repeatedly”

Logical removability and operational usefulness are distinct.

A regenerable Compact-V2-like cache could be justified even if no canonical event carrier is architecturally mandatory.

### Suspicion C — treating file bytes as self-interpreting

Source bytes require governed decoder semantics/config and often separate source authority.

Keep these jurisdictions separate.

### Suspicion D — proving positives while hand-waving negatives

Exact negative truth, completeness, routing/risk sets, all-missing declarations and unread/corrupt regions are where source-backed shortcuts are most likely to fail silently.

### Suspicion E — letting an accelerator become authority

If deleting Arrow/Parquet/DuckDB/bitmap/index state changes what C11/I03/I05 can truthfully discover rather than merely how quickly, the architecture has smuggled empirical authority into a cache.

### Suspicion F — benchmarking an obsolete Candidate A

Compact V2 materially changed the persistent-candidate terrain.
