## Governing reconciliation

Chetiri adopts the three-domain ontology for persistence analysis:

```text
THE PAST
  authoritative empirical evidence

THE LEARNED WORLD
  governed empirical-social learned products
  + governed structural learned products

THE LIFE
  V3-generated developmental history
```

This does not create a new numbered Rawlsing stage. It is a persistence/epistemic classification for already-required or newly recovered terminal capabilities.

The campaign must preserve the noncollapse:

```text
RAW SOURCE INCIDENCE
!= DISCOVERED CANDIDATE RELATION
!= VALIDATED EMPIRICAL SOCIAL RELATION
!= POSITIONED-SUBJECT EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY FIELD
!= STRUCTURALLY ADMITTED RELATION / PRIOR
!= V3 GENERATED ACTOR STATE
!= ACTOR BELIEF / RESPONSE
```

Chetiri's physical question is therefore broadened from:

> what must persist between source and the V3-facing structural substrate?

to:

> what authoritative evidence, learned-world empirical/structural products, serving surfaces, provenance and replay material must persist or remain lawfully regenerable across the complete Rawlsing lifecycle, and which physical empirical representations are actually necessary to produce or serve them?
