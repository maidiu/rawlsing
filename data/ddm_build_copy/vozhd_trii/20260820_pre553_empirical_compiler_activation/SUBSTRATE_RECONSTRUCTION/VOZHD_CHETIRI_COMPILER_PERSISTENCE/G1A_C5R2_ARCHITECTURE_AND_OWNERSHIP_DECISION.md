## 2. Why this is the smallest lawful architecture

Accepted P1/P2/P4/P7 and PRE553 law already establish the necessary boundaries:

- semantic claim identity is distinct from governing authority-instance identity and lifecycle/current-selection identity;
- semantic succession is typed;
- currentness is explicit;
- history is immutable;
- dependency effects are derivative;
- complete evidence/prerequisite binding is a prerequisite to authority-bearing execution;
- exact refs/replay must fail closed with no latest/default/fuzzy fallback;
- I04 may bind and consume semantic authority but does not own semantic truth;
- V553+ remains downstream and does not own semantic truth;
- reverse reachability may execute audit/revalidation consequences but may not infer lifecycle meaning;
- machine-readable/manual authority is not automatically machine-derivable.

Therefore no new numbered stage is required for the C5R2 proof. The missing capability is one producer-side semantic-maintenance owner plus a producer-side release executor operating over immutable append-only carriers and existing exact-ref/replay semantics.

The architecture does **not** appoint an owner from chronology, file location, code proximity, or convenience. It follows the already-banked ownership boundary: truth stays producer-side before I04/V553+.
