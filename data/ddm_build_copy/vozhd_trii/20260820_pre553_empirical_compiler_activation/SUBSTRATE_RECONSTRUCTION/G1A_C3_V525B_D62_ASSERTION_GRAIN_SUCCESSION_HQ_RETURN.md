## 3. Assertion-grain holdings

A physical d62 row is **not** an authority grain. A single physical row may carry multiple assertions of different kinds, and those assertions can have different succession dispositions. Classification is therefore per assertion, never per physical-row recency.

That mechanical precedence is classified:

`DEFECTIVE_SHADOW_FROM_IDENTITY_OR_CARDINALITY_COLLAPSE`

It is **not** a semantic succession rule.
