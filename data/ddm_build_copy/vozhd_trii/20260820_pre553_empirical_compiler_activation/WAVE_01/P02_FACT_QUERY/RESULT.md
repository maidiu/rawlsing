## Hostile representability

- **XOR:** represented by exact `AND/OR/NOT` respondent predicate algebra or a full joint target. Zero unary signal does not make the conjunction undiscoverable or unqueryable.
- **k-way parity:** requires exact k-way incidence/configuration topology; lower-order marginals are insufficient in general.
- **Simpson/reversal:** pooled and stratifier-conditioned queries remain distinct fact identities with distinct condition/denominator provenance. The reversal comparison is a derived diagnostic over exact facts.
- **same marginals / different joint:** yields different joint fact content and identity.
- **routed zero overlap:** is a typed `ZERO_OVERLAP`, not an omitted row, fabricated probability, or nearest-cohort repair.
