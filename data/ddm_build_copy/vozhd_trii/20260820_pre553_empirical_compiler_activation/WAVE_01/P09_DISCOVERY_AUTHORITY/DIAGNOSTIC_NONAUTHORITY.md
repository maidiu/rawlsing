## Holding

A diagnostic may help order, propose, screen, summarize, or explain a search. It does **not** become a canonical empirical fact or canonical discovery result because its score is large, small, significant-looking, stable-looking, or historically familiar.

The canonical empirical substrate is the D08 typed respondent-conditional/joint fact algebra: exact scope, condition predicate, target topology, risk set/routing law, measure basis, support, typed absence, provenance, and replay identity. Scalar or lossy summaries are derived from that richer object and are generally non-invertible.

## Diagnostic-only families

The following are diagnostic unless a later authority gives them a narrower explicit role:

- mutual information / NMI;
- Cramer's V;
- entropy reduction / information gain;
- lift and difference-from-baseline;
- heuristic confidence or salience scores;
- modal joint-cell summaries;
- top-k, beam, greedy, primary-star, or prevalence rankings;
- current/historical `shape_class` labels;
- cloud association strength;
- proposal priority scores.

Numerical equality does not establish empirical-fact identity. Different condition predicates, risk sets, source universes, topology laws, or measure bases remain different facts even if they yield the same number.

## Why this is binding for P09

D01 shows that a strong lawful pair can be missed by a primary-star search and that XOR can have zero unary signal. D02 shows that greedy/top-two recursion can omit minority categories and alternative branch paths. D03 shows that current cloud/signature artifacts preserve only slices of branch-conditioned or higher-order information. D04 shows concrete processing-order, replay, topology, and score defects in the current cloud implementation. These are implementation/genealogy facts, not authority for their diagnostics to become canonical truth.

D08 resolves the algebraic status: association scores are E4 derived functionals; the full governed contingency/incidence facts are the empirical basis. A score cannot reconstruct the source fact and cannot silently substitute for it.

## Permitted diagnostic uses

A DiscoveryAuthority may predeclare a diagnostic for:

1. proposal ordering;
2. adaptive traversal priority;
3. beam membership;
4. exploratory screen;
5. explanatory annotation;
6. deterministic winner ordering among **already exact-validated** candidates.

Such use must pin the formula/version, topology, measure basis, tie rule, and replay identity. Changing the diagnostic may change the adaptive trajectory, so it is a replay input even though it is not semantic authority.

## Forbidden promotions

The following are forbidden:

- `high score -> canonical empirical fact`;
- `low score -> relationship absent`;
- `not in top-k -> negative evidence`;
- `unary score == 0 -> do not test authorized interaction` while claiming exact interaction coverage;
- `modal joint cell -> full cotravel fact`;
- `association score -> branch-conditioned distribution`;
- `diagnostic significance -> structural interpretation`;
- `population diagnostic -> generated actor position, belief, knowledge, response, or persona`.

## Adaptive-search consequence

In `ADAPTIVE_EXPLORATORY`, a diagnostic may cause a candidate to be **PRUNED**, but the receipt must say so. `PRUNED_HEURISTIC` and `UNEVALUATED` are not negative evidence. A screen may support a completeness claim only when a separately accepted no-false-negative theorem applies to that exact discovery class and exact input semantics.

Unary-signal screening has no generic safe theorem for XOR. Therefore it cannot underwrite a general interaction-completeness claim.

## Exact-bounded consequence

In `EXACT_BOUNDED`, diagnostics may change execution order or enable mathematically equivalent batched computation, but they may not remove a candidate from the declared frontier while preserving `COMPLETE_FOR_EXACT_DECLARED_FRONTIER`, except under an authority-declared exact feasibility rule or separately accepted safe-equivalence theorem sufficient for that candidate's terminal disposition.

## Canonical-result rule

A discovery survives canonically only as a `DiscoveryResultReceipt` that binds:

- exact DiscoveryAuthority;
- exact candidate identity;
- exact underlying EmpiricalFact/E1/E2 query evidence;
- exact typed validation/result state;
- support/uncertainty/limit state;
- exact coverage mode and receipt;
- exact replay provenance.

Diagnostics may be attached. They do not own the result.
