# O09/O10 Execution-Economics Decision

**Decision:** begin with Vozhd only; do not manufacture rabochii packs yet.

## O10

O10 is a compact, tightly coupled grammar/release problem: 14 S14 candidate-generation rules + 16 S15 derived structural-information definitions in the R10C contract. Splitting 30 interdependent objects would cost more in pack construction, divergent interpretation, reconciliation and correction than a central hostile pass.

## O09

The raw P5-facing footprint is large, but it is not the true adjudication denominator. R10G's 3,274 structural S08 rows collapse through a much smaller driver/facet/analytical grammar. Therefore:

1. freeze a central P5 placement doctrine;
2. group/compress by canonical structural meaning + actor/target + position/context modifiers;
3. propagate only governed rules;
4. create a hostile exception queue;
5. measure **independent exception decisions**, not raw rows;
6. finish centrally if the queue is modest;
7. use only 2–3 workers if the residual is genuinely large enough to beat pack + synthesis overhead.

If workers are needed, shard exception classes under a frozen common doctrine rather than asking workers to invent P5 independently by source.

## Parallel work

O09 and O10 can proceed independently enough for one central execution session to alternate between them. O11 machine-contract design may also proceed in parallel, but O11 computation cannot be frozen before O09/O10 converge.

## Why this preserves quality

Parallelism is used only where the adjudication denominator is large and genuinely separable. Tightly coupled architecture remains central. Worker bandwidth is reserved for Job 3, where O12 will produce a finite relation denominator intentionally designed for parallel adjudication.
