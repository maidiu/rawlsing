Rawlsing Project Identity / Architecture Briefing
Current version: 2026-06-26

Purpose of this document
This document is the stable “what the project is” handoff. It explains Rawlsing as an architecture, not just a codebase or data pipeline. It is intended for a new ChatGPT session, Codex manager agent, reviewer agent, or human collaborator who needs to understand the thing being built before touching implementation.

The companion document, RAWLSING_CURRENT_STATE_AND_NEXT_HANDOFF_20260626.txt, explains where the repo is right now and what the next concrete task is.


1. What Rawlsing is

Rawlsing is a layered system for turning large-scale survey evidence into historically and socially grounded runtime person-generation material for an LLM chat app.

It is not a flat personality generator.

It is not a “survey says this person is X” pipeline.

It is an anti-fabrication architecture: survey evidence constrains structural priors, structural priors constrain temporal pressure, temporal pressure constrains seed affordances, seed affordances constrain mediation slots, mediation slots constrain pressure-flow candidates, pressure-flow candidates constrain renderer contracts, and only then may runtime vividness appear.

The governing maxim:

  Never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority.

The long-term goal is to generate an LLM-chattable person who feels psychologically alive while preserving strict separation between:

  - empirical survey evidence
  - structural interpretation
  - temporal pressure
  - family/household/carrier mediation
  - life-course pressure-flow
  - generated psyche
  - chat/runtime surface

Raw survey data is not persona output.
Structural priors are not autobiography.
Reviewable evidence is not certified evidence.
Thin contracts are not finished runtime products.


2. Core architecture in one sentence

Rawlsing turns cross-national survey data into evidence-constrained simulations of socialized persons by passing through authority-controlled layers, each of which knows what it may say, what it may not say, and what later layers must still mediate.


3. The major layer stack

The active DDM/V5 stack is roughly:

V550
  Empirical survey substrate, bridge/index material, single-wave source organization.

V550J
  Same-respondent joint-response morphology and association surfaces. It can preserve joint patterns and review surfaces, but must not treat association as causality or sparse co-occurrence as meaning.

V551
  Certified single-question relation evidence atoms. This is a row-level source of relation_axis / target_entity / driver_facet structural evidence.

V551J
  Joint/conditional evidence packet compiler. It may preserve joint/conditional evidence, certification status, reviewable sidecars, and joint context. It must not produce runtime-safe packets, actor psyche, parent biography, household story, carrier ideology, or Enneagram.

V552
  Structural pressure/status compiler. It produces row-level structural facts and pressure surfaces such as relation axis pressure, target entity pressure, driver facet pressure, structural channels, contradiction surfaces, and horizon constraints.

V552J
  Structural-dimension gateway / V553 handoff adapter. Its job is to legally carry V552 row-level structural facts forward into V553-facing handoff rows, while preserving permission/readiness boundaries. It is not a runtime layer.

V553
  Temporal structural pressure/probe compiler. Its job is to temporalize V552J handoff rows across explicit waves, build stable temporal keys, observations, deltas, gaps/absences, and V554 handoff contracts. At the present moment it is still probe-only, not authorized true temporal pressure.

V554
  Seed-affordance contract layer. Not implemented as completion-grade. Must consume V553 handoff only after V553 authority is clean.

V555
  Mediation layer: parent/co-parent/child/household/carrier mediation. Not implemented.

V556
  Pressure-flow candidate layer. Enneagram-like attractors may eventually emerge here from life-course pressure-flow history, but must not be assigned directly from survey data.

V557
  Runtime rendering contract. Must prevent prompt-shape leakage and forbid raw DDM surfaces from being treated as personhood.

V558+
  App-facing evaluation, audit, memory, narrative governance, and runtime safety.


4. Stable doctrine

The project’s core engineering doctrine is:

  Every row must know what kind of thing it is.
  Every row must know who authorized it.
  Every row must know what it may be used for.
  Every row must know what it may not be used for.
  Unknowns fail closed.
  Fallback never silently becomes authority.
  Diagnostic material must not enter core meaning surfaces.
  Candidate/review/certified/diagnostic/blocked surfaces must stay separate.
  Reviewable is not certified.
  Certified evidence is not runtime-safe.
  Structural pressure is not individual psyche.
  Association is not causality.
  Directionality must not be invented.
  Runtime consumes handoff contracts, not raw upstream tables.
  Every layer needs validators, forbidden-inference checks, readiness reports, and adversarial tests.
  The system must explain why it stopped.

6. The runtime/person ontology

Rawlsing’s target person model is not a trait bag.

A person emerges through:

  survey-derived structural evidence
  historical/cultural pressure
  carrier/institutional mediation
  parent/co-parent/household mediation
  family memory and assimilation/exclusion patterns
  orientation-to-strata over time
  expectation baselines
  striving / shame / pride / debt / resentment
  wound / compensation / vitality / contradiction
  pressure-flow routing
  eventual surface psyche and chat persona

The key missing runtime object is:

  orientation-to-strata over time

A person does not simply “have” a social stratum. Each actor has a shifting relation to each stratum:

  identity-with
  alienation-from
  lens-capture
  object-visibility
  felt legitimacy
  felt threat
  felt protection
  shame
  pride
  debt
  gratitude
  resentment
  aspiration
  repulsion
  fidelity
  contempt
  compassion
  predatory distance
  transformative energy
  comic/aesthetic/spiritual distance
  self-deception
  naivety
  contradiction load

The crucial distinction:

  Identification -> stratum becomes lens.
  Alienation -> stratum becomes object.

A stratum internalized as “common sense” may disappear from view.
A stratum alienated from may become visible, hated, mourned, mocked, analyzed, fled, exploited, or transformed.


7. Carrier mediation

Carrier mediation is essential.

Institutions such as churches, schools, workplaces, ethnic enclaves, family businesses, neighborhoods, media ecosystems, militaries, parties, and professional worlds do not merely “add culture.” They translate structural strata into lived local practice.

Example:
A rural non-denominational Protestant church may explicitly condemn secular government, worldly elites, liberal culture, and bureaucracy while implicitly reproducing American chosenness, settler-national providence, property hierarchy, racial innocence, patriarchal family order, and capitalist moral order.

A child does not simply copy the institution. Ethnicity, phenotype, language, immigrant generation, citizenship, family memory, exclusion, and assimilation pressure change the absorption profile.

Future runtime needs:

  StratumToStratumRelation
  CarrierRelationToStrata
  ActorCarrierAbsorptionProfile
  ActorStratumOrientationMatrix
  OrientationTransmission
  HouseholdOrientationConflict

But DDM evidence layers must not instantiate generated household/actor psyche directly.


8. Enneagram / pressure-flow position

Enneagram must not enter DDM certified evidence layers.

Enneagram must not be assigned by survey data, country, parent prior, or birth.

If used, Enneagram-like attractors should emerge later from life-course pressure-flow history:

  orientation-to-strata patterns
  expectation baselines
  striving patterns
  blocked needs
  caregiver channels
  vitality/fatigue routing
  compensation
  wound/metabolization
  crisis and rupture

DDM/V5 constrains the world.
v3 generates lived internalization and pressure-flow.
Enneagram-like attractors may emerge from pressure-flow history.

Do not implement Enneagram in V551J, V552J, or V553.

Rawlsing now has a serious emerging DDM evidence/structure pipeline and a promising runtime substrate. The old immediate focus from earlier handoff documents was V551J cleanup. The present focus has moved forward to getting the V552 -> V552J -> V553 bridge into a clean, protocol-governed state.

The current active work is around:

  - V552 row-level structural fact substrate
  - V552J map-bearing handoff to V553
  - V553 explicit three-wave temporal probe construction
  - agent-task protocol: contract -> builder -> proof payload -> checker -> reviewer -> next task

The current explicit waves are:

  1972
  1993
  2002

The present V553 state is probe-only. That is correct. It must not be upgraded to true temporal-field authority or V554 seed readiness until comparability/authority hygiene is genuinely in place.

One-sentence present architecture status:

  Rawlsing has crossed from “V553 cannot see the map” to “V553 can see the map, but the remaining work is lane discipline, diagnostic sidecar hygiene, and probe-authority cleanup before downstream V554 can safely begin.”
