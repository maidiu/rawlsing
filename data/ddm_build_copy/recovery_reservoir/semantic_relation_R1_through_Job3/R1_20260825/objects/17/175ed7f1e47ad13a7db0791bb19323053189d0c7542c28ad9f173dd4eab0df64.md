# R8K — Democratic Regime Orientation and Country Suitability Semantic Closure

R8K performs a source-first closure of two clean democratic-governance constructs without collapsing the broader 268-row democracy terrain into a single facet.

## Closed semantic cores

- **23 certified scalar implementations**: `democratic_regime_normative_orientation` — evaluations of a democratic political system, democracy as best/better, importance of democracy, or desired democratic regime degree. Proposition subtype is preserved, including the WVS Western-democracy modifier.
- **8 certified scalar implementations**: `democratic_regime_country_suitability` — judgments of whether democracy is suitable/appropriate for the respondent country.
- **15 related objects** remain explicit boundaries because they are forced regime choices, democracy-versus-development/problem-solving tradeoffs, or generic non-democratic tolerance/superiority objects. They are not converted into scalar democratic orientation.

The larger **268-row democracy text terrain is not claimed closed**. A planning-only partition is included so later releases can take satisfaction/performance, perceived democraticness, democratic content/pillars, performance critiques, compatibility/promotion, and residual objects as separate source-semantic classes.

## Legacy corrections

The historical QSM is retained only as recall evidence. R8K rejects several unsupported readings where the source item does not license them: generic democracy-best or democratic-system evaluations are not `democracy_content_matrix`; Latinobarómetro's generic democracy-best item is not elite capture or a security tradeoff; and democratic preference/suitability is not by itself a `developmental_authoritarian_bargain`. Where the old mapping's narrow axis insight remains useful (for example `democracy_suitability`), that lineage is preserved without promoting the old facet.
