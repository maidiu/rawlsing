# QA and Completeness Doctrine After the O12 Failure

## Governing principle

**Never let a mechanically green system substitute for proof that the system asked the right question.**

## Mandatory pre-completion gates

Before claiming PASS/FROZEN/DONE/COMPLETE or dispatching downstream workers:

1. **Contract check:** enumerate the exact upstream fields/relations the downstream algorithm assumes.
2. **Jurisdiction check:** prove each required field is actually governed in that jurisdiction; "present somewhere in the registry" is not enough.
3. **Independent expected-universe reconstruction:** the completeness validator may not simply read the generator's examined-scope ledger.
4. **Known-positive recall:** every governing R6 positive must be recovered or explicitly explained by a current supersession/block.
5. **Negative-space audit:** inspect what was not generated and why.
6. **Aggregate-shape audit:** extreme histograms and zeros require causal explanation, not reassurance.
7. **Shared-assumption audit:** identify whether generator and validator depend on the same proxy, schema omission, or inherited denominator.
8. **Cross-object audit:** source-native object closure may not stand in for cross-object relation discovery.
9. **Missingness semantics:** distinguish `NOT_PRESENT_AFTER_COMPLETE_SEARCH` from `INPUT_COVERAGE_INCOMPLETE`, `SOURCE_BLOCKED`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
10. **Cold replay + deterministic rebuild + Library roundtrip** only after semantic/completeness gates pass.

## User-trust rule

The user is not an external QA operator. Vozhd must proactively interrogate weird distributions, suspicious zeros, brittle closure arguments, and validator dependence before presenting a completion claim.
