# Composted predecessor diagnosis — design lessons only

The following lessons are retained; predecessor verdicts are not.

1. **R15 denominator distortion.** 92% of R15 atoms were T06, so its headline rejection rate was mostly a property of one high-recall Cartesian construction, not the health of the S14 graph.
2. **R15 authority regression.** Workers re-adjudicated RR_NORM keys already governed by C3 while having less source authority. Settled premises must be inherited, not rediscovered under a weaker ceiling.
3. **REJECT/BLOCK collapse.** Missing evidence was inconsistently converted into negative relation claims. R16D makes `PROVEN_NEGATIVE` require an explicit incompatible prerequisite; otherwise the result is `BLOCKED`.
4. **C4 branch truncation.** C4 was a coherent realized-endpoint branch, but not the whole of T06. It could not independently rediscover the sealed EVS 1999 norm↔willingness canaries because stated/intended conduct was outside its open denominator.
5. **Canaries must test discovery, not merely decorate packs.** Every assigned known R6 relation must be rediscovered from current inputs or explicitly retained with exact active members.
6. **Source-wave completeness beats guessed-pair completeness.** The new denominator is exhaustive source-wave semantic-role + source-object accounting, followed by deterministic role-compatible candidate compilation. Arbitrary pair subsets are not the denominator.
7. **Empirical co-travel is orthogonal.** No S14 negative may erase V550/V550J joint-response or demographic-conditioned attitude-cloud evidence.
