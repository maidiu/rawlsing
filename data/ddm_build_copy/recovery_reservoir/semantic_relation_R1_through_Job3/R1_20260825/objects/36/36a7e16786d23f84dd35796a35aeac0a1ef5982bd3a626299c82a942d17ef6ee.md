# Five Jobs → V5 Crosswalk

The five jobs are not replacement V-numbers. They are the authority-construction program required before the V5 machinery can safely run.

| Job | Governing question | Current authority surfaces | Downstream consumer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What exactly is every question? | native source, P2 exact nodes, continuity, response/routing authority | V550 |
| 2 | What structural information can it carry? | S08 semantic identity, S2 topology, S2B behavior/exposure boundaries | V550/V551/P5 prep |
| 3 | Which governed questions may be interpreted together, under what typed relation? | structural-information requirements, relation templates, R6/P3/P3C, future finite Job-3 graph | V550J/V551J |
| 4 | Which relations actually coexist for a respondent? | routing/universe/composability, respondent semantics, same-respondent configurations | V550J/V551J/configuration indicators |
| 5 | What structural pressure through time can those configurations support? | P4/P5/S16, V552/V552J/V553 and later | V554–V558/runtime |

The ordering is epistemic, not merely procedural. A later layer cannot repair an upstream meaning loss by inference.
