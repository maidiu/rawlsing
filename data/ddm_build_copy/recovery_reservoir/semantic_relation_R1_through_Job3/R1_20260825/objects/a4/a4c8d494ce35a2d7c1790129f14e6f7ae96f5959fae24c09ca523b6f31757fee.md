# R/T/C vs V550+ — runtime/construction Rosetta Stone

## The two layers

**R/T/C/O/S names are mostly construction and governance machinery.** They are used to build, audit, repair, and freeze the semantic/relational authority that the actual V550+ data pipeline consumes. They are not themselves the person-generation runtime.

**V550+ is the empirical/runtime-facing evidence pipeline.** Actual respondent microdata flows through V550/V550J and later governed surfaces.

### Construction side
- `S14`: typed inter-question / inter-object relation authority.
- `T03–T11`: specific relation species (principle↔application, goal↔means, commitment↔cost, norm↔conduct, hierarchy, institution class, reciprocal roles, matrices, complementary roles).
- `R11/R13/R14/R15/...`: temporary repair/reconstruction/workload campaigns used to find and adjudicate S14 opportunities. These are scaffolding, not Rawlsing organs.
- `C3/C4`: subcampaigns inside the current T06 repair. C3 governs RR_NORM eligibility; C4 adjudicates exact downstream norm↔conduct actor/target/action opportunities.

### Empirical pipeline side
- `V550`: governed empirical substrate, distributions, position-conditioned determinicity, outlier/recursive branches, co-signatures/attitude-cloud surfaces.
- `V550J`: same-respondent joint morphology / configurations.
- `V551`: certified structural/relation evidence atoms combining empirical observations with governed meaning/relations.
- `V551J`: joint/conditional evidence packets.
- `V552`: within-wave structural priors / pressure potential.
- `V553`: historical/temporal pressure field.
- `V554–V558`: bounded generative affordance → mediated life-course → pressure flow → phenomenological contract → runtime readiness.

## Critical non-substitution law
S14 typed relation authority **must enrich but never erase** valid empirical co-travel. A pair may lack a special `T06` relation and still be highly informative as an empirical co-signature inside a demographic/structural branch. Conversely, empirical co-travel may not be silently promoted into semantic causality or a typed S14 relation. Both authorities survive with separate provenance.
