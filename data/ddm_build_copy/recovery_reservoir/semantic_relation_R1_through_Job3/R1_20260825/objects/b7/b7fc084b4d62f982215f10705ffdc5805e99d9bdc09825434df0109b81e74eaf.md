# R8P — Moral Permissibility Target Semantic Closure

R8P performs a fresh 18,276-row recall for source objects whose text, response contract, or current P2 semantic subrole invokes justification/justifiability. The resulting **166-row recall universe** is terminally classified rather than equated with one moral construct.

## Source-first result

- **156 rows** are genuine target-specific moral-permissibility / justifiability judgments.
- They cover **47 normalized targets** while preserving every exact source question.
- **152 / 156** have currently certified scalar execution.
- Four semantic cores remain execution-blocked: Afrobarometer R9 `Q52A` because its current P2 use lane is context, and Latinobarómetro 2024 `Q7NOIJ–Q9NOIJ` because the printed question anchors and canonical response labels are reversed.
- **4 rows** are non-scalar violence/terrorism justification choice topology.
- **3 rows** are the already-governed R8O public-health democratic-exception objects; justification wording does not change their semantic owner.
- **3 rows** are a real P2/legacy contamination: environmental tax willingness had been tagged as moral justifiability. R8P repairs those rows to `environmentalRelation / environmental_tax_sacrifice`.

The 156 core rows touch 80 R7G units. Four were already current-standard R7P anchors; 76 were still open. The environmental repair closes one additional R7G unit. Therefore R8P closes **77 new relational semantic units**, moving Job 2 from **706 to 783 / 3,331**, leaving **2,548**.

## What this does not authorize

R8P does not create a single morality score. A respondent's view of bribery, abortion, divorce, euthanasia, terrorism, tax cheating, violence, sex, environmental sacrifice, or the death penalty remains target-specific evidence. Cross-target selectivity or consistency is a later typed-relation problem, not something inferred merely from sharing a response scale.

No live installation and no Job-3 relation authority are created.
