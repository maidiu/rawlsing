## Important ontology correction

The July-30 candidate facet `foreign_people_affinity` is too strong as a universal label. Latinobarómetro asks confidence in Argentinians, Chileans, Brazilians, etc. across a multi-country wave; a target can therefore be an in-group in one respondent country and an out-group in another. Arab Barometer likewise contains named national populations whose relation to the respondent cannot be fixed from the wave-level question identity alone.

R8E therefore proposes **`named_national_population_affinity`**. It preserves the target population and requires respondent-country binding before deriving in-group/out-group status. This is a semantic refinement, not a live ontology install.

## Boundary control

R8E explicitly keeps separate: confidence in the European Union as an institution; favorability toward the EU as a geopolitical bloc; bilateral relations with the EU; judgments of whether Turkey/Saudi Arabia are democratic; positive evaluation of Western culture; Chinese-vs-American policy comparisons; and national-conformity claims. People are not states, institutions, policies, or cultural models.

## Relation affordance

Several American-people items explicitly say that US foreign policy may be bad while ordinary Americans are good people. The class preserves that **people-affinity ↔ state-policy dissociation** as later relation-ready structural information rather than flattening it into generic foreign favorability.
