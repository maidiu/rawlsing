# APP-GATE A — Conditional attitude-cloud and inter-strata pressure preservation

## Why this is a release gate
The central Rawlsing use of survey microdata is not merely to attach isolated attitudes to demographic categories. It is to discover **how attitudes, perspectives, expectations and behaviors travel together differently across historically situated populations**, including majority configurations, minority configurations, exceptions, recursive subgroup splits, and cross-cutting alignments. Those empirical fields become part of the pressure relation between a generated person's configuration and the multiple strata in which that person is partially situated.

A configuration such as:

- government should provide X;
- respondent does not vote;
- respondent distrusts government;
- respondent answers collectivistically on another item;

may be anthropologically and structurally informative even where no special pairwise S14 relation exists between every pair. The information resides in the **joint empirical configuration and its population conditioning**.

## Non-negotiable preservation law
A valid V550/V550J empirical association or co-signature must **not be deleted merely because S14 cannot assign a special typed semantic relation to the same pair**.

Likewise, S14 typed relations may annotate/explain internal topology but may not manufacture empirical co-travel.

## Required 35-wave audit before Rawlsing can be called pipeline-ready
Reconstruct and test, across all 35 active waves / 9 survey families, whether the current governed implementation preserves the intended path:

1. response semantics and substantive-answer validity;
2. position-conditioned answer distributions;
3. majority response / baseline field;
4. `conforms`, `deviates`, `minority_converges`, `split`, and `underpowered` branch classification where applicable;
5. secondary splitters and recursive branches;
6. response-response co-signatures / attitude clouds within conditioned branches;
7. same-respondent joint morphology in V550J;
8. preservation of cloud/component identities, denominators, weights, confidence and provenance into V551/V551J;
9. preservation into V552 of aggregate pressure backdrops, person-position conditioning, contradiction, alignment/divergence, and explicit uncertainty without collapsing aggregate into interiority;
10. handoff into V553 such that temporal pressure can preserve persistence, intensification, divergence, minority resonance, rupture and non-comparability.

## Person↔stratum success criterion
For a generated person's materially relevant strata, the governed outputs must be capable of representing:

- stratum/population modal attitude configuration;
- relevant demographic/structural branch shift;
- recursively conditioned subgroup structure;
- supported minority configurations and exceptions;
- the person's own sampled/conditioned configuration;
- person↔stratum alignment, divergence, minority resonance and contradiction;
- cross-stratum reinforcement/opposition on particular components;
- temporal movement and confidence.

This must remain probabilistic/field-like. Demographic variables condition the empirical field but are not attitude nodes and do not become personality essence.

## Failure conditions
RED app-level failure includes any of the following without a governed explanation:

- major waves produce empty or near-empty attitude-cloud/branch surfaces despite adequate microdata;
- recursive subgroup/outlier information is silently flattened into modal stereotypes;
- V551/V551J drops empirically supported co-travel merely because no special S14 relation exists;
- V552 cannot retain person↔stratum divergence/minority resonance;
- demographic positions become direct attitude/personality assignments;
- downstream pressure fields are populated by fabricated fill rather than governed empirical structure.

## Placement in itinerary
This is an **additional app-level acceptance gate, not a renumbering of the governing 25-item repair stack**. Execute it after the relation authority is repaired/frozen enough to test the bridge honestly and before any claim that the respondent-composability / V552→V553→runtime path is production-ready. It should be attached to the nine-family downstream-canary phase and remain binding through Job-4/Job-5 and V553–V558 ascent.
