# Current S14 relation adjudication doctrine — common overlay for R15 W01–W06

This overlay supersedes **procedural/doctrine shorthand only** in the original R15 README/return-contract text. It does **not** change pack assignment, membership, evidence bytes, raw-origin IDs, rule release, relation-role ontology, or authority ceilings.

## Universal authority law

Exact source-governed semantics control. Shared driver/facet/domain, lexical overlap, embeddings, proximity, source co-membership, association, and thematic resemblance may help recall or organize review but **never authorize a relation**.

A negative decision is scoped to the exact pre-enumerated opportunity. Missing authority is `WORKER_BLOCKED_NEEDS_VOZHD`, not `REJECT`. Never turn failure to prove a relation into a global absence claim.

## T03 — principle ↔ application

Require an explicit general principle and a concrete application of that same principle. Common topic, political valence, driver/facet, or generic normativity is insufficient.

## T04 — goal ↔ means

Require an explicit goal/end and a means/mechanism semantically tied to that same end. Mere usefulness, correlation, or plausible strategy is insufficient.

## T05 — commitment ↔ cost-bearing application

Require an explicit commitment and an explicit personal/cost-bearing application of that same commitment. Do not infer cost from structural pressure or general sacrifice language without the source linkage.

## T06 — norm/approval ↔ expected/stated conduct or accepted behavior/exposure

T06 is now a mandatory **two-stage** adjudication.

### Stage A: RR_NORM classification

Decision identity is `(source_family, wave, semantic_unit_id)`, never semantic ID alone.

`POSITIVE_RR_NORM` requires source-governed semantics functioning as a prescription, obligation, permission/prohibition, rejection/support, policy recommendation, religious/legal norm, preference/criterion, or approval/disapproval of governance, conduct, a role, policy, rule, or social/political arrangement.

`NEGATIVE_RR_NORM` includes descriptive state/capacity/performance, causal belief, experience, factual belief, trust/confidence, expectation, personal salience/importance, self-reported commitment/cost, affiliation, or current-state description where the source does not prescribe/permit/approve/reject conduct, rule, role, policy, or arrangement.

Agreement/endorsement response format is terrain only. Broad R10G semantic-act role is evidence only. Modal wording is not automatically normative. Generic liking/favorability/affect, perceived fairness, sympathy, or outcome valence is not automatically RR_NORM. If the source does not settle the boundary, **BLOCK**.

For every unique T06 semantic key, complete your supplied `WORKER_HELPERS/<worker>_T06_RR_NORM_LEDGER_TEMPLATE.csv` once. You may propagate a source-governed **negative RR_NORM** disposition across that exact `(source_family,wave,semantic_unit_id)` key's T06 opportunities, because the norm-side prerequisite is impossible. This still yields exact-opportunity rejections only, not broader relation absence.

### Stage B: actor–target–action link

Only `POSITIVE_RR_NORM` reaches Stage B. Establish from exact source semantics:
- norm actor / role class;
- norm action, policy, conduct, permission, prohibition, or role occupancy;
- target/object/recipient;
- conditions/qualifiers;
- directionality where relevant.

Compare against the exact conduct/endpoint actor, target, and action/exposure. A relation requires exact or source-governed bounded correspondence. Same politics/health/climate/religion/etc. topic is never enough. Actor mismatch, target mismatch, action mismatch, or material qualifier mismatch defeats the exact T06 relation.

Positive RR_NORM is **eligibility only**; it never emits T06 relation truth by itself.

### T06 source insufficiency

If the pack does not preserve enough native stem/polarity/actor-target-action meaning to decide Stage A or B, BLOCK and identify the missing authority. Do not browse the web or reconstruct source meaning externally in a worker lane; source recovery belongs to Vozhd.

## T07 — reserved to Vozhd

T07 is not worker adjudication scope. **Every T07 row must still be returned exactly once** as `WORKER_BLOCKED_NEEDS_VOZHD` with blocker reason `T07_RESERVED_CENTRAL`. Do not skip or repair it.

## T08 — institution-class differentiation

Governed institution target/class mapping is a prerequisite. Class co-presence is not relation truth. Do not invent classes or repair mapping authority.

## T09 — reciprocal direction

Require explicit source-semantic actor/recipient direction or role reversal. Thematic symmetry, opposite sentiment, or parallel wording is insufficient.

## T10 — target × condition matrix

Require genuine crossed target and condition dimensions with governed cell identity. Repeated lists, batteries, or parallel question blocks are insufficient without both crossed dimensions.

## T11 — complementary role convergence

Require distinct authorized semantic roles that converge on one narrow common proposition. Broad shared target/topic, shared wording, or role diversity alone is insufficient.

## Composite T09;T10 atoms

Assess both hypotheses explicitly. `proposed_relation_objects_json` may contain one or both relation types only if each independently satisfies its rule. Do not treat failure of one as evidence for the other.
