# R14 — Worker Economics and Immutable Execution Partition

## Verdict

**#17 is DONE. Worker sharding is economically justified, but only after separating governance/binding work from independent semantic adjudication.**

R13 contains **2,599** deduped review containers and **120,970** rule-specific decision atoms. R14 classifies every R13 container exactly once:

- **2,433 containers / 85,892 atoms** → worker-eligible semantic adjudication;
- **166 containers / 35,078 atoms** → Vozhd central.

The 166 central containers are not held centrally because they are intellectually interesting; they are held centrally because they own prerequisites workers are forbidden to invent or repair: **55 pair-authority blockers, 32 T06 norm-role authority blockers, and 79 T07 hierarchy-concept implementation bindings.**

## Why six workers

The assignment treats each source-wave group as indivisible, preventing the same wave-specific source context from being independently interpreted by multiple workers. A planning-only effort metric combines container count, rule multiplicity, implementation count and within-container decision-atom breadth. It is explicitly **not semantic authority and not a wall-clock estimate**.

Sensitivity over 4–8 workers shows a clear knee. Five workers leave a maximum planning load of 4230.7; six reduce that to 3515.0; seven reduce it further to 3137.3, while adding another full pack/return/reconciliation channel. Eight cannot improve the maximum at all because the largest source-wave group becomes the indivisible bottleneck. Six therefore captures the useful parallelism without reflexively maximizing worker count.

The six selected shards have planning loads from **3475.7 to 3515.0** (max/min ratio **1.011**). No source-wave group is split.

## Important scope law

The **120,970 atoms are not 120,970 human jobs**. R13 compression/dedupe defines the human work grain. Conversely, a large packet cannot be treated as one automatic decision: worker returns must preserve rule-specific atom ancestry and may recommend only within the packet's frozen membership.

No worker may repair pair authority, assign missing norm-role authority, modify T07 hierarchy concepts, invent relation-role ontology, change evidence ceilings, or make completeness/Job-3 claims.
