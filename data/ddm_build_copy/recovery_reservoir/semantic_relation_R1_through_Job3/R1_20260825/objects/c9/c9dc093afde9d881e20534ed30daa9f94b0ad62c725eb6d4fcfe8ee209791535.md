# C3b10 doctrine — democraticness assessment only

Govern negative `RR_NORM` only for the five exact measured-predicate families that ask **how democratic a polity is** (current, past, expected future, or named/target country). These are descriptive/evaluative assessments of a political system, not prescriptions for respondent/government conduct.

This does **not** authorize negative RR_NORM for democracy suitability, superiority, preference, content/essentiality, institutional design, rights, reforms, or any other broader democracy terrain.

Decision identity remains `(source_family, wave, semantic_unit_id)`. Semantic-unit ID alone is forbidden as a decision key.
