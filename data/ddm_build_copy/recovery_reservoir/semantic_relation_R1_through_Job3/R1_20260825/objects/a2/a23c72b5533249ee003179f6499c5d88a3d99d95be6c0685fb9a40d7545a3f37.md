# R8G — Social Concern / Helping Structure Semantic Closure

R8G source-adjudicates **58 certified EVS implementations** as four distinct relation-ready semantic classes: **30 target-specific concern rows**, **5 target-specific willingness-to-help rows**, **22 helping-motivation rows**, and **1 voluntary-service moral-obligation row**.

The key structural move is not to invent relations yet, but to encode the dimensions that later relations require. Concern and willingness are predicates; family, neighborhood, region, fellow countrymen, Europeans, humankind, elderly people, unemployed people, immigrants, sick/disabled people, and poor children are targets. In the elderly/immigrant helping battery, moral duty, sympathy, societal interest, self-interest, and reciprocity/return are motives. Those dimensions are kept separate.

This immediately exposes lawful future questions: Does concern translate into willingness to help for the same target? Does a respondent endorse moral duty toward elderly people but self-interest toward immigrants? How broad is concern across social targets? Those are **semantic affordances only in R8G**. R7J's Job-3 relation-template/candidate-generation authority still owns whether and how such relations become accepted relation objects, and respondent composability remains a later gate.

R8G also updates the exact relational semantic progress register. Against the conservative **3,331-unit** R7G relational denominator, **562 units are now closed under the current source-first standard**, leaving **2,769**. The separate 2,525 structural-choice universe remains separate and is not hidden inside this count.
