# C3b5 ancestry — actual trust/confidence measures

C3a deliberately did not use broad source-bounded semantic subroles as automatic exclusions. C3b5 revisits only three trust/confidence-labelled subroles and requires the governed semantic summary or exact question text itself to explicitly contain `trust` or `confid*`. This yields 68 actual trust/confidence measures.

Hostile QA found three mislabeled subrole rows (`conrock`; `condemnd` in two waves) that do not contain trust/confidence semantics. They are preserved in the residual and explicitly recorded as exclusions from this rule.

Trust/confidence is epistemic/evaluative, not the T06 norm/obligation/approval shore. No T06 relation absence is inferred beyond removing these exact semantic units from RR_NORM eligibility.
