Read the three governed negative examples in `T06_GOVERNED_NEGATIVE_CALIBRATION_CASES.csv`.

1. **Positive RR_NORM without an expected actor/action contract does not link to arbitrary conduct** (South Asia democracy suitability).

3. **Topical adjacency is not actor-target-action identity** (government health/climate policy vs vaccination, awareness, media use, household experience, etc.).

These are calibration cases, not templates to overgeneralize. Exact source semantics still control each worker atom.
