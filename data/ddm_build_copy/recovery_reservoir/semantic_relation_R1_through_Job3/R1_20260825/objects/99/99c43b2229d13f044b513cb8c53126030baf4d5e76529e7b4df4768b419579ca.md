# C3b15 doctrine — proposition/endorsement large-batch RR_NORM adjudication

This checkpoint does **not** infer RR_NORM from the fact that a question uses an agree/disagree or endorsement response scale. `proposition_endorsement` is a terrain label only.

Positive RR_NORM requires governed source semantics that directly express prescription, permission/prohibition, obligation, rejection/support, policy recommendation, religious/legal norm, or approval of a governance/conduct form.

Negative RR_NORM requires governed source semantics that are descriptive state/capacity/causal/experience/factual belief and lack a direct conduct/policy prescription, permission, obligation, or approval claim.

Cost/commitment items, conduct items, source-shorthand items with insufficient polarity, and value/affect cases near the ontology's approval shore are retained fail-closed.

Exact decision identity is `(source_family, wave, semantic_unit_id)`. Semantic-unit ID alone is forbidden.

Positive RR_NORM does not emit a T06 relation: its primitive rows transfer to explicit actor-target-action link review. Negative RR_NORM retires only the impossible norm-shore path.
