## 1. What Rawlsing is

Rawlsing is a historically situated person-generation architecture for an LLM chat application. The survey/DDM work is upstream evidence machinery, not the product itself. The goal is to generate a psychologically alive person whose social position, historical conditions, institutional environment, family mediation, developing orientation, pressure-flow, and eventual surface psyche remain constrained by evidence and explicit transformation permissions.

The governing maxim is:

> **Never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority.**

Consequently, survey answers are not personality traits; structural priors are not biography; temporal pressure is not a childhood event; generated mediation is not empirical fact; pressure-flow is not source data; and rendered persona prose is never allowed to silently launder an upstream inference into fact.

The broad architecture is: empirical survey evidence → governed structural interpretation → within-wave structural pressure/status → temporal pressure/possibility → bounded seed affordances → generated life-course / carrier / household mediation → pressure-flow and attractor dynamics → renderer contract → runtime readiness → app-facing generated person.

## 6. The five-job mental model

1. **Know exactly what every survey question is.** Source text, wave, exact implementation identity, response options, routing/applicability, continuity, proposition identity.
2. **Know what structural information every useful question can measure.** Driver/facet/role/target/condition/action/topology/context/behavior boundaries.
3. **Know which questions can lawfully be interpreted together.** Typed relations such as principle/application, goal/means, commitment/cost, norm/conduct, target selectivity, condition sensitivity, complementary roles, etc.
4. **Apply those governed relations to actual respondents.** Direct same-respondent configurations first. Population association is optional enrichment, not semantic authority.
5. **Aggregate governed configuration evidence into structural pressure through time and eventually Rawlsing runtime.** V551/V552/V553+ consume authority; they do not manufacture it upstream.

## 8. J-family / relation authority distinction

Question-family authority begins only after individual question meaning is governed. Proposition identity is not respondent-level joint authority. A portable typed relation concept is not the same object as an exact source-wave implementation. A source-native family/structure is not automatically a J-family. Same-wave membership is not respondent composability.

V550J/V551J are downstream of governed semantic relation/family authority and executable routing/universe constraints. A semantically valid relation implementation may still be non-computable for a respondent because of split ballots, routing, country modules or universe restrictions. That does **not** erase the semantic relation; it blocks respondent configuration execution/capability.

R6's 366 accepted relations are bounded gold, not the full relation graph. P3/P3C is substantial governing proposition/family/boundary ancestry, but not the complete Job-3 graph.
