# C4b T06 actor-target-action doctrine

C4b applies S14-T06's requirement that actor, target, and action domain must match. Political/government topic overlap cannot create a relation.

The complete Afrobarometer R5 positive shore contains one RR_NORM-positive unit (`Q73B_ARB`: agreement that a woman can become the prime minister or president of a Muslim state) crossed with 41 accepted O04 endpoints.

The norm's actor/action object is a woman occupying the prime-minister/president role. None of the 41 endpoints reports that actor/action/target. They instead report respondent or household deprivation, safety exposure, news use, membership, electoral participation, contacting officials, bribery/service experience, public-service problems, or technology use. All 41 exact opportunities are therefore negative T06 relations.
