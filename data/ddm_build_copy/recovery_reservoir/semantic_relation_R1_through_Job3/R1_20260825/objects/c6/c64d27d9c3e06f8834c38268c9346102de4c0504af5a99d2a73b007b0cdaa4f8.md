## 1. What Rawlsing is

Rawlsing is a historically situated personality/person generator.

Its goal is not to produce generic character profiles or trait bundles. The eventual person must emerge from structured conditions including:

- place and historical period;
- class and material position;
- household and family;
- neighborhood and subnational region;
- nation-state and imperial/world-system position;
- religion, ideology, ethnicity, sect, and institutional fields;
- available social scripts and constraints;
- economic, political, and historical crises;
- meaning-horizons: justice, God, death, obligation, hope, despair, legitimacy;
- contingent life-course events;
- agency inside inherited structures.

The eventual app should take historical/social starting conditions and generate a plausible, LLM-chattable person whose vividness remains constrained by upstream evidence and authorized transformation layers.

The governing maxim is:

> **Never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority.**

## 4. The three work layers

### 4.1 Normalization

Raw PDF/SAV/DTA/source metadata becomes one canonical codebook row per released variable.

Rules:

- keep `var_name` stable;
- make `question_text` concise, searchable, and substantively discriminating;
- remove repetitive battery stems from every row;
- preserve response options and special codes faithfully;
- do not invent directionality;
- keep country/wave/source variants explicit;
- leave unresolved source contradictions blocked rather than guessed.

Important corrected schema fact:

```text
value_label_set
  = source/synthetic response-map identifier such as labels0

response_options_all
  = actual code → label serialization

response_options_substantive
  = substantive codes

response_options_special
  = DK/refusal/IAP/not-asked/etc.
```

Do not repeat the old mistaken claim that `value_label_set` itself contains the response semantics.

The current normalized baseline contains 36 active codebooks and uses GSS as the canonical formatting contract.

### 4.2 Packet-building

Packet-building is human governance, not heuristic discovery.

Each family has one sole-authority master:

```text
<family>_row_authority_master.csv
```

Every row receives one disposition:

```text
active_directional
preserved_non_directional
context_only
administrative_only
blocked_review
```

Packet-generated surfaces include:

- source registry;
- row signability ledgers;
- first-pass authority;
- semantic authority;
- position-conditioning authority;
- directionality authority;
- response-option authority;
- review and closure queues;
- readiness/status documents;
- overlays and runtime authority files.

All overlays must serialize the master ledger. Old keyword-review scripts must not overwrite completed packets.

### 4.3 Runtime/probes

V550 consumes:

- normalized codebooks;
- packet overlays and response authority;
- DB substrate;
- question structural mapping;
- source/wave/country aliases and crosswalks.

Probes are diagnostic. They prove the live runtime consumes packet authority correctly. They are not a new governance layer.

## 9. Exact current blocker: Asian W6 identifier collision

The W6 overlay contains two legitimate exact identifiers:

```text
ir3a_6_other
ir3a_6_other_
```

Lossy normalization collapses both to:

```text
ir3a_6_other
```

The first submitted repair stopped the crash by preferring whichever raw identifier equaled the normalized key. That is unsafe: a lookup for `ir3a_6_other_` can silently route to `ir3a_6_other`.

The SE9a–SE9e success-only rerun did not exercise either colliding variable, so it did not prove the fix safe.

The accepted repair design is:

```text
1. exact identifier match first, preserving punctuation/trailing underscores;
2. normalized fallback only when the normalized key has one unique candidate;
3. ambiguous normalized-only fallback fails closed and lists all candidates.
```

Correct repair artifact:

```text
Rawlsing_V550_ASIAN_W6_DIRECTIONALITY_COLLISION_FIX_20260714.zip
```

It changes only:

```text
data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_bridge.py
```

Do not delete, rename, merge, or arbitrarily choose between the two overlay rows.

## 13. Failure doctrine

The expensive failures in Rawlsing have been semantic and contractual more often than computational.

Every row and artifact must know:

- what kind of thing it is;
- who authorized it;
- what source/wave/country it belongs to;
- what it may be used for;
- what it may not be used for;
- whether it is active, preserved, context, admin, blocked, diagnostic, or review-only;
- whether directionality is certified or invented;
- whether it is association or causality;
- whether it is structural pressure or an individual trait.

Rules:

```text
unknowns fail closed
fallback never becomes silent authority
special responses never carry directionality
association is not causality
structure is not psyche
context is not an attitude
review is not certification
runtime consumes permissioned handoffs, not raw tables
```
