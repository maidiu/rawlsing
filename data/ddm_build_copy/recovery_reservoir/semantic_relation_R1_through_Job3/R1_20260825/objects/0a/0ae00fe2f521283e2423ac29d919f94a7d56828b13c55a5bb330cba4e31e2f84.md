**PASS WITH A REQUIRED COMMON ALIGNMENT OVERLAY.** The six original R15 packs remain the correct immutable worker evidence/assignment packages. They should **not** be rebuilt or edited. Current central checkpoints explicitly preserved them unchanged.

- one common project orientation and authority hierarchy;
- one common current doctrine for T03–T11;
- mandatory T06 semantic-unit RR_NORM ledger per worker;
- safe propagation/batching law with machine-readable propagation ledger;
- three governed C4 negative calibration cases;
- standardized return-field semantics and structured relation-role JSON;
- mandatory T07 blocking and W03 duplicate reservation;

## Guarantee boundary

No independent LLM worker can be literally guaranteed to reason identically. This dispatch design removes the major foreseeable *input/procedure/schema* sources of divergence and makes residual semantic disagreement visible and reconcilable rather than silently embedded in outputs.
