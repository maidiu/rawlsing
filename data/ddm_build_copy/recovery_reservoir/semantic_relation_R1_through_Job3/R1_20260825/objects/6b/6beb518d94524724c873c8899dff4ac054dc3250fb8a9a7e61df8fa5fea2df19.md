# Rawlsing V5: Inter-Pressuring Structural Personhood, DDM, Pressure-Flow, and Absolute Horizon

**Status:** canonical current-orientation synthesis for `RAWLSING_DDM_FULL_PROJECT_ORIENTATION_ROOT_20260810_R2`  
**Snapshot:** 2026-08-10 18:31 America/New_York  
**Authority boundary:** this document governs project orientation and future design interpretation. It does **not** itself create survey semantic authority, Job-3 relation authority, P5 row decisions, respondent configuration authority, temporal comparability, or runtime personhood.

This document exists because it is possible to understand every individual DDM artifact and still misunderstand Rawlsing. The survey/DDM program is not the product. Its purpose is to constrain the structural world from which a person may later be generated. Rawlsing is a historically situated person-generation architecture in which personhood emerges through **living flow moving through mutually pressuring structures of power over time**. The V550+ stack exists to carry evidence toward that generative engine without allowing downstream vividness to exceed upstream authority.

## 1. Rawlsing is not a trait generator

Rawlsing must never implement the liberal-individualist picture in which an autonomous individual first exists, receives a collection of personality traits, and is subsequently decorated with class, religion, nation, family, or historical context. The causal direction is substantially the reverse: the organism becomes a person while already situated inside relations of dependency, reproduction, provision, coercion, recognition, inheritance, legitimacy, violence, aspiration, love, language, memory, and meaning.

The person is therefore not a bag of traits sitting outside structure. Nor is the person a deterministic output of demographics. The person is the temporally developing and never-final organization of vitality, need, desire, attachment, attention, fear, curiosity, striving, care, aggression, imagination, loyalty, and interpretation as these flows pass through structured fields of power.

The governing maxim remains:

> **Never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority.**

Survey rows may constrain a structural prior. They may not become memories. Structural pressure may constrain a developmental possibility. It may not become biography. A generated family mediation may shape a developmental simulation. It may not be relabeled as empirical fact. A later psyche model may be generated under constraint. It may not be smuggled backward into DDM as if the survey measured it.

## 2. Structure, power, pressure, and flow

The cleanest ontology is four-part.

### Structure

**Structure** is the historically organized field of relations within which reproduction and social life occur. Rawlsing's current binding scaffold contains nine relational pressure strata:

1. **planetary** — world-system position, global capitalism, climate vulnerability, colonial residue, commodity/development hierarchy, migration regimes, sanctions, etc.;
2. **imperial block** — geopolitical patronage/alignment, occupation, proxy war, aid/military dependence, major power blocs;
3. **nation state** — regime legitimacy, citizenship, policing, courts, corruption, welfare, elections, national identity;
4. **sub-national region** — rural/urban, borderland, regional deprivation, internal coloniality, conflict zone, capital dominance, infrastructure unevenness;
5. **sect / ethno / class** — class fraction, caste/ethnicity/religious minoritization, sectarian location, language, education access, labor-market position;
6. **party / movement** — political party, union, movement, militia, religious movement, protest or organized ideological belonging;
7. **neighborhood / household** — local security, kin density, neighborhood trust, domestic economy, work/school exposure, local surveillance and immediate reproduction;
8. **family** — parent/spouse/child relations, discipline, obligation, gender order, intergenerational authority, affective economy and family transmission;
9. **self** — the immediately lived organism/person: agency, shame, fear, aspiration, attention, defense, meaning posture, psychic residue of structure.

These are not demographic buckets. They are a scaffold for **relations of pressure and mediation**. Not every case activates all strata equally. The same evidence can bear on several strata. Which stratum matters depends on respondent/person position, region, wave, target, actor, carrier, and context.

### Power

**Power** is a relation's capacity to set terms: to define normality, distribute resources, impose costs, make alternatives expensive or impossible, command legitimacy, grant or withhold protection, externalize suffering, demand loyalty, make some interpretations intelligible and others unthinkable, or determine whose needs are absorbable.

Power is therefore not only force. It includes provision, status, dependence, attention, recognition, legal authority, ownership, institutional control, inherited advantage, ideological common sense, and the capacity to make one group's reproduction conditional on another group's compliance.

### Pressure

**Pressure** is how structured power is encountered in concrete life: constraint, demand, permission, scarcity, obligation, inducement, threat, opportunity, shame, prestige, security, contradiction, expectation, protection, exclusion, debt, aspiration.

Pressure is not necessarily conscious. The strongest pressures often arrive as common sense rather than explicit coercion.

### Flow

**Flow** is the living movement through that field: needs, desires, curiosity, attention, attachment, fear, rage, loyalty, care, sexuality, imagination, play, striving, meaning-seeking, world-matching, efficacy, belonging, autonomy, recognition, fairness, beauty, prayer, humor, craft, fatigue, appetite, and the broader vitality of a living organism trying to continue and become.

Rawlsing's central question is therefore not merely **what pressure exists?** It is:

> **Where does living flow go under pressure? What forms can it take? What gets blocked, captured, poisoned, buffered, sublimated, amplified, redirected, mis-aimed, metabolized, or transformed?**

The resulting person is not simply pressure. The person is a historically accumulated pattern of **pressure-flow routing**.

## 3. Inter-pressuring: the strata do not form a one-way ladder

The nine strata must not be modeled as a simple cascade from planetary down to self. They mutually pressurize one another.

**Downward pressure** exists: world markets constrain states; states constrain regions; class/sect formations condition households; households condition children.

**Upward pressure** also exists: household reproduction sustains or destabilizes class formations; movements alter state legitimacy; local resistance alters imperial strategy; demographic/reproductive changes alter institutions; family strategies can reshape migration and labor markets.

**Lateral pressure** is pervasive: family and sect; class and religion; neighborhood and police; workplace and gender order; school and family; diaspora and nation; party and church; household and market all translate, amplify, contradict, or buffer one another.

Therefore a structural coordinate does not mean the same thing everywhere simply because its semantic label is stable. A class position under occupation, sectarianized welfare provision, or internal coloniality is embedded in a different pressure field from a superficially similar income/education position elsewhere.

The computational implication is fundamental: **P5/O09 cannot be a lookup table where one driver equals one stratum.** It must govern multi-strata relevance, target-sensitive routing, contextual modifiers, respondent-position conditions, direct versus mediated relevance, and lawful absence.

## 4. Orientation-to-strata: how structure becomes a person's lived relation

A person does not merely “have” strata. The crucial runtime object is **orientation-to-strata over time**.

Each actor can have shifting relations to a stratum including:

- identity-with;
- alienation-from;
- lens-capture;
- object-visibility;
- felt legitimacy;
- felt threat or protection;
- shame or pride;
- debt or gratitude;
- resentment;
- aspiration or repulsion;
- fidelity or contempt;
- compassion;
- predatory distance;
- transformative energy;
- comic/aesthetic/spiritual distance;
- self-deception or naivety;
- contradiction load.

The crucial distinction is:

> **Identification → the stratum becomes lens.**  
> **Alienation → the stratum becomes object.**

A deeply internalized stratum can disappear as “reality itself.” A deeply alienated stratum becomes visible and can be hated, mourned, mocked, analyzed, fled, exploited, reformed, or transformed.

This is why Rawlsing must not reduce political or moral development to an “awareness score.” **Awareness, solidarity, and viscerality are distinct.** A person may intellectually understand a structure but not feel solidarity; feel intense solidarity but misunderstand causal structure; or live inside the blast radius of a structure while remaining ideologically captured by it.

The system must preserve such incoherence. Real people can be lucid in one domain and distorted in another, courageous in one relation and insulated in another, materially solidaristic in one context and merely rhetorical in another.

## 5. Carriers, frameworks, buffers, and cracks

Raw pressure does not enter consciousness with its causal label attached. It is mediated through **carriers**: parents, family, school, church/mosque/temple, workplace, neighborhood, party, union, movement, ethnic enclave, military, media system, professional field, diaspora network, state institution.

A carrier can explicitly oppose one surface of a stratum while reproducing another. A religious community can oppose government bureaucracy while reproducing national chosenness, property hierarchy, racial innocence, family order, or capitalist morality. A revolutionary organization can teach emancipation while reproducing domination internally. A university can universalize critique while reproducing class distinction.

**Frameworks** are interpretive vessels produced through these mediated encounters. They can carry moral vocabulary, causal stories, shame/pride, concepts of deservedness, images of freedom, theories of suffering, visions of respectability, enemies, duties, ideals, and available futures.

People typically possess several partially incompatible frameworks. Their relative salience changes by domain and event. This creates **cracks**: contradiction becomes noticeable when an event cannot be metabolized by the currently dominant framework without distortion or cost.

A crack does not guarantee liberation. It may produce re-buffering, mis-aim, cynicism, retreat, fanaticism, reorientation, solidarity, or a new synthesis.

## 6. Vitality: the thing that actually moves

The later pressure-flow design treats **vitality** as the moving quantity, not as mood but as life-force under need: the capacity to attach, strive, resist, appease, protect, create, love, dominate, serve, escape, contemplate, play, rage, surrender, or continue.

Useful dynamic distinctions include:

- available vitality;
- usable vitality;
- blocked vitality;
- captured vitality;
- poisoned vitality;
- attenuated or amplified vitality;
- sublimated vitality;
- overflow vitality;
- depleted vitality.

Structural fields constrain channels and costs; they do not directly specify psyche. The same pressure can be metabolized into care, work, prayer, humor, control, withdrawal, organizing, fantasy, predation, despair, art, solidarity, or other routes depending on carriers, buffers, developmental history, available language, social support, risk, and vitality.

This prevents misery-determinism. A person is not the sum of wounds. Rawlsing must model love, play, appetite, beauty, sexuality, humor, curiosity, craft, care, stubbornness, rhythm, and ordinary pleasure alongside injury and domination.

## 7. Absolute Horizon: not stratum 10

**Absolute Horizon is categorically different from the nine finite strata.** It is an internal moral/meaning relation to ultimacy and limit: justice, death, mercy, judgment, God-talk, vocation, hope, despair, ultimate accountability, finitude, the worth of sacrifice, what may or may not be betrayed.

It is not religion affiliation. It is not religiosity. It is not a theological flavor field. An atheist can have an expansive Horizon; a devout believer can have a captured Horizon; a revolutionary, nationalist, nihilist, artist, parent, monk, billionaire, or militant can each organize ultimacy differently.

The cleanest formulation is:

> **The nine strata organize the finite claims through which a person is socially produced. Absolute Horizon is the evolving relation by which those finite claims can be absolutized, relativized, judged, mourned, refused, reconciled, or transcended-without-escaping mediation.**

### 7.1 The strata mediate Horizon

No child encounters “Justice Itself” outside social mediation. A person first encounters ultimacy through family fairness, religious language, national sacrifice, class respectability, political movements, death, love, art, scripture, teachers, grief, war, humiliation, solidarity, or other concrete carriers.

Thus every stratum can attempt to **colonize Horizon** by presenting its own reproduction as ultimate:

- family honor becomes Goodness itself;
- nation becomes sacred;
- career success becomes worth;
- race/sect becomes moral ontology;
- organization becomes Truth;
- self-preservation becomes the final law.

But mediation can generate something capable of judging its own mediator. A person can learn justice from family and later judge family by justice; learn God through church and later judge church by God; learn sacrifice through nation and later refuse national violence in the name of humanity; learn emancipation through a party and later judge the party by emancipation.

That is Horizon becoming **reflexive**: the mediation produces a criterion that can relativize the mediation.

### 7.2 Horizon and identification / alienation

Because identification turns a stratum into lens, weak Horizon-distance can allow a finite stratum to become effectively absolute. Nation, family, class, race, church, party, career, wound, or self can become the unquestioned source of reality and moral order.

With Horizon-distance, identification can become **critical fidelity**: “I belong to you, but you are not ultimate; I can refuse your corruption without ceasing to love what you were supposed to mediate.”

Alienation likewise has several routes. **Alienation without a larger orientation** can become resentment, cynicism, nihilism, flight, contempt, or despair. **Alienation with a strong Horizon** can become transformative refusal: this is false *because something is true*. The person may reject the form while remaining faithful to the good the form claimed to embody.

Thus Horizon changes what alienation can become.

### 7.3 Horizon changes the ordering of claims, not merely pressure magnitude

Consider national military loyalty. Several people can face the same external demand:

- identification with Nation may route it as duty;
- alienation plus fear may route it as compliance;
- alienation plus nihilism may route it as cynical survival;
- a religious Horizon may make non-killing outrank Nation;
- a revolutionary/transnational Horizon may make solidarity outrank Nation;
- a universalist vocabulary can still be defeated by status, security, family dependence, or risk.

Therefore Horizon should never be implemented as `moral_depth = 0.78`. It changes the **ordering of claims** and the amount of loss a person can metabolize in service of what has become ultimate.

### 7.4 Horizon is not a goodness variable

Horizon can be captured or corrupted. Encounter with limit can route toward despair, predation, fidelity, messianic/revolutionary energy, spiritual/artistic symbolic vessels, comic distance, humility, revenge, domination, or withdrawal.

Reality may refuse an identity project through failure, public shame, success that feels empty, illness, romantic rejection, family refusal, political defeat, grief, death, blocked ambition. The later engine must model **RealityRefusalMetabolism**: delusional preservation, resentful inversion, cynical disavowal, collapse, route transfer, sublimation, humble reinterpretation, structural lucid transmutation, reciprocal world-bearing conversion, extractive revenge conversion, contemplative conversion, and other bounded routes.

The design figures sometimes used in project discussion — Jesus/Che, Moloch/Epstein, suicide/collapse — are not survey labels or character templates. They function as **limit-regions of pressure-flow × Horizon metabolism**: reciprocal costly world-bearing; predatory/extractive insulation; catastrophic collapse of viable future/flow. They must never be assigned directly from DDM.

### 7.5 Horizon and awareness / solidarity / viscerality

Horizon interacts with but does not replace other axes:

- **Awareness:** what reality can I see?
- **Viscerality:** what reality can reach my body/lived reproduction?
- **Solidarity:** whose reality claims my concern?
- **Orientation-to-stratum:** through what social lenses do I experience it?
- **Absolute Horizon:** what finally has the right to reorder those loyalties, perceptions, costs, and identifications?

A person can be highly visceralized but morally particularistic; universally committed but materially distant; structurally lucid but unwilling to bear cost; intensely religious but captured by family/nation/class; atheist yet organized around universal human solidarity.

### 7.6 The richest Horizon operation: relativization without disembedding

Rawlsing must not imagine an enlightened subject who “escapes” all strata into abstract Universal Humanity. Nobody escapes mediation. The more interesting possibility is that a person remains concretely embedded while refusing to let any finite embedding become absolute.

One may love family without making outsiders disposable; belong to a people without requiring its innocence; participate in a movement without making organization synonymous with truth; inherit a religion without exempting institutions from judgment; care for self without making self-preservation the highest good.

This is not dis-identification but a different form of identification: **belonging under a non-total Horizon**.

A Horizon shift can then push downward through the Person↔Stratum matrix, changing what induces shame, what prestige attracts, whose judgment matters, whose suffering becomes visceral, which debts remain binding, which institutions retain legitimacy, which losses become bearable, and what futures can still organize vitality. The strata press back through material cost, family rupture, insecurity, exclusion, exhaustion, and temptation. Transformation is therefore unstable and historical rather than once-for-all.

## 8. Parent → partner → child as recursive structural genealogy

The intended parent/child generation logic is not “draw two adults from the same demographic distribution and average them.”

A first parent should become a **structurally situated developing person**. Their accumulated state — class/mobility, education, locality, family scripts, religion/sect, migration, relationship norms, reproduction constraints, active identity projects, rebellion/compliance, kin surveillance, material dependence, and developmental timing — defines a feasible **partner-space and conception-space**.

The second parent is then generated as a constrained attractor within that feasible encounter window. There must be enough structural compatibility for contact/coupling and enough difference for the union to be historically specific. The second parent may come from a different field when the first parent's mobility/exposure makes that plausible; similarity is not assumed.

The conception event is itself structurally situated: what life-stage is active, which frameworks dominate, what buffers exist, whether the relation is marriage/non-marriage/remarriage/travel/diaspora/university/work/family-arranged/etc., whether prior or later children exist, and what family/social costs attach to the event.

The child is not the arithmetic mean of two parents. The child inherits a **household pressure matrix**: material position, parental orientations, contradictions, conflict/solidarity, kin relations, conception timing, sibling order, migration histories, family memory, shame/legitimacy, carriers, and the wider historical field. Flow then begins again in the child: needs encounter that field; responses to need create early routing; the world expands; additional carriers and strata become available; existing frameworks crack or harden; vitality is redirected over time.

Raw structure may influence psyche. Raw structure may **not speak directly as psyche**.

## 10. How V550→V558 phases the ontology

V550 answers what is actually present in a source/wave/respondent substrate: exact variable/question identity, response contract, routing/applicability, distributions, bridge material, structural coordinates and exclusions. It does not generate personality or semantic relations from association.

V550J captures which governed responses/configurations actually coexist in the same respondents. It is empirical morphology, not semantic truth or causality.

V551 maps governed empirical observations into certified evidence atoms carrying relation axis, target, driver/facet and other structurally authorized semantics. It may not invent missing upstream meaning.

### V551J — joint/conditional evidence packets

V551J packages certified joint context while preserving component identities, denominators and provenance. It is an evidence/packaging layer, not a pressure or psyche compiler.

### V552 — within-wave structural priors / pressure potential

V552 is where evidence becomes structural: row-level structural facts, pressure/status surfaces, driver/facet, target, relation, channel, contradiction, conditional stratum/horizon routing and respondent-position conditioning. It must distinguish aggregate structural backdrop from lived person-level pressure.

### V552J — permissioned structural handoff

V552J carries authorized V552 material toward temporal processing without silently reconstructing missing dimensions or converting joint context into a new pressure score.

### V553 — historical/temporal structural field

V553 temporalizes structural priors across explicit comparable waves/corpora. It must preserve persistence, intensification, contradiction, gaps, absences, rupture and non-comparability. It creates the historically situated **possibility/pressure field**, not biography.

### V554 — bounded origin / seed affordances

V554 converts the temporal structural field into bounded seed/origin affordances. It specifies what downstream generation may seed and what remains blocked. It should define an admissible generative space, not fabricate a mother/father/person as empirical fact.

### V555 — life-course / household / carrier mediation

V555 models generated-but-traceable mediation through parents, co-parent, household, school, religion, work, locality, carriers and early developmental routing. Generated mediation remains explicitly generated. This is where the same structural field begins producing divergent lived developmental environments.

### V556 — pressure-flow / vitality / attractor dynamics

V556 is the living causal center and the natural rebasing site for the older V3 backbone. It models yearly becoming: vitality moving through Person↔Stratum relations, identity projects, self-interpretations, refusals, revisions, wounds, compensations, contradictions, Horizon events and re-routing. Enneagram-like attractors, if useful at all, may emerge only as late dynamic patterns from this history; they are never assigned by survey, country, class, birth, or parent priors.

### V557 — phenomenological renderer contract

V557 translates generated latent dynamics into an allowed surface-person contract: what the person knows, misattributes, avoids, can articulate, says publicly, says privately, feels without conceptualizing. It protects against omniscient persona narration and raw structural-explanation leakage.

### V558 — runtime boundary/readiness

V558 validates that the generated contract is safe, traceable and permissioned for app/runtime use. It is a gate, not a person generator.

## 16. The shortest complete mental model

**World/history/power** organize nine mutually pressuring structural strata.  
A positioned organism carries **needs/desire/vitality** through that field.  
Carriers and institutions translate structure into lived local practice.  
Frameworks, buffers, identifications and alienations form through that mediation.  
Pressure routes flow into awareness, solidarity, viscerality, shame, aspiration, care, displacement, domination, withdrawal, resistance, sublimation and contradiction.  
Cracks and events alter what can be seen and what frameworks remain viable.  
**Absolute Horizon** is the evolving relation to what is ultimately binding and can therefore absolutize or relativize finite strata without ever escaping mediation.  
Parents are generated as structurally situated lives; partner/conception space is constrained by those lives; the child inherits a household pressure matrix rather than a demographic average.  
V550–V553 constrain the empirical/structural/historical world; V554–V555 bound and mediate generative origins; **V556 models living pressure-flow becoming**; V557 constrains phenomenological expression; V558 gates runtime.  
The DDM authority work exists so none of those later layers has to fabricate the structural world it is supposed to metabolize.
