# R15 worker-return admissibility audit — R16C-W

## Verdict

**QUARANTINE: DO NOT RECONCILE THE SIX R15 RETURNS AS FINAL S14 AUTHORITY IN THEIR CURRENT FORM.**

The six workers completed their assigned row sets and passed the structural validators. The alarming low yield is real, but it is **not interpretable as "the survey corpus contains almost no lawful relations."** Two separate task-construction effects dominate the histogram, and one is a direct dispatch defect.

## 1. What the headline numbers actually are

Across **85,892** worker atoms:

- ACCEPT: **11**
- PARTIAL_ACCEPT: **18**
- RETAIN_EXISTING_GOVERNED_AUTHORITY: **390**
- REJECT: **79,757**
- BLOCKED: **5,716**

Thus **92.86%** of all returned atoms are REJECT.

However, T06 alone contributes **79,148 / 85,892 = 92.15%** of the entire worker universe. T06 outcomes are:

- ACCEPT: **0**
- PARTIAL_ACCEPT: **1**
- REJECT: **77,987**
- BLOCKED: **1,160**

So **98.53% of R15 T06 atoms are REJECT**. The overall R15 histogram is therefore overwhelmingly a statement about the construction of the T06 candidate surface, not about all T03–T11 relations.

Outside T06 there are **6,744** atoms:

- ACCEPT: **11**
- PARTIAL_ACCEPT: **17**
- RETAIN: **390**
- REJECT: **1,770**
- BLOCKED: **4,556**

That is only **26.25% REJECT**, while **67.56% BLOCKED**. The dominant non-T06 result is therefore *insufficient authority*, not negative relation truth.

## 2. Upstream construction explains much of the low yield

R15 inherits R13 decision atoms whose raw origins come from R11. R11 was constitutionally an **opportunity-search / negative-space / input-authority-gap release**, not a relation-probable or relation-truth denominator. R13 itself explicitly preserved rule-specific atoms without adjudicating relation truth.

Therefore a large R15 REJECT count cannot be read as a "test failure rate." The workers were filtering a deliberately high-recall opportunity universe.

This is especially severe for T06: the old R11/R2 machinery generated enormous norm-recall × endpoint opportunity surfaces. Most exact cross-products are expected not to satisfy actor + target + action correspondence.

## 3. Direct dispatch defect: workers re-adjudicated C3 with less authority

The R16C-V overlay was supposed to install completed C3 doctrine, but it required each worker to independently complete a new Stage-A RR_NORM ledger.

Comparison against the governed C3 parent universe gives:

- worker Stage-A keys: **1,477**
- keys overlapping the C3 3,119-key parent: **377**
- exact agreement with final C3: **327 / 377 = 86.74%**
- disagreement: **50**

The asymmetry is serious:

- governed C3-positive overlap keys: **145**
- worker correctly positive: **100**
- worker called negative: **9**
- worker BLOCKED: **36**
- therefore **45 / 145 = 31.03% of already-governed positive C3 keys were not inherited correctly**.

For governed C3-negative overlap keys:

- total: **232**
- correctly negative: **227**
- incorrectly positive: **4**
- blocked: **1**

Many of the positive misses are exactly the source-stem cases that Vozhd later resolved through central source recovery. The worker overlay simultaneously forbade external/source recovery and failed to include the final governed C3 disposition ledger. Workers were thus asked to re-solve a settled prerequisite using a weaker evidence ceiling.

Those Stage-A results are **not admissible substitutes for C3 authority**.

The other **1,100 worker Stage-A keys lie outside the C3 blocker-parent universe**; they mostly belong to the older R2-recall / same-target T06 terrain. They are not automatically wrong merely because C3 does not contain them, but they require a separate ancestry-aware audit rather than being conflated with the post-C3 C4 shore.

## 4. Negative-vs-block discipline is not sufficiently aligned outside T06

The worker reports show materially different postures on recall-only terrain. Some workers BLOCK when explicit relation-role authority is absent; others issue large REJECT batches for comparable rule families when the needed explicit relation tie is not present in the pack.

Because R11 explicitly says its named channels are recall/opportunity channels and that missing input authority is not relation absence, **a mass REJECT is admissible as negative authority only where the worker proves an exact incompatible prerequisite or a bounded exhaustive negative—not merely failure to recover a positive role tie from the pack.**

The supplied structural validator checked schema, coverage, JSON, immutable fields, T07 reservation, etc. It did **not** independently prove the semantic legitimacy of every REJECT/BLOCK choice. A validator PASS therefore cannot cure this task-design ambiguity.
