# C3b12 doctrine

Close negative RR_NORM only for the eleven exact temporal/comparison predicates listed in the authority CSV. These are source-reviewed assessments of change, state comparison, or intergenerational trajectory; they do not prescribe an action or norm. A phrase such as “better today than four years ago” is comparative content, not the prescriptive construction “it would be better if …”. Normative importance, democratic essentiality, preference, support, responsibility, obligation, permission, desirability, and other prescriptive/evaluative frontiers remain open. Exact decision identity is `(source_family, wave, semantic_unit_id)`; semantic ID alone is forbidden.
