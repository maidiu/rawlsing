# Rawlsing DDM — R8D Media Epistemic Trust Semantic Closure

## Result

R8D terminally dispositions **77** media-trust/credibility recall candidates against the canonical **18,276 / 35-wave** P2 terrain. It reconciles all **49** rows handed off by R8C and all **38** active rows that the legacy QSM had placed in `media_trust_bias`, while adding a source-first lexical audit for media reliability, trustworthiness, integrity, and bias.

The media-owned semantic/topology surface is **58 rows**. Of these, **50** are already certified as executable semantic cores under current P2 response semantics and **8** are semantically intelligible but must preserve non-scalar/topology or structural-choice status pending P4/measurement compatibility. Nothing is installed live.

## Semantic classes

* **47** direct media institution/platform confidence rows → `media_institution_confidence`.
* **1** certified social-vs-traditional-media comparison → `relative_media_credibility_orientation`.
* **1** local-media trust change relative to 2010 → `media_trust_temporal_change`; topology preserved.
* **5** GSS 2024 medium-specific news reliability rows → `media_source_reliability`; P4/topology pending.
* **1** Arab II most-trustworthy local-political-news-source ranking → `media_source_credibility_selection`; structural-choice evidence.
* **3** media epistemic-integrity rows → `media_epistemic_integrity_perception`, preserving fairness/honesty, deliberate-falsehood, and bias subtypes.

## Boundary findings

The legacy `media_trust_bias` facet was materially contaminated. Fifteen of its active rows are ownership, media use, language comprehension, source identification, or similar non-trust material and are rejected from this closure. Four additional legacy hits are legitimate media-field evidence but not media-trust/credibility evidence: social-media representativeness of public opinion, normative importance of independent press, perceived corruption among journalists/media, and the exogenous V-Dem media-bias context index.

## Governing distinctions learned

A media target does not make every media question a trust question. Direct institutional confidence, relative credibility, source reliability, epistemic integrity/bias, temporal trust change, and source ranking are distinct structural meanings. Likewise, a response scale that looks ordinal must not be silently promoted when current P2 preserves it as non-directional. These distinctions should later feed relation generation (e.g. confidence ↔ reliability, confidence ↔ perceived bias, general media confidence ↔ medium-specific credibility) rather than being erased at the semantic layer.
