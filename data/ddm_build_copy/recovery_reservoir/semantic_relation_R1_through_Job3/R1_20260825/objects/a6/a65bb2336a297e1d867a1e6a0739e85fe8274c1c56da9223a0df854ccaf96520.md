# C3b17 source recovery note

Historical/source recovery was performed before adjudicating shorthand cases.

- **Arab Barometer Wave V Q605A**: the official questionnaire stem asks whether respondents agree that **“Shari’a government means …”** the listed attributes (basic services, no corruption, physical punishment, restricting women’s role). These are definitional/conceptual beliefs about what Sharia government means, not respondent approval of those arrangements. Q605A_1..4 therefore receive **NEGATIVE RR_NORM**.
- **Arab Barometer Wave I**: official questionnaire text establishes explicit normative forms where present (e.g. banks **must be forbidden** from interest; women’s social principles; political-system suitability). Those support positive RR_NORM only when the proposition itself is normative/permission/approval.
- **LiTS 2006 q305_1..q305_6**: exact governed text is *perception of state involvement* in the listed economic roles, not respondent preference. These receive **NEGATIVE RR_NORM**.
- **WVS W1 V113/V115 and W4 V131**: proposed future social changes are evaluated as **Good thing / Don’t mind / Bad thing**, which is explicit approval/disapproval of a social change. These receive **POSITIVE RR_NORM**.
- **WVS confidence placeholders (e.g. V160P / W7 Q82_AFRICANUNION)** remain trust/confidence measures and are **NEGATIVE RR_NORM**.
- **17 source-shorthand cases remain fail-closed** because the recovered project authority does not preserve enough stem/polarity to distinguish norm/approval from descriptive, conduct, or cost semantics. No guess is promoted to authority.
