# Output law

Candidate adjudication states:
- `POSITIVE_RELATION`: every required relation-role and common-proposition/target/action condition is established.
- `PROVEN_NEGATIVE`: at least one necessary condition is explicitly incompatible under governed evidence. Must name the mismatch dimension and evidence.
- `BLOCKED_AUTHORITY_GAP`: no positive may be asserted, but no necessary condition is proven incompatible. Must name the missing authority.

Source-object states:
- `POSITIVE_STRUCTURE`, `PROVEN_NEGATIVE`, `BLOCKED_AUTHORITY_GAP`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `RETAIN_EXISTING_GOVERNED_AUTHORITY`.

A missing role tie, absent recall hit, or inability to reconstruct source context is **never** sufficient for `PROVEN_NEGATIVE`.

Positive relations must carry exact semantic-unit and implementation members, explicit role assignments, routing/universe compatibility, evidence basis, and confidence. Position-conditional relations must preserve the condition rather than universalize it.
