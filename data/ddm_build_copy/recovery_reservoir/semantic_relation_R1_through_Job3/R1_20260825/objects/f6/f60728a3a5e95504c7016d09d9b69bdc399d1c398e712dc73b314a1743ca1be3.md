## Third-pass defect: effort imbalance
P1 loads were superficially tight by row count (1,040–1,056), but W02 held the LiTS 2006 wave, which contains **506 of the 520 S2B** `blocked_response_authority_gap` rows plus 2 S2-core gaps, while W02 still held 1,056 total rows. S2 core contributes 12 response-authority-gap rows overall, so the full S2+S2B total is 532. LiTS 2006 therefore contains 508/532 total response-authority gaps. This is a source-recovery burden, not ordinary row throughput.

P2 therefore isolates LiTS 2006 in W01 as a specialist recovery shard (583 rows; 508 response-authority gaps). The remaining 5,711 rows are divided across W02–W06 at the mathematical row optimum: four workers with 1,142 and one with 1,143. Their S2 source/topology-review loads are 104, 115, 108, 111, and 111. The remaining 12 response-authority gaps are 0, 1, 5, 8, and 10.
