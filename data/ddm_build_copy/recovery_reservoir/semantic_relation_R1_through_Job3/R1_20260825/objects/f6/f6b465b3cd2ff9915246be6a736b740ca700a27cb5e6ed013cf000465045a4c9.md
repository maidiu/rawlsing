# App-level guardrail

Rawlsing needs two distinct but interoperating layers:

- V550/V550J: empirical same-respondent response co-travel, demographic/position conditioning, recursive branches, minority/outlier configurations, attitude clouds/co-signatures.
- S14: typed semantic/structural relations among survey objects.

S14 enriches the interpretation of empirical configurations. It does not decide whether two responses may empirically travel together and may not erase valid co-travel when no typed S14 relation is established.

The forward app acceptance gate remains: audit all 35 waves to verify that conditional attitude-cloud machinery survives V550/V550J → V551/V551J/V552 and can support person↔stratum pressure fields before Rawlsing is declared app-ready.
