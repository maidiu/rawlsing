# Rawlsing DDM — project map and current phase

## What the survey-DDM is for

Rawlsing is not trying to reduce respondents to isolated attitudes. The survey-DDM must recover structural information from governed configurations: same proposition/different target; same proposition/different condition; principle/application; goal/means; commitment/cost; norm/conduct; subtype/boundary; institutional differentiation; reciprocity; tradeoff; tension; breadth; target×condition, etc. Those configurations later feed respondent-level evidence, structural pressure, temporal fields, and ultimately the nine-strata inter-pressure/flow persona-generation model.

## Five large jobs

1. **Know exactly what each source question is.** Source, wave, proposition, routing, response options, continuity. Strong P2/P3/P3C foundation.
2. **Know what structural information each substantive question can measure.** This is the current major semantic-map phase.
3. **Build lawful typed cross-question relation concepts/implementations.** R6's 366 source-native edges are gold/calibration only, not the denominator.
4. **Apply those relations to actual respondent configurations.** Current V550/V551/V550J/V551J machinery exists but must consume accepted authority. Population association is optional enrichment, not semantic authority.
5. **Aggregate into structural pressure/time and later Rawlsing runtime.** V552/V553 and downstream stay frozen until upstream authority is trustworthy.
