# Rule cards

## T05
COMMITMENT_PERSONAL_COST: accept only when a commitment/stance is semantically tied to an explicit personal cost or cost-bearing application; public burden or correlated pressure is insufficient.

## T06
NORM_EXPECTED_CONDUCT: require actor, target, action-domain alignment; keep stated/intended vs realized modality explicit; O04 endpoint permission does not create the semantic link.

## T07
GENERAL_TARGET_SUBTARGET: require directional broader→narrower target relation; lexical generality, geography proximity, or same topic is insufficient.

## T08
INSTITUTION_CLASS_DIFFERENTIATION: require a common evaluative predicate applied across governed distinct institution classes; co-membership in a trust battery alone is insufficient.

## T10
TARGET_CONDITION_MATRIX: require genuine two-dimensional crossing—repeated targets across conditions and repeated conditions across targets—with exact cell provenance.

## T11
COMPLEMENTARY_ROLE_CONVERGENCE: require distinct authorized semantic roles converging on one narrow proposition; same target/topic or multiple broad roles is insufficient; no latent-scale inference.
