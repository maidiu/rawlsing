# Chat Wisdom and State Delta — 2026-08-11

## Purpose

This file preserves the **conceptual, architectural, diagnostic, and execution wisdom** developed in the 2026-08-11 Vozhd conversation so that a successor chat does not merely recover filenames and counts while losing why the state changed.

## 1. The trigger: the T01–T13 histogram exposed an epistemic problem

O12-R2 had been announced as repaired with an 811-row novel denominator. The user challenged the shape rather than accepting the validation headline: T01 had hundreds of new instances while several richer relation types had zero or almost zero. That challenge was correct and became the decisive diagnostic.

T01–T13 are **relation templates**, not survey-question categories. A T-code is the relation *type*; an O12 object is one concrete wave-level candidate instance/hyperedge of that type. This matters because a headline such as `T06 = 2 total` means only two governed norm↔conduct relation instances across the entire 35-wave corpus under the then-current authority—not two question types.

The extreme distribution was not to be normalized or balanced by fiat. It had to be causally explained. T01 dominance can be partly source-topological because surveys frequently repeat one predicate across many targets. But the rare-rule tail could not be trusted until comparability of discoverability was proven.

## 2. The user's memory was right: semantic work had been completed

R9D really did complete terminal substantive meaning for **3,324/3,324 relational semantic units**. That should not be reopened wholesale. The error was to let the phrase "semantic coverage complete" imply more than its jurisdiction.

R10G also really did complete canonical coordinate coverage for those units. R10F separately completed topology and behavior/exposure endpoint work. O06/O07/O08, O09, P2/P3/P3C, R14, and R6 each add other governed jurisdictions.

The architectural mistake was to assume that these independently completed surfaces jointly constituted a **single production-grade structural-semantic input resource for O10**. They did not.

## 3. Ontology mismatch: R10G roles are not O10 relational roles

R10G's canonical semantic-role ontology contains categories such as `NORMATIVE_OR_POLICY_STANCE`, `PERCEPTION_OR_BELIEF`, and `EVALUATIVE_JUDGMENT`. These answer questions like **what semantic act does this item perform?**

O10's richer templates require orthogonal **relational roles between questions**, for example:

- `PRINCIPLE` ↔ `APPLICATION`
- `GOAL` ↔ `MEANS` with a governed common end
- `COMMITMENT` ↔ `COST_BEARING_APPLICATION`
- `NORM` ↔ `CONDUCT` with actor/target/action alignment
- `GENERAL_TARGET` ↔ `SUBTARGET`
- institution class identity
- reciprocal actor/recipient direction
- matrix identity/dimensions/cells
- complementary roles converging on a narrow common proposition

Those structures were not installed as a comparable governed layer. O10 was therefore specified against a richer input contract than upstream actually exposed.

## 4. Coverage-grain mismatch: one registry was not one coherent semantic substrate

The 18,276 implementation registry could join information from several layers, but table co-location is not semantic integration. Different rows/fields had different authority ceilings and different denominators.

The project therefore had **complete things without having a complete combined thing**. A successor must never equate:

`all rows accounted for` → `all required relational semantics represented`.

Terminality is always jurisdiction-relative.

## 5. T06 is the clean smoking gun

There are hundreds of governed normative semantic units and **2,113 accepted behavior/exposure endpoints**, yet the two terrains had no common governed semantic-unit identity capable of directly supplying the actor/target/action relation O10's T06 needs.

O11 reduced T06 capability to a proxy like "a norm and some endpoint occur in this wave." O12 then correctly refused most joins because the exact bridge did not exist. The resulting low candidate count therefore could not be interpreted as "the surveys contain almost no norm↔conduct structure."

The missing artifact is a cross-lane link authority such as:

`norm_semantic_unit_id → conduct/intention endpoint`

with actor, target, action-domain, modality, universe, routing, and provenance.

During the central narrowing pass, the stated/intended-conduct side was reduced to **44 same-wave semantic pairs while still recovering both known R6 T06 positives**. This is a recall queue, not yet authority. A first realized-behavior narrowing based on generic word overlap was rejected as semantically noisy; it must use actor/target/action identity rather than words like "support/help."

## 6. O11 was not independently authoritative evidence of absence

Because the required relational structures were missing, O11 used proxy terrain or hard blocks for several rules. Examples included loosely principle-like/goals/cost words, same-wave norm+endpoint coexistence, lexical general-target cues, or no proxy at all for reciprocal roles.

O11 therefore cannot be cited as independent proof that a wave lacks T03–T11 opportunities. Its T03–T11 capability layer requires an append-only successor built from the repaired relational substrate.

## 7. O12-R2 did not actually regenerate T03–T11 from the full substrate

The R2 repair genuinely improved T12 and T13. But T03–T11 rows were inherited from the earlier 674-row build rather than freshly regenerated from a repaired full semantic substrate.

The subsequent "closure" argument reconciled the old R5→R6 source-native review denominator and concluded that rare-rule novel generation was closed. That proof was invalid as a global completeness proof.

Why: known accepted R6 authority itself contains **cross-object governed alignments** for exactly the endangered rule families. Both accepted T06 relations, the accepted T09 relation, and most T10 matrices are cross-object. A closure proof bounded to R14/R5 source-native objects is structurally incapable of proving the absence of additional cross-object opportunities.

Thus:
- R5/R6 remain valid within their bounded authority.
- They are not the maximum Job-3 discovery universe.
- O12-R2's `811` count is not the complete candidate denominator.

## 9. Completeness doctrine changed

The most important epistemic lesson of the conversation:

> **A validator can pass tens of thousands of checks and still prove the wrong thing.**

The old O12 hostile validator proved internal consistency relative to the generator's implemented search surfaces. It did not independently reconstruct the expected search universe from sealed parents. Therefore it could not detect that the generator itself omitted relation opportunities.

Going forward, no `PASS`, `FROZEN`, `DONE`, `COMPLETE`, or worker dispatch may rely solely on internal consistency. Before a completion claim:

- reconstruct the expected universe independently of the generator;
- test negative space and unexplained zeros;
- inspect aggregate shape for semantic plausibility;
- detect shared assumptions between generator and validator;
- verify known-positive recall;
- inspect large exclusion clusters;
- distinguish source absence from input-coverage absence;
- make every zero causally explainable.

The user should not have to serve as Vozhd's external QA layer.

## 10. Worker economics doctrine

Do not shard the ontology or schema. Vozhd freezes the contract and candidate denominator centrally.

The central mechanical work is economical to do in one place: unified spine, joins, candidate narrowing, target/matrix reconstruction, O11/O12 compilers, independent validators.

Only after narrowing should ambiguous semantic packets be measured. If the queue is small, central adjudication is cheaper and safer; if large, shard immutable candidate IDs with one shared schema and central reconciliation.

Current provisional narrowing evidence suggests the semantic tail may be much smaller than initially feared:

- T03–T06 had collapsed to roughly **32 review packets before cross-rule deduplication** at the last checkpoint.

These are **provisional workload measurements, not authority counts**. T07–T11 narrowing remained unfinished at the checkpoint.
