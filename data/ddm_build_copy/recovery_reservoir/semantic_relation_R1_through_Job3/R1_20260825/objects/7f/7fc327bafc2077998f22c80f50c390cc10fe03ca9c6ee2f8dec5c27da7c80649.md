# Method and hard rules carried forward

1. **Source structure outranks isolated child text.** Parent batteries can carry the predicate while children contain only targets.
2. **A semantic closure class is not complete until its parent batteries are enumerated.** Text search alone is insufficient.
3. **Response contracts are recall evidence.** Target-only children can be revealed by trust/distrust response scales.
4. **Old QSM is recall-only.** It may surface blind spots; it may not decide current driver/facet/axis/target semantics.
5. **Historical review-file presence is not semantic quality.** R7P formally demoted it as an execution shortcut.
6. **Semantic meaning and completeness are different.** Later denominator expansion can supersede “complete” without invalidating earlier source-first row meanings.
7. **Exact target/proposition subtype must survive.** Do not collapse direct trust, distrust/anomia, presumed trustworthiness, reliability/fairness, institutional confidence, actor credibility, performance, policy support, legitimacy, and association just because they are adjacent.
8. **Facet boundaries follow source terrain, not inherited labels.** Governance institutions and civic associations were split because source meaning required it. Foreign commercial actors were kept out of supranational-institution trust for the same reason.
9. **Secondary structural affordance is not primary measurement.** E.g. trust in foreign investors may later compose with `imperialAlignment`; it does not primarily measure geopolitical alignment.
10. **No live install.** Current packages are semantic-core proposals/checkpoints. P4, respondent composability, relation authority, P5, production rebuild and runtime remain separate gates.
11. **Never fabricate missing target/proposition text from battery position.** Retain explicit blockers.
12. **Do not use association/correlation to create semantic authority.**
13. **Do not quote obsolete completeness denominators as current.** R7W 534 and the subsequent 577 were both falsified; the 160-extra partition is interim.
14. **Driver closure requires a quiet adversarial recall net**, not merely the exhaustion of one retrieval query.
