# External / Supranational + Foreign-Commercial Trust Semantic Closure — R8A

## Result

The complete 27-row R7W external/supranational/foreign-enterprise candidate lane is terminally dispositioned at semantic-core level. No live install occurred.

- **22 implementations / 16 R7G units** map to `trustEcology / external_supranational_institution_trust`.
- **4 LiTS implementations / 3 R7G units** measure trust in **foreign investors** and therefore reuse the R7Z proposed `commercial_actor_trust` sibling with a `foreign_external` modifier.
- **1 Asian Barometer implementation / 1 R7G unit** measures trust in **foreign enterprises** and therefore uses `commercial_institution_trust` with a `foreign_external` modifier.

## Source-first correction

The July-30 candidate assigned LiTS II `q303k` to `external_supranational_institution_trust`. The native LiTS II questionnaire identifies item 3.03(k) as **Foreign investors**. That target is commercial, not supranational. R8A therefore corrects the candidate mapping rather than preserving an ontology error because it is old.

## International/supranational targets preserved

The 22 institutional rows retain exact target identities including European Union, United Nations, NATO, IMF, International Criminal Court, World Bank, WHO, and WTO. These questions measure confidence/trust in the named organization only. They do **not** by themselves measure geopolitical camp alignment, approval of the organization's policy, internationalism, anti-imperialism, or causal orientation.

## Foreign-commercial boundary

Foreign investors and foreign enterprises are not collapsed into the international-institution facet simply because the targets are foreign. Their primary measured predicate remains commercial-actor/institution trust. `imperialAlignment` is retained only as a possible **secondary structural affordance** for later governed composition; it is not inferred here.

## Evidence ceiling

All rulings are semantic-core successor proposals only. P4 portability, respondent composability, relation authority, and runtime installation remain separate gates.
