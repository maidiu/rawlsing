# R8O — Democracy Terrain Terminal Reconciliation

R8O reconciles the complete **310-row expanded democracy recall universe** established by R8M. It does not create another text-recall denominator. Instead, it takes the 253 rows already dispositioned by R8K–R8N and terminally adjudicates the remaining 57 against source text, response contract, current P2 role/topology, existing source-first closures, and the R7G Job-2 denominator.

- Residual terminally dispositioned by R8O: **57**

The zero residual is deliberately narrow: it means every row in the fixed R8M 310-row democracy recall denominator now has an explicit owner or exclusion. It does **not** mean future source evidence can never reveal another democracy-adjacent item elsewhere in the 18,276-question universe.

## Corrections to recent ancestry

R8O repairs three concrete denominator gaps rather than hiding them.

**R8L democraticness:** twelve direct current/target-country democraticness assessments were outside R8L's earlier closure: Arab Barometer II `q506–q511`, Arab Barometer V `Q511`, Arab Barometer VIII `Q508/Q508B/Q509/Q511`, and Latinobarómetro 2024 `Q19ST`. **R8I personalist rule:** Arab Barometer II `q5172` directly evaluates an authoritarian president who disregards parliament and elections. It belongs with personalist/non-parliamentary rule and closes one additional R7G unit.

**R8M democracy-content topology:** South Asia W1 `c24` asks respondents to choose the single most essential element of democracy. It is structural-choice topology, not a scalar content judgment.
