This matrix is intentionally compact. It names the semantic threads that must survive the downstream chain before local stage work can be considered complete.

| Capability | V553 responsibility | V554 responsibility | V555 responsibility | V556 responsibility | V557/V558 responsibility | Runtime consumer |

## Initial vertical acceptance slices

### VS-1 Temporal change
A multi-wave case with stable pressure, gradual drift, a real gap, and one rupture candidate must retain each distinction through V558.

### VS-2 Contradictory inter-strata pressure
A case in which two structurally different pressures bear on the same downstream domain must remain distinguishable and jointly available rather than averaged into one scalar.

### VS-3 Mediation and buffering
A structural pressure with more than one plausible mediation route, one buffering condition, and one amplification condition must survive to a runtime-consumable prior without instantiating a concrete biography.

### VS-4 Pressure-flow and attractor prerequisites
A case with competing potential routes must reach V556/V558 with enough information for V3 to realize different developmental pathways and Enneagram-like attractor formation depending on lived history.

### VS-5 Negative authority
A case containing missing evidence, noncomparability, uncertainty, and at least one forbidden inference must remain explicitly bounded through V558 and must not become a positive runtime fact.

## Open work

This is a starting matrix, not a frozen final ontology. It should be tightened from actual code inspection and runtime requirements, then frozen only enough to launch implementation. Any proposed new row must identify which acceptance slice or completion gate it unblocks.
