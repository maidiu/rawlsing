# Rawlsing Terminal Demand Envelope

**Authority:** Vozhd Dva whole-system architecture constraint  
**Purpose:** keep every downstream architecture-imagination pass grounded in the intended finished Rawlsing system rather than only the immediately adjacent consumer.  
**Status:** governing architecture lens; not a new ontology and not permission to invent unsupported semantics.

## Why this exists

Adjacent-stage reasoning is necessary but insufficient. A locally coherent V553→V554 or V554→V555 contract can still starve the final Rawlsing system if the architecture pass does not explicitly work backward from the structural-personhood model the pipeline exists to support.

Every maximal architecture pass from V553 through V558, and every later V3/V2 architecture pass, must therefore test its proposed stage against both:

1. the immediate downstream consumer; and
2. this terminal demand envelope.

A stage does not need to recreate or republish all terminal semantics. It must preserve or create the minimum information its own jurisdiction owes so that later authorized stages can realize them without guessing, flattening, or reaching behind an information-loss boundary.

## 1. Structural field: nine finite strata

The binding nine-strata scaffold is:

1. **Planetary**
2. **Imperial Block**
3. **Nation State**
4. **Sub-national Region**
5. **Sect / Ethno / Class**
6. **Party / Movement**
7. **Neighborhood / Household**
8. **Family**
9. **Self**

These are **relational pressure layers**, not demographic buckets and not nine independent scalar features. A single governed evidence object may bear on more than one stratum when lawful authority supports the relation.

The finished system must be able to represent pressure acting **downward, upward and laterally** among strata. Architecture must therefore preserve, where evidence permits, the identities needed to distinguish:

- source / driver stratum or structural position;
- receiving / target stratum or structural position;
- directionality;
- pressure / counter-pressure;
- simultaneous or competing pressure relations;
- conditions under which those relations apply;
- temporal persistence, drift, rupture, absence and noncomparability;
- provenance and epistemic limits.

No stage may collapse these into a single undifferentiated pressure score if doing so destroys later inter-strata reasoning.

## 2. Pressure is relational and historical

Rawlsing models historically situated structural pressure, not static trait lookup.

Pressure may function as constraint, provision, threat, expectation, opportunity, demand, exclusion, authorization, support, or other governed structural force. Its downstream significance depends on position, place, time, target, context and mediation.

The architecture must remain capable of representing:

- coexisting pressures without forced averaging;
- counter-pressure and contradiction;
- temporal change in pressure relations;
- pressure that is structurally present but not actor-realized;
- pressure that becomes actor-relevant only through mediation;
- pressure whose downstream use is blocked, uncertain, absent or noncomparable.

## 3. Mediation is an essential transformation, not a label

Structural pressure does not become personhood merely because a row names a stratum.

The finished system must support mediation through lawful structural mechanisms including, where governed and applicable:

- carriers and institutions;
- frameworks;
- identifications and alienations;
- household/family relations and positions;
- regional/local contexts;
- buffers and cracks;
- amplification, transmission, deflection, blockage and rerouting;
- competing or concurrent mediation pathways.

V555/V556 architecture must preserve the difference between a **structural mediation possibility** and an **actor-specific realized history**. The former may be upstream structural meaning; the latter belongs to runtime/V3.

## 4. Vitality / need / desire and pressure-flow

The final person is not generated by summing pressures. A living organism carries need, desire and vitality through the structured field.

The downstream architecture must leave V3 able to model a developing pattern of pressure-flow routing in which structural pressures encounter capacities, needs, identifications, alienations, buffers, cracks, relationships and prior history.

V553–V558 do not need to instantiate vitality or actor-specific desire. They must avoid destroying the structural distinctions V3 needs to compute how vitality can be routed, constrained, supported, redirected or transformed.

## 5. Orientation-to-strata

**Orientation-to-strata is a central actor-level runtime concept.**

V3 must eventually be able to represent, over time, how an actor relates to the nine strata: identification, alienation, dependence, resistance, aspiration, shame, pride, debt, obligation, rejection, attachment, or other lawfully derived developmental orientations.

Upstream stages must therefore preserve the structural positions, relations, pressures, mediation possibilities, temporal context and uncertainty needed for such orientation to form. They must not assign the actor's orientation themselves.

Every maximal V553–V558 architecture pass must answer explicitly:

> What information from this stage will V3 eventually need in order to form and update orientation-to-strata without inventing the structural field?

## 6. Absolute Horizon is separate from the nine strata

**Absolute Horizon is not stratum 10, not a synonym for religion, and not a demographic variable.**

It is the evolving relation to what is ultimately binding: the horizon within which finite strata can be absolutized, relativized, judged, mourned, refused, reconciled or transformed.

Where governed upstream material bears on Absolute Horizon, the downstream chain must preserve its distinct semantic status and its relations to finite strata without reducing it to biography, religiosity, ideology score, or static identity.

V3/V2 later realize lived orientation/expression. V553–V558 must preserve the lawful structural/horizon prerequisites and constraints without manufacturing actor belief.

## 7. Pressure-flow topology and feedback

By V556, the structural prior system must be rich enough to represent a typed, provenance-bearing topology of possible pressure flow rather than a list of generic route labels.

The terminal system requires the ability to distinguish, where governed:

- structural endpoints;
- mediation-route identity;
- route conditions;
- temporal applicability;
- transmission / buffering / amplification / deflection / blockage / rerouting potential;
- concurrent or competing routes;
- reinforcement and destabilization possibilities;
- feedback possibilities;
- typed uncertainty / absence / noncomparability;
- explicit dependence on actor history when topology alone is insufficient.

No numeric edge weight is required unless a governed formula gives it a lawful interpretation. Relational, ordinal, unknown and noncomparable states are preferable to invented precision.

## 8. Enneagram-like attractor formation is a required developmental outcome

Enneagram-like attractor formation is part of the intended Rawlsing design.

The jurisdictional seam remains:

- **V556:** structural pressure-flow topology and attractor prerequisites; reinforcement/destabilization possibilities; no final actor type.
- **V3:** actor-specific route realization over lived time, recurrent reinforcement/destabilization, basin entry/exit, developmental attractor formation and evolution.
- **V2:** phenomenological/expression layer over the developed actor state.

Every upstream architecture pass must ask whether it preserves enough conditioned, historical, mediation-aware structural information for multiple lawful developmental pathways to remain possible. A direct survey→Enneagram or structural-row→final-type shortcut is forbidden.

## 9. V3 actor-specific developmental causality

V3 is the causal developmental engine. It must eventually support an auditable actor history in which state changes are attributable to governed causes such as:

- generated initial conditions;
- structural pressures and temporal changes;
- mediation and relationship events;
- actor actions;
- generated life events;
- derived consequences and feedback.

Core downstream concepts include orientation-to-strata, exposure, absorption/resistance, pressure-flow routing, developmental feedback, relationship/household effects and attractor formation.

Raw structural evidence may influence psyche; raw structural evidence may not speak directly as psyche.

V553–V558 architecture must therefore preserve causal prerequisites and epistemic class while refusing actor-specific realization.

## 10. Genealogy / parent / partner / child recursion

The finished Rawlsing path must support historically constrained recursive generation:

`Parent 1 development → encounter → Parent 2 development → relationship / household field → child generation and development`

Parents, partners and children are not demographic averages or V5 facts. They are runtime-generated persons whose histories are conditioned by lawful structural priors and then become causal environments for one another.

Upstream architecture must preserve enough structural/temporal/mediation information for this recursion without pre-instantiating family biography.

## 11. V2 phenomenology and expression

V2 is the phenomenological/conversational membrane over causal state. It owns such things as expression, memory presentation, register, self-narration, concealment/misrecognition, symbolic language, voice and conversation.

V2 does not become causal authority. If conversation changes the actor, it must become an explicit event returned to the causal runtime/V3 path.

Upstream structural artifacts must never be treated as dialogue, autobiography or finished self-understanding.

## 12. Epistemic classes must remain distinct

The full path must preserve at least these distinctions:

1. **source evidence**;
2. **structural inference / prior**;
3. **generated runtime fact**;
4. **rendered expression**.

A downstream artifact may derive from an upstream one without inheriting an authority the producer did not possess.

Uncertainty, absence, noncomparability and forbidden inference remain first-class. Downstream vividness must never exceed upstream authority.

## 13. Mandatory terminal-demand questions for every maximal architecture pass

A maximal architecture worker must answer all of the following, even when the answer is "this stage does not own it":

1. **Nine-strata field:** Which stratum/position identities and relations are available at this stage, and what must this stage preserve or derive so later inter-strata pressure/counter-pressure remains possible?
2. **Relational direction:** Does the proposed architecture preserve source/target, directionality, pressure/counter-pressure, contradiction and simultaneous relations where governed?
3. **Time:** Can later stages distinguish persistence, drift, rupture candidate, gap, noncomparability and temporal applicability?
4. **Mediation:** What does this stage contribute to carriers/frameworks/identification/alienation/buffering/amplification/deflection/blockage/rerouting without instantiating an actor history?
5. **Orientation-to-strata:** What prerequisites must survive so V3 can later form actor-specific orientation to the nine strata?
6. **Absolute Horizon:** If Horizon-relevant governed material exists, is its distinct status preserved rather than flattened into finite-stratum or religiosity semantics?
7. **Pressure-flow:** What does this stage owe so V556 can construct structural pressure-flow topology and V3 can later realize actual routing?
8. **Attractors:** Does the stage preserve what V556/V3 need for multiple historically conditioned attractor pathways without direct final-type inference?
9. **Vitality/organism boundary:** Has the stage avoided pretending structural pressure itself is actor need/desire/vitality while preserving what later routing requires?
10. **Genealogy:** Does the architecture preserve what historically constrained parent/partner/child runtime generation may later need without pre-generating biography?
11. **Epistemic separation:** Are source evidence, structural prior, runtime fact and rendered expression still distinguishable by reference and permission?
12. **Negative authority:** Are absence, uncertainty, noncomparability and forbidden inference preserved as such?
13. **DAG reuse:** Can later stages still reach original authoritative objects where their original meaning remains needed, instead of relying on lossy serial republishing?
14. **No starvation proof:** Identify at least one terminal capability that would fail if each proposed primary object family were removed. If none exists, justify why the family is needed at all.

## 14. Recalibration law for previously completed architecture passes

S00/S00.5 and PRE-S01/PRE-S01.5 remain valuable evidence about current code, seams, object grains and defects. They are **not discarded**.

However, because their maximal architecture work was primarily constrained by immediate downstream demand, their architecture conclusions must be re-tested explicitly against this terminal envelope before implementation is authorized.

The recalibration sequence is:

`existing excavation + existing architecture blueprint + terminal demand envelope`
`→ terminal-demand backward derivation`
`→ identify starvation risks / misplaced jurisdiction / missing prerequisites`
`→ revise only affected architecture slots`
`→ retain unaffected code-topology and authority findings`
`→ issue a new architecture snapshot superseding the earlier maximal blueprint for implementation purposes`

A recalibration worker must not inflate a stage merely because the terminal system is rich. The governing question remains:

> What is the minimum stage-specific meaning required here so the complete Rawlsing system can later realize its intended structural-personhood dynamics without information loss or illicit invention?
