# Constraints and Priorities

## Priority order

1. Preserve explicit source truth, accepted semantic authority, and the closed whole-machine architecture.
2. Prevent downstream semantic starvation and avoid destructive rebuild/reownership.
3. When hard authority leaves more than one lawful choice, resolve the ambiguity under `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md`.
4. Achieve a complete demonstrable vertical path quickly enough to become a usable portfolio/resume artifact.
5. Deepen semantics only where required by named capabilities and runtime needs.
6. Expand horizontal source coverage after vertical semantics are proven.
7. Polish app/demo behavior after causal and provenance boundaries are sound.

## Hard constraints

- The whole-machine architecture from V553 through V2/application is closed. Current implementation debt, migration cost, missing rules, or compatibility pressure do not reopen it.
- No reopening V550–V552J without a concrete downstream proof that the defect belongs upstream.
- No stage-local optimization that drops information needed by a downstream capability.
- No silent promotion of source evidence into generated person facts.
- No direct survey→psyche or survey→Enneagram assignment.
- No LLM-controlled causal transitions in V3.
- No V2 direct mutation of causal state; conversational effects must become explicit runtime events if they matter causally.
- No source-family special cases downstream unless a governed semantic difference requires them.
- No large speculative ontology expansion without a blocked acceptance slice or gate.
- No architecture artifact created merely because it might be useful later.
- No invocation of `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md` as substitute empirical evidence, a missing semantic rule, or permission to move meaning across closed jurisdiction.

## Economy rules

- Prefer one compact matrix over several prose specifications.
- Prefer representative fixtures over full-corpus runs during active development.
- Prefer vertical integration tests over exhaustive local polish.
- Preserve high-resolution lineage by reference when carrying every field inline would create unnecessary bloat.
- Defer optional improvements aggressively once the app path is strong, coherent, and defensible.
- If two mechanisms are equally lawful, equally faithful to the governing spirit, and provide equivalent safety, choose the simpler one.
- Simplicity does not outrank preservation of mediation, contradiction, actor opacity, causal history, epistemic separation, or developmental possibility.

## Authority order for post-closure execution

1. Exact source semantics and producer-owned authority for the meaning in question.
2. Accepted canonical semantic authorities, rules, registries, rulings, and negative authority.
3. Closed whole-machine architecture, jurisdiction, seam hardenings, and noncollapse law.
4. `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md` as binding interpretive/tie-break law when 1–3 permit multiple lawful choices.
5. Current implementation as terrain, reusable machinery, compatibility evidence, and migration substrate—not as authority to weaken 1–4.
6. Historical V3/V2 material where explicitly authorized as continuity/authority-recovery evidence.
7. New proposals from Vozhd/Rabochiis, subject to explicit adoption.

If the governing spirit appears to conflict with items 1–3, surface the conflict to Vozhd. Do not silently reinterpret hard authority. If current implementation conflicts with accepted architecture, current implementation is migration debt rather than architectural authority.

## Governing-spirit tie-break

When explicit law does not uniquely choose a mechanism, prefer the design that most faithfully preserves the project-level commitments encoded in `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md`, especially:

- a person causally intelligible without being inevitable;
- structural causality without demographic/psychological essentialism;
- mediation instead of structure→psyche shortcuts;
- determination without transparency;
- actor opacity, misrecognition, memory/accessibility limits, and non-omniscient expression;
- contradiction, simultaneous pressures, and multiple partially incompatible frameworks;
- identification/internalization/naturalization/invisibility/`agent-of` and alienation/externalization/objectification/visibility/`agency-against` as dynamic relations;
- ordinary vitality, desire, humor, beauty, care and play alongside suffering/domination;
- developmental history and attractors rather than static type assignment;
- historically situated agency and genuine divergent possibility;
- Absolute Horizon as ordering of ultimate claims and capacity for finite identification to be relativized without pretending to escape mediation;
- generation that becomes vivid downstream without laundering invention into evidence;
- provenance, replay, explicit randomness and fail-closed uncertainty.

## Completion discipline

The objective is a high-quality finished system, not maximal theoretical completeness.

A feature or abstraction should be deferred when all are true:
- it is not needed by the vertical acceptance slices;
- it is not needed for horizontal source qualification;
- it is not needed for V3/V2/genealogy correctness;
- it is not needed for the demonstrable app path;
- omitting it does not create hidden semantic loss, provenance failure, or a governing-spirit violation in an otherwise ambiguous design choice.

## Known architectural commitments

- Rawlsing models structurally emergent personhood through inter-pressuring social strata and temporal pressure/flow.
- Orientation-to-strata is a central actor-level runtime concept.
- Parent/partner/child generation is recursive and historically constrained.
- Enneagram-like attractor formation is an intended dynamic component, with structural prerequisites upstream and actor-specific formation in lived developmental runtime.
- V2 is phenomenological/rendering authority, not causal authority.
- Provenance and epistemic class separation must survive the full path.
- `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md` governs lawful ambiguity across authority, implementation, review, and integration work.
