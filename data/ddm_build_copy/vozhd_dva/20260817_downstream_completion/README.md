4. `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md` is binding interpretive law when explicit source/semantic authority and closed architecture permit more than one lawful choice. It governs how ambiguity is resolved; it never creates missing authority or overrides jurisdiction.

10. Scope discipline is mandatory. Anything that does not block a named vertical capability, horizontal qualification, V3/V2 integration, genealogy, or the app path is deferred.
11. Quality is evaluated end-to-end. A locally elegant stage that starves downstream consumers is not complete.
12. Economy matters: prefer the smallest mechanism that gives reliable coordination, traceability, and regression protection.

## Interpretive authority hierarchy

When making a design or engineering choice:

1. obey explicit source semantics and accepted semantic authority;
2. obey closed architecture, stage jurisdiction, and binding seam/noncollapse law;
3. if genuine lawful ambiguity remains, apply `RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md`;
4. only then optimize for implementation convenience, elegance, or economy.

The governing spirit therefore has real decision force without becoming an excuse for philosophical invention. A worker who believes hard authority and the governing spirit conflict must surface the conflict to Vozhd rather than silently bending either one.
