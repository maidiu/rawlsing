# V3/V2 Recovery Evidence Priority Ruling

**Status:** `BINDING_GLOBAL_ARCHITECTURE_LAW`  
**Authority:** Vozhd Dva  
**Applies to:** TD-R05 V3, genealogy/runtime work, later V2 recalibration, and any compatibility discussion involving recovered V3/V2 material.

## 1. Absolute priority

The absolute priority is **V550+-grounded Rawlsing vision-integrity**.

The governing order is:

1. total Rawlsing vision / terminal personhood demand;
2. maximal lawful V550+ structural-world implementation and information preservation;
3. V3 causal runtime designed or redesigned as necessary to realize that world through generated life;
4. V2 phenomenology/expression designed or redesigned as necessary to express generated life without becoming causal authority;
5. recovered V3/V2 machinery reused, adapted, or replaced according to how well it serves 1–4.

No V3/V2 compatibility concern may weaken an earlier terminal capability.

## 2. Continuity preference

The project **desires continuity** with the latest extant V3/V2 implementation.

Where recovered machinery already performs a terminally required function and can accept the full V550+ output without semantic loss, prefer reuse or adaptation over needless rewriting. A serendipitous bridge is valuable and should be taken when it is truly lossless.

This is a continuity preference, not architectural sovereignty and not an upstream veto.

When continuity and vision-integrity conflict, vision-integrity wins absolutely and V3/V2 changes as much as necessary.

## 3. Non-reduction law

Nothing in recovered V3/V2 may be used to reduce, simplify, flatten, omit, reinterpret, or narrow accepted V553–V558 semantics.

If extant V3/V2 cannot consume an accepted structural distinction, **V3/V2 changes**.

This applies especially to:

- nine finite strata and distinct Absolute Horizon;
- source/target/direction identity;
- simultaneous contradictory/coexisting pressures;
- V553 observations, relations, limits, gaps and noncomparability;
- V554 affordances and applicability conditions;
- V555 atomic mediation-route identities and route conditions;
- V556 governed dyadic/n-ary interactions and attractor-prerequisite topology;
- native geographic scope and unresolved generated-position requirements;
- valid-empty, missing-authority, denied, negative and noncomparable states;
- item-level epistemic/use/provenance distinctions;
- temporal-proxy metadata without confusing selected evidence with lived actor history.

## 4. Recovered implementation status

The recovered V3/V2 snapshot is the latest extant downstream implementation/design evidence currently available. It must be studied carefully so accumulated capability is not lost through ignorance.

Every substantive recovered mechanism must receive an explicit disposition:

- `REUSE_UNCHANGED_IF_FULLY_COMPATIBLE`;
- `ADAPT_TO_TERMINAL_ARCHITECTURE`;
- `REPLACE_WITH_STRONGER_MECHANISM`;
- `DEFER_MISSING_AUTHORITY`;
- `REMOVE_AS_OBSOLETE_OR_REDUNDANT`.

Explicit disposition is an anti-amnesia requirement, not deference.

## 5. Decision test

For every recovered V3/V2 mechanism ask in this order:

1. What does the total Rawlsing vision require the finished machine to do?
2. What complete information and possibilities does accepted V550+ supply?
3. What must V3/V2 therefore be able to realize or express?
4. Does the recovered mechanism already do that without loss?
5. If yes, preserve/adapt it; if no, replace or redesign it.

Never reverse the reasoning by asking what V550+ should become so that old V3/V2 can stay comfortable.

## 6. Tie law

If two architectures are terminally equivalent and equally preserve V550+ semantics, prefer the one that preserves proven extant V3/V2 machinery and continuity with less unnecessary rewrite risk.

If they are not terminally equivalent, choose the architecture with greater Rawlsing vision-integrity regardless of compatibility cost.

## 7. Final law

**Continuity is desired. Vision-integrity is absolute.**

Preserve aggressively when lossless. Redesign without hesitation when necessary.
