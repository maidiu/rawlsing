# 8. Hardening H2 — two-channel refresh

A single `next_context_change_time` is insufficient as the complete invalidation mechanism because many context-relevant changes are V3-generated and therefore unknowable to V558 in advance.

## Channel A — repository-predictable structural refresh

V558 may emit deterministic known boundaries such as:

- query time crossing a known V553 support/observation/relation/limit boundary;
- policy or immutable repository bundle version change when caller selects a new bundle;
- other producer-governed deterministic context boundaries derivable from the fixed bundle/query.

## Channel B — caller/V3 generated-binding invalidation

V3/caller must immediately re-resolve the same repository whenever the query binding fingerprint changes, including:

- effective location/residence after a generated move;
- generated class/sect/party/institution/occupation or other structural-position binding;
- relationship binding;
- household/family binding;
- genealogical generation subject;
- development/generation phase where a producer predicate depends on it.

V558 does not predict these changes and does not turn them into structural events. It merely resolves a new query containing caller-owned facts.

# 9. Hardening H3 — generated-binding echo epistemic law

Some `ContextQuery` inputs necessarily describe V3-generated reality. Their presence at the resolver boundary does **not** transfer ownership to V558.

Every generated binding entering the resolver must be typed/treated as a caller-supplied runtime-fact reference, with enough provenance to replay the query:

- runtime fact ID;
- binding kind;
- effective time;
- structural-position/entity ref where applicable;
- binding fingerprint/source event ref where available;

The resolver may use such facts only to evaluate producer-governed scope/applicability and may echo them in query/context metadata for replay.

Forbidden:

- reclassifying binding as V558-generated structural prior;
- copying actor biography/relationship state into repository ontology;
- resolver using geography/prevalence to invent missing binding;

This resolves the hostile question of actor-history leakage in `ResolvedStructuralContext`: generated bindings may be *referenced* there, but they are not V558-generated content.
