## 4. Residual V554 obligation

### 4.1 What a bounded affordance / structural seed should provisionally mean

**`REQUIRES_LATER_BINDING_ADJUDICATION`** — A V554 bounded affordance should be a **governed downstream-use object**, not a mini-personality trait and not a second pressure fact. Its grain should be tied to exact upstream structural/temporal identity, and its semantic payload should answer only what V554 must add for the next transformation.

A provisional affordance needs:

- stable V554 affordance ID;
- exact source object references, not reconstructed labels;
- source structural fact ID(s) where the original structural value matters;
- V552J association/admission/use-permission references;
- exact V553 temporal key and relevant observation/relation/limit references when temporal meaning is used;
- governing semantic/directionality/position authority references where those authorities are part of the affordance rule;
- an affordance domain or role **only if grounded by an adjudicated mapping/rule**;
- typed downstream-use state: e.g. eligible, reviewable, blocked, not-applicable, unresolved;
- explicit conditions still required before V555 can form/evaluate a mediation route;
- negative authority / known absence / noncomparability / missing-authority state;
- uncertainty/provenance as inherited and/or explicitly derived, without converting uncertainty into a structural value;
- an epistemic statement such as `structural_mediation_input_affordance`, never “person has trait X.”

### 4.2 What V554 may lawfully derive

**`STABLE_FROM_ACCEPTED_UPSTREAM_LAW`** — It may derive only meanings supported by declared inputs and a governed transformation rule. It may preserve, narrow, deny, or condition downstream permission. It may compose original producer identities. It may state that further mediation evidence is required. It may not promote file presence, readiness, confidence, aggregate coverage or missing-value defaults into substantive pressure/position/person facts.

**`REQUIRES_LATER_BINDING_ADJUDICATION`** — Whether V554 may produce an ordinal/numeric affordance priority or “strength” is not established by accepted upstream law. The safe advisory default is **no new authoritative numeric seed strength**. V555 can reuse V552 pressure values, V553 deltas and producer confidence/authority directly. If S01 wants a V554-specific score, it needs a named governed formula, inputs, interpretation, and proof that the score is downstream-use metadata rather than a reconstructed structural fact.

### 4.3 What V554 must not create

**`STABLE_FROM_ACCEPTED_UPSTREAM_LAW`** — V554 must not create:

- an empirical pressure value that differs from/replaces V552;
- a temporal relation that differs from/replaces V553;
- semantic/directionality/position authority that belongs to earlier governance;
- synthetic exact temporal identity from country/year aggregate fields;
- person belief, personality, motive, memory, biography, household membership, parent/co-parent/child facts, institutional membership or actor orientation;
- a causal event claim from a temporal delta;
- an instantiated mediation route or response history merely to satisfy later schemas.
