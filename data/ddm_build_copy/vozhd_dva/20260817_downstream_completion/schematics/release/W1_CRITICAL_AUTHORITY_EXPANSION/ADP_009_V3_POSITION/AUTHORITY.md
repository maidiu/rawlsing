## 3. Position semantics

A V3 structural position is a time-indexed objective actor fact binding an actor to one exact governed position dimension/requirement and an exact target or an explicitly authorized absence.

It does not imply that the actor identifies with, understands, consciously knows, approves of, behaves according to, or remains permanently in that position.

ADP-009 consumes the existing finite-stratum vocabulary and creates none. Absolute Horizon is not a position stratum. `Self` is not a free trait bucket: v1 defines no generic Self-position rule, and no personality, diagnosis, ideology, moral-worth, Enneagram, preference or prose-biography label may be smuggled into Self as a position.

Facts are atomic by governed position dimension/requirement. Multiple independent facts may coexist. There is no authorized global `dominant_position`, scalar identity profile, or compulsory single position per stratum.

Moving location does not silently change class, sect, party, occupation, institution, household, family, or Self. A multi-effect transition may change several position facts only when one exact registered rule explicitly couples and independently supports every effect.

## 10. Mobility / prospective-history law

A generated position changes only through a committed ADP-009 event at exact effective time `T`:

```text
prior facts remain historical truth before T
-> POSITION_TRANSITION_COMMITTED @ T
-> new facts become active from T
-> generated-position binding delta
-> prospective ContextResolver re-resolution
```

Lawful trigger classes, only when the exact rule names their authority, are `INITIAL_BINDING_TRIGGER`, `GENERATED_CAUSAL_EVENT_TRIGGER`, `ADMITTED_EXTERNAL_EVENT_TRIGGER`, and `RULE_SCHEDULED_TRIGGER`. Scheduler invocation never supplies position semantics. A new structural context or prevalence table is not itself mobility.

Migration/location mobility may be a position transition where an exact location dimension/target is governed. The move does not automatically alter another dimension. New geography may change prospective option availability; it cannot rewrite pre-migration class, sect, party, work, institution, household/family, encounter/exposure, route, response or memory history.

`POSITION_ENDED` may prospectively end a fact without replacement. Ending does not equal explicit absence unless an exact rule separately creates an absence fact.
