## 6. Canonical expectation model

The recovered four qualitative postures survive:

- `ENTITLED`: generated history supports treating satisfaction/pursuit in the declared scope as expectable enough to anticipate;
- `HOPEFUL`: generated history supports possibility without entitlement/certainty;
- `FORBIDDEN`: generated history supports treating satisfaction/pursuit as disallowed, dangerous, prohibited or prohibitively costly;
- `HOPELESS`: generated history supports treating satisfaction/pursuit as unavailable/unattainable.

They are developmental motivational postures, not objective truth and not ADP-017 `KNOWLEDGE`/`BELIEF`.
