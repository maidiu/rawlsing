A generated memory is only a possible causal/access basis. Even an `ACCESSIBLE` ADP-016 memory is not self-executing knowledge, belief, articulation, or first-person claim permission.

## 3. Jurisdiction

### 3.1 This authority owns

`ADP-017` owns the generic V3 rule governing:

- canonical claim/proposition identity used for actor epistemic state;
- admission and persistence of generated actor epistemic stances;
- categorical distinction among knowledge, belief, uncertainty, positive actor nonknowledge, and system-unknown epistemic state;
- current claim accessibility as distinct from memory accessibility;
- analyst-only classification and first-person exclusion;
- analyst-side misrecognition relation only when an already-generated actor belief/interpretation and an exact objective referent are lawfully comparable;
- contradiction and coexisting-belief preservation without forced coherence;
- deterministic first-person semantic permission derivation from exact generated stance + current accessibility;
- immutable `ActorAccessibleRenderEnvelope` construction for V2;
- epistemic transition, permission and envelope provenance/replay;
- fail-closed handling of missing basis, missing comparison authority, stale state, hidden analyst material, and unauthorized V2 widening.

### 3.2 This authority does not own

It does **not** decide or generate:

- objective event/fact admission or truth;
- ADP-016 memory formation, retention, forgetting, recall or memory-accessibility transitions;
- domain-specific beliefs merely because an event or structural pressure occurred;
- a local explanation for structural causation when no generated actor interpretation exists;
- V2 phenomenological salience, response move, register, voice, wording, uncertainty style, relationship expression or renderer behavior;
- V2 transcript/session retention or summarization;
- external-event admission;
- structural-prior interpretation or raw V558 access;
- causal RNG semantics;
- causal scheduler cadence;
- a scalar coherence/confidence score;
- free-text semantic similarity as claim identity, contradiction, or belief evidence;
- the proposition that an actor knows they are misrecognizing something unless a separate generated actor epistemic transition establishes that meta-knowledge.

If a domain-specific V3 rule needs to create a belief, uncertainty, nonknowledge or knowledge effect, that rule must supply an exact typed `ActorEpistemicEffect` with causal parentage. ADP-017 validates and materializes the effect; it does not invent the effect's content.

## 4. Canonical claim identity/reference model

### 4.1 `CanonicalClaimRef`

Actor epistemic state SHALL target a semantic claim object, not prose. A conforming implementation SHALL represent the semantic equivalent of:

```text
CanonicalClaimRef {
  claim_id,
  claim_schema_version,
  subject_ref,
  predicate_ref,
  object_or_value,
  polarity,
  temporal_scope,
  subject_scope,
  referent_refs[],
  canonical_content_hash,
  integrity_state
}
```

Requirements:

- `predicate_ref` is a versioned semantic predicate/reference, never an LLM-generated paraphrase;
- `object_or_value` is typed and canonically serializable;
- `temporal_scope` prevents a fact true at one time from silently becoming a timeless/current claim;
- `referent_refs[]` preserve exact generated fact/event/relationship/claim refs where the proposition depends on them;
- free text may be attached downstream as expression only and never participates in canonical identity;
- claim identity is stable across paraphrase, language and V2 renderer/model changes.

A conforming deterministic identity is:

```text
claim_id = SHA256(
  "V3_CANONICAL_CLAIM"
  || claim_schema_version
  || canonical_json(subject_ref, predicate_ref, object_or_value,
                    polarity, temporal_scope, subject_scope, referent_refs)
)
```

### 4.2 Claim comparability

Two claims are contradictory, equivalent, narrower/broader, or otherwise comparable only when exact governed predicate/scope semantics establish that relation. Textual similarity, embeddings, common words, shared structural provenance, or LLM judgment are never sufficient.

Direct logical negation may be recognized only when the claim schema/predicate authority machine-binds the negation relation. Otherwise apparently conflicting claims remain separate without a manufactured relation.

### 4.3 Objective relation is analyst-side

For internal V3 validation, an exact claim may have one of these objective relations:

- `OBJECTIVE_SUPPORTED` — exact generated truth supports the claim in its declared scope;
- `OBJECTIVE_CONTRADICTED` — exact generated truth lawfully contradicts the claim in its declared scope;
- `OBJECTIVE_UNRESOLVED` — objective truth is missing, unknown, noncomparable, integrity-gapped or outside authority;
- `NOT_OBJECTIVE_TRUTH_CLAIM` — the claim is not adjudicated as an objective generated-fact proposition by this authority.

This relation is **analyst-side by default**. It never becomes actor knowledge or V2-visible “correctness” metadata merely because V3 can compute it.

## 5. Actor epistemic state model

### 5.1 `ActorEpistemicStateSet`

For each `(actor_id, claim_id)`, V3 maintains the semantic equivalent of:

```text
ActorEpistemicStateSet {
  actor_id,
  claim_id,
  active_stance_records[],
  current_claim_accessibility,
  accessibility_basis_refs[],
  analyst_relation,
  current_state_hash,
  last_transition_ref,
  integrity_state
}
```

`active_stance_records[]` is a **set**, not one compulsory winner. This is deliberate: generated contradiction, ambivalence and partially incompatible frameworks may coexist until an explicit causal transition changes them.

### 5.2 Generated stance kinds

The only generic v1 stance kinds are:

- `KNOWLEDGE` — a generated actor epistemic state tied to an exact objectively supported claim plus an exact actor-facing acquisition/recognition basis;
- `BELIEF` — a generated actor endorsement/interpretation of an exact claim; its objective relation may be supported, contradicted or unresolved;
- `UNCERTAINTY` — a generated actor state of uncertainty toward an exact claim or explicitly governed claim set;
- `NONKNOWLEDGE` — a **positive generated actor state** that the actor does not know / recognizes lack of knowledge regarding the exact claim/scope.

If no governed active stance exists, the materialized summary is:

- `EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN` — the system lacks authority to assert the actor's stance.

`EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN` is not an active stance and is not renderable as “I don't know.” System ignorance is not actor ignorance.

### 5.3 No automatic stance implications

ADP-017 does not automatically derive:

- `OBJECTIVE_SUPPORTED -> KNOWLEDGE`;
- `MEMORY_ACCESSIBLE -> KNOWLEDGE`;
- `KNOWLEDGE -> separate BELIEF record`;
- `BELIEF -> KNOWLEDGE`;
- `OBJECTIVE_CONTRADICTED -> actor recognizes error`;
- `EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN -> NONKNOWLEDGE`;
- `STRUCTURAL_CAUSATION -> local actor explanation`;
- `INACCESSIBLE -> forgotten objective fact`.

If a lawful source rule intends more than one stance, it must emit more than one explicit typed effect.

### 5.4 Knowledge admission gate

A `KNOWLEDGE` stance may be admitted only when all are true:

1. exact `CanonicalClaimRef` exists and verifies;
2. the claim is `OBJECTIVE_SUPPORTED` by exact generated V3 truth in the same temporal/subject scope;
3. an exact generated/admitted actor-facing event or V3 causal transition supplies a lawful acquisition/recognition basis;
4. the owning V3 rule explicitly emits a `KNOWLEDGE` `ActorEpistemicEffect` for this claim;
5. the actor's role in the basis is sufficient under that rule; mere co-presence, structural reachability or system observability is not sufficient;
6. all causal parent IDs/hashes and pre-state hashes verify;
7. no required authority is missing.

Objective truth is therefore necessary for this authority's `KNOWLEDGE` label but never sufficient. If objective relation is unresolved, the system may not assert `KNOWLEDGE`; separately generated `BELIEF`, `UNCERTAINTY` or `NONKNOWLEDGE` may still exist.

### 5.5 Belief admission gate

A `BELIEF` stance requires:

1. exact claim identity;
2. an exact generated/admitted V3 causal parent or governed actor-state transition;
3. an owning V3 rule that explicitly emits the belief content/polarity as an `ActorEpistemicEffect`;
4. verified actor, time, rule, pre-state and provenance;
5. no prohibited source promotion.

A belief may be objectively supported, contradicted or unresolved. Objective status never creates, edits or deletes the belief automatically.

### 5.6 Uncertainty admission gate

`UNCERTAINTY` requires a generated V3 epistemic effect. Missing data, missing authority, analyst disagreement, low structural evidence, or renderer hesitation do not create actor uncertainty.

Uncertainty may coexist with belief when separate generated effects support both. No hidden numeric confidence is inferred.

### 5.7 Nonknowledge admission gate

`NONKNOWLEDGE` requires an explicit generated actor epistemic effect. It may represent a generated recognition such as lack of information, failure to know, or explicit ignorance within the governed claim scope.

Absence of a knowledge record is insufficient. A missing or blocked epistemic state remains `EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN` and first-person nonknowledge is not permitted from that absence.

## 6. Epistemic transitions and causal inputs

### 6.1 `ActorEpistemicEffect`

A lawful V3 causal rule may request an ADP-017 transition through the semantic equivalent of:

```text
ActorEpistemicEffect {
  effect_id,
  actor_id,
  claim_ref,
  stance_kind,

  accessibility_effect?,
  owning_rule_id,
  owning_rule_version,
  owning_rule_hash,
  effective_causal_time
}
```

`stance_operation` is one of:

- `ADD`;
- `WITHDRAW`;
- `SUPERSEDE_EXACT_RECORD`;
- `NO_CHANGE`.

No bulk “make coherent” operation exists.

### 6.2 Admission decision states

Every attempted epistemic mutation yields one of:

- `APPLIED_EPISTEMIC_TRANSITION`;
- `NO_EPISTEMIC_TRANSITION` — lawful causal basis exists but no epistemic effect was emitted;
- `DEFERRED_MISSING_EPISTEMIC_BASIS` — required actor-facing basis, claim semantics, or separately owned rule authority is absent;
- `DENIED_NON_ACTOR_SOURCE` — basis is structural/analyst/session/render material rather than lawful actor causal material;
- `DENIED_UNAUTHORIZED_PROMOTION` — attempted objective-fact, memory, transcript, provenance or renderer promotion exceeds authority;
- `BLOCKED_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` — IDs/hashes/pre-state/rule/provenance do not verify.

`NO_EPISTEMIC_TRANSITION` is lawful. An event can occur, be remembered, and still produce no actor knowledge/belief record unless the governing rule says otherwise.

### 6.3 No hidden temporal mutation

No stance or claim-accessibility state changes merely because:

- wall-clock time passes;
- a yearly loop executes;
- V2 does not mention the claim;
- transcript retention changes;
- a renderer paraphrases a claim;
- an analyst updates a structural explanation.

A causal transition is required.

## 7. Memory interaction law — ADP-016 dependency

ADP-016 remains sovereign over memory formation, retention, degradation, feature availability, recall/reactivation and `memory_accessibility_state`.

ADP-017 consumes only the exact outputs ADP-016 authorizes:

- `memory_id`;
- representation kind;
- source-bound feature states;
- retention state;
- current memory-accessibility state;
- uncertainty state;
- provenance/integrity;
- analyst-only causal-trace designation.

### 7.1 Memory does not self-promote

Binding law:

```text
MEMORY_EXISTS
  != CLAIM_EXISTS
  != ACTOR_KNOWS
  != ACTOR_BELIEVES
  != ACTOR_MAY_CLAIM
```

An `ACCESSIBLE` `EVENT_REFERENTIAL_MEMORY` may be cited by a lawful V3 epistemic effect as one actor-facing basis. It does not itself create a stance.

A `CAUSAL_TRACE_MEMORY`, including pressure-flow groove/conductance history with `NOT_APPLICABLE_CAUSAL_TRACE`, is never a first-person claim basis merely because it causally affects later behavior.

### 7.2 Memory-access change does not automatically erase stance

A memory becoming inaccessible does not automatically delete an independently generated knowledge/belief stance. Conversely, a persisted stance does not guarantee current claim accessibility.

Current claim accessibility must record exact `accessibility_basis_refs[]`. If the **only** lawful accessibility basis for a claim is an ADP-016 memory feature that becomes `INACCESSIBLE` or `UNAVAILABLE`, claim permission derived from that basis must fail closed until another exact actor-facing generated basis exists.

If an independent generated actor-access basis remains valid, the claim may remain accessible under that basis. Implementations may not assume either outcome without the recorded basis graph.

### 7.3 No analyst rehydration

If ADP-016 marks source content `UNAVAILABLE`, ADP-017 cannot restore claim access by reading:

- objective event history;
- `GeneratedPersonRecord`;
- structural provenance;
- analyst pressure accounts;
- V2 transcripts;
- persona/backstory prose.

Re-acquisition requires a **new actor-facing generated/admitted causal event** and, where memory is involved, the ADP-016 source-bound transition required by that authority.

## 8. Current claim accessibility

### 8.1 Accessibility states

`current_claim_accessibility` is one of:

- `CLAIM_ACCESSIBLE` — exact semantic claim is currently available to the actor under verified generated basis;
- `CLAIM_PARTIALLY_ACCESSIBLE` — only exact declared subclaim/component refs are accessible;
- `CLAIM_INACCESSIBLE` — an actor epistemic stance/history may exist, but the exact claim is not currently available for first-person use;
- `CLAIM_ACCESS_UNKNOWN` — authority/integrity is insufficient to assert current access.

For `CLAIM_PARTIALLY_ACCESSIBLE`, the state must enumerate exact accessible component claim refs or field bindings. V2 may not reconstruct the inaccessible whole from context or plausibility.

### 8.2 Accessibility is actor state, not semantic truth

Claim accessibility may be changed only by a lawful V3 causal/epistemic transition with exact basis. It is not determined by:

- objective truth;
- analyst system access;
- structural provenance reachability;
- the presence of a memory record alone;
- a V2 keyword hook;
- transcript recency;
- a prompt needing an answer.

## 9. Misrecognition law

### 9.1 Misrecognition is a relation over already-generated state

ADP-017 authorizes an analyst-side `MISRECOGNITION_SUPPORTED` relation only when all are true:

1. an exact active generated `BELIEF` or actor interpretation claim exists;
2. that belief has verified causal parentage and rule authority;
3. an exact objective generated fact/claim relevant to the same governed referent/scope exists;
4. a versioned comparison/claim-relation rule establishes incompatibility or material mismatch;
5. both sides pass integrity checks.

If any condition is missing, the relation is `MISRECOGNITION_UNRESOLVED` or `NOT_EVALUATED`, not a fabricated belief.

### 9.2 Misrecognition is not actor meta-knowledge

`MISRECOGNITION_SUPPORTED` is analyst-only by default. It may not be placed in the actor-safe render envelope as “you are wrong,” “I know I am rationalizing,” or equivalent first-person awareness unless a separate generated actor epistemic transition establishes that meta-claim.

### 9.3 Structural causation does not create the mistaken description

The machine may possess exact structural provenance showing that a pressure helped cause an actor outcome while the actor has no generated explanation for it. In that case:

- the structural causal provenance remains analyst-only;
- no local explanation is invented;
- no belief is synthesized merely to make the actor “misrecognize” the cause;
- the actor may remain `EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN`, uncertain, nonknowing, or possess some separately generated interpretation.

This is the hard implementation of `Determination is not transparency.`

## 10. Contradiction and coexisting-belief law

### 10.1 No forced global coherence

Multiple active stance records may coexist for one claim or across lawfully related contradictory claims when each has independent generated authority. Examples include:

- `BELIEF(P)` + generated `UNCERTAINTY(P)`;
- `BELIEF(P)` + `BELIEF(not-P)` where exact claim relation authority establishes contradiction;
- time-scoped prior `KNOWLEDGE(P@t1)` + later `BELIEF(not-P@t2)`;
- beliefs tied to distinct relationships/strata/contexts that are incompatible when jointly considered.

The state machine must not choose a scalar winner, average them into one confidence, silently delete the older record, or generate reconciliation.

### 10.2 Transition law

A stance remains active until a lawful transition explicitly withdraws or supersedes that exact record. Historical transition receipts remain immutable after withdrawal.

If a later event generates revision, the revision can withdraw/supersede exact records according to the emitting rule. “Human consistency” is not a rule.

### 10.3 V2 consumption

If contradictory/coexisting stance records are simultaneously accessible and separately permitted, the actor-safe envelope may carry them both. V2 may later select or express them only under its own accepted phenomenology/expression rules and may not turn multiplicity into certainty, consensus or hidden synthesis.

## 11. First-person claim-permission law

### 11.1 Permission classes

ADP-017 derives only semantic permission, not wording. A permission is one of:

- `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_KNOWLEDGE`;
- `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_BELIEF`;
- `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_UNCERTAINTY`;
- `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_NONKNOWLEDGE`;
- `DENY_FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM`;
- `DEFER_MISSING_AUTHORITY`.

The deterministic minimum mapping is:

| active stance | current accessibility | semantic permission |
|---|---|---|
| `KNOWLEDGE` | `CLAIM_ACCESSIBLE` | `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_KNOWLEDGE` |
| `BELIEF` | `CLAIM_ACCESSIBLE` | `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_BELIEF` |
| `UNCERTAINTY` | `CLAIM_ACCESSIBLE` | `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_UNCERTAINTY` |
| `NONKNOWLEDGE` | `CLAIM_ACCESSIBLE` | `PERMIT_FIRST_PERSON_NONKNOWLEDGE` |
| any | `CLAIM_INACCESSIBLE` | `DENY_FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM` |
| any | `CLAIM_ACCESS_UNKNOWN` | `DEFER_MISSING_AUTHORITY` |
| no governed stance | any | `DENY_FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM` unless a positive separately governed nonknowledge stance exists |

`CLAIM_PARTIALLY_ACCESSIBLE` yields permission only for the exact enumerated accessible subclaim/component refs. It never grants permission for the whole claim.

### 11.2 No certainty widening

A downstream consumer may narrow or omit permission but may never widen it:

- belief permission does not become knowledge/factual permission;
- uncertainty permission does not become belief or knowledge permission;
- nonknowledge permission does not license guessing;
- denied/deferred material does not become a “plausible” first-person statement;
- accessible claim A + accessible claim B does not authorize inferred claim C unless C independently appears as a permitted claim.

### 11.3 Permission is snapshot-specific

Permission is bound to the exact V3 epistemic-state/checkpoint hash and effective causal time. A later V3 transition requires a new envelope. A cached V2/session copy cannot claim current authority after its V3 source hash is stale.

- `MISRECOGNITION_SUPPORTED` labels unless actor meta-knowledge is separately generated;
- objective relationship/household/genealogy state beyond actor-perceived/actor-accessible views;
- pending/rejected external-interaction proposals;
- V2 transcript/session state as a source of V3 truth;
- renderer prompt/model/audit material as actor biography.

`opaque_provenance_refs[]` may preserve audit locators/hashes where the V2/application contract requires them, but V2 may not dereference them for behavioral, belief, memory, relationship or persona authority.

### 12.3 V2 monotone-narrowing law

V2 may:

- select a subset of allowed entries;
- omit an allowed claim;
- preserve the supplied stance/uncertainty/nonknowledge boundary;
- render wording under separately accepted V2 rules.

V2 may not:

- add a claim not present in the envelope;
- raise its permission class;
- infer a hidden claim by joining permitted entries;
- dereference opaque provenance;
- convert an objective ref/hash into actor knowledge;
- use transcript persistence, user mention, keyword hooks or prior rendered text to widen access;
- convert analyst misrecognition metadata into actor self-awareness;
- mutate V3 epistemic state.

A rendered statement remains expression, not evidence that the actor had the state before rendering and not a causal state change afterward unless a separately governed in-world interaction is admitted by V3.

## 13. Analyst-only exclusions and system truth

The following distinctions are mandatory:

### `ANALYST_ONLY_OBJECTIVE_TRUTH`

The system possesses exact generated truth, but no generated actor epistemic/access state permits first-person use. It remains available for replay, causal processing and analyst/audit surfaces only.

### `ANALYST_ONLY_CAUSAL_PROVENANCE`

The machine can trace structural or developmental causes not actor-generatedly known. Causal reachability never grants actor access.

### `ANALYST_ONLY_MISRECOGNITION_RELATION`

The system can compare an already-generated belief to objective truth and label a supported mismatch. The comparison is not actor awareness.

### `ANALYST_ONLY_CAUSAL_TRACE`

Nondeclarative ADP-016 causal-trace memory may alter later causation without being consciously recallable or claimable.

### `SYSTEM_EPISTEMIC_UNKNOWN`

The machine lacks authority to say what the actor knows/believes. This is not evidence of actor ignorance and cannot be rendered as such.

Analyst-only state may affect later V3 causation only through the authority that already owns that causal state. ADP-017 does not turn analyst access into a new actor-facing event.

### 14.3 Replay law

Given the same:

- immutable V3 causal event ledger / generated fact state;
- exact ADP-016 memory objects and transition states;
- canonical claim registry/content hashes;
- epistemic effect and transition records;
- owning V3 rule IDs/versions/hashes;

replay must reproduce the same:

- active stance sets;
- objective relations used for validation;
- accessibility states/bases;
- analyst misrecognition relations;
- permission records;

- hidden wall clock;
- filesystem order;
- mutable `latest` pointers;
- unrecorded network lookup;
- V2 transcript retention/summary;
- prompt wording;
- renderer/model randomness;
- keyword hook selection;
- unrecorded LLM interpretation;
- analyst free-text annotation.

Missing/mismatched replay material yields blocked/unknown/denied state rather than heuristic reconstruction.

## 15. Fail-closed states

Implementations SHALL preserve these semantic distinctions even if serialized labels differ:

| state | meaning | required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| `EPISTEMIC_STATE_UNKNOWN` | no governed actor stance authority | do not infer knowledge, belief, uncertainty or nonknowledge |
| `CLAIM_ACCESS_UNKNOWN` | current actor claim access cannot be established | no first-person claim; preserve reason |
| `DEFERRED_MISSING_EPISTEMIC_BASIS` | required causal/actor-facing basis or rule missing | no mutation; no fallback belief |
| `DENIED_NON_ACTOR_SOURCE` | attempted basis is structural/analyst/session/render material | reject effect |
| `DENIED_UNAUTHORIZED_PROMOTION` | attempted truth/memory/transcript/provenance→stance shortcut | reject effect |
| `UNKNOWN_CLAIM_ID` | no valid canonical claim | reject/no mutation |
| `CLAIM_HASH_MISMATCH` | claim ID/content disagree | integrity failure |
| `MISSING_OBJECTIVE_SUPPORT_FOR_KNOWLEDGE` | requested `KNOWLEDGE` lacks exact objective support | reject knowledge effect; do not silently downgrade into belief |
| `MISSING_ACTOR_FACING_BASIS` | objective truth exists but actor acquisition basis absent | reject knowledge/belief effect |
| `MISSING_MEMORY_BASIS` | effect cites missing/invalid ADP-016 memory | block/defer |
| `MEMORY_BASIS_INACCESSIBLE` | sole cited access basis no longer actor-accessible | deny/defer claim permission; do not rehydrate from ledger |
| `UNAUTHORIZED_CAUSAL_TRACE_EXPORT` | nondeclarative trace offered as conscious claim | reject first-person export |
| `MISRECOGNITION_UNRESOLVED` | actor belief or objective/comparison authority insufficient | do not invent mismatch or belief |
| `UNAUTHORIZED_ANALYST_LEAK` | hidden objective/provenance/misrecognition content offered to V2 | exclude/deny |
| `UNAUTHORIZED_V2_WIDENING` | V2 adds/raises/infers claim permission | reject render plan/input |
| `STALE_RENDER_ENVELOPE` | envelope source state hash no longer current for requested current-state rendering | reject/rebuild from V3; V2 cache cannot self-update |
| `PRE_STATE_HASH_MISMATCH` | epistemic effect applied to stale state | integrity failure |
| `MISSING_RULE_VERSION` | exact emitting/authority rule unavailable | block/defer |
| `INTEGRITY_FAILURE` | other ID/hash/provenance invariant fails | block mutation and disputed downstream use |

Unknown is not nonknowledge. Inaccessible is not forgotten objective truth. Misrecognition unresolved is not correct recognition. Denied claim permission is not evidence that the proposition is false.

## 19. Governing-Spirit check

**GOVERNING_SPIRIT_CHECK = APPLICABLE__PASS**

**Genuine ambiguity:** hard architecture requires actor knowledge/belief/accessibility separation but does not uniquely require either (a) one convenience-oriented scalar/enum that resolves a claim to a single “actor knows/doesn't know” state or (b) orthogonal, provenance-bound stance/access/permission records capable of preserving contradiction and opacity. Hard authority also leaves open whether “misrecognition” should be generated whenever structural causation is hidden or only recognized when a generated actor belief already exists.

**Lawful alternatives:**

1. a flattened current-state representation with one winning epistemic label and consumer-friendly inferred misrecognition; or
2. a sparse `ActorEpistemicStateSet` with independent generated stances, exact current accessibility, exact first-person permission, analyst-only objective/misrecognition relations, and no belief invention.

**Chosen design:** alternative 2.

**Decisive Governing-Spirit principles:** `Determination is not transparency`; actor opacity and misrecognition rather than analyst omniscience; contradiction/multiplicity without forced coherence; uncertainty and lawful absence without fabrication; dynamic causal history over static classification; never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority; minimum sufficient machinery.

**Constraint on the tie-break:** the Governing Spirit supplies **no belief content**. Misrecognition is represented only after hard generated causal authority establishes the actor belief/interpretation itself.
