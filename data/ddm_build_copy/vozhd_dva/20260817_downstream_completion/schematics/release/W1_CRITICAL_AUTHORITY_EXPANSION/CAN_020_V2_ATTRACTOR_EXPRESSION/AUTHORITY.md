## 7. Uncertainty, competition, transition and no-winner law

The output plan is **monotone non-strengthening** relative to V3 input:

```text
V2 specificity <= supplied V3 specificity
V2 certainty   <= supplied V3 certainty
V2 claim scope <= ADP-017 / render-boundary permission
```

Therefore:

1. competing evidence remains separately referenceable; V2 may not sum, normalize, rank or choose a winner unless that exact relation is already supplied by V3;
2. underdetermination remains underdetermination;
3. transitioning evidence may preserve prior, current and emergent tendencies simultaneously where supplied; no stable label is inferred;
4. explicit V3 uncertainty may be preserved or expressed more cautiously/withheld, never decreased by V2;
5. absence of a label does not trigger matching;
6. absence of a final interpretation does not block ordinary rendering; it yields label-free or valid-empty attractor expression;
7. multiple same-authority interpretation refs without governed V3 precedence remain multiple/withheld; V2 does not select one;
8. a stale prior interpretation cannot override a newer supplied trajectory state merely because it is easier to render.

## 8. Actor-accessibility law

ADP-017 remains sovereign over actor epistemic stance, claim accessibility and first-person claim permission.

CAN-020 distinguishes:

- **expression-shaping permission:** exact V3 render-boundary permission for supplied attractor material to constrain or inform noncausal expression; and
- **first-person claim permission:** exact ADP-017 permission for the actor to assert a proposition such as “I am Type X,” “I have become X,” or an equivalent self-classification.

V2 may never derive either permission from the existence of an objective V3 record.

A supplied interpretation label without first-person claim permission must not become a first-person identity statement. It may influence expression only if the actor-safe render boundary separately permits that nonclaim use. If no such permission exists, CAN-020 withholds it.

Actor-inaccessible objective attractor evidence, analyst-only interpretation, supporting-history internals and system-only confidence are not renderable merely because they could make the dialogue more psychologically coherent.

A rendered utterance about type does not retroactively prove actor knowledge or update ADP-017 state.
