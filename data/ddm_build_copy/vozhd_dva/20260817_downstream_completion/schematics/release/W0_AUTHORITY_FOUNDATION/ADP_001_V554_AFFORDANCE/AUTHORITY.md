- `VALID_EMPTY`
- `INTEGRITY_FAILURE`

### 9.2 Precedence law

Evaluation order is binding:

**P0 — integrity precondition.**  
Schema/hash/reference/fact-coherence/reference-contract corruption prevents semantic evaluation. Result: `INTEGRITY_FAILURE`. It is not semantic denial and must not be serialized as F2/B1 structural meaning.

**P1 — exact producer precedence first.**  
Where a producer-governed authority object explicitly defines precedence among its own exact authority states, that precedence is followed by reference. V554 does not invent a new upstream tie-break.

**P2 — applicable explicit target-use denial / hard negative.**  
A trustworthy exact denial or hard negative authority that applies to this candidate and target use blocks F1 regardless of positive scores, magnitude, readiness, file presence or other positive-looking context. Preserve the exact denial ref.

**P3 — required static authority must be complete and resolved.**  
If any required static authority is absent, unknown or in unresolved conflict, no F1 exists. `MISSING` and `UNKNOWN_OR_CONFLICTING` remain distinct. Neither may be laundered into `DEFERRED` unless the missing fact is explicitly a legal runtime-bound predicate under K01–K03 rather than static authority.

**P4 — conjunctive positive eligibility.**  
Only after P0–P3 pass may the exact signature produce `ELIGIBLE`.

**P5 — runtime-deferred F2 evaluation.**  
K01–K03 may coexist with F1 and make immediate runtime use deferred. They do not reduce or increase F1 eligibility and they do not assert satisfaction.

**P6 — informational constraint.**  
K04 is preserved after the eligibility decision. It cannot create positive authority, overrule denial, or become a numeric weight.

### 9.3 Tie behavior

- exact duplicate authority refs are de-duplicated by stable ID/version only;
- distinct simultaneous structural facts/affordance candidates remain distinct; they are never averaged, merged or ranked into one winner;
- if exact same-jurisdiction authority objects conflict, use an explicit producer-governed precedence object if one exists;
- absent such authority, disposition is `NONASSERTABLE_UNKNOWN_OR_CONFLICTING_STATIC_AUTHORITY`; no F1;
- if an independently trustworthy explicit denial and an unrelated missing authority coexist, the primary semantic disposition is denial while the missing requirement remains separately preserved; if the denial's own applicability cannot be resolved because of missing identity/authority, the result is nonassertable rather than a guessed denial;
- no temporal-signature downgrade, score comparison, recency heuristic, majority vote or descriptive specificity rule is a legal tie-break.

### Negative / denial

### Missing

Only K01–K03 are authorized runtime-deferred kinds in tranche one. Deferred means downstream resolution is required against immutable exact authority; it never means static authority was missing.

### Valid-empty

A coherent V554 run with zero candidates that satisfy the rule is `VALID_EMPTY`. It emits no dummy F1, no minimum-count filler and no guessed seed. Exact nonpositive context may still be published where governed.

### Not applicable

An input that does not belong to an admissible source class or does not match one of the exact signature grammars is `NOT_APPLICABLE_SIGNATURE_OR_SOURCE_CLASS`, not semantic denial.
