Result: contradiction/coexistence survives without collapse or duplicate ownership.

### Life B — temporal gap / proxy edge

10. A rerender with different RNG has a different hash and cannot inherit the first candidate's admission.

Result: objective truth, actor-accessible truth, expression, transcript and causal history remain distinct.
