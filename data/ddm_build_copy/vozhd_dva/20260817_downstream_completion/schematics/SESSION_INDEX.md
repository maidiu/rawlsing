## Launch discipline

- Only one seam is active at a time.
- Inside that seam, at least two independent perspectives should normally be launched together: forward and backward.
- High-risk seams S02 and S03 may add a third hostile/ontology critic in the same breakout round.
- All workers in the active seam receive the same frozen upstream law but different analytical perspectives.
- Workers return proposals to the active session workspace; they do not edit neighboring session rulings.
- Vozhd Dva then reconvenes the active seam, compares proposals, resolves object-grain/responsibility collisions, and writes the accepted ruling in `schematics/synthesis/`.
- Only after that ruling is frozen does the next seam become `READY_TO_LAUNCH`.

## Why serial seam progression is required

The accepted output of each seam becomes the lawful input of the next. Launching S01 before S00 is adjudicated would force S01 workers to assume a V553 output grain that S00 may immediately change. The same dependency repeats throughout the chain. Serial seam progression therefore reduces speculative ontology and downstream rework while preserving parallel independent thought where it is useful: *inside* each seam.

## Progression

`R0-01 integrity closeout → S00 breakout → S00 synthesis → S01 breakout → S01 synthesis → S02 ... → S07 synthesis`

A later seam may be consulted during backward reasoning as a consumer requirement, but it is not itself launched or allowed to freeze architecture before its upstream input law exists.
