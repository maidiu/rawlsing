## 10. Required jurisdiction courts

### 10.1 Generated truth vs phenomenology vs expression

Define exact epistemic/ontological layers.

At minimum distinguish:

- `GENERATED_RUNTIME_FACT` / V3 causal truth;
- deterministic or governed V3-derived actor view;
- V2 phenomenological projection, if separately justified;
- V2 expression plan/constraints, if justified;
- rendered language/action surface;
- session/transcript state;
- application metadata.

Do not let a rendered sentence become evidence that the actor causally believes or experienced the sentence's content.

### 10.2 Actor knowledge / accessible memory / ignorance

A convincing persona cannot be omniscient.

Determine which layer owns:

- what happened to the actor;
- what the actor remembers;
- what is accessible now;
- what the actor knows or believes;
- what the actor is uncertain about;
- what the analyst/system knows but the actor cannot articulate.

Default law: persistent actor knowledge/memory/belief that affects later causal behavior belongs V3, not V2.

V2 may gate expression of supplied actor-accessible material; it may not infer hidden knowledge from raw structural priors or analyst-only causal traces.

### 10.3 Life container / life season / Today State

Determine whether these are:

- deterministic derived views from V3 history/current state;
- bounded V2 render/session projections;
- obsolete duplicate biography;
- or some mixture with exact boundary.

No second stored biography.

No generic fallback life facts.

### 10.4 Salience / appraisal / boundaries / effort / response moves

For each, determine whether it is:

- causal actor state V3 must own;
- deterministic derivation from V3 state;
- V2 expression-only planning;
- caller/session constraint;
- or unjustified duplicate state.

Do not bury causal psychology in a V2 "conversation" label.

### 10.5 Relationship memory and asymmetry

Separate:

- generated causal relationship history/state;
- actor memory of that relationship;
- conversation transcript/context;
- application/session continuity;
- presentation-only relationship framing.

V2 may not make a rendered prior conversation into generated causal relationship history unless it re-enters V3 through explicit event admission.

### 10.6 Conversation modes and causal feedback

Adjudicate whether the system requires at least two explicit modes:

1. **noncausal presentation/session interaction** — user converses with a representation of a generated person at a snapshot; expression/session state does not mutate generated life;
2. **causal in-world interaction** — an utterance/action is declared part of the generated timeline and therefore must return through typed V3 external-event admission before future causal state changes.

You may choose a different minimal architecture, but the causal distinction must be explicit and machine-enforceable.

- classify type from dialogue style and feed it upstream;

- convert V556 F2 into personality;
- force every utterance through a fixed type stereotype;
- erase competing/uncertain attractor evidence.

Determine what useful recovered Enneagram expression machinery survives.

### 10.8 LLM role

The LLM may render language under bounded state/constraint input.

Adjudicate whether it may also propose noncausal response moves or surface forms, and what deterministic/governed admission is needed.

It may not:
- invent biography;
- decide causal state;
- fill missing authority with plausible psychology;
- mutate relationships/life history because a generated sentence sounded consequential;
- silently promote prompt content into actor truth.
