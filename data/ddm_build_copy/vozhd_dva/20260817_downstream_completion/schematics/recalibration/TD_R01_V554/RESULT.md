## 3. Fourteen terminal-demand questions — V554 answers

### 3.1 Nine-strata field

V554 assigns no stratum. It consumes and preserves the TD-R00 `GOVERNED_STRUCTURAL_REFERENCE_SET`, including 0..n role-qualified finite-stratum refs where governed. A V554 rule may require a structural position before the affordance is usable. If that position is actor-specific and not yet generated, V554 emits a machine-readable applicability condition equivalent to `REQUIRES_RUNTIME_POSITION(<exact governed position requirement>)`; it never emits `ACTOR_HAS_POSITION`.

### 3.2 Relational direction

Structural source→target direction is preserved from governed earlier directionality/position refs. V554 temporal eligibility may refer to V553 change, but `increasing/decreasing` temporal delta never reconstructs structural direction. Two simultaneous exact pressure keys can yield two distinct V554 affordances; neither is averaged, winner-selected or made a conflict merely because both exist.

### 3.3 Time

V554 preserves exact V553 temporal key/observation/relation/limit identity whenever time is part of the V554 rule. It may also state that runtime applicability requires temporal resolution against a particular V553 key. It does **not** choose `DIRECT_TEMPORAL_SUPPORT`, `LATEST_PRIOR_OBSERVATION_PROXY`, `EARLIEST_POST_QUERY_PROXY`, `EARLIEST_POST_BIRTH_ANCHOR` or `NO_LAWFUL_TEMPORAL_ANCHOR`. Those are downstream resolver decisions. Evidence time remains unchanged.

### 3.4 Mediation

This is V554's unique bounded contribution: a structural basis can be admitted as a **mediation-input affordance** under explicit rule authority. V554 also states applicability requirements/limits that must be satisfied before a route may use it. V555 owns the mediation route/channel and route-specific effects; V3 owns realized mediation.

### 3.5 Orientation-to-strata

V554 preserves structural/stratum source/target refs and expresses any required generated runtime position as an unresolved applicability condition. It does not infer identification, alienation, dependence, resistance, aspiration, shame, pride, debt, obligation or other actor orientation.

### 3.6 Absolute Horizon

Generic Horizon constraint refs remain generic. Explicit Absolute-Horizon authority is preserved only if an upstream governed designation exists. If a V554 rule genuinely requires an AH designation and none exists, the result is typed missing authority and no positive affordance. V554 never infers AH from religion, ideology, wording or finite-stratum membership.

### 3.7 Pressure-flow

V554 provides exact eligible structural basis IDs, source/target/position/time refs and applicability conditions. V555 can transform these into structural paths; V556 can transform paths into pressure-flow edges/interactions. V554 does not create graph edges or interaction topology.

### 3.8 Attractors

V554 protects multiple historically conditioned pathways by preserving multiple distinct affordances and machine-readable conditions/negative authority. It creates no attractor candidate, basin, Enneagram type or actor outcome.

### 3.9 Vitality / organism boundary

A structural mediation-input affordance is not need, desire or vitality. V554 creates no organism state. It only preserves the structural conditions V3 may later encounter.

### 3.10 Genealogy

V554 objects are actor-independent and immutable. Parent 1, Parent 2, partner and child contexts can each resolve the same structural prior repository against their own generated time/place/position state. Any actor-specific family/household/sect/class/party/institution binding remains downstream-generated.

### 3.11 Epistemic separation

`BOUNDED_STRUCTURAL_AFFORDANCE` and `STRUCTURAL_MEDIATION_APPLICABILITY_CONDITION` are **derived structural priors**, not source evidence, runtime facts or rendered expression. Every row names the V554 rule/version and resolves its authoritative upstream objects. V557/V558 later add runtime publication/governance; V3 creates generated facts; V2 renders expression.

### 3.12 Negative authority

V554 preserves upstream denial, absence, gap, noncomparability and missing authority by exact reference, and derives only the **affected-use applicability effect** needed by its own rule. Build/schema/ref mismatch, synthetic fallback attempts, actor-inference attempts, geographic imputation attempts and runtime-proxy attempts are completion-court failures, not semantic conditions.

### 3.13 DAG reuse

V555/V556/V558 may directly resolve V552 structural facts, V552J admission/permission, earlier semantic/directionality/position authority and V553 temporal objects. V554 does not copy those meanings. Its semantic rows point to the exact authoritative objects and reference-contract versions needed to interpret the V554 eligibility/condition.

### 3.14 No-starvation proof

Removing F1 forces V555 to decide for itself whether an arbitrary upstream structural object is a lawful mediation input, which is precisely the missing V554 transformation. Removing F2 forces V555/ContextResolver to reconstruct applicability from prose or guess actor/time/location positions. Removing B1 forces the immediate consumer to rematch V554 semantic objects to candidate/use scopes and nonpositive conditions. Independent F3 and F4 families fail the no-bloat proof and are removed.
