## 0. Governing correction

The recovered V3/V2 snapshot is important because it is the latest extant V3/V2 implementation/design presently available. That fact does **not** make V3/V2 a starting point, a constraint on V550+, or a source of terminal-demand authority.

The governing order is:

`TOTAL RAWLSING VISION`
`→ MAXIMAL LAWFUL V550+ STRUCTURAL-WORLD IMPLEMENTATION`
`→ V3/V2 DESIGNED OR REDESIGNED AS NECESSARY TO REALIZE THAT WORLD WITHOUT LOSS`
`→ RECOVERED V3/V2 MECHANISMS REUSED WHERE THEY FIT THAT RESULT`.

The accepted V553–V558 architecture was derived from terminal demand and remains sovereign. No V3/V2 mechanism, schema, state vector, yearly loop, LLM pass, attractor model, conversation design, or compatibility concern may narrow, simplify, reinterpret, or reduce the function of V550+ even slightly.

If maximizing the finished Rawlsing machine requires substantial or total redesign of V3 and/or V2, then V3/V2 must change however much is necessary.

A serendipitous bridge between extant V3/V2 and accepted V550+ is highly desirable where it exists. It is an implementation advantage, not the primary reasoning axis.

## 1. Evidentiary status of the recovered snapshot

The user-supplied historical whole-project snapshot has split authority:

- its V5/DDM side is stale and nonbinding relative to accepted TD-R00–TD-R04;
- its V3/V2 side is the latest extant implementation/design evidence available;
- V3/V2 therefore deserves careful recovery so useful machinery is not destroyed through ignorance;
- V3/V2 does **not** receive a presumption of architectural survival.

Recovered V3/V2 answers the question:

> What machinery already exists that may save us from unnecessary reinvention, and what intended capabilities must we be careful not to lose accidentally?

It does **not** answer:

> What should V550+ produce, what should the total system mean, or what must V3/V2 remain?

## 2. No upstream accommodation law

TD-R05 and all later workers MUST NOT reason backward from extant V3/V2 limitations to reduce or reshape accepted V550+ semantics.

Forbidden examples include:

- dropping a V553 temporal distinction because extant V3 has no field for it;
- flattening V554/V555 mediation because extant V3 expects simpler channels;
- reducing V556 n-ary interaction/topology because extant V3 only supports pairwise or scalar flow;
- collapsing nine-strata or Absolute-Horizon information into extant V3's older field model;
- converting missing/noncomparable/negative authority into defaults because extant V3 expects populated numeric state;
- replacing mixed-producer graph identity with extant V3 slot names;
- choosing yearly integration or LLM proposal semantics because they are already implemented if a stronger terminal architecture requires something else.

The adaptation direction is one-way:

`accepted structural world → V3/V2 adaptation`.

Never:

`extant V3/V2 limitations → structural-world reduction`.

## 3. What “do not throw out unnecessarily” means

The recovered implementation must still be reconstructed carefully. Every substantive extant mechanism must receive an explicit disposition so capability is not lost by accident:

- `REUSE_UNCHANGED_IF_FULLY_COMPATIBLE`;
- `ADAPT_TO_TERMINAL_ARCHITECTURE`;
- `REPLACE_WITH_STRONGER_MECHANISM`;
- `DEFER_MISSING_AUTHORITY`;
- `REMOVE_AS_OBSOLETE_OR_REDUNDANT`.

This is an **anti-amnesia / anti-reinvention requirement**, not architectural deference.

There is no burden to preserve a mechanism merely because it exists. There is a burden to understand what capability it served before removing or replacing it, and to prove that the terminal design still supplies that capability where the total vision requires it.

A recovered mechanism may survive because:

1. it already implements a terminally required function well;
2. it can consume the new V550+ products without information loss;
3. adapting it is cleaner than rebuilding equivalent machinery.

A recovered mechanism must change or disappear when:

1. it limits the information or causal possibilities supplied by V550+;
2. it encodes stale V5 assumptions;
3. it duplicates authority now owned elsewhere;
4. it relies on ungoverned shortcuts or defaults;
5. a stronger architecture is required by terminal personhood;
6. preserving it would force the rest of Rawlsing to bend around an implementation accident.
