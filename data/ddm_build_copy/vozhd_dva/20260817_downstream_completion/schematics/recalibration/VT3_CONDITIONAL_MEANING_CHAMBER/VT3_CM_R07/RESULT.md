## 0. Holding

The broad V550 determinicity suite does **not** need to remain canonical merely because it is expensive or already materialized. The court found no irreducible terminal semantic requirement for canonical MI/Cramér's-V summaries, outlier summaries, secondary-predictor-gain summaries, or recursive branch trees **as those artifacts currently exist**.

However, a sparse architecture is lossless only under a stricter condition than “store a conditional response distribution plus provenance.” A minimal positive, unary `condition=value -> response distribution` object is insufficient. It loses lawful meaning in intersections, Simpson-style reversals, low-support historically informative cells, typed absence/noncomparability, actors carrying several simultaneous generated position facts, and future authority that admits a condition not materialized today.

The economized architecture survives this court only if the sparse conditional-evidence family preserves, at minimum:

1. **exact empirical scope** — source/family, wave/canonical wave or equivalent time anchor, country/native geography, question/variable identity, and response-semantics authority;
2. **complete response support** — the full categorical/ordinal response distribution, not a dominant response, mean, direction, score, branch label, or “best predictor”;
3. **conditioning identity as a governed set of predicates**, not a single string field; the set must be able to represent zero predicates for a baseline distribution, one predicate for an ordinary position-conditioned distribution, and multiple simultaneous predicates for governed intersections;
4. **weighted and unweighted sufficient statistics together**, with denominator basis, weight status/provenance, and no silent substitution of one for the other;
5. **support/uncertainty state without deletion** — low-support evidence remains historically present even when causal/pressure publication is denied;
6. **typed negative/limit authority** — absent variable, not asked, unavailable source field, valid-empty, unknown, unresolved, noncomparable, denied, and forbidden inference cannot collapse into “no row”;
7. **epistemic class and use permission** — a population association is evidence/structural prior and never becomes actor identity, actor belief, psyche, biography, or generated position merely because V3 knows a matching actor position;
8. **reconstructibility for future governed questions** — either a retained respondent-level evidence substrate or an exact immutable reference to equivalent authoritative respondent-level material must survive. Without it, a later-authorized condition or conjunction that was not precomputed cannot be recovered from sparse marginals.

The court therefore rejects two opposite mistakes:

- retaining the current broad determinicity suite as canonical because it exists; and
- deleting its recomputation substrate or low-support/negative-state meaning merely because current downstream consumers do not request those distinctions.

The correct no-loss boundary is: **canonical empirical sufficient statistics + typed limits/permissions + reconstructible respondent evidence; diagnostics are recomputable views.**

This is a falsification result, not a replacement architecture blueprint. Where the report states a missing field/object class, it states only the minimum semantic requirement exposed by a failed fixture.

## 2. What the existing V550 artifacts actually preserve

### 2.1 `indicator_answer_matrix` is the empirical core of unary conditioning

`indicator_answer_matrix_rows()` builds a contingency table for each available V550 indicator and response value. For each `indicator_name × indicator_value × response_value`, it emits:

- `n_unweighted`;
- `pct_unweighted`;
- `n_weighted`;
- `pct_weighted`.

This is substantially closer to irreducible empirical meaning than a predictor score. A sparse conditional-evidence architecture may replace the physical table, but it must preserve this meaning at equal or greater fidelity.

### 2.2 `position_indicator_summary` is a ranking/summary projection, not sufficient evidence

`indicator_summary_rows()` computes baseline entropy, conditional entropy, entropy reduction, MI, normalized MI, Cramér's V, uplift and a dominant-response summary. These values are useful diagnostics, but they collapse the underlying response topology. Two distinct conditional distributions can have similar or identical scalar association strength.

They therefore cannot substitute for the conditional distribution itself.

### 2.3 `outlier_cohort_summary` is thresholded and dominant-response based

`outlier_cohort_rows()`:

- groups separately by each indicator;
- suppresses values below the support threshold;
- compares only the subgroup dominant response against the baseline dominant response;
- labels `conforms`, `deviates`, or `minority_converges`.

This is an interpretive diagnostic view. It does not preserve the full subgroup distribution, and its support gate can erase exactly the small historically significant minority R07 is required to protect.

### 2.4 `secondary_indicator_summary` proves the need for intersections but is not itself the intersection authority

`secondary_indicator_summary_rows()`:

- ranks candidate primary indicators by MI/Cramér's V/support;
- selects one primary;
- forms a pairwise `primary || secondary` joint key;
- requires at least 50 joint rows;
- emits only positive `secondary_gain`.

This is decisive counterevidence against a unary-only sparse model: interactions can matter beyond marginals. But the current summary is itself selective and lossy. It does not preserve every governed joint cell or every weak interaction.

### 2.5 `recursive_branch_nodes` is especially unsuitable as canonical human/world meaning

`branch_nodes_rows()`:

- greedily chooses the available indicator with highest entropy gain;
- stops at bounded depth (default `max_depth=3`);
- stops under support thresholds or no positive gain;
- descends only into the **two most frequent values** of the chosen split.

A different tie, support threshold, depth, or category prevalence can change the tree. Rare categories disappear. The tree is useful exploration, not lossless evidence.

### 2.6 Current V551 conditional compilation is not a safe sparse precedent

`build_conditional_determinicity_index()` currently drops rows unless support is `sufficient_support`. That is lawful for a downstream pressure-eligible lane only if the underlying evidence survives elsewhere. It is unlawful as the canonical storage rule for all conditional empirical evidence because “underpowered” is not “did not exist.”

The emitted `_conditional_row` also does not make the conditioning variable/value a first-class published identity. A future sparse object cannot rely on `source_row_hash` archaeology to answer “conditioned on what?”

## 4. C. Irreducible missing semantic dimensions / objects

### C1. Governed condition-set identity — irreducible

A condition must not be serialized merely as free text such as `position_conditioning = "income"`.

The empirical object must identify the exact conditioning predicates whose joint cell generated the distribution. The minimum semantic capability is:

- zero conditions: baseline distribution;
- one condition: ordinary position-conditioned evidence;
- multiple conditions: governed intersection evidence;
- stable condition field/requirement identity;
- exact condition value identity;
- source/wave scope and condition-definition authority/provenance.

This is not an argument to materialize all Cartesian products. It is an argument that when a joint condition is lawfully computed, the object must be able to name it exactly.

**Without this dimension, fixtures 2, 3 and 6 fail.**

### C2. Typed conditional-evidence limit/negative state — irreducible

A positive distribution record cannot by itself distinguish:

- variable absent from source;
- question not asked;
- cell valid-empty;
- zero support;
- underpowered observed support;
- missing semantic authority;
- unresolved mapping;
- cross-wave noncomparability;
- denied use;
- forbidden inference.

The architecture therefore requires either:

1. these states as first-class states of the same conditional-evidence family; or
2. a companion typed limit/negative-authority record keyed to the same potential scope/condition.

This court does **not** choose the physical representation. It holds only that “no row” is insufficient.

**Without this dimension, fixtures 4, 7 and 9 fail.**

### C3. Reconstructible respondent-level evidence authority/reference — irreducible for future extension

No finite set of marginal or selected joint distributions can reconstruct every future joint distribution. Two different respondent-level datasets can have identical current marginals and different values for a newly admitted interaction.

Therefore, if future authority may admit a currently unused condition, one of the following must remain available:

- respondent-level evidence with stable respondent joins, responses, applicable condition variables, weights/special states and provenance; or
- an exact immutable reference to an authoritative source from which the same respondent-level joint evidence can be deterministically reconstructed.

The existing `v550_shared_respondent_substrate` is direct evidence that Rawlsing already possesses such a content class for V550/V550J co-execution. R07 does **not** promote that exact file/schema as the final architecture; it identifies the semantic reconstructibility requirement.

**Without this backing evidence, fixture 12 fails and recursive/joint diagnostics cease to be regenerable.**

### C4. Epistemic/use boundary — irreducible

A conditional distribution is not an actor fact. It must remain distinguishable from:

- generated V3 position;
- actor identity;
- actor knowledge;
- actor belief;
- actor response/history;
- rendered expression.

The minimum condition-evidence contract must preserve its source-evidence/structural-prior status and downstream permission state.

**Without this dimension, fixture 10 violates binding V3/ADP-009 law.**

### C5. Weight/denominator basis — irreducible

Weighted and unweighted distributions can point in opposite directions. A percentage without its denominator basis is not the same empirical fact.

The sparse object must therefore retain enough to distinguish and reproduce the two bases. The present `indicator_answer_matrix` already does this with `n_unweighted`, `pct_unweighted`, `n_weighted`, and `pct_weighted`.

**Without this dimension, fixture 8 fails.**

## 6. Regeneration proofs and failure boundaries

### 6.1 MI and Cramér's V

For a condition variable `X` and response `Y`, a complete contingency table `n(x,y)` contains the counts required by the current unweighted MI/Cramér's-V formulas in `ddm_v550_metrics.py`. Therefore the scalar summaries add no irreducible empirical information.

**Boundary:** if the sparse object retains only percentages but not support/denominators, exact regeneration may fail. Counts must survive.

### 6.2 Outlier classification

The current outlier classification uses:

- baseline dominant response/share;
- subgroup dominant response/share;
- support threshold;
- deterministic label logic.

All can be recomputed from baseline and subgroup full distributions plus the versioned support/classification rule.

**Boundary:** if low-support subgroup evidence is deleted, the system can no longer know the subgroup existed; the diagnostic becomes impossible and history has been erased.

### 6.3 Secondary-predictor gain

Secondary gain is a function of conditional entropy under a pairwise joint key relative to the chosen primary indicator.

**Unary marginals are not enough.** XOR is the proof. Regeneration is lossless only if the respondent substrate or exact pairwise joint contingency can be reconstructed.

### 6.4 Recursive branches

A branch tree can be regenerated from respondent-level condition/response rows and the exact algorithm/version. But a precomputed unary or bounded pairwise summary cannot in general reconstruct arbitrary depth-3 joint branches.

The current tree is itself lossy because it follows only the greedy best split and only the two most frequent values. Retaining the tree instead of the underlying joint evidence would preserve the heuristic while discarding alternatives — the reverse of the governing spirit.

### 6.5 Exact historical replay versus semantic replay

If exact historical diagnostic bytes are ever required, the old algorithm/version and deterministic input snapshot must remain addressable. That is an audit/replay requirement, not a reason to promote the diagnostic output as terminal semantic authority.

## 7. E. Governing-spirit violations exposed by the hostile court

The following reductions would violate binding interpretive law:

1. **Best-predictor sovereignty** — choosing the highest MI/Cramér's-V indicator and treating it as the position that “explains” the person. This converts exploratory ranking into ontology.
2. **Support-as-erasure** — dropping a small subgroup because it is not strong enough for pressure publication. Historical existence and causal eligibility are different questions.
3. **Dominant-response flattening** — replacing a multimodal or non-monotonic distribution with the most common answer, mean, direction, branch label, or scalar effect.
4. **Marginal-only simplification** — preserving A and B separately while deleting A×B when the intersection is where the pattern exists.
5. **Aggregate laundering** — allowing Simpson-style pooled reversal to override lawfully available stratified evidence.
6. **Weighted/unweighted substitution** — silently choosing one population representation where the two materially diverge.
7. **Absence collapse** — treating missing variable, not asked, zero support, underpowered, noncomparable, denied, and forbidden inference as one empty state.
8. **Correlation-to-identity leakage** — using conditional empirical association to generate actor class/sect/ethnicity/sex/party/household/belief or to treat a known generated position as proof of a matching response.
9. **Single-position collapse** — forcing actors with multiple simultaneous structural position facts through one “dominant” conditioned profile.
10. **Source/time pooling without authority** — making divergent historical/source distributions disappear in a synthetic average.
11. **Present-consumer amnesia** — deleting respondent-level reconstructibility because no current consumer asks for an unmaterialized condition, thereby preventing future lawful authority from asking a new question.
12. **Diagnostic reification** — making the current greedy branch tree, outlier label, or predictor ranking canonical merely because it is expensive to compute.

These violations all share one structure: the machine becomes cheaper by making the historical world simpler than the evidence permits.

## 8. Relationship to V3 generated positions

The court explicitly rejects a possible misuse of the sparse object.

Suppose V3 has lawfully generated:

- `class = worker`;
- `sect = S`;
- `region = R`.

That fact set may make one or more upstream conditional empirical objects **applicable for structural context**. It does not mean:

- the actor holds the modal response of workers;
- the actor has a probability-derived identity;
- the actor believes what the subgroup most often reported;
- the actor’s class was generated from the subgroup prevalence;
- A×B×C evidence may be invented if no governed joint cell exists.

The resolver/runtime must be able to return multiple lawful conditional evidence objects, including contradictory ones, and preserve which exact generated position fact(s) made each object applicable. V3 owns the later causal realization. The survey does not speak directly as psyche.

This is directly required by the accepted V3 and ADP-009 separation of structural prior from generated actor fact.

---

## 9. Temporal no-loss test

Position-conditioned evidence is historical evidence, not a timeless demographic lookup.

A sparse distribution must therefore remain anchored to the source wave/time that produced it. Later V553 temporal authority may compare or relate observations only when comparability is governed. The conditional architecture must not pre-collapse waves merely because condition and response labels look the same.

For changing positions between waves, R07 requires preservation of:

- exact wave/time scope;
- exact condition identity in that wave;
- response option/semantic authority in that wave;
- typed comparability/noncomparability when later temporal reasoning is attempted.

R07 does **not** infer respondent-level longitudinal trajectories from repeated cross-sectional surveys unless the source actually supports them.

---

## 10. Future-authority no-loss test

Fixture 12 is the strongest argument against treating the sparse materialized fact store as the entire empirical archive.

Assume current governed conditions are `{age_group, class}`. The respondent substrate also contains `occupation`, but no current authority permits occupation-conditioned structural use. A future authority version lawfully admits `occupation`.

If only these have survived:

- `P(response)`;
- `P(response | age_group)`;
- `P(response | class)`;
- selected `P(response | age_group × class)`;

then `P(response | occupation)` is mathematically unrecoverable in general. The missing association is not encoded in those marginals.

Therefore one of two things is required for losslessness:

- retain/reach respondent-level authoritative evidence and recompute the newly lawful condition; or
- have already retained a sufficient joint representation rich enough for any future condition — which would defeat the proposed sparse economy and still require knowing the future condition vocabulary.

The first is the only minimum requirement this court can establish without redesigning the architecture.

This is also why deleting `recursive_branch_nodes` is safe while deleting the respondent evidence that could regenerate them is not.

## 11. Counterevidence considered

The court actively considered reasons the broad suite might have to remain canonical.

### Counterevidence 1 — expensive diagnostics may encode discovery history

They do encode which split/ranking the old algorithm selected. That matters for exact audit replay, but the selection is algorithm output rather than irreducible historical evidence. Preserve algorithm/version/input identity if byte-for-byte historical diagnostic replay is required; do not confuse the output with world semantics.

### Counterevidence 2 — outlier labels may surface a historically important minority

True, but the label is weaker than the subgroup distribution and currently suppresses low-support cells. Canonicalizing the label would preserve less meaning than preserving the underlying cell with typed support status.

### Counterevidence 3 — secondary gain discovers interactions that a sparse architecture may never request

True. This breaks unary-only sparse storage. It does not require preserving `secondary_indicator_summary`; it requires preserving the ability to reconstruct or lawfully materialize joint conditionals from respondent evidence.

### Counterevidence 4 — recursive branches can expose higher-order combinations

True. This breaks an archive consisting only of precomputed unary/pairwise marginals. It does not make the current greedy branch tree canonical, because the tree omits alternative splits and rare categories. The no-loss object is the underlying joint evidence/recomputability.

### Counterevidence 5 — position prevalence may later help generate actors

Potentially, but accepted ADP-009 is explicit that raw prevalence does not become actor membership or probability. A future `GOVERNED_POSITION_DISTRIBUTION` authority could separately authorize candidate/weight semantics. R07 therefore requires that raw population evidence remain recomputable/reachable, not that today’s `position_distribution` be treated as actor-generation authority.

## 12. Unresolved questions

1. **Exact R06 schema unavailable on this shared base by design.** R07 cannot issue a field-by-field compliance verdict on sibling VT3-CM-R06. Vozhd Trii/R00 must compare the R06 result against the mandatory no-loss requirements in this record.
2. **Backing respondent evidence retention policy.** The economized architecture is semantically lossless only if respondent-level reconstructibility remains available. This court did not receive authority to decide the final physical retention mechanism.
3. **Survey-design uncertainty beyond support/weights.** Current V550 conditional summaries primarily expose counts, percentages, weight status and support tiers. If future authority requires design-based standard errors using PSU/strata/cluster variables, the current shared respondent substrate’s required-column contract may not by itself be sufficient. This is an existing/future-authority question, not a reason to retain the current diagnostic suite.
4. **Typed-limit physical shape.** R07 proves the necessity of typed absence/noncomparability/underpowered states but does not decide whether they are zero-payload states of `CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION` or a separate companion object.
5. **Cross-source harmonization.** Same-named condition values across sources are not assumed equivalent. A separate governed mapping/comparability authority remains required before cross-source pooling.
6. **Joint-materialization policy.** R07 proves that lawful conjunctions must be representable and future conjunctions must be reconstructible. It does not authorize unrestricted Cartesian materialization or choose which conjunctions should be precomputed.

None of these unresolved questions rescues the current broad diagnostic artifact suite as canonical. The blocking question for economization is narrower: **will exact respondent-level reconstructibility and typed evidence state survive?** If the answer is no, the verdict escalates from `LOSSLESS_WITH_REVISIONS` to `SEMANTIC_LOSS`.

## 13. F. Final verdict

# `LOSSLESS_WITH_REVISIONS`

### Why not `LOSSLESS`

A minimal positive/unary conditional distribution object fails required hostile fixtures 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10 and 12 unless additional semantic dimensions/backing evidence are guaranteed.

### Why not `SEMANTIC_LOSS`

The court found no terminal meaning uniquely owned by MI/Cramér's-V summaries, outlier labels, secondary-gain summaries, or recursive branch trees. Their lawful informational content is either:

- directly represented by full conditional distributions; or
- regenerable from governed joint/respondent evidence under explicit rules.

The broad suite can therefore be economized without losing history **provided the revisions in §0 and §4 are binding**.

### Why not `UNRESOLVED`

The remaining open questions concern physical representation, future uncertainty authority, and the sibling R06 schema. They do not prevent a semantic holding about what must survive and what may be demoted.
