## Executive ruling

The first R06 result survives only after substantial correction.

The chamber record still supports replacing the present broad V550 determinicity suite with a much smaller authority-first architecture. It does **not** support the first-pass physical assumption that permanent normalized respondent microdata feeding a durably canonical `CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION` (CED) is itself the minimum empirical memory. R08 moves durable physical authority beneath CED: Rawlsing should retain a **content-addressed lossless source capsule (or proved source-equivalent projection) plus exact pinned replay authority**. The normalized respondent substrate becomes a regenerable private cache after replay/hash certification. CED remains the canonical semantic interface for conditional evidence and may be selectively materialized as a serving fact.

R02/R07 also falsify unary-only sufficiency. Exact intersectional meaning requires same-respondent joint incidence or an information-equivalent retained source/joint authority. Sparse conjunction CEDs remain necessary where separately governed; the current recursive tree is a lossy diagnostic, not joint ontology. R11 closes the workload side: there is no lawful hidden default of every question crossed with all twelve V550 `INDICATOR_FIELDS`, and authority over `A` plus authority over `B` does not automatically authorize `A AND B`.

Corrected R01 requires another repair. Current V550 `position_distribution` is question-independent marginal respondent-coordinate prevalence, while `indicator_answer_matrix` is the active unary `P(response | indicator=value)` producer. Those are different empirical objects. Neither is ADP-009 generated actor-position authority.

R09 blocks the first-pass runtime shortcut. It found no accepted empirical-coordinate → CAN-006 generated-position binding mapping. A CED conditioned on `income_level=low`, `employment=unemployed`, `religion_label=X`, or another V550 coordinate remains an **empirical conditional fact**. Shared spelling, bridge metadata, predictive strength, geography, or prevalence cannot promote it into a V3 generated-position-conditioned runtime fact. Until separately accepted cross-ontology mapping/evaluator authority exists, Rawlsing must preserve the empirical fact while returning typed `DEFER`/`DENY` for actor-position-conditioned promotion.

R10 narrows uncertainty claims. Current Rawlsing carries descriptive sample evidence with raw support and selected scalar-weight semantics, but no governed PSU/stratum/replicate-weight/estimator substrate sufficient for design-based confidence intervals, standard errors, design effects, or effective sample size. The replacement must preserve raw support, weighted descriptive estimates, weight state, epistemic/authority uncertainty, noncomparability, and downstream admission as separate axes without manufacturing design-based precision.

Corrected R03 confirms that V553+ direct-DAG capability is **transport/reuse authority, not semantic recreation**. If an exact conditional object is absent upstream, later stages cannot repair it. It must be recomputed under a governed workload from retained source authority or remain typed missing.

The reconciled spine is therefore:

```text
durable empirical source authority + exact replay authority
    -> explicit governed conditional workload
    -> canonical CED semantic view / selective serving facts
    -> separately governed V551 signed conditional evidence
    -> separately governed V552 structural condition/pressure
    -> existing exact-object publication/reuse
    -> V558 runtime binding only where cross-ontology authority exists
```

Raw empirical response distributions remain evidence/provenance authority behind ordinary actor-facing structural context. They are not V3 response or belief priors.

# A. Reconciled chamber architecture candidate

## A1. Physical layers

The minimum whole-machine design separates:

1. **Durable empirical source authority** — `EMPIRICAL_SOURCE_CAPSULE` plus `EMPIRICAL_REPLAY_AUTHORITY_BUNDLE`.
2. **Regenerable build substrate** — normalized respondent-grain cache and intermediate contingency machinery.
3. **Compiled serving authority** — workload-specific CED/V551/V552 producer objects plus existing V557/V558-governed publication/repository artifacts.

## A2. Semantic jurisdictions

Four jurisdictions remain distinct:

- **empirical evidence:** what respondents in a governed historical/source slice actually did or said under exact empirical conditions;
- **structural interpretation:** what, if anything, that distribution is authorized to mean as actor-independent structural condition/pressure;
- **generated actor position:** ADP-009 actor-specific, time-indexed generated position facts;
- **runtime binding:** exact evaluation of producer-governed structural requirements against current CAN-006 bindings.

A fact may be lawful empirical evidence and permanently non-bindable at runtime. Non-bindability does not invalidate the evidence.

## A3. CED status

`CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION` has mixed status:

- canonical semantic interface/view: **YES**;
- sole irreducible durable physical source authority: **NO**;
- selectively materialized serving fact: **YES**;
- exactly reconstructible from deeper source/replay authority: **YES**, within the retained future-authority frontier;
- ordinary actor-facing context payload: **NO**.

CED means:

```text
P(response_or_measure
  | governed_empirical_condition_predicate,
    source/wave/time/geography,
    response topology,
    denominator basis,
    weight basis)
```

It does not mean:

```text
P(this actor believes/responds | generated actor position)
```

A CED may carry a generated-position requirement ref only after separately accepted cross-ontology mapping authority exists. That runtime link is not inherent in the empirical predicate.

## A4. Respondent substrate

The normalized respondent substrate is not permanently canonical merely because it is convenient. After exact source replay is certified, it is a `REGENERABLE_PRIVATE_BUILD_CACHE`. Before certification it may not be deleted.

## A5. Joint/intersectional meaning

Unary CED is insufficient. Present conjunction materialization requires all three:

1. a canonical predicate grammar capable of representing the conjunction;
2. same-respondent joint incidence reconstructible from durable source authority;
3. explicit conjunction workload authority.

The current recursive tree is not a substitute: it is greedy, bounded-depth, support-gated, top-two-value and lossy.

## A6. Position prevalence

```text
EMPIRICAL_COORDINATE_PREVALENCE_DISTRIBUTION
    = P(empirical coordinate value in respondent population)

CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION
    = P(response | governed empirical condition)

ADP-009 GENERATED POSITION
    = actor-specific generated structural-position fact
```

Legacy `position_distribution` belongs to the first lane. It is not V558 Phase-B membership fallback and cannot become ADP-009 actor probability without separate accepted `GOVERNED_POSITION_DISTRIBUTION` authority.

## A7. Workload compilation

Canonical compute begins only from `GOVERNED_CONDITIONAL_WORKLOAD_SPEC`. There is no canonical default:

```text
all questions × all 12 INDICATOR_FIELDS × observed values
```

and no automatic conjunction closure. An explicit diagnostic regime may explore unrequested coordinates/interactions, but its discoveries remain diagnostic/candidate authority until separately adopted.

## A8. Actor-facing boundary

Ordinary `ResolvedStructuralContext` should expose governed structural condition/pressure objects, not raw response distributions. Where a runtime structural fact is empirically grounded, context may carry compact support/weight/permission qualifiers and immutable CED/V551 evidence refs. Full CED dereference belongs to explicit evidence/audit use under `POPULATION_EVIDENCE_ONLY` semantics.

# H. Replay and deletion gates

## H1. Source-capsule acceptance

Require content-addressed source bytes/lossless projection, schema/format identity, declared future-authority frontier, respondent universe/linkage/incidence, routing/not-asked semantics, weights, and all future-authorizable empirical coordinates. Failure: do not delete normalized respondent material or any artifact still needed for reconstruction.

## H2. Normalized-substrate replay certification

Require deterministic regeneration from source capsule under pinned replay authority; expected logical hash match; typed response/missing/routing equivalence; weighted/unweighted primitive equivalence; same-respondent conjunction fixtures; respondent-overlap identity; and prevalence regeneration where governed. Failure: keep the substrate and mark `REPLAY_UNCERTIFIED`.

## H3. CED equivalence

Require exact predicate identity including source-native provenance; full response topology; raw numerator/denominator; weighted mass/denominator and weight state; support formula/status; completion-zero versus no-observation; typed absence/noncomparability; unary and authorized conjunction fixtures; and replay/content-hash receipt. Failure: do not retire the sole surviving predicate-bearing V550 evidence.

## H4. Serving-artifact replacement

Require rebuilding runtime-published V551/V552/other producer facts against final CED/workload authority; exact publication/repository reference closure; preserved binding state; and receipts that keep empirical-evidence lineage separate from generated-binding lineage. Failure: keep the prior serving package. Direct DAG may not repair it.

## H5. Diagnostic demotion

Require source/joint replay certification, no proved distinct canonical consumer, on-demand regeneration tests, and compatibility-consumer migration. Failure: retain the diagnostic physically if necessary, but do not upgrade it to semantic authority.

## H6. Source-authority deletion

Require a replacement proved information-equivalent over the full governed future-authority frontier, including same-respondent joint incidence and all source semantics. Finite aggregate closure is insufficient. Failure: retain the source capsule.

## H7. Terminal-serving availability

Even theoretically recomputable serving objects cannot simply disappear. Build the successor, prove exact reference closure, then move publication/repository refs. Runtime must never depend on ad hoc source replay to repair a missing published fact.

# Final chamber candidate

The minimum lawful architecture is **source-authority-first, workload-governed, joint-reconstructible, sparse-serving, and fail-closed at semantic promotion boundaries**.

Durable empirical memory is not the current broad determinicity suite and need not be permanent normalized microdata. It is a content-addressed source capsule (or proved source-equivalent projection) plus pinned replay authority sufficient to regenerate same-respondent incidence, response semantics, routing/not-asked states, weights, empirical coordinates, and governed future conditionals.

CED is the canonical semantic interface over that authority and may be selectively materialized. Unary and explicitly authorized conjunction CEDs preserve empirical heterogeneity; sparse serving does not destroy future capability because the durable source layer retains reconstructibility.

V551 and V552 remain distinct semantic jurisdictions. CED can exist without signed structural meaning. Signed evidence can exist without pressure eligibility. Structural pressure can exist without runtime actor binding. Runtime binding exists only after exact cross-ontology authority is separately accepted.

The actor-facing runtime consumes governed structural conditions with compact evidence qualification and immutable evidence references. Raw response distributions stay behind the evidence boundary. The machine thereby preserves the historical fact that populations responded differently under different conditions without turning that population evidence into a destiny, belief, or response prior for a concrete generated actor.

No production implementation is authorized by this result.
