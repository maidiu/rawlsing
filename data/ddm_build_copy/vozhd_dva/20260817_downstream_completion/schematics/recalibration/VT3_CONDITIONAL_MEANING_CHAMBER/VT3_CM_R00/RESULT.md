## 0. Binding chamber holding

The Conditional-Meaning Chamber closes with the R06-C2 architecture substantially sustained, but sharpened by this synthesis.

The current broad V550 determinicity suite is **not** the canonical architecture. It nevertheless contains real empirical conditional meaning. The chamber locates that meaning below the predictor rankings, dominant-response summaries, recursive trees and signatures: in exact respondent-derived response distributions conditioned on empirical coordinates, together with the same-respondent incidence needed to reconstruct lawful conjunctions, raw and weighted support, typed absence/noncomparability, time/geography, semantic authority and provenance.

The minimum lawful machine must therefore separate durable physical empirical authority from canonical semantic evidence. The durable empirical root is a **content-addressed lossless source capsule, or a formally proved information-equivalent source projection, plus an exact hash-bound replay authority bundle**. The normalized respondent substrate is a private regenerable build cache only after replay certification. `CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION` (`CED`) is the canonical semantic interface for conditional empirical evidence and may be selectively materialized; CED is not the ultimate physical source authority.

Canonical conditional computation must begin from explicit governed workload authority. The legacy hard-coded twelve-field `INDICATOR_FIELDS` loop is implementation behavior, not ontology and not, standing alone, workload authority. R11 is used with its stated caveat: it establishes that no universal question × twelve-field workload mandate was **demonstrated**; it is not silently strengthened into an exhaustive proof that no such authority exists anywhere.

The semantic promotion chain remains discontinuous. Compute/preservation authority, structural-interpretation authority and runtime binding authority are different jurisdictions. An empirical conditional may remain historically valuable and canonical evidence while being permanently non-bindable to an actor. A V3 generated structural position is an ADP-009 actor-specific generated fact, not a population prevalence. CAN-006 transports exact generated facts; it does not translate survey coordinates. R09 found no accepted V550 field/value → CAN-006 binding-key mapping at the frozen base. Until separately accepted cross-ontology mapping authority exists, generated-position-conditioned promotion must fail closed while the empirical evidence remains preserved.

The controlling noncollapse is:

```text

!= RESPONDENT JOINT INCIDENCE VIEW
!= CONDITIONAL EMPIRICAL DISTRIBUTION
!= STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
```

This architecture economizes computation by removing repeated analytical expansion, not by making historical people simpler.

## 2. Primary synthesis rulings

### 2.1 What empirical conditional meaning does current V550 create?

V550 creates several distinct empirical meanings.

`answer_distribution` and `country_answer_distribution` are baseline response distributions at source/wave and country grains. `indicator_answer_matrix` is the concrete unary conditional producer: for each question, exact empirical `indicator_name`, exact `indicator_value` and response value it preserves unweighted and weighted descriptive counts/shares. `position_distribution` is different: question-independent respondent-coordinate prevalence by `position_field` / `position_value` within source/wave/country. Predictor summaries, outlier labels, secondary gain, recursive branches and signatures are analytical projections over richer respondent evidence.

The current empirical evidence also contains support/weight state and typed response semantics. V550/V550J co-execution preserves same-respondent incidence for its selected union, which contains joint information not recoverable from unary marginals.

### 2.2 Which of that meaning is terminally valuable?

Terminally valuable empirical meaning is the complete scoped evidence needed to support lawful structural interpretation without demographic destiny: exact predicate identity, full response topology, raw counts and denominators, governed weighted mass and denominators, weight state/provenance, support state, typed absence/zero/noncomparability, time/geography, response semantics, source authority and replayable provenance.

Question-independent prevalence remains valuable only in its separate jurisdiction where explicitly governed. MI/NMI/Cramér's V, entropy reduction, dominant response, outlier labels, secondary gain, recursive trees and signatures have no demonstrated irreducible terminal semantic need as canonical objects.

### 2.3 Is the conditional meaning faithfully preserved or recreated downstream?

**No.** Corrected R03 controls. Current canonical V551 excludes the dedicated IAM conditional surface. The alternate V551 conditional helper is outside canonical publication and loses first-class condition identity. V551J packets preserve different joint/component semantics; they do not reconstruct `P(response | empirical A AND B)`. V552 accepts but does not consume `conditional_determinicity`, and `position_conditioning_profile` is a rollup of already-created pressure observations. V553+ can publish/reuse an existing exact producer object, but cannot estimate or reconstruct a missing empirical conditional.

Exact meaning can therefore survive only by: (a) exact direct reuse of an already retained/admitted object; or (b) governed recomputation from reconstructible empirical authority. Direct-DAG law is not semantic repair.

### 2.4 Canonical meaning versus analytical products

Canonical meaning resides in baseline/conditional empirical distributions and the deeper joint/source authority needed to reconstruct them. The current file shapes are not sacred. `position_distribution` is a separate prevalence view. `position_indicator_summary`, outlier cohorts, secondary-indicator summary, recursive branch nodes, branch-position signatures and scalar association metrics are diagnostics or authoring analyses.

The recursive tree is especially noncanonical: it is greedy, bounded depth, support-gated and follows only two high-frequency child values. It cannot stand for the historical joint distribution.

### 2.5 Actual irreducible physical empirical authority

R02 and R08 are reconciled rather than averaged. R02 identifies **same-respondent joint incidence** as the irreducible *information requirement* if arbitrary lawful conjunctions are to remain reconstructible. R08 identifies the minimum durable *physical carrier*: an immutable content-addressed lossless source snapshot or formally proved information-equivalent projection plus exact deterministic replay authority capable of regenerating that incidence and its expected logical hash.

Thus:

```text
DURABLE SOURCE/SOURCE-EQUIVALENT CAPSULE
        +
EXACT HASH-BOUND REPLAY AUTHORITY
        -> regenerable normalized respondent substrate
        -> governed CED / V550J joint products
```

The normalized substrate need not remain permanently canonical after certification. CED is canonical semantic evidence but not the durable root physical authority. Published V557/V558 serving authority remains physically available for runtime independently of source replay.

### 2.6 Are unary conditional distributions sufficient?

**No.** XOR/intersection and Simpson-reversal fixtures show that weak or identical marginals can coexist with decisive joint effects. Sparse governed conjunctions are required where separately authorized and supported. They remain reconstructible because same-respondent incidence survives through the source/replay authority.

A predicate grammar can represent a conjunction; it does not authorize one. `A` authorized plus `B` authorized does not imply `A AND B` authorized. The current recursive tree is neither the authority nor the joint substrate.

### 2.7 What determines the conditional workload?

Three independent jurisdictions:

1. **Empirical compute/preservation authority** — selects which conditional empirical facts Rawlsing may/should canonically compute or retain. The target seam is a versioned `GOVERNED_CONDITIONAL_WORKLOAD_SPEC`.
2. **Structural interpretation authority** — determines whether an observed conditional association may become V552 actor-independent structural condition/pressure.
3. **Runtime binding authority** — determines whether a structurally interpreted object can be evaluated against a lawfully generated V3 position through an exact requirement key.

`INDICATOR_FIELDS`, source availability, response topology, weighting rules, `allowed_as_demographic_predictor`, `allowed_as_branch_splitter`, association strength and existing bridge lanes do not collapse these jurisdictions.

### 2.8 Status of current V550 `INDICATOR_FIELDS`

The fixed fields are:

`sex`, `age_group`, `education_level`, `income_level`, `religion_label`, `urban_rural`, `region`, `ethnicity`, `language`, `employment`, `marital`, `household_size`.

They are evidence-era respondent coordinates used by implementation. Membership is not terminal structural-position ontology, not canonical workload authority, and not CAN-006 binding identity. Fields such as income, employment, education, religion and region may be historically/structurally relevant, but they remain empirical coordinates until an accepted authority says what structural predicate they mean.

### 2.9 What statistical uncertainty does current Rawlsing possess?

Current Rawlsing has **descriptive conditional sample evidence**, selected scalar-weight semantics, deterministic support heuristics and typed epistemic/admission states. It does not carry a governed survey-design variance substrate through the conditional path: no complete propagated PSU IDs, sampling strata, replicate weights and estimator method sufficient for design-based inference.

Therefore:

```text
UNDERPOWERED != STATISTICALLY INSIGNIFICANT
SUFFICIENT_SUPPORT != STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT
WEIGHTED_SHARE != POPULATION_TRUTH
CONFIDENCE_TIER != CONFIDENCE_INTERVAL
```

No design-based SE, CI, MOE, design effect or effective-N is authorized by current evidence. Design-based uncertainty is future authority debt and does not block this architecture.

## 4. Canonical object families

### 4.1 `EMPIRICAL_SOURCE_CAPSULE`

**Status:** durable root empirical authority.

Minimum content: source family/source/wave/geography; respondent universe and stable linkage; respondent-question incidence; question/variable/instrument identity; raw response codes and governed special states; routing/applicability sufficient to reconstruct not-asked; source-native weights; all empirical conditioning coordinates inside the governed future-authority frontier; content hash and format/schema identity.

A source projection is lawful only if discarded coordinates lie outside an explicitly governed future-authority frontier and information equivalence is proved. `Unused today` is never sufficient.

### 4.2 `EMPIRICAL_REPLAY_AUTHORITY_BUNDLE`

**Status:** durable replay authority.

Bind source-capsule hash; respondent relation/pseudonymization transform; questionnaire/instrument/routing/applicability authority; response-state/codebook authority; weight data and weighting contract; extraction/compiler commit/tree/content hash; response-semantics contract; question-family registry; V550J joint-configuration authority where applicable; empirical-coordinate derivation authority where applicable; selection inputs/hashes; schema/execution contract; expected regenerated normalized-substrate logical hash; serving-artifact compiler identity.

The normalized-substrate hash is a replay checkpoint, not the source authority.

### 4.3 `GOVERNED_CONDITIONAL_WORKLOAD_SPEC`

**Status:** canonical versioned compute/preservation authority.

It must answer why this question is conditioned on this dimension/predicate/conjunction at this scope and under these semantics. Required content includes workload ID/hash/version; source/wave/geography refs; question/measure authority; conditioning authority and granularity; authoritative dimension/value-domain refs; exact predicate AST or governed expansion; predicate cardinality/order; conjunction semantics where applicable; response topology; weighting/denominator; support-retention and typed-absence policy; semantic lane; allowed downstream use; optional structural/binding authority refs; future-evidence retention status; compiler/provenance refs and canonical hash.

### 4.4 `CONDITIONAL_EMPIRICAL_DISTRIBUTION` (`CED`)

**Status:** canonical semantic empirical evidence interface; selectively materialized serving fact.

Minimum content: CED ID/schema/hash; source/replay/workload refs; question/measure and response topology; exact empirical predicate AST; source-native + canonical empirical coordinate/value identity; predicate cardinality and conjunction authority where needed; source/wave/geography/evidence time; full response-value topology; per-response raw cell counts; raw denominator; respondent support; weighted cell/denominator mass if lawful; weight identity/status/basis/provenance; support formula/threshold/status; typed observed/completion-zero/no-observation/missing-authority/not-applicable/noncomparability state; explicit survey-design variance availability; epistemic/comparability state and provenance.

CED is sample/population evidence. It is never actor identity, generated position, belief, psyche or actor-response probability.

### 4.5 `CONDITIONAL_EVIDENCE_ADMISSION_RECORD`

**Status:** canonical governance decision.

Record CED ref/hash, requested use, evidence/support/comparability state, `ALLOW | DENY | DEFER`, decision reason, authority/version/hash, evaluation scope/time and provenance. ## 5. Physical retention and deletion gate

The normalized shared respondent substrate is `REGENERABLE_CACHE` only after replay certification. Before certification it is replay escrow.

Deletion requires all of the following:

1. **SOURCE_CAPSULE_IDENTITY** — immutable content-addressed source bytes or proved lossless projection, format/schema, source/wave/geography and future-authority frontier.
2. **RESPONDENT_REPLAY** — exact respondent universe/linkage, respondent-question incidence, normalized identities, response states and expected logical substrate hash.
3. **ROUTING_NOT_ASKED_REPLAY** — distinguish not-asked, routing/applicability, IAP, missing, source absence and extraction omission.
4. **WEIGHT_REPLAY** — exact selected weight identity, usable/missing/invalid weight coverage, raw support, weighted mass, denominators, aggregate status and lane-specific basis.
5. **CONDITIONING_COORDINATE_REPLAY** — every empirical coordinate inside the future-authority frontier, with source-native and canonical identity. Incidental unhashed cache columns do not count.
6. **JOINT_RECONSTRUCTIBILITY** — same-respondent unary and authorized conjunction fixtures, including XOR/intersection and Simpson reversal, plus current V550J same-respondent configurations.
7. **CED_EQUIVALENCE** — exact predicate identity, response topology, raw/weighted numerators+denominators, support formula/state, completion-zero vs no-observation, typed absence/noncomparability, time/geography/provenance and hash.
8. **LOW_SUPPORT_PRESERVATION** — underpowered/minority observed evidence survives even where V551/V552 admission is `DENY`.
9. **SERVING_REFERENCE_CLOSURE** — successor V551/V552/runtime objects built; producer IDs/hashes and evidence refs preserved; V557/V558 repository/reference closure verified.
10. **COMPATIBILITY_CONSUMER_AUDIT** — no distinct canonical consumer still depends on a demoted bridge/diagnostic/compatibility artifact, or that consumer has been migrated.

Finite present aggregate closure is rejected as sole durable empirical authority unless formally proved information-equivalent to the entire lawful future query frontier, including same-respondent incidence. Sparse serving is safe precisely because deeper source/replay authority remains.

## 6. Weighting, support, uncertainty and zero semantics

A replacement CED must preserve raw support separately from weighted mass; raw numerator/denominator separately from weighted numerator/denominator; selected weight identity/status/provenance; usable/missing/invalid weight coverage; support formula/version/status; and explicit weight-basis state.

Current lanes are not uniform. `partial` weights can produce weighted answer/branching evidence; `position_distribution` suppresses weighted prevalence unless the respondent slice is fully `weighted`; V552 can select a complete `pct_weighted` vector carrying `partial`. Numeric `n_weighted`/`pct_weighted` fields are therefore not self-authenticating.

Also:

```text
COMPLETION_ZERO != STRUCTURAL_IMPOSSIBILITY
NO_OBSERVATION != ZERO
MISSING_AUTHORITY != ZERO
NONCOMPARABLE != NEUTRAL
UNDERPOWERED_OBSERVED != DELETE
EVIDENCE_PRESENT != DOWNSTREAM_ADMISSION
```

Current design-variance state is effectively `DESIGN_VARIANCE_UNAVAILABLE_NO_GOVERNED_DESIGN_SUBSTRATE`. Future design-based statistics require separate authority and source fields; their absence does not block this architecture.
