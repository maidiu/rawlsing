## 3. Explicit answers to the fourteen terminal-demand questions

### 3.1 Nine-strata field

V553 does not own the nine-strata ontology or assign facts to strata. It must preserve **0..n exact governed stratum/structural-position references** where upstream authority supplies them, including role (`SOURCE/DRIVER`, `TARGET/RECEIVER`, or `CONTEXT`) and the authority object that licenses the classification. A fact may lawfully bear on multiple strata. The V553 key must not force a single `stratum` value merely for convenience.

If the current upstream authority provides only relation/target/driver/channel identities and no governed mapping to the nine named strata, V553 records no invented stratum classification. That is `MISSING_AUTHORITY`, not a V553 semantic failure.

### 3.2 Relational direction

V553 must preserve exact source/driver and target/receiving structural refs and any governed structural-directionality authority used by the key. It must keep **structural pressure direction** distinct from V553's own **temporal delta direction**. `delta_direction = increasing/decreasing` is not a source→target direction and cannot substitute for one.

Distinct simultaneous pressure keys are never merged merely because they share country/year, sign, target or aggregate slice.

### 3.3 Time

This is V553's central semantic ownership. Later stages must be able to distinguish observed anchor, measured zero, comparable ordered pair, gap, noncomparability, insufficient authority, optional persistence/drift annotation, and optional structural rupture candidate. No interpolation is canonical. External event identity and causal rupture remain unavailable absent separate authority.

### 3.4 Mediation

V553 contributes no mediation route. It contributes the exact structural-temporal basis a later route rule must inspect: structural fact, admission/permission, source/target/position/channel authority, temporal key/observation/relation/limit, uncertainty and provenance. V555 owns the route candidate/condition; V3 owns realized mediation.

### 3.5 Orientation-to-strata

V3 eventually needs to know what structural layer/position/relation each historical pressure concerned. V553 therefore preserves exact structural-position/stratum refs where governed, plus source/target role, temporal applicability, magnitude/status, permission and uncertainty. V553 must never assign actor identification, alienation, dependence, resistance, pride, shame, debt, obligation or other orientation state.

### 3.6 Absolute Horizon

A generic `horizon_constraint` is not sufficient evidence that an object is an Absolute-Horizon relation. V553 must preserve generic horizon-constraint identity exactly, and preserve a distinct `ABSOLUTE_HORIZON` authority/classification ref only when an upstream governed object explicitly grants that meaning. If no such authority exists, V553 emits no Absolute-Horizon assertion. Lived Horizon orientation remains V3/V2 jurisdiction.

### 3.7 Pressure-flow

V556 eventually needs typed endpoints, path/mediation refs, structural direction, temporal support, concurrency and negative authority. V553's obligation is only to preserve the exact upstream identity/authority refs and create its temporal objects so those later edges can be built without reconstructing history. V553 does not create an edge, interaction, buffer, amplifier or rerouting rule.

### 3.8 Attractors

V556/V3 require multiple historically conditioned possible pathways. V553 protects multiplicity by retaining exact sparse keys and temporal relations independently and by refusing aggregate dominance, winner selection, interpolation and direct type assignment. It creates no attractor axis, basin, type or actor outcome.

### 3.9 Vitality / organism boundary

Structural pressure is not need, desire or vitality. V553 does not instantiate organism state. Its only obligation is to preserve the differentiated structural history that V3 later encounters as one causal field among actor capacities, needs, relations and prior history.

### 3.10 Genealogy

Parent/partner/child recursion needs historically constrained structural priors that can be selected for each generated person's time/place/history. V553 therefore preserves exact time, source/country identity, structural identity and authority/negative state. It does not generate a parent, household, partner, child, exposure event or family relation.

### 3.11 Epistemic separation

Every canonical V553 semantic object is a **derived structural-temporal prior**, not a source-evidence row, runtime fact or rendered expression. V552 remains factual owner; V552J remains admission/permission owner; earlier authorities retain semantic/directionality/position ownership. V553 IDs and lineage must make that producer separation auditable.

### 3.12 Negative authority

Measured zero, valid empty, temporal gap, noncomparability, use denial, insufficient certified authority, uncertainty, transport/integrity failure and forbidden inference remain distinct. Transport/integrity failure is a run failure, not a semantic `TEMPORAL_LIMIT`. Missing terminal classification authority (for example a stratum or Absolute-Horizon mapping that V553 itself does not require for temporal comparability) is preserved as reference availability/missing authority, not miscast as a temporal pressure value.

### 3.13 DAG reuse

Later stages should directly resolve original V550/V551/V552/V552J objects where those producers still own the needed meaning. V553 therefore does not denormalize the full objects. It must, however, preserve exact locators tying a V553 key/observation/relation to the relevant original objects. Direct reuse without exact association would devolve into downstream rematching.

### 3.14 No-starvation test

Every required primary family is justified in §10 below. No terminal capability depends on the secondary aggregate; therefore the aggregate remains secondary. No terminal capability requires the optional temporal annotator when its rule is absent; therefore persistence/drift/rupture-candidate annotation remains disabled rather than invented. No terminal capability requires a new V553 mediation, conflict, stratum, Horizon, flow or actor family; those additions would violate the no-bloat rule.

## 13. Worked cases

### 13.1 Ordinary multi-strata temporal case

Assume upstream governance lawfully supplies two V552 facts, `F_t1` and `F_t2`, admitted by exact V552J associations `A_t1` and `A_t2`. The governed structural identity basis says both facts concern the same relation axis `RA`, the same exact driver/source-position ref `SP_SOURCE`, the same target/receiving-position ref `SP_TARGET`, and the same structural channel `CH`. Upstream position/stratum authority additionally classifies the source relation under one finite stratum ref and the receiving structural condition under two role-qualified finite-stratum refs. The classification is multi-valued by authority; V553 did not derive it.

V553 creates:

- temporal key `K`, whose identity basis is the governed exact structural identity and authority refs;
- observation `O1 = K × t1 × F_t1` with exact `A_t1`;
- observation `O2 = K × t2 × F_t2` with exact `A_t2`;
- temporal relation `R12 = K × O1 → O2` with authorized temporal delta/direction;
- no gap/limit if the pair is comparable.

`K` preserves the role-qualified multi-strata refs and the original structural directionality authority. `R12.delta_direction` states temporal change only. V553 does not say that an actor belongs to either receiving stratum, that the pressure traveled through a household/carrier, or that either pressure was absorbed/resisted.

V554 may later use `K/O1/O2/R12` plus the original V552/V552J/position authorities to decide bounded affordance eligibility. V555 may later construct a structural mediation route. V556 may later build an inter-strata pressure-flow edge. V3 may later generate an actor's orientation and realized path. No stage needs to guess which structural objects V553 temporalized.

### 13.2 Hostile flattening / starvation case

Assume two distinct exact temporal keys `K_A` and `K_B` exist at the same country/year. They have different exact source/target/direction authority refs and represent simultaneous structural pressures whose observed values happen to offset numerically in a country-year aggregate.

A lossy V553 architecture could average them to `0`, publish only the aggregate, or merge them because their display labels resemble one another. That would make later counter-pressure topology impossible: V556 would see “zero pressure” rather than two real concurrent structural relations.

The superseding architecture forbids that path:

- `K_A` and `K_B` remain separate canonical sparse identities;
- each has its own observations, lineage and structural-reference set;
- the handoff exposes both independently;
- a secondary aggregate may numerically summarize to zero, but contributor refs make the two canonical pressures recoverable and the aggregate cannot satisfy handoff/readiness by itself;
- V553 does **not** infer that `K_A` and `K_B` “contradict,” “compete,” “buffer,” or “cancel.” If such a relation is governed later, V554/V555/V556 may create it within their jurisdiction.

This protects simultaneous pressure/counter-pressure without making V553 a pressure-flow engine.
