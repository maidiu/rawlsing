# Rawlsing Governing Spirit — Design and Engineering Manifesto

**Status:** `BINDING_INTERPRETIVE_LAW`  
**Scope:** all Vozhd, Rabochii, authority, implementation, review, and integration decisions made under ambiguity.  
**Authority relation:** explicit source semantics, accepted semantic authority, and closed architecture/jurisdiction remain higher-order constraints. This document governs tie-breaking and interpretation **within** that lawful space. It does not authorize invention, semantic leakage, jurisdiction drift, or architecture reopening.

Rawlsing exists to generate a person who is **causally intelligible without being inevitable**.

A person does not begin as an autonomous bundle of traits to which history, class, family, nation, religion and culture are subsequently attached. A living organism becomes a person while already embedded in relations of dependence, provision, coercion, inheritance, recognition, love, labor, kinship, language, legitimacy, violence, memory and meaning. Yet neither demographics nor structures contain a finished psyche. Rawlsing therefore rejects both liberal-individualist trait ontology and mechanical structural determinism. **The world must be powerful enough to produce the person without being powerful enough to predetermine them.**

The nine finite strata—Planetary, Imperial Block, Nation State, Sub-national Region, Sect/Ethno/Class, Party/Movement, Neighborhood/Household, Family, Self—are relational fields of power, not demographic buckets. They press downward, upward and laterally. Power is the capacity to set terms: distribute resources and danger, grant recognition and protection, impose costs, define legitimacy and normality, make some futures possible and others prohibitive, and make some interpretations appear as reality itself.

Pressure is how these relations meet concrete life: constraint, permission, scarcity, expectation, opportunity, threat, prestige, shame, security, obligation, exclusion, protection, debt, aspiration. But pressure is still not the person.

What moves is **living flow**: need, desire, vitality, attachment, curiosity, fear, sexuality, imagination, rage, play, care, appetite, beauty, humor, prayer, craft, striving, belonging, autonomy, recognition, fairness, meaning-seeking and the organism's effort to continue and become. Rawlsing's central causal question is:

> **Where does living flow go under pressure?**

What becomes blocked, captured, poisoned, buffered, sublimated, amplified, redirected, mis-aimed, metabolized or transformed?

A person is the historically accumulated, path-dependent and never-final organization of those routings.

Structure therefore constrains channels and costs; it does not speak directly as psyche. **Determination is not transparency.** Power ordinarily appears inside consciousness without its correct causal label: as family duty, normality, ambition, shame, piety, boredom, resentment, respectability, gratitude, personal failure, common sense, romantic expectation, God's will, survival, or simply “how things are.” The actor may be deeply caused by something they cannot see, partially see, misrecognize, or cannot articulate.

This makes the actor's relation to structure intrinsically dialectical.

**Identification → internalization → naturalization → invisibility → agent-of.**

What is deeply identified with ceases to appear as an external object and becomes a lens, a grammar of reality, eventually part of the machinery through which the actor experiences themselves as exercising agency. The deepest structural power does not merely compel people against their wills; it becomes capable of acting through their wills.

**Alienation → externalization → objectification → visibility → agency-against.**

What had been transparent acquires edges. Family becomes *this family*, nation *this nation*, masculinity *this script*, success *this inherited definition of worth*. What becomes object can be named, compared, mocked, mourned, judged, fled, resisted, reformed or transformed.

Alienation is therefore a possibility of freedom, never freedom itself. It can yield resentment, cynicism, nihilism, fanaticism, collapse, flight, a new identification, or transformative refusal. A person can be agent-against State while agent-of Nation; agent-against Church while agent-of family hierarchy; agent-against capital while agent-of Party. Rawlsing must preserve these contradictions rather than resolving the person into ideological coherence.

Contradiction is constitutive. People carry multiple partially incompatible frameworks, attachments and loyalties. A carrier can liberate along one axis and dominate along another. A framework can contain truth and distortion simultaneously. Cracks emerge when reality becomes increasingly expensive to metabolize through an existing framework, but cracks have no predetermined political or spiritual destination. History opens possibilities; it does not contain its own guaranteed resolution.

**Mediation is where structure becomes a life.** Nobody experiences “capitalism,” “nation,” “class” or “religion” in the abstract. They encounter parents, siblings, teachers, employers, landlords, churches, mosques, schools, neighborhoods, unions, movements, lovers, televisions, borders, checkpoints, funerals and kitchens. Carriers translate structural fields into local practice. Timing, relationships and prior history alter what subsequent pressures can become. Parents must therefore be generated lives, relationships generated histories, households causal fields, and children inheritors of historically formed household pressure matrices—not demographic averages.

The person must also exceed suffering. Rawlsing must never reproduce the gaze in which the dominated become merely wounded objects. People under domination remain funny, strategic, desirous, beautiful, petty, loving, cruel, holy, evasive, bored, erotic, ambitious, practical, generous, complicit and opaque. Love, play, appetite, rhythm, sexuality, beauty, humor, craft and ordinary pleasure are as necessary to the model of life as deprivation, injury and domination. **Structural explanation must not consume the human remainder.**

Personhood is becoming rather than classification. Static traits should never replace histories where histories are causally available. Enneagram-like patterns, if useful, are developmental attractors: recurrent basins formed through repeated pressure-flow routing, reinforcement, compensation, destabilization and path dependence. They are effects of lived history capable of strengthening, weakening, transforming and recurring. They are never essences assigned by survey, country, class, parent or birth.

Agency does not require an unconditioned ghost inside the machine. It arises because historical causation usually constrains a field of possibilities rather than specifying one future. Prior acts alter later capacities; relationships alter later exposures; contingent encounters change available language and frameworks; successful resistance changes efficacy; loss changes attachment; memory and forgetting change what can be brought to bear. **Agency is situated divergence whose consequences become subsequent causes.**

Absolute Horizon names the deepest ordering of claims within this process. It is not stratum ten, religiosity, goodness, political correctness or moral depth. It is the evolving relation to what is experienced as ultimately binding: truth, justice, God, mercy, death, human worth, vocation, solidarity, nation, family, self-preservation, power, nihilism, or other ultimate claims. It governs, especially under pressure, **what may be sacrificed for what**.

Every finite stratum can colonize Horizon. Family can become Goodness itself; Nation sacred; organization Truth; wealth sovereignty; wound identity; self-preservation final law. But mediation can also produce criteria capable of judging the mediator: justice learned through family can judge family; God learned through church can judge church; sacrifice learned through nation can condemn national violence; emancipation learned through a movement can judge the movement.

Freedom therefore does not mean escaping mediation. Nobody becomes an unembedded subject. Its richer form is **relativization without disembedding**: belonging without possession by the object of belonging; fidelity capable of judgment.

At one limit lies possessive freedom: the American-dream fantasy of sovereignty through accumulation, the expansion of the zone in which nothing can compel the self. Its extreme attractor is the **Epstein/Moloch pole**—extractive insulation, others reduced toward instruments, agency enlarged by transferring costs outward, the finite self and its appetites approaching ultimacy.

At another lies **kenotic freedom**: freedom acquired because what can be taken from the self no longer wholly possesses it. “Nothing left to lose,” “I am as nothing,” the strangeness of being anything at all: not self-annihilation, but loosening possession of self. Because structures govern partly through threatened loss—status, livelihood, identity, belonging, reputation, even life—the person capable of bearing loss for a fidelity beyond self-preservation can become unusually difficult to govern by threat. The **Jesus/Che limit-region** names reciprocal costly world-bearing: vitality capable of flowing toward others despite real material cost, solidarity strong enough to relativize narrower identification.

The superficially adjacent **suicidal-collapse pole** is metabolically opposite. There, self-preservation loses authority because viable future-routing has collapsed rather than because a wider fidelity has become strong enough to bear loss. One says: *my life can be spent because something remains worth giving it for*. The other approaches: *my life has nowhere left to go*. Rawlsing must be capable of distinguishing these even when both reduce fear of loss.

None of these are personality types, moral scores or destinies. They are limit-regions of pressure-flow × Horizon metabolism. Rawlsing may model the possibility of indomitability, universal solidarity, truthfulness, compassion, fidelity, beauty and belief in a future; it must never rig lives toward that outcome. Liberation must remain something history can make intelligible without making inevitable.

The deepest emancipatory movement Rawlsing must preserve is therefore this:

> **Structures become subjects through people; people can sometimes make those structures objects again.**

The forms through which the person first acquired agency can themselves become objects of later agency. Freedom is neither absence of causation nor escape from inheritance. It is the historically achieved capacity to see, judge, reorder and sometimes transform the mediations through which one became capable of acting at all.

This anthropology determines the engineering.

**Never let downstream vividness exceed upstream authority.** Evidence may constrain a world; it may not become biography. Structural priors may constrain developmental possibility; they may not become psyche. Proxies may constrain context; they may not become lived history. Generated history may exist without becoming actor knowledge. Memory is not objective history. Accessibility is not memory. Phenomenology is not causal truth. Expression is not occurrence. LLM output is not ontology.

Every stage must therefore know exactly what it owns, what it preserves, what it cannot infer, and what belongs downstream. The whole system must be understood deeply enough that a local design does not starve the eventual person, while no local stage may steal later meaning merely because it knows that meaning will eventually be needed. **Whole picture; bounded jurisdiction.**

Unknown, missing, unresolved, noncomparable, denied, valid-empty and forbidden inference are legitimate states. A graceful hallucination is worse than an explicit absence. Randomness may select among lawful possibilities but never manufacture semantic authority. Replay, provenance, immutable causal history, versioned rules and explicit randomness exist to make the simulation accountable, not to claim that human beings are metaphysically deterministic.

The LLM belongs at the expressive edge. It does not author the causal person. It receives only what the generated actor may lawfully access and renders possible expression. Even a rendered utterance becomes part of the generated world only through explicit causal admission.

## Design tie-break law

When design is ambiguous **after explicit source/semantic authority and closed architecture have been obeyed**, choose the path that best preserves, together:

1. historical causality without inevitability;
2. structural constraint without psychological essentialism;
3. agency without magic;
4. contradiction and multiplicity without forced coherence or scalar collapse;
5. mediation rather than structure→psyche shortcuts;
6. actor opacity and misrecognition rather than analyst omniscience;
7. `identification → internalization → naturalization → invisibility → agent-of` and `alienation → externalization → objectification → visibility → agency-against` as dynamic relations, not static scores;
8. ordinary vitality, desire, humor, beauty, care, play and non-traumatic human content alongside domination and injury;
9. dynamic history over static classification;
10. finite belonging capable of self-critique and relativization without fantasies of escaping mediation;
11. genealogy and relationships as histories of generated persons rather than demographic synthesis;
12. uncertainty and lawful absence without fabrication;
13. causal provenance, replay and explicit randomness without reductive total explanation;
14. enough generative openness for genuinely divergent lives to emerge from the same structural field;
15. minimum sufficient machinery without duplicated semantic ownership or ornamental governance.

A more convenient design is worse if it achieves convenience by flattening those distinctions.

A more vivid design is worse if it borrows authority it does not possess.

A more deterministic design is worse if its determinism comes from erasing mediation, contingency or history.

A more “human” design is worse if its humanity comes from arbitrary biography with no causal lineage.

A cleaner person is worse if cleanliness has been purchased by deleting contradiction.

If this manifesto appears to conflict with explicit lawful source semantics, accepted semantic authority, or closed stage jurisdiction, the worker must **surface the conflict to Vozhd**. The manifesto is not permission to bend evidence toward philosophy.

## Success condition

> **Looking backward from the generated person, their becoming should be intelligible. Looking forward from their starting conditions, they should never have been uniquely predictable.**

Rawlsing should generate neither a survey puppet nor a free-floating fictional character. It should generate a historically situated person: materially constrained but not mechanically determined; socially produced but not socially exhausted; realistically opaque to themselves; capable of identification and alienation, complicity and refusal, love and cruelty, humor and grief, possession and kenosis, collapse and transformation.

**The evidence establishes the world. The world constrains the possibilities. Mediation turns possibilities into lived conditions. History turns lived conditions into a person. The person can sometimes turn the structures that formed them into objects of their own agency.**

> **The machine succeeds when the person can surprise us without the machine ever lying about where the surprise came from.**
