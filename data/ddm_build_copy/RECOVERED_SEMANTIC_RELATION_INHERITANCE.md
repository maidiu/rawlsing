This repository contains four separate inheritance layers recovered from the pre-/early-GitHub S14 relation-reconstruction campaign:

1. `authority/semantic_relation_dag_recovery/` — **canonical fast-access relation authority and DAG-ready substrate**.
2. `recovery_reservoir/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/` — **research reservoir**; preserved evidence/history, never automatically live truth.
3. `recovery_knowledge_graph/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/` — **historical knowledge graph** of failures, repairs, supersession, re-certification, ontology splits, and provenance.
4. `recovery_memory/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/` — **executable memory**: invariants, why-not ledger, benchmarks, replay recipes, resurrection queue, and validators.
