## 3. Status law: preservation is not benchmark exercise

The contract deliberately separates P10 preservation state from benchmark exercise state.

### Exercise state

PASS:
- governed operation was actually exercised;
- exercised must be true;
- evidence refs are mandatory.

FAIL:
- operation was actually exercised and failed its governed oracle;
- exercised must be true.

EXECUTION_FAILED:
- authority existed;
- execution was attempted;
- execution failed;
- typed execution_failure evidence is mandatory;
- exercised must be true.

BLOCKED_MISSING_AUTHORITY:
- the operation could not lawfully be executed because necessary authority was absent;
- exact missing-authority identity, governing demand ref and reopen condition are mandatory;
- execution_failure is forbidden;
- exercised must be false.

NOT_EXERCISED_BY_THIS_BENCHMARK:
- the benchmark did not exercise the capability;
- no PASS or failure may be inferred;
- exercised must be false.

NOT_APPLICABLE:
- the governed scope makes the operation inapplicable;
- exercised must be false.

Thus MISSING_AUTHORITY and execution failure are non-interchangeable.

### P10 preservation state

B07/B08 preservation uses the exact P10 disposition vocabulary. In particular:

PRESERVED_AS_EXACT_UNRESOLVED/BLOCKED_AUTHORITY

remains a valid preservation fact when exact meaning, provenance, ceiling, replay/reopen law and the unresolved obligation survive.

If the common benchmark does not exercise that function, the lawful pair is:

preservation = PRESERVED_AS_EXACT_UNRESOLVED/BLOCKED_AUTHORITY  
exercise = NOT_EXERCISED_BY_THIS_BENCHMARK

It is invalid to relabel preservation as benchmark PASS. It is equally invalid to relabel benchmark non-exercise as failure.
