## Exact classification

### 1. Identical basis / like-for-like

Two empirical relations/results may be treated as directly like-for-like only when the comparison-relevant identities are the same exact governed identities (or an already-governed semantic-survival/equivalence relation explicitly makes them the same for the proposed operation): target semantic unit/value semantics/topology, EmpiricalUniverse/population/frame, time scope, risk/routing set, measure basis including weight/design/estimand where applicable, and governing semantic interpretation authority.

Object identity itself is stronger than comparability. Two different objects may be comparable, but only under the operation-scoped rule below.

### 2. Distinct

Any difference in a material identity coordinate remains `DISTINCT_BY_DEFAULT` unless positive authority says what relation that difference bears to the proposed operation. Distinct does not mean false and does not automatically mean noncomparable. It means identity must not be collapsed.

### 3. Comparable for an exact operation

Distinct empirical results may be compared only when every material mismatch needed by the requested operation is either:

- shown irrelevant by an existing exact governed scope rule; or
- covered by the exact positive authority kind that owns that mismatch.

For multi-source/multi-wave operations this may require separate exact SourceUniverseRelation authorities for semantic equivalence, respondent linkage, overlap/nonduplication, sample pooling, estimand composition or backfill. One relation kind never grants another. Where cross-measure/value portability is a premise, a separately governed measurement-portability/equivalence authority is required; current HQ has explicitly not proved a general owner/load for that authority.

### 4. Compositionally related

Two or more distinct universes/results are compositionally related only for the exact composition operation positively authorized (for example `ESTIMAND_COMPOSITION` and, if actual sample pooling is performed, separately `SAMPLE_POOLING`, plus overlap/deduplication and design/weight/estimand premises as required). Composition preserves source/universe identities. It is not semantic equivalence, measurement equivalence, respondent linkage or global comparability.

### 5. Noncomparable

`NONCOMPARABLE` is a first-class governed negative state. It must be preserved when positively supplied by the exact source/universe, empirical-fact, discovery, admission or terminal authority for the relevant scope. A worker may not downgrade it to distinct, unknown, empty or comparable.

### 6. Missing/unknown authority

Missing bridge authority is **not** positive noncomparability unless an accepted rule explicitly classifies that case as `NONCOMPARABLE`. The lawful default is fail-closed unresolved comparison: no pooling, no equivalence, no composition, no backfill and no comparative inference that depends on the missing premise.

## Dimension rulings

- **Different variables:** same labels/codes/scales/question text do not establish equivalence. Positive binding to the same governed semantic unit may permit semantic reuse; cross-value/measurement comparison additionally requires the exact portability/equivalence authority when the operation depends on it.
- **Different waves:** raw/canonical wave and field/reference time remain universe identity. Cross-wave reuse/comparison requires positive wave/source semantic equivalence for the semantic premise and every other operation-specific bridge actually needed. `latest` is never a merge operator.
- **Different source universes:** distinct by default. Use only exact `SourceUniverseRelation` kinds; no generic `composable=true` is lawful.
- **Different populations/frames:** distinct estimand/universe identities. Comparison/composition requires exact target-population/frame and estimand authority plus any sample-pooling/overlap/deduplication authority demanded by the operation.
- **Different time scopes:** time is a first-class coordinate. No general current temporal-portability owner was proved by this court; cross-time comparison therefore requires an already-existing exact temporal/semantic operation authority or remains unresolved/noncomparable as governed.
- **Different weights/design regimes:** weight/design/estimand authority does not grant measurement equivalence. Same numeric result or common weight label is irrelevant. Compare only under the same exact measure basis or exact positive operation-specific bridge authority, including measurement portability if required.
- **Different routing/risk sets:** risk-set identity is constitutive of predicate/query/fact identity. Different risk sets are distinct conditioned populations; no denominator complement, incidence inference or implicit subgroup pooling is allowed. A comparison requires positive authority for the exact relation/estimand being asserted.
- **Different semantic authorities:** authority instance, semantic claim identity and current selection are distinct. A typed lifecycle event such as recertification may preserve the same claim while changing authority instance; correction/scope change/ontology expansion does not become equivalence by recency. Exact lifecycle/current-selection and semantic interpretation authority must be respected.

## Court disposition

The missing C07 authority can be narrowed to one HQ-adoptable comparison guard: compare only under exact identity or positively authorized operation-scoped bridges for every material mismatch; otherwise preserve distinctness and fail closed, while honoring explicit noncomparability.

What remains missing is not the anti-inference law. What remains missing are positive authority instances/owners for particular bridge classes—most importantly general measurement portability, plus any proposed temporal, risk-set, population or semantic bridge not already positively governed.

This court is non-dispositive until HQ adopts or revises the minimal rule.
