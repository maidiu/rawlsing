# L01 — DOWNSTREAM NONCOLLAPSE LAW

## 1. Learned empirical world, not actor interiority

The fracture map describes population/branch-conditioned empirical structure. It is PRE-V3 learned-world evidence.

It may describe:

- modal population response configurations;
- positional shifts in those configurations;
- supported minority and alternative routes;
- residual/category-fit failure surfaces;
- same-respondent cotravel/co-signature;
- uncertainty and support;
- branch-conditioned historical possibility.

It does not itself describe what a generated actor believes, remembers, chooses or will become.

`EMPIRICAL_BRANCH_FIELD != ACTOR_BELIEF`

`DEMOGRAPHIC_PROBABILITY != PERSONHOOD`

## 2. Predictor noncollapse

A demographic/structural/positional predictor belongs to the conditioning/determinicity layer. It may alter the empirical probability or branch distribution of responses. It must not be promoted into:

- an attitude node;
- a causal essence;
- a personality trait;
- an identity essence;
- a direct belief assignment;
- an individual prediction asserted as fact.

The later V550 directive states the same rule operationally: “A demographic split does not imply essence,” and forbids `raw survey variable -> persona prose`.

## 3. Field-prior law

The primary originary session allows branch results to generate relation modifiers/learned-world signals, but states the epistemic ceiling directly: **“field priors, not individual beliefs.”**

Therefore a learned branch output can lawfully say that a response/interpretive frame is more or less available, visible, prevalent, aligned, contradictory, or supported within an empirical population field. It cannot say that a particular future actor holds that response.

## 4. Relation/structural transformation law

Empirical cotravel or branch membership is not automatically a named semantic relation.

A lawful downstream bridge requires separately governed ingredients such as:

- exact response-option meaning;
- question structural mapping;
- relation-axis/target semantics where applicable;
- strata relevance;
- transformation/admission authority;
- forbidden-inference controls;
- provenance and uncertainty.

Thus:

`EMPIRICAL_COTRAVEL -> NAMED_RELATION` requires separate authority.

A semantic relation authority may annotate or interpret empirical topology; it may not manufacture empirical cotravel.

## 5. Person↔stratum downstream usability

The mandated app itinerary gives the downstream success criterion. For a generated person’s materially relevant strata, later governed surfaces must be capable of retaining:

- stratum/population modal attitude configuration;
- relevant positional branch shift;
- recursively conditioned subgroup structure;
- supported minority configurations and exceptions;
- the person’s separately sampled/conditioned configuration;
- person↔stratum alignment;
- divergence;
- minority resonance;
- contradiction;
- cross-stratum reinforcement/opposition on components;
- temporal movement and confidence.

This remains probabilistic/field-like. Aggregate population structure must not collapse into actor interiority.

## 6. Empirical possibility, not direct realization

The fracture map may contribute to a later **empirical possibility field** for a situated actor only after lawful comparability/conditioning and under separate governance. The result is still a field of historically supported possibilities.

Actor-specific realization belongs downstream. Authority 35 freezes the stage jurisdiction:

`PRE_V3 = ACTOR_INDEPENDENT_LEARNED_EMPIRICAL_WORLD`

`V3 = ACTOR_SPECIFIC_REALIZATION_HISTORY_BELIEF_RESPONSE`

L01 does not specify the V3 metabolism algorithm.

## 7. No fabricated fill

Where empirical support, semantic mapping, comparability or transformation authority is absent, downstream layers must preserve the absence/block/uncertainty. They may not fill the gap with plausible-sounding demographic stereotypes or invented pressure values.

The app itinerary marks fabricated fill and demographic-to-personality assignment as RED failures.

## 8. Minority noncollapse downstream

A later aggregate/structural/person-position surface must be able to preserve minority resonance and divergence from the modal stratum field. If downstream representation retains only the modal branch, it has destroyed a central originary purpose of the fracture map.

## 9. Negative ceiling

This law does not:

- define structural relation semantics;
- authorize a particular V551/V552/V553 design;
- determine actor comparability rules;
- determine V3 metabolism;
- select A/B/C or any physical architecture;
- assert that the current downstream implementation already preserves the required distinctions.

It freezes only the semantic noncollapse conditions HQ must use in later adjudication.
