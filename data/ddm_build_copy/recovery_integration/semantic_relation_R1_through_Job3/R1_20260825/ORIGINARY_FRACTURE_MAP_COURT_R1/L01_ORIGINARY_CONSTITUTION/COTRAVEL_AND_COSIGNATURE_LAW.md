# L01 — COTRAVEL AND CO-SIGNATURE LAW

## 1. Purpose

The fracture map is not complete when it has merely found a demographic/positional splitter. Once a supported branch is identified, the originary design asks:

**What other substantive responses/opinions travel with this branch among the exact same respondents?**

This is empirical cotravel/co-signature discovery.

## 2. Exact cohort binding

A cotravel result must be conditioned on the actual branch respondent universe. The anchor is at minimum the branch identity plus the anchor question/response or response pattern.

Preserve:

- source/source-family and wave/release identity;
- exact respondent universe/cohort identity;
- anchor question identity;
- anchor response-presence or response-pattern identity;
- candidate other question/response identity;
- denominators and support;
- weights where lawfully available;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- response-semantic/mapping authority;
- provenance and replay lineage.

Cotravel between two response labels without a lawful same-respondent universe is not the intended object.

## 3. Response-response, not demographic-response

The historical continuation pack and later V550 directive are explicit that attitude-cloud/co-signature features are other mapped substantive survey question responses. Demographic/position variables may define or condition the branch, but they are not cloud features.

`POSITIONAL_CONDITION -> BRANCH_MEMBERSHIP`

`OTHER_SUBSTANTIVE_RESPONSES -> COTRAVEL_FEATURES`

not:

`DEMOGRAPHIC_FIELD -> ATTITUDE_CLOUD_NODE`

This preserves the difference between:

1. **determinicity** — which social position predicts/structures a response branch; and
2. **cotravel** — which other responses are empirically associated inside that branch.

## 4. Branch-conditioned cotravel

The same response-response relationship may differ by branch. Therefore a global cloud cannot substitute for branch-conditioned clouds/co-signatures.

The learned empirical world must be able to preserve that:

- responses A and B travel together in branch X;
- they do not travel together, reverse, or become underpowered in branch Y;
- a higher-order configuration may exist even if every globally aggregated pair is weak.

This is why the app-level continuity gate says the information can reside in the **joint empirical configuration and its population conditioning**.

## 5. Pairwise ceiling

Historical V549/V549b evidence attests real branch/answer co-signature and attitude-cloud surfaces. It does not authorize the stronger statement that a set of pairwise associations is a complete representation of branch joint morphology.

`PAIRWISE_ASSOCIATION != COMPLETE_BRANCH_COSIGNATURE_TOPOLOGY`

Where the originary morphology depends on a same-respondent configuration of three or more responses, preservation requires the joint configuration or an information-equivalent reconstruction path; a collection of pair marginals cannot simply be presumed sufficient.

## 6. Empirical association != semantic relation authority

A co-signature says that responses empirically travel together in a governed respondent universe. It does not by itself establish a named semantic relation such as goal↔means, principle↔application, commitment↔cost, norm↔conduct, or contradiction.

Conversely, a semantic relation authority may annotate or help interpret empirical topology but may not manufacture cotravel that is absent from respondent evidence.

`EMPIRICAL_COTRAVEL != NAMED_SEMANTIC_RELATION`

`SEMANTIC_RELATION != EMPIRICAL_COTRAVEL_WITNESS`

Any bridge from cotravel to relation/structural evidence requires separate governed transformation/admission.

## 7. Minority cotravel

A supported minority/alternative/residual branch remains eligible for co-signature analysis. The machine may not reserve cotravel only for the modal branch; doing so would erase exactly the structurally informative configuration the fracture-map grammar was designed to expose.

## 8. Negative ceiling

This law does not:

- prescribe a storage format or architecture;
- select pairwise versus higher-order physical materialization strategy;
- make demographic coordinates attitude features;
- assign cotravel to an individual actor;
- treat a discovered association as causal;
- turn empirical association into a named structural relation without separate authority.
