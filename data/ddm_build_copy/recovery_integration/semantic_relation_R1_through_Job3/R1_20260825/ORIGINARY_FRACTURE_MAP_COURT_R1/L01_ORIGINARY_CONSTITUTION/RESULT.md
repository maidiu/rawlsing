## Central holding

The originary object is explicitly named in the primary Library session as the **“Recursive Multi-Label Positional Response Tree.”** The better zero-loss constitutional name is **Recursive Multi-Label Positional Response / Empirical Fracture Map** because its discovery unit is compositional rather than a conventional decision-tree node.

The machine begins from a governed substantive question/response surface, characterizes the overall response distribution or modal response pattern, discovers which social/positional coordinates structure that pattern, distinguishes the routes that reproduce or fracture it, recursively analyzes the discovered branches, and then asks what other substantive responses co-travel among the exact same respondents inside those branches.

## Required-question answers

### 1. Exact discovery unit

The root unit is a governed substantive **question/response surface**, but the operative recursive unit is an exact **respondent cohort / branch node** defined by source/universe, response semantics, response-presence or response-pattern membership, positional conditioning, and recursion lineage. A relation or attitude cloud is a derived empirical surface over the exact branch respondent universe; it is not the root unit by itself.

A branch node must retain at minimum its definition, respondent support, response distribution, splitters, shape, confidence/uncertainty and stop reason. The originary example is of the form `Lebanon + MA+ + non-SES`, not a free-floating label.

### 2. Initial/modal pattern

Start with the full substantive answer spread and identify the dominant response **configuration/pattern**. For multi-select items a modal branch such as `selected_SES` means response presence, not `SES only`. Concentration, alternatives, fragmentation and multimodality remain visible.

### 3. Primary positional predictor

A primary predictor is the demographic/structural/positional coordinate that best separates or predicts the response pattern or the probability of staying in/exiting it within the governed empirical universe. It is a determinicity/association result. It does **not** mean the predictor causally creates the answer, is an attitude, is a personality trait, or reveals an individual’s interior belief.

### 4. Branch roles

Authority 35 freezes the zero-loss branch ontology:

- **conformer** — predictor condition present and modal response/configuration reproduced;
- **deviant / predictor-violating exit** — predictor condition present but nonmodal response/configuration;
- **minority converger / alternative-route converger** — predictor condition absent but modal response/configuration reached anyway;
- **alternative/minority branch** — predictor condition absent and nonmodal supported response/configuration;
- **split / multimodal branch** — no single branch outcome lawfully dominates enough to erase competing supported configurations;
- **residual / none / category-failure branch** — a substantive residual response or supported failure of supplied categories remains typed empirical structure rather than junk;
- **underpowered** — evaluation state, not a substantive social type.

`predictor absent` here means the relevant predictor condition is not instantiated; it does **not** mean predictor data are missing. Missing predictor data remain a distinct missing/unresolved state.

### 5. Secondary predictors

Search for the next positional splitter **inside each supported branch**, including predictors of conformity, exit/deviation, alternative response, and residual/category-failure. The originary session says “splitters inside every branch.” A later V550 implementation directive narrowed its written secondary-search loop to outlier branches; that is historical implementation evidence and does not narrow this constitution.

### 6. Recursive continuation

Once a branch is discovered, that actual branch becomes the next conditioned respondent universe. Recompute response distribution/determinicity inside it, discover the next splitter and branch roles, and continue until a governed stop condition fires. Recursion follows empirical fracture and alternative routes; it is not arbitrary nested demographic slicing.

### 7. Same-respondent cross-question cotravel/co-signature

For each supported branch, the machine must be able to ask which **other mapped substantive attitude/opinion responses** disproportionately travel with the anchor response/pattern/branch among the exact same respondents. Demographic/position variables may condition the branch but may not become cloud features. Preserve respondent-universe identity, question/response identity, denominators, weights where available, confidence/uncertainty, provenance/authority and replay identity.

The historical V549/V549b lineage attests branch-level co-signature and answer/branch attitude-cloud functionality, but those pairwise/co-cloud surfaces do not by themselves prove complete higher-order branch topology.

### 8. Multi-select morphology

Both are mandatory:

1. **response-presence branches** — option selected/not selected;
2. **response-pattern branches** — exact or governed combinations such as `SES only`, `SES + religion`, `religion without SES`, `none without SES/religion`, and other multi-frame patterns.

Presence and pattern are non-substitutable. `SES + religion != SES only`; `religion present != religion without SES`.

### 9. Branch determinicity profiles

At minimum preserve distinct profiles for:

- overall determinicity;
- majority/conforming-branch determinicity;
- exit/deviant-branch determinicity;
- alternative/minority-branch determinicity;
- residual/none/category-failure determinicity.

One global top splitter cannot substitute for these branch-conditioned profiles.

### 10. Support, missingness, weighting, uncertainty and stops

The originary law requires hard support/stability rules: minimum unweighted support, minimum weighted support where applicable, minimum response-option support, maximum depth, confidence penalties for multi-select overlap, and no interpretation when underpowered. The primary session gives **illustrative**, not constitutional, numbers (`n < 50` exploratory, `n < 25` do not split, selected-option count `< 10` do not infer channel) and explicitly states that exact thresholds are owner-tunable but thresholds are mandatory.

Every node must retain n, weighted n where lawful, missingness, confidence/uncertainty and an exact stop reason. Later implementation material attests stop reasons such as underpowered, no gain/no meaningful split, max depth, no remaining indicators and pure/near-pure. Special/missing/DK/refusal/inapplicable responses are not ordinary substantive answers and must not be collapsed with a substantive `NONE_OF_THESE` option.

### 11. Branch shapes and minority visibility

The originary session requires shape visibility including:

- smooth gradient;
- sharp polarization;
- majority with stable minority;
- fragmented/multimodal;
- category-collapse into none-of-these.

Supported minority branches and exceptions are information, not noise. A modal stereotype is a failure if it erases them.

### 12. Learned-world / relation outputs

Branch results were intended to feed numeric branch determinicity and relation-channel implications, and later learned-world surfaces may preserve relation evidence, structural pressure, alignment/divergence, minority resonance, contradiction and cross-stratum reinforcement/opposition. But an empirical branch or co-signature may enter those surfaces only through separately governed semantic transformation/admission. Empirical co-travel is not itself a named semantic relation merely because two answers travel together.

### 13. Forbidden collapses

Forbidden:

- persona assignment from the fracture map;
- direct actor-belief assignment;
- demographic predictor -> attitude node;
- demographic probability -> personality/personhood;
- raw survey variable -> persona prose;
- fabricated substantive interpretation of residual/none/category-failure;
- semantic relation labels manufacturing empirical cotravel;
- flattening supported minority/recursive structure into the modal branch.

The originary session’s concise ceiling is: **“field priors, not individual beliefs.”**
