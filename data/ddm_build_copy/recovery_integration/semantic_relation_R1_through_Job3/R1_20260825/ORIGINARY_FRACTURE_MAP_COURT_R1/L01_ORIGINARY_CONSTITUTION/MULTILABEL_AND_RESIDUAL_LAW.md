# L01 — MULTI-LABEL AND RESIDUAL LAW

## 1. Multi-label noncollapse

The originary Arab Barometer exemplar is explicitly multi-select. Therefore a single-label decision-tree interpretation is unconstitutional.

Two simultaneous analytical views are mandatory:

### A. Response-presence morphology

For every governed substantive option, retain selected/not-selected membership where support permits.

Examples:

- `selected_SES = yes/no`
- `selected_religion = yes/no`
- `selected_region = yes/no`
- `selected_nationality = yes/no`
- `selected_none = yes/no`

This view asks which positions make each response/frame empirically available.

### B. Response-pattern morphology

Also retain exact or governed joint response patterns among the same respondents.

Examples from the originary session:

- `SES only`
- `SES + religion`
- `religion without SES`
- `none of these without SES/religion`
- `SES + nationality`
- multi-frame combinations

The constitutional identities are:

`RESPONSE_PRESENCE != RESPONSE_PATTERN`

`SES_PLUS_RELIGION != SES_ONLY`

`RELIGION_WITHOUT_SES != RELIGION_PRESENT`

`NONE_WITHOUT_SES_RELIGION != GENERIC_NON_SES`

A system that preserves only marginal option presence may not claim to preserve response-pattern morphology unless the exact same-respondent joint configuration is reconstructible.

## 2. Modal law under multi-select

For a multi-select question, a statement such as “SES is the modal branch” means, unless explicitly pattern-qualified, that SES is the dominant **selected option/presence**, not that `SES only` is the dominant exact pattern.

The machine must retain the rest of the option distribution and the joint response morphology. It may not create a false exclusive partition from overlapping option-presence branches.

## 3. Residual / NONE_OF_THESE law

A source-native substantive option such as `NONE_OF_THESE` is not automatically junk. It may become a lawful residual/category-fit branch with:

- exact respondent cohort identity;
- source/question/option semantics;
- n and weighted n where lawful;
- response distribution;
- positional splitters;
- determinicity profile;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- stop reason;
- further recursive fracture where supported.

The primary originary session associates such a branch with several **possible** readings—weak category fit, refusal, confusion, depoliticized opacity, unmeasured local grammar, nonsalience, danger of explicit naming—but these are not interchangeable and none is automatically selected by the response itself.

Therefore:

`NONE_OF_THESE_BRANCH != LICENSED_PSYCHOLOGICAL_INTERPRETATION`

The lawful empirical statement may be no stronger than: the supplied named response categories do not exhaust the supported response configuration for this cohort.

## 4. Substantive residual != missing/special response

A substantive `NONE_OF_THESE` option must remain distinct from:

- don’t know;
- refusal;
- no answer;
- inapplicable / IAP;
- skipped;
- uncodeable;
- not asked;
- missing data.

Later V550 implementation material preserves the same distinction by excluding special/missing responses from substantive determinicity/cloud features while retaining them in audit/missingness surfaces.

`SUBSTANTIVE_NONE != DK_REFUSAL_MISSING_INAPPLICABLE`

## 5. Minority preservation

After the dominant response is characterized, the machine must continue to expose supported alternatives. It may not repeatedly rediscover the dominant branch and treat everything else as one undifferentiated residual.

A supported minority may represent the most structurally informative fracture in the empirical regularity. Low prevalence does not make it noise.

## 6. Support and stop ceiling

Presence/pattern/residual branches remain subject to the exact support/uncertainty/stop law. Underpowered morphology is retained as underpowered; it is not upgraded to a stable minority pattern. Conversely, a supported minority may not be dropped merely because it is not modal.

## 7. Negative ceiling

This law does not:

- define new universal numeric thresholds;
- infer causal mechanisms from option combinations;
- treat demographics as response/attitude nodes;
- infer an actor’s belief from a branch;
- equate substantive none with refusal/missingness;
- authorize a specific interpretation of category failure without separate evidence.
