# L01 — STOP, SUPPORT, MISSINGNESS AND UNCERTAINTY LAW

## 1. Thresholds are mandatory; exact values are not universally frozen by the originary session

The primary Library record requires hard stop/support conditions at every recursive node:

- minimum unweighted n;
- minimum weighted n where weights are applicable;
- minimum response count per option/pattern;
- maximum tree depth;
- confidence penalty for multi-select overlap;
- no substantive interpretation when underpowered.

The same record says the exact thresholds can be **owner-tuned, but there must be thresholds**.

It supplies examples:

- `n < 50` -> exploratory only;
- `n < 25` -> report distribution but do not split;
- selected-option count `< 10` -> do not infer channel.

These are recovered examples, not universal constitutional constants.

## 2. Node support payload

Every branch node must expose enough information to distinguish evidence strength from branch meaning:

- `n_unweighted`;
- `n_weighted` where lawful/available;
- response-option or pattern support;
- missingness/special-response burden;
- confidence/uncertainty state;
- exact stop reason;
- source/universe/measure/weight authority needed to interpret those quantities.

A branch label without support and confidence is not a zero-loss branch result.

## 3. Weight law

Originary intent requires weighted and unweighted support to remain visible. Later implementation directives further operationalize:

- use weights where available;
- preserve unweighted n always;
- do not crash because weights are missing;
- carry weight status;
- use unweighted support for implementation quarantine thresholds in that later V550 design.

The constitutional ceiling is therefore:

- weights may affect estimates only under lawful source/measure authority;
- missing weights must be explicit;
- no fabricated weights;
- no universal claim that one later implementation’s threshold convention is the only lawful future implementation.

## 4. Missingness and substantive-response separation

Special/missing responses such as DK, refusal, no answer, IAP/inapplicable, skipped or uncodeable are not ordinary substantive answers.

They must remain auditable as missingness/special-response states and may have their own distributions where governed, but they may not be silently treated as substantive response branches.

A source-native substantive `NONE_OF_THESE` option is different: it can be a lawful substantive residual/category-fit branch.

`SUBSTANTIVE_NONE != SPECIAL_OR_MISSING`

## 5. Confidence and directional evidence

The Lebanon exemplar is explicit that very small branches may be **directional rather than stable**. This distinction is constitutional:

`OBSERVED_PATTERN != STABLE_INFERENCE`

A low-support branch may retain its observed distribution while being blocked from further splitting or relation-channel inference. Underpowered evidence must not be upgraded to a stable minority topology merely because it is interesting.

## 6. Required stop families

The primary record directly lists:

- low n;
- no meaningful split;
- maximum depth;
- unstable branch.

Later V550 implementation material further attests operational stop families such as:

- underpowered;
- no gain;
- no remaining indicators;
- pure or near pure;
- manual exclusion where governed.

The constitution requires an explicit stop reason, but does not make every later implementation label mandatory if an information-equivalent governed state exists.

## 7. Typed-negative and blocked-state noncollapse

Authority 35 requires the learned world to distinguish, where governed:

- supported;
- valid-empty;
- underpowered;
- noncomparable;
- unresolved/missing-authority;
- category-failure.

These states are not interchangeable.

`VALID_EMPTY != UNDERPOWERED`

`UNDERPOWERED != MISSING_AUTHORITY`

`MISSING_AUTHORITY != NONCOMPARABLE`

`CATEGORY_FAILURE != MISSING_DATA`

A system that collapses them into one null/empty state cannot claim zero-loss conservation.

## 8. Search/stop integrity

A stop rule must describe why this branch stopped; it may not silently erase unsearched alternative branches. The originary recursive objective is to follow majority, exit, alternative and residual routes until lawful stopping. Computational convenience is not itself semantic evidence that no further branch exists.

## 9. Negative ceiling

This constitution does not select exact universal numerical thresholds, confidence formulas, weighting estimators or search algorithms. It freezes the information and decision distinctions that any implementation must preserve.
