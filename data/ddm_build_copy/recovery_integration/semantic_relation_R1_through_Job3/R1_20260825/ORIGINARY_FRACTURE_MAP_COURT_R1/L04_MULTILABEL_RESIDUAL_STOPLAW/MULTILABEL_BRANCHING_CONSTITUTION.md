# MULTI-LABEL BRANCHING CONSTITUTION — L04 R1

Status: `RECOVERED__BINDING_ORIGINARY_SEMANTIC_CONSTITUTION`

Scope: multi-label / minority branching law only. This file does not judge current runtime completeness.

## 1. Governing distinction

The originary fracture-map grammar is not a generic decision tree over a single mutually exclusive label. For a multi-select source item, Rawlsing must preserve two non-substitutable views of the same respondent-level response morphology:

1. **response-presence branching** — for each governed option, whether that exact respondent selected it;
2. **response-pattern branching** — the exact governed co-selection pattern carried by that respondent.

The Wave VIII Lebanon discussion states this directly and names examples such as `SES only`, `SES + religion`, `religion without SES`, `none of these without SES/religion`, `SES + nationality`, and multi-frame respondents. The key law is:

`OPTION_PRESENT != OPTION_ONLY`

`SES_PRESENT != SES_ONLY`

`RELIGION_PRESENT != RELIGION_WITHOUT_SES`

`PRESENCE_BRANCH != PATTERN_BRANCH`

Both views are required.

## 2. Single-label versus multi-label discovery

### Single-label surface

Where source authority proves one mutually exclusive substantive response per respondent in the relevant risk set, response categories may form a partition. In that bounded case, dominant/majority and minority labels may describe disjoint response groups, subject to support and missingness law.

### Multi-label surface

Where source authority permits multiple co-occurring selections, no selected option defines an exclusive respondent class merely because it is the most prevalent option. The correct vocabulary is:

- **dominant response-presence**: the most prevalent selected option under an exact risk set;
- **dominant response-pattern**: the most prevalent exact co-selection pattern;
- **presence exit**: an exact predicate such as `NOT selected_SES`;
- **pattern alternative**: an exact pattern predicate such as `selected_religion AND NOT selected_SES`;
- **multi-frame branch**: a supported exact pattern containing multiple substantive selections.

Thus an “SES branch” in the originary Wave VIII discussion means respondents who selected SES, not respondents who selected only SES.

## 3. Majority/minority law

`MINORITY != NOISE`

The originary discussion makes the purpose of the recursive tree the discovery of where a dominant regularity fractures. A positionally structured minority deviation may be the visible contradiction and must remain discoverable when supported.

For multi-label items, `majority/minority` vocabulary is admissible only when its predicate is explicit. A respondent may belong simultaneously to multiple presence branches. Therefore:

- a “non-SES minority” is the exact `NOT selected_SES` branch, not `total N - SES mention count` as a derived pseudo-sample;
- a “religion minority” is ambiguous unless it means either `selected_religion` presence or an exact pattern such as `religion without SES`;
- a pattern may be minority-supported even when one of its component options is globally common;
- a supported minority branch retains its own secondary predictors, response distribution, support, uncertainty, and continuation/stop disposition.

## 4. Recursive fracture law

The recursion is not arbitrary demographic nesting. The recovered sequence is:

`dominant response/configuration`
`-> predictor`
`-> conforming versus violating/deviant branch`
`-> secondary predictor inside each branch`
`-> alternative/minority/residual response`
`-> further fracture where supported`

At each lawful node, the analyzer must be capable of asking separately:

- what predicts the dominant/conforming branch;
- what predicts exit from that branch;
- what alternative response or pattern the exit branch takes;
- what predicts that alternative;
- what happens in the residual/category-failure branch;
- whether further splitting remains supported/stable.

The originary determinicity profiles therefore include at minimum:

- `overall_determinicity`;
- `majority_branch_determinicity` / conforming determinicity;
- `exit_branch_determinicity`;
- `alternative_branch_determinicity`;
- `residual_branch_determinicity`.

## 5. Respondent-set law for multi-label residuals

Multi-select residuals must be formed by exact predicates over the original respondent records and risk set.

Forbidden:

- subtracting option marginal counts from total N to manufacture residual membership when selections overlap;
- removing respondents from the empirical universe after characterizing a dominant presence;
- treating `SES + religion` as belonging exclusively to either an SES-only or religion-only partition;
- inferring pattern membership from aggregate percentages.

Required examples:

- `non_SES := NOT selected_SES`;
- `non_SES_non_religion := NOT selected_SES AND NOT selected_religion`;
- `SES_only := selected_SES AND NOT any_other_governed_substantive_option`;
- `SES_plus_religion := selected_SES AND selected_religion` with the rest of the pattern retained where governed;
- `religion_without_SES := selected_religion AND NOT selected_SES`.

The source respondent identity and exact denominator/risk-set authority remain unchanged; only the branch predicate changes.

## 6. Branch-shape vocabulary

The originary node report names these empirical shapes:

- `SMOOTH_GRADIENT`;
- `SHARP_POLARIZATION`;
- `MAJORITY_WITH_STABLE_MINORITY`;
- `FRAGMENTED_MULTIMODAL`;
- `CATEGORY_COLLAPSE_INTO_NONE_OF_THESE`.

These are descriptions to be earned from governed evidence, not labels inferred from intuition.

### Stable minority

A stable minority is a materially non-dominant branch whose exact predicate has sufficient governed support and stability to survive the node’s uncertainty/stop law. No universal percentage cutoff was recovered.

### Fragmented / multimodal

Multiple supported response presences/patterns remain materially salient and the branch is not well described by a single dominant-versus-residual split. Multi-label overlap must remain visible rather than forcing an exclusive mode assignment.

### Polarization

A sharp, governed separation between competing response configurations or predictor-conditioned response regimes. Merely observing two response labels does not prove polarization.

### Smooth gradient

A governed response tendency varies gradually across an ordered predictor or comparable surface rather than producing a sharp supported fracture. No universal gradient metric was recovered in the originary evidence.

### Underpowered

`UNDERPOWERED` is not a substantive branch shape. It is a support/uncertainty disposition that limits or stops interpretation.

## 7. Node minimum report

The originary formulation requires every node to retain at least:

- exact node/branch predicate definition;
- unweighted `n`;
- governed weighted `n` when authorized;
- missingness/special-response accounting;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- response-presence distribution;
- response-pattern distribution where multi-label;
- top splitter(s) under a governed comparison rule;
- branch shape or typed inability to classify shape;
- stop/continue disposition and exact stop reason;
- provenance/risk-set/response-semantic authority sufficient for replay.

## 8. Wave VIII source check

The released Wave VIII questionnaire verifies that Q873 is explicitly `SELECT ALL THAT APPLY`. It also makes `None of these` exclusive: option 13 cannot be combined with another option and is selected only when the respondent says no to all substantive options. `Don't know` and `Refused to answer` are separate special responses.

This source morphology demonstrates why response-pattern law cannot be reconstructed from one winning option or from marginal counts alone.

## 9. Negative ceiling

This constitution does not authorize:

- treating demographic predictors as causal essences;
- assigning an individual belief/personality/persona from branch membership;
- inventing source response equivalences;
- fixed universal support thresholds;
- interpreting a residual motive without separate semantic authority;
- claiming that the current runtime already implements all of this law.
