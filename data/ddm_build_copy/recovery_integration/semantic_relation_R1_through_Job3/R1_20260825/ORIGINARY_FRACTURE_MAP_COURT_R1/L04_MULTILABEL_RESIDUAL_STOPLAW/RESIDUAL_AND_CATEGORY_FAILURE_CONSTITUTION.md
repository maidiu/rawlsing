# RESIDUAL AND CATEGORY-FAILURE CONSTITUTION — L04 R1

Status: `RECOVERED__BINDING_ORIGINARY_SEMANTIC_CONSTITUTION`

## 1. Residual search is predicate continuation, not destructive subtraction

The originary fracture-map grammar does not stop once the dominant response has been described. It asks where the regularity fails, what the exit branch selects instead, what predicts those alternatives, and then repeats recursively while support and stability remain sufficient.

For multi-label items this imposes a hard conservation law:

`RESIDUAL_BRANCH = EXACT_CONDITIONED_RESPONDENT_PREDICATE`

not:

`RESIDUAL_BRANCH = TOTAL_MINUS_DOMINANT_MARGINAL_COUNT`.

Because selections may co-occur, marginal subtraction can both double-count and falsely remove respondents. Residual membership must be evaluated from original same-respondent response records under the exact risk set.

Examples recovered from the Wave VIII Lebanon discussion:

- `Lebanon + MA+ + NOT selected_SES`;
- then `Lebanon + MA+ + NOT selected_SES + NOT selected_religion`;
- analogous continuation inside the no-formal-education branch.

The characterized dominant branch remains part of the empirical world. Residual search adds conditioned branches; it does not mutate or erase the source universe.

## 2. Alternative and residual routes remain first-class empirical structure

The originary system separately asks:

- who stays in the dominant branch;
- who exits it;
- where the exit branch goes;
- what predicts the alternative route;
- what remains after named alternatives are conditioned away.

A supported residual branch is therefore not an error term by default. It has its own:

- exact branch predicate;
- support/weight/missingness/uncertainty state;
- response distribution/pattern morphology;
- secondary predictors;
- shape classification where support permits;
- stop reason;
- replay lineage.

`DOMINANT_CHARACTERIZED != SEARCH_COMPLETE`

`RESIDUAL != NOISE`

## 3. `NONE_OF_THESE` law

The Wave VIII questionnaire makes `None of these` an explicit response option in Q873 and marks it exclusive: it may be selected only when the respondent says no to all other listed options. In other multi-select discrimination batteries, analogous `None of these` options are also exclusive.

Therefore, where exact source semantics establish such an option:

- `NONE_OF_THESE_SELECTED` is an observed response state/pattern, not missing data;
- it may form a valid empirical branch if its exact risk set and support satisfy the governed support law;
- because it is exclusive, its co-selection pattern must obey the source constraint rather than being treated as an ordinary toggle alongside substantive options;
- its selection can support the narrow empirical claim that the respondent selected the source-defined none-of-these category.

The originary discussion treats a rise of none-of-these inside a residual branch as potentially important category-fit evidence. It explicitly refuses to flatten it into junk.

## 4. Category-failure interpretation ceiling

The originary discussion lists several *possible* readings of none-of-these/category collapse:

- supplied categories may fail the respondent's lived grammar;
- discrimination may not be salient;
- discrimination may be perceived but not through supplied categories;
- explicit naming may be constrained by political/social danger;
- refusal, confusion, depoliticized opacity, or unmeasured local grammar may be possibilities.

Those are hypothesis surfaces, not facts licensed by the response token alone.

Allowed without further authority:

- `NONE_OF_THESE_SELECTED`;
- exact prevalence/support of that response in its branch;
- exact predictor associations with that response where governed;
- a typed `CATEGORY_FIT_FAILURE_SURFACE` or equivalent only if its definition is explicitly limited to “supplied substantive categories not selected” and preserves source semantics;
- report that none-of-these becomes dominant/materially salient in a residual branch.

Forbidden without separate semantic/transformation authority:

- “respondent is confused”;
- “respondent distrusts the survey/state”;
- “respondent is depoliticized”;
- “respondent fears naming the cause”;
- “discrimination is not actually salient to this person”;
- any individual belief, motive, personality, consciousness, or causal essence.

`NONE_OF_THESE != PSYCHOLOGICAL_EXPLANATION`

## 5. Source-semantic negative/special states are irreducible

The following states must never be merged merely because they are all non-substantive or non-modal:

### `NONE_OF_THESE`
An explicit source response that rejects all listed substantive options where the instrument defines it that way. It can be valid observed empirical structure.

### `OTHER`
A source-defined other/specify response or other-category. It may contain substantive residual content and is not equivalent to none, DK, refusal, or missing. If the source uses “other” as an administrative toggle value inside binary item encoding, that coordinate must be interpreted through its exact response topology rather than lexical text alone.

### `DK`
An explicit “don't know” response. It expresses a source-defined knowledge/answerability state, not none-of-these and not generic missingness.

### `REFUSED`
An explicit refusal/decline to answer. Distinct from DK and missing.

### `MISSING`
No recorded response under the governed source representation. No motive may be inferred.

### `NOT_APPLICABLE` / `NOT_ASKED`
The respondent was outside the exact applicability/routing surface or the question was not asked. This changes the lawful risk set/denominator and cannot be treated as a negative substantive answer.

### `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_SPECIAL`
A special response whose exact meaning is source-governed and cannot safely be collapsed into any generic bucket until authority resolves it.

### `UNRESOLVED_RESPONSE_SEMANTICS`
The source token/topology cannot be lawfully classified. Fail closed rather than normalize by suffix, numeric code, or lexical similarity.

## 6. Instrument corroboration

Wave VIII Q873 separates:

- substantive discrimination bases;
- exclusive `None of these`;

- `Refused to answer`.

Historical Arab Barometer reconstruction material further shows why suffix-based collapse is unsafe: a `_97` member can be a substantive response in one question while `_98/_99` can be special-response tails elsewhere. Exact label/topology/routing authority, not suffix convention alone, determines semantic role.

## 7. Residual continuation stop law

Residual search continues only while the node remains lawfully analyzable. The exact originary stop-reason vocabulary includes:

- `LOW_N`;
- `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT`;
- `MAX_DEPTH`;
- `UNSTABLE_BRANCH`.

Additional support dimensions—weighted support, per-option response count, missingness, multi-label overlap confidence, uncertainty—must be evaluated under a governed rule and may force an underpowered/limited state. They must not be silently repaired by pooling semantically distinct special responses or by widening the risk set.

## 8. Stability and residual shape

A residual may be:

- a stable minority;
- sharply polarized;
- fragmented/multimodal;
- a smooth gradient;
- category-collapse/none-of-these dominated;
- underpowered/unstable and therefore not substantively classifiable.

No fixed numerical boundary for these shapes was recovered as originary binding law. The shape claim must be tied to governed metrics and support/stability authority.

## 9. Negative ceiling

This constitution conserves the empirical residual/category-failure grammar only. It does not prove current implementation coverage, infer actor beliefs, or authorize any universal threshold or substantive motive for special/negative response states.
