# D03 — MINIMUM HQ LAW GAPS

D03 recovers the semantic retention/continuation lattice. It does **not** recover enough authority to choose all production controller cutoffs or model-selection criteria.

The following are the minimum remaining HQ/versioned-law gaps before a result-driven fracture-map controller can make these decisions without invention.

## G1 — Branch continuation support/stability policy

HQ must bind the exact rule deciding whether an observed branch has enough support/stability to enter the next recursion frontier.

The rule may consume exact producer quantities already available from I03, including raw denominator, resolved target support, cell/option/pattern support, governed weighted support where authorized, exact risk-set identity and uncertainty state.

No historical `50/25/10` value is binding. Current I05 `minimum_raw_denominator = 0` is an I05 post-evaluation terminal-classification rule meaning **no additional count floor**; it is not a branch recursion rule.

## G2 — `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT / NO_GAIN`

The stop concept is recovered; the universal decision criterion is not.

HQ must bind either:
- an exact governed metric plus cutoff;
- an exact comparison/test rule;
- or another deterministic nonnumeric decision law.

Absent that rule, the controller may not invent a gain threshold from data or diagnostics.

## G3 — `UNSTABLE_BRANCH`

The stop vocabulary is recovered, but no universal instability classifier is frozen. HQ must bind the stability inputs and deterministic classification rule before instability can control production recursion.

## G4 — Stable-minority / split / multimodal control semantics

The system must preserve these shapes when lawfully measurable. D03 does not freeze a universal numeric classifier.

If shape labels affect continuation, D02/HQ or another explicit morphology authority must bind:
- exact input distribution;
- comparison basis;
- stable-minority definition;
- split/multimodal definition;
- tie/ambiguity treatment;
- typed unresolved outcome.

Shape may never be inferred merely from a descriptive label.

## G5 — Maximum depth value

The existence of a maximum-depth stop is binding. Its value is operation/controller-specific.

The frozen AS3 benchmark `recursive depth = 18` belongs to that benchmark terrain and is not universal Authority35 fracture-map law. A production controller must bind its own exact maximum depth in versioned authority.

## G6 — Low-support exploratory continuation

`LOW_SUPPORT` must be retained. D03 does not find authority for automatically recursing from low-support evidence.

If HQ wants an exploratory-only continuation class rather than report-only stop, it must be separately versioned and must preserve:
- the LOW_SUPPORT producer state;
- uncertainty qualification;
- nonpromotion to structural/actor truth;
- a bounded frontier;
- deterministic replay.

## G7 — Predictor frontier exhaustion dependency

D03 can freeze:

`EXACT_GOVERNED_PREDICTOR_FRONTIER_EMPTY -> STOP_AND_RETAIN_BRANCH`.

It cannot define which predictors are eligible. That is D02/predictor-selection jurisdiction. The production stop becomes executable only after an exact governed predictor frontier identity exists.

## G8 — Co-signature question frontier exhaustion dependency

D03 can freeze:

`EXACT_GOVERNED_COSIGNATURE_QUESTION_FRONTIER_EMPTY -> STOP_COSIGNATURE_CONTINUATION_AND_RETAIN_BRANCH`.

It cannot define the substantive-question enumeration law. That is D04 jurisdiction.

## G9 — Pure / near-pure status

Historical implementation evidence attests pure/near-pure as a stop trace, but L04's exact originary stop vocabulary does not independently freeze it as a universal state.

HQ must choose one of two explicit treatments:
1. define a separately governed `PURE/NEAR_PURE` classifier; or
2. declare pure/near-pure an authorized reason under `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT`.

Until then it is corroborative historical vocabulary, not production controller law.

## What does NOT remain open

HQ need not reopen these principles:

- branch retention and recursion eligibility are distinct;
- stopping recursion does not delete the branch/result;
- supported minority/residual/none branches are not noise by default;
- underpowered may remain reportable while deeper splitting is blocked;
- valid-empty, underpowered, missing/unavailable, unresolved, noncomparable and zero-support are distinct;
- explicit `NONE_OF_THESE` is not missing/DK/refusal/not-applicable;
- exact support/measure/provenance/replay material must survive terminalization;
- a typed nonpositive result is retained rather than converted to row absence;
- continuation policy may not mutate producer I03/I05 semantic states;
- no continuation rule may create structural meaning, actor belief or V3 realization.
