## 1. Dispositive holding

The recovered law is two-dimensional:

`BRANCH_RETENTION != RECURSION_ELIGIBILITY`

and therefore:

`STOP_RECURSION != DELETE_BRANCH`.

A fracture-map branch is first an empirical result surface with exact respondent-universe, predicate/branch identity, response distribution, support, state, provenance and replay. Recursive continuation is a separate controller disposition over that retained result. A branch can lawfully survive as learned empirical evidence even when no further child search is permitted or useful.

The strongest zero-loss rule is:

`OBSERVE/EVALUATE BRANCH -> RETAIN EXACT RESULT OR TYPED NONPOSITIVE RECEIPT -> CLASSIFY CONTINUATION DISPOSITION -> IF STOP, RETAIN TERMINAL STOP REASON`

not:

`IF NOT RECURSED -> DISCARD BRANCH`.

This is supported independently by the originary constitution, L04 stop-law recovery, historical V548 behavior, and current PRE553 typed-state/replay law.

## 2. Exact retained object when recursion stops

Where a positive or reportable branch exists, stopping recursion must not erase the information already learned. Retention must preserve, at minimum where applicable:

- exact source / wave / EmpiricalUniverse identity;
- exact risk-set and branch predicate/cohort identity;
- response topology and response-semantic authority;
- raw denominator support;
- resolved target support and complete cell counts;
- governed weighted denominator/cell support when exact weight authority exists;
- missingness / special-response accounting available under the source semantics;
- uncertainty method or typed uncertainty state;
- branch role/shape only where separately governed;
- exact ancestry / parent branch reference;
- exact replay/provenance references;
- terminal continuation disposition and exact stop reason.

For a typed nonpositive state where no positive branch payload exists, the retained learned record is the exact typed result/receipt itself, not a fabricated branch.

## 3. Continuation disposition lattice

D03 recovers four controller-level dispositions. These are not replacements for I03/I05 empirical states.

### `RETAIN_AND_CONTINUE_ELIGIBLE`

The branch/result survives and may be presented to the next governed frontier-generation step. This does not mean recursion is mandatory. Eligibility additionally depends on exact support/stability law, depth budget, remaining governed predictor/question frontier, source/risk/measure authority and branch-role construction law.

### `RETAIN_REPORT_ONLY_STOP`

The branch/result survives as learned empirical evidence, but no deeper fracture-map recursion is authorized from it under the current law. Typical causes include underpowered/insufficient evidence, unstable branch, or a deliberately report-only low-support posture.

### `RETAIN_TERMINAL_STOP`

The branch/result is substantively usable at its current empirical grain but the recursion path is terminal, for example because maximum governed depth is reached, no meaningful split/gain remains, or no eligible continuation frontier remains.

### `RETAIN_TYPED_NONPOSITIVE_STOP`

No positive branch may be invented. The exact valid-empty, missing/unavailable, unresolved, noncomparable, zero-support or authority-limited result survives as a typed learned receipt with provenance/replay, and recursion stops.

These controller dispositions must reference the producer-owned empirical states. They may not overwrite `LOW_SUPPORT`, `VALID_EMPTY`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, etc.

## 4. State-by-state holdings

### Supported branch

`SUPPORTED_BRANCH = RETAINED`.

If all separately governed continuation predicates pass, it is `RETAIN_AND_CONTINUE_ELIGIBLE`. If continuation terminates for max depth, no meaningful split/gain, or exhausted lawful frontier, it becomes `RETAIN_TERMINAL_STOP` without losing the branch.

### Supported but not recurse-eligible

This is a lawful ordinary state. A supported branch can be complete enough to report but terminal under continuation law. Support never implies mandatory recursion.

### Stable minority

A supported stable-minority configuration is first-class empirical evidence and must be retained. `STABLE_MINORITY` is a shape/role description, not a command to prune or recurse. Continuation requires the same governed support, stability, depth and frontier rules as other supported branches. No universal stable-minority classifier was recovered by D03.

### Split / multimodal

Competing supported configurations must remain jointly visible. `SPLIT/MULTIMODAL` cannot be collapsed to a modal winner merely because the controller stops. Child continuation is lawful only after exact branch predicates/roles are governed and support/stability/continuation law permits them. No universal numeric split classifier is recovered here.

### Valid-empty

`VALID_EMPTY != MISSING`.

A valid-empty result is a positive statement about a lawfully evaluated empty result/frontier at an exact identity. Retain the typed valid-empty result plus authority/provenance/replay; do not fabricate a positive branch; recursion stops.

### Underpowered / insufficient support

`UNDERPOWERED/INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT != VALID_EMPTY`.

Observed branch/distribution evidence remains reportable and retained with its support and uncertainty state, while deeper splitting and overconfident interpretation are blocked under the governed support law. The originary constitution explicitly permits an underpowered branch to remain reportable even when splitting is stopped.

### Low support / exploratory-only

`LOW_SUPPORT` is a current I03 empirical fact state and must remain visible. Current production I05 rule `PRE553_PRODUCTION_I05_NO_ADDITIONAL_MINIMUM_RAW_DENOMINATOR@1.0.0` sets only the I05 additional count floor to zero; it explicitly does not establish statistical adequacy, power or recursion eligibility.

Therefore D03 does not promote `LOW_SUPPORT` to recurse-eligible. An exploratory/report-only continuation posture is lawful only if a separate versioned controller authority defines it. Until such authority exists, `LOW_SUPPORT` is retained and continuation fails closed rather than being silently upgraded.

### Residual / NONE / category-failure

A source-semantic substantive residual such as explicit `NONE_OF_THESE`, when observed under exact support law, is a first-class empirical branch and must be retained. It is not missingness. It may continue recursively if separately governed continuation predicates pass. No motive such as confusion, distrust, opacity or depoliticization may be inferred from the token itself.

### Missing / unavailable / unresolved / noncomparable

These states must be retained as exact typed nonpositive results/receipts. They do not authorize a positive branch or a deeper recursion. The controller may resume only in a future run after the missing data/authority/comparability condition is lawfully repaired and a new exact result/replay identity is produced.

### Maximum depth

`MAX_DEPTH` is a binding stop-reason category; its numeric value is not universal law. At the governed maximum, retain the current branch and mark it terminal. The AS3 benchmark depth of 18 is a benchmark-specific frozen parameter, not a universal fracture-map depth.

### No meaningful split / no gain

The originary vocabulary binds `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT` as a stop concept. The current evidence does not supply one universal metric/cutoff for “meaningful.” Once a separately governed criterion declares no meaningful split/gain, retain the current branch and stop recursion. No child branch is invented merely to maintain depth.

### Unstable branch

`UNSTABLE_BRANCH` is a binding stop concept. The branch/distribution remains retained with uncertainty/stability qualification. Deeper recursive splitting is blocked unless a future versioned law explicitly authorizes an exploratory continuation class. D03 recovers no universal instability classifier.

### No remaining eligible predictors / questions

Exhaustion of the exact governed continuation frontier is terminal and does not erase the branch. D03 defines the semantics of this stop, not the upstream enumeration law. Predictor eligibility belongs to the D02/predictor-selection authority surface; branch co-signature question enumeration belongs to D04. When either exact frontier is empty for the requested continuation operation, retain the branch and record the corresponding exhaustion stop.

### Pure / near-pure

Historical implementation material attests pure/near-pure as a stop trace, but the primary originary stop vocabulary recovered by L04 is `LOW_N`, `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT`, `MAX_DEPTH`, `UNSTABLE_BRANCH`. D03 therefore does not freeze `PURE/NEAR_PURE` as a new independent universal controller state. HQ may either govern it as a separate classifier or treat it as one governed reason for `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT`.

## 5. Binding stop vocabulary versus policy parameters

### Binding semantic stop concepts recovered

- `LOW_N / INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT`;
- `NO_MEANINGFUL_SPLIT / NO_GAIN`;
- `MAX_DEPTH`;
- `UNSTABLE_BRANCH`;
- `NO_REMAINING_ELIGIBLE_PREDICTORS`;
- `NO_REMAINING_ELIGIBLE_QUESTIONS`;
- typed nonpositive terminal causes: `VALID_EMPTY`, `MISSING/UNAVAILABLE`, `UNRESOLVED/MISSING_AUTHORITY`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, `ZERO_SUPPORT` where applicable.

### Not universally frozen by this court

- `n < 50` exploratory;
- `n < 25` no split;
- option count `< 10` no channel;
- a universal minimum weighted support;
- a universal stable-minority percentage;
- a universal multimodality/polarization cutoff;
- a universal gain/effect-size cutoff;
- a universal instability cutoff;
- a universal maximum depth;
- a universal minimum number of remaining predictors/questions.

The formative `50/25/10` values are illustrative and explicitly owner-tunable. They are not Rawlsing-wide law.

## 6. Current PRE553 support/measure relation

Current I03 already preserves exact quantities needed by any future controller rule:

- `raw_denominator_support`;
- `raw_numerator_support` / resolved target support;
- complete target-cell raw counts;
- governed weighted denominator and weighted cell support when authorized;
- exact risk-set membership;
- exact measure-basis identity;
- uncertainty method or typed uncertainty state;
- source/universe and replay identity.

I03 distinguishes `SUPPORTED`, `OBSERVED_ZERO`, `ZERO_SUPPORT`, `ZERO_OVERLAP`, `LOW_SUPPORT`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNAVAILABLE`, `UNCERTAINTY_UNQUANTIFIED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `VALID_EMPTY`, and `INTEGRITY_FAILURE`.

I05 separately distinguishes supported, valid-empty, insufficient-support, noncomparable, coverage-limited, authority-pruned and unevaluated discovery states.

The adopted I05 production support rule uses `minimum_raw_denominator = 0` specifically so I05 adds **no additional count-based adequacy floor** beyond producer states. It cannot be repurposed as a fracture-map recurse-all policy.

## 7. Required controller contract shape

A future implementation court can remain minimal if it treats continuation as a versioned decision object over exact producer refs. D03 does not authorize implementation, but the semantic contract must at least bind:

1. exact branch/result ref;
2. exact producer empirical state(s);
3. exact support/measure/uncertainty refs and quantities used;
4. exact recursion depth and parent ancestry;
5. exact remaining predictor/question frontier identity for the operation;
6. exact continuation-rule authority/version/hash;
7. disposition: continue-eligible, report-only stop, terminal stop, or typed-nonpositive stop;
8. exact stop reason(s);
9. no mutation/deletion of the retained producer result;
10. deterministic replay of the disposition from the same exact inputs/authority.
