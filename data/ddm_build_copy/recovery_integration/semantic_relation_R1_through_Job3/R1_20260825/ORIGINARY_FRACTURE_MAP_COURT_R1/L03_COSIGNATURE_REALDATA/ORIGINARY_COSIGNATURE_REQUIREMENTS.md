# ORIGINARY CO-SIGNATURE REQUIREMENTS — L03 REAL-DATA COURT R1

Status: `RECOVERED_AND_REALDATA_TESTED`

## 1. Recovered conceptual partition

The originary Rawlsing records distinguish a **population field** from an actor interior. They require position-conditioned distributions, recursive response branches, response-response co-signatures/attitude clouds inside those branches, and same-respondent joint morphology. The accepted PRE-V3 functional constitution independently preserves the same burden: exact respondent identity, reconstructible lawful joints, supported minority configurations, higher-order structure, and recursive branch-conditioned cotravel.

The following terms are therefore not synonyms.

| Term | Exact court meaning | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| `DEMOGRAPHIC_PREDICTOR` | A source-backed covariate/conditioner such as age, gender, education, geography or religiosity that may help characterize how branch prevalence or response morphology varies. | An attitude node, belief, personality trait, actor essence, or branch membership by itself. |
| `BRANCH_MEMBERSHIP` | Membership in a cohort generated by an exact response predicate. In this court: Lebanon and `Q873_1 == 1`, meaning the respondent selected **socioeconomic class** among the multi-select reasons people face discrimination. | “Socioeconomic class” as an objective class position; “SES only”; or an actor ontology. |
| `RESPONSE_NODE` | One substantive survey response variable with its exact source-native question/value semantics and typed special states. | A demographic predictor silently promoted into the attitude graph. |
| `SAME_RESPONDENT_COTRAVEL` | The observed co-occurrence of two or more response-node values on the **same respondent IDs**, inside an exact branch/risk set. | Correlation between aggregate marginals, semantic similarity between questions, or a causal edge. |
| `JOINT_PATTERN` | One exact pair/k-way response tuple or the empirical frequency tensor over such tuples for an exact branch and joint denominator. | A pairwise score detached from the underlying tuple counts. |
| `CO_SIGNATURE` | A branch-conditioned empirical morphology: exact response-node marginals plus same-respondent pair/k-way joint patterns, their denominators, weighting basis where used, and replay/provenance. | A personality profile, ideology label, scalar “type,” or causal model. |
| `ATTITUDE_CLOUD` | A broader population-level empirical field composed of co-signatures, minority modes, contradictions, uncertainty and branch structure. | An individual psyche or a claim that every component is semantically or causally related. |
| `HIGHER_ORDER_CONFIGURATION` | A k-way same-respondent joint structure (`k > 2`) carrying information not fixed by lower-order/pairwise summaries. | Merely “three variables were measured”; the information burden is the exact joint morphology. |

## 2. Originary functional burden

The recovered requirement is not merely “keep pairwise correlations.”

A lawful successor must preserve the ability to ask:

1. Which exact respondents belong to a discovered response branch?
2. Within that branch, how do other substantive answers distribute?
3. Which answers occur together on the same respondents?
4. Does that cotravel differ from the parent universe or complementary branch?
5. Do demographic or structural conditioners change branch prevalence or morphology without becoming response nodes themselves?
6. Are there supported minority or k-way configurations that disappear if the empirical object is reduced to marginals or pairwise summaries?
7. Can every answer be replayed to exact source/universe, branch predicate, respondent risk set, raw response semantics, denominator, weight basis and analysis rule?

This is why the originary itinerary treats co-travel as information residing in the **joint empirical configuration and its population conditioning**, while refusing to let a semantic relation system manufacture empirical cotravel that the respondents did not supply.

## 3. Exact branch used by this court

Source universe:

`Arab Barometer Wave VIII (2023–2024) / Lebanon / COUNTRY == 10`

Branch predicate:

`Q873_1 == 1`

Questionnaire semantics:

`Q873`: “In your opinion, do people in [your country] face discrimination because of…”; option 1 is `Socioeconomic class`; the item is **SELECT ALL THAT APPLY**.

Therefore this court's branch means:

> respondents in the Lebanon Wave VIII universe who selected socioeconomic class as one perceived basis of discrimination.

It does **not** mean “low SES respondents,” “working class respondents,” or “respondents whose only discrimination frame is socioeconomic class.”

Exact membership is replayed from respondent IDs rather than reconstructed from demographics.

## 4. What real data proves

The branch is not merely a different marginal on the anchoring discrimination item. Across a bounded substantive set, it carries different response distributions in governance, religion/state, democracy, and gender-role questions. More importantly, same-respondent relations also change with branch conditioning.

One clear pair example is:

`Q606_4 × Q867B`

- `Q606_4`: religious practice is a private matter and should be separated from socio-economic life.
- `Q867B`: children's lives expected to be better/same/worse than the respondent's life.

Raw complete-case Cramér's V:

- SES-discrimination branch: `0.1768354317`, `n=1208`
- Lebanon overall: `0.1166404038`, `n=2181`
- complement: `0.0543498407`, `n=973`

The metric is descriptive. The court does not infer that branch membership causes either response or their association. The information-level holding is narrower and stronger: **the pair relation is not invariant to the branch condition, so a whole-universe pair summary is not a substitute for branch-conditioned same-respondent evidence.**

## 5. Pairwise-only information loss is proved on real data

Primary higher-order case:

`Q201A_3 × Q606_2 × Q606_8`

- trust in the elected council/parliament;
- whether the country is better off if religious people hold public positions;
- whether religious leaders are as likely to be corrupt as non-religious leaders.

For each scope, the court fit the maximum-entropy/no-three-way-interaction table that reproduces **all three observed pairwise margins** by iterative proportional fitting. The pairwise margins are matched to numerical error below `5e-13`.

Within the SES-discrimination branch (`n=1265`):

- observed-vs-pairwise-model joint total-variation distance: `0.0839505583`
- likelihood-ratio `G² = 87.32678819`, `df=27`
- diagnostic `p = 2.7984e-08`
- largest cell residual at raw tuple `(4,4,1)`: observed `204`, pairwise-model expected `224.1389789`.

Within the complementary branch (`n=1004`):

- joint TV: `0.0440333882`
- `G² = 29.90147538`, `df=27`
- diagnostic `p = 0.3185989`.

The p-values are reported diagnostics, **not** a newly authorized threshold. The decisive information claim does not require adopting a significance rule: a table that has the exact pairwise margins can still differ materially from the observed three-way table. Hence pairwise margins/scores are not information-equivalent to the exact branch-conditioned joint.

Corroborating three-way cases are retained in `PAIRWISE_VS_BRANCH_CONDITIONED_MATRIX.csv`.

## 7. Minimum information-equivalent object

For semantic parity, Rawlsing needs either:

1. exact same-respondent incidence from which arbitrary authorized branch-conditioned joints can be regenerated; **or**
2. another representation proved information-equivalent for that authorized workload.

A retained list of pairwise association scores is insufficient because it loses:

- exact respondent membership;
- exact branch membership;
- joint denominators;
- raw tuple counts;
- k-way/minority morphology;
- the ability to condition or recurse again under a new lawful predicate;
- replay from a derived relation back to exact source responses.

A co-signature object may be cached or materialized, but its minimum semantic grain is fixed by the replay burden, not by a preferred physical architecture.

## 8. Negative ceiling

This court does not authorize:

- causal interpretation of any cross-question association;
- actor belief, identity, personality or ideology assignment;
- treating `Q873_1` branch membership as objective socioeconomic class;
- treating demographic predictors as attitude nodes;
- assuming every statistically visible association is substantively meaningful;
- a universal significance threshold;
- architecture A/B/C ranking;
- production mutation.
