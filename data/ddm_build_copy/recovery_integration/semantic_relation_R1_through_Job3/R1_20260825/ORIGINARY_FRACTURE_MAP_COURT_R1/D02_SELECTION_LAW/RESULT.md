Existing accepted/originary authority is sufficient to define **what the fracture-map controller must discover and preserve**, and it is sufficient to define the four semantic branch roles once a modal configuration and predictor condition have been lawfully chosen. It is **not sufficient to choose several of the semantic winners needed by an autonomous controller**.

The missing surface is therefore narrower than “build a discovery algorithm” and more important than “pick a convenient statistic.” HQ must govern the promotion boundary between:

`EXACT EMPIRICAL FACT ENUMERATION`

## A1 — Overall response morphology

### Holding

`PARTIAL_LAW`

Binding law already requires the controller to preserve the full governed response distribution/configuration, alternatives, multi-label pattern morphology, and the distinction between concentrated, split/multimodal, stable-minority, fragmented, gradient, polarization, and residual/category-failure surfaces where lawfully measurable.

The exact enumeration of governed response cells/patterns, their weighted and unweighted support, and exact ties is **not itself a semantic selection decision**. Those are empirical facts.

However, accepted authority does not completely specify:

1. which governed measure is authoritative for choosing the operational “modal” configuration when weighted and unweighted orderings differ;
2. how exact ties are represented for controller branching;
3. how near-ties become a `split` or `multimodal` state rather than a unique dominant configuration;
4. how `stable minority`, `fragmented/multimodal`, `polarization`, or `smooth gradient` are classified from exact cells.

Therefore:

- exact distribution enumeration: `NOT_A_SEMANTIC_SELECTION_DECISION`;
- unique argmax after a governing measure has already been lawfully selected: deterministic fact reduction, not permission to choose the measure;
- governing modal measure / tie policy / multimodal classifier: `NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED`;
- numeric cutoffs inside any adopted classifier: `OWNER_TUNABLE_PARAMETER_REQUIRED` unless separately fixed by authority.

Historical V547 shape thresholds are `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY`.

## A2 — Governed positional predictor universe

### Holding

`PARTIAL_LAW`

Originary/accepted law strongly constrains semantic eligibility:

- candidates must be governed demographic / structural / positional coordinates;
- predictors are association/determinicity surfaces, not causal essences, attitudes, personalities, or actor beliefs;
- source/wave/universe/routing/measure authority must survive;
- missing, inapplicable, not-asked, and unresolved coordinates are not interchangeable with predictor absence;
- exact support and comparability gates remain binding.

Current PRE553 machinery can represent governed coordinates and exact candidate queries when supplied. Historical V547 used a fixed positional vocabulary, but that fixed field list is not recovered as universal semantic law.

HQ still needs a controller-level candidate-universe construction law specifying which authorized coordinate families are eligible at a node, which are excluded, how routing/availability/noncomparability remove candidates, and whether special coordinates such as country may be used as selectable predictors versus retained only as scope/baseline controls.

Classification: `PARTIAL_LAW` for semantic eligibility; `NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED` for autonomous candidate-universe construction.

## A3 — Primary predictor scoring / ranking / selection

### Holding

`NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED`

Originary law establishes the objective: identify the positional coordinate that best separates or predicts the response configuration, including conformity/exit structure. It does **not** recover a binding scoring functional or comparison rule.

Historical V547/V548 used mutual information / determinicity-style scores and selected a strongest predictor. This is `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY`.

No accepted authority recovered here determines:

- MI versus NMI versus entropy reduction versus another governed criterion;
- weighted versus unweighted scoring;
- effect-size versus predictive-separation priority;
- support / uncertainty / stability adjustment;
- required improvement over null/baseline;
- tie-break law;
- whether a pair interaction may outrank every unary predictor;
- how missingness/comparability penalties participate in ranking rather than gating.

These are semantic promotion decisions because they determine which empirical regularity becomes the branch-generating predictor.

## A4 — Predictor-value / condition selection

### Holding

`NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED`

Once a predictor is selected, the controller still must choose the exact predictor condition that defines `predictor present` versus `predictor absent` for the four-way branch ontology.

Historical V548 selected one predictor value using a concrete ordering centered on modal-response conformity/share, lift, and support after a minimum-support gate. This is `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY`.

Accepted authority does not establish that ordering as law and does not fully govern:

- single value versus grouped values / ranges / partitions;
- tie handling;
- continuous/ordinal coordinate partitioning;
- special/missing value exclusion;
- multi-label response-pattern-conditioned value selection;
- stability/uncertainty requirements for the selected condition;
- whether more than one predictor condition may seed coequal branches.

The branch condition must be exact and replayable once selected; **which condition is selected requires new HQ law**.

## A5 — Four branch roles

### Holding

`EXACT_LAW_ALREADY_EXISTS`

The semantic ontology is recovered and accepted:

1. predictor-present + modal/conforming response -> conformer;
2. predictor-present + nonmodal response -> predictor-violating deviant / exit;
3. predictor-absent + modal response -> converger / alternative route to the same response;
4. predictor-absent + nonmodal response -> alternative/minority branch.

Important noncollapse:

`PREDICTOR_ABSENT != PREDICTOR_VALUE_MISSING`.

“Absent” means the lawfully selected predictor condition is not instantiated in an otherwise eligible respondent. Missing/not-asked/inapplicable/unresolved coordinates remain typed negative states and cannot be silently routed into the absent branch.

Given a lawful modal configuration, predictor, predictor condition, risk set, response semantics, and source authority, branch membership is exact Boolean predicate composition. That membership computation is `NOT_A_SEMANTIC_SELECTION_DECISION`.

What remains unresolved is **which supported branches the controller prioritizes or continues**, addressed below.

## A7 — Stable-minority / split / residual / category-failure classification

### Holding

`PARTIAL_LAW`

Several distinctions are already binding:

- minority-supported configurations must not disappear under majority summaries;
- residual/none/category-failure routes must remain discoverable;
- source-semantic `NONE/OTHER/DK/REFUSED/MISSING/NOT_APPLICABLE` states must remain distinct;
- an exact source-coded `NONE_OF_THESE` response is an empirical fact, not an inferred motive;
- underpowered/noncomparable/unresolved states must fail closed rather than receive substantive labels.

Thus direct response-state preservation and exact residual predicate construction are `EXACT_LAW_ALREADY_EXISTS` / `NOT_A_SEMANTIC_SELECTION_DECISION` once the governing predicate is supplied.

What is not recovered as binding law is the classifier mapping exact distributions into higher-order shape labels such as:

- stable minority;
- split/multimodal;
- fragmented/multimodal;
- polarization;
- smooth gradient;
- category-collapse.

Historical V547 `flat/main_effect/interaction/mixed/sparse` thresholds do not answer this and are `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY`.

HQ must adopt the classification logic: `NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED`. Numeric thresholds within that logic are `OWNER_TUNABLE_PARAMETER_REQUIRED` unless HQ deliberately fixes them.

## A8 — Pair interactions / higher-order predictor combinations

### Holding

`PARTIAL_LAW`

The evidence establishes three different propositions that must not be collapsed:

1. historical V547 autonomously enumerated a finite unordered pair frontier from a fixed predictor vocabulary;
2. current I05 can exactly evaluate governed pair or higher-order predicates/combinations when they are supplied inside the authorized frontier;
3. neither fact supplies a complete autonomous semantic law for **which interactions the fracture-map controller may generate, retain, or promote**.

Exact evaluation of a supplied authorized interaction is `NOT_A_SEMANTIC_SELECTION_DECISION`.

The general possibility of governed pair/higher-order candidate evaluation is already present, but autonomous generation/selection needs HQ law for:

- maximum interaction order;
- admissible coordinate combinations;
- heredity/hierarchy constraints, if any;
- support/overlap/comparability prerequisites;
- candidate-space bounds;
- pair-versus-unary promotion rule;
- interaction stability requirement;
- tie/multiplicity policy;
- whether interaction discovery changes future frontier reachability.

Historical exhaustive pair search in V547 is `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY`. Autonomous interaction generation/selection remains `NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED` within an otherwise `PARTIAL_LAW` capability surface.

## A9 — Branch continuation / retention law

Although not a separate numbered worker adjudication, it is inseparable from branch selection.

### Holding

`NEW_HQ_LAW_REQUIRED`

Authority35 requires recursion from **any discovered supported branch**, not just a predeclared deviation lane. G05 confirms current I05 does not choose which discovered child to pursue. Historical V548's fixed recursion geometry, primarily continuing the majority-position-deviation branch, is `HISTORICAL_HEURISTIC_ONLY` and is narrower than the originary constitution.

HQ must distinguish:

- retain branch as learned-world structure;
- expand branch recursively;
- stop expansion with exact stop reason;
- prune branch by authority;
- mark underpowered/noncomparable/unresolved.

A branch may be semantically important enough to preserve while lacking authority/support to expand. `RETENTION != EXPANSION`.

## Minimum HQ law surface

D02 finds **six irreducible promotion-law gaps**:

1. **Modal/morphology decision law** — governing measure, tie/multimodal policy, and higher-order shape classifier.
2. **Predictor candidate-universe law** — node-local governed positional candidate construction and exclusions.
3. **Predictor selection law** — scoring/comparison/ties/uncertainty/stability and unary-versus-interaction promotion.
4. **Predictor-condition law** — exact value/partition/condition selection for branch construction.
5. **Recursive branch promotion law** — secondary predictor selection plus retain/expand/stop semantics for every supported branch.
6. **Interaction-generation law** — admissible pair/higher-order frontier and bounded promotion rules.

Exact numeric parameters under any adopted laws may be owner-tunable. D02 does not propose values.

## What does NOT require new semantic selection law

The following are already exact facts or deterministic constructions once their governing inputs are authorized:

- enumerate response cells/patterns;
- preserve weighted/unweighted support and missingness;
- preserve source-semantic negative states;
- evaluate a supplied authorized predictor/predicate/query;
- evaluate a supplied authorized pair/higher-order interaction;
- construct four branch memberships from an already selected modal configuration and predictor condition;
- preserve exact respondent/risk-set/authority/replay lineage;
- transport an already discovered authorized child into N+1 under the accepted recursive handoff primitive.

The missing law is the **promotion edge**, not the arithmetic.
