# Minority / Divergence Preservation Law

Status: `L05_BINDING_COURT_FINDING__HQ_ADOPTION_REQUIRED`

## 1. The modal branch may not consume the population

The originary fracture-map grammar treats alternatives as structure, not leftovers.

A governed product must distinguish at least:

- predictor + modal conformer;
- predictor + nonmodal / predictor-violating deviant;
- predictor-absent + modal converger — a different route to the same response;
- predictor-absent + nonmodal alternative/minority;
- recursively discovered child fractures inside every supported branch;
- residual/none/category-failure branches.

No “majority first” optimization may erase a supported branch from future discovery or replay.

## 2. Preservation tuple

Every minority, alternative, divergent, or residual branch must retain, where applicable:

`branch_id`
`parent_path`
`source/wave/universe`
`risk_set/routing`
`response_semantics`
`conditioner/splitter definition`
`member/respondent identity or exact replay determinant`
`unweighted_n`
`weighted_support + measure authority`
`denominator`
`missingness`
`uncertainty/confidence`
`typed state`
`stop reason`
`co-signature/joint morphology refs`
`replay/authority refs`

A downstream stage may reference this tuple; it may not silently replace it with a global mode.

## 3. Category failure / none

`NONE_OF_THESE`, residual, refusal, or category-collapse states are not junk by default.

They may establish only what the governed response semantics and branch statistics establish. They do not by themselves prove alienation, depoliticization, distrust, opacity, false consciousness, or any other substantive structural/psychological interpretation.

Those are separately governed transformations.

## 4. Contradiction and multimodality

Contradictory or multimodal configurations are first-class outcomes.

Do not:

- average opposed modes into a fictitious center;
- keep only the modal cluster;
- erase a stable minority because it is small relative to the whole population;
- convert disagreement among source/stratal fields into a single posterior without composition authority.

Where multiple components or fields remain authoritative, preserve the vector/set and its uncertainty.

## 5. Minority resonance and divergence

A future actor-safe comparator may, under separate authority, establish that an actor's objective conditioning or realized configuration is:

- aligned with a modal field;
- divergent from it;
- closer to a supported minority mode;
- resonant with one stratal/source field while opposed to another.

That is an **alignment relation**, not causal authorship.

`MINORITY_RESONANCE != MINORITY_ESSENCE`
`DIVERGENCE != DEVIANCE_OF_PERSONALITY`
`FIELD_ALIGNMENT != CAUSAL_ORIGIN`.

## 6. Multiple strata / fields

A generated person may be partially situated in multiple strata and empirical scopes at once. Therefore downstream preservation must permit:

- simultaneous local/national/regional/global fields;
- simultaneous family/class/sect/party/state/etc. structural pressures;
- reinforcement, opposition, or weak relevance;
- source disagreement;
- temporal change.

No one demographic coordinate or “dominant” stratum may collapse the person into one class of being.

Cross-source pooling and source-to-stratum composition remain separate authority questions.

## 7. V3 boundary

Minority and divergence structure may constrain the learned-world possibility terrain. V3 still owns the actor's actual history and may realize a modal, minority, novel, contradictory, or transformed path subject to its own lawful metabolism/history.

The preservation purpose is precisely to make that divergence possible without either stereotyping or arbitrary generation.
