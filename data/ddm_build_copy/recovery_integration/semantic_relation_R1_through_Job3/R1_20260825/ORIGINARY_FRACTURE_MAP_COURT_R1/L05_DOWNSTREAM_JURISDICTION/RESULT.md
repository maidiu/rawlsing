## 2. Dispositive ruling

The originary Recursive Multi-Label Positional Response / fracture-map grammar is best understood as a **population- and branch-grain empirical geometry**.

A governed branch product may assert, at its exact source/wave/universe/risk-set/measure grain:

- branch membership and parent/child path;
- response distributions and multi-label/pattern morphology;
- primary and within-branch splitter/predictor association;
- modal conformity, predictor-violating deviation, alternative routes, supported minority branches, and residual/category-failure branches;
- support, missingness, weights, uncertainty/confidence, and exact stop state;
- branch shape such as polarization, stable minority, fragmentation/multimodality, or category collapse when those descriptors are operationally governed;
- same-respondent co-signature/cotravel among other responses within that exact branch.

It does **not** thereby assert:

- that any generated actor belongs to the branch;
- that a demographic coordinate is a structural stratum or causal essence;
- that a branch statistic is an individual belief, personality trait, psyche state, memory, knowledge, biography, or voice;
- that an empirical predictor caused the response;
- that a branch implies a named structural channel such as opacity, distrust, faith, dignity, resentment, or class consciousness;
- that an aggregate/modal/minority pattern is the actor's realized state.

The originary record itself contains the firewall: its branch relation modifiers are “field priors, not individual beliefs,” it prohibits persona assignment and runtime mutation, and it treats branch discovery as work that must occur *before* persona political consciousness is assigned.

## 4. Relation-channel inference — exact classification

Historical `branch_relation_channel_inference` / “each branch produces relation modifiers” language contains two different things and must be split.

### 4.1 Empirically lawful content

The branch can report exact measured facts such as:

- a response category becoming more or less prevalent in a conditioned branch;
- a predictor becoming stronger/weaker inside a branch;
- a supported exit from the modal response;
- a residual `none-of-these` branch;
- a same-respondent response configuration disproportionately co-traveling in that branch;
- support/uncertainty sufficient or insufficient for continued recursion.

Those are empirical outputs.

- `sect_ethno_class.object_visibility +`;

- `possible depoliticized opacity +`;

are **not made true merely by the branch statistics**. They are candidate semantic/structural interpretations. They become authoritative only if an independently governed mapping defines the source semantics, evidence threshold, allowed target structural object, epistemic ceiling, and replay identity, followed where required by structural-admission authority.

Therefore:

`RELATION_CHANNEL_INFERENCE = PROVENANCE-BEARING HEURISTIC/TRANSFORMATION CANDIDATE`

This preserves the useful originary insight without laundering speculative interpretation into empirical truth.

## 5. Minority, divergence, contradiction, alternative routes, and category failure

The fracture-map grammar was specifically designed not to let the modal branch eat the population.

A lawful downstream representation must preserve:

1. predictor+modal conformers;
2. predictor+nonmodal/deviant exits;
3. predictor-absent modal convergence — an alternative route to the same answer;
4. predictor-absent nonmodal/minority routes;
5. recursively discovered secondary fractures inside every supported branch;
6. same-respondent cotravel inside minority and residual branches;
7. `none`/category-failure/refusal as typed empirical structure when supported;
8. contradiction and multimodality without averaging them into a fake coherent center;
9. exact support/denominator/weight/uncertainty and stop state.

A minority mode can later be a **possible empirical field against which an actor-safe comparator might register alignment, divergence, or minority resonance**. That does not prove the actor's causal origin, identity, consciousness, or structural essence.
