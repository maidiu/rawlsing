# Empirical → Relation / Structural / Possibility Transformation Law

Status: `L05_BINDING_COURT_FINDING__HQ_ADOPTION_REQUIRED`

## 1. Core noncollapse

The empirical fracture map is a learned **population/branch object**. Downstream semantic and structural objects are transformations with narrower, explicitly authorized claims.

`EVIDENCE -> EMPIRICAL PRODUCT -> SEMANTIC TRANSFORMATION -> STRUCTURAL ADMISSION -> STRUCTURAL PRIOR -> POSSIBILITY BINDING -> V3 METABOLISM`

is a sequence of jurisdictions, not a sequence of automatic implications.

## 2. Levels

### E0 — Governed respondent evidence

Owns source/wave/universe/respondent/question/response/routing/risk-set/measure identity, raw/governed response semantics, missingness, and replay.

It does not own population generalizations until a governed analysis is executed.

### E1 — Fracture-map empirical product

May own:

- exact conditioned branch/cohort membership;
- response distribution and multi-label/pattern morphology;
- splitter/predictor association;
- conforming/deviant/alternative/residual branch topology;
- support, uncertainty, stop state;
- measurable branch-shape descriptors.

It may not own structural-stratum meaning, actor membership, or causal explanation.

### E2 — Branch-conditioned co-signature / joint morphology

May own exact same-respondent cotravel/joint configuration at the conditioned branch grain, including minority and contradictory configurations.

It does not establish source-semantic relation type, structural pressure, or causal origin.

### T1 — Relation-modifier transformation candidate

A T1 object may be generated only as a **candidate** unless separate authority positively maps an E1/E2 pattern into a defined semantic modifier.

Minimum required binding for positive authority:

1. exact E1/E2 input refs;
2. source/question response semantics;
3. transformation rule identity/version;
4. admissible evidence/support/uncertainty envelope;
5. output semantic family and epistemic ceiling;
6. negative/blocked states;
7. deterministic replay identity.

Without these, labels such as `opacity`, `distrust`, `class legibility`, `object visibility`, `faith`, `resentment`, or `dignity` remain heuristic proposals.

### A1 — Structural empirical admission

A semantic/empirical product becomes structural evidence only through explicit structural-admission authority.

`EMPIRICAL_ASSOCIATION != STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION`.

Admission may preserve exact upstream refs while adding only the new structural meaning it owns. It may not re-own or rewrite the empirical evidence.

### S1 — Actor-independent pressure backdrop / structural prior

May represent governed historical pressure, affordance, constraint, contradiction, or interaction at population/structural grain.

It is subsurface and actor-independent. It may not be interpreted as a person's conscious burden, personality, worldview, or belief.

### P1 — Positioned-subject empirical possibility geometry

The originary machine requires a field-like capability that can represent plausible joint configurations for empirically comparable positions, including common and minority modes.

L05 **does not invent the positive binding model**.

Before P1 may be produced for a generated actor, separate authority must govern at least:

- eligible objective actor facts;
- comparator projection;
- empirical-coordinate comparability;
- source/universe composition;
- effective-time alignment;
- uncertainty and negative states;
- field release/replay identity.

P1 remains an upstream probability/possibility field, not actor state.

### V3 — Developmental metabolism and realization

V3 alone owns actor-indexed traversal through opportunities, encounters, exposures, relationships, actions, learning, memory, biography, genealogy, path dependence, and realized attractor history.

A P1 or S1 prior can affect V3 only with explicit read/metabolism authority. No field mass, modal cell, minority cell, structural channel, or pressure score may directly mint actor belief/history/memory/relationship/persona.

## 3. Relation-channel inference rule

Historical “branch relation-channel inference” must be parsed into two layers:

**Empirical statement:**  
“Within branch B, response R becomes more prevalent; splitter X strengthens; configuration C co-travels; residual N is supported.”

**Semantic/structural proposal:**  
“This makes class less legible,” “sect becomes the active structural channel,” “opacity increases,” “survey distrust is present.”

Only the first is entailed by E1/E2. The second requires T1/A1 authority.

A transformation rule may ultimately authorize some second-layer claims, but its validity comes from the governing mapping and evidence ceiling—not from the mere fact that historical code emitted the label.

## 4. Provenance and ownership

Every downstream product must carry direct refs to the exact producer objects it consumes.

- T1 does not replace E1/E2.
- A1 does not replace T1 or empirical evidence.
- S1 does not replace admission evidence.
- P1 does not replace source-indexed empirical geometry.
- V3 does not rewrite any prior into a retrospective “cause” merely because it influenced a transition.

This is consistent with later direct-producer DAG law while remaining architecture-neutral.

## 5. Typed failure

At any gate, insufficient or absent authority yields a typed blocked/unknown state. It must not be converted into:

- a zero effect;
- a negative substantive claim;
- a nearest stereotype;
- a fallback demographic essence;
- a fabricated structural label;
- a direct actor assignment.
