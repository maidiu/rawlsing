## Position / analytic role authority

`OBL005 — POSITION_ROLE_AUTHORITY`

A source variable does not become POSITION, RESOURCE, CONDITIONING, SUPPLEMENTARY, EXCLUDED or another sociological role by being present in a model. Use a finite competent role court for the exact claim surface. No global runtime role inference is authorized by FIELD FORGE R2.

## External political-group identity

`OBL006 — external political-group crosswalk authority`

Named groups, source categories, survey respondent identities and external institutional/political actors cannot be equated by label similarity, country, ideological resemblance, geometry or model proximity. Specific hegemony/power/elite-alignment claims invoking external groups remain blocked until exact crosswalk authority exists.

## Weight / estimand / target-population authority

Where a FIELD FORGE relation targets more than the exact realized sample, the claim must bind target population/estimand, exact weight identity, design-purpose/provenance and admissibility for the declared transform. Block size, number of measurements or analytic convenience cannot supply weight.

## Continuous / ordinal transformation authority

Source-native order or cardinal meaning must be preserved exactly where present. Any binning, scaling, normalization, distance construction, rank transform or other representation that changes state semantics requires its own transformation/claim authority. No universal binning or scale-normalization law is minted here.

## Cross-context correspondence / transport authority

Transport requires an exact typed map and preservation claim. A map is not authorized by matching labels or visual similarity. No lawful map => `NO_TRANSPORT` / `NONCOMPARABLE` at that grain.
