# FIELD FORGE R2 — LEARNED STRUCTURAL-WORLD ARCHITECTURE R1

Status: `EXPLORATORY_TERMINAL_SYNTHESIS__AWAITING_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT`

## 1. Architectural location

FIELD FORGE occupies the actor-independent structural-observatory seam between already-governed empirical producers and later structural learned-world semantic owners.

It is not a replacement owner for source semantics, respondent identity, routing/exposure, weights/estimands, question identity, or source-native facts.

It is not V3.

Its architectural question is:

> What is the weakest governed representation of social relation, configuration, conditional dependence, alternative structure and structural possibility that preserves everything later actor-independent structural consumers lawfully need, while refusing to manufacture ontology from measurement convenience?

## 2. Producer-preserving rule

Every FIELD FORGE semantic relation must point to producer-owned determinants rather than laundering them into a new self-sufficient world table.

`PRODUCER_OWNED_FACT != FIELD_FORGE_RELATION`

`DIRECT_REFERENCE != SEMANTIC_REOWNERSHIP`

`REBUILDABLE != SEMANTICALLY_DISPOSABLE`

A FIELD FORGE relation may be materialized, cached or indexed, but its authority is defined by its exact producer identities, relation commitment, scope, permissions and replay law—not by its storage location.

## 3. Minimum universal layers

### L0 — governed upstream fact availability

FIELD FORGE consumes exact governed empirical identities and facts at their own producer grains.

Required capabilities are claim-dependent but may include:

- source/wave/context identity;
- question/measurement identity;
- source-native state identity;
- respondent identity and exact same-unit incidence;
- context capability, eligibility, exposure and response states;
- risk-set/universe identity;
- exact weight/estimand authority where invoked;
- role/PRA, TTAR or other frozen semantic authorities where invoked.

L0 is not a FIELD FORGE-owned universal table.

### L1 — exact relation commitment

When a FIELD FORGE claim says that two or more objects stand in a relation, that relation receives explicit typed semantic identity.

Examples include, where lawfully constituted:

- exact same-unit conjunction;
- exact conditional/support relation;
- within-state comparison;
- typed directional redistribution;
- exact alternative/branch membership;
- authorized cross-context correspondence;
- exact order/cardinality/topology relation when constitutive.

Atomic co-presence alone does not mint the relation.

`SAME_ATOMIC_INFORMATION != SAME_RELATION_COMMITMENT`.

### L2 — query and discovery operators

A relation need not always be persisted as a standalone payload object if its exact semantics can be lawfully reconstructed through a frozen query/discovery operator over authoritative producer facts.

Query/discovery identity must state:

- operands;
- empirical unit;
- scope/risk set;
- relation semantics;
- support/negative-state law;
- authority dependencies;
- deterministic replay recipe;
- quantifier ceiling.

Ephemerality does not erase the semantic relation asserted by the query.

### L3 — optional lossless reusable surfaces

Persistent carriers, exact configuration indexes, respondent-membership indexes, materialized conditionals, sufficient-statistic dictionaries and graph/index forms may be retained when they improve:

- replay economics;
- repeated discovery;
- query latency;
- finite candidate enumeration;
- auditability;
- downstream interchange.

They do not gain extra semantic truth solely through persistence or information breadth.

A stronger reusable surface becomes semantically required only when a named downstream starvation witness survives weaker-object ablation.

### L4 — claim-conditional relation geometry

Order, metric, neighborhood and topology are separate relation commitments.

FIELD FORGE does not have one default `GEOMETRY` primitive.

- nominal identity remains nominal when no lawful order exists;
- ordinal order is preserved when source/authority warrants order;
- cardinal distance requires separately warranted quantity/metric semantics;
- disconnected/local topology is allowed;
- coordinates/embeddings are normally lossy derived interfaces, not ontology.

A coordinate representation can never retroactively authorize the relation it visualizes.

### L5 — structural-interpretive admission

Neutral empirical precursors may support later stronger structural interpretation, but the stronger noun requires separate authority.

Examples:

- conditional redistribution/dependence -> possible pressure-footprint interpretation -> causal pressure pathway only with stronger design;
- relational arena candidate + differential resource valuation + conversion/autonomy evidence -> possible field/capital admission;
- prevalence/reach/counterpattern profile + independently grounded power alignment -> possible hegemony interpretation;
- independent position and disposition relations + hostile artifact controls -> possible homology admission;
- local invariant + authorized correspondence -> recurrence/transport claim.

No such arrow is automatic.

## 4. Discovery law

FIELD FORGE must support outcome-blind discovery where downstream functionality requires it, without requiring a motif ontology.

The weakest demonstrated bounded pattern is:

`LOSSLESS SAME-UNIT FACT AVAILABILITY`
→ `OUTCOME-BLIND EXACT ENUMERATION / PROFILE DICTIONARY`
→ `FROZEN CANDIDATE PREDICATE IDENTITY`
→ `CLAIM-SPECIFIC FOLLOW-UP QUERY`.

FFIF012F establishes that complete exact higher-order support discovery on the bounded eight-item surface can be regenerated from the exact profile-count dictionary/dynamic enumeration rather than respondent-membership persistence.

FFP036 establishes that a subsequently frozen external follow-up can be executed by direct producer re-query without FIELD-FORGE-local membership persistence.

Thus:

`UNKNOWN_CLAIM_DISCOVERY != MOTIF_ONTOLOGY_REQUIREMENT`

and

`DISCOVERY_TO_FOLLOWUP != UNIVERSAL_LOCAL_MEMBERSHIP_REQUIREMENT`.

## 5. Configuration law

An exact configuration is a lawful exact joint-state relation/view when a claim/discovery procedure commits it.

It is not a master social object.

A persistent configuration table is optional unless a separately named consumer requirement cannot lawfully be restored from weaker exact relations/query machinery.

Configuration does not by itself mean position, field, identity, ideology, class, worldview or person state.

## 6. Conditional/dependence law

Exact conditional distributions or redistributions are valid empirical structural relations when their risk set, denominator, predicates, support and negative-state law are frozen.

They are the neutral floor for many historical `pressure` intuitions.

But:

`CONDITIONAL_REDISTRIBUTION != STRUCTURAL_PRESSURE_MECHANISM`.

Reference/baseline choice remains claim-specific and must be declared. Causal or mechanism language requires stronger authority.

## 7. Transport law

Cross-context recurrence does not require one common coordinate space.

The default lawful pattern is:

`LOCAL_OBJECT_A + LOCAL_OBJECT_B + AUTHORIZED_TYPED_MAP + COMPARATOR + FALSIFIERS`.

The map may preserve identity, order, rank/quantile, conditional shape, topology, motif/signature or another specifically frozen invariant.

If no lawful map exists, the output is typed no-transport/noncomparability, not forced harmonization.

## 8. Strong-noun law

FIELD FORGE terminal synthesis does not make `FIELD`, `CAPITAL`, `HEGEMONY`, `HOMOLOGY`, `PRESSURE_PATHWAY` or similar nouns universal machine facts.

They remain late admitted interpretations over neutral, independently identified evidence.

Their absence from the universal primitive grammar is deliberate, not a claim that such social realities do not exist.

## 9. Negative-state law

Typed negatives are first-class structural information.

At minimum interfaces must distinguish, as applicable:

- observed positive;
- observed zero;
- exact zero difference;
- valid empty;
- zero-denominator abstention;
- undefined for object;
- out of scope;
- denied/not permitted;
- unsupported/not identified;
- noncomparable/no transport;
- authority blocked;
- observability blocked;
- replay/integrity failure.

A representation that fills one of these with a fabricated value is semantically lossy even if numerically convenient.

## 10. PRE-V3 firewall

FIELD FORGE outputs remain actor-independent.

They may say that an exact structural relation, constraint, alternative, contextual regularity, dependence, resource valuation precursor or other governed possibility exists for a population/context.

They may not say that a particular actor:

- believes it;
- internalizes it;
- identifies with it;
- encounters it;
- remembers it;
- is psychologically shaped by it;
- chooses it;
- has a personality because of it;
- follows a life trajectory because of it.

Those are downstream realization questions.

`POPULATION_FACT != POSITIONED_SUBJECT_POSSIBILITY != INDIVIDUAL_PREDICTION != V3_REALIZED_LIFE`.

## 11. Physical architecture neutrality

This synthesis does not decide persistent-canonical versus source-backed virtual versus hybrid accelerator architecture globally.

The semantic contract permits any physical realization that preserves:

- immutable semantic identity;
- exact authoritative producer references;
- permission/scope ceilings;
- information-loss declarations;
- dependency closure;
- deterministic replay;
- typed negative states;
- consumer interface equivalence at the declared claim grain.

Physical design may therefore be optimized independently where those invariants remain exact.
