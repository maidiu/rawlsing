# FFIF005 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL

## NDC001 — dedicated autonomous MAR

Rejected. It duplicates TTAR/PRA semantics and transport-contract design, creating rival authority and supersession seams.

Preserve only a nonauthoritative materialized mapping-plan receipt for preregistration/replay.

## NDC002 — candidate generation as admission

Rejected. Labels, theory, expert intuition, semantic search, embeddings, and feature matching nominate only.

## NDC003 — recurrence validates the map

Rejected as exact circularity. Transport scores cannot admit, narrow, select, or repair the map they test.

## NDC004 — one desired map is conservative

Rejected. A singleton family is valid only if independent authority truly rules out all alternatives. Otherwise it is a concealed family-too-narrow design.

## NDC005 — broad permutation family is rigorous

Rejected. Allowing arbitrary maps and reporting the best one guarantees discovery. Broad families are useful only as adversarial nulls or may yield nonidentifiability.

## NDC006 — string/translation identity

Rejected. Lexical resemblance may retrieve candidates, never establish source/project identity or transport equivalence.

## NDC007 — theory as privileged map authority

Rejected. Theory can define a hypothesis or candidate role vocabulary; it cannot supply the empirical/semantic authority that makes the predicted recurrence admissible.

## NDC008 — common geometry/embedding as bridge authority

Rejected. A joint representation cannot manufacture alignment and then certify the alignment as recurrence.

## NDC009 — generic role abstraction

Rejected when role breadth makes religion, sect, ethnicity, caste, race, region, language, and other formations freely interchangeable. Abstraction increases mapping freedom and must pay a discriminativeness burden.

## NDC010 — identity-variable equivalence

Rejected. `IDENTITY` is a discovery label, not a correspondence type.

## NDC011 — economic-domain equivalence

Rejected. Literal income, adequacy, food insufficiency, and subjective class do not become one measurement because all bear on material position.

## NDC012 — trust-factor equivalence

Rejected. Different institutions, batteries, scales, stems, and routing cannot be erased by a broad `TRUST` label or pooled factor.

## NDC013 — source-topology blindness

Rejected. Matrix, standalone, multiselect, split, and routed structures remain constitutive of the local objects and can counterfeit a bridge.

## NDC014 — many-to-one as mere map shape

Rejected when the operation makes distinct states one analytic state or removes their influence. That is TTAR-governed collapse.

## NDC015 — set-valued label hides aggregation

Rejected. Retaining ancestry is insufficient if computation aggregates distinct members without a TTAR.

## NDC016 — PRA by analogy

Rejected. Mapping `sectarian identity role` to `ethnic identity role` cannot create the local positional roles or their supposed equivalence.

## NDC017 — reverse map by convenience

Rejected. Directional partial/coarsening maps are not automatically invertible.

## NDC018 — map robustness proves the selected map

Rejected. If every map yields the same result, a weaker map-invariant claim may be identified, but the selected substantive correspondence is not thereby supported.

## NDC019 — representation dependence folded into map dependence

Rejected. Varying local views and varying the bridge are distinct uncertainty/authority axes with different remedies.

## NDC020 — downstream fit cures upstream authority

Rejected under FFIF004 precedence. Prediction, compression, agreement, interpretability, or theoretical fit cannot cure missing identity, TTAR, PRA, claim, or mapping-plan validity.

## NDC021 — no-map as project failure

Rejected. `NO_AUTHORIZED_MAP`, `NO_IDENTIFIABLE_MAP`, and `NO_TRANSPORT` are lawful, typed results. They preserve local evidence instead of forcing a global ontology.

## Strongest preserved negative

`OUTCOME-SELECTED MAPPING IS VALID ONLY AS THE STRONGEST NULL AGAINST ITSELF.`
