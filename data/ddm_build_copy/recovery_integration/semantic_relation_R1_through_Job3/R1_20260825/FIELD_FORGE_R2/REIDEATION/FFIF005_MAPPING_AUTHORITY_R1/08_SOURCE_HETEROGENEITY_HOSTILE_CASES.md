# FFIF005 — SOURCE HETEROGENEITY HOSTILE CASES

No respondent analysis is performed. These are correspondence-authority adjudications over real FIELD FORGE source-form heterogeneity.

## 1. Education

### Detailed credentials -> broad educational levels

Candidate generation is easy: labels, source order, and a generic education domain nominate a relation.

Admissibility is harder:

- exact measurement correspondence: generally `NO`;
- local order correspondence: possible only if each source independently authorizes the relevant order;
- a many-to-one credential coarsening: TTAR required, with explicit lost distinctions and claim scope;
- selection among several monotone coarsenings: must be fixed by independent credential evidence or retained as a finite set;
- cardinal/years equivalence: not implied.

### Credentials -> years

Years may generate a candidate rank/order bridge. It cannot become a universal conversion because a method wants continuity. If institutional tracks, completion, or credential value are constitutive, `NO_MAP` may be the only lawful result.

### Claim ceiling

Even a successful order map says neither equal credential value nor equal social-position return. The latter requires PRA and potentially a separate valuation/estimand court.

## 2. Religion / sect / ethnicity / caste / race

`IDENTITY_VARIABLE` is not an admissible map type.

Labels in these systems arise through different histories, states, colonial classifications, kinship regimes, religious institutions, racial formations, and survey grammars. Lexical resemblance, demographic minority status, or the fact that each is inherited cannot authorize a correspondence.

### Abstract role candidate

`LOCALLY_SALIENT_INHERITED_COLLECTIVE_IDENTITY_POSITION` may generate candidates. It becomes admissible only if:

- every term is operationalized locally rather than inferred from the desired analogy;
- local analytic/PRA authority independently establishes the role in each context;
- salience is evidenced independently of the target recurrence;
- the claim names a narrow relational invariant;
- a TTAR authorizes only that role-level transport;
- alternative collective-identity roles are retained in `H`;
- output forbids category identity, common mechanism, and homology.

If these conditions make the role so broad that language, region, sect, ethnicity, caste, and race can permute freely, the mapping space is non-discriminative. `NO_AUTHORIZED_MAP` is preferable.

## 3. Economic position

Literal income, income adequacy, food insufficiency, and subjective class bear on a shared topic but are not corresponding measurements.

- raw measurement map: `NO` by default;
- within-context order/rank map: possible only under local order/reference authority;
- derived `RESOURCE_POSITION` role: requires independent local PRA/analytic-role authority;
- transport of a conditional direction over that role: requires TTAR plus exact local contrast/risk-set/estimand definitions;
- subjective class cannot silently proxy literal resources, and food insufficiency cannot silently become income.

A transport claim may map derived local roles without mapping measurements only when the output remains explicitly role/indexed and does not back-propagate role correspondence into measurement equivalence. If the derived role exists only because cross-context recurrence looks good, reject it.

## 4. Trust / political attitudes

Broad `TRUST` or `POLITICAL_ATTITUDE` themes generate candidates only.

Admissible cases may include:

- exact institution-object correspondence under compatible source semantics;
- locally authorized ordered response states plus TTAR;
- a partial component map for the subset of institutions with exact relations;
- a role-level motif/signature where target roles are independently authorized.

Different institution sets, response scales, shared stems, battery order, and routing remain constitutive. A universal trust factor or mean cannot authorize the correspondence it requires.

## 5. Source-native matrix vs standalone items

A matrix grid and standalone items may share prose topics while differing in stem, response scale, block/order effects, routing, and respondent-facing act.

Lawful dispositions:

- map exact item meanings componentwise where independent semantic authority exists;
- retain `SOURCE_TOPOLOGY_DIFFERENT` in the transport object;
- test form/block sensitivity separately;
- do not assert block-topology equivalence;
- do not let a multiselect member set count as repeated measurements.

If a recurrence disappears after source-topology control, it is instrument-sensitive, not rescued by label similarity.

## 6. Southern Lebanon hostile intuition

Proposed analogy:

`Lebanon: sect x education articulation`

versus

`Elsewhere: ethnicity x education articulation`.

### Literal map

`sect <-> ethnicity`: `REJECT`.

### Generic minority map

`sectarian minority <-> ethnic minority`: `REJECT_BY_DEFAULT`. Prevalence does not establish relation to state, region, war, class, migration, education, or institutional power.

### Weak typed role map

Potentially lawful only as:

`INDEPENDENTLY_AUTHORIZED_COLLECTIVE_IDENTITY_CONDITIONER`

crossed with

`INDEPENDENTLY_AUTHORIZED_EDUCATION_ORDER/RESOURCE_ROLE`

for one exact configuration-specific contrast/reversal predicate.

Requirements:

- local role authority/PRA precedes comparison;
- the map connects role slots, not sect categories to ethnicity categories;
- TTAR authorizes the bounded transport use;
- link/contrast/conditioning/support/risk-set/estimand are frozen;
- competing identity-role maps are retained;
- result survives map saturation and representation perturbation;
- no claim of same position, mechanism, field, or homology.

### Default disposition

Absent that unusually strong package: `NO_AUTHORIZED_MAP`.
