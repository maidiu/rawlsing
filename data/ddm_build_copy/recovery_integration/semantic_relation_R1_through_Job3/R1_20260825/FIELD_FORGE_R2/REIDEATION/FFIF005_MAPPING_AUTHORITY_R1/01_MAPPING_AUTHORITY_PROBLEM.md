# FFIF005 — MAPPING AUTHORITY PROBLEM

## 1. The circular bridge

FFIF003 lawfully separates independently frozen local objects from a later transport test. That architecture remains incomplete if the bridge is chosen because it makes the target invariant recur:

`LOCAL OBJECTS -> OUTCOME-INSPECTED MAP -> RECURRENCE -> MAP JUSTIFIED BY RECURRENCE`.

This is not corrected by excellent local analysis, theory, visual intelligibility, or a high transport score. The map has learned the answer.

## 2. Five propositions that must remain separate

1. `CANDIDATE_GENERATION`: a heuristic proposes that objects might correspond.
2. `EDGE_AUTHORIZATION`: competent prior authority permits one exact endpoint relation for claim/invariant scope.
3. `MAP_FAMILY_ADMISSION`: a finite set/family is composed only of authorized edges and obeys declared structural constraints.
4. `MAP_SELECTION`: an outcome-independent rule selects one map or retains a finite unresolved set.
5. `TRANSPORT_TEST`: the frozen preservation predicate is applied after 1–4 are sealed.

Candidate generation has zero authority. Transport success has zero retroactive authority.

## 3. Authority stack

| layer | owns | cannot own |
|---|---|---|
| source/project semantics | literal/local identity, wording, category and source-structure facts | cross-context recurrence |
| TTAR | bounded permission to order, group, crosswalk, or treat distinct objects as transport-corresponding | social-position role; outcome-based selection |
| PRA/other role authority | local social/analytic role propositions within its jurisdiction | measurement decoding; map-selection statistics |
| claim identity | proposition, invariant, domain, estimands, quantifier, ceiling | endpoint correspondence authority |
| transport contract mapping plan | authorized family, selection rule, firewall, alternatives, nulls, test | new semantic edges; PRA; homology |
| transport result | whether invariant survives under the frozen plan | justification for changing the plan |

## 4. Minimal lifecycle

Prospective states:

1. `CANDIDATE_GENERATED__NO_AUTHORITY`
2. `ENDPOINT_IDENTITY_VALIDATED`
3. `MAP_EDGE_AUTHORIZED` or `MAP_EDGE_REJECTED`
4. `ADMISSIBLE_MAP_FAMILY_FROZEN`
5. `CLAIM_ADMISSIBLE` or `CLAIM_REJECTED`
6. `SELECTION_RULE_FROZEN`
7. `SELECTED_FOR_TEST`, `FINITE_MAP_SET_RETAINED`, `NO_AUTHORIZED_MAP`, or `UNRESOLVED`
8. `TRANSPORT_ADMISSIBLE`
9. `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL_RECURRENCE`, `REVERSAL`, `MAP_DEPENDENT`, `NONDISCRIMINATIVE`, or `NO_TRANSPORT`

`TRANSPORT_ADMISSIBLE` is a pre-test status. It must never mean that transport succeeded.

## 5. What can generate candidates

Candidate generation may consume:

- exact source-semantic resemblance or relation;
- shared governed project-semantic roles;
- independently established order/response-function/source-topology relations;
- predeclared theoretical analogies;
- deterministic retrieval over labels or authorized feature indexes;
- expert/manual nomination.

None of these alone admits a map edge. Theory and lexical similarity are especially dangerous because they can make a desired analogy appear to have arrived from outside the result.

## 6. What can authorize edges

An edge is admissible only through:

- literal/exact semantic identity already established by competent authority; or
- an exact TTAR whose claim, context, endpoint, invariant, allowed-use, loss, ancestry, and invalidation scope contain the proposed use; plus
- PRA or other analytic-role authority where the edge depends on a role proposition not already established.

Finite manual adjudication may issue or deny those authority objects. Deterministic code may validate or execute them. Neither expert confidence nor an algorithmic score is itself an authority class.

## 7. Selection is not authority

Once the family is authorized, selection may be:

- unique by exact identity;
- unique by a preregistered deterministic rule over outcome-independent authorized features;
- unresolved, requiring all finite alternatives to be retained;
- `NO_AUTHORIZED_MAP`.

If outcome-independent evidence does not distinguish maps, Rawlsing may not select the one with maximum recurrence. It must retain the family and report map dependence or nonidentifiability.

## 8. Closure

The bridge becomes governed when its semantic edges are authorized upstream and its design degrees of freedom are frozen before the target result. The authority is distributed; no new mapping sovereign is required.
