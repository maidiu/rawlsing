# FFIF005 — TTAR / PRA / TRANSPORT BOUNDARY

## 1. Nonduplication ruling

The bridge is governed by composition of existing jurisdictions, not by a new sovereign object:

`EXACT LOCAL OBJECTS -> TTAR/PRA EDGE AUTHORITY -> TRANSPORT MAPPING PLAN -> TEST RESULT`.

Later stages may not back-authorize earlier stages.

## 2. TTAR ends here

TTAR answers:

> For exact endpoints, claim `C`, invariant `I`, and context/direction scope `S`, may these distinct local objects/states be treated as corresponding, ordered, grouped, coarsened, crosswalked, or transport-equivalent, with what loss and forbidden use?

TTAR therefore owns:

- nonidentity map-edge membership;
- order-preserving correspondence permission;
- partitions, bins, category collapse, and coarsening;
- project/source crosswalk consumption;
- claim-scoped transport equivalence;
- source-native ancestry, replay, loss, invalidation, and supersession.

TTAR does **not** own:

- the proposition that an object functions as social position;
- choice among several already-authorized maps based on transport success;
- the preservation test result;
- measurement portability, common mechanism, or homology.

Exact identity already established by competent source/project authority does not need a redundant TTAR, but the transport claim still needs a contract.

## 3. PRA begins and ends here

PRA answers:

> Does this lawfully represented empirical object function as a social-position/resource/hierarchy role for the bounded local claim?

PRA is required before mapping whenever the proposed correspondence depends on such a role. Examples:

- `credential order functions as positional resource` — PRA;
- `sect categories condition a disposition contrast` — local analytic-role authority; PRA only if a position-bearing function is asserted;
- `sect and ethnicity occupy the same social-position role` — stronger PRA/role-equivalence proposition, not ordinary mapping selection.

PRA does not decode measurements, authorize order/collapse/crosswalk, or select the map. A valid local PRA does not itself authorize a cross-context edge. TTAR/transport-equivalence scope is still required when literal identity is absent.

## 4. Transport contract begins here

The transport contract answers:

> Given exact endpoints and already-authorized candidate edges, which finite maps are admissible for claim `C`; what independent rule selects one or retains several; and what test, nulls, alternatives, and claim ceiling apply?

It owns:

- local object refs/hashes;
- candidate-generation provenance;
- edge authority refs;
- `H`, the finite admissible map family;
- map form/directionality/complexity;
- selection/tie/unresolved rules;
- target-outcome firewall;
- alternative-map and multiplicity audit;
- preservation predicate and decision rule;
- transport outcome and inferential ceiling.

It cannot create an edge absent TTAR/identity authority, create a role absent PRA, or use outcome success to repair either.

## 5. Many-to-one boundary

Many-to-one can mean two different things:

### A. Semantic collapse

Several distinct local states are made one analytic state, or their distinctions no longer affect the preservation predicate.

Result: `TTAR_REQUIRED__CATEGORY_COLLAPSE_OR_CLAIM_SCOPED_EQUIVALENCE`.

The transform must remain ancestry-reversible and loss-explicit.

### B. Set-valued relational correspondence

Several exact local objects remain separately identified but jointly correspond to one signature component, and the preservation predicate explicitly consumes the set without asserting member identity.

Result: potentially a transport-contract relation, but only if each member's role/edge is independently authorized and no computation silently aggregates them. If the set is summarized into one value/state, case A applies.

## 6. Candidate generation boundary

Theory, labels, semantic search, and algorithmic matching may populate a review queue. They may not place an edge in `H`.

The transition from candidate to admissible edge must point to exact authority. The transition from admissible family to selected map must point to a frozen selection rule. The transition from selected map to supported transport must point to target evidence and nulls. No one pointer substitutes for another.

## 7. Exact boundary sentence

**TTAR authorizes what nonidentical local objects may be treated as corresponding for the bounded invariant; PRA authorizes any claimed local social-position function; the transport contract freezes and tests a map built only from those prior permissions.**
