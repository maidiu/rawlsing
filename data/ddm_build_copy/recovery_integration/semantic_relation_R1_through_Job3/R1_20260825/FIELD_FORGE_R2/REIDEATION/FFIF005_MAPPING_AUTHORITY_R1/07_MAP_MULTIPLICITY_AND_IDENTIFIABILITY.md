# FFIF005 — MAP MULTIPLICITY AND IDENTIFIABILITY

## 1. Distinct problem

`MAP_DEPENDENCE != REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENCE`.

Map dependence varies the authorized cross-context correspondence while holding local endpoints, semantic inputs, representations, claim, risk sets, and estimands fixed. Representation dependence varies lawful local views while holding the map fixed. They need separate receipts and precedence.

## 2. Required quantities

For admissible family `H` and preservation decision/statistic `S(phi)`:

- `N_H`: count of materially distinct maps, or exact bound/sampling frame;
- `N_success`: maps satisfying the frozen predicate;
- `success_fraction`: `N_success / N_H` when exact enumeration is meaningful;
- `registered_map_rank`: rank/tie position of preregistered map;
- `outcome_optimized_envelope`: minimum/maximum or best score attainable in `H`;
- `successful_map_equivalence_classes`: structurally distinct successful families after predeclared symmetry quotient;
- `component_sensitivity`: which edges/components change the result;
- `map_complexity`: partiality, one/many structure, free permutations, and effective degrees of freedom.

Do not report only the best map.

## 3. Status registry

### `UNIQUE_MAP`

One materially distinct map is authorized and selected independently of target result.

### `NEAR_UNIQUE_MAP`

Several maps differ only through a predeclared analytically immaterial symmetry that cannot change the claim output. “Near” may not be defined from similar high scores after testing.

### `SMALL_FINITE_MAP_SET`

Several materially distinct authorized alternatives remain. Report all results.

### `HIGH_MAP_MULTIPLICITY`

The family contains substantial correspondence freedom relative to endpoint size/claim complexity. This is descriptive until a claim-specific multiplicity rule is frozen.

### `NONDISCRIMINATIVE_MAPPING_SPACE`

The claimed recurrence is common across materially different maps or equally strong under the saturation null. The signature does not distinguish the registered relation from lawful alternatives.

### `RESULT_INVARIANT_ACROSS_MAPS`

All or the frozen material majority of authorized maps yield the same bounded result under the preregistered rule. This can support a **map-invariant weaker claim**, but it is evidence against using the result to justify one substantive correspondence.

### `RESULT_MAP_DEPENDENT`

Admissible maps yield materially different transport classifications or statistics. Preserve the map-indexed results. Do not average or choose the preferred story.

### `NO_IDENTIFIABLE_MAP`

No independent selection rule identifies a map and map-dependent results prevent a map-invariant claim.

## 4. Strongest null

`TYPE_CONSTRAINED_ADMISSIBLE_MAP_SATURATION_NULL`:

1. freeze authorized `H`;
2. compute target statistic for every materially distinct `phi in H`, or a preregistered auditable sample;
3. identify the maximum recurrence the researcher could have obtained by outcome selection;
4. compare the preregistered map to the full distribution/envelope;
5. classify discriminativeness and map dependence.

This null permits the forbidden operation—outcome optimization—only as an adversary. Its best result is evidence about researcher freedom, never the reported empirical map.

## 5. Many maps, same signature

If dozens of maps recover the same signature:

- do not infer that the chosen map is strongly supported;
- do not automatically reject the coarse signature as false;
- ask whether a weaker map-invariant predicate is the actual identified object;
- if the claim requires the substantive edge identities, return `NONDISCRIMINATIVE` / `NO_IDENTIFIABLE_MAP`;
- if the weaker predicate was not preregistered, bank it as exploratory and test later.

## 6. Unequal plausibility

Authorized does not mean equally plausible. But post-hoc analyst weights are another selection degree of freedom.

If maps carry prior plausibility weights, those weights require an independent frozen authority/evidence rule and must be included in the plan before target inspection. Report both weighted and unweighted family diagnostics where weighting could conceal multiplicity. Weighting cannot delete lawful alternatives.

## 7. Infeasible enumeration

When `H` is too large:

- reduce it only through independently justified constraints;
- define a reproducible sampler over exact `H`;
- audit coverage across map-shape/complexity strata;
- report that the outcome-optimized envelope is estimated, not exact;
- do not convert computational limits into `UNIQUE_MAP`.

## 8. Claim ceiling

Map identifiability establishes whether the chosen bridge is discriminative under the admitted family. It does not establish measurement portability, role equivalence, mechanism, or homology.
