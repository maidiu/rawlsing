# FFIF005 — TARGET-OUTCOME FIREWALL

## 1. Governing law

`MAP LEGITIMACY MUST BE FROZEN WITHOUT THE RECURRENCE RESULT THE MAP WILL LATER TEST.`

Complete human blindness is not always possible. A claim may openly name `education order`, `identity-conditioned reversal`, or `institutional-trust motif`. The workable firewall therefore prohibits **result-dependent admission/selection**, not knowledge of the claim's vocabulary.

## 2. Weakest workable firewall

### F0 — independent local freeze

Each endpoint must already exist through an immutable local-object ref/hash. Freeze local input/semantic manifest, representation contract, invariant definition, support posture, risk set, and estimand.

Neither local object may be rebuilt, rotated, recoded, thresholded, or relabeled after cross-context agreement is inspected.

### F1 — claim and predicate freeze

Freeze the normalized transport claim, invariant component(s), exact preservation predicate, decision rule, and forbidden inference.

The claim may identify the invariant family. It may not leave enough freedom to choose the invariant after seeing which one recurs.

### F2 — candidate-generation log

Record every candidate source and the effective candidate space:

- exact semantic retrieval;
- labels/translation;
- theory;
- manual nomination;
- feature matching;
- prior project authority.

Candidates remain `NO_AUTHORITY` until edge receipts are attached. Omitted candidates and exclusions must be recorded where they affect the apparent uniqueness of the chosen map.

### F3 — authority packet

For every allowed edge, freeze exact identity/TTAR/PRA/role refs, allowed and forbidden uses, direction, loss, and invalidation state. Exclude any evidence computed from the target recurrence.

### F4 — family and selection freeze

Freeze and hash:

- complete `H` enumeration or deterministic finite generator;
- map shapes, directionality, partiality, complexity budget;
- selection features and their authority refs;
- deterministic selection rule;
- tie, abstention, and unresolved rules;
- alternative-map/null plan;
- map-multiplicity materiality rule.

### F5 — seal before score

Create `mapping_plan_hash` before computing or revealing the target transport statistic. The execution stage must attest to the same hash.

### F6 — test without repair

Compute only the frozen predicate. Retain results for every required alternative map/null. Do not narrow `H`, change a role, alter a TTAR, switch subgroup, or redefine the invariant because the result is weak or reversed.

### F7 — post-outcome changes

A changed map is a new exploratory claim version. It must preserve the old failed result and cannot be called confirmatory until tested against genuinely uninspected evidence: a held-out context, held-out endpoint object, new wave under separate temporal authority, or other predeclared independent material.

## 3. Forbidden evidence in the mapping packet

- transport similarity, recurrence, fit, or preservation scores;
- which subgroup/configuration reverses or matches;
- cross-context outcome correlations;
- common-space coordinates, Procrustes alignment, joint embeddings, or pooled loadings;
- target-map performance from a previous search over the same evidence;
- feature engineering whose parameters were tuned against target recurrence;
- narrative summaries written after result inspection that selectively emphasize the successful analogy.

## 4. Potentially lawful pre-target evidence

Only when independently established and exactly referenced:

- source/codebook semantic relations;
- current project-semantic identity;
- local order/state/response-function authority;
- source-native structure/topology;
- independently accepted PRA/role authority;
- external current authority already admitted for the exact relation;
- deterministic features derived only from frozen endpoint semantics and not from joint outcome agreement.

If a feature is a function of both endpoints, the burden is to prove it is not functionally the target preservation statistic under another name.

## 5. Blinding levels

### `FULL_TARGET_BLIND`

Adjudicator cannot inspect target invariant values or transport outputs. Prefer where feasible.

### `RESULT_BLIND_CLAIM_AWARE`

Adjudicator knows claim/invariant names and endpoint semantics but cannot inspect target recurrence outputs. This is the minimum acceptable general regime.

### `RESULT_VISIBLE_MAP_FROZEN_EXTERNALLY`

Execution analyst sees results but cannot mutate the independently sealed mapping plan. Acceptable for execution, not for plan adjudication.

### `OUTCOME_EXPOSED_SELECTION`

Map/family/role can change after result inspection. Invalid for positive recurrence evidence; usable only as explicitly exploratory discovery or as a null search.

## 6. Map-family-too-narrow / too-wide audit

The firewall fails if only the desired map was admitted without an independent exclusion rule. It also fails if nearly arbitrary role permutations are admitted and the best-scoring map is reported.

Therefore every plan records:

- candidate count;
- authorized edge count;
- rejected edge count by reason;
- map-family cardinality or bound;
- symmetries/equivalence classes;
- independent reason for every structural constraint.

## 7. Closure

Chronological preregistration alone is insufficient if the map was already tuned informally. Blinding alone is insufficient if `H` was constructed to contain only the desired analogy. The minimum firewall is a sealed evidence/authority packet **plus** a complete map-family/multiplicity account **plus** no post-result repair.
