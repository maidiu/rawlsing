# FFIF002 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL R1

The following approaches were attacked and should not be rediscovered as apparently convenient solutions.

## 1. Universal equivalence layer

Rejected because it collapses different propositions—identity, bounded analytic permission, ordering, representation membership, and transport—into one relation family and invites unintended transitive closure.

## 2. Generic `same_as`

Rejected even if it carries a subtype column. The operational temptation is to consume it as identity and ignore the subtype/scope. Separate relation families and authority rules are required.

## 3. Global canonical social-category vocabulary

Rejected for religion, sect, ethnicity, caste, race, occupation, class, education, and analogous context-bound systems. A global vocabulary may be useful as a display/search aid, but it cannot silently become semantic identity authority.

## 4. Source-native absolutism

Rejected. Preserving local categories does not forbid comparison. Lawful project/claim/transport equivalence is necessary where evidence supports a bounded invariant.

## 5. Source-harmonization laundering

Rejected. A source-provided harmonized category is itself a source-native empirical object with a scope and loss profile. It is not proof of equivalence for every Rawlsing claim.

## 6. Numeric-type semantics

Rejected. Integer/float storage cannot establish:

- ordinal order;
- equal intervals;
- cardinal distance;
- meaningful zero;
- cross-context metric comparability.

Schema may constrain syntax. It does not supply measurement theory.

## 7. String-similarity crosswalks as authority

Rejected. String/translation resemblance may generate candidates for review, never mint project semantic identity.

## 8. Destructive recoding

Rejected. Project-created collapse/binning/normalization cannot overwrite the source-native state/value or sever its ancestry.

## 9. Runtime method code minting semantics

Rejected. A model's input requirement cannot authorize:

- binary collapse;
- rare-category merge;
- interval interpretation of ordinal codes;
- missing-state collapse;
- cross-context standardization;
- universal education-years conversion.

Method can execute an authorized transform or fail/admit inadmissibility. It cannot create the authority it needs.

## 10. Representation-specific preprocessing leakage

Rejected. An MCA/geometry/atlas/motif recode cannot migrate sideways into other views unless separately justified as common semantic authority.

## 11. One-NA semantic model

Rejected. Computational null sentinels cannot erase distinctions among DK, refused, not asked, routed nonapplicable, not in form, unresolved routing, and substantive missing.

## 12. Same-label temporal identity

Rejected. Repeated labels across waves do not prove stable meaning. Instrument wording/options/routing and historical context may change.

## 13. Project-wide rare-category `Other`

Rejected as default. `Other` may be source-native, a bounded analytic partition, or a display construct. These are different things. Method stability cannot silently convert one into another.

## 14. Pooled normalization as comparability proof

Rejected. Percentiles, ranks, and z-scores transform values inside a reference universe. They do not establish that the underlying measures instantiate a common invariant across contexts.

## 15. Literal mathematical invertibility as the sole reversibility rule

Rejected as too strict and misdirected. Category collapse and binning are inherently many-to-one but can remain lawful when exact source-native ancestry and replay are preserved and loss is explicit.

## 16. Ancestry-only without authority

Also rejected. Preserving originals does not make every transform lawful. A receipt must say who authorized what semantic proposition, for which scope and claim.

## 17. Authority-only without ancestry

Rejected. A transform may be substantively warranted yet still violate FIELD FORGE conservation if it destructively erases source-native states.

## 18. PRA as semantic repair layer

Rejected. Position Role Authority cannot be used to authorize basic measurement decoding, ordering, crosswalking, or category collapse merely because a positional representation needs them.

## 19. Automatic composition / transitive closure of transform receipts

Rejected. Scoped permissions do not compose into stronger identity propositions without new adjudication.

## 20. Forced comparability

Rejected. `NONCOMPARABLE` and `UNRESOLVED` are legitimate terminal semantic dispositions. A method that cannot operate without stronger equivalence may be inadmissible.

## 21. No-harmonization dogma

Rejected symmetrically. Rawlsing must not fetishize local difference so strongly that evidence-backed bounded comparison becomes impossible.

## Most dangerous negative pattern

`UNSCOPED_MANY_TO_ONE_PROJECT_CROSSWALK + PROJECT-WIDE REUSE`

It is dangerous precisely because it resembles routine cleaning. It can destroy local distinctions before any analyst sees them, after which every method appears neutral while consuming the ontology produced by the crosswalk.
