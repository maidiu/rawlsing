# SECOND_EXPERIMENT_NORMALIZED_QUESTION_CLASS

Design-only label, not a CIM and not current authority:

`BROAD_VS_EXACT_JOINT_CONDITIONED_DIRECTIONAL_HETEROGENEITY_WITH_COMPOSED_ASSERTION_INTERFACES@DESIGN_R1`

Representation-neutral question:

> Within one frozen same-unit risk set, for one preregistered contrast and one destination quantity, does the broad conditional redistribution relation persist, reverse, split, disappear, or otherwise materially differ when conditioned on exact adequately-supported conjunctions of a preregistered finite operand tuple; and can the complete compound assertion be answered by composing separately lawful support/joint-state/conditional/comparison interfaces without creating a new fused primitive empirical relation?

No representation family is named by the question.

## Normalized symbolic form

Let:

- `R` = one frozen governed empirical-unit/risk set;
- `C` = one frozen contrast operand with declared comparison states or contrast arms `c1` and `c0`;
- `D` = one frozen destination measurement/domain;
- `Z = (Z1...Zk)` = a preregistered finite tuple of same-unit conditioning operands, with `k >= 2` for the higher-order class;
- `s = (z1...zk)` = one exact joint condition predicate `AND_j[Zj = zj]` over source/project-semantic states already lawful for the claim;
- `SUPPORT(s,c)` = exact same-unit support under the frozen exposure/risk-set and support contract;
- `Q0` = the broad conditional redistribution/contrast over `D` between `c1` and `c0` in `R`;
- `Qs` = the same frozen conditional redistribution/contrast inside state `s`;
- `F` = a preregistered representation-neutral contrast/direction functional, when a signed/order classification is required;
- `REL(Q0,Qs)` = the frozen claim-native comparison rule producing the broad-vs-within relation status.

Neither `s` nor `F` is authorized here. `s` is a logical conjunction predicate, not a representation name. `F` may be as weak as an exact native destination-event probability contrast; any stronger ordering/scoring semantics require their existing competent authority later.

## Admissible result family

The design must allow all of the following without treating any as failure of the experiment:

- `PERSISTENCE`
- `REVERSAL`
- `SPLIT_ACROSS_ADMITTED_STATES`
- `DISAPPEARANCE_OR_NULL_EQUIVALENCE_WITHIN_CITC`
- `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE_WITHOUT_SIGN_REVERSAL`
- `NO_ADMITTED_JOINT_STATE`
- `ABSTAIN__INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT`
- `ABSTAIN__DIRECTION_OR_COMPARISON_NOT_AUTHORIZED`
- `ABSTAIN__CLAIM_UNDERIDENTIFIED`

No reversal is required to exist. The outcome-blind canary floor must not inspect the target broad or within-state effect values.

# REPRESENTATION_NEUTRAL_ASSERTION_SLOTS

The minimum claim bundle is seven slots. The exact machine-level rows are in `01_ASSERTION_AND_CHALLENGER_MATRIX.tsv`.

## AS01 — broad conditional redistribution / contrast

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

The claim must establish the broad `C -> D` conditional distribution/contrast under one exact risk set, estimand, baseline, special-state law, and support/uncertainty contract.

Without the broad assertion there is nothing for a within-state relation to reverse, persist against, or disappear relative to.

## AS02 — complete exact joint-conditioning predicate domain

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

The finite operand tuple `Z` and the exact source/project-semantic state identities entering each conjunction must be frozen before target outcomes are inspected. The experiment must deterministically identify the complete candidate joint-state domain relevant to the claim, not select attractive states after seeing reversal.

This is a relation over exact same-unit state conjunctions. It does not require a persistent configuration table.

If the candidate state roster is generated from observed same-unit conjunction support, that generator cannot be mislabeled a mere storage operation: it instantiates an exact joint-state relation at the relevant AOS grain.

## AS03 — exact support/admission state for each joint predicate and each contrast arm

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

For every candidate `s`, the experiment must report the support state required to decide `ADMITTED_FOR_WITHIN_STATE_QUERY` versus typed abstention. The support law and uncertainty contract are not set here.

Exact respondent-level membership is a **producer/replay prerequisite**, not automatically a normalized claim output. The normalized claim requires exact support and reproducibility; it does not need to publish respondent identities unless membership itself is separately asserted.

## AS04 — within-state conditional redistribution / contrast

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

For every support-admitted `s`, compute the same claim-defined conditional distribution/contrast as AS01 with the same destination, baseline, scale, estimand, and semantic universe, now adding `s` to the conditioning relation.

A state that fails support must emit `ABSTAIN`, not a pooled/backoff answer unless the later CIM/support contract explicitly makes that different estimand constitutive.

## AS05 — broad-versus-within direction/order/material-difference relation

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

Apply one frozen claim-native relation rule to `Q0` and each `Qs`.

Exact opposite sign under an already lawful signed contrast can identify a logical reversal. Claims of material difference, disappearance/null-equivalence, or persistence within tolerance require later CITC rows. No threshold is invented here.

## AS06 — compound classification across admitted states

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

The experiment must deterministically aggregate the AS05 state-level statuses into the declared compound status family without choosing states by observed target result.

Examples:

- at least one lawful opposite-direction state -> `REVERSAL_PRESENT` if the later comparison contract says the exact relation qualifies;
- two or more admitted states with incompatible direction/materiality states -> `SPLIT_ACROSS_ADMITTED_STATES`;
- broad material relation with admitted within-state null-equivalence -> `DISAPPEARANCE` under CITC;
- all admitted states preserving the broad relation within CITC -> `PERSISTENCE`;
- unresolved or unsupported slots -> typed abstention.

This is a claim-level logical/comparison operation over already lawful atomic assertions. It is not automatically a new fused empirical object.

## AS07 — explicit abstention coverage

`CLAIM_CONSTITUTIVE`

Every candidate joint state and every compound status path must end in a typed result or typed abstention. Unsupported states may not disappear from the denominator silently, because silent deletion can manufacture apparent persistence or reversal.

- a `minority` label for a reversal state;
- respondent IDs as a published assertion, provided exact membership is replayably available to prove support;

# WEAKEST_OBJECT_CHALLENGER_ORDER

The challenger order is claim-driven, not a prestige ranking.

## W1 — `DIRECT_CONDITIONAL_QUERY_SET`

First challenge:

Can a finite preregistered set/generator of direct exact queries return:

- broad contrast;
- joint-predicate support;
- within-state contrasts;
- broad-vs-within relation status;
- complete abstention coverage?

If yes, this may be a minimal sufficient query interface.

However, if the query system discovers/enumerates the observed exact conjunction domain, the exact joint-state relation must be typed honestly. Ephemeral execution does not make the empirical relation disappear.

## W2 — `OF001 DIRECT_FACT + OF003 DIRECT_CONDITIONAL` composed set

OF001 may supply exact conjunction existence/support when the required state predicates are already frozen; OF003 supplies the broad and within-state conditional distributions/contrasts. A preregistered exact join on the identical joint-predicate key plus the frozen AS05/AS06 comparison rule may form a lawful sufficient set.

This is the strongest obvious weak challenger because it does not retain arbitrary additional same-unit conjunction information after answering the claim.

## W3 — `EXACT_SAME_UNIT_STATE_CARRIER + COMPOSED_QUERY_OPERATORS`

FFIF008's carrier hypothesis is tested as architecture, not silently promoted to an FFIF007 object family.

A lossless carrier may support W1/W2 queries and exact replay. If the claim outputs are identical to W1/W2, the carrier has engineering/reuse value but no extra claim truth by itself.

The carrier does not authorize the joint relation, conditional estimand, or broad-vs-within relation merely because it contains the source states needed to compute them.

## W4 — `OF002 EXACT_CONFIGURATION_OBJECT`

Because exact same-unit conjunction is constitutive, OF002 is a known claim-relevant challenger. It may be sufficient by exact marginalization/conditioning if its lawful assertion interface returns every AS01-AS07 slot.

The decisive question is not whether a persistent configuration file is useful. It is whether OF002 preserves a claim-required invariant unavailable from W1/W2 without those weaker challengers themselves instantiating the same exact joint-state relation.

If an ephemeral exact joint-state query and a stored configuration object expose the same relation and commitments, persistence does not make them separate epistemic votes.

## W5 — `OF002 + OF003`

If an exact joint-state object supplies AS02-AS03 but lacks the full conditional interface, pair it with OF003. FFIF007 set law applies: every cross-member join must be preregistered; each member must be independently valid; and no fusion may hide inside set union.

## W6 — `OF010 COMPOSITE_FUSION_OBJECT`

Default disposition: `NOT_OWED_UNLESS_FUSION_TRIGGER_IS_IDENTIFIED_PREOUTPUT`.

A separate fused AOS is required only if answering the frozen claim creates a new primitive relation, learned bridge, semantic equivalence, cross-object interaction, or interaction estimand that is not a deterministic claim-level composition of the admitted atomic outputs.

The base broad-vs-within sign/order/materiality comparison is not, by itself, such a fusion.

## FFIF007 registered-family accounting

- `OF001 OBSERVATION_EVENT_OR_DIRECT_FACT_OBJECT` — **OWED / CLAIM-RELEVANT** for exact existence/support where its interface suffices.
- `OF002 EXACT_CONFIGURATION_OBJECT` — **OWED / CLAIM-RELEVANT** because exact same-unit conjunction is constitutive.
- `OF003 DIRECT_CONDITIONAL_OBJECT` — **OWED / CLAIM-RELEVANT** because broad and within-state conditional redistribution are constitutive.
- `OF004 ORDER_OR_NEIGHBORHOOD_RELATION_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: no local neighbor/order relation among empirical states is asserted. The AS05 direction relation is an order/sign relation among claim outputs, not a neighborhood relation among respondents/states.
- `OF005 CARDINAL_LOCAL_GEOMETRY_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: no cardinal state distance, interpolation, metric shell, or coordinate invariant is constitutive.
- `OF006 INCIDENCE_OR_TOPOLOGY_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: no connectivity/reachability/incidence assertion exists.
- `OF007 LATENT_MIXTURE_OR_MODEL_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: no predictive/model/mixture invariant exists.
- `OF008 MOTIF_OR_SIGNATURE_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: no recurrence/motif equivalence is asserted.
- `OF009 TRANSPORT_SIGNATURE_OBJECT` — **NOT OWED**: the base second experiment is local and contains no cross-context transport slot.
- `OF010 COMPOSITE_FUSION_OBJECT` — **CONDITIONAL ONLY** if the preoutput composition court finds a genuinely new primitive relation.

This accounting may be superseded by the eventual CIM. It does not mint a COCR universe here.

# QUERY_COMPOSITION_VS_FUSION_TEST

A compound answer is `QUERY_COMPOSITION_WITHOUT_FUSION` only when all of the following are true before outputs:

1. every atomic assertion slot is independently defined and has its own lawful input/authority contract;
2. the cross-interface key is exact identity already present in the frozen claim/common input, not an outcome-learned correspondence;
3. the final compound status is a deterministic preregistered function of atomic outputs plus CIM/CITC/support rules;
4. no new semantic equivalence, state collapse, metric, adjacency, learned bridge, smoothing across states, or cross-object model is introduced by composition;
5. no member queries another stronger object at runtime except through an explicitly admitted dependency already included in the sufficient set;
6. removing the presentation/package layer leaves every atomic empirical assertion unchanged.

Fusion trigger:

`NEW_PRIMITIVE_RELATION_CREATED_BY_COMBINATION`

Examples that would force OF010 consideration:

- a learned cross-state alignment selected because it maximizes reversal;
- a new interaction latent variable that is not reducible to the frozen conditional contrasts;
- outcome-dependent merging of joint states before the within-state query;
- a cross-object similarity/metric used to decide which states count as the same counterpattern;
- a joint model whose fitted interaction is the claim primitive rather than a deterministic comparison of separately frozen atomic assertions.

Not fusion:

- exact join on an identical frozen joint-predicate ID;
- arithmetic subtraction of two already-authorized contrasts;
- sign comparison;
- CITC application;
- deterministic `ANY/ALL/COUNT` logic over admitted state-level statuses;
- co-presenting several interfaces in one package.

# FALSIFIERS_OF_THE_FFIF008_CARRIER_QUERY_HYPOTHESIS

Hypothesis under attack:

`LOSSLESS_CARRIER_PLUS_TYPED_QUERY_COMPOSITION_IS_SUFFICIENT_FOR_COMPOUND_PRE_V3_CLAIMS`

This universal wording is stronger than FFIF008 actually established. One valid claim-class counterexample is enough to falsify it. FFIF009 therefore separates three nested propositions.

## H-CARRIER-DATA

> A lawfully bounded lossless same-unit carrier preserves all source-state information needed by the frozen claim.

Falsified for a canary if a constitutive empirical fact required by the frozen claim cannot be recovered from the carrier within its authority/loss ceiling and must be reacquired from the lower observation/provenance substrate.

A new metric/topology/model relation computed from carrier values does **not** falsify H-CARRIER-DATA; it shows relation incompleteness, not source-data loss.

## H-TYPED-QUERY-COMPOSITION

> The currently admitted atomic query/relation vocabulary over the valid carrier can cover every constitutive assertion slot by lawful composition.

A genuine falsifier requires all of the following:

1. claim identity/invariants, support contract, CITC, common input, and challenger universe were frozen preoutput;
2. the carrier is valid and lossless for the claim's source-state surface;
3. every already-authorized atomic query family that is owed by the claim has been fairly instantiated, not omitted by implementation convenience;
4. every lawful composition of those interfaces leaves at least one constitutive slot `UNDEFINED`, `FAIL`, or outside CITC/support;
5. a distinct independently admissible AOS or minimal object set supplies that exact missing slot within contract;
6. its success depends on a primitive empirical relation not expressible as deterministic composition of the frozen atomic outputs without adding a new relation commitment;
7. the result survives the applicable hostile/null/holdout regime.

If these conditions hold, the universal carrier-plus-current-query-vocabulary sufficiency hypothesis is false.

## H-NO-FUSION-FOR-THIS-CLASS

> The normalized higher-order reversal compound status is only a deterministic composition of broad contrast, exact state support, within-state contrasts, and the frozen comparison rule.

Falsified if the compound assertion cannot be defined or evaluated without a genuinely new primitive cross-interface relation satisfying FFIF007's OF010 trigger.

## What is **not** a falsifier

- a compiler omitted a known lawful within-state query;
- a query implementation cannot enumerate a finite domain that another implementation can enumerate under the same relation contract;
- a stored exact-joint-state object succeeds only by exposing the same exact predicates/support a compiled query could lawfully expose;
- a particular query materialization pruned a state while the carrier retained it;
- runtime/latency/storage makes one implementation inconvenient;
- a candidate has insufficient support and therefore correctly abstains;
- a missing PRA/TTAR/CITC/support authority prevents the experiment from being identified;
- a new lossy display or coordinate interface is convenient but not claim-constitutive.

Those outcomes show implementation incompleteness, engineering tradeoff, or underidentification—not empirical necessity of a stronger representation.

# HOSTILE_FIXTURE_REQUIREMENTS

FFIF008's fixture distinction is adopted explicitly:

`CONTRACT_VIOLATION_FIXTURE != EMPIRICAL_NULL_FIXTURE != CLAIM_OR_COMMON_INPUT_VARIANT`.

The exact design matrix is in `02_HOSTILE_FIXTURE_REQUIREMENTS.tsv`.

## Contract-violation fixture

Purpose: prove fail-closed behavior or invariance when an experiment framework is given a counterfeit or contract-breaking carrier/identity/input. Expected output is rejection or exact nonpromotion, not an empirical null estimate.

Required future cases include:

- R2F002-style questionnaire-block counterfeit / zero-new-information clone;
- postfreeze semantic tamper;
- counterfeit coexposure or respondent-link identity;
- ancestry contamination;
- silent state deletion / unsupported backoff presented as exact within-state evidence.

## Empirical-null fixture

Purpose: preserve the declared semantic identities/risk-set structure as far as the null requires while synthetically destroying the target empirical relation under an exact preregistered permutation/randomization generator.

Required future cases include:

- R2F003-style respondent-link association null where the claim crosses position/disposition surfaces;
- R2F016-style within-item/joint-state null preserving item marginals while destroying higher-order same-unit dependence;
- a second-experiment heterogeneity null that preserves the broad contrast and state supports as designed while breaking the state-specific modulation relation, if a lawful exchangeability generator can be preregistered.

An empirical null is a synthetic fixture variant, never evidence that the corresponding real common-input claim has changed.

## Claim/common-input variant

Purpose: test sensitivity to a **lawful changed empirical question/input**, not falsify the original claim.

Required future cases include:

- R2F004-style block removal;
- a different preregistered conditioning-operand tuple;
- a lawful TTAR state collapse/rebin/order variant;
- a different contrast pair or destination functional;
- a different support/uncertainty or CITC comparison contract.

These must receive a new comparison/common-input identity as applicable and cannot be scored as same-claim representation dependence.

No fixture bytes, generator, permutation receipt, or fixture authority is minted by FFIF009.

# OUTCOME_BLIND_EMPIRICAL_SELECTION_FLOOR

No source, wave, dataset, country, or variable is selected.

A future governed existing-data candidate may instantiate the **full** second-experiment class only if all hard floor rows in `03_OUTCOME_BLIND_SELECTION_FLOOR.tsv` pass without inspecting target broad/within-state effect results.

The floor is outcome-blind in the following exact sense:

Allowed before canary selection:

- currentness/provenance;
- questionnaire/source-structure type;
- governed respondent identity;
- actual exposure/coexposure;
- exact source/project semantic states;
- role-authority availability;
- counts required solely to evaluate the preregistered support law;
- global destination nondegeneracy needed to make the declared contrast possible;
- design/holdout/permutation feasibility;
- ancestry and source-block structure.

Forbidden before selection:

- broad target contrast magnitude/direction;
- within-joint-state target contrast magnitude/direction;
- which state reverses or looks interesting;
- counterpattern/minority selection based on destination outcome;
- representation outputs;
- geometry/topology/model outputs;
- any result used to pick `Z`, state roster, contrast functional, or support threshold.

Minimum full-class properties:

1. exact governed common-unit/respondent carrier;
2. positive actual exposure/coexposure for `C`, `D`, and all `Z` operands;
3. source-structure semantics proving separately respondent-facing measurements where the claim treats them as separate measurements;
4. at least two same-unit conditioning operands (`k >= 2`) with lawful finite state identities for the higher-order conjunction class;
5. one broad contrast with both contrast arms support-admissible in the risk set;
6. a destination with at least two lawful substantive states or an already-authorized directional/order functional sufficient for the planned contrast;
7. at least two distinct exact joint states support-admissible on the required contrast arms if the full `SPLIT_ACROSS_ADMITTED_STATES` status is to remain identifiable; a one-state candidate can instantiate only a narrower reversal/disappearance subclass;
8. exact membership/support recoverability for every admitted joint predicate, even if respondent IDs are not part of the final assertion;
9. outcome-blind structural possibility of heterogeneity: more than one admitted conditioning state and a nondegenerate destination, without computing the target state-specific effect;
10. holdout/null feasibility wherever the later inferential/support contract requires it;
11. respondent-link and within-item permutation/null feasibility if those hostile tests are declared required;
12. exact ancestry separation where position/disposition separation is constitutive;
13. bounded PRA availability or an exact finite PRA prerequisite list;
14. TTAR/state-order authority already available wherever the planned contrast requires non-native equivalence, collapse, binning, or order;
15. no new source acquisition required.

Support adequacy is evaluated later through the frozen support/uncertainty contract. FFIF009 sets no numeric minimum cell size.

# AUTHORITY_PREREQUISITES

No new current authority object is shown to be necessary by this design.

A future empirical instantiation must consume existing jurisdictions in this order:

1. **respondent / exposure / risk-set authority** — exact common-unit identity and actual coexposure;
2. **measurement/state/source-structure authority** — exact item and source-native state identity;
3. **TTAR where needed** — any non-native grouping, ordering, binning, collapse, semantic equivalence, or transport-like treatment;
4. **PRA / analytic-role authority where invoked** — condition/disposition/position roles must not be inferred from target output;
5. **CIM** — later freeze the exact proposition, operand tuple, quantifier, contrast, destination functional, compound status rule, and representation-neutral assertion slots;
6. **support/uncertainty contract** — later freeze state/contrast support adequacy and abstention law;
7. **CITC** — later authorize material difference, disappearance/null-equivalence, persistence tolerance, and any non-exact comparison margin;
8. **AOS/COCR preregistration** — later close the claim-relevant challenger universe before outputs if bounded necessity is to be tested;
9. **CVCR** — later compare valid object/package outputs against the frozen assertion slots;
10. **fixture registration/evidence receipts** — future nonauthoritative experimental receipts may bind fixture bytes/generators and replay, but no new semantic sovereign is required.

No metric, geometry, topology, motif, transport, latent model, or composite-fusion authority is required by the base question class.

If `F` is merely an exact contrast in probability of one native destination state, no new order authority follows. If `F` scores/aggregates ordered categories, uses a monotone scale, or treats distinct destination states as commensurable, the relevant existing semantic/order/TTAR authority must precede the claim.

# Final synthesis

The second experiment should not ask whether a configuration representation catches a reversal that a conditional representation misses. That framing would preload the result.

It should ask whether one frozen broad conditional relation changes when the same relation is recomputed inside exact same-unit conjunction predicates whose identity and support are frozen independently of the target outcome, and then ask what is the **weakest lawful empirical object or object set** capable of returning every normalized assertion slot.

That design creates a real hostile test of FFIF008:
