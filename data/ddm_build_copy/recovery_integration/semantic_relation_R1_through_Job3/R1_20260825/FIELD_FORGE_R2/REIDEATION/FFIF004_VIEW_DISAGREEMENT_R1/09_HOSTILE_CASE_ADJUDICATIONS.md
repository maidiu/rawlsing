# FFIF004 — HOSTILE CASE ADJUDICATIONS

No respondent analysis is executed here. These are formal/source-topology design cases only.

## Case 1 — rare minority configuration preserved by atlas, erased by geometry

### Facts assumed

- exact lawful atlas records the rare joint state and exact support;
- geometry passes its own basic computation but compression does not preserve the branch;
- common input and semantics are identical.

### Adjudication

If the frozen claim is only a majority-neighborhood/gradient claim and minority exact-state preservation is outside its required invariant bundle:

`DIFFERENT_INVARIANTS_PRESERVED` + `KNOWN_LOSS_EXPLAINS_DIFFERENCE`.

If the frozen claim includes existence/support of the rare configuration:

geometry is `INVALID_FOR_CLAIM` or `UNDEFINED_FOR_REQUIRED_ASSERTION_SLOT`; it cannot answer `FALSE` merely because the branch vanished in compression.

If a bundled downstream claim requires both exact minority existence and geometric neighborhood interpolation, proceed to the weaker-object test. Multi-view retention may be required.

### Not supported

The case alone does **not** establish nonreducibility.

## Case 2 — smooth positional gradient visible geometrically but not economically describable through sparse exact configurations

### Facts assumed

- local geometry preserves a stable neighborhood/rank relation under nuisance tests;
- exact configuration enumeration is sparse/fragmented;
- atlas remains exact at its floor but has no intrinsic metric/neighborhood.

### Adjudication

For a frozen gradient/neighborhood claim:

geometry can have `INCREMENTAL_VALUE` or be the only admitted view capable of the assertion slot.

For an exact configuration claim:

geometry's gradient is not a substitute.

“Economically describable” is an engineering criterion, not truth. Before retaining coordinates, test weaker objects such as a local rank/neighborhood relation or direct conditional smoother. If one preserves the required gradient relation, full geometry is not necessary.

If the claim jointly requires exact minority structure and stable gradient information and no weaker object preserves both, `MULTI_VIEW_RETENTION_REQUIRED` becomes possible.

## Case 3 — graph/hypergraph exposes disconnected measurement topology that pooled geometry hides

### Facts assumed

- source/routing topology lawfully identifies disconnected or unresolved components;
- pooled geometry constructs one common metric across them.

### Adjudication

This is not a valid-view disagreement.

If the geometry has bridged components without authority, it fails hard admissibility:

`INVALID_REPRESENTATION__FORCED_DISCONNECTED_COMPONENTS`.

The topology view is not “one perspective.” It is part of the lawfulness boundary governing whether the geometric representation may exist.

If a geometry is constructed separately inside lawful components, local geometric claims may survive.

## Case 4 — latent-profile model creates coherent classes while exact respondents remain internally contradictory

### Facts assumed

- mixture model has stable fit and heldout value;
- exact observations preserve within/respondent contradiction or minority configurations;
- common input is lawful.

### Adjudication

If the latent claim is only probabilistic compression:

`DIFFERENT_INVARIANTS_PRESERVED`.

The class/profile is model-derived and may coexist with exact contradiction beneath it.

If the model output is promoted to “respondent really belongs to coherent social class/type X,” the representation exceeds its contract:

`INVALID_INTERPRETIVE_PROMOTION`.

If hard assignment destroys claim-required contradiction/minority mass, hard-class output is `INVALID_FOR_CLAIM` even if the soft mixture remains useful.

No nonreducibility claim follows merely because the model is coherent and people are not.

## Case 5 — conditional redistribution reverses in a configuration while global geometry suggests one direction

### Adjudication A: global versus conditional claims were not frozen as identical

This is `CLAIM_MISMATCH` or `SAME_BROAD_QUESTION_DIFFERENT_INVARIANT`.

Global direction and configuration-specific direction are distinct assertions. The conditional reversal must not be averaged away, but it also does not logically contradict a correctly scoped global average.

### Adjudication B: claim asserts uniform direction across configurations

The conditional reversal directly falsifies the uniformity claim.

A geometry that cannot recover the reversal is inadequate for that claim, regardless of global fit.

### Adjudication C: bundled claim requires both global gradient and conditional reversals

Test weaker objects. A conditional distribution object may preserve both without full geometry. Only if weaker objects fail may multi-view retention become necessary.

## Case 6 — cross-context signature transport survives while literal category correspondence does not

### Adjudication

No contradiction.

FFIF003 already separates literal semantic/category equivalence from weaker invariant transport.

Possible result:

- literal category/measurement transport: `NO_TRANSPORT`;
- claim-scoped order/topology/conditional/motif signature transport: `SUPPORTED`.

This is `DIFFERENT_TRANSPORT_INVARIANTS`, not evidence that categories are “both same and different.”

The signature may not be promoted to homology, common field, or common world-space.

## Case 7 — one representation shows dramatic structure under the questionnaire-block counterfeit and another does not

### Facts assumed

A synthetic/native-ancestry-preserving block clone adds no substantive respondent information but changes representation weight/topology.

### Adjudication

If the dramatic structure materially changes under the counterfeit beyond its preregistered tolerance, that representation fails its hostile fixture for any claim of substantive structural robustness:

`INVALID_REPRESENTATION__SOURCE_TOPOLOGY_COUNTERFEIT_SENSITIVITY`.

The other view does not “win by vote.” It simply survives one hard attack the first view failed.

If the changing output is explicitly a measurement-topology diagnostic, sensitivity to the clone may be expected and lawful; claim identity decides which proposition was being tested.

## Cross-case lessons

1. Rare facts are not votes against gradients.
2. Compression value is not truth.
3. Measurement topology can invalidate a view before pluralism begins.
4. Model coherence cannot erase observed contradiction.
5. Global and conditional claims must be separated.
6. Transport invariant strength must be typed.
7. Hostile fixtures adjudicate validity, not aesthetic preference.
8. Nonreducibility is a last-stage minimal-sufficiency result, not the default name for any messy disagreement.
