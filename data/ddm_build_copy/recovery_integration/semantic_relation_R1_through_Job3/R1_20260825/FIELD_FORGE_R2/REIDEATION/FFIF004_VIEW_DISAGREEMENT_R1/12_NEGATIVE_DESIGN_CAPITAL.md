# FFIF004 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL

This file banks routes rejected by the court so later FIELD FORGE work does not rediscover them as attractive shortcuts.

## NDC001 — master-representation arbitration

Rejected:

> When valid views disagree, select geometry/atlas/model family as the privileged representation in advance.

Why rejected: FFIF001 already shows each family preserves different invariants and imports different equivalences/losses.

Preserved lesson: representation role is claim-scoped and must be earned against a weaker object.

## NDC002 — method majority vote

Rejected:

> Whichever result appears across the largest number of methods is robust truth.

Why rejected: methods are not independent/exchangeable voters and may share the same hidden ontology.

Preserved lesson: adjudicate claim identity, validity, invariant coverage, and evidence—not counts.

## NDC003 — metric majority vote

Rejected:

> The representation winning more metrics gets architectural priority.

Why rejected: metrics evaluate different capabilities; counting them is an informal scalar score.

Preserved lesson: capability matters only if the frozen claim requires it.

## NDC004 — compression as truth

Rejected:

> The representation preserving the phenomenon with fewer dimensions/bytes is more real.

Why rejected: compression value and descriptive fidelity are distinct. Compression can erase minority/contradictory structure.

Preserved lesson: utility selection follows empirical sufficiency, not vice versa.

## NDC005 — exactness as universal primacy

Rejected:

> Exact configuration representation is automatically the master because it loses least observed information.

Why rejected: exact configuration has no intrinsic metric/neighborhood, can fragment continuous structure, and may be unnecessary for claims answerable by weaker conditional/order objects.

Preserved lesson: exact facts remain a strong floor where lawful, but representational necessity is claim-specific.

## NDC006 — pluralism as invalidity shield

Rejected:

> A view that changes risk sets, semantics, estimands, or fails hostile fixtures can survive as “another perspective.”

Why rejected: plurality begins after validity.

Preserved lesson: invalidation precedence is hard and noncompensatory.

## NDC007 — known loss renamed nonreducibility

Rejected:

> A minority branch vanishes under a declared compression, therefore reality is nonreducible.

Why rejected: known loss explains the difference. If the lost invariant is claim-required, the lossy view is inadequate for that claim; no metaphysical conclusion follows.

Preserved lesson: known-loss court precedes nonreducibility.

## NDC008 — direct contradiction as privileged route to nonreducibility

Rejected:

> Two valid views saying opposite things about the same scalar claim are evidence of deep nonreducible structure.

Why rejected: first suspects are claim mismatch, uncertainty, nonidentifiability, admissibility failure, or representation sensitivity.

Preserved lesson: the strongest bounded nonreducibility case is usually non-substitutable preservation of co-required invariants, not logical contradiction.

## NDC009 — infinite nonreducibility claim

Rejected:

> Rawlsing can prove no possible representation could ever reduce the result.

Why rejected: finite search cannot quantify over unknown future representation families/transforms.

Preserved lesson: only claim-scoped bounded nonreducibility relative to a declared finite admissible-object universe is machine-identifiable.

## NDC010 — post-hoc claim identity

Rejected:

> After viewing outputs, rewrite the common question until the preferred representations appear to agree/disagree meaningfully.

Why rejected: claim identity becomes a result-fitting device.

Preserved lesson: freeze normalized assertion slots and invariant bundle before comparison.

## NDC011 — theory resolves empirical disagreement

Rejected:

> Choose the view that best resembles field/class/hegemony/homology theory.

Why rejected: structural interpretation is downstream; theory cannot cure empirical invalidity or choose semantic transforms.

Preserved lesson: theory may define later hypotheses and required empirical premises, never override the comparison court.

## NDC012 — multi-view as new ontology

Rejected:

> Because one master representation failed, the machine should canonize “multiple ontologies.”

Why rejected: multi-view necessity is a bounded representation-sufficiency result. A future weaker object can supersede it.

Preserved lesson: preserve plurality without reifying plurality.

## NDC013 — holdout ritual for exact existence facts

Rejected:

> Every claim, including deterministic exact source fact existence, must replicate in holdout before it can count.

Why rejected: proof burden must match proposition. Exact existence requires lawful provenance/replay; learned generalization requires independent falsification.

Preserved lesson: use holdout where logically relevant, not as generic epistemic theater.

## NDC014 — undefined means false

Rejected:

> If one representation cannot encode an invariant, treat its output as evidence that the invariant is absent.

Why rejected: representation incapacity is not a negative empirical observation.

Preserved lesson: keep `UNDEFINED_FOR_VIEW`, `FALSE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and `NOT_IDENTIFIED` distinct.
