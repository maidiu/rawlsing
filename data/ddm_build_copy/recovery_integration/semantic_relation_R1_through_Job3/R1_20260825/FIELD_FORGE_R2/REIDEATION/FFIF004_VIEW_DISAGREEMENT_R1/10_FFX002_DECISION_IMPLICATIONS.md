# FFIF004 — FFX002 DECISION IMPLICATIONS

## 1. Ruling

The FFX002 experimental machinery remains valuable. Its **winner semantics do not**.

Preserve:

- one lawful common input;
- respondent/exposure/risk-set hard gates;
- frozen semantic transforms;
- independent view production;
- holdout discipline;
- ancestry firewall;
- hostile/null/counterfeit fixtures;
- separate metric families;
- no scalar master score.

Replace the final role-selection logic with claim-scoped comparison receipts and minimal-sufficiency adjudication.

## 2. What must be removed or renamed

### Remove as terminal architecture verdict

`GEOMETRY_EARNS_PRIMARY_REPRESENTATIONAL_ROLE`

The phrase “primary representational role” conflicts with FFIF001's rejection of master representation and can be won by compression/downstream value even where another view preserves distinct claim-relevant facts.

### Replace with

`GEOMETRY_EARNS_CLAIM_SCOPED_INCREMENTAL_VIEW_VALUE`

or, where a frozen claim can only be answered by the geometric invariant and all weaker-object tests fail:

`GEOMETRIC_VIEW_REQUIRED_FOR_CLAIM_<id>`.

### Narrow

`ATLAS_FIRST_SUFFICIENT__GEOMETRY_ADDS_NO_MATERIAL_VALUE`

into:

`WEAKER_OBJECT_SET_SUFFICIENT_FOR_FROZEN_CLAIM_BATTERY__GEOMETRY_NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`

because an atlas is not privileged merely for being exact if an even weaker direct conditional/topology/rank object suffices.

### Reinterpret

`NON_SUBSTITUTABLE_MULTI_REPRESENTATION_ARCHITECTURE_SUPPORTED`

as:

`MULTI_VIEW_MINIMAL_SUFFICIENT_SET_REQUIRED_FOR_<claim_or_battery>`

with optional escalation to bounded nonreducibility only after the FFIF004 proof burden.

## 3. Add claim identity before comparison

Every FFX002 query in the frozen battery receives a `CLAIM_IDENTITY_MANIFEST` before view outputs are inspected.

Required fields include:

- normalized proposition;
- risk set;
- estimand;
- semantic/TTAR hash;
- target invariant(s);
- quantifier/locality/conditioning relation;
- support/uncertainty law;
- materiality/loss tolerance.

The atlas and geometry then map their outputs to common assertion slots.

A view may return `UNDEFINED_FOR_VIEW`; it may not coerce an unsupported answer.

## 4. Per-claim FFX002 recording law

For each frozen claim, record separately:

### `AGREEMENT`

Possible values:

- `VIEW_ROBUST_WITHIN_TOLERANCE`;
- `AGREE_BUT_LOW_SUPPORT`;
- `NOT_COMPARABLE_DIFFERENT_CLAIM`.

### `INCREMENTAL_VALUE`

Record a capability-specific value only:

- improved heldout prediction;
- compression at preserved fidelity;
- unique lawful neighborhood/rank query;
- unique exact/minority/J query;
- unique topology or conditional query.

Never turn the count of incremental-value wins into a winner.

### `REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENCE`

Record whether same-claim normalized answers differ materially after uncertainty.

### `KNOWN_LOSS`

Record exactly which declared loss explains which disagreement component and whether the lost invariant is claim-required.

### `MULTI_VIEW_NECESSITY`

Record only after the weaker-object test identifies a nonredundant minimal sufficient set.

### `INVALID_VIEW`

Record the failed hard gate/hostile fixture. Remove the view for that claim. Do not average it with valid views.

### `UNRESOLVED_COMPARISON`

Use for insufficient evidence, nonidentifiability, claim-identity ambiguity, or unclosed weaker-object universe.

## 5. Revised terminal states

A future execution may synthesize the per-claim receipts into one of these experiment-level states:

- `EXPERIMENT_INVALID__<typed_reason>`;
- `BOTH_VIEWS_LAWFUL__FROZEN_QUERY_BATTERY_VIEW_ROBUST`;
- `ONE_VIEW_INVALID__OTHER_REMAINS_CLAIM_SCOPED_ONLY`;
- `WEAKER_OBJECT_SET_SUFFICIENT__STRONGER_VIEW_NO_REQUIRED_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`;
- `REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENCE_DETECTED__NO_MULTI_VIEW_NECESSITY_PROVED`;
- `MULTI_VIEW_MINIMAL_SUFFICIENT_SET_REQUIRED`;
- `CLAIM_SCOPED_BOUNDED_NONREDUCIBILITY_SUPPORTED`;
- `BOTH_CURRENT_VIEW_FAMILIES_INADEQUATE__WEAKER_OR_NEW_OBJECT_REDESIGN_REQUIRED`;
- `INCONCLUSIVE__LAWFUL_COMPARISON_UNRESOLVED`.

These states describe the evidence. None crowns an ontology.

## 6. Metric registry change

Existing FFX002 metrics should be tagged by the empirical proposition they can evaluate:

- exact/minority preservation;
- higher-order preservation;
- conditional query fidelity;
- neighborhood/rank stability;
- compression;
- predictive/downstream value;
- null/counterfeit sensitivity;
- provenance/replay;
- uncertainty posture.

A metric on a capability intentionally undefined for another view is not evidence that the other view lost.

The comparator asks whether the capability is required by the claim.

## 7. Preservation-floor change

The current geometry preservation floor is useful as a claim-specific contract but must not become universal ontology.

Future execution should bind preservation floors to claim IDs/invariant bundles. A 90% minority recall threshold may be acceptable for one approximate interpolation claim and unacceptable for a claim about existence of every observed minority branch.

Loss budgets need explicit authority.

## 8. Downstream ablation change

Ablation may establish incremental predictive/query value. It does not establish empirical primacy.

Allowed conclusion:

> Adding view V to object set O improves frozen downstream query performance by X under claim C while preserving required invariants.

Forbidden conclusion:

> View V is therefore the truer or primary representation of the social world.

## 9. Nonreducibility in FFX002

FFX002 may emit `CLAIM_SCOPED_BOUNDED_NONREDUCIBILITY_SUPPORTED` only if execution adds:

- finite admissible-object universe declaration;
- transform-reduction tests;
- weaker-object sufficiency matrix;
- nonredundant invariant coverage proof;
- nuisance/holdout survival;
- supersession clause.

Without those additions it may emit at most:

`MULTI_VIEW_MINIMAL_SUFFICIENT_SET_REQUIRED_ON_TESTED_OBJECTS`.

## 10. Exact decision change in one sentence

**FFX002 changes from selecting the representation that earns the broadest role to identifying the minimal lawful empirical object/view set sufficient for each frozen claim, while recording representation dependence and incremental value without method voting or ontological promotion.**
