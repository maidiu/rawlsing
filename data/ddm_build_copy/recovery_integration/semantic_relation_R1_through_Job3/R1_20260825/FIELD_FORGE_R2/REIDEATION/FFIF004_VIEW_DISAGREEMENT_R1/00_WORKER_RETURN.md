## Exact distinction: representation dependence vs nonreducibility

`REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENCE` means:

> On the same lawful common semantic input and the same frozen empirical claim, two or more independently admissible representations produce materially different normalized claim outputs beyond the claim's declared tolerance, after ordinary uncertainty is accounted for.

It is a first-class empirical status. It does **not** imply that both views are equally informative, that both must be retained, or that reality possesses multiple ontologies. Representation dependence may be explained by a known loss, a scale/locality dependence, an underspecified query, unresolved evidence, or a genuine need for multiple views.

`CLAIM_SCOPED_BOUNDED_NONREDUCIBILITY` means:

> The frozen claim requires two or more co-required empirical invariants; independently admissible views each preserve nonredundant required invariants; no lawful transform between them and no strictly weaker object in a predeclared finite admissible-object universe preserves the complete required invariant bundle within the claim's loss/uncertainty budget; forcing one view to substitute for the other destroys verified claim-relevant information; and the necessity survives applicable nuisance/holdout attacks.

This is a claim about the **minimal sufficient empirical representation set for one bounded claim**, not a claim that society is metaphysically nonreducible.

Absolute `GENUINE_EMPIRICAL_NONREDUCIBILITY`—meaning no possible present or future representation could preserve the same information—is not finitely identifiable and is therefore rejected as a machine-emittable status.

## Hardest validity precedence rule

`NO DOWNSTREAM AGREEMENT, FIT, PREDICTIVE VALUE, COMPRESSION, INTERPRETABILITY, OR MULTI-METHOD SUPPORT MAY CURE A FAILURE OF COMMON INPUT, SEMANTIC AUTHORITY, CLAIM IDENTITY, OR A VIEW'S OWN ADMISSIBILITY CONTRACT.`

In particular, if a representation changes the risk set, estimand, source-native ancestry, special-state semantics, or TTAR-authorized category/state identity in order to become computable, the result is not representation dependence. It is `INVALID_COMMON_INPUT`, `SEMANTIC_INPUT_DIVERGENCE`, or `INVALID_REPRESENTATION` as appropriate.
