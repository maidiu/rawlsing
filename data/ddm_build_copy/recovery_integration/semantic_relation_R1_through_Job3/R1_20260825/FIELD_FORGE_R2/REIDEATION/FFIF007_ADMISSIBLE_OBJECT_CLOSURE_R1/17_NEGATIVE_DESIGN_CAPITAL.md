# FFIF007 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL

## Rejected: metaphysical closure

No finite court can establish that every possible representation, transform, parameterization, or future method fails. `ABSOLUTE_NONREDUCIBILITY` remains unidentifiable.

## Rejected: tested arms equal admissible universe

The FFX002 R2 A/B/C arms answer experimental design questions. They do not automatically cover direct fact, rank/order, topology, or other claim-specific weaker challengers.

## Rejected: universal method-family checklist

Atlas, geometry, graph, latent class, motif, and transport are neither universally required nor uniformly meaningful. Closure starts from CIM assertion slots, not canonical method prestige.

## Rejected: algorithm as object

PCA, MCA, UMAP, LCA, k-means, random forest, seeds, and hyperparameters do not receive independent universe seats unless they change the AOS identity tuple. Method zoo size is not evidence.

## Rejected: one geometry object by label

Ordinal neighborhood, cardinal metric distance, coordinate embeddings, and disconnected local domains can assert materially different relations. Coarse family labels cannot hide those differences.

## Rejected: split-on-result

If legal variants differ materially, first classify method sensitivity. A post-output AOS split cannot preserve the old closure hash or selectively isolate a threatening result.

## Rejected: scalar object strength

Compression, information volume, assumptions, inference, utility, and compute conflict. Weakness is a typed claim-relative partial order; noncomparability survives.

## Rejected: compute/storage as epistemic weakness

A cheaper object is not weaker. Engineering selection occurs after claim sufficiency and dominance.

## Rejected: averaging invariant loss

Hard-zero/constitutive invariant failure cannot be compensated by utility, predictive gain, or excellence on another slot.

## Rejected: known blocker as convenient exclusion

A currently owed missing authority/substrate dependency cannot be used to starve a claim-relevant challenger. Closure abstains.

## Rejected: every conceptual object blocks forever

An object requiring unknown future evidence or new unowed semantics lies outside current U with a declared limitation. Otherwise finite engineering judgment would be impossible.

## Rejected: receipt as new semantic sovereign

COCR organizes generation, dispositions, freeze, and bounded quantification. It cannot mint TTAR, PRA, claim, tolerance, mapping, measurement, or role authority.

## Rejected: unique minimal set presumption

All inclusion-minimal sets and the nondominated frontier must be reported. The first pair found is not canonical.

## Rejected: fusion hidden in subset search

If combining objects creates a new primitive relation, that fusion is its own preregistered AOS. Ordinary set union cannot launder it.

## Rejected: future supersession as retroactive universal falsification

A correctly scoped earlier result becomes `SUPERSEDED__EXPANDED_U`. It was dishonest only if it omitted a then-known challenger or violated its stated boundary.

## Rejected: structural ontology from architecture

Multi-object necessity within U cannot prove social ontology, dialectics, class, field, mechanism, or homology.
