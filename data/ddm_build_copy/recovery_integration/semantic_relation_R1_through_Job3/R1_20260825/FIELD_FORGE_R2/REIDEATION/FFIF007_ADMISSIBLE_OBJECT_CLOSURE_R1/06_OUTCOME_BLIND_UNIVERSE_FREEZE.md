# FFIF007 — OUTCOME-BLIND UNIVERSE FREEZE

## 1. Freeze boundary

The `CLAIM_SCOPED_OBJECT_CLOSURE_RECEIPT` must be sealed before any actor responsible for U membership, object grain, weakness, or exclusion can inspect comparative outputs relevant to the closure claim.

Prior exploratory evidence may be used only if explicitly declared as discovery capital and the final comparison uses genuinely sealed evidence or a new version/holdout. It cannot be relabeled confirmatory.

## 2. Minimum frozen payload

- proposition/comparison claim hashes;
- CIM, CITC, support/uncertainty, and common-input refs/hashes;
- atomic required invariant/assertion bundle;
- discovery-source and generator versions;
- candidate inventory including rejected candidates;
- AOS identity tuple and hash per included member;
- object-family ancestry and method-variant policy;
- exact inclusion/exclusion dispositions and evidence;
- known-blocked classification;
- commitment vectors and weaker-relation plan;
- transform challenge plan;
- sufficiency and minimal-set search rules;
- nuisance/hostile/holdout plan;
- fresh challenger packet and audit status;
- freeze timestamp/ref, producer, and seal hash;
- limitations, staleness, and future-expansion rule.

## 3. Forbidden post-output mutations

Without a new comparison version it is forbidden to:

- add a rescuing or threatening object;
- drop an object that makes one member sufficient;
- narrow or widen family/specification boundaries;
- reclassify an algorithm as a separate object to inflate evidence;
- redefine weakness/dominance;
- change claim invariants, support, or CITC tolerances;
- change known-blocked status;
- add a post-hoc composite/fusion object;
- resolve an excluded candidate's authority differently.

## 4. Discovery after freeze

An obvious missing challenger discovered after freeze has one of two consequences:

1. if outputs have not been inspected and seal governance permits an auditable pre-output amendment, issue a new U version and reseal;
2. if relevant outputs have been inspected, the original comparison becomes `EXPLORATORY_OR_CLOSURE_FAILED`; create a new comparison key, U hash, execution/holdout plan, and CVCR.

The new candidate cannot retroactively rescue or defeat the old closure claim.

## 5. Method tuning after freeze

Method variants may be tuned only inside the preregistered AOS variant policy and training/design partition. Final-holdout leakage invalidates the AOS result. If tuning changes a constitutive identity coordinate, it is a new AOS and new U version.

## 6. Separation of roles

Preferred role separation:

- claim/materiality authority issues CIM/CITC;
- closure designer generates and freezes U;
- independent object producers execute members;
- comparison court emits CVCR;
- engineering consumer chooses among equally lawful sufficient sets.

Where personnel cannot be separated, immutable sequencing and sealed evidence packets are mandatory. Self-benefiting post-output membership adjudication is never cured by disclosure alone.

- `FROZEN_USING_DECLARED_EXPLORATORY_CAPITAL__NEW_HOLDOUT_REQUIRED`
- `INVALID__RESULT_GUIDED_MEMBERSHIP`
- `INVALID__RESULT_GUIDED_GRAIN_CHANGE`
- `SUPERSEDED_BY_NEW_U_VERSION`
