# FFIF007 — FINITE ADMISSIBLE-OBJECT PROBLEM

## 1. The quantifier defect

FFIF004 correctly rejected absolute nonreducibility. Its surviving bounded status still depended on an underdefined quantifier: “all weaker objects in a predeclared finite admissible universe.” A convenient universe can manufacture necessity as easily as a convenient metric can manufacture geometry.

The court therefore separates four claims:

1. `TESTED_ARM_RESULT`: what happened among objects actually run;
2. `KNOWN_UNIVERSE_RESULT`: what happened among a frozen finite project-known universe;
3. `CLAIM_COMPLETE_UNIVERSE_RESULT`: what happened after a claim-driven challenge and disposition process closed obvious lawful gaps;
4. `ALL_POSSIBLE_REPRESENTATIONS_RESULT`: unidentifiable and forbidden.

Only the third can support the strongest finite engineering statement. None reaches the fourth.

## 2. Proposition proved by closure

A passed closure receipt proves only:

> Before relevant comparative outputs, Rawlsing applied a replayable claim-to-candidate procedure, admitted or explicitly disposed every currently known lawful claim-relevant challenger revealed by that procedure, left no blocking known challenger unresolved, and froze the resulting finite specification universe.

Closure is therefore an audit proposition about the adequacy and integrity of a bounded search space. It is not a property of reality and not a discovery that no other representation exists.

## 3. Authority consumed

Closure consumes but does not mint:

- CIM proposition/comparison hashes and atomic assertion slots;
- CITC rows, hard-zero and bounded-loss rules;
- support/uncertainty contract;
- common semantic input and source-native ancestry;
- TTAR/PRA/measurement/role refs where constitutive;
- admitted representation contracts and object-family registry;
- project-known negative/design capital;
- transform and method-variant rules;
- outcome-blind freeze evidence.

## 4. What closure does not authorize

It cannot authorize:

- a new semantic equivalence, metric, edge, order, role, or transport map;
- claim identity or tolerance;
- respondent/exposure authority;
- a view's empirical validity;
- theory labels or structural ontology;
- exclusion merely because execution is inconvenient;
- an assertion that future objects cannot supersede the result.

## 5. Why a universal family list fails

`ATLAS, GEOMETRY, GRAPH, LATENT, MOTIF` is method-history, not claim completeness.

For `P(D|C)`, a direct conditional object may exhaust the required assertion interface. Geometry, graph, and latent mixtures are irrelevant unless the CIM requires their distinct invariants.

For `exact minority existence + local ordinal neighborhood`, the generator must search carriers of both slots and lawful lower-commitment projections. That may yield exact configurations, a direct existence ledger, a neighborhood order relation, or a claim-specific composite. Family prestige is irrelevant.

## 6. The finite compromise

The universe is finite because it is bounded by:

- one immutable comparison claim;
- a finite invariant/assertion-slot bundle;
- a finite current semantic input;
- a finite bank of currently known object families/specifications;
- finite commitment-lowering generators and exclusions;
- finite authority-valid instantiations;
- one sealed discovery deadline.

Parameter grids do not multiply the philosophical universe unless their variants change object identity. Unknown future methods are outside the quantifier, visibly.

## 7. Failure modes

Closure fails or abstains when:

- a known lawful challenger was omitted;
- the generator did not cover a constitutive invariant slot;
- a family label hides multiple specifications;
- an exclusion depends on results;
- a known owed blocker starves a challenger;
- object identity or weaker relations remain unresolved;
- the universe cannot be replayed from its hash;
- the fresh challenger audit finds an undisposed obvious candidate.

The failure result is not “nonreducibility disproved.” It is `UNIVERSE_CLOSURE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
