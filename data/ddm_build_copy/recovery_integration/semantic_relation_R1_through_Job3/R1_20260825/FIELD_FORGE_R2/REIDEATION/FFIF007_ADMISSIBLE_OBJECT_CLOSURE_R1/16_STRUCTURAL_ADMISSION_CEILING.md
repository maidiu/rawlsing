# FFIF007 — STRUCTURAL-ADMISSION CEILING

## 1. Lawful inputs

A later structural-admission court may consume:

- `VIEW_ROBUST_FOR_CLAIM`;
- `REPRESENTATION_DEPENDENT_FOR_CLAIM`;
- `KNOWN_LOSS_EXPLAINS_DIFFERENCE`;
- `SINGLE_OBJECT_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_U`;
- `NO_SINGLE_OBJECT_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_U`;
- all nondominated minimal sufficient sets and their invariant assignments;
- exact U/CIM/CITC/common-input hashes;
- support, uncertainty, hostile, and supersession states.

## 2. Ceiling sentence

The strongest transferable empirical statement is:

> Under the named claim, evidence, tolerance contract, and finite closed object universe, the complete empirical invariant bundle requires the reported minimal multi-object set(s); this requirement is representation- and universe-scoped and remains supersedable.

## 3. Forbidden promotions

No structural court may turn that statement into:

- `SOCIAL_REALITY_IS_ONTOLOGICALLY_PLURAL`;
- `DIALECTICAL_CONTRADICTION_PROVED`;
- `FIELD_OR_CLASS_REQUIRES_MULTIPLE_ONTOLOGIES`;
- `METHOD_DISAGREEMENT_CONFIRMS_THEORY`;
- `NO_FUTURE_REPRESENTATION_CAN_UNIFY_THE_RESULT`;
- `MULTIPLE_MINIMAL_SETS_ARE_MULTIPLE_SOCIAL_WORLDS`.

Minimal multi-object sufficiency is an architecture/evidence result. It is not an ontological theorem.

## 4. Theory boundary

Theory may:

- nominate an empirical challenger before freeze;
- propose a downstream historical/structural question;
- explain why an invariant might matter, subject to CIM/CITC authority.

Theory may not:

- earn a method seat;
- declare U complete;
- convert a representation family into a social entity;
- erase representation dependence through semantic storytelling;
- treat non-single-object sufficiency as validation of a theorist.

## 5. Required derivative provenance

Any downstream sentence consuming a within-U result must carry or resolve:

- comparison claim key;
- U hash and closure status;
- exact minimal set/frontier;
- invariants assigned to each member;
- forbidden promotions;
- known limitations and supersession.

Dropping U from the derivative proposition changes a bounded result into an unlawful absolute claim.

## 6. Supersession

If a later expanded U yields a single sufficient object, downstream structural claims dependent on multi-object necessity become stale and must be re-adjudicated. Historical statements about the earlier scoped result remain true if the earlier closure was procedurally valid.
