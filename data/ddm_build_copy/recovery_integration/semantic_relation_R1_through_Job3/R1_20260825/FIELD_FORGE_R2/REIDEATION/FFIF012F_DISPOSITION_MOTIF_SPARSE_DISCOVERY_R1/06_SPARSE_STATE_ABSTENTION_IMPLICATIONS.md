# FFIF012F — Sparse-state and abstention implications

## Holding

Sparse exact states are not errors to be repaired. On the fixed 19,210-respondent eight-disposition surface, `1,856` exact full profiles are observed, with support from `1` to `1,169`. Exactly `947` profiles have support `1`, `252` have support `2`, and `127` have support `3`.

Those are exact realized-sample descriptive facts. They do **not** authorize a minimum-support threshold, pooling, smoothing, category fusion, or deletion.

The equality between `947` singleton profiles and the previously banked `947` disposition respondents lacking a same-profile peer is mechanically intelligible: every singleton exact profile has one respondent and therefore no identical-profile second respondent. This does not make a metric relation necessary; it merely marks where same-profile equivalence stops answering a nearest-distinct-profile question.

## Special-state preservation

The complete native surface retains special/non-substantive states. Of the 1,856 exact profiles, 1,354 contain at least one state outside the substantive Q76 `{1,2,3}` / Q86 `{0,1}` sets, covering 3,490 respondents. The all-substantive subdomain contains 15,720 respondents and 502 observed exact profiles.

The all-substantive subdomain is a diagnostic slice, **not** a replacement risk set. Special-state profiles remain in the complete discovery roster.

## Abstention rule

For unconditional exact existence/support, every observed profile has positive support by definition; there is no zero-denominator abstention.

For a later conditional claim over a discovered profile, each required conditional denominator must be checked independently. `n=0` yields typed abstention; it does not justify deleting the discovered profile or backing off to a coarser motif.

## Stability firewall

`EXACT_PROFILE_EXISTS_WITH_SUPPORT_n` is separate from persistence under respondent deletion/resampling, population prevalence, statistical significance, material importance, recurrence across contexts, and repertoire status. No one of those stronger statuses is issued here.
