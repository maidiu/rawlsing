# FFIF013 — PREOUTPUT FREEZE + AUTHORITY PREREQUISITES R1

Status: `DESIGN_ONLY__CENTRAL_JOIN_REQUIRED_BEFORE_EXECUTION`

## 1. Mandatory preoutput freeze

Before any target predicate × follow-up output is inspected, the central successor must freeze:

### FZ01 — exact discovery surface

- exact `S` identity/version;
- source/wave/context;
- empirical unit;
- exact risk-set/coexposure rule;
- native-state identities and special-state law;
- immutable producer ancestry.

### FZ02 — exact discovery predicate roster

- deterministic discovery procedure/ref;
- exact predicate definitions or deterministic roster generator;
- complete admitted roster hash;
- exact original support/status for every admitted predicate;
- deterministic subset rule if bounded execution does not use the whole roster.

No subset rule may depend on the observed relation to `X`.

### FZ03 — exact follow-up operand/context

- `X` or finite `F_X` identity/domain;
- proof it is outside the discovery surface;
- currentness and same-unit/queryability authority;
- outcome-blind selection receipt showing selection used identity, exposure/domain existence, authority, and feasibility only.

### FZ04 — exact follow-up query relation

- normalized proposition/assertion keys;
- relation/query spec;
- empirical-unit and risk-set law;
- conditioning/baseline relation;
- support/denominator/status law;
- estimand/weight target where applicable;
- exact value/status domain.

### FZ05 — exact challenger universe

- W0–W5 identities and any lawfully triggered W6;
- AOS split rules;
- method-variant policy;
- blocked/excluded challenger accounting;
- weaker/dominance relation;
- fresh adversarial review receipt;
- finite universe hash.

### FZ06 — exact replay oracle

For each challenger:

- immutable source/materialization refs;
- allowed query-time producer dependencies;
- deterministic reconstruction/query recipe;
- canonical normalization/status logic;
- exact equality/replay check;
- independent reconstruction requirement.

### FZ07 — result firewall

Freeze a receipt that prohibits inspection or use of:

- predicate × `X` distributions;
- relation direction/effect/contrast;
- reversal/persistence;
- subgroup ranking;
- association strength;
- predictive value;
- target-conditioned challenger mutation;
- target-conditioned candidate `X` replacement.

## 2. Authority prerequisites by layer

### A01 — producer-owned empirical identity

Authority31/current producer law supplies the governing architecture theorem: authoritative producer objects remain directly dereferenceable; physical copying does not re-own their semantics; exact information-equivalent capability may satisfy same-unit query needs.

FFIF013 consumes this law but does not mint producer authority.

### A02 — empirical-unit / risk-set / exposure

Required wherever respondent/context claims are made. Response presence may not be substituted for exposure, eligibility, or same-unit incidence.

If current exposure/queryability is unresolved, execution must fail closed.

### A03 — discovery authority and predicate identity

The discovered predicate roster must come from a lawful outcome-blind discovery procedure/receipt. FFIF013 does not recreate or reinterpret discovery results.

### A04 — external follow-up operand identity

`X` must have exact current source-native/context identity and lawful same-unit relation to the query risk set. If a transformed or role-bearing meaning is needed, consume prior TTAR/PRA; do not mint it here.

### A05 — follow-up query/relation authority

The exact relation and estimand must be declared. A join key is not by itself semantic permission to make the query.

### A06 — support/status/negative authority

Zero, valid empty, zero-denominator abstention, unsupported, blocked, out-of-scope, and undefined-for-object states must be governed distinctly.

### A07 — replay/provenance/dereference permission

A W1 PASS requires exact lawful access to immutable/reconstructible producer facts under the frozen query. A cache silently substituted for unavailable producer re-query invalidates W1 rather than proving it.

## 3. Authorities not required by default

For the neutral exact linkage proposition, do not require unless the frozen candidate specifically invokes them:

- structural empirical admission;
- field/hegemony/pressure/motif/homology authority;
- geometry/metric/order authority;
- causal/mechanism authority;
- V3/person authority;
- general materiality authority.

PRA is unnecessary if `X` is consumed only as an exact context/native attribute with no analytic-position role. TTAR is unnecessary if source-native states are used unchanged.

## 4. CITC gate

No substantive CITC is required when the entire court is exact discrete identity/support/status/replay with no tolerated approximation.

If the central join changes the claim to permit approximation, material difference, statistical stability, or substantive thresholding, execution must stop until competent CITC/support/uncertainty authority is frozen.

## 5. Fail-closed states

The central successor must use typed blockers such as:

- `BLOCKED__DISCOVERY_PREDICATE_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`;
- `BLOCKED__FOLLOWUP_OPERAND_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`;
- `BLOCKED__SAME_UNIT_OR_EXPOSURE_UNRESOLVED`;
- `BLOCKED__FOLLOWUP_QUERY_AUTHORITY_UNRESOLVED`;
- `BLOCKED__PRODUCER_DEREFERENCE_OR_REPLAY_UNAVAILABLE`;
- `CLOSURE_ABSTAIN__KNOWN_BLOCKED_CHALLENGER`;
- `INVALID__RESULT_FIREWALL_BREACH`;
- `INVALID__COMMON_INPUT_OR_RISK_SET_DIVERGENCE`.

A blocker is a valid campaign result. It may not be relabeled as W1 failure, evidence for persistence, or permission to choose a more convenient candidate.
