## 1. Core proposition

The architecture-changing proposition is not “which persisted representation is best?” It is:

`DISCOVERY_TO_FOLLOWUP_LINKAGE_MINIMUM@1`

> Given one frozen lawful same-unit discovery surface `S`, one outcome-blind exact discovery procedure producing a frozen predicate roster `P`, and one preregistered exact follow-up query relation `Q` over respondent/context information `X` outside `S`, what is the weakest lawful information/replay capability that can reproduce the complete normalized assertion bundle without changing empirical-unit identity, risk set, native-state semantics, query relation, negative states, or producer ancestry?

## 3. Terminal-relevant quantifier

- `Q1_ONE_PREREGISTERED_FOLLOWUP`: one frozen `X/Q` over the frozen predicate roster.
- `QF_FINITE_PREREGISTERED_FOLLOWUP_FAMILY`: a finite frozen family `{X_i,Q_i}` declared before outputs.
- `QINF_ARBITRARY_FUTURE_FOLLOWUP`: any unregistered future operand/query.
