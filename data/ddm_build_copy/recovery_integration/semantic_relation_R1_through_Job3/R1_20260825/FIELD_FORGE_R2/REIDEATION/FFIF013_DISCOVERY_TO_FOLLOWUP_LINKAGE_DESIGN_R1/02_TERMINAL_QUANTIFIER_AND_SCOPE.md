# FFIF013 — TERMINAL-RELEVANT QUANTIFIER + SCOPE R1

Status: `DESIGN_ONLY__ASYMMETRIC_FALSIFICATION_LOGIC_FROZEN`

## 1. Three noninterchangeable quantifiers

### Q1 — one preregistered follow-up

`FOR ALL p IN P, EVALUATE ONE FROZEN Q(p,X)`.

This is the smallest executable discriminator and is the quantifier expected from the independent current-carrier preflight unless the central join finds it insufficient.

### QF — finite preregistered follow-up family

`FOR ALL p IN P, FOR ALL q IN F, EVALUATE q(p)` where finite `F` is frozen before target outputs.

This can support a stronger current no-starvation statement when `F` is shown to cover every currently owed downstream follow-up interface relevant to the architectural proposition.

### QINF — arbitrary future follow-up

`FOR ALL PRESENT OR FUTURE UNREGISTERED q`.

This quantifier is not finitely identifiable. It cannot be established by one canary, one survey family, one carrier, or one finite closure receipt.

## 2. Which quantifier is terminal-relevant now

The central frontier asks whether FIELD FORGE must carry local respondent membership as a **universal minimum grammar requirement**.

That universal-necessity proposition has the form:

`U_LOCAL_MEMBERSHIP_NECESSITY = FOR EVERY LAWFUL DISCOVERY_TO_FOLLOWUP QUERY, FIELD_FORGE_LOCAL_RESPONDENT_MEMBERSHIP IS REQUIRED.`

A single lawful counterexample is sufficient to falsify it:

`EXISTS Q1 SUCH THAT W1_DIRECT_UPSTREAM_REQUERY PRESERVES AS01_AS08 EXACTLY -> U_LOCAL_MEMBERSHIP_NECESSITY = FALSE`.

Therefore `Q1` is terminal-relevant as a falsifier of universal local-membership necessity.

The reverse inference is invalid:

`ONE Q1 WHERE W1 FAILS AND W4/W5 PASSES` does **not** imply `U_LOCAL_MEMBERSHIP_NECESSITY = TRUE`.

It proves, at most, a claim-scoped linkage requirement for that exact frozen query. A universal positive requirement would need a separately closed finite current no-starvation family or another valid architectural theorem establishing that the failed capability is constitutive for every relevant downstream interface.

## 3. Why arbitrary future capability is not the stopping criterion

Authority31 conserves exact information recoverability and direct producer dereference, not a prophecy that every future claim is already representable by one local object.

Future claims remain governed by their own producer availability, semantic authority, risk set, query relation, and replay law. If a future query needs information not currently available/recoverable, that is a new typed blocker or successor obligation, not evidence that FIELD FORGE should preemptively persist every respondent fact.

Accordingly:

`NO_ARBITRARY_FUTURE_QUANTIFIER_LAUNDERING`.

A W1 PASS licenses:

`LOCAL_MEMBERSHIP_NOT_UNIVERSALLY_NECESSARY_WHEN_EXACT_PRODUCER_REQUERY_IS_LAWFUL`.

It does not license:

`DIRECT_REQUERY_SUFFICIENT_FOR_ALL_FUTURE_QUERIES`.

## 4. Claim-relative positive requirement

If every lawful weaker challenger is invalid, undefined, or exactly insufficient while W4 passes, the exact claim may require:

`RESPONDENT_MEMBERSHIP_OR_INFORMATION_EQUIVALENT_LINKAGE@<claim hash>`.

If only W5 passes, the exact claim may require:

`FULL_LOSSLESS_SAME_UNIT_INFORMATION_CAPABILITY@<claim hash>`.

The architecture-changing force of that result depends on whether the central successor can show that the claim is itself a mandatory current downstream no-starvation witness. FFIF013 does not make that judgment.

## 5. Support-only and follow-up-specific summaries

A W3 follow-up-specific summary may be minimal for `Q1` or a declared `QF` even if it cannot answer any other future query. That is lawful.

It is forbidden to promote:

`W3_SUFFICIENT_FOR_Q1`

to

`W3_SUFFICIENT_FOR_QINF`.

Likewise, the court may not reject W3 merely because it lacks arbitrary future flexibility when the frozen claim requires only Q1/QF.

## 6. Terminal stopping implication

After empirical execution, the central FIELD FORGE court should rerun SC-01..SC-10.

- A clean W1 counterexample can close the universal-local-membership hypothesis negatively while leaving future claims successor-governed.
- A claim-scoped W4/W5 necessity result may expose a new named no-starvation requirement and therefore keep terminal synthesis open.
- A typed authority/observability blocker keeps the frontier open at that exact grain rather than fabricating closure.

No result from FFIF013 alone declares terminal synthesis readiness.
