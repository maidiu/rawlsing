# FFIF013 — CITC, PASS/FAIL, AND NONGENERALIZATION CEILING R1

Status: `DESIGN_ONLY__EXACT_INFORMATION_REPLAY_COURT`

## 1. Does this experiment require materiality?

No, if the target remains exactly as designed.

The proposition is about exact information availability, exact query capability, exact support/status preservation, and exact replay. The constitutive comparison is binary at the semantic interface:

- exact same identity or not;
- exact same risk set or not;
- exact same predicate/query relation or not;
- exact same support/value/status or not;
- replayable from lawful frozen evidence or not;
- defined for the object or not.

There is no “small enough” discrepancy, no effect-size threshold, no prediction score, and no compensatory aggregate.

Therefore:

`MATERIALITY_JUDGMENT = NOT_REQUIRED`

and

`APPROXIMATE_LOSS_BUDGET = NOT_ALLOWED_BY_DEFAULT`.

## 2. Relationship to FFIF006/CITC doctrine

FFIF006 correctly requires competent CITC authority whenever a comparison judges tolerated empirical loss/material difference.

This target avoids that jurisdiction by making exactness constitutive of the proposition itself.

FFIF013 does not mint a CITC. If existing CVCR/COCR machinery requires a comparison-contract field syntactically, the central successor must either:

1. bind an already-authorized exact hard-zero/no-approximation contract whose authority derives from the exact proposition; or
2. use an explicit `CITC_NOT_APPLICABLE__EXACT_IDENTITY_REPLAY_COURT` state if the schema permits it.

It may not invent a numerical tolerance merely to satisfy infrastructure.

## 3. When CITC would become mandatory

Stop and obtain competent pre-output CITC/support/uncertainty authority if the claim is changed to include:

- approximate support equality;
- sampling/generalization uncertainty;
- stability/resampling/holdout performance;
- material effect size;
- prediction quality;
- compression loss;
- probabilistic reconstruction;
- lossy encoding;
- “close enough” profile or state equivalence;
- performance/latency tradeoff as a truth criterion.

Engineering preferences may be compared only after semantic sufficiency and do not themselves require empirical materiality authority unless promoted into a claim.

## 4. PASS semantics

A challenger receives `PASS__CLAIM_RELATIVE_EXACT_SUFFICIENCY` only when:

1. common semantic input/risk set is valid;
2. all its required authority dependencies are valid;
3. every AS01–AS08 constitutive slot is represented at exact frozen identity/value/status;
4. no negative-state collapse occurs;
5. its replay contract succeeds independently;
6. no result-firewall or closure violation occurs.

PASS is noncompensatory.

## 5. FAIL/abstain semantics

Do not emit one undifferentiated FAIL.

Use typed states:

- `FAIL__EXACT_ASSERTION_MISMATCH` — valid comparison, wrong exact output.
- `UNDEFINED_FOR_OBJECT` — challenger does not expose the required assertion/replay interface.
- `BLOCKED__AUTHORITY_OR_OBSERVABILITY` — required lawful input/query/replay authority unavailable.
- `REPLAY_OR_INTEGRITY_FAILURE` — result exists but cannot be independently regenerated under frozen ancestry.
- `INVALID__COMMON_INPUT_DIVERGENCE` — challenger changed claim/risk set/semantics.
- `INVALID__OUTCOME_FIREWALL_OR_CLOSURE_BREACH` — preoutput design contaminated.
- `CLOSURE_ABSTAIN__KNOWN_BLOCKED_CHALLENGER` — finite necessity/minimality cannot be adjudicated fairly.

Only the first is an empirical mismatch. The others are capability, authority, integrity, or validity statuses.

## 6. What a W1 PASS proves

Exactly:

> For the frozen claim and follow-up query, exact discovered-predicate identity plus lawful direct producer-owned same-unit re-query preserves and independently replays the complete frozen assertion bundle without a FIELD-FORGE-local respondent-membership carrier.

Additionally, because the challenged architecture proposition is universal necessity of local membership, this one lawful counterexample falsifies that universal necessity proposition.

It does not prove W1 sufficiency for unregistered future claims.

## 7. What a W1 failure does not prove

A W1 mismatch/undefined/blocker does not automatically prove W4 or W5 necessary.

Necessity requires:

- every weaker/incomparable lawful challenger accounted for under a frozen finite closure universe;
- no known owed challenger blocked;
- exact common-input validity;
- the stronger candidate itself passing;
- no post-output fusion or query mutation.

Even then the result remains claim-scoped within `U@hash`.
