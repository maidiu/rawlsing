# FFIF003 — UNIVERSAL-SPACE HOSTILE AUDIT

## 1. Target

The danger is not “using standardization” in the abstract. The danger is allowing a transformation that **creates common coordinates** to serve as evidence that the underlying local structures were already comparable in those coordinates.

Every method below can have legitimate uses. None may become the universal court of cross-context recurrence without a separate proof that its constitutive equivalences are authorized.

## 2. Global z-scores

### Genuine utility
- place variables on a common numerical reporting scale;
- stabilize optimization;
- compare deviations relative to a declared pooled reference distribution.

### Manufactured comparability
A z-score assumes the pooled mean/variance is a meaningful denominator. Different local distributions, bounded/ordinal source semantics, and unequal risk sets can be recoded into apparently commensurable “standard deviations.”

### Lawful ceiling
Descriptive transformation only unless metric portability and denominator authority are separately established. Prefer local standardization for local models when the claim is local; do not infer structural equivalence from equal z-values.

## 3. Global percentile maps

### Genuine utility
Compare rank in one explicitly governed pooled population.

### Manufactured comparability
If contexts have different target populations, sample designs, support, or construct semantics, pooling creates the reference population that makes equal percentiles possible. Equal percentile is then partly an artifact of the imposed denominator.

### Lawful ceiling
Within-context rank/quantile transport can be lawful with frozen local denominators. Global percentile identity is not the default.

## 4. Common MCA axes

### Genuine utility
A deliberately common categorical embedding can reveal whether a specified pooled representation has stable predictive/descriptive structure.

### Manufactured comparability
MCA axes depend on active categories, coding, marginal frequencies, block balance, weighting, and pooled composition. The position of context A is therefore partly defined by context B. If common axes are then cited as evidence of recurrent local geometry, the proof is circular.

### Lawful ceiling
Use as a joint model with explicit representation dependence, not as a witness that independently frozen local geometries are homologous or even aligned.

## 5. Pooled factor models / measurement models

### Genuine utility
Test an explicit invariance model; compress correlated batteries; predict held-out responses under a declared common factor structure.

### Manufactured comparability
Common loadings, intercept constraints, factor orientation, residual assumptions, and item selection can force shared latent coordinates. Fit may reflect model regularization or dominant contexts rather than local structural recurrence.

### Lawful ceiling
A common factor model can itself be a falsifiable hypothesis. Its success does not retroactively prove measurement portability or universal latent structure. Invariance constraints must be tested, not assumed.

## 6. Universal latent classes

### Genuine utility
Ask whether one mixture vocabulary compresses multiple contexts predictively.

### Manufactured comparability
A fixed class count and shared class definitions partition contexts using one ontology. Local contradictory/minority organizations may be merged because they do not earn a global class.

### Lawful ceiling
Model-compression result only. Local class/profile solutions must remain inspectable, and class-label equality cannot establish social-role equality.

## 7. Universal graph embeddings

### Genuine utility
Share information for prediction/link tasks when node/edge semantics are already governed.

### Manufactured comparability
Joint training can align nodes because of shared parameters, anchor choices, negative sampling, graph density, or instrument topology. The embedding can make unrelated local neighborhoods look close by construction.

### Lawful ceiling
No structural recurrence claim unless independently defined local topology is compared through an external, typed correspondence and survives embedding perturbation.

## 8. Canonical demographic slots

### Genuine utility
Index candidate variables and make project inventory navigable.

### Manufactured comparability
Labels such as `RACE`, `ETHNICITY`, `RELIGION`, `CLASS`, `EMPLOYMENT`, `EDUCATION` can imply that source variables occupying the slot share semantic or social-position roles. FFIF001's Asian Barometer employment example is a direct warning: an occupation proxy and a later cleaner employment-status variable do not become equivalent because both can be filed under `EMPLOYMENT`.

### Lawful ceiling
Discovery/index role only until each cross-context correspondence is separately authorized.

## 9. Globally harmonized category vocabularies

### Genuine utility
Create a reporting vocabulary where exact provenance and loss are preserved and the harmonization question itself is governed.

### Manufactured comparability
Many-to-one recodes can erase locally meaningful distinctions, change minority/majority topology, alter ordering, and delete category combinations. Once the source-native categories disappear, the harm is not auditable.

### Lawful ceiling
Never replace source-native identity. Harmonized categories may exist as reversible analytic views with explicit loss receipts, not as current truth by default.

## 10. Strongest rejected architecture

`POOLED_COMMON_LATENT_WORLD_SPACE`

A combined architecture of globally harmonized categories + pooled MCA/factor/embedding coordinates is rejected as a proof system for recurrence because it performs the very alignment it later claims to discover.

## 11. Hostile decision rule

A universal-space method fails FFIF003 whenever:

1. local objects cannot be reconstructed without the other contexts;
2. removing/adding a context materially changes the alleged local structure;
3. the common encoding imports unproven category/metric/role equivalence;
4. recurrence vanishes when independently frozen locals are compared;
5. negative-control mappings perform similarly;
6. representation dependencies are hidden behind one common score/axis/class label.

The correct remedy is not “never pool.” It is: **do not let pooling certify the equivalence that made pooling possible.**
