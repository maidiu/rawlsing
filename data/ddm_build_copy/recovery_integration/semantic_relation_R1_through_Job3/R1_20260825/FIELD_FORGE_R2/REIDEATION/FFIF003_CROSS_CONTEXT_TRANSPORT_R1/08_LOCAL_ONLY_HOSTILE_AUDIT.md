# FFIF003 — LOCAL-ONLY HOSTILE AUDIT

## 1. Target

Rejecting a universal world-space does not make local analysis self-validating. Unconstrained localism can turn cross-context comparison into theory-by-eyeballing.

The transport architecture must therefore constrain the researcher more strongly than “construct good local analyses and compare what seems analogous.”

## 2. Failure modes

### A. Incomparable idiosyncratic labels

Each local analyst invents labels such as “secure traditionalists,” “precarious cosmopolitans,” or “institutional outsiders.” The names then do the comparison work.

**Failure:** interpretive labels have replaced empirical correspondence.

**Control:** transport primitive must be a declared invariant over replayable local objects, not a prose label.

### B. Plot resemblance

Two local geometries have visually similar clusters/oppositions.

**Failure:** rotations, axis choices, scaling, sparsity, and plotting conventions create many possible resemblances.

**Control:** freeze coordinate-independent preservation predicates or preregister the exact geometry-alignment claim and perturbation tests.

### C. Cherry-picked motif similarity

A researcher searches many possible local motifs and reports the pair that matches.

**Failure:** effective hypothesis space is unrecorded; recurrence probability under arbitrary matching is unknown.

**Control:** preregister motif family, candidate correspondence space, multiplicity rule, and negative-control motifs.

### D. Researcher-decided correspondence after outcomes

The mapping between local roles/categories is selected because it yields the desired recurrence.

**Failure:** the map has learned the answer.

**Control:** mapping authority and selection rule must be outcome-independent or the claim becomes exploratory and cannot close recurrence.

### E. Theory labels smuggled in as semantics

“Religious minority,” “working class,” “modernizing elite,” or “precarious household” is used to bridge sources.

**Failure:** a theoretical interpretation is functioning as category/position-role equivalence.

**Control:** preserve local source-native identity; require separate role correspondence; state forbidden theoretical promotion.

### F. Representation-shopping

Atlas, geometry, graph, latent class, and conditional views disagree; the researcher reports the representation that recurs.

**Failure:** representation choice becomes an unacknowledged multiple-testing dimension.

**Control:** freeze claim-relevant view family or report typed representation dependence; no majority vote and no winner by narrative appeal.

### G. Estimand substitution

Local analyses describe different populations/risk sets but are compared as though the estimand were one object.

**Failure:** “structural difference” may be composition/exposure difference, or “recurrence” may rely on selective overlap.

**Control:** local estimands and risk sets are immutable endpoint metadata; incompatibility can force `NO_TRANSPORT`.

### H. Null blindness

A signature is treated as meaningful because it looks complex.

**Failure:** no evidence that unrelated blocks or randomized admissible maps would not generate the same signature.

**Control:** map-permutation, unrelated-block, local-association, representation-perturbation, and holdout nulls.

## 3. Strongest local-only failure

`UNREGISTERED_ANALOGICAL_CORRESPONDENCE`

This is stronger than mere cherry-picking because it can survive impeccable local analysis. The local objects may each be valid. The failure occurs in the ungoverned bridge between them.

A correspondence is not lawful merely because:

- both local scholars would use the same English gloss;
- two plots look similar;
- a theoretical tradition predicts an analogy;
- a motif can be narrated in the same sentence;
- a canonical slot name exists.

## 4. Minimum anti-localism contract

Every cross-context claim needs:

1. frozen local object refs/hashes;
2. invariant definition;
3. endpoint semantic/role authority;
4. admissible map family;
5. map-selection rule;
6. preservation predicate;
7. local risk sets and estimands;
8. alternative maps/negative controls;
9. representation dependencies;
10. forbidden inference and claim ceiling.

Without these, Rawlsing should preserve the local findings but emit `NO_TRANSPORT_ESTABLISHED`.

## 5. The dialectic resolved operationally

Universal space centralizes discretion in the common model. Local-only comparison decentralizes discretion into unrecorded researcher analogy.

The lawful intermediate architecture does neither. It makes **the bridge itself an auditable empirical object**.
