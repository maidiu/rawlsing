# FFIF003 — CROSS-CONTEXT TRANSPORT PROBLEM

## 1. The false binary

The architecture must reject two mutually reinforcing errors:

1. `COMMON_WORLD_SPACE_OR_NOTHING`: contexts are comparable only after they occupy one standardized metric, graph, factor space, latent class vocabulary, or harmonized category system.
2. `LOCAL_SOVEREIGNTY_MEANS_INCOMPARABILITY`: any cross-context comparison is illicit unless literal measurements/categories are identical.

Both errors confuse **what is compared** with **how much equivalence the comparison asserts**.

A lawful middle architecture is possible only if comparison is decomposed into typed claims and if every local object exists independently before the cross-context map is fitted or adjudicated.

## 2. Seven layers that may not bleed together

| layer | question | authority ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| `MEASUREMENT_IDENTITY` | Is this the same governed measurement implementation/meaning? | source and measurement identity authority |
| `MEASUREMENT_PORTABILITY` | May empirical quantities from the measurements be transported/compared for the stated claim? | separate portability court; not closed here |
| `CATEGORY_EQUIVALENCE` | Are response/category states substantively equivalent? | explicit category correspondence authority |
| `POSITION_ROLE_EQUIVALENCE` | Does a variable/state occupy the same social/analytic role? | much stronger than semantic similarity; not presumed |
| `REPRESENTATIONAL_CORRESPONDENCE` | Can one local analytical object be mapped to another while preserving a named invariant? | claim-scoped transport contract |
| `STRUCTURAL_RECURRENCE` | Does the named invariant survive the authorized map and falsification regime? | empirical transport result only |
| `THEORETICAL_INTERPRETATION` | Does recurrence instantiate homology, field conversion, class reproduction, etc.? | downstream theory; never emitted automatically |

A positive ruling at a lower layer does not automatically license the next layer.

## 3. What recurrence can mean without common coordinates

The phrase “same structural pattern” is invalid until reduced to a preservation predicate. Candidate predicates include:

- same exact governed semantic relation;
- same local order relation;
- same within-context rank relation;
- same adjacency/connectivity/opposition relation;
- same direction or bounded form of conditional redistribution;
- same typed relational motif;
- same finite signature of several independently established invariants.

These are not equivalent. They differ in semantic burden, map freedom, susceptibility to representation choice, and inferential ceiling.

## 4. Example: education and institutional trust

The sentence “higher education is associated with lower institutional trust in both contexts” does not logically require identical education categories, identical trust scales, shared axes, or an identical social meaning for education.

But the sentence is not automatically lawful either. A bounded direction-of-association transport would still require, at minimum:

- local authority that the education states possess a relevant order in each context;
- local authority that the trust destinations answer sufficiently corresponding claim roles;
- independently frozen local risk sets and estimands;
- an association summary whose direction is invariant to the admitted transformations;
- a correspondence fixed without using the observed cross-context agreement to choose it;
- null/negative-control evidence showing that the same recurrence is not easy to obtain under arbitrary admissible maps.

If one context measures household education, another respondent education, one trust battery is routed only to a subgroup, or the sign changes under a lawful scale/link representation, the proposed recurrence may fail even when the prose labels sound equivalent.

## 5. Local-first proof burden

For contexts `a` and `b`:

1. construct `R_a = f_a(O_a; A_a)` and `R_b = f_b(O_b; A_b)` under their own source/semantic/representation authorities;
2. freeze the local objects and extract named local invariants `I_a` and `I_b`;
3. register a correspondence `phi` from an explicit admissible map family `H` using authority independent of the target recurrence result;
4. define a preservation predicate `P(I_a, phi(I_b))` and decision rule;
5. test nulls, alternative maps, and representation perturbations;
6. emit only the narrowest transport claim that survives.

A joint model `F(O_a,O_b)` may be used descriptively or predictively for an explicitly different claim, but its induced alignment cannot prove that independently existing local structure recurs if the model itself created the aligned structures.

## 6. Recurrence statuses

### `GLOBAL_RECURRENCE`
The same declared invariant is preserved over the full admitted local estimands/risk sets and remains stable under predeclared configuration decomposition.

### `CONDITIONAL_RECURRENCE`
The invariant recurs only under a predeclared conditioning relation whose local realizations have an authorized correspondence.

### `CONFIGURATION_SPECIFIC_RECURRENCE`
A recurrence is confined to one or more mapped local configurations. It must not be generalized to the full context.

### `REVERSAL`
Under an otherwise admissible correspondence, the relevant relation changes direction/opposition/branch behavior. Reversal is a positive structural finding against global recurrence, not a nuisance to average away.

### `PARTIAL_RECURRENCE`
Only a strict subset of a declared signature or mapped domain is preserved.

### `NO_TRANSPORT`
No admissible correspondence with sufficient authority and discriminative falsification support survives. This is a first-class result.

## 7. Claim ceiling

Cross-context transport answers: **what relation survives what authorized transformation?**

It does not, by itself, answer:

- whether measurements are portable;
- whether categories are equivalent;
- whether social positions are equivalent;
- whether causal mechanisms are shared;
- whether a universal field/class/capital structure exists;
- whether the same relation persists through time.

The architecture is therefore neither universal harmonization nor contextual relativism. It is a typed proof system for bounded recurrence claims.
