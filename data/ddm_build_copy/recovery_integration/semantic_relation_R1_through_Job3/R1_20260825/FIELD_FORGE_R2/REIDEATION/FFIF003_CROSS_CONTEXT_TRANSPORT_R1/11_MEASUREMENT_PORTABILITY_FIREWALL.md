# FFIF003 — MEASUREMENT PORTABILITY FIREWALL

## 1. Direct answer

**Yes: Rawlsing can in principle transport some structural invariants without claiming full measurement portability.**

But only because the transported claim can be strictly weaker than a metric/category/estimand equivalence claim. This court does not establish that any particular current measurement pair is portable.

`STRUCTURAL_INVARIANT_TRANSPORT != MEASUREMENT_PORTABILITY`.

## 2. Positive architectural examples

These are proof-of-architecture examples, not empirical findings.

### A. Ordered education ladders without cardinal equivalence

Context A may encode education through credentials; context B through differently grouped levels. If each local source independently authorizes a “more education” order and the transport claim asks only whether a named local conditional relation is monotone in that order, an `ORDER_PRESERVING_TRANSPORT` may be possible.

It does not imply:

- equal years between categories;
- equal credential value;
- same labor-market/class meaning;
- same distribution;
- one-unit effect comparability.

### B. Local rank without common units

Two locally measured economic-resource variables may admit within-context ranks even when currency/scale or category semantics differ. A bounded rank/concentration claim can compare relative ordering under frozen local denominators.

It does not imply a common income metric or that the same percentile occupies the same social position.

### C. Topological motif without common coordinates

Two independently constructed local measurement/structural views may each contain a typed branching relation. A partial role map can test whether adjacency/branching is preserved without embedding either graph in the other's coordinate system.

It does not imply node/category identity, metric distance, or homology.

### D. Conditional direction without identical destination scales

If two local outcome measures have separately authorized ordered semantics and the claim uses a contrast invariant under the allowed monotone transforms, a direction-of-redistribution claim may be comparable even though score units differ.

It does not imply equal effect magnitude or scale portability.

## 3. Negative examples — where portability/equivalence is doing indispensable work

### A. One-unit slope comparison

“The effect is -0.4 units in both contexts” requires meaningful unit comparability (plus much more). Order transport cannot license it.

### B. Mean difference on pooled z-scores

Equal z-score differences do not prove equal substantive differences when the pooled mean/variance constructs the common scale.

### C. Common factor score

Comparing latent factor scores as one construct requires a measurement/invariance argument. A structural signature cannot substitute for that authority.

### D. Canonical category claim

“Secondary education is the same social position in both contexts” is a category/position-role equivalence claim, not order transport.

### E. Global percentile claim across different targets

If ranks are computed against different target populations or sampling/weighting frames, calling the 80th percentile the “same position” requires additional target/role authority. If computed against one pooled target, the pooled target itself needs justification.

## 4. Firewall rules

1. Every transport object carries `measurement_portability_status_ref`.
2. Absence of portability authority is represented as `NOT_ADJUDICATED` / `UNAVAILABLE`, never inferred from successful structural recurrence.
3. No transport claim may report cardinal effect equivalence unless separate measurement/estimand authority permits it.
4. Exact semantic transport is not sufficient by itself for empirical portability; local risk sets, sampling frames, weights, and estimands can still differ materially.
5. A failed portability claim does not automatically destroy every weaker invariant transport; the surviving weaker claim must be independently proved.
6. A successful weak transport never upgrades portability.

## 5. Shared ancestry firewall

Related instruments may provide stronger evidence for semantic correspondence than unrelated instruments. But:

`SHARED_ANCESTRY -> EVIDENCE_FOR_ANCESTRY`

not:

`SHARED_ANCESTRY -> PORTABILITY`

and certainly not:

`SHARED_ANCESTRY -> HOMOLOGY`.

## 6. Current authority disposition

Measurement portability remains a separate current authority problem. FFIF003 closes no portability obligation, mints no harmonized category system, and declares no current survey pair empirically portable.
