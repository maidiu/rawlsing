# FFIF003 — HOMOLOGY AND ARTICULATION BOUNDARIES

## 1. Homology firewall

The following entailments are forbidden:

`SHARED_MEASUREMENT_ANCESTRY -> HOMOLOGY`

`PAIRWISE_SIMILARITY -> STRUCTURAL_HOMOLOGY`

`COMMON_GEOMETRIC_SHAPE -> HOMOLOGY`

`SAME_MOTIF -> HOMOLOGY`

`SIGNATURE_TRANSPORT -> HOMOLOGY`

All are invalid without substantial downstream theoretical argument, and that theoretical argument remains outside this court's empirical ruling.

### What this court can establish

It can establish a bounded statement of the form:

> Under transport contract `T`, local invariant bundle `S_a` corresponds to `S_b` over the declared endpoint objects, map, estimands, and falsification regime.

It cannot transform that statement into:

> The two contexts instantiate homologous fields/positions/capitals/mechanisms.

The second claim adds theory about what produces and organizes the recurrence.

## 2. Bourdieu-style homology: lawful location in the stack

If Rawlsing later uses “homology” in a Bourdieu-style sense, it should occur **after**, not inside, cross-view transport adjudication.

Minimum downstream prerequisites would include:

- multiple independently established correspondences, not one pairwise similarity;
- stability across relevant local representations or an explicit account of representation dependence;
- alternative-map and negative-control failure of easy analogies;
- configuration and reversal analysis;
- an account of why the related positions/relations have corresponding structural functions rather than merely similar shapes;
- theory and historical evidence not derivable from the transport geometry itself.

Even satisfying these prerequisites would make homology arguable, not automatically true.

## 3. Articulation is a direct attack on global recurrence

A relation can change with configuration. Therefore every cross-context recurrence claim that could conceal articulation must classify its scope.

| status | evidence required | forbidden promotion |
|---|---|---|
| `GLOBAL_RECURRENCE` | invariant preserved over full local estimands and survives predeclared configuration decomposition | cannot infer common mechanism/homology |
| `CONDITIONAL_RECURRENCE` | same invariant preserved only under mapped conditioning states with adequate support | cannot describe whole-context recurrence |
| `CONFIGURATION_SPECIFIC_RECURRENCE` | mapped local configurations show recurrence; others may differ | cannot average into global law |
| `REVERSAL` | direction/opposition/branch relation flips under otherwise admissible map | cannot call it noisy recurrence |
| `PARTIAL_RECURRENCE` | strict subset of signature/domain survives | cannot report the full signature |
| `NO_TRANSPORT` | correspondence lacks authority, discriminativeness, support, or stability | visual/theoretical resemblance cannot override |

## 4. Southern Lebanon hostile case

Historical intuition:

- southern Lebanese Shia + poor;
- southern Lebanese Shia + wealthy + internationally educated + highly mobile.

The same named relational identity can have different implications under different configurations. The case therefore forbids treating identity labels as self-sufficient coordinates.

### Cross-context generalization

Suppose a future claim tries to compare a `sect x education` articulation in Lebanon with a `religious/ethnic x education` articulation elsewhere.

#### Option A — literal identity equivalence

`REJECT_BY_DEFAULT`.

“Shia,” “religious minority,” an ethnic category, or another polity-specific identity cannot be declared equivalent because each occupies a locally historical, institutional, demographic, and relational position. The labels are not portable slots.

#### Option B — generic functional-role equivalence

For example, map both to `MINORITY_RELIGIOUS_GROUP x HIGH_EDUCATION`.

`REQUIRES_SEPARATE_ROLE_AUTHORITY__NOT_GRANTED_HERE`.

Minority status by prevalence does not establish the same structural relation to state institutions, class formation, regional power, sectarian governance, war, migration, or education. A generic role can therefore erase precisely the articulation FIELD FORGE wants to observe.

#### Option C — bounded abstract motif

For example, each local context independently shows a predeclared pattern:

`IDENTITY_ROLE x RESOURCE_ORDER -> DISPOSITION_BRANCH_REVERSAL`

`POSSIBLY_LAWFUL_AS_MOTIF_OR_SIGNATURE_TRANSPORT` if and only if:

- each role is locally authorized before comparison;
- the motif is defined relationally rather than by pretending the identities are the same;
- the partial correspondence is fixed independently of observed agreement;
- risk sets/estimands and conditioning support remain local and explicit;
- alternative role maps/null motifs do not produce the pattern cheaply;
- the output forbids identity, mechanism, field, and homology equivalence.

This is the strongest lawful possibility found by the court. It is deliberately thinner than “the same social structure.”

#### Option D — nothing transports

`ALWAYS_ADMISSIBLE`.

If the abstract motif becomes so generic that many unrelated identities/resources fit it, or if the local roles cannot be authorized without cross-context theory doing the mapping, `NO_TRANSPORT` is the correct result.

## 5. Articulation sign is not scale-free by default

Interaction signs can change with:

- link function;
- outcome scaling;
- category aggregation;
- conditioning-set choice;
- local support;
- weighting/estimand;
- Simpson-type composition changes.

Therefore `INTERACTION_SIGN_RECURS` is not a primitive cross-context fact. The transport object must state the contrast definition and admitted transformations under which the sign is supposed to be invariant.

## 6. Exact boundary sentence

**Structural transport can provide empirical premises for a later homology argument; it can never supply homology as the name of the transported invariant.**
