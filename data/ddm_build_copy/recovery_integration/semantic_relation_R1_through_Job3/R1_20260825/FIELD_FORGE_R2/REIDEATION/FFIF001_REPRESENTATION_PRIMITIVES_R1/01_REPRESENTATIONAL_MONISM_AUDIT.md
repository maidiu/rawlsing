# FFIF001 — REPRESENTATIONAL MONISM AUDIT

Status: `CRITICAL_REIDEATION_COMPLETE`

## 1. Object of attack

FIELD FORGE already demoted `STRATA` and R2 already refused to let `GEOMETRY` become a replacement ontology. FFX002 then staged a useful `ATLAS_FIRST` versus `GEOMETRY_FIRST` duel. This court asks the prior question:

> Why should either side own what empirical social relational structure “really is”?

The answer is not “because pluralism is philosophically attractive.” It is empirical and engineering-specific: the representation families preserve different invariants, fail under different sparsity/topology regimes, and require different authority choices.

## 2. The seven propositions

### P1 — SOCIAL REALITY HAS ONE PRIVILEGED EMPIRICAL REPRESENTATION

`REJECT`.

No tested family is information-ordering neutral. A privileged representation would have to preserve every claim-relevant invariant while not adding an unauthorized equivalence, metric, partition, adjacency, or conditioning frame. None does.

This does not prove that reality itself is “plural.” It proves that Rawlsing currently lacks grounds to identify one analytical encoding with empirical reality.

### P2 — POSITIONAL_CONFIGURATION_ATLAS CAN BE THAT REPRESENTATION

`REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

Strengths:
- exact conjunction;
- minority/contradictory branch retention;
- transparent higher-order order-k facts;
- no distance required.

Failures:
- continuous and ordered resources require a state-identity rule or partition before exact configuration exists;
- order-k selection is itself a projection of the full respondent state;
- exact configuration identity fragments rapidly with dimensionality;
- disconnected exposure components cannot lawfully define one universal configuration;
- similarity, generalization, and compression need added machinery;
- overlapping formations are not naturally represented by mutually exact rows unless multiple set views are admitted.

Atlas survives as a view, not an ontology.

### P3 — GEOMETRIC SPACE CAN BE THAT REPRESENTATION

`REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

Geometry adds visibility unavailable to exact tables: neighborhoods, gradients, rank relations, opposition, continuous transitions, and compression. But these appear only after a distance/embedding family, active/supplementary choices, category treatment, estimand, weighting, block handling, and connectivity rule are frozen.

FFX002 correctly makes method sensitivity itself an output. That is incompatible with geometry being the pre-method empirical primitive.

### P4 — GRAPH/HYPERGRAPH CAN BE THAT REPRESENTATION

`REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

Graph language looks weak because it can represent arbitrary relations. That is precisely the danger. A graph becomes ontology at the moment node and edge identity are left implicit.

For Q629, are nodes the parent prompt, answer categories, selected answer events, or respondents? For Q86, are hyperedges questionnaire blocks, coexposed respondent sets, or observed response patterns? Those are different objects.

Graph/hypergraph is excellent for explicit measurement topology and some exact incidence relations. It is not the neutral answer to “everything is relational.”

### P5 — LATENT CLASSES/PROFILES CAN BE THAT REPRESENTATION

`REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

Latent classes/profiles can expose multimodality, soft membership, and recurrent response organizations. But model family, class count, local-independence assumptions, priors/regularization, and assignment rule are constitutive. Hard class realization can erase contradiction and minority branches; soft membership adds a probabilistic ontology not present in source facts.

Use only when the explicit claim is about whether a bounded latent mixture representation earns predictive/compressive value.

### P6 — DIRECT CONDITIONAL DISTRIBUTIONS CAN BE THAT REPRESENTATION

`REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

Direct `P(D|C)` objects are unusually close to FIELD FORGE downstream needs. They preserve destination alternatives and avoid global metric assumptions. But they still depend on:
- exact C and D identity;
- conditioning-set selection;
- exposure/risk set;
- support/backoff law;
- estimand/weight target;
- comparison baseline;
- transport rule across contexts.

They answer a claim. They do not define the whole social structure.

### P7 — METHOD-INDEPENDENT FACT LAYER PLUS MULTIPLE CLAIM-SCOPED VIEWS

`REWRITE_THEN_SUPPORT`.

The phrase `FACT_LAYER` is too permissive. FFX002's `ATLAS_RESPONDENT_FACT` is a powerful candidate floor, but once it stores already-authorized `position_state_refs[]` and `disposition_state_refs[]`, it has crossed into analytic-role partitioning. That is acceptable inside FFX002, not method-independent enough for this court's primitive.

The weaker floor is:

`GOVERNED_OBSERVATION_EVENT_SUBSTRATE`
+
`SOURCE_NATIVE_MEASUREMENT_TOPOLOGY_AND_PROVENANCE`

Only after a claim declares its semantic/role authority should it derive configurations, dependence facts, graphs, geometry, latent classes, or motifs.

## 3. What “method-independent” can honestly mean

Nothing in a data system is interpretation-free. The defensible claim is relative:

`METHOD_INDEPENDENT`
means
`DOES_NOT REQUIRE CHOOSING AMONG THE SIX TESTED SOCIAL-STRUCTURAL REPRESENTATION FAMILIES`.

It may still require finite source/identity authority.

A lawful observation event minimally needs:
- opaque empirical-unit identity ref;
- source family / wave / exact context/scope;
- exact question implementation / measurement identity;
- source-native structure identity and member role;
- eligibility/exposure state where actually governed;
- source-native response state/value where observed;
- response semantics for substantive/refusal/DK/etc.;
- source/instrument ancestry;
- routing/predicate refs;
- sample/design/weight-purpose refs where applicable;
- evidence/currentness/provenance.

What it must not contain by default:
- social configuration ID;
- neighbor relation;
- distance;
- graph edge interpreted as social relation;
- latent class;
- motif;
- conditional-dependence edge;
- cross-context equivalence;
- field/class/hegemony/mechanism label.

## 4. Why provenance is part of fact identity

FFX001-A establishes that `SOURCE_PRESENCE != ELIGIBILITY != EXPOSURE != RESPONSE` and that source structure type matters. Q629 and Q86 have superficially similar multi-member carriers but radically different measurement semantics. Therefore a value without its measurement identity/routing/source-structure ancestry is not the same empirical fact for FIELD FORGE purposes.

Provenance is not an audit sidecar added after analysis. At this boundary it is constitutive of replayable empirical identity.

## 5. Why higher-order J is not primitive

Higher-order same-respondent J is exceptionally important, but it is derived from:
- governed unit identity;
- governed exposure/coadministration;
- exact measurement identity;
- response-state identity;
- a declared member set and order.

That member-set projection is representational. Therefore J should be treated as a high-authority derived empirical view, not as the pre-representation substrate itself.

## 6. Monism failure does not imply method relativism

Multiple representations are not interchangeable stories. Each must carry:
1. claim type;
2. input manifest;
3. exact invariant-preservation contract;
4. losses introduced;
5. authority dependencies;
6. falsifiers;
7. replay/provenance;
8. admissible and forbidden interpretations.

A view that fails its contract is invalid. A view that answers a different claim is not a competitor. A view that disagrees after both validly preserve their declared invariants creates a typed representation-dependence result.

## 7. Hard conclusion

FIELD FORGE should not search for a single master picture of society. It should preserve the weakest governed empirical substrate capable of regenerating multiple lawful pictures, then force every stronger picture to declare what it adds, what it loses, and exactly which downstream claim needs it.
