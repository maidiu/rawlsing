# FFIF001 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL

These are design routes this court attempted to preserve only as rejected capital so later FIELD FORGE work does not rediscover them as clever defaults.

## N1 — ONE MASTER REPRESENTATION

Rejected.

No tested representation preserves all claim-relevant invariants without adding constitutive assumptions. Masterhood would hide representation-dependent loss.

## N2 — ATLAS IS LOSSLESS, THEREFORE PRIMARY

Rejected.

Losslessness relative to already selected discrete states is not neutrality. Continuous-state identity, order-k selection, coexposure components, member selection, and similarity/compression remain unresolved.

## N3 — GEOMETRY IS RELATIONAL, THEREFORE ONTOLOGICALLY SUPERIOR

Rejected.

Relationality does not make a metric source-native. Geometry adds useful structure under explicit method choices; it does not discover the one preexisting coordinate system by default.

## N4 — EVERYTHING IS A GRAPH

Rejected.

Graph universality is syntactic. Node/edge/projection/nonedge semantics are the ontology surface.

## N5 — DIRECT CONDITIONALS ARE “JUST THE DATA”

Rejected.

Conditioning set, risk set, baseline, support, and estimand are claim choices. Conditionals are powerful views, not primitive facts.

## N6 — HIGHER-ORDER J AS THE EMPIRICAL PRIMITIVE

Rejected.

J is authoritative same-unit evidence once its member/risk-set relation is lawful, but the choice of member set/order is already a projection. Preserve the events from which J can be replayed.

## N7 — LATENT CLASS AS SOCIAL TYPE

Rejected.

Class/profile model output may be useful compression or multimodality evidence. It cannot become social class, field, ideology, or actor type without separate interpretation authority.

## N8 — FACT LAYER CONTAINING ALL DERIVED FACTS

Rejected.

A layer containing every marginal, J object, conditional, graph, geometry, motif and model is not method-independent; it is a warehouse of representations. It also pushes arbitrary selection to consumers without clarifying claim scope.

## N9 — LOSSLESS MEANS STORE EVERYTHING AND DECIDE LATER

Rejected.

Raw preservation is necessary for replay but insufficient as an interface. Typed materialized views are needed. The law is weaker substrate + explicit view contracts, not raw-event fundamentalism.

## N10 — CONFIGURATION SIMILARITY BY “OBVIOUS” OVERLAP

Rejected.

Hamming/Jaccard/shared-membership/semantic similarity all add metrics or equivalences. If needed, name and test them as separate views.

## N11 — CROSS-CONTEXT ALIGNMENT CREATES COMPARABILITY

Rejected.

Procrustes/common embedding/harmonized labels can manufacture portability. Transport requires explicit semantic and invariant authority before alignment.

## N12 — ABSENT GRAPH EDGE = NO RELATION

Rejected.

It may mean noncoexposure, routing incompatibility, unresolved authority, threshold pruning, untested pair, or true zero. Typed nonedges are required.

## N13 — SOURCE BLOCK = EMPIRICAL/SOCIAL BLOCK

Rejected.

Source instrument block, analytic role block, and empirical coadministration block are distinct. Q629/Q86 make structure type especially non-negotiable.

## N14 — STABILITY CURES ONTOLOGY

Rejected.

A representation can be stably driven by questionnaire design, coding, or a frozen bad equivalence. Stability is evidence only after input identity/admissibility is lawful.

## N15 — MULTIPLE REPRESENTATIONS = EPISTEMIC VOTING

Rejected.

Cross-view agreement is not truth by majority. Disagreement is classified by invariant contracts, validity, evidence sufficiency and downstream claim.

## N16 — CURRENT RESPONDENT SUBSTRATE ASSUMED BECAUSE ARCHITECTURE NEEDS IT

Rejected.

FFX001-B/C remain blocking at current respondent-binding grain. Architecture cannot manufacture the carrier it wishes existed.
