# FFIF001 — MULTI-REPRESENTATION ARCHITECTURE CANDIDATES

Status: `CANDIDATE_ARCHITECTURE__NO_AUTHORITY_MINTED`

## Candidate A — MASTER ATLAS

```text
observation rows
 -> positional/disposition configuration atlas
 -> optional geometry/model summaries
 -> downstream structural interpretation
```

### Strength
Exact conjunction and minority preservation remain central.

### Fatal weakness
The atlas must decide state/configuration identity before all other views, privileges discrete conjunction, and becomes awkward for continuous resources, gradients, disconnected surfaces, and overlapping projections. Optional summaries then inherit atlas selection choices.

Ruling: `REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

## Candidate B — MASTER GEOMETRY

```text
observation rows
 -> local/global geometry
 -> configurations/regions/dependence as projections
```

### Strength
Powerful compression and neighborhood/gradient visibility.

### Fatal weakness
Metric and admissibility choices become constitutive; exact minority/J information is downstream reconstruction; disconnected measurement cannot honestly share one space.

Ruling: `REJECT_AS_MASTER`.

## Candidate C — UNIVERSAL TYPED GRAPH

```text
all empirical objects -> nodes
all relations -> typed edges/hyperedges
```

### Strength
Can store heterogeneous relations and overlap.

### Fatal weakness
Typing does not eliminate ontology; it relocates it to node/edge registries. Generic graph algorithms then invite illicit projections/community reification.

Ruling: `REJECT_AS_MASTER_STORAGE_SEMANTICS`; retain graph views where useful.

## Candidate D — EMPIRICAL FACT LAYER + MULTIPLE VIEWS

### Naive form
```text
marginals + J + configurations + conditionals
 -> atlas/geometry/graph/model views
```

### Fatal weakness
The “facts” are already projections with member/conditioning/equivalence choices.

Ruling: `REJECT_NAIVE_FACT_LAYER`.

## Candidate E — PROVENANCED OBSERVATION SUBSTRATE + TYPED CLAIM-SCOPED VIEWS

```text
SOURCE-NATIVE IDENTITY / STRUCTURE / ROUTING AUTHORITY
                 +
GOVERNED EMPIRICAL-UNIT / EXPOSURE / RESPONSE EVENTS
                 +
SAMPLE / ESTIMAND / EVIDENCE / ANCESTRY PROVENANCE
                 ↓
GOVERNED OBSERVATION EVENT SUBSTRATE
                 ↓
CLAIM + SEMANTIC/ROLE AUTHORITY MANIFEST
                 ↓
CLAIM-SCOPED REPRESENTATION CONTRACT
      ↙          ↓          ↓          ↘
CONFIGURATION  CONDITIONAL  GEOMETRY  GRAPH/HYPERGRAPH
      ↘          ↓          ↓          ↙
       LATENT/PROFILE and MOTIF views where admitted
                 ↓
INVARIANT-LOSS + ROBUSTNESS + DISAGREEMENT RECEIPT
                 ↓
STRUCTURAL ADMISSION / ABSTENTION
```

### Why it survives
It is the only candidate that does not require selecting a social-structural representation before preserving the empirical events needed to falsify that representation.

### Remaining danger
The substrate can still hide ontology in preprocessing/equivalence. Candidate E is valid only with `EQUIVALENCE_BEFORE_METHOD` governance.

Ruling: `SURVIVES_HOSTILE_AUDIT`.

## Candidate F — IRREDUCIBLE PARALLEL PRIMITIVES

```text
configuration primitive
geometry primitive
graph primitive
conditional primitive
...
(no common replay floor)
```

### Strength
Maximally respects method differences.

### Fatal weakness
Without a weaker common observation/provenance substrate, view disagreement cannot be localized: different inputs can masquerade as representational disagreement, and exact replay becomes brittle.

Ruling: `REJECT_AS_ENGINEERING_ARCHITECTURE`.

Prospective object:

```text
REPRESENTATION_CONTRACT {
  claim_id

```

The representation family is selected by a finite claim-to-method admissibility bank, not by runtime fit alone.

## Prospective authority banks identified, not minted

1. `OBSERVATION_IDENTITY_AND_EQUIVALENCE_AUTHORITY`
   - source-native value identity;
   - reversible recodes;
   - category harmonization permissions;
   - unit identity/crosswalk law.

2. `CLAIM_TO_REPRESENTATION_ADMISSIBILITY_AUTHORITY`
   - which representation families may answer which claim classes;
   - minimum invariants each must preserve;
   - forbidden losses/interpretations.

3. `CROSS_CONTEXT_TRANSPORT_AUTHORITY`
   - exact invariant being compared;
   - semantic mapping/measurement comparability;
   - whether alignment is descriptive, model-based, or prohibited.

4. `CROSS_VIEW_DISAGREEMENT_CLASSIFICATION_AUTHORITY`
   - finite rule for invalid-view vs invariant-tradeoff vs insufficient-evidence vs nonreducibility.

## Precompute-before-automate

Freeze finite semantic surfaces once where possible. Then deterministic producers may generate/replay views. Runtime optimization may tune numerical parameters only inside a preregistered family and may never silently choose semantic identity, social role, cross-context equivalence, or ontology.

## Terminal architecture recommendation

Adopt no master representation. Bank Candidate E as the strongest re-ideation result for Vozhd review, subject to a future authority court and respondent-carrier closure.
