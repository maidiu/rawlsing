## Terminal ruling

`MASTER_REPRESENTATION_REJECTED__PROVENANCED_OBSERVATION_SUBSTRATE_PLUS_TYPED_CLAIM_SCOPED_VIEWS_SUPPORTED`

This is deliberately narrower and more hostile than generic `FACT_LAYER_PLUS_TYPED_MULTI_REPRESENTATION_VIEWS_SUPPORTED`.

A method-independent **social-structural fact layer** is not supported. Configurations, higher-order J, conditional dependence, graphs/hypergraphs, geometry, latent profiles, and motifs all make representational choices. Calling any of them the fact layer would launder those choices into ontology.

The weakest defensible pre-representation floor found by this court is instead a governed, source-faithful **observation-event / measurement-topology / provenance substrate**. Its job is to preserve what was measured, under what source-native structure and exact eligibility/exposure conditions, for what empirical unit, with what response state, ancestry, sample/estimand metadata, and evidence status. It may not create social distance, configuration similarity, graph adjacency, latent membership, conditional-dependence edges, or cross-context equivalence.

That substrate is not metaphysically neutral. It still depends on finite authority over empirical-unit identity, measurement identity, source-native value identity, routing/exposure, and provenance. The claim is only that these are weaker than any tested social-structural representation and are necessary to replay all of them.

## Strongest representation rejected

`POSITIONAL_CONFIGURATION_ATLAS_AS_MASTER_EMPIRICAL_REPRESENTATION`

It is the strongest rejected master because it preserves exact observed conjunctions, contradiction, minority branches, and higher-order structure better than the compressive alternatives. But as a master it fails on continuous coordinates without authorized partitioning, combinatorial order, disconnected measurement components, overlapping formations, and any claim of configuration similarity. Compression that cures these failures tends to reintroduce a metric, model, or semantic equivalence under another name.
