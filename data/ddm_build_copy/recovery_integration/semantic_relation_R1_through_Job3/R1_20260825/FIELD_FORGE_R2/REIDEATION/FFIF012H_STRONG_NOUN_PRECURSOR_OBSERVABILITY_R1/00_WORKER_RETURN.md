## 4. FIELD / CAPITAL side

### 4.1 `RELATIONAL_ARENA_CANDIDATE`

**Disposition:** `POSSIBLE_BUT_AUTHORITY_BLOCKED_PRECURSOR` + `EMPIRICALLY_TESTABLE_ADMISSION_HYPOTHESIS`.

A neutral candidate can be built only from an independently frozen domain/scope boundary, lawfully coexposed variables/relations within that domain, exact support/provenance, internal relation/configuration structure, and negative-control or out-of-domain comparisons sufficient to show the candidate is not merely questionnaire-block coadministration.

Current surveys can observe internal association, exact configuration, conditional redistribution, organization membership, geography, and other bounded source-native relations where governed. They do not by themselves establish that those relations constitute a sociological field.

**Falsifiers:** disappearance under questionnaire-block controls; equivalent relation structure outside the proposed domain; arena boundaries recoverable only after inspecting the target pattern; inability to separate routing/coadministration from substantive relation.

### 4.2 `DIFFERENTIAL_RESOURCE_RETURN_OR_VALUATION_PROFILE`

**Disposition:** strongest current lawful object is `RESOURCE_CONDITIONAL_ASSOCIATION_PROFILE`; stronger return/valuation language is `POSSIBLE_BUT_AUTHORITY_BLOCKED`.

With governed resource variables and governed outcomes, Rawlsing can compare exact outcome distributions conditional on resource states/configurations. If the outcome is independently material, the result is still a descriptive differential-outcome association unless a stronger design authorizes a return/valuation estimand.

Survey approval, status, prestige, trust, or attitude alignment cannot silently become the value of a resource.

### 4.3 `CONVERSION_PATTERN`

**Disposition:** `UNOBSERVED_AS_CONVERSION` for ordinary cross-sectional co-occurrence; `POSSIBLE_BUT_OBSERVABILITY_AND_AUTHORITY_BLOCKED_PRECURSOR` where genuine time/trajectory/order evidence exists.

Cross-sectional possession of resource A together with resource B supports co-occurrence or conditional association. It does not identify A becoming B, A purchasing B, or a mechanism by which one resource is translated into another. Directional conversion requires independently justified temporal/trajectory/process evidence and a conversion identity not defined circularly by the alleged field.

### 4.4 `PARTIAL_AUTONOMY_EVIDENCE`

**Disposition:** `POSSIBLE_BUT_AUTHORITY_BLOCKED_PRECURSOR`.

A neutral precursor would be domain-specific relation divergence that survives frozen lawful conditioning and negative controls and cannot be reduced to routing, source block, or common measurement ancestry. This can be tested without calling the domain a field. Strong field autonomy remains downstream interpretation.

## 5. HEGEMONY side

No scalar or binary hegemony object is currently justified. The weakest useful precursor is a typed, incomplete component profile whose missing/blocked components remain explicit.

Component rulings:

- `PREVALENCE`: currently observable as an exact realized-sample fact; population prevalence requires the appropriate weight/design estimand.
- `CROSS_POSITION_REACH`: observable only where relevant position roles and same-unit exposure are already governed; no global position ontology is implied.
- `DIFFUSION_CONCENTRATION`: cross-sectional spread/concentration is observable; temporal diffusion as a process is not inferred from one cross-section.
- `HIGH_POSITION_ALIGNMENT`: authority-blocked unless the position domain has a governed high/low order or threshold. PRA membership alone does not manufacture hierarchy.
- `GEOGRAPHIC_REACH`: observable within governed geography and sample coverage; sample coverage is not automatically population reach.
- `TEMPORAL_PERSISTENCE`: possible only under typed measurement/transport, wording/routing/composition, and estimand controls. Label continuity is insufficient.

- `EXTERNALLY_GROUNDED_MATERIAL_OR_INSTITUTIONAL_POWER_ALIGNMENT`: physically possible in principle because EPR/GeoEPR and other macro evidence exist, but presently authority-blocked wherever a survey group must be linked to an external group. OBL006 remains binding.

## 6. PRESSURE side

### 6.1 `CONDITIONAL_REDISTRIBUTION / DEPENDENCE`

**Disposition:** `CURRENTLY_OBSERVABLE_NEUTRAL_PRECURSOR`.

Exact conditional distributions and directional contrasts are lawful finite-sample descriptive facts when unit identity, risk set, exposure/coexposure, state identity, joint predicate, estimand/weight target, support, and provenance are frozen. Zero denominators remain typed abstentions. No universal minimum-n threshold, causal effect, inferential adequacy, or materiality follows automatically.

The second genuine canary is direct evidence that broad and exact joint-conditioned directional facts can be represented without elevating any representation into social ontology.

### 6.2 Lawful descriptive reference families

- `GLOBAL_OR_BROAD_MARGINAL_REFERENCE`: lawful when the frozen claim defines the broad context/risk set. Descriptive, not counterfactual.
- `EXACT_STRATIFIED_OR_CONFIGURATION_REFERENCE`: lawful when the exact same-unit predicate and support are frozen. The second canary exercises this family.
- `MATCHED_OR_LOCAL_REFERENCE`: only if match variables/roles and the matching rule are frozen before outputs. The result remains a matched descriptive comparison absent causal authority.
- `METRIC_OR_NEIGHBORHOOD_REFERENCE`: requires separate order/metric/neighborhood relation authority; mere computability from a carrier is insufficient.
- `CROSS_CONTEXT_REFERENCE`: requires FFIF003-style typed transport and preserves local risk-set/estimand independence.

### 6.3 `PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT`

**Disposition:** `EMPIRICALLY_TESTABLE_ADMISSION_HYPOTHESIS`, not admitted.

The weakest defensible candidate is a bounded provenance-rich vector/profile of preregistered exact conditional redistributions across one or more lawful reference families, preserving support, abstention, reference identity, and representation/transport ancestry. The noun must not imply a causal force, mechanism, counterfactual intervention, or psychological influence.

Its immediate hostile test is reference sensitivity: if the alleged footprint exists only under one arbitrary neighborhood/reference definition or changes identity when a lawful weaker reference is substituted, the noun is underidentified.

### 6.4 `PRESSURE_PATHWAY / STRUCTURAL_MECHANISM`

**Disposition:** `UNOBSERVED_OR_DOWNSTREAM_INTERPRETATION_ONLY` from the current cross-sectional conditional evidence.

Conditional redistribution can identify pattern; it cannot by itself identify the mechanism producing the pattern, the direction of social force, or a causal pathway.
