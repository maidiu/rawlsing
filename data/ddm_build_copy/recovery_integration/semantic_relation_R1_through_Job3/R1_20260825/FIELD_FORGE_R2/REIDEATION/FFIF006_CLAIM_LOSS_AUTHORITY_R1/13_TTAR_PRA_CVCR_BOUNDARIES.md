# FFIF006 — TTAR / PRA / CVCR BOUNDARIES

## TTAR boundary

TTAR owns the permission and loss profile for transformations that alter sameness, difference, order, grouping, comparability, or semantic eligibility.

The CIM binds the exact ordered TTAR sequence through the common semantic input hash. Changing TTAR ancestry changes the empirical proposition or creates an explicitly separate semantic variant.

TTAR may state `this category collapse loses distinctions X/Y`. It may not state `that loss is tolerable for claim C` unless a separately competent CITC authorizes it.

## PRA boundary

PRA owns whether a lawfully represented measurement/state functions as `ACTIVE_POSITION`, `ACTIVE_DISPOSITION`, `SUPPLEMENTARY`, `CONDITIONING_ONLY`, `DIAGNOSTIC_DESIGN`, or `EXCLUDED` for the bounded analytic role.

When a claim operand is role-bearing, the CIM binds the exact PRA ref. Changing education from `ACTIVE_POSITION` to `CONDITIONING_ONLY` changes the proposition's operands/conditioning relation or invalidates an arm-local change.

PRA may identify role-semantic distinctions that a claim must preserve. It may not select a numeric/materiality tolerance merely because the desired role model needs one.

## CVCR boundary

The `CLAIM_SCOPED_VIEW_COMPARISON_RECEIPT` consumes:

- frozen CIM ref, proposition hash, and comparison claim hash;
- common semantic input and TTAR hashes;
- PRA refs where constitutive;
- frozen CITC ref/hash;
- frozen support/uncertainty contract;
- independently produced view outputs/contracts;
- weaker-object court and hostile/holdout results as applicable.

The CVCR may deterministically:

- verify hash equality;
- normalize outputs under registered mappings;
- apply frozen tolerances and uncertainty rules;
- mark hard-zero failures;
- emit componentwise materiality states;
- distinguish invalidity, known loss, representation dependence, sufficiency, and unresolved comparison.

It may not:

- choose or revise the claim;
- choose the invariant bundle;
- choose a tolerance after outputs;
- let one view use a different tolerance;
- convert predictive/downstream utility into empirical truth;
- self-authorize finite-universe closure or bounded nonreducibility.

## Dependency chain

`SOURCE/MEASUREMENT AUTHORITY -> TTAR -> PRA (where role-bearing) -> CIM -> CITC + SUPPORT/UNCERTAINTY CONTRACT -> INDEPENDENT VIEWS -> CVCR -> WEAKER-OBJECT/NONREDUCIBILITY COURTS -> DOWNSTREAM UTILITY -> ENGINEERING SELECTION`

No later stage may back-authorize an earlier one.

## Exact CVCR implication

A CVCR without a matching frozen `comparison_claim_hash` and `invariant_tolerance_contract_hash` is not merely incomplete. It is unauthorized to emit `MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE`, `WEAKER_OBJECT_SUFFICIENT`, `MULTI_VIEW_RETENTION_REQUIRED`, or `BOUNDED_NONREDUCIBILITY_SUPPORTED`.
