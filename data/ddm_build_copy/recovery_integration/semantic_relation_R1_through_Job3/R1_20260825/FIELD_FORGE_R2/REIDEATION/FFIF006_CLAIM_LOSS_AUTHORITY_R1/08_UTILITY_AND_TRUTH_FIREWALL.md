# FFIF006 — UTILITY AND TRUTH FIREWALL

## Four noninterchangeable statuses

### `EMPIRICAL_SUFFICIENCY_FOR_ADMITTED_VIEW_ROLE`

A view is lawfully generated from the frozen common input and preserves the invariants required by its own admitted empirical view contract. It may lawfully emit `UNDEFINED_FOR_VIEW` outside that role. This is stronger than mere computability but is not universal empirical sufficiency.

### `CLAIM_SUFFICIENT`

An empirically admissible object or object set answers every atomic assertion slot required by one CIM within the frozen invariant-tolerance and support/uncertainty contracts. Sufficiency is claim-relative.

### `DOWNSTREAM_USEFUL`

An object improves a frozen consumer query, target-specific prediction, retrieval, compression, or coverage. Utility is consumer- and target-relative. It may exist without claim sufficiency and cannot repair claim insufficiency.

### `ENGINEERING_PREFERRED`

Among lawful objects already sufficient for the relevant claim/consumer contract, one is preferred for storage, latency, compute, maintainability, or replay economy.

The legal order is:

`EMPIRICAL_SUFFICIENCY_FOR_ADMITTED_VIEW_ROLE -> CLAIM_SUFFICIENCY -> DOWNSTREAM_UTILITY -> ENGINEERING_PREFERENCE`

Later stages never cure earlier failure.

## Strongest firewall

`NO DOWNSTREAM OR ENGINEERING GAIN MAY AUTHORIZE, HIDE, OFFSET, OR RETROACTIVELY RECLASSIFY LOSS OF A CLAIM-CONSTITUTIVE EMPIRICAL INVARIANT.`

Consequences:

- geometry improving a PRE-V3 consumer does not make its social picture truer;
- a faster compressed view cannot erase exact minority existence required by the claim;
- a lossless atlas may remain empirically sufficient for its admitted exact-view role but be claim- or consumer-insufficient;
- a predictively useful view may remain invalid for an exact configuration claim;
- a view can be `USEFUL__CLAIM_INSUFFICIENT` or `CLAIM_SUFFICIENT__LESS_USEFUL`.

## Prediction ceiling

Heldout success may establish only what the frozen predictive claim names:

- `PREDICTIVE_ADEQUACY_FOR_TARGET_Y`;
- target/partition/metric-specific incremental prediction;
- calibration or reconstruction fidelity if directly tested;
- compression at a frozen fidelity/query contract.

It does not establish:

- minority or contradiction preservation unless separately measured;
- higher-order exactness;
- neighborhood or topology truth;
- general representational sufficiency;
- class, field, capital, hegemony, homology, or mechanism;
- causal adequacy;
- social ontology.

A predictive metric may enter empirical materiality only when the normalized proposition is itself predictive adequacy for the exact target, partition regime, and metric. Otherwise it remains downstream utility.

## Bundle firewall

A consumer may require several atomic outputs together. This creates `CONSUMER_BUNDLE_SUFFICIENCY`. It does not by itself create one empirical proposition or prove empirical bounded nonreducibility. Consumer-independent empirical co-requirement needs separate bundle authority.

## Noncompensation

Do not aggregate empirical information loss, representational distortion, estimation error, downstream utility, and engineering cost into one scalar. Report a typed vector. Only claim-sufficient candidates proceed to utility/cost selection, except when the explicit result is that no current candidate is sufficient.
