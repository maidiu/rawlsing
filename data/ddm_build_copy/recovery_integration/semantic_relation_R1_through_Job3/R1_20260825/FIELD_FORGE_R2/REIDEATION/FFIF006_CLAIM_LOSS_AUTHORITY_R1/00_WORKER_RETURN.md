## Terminal ruling

`CLAIM_IDENTITY_AND_LOSS_BUDGET_REQUIRE_SEPARATE_TYPED_AUTHORITY__BOUND_BY_COMPARISON_HASH__CURRENT_FFX002_TOLERANCES_NOT_AUTHORIZED`

One broad prose claim cannot carry empirical identity. One scalar loss cannot carry materiality. And one downstream utility result cannot decide empirical truth.

Rawlsing needs two distinct but hash-bound objects:

1. `CLAIM_IDENTITY_MANIFEST` (`CIM`) — identifies the normalized empirical proposition and the exact comparison version.
2. `CLAIM_INVARIANT_TOLERANCE_CONTRACT` (`CITC`) — assigns typed criticality/tolerance, uncertainty interaction, evidence basis, authority, allowed use, and forbidden promotion per claim-invariant/loss dimension.

The CIM contains:

- `EMPIRICAL_PROPOSITION_KEY`, excluding tolerance and implementation details;
- `COMPARISON_CLAIM_KEY`, which adds the frozen CITC and support/uncertainty hashes.

Thus changing a tolerance does not pretend the proposition changed, but it does create a different comparison and cannot be hidden beneath the same claim ID.

## Minimum lawful claim-identity object

At minimum the CIM binds:

- normalized proposition and assertion slot;
- operands and exact TTAR/PRA semantic refs where applicable;
- claim scope, empirical unit, risk set, population/context/time;
- estimand and weight target;
- target invariant or independently authorized bundle;
- quantifier, conditioning set, baseline, scale/value domain, inferential class;
- common semantic input hash and source-native ancestry ceiling;
- support/uncertainty contract hash;
- CITC hash;
- representation-neutral default, with restriction only when genuinely constitutive;
- authority, validity, forbidden promotions, and supersession;
- separate proposition and comparison hashes.

Algorithm, estimator, seed, output, observed support, storage, and latency remain execution metadata.

## Minimum lawful loss/materiality object

The CITC is a separate row-typed contract keyed by proposition, assertion slot, invariant, and registered loss dimension. Each row binds:

- criticality state: hard zero, bounded approximation, not required, unresolved, or not applicable;
- tolerance type/value/rule, unit, scale, and direction of worse loss;
- noncompensatory aggregation rule;
- support and uncertainty interaction;
- source resolution and error/design evidence where relevant;
- authority basis/ref and evidence refs;
- allowed/forbidden uses;
- downstream requirement ref only as utility authority;
- pre-output freeze receipt, hash, validity, and supersession.

Realized loss stays in view loss manifests. The CITC authorizes how that loss may be judged; it does not contain the result.

## Who may authorize empirical materiality

Only a competent claim-materiality authority acting before results, with an evidence basis tied to:

- claim semantics/logical quantifier;
- source/measurement resolution;
- authorized design precision/error model;
- a claim-warranted substantive minimum effect or bounded actor-independent analytic purpose where applicable.

No universal 5% rule exists.

TTAR describes transformation and loss but not tolerability. PRA authorizes role semantics but not loss margins. View producers report results. CVCR applies frozen contracts. PRE-V3 consumers set consumer requirements. Engineering owners choose among lawful sufficient objects. None of the latter may mint empirical materiality.

This court designs the jurisdiction; it does not establish the current project office/person empowered to issue CITCs.
