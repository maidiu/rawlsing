# FFIF006 — NEGATIVE DESIGN CAPITAL

## NDC001 — broad prose as identity

Rejected: `Does education matter?` as a stable comparison key.

Reason: destination, role, risk set, estimand, conditioning, baseline, scale, quantifier, and inferential class remain free.

## NDC002 — every implementation detail in identity

Rejected: including algorithm, seed, code version, coordinates, or output hash in the proposition.

Reason: this would make every representation answer a different claim and abolish comparison.

## NDC003 — embedded untyped scalar loss

Rejected: `loss = 0.17` or one generic threshold inside a claim row.

Reason: empirical information loss, representational distortion, estimation error, utility loss, and engineering cost are not commensurable.

## NDC004 — preregistration cures arbitrariness

Rejected: any number becomes epistemically valid if frozen early.

Reason: freeze prevents result-fitting; it does not supply an authority/evidence basis.

## NDC005 — universal five-percent rule

Rejected.

Reason: conventional significance, substantive materiality, equivalence, source resolution, and exact existence are different propositions.

## NDC006 — non-significance equals no difference

Rejected.

Reason: unresolved evidence is not equivalence or immateriality.

## NDC007 — prediction as general truth

Rejected: heldout success licenses the representation as generally truer.

Reason: it proves only target/metric/partition-specific predictive adequacy and cannot establish untested invariants or mechanism.

## NDC008 — downstream consumer sets empirical truth

Rejected: a PRE-V3 query need makes its preferred view empirically primary.

Reason: consumer requirements set utility/bundle sufficiency; they do not rewrite atomic empirical claims.

## NDC009 — engineering cost buys loss permission

Rejected: smaller/faster object may erase a critical invariant.

Reason: engineering choice follows claim sufficiency.

## NDC010 — PRA owns tolerances

Rejected.

Reason: PRA authorizes role use; it cannot set acceptable empirical loss because a role model needs stability.

## NDC011 — TTAR loss profile equals tolerance

Rejected.

Reason: describing what a transform loses is not authorizing that loss for a later claim.

## NDC012 — CVCR chooses threshold

Rejected.

Reason: the comparator applies frozen authority; it cannot create the rule that determines its result.

## NDC013 — post-hoc minority threshold

Rejected.

Reason: low observed mass cannot erase exact existence or define materiality after inspection.

## NDC014 — aggregate utility/loss score

Rejected.

Reason: predictive gain, bytes saved, minority erasure, and topology loss cannot compensate one another without laundering values into truth.

## NDC015 — globally weaker representation

Rejected.

Reason: weakness is a claim-relative partial order over commitments and invariant sufficiency, not method prestige or object size.

## NDC016 — bundle after viewing unique wins

Rejected.

Reason: it manufactures multi-view necessity by definition.

## NDC017 — same proposition means same comparison

Rejected.

Reason: different uncertainty or tolerance contracts produce different comparison claims even when the empirical proposition is unchanged.

## NDC018 — current FFX002 guidance is already authority

Rejected.

Reason: its thresholds are disciplined design capital, but FFIF006 was explicitly forbidden to mint current canary tolerances.
