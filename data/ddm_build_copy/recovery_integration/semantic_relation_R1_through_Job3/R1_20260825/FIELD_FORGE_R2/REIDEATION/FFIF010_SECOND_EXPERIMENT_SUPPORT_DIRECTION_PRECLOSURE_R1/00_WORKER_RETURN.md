# A — DIRECTION FUNCTIONAL COURT

## 2. Weakest lawful scalar functional

The weakest lawful `F` capable of supporting exact signed broad-vs-within direction assertions is:

`F01__EXACT_NATIVE_SINGLE_STATE_EVENT_CONTRAST`

For one future preregistered exact source-native destination state `d*`:

`Delta_E(h) = P_sample(D=d* | R, C=c1, h) - P_sample(D=d* | R, C=c0, h)`

where `h` is either the broad context or one exact support-admitted joint predicate `s`.

The exact direction output is:

`sign(Delta_E(h)) in {-1,0,+1}`.

This is lawful without ordering or scoring the full destination domain. It compares two scalar probabilities of one already-identified native event. The subtraction operation does not imply that the other destination states are ordered, adjacent, commensurable, or reducible to a score.

Therefore, for a nominal multi-state destination, one exact native event `D=d*` is sufficient to create a lawful signed relation **if and only if** the future claim can truthfully preregister `d*` as the destination event before target outcomes are inspected.

No ordinal scale is required.

## 3. Exact directional statuses available without a materiality threshold

With a lawful scalar `F` and exact realized-sample contrasts:

### Exact directional reversal

For broad contrast `Delta_0` and admitted within-state contrast `Delta_s`:

`sign(Delta_0) * sign(Delta_s) = -1`

with both signs nonzero.

This supports only:

`EXACT_DIRECTIONAL_REVERSAL`

It does not assert material magnitude, statistical significance, population reversal, causality, or null separation.

### Exact directional persistence

`sign(Delta_0) = sign(Delta_s)`

with both signs nonzero.

This supports only:

`EXACT_DIRECTIONAL_PERSISTENCE`

or equivalently `DIRECTIONAL_PERSISTENCE`.

It does **not** establish magnitude persistence or equivalence within tolerance. Any such stronger use remains CITC jurisdiction.

### Exact directional split

Across the future CIM's complete support-admitted state set, if at least one state has positive `Delta_s` and at least one has negative `Delta_s`, the exact sign pattern supports:

`EXACT_DIRECTIONAL_SPLIT`.

When broad sign is nonzero this may equivalently contain at least one persisting and at least one reversing state.

Exact zero or unresolved states may be recorded, but they may not by themselves manufacture a split and may not be silently deleted from the state roster.

`EXACT_IDENTITIES_AND_REPLAY + EXACT_NUMERATOR_COUNTS + POSITIVE_DENOMINATORS -> EXACT_REALIZED_SAMPLE_CONDITIONAL_IDENTIFIED`
