# ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESES_STRENGTHENED

## H1 — exact configuration is closer to a reusable derived carrier than a peer representation

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H1 is strongly strengthened, with one important rewrite.

The architecture should not privilege the noun `configuration`. The more general candidate primitive is an **exact same-unit finite active-state carrier** whose defining property is lossless recoverability of the frozen state vector and empirical-unit membership under explicit coexposure, semantic-state, risk-set, and provenance authority.

Under the first canary, at least three physical/organizational encodings are losslessly equivalent at this bounded surface:

1. respondent-by-measurement exact state rows;
2. unique state-profile/configuration dictionary plus respondent membership;
3. the exact profile quotient used as B's pre-metric carrier.

Treating those as three epistemically independent views would count storage/index organization as empirical plurality.

`SPECULATIVE_ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS`

A future FIELD FORGE architecture may define a claim/experiment-scoped `EXACT_SAME_UNIT_STATE_CARRIER` abstraction with multiple reversible physical encodings. Exact configuration membership would be one lawful interface over that carrier, not a master ontology.

What follows if this survives future testing:

- C-like conditional objects can be compiled from the carrier without retaining the full carrier in the final claim artifact;
- exact conjunction, minority, counterpattern, higher-order J, and arbitrary finite cross-tab queries can be regenerated without reacquiring source data;
- B-like exact state-profile metrics can be generated from the same carrier after an explicit metric relation is frozen;
- duplicate exact carriers need not be materialized separately for each derivative family if replay and provenance remain exact.

What absolutely does **not** follow:

- configuration is the social world;
- every source form admits one finite configuration carrier;
- continuous measurements may be discretized merely to enter the carrier;
- disconnected exposure components may be concatenated into one vector;
- the carrier authorizes metric similarity, topology, recurrence, transport, social roles, causal claims, or theoretical nouns;
- a deterministic function of the carrier is semantically authorized merely because it is computationally derivable;
- the carrier supersedes the lower observation/provenance substrate.

The strongest bounded statement is:

`DATA_COMPLETE_FOR_THE_FROZEN_ACTIVE_STATE_SURFACE != SEMANTICALLY_COMPLETE_FOR_ALL_DERIVED_RELATIONS`.

## H2 — direct conditional objects are claim-native query operators

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H2 is strongly strengthened.

C's radical information deletion is a feature for Q03. It retains the exact assertion interface the claim needs and discards conjunctions, minority configurations, higher-order structure, and unrelated local relations that Q03 does not assert.

The first canary therefore suggests that `DIRECT_CONDITIONAL_OBJECT` is often better conceptualized as:

`CLAIM_NATIVE_QUERY_OPERATOR + OPTIONAL_MATERIALIZED_QUERY_VIEW`

rather than as a persistent world representation.

A query view may be persisted for replay, latency, or audit. Persistence does not change its epistemic layer.

This also explains why C can be the weakest sufficient object for Q03 without being a good reusable carrier for future unknown questions.

## H3 — geometry should be decomposed and moved downstream

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H3 is strongly strengthened, but `GEOMETRY` is too coarse a bucket. Core B currently bundles at least three different things:

1. a lossless exact state-profile carrier already available from A-like information;
2. an exact metric/order/opposition relation constructed after a metric commitment;
3. a lossy low-dimensional coordinate serialization used for compression/exploration/interface convenience.

These should not inherit one evidentiary status.

The strongest candidate decomposition is:

- `EXACT_METRIC_RELATION_OBJECT` — cardinal pairwise distance under a frozen metric contract;
- `ORDER_NEIGHBORHOOD_RELATION_OBJECT` — weaker ordinal/local relation derived from a metric or defined directly;
- `OPPOSITION_RELATION_OBJECT` — exact farthest/opposition query relation where declared;
- `COORDINATE_COMPRESSION_VIEW` — lossy embedding/serialization with explicit retained-loss contract.

Full coordinate geometry is **not necessary** merely because a claim asks about neighborhoods. A weaker exact order relation may suffice. Cardinal geometry is not necessary merely because a claim asks about order. The weakest object that carries the claim invariant must be challenged first.

What kind of claim makes geometry genuinely necessary?

- an exact cardinal-distance claim whose magnitude, not only rank, is constitutive;
- a claim about a metric shell or distance-decay relation where replacing cardinal distances with order changes the proposition;
- a downstream model whose declared input is a coordinate representation and whose proposition is explicitly model/representation indexed.

By contrast, exact nearest-neighbor order, adjacency, connectivity, and opposition may be answerable by weaker relation objects. Low-dimensional coordinates are ordinarily a compression/display/model interface, not necessary evidence for the exact empirical relation beneath them.

## H4 — persistent carriers and ephemeral claim/query views are distinct architecture layers

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H4 is strongly strengthened.

The first canary exposes a category error in treating A/B/C as coequal representations. The current bundle mixes:

- source-faithful observation/provenance substrate;
- claim/experiment-scoped exact same-unit state carrier;
- reversible physical encodings / quotients of that carrier;
- newly committed exact relations over the carrier;
- claim-native query operators;
- lossy exploratory/compression views;
- downstream models;
- interface packages that merely co-present prior objects.

These layers have different persistence, authority, loss, and comparison rules. They should not enter one flat `view` registry without a layer/type coordinate.

## H5 — “multiview” should become derivative-family comparison, not peer-picture pluralism

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H5 is strongly strengthened.

The interesting pipeline is closer to:

`carrier -> relation/query derivative families -> claim-specific normalized assertions -> sufficiency/loss court`

than to:

`A picture vs B picture vs C picture`.

A and C answering Q03 exactly is not mysterious cross-view agreement. C is a deliberately narrow projection of information that A retains. B's exact metric relation is likewise computable from the same active states after a new relation contract is introduced.

A future `multiview` comparison should therefore ask at least three different questions separately:

1. **data retention:** what source-state information is still recoverable?
2. **relation commitment:** what new equivalence/order/metric/topology/model relation has been introduced?
3. **claim interface:** what normalized assertion slots can be answered without consulting a stronger object at query time?

Only the third enters claim sufficiency directly. The first two explain why one object may be more reusable, more committal, or more lossy without being more sufficient for the current claim.

## H6 — the first canary is too easy for representation-dependence, but not a failed experiment

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H6 is strengthened.

Q03 almost optimally favors C because its assertion is itself a conditional redistribution interface. That limits what the first canary can teach about representation dependence at the claim layer.

But this is not wasted evidence. The canary successfully falsified a stronger architectural expectation: mandatory multiview execution does not imply multiview necessity. It also exposed the hidden layer structure among A/B/C.

The next experiment class should therefore freeze **invariants first**, not representation names first, and ask whether the weakest lawful object can carry them. If a claim-specific direct composite object beats every richer view, that is a successful result, not an experiment failure.

## H7 — missing hostile fixtures expose a real design gap, but not a new semantic authority gap

`DESIGN_INFERENCE`

H7 is strengthened as an experimental-design issue.

The first canary had hostile propositions whose execution required altered or null input objects that did not exist as separately governed fixtures. That is a real gap whenever a future experiment intends those hostile checks to be part of its validity argument.

However, the gap should not be solved by creating one broad `fixture authority`. A future architecture cycle should distinguish at least:

- `CONTRACT_VIOLATION_FIXTURE`: deliberately malformed/counterfeit input used only to prove fail-closed producer behavior;
- `EMPIRICAL_NULL_FIXTURE`: a lawful permutation/resampling/null transform over genuine evidence, with exact preservation/destruction rules;
- `CLAIM_OR_COMMON_INPUT_VARIANT`: an altered semantic/input object that is no longer the same comparison and therefore requires a new claim/common-input identity rather than masquerading as a hostile replay of the old one.

Candidate classification for the first canary's missing hostiles:

- R2F002 questionnaire-block counterfeit -> primarily `CONTRACT_VIOLATION_FIXTURE`;
- R2F003 respondent-link association null -> `EMPIRICAL_NULL_FIXTURE` if implemented by a preregistered lawful link permutation preserving declared margins; otherwise a contract fixture;
- R2F004 block-removal common-input variant -> ordinarily `CLAIM_OR_COMMON_INPUT_VARIANT` or a producer mismatch/fail-closed fixture, not evidence about robustness of the unchanged Q03 claim;
- R2F016 within-item J null -> primarily `CONTRACT_VIOLATION_FIXTURE` against counterfeit same-respondent J semantics.

`SPECULATIVE_ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS`

A nonauthoritative `EXPERIMENT_FIXTURE_REGISTRATION_RECEIPT` may be worth designing later to freeze exact fixture bytes/generator, invariant-preservation obligations, intended destroyed relation, expected producer disposition, and authority refs. It should not mint semantic truth.

# NEW_DISTINCTIONS_REQUIRED

## D1 — source substrate vs derived carrier

The observation/provenance substrate is lower and more general. A same-unit state carrier already chooses a finite member set, exact coexposure relation, state identity, and often analytic roles. It is therefore derived and bounded even when lossless.

## D2 — semantic/empirical object vs physical encoding

Two byte/storage organizations that are replayably bijective under the same empirical relation are not automatically two views. Row matrices, profile dictionaries, and reversible quotients may be physical encodings of one object.

## D3 — atomic source information vs derived relation information

A metric or order computed deterministically from state values adds no new source observations, but it does add a new analytic relation commitment and a new query interface.

## D4 — computational derivability vs semantic authorization

`CAN_COMPUTE_FROM_CARRIER != AUTHORIZED_TO_ASSERT_RELATION`.

A can contain every value needed to compute Hamming distance without thereby authorizing Hamming as the claim-relevant similarity relation.

## D5 — data completeness vs relation completeness

A carrier may be data-complete for the frozen active state surface while remaining intentionally silent about metric, topology, recurrence, transport, or model structure.

## D6 — claim sufficiency vs information utility vs engineering utility

These are separate courts:

- claim sufficiency: can the object answer the frozen assertion slots within CITC?
- information utility: what additional exact/derived queries become possible?
- engineering utility: storage, speed, interface, compression, model compatibility.

The first canary directly demonstrates that rankings across these courts can differ.

## D7 — exact relation vs lossy realization

Metric/order can be exact while an embedding is lossy. The architecture must not let a convenient realization inherit the evidentiary status of the exact relation it approximates.

## D8 — identical-profile neighborhood vs nonidentical-profile neighborhood

They answer different questions. The former is largely an equivalence-class multiplicity fact; the latter begins to exercise a local relation between distinct states.

## D9 — query operator vs materialized query view

A conditional table may be generated ephemerally or persisted. Persistence is an engineering choice, not a change in empirical semantics.

## D10 — interface co-presence vs fused primitive relation

A package that exposes several existing interfaces together is not a new empirical object. A fusion that creates a new primitive relation must be preregistered as its own AOS under FFIF007.

## D11 — contract fixture vs empirical null vs claim/input variant

Fail-closed testing, empirical falsification, and a changed comparison must not be collapsed into one “hostile fixture” bucket.

## D12 — representation necessity vs relation necessity

A claim may genuinely require an order relation without requiring cardinal geometry, may require cardinal distances without requiring coordinates, and may require topology without requiring any metric. Necessity should be adjudicated at the weakest relation layer.
