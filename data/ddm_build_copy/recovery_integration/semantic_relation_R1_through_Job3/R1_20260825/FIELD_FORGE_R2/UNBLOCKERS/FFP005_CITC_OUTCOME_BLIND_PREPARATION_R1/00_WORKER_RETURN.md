## Outcome-blindness ruling

The CITC must be frozen before any compared-view output or final-holdout comparative result is available to the materiality adjudicator. A threshold may not be moved to induce agreement, rescue a preferred view, sharpen an exciting disagreement, erase a rare branch after observing its prevalence, switch metrics after seeing results, or alter the uncertainty/equivalence regime after comparison.

Any post-freeze amendment creates a new comparison-claim version and requires replay from immutable raw outputs. It may never overwrite the prior court decision.
