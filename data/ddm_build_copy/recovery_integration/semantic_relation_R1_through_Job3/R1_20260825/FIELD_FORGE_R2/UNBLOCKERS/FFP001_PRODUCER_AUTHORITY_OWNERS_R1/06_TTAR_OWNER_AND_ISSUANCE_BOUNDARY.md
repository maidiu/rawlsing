# TTAR owner and issuance boundary

FFIF002 `7043eb0686cac766f19b1741c35de96462f70e1e` establishes TTAR as a typed receipt, not a global equivalence sovereign.

## Jurisdiction by proposition

- exact source-native identity/meaning/order already proved: `SOURCE_NATIVE_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY`;
- finite cross-source/project semantic mapping: `PROJECT_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY` preserving ancestry/loss/scope;
- permission to treat distinct objects as corresponding for a named claim/invariant: `CLAIM_SCOPED_ANALYTIC_AUTHORITY`;
- social-position role: NOT TTAR; PRA;
- empirical materiality/tolerance: NOT TTAR; CITC;
- outcome-blind map selection/test plan: NOT TTAR; transport court.

## Adoption boundary

The **jurisdiction classes** already exist in Rawlsing. The **TTAR contract/schema** is FIELD FORGE exploratory design and is not current merely because FFIF002 passed. Competent project authority must adopt the schema or an equivalent typed receipt before authoritative issuance.

Once adopted, a bounded semantic/measurement court may issue TTARs inside its exact scope. No new global authority office is required merely to write a receipt; a new delegated claim-materiality office is not TTAR's job.

Required immutable return: TTAR with exact endpoints/source-native ancestry, transformation type, semantic assertion, allowed/forbidden uses, loss, claim/invariant scope, evidence/authority refs, replay, invalidation and supersession.
