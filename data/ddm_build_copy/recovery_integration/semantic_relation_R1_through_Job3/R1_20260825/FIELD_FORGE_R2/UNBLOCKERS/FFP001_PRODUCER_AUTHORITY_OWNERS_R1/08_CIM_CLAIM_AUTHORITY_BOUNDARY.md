# CIM / claim authority boundary

FFIF004/006 establish that broad prose is not claim identity and that a `CLAIM_IDENTITY_MANIFEST` binds a normalized empirical proposition plus exact constitutive coordinates.

CIM authority is layered:

1. FIELD FORGE may draft a candidate claim, split broad questions into atomic assertion slots, and mechanically serialize a manifest.
2. Existing operand owners must already authorize measurement/TTAR/PRA/risk-set/estimand/weight semantics used by the claim.
3. A competent `CLAIM_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY` must freeze the normalized proposition, quantifier, domain, invariant bundle and constitutive dependencies.
4. CITC remains separate. Changing a tolerance changes the comparison contract, not the empirical proposition.

Thus creation of a file called CIM is not project authority. Authoritative CIM issuance is a bounded claim-semantic act consuming already-authorized operands. The CIM may not make an operand current, invent a PRA role, or smuggle FIELD/HOMOLOGY/etc into empirical claim identity.

The schema itself remains exploratory until competent project adoption.
