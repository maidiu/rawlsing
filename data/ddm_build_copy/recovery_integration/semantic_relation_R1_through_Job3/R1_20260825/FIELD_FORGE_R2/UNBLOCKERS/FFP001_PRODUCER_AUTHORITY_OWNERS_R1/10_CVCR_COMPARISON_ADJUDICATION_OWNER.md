# CVCR comparison adjudication owner

A CVCR does not need independent semantic sovereignty. It is a post-production adjudication consumer.

Owner class: `COMPARISON_ADJUDICATOR / BOUNDED EXPERIMENT COURT` consuming immutable common-input, CIM, CITC, TTAR/PRA refs, support/uncertainty contract, view contracts and execution evidence.

It may determine agreement, known loss, representation dependence, incremental value, weaker-object sufficiency among the tested/frozen universe, or unresolved comparison exactly as the frozen rules permit.

It may not choose/repair a tolerance, redefine claim identity, create an equivalence/role, change risk set/estimand, or validate an otherwise invalid view because outputs agree.

Independence rule: individual view producers may not self-certify cross-view robustness/non-single-object sufficiency. One separately commissioned comparison worker/court is sufficient; institutional separation beyond the conflict actually prohibited is not required.

Required artifact: `CLAIM_SCOPED_VIEW_COMPARISON_RECEIPT` with all input hashes, validity gates, comparable assertion slots, uncertainty/support results, known-loss explanation, weaker-object test, outcome and ceiling.
