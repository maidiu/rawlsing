# COCR closure certification owner

## Direct answer

`EXISTING_OWNER_CLASS_IDENTIFIED__HOSTILE_INDEPENDENT_PROJECT_METHODOLOGY_AUDIT__CURRENT_COMMISSIONER_UNRESOLVED`

FFIF007 makes COCR explicitly nonauthoritative. What still requires jurisdiction is certification that the claim-to-challenger procedure was complete at the declared finite scope, project-known design capital was searched, known challengers were disposed, no owed admissible blocker remained, and freeze occurred before outcomes.

Current Ground governance already recognizes `HOSTILE_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT`: an independent auditor may rule on its audit question but cannot self-adopt resulting architecture/project state. W54/W55C/W56J demonstrate this separation operationally.

Therefore no new semantic owner is needed. A competent current project commissioner must explicitly authorize a hostile closure audit with access/jurisdiction over the bounded project-known design capital and owed-blocker ledger relevant to the claim.

### Independence

The same worker that produced one or more compared representations may **not** certify COCR closure. That creates the exact omission/self-benefit hazard FFIF007 targets. A worker that did not produce the compared views may perform both challenger census and closure certification if the commission explicitly grants the audit scope and freezes the rules prospectively.

Required returns:

- nonauthoritative `CLAIM_SCOPED_OBJECT_CLOSURE_RECEIPT`;
- authoritative audit/certification receipt stating pass, abstention, blocked challenger, or incomplete closure;
- immutable universe hash and challenger/disposition ledger.
