FIELD FORGE is not a sociology side project and not a dashboard project.

```text

!=

!=

```

```text

!=

!=
```

PRE-V3 should become the thickest lawful actor-independent account of **how the social world presses upon occupants of positions without saying what a particular actor actually becomes**.

# 3. THE ORIGINAL PROBLEM: WHY `STRATA` BECAME SUSPECT

The early intuitive model imagined social life as multiple strata/layers inside other strata, with pressure moving among them.

That intuition captured something real: social formations can be nested. A local religious movement can exist inside a wider religious formation; a household can sit inside a local community; a school can sit inside a national educational order.

But nestedness is only **one kind of relation**.

Real social determination is also cross-cutting, overlapping, field-relative, historically conjunctural, and configuration-dependent.

A canonical hostile case is:

```text
southern Lebanese Shia + poor
```

versus:

```text
southern Lebanese Shia + wealthy + internationally educated + highly mobile
```

The `southern Lebanese Shia` position need not exert the same empirical footprint in both configurations.

And the analogous change from poor -> wealthy/internationally educated among southern Lebanese Sunni respondents need not produce the same redistribution that occurs among Shia respondents.

That defeats a naive additive model:

```text
SHIA_EFFECT
+
SOUTH_EFFECT
+
WEALTH_EFFECT
+
EDUCATION_EFFECT
```

The social meaning/effect of one position may be articulated through the configuration of others.

A second hostile family is local cultural capital:

A repertoire that gives someone enormous symbolic capital inside a suburban youth/peer arena can convert poorly or even negatively into an elite metropolitan/international-school arena.

Therefore:

```text
CAPITAL_VALUE IS FIELD-RELATIVE
```

and:

```text
CAPITAL_CONVERSION
CAN BE POSITIVE, WEAK, ZERO, OR NEGATIVE
```

The resulting R1 move was:

> Demote `STRATA` from master ontology.

A `stratum` may remain a useful derived view when the world really does have layered structure, but Rawlsing should not assume that the social world consists of exactly N vertically nested strata.

R2 now pushes the skepticism one step further:

> Do not automatically replace `STRATA` with `GEOMETRY` and call the problem solved.

Geometry itself can become a hidden ontology machine if its axes are artifacts of questionnaire topology, active-variable choices, missingness treatment, block size, or disconnected coadministration.

# 7. THEORETICAL STACK: USE THEORY AS ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

FIELD FORGE is not trying to become a survey of social theory. A theorist belongs here only when the theory changes how Rawlsing should represent, compute, abstain, or adjudicate.

## 7.1 Pierre Bourdieu — primary relational geometry and field/capital discipline

Bourdieu contributes:

- social space as relational rather than a ladder of labels;
- capital **volume**;
- capital **composition**;
- trajectory / origin-current-position relations;
- field-specific capital;
- capital conversion;
- symbolic valuation/consecration;
- doxa;
- hysteresis;
- homology as a claim requiring evidence, not a synonym for correlation.

Key law:

```text
resource/disposition R
has value V
within arena/field F
at historical moment T
```

Do not encode:

```text
CULTURAL_CAPITAL = 82
```

as though cultural capital had one universal exchange rate.

Two respondents with similar crude SES can occupy very different relational positions:

```text
high economic resources + modest credentials
!=
modest economic resources + high credentials
```

Likewise `middle class` should not begin as a fixed income percentile band. A relationally intermediate position may emerge through capital volume/composition/trajectory and then exhibit distinctive moral/dispositional structure.

Bourdieu also motivates MCA/geometric data analysis, but **does not license MCA by default**. Geometry must earn its estimand and survive Rawlsing-specific exposure, routing, weighting, and instrument-topology tests.

## 7.2 Antonio Gramsci — hegemony/common sense/power distinction

Gramsci prevents:

```text
MAJORITY PREVALENCE = HEGEMONY
```

Hegemony should remain a multidimensional interpreted profile involving some lawful subset of:

- common-sense reach across positions;
- positional diffusion/concentration;
- alignment with high-resource/high-position regions actually observed;
- independently grounded political/institutional power where exact crosswalk authority exists;
- geographic reach;
- temporal persistence where lawful;
- counter-complexes;
- bounded/local dominance.

Hard laws:

```text
HEGEMONIC != MAJORITY
SUBALTERN != MINORITY
```

## 7.3 Stuart Hall — articulation and conjuncture

Hall is an engineering constraint against universal additive social coefficients.

A position's empirical footprint can depend on the configuration in which it is articulated:

```text
sect × geography × education
```

may produce a different disposition topology from the same education movement under a different sect/geography configuration.

Therefore interaction/coupling should be a first-class empirical problem, not a nuisance correction after fitting a universal additive model.

## 7.4 Bernard Lahire — plural socialization and heterogeneous dispositions

Lahire is crucial for Rawlsing's refusal to turn every respondent into one coherent latent ideology.

A person/population position can lawfully contain a repertoire such as:

```text
economically redistributive
+
religiously conservative
+
anti-institutional
+
nationally chauvinist
+
pro-labor
+
tolerant on an unrelated axis
```

Do not treat this automatically as noise needing ideological cleanup.

The empirical object should be capable of preserving contradictory or context-sensitive co-traveling dispositions.

## 7.5 Ann Swidler — repertoires/toolkits

Swidler reinforces the idea that dispositions/resources can be available as repertoires without being continuously activated as one global worldview.

For PRE-V3 this is a warning against projecting every empirically available repertoire element into a realized actor.

## 7.6 Michèle Lamont — symbolic and moral boundaries

Lamont matters for class/middle-position morality, dignity, deservingness, respectability, boundary-making, and non-economic status criteria.

This lets Rawlsing ask whether an intermediate relational position carries recurring moral-boundary complexes rather than assuming that class is only economic resources.

Again:

```text
COMPUTE THE PATTERN BEFORE NAMING THE SOCIOLOGY
```

## 7.7 Raymond Williams — dominant / residual / emergent

Williams is useful for temporal/conjunctural classification of cultural formations and for keeping multiple temporal layers visible.

But labels like `DOMINANT`, `RESIDUAL`, `EMERGENT` require lawful temporal comparability. Do not use them when the observed change may be questionnaire drift or measurement change.

## 7.8 Karl Mannheim — cohort/generation

Mannheim sharpens the difference between cohort location and mere age. Useful only where Rawlsing can distinguish age/cohort/period sufficiently to avoid confounding.

## 7.9 Thornton / Eckert / Willis / Lareau — local capital and socialization as ontology stress tests

These theorists are useful primarily as **constraints on the grammar**, not invitations to acquire youth data.

They help preserve the possibility that:

- local peer arenas possess distinctive stakes;
- capital highly valuable inside one arena can convert poorly elsewhere;
- family/socialization practices alter trajectories;
- resistance/subculture can generate local status without broader field power.

If the existing surveys do not observe a fine-grained child peer field, FIELD FORGE records the limit and leaves realization to later V3 context rather than inventing empirical support.

## 7.10 Crenshaw / relational sociology / assemblage as anti-additivity checks

Intersectionality and relational sociology are useful warnings against treating social categories as independent substances with universal additive effects.

Do not turn them into an unconstrained `everything is a network` ontology.

# 10. DO NOT LET GEOMETRY BECOME THE NEXT STRATA

Historical R1 moved toward dual geometric spaces. That remains powerful design capital.

But R2 correctly asks whether geometry itself is being reified.

## Historical dual-space hypothesis

Construct independently:

```text
POSITION / CAPITAL SPACE
```

and:

```text
DISPOSITION / ATTITUDE SPACE
```

Then link them by respondent identity and examine dependence/homology only after the spaces are independently frozen.

This avoids a circular design in which attitudes define the social position that is then said to explain the attitudes.

## Why this is still attractive

A position space can preserve relational distinctions like capital volume/composition rather than crude scalar SES.

A disposition space can preserve relational attitude complexes rather than force one latent ideology.

Their correspondence can reveal:

- concentration;
- diffusion;
- topology shifts;
- minority repertoire regions;
- position-disposition mismatches;
- configuration-dependent redistribution.

## Why it is dangerous

MCA/GDA/factor methods make design choices that can manufacture apparent ontology:

- active vs supplementary variables;
- active vs passive modalities;
- continuous/ordinal handling;
- rare modality leverage;
- routing/missingness treatment;
- block weights;
- number of variables in each questionnaire module;
- weights/estimands;
- disconnected coadministration.

Hard law:

```text
NUMBER OF QUESTIONS IN A BLOCK
!=
SOCIOLOGICAL IMPORTANCE
```

and:

```text
AXIS 1
!=
ECONOMIC CAPITAL
```

until the actual modalities/contrasts justify such an interpretation.

## More neutral R2 alternative to test

Do not assume geometry is primitive.

First build:

- a lossless `POSITIONAL_CONFIGURATION_ATLAS`;
- a lossless `DISPOSITION_COMPLEX_ATLAS`;
- direct conditional redistribution/dependence maps;

then compare whether a claim-scoped geometric projection preserves useful information or merely prettifies it.

Geometry may become a derived view.

This is an active ideation frontier, not a settled reversal of R1.

# 13. PRESSURE: THE CRITICAL SIMPLIFICATION

The original structural vision risked asking surveys to explain society's causal mechanism.

FIELD FORGE does **not** need to solve the grand sociological `why` before it can serve persona generation.

The surveys can establish a descriptive fact of patterned social determination:

```text
POSITIONAL CONFIGURATION C
is associated with a reproducible redistribution
of probability over DISPOSITION SPACE A
within CONTEXT K
```

Historical R1 called this:

```text
POSITIONAL_PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT
```

R2 currently asks whether even `PRESSURE` is too strong a primitive and whether the neutral object should first be:

```text
CONDITIONAL_REDISTRIBUTION_PROFILE
```

or:

```text
POSITION_DISPOSITION_DEPENDENCE_PROFILE
```

This is a good interrogation.

Preserve the distinction:

```text
EMPIRICAL PRESSURE FOOTPRINT
=
descriptive conditional redistribution / patterned constraint
```

versus:

```text
PRESSURE MECHANISM
=
causal sociological explanation for why that redistribution exists
```

Persona generation primarily needs the first.

Do not infer mechanism merely because a footprint is stable.

# 14. COUPLING / ARTICULATION: THE REAL SUCCESSOR TO `INTER-STRATA PRESSURE`

A major R1 insight was that `inter-pressure` should not mean adding coefficients from separate strata.

The empirical primitive should be capable of saying:

> Position/configuration B changes the disposition redistribution associated with position/configuration A.

Example:

```text
education-associated disposition shift
among southern Lebanese Shia
!=
education-associated disposition shift
among southern Lebanese Sunni
```

This is a **coupling/articulation footprint**.

An interaction term can be evidence for such a pattern, but the interaction coefficient is not the sociology.

Potential object:

```text
COUPLING_PROFILE {
  exact_context,
  configuration_A,
  configuration_B,
  disposition_target_or_complex,
  lawful_reference,
  direct_observed_evidence,
  redistribution_shape,
  sign_or_destination_change,
  uncertainty,
  evidence_status,
  provenance
}
```

Do not assume pairwise coupling is enough. Some socially real configurations may be genuinely higher-order.
