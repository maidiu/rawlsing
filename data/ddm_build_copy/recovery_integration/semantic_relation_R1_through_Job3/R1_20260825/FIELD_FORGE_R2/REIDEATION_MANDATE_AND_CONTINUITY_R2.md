# FIELD FORGE R2 — RE-IDEATION MANDATE AND CONTINUITY

Status: `EXPLORATORY_UNRECONCILED`

This file restores the full FIELD FORGE control-room mandate after PRE-MAIN re-grounding.

## 1. FIELD FORGE has two simultaneous lanes

### Lane A — governed producer / obligation closure

Use current PRE-MAIN law and current carriers to close exact prerequisite seams such as observability, role authority, geometry admissibility, weight estimands, crosswalks, and other inherited obligations. FFX001-B belongs here.

### Lane B — critical re-ideation / engineering interrogation

Continue the original FIELD FORGE work of turning the proposed structural machine inside out. Do not assume that a named object, inherited contract, sociological term, or preferred method deserves to remain a primitive merely because R1 named it. Ask whether the object is actually necessary, whether it can lawfully be generated from the fixed current substrate, whether its producer is specified, whether its semantics have been smuggled into compute, and whether a more neutral/lower-level object would preserve more reality with fewer assumptions.

**Lane A does not supersede Lane B.** An obligation ledger is not the complete ideation frontier. FIELD FORGE must not collapse into a linear implementation-closure campaign.

## 2. Scope firewall: V3 is downstream, not FIELD FORGE's workstream

FIELD FORGE may remain aware of what a downstream realized-person system will eventually need so that PRE-V3 does not emit an impoverished or unusable social-world representation.

But V3/person realization is not FIELD FORGE's active responsibility. In particular, FIELD FORGE does not currently design personality realization, biography, internal disposition-to-disposition topology, Enneagram realization, developmental dynamics, or any other actor-specific machinery.

The only legitimate V3-facing question here is an interface adequacy question: **what actor-independent positioned social-world information must FIELD FORGE/PRE-V3 preserve so a later system can lawfully realize persons without FIELD FORGE itself typing the actor?**

Any historical V3 sidecar is therefore downstream design capital / a consumer constraint, not the active continuation point.

## 3. Fixed-source law survives

No ideal-data sociology. No new-source wishlist. No theory tourism.

Theoretical insight must cash out against existing governed surveys, existing external structural assets already admitted by Rawlsing, and the current semantic/routing/J/fracture/respondent infrastructure. A theory may reveal that something is not observable. That produces a typed observability limit, not a procurement request.

## 4. Current authority first; historical FIELD FORGE as design capital

Current PRE-MAIN authority determines currentness, jurisdiction, carriers, and admissibility. Historical FIELD FORGE R1 remains nonbinding but unusually rich design capital. Its conclusions survive only where current law/evidence does not defeat them.

The 2026-08-30 user-visible transcript preserves the design trajectory but is not literally complete: some long user interventions are represented by bracketed summaries and hidden reasoning/tool calls are absent. Therefore the transcript is trajectory evidence, not a byte-complete substitute for the original room. The R1 00–28 corpus carries forward the resulting architecture and hostile corrections much more fully.

## 5. Restore the original “BUT HOW?” audit

Every proposed FIELD FORGE object or transformation must be attackable with at least these questions:

- What exactly is this object?
- Why does Rawlsing need it rather than a weaker/more neutral object?
- Who or what is allowed to say it exists?
- From what exact current inputs can it be generated?
- How are candidates generated without already assuming the ontology being tested?
- What is deterministic computation allowed to do?
- What semantic judgment must be precomputed/adjudicated instead?
- How does source/context/instrument topology alter the object?
- What positively substantiates it?
- When must Rawlsing abstain?
- What exact typed object is emitted?
- How is it revised, invalidated, replayed, and superseded?
- What hostile/null/control example would falsify the claimed interpretation?
- What downstream consumer gains from the object over a simpler conditional representation?

Useful engineering-state labels remain:
`ENGINEERED_AND_PROVED`, `ENGINEERED_BUT_UNPROVED`, `THEORIZED_BUT_NOT_ENGINEERED`, `NAMED_REQUIREMENT_ONLY`, `MISSING_AUTHORITY_SOURCE`, `MISSING_GENERATIVE_MECHANISM`.

## 7. Current re-ideation hypothesis: geometry may be the next hidden ontology machine

R1 correctly demoted `STRATA` from primitive ontology. But FIELD FORGE must now ask whether it merely moved the same reification one level down into `POSITION_GEOMETRY` / `DISPOSITION_GEOMETRY`.

A more neutral candidate architecture to test is:

`POSITIONAL_CONFIGURATION_ATLAS`
→ `DISPOSITION_COMPLEX_ATLAS`
→ `CONDITIONAL_REDISTRIBUTION_MAP`
→ optional, claim-scoped `GEOMETRIC_VIEW`
→ only then stronger structural interpretation.

Why test this:

- routing and J make the empirical measurement topology conditional and sometimes disconnected;
- position indicators are heterogeneous roles (credential, resource, identity, self-placement, geography, household relation) and are not automatically commensurable coordinates;
- MCA/GDA requires choices about active/passive modalities, block weights, rare modalities, routing/missingness, and continuous/ordinal handling that can manufacture axes;
- higher-order disposition complexes can contain information not reducible to metric nearness;
- a pressure footprint `P(D | C, K)` can be estimated without presuming one global social-space metric.

Geometry remains potentially powerful, especially in the Bourdieusian tradition, but it should have to earn primitive status rather than inherit it from theory.

## 8. Current re-ideation hypothesis: field ↔ capital has a bootstrap/circularity problem

R1 requires differential capital valuation to admit a field and field-specific valuation to call a resource capital. Taken incautiously, this can become:

`FIELD exists because resource R has field-specific value`
while
`R is capital because FIELD values it`.

FIELD FORGE should therefore test neutral precursor objects such as:

- `RELATIONAL_ARENA_CANDIDATE`
- `DIFFERENTIAL_RESOURCE_RETURN_OR_VALUATION_PROFILE`
- `CONVERSION_PATTERN`
- `PARTIAL_AUTONOMY_EVIDENCE`

and allow a later manual structural court to jointly admit `FIELD` and `FIELD_SPECIFIC_CAPITAL` only when independently observable precursors are jointly sufficient. Neither term should be allowed to prove the other by definition.

## 9. Hegemony producer discipline

Survey evidence can potentially support descriptive components such as common-sense reach, positional diffusion, local/default salience, countercomplex density, and differential alignment by observed positions. External structural/political assets may support bounded power-alignment components where exact crosswalk authority exists.

`HEGEMONY` itself should remain an interpreted multidimensional profile/admission, not a synonym for majority prevalence, survey centrality, or elite preference.

## 10. Banking law for future ideation

New conjectures, alternative primitives, hostile examples, and theoretical imports may be banked as FIELD FORGE exploratory design capital. They are not thereby adopted into current Rawlsing authority.

For each consequential ideation pass:
1. ground against actual current source/codebook/carrier surfaces where feasible;
2. distinguish observation from inference from interpretation;
3. record what the pass defeats as well as what it proposes;
4. add new frontier questions rather than silently replacing old ones;
5. preserve typed uncertainty;
6. keep V3 outside the active workstream except as downstream interface pressure.

FIELD FORGE's job is not to make the inherited architecture prettier. It is to discover the strongest feasible social-observatory architecture Rawlsing can actually support from the fixed governed material, and to prevent elegant sociological nouns from becoming unearned machine facts.
