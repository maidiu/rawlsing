For every materially relevant negative lesson record:

1. tempting idea / mechanism;
2. why it originally seemed reasonable;
3. exact observed or argued failure;
4. layer of failure: empirical / authority / ontology / information-loss / stage-jurisdiction / runtime / product-goal / implementation;
5. correction or successor if any;
6. whether the underlying FUNCTION remains useful;
7. present FIELD FORGE concept most at risk of repeating it;
8. exact receipts.
