## 1. What Rawlsing is trying to build

Rawlsing is a governed machine for carrying source-native empirical meaning through exact identity, authority, provenance, composability, learned-world structure, PRE-V3 structural construction, and eventually V3/person-world realization without laundering uncertainty or turning implementation convenience into ontology.

The machine must distinguish, at minimum:

- what producers actually supplied;
- what a source structure semantically means;
- what operations are authorized on that structure;
- what same-respondent or otherwise lawful joint information exists;
- what may be reconstructed versus what must be materialized;
- what belongs to empirical evidence, generated structural positions, learned products, replay state, and realized actor life;
- what is currently binding, merely preserved, exploratory, blocked, historical, superseded only at a grain, or still unknown.

The end-product ambition is larger than a survey compiler. Rawlsing ultimately wants historically intelligible persons situated in material/social worlds without converting population evidence into biography, consciousness, personality or fate. That originary ambition survives as design capital, not current ontology.

The project therefore has two inseparable responsibilities:

1. build a technically exact, authority-preserving machine;
2. preserve the accumulated conceptual memory required to know what that machine is allowed to mean.

A runtime that forgets the second responsibility is not a complete Rawlsing inheritance.
