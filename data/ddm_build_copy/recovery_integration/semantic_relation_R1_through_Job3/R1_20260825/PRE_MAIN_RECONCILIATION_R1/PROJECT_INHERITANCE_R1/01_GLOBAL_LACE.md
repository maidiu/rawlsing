## Anti-collapse rule

Whenever two campaigns use neighboring words, do not assume sameness. Rawlsing's history is largely a history of correcting exactly that error: source structure vs joint configuration; semantic relation vs respondent composability; empirical coordinate vs generated position; population evidence vs actor state; pressure footprint vs mechanism; preservation vs promotion; current vs newest.
