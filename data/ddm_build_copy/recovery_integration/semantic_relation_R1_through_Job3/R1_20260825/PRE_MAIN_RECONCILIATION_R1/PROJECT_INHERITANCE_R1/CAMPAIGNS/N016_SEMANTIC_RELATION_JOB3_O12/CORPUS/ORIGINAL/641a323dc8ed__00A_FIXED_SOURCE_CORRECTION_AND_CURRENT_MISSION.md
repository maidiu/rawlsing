# 2. RAWLSING GOAL, NOT GENERAL SOCIOLOGY

FIELD FORGE is not trying to build a universal sociological explanation engine.

The product goal is a lawful empirical/structural world that improves persona generation.

PRE-V3 needs to know, as far as existing evidence permits:

- what positions/configurations exist in the observed social space;
- what attitudes/dispositions/repertoires co-travel;
- how distributions of those complexes shift across positional configurations;
- what contradictory/minority complexes remain live;
- what position-specific resources/capitals appear to have different value across contexts/fields;
- what attitude complexes have broad common-sense reach versus bounded/local reach;
- what observed position/configuration patterns align with already-known political/power structure where such authority exists;
- what empirical possibility landscape a generated actor may inhabit.

It does **not** have to solve the ultimate causal sociological `why` before it can represent a real empirical pressure footprint.

```text
PRESSURE FOOTPRINT
!=
FULL CAUSAL MECHANISM
```
