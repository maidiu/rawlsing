## Exact engineering destination, conditional on qualified evidence and owner admission

A. **Independent context retrieval**: source-native record selector (release, entity, time, variable/classification, universe, evidence status) → qualified value or negative. Reuse existing source/metadata/authority and publication owners where suitable; no claim every source needs C11 respondent indexing. A country-year join is only context association, never a respondent join. Explicit effective vs publication dates, source-specific boundaries, coverage and uncertainty. Date interval and population mappings require evidential crosswalks; relation types EXACT/SUBSET/SUPERSET/PARTIAL/AGGREGATE/NON_EQUIVALENT/UNKNOWN are conceptual dispositions not new mandatory enum.

B. **Observed material position and conditional disposition**: original35 S01–S05/I03/I05/S04 already owns same-unit observed joint queries. Use ES09/10/11/12/13 to qualify context-specific resource/position distributions and generate questions to existing FF01; ES01–08/14 qualify separately evidenced institutions/group positions. Compare P(disposition configuration | observed position, lawful source/risk/context) between supported contexts. Require compatible estimands and distinguish contextual covariates from jointly measured quantities. A difference is descriptive, not identified causal effect; no individual inference from aggregate proportions. Distinguish distributional calibration of generated positions from actual objective position issuance.

C. **Structural meaning and power**: S06.2, PP26-15–19/21; no invented EmpiricalFact for independent institutional source. Route to competent material/source/semantic issuer for actual position/role/relation, group power, applicable provision or independent constraint. Qualified fact can be retrieved without a strong FIELD/CAPITAL/HEGEMONY/MECHANISM claim. Hegemony remains a multidimensional inquiry: prevalence, cross-position reach, observed high-position alignment, independently supported group-power alignment, geography/time, countercomplexes; do not collapse into scalar. A credible association need not wait for a strong theory; power is not majority or rich tail.

D. **World and possibility**: N6 existing `prev3_objective_position_materializer.py::materialize_objective_position` seals authorized input, does not infer it. Existing M4/M5 publication and selected-world serving, N9 and N10E/N10W/P41/R112 bounded readers are reuse targets; sourcefree_world/nonparent.py existing five-field consumer proves a bounded operation, not general use of new source. First-reader design asks if qualifying an independent relation changes an actual option, cost, sanction, availability or constraint at named reader. Use existing G4 S06.4, S09, S10 and SEAM contracts; actual general applicability still unproved. Some facts may route directly to the world owner rather than through FF comparison. No new household, firm or psychic engine here.

E. **Realized history**: SOROK owns actual resource stocks, counterparts, labor/care/institutional actions, consequences and psychological reconstitution, including separately constructed parent and child. Source-backed position distribution is not actual event; actor receives only enacted exposure. Preserve two entrances, continuing psychology, historical backfill vs actual immature development. No demographic destiny or magical “pressure score”.

## Cross-source question courts (not commissioned implementation)

1. Same measured education/occupation, different wage/own-account/formal employment distribution: ES09/10/11 contextualize independently; do not map survey job title directly to productive asset control. Reader question: independently qualified role and possible employment alternatives.
2. Same household nominal income, different independent assets/financial access and applicable marriage/property/childcare provision: ES08/11/12/09. Distinguish actual control and ability to sustain withdrawal from mere availability; model separate costs and practical alternatives, only on warranted rules. Reader question: existing SOROK world labor/care owner, N6/N9 relevant admitted context.
3. Same reported religion, different group recognition and political access: ES01–07/14. Source-local observed group repertoires and external power alignment remain different; group membership crosswalk must not fabricate respondent identity. Reader question: competing institutionally differentiated opportunities.
4. Same nominal political/civic attitudes, different association/repression conditions: ES14/01/05/07. No individual causal assignment from expert-coded national indicator; use independent legal or material rule only where warranted. Reader question: N9 selected civil possibility, then actual SOROK activity.
5. Historical reconstruction: temporal coverage at each source's own grain, no future leakage to parents' past, no spurious longitudinal respondent and no silently interpolated law. Any modeled estimate explicitly distinct from native observation.
