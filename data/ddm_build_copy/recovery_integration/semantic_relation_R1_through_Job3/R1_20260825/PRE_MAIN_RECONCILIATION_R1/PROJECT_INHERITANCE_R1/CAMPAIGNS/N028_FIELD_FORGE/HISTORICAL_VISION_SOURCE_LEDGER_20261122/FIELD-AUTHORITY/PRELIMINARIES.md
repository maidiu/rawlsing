## 3. What FIELD FORGE is trying to know — the generative question, not a finished taxonomy

FIELD FORGE investigates relations among independently situated people and the arrangements which condition their possibilities. In the survey-internal domain, its crucial empirical object is not a ranked person's ideology, nor a list of population averages. It is the distribution of actual plural and potentially contradictory repertoires across independently measured social positions, actions, relationships and experiences. In the independent material domain it needs knowledge of actual resource conditions, institutions, dependencies, rights and constraints. Their historically specific interrelation matters; one must not be inferred from the other by definition. Relative association with advantaged positions is a useful positive descriptive result even without an external source pack; it is not verified institutional power or hegemony.

Develop the positive questions in parallel. What forms of information could differentiate the *conditions of existence* of persons and groups? What forms could differentiate their *conscious apprehension* of particular parts of those conditions? What additional observations might show how the two meet in lived activity, institutional encounters or anticipated social sanctions? What changes when the object shifts from one's own role to another group, to a specific social relationship, to an institution, a practice, or a possible arrangement? Is a question's actual referent a group of persons, a practice, a right, a proposition, a country, a government, a decision, a relationship or an experience? Do not conflate these.

Some productive analytical directions, not proposed universal ontological slots:

- **Relations rather than mere attributes.** An employee/employer relation, a supervisory chain, a source of household provision, a creditor relation, a relation to a state office, or membership in a collective might locate an actual counterpart and possible dependency. A reported attribute such as age, education or income also matters, but its significance is arena-specific. Determine separately who has resources, who controls them, who can use them, and who decides; only infer what the available question actually distinguishes.
- **Actions, attempts and possibilities.** Actual action, reported repeated practice, unsuccessful attempt, not having tried, stated willingness, refusal, perceived ability and effective opportunity can differ. Look for why an answer alternative carries qualitatively different information. The fact that a source did not ask a question is not a person's negative answer.
- **Subjective relation to the concrete other.** A judgment of women's entitlement to employment is not judgment of all women; approval of a police action is not general police trust; a judgment about the USA's foreign aid is not a judgment of all US persons, nor verified geopolitical intent. Seek the target, relational direction, mode, conditions, scope and response alternatives. Explore how the survey itself structures the question's possible meanings.
- **Social reproduction and history.** Own credential vs parental credential; current job vs former job; respondent vs household vs spouse; presence of children vs childcare provision; origin vs subsequent movement. Historical experience and changes in access matter but do not manufacture a life history from one cross-section.
- **Consciousness of consciousness.** Own view, perceived others' views, perceived expectations, anticipated sanctions, personal encounter, reported general practice and empirically observed distribution are different. Arab8 Q601_3/Q630 is a particularly strong candidate for developing this problem. Ask what additional measures would be needed to distinguish perceived normative climate, misperception, conformity and actual social enforcement. Neither majority opinion nor perceived majority establishes coercive social power.
- **Principle, application, cost, practice and attribution.** An apparently universal norm may coexist with a particularistic allocation rule. Different resource situations may alter perceived capacity to pay; different mechanisms may be understood differently. Preserve possible contradiction without deciding in advance that it is hypocrisy, strategic assent or class consciousness. Explore other domains where the same theoretical question is productive and where the apparent analogy breaks down.
- **Heterogeneity and social reach.** Seek both different AND shared repertoires across independently measured positions; within-position minority alternatives, intersecting position effects, reverse composition, concentration, actual sample reach, context-specific reversal. Distinguish a widespread repertoire from one concentrated near independently evidenced decision-making power. Do not define position using the attitudes that are its explanandum.
- **Perceptual and normative frames not reducible to class.** Gendered, racialized, national, religious, geopolitical and familial relations may have specific institutional histories, relative autonomy and material entanglement. Do not flatten these into either economic rank or disconnected identity labels. Search for actual measures of the relation and separately of its lived and subjective apprehension.

These questions are not a list of required columns. The eventual authority may need multiple roles per measurement, answer-state distinctions, target/link dimensions, operation-dependent limits or a different organization found by the preliminary inquiry. Do not decide its physical form prematurely.

## 4. Intellectual hygiene: how to use our analytical framework productively

The campaign inherits a Marxist, historically situated and non-reductionist analytical orientation. It does not accept questionnaire design as the ultimate ontology of social reality. Nor can a powerful theory make an unavailable observation exist. The distinction is between the **meaning of a measurement**, **theoretically reasoned relevance**, **evidence of a relationship**, and **an interpretation of its social or personal significance**. These can each be useful independently.

Read the existing theoretical synthesis in HOME 02 and source ledger; targeted originals as needed. Project resources include Wright's Classes, Kohn/Schooler's Work and Personality and Kohn/Słomczyński's Social Structure and Self-Direction, Lahire's The Plural Actor, Leontiev's Activity, Consciousness and Personality (Portuguese title in the mounted collection), Holzkamp's Grundlegung der Psychologie, Sève's Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality, Elder's Children of the Great Depression, Luria's Cognitive Development, Dreier's Psychotherapy in Everyday Life, Mortimer's Working and Growing Up in America, Archer's Structure, Agency, and the Internal Conversation, and Bhattacharya's Social Reproduction Theory / Making Death and Life in Palestine. These are source routes, not a claim that their entire contents were newly read in this turn. Use specific passages when a distinction depends on them; preserve differences among theorists rather than synthesize them into a universal formula.

Illustrative questions for theory-guided inquiry (not attributed doctrines or already issued definitions): Does an item distinguish control over productive assets from owning a personal asset? Does education indicate a credential, acquired competence, cultural participation, parental origin, or only the answer to one question? Do work autonomy and managerial authority differ? Is the activity actually performed or merely endorsed? Is a perceived possibility objectively afforded? Are reported views stable dispositions, context-specific responses, or not determinable here? Is the household's material reproduction visible beyond income? Is the relevant institutional consequence different across contexts? Can the concept be contradicted by an actual result? If not, formulate a weaker, empirically answerable question.

Use the five claim levels and typed link distinctions already in README §3. But do not treat that provisional taxonomy as sacred. Develop its precision with actual source cases. An explicit literal referent can be very certain while the bridge to a Marxist concept is contestable; a theoretical link can be strong while the claimed person-level motive is unsupported. These are not a single confidence score. A sufficiently weak warranted proposition should remain available even when its stronger interpretation fails. A real contradiction might be between an institution's promise and operation, between material dependence and a normative stance, between two explicitly incompatible propositions, or between differently situated repertoires. They are not the same sort of contradiction. The source may permit only one of them to be investigated. No inferred personal motive or actual social consequence from an opinion alone.

### 5-B. Preliminary encounter — the position, the encounter, the experienced possibility (2026-11-26)

**Question:** When does an observation locate somebody within a social relation, and when does it describe a particular enactment, apprehension or anticipation of that relation? This is not a search for a third universal variable class between objective and subjective. It changes the question of what a role decision is a decision *about*. A structural relation can be directly reported without being independently verified; a practical event can be objectively consequential while its report is mediated by interpretation; a subjective expectation can be socially situated without being a report of actual opportunity. These distinctions must be conserved before a class of authority is chosen.

**The material and theoretical encounter.** Directly inspected the uploaded Holzkamp, *Grundlegung der Psychologie*, chapter 7.5 discussion (text lines 6140–6145, 6252–6276; printed page markers 372–373 in the latter passage). His distinction between a person's position-specific access to societal conditions and participation in control over those conditions, and his analysis of risking existing practical capacity to extend it, suggest a more exact question than whether someone endorses or rejects authority. What actual alternatives can be realized, what conditions sustain their present capacity, what alternatives are apprehended as possible, and what risks would threaten that capacity? In this account a failure to pursue expanded control can be subjectively intelligible in threatened circumstances. This is theoretical intelligence, not a coding rule for restrictive/generalized agency, class consciousness or motive. Neither accepting authority nor declining protest demonstrates a person's subjective premises. A person's reported readiness may exceed actual opportunity; an actual opportunity may not be apprehended; and an action may be undertaken despite anticipating failure.
