# FIELD FORGE R1 — DISPOSITION REPERTOIRE / JOINT COMPLEX CONTRACT

Status: **EXPLORATORY / NON-BINDING / PROSPECTIVE PRE-V3 PRODUCT**

Rawlsing should not generate adults from a bag of independent attitude probabilities.

The existing semantic, directional, routing, joint, fracture, and higher-order work makes a stronger object feasible:

```text
DISPOSITION_REPERTOIRE
```

A repertoire is a respondent-backed structured set of co-possessed attitudes/dispositions whose internal relations are empirically observed and whose contradictions are preserved rather than normalized away.

---

# 1. CORE LAW

```text
MARGINAL ATTITUDE PROBABILITIES
!=
A PLAUSIBLE WORLDVIEW
```

and:

```text
COHERENT HUMAN
!=
PERFECTLY IDEOLOGICALLY CONSISTENT HUMAN.
```

Rawlsing must preserve empirical contradiction.

---

# 2. INPUTS

A disposition-complex producer may consume only governed respondent-backed empirical objects, including as available:

- semantic authority;
- directional/role authority where relevant;
- routing/applicability;
- same-respondent J2/J3 authority;
- response morphology;
- source-native batteries/structures;
- fracture-map branches;
- co-signature structures;
- minority branches;
- weights/design;
- exact context/scope.

It may not infer co-possession from separate aggregate marginals.

Hard law:

```text
P(A) + P(B)
DOES NOT CREATE
P(A,B).
```

---

# 3. THREE LEVELS OF OBJECT

## A. ATTITUDE ITEM / PROPOSITION

The smallest semantically governed attitude object.

## B. JOINT DISPOSITION COMPLEX

A directly respondent-backed set of attitudes that co-occur with enough exact support to be represented jointly.

Potential examples:

- institutional trust + democratic legitimacy + corruption judgment;
- redistribution + work morality + welfare deservingness;
- religious observance + social conservatism + national identity;
- geopolitical alignment + threat perception + regime attitude.

These examples are illustrative only. Actual complexes must emerge from the governed data.

## C. DISPOSITION REPERTOIRE

A broader profile that may contain multiple complexes, including mutually tensioned or contradictory ones.

A repertoire is not required to be one latent ideology.

---

# 4. HOW COMPLEX CANDIDATES ARE GENERATED

Candidate generation may use compute to nominate patterns such as:

- high same-respondent co-occurrence;
- direct J2/J3 structure;
- stable co-signature across multiple related items;
- recurring fracture-map branch combinations;
- graph/community structure over lawful joint edges;
- source-native batteries with demonstrated joint respondent semantics;
- conditional dependence after preserving exact risk sets.

Candidate generation is descriptive.

The machine may not automatically label a complex:

```text
LIBERAL
CONSERVATIVE
AUTHORITARIAN
RELIGIOUS NATIONALIST
NEOLIBERAL
WORKING CLASS
BOURGEOIS
```

Those are semantic interpretations requiring a separate manual court.

---

# 5. CONTRADICTION IS FIRST-CLASS

Lahire/Swidler-compatible engineering law:

```text
CONTRADICTORY CO-POSSESSION
IS INFORMATION,
NOT NOISE TO BE REGULARIZED AWAY.
```

A respondent group may lawfully co-possess:

- egalitarian economic preferences;
- socially conservative morality;
- anti-elite distrust;
- strong nationalism;
- tolerance on a different social dimension.

Rawlsing should preserve the joint pattern if observed.

Potential object:

```text
CONTRADICTORY_COMPLEX {
    exact_member_refs
    contradiction_relation_refs
    respondent_support
    context
    prevalence
    position_distribution
    provenance
}
```

The term `contradictory` itself may require semantic authority when the contradiction is not purely logical.

---

# 6. COMPLEX IDENTITY

A complex identity must include at least:

```text
member proposition identities
member polarity/direction where applicable
same-respondent authority
exact source/context scope
completion law
risk-set/applicability law
joint configuration semantics
```

A complex with one member changed is not automatically the same complex.

A cross-wave/cross-source successor requires separate equivalence/portability authority.

---

# 7. COMPLEX SUPPORT PROFILE

For each lawful complex, compute:

```text
weighted prevalence
unweighted support
member completion profile
minority variants
position-space distribution
within-complex heterogeneity
fracture sensitivity
context dependence
uncertainty
```

Do not collapse all of this into one `complex_strength` scalar unless a later court proves such a scalar preserves required distinctions.

---

# 8. COMPLEX VARIANTS / FAMILY RESEMBLANCE

Some empirical complexes may exist as related variants rather than one exact universal object.

Example structure:

```text
COMPLEX FAMILY F
    variant A in context K1
    variant B in context K2
    shared core members
    different peripheral members
```

Do not create such a family from semantic similarity alone.

A manual/cross-context equivalence court must determine whether variants genuinely belong to a common structural family.

This lets Rawlsing preserve local articulation while still learning recurring patterns across surveys.

---

# 9. REPERTOIRE OBJECT

Proposed object:

```text
DISPOSITION_REPERTOIRE {
    repertoire_id
    context
    supporting_complex_refs[]
    co_possession_relations[]
    tension_or_contradiction_refs[]
    prevalence_profile
    position_distribution
    minority_variants
    uncertainty
    provenance
    replay_refs
}
```

Important:

```text
REPERTOIRE
!= ACTOR PSYCHE.
```

It is a population-backed possibility structure.

V3 remains responsible for which parts of the repertoire become salient/activated in a particular actor and life context.

---

# 10. POSITION-SPACE PROJECTION

Every complex/repertoire may be projected onto the local position space as a supplementary structure.

Compute:

- prevalence by local region/neighborhood;
- gradients across dimensions;
- concentration and dispersion;
- interaction with exact positional configurations;
- minority pockets;
- local reversals.

This is the bridge to pressure-footprint analysis.

---

# 11. PERSONA GENERATION

For an adult parent with known profile P:

```text
P
-> probabilistic local position
-> candidate disposition-complex distribution
-> sample coherent joint complexes/repertoire
-> preserve empirical contradiction/minority possibility
-> hand to V3/persona realization
```

Do not independently sample each attitude unless the joint relation is genuinely unknown.

When exact joint authority is absent, preserve the epistemic gap rather than counterfeit coherence.

---

# 12. HOSTILE CASES

Reject or flag:

- reconstructing J3 from pairwise margins;
- merging semantically similar but respondent-disjoint batteries;
- forcing every respondent/profile onto one ideology axis;
- deleting minority variants as outliers;
- interpreting co-occurrence as causal mechanism;
- treating source-native grouping as joint authority;
- treating cross-wave similar labels as the same complex without portability authority;
- using imputed respondent combinations as if directly observed without typed distinction.

---

# 13. FIELD FORGE RECOMMENDATION

The disposition side of the observatory should be built from the current J/fracture program rather than from a new unsupervised ideology classifier.

The novel work is primarily:

```text
existing governed empirical relations
-> complex candidate enumeration
-> manual semantic interpretation where needed
-> reusable disposition repertoire objects
```

This is a major area where Rawlsing's expensive precomputed semantic authority can pay off downstream.
