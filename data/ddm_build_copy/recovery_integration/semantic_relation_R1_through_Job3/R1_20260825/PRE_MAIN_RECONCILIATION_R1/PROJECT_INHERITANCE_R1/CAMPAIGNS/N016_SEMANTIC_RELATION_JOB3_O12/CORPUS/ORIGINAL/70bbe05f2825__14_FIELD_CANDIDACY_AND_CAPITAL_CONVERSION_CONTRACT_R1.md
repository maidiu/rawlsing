# FIELD FORGE R1 — FIELD CANDIDACY / CAPITAL CONVERSION CONTRACT

Status: **EXPLORATORY / NON-BINDING / PROSPECTIVE STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION CONTRACT**

The word `field` is too strong to be emitted by ordinary clustering.

Bourdieu's field concept implies a relatively autonomous structured arena with positions, stakes, valued resources/capitals, and conversion relations.

FIELD FORGE therefore treats `FIELD` as an interpreted/admitted object whose candidacy must be supported by a richer empirical pattern.

---

# 1. PRIMARY LAW

```text
CLUSTER != FIELD
TOPIC != FIELD
DEMOGRAPHIC GROUP != FIELD
QUESTION MODULE != FIELD
```

A field candidate must show evidence of **local structured valuation and partial autonomy**.

---

# 2. EMPIRICAL EVIDENCE AVAILABLE FROM EXISTING RAWLSING SOURCES

A field-candidacy packet may combine, where observed:

- local position-space geometry;
- recurrent disposition complexes;
- pressure-footprint topology;
- local symbolic/moral boundaries;
- source-native institutional/organizational position variables;
- identity/region position structure;
- differential value of resources/dispositions across contexts/regions;
- capital-conversion candidates;
- hegemonic/subhegemonic evidence profiles;
- temporal recurrence where comparable.

No new data source is required or permitted by FIELD FORGE.

If these sources cannot substantiate a field, keep the field unresolved.

---

# 3. MINIMUM FIELD-CANDIDACY DIMENSIONS

A strong field candidate should exhibit several of the following:

## A. INTERNAL DIFFERENTIATION

The candidate arena contains recurring relational positions rather than a homogeneous category.

## B. STAKES / DISPOSITIONAL ORGANIZATION

Some disposition/resource distinctions matter systematically within the arena.

## C. DIFFERENTIAL VALUATION

A resource, credential, identity, disposition, or repertoire has a different empirical value inside the candidate arena than outside it.

## D. PARTIAL AUTONOMY

The local geometry/valuation cannot be reduced completely to broader position axes.

## E. REPRODUCIBILITY

The pattern survives reasonable resampling/subscope checks and is not a sparse-cell artifact.

## F. BOUNDARY / ENTRY / MEMBERSHIP STRUCTURE WHERE OBSERVABLE

The survey may reveal organizational membership, identity boundaries, institutional position, geography, or other evidence that constrains who participates in the arena.

Not every field candidate requires direct institutional membership data.

---

# 4. CAPITAL VALUE IS RELATIONAL

Proposed object:

```text
FIELD_SPECIFIC_VALUE_RELATION {
    candidate_field_ref
    resource_or_disposition_ref
    context

    local_position_association
    local_status_or_symbolic_association_if_observed
    disposition_outcome_association
    comparison_outside_field

    valuation_direction
    uncertainty
    evidence_refs
}
```

Do not call something `capital` merely because it predicts an outcome.

The manual interpretation court must determine whether the resource plausibly functions as a valued, convertible advantage within the candidate field.

---

# 5. CAPITAL CONVERSION

A central Rawlsing insight is that one resource bundle can be valuable in one arena and neutral or stigmatized in another.

Proposed object:

```text
CAPITAL_CONVERSION_RELATION {
    resource_or_disposition_ref
    source_field_or_region_ref
    target_field_or_region_ref
    exact_context

    source_value_profile
    target_value_profile

    conversion_direction
    conversion_strength_or_profile
    devaluation_or_stigma_state
    uncertainty

    evidence_refs
    authority_refs
}
```

Possible descriptive states:

```text
POSITIVE_CONVERSION_CANDIDATE
WEAK_CONVERSION_CANDIDATE
NONCONVERSION_CANDIDATE
NEGATIVE_CONVERSION / DEVALUATION_CANDIDATE
ASYMMETRIC_CONVERSION_CANDIDATE
UNOBSERVED
UNRESOLVED
```

These are evidence states, not necessarily final Bourdieusian semantic labels.

---

# 6. HOW EXISTING SURVEYS CAN SUPPORT CONVERSION EVIDENCE

The surveys do not need direct questions asking `does cultural capital convert?`

Conversion evidence can arise when a resource/disposition bundle that is positively associated with status/advantage or dominant disposition in one observed social region is neutral/negative in another.

Examples of feasible observable patterns:

```text
credential level
    strongly associated with complex A among urban professional region
    weakly associated with A in rural/communal region

religious institutional embeddedness
    locally associated with status/trust complex in region F
    negatively associated with high-position complex in region G
```

These remain descriptive conversion candidates until interpreted.

Hard law:

```text
DIFFERENT CONDITIONAL ASSOCIATION
!=
PROVED CAPITAL CONVERSION MECHANISM.
```

---

# 7. SUBCULTURAL / LOCAL CAPITAL

FIELD FORGE must preserve the possibility that:

```text
RESOURCE R
high value in bounded field F
low/negative value in broader field G.
```

This is the engineering generalization of subcultural capital and local symbolic dominance.

The current adult survey corpus may only observe some such relations coarsely.

Where fine-grained local cultural valuation is unobserved:

```text
UNOBSERVED
```

is the correct result.

Do not invent local taste hierarchies.

# 9. MANUAL FIELD ADMISSION COURT

Possible decisions:

```text
ADMIT_FIELD
ADMIT_BOUNDED_FIELD_WITH_LIMITS
FORMATION_OR_ARENA_BUT_NOT_FIELD
POSITION_REGION_ONLY
SUBHEGEMONIC_LOCAL_ARENA
INSUFFICIENT_AUTONOMY
INSUFFICIENT_DIFFERENTIAL_VALUATION
EMPIRICALLY_THIN
UNRESOLVED
REJECT_FIELD_INTERPRETATION
```

A rejected `FIELD` interpretation does not delete the underlying empirical motifs.

The lower-level geometry remains valid.

---

# 10. FIELD VS FORMATION

FIELD FORGE should preserve a broader `FORMATION` concept for historically meaningful social assemblages that may not satisfy field autonomy/valuation requirements.

For example, an identity/political/religious formation may strongly organize pressure footprints without behaving as a relatively autonomous field in the technical Bourdieusian sense.

Therefore:

```text
FORMATION
!= FIELD
```

This distinction prevents theory from overclaiming.

---

# 11. PERSONA-GENERATION VALUE

A field/formation object can inform V3 about the social worlds in which a person's resources/dispositions may be differently valued.

PRE-V3 can expose:

```text
position P
resource bundle R
field/formation context F
expected disposition landscape D
conversion/devaluation possibilities
hegemonic/local-common-sense profile
```

It does not decide whether the actor actually enters F, succeeds in F, recognizes its stakes, or values its rewards.

Those are developmental/biographical questions.

---

# 12. HOSTILE CASES

Reject:

- cluster -> field promotion;
- religion -> religious field by label alone;
- occupation sector -> professional field automatically;
- local prevalence -> symbolic capital;
- predictive coefficient -> capital value;
- interaction coefficient -> conversion mechanism;
- high-income group -> dominant field;
- one questionnaire module -> arena ontology;
- field portability across countries without contextual recourt.

---

# 13. CURRENT RECOMMENDATION

Do **not** build the 553+ ontology around a fixed list of fields yet.

First build enough local position/disposition/pressure evidence to discover whether technically meaningful field candidates repeatedly appear.

The correct sequence is:

```text
EMPIRICAL GEOMETRY
-> DIFFERENTIAL VALUATION / CONVERSION CANDIDATES
-> FIELD CANDIDACY
-> MANUAL ADMISSION
```

not:

```text
THEORY SAYS FIELD X EXISTS
-> FORCE VARIABLES INTO IT.
```
