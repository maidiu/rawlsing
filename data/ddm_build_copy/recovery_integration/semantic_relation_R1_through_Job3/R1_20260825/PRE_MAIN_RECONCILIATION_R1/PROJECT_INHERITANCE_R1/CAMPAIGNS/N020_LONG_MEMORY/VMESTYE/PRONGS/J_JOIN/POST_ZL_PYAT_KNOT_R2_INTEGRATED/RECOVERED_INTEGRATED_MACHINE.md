## 1. The machine's purpose, rather than its file chronology

The old DDM's most consequential capability was not making a set of country attitudes or an Enneagram vector. It was building a potentially reusable **process of empirical inquiry**: select genuinely authorized respondents and item meanings, prepare their observed relations once where possible, discover conditionals and exceptions, preserve their statistical risk sets and permitted uses, and let later stages learn something that no isolated frequency count contains. The later recovery programmes tried to preserve, qualify, or replace parts of that accumulating knowledge while repairing original errors.

Three kinds of intelligence must be conserved *separately*.

- **Computational:** shared source/country/wave respondent frames, wide respondent×variable pivots, ancestor-derived branch masks, prepared joint families, source-specific query caches, checkpoint/replay and prepared query execution. A modern query may produce the correct number but lose the capacity to generate an entire field of comparable numbers economically. Source-static reusable code is not measured faster runtime.
- **Scientific/analytical:** the actual eligible population, questionnaire meaning, selected authority and refusal, category and signed-atom semantics, joint family members, conditional distributions, minority/recursive findings, and the difference among conditional, complement, overlap and baseline denominators. A named `mutual_information` field is not necessarily mathematical mutual information; a statistic's name cannot substitute for its formula.
- **Compositional:** novel knowledge formed by **branching and recombining** earlier evidence: a branch-specific attitude co-travel; an ordered same-person family joined with independently qualified structural evidence; a selected/denied structural fact set; or source-qualified sparse temporal context. A linked schema, provenance hash, source name, or static caller does not alone establish transferred meaning.
