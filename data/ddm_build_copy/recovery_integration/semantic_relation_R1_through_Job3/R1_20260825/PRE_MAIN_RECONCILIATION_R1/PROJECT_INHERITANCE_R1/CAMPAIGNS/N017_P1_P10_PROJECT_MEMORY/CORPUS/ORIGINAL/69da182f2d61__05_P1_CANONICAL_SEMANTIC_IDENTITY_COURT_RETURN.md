# 2. P1 CENTRAL HOLDING

## `P1-HOLDING-01`

The minimum lawful identity layer is **not one undifferentiated content hash**.

Rawlsing must distinguish at least these logically separate identities:

### A. `RELATION_PROPOSITION_IDENTITY`

What typed semantic relation is being proposed/asserted, independent of whether a particular court has yet accepted, rejected or blocked it.

This identity is determined by:

> **family/rule semantic definition + exact relation topology + family-specific constitutive semantic payload + constitutive semantic scope.**

### B. `SEMANTIC_CLAIM_IDENTITY`

For truth-bearing semantic authority:

> `RELATION_PROPOSITION_IDENTITY + semantic assertion polarity/state`

Thus:

- positive T06 claim and independently proven exact-negative T06 claim may concern the same underlying relation proposition;
- they are **not the same semantic claim**.

An `EXACT_CANDIDATE_REJECT` is not a negative semantic claim. It is an adjudication disposition **about** an exact proposed relation proposition.

`BLOCKED` is likewise an unresolved adjudication state, not a semantic truth value.

This preserves G5's state noncollapse.

### C. `GOVERNING_AUTHORITY_INSTANCE_IDENTITY`

Which exact authority/court/evidence version governs that claim at a particular point.

This may change while the semantic claim remains identical.

### D. `LIFECYCLE_CURRENT_SELECTION_IDENTITY`

What exact governed succession/current-selection relation tells Rawlsing which authority instance is current, historical, recertified, corrected, withdrawn, etc.

### E. `CONSUMER_PERMISSION_IDENTITY`

What a downstream consumer is presently allowed to do with the claim.

This may narrow without altering the semantic claim.

## P1 equivalence theorem

Two carriers or authority versions preserve **the same typed semantic claim** iff, after lawful typed canonicalization:

1. they resolve to the same `RELATION_PROPOSITION_IDENTITY`;
2. they carry the same truth-bearing semantic assertion polarity/state;
3. every family-required constitutive qualifier is semantically equal;
4. any differing source/evidence/policy material is proved **non-constitutive of the relation claim** and belongs instead to authority, lifecycle, provenance, or consumer permission;
5. no family-specific meaning has been lost through set-flattening, role erasure, scope erasure, source-boundary erasure or generic JSON normalization.

Different authority IDs, court versions, evidence refs, packaging, filenames or R3 row IDs therefore **do not by themselves imply different semantic claims**.

Conversely, identical authority naming or identical member sets **do not imply claim sameness** if a constitutive role, rule, scope or family-specific semantic dimension differs.

# 6. P1-D — SOURCE / EVIDENCE BINDING LAW

The false dichotomy:

`SOURCE_REF_ALWAYS_CONSTITUTIVE`

versus

`SOURCE_REF_NEVER_CONSTITUTIVE`

is rejected.

P1 freezes four source/evidence classes.

## D1. Constitutive source-semantic binding

A source/routing/wave binding is constitutive when changing it changes **what relation is being asserted**.

Examples:

- T12's exact gate, route predicate, eligibility universe and contingent structure;
- T06 where source-native routing or a qualifier changes which actor/action/target conduct relation exists;
- T10 source-native matrix coordinates where the matrix cell structure defines the hyperedge;
- T15 source-wave/realization scope where the realization claim is explicitly source-bounded.

R3's governing law already requires relation authority to bind exact implementations and preserve source family/wave grain, and T06 specifically requires source/wave plus routing/respondent-universe compatibility.

## D2. Separately governed evidence/authority binding

A source ref is authority-only when replacing it changes **why Rawlsing believes the same relation**, not the relation asserted.

Examples:

- a corrected PDF page locator containing substantively identical authoritative wording;
- a stronger codebook/source document independently re-proving the same exact T06 relation;
- a new court authority re-certifying the same source-semantic alignment;
- a replacement bridge-authority instance that proves exactly the same T15 bridge semantic kind.

Result:

`SEMANTIC_CLAIM_SAME`

`GOVERNING_AUTHORITY_INSTANCE_DIFFERENT`

## D3. Provenance-only source material

Once exact constitutive meaning and governing evidence refs survive elsewhere, the following need not participate in claim identity:

- duplicated source quotation;
- historical worker extraction path;
- page-excerpt convenience text;
- package path;
- reviewer ID;
- confidence labels;
- redundant copies of the same document evidence.

## D4. Prohibited inference material

The following may nominate/search but cannot establish semantic identity:

- lexical similarity;
- embeddings;
- driver/facet coincidence;
- questionnaire proximity;
- empirical co-travel;
- page-level neighboring content.

Recovered law explicitly makes these nomination-only and warns that page excerpts can bleed neighboring questionnaire material into cross-object reasoning.

The hostile fixture corpus independently preserves exactly these failures.

### Source-ref substitution theorem

Changing an exact evidence ref preserves semantic claim identity **only if** a governed court positively proves:

`OLD_REF_AND_NEW_REF_SUPPORT_THE_SAME_CONSTITUTIVE_SEMANTIC_PROJECTION`

Absence of detected difference is not enough.

# 7. P1-E — POLICY IDENTITY LAW

Three policy jurisdictions must remain distinct.

## E1. Semantic-definition policy

This defines what counts as the relation itself.

Examples:

- T06's required actor/target/action alignment;
- whether `STATED_OR_INTENDED_CONDUCT` is distinct from observed behavior;
- T15's manifestation subtype, realization distance or permitted bridge semantics;
- T09 role reversal semantics.

If a semantic-definition policy changes materially:

`SEMANTIC_CLAIM_IDENTITY_CHANGES`

or old/new sameness remains `UNRESOLVED` pending an explicit equivalence theorem.

Its effective semantic definition therefore participates in claim identity.

## E2. Evidence ceiling / prohibited-inference authority

This governs **how far the adjudicated claim may be interpreted**.

Sometimes the restriction simply limits extrapolation:

> do not infer a global trait from a T01 relation.

That does not change the narrow relation itself; it belongs to governing authority/ceiling identity.

Sometimes what appears syntactically as a prohibition actually expresses a definitional boundary:

> stated/intended conduct must not be interpreted as observed conduct.

Then the underlying distinction is part of T06 semantic definition.

The classification must therefore be by semantic function, **not by which historical CSV column contained the sentence**.

## E3. Downstream use-permission policy

This governs whether a consumer may use an already-defined claim for a particular structural/runtime purpose.

It does not ordinarily alter semantic claim identity.

A V557 narrowing therefore changes:

`CONSUMER_PERMISSION`

not automatically:

`SEMANTIC_CLAIM`.

G5 freezes exactly this non-reownership law.

### Policy version theorem

`POLICY_ALWAYS_CLAIM_IDENTITY = FALSE`

`POLICY_NEVER_CLAIM_IDENTITY = FALSE`

Instead:

> a policy version belongs in semantic claim identity iff its effective governed content is constitutive of the relation definition.

Otherwise it belongs to authority ceiling or consumer permission.

### Historical-factoring qualification

A stricter shared current policy is **not** proof that the historical edge-specific policy was losslessly preserved.

G5 requires exact semantic equivalence for policy factoring; stronger policy may be new narrowing, weaker policy is forbidden widening.
