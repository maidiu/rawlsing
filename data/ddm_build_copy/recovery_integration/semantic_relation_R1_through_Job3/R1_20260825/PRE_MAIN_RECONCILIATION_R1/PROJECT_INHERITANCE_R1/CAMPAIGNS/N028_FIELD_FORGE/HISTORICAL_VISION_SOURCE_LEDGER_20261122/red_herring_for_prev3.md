## Context-specific rejected plans are NOT rejected human questions

Bourdieu's questions about dominance, conversion, distinction and class reproduction; Gramsci/Hall's questions about hegemony/articulation; Lahire's plural dispositional action; Lamont's symbolic boundaries; working-class/caste/colonial material history; and feminist social reproduction remain intellectually live. What gets rejected is **false authorization or untested computational equivalence**, not the phenomena.

For example, an employer controlling a shift schedule *can* produce materially stronger constraints on a caregiver than a wage-level category does. That is worth modeling through separately issued role/world rules. It is not permission for Field Forge to replace external material evidence with a clever correlation from a survey.

## Re-entry rule after a red-herring verdict

A rejected universal principle may contain a valid **narrow claim-specific** method, but resurrection requires: original source reread; defined actor-independent question and first consumer; actual current existing function comparison; independently qualified measurement/source/role/estimand; positive and hostile negative tests; distinct competent authority if strong term; owner receiving. Otherwise its verdict here remains **DO NOT BUILD AS FF**.
