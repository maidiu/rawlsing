## A respondent becomes a joint claim only under several independent permissions

Imagine one of the nine bounded survey-family/wave/country configurations — say the GSS 2010/US control. A J configuration specifies several questions that matter **together**, their intended order, response semantics, item roles and governing release. The registry is the *authority gate*, not a discovery catalog that makes every value executable merely because the file exists. A draft release (historical AR-0051) stays a draft. A single local candidate (AR-0052) does not become a tracked original issuer. Historical selected AR-0011 and AR-0053 are meaningful as *selection at their documented historic inventory cut*; their actual application to a specific current result still requires evidence.

The preparatory operation pulls respondent-aligned source rows into a working Parquet carrier. Its cache signatures and manifest integrity matter because a stale response mapping or source snapshot could change what a response means. However, checking that two bytes hashes match **does not issue a scientific license**. A missing cached file might justify a source-qualified rebuild or a stop; it does not mean the empirical cell is zero.

The pattern compiler asks whether **the same respondent** answered the configured items. It groups by `respondent_id`; it does not combine one person's answer to A with another person's answer to B merely because both appear in the same survey wave. The resulting joint signature, joint N/support and response/association status are meaning-producing, not merely plumbing. Unknown response mappings, incomplete coanswers and unsigned directionality are retained as explicit uncertified or nonpublishable states. To demonstrate true modern succession, a respondent identity, item role, risk set, conditioning direction and authority rule must reach the relevant result and its real consumer.

The V551J packet stage does additional work. It matches source item/response identity and signed atoms **at the same scope**, preserves component IDs and order, distinguishes a special context/ambiguous match from a certified relation, checks component denominators and independent authority dimensions, and assigns each component an evidence or certification-contribution role. A permitted *review* lane is not the same as an *ALLOW* certificate; a measured support score is not a permission to infer causal, actor or temporal claims. The original V551J code supports these distinctions, but this documentary commission did not execute it with original respondent data.

The V552J structural bridge then intersects V551J certified/review packets with V552 structural facts on exact source, wave and geographic/shard keys before scoring a structural match. Its published `v552j_structural_join_index.parquet`, `v552j_v553_input_index.parquet` and `v552j_to_v553_handoff_index.parquet` are reduced routing carriers. The two compatibility indexes are copies/renamings of that reduced join; they are not new V551J component-index readers.

## What really happens at the old first reader

The relevant *historical first consuming code* is `ddm_v553_canonical_harmonization.inspect_v552j_canonical_surface` / `build_v553_wave_harmonization`, called by the V553 wave harmonization compiler. It checks producer manifests, expected hashes/row counts and exact scope before accepting the reduced indexes. Those are real fail-closed carrier checks.

What it does **not** do is fetch V551J's ordered component index or its original same-grain certified joint packet to restore the lost fields. This is why Dva's C4R1 review could simultaneously find six legitimate conservations and seven semantic losses:

- The **six** conserved things are scope identity, certified-versus-review lane, direct upstream manifest reference, downstream ALLOW/DENY ceiling, unresolved/block state and reduced-carrier provenance/certification lineage.
- The **seven** lost things are native J semantic identity, same-respondent joint premise, component identity/order/contract, independent authority vector, evidence/certification contribution roles, nonscalar component vectors/lawful scalarization and denominator/context certification.

Neither result is an abstract analogy. The exact historical crosswalk `C4R1-01` through `C4R1-13` names a source carrier, V552J field or absence, V553 consumer, transformation, authority ceiling and disposition for each. `FINDINGS.tsv` uses seven dedicated finding IDs for the positive-loss subset, and `EVIDENCE.tsv` preserves all thirteen outcomes. The crosswalk is outside ZL-01's frozen 32-estate census; it is cited as exact out-of-P0 evidence with open census unjoins, **not invented P0 rows**.

## A modern piece may be valuable without being the old whole

The current O11 chain — N10E empirical possibility provider through C1 exact conditioned composition to F8 — demonstrates a useful mechanism with a **synthetic** same-person 58-row A/B/C/Y fixture. Its reports retain conditional counts 32, 16 and 8 and distinct conditional fractions 9/16, 5/8 and 3/4. It also deliberately rejects wrong order and cross-person composition. Those cases are meaningful, but they did **not** establish that an actual source-native V550J/V551J config is chosen for that caller, or that a non-test reader consumes an equivalent original J product.

The current O14/M1 role machinery can build J family/item-role vectors and validate registration/certification at source-free test granularity. Historical C4-J01 says the mechanism exists, while C4-J02 has only a static callsite/no proved non-test selected invocation. A static search is not proof of global absence, nor is a test invocation evidence that signed original source data flowed through the protected product. The modern structural-admission API has real rule checking, but its existence is not a demonstrated recreation of the discarded same-grain V551J authority vector.

It would be as mistaken to declare the entire old J chain still required as to declare it obsolete solely from these APIs. The correct disposition is: keep the actual function/operation and exact negative, identify candidate owners, and require an evidence-bearing comparison of the two meanings at the *same grain* with observed use.

## Nine scopes, not all survey waves

The old canary had nine named configurations, one per family/wave/country, with 26 configured items. The historical Section85/Dva return said the **required positive original-native load was unproved for all nine**; the respondent-level archive was not present in the source package and a live database/PyArrow execution was not performed. Thus `0/9 PROVED` refers to evidence coverage, not a claim that the surveys contained zero records.

Especially do not turn that bounded canary into an Archive08/all-wave grant. A survey source can change variables, translation, missing-value conventions, geography, sampling, weighting, rights or item order across waves. A legitimate all-wave J release needs separately specified source/wave rights and role/response/denominator provenance. The commission also names 2,499 raw structures and 15,990 memberships, 99 routing predicates and 600 absence checks; they remain commission-reported source-denominator/routing/absence registers until their exact original sources and executable receipts are supplied, not tests that ZL-03 ran.

## What a future receiver must see before any "successor" declaration

A genuinely adequate proof for one J function would identify:

1. **Input:** immutable source/cut, respondent/file scope, item/response keys, selected authority issuer, release, rights and the P0 source five-tuple.
2. **Meaning-producing operation:** same-person grouping or component/vector/structural matching at exact source grain, including the rule's applicability and actual selection.
3. **Output and refusal:** the normal certified output **and** typed absence, ambiguity, context, review, denial or nonpublishability behavior; a score is not a signed vector.
4. **First actual reader:** the specific current non-test consumer, exact version, field/denominator it dereferences, and the decision or behavior the output changes.
5. **Consequence:** qualified comparison to the original, or an explicitly issued lawful retirement/replacement of that function. Neither a hash nor a name match suffices.

A missing link is a row in `UNJOINS.tsv`, not a hidden assumption. The five core historical function source IDs and their known C6 release overlays remain row-wise joined to the accepted P0 census, while original code/Dva crosswalk sources have a separate typed `UNRESOLVED` census relation. The current candidate APIs are source-read at the protected cut but not promoted as completed equivalence. No S2/S3 was invoked by ZL-03.
