## 1. The compact answer

Trii correctly insisted that Rawlsing must be capable of answering exact empirical questions, including same-respondent combinations, with correct source/routing/topology/measure identity, authority selection, typed negative answers, replay and eventually governed discovery and learned-world publication. Trii's D15 also chose a **persistent canonical normalized event carrier** as a physical strategy. Chetiri challenged that last physical necessity. It did *not* challenge the first semantic obligations.

A respondent source snapshot may yield an exact raw physical value again when the source bytes, decoder/version and native coordinates are pinned. But a value does not explain what the question **means**, how a repeated slot maps to a canonical occurrence, who belongs to a risk set, which weight or estimand is legally selected, which source universes may be compared, or why a learned release was admitted. Those require governed authority/occurrence/topology/routing/replay material independent of raw bytes. That is why Chetiri's conclusion is neither "save every transformed event forever" nor "just keep SAV/DTA and discard everything else."

This is captured by the original G0 adjudication: [source-backed raw payload and exact named side material](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/797b358652b321a363d98f9d51c07c47c61d38db/data/ddm_build_copy/vozhd_trii/20260820_pre553_empirical_compiler_activation/SUBSTRATE_RECONSTRUCTION/VOZHD_CHETIRI_COMPILER_PERSISTENCE/G0_C02R2_HQ_ADJUDICATION/G0_FINAL_ADJUDICATION.md). Its historical executor reported 35/35 exact-source decodes, 887,232 source-row coordinates, 18,413 native variable identities, 18 native multiple-response sets and 250 members. The WVS7 equal-value/different-slot witness shows that dropping source slot identity destroys occurrence meaning. These are **historical tested source-native reconstruction facts**, not this commission's S3 execution, not C11 all35 release, not production I05 and not a current whole-machine proof.
