# FOR DOWNSTREAM FROM FIELD FORGE — OWNER-PRESERVING TRANSFER HOME

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL DISPOSITION / SOURCE-STATIC` · **Date:** 2026-11-23. This is not a commission or a proposal for Field Forge to implement all the mechanisms below.

## The dividing line

**FIELD FORGE owns lawful actor-independent observed and claim-qualified relational evidence and the capability to reconstruct it.** Downstream systems own *what actually exists in a selected world*, the meaning and temporal force of independently admitted relations, specific alternatives/actions, psychological response, performed world consequence, acquisition and continuing life. Field Forge can produce a qualified contrast relevant to an actor; it must not generate the actor's thought, practice, world-state writer or biographical history.

**R2 law:** [Global HQ adoption](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/096b419ed8fd3d07c87cb74bcc4890c2f1a8ec54/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/GLOBAL_HQ_FIELD_FORGE_R2_TERMINAL_ACCEPTANCE_R1/00_GLOBAL_HQ_ACCEPTANCE_RETURN.md) explicitly keeps FF actor-independent; names strong FIELD/CAPITAL/HEGEMONY/HOMOLOGY/MECHANISM admissions HELD where competent issuer does not exist; preserves separate V553–V558/V3 jurisdictions. A downstream owner being conceptually attractive does **not** establish an authorized implemented mechanism.

## II. Transfer contract: what passes the seam

A valid output to the next owner is **claim-specific**, not a fixed Field Forge master-packet. Its minimum supported components, each only if genuinely applicable:

1. **Exact evidence:** source family, wave, population, respondent/source index, raw measurement roles, risk/asked/eligible/recorded and coadministration, weights and relevant rights; immutable producer references.
2. **Relational answer:** exact observed conditional/joint profile or separately authorized structural relation; target, source/position conditioning, direction, comparisons, effective denominator, support and confidence/limitations.
3. **Heterogeneity:** majority and rare genuine configurations, exceptions, paradoxes, conditioned splits, n-way interactions, lawful absence/noncomparability and backoff.
4. **Authority grade:** `OBSERVED`, `SOURCEFREE/HYPOTHETICAL`, `MODEL_ESTIMATE`, `STRUCTURAL_ADMITTED`, `HELD`, `NOT_COMPARABLE`, plus typed denials. These labels here are conceptual categories, **not a proposal to silently add enums to the current ABI**.
5. **Scope and temporal compatibility:** source/design/time/location/participant boundaries, whether transported across contexts, and a refusal to treat cross-sections as the same individual.
6. **Typed reconstruction:** query/relation ID, reproducible dependence roots, exact version/source/permissions and enough information for the demanded first consumer to re-query or cold replay.

The receiving owner returns *its own* evidence of admitted use. If N9/SOROK cannot apply a given rule, FF cannot repair the gap by fabricating a persuasive vignette or an additional memory system.

## III. Specific false ownership transfers to forbid

- FF observed `P(Y|D)` **does not** issue `Y` as a particular parent/Nadia's worldview, personality or history.
- FF observed gender/sect/class association **does not** issue an individual's actual control of property, authority or means of production.
- FF higher-order association **does not** issue a social mechanism or absorbed psychic contradiction.
- FF geometric proximity **does not** issue the lawful substitute respondent or matching historical culture.
- FF class/field/hegemony profiling **does not** issue a police sanction, rent, job termination or care-hour obligation.
- FF context source **does not** issue an experiential memory, acquired PRACTICE, unconscious motive or practical analogy.
- FF reconstructed past context **does not** authorize retrospective invention of private motives or institutional facts.
- FF sourcefree canary **does not** issue original35 survey/science credibility.
- FF model of a user/visitor **does not** issue the real human's interiority or a finished social relationship.

## IV. Returned downstream questions that *can* change upstream Field Forge

The boundary is not an excuse to starve downstream. Existing first consumers may require FF to recover a source-backed distinction they cannot lawfully reconstruct themselves:

- Does the current source contain the three-way (rather than only pairwise) response configuration that would distinguish two materially different parent-context possibilities?
- Is a minority configuration positively supported and actually eligible, rather than an imputation or false zero?
- Can a position-specific social rule be supported from separate objective evidence, or is all we have a survey self-label?
- Are two time periods genuinely comparable in respondent/source/semantic terms, or must N9 keep them separate?
- Is a sourcefree structural input clearly hypothetical, or is a supposedly native rule smuggling unsourced local world facts?
- Does the actual N9/N10W/SOROK reader receive changed substantive value when a qualified FF observation changes, or merely a renamed hash?

**Only a demonstrated first-reader starvation** should justify extending a retained FF producer or creating a stronger retained claim-specific surface under R2. The rest remains with the actual downstream owner.
