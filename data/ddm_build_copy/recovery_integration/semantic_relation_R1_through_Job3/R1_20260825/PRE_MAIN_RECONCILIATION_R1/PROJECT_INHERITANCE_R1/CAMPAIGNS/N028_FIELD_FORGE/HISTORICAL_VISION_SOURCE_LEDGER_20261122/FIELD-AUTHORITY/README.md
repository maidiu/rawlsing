## 3. The central methodological law: relation relevance is not a person inference

The user's Marxist framework is an explicit analytical resource (relations of production/reproduction, control and dependency, material constraints, class contradictions; enrich with existing Wright, Bourdieu/Lahire, activity, institutional and life-course insights), not an invisible source fact or a doctrine that predetermines survey findings. Distinguish five claims:

1. **Native observation:** respondent selected this exact response to this exact administered question (or the file reports a condition); source native domain and unit/risk.
2. **Literal measurement interpretation:** what is expressly asked/asserted, referent, mode (report, evaluation, norm, perception, practice, hypothetical choice, self-placement), scope, alternatives and polarity. Source-supported only.
3. **Analytical relation relevance:** explicit named theoretical reason the measurement bears on a social relation. This can be highly defensible even when the item does not measure the relation directly; record framework, bridge, rival interpretations and limits. A link is not a verdict about a respondent. No source-source equivalence.
4. **Empirical association:** supported observed joint or contrast across independently measured position and disposition, same respondent/risk/measurement ancestry; support, uncertainty, weights and comparison grade. No causal conclusion or hidden group essence.
5. **Strong structural/causal/person interpretation:** separately qualified claim about institutional command, exploitation, material interests, legitimation function, counterhegemony, durable disposition, conduct, motive or psychological formation. Require relevant independent evidence, actual operational definition, competing explanations, test and competent authorization. Otherwise HYPOTHESIS/UNRESOLVED, never implicit in 1–4.

Keep relation-target links typed: DIRECT_REFERENT (relation explicitly named), EXPLICIT_APPLICATION (norm/scenario about relation), FRAMEWORK_RELEVANCE (explicit bounded analytical interpretation), HYPOTHESIS_TO_TEST (not admitted semantic association), NO_SUPPORTED_LINK / UNRESOLVED. Multiple links permitted; do not force all questions to be 'about' a prechosen class relation. For each link record the **weakest warranted statement** and the **stronger tempting but forbidden inference**, rival explanations and required evidence for strengthening. Exact response-option scope where meaning varies.

Examples (provisional, source-local):
- WVS7 Q107 private vs state business ownership: direct policy referent, an evaluative response about ownership arrangements. A Marxist analysis can examine its correspondence to ownership relations; the answer is not proof of ownership, capitalist class interest, exploitation, endorsement of every private-property relation or even a fully formed political ideology.
- WVS7 Q33 gender employment priority: explicit normative allocation under narrated scarcity. Framework relevance to gendered division of labour and social reproduction. Neither actual labour-market exclusion nor respondent's caregiving arrangement, behavior, motive or general patriarchy is measured. Q34 national priority analogous; citizenship/migration status and employer actual hiring must be separately observed.
- LiTS IV q310=4: reported self-employed with employees, a meaningful productive-relation indicator. Not necessarily control of productive assets, comprehensive bourgeois location or institutional power. q401f and q405e may reveal principle/cost tension; specific tax mechanism, hypocrisy, self-interest and actual sacrifice remain unwarranted.
- EVS5 v84 care duty: normative filial responsibility; relevant to reproduction relations but no observed hours, provision or gendered caregiver identity.
- GSS2010 class: self-placement, not objective class input. Religious affiliation is position/identity where relevant, belief is disposition, attendance is reported practice; same active ancestry cannot define both spaces.
- Afro9 Q6E: reported household deprivation event/frequency; not a pure income-level measurement. Asian6 q103: belief about government treatment, not independent evidence of equal treatment. WVS7 Q206: information use, not access or modernity.
- LiTS parental education: origin vs own credential; derived labels conflict with native referent. Hold dependent authority until exact source resolution; do not silently correct all downstream records or infer current C11 is affected.
