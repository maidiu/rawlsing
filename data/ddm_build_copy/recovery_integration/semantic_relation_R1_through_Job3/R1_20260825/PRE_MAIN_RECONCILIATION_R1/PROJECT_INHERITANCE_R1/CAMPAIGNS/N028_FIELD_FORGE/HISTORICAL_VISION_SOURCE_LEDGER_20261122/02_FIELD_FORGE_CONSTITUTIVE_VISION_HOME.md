## I. The single fundamental problem Field Forge must actually solve

**The world that conditions a future person is neither a list of demographic attributes nor an already completed personality.** It consists of objective and social positions, distributed material resources, institutions, enforceable permissions and sanctions, shared practices, measured orientations, competing coherent attitude configurations, historical situations and evolving material conflicts. Our available surveys show fragments of people's positions and dispositions at historically specific moments. They rarely directly record the full material control relations or an individual's lifetime.

If downstream SOROK is to generate a parent and, eventually, Nadia, **what does the observed and explicitly hypothesized social world make possible, likely, available, expensive, risky, attractive, blocked or contradictory, for which kinds of socially situated positions—and under what qualification of evidence?**

The terminal questions, repeatedly expressed in G4, are:

1. **What is possible?** Distinguish real source-observed/structurally admitted/hypothetical/unknown alternatives, not just modal outcomes.
2. **What is facilitated?** Measure/qualify context-specific opportunity, support, conditional co-travel and material enabling relations—without making a predicted attitude into a person's desire.
3. **What is inhibited, repressed or prohibited?** Differentiate statistical scarcity, an institutionally enforceable prohibition, an economic cost and a person's later internal inhibition.
4. **By which forces or powers?** Use actual actors/institutions/ownership/authorization/mediating structures when separately sourced; do not infer power from prevalence or identity labels.
5. **In contradiction with what other tendencies?** Preserve empirical counterpositions, minority co-travel, conflicts among material requirements, and nonadditive context effects without a single moral or class scalar.
6. **Under what changes of conditions might these relations shift?** Field Forge answers the *counterposed evidence/conditioned relations*; downstream N9/SOROK executes the actual counterfactual world change and historical activity.

**The constitutive unit is a source- and claim-qualified answer or typed relation**, reconstructable from its competent producer, not a mandatory one-size-fits-all population object, graph or static manifold. A future person is a first consumer of those conditions, **not** their statistical realization.

## III. The *constitution*: minimum R2 grammar that every richer vision must obey

[The adopted R2 act](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/096b419ed8fd3d07c87cb74bcc4890c2f1a8ec54/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/GLOBAL_HQ_FIELD_FORGE_R2_TERMINAL_ACCEPTANCE_R1/00_GLOBAL_HQ_ACCEPTANCE_RETURN.md) and [protected capsule](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/827714e83743056939894a39736d72ea16bdc6dd/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/PROJECT_INHERITANCE_R1/FIELD_FORGE_R2_ADOPTED_AUTHORITY_R1/01_ADOPTED_AUTHORITY_CAPSULE.md) require:

1. **Producer-owned exact information** remains accessible by lawful dereference or materialization; Field Forge never copies raw data to claim the source's authority as its own.
2. **An explicit typed actor-independent claim/relation or query identity** is declared, with exact source/measurement/unit/scope/risk set, applicable permission and negative state.
3. **Immutable provenance closure and deterministic replay** bind any relation to what was actually observed; a source query can remain authoritative without a newly stored universal FF carrier.
4. **Representation follows the claim.** Identity, order, distance, topology, embedding, conditional probability and structural mechanism demand different evidence. More coordinates are not automatically more truth.
5. **Negative evidence is primary data.** Observed zero, not exposed, nonapplicable, unresolved, refused, unsupported, incompatible, and no certified causal meaning are not one interchangeable FALSE.
6. **Cross-context transport is a separate operation.** `Local_A + Local_B + authorized typed map + comparator + falsifiers`, or `NO_TRANSPORT`; no fixed world coordinate system.
7. **Strong sociological names require a separately competent issuer.** FIELD, FIELD_SPECIFIC_CAPITAL, HEGEMONY/SUBHEGEMONY, HOMOLOGY, CONVERSION, PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT beyond neutral association, PRESSURE_PATHWAY and STRUCTURAL_MECHANISM remain `BLOCKED_EXPLICIT_AUTHORITY` where such authority is absent. The social phenomenon is not declared unreal.
8. **Absolute actor boundary.** A source-population relation can condition a fictive actor's possibilities but never itself issue beliefs, memories, motive, childhood facts, resistance or an acquired psychic organization.
9. **Physical implementation is semantically subordinate.** Query/virtual, persistent, index, reduced sufficient statistic or hybrid carriers are legal alternatives; require changed downstream value and resource measurements before selecting one.

These constraints are not a bureaucratic tax on the vision. They prevent the model from manufacturing a world in which the scientific label becomes truer than the people and institutions it purportedly describes.

## IV. The empirical observatory: preserve exactly what respondents genuinely share

### IV.1. Source capability is not respondent exposure

The original 2026 N028 source stress pass ([E031](00_VISION_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PREIMPLEMENTATION_SOURCE_LEDGER.md), Arab Barometer VIII example) establishes at least these distinct questions:

- Is measurement X in this source/wave/country/form at all?
- Was respondent i **eligible** and **shown** X under routing, random split and prior answer constraints?
- Was X answered; how many answers were permitted; what is the source-native value?
- Can the same respondent legitimately possess X and Y (and Z) for this claim?
- Is nonresponse an observed refusal, don't-know, user-missing, not asked, structurally nonapplicable, or authority unresolved?
- Does the source authorize using this sample/weight/design for this *particular estimand*?

The Arab Barometer VIII questionnaire mixes randomized section order, split forms, country versions, select-all, answer-piped follow-ups and branch-exposure predicates. **A data column being present does not make it a measure for every record.**

An FF observable joint is not all columns with matching names; it is a source-qualified intersection of actual respondent exposure and valid *shared* risk sets. Its representational topology can be disconnected: no amount of imputation automatically makes absent coadministration real.

### IV.2. Complete coherent repertoires precede typology

A genuine k-way observed respondent configuration may combine apparent ideological opposites: social welfare support, electoral abstention, low state trust and obligations to religious kin. The empirical object is the *entire observed combination and its conditional support*, not a generic "collectivist", "religious", "alienated" or "Type 6" latent property.

Field Forge should be able to answer, without factorizing away correlations:

- What configurations of Y1…Yk are actually measured together and observed in context C?
- Which conditioning dimensions materially redistribute `P(Y1,…,Yk|C)`, and which do not?
- What is the majority configuration? What are the supported minority co-travel alternatives and unusual counterpositioned combinations?
- What does a targeted recursive subdivision reveal, and does its full joint change or merely its marginal frequencies?
- What is an exact source-local comparison baseline and where is support absent?
- What observed exceptions are stable enough to merit follow-up, and where must inference be withheld?

**No uniform forced significance or causal interpretation.** The full joint, raw positives, exact denominator and feasible bounded re-query are valuable even before a source-specific analytic statistical court issues population interval estimates.

### IV.3. The population is not one person

The F5 observatory supplies population-configured *possibility*. It does not supply a preordained psychological subject. Multiple legitimate outcomes can exist at a given social position; the minority outcome can be the only historically plausible one for a particular fictional actor after independent individual history is constructed. A raw 60% answer does not make that person 60% committed.

There is an asymmetry: survey members may share an observed multiway profile *as respondents*; fictional parent and partner must not be assumed jointly co-drawn from a survey that never measured the same household, and cross-sectional waves are not their mutual childhood.

## V. Position without fetishizing variables: the independent social-position side

**Question:** Where is the respondent/population configuration *relationally* situated, and how does that constrain the distribution of orientations without turning either into a personality attribute?

N028's [Position Role Authority](../CORPUS/EXPLORATORY/f154bcad631b__09_POSITION_ROLE_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT_R1.md) proposes *claim-relative roles*, not intrinsic global variable identities. Its nine categories include:

- active position coordinate;
- candidate resource/capital proxy;
- relational identity position;
- trajectory/origin indicator;
- supplementary position descriptor;
- attitude/disposition-only;
- context/scope-only;
- administrative/ineligible;
- unresolved role.

A variable can have more than one valid **candidate interpretation** for different scientific claims. But at a specific comparison it cannot be simultaneously treated as an active position variable and an active disposition variable and then "discover" their homology. **Multi-role authority needs alternate builds, not double counting.**

Operationally retain source-local and context-specific position variables such as resource availability/deprivation, employment/occupation, education/credential, household reproduction, region/urbanity, religious/ethnic/caste/sectarian community as *relational position* where the instrument measures it—and objective property/control/institutional authority only when independently warranted. An ideological or moral attitude is not objective class.

**Crucial distinction:** the source's reported "middle class", occupation or income is a measured response/status claim. It is not a complete control-of-assets, rent/land, productive power or labor dependency relation. N6 separately qualified objective positions must be *derived from real independent evidence*, not constructed by naming an FF geometry "class".

The ontology can be a plurality of position views in an underobserved survey: economic resources, occupational relations, credentialed education, relational identities and household position. Do not force absent dimensions into a single fully populated social space.

## VI. Disposition without a magic ideology axis: an atlas of coadministrable relations

Original N028 hypothesized a rich local disposition geometry, but the later codebook stress test substantially corrected it:

1. Govern *coadministration topology* first; only risk-set connected measurements can legitimately inhabit a common metric.
2. Source-native questionnaire blocks, analytic-role blocks and same-respondent exposure blocks are three distinct things. Module size is not sociological importance.
3. Distinguish **exact joint repertoire** (the observed combinations) from **optional disposition geometry** (derived shape/position of variables/people under a specified estimand). They do not replace one another.
4. Some survey contexts produce several disconnected *local* disposition atlases and cannot be forced into one universal, complete ideological manifold.
5. Sparse/rare modalities must not disappear through majority smoothing merely because they complicate a neat embedding.
6. Projection into a measured dimension is not license to infer a psychologically acquired intention, especially for children or people outside the original survey risk set.

Where the joint is already sufficient, reconstruct it directly via I03/S04/FF01. Geometry is method-contingent: compare with an exact conditional baseline, and admit only a typed additional consumer value.

## VII. Local geometry is a possible method, not the constitutional object

A source may support MCA/GDA, categorical MDS, partial supplementarity or several linked local views. The historical FFX B implementation already calculates local distances, embeddings, neighborhoods, stress and jackknife stability. **That concrete existing code is to be reused before proposing another.**

Geometry requires a named **representation target/estimand**:

- Does it represent actual sampled respondent distribution, a target population under a purpose-specific weight, an ordinal relation, or source-limited categories?
- Which variables are active or merely projected supplementary?
- Are rare modalities an intended sociological signal or an accidental leverage point?
- Does one question battery dominate because it is large, not because the world gives it more weight?
- Are continuous/ordinal measurements discretized? How is information lost and the choice justified?
- Are source/country/risk-set/time comparable in this local view?
- How much reconstruction stress and bootstrap/leave-one-out instability attends a proposed neighborhood?
- Are design or calibration factors already associated with active position inputs, thus contaminating interpreted axes?

The original R1 vision correctly demanded **separate position and disposition spaces** before any claim of homology. The later E032 stress pass adds a stronger identification law: construct both independently; record active input ancestry; link later by exact respondent identity; run lawful permutation/cross-fit and measurement-regime sensitivity. A supervised method maximizing position↔disposition correspondence may be useful exploratory mathematics, but it is not independent evidence that the social world itself exhibits the discovered homology.

If sources lack an identifiable common metric, **keep separate objects and report no comparison**. Strong HOMOLOGY remains unissued absent its own authority.

## VIII. Empirical pressure: reconfiguration is observable; causation is not magically supplied

Here the original N028 vision is most potent and most easily abused.

### VIII.1. Neutral footprint

Given source-admitted position configuration `C`, disposition-repertoire joint `Y` and lawful comparison `C'`, calculate:

`p_C(y) = n(C,Y=y) / n(C)`, `p_C'(y) = n(C',Y=y) / n(C')`,

under the actual appropriate survey design/weight laws if authorized. Preserve the full vector `{p_C(y)}`, support and nonpositive states, not only an averaged attitude scalar. A descriptive contrast `Δ_C,C'(y) = p_C(y) − p_C'(y)` is a useful **distributional footprint**.

Candidate optional diagnostics include total variation, Jensen–Shannon distance, conditional mutual information, log-linear n-way terms and profile shifts across position-neighborhoods. None by itself proves coercive exposure, a school or family causal mediation, or that a person "internalized" the group's politics.

For instance, compare two employment-control positions with equal incomes. If source self-report does not record actual productive control, the direct descriptive **C must remain source-scoped and noncausal**; an independently admitted employer role may then change an N9 model's opportunities *without rewriting the measured survey distribution*.

### VIII.2. Nonadditivity / articulation

The position world is interactive. The distribution under race/sect, region, employment, household and institutional experience can change in ways that **cannot be reconstructed as the sum of isolated factor main effects**. A crucial question is whether `P(Y | A,B)` differs from what the available `P(Y|A)` and `P(Y|B)` would predict, *under the true same-person risk set*.

The method must preserve:

- exact higher-order cells and their denominators;
- reconfiguration that disappears when one condition is omitted;
- minority/counterpattern trajectories within a composite context;
- conditional reversals and competing cohorts;
- asymmetry of material power *only when sourced*, not inferred from a symmetric statistical interaction;
- nonidentification when the needed J combination was not actually administered together.

This is empirical **coupling** as a neutral typed statistic. An actor-independent "articulation" label or causal "buffer" requires additional theory/court. The reader can ask the stronger question without the answer being forged.

### VIII.3. Identity and social pressure do not coincide

Two people occupying similar reported income and education positions can confront very different enforceable terms: citizen versus precarious worker, landlord versus tenant, employer versus wage-dependent worker, kin-supported versus care-isolated parent, legally protected versus excluded minoritized group. **Income score is not power.** A meaningful full machine needs a pathway for *independently admitted material institutions and group relations* to qualify downstream opportunities—not an FF classifier that reads a demographic tuple and asserts a class destiny.

## IX. The political-economic and hegemonic problem

The early N028 R1 corpus makes a hard distinction: **hegemonic is not majority, subaltern is not minority**. Real power comprises institutionally defended relations, the ability to distribute, impose, recognize, exclude, withhold, reorganize and turn others' reproduction into dependence. A numerically small group can dominate property/state control; a prevalent worldview can coexist with politically subordinate subjects.

An exploratory *evidence profile* may legitimately include, **only where individually supported**:

- survey prevalence and the whole joint attitude/repertoire pattern;
- reach across separately measured source-local social positions;
- alignment of high-resource/status groups in *observed* survey data, with sample-tail caveats;
- an independently admitted political/economic/institutional group-power crosswalk, at exact geography/period;
- local symbolic defaultness, recognition/boundary/aspiration evidence where measured;
- competing and counterpositional repertoire patterns;
- recurrence through legally comparable time/source contexts;
- field-specific differential valuation and possible conversion candidates, with uncertainty and missing dimensions.

Without independently authorized group-power/elite alignment inputs, an observed profile must not pretend to answer "which class actually rules here?" EPR/GeoEPR or other external sources cannot be joined by similarity of group names alone. Data coverage of the very wealthy and institutions is an actual source limitation, not a license to impute one.

The **source-observed difference in resource valuation** is a possible neutral precursor. `CAPITAL`, `FIELD`, `CONVERSION`, `HEGEMONY` and `PRESSURE_PATHWAY` remain separate strong interpretive claims requiring an issuer. This HOME preserves the question and explanatory stakes while honoring R2's non-adoption of those strong nouns.

There is no one scalar hegemony score or total "progressiveness" axis, and political majority cannot turn into an objective power variable through model narration.

## X. Historical social variation without fictitious longitudinal people

The social field changes over time, but what is measurable depends on particular source/wave instruments. Field Forge can provide:

- source-wave-local position and attitude configurations as of period T;
- comparable/held difference evidence across independent cross-sections;
- explicit source/measurement/response/weight/domain transport maps, with falsifiers;
- evidence that some relational pattern recurs or fails to recur under admissible comparisons;
- uncertainty and noncomparability rather than forced narratives of progress.

It cannot infer a particular survey respondent's full developmental life from aggregate changes between 1995 and 2010. A fictional parent could have a developmental history later constructed by SOROK, but that must remain **fictional, specifically generated history**, not survey-measured panel data.

The downstream V553/F7 and actual historical world are responsible for interpreting temporality and change in a qualified structural/actor case. Field Forge supplies source-native historical *evidence* and comparison conditions.

## XI. The material-social information that must reach the actual world

A full theoretical field isn't completed by a bundle of population attitude ratios if downstream cannot answer which alternative really exists. The source/knowledge frontier therefore includes *independently grounded* objective material statements:

- role: employee, employer, controller of production, owner, dependent, caretaker, union representative, official, household member;
- relation: who can set hours, withdraw pay, impose sanction, offer care, demand duty, own or transfer an asset, speak for an institution;
- scope: to which entity, in which place/time/institution, under which legal/moral/practical conditions;
- material cost and binding constraint: time, labor, money, resource stock, travel, housing, care capacity and actual enforcement;
- contradicting role claims/authority and permission/negative states.

**This is a requirements interface to a competent structural/world owner, not a declaration that FF already calculates these social mechanics.** The protected objective-position materializer consumes an already-admitted `ObjectivePositionInput`; sourcefree N9/P41 already reads and applies a small set of structural values. SOROK already has world/labor/care/institution code. We must attempt the existing producer/read boundary before deciding whether a new specific source interpretation or adapter belongs in FF/N6/N9.

Material source roles cannot be made "empirical" by manufacturing an I03 respondent fact: I04's implementation genuinely requires `EmpiricalQuerySpec` and `EmpiricalFact`. Independent material observations have a different authorizing path.

This is the crossing from *descriptive social configuration* to *materially inhabited social relations*, while keeping empirical law, source conjecture, world actuality and fictional actor knowledge separate.

## XIII. A representative full circuit, showing where Field Forge ends

Imagine a historically situated survey population with measured household resource, employment, institutional trust and family-duty responses, plus an *independently modeled sourcefree* parent-world situation. All numerical values below are illustrative SOURCEFREE test conditions—not original-survey observations.

**Empirical:**
- I03/source authenticates which same respondents answered household resource `D`, institutional trust `T`, childcare-duty orientation `Y` and (if measured) labor situation `L`.
- FF01 computes `P(T,Y|D,L)`, preserves exceptions and exact source risk; if `L` was not coadministered, it refuses the false four-way joint.
- Alternate demographic strata with identical one-way support but opposite two-way/triple interactions remain different substantive empirical worlds. The later fictional adult is not pre-assigned the modal combination.

**Objectively modeled material context:**
- A separately qualified sourcefree **employer E** can deny schedule flexibility to worker P under a condition; caregiver C is available 0 versus 3 hours; child N requires supervision. A co-worker, institution or collective K may offer support, sanction or organized resistance.
- These rules are not extracted as "cause" from survey `P(Y|D,L)`. The real owner/world records them with source/claim/permission and holds where no authority is available.
- Existing N9/SOROK world machinery tests options: accept shift, seek care, ask for leave, refuse work, withdraw labor, use a fund. Cash without caregiving time is not automatically a substitute for care; a working spouse does not mechanically mean available care.

**First reader and actor:**

- SOROK first selects/constrains the particular parent and, through actual social practice, develops separate mother/father/child histories. The parent might obey, bargain, lie, pray, resist or accommodate; the choice is not a statistical personality assignment.
- Nadia's actual reception may differ from her parent's intention. She might learn a practical routine, a false analogy or nothing psychologically durable. That is **SOROK**, not an FF tuple.

**Verification:** change the source's genuine observed four-way cell and check the FF descriptive output differs; separately change the employer world rule and check N9/SOROK material options differ. Swapping one without changing the other must not contaminate the other layer. Then inspect an actual later SOROK state read, not a convincing narrative. ## XIV. Hard adversarial architecture and evidence tests

1. **Pairwise parity trap:** two same-person triple joints agree on every one- and two-way margin yet disagree on `P(Y|A,B)`; the returned FF evidence must distinguish them.
2. **Exposure topology trap:** a source has two respondent-disjoint randomized forms; no four-way shared psychological complex may be asserted across them.
3. **Follow-up routing trap:** source-column present but respondent not eligible, should not become a genuine negative response.
4. **Multi-answer trap:** repeated/select-all distinct valid endorsements must not be silently coerced into scalar-singular false co-travel.
5. **Cartesian explosion trap:** a genuine supported sparse high-dimensional query must be tested against existing ≤16,384-cell FF01 capacity; if held, do not invent dense probabilities.
6. **Minority and privacy trap:** a genuine rare positive survives within lawful release rights; a privacy-restricted case denies, both without turning held into observed zero.
7. **Respondent-axis trap:** new joint dimension requires original coadministered I03 evidence; no joining separate marginal tables on column names.
8. **Weighted estimand trap:** sample geometry and target-population geometry cannot be named identical without weight/design authority.
9. **Homology ancestry trap:** an attitude used as position coordinate must be excluded or supplementary in disposition space in the primary independent test.
10. **Homology null trap:** apparent cross-space correspondence dies under legitimate held-out/permutation/relevant measurement-mode controls: retain negative evidence, not HOMOLOGY.
11. **World group-power trap:** equal survey prevalence and different separately sourced institutional power may produce equal **observed** attitude frequency but different applicable material-world constraints; do not overwrite either.
12. **False hegemony trap:** 60% answer prevalence cannot issue hegemony without independent power/field and competent semantic admission.
13. **Coupling asymmetry trap:** symmetric observed A–B interaction is not proof both exert equal control or that either causes the other.
14. **Conditional backoff trap:** `36 YES / 24 NO / 40 unresolved` out of 100 eligible does not justify treating unresolved as NO or a fixed 36% population outcome; support/identified bounds or truthful held state required at exact measurement grade.
