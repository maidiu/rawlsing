# VMESTYE ZL-10 — Human story of an empirical relationship

Take one real respondent who answered three distinct questions in a specific wave, household, language, interviewer mode and eligibility regime. A historically valid source may say what those answers meant, whether the respondent was entitled to answer each one, how the sampling design weights that person, and whether any two response codes are substantively comparable. Rawlsing must not invent any of those facts from the numerical answers.

**First, source into evidence.** Correct source-owner and response-rule selections admit a versioned C11 release of respondent-linked events; I03 chooses a legal risk set and reads exact event memberships. A value observed in one respondent counts in a joint cell only when all required components really belong to that respondent and selection. A zero count in an eligible risk set is not the same as no eligible respondent or an unanswered question. This is what `pre553_empirical_query.execute_empirical_query` and `_pre553_query_eval` can computationally enforce on *supplied* authorized objects.

**Second, empirical conditional reasoning.** For some A, B, C and Y, the wanted claim is `P(Y | A,B,C)` in a named original population. One cannot multiply `P(Y|A)`, `P(Y|B)`, and `P(Y|C)`: the same-person co-occurrence might be parity-like, a minority pocket or a Simpson reversal. The current I03 k-way facts and C1's exact joint geometry are precisely the reusable computation. To reverse the query, `P(A|Y,B,C)` needs a new condition/target/risk relationship and denominator; reversing a label on an old fraction is forbidden. Current synthetic tests illustrate the difference; original35 scientific weights and grants remain unproved.

**Third, discovery and alternative branches.** I05 exact bounded search tests every member of its issuer-authorized finite frontier. Adaptive search instead might stop early, so unknown future candidates remain explicitly unknown. A supported minority respondent subset can become a new branch whose parent and member identities persist through a process restart. The branch is no new person, no prophecy, no structural fact: it is a governed empirical possibility. This is why losing minority cells, flattening joint topology, or declaring an unvisited candidate absent is not an optimization.

**Fourth, lawful first consumption.** C1 actually invokes the unchanged N10E possibility evaluator with its *computed* evidence, and E41 can carry existing direct/discovered/recursive results into a sourcefree learned consumer. For a positioned human in a generated objective world, however, more is required: an independently constituted N9 world, exact compatible empirical source and selected model-use grants, and a real non-test reader. Neither the world nor the joint table determines the individual's motives or reaction.

**Historical continuity:** V550/V550J already modeled same-respondent substrata, patterns, relation authorities and derived attitude clouds. The old cloud *table* was retired because its summary mixed kinds of evidence and denominators. That does not retire its substantive aim of a survey-grounded multivariate attitude configuration. Rawlsing's current path can reconstruct important parts directly from respondent-bound facts, not by reintroducing a lossy universal cloud score.

**What would change the finding?** A source-qualified original35 receipt that names the exact historical source/edition and governing response/item/risk/weight rule, shows changing that actual selected rule changes or rejects the computed F1/F2/F4 fact appropriately, preserves same-person k-way branches and true conditioning direction, and records an unchanged qualified first consumer's read. A code snapshot, fixture, old callsite or synthetically passing test alone cannot supply this chain.

The scientific obligation is conditional reality, not statistical destiny. A historically situated person may occupy an improbable joint cell, and their conduct is not deducible from that cell. The system's empirical machinery must make their **possibility** intelligible without laundering survey patterns into a script.
