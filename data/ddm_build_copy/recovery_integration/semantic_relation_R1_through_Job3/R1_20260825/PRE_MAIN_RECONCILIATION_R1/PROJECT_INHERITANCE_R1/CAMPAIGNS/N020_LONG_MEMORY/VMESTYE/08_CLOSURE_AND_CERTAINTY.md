Record which exact decision its retrieval can change, what existing indexes have been searched, and a bounded search path. If the item is only a historical wrapper/chronology nicety or already sufficiently characterized by preserved source and tests, do not turn it into a global blocker.

If authority or science is absent, identify its competent issuer and the precise decision required.
