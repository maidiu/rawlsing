## The question this home answers

This is the conceptual inheritance layer for **changing or redesigning Rawlsing**. It is not the operating-authority entrypoint. If you are validating or operating current Rawlsing, begin instead at `../00_CANONICAL_ENTRYPOINT.md` and `../CANONICAL_GOVERNANCE_R1/`.

Rawlsing is trying to build a governed machine that can carry source-native empirical meaning through exact authority/provenance, composability, learned-world structure, PRE-V3 structural construction, and eventually V3/person-world realization without laundering uncertainty, collapsing distinct semantic grains, or allowing implementation convenience to become ontology. Its history is therefore not a linear software release train. It is a succession of architectural and epistemic courts in which later campaigns often corrected only one grain of an earlier design while leaving its conceptual lesson, open question, or failed alternative alive.

## Constitutional rule

Three dimensions are independent:

1. **Authority status** — what may govern current execution or interpretation.
2. **Conceptual/exploration status** — whether the question is closed, partially closed, open, suspended, return-required, superseded only at a grain, abandoned, stranded, or unresolved.
3. **Ideation relevance** — what a future architect must still factor even when it is historical or nonbinding.

W55 adds a fourth operational dimension: **reintegration status** — whether significant work has been centrally reconciled and canonically inherited, or remains local/unbanked/stranded.

`CURRENT_AUTHORITY != MUST_FACTOR_INTO_IDEATION`.
`NOT_CURRENT != NOT_INTELLECTUALLY_IMPORTANT`.

## The major intellectual succession

### 1. Originary person-world / Structural Persona / Pax Marxica

The early project asks how a person can be structurally situated without turning population-level evidence into biography, consciousness, or fate. Durable lessons recovered by W49: social position is not consciousness; theory priors are not empirical facts; historical intelligibility is not deterministic identity; and an ontology without writer/reader/provenance/test machinery is decorative. The original corpus is historical, but these constraints remain `MUST_FACTOR` for any V3/person-world design. See N033.

### 2. Source-wave identity and early authority transport

SWID and the V550→V552J lineage force identity and provenance to become first-class. Same label, same row, same bytes, later timestamp, or downstream success do not by themselves establish semantic identity or current authority. Applicability, certification, promotability, and authority-bearing carrier fields are distinct. SWID's static issue closure is not evidence that its runtime successor problem was fully closed. See N001 and N035.

### 3. Dva

Dva develops the producer-owned semantic DAG and the decomposition from source availability through applicability/opportunity/encounter/exposure/route realization/response/persistent change. It separates structural possibility from actor realization and objective fact from access, phenomenology, and expression. Its architecture closure did **not** equal implementation closure: W0–W2 returned; W3 was released and remained stranded, with later planned releases never executed. Dva's frozen physical layout is not current universal law, but its ownership and semantic distinctions survive. See N002–N005.

### 4. Trii

Trii turns the problem information-theoretic and empirical. It proves that semantic/use permission is not workload authority; queryability is not a command to materialize everything; same-respondent selectable joints require respondent-bound incidence or an information-equivalent carrier; empirical coordinates, generated structural positions, learned products, replay identity, and V3 life are different objects; and discovery needs authority before search. Its D15 append-only C11 physical choice was later challenged, while the information burden survived. See N006–N009.

### 5. Chetiri

Chetiri attacks the illicit inference from information obligation to permanent transformed storage. Its durable theorem is: source bytes alone are not governed semantics, but governed semantics do not require one eternal physical fossil. It distinguishes **THE PAST** (authoritative empirical evidence), **THE LEARNED WORLD** (governed products/services/replay), and **THE LIFE** (V3 actor realization). It did not choose the persistent/virtual/hybrid winner. Production C05R2B, C08 final reconciliation, compiler composition, validation, comparability, PSEPF, source-universe composition, and economics remain live. See N010–N012.

### 6. Verkhovod / PRE-V3 / MX / fracture map

These campaigns synthesize the producer-DAG and information laws while discovering that PRE-V3 is not V3, reconstructibility is not eager persistence, discovered-child handoff must itself be governed, component scalarization is lawful only under complete/unanimous conditions, exhaustive and adaptive discovery are distinct, and proxies must remain visibly proxies. MX and fracture-map work expose missing contracts and later readmission obligations. No campaign lawfully selected the final physical architecture. See N012–N015.

### 7. Semantic Relation / Job3 / O12 / P1–P10 / authority revalidation

This era turns repeated failures into explicit constitutional law. Same bytes/ID/row do not imply same semantic claim; newest does not mean current; `REJECT != negative semantic truth`; `BLOCKED != false`; source structure is not joint configuration; semantic relation is not respondent composability; implementation is not admission; delegated acceptance is not global sovereignty; preservation is not promotion. Job3 produced 552 ACCEPT, 697 REJECT, and 15 BLOCKED candidate verdicts, while O12 preserves successor obligations instead of mutating old verdicts. P1–P10 separate proposition, claim, authority-instance, current-selection, consumer permission, and preservation identities. See N016–N019.

### 8. Long Memory / recovery / conservation / whole-history inheritance

These campaigns discover that a project can preserve bytes and still lose its mind. The R3 project-memory design distinguishes live/DAG-ready authority, a research reservoir, a historical knowledge graph of failures/repairs/supersessions, and executable WHY-NOT memory. Authority39 and later recovery work establish living succession/conservation methods, but W46 shows major reasons, examples, orphan goals, and old intent surfaces remained difficult to discover from current command. W50-W54 substantially close the current project-home discoverability failure; W55 converts discoverability and reintegration into an ongoing central-state obligation rather than pretending preservation stays discoverable automatically. See N020–N022 and N030–N031.

### 9. R5, source preparation, current admission, Current Joint, C06

R5 is preserved `INACTIVE_NON_SERVING`; it is not the selected runtime. Later source-preparation/current-admission work reconstructs a current source-structure/candidacy universe and current routing contract under delegated ceilings. C06 Contract@2 is current at its admitted grain, but J01-B / `CURRENT_JOINT_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT@1` remains return-required. Historical denominators and carriers do not regain authority because they are older, larger, or convenient. See N023–N027.

### 10. Field Forge and Structural Pressure Observatory

Historical Field Forge is a forward architectural exploration that asked how respondent exposure topology, position-role authority, geometry families, disposition measurement, homology, pressure footprints, articulation/hegemony, capital conversion, positioned social-world packets, and eventual V3 interfaces might be built without mistaking questionnaire form for ontology. W48 correctly fixed that historical/pre-adoption corpus at `EXPLORATORY_NONBINDING`.

The later FIELD FORGE R2 campaign re-grounded the problem, ran the required empirical/weakest-object courts, underwent independent architecture and evidence audits, repaired the owner-seam defect, passed independent rereview, and was then Global-HQ-adopted at exact semantic/architecture grain by `096b419ed8fd3d07c87cb74bcc4890c2f1a8ec54`.

Its current adopted route is `FIELD_FORGE_R2_ADOPTED_AUTHORITY_R1/`. The adopted R2 architecture is capability/relation based, producer-preserving, physically neutral, actor-safe, and explicit about strong-noun blockers and typed negatives. This does **not** promote all historical Field Forge proposals, close claim-specific PRA/crosswalk/estimand/transport/strong-noun authorities, or make Structural Pressure Observatory current. SPO remains a separate exploratory body and must not be collapsed into Field Forge merely because they touch neighboring questions. See N028–N029 and `FIELD_FORGE_REINDUCTION_BRIDGE.md` for historical inheritance only after the adopted capsule.
