# W43 CAMPAIGN MAP — TRII PROBLEM SUCCESSION

This map treats Trii as a sequence of problem transformations. A later court closes only the question it actually owns; it does not retroactively erase the earlier discovery that made the successor necessary.

## Phase 0 — inherited empirical-discovery capital

### V526c / V547: salience and interaction

Historical V526c separated predictor salience, interaction salience and shape classes. Executable V547 implemented an unweighted discrete-MI question diagnostic with exhaustive pair search over its finite non-country predictor set. Trii D01 established that current V550 was not a faithful continuation: it gained richer unary conditional topology but narrowed pair search to a primary-star geometry and changed the mathematics/grain.

Succession problem opened: **which discovery capabilities matter independently of the historical algorithm or table?**

### V526d / V548: recursive breakage

Historical V548 searched majority-carrying position → response deviation → deeper positional breakage. V550 instead used a greedy positive entropy-gain tree over the two most frequent supported values. D02 found the two search geometries non-equivalent and neither tree artifact lossless because respondent membership was not retained.

Succession problem opened: **what information must survive so recursive/subgroup discovery can be regenerated without canonizing a tree implementation?**

### V526e / V549 / V549b: branch-conditioned cotravel and profile covariance

D03 recovered a real historical distinction between branch-conditioned same-respondent cotravel and current generic cloud/signature diagnostics. V549/V549b actually conditioned on reconstructed branch membership. Current signatures did not. Current attitude cloud did make same-respondent cross-question joins, but only pairwise and with a modal-cell/composite summary. The historical `profile_cluster_summary` and `profile_relation_vector` ambitions were not realized in the examined lineage.

Succession problem opened: **what is the deeper empirical object beneath branch/cloud artifacts, and what of the historical profile ambition is genuinely terminal?**

## Phase 1 — hostile current-artifact audit

### D04: current cloud correctness

D04 showed that the current V550 attitude-cloud object could not safely serve as canonical empirical truth: processing-order pair loss, special/missing-state leakage, nats/bits NMI mismatch, broken resume rehydration, pre-registration gating, and unresolved weighting/repeat/multiselect/tie/risk-set authority.

This destroyed the shortcut “current artifact exists, therefore preserve it.”

### D05: populated substrate reality

D05 constructed an executable populated-substrate audit but could not access a production-equivalent populated database. It therefore refused schema-as-fact and refused database-internal parity as source equivalence. Source-native routing/not-asked semantics, weights/design, original code/label identity, topology, geography/demographic detail, multiple-response semantics and ingest provenance remained part of the proof burden.

This opened the later source-equivalence and source-retirement frontier.

## Phase 2 — authority decomposition

### D06: relation authority is not universal compute authority

D06 R2 found a plural execution topology: Final R1 relation meaning, QUC permissions, QSM base selection, V550J configuration authority, shared extraction, resource policy, fixed implementation inventories, association publication authority and downstream re-resolution. A semantic/use permission did not automatically command a workload.

### D07: reconstructibility without materialize-all

D07 separated three propositions:

- `MATERIALIZE_ALL_PERMITTED`: not established;
- `RECONSTRUCTIBLE_ON_DEMAND`: governing at a future-authorized query frontier;
- authority-selected bounded materialization: established for some V550J work, not globally.

This became one of Trii's central architectural laws: **preserve queryability/information, not every present derived table.**

## Phase 3 — empirical ontology recovered

### D08: governed respondent-conditional relation algebra

D08 unified unary conditionals, conjunctions, response-conditioned facts, branch-conditioned facts and joint patterns under a deeper typed algebra. Primitive information included respondent-bound response events, empirical coordinates, routing/applicability, branch membership, explicit condition predicates, risk sets, measures and provenance.

The result was not “one table.” It was an ontology of empirical fact identity:

- E0 respondent incidence;
- E0B branch/cohort incidence;
- E1 conditional distribution;
- E2 joint outcome incidence;
- E3 derived composite view;
- E4 derived association functional.

Risk-set/denominator, topology, measure, source scope and predicate provenance are identity-bearing. Numerical equality does not imply fact identity. E3/E4 cannot recreate E0/E1/E2.

### D09 and D09A: same answer / different cloud and hostile semantic appeal

D09 pushed the insight toward a named population-meaning-field object. D09A correctly retained the information-theoretic floor—different predicates/risk sets/scopes/topologies/provenance can produce different empirical fact identities, and different E2 incidence can produce different empirical configuration identities—but hostilely rejected promotion of that difference into a current producer-owned `POPULATION_MEANING_FIELD` or a generic multi-lane semantic use.

Succession result: **fact difference is real; structural/meaning promotion remains separately governed.**

### D10: structural predicate authority gap

D10 found no current general cross-ontology authority from survey field/value to generated structural predicate/binding. Demographic categories, geography, income, religion, employment and similar coordinates remained empirical conditioners unless a contextual transformation was separately governed. Nine strata were relational fields, not demographic buckets.

This is a direct ancestor of later PRE-V3 structural-admission work and Field Forge's refusal to freeze structural ontology from theory or unsupervised computation.

## Phase 4 — downstream reachability versus terminal necessity

### D11: current consumption and stranded meaning

D11 traced the actual V550/V550J→V551/V551J→V552/V552J→V553 path. It found exact position conditionals, arbitrary conjunctions, historical branch membership/cotravel and the current cloud stranded from canonical downstream use, while bounded V550J joint patterns were consumed after transformation. Respondent membership itself stopped at V550J.

The decisive lesson was negative: **nonconsumption is not proof of unnecessity or retirement authority.** It set up a terminal-capability/starvation court rather than using current consumers as the ontology oracle.

## Phase 5 — terminal capability and actor boundary

### D12C: mediation/development/actor-opacity boundary

D12C located the actor-independent chain:

population empirical evidence → governed structural interpretation → structural affordance → mediation/topology availability → V558 resolved structural context → V3 generated facts/events/history → V3 memory/epistemic/response state → bounded expression.

Every arrow is separately owned. V558 resolves; it does not invent. The individual life begins only with actor-specific generated acts in V3, not at survey observation, structural fact, affordance, route, topology or context resolution.

### D12D: actor empirical comparability

D12D established a separate seam from D10: an independently generated objective actor fact may, under a field/source/time-specific comparator, produce an empirical conditioner. Structural position is not a generic bridge. The comparator may require exact equality, ranges, normalization, geography hierarchy, source-specific or temporal equivalence, or explicit noncomparability. No general positive comparator authority was found.

### D12E: positioned-subject empirical possibility

D12E defined the PSEPF as source/historical-scoped population evidence selected by an actor-relative lawful conditioner, with exact requested joint support/mass geometry. It is not actor state. Fixed exact contingencies can answer fixed bounded queries; future-selectable targets/conditioners require deeper respondent incidence or information equivalence. Direct actor assignment and nearest-cohort fallback are forbidden.

### D12F: autonomous forward social discovery

D12F established authority-before-search: authority fixes a finite/finitely constructible candidate grammar, source/respondent scope, topology, coordinate eligibility, composition/depth, risk sets, support/validation and negative authority; data select the winners. Neither individually predeclaring every relationship nor unbounded search is acceptable. Coverage/pruning behavior is part of the semantic/replay contract.

### D12G: cross-survey / multi-universe composition

D12G separated semantic comparability from respondent-universe identity, respondent linkage, sample pooling and service admissibility. Default: distinct universes; compare separately where lawful; preserve source-indexed components; disagreement is information; joining/pooling/backfill require positive authority. String-equal respondent IDs do not establish same persons.

### D12 final: three terminal services

D12 synthesized the feeders into three terminal obligations:

1. autonomous forward social discovery;
2. reverse positioned-subject empirical possibility inference;
3. structural/stratal empirical field production.

It also froze the deeper information laws: unary/pairwise summaries do not generally determine higher-order joint reality; supported minorities survive; low support is not impossibility; observed zero is not metaphysical impossibility; no nearest-cohort repair for zero-support conditions; cross-universe composition fails closed.

## Phase 6 — physical information and replay

### D13: minimum information substrate

D13 selected C11—minimum normalized respondent-event/coordinate incidence plus authority/provenance—as sufficient for the D12 frontier at **specification-level information equivalence**, not as a unique implementation. Its M01–M18 burden includes universe/respondent/question/event identity, topology/multiplicity/order, typed states, routing/risk sets, future coordinates, weights, source semantics, cross-universe relations, predicate/replay identity, actor comparators, branch/discovery replay, structural admission, uncertainty, immutable authority and deterministic replay.

Current Postgres/facet/shared V550 carriers and aggregate-only surfaces were not sufficient for the full future frontier. Populated source equivalence remained blocked.

## Phase 7 — compute/materialization

### D14: semantic cost versus implementation cost

D14 established that Rawlsing must pay for every exact distinction and every candidate included in a claimed discovery frontier, but does not have to pay for the current eager artifact suite. Exact same-respondent binding, topology, routing, measure, source/universe identity, comparator resolution, replay and claimed search coverage are unavoidable; scan strategy, indexes, precompute/cache, physical layout, traversal, parallelism and serialization are contingent.

It specifically preserved zero-lower-order-signal/XOR discoverability against unsafe unary pruning and preserved minority evidence against cost-driven top-k suppression.

Production wall-clock/storage economics remained unknown.

## Phase 8 — D15 first architecture, and the questions it actually launched

D15 selected a first PRE-V3 architecture around C11, exact query service, authority-bounded exact/adaptive discovery, actor comparator, source-indexed PSEPF, structural admission, revalidated V553+, and a thin PSEPF→V3 input.

But D15 also enumerated fourteen authority additions/repairs still required: source-ingest equivalence, query authority, autonomous discovery, discovery validation/coverage, actor comparator, service source applicability, source-universe relation, structural admission, PSEPF production, V3 possibility metabolism, uncertainty method, semantic replay integrity, cache/materialization policy and source-retirement equivalence.

Therefore D15 closed **first architecture synthesis**, not the entire intellectual campaign.

## Phase 9 — later Trii correction: direct-DAG ownership and stage necessity

The later Trii corrected upstream/downstream R03 synthesis did not reopen D15's upstream information laws. It qualified the physical DAG:

- V553/V554/V555/V556 transformations are conditional on the exact semantic service needed;
- independently runtime-authorized producers may publish directly to V557;
- V555 remains the canonical base edge for V556 where V556 meaning is needed;
- logical semantic authorities survive even if stages are physically co-packaged;
- V557 publication/use governance and V558 immutable repository/context resolution remain distinct logical authorities;
- source-indexed plurality remains default;
- PSEPF bypasses the structural lane and enters V3 through a thin admission boundary;
- V3 metabolism authority was still missing and was an implementation/close-read blocker;
- a terminal discovery result may be a lawful endpoint even with no runtime consumer.

This is the direct-DAG/minimality correction: **minimize physical stages, not semantic ownership.**

## Phase 10 — Chetiri challenge and preservation

Chetiri challenged D15's assumption that the normalized C11 payload itself must remain permanently materialized. Its strongest source-native adjudication showed raw payload reconstructable from governed source bytes while semantic occurrence identity still required source coordinates and governed meaning. Thus permanent duplicate payload was not proven necessary, while topology/routing/meaning/replay side information and learned products remained first-class.

Chetiri preserved carrier-neutral Trii laws and sharpened `rebuildable != semantically disposable`. It did not positively close generic production discovery, actor comparability, PSEPF model law or universal source composition.

## Phase 11 — Verkhovod / PRE-V3 / authority revalidation

Later PRE-V3 work implemented bounded successors for exact query, structural admission, exact/adaptive discovery and source-backed carrier paths, but later prerequisite courts still marked recursive topology, component vectors, WVS7 source closure, production discovery execution, positive learned-relation promotion, positive comparability, PSEPF model authority and source composition as incompletely closed at various grains.

Authority revalidation R01/R08 independently preserved provider debt for production DiscoveryAuthority/frontier, branch/cohort replay, general post-discovery validation, positive comparator rules, production PSEPF/V3 transition, learned-product lifecycle and end-to-end carrier-neutral replay.

These later campaigns therefore **revisited** much of Trii's frontier; they did not erase it.

## Phase 12 — Field Forge inheritance

Field Forge inherits the Trii constitution in a more explicitly sociological design room:

- no structural ontology from theory alone or unsupervised compute;
- survey/routing/J/semantic authority before exact claims;
- separate population/world structure from V3 realized-person development;
- preserve observability limits instead of inventing facts;
- treat structural pressure as pressure, not destiny;
- move finite semantic judgment into explicit authority, leaving arithmetic/joins/enumeration/replay to deterministic computation.

Field Forge's newer disposition/pressure/field/hegemony vocabulary is therefore not a replacement for Trii's boundary law. It is a downstream attempt to operate inside it.

## W4B synthesis instruction

Carry Trii forward as:

1. **binding conceptual constraints** where later authority preserved them;
2. **superseded implementation shapes** where later work changed the physical/search architecture;
3. **revisited but still open frontiers** where later courts still lack positive authority/execution;
4. **return-required unexplored capital** where the original question was reframed or stranded without a competent descendant closure.

Do not compress categories 3 and 4 into “unfinished old work,” and do not compress category 2 into “rejected idea.” The entire purpose of W43 is to preserve problem succession.
