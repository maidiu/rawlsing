# W45 STATUS SEMANTICS

W45 uses the Wave-4 inheritance status vocabulary as state of the *question/campaign at the adjudicated grain*, never as a scalar verdict on an entire branch or era.

- `CLOSED` — the exact question at the stated grain has a competent dispositive answer/closure. Does not imply broader architecture closure.
- `PARTIALLY_CLOSED` — one or more exact subquestions are closed but a material remainder survives.
- `OPEN` — a live proposition/question remains unresolved and requires competent future adjudication.
- `SUSPENDED_ON_DEPENDENCY` — work is live in principle but cannot lawfully proceed until a named prerequisite/substrate/authority becomes available.
- `SPAWNED_SUCCESSOR_PROBLEM` — the episode answered enough to expose a narrower/new prerequisite that became a successor campaign.
- `RETURN_REQUIRED` — a parent question was not retired; later synthesis must explicitly return to it before claiming closure at that grain.
- `SUPERSEDED_AT_GRAIN` — a prior mechanism/claim/selection is no longer current at the stated grain, while surviving theorem/evidence may remain binding or preserved.
- `ABANDONED_BY_DECISION` — competent authority explicitly chose not to continue the question/path. Mere inactivity does not qualify.
- `INTERRUPTED_STRANDED` — work stopped without competent closure, supersession, or dependency disposition and therefore remains conservation-sensitive.

Additional non-status qualifiers used in W45:

- `BINDING_THEOREM` — presently binding proposition/law inherited into current governance.
- `ADMITTED_FUNCTION` — executable/function capital admitted at its exact scope and grain.
- `PRESERVATION_ONLY` — evidence/function capital must remain reachable but is not serving/current authority merely by preservation.
- `DEPENDENCY_BLOCKED` — concrete blocked condition attached to a `SUSPENDED_ON_DEPENDENCY` or `OPEN` row.

Noncollapse rules:

`CLOSED_AT_THEOREM_GRAIN != ARCHITECTURE_WINNER`

`IMPLEMENTATION_PASS != ADMISSION`

`ADMISSION != GLOBAL_SOVEREIGNTY`

`PRESERVED != CURRENT_SERVING`

`SUPERSEDED_MECHANISM != THEOREM_ERASED`

`BLOCKED != REJECTED`

`INACTIVITY != ABANDONED_BY_DECISION`

`SPAWNED_SUCCESSOR_PROBLEM != PARENT_FULLY_CLOSED`
