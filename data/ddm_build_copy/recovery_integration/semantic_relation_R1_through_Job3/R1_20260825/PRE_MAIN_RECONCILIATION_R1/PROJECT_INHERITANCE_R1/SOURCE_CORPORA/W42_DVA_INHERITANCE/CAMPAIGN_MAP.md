# Dva Campaign Map — intellectual lineage, closure dimensions, and unfinished frontier

## 1. Campaign identity

Dva was not a single V553–V558 redesign. It was a sequence of increasingly strict attempts to answer one larger problem:

**How can Rawlsing turn governed structural evidence into synthetic lived development and expression without letting any downstream layer fabricate, flatten, or re-own meanings that belong elsewhere?**

The campaign moved through the following phases.

These are functional succession edges, not evidence that Dva's entire architecture was replaced.
