## Executive holding

Trii was not one architecture proposal. It was a succession of courts that took an inherited V547/V548/V549/V550 empirical-discovery lineage apart, separated capabilities that had been accidentally conflated, reconstructed the minimum information and authority needed to preserve the valuable ones, and then drew a hard boundary between a learned population/world and the realized individual life.

Its durable contribution is therefore not the historical V547→V549 implementations and not even D15's first physical C11 strategy. The durable contribution is a set of noncollapse laws and capability obligations:

1. semantic/use permission is not workload authority;
2. queryability/reconstructibility is not materialize-all;
3. respondent-bound incidence, or an exact information-equivalent carrier, is the generator needed for future-selectable same-respondent conditional/joint questions;
4. predicate, risk set, topology, measure, source universe and provenance are part of empirical fact identity;
5. equal numerical answers under different governed conditions can be different empirical facts, but that alone does not create a new structural/meaning ontology;
6. empirical coordinates are not generated structural positions;
7. structural promotion requires explicit admission authority;
8. autonomous discovery is a terminal capability, but must be bounded by authority before search and must state its coverage/blind spots;
9. generated actor facts require field/source/time-specific comparability before they can condition historical survey evidence;
10. a positioned-subject empirical possibility field is population evidence selected relative to an actor, not actor belief, propensity, personality or realized state;
11. respondent universes are distinct by default; semantic comparability, respondent linkage, pooling and backfill are separate authorities;
12. learned-world evidence, actor-independent structural topology, and V3 realized life are distinct jurisdictions;
13. replay/provenance identity is not cache identity or physical carrier identity;
14. logical producer/consumer authority is not proof that every historical stage must remain a separate physical stage;
15. current nonconsumption or terminal endpoint status is not retirement authority.

Those laws became constraints on PRE-V3, Chetiri, Verkhovod and Field Forge. Chetiri later challenged D15's stronger physical presumption that a permanently materialized canonical C11 payload was necessary, but it preserved the underlying information, source-identity, authority, replay, query, discovery and V3-boundary laws. Later Verkhovod/PRE-V3 work likewise retained the actor-comparator, PSEPF, source-composition, recursive-topology and discovery frontiers as prerequisites rather than treating D15 as complete implementation proof. Field Forge now directly inherits the Trii discipline that population/world structure must not be confused with realized personhood, and that structural ontology cannot be frozen from theory or unsupervised compute alone.
