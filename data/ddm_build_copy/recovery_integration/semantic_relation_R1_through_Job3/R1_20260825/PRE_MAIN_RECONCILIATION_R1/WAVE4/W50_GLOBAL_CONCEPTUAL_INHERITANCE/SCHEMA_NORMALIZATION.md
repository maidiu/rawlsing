# W50 STATUS NORMALIZATION

Wave-4A local vocabularies are normalized into three independent dimensions.

**Authority:** `CURRENT_BINDING`, `PROJECT_CURRENT_AT_GRAIN`, `DELEGATED_ACCEPTED`, `HQ_BANKED`, `PRESERVATION_ONLY`, `HISTORICAL`, `EXPLORATORY_NONBINDING`, `NOT_ADMITTED`, `BLOCKED`, `UNKNOWN`.

**Conceptual/exploration:** `CLOSED`, `PARTIALLY_CLOSED`, `OPEN`, `SUSPENDED_ON_DEPENDENCY`, `SPAWNED_SUCCESSOR_PROBLEM`, `RETURN_REQUIRED`, `SUPERSEDED_AT_GRAIN`, `ABANDONED_BY_DECISION`, `INTERRUPTED_STRANDED`, `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_SYNTHESIS`.

**Ideation relevance:** `MUST_FACTOR`, `SHOULD_FACTOR`, `REFERENCE_IF_RELEVANT`, `HISTORICAL_ONLY`.

No dimension is derived mechanically from another. Physical copying does not alter any dimension.
