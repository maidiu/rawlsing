# DELEGATED AUTHORITY RULE — W22 R1

Status: `YES__DELEGATED_BINDING_AUTHORITY_EXISTS__STRICTLY_SCOPE_BOUNDED`

## Question

Can a properly authorized delegated campaign create binding project authority inside its delegated jurisdiction without a later global-HQ banking commit?

## Ruling

**YES.**

Rawlsing repository law positively establishes a constitutional layer between worker result and global HQ adoption.

The Current Joint campaign charter is dispositive because it simultaneously states:

- `CAMPAIGN ADOPTION != HQ ADOPTION`;
- `CAMPAIGN CONTRACT FREEZE != FINAL HQ ADOPTION`;
- family courts must execute against the same frozen campaign contract;
- the J campaign consumes the exact joined/admitted routing ledger at its proved grain and does not alter routing dispositions;
- the campaign does not supersede HQ;
- HQ alone decides final architecture release and final adoption beyond the campaign.

Those clauses would be incoherent if campaign freeze/adoption had no binding effect until HQ later re-banked every object. Their function is precisely to grant **inwardly binding, outwardly nonsovereign** authority.

## Exact rule

A delegated controller may create `DELEGATED_ACCEPTED_AUTHORITY` when and only when all are true:

1. **Delegation exists.** A competent superior authority creates the campaign/control line and grants a bounded jurisdiction.
2. **Jurisdiction is named.** The campaign identifies the semantic/function scope it may adjudicate and the decisions still reserved upward.
3. **Object identity is exact.** The controller names or freezes the exact claim/artifact/ref/hash/denominator required for downstream use.
4. **Acceptance is dispositive.** The controller explicitly admits/adopts/freezes the object or commands authorized downstream courts to consume it as a closed dependency.
5. **Proof prerequisites are satisfied.** Required QA, evidence, provenance, hashes, typed gaps, and negative ceilings survive.
6. **No widening occurs.** A routing result stays routing authority; evidence stays evidence; preservation stays preservation; a campaign-current holding stays current only at campaign scope.
7. **Downstream reliance stays inside the delegated DAG.** The acceptance binds only work whose authority descends from or is explicitly governed by that campaign charter.
8. **Superior reservations are obeyed.** If HQ reserves architecture release, global adoption, main mutation, or global sovereignty, delegated acceptance cannot perform those acts.
9. **No superior revocation exists.** A later competent HQ stay/correction/supersession controls.

## What delegated acceptance permits

Inside the delegated jurisdiction, downstream workers may:

- rely on the accepted/frozen object without reopening its already-adjudicated grain;
- treat it as the campaign's current dependency when the controller has explicitly frozen/current-selected it;
- derive later campaign products from it while preserving its proof ceiling;
- reject alternate worker artifacts that conflict with the frozen campaign contract absent new authorized correction.

This is real project authority, not merely a recommendation, because it governs authorized project work. Its scope is constitutionally bounded.

## What delegated acceptance does not permit

It cannot by itself:

- make the campaign branch the global sovereign trunk;
- alter global HQ law outside the charter;
- claim global-current status merely from campaign-current status;
- authorize project-wide architecture release when HQ reserved that power;
- define a canonical global Git base;
- turn every descendant commit into accepted authority;
- turn worker PASS into acceptance at unrelated grains;
- retire HQ-preserved capital without a competent succession ruling.

## HQ re-banking rule

Later global HQ banking is **required** for any of the following transitions:

`DELEGATED_ACCEPTED_AUTHORITY -> HQ_BANKED_AUTHORITY`

`CAMPAIGN_CURRENT -> GLOBAL_PROJECT_CURRENT`

`SUBORDINATE_CONTROL -> GLOBAL_SOVEREIGN_CONTROL`

`CAMPAIGN_WORKER_BASE -> CANONICAL_GLOBAL_GIT_BASE`

or any action expressly reserved to HQ by the charter.

Later HQ banking is **not required** merely to validate the delegated controller's power to bind its own authorized campaign workers at the already-chartered grain.

## Why this does not create multiple global sovereign trunks

Authority is indexed by jurisdiction.

The campaign's power is delegated from the HQ cut and remains defeasible by superior HQ authority. Its charter expressly says `This campaign does not supersede HQ.` The delegated line therefore possesses adjudicative competence without possessing constituent power over the whole project.

In compact form:

`DELEGATION = BOUNDED GOVERNING POWER`

`SOVEREIGNTY = POWER TO DEFINE/TRANSFER THE GLOBAL GOVERNING FRAME`

The former can exist without the latter.

Therefore C06 is binding/current **inside that routing dependency jurisdiction** even though no later frozen global-HQ commit separately banks terminal C06 R2.

Its correct classification is:

`DELEGATED_ACCEPTED_AUTHORITY__PROJECT_CURRENT_WITHIN_CURRENT_JOINT_ROUTING_GRAIN__HQ_UNBANKED__NO_GLOBAL_SOVEREIGNTY_EFFECT`

This reconciles W07's terminal-current-routing ruling with W04's `AWAITING_HQ_BANK` finding: W07 identifies currentness at routing/carrier grain; W04 identifies the absence of global HQ re-anchoring. Neither negates the other.
