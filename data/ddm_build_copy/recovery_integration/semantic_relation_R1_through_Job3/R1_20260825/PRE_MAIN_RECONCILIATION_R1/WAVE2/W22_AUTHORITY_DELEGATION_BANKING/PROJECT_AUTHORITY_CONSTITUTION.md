## 1. Holding

Rawlsing does **not** operate one scalar authority ladder in which every stronger semantic holding must also be newer, on the global trunk, ancestrally contained, and globally banked.

The repository instead implements a **scope-indexed authority lattice** with at least four independent dimensions:

1. **creator / governing layer** — worker, delegated campaign/control, global HQ;
2. **acceptance grain** — evidence, preservation, source semantics, routing, J authority, architecture, runtime, etc.;
3. **currentness** — whether an authorized governing layer has selected/frozen the holding as current at that grain and scope;
4. **carrier relation** — Git ancestry, exact immutable-ref adoption, explicit re-inheritance, downstream contract consumption, or preservation-only reference.

Consequently:

`ACCEPTED != GLOBAL_TRUNK`

`CURRENT != NEWEST`

`OFF_TREE != UNACCEPTED`

`WORKER_PASS != PROJECT_ACCEPTANCE`

`PRESERVATION != LIVE_PROMOTION`

and additionally:

`GIT_ANCESTRY != AUTHORITY_INHERITANCE`

`DELEGATED_BINDING != GLOBAL_SOVEREIGN`

`EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE != EXECUTABLE_AUTHORITY`

`BANKING != CURRENT_SELECTION`

`PHYSICAL_BRANCH_SUCCESSION != SEMANTIC_SUCCESSION`

These are constitutional separations, not temporary exceptions.

## 2. Refined project-acceptance rule

Wave 1 W03 proposed:

> A Rawlsing claim counts as project-accepted only at the exact grain and jurisdiction for which an authorized governing layer explicitly admits/adopts/banks the exact identified claim or artifact, with required evidence preserved, and no later binding authority has revoked or superseded that holding.

W22 **AFFIRMS WITH REFINEMENT**.

A claim or artifact is **PROJECT-ACCEPTED AT SCOPE `S` AND GRAIN `G`** only when all applicable conditions below hold:

### A. Exact identity

The accepted object is fixed sufficiently to prevent silent substitution. Depending on the carrier this means an exact commit SHA, exact artifact path, exact row/identity universe, exact payload hash, Library identity plus hash, or another immutable identity required by the governing court.

`SIMILAR OUTPUT != IDENTICAL ACCEPTED OBJECT`

### B. Lawful accepting layer

The layer performing the acceptance possesses authority over `S/G`.

That authority may be:

- global HQ / global sovereign control; or
- a delegated campaign/control branch whose parent authority explicitly grants adjudicative or freeze power over the bounded campaign jurisdiction.

A worker does not acquire this power merely by being assigned execution.

### C. Positive act of admission, adoption, freeze, or binding consumption

The authorized layer must do something constitutionally dispositive. Valid forms include:

- explicit `ADOPT`, `ACCEPT`, `BANK`, `INHERIT`, or equivalent ruling;
- explicit immutable-ref adoption;
- a binding campaign freeze that downstream courts are ordered to consume without reopening;
- explicit successor-contract consumption that names the exact prior holding as closed/frozen/immutable.

Mere existence, PASS status, timestamp, branch name, PR closure, or descendant ancestry is insufficient.

### D. Evidence preconditions and proof ceiling

All evidence sealing, hostile-QA, hash/count, provenance, replay, manual-court, or other prerequisites imposed by the governing law must be satisfied. Acceptance never enlarges the proof ceiling beyond what was adjudicated.

Thus a carrier accepted as evidence remains evidence; a preservation envelope accepted for zero-loss obligations remains preservation capital; a compiler accepted as a validator does not become a source-semantic decision maker.

### E. No unresolved superior contradiction

No later same-scope or superior binding authority may have revoked, corrected, narrowed, superseded, or stayed the holding.

Supersession is typed. A later artifact does not supersede an earlier holding merely because it is newer.

### F. Currentness is separately established

`ACCEPTED` and `CURRENT` are separate predicates.

A holding becomes current at `S/G` only when an authorized layer:

- explicitly selects it as current;
- freezes it as the current denominator/contract/ledger;
- or creates a successor dependency that expressly treats the exact holding as closed and immutable for current downstream execution.

Timestamps and branch recency do not establish currentness.

## 3. Delegation doctrine

**YES:** a properly authorized delegated campaign can create binding project authority **inside its delegated jurisdiction** without a later global-HQ banking commit.

This is not inferred from convenience. The Current Joint campaign charter explicitly separates:

`CAMPAIGN ADOPTION != HQ ADOPTION`

while also authorizing a campaign contract freeze and requiring later family courts to execute against the same campaign contract. It further states that the J campaign consumes the exact joined/admitted current routing ledger at its proved grain and does not alter routing dispositions.

Therefore the repository itself recognizes a bounded intermediate constitutional state:

`DELEGATED_ACCEPTED_AUTHORITY__BINDING_INSIDE_CHARTER__HQ_ADOPTION_PENDING`

### Delegated authority requirements

A delegated holding is binding only if:

1. a superior governing authority creates the campaign/control jurisdiction;
2. the charter states the allowed semantic/function grain and prohibited promotions;
3. the delegated controller admits, freezes, or mandates consumption of an exact identified object/holding;
4. required proof is present and its negative ceilings are preserved;
5. downstream reliance remains inside the chartered DAG/scope;
6. the delegated holding does not claim powers expressly reserved to HQ;
7. no superior authority has stayed, corrected, or revoked it.

### Delegated authority limits

Delegated acceptance does **not** by itself:

- supersede global HQ;
- convert the delegated control branch into the global sovereign trunk;
- authorize architecture release if HQ reserved that decision;
- define the canonical global Git base;
- make every artifact on the delegated branch project-accepted;
- widen a routing holding into source or J semantic authority;
- widen campaign-current into globally-current.

### When later HQ banking is required

Later HQ banking is required when a holding is to:

- bind project work **outside** the delegated jurisdiction;
- become global-HQ authority rather than campaign authority;
- alter or replace global project sovereignty;
- authorize an action the charter expressly reserves to HQ;
- participate as a globally banked preservation/semantic/runtime holding where the parent law requires HQ adoption;
- justify selection of a new canonical global Git control base.

HQ banking is **not** required merely so that a delegated campaign can bind its own authorized workers to an exact admitted/frozen input or closed finding.

## 6. Canonical Git ancestry

Canonical Git ancestry is an orthogonal carrier relation.

W01 proves topology only. A topologically maximal branch is not thereby accepted, current, or sovereign.

Rawlsing supports at least these authority-carrier relations:

- actual Git merge/ancestral containment;
- direct downstream inheritance;
- selective/direct adoption;
- exact immutable-ref adoption;
- operational re-inheritance;
- preservation-only reference.

Semantic authority can survive off-tree. Conversely, a branch can be ancestrally downstream yet remain subordinate or nonbinding.

Therefore no Wave-2 synthesis may use `git merge-base`, commit recency, maximality, or descendant status as a substitute for an authority ruling.

## 7. Currentness doctrine

Currentness is a typed selection, not chronology.

A current object must be current **at a named grain and jurisdiction**.

Examples:

- C06 Contract@2 is current routing/carrier authority inside the Current Joint campaign's admitted routing dependency, but is not thereby globally HQ-banked routing law.
- J01-A becomes current-only denominator authority inside the campaign after the binding freeze and downstream immutable consumption; it does not become global project sovereignty.
- P10 is accepted and preservation-current as an obligation envelope, while explicitly not a live semantic owner.
- Long Memory is an inherited baseline current as a zero-loss requirement envelope, while explicitly not proving current-machine zero loss.

Thus:

`CURRENT_AT_GRAIN_X != CURRENT_AT_ALL_GRAINS`

## 8. Preservation doctrine

Preservation is an affirmative authority state, not a weak synonym for live promotion.

A preservation-only accepted object may bind future work negatively and conservationally:

- it must not be lost;
- its unresolved obligations must remain visible;
- later candidates must prove same-or-stronger conservation before retirement;
- it may constrain architecture without itself becoming runtime authority.

P10 is the decisive example: HQ accepts the complete preservation envelope, closes the campaign, and explicitly forbids live promotion, main mutation, semantic-owner installation, and carrier retirement.

Therefore:

`PRESERVATION_ONLY_ACCEPTED_CAPITAL` is project authority at preservation grain even when it is not current live runtime/semantic authority.

## 9. Evidence-only acceptance

Repository law repeatedly accepts recovered carrier material as evidence while withholding executable or semantic promotion.

Accepted evidence may be relied on **only for the proposition actually admitted**. It cannot self-upgrade by downstream convenience.

`ACCEPTED_BYTE_IDENTITY != CURRENT_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY`

`RECOVERED_CARRIER != EXECUTABLE_AUTHORITY`

## 10. Supersession doctrine

A holding is superseded/revoked only by a competent same-scope or superior authority that explicitly or functionally displaces it at the same grain.

Supersession must record:

- predecessor identity;
- successor or revocation identity;
- exact grain displaced;
- surviving narrower holdings, if any;
- proof that conservation obligations are not silently lost where relevant.

`NEWER != SUPERSEDING`

`MORE COMPLETE != SEMANTICALLY SUPERSEDING` unless the governing ruling says so.
