# WSC001H — GOLDEN THREAD 0 CONTRACT R1

## 0. Purpose

Golden Thread 0 is the smallest end-to-end proof that Rawlsing can carry a **real governed source fact** through the proposed whole spine without:

- changing its producer-owned meaning;
- widening permission;
- inventing source composition, measurement, transport, strong-noun, actor, phenomenological or rendering authority;
- converting an unresolved/blocked state into a value;
- selecting a physical architecture;
- turning population/structural evidence into biography, belief or speech.

GT0 proves **semantic conservation and jurisdiction boundaries**. It is not required to prove a rich positive developmental story.

`GT0_SEMANTIC_CONSERVATION_BASELINE@1`
