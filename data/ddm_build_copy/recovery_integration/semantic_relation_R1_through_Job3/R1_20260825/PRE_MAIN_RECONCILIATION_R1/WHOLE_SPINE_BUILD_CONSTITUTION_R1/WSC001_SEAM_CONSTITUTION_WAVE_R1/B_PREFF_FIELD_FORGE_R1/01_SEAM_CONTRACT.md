## 5. Permission law

Permissions are first-class semantic identity.

- FIELD FORGE may use only producer information permitted for the exact target use.
- Permission may narrow downstream.
- Permission may not widen because data are physically accessible, cached, joined, materialized, indexed, or queried successfully.
- Direct reference does not transfer ownership.
- A derived relation may not expose producer information beyond the producer permission ceiling.
- `DENIED` is not missing data and is not a zero.

## 6. Relation/discovery/query law

### 6.1 Authority before search

Search/discovery must operate under an already identified lawful producer/query/permission envelope. Discovery success does not self-promote into semantic authority.

### 6.2 Discovery identity

A discovered predicate intended for follow-up must freeze enough identity to be deterministically re-run, including exact query/predicate definition, producer/dependency identity, scope/risk-set, authority pins, coverage/replay receipt, and relevant negative semantics.

Exact discovered predicates may later be followed up through lawful producer re-query where same-unit facts remain dereferenceable. This is a bounded R2 result, not proof of arbitrary-future follow-up sufficiency.

### 6.3 Relation-specific representation

Identity, order, cardinal magnitude, neighborhood, topology and coordinates are distinct commitments. Representation may serve a relation; it does not authorize it.

`GEOMETRIC_COORDINATE != RELATION_AUTHORITY`.

No universal geometry primitive is created by this seam.
