## 2. Developmental sequence / phase model

### 2.1 Minimum lawful phase grammar

The inherited Dva phase authority permits only the minimum base distinction:

- `GENERATION_PENDING`
- `DEVELOPMENT_ACTIVE`

`DEVELOPMENT_ACTIVE` may be **unsegmented**. The accepted registry may lawfully contain zero finer segments. A finer developmental phase vocabulary may exist only when separately governed.

The previously proposed universal ladder

`FOUNDATIONAL -> TRANSITIONAL -> OPEN`

was withdrawn and carries no authority. It must not be resurrected as a default human-development sequence, hidden enum, visualization axis, validator expectation, or Research_Class proxy.

Therefore the whole-spine inheritance is:

`MINIMUM_PHASE_STATE != UNIVERSAL_DEVELOPMENTAL_STAGE_THEORY`.

### 2.2 Developmental sequence is causal, not merely ordinal

Within active development, the architecture requires an actor-indexed causal sequence whose links are individually governed. The conceptual chain is:

`lawful structural context`
`-> actor-specific generated binding when required`
`-> opportunity`
`-> encounter / exposure`
`-> exact mediation-route realization where governed`
`-> actor response / metabolization`
`-> bounded middle-state change where governed`
`-> possible persistent developmental change`
`-> possible rupture / recovery / reinforcement / destabilization`
`-> possible realized attractor trajectory`

The arrows above are **not automatic transition authority**. They show the separations a validator/observer must retain. Every arrow requires its own accepted producer/rule authority where Dva assigned one. Missing authority stops the transition and yields a typed non-assertion state; it never licenses a plausible shortcut.

### 2.3 Progressive generated-position sequence

Dva's accepted developmental boundary requires progressive position binding rather than structural down-imputation:

`Phase-A context with unresolved actor-position-conditioned material`
`-> governed V3 generated position / relationship / household fact or event`
`-> exact generated fact reference`
`-> Phase-B re-resolution of the same immutable structural repository`
`-> position-specific resolved context`.

Geography, prevalence, source grouping, structural applicability, or a position-like population pattern cannot themselves become the actor's class, sect, party, institution, household, family, or other actor identity.

### 2.4 Formation-aware state

The developmental architecture requires the observer to distinguish at least:

- generated actor/entity identity;
- current generated positions and their transition history;
- structural context selected for the actor;
- opportunities, encounters, exposures and route realizations;
- actor responses/effects such as buffering, amplification, deflection, blockage, rerouting or transformation only where separately governed;
- material / organismic / regulatory state without treating it as personality, morality, agency, or total well-being;
- need, expectation and striving as separate causal meanings;
- developmental middle-state material such as governed framework/buffer/crack/wound/practice/infrastructure-like state rather than event-to-trait shortcuts;
- relationship, household and genealogy state with causal parentage;
- causal memory and accessibility state;
- rupture/metabolization state;
- realized attractor trajectory, if and only if separately governed from the underlying life history;
- actor-accessibility / first-person permission and V2 expression state as downstream views, not causal replacements.

No one scalar "development," "maturity," "pressure," "well-being," "formation," or "observability" score is inherited.

## 3. Allowed transitions

An allowed transition means only that Dva assigned the transition to a lawful owner and/or accepted a rule family at that grain. It does not imply that every concrete implementation rule is complete.

### 3.1 Phase activation

`GENERATION_PENDING -> DEVELOPMENT_ACTIVE`

is allowed only under the accepted actor-initialization/developmental-phase authority. No finer universal segmentation is implied.

### 3.2 Generated binding and context refresh

An unresolved actor-position requirement may progress to a generated actor binding only through the V3-owned generated-position/relationship/household/genealogy mechanism. The resulting exact generated fact may trigger Phase-B re-resolution.

Structural/repository refresh and generated-life requery remain two channels:

1. repository/structural time boundaries may cause deterministic structural re-resolution;
2. generated actor changes such as location, position, relationship, household, family, genealogy or governed developmental-phase binding may cause caller/V3-driven requery.

The resolver may consume a generated binding as query input and echo its provenance; it does not re-author that binding as structural truth.

### 3.3 Opportunity -> encounter / exposure

Opportunity, encounter and exposure are separately observable states. A lawful transition requires the accepted opportunity/encounter/exposure realization authority and its causal/replay inputs. Structural availability or applicability alone is insufficient.

### 3.4 Encounter / exposure -> route realization

This transition is architecturally required but **not closed at concrete Dva authority grain**.

The W3 ADP-011 route-realization authority cell was released and required a future rule to bind:

- exact V555 route identity/hash;
- actor identity;
- causal parent/event;
- opportunity/exposure lineage;
- route-condition results;
- effective causal time;
- realization result;
- replay provenance.

That worker never returned. The required observability envelope is inherited; the concrete realization rule is not.

### 3.5 Route realization -> actor response

Actual route realization may feed an actor-response/metabolization court. Structural pressure itself does not become actor response. A response may be absent, deferred, blocked, contradictory, buffering, amplifying, deflecting, rerouting, transforming or otherwise governed without rewriting the structural route that was traversed.

### 3.6 Event / response -> middle state -> persistent development

A generated event or response may affect a separately governed developmental middle state. Persistence, rupture, wound, practice uptake, framework change, or durable attractor change require their own rule/causal history. No single adverse event automatically produces a wound or rupture; no single pressure automatically produces a durable trait.

### 3.7 History -> causal memory -> accessibility

Objective generated history may become causal memory only through the governed causal-memory rule. Causal memory may be inaccessible or forgotten. Current accessibility/recall and first-person claim permission remain separate from memory existence.

Thus:

`OBJECTIVE_HISTORY`
`!= CAUSAL_MEMORY`
`!= CURRENT_ACCESSIBILITY_OR_RECALL`
`!= FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM_PERMISSION`.

### 3.8 V2 candidate expression -> causal occurrence

V2 output is noncausal by default. In an in-world causal interaction, an exact rendered artifact remains a candidate pending V3 admission. Only exact-artifact admission by V3 may promote the observable utterance/action into generated causal history. Rejected candidate artifacts remain audit/noncausal material.

### 3.9 Finer phase segmentation

A transition among any future finer developmental phases is allowed only when a separately governed phase segmentation authority exists. No sequence may be inferred from age, elapsed time, questionnaire pattern, structural position, event count, material state, attractor label, or conversational style.

## 5. Formation / longitudinal memory requirements

### 5.1 Bounded causal ledger

Dva's V3 architecture requires a bounded immutable developmental event ledger, or a terminally information-equivalent mechanism, sufficient to preserve:

- event identity/hash;
- actor/entity identity;
- causal parents;
- effective causal time;
- exact structural/context/route references consumed by the event;

- admission result where an external event is involved;
- RNG stream/candidate-set receipt where a governing rule lawfully consumes randomness;
- generated state transition caused by the event;
- replay identity;
- genealogy/relationship parentage where relevant.

Unchanged time ticks are not events. Checkpoints are not events. Derived views are not events. Render artifacts are not events merely because they are stored.

### 5.2 Checkpoints and derived state

Authoritative stored causal state, checkpoints, and pure derived views must remain distinguishable. Derived state may be cached only as a recomputable view under its governing derivation authority; cache persistence does not make it causal.

Migration/replay must preserve unknown, missing, not-applicable and valid-empty states. Historical fields that imply obsolete independent personality/type/default-life authority must not be silently migrated into causal truth.

### 5.3 Causal memory

Memory observability must permit separate inspection of:

- objective event/history existence;
- whether a causal-memory record was formed;
- forgetting/retention state where governed;
- current accessibility/recall state;
- first-person claim permission;
- V2 session/transcript references.

A validator must be able to identify the exact layer at which information is absent rather than report only "memory missing."

### 5.4 Genealogy and relationship longitudinality

Parents, partners, households and children are generated persons/relations/events, not structural priors. Genealogical provenance is a finite causal DAG. The observer must retain parent/partner/household event lineage sufficient to distinguish inherited causal environment from source/population priors.

Future child events may not retroactively become causes of earlier parent events.

### 5.5 Multi-timescale scheduling and replay

Dva permits a hybrid causal schedule composed of discrete events, deterministic structural context-change boundaries, generated-life state changes, separately governed slow-process cadence, and optional checkpoints. A recovered annual loop is not inherited as universal temporal ontology.

Replay requires that the same immutable repository bundle, resolver policy, runtime rule registry, initial conditions, admitted external events, and named random streams reproduce the same generated causal history/checkpoint hashes at the governed replay grain.

## 7. Abstention taxonomy

Dva inheritance requires a typed taxonomy of **why the machine did not assert or realize a developmental proposition**. Some routed artifacts provide exact tokens; others provide exact semantic distinctions without one globally canonical enum. This file preserves that distinction and does not falsely normalize all cases into a new universal enum.

### 7.1 Authority / permission abstention

Keep distinct:

- missing governing authority (`MISSING_AUTHORITY` where that exact state is used);
- denied use / producer permission denial;
- valid-empty because no governed rule exists;
- valid-empty because no eligible authorized basis exists;
- actor-accessibility/first-person permission denied;
- separately gated physical producer publication absent.

A missing authority is not a negative fact. A denied use is not evidence that the underlying proposition is false. A valid-empty registry is lawful semantic output, not an invitation to fill the gap heuristically.

### 7.2 Evidence / comparison abstention

Keep distinct:

- noncomparability;
- uncertainty;
- absence of required evidence;
- temporal limit / unsupported temporal comparison;
- proxy fidelity insufficient for the requested use;
- semantic-equivalence unresolved where a governed evaluator cannot positively establish it.

Where W2's translation/equivalence boundary applies, missing evaluator authority may yield the exact failure meanings `MISSING_PLAN_ALIGNMENT_AUTHORITY` and/or `EQUIVALENCE_UNRESOLVED`; pinned model provenance cannot override them.

### 7.3 Dependency / context abstention

Keep distinct:

- deferred structural context;
- deferred runtime position;
- unresolved generated position;
- deferred actor/relationship/household/genealogy history;
- route blocked;
- query-relative not-applicable;
- prerequisite not yet realized/satisfied;
- external event candidate pending V3 admission.

These states indicate different future dependencies and must not collapse into a generic pending flag if downstream diagnosis needs to know which producer or causal event can change the state.

### 7.4 Integrity / replay abstention

Keep distinct:

- broken/missing exact reference;
- hash/content mismatch;
- build/publication integrity failure;
- stale causal parent/cursor;
- replay mismatch;
- duplicate-submission conflict;
- missing required receipt/provenance.

An integrity failure is not semantic rejection.

### 7.5 Causal nonoccurrence

Keep distinct, at the exact governing contract grain:

- lawful no-op;
- rejected external-event candidate with zero mutation;
- route not realized despite route existence;
- opportunity not realized as encounter/exposure;
- response absent despite exposure;
- no persistent change despite response/event;
- no rupture despite adversity;
- no attractor transition despite prerequisite evidence.

A causal nonoccurrence may itself be informative longitudinal evidence. It does not authorize the observer to infer an opposite trait or disposition.
