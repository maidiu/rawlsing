## 0. Governing law

A plausible story, a source construct, a statistical association, a study coefficient, a theory label, or a mechanism-class mapping is not enough to mint an executable D1' rule.

Whenever the evidentiary chain cannot establish the requested governed transformation at the requested scope:

`UNLICENSED / NEEDS SEPARATE RULE AUTHORITY`.

## 11. Source constructs barred from direct runtime fact/operator status

These constructs may be analyst/theory labels, explanatory summaries, hypothesis vocabulary or source-side measurement concepts. The corpus does **not** license their direct promotion into D1' runtime facts/operators:

- `CLASS` or contradictory class location as a person essence;
- `SOCIAL_REPRODUCTION`, `TOTALITY`, `CO-CONSTITUTION`, `CARE_CRISIS`, `OPPRESSION` as causal operators;
- generic `SOCIAL_CAPITAL` stock/scalar;
- generic `CULTURE`, `MACROSYSTEM`, `ECOLOGICAL_LAYER` effect;
- `HABITUS`, `HEGEMONY`, `SOCIALIZATION`, `INTERNALIZATION` without a specified concrete route;
- Holzkamp `HANDLUNGSFÄHIGKEIT`, restrictive/generalized action-capacity person types, subjective premise/reason or subjective possibility-space as PRE-V3 facts;
- Sève's Sector I/Sector II, P/N scalar, fixed-capital analogy, four-cycle topology, falling-rate-of-progress hypothesis or theory-specific use-time topology as runtime operators;
- `TURNING_POINT` as intrinsic event type;
- `CUMULATIVE_ADVANTAGE/DISADVANTAGE` as generic multiplier;
- `LINKED_LIVES` as a transfer operator;
- `RESILIENCE` as an adversity benefit rule;
