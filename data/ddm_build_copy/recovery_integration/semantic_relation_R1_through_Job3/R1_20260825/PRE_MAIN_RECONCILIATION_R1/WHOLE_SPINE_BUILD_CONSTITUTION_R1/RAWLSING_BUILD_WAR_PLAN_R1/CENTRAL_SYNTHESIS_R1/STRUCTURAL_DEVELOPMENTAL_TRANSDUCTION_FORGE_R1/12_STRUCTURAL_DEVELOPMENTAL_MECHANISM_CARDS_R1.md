## MC01 — MATERIAL_RESOURCE_SECURITY_DEPENDENCY

**SOURCE:** Marx *Capital* I chs. 6/23; Wright; T3; Elder/T4/T5 empirical comparators.  
**CLAIM STATUS:** theoretical + observational/longitudinal support.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** property relation, wage relation, income/service entitlement, asset/access rule.  
**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** control/noncontrol of means/resources changes material security, exit options, bargaining dependency and recurrent need to traverse particular institutions/markets.  
**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** resources, scarcity, dependency, eligibility, exit options, time pressure.  
**REALIZATION LAW:** actor must occupy the governed relation and the resource/right/deprivation must be operative in the bound world/time/context.  
**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** action may preserve, exit, negotiate or collectively alter the relation; perception is not required for objective material consequence.  
**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** only downstream through realized strain/activity/choice/learning/history.  
**TEMPORAL FORM:** relation + condition + cumulative process.  
**RELATIONAL SCALE:** household/organization/class relation/state.  
**RECIPROCITY:** action/collective organization can alter later resource relation.  
**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** historical regime, household sharing, institutional eligibility, alternative resources.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `CLASS/POVERTY != TRAIT`; resource availability != mobilization.  
**RAWLSING MAPPING:** PRE-V3 structural pressure/affordance + actor/context binding; narrow OBL058/059 applicability exists; general actor-relative resource/dependency derivation undergeneralized.  
**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE + NEW_CONNECTIVE_SEMANTICS_REQUIRED`.

## Normalization terminal

The twenty cards reduce the cross-tradition corpus to a small set of causal functions. No theory name is required as ontology. The dominant architectural result is not `NEW_PSYCHOLOGY`; it is a need to **generalize and connect actor-indexed social-ecology derivation, persistence/rebinding, and reciprocal world-update semantics while reusing PRE-V3 possibility and existing V3 metabolism/development owners**.
