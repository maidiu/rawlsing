# M3 SÈVE ACTS ↔ CAPACITIES COURT R1

STATUS: `COMPLETE__ACTS_CAPACITIES_SPECIAL_COURT_R1`

## 1. Exact source basis

Primary passages: Ch. IV pp. 304–315, 356–369, 381–384; Third-edition Postscript pp. 456–460. All are subject to Sève's own Ch. IV “indicative hypothesis” ceiling at pp. 297–303 and later reaffirmations at pp. 487–488.

## 2. What Sève actually proposes

Sève's minimum loop is:

```text
CURRENT CAPACITY
-> ENABLES SOME ACT
-> ACT IS ACTUALLY PERFORMED IN A SOCIAL ACTIVITY
-> SOME SETS OF ACTS MAY PRODUCE / DEVELOP / MAINTAIN CAPACITY
-> CHANGED CAPACITY ALTERS LATER FEASIBLE ACTIVITY
```

The strongest source qualification occurs in the same pages that introduce sector I/sector II: concrete acts can belong to both capacity-forming and capacity-using activity, and in a broad sense every activity may combine learning and exercise. Sector I/II is therefore an analytical distinction, not a source-validated binary classification.

## 3. Capacity → act

**Source claim:** capacity is an “actual potentiality” to carry out acts. It is a precondition, not the act itself.

**M3 reading:** this is a lawful separation useful to Rawlsing:

```text
CAPACITY_AVAILABLE != ACTION_OCCURRED
```

An actor may possess a capacity but lack opportunity, time, motive, authorization, counterpart, or institutional gate to exercise it.

## 4. Acts → capacity development

**Source claim:** many capacities are produced/developed by sets of acts.

**M3 qualification:** the book does **not** establish a universal dose-response rule, a repetition threshold, a monotonic learning coefficient, or a rule that every act improves the capacity it uses. The source itself makes classification difficult and later treats its developmental “laws” as hypothetical.

Required Rawlsing firewall:

```text
ACT OCCURRED
!= CAPACITY DEVELOPED

REPEATED ACTS
!= CAPACITY DEVELOPED

CAPACITY USED
!= CAPACITY IMPROVED
```

A competent downstream developmental owner must decide whether the realized activity produces acquisition, maintenance, transfer, fatigue, under-use, atrophy, or no durable effect.

## 5. Positive feedback

Sève's scheme permits a **typed positive feedback possibility**:

```text
capacity_t
-> feasible act_t
-> capacity-forming activity_t
-> capacity_t+1
-> enlarged feasible activity_t+1
```

But “permits” is the correct source status. It is not a validated general positive-feedback law. Whether the loop amplifies depends on actual activity content, time, opportunity to use new capacities, and social gates.

**Verdict:** `THEORETICALLY_SUPPORTED_FEEDBACK_SCHEMA__NO_GENERIC_GAIN_OPERATOR`.

## 6. Depletion / maintenance / atrophy

Sève's expanded-reproduction discussion implies maintenance costs and discusses under-use/atrophy, especially where activity is withdrawn. The old-age section also explicitly preserves biological/social qualifications.

**Verdict:** `POSSIBLE_MAINTENANCE_AND_ATROPHY_SCHEMA__NO_UNIVERSAL_DEPLETION_LAW`.

Do not create a generic capacity-decay scalar from this source.

## 7. Opportunity cost

This is the strongest acts-capacities result added by the full-book read. Capacity formation consumes finite use-time. At p. 340 Sève explicitly gives a simplifying example in which more learning time means less time available for activity using existing capacities.

Required compositional law:

```text
FINITE ACTOR TIME
+ CAPACITY-FORMING ACTIVITY REQUIREMENT
+ CAPACITY-USING / CARE / WORK / RECOVERY / OTHER REQUIREMENTS
-> COMPETING TIME CLAIMS
-> REALIZED ACTIVITY COMPOSITION
```

**Verdict:** `STRONGLY_SUPPORTED_AS_THEORETICAL_ALLOCATION_CONSTRAINT__NOT_EMPIRICAL_OPTIMUM`.

## 8. Sequence dependence

The capacity/activity loop is path-dependent in Sève's scheme because current capacities condition future acts and biography is a finite ordered history. Later activities can also reorganize earlier acquired structures/material.

**Verdict:** `SUPPORTED_AS_THEORETICAL_TRAJECTORY_REQUIREMENT`.

No source passage supplies a universal order-sensitive transition function.

## 9. Threshold effects

The book uses language of crisis, stagnation, reorganization, proportionality, and correspondence, but supplies no empirically established threshold at which repetition or capacity mismatch must trigger a transition.

**Verdict:** `NO_GENERAL_THRESHOLD_LAW_PROVED__DEFER`.

## 10. Sector I / sector II court

Retain only the functional distinction:

```text
ACTIVITY MAY CONTRIBUTE TO CAPACITY FORMATION/MAINTENANCE
versus
ACTIVITY MAY PRIMARILY USE EXISTING CAPACITY FOR ANOTHER PRODUCT
```

Reject as Rawlsing ontology:

- a permanent `SECTOR_I` person store;
- a permanent `SECTOR_II` person store;
- a hard partition of every act;
- a fixed optimal ratio;
- the economic “fixed capital” analogy as a psychological law.

## 11. Jurisdiction court

### Upstream / seam jurisdiction

The seam may lawfully own only the objective conditions of capacity-relevant activity:

- task/activity availability or requirement;
- actual schedule/time constraint;
- counterpart/institutional access;
- recurrence identity and duration;
- realized act/activity occurrence when type-compatible;
- provenance from the social relation that structured the activity.

### V3 jurisdiction

V3 should retain:

- learning;
- durable skill/capacity update;
- practice change;
- memory;
- motivational/need reorganization;
- transfer/generalization;
- path-dependent person state.

### Seam-spanning composition

```text
D1: objective actor-relative activity/time ecology
+
D2-compatible interval/recurrence history
-> actual acts / recurrent activity
-> V3 capacity update if earned
-> changed V3 state can later alter feasible action/use
-> any changed external ecology still requires a real gate/world response
```

## 12. D1/D2/D3 adjudication

- **D1:** `SUPPORTED_AND_STRENGTHENED` — especially `ACTIVITY_OR_TASK_OPPORTUNITY_REQUIREMENT` and `TIME_OR_SCHEDULE_CONSTRAINT`.
- **D2:** `SUPPORTED_AS_SEMANTIC_REQUIREMENT__NO_STANDALONE_OWNER_PROVED` — recurrence, duration, sequence and before/after requalification matter.
- **D3:** `NOT_REQUIRED_FOR_INTERNAL_CAPACITY_UPDATE`; only required if action changes external social conditions through lawful response.
