## 0. Executive verdict

The finite-person frontier question survives the no-duplication test, but only in a narrow form.

Rawlsing already has most of the required nouns and process families:

- need-relevant meanings;
- current need pressure / urgency;
- recruitment;
- salience;
- conscious accessibility;
- attentional focality / present attentional priority;
- appraisal;
- expectation;
- motive / striving / pursuit;
- memory and history;
- recurrent bias / attractor gravity;
- hysteresis and sensitization;
- developmental perturbation and plasticity.

Therefore Rawlsing does **not** presently justify a new instinct subsystem, an instinct type, an instinct stack, a motivational-weight vector, or a second need ontology.

A semantic remainder nevertheless remains:

> A person can display a durable, history-shaped, context-sensitive, and overrideable tendency for some **classes of need-relevant concern** to obtain preferential claim on recruitment, attention, appraisal, and effort when multiple live concerns compete, even when no single currently strongest need, focal concern, explicit goal, or recent residue explains that repeated pattern.

This remainder is best banked as a **connective prioritization relation**, not as a new state kind.

Current working terms:

`MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY`

and, more precisely where ambiguity matters:

`PREFERENTIAL_CONCERN_CLAIM`

These are provisional semantic handles, not architecture names.

Strongest present holding:

`DISTINCT_SEMANTIC_RELATION = YES`

`DISTINCT_ARCHITECTURAL_PRIMITIVE = NOT ESTABLISHED`

`DISTINCT_INSTINCT_SUBSYSTEM = NO`

`FIXED_INSTINCT_TYPE / STACK = REJECT`

`BIRTH_PROFILE = NOT INFERRED`

## 1. Exact question

File `61` asks whether the Enneagram-adjacent intuition called instinctual priority is already fully implicit in Rawlsing's existing concepts or whether a real explanatory remainder remains.

The no-gadget test is:

> If need axes + present salience + attention + recruitment + motive/pursuit + recurrent bias already explain the phenomenon without loss, do not mint anything else.

The counter-question is:

> Can two persons encounter broadly comparable need-relevant situations, with no decisive difference in current need intensity, yet repeatedly organize attention and effort around different classes of concern in a way that is coherent across situations and histories?

If yes, Rawlsing needs language for that **relational priority tendency**, while still refusing deterministic typing.

## 2. Cross-check against existing Rawlsing concepts

### 2.1 CAN-010 need axes do not already solve actor priority

File `24` preserves the V3 need contract and makes a critical distinction: the need axes are reference semantics, not actor birth profiles, temperament classes, or priority rankings.

A need axis answers roughly:

> What kind of humanly relevant concern is implicated?

It does not by itself answer:

> Which classes of concern tend to obtain earlier or stronger claim on this person's finite attention and motivational organization under competition?

Therefore:

`NEED_DOMAIN != PERSON_PRIORITY`

`NEED_REFERENCE_SEMANTICS != PRIORITY_ORDER`

No second need ontology is required.

### 2.2 Current need magnitude / urgency is state pressure, not durable priority

A safety concern can become extremely urgent for almost anyone under sufficiently strong conditions. A belonging concern can dominate one evening after a rupture. Recognition can become focal after public evaluation.

Those cases concern current state.

The frontier asks about a different phenomenon: whether some classes of concern **recurrently gain claim more readily** for a particular person across multiple situations, including cases where the immediate environment does not force a universal ordering.

Therefore:

`CURRENT_NEED_INTENSITY != DURABLE_MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY`

A priority relation may influence which need-relevant material becomes active, but it cannot replace current need magnitude or environmental constraint.

### 2.3 Present attentional priority / focality already exists and must not be duplicated

Files `10`, `31`, and `37` already separate:

- activation;
- accessibility;
- focality / attentional priority;
- causal influence;
- intention;
- expression.

That is important existing PFF law.

Present attentional priority answers:

> What has the strongest current claim on limited attention now?

The proposed remainder answers:

> What classes of concern tend, all else not decisive, to gain such claim more readily for this person across changing situations?

Therefore:

`PREFERENTIAL_CONCERN_CLAIM != CURRENT_FOCALITY`

The first can bias the formation of the second without determining it.

### 2.4 Recruitment is a process, not the whole explanation for repeated domain preference

Files `14` and `40` treat retrieval/recruitment as psychic activation rather than a literal database search. Need-state, memory, relationship meaning, expectation, unresolved tension, self-model, and current context can recruit material into the live field.

That process family is already broad enough to realize priority effects later.

But saying simply:

`some material is recruited`

does not yet explain why one person's recruitment repeatedly gives earlier claim to status implications while another person's repeatedly gives earlier claim to attachment, autonomy, coherence, or safety implications under otherwise non-decisive competition.

Therefore:

`MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY MAY BIAS RECRUITMENT`

but:

`MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY != RECRUITMENT`

No new recruitment engine is implied.

### 2.5 Striving / pursuit is downstream organization around an object

CAN-010 and Files `24` and `54` preserve motive, striving, pursuit, frustration, and conflicting objects.

A striving answers roughly:

> What is the person trying to secure, avoid, preserve, repair, become, or accomplish here?

The priority remainder concerns a prior or concurrent selection pressure:

> Which kinds of concern tend to become motivationally privileged enough to organize such striving?

A person can also hold several conflicting strivings at once.

Therefore:

`PREFERENTIAL_CONCERN_CLAIM != CURRENT_STRIVING`

`PRIORITY != SINGLE_STRONGEST_MOTIVE`

### 2.6 Recurrent bias / attractor gravity is broader than concern-domain priority

Files `55` and `56` bank probabilistic attractor gravity and topology: histories can make some psychic organizations easier to re-enter, with multiple routes, basins, escapes, competing pulls, and overrideability.

That machinery family is intentionally broad.

A recurrent attractor may concern:

- a canonical problem-form;
- a defensive solution;
- a relationship role;
- an interpretive pattern;
- a pursuit/frustration loop;
- a self-model configuration;
- a need-relevant concern.

Motivational priority is narrower. It concerns **competitive claim among classes of concern**, not recurrence in general.

Therefore:

`MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY CAN PARTICIPATE_IN ATTRACTOR_DYNAMICS`

but:

`MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY != ATTRACTOR_GRAVITY_IN_GENERAL`

This is another reason not to create a new mechanism family now: existing recurrent-bias / attractor concepts may eventually realize the relation.

### 2.7 Hysteresis / sensitization explains path-dependent carryover, not baseline cross-situation priority

File `12` distinguishes continuation, inertia, residue, priming, sensitization, rebound, accumulation, dormancy, and hysteresis.

A recent humiliation can temporarily lower the threshold for status-related interpretations. A recent rupture can temporarily sensitize attachment-related recruitment.

That does not establish that status or attachment has a durable preferential claim for the person outside that live trajectory.

Therefore:

`RECENT_SENSITIZATION != DURABLE_MOTIVATIONAL_PRIORITY`

although recent state can temporarily amplify, suppress, or reverse the person's longer-run tendency.

### 2.8 Developmental perturbation and history supply lawful shaping without deterministic molding

File `58` preserves the bridge:

`STRUCTURED_WORLD -> PERSPECTIVE_LIMITED_LIVED_PERTURBATION -> PERSON_SPECIFIC_METABOLIZATION -> TRAJECTORY`

This is directly compatible with motivational priority.

Repeated environments, remembered outcomes, relationships, practices, developmental sensitivities, and self-organizing history may shape what repeatedly gains claim.

But:

`EXPOSURE != FIXED_PRIORITY`

`SAME_EXPOSURE != SAME_PRIORITY_TRAJECTORY`

`SIMILAR_PRIORITY != SAME_CAUSAL_HISTORY`

Equifinality and multifinality remain mandatory.

## 3. What the Enneagram witness contributes — and what Rawlsing rejects

File `05` correctly uses the Enneagram only as a conceptual witness.

The useful witness is not the imported three-instinct taxonomy itself. The useful question is whether a psychologically rich person requires something like:

> stable-but-not-absolute differences in what kinds of concerns claim attention and energy first.

Rawlsing can retain that question while rejecting:

- three canonical instinct buckets as project law;
- instinctual subtype labels;
- primary/secondary stack typing;
- fixed dominance rankings;
- deterministic behavioral predictions;
- personality inference from a small set of observed choices;
- claims that priority is innate merely because it is durable.

Therefore the source theory supplies a **phenomenological challenge**, not an ontology.

---

## 4. The surviving semantic remainder

A useful minimum semantic proposition is:

> **Preferential concern claim** is a person-specific, history-shaped, context-sensitive, probabilistic, and overrideable relation by which some classes of need-relevant concern tend to obtain motivational access to limited recruitment, attention, appraisal, anticipation, and effort more readily than competing concerns when the situation does not itself determine a universal ordering.

This wording is deliberately relational.

It does **not** say that a person possesses a hidden object called an instinct.

It does **not** say that one domain always dominates.

It does **not** require a scalar weight.

It does **not** guarantee conscious awareness.

It does **not** directly cause behavior.

It does **not** prevent contradictory action.

It does **not** preclude learning, repair, development, relationship-specific reorganization, or temporary reversal.

---

## 5. Why the relation matters for synthetic personhood

Without this relation, two otherwise richly modeled persons can still converge toward a shallow strongest-signal system:

```text
largest current need pressure
-> largest salience
-> current goal
-> behavior
```

That loses a common form of coherent human difference.

Consider two people facing the same ambiguous invitation into a new group.

Both may care about:

- belonging;
- recognition;
- autonomy;
- safety;
- epistemic clarity.

Person A may rapidly organize around:

`Will I be welcomed / excluded?`

Person B around:

`Will I be constrained / controlled?`

Person C around:

`What is really going on here?`

Person D around:

`How will I be positioned / regarded?`

Any of them can later shift concern, override the first pull, behave against it, or discover that the situation makes another concern objectively urgent.

The psychologically important difference is not merely the final action. It is the **route by which finite attention and motive become organized**.

That route can generate coherent surprise while preserving agency and context sensitivity.

## 6. Priority is distributed and conditional, not a total ranking

Rawlsing should not assume:

```text
priority(A) > priority(B) > priority(C) forever
```

The relation may be:

- context-sensitive;
- relationship-specific;
- object-specific;
- developmental-stage-sensitive;
- state-modulated;
- multi-domain;
- conflict-bearing;
- nonlinear;
- locally reversible.

A person may usually privilege autonomy concerns in institutional settings while belonging concerns obtain first claim with one attachment figure.

A person may privilege epistemic coherence when calm but safety when exhausted or threatened.

A person may notice recognition implications early while consciously disavowing them.

Therefore:

`DURABLE_TENDENCY != TOTAL_ORDER`

`DURABLE_TENDENCY != GLOBAL_DOMINANCE`

`DURABLE_TENDENCY != CURRENT_WINNER`

## 7. Consciousness, intention, and expression firewalls

The relation must respect the matrix already banked in Files `10`, `31`, and `37`.

A preferentially recruited concern may be:

- strongly active but inaccessible;
- accessible but not focal;
- focal but rejected;
- interpreted incorrectly by the actor;
- intentionally overridden;
- concealed in expression;
- indirectly leaked;
- transformed into another consciously available rationale.

Therefore:

`PRIORITY_EFFECT != AWARENESS`

`PRIORITY_EFFECT != INTENTION`

`PRIORITY_EFFECT != EXPRESSION`

`PRIORITY_EFFECT != SELF_EXPLANATION`

This prevents the concept from becoming a covert narration device that tells the generated person why they “really” did something.

## 8. Seed / trajectory / plasticity firewall

No present evidence authorizes Rawlsing to decide whether a person's preferential concern claim originates from:

- organismic sensitivity;
- early learning;
- attachment history;
- repeated social position;
- cultural shaping;
- reinforcement;
- deliberate practice;
- self-model development;
- interaction among several of these;
- other lawful developmental causes.

Therefore:

`DURABLE_PRIORITY != INNATE_PRIORITY`

`EARLY_PRIORITY != IMMUTABLE_PRIORITY`

`OBSERVED_PRIORITY != BIRTH_PROFILE`

The correct pre-architecture stance is to preserve both possible seed differences and trajectory formation without deciding the mixture prematurely.

File `58`'s equifinality/multifinality constraint is controlling:

- similar histories may produce different priority organizations;
- different histories may produce similar priority organizations.

The arrows are explanatory prose, not an adopted pipeline.

## 12. Synthetic-person test obligations

The concept earns its keep only if it improves personhood under tests like these:

### Test A — same event, different first concern

Two persons receive the same ambiguous social cue. Different need-relevant implications repeatedly gain early claim without forcing different overt behavior.

### Test B — same person, context override

A person's usual concern bias is overridden by an objectively urgent threat, a strongly valued relationship, explicit principle, or deliberate choice.

### Test C — hidden priority effect

A concern repeatedly shapes interpretation/recruitment while remaining only partly accessible to consciousness.

### Test D — developmental change

A long period of lived experience changes which concerns tend to gain claim without requiring a categorical type conversion.

### Test E — relationship specificity

A person's broad tendency changes locally with a particular counterpart without erasing the wider organization.

### Test F — conflict

Two high-claim concern classes remain simultaneously active, producing ambivalence or oscillation rather than a clean winner.

### Test G — no behavioral determinism

Two episodes with similar initial concern priority yield different actions because appraisal, principle, expectation, relationship meaning, opportunity, or conscious override differs.

If the relation cannot pass these tests without collapsing into a simple weight, it has been modeled incorrectly.
