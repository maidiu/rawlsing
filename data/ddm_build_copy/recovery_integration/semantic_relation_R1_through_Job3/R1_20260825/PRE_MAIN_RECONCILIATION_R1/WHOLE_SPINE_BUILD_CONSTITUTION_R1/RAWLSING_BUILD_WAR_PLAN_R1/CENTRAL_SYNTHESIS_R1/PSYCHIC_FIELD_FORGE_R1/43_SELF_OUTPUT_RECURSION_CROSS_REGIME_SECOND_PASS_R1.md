## 1. Primary conclusion

The strongest current model is:

> **Self-output feedback is not a separate psychological mechanism. Once the actor's action or utterance actually occurs, that occurrence becomes a new actor-facing event that can be perceived, interpreted, recruited, anticipated, and responded to by the same person through the same general recursive machinery used for any other meaningful event.**

Therefore the basic loop is:

`PERSON`
`-> situated action / expression formation`
`-> renderer or action realization`
`-> ACTUAL OCCURRENCE`
`-> actor perceives own occurred act`
`-> situated recruitment / awareness / metaperception / anticipation`
`-> next response`.

Durable consequences return only through exact V3 owners.

Central laws:

`GENERATED CANDIDATE != OCCURRED OUTPUT`.

`OCCURRED OUTPUT != PROOF OF PRIOR HIDDEN MOTIVE`.

`SELF_OBSERVATION != PRIOR_CAUSAL_SELF_KNOWLEDGE`.

`SELF_INTERPRETATION != OBJECTIVE CAUSAL TRUTH`.

`OWN_OCCURRED_ACTION = POSSIBLE NEXT ACTOR-FACING INPUT`.

`SELF_OUTPUT_RECURSION = SHARED PERSON PHENOMENON`.

## 2. The occurrence gate is essential

The first pass already separated:

1. selected/formed expression semantics;
2. rendered candidate;
3. actually emitted/occurred expression;
4. actor registration/perception of the occurrence;
5. local consequences;
6. any durable V3 consequences.

The second pass treats this separation as constitutional.

A draft sentence that was never emitted cannot:

- embarrass the actor;
- bind the actor publicly;
- change what the counterpart heard;
- become a real relationship event;
- create a memory of saying it;
- count as evidence in the actor's own life.

Therefore:

`MODEL DRAFT -/-> SELF_FEEDBACK`.

`RENDERED BUT NONOCCURRED CANDIDATE -/-> SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE`.

Only the lawfully occurred act may re-enter the person's experienced event stream.

---

## 3. Own output is one special source class of ordinary input

After occurrence, the act is unusual because the actor is both producer and observer.

But that does not require a new general psychology.

The actor can perceive:

- exact words they just said;
- tone/force/extent as lawfully observable;
- that they disclosed something;
- that they interrupted;
- that they made a promise;
- that they insulted someone;
- that they hesitated or changed direction;
- that an action did or did not match conscious intent.

The same event can then recruit:

- self-model;
- shame/status concerns;
- values;
- memories;
- relationship expectations;
- anticipated counterpart reaction;
- defensive mediators;
- current motives.

Thus:

`SELF_OUTPUT_FEEDBACK` is better decomposed into:

`OWN_OCCURRED_EVENT`
`+ SELF-PERCEPTION`
`+ ORDINARY SITUATED RECRUITMENT`
`+ SELF-INTERPRETATION / METAPERCEPTION / ANTICIPATION`.

No separate feedback ontology is yet justified.

---

## 4. The actor may know what they did without knowing why

This is a central opacity case.

A can know:

`I just made a cutting joke.`

while not knowing:

`I did it partly because I felt exposed and wanted to regain status.`

Likewise the machine may have a multi-causal account while the actor forms a different explanation:

- `I was just joking.`;
- `I guess I'm angrier than I thought.`;
- `He deserved it.`;
- `Why did I say that?`;
- `Maybe I wanted to hurt him.`.

Therefore:

`ACTION KNOWLEDGE != MOTIVE KNOWLEDGE`.

`SELF-OBSERVED FORM != CAUSAL SELF-TRANSPARENCY`.

This is one of the strongest synthetic-personhood requirements because a human-like actor must be able to surprise, misread, defend against, or gradually discover themselves.

---

## 5. Renderer variation must remain forward-only evidence

The expression second pass separated:

`UPSTREAM INTENDED / CONSTRAINED SEMANTICS`

from:

`RENDERER REALIZATION VARIATION`

from:

`OCCURRED OUTPUT`.

Suppose the renderer chooses a metaphor, emphasis, or lexical form not specifically dictated by upstream psychology but still within lawful semantic bounds.

Once emitted, the actor may notice that wording as part of what they said.

But the system may not reason backward:

`renderer happened to choose phrase P`
`therefore hidden memory/motive M must have caused P before rendering`.

Hard law:

`RENDERER VARIATION = POSSIBLE NEW FORWARD EVENT DETAIL AFTER OCCURRENCE`.

`RENDERER VARIATION != RETROACTIVE PRIOR-PSYCHE EVIDENCE`.

This blocks renderer creativity from secretly authoring the actor's past unconscious state.

## 6. Self-surprise falls out of the opacity architecture

No special `surprise engine` is required.

Self-surprise becomes possible whenever:

1. the actor had a pre-output expectation/intention about their own act;
2. the occurred act differs materially from that expectation or reveals an unanticipated implication;
3. the actor can perceive that mismatch;
4. the cause of the mismatch is not fully actor-accessible.

Example:

A intends to tell a neutral story.

The lawful expression becomes sharper and more emotionally pointed than A expected.

After occurrence A notices:

`That came out harsher than I meant.`

or:

`Why did I bring that up?`.

This does not require the machine to script surprise in advance.

`PRE_ACT SELF-EXPECTATION × OCCURRED OUTPUT MISMATCH -> POSSIBLE SELF-SURPRISE`.

---

## 7. Own output changes metaperception immediately

The actor's act can alter what they think the counterpart now knows, suspects, expects, or sees before the counterpart responds.

Examples:

- `Now she knows I care.`;
- `He probably realizes I lied.`;
- `I showed too much.`;
- `Now they think I'm incompetent.`;
- `She knows I remembered.`.

These are actor-local hypotheses.

They do not establish the counterpart's actual state.

Thus:

`A'S OUTPUT OCCURRED`
`-> A'S MODEL OF B'S UPDATED VIEW`

while:

`A'S MODEL OF B'S UPDATED VIEW != B'S ACTUAL VIEW`.

Self-output therefore naturally recurses into the existing metaperception/asymmetry problem.

## 8. Self-output can alter the conscious field without durable update

An occurred act may make something newly foregrounded:

- embarrassment;
- guilt;
- affection;
- contradiction;
- uncertainty;
- relief;
- an associated memory;
- awareness of a desire;
- awareness of having crossed a line.

This is a live PFF change.

It does not automatically produce:

- durable new belief;
- durable memory-access state;
- durable self-model revision;
- durable relationship truth;
- durable attractor change.

Current law:

`POST_OUTPUT LIVE AWARENESS CHANGE != AUTOMATIC V3 DURABLE UPDATE`.

The same local insight may later become durable through exact V3 admission or disappear/reconfigure as the interaction continues.

---

## 9. Self-inference is evidence-based but nonunique

Own behavior is legitimate evidence about the self.

But action is multiply caused.

Repeatedly defending B might support actor hypotheses such as:

- `I care about B.`;
- `I think B is being treated unfairly.`;
- `I enjoy arguing.`;
- `I feel responsible for B.`;
- `I'm threatened by the criticism.`.

No one action uniquely identifies motive.

Therefore:

`OWN ACTION = POSSIBLE SELF-EVIDENCE`.

`OWN ACTION != UNIQUE MOTIVE DECODER`.

`REPEATED PATTERN MAY SUPPORT GRADUAL SELF-HYPOTHESIS`.

This is highly compatible with actor epistemic uncertainty rather than a special self-discovery authority.

---

## 10. Enacted self-discovery can be gradual and recursive

The actor may discover themselves through a sequence rather than a single insight event:

`act`
`-> notice`
`-> tentative interpretation`
`-> counterpart response`
`-> reinterpret prior act`
`-> new act`
`-> notice recurrence`
`-> stronger self-hypothesis`.

Example:

A repeatedly mentions B, seeks B's reaction, defends B, becomes jealous, and denies interest.

The machine need not force `A is now consciously in love` after any one step.

The actor may eventually think:

`Maybe I care about B more than I thought.`

That hypothesis has its own history and can be mistaken.

This is a major reason to preserve:

`MACHINE CAUSAL ACCOUNT != ACTOR DISCOVERY TIMELINE`.

---

## 11. Doubling-down, repair, rationalization and curiosity are all ordinary next responses

After self-observation, several routes can be recruited:

### Repair

- apologize;
- retract;
- clarify;
- soften;
- disclose more accurately.

### Defensive persistence

- justify;
- blame;
- minimize;
- escalate;
- insist the act was intentional.

### Reflective opening

- pause;
- ask why the act happened;
- acknowledge contradiction;
- hold uncertainty.

### Relief / commitment

- feel less concealment pressure;
- experience public statement as self-binding;
- feel newly obligated by a promise.

These are not special self-output transition classes. They are normal response possibilities after a new event has changed the live configuration.

`SELF_OUTPUT EVENT -> ORDINARY NEXT RESPONSE DYNAMICS`.

---

## 12. Social commitment is not belief update

An actually uttered promise, declaration, accusation, or public stance can alter future pressures.

The person may now anticipate:

- inconsistency costs;
- reputation effects;
- relationship expectations;
- obligation;
- face loss if they retreat.

But saying X does not prove believing X, and public commitment does not automatically increase epistemic conviction.

Therefore:

`PUBLIC COMMITMENT PRESSURE != BELIEF CHANGE`.

`SAYING X != KNOWING X`.

`SAYING X != DURABLE STRIVING CREATION`.

This prevents output from overwriting upstream epistemics merely to maintain narrative consistency.

---

## 13. Self-output can lawfully become a memory/recruitment cue only through the same upstream boundaries

An own action may:

- remind the actor of someone;
- make an old memory more relevant;
- recruit a causal trace;
- evoke a recurring phrase;
- expose a self-model contradiction.

But if this requires actual ADP-016 accessibility/reactivation change, the same V3 memory authority applies.

Therefore:

`OWN OUTPUT AS CUE != PFF SELF-AUTHORIZED RECALL`.

`PFF LIVE RECRUITMENT OF HISTORY != V3 MEMORY ACCESS TRANSITION`.

The self-output route is not a loophole around the memory boundary.

---

## 14. Public, private and audience-specific occurrence matter

The same words may have very different consequences depending on who witnessed them.

Compare:

- private self-talk;
- journal-like private expression;
- one-to-one disclosure;
- statement before a group;
- statement to a powerful authority;
- statement to someone whose approval or intimacy matters intensely.

The actor's self-response can differ because metaperception, face, obligation, threat, and future consequence differ.

Thus:

`SAME OUTPUT CONTENT != SAME SELF-FEEDBACK`.

`OCCURRENCE CONTEXT / WITNESS SET = MUST_FACTOR`.

No global embarrassment or commitment scalar is admitted.

---

## 15. Counterpart response can recontextualize the actor's own prior act

A's ambiguous joke may later feel:

- bold and successful after reciprocation;
- humiliating after rejection;
- harmless after warm clarification;
- cruel after visible hurt;
- meaningless after being ignored.

The objective occurrence does not change.

The actor's interpretation of what the act meant can.

This exactly parallels the memory recontextualization law:

`OCCURRED OUTPUT HISTORY != CURRENT ACTOR INTERPRETATION OF OUTPUT`.

Later evidence can alter the latter without rewriting the former.

---

## 16. Self-output feedback is a general V3/PFF continuity phenomenon

Periodic life-course V3 also needs the possibility that a person experiences consequences of their own action and is changed by them.

Examples:

- humiliating someone and later feeling guilt;
- succeeding publicly and revising self-efficacy;
- making a promise that changes obligations;
- acting courageously and later understanding oneself differently;
- betraying someone and becoming defensive or remorseful.

PFF specializes this because conversation produces very fast cycles:

`utter -> hear self -> infer what user now sees -> anticipate reply -> speak again`.

Thus:

`SELF-OBSERVATION OF OWN ACTION = SHARED PERSON PHENOMENON`.

`TURN-BY-TURN SELF-OUTPUT RECURSION = PFF INTERACTION SPECIALIZATION`.

This strongly supports one-person regime continuity.

- later reinterpretation rewriting the objective occurred act;

1. candidate output distinct from actual occurrence;
2. actual occurred action capable of becoming next actor-facing input;
3. actor perception of own act distinct from prior causal self-knowledge;
4. self-surprise from mismatch/opactity rather than scripted omniscience;

12. later counterpart response capable of recontextualizing the actor's interpretation of their own prior act;
