## Terminal answers

### A. What is the strongest macro→person mechanism actually demonstrated?

The strongest defensible chain is:

```text
Depression-era economic contraction
→ situated father's work/income exposure
→ realized unemployment/underemployment/income loss
→ household resource shortfall
→ household maintenance and parent-role reorganization
→ child receives actual economic/domestic obligation
→ child repeatedly performs paid/domestic work
→ child's resource/authority/counterpart/time ecology changes
→ near-term measured developmental/social change
```

The cleanest terminal example is boys' increased social independence through paid employment: future workers were not already more independent in junior high; employed boys later showed the largest gains, though nonrandom selection remains possible.

### B. What is the strongest linked-life mechanism?

`parent work/resource transition -> household labor/resource reallocation -> child obligation/activity -> changed child time/resource/authority/counterpart ecology`.

Mother's employment and father's incapacity/absence are source-person changes that reach the child through real household relations and tasks, not psychological contagion.

### C. Does age operate as mechanism or qualifier?

**Qualifier / route selector.** Age matters by changing dependency, role eligibility, school/work position, and opportunity structure at the moment of historical exposure. Same shock + different age can therefore activate a different process.

### D. Does D1 suffice?

**Yes**, provided linked-life derivations are relation-qualified by mandatory source actor, target actor, exact relation/household binding, source realized state/transition, target ecology delta, time, realization and provenance.

### E. Does D2 suffice?

**Yes.** Elder requires interval conditions, recurrent activities, transitions, ordered trajectories, and a derived—not primitive—turning-point predicate.

### F. Does D3 capture reciprocal/selection pathways?

**Yes, with an identification guard.** D3 can encode action→external gate/response→new social fact. Elder explicitly shows reciprocal/selection possibilities (job selection; drinking↔unemployment), but unresolved direction must remain unresolved rather than be promoted to two causal arrows.

### G. Is D4 needed?

**No.** A separate linked-life capability would duplicate D1's counterpart/relationship binding and `LINKED_LIFE_TRANSMISSION`, plus D2's transition rebinding.

### H. Does Elder support turning point as derived rather than primitive?

**Yes.** Dramatic events and transitions—military service, marriage, work entry, educational change—become turning points only if later trajectory demonstrates sustained redirection. Elder repeatedly shows route/timing change without necessary endpoint change.
