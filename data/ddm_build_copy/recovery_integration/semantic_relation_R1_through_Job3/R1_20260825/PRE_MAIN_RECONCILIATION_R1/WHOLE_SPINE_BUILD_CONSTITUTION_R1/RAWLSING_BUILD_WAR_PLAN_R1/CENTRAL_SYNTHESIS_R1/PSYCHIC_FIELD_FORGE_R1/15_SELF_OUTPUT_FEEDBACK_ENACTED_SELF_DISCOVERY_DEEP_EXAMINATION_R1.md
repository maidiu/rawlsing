# PSYCHIC FIELD FORGE R1 — SELF-OUTPUT FEEDBACK / ENACTED SELF-DISCOVERY — DEEP EXAMINATION R1

**Status:** `PRE_ARCHITECTURE_CONCEPT_EXAMINATION__BANKED__MUST_FACTOR__NOT_ARCHITECTURE_ADOPTION__NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY`

## 0. Purpose

This file deeply examines PFF frontier pocket 17, `self-output feedback / enacted self-discovery`.

The originating intuition is simple but far-reaching:

> A person can say or do something, perceive their own output, become surprised by it, reinterpret themselves because of it, become more or less committed to it, become embarrassed that another person heard it, or discover a motive only after the expression has occurred.

A one-way system:

`person -> psyche -> expression`

is therefore psychologically incomplete.

The central questions are:

1. How does the actor's own observable output become input to the next live PFF configuration?
2. What is the difference between prior cause of the output and the actor's later interpretation of what they did?
3. Can an expression make previously inaccessible material more available without silently rewriting V3?
4. How can the actor be surprised by their own output if the machine already had a causal explanation for it?
5. How do embarrassment, commitment, rationalization, doubling-down, regret, relief, and self-recognition arise after expression?
6. What is permitted to persist locally, and what requires V3 causal admission?

Current conclusions:

`ACTOR_OUTPUT_CAN_BE_NEXT_PFF_INPUT = MUST_FACTOR`

`SELF_OBSERVATION != PRIOR_CAUSAL_SELF_KNOWLEDGE`

`ACTOR_CAN_LEARN_FROM_OWN_OUTPUT = MUST_FACTOR`

`ACTOR_SURPRISE_AT_OWN_OUTPUT = MUST_FACTOR`

`RENDERED_OUTPUT != RETROACTIVE_PROOF_OF_PRIOR_HIDDEN_STATE`

`OUTPUT_CAN_CHANGE_METAPERCEPTION_BEFORE COUNTERPART_REPLY = MUST_FACTOR`

`SELF_INTERPRETATION_AFTER_OUTPUT MAY BE CORRECT, PARTIAL, OR WRONG`

`SESSION_LIVE_SELF_UPDATE != AUTOMATIC_V3_DURABLE_CHANGE`

`OWN_WORDS_CAN_BECOME CAUSAL EVENTS ONLY THROUGH LAWFUL OCCURRENCE / RE-ENTRY`

`NEW_SELF_FEEDBACK_SUBSYSTEM = NOT ADMITTED`

## 1. Governing Rawlsing boundary: rendered expression is not automatically causal truth

Existing V3/V2 law already separates:

`RENDERED_EXPRESSION != MEMORY_EVIDENCE`

and more generally:

`RENDERED_EXPRESSION != CAUSAL_OCCURRENCE`.

A rendered candidate becomes a generated-life event only where exact interaction/admission authority establishes that it occurred causally.

Therefore PFF must distinguish at least:

1. proposed/selected expression semantics;
2. rendered expression candidate;
3. actually emitted/occurred expression where the interaction mode makes it causal;
4. actor perception/registration of their own occurred output;
5. local PFF consequences;
6. any durable V3 consequence after lawful admission.

Hard law:

`MODEL_GENERATED_TEXT != AUTOMATIC_ACTOR_LIFE_EVENT`.

This examination concerns self-feedback **after the relevant output is lawfully treated as the actor's observable act**, not model drafts that never occurred.

## 2. First major distinction: causal generation versus retrospective self-interpretation

Suppose A makes a cutting joke at B.

Machine-side causal account may include:

`attraction + shame + perceived exposure + status defense -> cutting joke`.

After saying it, A may consciously think:

- `Why did I say that?`;
- `That was meaner than I intended.`;
- `I guess I'm more annoyed than I thought.`;
- `I was just joking.`;
- `Maybe I wanted to hurt him.`;
- `Oh God, now he knows I care.`

These are **new actor-level interpretations after the act**, not necessarily the causes that generated it.

Therefore:

`PRIOR_MACHINE_CAUSAL_ACCOUNT != POST_OUTPUT_ACTOR_SELF_EXPLANATION`.

`POST_OUTPUT_SELF_EXPLANATION != OBJECTIVE_CAUSAL_TRUTH`.

The actor may discover something real, misattribute the output, or defend against its implications.

## 3. Self-output changes the social world immediately

Once A says something to B, the fact that B has now heard it can matter to A before B responds.

Examples:

- A discloses a secret and immediately feels exposed;
- A flirts more openly than intended and realizes B can now infer interest;
- A insults B and anticipates retaliation;
- A says `I love you` and suddenly experiences the stakes differently;
- A admits uncertainty and feels loss of status;
- A makes a promise and experiences increased commitment pressure;
- A reveals grief and feels both relief and vulnerability.

Thus:

`OUTPUT_OCCURRED -> A_MODEL_OF_WHAT_B_NOW_KNOWS/SEES -> NEW_PFF_STATE`

can happen before any counterpart reply.

This tightly couples self-output feedback to metaperception.

## 4. Self-output can alter metaperception without proving the counterpart's actual interpretation

A may think:

`B now knows I am attracted`.

That belief may be wrong; perhaps B interpreted the remark differently.

But the metaperception is still causally potent.

Therefore:

`A_OUTPUT -> A_BELIEF_ABOUT_B_VIEW_OF_A` is possible,

while

`A_OUTPUT -> B_ACTUAL_VIEW_OF_A` remains separately governed and uncertain from A's perspective.

This preserves:

`SYSTEM KNOWS OUTPUT`
`!= A KNOWS B'S INTERPRETATION OF OUTPUT`.

## 5. Self-surprise requires a real opacity gap

For an actor to surprise themselves, Rawlsing must preserve a distinction between:

- the machine's causal account;
- the actor's pre-output conscious field;
- the actor's intended expression;
- the actual expression produced within lawful bounds;
- the actor's post-output perception/interpretation.

Example:

A consciously intends to tell a neutral story.

The final lawful expression contains an unusually pointed detail or unexpected emotional emphasis arising from multi-motive expression/leakage.

A notices what they said and thinks:

`Why did I bring that up?`

If the renderer had been given the hidden cause and instructed to reveal it, this would be fake self-surprise: the system would be staging a surprise already explicitly authored by a surface model.

The PFF hidden-cause firewall therefore supports genuine self-surprise by allowing hidden causes to constrain expression without making them renderer propositions.

## 6. Several classes of post-output self-response must remain possible

These are phenomenon classes, not states/classes to freeze.

### 6.1 Surprise

Output differs from actor's conscious expectation of self.

### 6.2 Embarrassment / exposure

The actor realizes something private, clumsy, needy, hostile, revealing, or status-threatening has become public.

### 6.3 Regret

The actor evaluates the action negatively relative to values, relationship goals, or anticipated consequences.

### 6.4 Relief / release

Expression reduces concealment/control demand or fulfills a blocked need.

### 6.5 Commitment / self-binding

Having publicly said or promised something changes future pressures even if the underlying belief/motive has not changed instantly.

### 6.6 Doubling-down

Threat to self-image or status causes the actor to defend/reaffirm the output rather than retract it.

### 6.7 Rationalization

The actor generates a self-protective explanation after the action.

### 6.8 Recognition / insight

The actor uses their own output as evidence and arrives at a more accurate understanding of their motive/state.

### 6.9 Misrecognition

The actor learns the wrong lesson from their output.

### 6.10 Curiosity / reflective opening

Unexpected expression causes the actor to investigate themselves further without immediate conclusion.

### 6.11 Identity pressure

The action conflicts with or supports the standing self-model.

### 6.12 Relationship-state anticipation

The actor now expects the relationship to change because of what they said/did.

These may coexist.

## 7. Own-output evidence is special but not infallible

A person's own behavior is legitimate evidence about themselves, but behavior is multiply caused.

Example:

A notices they keep returning to B in conversation.

Possible actor interpretations:

- `I must like B`;
- `I'm worried about B`;
- `B irritates me`;
- `I'm obsessed because I feel threatened`;
- `I just happen to have lots of relevant stories`.

Machine-side cause may itself be plural/uncertain.

Therefore:

`OWN_BEHAVIOR = SELF_EVIDENCE`

but

`OWN_BEHAVIOR != UNIQUE_SELF_DIAGNOSIS`.

Rawlsing should permit self-inference without making it omniscient.

## 8. Expression can move material across the conscious boundary

Some content becomes consciously recognizable only after enactment.

Examples:

- A hears themselves defend B and realizes they care more than expected;
- A becomes jealous in speech and then recognizes jealousy;
- A says a parent's phrase verbatim and notices the identification;
- A apologizes and recognizes guilt that was previously vague;
- A tells a story and finally sees its connection to the present situation.

This is a plausible pathway:

`HIDDEN / PARTIAL LIVE CAUSE`
-> `BOUNDED EXPRESSION`
-> `SELF-OBSERVED OUTPUT`
-> `NEW CONSCIOUS HYPOTHESIS / ACCESS`

But the final arrow cannot bypass V3 actor-access authority where it establishes durable proposition-level memory/belief/knowledge.

Current pre-architecture distinction:

- PFF may support a live self-reading / conscious hypothesis arising from own output;
- V3 must still own durable epistemic admission/persistence where required.

## 9. Self-output can change accessibility without creating history from nothing

Telling a story may make associated memories more accessible.

Naming a feeling may make related experiences easier to recognize.

Publicly committing may make reasons for/against the commitment more salient.

However, the material must already have lawful roots in the person/history.

`SELF_OUTPUT -> ACCESSIBILITY_CHANGE` may be psychologically real,

but exact V3 memory/accessibility authority determines whether/how the transition occurs.

PFF cannot create a memory merely because the renderer produced evocative language.

## 10. Self-output can generate new genuine causal material

Once an expression actually occurs in generated life, it is itself a new event.

Examples:

- `I lied to her` can become a real fact about A's history;
- `I confessed` can change relationship trajectory;
- `I promised` can create obligation/expectation;
- `I humiliated him publicly` can produce later guilt/shame/relationship consequences;
- `I told her I loved her` can alter future interaction.

This is not retroactive validation of the hidden cause that produced the expression.

It is new forward causal material.

Thus:

`OUTPUT_AS_OCCURRED_EVENT -> FUTURE_CAUSAL_HISTORY` is possible through V3 admission,

while

`OUTPUT -> PROOF_OF_PRIOR_HIDDEN_MOTIVE` is forbidden.

## 11. Renderer creativity must not become unconscious causation by accident

This is a crucial risk.

Suppose the bounded semantic plan permits several natural phrasings and the renderer chooses an unusual metaphor.

The system must not automatically treat that renderer-specific flourish as evidence of a deep hidden memory or motive.

Otherwise stochastic/linguistic realization becomes a backdoor author of psyche.

Hard law:

`UNGOVERNED_RENDERER_DETAIL != PRIOR_PSYCHIC_EVIDENCE`.

Self-output feedback may respond to the occurred wording as a new event where lawful, but it must distinguish:

- semantics intentionally/causally constrained upstream;
- renderer realization variation;
- genuinely actor-attributable occurred output;
- what inferences may lawfully be drawn afterward.

This seam will require exact architecture later.

## 12. Self-output feedback can amplify or dampen existing states

Examples:

### 12.1 Amplification

A says something hostile, hears the hostility, and becomes more convinced they are angry.

### 12.2 Dampening

A finally articulates grief and experiences relief.

### 12.3 Cross-conversion

A accidentally discloses need, experiences exposure, and converts attachment into defensive anger.

### 12.4 Polarization

A makes a public status claim and becomes less willing to back down because retreat now threatens face.

### 12.5 Reorganization

A hears their own contradiction and becomes reflective rather than choosing either prior side.

Thus self-output is part of the nonlinear live system, not only a terminal product.

## 13. Public versus private self-output matters

Not all self-expression has the same social consequence.

Compare:

- private internal verbalization;
- journal-like private expression;
- one-to-one disclosure;
- public statement before a group;
- statement to an authority;
- anonymous expression;
- expression to a person whose approval matters intensely.

The actor's model of who witnessed the act changes metaperception, face, commitment, and later behavior.

Therefore:

`OUTPUT_CONTENT_SAME != PSYCHIC_CONSEQUENCE_SAME`.

Situation/audience binding matters.

## 14. Social commitment effects are distinct from belief change

Publicly stating X may create pressure to remain consistent with X.

That pressure may arise through:

- self-image;
- anticipated judgment;
- role obligation;
- promise/commitment;
- status;
- cognitive consistency;
- relationship expectation.

But Rawlsing must not convert:

`A said X`
into
`A now believes X more strongly`
by default.

`PUBLIC_COMMITMENT_PRESSURE != AUTOMATIC_EPISTEMIC_UPDATE`.

## 15. Doubling-down and repair are both possible after self-observation

A notices their output crossed a line.

Possible routes:

### Repair

- acknowledge;
- clarify;
- apologize;
- soften;
- retract;
- disclose the underlying concern if actor-accessible.

### Defense / doubling-down

- justify;
- blame B;
- escalate;
- minimize;
- pretend intent;
- reinterpret the output as deserved.

The same initial self-awareness can therefore lead to opposite trajectories depending on self-model, status threat, values, relationship significance, and operating condition.

## 16. Self-output can reveal a conflict without resolving it

Example:

A says:

`I don't care what she thinks—why hasn't she answered?`

The actor may notice the contradiction.

That does not require the system to resolve whether A “really cares.”

Instead the output can increase awareness of coactive motives.

`SELF_OBSERVED_CONTRADICTION != FORCED_SELF_RESOLUTION`.

This preserves psychologically realistic ambivalence.

## 17. Self-output can become a new retrieval/recruitment cue

The retrieval examination already established this seam.

Examples:

- a phrase reminds A of a parent;
- telling a story recruits another memory;
- a joke resembles a previous conflict pattern;
- saying someone's name makes attraction/grief more salient;
- an accidental metaphor evokes a related self-model theme.

Again, renderer invention cannot retroactively create historical facts, but the actually occurred output may lawfully become current input.

## 18. Self-output can alter the actor's model of the counterpart even before response

A may reason:

- `Now B will think I'm weak.`
- `Now B knows I remembered.`
- `Now she knows I lied.`
- `He'll probably laugh.`
- `I may have scared him.`

These are prospective social readings triggered by A's own action.

This creates a bridge between self-output feedback and anticipation.

## 19. Counterpart response can retroactively change the meaning of A's own output to A

Example:

A makes an ambiguous flirtatious joke.

If B reciprocates, A may experience the joke as bold/playful.

If B looks offended, A may reinterpret it as a mistake.

If B ignores it, A may feel embarrassment and insist it meant nothing.

Thus the actor's self-interpretation of their own prior action can be **trajectory-dependent**.

`MEANING_TO_ACTOR_OF_OWN_OUTPUT[t]` may change after later evidence.

This does not change the objective bytes/event that occurred.

## 20. Self-discovery can be gradual rather than one insight event

Repeated outputs can form a pattern the actor eventually recognizes.

Example:

A repeatedly:

- mentions B;
- seeks B's reaction;
- defends B;
- becomes jealous around B;
- denies interest.

No single act proves attraction.

But across time, A may form a conscious hypothesis:

`Maybe I actually care about B.`

This is distinct from the machine knowing a hidden cause earlier.

The actor's discovery has its own causal history and uncertainty.

## 21. Self-output can stabilize a self-story even when the story is inaccurate

Repeatedly explaining behavior in one way may strengthen a conscious narrative.

Example:

`I only criticize him because I care about standards.`

Repeated public/private explanation may make this self-model more accessible/coherent even if resentment/status motives also contribute.

PFF must preserve:

`SELF_NARRATIVE_REHEARSAL CAN STABILIZE CONSCIOUS INTERPRETATION`

without treating it as objective causal truth.

Durable self-model change remains V3-owned.

## 22. Self-output can be performative in the ordinary social sense

Some speech acts change the social situation by being uttered:

- promise;
- apology;
- confession;
- refusal;
- invitation;
- acceptance;
- declaration of commitment;
- accusation;
- boundary statement.

The exact event-kind authority belongs elsewhere, but PFF must be able to respond to the actor's awareness that they have now done something socially consequential.

This is another reason self-output cannot be ignored until the next interlocutor turn.

## 23. Generated-generated and generated-external cases

### 23.1 Generated-generated

Both actors may receive their own lawful self-output feedback and separately perceive the other's output.

The system must preserve opacity:

A's self-discovery does not automatically become B's knowledge.

### 23.2 Generated-external/human

The generated actor can self-observe its own output, but the human's hidden response remains unknown until observed/inferred.

This preserves uncertainty and makes anticipation/metaperception important.

## 24. Temporal scope: immediate self-feedback versus durable self-development

Need to distinguish:

- same-turn self-realization;
- next-turn live PFF residue;
- session-scale changed self-consciousness;
- episode-scale concern;
- durable V3 self-model / memory / attractor consequence.

Hard law:

`ACTOR_NOTICED_OWN_OUTPUT != DURABLE_V3_SELF_CHANGE`.

Repeated/admitted life consequences may eventually produce V3 change, but PFF cannot promote them automatically.

## 25. Self-feedback and defense can form recursive loops

Example:

1. hidden attraction contributes to teasing;
2. A hears teasing and fears B noticed attraction;
3. shame increases;
4. A becomes colder;
5. A notices own coldness and rationalizes `B is annoying`;
6. B withdraws;
7. A interprets withdrawal as rejection;
8. attraction/rejection conflict intensifies.

This is a fully actor-local + dyadic recursive loop with no need for a third psyche.

## 26. Self-output should not be read as a diagnostic oracle

Hard negative:

`A said X, therefore hidden state Y`.

Behavior is multiply determined and the renderer has realization freedom within lawful constraints.

Self-output can update hypotheses, create new evidence, and alter live state, but it cannot be used as a shortcut to infer exact hidden cause without independent authority/evidence.

## 27. No-gadget semantic remainder test

Before creating a dedicated self-feedback subsystem, attempt to preserve the phenomenon through:

1. exact occurred output artifact/event identity;
2. existing PFF perception/receiver transformation applied reflexively to self-output where appropriate;
3. current conscious-field/self-model processes;
4. metaperception of what counterpart now knows/sees;
5. current nonlinear conflict/defense dynamics;
6. PFF temporal residue;
7. V3 admission for durable effects;
8. actor-access authority for any new proposition-level self-belief/knowledge.

A special subsystem is justified only if a semantic remainder survives.

## 28. Hard negatives

Reject unless independently justified:

- actor's own output ignored until counterpart responds;
- renderer flourish treated as proof of hidden motive;
- every output automatically added as durable V3 self-belief;
- self-surprise staged by explicitly telling renderer the hidden secret;
- actor instantly understands hidden cause after leakage;
- public statement automatically strengthens belief;
- apology automatically resolves guilt/relationship rupture;
- contradiction automatically resolved;
- own behavior treated as unique causal diagnosis;
- model draft that was never emitted treated as actor action;
- session-local regret written directly into durable personality;
- machine causal account copied into actor post-hoc self-explanation;
- actor's expectation of counterpart reaction treated as counterpart fact.

Any successful PFF architecture must eventually preserve:
