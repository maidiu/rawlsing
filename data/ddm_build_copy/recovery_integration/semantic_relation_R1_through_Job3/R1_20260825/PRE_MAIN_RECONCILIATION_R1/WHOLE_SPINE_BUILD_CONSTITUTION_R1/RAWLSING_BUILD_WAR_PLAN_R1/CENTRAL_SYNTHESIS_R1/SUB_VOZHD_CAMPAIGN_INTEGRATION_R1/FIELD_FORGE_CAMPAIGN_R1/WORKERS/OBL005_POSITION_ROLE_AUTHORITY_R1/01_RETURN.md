## 4. Evidence sufficiency law

Evidence for a position-side role must establish the exact semantic/use proposition being authorized.

Minimum positive evidence includes:

- producer-owned exact measurement/source semantics;
- exact empirical-unit/actor grain;
- exact claim/docket analytic use;
- evidence that the proposed role is semantically compatible with that exact use;
- any exact precedent relied upon, with its scope and negative ceilings;
- exact transform status;
- exact owner/court jurisdiction;
- exact permission/provenance/replay closure.

The following are insufficient role evidence by themselves or in combination:

- coordinates;
- embedding clusters;
- geometric-region labels;
- topology or graph neighborhood;
- questionnaire/source-block membership;
- coadministration or routing/module placement;
- block/question/category/respondent/component size;
- variable names, normalized labels, source codes or historical ontology labels;
- recurrence/frequency of use;
- association strength or target-result fit;
- downstream need for a position variable;
- a desired theoretical interpretation.

`CORRELATION_OR_FIT != ROLE_EVIDENCE`.

## 5. Claim-boundedness and reissuance law

A role ruling is not an intrinsic eternal property of a measurement object.

It is valid only for its exact authorized claim/docket/use and exact scope/context/time.

Therefore:

`PRA_FOR_CLAIM_A != PRA_FOR_CLAIM_B`.

A prior role may be cited as precedent but does not automatically carry forward.

A new docket may reissue a prior semantic family only when a competent current owner independently verifies:

1. exact unchanged measurement/object identity;
2. exact new claim/docket/use;
3. semantic compatibility of the new use with the proven source semantics;
4. no ungoverned state recoding, transform, equivalence or transport;
5. all relevant prior negative ceilings preserved or narrowed;
6. exact new owner/evidence/provenance/replay binding.

If any condition fails or is unresolved, preserve the exact blocker rather than carrying the role forward.
