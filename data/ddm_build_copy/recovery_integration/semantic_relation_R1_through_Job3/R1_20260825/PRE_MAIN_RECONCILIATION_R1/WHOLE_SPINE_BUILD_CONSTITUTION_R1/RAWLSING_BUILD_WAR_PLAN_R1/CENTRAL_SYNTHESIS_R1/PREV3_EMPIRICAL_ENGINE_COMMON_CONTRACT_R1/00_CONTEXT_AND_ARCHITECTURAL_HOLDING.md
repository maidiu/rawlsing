`PRE-V3 WORLD != V3 DEVELOPMENTAL RUNTIME`.

## 2. What Dva, Trii, and Chetiri each contributed

### Dva

Dva established that PRE-V3 is not merely a survey-export layer. Its durable capital includes:

- actor-independent structural positions and relations;
- temporal structural relation;
- structural pressure;
- applicability/affordance;
- contradiction;
- mediation routes and conditions;

- attractor prerequisites;

- a strict PRE-V3 -> V3 boundary.

Historical Dva assumed much of V550-V552J had already produced valid upstream empirical/learned products. That inherited assumption is precisely why Dva cannot by itself close the current machine.

### Trii

Trii recovered the deep empirical/query obligations that the older artifact-oriented V550+ machine only partially and inefficiently represented. Its D15 semantic DAG requires preservation or exact information-equivalence for:

- source/respondent/event/coordinate identity;
- routing/risk sets;
- weights/design/estimands;
- exact query identity;
- same-respondent conditional/joint queries;
- autonomous discovery and coverage accounting;
- typed failures/absence;
- structural empirical admission;
- positioned-subject empirical possibility;
- exact replay and authority identity.

Trii's core theorem was informational/semantic, not a proof that one physical normalized table was the only lawful implementation.

### Chetiri

Chetiri reopened the stronger D15 physical presumption that the logical empirical substrate had to exist as one permanently materialized canonical normalized respondent/event payload carrier.

Chetiri did **not** reopen the need for an exact governed PRE-V3 empirical semantics layer.

Its durable holding is:

> source payload may be reconstructible from immutable governed source evidence, while non-source-derivable semantic/topology/routing/replay material and independently justified learned products still require governed identity and selective persistence.

Therefore persistence is product- and lifecycle-specific. A learned object may be semantically first-class, deterministically rebuildable, worth retaining operationally, and still subordinate to source evidence. Those are independent predicates.

This is why an eventual production answer may lawfully be a hybrid persistence policy rather than a crude "persist everything" / "persist nothing" dichotomy.

Field Forge must consume a logical empirical interface. It must not determine the physical storage architecture by accident.

1. observed evidence;
2. governed semantic interpretation;
3. graded inference;
4. empirical association/predictive relation;
5. theory-supported structural mechanism;
6. V3 developmental interpretation/realization.

Governance exists to stop layer 3-6 claims from masquerading as layer 1. It must not prohibit inference.

`UNCERTAINTY_IS_REPRESENTED__NOT_ELIMINATED_BEFORE_EXECUTION`

`UNKNOWN_FACTS_DO_NOT_ERASE_KNOWN_EVIDENCE`

A respondent answering an employment, ownership, supervision, insecurity, trust, authority, moral, religious, existential, or interpersonal item may contribute evidence to structural or attitudinal inference without being falsely represented as perfect ontological proof.

DDM attitude clouds/predictors are therefore core PRE-V3 empirical capital:

- answer-to-answer tendency;

- subgroup/country/wave heterogeneity;
- conditional relations;
- minority configurations;
- redundant/complementary indicators;
- cross-survey convergence where separately authorized;

These are not actor biography and not causal proof. They are governed empirical possibility/association knowledge that V3 can later metabolize probabilistically.
