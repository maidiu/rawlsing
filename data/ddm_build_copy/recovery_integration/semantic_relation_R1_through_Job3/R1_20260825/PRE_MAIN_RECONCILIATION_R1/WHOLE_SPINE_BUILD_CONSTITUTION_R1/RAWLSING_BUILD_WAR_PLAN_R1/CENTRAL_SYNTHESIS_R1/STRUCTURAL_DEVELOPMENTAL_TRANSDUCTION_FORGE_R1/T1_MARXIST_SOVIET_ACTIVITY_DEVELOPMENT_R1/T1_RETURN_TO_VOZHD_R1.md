## Top retained mechanisms

1. `ORGANIZED_ACTIVITY_ECOLOGY`: structural relations matter by organizing actor-specific tasks, tools, counterpart roles, obligations, discretion and recurrent activity possibilities.
2. `ACTION_TO_OPERATION`: repeated/restructured goal-directed action can become condition-regulated skilled operation; conscious appraisal is not required on every iteration.
3. `SOCIAL_SIGN_TOOL_MEDIATION`: socially distributed sign/tool use reorganizes action and can become self-regulation through transformed repeated use.
4. `GUIDED_TO_INDEPENDENT_PERFORMANCE`: collaboration/instruction can create reachable supported action that becomes an independently available capacity.
5. `ACTS_CAPACITIES_REPRODUCTION`: capacities enable acts; capacity-forming acts expand future capacity and therefore future activity possibility.
6. `USE_TIME_ACTIVITY_ALLOCATION`: finite, socially constrained time allocates recurrent opportunity among capacity-forming, capacity-using, care, labour, recovery and other activities.
7. `OBJECTIVE_MEANING_TO_PERSONAL_SENSE`: shared social meaning becomes actor-specific only through its relation to concrete motive/activity.
8. `ACTIVITY_MOTIVE_HIERARCHY_REORGANIZATION`: personality change is a reorganization of activity/motive relations across biography rather than environment-labelled trait accumulation.
9. `SUBJECT_MEDIATED_REALIZATION`: similar external conditions can yield different outcomes because effects operate through current capacities, motives, histories and action.
10. `PRACTICE_RECIPROCITY`: activity can reproduce or alter the relations that organize it; prior person state also changes later selection into activity.
