## 0. Court standard

Every major claim is classified as one of:

```text
DIRECTLY OBSERVED PERFORMANCE DIFFERENCE
PLAUSIBLE ACTIVITY/EDUCATION MEDIATION
AUTHOR CAUSAL INTERPRETATION
NOT IDENTIFIABLE FROM DESIGN
```

A claim may receive more than one label where the observation is secure but its causal explanation is not.

## 1. Primary causal structure actually observed

The data identify this broad structure:

```text
historical transition
   |
   +-> schooling/literacy/course access
   +-> collective-farm role / administration
   +-> recurrent planning/accounting/evaluation
   +-> widened counterpart/institution contact
   +-> information exposure
   +-> altered gender/public-role access
   +-> occupational/activity change
   +-> cohort/selection differences

all bundled with

age + sex + locality + self/administrative selection + practical expertise

then

interactive experiment
(prompt, translation, examples, debate, group setting, assistance)

-> observed task response
```

The book often narrates the top node as the cause. The design does not identify which outgoing path, combination, selection process, or experimental interaction produced the response difference.

## 4. Assistance / learning court

The study contains genuine within-session interventions: examples, repeated instructions, conditional reformulations, hypothetical-person prompts, task-specific teaching, making conditions more explicit, and supplying imaginary scenarios.

What this can establish:

`ASSISTANCE_t -> CHANGED RESPONSE_t_or_t+1` for specific observed interactions.

What it cannot establish without further design:

```text
ASSISTANCE -> GENERAL CAPACITY ACQUISITION
ASSISTANCE -> DURABLE DEVELOPMENT
ASSISTANCE -> TRANSFER ACROSS DOMAINS
ASSISTANCE -> POPULATION DEVELOPMENTAL STAGE
```

This is relevant to guided performance only at the event/task grain.

## 5. Temporal identification court

No same-person repeated cognitive battery across the historical transition is provided. No exposure start dates are systematically aligned with outcome change. No untreated matched comparison follows the same period.

Therefore:

- `DURATION OF EXPOSURE -> EFFECT SIZE`: not identified;
- `TRANSITION EVENT -> WITHIN-PERSON COGNITIVE CHANGE`: not identified;
- `SEQUENCE schooling vs collective role vs literacy -> outcome`: not identified;
- `PERSISTENT ACTIVITY HISTORY -> stable psychological change`: plausible theory, not directly identified.

## 6. Cole-versus-Luria interpretation court

Two readings fit much of the same evidence:

### Luria

```text
new social practices / schooling
-> new cognitive structures / new modes of thought
```

### Cole's narrower alternative

```text
new experience / discourse practices
-> different application of available modes of thought
   to the experimental setting
```

The study design cannot decisively distinguish these. Rawlsing must therefore keep the **observed performance** and the **causal-developmental interpretation** in separate authority layers.

## 7. Colonial/evolutionary terminology court

The book explicitly rejects racial inferiority accounts yet repeatedly uses hierarchical historical language such as "backward," "culturally advanced," "primitive," higher/lower stages, and a centuries-leap narrative. Those terms are not neutral empirical variables.

Rawlsing shall preserve them only as provenance-bearing author language when needed to interpret the source, never as architecture categories or actor priors.

The same applies to sweeping claims that Islamic influence held back independent thought. The admissible object is a concrete role, authority, mobility, sanction, schooling-access, or communication fact—not a religion label.

## 8. Causal-identification terminal

The source is highly valuable for **mechanism hypothesis generation, task-context discipline, and the need for actor-relative activity ecology**. It is not lawful authority for a one-step causal transduction from population/historical regime to psychological state.
