## 3. The semantic remainder that survives — actor uptake

A useful phenomenon-level placeholder still survives after decomposition:

> **ACTOR UPTAKE** = the event-side description of how an observable/available occurrence becomes the psychologically operative received event for this particular generated person in this particular situation.

This is deliberately not a new stored state or architecture object.

It names the distinction between:

```text
WHAT WAS PRESENTED / OBSERVABLE
```

and:

```text
WHAT THIS PERSON ACTUALLY RECEIVED AS PSYCHOLOGICALLY OPERATIVE
```

Possible consequences of uptake include:

- some aspect barely registers;
- another aspect becomes strongly important;
- ambiguity is resolved or preserved through an expectation;
- care is received as control;
- praise is received as pity or sarcasm;
- an implication becomes threatening while the literal proposition is understood;
- an event recruits attachment/status/autonomy conflict;
- a clear proposition is understood but its psychologically threatening center is defended against;
- one reading is focal while another remains peripheral;
- the actor is affected without consciously knowing what affected them.

Core noncollapse:

```text
OBJECTIVE_OCCURRENCE != ACTOR_UPTAKE
SYSTEM_CANDIDATE_READING != ACTOR_UPTAKE
USER_TRUE_INTENTION != ACTOR_UPTAKE
ACTOR_UPTAKE != ACTOR_CONSCIOUS_INTERPRETATION
ACTOR_UPTAKE != DURABLE_ACTOR_BELIEF
ACTOR_UPTAKE != EXPRESSION_PLAN
ACTOR_UPTAKE != RENDERED_REPLY
```

And:

```text
MAXIMAL_MACHINE_COMPREHENSION != MAXIMAL_ACTOR_RECEPTION
PARTIAL_RECEPTION != RANDOM_DEGRADATION
PLAUSIBLE_MISRECOGNITION_REQUIRES_PERSON_SPECIFIC_CAUSAL_BASIS
```

## 4. Actor uptake and situated recruitment are paired views of one coupling

The strongest conceptual refinement produced by this pass is that `actor uptake` and `situated recruitment` should not be treated as two independent machines in a strict serial chain.

They are better understood as two perspectives on the same event-person coupling.

### Event-side question — actor uptake

> What did this event/message become **for this person**?

### Person-side question — situated recruitment

> What of this developed person became operative **because of this event/message here and now**?

These can be mutually conditioning.

Example:

```text
USER: "You don't have to prove anything to me."

observable/candidate readings:
  reassurance
  intimacy
  possible condescension
  competence/status implication

DURABLE PERSON:
  competence striving
  history of pity as humiliation
  attachment desire
  protective self-sufficiency mediator

COUPLING:
  competence/status significance becomes amplified
  attachment invitation remains present but threatening
  protective mediator recruits

ACTOR UPTAKE:
  "she thinks I'm trying too hard / she can see I need approval"
  with background possibility of care

SITUATED RECRUITMENT:
  shame + attraction + self-protection + desire to restore status
```

It would be artificial to demand that one of those is fully computed before the other begins.

Therefore:

```text
ACTOR_UPTAKE = EVENT_SIDE_VIEW_OF_PERSON_EVENT_COUPLING
SITUATED_RECRUITMENT = PERSON_SIDE_VIEW_OF_PERSON_EVENT_COUPLING
ACTOR_UPTAKE != SITUATED_RECRUITMENT
ACTOR_UPTAKE_AND_RECRUITMENT_MAY_BE_CO_DETERMINING
```

No graph, loop, state object, or solver is adopted by this language.

## 7. The important V3 enrichment seam

This pass exposes a particularly valuable continuity problem in current/recovered V3 semantics.

ADP-010 carefully distinguishes `EXPOSURE` from later actor psychology. Exposure means that an actor is objectively presented with / impinged upon by something through a realized encounter. It explicitly does not itself establish noticing, understanding, memory, belief, response, or psychological change.

CAN-008 then owns a governed actor-response transaction over exact admitted causal input streams, including path-dependent multi-stream transforms such as buffering, amplification, deflection, rerouting, transformation, and blocking. It deliberately refuses to invent under-governed psychology.

## 8. Why a single preferred actor reading is also insufficient

Actor uptake is not necessarily a scalar or a single rewritten sentence.

An input can remain multiply received:

```text
"Maybe you should stay tonight."

for one actor:
  probable intimacy invitation
  + possible pity
  + fear of appearing eager
  + hope of belonging
```

The actor may consciously foreground only one of these while several remain causally active.

Thus reject:

```text
SYSTEM MULTI-READING
-> ONE ACTOR REWRITE STRING
-> RESPONSE
```

as a universal psychology.

The person can preserve uncertainty, mixed significance, contradiction, and differential intensity/confidence.

`ACTOR_UPTAKE != ONE_HEARD_AS_SENTENCE`.

This is another reason not to restore historical `HOOK_NEIGHBORS` substitution.

## 9. Perfect comprehension and arbitrary error are both synthetic-personhood failures

The design target is a bounded middle.

### Failure A — service-agent omniscience

The system recognizes ten nuances and the persona responds accurately to all ten despite strong person-specific reasons why only three should matter.

This demonstrates that the renderer or upstream LLM is bypassing the persona.

### Failure B — fake-human noise

The persona misunderstands a clear proposition merely because a randomness rule decided a human should be imperfect.

This demonstrates manufactured quirk rather than personhood.

### Success candidate — lawful angled reception

The system richly recognizes the input; the generated person receives a narrower/differently weighted/mildly transformed subset because of their exact history, relationship model, expectations, mediators, self-model, current conflict, defense, anticipation, and live residue.

Their reply can therefore be incomplete, slanted, surprisingly focused, or mildly wrong **for a reason that belongs to them**.

This is the central recovered historical V2 aim in stronger current form.

## 10. Renderer anti-rescue law

A new hard negative should be carried into #20 and architecture-input work:

> Once the system has formed a rich machine-side candidate reading, the final renderer must not use that richer analyst understanding to repair the persona into a maximally comprehensive conversational agent.

Example:

```text
SYSTEM sees A+B+C+D.
PERSONA uptake/recruitment makes B+C operative; A is missed and D is defended away.
PFF/V2 lawful expression forms around B+C.
```

Forbidden rescue:

```text
renderer notices A+D in original user text
and helpfully addresses them anyway.
```

The final renderer still needs the real user discourse for linguistic coherence and answer grounding, but it must remain constrained by the actor's lawful expression formation.

Therefore:

```text
RENDERER_RE_GROUNDING_IN_USER_MESSAGE
!=
RENDERER_PERMISSION_TO_RESTORE_SYSTEM_OMNISCIENCE
```

This is a critical complement to the hidden-cause firewall.

## 11. Cross-regime examples

### 11.1 User interaction

User says:

> "I wasn't criticizing you. I just thought you'd want to know."

System candidate reading may preserve reassurance, defensiveness, correction, possible face-saving, and ambiguity.

Actor A, with humiliation sensitivity and a relationship history of feeling patronized, receives primarily:

> `they think I can't take criticism / they are backing away from what they said`.

Actor B, secure with the same user, receives primarily:

> `they are clarifying and trying not to hurt me`.

Same observable input. Different lawful uptake. Neither reading becomes user hidden truth.

### 11.2 Periodic V3 life event

Two generated actors receive the same objectively constituted event: a supervisor publicly corrects them.

Actor A's developed organization may receive it as diagnostic humiliation and status loss.

Actor B may receive it as ordinary competence feedback.

The difference can arise from durable expectations, mediator organization, prior relationship history, self-model, causal traces, current condition, and anticipated consequences.

The event should therefore not directly imply a generic shame/confidence delta.

### 11.3 Same person, changed history

The same user's terse message may initially be received as rejection, but after a long history in which terseness repeatedly coexists with reliability and affection, the same person may later receive it as ordinary low-energy communication.

That demonstrates development/relationship history changing uptake without changing the literal message.

## 12. Pre-#20 synthetic-personhood obligations added/refined

The late synthetic-personhood court must now test at least:

1. **Machine-rich / actor-bounded comprehension** — the system can identify nuance the actor does not fully receive.
2. **No arbitrary degradation** — partiality/misrecognition must be explainable from the generated person/current coupling.
3. **Same message, different people** — developed persons exhibit materially different uptake for grounded reasons.
4. **Same person, different relationship history** — uptake changes as lawful history accumulates.
5. **Multiple simultaneous significance** — actor uptake can remain ambiguous/contradictory rather than becoming one paraphrase.
6. **Causal without conscious** — something can shape response without becoming focal/recognized.
7. **Psychological avoidance with semantic competence** — actor can understand literal content while responding around threatening significance.
8. **Correction/repair** — user correction can change current actor reading without retroactively deleting the earlier misreading.
9. **Renderer anti-rescue** — final rendering cannot smuggle omitted system-understood content back into the reply.
10. **No forced service completion** — unanswered portions are lawful when actor-specific uptake/effort/defense makes them nonoperative.
11. **V3 homology** — the same person-specific reception principle must explain psychologically mediated generated-life events, not only chat.
12. **Perspective binding** — actor uptake never becomes user intention or objective social truth merely because it caused a response.
13. **Continuity through self-output** — the actor's own occurred response becomes a fresh event whose meaning to them is again person-specific.
14. **No whole-person prompt shortcut** — success must depend on the developed person and governed transitions, not an LLM being told to roleplay selective reception.
