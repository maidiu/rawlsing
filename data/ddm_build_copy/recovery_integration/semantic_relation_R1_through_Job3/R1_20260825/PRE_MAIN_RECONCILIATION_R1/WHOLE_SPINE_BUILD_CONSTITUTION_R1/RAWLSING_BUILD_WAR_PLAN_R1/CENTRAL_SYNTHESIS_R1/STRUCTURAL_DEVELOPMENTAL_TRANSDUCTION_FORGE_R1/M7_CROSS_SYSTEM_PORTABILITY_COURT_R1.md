## 0. Governing question

For every retained mechanism:

```text
IS THIS A GENERAL RAWLSING MECHANISM
OR
A HISTORICALLY / INSTITUTIONALLY QUALIFIED INSTANCE?
```

No portability finding is licensed by the mere fact that the same broad label exists in two countries.

## 1. Mandatory M7 portability states

This court uses exactly these evidentiary dispositions where applicable:

```text
MECHANISM REPLICATES
MECHANISM CHANGES MAGNITUDE
MECHANISM REVERSES
MECHANISM DISAPPEARS
MEASUREMENT DOES NOT TRANSLATE
EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE
```

A mechanism may receive more than one status only at distinct grains. Example: the occupational-self-direction mechanism can `REPLICATE` for values while `DISAPPEAR` for Polish distress.

## 3. Portability court: occupational self-direction

### What generalizes

Across capitalist US and socialist Poland, actual work conditions facilitating/restricting occupational self-direction explain much of the relationship of class/stratification with:

- parental valuation of self-direction;
- intellectual flexibility;
- self-directedness of orientation.

This is not merely a common correlation. The US longitudinal models and Polish quasi-longitudinal models support direction from work condition to psychological functioning while retaining reciprocal person-to-job effects.

### What does not generalize unchanged

- the relative psychological weight of substantive complexity versus close supervision;
- the distress effect;
- the exact class positions producing the work conditions;
- the timing of education into occupational careers;
- the institutional meaning of authority and protection.

### Court verdict

`GENERAL RAWLSING MECHANISM FAMILY, WITH SYSTEM-CONDITIONED UPSTREAM PRODUCTION AND OUTCOME-SPECIFIC PORTABILITY`.

## 4. Portability court: class and stratification

The book does **not** prove “class” as a portable causal token. It proves that class can be conceptualized relationally across systems only by reconstructing each system's actual ownership/control/labor-power relations.

Thus:

```text
PORTABLE:
  relational class-construction grammar

NOT PORTABLE AS AN UNPACKED TOKEN:
  employer / manager / supervisor / nonmanual / manual / production worker
```

Likewise, stratification is portable as a multi-dimensional hierarchical construct, while the relative weights of education, occupational status and income vary.

**Court verdict:** `PORTABLE CONCEPTUAL GRAMMAR; INSTANCE MUST CARRY SYSTEM/INSTITUTIONAL PROVENANCE`.

## 5. Portability court: distress

Distress is the book's hostile case against simple generalization.

The same work-self-direction construct does not have the same distress consequence in the two settings. Moreover, class and stratification have substantially different or reversed relationships with distress.

The source's own response is methodologically decisive:

1. test measurement artifact first;
2. when measurement comparability survives, search for omitted concrete conditions of life;
3. identify job protections and other institutional benefits where data permit;
4. leave the remainder unexplained rather than baptizing `culture` or `country` as a mechanism.

**Court verdict:** `PORTABILITY REQUIRES MECHANISM, NOT OUTCOME SIGN INVARIANCE`.

This is strong support for Rawlsing's category-shortcut firewall.

## 6. Portability court: family

The broad family-linked transmission chain replicates, but the operative counterpart differs.

```text
SAME FAMILY LABEL
!= SAME COUNTERPART ROLE ECOLOGY
```

The source's tentative account points to gendered division of family labor, rural/urban historical continuity, and church/state role organization in Poland. Those are institution/role hypotheses; they do not justify a direct `POLAND -> MATERNAL TRANSMISSION` edge.

**Court verdict:** `LINKED-LIFE MECHANISM PORTABLE; COUNTERPART/ROLE PATH SYSTEM-QUALIFIED`.

## 7. Special court — does the same observed construct arise from the same structural mechanism?

Answer: **sometimes, not always.**

Occupational self-direction is a comparable observed construct in both countries. Yet:

- its class-production pathways differ because class structures differ;
- supervision is more tightly organized by Polish class/stratification;
- education feeds careers differently;
- downstream distress differs;
- the relative weight of complexity versus supervision differs.

Thus a Rawlsing mechanism cannot be certified portable merely by measurement invariance of its output.

Required decomposition:

```text
MEASURE_EQUIVALENCE
PORTABILITY_OF_UPSTREAM_PRODUCTION
PORTABILITY_OF_ACTOR_RELATIVE_MEDIATOR
PORTABILITY_OF_DOWNSTREAM_EFFECT
```

These are distinct questions.

## 8. Special court — institutional configuration provenance

**Question:** Does D1 need actor-relative derivation to carry system-level institutional configuration as upstream provenance?

**Verdict:** `YES`.

Without this, Rawlsing could derive the same actor-relative token (`MANAGER`, `SUPERVISED`, `PROTECTED`, `EDUCATED`, `MANUAL_WORKER`) while throwing away the institutional rules that make its causal content differ across systems.

Minimum semantic requirement for D1:

```text
ACTOR_RELATIVE_DERIVATION
must preserve, where causally applicable:
  INSTITUTIONAL_CONFIGURATION_REF(S)
  + RULE / ORGANIZATIONAL SOURCE REF(S)
  + VALID HISTORICAL INTERVAL
  + ACTOR POSITION / COUNTERPART BINDING
  + PORTABILITY / BOUNDARY STATUS
```

This can be satisfied by extending the existing `STRUCTURAL_SOURCE_FACTS`, `BOUNDARY_CONDITIONS`, and derivation provenance fields; it does not necessarily require a new storage class.

## 9. Special court — portability beyond the observed systems

The authors intend a general interpretation for industrialized societies and review supporting evidence from Japan and several other industrial settings. The third-country Japanese evidence is particularly important because it weakens any claim that the US/Poland commonality is merely “Western culture.”

But the book does not validate a universal human mechanism independent of industrial organization. Evidence from less/partially industrialized settings is sparse, mixed, or methodologically uncertain.

**Rawlsing rule:** a mechanism learned from this source should be stored as a general mechanism family with explicit source/evidence scope, not an unbounded universal.

## 10. Portability law proposed to Vozhd

Retain this law:

```text
A MECHANISM IS PORTABLE ONLY IF
THE SAME TYPED ACTOR-RELATIVE TRANSDUCER
CAN BE IDENTIFIED ACROSS SETTINGS
WITHOUT USING COUNTRY / CLASS LABEL AS THE CAUSAL EDGE.

A PORTABLE MECHANISM MAY STILL HAVE
SYSTEM-SPECIFIC:
  upstream institutional production,
  actor-position realization,
  component magnitude,
  counterpart/role routing,
  downstream outcome reach,
  historical validity interval.

WHERE THESE ARE NOT EXPLAINED,
STORE THE DIFFERENCE AS RESIDUAL / INCONCLUSIVE,
NOT AS A COUNTRY EFFECT.
```
