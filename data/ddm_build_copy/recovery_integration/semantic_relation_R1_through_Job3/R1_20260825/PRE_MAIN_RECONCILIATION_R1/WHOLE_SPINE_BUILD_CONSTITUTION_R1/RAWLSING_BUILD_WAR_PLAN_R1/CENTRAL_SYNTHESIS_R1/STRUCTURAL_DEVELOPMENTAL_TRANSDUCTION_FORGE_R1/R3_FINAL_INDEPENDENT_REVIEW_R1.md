`REALIZATION_WITNESS VERDICT: PASS_WITH_NARROWING__GLOBAL_VALIDITY_POLARITY_LAW_REQUIRED`

R3 requires realization witnesses, temporal/lifecycle requirements and negative ceilings, but scenario 28 exposes a remaining ambiguity: an expired or superseded positive witness could be mistakenly carried forward while the source rule remains valid, and missing/stale evidence could be misread as negative truth.

The exact required addition is:

> **UNIVERSAL REALIZATION-WITNESS VALIDITY / POLARITY LAW.** Every `ACTIVE` or `REALIZED` consequence assertion MUST cite at least one type-compatible competent realization witness whose validity overlaps the asserted consequence interval and that is not expired, superseded, terminated, invalidated, or otherwise outside the rule's lifecycle requirements. For recurrent-activity or continuing-state claims, the witness schema MUST define sufficient coverage/currentness/freshness for the interval asserted. Rule validity or qualification alone MUST NOT extend a realization witness. Missing, expired, superseded, invalidated, or insufficiently current positive evidence yields `UNKNOWN` / `NOT_PROVEN_REALIZED`, never a negative consequence. A denial, absence, non-occurrence, termination, unavailability, or other negative actor fact may be emitted only from explicit competent negative evidence, an explicit denial/termination witness, or a rule-authorized closed-world observation whose negative semantics are provenance-bearing.
