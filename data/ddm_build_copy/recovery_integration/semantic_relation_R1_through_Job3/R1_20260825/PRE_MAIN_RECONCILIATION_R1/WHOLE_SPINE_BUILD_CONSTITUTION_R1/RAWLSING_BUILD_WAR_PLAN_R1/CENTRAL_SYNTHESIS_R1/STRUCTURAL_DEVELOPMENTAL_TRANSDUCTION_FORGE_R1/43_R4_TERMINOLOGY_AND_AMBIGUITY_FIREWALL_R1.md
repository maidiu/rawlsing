## 0. Purpose

The PRE-V3 -> D1' -> V3 seam is especially vulnerable to ordinary words that silently switch meanings between:

- structural possibility;
- actor-relative objective consequence;
- current realized world/social fact;
- subjective appraisal/experience;
- downstream durable development.

This firewall fixes canonical meanings for the R4 seam and requires more explicit terms where ambiguity would cross jurisdiction.

---

## 1. `STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY`

Meaning:
actor-independent or not-yet-actor-bound social/world possibility admitted by PRE-V3/source authority.

Never means:
- this actor currently has the consequence;
- the consequence was realized;
- the actor noticed or valued it.

Binding:

```text
STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY != OBJECTIVE POSITIONED CONSEQUENCE
```

---

## 2. `OBJECTIVE POSITIONED SOCIAL CONSEQUENCE`

Meaning:
a D1'-lawful objective proposition about a particular actor under exact world/context/position/time bindings and an exact governed rule.

Never means:
- subjective premise;
- appraisal;
- motive;
- distress;
- learning;
- trait;
- durable development.

---

## 3. `ASSERTION KIND`

Canonical proof kinds:

```text
K1 CONSTITUTIVE_POSITIONAL
K2 QUALIFIED_POSSIBILITY
K3 REALIZED_OCCURRENCE
K4 ACTIVE_DURATIVE
```

Assertion kind answers **how the objective proposition is proved**.
It is orthogonal to P1-P5 mechanism class.

---

## 4. `QUALIFIED` / `APPLICABLE`

Canonical meaning:
the exact actor/position/context satisfies the rule-defined conditions for an objective possibility, route, gate, channel or consequence applicability.

Never means:
- occurred;
- received;
- used;
- enforced;
- perceived.

Prefer `K2 QUALIFIED_POSSIBILITY` where ambiguity exists.

---

## 5. `CONSTITUTIVE`

Canonical meaning:
a competent operative rule/state plus exact position/binding makes an objective relation/right/duty/status true over the asserted interval.

Examples:
- entitlement;
- legal eligibility;
- authority scope;
- active obligation;
- valid restriction.

Never means the right/duty was exercised or enforced.

Prefer `K1 CONSTITUTIVE_POSITIONAL`.

---

## 6. `REALIZED`

Use only for a proposition that asserts an actual contingent occurrence/treatment/transfer/exercise and has competent K3 proof.

Examples:
- payment arrived;
- gate decision rendered;
- contact occurred;
- sanction imposed;
- task performed.

Do not use `realized` as a synonym for:
- eligible;
- available;
- current continuing state;
- subjectively experienced.

Prefer `K3 REALIZED_OCCURRENCE`.

---

## 7. `ACTIVE`

Use only for an objectively continuing fact over an interval with lawful K4 currentness proof.

Examples:
- active authority relation;
- continuing dependency;
- ongoing deprivation/access state;
- current recurrent activity;
- continuing channel/relation where competent owner proves persistence.

`ACTIVE` does not mean psychologically salient.

Prefer `K4 ACTIVE_DURATIVE`.

---

## 8. `CURRENT`

Meaning:
proved at the query/assertion time under the assertion-kind-specific lifecycle law.

Never infer:

```text
RULE CURRENT -> FACT CURRENT
HISTORICALLY TRUE -> CURRENT
OLD WITNESS -> CURRENT
```

`CURRENT` is orthogonal to assertion kind.

---

## 9. `HISTORICAL`

Meaning:
a proposition was lawfully true over a prior proved interval but is not currently supported as current.

Historical does not mean false.
Historical does not reactivate itself as current.

---

## 10. `UNKNOWN` / `NOT PROVEN`

Canonical form:
`UNKNOWN_NOT_PROVEN`.

Meaning:
positive and negative proof both fail at the exact proposition/grain.

It is an adjudication result, **not an actor fact**.

Never write:

```text
UNKNOWN -> NEGATIVE
NO RECORD -> NEGATIVE
```

---

## 11. `NEGATIVE`

Meaning:
an explicitly proved negative proposition under competent negative evidence or exact authorized closed-world semantics.

Examples may include:
- application explicitly denied;
- relation explicitly terminated;
- complete closed-world source proves no occurrence in exact interval.

Never infer negative truth from absent/stale positive evidence.

## 50. Binding ambiguity law

When a term could denote both an objective D1' proposition and a subjective/person-side V3 state, the unqualified term is forbidden in normative seam records.

Use the typed objective term or route the subjective meaning downstream.

Examples:

```text
security -> RESOURCE/ACCESS SECURITY vs FELT SECURITY
support -> SUPPORT TRANSFER vs PERCEIVED SUPPORT
control -> DECISION SCOPE vs PERCEIVED CONTROL
strain -> OBJECTIVE LOAD vs FELT STRAIN
opportunity -> QUALIFIED ROUTE vs SUBJECTIVE POSSIBILITY/PREMISE
```
