# RAWLSING WAVE 1A — FIRST POSITIVE EMPIRICAL CLAIM SELECTOR R1 — FINDINGS

## 1. Selection objective

The first positive post-GT0 empirical claim should prove one narrow thing:

`REAL_CURRENT_SOURCE + EXACT_RESPONDENT/RISK_SET + EXACT_SOURCE_NATIVE_TARGET + EXACT_QUERY_AUTHORITY -> PRODUCER_OWNED_SUPPORTED_EMPIRICAL_FACT`

It should not pay for future semantics early.

For W1A, “cheapest” therefore means the least additional authority required after current exact evidence is reused. Costs include:

- same-respondent jointability;
- routing/exposure/risk-set proof;
- target-domain/response-topology proof;
- weights/estimands;
- source composition/pooling/backfill;
- measurement portability/transport;
- discovery;
- source retirement/equivalence;
- harmonization;
- structural interpretation.

The selector does not optimize for thematic importance or continuity with GT0.
