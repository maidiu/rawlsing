## 2. Top retained T4 mechanisms

### A. Organized work → recurrent activity → person

The strongest Kohn/Schooler result is not an occupation/class-to-trait shortcut. It is a mechanism in which substantive complexity, supervision and routinization determine what a worker repeatedly has occasion or obligation to do. Recurrent thought, independent judgment, initiative and nonroutine problem solving are the proposed developmental transducer; close supervision and routinization constrain that activity ecology.

Normalized candidate: `STRUCTURE_TO_RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_ECOLOGY`.

Evidence: strong longitudinal observational reciprocal modeling, reinforced by later longitudinal/fixed-effects work.

Rawlsing disposition: `MISSING + REUSE` — reuse PRE-V3 structural possibility and V3 developmental owners; prove/generalize the positive derivation of actor-specific recurrent activity rather than invent a new psychology owner.

### B. Structural control → exercised control history → mastery/competence

Formal autonomy is not identical to experienced or exercised autonomy. Mortimer/Lorence, Wu et al., and Staff/Mortimer support a typed chain from work discretion/self-direction into actor-exercised control and mastery/competence/control orientation, with important negative evidence against collapsing skill use and autonomy into one scalar.

Normalized candidate: `STRUCTURAL_CONTROL_TO_EXERCISED_CONTROL_HISTORY`.

Disposition: `GENERALIZE` seam realization semantics; `REUSE` existing V3 developmental owners.

### C. Discrete event → persistent material/role condition → person

Pearlin et al. provide direct partial-mediation evidence that involuntary job disruption can alter material resources and produce enduring economic strain, which in turn can erode self-esteem/mastery and elevate depressive symptoms.

Canonical law: `JOB_DISRUPTION_EVENT != ECONOMIC_STRAIN_CONDITION`.

Normalized candidate: `EVENT_TO_PERSISTENT_SOCIAL_CONDITION_PROPAGATION`.

Disposition: `MISSING + REUSE` — reuse lawful event/OEE and V3 response/development owners; the connective propagation into a persistent actor-relative social condition remains the positive semantic demand.

### D. Chronic strain without a decisive event

Pearlin’s stress-process evidence explicitly permits enduring strains that are not reductions of a salient event. Persistent material insufficiency, role demands, authority constraints or uncertainty require interval-sensitive actor-relative condition semantics.

Normalized candidate: `PERSISTENT_STRAIN_CONDITION`.

Disposition: `GENERALIZE_IF_PROVABLE_ELSE_MISSING` pending Lane R’s exact test of existing state/context/history carriers.

### E. Possible network → realized support process

Network topology, tie existence, support availability, support mobilization, support receipt and support efficacy are distinct. Support is counterpart-qualified and problem-specific; in Pearlin’s job-disruption model it more clearly protected self-concept than prevented economic strain or directly suppressed depression.

Normalized candidate: `NETWORK_TO_REALIZED_SUPPORT_PROCESS`.

Disposition: `GENERALIZE + REUSE` existing topology and V3 relationship/history; reject a free-standing support scalar detached from actual relationships.

### F. Stressor → cross-domain social-condition proliferation

A demanding role can reconfigure time, obligation, activity and resource ecology such that secondary strains arise in other domains. Co-occurrence alone does not prove proliferation; a typed propagation route is required.

Normalized candidate: `STRESSOR_TO_STRESSOR_CROSS_DOMAIN_PROLIFERATION`.

Disposition: `MISSING_OR_GENERALIZE` the cross-domain connective function; no new distress owner.

### G. Person/action → gated later position/exposure

Kohn/Schooler, Mortimer/Lorence and related longitudinal work supports reciprocal selection: prior flexibility, orientation, competence or values can influence job choice, performance, attainment or molding, which changes later structural exposure. This path must remain gated by organizations, labor markets, credentials, counterpart decisions and other structural conditions.

Normalized candidate: `PERSON_ACTION_TO_LATER_POSITION_EXPOSURE_FEEDBACK`.

Canonical law: `PREFERENCE/CAPACITY != POSITION_CHANGE`.

Disposition: `MISSING_OR_GENERALIZE` explicit feedback seam semantics; do not create a direct psychology→structure edge.

## 3. Reciprocal findings

T4 returns the following empirically supported family-level grammar:

```text
STRUCTURE
→ ACTOR-RELATIVE EXPERIENCE / RECURRENT ACTIVITY
→ DEVELOPMENTAL CHANGE
→ ACTOR ACTION / SELECTION
→ GATED LATER POSITION / EXPOSURE
→ NEW EXPERIENCE
```

Strongest reciprocal evidence:

- Kohn & Schooler 1978: substantive complexity of work ↔ intellectual flexibility across a 10-year panel; job→person persists despite high stability and the person→job path is described as lagged/gradual.
- Kohn & Schooler 1982: self-directed/oppressive work conditions predict later specific psychological outcomes while prior orientation/flexibility predicts later job responsibility/self-direction.
- Mortimer & Lorence: autonomy/work experience and competence/values show longitudinal two-way relations in a narrower educated male cohort.
- Andrisani & Nestel: control orientation predicts work success and work success predicts later internal-control expectancy in a national longitudinal male sample.

Important calibration:

- Wu et al. supports autonomy→internal locus of control, but skill utilization and proposed indirect effects were weak/null.
- Staff & Mortimer’s long fixed-effects panel strongly reduces stable-person selection explanations for proximal work-quality/mental-health covariation, but one-wave lagged work effects were null and most reverse-lagged effects were null. T4 therefore does not infer one universal lag or automatic durable effect.
- Pearlin et al. provide a strong forward partial-mediation chain but do not prove an equally strong symmetric reverse pathway; untested reciprocal alternatives remain `DEFER`, not invented evidence.

## 4. Temporal-form return

T4 adjudicates the commissioned forms separately:

- `EVENT`: discrete occurrence; job disruption is the central case.
- `CONDITION`: persistent supervision, routinization, economic strain, ongoing demands; cannot be collapsed into an event token.
- `RELATION`: supervisor–worker, household/care and support ties; requires counterpart identity/participation and persistence where applicable.
- `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY/PRACTICE`: autonomous judgment, complex problem solving, repetitive work, coping; central developmental transducer in the occupational-socialization evidence.
- `TRANSITION`: job loss, promotion/mobility or change into a different work regime; requires before/after position plus a realized gate.
- `TRAJECTORY`: ordered career and stress-exposure histories.
- `TURNING_POINT`: `DEFER` as a distinct T4 causal operator; no inspected T4 source independently earns it apart from trajectory-relative interpretation.
- `CUMULATIVE_PROCESS`: chronic strain, repeated practice and stress proliferation; must remain typed rather than become an undifferentiated exposure sum.
