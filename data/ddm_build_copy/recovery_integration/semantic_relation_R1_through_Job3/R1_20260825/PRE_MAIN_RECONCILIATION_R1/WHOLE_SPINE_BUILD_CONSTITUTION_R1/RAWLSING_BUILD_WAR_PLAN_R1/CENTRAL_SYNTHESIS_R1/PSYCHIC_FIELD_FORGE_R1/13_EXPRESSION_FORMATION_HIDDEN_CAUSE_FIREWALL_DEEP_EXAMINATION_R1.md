# PSYCHIC FIELD FORGE R1 — EXPRESSION FORMATION / HIDDEN-CAUSE FIREWALL DEEP EXAMINATION R1

**Status:** `PRE_ARCHITECTURE_CONCEPT_EXAMINATION__BANKED__MUST_FACTOR__NOT_ARCHITECTURE_ADOPTION__NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY`

## 0. Purpose

This file deeply examines PFF frontier pockets 14 and 15 together:

- `expression formation before rendering`;
- `hidden-cause epistemic firewall`.

The question is now forced by prior PFF examinations.

PFF has already established, provisionally and pre-architecturally, that:

- durable V3 organization can be selectively recruited into a live interaction;
- several motives/readings/defenses can remain coactive and contradictory;
- actor awareness can differ materially from the machine's causal account;
- metaperception can change expression even when false;
- defense can operate before awareness, within interpretation, and after awareness;
- hidden material can influence expression through leakage without becoming explicit actor knowledge;
- temporal residue and path dependence mean the current expression is not a stateless translation of the latest turn.

The central question is:

> What may cross from this psychologically rich live PFF configuration toward V2 so that the final expression remains causally faithful to the person while the renderer is prevented from seeing, asserting, or improvising from hidden causal truths that the actor does not know?

Current conclusions:

`PFF_TO_V2_HANDOFF != HIDDEN_PSYCHIC_DUMP`

`PFF_TO_V2_HANDOFF != FINAL_WORDING`

`PFF_TO_V2_HANDOFF != ONE_EMOTION_LABEL`

`PFF_TO_V2_HANDOFF != ONE_RESPONSE_MOVE`

`CAUSE_LEVEL_SEMANTICS != SURFACE_EFFECT_LEVEL_SEMANTICS`

`HIDDEN_CAUSE_CAN_SHAPE_SURFACE_EFFECT_WITHOUT_BECOMING_RENDERER_VISIBLE_PROPOSITION`

`ACTOR_ACCESSIBLE_CONTENT + NONPROPOSITIONAL_BOUNDED_EXPRESSION_EFFECTS = MUST_FACTOR`

`NEGATIVE_CONSTRAINTS / WITHHOLDING = AS IMPORTANT AS POSITIVE PRESSURES`

`FINAL_RENDERER_MUST_RECEIVE_ACTUAL_SURFACE_INTERACTION AGAIN = MUST_FACTOR`

`FINAL_RENDERER_MUST_NOT_DISCOVER_PSYCHIC_TRUTH = MUST_FACTOR`

`MONOLITHIC_EXPRESSION_TRAJECTORY_OBJECT = NOT YET ADMITTED`

## 1. Existing Rawlsing boundaries that constrain this seam

The current/historical downstream authorities already reserve distinct V2 jurisdictions.

### 1.1 ADP-017 — actor epistemic ceiling

ADP-017 separates:

`OBJECTIVE_GENERATED_FACT`
`!= V3_CAUSAL_MEMORY`
`!= CURRENT_MEMORY_ACCESSIBILITY`
`!= GENERATED_ACTOR_EPISTEMIC_STANCE`
`!= CURRENT_CLAIM_ACCESSIBILITY`
`!= FIRST_PERSON_CLAIM_PERMISSION`.

PFF therefore cannot turn analyst-side causal knowledge into actor-expressible proposition content.

### 1.2 CAN-016 — salience

CAN-016 preserves:

`ACCESSIBLE != SALIENT`

and also:

`SALIENT != PHENOMENOLOGY`
`SALIENT != REGISTER`
`SALIENT != RESPONSE_MOVE`
`SALIENT != CAUSAL_ACTION`.

PFF must therefore not absorb downstream salience ownership simply because psychic material is strongly activated.

### 1.3 CAN-017 — expression register

CAN-017 preserves:

`FIRST_PERSON_PERMISSION != EXPRESSION_REGISTER`

`SALIENT != REGISTER`

`REGISTER != RESPONSE_MOVE`

`REGISTER != PERSISTENT_PERSONALITY`

`RENDERER_OUTPUT != REGISTER_AUTHORITY`.

PFF may provide upstream reasons/constraints relevant to expression, but should not silently become the generic register owner.

### 1.4 CAN-018 — response move

CAN-018 owns the generic response-operation registry/selection membrane over already-lawful V2 inputs and explicitly excludes actor knowledge, salience, register, uncertainty disclosure, relationship expression, boundary/face-risk, and V3 causal state.

PFF should not choose a downstream response move merely because a particular psychic configuration exists.

### 1.5 CAN-019 — relationship expression

CAN-019 preserves actor-specific perspective and forbids one actor's relationship view from becoming reciprocal/objective relationship truth.

PFF hidden attraction, fear, longing, resentment, etc. must therefore not become invented relationship facts.

### 1.6 ADP-022 — uncertainty/disclosure

ADP-022 consumes ADP-017 permission and may express/narrow already-lawful uncertainty, belief, knowledge, or nonknowledge. It cannot form/reclassify actor epistemic state.

PFF cannot use emotional pressure to upgrade uncertainty into belief or belief into knowledge.

### 1.7 ADP-023 — boundary / face-risk / effort / attention

ADP-023 owns turn/situation-scoped presentation constraints and explicitly distinguishes:

`FIRST_PERSON_PERMISSION != WILLINGNESS_TO_DISCLOSE != RESPONSE_OPERATION`.

Its face-risk relation is a presentation constraint, not shame/fear/personality/relationship truth.

PFF may cause shame or exposure sensitivity upstream, but downstream boundary/face-risk semantics must remain properly owned.

## 2. First major distinction: cause-level versus effect-level semantics

The most important firewall distinction is:

### 2.1 Cause-level semantics

Examples:

- `A is attracted to B but does not know it`;
- `A's anger is partly caused by shame`;
- `A's coolness is defensive against dependency need`;
- `A is reactivating a relationship-specific rejection expectation`;
- `A's urge to tell this story is driven by unconscious resonance with B`;
- `A's apparent confidence is partly overcompensation for exposure fear`.

These may be valid machine-side causal interpretations where lawfully grounded.

They are not automatically actor-known propositions.

### 2.2 Surface-effect-level semantics

Possible consequences of those causes may include:

- sustained attention toward B;
- reluctance to disengage;
- increased self-consciousness;
- increased playful/teasing pressure;
- explicit-disclosure inhibition;
- preference for deniable/indirect approach;
- increased concern with B's reaction;
- topic-return pressure;
- defensive coolness;
- overexplaining;
- heightened sensitivity to perceived rejection;
- contradiction between explicit stance and interactional pattern.

These describe **what the hidden cause is doing to expression formation**, not the hidden proposition itself.

The architecture later must establish how such effects are represented and which downstream V2 owner consumes each one.

Hard law:

`CAUSE_LEVEL_PROPOSITION -/-> RENDERER`

`CAUSE_LEVEL_PROPOSITION -> PFF_MEDIATED_SURFACE_EFFECT = POSSIBLE`.

## 3. Why directly giving hidden causes to the LLM is unacceptable

A superficially capable implementation might prompt the renderer with:

> She is secretly attracted to him but is not consciously aware of it. Make her sound subtly flirtatious without saying so.

This fails the PFF vision for several reasons.

1. The renderer now possesses causal truth the actor lacks.
2. Nothing prevents the model from paraphrasing or overexposing the hidden content.
3. The final wording becomes dependent on model discretion rather than governed semantic mediation.
4. The renderer can collapse uncertainty, defense, conflict, and leakage into a coherent omniscient narrative.
5. Replay may become model-specific rather than semantics-specific.
6. The LLM is effectively deciding how unconscious causation becomes behavior.

Therefore:

`HIDDEN_CAUSE_PROMPT_INJECTION = REJECT`.

The renderer must receive only the actor-lawful and expression-lawful consequences that survived the mediation process.

## 4. Expression formation is not a translation step

PFF should not be conceptualized as producing:

`internal truth -> translated sentence meaning`.

The prior examinations show that expression formation may involve:

- several active motives;
- several competing social readings;
- actor uncertainty;
- conscious and unconscious motives;
- strategic anticipation;
- defense;
- inhibition;
- disclosure willingness;
- relationship-specific accessibility;
- performance/self-presentation;
- leakage;
- current boundary/face exposure;
- target-specific attention;
- unresolved residue from prior turns.

The result may therefore be a **bounded family of expression pressures and prohibitions** rather than one proposition.

Example:

Actual live configuration:

`attraction + shame + uncertainty about reciprocation + desire for deniability + playful dyadic mode + fear of being seen`

Potential expression consequences:

- engage rather than exit;
- attend closely to B;
- permit humor/teasing;
- inhibit explicit attraction claim;
- preserve ambiguity;
- monitor B's response;
- allow some warmth/interest to remain visible;
- avoid causal self-explanation.

No single sentence-level semantic summary captures this without losing structure.

## 5. Three broad classes of material approaching the PFF→V2 seam

These are conceptual classes, not adopted packet fields.

### 5.1 Actor-accessible proposition/content

Material the actor may lawfully know/believe/entertain/claim under exact actor-access authority.

Examples:

- `I am upset` where actor access permits it;
- `I think she may be flirting` where the actor consciously entertains that hypothesis and claim permission permits it;
- `I do not know why this bothers me` where nonknowledge is itself authorized.

This can potentially become explicit semantic content downstream.

### 5.2 Actor-conscious but nonpropositional orientation

Examples:

- wanting to avoid a topic;
- wanting to appear composed;
- feeling hesitant;
- wanting reassurance but not wanting to ask directly;
- wanting to repair;
- feeling pulled to stay engaged.

Some of this may eventually map onto V2 disclosure, boundary, response-move, salience, or register owners.

PFF should not pre-own the downstream categories but must preserve the phenomenon.

### 5.3 Hidden-cause-derived bounded effects

Material not available as actor proposition, but which legitimately changes expression conditions.

Examples:

- hidden attraction causing unusually sustained attention;
- unrecognized shame increasing defensiveness;
- reactivated wound making one topic unusually difficult to approach;
- unconscious comparison with a past relationship increasing vigilance;
- suppressed anger creating abruptness or overcontrol.

These effects must cross only in forms that cannot be mistaken for actor-known causal explanation.

## 6. A fourth class: explicit negative semantic constraints

PFF-to-V2 mediation cannot be positive-only.

Examples:

- do not assert attraction;
- do not claim to know B's intention;
- do not reveal analyst-only causal memory;
- do not name a hidden wound as self-knowledge;
- do not convert suspicion into fact;
- do not express relationship reciprocity not actor-known;
- do not make explicit a motive that remains inaccessible;
- do not imply memory access where only causal resonance exists.

These negative constraints are central to preserving actor opacity.

`WHAT_MAY_NOT_BE_SAID = FIRST_CLASS_SEMANTIC INFORMATION`.

The downstream pipeline may further narrow, but may not widen beyond these ceilings.

## 7. Leakage requires bounded permission, not free improvisation

The defense examination established:

`LEAKAGE_FORM != HIDDEN_CONTENT_FORM`.

Therefore leakage should not be implemented by telling the renderer to infer a subtle clue from a secret cause.

Instead PFF must eventually be able to mediate:

`HIDDEN_CAUSE`
`-> specific allowed surface-level pressure / asymmetry / indirectness`
`+ explicit forbidden direct content`
`-> downstream V2 planning`
`-> renderer realization`.

Example:

Hidden causal account:

`A strongly wants B's approval but does not recognize the dependency need.`

Possible lawful effects:

- increased attention to B's evaluation;
- stronger impulse to justify self;
- difficulty disengaging from criticism;
- defensive minimization of caring;
- disproportionate elaboration.

Forbidden:

- `I desperately need your approval` unless actor access independently permits such self-recognition.

## 8. Expression may be multi-motive without becoming incoherent

The nonlinear-conflict examination established that one action may serve several motives.

Example teasing response may simultaneously:

- approach B;
- preserve deniability;
- test interest;
- defend status;
- discharge tension;
- maintain current playful chemistry.

The PFF→V2 seam must not force architecture to choose one hidden motive as the public explanation.

Possible downstream consequences may therefore arise from a **compromise formation**.

Hard requirement:

`ONE_SURFACE_MOVE != ONE_CAUSAL_MOTIVE`.

The renderer should realize the lawful compromise, not narrate the hidden causal plurality.

## 9. Expression trajectory is a useful metaphor but not yet an object

The phrase `expression trajectory` is useful insofar as it means:

> a constrained direction through the current space of possible expressions, shaped by conscious content, social intent, inhibition, defense, salience, relationship context, uncertainty, and hidden-cause-derived surface effects.

But architecture has not earned:

- a required `ExpressionTrajectory` class;
- a fixed list of trajectory dimensions;
- one numeric vector;
- one universal trajectory update formula.

Current status:

`EXPRESSION_TRAJECTORY = CONCEPTUAL SHORTHAND ONLY`.

The later architecture must determine whether the same semantics are better distributed across existing V2 owners plus one narrow PFF bridge.

## 10. PFF should probably output reasons-to-constrain, not downstream owner decisions

A major anti-gadget refinement is that PFF need not duplicate CAN-016/017/018/019/ADP-022/023 outputs.

Instead PFF may eventually supply exact, source-bound semantic facts/constraints that those owners lawfully consume.

Conceptual example:

PFF establishes:

- actor consciously entertains but is uncertain about B's flirtation;
- actor wants continued engagement;
- direct disclosure of attraction is inhibited;
- perceived exposure is high in the current live configuration;
- actor is unusually attentive to B;
- hidden attraction may affect warmth/playfulness but is not actor-known.

Then existing V2 owners may independently determine:

- which content is salient;
- which register constraints apply;
- which response operation is lawful;
- which relationship-expression refs are permitted;
- what uncertainty disclosure operation is legal;
- what face/boundary constraints apply.

This is preferable to PFF directly manufacturing all downstream decisions.

## 11. The seam likely requires provenance without semantic exposure

A machine may need to replay why a surface constraint existed while preventing that causal provenance from becoming renderer-visible content.

Therefore architecture may need two different visibility scopes:

### 11.1 audit/causal provenance

Records sufficient to explain:

- which V3/PFF causes produced the constraint;
- which authority/version applied;
- what transformation occurred;
- why the effect was permitted.

### 11.2 renderer semantic surface

Only the minimal lawful downstream semantic effect and prohibited-content constraints.

Thus:

`AUDIT_PROVENANCE != RENDERER_CONTEXT`.

`PROVENANCE_PRESERVATION != SEMANTIC_DISCLOSURE`.

This distinction is essential for both replay and actor opacity.

## 12. The renderer must receive the actual surface interaction again

The zero-loss PFF vision requires that the final renderer semantically respond to the actual thing that was said/done.

A dangerous alternative would be:

`incoming message -> encoder -> psyche coordinates -> renderer`

where the renderer receives only derived psychic coordinates and no longer sees the actual discourse.

That would make expression a translation of internal coordinates rather than a response to conversation.

Hard requirement:

`FINAL_RENDERER_INPUT` must include, subject to exact later contract:

- the actual surface/discourse content being responded to;
- actor-lawful semantic content/context;
- bounded PFF/V2 expression constraints/decisions;
- no hidden causal propositions unless independently actor-accessible.

Thus:

`SURFACE_DISCOURSE_REENTRY = MUST_FACTOR`.

## 13. The final renderer is a realization engine, not a psyche reasoner

The renderer may use language competence to:

- phrase;
- sequence;
- choose natural lexical/syntactic realization;
- preserve conversational coherence;
- satisfy explicit semantic/constraint inputs.

It must not decide:

- what the actor secretly feels;
- whether a defense exists;
- what the actor actually knows;
- whether B is attracted;
- which hidden motive should leak;
- whether relationship intimacy has increased;
- which V3 memory is now accessible;
- which psychic explanation is narratively plausible.

`RENDERER = SURFACE REALIZER`

not

`RENDERER = FINAL PSYCHOLOGICAL AUTHORITY`.

## 14. Leakage can be realized without cause disclosure

A subtle but important point:

The renderer does not need to know *why* a constraint exists in order to realize its effect.

Example renderer-visible plan may eventually contain lawful effects equivalent to:

- maintain engagement;
- direct more attention to B's last remark;
- allow mild playful indirectness;
- do not make explicit romantic admission;
- preserve uncertainty about B's motive;
- avoid explicit self-explanation of why the topic matters.

The renderer can realize this naturally from the actual conversation.

It need not know:

`A is secretly attracted to B due to reactivated eros + shame + rejection history`.

This is the core firewall principle.

## 15. Surface effects themselves must remain semantically bounded

Even effect-level semantics can accidentally imply too much.

Example:

`playful` might be compatible with many causes.

But if the downstream representation says:

`ROMANTIC_FLIRTATION_REQUIRED`,

that may already overstate the actor's accessible/expressible state.

Therefore later architecture must distinguish:

- broad expression affordance/pressure;
- actor-conscious intent;
- relationship semantic claim;
- hidden-cause-derived modulation.

No effect label may smuggle a forbidden proposition by another name.

## 16. Causal opacity should survive inversion attempts

A well-designed surface-effect representation should not automatically authorize reverse inference:

`playfulness -> hidden attraction`

or

`withdrawal -> hidden shame`.

The same surface effect can arise from many causal configurations.

This is psychologically correct and privacy/epistemically important.

Hard law:

`SURFACE_EFFECT != UNIQUE_CAUSAL_DECODER`.

The system may retain causal provenance internally, but downstream consumers may not infer hidden cause merely from effect class.

## 17. Actor-conscious intention and hidden cause can point in opposite directions

Example:

Hidden attraction increases engagement pressure.

Conscious intention is to appear uninterested.

Final expression may therefore combine:

- sustained conversation;
- verbally dismissive stance;
- unusually quick reply;
- teasing;
- no explicit admission.

PFF must preserve this contradiction rather than selecting either the hidden attraction or conscious intention as the single truth of expression.

`CONSCIOUS_INTENTION != TOTAL_CAUSAL_CONTROL`.

`HIDDEN_CAUSE != SURFACE_DESTINY`.

## 18. Willingness to disclose is not epistemic access

An actor may know something and refuse to say it.

An actor may be uncertain and still disclose the uncertainty.

An actor may not know a hidden cause and therefore cannot intentionally disclose it even if it affects expression.

Thus:

`KNOWING X`
`!= WANTING TO SAY X`
`!= BEING ALLOWED TO SAY X`
`!= X AFFECTING HOW SOMETHING ELSE IS SAID`.

This is one of the central reasons the PFF→V2 seam must preserve multiple non-collapsible dimensions.

## 19. Strategic ambiguity is a legitimate expression outcome

Humans often intentionally preserve ambiguity:

- flirt without admitting attraction;
- test without revealing the test;
- soften disagreement without capitulating;
- imply concern without naming vulnerability;
- leave room to retreat from a status challenge;
- ask a question whose personal stakes remain hidden.

Strategic ambiguity may be conscious, defensive, or mixed.

PFF should preserve the conditions that make ambiguity useful without the renderer being told the hidden stakes in unrestricted form.

`AMBIGUOUS_EXPRESSION != SEMANTIC FAILURE`.

## 20. Silence, omission, and non-selection are expressive consequences too

Expression formation is not only about what appears.

The actor may:

- omit one accessible topic;
- answer only the safe part of a question;
- not pursue a salient opening;
- delay acknowledgment;
- keep a joke rather than disclose;
- refuse to explain a reaction;
- remain engaged while withholding one target.

These can be psychologically meaningful without becoming new V2 response-move labels.

The PFF requirement is to preserve **selective non-expression** as a possible consequence of live psyche.

## 21. Text-only versus richer media

PFF must not fabricate leakage channels that the interface does not support.

In text-only interaction, expression may vary through:

- semantic choice;
- topic selection;
- omission;
- timing where actual timing is governed/available;
- length/degree of elaboration where separately lawful;
- indirectness;
- questions;
- recurrence;
- lexical/register realization through V2 authority;
- contradiction across turns.

It must not invent:

- gaze;
- facial tension;
- body posture;
- vocal prosody;

unless those modalities are actually part of the governed medium.

`LEAKAGE_CHANNEL MUST BE MEDIUM-SUPPORTED`.

## 22. Generated-generated versus generated-external interaction

The firewall must work in both cases.

### 22.1 generated-generated

The system may internally know both actors' hidden V3/PFF states.

But A's renderer must still not receive B's hidden state unless A can lawfully know it.

`SYSTEM_KNOWS_B != A_KNOWS_B`.

### 22.2 generated-external/human

The human interlocutor has no governed hidden psyche available to Rawlsing.

PFF must therefore operate on observable interaction + actor-local readings/uncertainty.

The renderer must not hallucinate hidden user motives merely because they would make the dialogue psychologically satisfying.

## 23. Relationship truth must remain upstream-bounded

A PFF state such as:

`A feels unusually safe with B`

must not automatically become:

`A and B have a trusting relationship`.

Likewise:

`A strongly attends to B`

must not become:

`B is important to A in durable V3 relationship truth`.

Current interaction expression may reflect the local state while durable relationship facts remain separately owned.

## 24. Hidden-cause effects can be positive, negative, or contradictory

A hidden cause may:

- increase engagement;
- inhibit explicit content;
- amplify attention;
- reduce willingness to disclose;
- increase joking;
- increase coldness;
- produce both approach and withdrawal pressures.

Therefore there is no universal mapping such as:

`attraction -> warmth`

or

`shame -> withdrawal`.

The PFF mediation must consume the actual live configuration, including defenses and competing motives.

## 25. No universal surface ontology is yet justified

It may eventually be tempting to define a comprehensive vector such as:

- warmth;
- dominance;
- directness;
- disclosure;
- playfulness;
- intensity;
- distance;
- formality;
- etc.

This examination does **not** adopt such a vector.

Reasons:

- some dimensions may already belong to V2 owners;
- some may be medium-specific;
- some may not be independent;
- some may be context-specific;
- numeric scales have not been justified;
- expressive meaning may be relationally nonmonotonic.

The architecture must derive the minimum sufficient surface semantics from actual upstream/downstream obligations.

## 26. No-gadget stitching law

Before creating a new PFF expression-plan object, architecture must attempt to stitch the required semantics through existing owners:

1. ADP-017 actor-access/claim permission;
2. CAN-016 salience;
3. CAN-017 register;
4. CAN-018 response move;
5. CAN-019 relationship expression;
6. ADP-022 uncertainty/disclosure;
7. ADP-023 boundary/face-risk/effort/attention;
8. accepted renderer-bounded realization contract;
9. PFF-specific hidden-cause-to-surface-effect mediation only where a semantic remainder survives.

The purpose of PFF is not to replace this downstream decomposition with one giant `PsychePlan`.

## 28. Hard negatives

Reject unless independently justified:

- hidden psyche dumped into renderer prompt;
- renderer asked to decide what should leak;
- one hidden motive translated directly into one surface trait;
- PFF selecting all V2 downstream decisions itself;
- response move treated as psychic motive;
- salience treated as strongest activation;
- register treated as personality/type;
- relationship warmth treated as trust/intimacy truth;
- actor uncertainty widened into guessed belief;
- strategic ambiguity resolved by renderer into explicit motive;
- hidden-cause provenance dereferenced by surface generator;
- one monolithic style vector standing in for expression formation;
- one LLM used simultaneously as encoder, psyche decider, and renderer without authority separation;
- final renderer seeing only psyche coordinates and not the actual interaction;
- renderer wording used afterward as evidence of hidden actor state without lawful re-entry/admission;
- type/wing/health label injected into renderer as behavior instruction.

## 29. Positive obligations for later architecture

Any successful PFF architecture must eventually preserve:

1. actor-accessible content distinct from hidden causal content;
2. actor-conscious intention distinct from total causal influence;
3. hidden causes producing bounded surface effects without direct semantic disclosure;
4. explicit negative constraints/withholding;
5. leakage without hidden-proposition prompt injection;
6. multi-motive compromise expression;
7. strategic ambiguity;
8. selective non-expression;
9. uncertainty without forced resolution;
10. downstream V2 owner separation;
11. audit provenance distinct from renderer context;
12. no reverse authorization from surface effect to hidden cause;
13. actual discourse re-entry at final renderer;
14. renderer as realization, not psychological authority;
15. medium-supported leakage only;
16. generated-generated opacity and generated-human uncertainty;
17. exact durable-causal re-entry to V3 only after separately lawful admission.
