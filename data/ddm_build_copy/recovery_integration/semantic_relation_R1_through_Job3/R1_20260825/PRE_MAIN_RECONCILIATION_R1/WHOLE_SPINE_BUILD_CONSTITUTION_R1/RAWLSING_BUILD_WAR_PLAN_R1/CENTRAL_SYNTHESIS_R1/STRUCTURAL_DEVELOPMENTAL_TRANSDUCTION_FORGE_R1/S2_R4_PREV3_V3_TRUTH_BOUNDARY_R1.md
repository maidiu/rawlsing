## 3. Objective active condition is not subjective premise

An objectively current condition can be socially real without proof that the actor knows, notices, understands, appraises, or experiences it in any specific way.

Examples:

```text
OBJECTIVE ACTIVE BENEFIT ELIGIBILITY
!= ACTOR KNOWS THEY ARE ELIGIBLE

OBJECTIVE WAGE DEPENDENCY
!= FELT INSECURITY

OBJECTIVE ACTIVE AUTHORITY RELATION
!= FELT CONTROL / SUBORDINATION

OBJECTIVE SERVICE UNAVAILABILITY
!= PERCEIVED DEPRIVATION
```

Any transformation into person-side premise/meaning remains downstream.
