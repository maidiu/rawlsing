# VOZHD / HQ — BOUNDED EMPIRICAL INCOMPLETENESS RUNTIME LAW R1

Status:
`HQ_ADOPTED_ARCHITECTURAL_LAW__IMPLEMENTATION_AND_HOSTILE_RUNTIME_COURT_REQUIRED`

Authority grain:
application runtime admissibility under heterogeneous, partially unavailable empirical-source authority.

This law is adopted because Rawlsing must preserve strict source authority **without making application existence contingent on perfect archival survival across every survey family, country and wave**.

## 1. Two gates are permanently distinct

### FULL_SOURCE_CERTIFICATION

A source/family/country/wave may claim population-level or other strong empirical semantics only when the exact authority required by that operation is positively established.

Full certification remains an active quality/coverage target. It is never weakened by this law.

### APPLICATION_RUNTIME_ADMISSIBLE

The application may execute when every empirical contribution it actually consumes has a lawful bounded state and every unavailable contribution is handled by a precommitted runtime fallback rule.

`FULL_SOURCE_CERTIFICATION != APPLICATION_RUNTIME_ADMISSIBLE`.

Failure to establish population target/frame/design for a source scope must reduce or remove the claims that scope may make. It must **not by itself brick the generator**.

## 2. Runtime evidence tiers

Every empirical contribution reaching the runtime must be classified at or below one of these ceilings:

1. `POPULATION_CERTIFIED`
   - exact source/replay identity;
   - sufficient positive target/coverage, frame and actual-design authority for the population claim being made;
   - any routing/weight/estimand/relation authority additionally required by the exact operation.

2. `SOURCE_LOCAL_CERTIFIED`
   - exact source/replay identity and source-local semantics are governed;
   - population representativeness/generalization is not authorized;
   - may support source-local descriptive/structural evidence only where the consumer explicitly accepts that grain.

3. `PARTIAL_AUTHORITY`
   - only explicitly supported components may contribute;
   - every unsupported component is typed unavailable/unknown/not-authorized at its exact grain;
   - no complement, missingness, country-label, neighboring-wave or conventional-practice inference.

4. `UNAVAILABLE`
   - contributes no empirical point estimate or fact;
   - absence is not zero, neutral evidence, negative evidence or nearest-neighbor evidence.

These ceilings compose with existing PRE553 typed states such as `UNKNOWN_SOURCE_RELATION`, `NONCOMPARABLE`, `WEIGHT_NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `UNCERTAINTY_UNQUANTIFIED`, `COVERAGE_LIMITED` and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## 3. Missing evidence changes uncertainty, not truth

When an intended survey contribution is absent or weaker than planned:

- do not silently replace it with another source;
- do not silently renormalize the remaining empirical-source weights;
- do not impute a neighboring country, wave, family or demographic cell without exact relation/backfill authority;
- do not convert missing empirical support into a point value.

The lawful runtime response is one of:

A. consume an explicitly authorized alternate source;
B. consume an explicitly authorized broader geographic/temporal prior;
C. consume a separately governed model prior that is **not represented as empirical evidence**;
D. widen the driver's uncertainty/support envelope;
E. omit the unsupported driver contribution while preserving a valid generator state.

Any source substitution, geographic pooling, temporal backfill or harmonization requires its own positive relation authority.

## 4. No implicit reweighting

Suppose a driver was designed to receive contributions from multiple evidence channels.

If one channel is unavailable, the surviving channels do **not** automatically inherit its mass.

Each driver must own an explicit missing-evidence rule specifying:

- original evidence-channel role;
- whether alternate evidence is permitted;
- whether broader-prior fallback is permitted;
- whether a non-empirical model prior is permitted;
- how uncertainty expands;
- whether the contribution is omitted;
- whether any coefficient reallocation is permitted and under what separately governed rule.

`MISSING_SOURCE != RENORMALIZE_SURVIVORS`.

## 5. Generator fallback is modeling law, not fabricated empirical authority

A broad/non-informative/model prior may be used to keep the generator executable only if it is explicitly versioned and identified as a **runtime modeling prior**.

It must never be emitted, stored or described as:

- an observed national mean;
- a survey-derived cultural fact;
- a population estimate;
- evidence that the unavailable source would have supplied.

The resulting generated person may be less tightly constrained. That is lawful.

False precision is not.

## 6. Coverage must travel with output

Runtime output must be able to expose or internally retain a deterministic empirical-coverage receipt recording, at minimum:

- which source families/scopes actually contributed;
- their evidence tier;
- which intended channels were unavailable;
- fallback rule used per missing channel;
- uncertainty/support widening caused by missing channels;
- any authorized relation/backfill authority consumed.

This receipt is diagnostic/provenance authority. It must not leak hidden generator internals into ordinary end-user prose unless the product explicitly requests that surface.
