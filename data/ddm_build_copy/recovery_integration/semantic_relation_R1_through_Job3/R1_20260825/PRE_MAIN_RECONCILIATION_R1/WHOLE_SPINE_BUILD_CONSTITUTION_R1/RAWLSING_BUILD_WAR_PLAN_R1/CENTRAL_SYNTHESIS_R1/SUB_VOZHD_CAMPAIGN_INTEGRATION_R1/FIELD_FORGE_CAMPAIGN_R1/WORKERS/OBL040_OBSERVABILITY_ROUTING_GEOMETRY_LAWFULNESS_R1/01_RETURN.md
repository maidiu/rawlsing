## 4. Relation-specific commitment law

A geometry-like commitment is lawful only when the asserted relation itself requires that semantic commitment and the prerequisite authority exists.

### Identity

Exact identity/equality may be asserted when identity is already governed at the relevant grain. Identity does not imply order or distance.

### Order

Order is lawful only when source-native order or a separately authorized ordering relation is constitutive of the claim. Numeric category codes alone do not authorize order.

### Cardinal metric magnitude

Metric magnitude is lawful only when an exact quantitative unit and scale semantics are governed for the variables/objects being compared and the claimed operation is valid at that scope. Ordinal ranks, category codes and arbitrary coordinates do not become cardinal units.

### Neighborhood / adjacency

Neighborhood is lawful only under an explicit adjacency/neighborhood rule with declared object universe, relation identity, scope and replay law. Coappearance or lexical proximity is insufficient.

### Topology

Topology is lawful only when explicit relation edges/incidence and their scope constitute the claim. A topology may be disconnected. Missing bridges may not be filled by embedding or convenience.

### Disconnected components

When no authorized bridge/map exists between components, preserve the components as disconnected. Cross-component distance/order is not defined by default.

### Coordinates / embeddings

Coordinates or embeddings are derived views unless a separate authority makes their coordinate system constitutive. They never retroactively authorize the relation they visualize.

### `NO_GEOMETRY`

If the actor-independent claim is fully answerable by exact identity/joint/conditional/reference relations without order, metric, neighborhood or topology, the lawful state is `NO_GEOMETRY_REQUIRED`. No stronger representation is minted merely because it is convenient.

### `NONCOMPARABLE` / `NO_TRANSPORT`

If a requested comparison needs a bridge/map whose authority is absent, the cross-object or cross-context assertion is `NONCOMPARABLE` / `NO_TRANSPORT`; local lawful objects may still remain valid at their own grains.
