## Critical distinctions

### Event is the strongest current form

Rawlsing has unusually exact first-OEE event machinery: source authority contracts, occurrence and independence separation, acceptance-gated execution, replay, and an explicit prohibition on treating co-location/co-presence as encounter. This is strong evidence that `EVENT` is not a theoretical gap.

But current machinery and current positive fact are separate. OBL061 states that the first current contact occurrence, independence fact, positive acceptance entries, ENCOUNTER and OEE are absent. Therefore:

`EVENT SEMANTICS/EXECUTOR = EXPLICIT`

while

`FIRST CURRENT POSITIVE EVENT INSTANCE = ABSENT AT COMMISSIONED TIP`.

### Condition and relation are split across jurisdictions

PRE-V3 condition/relation semantics are actor-independent. V3 exact positive applicability/contact facts are downstream and must be separately owned. This prevents the invalid shortcut:

`STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY → REALIZED ACTOR CONDITION/RELATION`

without actor/context/runtime evidence.

### Repetition is not practice

A list or history of repeated events is not by itself a `RECURRENT ACTIVITY/PRACTICE`. CAN-011 gives practice a persistent historically formed semantic home, but the current evidence does not prove a general law by which structured-world opportunities/dependencies become such practice.

### Event-to-transition is not transition-to-development

CAN-008/TD-R05 make the crucial separation:

`EVENT != RESPONSE != PERSISTENT_CHANGE`.

That separation is owned semantically. The full connective rule that earns persistent change is not thereby owned or implemented.

### Trajectory is not PRE-V3 attractor possibility

PRE-V3 attractor prerequisites remain actor-independent structural possibility. Realized actor attractor history/trajectory belongs downstream to V3. The two may be composed only through an actor-specific developmental mechanism; they may not be collapsed.

### Turning point remains a real uncovered form

No exact current owner recovered by this lane supplies criteria by which an event/transition becomes a turning point in an actor's trajectory. This is the clearest temporal-form positive gap.
