## 2. Strongest external-theory leads from prior reconnaissance

### 2.1 Bernard Lonergan — relative / absolute horizon; conversion

**Reconnaissance finding:** The phrase `absolute horizon` is already a technical expression in Lonergan's work. The prior chat reported a particularly close structural resemblance between:

- horizons shaped by psychological/social/cultural development;
- an absolute horizon associated with conversion / non-conversion and self-constitution;
- the horizon's role in what can count for the person as real, valuable, knowable, or genuinely possible;
- an `originary pole` associated with possibility of conversion/self-constitution versus an `existential pole` associated with the person actually constituted.

**Why PFF cares:** This may be the single closest deep structural witness for Rawlsing's distinction between:

```text
FINITE_HISTORY_SHAPED_RELATIVE_WORLD
versus
TRANSFORMATION_IN_WHAT_CAN_COUNT_AS_REAL / VALUABLE / POSSIBLE
```

and for the future distinction:

```text
CONSTITUTIVE_CAPACITY
!=
ACTUAL_DEVELOPMENTAL_REALIZATION
```

**Important divergence to preserve:** Rawlsing currently reserves an `ABSOLUTE_ABSOLUTE` all-dimensional limit case and needs a richer account of partial/captured/distorted realizations. Do not assume Lonergan's exact ontology, theological commitments, or terminology maps one-to-one.

**Future court:** primary-source excavation of horizon, conversion/non-conversion, originary/existential poles, and whether Lonergan permits inauthentic absolute horizons in the exact way the prior chat reported.

## 5. Project-origin north star

The user identified this phenomenon as closely connected to the lived origin of Rawlsing.

The personal details are intentionally minimized in this durable repository bank; the semantic motivation is preserved.

The motivating problem is:

> A person can discover that close others are unable to humanize people on the far side of a morally and politically charged conflict, even where the user experiences the others as fundamentally decent and had assumed a deeper shared human/moral horizon. The resulting rupture creates an urgent practical question: how can people come to understand and relate to persons very different from them in a way that preserves real difference while making the other still feel, somehow, of the same human kind?

Rawlsing's north-star ambition therefore includes:

```text
REAL_DIFFERENCE
WITHOUT
ALIEN_KIND_OF_HUMAN
```

and:

```text
UNDERSTAND_PERSON_AS_HISTORY_SHAPED_OTHER
WITHOUT
REDUCING_PERSON_TO_DEMOGRAPHIC_STEREOTYPE
```

and:

```text
PRESERVE_CONFLICT / RESPONSIBILITY / STRUCTURE / DIFFERENCE
WITHOUT
DEHUMANIZING_THE_OTHER_INTO_AN_UNRELATABLE_OBJECT
```

This does **not** mean Rawlsing must force sympathy, agreement, reconciliation, forgiveness, equivalence, or denial of wrongdoing.

The target is more basic and more difficult:

> construct persons whose social-historical formation, psychic organization, bounded horizons, contradictions, partial openings, and possible transformations are intelligible enough that radical difference does not require ontological othering.

This project-origin north star should later become a high-value synthetic-personhood court.
