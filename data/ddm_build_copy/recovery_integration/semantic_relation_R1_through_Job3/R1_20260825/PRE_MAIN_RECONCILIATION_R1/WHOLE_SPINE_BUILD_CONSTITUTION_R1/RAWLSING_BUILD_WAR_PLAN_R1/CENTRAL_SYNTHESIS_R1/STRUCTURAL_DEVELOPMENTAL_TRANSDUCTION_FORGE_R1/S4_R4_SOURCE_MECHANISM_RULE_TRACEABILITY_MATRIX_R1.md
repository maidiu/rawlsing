## 0. Governing proof law

This matrix traces already-extracted source evidence into the five R4 mechanism-rule classes. It does **not** convert theory or empirical findings into executable authority.

```text
PRIMARY / EMPIRICAL SOURCE
!= SOURCE CLAIM
!= NORMALIZED MECHANISM
!= P1-P5 CLASS
!= GOVERNED MECHANISM RULE
!= CALIBRATION
!= EXECUTABLE ACTOR-FACT AUTHORITY
```

A source can justify a distinction, a candidate mechanism, a rule *shape*, or an evidence ceiling without licensing a current production rule or a numeric actor-level effect.

Every D1' row ends at an **objective positioned social consequence**. Any premise, appraisal, motive, mastery, distress, cognitive style, identity, trait, capacity, learning or durable developmental outcome is downstream only.

## 1. Evidence-status vocabulary

`CONCEPTUAL/THEORETICAL` — source develops distinctions or mechanism grammar but does not causally identify a portable actor effect.

`OBSERVATIONAL` — source reports associations or case-patterns without a design sufficient to isolate the claimed causal effect.

`LONGITUDINAL_OBSERVATIONAL` — temporal ordering/reciprocity is better observed, but selection and model assumptions remain.

`QUALITATIVE/HISTORICAL` — mechanism-rich process evidence; not population calibration.

`FIELD/LAB EXPERIMENT` — stronger causal identification for the manipulated cue/treatment and measured outcome in the study scope; transport remains separate.

`CONSTITUTIVE/DEONTIC RULE EVIDENCE` — an operative rule/state can itself establish an objective entitlement, obligation, authority scope, restriction or eligibility when the semantics are constitutive; contingent receipt/treatment/performance still needs an occurrence witness.

## 4. Traceability proof result

All important **objective seam** mechanism families recovered by T1–T6 and M1–M11 map without distortion to one or a composition of P1–P5. Apparent exceptions fall into one of four non-P6 categories:

1. **subject mediation/development** — downstream V3;
2. **temporal/history qualification** — rule applicability/lifecycle/history binding, not a mechanism primitive;
3. **social-world mutation/reciprocity** — separate external owner/writer, after which D1' may consume the new fact;
4. **measurement/source/calibration governance** — companion rule-admission law, not a social-causal primitive.

`P6_REQUIRED = NO`.

The corpus therefore supports the **shape** of the R4 five-class topology while simultaneously forbidding source citation, theory language, population association or effect estimate from functioning as executable actor-rule authority by itself.
