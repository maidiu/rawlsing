## 1. Primary conclusion

The strongest current model is:

> **Anticipation is a future-facing mode of situated recruitment: the actor forms or carries sparse, relevance-bounded expectations/hypotheses about possible consequences, and those expected consequences can recruit present motive, defense, attention, inhibition, and communicative intention before the future event actually occurs.**

This yields the core distinction:

`DURABLE V3 EXPECTATION / STRIVING`

from:

`CURRENT SITUATION-SPECIFIC PROSPECTIVE HYPOTHESIS / ORIENTATION`

from:

`DESIRED OR FEARED OUTCOME`

from:

`CURRENT ACTION / COMMUNICATIVE INTENTION`

from:

`ACTUAL FUTURE OUTCOME`.

Central laws:

`DURABLE_EXPECTATION != CURRENT_PREDICTION`.

`CURRENT_PREDICTION != DESIRED_OUTCOME`.

`DESIRED_OUTCOME != CURRENT_INTENTION`.

`CURRENT_INTENTION != ACTUAL_OUTCOME`.

`PREDICTED_COUNTERPART_RESPONSE != COUNTERPART_ACTUAL_RESPONSE`.

`EXPECTED_FUTURE_CAN_CAUSE_CURRENT_RESPONSE_WITHOUT_BECOMING FACT`.

## 2. Durable expectations already supply much of the deep basis

V3 already owns durable learned expectations and strivings.

Examples of durable expectation-like organization may include:

- showing need leads to withdrawal;
- authority punishes disagreement;
- competence earns approval;
- intimacy tends toward control;
- apology restores connection;
- public weakness reduces status.

These are not the same as a present forecast.

A current situation can instantiate one of many possible actor-local predictions:

`If I ask B for reassurance now, B may think I am needy.`

`If I challenge this boss here, I may lose the opportunity.`

`If I disclose attraction in this context, B may reciprocate or reject me.`

Thus anticipation does not require a duplicate `ExpectationState` in PFF.

The likely PFF remainder is **current application/projection of durable organization into a specific possible future**.

---

## 3. Prospective psychology is shared across V3 and PFF

Generated life decisions already require expected consequences.

A person may:

- endure current difficulty for future gain;
- avoid risk because of expected loss;
- repair a relationship to prevent rupture;
- pursue education for future competence/status;
- refuse temptation because of future self-concept;
- leave danger before harm occurs;
- act courageously despite fear because of future value/identity.

Therefore future-facing cognition is not chat-specific.

Shared law:

`CURRENT RESPONSE CAN BE SHAPED BY EXPECTED CONSEQUENCES`.

PFF specializes this because interaction contains unusually rapid social prediction:

- what will the user think if I say this?;
- will they answer warmly?;
- can I still retreat if I imply interest?;
- will they notice I am upset?;
- will this repair or worsen the interaction?;
- what do they now expect from me after what I just said?

Thus:

`PROSPECTIVE RESPONSE FORMATION = SHARED PERSON PHENOMENON`.

`HIGH-RESOLUTION COUNTERPART/AUDIENCE FORECASTING = PFF INTERACTION SPECIALIZATION`.

---

## 4. Actor-local prediction is not machine prediction of the world

This distinction is critical.

The machine may have access to more objective state than the actor.

PFF anticipation concerns what **this actor expects, fears, hopes, or implicitly prepares for**, given their own accessible evidence and historically formed organization.

A may predict:

`B will reject me.`

while B is in fact strongly attracted.

The prediction may still cause A to withdraw.

Therefore:

`SYSTEM WORLD MODEL != ACTOR PROSPECTIVE MODEL`.

`ACTOR PREDICTION ACCURACY != PREDICTION CAUSAL POTENCY`.

The actor can be wrong and still act coherently from their own perspective.

---

## 5. Prospective representation need not always be proposition-like

Some expected futures are explicit:

`If I tell him, he will leave.`

Others are diffuse:

- dread;
- hope;
- sense that something may go wrong;
- readiness for rejection;
- expectation of awkwardness;
- attraction toward an undefined possibility;
- vigilance around an unresolved reply.

Therefore future-facing pressure may be:

- explicit forecast;
- partial hypothesis;
- broad prospective orientation;
- conditioned readiness derived from durable expectation.

Hard law:

`ANTICIPATORY_CAUSATION != NECESSARILY_EXPLICIT_FUTURE_SENTENCE`.

This preserves actor opacity and avoids forcing all prospective state into prose.

---

## 6. Anticipation reaches backward into present perception and recruitment

Future models do not operate only after perception.

A expects rejection.

B gives an ambiguous short answer.

Because rejection is prospectively active, the reading `B is pulling away` may become more recruitable than `B is busy`.

Likewise anticipated reward can heighten attention to weak opportunity cues.

Thus:

`DURABLE EXPECTATION + CURRENT PROSPECTIVE ORIENTATION`
`-> CURRENT APPRAISAL / RECRUITMENT BIAS` is possible.

This is another reason PFF cannot be reduced to a linear pipeline from perception to planning.

The live system is recursive.

---

## 7. Desired future, feared future, predicted future and intended act must remain noncollapsed

Example:

A wants reassurance.

A predicts direct reassurance-seeking will reduce B's respect.

A fears appearing needy.

A intends to make an indirect joke instead.

These four facts can coexist:

`DESIRED = reassurance`.

`PREDICTED CONSEQUENCE OF DIRECT ASK = status/exposure cost`.

`FEARED = being seen as needy`.

`INTENDED ACT = indirect test/joke`.

A shallow planner would collapse these into one goal.

Rawlsing must preserve their conflict.

---

## 8. Testing is best understood as an action function, not a new behavior class

The first pass identified teasing, withdrawing, partial disclosure, rival mention, boundary-setting, indirect questions and status challenges as possible tests.

The second pass suggests the underlying phenomenon is:

`UNCERTAINTY ABOUT RELEVANT FUTURE / COUNTERPART STATE`
`+ DESIRE TO REDUCE THAT UNCERTAINTY`
`+ ACTION EXPECTED TO PRODUCE DIAGNOSTIC EVIDENCE`.

That function can be realized through many ordinary actions.

Therefore no `TestingBehavior` module is justified.

`TESTING = POSSIBLE CURRENT FUNCTION OF ACTION`.

`SAME ACTION MAY TEST + APPROACH + DEFEND + PROVOKE SIMULTANEOUSLY`.

---

## 9. Testing can create the evidence it later reads

A expects abandonment and withdraws to see whether B pursues.

B interprets withdrawal as disinterest and gives space.

A then reads the lack of pursuit as confirmation that B does not care.

The resulting dyadic sequence is real.

But it does not provide clean evidence for the original actor hypothesis.

Conceptual loop:

`ACTOR PREDICTION`
`-> TESTING / PREEMPTIVE ACTION`
`-> COUNTERPART RESPONSE CHANGED BY THAT ACTION`
`-> ACTOR INTERPRETATION`
`-> EXPECTATION REINFORCEMENT OR CHANGE`.

This is a major bridge into future V3 relationship/developmental update, but no durable reinforcement is automatic.

---

## 10. Preemptive defense is ordinary defense recruited by an expected threat

Defense need not wait for injury.

A may:

- reject before rejection;
- mock before being mocked;
- withdraw before dependence deepens;
- dominate before expected control;
- confess a flaw first to control exposure;
- become angry before vulnerability becomes visible.

No separate future-defense subsystem is needed.

The same defense/regulation logic can be recruited by an anticipated event:

`EXPECTED THREAT`
`-> CURRENT DEFENSIVE OPERATION`.

Thus anticipation stitches directly into the defense second pass.

---

## 11. Strategic ambiguity is prospective optionality

Ambiguity may be intentional even when the actor is not confused.

A flirtatious joke can:

- invite reciprocation;
- gather evidence;
- preserve deniability;
- protect status;
- allow retreat;
- sustain play.

The psychologically important property is not `ambiguity` as a style field but the actor's relation to future branches.

`AMBIGUOUS ACT CAN PRESERVE OPTIONALITY`.

This becomes one contributor to situated communicative intention, while V2 still owns the exact lawful expression realization.

---

## 12. Prospective consequences can span several domains simultaneously

A current act may be shaped by expected consequences involving:

- counterpart reaction;
- relationship trajectory;
- material/practical outcome;
- audience/reputation/status;
- self-image;
- future self;
- obligation;
- internal emotional consequence;
- memory/self-model destabilization.

These need not be collapsed to one utility score.

Example:

Confessing attraction may simultaneously promise:

- intimacy;
- relief;
- rejection;
- exposure;
- status loss;
- future clarity.

Thus:

`EXPECTED_FUTURE_VALUE != ONE_VALENCE`.

`PROSPECTIVE_CONFLICT = NONLINEAR / MULTI-OBJECTIVE`.

No universal utility function is admitted.

---

## 13. Future self is a relation to self-model, not a sub-agent

Statements such as:

`I will hate myself tomorrow if I do this.`

`I don't want to become someone who cheats.`

`This is the risk I said I wanted to take.`

show that current action can be organized by an imagined later self-evaluation.

This does not require a literal future-self agent or timeline simulator.

It can be represented conceptually as:

`CURRENT ACTION POSSIBILITY`
`× STANDING SELF-MODEL / VALUES / STRIVINGS`
`-> ANTICIPATED SELF-RELATION`.

This is another stitch into existing person organization.

---

## 14. Open future branches can persist as fast residue

An unresolved interaction can leave:

- waiting for a reply;
- expecting an apology;
- fearing departure;
- wondering whether B noticed;
- anticipating a later confrontation;
- hoping a disclosure changes the relationship.

This is not just affective residue.

It is a still-relevant possible future organizing present attention and interpretation.

Current hypothesis:

`OPEN PROSPECTIVE BRANCH = POSSIBLE SESSION/EPISODE-LIVE RELATION`.

It is not automatically a durable V3 expectation.

This may be one of the narrowest genuine PFF semantic remainders, but no persistent `FutureBranchState` is admitted yet.

---

## 15. Actual outcomes become new evidence, not automatic expectation reset

When the future resolves, the actual result re-enters as an event.

A expects rejection.

B responds warmly.

A may:

- update quickly;
- distrust the warmth;
- feel relief;
- feel shame about having expected rejection;
- reinterpret warmth as manipulation;
- remain sensitized despite opening.

Therefore:

`DISCONFIRMING OUTCOME != AUTOMATIC DURABLE EXPECTATION UPDATE`.

The event may change the current PFF configuration immediately, while durable expectation/relationship/self-model changes remain V3-bound.

This is exactly the same one-person recursive law already established for self-output.

---

## 16. Anticipation after own output is an especially important fast loop

Once A says something, the future landscape changes immediately.

A may think:

- now B may reject me;
- now B knows I lied;
- now I may need to repair;
- now she may reciprocate;
- now I cannot easily retreat;
- now my promise has consequences.

Thus:

`OWN OUTPUT OCCURRED`
`-> UPDATED ACTOR-LOCAL FUTURE MODEL`
`-> CURRENT RECRUITMENT / DEFENSE / INTENTION`
`-> NEXT OUTPUT`.

This is a natural extension of ordinary recursion, not a separate conversational planning layer.

## 17. Enneagram-derived pursuit/frustration distinctions mostly stitch rather than add machinery

The earlier Enneagram decomposition distinguished:

`PURSUIT STRATEGY != FAILURE / FRUSTRATION COPING STRATEGY`.

Prospective psychology clarifies why this distinction matters.

A person may characteristically attempt to produce a desired future by:

- pressing/asserting;
- accommodating/earning;
- withdrawing/disengaging;

and then respond differently when the anticipated route fails:

- reframe positively;
- control/solve;
- reactively intensify.

But these need not become Hornevian/Harmonic runtime classes.

They can emerge as recurring patterns over:

`striving + expectation + mediator + current forecast + current response transformation`.

Thus the Enneagram material again appears to clarify connective psychology rather than require an additional engine.

---

## 18. Prospective modeling must be sparse and relevance-bound

A complete simulation of every possible counterpart response or world branch is neither required nor psychologically justified.

The actor does not consciously enumerate the full game tree of every conversation.

Future architecture should attempt:

> model only those possible consequences that are sufficiently relevant to current strivings, fears, obligations, self-model, relationship, uncertainty, and active conflict to participate in present causation.

Therefore:

`PROSPECTIVE MODEL != WORLD SIMULATOR`.

`MORE BRANCHES != MORE REALISM`.

`RELEVANCE-BOUNDED SPARSE FUTURE ORIENTATION = STRONG CURRENT HYPOTHESIS`.

---

## 19. PFF anticipation should supply reasons/constraints, not select the final response operation

PFF may establish facts such as:

- actor expects direct disclosure may be rejected;
- actor hopes continued engagement will yield reassurance;
- actor wants information about reciprocation;
- actor anticipates face loss if challenged publicly;
- actor fears a promise will create obligation;
- actor expects silence may cause B to pursue.

These can shape current communicative intention.

But PFF should not leap directly to:

`RESPONSE_MOVE = DEFLECT`.

Existing downstream V2 owners remain responsible for their own lawful planning decisions.

Thus:

`ANTICIPATORY PRESSURE != RESPONSE MOVE`.

`PREDICTED CONSEQUENCE != DISCLOSURE DECISION`.

`PFF PROSPECTIVE MODEL != V2 PLANNER`.

Reject unless independently justified:

- durable expectation copied into PFF as duplicate persistent prediction state;
- actor prediction treated as future fact;
- system omniscience substituted for actor expectation;

- every anticipation forced into a proposition;
- testing behavior treated as proof of one hidden motive;
- anticipated threat treated as actual harm;
- strategic ambiguity treated as confusion by default;
- future self instantiated as separate sub-agent;
- disconfirming outcome automatically erasing durable expectation;
- PFF selecting V2 response move directly from prediction;
- future-branch residue automatically persisted as V3 expectation;
- a dedicated counterpart simulator required for every turn.
