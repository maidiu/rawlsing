## 1. Court method

This court was instructed to collapse the five mechanism classes wherever their distinctions were merely domain labels, and to search positively for a sixth class wherever the five could not express the source-backed objective social consequence without distortion.

The five candidate classes are:

- `P1 MATERIAL_ACCESS_DEPENDENCY`
- `P2 DIRECTED_AUTHORITY_OBLIGATION_SANCTION`
- `P3 ORGANIZED_ACTIVITY_TIME`
- `P4 INSTITUTIONAL_GATE_TREATMENT`
- `P5 COUNTERPART_CHANNEL_RELATION`

The classes are mechanism families, not theory-name ontology and not exhaustive nouns for social life. Domain phenomena may be compositions.

## 2. Pairwise collapse court

### P1 cannot collapse into P5

Material entitlement, resource possession, transfer receipt, dependency and security state are not equivalent to having a counterpart relation or an available channel. A support tie may exist without material provision; a benefit may be received through an institutional pipeline without the relationship semantics that P5 carries.

Verdict: `KEEP_DISTINCT`.

### P2 cannot collapse into P3

Directed authority, control rights, obligations, responsibility and sanctionability are relational/normative control facts. Task complexity, routinization, schedule, duration, recurrence and finite-time organization are activity facts. A manager can have a title but perform tightly scripted work; a complex worker can have little authority.

Verdict: `KEEP_DISTINCT`.

### P4 cannot collapse into P2, P5 or P1

A gate/evaluation/treatment mechanism has a distinctive conditional ladder: rule → applicability → gate encounter/application → presented/recognized classification → evaluation/treatment → downstream consequence. Authority may administer a gate, a channel may carry an application, and a resource may be the downstream result, but none is the selection/treatment operation itself.

Verdict: `KEEP_DISTINCT`, with the hard narrowing that P4 means an exact rule-bound institutional selection/evaluation/treatment process, never generic recognition or stigma.

### P5 cannot collapse into P1/P2/P3

Counterpart reachability, contact, information flow, co-participation, linked-life contribution and provision channels can exist without material transfer, authority, or task organization. P5 is necessary for exact actor-to-counterpart topology at the actor-relative level.

Verdict: `KEEP_DISTINCT`.

### P3 cannot be reduced to temporal metadata

P3 contains objective organization of activity: what is done, under what task structure, with what recurrence/intensity/duration/schedule and finite-time competition. Valid-time metadata merely says when a fact holds. Kohn/Schooler, Mortimer, Sève and Dreier all require the stronger activity organization content.

Verdict: `KEEP_DISTINCT`.

## 3. Domain-composition court

The following do **not** warrant new primitives:

- household/care ecology = exact combinations of P5 relation/provision, P2 obligations, P3 tasks/time, and P1 resources;
- work ecology = P2 authority/control + P3 activity/time + P1/P5 where material access or counterpart channels matter;
- differential institutional treatment = P4 treatment plus whatever P1/P2/P3/P5 consequence is actually produced;
- linked lives = P5 exact relation plus an externally admitted source state/action/transition, an exact governed target rule and any resulting P1/P2/P3/P4/P5 consequence;
- social reproduction = a source-theoretic domain requiring compositions of resources, labor, care, obligations, institutions and counterpart relations, not an umbrella runtime primitive;
- class = a structural bundle/source classification whose causal use must decompose through exact positions and P1-P5 mechanisms;
- network = topology/channel conditions under P5 plus separately witnessed contact/provision/exposure where applicable;
- space/geography = a structural input/qualifier that can alter P1 access, P5 reachability or P3 time burden under an exact rule;
- phase/age = a validity/applicability qualifier, not a consequence mechanism;
- historical timing = rule/configuration validity and objective-social-history input, not a sixth primitive;
- uncertainty/probability = evidence/calibration or source-owner issue, not actor consequence truth;
- chronic strain/mastery/support = objective burden/resource/channel antecedents may be P1-P5; felt strain, mastery and perceived support are downstream person-side constructs.
