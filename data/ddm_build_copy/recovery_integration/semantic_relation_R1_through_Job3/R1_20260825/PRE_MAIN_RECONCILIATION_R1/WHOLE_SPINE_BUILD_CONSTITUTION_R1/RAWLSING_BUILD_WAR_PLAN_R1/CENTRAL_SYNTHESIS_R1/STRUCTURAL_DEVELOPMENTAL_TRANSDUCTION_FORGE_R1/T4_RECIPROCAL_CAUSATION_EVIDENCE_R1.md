# T4 RECIPROCAL CAUSATION EVIDENCE R1

STATUS: `T4_RECIPROCAL_EVIDENCE_ADJUDICATED_R1`

## 0. Question adjudicated

The commission requires evidence for both directions:

```text
STRUCTURE → EXPERIENCE / ACTIVITY → PERSON
PERSON → SELECTION / ACTION → LATER STRUCTURAL POSITION OR EXPOSURE
```

T4 finds credible longitudinal evidence for both directions, but **not** evidence that every retained structural-developmental chain is fully identified causally. The correct output is a family of reciprocal mechanisms with graded evidence and explicit gates, not a global claim that structure and personality “cause each other.”

## 1. Evidence scale

| Grade | Meaning in this lane |
|---|---|
| `A-` | Longitudinal temporal ordering plus serious selection/measurement treatment and a directly observed reciprocal or mediating path; still observational, not randomized. |
| `B+` | Longitudinal/fixed-effects or repeated-measures evidence that materially reduces stable selection explanations, with residual time-varying confounding or temporal ambiguity. |
| `B` | Longitudinal observational relationship with measured controls but narrower sample or broader construct. |
| `C+` | Cross-sectional/observational population pattern that strongly supports an exposure/distribution mechanism but not individual causal direction. |
| `T` | Theoretical/review normalization only; not independent causal evidence. |

No retained T4 source earns an experimental `A` grade for the full social-structural chain.

## 3. What the classic reciprocal models actually earn

### 3.1 Kohn & Schooler 1978

The strongest clean reciprocal finding in the lane is the 10-year substantive-complexity/intellectual-flexibility model. The study explicitly separates measurement error from true change with confirmatory factor analysis and estimates reciprocal structural paths. The forward job→person effect remains despite high stability of both constructs; the person→job effect is stronger and develops with a lag.

**Lawful normalization:**

```text
ACTUAL COMPLEX WORK PRACTICE(t0..t1)
→ repeated decisions / independent judgment
→ FLEXIBILITY(t1)

FLEXIBILITY(t0)
→ selection / performance / mobility over time
→ ACTUAL COMPLEXITY OF LATER WORK(t1)
```

**Not earned:** that the occupational title itself changes flexibility, or that flexibility alone causes attainment independently of gates and opportunity structure.

### 3.2 Kohn & Schooler 1982

The expanded model adds job structure, occupational self-direction, pressures/risks/rewards and multiple personality dimensions. The empirical logic matters architecturally: the job side is decomposed into concrete conditions; the person side is decomposed into specific outcomes; reciprocal paths are temporally modeled.

The strongest T4 architectural extraction is therefore **not** “social structure shapes personality.” It is:

```text
STRUCTURED JOB
→ actor actually occupies it
→ recurrent task + authority ecology
→ repeated exercise / suppression of thought, judgment, initiative
→ actor-specific learning/generalization if realized
→ later orientation/flexibility
```

and the return path:

```text
PRIOR ORIENTATION / FLEXIBILITY
→ action + selection + performance under gates
→ later job responsibility / latitude
→ changed future activity ecology
```

## 4. Stress-process asymmetry is a feature, not a defect

Pearlin et al. 1981 gives unusually useful evidence for **within-chain mediation** but does not give an equally strong symmetric person→structure path. T4 therefore refuses to manufacture reciprocity at every edge.

The empirically supported forward sequence is:

```text
INVOLUNTARY JOB DISRUPTION
→ actual income/material change
→ increased enduring economic strain
→ erosion of self-esteem/mastery
→ increased depressive symptoms
```

The paper reports that controlling economic strain reduces the direct job-disruption→depression path, and its models show economic strain predicting change in self-concept. Coping/support modify particular junctures rather than acting as universal direct buffers.

The authors also explicitly acknowledge that some paths treated as unidirectional are probably reciprocal and that alternative starting points (for example depression increasing later event exposure) are conceptually possible. That acknowledgment is **not** evidence of the reverse path. T4 marks it as an open reciprocal frontier.

## 5. Selection is constitutive, not merely a nuisance control

Across Kohn/Schooler, Mortimer/Lorence and Andrisani/Nestel, person characteristics predict later work position or work outcomes. This matters for Rawlsing in two ways:

1. A lawful model cannot read a person’s current psychology straight off current work structure because prior person/history partly shaped selection into that structure.
2. A lawful developmental model must permit completed development and actor action to change future structural exposure without making structural opportunity endogenous to wish or preference alone.

The feedback therefore needs at least four typed gates:

```text
PERSON/HISTORY
→ INTENTION / PREFERENCE / CAPACITY / ACTION
→ INSTITUTIONAL OR RELATIONAL GATE
→ ACTUAL POSITION / TASK / EXPOSURE CHANGE
```

A direct `PERSON → STRUCTURAL_POSITION` edge would erase employers, credentials, labor demand, discrimination, household constraint and other counterpart/institutional mechanisms.

## 6. Modern evidence: stronger selection control, narrower temporal claim

Staff & Mortimer 2024 is an important calibration source. The fixed-effects models compare people to themselves over time and therefore absorb all stable person-level differences, including stable selection factors. Within individuals, higher work self-direction and learning/skill opportunities covary with higher mastery/self-esteem, while more work stressors covary with lower mastery/self-esteem and higher depressive affect.

However:

- the main models are proximal/contemporaneous, not lagged;
- average gaps between the 11 waves are about 3.1 years;
- one-wave-lagged work dimensions did not significantly predict later mental health;
- lagged mental health mostly did not predict later employment/work quality, except prior mastery→advancement opportunities and prior self-esteem→learning opportunities in the reported supplemental analyses;
- the authors explicitly suggest long and variable intervals may weaken detection of lagged effects.

Thus this source strengthens `CHANGING_WORK_QUALITY ↔ CHANGING_MENTAL_HEALTH` against stable-person confounding, while simultaneously warning against pretending that one fixed lag captures the temporal process.

## 7. Negative reciprocal findings retained

### NR-01 — Not every job feature socializes every person dimension

Wu et al. found autonomy, but not skill utilization, predicted later internal locus of control; the proposed indirect effects through job satisfaction were weak. Retain typed work features and typed targets.

### NR-02 — Contemporary covariation is not a lagged developmental effect

Staff & Mortimer’s fixed-effects results are strong against stable selection but mostly do not survive a one-wave lag specification. Rawlsing needs explicit temporal forms rather than a generic “past exposure” operator.

### NR-03 — Not every event works through mastery/self-esteem

Pearlin et al. discuss event types whose distress consequences need not operate through diminished mastery/self-esteem. A stress-process mechanism family must allow bypasses and alternative routes.

### NR-04 — Support does not erase material strain

In the job-disruption analysis, social support protected self-concepts more clearly than it prevented economic strain or directly reduced depression. Support must be placed at the empirically relevant juncture.

### NR-05 — Coping is bounded by structure

Pearlin et al. specifically emphasize the relative ineffectiveness of individual coping against problems located in formal organization, authority and reward systems. Actor agency cannot substitute for structural change.

## 8. Temporal-form adjudication

| Temporal form | T4 evidence | Required semantic treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `EVENT` | job disruption | discrete occurrence; may initiate but is not identical to downstream strain. |
| `CONDITION` | economic strain, supervision, routinization, persistent demands | interval persistence; cannot be represented merely by an event token. |
| `RELATION` | supervisor–worker, household/care, supportive tie | counterpart-qualified and potentially persistent. |
| `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY/PRACTICE` | complex work, autonomous decision making, repetitive work, coping practices | repeated enacted pattern; key Kohn/Schooler transducer. |
| `TRANSITION` | job loss, promotion/mobility, change in work conditions | change between structural states with gate/history. |
| `TRAJECTORY` | career movement toward/away from self-directed work; long stress history | ordered history of changing conditions/positions. |
| `TURNING_POINT` | not independently demonstrated as a distinct T4 mechanism | `DEFER`; do not rename any event as turning point without trajectory-relative evidence. |
| `CUMULATIVE_PROCESS` | chronic strain, stress proliferation, repeated work practice | typed accumulation/propagation; not scalar “more exposure.” |

## 9. Reciprocal-causation terminal judgment

`RECIPROCITY_SUPPORTED__BUT_TYPED_GATED_AND_EVIDENCE_GRADED`

The corpus supports a real two-way architecture at the family level:

```text
STRUCTURE
→ ACTOR-RELATIVE EXPERIENCE / RECURRENT ACTIVITY
→ DEVELOPMENTAL CHANGE
→ ACTOR ACTION / SELECTION
→ GATED LATER POSITION / EXPOSURE
→ NEW EXPERIENCE
```

But it does **not** support collapsing that architecture into either structural determinism or personality-driven selection. Both directions require intermediate realizers, explicit time, and institutional/relational gates.
