## 2. Existing owners Mortimer tells us to reuse, not duplicate

### PRE-V3 and narrow seam owners

- PRE-V3 Functional Acceptance Constitution: structural possibility, pressure, affordance/applicability, mediation route, topology.
- OBL032 selector normalization and OBL031 comparator authority for exact actor/context-scoped evaluation where applicable.
- ADP-007 actor activation and ADP-008 active phase through OBL061 Stage A.
- OBL058 causal-use permission, OBL059 applicability, OBL056 acceptance pattern where their exact authority grain applies.
- ADP-019 occurrence, OBL060 witness independence and OBL055 event/OEE execution for narrow realized-event cases.

Mortimer does **not** justify turning every recurrent work condition into an OEE event. Long-lived job conditions and practices require D1/D2 interval/recurrence semantics rather than event inflation.

The frozen synthesis already identifies TD-R05/CAN/ADP substrate for response, needs/expectations/strivings, mediators/practices, memory/access, history and trajectory. Mortimer especially reinforces the jurisdictional firewall:


## 3. Candidate capabilities Mortimer strengthens

### D1 strengthened

Mortimer's strongest contribution is empirical pressure for a genuinely typed actor-relative ecology. The work world must resolve into simultaneous, sometimes contradictory resource/opportunity/obligation/time/stressor/support/status facts. D1 should not expose a single `job_quality` cause.

### D2 strengthened

Mortimer directly requires interval duration, weekly intensity, recurrence pattern, post-exposure persistence and transition rebinding. A total-hours scalar or snapshot employment flag cannot reconstruct the findings.

### D3 strengthened

Mortimer's selection analyses show that prior actor state affects later work selection; his development analyses show some work conditions predict later actor states; later efficacy/plans/actions participate in educational transitions. The external gate remains indispensable, so frozen D3 is the correct form.

## 4. What remains missing at the frozen synthesis grain
