## 1. Mapping principle

Luria is not authority for a one-step structural-to-psychological rule. The lawful mapping separates at least four layers:

```text
A. STRUCTURAL / INSTITUTIONAL POSSIBILITY
B. ACTOR-RELATIVE ACTIVITY / EDUCATION / COMMUNICATION ECOLOGY
C. REALIZED TASK / ENCOUNTER / PRACTICE HISTORY
D. OBSERVED RESPONSE / DOWNSTREAM DEVELOPMENTAL INTERPRETATION
```

A cannot skip B/C to mint D.

## 3. Stage B / realization requirements

Luria repeatedly shows why Stage-A qualification must not be mistaken for realization.

Examples:

```text
school exists
!= actor attends

actor attends
!= actor performs specific theoretical practice

meeting exists
!= actor speaks / receives evaluation

information channel exists
!= information transferred

farm role exists
!= planning/accounting enacted

prompt offered
!= actor uses prompt
```

Rawlsing should require actual activity/contact/transfer/performance history where the mechanism depends on it.

## 4. Testing encounter mapping

The field experiment itself is a realized social encounter with:

```text
EXPERIMENTER
ACTOR
LANGUAGE
TASK
INSTRUCTION
PROMPT / COUNTERARGUMENT / EXAMPLE HISTORY
OTHER PARTICIPANTS PRESENT

```

The study demonstrates that response can change when these conditions change. Therefore any imported evidence must preserve the encounter context rather than store a naked trait-like result.

This is compatible with the frozen constitution's distinction:

```text
ACTIVE/REALIZED SOCIAL FACT != ACTOR UPTAKE
ACTOR UPTAKE != DURABLE DEVELOPMENT
```

## 5. ADP-017 / knowledge-access relevance

Syllogism protocols strongly support keeping:

```text
OBJECTIVE OR STIPULATED PREMISE
!=
ACTOR KNOWLEDGE / WARRANT / ACCEPTANCE
```

A participant may understand a requested inference yet refuse to assert a premise he or she considers unsupported. Luria therefore reinforces the existing Rawlsing boundary that objective truth and actor knowledge/access are separate.

## 6. CAN-011 / recurrent-practice relevance

The plausible mediators in the book are not single exposures but repeated practices:

- reading/writing;
- school task routines;
- planning;
- accounting;
- collective discussion;
- evaluation;
- practical work;

This is consistent with recurrent-practice/history reception. Luria does not justify directly minting a persistent practice or capacity from group membership; actual history must be evidenced.

## 7. CAN-008 / response-boundary relevance

Observed responses under experiment are not actor-independent social facts and are not automatically durable psychology. They belong downstream of the encounter with exact response provenance. D1 must stop before it creates motive, belief, capacity, trait, or attractor.
