# SUB-VOZHD INTER-CAMPAIGN ORCHESTRATION LAW R1

Repository: `maidiu/rawlsing`

Status:

`BINDING_AFTER_VOZHD_ADOPTION`

This law governs every current and future SUB-VOZHD campaign operating under
`SUB_VOZHD_CAMPAIGN_INTEGRATION_R1`.

It supplements, and does not widen, each campaign's local charter.

Its purpose is to preserve lawful parallelism while preventing campaign-local
integration authority from becoming competing project sovereignty.

---

## 0. Core constitutional invariant

```text
VOZHD OWNS THE GLOBAL DEPENDENCY GRAPH.
SUB-VOZHD OWNS ONE CAMPAIGN-LOCAL PROJECTION.
RABOCHII OWNS ONE BOUNDED PROOF / EXECUTION TASK.
```

A SUB-VOZHD may optimize the route inside its charter.

A SUB-VOZHD may not change the project-wide destination, ownership graph,
cross-campaign authority relation, or project-current state.

---

## 1. ONE GRAIN / ONE CAMPAIGN OWNER

At any exact obligation, semantic, authority, architecture, compiler, runtime,
or proof grain, there must be exactly one current campaign owner.

A sibling SUB-VOZHD may:

- inspect that owner's immutable evidence;
- cite it;
- discover a dependency on it;
- consume it after lawful project-current or explicitly authorized bounded
  disposition.

A sibling SUB-VOZHD may not:

- commission work inside that owned grain;
- repair that owner's worker;
- redefine the grain;
- create a competing candidate;
- silently treat the owner's candidate as current authority.

If ownership is ambiguous, stop only the ambiguous lane at:

`READY_FOR_VOZHD_HQ_ADJUDICATION__OWNERSHIP`

and continue unrelated campaign work.

---

## 2. CROSS-CAMPAIGN DEPENDENCY DISCOVERY

When campaign A discovers that one exact lane requires a result owned by
campaign B:

1. A freezes only the exact dependent lane.
2. A does not commission B-domain work.
3. A records an exact cross-campaign dependency packet.
4. A continues all unrelated lawful work.
5. Central Vozhd adjudicates/routs the dependency.
6. B may then execute it if the work lies inside B's existing charter.
7. If project-current adoption is required, B returns the candidate to Vozhd.
8. A consumes only the exact HQ-adopted or explicitly HQ-authorized result.

No global sibling barrier is created merely because one lane is cross-campaign
blocked.

### Required packet identity

Preferred form:

`CROSS_CAMPAIGN_DEPENDENCY__<REQUESTING_CAMPAIGN>__REQUIRES__<OWNING_CAMPAIGN>__<EXACT_GRAIN>`

The packet must state:

- requesting campaign branch and tip;
- owning campaign if known;
- exact required proposition/object/authority/result;
- exact reason it is required;
- exact current evidence proving the dependency;
- authority ceiling;
- what is blocked;
- what remains independently runnable;
- what exact result would unblock the lane;
- whether HQ adoption is required before consumption.

Do not send narrative archaeology when immutable refs suffice.

---

## 3. NO DIRECT SIBLING MERGES

A SUB-VOZHD may not merge another SUB-VOZHD campaign branch into its own.

Forbidden:

```text
SUB-A -> SUB-B
SUB-B -> SUB-C
SUB-C -> SUB-A
```

Cross-campaign state becomes consumable only through an exact central/HQ act or
an already-current immutable authority explicitly lawful for the consumer.

Read-only inspection of sibling branches is allowed and encouraged for:

- collision avoidance;
- duplicate-proof avoidance;
- dependency discovery;
- exact evidence verification.

Read-only inspection is not semantic consumption.

---

## 4. CANDIDATE != CURRENT ACROSS CAMPAIGNS

A sibling campaign's:

- PASS;
- candidate;
- governed court;
- banked campaign result;
- campaign-local closure;

does not become current authority for another campaign merely because it exists.

Cross-campaign semantic/runtime dependence requires one of:

1. an already-current authority whose exact consumer permission covers the use;
2. an explicit Vozhd/HQ adoption;
3. an explicit Vozhd/HQ bounded consumption disposition naming the exact
   candidate, consumer, scope, and ceiling.

Ancestry alone does not satisfy this rule.

---

## 5. NO DUPLICATE PROOF OWNERSHIP

Before commissioning a worker, each SUB-VOZHD must make a bounded live-state
check sufficient to determine whether another campaign or worker already owns
the exact proof.

If a sufficient proof owner is already in flight:

`WAITING_CAN_BE_PROGRESS`

and no duplicate worker is commissioned.

A new worker is lawful only when it proves a distinct hypothesis/grain or when
the existing proof owner has reached an exact terminal state that requires a
successor.

This law applies across campaign boundaries, not only inside one campaign.

---

## 6. NO CROSS-CAMPAIGN "HELPING"

A SUB-VOZHD may not solve a sibling's blocker merely because it appears small,
obvious, or adjacent.

In particular it may not:

- create a sibling's missing authority;
- patch a sibling's runtime;
- execute a sibling's court;
- create a sibling's adoption packet as if it were owner;
- import sibling work into its own charter to maintain utilization.

If the blocker belongs to a sibling campaign, route it.

If the blocker belongs to no existing campaign, return it to Vozhd for ownership
assignment or new chartering.

`ACTIVITY != PROGRESS`

---

## 7. TRAJECTORY LAW

Central Vozhd owns:

- campaign destination;
- campaign jurisdiction;
- major dependency gates;
- cross-campaign sequencing;
- project-current adoption/closure;
- project-wide architecture/topology choices.

A SUB-VOZHD owns:

- local frontier reconstruction;
- maximum lawful fanout;
- worker ordering among locally independent tasks;
- smallest successor after a local PASS/FAIL;
- local retirement/supersession/dormancy classification when exact evidence
  supports it and the classification does not alter the campaign destination or
  another campaign's ownership.

A SUB-VOZHD may change **how** its chartered destination is reached.

A SUB-VOZHD may not change **what the destination is**.

If exact evidence shows that the chartered destination itself is obsolete,
ill-posed, cross-campaign, or requires a new authority grain, return:

`READY_FOR_VOZHD_HQ_ADJUDICATION__TRAJECTORY_AMENDMENT`

with the smallest proposed amendment.

Do not self-amend the campaign charter.

---

## 8. HQ DECISION PROPAGATION

A central/HQ act affecting a campaign must be consumed by exact immutable ref.

A SUB-VOZHD may not infer a new project-current state from:

- central branch movement alone;
- PRE-MAIN movement alone;
- descendant ancestry alone;
- a sibling's narrative;
- a worker's PASS;
- a newly visible file.

The campaign must bind the exact HQ disposition/adoption identity that authorizes
the next step.

Existing in-flight SUB-VOZHD branches need not rebase merely to consume a new
lawful HQ disposition when the disposition explicitly permits exact immutable-ref
consumption.

Rebase/merge is a topology choice, not an authority requirement.

---

## 9. CROSS-CAMPAIGN COLLISION RULE

If two SUB-VOZHDII independently produce results that appear to claim the same
semantic/authority/runtime ownership:

1. neither resolves the collision;
2. neither overwrites or supersedes the other;
3. both freeze only the colliding lanes;
4. both preserve exact evidence;
5. central Vozhd adjudicates owner, succession, coexistence, or nonoverlap.

Until then:

`PENDING != FAILED`

and neither candidate gains project-current effect.

---

## 10. SHARED DEPENDENCY / MULTIPLE CONSUMERS

One owner may produce an authority/result consumed by multiple sibling
campaigns.

That does not transfer ownership to consumers.

If A owns X and both B and C require X:

```text
        A owns X
          |
       VOZHD/HQ
        /     \
       B       C
    consume  consume
```

B and C do not independently re-prove X.

If B or C requires a narrower consumer-specific binding beyond X, the owner of
that new binding is determined by existing charter law or Vozhd if ambiguous.

---

## 11. LOCAL FAILURE VS CROSS-CAMPAIGN AUTHORITY QUESTION

A SUB-VOZHD may commission its own bounded repair after a worker FAIL only if the
repair is wholly inside already-current authority and its charter.

If the FAIL reveals:

- missing authority;
- ambiguous authority ownership;
- cross-campaign semantic dependence;
- required project-wide interpretation;
- required project-current adoption before repair;

the SUB-VOZHD must stop that lane at HQ.

It may not hide an authority decision inside an implementation repair.

---

## 12. GLOBAL PARALLELISM RULE

The purpose of this law is not to serialize SUB-VOZHDII.

Default state:

`PARALLEL_UNLESS_EXACT_DEPENDENCY_PROVED`

Therefore:

- A does not wait for B merely because both are active;
- B does not wait for C merely because their future outputs may eventually meet;
- C does not wait for A merely because central HQ is processing A;
- exact dependencies block exact lanes only;
- sibling campaign fanout continues whenever locally lawful.

Necessary discipline serializes dependent authority claims, not the whole
project.

---

## 13. REQUIRED CAMPAIGN FLIGHT STATE

Every SUB-VOZHD should maintain enough campaign-local flight state to answer:

- what exact workers are in flight;
- what exact proof each owns;
- what is waiting;
- what exact dependency it waits on;
- which dependency is local vs cross-campaign vs HQ;
- what next worker becomes lawful on each terminal trigger;
- what work remains independently runnable.

No mandatory file format is imposed if an existing campaign manifest already
provides these facts.
