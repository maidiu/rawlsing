## 2. Campaign question

Answer this, operationally rather than rhetorically:

> How do historically structured social relations become differential actor-relative conditions, activities, relations, opportunities, constraints, exposures and event ecologies, such that repeated and realized life processes can lawfully shape a generated person's development without Rawlsing asserting a direct structure→trait shortcut?

The target is a causal grammar capable of expressing at least:

```text
STRUCTURAL POSITION
→ SOCIAL MECHANISM
→ ACTOR-RELATIVE CONDITION / POSSIBILITY
→ ACTUAL ACTIVITY / RELATION / EXPOSURE / EVENT
→ SUBJECTIVE UPTAKE / RESPONSE WHERE RELEVANT
→ REPETITION / ACCUMULATION / TRANSITION / TURNING POINT
→ DURABLE DEVELOPMENT IF EARNED
↘
  RECIPROCAL ACTOR ACTION MAY ALTER POSITION / RELATION / EXPOSURE
```

A theory that only says `social structure affects personality` is insufficient.
A model that only enumerates correlations is insufficient.
A mechanism that jumps directly from social category to psychological trait is prohibited.

## 3. Theoretical orientation

This campaign is intentionally theory-heavy. Crack open primary works.

Marxist and materialist traditions are central because the problem is precisely how social relations and historically organized activity become individual development. However, no school receives oracle status. Adjacent non-Marxist traditions may be used where they provide mechanisms or empirical evidence that the Marxist literature leaves underdescribed.

Primary-source reading is preferred. Secondary work may locate, contextualize or criticize a primary source, but should not silently replace it.

`THEORIST'S CLAIM`
`!= EMPIRICALLY ESTABLISHED RESULT`

### LANE T1 — MARXIST / SOVIET ACTIVITY AND DEVELOPMENT

Core authors/works to investigate include, as relevant:

- Marx on the ensemble of social relations, labour/activity, reproduction, alienation, division of labour and formation of capacities;
- Vygotsky on social genesis, internalization/transformation, mediation, development and social situation of development;
- A. N. Leontiev, especially *Activity, Consciousness, and Personality*;
- S. L. Rubinstein;
- A. R. Luria where sociocultural reorganization of cognition is relevant;
- Davydov / activity-theory successors where they sharpen developmental mechanism;
- Lucien Sève, especially *Man in Marxist Theory* / personality as a living system of social relations between acts, use-time, capacities, needs, biography and expanded reproduction.

Question:

> How do socially organized activity and relations become capacities, motives, meanings, practices and personality over biography without direct social-category determinism?

### LANE T2 — CRITICAL PSYCHOLOGY / SUBJECT-SCIENCE

Core:

- Klaus Holzkamp;
- Ute Osterkamp;
- Ole Dreier and relevant successors/comparators.

Question:

> How do objective societal possibilities/constraints become subject-relative premises and grounds for action while preserving agency, standpoint, collective action and the possibility of transforming one's conditions?

`GENERALIZED SOCIETAL ACTION POSSIBILITY != ACTUAL SUBJECT ACTION`

## 5. Shared extraction grammar

Every theory lane must produce **mechanism cards**, not book reports.

For every retained mechanism record:

1. **Source identity** — exact author/work/chapter/page or stable section where available.
2. **Claim status** — theory / qualitative / observational / longitudinal / experimental / review / contested.
3. **Structural input** — objective relation, position, institution, condition, activity organization or historical circumstance.
4. **Mediating mechanism** — the actual process proposed.
5. **Actor-relative consequence** — resource, dependency, obligation, opportunity, constraint, counterpart set, activity form, sanction, exposure, information, practice, etc.
6. **Realization law** — what must actually occur before biography changes.
7. **Subject mediation** — agency, interpretation, motive, premise, choice, resistance, collective action, adaptation, etc.
8. **Developmental target** — capacity, expectation, striving, mediator, tension, practice, memory, relationship, self-model, affective tendency, epistemic state, attractor movement, etc.
9. **Temporal form** — event / chronic condition / recurrent practice/activity / transition / trajectory / turning point / cumulative process.
10. **Relational scale** — dyad / household / network / organization / institution / class relation / community / state / historical conjuncture.
11. **Reciprocity** — whether person/action can alter later structure, position, relation or exposure.
12. **Moderators / boundary conditions** — age, developmental timing, duration, sequence, role, support, network, institutional context, historical context, etc.
13. **Negative ceiling** — what the theory does **not** license Rawlsing to infer.
14. **Rawlsing mapping** — exact existing PRE-V3 owner(s), V3 owner(s), and any missing connective function.
15. **Disposition candidate** — `REUSE`, `GENERALIZE`, `MISSING`, `REJECT`, or `DEFER`.

Required firewall for every card:

```text
STRUCTURAL CATEGORY != PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAIT
STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY != REALIZED EXPERIENCE
REALIZED EXPERIENCE != ACTOR UPTAKE
ACTOR UPTAKE != DURABLE CHANGE
DURABLE CHANGE != DIRECT DEMOGRAPHIC PRIOR
```

## 7. Temporal ontology court

This is mandatory.

Rawlsing must not force every social cause into a discrete event.

The campaign must test at least:

```text
EVENT
CONDITION
RELATION
RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_OR_PRACTICE
TRANSITION
TRAJECTORY
TURNING_POINT
CUMULATIVE_PROCESS
```

Examples of the distinction:

- being fired is an event;
- low control over work may be a chronic condition;
- supervising/subordination is a relation;
- repeatedly solving complex problems with autonomy is recurrent activity;
- entering parenthood or leaving school may be a transition;
- progressive occupational advancement is a trajectory;
- a specific rupture may function as a turning point;
- years of accumulating insecurity may be a cumulative process.

Determine what existing V3 can already causally metabolize, what can be represented as conditions/bindings on event processing, what requires repeated activity state, and whether any genuinely new temporal carrier is required.

Do not invent one unless exact loss is proved.

- supply a currently missing mechanism;
- materially challenge an adopted mechanism;

- expose an omitted temporal/relational scale.
