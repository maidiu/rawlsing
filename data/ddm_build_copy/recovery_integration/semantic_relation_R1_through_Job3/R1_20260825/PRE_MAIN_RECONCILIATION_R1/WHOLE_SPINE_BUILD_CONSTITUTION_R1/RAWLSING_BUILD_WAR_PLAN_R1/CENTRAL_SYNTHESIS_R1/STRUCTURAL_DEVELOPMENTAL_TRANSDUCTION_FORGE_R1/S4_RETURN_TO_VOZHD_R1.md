### B. Which rule shapes are strongly supported?

Strongest support occurs in two distinct senses.

**Bounded causal-identification strength:** P4 gate/treatment transformations where an actual presented cue/status signal was manipulated or tightly matched and a gate decision was measured. The source still supports only its exact cue/gate/outcome scope.

**Constitutive/deontic strength:** P1/P2/P4 rules where a competent current legal/institutional/organizational rule itself establishes an objective entitlement, eligibility, obligation, authority scope or restriction for an exactly positioned actor. Realized receipt/treatment/performance still requires a separate occurrence witness where contingent.

P3 actual task/time/supervision conditions are strongly empirically motivated and measurable, but downstream person-effect coefficients remain observational rather than production-calibrated.
