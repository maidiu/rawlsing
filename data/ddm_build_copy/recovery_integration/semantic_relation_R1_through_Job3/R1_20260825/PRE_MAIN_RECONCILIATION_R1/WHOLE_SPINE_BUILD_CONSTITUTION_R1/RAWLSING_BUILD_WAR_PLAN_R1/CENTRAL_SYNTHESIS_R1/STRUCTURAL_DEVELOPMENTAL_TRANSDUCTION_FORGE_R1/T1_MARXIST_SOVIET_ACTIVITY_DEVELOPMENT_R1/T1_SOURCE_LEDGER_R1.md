## Scope and independence

This ledger answers only the T1 question: how socially organized relations and activity can become capacities, motives, meanings, practices, and personality across biography without converting structural categories into psychological traits. The worker inspected only the campaign commission, the Phase-0 PRE-V3/V3 seam map, and T1 source material. No T2-T6 findings were inspected or harmonized.

Source classes used below are deliberately explicit: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL`, `PRIMARY_EMPIRICAL_OR_EXPERIMENTAL`, `PRIMARY_MIXED_THEORY_FIELD_EVIDENCE`, `SCHOLARLY_SECONDARY`, and `CONTESTED_OR_LIMITED_EVIDENCE`. Theoretical propositions are not promoted to empirical laws merely because they are canonical.

## Primary ledger

### P01 — Karl Marx, *Theses on Feuerbach* (1845)

- Exact location retained: Theses I, III, VI-VIII, XI.
- Access: Marx/Engels Internet Archive, 1969 Selected Works translation: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/theses/theses.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL`.
- T1 yield: practice is active/sensuous rather than passive reception; circumstances and human activity are reciprocal; the human essence is not an abstraction inside isolated individuals but an ensemble of social relations; social life is practical.
- Ceiling: does not itself specify a micro-developmental mechanism, dose, timescale, or person-specific realization law. Thesis VI forbids the move `CLASS/POSITION -> TRAIT` unless mediating relations and activity are supplied.

### P02 — Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, *The German Ideology*, Part I (1845-46)

- Exact locations retained: Part I.A, "History: Fundamental Conditions" and "Private Property and Communism"; Part I.C, "Division of Labour: Town and Country," "The Rise of Manufacturing," and discussion of individuals reproducing relations.
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/german-ideology/ch01.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/german-ideology/ch01a.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/german-ideology/ch01c.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HISTORICAL`.
- T1 yield: division of labour/property organize which activities people repeatedly perform, with whom, under what dependencies, and with which products; people reproduce relations through their life activity while also being capable of changing them.
- Ceiling: historically polemical and macrosocial; no direct individual psychological effect size and no demographic prior follows.

### P03 — Karl Marx, *Capital*, Vol. I, Chapter 15, "Machinery and Modern Industry" (1867)

- Exact locations retained: §1 development of machinery; §3 proximate effects on the worker; §8 changes in manufacture/domestic industry.
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch15.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HISTORICAL_WITH_DOCUMENTARY_EXAMPLES`.
- T1 yield: production organization can relocate, narrow, broaden, devalue, or routinize skills by changing tool-control, task allocation, training, cooperation, and dependency. This is a concrete structural-to-activity bridge, not a claim that occupation names encode personality.
- Ceiling: nineteenth-century factory analysis; psychological development is inferred through task structure and training, not measured with modern longitudinal methods.

### P04 — Lev S. Vygotsky, "The Instrumental Method in Psychology" (1930)

- Exact locations retained: propositions 3-5, 9-18, 20-24.
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/vygotsky/works/1930/instrumental.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_WITH_EXPERIMENTAL_PROGRAM`.
- T1 yield: socially produced signs/tools can become a middle term in action; use of the tool reorganizes the functional structure and course of action; education can deliberately reorganize developing functions.
- Ceiling: the mechanism is sign/tool use in activity, not "culture" or "internalization" as an unexplained causal word.

### P05 — Lev S. Vygotsky, "Genesis of Higher Mental Functions" (1931)

- Exact locations retained: sign first as social connection; relations among people as genetic precursors of higher functions; general genetic law; external-to-internal transition changes structure and function.
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/vygotsky/works/1931/higher-mental-functions.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_WITH_DEVELOPMENTAL_EXPERIMENTAL_BACKGROUND`.
- T1 yield: a distributed social relation can become self-regulation only through mediated transformation; the outcome is not a copy of the relation.
- Ceiling: not every social relation becomes a durable function; repeated participation, tool/sign mediation, actor use, and developmental conditions must be specified.

### P06 — Lev S. Vygotsky, "The Problem of Teaching and Mental Development at School Age" (1931; Mitchell translation 2017)

- Exact location retained: argument on learning/development and the zone of proximate development, especially guided/imitation-supported performance becoming independent performance.
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/vygotsky/works/1931/school-age.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_DEVELOPMENTAL`.
- T1 yield: organized instruction and collaboration can create a transition from supported action to independently available capacity.
- Ceiling: successful assistance is conditional on what is developmentally reachable; help is not equivalent to durable mastery.

### P07 — A. N. Leontiev, *Activity, Consciousness, and Personality* (1978)

- Exact locations retained: Ch. 3 §§3.2-3.5; Ch. 4 §§4.3-4.4; Ch. 5 §§5.3-5.5.
- Access/index: https://www.marxists.org/archive/leontev/works/1978/index.htm ; whole-book PDF: https://www.marxists.org/archive/leontev/works/1978/activity-consciousness-personality.pdf
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_WITH_EXPERIMENTAL_TRADITION`.
- T1 yield: social conditions do not directly become psyche; activity mediates. Need becomes motive through encounter with an object; activity is organized by motive, actions by conscious goals, operations by conditions. Actions can become operations through repeated/restructured performance; meanings are appropriated through activity and communication but acquire actor-specific personal sense through their relation to motives. Personality is formed through the hierarchy and transformation of activities/motives realized in social relations.
- Ceiling: Leontiev explicitly rejects simple direct transformation of social conditions into psychological contents. Activity labels alone are insufficient; one must recover object/motive, goal, conditions, operations, and the actor's actual relations.

### P08 — S. L. Rubinstein, *Foundations of General Psychology* and *Being and Consciousness*

- Exact locations retained: *Foundations of General Psychology*, Part IV, Ch. XIII "Action"; *Being and Consciousness* (1957), Ch. IV on psychic activity and personality, plus the determinism formulation discussed in the work.
- Primary-text access (Russian reproductions): https://www.gumer.info/bibliotek_Buks/Psihol/rubin/13.php ; https://www.universalinternetlibrary.ru/book/46470/ogl.shtml
- Institutional bibliographic check: Institute of Psychology, Russian Academy of Sciences, "The scientific school of S. L. Rubinstein": https://www.ipras.ru/cntnt/eng/informatio/the_scient.html
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL`; web reproduction quality requires normal textual caution.
- T1 yield: consciousness and activity are a unity without being identical; activity both expresses and forms psychological organization. Rubinstein's determinism stresses that external causes operate through internal conditions, preserving subject mediation.
- Ceiling: "internal conditions" must not become a replacement black box. For Rawlsing it is a boundary law requiring the current organization/history of the actor, not a complete transduction mechanism.

### P09 — A. R. Luria, *Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations*, Ch. 1 "The Problem" (research 1931-32; English 1976)

- Exact locations retained: "Initial Assumptions," "The Research Situation," subject groups, "Procedures," and "Research Plan."
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/luria/works/1976/problem.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_MIXED_THEORY_FIELD_EVIDENCE__CONTESTED_CAUSAL_IDENTIFICATION`.
- T1 yield: groups differed not only in category labels but in practical activity, participation in collective planning, communication, schooling/literacy, and access to technical knowledge; the program tested whether these organized practices covaried with forms of classification/reasoning. Field procedures were qualitative/clinical and included learning assistance.
- Ceiling: groups were not randomized and differed on many dimensions simultaneously. The study cannot identify "socialism," class, ethnicity, religion, literacy, or collectivization as a single causal treatment. Its colonial/evolutionary terminology is historically problematic. Retain only the narrower evidence that differences in organized practice/communication/education were associated with different cognitive performances under the study conditions.

### P10 — V. V. Davydov, *Types of Generalization in Instruction* (English edition 1990)

- Exact locations retained: printed pp. 151 and 161 (PDF pages 151 and 161 in the inspected copy), discussion of activity underlying concept formation and instructional "quasi-investigation."
- Access: https://www.marxists.org/archive/davydov/generalization/generalization.pdf
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_PEDAGOGICAL_WITH_RESEARCH_PROGRAM`.
- T1 yield: concept mastery is not receipt of a ready-made definition; instruction can organize object-related activity that reproduces, in compressed form, the relations/operations from which the concept is generated.
- Ceiling: the text develops a pedagogical theory and research program; it does not license arbitrary extension from school concept learning to every adult personality process.

### P11 — Lucien Sève, *Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality* / online *Man in Marxist Theory* (English text)

- Exact locations retained: "Natural Relations and Social Relations between Acts"; "The Personality as a Living System of Social Relations between Acts"; "Basic concepts: acts, capacities, and the problem of needs"; "Infrastructures and superstructures. Use-time"; "Laws of development and problems of expanded reproduction. Biography."
- Access/index: https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/index.htm
- Exact sections: https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch3/3.1.1.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch3/3.1.2.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.1.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.2.htm ; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.3.htm
- Claim status: `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS`.
- T1 yield: an act is simultaneously a moment of a singular biography and of an objective social world; capacities are actual potentialities for acts, while many capacities are themselves produced/developed by acts. Sève distinguishes Sector I activity that develops capacities from Sector II activity that mainly uses existing capacities. "Use-time" is proposed as the actual temporal relation among objective activity categories, with finite time allocation constraining expanded reproduction of capacities. Biography is therefore not a trait list but a changing system of acts, capacities, products/needs, and time allocation under social constraints.
- Ceiling: Sève repeatedly labels the topology schematic/hypothetical, rejects reducing singular biographies to a stencil or typology, recognizes contingency, and notes unresolved methodological problems. His abstract/concrete activity dichotomy was later qualified. Retain the general acts-capacities/use-time logic; do not import a class-personality typology.

## Evidence hierarchy used in retained corpus

1. `HIGH_THEORETICAL_PROVENANCE`: exact primary text and mechanism explicitly stated (Marx, Vygotsky, Leontiev, Sève, Rubinstein, Davydov).
2. `MODERATE_DEVELOPMENTAL_MECHANISM_SUPPORT`: primary theoretical propositions embedded in experimental/developmental programs (Vygotsky/Leontiev/Davydov).
3. `LOW_TO_MODERATE_CAUSAL_EMPIRICAL_SUPPORT`: Luria field comparisons, because treatment bundles and selection prevent clean causal identification.
4. `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: direct category-to-trait effects; invariant class personality; demographic priors; universal dose-response; a single generic "internalization" operator.

## Ledger conclusion

The strongest T1 material does **not** support `STRUCTURAL CATEGORY -> PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAIT`. It supports a mediated chain of the form:

`SOCIAL RELATION / MATERIAL-ORGANIZATIONAL CONDITION -> OBJECTIVE ACTIVITY ECOLOGY -> REPEATED/SUPPORTED ACTS USING TOOLS, SIGNS, GOALS AND OPERATIONS -> ACTOR-SPECIFIC SENSE/MOTIVE/CAPACITY CHANGE -> CHANGED FUTURE ACTIVITY AND BIOGRAPHICAL POSSIBILITIES`.

The sources also support reciprocity: actors reproduce or alter circumstances through activity, and prior capacities/motives alter which activities are selected, sustained, or transformed. None of that licenses demographic trait priors.
