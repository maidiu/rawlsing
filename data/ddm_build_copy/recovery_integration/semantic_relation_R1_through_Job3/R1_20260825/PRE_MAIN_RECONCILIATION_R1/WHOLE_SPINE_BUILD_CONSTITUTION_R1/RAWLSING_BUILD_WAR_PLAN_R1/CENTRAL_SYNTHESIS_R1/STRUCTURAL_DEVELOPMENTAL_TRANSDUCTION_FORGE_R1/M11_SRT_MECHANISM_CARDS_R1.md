# M11 SRT MECHANISM CARDS R1

STATUS: `26_SOURCE_NORMALIZED_MECHANISM_CARDS__NO_THEORY_NAME_OBJECTS`

These cards are deliberately mechanism-grained. Each requires actor, world/context, valid time, exact position/relation, counterpart or institution where applicable, realization status, and provenance.

## M01 — PROPERTY_DISPOSSESSION_TO_SUBSISTENCE_DEPENDENCY

- **Structural/input facts:** property/land/means-of-production loss; absence of independent subsistence.
- **Mechanism:** actor lacks direct means of subsistence and must traverse wage/credit/market relations.
- **Actor-relative output:** dependency, exit constraint, labor-market necessity.
- **Temporal form:** persistent relation + transition.
- **Source basis:** Ch3 `L611-L626`; Ch4 `L999-L1004`; Ch9 `L1937-L1955`.
- **Rawlsing mapping:** D1 resource/dependency.
- **Negative ceiling:** dispossession does not determine motive, compliance, identity or trait.

## M02 — WAGE_INCOME_TO_PROVISIONING_CAPACITY

- **Structural/input facts:** realized wage/income plus prices and household claims.
- **Mechanism:** income permits/limits purchase of food, housing, care, transport, education.
- **Actor-relative output:** actor-specific resource/security and provisioning options.
- **Temporal form:** condition + recurrence.
- **Source basis:** Ch1 `L230-L248`; Ch3 `L598-L606`; Ch4 `L1009-L1066`.
- **Rawlsing mapping:** D1 resource; D2 interval.
- **Negative ceiling:** wage level is not well-being or development.
