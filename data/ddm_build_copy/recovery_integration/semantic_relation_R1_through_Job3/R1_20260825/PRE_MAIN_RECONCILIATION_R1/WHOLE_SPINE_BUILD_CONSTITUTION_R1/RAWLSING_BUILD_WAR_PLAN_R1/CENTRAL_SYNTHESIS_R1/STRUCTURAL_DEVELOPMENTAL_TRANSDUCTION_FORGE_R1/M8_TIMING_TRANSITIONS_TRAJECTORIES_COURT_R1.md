## High-value nulls and anti-shortcut evidence

1. **Economic hardship does not mechanically produce a single parent response.** A sizable number of deprived families remained father-dominant and emotionally stable (p. 106).
2. **Class origin does not explain children's role allocation by itself.** Once actual household needs and deprivation are considered, children's paid/domestic work is organized through concrete family requirements (pp. 65–70).
3. **Girls' social independence is not generally explained by deprivation.** Most effects are small or context-specific; working-class domestic burden is the clearest restricted-leisure route (pp. 77–80).
4. **Employment is not a pure treatment.** Elder notes selection into jobs by industry/mastery and cannot fully identify the direction for several work-linked outcomes (pp. 70, 75).
5. **Peer popularity is not broadly damaged by hardship/child work.** Staff observations do not support severe generalized social limitation (p. 78).
6. **Adult effects are not uniform persistence.** Later status and adult situations condition continuity; some deprived and nondeprived groups converge or reverse (chaps. 7–10).
7. **Father drinking is reciprocal with unemployment, not a one-way stress response.** The source explicitly says both cause and consequence (p. 61).
8. **Family adaptations can be adaptive for household survival and costly for individual members at the same time** (pp. 12–15).

## Source-level conclusion

Elder is unusually strong authority for the SDTF seam because the book's own analytic discipline is the same discipline Rawlsing needs: macro conditions acquire developmental force only after actor-specific exposure is carried through concrete resources, roles, relations, activities, transitions and histories. Its strongest evidence is not "Depression effects." It is the demonstration that the same macro event decomposes into heterogeneous household losses, household adaptations, linked-person role changes, child activities, and historically timed trajectories.

The source therefore supports explicit transduction and strongly rejects a primitive `MACRO_EVENT -> PERSON_STATE` rule.
