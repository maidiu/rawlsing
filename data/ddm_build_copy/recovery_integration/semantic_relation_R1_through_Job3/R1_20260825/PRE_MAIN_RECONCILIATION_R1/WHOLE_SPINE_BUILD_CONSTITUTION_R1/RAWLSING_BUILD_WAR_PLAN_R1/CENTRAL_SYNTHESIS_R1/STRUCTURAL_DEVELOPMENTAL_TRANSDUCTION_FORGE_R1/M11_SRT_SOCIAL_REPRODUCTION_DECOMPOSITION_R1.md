## 4. What Rawlsing should represent directly

Rawlsing should directly represent the **typed consequences and transfers** needed to distinguish actors: resource access/transfer, dependency/exit constraints, directed care/obligation, authority/sanction, time schedules, recurrent activities, eligibility/gate status, realized treatment, migration/legal status consequences, counterpart/support/information channels, and typed transitions. It should also preserve explicit collective action and external response when a later structural fact changes.

Rawlsing should **not** directly represent “social reproduction,” “care crisis,” “oppression,” “class formation,” “totality,” “heteronormativity,” or “financialization” as causal operators. Those labels may index source arguments or structural contexts, but execution must pass through the concrete mechanisms above.

## 5. Which are combinations of existing relations

Most of the 26 are not new ontology families. They are compositions of the frozen candidate's already normalized families: `RESOURCE_DEPENDENCY`, `AUTHORITY_CONTROL_SANCTION`, `ACTIVITY_ORGANIZATION`, `TIME_ALLOCATION`, `INSTITUTIONAL_GATE`, `ROLE_OBLIGATION`, `HOUSEHOLD_CARE_DEPENDENCY`, `COUNTERPART_FORMATION`, `NETWORK_INFORMATION_SUPPORT`, `CLASSIFICATION_TO_REALIZED_TREATMENT`, `PERSISTENT_STRAIN_CONDITION`, `CROSS_DOMAIN_PROPAGATION`, `TRANSITION_REBINDING`, `LINKED_LIFE_TRANSMISSION`, `CUMULATIVE_ITERATIVE_FEEDBACK`, and `COLLECTIVE_STRUCTURAL_FEEDBACK`.

The SRT volume therefore **deepens rule exemplars and evidence provenance** rather than forcing a new module.

## 6. Temporal reproduction

What actually recurs is concrete: meals, cleaning, caregiving, paid shifts, overtime, commuting, school attendance, pension payments, support exchanges, remittances, benefit eligibility intervals, supervision, gate decisions, legal status, and policy regimes. The word “reproduction” often obscures whether the temporal form is recurrence, persistence, generational replacement, transition, or institutional continuity. D2 must preserve those distinctions.
