# T1 NEGATIVE CEILINGS AND OPEN QUESTIONS R1

STATUS: `COMPLETE__ANTI-DETERMINISM_AND_EVIDENCE_CEILINGS_FROZEN`

## A. Hard negative ceilings

### NC-01 — No structural-category personality shortcut

`class`, `occupation`, `family form`, `institution`, `education`, `culture`, `region`, `gender`, or any other structural/demographic category is not a psychological trait and cannot be emitted as a trait prior. T1 sources become most informative precisely when they specify the intervening activity relations.

### NC-02 — Possibility is not realized activity

A position may make an activity available, obligatory, likely, or costly without proving that the actor actually performed it. PRE-V3 structural possibility must remain distinct from OEE/realized history.

### NC-03 — Realized activity is not uptake

Participation in instruction, labour, family practice, political activity or communication does not by itself establish actor uptake, learning, motive change, personal sense or identity change.

### NC-04 — Uptake is not durability

Supported performance, a successful act, or momentary personal sense does not prove durable capacity/personality change. Persistence must be evidenced through later independent performance, traces, recurrence, changed selection, or other V3-owned carriers.

### NC-05 — "Internalization" is not an acceptable causal black box

Vygotsky's own formulation says the transition transforms structure/function. A lawful implementation must name the interpersonal function, sign/tool, repeated action/use, transition and resulting independent functional organization.

### NC-06 — "Socialization" and "culture" are not mechanisms

Leontiev and Luria both insist on concrete practices, communication, tools, schooling, work organization and social relations. `CULTURE -> MIND` is too coarse for this lane.

### NC-07 — Repetition has no universal positive sign

Repeated action may form fluent operation, but may also stabilize narrow/routine skill, become obsolete under changed conditions, impose fatigue, or crowd out other capacity-forming activity. Recurrence needs task/feedback/condition semantics.

### NC-08 — Conscious appraisal is not required on each skilled iteration

Leontiev supplies a counterexample to any architecture that requires explicit conscious evaluation for every developmental effect: an action may become an operation and be executed conditionally/automatically. This ceiling applies to repeated **performed activity**, not generic unnoticed environmental exposure.

### NC-09 — Objective meaning is not personal sense

A public meaning/norm can be available without being understood, endorsed, motivating, or durable for an actor. Personal sense must remain actor- and motive-relative.

### NC-10 — Activity membership is not activity structure

Saying that someone "works," "studies," "parents," "belongs to a class," or "participates" is insufficient. Recover object/motive, goals, operations, tools, counterpart roles, discretion, products, schedule and recurrence where causally relevant.

### NC-11 — Sève's topology is not a validated typology

Sector I/II and use-time are high-value analytic hypotheses. Sève explicitly calls the topology schematic/hypothetical and rejects a personality stencil. Do not map his sample child/student/worker/retiree diagrams into demographic priors.

### NC-12 — Abstract/concrete labour is not a ready-made psychological axis

Sève's early abstract/concrete activity distinction was later qualified; even in the primary text the reusable mechanism is mediation by social relations and time/activity composition, not a universal one-dimensional personality score.

### NC-13 — Luria does not identify a single treatment effect

The Uzbekistan/Kirghizia groups differed simultaneously in literacy, schooling, collective-work roles, communication, technology access, geography, gendered seclusion and political-economic transition. The field study cannot establish a direct effect of "socialism," collectivization, religion, ethnicity or class on cognition.

### NC-14 — Historical terminology is not present-day measurement validity

Luria's period terminology and some developmental/evolutionary framings are historically situated and sometimes objectionable. They cannot be treated as modern validated constructs.

### NC-15 — Rubinstein's internal conditions cannot become latent magic

"External causes act through internal conditions" is useful only as a realization constraint. Unless the relevant capacity/motive/history is named, `internal_condition` would merely hide unexplained variance.

### NC-16 — No theory-name ontology

Nothing in T1 licenses entities called `VYGOTSKIAN_INTERNALIZATION`, `LEONTIEVIAN_ACTIVITY`, `SEVE_USE_TIME_PERSONALITY`, or similar theory labels. Normalize mechanisms into domain semantics.

## C. Smallest unresolved questions for Vozhd synthesis

1. **Carrier sufficiency:** can existing PRE-V3 structural affordance/pressure + mediation-route + interaction topology + OEE already encode a recurring `ACTIVITY_ECOLOGY` with task/tool/counterpart/time structure, or is one small connective carrier required?
2. **Recurrence representation:** where is recurrence/duration represented before V3 learning without turning every condition into an event stream?
3. **Activity identity:** what is the minimum canonical description needed to distinguish two activities with identical surface behavior but different objective social relation, object/motive, counterpart or product?
4. **Time control:** can existing condition/constraint semantics express finite schedule competition and crowd-out, or is a compositional budget relation missing?
5. **Guided-action transition:** does current OEE + V3 learning distinguish `supported performance` from `independent capacity`, including the transition criterion?
6. **Feedback direction:** what existing owner represents person/action feedback that changes future local opportunity/role allocation without prematurely redesigning macro structure?
7. **Selection/endogeneity:** how will the system preserve that prior capacities/motives affect entry, persistence and exit from activities, avoiding attribution of selected histories wholly to structure?
8. **Empirical calibration:** T1 largely supplies mechanism theory; which later empirical lanes can calibrate probability, heterogeneity, dose, timing and boundary conditions without changing the mechanism's semantic identity?

## E. Open-source/provenance status

No exact source blocker remains for the T1 core. The full English Sève sections needed for acts, capacities, use-time and biography were located and inspected directly. Rubinstein is supported through Russian primary-text reproductions plus an institutional bibliographic check; because web reproduction provenance is weaker than a critical edition, only his high-level subject-mediation boundary law is retained. Luria is explicitly evidence-bounded. Davydov's inspected PDF pages support the pedagogical activity mechanism. These limitations are compatible with a normalization-ready mechanism corpus because they are carried as ceilings rather than silently upgraded.
