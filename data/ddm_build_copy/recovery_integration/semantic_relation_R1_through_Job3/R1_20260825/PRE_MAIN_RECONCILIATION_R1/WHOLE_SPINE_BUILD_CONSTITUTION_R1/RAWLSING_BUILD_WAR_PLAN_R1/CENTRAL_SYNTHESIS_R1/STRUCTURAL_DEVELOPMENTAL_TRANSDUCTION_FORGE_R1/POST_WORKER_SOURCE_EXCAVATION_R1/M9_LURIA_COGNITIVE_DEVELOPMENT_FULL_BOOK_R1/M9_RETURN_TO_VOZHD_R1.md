## Executive verdict

Luria gives unusually rich evidence that **performance in supposedly abstract cognitive tasks depends on concrete histories of activity, instruction, communication, information access, and on the task encounter itself**. He does **not** identify a causal effect of "culture," socialism, collectivization, literacy, schooling, or collective work considered separately.

The source therefore strengthens D1 chiefly as a **firewall against bundled group-to-psychology inference**. It gives only requirement-level support to D2 (persistent/recurrent activity matters conceptually; within-person temporal change is not measured) and no meaningful positive qualification for D3. It does not justify a D4.

## A. What is directly established?

1. Sharp between-group differences in color/shape naming and grouping, object classification, syllogistic response, school-like problem solving, question production, and self-description.
2. Many remote/unschooled participants display strong practical reasoning while rejecting the experimenter's abstract or hypothetical convention.
3. Refusal of unfamiliar syllogistic conclusions is often explicitly grounded in lack of firsthand knowledge, not an inability to manipulate the premises.
4. Prompting, conditional reframing, examples, increased specificity, and imaginary scenarios can change current performance for some participants.
5. Participants with schooling and/or collective administrative experience much more consistently adopt the experimenter's taxonomic, stipulated-premise, and decontextualized discourse conventions.
6. Practical expertise generates its own rich categorizations; it is not cognitive emptiness.

## B. What is only Luria's interpretation?

Notably:

- that socialist transformation caused radical restructuring of cognitive systems;
- that collectivized labor caused new motives and higher forms of thought;
- that limited schooling/collective work was sufficient for fundamental mental transformation;
- that perception itself was globally reorganized rather than category deployment/task application changing;
- that collective evaluation created a new inner psychological world;
- that Islamic influence inhibited independent cognition;
- that the compared groups instantiate historical developmental stages of consciousness.

These claims may motivate hypotheses; the design does not identify them.

## D. What can D1 learn from the study?

A great deal. D1 is necessary precisely because Luria's own broad group labels are causally underdetermined.

D1 must not accept:

```text
PEASANT / ILLITERATE / MUSLIM / KOLKHOZ / WOMAN / "TRADITIONAL"
-> developmental premise
```

It should require exact activity/education/communication/information/role bindings with time, context, counterpart/institution, and provenance. The concrete route must survive into the derived ecology fact.

D1 should also preserve a negative ceiling that an observed task response under a prompt history is not a context-free capacity.

## E. Is there meaningful D2 evidence?

`YES, BUT ONLY AT REPRESENTATION-REQUIREMENT GRAIN.`

The hypothesized mechanisms are recurrent and persistent: schooling, literacy practice, planning, accounting, meetings, evaluation, work, communication. Event-spam would misrepresent them.

But there is no same-person pre/post study. Luria cannot establish transition timing, recurrence dose, trajectory redirection, or durable change. D2 must represent independently established intervals/recurrences; it cannot use this source to infer development.

## F. Is there meaningful D3 evidence?

`NO.`

The social revolution is background context. The study does not recover the D3 transaction chain from actor action through external institutional response to a provenance-bearing structural update and later actor ecology. Do not cite Luria as qualifying D3.

## G. Any D4?

`NO NEW D4.`

There is a real guidance/scaffolding phenomenon: prompts and reframing sometimes alter current performance. That should be represented as encounter/task/prompt history and consumed by competent downstream response/learning machinery. A new SDTF developmental operator would trespass into psychology.
