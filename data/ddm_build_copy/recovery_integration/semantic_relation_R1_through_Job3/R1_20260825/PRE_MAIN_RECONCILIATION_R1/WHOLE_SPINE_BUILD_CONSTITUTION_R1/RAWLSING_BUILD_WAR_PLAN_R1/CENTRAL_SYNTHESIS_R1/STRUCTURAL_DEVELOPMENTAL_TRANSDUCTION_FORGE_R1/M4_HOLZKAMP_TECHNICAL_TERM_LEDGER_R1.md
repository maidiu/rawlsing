## 1. Critical semantic separations

### `Bedeutung` is not subjective meaning

Holzkamp’s `Bedeutungen` at the relevant mediation level are objective societal action/thought possibilities. The p. 359 footnote expressly postpones their subjective realization. Therefore:

```text
BEDEUTUNG
!= SUBJECTIVE INTERPRETATION
!= PREMISE
!= BELIEF
```

### `Prämisse` is not a PRE-V3 prerequisite

A `Prämisse` belongs to a subjectively functional reason nexus. At pp. 352–353 external and personal conditions are premises only in the way they are given/experienced in the subject’s situation — their `phänomenaler Aspekt`.

Rawlsing translation discipline:

```text
PRE-V3 PREREQUISITE / APPLICABILITY
!= HOLZKAMPIAN PRÄMISSE
```

### `Handlungsfähigkeit` is not a generic agency variable

`Handlungsfähigkeit` is defined relationally through participation in practical disposal over socially produced conditions of life. It is not a decontextualized quantity “inside” the person.

### Restrictive/generalized forms are not person classes

Holzkamp explicitly says the alternative characterizes situations and the direction of action, not people or personality constants. It arises anew under concrete conditions.

### `Begründetheit` is not causal necessity

A grounded/reasoned action is intelligible relative to subjectively experienced interests and premises. It can be objectively mistaken, fail, or have effects opposite to what the actor sought.
