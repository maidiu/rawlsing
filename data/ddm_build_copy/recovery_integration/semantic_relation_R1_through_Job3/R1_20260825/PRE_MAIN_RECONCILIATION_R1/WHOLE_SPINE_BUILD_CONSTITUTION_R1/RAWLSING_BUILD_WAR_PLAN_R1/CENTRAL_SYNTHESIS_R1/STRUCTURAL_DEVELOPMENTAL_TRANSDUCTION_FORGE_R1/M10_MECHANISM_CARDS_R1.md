# M10 — WRIGHT MECHANISM CARDS R1

STATUS: `MECHANISM_EXTRACTION_COMPLETE`

Every card obeys:

```text
STRUCTURAL RELATION
-> ACTOR-RELATIVE QUALIFICATION
-> REALIZATION
-> DOWNSTREAM PERSON PROCESS ONLY IF EARNED
```

## M10-M01 — PROPERTY SEPARATION / WAGE DEPENDENCY

**Inputs:** effective control/ownership of means of production; actor labour-market position; available independent productive assets; contract/employer binding.

**Derive:** insufficient independent productive assets + active wage position -> dependence on labour-market sale for income/subsistence; employer relation; bounded exit set.

**Realize:** actual employment, wage receipt, unemployment, quit/dismissal, or alternative-resource activation.

**Outputs:** `ACTIVE_DEPENDENCY_RELATION`, income channel, exit constraint, employer counterpart, job-loss risk.

**Negative ceiling:** no personality, compliance, resentment, consciousness or fatalism follows.

**SDTF:** strengthens `RESOURCE_DEPENDENCY` under D1.

## M10-M02 — CAPITAL CONTROL / APPROPRIATION

**Inputs:** productive asset control, worker counterpart, production relation, claims on product/surplus.

**Derive:** control enabling use of others' labour + claim over resulting surplus -> exploitation-qualified relation.

**Realize:** actual productive activity and appropriation/claim execution.

**Outputs:** resource accumulation channel; worker/employer interdependency; investment/hiring/disinvestment powers.

**Ceiling:** income inequality alone is insufficient.

**SDTF:** D1; possible D3 only when exercised powers change later relations.

## M10-M03 — EXPLOITATION AS INTERDEPENDENCY, NOT SCORE

**Inputs:** actor A/B; asset relation; oppression/constraint; appropriation; productive contribution.

**Rule:** `EXPLOITATION` requires both economic disadvantage under the relevant property relation **and** appropriation/interdependence such that exploiter welfare depends on exploited productive activity.

**Output:** typed exploitation relation with asset basis, counterpart and provenance.

**Ceiling:** not all oppression/deprivation is exploitation; never store a generic `exploitation_level` in lieu of relations.

## M10-M04 — ORGANIZATION CONTROL

**Inputs:** organizational unit; planning/coordination domain; decision-right scope; actor position.

**Derive:** effective control over coordination/integration decisions -> organization-control applicability.

**Realize:** actual policy/coordination decision or durable delegated authority state.

**Outputs:** decision power, resource-allocation capability, information access, responsibility, counterpart set.

**Ceiling:** organization asset != title; organization asset != authority as such.

**SDTF:** `AUTHORITY_CONTROL_SANCTION` + `ACTIVITY_ORGANIZATION`, D1.

## M10-M05 — SUPERVISION / TASK / SANCTION AUTHORITY

**Inputs:** directed superior-subordinate relation; task-assignment right; procedure/pace right; sanction rights; effective enforceability.

**Derive:** exact directed powers by scope.

**Realize:** active supervisory relation; particular assignment/sanction events where applicable.

**Outputs:** obligations and constraints for subordinate; powers and responsibility for supervisor; sanction risk and decision channel.

**Ceiling:** `MANAGER`, `SUPERVISOR`, or hierarchy score cannot substitute for exact powers.

**SDTF:** strong D1 evidence.

## M10-M06 — CREDENTIAL CLOSURE / SKILL SCARCITY

**Inputs:** credential; actual skill; credentialing rule; supply restriction; job requirement; actor eligibility; vacancy/market.

**Derive:** credential/skill + institutional closure + matching job -> restricted eligibility and possible bargaining/rent opportunity.

**Realize:** actual application/hire/assignment/pay determination/negotiation.

**Outputs:** gate eligibility, replacement scarcity, bargaining option, income opportunity, task assignment.

**Ceiling:** credential != skill; skill != scarcity; scarcity != job assignment; job assignment != rent. Wright's skill-as-class theory remains weaker than his capital/organization relations.

**SDTF:** `INSTITUTIONAL_GATE` + `RESOURCE_DEPENDENCY`, D1; temporal devaluation invokes D2.

## M10-M07 — MULTIPLEX / CONTRADICTORY LOCATION

**Inputs:** all active typed asset/control/exploitation relations for actor at time t.

**Derive:** detect heterogeneous signs across dimensions, e.g. capitalistically exploited + organization-control exploiter.

**Output:** optional analyst predicate `CONTRADICTORY_RELATION_BUNDLE` with source relations.

**Ceiling:** no special psychology, preference or behavior; no averaging to `net class` unless a particular analytic question defines and warrants it.

**SDTF:** D1 needs no new primitive beyond multiple relation support.

## M10-M08 — OBJECTIVE INTEREST / STRUCTURAL STAKE

**Inputs:** asset/control/dependency relation; feasible structural alternatives/counterfactuals.

**Derive:** actor has an objective material stake where relation changes would alter consumption/toil/resources/capacity/exit in a determinate direction.

**Output:** `STRUCTURAL_STAKE` or analyst inference linked to exact relation.

**Ceiling:** `OBJECTIVE_INTEREST != PERCEIVED_INTEREST != PREFERENCE != REASON != MOTIVE != ACTION`.

**SDTF:** D1 may expose grounds/options; V3 owns subjective representation.

## M10-M09 — LIFE CHANCES DECOMPOSITION

**Inputs:** resources, property, credentials, vacancies, legal/institutional gates, organizational decisions, labor markets, household transfers, time/history.

**Derive:** a set of actor-specific feasible alternatives and constraints across an interval.

**Realize:** applications, selections, jobs, moves, failures, gains/losses.

**Output:** typed opportunity/gate history rather than a `life_chances` scalar.

**Ceiling:** Wright uses `life chances` as a summary phrase; Rawlsing must not.

**SDTF:** D1 + D2.

## M10-M10 — CLASS MOBILITY / POSITION REBINDING

**Inputs:** prior position; credential/asset change; vacancy; recruitment/promotion gate; organizational decision; labor-market conditions; time.

**Rule:** prior relation does not change merely because an actor ages or aspires. A transition needs an admitted gate and realized selection/asset change.

**Realize:** promotion, job change, acquisition/loss of productive assets, credential-job match change.

**Outputs:** deactivate prior bindings; activate new dependency/authority/resource relations; preserve history.

**Ceiling:** cross-sectional age patterns cannot identify mobility; Wright explicitly warns career and cohort processes are confounded.

**SDTF:** strong D2 support; no D4.

## M10-M11 — HOUSEHOLD RESOURCE / DEPENDENCY TRANSFER

**Inputs:** member-specific employment/property relations; pooled income; ownership/control of household assets; unpaid labour/care; transfer rules; dependency/exit.

**Derive:** member A's external class relations alter member B only through actual shared resources, obligations, control or dependence.

**Realize:** transfers, expenditures, care work, control decisions, separation, labor-force transition.

**Outputs:** `ACTIVE_RESOURCE_STATE`, obligation relation, time constraint, support/dependency channel.

**Ceiling:** spouse's class label is not an actor fact for the focal person.

**SDTF:** `HOUSEHOLD_CARE_DEPENDENCY`, `LINKED_LIFE_TRANSMISSION`, D1/D2.

## M10-M12 — CLASS FORMATION / ORGANIZATIONAL MEDIATION

**Inputs:** actor structural stakes/capacities; organization membership/recruitment; party/union/association rules; network ties; discourse/information; coordination resources.

**Derive:** possible organization/coalition channels.

**Realize:** actual membership, coordination, commitment, collective decision and action.

**Outputs:** organization relation and collective capacity, not a collective psyche.

**Ceiling:** class structure supplies terrain/probabilities; it does not uniquely generate formation.

**SDTF:** D1 qualification + D3 for realized collective action/effects.

## M10-M13 — POLITICAL / INSTITUTIONAL FEEDBACK

**Inputs:** organized actors; resources; strategy/action; institutional gate/counterpart; law/policy/organizational response.

**Rule:**

```text
STRUCTURAL POSITIONS
-> ORGANIZATION / COORDINATION (contingent)
-> REALIZED COLLECTIVE ACTION
-> EXTERNAL INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE/GATE
-> CHANGED LAW / ORGANIZATION / ASSET ACCESS / LABOUR RULE
-> NEW ACTOR-RELATIVE ECOLOGY
```

**Outputs:** new external rules/relations with provenance; later D1 derivations.

**Ceiling:** failed action must be representable; action cannot directly rewrite structure.

**SDTF:** strong confirmation of D3.

## M10-M14 — POLITICAL MEDIATION OF EXPERIENCE

**Inputs:** objective event/condition (e.g. unemployment); party/union/state/school/media interpretive environments; actor exposure/access.

**Derive:** objective event plus reachable interpretive channels, not an interpretation.

**Realize:** actual information/contact/participation; downstream V3 may form perception/theory/preference.

**Ceiling:** the same unemployment fact can be experienced as personal failure, injustice or otherwise; event does not dictate meaning.

**SDTF:** validates seam/V3 boundary.
