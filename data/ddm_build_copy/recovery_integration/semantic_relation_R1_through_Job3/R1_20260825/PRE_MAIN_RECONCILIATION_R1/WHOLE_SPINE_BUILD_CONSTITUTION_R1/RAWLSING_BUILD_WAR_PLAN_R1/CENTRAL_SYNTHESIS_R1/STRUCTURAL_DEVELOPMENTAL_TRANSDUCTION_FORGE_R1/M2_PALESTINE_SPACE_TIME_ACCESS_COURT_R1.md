## What “space” decomposes into

The volume does not support a generic `SPACE` variable. Every high-value spatial mechanism decomposes into one or more of:

```text
JURISDICTION_BOUNDARY
ROUTE_ELIGIBILITY
PHYSICAL_ROUTE_AVAILABILITY
TRAVEL_DISTANCE
QUEUE / WAIT
OPEN/CLOSED INTERVAL
SERVICE_CATCHMENT
PARCEL_REACHABILITY
COUNTERPART_REACHABILITY
RESOURCE_LOCATION_RELATIVE_TO_LEGAL_BOUNDARY
REQUIRED_RETURN_DEADLINE
ALTERNATIVE_ROUTE
```

These terms matter only through their effect on an exact actor position/activity/counterpart. A wall or checkpoint is not itself an actor effect.

## What “time” decomposes into

The volume supplies strong evidence that D2 cannot be implemented as a bag of event timestamps. Required temporal semantics include:

```text
ACTIVE_INTERVAL(rule/service/closure)
VALIDITY_INTERVAL(permit)
DAILY_RECURRENCE(commute/care/provisioning)
WAIT_DURATION(queue/gate)
DEADLINE(return-before-expiry; specimen viability)
SCHEDULE_COUPLING(linked household members)
UNCERTAIN_DELAY(checkpoint/roadblock)
INTERRUPTION(class/work/care)
BEFORE_AFTER_REBINDING(permit cancellation/reopening; authority transfer)
RECURRENT_REPAIR(after raids/destruction)
CUMULATIVE_TYPED_UPDATE(resource depletion/noncultivation history)
```

The same actor may move among `AVAILABLE`, `QUALIFIED`, `BLOCKED`, `DELAYED`, `REALIZED`, and later `REBOUND` states without any new dramatic event being the causal unit.


```text

-> EXTERNAL RESPONSE OR ACTUAL EXCHANGE
    ↓ D3

-> LATER D1+D2 DERIVATION
```

## D4 failure test

A distinct D4 would be warranted only if the source forced a causal step with its own inputs/outputs that cannot be represented by:

1. D1 actor-relative qualification/realization,
2. D2 interval/recurrence/rebinding, and
3. D3 provenance-preserving later-world update where action changes routes/resources/relations.

M2 found no such step. Even the hardest cases compose:

### Linked household schedule

```text
WORK_GATE + ACTOR_A
-> A COMMUTE INTERVAL / PRESENCE LOSS                 [D1+D2]
-> HOUSEHOLD CONTRIBUTION WITHDRAWAL                 [D1 typed relation]
-> TASK/OBLIGATION TRANSFER TO ACTOR_B               [D1 linked-life]
-> B RECURRENT TIME/ACTIVITY HISTORY                  [D2]
```

The needed correction is to make `contribution withdrawal -> obligation/task transfer` explicit inside D1/G5, not to introduce a space-time D4.

### Temporary market under controlled roads

```text
PASS/ROAD RULE
-> ROUTE CONSTRAINT                                  [D1+D2]
+ BOYCOTT/COORDINATION
-> ALTERNATIVE PRODUCTION/TRANSPORT/EXCHANGE         [D3]
-> TEMPORARY MARKET FACT
-> LATER PARTICIPANT RESOURCE/COUNTERPART ECOLOGY    [D1]
```

### Sperm-smuggling reproductive route

```text
PRISON/CONJUGAL-ACCESS CONSTRAINT                    [D1+D2]
-> COORDINATED SPECIMEN ROUTE + IVF COUNTERPARTS     [D3]
-> REALIZED PROCEDURE/BIRTH IF SUCCESSFUL
-> SEPARATE PATERNITY/STATUS GATE                    [D1]
```

Again no D4.

## Required architectural consequence

M2 licenses **tests and interface specificity**, not a new top-level capability:

- D1 must treat route/access/time qualifiers as typed consequences of exact rules and actor positions, not as generic context.
- D2 must support schedule coupling across linked actors, wait/deadline intervals, recurrent task identity, and atomic before/after rebinding.
- D1/G5 must encode linked-person contribution withdrawal and task/obligation transfer.
- D3 must allow alternative-route/resource/network creation and temporary/suppressed outcomes without claiming governing-rule change.
