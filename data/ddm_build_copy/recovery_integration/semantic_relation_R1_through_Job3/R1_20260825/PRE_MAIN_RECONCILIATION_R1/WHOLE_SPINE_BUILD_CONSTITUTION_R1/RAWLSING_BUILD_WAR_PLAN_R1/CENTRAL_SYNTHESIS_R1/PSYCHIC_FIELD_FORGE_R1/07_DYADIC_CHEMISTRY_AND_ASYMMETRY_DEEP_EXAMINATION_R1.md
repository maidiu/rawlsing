## 0. Purpose

This file deeply examines PFF frontier pocket 5, `dyadic chemistry / anti-chemistry`, together with the inseparable perspective/asymmetry, recruitment, actor-model, persistence, defense, and V3/V2 boundary questions.

The central question is:

> What is genuinely emergent from actor × interlocutor × relationship history × current exchange, rather than owned by either actor alone, and does preserving that emergence require a new dyadic psyche/state object?

Current conclusion:

`GENUINELY_DYADIC_PROCESS = YES`

`GENUINELY_DYADIC_PSYCHIC_ENTITY = NOT_ESTABLISHED`

`NEW_PERSISTENT_DYAD_STATE_GADGET = NOT_JUSTIFIED`

The strongest present hypothesis is that chemistry is primarily a **property of coupled, direction-specific transition dynamics over time**, not a global compatibility scalar and not a third mind located between the participants.

## 2. First major distinction: dyadic emergence does not imply dyadic substance

A system can have properties that exist only at the level of the pair/sequence without requiring a separately stored third psychic substance.

Example:

- A presses for reassurance;
- B experiences pressure and withdraws;
- A experiences withdrawal as abandonment and presses harder;
- B experiences increased pressure and withdraws harder.

The resulting `pursue -> withdraw -> stronger pursue -> stronger withdraw` pattern is genuinely dyadic. It cannot be described adequately by a static trait of A alone or B alone.

But the existence of that pair-level pattern does not prove the need for a mutable object named `DyadicChemistryState`.

It may be completely preservable as:

- A's durable V3 organization;
- B's durable V3 organization where B is generated;
- each participant's relationship-specific history/view;
- each participant's current live PFF configuration;
- the actual exchanged interaction sequence;
- lawful shared/objective relationship history/current situation;
- transition effects of each participant's output upon the other.

Thus:

`EMERGENT_PATTERN != NECESSARILY_NEW_STATE_VARIABLE`

`DYADIC_PROCESS != THIRD_MIND`

`PAIR_LEVEL_DESCRIPTION != PAIR_LEVEL_CAUSAL_OWNER`

## 3. Chemistry is better treated provisionally as coupling than compatibility

The term `chemistry` should not be frozen as a scalar such as:

`chemistry(A,B) = 0.82`

That would collapse too much.

A more useful pre-architecture conception is:

> Chemistry is the characteristic pattern by which signals/actions produced by one participant recruit, inhibit, amplify, redirect, soothe, threaten, unlock, or destabilize live organization in the other, and how the resulting response then feeds back across repeated turns.

This is inherently directional:

`A -> B EFFECT != B -> A EFFECT`

A may strongly recruit B while B weakly recruits A.

A may soothe B while B excites A.

A may feel attraction while B feels curiosity.

A may feel safe enough to become unusually open while B remains guarded.

Therefore:

`CHEMISTRY != MUTUAL_ATTRACTION`

`CHEMISTRY != RECIPROCITY`

`CHEMISTRY != RELATIONSHIP_QUALITY`

`CHEMISTRY != COMPATIBILITY`

`CHEMISTRY_INTENSITY != POSITIVE_VALENCE`

`AMPLIFICATION != HEALTH`

`DAMPENING != HARM`

## 4. Four non-collapsible perspectives on one interaction

At minimum preserve:

### 4.1 A's actor-local reality concerning B

Includes, where lawfully generated/accessible:

- A's memory/history of B;
- A's expectations of B;
- A's current perception of B;
- A's hypotheses about B's motives/intentions;
- A's model of what B thinks/feels about A;
- A's attraction/aversion, fear, trust, shame, etc., where actually caused/owned;
- A's current live recruitment and conscious field.

### 4.2 B's actor-local reality concerning A

Distinct and potentially contradictory.

### 4.3 Objective generated/shared interaction history

What actually occurred between them, including admitted events, commitments, relationship-kind facts, and other objective generated relationship history where V3 owns them.

### 4.4 Pair-level interaction pattern

A derived/observable pattern over the sequence, e.g.:

- pursue/withdraw;
- escalating dominance/submission;
- mutual joking amplification;
- repeated failed repair;
- mutual disclosure ratchet;
- one person's warmth repeatedly disarming the other's vigilance;
- reciprocal status challenge;
- asymmetrical flirtation that becomes self-reinforcing because one participant rewards it without sharing the same motive.

The fourth item is real as a pattern while remaining distinct from reciprocal inner truth.

## 5. The key candidate mechanism: composition of receiver transforms

PFF already needs a person-specific receiver transformation:

`surface/discourse signal -> receiver-specific perception -> psychic effect`

Dyadic chemistry emerges when these transformations are recursively coupled:

`B output -> A receiver transform -> A live configuration -> A output`

then

`A output -> B receiver transform -> B live configuration -> B output`

and so on.

The important object of conceptual study is therefore not a pair score but the **composition of the two directional transformations through time**.

Possible loop properties include:

- gain/amplification;
- dampening;
- oscillation;
- polarization;
- lock-in;
- repair;
- sensitization;
- mutual inhibition;
- asymmetric attraction;
- asymmetric regulation;
- convergence;
- divergence;
- mode switching.

These are conceptual process properties only; no equations or runtime representation are adopted here.

## 6. Scientific support for a coupled-process interpretation

External psychology/relationship-science literature is useful here as non-authoritative design evidence.

### 6.1 Interpersonal complementarity

Interpersonal circumplex research organizes interaction around agency/control and communion/affiliation. A recurring finding is that interpersonal behavior tends to **invite** characteristic partner responses rather than deterministically cause them: warmth often invites warmth; dominance often invites submission. Horowitz et al. further emphasize that the complementary response is the response that satisfies the motive behind the initiating behavior, and what counts as complementary can differ across individuals.

Design implication:

`SAME_BEHAVIOR != SAME_PARTNER_EFFECT`

`BEHAVIOR_INVITES_RESPONSE != BEHAVIOR_DETERMINES_RESPONSE`

This aligns strongly with PFF receiver-specific transformation.

### 6.2 Actor-partner interdependence

Dyadic research distinguishes actor effects from partner effects precisely because outcomes in a dyad are non-independent. One person's state/behavior can affect both their own subsequent state and their counterpart's state.

Design implication:

PFF should preserve both actor-local transition and counterpart-caused transition without collapsing them into one shared state.

### 6.3 Demand/withdraw loops

Relationship research repeatedly observes demand/withdraw cycles in which one participant's demanding is associated with the other's withdrawing, which in turn can recruit further demanding. Such patterns vary across couples and across segments of the same interaction.

Design implication:

A stable person-level trait description is insufficient. Pair-specific interaction loops can emerge and intensify over minutes.

### 6.4 Dyadic regulation

Attachment research shows that partner behavior can regulate insecure reactions and, through repeated interaction, contribute to a more secure relational environment over time.

Design implication:

A counterpart is not merely a trigger. They can function as a recurrent regulator/destabilizer of the actor's live organization. Repeated lawful effects may later become durable only through V3 admission.

## 7. Enneagram contribution: internal logic, not pair matching

The Enneagram material should not produce a type-pair compatibility table.

Useful inherited logic instead includes:

### 7.1 Instinctual priority alignment/misalignment

Riso/Hudson treat Self-Preservation, Social, and Sexual/one-to-one intensity as independent motivational priorities and explicitly note that people with similar instinctual priorities may share values while different priorities can create recurrent conflict.

Rawlsing translation:

> Participants may preferentially organize attention and action around different motivational domains, producing friction or resonance.

Do not retain:

`SP + SX = X compatibility score`

Retain the deeper question:

`MOTIVATIONAL_DOMAIN_ALIGNMENT / MISALIGNMENT -> HOW DOES IT ALTER RECRUITMENT?`

### 7.2 Dominant relational affect

Attachment/frustration/rejection groupings suggest different recurrent relational problem forms.

Rawlsing translation:

The same partner behavior may recruit different causal organizations depending on whether the actor is especially organized around maintaining attachment, experienced deprivation/frustration, or anticipated rejection.

Again, this is actor-local receiver logic, not shared relationship truth.

### 7.3 Pursuit strategy × frustration response

Hornevian-like pursuit strategies and Harmonic frustration responses can create complementary or conflicting loops.

Examples:

- one participant presses while the other withdraws;
- one seeks emotional reaction while the other moves into competency/control;
- one reframes positively while the other experiences the reframing as invalidation;
- two assertive strategies create status escalation;
- two withdrawal strategies create mutual deadening without either explicitly rejecting the other.

### 7.4 Levels / operating condition

The same pair can exhibit different chemistry as durable developmental condition and current local condition change.

A more flexible actor may tolerate ambiguity and permit correction; a more captured/defensive condition may convert the same cue rapidly into the dominant threat/motive pathway.

Thus:

`PAIRING != FIXED_CHEMISTRY`

`CHEMISTRY = STATE_DEPENDENT`

### 7.5 Security-specific repertoire unlocking

Riso/Hudson's `Security Point` idea is especially relevant: they describe people as accessing behaviors/capacities with people they feel secure with that they do not generally express under uncertain relationships.

Rawlsing translation:

> Some dyads may make otherwise weakly accessible capacities locally reachable without changing the durable person.

This is an important positive form of chemistry: not only triggering pathology or attraction, but **unlocking latent flexibility, tenderness, humor, courage, play, reflection, or dependence tolerance**.

## 8. Chemistry can be asymmetrical in at least six distinct senses

Do not use `asymmetry` as one generic property.

### 8.1 Valence asymmetry

A likes B; B dislikes A.

### 8.2 intensity asymmetry

Both are attracted/interested, but one much more strongly.

### 8.3 meaning asymmetry

A experiences teasing as intimacy; B intends/experiences it as casual humor.

### 8.4 regulatory asymmetry

B strongly calms A; A does little to B.

### 8.5 epistemic asymmetry

A correctly/incorrectly believes B understands them deeply; B has a different model entirely.

### 8.6 dependency/power asymmetry

One participant's outcomes, status, resources, attachment security, or future plans depend more heavily on the other.

These forms can coexist and should not be inferred from one another.

## 9. Being seen is a distinct dyadic mechanism

PFF frontier pocket 6 should be treated as tightly coupled to chemistry.

A participant is affected not only by B but by **A's model of B's perception of A**.

Example loop:

1. A feels attraction.
2. A believes B can see it.
3. Being seen recruits shame/self-concealment.
4. A becomes cool or dismissive.
5. B perceives disinterest and withdraws.
6. A reads B's withdrawal as rejection.
7. rejection intensifies A's original defensive configuration.

No participant needs an accurate model of the other for the loop to be real.

Therefore:

`METAPERCEPTION_CAN_BE_CAUSAL_WITHOUT_BEING_TRUE`

`MUTUAL_MISREADING_CAN_GENERATE_A_REAL_INTERACTION_PATTERN`

This may be one of the most important mechanisms behind anti-chemistry.

## 10. Chemistry is domain-specific, not globally scalar

Two people may have:

- strong intellectual chemistry;
- weak emotional comfort;
- strong sexual/attraction charge;
- poor conflict regulation;
- unusually good humor synchrony;
- status competition;
- caregiving complementarity;
- poor practical coordination.

A single `chemistry` value would destroy this structure.

The pre-architecture requirement is therefore to preserve **mode/domain specificity** without prematurely adopting a fixed list of domains.

## 11. Chemistry is not relationship health

Some powerful dyadic patterns are harmful.

Examples:

- jealousy/reassurance spirals;
- dominance/submission lock-in;
- mutual contempt escalation;
- trauma-like attraction/rescue loops;
- demand/withdraw;
- mutual grandiosity;
- reciprocal avoidance.

Likewise, dampening can be beneficial:

- one participant reliably de-escalates another;
- humor interrupts shame spirals;
- calm boundary setting prevents status escalation;
- secure responsiveness reduces abandonment panic.

Therefore:

`HIGH_DYADIC_GAIN != GOOD_RELATIONSHIP`

`LOW_DYADIC_GAIN != BAD_RELATIONSHIP`

`EXCITEMENT != SAFETY`

`SAFETY != BOREDOM`

`STABILITY != MUTUALITY`

## 12. Generated-generated versus generated-external interaction

PFF cannot assume both participants possess Rawlsing V3/PFF internals.

### 12.1 Generated actor <-> generated actor

Potentially both sides have:

- durable V3 personhood;
- actor-local memories/views;
- PFF live state;
- lawful objective generated relationship history.

The coupled system can therefore be computed symmetrically while still preserving perspective asymmetry.

### 12.2 Generated actor <-> external/human interlocutor

Only the generated actor has governed internal person state.

The external interlocutor supplies:

- observable discourse/actions;
- caller/application context where lawful;
- no guaranteed access to hidden intention or internal state.

Therefore chemistry must still function when one side is epistemically opaque.

This strongly argues against any PFF definition that requires a shared hidden dyadic state or full dual-psyche access.

Hard requirement:

`PFF_DYADIC_PROCESS_MUST_SUPPORT_ASYMMETRIC_INTERNAL_OBSERVABILITY`

## 13. Candidate taxonomy of genuinely dyadic phenomena

These are phenomenon classes, not component names.

### 13.1 Invitation-response complementarity

One behavior shifts probability/availability of counterpart responses.

### 13.2 Mutual amplification

Each participant's response strengthens the other's prior activation.

### 13.3 Mutual dampening/regulation

Each participant reduces or organizes the other's activation.

### 13.4 Polarization / role crystallization

Repeated coupling creates increasingly differentiated roles, e.g. pursuer/withdrawer, teacher/rebel, caretaker/dependent, controller/resister.

Important: a role in the dyad is not necessarily the participant's general personality.

### 13.5 Mutual unlocking

The pair makes capacities available that neither participant ordinarily expresses broadly.

### 13.6 Mutual inhibition / deadening

Attempts at activation repeatedly receive responses that extinguish them.

### 13.7 Escalation / lock-in

Each response narrows the other's next response range and makes reversal harder.

### 13.8 Repair loop

One participant recognizes/responds to rupture in a way that reopens options and lowers defensive capture.

### 13.9 Shared convention / groove

Repeated interaction establishes a recognizable local mode such as banter, formal distance, ritual reassurance, or conflict choreography.

This may be derivable from objective history/session continuity and actor-local expectation; no independent PFF dyad object is yet justified.

### 13.10 Mutual miscalibration

Each participant's model of the other's model causes responses that confirm the initial misreading.

## 14. Where could a true pair-level state be justified?

A new pair-level mutable PFF state should be admitted only if a semantic remainder survives all of the following attempts:

1. represent it as objective V3 relationship history/state;
2. represent it as A's relationship-specific V3/PFF state;
3. represent it as B's relationship-specific V3/PFF state;
4. represent it as current session/transcript/context continuity;
5. represent it as a derived property of the interaction sequence;
6. represent it as a transition/coupling relation rather than stored state.

Only if the phenomenon cannot be preserved through those routes should new pair-level state be proposed.

Current examination has not established such a remainder.

## 16. Relationship-live persistence

A major unresolved issue is whether current live relationship-specific residues should persist beyond one conversational session.

Need to distinguish:

- turn residue;
- session residue;
- relationship-episode residue;
- V3 durable relationship change.

The existence of a recurring dyadic pattern does not itself authorize persistence outside the current lawful scope.

Future PFF work must determine whether relationship-live state is:

- merely session-live;
- recoverable from V3 relationship history plus fresh activation;
- a lawful intermediate persistence class;
- or unnecessary once V3/PFF boundaries are correctly drawn.

## 17. Interaction trajectory versus objective relationship truth

A pair can be in a flirtatious, hostile, playful, tense, intimate-feeling, or avoidant **interaction trajectory** without that trajectory becoming an objective durable relationship fact.

Therefore:

`CURRENT_INTERACTION_MODE != V3_RELATIONSHIP_KIND`

`CURRENT_FLIRTATION != MUTUAL_ATTRACTION`

`CURRENT_WARMTH != TRUST`

`CURRENT_DISCLOSURE != INTIMACY_STATE`

`CURRENT_ARGUMENT != RELATIONSHIP_RUPTURE`

These distinctions are essential to prevent PFF vividness from overwriting V3 truth.

## 18. Relationship-specific accessibility and security

A relationship can alter what is locally reachable in an actor without altering durable identity.

Possible phenomena:

- a person who is guarded generally becomes tender with one counterpart;
- a person who is submissive generally becomes assertive with someone trusted;
- a person who is highly verbal generally becomes inhibited around one critic;
- a person who rarely accesses grief does so with one safe friend;
- a person who is usually self-contained becomes reassurance-seeking with one attachment figure.

This suggests that B-band recruitment is not merely choosing which already-visible trait to express. It may change the **local accessibility landscape** over the durable person.

This is a major PFF function candidate.

## 19. Type/wing/level implications for chemistry

Enneagram type, wing, and Level should not directly determine chemistry.

Their useful contribution is to supply hypotheses about:

- recurrent motivational organization;
- secondary organization;
- defensive capture/flexibility;
- characteristic pursuit strategy;
- characteristic response to frustration;
- relational affect organization;
- instinctual priority;
- security-specific repertoire access.

The correct Rawlsing direction is:

`V3 REALIZED PERSON + CURRENT DYAD -> PFF COUPLING DYNAMICS`

not:

`TYPE PAIR -> CHEMISTRY PROFILE`

## 20. Hard negatives

- `relationship_chemistry_score` as a universal scalar;
- type-pair compatibility lookup;
- symmetrical attraction inference;
- shared hidden dyadic psyche;
- transcript friendliness -> trust/intimacy;
- interaction tone -> durable relationship truth;

Any successful PFF architecture must eventually be able to preserve:

1. direction-specific partner effects;
2. asymmetric perception and motive;
3. reciprocal loops without reciprocal truth;
4. pair-specific amplification/dampening;

6. actor model of being seen/metaperception;
7. state- and history-dependent chemistry;
8. domain/mode-specific chemistry rather than one scalar;
