## 1. T2 terminal answer to the central question

Critical Psychology supplies a non-deterministic and non-voluntarist mediation grammar:

```text
OBJECTIVE SOCIETAL POSSIBILITY / CONSTRAINT
        ↓  [must be positionally and practically relevant]
ACTOR-RELATIVE SCOPE / CONDITION / RELATION
        ↓  [subject relates to determinate aspects]
SUBJECT-RELATIVE PREMISES
        ↓  [premises + perceived life interests]
GROUNDS / REASONS FOR ACTION
        ↓  [grounded alternative is actually enacted]
REALIZED ACTION / PARTICIPATION
        ↓
REPRODUCTION OR MODIFICATION OF PRACTICE / CONDITION
        ↘
          REALIZED BIOGRAPHICAL CONSEQUENCE
                 ↓
          DOWNSTREAM DEVELOPMENT IF EARNED
```

This blocks structural determinism because objective possibility is not action and because the subject’s premise/reason relation is not bypassed. It blocks voluntarism because premises and feasible action alternatives are constrained by concrete positions, relations, dependencies, obligations, resources, institutions, other actors and historically available scopes. Collective transformation must itself be materially organized and causally effective.

## 3. Rawlsing seam fit

The strongest Phase-0 lawful chain is:

```text
PRE-V3 structured possibility
→ actor/context binding
→ actor-relative opportunity/constraint/encounter
→ realized biography
→ V3 uptake/response
→ V3 memory/learning/path dependence/development
```

T2 does not challenge this chain. It sharpens the upstream middle at four specific points:

1. **Applicability must not be mistaken for a premise.** An actor can be objectively exposed to a possibility/constraint without organizing action around it.
2. **Actor binding must be socially contentful.** Concrete participation positions produce differentiated obligations, relations, information and influence; the Phase-0 map itself says generic binding does not derive these.
3. **Opportunity must not be mistaken for action.** The subject’s reason relation and realized participation preserve nonmodal, resistant, bypassing and locally securing routes.
4. **The arrow can run back.** Realized individual/collective participation can reproduce or alter later social conditions, so a one-way structure→person pipeline is incomplete.

T2 therefore supports the Phase-0 name `STRUCTURED_WORLD_TO_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_TRANSDUCTION`, but does not license one opaque monolithic operator. The theory resolves into smaller connective functions F1–F6.

## 5. Mandatory temporal-form court

### EVENT

T2 can analyze an episodic dilemma, encounter, sanction, opportunity or decision as an event, but its distinctive contribution is not an event carrier. A structural event becomes a premise only if actor-relative relevance is established. An event entering biography still does not guarantee uptake or durable change.

**T2 judgment:** `COMPOSABLE_WITH_EXISTING_EVENT/OEE__NO_NEW_T2_EVENT_CARRIER`.

### CONDITION

Persistent dependency, limited influence, institutional arrangement, ongoing obligation and cross-context conflict are central. They are not adequately represented merely by repeating a point event. They condition what becomes possible/relevant over intervals.

**T2 judgment:** `POSITIVE_SEMANTICS_REQUIRED__EXISTING_PHASE0_CONDITION_SUPPORT_UNDERGENERALIZED`.

### RELATION

Power, dependency, differentiated participation, obligation and collective coordination are relational. Objective structural relation and actor-specific lived relationship/history remain distinct.

**T2 judgment:** `BOOKENDS_PRESENT__CONNECTIVE_RELATIONAL_SEMANTICS_REQUIRE_GENERALIZATION`.

### RECURRENT ACTIVITY / PRACTICE

Conduct of everyday life and participation are recurrent practices. Repetition matters because practical arrangements, routines, reproduction and change occur through activity, not only salient events.

**T2 judgment:** `POSITIVE_COMPOSITION_REQUIRED__DO_NOT_COLLAPSE_TO_EVENT_STREAM`.

### TRANSITION

Institutional routes can prearrange transition possibilities, but actual transition requires realized movement/change in participation.

**T2 judgment:** `GENERALIZE_ROUTE_PLUS_REALIZATION_SEMANTICS`.

### TRAJECTORY

Personal trajectory is a changing configuration of participation across contexts. Institutional scripts can structure it but do not determine it.

**T2 judgment:** `GENERALIZE_BIOGRAPHY_PATH_DEPENDENCE_WITH_STRUCTURAL_ROUTE_REALIZATION`.

### TURNING POINT

A changed premise/reason constellation or realized transition can later prove trajectory-changing, but inspected T2 theory gives no universal threshold for calling an occurrence a turning point.

**T2 judgment:** `DEFER_DISTINCT_CARRIER__ASSIGN_ONLY_FROM_REALIZED_LATER_HISTORY`.

### CUMULATIVE PROCESS

Repeated participation can reproduce conditions, stabilize routines, or change later opportunities. Yet T2 does not provide a quantitative dose/accumulation law.

**T2 judgment:** `HISTORY_COMPOSITION_PLAUSIBLE__POSITIVE_ACCUMULATION_LAW_NOT_SUPPLIED`.

## 6. T2 answer to deterministic and voluntarist failure cases

| Failure case | T2 resolution |
|---|---|
| same structural category, different lives | category does not specify concrete position, participation, context, premise constellation or realized trajectory |
| objective opportunity never used | possibility must become actor-relevant premise/reason and action must occur |
| actor resists structurally likely route | subject-grounded alternatives remain open within feasible scope |
| actor accommodates a constraint | locally securing/restrictive action may be grounded without becoming a fixed trait |
| chronic dependency without salient event | model as condition/relation and recurrent conduct, not forced eventization |
| collective action changes conditions | require organization, realized action and causal structural update before changing later ecology |
| institution supplies a normative path | pathway possibility remains distinct from actual transition/trajectory |
| one context predicts whole person | cross-context conduct composes multiple positions, relations and commitments |
| later person/action changes exposure | preserve feedback from realized action/selection to later position/relation/exposure |
| demographic similarity is used as psychological prior | prohibited: structural category never licenses direct trait inference |

## 8. Smallest exact T2 contribution to the SDTF seam

T2’s strongest architectural contribution is **not** a new psychology of social categories. It is a mediation discipline plus three upstream connective needs:

```text
A. OBJECTIVE POSSIBILITY
   → ACTOR/POSITION-SPECIFIC RELEVANCE
   → SUBJECT-RELATIVE PREMISE

B. STRUCTURED PRACTICE/POSITION
   → ACTOR-SPECIFIC SCOPE / OBLIGATION / INFORMATION / COUNTERPART ECOLOGY
   → REALIZED PARTICIPATION ACROSS CONTEXTS

C. REALIZED ACTION / PARTICIPATION
   → REPRODUCED OR MODIFIED LATER CONDITION
   → UPDATED OPPORTUNITY / CONSTRAINT / EXPOSURE ECOLOGY
```

These functions can be normalized against T1/T3–T6 and the independent R lane. T2 does not prove that each requires a new carrier or owner; it proves the semantic distinctions that any composition must preserve.
