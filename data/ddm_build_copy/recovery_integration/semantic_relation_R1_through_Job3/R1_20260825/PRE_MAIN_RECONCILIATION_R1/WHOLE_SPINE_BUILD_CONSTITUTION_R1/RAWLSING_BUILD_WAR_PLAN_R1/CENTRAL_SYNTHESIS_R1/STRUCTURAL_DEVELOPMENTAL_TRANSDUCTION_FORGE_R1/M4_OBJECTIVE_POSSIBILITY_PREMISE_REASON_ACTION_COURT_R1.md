# M4_OBJECTIVE_POSSIBILITY_PREMISE_REASON_ACTION_COURT_R1

QUESTION:

> What is the minimum lawful transduction from objective societal possibility to subject-relative premise, reason and action in *Grundlegung der Psychologie*?

PRIMARY SOURCE: Holzkamp, especially Ch. 6 pp. 229–243 and Ch. 7 pp. 315–369.

---

## 1. Court holding

The source requires the following non-collapsible sequence:

```text
OBJECTIVE SOCIETAL CONDITION / RELATION
        ↓
OBJECTIVE SOCIETAL MEANING / ACTION STRUCTURE
        ↓
LIFE-SITUATION / POSITION-SPECIFIC MEANING CONSTELLATION
        ↓
OBJECTIVE ACTION / THOUGHT POSSIBILITY FOR THIS POSITION
        ↓
SITUATIONAL-PERSONAL / PHENOMENAL GIVENNESS TO THE SUBJECT
+ BIOGRAPHICAL / PERSONAL CONDITIONS
        ↓
SUBJECTIVE POSSIBILITY SPACE
        ↓
PREMISE(S) OF A REASON NEXUS
        ↓
SUBJECTIVE GROUND / REASON
        ↓
MOTIVATED OR COERCED ORIENTATION / ACCEPTANCE / REFUSAL
        ↓
ACTION OR NONACTION
        ↓
REALIZED EFFECT IF ANY
        ↓
DURABLE CHANGE ONLY IF SEPARATELY EARNED
```

The book does **not** authorize contraction of this chain into either:

```text
STRUCTURE -> ACTION
```

or

```text
OBJECTIVE APPLICABILITY -> PREMISE
```

## 2. Stage-by-stage adjudication

### Stage 0 — objective societal condition

**Source:** Ch. 5 pp. 191–203.

The socially produced material world has objective economic structures and social relations. It exists as a supra-individual objective fact before any particular subject’s psychic realization.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**Court:** PRE-V3-safe in Rawlsing terms.

### Stage 1 — objective societal meaning/action structure (`Bedeutungsstruktur`)

**Source:** Ch. 6 pp. 229–238.

Societal relations are represented in objective meaning/action/thought structures. For the individual, these are primarily societal action possibilities.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**Court:** still not a subject premise.

### Stage 2 — position/life-situation specification

**Source:** Ch. 5 pp. 195–203; Ch. 7 pp. 358–367.

The individual does not relate directly to “society as a whole.” Concrete `Lebenslage` and `Position` select and refract the objective meaning relations. Holzkamp calls the life-situation-specific formations `Infrastrukturen` of the larger meaning structures.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**Court:** this is the strongest Holzkampian source basis for D1.

### Stage 3 — actor-relative objective action/thought possibility

**Source:** Ch. 6 pp. 233–238; Ch. 7 p. 359 and p. 367 ff.

This stage is still objective. The p. 359 footnote expressly states that the meaning analysis is identifying objectively given action/thought possibilities, **not their subjective realization**.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

**Court:** Rawlsing D1 may own an equivalent actor-relative objective fact if provenance and typing are preserved.

### Stage 4 — phenomenal/situational-personal givenness

**Source:** Ch. 7 pp. 352–353 and 367–369.

Objective and personal conditions do not determine action “as such.” They enter the subject’s current situation in their `phänomenaler Aspekt` — as experienced situation, capacities, past/future orientation, emotional evaluation and practical possibilities. At pp. 367–369 the position-specific meanings determine the actual subjective possibility space only as they are situational-personally given.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION / CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

**Court:** this is the PRE-V3/V3 border pressure point. It is subject mediation.

### Stage 5 — subjective possibility space (`subjektiver Möglichkeitsraum`)

**Source:** Ch. 7 pp. 367–369.

The current possibility space is individual, first-person, historically shaped and ascertainable only from the subject’s concrete standpoint. Prior realizations/nonrealizations matter; actors may lag behind or misjudge objective possibilities.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**Court:** this must not be emitted by PRE-V3 merely from structural applicability. It belongs to subject-side state/mediation.

### Stage 6 — premise (`Prämisse`)

**Source:** Ch. 7 pp. 351–356, 367–369.

Historically specific external and personal conditions form premises of a reason nexus only in their subject-relative mode. A premise is not a structural prerequisite. It is a condition as operative within the subject’s reason relation.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

**Court:** actual premise status requires subject-side ownership. It need **not** mean explicit propositional belief; “epistemic” must be broad enough to include first-person/phenomenal availability and emotionally evaluated situation.

### Stage 7 — ground/reason (`subjektiver Handlungsgrund`)

**Source:** Ch. 7 pp. 348–356.

Reasons are formed in the medium of meaning/action structures and are subjectively functional relative to the subject’s experienced needs/life interests. They are neither arbitrary nor merely externally caused.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**Court:** V3 subject mediation. A reason does not compel action.

### Stage 8 — motivation/coercion/acceptance/refusal

**Source:** Ch. 7 pp. 315–324.

A recognized societal goal may be accepted or rejected. Objective interests may be misrepresented. Coercive action may occur without motivated adoption. Holzkamp therefore supplies no simple reason→action function.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

### Stage 9 — action (`Handlung`)

**Source:** Ch. 6 p. 234; Ch. 7.

Action is the subject’s realized life activity under societal conditions. The actor could have acted differently within the relevant possibility relation, subject to historically concrete constraints.

**Status:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

### Stage 10 — effect

**Source:** p. 322; pp. 377–378; Ch. 5 p. 191; Ch. 7 collective-action passages.

Action may affect relations/conditions, but intended action does not equal successful effect. Effects depend on actual relations, other persons and wider conditions.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

### Stage 11 — durable development

**Source:** Ch. 8 pp. 417 ff.

Durable developmental change requires individual history. Chapter 8’s categorial process types do not make a single action automatically developmental.

**Status:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`, with explicit empirical ceiling.

## 3. The minimum lawful Rawlsing transduction

The user-specified court proposed:

```text
STRUCTURED POSSIBILITY
+ ACTOR POSITION
+ CURRENT CONDITIONS/HISTORY
→ SUBJECT-RELATIVE PREMISE POSSIBILITY
```

M4 adjudicates this as follows.

### Lawful PRE-V3/D1 output

```text
ADMITTED STRUCTURED POSSIBILITY
+ EXACT ACTOR POSITION / LIFE-SITUATION BINDING
+ WORLD / CONTEXT / TIME
+ RELEVANT OBJECTIVE HISTORY
+ TYPED SOCIAL MECHANISM
→ ACTOR-RELATIVE OBJECTIVE POSSIBILITY / CONSTRAINT / RELATION
  THAT IS PREMISE-RELEVANT FOR THIS ACTOR
```

Call this a **premise-relevant actor-relative social fact**, not a `Prämisse`.

### Required V3-side transformation

```text
PREMISE-RELEVANT ACTOR-RELATIVE SOCIAL FACT
+ FIRST-PERSON ACCESS / PHENOMENAL GIVENNESS
+ PERSONAL/BIOGRAPHICAL STATE
+ PERCEPTION / KNOWLEDGE / MISRECOGNITION / EMOTIONAL EVALUATION WHERE RELEVANT
→ ACTUAL PREMISE WITHIN A SUBJECTIVE REASON NEXUS
```

This transformation is genuinely required by the source.

### Ownership holding

`FUNCTION_REQUIRED__NEW_PREV3_SEAM_OWNER_NOT_PROVED`.

M4 finds a genuine missing **relation/interface semantic** if Rawlsing currently jumps from objective actor-relative applicability to a subjective premise. But Holzkamp places the constitutive step on the subject side. Therefore M4 does not authorize D1/PRE-V3 to mint premises.

The frozen synthesis already routes the boundary toward ADP-017/CAN-008/010/011. M4 strengthens the need for that reconciliation and sharpens what it must preserve.

## 4. Does actual premise status require “epistemic ownership”?

**YES, WITH A QUALIFICATION.**

It requires subject-side first-person/phenomenal constitution. But Holzkampian `Prämissen` should not be reduced to explicit propositional knowledge. The source permits:

- partial or fragmented grasp;
- ideologically distorted grasp;
- discrepancy between real and phenomenal aspect;
- emotional evaluation;
- biographical constraints on what is available “for me.”

Therefore the Rawlsing receiver must be able to represent more than `ACTOR_KNOWS_FACT = true`.

Minimum V3 semantic requirement:

```text
OBJECTIVE FACT TRUTH
!= ACTOR ACCESS
!= PHENOMENAL GIVENNESS
!= PREMISE STATUS
!= REASON
```

ADP-017 may supply the access/knowledge firewall; premise formation may require composition with CAN-010/011/CAN-008 rather than a new owner.

## 5. Reasons court

The source requires:

```text
PREMISE
!= REASON
!= MOTIVE
!= DECISION
!= ACTION
!= SUCCESSFUL EFFECT
!= DURABLE CHANGE
```

### Premise != reason

A premise is an element/condition within the subject’s reason nexus. The reason is the intelligible relation that makes an action subjectively functional under those premises.

### Reason != action

A reason supports intelligibility, not deterministic production. Alternatives, refusal and changed interpretation remain possible.

### Motive != action

Motivation concerns the anticipated subjectively functional relation of action to conditions/interests. Action may still fail to occur; action may also occur coercively without motivated adoption.

### Action != successful effect

Other actors, institutions, material conditions and wider social relations mediate outcomes.

### Effect != durable change

A realized effect must still enter later history and be metabolized/developmentally preserved.

## 6. Structural determinism court

**FAIL for structural determinism.**

The strongest source passages are pp. 344–345 and pp. 367–369. Even a precise objective description of life conditions is insufficient to derive the action. Objective position does not directly determine the subjective possibility space.

Rawlsing prohibition:

```text
STRUCTURAL_POSITION
-> PSYCHOLOGICAL_STATE
```

is invalid without the mediation chain.

---

## 7. Voluntarism court

**FAIL for unconstrained voluntarism.**

Holzkamp repeatedly states that the dimensions and degree of actual possibilities are objectively historically conditioned. The subject’s freedom is a relation to concrete possibilities and limits, not arbitrary choice.

Rawlsing prohibition:

```text
SUBJECT_REASON_OR_WILL
-> ANY_POSSIBLE_ACTION_OR_WORLD_STATE
```

is invalid.

## 8. Source-derived negative ceilings for this court

1. `Bedeutung != subjective meaning`.
2. `objective action possibility != actual subjective possibility space`.
3. `PRE-V3 applicability != Prämisse`.
4. `Prämisse != explicit belief by definition`.
5. `reasonedness != causal necessity`.
6. `subjective functionality != objective success`.
7. `objective interest != motive`.
8. `recognized goal != accepted goal`.
9. `accepted goal != action`.
10. `coerced action != motivated action`.
11. `action != successful external effect`.
12. `external effect != durable development`.
13. the p. 355 “second possibility never zero” thesis is not a runtime guarantee of an executable alternative at every moment.
