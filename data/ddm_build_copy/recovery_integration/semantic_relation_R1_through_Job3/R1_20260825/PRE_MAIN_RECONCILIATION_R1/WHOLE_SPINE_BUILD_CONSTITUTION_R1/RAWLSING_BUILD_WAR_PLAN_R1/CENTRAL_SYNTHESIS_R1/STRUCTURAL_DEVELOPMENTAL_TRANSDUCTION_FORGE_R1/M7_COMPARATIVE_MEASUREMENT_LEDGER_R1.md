## 0. Court question

Can the United States and Poland be compared strongly enough to distinguish a portable mechanism from a system-specific institutional realization without collapsing either side into a country label?

**Verdict:** `YES, WITH CONSTRUCT-BY-CONSTRUCT EQUIVALENCE AND EXPLICIT HISTORICAL/INSTITUTIONAL QUALIFICATION`.

The book's comparison is not licensed by identical wording or identical social categories. It is licensed only where the authors establish:

```text
COMMON CONCEPTUAL TARGET
+ WITHIN-SYSTEM VALID OPERATIONALIZATION
+ TESTED MEASUREMENT RELATION
+ KNOWN DIFFERENCES RETAINED RATHER THAN ERASED
```

This is directly relevant to Rawlsing portability law. Surface identity is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## 1. Country, population, sample, and historical-time ledger

| Dimension | United States | Poland | Comparative consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline field period | 1964 | 1978 | Not contemporaneous; historical configuration must remain provenance. |
| Principal population | Civilian employed men, age 16+, working at least 25 hours/week | Urban civilian employed men, age 19-65, full-time | Comparability is bounded to employed men under different eligibility rules. |
| Baseline N | 3,101 | 1,557 | Both probability samples; unequal N is not itself a comparability defect. |
| Sampling frame | National area-probability design | Three-stage urban probability design: urban centers -> electoral districts -> voter registers | Polish rural peasantry omitted; actor ecology cannot be generalized to all Poles. |
| Major exclusions | Women; current unemployed; military | Women; rural peasantry; not-full-time employed; military/civilian exclusions | Strong negative ceiling on demographic/general-population portability. |
| Follow-up | 1974 panel subsample, N=687 men, plus wives/children | No comparable male panel; 1979-80 mother/child follow-up, N=177 families | Reciprocal causal evidence is asymmetrically strong. |
| Historical regime | Advanced capitalist labor market, decentralized firms, unions/civil service protections vary | Centralized state-socialist economy, nationalized employment, central planning, party/state allocation, recent labor-law equalization | `COUNTRY` must decompose into concrete institutional rules. |

### Measurement verdict

`MEASUREMENT COMPARABILITY: ADEQUATE FOR RELATIONSHIPS, NOT LICENSE FOR ABSOLUTE-LEVEL IDENTITY`.

The authors' principal comparisons concern relations among constructs, not an assumption that a raw score has the same absolute social meaning in both settings.

## 2. Data-collection comparability

The Polish survey was designed as a replication of the pertinent US instrument. The authors did **not** treat literal translation as sufficient.

They used:

- connotative translation rather than word-for-word translation;
- alternate translations and expert linguistic review;
- culturally analogous replacement situations where the original situation was not available or meaningful;
- pilot interviewing across high/low educational and occupational positions;
- revision where questions generated systematic incomprehension or formally improper responses;
- post-field coding and quality checks.

A classic example is replacing a US-specific hamburger-stand situation with a Polish kiosk analogue while preserving the intended social/cognitive demand.

**Rawlsing rule earned:**

```text
PORTABLE CONSTRUCT
!= IDENTICAL TOKENIZATION / SURFACE ITEM

PORTABLE CONSTRUCT
= PROVED FUNCTIONAL / RELATIONAL EQUIVALENCE
  WITH LOCAL VALIDITY RETAINED
```

## 3. Social class measurement

### 3.1 Common conceptual target

Class is a **relational/nominal** structure defined principally by:

- ownership/control of means of production;
- control over others' labor power;
- employment relation and organizational location.

It is deliberately kept analytically distinct from social stratification and from occupational self-direction.

### 3.2 US operationalization

- employers;
- self-employed;
- managers;
- first-line supervisors;
- nonmanual workers;
- manual workers.

Concrete discriminators include ownership share, number of non-family employees, number of hierarchical levels below, direct supervisory authority, and manual/nonmanual work.

### 3.3 Polish operationalization

- managers;
- first-line supervisors;
- nonmanual workers;
- production workers;
- nonproduction workers;
- self-employed.

Concrete discriminators include top decision position in state/cooperative enterprises, number/layers supervised, manual/nonmanual work, location in the centralized productive core versus secondary/support/service sectors, and private ownership under state-constrained conditions.

There is no Polish analogue of the US employer class with the same capitalist ownership relation.

### 3.4 Court verdict

`CONCEPT TRANSLATES; CATEGORY SET DOES NOT TRANSLATE ONE-FOR-ONE`.

This is a decisive M7 finding. A class category cannot be treated as an actor property with portable intrinsic consequences. The portable object is the **relation-generating rule**, while the concrete class map must be derived from the institutional configuration.

The source also provides an important anti-circularity rule: occupational autonomy/self-direction cannot be built into the class definition if self-direction is then to be tested as a mediator of class effects.

## 4. Social stratification measurement

Common latent target: hierarchical position in terms of power, privilege and prestige.

Indicators in both countries:

- educational attainment;
- occupational status/prestige;
- job income.

Confirmatory models support a comparable latent structure, but the indicator weights differ:

- US: education and occupational status are roughly co-dominant; income weaker;
- Poland: occupational status dominates more strongly; education is secondary; income remains weakest.

Some of this difference is associated with US longitudinal versus Polish cross-sectional measurement.

**Verdict:** `MECHANISM/CONSTRUCT TRANSLATES WITH DIFFERENTIAL INDICATOR WEIGHTS`.

No Rawlsing rule may reduce stratification to income, education, occupation, or a generic socioeconomic scalar when a concrete causal decomposition is required.

## 5. Occupational self-direction measurement

Common conceptual definition:

`USE OF INITIATIVE, THOUGHT, AND INDEPENDENT JUDGMENT IN WORK`.

The second-order construct is explicitly disaggregated.

### 5.1 Substantive complexity of work

Measures how much the actual work requires thought and independent judgment. Indicators draw on detailed descriptions of work with things, data/ideas and people, overall complexity, and time spent in relevant activities.

**Comparability:** strong. It is the dominant component of occupational self-direction in both countries.

### 5.2 Closeness of supervision

Indicators include:

- freedom to disagree with a supervisor;
- how closely one is supervised;
- whether the supervisor tells versus discusses;
- importance of doing what one is told.

**Comparability:** adequate, with an important substantive difference: supervision is more tightly related to class/stratification and relatively more important psychologically in Poland.

### 5.3 Routinization

Measures repetition/predictability and variability of work.

The surface index is not identical. Predictability contributed little to the Polish model and was not retained in exactly the US form.

**Verdict:** `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES AT THE LATENT CONSTRUCT LEVEL; SURFACE INDICATOR SET PARTLY DIFFERS`.

### 5.4 Second-order self-direction

The second-order models are similar enough for comparison; substantive complexity is the strongest component in both.

**Critical M7 distinction:**

```text
SAME OBSERVED LATENT CONSTRUCT
DOES NOT IMPLY
SAME COMPONENT MAGNITUDE OR SAME UPSTREAM INSTITUTIONAL PRODUCTION
```

The source proves exactly the distinction Rawlsing needs between portable mechanism family and historically organized instance.

## 6. Parental valuation of self-direction

Parents rank qualities desired for children. The central latent contrast is self-direction versus conformity to external authority.

The measurement model is made comparable through the same general ranking logic and Polish pretesting.

**Verdict:** `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES`.

But the **family pathway does not translate identically**: both parents matter in the US model; mothers dominate the Polish transmission model and the father's direct value-transmission path is not significant in the final Polish model.

## 7. Intellectual flexibility

The analysis focuses on the ideational component of intellectual flexibility, using performance and interviewer-based indicators. Culturally specific item surfaces are adapted where needed.

One relationship among perceptual and ideational indicators differs cross-nationally, but the authors retain the ideational construct as adequately comparable for the principal causal analyses.

**Verdict:** `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES FOR THE RETAINED IDEATIONAL TARGET; FULL PSYCHOLOGICAL BATTERY IS NOT IDENTICAL`.

## 8. Orientations to self and society

Shared first-order dimensions used in comparison:

- authoritarian conservatism;
- standards of morality;
- trustfulness;
- idea-conformity;
- self-confidence;
- self-deprecation;
- anxiety.

US analyses additionally had access to fatalism; the authors could not construct an adequate Polish fatalism measure.

### Court disposition

- common retained first-order dimensions: `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES`;
- fatalism: `MEASUREMENT DOES NOT TRANSLATE`;
- fatalism must therefore not be silently imputed to Poland or folded into a cross-national actor trait.

## 9. Second-order self-directedness and distress

The authors recover second-order dimensions of:

- self-directedness versus conformity;
- distress versus well-being.

The first-order loading relations supporting distress are sufficiently similar that the authors reject measurement artifact as the explanation for the substantive cross-national reversal.

But the relationship **between** self-directedness and distress differs:

- US: more self-direction tends to align with greater well-being;
- Poland: self-direction tends to align with more distress at the second-order orientation level.

**Verdict:** `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES; STRUCTURAL RELATION REVERSES/CHANGES`.

This is exactly why Rawlsing must not convert measurement invariance into mechanism invariance.

## 10. Distress and job-protection measurement asymmetry

The US follow-up includes an explicit job-protection index based on:

- seniority/contract/union/Civil Service or other job protection;
- formal grievance procedure;
- paid sick leave.

Comparable individual-level Polish job-protection measures are absent. The authors know from law/institutional history that nationalized-sector Polish workers had strong employment security, sick leave, vacations and related protections, but they cannot estimate the same individual-level mediation model in Poland.

**Verdict:**

```text
US JOB-PROTECTION MECHANISM: MEASURED
POLISH INSTITUTIONAL PROTECTION CONFIGURATION: HISTORICALLY DOCUMENTED
CROSS-SYSTEM INDIVIDUAL MEDIATION: EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE
```

Rawlsing must preserve this distinction. Institutional provenance is not a substitute for an observed actor-level mediator when the claimed causal grain is actor-level mediation.

## 11. Education measurement and timing

Education is measured in both systems and contributes to stratification and occupational self-direction.

The substantive timing differs:

- US: most men had completed formal education before baseline;
- Poland: continued formal education during occupational careers was substantially more common.

The authors tie the Polish pattern to post-war industrialization, shortage of technically educated personnel, state job allocation, and education as a formal credentialing rule.

**Verdict:** `MEASUREMENT TRANSLATES; CAREER-TIMING MECHANISM IS SYSTEM-QUALIFIED`.

No Rawlsing derivation may treat “education” as one timeless dose independent of when, why, and under what allocation rules it occurs.

## 12. Family measurement and linked lives

US family analyses use a larger family sample and longitudinal information; the Polish family study consists of fathers from the 1978 baseline and mothers/children interviewed roughly 1.5-2 years later.

The authors sometimes approximate the Polish family measurements as contemporaneous in causal modelling.

**Verdict:** `LINKED-LIFE RELATION MEASURABLE; TEMPORAL PRECISION UNEQUAL AND LIMITED`.

The studies are restricted to families meeting the sampling design, especially intact families with children in the relevant age range. No generalization to all family forms is licensed.

## 13. Temporality measurement court

### US men

Two principal observations, 1964 and 1974, support real longitudinal causal modelling.

### Poland

Cross-sectional baseline plus retrospective occupational information and simulated/quasi-longitudinal psychological models.

### Family

Mixed timing; Polish parent/child sequence is only partially longitudinal for the modeled constructs.

### Ceiling

The source explicitly cannot establish:

- exact onset of effects;
- whether effects are continuous, episodic, thresholded or delayed;
- whether effects taper, accumulate or oscillate;
- full career sequences;
- event-history timing;
- clean separation of age, cohort, career and historical-period effects.

**Rawlsing negative rule:**

```text
CROSS-SECTIONAL OR TWO-WAVE ASSOCIATION
!= DEVELOPMENTAL TRAJECTORY PROOF
```

and

```text
NET TEN-YEAR EFFECT
!= KNOWN RECURRENCE MICRO-TIMING
```

## 14. Behavioral, meaning, motive, and uptake measurement

The book explicitly lacks general behavioral measures beyond interview behavior. It does not directly observe the microprocess by which occupational experience becomes value, cognitive flexibility or orientation.

It therefore does not directly measure:

- moment-to-moment uptake;
- actor meaning-making during work;
- motive formation;
- reason/grounds construction;
- repeated conscious appraisal;
- action strategy as a general outcome;
- actor perception accuracy for objective work conditions.

The authors infer learning/generalization from robust reciprocal longitudinal relationships, but chapter 9 treats the microdynamics as an unresolved research problem requiring observation, experiments, or denser longitudinal measurement.

**Verdict:** `PSYCHOLOGICAL OUTCOME RELATIONS ARE MEASURED; SUBJECT-MEDIATION MICROPROCESS IS NOT`.

## 15. Comparative-measurement portability law

Rawlsing should preserve:

```text
PORTABLE_MECHANISM_CANDIDATE
requires
  conceptual equivalence
  + within-context measurement validity
  + actor-relative concrete mediator
  + explicit historical/institutional provenance
  + temporal/evidence ceiling

SURFACE_ITEM_IDENTITY is neither necessary nor sufficient.
```

Where a measure fails to translate, record `MEASUREMENT_DOES_NOT_TRANSLATE` rather than backfilling from theory, country stereotypes, or neighboring variables.
