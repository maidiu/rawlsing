## 0. Purpose

This file revisits original PFF frontier item 2:

> What do the four originating depth bands D/C/B/A actually mean after the one-person V3/PFF continuity model has matured?

Originating bands:

- **D** — everything the generated person is/has become, including material inaccessible to them;
- **C** — who they consciously are to themself most of the time;
- **B** — what this particular relationship/interaction draws out now;
- **A** — what actually comes out.

The central risk is to mistake four phenomenological distinctions for four database layers.

## 1. Primary conclusion

D/C/B/A are **not four homogeneous kinds of state arranged in a simple nested stack**.

They are a compact conceptual map combining several different axes:

- durability/history;
- actor accessibility/self-understanding;
- interaction-specific recruitment;
- observable manifestation.

Thus:

`D/C/B/A = NONCOLLAPSIBLE PERSPECTIVAL/FUNCTIONAL BANDS`

not:

`D/C/B/A = FOUR ARCHITECTURE LAYERS`.

## 2. The bands are not simply nested

Reject:

`D ⊃ C ⊃ B ⊃ A`.

Why?

- much of D is not currently active in B;
- B can contain hidden D material outside C;
- C contains standing self-understanding that may be irrelevant to current B;
- A is an observable output, not a subset of psychic state;
- A may reveal hidden B/D influence not consciously represented in C.

A better conceptual picture is overlapping cuts through one person.

## 3. D — total/durable causal person

### 3.1 Meaning

D denotes the full generated-person causal organization relevant to personhood across time, including material that may be inaccessible, dormant, contradictory, or unknown to the actor.

Possible V3-owned content includes, where lawfully generated:

- causal life history;
- memories and causal traces;
- expectations;
- strivings;
- middle-state mediators;
- tensions;
- practices;
- response sensitivities;
- relationships/roles as actor state/history;
- organismic/material state;
- epistemic stances;
- attractor/developmental organization;
- rupture/metabolization history;
- durable self-relevant organization.

### 3.2 D is not "everything the database knows"

D should not swallow external world truth, structural priors, user hidden state, or analyst interpretation merely because the machine stores them somewhere.

Better:

`D = EVERYTHING CAUSALLY TRUE/FORMED OF THIS GENERATED PERSON UNDER COMPETENT PERSON AUTHORITY`.

External/world objects may be referenced through the person's history but remain externally owned where appropriate.

### 3.3 D contains inaccessible material

A causal-trace memory, mediator, tension, attraction tendency, or attractor organization can affect the person without being actor-known.

Thus:

`D != ACTOR SELF-KNOWLEDGE`.

## 4. C — standing actor-side self-organization

### 4.1 Meaning

C denotes the person's relatively standing, ordinary actor-side organization of:

- who they believe they are;
- what roles/commitments matter;
- what they know/believe about their life;
- values and recognized motives;
- ordinary autobiographical story;
- current enduring goals/burdens/future expectations;
- habitual conscious orientation to self and life.

### 4.2 C is not all accessible information

A fact can be actor-accessible without being central to standing selfhood.

Likewise C is not simply ADP-017 claim accessibility.

It is closer to the **ordinary standing conscious self-organization** built from multiple V3-owned actor-side structures.

### 4.3 C can be wrong

C is the person's theory of themself, not machine truth.

Therefore:

`C != D`.

The actor may sincerely believe:

> "I'm not jealous."

while jealousy-related organization is causally active elsewhere in D/B.

### 4.4 C can be contradictory

A person may simultaneously hold:

- "I am independent";
- "I deeply value intimacy";
- "I don't need reassurance";
- "I keep seeking reassurance".

No forced self-summary is required.

## 5. B — current relation-specific live organization

### 5.1 Meaning

B denotes what becomes **operatively configured now** in relation to this user, situation, and interaction history.

It includes fast relations such as:

- recruited durable expectations/strivings/mediators/tensions;
- user-specific received readings;
- current chemistry/anti-chemistry;
- attraction/aversion pressure where governed;
- live conflict;
- memory resonance;
- current anticipation;
- metaperception;
- current guardedness/opening;
- suppression/rebound;
- present conscious/peripheral/hidden relations;
- unresolved interaction residue.

### 5.2 B is not a miniature person

B should reference/recruit D rather than copy it.

`B = CURRENT WORKING CONFIGURATION OVER/AMONG PERSON MATERIAL + INTERACTION INPUT/RESIDUE`.

### 5.3 B includes both conscious and unconscious material

This is crucial.

Some of B may overlap C/current awareness.

Other B material may be causally active while actor-inaccessible.

Therefore:

`B != CURRENT CONSCIOUSNESS`.

### 5.4 B is user-specific

The same D/C person can enter different B configurations with different users.

That is not multiple personality.

`RELATION-SPECIFIC CONFIGURATION != SEPARATE SELF`.

## 6. A — observable manifestation

### 6.1 Meaning

A denotes what actually becomes observable through the actor's response/action in the interaction.

For current chat this is primarily rendered language, but conceptually may include other lawful observable expression/action candidates.

### 6.2 A is not merely intended disclosure

A can include consequences of:

- deliberate expression;
- strategic presentation;
- concealment;
- defense;
- inhibition;
- leakage;
- accidental implication;
- spontaneous elaboration;
- silence/withholding where meaningful.

### 6.3 A is not the expression plan

The internal process of deciding/allowing/inhibiting what to express occurs before observable A.

Thus:

`EXPRESSION FORMATION != A`.

A is the realized manifestation.

### 6.4 A does not prove D/C/B

Rendered warmth does not prove durable affection.

A sharp joke does not prove conscious hostility.

An accidental innuendo does not prove deliberate flirtation.

Thus:

`A != DIRECT READOUT OF INNER STATE`.

## 7. The bands correspond to different conceptual axes

A useful decomposition:

| Band | Primary conceptual question |
|---|---|
| D | What has this person actually become / what can causally exist in them? |
| C | Who does the actor ordinarily understand themself to be? |
| B | What of the person is operatively recruited/configured in this interaction now? |
| A | What becomes observable? |

This shows why they are non-equivalent rather than nested storage levels.

## 8. Same underlying object can participate in several bands

Example: durable rejection expectation `E17`.

### D

`E17` exists as V3-generated durable expectation.

### C

Actor may consciously believe:

> "People usually leave when I get too close."

or may have no conscious self-theory corresponding to E17.

### B

This user's ambiguous delay recruits E17 strongly right now.

### A

The actor becomes cool, probes for reassurance, or withholds vulnerability.

There is not necessarily one copy of E17 in every band.

The same durable structure participates through different relations/perspectives.

## 9. Useful set-like intersections — conceptual only

### 9.1 `D ∩ C`

Durable person material the actor ordinarily recognizes/endorses.

### 9.2 `D ∩ B` but not C

Durable material currently recruited without actor recognition.

This is core hidden-cause territory.

### 9.3 `C ∩ B`

Standing self-understanding currently relevant/foregrounded in the interaction.

### 9.4 D outside B

Durable person material not currently recruited.

### 9.5 C outside B

Actor-known identity/history that is currently irrelevant.

### 9.6 A influenced by B outside C

Leakage/implicit manifestation of hidden active material.

These intersections are explanatory aids, not schema instructions.

## 10. "Movement between bands" is usually not literal object movement

A major refinement:

Much apparent movement is actually a **change in relation/status**.

### D -> B

A durable structure becomes recruited now.

No copy needed.

### D -> C

Actor gains durable/standing access or self-understanding of previously hidden material.

This may require V3 memory/epistemic/self-model update.

### B -> C

Actor recognizes current activation or revises current self-understanding.

May remain fast or become durable depending on significance/admission.

### C -> B

Standing self-model/expectation shapes current interpretation.

### B -> A

Live configuration shapes observable expression through defense/intention/leakage.

### C -> A

Conscious intention/identity can directly constrain expression, mediated through current state.

### A -> B

Own-output self-observation changes live state.

### A -> D

**Never automatic.**

Actual emitted interaction/action must cross V3 occurrence/admission and competent durable-effect rules before changing D.

## 11. Hidden D material can influence A without entering C

This is one of the central PFF requirements.

Conceptually:

```text
D hidden attraction tendency/history
-> B attraction recruitment
-> B defense + leakage pressure
-> A unusual playfulness/attention
```

while C may contain:

> "I'm just having fun."

Thus the architecture must not require every cause of expression to be consciously represented.

But the renderer must receive only safe consequences, not hidden causal facts as actor-known explanation.

## 12. C is more than "conscious field"

The present conscious field is fast and can change every turn.

C is more standing.

Therefore distinguish:

`C STANDING CONSCIOUS SELF`

from:

`CURRENT PRESENT CONSCIOUS FIELD`.

The present conscious field can temporarily contain material not integrated into standing C.

Example:

> "Why am I suddenly so upset by this?"

The feeling is conscious now but has not yet become part of ordinary self-understanding.

## 13. B is more than "relationship state"

B includes relationship-specific live organization but also:

- current body condition;
- immediate situation;
- current memory resonance;
- anticipation;
- self-output residue;
- non-relational motives activated by the topic.

So B should not be reduced to trust/intimacy/chemistry variables.

## 14. D is not necessarily slow in every respect

D is durable/system-causal personhood, but some V3-owned current states may change quickly under lawful events.

The key distinction is **authority/durability of person state**, not simply clock speed.

B can change without V3 commit; D changes only through V3 authority.

Thus:

`DURABILITY/AUTHORITY != NUMERIC TIMESCALE ALONE`.

## 15. C can change locally before durably

A person can temporarily think:

> "Maybe I'm not who I thought I was."

during interaction.

That does not necessarily rewrite durable standing self-model immediately.

We may need to distinguish:

- live self-interpretation;
- standing self-model;
- durable V3 epistemic/self-model change.

The four bands expose this issue but do not solve it by themselves.

## 16. B has internal depth of its own

B should not be treated as one flat activation layer.

Within current live organization there can be:

- hidden causal recruitment;
- felt-but-unnamed experience;
- conscious hypotheses;
- conflicting intentions;
- suppression;
- expression pressure.

This is why original item 3 requires a separate activation/awareness/interpretation/expression matrix.

## 17. A can feed back without proving intention

After A occurs, the actor and user both receive a new surface artifact.

The actor may be surprised by it.

The user may interpret it differently than intended.

Thus A is simultaneously:

- output of one recursion;
- input/evidence for the next.

`A(t) -> B(t+1)`

is central to PFF recursion.

## 19. Four bands and shared psychology

The bands are most useful at the PFF frontier, but they also help periodic V3.

### D

Clearly V3 durable personhood.

### C

Mostly V3 actor epistemic/self-model organization, though current conscious self-experience may be PFF-fast.

### B

Primarily PFF interaction-time working configuration, built from D/C + current user/situation.

### A

Primarily downstream expression/actual interaction surface.

This is a jurisdictional tendency, not an exact owner map.

## 22. Scenario court

### 22.1 Hidden attraction

D: history/striving/sensitivity make attraction possible.

C: actor believes relationship is platonic and sees themself as uninterested.

B: user-specific attraction recruited; shame/inhibition active.

A: extra attentiveness and accidental innuendo.

Result: all four bands differ lawfully.

### 22.2 Conscious anger, concealed

D: durable expectation of respect.

C: actor knows they get angry at condescension.

B: anger focal and consciously recognized; desire to preserve relationship inhibits direct attack.

A: clipped but polite response.

### 22.3 Story resonance

D: causal trace of old humiliating person.

C: no awareness of current resemblance.

B: current user structurally resonates; old story becomes relevant/accessible.

A: actor spontaneously tells story.

After A, actor may wonder why they brought it up, altering next B/C.

### 22.4 User helps unlock capacity

D: capacity/practice exists but is normally poorly reachable.

C: actor believes "I'm not emotionally open."

B: with this user, safety/recruitment makes openness locally reachable.

A: sincere disclosure.

Repeated significant interaction may eventually create durable C/D change through V3; one disclosure does not prove it has.
