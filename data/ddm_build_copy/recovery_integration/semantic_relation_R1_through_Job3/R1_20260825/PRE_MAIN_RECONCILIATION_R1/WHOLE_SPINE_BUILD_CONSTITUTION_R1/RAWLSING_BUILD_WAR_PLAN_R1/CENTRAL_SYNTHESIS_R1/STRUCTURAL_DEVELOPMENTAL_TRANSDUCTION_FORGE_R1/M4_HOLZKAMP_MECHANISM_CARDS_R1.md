## M4-H01 — OBJECTIVE_SOCIAL_WORLD_TO_MEANING_STRUCTURE_MEDIATION

**SOURCE:** Ch. 5, pp. 189–203; especially pp. 191–193.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION / CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** socially produced objective material-economic relations, division of labor, social relations.

**MECHANISM:** the objective social world is produced historically and confronts the individual as supra-individual objective reality; individual-science categories must mediate between objective-material and psychic determinations.

**OUTPUT:** objective societal meanings/action relations that can become individually relevant without yet being psychic states.

**REALIZATION LAW:** none at this level. The structural world can exist without being subjectively realized by a particular actor.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** explicitly downstream.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** historical condition/relation.

**RECIPROCITY:** the individual’s realized activity may participate in reproduction/change of social relations, but this card does not specify the gate.

**BOUNDARY:** the social world is not a primary psychic fact.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** supports PRE-V3/V3 separation and need for a positive seam; does not license direct `STRUCTURE -> PERSON_STATE`.

**D1/D2/D3:** supports D1 problem existence; background support for D3.

## M4-H02 — SOCIETAL_MEANING_TO_OBJECTIVE_ACTION_POSSIBILITY

**SOURCE:** Ch. 6, pp. 229–238; especially pp. 233–237.

**STATUS:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** objective societal meaning/action structures (`Bedeutungsstrukturen`, `Bedeutungs-/Handlungszusammenhänge`).

**MECHANISM:** socially produced meanings confront the individual as societal action/thought possibilities.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** possible actions, constraints, thought forms, practical routes.

**REALIZATION LAW:** objective possibility does not determine action; the actor may not act or may act otherwise.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** conscious relation-to (`bewußtes Verhalten-Zu`) is required for specifically subjective realization.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** possibility/condition.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `BEDEUTUNG != PREMISE != ACTION`.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** PRE-V3 may own objective possibility; D1 may qualify it to the actor, but must not mint a subjective premise.

**D1:** strong support.

## M4-H03 — POSITION_LIFE_SITUATION_INFRASTRUCTURE_SPECIFICATION

**SOURCE:** Ch. 5 pp. 195–203; Ch. 7 pp. 356–367, especially pp. 358–361.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`; concrete contents require `SINGLE_THEORY/CURRENT_EMPIRICAL` research.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** historically specific societal organization plus the actor’s actual `Position` and `Lebenslage`.

**MECHANISM:** total societal meaning structures differentiate, from the actor’s life situation, into `Infrastrukturen` and position-specific `Bedeutungskonstellationen` presenting particular action/thought possibilities.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** different work actions, institutional possibilities, relational forms, norms, obligations, access paths and restrictions depending on concrete position/life situation.

**REALIZATION LAW:** these are still objective action/thought possibilities; p. 359 expressly postpones subjective realization.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** none yet.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** condition/relation; historically variable.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** formation, institution, concrete position, life situation, available meaning relations.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** class/category labels do not themselves derive the concrete possibility ecology.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** direct source support for D1’s position/context typed derivation. However Holzkamp does not provide a complete mechanism registry; exact institutional outputs require additional theory/empiry.

**D1:** `SURVIVES_WITH_NARROWING`.

## M4-H04 — OBJECTIVE_POSITIONED_POSSIBILITY_TO_SUBJECTIVE_POSSIBILITY_SPACE

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 367–369.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION / CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** position/life-situation-specific meaning constellations and restrictions.

**MECHANISM:** such objective constellations matter for the subject only in the way they are situational-personally given (`situational-personal gegeben`) in their phenomenal aspect (`phänomenaler Aspekt`), with the personal functional basis shaped by prior realizations/nonrealizations.

**OUTPUT:** actual subjective possibility space (`subjektiver Möglichkeitsraum`).

**REALIZATION LAW:** objective life position does not directly determine the subjective possibility space; the actor can lag behind objective possibilities or misjudge them.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** first-person/phenomenal availability plus biography.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** current state conditioned by biography; recurrent requalification.

**RECIPROCITY:** prior actions/realisations alter later personal basis.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** PRE-V3 applicability is not the same object as a Holzkampian subjective possibility space.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** this arrow belongs at the D1→V3 boundary. D1 can emit premise-relevant actor-relative social facts; actual `subjektiver Möglichkeitsraum` requires V3-side phenomenal/epistemic ownership.

**D1:** sharp boundary constraint.

**D2:** supports history-sensitive rebinding/requalification.

## M4-H05 — LIFE_CONDITION_TO_PREMISE_IN_PHENOMENAL_ASPECT

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 351–356; especially pp. 352–353; pp. 367–369.

**STATUS:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** historically specific external conditions and personal/biographical conditions.

**MECHANISM:** conditions become premises (`Prämissen`) of subjective ground/reason contexts only as subjectively given in the phenomenal aspect: experienced as situation, capacities, past/future orientation, emotionally evaluated, etc.

**OUTPUT:** premise set for a subjective reason/ground nexus.

**REALIZATION LAW:** objective truth, eligibility or accessibility is insufficient; subject-side givenness is required. The premise need not be a fully explicit discursive proposition.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** constitutive.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** current subject situation with historical depth.

**BOUNDARY:** real and phenomenal aspects may diverge.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `OBJECTIVE APPLICABILITY != PRÄMISSE`.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** a premise relation is genuinely required as interface semantics, but M4 does not authorize PRE-V3/D1 to own actual premise state. Reconcile with V3 ADP-017/CAN-008/010/011 region.

**D4:** not independently licensed by source; owner question remains downstream reconciliation.

## M4-H06 — PREMISE_TO_SUBJECTIVE_GROUND_REASON_NEXUS

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 348–356.

**STATUS:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** premises in the medium of objective meaning/action/thought structures.

**MECHANISM:** the subject’s action is not merely externally conditioned (`Bedingtheit`); it is grounded/reasoned (`Begründetheit`) relative to the subject’s experienced needs/life interests and available alternatives.

**OUTPUT:** subjective grounds/reasons (`subjektive Handlungsgründe`) and a reason nexus (`Begründungszusammenhang`).

**REALIZATION LAW:** grounds make action intelligible, not mechanically necessary.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** central.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** decision/orientation process.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** grounds can be objectively mistaken or based on distorted/partial premises.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** reason formation is V3 subject mediation, not social-ecology derivation.

## M4-H07 — REASON_MOTIVATION_COERCION_ACTION_SEPARATION

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 315–324 and pp. 348–355.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**INPUT:** societal goal/action possibility, subject cognition, life-interest relation, premise/ground configuration.

**MECHANISM:** motivated adoption requires more than objective availability. Recognition may be accurate or distorted; a recognized goal can be rejected; action can also occur under coercion (`Zwang`) against longer-run interests to avoid immediate threat.

**OUTPUT:** possible motivated orientation, refusal, coerced action, or no action.

**REALIZATION LAW:** no deterministic reason→action rule.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:**

```text
PREMISE != REASON
REASON != INTENTION
INTENTION != ACTION
ACTION != SUCCESSFUL EFFECT
```

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** existing V3 action/response owners must preserve optionality and coercion; D1/D3 cannot infer action from opportunity.

## M4-H08 — RESTRICTIVE_VS_GENERALIZED_ACTION_CAPACITY_AS_SITUATIONAL_ALTERNATIVE

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 370–402.

**STATUS:** `CONCEPTUAL_DEFINITION / THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** concrete threat/restriction/dependency within a subjective possibility space.

**MECHANISM:** under some premises the subject may seek to secure action-capacity within existing power relations (`restriktive Handlungsfähigkeit`); under others the subject may pursue expansion of common control over conditions (`verallgemeinerte Handlungsfähigkeit`).

**OUTPUT:** direction of situated action/relations.

**REALIZATION LAW:** alternative arises anew in relevant situations; inconsistent movement, reversals and regression remain possible.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** grounds/reasons and perceived realizability.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** recurrent situational alternative, not fixed developmental stage.

**RECIPROCITY:** restrictive action may reproduce dependence; generalized action may contribute to changed conditions if collective route succeeds.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** explicitly not a personality trait, group type, ideology type or moral score.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** retain only decomposed situational mechanism; reject theory-name/agency-state object.

## M4-H09 — ASSOCIATION_COUNTERPOWER_TO_EXPANDED_CONDITION_CONTROL

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 353–355, 371–375, 395–401; Ch. 8 where applicable.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION / PROGRAMMATIC_CLAIM`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** constrained individual possibility space and objective possibilities for cooperation/association.

**MECHANISM:** expansion of control over conditions often requires joining with others (`Zusammenschluß`), possibly organized, development of counter-power (`Gegenmacht`), means, alliance base and temporal strategy.

**OUTPUT:** increased practical possibility of changing conditions.

**REALIZATION LAW:** shared interest or abstract possibility does not produce collective efficacy automatically. Association, resources, coordination and external relations must be real.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** actors must have subjectively grounded reasons to participate; there is no supra-individual collective mind.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** recurrent practice/collective process.

**RECIPROCITY:** core.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** collective action does not guarantee successful structural change.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** strongly supports D3’s requirement for collective/institutional intermediate gates, while the exact gate schema is Rawlsing’s own architectural inference.

## M4-H10 — ACTION_TO_LATER_POSSIBILITY_SPACE_FEEDBACK

**SOURCE:** Ch. 7 pp. 377–378; p. 322; broader pp. 370–401.

**STATUS:** `THEORETICAL_DERIVATION`.

**INPUT:** realized action/relationship strategy.

**MECHANISM:** actions alter relational conditions that constitute later possibilities. Holzkamp’s clearest example: instrumentalizing/isolation may yield short-term control yet reduce long-term cooperation and narrow the future possibility for shared condition-control.

**OUTPUT:** later altered relation/support/cooperation possibility and subjective possibility space.

**REALIZATION LAW:** the effect is mediated through what actually happens in relations/social conditions, not by intention alone.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** cumulative/recursive.

**RECIPROCITY:** explicit.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** this is not permission for a person state to directly rewrite PRE-V3 structure.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** D3 survives. Rawlsing’s provenance-preserving external-action→response/gate→new social fact protocol is an architectural discipline not named by Holzkamp.

## M4-H15 — CONDITION_RESPONSE_SHORTCUT_REJECTION

**SOURCE:** Ch. 9 pp. 522–545; pp. 568–573.

**STATUS:** `METHODOLOGICAL_CLAIM`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** externally described condition or experimental/statistical variable.

**MECHANISM CRITIQUE:** a variable-model short circuit from externally set conditions to activities/reactions erases objective meanings, subject grounds and possibility relations.

**POSITIVE QUALIFICATION:** condition/statistical models may still be appropriate for bounded factual constraints beyond current control; this does not universalize conditionedness as the form of all human action.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** evidence of association/constraint does not constitute a complete action explanation.

**RAWLSING INFERENCE:** retain PRE-V3 statistics/structural pressure as evidence, but require D1 + V3 subject mediation for action explanations.

## 2. D1/D2/D3 source pressure

### D1

`SURVIVES_WITH_NARROWING`.

Holzkamp strongly supports an explicit move from objective societal structures through exact life situation/position to objective actor-relative action/thought possibilities. But the source itself prohibits equating that product with `subjektiver Möglichkeitsraum` or `Prämisse`, and explicitly leaves the exact contents of institutional/positional possibility structures to further theory/current empirical work.

### D2

`SURVIVES_AND_GAINS_HISTORY_SENSITIVE_REQUALIFICATION_SUPPORT`.

The source adds biography, prior realization/nonrealization, recurrent threat situations, development/stagnation/regression and institutionally reconfigured meaning environments. It does not by itself prove a new transition carrier or an atomic rebinding implementation.

### D3

`SURVIVES_WITH_EXPLICIT_COLLECTIVE_AND_EXTERNAL_EFFECT_GATES`.

Holzkamp directly supports participation in reproduction/change of social conditions and gives recursive examples. But intention or collective association does not equal successful world change. Rawlsing must preserve realized external action, counterpart/institution/collective response, causal admission and provenance.
