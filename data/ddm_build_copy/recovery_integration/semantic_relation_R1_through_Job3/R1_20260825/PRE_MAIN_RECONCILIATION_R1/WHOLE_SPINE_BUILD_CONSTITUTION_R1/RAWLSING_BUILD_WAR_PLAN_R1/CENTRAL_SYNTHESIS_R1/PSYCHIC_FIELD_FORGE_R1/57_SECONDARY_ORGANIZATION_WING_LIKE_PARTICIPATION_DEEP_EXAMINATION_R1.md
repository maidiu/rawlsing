## 1. Executive holding

The strongest result is:

> **The psychological phenomenon survives, but it does not presently justify a new ontological kind called a wing or secondary personality.**

V3/PFF already possesses or intends the relevant psychological material from which more than one recurrent organization can arise:

- multiple expectations and strivings;
- multiple persistent mediators;
- multiple tensions and practices;
- memory and self-model organization;
- attractor history;
- simultaneous response streams without forced scalar winner-selection;
- context-sensitive recruitment;
- relationship-specific recurrence;
- condition, correction, capture and recovery;
- history-conditioned gravity.

What is not yet cleanly constituted is the **longitudinal relation among recurrent organizations** when one remains broadly dominant while another repeatedly and coherently participates in how the same person receives, pursues, defends, interprets, repairs, and expresses themselves.

The smallest missing statement is therefore relational:

```text
HISTORY_STABILIZED_INTER_ORGANIZATION_PARTICIPATION
```

Phenomenon-level definition:

> A history-earned, context-sensitive relation in which a recurrent organization that is not broadly dominant remains reliably available across time and, under some perturbations or relationships, is co-recruited with, modulates, competes with, sequences with, or locally overrides aspects of another recurrent organization in ways that systematically change the same person's realization without replacing that person's recursive identity.

This is deliberately a **relation claim**, not an architecture proposal.

Primary disposition:

```text
SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_AS_NEW_ONTOLOGICAL_KIND
= REJECT

STABLE_SECONDARY_PARTICIPATION_PHENOMENON
= ALREADY_PRESENT__RELATION_MISSING
```

Under the project disposition vocabulary, the remaining gap is **not yet `TRUE_SEMANTIC_REMAINDER` requiring a new noun**. Existing nouns appear adequate. The missing semantic work is the durable participation/modulation relation among them.

## 2. Remove the theory label first

The Enneagram term `wing` carries several claims that must be separated before Rawlsing can use any of them.

Possible source-theory claims include:

1. a person has a primary organization;
2. a neighboring organization can persistently influence it;
3. the influence can vary in strength;
4. the secondary organization can complement, counterbalance, intensify, or redirect aspects of the primary;
5. the secondary organization may itself appear in more or less flexible/constricted forms;
6. only diagram-adjacent organizations count as wings;
7. usually one or both adjacent organizations are relevant;
8. a compact label such as `5w4` usefully summarizes the person.

These are not one phenomenon.

After removing the Enneagram label, the psychologically serious claim is narrower:

> **One recursively continuous person may develop more than one recurrently coherent organization, with unequal breadth or return tendency, and a subordinate organization may repeatedly participate in the realization of a more dominant one.**

That claim is worth preserving independently of whether adjacency, wing naming, or nine-family topology survives.

Therefore:

```text
WING_THEORY
!=
STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_PHENOMENON
```

and:

```text
STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_PHENOMENON
!=
ADJACENT_WING_RESTRICTION
```

## 3. One person can contain multiple recurrent organizations without containing multiple persons

The central anti-collapse law is:

```text
MULTIPLE_RECURRENT_ORGANIZATIONS
!=
MULTIPLE_PERSONS
```

A person is already allowed to contain:

- contradictory expectations;
- incompatible strivings;
- mutually reinforcing or inhibiting mediators;
- unresolved tensions;
- several learned practices;
- memories with differing accessibility and significance;
- self-model claims that are partial or internally inconsistent;
- several simultaneous causal response streams;
- relationship-specific patterns;
- alternate routes that become more or less available with condition.

A recurrent organization is therefore not a little person inside the person.

It is a **coherent recurring organization of relations among person-state and person-event processes**.

A secondary recurrent organization does not need:

- its own identity;
- its own biography;
- its own consciousness;
- its own memory store;
- its own renderer;
- its own goal system;
- its own independent developmental clock.

It is still this one person's developed psychology.

Hard law:

```text
ONE_PERSON
CAN_HAVE
MULTIPLE_COHERENT_RECURRENT_ORGANIZATIONS
WITHOUT_IDENTITY_FISSION
```

## 4. Primary and secondary are comparative roles, not ontological classes

The words `primary` and `secondary` risk implying two different state kinds.

That is not required.

A better reading is comparative:

### 4.1 Broadly dominant organization

A recurrent organization is broadly dominant when, relative to alternatives, its history-conditioned relations have greater **generalized organizing reach and return tendency** across sufficiently varied perturbations and contexts.

This does not mean it is always active.

It does not mean it wins every conflict.

It does not mean every relationship expresses it equally.

### 4.2 Stable secondary organization

A recurrent organization is secondary when it has less broad organizing reach than the dominant organization but nevertheless shows:

- history-earned recurrence;
- coherent cross-domain relations;
- nontrivial return tendency under some perturbations;
- future-predictive consequences beyond surface style;
- stable ability to alter another organization's realization;
- persistence beyond one mood, role, turn, or isolated episode.

Thus:

```text
PRIMARY_ORGANIZATION
AND
SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
=
COMPARATIVE_POSITIONS_WITHIN_ONE_PERSON_SPECIFIC_REALIZED_GEOMETRY
```

not:

```text
TWO_DIFFERENT_KINDS_OF_PSYCHIC_OBJECT
```

This also means rank can be conditional.

An organization that is secondary in broad cross-context reach may become locally dominant in one relationship or domain without becoming the person's new global primary organization.

## 5. Existing V3/PFF coverage is already extensive

The wing question initially looked as though it might require a new concept because ordinary personality shorthand tends to represent a person as one dominant type.

Rawlsing's existing conceptual direction is already much less compressive.

### 5.1 Expectations

V3 already permits sparse, historically updateable, contradictory expectation records rather than one global expectation posture.

A secondary organization may therefore draw on a recurrent constellation of expectations without needing a secondary expectation store.

### 5.2 Strivings

V3 already permits multiple simultaneous and incompatible durable strivings without forced winner-selection.

A secondary organization may alter which striving becomes salient, how a striving is pursued, or what failure means without needing a second motivational system.

### 5.3 Mediators

V3 mediators already permit multiplicity, contradiction, dormancy/reactivation and historically formed interpretive/protective influence.

This is a strong existing home for much of what makes one recurrent organization differ from another.

### 5.4 Tensions

Persistent unresolved tensions already allow opposing tendencies to coexist without a single winner.

A secondary organization may participate by repeatedly recruiting one side, reorganizing the conflict, or making a different route accessible.

### 5.5 Practices

Practices already preserve history-earned response capacities/routes.

A secondary organization may therefore be partly manifested through a stable alternate practice repertoire without requiring a wing-specific action library.

### 5.6 Memory and causal traces

History can shape current response without explicit episodic retrieval.

A secondary organization may therefore be conditioned by distributed history rather than a dedicated memory bundle.

### 5.7 Self-model

The actor's self-model can be partial, contradictory and context-specific.

A secondary organization may systematically make different self-understandings more accessible without becoming a second self.

### 5.8 CAN-008 response-stream preservation

The continuity work already preserves simultaneous causal streams, transformation, inhibition/amplification/rerouting, and no default scalar net-pressure winner.

That is important because stable secondary participation cannot be constituted if the system prematurely collapses person-event processing to one winning organization.

### 5.9 PFF situated recruitment

PFF already needs context-sensitive recruitment of durable person psychology.

A stable secondary organization can therefore be weakly available most of the time and strongly recruited by one event, relationship, anticipated consequence, memory resonance, or current condition.

### 5.10 Attractor gravity, condition and capture

Banks `54`–`56` already distinguish:

```text
ATTRACTOR_GRAVITY
!=
CURRENT_RECRUITMENT
!=
CAPTURE
!=
MOOD
```

and already preserve multi-organization participation.

The nouns are therefore substantially present.

The missing part is how **persistent subordinate participation itself** is recognized across history without becoming one-hot type competition.

## 6. The missing relation — stable participation is more than coexistence

Mere multiplicity is insufficient.

A person can contain many mediators, tensions, practices and strivings without those elements composing a stable secondary recurrent organization.

Likewise, two organizations can both exist in the person's developmental possibility space without one materially modifying the other's realization.

The stronger relation requires at least the phenomenon-level possibility of:

```text
ORGANIZATION A
= broader dominant return organization

ORGANIZATION B
= less broad but recurrent history-earned organization

B PARTICIPATES IN REALIZATION OF A
under some contexts / relationships / conditions

WITHOUT
B REPLACING A
AND WITHOUT
A ABSORBING B INTO MERE SURFACE FLAVOR
```

The participation may appear as:

- **modulation** — B changes how A interprets, pursues, defends, repairs or expresses;
- **competition** — B supplies a recurrent counter-pressure against A;
- **co-recruitment** — both organize different aspects of the same person-event response;
- **sequencing** — one recurring organization reliably hands off to another after a certain kind of perturbation or frustration;
- **relationship-local override** — B organizes a particular relational domain while A retains wider return dominance;
- **alternate-route availability** — B makes a response reachable that A alone would tend not to supply.

These are explanatory possibilities, **not an adopted enum or mechanism family**.

The core relation is longitudinal:

> The secondary organization matters because its participation is history-stable and predictively recurs, not because it happens to be active now.

Thus:

```text
CURRENT_COACTIVATION
!=
STABLE_SECONDARY_PARTICIPATION
```

## 7. Is stable secondary organization a true semantic remainder?

Under the bank's required disposition discipline, the answer is nuanced but firm.

### 7.1 The phenomenon is real enough to preserve

A system that can represent only:

```text
ONE_DOMINANT_ORGANIZATION
OR
TEMPORARY_NOISE_AROUND_IT
```

would be conceptually too weak.

It could not explain a person whose less-dominant recurring organization:

- returns over years;
- is recruited predictably by certain relationships;
- materially changes the dominant pattern's realization;
- has its own history-conditioned reinforcement/correction trajectory;
- remains subordinate at broad scale;
- and cannot be reduced to mood or role.

So the phenomenon cannot be discarded.

### 7.2 But the noun is not missing

Existing V3/PFF concepts already permit the material from which multiple organizations are formed and recruited.

Creating a new durable entity named `SecondaryOrganization` or `WingState` would therefore duplicate rather than explain.

### 7.3 The formal disposition

```text
STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_PHENOMENON
= MUST_PRESERVE

SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_AS_SEPARATE_STATE_KIND
= REJECT

CURRENT_RAWLSING_GAP
= ALREADY_PRESENT__RELATION_MISSING
```

The relation that must survive later architecture-input constitution is:

```text
HISTORY_STABILIZED_INTER_ORGANIZATION_PARTICIPATION
```

If later V3 examination proves that recurrent organizations themselves are not adequately representable without a new native construct, that could reopen `TRUE_SEMANTIC_REMAINDER`.

This file does not find that burden met.

```text
ANY_HISTORY_REALIZED_RECURRENT_ORGANIZATION
MAY_HAVE
CONDITIONAL_PROPENSITY_FOR_RECRUITMENT_REENTRY_PERSISTENCE_AND_REINFORCEMENT
```

```text
SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION_CAN_HAVE_CONDITIONAL_GRAVITY = YES
```

```text
SECONDARY_SPECIFIC_GRAVITY_PROPERTY = NOT_JUSTIFIED
```

A secondary organization's gravity may be:

- weak globally;
- strong in a particular relationship;
- strong under criticism but weak under ordinary warmth;
- strengthened by one developmental period;
- weakened through correction;
- largely dormant until a familiar problem-form appears.

This is compatible with bank `56`'s law that gravity is conditional rather than one global scalar.

## 10. Can a secondary organization itself be captured?

Yes at phenomenon level, provided capture is kept distinct from identity and global dominance.

A secondary recurrent organization may become strongly monopolizing under a particular perturbation or relationship.

For example:

```text
BROAD_PERSON_ORGANIZATION: A usually has greatest return reach

RELATIONSHIP R:
B is repeatedly recruited
-> narrows interpretation
-> recruits characteristic defense/pursuit
-> reduces alternate routes
-> persists after immediate trigger
```

This can count as **relationship-local or episode-scale capture by B** without implying that B has become the person's global dominant attractor.

Preserve:

```text
LOCAL_CAPTURE_BY_SECONDARY
!=
GLOBAL_DOMINANCE_SWITCH
```

and:

```text
LOCAL_CAPTURE_BY_SECONDARY
!=
SECOND_PERSONALITY
```

No secondary-specific capture engine is justified. General capture/recovery semantics should apply where the phenomenon fits.

Entry and exit may remain asymmetric:

```text
ENTRY_THRESHOLD != EXIT_THRESHOLD
```

A relationship may recruit B quickly and release it slowly.

## 11. Multiple secondary organizations must remain possible

Nothing in current Rawlsing psychology demonstrates that one developed person can have exactly one stable subordinate organization.

Indeed, existing multiplicity argues against introducing an arbitrary limit.

A person may plausibly have:

- one broadly dominant recurrent organization;
- one secondary organization that strongly participates in intimacy;
- another that recurrently organizes status/competence situations;
- an older relationship-specific organization that remains easy to re-enter with family;
- several weaker organizations whose coherence never becomes broad enough to call them stable secondary organizations.

The exact individuation criteria remain open.

But the constitutional constraint should be:

```text
MULTIPLE_STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATIONS
= MUST_REMAIN_POSSIBLE_PENDING_FALSIFICATION
```

not:

```text
EXACTLY_ONE_WING
```

This does not imply an unlimited catalog of little personalities.

The burden remains on each candidate organization to show recurrent coherent organization, history dependence and return tendency beyond surface mixture.

## 12. Secondary organization versus mood

A mood is an immediate affective/condition state.

A stable secondary organization is a history-conditioned recurrent pattern across multiple psychological relations.

Distinguishing court:

```text
MOOD CHANGES / SETTLES
```

while the same secondary organization remains predictively recruitable under later relevant perturbation.

Expected distinction:

```text
SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
!=
MOOD
```

A mood can make B easier to recruit without constituting B.

## 13. Secondary organization versus role

A role can produce stable-looking surface behavior because the environment repeatedly cues:

- professional conduct;
- parent/child expectations;
- teacher/student asymmetry;
- public presentation;
- status scripts;
- institutional constraints.

Role recurrence is therefore insufficient evidence of secondary organization.

A stable secondary organization should demonstrate organization beyond the role cue, including recurrent relations among uptake, expectation, pursuit, defense, self-model, frustration and correction.

Distinguishing court:

```text
REMOVE_OR_CHANGE_ROLE_CUE
```

If the pattern disappears because the script disappeared, role explains it better.

If deeper organization recurs across nonidentical settings with history-dependent return, secondary organization remains plausible.

## 14. Secondary organization versus current recruitment

This distinction is essential.

One interaction can strongly recruit a pattern that has never previously been stable.

That may be:

- a live excursion;
- a novel response;
- an experimental practice;
- situational regulation;
- temporary conflict;
- a new developmental possibility.

It is not yet a stable secondary organization.

Thus:

```text
STRONG_CURRENT_RECRUITMENT
!=
STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
```

and:

```text

CANNOT_BY_ITSELF
CERTIFY_DURABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
```

## 15. Secondary organization versus ordinary contradiction

A person can be contradictory without possessing multiple recurrent organizations.

Examples:

- wanting intimacy and privacy;
- believing oneself generous while resenting demands;
- feeling affection and irritation;
- wanting both recognition and invisibility.

Contradiction becomes evidence for a secondary organization only if a recurring subset of relations coheres and returns in a recognizable history-dependent pattern.

Required difference:

```text
ORDINARY_CONTRADICTION
= incompatible or unstable contents

STABLE_SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
= recurrent cross-domain organization with return and participation effects
```

No coherent recurrent bundle, no secondary-organization claim.

## 16. Secondary organization versus attractor switching

A stable secondary organization can materially change the dominant organization's realization **without replacing its broad return hierarchy**.

That is the key contrast with attractor switching or durable reorganization.

### Participation case

```text
A remains broadly dominant over time
B repeatedly modifies A in contexts C1/C2
A tends to reappear after perturbation
```

### Durable switch/reorganization case

```text
B or another organization acquires broader return reach
old hierarchy no longer predicts recovery/re-entry
future cross-context organization is durably changed
```

Thus:

```text
SECONDARY_PARTICIPATION
!=
ATTRACTOR_SWITCH
```

A strong relationship-local period of B does not by itself prove a durable global switch.

## 17. Secondary organization versus surface behavioral mixture

This is the most important false-positive court for wing-like interpretation.

Surface behavior can look mixed because of:

- politeness conventions;
- mood;
- role;
- imitation;
- one learned skill;
- renderer style;
- situational incentives;
- fatigue;
- user prompting;
- demographic stereotype;
- dossier instruction;
- temporary response strategy.

A true stable secondary organization should explain **why different behaviors hang together** through recurrent psychological relations.

Evidence should reach upstream of style into:

- what the person takes the problem to be;
- what they expect;
- what they pursue;
- how they interpret blockage;
- which defenses recur;
- which self-model claims become available;
- how corrective evidence is metabolized;
- how the pattern returns after perturbation.

Hard law:

```text
BEHAVIORAL_BLEND
!=
SECONDARY_ORGANIZATION
```

## 18. Relationship specificity does not defeat secondary organization

Bank `56` correctly distinguishes global personality attractor from local recurrent loop.

This examination adds a middle possibility:

> A genuinely stable secondary organization may have **limited domain breadth** while still being more than a relationship-local habit.

For example, a pattern may be:

- weak in ordinary social contact;
- strongly recruited in attachment relationships;
- expressed differently with different close others;
- traceable across several developmental periods;
- coherent in its problem-form, expectations, pursuit and defense;
- capable of shaping later non-chat life events.

So neither of these extremes is adequate:

```text
ONLY_GLOBAL_CROSS_CONTEXT_PATTERNS_ARE_REAL
```

or:

```text
EVERY_REPEATED_DYADIC_LOOP_IS_A_SECONDARY_ATTRACTOR
```

The deciding evidence is developmental coherence and return under varied but relevant perturbations.

## 19. How secondary participation may alter dominant realization

The phrase `materially alter` needs content or wing-like participation becomes decorative.

B should count as materially participating only when its recurrence can change one or more psychologically upstream consequences such as:

- what part of an event is salient to the actor;
- the actor's received reading;
- which durable expectation is recruited;
- which striving becomes action-relevant;
- the experienced relational problem-form;
- pursuit/management route;
- frustration response;
- defensive transformation;
- willingness to absorb correction;
- self-model accessibility;
- actor model/metaperception;
- anticipation;
- effort allocation;
- available recovery route;
- expression pressure before V2 rendering.

It is not enough that B changes adjectives, tone, hobbies, or verbal mannerism.

Thus:

```text
SECONDARY_PARTICIPATION
MUST_BE_UPSTREAM_CAUSAL_ORGANIZATION
NOT_STYLE_FLAVOR
```

This is a strong protection against the compact `5w4 -> voice` shortcut.

## 20. Developmental formation — co-crystallization, not label attachment

A stable secondary organization must be earned by history just as dominant attractor gravity is.

A plausible phenomenon-level trajectory is:

```text
EARLY MULTIPLICITY / PARTIAL ORGANIZATIONS
        |
        v
REPEATED PERSON-WORLD LOOPS
        |
        +--> some relations stabilize broadly
        |       -> dominant recurrent organization
        |
        +--> some other relations stabilize conditionally
                -> secondary recurrent organization(s)
        |
        v

        |
        v
HISTORY-STABILIZED PARTICIPATION RELATION
```

This does not presume that A crystallizes first and B is attached later.

They may:

- co-crystallize;
- differentiate gradually;
- exchange comparative prominence;
- become linked through repeated relational sequences;
- weaken independently under correction;
- generalize at different rates.

The developmental law is therefore not:

```text
PRIMARY_TYPE_SELECTED
-> WING_ATTACHED
```

It is closer to:

```text
HISTORY
-> UNEQUAL_CRYSTALLIZATION_OF_MULTIPLE_RECURRENT_ORGANIZATIONS
-> STABLE_RELATIONS_AMONG_THEM
```

No exact developmental update law is adopted.

## 21. Enneagram adjacency — what survives?

Traditional wing theory adds a specific topological claim:

> Stable secondary participation is especially or exclusively associated with an organization adjacent to the dominant organization on the Enneagram symbol.

Current Rawlsing evidence does not justify that restriction.

The useful remainder is empirical/topological, not constitutional.

### 21.1 What adjacency could mean if it survives

Adjacency would have value only if it predicts something beyond naming convenience, for example:

- co-crystallization is disproportionately common between adjacent prototype families;
- one adjacent organization is easier to recruit from another;
- adjacent organizations share developmental mediators or problem-form transitions;
- adjacency predicts characteristic modulation rather than generic trait mixture;
- adjacent routes remain easier after controlling for history and context.

### 21.2 Rival explanations

Any apparent adjacency effect must compete with:

- generic motive compatibility;
- shared need domains;
- overlapping mediators;
- trait covariance;
- social-role learning;
- developmental path proximity;
- relationship-specific reinforcement;
- common environmental pressures;
- analyst classification bias;
- the fact that theory descriptions themselves were built to look adjacent.

### 21.3 Current disposition

```text
ADJACENT_WING_AS_RUNTIME_RULE
= REJECT

ADJACENCY_AS_THEORY_DERIVED_TOPOLOGICAL_HYPOTHESIS
= DEFER

ADJACENCY_AS_HUMAN_READABLE_INTERPRETIVE_LENS
= INTERPRETIVE_ONLY
```

If later courts show that adjacency robustly improves developmental coherence prediction, it might inform the **common latent topology as a soft prior**.

It may not veto V3 history.

Hard counterexample law:


## 25. Developmental and scenario courts

A real secondary organization must survive stronger courts than one transcript or one personality-description match.

### Court 1 — longitudinal recurrence

Hold the developed person fixed and expose them across nonidentical but relevant perturbations over time.

A stable secondary organization should recur through coherent relations among uptake, expectation, pursuit, defense, self-model and repair rather than merely repeat one behavior.

### Court 2 — same dominant organization, different developmental history

Develop two generated persons toward similar dominant organization but different histories.

If one history establishes B and another does not, later responses should diverge lawfully even while both remain recognizably organized around A.

This is especially important against dossier/type equivalence.

### Court 5 — role removal

Remove or alter the role/audience cue.

A role-produced style should collapse with the role.

A true secondary organization may change expression but should retain deeper history-conditioned return under relevant perturbation.

### Court 6 — recruitment-only false positive

Strongly recruit B-like organization once.

Without prior history or later recurrence, do not certify stable secondary organization.

### Court 7 — ordinary contradiction

Create repeated incompatible motives or beliefs without cross-domain recurrent coherence.

Do not overcall this as secondary organization.

### Court 8 — participation versus switching

Recruit B strongly enough to alter A's realization.

Then perturb the person again across other contexts.

If A's broader return hierarchy persists, this supports stable secondary participation.

If B now has broad durable return dominance, investigate reorganization/switch rather than calling it merely a wing effect.

without forcing global identity change.

### Court 10 — multiple-secondary court

Develop different subordinate recurring patterns through different histories/relationships.

A one-wing implementation fails this court by construction.

### Court 13 — quiescence

### Court 14 — corrective plasticity

## 26. Strong counterexamples that would weaken the concept

The secondary-organization hypothesis should be pruned if scenario work shows any of the following:

### Counterexample A — lower-level state fully explains everything

If expectations, strivings, mediators, tensions, practices, history and context predict all observed recurrence without any additional explanatory value from recognizing an inter-organization participation relation, then the named secondary-organization concept may remain interpretive only.

### Counterexample B — no longitudinal invariance

If B-like behavior appears only when directly cued and lacks return across perturbations, it is not a stable secondary organization.

### Counterexample C — no upstream causal difference

If adding/removing the secondary interpretation changes only rendered style, not uptake, pursuit, defense, correction, self-model or recovery, it is decorative.

### Counterexample D — primary/secondary rank is unstable noise

If alleged organization rank changes arbitrarily with every event and has no history-dependent persistence, the attractor framing is too strong.

### Counterexample E — wing label does all the work

If the system requires knowing `5w4` before it can generate the pattern, development has been bypassed.

That is a failure, not evidence for wing machinery.
