## 1. D1 — TYPED_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_DERIVATION

### 1.1 Source challenge

Can broad structure be connected to a particular actor's lived conditions without a category shortcut?

The book's central thesis is almost exactly this demand:

```text
SOCIAL STRUCTURE / POSITION
-> SYSTEMATICALLY DIFFERENTIATED PROXIMATE CONDITIONS OF LIFE
-> PSYCHOLOGICAL FUNCTIONING
```

The cross-system comparison strengthens the requirement. “Same class” is not even a stable category inventory across the US and Poland. The authors must reconstruct ownership, labor-command, hierarchy, sector, education/status/income, task complexity, supervision and routinization before interpreting person outcomes.

### 1.2 Positive evidence for D1

D1 is supported by multiple source-earned derivations:

```text
OWNERSHIP / HIERARCHY / CLASS POSITION
-> OPPORTUNITY FOR COMPLEX / SELF-DIRECTED WORK

SUPERVISORY ORGANIZATION
-> CLOSENESS OF SUPERVISION

DIVISION OF LABOUR / TASK ASSIGNMENT
-> SUBSTANTIVE COMPLEXITY / ROUTINIZATION

CREDENTIAL / EDUCATIONAL ALLOCATION RULE
-> OCCUPATIONAL POSITION / CONTINUED EDUCATION PATH

LABOR LAW / CONTRACT / UNION / CIVIL-SERVICE RULE
-> JOB-PROTECTION POSSIBILITY / ENTITLEMENT

FAMILY ROLE CONFIGURATION
-> PARENTAL LINKED-LIFE TRANSMISSION PATH
```

The source thereby attacks any D1 implementation that would derive actor ecology from category membership rather than concrete relation/rule/position.

### 1.3 New M7 constraint on D1: system configuration must remain upstream provenance

The frozen D1 candidate already requires `STRUCTURAL_SOURCE_FACTS`, `POSITION_BINDING`, `MECHANISM_RULE`, context/time/history, counterpart/institution binding, boundary conditions and derivation provenance. M7 finds this grammar correct but identifies one semantic obligation that must be made impossible to omit when the mechanism is system-dependent:

```text
INSTITUTIONAL_CONFIGURATION_REF(S)
```

or an exact equivalent carried inside the existing structural-source/provenance tuple.

Why? Because the same actor-relative output token can have different causal production and implications under different institutional structures:

- `MANAGER` in a capitalist firm is not the same relational configuration as `MANAGER` in a centralized state enterprise;
- `MANUAL WORKER` under union/contract protection differs from a manual worker under recent universalized state-sector labor protection;
- `EDUCATED` has different career-ordering implications where formal education continues after occupational placement;
- `SUPERVISED` differs where supervisor authority is embedded in wider institutionally salient authority relations.

Dropping the institutional source after deriving the actor condition would destroy the exact information M7 was commissioned to recover.

### 1.4 D1 verdict

`STRENGTHENED AND REQUIRED, WITH PROVENANCE NARROWING`.

Proposed exact requirement:

```text
D1 derivation must preserve the concrete institutional / organizational / legal
configuration that generated the actor-relative condition whenever that configuration
is causally constitutive or a portability boundary.

Country/system label alone is not a valid configuration reference.
```

No new psychology is introduced.

## 2. D2 — INTERVAL_RECURRENCE_AND_TRANSITION_REBINDING

### 2.1 Source challenge

Does the source require more than discrete events?

**Yes.** The central occupational mechanism is not an event mechanism. It concerns persistent/recurrent work conditions and repeated experience:

```text
COMPLEX / ROUTINE TASK STRUCTURE
+ CLOSER / LOOSER SUPERVISION
OVER TIME
-> PSYCHOLOGICAL FUNCTIONING
```

Turning every hour of supervision or complex work into an OEE event would lose the causal grain the source studies.

### 2.2 Strong support: interval and recurrence

The source strongly supports D2's need to represent:

- persistent work condition;
- repeated task/activity structure;
- duration of an occupational position;
- ongoing supervision relation;
- continuing education during occupational career;
- continuing family-linked exposure;
- longitudinal ordering of earlier/later occupational self-direction.

This is strong evidence for the frozen temporal court's distinction among `CONDITION`, `RELATION`, and `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY`.

### 2.3 Moderate support: trajectory/history

The book also supports ordered histories in a bounded form:

- prior and later jobs;
- education before/during career;
- 1964/1974 occupational self-direction;
- parents earlier than child follow-up in Poland;
- historically different cohorts.

But it explicitly says it does not adequately model full career sequences, exact life-course timing, or event histories.

### 2.4 Weak/indirect support: transition rebinding

The source contains real transitions—job changes, educational continuation, movement into later occupational positions—but the empirical design does not directly reconstruct an atomic post-transition ecology update of all affected relations/resources/obligations/activities.

Therefore M7 cannot claim that Kohn/Słomczyński independently proves the **transition-rebinding** portion of D2 in its full architectural strength.

It is compatible with D2 and provides examples that would need rebinding, but this source's direct evidentiary force is mainly for:

```text
ACTIVE_INTERVAL
RECURRENT_ACTIVITY
ORDERED_EARLIER/LATER_STATE
```

not a fully observed transition transaction.

### 2.5 Temporal ceiling

The source explicitly warns that:

- Polish male directionality is quasi-longitudinal;
- the US male panel has only two main waves ten years apart;
- exact onset, accumulation, tapering, thresholding and episodic timing cannot be inferred;
- cohort differences cannot cleanly distinguish age/cohort/career/history.

D2 therefore must not be justified by pretending the source observes more temporal structure than it does.

### 2.6 D2 verdict

`PARTIALLY STRENGTHENED`.

Exact M7 disposition:

```text
INTERVAL / RELATION / RECURRENCE: STRONGLY SUPPORTED
ORDERED HISTORY / TRAJECTORY: SUPPORTED WITH CEILING
TRANSITION REBINDING: ARCHITECTURALLY COMPATIBLE, NOT FULLY PROVED BY THIS SOURCE
```

No D2 deletion is warranted. No expansion beyond the frozen temporal grammar is warranted either.

## 3. D3 — PROVENANCE_PRESERVING_RECIPROCAL_SOCIAL_WORLD_UPDATE

### 3.1 Hostile source question

Does this book actually show:

```text
ACTOR ACTION
-> REALIZED EXTERNAL ACTION/EVENT
-> INSTITUTIONAL / COUNTERPART / COLLECTIVE RESPONSE
-> CHANGE IN RELATION / POSITION / RULE / RESOURCE / NETWORK
-> NEW SOCIAL/WORLD FACT
-> LATER ACTOR ECOLOGY
```

**No, not at that full grain.**

### 3.2 What the book does support

It supports a narrower reciprocity:

```text
OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION
-> PSYCHOLOGICAL FUNCTIONING

PSYCHOLOGICAL FUNCTIONING
-> LATER OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION
```

through some combination of:

- actor job selection;
- employer selection;
- actor molding of the job.

US analyses also support reciprocal relations between occupational self-direction and ownership/hierarchical position.

This is valuable evidence for `GATED_RECIPROCAL_SELECTION` or bounded person-position feedback.

### 3.3 What is missing for D3

The source does not directly observe or identify a general sequence of:

- actor intention/action;
- exact external institutional response;
- a changed rule/property/network/organizational fact;
- admission of that change as a new social-world fact;
- subsequent actor-relative rederivation.

The book does not study collective action as a structural update mechanism, does not model rule change caused by respondents, and has no general behavior/action measure capable of supporting such a claim.

The historically important changes it discusses—Polish labor-law reform, industrialization, central planning, union protections, institutional family roles—are largely **upstream historical structures**, not changes caused by the surveyed actors through a modeled D3 pathway.

### 3.4 D3 verdict

`NOT INDEPENDENTLY VALIDATED BY M7 SOURCE`.

This is **not** a proof that D3 is wrong or unnecessary in Rawlsing. It is a source-authority verdict:

```text
KOHN/SLOMCZYNSKI MAY SUPPORT:
  GATED_RECIPROCAL_PERSON_POSITION_SELECTION
  JOB_MOLDING
  LATER_POSITION_FEEDBACK

KOHN/SLOMCZYNSKI DOES NOT BY ITSELF SUPPORT:
  GENERAL PROVENANCE_PRESERVING SOCIAL_WORLD_UPDATE
```

If D3 remains in the constitution, its authority must come from other independent source lanes or architectural necessity, not be silently attributed to M7.

The frozen candidate already prohibits:

```text
CLASS/RACE/GENDER/POVERTY/RELIGION -> PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAIT
```

M7 strengthens the prohibition and extends its evidentiary logic:

```text
COUNTRY / SOCIAL_SYSTEM -> ACTOR PSYCHOLOGY        [PROHIBITED]
CLASS_CATEGORY -> ACTOR SOCIAL FACT                [PROHIBITED WITHOUT MECHANISM]
OCCUPATIONAL_TITLE -> ACTUAL AUTHORITY/AUTONOMY    [PROHIBITED WITHOUT POSITION/RULE]
INSTITUTIONAL_RULE -> REALIZED ACTOR TREATMENT      [PROHIBITED WITHOUT APPLICABILITY/REALIZATION]
```

The last edge is especially important. Polish national labor law may establish a protection entitlement or system-level possibility, but actor-level realized protection/use requires the exact applicable relation where the target claim is actor-level.
