## Candidate challenge summary

The cards do **not** establish a distinct D4. Space/time/access is repeatedly expressible as:

```text
D1 typed qualification/realization
+
D2 interval/recurrence/transition semantics
```

with D3 only when actor/collective activity itself generates, preserves, suppresses, or reroutes later social-world facts.

The strongest source-licensed generalizations are narrower:
