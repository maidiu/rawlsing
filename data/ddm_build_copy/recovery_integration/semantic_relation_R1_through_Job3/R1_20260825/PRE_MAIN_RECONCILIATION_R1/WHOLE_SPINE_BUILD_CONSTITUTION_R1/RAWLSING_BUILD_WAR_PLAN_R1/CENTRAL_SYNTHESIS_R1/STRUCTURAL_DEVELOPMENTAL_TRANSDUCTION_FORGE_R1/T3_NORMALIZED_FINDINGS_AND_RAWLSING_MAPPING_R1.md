This file normalizes T3 source-specific cards into mechanism families. It does not adopt architecture. Status judgments are T3 recommendations to the Vozhd.

## 1. T3 answer to the central question

Class position, property, division of labour, organizations, institutions, households and authority do not lawfully change a person by carrying a class-specific psychology. They alter the **relations and rules through which an actor obtains resources, must meet dependencies/obligations, performs recurrent activities, encounters sanctions, gains or loses institutional eligibility, accesses counterpart networks, and allocates time**.

The T3 positive grammar is therefore:

```text
HISTORICALLY SPECIFIC STRUCTURAL RELATION / RULE
        ↓
POSITION-RELATIVE MECHANISM
  property/resource access
  authority/control/sanction
  work-time/activity organization
  information/skill allocation
  institutional gate/mobility/right
  household/care obligation
  network/counterpart arrangement
        ↓
ACTOR-RELATIVE SOCIAL ECOLOGY
  resource / dependency / obligation
  activity form / time use / pace
  sanction / right / gate / mobility route
  counterpart / support / care relation
  information / skill-use opportunity
        ↓
ACTUAL RELATION / PRACTICE / EVENT / CONDITION REALIZED
        ↓
EXISTING V3 UPTAKE / RESPONSE / HISTORY / DEVELOPMENT OWNERS
        ↓
DURABLE CHANGE ONLY IF EARNED
```

The middle cannot be replaced by `CLASS → TRAIT`, `GENDER → CARE ROLE`, `OCCUPATION → PERSONALITY`, `HABITUS → DISPOSITION`, or `REPRODUCTION → SAME PERSON TYPE`.

## 3. Rawlsing owner mapping

### 3.1 PRE-V3 owners T3 recommends reusing

T3 found no reason to duplicate the already-governed PRE-V3 structural possibility side:

- positioned-subject empirical possibility;
- structural pressure;
- structural affordance/applicability;
- mediation-route possibility;
- interaction topology;
- attractor prerequisites where relevant;
- phase/world/context binding as recovered in the Phase-0 seam map.

These can state that a rule/relation/route is structurally possible or applicable. Their negative ceiling remains decisive: they cannot by resolution alone create actor belief, action, relationship, exposure or trait truth.

### 3.2 Existing actor-relative / realization surfaces to generalize rather than duplicate where sufficient

T3 mechanism families repeatedly need:

- actor-specific opportunity/constraint;
- contact/encounter/counterpart realization;
- route traversal;
- OEE/event realization where a true event exists;
- actor/world/context binding.

These surfaces are close to T3-N02, N05–N08 and N11, but the coordinator must test whether they can express **persistent relations, conditions and recurrent practices** without reducing them to isolated events.

### 3.3 Existing V3 owners T3 explicitly does not seize

When an actor actually lives a work, care, institutional or peer relation, downstream person change remains with existing V3 owners. Depending on the realized material, competent owners include:

- `ADP-019` for lawful external occurrence/admission where type-compatible;
- `CAN-008` for immediate person × event coupling/response where type-compatible;
- `CAN-011` for practices/mediators/tensions;
- `ADP-015` for actor-perceived/realized relationship state;
- `ADP-016` for causal memory/traces;
- `ADP-017` for knowledge/belief/uncertainty/access;
- `CAN-010` for needs/expectations/strivings where earned;
- `CAN-012` for rupture/metabolization where applicable;
- `MUST-018` for attractor transition jurisdiction;
- the current embodied-condition owner where bodily consequences are actually realized.

T3 does not claim that every T3 social condition maps to every listed V3 owner. Exact target routing remains type- and event-specific.

## 5. Temporal causal forms court — T3 return

T3 positively requires Rawlsing to preserve all eight commissioned forms as semantically distinct, though not necessarily as eight new storage objects.

| Form | T3 exemplar | What must be preserved | T3 judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EVENT` | firing, sanction, grievance outcome, promotion, school punishment | occurrence, timing, counterpart, consequence | existing OEE/event machinery likely reusable where type-compatible |
| `CONDITION` | wage dependency, time poverty, low discretion, care shortage | persistence/duration without fabricating a decisive event | `UNDERGENERALIZED__MUST_NOT_FORCE_TO_EVENT` |
| `RELATION` | employer–worker authority, supervisor–worker, household care dependency, union representation | directed rights/dependencies/powers between counterparts | `CONNECTIVE_SEMANTICS_REQUIRED` |
| `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_OR_PRACTICE` | machine tending, fragmented task work, care work, shop-floor game, anti-school practice | repeated action organization and frequency, not merely repeated identical events | `CONNECTIVE_OR_COMPOSITION_PROOF_REQUIRED` |
| `TRANSITION` | school→work, promotion/demotion, entry to care role, migration | before/after state and timing | existing state/phase change may suffice; life-process meaning must remain explicit |
| `TRAJECTORY` | seniority career path, repeated job mobility, long care/work sequence | ordered sequence, duration and path dependence | V3 history carrier likely reusable but structural trajectory derivation underproved |
| `TURNING_POINT` | one grievance, policy reform, firing, strike outcome only when it redirects later trajectory | event plus demonstrated trajectory redirection | `DO_NOT_LABEL_FROM_EVENT_MAGNITUDE_ALONE` |
| `CUMULATIVE_PROCESS` | years of repeated work-time extraction, care load, skill practice/nonuse, reproduced wage dependence | accumulation over time with no single decisive event | existing V3 accumulation may carry consequences; upstream accumulation law underproved |

### Temporal conclusion

No new temporal carrier is licensed by T3 alone. But an architecture that can represent only discrete OEE events is insufficient for the T3 corpus. The minimum court question for the coordinator is whether persistent relation/condition state plus event-sourced history and recurrent activity composition can losslessly express the eight forms.

## 6. Reciprocity court

The T3 corpus rejects one-way structure→actor determinism.

Positive reciprocal paths include:

```text
worker organization → legal working-day / contract / grievance rights
worker mobility/bidding → supervisor behavior / internal allocation
worker resistance / tacit practice → changed labour-process control
credential acquisition / job selection → later class/authority position
household negotiation / migration → later care and paid-work arrangement
collective care/labour politics → public provision / institutional rules
```

Rawlsing therefore needs a lawful return path from **real actor action / collective action** to changed later position, relation, rule, opportunity or exposure. That return must not permit one actor's preference to rewrite macro structure magically; the action must have an actual causal route and sufficient collective/institutional efficacy.

## 8. Reuse / generalize / missing / reject / defer summary

### `REUSE`

- PRE-V3 actor-independent structural possibility jurisdiction.
- mediation-route and interaction-topology possibility.
- V3 developmental target owners.
- event/OEE machinery for actual discrete occurrences.

### `GENERALIZE`

- actor-specific opportunity/constraint to include typed institutional rights/gates, time constraints and recurring opportunity structures;
- contact/counterpart machinery to cover recurring organizational/household/peer relations without equating relation with encounter;
- route/traversal semantics for institutional mobility, credential, grievance and school-to-work routes;
- reciprocal action path where existing event-sourced causality can lawfully update later social position/relation.

### `MISSING`

At theory level, positive structure→actor ecology functions for property/resource dependency, organizational authority/sanction, activity/knowledge organization, household/care dependency and care-time institutional allocation are not proved by the Phase-0 owner bookends alone.

### `REJECT`

```text
CLASS → PERSONALITY
GENDER → CAREGIVER
POVERTY → DISTRESS/TRAIT WITHOUT MEDIATION
OCCUPATION TITLE → AUTONOMY/COMPLEXITY
MANAGER LABEL → AUTHORITY
CREDENTIAL → COMPETENCE
HEGEMONY → CONSENT WITHOUT PRACTICE
HABITUS → TRAIT AS BLACK BOX
SOCIAL REPRODUCTION → SAME PSYCHOLOGY
AVAILABLE OPPORTUNITY → USED OPPORTUNITY
GROUP ASSOCIATION → INDIVIDUAL CAUSAL HISTORY
```

### `DEFER`

- Gramsci vocabulary unless later synthesis proves an uncovered institutional/cultural mechanism.
- Bourdieu habitus; network/capital mechanisms belong to T6 only if decomposed causally.
- exact new temporal carrier decision.
- downstream theory of capacity/personality development (T1/T4/V3).
- life-course formalization beyond T3's temporal requirements (T5).

## 9. T3 terminal synthesis

The T3 corpus supports a **typed actor-relative social ecology transduction**, not a class-effect scalar and not a sociology-side psychology module. The strongest minimal architectural hypothesis for Vozhd testing is:

```text
EXISTING PRE-V3 STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY OBJECTS
+ POSITIONED ACTOR / WORLD / PHASE BINDING
        ↓
GENERALIZED / POSITIVE RELATION-TO-ECOLOGY CONNECTIVE SEMANTICS
  [resource, dependency, authority, time, activity, gate,
   right, sanction, household/care, counterpart, information]
        ↓
ACTOR-RELATIVE CONDITION / RELATION / ACTIVITY / OPPORTUNITY ECOLOGY
        ↓
ACTUAL REALIZATION / PRACTICE / OCCURRENCE
        ↓
EXISTING V3 OWNERS
```

T3 proves the need for the **semantic functions** in the middle. It does not prove that Rawlsing needs a new owner rather than a bounded generalization of existing route/opportunity/constraint/activation machinery.
