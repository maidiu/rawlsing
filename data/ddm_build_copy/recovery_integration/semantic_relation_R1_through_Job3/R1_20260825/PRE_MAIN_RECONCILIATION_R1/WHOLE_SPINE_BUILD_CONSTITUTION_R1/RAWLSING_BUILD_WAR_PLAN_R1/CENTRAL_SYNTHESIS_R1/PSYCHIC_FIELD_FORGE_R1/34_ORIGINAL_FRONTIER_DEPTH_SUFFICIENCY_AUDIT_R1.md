## 0. Purpose

This audit corrects a potentially misleading interpretation of `32_ORIGINAL_FRONTIER_COVERAGE_LEDGER_R2.md`.

The statement:

`19_OF_20 ORIGINAL ITEMS HAVE DEDICATED DEEP TREATMENT`

is a **coverage statement**.

It is not equivalent to:

`19_OF_20 ITEMS ARE DEEP ENOUGH FOR ARCHITECTURE`.

The current campaign has produced substantial dedicated examinations, generally with phenomenon decomposition, noncollapse laws, scenarios, hard negatives and open questions. But several were written before the one-person/two-recursive-regime continuity model matured, and therefore require a cross-regime second pass before architecture admission.

## 1. Sufficiency vocabulary

### `CURRENT_STAGE_STRONG`

The dedicated examination is substantial enough to guide current pre-architecture synthesis. It may still have open questions and will still require architecture-time exactness.

### `SECOND_PASS_REQUIRED_BEFORE_ARCHITECTURE`

The first dedicated examination is substantial, but a later discovery materially changes its scope or jurisdiction. A dedicated second pass/reconciliation is required before architecture admission.

### `DEDICATED_SYNTHESIS_OPEN`

Substantial material exists across other banks, but the original question has not yet received its own sufficiently integrated dedicated synthesis.

### `LATE_SYNTHESIS_COURT`

Intentionally deferred until prior conceptual work matures.

No category means `DONE FOREVER`.

## 2. Audit criteria

An original-frontier item is judged against the following pre-architecture burdens:

1. phenomenon decomposition rather than evocative labeling;
2. noncollapse against adjacent concepts;
3. multiple positive scenarios;
4. counterexamples/hard negatives;
5. uncertainty/perspective handling where relevant;
6. V3/PFF/V2 jurisdiction awareness;
7. compatibility with the one-person/two-recursive-regime model;
8. explicit unresolved forks rather than silent decisions;
9. no premature architecture/gadget adoption;
10. enough conceptual specificity to constrain later architecture.

Scientific/theoretical reconciliation is desirable but is not required to mark a first-pass phenomenon examination `CURRENT_STAGE_STRONG`; it remains a separate expanded-frontier obligation.

## 5. Why the second-pass group clusters where it does

The seven second-pass items share one structural reason:

They were initially explored primarily as PFF/chat phenomena, but the campaign later discovered that much of the underlying psychology may be shared with periodic V3 recursion.

Therefore they now require the question:

> Is this a general person mechanism that should operate in both developmental and interactive recursion, a fast-state specialization of a shared mechanism, or genuinely interaction-only?

The second pass should **not** restart them from scratch.

It should consume the existing deep bank and perform a continuity/reconfiguration reconciliation.
