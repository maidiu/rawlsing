## M6-KS-01 — STRUCTURAL POSITION -> CONCRETE WORK-CONDITION ECOLOGY

**SOURCE:** chs. 1-2; ch. 6 pp. 126-143; ch. 7 pp. 154-190.

**CLAIM STATUS:** longitudinal/cross-sectional structural-equation evidence for relations among organizational position and job conditions; not randomized assignment.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** ownership, bureaucratization, hierarchical position, education/status placement, organization/economic location.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** structural position changes the probability/availability of substantively complex tasks, supervisory constraint, routinization, protections, risks, income and other job imperatives.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** concrete task demands, discretion limits, repetitive/predictable organization, risk/protection/time/physical conditions.

**REALIZATION LAW:** actor must actually occupy the job and be subject to the condition; occupation title/status alone does not realize it.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** none required for the condition to be objectively active; perception may alter downstream response but is analytically distinct.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** none at this stage; this is an upstream social-ecology derivation.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** condition/relation, possibly transition when job/position changes.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** organization/labor market/class relation.

**RECIPROCITY:** later person/action may affect later placement; see M6-KS-09.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** job-specific heterogeneity; status proxies attenuate actual work-content variation.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `STATUS/CLASS != JOB_CONTENT != PSYCHOLOGY`.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** supports D1; requires typed output rather than generic “SES/job quality.”

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-02 — SUBSTANTIVE COMPLEXITY -> RECURRENT JUDGMENT/PROBLEM-SOLVING -> IDEATIONAL FLEXIBILITY

**SOURCE:** ch. 5 pp. 103-124; app. B pp. 321-325.

**CLAIM STATUS:** `LONGITUDINAL_SEM__STRONG_OBSERVATIONAL` plus explicit task measurement; recurrent exercise is theoretically inferred from ongoing work requirements rather than continuously observed.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** job organized so performance requires complex handling of data/people/things and decisions under ill-defined/conflicting contingencies.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** repeated necessity to analyze, coordinate, synthesize, solve unforeseen problems, select among alternatives, negotiate/teach/supervise, or make adjustments using independent judgment.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** recurrent required exercise of thought and judgment in actual work.

**REALIZATION LAW:** actor must actually perform the substantively complex work; nominal occupation or an unencountered task possibility is insufficient.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** active cognitive engagement is intrinsic to task performance, but the source does not measure conscious appraisal on every iteration.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** ideational/intellectual flexibility.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** recurrent activity nested in a persistent job condition; longitudinal change across a decade.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job/organization.

**RECIPROCITY:** ideational flexibility predicts later substantive complexity, predominantly lagged.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** two-wave timing; no direct daily dose measure; omitted life events possible.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** SEM path `.17/.18` is not a randomized treatment effect and does not identify a universal dose-response law.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** D1 must derive/realize complex task demands; D2 must preserve repeated actual activity; V3 receives realized activity/history, not occupation label.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-03 — SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY -> DISCRETION CONSTRAINT -> REDUCED EXERCISED SELF-DIRECTION

**SOURCE:** ch. 1 pp. 22-25; ch. 6 pp. 127-143.

**CLAIM STATUS:** measurement + cross-sectional and longitudinal SEM; effects smaller/less uniform than substantive complexity.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** supervisor has authority over what/how/speed and expects obedience.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** supervision constrains the range of decisions available to the worker and the ability to disagree/control pace.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** reduced opportunity to exercise independent judgment.

**REALIZATION LAW:** authority relation must be actually active for this actor/job; formal hierarchy alone does not establish close supervision.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** worker may interpret/resist, but objective discretion limits can exist independently of correct perception.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** selected values/orientations, ideational flexibility, distress/anxiety depending model.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** persistent relation/condition with recurrent episodes.

**RECIPROCITY:** self-directedness predicts later reduced closeness of supervision in some models; pathway inferred as selection/molding.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** women show less consistent supervision effects; measure is self-report.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `NO_SUPERVISOR != ACTUAL_SELF_DIRECTION`.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** D1 `AUTHORITY_CONTROL_SANCTION`; D2 exercised-control history.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-04 — ROUTINIZATION -> LOW VARIETY/PREDICTABILITY -> FEWER OCCASIONS FOR INITIATIVE

**SOURCE:** ch. 1 pp. 26-27; ch. 6 pp. 127-129, 136-149.

**CLAIM STATUS:** mixed longitudinal associations; weaker measure/effects than complexity; some paths small or null.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** work stream organized as repetitive and predictable.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** fewer situations require choosing among alternative approaches or responding to novel contingencies.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** constrained opportunity for active judgment/self-direction.

**REALIZATION LAW:** actual repeated task pattern over time; a formal job description saying “routine” is insufficient.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** not necessarily conscious.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** ideational flexibility/values/orientations in some specifications.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** recurrent activity/condition.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job/work process.

**RECIPROCITY:** reverse paths from flexibility to routinization are weak/null in the broad model.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** routinization longitudinally has essentially a single indicator; its first-order effect is not robust across every outcome.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** recurrence itself is not harmful; the relevant distinction is repetitive fixed activity versus repeated complex judgment.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** D2 must carry quality/type of recurrence, not merely count events.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE_WITH_CAUTION`.

## M6-KS-05 — OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION -> SELF-DIRECTED VALUES/ORIENTATION

**SOURCE:** ch. 7 pp. 171-189, Table 7.4.

**CLAIM STATUS:** constrained longitudinal SEM; work -> person paths broadly significant.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** realized combination of complexity, low supervisory constraint and low routinization.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** repeated experience of exercising initiative/thought/judgment is interpreted by authors as learned/generalized orientation toward self-direction.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** repeated self-directed action at work.

**REALIZATION LAW:** occupational self-direction must be actually exercised, not merely structurally possible.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** learning/generalization; precise subjective process undermeasured.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** parental/self valuation of self-direction, self-directedness of orientation, authoritarian/conformist facets.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** recurrent activity -> durable orientation change.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job to person/nonwork life.

**RECIPROCITY:** several orientations predict later self-direction; some do not.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** heterogeneous reciprocal paths; ideational flexibility is not the universal mediator.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** no direct class/status -> value edge.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** D1+D2 upstream; existing V3 must own learning/durable change.

**DISPOSITION:** `REUSE_AS_EVIDENCE_FOR_SEAM`.

## M6-KS-06 — OPPRESSIVE JOB CONDITIONS -> DISTRESS

**SOURCE:** ch. 6 pp. 145-153; ch. 7 Table 7.4; ch. 12-13.

**CLAIM STATUS:** longitudinal SEM with cross-national/contextual limits.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** close supervision, weak protections, dirty/onerous conditions, low control, selected pressures/risks.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** persistent exposure to job pressures/uncertainty/control constraints; exact psychophysiological/appraisal process not identified.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** chronic aversive work condition.

**REALIZATION LAW:** condition must persist/occur; risk index alone does not imply experienced distress.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** not fully modeled; ch. 13 calls for joint objective and subjective appraisal measurement.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** distress/anxiety/self-deprecation facets.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** persistent condition/cumulative exposure, not necessarily discrete event.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job/organization.

**RECIPROCITY:** distress -> job condition paths are mostly weak/null.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** distress results less cross-nationally universal; job context/support may moderate.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** “low class” is not distress; nor does a condition guarantee distress.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** supports `PERSISTENT_STRAIN_CONDITION`; D2 must not eventize it.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-07 — WORK EXPERIENCE -> NONWORK ACTIVITY GENERALIZATION

**SOURCE:** ch. 9 pp. 217-241; ch. 13 pp. 300-301.

**CLAIM STATUS:** longitudinal SEM for men + reported behavior; quasi-longitudinal sensitivity for women.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** substantive complexity and other job conditions.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** learned patterns of intellectual engagement and facilitation/constraint (time/resources) carry into leisure behavior.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** actual engagement in more/less intellectually demanding leisure activities.

**REALIZATION LAW:** nonwork activity must actually be selected/performed.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** learning-generalization; pure ideational-flexibility mediation is not supported.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** practice/activity ecology beyond work; possibly further person development downstream.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** cross-domain recurrent practice.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job -> personal nonwork life.

**RECIPROCITY:** earlier leisure intellectuality predicts later complexity for men.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** self-report; not direct observation; women have simulated prior leisure stability.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** do not reduce generalization to one hidden trait.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** supports cross-domain realized-practice routing; D2 recurrence/trajectory and existing V3 practice/learning owners.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-08 — STRATIFICATION/CLASS -> SELF-DIRECTION CONDITIONS -> PSYCHOLOGICAL OUTCOMES

**SOURCE:** chs. 1 and 7, especially pp. 154-190.

**CLAIM STATUS:** decomposition/mediation in observational/longitudinal SEM.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** social-stratification position or class position.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** differential access to/placement in jobs with particular complexity, supervision and routinization.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** actual occupational self-direction conditions.

**REALIZATION LAW:** actor must occupy concrete job with measured conditions; category membership alone is insufficient.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** existing V3 side.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** multiple distinct values/orientations/cognitive outcomes.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** position -> condition -> recurrent activity -> developmental process.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** class/stratification -> organization/job -> person.

**RECIPROCITY:** occupational self-direction and later placement are reciprocal.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** education/income retain some independent effects; mediation is substantial, not complete.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** class/stratification does not directly instantiate a trait.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** strongest M6 support for D1.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## M6-KS-09 — PERSON -> LATER JOB CONDITION THROUGH DELAYED SELECTION/MOLDING

**SOURCE:** chs. 5-7, especially ch. 5 pp. 115-123; ch. 6 pp. 132-153; ch. 7 pp. 171-175.

**CLAIM STATUS:** `LONGITUDINAL_SEM` for person -> later work-condition association; exact action/gate pathway is `AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION`.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** prior psychological functioning/capacity/orientation.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** hypothesized job search, promotion, employer selection, persistence/exit, negotiation or gradual job modification.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** later job with different substantive complexity/supervision/self-direction.

**REALIZATION LAW:** a real external action/organizational or labor-market response must occur; psychological state may not rewrite position directly.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** capacity/orientation can affect action/performance/preferences.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** none directly; this is reciprocal world change feeding later development.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** lagged transition/selection.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** person -> organization/labor market -> later job.

**RECIPROCITY:** strong for ideational flexibility -> later substantive complexity; mixed for other outcomes; direct effects on ownership/hierarchy are weak.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** gate/action unobserved; two waves; could combine selection and job molding.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** no `PERSON_STATE -> STRUCTURAL_POSITION` primitive.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** strongly supports D3's gate/provenance firewall.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE_WITH_EXPLICIT_GATE`.

## M6-KS-10 — CUMULATIVE RECIPROCAL AMPLIFICATION

**SOURCE:** ch. 5 discussion pp. 121-124; ch. 6 conclusion; ch. 13 timing cautions.

**CLAIM STATUS:** `AUTHOR_INTERPRETATION` supported by reciprocal longitudinal paths, not directly observed multi-cycle trajectory.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** initial difference in job complexity/self-direction.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** work changes person; changed person contributes to later work selection; later work generates new exercise.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** potentially widening divergence in work/activity histories.

**REALIZATION LAW:** each cycle requires actual later job/condition change and renewed exposure.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** prior person state affects action/selection; subsequent development remains contingent.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** cumulative change in cognitive/orientational functioning.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** cumulative iterative feedback/trajectory.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** biography + labor market/organization.

**RECIPROCITY:** constitutive.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** only one ten-year transition directly measured; repeated multi-cycle process remains theoretical extrapolation.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** no generic cumulative scalar and no inevitability.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** D2 + D3 composition; no new D4 required.

**DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE_AS_COMPOSITION`.

## M6-KS-11 — OBJECTIVE CONDITION -> SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE IS AN OPEN MEDIATION PROBLEM

**SOURCE:** ch. 13 pp. 297-299.

**CLAIM STATUS:** explicit unresolved issue.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** objectively characterized job condition.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** subjective appraisal/awareness may alter how condition recruits downstream response.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** objective condition can exist even when worker appraises it differently.

**REALIZATION LAW:** objective condition and appraisal must be separately represented if both are used.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** direct subject-side boundary.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** potentially any value/orientation/distress target; not established as one universal mediator.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** condition + episode/history.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** job/person.

**RECIPROCITY:** not central.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** source lacks ideal joint measurement.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `OBJECTIVE_CONDITION != PERCEIVED_CONDITION`.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** supports frozen seam's separation from V3 appraisal owners; no second psychology in D1/D2.

**DISPOSITION:** `REUSE_BOUNDARY`.

## M6-KS-12 — FAMILY/INTERGENERATIONAL TRANSMISSION IS NOT A DIRECT CLASS-INHERITANCE LAW

**SOURCE:** ch. 13 pp. 309-310; related parental-value analyses throughout; ch. 11 as historical cultural speculation.

**CLAIM STATUS:** unresolved/theoretical; direct parent-child value transmission is not established by this book.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** parents' job/social conditions plus family/school/wider environment.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM:** relational interaction, child conditions of life, schooling, direct/vicarious experience; not simply parental class label.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** child's specific opportunities/constraints/experiences.

**REALIZATION LAW:** actual relational/practice transmission must occur.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION:** child acts/interprets within distinct environments.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** child's values/orientation.

**TEMPORAL FORM:** linked lives/intergenerational process.

**RELATIONAL SCALE:** household/school/community.

**RECIPROCITY:** not established here.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** cited parent-child value correlations modest; data insufficient for complete test.

**NEGATIVE CEILING:** no `PARENT_CLASS -> CHILD_TRAIT` and no automatic `PARENT_VALUE -> CHILD_VALUE`.

**RAWLSING MAPPING:** existing `LINKED_LIFE_TRANSMISSION` family is sufficient as a research category; this source does not justify new owner semantics.

**DISPOSITION:** `DEFER_AS_ARCHITECTURAL_EVIDENCE`.

## 13. Card-corpus terminal

The source produces no defensible mechanism that requires a new broad social-developmental owner beyond the candidate D1/D2/D3 composition. Its strongest contribution is finer grain:

```text
STRUCTURAL POSITION
-> ACTOR-SPECIFIC JOB CONDITION
-> ACTUAL TASK/CONTROL REQUIREMENT
-> RECURRENT EXERCISED ACTIVITY
-> DISTINCT PERSON TARGET
-> GATED LATER JOB/CONDITION SELECTION
-> NEW RECURRENT ACTIVITY
```

The non-negotiable additions are invariants and realization requirements, not a D4.
