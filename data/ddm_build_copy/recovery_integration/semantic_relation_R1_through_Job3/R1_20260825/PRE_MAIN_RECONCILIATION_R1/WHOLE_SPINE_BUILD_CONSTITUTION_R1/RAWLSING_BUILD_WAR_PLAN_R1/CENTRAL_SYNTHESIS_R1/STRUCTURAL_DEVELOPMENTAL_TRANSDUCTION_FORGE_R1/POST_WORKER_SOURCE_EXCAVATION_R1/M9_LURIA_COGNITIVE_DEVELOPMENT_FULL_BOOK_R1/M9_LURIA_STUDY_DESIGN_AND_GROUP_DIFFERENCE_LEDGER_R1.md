# M9 — LURIA STUDY DESIGN AND GROUP-DIFFERENCE LEDGER R1

## 1. Design type

The study is best treated as a **purposive, cross-sectional, natural-historical comparative field study with interactive clinical experimentation** during a period of rapid social change.

It is not:

- randomized;
- longitudinal within person;
- a panel;
- a clean before/after study;
- a factorial decomposition of schooling, literacy, work, collectivization, communication, sex, age, or locality;
- a representative population survey;
- a standardized psychometric comparison.

The historical transition is real. The causal decomposition is not.

## 2. Location and historical setting

Luria reports work in remote villages of Uzbekistan and some mountainous regions of Kirghizia, including agricultural villages and mountain-pasture settings. Protocols name multiple localities. The fieldwork occurred in 1931–1932, amid:

- collectivization and changes in agrarian property/work organization;
- new collective-farm offices and administrative roles;
- expanding schools and literacy programs;
- short preschool/agronomy courses;
- beginnings of industrial/technical development;
- changes in women's public participation and seclusion practices;
- widened institutional and interpersonal contact;
- changing religious authority;
- new access to formal/theoretical knowledge.

These changes co-occur. They are not independently assigned.

## 5. Testing procedure and its causal consequences

The testing situation is itself an active social relation, not a transparent window into latent capacity.

Recovered features:

1. long preliminary conversations, sometimes repeated;
2. relaxed tea-house, field, pasture, or camp settings;
3. individual and group discussions mixed;
4. bystanders/other subjects could comment or argue;
5. tasks introduced gradually as familiar "riddles";
6. experimenter follows responses with questions/debate;
7. some tasks include explicit examples, rephrasing, counterarguments, or hypothetical opponents;
8. assistance/teaching is sometimes intentional;
9. exchanges are conducted in Uzbek and recorded by an assistant, with later clean-copy preparation;
10. standard tests were deliberately rejected in favor of locally meaningful, open-ended problems.

### Consequence

The method strongly supports **process tracing of task interpretation** and weakens clean score comparability. The response is a product of participant + prompt + interaction + discourse context.

Rawlsing must never treat the recorded output as a context-free measure of a stable psychological property.

## 7. Temporal court

The book repeatedly uses developmental and transition language, but the empirical comparisons are overwhelmingly between different people occupying different locations in the historical transition.

The study does **not** provide:

```text
same actor at t0
-> observed exposure transition
-> same task battery at t1
-> within-person change estimate
```

It instead provides approximately:

```text
GROUP A with bundle EA at historical time T
vs
GROUP B with bundle EB at historical time T
-> different task performance distributions
```

Individual retrospective statements about "before" and "now" are testimony about biography, not longitudinal cognitive measurement.

Therefore:

`HISTORICAL_TRANSITION_CONTEXT != WITHIN_PERSON_DEVELOPMENTAL_IDENTIFICATION`.

## 8. Group-composition / selection court

Selection is structurally plausible and unmeasured:

- people entering courses may already differ in curiosity, age, aptitude, institutional ties, or family position;
- people selected as chairmen/brigade leaders may already differ in verbal fluency, confidence, arithmetic skill, political standing, or social network reach;
- younger participants are overrepresented in school groups;
- women's groups are concentrated in some conditions;
- location covaries with exposure;
- activist roles themselves alter interviewer familiarity and willingness to treat questions as formal tasks.

The study offers no mechanism for eliminating these selection paths.

## 9. Religion / traditional life

Luria sometimes treats Islam and "traditional" life as inhibitors of independent thought. That is an author-level historical/ideological claim, not a tested exposure contrast. Religion is entangled with sex-segregated contact, schooling access, institutional authority, locality, household roles, and state transformation.

Rawlsing disposition:

`RELIGIOUS_CATEGORY -> PSYCHOLOGY` is forbidden.

Only concrete actor-relative facts such as restricted mobility, institutional authority, schooling access, role obligations, or contact-network constraints may enter, if separately evidenced.

## 10. Design verdict

The study is valuable precisely when decomposed into **actor-relative activity, instruction, communication, institutional role, practical expertise, and testing situation**. It is least reliable when converted back into a civilization-level developmental ranking.
