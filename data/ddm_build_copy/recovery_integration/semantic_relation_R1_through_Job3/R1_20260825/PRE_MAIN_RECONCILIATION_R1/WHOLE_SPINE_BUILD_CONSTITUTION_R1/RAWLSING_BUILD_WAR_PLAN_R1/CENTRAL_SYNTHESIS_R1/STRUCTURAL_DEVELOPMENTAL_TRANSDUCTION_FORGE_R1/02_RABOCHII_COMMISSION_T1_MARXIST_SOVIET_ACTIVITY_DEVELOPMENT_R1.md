## Mission

Central question:

> How do socially organized relations and activity become capacities, motives, meanings, practices and personality over biography without social-category determinism?

Priority chain:

```text

→ ORGANIZED ACTIVITY
→ ACTS / PRACTICES
→ CAPACITY / MOTIVE / MEANING
→ BIOGRAPHICAL DEVELOPMENT
```
