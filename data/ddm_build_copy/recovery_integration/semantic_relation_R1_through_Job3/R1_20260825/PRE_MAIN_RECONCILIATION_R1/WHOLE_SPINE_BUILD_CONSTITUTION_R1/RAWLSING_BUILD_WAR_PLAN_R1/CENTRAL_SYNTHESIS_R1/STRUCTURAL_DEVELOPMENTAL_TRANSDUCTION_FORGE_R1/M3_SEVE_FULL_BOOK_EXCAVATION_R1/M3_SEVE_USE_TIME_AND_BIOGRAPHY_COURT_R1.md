
## 2. What use-time adds

Sève distinguishes:

1. **phenomenal/empirical schedule** — what a time budget or lived report shows;
2. **ideal/intended schedule** — what the person plans/wishes;
3. **real use-time** — his proposed system of actual temporal relations among objective categories of activity.

The architecture-relevant content is not his terminology but the insistence that **equal clock durations can represent different causal relations** when the underlying activity, obligation, social relation, or product differs.

## 4. Finite actor time court

At p. 340 Sève explicitly makes total disposable time finite and, while “simplifying in the extreme” and holding conditions constant, states the opportunity-cost relationship between more learning time and less time for capacity-using activity.

This earns one strong semantic requirement:

`ACTIVITY_TIME_CLAIMS_MUST_BE_JOINTLY_COMPOSABLE_FOR_ONE_ACTOR_OVER_OVERLAPPING_INTERVALS`.

Rawlsing should be able to detect or represent:

- crowd-out;
- competing obligations;
- schedule overlap;
- duration/frequency;
- sequence;
- time made available for learning/use/recovery;
- changed allocation after a transition.

It does **not** earn:

- one universal psychological “time budget” scalar;
- a validated optimum sector-I/sector-II ratio;
- direct inference from long hours to a specific trait;
- the capitalism-specific four-cycle scheme as universal ontology.

## 5. Does use-time require D4?

**Verdict:** `NO_D4_PROVED`.

Frozen D1 already includes `TIME_OR_SCHEDULE_CONSTRAINT` / `TIME_ALLOCATION` in the actor-relative ecology grammar, and D2 is explicitly the interval/recurrence/transition-rebinding candidate. Sève materially strengthens the requirement that these compose across finite actor time, but does not prove a conceptually separate temporal owner.

Required narrowing/generalization:

```text

A conformance rule must permit cross-activity finite-time competition/crowd-out.
```

If an implementation cannot represent this without a new data structure, that is a later compatibility finding, not source evidence for D4.

## 9. Biography as causal/developmental object

Across Ch. III, Ch. IV, conclusion and third-edition postscript, biography is the singular ordered history through which:

- social relations are instantiated in concrete activities;
- acts accumulate in a finite life;
- capacities condition and are conditionally changed by later acts;
- activity structures can be reorganized;
- earlier material can be reworked by later life;
- contingency remains inside socially structured limits.

This is explicitly opposed to personality typology.
