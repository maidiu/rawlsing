## 5. Universal realization-witness validity / polarity law — R4 narrowing

This section is the independent-review-required correction.

Every `ACTIVE` or `REALIZED` consequence assertion MUST cite at least one type-compatible competent realization witness whose validity overlaps the asserted consequence interval and that is not:

- expired;

- invalidated;
- outside the governing rule's lifecycle requirements.

For recurrent-activity or continuing-state claims, the governing witness schema MUST define sufficient:

```text

```

for the exact interval asserted.

### 5.3 Polarity / negation law

```text
MISSING POSITIVE EVIDENCE
!= NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

!= NEGATIVE FACT

NO RECORDED TRANSFER
!= PROVED NONTRANSFER

NO RECORDED DECISION
!= DENIAL

!= NO CHANNEL
```

Missing, expired, superseded, invalidated, or insufficiently current positive evidence yields:

```text

or
```

never a negative actor fact merely by absence.

A negative fact such as denial, absence, non-occurrence, termination, or unavailability requires one of:

1. explicit competent negative evidence;
2. explicit denial/termination/non-occurrence witness;
3. provenance-bearing rule-authorized closed-world observation with exact negative semantics.

Not all objective facts require an event witness.



Thus:

