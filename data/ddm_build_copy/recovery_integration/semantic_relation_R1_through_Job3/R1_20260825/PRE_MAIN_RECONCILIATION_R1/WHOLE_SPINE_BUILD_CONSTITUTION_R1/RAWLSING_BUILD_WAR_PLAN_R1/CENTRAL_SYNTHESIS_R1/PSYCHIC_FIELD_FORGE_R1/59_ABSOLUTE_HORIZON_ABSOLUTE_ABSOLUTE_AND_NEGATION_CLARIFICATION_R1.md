## 0. Purpose

This file preserves a user-directed correction to the current PFF Absolute Horizon understanding before the main finite-person development campaign continues.

It does **not** attempt to solve the full Absolute Horizon theory now.

Its purpose is narrower and more important:

1. prevent PFF from mistaking domain-bounded openness for the terminal Absolute Horizon concept;
2. preserve the existence of an `ABSOLUTE_ABSOLUTE` / all-dimensions limit case;
3. distinguish partial, distorted, captured, or sectorally radical horizon-forms from the vanishingly rare all-cylinders exemplar;
4. correct the provisional `HORIZONAL_FLARE` idea so that flare can arise through the negation of a larger value/horizon projected into other persons, institutions, or collectivities;
5. preserve the decisive developmental fork between collapse into internalized impossibility and intensified trans-bounded orientation;
6. connect this clarification to the PRE-V3 -> V3 developmental bridge banked in File 58 without prematurely adopting mechanism.

This file is therefore a **constitutional reservation and correction**, not a completed ontology.

## 2. The all-cylinders exemplar

The user-supplied contrast between Che Guevara and Jesus is not being adopted here as a historical adjudication of either figure.

It is a **project exemplar pair** designed to prevent doctrinal reduction.

The important structural point is:

- one exemplar may be dialectical-materialist;
- another may be theistic;
- therefore Absolute Horizon cannot be identified with a particular metaphysical doctrine, religious creed, ideology, or vocabulary.

The shared target is instead something like:

```text
FINITE_WORLD_RECOGNIZED
AND
FINITE_WORLD_NOT_GRANTED_FINALITY
ACROSS_THE_PERSONS_MAJOR_DIMENSIONS_OF_ORIENTATION
```

The all-cylinders exemplar is not merely a person with one radical commitment.

It is a person for whom horizon-opening has generalized so deeply that inherited closures cannot remain comfortably absolute merely because they occur in another stratum.

The user example supplies a decisive court:

> If a Jesus-like figure retained a final ethnonational boundary such that the beyond applied only within one people, the figure would not instantiate the same all-dimensional limit case.

The exact theological, historical, and empirical truth of that example is outside this bank's authority.

The **semantic test** is preserved:

```text
TRANSCENDENCE_IN_ONE_STRATUM
+
FINAL_CLOSURE_IN_ANOTHER_STRATUM
!=
ABSOLUTE_ABSOLUTE_ALL_CYLINDERS_REALIZATION
```


## 6. Absolute Horizon projection into other persons

The user correction to `HORIZONAL_FLARE` is especially important.

The prior PFF idea treated flare too generically as an event that makes the previous world inadequate.

The stronger phenomenon is relational and projective.

A person may be anchored to something larger than the immediate institution, role, or relationship and may **project participation in that larger horizon into others**.

Example structure supplied by the user:

```text
"My church does not attend to the political things I attend to,
 but they are good people;
 not everyone needs to care about the same things."
```

That accommodation can remain stable because the actor assumes a deeper shared floor:

```text
DIFFERENT_PRIORITIES
BUT
SHARED_HUMAN_OR_MORAL_HORIZON
```

Then an extreme event occurs — the user's scenario is genocide — and the institution:

- ignores it;
- pathologizes caring about it;
- affirms a mythology that makes the violence necessary, acceptable, or invisible.

The psychic event is not merely:

```text
I_DISCOVERED_A_NEW_POLITICAL_FACT
```

It can be:

```text
THE_LARGER_GOOD_I_ASSUMED_YOU_PARTICIPATED_IN
IS_NOT_ACTUALLY_GOVERNING_YOU_HERE
```

or more strongly:

```text
MY_PROJECTED_ABSOLUTE_HORIZON_IN_YOU
HAS_BEEN_NEGATED_BY_YOUR_ACTUAL_RESPONSE
```

This is a candidate phenomenon of major importance.

It can reorganize:

- trust;
- belonging;
- relational expectation;
- moral recognition;
- institutional identification;
- self-model;
- future association;
- political or spiritual commitment;
- perceived inhabitable community;
- perceived possibility of solidarity.

It may leave the actor's deeper horizon intact, wound it, intensify it, broaden it, narrow it, or force reconstitution of its finite mediations.

```text

!=
ALLOW_FINITE_ROLE_TO_EXHAUST_PERSONHOOD
```



That may be an important manifestation of an enlarging horizon: the oppressed person refuses both the regime's external order and the psychic ontology the regime invites everyone to internalize.

The later theory must distinguish this from naïveté, denial, appeasement, compulsory forgiveness, or failure to recognize danger.

## 13. Cross-stratum propagation is now a central research problem

If the Absolute-Absolute is all-dimensional, then the later theory must explain **propagation**.

A person may first discover non-finality in one arena.

The critical question is whether and how that discovery generalizes.

Candidate developmental sequence:

```text
ONE_APPARENT_NECESSITY_IS_BROKEN
        ↓
THE_PERSON_LEARNS_NOT_ONLY_NEW_CONTENT
BUT_THE_FALLIBILITY_OF_NECESSITY_AS_EXPERIENCED
        ↓
OTHER_FINITE_CLOSURES_BECOME_QUESTIONABLE
        ↓
POSSIBLE_CROSS_STRATUM_PROPAGATION
```

But propagation is not guaranteed.

The same person may remain radically open politically and closed relationally, spiritually open and materially closed, ethically open and nationally closed, or vice versa.

Therefore the later theory must explain:

```text
WHY_DOES_HORIZON_OPENING_PROPAGATE_SOMETIMES
AND_STOP_AT_A_FINITE_BOUNDARY_OTHER_TIMES?
```

This is stronger than simply asking whether capacity is domain-specific.

The terminal object is all-dimensional; the developmental path may be uneven, local, arrested, distorted, reversible, or discontinuous.
