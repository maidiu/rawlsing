## 3. Claim-status classes used in M3

`PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_ARGUMENT` — a substantive claim advanced in Chapters I–III or conclusion.

`INDICATIVE_HYPOTHESIS` — Chapter IV mechanism or law proposed by Sève under his own explicit hypothesis ceiling.

`SELF_QUALIFICATION` — passage where Sève narrows, questions, corrects, or marks prior language as incomplete/tentative.

`RECEPTION_COMPARATOR` — criticism or proposal reported in the postscripts. It does not become Sève's claim unless he explicitly accepts it; even then it remains theoretical rather than empirical proof.

`HISTORICAL/POLITICAL_EXAMPLE` — example used by Sève to illustrate a theoretical relation. It is not converted here into a general empirical effect law.

## 5. Full-book source-frontier judgment

The full book materially deepens the earlier T1 extraction in four ways:

1. it makes **finite actor time and activity allocation** a central theoretical mediator rather than a side note;
2. it makes **biography as ordered activity history** the principal scale on which singularity and development are to be explained;
3. it sharply qualifies reciprocity: psychological individuality does not directly author social structure; transformation proceeds through socially situated action and external social mechanisms;
4. it contains unusually strong self-limitations that prohibit treating sector I/II, P/N, use-time topology, “laws” of progress, or capitalist four-cycle diagrams as validated personality architecture.
