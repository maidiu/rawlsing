## M7-MC01 — SYSTEM_CONFIGURATION_TO_RELATIONAL_CLASS_POSITION

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 3, pp. 31-42; ch. 8; ch. 9.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** conceptualization + comparative observational analysis.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** ownership regime, state/private control, enterprise organization, labor-command relation, hierarchical authority, manual/nonmanual division, sector location.  
**MECHANISM:** the institutional organization of production determines which ownership/control/labor-power relations exist and therefore which concrete class positions can be occupied.  
**ACTOR-RELATIVE OUTPUT:** occupied employer/self-employed/manager/supervisor/nonmanual/manual or production/nonproduction relation, with exact associated powers/dependencies only where separately demonstrated.  
**PORTABILITY:** `GENERAL FORM REPLICATES; CATEGORY REALIZATION IS SYSTEM-SPECIFIC`.  
**US INSTANCE:** employer class and capitalist private-ownership distinction exist; manager/supervisor/manual/nonmanual structure instantiated through firm ownership and hierarchy.  
**POLISH INSTANCE:** no comparable employer class; centralized state economy makes decision location, hierarchical command and production/nonproduction sector position constitutive of the class map.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** class name does not itself entail psychology, autonomy, distress, income, authority magnitude, or a universal activity ecology.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** D1 needs system/institution configuration as upstream provenance. `CLASS_LABEL -> ACTOR_FACT` is unlawful without concrete relation derivation.

## M7-MC02 — CLASS_POSITION_TO_OCCUPATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 5, pp. 113-25; ch. 6, pp. 153-56.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** comparative observational + US longitudinal / Polish quasi-longitudinal directionality.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** concrete class relation and organizational position.  
**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONDITION:** differential access to complex tasks, freedom from close supervision, and nonroutine work.  
**RECURRENT EXPERIENCE:** repeated exercise or restriction of initiative, thought and independent judgment.  
**OUTCOME:** occupational self-direction.  
**PORTABILITY:** `MECHANISM REPLICATES` strongly in US and Poland. Class-OSD associations are very large in both systems despite different class maps.  
**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** exact class definition, organizational hierarchy, task allocation, supervisory regime, sector.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** class is not occupational self-direction; autonomy cannot be built into class and then counted as class's explanatory effect.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** strong support for typed D1 derivation plus D2 recurrence/interval representation.

## M7-MC03 — STRATIFICATION_TO_OCCUPATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 5, pp. 126-49; ch. 6.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** comparative structural equation modelling + US longitudinal evidence.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** education, occupational status, income as dimensions of stratification.  
**MECHANISM:** stratification affects occupational positioning and the actual conditions of work that facilitate or restrict self-direction.  
**ACTOR-RELATIVE OUTPUT:** differential substantive complexity, supervision and routinization.  
**PORTABILITY:** `MECHANISM REPLICATES`, but component weights and institutional routes differ.  
**SYSTEM QUALIFICATION:** education has a distinct career-timing/credential route in Poland; occupational status has greater latent weight there.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** a generic rank or SES scalar is not a mechanism. Each dimension must be preserved where causal use requires it.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** D1 should allow multiple upstream structural facts to derive the same typed ecology dimension without collapsing them into one rank.

## M7-MC04 — RECURRENT_OCCUPATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION_TO_VALUES_AND_COGNITIVE_ORIENTATION

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 5, pp. 118-49; ch. 6, pp. 156-67; ch. 9, pp. 232-62.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** cross-system comparative + US longitudinal + Polish quasi-longitudinal + third-country review.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** actual occupational self-direction, decomposed into task complexity, supervision and routinization.  
**RECURRENT EXPERIENCE:** work repeatedly requires or restricts initiative, judgment, problem-solving and independent action.  
**OUTCOME:** parental valuation of self-direction, ideational/intellectual flexibility, self-directedness of orientation.  
**PORTABILITY:** `MECHANISM REPLICATES` across US and Poland; Japanese evidence reviewed by the authors provides further support.  
**RECIPROCITY:** psychological functioning also affects later occupational self-direction through selection and job molding.  
**SUBJECT-MEDIATION CEILING:** the source shows net causal relations but does not observe the moment-to-moment learning/generalization process.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** repeated work condition does not automatically mint motive, belief, practice, capacity or trait in Rawlsing; V3 must own any earned psychological consequence.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** one of the strongest empirical supports for D2 `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY/CONDITION` and for keeping V3 uptake distinct from social condition.

## M7-MC05 — COMPLEXITY_VERSUS_SUPERVISION_CONTEXT_WEIGHTING

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 5, pp. 147-49; ch. 8, pp. 204-07.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** comparative correlations/regressions; institutional interpretation partly inferential.  
**COMMON MECHANISM FAMILY:** occupational self-direction.  
**US EMPHASIS:** substantive complexity of work is relatively more strongly connected to intellectual flexibility and self-directed orientation.  
**POLISH EMPHASIS:** closeness of supervision is relatively more important for the same broad psychological family and more tightly linked to class/stratification.  
**PORTABILITY:** `MECHANISM CHANGES MAGNITUDE`.  
**POSSIBLE SYSTEM MECHANISM:** centralized workplace authority, broader salience of formal authority, and authority relations extending beyond the immediate task may intensify the experiential force of supervision in Poland.  
**EVIDENCE CEILING:** the authors cannot fully explain why the complexity relation is stronger in the US; the authority explanation is plausible but not experimentally demonstrated.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** do not turn `US` or `POLAND` into a modifier coefficient. Preserve the institutional configuration if known; otherwise record unexplained moderation.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** mechanism identity and parameter/magnitude portability must be separately governed.

## M7-MC06 — JOB_PROTECTION_AND_SELF_DIRECTION_COUNTERVAILING_DISTRESS_PATHS

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 8, pp. 215-23.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** US longitudinal/structural modelling; Polish institutional-historical evidence without comparable actor-level mediator data.  
**US STRUCTURAL INPUTS:** occupational position, union/contract/Civil Service protections, grievance procedure, paid sick leave.  
**US PATHS:**

```text
HIGHER OCCUPATIONAL POSITION
-> MORE OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION
-> LESS DISTRESS

HIGHER OCCUPATIONAL POSITION
-> FEWER JOB PROTECTIONS
-> MORE DISTRESS
```

**PORTABILITY:** US mechanism is directly supported; Polish cross-system actor-level replication is `EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE`.  
**POLISH CONFIGURATION:** nationalized-sector legal protections, late-1960s/early-1970s labor-law equalization, strong manual-worker labor demand, housing/workplace-health/education benefits.  
**POLISH OUTCOME:** manual workers show unexpectedly high well-being; nonmanual workers/managers show greater distress.  
**EVIDENCE CEILING:** the Polish survey did not measure job protections at the individual level in a way that allows the same mediation test.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** institutional law is not automatically an individual realized protection state; the actual actor relation must be established if Rawlsing uses it at actor grain.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** D1 needs legal/institutional provenance plus realized actor entitlement/position; category residual remains until mediation is earned.

## M7-MC07 — EDUCATION_CREDENTIAL_ALLOCATION_AND_CAREER_CONTINUITY

**SOURCE LOCUS:** chs. 3, 5, 6; ch. 8, pp. 202-04.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** comparative observational/historical interpretation.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** education/credential rules, post-war industrial labor demand, state allocation of jobs, technical/managerial staffing needs.  
**ACTOR CONDITION:** whether education precedes occupational entry or continues after occupational placement.  
**POLISH PATH:**

```text
POSTWAR INDUSTRIALIZATION
+ SHORTAGE OF CREDENTIALED TECHNICAL/MANAGERIAL LABOR
+ CENTRALIZED JOB ALLOCATION / CREDENTIAL RULES
-> ENTRY INTO OCCUPATIONAL POSITION BEFORE FULL CREDENTIAL COMPLETION
-> CONTINUED FORMAL EDUCATION DURING CAREER
-> LATER OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION / PSYCHOLOGICAL RELATIONS
```

**PORTABILITY:** education's relation to occupational self-direction broadly replicates; its timing and allocation route are `HISTORICALLY/INSTITUTIONALLY QUALIFIED`.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** educational attainment is not one context-free causal dose; time/order and institutional function matter.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** support for `INSTITUTIONAL_GATE`, `HISTORICAL_TIMING_QUALIFICATION`, and D2 trajectory/history semantics.

## M7-MC08 — FAMILY_LINKED_VALUE_TRANSMISSION

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 7, pp. 171-201; ch. 8, pp. 207-08.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** comparative family data; mixed longitudinal timing.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** family class/stratification, parents' occupational self-direction, parental roles and recurrent parent-child relation.  
**PATH:**

```text
FAMILY STRUCTURAL POSITION
-> PARENT OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION
-> PARENTAL VALUATION OF SELF-DIRECTION
-> OFFSPRING VALUATION OF SELF-DIRECTION
```

**PORTABILITY:** broad linked-life transmission chain `REPLICATES`.  
**SYSTEM DIFFERENCE:** US fathers and mothers both carry substantial transmission; Polish mothers dominate and the direct father-value path is not significant in the final model.  
**POSSIBLE INSTITUTIONAL HISTORY:** gendered family division, rural-to-urban cultural inheritance, church/state assignment of child-socialization responsibility, and a paternal control/punishment role.  
**EVIDENCE CEILING:** these role explanations are not directly measured as the mediating process; father/mother difference remains partly residual.  
**SUBJECT-MEDIATION CEILING:** child accurate perception of the father's values is not required for the statistical effect, but the actual environmental/interpersonal carrier is not observed.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** linked-life mechanism requires exact counterpart/role provenance; no `FAMILY -> CHILD_TRAIT` shortcut.

## M7-MC09 — EDUCATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION_AS_NONWORK_STRUCTURAL_IMPERATIVE

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 7 and ch. 9 review of educational self-direction.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** US evidence only for the key schoolwork-complexity analyses.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** complexity of schoolwork, freedom from close supervision, actual educational demands.  
**RECURRENT EXPERIENCE:** self-directed problem solving and independent educational activity.  
**OUTCOME:** valuation of self-direction and related psychological functioning.  
**PORTABILITY:** `EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE CROSS-SYSTEM`; Polish data lack an adequate directly comparable schoolwork-complexity measure.  
**THEORETICAL IMPORTANCE:** supports the broader proposition that self-direction can operate outside paid work when an activity is a genuine structural imperative.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** a school enrollment label is not educational self-direction; opportunity must become recurrent activity.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** supports general activity-organization grammar but not a Poland-specific positive claim.

## M7-MC10 — RECIPROCAL_PERSON_JOB_SELECTION_AND_MOLDING

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 6, pp. 152-70.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** strongest in US longitudinal panel; constrained/quasi-longitudinal in Poland.  
**FORWARD PATH:** occupational self-direction affects later values, intellectual flexibility and self-directed orientation.  
**RETURN PATH:** intellectual flexibility/self-directed orientation can affect later occupational self-direction by job selection, employer selection, and/or molding the job.  
**ADDITIONAL RETURN:** occupational self-direction relates reciprocally to ownership/hierarchical position in the US models.  
**TEMPORAL DETAIL:** in the best-identified US models, OSD -> ideational flexibility is mainly contemporaneous while ideational flexibility -> OSD is lagged; analogous self-directed-orientation findings are consonant but less decisive.  
**PORTABILITY:** `RECIPROCAL JOB-PERSON MECHANISM SUPPORTED; CROSS-SYSTEM TIMING/PATH STRENGTH PARTLY INCONCLUSIVE`.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** this is **not** evidence for actor action rewriting institutional rules, collective structure, property regimes or social-world law.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** supports bounded `GATED_RECIPROCAL_SELECTION`; does not independently validate D3's full `PROVENANCE_PRESERVING_RECIPROCAL_SOCIAL_WORLD_UPDATE`.

## M7-MC11 — HISTORICAL_LABOR_LAW_TO_CLASS_ECOLOGY

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 8, pp. 216-23.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** institutional history + cross-sectional class outcome pattern; mediator incompletely measured.  
**STRUCTURAL INPUT:** Polish labor-law reforms extending employment security, paid sickness/vacation rights and protections to manual workers; high demand for manual labor; class-linked benefits.  
**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONDITION:** security, entitlement, reduced dismissal exposure, housing/health/education access, relative gain or loss compared with prior class position.  
**OUTCOME CANDIDATE:** altered distress/well-being pattern.  
**PORTABILITY:** `HISTORICALLY QUALIFIED INSTANCE`; no general “socialism protects workers psychologically” law follows.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** because individual protection mediation is not observed in Poland, the final causal chain remains partly `EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE`.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** strongest M7 illustration of why D1 must preserve system-level institutional source provenance rather than emit only an actor condition with its institutional cause discarded.

## M7-MC12 — STRUCTURAL_IMPERATIVE_DOMAIN_PORTABILITY

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 9 review of paid work, educational activity, and housework.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** US longitudinal/cross-sectional supporting comparisons; not equally cross-national for every domain.  
**MECHANISM:** substantive complexity/self-direction has developmental force where the activity is an actual recurring structural demand, not merely an optional or occasional surface behavior.  
**EXAMPLE:** substantively complex housework for women shows relations analogous to complex paid work; the same measure does not operate identically for men, for whom housework generally lacks the same structural imperative.  
**PORTABILITY:** `GENERAL MECHANISM FAMILY PLAUSIBLE; INSTANCE REQUIRES ROLE/OBLIGATION QUALIFICATION`.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** `SAME ACTIVITY LABEL != SAME SOCIAL FUNCTION`; frequency alone is insufficient.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** recurrent activity must carry obligation/role/context provenance where those properties determine its causal meaning.

## M7-MC13 — PARTY_MANAGER_POSITION_UNCERTAINTY_DISTRESS

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 8, pp. 221-24.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** small-cell Polish comparison + interpretive institutional analysis.  
**OBSERVATION:** non-party Polish managers are exceptionally distressed relative to party-member managers and other classes; party membership by itself does not generally predict distress.  
**PROPOSED MECHANISM:** managerial responsibility inside centralized planning without corresponding political security/resources may create uncertainty and exposure.  
**PORTABILITY:** `EVIDENCE INCONCLUSIVE / HISTORICALLY QUALIFIED`.  
**NEGATIVE CEILING:** neither `MANAGER` nor `NONPARTY` is itself a distress mechanism; the institutional relation must be demonstrated.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** retain as a residual-targeted candidate, not a portable rule.

## M7-MC14 — COHORT_STABILITY_WITH_HISTORICAL_CEILING

**SOURCE LOCUS:** ch. 9, pp. 238-41.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** cross-sectional/cohort comparison nested within historical periods.  
**OBSERVATION:** relationships such as occupational self-direction -> intellectual flexibility appear broadly similar across younger, middle and older cohorts in both countries despite different formative histories.  
**PORTABILITY:** `MECHANISM REPLICATES ACROSS OBSERVED COHORTS`, provisionally strengthening the claim that the core work-experience mechanism is not merely one generation's artifact.  
**CEILING:** age, cohort, career stage and historical period cannot be cleanly separated.  
**RAWLSING CONSEQUENCE:** historical timing belongs in provenance/boundary conditions even when no moderation is detected.

## 15. Mechanism-card synthesis

The full source does **not** support a portable rule of the form:

```text
SOCIAL SYSTEM / COUNTRY / CLASS
-> PERSONALITY
```

It supports a portable analytic grammar:

```text
INSTITUTIONAL / ORGANIZATIONAL CONFIGURATION
-> RELATIONAL ACTOR POSITION
-> CONCRETE CONDITION / RECURRENT ACTIVITY / GATE / PROTECTION / COUNTERPART ECOLOGY
-> OBSERVED OUTCOME
```

and shows that portability can take at least four distinct forms:

1. same mechanism, similar effect (`REPLICATES`);
2. same mechanism, different component magnitude (`CHANGES_MAGNITUDE`);
3. same broad structural category, different/reversed outcome because the attached concrete conditions differ (`REVERSES` at category relation, not necessarily at mediator mechanism);
4. one mechanism no longer operates for a particular outcome (`DISAPPEARS`, as occupational self-direction does for Polish distress).
