## T1-M01 — PRACTICE-MEDIATED CIRCUMSTANCE / SELF-CHANGE RECIPROCITY

- **SOURCE:** Marx, *Theses on Feuerbach* (1845), I, III, VI-VIII; https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1845/theses/theses.htm
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL__FOUNDATIONAL_BOUNDARY_AND_RECIPROCITY_LAW`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** objective social relations/circumstances and their organization of practical possibilities.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** concrete human activity/practice in which actors encounter, reproduce, and sometimes alter circumstances.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** changed practical relation to objects/others; potentially changed future possibilities and self-organization.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** circumstances do not manufacture a trait. They matter when instantiated in practical relations/actions; actor action can in turn alter the circumstances.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** constitutive; the actor is an active pole of practice rather than a passive recipient.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** generic self-change / future action organization; no specific trait guaranteed.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `RELATION + REPEATED PRACTICE + TRAJECTORY`; revolutionary change can also be a `TRANSITION`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** person↔social relation; collective practice may modify macro conditions.
- **RECIPROCITY:** explicit and strong.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** requires actual participation/encounter; position alone is insufficient.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** no direct `CLASS -> PERSONALITY` or `UPBRINGING -> TRAIT` inference.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** PRE-V3 structural pressure/affordance supplies objective side; V3 uptake/metabolism supplies downstream actor response; missing connective function is activity-mediated realization between them.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M02 — DIVISION-OF-LABOUR → ACTIVITY-ALLOCATION → SKILL/DEPENDENCY PATH

- **SOURCE:** Marx & Engels, *German Ideology*, Part I.A/C; Marx, *Capital* I Ch.15 §§1,3,8.
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HISTORICAL__MECHANISM_CANDIDATE`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** division of labour, property/control of instruments, task architecture, cooperation, training access.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** allocation to recurring tasks and counterpart relations; tool/process design changes what actions are repeatedly required and what learning is useful/possible.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** specialization, routinization, dependency, skill acquisition/atrophy, altered portability of capacity.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** developmental consequences track the actor's actual task history, duration, tool relation, training, discretion and mobility—not occupational or class label alone.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** prior skill and action affect task performance and mobility; collective action can change organization.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** practical skill/capacity; action repertoire; dependency/autonomy.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `CONDITION + REPEATED PRACTICE + CUMULATIVE PROCESS + TRAJECTORY`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** work group/organization/market relation ↔ individual activity.
- **RECIPROCITY:** workers reproduce the labour process; accumulated skill/collective action can alter it, though control is asymmetric.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** task content, technology, control, training, duration, transferability, alternatives.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** occupation/class category is not a psychological state; historical factory examples are not universal effect estimates.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** strong candidate for `STRUCTURED_WORLD_TO_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_TRANSDUCTION`, especially authority/control and division-of-labour subproblems; existing V3 should own subsequent learning/memory/response.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M03 — SOCIALLY DISTRIBUTED SIGN USE → SELF-REGULATORY FUNCTION REORGANIZATION

- **SOURCE:** Vygotsky, "Instrumental Method" (1930) §§3-18,22; "Genesis of Higher Mental Functions" (1931), general genetic law.
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_WITH_EXPERIMENTAL_PROGRAM`.
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** social availability of language/sign systems and relations in which signs are used to direct or coordinate action.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** the sign/tool enters an action as a functional middle term; initially interpersonal use is repeatedly enacted and transformed into self-directed organization.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** reorganization of attention, memory, planning, inhibition, classification, or other function depending on tool/activity.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** a sign has developmental effect only when functionally used in activity; transition inward changes structure/function rather than copying social content.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** mastery/use matters; the actor recruits the tool to regulate action.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** higher functional organization / self-regulation.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `RELATION + REPEATED PRACTICE + TRANSITION`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** dyad/group → individual functional organization.
- **RECIPROCITY:** interpersonal direction becomes self-direction; the developed actor can then direct/coordinate others.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** access, intelligibility, repeated functional use, developmental readiness, task relevance.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** "internalization" alone is not the mechanism; mere exposure to language/culture does not prove durable change.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** PRE-V3 social contact/interaction topology and actor-specific OEE can feed existing V3 mediator/practice, knowledge, memory and uptake owners; missing connective function is positive specification of sign-mediated activity before durable V3 state change.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M04 — GUIDED COLLABORATION → SUPPORTED PERFORMANCE → INDEPENDENT CAPACITY

- **SOURCE:** Vygotsky, "The Problem of Teaching and Mental Development at School Age" (1931/2017).
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_DEVELOPMENTAL_THEORETICAL__PEDAGOGICAL_MECHANISM`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** organized instruction, access to a more capable partner, task sequence and pedagogical support.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** collaboration/imitation/scaffolding enables action above current independent performance; repeated successful organization is reorganized into independently executable activity.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** newly available capacity and altered next developmental frontier.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** assistance predicts development only for operations within a reachable developmental zone and only when supported performance becomes independently reproducible.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** learner participation and current organization constrain what help can become usable.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** skill/concept/problem-solving capacity.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `EVENT/EPISODE + REPEATED PRACTICE + TRANSITION`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** instructional dyad/group/institution → individual capacity.
- **RECIPROCITY:** learner responses reshape assistance; independent capacity changes future instructional relation.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** task reachability, quality/timing of support, opportunity to practice, later independent demonstration.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** being taught is not equivalent to learning; successful prompted performance is not yet durable change.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** PRE-V3 institutional gate/contact and V3 uptake/learning owners exist; missing function is relation-specific supported-action transition.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M05 — ACTION → OPERATION TRANSFORMATION THROUGH REPEATED/RESTRUCTURED PRACTICE

- **SOURCE:** Leontiev, *Activity, Consciousness, and Personality* (1978), Ch.3 §3.5.
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL__STRONG_TEMPORAL_MECHANISM`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** stable task conditions, tools, goals, and repeated opportunities to perform an action inside a broader activity.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** an initially goal-conscious action is incorporated into another action/activity and its execution becomes conditioned/operational; repeated performance supports technization/automatization.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** skill fluency, reduced need for focal conscious regulation, freed attention for higher-level goals.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** repeated activity can form capacity **without conscious appraisal on every iteration** once the action has become an operation; consciousness can return if conditions fail or the operation becomes a focal goal again.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** initial goal-directed learning and current task control matter; operation remains part of the actor's activity.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** procedural skill/operation/action repertoire.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `REPEATED PRACTICE + TRANSITION + CUMULATIVE PROCESS`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** person-task-tool within a socially organized activity.
- **RECIPROCITY:** improved operations alter what higher-order actions become feasible.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** sufficient repetition/stability, feedback, task continuity; changing conditions may deautomatize or invalidate the operation.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** no requirement that every repetition be consciously evaluated; no claim that repetition always improves skill.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** existing V3 memory/trace/learning and mediator/practice owners can retain durable effects; upstream missing connective function should represent recurring activity opportunity/task conditions rather than duplicate learning.
- **DISPOSITION:** `REUSE + GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M06 — OBJECTIVE MEANING × MOTIVE RELATION → PERSONAL SENSE

- **SOURCE:** Leontiev, *Activity, Consciousness, and Personality* (1978), Ch.4 §§4.3-4.4 and Ch.5 §5.4.
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL__ACTOR-RELATIVE_MEANING_MECHANISM`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** socially/historically available meanings, norms, objects and activity roles.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** the objective meaning is encountered/appropriated in activity and communication, while its personal sense is generated by the relation of the object/action to the actor's motive/activity hierarchy.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** same structural meaning can matter differently to different actors or to the same actor across activities.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** objective significance is neither actor uptake nor personal sense. Sense requires placement within an actually motivating activity.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** maximal; motive relation differentiates actor-specific consequence.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** personal sense, motive organization, meaning-use.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `RELATION`; repeated use can become `TRAJECTORY/CUMULATIVE`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** society/institutional meaning ↔ actor activity.
- **RECIPROCITY:** actor activity can reproduce or change shared meanings/products.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** intelligibility/access, actual activity relevance, motive configuration, competing meanings.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** shared norm/category cannot be read directly as shared subjective meaning or trait.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** aligns with ADP-015 objective vs actor-perceived relationship and CAN-011 mediator/practice; upstream forge should deliver objective role/meaning exposure, not pre-fill personal sense.
- **DISPOSITION:** `REUSE`.

## T1-M07 — ACTIVITY/MOTIVE HIERARCHY REORGANIZATION → PERSONALITY DEVELOPMENT

- **SOURCE:** Leontiev, *Activity, Consciousness, and Personality* (1978), Ch.5 §§5.3-5.5.
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL__BIOGRAPHICAL_MECHANISM`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** plurality of real social relationships instantiated as activities, objects, obligations and opportunities.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** activities/motives intersect and change rank; action-object relations can acquire independent motivating force; new connections can reorganize the hierarchy of activities.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** changed priorities, motive system, self-relation and stable patterning of future activity.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** personality is produced through transformations in the system/hierarchy of activities, not by adding traits corresponding to environments.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** prior motive hierarchy filters/reweights new activity; actions can create new motives.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** motive hierarchy / personality organization.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `RELATION + TRANSITION + TRAJECTORY + TURNING-POINT-CANDIDATE`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** multiple life domains/social relationships ↔ person.
- **RECIPROCITY:** changed person selects and reorganizes future activities/relations.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** salience, duration, cross-domain connection, conflict among motives, available alternatives.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** occupation/activity membership says nothing by itself about personality; turning point is not automatically a distinct carrier.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** downstream V3 motive/need/expectation, rupture/metabolization and attractor-transition owners are relevant; missing upstream function is delivery of patterned activity ecology and cross-domain relations, not a second personality engine.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M08 — ACTS ↔ CAPACITIES REPRODUCTION

- **SOURCE:** Sève, *Man in Marxist Theory*, "Basic concepts: acts capacities, and the problem of needs," especially definition of capacities and Sector I/II activity; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.1.htm
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS__HIGH_RELEVANCE`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** socially positioned set of objectively possible/required acts and access to activities in which capacities can be developed or merely exercised.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** capacities enable acts; many acts in turn produce/develop capacities. Sector I denotes capacity-producing/developing activity; Sector II chiefly uses existing capacities to produce other effects.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** differential expansion, maintenance or stagnation of practical capacities across biography.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** capacity change depends on the composition and sequence of actual acts, not social category. Same category can yield different biographies because activity composition differs.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** existing capacities shape which acts can be performed; acquired capacities then change reachable activity.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** capacity stock/repertoire and its reproduction.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `REPEATED PRACTICE + CUMULATIVE PROCESS + TRAJECTORY`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** social activity system ↔ singular biography.
- **RECIPROCITY:** explicit act↔capacity loop.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** opportunity, time, task quality, feedback, transfer, competing obligations, biological constraints.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** Sève's Sector I/II is a theoretical analytic distinction, not a validated universal metric or typology.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** complements existing V3 learning/memory with an upstream account of how structural organization changes the **distribution of capacity-forming practice**; candidate missing connective function under division-of-labour/activity form.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M09 — USE-TIME ALLOCATION → DIFFERENTIAL CAPACITY REPRODUCTION

- **SOURCE:** Sève, "Infrastructures and superstructures. Use-time," especially the definition of real use-time and finite time allocation; https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.2.htm ; "Laws of development... Biography," https://www.marxists.org/archive/seve/works/1974/ch4/4.2.3.htm
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS__TEMPORAL_FRONTIER`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** externally constrained schedules, labour/care obligations, institutional timetables, control over discretionary time, and objective activity categories.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** finite available time creates a composition of actual activity; increasing time in capacity-producing activity necessarily competes with other uses under fixed time budget; social relations constrain how freely that composition can change.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** unequal opportunity for repeated learning, practice, recovery, social participation and therefore capacity development.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** objective time constraint -> actual activity allocation -> repeated acts -> capacity/motive consequences. The path may operate even when the actor does not consciously appraise each iteration.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** actors plan/resist/reallocate within constraints; existing capacities and motives change preferred/possible allocations.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** capacity trajectory; activity/motive organization; potentially biography-wide development.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `CONDITION + REPEATED PRACTICE + TRAJECTORY + CUMULATIVE PROCESS`; local reallocation may be a `TRANSITION`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** household/work/institutional schedule ↔ person; cross-domain.
- **RECIPROCITY:** actor capacities/actions can alter future schedule/access, but external control may dominate.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** actual schedule control, substitutability of activities, quality of practice, resource constraints, life stage, chance events.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** clock time alone is not psychological exposure; Sève calls the topology hypothetical and warns against confusing empirical time budgets with theoretically meaningful use-time.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** highly specific candidate for PRE-V3 `space/time control` and `activity ecology` transduction; downstream capacity/motive changes remain V3-owned.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## T1-M10 — EXTERNAL CONDITION × INTERNAL CONDITION → SUBJECT-MEDIATED RESPONSE

- **SOURCE:** Rubinstein, *Being and Consciousness* (1957), determinism discussion and Ch.IV; *Foundations of General Psychology*, Ch.XIII "Action."
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL__BOUNDARY/REALIZATION_LAW`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** external causal condition or social demand.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** effect occurs through the actor's current internal conditions—formed organization, capacities, motives, consciousness—expressed in activity.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** heterogeneous response to nominally similar external conditions.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** external cause is neither sufficient description nor direct psychological content; current person organization conditions the realized effect.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** explicit.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** variable; response may alter later internal conditions through activity.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `EVENT/CONDITION -> RESPONSE`; repeated cycles can form `TRAJECTORY`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** environment/social relation ↔ person.
- **RECIPROCITY:** activity changes world and actor.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** must specify which internal condition and how it mediates; otherwise formula is empty.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** "internal conditions" cannot be used as an unexplained latent trait or residual term.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** existing V3 actor-state/mediator owners already embody much of the downstream half; T1 contribution is mainly a law against direct structure→person jumps.
- **DISPOSITION:** `REUSE`.

## T1-M11 — ORGANIZED CONCEPT-GENERATING ACTIVITY → CONCEPTUAL CAPACITY

- **SOURCE:** Davydov, *Types of Generalization in Instruction*, printed pp.151,161; https://www.marxists.org/archive/davydov/generalization/generalization.pdf
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_PEDAGOGICAL__RESEARCH-PROGRAM_SUPPORTED`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** curriculum/task design, teacher organization, access to objects/models and sequences of investigative action.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** learner performs structured object-related actions that reconstruct the generative relation of a concept in compressed "quasi-investigative" form.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** capacity to act conceptually/generatively rather than merely recall a verbal definition.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** concept mastery follows from adequate activity with the relevant relation/object, not from exposure to the concept label alone.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** learner must execute/coordinate the operations; teacher cannot substitute performance for learner.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** conceptual/theoretical thinking and transferable method of analysis.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** `REPEATED PRACTICE + TRANSITION`; curriculum supplies a `CONDITION`.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** classroom/institution/task system ↔ learner.
- **RECIPROCITY:** learner performance changes subsequent instruction and available tasks.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** quality of activity model, prior knowledge, object relation, opportunity to execute and generalize.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** school learning mechanism is not automatically a universal personality law.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** candidate exemplar of institutional gate -> organized activity ecology -> V3 learning; shows why positive upstream activity structure must be represented separately from downstream memory/knowledge state.
- **DISPOSITION:** `GENERALIZE`.

## Empirical corroboration card — bounded, not architectural authority

### T1-E01 — LURIA FIELD COMPARISON OF PRACTICE/EDUCATION/COMMUNICATION AND COGNITIVE FORM

- **SOURCE:** Luria, *Cognitive Development* (1976), Ch.1 "The Problem," especially Research Situation, groups 1-5, Procedures and Research Plan; https://www.marxists.org/archive/luria/works/1976/problem.htm
- **CLAIM STATUS:** `PRIMARY_FIELD_COMPARATIVE__CAUSALLY_CONFOUNDED`
- **STRUCTURAL INPUT:** bundled changes in schooling/literacy, collective work/planning, communication, technology access and social relations.
- **MEDIATING MECHANISM:** hypothesized participation in new practices and linguistic/symbolic activity.
- **ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE:** differences in classification, abstraction and reasoning performances.
- **REALIZATION LAW:** only associative under this design; cannot isolate any one structural cause.
- **SUBJECT MEDIATION:** practical experience, language and learned problem framing visibly matter.
- **DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET:** cognitive strategy/system organization.
- **TEMPORAL FORM:** mainly `CONDITION/TRANSITION`; design is cross-sectional comparative rather than true longitudinal trajectory.
- **RELATIONAL SCALE:** rapidly changing regional/institutional practice ↔ participant.
- **RECIPROCITY:** not cleanly estimated.
- **BOUNDARY CONDITIONS:** selection, literacy, schooling, role, gender, geography, political transition, interviewer/task effects.
- **NEGATIVE CEILING:** no `SOCIALISM/ETHNICITY/CLASS -> COGNITIVE TRAIT` inference.
- **RAWLSING MAPPING:** evidence that activity ecology is worth representing, but not sufficient to define its universal causal law.
- **DISPOSITION:** `DEFER_AS_EMPIRICAL_CORROBORATION`.
