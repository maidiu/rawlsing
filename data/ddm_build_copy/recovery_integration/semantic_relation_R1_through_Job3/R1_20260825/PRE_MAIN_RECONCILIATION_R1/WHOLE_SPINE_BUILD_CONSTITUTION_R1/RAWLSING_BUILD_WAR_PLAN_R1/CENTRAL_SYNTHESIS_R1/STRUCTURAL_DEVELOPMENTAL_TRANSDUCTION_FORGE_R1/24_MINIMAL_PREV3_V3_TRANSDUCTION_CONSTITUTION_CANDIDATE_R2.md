## 0. Constitutional purpose

Own exactly one missing/generalized positive seam function:

```text
ADMITTED ACTOR-INDEPENDENT SOCIAL / STRUCTURAL FACTS
+ EXACT ACTOR / POSITION / CONTEXT / TIME / HISTORY / COUNTERPART BINDINGS
+ EXACT TYPED MECHANISM RULE
```

## 2. Required inputs

A positive D1' derivation requires the exact applicable subset of:

1. `STRUCTURAL_SOURCE_FACTS`
   - source-owned empirical/social facts;
   - PRE-V3-admitted structural facts where structural meaning is claimed;
   - property, institutional, authority, rule, resource, route, topology or other competent facts.

2. `ACTOR_BINDING`
   - exact actor identity;
   - active developmental phase where relevant;
   - world/context/time binding.

3. `POSITION_BINDING`
   - actual position/role/relation/eligibility/location-in-practice relevant to the mechanism;
   - never demographic category alone.

4. `MECHANISM_PRIMITIVE`
   - one or a composition of the five admitted primitive classes in §5.

5. `COUNTERPART_OR_INSTITUTION_BINDING`
   - exact counterpart, household member, supervisor, employer, gate, school, clinic, state office, network channel or organization where relational.

6. `HISTORY_BINDING`
   - prior institutional mark, position, transition, relation, resource, recurrence or realized history only where required for current applicability.

7. `INSTITUTIONAL_CONFIGURATION_PROVENANCE`
   - mandatory where system/institutional configuration constitutes the mechanism or limits portability.

8. `VALIDITY / EVIDENCE CEILING`
   - historical/context scope;
   - source-claim status;
   - any portability or identification limit needed to prevent overgeneralization.

All inputs must be provenance-bearing and competent under project-current authority.

## 5. Five primitive mechanism classes

D1' admits only five primitive mechanism classes at constitutional registry grain.

### P1 — MATERIAL_ACCESS_DEPENDENCY

Function:

```text
property/resource/service/transfer relation
+ exact actor position
-> objective material access, entitlement, deprivation,
   dependency, security, exit or resource-transfer consequence
```

Examples may include wage dependence, ownership, public benefit access, care-resource transfer, infrastructure/service capacity, debt/resource loss, housing/resource access.

`CLASS`, `POVERTY`, `WEALTH`, `SOCIAL CAPITAL` and similar summary labels are not primitives.

### P2 — DIRECTED_AUTHORITY_OBLIGATION_SANCTION

Function:

```text
directed authority/role/obligation rule
+ source/counterpart
+ target actor
-> objective control, command, duty, right, sanction,
   responsibility or maneuver-space consequence
```

Directionality and scope are mandatory.

Examples: supervisor-worker authority, household care obligation, enforceable institutional duty, role-set sanction, grievance right.

### P3 — ORGANIZED_ACTIVITY_TIME

Function:

```text
organized task/activity/schedule relation
+ actor participation/position
-> objective task content, activity requirement/opportunity,
   discretion, routinization, schedule, duration, intensity,
   recurrence or finite-time competition consequence
```

This primitive may encode objective work/activity quality and time organization, but never downstream learning/capacity/motive change.

### P4 — INSTITUTIONAL_GATE_TREATMENT

Function:

```text
institutional rule/record/classification/eligibility
+ exact gate/application/decision context
-> objective eligibility, evaluation, treatment,
   admission/denial or route consequence
```

Required noncollapse:

```text
RULE != APPLICABILITY != ENCOUNTER != TREATMENT != ACCESS/DENIAL
```

A demographic/classification cue matters only if source-owned evidence shows it is presented/recognized at a concrete gate and actual treatment is separately realized.

### P5 — COUNTERPART_CHANNEL_RELATION

Function:

```text
relation/network/activity-focus/channel structure
+ exact actor/counterpart bindings
-> objective counterpart reachability, information/support/obligation channel,
   linked-life transmission route, co-participation possibility,
   contribution/withdrawal or relation-specific transfer consequence
```

`NETWORK MEMBERSHIP`, `TIE COUNT`, `KINSHIP`, `HOUSEHOLD` and `SOCIAL SUPPORT` are not self-executing primitives.

```text
  credential/gate, household transfer and counterpart relations
```

## 8. Multi-context concurrency law

One actor may simultaneously occupy multiple contexts and positions.

```text
LOCATION != POSITION
ONE ACTOR != ONE CURRENT CONTEXT
```

D1' must permit simultaneous consequences from work, household, school, care, institutional, network and other contexts without collapsing them into one monolithic context or one social-ecology scalar.

Cross-context consequence requires a typed carrier:

- material/resource transfer;
- time/schedule coupling;
- exact relation/counterpart;
- information channel;
- institutional route;
- realized actor action.

Generic `spillover` is not a mechanism.
