This examination opens the seam exposed after the Enneagram attractor-topology and secondary-organization passes:

> How does a finite historical/social world constituted upstream of V3 become the actual lived developmental history through which one generated person acquires expectations, strivings, mediators, tensions, practices, memories, relationship patterns, self-model, defensive organization, knowledge/accessibility structure, recurrent organization, attractor gravity, condition and other durable person-specific consequences — without PRE-V3 writing a demographic personality dossier into the actor?

The strongest answer is not a direct mapping from demographic profile to psyche.

It is a **causally staged developmental bridge** in which upstream structure shapes the distribution and consequence-geometry of possible life events, while only actual actor-specific V3 history can realize psychological development.

## 6. Availability, applicability, opportunity and encounter must remain distinct

A major bridge error would be to collapse:

```text
AVAILABLE_IN_WORLD
=
AVAILABLE_TO_THIS_POSITION
=
REALISTIC_OPPORTUNITY_FOR_THIS_ACTOR
=
ACTUALLY_ENCOUNTERED
=
PSYCHOLOGICALLY_INTERNALIZED
```

These are non-equivalent.

A disciplined developmental chain is closer to:

### 6.1 Structural availability

A route, institution, practice, role, counterpart type or event exists in the relevant finite world.

### 6.2 Position-relative applicability

The route or condition is relevant to a generated actor's social/material/legal/temporal position.

### 6.3 Actor-relevant opportunity or constraint configuration

Given the actor's current position and prior history, the route is sufficiently reachable, avoidable, costly, blocked, incentivized, dangerous or facilitated to form part of the actor's current possibility set.

### 6.4 Encounter/exposure

The actor actually comes into contact with an event, institution, person, practice, demand, sanction, resource, symbol or route.

### 6.5 Route realization

A path is actually taken, imposed, refused, interrupted, completed or otherwise historically realized.

### 6.6 Actor-specific uptake

The event becomes this actor's received event through their current person state, history, expectations, mediators, knowledge, relationships, self-model and condition.

### 6.7 Response/metabolism

The actor transforms, buffers, resists, reproduces, deflects, amplifies, reroutes, practices, accepts, rejects or otherwise responds.

### 6.8 Consequence

The world and relationships return actual consequences.

### 6.9 Durable update if earned

Only competent V3 causal rules may make persistent changes.

Therefore:

```text
AVAILABILITY
!= APPLICABILITY
!= OPPORTUNITY
!= ENCOUNTER
!= UPTAKE
!= RESPONSE
!= DURABLE_CHANGE
```

This chain is the basic anti-stereotype machinery of historical person formation.

## 18. Order and path dependence are essential

The same multiset of events can produce different development if the order differs.

For example:

```text

-> later institutional rejection
```

may be metabolized differently from:

```text

-> later safe attachment
```

Likewise:

- acquiring a practice before crisis differs from learning it after repeated failure;


