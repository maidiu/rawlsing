## 1. Terminal demand

Rawlsing must not render a generated person by giving an LLM a static persona dossier and asking it to roleplay.

The machine must preserve a real generated person whose whole causal organization exists independently of any one prompt, while making only the appropriately activated parts of that person available to expression in a particular interaction with a particular interlocutor.

The central question is:

> Given everything this person is and has ever been, what is available to them right now; how are they activated by this interaction with this particular person; what becomes conscious, peripheral, affectively felt, distorted, inhibited or unexpectedly emergent; and what actually comes through into observable expression, intentionally or involuntarily?

The result is not a prose description of psychology. It is a governed state transformation between generated personhood and rendered discourse.

## 2. Four noncollapsible depth bands

### D — TOTAL PERSON / EVERYTHING THEY ARE

Persistent generated causal personhood, principally V3-owned:

- generated life history;
- positions, transitions, relationships, household and genealogy;
- organismic/material/regulatory state;
- causal memories and traces;
- dispositions and disposition-relations;
- wounds, defenses, habits and learned strategies where governed;
- latent motives and needs;
- attractor organization;
- contradictions and compartments;
- standing relational expectations;
- inaccessible memories and causal traces;
- everything causally efficacious whether or not consciously accessible.

This is not a prompt surface.

### C — STANDING CONSCIOUS SELF / WHO THEY GENERALLY ARE TO THEMSELF

Relatively persistent self-known organization:

- self-descriptions;
- consciously endorsed values and goals;
- autobiographical self-narrative;
- ordinary self-understanding;
- recognized motives;
- known relationships;
- conscious worldview;
- current life situation and habitus as experienced by the actor;
- family/friend/job/partner/living situation;
- optimism/pessimism, goals, burdens and ordinary future orientation.

This layer is historically produced and revisable. It can be destabilized by rupture, breakup, grief, drugs, conversion/deconversion, migration, humiliation, illness, love, parenthood, war, status loss or other sufficiently strong generated events.

`TOTAL_PERSON != STANDING_SELF_MODEL`.

### B — RELATIONAL ACTIVATION / WHO COMES ALIVE HERE

The dynamically activated configuration produced by:

`actor × interlocutor × relationship history × current utterance/action × current situation × prior live state`.

This is not merely topic relevance. It includes activation of:

- dispositions;
- motives;
- relational expectations;
- memories and memory echoes;
- attachment/security/threat patterns;
- attraction/aversion;
- shame/pride;
- status dynamics;
- self-versions;
- defenses and inhibition;
- transferential/resonance patterns;
- contradictory subsystems;
- bodily/affective activation;
- unresolved material from preceding turns.

The same actor may exhibit stable but different relational organizations with different people without becoming multiple personalities.

### A — WHAT COMES OUT

Observable surface manifestation after consciousness, inhibition, defense and self-presentation mediation:

- deliberate disclosure;
- concealment;
- fronting;
- playing cool/coy/tough;
- piety;
- status or success performance;
- narcissistic performance;
- intellectualization;
- humor/irony;
- attack;
- confession;
- softness;
- silence/deflection;
- involuntary leakage;
- hesitation;
- unintended warmth;
- accidental innuendo;
- story/memory selection whose deeper trigger is not recognized by the actor;
- tone or phrasing that gives something away without conscious intention.

`ACTIVATED != CONSCIOUS != SELF_RECOGNIZED != INTENDED != EXPRESSED`.

1. discourse -> fixed machine psychic coordinates;

The psyche engine between them is not the LLM.

## 5. Numerical, dimensional, directional, ambiguous

The target representation is not binary labels.

For every registered dimension, exact semantics must determine whether it admits:

- magnitude;
- direction/polarity;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- target;
- participant role;
- temporal/discourse scope;
- relational scope;
- multiplicity of interpretations.

Intensity and confidence are distinct:

`HIGH_INTENSITY_LOW_CONFIDENCE != LOW_INTENSITY_HIGH_CONFIDENCE`.

An utterance may preserve several non-equivalent readings rather than selecting one winner.

Example conceptually:

```text
READING_1 p=.55 -> teasing/intimacy/flirtation weighted strongly
READING_2 p=.33 -> criticism/presumed-knowledge/face-threat
READING_3 p=.12 -> situational commentary with weak personal targeting
```

These numbers are illustrative only. Numeric semantics must be explicitly governed, calibrated and replayable.

## 6. Signal as sent != signal as received

The machine must preserve at least:

```text
UTTERANCE
!= SURFACE INTERACTION ENCODING
!= SPEAKER INTENTION
!= ACTOR-PERCEIVED INTERACTION
!= PSYCHIC EFFECT
```

The discourse encoder estimates interaction form from available conversational evidence.

Then a persona-specific perceptual transformation consumes:

- standing self-model;
- relationship state/history;
- current RAW state;
- perceptual sensitivities/blind spots;
- active expectations;
- affect;
- defenses;
- learned relational topology.

This transform may amplify, attenuate, reweight or cross-convert dimensions.

Examples:

```text
weak distance signal -> strong perceived rejection
care -> perceived control
curiosity -> perceived intrusion
teasing -> perceived humiliation
deference -> perceived insincerity
ambiguous friendliness -> perceived flirtation
```

The same incoming signal can therefore strike different generated persons differently.

This is the required `friction` / receiver-specific distortion layer.

## 7. Persistent live Relational Activation Workspace

PFF requires an actual stored live state, not prompt-only planning.

The RAW is the small working-state representation of the person-in-this-interaction.

It must support at least the eventual semantic equivalents of:

- currently activated refs;
- recently active refs;
- inhibited refs;
- unresolved tensions;
- active relationship-specific expectations;
- active memory/resonance refs;
- current conscious-access refs;
- peripheral/felt-but-unarticulated refs;
- presentation/defense state;
- involuntary leakage tendencies;
- activation origins and causal/session provenance;
- persistence/hysteresis/reinforcement;
- decay or persistence class only where governed.

The exact object model is for the PFF architecture campaign to derive.

A turn may alter RAW substantially without durably changing V3.

`RAW_TURN_OR_SESSION_CHANGE != DURABLE_V3_PERSON_CHANGE`.

## 8. Present conscious field

The present conscious field is not identical to the RAW and not identical to total actor-accessible knowledge.

It must be able to distinguish, where governed:

- focal;
- peripheral;
- affectively felt but unnamed;
- bodily/organismic felt;
- remembered;
- conceptually recognized;
- self-recognized motive;
- uncertain;
- intrusive;
- suppressed;
- inaccessible despite causal activation;
- actor interpretation/self-explanation;
- actor nonrecognition or misrecognition.

The person may be causally governed by material they do not know about.

`DETERMINATION != TRANSPARENCY`.

## 9. Expression / defense mediation

The next state is not prose. It is a bounded directional expression trajectory.

It must support the distinction between:

- spontaneous expression;
- deliberate disclosure;
- concealment;
- inhibition;
- defensive transformation;
- self-presentation;
- relationship-specific posture;
- involuntary leakage.

A hidden cause should generally not be exposed to the final renderer as actor knowledge.

Example architecture:

```text

latent attraction active
actor does not recognize it
interlocutor resonates with earlier relational pattern

FINAL RENDERER RECEIVES:
slightly increased playfulness
higher attentiveness
possible unintended double meaning
do not explicitly acknowledge attraction
do not explain hidden causal dynamics
```

This permits unconscious consequences without omniscient first-person narration.

## 10. Memory and self-model plasticity

The immutable object is what happened in generated history, not the actor-side meaning of the memory.

PFF/V3 boundary work must preserve:

```text
OBJECTIVE GENERATED EVENT
!= GENERATED MEMORY STATE
!= CURRENT MEMORY ACCESSIBILITY
!= CURRENT MEMORY MEANING
!= STANDING SELF-MODEL
```

Retrieved memories may lawfully change actor-side:

- accessibility;
- certainty;
- affective character;
- associative links;
- self-relevance;
- interpretation;
- relation to current motifs/self-model.

No retrieval automatically rewrites objective history.

Strong generated events may destabilize a standing self-configuration, expose previously suppressed material, reorganize autobiographical interpretation and eventually alter durable V3 person state through separately governed V3 transitions.

Depression-like states may affect retrieval/access/specificity/affective coloration without deleting objective history.

## 11. Context-window law

The full generated person must live outside the LLM prompt.

Working analogy:

```text
V3 total person / durable stores = disk
RAW live relational workspace     = RAM
compiled renderer context         = cache
LLM renderer                      = surface realization engine
```

The renderer receives a bounded `THIS_PERSON_RIGHT_NOW` packet, not the entire biography.

Deep candidate retrieval should use multiple channels, not only semantic nearest-neighbor search:

- direct discourse semantics;
- interlocutor/relationship;
- standing self-model;
- active session state;
- associative memory;
- defensive/threatened material;
- attractor/motif organization;
- contradiction/compartment activation.

Candidate nomination never self-authorizes truth.

## 12. Self-output feedback

The actor's own rendered/observable expression may itself become a new perceptual event.

A person can say something before consciously realizing its implications.

Therefore:

```text
rendered utterance
-> actor self-observation
-> possible RAW/conscious-field update
```

In `IN_WORLD_CAUSAL` mode, only exact V3 admission can turn the observable interaction into durable generated history/effects.
