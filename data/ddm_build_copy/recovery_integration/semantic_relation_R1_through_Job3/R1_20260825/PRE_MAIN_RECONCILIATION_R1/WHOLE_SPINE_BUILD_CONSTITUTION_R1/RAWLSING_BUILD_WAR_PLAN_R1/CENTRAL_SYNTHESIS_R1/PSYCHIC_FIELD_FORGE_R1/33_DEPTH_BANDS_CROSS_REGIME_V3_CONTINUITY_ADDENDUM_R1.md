# PSYCHIC FIELD FORGE R1 — DEPTH BANDS CROSS-REGIME V3 CONTINUITY ADDENDUM R1

**Status:** `PRE_ARCHITECTURE_CROSS_REGIME_REFINEMENT__BANKED__MUST_FACTOR__NOT_ARCHITECTURE_ADOPTION__NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY`

## 0. Purpose

This addendum corrects an under-extension in `30_FOUR_DEPTH_BANDS_DEEP_REVISIT_R1.md`.

The prior revisit correctly rejected D/C/B/A as four stacked state layers and noted that the bands can help periodic V3. The stronger current conclusion is:

> For psychologically mediated generated-person development, D/C/B/A are not merely a PFF/chat-frontier explanatory device. They may describe a general recursive person-processing pattern that can operate in both periodic/developmental V3 recursions and live user-interaction recursions, with regime-specific inputs, outputs, persistence and admission rules.

This does **not** make D/C/B/A runtime modules, schemas, or mandatory stages for every V3 event.

## 1. Refined cross-regime meanings

The originating mnemonics can be generalized carefully.

### D — durable/deep generated person

What the person has actually become under V3 causal authority, including actor-inaccessible organization.

Examples where governed:

- history;
- memories/causal traces;
- needs, expectations and strivings;
- mediators, tensions and practices;
- response sensitivities;
- relationships and roles;
- organismic/material state;
- epistemic stances;
- attractor/developmental organization;
- rupture/metabolization history;
- durable self-relevant organization.

D is the durable causal person in both regimes.

### C — standing actor-side self-organization

The relatively standing actor-side organization of who they believe they are, what they know/believe about their life, recognized motives, commitments, roles, autobiographical understanding and anticipated self/future.

C is not necessarily invoked explicitly in every transition and need not contain all conscious material.

It can shape how an event is interpreted while itself remaining partially wrong about D.

### B — situated working configuration

The prior PFF wording `current interaction-specific live configuration` is too narrow for the shared-psychology hypothesis.

Generalized B asks:

> What of this person becomes operatively recruited/configured **in this situation/event/encounter now**?

For user chat, B is specifically user/interlocutor-bound.

For periodic V3 development, B may be event-, encounter-, relationship-, rupture-, decision-, or situation-bound.

Possible B phenomena include:

- selective recruitment of durable expectations/strivings/mediators/tensions;
- actor-specific appraisal of what is happening;
- current conflict among recruited motives;
- active memory resonance;
- anticipation of consequences;
- defensive/regulatory recruitment;
- local accessibility changes;
- current affective/organismic participation;
- path-dependent response readiness;
- relation-specific organization where another person is involved.

B remains a **working configuration over D/C plus current causal input/context**, not a miniature copy of the person.

### A — enacted/observable manifestation

The prior chat-centered definition `what actually comes out` can also generalize.

In PFF/chat, A is primarily the persona's observable response/expression.

In periodic V3, A may be an actor response, action, decision, enacted practice, withdrawal, approach, refusal, coping response, relationship behavior, or other generated actor manifestation produced under the relevant governing rules.

A is not automatically durable change.

`A -> D` still requires exact V3 causal admission/effect authority where a durable update is proposed.

## 2. Shared recursive pattern

For psychologically mediated V3 development, a useful conceptual pattern is:

```text
DURABLE PERSON D
+ STANDING SELF C
+ GENERATED/ADMITTED SITUATION OR EVENT
        |
        v
ACTOR-SPECIFIC APPRAISAL / RECRUITMENT
        |
        v
SITUATED WORKING CONFIGURATION B
  - active expectations/strivings
  - mediators/tensions
  - memory resonance
  - anticipation
  - conflict/regulation
  - conscious + nonconscious participation
        |
        v
CURRENT EXPERIENCE / INTERPRETATION
(where psychologically relevant)
        |
        v
RESPONSE / ACTION / MANIFESTATION A
        |
        v
ACTUAL CONSEQUENCES + EXACT V3 ADMISSION/EFFECT RULES
        |
        v
POSSIBLE DURABLE D/C CHANGE
```

For live user interaction the homologous pattern is:

```text
D + C + CURRENT FAST STATE
+ USER CONTRIBUTION
        |
        v
USER-SPECIFIC APPRAISAL / RECRUITMENT
        |
        v
B_USER
        |
        v
PRESENT CONSCIOUS FIELD / DEFENSE / EXPRESSION FORMATION
        |
        v
A_CHAT
        |
        +----> NEXT FAST B
        |
        +----> DURABLE V3 ADMISSION CANDIDATE IF SIGNIFICANT
```

The point is **one psychology operating under different temporal/persistence regimes**, not identical pipelines.

## 3. Why this matters for V3 quality

A generated life event should not generally be assumed to have the same psychological effect on every actor merely because the external event kind is the same.

The person's response can depend on:

- what they expected;
- what they were striving for;
- what mediators/tensions were already formed;
- what they remember or causally carry;
- what they consciously understand;
- which self-conceptions are threatened;
- what they anticipate next;
- what regulatory/defensive pathways are recruited;
- what prior path dependence makes easier/harder to activate;
- the exact relationship and situation.

Thus a shared D/C/B/A-informed response model can prevent a shallow V3 rule such as:

`BREAKUP_EVENT -> FIXED_WOUND_DELTA`

when the richer person-specific phenomenon may be:

`BREAKUP_EVENT x THIS PERSON'S DURABLE ORGANIZATION -> SITUATED RECRUITMENT/METABOLISM -> RESPONSE -> POSSIBLE DURABLE CHANGE`.

No numeric or implementation rule is adopted here.

## 4. Strong bridge to existing V3 capital

This refinement does not imply that V3 lacks all relevant machinery.

Current/recovered V3 authority already contains useful pieces:

- CAN-010: durable needs / expectations / strivings;
- CAN-011: persistent mediators / tensions / practices with contradiction and path dependence;
- CAN-008: multi-stream actor response transaction with buffering/amplification/deflection/rerouting/transformation/blocking and no scalar-net-pressure collapse;
- ADP-016: causal memory and accessibility distinctions;
- ADP-017: actor epistemic stance / claim-access distinctions;
- CAN-012: rupture/metabolization;
- V3 attractor-development authority/design capital.

The likely task is therefore often:

`EXPOSE / RECONFIGURE / CONNECT EXISTING V3 PERSON ELEMENTS`

not:

`ADD A SECOND PFF VERSION OF THEM`.

## 5. B becomes a particularly important shared concept

The strongest refinement is that B should no longer be thought of as inherently `chat state`.

Conceptually:

`B = SITUATED PERSON CONFIGURATION`

with specialized regimes:

- `B_CHAT`: this user, this turn/session, this conversational residue;
- `B_V3_EVENT`: this generated event/encounter/decision/relationship episode during developmental recursion.

These labels are explanatory only, not proposed classes.

B allows the same durable D person to receive different events differently without rewriting D before the response even occurs.

It is where person-specific causal participation becomes locally organized.

## 6. C is also dynamically relevant to V3 recursion

C is not merely what the chat persona may say about themself.

Standing self-understanding can shape developmental response.

Examples:

- an event threatening `I am competent` may recruit different responses from an event threatening `I am lovable`;
- a person who consciously understands a recurring defensive pattern may metabolize a similar event differently from someone with the same deeper tendency but no such self-recognition;
- an event can expose material inconsistent with C, producing self-model tension before any durable revision;
- repeated admitted experiences may eventually revise C under competent V3 epistemic/self-model authority.

Therefore:

`C = CAUSALLY RELEVANT ACTOR-SIDE ORGANIZATION WHERE GOVERNED`

not merely presentation content.

## 7. A in V3 must remain non-equivalent to durable effect

In periodic recursion, an actor's immediate response/action is not automatically a personality update.

Preserve:

`EVENT != B`

`B != A`

`A != DURABLE D CHANGE`

`OBSERVED RESPONSE != ATTRACTOR TRUTH`

`ONE ACTION != STANDING SELF-MODEL REVISION`

`REPEATED ACTION != DURABLE UPDATE WITHOUT GOVERNED RULE`

This mirrors the chat-side rule that rendered expression cannot be fed back as hidden-person truth merely because it occurred.

## 8. Not every V3 transition requires full D/C/B/A processing

This refinement applies to **psychologically mediated actor development**, not indiscriminately to every V3 state change.

Examples that may not require the full lens include purely material/organismic transitions whose semantics are owned independently and do not involve actor appraisal/response.

Likewise a rule may lawfully operate with only some distinctions explicit.

The bands are a conceptual diagnostic:

> If the change depends on how the person receives, interprets, responds to or metabolizes an experience, which D/C/B/A distinctions must remain noncollapsed?

## 9. Cross-regime continuity law

`D/C/B/A_CHAT != D/C/B/A_V3_DUPLICATE_STACKS`.

Better:

`ONE PERSON + SHARED PSYCHOLOGICAL DISTINCTIONS + REGIME-SPECIFIC WORKING/PERSISTENCE RULES`.

`B_CHAT != B_V3_EVENT IN TEMPORAL/PERSISTENCE SCOPE`.

`B_CHAT AND B_V3_EVENT MAY SHARE TRANSITION LOGIC`.

`A_CHAT != A_V3_EVENT IN SURFACE/CAUSAL ROLE`.

`A_CHAT AND A_V3_EVENT MAY BOTH BE MANIFESTATIONS OF PERSON-SPECIFIC RESPONSE`.

`C MAY SHAPE BOTH REGIMES`.

`D REMAINS V3 DURABLE PERSONHOOD`.

## 10. Architecture burden created by this refinement

A future architecture must demonstrate that it does **not** implement:

- one V3 response psychology for life simulation;
- a second PFF response psychology for chat;
- divergent self-model/memory/defense theories across the two regimes.

It must instead show either:

1. valid shared transition semantics with regime-specific adapters/state; or
2. a demonstrated semantic reason why some mechanism truly differs by regime.

No common component is pre-authorized by this requirement.
