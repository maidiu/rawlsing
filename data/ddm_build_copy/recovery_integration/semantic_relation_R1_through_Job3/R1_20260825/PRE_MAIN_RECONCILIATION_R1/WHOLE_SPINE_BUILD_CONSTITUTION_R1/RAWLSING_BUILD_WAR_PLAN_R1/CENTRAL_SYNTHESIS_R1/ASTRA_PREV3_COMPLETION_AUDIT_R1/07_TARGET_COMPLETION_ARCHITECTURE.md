# Target completion architecture

**Packet status: independent audit evidence and proposed construction program; NOT HQ adoption.** Current accepted authority is cited at its exact scope. Machine registers mirror this Markdown packet and have no independent authority.

## Three distinct architecture layers

| Layer | Required / proposed form | Decision status |
|---|---|---|
| SEMANTIC ARCHITECTURE | Objective evidence, learned/structural/world facts, events/non-events, currentness, authority and replay; V3 owns all person-state. | Task law plus exact adopted component authority; missing exact contracts remain proposals. |
| LOGICAL RUNTIME ARCHITECTURE | Producer-owned DAG, immutable world/release/context roots and governed resolution; direct earlier-producer reuse; no forced eight-stage traversal. | Auditor recommendation derived from terminal responsibilities; requires competent exact adoption. |
| PHYSICAL STORAGE / SERVING ARCHITECTURE | Persistent, source-backed/virtual or hybrid may satisfy the same logical contract. A materialized publication layer is required only where exact semantics/operations demand it. | No universal winner selected. Scoped operating/retention decisions require equal semantics and evidence. |

### 3.1 Semantic answer

The terminal requirement is a **governed, replayable objective-world resolution capability over producer-owned objects with shared immutable world/release roots**. A manifest without resolvable content is insufficient. A query service without fixed authority and historical replay roots is insufficient. A giant serialized payload is unnecessary unless workload evidence justifies it.

The most defensible logical form is a producer-owned DAG plus a world/release/context manifest and governed resolution services. This combines semantic responsibilities; it does **not** select a persistent, virtual or hybrid physical winner. Existing components should be reused or extended according to exact responsibility. No new mandatory V550–V558 serial traversal is implied. The minimal logical request identifies the world/release, as-of basis, target objective entities/relations, requested operation/use and consumer authorization. Its result identifies the selected producer objects, exact authorities/dependencies, validity and coverage, permission ceiling and replay receipt. Concrete type and field names should be finalized by the competent seam owners; this report does not declare a new adopted wire protocol.

An objective scenario may be governed rather than a literal historical census. It must be labeled as such and derive its objective facts under an explicit world-construction rule. Neither a fixture nor an invented context becomes historical evidence. Source observations, governed models and generated objective world facts need distinct epistemic types.

### 3.2 What must remain outside

No PRE-V3 output may contain a generated person’s persona, belief, ideological stance, affect, appraisal, preference, motive, trait, type, interpretation, uptake, response, developmental history or durable subjective state. This restriction applies to latent vectors and convenient “initialization” fields as much as to prose.

PRE-V3 can answer that an actor holds a tenancy, has a legal eligibility, is located within an institutional jurisdiction or faces a material constraint when those are objective facts under competent authority. It cannot answer that the actor therefore feels insecure, prefers hierarchy, identifies with a group, experiences an encounter or has a personality type.

### 3.3 Physical choices remain open

Persistent products, exact source-backed evaluation and a hybrid can all implement the same semantic duties. Chetiri has demonstrated source-backed feasibility and a common harness; it has not selected a production winner. Workload semantics, source retention, admitted authorities and negative behavior must be equal before comparing cost, latency, memory, reconstruction and operational behavior. A winner may legitimately differ by product class; no evidence requires one storage strategy for the entire system.
