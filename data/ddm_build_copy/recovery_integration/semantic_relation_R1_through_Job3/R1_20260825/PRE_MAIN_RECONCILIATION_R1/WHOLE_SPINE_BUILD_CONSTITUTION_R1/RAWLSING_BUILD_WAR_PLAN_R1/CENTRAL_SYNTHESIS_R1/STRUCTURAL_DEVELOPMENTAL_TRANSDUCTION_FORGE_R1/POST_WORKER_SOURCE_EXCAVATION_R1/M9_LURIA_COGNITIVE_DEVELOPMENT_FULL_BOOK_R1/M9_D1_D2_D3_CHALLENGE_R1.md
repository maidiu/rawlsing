## 1. D1 court — does Luria support actor-relative ecology before developmental inference?

### Verdict

`YES__STRONGLY_AS_AN_IDENTIFICATION_REQUIREMENT__NOT_AS_DIRECT PSYCHOLOGY AUTHORITY`

The book is almost a demonstration of why D1's firewall is necessary. Luria's own groups bundle multiple social facts that plausibly matter through distinct routes:

- school attendance;
- literacy practice;
- theoretical/technical instruction;
- agricultural/domestic occupational activity;
- planning and accounting;
- leadership/supervision;
- recurrent collective discussion;
- mutual evaluation;
- broader counterpart networks;
- information access;
- institutional contact;
- gendered mobility/contact restrictions;
- role obligations and schedules;
- practical expertise.

If Rawlsing begins at `PEASANT`, `ILLITERATE`, `MUSLIM`, `KOLKHOZ`, `WOMAN`, `TRADITIONAL`, or `SOCIALIST`, it recreates exactly the identification failure in the book.

D1 should instead require:

```text
ACTOR
+ exact time/context
+ actual role/position
+ actual recurrent activity or instruction
+ actual counterpart / institution where relevant
+ actual communication/information channel where relevant
+ provenance
-> typed actor-relative ecology fact
```

### Narrowing requirement on D1

Luria indicates that `ACTIVITY_ORGANIZATION` is too coarse if it erases the distinction among:

```text
FORMAL_INSTRUCTION
LITERACY_PRACTICE
PRACTICAL_OCCUPATIONAL_ACTIVITY
PLANNING_ACCOUNTING_ACTIVITY
COLLECTIVE_DISCUSSION_EVALUATION
INFORMATION_EXPOSURE
COMMUNICATION_CHANNEL_HISTORY
```

These need not become new top-level ontology classes, but the rule output/provenance must preserve enough type to prevent them from collapsing back into `group exposure`.

### Additional D1 firewall earned by the source

Every performance inference should be able to preserve the **testing/encounter context**:

```text
observed response
!=
context-free actor capacity
```

This is downstream evidence discipline, not a second psychology.

## 2. D2 court — interval, recurrence, transition rebinding

### Verdict

`MEANINGFUL_REQUIREMENT_LEVEL_SUPPORT__WEAK_DIRECT_TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE`

Luria's proposed mechanisms are inherently recurrent and temporally extended:

- school attendance occurs across sessions;
- literacy is practiced;
- work planning/accounting recurs;
- meetings and mutual evaluation recur;
- occupational expertise accumulates;
- communication networks persist;
- role transitions change which activities and information channels become available.

A Rawlsing representation that reduces these to one dramatic event would misrepresent the hypothesized mechanism. This supports D2's insistence that persistent conditions and recurrent activities not be fictionalized as event spam.

But the book does **not** provide sufficient within-person temporal observation to prove:

- exact transition onset;
- before/after cognitive response change;
- recurrence dose-response;
- duration threshold;
- persistence after activity stops;
- turning-point status;
- trajectory redirection.

Therefore Luria supports D2 as a **representation requirement** for independently established social/activity history, not as evidence that D2 can lawfully infer developmental change.

### D2 narrowing

Do not cite the book to justify a new generic `TRANSITION_CAUSES_DEVELOPMENT` operator. Preserve recurrence/interval facts and hand them downstream; the developmental receiver must earn uptake/change.

## 3. D3 court — reciprocal social-world update

### Verdict

`NO_MEANINGFUL_POSITIVE_EVIDENCE_FROM_THIS STUDY`

The historical background certainly involves humans collectively changing institutions, work organization, schools, and social relations. But the empirical study does not trace the commissioned D3 chain at actor/event grain:

```text
actor-generated action
-> external response / institutional gate
-> admitted change in position/relation/rule/resource/network
-> new structural fact with provenance
-> later actor-ecology derivation
```

Luria mostly treats the social transformation as already occurring contextual background. He does not reconstruct provenance-preserved actor-to-institution-to-structure transactions in a way that can qualify D3.

The study therefore cannot strengthen D3 beyond the general theoretical truism that social practice and institutions are reciprocal over history.

Disposition: `NO_NEW_D3_AUTHORITY`.
