## 1. Binding T2 negative ceilings

### NC1 — No structural-category → psychological-trait shortcut

A class, race, gender, poverty, religion, occupational, institutional or other structural/demographic category does not itself establish a motive, personality trait, disposition, belief, reason, coping style, agency type or developmental state.

```text
STRUCTURAL CATEGORY != PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAIT
```

Holzkamp’s restrictive/generalized distinction is particularly prohibited from being turned into a person/group typology.

---

### NC2 — Objective societal possibility is not a subject-relative premise

An opportunity, constraint, rule, route, structural meaning or institutional pathway may exist without becoming salient/relevant in the subject’s grounds for action.

```text
OBJECTIVE POSSIBILITY != SUBJECTIVE PREMISE
```

Rawlsing must not treat structural applicability alone as proof of actor perception, interpretation, endorsement, or use.

---

### NC3 — Premise is not reason

A circumstance may be actor-relevant yet support different grounded actions depending on the subject’s perceived life interests, other premises, dependencies, commitments and feasible alternatives.

```text
PREMISE != GROUND / REASON FOR ACTION
```

The same premise constellation does not license a deterministic action rule.

---

### NC4 — Reason is not realized action

Reasons ground an action from the first-person standpoint; they do not prove that the action happened.

```text
GROUND FOR ACTION != ACTION
```

Competing grounds, changed circumstances, inability, coordination failure and interruption can block realization.

---

### NC5 — Action is not durable development

A realized act/participation enters biography but does not itself establish lasting psychological change.

```text
ACTION / EXPERIENCE != ACTOR UPTAKE != DURABLE CHANGE
```

Downstream response, memory, learning, repetition, relationship history or other V3 processes remain required where applicable.

---

### NC6 — Restrictive/generalized agency is not a personality taxonomy

The distinction concerns subjectively grounded alternatives in concrete situations under limited/threatened agency. It must not be used to label people as “restrictive,” “generalized,” “opportunistic,” “resistant,” “conformist,” or similar enduring types.

No social category receives either label by default.

---

### NC7 — Structural disadvantage does not entail resistance; structural advantage does not entail reproduction

Critical Psychology preserves the possibility of accommodation, bypass, reinterpretation, resistance, coordination and transformation. No one route is guaranteed by structural location.

Population tendency, if separately evidenced, still does not erase a lawful minority/nonmodal route.

---

### NC8 — Life interest is not an exogenous utility scalar

Holzkamp’s life-interest language concerns the subject’s relation to sustaining/expanding influence over life conditions and life quality. R1 does not license converting this into a single observer-assigned optimization score, fixed preference, or hidden trait.

---

### NC9 — Subjective groundedness is not objective correctness or moral approval

An action can be intelligible from a subject’s premise/reason constellation without being factually accurate, objectively beneficial, socially desirable, or morally justified.

This distinction is essential where a subject misperceives a condition while an institution still produces an external causal consequence.

---

### NC10 — Formal position is not enacted participation

An institutionally defined position or rule does not prove that the actor participated in the relevant activity, encountered the relevant counterpart, possessed the expected information, exercised formal authority, or complied with the rule.

```text
FORMAL POSITION != ENACTED POSITION / PARTICIPATION
```

---

### NC11 — One context is not the whole person

Persons participate across multiple contexts with distinct positions, relations, commitments and rhythms. A work, school, household, clinical, peer or institutional context cannot automatically be generalized into a whole-person trait or global life condition.

---

### NC12 — Institutional pathway is not realized trajectory

An age-graded, track-graded, role-graded or normatively expected path is a structured possibility. Transition requires actual realization; trajectory requires a realized sequence/configuration of participation.

```text
PATHWAY POSSIBILITY != TRANSITION != TRAJECTORY
```

---

### NC13 — Transition/event is not automatically a turning point

A turning point is a retrospective/causal role in subsequent trajectory, not a label earned merely by salience or event magnitude. The inspected T2 corpus supplies no universal turning-point threshold.

---

### NC14 — Persistent condition/relation is not just repeated event

Dependency, low influence, ongoing obligation, institutional arrangement, and cross-context conflict may be interval/persistent causal forms. Eventizing them can erase duration, relational continuity, and background constraint.

---

### NC15 — Recurrent practice does not automatically imply skill or personality change

Repeated participation may reproduce arrangements or supply learning opportunities, but T2 does not provide a universal dose→capacity/trait law. Development remains downstream and contingent.

---

### NC16 — Collective possibility is not collective organization or success

A shared structural problem, common category, or compatible interest does not itself create organization, solidarity, coordinated action, or effective power.

```text
SHARED CONDITION
!= COMMON PREMISE
!= COLLECTIVE ORGANIZATION
!= REALIZED COLLECTIVE ACTION
!= SUCCESSFUL STRUCTURAL CHANGE
```

---

### NC17 — Intention is not structural effect

A subject may intend to reproduce, preserve, evade or transform a condition, while realized structural consequences differ from intention. A world update requires an actual causal path from action to relation/condition.

---

### NC18 — Local reproduction/change is not automatic cross-scale propagation

An action that stabilizes a household or local organizational relation does not automatically reproduce a class relation at macro scale; a local successful change does not automatically transform an institution or broader structural field. Scale must be explicit.

---

### NC19 — T2 does not supply a comprehensive allocation theory

The inspected T2 corpus is strong on subject mediation, situated participation and conduct across contexts. It is **not** sufficient by itself to derive exact distributions of:

- wealth/material resources;
- property relations;
- labor-process authority;
- institutional gatekeeping decisions;
- discrimination/treatment;
- network closure/brokerage;
- neighborhood/spatial access;
- chronic stress exposure;
- cumulative advantage/disadvantage.

These remain for cross-lane normalization. T2 may specify how such conditions become actor-relative premises/action contexts once independently established.

---

### NC20 — The inspected corpus is mainly theoretical, not an experimental causal court

Holzkamp, Osterkamp and Dreier are used here as theory/mechanism sources. R1 does not promote their conceptual distinctions to empirically quantified effect sizes or population frequencies. Empirical validation of specific social-causal chains requires appropriate observational, longitudinal, experimental, qualitative or review evidence from relevant lanes/sources.

---

### NC21 — Premise/reason articulation may underdescribe embodied/preverbal subjectivity

Juhl (2019), inspected only in abstract, explicitly raises the problem that bodily/emotional activity—especially preverbal subjectivity—may not be adequately reconstructed as articulated proposition-like reasons.

R1 therefore prohibits pretending that a preverbal actor has verbalizable premises/reasons merely to fit the framework.

---

### NC22 — T2 Rawlsing mapping is deliberately bounded

Per commission, this lane uses only the campaign commission and Phase-0 seam map for Rawlsing mapping. It does not claim to have independently verified exact live implementations/owners for GT3/GT4, OBLs, CANs, ADPs or other repository mechanisms.

Any `MISSING` or `GENERALIZE` judgment is therefore a candidate for Vozhd normalization against the independent R lane, not final architecture adoption.

## 2. Open mechanism questions for Vozhd synthesis

### OQ1 — Does Rawlsing need an explicit subject-relative premise state?

T2 proves the semantic separation:

```text
structural applicability != subject-relative premise
```

But it does not prove that a new carrier is necessary. The Vozhd/R lane must test whether existing actor-perceived/knowledge/access/response owners can represent the distinction compositionally without conflating possibility, perception, premise, reason and action.

**Decision target:** `REUSE_EXACT_EXISTING_OWNER` vs `GENERALIZE_EXISTING_OWNER` vs `NEW_CONNECTIVE_SEMANTICS_REQUIRED`.

---

### OQ2 — What exactly derives actor-relative social ecology from concrete position?

Phase 0 already identifies the missing explanatory compression. T2 sharpens one part: concrete participation positions produce scopes, obligations, counterpart relations, partial information and influence.

Open question: which of these are already lawful outputs of existing opportunity/constraint/topology/context machinery, and which require generalized semantics?

---

### OQ3 — Can cross-context conduct be composed from existing context bindings without loss?

Dreier’s person is simultaneously/serially involved in multiple contexts whose obligations, rhythms and conflicts cross boundaries.

Open loss test:

```text
multiple independent context bindings + biography history
?=
actor-specific cross-context obligation/time/relation ecology
```

If the left side cannot represent conflicts and coordination among active contexts, `MULTI_CONTEXT_PARTICIPATION_TO_CROSS_CONTEXT_ACTIVITY_OBLIGATION_TIME_AND_RELATION_ECOLOGY` remains a real gap.

---

### OQ4 — Where should realized action update later structural possibility?

Phase 0 recognizes reciprocal selection/position/exposure and collective transformation as unresolved. T2 requires the world not to be a one-way source of inputs: realized action can reproduce or alter relations.

Open owner question: whether existing PRE-V3 structural objects are immutable possibility publications requiring a separate temporal world-state producer, or whether an existing lawful update mechanism already exists and simply needs routing.

---

### OQ5 — How should structural effects that bypass subjective uptake be represented?

Subject mediation is essential to reasons/actions, but not every causal institutional effect depends on correct perception. A denied application, schedule change, eviction, reassignment, sanction, or access barrier can alter objective opportunity/constraint ecology even when the subject misunderstands it.

The synthesis must preserve two paths where needed:

```text
objective condition → objective actor-relative consequence
objective condition → subject-relative premise/reason/action
```

These can interact but must not be collapsed.

---

### OQ6 — How are nonmodal/resistant routes kept alive without voluntarism?

The architecture must permit a subject to resist/bypass a likely route, but only when a feasible route/resources/relations make that action possible. It must neither force the modal tendency nor manufacture unconstrained free choice.

Open test: same structural position, different support/network/institutional routes, one actor preserves existing relation while another joins a collective change effort.

---

### OQ7 — What is the minimum temporal representation for condition and recurrent practice?

T2 strongly rejects reducing persistent dependencies or conduct-of-life arrangements to isolated events. The Vozhd should test whether existing state/context bindings plus event-sourced biography can preserve:

- start/end or validity interval;
- persistence without repeated occurrence;
- recurrence frequency/rhythm;
- relation continuity;
- cross-context overlap;
- cumulative consequences without arbitrary event multiplication.

No new carrier should be invented unless exact semantic loss is shown.

---

### OQ8 — Does institutional route machinery distinguish expected from realized trajectories?

Dreier requires at least:

```text
institutionally arranged path
→ actor-relative applicable transition
→ realized transition
→ changing participation configuration
→ trajectory
```

Open test: can an actor decline, fail, delay, reverse or redirect the expected transition without falsifying the structural pathway object?

---

### OQ9 — How is collective agency represented without category essentialism?

Collective action must be assembled from actual participants, communication/coordination, resources, relations, conflicts and realized action. A demographic/class category cannot itself become an agent object by fiat.

Open test: shared structural condition with failed organization versus successful organization.

---

### OQ10 — Embodied/preverbal mediation

If later synthesis requires subject mediation before articulated reasons, inspect Juhl (2019) fully and compare against the existing embodied-condition V3 owner before proposing anything new.

Current R1 status: `DEFER_NONCRITICAL__SOURCE_AND_OWNER_EXACTNESS_INSUFFICIENT_FOR_ADOPTION`.
