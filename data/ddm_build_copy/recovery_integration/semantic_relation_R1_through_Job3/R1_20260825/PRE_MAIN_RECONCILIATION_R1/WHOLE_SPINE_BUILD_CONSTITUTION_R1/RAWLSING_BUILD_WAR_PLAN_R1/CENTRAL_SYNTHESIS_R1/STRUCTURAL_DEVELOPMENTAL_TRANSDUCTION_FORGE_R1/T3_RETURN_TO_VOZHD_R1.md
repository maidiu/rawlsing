## 3. Top retained normalized mechanisms

### `T3-N01 PROPERTY_RESOURCE_DEPENDENCY`

Property/resource relations alter actor material security, exit options, labour-market counterpart sets and recurring income dependency through concrete access/deprivation relations, not via a class psychology.

### `T3-N02 AUTHORITY_TIME_CONTROL`

Employment authority plus schedule/rule enforcement alters actor work duration, recovery/social/care time and exposure duration; legal/collective action can change the rule.

### `T3-N03 ORGANIZATIONAL_COMMAND_SANCTION_EXPOSURE`

Hierarchy, supervision, machine/workflow pacing and sanction rules produce actor-relative command, discretion, pace, sanction and physical exposure ecologies only for actors actually occupying the governed role.

### `T3-N04 ACTIVITY_ORGANIZATION_AND_KNOWLEDGE_CONTROL`

Division of labour and conception/execution separation can alter task breadth, information access, skill exercise and dependence on planning/technical counterparts; universal deskilling is rejected.

### `T3-N05 INFORMAL_WORK_PRACTICE_AND_PEER_RULES`

Formal production rules plus peer-created recurrent practices can alter effort choices, local status/rewards and situated meanings. Participation does not prove subjective or durable ideological consent.

### `T3-N06 INSTITUTIONAL_ALLOCATION_AND_MOBILITY`

Seniority, vacancy, credential and skill-scarcity rules change actor eligibility, internal mobility, switching cost, bargaining power and promotion routes.

### `T3-N07 INSTITUTIONAL_RIGHTS_REPRESENTATION_AND_GRIEVANCE`

Collective agreements, representation and grievance machinery create actor-relative rights, obligations and action routes and can constrain managerial discretion when actually accessible/enforced.

### `T3-N08 PEER_INSTITUTIONAL_ROUTE_MEDIATION`

Peer culture plus school authority/credential rules can alter recurrent practice and the school-to-work route. Working-class origin alone cannot supply the peer culture or outcome.

### `T3-N09 HOUSEHOLD_CARE_DEPENDENCY_AND_OBLIGATION`

Maintenance/replacement of labour-power requires actual care/material arrangements, but no invariant family/gender allocation follows. Concrete household/kin/state/market relations distribute care obligations, support, time and mobility constraints.

### `T3-N10 CARE_TIME_POLICY_MARKET_MIGRATION_ALLOCATION`

Wage, welfare, public-care, labour-market, household and migration arrangements jointly allocate paid/unpaid work time, care counterpart chains, service access and time poverty.

### `T3-N11 COLLECTIVE_ORGANIZATION_STRUCTURAL_FEEDBACK`

Actual collective organization/action can change working-time law, contracts, grievance rights, managerial discretion, public provision and hence later structural/ecological conditions. This is a required reciprocal path, not automatic efficacy.

### `T3-N12 RECURRENT_RELATION_REPRODUCTION`

Class/social reproduction is normalized as repeated renewal of actual resource, dependency, authority, care and allocation relations across time—not reproduction of a fixed personality.
