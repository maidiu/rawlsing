## 3. Source-backed convergence checks

### Sève

Direct project text supports: acts as the primary biographical unit; capacities as both enabling and produced by acts; personality as activity across social relations rather than a trait stencil; `use-time` as actual temporal relations among objective activity categories; and a strict warning against jumping directly from concrete biography to psychological explanation without analysis of socially structured activity. Retain as `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS`, not empirical effect law.

### Holzkamp / Dreier

Holzkamp directly centers `Handlungsfähigkeit` on practical relation to life conditions and distinguishes restrictive/generalized action possibilities. Dreier's full book strengthens the T2 cross-context result: lives are conducted across structurally arranged places, rhythms, tasks, obligations and partially shared family trajectories; learning/change may be intentional or arise through participation in arrangements. Retain as `PRIMARY_THEORETICAL / PRACTICE_ANALYTIC`.

### Kohn / Schooler / Słomczyński

The full books strengthen T4 substantially. `Work and Personality` explicitly contains longitudinal reciprocal models in which substantive complexity, closeness of supervision and routinization compose occupational self-direction, while psychological functioning also predicts later work structure. It separately models housework by actual task complexity and time rather than gender label. `Social Structure and Self-Direction` extends cross-national and family analysis. Retain as `LONGITUDINAL_OBSERVATIONAL / COMPARATIVE`, never randomized causation.

### Elder / Mortimer

The full books reinforce timing, sequence, linked family relations, career histories, work intensity/quality and selection. `Children of the Great Depression` is especially important for the route `historical shock -> household/resource reorganization -> altered roles/activity -> later life course`, with heterogeneity by timing and prior position. Mortimer directly separates work quality, hours, learning opportunities and selection. Retain as `LONGITUDINAL_OBSERVATIONAL`, with cohort/design ceilings.

### Wright / Bhattacharya / Luria

Wright reinforces relational rather than gradational class analysis and prevents `class label -> actor consequence` compression. Bhattacharya's volume decomposes social reproduction into concrete labor, care, wage, migration, institutional and household arrangements rather than demographic essence. Luria remains useful corroboration for organized practice/education/communication and cognition but has bundled treatments and weak causal identification.
