# M2 PALESTINE — NEGATIVE CEILINGS AND EVIDENCE LIMITS R1

STATUS: `SOURCE_DISCIPLINE_COURT_COMPLETE`

PURPOSE: prevent the 2025 volume's political/theoretical vocabulary, documentary material, qualitative accounts, statistics, and secondary syntheses from being converted into stronger Rawlsing causal authority than the evidence supports.

M2 does not adjudicate the truth of every political or legal characterization used by contributors. It records what the named chapter asserts, what evidence it actually presents, and which narrower causal mechanisms can survive independently of broader contested claims.

---

# Volume-wide evidence ceilings

## EC-01 — Edited-volume attribution ceiling

Each contributed chapter is an independent source for M2. A claim by Ruth Wilson Gilmore, Asmaa AbuMezied, Mai Abu Moghli and Rachel Rosen, Tithi Bhattacharya, Tal-Hi Bitton, Susan Ferguson, Jemima Repo, Mai Taha, Sigrid Vertommen/Weeam Hammoudeh/Michal Nahman, or Fady Joudah is not attributed to another contributor merely because Bhattacharya and Ferguson edited the volume.

`EDITORSHIP != AUTHORSHIP OF CONTRIBUTED CLAIM`.

## EC-02 — Political/theoretical vocabulary ceiling

Terms including `settler colonialism`, `genocide`, `scholasticide`, `social reproducticide`, `decolonialism`, `sumud`, `flourishing`, `protagonism`, `unchilding`, `abolition geography`, and related political/theoretical terms remain the vocabulary/claims of the relevant contributor unless a narrower empirical proposition is separately evidenced.

Rawlsing may retain a concrete mechanism such as:

```text
CHECKPOINT RULE -> DELAY/DENIAL -> HOSPITAL ACCESS HISTORY
```

without adopting the contributor's full explanation of purpose, regime character, or political meaning.

## EC-03 — Intent ceiling

Observed rule/infrastructure effects do not prove intent.

```text
REALIZED CONSEQUENCE != PROOF THAT CONSEQUENCE WAS THE POLICYMAKER'S PURPOSE
```

Where chapters state deliberate strategies of destruction, elimination, disability creation, “hope” manipulation, or demographic engineering, M2 distinguishes documentary/policy evidence from authorial interpretation. The actor-relative mechanism can often be retained without resolving intent.

## EC-04 — Aggregate-to-individual ceiling

Population counts, unemployment/GDP statistics, permit totals, casualty statistics, school closures, health-system counts, and demographic comparisons establish aggregate conditions. They do not identify an individual actor's realized history absent exact position/gate/contact evidence.

`GROUP STATISTIC != ACTOR FACT`.

## EC-05 — Category-to-relation ceiling

Nationality, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender, age, class, marital status, location, disability, or political status does not by itself establish work, care, dependency, motive, treatment, or psychology.

A category may enter a D1 derivation only through an explicit institutional/organizational/household rule that uses it.

The ch.9 same-religion egg-donation rule is a good lawful example: religion matters because the legal gate explicitly makes it an eligibility input. It does not become a general actor prior.

## EC-06 — Rule/applicability/encounter/outcome ceiling

For every gate:

```text
FORMAL RULE
!= ACTOR APPLICABILITY
!= APPLICATION / ATTEMPT
!= GATEKEEPER TREATMENT
!= REALIZED ACCESS/DENIAL/DELAY
!= DOWNSTREAM EFFECT
```

This applies to work permits, medical permits, water-installation permits, checkpoints, detention, educational access, egg donation, posthumous retrieval, and paternity recognition.

## EC-07 — Infrastructure ceiling

```text
INFRASTRUCTURE DAMAGE/CONTROL
!= ACTOR EFFECT
```

Required intermediate route:

```text
INFRASTRUCTURE CONDITION
-> SERVICE / RESOURCE / ROUTE CAPACITY
-> ACTOR-SPECIFIC ACCESS / TIME / TASK / DEPENDENCY CHANGE
-> REALIZED HISTORY
-> DOWNSTREAM EFFECT IF EARNED
```

No infrastructure->psychology shortcut is licensed.

## EC-08 — Household/kinship ceiling

`KINSHIP LABEL != SUPPORT/CARE/OBLIGATION`.

Repo's strongest linked-life evidence is lawful because it identifies an actual work/commute constraint, household relation, necessary tasks, the husband's reduced presence/contribution, and wives' reported assumption of specific activities. The mechanism cannot be generalized to all spouses or women without those relational facts.

## EC-09 — Developmental ceiling

The volume is not a longitudinal developmental study. It contains important evidence about schooling, healthcare, displacement, family contact, detention, child activity, and material conditions, but usually not evidence sufficient for a general developmental causal law.

```text
SCHOOL INTERRUPTION != DEVELOPMENTAL DEFICIT
FATHER ABSENCE/LOW CONTACT != CHILD OUTCOME
DISPLACEMENT != TRAIT
VIOLENCE EXPOSURE != UNIFORM PSYCHOLOGY
CURRICULUM != BELIEF
```

The correct Rawlsing output is often realized activity/exposure/history to be consumed by V3 only where competent evidence/authority earns change.

## EC-10 — Subject-mediation ceiling

Fear, humiliation, commitment, hope, refusal, solidarity, practical reasoning, adaptation, strategy, and motive can be retained where a participant directly reports them or the source provides adequate qualitative evidence. They cannot be inferred for an entire nationality, sex, class, or status group.

Objective gate treatment can occur without correct perception; actor belief/meaning remains separate.

## EC-11 — Collective-action ceiling

```text
ACTION != SUCCESS
ALTERNATIVE PRACTICE != RULE CHANGE
RESISTANCE != EMANCIPATION
```

D3 requires exact coordination/resources, external counterpart/transaction, and realized later-world update. The volume supplies success, temporary success, suppression, sanction, circumvention, nonrecognition, and cases with no demonstrated structural effect.

## EC-12 — Cumulation ceiling

“Chronic,” “cumulative,” “ongoing,” and “intergenerational” are not self-executing causal labels.

A cumulative claim must identify iterative typed updates:

```text
STATE_t + REALIZED EVENT/PRACTICE/RULE_t
-> STATE_t+1
-> altered later opportunity/gate/resource relation
```

AbuMezied's rebuilding-resource depletion and Repo's route restriction->noncultivation->later property/use vulnerability are usable only at this typed grain.

## EC-13 — Temporal ceiling

Persistent blockade, imprisonment, service deprivation, repeated checkpoint delay, care burden, long-term displacement, and recurrent repair must not be represented merely as repeated independent event tokens. The causal state often lies in the active interval, recurrence identity, deadline, wait, or schedule coupling.

## EC-14 — Counterfactual ceiling

Most chapters do not provide experimental or quasi-experimental identification. Historical sequence, interview testimony, reports, and statistics can support a plausible or documented mechanism without establishing necessity, sufficiency, or an exact counterfactual effect size.

Use `MECHANISM_EVIDENCE` rather than `UNIVERSAL_CAUSAL_LAW` where appropriate.

# Source-form specific limits

## Primary documentary / archival evidence

Taha's archival photographs and historical documents can establish material scenes, official practices, or the existence of a temporary market. A photograph cannot independently establish participant motive, prevalence, causal direction, or the full process that produced the scene.

## Historical evidence

Historical laws, policy changes, institutional transfers, and documented sequences are strong for rule/position/rebinding mechanisms. Secondary histories remain dependent on their cited archive and should not be treated as direct primary observation.

## Ethnographic / qualitative evidence

Repo's interviews and the ch.9 ART studies can establish participant-reported routines, meanings, and pathways. They are not population prevalence estimates unless sampling supports that use.

Repo's chapter does not provide a full sample size/sampling-method statement for the Al-Walajah interviews in the chapter text. Its strongest use is mechanism discovery/participant report, not population frequency.

## Retrospective oral history

Taha explicitly relies heavily on oral histories collected decades after the 1936–39 revolt. They are valuable for otherwise poorly archived household practices but carry recall, retrospective interpretation, survival, and selection limits. Reported practices can be retained with this provenance; precise prevalence and counterfactual claims cannot.

## Statistical / demographic evidence

Counts and rates can establish distributions and disparities but normally do not disclose the actor-specific transduction route. M2 pairs them with a separate rule/gate/activity mechanism when possible.

## Legal / policy evidence

Law can establish formal eligibility, authority, prohibition, or transition. It cannot establish whether a particular actor applied, was treated in a certain way, gained access, complied, resisted, or experienced downstream effects.

## Journalistic / report evidence

UN, OCHA, WHO, rights-organization, press, NGO, and institutional reports are used as report evidence at the grain they support. M2 does not silently promote them to experimental causal proof.

## Social-media / family/friend narratives

AbuMezied explicitly draws in part on social media and stories from family/friends. These are useful for concrete lived mechanisms and first-person examples but do not establish representativeness or prevalence.

## Literary/reflexive evidence

Joudah's “Onwards” is a literary/medical reflection. It is not used as evidence for a new Rawlsing mechanism. Evocative description is not causal warrant.

# Positive evidence discipline

M2's retained architectural material survives because it usually does **not** require agreement with the strongest political/theoretical claim. For example:

```text
WORK-PERMIT CRITERIA -> ACTOR ELIGIBILITY -> EMPLOYMENT ACCESS
CHECKPOINT WAIT -> COMMUTER PRESENCE LOSS -> LINKED HOUSEHOLD TASK TRANSFER
ELECTRICITY WINDOW -> APPLIANCE AVAILABILITY -> CARE TASK TIMING
SCHOOL CLOSURE -> COMMUNITY COORDINATION -> MAKESHIFT CLASS -> SUPPRESSION
PASS REGIME + BOYCOTT -> ALTERNATIVE PRODUCTION/TRANSPORT -> TEMPORARY MARKET
EGG-DONATION LAW -> RELIGION-MATCH GATE -> DONOR SET -> TREATMENT OPPORTUNITY
PRISON CONJUGAL RESTRICTION -> SPERM ROUTE + IVF -> BIRTH IF SUCCESSFUL -> SEPARATE PATERNITY GATE
```

Each can be encoded with its evidence ceiling, provenance, boundary conditions, and negative ceiling.
