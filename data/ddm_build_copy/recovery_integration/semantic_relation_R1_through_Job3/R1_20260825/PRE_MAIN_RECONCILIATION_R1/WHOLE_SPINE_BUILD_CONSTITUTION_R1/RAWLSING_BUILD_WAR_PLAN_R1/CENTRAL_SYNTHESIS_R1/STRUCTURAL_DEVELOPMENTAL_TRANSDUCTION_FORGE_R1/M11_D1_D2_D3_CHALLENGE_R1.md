## 1. D1 — TYPED_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_DERIVATION

### Attack
Can all SRT material be represented without D1 by PRE-V3 structural facts plus generic actor/context binding and OEE? **No.** The volume repeatedly requires derivations that are actor- and counterpart-specific but not yet discrete events: who has income, who owes care, who is eligible for pension/relief, who is tied to an employer by migration status, whose school attendance is constrained, who can draw on a church referral, whose time is subject to overtime.

### Source result
The strongest D1 evidence is not the rhetoric of “reproduction” but concrete cases in Chapters 3, 7 and 8. Teeple Hopkins especially shows how one legal/work arrangement yields distinct dependency, time and support facts for actual workers (`SRC:L1578-L1660`).

### Verdict
`D1 CONFIRMED`. The existing candidate family registry is broad enough. SRT does **not** require a new `SOCIAL_REPRODUCTION` family. It does require that `HOUSEHOLD_CARE_DEPENDENCY` and linked-life/resource paths be directionally actor-specific and that institutional-policy facts can produce access/obligation/time outputs without pretending those outputs are events.

## 2. D2 — INTERVAL_RECURRENCE_AND_TRANSITION_REBINDING

### Attack
Could SRT be represented as event histories only? **No.** Care, housework, paid shifts, school attendance, pension income, migration-status dependency and support ties are recurrent or persistent. Event-spam would erase duration, recurrence identity, schedule conflicts and stable counterpart relations.

### Strongest new sharpening
The volume makes **finite-time allocation/crowd-out** central. A D2 implementation that merely records `ACTIVE_INTERVAL` and `RECURRENCE` but cannot compare actual schedule occupancy is semantically incomplete for M11. Time poverty requires:

```text
RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_SET(actor, interval)
+ duration/frequency/schedule windows
+ obligation/fixity
-> feasible residual/overlap
-> typed CROWD_OUT only when demonstrated
```

This is not a new D4; it is a required D2 rule over recurrent activities.

### Verdict
`D2 CONFIRMED_AND_SHARPENED__FINITE_TIME_BUDGET_COMPOSITION_REQUIRED`.

## 3. D3 — PROVENANCE-PRESERVING RECIPROCAL SOCIAL WORLD UPDATE

### Attack
Does the volume show that actor/collective action needs a route back to later structural facts? **Yes**, especially historical welfare, labor and feminist struggles. Does it show direct `ACTION -> STRUCTURE`? **No.** The useful cases all contain employers, police, relief offices, legislatures, parties, or state agencies as response nodes.

### Verdict
`D3 CONFIRMED`. Require explicit external response, outcome type including `none`, effective time, and later ecology rebinding. No collective psychological subject.
