# M7_CATEGORY_RESIDUALS_AND_NEGATIVE_CEILINGS_R1

STATUS: `M7_CATEGORY_RESIDUAL_COURT_COMPLETE_R1`

SOURCE BASIS: full Kohn/Słomczyński book.

## 0. Court law

The M7 commission requires:

```text
ANY REMAINING COUNTRY / CLASS ASSOCIATION
= UNEXPLAINED_RESIDUAL
UNLESS AN ACTUAL MECHANISM IS SUPPLIED.
```

This file applies that rule aggressively.

A residual is not a failure of the book. It is a limit on what Rawlsing is authorized to turn into actor-level causal fact.

## 1. Broad country association is never a mechanism

The source repeatedly finds US/Polish differences. The book itself does not license:

```text
UNITED_STATES -> PSYCHOLOGY
POLAND -> PSYCHOLOGY
CAPITALISM -> TRAIT
SOCIALISM -> TRAIT
```

Where the authors can identify an institutional difference, M7 retains it as an upstream mechanism candidate. Where they cannot, the country difference remains `UNEXPLAINED_RESIDUAL`.

Examples of potentially mechanized differences include:

- labor-law job protections;
- central job allocation and credential rules;
- workplace authority organization;
- family-role allocation;
- party/managerial relation;
- sector location in the Polish nationalized economy.

But each remains causal only to the extent its actor-level route is actually evidenced.

## 3. Class residual court

### 3.1 Class is not permitted to survive as an unexplained direct psychological cause

The source's own central thesis is that class matters through differentiated conditions of life. Therefore Rawlsing should treat a remaining class coefficient after measured mediators as one of:

```text
KNOWN MEDIATOR NOT YET REPRESENTED
UNKNOWN MEDIATOR
MEASUREMENT / MODEL RESIDUAL
```

not as:

```text
CLASS ESSENCE -> PSYCHOLOGY
```

### 3.2 Manager residuals

`MANAGER` is especially dangerous as a cross-system shortcut.

US and Polish managers occupy different property, political, organizational and security configurations. Polish non-party managers' distress is suggestive precisely because manager status alone is not enough: party relation and centralized-plan responsibility alter the ecology.

M7 rule:

```text
MANAGER_CATEGORY
must decompose where causally used into such dimensions as:
  ownership relation
  decision authority
  supervisory span / layers
  organizational location
  political/institutional dependence
  job security / protection
  resource/control capacity
  task complexity
  supervision received
  income/status/education
```

Any leftover manager association remains residual.

### 3.3 Manual-worker residuals

`MANUAL WORKER` likewise cannot fire directly. Polish production/nonproduction workers and US manual workers do not occupy identical labor-market, legal-protection or enterprise-sector relations.

M7 requires decomposition into:

- production-sector location;
- actual task complexity/routinization;
- supervision;
- job protection/security;
- labor-demand position;
- union/contract relation where applicable;
- housing/health/education entitlements where evidenced.

## 4. Distress negative court

Distress is the source's clearest warning against overgeneralization.

### What is earned

- US occupational self-direction ameliorates distress.
- US job protections independently ameliorate distress.
- US occupational position has countervailing effects through self-direction and protection.
- Polish occupational self-direction does **not** reliably ameliorate distress.
- Polish labor-law and benefit structures plausibly alter class-specific well-being.

### What is not earned

- no source-supported universal `HIGHER CLASS -> WELLBEING`;
- no source-supported universal `SELF-DIRECTION -> WELLBEING`;
- no causal `SOCIALISM -> MANUAL-WORKER WELLBEING`;
- no causal `CAPITALISM -> MANAGER WELLBEING`;
- no complete explanation of the Polish reversal;
- no measured Polish actor-level job-protection mediation comparable to the US model.

Therefore the unexplained Polish class/distress pattern remains explicitly banked as `UNEXPLAINED_RESIDUAL`.

## 5. Measurement-failure ceiling

When a measure does not translate, Rawlsing may not infer the missing construct from correlated indicators.

Concrete case:

```text
FATALISM_US_AVAILABLE
FATALISM_POLAND_NOT_ADEQUATELY_MEASURED
```

Required result:

`MEASUREMENT_DOES_NOT_TRANSLATE`, not `POLISH_FATALISM_ESTIMATED_FROM_COUNTRY/CLASS`.

Similarly, Polish individual job protections cannot be reconstructed from the existence of national labor law alone if the needed grain is whether a particular actor had, knew, could invoke, or benefited from a particular protection.

## 6. Temporality negative ceilings

The source does not authorize Rawlsing to infer within-person development from cross-sectional group difference.

Forbidden transformations:

```text
OLDER_COHORT_DIFFERS_FROM_YOUNGER
-> INDIVIDUAL DEVELOPED THAT WAY              [NOT EARNED]

POLISH CROSS_SECTIONAL ASSOCIATION
-> WITHIN_PERSON CAUSAL TRAJECTORY             [NOT EARNED]

TWO_WAVE_10_YEAR NET EFFECT
-> CONTINUOUS LINEAR CHANGE                    [NOT EARNED]

ASSOCIATION WITH RECURRENT WORK CONDITION
-> EXACT ONSET / THRESHOLD / ACCUMULATION LAW [NOT EARNED]
```

The source explicitly says the two-wave design cannot resolve onset, continuity, tapering, accumulation, thresholds or detailed career sequences.

## 7. Subject-mediation negative ceilings

The book measures important psychological outcomes but does not supply a microtheory or direct observational measure of uptake.

Rawlsing must therefore refuse these shortcuts:

```text
OBJECTIVE WORK CONDITION -> ACTOR MEANING       [NOT DIRECTLY OBSERVED]
OBJECTIVE WORK CONDITION -> MOTIVE              [NOT DIRECTLY OBSERVED]
OBJECTIVE WORK CONDITION -> CONSCIOUS APPRAISAL [NOT REQUIRED/NOT OBSERVED]
PARENT VALUE -> CHILD INTERNALIZATION           [MECHANISM NOT OBSERVED]
COMPLEX WORK -> DURABLE CAPACITY                [ONLY SPECIFIC OUTCOMES EARNED]
```

The source supports net learning/generalization effects, not a generic `INTERNALIZATION` operator.

This is consistent with the frozen synthesis firewall separating actor-relative social fact from V3 uptake/development.

## 8. Behavior/action negative ceiling

The authors explicitly note the lack of behavioral measures other than interview behavior. Consequently M7 cannot promote this source into general action-generation law.

The book can support:

- objective job condition;
- measured orientation/value/cognitive outcome;
- reciprocal selection into/molding of work.

It cannot, by itself, specify:

- action choice rules;
- collective mobilization;
- institutional contestation sequence;
- rule change caused by action;
- political behavior generated by self-direction.

This sharply limits what M7 can say about D3.

## 9. Reciprocal causality negative ceiling

The strongest reciprocal relation is:

```text
OCCUPATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION
<-> PSYCHOLOGICAL_FUNCTIONING
```

and, for US men:

```text
OCCUPATIONAL_POSITION / OWNERSHIP / HIERARCHY
<-> OCCUPATIONAL_SELF_DIRECTION
```

But this does **not** establish:

```text
ACTOR ACTION
-> EXTERNAL INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSE
-> RULE / NETWORK / PROPERTY / ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE
-> NEW STRUCTURAL WORLD FACT
```

Thus D3 cannot cite this source as direct proof of generalized reciprocal social-world update.

## 10. Family/generalization ceilings

The family evidence is restricted by sample and design.

No automatic generalization to:

- single-parent families;
- children outside the sampled age range;
- all household forms;
- all mother/father role configurations;
- same-sex/opposite-sex transmission differences beyond observed design;
- all women or all men;
- societies outside the industrialized comparative scope.

A `FAMILY` token is not a mechanism. Exact linked actors, roles, activity/interaction channels, and structural conditions must be represented.

## 11. Historical-scope ceiling

The Polish evidence describes 1978 state-socialist Poland, not a timeless `Poland` and not every socialist institutional form. The US evidence centers on 1964/1974, not a timeless `United States` or every capitalist labor regime.

Required Rawlsing representation:

```text
INSTITUTIONAL CONFIGURATION
+ VALID HISTORICAL INTERVAL
+ SOURCE PROVENANCE
```

not a permanent nation modifier.

## 12. Industrial-society ceiling

The authors mean their thesis as a general interpretation for industrialized societies and review third-country evidence accordingly. The book does not prove the same mechanism across nonindustrialized or partially industrialized social formations.

Therefore M7 assigns:

`PORTABILITY_OUTSIDE_OBSERVED_INDUSTRIALIZED_SCOPE = EVIDENCE_INCONCLUSIVE`.

## 13. Category-shortcut firewall proposed to Vozhd

```text
NO COUNTRY -> PSYCHOLOGY
NO CLASS -> PSYCHOLOGY
NO OCCUPATIONAL TITLE -> PSYCHOLOGY
NO EDUCATION LEVEL -> PSYCHOLOGY WITHOUT ROUTE WHEN ROUTE MATTERS
NO FAMILY LABEL -> CHILD PSYCHOLOGY
NO PARTY LABEL -> PSYCHOLOGY
NO DEMOGRAPHIC CATEGORY -> ACTOR SOCIAL FACT
```

Broad categories may index where to search for differentiated conditions. They do not themselves satisfy actor-relative derivation.

## 14. Residual handling rule

Every comparative model should permit an explicit state:

```text
UNEXPLAINED_RESIDUAL {
  observed_association_ref,
  categories/settings,
  mediators_already_tested,
  candidate_but_unmeasured_mechanisms,
  measurement_ceiling,
  temporal_ceiling,
  no_actor_level_inference=true
}
```

This need not be a new implementation object. It is an evidentiary discipline: unexplained association must remain visibly unexplained.
