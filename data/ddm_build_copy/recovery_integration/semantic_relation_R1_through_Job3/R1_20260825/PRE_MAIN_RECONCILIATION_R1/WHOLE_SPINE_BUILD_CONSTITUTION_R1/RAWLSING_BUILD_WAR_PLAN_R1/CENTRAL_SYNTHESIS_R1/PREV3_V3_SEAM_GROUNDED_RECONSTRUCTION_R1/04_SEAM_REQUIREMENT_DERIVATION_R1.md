`PROVED_CURRENT`: current PRE-V3, Field Forge, V3, CAN-008 R2 and SDTF law jointly require these noncollapses at their respective grains.

## A2. What the seam may assert

`PROVED_CURRENT`: it may expose/assert an objective world, relation, position, consequence, opportunity/applicability basis, event or other fact **only** when a competent semantic owner and authority instance support that exact proposition and currentness/valid-time grain.

`PROVED_CURRENT`: SDTF D1' permits objective actor-positioned social consequences without promoting them to subjective/developmental state.

## A3. What the seam may only make available

`PROVED_CURRENT`: population evidence, route possibility, structural affordance, unresolved position basis, applicability/opportunity basis and other possibilities remain possibilities until the downstream realization owner acts.

`REASONED_INFERENCE`: a resolver may make such information queryable without eagerly materializing it, provided exact producer authority, release identity, replay and negative semantics survive.

## A4. Actor-side ownership

`PROVED_CURRENT`: the seam may not precompute actor belief, motive, appraisal, response, memory, learning, durable development, relationship-as-perceived, biography or realized attractor from objective structural evidence.

`PROVED_CURRENT`: CAN-008 R2 owns immediate actor receivability, uptake/appraisal, situated recruitment and response/metabolism for an actual generated/admitted event.

`REASONED_INFERENCE`: an acceptable seam should prefer exact producer-owned derivation/query over duplication where replay/currentness can remain exact, while preserving a stable release/root identity for reproducible actor/world/time binding.

# G. V3 INGRESS REQUIREMENTS

The seam must distinguish the following ingress classes:

## G1. OBJECTIVE WORLD FACT

Exists independently of actor-private state; may be resolved/read by V3 under exact authority/currentness.

## G2. OBJECTIVE ACTOR POSITION / POSITIONED CONSEQUENCE

May be actor-indexed while remaining objective social/world fact. SDTF D1' is the clearest current lawful owner family.

## G3. OPPORTUNITY / APPLICABILITY

Actor-relative possibility/basis; does not equal occurrence, encounter or exposure.

## G4. EVENT / OCCURRENCE

Requires actual generated/admitted occurrence authority.

## G5. ENCOUNTER / EXPOSURE

V3-owned realized developmental contact states; not implied by event existence alone.

## G6. NON_EVENT CONTEXT

`PROVED_CURRENT`: generic ingress remains missing/open as current OBL062 for conditions, directed relations and recurrent activities. This requires an explicit route that does not event-launder.

## G7. PERSON-STATE

V3-owned durable/current person state; upstream objective structure cannot directly author it.

`PROVED_CURRENT`: private V3 state likewise cannot directly author objective world history. Actor-to-world effects require externalized occurrence plus competent objective world-response/source admission.

# REQUIREMENT FREEZE HOLDING

The architecture comparison may therefore optimize implementation only **after** preserving these requirements.

The requirements imply that a single undifferentiated “context payload” is insufficient unless its internal contract still preserves all relevant semantic/authority/identity/temporal jurisdictions.

They also imply that a pure query service is insufficient unless it can bind reproducible world/release identity and currentness without hidden-latest semantics.

They do **not** imply that every lawful fact must be eagerly materialized.
