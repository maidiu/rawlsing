## 1. Source-use discipline

This is a source-excavation ledger, not a theory summary. Every retained item below is classified at the strongest evidence level the book licenses. Mortimer's causal language is not automatically promoted to experimental causation. YDS evidence is observational and longitudinal; where prior state is controlled, the result is described as prospective/selection-sensitive rather than randomized causal proof. Findings Mortimer reports from other studies are identified as such rather than treated as YDS estimates.

Key source distinctions preserved throughout this lane:

```text
EMPLOYMENT != HOURS != DURATION != WORK-INVESTMENT PATTERN != WORK QUALITY
OBJECTIVE JOB FEATURE != SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE
POSITION/OPPORTUNITY != ACTUAL RECURRENT ACTIVITY
ACTUAL ACTIVITY != UPTAKE
UPTAKE != DURABLE CHANGE
CONTEMPORANEOUS DIFFERENCE != PERSISTENT EFFECT
SELECTION INTO WORK != SOCIALIZATION BY WORK
ADOLESCENT WORK CONDITION != ADULT EFFECT OF NOMINALLY SAME CONDITION
```

## 3. Full-book mechanism families retained

The full reading yields ten mechanism families sufficiently distinct to retain for SDTF comparison:

1. `INSTITUTIONALLY_GATED_WORK_ACCESS` — labor rules, school calendar, employer opportunity and family context alter possible work.
2. `TYPED_WORK_CONDITION_VECTOR` — hours, duration, pay, learning, autonomy, stress, supervision, advancement, status, compatibility, etc. coexist without one scalar valence.
3. `REALIZED_RECURRENT_WORK_ACTIVITY` — job opportunity/position matters developmentally only through actual tasks, relations, schedules and repeated experience.
4. `CROSS_DOMAIN_TIME_REBINDING` — paid work changes available time, but substitutions differ by actor and existing activity configuration.
5. `SELECTION_INTO_WORK_PATTERN_AND_QUALITY` — prior educational, behavioral, motivational and social states predict later employment pattern/quality.
6. `PHASE_HISTORY_QUALIFIED_TRANSDUCTION` — autonomy/responsibility can have different effects for novice adolescents than for adults.
7. `QUALITY_SPECIFIC_UPTAKE` — stress, learning, reward/status, advancement and school compatibility connect to different psychological/behavioral outcomes.
8. `TRANSIENT_VERSUS_PERSISTENT_EFFECT_SEPARATION` — alcohol/problem-behavior and earnings differences can dissipate while educational/family-transition associations persist differently.
9. `WORK_HISTORY_PATTERN_EFFECT` — duration × intensity configurations predict later outcomes more usefully than current employment or cumulative hours alone.
10. `RECIPROCAL_SELECTION_DEVELOPMENT_SEQUENCE` — prior person state affects gated work selection; realized work can alter efficacy/values; those person states can alter later plans/actions; institutional gates then govern later schooling/work positions.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy and strongest source limits

The strongest YDS evidence is prospective and selection-sensitive, not experimental. Mortimer repeatedly controls prior levels/backgrounds where possible, but unobserved heterogeneity is not eliminated by ordinary regression. The book itself notes external studies in which apparent wage returns to adolescent work are attenuated or eliminated under stronger controls for unobserved heterogeneity. Accordingly, this lane does not convert association into mechanism merely because temporal order is available.

The book also cannot establish a single end-to-end reciprocal loop from person change through a specifically observed employer/institutional gate into a newly realized work ecology and then back into further person change. It supplies components and prospective links strongly consistent with such a loop. D3 is therefore strengthened as a required architecture, not empirically closed by Mortimer alone.
