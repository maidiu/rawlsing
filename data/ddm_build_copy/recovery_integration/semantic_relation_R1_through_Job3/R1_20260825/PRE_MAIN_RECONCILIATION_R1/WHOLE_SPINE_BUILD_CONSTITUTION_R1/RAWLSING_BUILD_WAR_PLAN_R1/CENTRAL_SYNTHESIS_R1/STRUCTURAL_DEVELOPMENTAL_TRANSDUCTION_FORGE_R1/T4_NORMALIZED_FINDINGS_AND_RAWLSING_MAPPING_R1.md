# T4 NORMALIZED FINDINGS AND RAWLSING MAPPING R1

## 0. Central normalized finding

The T4 literature does not require a direct bridge of the form:

```text
SOCIAL CATEGORY → PERSONALITY
```

It repeatedly requires a longer and more concrete form:

```text
ACTOR-INDEPENDENT STRUCTURE
→ POSITION / ROLE / TASK / RESOURCE / RELATIONAL POSSIBILITY
→ ACTOR-RELATIVE REALIZED CONDITION OR RECURRENT ACTIVITY
→ SUBJECT/COUNTERPART MEDIATION
→ SPECIFIC PERSON CONSEQUENCE
→ LATER ACTION / SELECTION
→ GATED POSITION OR EXPOSURE CHANGE
```

This is independently convergent with the Phase-0 diagnosis that Rawlsing’s underproved region is the positive derivation of an actor-relative social ecology from structured world conditions.

## 1. Candidate mechanism families for Vozhd normalization

### NF-01 — `STRUCTURE_TO_RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_ECOLOGY`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
ORGANIZED TASK + AUTHORITY CONDITIONS
→ actor receives concrete latitude / supervision / routinization / complexity
→ recurrent enacted work practice
→ learning/generalization or distress pathway
→ specific developmental outcome
```

**Evidence strength:** `A-/B+` longitudinal observational, replicated across classic and modern cohorts.

**Primary anchors:** Kohn & Schooler 1978/1982; Miller et al. 1979; Staff & Mortimer 2024.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** structural pressure, affordance/applicability, mediation-route possibility.

**V3 reuse:** actor-specific realization, response, memory/learning, biography/path dependence and existing developmental owners.

**Missing connective function:** positive derivation from work organization into actor-specific **recurrent activity ecology**. A task is not developmentally operative merely because it exists in the job definition.

**Disposition:** `MISSING + REUSE`.

**Do not normalize as:** “occupation causes personality.”

---

### NF-02 — `STRUCTURAL_CONTROL_TO_EXERCISED_CONTROL_HISTORY`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
ALLOCATED DECISION LATITUDE / AUTONOMY
→ actual opportunities to make consequential work choices
→ exercised control / efficacy experience
→ mastery / competence / internal-control change if realized
```

**Evidence strength:** `B+/B` longitudinal observational.

**Primary anchors:** Mortimer & Lorence 1979b; Wu, Griffin & Parker 2015; Staff & Mortimer 2024.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** work-control affordance/applicability.

**V3 reuse:** activity, response, learning, self-development.

**Missing connective function:** the distinction among `FORMAL_LATITUDE`, `ACTOR_AVAILABLE_LATITUDE`, and `EXERCISED_LATITUDE`.

**Disposition:** `GENERALIZE` the seam/activity derivation; `REUSE` developmental owners.

**Negative evidence:** skill utilization did not independently change locus of control in Wu et al.; do not collapse autonomy, complexity, skill use and good work into one scalar.

---

### NF-03 — `EVENT_TO_PERSISTENT_SOCIAL_CONDITION_PROPAGATION`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
DISCRETE STRUCTURAL EVENT
→ changed resource / role configuration
→ persistent actor-relative strain condition
→ later self-concept/mastery/distress consequences
```

**Evidence strength:** `A-/B+` longitudinal observational partial mediation.

**Primary anchor:** Pearlin et al. 1981.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** event possibility, resource dependency, structural pressure/route.

**Existing realization reuse:** OEE/event-history machinery can represent the discrete realized occurrence where lawful.

**V3 reuse:** response and developmental consequences.

**Missing connective function:** an event must be able to generate a new persistent material/role condition without pretending the event itself persists.

**Disposition:** `MISSING + REUSE`.

**Canonical firewall:** `JOB_LOSS_EVENT != ECONOMIC_STRAIN_CONDITION`.

---

### NF-04 — `PERSISTENT_STRAIN_CONDITION`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
ONGOING RESOURCE / ROLE / AUTHORITY / DEMAND MISMATCH
→ persistent actor-relative difficulty
→ repeated unresolved strain
→ cumulative or developmental consequence
```

**Evidence strength:** `A-/B+` within Pearlin stress-process lineage; supported by modern work-stressor within-person evidence.

**Primary anchors:** Pearlin et al. 1981; Pearlin 1989; Staff & Mortimer 2024.

**Phase-0 state:** CONDITION is explicitly undergeneralized/fragmentary.

**Architectural judgment:** T4 cannot tell whether existing context/state/history carriers are semantically sufficient. Lane R must test positive preservation of persistence/duration rather than infer sufficiency from names.

**Disposition:** `GENERALIZE_IF_PROVABLE_ELSE_MISSING`.

**Do not normalize as:** repeated copies of the same event.

---

### NF-05 — `NETWORK_TO_REALIZED_SUPPORT_PROCESS`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
POSSIBLE TIE / NETWORK TOPOLOGY
→ relationship quality + counterpart willingness + actor access
→ support mobilized and received
→ juncture-specific effect on strain/self-concept/outcome
```

**Evidence strength:** `B+` longitudinal observational for Pearlin’s job-loss process; `T` review reinforcement from House et al.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** interaction topology / possible counterpart relations.

**V3 reuse:** relationship/history and actor-specific uptake/action.

**Missing connective function:** positive realization of support from a possible tie while preserving relational quality, counterpart action and problem fit.

**Disposition:** `GENERALIZE + REUSE`.

**Canonical firewall:**

```text
NETWORK_EXISTS
!= SUPPORT_AVAILABLE
!= SUPPORT_MOBILIZED
!= SUPPORT_RECEIVED
!= SUPPORT_EFFECTIVE
```

---

### NF-06 — `PROBLEM_SPECIFIC_COPING_ACTION`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
REALIZED PROBLEM / STRAIN
→ actor selects and enacts problem-specific coping
→ situation / meaning / symptom path is modified at a specific juncture
```

**Evidence strength:** `B+` longitudinal observational interaction evidence.

**Primary anchor:** Pearlin et al. 1981; theoretical lineage Pearlin & Schooler 1978.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** only the external problem/constraint possibility; coping itself must not be projected backward into PRE-V3.

**V3 reuse:** actor action/response.

**Missing connective question:** can enacted coping lawfully modify later structural/social condition or only internal response? Lane R/Vozhd should distinguish these cases.

**Disposition:** `REUSE` V3 actor action; `GENERALIZE` only the feedback edge if absent.

**Structural ceiling:** individual coping was reported as relatively ineffective for problems rooted in formal organization, authority and reward systems. No voluntarist override of structure.

---

### NF-07 — `STRESSOR_TO_STRESSOR_CROSS_DOMAIN_PROLIFERATION`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
PRIMARY ROLE/CONDITION DEMAND
→ actor time/resource/obligation ecology is reconfigured
→ strain appears or expands in another role/activity domain
→ downstream stress process
```

**Evidence strength:** `B` empirical observational mechanism study plus stress-process synthesis.

**Primary anchor:** Pearlin, Aneshensel & LeBlanc 1997.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** role/interaction topology and route possibility.

**V3/realization reuse:** actor-specific condition/history.

**Missing connective function:** typed propagation from one realized social condition into another domain’s actor-relative opportunity/constraint configuration.

**Disposition:** `MISSING_OR_GENERALIZE`, architecture deferred.

**Canonical ceiling:** co-occurrence is not proliferation. A propagation route must be represented.

---

### NF-08 — `POSITION_TO_DIFFERENTIAL_EXPOSURE_WITHOUT_TRAIT_PRIOR`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
SOCIAL / INSTITUTIONAL POSITION
→ structured distribution of resources, obligations, authority, insecurity
→ differential probability/configuration of concrete exposure
→ actor-specific exposure only after realization
→ downstream stress/development if realized
```

**Evidence strength:** `C+` observational distribution evidence + `T` theoretical normalization.

**Primary anchors:** Turner, Wheaton & Lloyd 1995; Pearlin 1989.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** positioned-subject empirical possibility and structural pressure/affordance.

**Actor/V3 reuse:** actor binding and actual exposure/history.

**Missing connective function:** positive, non-deterministic resolution of structural position into actor-specific exposure ecology.

**Disposition:** `REUSE + MISSING`.

**Mandatory rejection:** `GROUP_STATUS → INDIVIDUAL_DISTRESS/TRAIT`.

---

### NF-09 — `PERSON_ACTION_TO_LATER_POSITION_EXPOSURE_FEEDBACK`

**Normalized empirical form**

```text
PRIOR PERSON / LEARNED ORIENTATION / COMPETENCE / VALUE
→ preference / performance / action
→ institutional or relational gate
→ actual transition / job molding / attainment
→ changed later structural conditions and exposure
```

**Evidence strength:** `A-/B` across Kohn/Schooler, Mortimer/Lorence, Andrisani/Nestel; modern Staff/Mortimer constrains simple lag assumptions.

**PRE-V3 reuse:** opportunity/gate structure remains actor-independent until resolved.

**V3 reuse:** prior person/history and action.

**Missing connective function:** lawful causal feedback from actor action through external gates into later position/exposure, without making structural facts a direct product of psychology.

**Disposition:** `MISSING_OR_GENERALIZE` feedback seam; exact owner deferred.

**Canonical firewall:** `PREFERENCE/CAPACITY != POSITION_CHANGE`.

## 2. Rawlsing seam summary

The commissioned Phase-0 map supplies lawful bookends:

```text
PRE-V3 STRUCTURAL / EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY WORLD
→ actor binding / activation
→ actor-specific opportunity/constraint/contact/activity history
→ V3 uptake / response / memory / learning / biography / path dependence
```

T4 independently confirms that the hand-wavy region is **not one edge**. It contains at least five typed derivations that should not be silently merged:

1. `STRUCTURE → RECURRENT_ACTIVITY`
2. `EVENT → PERSISTENT_CONDITION`
3. `NETWORK_TOPOLOGY → REALIZED_SUPPORT_RELATION`
4. `REALIZED_CONDITION → CROSS_DOMAIN_CONDITION`
5. `PERSON/ACTION → GATED_LATER_POSITION/EXPOSURE`

Vozhd may normalize these into fewer architectural primitives **only if** the primitive preserves their different realization laws and temporal forms.

## 3. Temporal semantics required by T4

### EVENT

Keep discrete. A job disruption can initiate a process but cannot stand in for the economic/role condition it creates.

### CONDITION

Needs interval persistence and actor-relative applicability. Central examples: economic strain, close supervision, routinization, sustained work stressors.

### RELATION

Needs counterpart identity/participation and quality. Central examples: supervisor relation, support relation, caregiving relation.

### RECURRENT ACTIVITY / PRACTICE

Needs repeated enacted behavior under structured conditions. Central examples: complex problem solving, autonomous judgment, repetitive work, coping.

### TRANSITION

Needs before/after position plus a realized gate/event. Central examples: job loss, promotion, mobility, change into more self-directed work.

### TRAJECTORY

Needs ordered history. Central examples: career movement and evolving stress exposure.

### TURNING POINT

T4 found no source that earns a distinct general turning-point operator independent of a trajectory-relative interpretation. `DEFER`.

### CUMULATIVE PROCESS

Needs typed propagation/accumulation. Chronic strain and stress proliferation cannot be represented as an untyped scalar sum of events.

## 4. Developmental targets: reuse, do not duplicate

T4 sources mention or measure:

- intellectual/ideational flexibility;
- self-directed orientation;
- competence/personal efficacy;
- locus/internal control;
- mastery;
- self-esteem;
- depressive affect/distress;
- occupational values.

These are **evidence targets**, not instructions to create parallel T4-owned psychology. Where Rawlsing already has V3 response, learning, memory, relationship, biography/path-dependence and developmental machinery, T4’s architectural demand is to deliver typed actor-relative experience/activity/condition histories into those owners.

**Disposition:** `REUSE_V3_DEVELOPMENTAL_OWNERS`; identifier-level sufficiency remains coordinator/Lane-R authority.

## 5. Negative ceilings for normalization

1. `SOCIAL_CLASS != PERSONALITY`.
2. `OCCUPATIONAL_TITLE != ACTUAL_WORK_ACTIVITY`.
3. `FORMAL_AUTONOMY != EXERCISED_AUTONOMY`.
4. `JOB_LOSS_EVENT != CHRONIC_ECONOMIC_STRAIN`.
5. `NETWORK_MEMBERSHIP != SUPPORT`.
6. `COPING_AVAILABLE != COPING_EFFECTIVE`.
7. `STRESSOR_COOCCURRENCE != STRESS_PROLIFERATION`.
8. `GROUP_EXPOSURE_DISTRIBUTION != ACTOR_EXPOSURE`.
9. `PRIOR_TRAIT/PREFERENCE != LATER_POSITION` without action and a gate.
10. `LONGITUDINAL_MODEL != RANDOMIZED_CAUSAL_IDENTIFICATION`.
11. `FIXED_EFFECTS != CONTROL_OF_TIME_VARYING_CONFOUNDING`.
12. `CONTEMPORANEOUS_WITHIN_PERSON_ASSOCIATION != LAGGED_DEVELOPMENTAL_EFFECT`.
13. `RECURRENT_CONDITION != EVENT_TOKEN_REPETITION`.
14. `PERSON_CHANGE != DURABLE_CHANGE` unless time/persistence evidence earns durability.

## 7. What T4 explicitly rejects

- a theory-name primitive such as `KOHN_SCHOOLER_SOCIALIZATION` or `PEARLIN_STRESS_PROCESS`;
- a direct class/race/gender/poverty/religion/occupation → trait or distress rule;
- an event-only ontology for chronic strain or recurrent work practice;
- a “support score” that becomes efficacious without an actual relation/counterpart process;
- a generic coping/resilience attribute that overrides organizational power;
- a reciprocal person→position edge without action, institutional gate and actual transition;
- a single undifferentiated exposure scalar standing in for event, condition, relation, activity, trajectory and cumulative process.
