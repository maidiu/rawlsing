# 3. WHOLE_MACHINE_CONTEXT_FOR_THE_SEAM

The seam sits inside this functional trajectory:

```text
SOURCE-NATIVE EVIDENCE
    -> IDENTITY / AUTHORITY / PROVENANCE
    -> COMPOSABILITY / JOINT INFORMATION
    -> LEARNED-WORLD / STRUCTURAL CONSTRUCTION
    -> PRE-V3 OBJECTIVE STRUCTURAL WORLD
    -> PRE-V3 / V3 GOVERNED TRANSITIONS
    -> V3 ACTOR / PERSON / LIFE REALIZATION
    -> V2 EXPRESSION / APPLICATION
```

The boundary is dangerous because the same noun may appear on both sides while meaning something different.

A household rule may be an objective institutional relation upstream. The actor's awareness of it is not identical to the rule. The actor's appraisal of it is not identical to awareness. An encounter in which the rule becomes consequential is not identical to the condition's existence. A response is not identical to durable change. A later objective world consequence of the actor's action is not identical to private actor state.

Designing the seam incorrectly can falsify the machine in two opposite ways.

### Failure mode 1 — upstream imperialism over the person

```text
CLASS / HOUSEHOLD / WORK / DEMOGRAPHIC / GROUP EVIDENCE
        ->
DIRECT PERSONALITY / BELIEF / DISTRESS / MOTIVE / BIOGRAPHY
```

This would counterfeit actor history from structural evidence. Rawlsing's entire Dva/Trii/PRE-V3/V3/SDTF/PFF lineage rejects that move.

### Failure mode 2 — downstream solipsism over the world

```text
PRIVATE ACTOR STATE / MODEL INTERPRETATION
        ->
OBJECTIVE WORLD OR SOCIAL HISTORY
```

This would let V3 or conversational interpretation author facts about institutions, relationships, sanctions, resources or others without externalized occurrence and competent world/source admission. Current SDTF law rejects that move.

The seam therefore cannot merely “serialize context.” It must preserve **jurisdiction**.

## B. RICH CONTENT-ADDRESSED SEAM

PRE-V3 materializes a broad immutable world packet with explicit provenance/currentness/indexes for V3.

**Strengths:** excellent replay, auditability, bounded snapshot consistency, easy deterministic actor-context binding.

**Weaknesses:** highest risk of resurrecting physical fossilization, duplicating producer authority, freezing provisional ontology, forcing eager materialization of safely reconstructible facts, and confusing world packet membership with semantic truth.

**Status:** `[DESIGN_PROPOSAL]` not justified as global winner; physical no-winner law remains.

## C. VIRTUAL / QUERYABLE SEAM

V3 interacts primarily with a governed resolver/query layer over producer-owned source-backed structural products.

**Strengths:** excellent producer ownership and freshness; avoids mandatory global materialization; aligns with Dva/Chetiri/Field Forge relation-owned semantics.

**Weaknesses:** consistent replay/world snapshot identity becomes harder; hidden-latest/currentness drift can enter through queries; source retirement and expensive reconstruction require explicit release/ref closure; OBL061 proves “query whatever is current” is insufficient for the first-canary concrete world binding.

**Status:** `[DESIGN_PROPOSAL]` strong component, not sufficient alone.

## D. HYBRID SEAM

Immutable content-addressed core world/release manifest plus governed on-demand context resolution.

**Strengths:** balances stable replay identity with producer-owned lazy derivation; can keep content-addressed root small while allowing rich context; compatible with source-backed reconstructibility.

**Weaknesses:** if carelessly designed, the manifest becomes a de facto master ontology or the cache becomes hidden authority; still risks implying one monolithic seam object.

**Status:** `[DESIGN_PROPOSAL]` strongest conventional candidate.

## E. CONTINUOUS PRODUCER DAG / NO SINGLE SEAM

No publication boundary; V3 follows producer-owned edges and queries directly through a governed dependency graph.

**Strengths:** strongest ownership fidelity; naturally preserves producer semantics and direct dereference; avoids redundant repackaging.

**Weaknesses:** runtime discoverability, consistent world/time binding, replay snapshotting, coordinated currentness and first-canary concrete root identity become difficult; operationally it may simply relocate the seam into resolver semantics without naming it.

**Status:** `[DESIGN_PROPOSAL]` strong semantic model, incomplete runtime contract by itself.

## F. FACTORIZED GOVERNED TRANSITION CONSTELLATION

This candidate treats “the seam” as a set of distinct jurisdictional transitions rather than a single file/service.

### F1 — WORLD RELEASE / IDENTITY PLANE

A stable content-addressed **release/root manifest identity**, not necessarily a full materialized world. It binds the exact producer releases/ref closure, world identity, validity/current-selection context, replay identity and dependency closure required for a downstream run.

It may reference source-backed/virtual/persistent/hybrid producer objects. Membership is not semantic truth; each referenced object retains its own owner/authority.

### F2 — CONTEXT RESOLUTION / QUERY PLANE

Governed actor/world/time/context queries resolve objective structural facts, position bases, applicability/opportunity bases and SDTF consequences without manufacturing actor-private state.

Query result identity binds query semantics, producer versions, relevant currentness/valid-time and the F1 root/release context.

### F3 — OBJECTIVE NON-EVENT INGRESS PLANE

A dedicated OBL062 contract admits lawful objective `CONDITION`, `DIRECTED_RELATION`, `RECURRENT_ACTIVITY`, and other non-event context into V3 as **objective inputs**, preserving source/rule/application/fact/witness identity and currentness.

It does not synthesize an event and does not perform actor appraisal.

### F4 — OCCURRENCE / DEVELOPMENTAL REALIZATION PLANE

Actual occurrences/events pass through event admission and V3's own encounter/exposure/route machinery. CAN-008 R2 owns person x event receivability, uptake/appraisal, recruitment and response. Durable effects go only to their competent V3 owners.

V3-generated/externalized actions can affect later objective world history only through competent occurrence + external world-response/source-admission chains.

### F5 — INTERACTION / EXPRESSION WRAPPER

Where PFF/chat modality applies, bounded interaction-fast context/hypothesis/residue wraps the CAN-008 process and hands lawful expression constraints to V2. This plane remains subordinate to eventual independent review/HQ adoption of the PFF interface candidate.

**Strengths:** explicitly maps the actual current authority boundaries; preserves non-event vs event; supports hybrid physical realization without preselecting storage; answers OBL061's root-identity need without demanding eager global materialization; makes currentness and negative semantics inspectable; prevents V3 from becoming either a structure-reading free-for-all or a world-writing solipsism.

**Weaknesses:** more interfaces and therefore more explicit identity/currentness plumbing; requires a formal seam constitution; may be accused of renaming the producer DAG unless the four transition contracts are concretely specified and tested; does not itself settle physical performance tradeoffs.

**Status:** `[DESIGN_PROPOSAL]` provisionally preferred.

# INTERNAL HOSTILE COURT

The preferred F design was attacked against the commission's hostile questions.

## Attack 1 — Is F just Continuous DAG with extra nouns?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** F is acceptable only if F1-F4 are formal jurisdictional contracts with testable identities/currentness/negative semantics. If they remain labels over a resolver, collapse F back into E and do not pretend a new architecture exists.

## Attack 2 — Does F1 resurrect the rich master world packet?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** F1 must be a release/root identity and reference closure, not a universal eager semantic payload. Producer objects keep independent authority. If F1 requires co-materializing every fact, reject F in favor of a narrower hybrid/query arrangement.

## Attack 3 — Does actor-indexed objective context get misclassified as psychology?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** SDTF proves actor-positioned objective social facts are lawful upstream. F3 must preserve that category. The firewall is objective-vs-subjective/developmental, not actor-indexed-vs-nonactor-indexed.

## Attack 4 — Does OBL061's world-root blocker falsely prove a universal architecture requirement?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** no. It proves only that the concrete first-canary runtime needs a lawful content-addressed world/context root. C3 must adjudicate the generalization ceiling.

## Attack 5 — Does F let non-events enter CAN-008 directly?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** no. Objective non-event context may condition V3 state/context through F3; CAN-008 R2's precipitating-event path still requires an actual generated/admitted event where its event transaction is invoked.

## Attack 6 — Does the model's own interpretation become actor appraisal?

**Risk:** high.

**Disposition:** no. Optional modality/interpretive evidence is evidence available to the CAN-008 coupling process; actor uptake/appraisal is separately computed and may be partial, distorted, uncertain or valid-empty.

## Attack 7 — Does private V3 state world-write?

**Risk:** fatal if yes.

**Disposition:** forbidden. Only externalized occurrence plus competent external objective response/source admission may change objective history.

## Attack 8 — Does F prematurely choose virtual/hybrid storage?

**Risk:** medium-high.

**Disposition:** F is physical-neutral. It requires stable identity/ref closure and lawful resolution; it does not require the referenced object itself to be virtual or persistent. The eventual A/B/C court remains sovereign over measured physical tradeoffs at its authorized grain.

## Attack 9 — What historical Rawlsing failure would F repeat?

Most dangerous repetition: turning a useful runtime bundle into a semantic master carrier and then allowing “present in the bundle” to become authority. The cure is explicit producer ownership + release/carrier separation + no hidden-latest + no cache authority.

## Attack 10 — What would falsify the preference?

F should be downgraded or rejected if a formal court proves any of the following:

- the current authorities cannot be composed without inventing a new semantic owner;
- a stable root identity cannot coexist with producer-owned currentness/replay;
- generic non-event ingress requires semantics incompatible with the proposed F3 boundary;
- the physical A/B/C court proves a simpler architecture can preserve every hard invariant with materially less complexity and no hidden-authority tradeoff;
- F1-F4 cannot be given independent conformance tests and collapse into decorative naming.

No such falsification is presently proved. `[REASONED_INFERENCE]`
