## 1. Evidence discipline

This ledger distinguishes text actually inspected from bibliography merely identified. The T3 lane uses theory as mechanism evidence, not as automatic Rawlsing authority. Claim status is carried into every mechanism card.

Binding firewall:

```text
THEORIST CLAIM != EMPIRICALLY ESTABLISHED RESULT != RAWLSING ADOPTION
STRUCTURAL CATEGORY != PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAIT
STRUCTURAL POSSIBILITY != REALIZED EXPERIENCE
REALIZED EXPERIENCE != ACTOR UPTAKE
ACTOR UPTAKE != DURABLE CHANGE
```

The user's File Library was checked for the named T3 corpus before web excavation; no usable primary T3 source copy was recovered there. Public primary texts, publisher-hosted material, author retrospectives, and official empirical reports were therefore used. No claim below implies inspection of inaccessible portions of a copyrighted book.

## 2. Inspected sources retained for mechanism extraction

### S01 — Karl Marx, *Capital*, Volume I, Chapter 6, “The Buying and Selling of Labour-Power”

- SOURCE TYPE: primary theoretical text.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical / historical-materialist.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Chapter 6, especially the passage defining the legally free labourer who owns labour-power yet lacks the independent means required to realize it.
- INSPECTION STATUS: full public chapter inspected.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch06.htm
- MECHANISM VALUE: property separation + formal market freedom can generate a recurrent material dependency on selling labour-power. This is a relation, not a personality prior.

### S02 — Karl Marx, *Capital*, Volume I, Chapter 10, “The Working-Day”

- SOURCE TYPE: primary theoretical text drawing extensively on historical/official material.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical + historical/observational source use.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Sections 1, 5–7; conflict over the duration of the working day, Factory Acts, and collective struggle over legally bounded work-time.
- INSPECTION STATUS: full public chapter inspected.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch10.htm
- MECHANISM VALUE: the wage/property relation becomes an actor-relative time-control, fatigue, social-time and legal-regulation ecology only through concrete employment and regulatory relations; collective action can alter that ecology.

### S03 — Karl Marx, *Capital*, Volume I, Chapter 15, “Machinery and Modern Industry”

- SOURCE TYPE: primary theoretical text drawing on factory reports.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical + historical/observational source use.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Section 3, “The Proximate Effects of Machinery on the Workman,” and Section 4, “The Factory.”
- INSPECTION STATUS: full public chapter inspected.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch15.htm
- MECHANISM VALUE: technical organization, supervision, machine pacing, work-group composition, penalties and workplace conditions concretely alter activity form, authority, sanction and exposure.

### S04 — Karl Marx, *Capital*, Volume I, Chapter 23, “Simple Reproduction”

- SOURCE TYPE: primary theoretical text.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical / historical-materialist.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Chapter 23, especially recurrent reproduction of separation between labour-power and means of labour and the periodic return of the wage labourer to the labour market.
- INSPECTION STATUS: full public chapter inspected.
- URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch23.htm
- MECHANISM VALUE: social position can be reproduced through recurrence of material relations and dependencies, without assuming reproduction of a psychological trait.

### S05 — Harry Braverman, *Labor and Monopoly Capital: The Degradation of Work in the Twentieth Century*, 25th Anniversary ed. (Monthly Review Press, 1998; orig. 1974)

- SOURCE TYPE: theoretical/historical labour-process analysis.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical + historical synthesis; generality contested in later labour-process literature.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Part I, Chapter 3 “The Division of Labor” and Chapter 4 “Scientific Management,” especially pp. 78–83 in the 25th Anniversary edition: dissociation of the labour process from workers' skills, separation of conception from execution, and managerial concentration of production knowledge.
- INSPECTION STATUS: exact primary-text pages were inspectable in a public indexed PDF; publication metadata cross-checked against Monthly Review Press / NYU Press.
- PUBLISHER: https://monthlyreview.org/9780853459408/labor-and-monopoly-capital/
- MECHANISM VALUE: managerial organization can redistribute information, discretion, task breadth, pace and skill exercise. This does not license universal deskilling or an automatic psychological effect.

### S06 — Michael Burawoy, *Manufacturing Consent: Changes in the Labor Process Under Monopoly Capitalism* (University of Chicago Press, 1979/1982 editions)

- SOURCE TYPE: workplace ethnography + Marxist labour-process theory.
- CLAIM STATUS: qualitative/ethnographic + theoretical; historically and organizationally bounded.
- EXACT LOCATIONS USED: Chapter 5 “The Labor Process as a Game,” pp. 77–94; Chapter 6 “The Rise of an Internal Labor Market,” pp. 95–108; Chapter 7 “Consolidating an Internal State,” pp. 109–122.
- INSPECTION STATUS: publisher-hosted chapter boundaries/metadata inspected; detailed mechanism statements were cross-checked against Burawoy's own retrospective account rather than inferred from chapter titles alone.
- BOOK: https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/M/bo23899991.html
- AUTHOR RETROSPECTIVE USED: Michael Burawoy, “Ethnographic fallacies: reflections on labour studies in the era of market fundamentalism,” *Work, Employment and Society* 27(3) (2013): 526–536, DOI 10.1177/0950017012460316.
- MECHANISM VALUE: work games, internal labour markets, grievance machinery and collective bargaining can alter incentives, mobility, rights, obligations, peer relations and managerial discretion while preserving worker agency and conflict.

### S07 — Paul Willis, *Learning to Labour: How Working Class Kids Get Working Class Jobs* (Saxon House, 1977)

- SOURCE TYPE: ethnography + theoretical interpretation.
- CLAIM STATUS: qualitative/ethnographic; small, historically specific sample; contested as a general account of class reproduction.
- EXACT LOCATIONS USED: Introduction pp. 1–3; “Elements of a Culture” beginning p. 11; “Class and Institutional Form of a Culture” beginning p. 52; “Labour Power, Culture, Class and Institution” beginning p. 89. Primary preview also exposes the school-to-work argument and actors' active cultural mediation.
- INSPECTION STATUS: publisher/Google Books and publisher preview excerpts inspected; only exposed text is treated as read.
- PREVIEW: https://books.google.com/books?id=3zmVaLrGIDEC
- MECHANISM VALUE: peer culture, school authority, sanctions, status, credential practices and interpretations can alter the realized route from school into work. Resistance can reproduce a subordinate route without being mere passive socialization.

### S08 — Erik Olin Wright, *Class Counts*, Student Edition (Cambridge University Press, 2000)

- SOURCE TYPE: Marxist class theory + comparative empirical class analysis.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical + observational/comparative empirical.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Chapter 1, pp. 16–18 in the public author PDF: employee differentiation by authority and skill/expertise; surveillance/sanctions/hierarchy; managerial “loyalty rent”; scarce skills/credentials as labour-market power. Chapter 2 is additionally useful for the warning that class structure is inferred from concrete positions rather than directly observed as a person essence.
- INSPECTION STATUS: public full-text author PDF inspected.
- STABLE RECORD/PDF: https://www.aacademica.org/erik.olin.wright/49
- MECHANISM VALUE: “class” decomposes into property, authority and skill/credential relations that alter command, earnings, promotion, bargaining power and life chances through concrete mechanisms.

### S09 — Lise Vogel, *Marxism and the Oppression of Women: Toward a Unitary Theory* (1983; Historical Materialism ed. 2013)

- SOURCE TYPE: Marxist-feminist theoretical reconstruction.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical / historical-materialist; family/gender conclusions explicitly require historical specification.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Chapter 10, “The Reproduction of Labour-Power,” pp. 141–156; Chapter 11, “Beyond Domestic Labour,” pp. 157–182.
- INSPECTION STATUS: primary text excerpts and full chapter spans publicly inspectable; publisher table of contents cross-checked.
- PUBLISHER TOC: https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/book-series/marxism-and-the-oppression-of-women/
- MECHANISM VALUE: maintenance and generational replacement of bearers of labour-power require socially allocated necessary labour, but no invariant family form follows. Household, state, migration and other arrangements are historically variable and contested.

### S10 — Nancy Fraser, “Contradictions of Capital and Care,” *New Left Review* 100 (July–August 2016): 99–117

- SOURCE TYPE: social-theoretical/historical synthesis.
- CLAIM STATUS: theoretical + interpretive historical comparison.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: pp. 99–117, especially the production/reproduction separation, historically changing care regimes, financialized two-earner household, time pressure, commodification and transnational care-chain discussion.
- INSPECTION STATUS: full author article inspected on NLR.
- URL: https://newleftreview.org/issues/ii100/articles/nancy-fraser-contradictions-of-capital-and-care
- DOI: 10.64590/nt2
- MECHANISM VALUE: institutions, wages, welfare provision, labour-market participation and migration can jointly reallocate care time, costs and counterpart dependencies.

### S11 — Laura Addati, Umberto Cattaneo, Valeria Esquivel & Isabel Valarino, *Care Work and Care Jobs for the Future of Decent Work* (ILO, 2018)

- SOURCE TYPE: official cross-national empirical report.
- CLAIM STATUS: observational/descriptive comparative evidence + policy analysis.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: Executive Summary and Chapter 2 on unpaid care work and gender inequalities at work; report contains original data from more than 90 countries and time-use comparisons.
- INSPECTION STATUS: official ILO report page and indexed PDF findings inspected.
- URL: https://www.ilo.org/publications/major-publications/care-work-and-care-jobs-future-decent-work
- MECHANISM VALUE: empirically establishes that care-time allocation varies by institutional and socioeconomic conditions and is tightly linked to paid-work opportunities; it supports variable actor-relative time/resource ecology, not a gender essence.

### S12 — Jacques Charmes, *The Unpaid Care Work and the Labour Market: An Analysis of Time Use Data Based on the Latest World Compilation of Time-use Surveys* (ILO, 2019)

- SOURCE TYPE: cross-national time-use synthesis.
- CLAIM STATUS: observational/descriptive comparative evidence.
- EXACT LOCATION USED: report overview and sections on variation in unpaid care work by geography, age, education, activity/employment status, income, marital status, and presence/age of children.
- INSPECTION STATUS: official ILO record and report metadata inspected; used only for boundary-condition support.
- URL: https://www.ilo.org/publications/unpaid-care-work-and-labour-market-analysis-time-use-data-based-latest
- MECHANISM VALUE: demonstrates strong heterogeneity inside demographic categories and therefore supports explicit household/institution/time-use mediators instead of demographic priors.
