`YES_AS_A_REQUIRED_BOUNDARY_TRANSFORMATION__NO_AS_LICENSE_FOR_PREV3_TO_MINT_PREMISES`.

Holzkamp explicitly makes objective meanings/action possibilities distinct from subjective realization. At pp. 352–353 and 367–369, objective/personal conditions become premises only through subject-relative phenomenal/situational givenness and biography.

Rawlsing therefore needs an explicit lawful receiving path:

```text
ACTOR_RELATIVE_OBJECTIVE_SOCIAL_FACT
-> FIRST_PERSON / PHENOMENAL AVAILABILITY
-> PREMISE
```

but M4 does not authorize a new PRE-V3 psychology owner.

**PRE-V3/SDTF end:** typed, provenance-preserving actor-relative **objective** social fact/action possibility with exact actor/position/context/time/provenance.

**V3 begins:** first-person/phenomenal availability, subjective possibility space, premise, ground/reason, motive, refusal/coercion/decision, response, memory, learning and durable development.

The exact firewall is:

```text
ACTOR_RELATIVE_OBJECTIVE_SOCIAL_FACT
!= SUBJECTIVE_PREMISE
```

### D. What part of restrictive/generalized agency survives normalization?

Only a situated relational/action alternative:

```text
RESTRICTIVE DIRECTION:
  secure action-capacity within existing dependency/power relations,
  potentially through accommodation/instrumentalization,
  with possible short-run gains and long-run narrowing of collective possibilities

  practically expand shared control over conditions through real cooperation/association,
  resources/means, organization/counter-power and external relational gates
```

It is **not** a personality type, group type, ideology type, moral score or scalar “agency” state.
