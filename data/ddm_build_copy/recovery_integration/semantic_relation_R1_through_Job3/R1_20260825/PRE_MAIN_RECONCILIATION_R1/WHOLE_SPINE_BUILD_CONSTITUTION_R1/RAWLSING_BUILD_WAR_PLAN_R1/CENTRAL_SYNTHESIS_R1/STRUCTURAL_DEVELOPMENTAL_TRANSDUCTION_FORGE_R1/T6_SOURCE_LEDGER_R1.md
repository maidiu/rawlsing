## 2. Source-level adjudications

### S01 Feld — retained mechanism

The useful object is not “network” in the abstract. It is the proposition that **extra-network foci organize joint activity and therefore alter who repeatedly encounters whom**, which can generate ties and clusters. This supplies a positive production rule for counterpart/contact ecology missing from a topology-only representation.

### S02 Granovetter — retained with strict boundary

The retained mechanism is **bridging position → access to otherwise nonredundant circles/information → changed opportunity visibility**. The actor still must receive, interpret and use information. The structural fact is neither a guarantee of contact nor a guarantee of benefit. Granovetter’s own p. 1364 caveat that weak ties are not automatically bridges is binding for T6.

### S03 Burt — retained with actor-action firewall

Burt sharpens the bridge mechanism into brokerage across group separation and identifies information arbitrage/translation as the positive mediator. The especially useful negative ceiling is that **networks do not act**; they are contexts for action. Therefore T6 maps brokerage to opportunity/information ecology, not directly to development.

### S04 Coleman — decomposed; generic stock rejected

Retained: obligations/expectations backed by trustworthiness; information channels; closure-enabled sanction capacity; appropriable organization. Rejected: a generic scalar “social capital” prior that directly raises development. Each mechanism must be represented through concrete obligations, information, sanctions, support or organized capacity.

### S05 Merton — retained role-set mechanism

A status can place an actor into a **set of counterpart relations with heterogeneous expectations and powers**. Consequences depend on observability, power distribution, conflict among expectations, insulation, peer organization and the actor’s ability to abridge or negotiate relations. This blocks `STATUS -> SINGLE ROLE EFFECT` compression.

### S06–S08 discrimination/status evidence — retained as gate-realization evidence

These studies demonstrate a causal or quasi-causal route from a presented classification cue/status signal to **differential institutional evaluation and access outcomes** in specific hiring contexts. T6 does not infer a person’s traits from race, criminal record, sex or parenthood. The mechanism-bearing object is the gatekeeper’s realized treatment conditional on a cue and institutional decision process.

### S09 Bronfenbrenner — retained cross-setting linkage, labels rejected

The useful result is the explicit multi-step causal requirement for exosystem effects and the mesosystem focus on linkages between settings. Merely naming “micro/meso/exo/macro” creates no explanatory gain and is rejected.

### S10 Emirbayer & Mische — retained temporal agency grammar

Iteration, projection and practical evaluation are retained as **analytical dimensions of actor action selection under a history and present context**, not as durable personality dimensions. Their function is to prevent a structure→action deterministic shortcut and to represent how prior practice, possible futures and current contingencies jointly condition action.

### S11 Sewell — retained reciprocity/resource mobilization check

The useful mechanism is not a new universal definition of structure. It is that actors can have unequal access/control over resources and can apply schemas across contexts; action can thereby reproduce or alter later relations. This supplies a candidate bridge for reciprocal `PERSON/ACTION -> LATER POSITION/RELATION/EXPOSURE` without voluntarism.

## 3. Rejected / deferred literature families

- Generic “social capital” as a single fungible scalar — `REJECT`; decomposed into information, obligations, sanctions, support and brokerage/closure mechanisms.
- Generic “culture affects behavior” — `REJECT`; must cash out as classification, legitimacy, expectation, sanction, information or gate treatment.
- Generic “ecological layers” — `REJECT`; retain only cross-setting causal linkage and counterpart/activity consequences.
- Generic “agency” as an unconstrained personal power — `REJECT`; retain temporally situated action under concrete resources, relations and contingencies.
- Generic “role” as a direct trait prior — `REJECT`; role consequences require counterpart-specific expectations, observability, sanctions and realized interaction.
- Network centrality or tie count without a mechanism for access, obligation, support, information, sanction or counterpart formation — `DEFER/REJECT` depending on use.
