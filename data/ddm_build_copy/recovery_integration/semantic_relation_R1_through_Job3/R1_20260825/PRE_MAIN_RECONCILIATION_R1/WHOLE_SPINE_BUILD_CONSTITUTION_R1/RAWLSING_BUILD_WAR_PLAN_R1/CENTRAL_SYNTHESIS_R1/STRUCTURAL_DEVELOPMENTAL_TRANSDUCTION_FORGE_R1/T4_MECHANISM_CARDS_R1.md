## T4-M01 — OCCUPATIONAL SELF-DIRECTION AS RECURRENT PRACTICE ECOLOGY

**SOURCE**  
Kohn & Schooler 1978, *AJS* 84(1):24–52, DOI `10.1086/226739`; Kohn & Schooler 1982, *AJS* 87(6):1257–1286, especially p. 1261 and pp. 1281–1282, DOI `10.1086/227593`; corroborative boundary source Miller et al. 1979, *AJS* 85(1):66–94, DOI `10.1086/226974`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal observational reciprocal structural-equation evidence in Kohn/Schooler; observational causal/path analysis in the women’s replication. Stronger than cross-sectional association; not randomized causal identification.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Concrete organization of work: substantive complexity, closeness of supervision, routinization/repetitiveness, and the latitude these jointly permit for occupational self-direction. Not occupational class as a psychological proxy.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
The job repeatedly requires or suppresses thought, independent judgment, initiative, decisions under ill-defined/conflicting contingencies, and variable/nonroutine problem solving. The causal candidate is recurrent practice under organized task/authority conditions, not mere membership in an occupational category.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
A particular worker actually receives more or less latitude to decide, judge, initiate and solve; faces more or less repetitive/predictable task sequences; and is more or less tightly directed by a supervisor.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
A structurally possible self-directed job is not enough. The actor must actually occupy the job and repeatedly perform under its concrete task and supervision conditions. `AVAILABLE_AUTONOMY != EXERCISED_SELF_DIRECTION`.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
The actor can use, avoid, resist, reinterpret or fail to exploit latitude. Prior flexibility and orientation also affect later selection/attainment. The source does not license deterministic uptake.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Ideational/intellectual flexibility and self-directed orientations to self and society; related work also links self-directed work to self-evaluation/control orientations.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`RECURRENT_ACTIVITY / CONDITION / TRAJECTORY`. The mechanism is not well represented as one event.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Organization/work role/supervisory relation embedded in occupational stratification.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Earlier ideational flexibility/self-directed orientation predicts later movement toward more responsible jobs with greater self-direction. Kohn & Schooler 1978 describe the person→complexity path as lagged and gradual.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Job duration and actual task exposure; supervision; routinization; available mobility; historical labor market; prior capacities; sex/cohort/generalizability. Miller et al. supports generalization beyond the original male panel but does not erase other context limits.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
No `CLASS → FLEXIBILITY`, `OCCUPATION → TRAIT`, or `AUTONOMY_AVAILABLE → AUTONOMY_USED` inference. “Learning generalization” names an interpretation of repeated experience; it cannot substitute for the actual practice/uptake chain.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
- PRE-V3 owner: structural pressure/affordance/applicability + mediation-route possibility can represent actor-independent task/authority possibilities.
- Existing actor/V3 side: actor binding; opportunity/constraint; realized activity/history; uptake/response; memory/learning/biography/path dependence.
- Missing connective function candidate: `WORK_ORGANIZATION_TO_ACTOR_RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_ECOLOGY`, a specialization of `STRUCTURED_WORLD_TO_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_TRANSDUCTION`.

**DISPOSITION**  
`MISSING` for the general positive derivation of recurrent activity ecology; `REUSE` existing PRE-V3 possibility and V3 developmental owners.

## T4-M02 — SUPERVISION / ROUTINIZATION / PRESSURE AS CONSTRAINT AND DISTRESS CONDITIONS

**SOURCE**  
Kohn & Schooler 1982, especially p. 1261 definitions and pp. 1281–1282 conclusions; Miller et al. 1979, pp. 66–94.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal observational reciprocal model plus observational replication/extension.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Close supervision, repetitive/predictable work, job pressures and uncertainties, and other “oppressive” working conditions.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Close supervision and routinization constrain repeated initiative/judgment; pressures and uncertainty repeatedly impose demands whose consequences cannot be reduced to status labels.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
Reduced decision latitude; repeated compliance demands; repetitive task ecology; uncertainty/pressure actually encountered in work.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
The actor must be subject to the supervision/task regime or pressure in practice. Formal hierarchy without actual supervisory control does not suffice.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Prior orientation can affect job movement and response. Distress is an outcome to be metabolized, not an automatic structural inscription.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Distress and, depending on work condition, flexibility, orientation and self-evaluation.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`CONDITION + RECURRENT_ACTIVITY`; potentially cumulative.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Supervisor–worker relation and organization.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Personality/orientation affects later job placement; therefore later exposure to supervision/routinization can partly be selection-mediated.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Actual supervisory practice, job tenure, mobility opportunities, task variability, pressure type, historical work organization.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Do not infer distress from low occupational rank, nor identify every structured work demand as “stress.” The sources do not prove a universal one-to-one mapping from any single work condition to a durable trait.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
- PRE-V3: structural pressure; authority/control affordance/constraint; route possibility.
- V3 side: actor-relative constraint/encounter/history and existing response/development machinery.
- Candidate gap: `AUTHORITY_TASK_REGIME_TO_REALIZED_RECURRENT_CONSTRAINT_EXPOSURE` plus persistent-condition semantics if existing carriers cannot preserve duration.

**DISPOSITION**  
`GENERALIZE` existing structural pressure/opportunity machinery to preserve actor-relative repeated condition; do not add a psychology owner.

## T4-M03 — WORK AUTONOMY → COMPETENCE / MASTERY / CONTROL ORIENTATION

**SOURCE**  
Mortimer & Lorence 1979, “Occupational Experience and the Self-Concept,” *SPQ* 42(4):307–323, DOI `10.2307/3033802`; Wu, Griffin & Parker 2015, *Journal of Vocational Behavior* 89:102–108, DOI `10.1016/j.jvb.2015.05.004`; Staff & Mortimer 2024, *Social Forces* 103(1):305–326, DOI `10.1093/sf/soae067`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal observational panel; three-wave latent-difference model; 31-year prospective fixed-effects cohort. No randomized work assignment.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Actual job autonomy/self-direction: discretion over work activity/decisions, plus related learning/skill opportunities.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Repeated opportunities to make consequential choices and exercise competence provide experience that actions can affect outcomes. Wu et al. specifically found a direct autonomy→internal-control path while proposed indirect paths through job satisfaction were weak; skill utilization did not independently shift locus of control in that study.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
The actor actually has and exercises work discretion, receives learning opportunities, and experiences successful/unsuccessful control over work tasks.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
Formal job autonomy must become experienced decision latitude; actor must engage tasks. Skill requirements on paper are not equivalent to skill use.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Prior competence/control orientation can help select into better work and affect how latitude is used.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Competence/personal efficacy, mastery, self-esteem, internal locus/control orientation.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`RECURRENT_ACTIVITY + CUMULATIVE_PROCESS`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Work role/organization.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Mortimer/Lorence and the larger Kohn/Schooler corpus support competence/orientation→later work outcomes; selection is part of the mechanism, not merely nuisance variance.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Available discretion, duration, actual success/failure, prior competence, job changes, labor-market opportunity. Staff & Mortimer’s main effects are proximal/contemporaneous; their lagged analyses were mostly null, limiting claims about long-delay persistence.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Do not equate internal locus of control with global agency or moral responsibility. Do not infer that every “high-skill” job increases mastery. Wu et al.’s null skill-utilization path is explicit counterevidence to a unitary good-work effect.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
- PRE-V3: work-control affordance/applicability.
- V3: realized practice → actor uptake/response → learning/memory/development owners.
- Candidate gap: concrete transduction from organizational allocation of control into actor-exercised control history.

**DISPOSITION**  
`GENERALIZE` existing route/opportunity/practice semantics; developmental target is `REUSE` under V3, not a new T4 psychology object.

## T4-M04 — DISCRETE JOB DISRUPTION → MATERIAL STRAIN → SELF-CONCEPT → DISTRESS

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin, Menaghan, Lieberman & Mullan 1981, “The Stress Process,” *JHSB* 22(4):337–356, especially pp. 344–350, DOI `10.2307/2136676`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Four-year longitudinal observational panel with path and interaction models; explicit partial mediation evidence.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Involuntary job disruption (firing, layoff, downgrade, work exit due illness in the selected event set) plus labor/income dependence.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Job disruption can reduce material resources/income and intensify enduring economic strain. Economic strain is measured as difficulty obtaining necessities such as food, clothing, housing and medical care plus other consumption needs. In the reported path model, controlling economic strain reduced the direct disruption→depression coefficient, supporting partial mediation. Increased strain also predicted erosion of self-esteem/mastery, which related to increased depressive symptoms.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
Actual loss/change of job; actual household income/resource change; concrete difficulty meeting needs; ongoing role/economic demands.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
`JOB_DISRUPTION_EVENT != ECONOMIC_STRAIN`. The event must causally alter material/role conditions for the strain path. The same event can have different consequences if resources, household obligations or recovery differ.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Coping/support can intervene at particular junctures; appraisal and prior self-concepts may alter response. Pearlin et al. explicitly reject a single deterministic path.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Mastery/self-esteem and depressive distress; not “personality” as an undifferentiated whole.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`EVENT → CONDITION → CUMULATIVE_PROCESS`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Work organization/labor market → household material ecology → person.

**RECIPROCITY**  
The 1981 authors explicitly note that some modeled unidirectional paths are likely reciprocal and give reverse formulations as plausible alternatives; this study itself does not identify the reverse path strongly enough to normalize it as proven.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Income replacement, savings/resources, household needs, duration, later reemployment, support, coping, event timing and the four-year observation window.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
No `UNEMPLOYED → DEPRESSED` prior. A job-loss event does not guarantee strain; strain does not guarantee diminished mastery; diminished mastery does not guarantee depression. The paper’s own alternative-model and generality caveats are binding.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
- PRE-V3: actor-independent possibility of disruption, resource dependency, structural pressure and route.
- Realization/V3: OEE/encounter can carry the discrete disruption; actor history can carry realized consequences; existing V3 owners receive mastery/distress effects.
- Candidate gap: `REALIZED_EVENT_TO_PERSISTENT_SOCIAL_CONDITION_PROPAGATION` and material-resource/role-strain transduction. Event carrier alone is insufficient.

**DISPOSITION**  
`MISSING` connective social-condition propagation; `REUSE` event/OEE and V3 response/development owners.

## T4-M05 — CHRONIC ROLE / ECONOMIC STRAIN WITHOUT A DECISIVE EVENT

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin et al. 1981, especially pp. 338–345 and 351–352; Pearlin 1989, *JHSB* 30(3):241–256, DOI `10.2307/2136956`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal empirical model plus theoretical/review generalization.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Persistent economic difficulty, role overload/conflict, recurring demands, uncertainty or other chronic strain-producing conditions.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Repeated unresolved demands and material insufficiency persist across time and can erode positive self-concepts and elevate distress. Pearlin et al. explicitly state that stress-provoking strains can be present without precipitating events.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
Ongoing inability to meet needs, recurrent demands/obligations, persistent mismatch between resources and requirements, continuing uncertainty.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
The condition must remain operative for the actor over an interval. Repeating an event token is not semantically identical to a continuous role/material condition.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Coping, support, meaning and action may change severity/duration or terminate the condition; individual coping can be structurally limited.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Mastery, self-esteem, distress and downstream behavior/health manifestations depending on the specific process.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`CONDITION + CUMULATIVE_PROCESS`; may be part of a trajectory.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Household, organization, role system, institution.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Actor actions may resolve, intensify or relocate strain; altered psychological state may influence later selection/exposure, but these reverse pathways require separate evidence.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Duration, intensity, competing strains, resource reserves, support, role constellation, historical context and observation window.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Do not reduce chronic strain to an OEE event solely for implementation convenience. Do not infer that all exposure accumulates linearly or that duration alone determines effect.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
Phase-0 marks CONDITION as undergeneralized/fragmentary. Existing context/state/history may compose some cases, but positive persistence semantics are unproved.

**DISPOSITION**  
`GENERALIZE` if existing state/history owners can lawfully preserve interval persistence; otherwise report a seam-level `MISSING` persistent-condition connective semantics. T4 does not choose the carrier before Lane R.

## T4-M06 — COPING AS PROBLEM-SPECIFIC SUBJECT ACTION AT DISTINCT JUNCTURES

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin et al. 1981, pp. 340–341 and 348–351; concept lineage Pearlin & Schooler 1978, DOI `10.2307/2136319`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal observational interaction/path evidence, with theoretical functional distinctions.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
A realized stressor/strain that presents an actor with a concrete problem.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Coping can modify the situation, modify its meaning, or manage symptoms. In the job-disruption analysis, coping did not simply exert a uniform direct depression buffer; it affected earlier parts of the chain, including economic strain and self-concept, and interacted with job disruption.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
The actor has available coping practices and actually mobilizes a problem-appropriate response.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
`COPING_REPERTOIRE_AVAILABLE != COPING_USED != COPING_EFFECTIVE`. Efficacy must be assessed against the problem and juncture.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
This is explicitly an agency/action locus. Coping is not a fixed generalized disposition in the retained Pearlin formulation.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Primarily modifies exposure/strain/self-concept/distress process; any durable person change remains downstream and must be earned.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`ACTION / RECURRENT_PRACTICE` occurring at one or more process junctures.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Actor within role/household/organization.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Effective coping can alter the condition itself and therefore future exposure; failure can leave structural conditions intact. This is bounded agency, not voluntarism.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Problem type. Pearlin et al. emphasize that individual coping is relatively ineffective against problems rooted in formal organization, authority and reward systems, while more effective in some face-to-face relational problems.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
No generic `RESILIENCE_SCORE → NO_STRESS`. Do not let coping erase material constraint or organizational authority.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
Actor action/response belongs on the V3/realized side; no new PRE-V3 psychology. The seam must expose the actual problem/constraint to existing action and response owners.

**DISPOSITION**  
`REUSE` V3 action/response machinery; `GENERALIZE` seam routing only if problem-specific action can currently alter later condition/exposure.

## T4-M07 — SOCIAL SUPPORT REQUIRES REALIZED RELATIONAL QUALITY, NOT NETWORK MEMBERSHIP

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin et al. 1981, pp. 340–341 and 348–351; House, Umberson & Landis 1988, *Annual Review of Sociology* 14:293–318, DOI `10.1146/annurev.so.14.080188.001453`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Longitudinal observational stress-process evidence plus review.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Potential network ties, groups, organizations and relationships capable of providing emotional/instrumental support.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Support depends on realized involvement, concern, trust, solidarity, intimate communication and/or instrumental assistance. In Pearlin’s job-loss model, support chiefly protected self-concept/mastery rather than preventing economic strain or directly suppressing depression.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
A particular actor can actually access and use sustaining relational resources from specific ties.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
`NETWORK_TIE_EXISTS != SUPPORT_AVAILABLE != SUPPORT_MOBILIZED != SUPPORT_RECEIVED != SUPPORT_EFFECTIVE`.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Help-seeking, trust, relationship history, interpretation and counterpart willingness matter; support is relational, not unilateral possession.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Can preserve mastery/self-esteem and indirectly reduce downstream distress; broader outcomes require separate evidence.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`RELATION + ACTION + CONDITION`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Dyad/network/household/group/organization.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Actors maintain, withdraw from, mobilize or damage support relations; stress can itself reshape network availability. The retained sources prove the distinction more strongly than all reverse pathways.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Tie quality, problem fit, reciprocity, trust, availability, burden on supporter, timing and type of aid.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
No `NETWORK_SIZE → PROTECTION`; no automatic benefit from family/friend existence; no assumption that support neutralizes material deprivation.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
- PRE-V3 interaction topology can represent possible ties/configurations.
- V3 relationship/history can represent actor-specific realized relations.
- Candidate connective gap: `POSSIBLE_NETWORK_TO_REALIZED_SUPPORT_PROCESS`, preserving counterpart participation and tie quality.

**DISPOSITION**  
`GENERALIZE` topology→relationship realization; `REUSE` V3 relationship/history. Reject any scalar “support” shortcut detached from realized relations.

## T4-M08 — STRESS PROLIFERATION ACROSS ROLES AND ACTIVITY DOMAINS

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin, Aneshensel & LeBlanc 1997, *JHSB* 38(3):223–236, DOI `10.2307/2955368`; Pearlin 1989 as conceptual precursor.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Empirical observational mechanism study plus review/theory.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
An enduring role whose demands increase or collide with other roles/activities; AIDS caregiving is the empirical case, not the ontology.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Primary hardship expands within the originating role (e.g., overload/captivity) and imposes constraints on other roles, generating secondary stressors in social, leisure and occupational domains.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
More time demand, obligation, restricted activity, role conflict, lost opportunities and new strains in domains not identical to the source domain.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
A source strain must actually alter the actor’s other role/activity ecology. Co-occurring stressors are not automatically proliferation; a route of constraint/propagation must exist.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Actors can reorganize roles, seek help, withdraw, resist or alter participation; institutions/counterparts can redistribute demands.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
Indirectly distress, mastery/self-concept and other downstream outcomes after proliferation is realized.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`CUMULATIVE_PROCESS + TRAJECTORY + RELATION/CONDITION`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Household/care dyad → network/work organization and other role domains.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Changed action in one domain alters later conditions in another; later psychological/resource changes may in turn alter role capacity, but the study primarily supports stressor→stressor propagation.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Demand growth, substitute caregivers/resources, role centrality, time control, institutional accommodation and historical context.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Do not model “stress” as a conserved scalar that simply increases. Proliferation requires typed source and secondary conditions plus a propagation route.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
PRE-V3 route/topology plus actor-specific condition/history can represent endpoints; Phase-0 does not prove a general mechanism for one realized social condition to reconfigure the actor’s broader opportunity/activity ecology.

**DISPOSITION**  
`MISSING` or `GENERALIZE` a cross-domain condition-propagation connective function; no new distress owner.

## T4-M09 — SOCIAL POSITION → DIFFERENTIAL STRESS EXPOSURE, NOT SOCIAL POSITION → DISTRESS

**SOURCE**  
Pearlin 1989, *JHSB* 30(3):241–256; Turner, Wheaton & Lloyd 1995, *ASR* 60(1):104–125, DOI `10.2307/2096348`.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Theoretical/review formulation plus observational population evidence.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
Social/institutional position and its concrete allocation of resources, obligations, authority, insecurity and role conditions.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Positions alter distributions of exposure to events/strains, their meaning and access to mediating resources. Turner et al. show measured exposure differences account for substantial portions of observed mental-health differences across several status groupings.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
Different frequency, duration and configuration of concrete stressors and mediating resources for the actor.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
Population-level elevated exposure probability must still be resolved into the particular actor’s actual conditions/events. `GROUP_EXPOSURE_RATE != ACTOR_EXPOSURE`.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Meaning, coping, support, action and prior history modify realized impact after exposure; some structural conditions constrain available responses.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
None directly from status. Only realized stress process may alter distress, mastery, self-concept or other outcomes.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`POSITION/RELATION → CONDITION/EVENT DISTRIBUTION → TRAJECTORY/CUMULATIVE_PROCESS`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Institution/occupation/household/community/social stratification.

**RECIPROCITY**  
Person/action may affect later status/occupation and hence later exposure; this is separately supported in the work-selection corpus.

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Historical period, institution, role regime, labor market, resources, network and nonmodal routes.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Absolutely no direct demographic or class psychology. Turner et al.’s group differences are evidence about exposure distribution, not permission to instantiate an actor’s mental state from sex, age, marital or occupational category.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
PRE-V3 positioned-subject empirical possibility can lawfully store population evidence; actor binding/realization must resolve it without converting probability to truth. The missing positive middle is the exact route from structured position to actor-specific exposure ecology.

**DISPOSITION**  
`REUSE` PRE-V3 empirical possibility; `MISSING` general actor-relative exposure transduction; `REJECT` any group-level trait prior.

## T4-M10 — RECIPROCAL SELECTION: DEVELOPED PERSON/ACTION CHANGES LATER WORK POSITION AND EXPOSURE

**SOURCE**  
Kohn & Schooler 1978 and 1982; Mortimer & Lorence 1979a/1979b; Andrisani & Nestel 1976; Staff & Mortimer 2024 as a modern selection-control comparator.

**CLAIM STATUS**  
Multiple longitudinal observational studies with reciprocal/path/regression models; modern fixed-effects study controls stable person selection but finds limited long-lag evidence over its irregular survey intervals.

**STRUCTURAL INPUT**  
A stratified field of jobs/roles with differing complexity, autonomy, rewards and stressors plus nonuniform selection/mobility gates.

**MEDIATING MECHANISM**  
Prior flexibility, self-directed orientation, competence, work values or control orientation affect occupational choice, performance, attainment, job molding or mobility; this changes later exposure to work conditions. The resulting conditions can then feed back into later psychological development.

**ACTOR-RELATIVE CONSEQUENCE**  
The actor seeks, qualifies for, receives, retains, modifies or exits particular jobs/roles and therefore changes the later opportunity/constraint/activity ecology.

**REALIZATION LAW**  
`PREFERENCE/CAPACITY != POSITION_CHANGE`. A selection path requires an action/gate/counterpart decision and an actual transition or alteration of job conditions.

**SUBJECT MEDIATION**  
Central: motive, preference, competence and action participate, but institutional gates and available jobs bound what action can realize.

**DEVELOPMENTAL TARGET**  
No direct target at this stage; reciprocity changes later exposures that may subsequently affect development.

**TEMPORAL FORM**  
`TRAJECTORY + TRANSITION + CUMULATIVE_FEEDBACK`.

**RELATIONAL SCALE**  
Actor ↔ organization/labor market/occupational structure.

**RECIPROCITY**  
This card is the reciprocal form itself:

```text
PRIOR PERSON/HISTORY
→ SELECTION / ACTION / PERFORMANCE
→ ACTUAL LATER POSITION OR JOB CONDITIONS
→ CHANGED RECURRENT EXPOSURE
→ LATER PERSON CHANGE IF EARNED
```

**BOUNDARY CONDITIONS**  
Opportunity structure, credentials, discrimination/gatekeeping, labor-market shocks, household constraints, health, mobility, prior history, and measurement lag.

**NEGATIVE CEILING**  
Do not explain unequal positions solely by personality or preferences. Person→position effects coexist with structural gates and do not erase constraint. Do not infer a feedback loop merely because variables correlate contemporaneously.

**RAWLSING MAPPING**  
Existing V3 history/action can supply prior person/action; PRE-V3 can describe actor-independent opportunities/gates; actor-relative realization can record transitions. Phase-0 does not prove the feedback connector that lets actor action lawfully reshape later structural position/exposure while preserving PRE-V3/V3 jurisdiction.

**DISPOSITION**  
`MISSING` or `GENERALIZE` explicit `PERSON_ACTION_TO_LATER_POSITION_EXPOSURE_FEEDBACK` seam semantics; architecture choice deferred to Vozhd + Lane R.

## 11. Card-level synthesis

The T4 corpus does **not** support a single `SOCIALIZATION` black box. It supports separable causal forms:

```text
WORK ORGANIZATION
→ RECURRENT TASK/AUTHORITY PRACTICE
→ PERSON CHANGE

DISCRETE DISRUPTION
→ PERSISTENT MATERIAL/ROLE STRAIN
→ SELF-CONCEPT/MASTERY CHANGE
→ DISTRESS

PERSISTENT CONDITION
→ CUMULATIVE STRAIN
→ PERSON CONSEQUENCE

NETWORK POSSIBILITY
→ REALIZED SUPPORT RELATION/USE
→ JUNCTURE-SPECIFIC MODIFICATION

SOURCE STRAIN
→ CROSS-DOMAIN ROLE/ACTIVITY RECONFIGURATION
→ SECONDARY STRAIN

PRIOR PERSON/ACTION
→ GATED SELECTION/ATTAINMENT
→ LATER POSITION/EXPOSURE
```

The consistent architectural lesson is a typed, actor-relative, temporally explicit connective middle between structural possibility and V3 uptake. T4 supplies evidence for that middle; it does not license T4 to create its owner.
