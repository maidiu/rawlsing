## 1. Primary conclusion

The strongest current answer is two-level:

> **Current actor awareness is a general person-level causal phenomenon. A rich, continuously refreshed present-conscious field is an interaction-time specialization required by PFF because every user turn immediately depends on what the actor presently feels, notices, interprets, intends and can express.**

Therefore:

`CURRENT_ACTOR_AWARENESS = SHARED_PERSON_PHENOMENON`.

But:

`FULL_HIGH_RESOLUTION_PFF_PRESENT_CONSCIOUS_FIELD != REQUIRED_FOR_EVERY_V3_TRANSITION`.

A psychologically mediated V3 event may require exact distinction between what is causally active and what the actor currently recognizes, without requiring a full narrated inner field.

`ADP017_ACTOR_ACCESS != COMPLETE_CURRENT_AWARENESS`.

This does **not** imply a new durable consciousness store.

## 3. The general person distinction that V3 must preserve when causally relevant

For psychologically mediated action, at least the following can produce different causal outcomes:

```text
CAUSALLY ACTIVE
        |
        +--> NOT PRESENT TO ACTOR
        |
        +--> FELT / EXPERIENCED BUT UNNAMED
        |
        +--> CURRENTLY ACCESSIBLE / ATTENDED
        |
        +--> RECOGNIZED / SELF-ATTRIBUTED
        |
        +--> EXPLAINED BY ACTOR, ACCURATELY OR INACCURATELY
```

These are not merely chat-expression distinctions.

They can change what a person does in life.

Example:

A person with causally active jealousy may:

- remain unaware and withdraw indirectly;
- feel unease without naming jealousy;
- consciously recognize jealousy and suppress it;
- recognize jealousy and apologize for it;
- misattribute jealousy to the partner's supposed dishonesty;
- consciously reject the jealousy as inconsistent with self-image and overcompensate.

Those are different developmental/action trajectories even before anyone renders dialogue.

Thus:

`SAME_DURABLE_D + SAME_EVENT + DIFFERENT_CURRENT_AWARENESS_RELATION -> POTENTIALLY_DIFFERENT_A`.

This is sufficient to make current awareness a general person-level causal concern.

## 4. But not every V3 event needs a conscious-field pass

The opposite overreach must also be rejected.

Many actor transitions may be:

- automatic;
- habitual;
- bodily/organismic;
- institutionally constrained;
- below conscious recognition;
- causally mediated without present phenomenological richness.

Therefore reject:

`EVERY_V3_EVENT -> FULL_CONSCIOUS_FIELD_OBJECT -> ACTION`.

The more disciplined rule is:

> Where an exact response/development rule depends on what the actor currently notices, recognizes, remembers, believes, doubts, intends, or consciously regulates, the architecture must preserve those distinctions. Where it does not, no universal inner-monologue computation is required.

`CURRENT_AWARENESS_IS_CONDITIONALLY_CAUSAL`, not a mandatory universal stage.

## 5. D/C/B/A under the cross-regime awareness model

### D — durable/deep person

Contains durable generated-person organization, including actor-inaccessible causes.

### C — standing actor-side self-organization

Contains relatively standing self-understanding, roles, beliefs, autobiographical orientation and recognized motives.

C is not what the actor happens to be thinking this instant.

### B — situated working configuration

Contains what is currently recruited/configured by this event/user/situation, including both hidden and conscious material.

### Present awareness — a current relation/cut through B and actor-accessible D/C

The present field is not a fifth durable band.

It is a **current awareness relation over situated person material**.

Some B material is hidden.

Some is felt but unnamed.

Some becomes focal/recognized.

Some C material is standing but irrelevant and therefore absent from current awareness.

Thus:

`PRESENT_AWARENESS != C`.

`PRESENT_AWARENESS != B`.

`PRESENT_AWARENESS = CURRENT_RELATION_TO_A_SUBSET/PROJECTION_OF_SITUATED_PERSON_MATERIAL` is a useful conceptual approximation, not architecture.

### A — enacted/observable manifestation

What the actor does/says can depend on present awareness but is not a direct readout of it.

## 7. Why PFF needs higher temporal resolution

In user interaction, what is present to the actor can change multiple times within a short exchange:

- a joke initially feels harmless;
- a second cue makes flirtation plausible;
- the actor notices their own excitement;
- noticing excitement triggers shame;
- shame makes the user's attention feel exposing;
- the actor decides not to disclose;
- their own teasing reply surprises them;
- next turn they consciously wonder whether they are attracted.

The durable person may remain unchanged through several of these turns.

Therefore PFF needs a fast recursive account of:

- focality;
- peripheral/fringe awareness;
- felt-but-unnamed experience;
- current social hypotheses;
- recognized motive;
- current self-interpretation;
- current intention;
- suppression/intrusion;
- awareness changes caused by own output.

This is why PFF cannot be replaced by ADP-017 plus a renderer.

## 8. Periodic V3 may need sparse event-time awareness rather than a persistent field

A likely conceptual economy emerges:

### 8.1 Durable V3 state

V3 persists:

- memory/accessibility where owned;
- actor epistemic stance where owned;
- self-model and relevant durable developmental organization where later authority places it.

### 8.2 Event-time awareness

During a response transaction, the engine may need to establish temporary causal facts such as:

- `actor currently recognizes X`;
- `actor currently notices contradiction Y`;
- `memory M is consciously recalled in this event`;
- `affect F is felt but not causally understood`;
- `actor intentionally suppresses disclosure/action Z`.

Those need not become durable standing state merely because they occurred.

### 8.3 Replay/provenance implication

If a transient awareness distinction materially affects an admitted causal action, replay must be able to reproduce or justify that distinction.

This may require event/transition evidence or receipts later, but **does not imply durable consciousness-state storage**.

`CAUSALLY_RELEVANT_TRANSIENT_STATE_MUST_BE_REPLAYABLE`

without:

`CAUSALLY_RELEVANT_TRANSIENT_STATE_MUST_BE_PERMANENT_PERSON_STATE`.

This is an architecture burden, not an adopted representation.

## 9. Awareness can itself become a trigger

The original activation matrix established that recognition is not passive readout.

Examples:

- `I am jealous` -> shame or corrective self-control;
- `I am afraid` -> relief, panic, or deliberate approach;
- `I actually want this` -> conflict with standing self-model;
- `this reminds me of my father` -> new interpretation of present relationship;
- `I think she can see through me` -> defensive escalation;
- `I was cruel` -> guilt and repair attempt.

Thus in both regimes:

`RECOGNITION(t) -> NEW_CAUSAL_INPUT_TO_SAME_PERSON(t+)`.

This is especially visible in chat, but it is also plausible in V3 developmental episodes.

## 10. Naming is not neutral

Putting experience into a category or self-explanation can reorganize response.

Examples:

- vague unease named as jealousy;
- exhaustion interpreted as depression-like hopelessness versus temporary stress;
- attraction named as admiration;
- humiliation named as injustice;
- grief interpreted as weakness or as love continuing after loss.

The label may be accurate, partial or wrong.

Therefore:

`CURRENT_EXPERIENCE != ACTOR_LABEL`.

`ACTOR_LABEL != MACHINE_CAUSAL_ACCOUNT`.

`ACTOR_LABEL_CAN_STILL_CAUSE_LATER_RESPONSE`.

This gives periodic V3 a route by which self-interpretation can affect development without making machine truth actor truth.

## 11. Defense and awareness are mutually shaping

The defense second pass implies:

- durable mediator can shape what enters awareness;
- present awareness can recruit new defense;
- successful suppression can leave hidden material active;
- defensive reinterpretation can produce sincerely false causal self-explanation;
- defense failure can reveal previously hidden material;
- being seen by another person can abruptly change awareness.

Therefore no architecture should assume:

`B -> awareness` is a transparent projection.

It is itself person-specific and historically mediated.

## 12. Attention / focality must remain distinct from accessibility

A durable actor can be able to know/recall something without thinking about it now.

Thus:

`V3_ACCESSIBLE != CURRENTLY_FOCAL`.

Likewise:

`CURRENTLY_FOCAL != MOST_CAUSALLY_POWERFUL`.

A hidden mediator may strongly shape response while a trivial conscious detail occupies attention.

And:

`CURRENTLY_FOCAL != V2_SALIENCE`.

V2 expression salience concerns what lawful information is selected/prioritized for expression; present actor attention is a first-person/state phenomenon.

They may correlate but must not be merged.

---

## 13. Fringe and felt-but-unnamed states are not decorative realism

They matter because they allow psychologically lawful transitions that would otherwise require abrupt hidden->explicit jumps.

Examples:

- `something about this bothers me`;
- `I feel exposed but I don't know why`;
- vague familiarity preceding explicit recall;
- bodily excitement before attraction recognition;
- low-level suspicion before a concrete hypothesis;
- sadness before the actor links it to an anniversary or relationship loss.

These intermediate phenomenal conditions can:

- bias attention;
- alter action;
- recruit further memory;
- become consciously named later;
- disappear without ever being explained.

A later architecture must preserve the phenomenon, not necessarily a fixed fringe-state enum.

---

## 14. Conscious contradiction must remain lawful in both regimes

A person can currently know/feel:

- love and resentment;
- attraction and fear;
- certainty in one proposition and uncertainty in another;
- desire to leave and desire to repair;
- pride and shame;
- relief and grief.

The field must not produce a single winner merely because the actor is conscious of multiple states.

CAN-008's preservation of simultaneous causal streams and CAN-011's preservation of contradictory durable middle state support this general noncollapse.

`CONSCIOUSNESS != GLOBAL_RESOLUTION`.

## 15. Current awareness is not always language-like

Especially for periodic V3, architecture must not assume that actor awareness has to be represented as prose or an inner monologue.

A causal distinction such as:

- consciously afraid;
- recognizes the person as familiar;
- notices hostility;
- intends not to reveal weakness;

can be represented semantically without generating internal narrative text.

For PFF, the renderer may eventually need language-relevant actor content, but even there:

`INTERNAL_AWARENESS != PREWRITTEN_RESPONSE_TEXT`.

This protects against turning consciousness into prompt prose.

## 16. Present awareness versus standing C

A crucial self-model distinction:

An actor can presently recognize something that has not yet become integrated into their ordinary self-understanding.

Example:

> `I think I am actually jealous.`

Current awareness changes immediately.

Standing C may remain:

> `I'm not a jealous person; this is weird.`

Repeated/admitted development may later revise C.

Therefore:

`CURRENT_RECOGNITION != STANDING_SELF_MODEL_UPDATE`.

Conversely C can shape what is difficult to recognize:

> `I am independent` can make dependency states harder to name or accept.

Thus:

`C <-> CURRENT_AWARENESS` is recursive, not containment.

## 17. PFF/V2 boundary

The conscious-field examination must not silently absorb downstream expression jurisdiction.

Conceptually:

### Shared/current-person side

- what is felt;
- what is focal/peripheral;
- what is recognized;
- what the actor thinks the cause is;
- what they intend;
- what they suppress;
- what they think the user sees.

### Expression side

- what actor-safe material is selected for saying;
- how it is phrased;
- register/style;
- final linguistic realization.

Thus:

`PRESENT_AWARENESS != EXPRESSION_PLAN`.

`EXPRESSION_PLAN != RENDERED_OUTPUT`.

The exact repartition among future PFF and current V2 authorities remains architecture/HQ work.

## 18. Hidden-cause firewall remains intact

A machine may know:

`humiliation history -> defensive mediator -> irritation`.

The actor may know only:

`I feel irritated.`

Or:

`I think the user is being condescending.`

The current awareness representation must therefore be derived from actor-side access/interpretation, not simply expose the machine causal graph.

`MACHINE_CAUSAL_LINEAGE != CURRENT_ACTOR_AWARENESS`.

If the actor later recognizes the hidden relation, that recognition must itself be causally generated.

## 19. Example court — periodic V3 decision with hidden versus recognized motive

Generated actor is offered a prestigious job far from family.

D:
- status/competence striving;
- attachment/family striving;
- prior abandonment sensitivity;
- self-model: ambitious and independent.

B_EVENT:
- excitement;
- loss threat;
- attachment conflict;
- prestige opportunity.

Case 1 — hidden attachment fear:
- actor consciously emphasizes practical objections;
- turns job down;
- later explains decision as finances/logistics.

Case 2 — recognized attachment fear:
- actor knows separation is the real concern;
- negotiates remote work or discusses conflict with family.

Case 3 — attachment fear recognized but rejected by self-model:
- actor accepts job partly to prove independence.

Same event and durable person, different awareness relation, different action and future consequences.

This is why present awareness can matter to V3 development.

`USER_INTERPRETATION != ACTOR_SELF_KNOWLEDGE`.
