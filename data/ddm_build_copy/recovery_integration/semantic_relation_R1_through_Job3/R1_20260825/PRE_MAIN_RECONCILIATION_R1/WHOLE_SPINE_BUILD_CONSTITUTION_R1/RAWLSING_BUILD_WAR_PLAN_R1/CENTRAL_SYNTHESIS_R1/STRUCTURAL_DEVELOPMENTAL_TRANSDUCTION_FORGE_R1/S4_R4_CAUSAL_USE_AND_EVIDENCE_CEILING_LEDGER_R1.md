# S4 R4 — CAUSAL-USE AND EVIDENCE-CEILING LEDGER R1

STATUS: `S4_CAUSAL_USE_COURT_COMPLETE__CALIBRATION_SEPARATED_FROM_SOURCE_SUPPORT`

## 0. Governing distinction

A source may support one or more of the statuses below. No status automatically upgrades another.

```text
SOURCE SUPPORTS CONCEPTUAL DISTINCTION
SOURCE SUPPORTS MECHANISM PLAUSIBILITY
SOURCE SUPPORTS OBSERVATIONAL ASSOCIATION
SOURCE SUPPORTS STRONGER CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION
SOURCE SUPPORTS CALIBRATION
SOURCE DOES NOT LICENSE EXECUTABLE ACTOR RULE WITHOUT ADDITIONAL AUTHORITY
```

The last status is compatible with any of the first five. Even a randomized field experiment does not, by itself, establish a universal production rule, transport scope, generated-world implementation, or project-current authority.

## 1. Identification ladder

| Code | Meaning | What it can license for S4 | What it cannot license |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CD` | conceptual distinction | schema/firewall/rule-shape distinction | actor fact, causal coefficient, calibration |
| `MP` | mechanism plausibility | candidate typed mediator and required inputs | execution, prevalence, effect magnitude |
| `OA` | observational association | empirical relevance under observed scope; can motivate rule testing | unconfounded actor-level causal law |
| `LO` | longitudinal observational | temporal ordering/reciprocity evidence stronger than cross-section | randomized-treatment interpretation; universal lag/effect |
| `QH` | qualitative/historical process evidence | concrete pathway, counterpart, institutional and sequence decomposition | population prevalence or transportable effect size |
| `SCI` | stronger causal identification | bounded manipulated/identified treatment→measured outcome relation | universal portability, actor psychology beyond measured outcome, production calibration |
| `CAL` | source supplies usable calibration candidate under exact scope | candidate numeric parameter only after separate calibration admission | global/default coefficient, scope-free portability |
| `CR` | constitutive/deontic rule evidence | current rule+state+position may establish entitlement/duty/authority/eligibility | performance, transfer, treatment, use, psychological response |

## 3. Causal-use court by P class

### P1 — MATERIAL_ACCESS_DEPENDENCY

**Source support:** strong conceptual convergence and substantial observational/historical evidence from Wright, Elder, SRT, Marxist work and life-course materials.

**Causal identification:** usually not sufficient for a portable empirical coefficient. Exact constitutive property/benefit/service rules can nevertheless establish objective entitlements, dependencies or restrictions when those consequences follow by rule semantics.

**Permitted use:**

```text
CURRENT PROPERTY / RESOURCE / BENEFIT / SERVICE RULE
+ EXACT ACTOR POSITION / ELIGIBILITY / COUNTERPART
+ CONTEXT / TIME
-> OBJECTIVE ACCESS / ENTITLEMENT / DEPENDENCY / RESTRICTION FACT
```

where the rule is separately governed.

**Ceiling:** actual receipt, transfer, use, deprivation experience or psychological consequence requires further evidence/owner.

### P2 — DIRECTED_AUTHORITY_OBLIGATION_SANCTION

**Source support:** Wright, Kohn/Schooler, Merton, Dreier and household/care materials strongly support distinctions among position, authority, obligation and realized exercise.

**Causal identification:** primarily conceptual/observational. Operative organizational/legal rule can establish authority/duty constitutively.

**Ceiling:** authority title does not establish exercise; obligation does not establish compliance; supervision does not establish subjective control/mastery.

### P3 — ORGANIZED_ACTIVITY_TIME

**Source support:** especially strong empirical relevance from Kohn/Schooler, Mortimer, Elder and qualitative SRT work; theoretical reinforcement from activity traditions and Sève.

**Causal identification:** recurrent objective task/time conditions are measurable and longitudinally associated with downstream outcomes, but the corpus does not provide a portable production coefficient from task condition to person state.

**Ceiling:** employment/occupation/school membership does not establish actual task, schedule, recurrence or intensity. Recurrent facts require interval/currentness evidence under R4.

### P4 — INSTITUTIONAL_GATE_TREATMENT

**Source support:** strongest bounded causal identification in the corpus because of randomized/matched field experiments and laboratory/audit work.

**Causal identification:** `SCI` for specific presented cues, gate contexts and measured decisions. This supports the **rule shape**:

```text
PRESENTED / RECOGNIZED CUE OR RECORD
+ ACTUAL GATE ENCOUNTER
+ EVALUATION PROCESS
-> OBSERVED DECISION / TREATMENT
```

not:

```text
ATTRIBUTE -> TREATMENT
```

**Ceiling:** experimental average effect does not automatically become a generated-person prior or universal callback coefficient. Transport, institutional mapping, prevalence and calibration require separate authority.

### P5 — COUNTERPART_CHANNEL_RELATION

**Source support:** broad theoretical/observational support from Feld, Granovetter, Burt, Coleman, Dreier, House-derived T4 distinctions, Elder linked lives and SRT support/referral cases.

**Causal identification:** generally mechanism plausibility/observational, with some directly observed transactions.

**Ceiling:** topology/tie/focus/channel does not prove transfer, support, information receipt, influence or benefit. Actual transaction needs a type-compatible witness.

## 4. Calibration court

The corpus contains empirical coefficients, regression paths, group differences, effect estimates, callback rates and qualitative frequencies. These are **evidence objects**, not automatically production parameters.

Binding S4 law:

```text
EMPIRICAL EFFECT ESTIMATE
!= CALIBRATION AUTHORITY
!= GENERATED-WORLD PARAMETER
!= ACTOR-SPECIFIC CONSEQUENCE FACT
```

A numeric mechanism rule requires a separate calibration record identifying at minimum:

```text
CALIBRATION_ID
RULE_ID / VERSION
SOURCE_ESTIMATE_REF(S)
TARGET_POPULATION / WORLD / INSTITUTION
TRANSPORT_ASSUMPTIONS
MEASUREMENT_MAPPING
PARAMETER_VALUE / DISTRIBUTION
UNCERTAINTY
VALID_TIME / HISTORICAL SCOPE
VALIDATION EVIDENCE
COMPETENT_CALIBRATION_AUTHORITY
SUPERSESSION / CURRENTNESS
```

Absent such authority, the source can still license a qualitative/constitutive rule shape where otherwise governed, but cannot supply an invented numeric effect.

**S4 calibration verdict:** `GENERAL_PRODUCTION_CALIBRATION_NOT_LICENSED_BY_CORPUS`.

## 5. Portability court

Every trace must preserve the difference between identifying a mechanism in one setting and transporting it elsewhere.

Required portability statuses:

- `SOURCE_SCOPE_ONLY`
- `MECHANISM_GENERALIZATION_PLAUSIBLE__RULE_SCOPE_SEPARATELY_REQUIRED`
- `INSTITUTION_SPECIFIC`
- `HISTORICAL_CONFIGURATION_SPECIFIC`
- `POPULATION/COHORT_SPECIFIC`
- `MEASUREMENT_SPECIFIC`
- `TRANSPORT_VALIDATED` only under separate evidence/authority.

A scope mismatch makes a source-based rule **inapplicable/unauthorized**, not evidence that the opposite consequence is true.

## 6. Evidence absence / negation court

R4's witness-polarity law controls every source-backed application:

```text
MISSING POSITIVE EVIDENCE
EXPIRED EVIDENCE
SUPERSEDED EVIDENCE
OUT-OF-SCOPE EVIDENCE
INSUFFICIENT RECURRENCE COVERAGE
```

can yield `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_PROVEN_REALIZED`, never a negative actor fact by absence.

Examples:

```text
NO RECORDED TRANSFER != PROVED NONTRANSFER
NO CALLBACK RECORD != DENIAL
NO CONTACT OBSERVATION != NO CHANNEL
NO TASK OBSERVATION != NO TASK
```

## 7. Final causal-use verdict

1. **Every P1-P5 class is source-grounded as a mechanism family.**
2. **P4 has the strongest bounded causal-identification evidence.**
3. **P1/P2/P4 constitutive rule cases can be strong without experimental evidence when a competent operative rule itself makes the objective consequence true.**
4. **P3 and most P5 mechanisms are strongly motivated by longitudinal/observational/process evidence but do not supply portable actor-effect coefficients.**
5. **Theoretical sources are architecturally valuable primarily for decomposition, necessary distinctions, mediating variables and negative ceilings.**
6. **The corpus alone does not decide production calibration.**
7. **No source citation or effect estimate licenses an executable actor rule without separate rule/currentness/causal-use authority.**

`CAUSAL_USE_TERMINAL = SOURCE_GROUNDS_RULE_SHAPES_AND_CEILINGS__EXECUTABLE_RULE_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_SEPARATE`.
