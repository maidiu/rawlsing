## Terminal questions

### A. What remains of `class` after executable decomposition?

A **derived analyst map** of patterned relation bundles. Runtime causality resides in the underlying typed relations. Keep `class` for source alignment, reporting, aggregate comparison and derived queries; do not use it to drive a person.

### B. Which property/dependency mechanisms strengthen D1?

- separation from sufficient means of production -> wage/income dependence;
- ownership/control -> hiring, investment/disinvestment and claims on surplus;
- unequal productive assets -> differential exit/market strategies;
- exploitation -> oppression + appropriation + productive interdependence with an exact counterpart;
- household pooling/control -> member-specific resource/dependency effects.

### C. Which authority mechanisms strengthen D1?

- policy/coordination decision rights;
- task/procedure/pace assignment;
- promotion/pay/warning/suspension/firing powers;
- effective enforceability and subordinate counterpart;
- upward approval/subordination constraints;
- organization control distinguished from authority activity and from title.

### D. Does Wright add temporal requirements to D2?

**YES, but not a new temporal primitive.** He requires histories of positions, career transitions, promotions, asset acquisition/loss, credential devaluation, changing job matches, household transitions and separation of actor career change from cohort/world structural change. Existing interval/history/transition rebinding is the correct grain.

### E. What collective/structural feedback supports D3?

Class structure -> resource/capacity terrain -> contingent party/union/organization/coalition formation -> realized action -> employer/state/institution response -> success/failure/partial rule or resource change -> altered later structural terrain. Wright's `nonreformist reform` logic is a particularly clear example of action changing the conditions of subsequent action.

### F. Do contradictory locations require any new primitive?

**NO.** They demonstrate the need for simultaneous typed relations. A derived `CONTRADICTORY_RELATION_BUNDLE` query is sufficient and must point back to the underlying relations.

### G. Is D4 needed?
