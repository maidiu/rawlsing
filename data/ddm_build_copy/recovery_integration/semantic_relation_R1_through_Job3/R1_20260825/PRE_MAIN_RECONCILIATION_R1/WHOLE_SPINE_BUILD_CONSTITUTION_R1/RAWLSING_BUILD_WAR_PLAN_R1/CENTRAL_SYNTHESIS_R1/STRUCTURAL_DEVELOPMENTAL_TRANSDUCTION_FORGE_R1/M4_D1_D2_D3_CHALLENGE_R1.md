## D1 attack 1 — Does Holzkamp actually require a mediated actor-relative layer?

**YES. Strongly.**

### Primary-source basis

- Ch. 5 pp. 191–193: objective societal relations are not psychic facts; individual-science mediation categories are required between objective material-economic and psychic determinations.
- Ch. 5 pp. 195–198: individuals relate to societal totality through historically specific positions, not directly.
- Ch. 6 pp. 233–238: objective societal meanings confront individuals as action possibilities, not determinations.
- Ch. 7 pp. 358–367: objective societal meaning structures differentiate into life-situation/position-specific `Infrastrukturen` and `Bedeutungskonstellationen`.

### Verdict

`D1_NECESSITY_SUPPORTED`.

Rawlsing needs a positive actor-relative qualification/derivation layer if it is to explain why a particular actor faces these possibilities/constraints rather than merely asserting that a general structural relation exists.

---

## D1 attack 2 — Does Holzkamp supply a complete typed social-ecology derivation registry?

**NO.**

At pp. 360–367 Holzkamp sketches work/nonwork/institutional domains, then explicitly says the exact possibilities, restrictions and ideological references in particular positions/institutions must be clarified through single-theory/current-empirical investigation beyond the book.

The source supplies:

- a categorical level distinction;
- position/life-situation specification;
- the need to identify concrete action/thought possibilities;
- examples of domains and relations.

It does **not** supply a complete registry for every resource, network, eligibility, discrimination, care, gate, support or time mechanism.

### Verdict

`D1_NOT_PROVED_AS_HOLZKAMP_COMPLETE_SOCIOLOGY_ENGINE`.

The frozen typed families remain a cross-lane Rawlsing synthesis, not “Holzkamp’s list.”

### Verdict

= ACTOR-RELATIVE OBJECTIVE SOCIAL POSSIBILITY / CONSTRAINT / RELATION

# II. D2 COURT

## Candidate

`INTERVAL_RECURRENCE_AND_TRANSITION_REBINDING`

Frozen candidate role: preserve interval conditions/relations, recurrent activity, transitions, after-state rebinding and typed history rather than flattening everything into events.

---

## D2 attack 1 — Does Holzkamp require persistent conditions/relations rather than event-only causation?

**YES.**

The entire Ch. 5–7 grammar concerns historically persistent social relations, positions, dependencies, possibility spaces and power relations. These are not naturally reducible to event tokens.

### Verdict

`INTERVAL_SEMANTICS_SUPPORTED`.

---

## D2 attack 2 — Does Holzkamp require recurrence?

**YES, but not as a special storage ontology.**

Restrictive/generalized alternatives arise anew in concrete threat/dependency situations. Ch. 7 describes ongoing reproduction, repeated instrumental relations, chronic threat fixation and practical association. Ch. 8 treats individual history as a process whose earlier results persist into later current states.

### Verdict

`RECURRENT_REQUALIFICATION_SUPPORTED`.

---

## D2 attack 3 — Does prior realized history affect later possibility?

**YES. Strongly.**

At pp. 367–369 prior realizations/nonrealizations of meaning-consistent possibilities shape the personal functional basis, so actors can lag behind objective possibilities or misjudge them. At pp. 377–378 restrictive instrumental action can narrow later cooperative possibility. Ch. 8 pp. 420–421 makes present action-capacity historically deep, containing development, stagnation and regression.

### Verdict

`HISTORY_SENSITIVE_REBINDING_SUPPORTED`.

---

## D2 attack 4 — Does Holzkamp supply a transition concept that changes later ecology?

**PARTLY.**

Ch. 8 pp. 473–493 reconstructs practical movement from the household/support frame into multiple societal/institutional centers. New institutions have different objective meaning constellations, requirements, supports/dependencies and possible premises. This is semantically close to “transition→ecology rebinding.”

But Holzkamp explicitly says exact chronology and concrete institutional-developmental effects require current empirical research.

### Verdict

`TRANSITION_REBINDING_SEMANTICS_SUPPORTED__ATOMIC_GENERIC_TRANSITION_RULE_NOT_SOURCE_PROVED`.

---

## D2 attack 5 — Does Holzkamp justify a fixed developmental transition schedule?

**NO. Explicitly rejected.**

At pp. 417–425 Holzkamp rejects biologically preprogrammed universal human developmental phase sequences and treats his reconstruction as developmental-logical (`entwicklungslogisch`), not an observed universal chronology.

### Verdict

`NO_STAGE_TABLE`.

---

## D2 attack 6 — Is a new temporal carrier demanded?

**NO.**

Nothing in the full book defeats the frozen synthesis court’s strategy of generalizing existing condition/relation/history/binding carriers and preserving temporal semantics compositionally.

### D2 final verdict

`SURVIVES_AND_GAINS_SUBSTANTIAL_SOURCE_SUPPORT__NO_NEW_HOLZKAMP_TEMPORAL_OBJECT_REQUIRED`.

### What M4 adds to D2

D2 should preserve at least:

```text
active interval of objective condition/relation
repeated/recurrent action context
history of prior realization/nonrealization
changed institution/context after transition
requalification of actor-relative objective ecology
later V3 subject state derived separately
```

The source especially strengthens the **history-sensitive requalification** aspect.

### Answer F

**Does D2 gain anything substantial?** `YES`.

It gains primary-source support for:

- biographical conditioning of later possibility;
- recurrence of situated alternatives;
- transition between materially distinct social centers;
- nonmonotonic development/stagnation/regression;
- the need to recalculate possibilities after context/institution changes.

It does **not** gain a source mandate for a new code module or universal transition stage system.

## D3 attack 1 — Does Holzkamp actually license reciprocal subject→social-world direction?

### Verdict

`RECIPROCAL_DIRECTION_SOURCE_SUPPORTED`.

---

## D3 attack 2 — Can individual intention directly change structure?

**NO.**

Holzkamp’s account is practical and relational. Generalized action-capacity requires real possibilities of cooperation/association, means, alliances and counter-power. The social world is objective and supra-individual.

### Verdict

`DIRECT_WILL_TO_WORLD_UPDATE_REJECTED`.

## D3 attack 3 — Is collective organization automatically produced by shared interest?

**NO.**

Objective common interest and action possibility remain possibilities. The actor must have grounds to participate; association must actually occur; organization/resources must be adequate.

### Verdict

`SHARED_INTEREST != ORGANIZATION`.

## D3 attack 4 — Does organized action guarantee success?

**NO.**

The book has no universal institutional response or success law. Concrete power relations and the practical means/alliances matter.

### Verdict

`ORGANIZATION != EFFECT`.
