## 2. Executive source verdict

The full book does not sustain a useful `CLASS -> PERSONALITY` primitive. Its mature empirical program decomposes the relationship:

```text
SOCIAL / CLASS / ORGANIZATIONAL POSITION
-> CONCRETE ACTOR-SPECIFIC JOB CONDITIONS
   {SUBSTANTIVE COMPLEXITY,
    CLOSENESS OF SUPERVISION,
    ROUTINIZATION,
    PROTECTIONS/RISKS,
    TIME/PHYSICAL PRESSURES,
    INCOME}
-> ACTUAL REQUIREMENTS / OPPORTUNITIES FOR
   INITIATIVE, THOUGHT, JUDGMENT, PROBLEM SOLVING, CONTROL
-> RECURRENT WORK ACTIVITY / PERSISTENT EXPOSURE
-> DISTINCT PERSON OUTCOMES
-> DELAYED PERSON-DEPENDENT ACTION/SELECTION WHERE SUPPORTED
-> EXTERNAL ORGANIZATIONAL/LABOR-MARKET GATE
-> LATER JOB CONDITION
```

This is unusually direct empirical support for the positive structural-developmental seam, but it remains observational and incomplete at the micro-transition level.

## 3. Terminal question A — What exact job conditions do the work?

**Answer:** The strongest condition is **substantive complexity**, defined by the actual task demands for thought and independent judgment under complex/ill-defined contingencies. The other central occupational-self-direction components are **closeness of supervision** and **routinization/predictability**. The book also identifies distinct effects of job protections/security, responsibility outside one's control, time pressure, hours, dirtiness/heaviness, income, hierarchical position, ownership and bureaucratization.

The conditions are heterogeneous and must not be collapsed into `job quality` or `occupational status`.

Verdict: `TYPED_FIRST_ORDER_JOB_CONDITIONS_REQUIRED`.

## 4. Terminal question B — What actual recurrent activities mediate them?

**Answer:** For substantively complex work, Appendix B and Chapters 5-6 identify repeated performance involving analysis, synthesis, coordination, problem solving, evaluation of alternatives, independent judgment under unpredictable contingencies, negotiation/instruction/mentoring, precision adjustment and other nonmechanical decisions.

For supervision, the recurrent experience is constrained decision authority, instructions/obedience, pace control and limits on disagreement. For routinization, it is repeated/predictable task execution with fewer occasions for choice among alternative approaches.

The exact daily recurrence history was not directly logged, so `RECURRENT_EXERCISE` is strongly supported as the theoretical/measurement middle but not given a universal dose-response coefficient.

Verdict: `PASS__RECURRENT_ACTIVITY_SEAM_REQUIRED`.

## 5. Terminal question C — How strong is job -> person evidence?

**Answer:** `STRONG_OBSERVATIONAL`, strongest for substantive complexity/occupational self-direction and ideational flexibility/self-directed orientation. Chapter 5's preferred models estimate current substantive complexity -> current ideational flexibility around `.17-.18`; the direct ten-year cross-lag from 1964 complexity to 1974 flexibility is only about `.05` and nonsignificant, implying an ongoing/current effect rather than a simple delayed event.

Broad Chapter 6-7 models show work -> person effects across several values/orientations and distress-related outcomes, but effects vary by condition and target.

This is longitudinal SEM, not randomized treatment identification.

Verdict: `STRONG_OBSERVATIONAL__NOT_RANDOMIZED__TARGET_SPECIFIC`.

## 6. Terminal question D — How strong is person -> job evidence?

**Answer:** Strongest for prior ideational flexibility -> later substantive complexity, approximately `.41-.45` in preferred Chapter 5 formulations. Self-directedness and selected orientations also predict later occupational self-direction. Several other reverse paths are weak or null: distress, anxiety, trustfulness and own valuation of self-direction do not show equivalent feedback.

The source does not directly observe the intervening job-search, performance, promotion, employer-selection, transfer, exit or job-redesign process.

Verdict: `PASS__LAGGED_PERSON_TO_JOB_ASSOCIATION__EXTERNAL_GATE_UNOBSERVED_BUT_REQUIRED`.

## 13. Key nulls and negative ceilings returned

- longitudinal SEM coefficients are not randomized causal effects;
- the 10-year two-wave design does not identify onset/dose/threshold/habituation;
- the broad model cannot freely estimate every contemporaneous and lagged path;
- substantive-complexity -> flexibility is principally contemporaneous/ongoing, not a clean ten-year lag;
- routinization measurement is weaker because a second indicator cross-loaded;
- household supervision analogue failed;
- ideational flexibility is not the universal mediator of work effects;
- several person -> work paths are null/weak;
- women lack equivalent longitudinal baseline measurement;
- cross-national self-conception/distress findings are less universal;
- occupation-level proxies attenuate actor-specific work-content relationships;
- parent/ethnic/historical category does not directly transmit traits;
- objective job condition and subjective appraisal remain distinct;
- observational evidence does not by itself prove effects of deliberate job redesign.

## 15. Final worker finding

The source provides unusually strong empirical support for the exact conceptual move Rawlsing's frozen seam is trying to make: replace a remote social-category -> person shortcut with a typed chain through actual work conditions and recurrent activity, then preserve reciprocal actor/world feedback through externally realized gates.

It also shows why the chain must remain disciplined: occupation titles are lossy; self-direction is composite; recurrence quality matters; reciprocal paths are selective; and the longitudinal statistical design does not expose every causal transition.
