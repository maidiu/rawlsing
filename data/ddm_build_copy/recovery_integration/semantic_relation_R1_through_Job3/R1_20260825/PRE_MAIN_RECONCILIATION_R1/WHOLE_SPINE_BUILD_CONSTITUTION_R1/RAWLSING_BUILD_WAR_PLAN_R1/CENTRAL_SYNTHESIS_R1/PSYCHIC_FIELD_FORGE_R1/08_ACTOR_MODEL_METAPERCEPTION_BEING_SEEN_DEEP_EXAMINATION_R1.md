# PSYCHIC FIELD FORGE R1 — ACTOR MODEL / METAPERCEPTION / BEING-SEEN DEEP EXAMINATION R1

**Status:** `PRE_ARCHITECTURE_CONCEPT_EXAMINATION__BANKED__MUST_FACTOR__NOT_ARCHITECTURE_ADOPTION__NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY`

## 0. Purpose

This file deeply examines PFF frontier pocket 6, `actor model of interlocutor / being seen`, together with its inseparable epistemic, dyadic, self-model, defense, salience, anticipation, and V3/V2 boundary questions.

The central questions are:

1. What minimum social-modeling capacity must PFF preserve for a generated actor to participate in psychologically realistic interaction?
2. How can an actor be causally affected by what they think another person thinks, wants, notices, or sees in them without converting those inferences into objective truth?
3. How much recursive theory-of-mind is actually necessary before it becomes unnecessary or duplicative machinery?
4. Which of these phenomena are already V3 actor epistemic state, which are live PFF social readings, and which are merely machine-side candidate interpretations?

Current conclusions:

`ACTOR_MODEL_OF_INTERLOCUTOR = MUST_FACTOR`

`METAPERCEPTION / BEING_SEEN = MUST_FACTOR`

`METAPERCEPTION_TRUTH = NOT_REQUIRED_FOR_CAUSAL_EFFECT`

`FULL_HIDDEN_MODEL_OF_INTERLOCUTOR = NOT_REQUIRED`

`PERSISTENT_UNBOUNDED_RECURSIVE_THEORY_OF_MIND = NOT_JUSTIFIED`

`FIRST_ORDER_SOCIAL_READING + PERSONAL_SECOND_ORDER_METAPERCEPTION = CORE PHENOMENAL REQUIREMENTS`

`HIGHER_ORDER_RECURSION = CONTEXTUAL / ON_DEMAND / NOT YET A PERSISTENT STATE REQUIREMENT`

## 1. Governing Rawlsing boundaries

ADP-017 already establishes that objective truth does not automatically create actor belief, uncertainty, or claim permission; generated belief requires exact causal authority; analyst-side misrecognition may be recognized only over an independently generated actor belief/interpretation; and analyst truth does not become actor meta-knowledge.

Therefore:

`B_OBJECTIVE_MENTAL_STATE != A_BELIEF_ABOUT_B`

`A_BELIEF_ABOUT_B != A_CURRENT_SOCIAL_READING_OF_B`

`A_CURRENT_SOCIAL_READING != MACHINE_CANDIDATE_INTERPRETATION`

`MACHINE_CANDIDATE_INTERPRETATION != OBJECTIVE_B_INTENTION`

`A_METAPERCEPTION_OF_B_VIEWING_A != B_ACTUAL_VIEW_OF_A`

`MISRECOGNITION_CAN_EXIST_WITHOUT_ACTOR_KNOWING_IT_IS MISRECOGNITION`

Historical V2 relationship-expression law additionally requires perspective preservation and forbids reciprocity inference and objective synthesis from one actor's view.

This examination therefore treats social inference as actor-local and uncertainty-bearing unless exact upstream authority establishes more.

## 2. First major distinction: an actor model is not a miniature copy of the interlocutor

PFF does not need to construct a complete hidden simulation of B.

The psychologically relevant requirement is much narrower:

> A must be able to form situation-specific expectations and hypotheses about those aspects of B that matter to A's current recruitment, action selection, self-presentation, and anticipation.

Examples include:

- what A thinks B currently wants;
- what A thinks B intends by the current move;
- what A thinks B feels toward A;
- what A thinks B knows or has noticed;
- what A thinks B expects A to do;
- what A thinks B is likely to do next;
- what A thinks B values or disapproves of in A;
- whether A thinks B is safe, dangerous, attracted, rejecting, judging, admiring, contemptuous, dependent, manipulative, vulnerable, etc.;
- what A thinks B can see through in A.

This is a **sparse relevance-bound social model**, not a general duplicate person model.

`ACTOR_MODEL != INTERLOCUTOR_TWIN`

`SOCIAL_RELEVANCE != COMPLETE_PERSON_RECONSTRUCTION`

## 3. Three non-collapsible inferential strata

### 3.1 Observable social evidence

What B actually says/does plus lawful situational context.

This does not by itself establish B's hidden intent or state.

### 3.2 First-order social reading

A's current reading of B:

- `B seems angry`;
- `B may be flirting`;
- `B wants reassurance`;
- `B is testing me`;
- `B is probably bored`;
- `B may be hiding something`.

These may be accurate, partially accurate, ambiguous, or wrong.

### 3.3 Personal second-order metaperception

A's current model of B's model of A:

- `B thinks I am nervous`;
- `B can tell I am attracted`;
- `B thinks I am weak`;
- `B knows I am bluffing`;
- `B sees me as competent`;
- `B does not understand me`;
- `B expects me to yield`.

This is the core phenomenon of **being seen**.

The third stratum is not reducible to the second. A may think B is kind while simultaneously believing B sees A as pathetic; A may think B is attracted while believing B thinks A is uninterested.

## 4. Being seen must be decomposed

`BEING_SEEN` is too broad to freeze as one scalar or boolean.

At least the following phenomenal distinctions must remain representable:

### 4.1 Perceived observation

`B noticed X about me.`

### 4.2 Perceived interpretation

`B interprets X as meaning Y.`

### 4.3 Perceived evaluation

`B approves/disapproves/values/devalues me because of Y.`

### 4.4 Perceived exposure

`B can see something I am trying not to reveal.`

### 4.5 Perceived recognition / mirroring

`B sees something real/important in me that others miss.`

### 4.6 Perceived misrecognition

`B sees me, but wrongly.`

### 4.7 Perceived opacity

`B cannot see what is actually happening in me.`

### 4.8 Perceived predictability / control

`B knows how I will react` or `B thinks they can manipulate/predict me.`

These can have radically different psychic effects even when the observable interaction is identical.

## 5. Being seen is not universally positive or negative

The Enneagram source material is useful here as internal logic, not as type law.

It provides examples in which:

- approval/mirroring can become central to self-worth and self-presentation;
- not being seen can organize longing, alienation, and attempts to become uniquely recognizable;
- being seen through can threaten camouflage, security, or defensive presentation;
- fear of rejection can make visibility itself threatening;
- a trusted relationship can make previously unavailable behavior locally reachable.

Rawlsing translation:

> The value of being seen depends on **what is believed to be seen, by whom, with what expected evaluation/consequence, and what part of the actor's standing self is implicated**.

Therefore:

`VISIBILITY != VALIDATION`

`RECOGNITION != APPROVAL`

`EXPOSURE != INTIMACY`

`BEING_UNDERSTOOD != BEING_LIKED`

`BEING_ACCURATELY_SEEN != FEELING_SAFE`

`BEING_MISSEEN != ALWAYS NEGATIVE`

A person may prefer flattering misrecognition to threatening accurate recognition; another may prefer painful accurate recognition to superficial approval.

## 6. Metaperception can be causally potent while false

PFF must preserve cases where A's belief about B's view of A is wrong but nevertheless reorganizes A's current psyche and behavior.

Example:

1. A is attracted to B.
2. A believes B has noticed.
3. A believes B will regard that attraction as humiliating or weak.
4. Shame/self-concealment is recruited.
5. A becomes cool or dismissive.
6. B reads the coolness as disinterest and withdraws.
7. A reads withdrawal as rejection.
8. The original metaperception becomes self-reinforcing despite being false.

Thus:

`FALSE_METAPERCEPTION -> REAL_PSYCHIC_EFFECT = POSSIBLE`

`FALSE_METAPERCEPTION -> REAL_DYADIC_LOOP = POSSIBLE`

This is not a bug; it is a core form of interpersonal causation.

## 7. Metaperception is partly self-model filtered

External research is useful as non-authoritative evidence. Work on social anxiety finds that people can believe others see them more negatively than others actually do, with metaperceptions substantially influenced by their own self-perceptions. Research on meta-accuracy also distinguishes general beliefs about how one is viewed from relationship-specific/dyad-specific accuracy.

Rawlsing implication:

`A_MODEL_OF_B_MODEL_OF_A`
may be generated partly from:

- B's actual current behavior;
- A's standing self-model;
- A's relationship history with B;
- A's current shame/pride/fear/attraction;
- A's expectations of rejection/approval;
- A's history of being seen or misseen;
- current defensive condition;
- current uncertainty.

Therefore the actor model cannot be merely an LLM reading of B's latest sentence.

## 8. Target significance must modulate metaperceptive force

The same believed judgment should not have equal psychic weight from every observer.

Potential relevance factors include:

- attachment significance;
- desired approval;
- authority/status power;
- attraction/desire;
- dependency;
- anticipated future contact;
- feared rejection;
- self-model vulnerability;
- group/audience importance;
- prior history of accurate or painful seeing;
- practical consequences.

Example:

`stranger thinks I am foolish`
may be weakly relevant,
while
`person I desperately admire thinks I am foolish`
may reorganize the entire live field.

No new `importance scalar` is adopted here; the requirement is only that target significance not be erased.

## 9. Minimal recursion: how many orders of mentalizing are needed?

External mentalizing literature supports a useful distinction:

- first-order mentalizing: `what does B think/want/feel?`;
- personal second-order mentalizing: `what does B think/know/see about me?`;
- higher-order recursive reasoning: `what does B think I think B wants?`, etc.

Second-order mentalizing appears especially relevant to live conversational inference and self-presentation.

Current PFF disposition:

### 9.1 Core requirement

PFF must be able to preserve **first-order social reading** and **personal second-order metaperception**.

Without first-order reading, the actor cannot meaningfully interpret another mind.

Without personal second-order metaperception, the actor cannot model being seen, exposure, bluffing, concealment, impression management, humiliation, validation, or many forms of flirtation/status play.

### 9.2 Higher-order recursion

Third-order and deeper reasoning is sometimes genuinely relevant:

- deception;
- negotiation;
- strategic testing;
- bluffing;
- audience effects;
- complex coalition politics;
- shared secrets;
- deliberate manipulation;
- common-knowledge situations.

But it should not become a permanently materialized recursive tree.

Provisional law:

`HIGHER_ORDER_SOCIAL_RECURSION = DEMAND_DRIVEN`

`RECURSION_DEPTH != PSYCHOLOGICAL_DEPTH`

`MORE_NESTING != MORE_REALISM`

`PERSISTENT_INFINITE_THEORY_OF_MIND = REJECT`

The architecture must later preserve the ability to reason deeper when a specific situation requires it without treating every interaction as an unbounded recursion problem.

## 10. Confidence, intensity, and importance remain separate

A may:

- weakly suspect B is judging them but care enormously;
- be nearly certain B is judging them but not care;
- be uncertain whether B is flirting yet experience strong activation;
- confidently but wrongly believe B understands them;
- hold two competing readings at once.

Therefore:

`SOCIAL_READING_CONFIDENCE != PSYCHIC_IMPACT`

`SOCIAL_READING_CONFIDENCE != TARGET_SIGNIFICANCE`

`PSYCHIC_IMPACT != OBJECTIVE_ACCURACY`

`MULTIPLE_SOCIAL_READINGS_MAY COEXIST`

This inherits the broader PFF rule `intensity != confidence`.

## 11. Actor model should be selective and action-relevant

The actor does not need to continuously infer every possible fact about B.

Social modeling should become active where it changes:

- interpretation of the current move;
- recruitment of durable actor organization;
- felt safety/threat/attraction/status;
- disclosure or concealment;
- anticipation;
- response selection;
- defense or posture;
- repair/escalation;
- self-model pressure.

This suggests a **relevance-gated inferential process**, not a complete static interlocutor dossier.

## 12. The most important seam: live social reading versus V3 belief

A serious semantic question remains unresolved.

V3 ADP-017 owns generated actor epistemic stances such as belief, uncertainty, nonknowledge, and first-person claim permission. PFF, however, appears to require fleeting present-tense readings such as:

- `she seems annoyed`;
- `maybe he is flirting`;
- `I think she can tell`;
- `he looks like he expects me to apologize`.

These may arise and disappear within seconds and can alter the current conscious field without obviously deserving durable V3 belief persistence.

Therefore architecture must eventually constitute an exact boundary among:

`MACHINE INTERPRETATION CANDIDATE`

`PFF LIVE SOCIAL READING / IMPRESSION`

`ACTOR CONSCIOUSLY ENTERTAINED HYPOTHESIS`

`V3 GENERATED EPISTEMIC STANCE`

`V2 EXPRESSIBLE FIRST-PERSON CLAIM`

Current pre-architecture hypothesis:

> A live social reading may be a transient phenomenological/inferential state that influences PFF without automatically becoming a durable proposition-level V3 belief; persistence, explicit endorsement, downstream causal importance, or other exact criteria may trigger V3 admission later.

This is a hypothesis only. No ownership or transition rule is adopted here.

## 13. Relation to defense and self-presentation

Metaperception is one of the main bridges between hidden activation and strategic/defensive expression.

Examples:

- `B can see I am afraid` -> bravado;
- `B thinks I am incompetent` -> overexplaining/performance;
- `B knows I want approval` -> ironic detachment;
- `B sees through my anger to hurt` -> increased anger or collapse;
- `B thinks I am desirable` -> confidence, coyness, or anxiety depending on actor organization;
- `B does not see my distress` -> escalation, withdrawal, or resignation;
- `B sees the real me` -> relief, panic, attachment, distrust, or intensified testing.

Defense therefore cannot be modeled independently of the actor's model of the audience/interlocutor.

## 14. Relation to chemistry

The dyadic chemistry examination established that chemistry is best treated provisionally as properties of directional coupling.

Actor modeling/metaperception explains one of the most powerful coupling channels:

`B observable move`
`-> A reading of B`
`-> A model of B's view of A`
`-> A recruitment / defense / expression`
`-> B receives A move`
`-> B equivalent process`

Pair-level loops can therefore be generated by **models of models**, not only direct reactions to behavior.

This is how mutual misunderstanding can become stable interaction structure.

## 15. Generated-generated versus generated-external interaction

### 15.1 Generated-generated

If both participants are generated, Rawlsing may possess governed internal state for both. Even then, A must not automatically receive B's hidden state.

A's actor-local model of B should still be generated from what A could lawfully perceive/know.

`SYSTEM_HAS_B_STATE != A_HAS_B_STATE`

This preserves actor opacity.

### 15.2 Generated-external/human

For an external/human interlocutor, hidden mental state is unavailable.

PFF must operate from:

- observable communication;
- lawful context;
- generated actor history/model;
- ambiguity;
- uncertainty;
- current live state.

This makes uncertainty and competing readings fundamental rather than exceptional.

## 16. Source-theory refinements from the Enneagram material

The Enneagram sources contribute internal logic, not actor facts.

Useful source-derived insights include:

1. **Approval-oriented mirroring:** a person may adapt presentation around the anticipated approving gaze of a valued other.
2. **Identity mirroring:** being accurately recognized may carry exceptional weight where identity has historically felt unseen or misunderstood.
3. **Visibility threat:** being seen through can threaten defensive camouflage and security.
4. **Rejection-sensitive visibility:** the prospect of being seen can itself recruit withdrawal when exposure is linked to humiliation or rejection.
5. **Security-specific access:** trusted relationships may unlock behavior/capacities not broadly available elsewhere.

Rawlsing should not retain these as fixed type behaviors. It should use them as examples of distinct **functions of social perception and metaperception** that the general machinery must be able to explain.

## 17. Hard negatives

Reject unless later independently justified:

- a complete hidden-state clone of the interlocutor;
- objective B state injected directly into A as A's knowledge;
- `metaperception = truth`;
- one global `how_seen_score`;
- one global `social_anxiety` shortcut controlling all metaperception;
- unlimited persistent recursive theory-of-mind trees;
- forced single winning interpretation of ambiguous social cues;
- confidence used as psychic intensity;
- external/human interlocutor hidden-state hallucination;
- type label -> fixed theory-of-mind style;
- rendered claim about B used as proof that A previously believed it;
- session transcript used to create durable V3 beliefs without proper authority.

## 18. Positive obligations for later architecture

Any successful PFF architecture must eventually preserve:

1. first-order actor-local readings of interlocutor state/intention;
2. personal second-order metaperception / being-seen phenomena;
3. wrong-but-causally-potent metaperceptions;
4. multiple competing social readings;
5. separate confidence, impact, target significance, and accuracy;
6. relationship/history/self-model dependence of social inference;
7. target-specific significance;
8. opacity of counterpart hidden state even when Rawlsing internally possesses it;
9. operation with external interlocutors whose hidden state is unavailable;
10. demand-driven higher-order recursion where genuinely required;
11. a strict boundary between machine candidate interpretation, live social reading, V3 belief, and V2 claim permission;
12. causal effects of perceived recognition, exposure, evaluation, misrecognition, and opacity;
13. defense/self-presentation driven partly by anticipated counterpart perception;
14. no requirement for a complete persistent interlocutor simulation.

## 20. Next questions forced by this examination

The next highest-value work is now:

1. **psychic conflict / nonlinear combination** — determine how multiple recruited organizations and competing social readings combine, inhibit, displace, cross-convert, or tip;
2. **conscious field** — determine how current social readings, metaperceptions, hidden activation, and bodily/affective material become phenomenal experience;
3. **defense / performance / leakage** — determine exactly how anticipated evaluation and exposure alter accessibility and expression;
4. **time / persistence / hysteresis** — determine how long a social reading or metaperceptive residue persists and when repeated impressions become V3 belief/relationship history;
5. **live social reading -> V3 epistemic stance seam** — later architecture must establish exact admission criteria rather than allowing silent promotion.
