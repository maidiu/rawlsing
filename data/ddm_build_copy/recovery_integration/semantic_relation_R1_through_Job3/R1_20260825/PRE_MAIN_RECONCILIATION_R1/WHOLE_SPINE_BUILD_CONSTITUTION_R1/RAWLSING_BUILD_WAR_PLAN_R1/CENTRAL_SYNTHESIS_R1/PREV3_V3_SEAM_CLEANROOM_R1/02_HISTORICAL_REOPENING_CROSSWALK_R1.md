Almost nothing semantic.

1. The clean target should explicitly use a **producer-owned DAG / direct-reference** model rather than copying everything into a seam-owned schema.

History does **not** revive:

- a universal terminal vector;
- a universal typology;
- a fixed driver registry;
- a mandatory persistent master carrier;
- a serial courier pipeline.
