# M1 — MORTIMER NULLS / BOUNDARIES / HETEROGENEITY LEDGER R1

STATUS: `M1_MORTIMER_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE_COURT_COMPLETE`

Source: Jeylan T. Mortimer, *Working and Growing Up in America* (2003), full-book excavation.

This ledger is intentionally negative-evidence heavy. Its purpose is to prevent a future SDTF implementation from turning Mortimer into a monotonic “work is developmental” theory.

## 2. Strongest contradiction sets

### 2.1 Work quantity versus work quality

The book repeatedly invalidates a single exposure axis:

```text
HOURS HIGH
can coexist with
LEARNING HIGH + ADVANCEMENT HIGH + STRESS HIGH + STATUS HIGH

HOURS LOW/MODERATE + DURATION HIGH (“steady”)
can coexist with
better later educational attainment than long-duration/high-intensity work.
```

Therefore neither `EMPLOYMENT_STATUS` nor `HOURS` can serve as a sufficient actor-relative ecology representation.

### 2.2 Immediate versus durable consequence

```text
ADOLESCENT HIGH-INTENSITY WORK
-> higher contemporaneous drinking / some problem behavior

BUT

FOUR YEARS AFTER HIGH SCHOOL
-> no work-pattern drinking difference
```

Likewise early earnings advantages substantially converge by six years after high school. The source therefore directly supports the Rawlsing firewall:

`UPTAKE/CONTEMPORANEOUS RESPONSE != DURABLE CHANGE`.

### 2.3 Same nominal autonomy, different developmental phase

The adolescent male autonomy/control result is a direct warning against transporting adult work/self-direction coefficients across developmental phases. Mortimer's plausible mechanism is not “autonomy reverses by age” as a primitive law; it is that autonomy is enacted under different prior competence, role clarity, support, responsibility and experience. This is precisely why phase/history belongs in the transduction binding.

### 2.4 Selection versus socialization

Mortimer's Ch. 5 selection results and Ch. 6–7 downstream associations jointly reject both one-sided stories:

```text
PERSON STATE -> WORK PATTERN/QUALITY            [observed prospectively]
WORK QUALITY/PATTERN -> LATER OUTCOME           [some associations persist under controls]
```

Neither edge licenses direct person→position causation. The labor market/employer/school gate remains external.

## 3. Strongest negative/null finding in the book for SDTF

The single strongest *architectural* negative finding is the **failure of a monotonic quantity model across time and targets**:

- more/high-intensity work is associated with some near-term problem behavior and early earnings;
- those behavioral differences can disappear after high school;
- early earnings differences converge;
- steady moderate work is more favorable for B.A. attainment than the most-invested pattern;
- family-transition differences are absent at 21–22 and emerge later at normative ages;
- hours do not generate intrinsic work values in the way learning opportunity does.

This compound negative result is stronger than any isolated null because it rules out a broad architecture family: `EMPLOYMENT/HOURS -> MONOTONIC DEVELOPMENT`.

## 4. Design ceilings

No M1 product may represent the following as demonstrated by Mortimer:

1. random-assignment causality;
2. a full fixed-effects/within-person estimate for every mechanism;
3. a complete continuous trajectory of job quality;
4. a uniquely identified mediator for alcohol/problem behavior;
5. a fully observed end-to-end D3 feedback cycle through employer/institution response;
6. universal coefficients outside the YDS institutional/historical context;
7. a scalar latent “good work” cause;
8. a direct demographic-category → psychological-trait rule.
