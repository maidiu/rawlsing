# T1 NORMALIZED FINDINGS AND RAWLSING MAPPING R1

STATUS: `COMPLETE__NORMALIZED_FOR_VOZHD_SYNTHESIS__NO_ARCHITECTURE_MUTATION`

## 1. Central answer

The T1 tradition supplies a coherent but bounded answer to the campaign question. Social organization becomes developmentally consequential **not because a category carries a trait**, but because objective relations organize an actor's reachable and recurrent activities: tasks, tools, counterpart roles, instructions, schedules, products, dependencies, and forms of cooperation. Those activities are realized as acts. Acts can reorganize functional systems, form or exercise capacities, acquire personal sense through motive relations, become operations through repeated practice, and alter the hierarchy of motives/activities. The resulting capacities and motive organization then change later activity selection and performance.

Normalized chain:

`STRUCTURAL RELATION/CONTROL/DIVISION-OF-LABOUR/TIME RULE`
`-> ACTOR-RELEVANT ACTIVITY POSSIBILITY`
`-> ACTUAL PARTICIPATION + TASK/TOOL/COUNTERPART CONFIGURATION`
`-> ACTS / GUIDED ACTIONS / REPEATED OPERATIONS`
`-> CAPACITY, PERSONAL SENSE, MOTIVE-HIERARCHY OR SELF-REGULATION CHANGE`
`-> CHANGED FUTURE ACTIVITY REPERTOIRE / SELECTION`
`-> BIOGRAPHICAL TRAJECTORY`

Every arrow is conditional. No upstream category is itself a psychological state.

## 2. Highest-confidence normalized mechanism families

### NF-01 — Organized activity ecology

Structural relations determine, probabilistically and position-specifically, which activities are available, required, rewarded, sanctioned, timed, or blocked. Division of labour and control of tools are canonical cases. The psychologically relevant exposure is the **activity configuration actually realized**, not the structural label.

Rawlsing judgment: `MISSING_CONNECTIVE_FUNCTION`, not a new downstream V3 trait owner. This directly fits the Phase-0 seam `STRUCTURED_WORLD_TO_ACTOR_RELATIVE_SOCIAL_ECOLOGY_TRANSDUCTION`.

### NF-02 — Repeated-practice / action-operation formation

Leontiev provides an explicit answer to the commission's difficult temporal question: repeated activity can form skilled operations without conscious appraisal on every iteration. Conscious goal-directed action can become condition-regulated operation after repeated/restructured performance. This is not passive exposure: it presupposes an action embedded in an activity and appropriate conditions/feedback.

Rawlsing judgment: upstream forge needs a lawful carrier of `RECURRING ACTIVITY / PRACTICE OPPORTUNITY`; existing V3 memory/learning machinery should own durable learning. Do not duplicate learning inside PRE-V3.

### NF-03 — Socially distributed mediation / sign-tool reorganization

Vygotsky makes the mediator concrete: a socially produced sign/tool inserted into an act changes its functional organization. Interpersonal direction can become self-direction through transformed use. The source explicitly says the inward transition changes structure/function, so a generic copy/internalization operator is rejected.

Rawlsing judgment: likely composition of existing contact/OEE, mediation-route possibility, CAN-011 practices/mediators, ADP-017 knowledge/access and ADP-016 traces rather than a theory-named new entity.

### NF-04 — Guided relation → supported action → independent performance

Instruction/collaboration shows a relation whose effect cannot be reduced to mere contact. The relation organizes a reachable action beyond current independent performance; durable developmental consequence requires later independent executability.

Rawlsing judgment: positive relation/activity transduction is upstream-missing; learner uptake and consolidation are downstream V3.

### NF-05 — Acts-capacities reproduction loop

Sève's most reusable contribution is not a class personality. It is the reciprocal analytic distinction: capacities make acts possible; many acts produce/develop capacities. Different biographies contain different proportions/sequences of capacity-forming versus capacity-using activity.

Rawlsing judgment: good general law for how an `ACTIVITY ECOLOGY` can matter developmentally, but the capacity state itself should remain with current V3 learning/competence machinery unless synthesis proves an owner gap.

### NF-06 — Use-time as structural distribution of developmental opportunity

Finite time plus externally constrained schedules produces a composition of actual activity. Work, care, institutional timetables, commute, and discretionary control can increase or crowd out recurrent capacity-forming activity. The important variable is not "hours" abstractly but actor-relative time in objective activity configurations.

Rawlsing judgment: strong positive content for Phase-0 `space/time control` / `activity ecology` seam. Treat Sève's topology as theoretical hypothesis, not measurement law.

### NF-07 — Objective meaning != personal sense

Shared meanings/norms are historically available; their personal sense is actor-specific and depends on motive/activity relation. This provides a strict anti-determinist separator between social meaning and subjective uptake.

Rawlsing judgment: largely `REUSE`; ADP-015 and existing mediator/meaning machinery appear to own the downstream distinction. Upstream only needs to represent objective meaning/role exposure and the activity in which it is encountered.

### NF-08 — Motive/activity hierarchy transition

Personality development is better represented as reorganization among activities/motives than as accumulation of environment-corresponding traits. New actions/objects can gain motive force; cross-domain activity ties can alter hierarchy and future selection.

Rawlsing judgment: likely V3-owned by motive, rupture/metabolization and attractor-transition mechanisms. Forge contribution is to supply patterned external activity relations that can trigger those transitions.

### NF-09 — Subject-mediated realization

Rubinstein's "external causes through internal conditions" and the broader activity tradition jointly prohibit deterministic projection from structure to psyche. Existing capacities, motives, meanings and history condition how the same objective relation is realized.

Rawlsing judgment: `REUSE_AS_BOUNDARY_LAW`; not a new black-box mechanism.

## 5. Reciprocity normalization

T1 rejects a one-way structure→person pipeline. The minimum lawful loop is:

`STRUCTURE -> ACTIVITY ECOLOGY -> REALIZED ACTION/PRACTICE -> PERSON CHANGE -> CHANGED ACTION SELECTION/PERFORMANCE -> LOCAL REPRODUCTION OR MODIFICATION OF RELATIONS`.

This reciprocity must not erase asymmetry. Property, authority, institutional rules and time control can make some actors far more able than others to alter the ecology. Conversely, a person's prior capacities/motives make selection into tasks and relations endogenous. Rawlsing should therefore separate:

- structural allocation/constraint;
- actor selection/avoidance;
- realized participation;
- uptake/learning;
- feedback from action to local structure.
