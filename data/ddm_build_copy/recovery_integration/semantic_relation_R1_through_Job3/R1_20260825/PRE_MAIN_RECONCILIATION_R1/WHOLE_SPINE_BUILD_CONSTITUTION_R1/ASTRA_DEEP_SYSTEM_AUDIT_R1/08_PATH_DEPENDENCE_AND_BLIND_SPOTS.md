# Path dependence, blind spots and explicit special questions

The strongest evidence does not show that deliberate architectural distinctions should be discarded. It shows that confidence in a correctly guarded path can hide a neighboring shared path and that a one-time validation seal can become a lifetime assumption without immutable state. These claims are based on the ledger controls, not an account of what the team believes or a diagnosis of individual engineers.

Three blind spots deserve attention: treating the outer frozen type as a deeply immutable value; treating successful wrapper tests as coverage of all shared composition; and treating current zero-effect receipts as proof of future effectful behavior. The active representation mismatch is unusually useful evidence because the exact current query can be reconstructed cheaply and independently of the expensive fact.

Known prior scaling, persistence, current/historical and permission-translation incidents were not used as a finding template. The post-seal identity lifetime problem was prioritized by reachable source behavior. The cost opportunity is supported by a new bounded byte calculation, while already implemented compact R4A and active I03 repairs are acknowledged as counterevidence. No unsupported claim is made that a legacy subsystem has lost its semantic purpose.

## 1. Where is the conceptual distinction sharper than the executable distinction?

Immutable/sealed source identity is sharper than shallow outer freezing (ASTRA-001). Scoped ALLOW and adopted producer permission are sharper than the shared operations that consume them (ASTRA-002). The named current object boundary also assumes a common representation that ordinary construction does not supply (ASTRA-003).

## 2. Where is the executable distinction sharper or more expensive than semantics requires?

Repeated expanded dictionaries and canonical JSON make one exact ref materially expensive in several provenance roles (ASTRA-005). Those roles remain semantically distinct; repeated residence and traversal can disappear without deleting their commitments. No claim is made that exact risk membership itself is over-specified.

## 3. Which invariants are enforced in exactly one relevant place and vulnerable to bypass?

For the ordinary I03 composition examined, the frozen GT12 plan builder performs family/source fitness that the shared execution path omits. For the GT2 relation M4 descent, the governed publish wrapper derives singleton CONTEXT while the shared relation adapter accepts caller uses. These are local one-court dependencies, not a claim that the entire repository has only one validator implementation.

## 4. Which laws are independently represented in multiple places and vulnerable to drift?

The authority candidate/adoption documents, frozen authority constants, admission mapping, typed M1 projection, M4 permission tuple and consumer checker encode related law in distinct formats. Current adopted hashes and wrapper checks agree. Ref type conversion demonstrably drifted at the active checker; a broader current authority-constant mismatch was not found. Generate mechanical encodings where useful, but keep owner/admission/consumer responsibilities distinct.

## 5. Which tests can remain green despite violating their intended property?

Ingest-integrity tests omit mutation through public aliases after sealing; dedicated scope-validator tests omit shared I03 composition; a wrapper-signature test omits the other exported relation API; active public-entry negatives fail before the inconsistent target comparison, and the positive synthetic test calls the private chain. Returned guard zero counters do not prove a future owner has no state effects. The 298/33 green focused counts coexist with these gaps.

## 6. Which content-addressed objects bind less than consumers assume?

Sealed PRE553 refs cease to identify the live nested body after public alias mutation. The fact hash itself still binds the changed output: this is an upstream lifetime guarantee failure, not a collision or omitted count. Structural dependency-role metadata is a separate OPEN_QUESTION; ordinary constructor/edge validation may intentionally own that obligation.

## 7. Which objects may bind more material than the semantic contract needs?

The empirical fact repeats extensive exact refs across dependency, provenance, risk and denominator roles. Evidence proves representational repetition, not that any role can be removed from semantic identity. Whether a smaller new commitment could replace those arrays is an HQ semantic decision, not an audit-approved optimization.

## 8. What consequential plausible defect was successfully falsified?

Caller-claimed positive authority or a registry override cannot make the production V3 guard positive. The accepted-positive registry is empty and immutable; the public interface has no override. The governed typed GT12 wrapper also rejects wrong authority/adoption and non-CONTEXT use shapes.

## 9. What is the highest-value improvement requiring no accepted semantic or authority change?

Make sealed identity-bearing source state actually immutable and keep exported projections detached (ASTRA-001). This repairs the foundational reference lifetime law and makes later validation reuse defensible. The highest-value performance opportunity is separately ASTRA-005: compact transport with byte-identical canonical commitments.

## 10. What assumption becomes dangerous when positive V3 authority exists?

That a structurally valid evidence/delegation receipt is sufficient authorization for an actor-specific effect. Today an empty registry prevents that implication from producing a positive transition. Future source applicability, actor/operation binding and replay semantics need an accepted owner court.

## 11. Where would a future serious correctness investigation begin?

At the sealed C11 -> ordinary I03 boundary: compare live body projections with their stored hashes before trusting source refs, then inspect operation-authority fitness. ASTRA-001 shows a minimal way to preserve marginal plausibility and provenance labels while changing the joint evidence that downstream decisions consume.

## 12. Which recurring complexity could be deleted while preserving semantics?

Handwritten conversion/projection code that repeatedly reconciles the same exact-reference and authority shapes. Centralize or generate tested mechanical codecs with explicit role/order preservation. Do not collapse distinct producers, authorities, semantic stages or admission courts into one generic object merely to shorten code.

## 13. Do tests prove system invariants or implementations against themselves?

Both. Joint-incidence/risk-set counterexamples, typed-negative tests, exact permission rejection and itemwise dependency checks prove substantive local laws. Signature inspection, early wrong-current negatives and a private-chain positive test prove narrower properties than full public integration. A fixture with patched risk size plus a literal-constant test does not constitute actual current execution.

## 14. Where is semantic ownership ambiguous?

Authority derivation is clear in the adopted wrapper but not compulsory at the shared relation object constructor or shared I03 operation. Dependency obligations versus opaque lineage evidence after M4 reconstruction remain an explicit question. Producer, admission and future causal owner must each own their own semantic act; transport must not acquire it by being the last place to touch a dictionary.

## 15. Can evidence that X happened become authority to cause X?

Shared I03 treats the presence of an ALLOW authority snapshot as enough despite independent scope unfitness; the shared relation adapter accepts caller permission despite exact upstream lineage. These are present authority-fitting gaps. A future consumer could also overread V558 or delegation evidence as causal permission, but the current public V3 guard blocks that promotion. No present positive V3 effect is claimed.

Evidence and exact commits for each answer are in the referenced ASTRA ledger records and invariant matrix. The answers do not introduce additional findings beyond those six records.
