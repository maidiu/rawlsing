## Ownership that remains correctly bounded

FIELD FORGE constructs actor-independent exact conditional support. PRE-V3 admits that relation under its own exact authority. Relation-specific M1/V554 does not acquire empirical-direct or V553/V555/V556 substitute authority. The governed GT12 wrapper validates the adoption receipt and derives only CONTEXT. V558 checks exact structural resolution and each dependency's permission. The public V3 guard has no accepted positive authority. Focused tests support these named boundaries; ASTRA-002 concerns composing around a selected wrapper, not its internal widening.

A reference-preserving transport is permitted to carry opaque provenance without becoming its semantic owner. The ledger therefore does **not** declare absence of every upstream body from M4 a defect. ASTRA-006 asks which refs are dependency obligations and who revalidates that distinction after a real codec roundtrip.
