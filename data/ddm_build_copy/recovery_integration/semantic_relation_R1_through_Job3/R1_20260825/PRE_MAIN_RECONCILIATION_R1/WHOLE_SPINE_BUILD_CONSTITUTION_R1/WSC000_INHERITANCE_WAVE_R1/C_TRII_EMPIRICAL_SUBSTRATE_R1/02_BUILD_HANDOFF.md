# WSC000C — BUILD HANDOFF

This file carries only the implications the later Whole-Spine Build Constitution must preserve.

## 1. Source/current-empirical ownership is upstream and exact

The `SOURCE/CURRENT EMPIRICAL -> PRE-FF GOVERNED COMPILER` seam must consume exact source-native semantics, universe identity, routing/risk-set state, response topology, support/weight semantics, and authority/provenance without re-owning or silently normalizing their meaning.

`COMPILER_OR_MATERIALIZER_PRESERVES_AUTHORITY != COMPILER_OR_MATERIALIZER_CREATES_AUTHORITY`.

FIELD FORGE or any later structural consumer may not infer ontology from empirical availability, label similarity, prevalence, or physical co-location.

## 2. Same-respondent jointability is an information-and-authority obligation

Any conditional, conjunction, response-response joint, branch cotravel, or k-way claim must have either:

- exact same-respondent incidence under the exact respondent universe/risk set; or
- an exact information-equivalent representation sufficient for the requested arity, conditioning, topology, support and measure semantics.

Marginals, one modal joint cell, scalar association scores, or a historical aggregate carrier are not general substitutes.

Current J01-A and C06 Contract@2 are current/delegated only at their exact denominator/routing grains. They do not manufacture general respondent-composability or measurement-portability authority.

## 3. Source universes default distinct

The spine must require separate exact authority for each operation that crosses source universes: respondent linkage, semantic/topology equivalence, nonduplication, pooling, estimand composition, weighting/design equivalence, backfill, temporal comparison, or measurement portability.

Shared IDs, labels, canonical aliases, country, or apparent questionnaire similarity are not linkage/comparability evidence.

Source disagreement must remain source-indexed information unless a competent combination rule explicitly governs the target estimand.

## 4. Carrier neutrality is binding; a physical winner is not

The semantic spine must preserve information sufficiency, producer ownership, exact replay, and reconstruction obligations independently of physical packaging.

Do not encode Trii D15's append-only persistent C11 choice as constitutional law.

`PERSISTENT != VIRTUAL/SOURCE_BACKED != HYBRID` remains an unresolved physical architecture choice under OBL001/OQ01.

The build may reuse C11-oriented logical objects/query/replay machinery without declaring a C11 physical fossil mandatory.

## 5. Replay is exact and non-authorizing

Replay must pin exact source-content refs, semantic projections, and historical authority id/version/hash tuples.

Forbidden fallback classes include latest/default/fuzzy/nearest reinterpretation.

Provenance, repository presence, a newer authority, or a successful reconstruction may not be substituted for the exact historical permission required by the replayed object.

Keep `OBL029` open until source->carrier equivalence and source-retirement are proved at the required grain.

## 6. Discovery authority precedes search

The governed compiler must separate:

- declared candidate/search/validation/coverage authority;
- search scheduling/ranking;
- produced empirical results;
- downstream structural admission.

Exact-bounded discovery may claim completeness only for its exact declared frontier.

Adaptive exploration may return exact results for visited sealed units, but unvisited/pruned territory remains unknown and cannot become negative evidence.

Reuse `rawlsing_next/pre553_discovery_adaptive.py` and its hostile tests where the adaptive mode is required; do not treat that implementation as closure of `OBL030`.

## 7. Empirical coordinates do not become structural positions

`EMPIRICAL_COORDINATE != GENERATED_STRUCTURAL_POSITION`.

Any empirical fact proposed to FIELD FORGE/PRE-V3 structural machinery needs competent structural admission at the exact use grain. No demographic/geographic/coordinate value may directly instantiate a structural position by shared label, prevalence, or plausibility.

This is the empirical side of the Authority31 admission boundary.

## 8. Comparator authority is explicit, not heuristic

Generated-subject/actor facts may condition empirical queries only through an exact field/source/time-specific comparator/predicate ancestry with replayable authority.

No nearest-cohort, fuzzy-label, structural-position, or source-agnostic comparator fallback is lawful.

Carry `OBL031` explicitly at the reverse-query/PSEPF seam.

## 9. Learned empirical possibility remains distinct from realized life

The spine must preserve at least this noncollapse:

`POPULATION_EMPIRICAL_FACT != POSITIONED_SUBJECT_EMPIRICAL_POSSIBILITY_FIELD != V3_DEVELOPED_OR_REALIZED_DISPOSITION != ACTOR_BELIEF_OR_RESPONSE`.

A possibility field may constrain a later developmental consumer only through its own competent metabolism/admission rule. Do not convert population evidence directly into actor belief, action, personality, or realized life.

Keep downstream `OBL032` open until the production PSEPF transition/metabolism is actually proved.
