# G4 SERIAL DIALECTICAL CROSS-EXAMINATION PROTOCOL

Status: `AUTHORIZED__SERIAL_COUNCIL__TURN_ORDER_FIXED__HQ_FREEZE_BETWEEN_TURNS__NON_DISPOSITIVE`

The earlier parallel-G4 design is superseded by this serial protocol.

## 1. Why serial now

G0-G2 already supplied independent blind evidence. G4 therefore optimizes for adversarial interaction rather than further isolation.

The speaking order is fixed:

`TURN_A_TRII_CONSERVATION_THESIS`
→ `TURN_B_DVA_ARCHITECTURAL_RESPONSE`
→ `TURN_C_CHETIRI_EXECUTABILITY_RESPONSE`
→ `TURN_D_TRII_SEMANTIC_REBUTTAL`
→ `TURN_E_DVA_WHOLE_MACHINE_SYNTHESIS`
→ `HQ_G5_ADJUDICATION`

No agent may skip ahead or submit a later turn before HQ has frozen the immediately preceding turn in the shared discourse space.

## 2. Shared discourse space

HQ will bank each accepted turn verbatim or losslessly under:

`G3_COUNCIL/SERIAL_DISCOURSE/`

Canonical turn names:
- `01_TRII_CONSERVATION_THESIS.md`
- `02_DVA_ARCHITECTURAL_RESPONSE.md`
- `03_CHETIRI_EXECUTABILITY_RESPONSE.md`
- `04_TRII_SEMANTIC_REBUTTAL.md`
- `05_DVA_WHOLE_MACHINE_SYNTHESIS.md`

A later speaker may consume only the frozen prior-turn artifacts authorized by its role-native turn authority, plus the already-open G3 claim matrix and G0-G2 evidence. Do not use informal summaries in place of the frozen turn.

## 3. Dialectical order

### Turn A — Trii: meaning before architecture

Trii must state the narrowest lossless semantic/source-authority conservation thesis: what must survive, at what authority ceiling, what may be separately referenced, what is not required, and what maintenance/completeness functions remain necessary.

Trii is forbidden to choose implementation architecture.

### Turn B — Dva: architecture answers the semantic thesis

Dva receives frozen Turn A and tests where the claimed semantic capital lawfully belongs in the whole machine with minimum architectural disturbance. Dva must try to reuse existing stages/jurisdictions rather than invent a new numbered stage.

### Turn C — Chetiri: execution answers meaning + architecture

Chetiri receives frozen Turns A+B and attacks whether the proposed conservation contract and architectural placement are exactly representable, addressable, queryable, replayable, invalidatable, maintainable, and computationally sane.

### Turn D — Trii: semantic red-team of the proposed machine

Trii receives frozen Turns A+B+C and tests whether architecture/execution silently weakened, widened, collapsed, or invented semantic authority.

### Turn E — Dva: final whole-machine synthesis

Dva receives all prior frozen turns and proposes the smallest whole-machine integration surviving both Trii semantic red-team and Chetiri executability red-team. Dva may leave disputes unresolved; it may not resolve them by convenience.

Then all discourse stops. HQ alone performs G5 adjudication.

## 6. Court law

Majority agreement is not evidence.

Later speakers must state the strongest version of the prior claim before attacking it.

No one may turn:
- implementation affordance into semantic ownership;
- semantic truth into empirical co-travel;
- empirical association into semantic authority;
- exact candidate REJECT into global nonedge;
- BLOCKED into false;
- current representability into proof of actual governed load;
- historical survival into current authority;
- lack of current owner into automatic proof that a new numbered stage is required;
- exact frontier completion into whole-machine jurisdiction completeness.

No live mutation, shared-conservation write, A/B/C choice, V553+ reopen, I05 scope change, PR #125 release decision, or R3 live-authority installation is authorized.

## 7. HQ freeze law

After each turn, HQ will:
1. hostile-review the return;
2. bank the accepted turn in `SERIAL_DISCOURSE/`;
3. advance the coordination branch;
4. issue only the next authorized handoff.

A speaker must refuse to proceed if its required immediately prior frozen turn is absent.
