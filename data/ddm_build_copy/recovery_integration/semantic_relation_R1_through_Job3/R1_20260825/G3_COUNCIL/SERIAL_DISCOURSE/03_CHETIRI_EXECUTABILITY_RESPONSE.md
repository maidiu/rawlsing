### C-TC02 — ExactRef typed identity

Disposition: `SPLIT_BY_JURISDICTION`.

`ExactRef` is sufficient as an address primitive only if its content hash covers the canonical semantic projection of the relation. A blob/path hash is not semantic relation identity. `ExactRef` is not itself the semantic schema, validator, authority resolver, or lifecycle owner. `AuthorityRef` identifies exact authority version but is not the relation object.

No new low-level reference primitive is required. Canonical semantic projection + resolver remains unproved.

### C-TC03 — 697 REJECT compaction

Disposition: `NARROWED`.

Dva is right that 697 runtime graph roots are unnecessary, but compaction must be lossless normalization, not tombstones.

A normalized REJECT must preserve at least:
- exact candidate ref/fingerprint;
- rule/template version;
- exact members/member-set ref;

- terminal state `EXACT_CANDIDATE_REJECT`;

- policy ref;
- per-candidate ceiling/prohibition deltas;

Repeated policy/prose may be interned only after exact equivalence is proved. Unique adjudicative meaning may not be compressed away. A 697/697 semantic-losslessness court is required before retiring the legacy carrier.

Disposition: `REMAINS_UNRESOLVED`.

1. shared policy fully entails omitted edge-level restrictions;
2. policy version is exact/content-addressed;
3. policy ref participates in authority closure/semantic identity wherever policy change changes legal meaning;

Current V557 REVIEW/CONTEXT/ACTIVE_CAUSAL permissions are too coarse to substitute for R3-specific semantic prohibitions. No field deletion is authorized absent exact entailment + mandatory-consumer proof.
