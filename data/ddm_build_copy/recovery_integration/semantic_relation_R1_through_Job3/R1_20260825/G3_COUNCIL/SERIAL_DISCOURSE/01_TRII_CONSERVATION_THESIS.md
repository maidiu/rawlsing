## 4. Relation-state law

The semantic relation state space is not one boolean edge/nonedge variable.

### Positive typed semantic relation
Exact members satisfy the exact relation under governed semantic authority. Ceiling: that relation type, members and exact scope/condition envelope. It does not imply respondent join authority, empirical association, actor trait, causal permission, or downstream realization.

### Independently proven exact typed negative
Positive evidence establishes that the exact proposed typed relation does not hold at the certified semantic grain. Ceiling: that exact negative claim only. It does not imply empirical nonassociation or global topic-wide absence.

### Exact-candidate `REJECT`
The exact candidate as proposed under its frozen rule/template/evidence scope failed adjudication. Ceiling: candidate-disposition authority only.

**Governing terminology:** `EXACT_CANDIDATE_REJECT`.

The phrase `REJECT = semantic nonedge` is **REJECTED AS GOVERNING TERMINOLOGY** because it naturally widens an exact failed candidate into a universal ontology-level assertion.

- `REJECT -> PROVEN_TYPED_NEGATIVE`
- `REJECT -> NO_SEMANTIC_EDGE_EXISTS_BETWEEN_MEMBERS`

### `BLOCKED`
The semantic relation question is known but unresolved because required authority/evidence is unavailable or insufficient. Ceiling: unresolved state plus blocker/reopen obligation. It creates neither positive nor negative truth.

### Unexamined / outside semantic search
No governed adjudication occurred in the certified search jurisdiction. Ceiling: none. Missing row is not evidence.

### Zero positives in a cell
No positive result exists in that surface. Ceiling: none unless separate licensed-absence prerequisites exist.

### Licensed semantic absence
A bounded absence conclusion is licensed only by its exact negative/coverage/derivation authority and only for that exact certified operation/rule/source scope.

### Empirical association/nonassociation
