## 2.2 The 697 exact-candidate REJECTs

Trii is correct that the adjudicative fact cannot simply vanish:

`this exact candidate under this frozen rule/member/evidence scope was adjudicated REJECT`.

But **697 first-class persistent semantic graph objects are unnecessary**.

A REJECT is not live semantic topology. It is an immutable court disposition over a candidate.

The minimum lossless carrier is a compact adjudication-memory record keyed by a stable exact candidate fingerprint containing only the irreducible judgment:

```text
candidate_id/fingerprint
rule/template version
exact member refs
frozen source/evidence-scope refs
terminal disposition = EXACT_CANDIDATE_REJECT
decisive mismatch / structured reason or exact reason-ref
scope/ceiling policy-ref
per-candidate override where required
authority/provenance version
successor-obligation ref if one exists
```

The existing recovered REJECT rows demonstrate why that memory is genuinely non-derivable. For example `O12C-01eedb4f9fdc5ecb472b3b9c` records that two Afrobarometer items share response topology but not a proposition; its rejection is exact-candidate scope only and explicitly forbids rescuing a coherent subset or retyping the candidate. Other REJECTs explicitly say a differently typed relation or corrected successor remains possible.

Dva already has an architectural precedent for exactly this persistence economy: immutable identity/rejection lineage is separable from indefinite payload retention. W0 accepted that terminally rejected noncausal content may eventually be removed under explicit policy while immutable rejection lineage survives.

So:

```text
697 historical adjudications must remain knowable
!=
697 runtime graph objects must remain materialized
```

They may live in **one immutable compact adjudication ledger**.

They may be retired even further only if somebody proves that frozen inputs plus frozen adjudicator law deterministically regenerate the same 697 human/source-semantic judgments. No such proof exists.

**Disposition:** `NARROW`.
