## 2. Certified semantic law

### D01 — ExactRef

`ExactRef` may address an exact typed semantic claim only after a lawful canonical semantic-claim definition exists. ExactRef is not relation ontology, authority, lifecycle, policy, validator, or source-evidence court.

A common envelope is permissible; one untyped generic relation payload is not certified. Family-specific typed payload law remains required where T01–T15 semantics differ.

### D02 — claim identity vs authority-instance identity

The machine must distinguish:

- `SEMANTIC_CLAIM_IDENTITY`
- `GOVERNING_AUTHORITY_INSTANCE_IDENTITY`

Recertification may change governing authority while preserving the same semantic claim. Correction of members, material roles, constitutive scope, rule meaning, or constitutive actor/target/action/modality changes semantic claim identity.

Current-selection/use-policy changes may alter current authority or consumer permission without minting a new semantic claim.

### D03 — source/evidence binding

Source/question/routing/evidence payload may remain exact references to separately governed authority. But the exact binding that licenses the relation must remain recoverable.

Evidence-only recertification may preserve semantic claim identity. Meaning-bearing rebinding changes semantic identity.

### D04 — REJECT/BLOCKED normalization

Compaction is certified in principle; historical carrier retirement is not.

Required before retirement:

- `697/697_REJECT_SEMANTIC_PARITY`
- `15/15_BLOCKED_SEMANTIC_PARITY`

Parity must preserve exact candidate grain, rule/template, members, evidence/source scope, decisive reason, ceiling/prohibited inference, scope/generator defects, differently typed/corrected successor possibility, subset/retyping/globalization restrictions where present, successor/reopen obligations, blocker-specific evidence requirements, authority effect, and court/replay lineage.

`EXACT_CANDIDATE_REJECT` remains candidate-disposition authority only.

`BLOCKED` remains unresolved authority with reopen obligation, not false and not negative.

### D05 — shared policy factoring

`SHARED_POLICY_REF + PER_OBJECT_DELTA` is lossless only under exact semantic equivalence.

A weaker shared policy widens authority and is forbidden.

A stronger shared policy may be a lawful new current narrowing, but does not by itself prove lossless replacement of historical authority.

Historical edge-field retirement requires exact equivalence, mandatory fail-closed policy resolution for every relevant consumer, explicit per-object deviations, and proof that no omitted prose contains unique adjudicative capital.

If policy changes relation definition, policy version participates in semantic claim/schema identity. If it changes only permitted use, it belongs to authority/use-permission identity.

V557 may narrow producer permission; V557 does not own the producer semantic ceiling.
