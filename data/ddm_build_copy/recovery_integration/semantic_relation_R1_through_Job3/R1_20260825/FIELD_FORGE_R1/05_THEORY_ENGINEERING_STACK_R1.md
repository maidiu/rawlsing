# FIELD FORGE R1 — THEORY → ENGINEERING STACK

## Fixed-source law

No new survey/data-source acquisition. Every theoretical construct must be translated into the strongest lawful operation supported by the existing Rawlsing evidence; unsupported dimensions remain typed limits.

---

# 1. BOURDIEU — PRIMARY GEOMETRY

## Theoretical capital

Use seriously:

- social space;
- field;
- capital volume;
- capital composition;
- trajectory;
- economic/cultural/social/symbolic capital;
- field-specific valuation and capital conversion;
- habitus;
- hysteresis / habitus clivé;
- doxa;
- symbolic power;
- homology between social-position space and disposition/lifestyle space.

## Engineering translation with existing surveys

Construct **context-local position spaces** from governed indicators already present:

- income/material deprivation/assets;
- occupation/employment relation;
- education/credentials;
- parental origin where available;
- geography/urbanity;
- organizational embeddedness where available;
- religion/sect/ethnicity/race/caste as relational position markers where source semantics support that use;
- migration/mobility/language where present;
- other exact context-local resource/position variables.

Do not define one universal global `capital score`.

Prefer geometric-data-analysis / MCA-like relational geometry for categorical survey position variables. Treat axes/regions as **descriptive geometry**, not automatic class/field semantic authority.

Project attitude/disposition complexes as supplementary structure rather than using those same attitudes to define the position geometry and then claiming position explains them.

Output candidates:

```text
POSITION_SPACE
CAPITAL_COMPOSITION_PROFILE
POSITION_REGION
TRAJECTORY_PROXY (only where origin/temporal evidence exists)
POSITION↔DISPOSITION_HOMOLOGY
FIELD_SPECIFIC_VALUE_CANDIDATE
CAPITAL_CONVERSION_CANDIDATE
```

No candidate label becomes semantic authority automatically.

# 2. GRAMSCI — HEGEMONY / COMMON SENSE / RELATION OF FORCES

## Theoretical capital

Use:

- hegemony != numerical majority;
- civil society;
- common sense;
- contradictory consciousness;
- historical bloc;
- relation of forces;
- leadership/consent plus coercive/political power.

## Engineering translation

Derive a **multi-dimensional hegemonic footprint**, never one prevalence score.

Using only current/adopted evidence, combine dimensions such as:

```text
population prevalence of attitude complex
cross-position reach
cross-group diffusion
alignment with high-capital survey positions
alignment with politically empowered identities/groups where EPR/GeoEPR supplies authority
alignment with existing economic/institutional power indicators already adopted by Rawlsing
persistence across governed contexts/time where comparable
presence of organized counter-complexes
```

Output:

```text
HEGEMONIC_ALIGNMENT_PROFILE
SUBHEGEMONIC_ALIGNMENT_PROFILE
COUNTERHEGEMONIC_COMPLEX
COMMON_SENSE_REACH_PROFILE
```

Hard law:

```text
MAJORITY != HEGEMONY
MINORITY != SUBALTERN
SURVEY HIGH-INCOME TAIL != RULING CLASS
```

Where actual elite/power alignment is unobserved by existing adopted evidence, mark unavailable.

# 3. STUART HALL — ARTICULATION / CONJUNCTURE

## Theoretical capital

Use:

- identities/positions do not possess one necessary permanent political effect;
- relations are historically articulated;
- conjuncture matters;
- the same element can be linked into different formations across contexts.

## Engineering translation

Never freeze global additive rules such as:

```text
RELIGION=Shia → attitude X
EDUCATION=high → disposition Y
```

Instead key pressure footprints to exact context and allow interactions/configurations:

```text
identity × geography × education × economic position × period
```

Permit sign reversal and different attitude-complex destinations across countries/waves/fields.

Output:

```text
CONTEXTUAL_ARTICULATION_PROFILE
CONFIGURATION_DEPENDENT_PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT
CONJUNCTURAL_RELATION_CHANGE
```

Hall is the main anti-essentialism law for position variables.

# 4. BERNARD LAHIRE — PLURAL ACTOR / HETEROGENEOUS DISPOSITIONS

## Theoretical capital

Use as correction/stress-test to monolithic habitus:

- multiple socializations;
- heterogeneous/contradictory dispositions;
- context-specific activation;
- actors need not instantiate one coherent worldview.

## Engineering translation

Use same-respondent joint authority to preserve **co-possessed but potentially contradictory repertoires**.

Do not force every respondent/profile onto one latent ideology dimension.

Construct:

```text
DISPOSITION_REPERTOIRE
CO-POSSESSION_STRUCTURE
CONTRADICTORY_COMPLEX
CONTEXT_ACTIVATION_UNKNOWN
```

Surveys usually cannot observe which repertoire the same person activates in every life context. Therefore:

```text
CO-POSSESSED_REPERTOIRE
!= OBSERVED_CONTEXTUAL_ACTIVATION
```

PRE-V3 may expose possibility; V3 handles realized activation through biography/context.

- immigration;
- religion;
- gender;
- national identity;
- tolerance;
- trust;
- authority;
- work/morality;
- welfare/redistribution;
- corruption/fairness.

Manual semantic authority can identify boundary-relevant item complexes. Compute can recover joint boundary dispositions and their position-space distribution.

Output:

```text
SYMBOLIC_BOUNDARY_COMPLEX
MORAL_BOUNDARY_COMPLEX
BOUNDARY_POSITION_ASSOCIATION
BOUNDARY_HEGEMONIC_REACH
```

This is particularly important for empirically representing `middle-class morality` without assuming it from income alone.

# 6. ANN SWIDLER — CULTURAL TOOLKITS / REPERTOIRES

## Theoretical capital

Use:

- culture supplies repertoires/strategies rather than one deterministic value system;
- settled vs unsettled periods can alter how culture organizes action.

## Engineering translation

Treat many attitude complexes as **available repertoires** rather than fixed causal traits.

Repeated waves may reveal shifts in repertoire prevalence/association during conjunctural change if measurement comparability is proven.

Output:

```text
CULTURAL_REPERTOIRE_COMPLEX
REPERTOIRE_AVAILABILITY
REPERTOIRE_COEXISTENCE
TEMPORAL_REPERTOIRE_SHIFT
```

No survey response alone proves strategy-in-use.

# 7. BOLTANSKI / THÉVENOT — PLURAL ORDERS OF WORTH

## Theoretical capital

Use cautiously:

- people justify evaluations through multiple grammars of worth;
- moral evaluation is plural and context-dependent.

## Engineering translation

Only where existing survey semantics explicitly support judgment/justification dimensions, allow manual semantic authority to group compatible items into candidate justificatory grammars.

Do not infer a full `order of worth` merely from generic policy preference.

Potential output:

```text
JUSTIFICATION_GRAMMAR_CANDIDATE
MORAL_EVALUATION_COMPLEX
```

Likely secondary to Lamont for current survey feasibility.

# 8. RAYMOND WILLIAMS — DOMINANT / RESIDUAL / EMERGENT

## Theoretical capital

Use:

- dominant;
- residual;
- emergent;
- structure of feeling.

## Engineering translation

After exact temporal comparability, recurring attitude complexes may be descriptively typed by trajectory:

- high/stable/wide reach;
- declining but persistent;
- newly increasing/expanding;
- cohort-local or region-local emergence.

Do not let age gradient alone create `emergent` or `residual` semantics.

Potential output:

```text
TEMPORAL_COMPLEX_STATE
DOMINANT_CANDIDATE
RESIDUAL_CANDIDATE
EMERGENT_CANDIDATE
```

These labels should remain manual/governed semantic classifications over exact temporal evidence.

## Theoretical capital

Use:

- birth cohort alone does not make one generation consciousness;
- common historical exposure interacts with social position;
- multiple generation units can exist within the same cohort.

## Engineering translation

Where repeated surveys allow pseudo-cohort comparison:

```text
cohort × position configuration × attitude complex × period
```

Do not treat `young respondents` as one culture.

Output:

```text
COHORT_POSITION_COMPLEX
GENERATION_UNIT_CANDIDATE
AGE_PERIOD_COHORT_UNRESOLVED
```

No causal APC identification may be implied without a lawful model.

# 10. THORNTON / ECKERT / WILLIS / LAREAU — FIELD-SPECIFIC YOUTH / SOCIALIZATION LESSONS UNDER FIXED DATA

These theorists are retained **as ontology constraints**, not as justification for acquiring new datasets.

## Thornton

`subcultural capital` demonstrates that capital value is field-specific and conversion may be negative.

Engineering law:

```text
NO GLOBAL CULTURAL_CAPITAL SCALAR.
```

## Eckert

Youth social categories are locally produced through institutions, class trajectories, language/style, and peer organization.

Engineering law:

```text
AGE GROUP != UNIFORM CULTURAL FIELD.
```

## Willis

Local resistance can reproduce broader structural position.

Engineering law:

```text
COUNTERHEGEMONIC_LOCAL_IDENTITY
!= STRUCTURAL EMANCIPATION.
```

## Lareau

Family class position changes institutional capacities and socialization practices, not merely explicit attitudes.

Engineering implication:

Where current surveys provide parental origin/education/occupation/family context, preserve these as trajectory/socialization proxies. Where they do not, do not fabricate them.

For child persona generation, adult surveys chiefly constrain the **adult/family/social environment**. Child-specific peer-field details not observed by current sources remain V3 uncertainty rather than PRE-V3 pseudo-facts.

# 11. PRIMARY ENGINEERING SYNTHESIS

The strongest fixed-source architecture currently appears to be:

```text
EXISTING SURVEY / STRUCTURAL AUTHORITY
        ↓
POSITION / CAPITAL SPACE
        ↓
ATTITUDE / DISPOSITION SPACE
        ↓
SAME-RESPONDENT EMPIRICAL MOTIFS
        ↓
CONFIGURATION-DEPENDENT PRESSURE FOOTPRINTS
        ↓
HEGEMONIC / SUBHEGEMONIC ALIGNMENT PROFILES
        ↓
MANUAL STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
        ↓
MINIMAL CONTEXT-RESPONSIVE SOCIAL-WORLD GRAMMAR
        ↓
PRE-V3 POSITIONED-SUBJECT POSSIBILITY
        ↓
V3 REALIZED BIOGRAPHY
```

Use Bourdieu for geometry, Gramsci for power/common-sense relations, Hall for context-contingent articulation, Lahire/Swidler for plural repertoires, Lamont for boundary/moral differentiation, Williams/Mannheim for temporal/cohort structure, and youth/socialization theory primarily as constraints against overgeneralizing adult field structure.

No theorist gets to generate evidence the surveys do not contain.
