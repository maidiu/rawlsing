# FIELD FORGE R1 — THEORY AS ENGINEERING CONSTRAINT, NOT SOURCE EXPANSION

Theoretical work in FIELD FORGE has one practical purpose:

> Given the fixed Rawlsing source universe, design the strongest feasible relational/structural inference that preserves what the data actually support and refuses what they do not.

For every theorist or concept adopted, require an engineering translation:

```text
THEORETICAL CLAIM
→ observable proxy or empirical relation in existing sources
→ computation that may lawfully be performed
→ semantic judgment that must remain manual/governed
→ explicit unsupported residue
```

Do not adopt theoretical constructs merely because they are elegant. Prefer constructs whose empirical consequences can be partially observed in the current surveys and whose unobserved components can be cleanly represented as unavailable rather than hallucinated.

FIELD FORGE is therefore a theory-guided compression of the existing evidence, not a data-acquisition roadmap.
