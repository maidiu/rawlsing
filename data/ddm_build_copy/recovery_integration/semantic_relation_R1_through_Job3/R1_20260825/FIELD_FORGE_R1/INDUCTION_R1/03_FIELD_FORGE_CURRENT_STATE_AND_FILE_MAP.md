## Settled direction within FIELD FORGE vs still exploratory

These are not automatically HQ law, but FIELD FORGE should not casually reopen them without evidence:

- fixed-source constraint;
- stable analytical grammar + context-specific empirical manifestation;
- `strata` demoted from assumed primitive ontology;
- dual-space architecture: independent position/capital geometry and disposition/attitude geometry;
- homology/pressure relation studied after independent construction;
- configuration-dependent pressure footprints rather than universal demographic coefficients;
- pressure footprint != ultimate causal mechanism;
- hegemony != majority prevalence;
- field != cluster;
