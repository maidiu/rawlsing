# 06 — EMPIRICAL OBSERVATORY ENGINEERING

## Goal

PRE553 should expose an empirical social world rich enough to challenge and discipline structural theory before 553+ freezes a grammar.

FIELD FORGE currently prefers three separable empirical layers:

```text
1. POSITION / CAPITAL SPACE
2. DISPOSITION / ATTITUDE SPACE
3. POSITION ↔ DISPOSITION HOMOLOGY / PRESSURE TOPOLOGY
```

The separation is important. Do not construct class/position from the same attitudes that are later presented as outcomes of class/position by construction.

## A. Position Role Authority

Likely first new FIELD FORGE manual authority bank.

Question:

> What may this exact current survey variable lawfully do in this exact local position-space construction?

Candidate record fields:

```text
position_role_authority_id
source_family
wave/year
country/scope
variable_id
source_semantic_authority_ref
respondent_universe
routing/applicability_ref
role
geometry_status: ACTIVE | SUPPLEMENTARY | PASSIVE | EXCLUDED
role_scope
limitations
negative_assertions
provenance
review_status
version
```

Possible roles are descriptive and source/context specific, e.g.:

- economic-resource indicator;
- material-deprivation indicator;
- occupational-position indicator;
- educational-credential indicator;
- origin/parental-position proxy;
- subjective class/status self-placement;
- relational identity position;
- geography/urbanity position;
- migration/mobility proxy;
- organizational embeddedness candidate;
- not admissible for position geometry.

Do not build a universal ontology by matching column names.

Examples of explicit negative information:

```text
education -> ACTIVE_EDUCATIONAL_CREDENTIAL_POSITION
NOT automatically FULL_CULTURAL_CAPITAL

subjective class -> SUPPLEMENTARY_SELF_PLACEMENT
NOT objective class

sect -> RELATIONAL_IDENTITY_POSITION
NOT attitude
NOT resource
NOT universal causal coefficient
```

This is ideal for high-intelligence finite adjudication followed by deterministic consumption.

Candidate methods:

- sensitivity builds with alternative active/supplementary choices;
- model-based supplements only if their epistemic status remains distinct.

Required outputs should include:


Hard law:

The computer may calculate an axis. It may not decide that Axis 1 = class/economic capital merely because that interpretation sounds plausible.

## C. Independent Disposition / Attitude Space

Inputs are the governed semantic question universe and lawful respondent-backed joint/fracture/repertoire material.

Active objects can include:
- exact attitude variables where suitable;
- semantically governed directional/role objects;
- direct J2/J3 complexes;
- fracture/minority/co-signature objects;
- moral/symbolic boundary complexes;
- institution/religion/economic/political disposition components without forcing them into one ideology scalar.

Position variables should normally be supplementary in this space so the attitude geometry exists independently of the position geometry.

### Disposition Repertoire

A repertoire is not a bag of independently sampled attitudes and not necessarily a single coherent ideology.

It should preserve exact respondent-backed co-travel and tension:

```text
economically redistributive
+
religiously conservative
+
anti-institutional
+
nationally chauvinist
+
pro-labor
+
tolerant on another axis
```

If this complex is empirically real, do not clean it into a latent ideological cartoon.

Candidate properties:
- member semantic identities;
- exact J/coadministration proof where needed;
- support and scope;
- branch/minority structure;
- response morphology;
- internal empirical co-travel strength;
- contexts in which the complex fractures;
- typed uncertainty/underpowering.

## D. Position ↔ Disposition Homology / Pressure Footprint

The central descriptive object is not “X causes Y.” It is:

> Occupying positional configuration C is associated with a reproducible redistribution of probability over disposition space D within exact context K.

Call this a `POSITIONAL_PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT`.

Possible fields:

```text
footprint_id
context
position_configuration_ref
comparison_configuration_ref
position_space_ref
disposition_object/ref
exact respondent scope
direct support
probability/distribution difference
heterogeneity
minority/counterpattern
uncertainty
evidence tier
model dependence if any
routing/J/provenance refs
```

Hard:

```text
PRESSURE FOOTPRINT != CAUSAL MECHANISM
ASSOCIATION != SOCIOLOGICAL WHY
```

For persona generation, the footprint can still be enormously useful because it gives lawful configuration-dependent priors without claiming an ultimate causal explanation.

## E. Pressure Coupling / Articulation Footprint

This represents “inter-pressure” more precisely:

> the footprint associated with A changes inside different states/positions of B.

Canonical shape:

```text
Δ disposition associated with education
within South × Shia
!=
Δ disposition associated with education
within South × Sunni
```

This can reveal sign changes, destination-complex changes, magnitude changes, heterogeneity changes, or the emergence/disappearance of minority branches.

The interaction statistic is evidence. The sociological label is separate.

## F. Sparse-configuration evidence ladder

Multiway configurations get thin quickly. Every output must carry provenance tier:

```text
E0 DIRECT_EMPIRICAL
E1 DIRECT_BUT_UNDERPOWERED
E2 PARTIALLY_POOLED_MODEL_ESTIMATE
E3 LAWFUL_POSITION_OR_PROXY_WALKBACK
E4 MODEL_EXTRAPOLATION
```

Never collapse these into one number.

If the persona packet says `P(complex)=.42`, it must also say whether .42 came from direct respondents, partial pooling, lawful proxy/walkback, or extrapolation.

Hard:

`GEOMETRIC NEARNESS != LAWFUL SOCIOLOGICAL PROXY`

Position geometry may nominate candidate neighborhoods; only a governed proxy/walkback authority may authorize substitution.

## G. Empirical Motif Atlas

Before structural ontology freeze, produce many richly described local empirical motifs.

Example shape:


The motif atlas is what lets theory confront actual diversity rather than build a beautiful ontology from memory.
