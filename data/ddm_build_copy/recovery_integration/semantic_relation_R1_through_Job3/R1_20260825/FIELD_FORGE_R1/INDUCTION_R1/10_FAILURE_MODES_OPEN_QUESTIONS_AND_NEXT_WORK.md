# 10 — FAILURE MODES, OPEN QUESTIONS, AND NEXT WORK

## Failure modes FIELD FORGE must actively reject

### Source/authority failures

- inventing new source meaning from column names;
- treating source presence as respondent applicability;
- inferring J/coadministration from shared file, shared label, adjacency, or historical grouping;
- using historical 2,499/15,990 carrier counts as current denominator/authority;
- manufacturing measurement portability from similar wording, common scales, harmonization, or geometry;
- using geometry to authorize a proxy relation;
- collapsing routed nonapplicable, not asked, refusal, don't know, missing, and unresolved routing.

### Geometry failures

- `Axis 1 = economic capital` by intuition;
- letting the block with more questions dominate social ontology;
- treating rare modalities as intrinsically important because they distort inertia;
- treating one MCA build as natural truth instead of testing active/supplementary/block choices;
- combining position and attitude variables into one space and then claiming one explains the other;
- treating a cluster as a class, field, formation, or ideology;
- comparing raw coordinates across surveys without portability authority.

### Sociological failures

- reinstating nine strata because the vocabulary is familiar;
- treating class as scalar income/SES;
- treating education as full cultural capital;
- treating demographic identity as a universal causal coefficient;
- treating majority opinion as hegemony;
- treating minority status as subalternity;
- inferring individual attitudes from EPR/GeoEPR group-power status;
- treating local high capital as globally high capital;
- assuming capital converts positively across fields;
- forcing contradictions into one latent ideology;
- calling a correlation/interaction a mechanism, mediation, buffering, amplification, or causal pressure without separate authority;
- naming a descriptive mismatch `misrecognition` before theory admission.

### Modeling/provenance failures

- partial pooling presented as direct empirical proof;
- underpowered direct cells silently replaced with model estimates;
- extrapolation presented as a respondent-backed motif;
- missing authority treated as empirical falsity;
- sampling from many independent marginal probabilities to fabricate a “joint worldview”;
- failing to propagate model/evidence tier into persona generation;
- hiding uncertainty to make generated people seem coherent.

### PRE-V3/V3 failures

- population fact → individual belief;
- pressure footprint → actor exposure;
- actor exposure → internalization;
- demographic profile → realized biography;
- disposition repertoire as a bag of traits without edge topology;
- Enneagram type assigned first and used to force internal relations;
- assuming a realized relation among dispositions because the population-level items co-travel;
- making local child/peer-field claims from absent adult-survey evidence.

### Genealogy failures

- archaeology amnesia: declaring function absent because current branch/file lacks it;
- archaeology necromancy: resurrecting historical mechanism because it once carried a real function;
- promoting a worker/prototype because tests pass without current admission;
- letting FIELD FORGE design notes masquerade as adopted HQ law.

## Open questions that require deliberate work

### Position Role Authority

- What is the exact adjudication grain: variable × country × wave × form/module × target geometry?
- Which roles are sufficiently reusable to be an ontology versus merely labels in a specific court?
- Can some variables lawfully hold multiple roles in different builds?
- How should identity variables alternate between active and supplementary status?
- What review/hostile-test process is needed before a manual authority bank is trusted?

### Continuous/ordinal governance

- When should age/income/education remain ordinal/continuous?
- When can source-native categories be active directly?
- What partitioning, if any, can be lawful without inventing class structure?
- Can geometric methods consume continuous supplements while preserving source meaning?

### Geometry governance

- When MCA vs specific MCA vs MFA/block balancing?
- How should survey weights/design enter geometry and uncertainty?
- What sensitivity differences make a space unstable enough to withhold interpretation?
- How should rare modalities be treated?
- How do we prevent routed questionnaire architecture from becoming “social structure”?

### Disposition space/repertoire

- What objects are active: single items, semantic families, J objects, fracture branches, motifs?
- How do we prevent duplicated/overlapping questions from overweighting a semantic domain?
- What is the completeness standard for candidate repertoire discovery?
- How do we preserve contradiction without treating every random combination as meaningful?

### Pressure footprint

- What is the lawful comparison neighborhood in relational position space?
- When exact cells are sparse, what partial-pooling model is acceptable?
- How should nonlinearity and high-order articulation be searched without combinatorial false discovery?
- What held-out/replication rules establish a stable motif?
- What is the difference between configuration-specific description and overfitted interaction hunting?

### Cross-context comparison

- What structural signatures can be compared without claiming coordinate equivalence?
- Which comparisons require measurement portability?
- Can homology/topology classes travel while raw coordinates do not?
- How should an interpretation court distinguish recurrent grammar from superficially similar geometry?

### Field/hegemony/capital

- What minimum evidence makes `FIELD_CANDIDATE` worth semantic review?
- What evidence establishes partial autonomy?
- What counts as differential valuation rather than mere subgroup prevalence?
- How should capital conversion/devaluation be measured from available survey evidence?
- Which hegemony-profile dimensions are mandatory versus optional/unobserved?
- When is “subhegemony” actually useful to persona generation rather than a grand-theory label?

### Temporal/trajectory

- How much can parental education/occupation, cohort, migration, or subjective status support trajectory/hysteresis claims?
- What remains merely current position with origin proxy?
- Which temporal claims require portability authority that we do not yet have?

### V3 personal relational field

- What is the smallest useful disposition-edge ontology?
- Are edges generated from events/relationships, from cognitive/narrative processes, from population priors, or some combination?
- How much population evidence can inform edge priors without laundering co-travel into individual motivation?
- How do edges change over time and situation?
- How do conscious contradictions differ from compartmentalized relations in the generative model?
- What hostile tests distinguish two actors with same nodes/different edges?
- Does Enneagram affinity add generative value beyond the relational topology itself?
- Can emergent attractor dynamics be represented without typological determinism?
