# 08 — PRE553 → 553+ BOOTSTRAP AND READINESS

## Why a readiness gate is necessary

FIELD FORGE exists partly because Rawlsing's structural vocabulary became richer than the explicit generative machinery beneath some of it.

Terms such as strata, structural pressure, mediation, buffering, amplification, deflection, blockage, rerouting, temporal rupture, attractor prerequisites, and higher-order pressure topology can be valuable requirements while still lacking a fully specified producer.

The response is not to abandon the vision and not to code a heuristic under each noun. It is to require enough lawful empirical morphology before a structural ontology is allowed to crystallize.

Hard laws:

```text
DO NOT FREEZE FINAL STRUCTURAL ONTOLOGY FROM THEORY ALONE.
DO NOT LET UNSUPERVISED COMPUTE FREEZE IT EITHER.
```

## Productive interpretation of PRE553 / 553+

FIELD FORGE currently proposes this functional distinction:

```text
PRE553
= make the empirical social world rich, exact, respondent-backed,
  context-scoped, and lawful enough to be interpreted

553+
= transform/admit that empirical world into structural knowledge
```

This is not a claim that every historical file boundary should survive. It is a functional readiness seam.

## Bootstrap sequence

### FF-B0 — Current observability matrix

Across current CLOSED_35 contexts, enumerate what the source actually observes and at what semantic/routing scope:

- economic resources/deprivation;
- occupation/employment;
- education/credentials;
- origin/parental position;
- subjective class/status;
- geography/urbanity;
- religion/sect;
- ethnicity/race/caste;
- migration/mobility;
- organizational/media exposure;
- attitudinal batteries;
- other current variables relevant to position/disposition.

Do not infer roles yet.

### FF-B1 — Position Role Authority pilot

Manually adjudicate variable roles in a deliberately diverse bounded subset using exact codebooks and current semantic/routing authority.

This is a semantic court, not an automated classifier.

### FF-B2 — Design contexts + held-out contexts

Choose multiple current contexts to maximize variation in:

- survey family;
- national/regional social structure;
- religious/ethnic positional systems;
- observability pattern;
- capital indicators;
- attitude battery structure.

Reserve current contexts not used to design the grammar.

The held-out attack is essential. If an ontology built on GSS requires special pleading when it reaches Arab Barometer or LiTS, fix the ontology rather than patching the source.

### FF-B3 — Local position spaces

Build multiple lawful local position geometries with diagnostics/sensitivity builds.

Do not name axes automatically.

### FF-B4 — Local disposition spaces/repertoires

Use semantic/J/fracture machinery to build independent disposition geometry and preserve contradictions/minority complexes.

### FF-B5 — Pressure footprints + coupling

Map configuration-dependent redistribution between the spaces. Distinguish direct evidence, underpowered cells, partial pooling, lawful walkback, and extrapolation.

### FF-B6 — Hegemony / field / capital-conversion evidence profiles

Compute only those dimensions actually observable. Leave missing dimensions typed as unobserved.

### FF-B7 — Empirical motif atlas

Produce a rich atlas across contexts. This is the empirical material on which structural concepts should be tested.

### FF-B8 — Structural grammar court

Use the strongest relevant theory—Bourdieu, Gramsci, Hall, Lahire, Swidler, Lamont, Williams/Mannheim and other bounded aids—to propose the minimal grammar that represents the motif atlas without forcing unlike contexts into one scheme.

The court must separate:

- descriptive empirical pattern;
- theory-supported interpretation;
- unresolved candidate;
- unobservable proposition.

### FF-B9 — Held-out attack

Apply the grammar to held-out current source contexts.

Failure cases are evidence about the ontology.

Do not repair them with hidden exceptions unless the exception itself can be formulated as a principled context-sensitive rule and survives further held-out testing.

## Readiness conditions before ontology freeze

The exact numeric threshold remains an open design question, but the qualitative gate requires:

1. lawful semantic respondent substrate for the design contexts;
2. adequate routing/applicability and J/coadministration for operations actually used;
3. Position Role Authority with independent review;
4. multiple stable local position spaces with sensitivity diagnostics;
5. multiple stable local disposition/repertoire spaces;
6. direct pressure-footprint examples plus hostile cases where additive models fail;
7. explicit sparse/model provenance;
8. diverse motif atlas across families/contexts;
9. held-out contexts not used to derive the candidate grammar;
10. explicit observability-limit map;
11. a persona canary showing that the richer PRE-V3 packet materially improves plausible, non-stereotyped joint-worldview generation;
12. a structural court that can state what remains unresolved rather than filling every slot.

## Interaction with current J/routing closure

FIELD FORGE does not have to idle until every global routing/J residual is closed.

It may prototype on exact bounded subsets whose needed source semantics, routing, respondent scope, and J authority are already lawful.

But:

```text
BOUNDED EXPLORATION ON ADMITTED SUBSET
!=
PRODUCT-WIDE ONTOLOGY FREEZE
```

Final freeze requires enough diverse lawful substrate to ensure that missing authority is not shaping the theory by accident.

## Required-function perspective

Do not equate “every candidate disposition has a typed status” with “structural parity is ready.” Ask which unresolved functions are actually required by current governed downstream consumers.

Likewise, do not block useful FIELD FORGE learning on an unresolved source object that no prototype/consumer requires.

Critical path should be function-grain, not count fetish.
