# 01 — RAWLSING CONSTITUTION AND JURISDICTION

## The target object

Rawlsing is trying to construct a learned world capable of representing how social reality presses upon persons without mistaking pressure for destiny.

The central noncollapses are binding design constraints:

```text
SOURCE-NATIVE AUTHORITY != EMPIRICAL DERIVATIVE
EMPIRICAL DERIVATIVE != STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION != ACTOR STATE

POPULATION FACT != POSITIONED-SUBJECT POSSIBILITY
POSITIONED-SUBJECT POSSIBILITY != INDIVIDUAL PREDICTION
INDIVIDUAL PREDICTION != V3 REALIZED LIFE
```

PRE-V3 should be the thickest actor-independent learned account of the world pressing on a possible person. V3 is the realized developmental life: encounter, memory, attachment, wounds, recognition, socialization, action, resistance/accommodation, contradiction, interpretation, and time.

FIELD FORGE operates primarily on the PRE-V3 structural/world-model boundary and on the contract by which that material can later become input to V3. It must not infer realized actor state from population structure.

## Dva / Trii / Chetiri jurisdiction

The earlier architecture genealogy matters because FIELD FORGE sits near the seam those programs were attempting to clarify.

### Dva

Dva carries durable downstream functional capital: direct-DAG reuse, learned products, structural transformation, downstream consumption of empirical authority, and functional inheritance across stages.

Dva's downstream ideas can remain valuable even when its old assumptions about upstream empirical authority need reconstruction.

### Trii

Trii became the upstream empirical-authority / PRE553 reconstruction program. Its core question was who owns semantic truth and how exact governed empirical evidence becomes learned-world objects without reconstruction by convenience.

Trii is especially relevant to FIELD FORGE because its mature design recognized a real seam between empirical discovery and structural admission.

### Chetiri

Chetiri properly challenges persistence, caching, materialization, compiler topology, replay, and physical execution strategy. It does not have jurisdiction to rewrite source semantics, empirical truth, structural truth, generated-person meaning, or V3 actor state.

### Architecture competition law

Physical architectures may compete only after they are shown to perform the same semantic work. A candidate cannot win because it silently discards an expensive semantic distinction.

## C4/C5 information conservation

FIELD FORGE must inherit two broader laws:

`FIELD_PRESENT != AUTHORITY_ROLE_PRESERVED`

Information conservation requires not only values but provenance, authority role, consumer access, and dependency/invalidation lineage.

Maintenance is also part of conservation:

```text
EVIDENCE
→ CANDIDATE AUTHORITY
→ ADJUDICATION
→ CORRECTION / REVISION
→ INVALIDATION
→ RECOMPUTATION
→ VALIDATION
→ ATOMIC PROMOTION
→ CURRENT RELEASE

```

A beautiful frozen social-space model that cannot be lawfully recomputed when source authority changes is not a finished Rawlsing object.

## FIELD FORGE jurisdiction rule

FIELD FORGE may:
- discover missing conceptual producers;
- design prospective contracts;
- prototype on lawfully admitted bounded subsets;
- compare theoretical grammars against actual survey observability;
- specify new candidate semantic authority banks where runtime inference would be inferior;
- design hostile tests and readiness courts.

FIELD FORGE may not:
- promote itself to current HQ architecture;
- reinterpret current survey authority without its source/semantic court;
- manufacture routing, coadministration, J membership, or measurement portability;
- infer realized-person state from population regularities;
- treat historical implementation as current authority without succession.
