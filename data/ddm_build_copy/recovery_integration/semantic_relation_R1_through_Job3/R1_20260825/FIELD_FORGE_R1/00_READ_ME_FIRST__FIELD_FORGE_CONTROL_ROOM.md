# 1. THE IMMEDIATE CONCEPTUAL CORRECTION

Treat the historical concept of **nine strata** as provisional.

It may ultimately survive as a useful projection, but it must not be assumed to be the primitive ontology of the social world.

Current working hypothesis:

```text
STRUCTURAL WORLD
    = overlapping historically specific fields/formations
    + relational positions
    + resources/capitals
    + field-specific valuation of those capitals
    + attitude/disposition complexes
    + pressure footprints linking position to changed attitude probability
    + field-to-field capital conversion/devaluation
    + hegemonic/subhegemonic/counterhegemonic alignment
    + temporal/conjunctural transformation
```

A `STRATUM` may later be a derived or lossy view of this topology where actual hierarchical layering is useful.

Hard provisional law:

```text
NESTEDNESS MAY BE A REAL RELATION.
NESTEDNESS IS NOT ASSUMED TO BE THE MASTER GEOMETRY.
```

Example: a high-demand religious movement may genuinely be contained within a wider evangelical formation, but class position, racial hegemony, peer subculture, university cultural capital, media ecosystems, geopolitics, and professional fields cut across that containment rather than nesting neatly around it.

# 2. WHAT RAWLSING ACTUALLY NEEDS FOR PERSONA GENERATION

Do **not** require PRE-V3 to solve the ultimate sociological causal `why` before it may model patterned social determination.

Distinguish:

```text
EMPIRICAL PRESSURE FOOTPRINT
    = a reproducible conditional redistribution of probability
      over attitudes/dispositions when social position/configuration changes,
      under exact governed source/respondent scope.

STRUCTURAL PRESSURE MECHANISM
    = a stronger sociological explanation of why that footprint exists.
```

For persona generation, the first object is already extremely valuable.

If respondents occupying configuration C1 have a reliably different **joint attitude distribution** from respondents occupying C2, that is a lawful empirical fact even when Rawlsing does not possess authority to explain the full causal machinery producing it.

Therefore:

```text
SURVEY FOOTPRINT != CAUSAL MECHANISM

LACK OF CAUSAL-MECHANISM AUTHORITY
!=
PERMISSION TO IGNORE A REAL EMPIRICAL PRESSURE FOOTPRINT
```

For adult persona generation the practical target is approximately:

```text
P(JOINT ATTITUDE / DISPOSITION COMPLEX
  | governed social-position configuration,
    context,
    period,
    source scope)
```

For child/developmental generation, PRE-V3 supplies the **possible pressure landscape**; V3 remains responsible for which pressures the particular actor encounters, notices, understands, accepts, resists, transforms, or carries forward through biography.

Hard existing Rawlsing law remains:

```text
POPULATION FACT
!= POSITIONED-SUBJECT POSSIBILITY
!= INDIVIDUAL PREDICTION
!= V3 REALIZED LIFE
```

# 3. TWO EMPIRICAL SPACES, NOT ONE MAGIC GRAPH

FIELD FORGE should test a two-space architecture inspired strongly by Bourdieu.

## A. POSITION / CAPITAL SPACE

Answers:

> Where is this respondent/configuration relationally located?

Candidate active information includes, where source-authorized:

- economic resources and deprivation;
- occupation / employment relation;
- education / credentials;
- parental education/occupation where available;
- household assets / housing;
- urban/rural or regional location;
- ethnicity / race / caste / sect / religion where these are social-position variables;
- migration / mobility / language;
- organizational embeddedness;
- social-network capital where genuinely measured;
- political-power or institutional-power alignment from external authority;
- other context-specific capital proxies.

Do **not** treat every demographic attribute as a capital.

Do **not** assume one variable has the same capital value in every context.

Core relation:

```text
VALUE(resource_or_disposition R)
    = field-relative
    + context-relative
    + historically relative
```

## B. ATTITUDE / DISPOSITION SPACE

Answers:

> Which beliefs, loyalties, moral judgments, institutional trusts, political orientations, religious dispositions, fears, hopes, tastes, boundaries, and other attitudes actually co-travel among respondents?

This consumes the PRE553 source-semantic, routing, joint, fracture, and response-morphology machinery.

It should preserve:

- same-respondent joint structure;
- minority complexes;
- contradictory complexes;
- response morphology;
- conditional fracture;
- co-signature patterns;
- direct higher-order structure where governed;
- uncertainty and non-comparability.

## C. HOMOLOGY / PRESSURE MAP

Answers:

> How does position in A redistribute probability over B?

This is the key prospective PRE-V3 structural product.

Do not define position space from the same attitude variables and then claim position explains those attitudes without an explicit circularity court.

Prefer:

```text
POSITION/CAPITAL SPACE
        ↕
EMPIRICAL HOMOLOGY / PRESSURE FOOTPRINT
        ↕
ATTITUDE/DISPOSITION SPACE
```

This mirrors the strongest part of Bourdieusian empirical practice: social position and the space of lifestyles/dispositions are related, but they are analytically distinguishable.

# 4. BOURDIEU IS A SERIOUS ENGINEERING BACKBONE

Use Bourdieu as more than vocabulary.

Relevant primitives:

## Social space

Model position relationally, not as a fixed income bucket. Important axes in Bourdieusian work include:

- **capital volume**;
- **capital composition** (e.g. economic vs cultural capital);
- **trajectory** — change in volume/composition through time.

## Field

A field is not simply a topic category. A field is a relatively structured arena with stakes, positions, forms of capital, distinctions, and conversion rules.

A resource may be valuable in one field and worthless or negatively marked in another.

```text
SUBCULTURAL CAPITAL IN FIELD A
may convert poorly or negatively into FIELD B.
```

This is essential for youth, school, cultural, religious, professional, political, and elite contexts.

## Capital

Do not assign one global cultural-capital scalar.

At minimum distinguish the possibility of:

- economic capital;
- cultural capital;
- social capital;
- symbolic capital;
- field-specific/subcultural capital;
- political/institutional power access as an externally grounded resource relation.

The conversion rate is itself a relation to be estimated/governed.

## Habitus

Habitus is a durable, embodied system of dispositions generated by position and trajectory, not a questionnaire label. PRE-V3 may model population-level **dispositional possibility**. It may not claim to have observed the realized habitus of a particular actor.

## Hysteresis / cleft habitus

Social mobility or rapid field change can leave dispositions out of register with a new position. This matters greatly for generation: moving upward does not imply instantly acquiring the dispositions statistically typical of the destination field.

## Doxa / symbolic power

Dominant classifications can function as neutral/common-sense descriptions of reality. This is one reason prevalence alone cannot establish hegemony.

## Method: MCA / geometric data analysis

FIELD FORGE should examine Multiple Correspondence Analysis / geometric data analysis as a primary **geometry-discovery** instrument, especially before latent-class bucketing.

Do not mistake MCA axes for semantic authority. They are descriptive geometry requiring interpretation.

Strong default experiment:

```text
ACTIVE VARIABLES:
    governed position/capital indicators

SUPPLEMENTARY VARIABLES:
    attitudes/dispositions/identities not used to construct the geometry

OUTPUT:
    relational social space + projected disposition structure
```

This avoids prematurely forcing respondents into discrete classes and better preserves relational position.

Latent classes may still be useful as a derived view, but should not automatically become ontology.

# 5. THEORISTS THAT DIRECTLY CONTRIBUTE ENGINEERING PRIMITIVES

The following are not decorative references. Each supplies a concrete design warning or object.

## Pierre Bourdieu

Read/consult around `Distinction`, `The Forms of Capital`, `The Logic of Practice`, `Homo Academicus`, `The State Nobility`, `Pascalian Meditations`, social-space/field papers.

Engineering contribution:

- position is relational;
- capital is multidimensional;
- capital value is field-specific;
- trajectory matters;
- social and lifestyle/disposition spaces can display structural homology;
- doxa and symbolic power matter;
- objective class position is not identical with conscious class identity.

## Brigitte Le Roux / Henry Rouanet and empirical Bourdieusian geometric-data-analysis tradition

Engineering contribution:

- correspondence/MCA machinery for constructing relational spaces;
- active vs supplementary variables;
- contribution/quality diagnostics;
- avoid arbitrary Euclidean feature engineering where categorical relational geometry is appropriate.

Also examine `Culture, Class, Distinction` (Bennett, Savage, Silva, Warde, Gayo-Cal, Wright) for a post-Bourdieu large-survey implementation and its use of MCA.

## Gramsci

Key objects:

- hegemony as leadership + domination, not numerical majority;
- civil society;
- common sense;
- contradictory consciousness;
- historical bloc;
- relation of forces;
- organic intellectuals.

Engineering contribution:

`HEGEMONY` should be a multi-source profile linking population attitudes to actual power/institutional alignment rather than a prevalence score.

## Stuart Hall

Key object: **articulation / conjuncture**.

Engineering contribution:

No identity/resource/disposition has one necessary permanent effect. Connections are historically produced, maintained, severed, and recombined.

This strongly supports context-specific position configurations over universal additive identity coefficients.

## Raymond Williams

Key objects:

- dominant / residual / emergent;
- structure of feeling.

Engineering contribution:

Potential temporal/cohort descriptors for attitude/disposition complexes after exact comparability is established. These should not be generated from age gradients alone.

## Bernard Lahire

Use as a stress test even if the core ontology remains orthodox Bourdieusian.

Key object: the **plural actor** produced by multiple contexts of socialization, carrying heterogeneous dispositions that activate differently by situation.

Engineering contribution:

Do not force one globally coherent respondent ideology when empirical joint structure supports multiple context-dependent repertoires.

## Michèle Lamont

Key objects:

- symbolic boundaries;
- moral boundaries;
- social boundaries;
- dignity / evaluation / recognition.

Engineering contribution:

Middle-class or working-class morality cannot be reduced to economic position or highbrow taste. Attitude complexes may encode principles of worth and exclusion that operate as independent cultural resources/boundaries.

## Boltanski & Thévenot

Key object: plural **orders of worth / justification grammars**.

Engineering contribution:

A respondent may justify judgments through multiple incompatible moral grammars. Survey attitude complexes can potentially reveal recurring justification regimes without forcing them into one left-right axis.

## Ann Swidler

Key object: cultural **toolkits / repertoires** and different operation of culture in settled vs unsettled periods.

Engineering contribution:

Treat some attitudes as available repertoires rather than fixed internal causes. Historical disruption may alter which repertoire is activated.

## Sarah Thornton

Key object: **subcultural capital**.

Engineering contribution:

A person can possess enormous symbolic/cultural capital inside a bounded youth/subcultural field while that bundle converts poorly or negatively into an elite surrounding field.

This is an explicit warning against one global cultural-capital score.

## Penelope Eckert

Read `Jocks and Burnouts` and communities-of-practice work.

Engineering contribution:

Adolescent social categories can be generated through an institutional setting, peer organization, class trajectory, language/style, and local symbolic opposition. School-age fields can have their own social structure.

## William Corsaro

Key object: **peer culture / interpretive reproduction**.

Engineering contribution:

Children do not merely absorb adult culture linearly. They collectively construct peer cultures while reproducing and transforming wider structures.

## Paul Willis

Key object: counter-school culture / resistance reproducing class structure.

Engineering contribution:

Counterhegemonic identity is not automatically structurally emancipatory. A locally oppositional field can reproduce the position it resists.

## Annette Lareau

Key objects: classed childrearing logics, institutional navigation, concerted cultivation vs accomplishment of natural growth.

Engineering contribution:

Family position alters children's accumulation of institutional capacities, language practices, entitlement, and navigation—not simply their explicit opinions.

## Karl Mannheim

Key objects: generation location, generation actuality, generation units.

Engineering contribution:

Age/cohort is not one homogeneous cultural field. Common historical exposure interacts with class and other position to generate different generation units.

## Beverley Skeggs / Sam Friedman / mobility literature

Engineering contribution:

- not every valued practice converts equally across classed fields;
- upward mobility can produce mismatch, emotional cost, cleft habitus, and incomplete conversion;
- recognition of `merit` itself is socially structured.

## Richard Peterson / omnivore literature

Engineering warning:

Do not fossilize `highbrow taste = cultural capital`. Elite distinction can change historically and may operate through selective omnivorousness rather than old canonical forms.

# 6. CHILDREN ARE A SPECIAL SOURCE PROBLEM

The current nine-family Rawlsing survey substrate is overwhelmingly adult public-opinion data.

That means it can give PRE-V3:

- parental/adult position distributions;
- adult common-sense/disposition distributions;
- family/religious/class/political context;
- age/cohort differences among adults or young adults.

It cannot by itself give a sufficiently rich empirical account of **school-age peer fields**, child-specific subcultural capital, school belonging, peer status, youth taste hierarchies, or child institutional experience.

Therefore create a prospective gap:

```text
FF-GAP-CHILD-001
CURRENT ADULT BAROMETER SUBSTRATE
DOES NOT SUFFICIENTLY OBSERVE CHILD/ADOLESCENT PEER FIELDS.
```

Candidate future acquisitions to evaluate rather than assume:

- OECD PISA student/context questionnaires;
- HBSC (Health Behaviour in School-aged Children);
- ICCS (International Civic and Citizenship Education Study);
- TIMSS/PIRLS student/home/school questionnaires;
- nationally specific youth longitudinal surveys where needed.

PISA is especially interesting because it can join parental education/occupation/home possessions, school context, belonging/aspiration, and adolescent outcomes. It is not a substitute for cultural ethnography, but it may provide a cross-national positional substrate much closer to the actual developmental target than adult barometers.

Do not ingest any such source until source authority, licensing, semantic role, and product need are adjudicated.

# 8. WHY HEGEMONY MUST BE A PROFILE, NOT A PREVALENCE SCORE

Hard law:

```text
HEGEMONIC != MAJORITY
SUBALTERN != MINORITY
```

A worldview can be numerically common but institutionally subordinate; a demographically small group can dominate political/economic institutions.

Proposed object:

```text
HEGEMONIC_ALIGNMENT_PROFILE {
    context
    attitude_complex_or_formation

    population_prevalence
    cross-position_reach

    political_power_alignment
    economic_power_alignment
    institutional_alignment

    elite_position_alignment
    symbolic/defaultness evidence
    aspirational_gradient evidence

    field_specific_dominance
    cross_field_capital_convertibility

    counterhegemonic_complexes
    subhegemonic_complexes

    evidence_refs
    uncertainty
    unavailable_dimensions
}
```

No requirement that every dimension be observed in every context.

No scalar `hegemony_score` unless a later court establishes a lawful estimand and proves that collapsing dimensions preserves meaning.

Potential sources:

- weighted survey prevalence / joint attitude complexes;
- EPR political-power access by ethnic group;
- GeoEPR spatial group distribution;
- external economic/institutional authority;
- survey high-capital-position distributions where sample support is adequate;
- state/religion, party, legislative, institutional, media or ownership datasets where explicitly adopted.

Important limitation:

National probability surveys generally under-sample the very wealthy and may top-code or weakly observe elite networks. Do not call a rich survey tail `the ruling class` without external authority.

Potential new authority gap:

```text
FF-GAP-POWER-001
FIELD_OF_POWER / ELITE_ALIGNMENT AUTHORITY
may require external data or manual country-context adjudication.
```

# 9. SUBHEGEMONY AND FIELD-SPECIFIC CAPITAL

Working definition candidate:

```text
SUBHEGEMONIC FORMATION
    = high local symbolic/common-sense dominance
      within bounded field F
      + limited, asymmetric, or negative conversion
        into a broader/more powerful field G.
```

Example pattern:

```text
RESOURCE BUNDLE R
    high value in suburban youth field
    low or negative value in elite international-school field
```

This is not a joke example; it is the direct object that Sarah Thornton's `subcultural capital` and Bourdieu's field-specific valuation warn us to preserve.

Engineering candidate:

```text
CAPITAL_CONVERSION_RELATION {
    resource_or_disposition
    source_field
    target_field
    context
    period

    source_field_value
    target_field_value
    conversion_direction
    conversion_strength
    devaluation_or_stigma

    evidence
    uncertainty
}
```

Do not assume conversion is positive.

# 10. EMPIRICAL PRESSURE FOOTPRINT: FIRST ENGINEERING SKETCH

For a governed attitude complex A and social-position configuration C:

1. define exact respondent/risk-set universe;
2. apply weights/design law;
3. preserve same-respondent joint semantics;
4. estimate the distribution of A under C;
5. compare against lawful neighboring/reference positions C';
6. measure distributional displacement and non-additive interaction;
7. preserve minority/counter-patterns;
8. emit evidence, not causal interpretation.

Potential descriptive statistics/evidence tools include:

- conditional distributions;
- total-variation / Jensen-Shannon style distributional distance;
- conditional mutual information;
- exact contingency/log-linear structure;
- multilevel or hierarchical models where sampling/context law allows;
- interaction surfaces;
- fracture-map branch shifts;
- direct J2/J3 configuration differences;
- temporal recurrence where comparability exists.

None of these statistics has permission to emit semantic labels such as `HEGEMONIC`, `MEDIATES`, `BUFFER`, `CLASS`, `FIELD`, or `CAUSES` by itself.

Candidate raw object:

```text
POSITIONAL_PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT {
    position_configuration
    attitude_complex
    exact_context
    exact_scope

    respondent_support
    weighted_distribution
    comparison_positions
    displacement_profile
    interaction_profile
    heterogeneity
    minority_structure
    temporal_recurrence_if_lawful

    routing_refs
    joint_refs
    semantic_refs
    provenance
    uncertainty
}
```

# 11. EMPIRICAL MOTIFS: DATA MAY SURPRISE THEORY WITHOUT INVENTING ONTOLOGY

```text

    stable joint attitude complex
    repeatedly fractured by configuration C
    minority branch B
    recurs across k governed scopes
```

That is an empirical discovery.

The machine may nominate it for interpretation.

The machine does **not** automatically declare:

- sectarian embedding;
- class fraction;
- field;
- habitus;
- hegemony;
- cultural capital;
- mediation;
- causal mechanism.
