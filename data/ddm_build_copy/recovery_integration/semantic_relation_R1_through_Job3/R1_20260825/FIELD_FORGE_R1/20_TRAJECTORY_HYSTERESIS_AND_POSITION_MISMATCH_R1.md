# FIELD FORGE R1 — TRAJECTORY / HYSTERESIS / POSITION-MISMATCH EVIDENCE

Status: **EXPLORATORY / NON-BINDING / PROSPECTIVE PRE-V3 PRODUCT**

Some current Rawlsing surveys contain origin/current-position evidence such as parental education, parental occupation, migration origin, prior class/status, or subjective current class.

FIELD FORGE should use those sources to represent **trajectory-sensitive possibility** without pretending cross-sectional survey data contain a full life history.

---

# 1. HARD LAW

```text
CURRENT POSITION
!=
ORIGIN POSITION
!=
TRAJECTORY
!=
REALIZED HABITUS.
```

Where origin/current evidence exists, Rawlsing may represent mismatch and mobility possibility.

It may not infer the actor's subjective experience of mobility.

---

# 2. BASIC EVIDENCE OBJECTS

## ORIGIN_POSITION_PROXY

Examples where source-authorized:

- parental education;
- parental occupation;
- childhood household status;
- migration origin;
- family background category.

## CURRENT_POSITION

Derived from the local position space.

## SUBJECTIVE_POSITION

Self-described class/status or perceived economic rank projected supplementary.

---

# 3. POSITION MISMATCH

Potential descriptive relations:

```text
ORIGIN_CURRENT_DISTANCE
CURRENT_SUBJECTIVE_MISMATCH
RESOURCE_CREDENTIAL_COMPOSITION_MISMATCH
DISPOSITION_POSITION_MISMATCH
```

These are empirical relations, not automatically `hysteresis` or `habitus clivé`.

---

# 4. DISPOSITION-POSITION MISMATCH

A respondent/group may occupy position region P2 while retaining disposition complexes much more typical of P1.

Compute descriptively:

```text
observed repertoire distribution at P2
vs
expected/common repertoire distribution at P2
vs
origin-associated repertoire if origin evidence exists
```

Potential state:

```text
POSITION_DISPOSITION_MISMATCH_CANDIDATE
```

Manual structural interpretation may later consider:

- hysteresis;
- cleft habitus;
- incomplete capital conversion;
- retained origin repertoire;
- conjunctural field change.

Do not automatically choose among these explanations.

---

# 5. SUBJECTIVE CLASS AS SUPPLEMENTARY EVIDENCE

Subjective class/status is especially valuable when projected onto an objective relational position space.

Possible empirical patterns:

```text
objective intermediate position
+ subjective upper-middle identification

objective high-resource position
+ subjective middle identification

objective upward mobility proxy
+ retained origin-class identification
```

These differences are themselves meaningful empirical motifs.

Do not force subjective class to equal objective class.

---

# 6. CAPITAL COMPOSITION AND MOBILITY

A person can move upward in total resource volume while remaining unusual in capital composition.

Example:

```text
high economic resources
+ modest credential/cultural-position markers
```

versus:

```text
modest economic resources
+ high credential/cultural-position markers
```

FIELD FORGE should preserve these compositions rather than collapse both into one socioeconomic-status score.

This is one of the strongest reasons to prefer relational geometry over a scalar SES index.

---

# 7. PERSONA-GENERATION USE

For generated adults, trajectory evidence may alter the plausible repertoire distribution.

For example:

```text
same current position
but different origin proxies
-> different lawful disposition/repertoire probabilities
```

where the survey actually supports such differences.

For generated children, parental origin/current mismatch may alter the family environment V3 receives.

Again:

```text
TRAJECTORY FOOTPRINT
!= BIOGRAPHY.
```

---

# 8. TEMPORAL LIMIT

Cross-sectional parent/current variables provide trajectory proxies, not temporal causal sequences.

Repeated survey waves describe population change, not individual longitudinal development.

Keep these distinct.

---

# 9. HOSTILE CASES

Reject:

- calling any upwardly mobile respondent `cleft habitus`;
- inferring parental class from parental education alone without role authority;
- interpreting subjective/objective mismatch as false consciousness by default;
- collapsing economic and credential mobility into one direction;
- treating wave-to-wave aggregate change as individual trajectory;
- treating unusual current disposition as retained origin disposition without origin evidence.

---

# 10. CURRENT RECOMMENDATION

Trajectory/hysteresis should be a **selective enrichment layer** used only in contexts where the existing survey substrate actually observes origin/current-position differences.

It should not be required for every FIELD FORGE context.
