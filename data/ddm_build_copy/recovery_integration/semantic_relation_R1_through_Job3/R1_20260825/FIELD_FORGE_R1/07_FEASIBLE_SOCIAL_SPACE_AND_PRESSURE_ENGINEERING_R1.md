# FIELD FORGE R1 — FEASIBLE SOCIAL-SPACE / PRESSURE ENGINEERING WITH CURRENT RAWLSING EVIDENCE

Status: **EXPLORATORY DESIGN / NON-BINDING**

This file turns the current Bourdieu/Gramsci/Hall/Lahire sketch into a feasible engineering sequence using only Rawlsing's existing evidence envelope.

---

# 1. DO NOT BUILD ONE UNIVERSAL GLOBAL SOCIAL SPACE

Because measurement portability and field meaning are context-sensitive, the default unit should be a **context-local empirical social space**.

```text
COUNTRY / WAVE / SOURCE-SCOPE K
    ↓
LOCAL POSITION GEOMETRY K
    ↓
LOCAL ATTITUDE GEOMETRY K
    ↓
LOCAL POSITION↔ATTITUDE PRESSURE MAP K
```

Cross-survey comparison occurs at the level of governed derived structure, not raw coordinate identity, unless explicit measurement-portability authority permits stronger comparison.

Hard law:

```text
MCA_AXIS_1_IN_CONTEXT_A
!=
MCA_AXIS_1_IN_CONTEXT_B
```

without a later interpretive/comparability court.

# 2. PRECOMPUTE A POSITION-ROLE AUTHORITY BANK

This is a likely manual-agentic authority surface analogous to semantic/directional authority.

For each current context/source/wave, adjudicate candidate variables into roles such as:

```text
ACTIVE_ECONOMIC_RESOURCE
ACTIVE_MATERIAL_DEPRIVATION
ACTIVE_EDUCATIONAL_CREDENTIAL
ACTIVE_OCCUPATIONAL_POSITION
ACTIVE_ORIGIN_TRAJECTORY_PROXY
ACTIVE_GEOGRAPHIC_POSITION
ACTIVE_IDENTITY_POSITION
ACTIVE_ORGANIZATIONAL_EMBEDDEDNESS
SUPPLEMENTARY_SUBJECTIVE_CLASS
SUPPLEMENTARY_ATTITUDE
ADMIN / NONPOSITIONAL
AMBIGUOUS
UNUSABLE_FOR_POSITION_GEOMETRY
```

One variable may carry multiple roles only when that is semantically justified.

Role authority must be source/context-specific.

Examples of why:

- education may act as credential capital but not fully measure embodied cultural capital;
- religion may be a social-position coordinate in one analysis and an attitude/disposition object in another;
- subjective social class should usually be supplementary when constructing objective position geometry, then projected for comparison;
- income may be incomparable across contexts without local normalization/authority;
- race/sect/caste should not be treated as a `resource`; they are relational positional coordinates whose effects depend on context.

# 3. BUILD POSITION SPACE WITH GEOMETRIC DATA ANALYSIS

Primary experimental default: MCA / specific MCA / related geometric-data-analysis methods appropriate to categorical survey data.

Why this fits Rawlsing:

- categorical/ordinal survey inputs are common;
- relational location matters more than a scalar score;
- capital composition should remain multidimensional;
- active vs supplementary variable separation is explicit;
- respondents, categories, and projected variables can be examined together;
- the method does not require predeclaring discrete classes.

Outputs should include:

```text
POSITION_SPACE_GEOMETRY {
    context
    active_variable_authority_refs
    category_coordinates
    respondent_coordinates_or_equivalent
    axis_contributions
    modality_contributions
    inertia / diagnostic material
    supplementary projections
    support / weighting law
    provenance
}
```

Do not emit `MIDDLE_CLASS`, `ELITE`, `FIELD`, or `CULTURAL_CAPITAL` automatically.

# 4. RELATIONAL CLASS / FRACTION CANDIDATES COME AFTER GEOMETRY

A class/fraction candidate should be nominated from a conjunction of evidence such as:

- position in capital-volume/composition geometry;
- occupation/employment relation;
- material resources;
- credentials/origin trajectory where observed;
- subjective class projection;
- recurrent disposition complexes;
- stable boundaries against neighboring regions.

The agentic court may decide that a region is meaningfully describable as an intermediate/middle position, dominant fraction, dominated fraction, credential-rich/economic-poor fraction, etc.

But the geometry must remain available even if no discrete class partition is admitted.

Hard law:

```text
RELATIONAL SPACE
DOES NOT REQUIRE
DISCRETE CLASS BUCKETS.
```

# 5. THE MIDDLE-CLASS PROBLEM BECOMES TESTABLE

A prospective Rawlsing experiment:

1. derive a local position space from objective/relational position variables;
2. project subjective class identity where available;
3. identify intermediate regions in volume/composition space without using attitude variables to construct them;
4. project moral, economic, political, status, trust, boundary, and aspiration-relevant disposition complexes;
5. compare those complexes with the high-capital/dominant regions actually observed in the same survey;
6. preserve mismatch rather than assuming middle-position aspiration reproduces dominant dispositions.

Possible empirical object:

```text
INTERMEDIATE_POSITION_DISPOSITION_MISMATCH {
    context
    intermediate_position_region
    dominant_position_region
    disposition_complex
    observed_distribution_intermediate
    observed_distribution_dominant
    displacement
    support
    uncertainty
}
```

Do not call mismatch `misrecognition` unless a separate semantic/theoretical court licenses that interpretation.

# 7. PRESSURE IS A DISTRIBUTIONAL DISPLACEMENT OBJECT

For persona generation, the central observable relation is:

```text
POSITION CONFIGURATION C
→ CHANGED DISTRIBUTION OVER DISPOSITION COMPLEX A
```

without automatic causal interpretation.

Do not rely only on additive main effects.

Explicitly search for configuration dependence:

```text
religion × region
religion × education
religion × income
religion × region × education
class-position × cohort
identity × power-alignment × education
etc.
```

The exact dimensions depend on current source authority and sample support.

Engineering tools may include:

- hierarchical log-linear models;
- multinomial/ordinal models where semantics permit;
- tree/partition candidate generation under governed partition law;
- conditional mutual information;
- exact/direct J2/J3 distribution comparison;
- fracture-map differential structure;
- partial pooling across subregions only where source/comparability law permits.

Machine learning may nominate interactions, but no opaque predictive model gets semantic sovereignty.

# 8. CONFIGURATION-FIRST, NOT IDENTITY-COEFFICIENT-FIRST

Stuart Hall's articulation principle should become an engineering invariant.

Bad default model:

```text
attitude = β0 + β_shia + β_south + β_education + β_income
```

Useful as a diagnostic baseline, not as ontology.

Rawlsing should be able to discover that:

```text
SHIA × SOUTH × HIGH_EDUCATION × HIGH_RESOURCES
```

has a different disposition distribution than:

```text
SUNNI × SOUTH × HIGH_EDUCATION × HIGH_RESOURCES
```

and that these differences do not decompose cleanly into universal additive coefficients.

Output configurations must retain exact context and support.

# 9. HEGEMONY: WHAT WE CAN ACTUALLY ESTIMATE

Within fixed evidence, split hegemony into observable dimensions.

## A. common-sense reach

How broadly does a disposition complex appear across position space?

Potential evidence:

- weighted prevalence;
- low/high variation across major position regions;
- presence across identity groups;
- recurrence across lawful time/context windows.

## B. high-position alignment

Does the complex disproportionately characterize high-capital / dominant observed regions of local survey position space?

Caution: survey high-position region is not automatically the ruling class.

## C. political-power identity alignment

Where existing EPR/GeoEPR authority lawfully maps identities/groups to executive power/geographic distribution, compare the attitude/identity configuration cautiously.

Do not infer individual elite attitudes from group power access.

## D. subhegemonic/local dominance

A complex may be dominant inside a bounded region/identity/field candidate while having limited reach or poor conversion outside it.

## E. counter-complex

A coherent alternative complex may persist as a structured minority or opposition.

Candidate object:

```text
HEGEMONY_EVIDENCE_PROFILE {
    context
    complex_ref
    common_sense_reach
    position_space_distribution
    high_position_alignment
    existing_power_alignment_refs
    local_dominance_regions
    counter_complex_refs
    unobserved_dimensions
    provenance
}
```

Semantic label `HEGEMONIC` remains an adjudicated interpretation.

# 10. FIELD CANDIDACY: DO NOT EQUATE CLUSTER WITH FIELD

A field candidate should require stronger evidence than clustering.

Possible evidentiary signature:

1. a bounded arena/context with a recurrent set of stakes/dispositions;
2. internally differentiated positions;
3. evidence that particular resources/dispositions have different value inside the arena than outside it;
4. relatively stable local relations among position and disposition;
5. partial autonomy: local hierarchy cannot be reduced completely to the broader position space;
6. identifiable conversion/devaluation relations to neighboring arenas.

Surveys may support some field candidates strongly and others weakly.

When evidence is insufficient:

```text
FIELD_CANDIDATE_UNRESOLVED
```

not a generic `FIELD` label.

# 11. CAPITAL VALUE AND CONVERSION: FEASIBLE PROXIES

The current surveys do not give a complete census of every form of capital.

Use only what they observe.

Examples of feasible relations:

```text
education ↔ income/resource position
parental education ↔ respondent education/resource position
occupation ↔ economic position
education ↔ subjective status/class
organizational membership ↔ political/social attitude structure
religious/sect position ↔ institutional trust/identity complexes
migration/mobility ↔ position/disposition shifts where observed
```

These can reveal candidate conversion rates and mismatches.

Hard limitation:

```text
EDUCATIONAL CREDENTIAL
!=
FULL CULTURAL CAPITAL
```

Fine embodied taste/style capital may remain weakly observed. Preserve the limitation rather than inventing a proxy total.

# 12. TEMPORAL / TRAJECTORY WORK MUST AVOID APC FANTASY

Age, cohort, and period are not interchangeable.

Mannheim is useful as a theory constraint:

```text
SAME BIRTH COHORT
!=
SAME GENERATION UNIT
```

Within fixed survey data, lawful temporal objects may include:

- cohort-position differences within a wave;
- repeated-wave changes where measurement portability/comparability is established;
- interaction of cohort with class/education/religion/etc.;
- parental-origin/education trajectory proxies where available.

Do not claim full life-course trajectory from repeated cross-sections alone.

Do not call age difference `generational change` automatically.

## Child generation

- parental/adult disposition environment;

- context-specific attitude possibilities.

# 14. BOOTSTRAP / ONTOLOGY-FREEZE EXPERIMENT

Do not use all contexts to invent the grammar and then declare success.

Suggested experiment:

```text
TRAINING / DESIGN ATLAS
    several deliberately diverse current survey contexts

↓
manual theorized grammar proposal

↓
HELD-OUT CONTEXTS
    from other families/societies

↓
HOSTILE TEST:
    can the same grammar represent them
    without arbitrary new primitives or forced equivalence?
```

Failure modes:

- endless context-specific exceptions;
- one survey family dominates the ontology;
- axes/categories require universal labels despite different empirical meaning;
- theory term depends on information absent from the fixed evidence;
- structural object cannot be connected back to exact empirical provenance;
- generated persona output changes because of arbitrary naming rather than empirical geometry.
