# SHARED COUNCIL / CROSS-EXAMINATION LAW — R1

Status: `SEALED_UNTIL_ALL_THREE_G1_RETURNS_BANKED`

This is the delayed shared space for Dva/Trii/Chetiri recovered-inheritance integration.

## Why the council is delayed

The three Vozhdy are useful because they embody different theories of what matters. Premature discourse would create anchoring, shared vocabulary convergence, and false independence.

Therefore:

`NO_SIBLING_FINDING_CONSUMPTION_BEFORE_G1_BANK`

A Vozhd may know that the other roles exist and may read the common campaign charter. It may not read or rely on another role's G0/G1 findings until its own G1 return is complete and immutable enough to compare honestly.

## Council opening condition

The council opens only after:
- Dva G0 + G1 complete;
- Trii G0 + G1 complete;
- Chetiri G0 + G1 complete.

## First council exchange

Each role supplies:
1. its 10–20 highest-value collisions;
2. exact current-system object(s) implicated;
3. exact recovered object(s) implicated;
4. why the collision matters from that role's jurisdiction;
5. what the finding does NOT prove;
6. 3–5 findings likely to affect another role;
7. 5 questions addressed to the other roles;
8. proposed experiment(s), if any.

## Cross-examination directions

### Dva → Trii/Chetiri
Ask whether a local semantic/compiler insight changes a whole-machine obligation, stage seam, terminal capability, or architecture economy; also ask whether an apparently new mechanism is actually a re-expression of an older architectural abstraction.

### Trii → Dva/Chetiri
Ask whether source authority, exact semantic grain, role/routing, negative-state distinctions, or information-conservation law has been silently weakened by architectural elegance or compiler convenience.

### Chetiri → Dva/Trii
Ask whether claimed preserved meaning is actually executable, discoverable, replayable, incrementally invalidatable, and falsifiable without smuggling in permanent materialization or hidden human adjudication.

## No-consensus law

- Agreement is not evidence.
- Disagreement is not failure.
- A contradiction may indicate different jurisdictions rather than incompatible truth.
- A recovered artifact may be obsolete while the function it embodied remains binding.
- A current architecture may be superior while still having lost a distinction worth restoring.

## Experiment queue

A cross-role dispute should become an experiment when an observable result can discriminate between competing claims.

Every experiment proposal must state:
- exact question;
- competing hypotheses;
- lawful inputs;
- expected discriminating observation;
- prohibited inference;
- owner;
- whether the result could change authority, architecture, compiler law, or only confidence.

## Final synthesis

The shared council does not directly mutate Rawlsing. It produces contested and uncontested findings for the Vozhd-to-the-Vozhdy synthesis gate.
