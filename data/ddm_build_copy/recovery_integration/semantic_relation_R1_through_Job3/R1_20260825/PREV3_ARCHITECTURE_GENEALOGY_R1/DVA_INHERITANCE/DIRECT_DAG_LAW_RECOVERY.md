# DIRECT-DAG LAW RECOVERY

## Verdict

`DURABLE_DIRECT_DAG_CAPITAL`

Dva's mature architecture rejects an immediate-predecessor-only pipeline. The rule is architectural law: semantic objects remain owned by their producer and may be directly reused by any lawful downstream consumer. Intermediate stages create only their own unique semantic transformations and must not become mandatory semantic couriers.

## Problem solved

### 1. Authority laundering

If V553 copied all V552 structural semantics into a V553 packet and later stages treated the copy as canonical, producer ownership would silently migrate. The direct-DAG law instead requires the V553 object to carry exact V552/V552J/earlier authority refs while owning only temporal meaning.

### 2. Terminal starvation

TD-R00 identifies the concrete starvation risk: late stages need exact source/driver, target/receiver, position/stratum, relation/channel, permission and negative-authority identities. A thin courier packet containing only labels, scores or aggregates cannot reconstruct these lawfully.

### 3. Semantic flattening

Serial interfaces tend to collapse:

- structural direction into temporal delta direction;
- simultaneous/opposed pressures into one scalar;
- missing/noncomparable/denied into null/default;
- n-ary interactions into dyads;
- producer-owned predicates into copied downstream fields;
- role/cardinality/order into unordered labels.

Direct producer references preserve those distinctions.

### 4. Reconstruction drift

Without direct refs, a downstream stage must rematch by text, proximity, country/year, shared endpoint or semantic resemblance. Mature Dva authority repeatedly forbids descriptive/fuzzy rematch and hidden reconstruction.

## Stage-by-stage observance

### V553

V553 creates temporal structure only. It preserves exact structural producer refs and explicitly allows later stages to reuse original upstream authority. It must not become the owner of structural-position, directionality, mediation or actor semantics.

### V554

ADP-001 makes the rule concrete. A lawful F1 affordance is grounded in exact V552 structural fact, exact V552J admission/use authority, governed structural refs and—in temporal modes—exact V553 temporal objects. Non-temporal signatures do not require a synthetic V553 wrapper.

### V555

ADP-002 consumes an exact V554 F1 and produces one canonical atomic route. Inherited V554 F2 applicability conditions are `REFERENCE_ONLY__NEVER_COPIED_INTO_V555_ROUTE_BORN_CONDITION`. That is direct-DAG law expressed as nonduplication.

### V556

V556 interaction rules consume exact V555 canonical route IDs as atomic base edges. They do not re-identify or duplicate those edges. Coexistence or common endpoint/time is insufficient to invent an interaction.

### V557

Publication is mixed-producer. A lawful V555 object can be published directly even where no V556 interaction applies. Requiring a filler V556 transformation would violate the producer DAG.

### V558

Repository/index/resolution preserves producer-owned IDs and exact reference closure. Resolver output is a deterministic selection/binding surface, not a semantic re-authoring event.

### V3

V3 may encounter, bind and realize PRE-V3 objects, but the structural prior remains producer-owned. Route existence is distinct from route applicability, actor opportunity, encounter/exposure, traversal, response and later state.

## Architectural law versus implementation convenience

The rule cannot be reduced to an optimization because its invariants are semantic:

1. exact semantic owner must remain identifiable;
2. permission may narrow but not silently widen;
3. negative/missing/noncomparable states must survive;
4. replay must resolve the same producer object/version;
5. no downstream stage may infer missing authority from descriptive similarity;
6. an intermediate stage with no unique semantic contribution must not fabricate a filler object.

Changing these invariants changes what downstream claims mean. Therefore direct-DAG reuse is part of the model's epistemic architecture.
