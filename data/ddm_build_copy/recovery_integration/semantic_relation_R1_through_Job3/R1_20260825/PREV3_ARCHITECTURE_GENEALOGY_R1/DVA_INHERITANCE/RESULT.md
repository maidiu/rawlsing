## 3. Direct-DAG law recovered

`ARCHITECTURE_PASS_LAW.md` and TD-R00 make the rule explicit: a stage owns only its unique semantic transformation; earlier authoritative objects remain directly reusable; serial republishing is neither required nor semantically safe.

The rule solved four concrete failure modes:

- **authority laundering** — an intermediate stage could appear to become owner of a meaning it merely copied;
- **reference starvation** — later stages could be left with labels/aggregates rather than the exact source/target/channel/position/permission objects needed to interpret a producer object;
- **semantic flattening** — negative, noncomparable, multi-pressure, role/cardinality/order information could disappear in courier packets;
- **reconstruction drift** — downstream stages could be forced to rebuild associations from strings, proximity or semantic resemblance.

Direct-DAG reuse is therefore architectural law, not an implementation optimization.

Examples proved by the recovered architecture:
