# ARCHITECTURE-NEUTRAL CONSERVATION REQUIREMENTS

These are functional conservation requirements only. They do not select stage names, physical packaging, database layout or implementation strategy.

## CR-01 — Producer-owned identity

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve exact producer ownership and stable object identity because exact Dva evidence proves later consumers cannot lawfully distinguish inherited fact/authority from newly derived meaning if intermediate republishing re-owns the object.**

Minimum invariant: producer ID/version/provenance/reference lineage remains dereferenceable through publication, repository and resolution.

## CR-02 — Direct producer reuse

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must permit a downstream consumer to directly reuse an earlier producer-owned object because exact Dva evidence proves forcing every object through each immediate predecessor creates authority laundering, reference starvation and filler transformations.**

Minimum invariant: semantic dependency is a DAG, not a mandatory courier chain.

## CR-03 — Exact structural basis

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve exact structural source/driver, target/receiver, relation/channel, position/stratum and use-authority references where governed because exact Dva evidence proves V555/V556/V3 cannot reconstruct route endpoints, direction or applicability from labels, scores or aggregates without illicit rematching.**

## CR-04 — Temporal structural meaning

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must provide governed temporal identity, observed anchors, ordered comparison/relations and typed temporal limits because exact Dva evidence proves downstream structural mediation and pressure interaction otherwise must recompute temporal meaning or accept interpolated proxies as fact.**

Observed zero, missing, gap and noncomparability must remain distinct.

## CR-05 — Structural direction versus temporal direction

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must keep structural source→target direction separate from temporal increase/decrease because exact Dva evidence proves temporal delta sign cannot identify the structural direction of pressure or mediation.**

## CR-06 — Multiplicity without forced scalar collapse

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve simultaneous, competing and opposed structural pressures as separately identifiable objects because exact Dva evidence proves later interaction/topology becomes impossible if those pressures are averaged or deduplicated before V556-equivalent composition.**

## CR-07 — Affordance distinct from actor fact

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must represent actor-independent structural affordance separately from actor-specific satisfaction/position/encounter because exact Dva ADP-001/CAN-001 evidence proves structural eligibility can exist while runtime binding remains deferred.**

No readiness score, confidence score, interpolation or descriptive rematch may substitute for missing semantic authority.

## CR-08 — Mediation route distinct from traversal

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must represent an actor-independent mediation route independently of actor traversal because exact Dva ADP-002/ADP-011 evidence proves `ROUTE EXISTS` is not equivalent to applicability, opportunity, encounter/exposure, traversal or response.**

A structural route may remain unrealized for every actor.

## CR-09 — Atomic route identity and inherited-condition nonduplication

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve canonical atomic route identity and keep inherited applicability conditions producer-owned because exact Dva evidence proves copying inherited F2 conditions as new route-born semantics corrupts authority and replay identity.**

## CR-10 — Governed interaction only

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must require explicit rule authority before asserting cross-route/cross-pressure interaction because exact Dva V556 evidence proves common endpoint, time, geography, adjacency or co-resolution is insufficient to infer an interaction.**

Where the governed registry supplies no interaction, valid-empty/no-interaction is lawful.

## CR-11 — N-ary role/cardinality/order preservation

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve participant role, cardinality, order and multiplicity because exact Dva/Trii evidence proves higher-order structural interactions cannot be losslessly reduced to convenient dyads or unordered sets.**

## CR-12 — Attractor prerequisites distinct from realized attractor history

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must keep structural attractor prerequisites upstream and realized attractor formation downstream because exact Dva evidence proves actor history/path dependence is required for realization and is V3-owned.**

## CR-13 — First-class negative authority

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve denial, missing, unknown, deferred, noncomparable, integrity-failure and valid-empty as distinct typed states because exact Dva evidence proves null/default collapse manufactures positive semantics and makes fail-closed downstream resolution impossible.**

## CR-14 — Lossless mixed-producer publication

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must support publication of a mixed set of producer-owned objects without re-identifying them because exact Dva/Trii evidence proves a lawful V555 object may need direct publication even when no V556 object exists.**

No filler transformation may be required solely to preserve stage adjacency.

## CR-15 — Permission monotonicity

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must prevent repository/publication/resolution layers from widening producer-granted use permission because exact Dva evidence proves object presence or successful resolution does not itself authorize semantic use.**

## CR-16 — Immutable standalone reference closure

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must support an immutable, replayable repository/reference graph whose interpretation-critical refs close without access to mutable producer directories because exact Dva/Trii evidence proves hidden-latest, fuzzy repair or mutable lookup destroys replay and provenance.**

Failure to resolve must fail closed.

## CR-17 — Resolver nonauthoring

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must keep context selection/resolution distinct from semantic creation because exact Dva evidence proves a resolver that invents actor position/history or repairs missing structural meaning would silently cross both producer jurisdiction and the V3 boundary.**

## CR-18 — PRE-V3/V3 causal firewall

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must preserve a hard causal firewall between actor-independent structural possibility and actor-specific developmental realization because exact Dva evidence proves structural possibility alone cannot establish encounter, exposure, traversal, response, memory, knowledge, relationship, biography, genealogy or realized attractor state.**

## CR-19 — Re-query versus re-bind distinction

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must distinguish refreshing/re-querying an upstream structural object from rebinding an actor to an already-governed structural possibility because exact Dva/Chetiri evidence proves producer refresh authority and actor runtime binding are different ownership questions.**

## CR-20 — Re-prove inherited upstream sufficiency

**Any viable PRE-V3 architecture must independently prove the structural/evidentiary authority that Dva assumed from V550–V552J because exact Dva evidence shows its downstream correctness depends on those producer refs but does not itself establish that the old upstream supplied them completely or correctly.**

This is the decisive separation between durable Dva capital and Dva's obsolete premise.

## Conservation checksum

A future architecture may rename, merge or physically co-package stages only if it can prove semantic/replay equivalence for every conserved object/function above. Same field names, same sequence order, or a nominal V553/V554/V555/V556 label is insufficient evidence of conservation.
