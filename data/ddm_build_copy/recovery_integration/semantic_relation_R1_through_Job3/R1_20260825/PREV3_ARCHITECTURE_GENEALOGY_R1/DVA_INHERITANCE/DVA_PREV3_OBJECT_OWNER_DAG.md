## Non-adjacent edges that matter

1. **V554 → V552/V552J directly.** A non-temporal V554 signature needs no invented V553 wrapper. A temporal signature adds exact V553 refs without replacing the structural producer refs.
2. **V557 → V553/V554/V555/V556 directly.** Publication is mixed-producer. No filler V556 object is required to publish a valid V555 route where no governed V556 interaction exists.
3. **V558 → original producer IDs through V557 governance.** Repository/resolution preserves producer identity rather than rebadging everything as V558 meaning.
4. **V3 → resolved exact producer objects.** V3 may bind/realize them but does not transform their structural meaning into V3-owned prior semantics.

## Why this is a DAG rather than a serial pipeline

The semantic graph is ordered by ownership and dependency, but physical passage through every intermediate stage is not required. Dva's mature law explicitly allows later stages to reuse earlier authority directly. A stage that has nothing semantically new to say for a particular object must not create a filler copy merely to maintain stage adjacency.

That rule is indispensable to preserve exact provenance, semantic jurisdiction, negative authority, role/cardinality/order and replay identity.
