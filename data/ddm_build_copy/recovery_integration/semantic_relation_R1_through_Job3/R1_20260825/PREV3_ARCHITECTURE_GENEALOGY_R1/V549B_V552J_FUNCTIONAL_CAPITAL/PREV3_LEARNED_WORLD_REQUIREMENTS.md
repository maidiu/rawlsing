# PRE-V3 Learned-World Functional Requirements

These are conservation requirements recovered from V549/V549b → V552/V552J history. They do **not** select an architecture and do not adjudicate Dva/Trii/Chetiri.

## R1 — Preserve empirical grain explicitly

A response-category empirical atom and its parent question distribution are different objects. A lawful PRE-V3 product must preserve both identities and must not use a representative category row as the parent distribution.

## R2 — Preserve exact source, wave, universe and respondent-risk-set provenance

Learned-world evidence must remain tied to the exact source/wave/country or shard scope and, for joint evidence, the exact respondent/risk-set universe. Source co-occurrence, shared metadata or compatible marginals cannot substitute for same-respondent authority.

## R3 — Response meaning must come from governed authority, not downstream re-inference

Raw source response codes/labels/scale geometry are evidence. Canonical semantic direction/intensity is a separately governed projection. Exact `response_semantics_contract_hash` identity must be inherited from authority; shared labels, family resemblance, artifact hashes or locally reconstructed geometry are insufficient.

## R4 — Keep denominators distinct

Question-distribution denominator, joint-pattern N/effective N/probability and category counts are not interchangeable. Exact question `n_respondents` must be carried forward rather than recomputed by summing categories or replaced by packet-level joint measures.

## R5 — Preserve typed absence; null is not zero

At minimum distinguish observed numeric zero, neutral represented mass, not-computable/null, review-required, unresolved, not-applicable, valid-empty, missing authority and explicit prohibition. Downstream convenience may not coerce these states into one scalar value.

## R6 — Preserve logical joint configuration separately from executable variant and observed pattern

A source-native logical configuration must retain exact members, roles/order, targets/conditions, allowed/forbidden inference, response-portability ceiling and provenance. Executable wave/country/form/module/split/routing/risk-set realization is a second authority. Observed same-respondent pattern evidence is a third. None may manufacture the others.

## R7 — Preserve packet and component morphology

A V551J-style packet is not a scalar row. Component membership/order, category and parent-distribution identities, question denominators, roles, target layers, response semantics, provenance and negative states must remain recoverable at component grain.

## R8 — Structural pressure remains its own transformation

V552-style pressure is a bounded parent-distribution structural transformation from governed signed components and observed distribution weights. Support, confidence, joint probability, association strength and packet score are not pressure. Joint context may qualify or contextualize pressure but may not silently reweight/recompute it.

## R9 — Preserve certified and review/context lanes separately

Reviewable morphology can remain visible without becoming certified evidence. Certification tokens may be renamed only if an exact semantic mapping prevents lane leakage in either direction.

## R10 — Recursive branch topology must be replayable if full V549/V549b equivalence is claimed

The historical learned world could condition cross-question cotravel on **actual recursively reconstructed branch membership**. A PRE-V3 successor that claims equivalent or stronger coverage must demonstrate exact branch predicate/cohort reconstruction and same-respondent branch-conditioned cotravel. Generic pairwise response clouds do not satisfy this requirement by themselves.

## R11 — Higher-order joint structure requires adversarial conservation proof

Pairwise associations and marginals cannot prove preservation of k-way configurations, profile covariance, XOR-like structure, ranked/multiselect topology or minority-supported combinations. Where PRE-V3 depends on such learned structure, conservation must be tested with fixtures whose lower-order marginals are deliberately insufficient.

## R12 — Component-carried structural authority cannot be scalarized by convenience

Where roles/strata/horizon/runtime or other authority varies by component, preserve the component vector. A packet scalar is lawful only when all required components are exactly matched **and** every component has the same non-empty governed value for that projected field. Agreement among a matched subset is not unanimity.

## R13 — Negative authority is part of the learned world

Known absences, prohibited inference, unresolved applicability, blocked response authority and review states are not decorative metadata. A carrier that preserves positive values but drops their prohibitions or uncertainty is functionally lossy.

## R14 — Bounded real-source authority must not become global authority by breadth

A real respondent-backed bounded canary can be authoritative inside its exact jurisdiction without proving product-wide completeness. Conversely, all-wave/generated registries do not become authority through row count. Raw source-native adjudication, exact executable variants and hostile validation remain required for broader claims.

## R15 — Schema preservation is insufficient; generator/replay proof is required

`SCHEMA_EXISTS != GENERATOR_EXISTS`. For every function claimed conserved, PRE-V3 must identify an executable or exactly regenerable producer, its inputs and provenance, output semantic grain, authority status, negative states and replay/validation evidence. Historical courier files may be retired only after this functional equivalence is proved.

## R16 — Temporal/source comparison must retain the evidence that licenses comparison

Single-wave pressure cannot authorize temporal-field meaning. Cross-wave or cross-source comparison must preserve exact source/wave identity, measurement contract, response-semantic compatibility, population/risk-set basis and explicit temporal/comparability authority. Horizon absence is not a horizon constraint.

## Minimum hostile conservation set

A PRE-V3 conservation court should be able to distinguish at least: recursive subgroup forks; minority-supported structures; equal-marginal/different-k-way configurations; denominator conflicts; source-zero versus semantic-zero scale cases; null versus observed-zero pressure; lawful parallel target layers; certified versus review lanes; incomplete/heterogeneous component-vector scalarization cases; source-routing signals without executable predicates; synthetic joint-family contamination; ranked/multiselect topology; and cross-source semantic-contract incompatibility.

## Functional completeness rule

An old physical artifact is dispensable only when the successor can reproduce the artifact's valuable functions at the same or stronger empirical grain with exact authority and replay. A newer carrier, a copied schema, or a surviving authority document is not by itself proof of functional succession.

No architectural recommendation is made here.
