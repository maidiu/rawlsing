## Chronology versus succession

Two historical motions overlap and must not be collapsed:

1. **Learned-world generation:** V549/V549b learned recursive subgroup/cotravel structure; V550/V550J shifted toward governed source/response and joint empirical authority; V551/V551J/V552/V552J refined grain, transformation ownership, and handoff semantics.
2. **Later contract-recovery wave:** 2026 repairs corrected response-semantic identity, category-vs-parent distribution grain, denominator propagation, V551J target/packet semantics, nullable V552 pressure, V552J lane preservation, and producer/consumer contracts.

The later repair wave can prove that a historical function survives under a changed carrier, but it cannot retroactively erase a function that no successor implements.
