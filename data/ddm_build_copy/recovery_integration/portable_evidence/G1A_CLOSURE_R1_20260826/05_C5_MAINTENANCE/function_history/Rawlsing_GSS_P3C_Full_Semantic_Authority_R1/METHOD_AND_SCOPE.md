# Method and scope

## Governing distinction

This checkpoint concerns **P3C proposition identity** only. It does not create V550J respondent-level joint-configuration authority. Source batteries and questionnaire structures are evidence inputs, not automatic proposition families.

## Full authority funnel

The pass reconstructs all 2,850 GSS source-local lineages from the 6,021 implementation rows and applies the later all-source semantic machinery to the complete GSS surface. Candidate generation is deliberately wider than the superseded repair:

| Surface | Obligations |
|---|---:|

Every effective pair has two source-evidence rows and exactly one final disposition. Every GSS lineage has exactly one full-pass disposition.

## Discovery versus adjudication

Axis, target, operator, text similarity, variable-version continuity, response topology, wave continuity, existing P2 pair facts, and R184 family membership are discovery and evidence surfaces. Similarity does not decide proposition identity.

Final rulings follow this precedence:

1. final R184 canonical membership and stronger boundaries;
2. explicit source-native GSS version/wording adjudication;
3. two-sided cross-source proposition evidence;
4. fail-closed exclusion where a single respondent-facing operator and target cannot be recovered.

The full machine-generated within-GSS component surface was reviewed component by component. It produced 118 accepted components, 29 rejected false equivalences, and 5 split or partially accepted components. The rejected surfaces include actor, target, slot, timeframe, medium, directional-operator, election, access/use, and direct/constructed-variable shifts.

## What the 147 candidate families represent

They are not 147 arbitrary themes. Most are source-version continuities that the old GSS pass left as separate lineages: questionnaire-form variants, web volunteered-response variants, renamed release variables, hour/minute response variants, national-spending wording experiments, institutional-confidence wording versions, household-composition releases, income-band releases, and other exact respondent-facing proposition continuities.

They remain candidate successor families until reconciled with the user's current live repository and database. Their semantic adjudications are closed; their numeric live identifiers are intentionally unallocated.

## Completeness boundary

This checkpoint completes the packaged full GSS semantic-authority pass under the inherited all-source candidate-generation and fail-closed contracts. It does not claim metaphysical infallibility, redesign the global ontology, or reopen every possible pair among all 2,850 lineages indiscriminately. It closes every relation emitted by the full governed discovery surfaces and gives every lineage a no-loss disposition.
