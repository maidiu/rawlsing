# DAG invariants and discovery law recovered from R1→Job3

## Identity and evidence

- Semantic-act/node roles and relation roles are orthogonal. A node classified as `NORMATIVE_OR_POLICY_STANCE`, `SOURCE_BOUNDED_RELATIONAL_MEASURE`, etc. does not by itself satisfy a relation-role slot such as GOAL, MEANS, COMMITMENT, COST, PRINCIPLE, APPLICATION, ACTOR, TARGET, or ACTION.
- Relation authority must bind exact implementation/member identities and preserve source family/wave grain.
- Source-native wording/structure may establish a typed relation; driver/facet coincidence, lexical similarity, embeddings, questionnaire proximity, or empirical co-travel may only nominate candidates.
- Page excerpts can bleed neighboring questionnaire content and therefore cannot be used as cross-object relation evidence.

## T06 / actor-target-action law

A norm↔conduct candidate requires actor alignment, target alignment, action-domain alignment, modality distinction, source/wave compatibility, and sufficient routing/respondent-universe compatibility. Endpoint permission is not a semantic-link generator.

## Open-world negative law

- Positive typed relation authority, exact proven typed-relation negative authority, and BLOCKED/unknown are distinct states.
- Zero positives are not absence.
- A typed semantic negative has an authority ceiling: it does not assert absence of empirical response co-travel or downstream association.
- Search termination/absence claims require certified coverage of the prerequisite input-contract fields, not merely exhaustion of a generator's own recall surface.

## Denominator law

- Raw pair count is not human-review workload. Hyperedge/packet compression is lawful only after preserving exact candidate identity.
- A pair census can be exhaustive inside a pair jurisdiction while missing non-pair/source-object relation channels. R9 is the canonical failure fixture: 364,094 same-wave pairs were complete within the 3,324 relational-semantic-unit pair jurisdiction, yet 19/117 sealed canaries lay outside it.
- Frozen denominators may not be silently mutated after adjudication. Generator-axis defects discovered during Job3 become append-only successor obligations.

## Job3 terminal-state law

- ACCEPT creates typed relation authority at exact candidate grain.
- REJECT rejects the exact proposed typed relation; it does not create a global semantic or empirical negative beyond its grain.
- BLOCKED records unresolved authority and creates no positive or negative relation.
- Job3 created no respondent joins, respondent composability decisions, pressure/personality states, or runtime persona claims.

## App / empirical anti-erase law

Semantic relation truth and empirical response co-travel are independent layers. A semantic reject/block/absence may not erase empirical co-travel; empirical co-travel may not manufacture semantic relation truth.
