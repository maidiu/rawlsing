# Uniform negative replay law — R16F

A worker-proposed negative is not final authority merely because a worker validator passed.

Final negative authority requires:
- an exact current-grain object/member identity;
- a rule-specific necessary condition;
- governed current evidence that the necessary condition is incompatible;
- a bounded negative claim that negates only the typed relation, not empirical co-travel or unrelated semantics.

Cross-worker heuristics, keyword/topic mismatch, failure to recover a tie, or source-context incompleteness are insufficient.

Application at R16F:
- 26,507 worker-proposed candidate negatives were re-opened.
- Only six exact candidate antijoins are licensed by a revalidated current-grain prior negative authority (S14R8-4774be863a36: 2 T05 + 4 T06).
- All other worker-proposed candidate negatives become BLOCKED.
- Of nine prior R8C T03–T11 negative rulings, two have current exact R10 mapping and are revalidated; seven lack current mapped implementations and therefore remain blocked ancestry rather than negative authority.
