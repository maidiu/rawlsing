# Required judgments and methodological coverage

## Fourteen explicit answers

1. **Months, years or effectively never?** A bounded release using existing functions could plausibly finish in months once inputs/execution are available. This is a planning judgment, not measured throughput. Literal bespoke expansion is years-scale; unbounded prerequisite reopening is effectively nonterminating.
2. **How many Wright-like builds?** Fourteen cards are observed. Copying eleven scheduled roles across them gives 154 task instances. A 50–200-mechanism planning scenario at 10–25 roles gives 500–5,000. Replication at that granularity is unacceptable and contrary to current batching law.
3. **Maximum canaries?** One M10 exemplar plus at most two materially distinct operator cases before routine batches. Three is a budget, not proof of universal coverage. New content alone does not justify a new campaign.
4. **Employee answer without named employer?** Yes as an observed report and exact semantic employment indicator, contributing to a graded composite. It does not prove assetlessness, exploitation, employer-specific relations or the complete D12 instance.
5. **Attitudes as psychological evidence?** Yes, noisy expressed orientations tied to a measurement context. Use them for measurement/conditioning and validate on disjoint outcomes/semantic families; do not predict an item that defines the construct.
6. **DDM with uncertain causal direction?** Yes for descriptive and validated predictive use. Preserve scope, denominator, semantic meaning, uncertainty and exact permission. No intervention effect or deterministic biography follows.
7. **Unknown reduces confidence?** Sometimes. Preserve known evidence; marginalize or bound relevant unknowns. Some unknowns are irrelevant to the narrower claim, while a decisive missing premise blocks the exact composite. Contradictions can require rival models.
8. **Too much T03/N04 jurisdiction?** Yes if treated as universal evidence admission. Current law already distinguishes uses; R4C's pending return explicitly preserves non-T03 usefulness. The correction is a served positive route, not lower structural standards.
9. **Finish broad PRE-V3 before final seam design?** Yes: ground design in actual evidence products. Start a small V3 pilot when minimum outputs exist, then widen; do not wait for all surveys or a complete theory of psyche.
10. **What is finished PRE-V3?** A declared corpus release reproducibly supplies useful observations, semantic/structural indicators, empirical/model products and consumers with explicit gaps. A minimum seam-ready pilot differs from broad corpus completion; typed residual accounting alone satisfies neither empirical usefulness nor semantic completeness.
11. **What old machinery should return?** Concepts and proxy/item hypotheses as repaired, provisional comparisons; useful uncertainty principles and lineage. Preserve a historical comparator if separately authorized. Do not restore the quarantined terminal matrix or obsolete carrier topology.
12. **Role of expert judgment?** Necessary for candidate constructs, priors, interactions and scope under uncertainty. Label it, compare alternatives, disclose sensitivity, test and replace. Expert inference cannot create source meaning or observed data.
13. **Best sociological use of DDM?** Run governed discovery, confirmation, cross-context tests and theory-guided moderation over joint respondents; retain surprises, minorities and negative results. Let findings revise proxies/constructs while keeping association distinct from cause.
14. **Fastest path to better persons?** Consume ready returns, execute adopted empirical services, complete useful meanings, compare simple conditional models with current baselines, then approve the smallest V3 consumer. Retain heterogeneity and later life processes. If complexity fails, ship the simpler honest model within scope.

## What would change the verdict?

A currently adopted broad positive V3 predictive consumer with demonstrated scope/validation would narrow the missing-use finding. A real current batch serving the 42 non-T03 rows would close the demonstrated output concern. Measured predictive improvement from counterpart detail could justify that field for that task. Better calibration/transfer from more complex theory could earn its maintenance cost; failure would favor the smaller model.

This is a revisable evaluation. It does not declare the current contracts sacred, nor treat a promising hypothesis as established fact.
