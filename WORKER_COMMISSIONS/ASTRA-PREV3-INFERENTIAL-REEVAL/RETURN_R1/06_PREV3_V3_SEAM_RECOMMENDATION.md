# Recommended empirical-to-V3 seam

## Choose functional completion with a bounded model-use extension

| Alternative | Benefit | Defect | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact-world-only route | Strong rule/fact/application distinctions | Starves statistical use of co-observed attitudes if made universal | Keep for exact consequences, reject as sole route |
| Restored historical driver→type pipeline | Existing intuitions and quick outputs | Duplicated proxies, uncalibrated weights, obsolete topology and quarantine | Preserve hypotheses/comparator, reject production resurrection |
| Current empirical/query/PSEPF services plus scoped theory and V3 model use | Preserves inherited functions and permits tested inference | Requires concrete evidence/model products and exact permission where absent | RECOMMENDED |

Current Trii/PRE553/PSEPF and FIELD FORGE contracts are the starting point. The exact defect is not absence of a conceptual empirical-to-person route; it is insufficient demonstrated breadth and a general calibrated model-use permission/consumer for the proposed seeding task. Determine the small missing interface against actual outputs, not an imagined new universal architecture. ([E23](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e23)–[E25](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e25))

## Data and ownership flow

1. Source owners provide observations, category/routing meaning, identity and time.
2. Current empirical services preserve same-respondent structural and attitude information. Composite indicators remain explicitly model-derived unless source semantics independently warrant them.
3. Exact/adaptive discovery yields scoped query/fact products. A confirmation step fits and validates models with compatible denominators and declared search coverage.
4. FIELD FORGE can serve neutral relations; SDTF supplies exact objective consequences where warranted and mechanism hypotheses elsewhere. Neither may silently own psychological state.
5. Current comparator/PSEPF functions condition empirical possibilities when their exact authority applies. V3 owns any generated tendency and integration with its existing person architecture.

A synthetic actor ID is not a surveyed respondent ID. A recorded attitude is evidence of a response, not an objective institutional fact. It need not be relabeled “world structure” to survive upstream.

## Proposed consumer contract

The smallest first operation is **probabilistic seeding from a conditional empirical model**, explicitly distinguished from intervention, current actor transition and life-course development.

The contract records evidence/model/version, source scope and time, analysis unit, output meaning, constitutive/predictor/context roles, forbidden target lineage, shared-evidence dependencies, missingness/uncertainty, validation, permitted operation and stochastic/replay identity. Reuse OBL034 current selection, producer publication and existing V3 owners rather than invent parallel ownership.

If a current positive interface only accepts ACTIVE_CAUSAL, review a distinct predictive use at that boundary. Do not forge causal permission. This proposal does not close OBL061 or OBL062, nor settle PFF/V3 convergence or generic non-event ingress. Current CONTEXT-only GT12 and OBL063 quarantine remain intact.

## Statistical composition without double counting

A possible factorization is:

P(A_test | S, C, A_in) = integral P(A_test | Z, C) P(Z | S, C, A_in) dZ.

Z is a modeled latent tendency; S structural indicators, C context, A_in permitted attitude inputs, A_test disjoint outcomes. This is not an identified causal graph. Direct conditional prediction is a legitimate simpler competitor.

An item used to measure Z cannot also count as an independent DDM pressure. Hierarchy and authorityStyle computed from one country score are one empirical source. Theory describing an observed association is not independent replication. Model covariance/shared lineage or select a single contribution.

Expert mappings can use bounded effect ranges and no-effect/opposite-effect sensitivity where plausible. They are model assumptions; call them posterior probabilities only when a probability model and data justify that term.

## Theory as testable contrasts

The Forge's coordinator synthesis covers eleven source returns; it was inspected as repository evidence, not treated as fresh reading of every book. ([E22](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e22))

| Tradition | Distinction to test |
|---|---|
| Kohn/Schooler, Kohn/Słomczyński, Mortimer | Work content, recurrence and institutional context versus job title/hours; reciprocal selection |
| Sève and Dreier | Concurrent activity contexts under finite time; location is not position or motive |
| Elder and social reproduction | Linked lives redistribute resources/tasks; age qualifies pathways rather than directly causing a trait |
| Holzkamp | Objective conditions, perceived possibilities, reasons and actions are distinct |
| Luria | Historical/population contrasts bundle education/practice/institutions; group difference is not within-person development |

A few regularized feature blocks and interactions can test these contrasts. Eleven books need not create eleven new operator campaigns. Failed operationalization should simplify or revise the model.

## Worked incomplete example

A respondent reports wage employment, resource strain and a control-related answer. Assets and employer identity are unknown.

Preserve all reports and scope. Employee status is semantically usable; exact asset-separated wage dependency remains uncertain. Structure-only prediction withholds the control answer as outcome. Evidence-conditioned generation may use it, but validates against another outcome. V3 samples a distribution for a synthetic person within scope; no deterministic trait, trauma history or inevitable future is assigned. No employer-specific consequence is fabricated.

## Development remains a separate problem

Cross-sectional distributions can improve initialization but do not specify a transition kernel. Applying the same association every simulated year would repeatedly count the same evidence. A provisional persistence assumption may hold a sampled tendency stable, with sensitivity analysis; subsequent change must use independently specified temporal/process models and existing V3 owners.

The research target is an improved prior or conditional distribution, followed by richer life processes. A better held-out attitude score is evidence of one gain, not proof of a complete historically plausible person. Preserve heterogeneity, contradictions, agency and the possibility of changed conditions.
