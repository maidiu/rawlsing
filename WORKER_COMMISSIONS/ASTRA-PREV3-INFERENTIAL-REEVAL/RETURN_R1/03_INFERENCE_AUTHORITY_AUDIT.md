## Independent epistemic axes, not a ladder

A sequence observation → theory → psyche falsely implies increasing certainty. Use independent dimensions:

| Axis | Values / meaning |
|---|---|
| Claim kind | observation; semantic interpretation; composite measurement; association; validated prediction; latent estimate; mechanistic hypothesis; identified causal effect; expert prior; generated state |
| Unit/scope | respondent/item/time; household/institution; source/population/context; synthetic actor |
| Support | supporting, contradictory, unknown; coverage and shared-evidence dependencies |
| Uncertainty | sampling, measurement, model, transport, time |
| Validation | untested; internal; held-out respondent/item/context; replication; causal design |
| Use | inspection, measurement, prediction, seeding, scenario, intervention, exact world derivation |
| Provenance/status | evidence and model versions; source currentness distinct from model selection; provisional/released/superseded/quarantined |

A confidence label is not an estimated probability. Exact provenance does not establish construct validity. Released-for-prediction is not causally established. Strong FIELD/CAPITAL/MECHANISM names still need competent semantics; neutral dependence evidence remains usable without them.

## Employee status without a named employer

Yes: a source-scoped anonymous respondent key can bear an observed self-report. With exact question meaning, it can support a semantic employment indicator. A named employer is unnecessary for that claim.

The public WVS7 instrument distinguishes current/main-job employment (Q279), current or last occupation (Q281), and father's occupation at respondent age 14 (Q283). Q285–Q288 distinguish chief-earner status, finances, subjective class and household income. These are documentary examples, not newly adopted Rawlsing bindings. ([E16](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e16); [E15](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e15) confirms occupation-subject/time distinctions)

Represent separately:

- reported wage employment and temporal scope;
- productive-asset evidence, if measured;
- household resources, transfers and income-source dependence;
- authority/discretion or contractual conditions, if measured;
- supporting, contradictory and unknown indicators.

Employee status alone is evidence of participation in wage work, not strong evidence of insufficient assets. Combined independent resource/income indicators can support graded livelihood-dependence inference. An unresolved counterpart role can represent an existential relation; it must not become a fictitious employer edge.

Wright's supplied text itself distinguishes concept formation constrained by theory from empirical adjudication between rival concepts. The inspected survey operationalization uses task/sanction/decision variables rather than waiting for respondents to supply a finished class ontology. That supports testing transparent composites, while preserving the difference between proxy, relation and psychological consequence. This is an inference from inspected passages, not a claim that Wright validates our proposed mapping. ([E13](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e13), [E27](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e27))

## Unknown does not erase known

Unknown is not false. Retain atomic observations even when a composite cannot be estimated. Marginalize missing predictors under an explicit model or give sensitivity bounds. Informative missingness requires assumptions or bounds, not a mechanical confidence discount.

Nor does every unknown necessarily reduce confidence. Employer identity may be irrelevant to employee status and essential to an employer-specific sanction. Missing a decisive premise can block the exact composite without invalidating its inputs. Contradictory evidence may require alternative models; more data can reduce support.

Hard stops remain appropriate for ambiguous respondent joins, unidentified source, unknown category meaning for the intended interpretation, undefined probability denominator, and an active-world assertion without its required validity evidence. They block the affected use.

## Theory and expert judgment

Expert judgment may choose constructs, weak priors, interactions, measurement alternatives and scope. Record rationale, countermodels, evidence lineage, uncertainty, sensitivity and a replacement/validation condition. Include zero and opposite effects when substantively plausible. Do not call an expert range a posterior without a probability model.

The Forge's D1/D2/D3 → D1′ reduction limited ownership, not the scientific value of noncausal models. Theory can propose mediators and boundary conditions without monopolizing discovery. DDM may confirm, contradict or replace a legacy proxy. No analyst model may relabel its assumptions as source semantics to avoid the distinction.

This evaluation recommends changed jurisdiction and specific future model uses; it grants none.
