# A finite PRE-V3 finish line

## Two milestones with honest scope

Finish useful empirical representation before trying to settle the entire structure-to-personality theory. Begin a V3 research consumer against early real outputs, then broaden the same production path. Do not require every governed scope, driver or mechanism before starting.

Distinguish **SEAM_READY_MINIMUM** from **CORPUS_PRODUCTION_COMPLETE_FOR_RELEASE**. Neither means complete sociology or final personality validity.

## SEAM_READY_MINIMUM

These are proposed acceptance gates, not current achievements.

| Criterion | Nonvacuous acceptance |
|---|---|
| Fixed denominator | Account for all 18,276 current implementation IDs, including 41 review and 22 unresolved; enumerate actual source-family/scope separately |
| Real joint observations | Process complete selected accessible sources from at least two families and three country/wave slices, with source-scoped keys and reconciled counts |
| Useful semantics | Each selected family supplies at least three structural/material domains and three attitude domains, with exact response/missingness/routing meaning and exclusions |
| Partial knowledge survives | Observed evidence, semantic references, time, known/unknown/conflicting status retained for each processed respondent; blocked composite does not delete observations |
| Empirical bridge | One structural-to-attitude and one attitude-to-attitude model on a frozen panel, with baseline, uncertainty and held-out results |
| Reusable mechanism path | One rule envelope compiled/replayed through adopted generic machinery; no fabricated M10 actor witness |
| Consumer | Existing producer/query/publication functions supply an actual V3 sandbox distribution under an explicitly approved model use |
| Reproducibility | Rebuild one full slice from pinned data/code/semantics/model inputs and demonstrate local invalidation without unrelated loss |
| Practicality | Measure time/memory on real data and project scale; no unexplained per-question manual loop |

Three domains and two families are budgeted minimum breadth, not psychometric sufficiency. Select them before viewing held-out performance. A physically neutral release can expose exact dereference/query recipes; no persistent master payload is required.

The representation may succeed while the complex model fails. Keep the simpler validated model and record the negative result. Completion is not conditional on proving a hybrid superior.

## CORPUS_PRODUCTION_COMPLETE_FOR_RELEASE

For the explicitly frozen available corpus:

- all accessible carriers are processed through accepted identity/decoder routes;
- every implementation receives an observation/semantics/permitted-use disposition;
- all rows eligible for each declared empirical product are produced through the batch path;
- respondent, routing, time, nonresponse and source distinctions survive;
- consumers retrieve positive evidence/models as well as absence statuses;
- validation and uncertainty match the claimed source/population/transport scope;
- a release manifest lists inaccessible carriers, unsolved meanings and deferred products.

Accounting for a row is not making its meaning usable. A release with 18,012 unresolved response-semantic joins cannot be called semantically complete merely because it has dispositions. Publish the useful subset honestly and keep the remaining coverage obligation visible. Freeze scope prospectively; never improve the score by deleting difficult cases after results arrive.

The inherited 9-family/35-scope inventory is not “35 survey families”; WVS7's later 66 country scopes use another denominator. Keep every denominator named. ([E23](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e23))

## Necessary prerequisites and later backlog

Before a model's named use: lawful respondent linkage, meaningful outcome categories, proper risk sets, response/missingness handling, target-leakage controls, exact provenance and permitted use. Population weighting, pooling, transport and exact current-world claims each need their own evidence when claimed.

Later improvements that do not block the minimum:

| Enhancement | Why it can wait |
|---|---|
| Remaining source families/scopes | Extend the proven production route while early outputs inform the seam |
| Broad translation/measurement invariance | Needed for broader comparability claims, not honest source-local results |
| Rich nonlinear clouds/latent models | Compare simple conditional models first |
| Longitudinal transition kernels | Cross-sectional data can inform seeding, not identify developmental dynamics |
| Complete employer/household topology | Needed for particular exact-world claims, not all empirical modeling |
| Every P class and every legacy driver | Preserve hypotheses without forcing a complete vector |
| General physical architecture winner | Execute the selected bounded workload without claiming universal backend superiority |
| Comprehensive psychological realism | Begin with observable constraints and criterion performance; do not equate them with a complete person |

Current exact PSEPF/comparator use remains constrained by its admitted premises. Broad pilot readiness does not authorize an encounter, current actor fact or OBL061 transition by implication.

## Budgets and stop rules

After an accepted execution surface is usable, budget two weeks for a full evidence slice, baseline, first conditional model and readable payload; one further two-week interval for a distinct context and comparison. These are scheduling limits, not measured forecasts.

If two intervals produce only receipts, HQ changes scope, owner or execution surface and names the obstruction. It does not commission another general architecture review. After two unsuccessful complexity increments, choose the simpler model unless new measurement explains the failure.

A local missing source, failed transport or blocked exact fact blocks that use, not unrelated empirical production. HQ's no-all-lanes-barrier law remains binding.
