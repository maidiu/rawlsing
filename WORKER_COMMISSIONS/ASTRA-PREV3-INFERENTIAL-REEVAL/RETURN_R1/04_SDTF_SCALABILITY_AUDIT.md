# SDTF scalability and the canary stop rule

## What the canary bought

Adopted SDTF D1′ has five primitive classes: material access/dependency; directed authority/obligation/sanction; organized activity/time; institutional gate/treatment; counterpart/channel relation. Rule, application, consequence and witness have distinct identity/currentness. Unknown does not prove a negative; invalidation/replay is local. These prevent real errors. ([E12](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e12))

The original Forge already sought a finite bridge, not completion of sociology. Its post-review reduction from eighteen families to five primitives reused temporal and reciprocal owners. Later P/Q/R/S/T work separated reusable position meaning from occupant identity, specified 28 determinants and six batch packages, and built generic adapters. HQ decisions 022/024/028 explicitly prohibit repeated bespoke infrastructure. ([E01](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e01), [E22](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e22))

Formal consistency and traceability do not prove predictive validity or generated-person usefulness. They protect the integrity of the claim being made. Requiring an actual world application before any noncausal prediction would protect the wrong claim.

## Observed workload and transparent extrapolation

Fourteen M10 mechanism cards were inspected. D05–D14 plus D10Q comprise eleven named work roles: eight rule/reference/assembly/selection roles and three witness/materialization/application roles. This is a role count, not measured agent-hours. Earlier generic infrastructure is not charged anew to each content item. ([E13](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e13), [E14](11_EVIDENCE_INDEX.md#e14))

A bounded release using existing machinery is plausibly a months-scale project. A literal artisanal regime is years-scale; indefinitely reopened prerequisites are effectively nonterminating. Actual throughput and mechanism inventory must be measured before stronger scheduling claims.

At the sealed checkpoint D05–D08 are accepted, leaving four rule-side units: D09 → D10 → D10Q → D11. The pending D09 return now supplies its explicit nonnumeric calibration N/A; after adoption, three rule-side units remain. D12's missing real witness still blocks D13 and a real D14 application locally. Generic batch machinery does not wait for D14.

## What becomes data-driven batch work

Separate two responsibilities:

1. Content/model authorship supplies meaning, required inputs, claim type, scope, competing interpretations, uncertainty and validation.
2. Deterministic compilation validates exact bindings, preserves provenance, computes identities, deduplicates exact-equal cores, rejects contradictions and emits the existing constructor shape.

A compiler enforces a warranted contract; hashing cannot discover a warrant or choose the true theory. For exact D1′ rules retain the 28 determinants/six packages. Reference stable class-level obligations and review content-specific changes only. Bundle genuinely applicable nonnumeric N/A policies rather than repeat separate ceremonies for unchanged premises.

Predictive models use empirical/model manifests and permitted uses. Do not force every conditional distribution into an R4 causal-rule envelope. Current learned-product lifecycle and FIELD FORGE query contracts should carry the reusable portions. No new generic compiler project is justified by content volume alone.

## Canary ceiling and hard stop

**At most three hand-built operator cases before routine batching** is the recommended budget:

- one nonnumeric exact/qualified objective consequence, using M10;
- one calibrated measurement/prediction route with held-out results;
- one temporal/termination or dependency-composition case genuinely needed by the first release.

These cover failure modes, not three favorite theorists. The additional cases are not universal prerequisites for the first batch. Five P classes may receive inexpensive conformance fixtures without five full source campaigns.

Three is an engineering budget, not a theorem of completeness. A fourth bespoke case needs a demonstrated new operator/defect, affected consumer, bounded repair and removal of redundant procedure. A new survey, author or rule paragraph is insufficient.

Finish D11, freeze the rule row and reusable checks, then batch new eligible content. Do not continue mining a theory book for a real employer witness. A real witness can later unlock D13/D14 without freezing statistical research.
