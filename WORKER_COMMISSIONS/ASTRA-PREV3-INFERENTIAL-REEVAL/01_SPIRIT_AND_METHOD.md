# ASTRA PRE-V3 INFERENTIAL RE-EVALUATION — BINDING SPIRIT AND METHOD DIRECTIVE

This file is a **binding methodological supplement** to `00_COMMISSION.md`.

It exists because the quality of this review depends not only on what Astra inspects, but on the intellectual posture with which it inspects it.

The review must not become another compliance audit whose highest virtue is finding reasons that Rawlsing cannot act.

Nor may it become a license for arbitrary hand-waving.

The required posture is **rigorously creative social-scientific model building under uncertainty, oriented toward a finite working Rawlsing**.

---

# 1. OPERATING SPIRIT

Operate as a scientist-engineer-theorist trying to make the best possible inferential machine from imperfect evidence, not as a clerk checking whether reality has already supplied completed ontology objects.

Be willing to say:

> `the evidence does not prove X, but X is the best current model because A/B/C converge, DDM supports it, theory explains it, alternatives fit worse, and uncertainty can be represented explicitly.`

That is not a failure of rigor.

That is often what serious empirical social science actually requires.

The project should distinguish:

- direct observation;
- semantic interpretation;
- statistical association;
- prediction;
- clustering / attitude-cloud structure;
- abductive inference;
- theory-guided interpretation;
- expert/model judgment;
- causal hypothesis;
- developmental/personality pressure;
- synthesized person-state.

Do not collapse these into one binary of `PROVED` versus `UNAUTHORIZED`.

---

# 2. THE CENTRAL QUESTION IS NOT "CAN THIS BE PROVED?"

For any proposed mapping or inference, ask instead:

1. **What evidence bears on it?**
2. **How strong is that evidence?**
3. **What kind of evidence is it?**
4. **What alternative interpretations exist?**
5. **Does DDM support, weaken, condition, or contradict it?**
6. **Does serious social-scientific theory supply a plausible mechanism or boundary condition?**
7. **Can the inference be represented probabilistically or with graded confidence rather than forced into a Boolean?**
8. **Can it be validated on held-out items, another survey, another country/wave, another respondent subset, or another source family?**
9. **Would using it make Rawlsing materially better at generating plausible socially situated persons?**
10. **What is the cost of refusing to infer?**

The burden is not to eliminate uncertainty before acting.

The burden is to **represent uncertainty honestly and make the best available inference anyway when that is scientifically and practically justified**.

---

# 3. RAWLSING IS ALLOWED TO DISCOVER THINGS

Do not treat external theory as the only source of sociological knowledge.

Rawlsing's own empirical artifacts may reveal relationships that no source text states explicitly.

In particular, DDM / attitude-cloud outputs can show:

- response X predicts response Y;
- structural pattern S shifts the distribution of attitude A;
- multiple attitudinal variables form a stable or conditional cloud;
- demographic/material/institutional variables differentiate psychological or value responses;
- a supposed proxy is weak while an unexpected variable is much stronger;
- a relationship holds only in some countries, cohorts, classes, religions, institutions, or historical settings;
- apparently separate indicators are redundant;
- apparently similar constructs behave differently;
- conditional interactions matter more than simple main effects.

These are empirical findings.

They are not automatically causal findings.

But they may legitimately constrain, motivate, weight, qualify, or revise PRE-V3 -> V3 mappings.

Astra should actively look for ways to let **Rawlsing's own empirical discoveries discipline Rawlsing's theory**, and vice versa.

---

# 4. DO NOT UNDERVALUE ATTITUDINAL QUESTIONS

Many survey questions are already partial observations of psychological tendencies.

A response expressing:

- distrust;
- conformity;
- authoritarian preference;
- fear;
- optimism;
- punitive moral judgment;
- interpersonal suspicion;
- traditionalism;
- openness to difference;
- autonomy preference;
- deference;
- egalitarianism;
- religiosity;
- locus of control;
- norm rigidity;
- social dominance;
- family obligation;
- tolerance;
- risk orientation;
- future orientation;

is not merely metadata about a social world.

It is psychologically relevant evidence attached to a respondent who also carries structural and demographic attributes.

The coexistence of these fields is one of Rawlsing's most valuable empirical resources.

Treat the **joint respondent record** as the central object of inference.

---

# 5. USE ABDUCTION, NOT JUST DEDUCTION

Astra is explicitly authorized to use **abductive reasoning**:

> what model best explains the observed pattern, given the evidence, theory, and alternatives?

This includes proposing candidate latent drivers, mediator concepts, interaction effects, evidence tiers, graded predicates, and probabilistic V3 pressures even when no single source explicitly names them.

Such proposals must be:

- labeled as inference or hypothesis rather than observation;
- grounded in actual evidence;
- compared against plausible alternatives;
- made falsifiable or at least testable where possible;
- given scope/boundary conditions;
- assigned uncertainty/confidence rather than declared metaphysical truths.

The absence of a source sentence saying `X causes Y` is not itself a reason to refuse a model if the empirical and theoretical case is otherwise strong.

---

# 6. CHUTZPAH IS REQUIRED — BUT DISCIPLINED CHUTZPAH

Do not merely catalog problems.

Generate solutions.

If the current ontology lacks an epistemic grade needed to express a useful inference, propose one.

If the old driver/facet mappings contain useful intuition but bad implementation, salvage and transform them.

If DDM exposes a better predictor than the legacy proxy, say so.

If an SDTF mechanism is too coarse, condition it on empirical modifiers.

If the survey corpus supports a graded structural inference rather than an exact structural fact, propose the graded representation.

If the current PRE-V3/V3 boundary is misplaced, argue for a better boundary.

If one generic compiler can replace fifty bespoke courts, design the compiler contract at the level necessary to prove the point.

If three different inferential routes should coexist rather than be forced into one ontology, propose that.

If a legacy mapping is scientifically dubious but useful as a baseline, preserve it as a baseline rather than pretending it is authority.

If no published theory perfectly bridges an empirical pattern to a V3 facet, Astra may propose a **provisional expert mapping** when the case is reasonable, provided the mapping is marked as such and can later be calibrated or replaced.

The audit should contain **ideas Rawlsing does not already have** when those ideas follow from the evidence and resolve real project problems.

Do not confuse originality with architecture churn: innovate only where innovation buys inferential power, scalability, validation, or completion.

---

# 7. PRACTICALITY IS AN EPISTEMIC CONSTRAINT TOO

The user's need to finish Rawlsing is not an embarrassing externality. It is a real engineering constraint.

A method that is theoretically immaculate but requires ten years of artisanal adjudication is not automatically superior to a slightly more approximate method that:

- preserves provenance;
- exposes uncertainty;
- can be validated empirically;
- scales across the corpus;
- produces useful outputs;
- and can improve later.

Astra must explicitly evaluate **marginal epistemic return per unit of complexity/work**.

Ask repeatedly:

> Does this additional court/ontology distinction/authority layer materially improve empirical validity or prevent a serious class of error?

If not, it is a candidate for removal, generalization, batching, or demotion from mandatory gate to diagnostic metadata.

Do not equate more procedure with more truth.

---

# 8. PRESERVE THE BEST PARTS OF THE OLD RAWLSING

The legacy question -> driver/facet -> personality-pressure architecture should not be discarded merely because some of its mappings were heuristic.

Treat it as:

- accumulated domain insight;
- a hypothesis set;
- a baseline model;
- a source of candidate mappings;
- a comparison target for the new machinery.

For each legacy component, ask whether it should be:

- retained;
- empirically recalibrated;
- semantically repaired;
- split into finer constructs;
- merged with DDM-derived structure;
- constrained by SDTF theory;
- downgraded to provisional/expert status;
- or rejected.

A useful old heuristic should not be thrown away merely because the new system can describe its uncertainty more honestly.

---

# 9. THEORY SHOULD CONSTRAIN AND EXPLAIN — NOT MONOPOLIZE THE MODEL

SDTF theory mining should provide:

- candidate mechanisms;
- causal stories;
- boundary conditions;
- mediators;
- moderators;
- expected directions;
- counterexamples;
- institutional and historical contingencies;
- concepts that help interpret DDM structure.

It should not require Rawlsing to wait until every empirical relationship has a bespoke theorist-authored warrant.

The desired relation is:

`theory <-> empirical corpus <-> DDM <-> ontology/model`

Each may correct the others.

Theory without corpus discipline risks scholasticism.

Corpus association without theory risks uninterpretable pattern-mining.

The project should synthesize them.

---

# 10. RUN COMPETING MODELS AGAINST ONE ANOTHER

Where possible, Astra should propose evaluation designs that compare:

- the legacy heuristic/vector pipeline;
- a semantics-only pipeline;
- a DDM-informed pipeline;
- an SDTF/theory-informed pipeline;
- a hybrid DDM + theory + semantic pipeline.

Use held-out questions, surveys, waves, countries, or respondent subsets where possible.

If the expensive new machinery does not improve prediction, stability, interpretability, portability, or calibration, that is evidence against retaining its complexity.

The project must earn complexity.

---

# 11. BE PRECISE ABOUT CAUSATION WITHOUT BECOMING PARALYZED BY IT

Do not call association causation.

But do not behave as though only causal identification can inform a generative model.

Rawlsing's task is not to publish a single causal-effect estimate.

It is to generate plausible socially and historically situated persons.

Predictive and associational information may therefore be legitimately useful even when causal direction remains uncertain, especially if:

- the model labels the relation appropriately;
- theory supplies plausible directionality;
- alternative causal stories are represented;
- the effect is bounded rather than deterministic;
- validation shows predictive utility;
- leakage is controlled.

Treat causal uncertainty as a parameter of the model, not an automatic veto.

---

# 12. DO NOT LET "FAIL CLOSED" MEAN "THROW AWAY PARTIAL KNOWLEDGE"

Fail-closed behavior is appropriate when a contract requires a specific authority and execution would otherwise fabricate it.

It is not appropriate as a universal epistemology.

If five indicators support a structural inference and one premise is unknown, Rawlsing should usually preserve the five known indicators and represent the remaining uncertainty.

Unknown != false.

Partial evidence != no evidence.

Conflicting evidence != no model.

A good system may output:

- strong support;
- moderate support;
- weak support;
- contradictory evidence;
- unknown coverage;
- multiple plausible states.

Astra should identify where current PRE-V3 contracts prevent this unnecessarily.

---

# 13. SEEK THE FINITE FINISH LINE

The audit must not merely produce a better philosophical description of Rawlsing.

It must answer:

> **What is the smallest scientifically defensible PRE-V3 we can actually finish that gives us enough real empirical structure to design and calibrate the V3 seam?**

Astra should actively search for a finish line where:

- all relevant survey evidence is semantically usable;
- respondent-level structural and attitudinal evidence can be represented;
- DDM products can inform probabilistic inferences;
- a bounded set of generic SDTF mechanism machinery is proven;
- legacy drivers/facets are reconciled rather than discarded;
- uncertainty is explicit;
- batch production replaces artisanal adjudication;
- objective-world artifacts are sufficiently populated to support the seam;
- and remaining refinements can occur **after** an end-to-end Rawlsing exists.

If a proposed requirement does not need to precede the first end-to-end system, say so and move it to a later refinement stage.

---

# 14. DO NOT BE CAPTURED BY CURRENT ARCHITECTURE

Current contracts, names, lanes, and ontologies are evidence about what has been built. They are not sacred metaphysics.

Respect already-proven invariants when they protect real correctness.

But if a current abstraction is preventing Rawlsing from expressing scientifically useful graded inference, Astra may recommend changing it.

Do not assume the current ontology is correct simply because much work has accumulated around it.

Likewise, do not recommend wholesale redesign unless the evidence shows it is necessary.

Seek the **smallest high-leverage correction**.

---

# 15. REQUIRED TONE OF THE RETURN

The return should be:

- intellectually ambitious;
- empirically grounded;
- skeptical of false certainty;
- skeptical of false impossibility;
- willing to synthesize across sociology, psychometrics, predictive modeling, survey research, causal reasoning, and software architecture;
- willing to make provisional judgments;
- brutally alert to scalability;
- biased toward a finite executable model rather than infinite preparatory work;
- explicit about uncertainty;
- concrete enough that HQ can act on it immediately.

Do not reward the existing project merely for complexity already incurred.

Do not destroy useful rigor merely because it is inconvenient.

Determine what rigor is **actually doing epistemic work**, what has become procedural overgrowth, and what new inferential machinery would make Rawlsing both more scientifically intelligent and more finishable.

---

# 16. FINAL MANDATE

Approach this as though the future of the project depends on answering one question correctly:

> **How can Rawlsing think boldly from imperfect evidence without lying to itself about what it knows?**

The answer must preserve both halves.

A system that invents freely without evidence is useless.

A system that refuses to infer from evidence is also useless.

Find the disciplined middle that lets Rawlsing **learn, infer, model, generate, validate, revise, and finish**.
