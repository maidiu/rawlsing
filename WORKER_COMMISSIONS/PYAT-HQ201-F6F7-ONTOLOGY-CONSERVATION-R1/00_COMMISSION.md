## Core ontology questions

Answer each with an exact evidence class and current owner/disposition.

### O1 — What persists through time?

What object can be said to be "the same thing at t1 and t2"?

Candidates to distinguish:
- source-native question/response surface
- empirical coordinate
- predictor surface
- branch/configuration
- joint/cloud object
- structural proposition
- structural field coordinate
- pressure relation

State the exact identity ingredients required:
semantic identity, response surface, universe/routing, estimand, weights, structural interpretation, effective time, etc.

If different object families need different temporal identity contracts, say so.

### O2 — What is a cross-wave relation?

Define the lawful relation between two comparable same-survey wave objects.

Distinguish:
- identity continuity
- comparability
- difference/delta
- directionality
- trajectory
- persistence
- rupture
- absence/noncomparability

Do not treat any one as automatic consequence of another.

### O3 — What is a cross-source relation?

If WVS and EVS both bear on a social-world proposition, what may lawfully relate them?

Distinguish:
- coexistence in one objective world
- corroboration
- contradiction
- complementarity
- structural composition
- portability
- respondent pooling
- same-respondent jointness

The last two are forbidden absent impossible-to-assume linkage authority.

Determine whether cross-source composition needs a typed relation object rather than a merged metric.

### O4 — How do F6 and F7 compose?

Model at least these cases:

A. same survey, two comparable waves
B. two surveys, same rough period, related proposition
C. two surveys with nonaligned fieldwork intervals
D. two surveys, each with its own temporal trajectory
E. one source comparable through time, another source only single-wave
F. conflicting temporal movement across sources
G. temporal relation blocked by noncomparability but cross-source structural coexistence still lawful

Determine what is orthogonal versus serial.

Explicitly adjudicate the prior HQ phrasing:
"F7 cannot honestly close before F6."

State the corrected dependency law:
- what F7 can close independently;
- what integrated PRE-V3 world claim requires F6+F7 composition.
