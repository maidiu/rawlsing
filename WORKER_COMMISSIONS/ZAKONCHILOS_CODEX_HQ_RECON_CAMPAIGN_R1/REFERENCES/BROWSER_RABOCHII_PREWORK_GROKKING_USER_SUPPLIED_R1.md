## 1. Common project intelligence — must be understood by every worker

### 1A. What Rawlsing is for

Rawlsing seeks **historically intelligible but not predetermined, independently continuing particular lives within materially and socially reproduced worlds**, eventually available for genuine lawful user encounter. Survey precision alone is not a person; a fluent character script is not a life. Persons are conditioned by work, care, property, organizations, relations, permissions, conflict and history, while their situated activities may reproduce or transform their conditions.

**Three different starting orders — do not swap them:**

1. **Ontology/explanation:** persons are formed through historical relations and activity, not synthesized directly from national survey averages.
2. **Rawlsing empirical production:** available evidence starts with instrument-, respondent-, wave-, context- and rights-qualified empirical sources, then warrants bounded distributions and alternatives. Plausible particulars in a synthetic world must be **constructed and admitted**, not mislabeled as observations.
3. **Design:** derive required capabilities **backward** from actual terminal person–world behavior, identify indispensable transformation and recipient at each owner, then build and prove the actual path **forward**. Do not require every optional physical stage to be traversed.

Five things must stay separate: **externally observed source fact; qualified scientific/model inference; lawfully generated canonical synthetic-world fact; what a particular actor knew/experienced/did; what a user can perceive or cause.** A generated world can be objective *within its admitted fictional continuity* without being a claim that each fictional particular was observed historically. A group mean, survey response or structural position cannot decree one person's belief, desire, destiny or utterance.
