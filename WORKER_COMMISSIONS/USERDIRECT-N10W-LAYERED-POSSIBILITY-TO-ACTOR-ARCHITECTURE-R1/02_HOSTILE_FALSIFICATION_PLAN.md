## Counterexample against too-facile demographic mechanism

A world may validly imply that people at position P frequently encounter schooling option O. It does **not** follow that actor i at P knows O. Nor that, if aware, O evokes the same desirability/moral meaning for i and j. An actor's objective past relations shape salience and practical capacities without fixing all possibilities. Thus the **mechanical element is the conditional historically warranted production of opportunity/contact/formation**; the conditional actor policy still needs an independently defended process. A deterministic all-class classifier fails T04; a pure LLM storyteller fails T18/T19; a fixed yes/no opportunity menu fails T10/T12.
