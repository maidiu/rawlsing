# Frozen candidate H4 — historically formed opportunity, meaning, action and becoming

**Status:** SCIENTIFIC_HYPOTHESIS_FROZEN_FOR_ADVERSARIAL_COURT__NOT_ADOPTED.

## 1. Unit of explanation

The explanatory unit is **a particular person in concrete social activity over historical time**, not a demographic row plus an independent probability of selecting a generic life route. Other actors and institutions are independent causal participants; the objective world has dynamics outside the focal person's perception. The person's actual formation gives a structured, changing orientation toward a finite, encountered field of action. Neither deterministic necessity nor randomness alone is adequate.

Hypothesis H4 requires three **composed** kinds of scientific apparatus:
1. **Empirical/structural priors and constraint sets:** historical-class/material/social/institutional conditioning, selected world rules and uncertainty, partial bounds where identified.
2. **Generated, witnessed formation:** birth/development/participation/relations/practical learning/history and partly inaccessible traces; initial priors are not the mature person.
3. **Real event-time causal decision process:** current exposure and material feasibility jointly with concrete actor history, projects, affect/expectation, conflicts, counterparty responses and lawful stochastic contingency; realized event and durable updates feed future conditions.

## 3. Two different probabilistic objects, plus interval knowledge

### Empirically estimable frequency
`P(route_attempt_by_T | actual qualified population, shared position, defined observation, observation process)`.
Requires a defined population, route event, time horizon, observable joint outcomes, inclusion/denominator, censoring/selection/informal channels, source rights and validation/calibration. Sourcewise marginal frequencies alone do not identify this. This is **not** the probability that a particular simulated person has personally conceived the route.

### Internally generative policy
`pi_model(attempt | actual actor history, accessible opportunity, situated relation, event and current state)`.
A lawfully approved stochastic selection among *physically/admissibly feasible* candidate actions may generate contingent lives even without historical joint samples. Until validated, **it is a declared simulation hypothesis, not an empirically justified historical propensity**. Exact random seed, model/prompt/owner rule and candidate-set semantics must be replayable; no magic category multipliers, covert value judgments or false precision. A low selection score cannot legalize an inadmissible action; a high score cannot guarantee successful external consequence.

### Partial identification/uncertainty
Where missingness, excluded populations or qualified monotonic constraints permit an interval, keep the set or interval. When no identified behavioral bridge exists, the full [0,1] empirical propensity set may remain. Do not collapse ignorance into a numerical selection prior, invented standard error, or false `0.5`. Model uncertainty, population heterogeneity, process stochasticity and unknown fact must be tagged separately.

### Useful conceptual factorization, NOT an adopted equation
With `W` world, `R` resources/position, `H` actual history, `O` practical opportunity, `E` exposure, `M` situated meaning, `A` attempted action, `K` counterpart/world contingencies:

```
P_model(A | W,R,H,context)
  = sum over (O,E,M,K) of
      P_model(A | O,E,M,K,W,R,H,context)
    × P_model(M | E,O,W,R,H,context)
    × P_model(E | O,W,R,H,context)
    × P_model(O,K | W,R,H,context)
```

This is only a possible full joint factorization once units and conditionals are defined; `K` may need to be temporally placed before or after a specific event. There is **no claim of conditional independence, identifiability, stationarity or adopted causal coefficients**. The point is that one global "route probability" currently compresses many causally distinct distributions with different warrants.

## 4. Historical habitus is not a frozen coefficient vector

- Class, gendered institution, colonial order, family economic position, social networks, education and generation can structure available encounters and the distribution of formation histories; they do not directly determine the person's exact subsequent acts.
- Habitus is historically acquired through participation, practices, dispositions, situated expectations, forms of recognition and constraints. Its manifestations can be plural and contradictory across contexts and epochs.
- One concrete person's computed history may include multiple conflicting projects, procedural tendencies and different degrees of conscious articulation. No need to collapse them into nine traits, an Enneagram type, a utility scalar or a single stable "pressure".
- Change must arise via causal learning/participation/occurrence/history update and declared stochastic contingency; cannot be assigned by a renderer making a plausible explanation.
- "What this represents to them" is often *derived at encounter time* from history + situation, not a stored fact for every hypothetical option. Actor introspection and model explanation are not guaranteed to coincide with operative causes.

## 5. Why the three methods are complementary

1. A structural feasible set says **what institutions and material positions make possible** (subject to uncertainty, illegality, informal access).
2. Partial bounds characterize **what the evidence excludes or cannot exclude**, often without determining a numeric choice.
3. A qualified recursive actor mechanism says **how actual developed persons differentially encounter, interpret, attempt, refuse and transform these possibilities**, with nonaction and history effects.
4. When real joint data exist, constrain or calibrate the stochastic components *at their own estimand*; never paste an unconditional cell frequency into a particular person's deliberation.
