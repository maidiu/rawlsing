## Specific competing tests required for genuine durable practice science
**H1 against H0:** register baseline competence before material opportunity exposure, actual later performed independent acts and *matched later opportunities* in contexts with different tasks, roles, incentives, interruptions and scarce resources. Count opportunities **including noaction and B substitution**. Ask whether subsequent A enactment after withholding tools/role changes is different *conditional on original sourced A activities* rather than preexisting ability or selection. Use independent process, repeated observation, properly qualified longitudinal comparisons or interventions, ideally negative-match controls and external domain checks. A fictional actor replaying its own sourcefree Rule_T is not confirmation.

**H2:** hold A history/role/material initial world constant, vary independent B's owner-attested performed share/refusal. Distinguish objective opportunity change from A's actually accessible observation, A's immediate action, and A's knowledge of B's private motivation. It is entirely lawful for differently sourced B acts to produce the same later spoken refusal.

**H3:** record dated competing projects, constraints and nonparticipation; do not assume a stable dominance ordering, universal conflict or guaranteed integrated growth. Under matched material opportunity, future acts may diverge depending on independent current commitments; positive observed action does not erase its contrary history.
