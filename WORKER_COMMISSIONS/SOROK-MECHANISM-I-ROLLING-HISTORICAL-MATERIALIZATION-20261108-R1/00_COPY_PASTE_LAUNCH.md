## 00. The single operation to implement

> A lawful foreground event makes an object, place, collection, artifact, resource or other addressable world particular available. Its asserted existence and specified surface facts become binding under the existing world owner. Immediately, a second, asynchronous LLM is given this exact newly admitted target, the already-existing Nadia and independent world/history/actor records that constrain it, and a bounded unresolved historical scope. It proposes physical particularity and historical/social provenance *behind* the surface. Rawlsing decides, under the present causal and custody owners, which proposed facts are lawful, and commits them with typed links back into existing world, persons, events, activities and actor-state references. The next inspection or independent actor/world activity can retrieve that already committed hidden depth instead of regenerating it; a newly exposed detail or required dependency creates the next bounded materialization job. The process rolls ahead of user attention without being authored by user attention.

The implementation is **not** an object-description generator and **not** a pile of lore paragraphs. It is the actual mechanically operating, persistent, provenance-bearing, causally constrained **historical-completion process**, with both foreground and background LLM calls (or exact compatible existing foreground producer) correctly joined to current owner state. The meaningful product is a richer **independently existing world**, not a more colorful response at one point of interaction.

The originally expressed scene pattern — Nadia has sandals, a bookshelf, an instrument, a mug — is an illustration of the *kinds* of truth to be generated; no such inventory, house, character stereotype or scene must be hardcoded into production. The generator must handle fresh, unseen targets through one typed engine, including objects Nadia values, objects she resents, objects whose social importance is not interpersonal, items she merely tolerates or cannot afford to replace, and ordinary objects with no remarkable past or emotional charge.

### One-line acceptance

`SURFACE ADMITTED → TARGET IDENTITY + DURABLE BACKGROUND DEMAND → REAL MODEL CANDIDATE → CAUSAL/HISTORICAL ADMISSION → OWNER-LINKED INDEXED DEPTH → ACTUAL LATER READER → NEW BOUNDED DEPTH DEMAND`.

## 02. Where the intellectual kernel genuinely enters the mechanism

The central ontology is **material particulars as historically constituted nodes of activity and social relations**. An object can hold traces of labor, production, class position, property, exchange, care, deprivation, access, durability, neglect, aesthetic practice, social norms, sacred or symbolic forms, a private actor's investments and relations with particular others. Different strata may coexist, collide or not exist. The system must be capable of *representing and retrieving those histories*, not assigning a universal symbolic label.

Three historical movements matter inside Mechanism I:

- **Sedimentation:** past actual activity and material conditions have produced the present target and its physical/historical relations. Sandal wear, inscriptions, position on a shelf, a book's pristine spine, a gift retained, a tool's repair, a photograph, a prestige commodity's purchase or a sacred object are specific material outcomes; they cannot be inferred merely from archetypal psychology.
- **Mediation:** the target has objective affordances, constraints and positions in existing material/social life. A book is readable/borrowable/replaceable, a chair occupies space, a car may be used for transport/display, an heirloom may be owned but practically useless. The materializer stores/source-links the conditions in which later activity *can* happen.
- **Historical linkage:** which qualified elements of Nadia's *already constituted* history, practices, relationships, needs, memory/access and social world have actually been involved with this target? Store exact parent/referent links and disclose unknowns without inventing durable psychic state.

**Fourth movement — situated psychological recruitment — is not implemented here.** The linked history is available for existing competent actors/world rules to use later, but this worker does not decide that Nadia is pleased, defensive, transformed or intimate when someone admires her book.

### The refinement that matters

`EVERY OBJECT IN NADIA'S ENVIRONMENT PARTICIPATES IN THE MATERIAL HISTORY OF HER LIFE; IT IS NOT NECESSARILY HER CHOSEN EXPRESSION, A LOVE TOKEN, OR PSYCHOLOGICALLY IMPORTANT.`

A landlord's appliance may represent material dependence rather than preference. A retained book may reflect obligations, difficulty discarding, class practice, personal learning, decorative display, a child's ownership, inertia or chance. A consumer object may matter through power/possession/status without a particular interpersonal memory. A ceremonial item may connect to institutional authority, use and devotion without Rawlsing treating any metaphysical religious claim as adjudicated world source fact. A work tool may involve other persons' alienated labor but no intimate relationship. The same object may possess mutually contradictory links.

Therefore no `GIFT_MEANS_CARE`, `LUXURY_MEANS_VANITY`, `BOOK_MEANS_INTELLECTUAL`, `RELIGION_MEANS_OBEDIENCE` or mandatory path `OBJECT→IMPORTANT_PERSON→NADIA_EMOTION`. These are **forbidden historical generators**. Preserve the possibility that a thing is just there, that evidence is thin, and that Nadia's own understanding may be partial or mistaken.

### Three independent axes, not one object-value number

The materializer needs to distinguish:
- **Objective material/historical life:** what an item is, how it came to exist, its production/circulation/use/condition/affordances and social-institutional place.
- **Qualified actor–target historical participation:** what Nadia actually did with or to it, who else did, what actual life episode and current owner-state it connects to. These are references to existing histories, not new motives.
- **Actor attribution/epistemics:** what Nadia knows, remembers, believes or claims about it **only insofar as competent existing actor/epistemic owners establish it**. Actor knowledge cannot be created by model suggestion.

A symbolic/social form may be independently real as a practice or institution without its supposed metaphysical cause being declared objectively true; an actor's claim about that form is actor-bound. Market/exchange, material usefulness, sensory gratification, devotion, identification and alienation are different relations. Mechanism I represents objective and already-established relations where feasible; it does not quantify or synthesize their psychic total.

### Crucial orthogonality

Sorok 70 H0/H1/H2/H3 describe **historical insertion's causal impact**, NOT how much someone loves a thing. H0 acquisition may be significant in an already-existing actor relation; H2 historical participation need not create current attachment. An object may be meaningful through present V3 organization without a dramatic object-specific origin event. The generator must not upgrade H0 to H2 because a backstory “feels emotionally important.”

## 04. The two cooperating LLM lanes, physically distinguished

The foreground and historical lanes have separate jobs, I/O and authority. They may be implemented through the current model abstraction; **two functional lanes do not imply exactly two total LLM calls**. Ordinary scene transduction/actor realization may already use two calls; the historical completion call is extra background inference, not a free reuse.

### Lane F — foreground lawful presentation

Inputs: existing authorized actor/world snapshot, present scene/action, material conditions, actor access, relevant previously committed historical depth and pending-result status.

Outputs: proposed outward occurrence and material assertions that existing world/actor owners may admit. For example, Nadia's living environment visibly contains sandals on the floor and books on a shelf. The target's objective existence and whatever specific attributes were actually asserted are then admitted **before** user-facing publication. The narrator must never assert a detail that is still merely speculative, unless expressly framed as actor testimony/perceptual uncertainty.

New target extractor receives only **admitted objective assertions**, not free text from any arbitrary speaker.

### Lane H — asynchronous historical completion

Input: newly admitted target IDs plus a consistent snapshot of existing world, Nadia's qualified computational/person history, relevant nearby events, real institutional/mobility/resource limits, other existing actors and social forms, and the **unresolved historical dimensions** of this target.

Output: proposed structured material details, historical occurrences, provenance chains, social-form links, qualified actor-history references and further unresolved dependencies. No direct user exposure; no new actor state write; no automatic emotional narrative.

The lane begins as soon as the admitted surface exists and runs while the user reads/thinks/acts. Its result must enter proper owner admission before any later foreground uses it as truth.

### Identity flow

`WORLD ROOT/BRANCH → OBSERVABLE EVENT ADMITTED → NEW TARGET ID → HISTORICAL JOB → CANDIDATE CLAIMS → OWNER COMMIT/REJECT → TYPED INDEX VERSION → DEPTH READER → NEXT FOREGROUND/ACTOR OPERATION`.

The trigger must be recorded in the event/outbox transaction. A crash between visible target appearance and queue wake-up must not silently lose work.

## 05. Truth depth and the two independent frontiers

Sorok 60/80's L0–L5 are not an access ladder. They describe possible strata of historical truth and actor relation:

| Stratum | What is materialized | Mechanism I responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| L0 — Structural space | layout, time, geographical and institutional/world conditions | read existing world and ensure feasibility; no automatic new PRE-V3 generation |
| L1 — Visible inventory | ordinary object presence and coarse identity | bind from valid foreground event; stable canonical target |
| L2 — Inspectable physical particularity | texture, make, wear, damage, mark, title/cover, accessible geometry, state | generate ahead of inspect where unresolved; admit physically compatible particulars |
| L3 — Objective provenance and history | maker, acquisition, exchange, repair, labor, prior location/use and actors/events | propose and admit bounded objective historical episodes and source links |
| L4 — Actor-private history/knowledge relation | what Nadia did with, experienced, remembers, believes or could know about object | **reference existing authority; no new psychological issuance**; qualify what is objective history versus actor knowledge |
| L5 — Present actor recruitment/expression | how current event interacts with Nadia's exact current person and counterpart | **OUT OF IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE**; existing actor mechanics may consume L2–L4 reads, but do not build new ones |

Truth materialization may get substantially ahead of the user's discovery; user epistemic visibility remains narrow. L3 may exist and be perfectly canonical while wholly inaccessible to user and actor. Actor testimony can be false or partial even when L3 is known. An actor may unknowingly preserve a meaningful material history. Neither user's curiosity nor the background model automatically gives Nadia insight into her own life.

**Two separate frontiers:**
- **Canonical materialization frontier**: what the system has actually admitted, at what resolution and historical time.
- **User epistemic/disclosure frontier**: what the user can actually perceive, read, recognize, infer and learn, given lawful physical access and event outcomes.

Do NOT encode these in one scalar `depth` or let `L3 generated` imply `L3 disclosed`. Current access plus domain-specific inspectability determine whether a physical mark/title is observable; historical cause usually needs documentary evidence or testimony. The user looking at stained sandals does not learn who stained them.

## 06. Generalized identity model — existence and incompleteness

The minimum common target needs:
- Canonical referent ID, domain/type, root and branch scope.
- An identity relation to the exact admitted foreground claim/occurrence.
- Current valid-time material state and basic physical properties/constraints where applicable.
- Its current known facts/negative facts, version, source/admission provenance.
- A **typed unresolved-fact frontier**, not one unbounded boolean `has_backstory`.
- Links to known world persons, collections, spaces, resources, historical events and existing actor states through competent owners.
- Distinction of individual versus class, collection versus member, actual artifact versus represented ideal/social form.
- Actor-specific visibility/recollection separate from objective existence.
- Generation and admission receipts; relevant dependency/readset and effective time.

The same object may be named differently, misidentified, or not recognized by different actors or users. Target identity is not identical to the label the foreground selects.

Resolve duplicate descriptions: `a pair of leather sandals` may be the already existing target; `one sandal` should be a part or member, not a new unrelated object; `photograph of sandals` is a distinct photograph artifact referring to another object; `a book title` as text/carrier is distinct from the specific physical copy.

The common kernel must be reusable for at least material items, artifacts/documents, collections, and socially situated targets where existing owner supports them. Domain adapters are thin: they supply physical/historical attributes and validation constraints, NOT per-domain story scripts or psychologically deterministic symbolism.

## 07. Relational-historical model — the exact architecture missing in the shorter commission

The background output must not be a prose blob plus an embedding. **Every admitted detail that might later matter must be retrievable as a typed reference to a world entity, event, relation, or competent existing actor state.**

### The object is not the only node

At least these logical node kinds must be representable by existing owners/adapters:
1. Stable target/material particular and its state changes;
2. An occurrence/episode with time, place, parties, operations, roles and sources;
3. Other persons (even if not interactive full agents) and their actual contributions;
4. Resource/practice/work/project and objective constraints;
5. Institutional/market/ritual/social-form participation under supported world claims;
6. Existing Nadia person-state, need/expectation/striving, mediator, practice, memory/access and relationship **references** (not newly stored psyche);
7. Actor's partial interpretation, belief or testimony **only from qualified owner records**;
8. Derived link, carrying provenance, time, scope and uncertainty.

A single historical episode may have many participants, materials, institutions and effects. Represent event-role links (a typed multi-party episode) rather than reducing everything to “Nadia and her beloved aunt.”

### Typed edge families

- **Material:** located-in, part-of, physically constrained by, manufactured/assembled from, maintained/repaired, moved, damaged, accessible to;
- **Property and circulation:** purchased, gifted, lent, inherited, controlled, exchanged, withheld, disposed of; distinguish lawful ownership from practical control;
- **Production and social form:** maker/labor, institution, market exchange, commodity standing, prestige classification, norm or ritual (all with qualified world provenance);
- **Activities:** used-in, practiced-with, kept-as-part-of, project-resource, failed/uncompleted activity, ongoing obligation;
- **Other actors:** participant, contributor, care provider, prior owner, institutional agent; other people's intentions not automatically Nadia's knowledge;
- **Existing actor-world history:** participated in exact event, owned/practiced/avoided/used, current owner-state refs, prior exposure, existing qualified appraisal/striving/practice relation;
- **Causal evidence:** materially caused, contributed, co-occurred, shares-common-parent, actor-attributed cause, plausible-but-unproven — not one `RELATED`;
- **Epistemic:** objectively true, actor-known, actor-believed, actor-forgotten/inaccessible where owner can establish, witness testimony, user-perceived, inferred by analyst — strict grade separation.

Names above are *semantic requirements* to map to actual current ABI, not a demand to create a giant new master enum. Object-specific descriptive statements can be typed fact rows with source refs. Canonical world history is authoritative; graph/index projections are derived and disposable. References to V3 must be versioned source-qualified reads, not duplicated psyche state.

### “Who Nadia is through this object; what of her is in it; who else is in it”

This question must be implemented as a *historical join and read* over:
- objective material target and its admitted history,
- actual activities/episodes involving Nadia,
- other-person/institutional contributions and shared social forms,
- pre-existing Nadia state references or historically qualified source relations,
- actor epistemic access, where available,
- and what remains unresolved or merely inferred.

The system should answer in *machine-readable references* before any lyrical narration. It may truthfully say there is no available evidence of personal psychic significance. The question must not force an emotional interpretation. A high-status consumer object may have intense actor investment through already developed social-identification patterns with no memorable giver. A book from a former friend may remain unread, undamaged and retained despite active dislike. A work tool may bear collective labor without Nadia knowing its producers. Each reflects a distinct graph.

### Indexing directionality and cross-object links

Support these queries with source/versioned IDs:
- `target → physical state / events / historical parents`;
- `target → Nadia's admitted activities and existing actor-state links`;
- `target → other actors/institutions/social forms`;
- `other person / project / activity → connected objects`;
- `Nadia's existing practice/mediator/striving owner ref → historically associated targets` (reverse link if issued relation exists);
- `one object → another through actual episode, photo, work, gift, storage or repair`;
- `event → all affected material targets and actors`;
- `time interval → target state and legal possibilities`;
- `actor access → which subset of this objective history is known, believed, unrecognized or inaccessible`.

Index lookup is not the psychological recruitment algorithm. It prepares accurate available history for the **existing** future actor/world owner. The same indexed owner graph will eventually support richer situated recruitment, but Mechanism I need not implement that later program.

### The crucial graph failure to prevent

`TWO PROSE PARAGRAPHS LOOK SEMANTICALLY SIMILAR` does not mean the objects share a real causal parent. Conversely two very different artifacts may share one acquisition event or creator. Vector search may locate candidates for typed reconciliation, NEVER establish causal truth.

## 08. Boundaries of model authority and evidence

Distinguish three domains:

**A. Verified / source-qualified external world facts:** public historical circumstances, prices, institutional dates, real production methods, geographical constraints, social distribution. A model's learned prior is not independent verification of a changing or niche real-world fact. Use existing PRE-V3 sources/grades; where unverified, label bounded hypotheses or leave unresolved.

**B. Synthetic-but-canonical private history:** Nadia's fictional prior possessions, gifts, books, relationships, experiences, material condition. These can be generated when compatible with the accepted person/world and closure law, but require exact world owner admission to be true in the simulation. They are neither “historically sourced” from a real biography nor arbitrary floating lore.

**C. General learned physical/social prior:** ordinary world knowledge the model may use to propose plausible physical details and small historical contingencies. This informs *candidate generation*; it never licenses impossible object states or unsourced empirical inferential rules about Nadia's psyche.

The proposal pipeline must annotate source grade/authority, effective time and uncertainty for each claim and preserve hard known negatives. **Every plausible new claim starts as CANDIDATE, not FACT.**

A private actor intention, memory, valuation or self-model requires existing actor authority. When it is absent, source-qualified objective details may still be admitted while the actor-private proposal is held. LLM literary confidence is irrelevant to owner permission.

## 09. Foreground capture, target resolution and durable enqueue

### Foreground → owner sequence

1. Get exact qualified scene/world/actor snapshot and event-time readset.
2. Foreground model (or existing deterministic world/actor engine) proposes observable facts. Its wording is not yet binding.
3. Extract candidate *objective* referents, distinguish already-known from new and actual assertion from quotation, hypothesis, metaphor, actor speech or user suggestion.
4. Existing authoritative world owner validates and commits the objective occurrence and stable IDs. The actual observable surface is derived from that commit; not the other way around.
5. In the same transaction if possible, write an **outbox obligation** to materialize admitted targets' unresolved slots. If current architecture cannot provide atomic same-store enqueue, journal an idempotent guaranteed post-commit scan keyed by occurrence ID that heals missed jobs on restart.
6. Publish the lawful observable surface. Start/dispatch background workers immediately without blocking on optional L3 completion.
7. Any later material claim must reference this exact target and source occurrence; no reparsing user-facing prose to mint a different object ID.

**Required falsifier:** hard crash after publication cannot leave a permanently orphaned visible object with no durable materialization obligation, unless it is a deliberate valid-empty/no-work target. Retry cannot duplicate the object.

### Target classification

Keep the original emitted surface's precise resolution. If it says “a small stack of books,” that establishes a stack/collection and some members, not automatic titles or complete library inventory. If it says “a scarred guitar,” condition is binding but brand/provenance may remain unresolved. If it says “a photograph of a woman,” the photograph is a physical object, whereas the photographed woman's exact identity is not necessarily determined.

**Do not turn ordinary observation into omniscient identification.** The user can see more physical specificity over time without being granted true hidden history.

## 10. Context construction for historical proposals

The historical LLM cannot compose a valid past from scene text alone. It needs a bounded **HistoricalConstraintPacket** assembled by code from existing canonical state.

Required inputs, when available:
- Target identity, type, immutable admitted visible facts, current location/state, actually unknown dimensions;
- Current world time, period and geography, resource/material feasibility and relevant source-qualified social formation;
- Nadia's identity/branch, established home/material ecology, relevant practices/projects/work/school/travel/relations, limited source-qualified summary of constituted person-state;
- Existing historical episodes around the target's acquisition/production window, including other actors/institutions and earlier linked objects;
- Known negatives and impossible events (incompatible dates, impossible budgets, already owned elsewhere, different confirmed custody);
- Causal closure of prior actor V3 and world events; allowed H0–H3 impact ceiling; any explicitly qualified unresolved causal allocation;
- Root/branch policy, qualified user-independent root model seed/job key, model/source/schema version, token/cost budget;
- Historical materialization question = what **dimension** to resolve next, never a desired answer or user psyche.

Provide a **reference-oriented compact packet**, not full V3 text and not the entire Rawlsing ledger. Retrieve connected sources once for co-generated groups sharing an era or actor. Yet do not truncate the particular fact/rule that makes an event impossible or a claimed link inadmissible; bounded context insufficiency must be marked, not guessed away.

The content of the user's question is not allowed as a historical constraint beyond target locus and demanded objective dimension (e.g., “inspect the markings” selects markings to resolve, not “probably a gift from Dad”). Nadia's current relationship with that user never feeds **shared pre-contact historical truth**.

### Actor-state limitation

A summary of Nadia's established value organization can help *avoid* incongruent historical proposals and identify existing qualified relation parents. It does not authorize generating a brand-new psychological cause, whether flattering, dark or dramatic. If the model proposes that a book is connected to Nadia's interest in a practice, the output should reference a currently accepted practice/history ID or be held as unsupported. She may enjoy a commodity, but the materializer cannot deduce that enjoyment simply because the model knows wealthy people sometimes desire status.

## 12. Admission algorithm — the causal historical bridge, not merely schema validation

The **world/history owner** (or exact authorized adapter) must determine whether proposed history can be admitted. The LLM never self-accepts.

For each candidate dependency group, implement this order:

1. **Authority check:** snapshot/root/branch/time/actor/source, existing owner availability, proposer role, current write lease.
2. **Target identity:** target still exists in the relevant continuity, matching admitted surface constraints and physical state; no duplicate or mistaken member/carrier.
3. **Syntax/semantic typing:** claims, units, times, persons, predicates, event roles and source refs are valid; no unsupported new owner class.
4. **Deduplication:** exact same historical fact/event/link is idempotent; incompatible second claim cannot overwrite it.
5. **Historical world compatibility:** place/time, resource/physical feasibility, institutional period, other persons and ownership/production chain; preserve actual material law.
6. **Known negative and dependency closure:** no dangling targets, source events, incompatible dispositions or incomplete compound episode.
7. **Backward causal anchor:** what existing person/world state allows the proposed fact? Is it an unresolved slot, inert contingency or specifically authorized relation? Unknown does not mean unlimited permission.
8. **Impact class H0–H3:** check whether the proposed history would affect prior actor capacities, beliefs, practices, memory, relationships or response; revise classification upward if proposal undershoots its true impact, never down-class to sneak it through.
9. **Forward reconciliation:** would the inserted past, had it been present earlier, have changed any causally closed actual event or owner-state? Reject/hold incompatible proposals even if no literal biography sentence contradicts them.
10. **Actor epistemics and durable state:** objective history may be accepted while proposed Nadia memory/valuation remains rejected; only qualified existing source-owner transition may admit it.
11. **Root/fork check:** would this proposed shared pre-contact history conflict with any affected branch or user-derived closed fact? If so, root admission held/rejected; do not silently rewrite others.
12. **Atomic commit and receipt:** accepted related claims as one dependency group; appropriate world/event owner commit, effective/admission timestamps, full readset hashes, negative/held entries, queued index update and child targets.
13. **Post-commit derived index:** index updates, version invalidation and ready-to-read acknowledgment; reader must refuse a stale view rather than serve incompatible fact.
14. **Repair only by legitimate proposal:** if candidate fails, regenerate bounded alternatives respecting the reason, or retain unresolved. Do not rewrite earlier canon just to achieve a pleasant narrative.

**The model may not smuggle a major formative life event through an ordinary object acquisition.** A book's condition and ownership may be freely resolved within existing constraints; inventing years of hidden study, a crucial deceased relative, a wholly new practice or a major childhood ideological conversion may retroactively alter existing V3. Those are higher-impact proposals and require previously grounded causal availability or explicit hold.

### H0–H3 as required admission analysis

| Class | Eligible examples | Constraints |
|---|---|---|
| H0 inert/descriptive | color/wear, standard manufacture, routine purchase, harmless physical details | no new actor psyche or memory |
| H1 episodic/associative | ordinary gift, travel souvenir, one prior use with actor, photo with earlier appearance | objective event can admit, but memory/access distinct and owner-qualified |
| H2 bounded contributory | target materially participated in an already established practice/project/relationship | only if link maps to qualified prior historical state/allowed causal slack; sparse forward impact |
| H3 formative | previously unknown major apprenticeship, trauma, family restructuring, conversion, repeated practice creating capability | presumptively reject as casual late addition; owner/source issue required |

H classes are not expressive priority, sentiment scale, stigma or biography drama. Historical compatibility must preserve human contradictions; causal incompatibility must not be romanticized as complexity.

### Sparse forward propagation, not total re-simulation

If an H0/H1 fact can alter the present material world, carry that physical/state consequence forward with exact event parents. If an H2 fact is eligible, use existing competent owner semantics to qualify any already present developmental residue. Do NOT recompute Nadia's entire life or invent replacement person-state outputs. If deeper psychological effect cannot be issued, admit objective fact only if it remains independently compatible and retain `EFFECT_HELD`, or reject the entire dependency group when inseparable.

## 13. Stored truth, derived indexes and fast current read

The source truth remains the existing world/event and actor owners. The proposed new mechanism needs storage only for:
- materialization job/outbox/proposal/receipt where not already covered;
- accepted newly generated world material facts and historical events through competent world owner;
- **derived** indexes linking those facts to existing actor/world history;
- bounded ready-to-read context cache keyed by authoritative state versions.

Do not create another canonical actor psychology store, new all-purpose event clock or ontology of universal symbolic worth.

### Minimum queryable link schema

A link to Nadia's existing CAN/ADP organization is a **reference or derivation with source parentage**, never a copied psychological fact. An object's social meaning can be represented through source-qualified association to commodity/ritual/status/practice institutions and existing actor histories without automatically becoming a person's feeling.

### Index families (logical, not necessarily seven physical stores)

1. Target identity/material state → admitted attributes and physical feasibility;
2. Target → objective acquisition/circulation/repair/production/ownership events;
3. Event/episode → all participant actors, artifacts and role-specific contributions;
4. Target ↔ other target/collection/place via actual shared historical parent;

6. World social form/institution/market/project/practice ↔ actual linked material targets;

Indexes must be bidirectional for meaningful source queries, reference their originating owner/source/version and be reconstructible by replay. **Required cold proof:** after killing the application process and restarting on current canonical stores, a lookup returns the same committed target and provenance without asking the LLM to regenerate the past.

## 14. Bounded rolling scheduler: how far ahead, and on whose demand?

The background engine is **not** an infinite world microevent simulator. Its task is a bounded causal/material overhang.

### Trigger classes

**T1 — New-world-particular:** a binding foreground/world occurrence just introduced an unresolved target. Enqueue immediate L2 and selected L3/qualified L4 work.

**T2 — New-detail dependency:** a materialized repair, photograph, inscription, gift event or other newly discovered partial introduces a specific linked target/history dependency. Enqueue only those unresolved necessary dimensions.

**T3 — Explicit user ATTEND/inspection:** user attempts to observe/use/ask about a target. Immediately return already admitted facts permitted to this user, promote causally necessary pending dimensions; do not derive desired historical content from user wording.

**T4 — Actor/world independent action:** Nadia or another actor actually needs a material property, resource, possession, document, capability-relevant object history or environmental fact to complete their own activity. Enqueue or block on that causal prerequisite without any user's gaze.

**T5 — Root historical integrity:** a new admitted fact has an unresolved parent needed for consistency with another target, chronology or previously generated action. Resolve bounded supporting dependency (not dramatic optional lore).

**T6 — Low-priority idle enrichment:** optional small overhang for nearby introduced targets within a token/compute budget, without assuming an inferred user attention distribution.

### Scheduling priorities

1. Blocking lawfulness of current world/actor action (cannot commit dependent event while fact unknown).
2. Already occurring physical inspection requiring an unresolved property.
3. Immediate newly introduced targets and shared parent context.
4. Other independently advancing actor/world activities.
5. Optional depth enrichment within budget.

Do not privilege an object because user-specific psychographics predict emotional salience. Actual spatial availability, causal urgency, physical inspectability and current scene/actor operation are admissible priority inputs.

### Rolling recursion termination

Budget by materialization horizon, job depth, target/event fanout, token and time limits, source/owner availability and historical impact ceiling. A child target is not necessarily another full biography; some dependencies are one inert fact. Detect cycles and repeated parent work, coalesce by canonical root+target+dimension+snapshot/policy, cache already completed subgraphs and record intentionally unresolved remainder.

Do not recursively generate the entire life history of every person who touched an object. Supporting actors remain bounded according to their material causal role and existing source/actor model; no continuous simulation of all producers or sellers.

## 15. Concurrency, deterministic root canon and query-order invariance

A shared initial Nadia is a common pre-user historical root. Each user relationship after contact is a separate copy-on-write continuity unless actual architecture says otherwise.

- Shared pre-contact generation is keyed to **root + canonical target + historical dimension + source/model policy**, not user ID or question phrasing. A given accepted choice is written once with receipt. Concurrent requests must singleflight/deduplicate, not race two independent invented histories.
- LLM API sampling is not mathematically deterministic just because temperature=0 or a key is supplied. **Canonical stability comes from the admission ledger, recorded choice and versioned root/branch consistency**, not a promise of deterministic language-model output across uncached reruns.
- A new backfilled shared-root fact after branches fork requires compatibility with all affected closed branch dependencies. Otherwise hold/reject; never force incompatible branches to accept a changed past.
- Post-contact physical modification, new gifts, user damage, repair and actual interaction belong to the branch where they happened. Shared root read may provide historical provenance but branch present state may differ.
- Inspection-order permutations of the same initial world/root must not produce differently constituted pre-contact truth simply because one user asked about the album before the instrument.
- If only the object locus is selected by ATTEND, all other selection can be source- and policy-governed; if forced to generate one candidate among several, record arbitration and version for cold reproducibility.

### Stale worker results

Job carries snapshot/readset. On completion, re-validate target, current world state, source and causal closure. Distinguish:
- `COMMITTED` — compatible and authorized;
- `REBASED_COMPATIBLE` — compatible after explicit revalidation, no silent rewrite;
- `PARTIAL_HELD` — independent fact groups accepted, dependencies held;
- `STALE_REJECTED` — snapshot incompatible;
- `SOURCE_HELD` — missing empirical/actor owner source;
- `HISTORY_HELD` — causal closure or root conflict;
- `MODEL_FAILURE` — malformed/no provider/timeout/budget error;
- `ALREADY_COMMITTED` — idempotent duplicate.

Names provisional; implement actual typed error states. Never permit stale model candidate to override a later present-state alteration or previously observed detail.

### Crash and exactly-once effect

At-least-once provider/task delivery plus **idempotent canonical world admission** is acceptable; never falsely claim exactly-once model invocation. The owner/outbox/result journals together must guarantee no duplicate *world effect*. Recover from:
- foreground admitted but enqueue not yet dispatched;
- provider request sent but receipt not persisted;
- candidate persisted but owner commit absent;
- owner committed but index not updated;
- index updated but completion acknowledgment absent;
- restart with pending job and newer owner snapshot;
- branch fork while shared root job in flight.

Reuse actual P3 transaction patterns where applicable but do not overwrite P3 psyche store as a convenience.

## 16. Hard cases showing that we understand the historical kernel

These are **semantically discriminating tests**, not production if/else content.

### Bookshelf and the unliked book

Nadia's current computational state may support enjoying some literary practices, keeping gifts, pursuing prestige, caring for materials, abandoning plans or none of these. Generate a **heterogeneous actual inventory** through lawful acquisitions, retention, other people's ownership and ordinary contingency. A read-worn book could show marginalia; an unread book may be pristine; a pristine favorite may be carefully handled; an annotated item may contain another person's marks. Avoid inferring reading from possession or ideology from book title. If there is a gift, the giver is a typed historical role and existing relationship ref; no new parent-memory is created.

### Sandals, repair and the old photograph

The current surface admits worn sandals. Background generates lawful physical wear and possible repaired seam. It may link a repair to an actual past event if feasible. Another photo artifact later turns out to depict the same sandals — only if target identity/time and event provenance actually permit it. Visible blue thread ≠ automatically knowing the repairer's identity; actor testimony ≠ world history. If photo's content includes additional unresolved objects, queue bounded next-layer historical jobs.

### Prestige commodity

A vehicle or watch can be historically produced, owned, marketed and socially coded as prestige. It may be a satisfying practical tool or a privately gratifying possession. Historical materialization can link to existing market/ownership/practice/actor-history refs. It cannot write `Nadia is a status seeker` as a new psyche fact. A status form can be objective even if Nadia rejects it; use-value, market price and actor investment remain distinct.

### Devotional artifact / conceptual ideal as represented materially

A religious image/text, ritual object or heirloom may belong to institutional practices, labor, exchange, devotion and actor belief. The model can form objective artifact provenance and existing institution/practice links. It cannot settle the ontological existence of God, automatically declare alienation or assume institutional authority explains every act. The actor may hold a theological claim as actor belief; its objective material source and authority are separately represented.

### Ordinary appliance or work tool

The item may have no sentimental connection whatsoever. Its presence may follow tenancy, price constraints, employment, daily use, maintenance, planned repair, labor exploitation or simple convenience. Objective materiality and practical affordances matter even when psychological link is absent. A broken machine cannot work because a model decided the scene needs symbolism.

### Other-actor independent need

Nadia needs a specific tool to complete an ongoing action. Its required physical state is not materialized, and the user is not looking. This causal dependency triggers a bounded job. The actual action resolves using committed physical facts or is held lawfully. Nothing in the job is tuned to entertaining the user.

## 17. Cross-object integrity and historical continuity

Multiple objects in one space are not independently generated story cards. They may share actual acquisition events, households, institutions, owners, moves, repairs, social production chains or family projects. The generation planner must identify **existing shared event parents** before drafting independent candidates, and permit co-materialization of a bounded group with common history.

However “these two artifacts both suggest nostalgia” is not sufficient to create an event. Shared causation requires parent evidence/admission. Two objects may be materially adjacent and historically unrelated. Narrative resonance may emerge from independent history without author-planting a secret pattern.

A snapshot graph should answer both:
- **Object-centered:** `What is this? What made it thus? Whose activity is physically in it? What can I lawfully know now?`
- **World/person-centered:** `What artifacts, practices or resources are linked to this already established historical period, other person, action, project or existing actor-state?`

The second query is critical to independent-world operation. If Nadia's current project invokes a tool, the engine should retrieve existing object resources by reverse context rather than wait for the user to point at them.

## 18. Epistemic membrane: five distinct claims about the same object

Do not collapse:

1. `World truth`: object was acquired in 2018 and repaired in 2023.
2. `Actor causal relation`: repaired/used artifact participated in an existing Nadia activity/history where authorized.
3. `Actor-accessible knowledge`: Nadia remembers repairing it (only if ADP-016/017 or existing owner supports).
4. `Actor testimony`: Nadia says she repaired it; that speech may be accurate, mistaken, selective or deceptive.
5. `User perceptual knowledge`: user saw a worn seam; may infer something, but history is not directly present in the stitch.

The historical background generator may possess analyst-level objective facts but **must not publish them as dialogue or narration**. The foreground DepthRead returns consumer-filtered data. Current actor response, affect and relationship-specific expression are existing-owner matters outside this commission. The future user-psyche modeling project remains explicitly separate: user attention can initiate a materialization demand but cannot determine its content.

## 22. Engineering implementation workflow — enough direction without prescribing a new monolith

Work in the following actual-dependency order, integrating from the first code change:

**A — Source/ABI cut.** Freeze live protected SHA, current exact owner/callsite matrix and source law. Select the smallest existing world/event target representation and one concrete later consumer. No repository-wide rewriting.

**B — One canonical fact from a new surface.** Prove valid surface→target ID→world owner commit→later fast read before adding model costs. Determine which existing code provides objective occurrence.

**C — Durable outbox and candidate API.** Add queue/recovery and strict schema; inject deterministic candidates and prove owner admission, not new psyche.

**D — Historical bridge.** H0–H3, causal closed event/negative test, version/race/branch semantics; do not trust candidates merely because typed.

**E — Real provider.** Connect actual model adapter with bounded source/actor context; tests include generated new target not present in fixture enumeration.
