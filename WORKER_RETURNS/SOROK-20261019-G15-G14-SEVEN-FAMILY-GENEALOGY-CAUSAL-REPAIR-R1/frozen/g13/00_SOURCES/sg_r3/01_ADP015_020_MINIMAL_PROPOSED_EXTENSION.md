## 1 — Non-negotiable owner boundary

Historical-material social world constitutes positions, available encounters and the actor's accumulated life; it is not a late personality modifier. PRE-V3/Pyat supplies source-grounded, **generic** positioned-subject possibilities/structural constraints and their exact lineage, not parents, relationships, persons, biologically realized conception or household state. V3 owns actor trajectory and event causality, partner scenario generation/use, genealogy, shared relations and household when actually generated. Actor knowledge/perception, objective shared relation, transmitted event and user-visible narration stay distinct. Source-conditioned possibility and model-conditioned scenario likelihood are not observed individual truth.

ADP-015's accepted historical construction is two independently generated parent histories → encounter/relationship/household if governed → `CHILD_GENERATED` → child ref → ADP-007 initialization/development (V3_COMPLETION §12; TD-R05 §20). Those texts do **not** themselves permit constructing a parent retrospectively from a selected endpoint. Proposed alternative schedules **software construction** as mother → conception envelope → finite scenario/qualified selection → constrained whole second-parent construction, while the represented **causal chronology** still requires both histories before encounter/conception and child generation. The real parent histories must be independently replayable; independence does *not* mean unconditional independent random draws or absence of real relations.

## 3 — Two DAGs and sampling classification

The *construction dependency* DAG permits a future provisional endpoint to guide which earlier candidate histories are searched, with no causal/event truth implied. The *represented causal* DAG requires root→parent development→encounter→conception if admitted→child generation (separate event)→birth if governed→child own development. See 02 for machine-readable edge types. Do not put a construction edge into a causal parent list or into a participant's recollection.

This is **constrained constructive search**, not a posterior sample over actual parents. A genuine posterior requires an expressly scoped prior over entire histories, likelihood for endpoint observation under each history, normalized proposal-to-target correction, chance of inclusion/rejection, validated calibration and a declared inference target; none is supplied by ADP-015, ADP-020 or the user correction. Retrospective construction admits *one possible fictional life course*, not empirically estimated truth of a real person's past.
