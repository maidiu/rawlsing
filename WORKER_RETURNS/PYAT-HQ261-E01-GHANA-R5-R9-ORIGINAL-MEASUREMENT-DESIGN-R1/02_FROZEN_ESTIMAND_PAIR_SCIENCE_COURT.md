## Single proposed two-wave estimand

For each independent Ghana Afrobarometer wave t = 2012 R5 Q43 or 2022 R9 Q31, target Ghanaian citizens age 18+ under that year's household-area probability sampling/coverage. Candidate wave-specific outcome: the population design-weighted **share reporting fairly satisfied (code 3) or very satisfied (code 4) with the way democracy works in Ghana, conditional on having a substantive native Q43/Q31 response in {0,1,2,3,4}**. The denominator is neither automatically all citizens nor the wave headline sample of 2,400: it is actual eligible/asked respondents represented by the design with a valid substantive response, as mapped under future authorized retained-source inspection.

Proposed ratio (symbolic, not executed): p_t = [sum_i w_it * 1(country Ghana, eligible/asked citizen18+, Q_t in {3,4})] / [sum_i w_it * 1(country Ghana, eligible/asked citizen18+, Q_t in {0,1,2,3,4})]. Use same validated per-wave survey weight in both sums; require appropriate domain variance computation, not accidental deletion of invalid respondent design observations before variance design registration. The answer is a **conditional attitudinal response share**, not institutional-democracy quality or causal governance assessment.

**0 is valid, semantically distinct and separately reported:** “Ghana is not a democracy” is NOT “not at all satisfied” and is not a fifth ordered satisfaction rating. In this predeclared candidate estimand it stays in the valid 0..4 denominator, but the outcome must publish the full 0/1/2/3/4 response cells, including 0 separately. A strictly four-category 1..4 conditional satisfaction estimate is a **distinct sensitivity estimand**, not silently substituted. R5 refused=998; R9 refused=8; DK=9 and missing=-1 excluded from primary denominator but require per-wave weighted/unweighted accounting and differential nonresponse sensitivity.

A future authorized, uncertainty-bearing **delta = p_R9(2022) - p_R5(2012)** would contrast two independent repeated cross-sections. No shared persons, no interpolation between survey dates, no inferred causal political change. Compare **valid-response population shares**, not unqualified unconditional citizen sentiment.

Wording correspondence does not establish translation invariance or removal of nonresponse bias.
