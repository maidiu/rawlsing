## PRIMARY: actual Ghana 2012 national administered questionnaire, not merged generic

[Official Ghana R5 English questionnaire, finalized 19 April 2012](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/gha_r5_questionnaire.pdf), original survey instrument **PDF page index 8 (printed p.9), Q43**. Exact stem: “Overall, how satisfied are you with the way democracy works in Ghana? Are you:”. The interviewer must **read** the single-select option list in the language of interview, pronounce “democracy” in English and translate it into the local language only if respondent does not understand the English term. Printed response codes: **4 Very satisfied; 3 Fairly satisfied; 2 Not very satisfied; 1 Not at all satisfied** [read options]; **0 Ghana is not a democracy [Do not read]; 9 Do not know [Do not read]**. This is the original, country-indigenized **2012 Ghana** field instrument; previous HQ260 E01 had only a merged R5 codebook. It is NOT evidence that all 2012 respondents heard a specific translated equivalent or were shown a separate Q43 show-card.

R5 questionnaire **PDF p.8 Q42 immediately before Q43** asks degree of democracy; neither Q43 nor the published immediate question sequence states a Q42-dependent skip. Survey instruction before the block requires democracy questions of all respondents, even when the concept is unfamiliar (check instrument PDF p.7). Q43 cannot silently be conditioned on prior affirmation that Ghana is democratic. Questionnaire p.2 (PDF index 2) instructs interviewer to write 'refused' for firm refusals to answer individual questions; **998** is a *national codebook data code*, not a Q43 response category read aloud.

[Original Ghana R5 national codebook, Danielle Carter, June 2012](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/gha_r5_codebook.pdf), **PDF index 21 (check printed pagination) for Q43**: codes **0,1,2,3,4,9,998,-1**, with 998=refused to answer and -1=missing data; its source annotation Eurobarometer. Its first technical overview is **PDF p.1** (country-specific, not a whole-continent release). Country codebook and field questionnaire have distinct functions.

## PRIMARY: actual Ghana 2022 national administered questionnaire

[Official Ghana R9 indigenized English questionnaire, finalized 30 March 2022](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/GHA_R9.Indigenized-Questionnaire-_30mar22-FINAL.docx.pdf), **PDF page index 12 (printed p.13), Q31**. Exact substantive stem again: “Overall, how satisfied are you with the way democracy works in Ghana? Are you:”. The interviewer reads 4 Very satisfied, 3 Fairly satisfied, 2 Not very satisfied, 1 Not at all satisfied (the PDF prints stray '?' after the latter two labels); does NOT read **0 Ghana is not a democracy** or **9 Don't know**. As in 2012, the interview-language sentence explicitly directs English “democracy” unless the respondent requires translation. R9 Q30 is immediately prior; no Q30-democracy-status gate is indicated for Q31. The 2022 paper field form does not list the refused data code as a prompted option.

[Original Ghana R9 national codebook (2024)](https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/GHA_R9.Codebook_21May24.final_.pdf), **PDF index 16 (printed p.17), Q31**: codes **0,1,2,3,4,8,9,-1**, with **8=refused**, 9=don't know, -1=missing; source Eurobarometer, original language instruction repeated. The 2012-to-2022 **998→8** refusal difference is a real release-level missing-code reconciliation, not attitude movement. The 2022 preceding Q30 additionally uses a different distinct refusal code **98**; never import Q30's categories into Q31.

## Exact positive correspondences and ceilings
