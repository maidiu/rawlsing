**What the index can say.** The inherited program requires early scoped care, challenge/repair, durable relational change, a continuing C12 person and a separate real-world encounter. C4/C5/C6/C12 and A0–A3 have exact historical definitions; the sourcefree C12/I1/CP-A5/C9C12 candidates provide bounded same-root encounter and read evidence.

**Where it is HELD.** N02, N04, N05 and N12 remain reviewed requirements without an issued causal rule. O02, O04 and O16 remain original owner decisions. CP-A5 is a test-only read-only encounter with a fixture coactor C, not an authorized human A5. C12/A0–A3 sourcefree candidates do not prove a native person. E10/E12/E13 have no qualified native consumers. The path must stop at `A5_HUMAN_ALPHA = NOT_PROVEN`.

**Correct answer behavior.** Describe the obligation path and the evidence that exists; do not say a native person was formed, that A5 was tested with a human, or that care automatically produces a skill, ideology or wound.

**Correct answer behavior.** A later narrator’s story cannot substitute for the earlier actor’s history. No-change is not the same as `HOLD_MISSING_RULE`; a changed ideology is not evidence of a native causal law, and a survey/structural prior cannot create a personal biography.

## Traversal invariant

Every traversal starts at an L0 product obligation, passes through typed science/source/consumer layers, and ends at an L5 evidence or held node. The validator checks that all referenced nodes exist and that each traversal retains its answer limits; it does not execute the underlying candidate code.
