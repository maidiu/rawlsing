## 3. Test-order and hard stops

1. **Cheapest first:** preflight source/stream/choice authority, mother checkpoint/older-child freeze and hidden-child noninterference **before** paying for model calls. On insufficient authority: zero draws, zero paid calls.
2. Reject scenario with existing named counterpart conflict, unavailable world route, altered past, unsupported dyad estimate or forbidden future-child field immediately **with typed reason**; mark UNKNOWN support differently from hard infeasibility.
3. Freeze semantic-class candidate set **before choice**; demonstrate dedup under rephrasing/reorder and no implicit mass multiplication. If selection weights are only ordinal, zero draw/HOLD. If 0 eligible classes: actual `VALID_EMPTY` only from competent complete eligibility rule, otherwise `UNKNOWN/UNBOUND`. One eligible class: deterministic with no draw.
4. If authorized selected class requires invalid past: persist *all attempts* and `SG_ENDPOINT_HISTORY_HOLD`; do not silently choose an easier survivor. A failed candidate remains in class-level attrition accounting.
5. **Zero-tolerance NO-GO** on mutated mother/older-child identity, imported future-child target, source-to-personality shortcut, non-owner background event, reverse-time causal edge, fabricated objective relationship/reciprocity, nonstable seed/draw/replay, hidden retry, or false conception/birth/child event.
6. Positive cell requires **4/4** independent history witnesses with separate nonmother obligations and ties, owner-derived forward replay, exact anchored world/encounter status and matching necessary endpoint. Under synthetic rules, label `LAB_PASS` only; never `ADP_OWNER_PASS`.
7. **Feasibility kill trigger:** at least **2 of 4** required witnesses irrecoverably fail their ≤3 attempts, **or** the remaining work cannot finish under *any one* call/token/cash/time ceiling. Stop and report `BOUNDED_LAB_INFEASIBLE_AT_DECLARED_GRAIN`, not an infinite rerun. A single failed witness is already **not a positive pass** and requires a new independent preregistered review, not extra hidden attempts.
8. Proposal set sensitivity: frozen-record replay proves determinism *conditional on acquired proposals*, not acquisition distribution. In next permitted lab compare ≥2 independently acquired proposal sets and report class coverage variance, dedup and selection-law sensitivity; retain missing OTHER.
9. **Whole-person white whale:** hold endpoint constraints fixed; require two distinct prior histories with consequential non-mother responses to independent held-out situations; exact non-mother causal ancestry, phase/material/routing validity and actor memory/access differences must be inspected by an **independent** case custodian. One good biography narrative is insufficient.
10. Any resulting conception must be admitted separately under its new event owner, followed only as lawfully applicable by ADP015 child-generation and separately issued birth. A pending situation, an actual encounter, or chosen candidate is not an occurrence.

## 4. Attrition and epistemic interpretation

Retain per proposed semantic class: count of phrasings, deduplicated class ID, proposal/eligibility rejection reasons, intended policy weight `q_i` **only if issued**, number history attempts, successes, failures, timeouts and untried classes. Do not report an empirical `a_i` or likelihood from a mere small trial unless the sample/design/estimand and observer authority justify it.

For any hypothetical policy `q_A=q_B=1/2` and conditional success chances `a_A=1`, `a_B=1/4`, silent redraw-until-success would yield `q'_A=4/5, q'_B=1/5`, proving why success filtering is a different sampler. This is arithmetic **not measured acceptance**. Real selection or posterior calibration is independently HELD.

If an arbitrary model generates a finite attractive set, it may omit an entire scenario class. Even uniform selection **among enumerated classes** is a *synthetic design conditional on proposal coverage*, not uniformity among all socially possible conception routes. `OTHER_UNRESOLVED` is a coverage warning/held area, not an invented probability bucket.
