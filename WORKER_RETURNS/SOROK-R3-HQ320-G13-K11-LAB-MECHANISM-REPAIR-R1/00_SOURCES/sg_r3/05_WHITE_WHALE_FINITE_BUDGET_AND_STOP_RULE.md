## Necessary vs unproved capability

The product **requires** a second parent who is a real independent generated historical person, selected in light of a fully developed mother without changing her history, and one shared causal life with nonidentical perspectives and a child whose later outcomes are earned. A narrative profile cannot substitute. This requires at minimum: (1) exact generic structural/empirical encounter-possibility support, (2) lawful finite candidate/weight selection, (3) tractable *whole-life endpoint-constrained* construction with ordinary forward owner replay, (4) lawful conception/child-generation/birth separation and (5) causal child development/relationship continuation without teleology.

The first two have partially specified substrates; the third is **not demonstrated** by either ADP015 or Astra's prose. The fourth has explicit missing occurrence issuers; the fifth depends on separately proven V3 transition science. `WHITE_WHALE_RISK = MATERIAL`; `INTEGRATED_FEASIBILITY = UNRESOLVED`. If meaningful independent histories are too costly or fail forward validation, this may require redesign rather than extra prompt detail.

## Frozen finite cell and resource ceilings

- **Exactly two mother variants**: M0 first-child pre-conception checkpoint; M1 already has at least one generated older child with stable immutable identity/anchors. Do not replace an existing partner or earlier child. Both are independently provided, real V3-generated checkpoints **after** grant.
- **Exactly four required positive history witnesses:** two independently differentiated second-parent histories per mother (M0-Ha/Hb and M1-Ha/Hb), using eligible distinct endpoint candidate/route or independently varied lawful past under fixed necessary endpoint constraints. They may not be four rephrasings or duplicate roots. If actual eligible route variability cannot be lawfully generated, report `NONDISCRIMINATING_HOLD`, not force diversity.
- **Maximum candidate proposals:** four distinct de-duplicated scenario classes per mother, eight total, including explicit unsupported/other bookkeeping. Minimum two eligible classes per mother required to exercise a nondegenerate selection; this minimum is a **test precondition**, not license to invent routes. If impossible, return typed no-positive-witness.
- **Maximum history construction attempts:** three per required witness, twelve total. Each rejected attempt is retained. No extra invisible retries or father-specific manual polishing.
- **Model budget:** at most 24 LLM calls total, 120,000 model input tokens and 40,000 model output tokens across candidate proposal, history search and permitted evaluation **combined**. Precommit call roles and share the receipt ledger. No hidden tool-side calls. A model that cannot expose usage/cost/identity cannot be used in this trial.
- **Cash ceiling:** $20 **maximum authorized prospective lab expenditure only if separately approved**; stop *before* invoking a model call whose published or contractually available worst-case rate could push the ceiling above $20. No claim that any provider can meet this ceiling is made. If provider's rate/cost basis is unavailable, **do not run**.
- **Time/compute:** at most two hours operator elapsed time and 60 minutes cumulative model/network wait at lab scope, when authorized. No unbounded overnight/backtracking loops.
- **Source budget:** only preapproved retained/source-accessible evidence and exact bounded queries; zero raw original-source execution absent source grant. No broad all-35 run by implication.
- **Held-out controls:** preregistered future-child target swap, unaltered inputs/seed replay, candidate input ordering mutation, existing-father identity conflict, negative route, disabled reverse-query authorization and 2-way shared perception nonreciprocity. Controls receive reserved within-budget calls; do not drop controls to meet positive-witness count.
- **No preliminary pilot counts as K11:** an explicit dry-run/test-only schema check can be performed without sources/actors only after authorization, labeled `CONTRACT_ONLY`.

## Go/no-go gates: scientific and operational

1. **Integrity/jurisdiction: zero tolerance.** Any mutation of a mother anchor, named existing partner/child replacement, child future leakage, invented source denominator, borrowed model authority, source→actor leap, random source fallback, causal-time inversion, false occurrence, failed deterministic replay, lost rejection record or unintended reciprocity immediately stops and makes that attempted implementation **NO-GO**, not a cosmetically corrected pass.
2. **Mechanical success: 4/4 required positive history witnesses.** Each must have independent non-mother history and projects, accepted root/effects, coherent correct world times, a forward replay to the frozen endpoint, exact two parent anchors and shared-event reconciliation. One missing witness makes `K11_MECHANICAL_PASS = FALSE` for this fixed cell; provider/context failure is separately tagged `BLOCKED/HOLD`, not invented mechanism failure.
3. **Feasibility stop threshold:** 2 or more of four witness branches irrecoverably fail within their three-attempt allotment, or all attempts cannot finish before any of the hard 24-call/160k-token/$20/2h ceilings → `BOUNDED_LAB_INFEASIBLE_AT_DECLARED_GRAIN` and **stop/redesign**, not request unbounded calls. One branch failure is still nonpass and requires a new preregistered independent court; don't silently raise the budget.
4. **Outcome embargo:** swapping withheld hypothetical future-child traits while keeping all accessible inputs fixed must produce identical candidate set, selected decision/receipt, second-parent histories and admissible parent effects. Any difference in hidden metadata/test harness output that reaches model/validator breaks isolation → immediate NO-GO.
5. **Selection bias:** report proposed q, eligibility, rejected endpoints, history completion counts/rates per class, and realized q times acceptance rates **only if the calibrated interpretation is actually justified**. If rates/normalizer are unknown, output *construction-run distribution only*; never report a calibrated paternal posterior or represent retained winners as unbiased original q.
6. **Scientific significance:** 4 mechanical passes are not validity. Blinded independent evaluators and held-out counterfactuals must compare narrative-only, demographic shell and owner-transition baseline under *equal* allowed calls and evidence, with a predeclared discriminating causal target. No support for general independent psychological coherence, simulation validity, human-level agency or full product feasibility can be inferred from four examples.

## Constrained construction versus posterior sampling

Construction solves `find history h with lawful forward transition F(h, world) satisfying necessary endpoint constraints E`. It need not assign probabilities to histories. Many lawful h may satisfy E; record multiplicity and never treat one successful story as *the* causal explanation.

A posterior `P(h | E, source, model)` is a different scientific product: it requires a justified prior over complete history roots and transitions, an endpoint likelihood, normalizable proposal measure, acceptance/rejection accounting (including never-proposed classes), exact conditioning/selection scope, and independent calibration/validation. Neither LLM-generated scenario prose nor ADP015's deterministic explicit-trigger rule supplies those quantities. **K11 does not claim posterior draws.**

## Cheapest decisive negative probes

First fail-closed source/weight and owner-contract check, then (with grant) one M1 mother with an older child and a preexisting known partner: if selection silently invents a different father or reworks her child, the schedule fails immediately. Next inject a hidden desired-child label with all permitted inputs held constant; any parent change is a teleology failure. Next hold endpoint facts fixed and require two different lawful non-mother histories that replay to the exact endpoint: a single canned biographical path, or a choice only justified by prose, fails positive adequacy. Finally account for every expired call, timeout and rejected attempt; a hand-selected surviving case is not a positive K11 result.
