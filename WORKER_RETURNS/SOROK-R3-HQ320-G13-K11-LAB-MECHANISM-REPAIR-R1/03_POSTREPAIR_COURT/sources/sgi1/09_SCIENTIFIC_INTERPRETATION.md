# Scientific interpretation — S-GI1 source-free genealogy lab

**Status:** `PROVISIONAL_LAB_RULE__NOT_ADOPTED`. Interpretation of what the executed K11 trial does and does not show.

## 1. What was actually shown

At the declared lab grain (synthetic mother fixtures M0/MP/M2 with full causal event histories; synthetic world premise; finite LLM-proposed scenario sets frozen at acquisition; rule-driven forward second-parent construction with precommitted attempt budgets), S-G's proposed `V3_CONCEPTION_CONDITIONED_RETROSPECTIVE_CONSTRUCTION_RULE@0.1-PROPOSAL` is **mechanically executable end-to-end** and its hardest invariants hold:

1. **The construction contract is implementable without teleology.** Endpoint state is derived by forward replay of the father's causal event chain and only then compared to scenario endpoint constraints. When the endpoint is made unconstructible (K11-RETRY-BIAS), every attempt fails typed `ENDPOINT_INFEASIBLE`, all four attempt receipts persist, a selection-bias report is emitted, and no causal commit occurs. The machinery does not silently resample until a pleasing father appears.
2. **The future-child embargo is structurally enforceable.** Because selection scope hashes, proposal prompts, and construction inputs are all computed from pre-boundary material only, changing hidden future-child metadata produces byte-identical candidate sets, selection decisions, and father histories (K11-A/A2). This is not a policy statement; it is a byte-equality measurement.
3. **Second-parent construction at this grain is a genuine whole-person fixture, not a profile shell.** Fathers carry material position, organismic history, two non-Mom consequential ties (a collective project with durable ledger effects and a friendship with mutual-aid debt), attitudes/practices each with causal event ancestry, memory traces with experienced bases, and a lawful endpoint state. Two frozen held-out non-Mom probes (work-collective pump failure; friend tool damage) are evaluated purely from actor state with zero Mom/child content and produce typed machine-state effects (K11-E, K11-B).
4. **Plural lawful pasts exist and are reachable.** Identical endpoint constraints admit ≥2 distinct non-endpoint causal histories under separately governed seeds (K11-B). This empirically confirms S-G's nonidentifiability point (R3): no unique father childhood is inferable; the contract correctly constructs *a* lawful history, not *the* history.
5. **The two-DAG discipline is satisfiable.** The `RETROSPECTIVE_CONSTRUCTION_DEPENDS_ON` E→H edge can live in a construction DAG outside causal parenthood while the represented causal DAG stays chronological and acyclic with zero endpoint→childhood inversions (K11-GRAPH).
6. **Selection epistemics survive hostile controls.** Phrasing multiplicity cannot multiply probability mass (K11-DEDUPE: 11 inputs → same 3 classes, same class-set hash, same selection receipt); ordinal LLM rankings without a policy owner produce `SELECTION_AUTHORITY_UNBOUND` and zero draws (K11-WEIGHT-ORDINAL); joint/dyad empirical requests against single-person marginals return `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, never zero plausibility and never population coupling (K11-EMPIRICAL-CEILING).
7. **Exact replay is real.** Fresh acquisition vs frozen-record replay produce identical per-case fingerprints across all 24 cases, and the frozen-record replay of the entire suite runs in ~0.14 s.

## 2. What was NOT shown — limits of the claim

- **No population adequacy.** Synthetic world; single-person marginals; no calibrated fertility/encounter frequencies. `SIM_UNIFORM_CLASSES` / `SIM_INTEGER_WEIGHT_A/B` are exploration designs, explicitly not "real fathers are equally likely."
- **No biological semantics.** `LAB_CONCEPTION_BOUNDARY` is a placeholder. Conception/gestation/birth/`CHILD_GENERATED` timing authorities remain HOLD. The child artifact is a developmental-environment fixture, not an admitted child actor.
- **No psychological validity.** Attitude/practice dynamics are rule-shaped causal tags, not a validated personality model. The held-out probes demonstrate independent causal mechanism, not predictive accuracy about humans.
- **No source authority.** Zero survey reads; zero real-person inference; generic possibility answers live entirely inside the synthetic world's declared universe with a use ceiling label.
- **LLM proposals are scenarios only.** Model output never set probabilities, never wrote causal state, never minted a father, never wrote memories as fact. All LLM-derived content enters only as finite route/endpoint proposals under a dedup court.
- **Coverage is not claimed complete.** `OTHER_UNRESOLVED` coverage receipts accompany every proposal set; unenumerated scenario classes may exist.

## 3. Feasibility judgment (the commissioned question)

Whole-person back-generation is **NOT computationally ridiculous at lab grain**: ~1 attempt typical, 4 max (precommitted), ~19–22 typed events per father, milliseconds per attempt, 12K tokens total for all proposal acquisition. S-G's R1 explosion risk is an architecture choice, not a law: it appears only if construction is delegated to per-event/per-ancestor LLM generation or unbounded recursion. The stopping law's bounded observational closure (additional ancestors must not change tested endpoint/independence observables) is the control that keeps the search finite. The genuine cost centers at production scale would be (a) LLM calls if used beyond proposal, (b) generic source queries with real use permissions, (c) reviewer cost for held-out judgments — not the deterministic machinery.

## 4. Residual risks (scientific, not bureaucratic)

1. **Reviewer-independent truth of held-out probes.** The probe scorer is part of the lab; an independent expected-case custodian should re-score held-out actions blind before adoption (S-G `04` §1 requirement not yet independently staffed).
2. **Single-world premise.** Route/institution variety is synthetic; a hostile second world premise could expose hidden coupling between the world fixture and construction rules.
3. **Selection-bias bounds remain partially quantified.** K11-RETRY-BIAS reports acceptance rate for the failed class and declares the conditional sampler; acceptance rates of unselected classes are unmeasured in-run. S-G's `SELECTION_BIAS_UNQUANTIFIED_HOLD` would trigger if a production trial cannot prequalify classes.
4. **Model nondeterminism is quarantined, not removed.** Replay is exact only against frozen proposal records; a fresh acquisition samples a different finite proposal set. Proposal-set sensitivity across independent acquisitions was not measured here (one acquisition pass of 7 calls).

## 5. Bottom line

The S-G contract is not merely documentary: under the commissioned lab grant its mechanisms execute, refuse correctly, refuse *for the right typed reasons*, and reproduce exactly. The remaining distance to any adoption is authority distance (biological timing owner, selection-policy owner, source/use permissions, actor-model grant scope, independent review), not missing machinery.
