## Ordered go/no-go thresholds

**Gate 0 — authority and integrity:** before trials, exact lab/model/source permissions, rule and expected-output versions, finite test manifest and read cut. Every case has executed status and artifacts; zero absent required receipts. No grant means NOT_EXECUTED/HOLD, not failed science.

**Gate 1 — port and jurisdiction (K9/K10):** all enumerated critical conservation, clock, permission, lineage and no-upstream-actor invariants pass in every deterministic fixture/mutation. One breach is NO-GO for dependent integration. These are strict safety/identity invariants, not statistical thresholds.

**Gate 2 — minimal causal cell (K1–K6):** each positive control demonstrates its predeclared machine-level contrast, each null control is invariant within its specified exact/tolerant semantics, all access constraints hold. A changed adjective without a changed permitted computation does not pass. Failure revises/removes the primitive or rule rather than adding narrative rescue.

**Gate 3 — family/history composition (K7/K8/K11):** all required ancestry/chronology/replay checks exact; every shared event has consistent multi-actor anchors; construction succeeds within a preregistered finite search/call budget for the declared fixture set. No hidden failures may be dropped. At least two distinct lawful family outputs across the deliberately nondegenerate seed/route set. Single successful handpicked family is insufficient.

**Gate 4 — scientific adequacy:** bounded mechanical PASS does not prove psychology. Before F3 causal-quality claims, independently select held-out scenarios and a primary outcome/minimum meaningful effect or equivalence margin. Report all runs, disagreement, confidence/uncertainty and sensitivity to weights/prompts. If sample size cannot distinguish the alternatives, retain FU; do not invent a universal numeric threshold here. Rich alternative must beat the baseline on the predeclared substantive endpoint without violating integrity or budget, otherwise omit its extra state.

**Gate 5 — product:** authorized real generic PRE-V3 inputs, N11/N12 and physical weld conditions precede operative C11/C12. Then test several pipeline-produced people, longitudinal reentry and human A5 experience. Lab tests alone cannot grant this step.

Count only genuinely independent tests. Reuse a fixture to reduce cost, not to multiply proof totals. New K11 does not replace K7: K11 tests construction order/independence; K7 tests subsequent relational causation.
