## Weight/admissibility taxonomy

- **SOURCE_SUPPORTED** — measured conditional/joint population support in native unit, with exact denominator/design/risk set/precision and permitted use. It may constrain *possibility*; it is **not** by itself a partner/encounter likelihood or causal propensity.
- **MODEL_SPECIFIED** — an independently justified generative/decision model with version, target estimand, calibration, uncertainty and competence; includes clearly labeled *synthetic selection policy* that explicitly makes no empirical-frequency claim.
- **UNKNOWN_OR_BLOCKED** — source missing, sparse cell, unsuitable unit/topology, wrong release, reverse denominator unavailable, contested model or absent license. Never impute zero, divide marginals as if pair data, infer father attitude from mother, or substitute LLM likelihood.

Even `SOURCE_SUPPORTED` and `MODEL_SPECIFIED` evidence are not ADP020 weights until an exact ADP015 semantic owner maps them to integer units for the declared scenario frame. An ordinal ranking is not a probability. A model may be calibrated to respondents while entirely missing the dyadic *encounter* selection mechanism.
