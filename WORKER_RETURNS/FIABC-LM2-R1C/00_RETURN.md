## Ontology holding

The authority ontology separates the claims:

`SOURCE_SEMANTICS_TOPOLOGY`

covers native question/component meaning, response values/special states, and response topology/order/rank.

`NATIVE_CANONICAL_MAPPING_HARMONIZATION`

covers native-to-canonical identifier/value transformation.

The latter name is broader-sounding than its actual ceiling. The accepted ceiling does **not** itself assert cross-source equivalence. Cross-source/source-relation claims have separate authority kinds including `SOURCE_EQUIVALENCE`, `SOURCE_COMPOSITION_POOLING`, and related relation authorities.

Therefore:

- source-native meaning/topology does not itself authorize a canonical value field;
- native→canonical transformation does not itself authorize cross-source equivalence;
- cross-source equivalence may not be smuggled into this repair.
