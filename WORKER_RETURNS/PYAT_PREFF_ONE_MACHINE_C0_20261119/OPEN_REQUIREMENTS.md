## Admission and priority discipline

A **defect** means source-inspected violation or credible malfunction of an actual implemented contract. An **unsatisfied requirement** means a missing end-to-end capability, scientific method, owner authorization, performance qualification, or first-consumer relationship: it need not indicate malfunction of existing code. **Tests/harness faults** are neither production defects nor requirements gaps; those live in Queue T of `OPEN_DEFECTS.md`. Do not misclassify an unexecuted candidate test as a product defect.

**Work order:** production defects remain REMONT's primary Queue P, in original PF order, following source→downstream consequences; Queue T only after the production queue has been worked as far as lawful implementation permits. If a new capability gap is noticed while implementing a PF, flag it in the working notes without switching tasks. After the current PF repair, append an exact PYAT-REQ row, then resume Queue P. **Before ending every turn**, publish any accumulated gaps here and announce their IDs, statuses and receiving barriers. Only a directly destructive fault, corrupt source, or untestable current integration justifies interruption.

Each row must name first owner/source, real consumer and negative consequence, minimum closure evidence, authority or science boundary, and factual status. An implementation candidate is not closure without its agreed tests, independent grade where required, original source/owner decision and protected receiving. Preserve all historical rows and track subsequent evidence additively. Link matching PF defects; do not duplicate the same incident into a second defect campaign.
