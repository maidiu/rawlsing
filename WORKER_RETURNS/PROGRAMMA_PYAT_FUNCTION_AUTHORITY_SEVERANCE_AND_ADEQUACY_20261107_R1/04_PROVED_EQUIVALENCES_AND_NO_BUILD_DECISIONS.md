# 04 — Proved equivalences, five-test sublation and no-build decisions

**Rule:** an equivalence is accepted only with a positive and negative first-consumer proof, or it is explicitly `UNKNOWN`/`UNPROVED`. A sourcefree test, a fixture `AuthoritySnapshot`, a manifest pointer, a hashed release, an unmerged reader or an old source permission is **not** a current authorized selected operative dependency. The five-test sublation is applied to every proposed reuse/build:

1. **Input/output ABI meaning** — exact kind, operation, object family and semantic payload preserved.
2. **Source/question/response role** — source family, wave, question, response role and scope preserved.
3. **Sample/grain/coexposure** — same-person/higher-order grain, denominator, risk set and overlap preserved; no pairwise reconstruction of a higher-order joint.
4. **Sign/direction and eligible/blocked/no-observation** — polarity/direction, positive/negative, observed zero, blocked, reject, missing and no-opportunity remain distinct; no false absence.
5. **Scope/rights/revision selection/dynamic withdrawal** — selected version, revision, shadow/revocation, rights and cold restore preserved.

A candidate that fails or cannot evidence any test is not “FULLY EQUIVALENT.” It may still be an adequate **sourcefree** or **bounded canary** capability at a declared scope.

## 1a. C7-003 historical-only/current-label audit (all 19 + 10)

The C7 review correctly held that `HISTORICAL_ONLY_OR_NO_JUSTIFIED_CURRENT_CONSUMER` requires a five-test retirement/no-justified-consumer court or an archived negative. That proof was **not** supplied for any of the 19 prior instances. The correction therefore:

- **Withdrew all 19 historical-only frozen labels** and retyped them to `NOT_RUN` with a typed `DEMAND_UNPROVED...` hold.
- **Preserved genuine historical evidence independently** through `historical_evidence_class`: `GENUINE_HISTORICAL_PROVENANCE_DOCUMENT` (F22/AR-0019), `GENUINE_HISTORICAL_NEGATIVE_OR_SUCCESSION_EVIDENCE` (F23/AR-0020–AR-0024), `GENUINE_HISTORICAL_SUCCESSION_AND_RULING_LEDGER` (F25/AR-0023/24/45), and `NONRUNTIME_MEMBER_SET_PRODUCER_INPUT` (F07/AR-0054–0057). These are **not** retirement findings.
- **Audited the 10 C6 `FUNCTION_CURRENT_BUT_AUTHORITY_PROVENANCE_UNPROVED` labels.** Seven (F01/F02/F06/F09/F10/F11/F17) have an exact current generic C11/M1 code ref from C1–C5 and no selected current operation; three (F03/F05/F08) lack an exact current function/first-consumer proof and are retyped `NOT_RUN` / `UNPROVED_CURRENT_SELECTION`. No case claims operational currentness from a domain/O-operation link alone.
- **Final taxonomy:** 51 historical snapshot = 21 `FUNCTION_CURRENT_BUT_AUTHORITY_PROVENANCE_UNPROVED` + 30 `NOT_RUN`; current 55-case return after C9 append = 25 `FUNCTION_CURRENT...` + 30 `NOT_RUN`; 0 historical-only, 0 implementation-inadequate.
