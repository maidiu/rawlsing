## 3. What is scientifically irreducible versus merely representational

**Irreducible powers at Rawlsing’s specified grain:** source ownership of different actors, *performed* activity and its material cost, formative/revisable rules from actual chronological experience, actor-independent world and genuinely independent others, non-omniscient sensitivity/ignorance, competing ends, no effect, actor’s own temporal continuance and a later action changed by its own lawful history. An implementation may store just a compact evidence-aware sufficient statistic if it preserves these distinctions and has exact historical read/replay; it need not simulate “consciousness as a module.”

**Derived/disposable or dangerous:** Enneagram/type category as initial cause; trait score without governing measurement; one adult seed with history-label swaps; compressed story as enacted event; universal “security”/“resilience” class; uncalibrated probabilities; imagined inner dialogue as authorized BELIEF; self-myth as authoritative past; class as value/political choice; objective possible joint encounter as actual perceived intimacy; LLM generative sample as independent parent consent. These may be observational labels, speculative model hypotheses or renderer text only if bounded and attributed, never original causal truth.

**Science cannot be replaced by more fields.** A strong system needs (i) **competently evaluated behavioral rule families** linking evidence to candidate effect, (ii) a normative decision about what counts as practice/commitment/claim/other initiative, (iii) test-relevant uncertainties and counterevidence, (iv) admissible actor-source and legal rights. Software can build owner adapters, compilers, immutable event histories and negative controls **now**, without inventing those scientific rates or claiming the native transition is already permitted. Conversely “no issuer” is no excuse to omit those buildable functions (00E).
