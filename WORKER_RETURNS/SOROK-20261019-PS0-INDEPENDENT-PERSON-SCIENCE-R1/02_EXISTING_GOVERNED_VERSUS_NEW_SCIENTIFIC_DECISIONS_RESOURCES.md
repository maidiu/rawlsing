## Grading and resource distinction

- **G — adopted source-authority/semantic grammar:** constraints, owner, admissible operations, negatives. Not equivalent to executed behavior or empirical truth of a particular life.
- **P — provisional Sorok product-science hypothesis:** condition for a compelling person, not an accepted population or individual psychological mechanism.

- **U — unissued original science or permission:** needs qualified decision or real source custody and data/use authorization. An exact hash is not a grant.
- **R — retained theory/research resource:** conceptual hypothesis relevant for discriminating mechanisms, not binding scientific constants.

The key architecture is **not one new actor compiler**. ## Scientific advances and unresolved inference problems

1. **Practice acquisition/selection.** Accepted CAN011 says `V3MS_PRACTICE` must be supported by recurrent enacted action, but does not say which observed recurrent performances justify a specific operation, which methods/objects/tasks are comparable, whether transfer to a different context is warranted, or when a practice is withdrawn. Compilable schema/source checks are engineering; **predicate relevance, exceptions and coverage are a scientific decision**, should admit `UNKNOWN` and `NO_CHANGE`.
2. **Subjective appraisal.** Adopted CAN008 R2 defines *where* actor-specific uptake belongs but not a universal psychological function from position, gender, age or event kind to response. Needed: specific evidence-bound, case-limited interpretive/response hypotheses with competing explanations, actor access and source/target grants. No universal `recognized_plan` map, attachment score, Enneagram driver or free-floating stochastic propensity.
3. **Need/intent formation.** Need is not merely a narrative desire statement or root survey conditional frequency. Material projects and alternatives require historically enacted investments and current constraints; which branch an actor selects requires a governed action/choice rule with honest valid-empty and finite alternatives. Human agency is not an unpredictable random-number knob.
4. **Relational constitution.** There is no warrant to store one global psychological effect for every relation. Sorok 55/56/74 (blobs `346ea0080094f77a75c64001be8deeac8db7643b`, `ce2b2eeeee4a86dd5108a44473daf23788afab10`, `26193e4b7c15dc03a76d5fd86222b1f65cd7d38d`) propose **composing** existing owner-states and source-authenticated relations. Needed science is whether and when that composition is insufficient for actual response distinctions. No new relational/self-myth subsystem merely because a phenomenon can be named.
5. **Temporal person integrity.** Human nonlinearity and contradiction are permitted without both total harmony and inconsistent world facts. Sorok 70 (blob `9ae2a42049014b7af2b00185d52ad144d0b5aa5d`) explicitly distinguishes late historically constrained materialization from retcon and preserves plausible self-contradiction. An engineered history bridge cannot create a formative childhood cause retroactively once downstream histories have closed.
6. **Memory/knowledge.** Sourcefree M01 shows independent parallel state conditioning; M02 shows two live claim selection/time REDs and unchanged nine CAN3 historical RED; MS4 serial proposed P3b fails despite repaired provenance. **Do not let a memory/epistemic adapter's existence count as validated content→belief mediation**. Return separate scientific rule docket if needed, not another fixture with labels.
7. **Empirical calibration.** Theoretical compatibilities with materialist activity (Leontiev), multiple contexts/dispositions (Lahire), reflexive self/agency (Archer), longitudinal life-course dependence (Elder/Mortimer), and material relations of class/work (Kohn/Schooler/Wright) are **possible design heuristics only** here. The PS0 court did not adjudicate empirical findings from those books, and their presence in uploaded/retained archives is **not project scientific adoption**. They cannot yield source-native generative actor coefficients from original35.
