## Especially important independent methodological objections

**A. A historical individual is neither a theory exemplar nor a random deviation.** A model may use class/household/work structures to delimit *opportunities* and registered practices; those are not sufficient to determine the individual's psyche. Similarly agency is neither a miraculous sovereign chooser nor unconditioned RNG. The scientific question is which **actual material activity, relations and response histories** mediate a presently available course of action. A complete state representation does not itself answer the transition-law question.

**B. Distinct worlds do not suffice without distinct actual biographies.** Even if source-world positions are different, both actor trajectories might be copied from a universal seed with different resource stock. Need at least two **separately causally generated actions/history sources**, the ability of their own other-person social histories to disagree, and a per-actor effect consumer. This remains a strictly bounded requirements court: it does not settle how much biographical breadth the product needs for full human plausibility.

**C. Need and contradiction do not entail a named moral lesson.** A materially strained worker may maintain both work and care obligations, publicly cooperate while privately dissenting, or fail to act at all. CAN011 state may persist without automatic reinforcement and external consequences need not make the actor wiser or nicer. No universal probability, class psychology or type label supplied here.

**D. Research literature is a heuristic/interpretive input, not an issued mechanism registry.** Activity theory (Leontiev), social dispositional plurality (Lahire), internal conversation (Archer), temporal life-course research (Elder/Mortimer), class/work effects (Kohn/Schooler/Wright), critical psychology (Holzkamp/Sève/Dreier), and social reproduction (Bhattacharya/Ferguson) suggest distinct hypotheses or constraints; this PS0 did **not** perform a primary-text scholarly adjudication establishing accepted causal predicates. Their archived presence cannot make authorial numerical rules scientifically issued. A competent scientist must specify observable individual-level evidence, counterfactual limits and rival mechanisms before any adopted implementation.
