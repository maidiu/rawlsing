## Frozen decision/authority law

- A complete token is permitted only when `candidate.distribution is not None`
  and `uncovered_target_mass == 0`.
- A null full-target distribution must never be presented as a complete joint.
- An unverified `admit=True` callback is not real-source authority.
- Caller `test_origin=True` and mixed-origin bundles never become real-use
  authority.
- Every `mapped:*` node must carry an authority ceiling no higher than its
  native parent.
- Aggregate support cannot silently renormalize away a rare/minority source into
  a complete population estimate.
- Producer/source hash and risk-scope substitution must be refused or exactly
  rebound, never silently stale.
- Genuine complete sourcefree positives must remain exact and consumer-usable.
- Test-only calculus is never called real permission.
