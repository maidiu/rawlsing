# R97 — decisions for the user, in plain language

You do **not** need to find scientific owners, certify code, or approve a new
architecture. The architecture is settled. These are product/permission choices
about what claims the system may make. Each section is independent.

The safe default is: **keep all three generic claims on hold** and keep using the
accepted R06 synthetic world product. Nothing is lost by holding; the system
still demonstrates the software path.

## 1. Structural fields (FF)

**Plain question:** May a specific relationship between two observed fields be
treated as a structural rule that changes the selected world view, or must it
remain context only?

Current behavior: context only. A real field/relation/admission object is not
enough, because the current adopted authority only permits context.

Choose one:

- **1A — Keep context only. Recommended default.**
  - Effect: fields can inform the view, but cannot change structural routes.
  - New work: none.
- **1B — Allow one named relationship to act structurally.**
  - Pick the relationship. Pick one handling model:
    - **A:** allow it only inside the current process, without publishing the
      internal authorization object;
    - **B:** keep a separate, tightly limited stored copy for replay;
    - **C:** authenticate from the published result plus exact provenance,
      without the internal object.
  - Effect: that one relationship may change one selected structural view. Other
    relationships do not inherit the permission.
  - New work: a bounded authority decision plus a small integration path; no new
    FF engine.
- **1C — Open structural use broadly.**
  - Effect: many relationships could alter structure.
  - Why not now: this needs a new scientific/authority decision and much more
    evidence. It is not supplied by the existing treatment.

If you choose 1B, say which relationship and A/B/C. If you choose 1A, nothing
further is needed.

## 2. Cross-unit fields (F6)

**Plain question:** May information from two different populations/units be
combined, or must it stay separate?

Current behavior: separate. Even with a transport label, a cross-unit combined
query is unavailable. That is deliberate: a label is not a working link.

Choose one:

- **2A — Keep cross-unit unavailable. Recommended default.**
  - Effect: no cross-population combined conclusion; same-unit two-field result
    remains valid.
- **2B — Allow one named cross-unit comparison with bounded conclusions.**
  - Pick the two sources, the target population/unit/estimand, the actual link or
    index evidence, and the missing-data/positivity policy.
  - Effect: you may get a bounded conditional statement, not a same-person joint
    and not a guaranteed single number.
  - New work: named transport/link decision and source access or derived
    non-person products.
- **2C — Allow a model-based cross-unit estimate.**
  - Effect: a stronger point/interval claim.
  - Why not now: needs a new model/validation decision, not just a link.
- **2D — Link actual respondents/households.**
  - Effect: a true linked record analysis.
  - Why not now: needs current source/link/privacy rights and actual indexed
    evidence; not a code-only choice.

If you choose 2B, say which two sources and which estimand. If you choose 2A,
nothing further is needed.

## 3. Time comparison (F7/F8)

**Plain question:** May the system report a measured change between two real
observed waves, or only demonstrate temporal behavior on synthetic inputs?

Current behavior: synthetic only. The R06 time pair is a modeled software test;
it is not a measured trend. The qualified source bank currently has no observed
pair.

Choose one:

- **3A — Keep synthetic temporal proof only. Recommended default.**
  - Effect: no measured trend or original temporal claim.
- **3B — Allow one named observed wave pair.**
  - Pick the two waves, the source editions, the country/population/estimand and
    wording, the observed fieldwork/publication dates, the harmonization, and
    whether you have design variance/covariance.
  - Effect: one measured comparison with explicit uncertainty. No respondent
    linkage or universal trend follows.
  - New work: source/F7/F8 permission and the named harmonization/design
    decision.
- **3C — Allow a broader multi-pair temporal program.**
  - Effect: more measured comparisons.
  - Why not now: much larger source/design/method burden; start with one pair.
- **3D — Adopt a new temporal method/estimand.**
  - Effect: changes what "change" means scientifically.
  - Why not now: this is a High/Astra-class scientific choice, not an R97
    implementation choice.

If you choose 3B, say which pair and whether only the honest uncertainty range
is allowed or a covariance-qualified interval is intended. If you choose 3A,
nothing further is needed.

## What these choices do not do

- They do not make synthetic evidence into original science.
- They do not open the original 35/1890/global F6/F7/F8 program automatically.
- They do not require you to certify code or appoint imaginary external people.
- They do not change N11/N12, J52/W52, CI/prod or Sorok holds.
- A named pilot still needs its source/use permission where original data is
  involved; that is a separate, explicit permission, not an assumption.
