    66	## 3. Full exact membership (G4-B)
    67
    68	`_build_fact` sets `risk_set_member_refs=evaluation.risk_members` and
    69	`denominator_member_refs=evaluation.denominator_members` (lines 986/987).
    70	These are full exact ordered member refs, not counts.
    71
    72	`SelectionMembership` **must** carry:
    73	- `candidate_member_refs`: exact ordered `ExactRef` stream for the selected
    74	  population;
    75	- `risk_member_refs`: exact ordered refs for the risk population;
    76	- `denominator_member_refs`: exact ordered refs for the denominator population;
    77	- nonselected eligible/answered/weighted state refs/members where the native
    78	  fact requires them;
    79	- counts only as diagnostics.
    80
    81	**Ordering/materialization:** use the exact `_ReleaseIndex` population order
    82	(`native_verified_physical_respondent_order` where applicable); member refs are
    83	the same exact `ExactRef` identities the evaluator would append. Hash/bind the
    84	membership stream to release/universe/query/predicate/risk/measure/authority/
    85	current. Replay re-derives the same refs; counts never construct facts.
    86
    87	**Baseline:** the current full `_ReleaseIndex` route always preserves full
    88	membership and remains valid. A compact membership sidecar is a **proposed,
    89	owner-qualified** option; it cannot exclude needed risk members to save space.
    90	A selected target page may be read selectively, but risk/denominator/routing/
    91	weight population validation and member-ref materialization remain full unless
    92	a qualified exact index proves otherwise.

    94	## 4. Cost accounting (G4-C)
    95
    96	Separate:
    97	- **selected page cost** — selected event/coordinate body bytes;
    98	- **full population validation** — all `R` respondents, routing/eligibility,
    99	  weights, condition/predicate evaluation;
   100	- **full risk/denominator membership** — exact member refs for every included
   101	  respondent;
   102	- **index/refs/output** — `_ReleaseIndex` population, member-ref
   103	  serialization, fact/replay output.
   104
   105	The baseline is `O(R)` population/routing/weight work per operation plus member
   106	refs and output; selected pages do not remove that. A compact index/sidecar may
   107	reduce repeated scan only if it preserves exact full membership and passes
   108	binding/invalidation. **No bounded-RAM or Q-reduction claim** for full-domain
   109	facts before measurement; materializing v1 full risk refs/output may dominate
   110	RSS/O. The 6 GiB guarantee remains unproven.
