## Hostile restrictions

A constant mode plus decoration fails if it cannot express contradictory acceptance/rejection/performance under independently grounded choices. A candidate that fabricates speech, a future habit, private causal motive, earlier memory, target-owner grant, identity, or real-world action is a **hard refusal**, not a low design weight. Unavailable evidence cannot be converted into `ABSENT`; externally observed trace does not grant a first-person memory. A later compassionate event can narrow, reinforce, differentiate, contradict or have no relevance; no 'healed by romance' predetermined result.

## Scientific and computational limitations

This is a finite sourcefree mechanism demonstrating **representation and admissibility**, not the psychological probability or empirical validity of any response. The `History.activity` witness is externally authored; parent history changes are deliberately not direct-to-child effects unless mediated by accessible events. No actual caregiver choice is generated during test runs. Rationale/meaning labels `NARROW`, `CONTRADICT` etc. are unconstrained research vocabularies pending qualified independent science. Initial sourcefree Python suite passing 37 cases establishes only behavior of its own fixtures.
