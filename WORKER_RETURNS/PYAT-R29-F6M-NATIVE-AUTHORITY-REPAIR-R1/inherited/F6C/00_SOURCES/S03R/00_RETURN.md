## Exact estimator and interpretation

For joint vector j and target stratum z, the implementation computes:
Q(j) = sum over z of T(z) times sum over eligible source s of [a_s / sum of a_r among sources eligible at z] P_s(j | z).

T(z) is an externally governed target-population share; a_s is a declared **policy mixture weight**, neither an estimated precision weight nor a survey sampling weight. The P_s entries come from **within-respondent native survey joint tables**, optionally recoded into a reviewed common category topology. Native survey mass/weights are upstream inputs, not appended to one fictional cross-survey sample. Each z can have a DIFFERENT effective mixture when source support varies; this is not a common population-invariant source-mixture estimand. An independently issued target, support and transport policy is indispensable.

The conditional reader computes Q(Y|conditions) from the inferred joint only and reports modeled denominator mass, not observed respondent risk. Sampling covariance for conditioned probabilities is not produced; total sample size remains null. The cellwise variance sum of squared mixture coefficients times supplied mapped-cell design variances is algebraically correct **only given** disjoint source/stratum estimating errors, fixed T and weights, and correct external covariance/design reductions. It omits uncertainty in target shares, design covariance, weight estimation, complex clustering, conditional ratios, nonresponse and mapping/transport error. Sensitivity bands are declared, uncalibrated scenarios; they are not intervals with statistical coverage.
