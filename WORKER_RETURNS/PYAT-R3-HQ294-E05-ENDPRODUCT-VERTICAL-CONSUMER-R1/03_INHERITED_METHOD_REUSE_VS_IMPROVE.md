## Comparative model choices, by claim grain (not model shopping)

1. **Exact native cell / conditional (default when observed, permitted):** strongest interpretation and replay, but high-dimensional cells may be sparse. Test calibration on held-out respondents inside permitted survey use; design-aware uncertainty, routing/weight.
2. **Hierarchical partial pooling (candidate, separate issuer):** share statistical strength within compatible instrument, country/cohort/design strata; benefits sparse cells; dangers shrink minority effects, hide incomparable instruments and transport biased priors. Demand posterior predictive checks, effective sample/cluster-aware uncertainty and no unauthorized wave pooling.
3. **Proxy or latent-driver candidate (candidate):** may condition non-measured context using a calibrated external domain relation, never claim direct observation or respondent linkage. Evaluate on held-out domain/age/time, disclose specificity loss and decision sensitivity, keep structural vs subjective categories separate.
4. **Temporal as-of state-space (candidate):** valid only when source-native comparability, official source-version/time and temporal use granted. Compare simple piecewise constant, bounded interpolation and uncertainty-aware drift. Prohibit extrapolation by default, preserve ruptures and age-at-event rather than imputing false biography.
5. **No-measurement source-free causal **design** experiment:** typed restrictions and counterfactual route supports can be demonstrated synthetically, but MUST NOT receive an empirical-world or model-use grade. It is the immediate E05 test contract, not source evidence.

## Scoring and acceptance proposals

- **Predictive**: proper scoring rule (multiclass log loss/Brier, negative log predictive density only with fit and target truth); calibration error within each eligible source/population and after independent source/domain transfer. No numeric claim in this review.
- **Quality/coverage**: authorized expected cells and observed consumer receipts / independently frozen expectations; retain unmatched atlas mappings, 35/35 estate and negative cases. Do not reward denial as success.
- **Uncertainty**: nominal 50%/90% interval coverage, design-based sensitivity, nonresponse/weights/scenario perturbation, how much probability stays UNKNOWN. Hidden held families/countries/epochs.
- **Persona relevance (actor-free)**: variant-versus-control distance in conditional **route support or approved probability geometry**, contrast direction consistent with typed institutional premise, surviving minority/alternative routes, causal-permission leakage count (must be 0), and same-world rerun identity.
- **Future actor eval (not authorized here)**: trained/blind judges of historically plausible contradictory developments, retrospective causal ledger trace, resilience to stereotype swaps and counterfactual consistency. No persona scores until V3 is lawfully executed.

## Sharp failure cases

A success must FAIL if: country label alone changes personality type; prose calls source attitude a statute; a frame/route/universe mismatch yields a 'smooth' trend; a model version without issuer yields confident probability; a family marginal is multiplied into an unobserved dyad; original nine/36 atlas shrinks/remaps active 35 silently; a historical fixture is promoted CURRENT; V3/V2 called during PRE-V3; a rare/contradictory possibility is dropped by a top-k heuristic; estimates get measured labels; callback produces generated parent histories before authority.
