## 1. Critical dependency, not an engineering confidence claim

The unproved indispensable ability is to turn world-conditioned, actually admitted lived encounters into reproducible differentiated future response mechanisms **without either** (a) conjuring subjectivity from sociological labels, (b) importing analyst-only inaccessible history into actor self-explanation, or (c) using a large model's prose to conceal missing state/rule authority. This core matters even if IDs, ledgers, snapshots and access validators are elegantly engineered. CAN008_R2's adoption establishes *jurisdiction and invariants*, not a compiled calibrated positive rule library. Existing GT0 static code explicitly terminates at blocked positive actor path. This is more serious than an incidental missing API adapter.

At the next scale, the real challenge includes unbounded heterogeneous events, shifting worlds/institutions, multi-person temporal consistency, memory access, world/memory invalidation, open-ended user actions, long-run retrieval, concurrency, causal observability and ongoing compute. A small scripted K1–K6 court would deliberately hold most of these fixed and cannot inherit their feasibility grade.

## 2. Bounded comparison workload — counts are design counts, not executed measurements

Frozen K1–K6 primary arms have counts `2 + 2 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 16` per candidate; each case lists three independent null controls, up to another `6 × 3 = 18` test interventions per candidate. If implemented as one evaluation per intervention: `16 + 18 = 34` per candidate and `68` for B0 vs B1, **before** independent repeats, replay checks, held-out samples, prompt variation and genuine time integration. These are not independent empirical subjects; shared fixtures count as shared evidence. Some nulls are assertions that could piggyback without a fresh invocation. Neither an actual cost nor an empirical success rate can be inferred from 68.

Minimum first mechanistic gate: two predeclared contrast families, **K3 inaccessible-trace causal influence and K5 crossed institutional-vs-felt recognition**, with their leak/nonauthority nulls. These are the quickest way to discover whether actual concrete current owner effects exist and maintain boundaries. If those two cannot produce typed differing outcomes under exact rules and grants, halt expansion to full K1–K6 or rich personality matrices. This is a **staged test**; a K3/K5 pass alone cannot count as all-six PASS.

## 5. Why currently not to spend on a large model trial

A large model may improvise a credible life narrative while omitting the required role topology, inaccessible trace firewall or exact owner-effect lineage. Such a trial would reward storytelling rather than distinguish the causal hypotheses. Until an exact rule consumes evidence and produces a typed owner change with a later effect, the richest model and prompt cannot close the central gap. A competent positive deterministic microcell plus independent negative tests is the cheaper prerequisite. Even that is a **test scaffold**, not a person scientifically learned from history.

Do not use a toy fixture's repeated 68 runs as 68 independent individuals, nor describe a hand-authored age-seven history as an organically generated person's childhood. Nor can fiction-like emotional plausibility prove class, social reproduction, psychological development or historical specificity. The upstream all-35 survey program remains separate; no population frequency or real cohort causal estimate is generated here.
