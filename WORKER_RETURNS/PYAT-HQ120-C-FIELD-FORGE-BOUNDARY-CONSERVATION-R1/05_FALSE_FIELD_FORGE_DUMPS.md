# PYAT HQ120-C — False Field Forge dumps

Status: `PASS__ANTI_DUMP_BOUNDARY_FROZEN`

The governing error pattern is:

`FIELD_NAME_LOOKS_STRUCTURAL != SEMANTIC_ACT_IS_FIELD_FORGE`.

Field Forge owns a new actor-independent structural **relation proposition or structural refusal** only after exact producer-owned empirical information, scope/risk/permission/negative authority, provenance and replay are already available. It does not become the retroactive owner of the evidence that makes that proposition possible.

## False dump classes

1. **Source semantics → Field Forge.** Wrong. Question/use meaning, response labels, missing/special states and source-wave semantics remain source-specific empirical authority.
2. **Respondent identity/universe → Field Forge.** Wrong. Exact eligible unit/risk set is an empirical premise.
3. **Routing/split/form/module law → Field Forge.** Wrong. OBL040 can adjudicate relation observability using routing facts; it does not re-own source routing.
4. **Measurement/denominator/design/weight/estimand → Field Forge.** Wrong. OBL042 preserves local refs and refusal ceilings; it does not manufacture the underlying measure or weight permission.
5. **Response polarity/signed projection → Field Forge because it says “direction.”** Wrong. V551 direction is empirical response semantics. Relation-level direction is a different claim.
6. **Same-respondent joint premise → Field Forge because it says “joint.”** Wrong. Exact respondent incidence, overlap denominator and joint morphology stay pre-Forge. Forge may form a joint/conjunction relation from them.
7. **Component roles/order/cardinality/authority vector → Field Forge.** Wrong. C4R1 proved that dropping these while preserving a reduced “structural” carrier is information loss.
8. **Empirical conditional/base distributions → Field Forge because they support a conditional relation.** Wrong. I03/I05 own the facts; Forge may later own the conditioned redistribution/difference relation.
9. **Validation/certification/review state → Field Forge.** Wrong. M1 owns family-rule-bound learned empirical adjudication and negative-state conservation.
10. **Learned release/dereference → Field Forge.** Wrong. M2 owns immutable ReleaseCore/PublicationBinding semantics.
11. **Learned publication/current-selection/supersession/invalidation → Field Forge.** Wrong. B5 is the current learned-product lifecycle owner.
12. **Historical V552 pressure score → Field Forge because it says “structural pressure.”** Wrong as a blanket assignment. A derived pressure observation is producer-owned empirical-social analysis; only a separately adjudicated actor-independent relation belongs to Forge.
13. **Historical target/axis/driver/channel fields → Field Forge because they look structural.** Wrong. They remain producer-owned semantic coordinates until a claim-specific relation uses them constitutively.
14. **Homology from matching labels, ancestry or shared measures → Field Forge.** Wrong. Current OBL042 is explicitly a homology-refusal ceiling absent positive mapping/transport authority.
15. **Position role from geometry → Field Forge automatically.** Wrong. OBL005 makes role claim-bounded and evidence-bound; geometry does not self-mint role.
16. **FIELD/hegemony/capital/conversion from a strong association → Field Forge automatically.** Wrong. FF02-A blocks these strong nouns; OBL043 distinguishes association/capital/conversion rather than granting a universal positive producer.
17. **Mediation/pathway from coexistence/dependence → Field Forge.** Wrong. Current `STRUCTURAL_MEDIATION_INPUT` is a claim-specific PRE-V3 target-use token, not generic relation permission.
18. **Causal direction/mechanism from dependence or conditioned difference → Field Forge.** Wrong. FF02 explicitly limits dependence to non-directional dependence and difference to noncausal difference.
19. **P4’s 25 exact joint cells → one branch/alternative/topology object.** Wrong. FF02-E requires separate set proposition, membership, cardinality, completeness and set-level authority.
20. **V552J readiness/probe scalar → Field Forge semantic permission.** Wrong. Historical thin/readiness is orchestration context; it cannot substitute pressure, comparability, semantic authority or downstream permission.
21. **Current Field Forge relation → PRE-V3 permission.** Wrong. `FIELD_FORGE_RELATION_TRUTH != RELATION_CURRENT_SELECTION != PREV3_TARGET_USE_ADMISSION`.
22. **Typed upstream negative → structural zero.** Wrong. `VALID_EMPTY`, missing, blocked, noncomparable and integrity failure remain distinct; Forge may return a structural refusal without rewriting the empirical state.

## The reciprocal error

The anti-dump rule does **not** keep everything upstream.

Once exact upstream premises are complete, these semantic acts are genuinely Field Forge:
- exact support relation;
- exact non-directional dependence relation;
- exact conditioned redistribution/difference relation;
- exact source-local joint relation;
- claim-local observability/routing relation or refusal;
- relation-indexed geometry where OBL041 and the claim require it;
- claim-bounded position-role receipt where OBL005 is actually satisfied;
- relation-level neutral comparison/direction under exact comparator authority;
- typed structural refusal/nonpromotion at the exact claim grain.

The split is therefore **act-based, not noun-based**.
