## R5 country Q7 — indexed codebook and Uganda summary corroboration, not original administered manuscript

The June 2012 Uganda **country** codebook identifies **Q7** as overall country direction; its indexed English question reads:

> What about the overall direction of the country? Would you say that the country is going in the wrong direction or going in the right direction?

Its country-specific released coding topology is **1 = going in the wrong direction; 2 = going in the right direction; 9 = don't know; 998 = refused to answer; -1 = missing data**. Uganda's contemporaneous 2012 summary indexes the same question as Q7. Neither the summary nor the codebook certifies the complete enumerator script, prompt-adjacent setup, spoken translations or routing in the field. **No original Uganda R5 administered questionnaire** was recovered in the bounded official Afrobarometer and archive search. The correct next owner is **Afrobarometer Data Management / Uganda R5 national research team (Wilsken Agencies / Robert Sentamu; historic contact in 2012 summary)**: request the *Uganda indigenized Round 5 interview instrument and final fielded translations, 2011–2012*, plus alteration log or provenance. Do not substitute a non-Uganda R5 instrument.

## R9 Uganda Q3 — original country-specific English draft and released codebook agree in indexed text

The Uganda-specific instrument at **PDF index 5, questionnaire printed p. 6** has the fuller lead-in, quoted here as *indexed text, subject to page-image verification*:

> Let's start with your general view about the current direction of our country. Some people might think the country is going in the wrong direction. Others may feel it is going in the right direction. So, let me ask YOU about the overall direction of the country: Would you say that the country is going in the wrong direction or going in the right direction?

Displayed Q3 choices **1 = wrong direction; 2 = right direction; 9 = don't know [do not read]**. R9 codebook adds **8 = refused; -1 = missing**; questionnaire generic refusal instruction appears at PDF index 4. Absence of a printed Q3-specific refused row does not imply refusal is impossible. No “neither,” neutral third outcome or “not asked” choice is authorized by the Q3 response domain.

**Construct:** a respondent's *subjective direction-of-country appraisal* (binary substantive judgement), NOT objective country performance, not institutional approval and not “political stability.” Q7 to Q3 is a candidate conceptual continuity edge only. R9's more elaborate neutralizing/balancing lead-in and “let me ask YOU” is a material **measurement-invariance question**; it could change acquiescence, prompt interpretation and DK/refusal propensity. No equal measurement effect is demonstrated. R9 instrument date, April 2023 codebook and November 2023 country release must not be flattened into one byte-identical artefact.
