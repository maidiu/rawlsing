The following is circular and cannot parent mapping authority:

1. P2 queries have domain `"1"`–`"5"`;
2. therefore C11 must emit `"1"`–`"5"`;

`CONSUMER_EXPECTS_X__THEREFORE_PRODUCER_MAY_CALL_NATIVE_VALUE_X`.

Target-domain membership alone is not semantic evidence of a native→target relation.

Lexical or numeric resemblance is likewise insufficient.
