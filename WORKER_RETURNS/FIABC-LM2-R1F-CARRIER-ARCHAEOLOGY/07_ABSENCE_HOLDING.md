# FIABC-LM2-R1F — Absence Holding

## Holding

The required frozen Q291 physical-cell carrier is absent from the browser-accessible estate inspected by this court.

This is an absence of **payload**, not an absence of identity, semantics, provenance, or source availability. The estate is rich in all four:

- the exact WVS7 package and contained SAV are identified;
- exact source SHA, dimensions, and historical decodes are proved;

What is not preserved is the one thing R1E Gate 4 still requires: the ordered physical observations themselves for `Q291G5` and `Q291P5`.

## Why the surviving estate cannot be inverted

For each of the 97,220 rows, R1E needs two raw physical witnesses plus their physical missing-state/declaration relation. That is 194,440 ordered cell facts.

The surviving objects constrain that missing sequence but do not encode it:

- source SHA authenticates the whole SAV, not two selected columns;
- row-binding hashes authenticate row order, not response values;
- selected-projection hashes authenticate historical projections whose bodies are absent and whose selected columns do not include Q291;
- C11 release/replay/inventory hashes authenticate object sets whose bodies are absent;
- P2F2 semantic tokens `"1"` through `"5"` state comparator semantics, not the observed response at any row;
- Q291 missing declarations classify a supplied raw value; they do not supply that value;
- questionnaire/source-structure material identifies variables and meanings, not respondent observations;
- aggregate counts, digests, resource measurements, and court receipts cannot restore the ordered preimage.

Any attempt to synthesize the missing rows from those materials would be fabrication, not recovery.
