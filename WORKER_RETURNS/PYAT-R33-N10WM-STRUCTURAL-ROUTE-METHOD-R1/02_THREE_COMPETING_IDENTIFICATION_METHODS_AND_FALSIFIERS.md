E05R's native client expresses `ELIGIBLE/BLOCKED_BY_MODELED_RULE/UNAVAILABLE_AUTHORITY_OR_CLOCK` and `WITHIN_MODELED_BUDGET/RESOURCE_CONSTRAINED`. No actor, exposure or route-selection claim.

Missing or revoked operands yield UNKNOWN, not False. These are modeled rules, not observed enforcement. A formally permitted opportunity can still be unusable because of unaffordable fee, police discretion, legal status, geography, disability, discrimination, hidden selection or household labor obligations. Particularist legal access and actual lived opportunity are separate estimands.

Same-rule label/display change produces byte-identical semantic product; only empirical law changes counts and minority cells; access=True + fee=7 + budget=5 blocks native nonparent training; civil_open=True + sanction=True blocks civil; revoked/unknown field refuses a definitive state; selected 2027-effective but not-yet-published revision does NOT supersede published historical rule. ## B — Partial identification

**Estimand:** a population target distribution `P_T(Y=y)` under a named target/risk scope and valid source-specific mapping. Distinct from `P(person chooses route|world)`.

With covered target submass `q_y` and unknown target mass `u`, and no information on allocation among cells, sharp marginal bounds are `q_y <= P_T(Y=y) <= q_y+u`. Sharpness: all unknown mass could be assigned to that cell or none; individual upper endpoints are not generally jointly attainable. When `u=1`, interval=[0,1]. If conditioned denominator is not supported across covered/unknown masses, no numerical conditional is issued. Valid monotonicity/exclusion can tighten bounds only after its operational definition and independent empirical test; do not assume legal permission implies monotone take-up (repression can alter route selection and enforcement). Sampling risk, source disagreement, missing target strata and mapping/transport assumptions are different uncertainties.

**Actual inherited complete demonstration:** F6M world A uses two independently declared lineages and target/source weights. Its four joint masses are 13/25, 1/10, 1/10, 7/25; formal|secure=26/31. Conditional uncertainty explicitly remains unidentified. World B has remote-territory uncovered share=1/5 and cannot issue complete joint conditional. The excluded-source stress case preserves unknown weight 1/2 and cellwise [1/8,5/8]. An aggregate source-stratum count gate does NOT protect minority cells; absence of a qualified minor subgroup is not a population zero.

**Falsifiers:** independent lineage and risk refs survive, no concatenation of respondent ids from incompatible sources; excluded strata are not survivor-renormalized; missing design variance stays null; reversed outcome role gets its own denominator; zero-support remains typed unknown; minority counterpattern survives; original GF1/GF1G substantive RED cannot be treated as a qualified replacement. Same public rule change does not change F6M's separately defined target estimator unless its target policy explicitly includes the rule; empirical-only change can change its measured cells if it belongs to the correct source.

**Grade:** F6M `F6M_I2_METHOD_CANDIDATE__NO_INSTALL`; correct **synthetic target distribution/bounds** at declared assumptions, **not route possibility selection probability**.

## C — Point propensity and selected-pathway weight

**Estimand:** `P_T(R=r | person eligible at t, structural world W, exposure E, history H, actor knowledge K)` for a named population/survey design and fully defined observed route outcome; choice or reachability must be distinguished from formal opportunity. For causal propensity under a proposed change, additionally specify `P(R^w=r)` and a defended identification design (valid exogeneity/adjustment/instrument, interference limits, selection/measurement law, positivity and transport). An empirical conditional frequency alone is NOT an intervention contrast.

**Minimum competent external model:** model owner independently declares outcome operationalization and time horizon, complete route universe including refusal/illegal/informal alternatives, eligible denominators and observation/censoring risk set, source and joint longitudinal linkage rights, class/colonial/geographic exposure stratification, effective enforcement and budget measures, actor knowledge evidence and private clock, external validation, missingness/survivor bias policy, uncertainty calibrated under sample design, identifiability and source-family revocation, valid-time/publication/request comparison, exact selected-world rule refs, C1/N10E evidence, and a named receiver/use-bound model issuer. A selection distribution needs a separate exhaustive mutually exclusive scenario family, base rates and identified normalization; a single feasible proposal is not its frequency.

**Nonidentification proof:** data contain modeled rule values plus source-local attitudes, *no route/actor outcome joint*. Construct two data-generating processes sharing every supplied observed value: all eligible actors choose route r vs none choose r. Both satisfy currently declared source facts. Therefore `P(R=r)` can be 0 or 1; any intermediate mixture likewise: the sharp identified set is [0,1]. In a formally blocked route, even an asserted zero concerns only the definition of **legally permitted execution**, not actual informal/defiant access. Arbitrary WP1 coefficients, unrelated marginal products, or normalized propensity guesses add an unfalsified assumption, not evidence.

**Controls/falsifiers:** same-rule relabel no effect; public rule change changes only correctly mediated eligibility component (not necessarily propensity without behavioral bridge); empirical-only source change updates evidence but does not automatically change a structural model; unknown actor awareness cannot be populated from publication date; false/revoked model issuer refuses, incompatible population denominator or clock refuses; minority/colonial counterexample with identical formal permission but unequal material access must defeat world-as-nine-scalars prediction.

**Grade:** UNIDENTIFIED; `route_probability=None`, `selection_weight=None`, no authorized model or selection issuer.

## Four-clock and structural power law (all methods)

**effective** = institutional rule operative at world-valid date; **publication** = knowledge publicly available by publication cut; **request** = receiver's selected as-of/use-bound time; **actor knowledge** = particular actor's opportunity to know, separate evidence and owner. Public availability is neither individual knowledge nor actual experience. Material barriers are class/property, territorial/police, colonial, gender, disability, language, kinship and social-reproductive constraints. Formal legal permission can coexist with exclusion through administrative discretion or violence; colonized or minoritized populations are not marginal numerical noise. Original historical world, actual event and private knowledge all remain outside this TEST_ONLY court.
