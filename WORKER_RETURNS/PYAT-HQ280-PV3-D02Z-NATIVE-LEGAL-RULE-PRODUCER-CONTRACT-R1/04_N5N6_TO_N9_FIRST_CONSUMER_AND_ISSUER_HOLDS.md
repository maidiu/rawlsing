## Rejection of dangerous apparent shortcuts

1. `EmpiricalFact` is not a generic fact about the world; P06 is an empirical-source universe and positive structural rule admission service. Legal text must not be coerced into it.
2. `pre553_source_local_universe_admission.DocumentaryField` documents a **survey source field**, not source text of Ghanaian statutes.
3. M4's `make_structural_object` can hash a caller-authored JSON body; it **does not prove institutional provenance or production**. Re-labeling as V554 to pass a whitelist is counterfeit.
4. Existing J04 `PREV3POS` with Q73A CONTEXT doesn't carry Ghana Act 591.
5. An OC02 `PolicyInput` with caller-created source/principal labels can appear positive; D02 documented this forged provenance vulnerability, so the source-science principal must be authenticated before any optional policy reading.
6. Date-cohort 1993 does not make Act 591 enacted in 1993; 2000 assent doesn't prove first effective date; Gazette notification doesn't by itself prove statutory commencement; 2016 reproduction doesn't prove all amendments or 2026 selected law.
7. Conditional citizen-by-birth legal text is neither a person-specific outcome nor state-capacity/access/administrative implementation. No claim that a legal restriction causes a particular behavior absent scientific causal source and rule.
8. Text-only archival rule context cannot silently become material pressure, objective affordance, a selected-current world, G0b or actor V3.
