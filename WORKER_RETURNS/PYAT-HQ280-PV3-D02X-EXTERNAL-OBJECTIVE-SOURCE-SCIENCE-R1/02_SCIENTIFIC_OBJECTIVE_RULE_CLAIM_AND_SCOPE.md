# D02X — original source fact, legal chronology and exact claim ceiling

## Narrow positive: objective institutional rule

A real external original is now in view. Ghana Parliament's **Citizenship Act, 2000 (Act 591)** is independently catalogued in [Parliament's repository](https://repository.parliament.gh/handle/123456789/1773). The complete clause can be read at Ghana's government-hosted [Act 591 PDF](https://www.brr.gov.gh/acc/registry/docs/Citizenship%20Act%20-%20ACT%20591.pdf), Part I s.7, PDF viewer pp. 2–3 (0-based 1–2). A second distinct official primary source, [1992 Constitution scan hosted by the Office of the Special Prosecutor](https://www.osp.gov.gh/media/Resources/constitutionofghana.pdf), Chapter Three Article 6(2), 0-based PDF p.17, contains the corresponding constitutional text. Both pages were visually examined, not just accepted from search snippets.

**Text-level proposition** (paraphrase, not a legal status determination): for the post-7 January 1993 birth cohort, Ghanaian citizenship by birth is stipulated by a qualifying Ghanaian citizenship relationship of parent or grandparent, whether birth occurs in or outside Ghana, subject to the governing Constitution. This gives a national **conditional legal rule**, not a measured distribution or record of individual citizenship awards.

The statute's Section 2 explicitly frames Part I as a period-indexed restatement of birth-citizenship law. Earlier births fall into other provisions, not s.7. Sections 8–14 describe **distinct** foundling, adoption, registration and naturalisation paths. These must not be collapsed into s.7's post-1993 birth rule.

## Institutional rule versus things it does not prove

1. **Enacted-rule text:** positively sourced: a legal citizenship-by-birth eligibility criterion with conditional ancestry/period.
2. **Actual rule interpretation and live validity:** contingent on complete legal version, commencement and relevant judicial construction at precise date.
3. **Implementation:** no measurement of registry coverage, passport documentation, officer conduct or administrative consistency.
4. **Experienced access:** no person-specific citizenship determination, application, accommodation or entitlement experienced as service.
5. **Causality:** no direction, mechanism strength, changed-determinant or consequence, merely because an institutional rule exists.
