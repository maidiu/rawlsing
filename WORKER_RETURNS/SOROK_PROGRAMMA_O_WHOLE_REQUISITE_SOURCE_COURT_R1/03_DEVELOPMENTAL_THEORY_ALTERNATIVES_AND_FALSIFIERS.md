## T01_ACTIVITY_MOTIVE — How does a motive take its object in practical activity?
**Competing accounts and causal mechanisms**
1. **Leontiev activity/motive.** Need becomes objectively oriented through socially situated action/object within activity; do not read an object label as subjective motivation *Source grade:* Verified Leontiev Portuguese PDF title only; precise chapter/page not independently extracted, correspondence HYPOTHESIS.
2. **Stable preference/identity seeding.** Read intrinsic motive directly from class, type, age or trait *Source grade:* Historically old V3 M15/M16/M31 source is exact; rejected as causal warrant.
**Discriminating case:** Two persons with same available object but divergent historical participation/practice and constraints; compare performed later choices after access equalized

**Rival / limit / cost:** Material opportunity alone might explain outcome; don't infer motive without history **Original decision owner:** CAN009/010/011; S3/S6. **Disposition:** `THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS__NO_ISSUER_RULE`. **Reference set:** Leontiev; Original V3 M15/M16/M31.

## T02_PLURAL_CONTEXT — Is a relatively stable disposition one total trait or a repertoire activated by situation/role?
**Competing accounts and causal mechanisms**
1. **Lahire plural actor.** Dispositions arise across heterogeneous socializing contexts; latency/inhibition and situation are necessary variables *Source grade:* Verified The Plural Actor PDF contents: 'plurality of social contexts and repertoires of habits'; 'negative power of context' headings.
2. **Cross-context durable dispositions.** Some formed operations carry across contexts but with scope and support limits *Source grade:* R3 Sorok C3/M21, original provisional ontology person-stable versus role priors.
**Discriminating case:** Compare identical task under family/work/school/stranger roles with same resource but different history; observe both transfer and role-specific failure

**Rival / limit / cost:** Context selection can correlate with resource/competence differences **Original decision owner:** CAN011/PFF/C9; S1/S3/S4. **Disposition:** `THEORETICAL_HYPOTHESIS__NO_ACTOR_RULE`. **Reference set:** Lahire; Sorok original ontology.

## T05_PRACTICE_ACQUISITION — When is a new operation genuinely acquired?
**Competing accounts and causal mechanisms**
1. **Leontiev/Dreier practice across activity contexts.** Action developed by participation and transfer across everyday contexts, contingent on actual means *Source grade:* Dreier verified PDF introduction describes therapy making effect in everyday practice across places; Leontiev text title verified.
2. **One successful action equals learned habit.** Completion immediately adds a global competence weight *Source grade:* B3 and CAN011 original adversarial contrast; not a valid general law.
**Discriminating case:** Repeat task with/without helper after source-supported training; distinguish performed independent operation, assistance, nochange, unobserved status

**Rival / limit / cost:** Success after helper withdrawal may follow environment simplification, not learning **Original decision owner:** CAN011 O05/O07 and C9 E2. **Disposition:** `ORIGINAL_RULE_T_UNISSUED`. **Reference set:** Dreier; Leontiev; B3AS X3/S3 source review.

## Cross-theory contradictions that must survive a current product
- **Relational constitution ≠ total dyad ownership.** A durable act/competence might transfer across roles even where particular trust does not. Conversely a role can inhibit an existing repertoire (Lahire versus an atomized stable trait).
- **Material permission ≠ motive ≠ effective action.** Real poverty, caregiving obligations and work schedules constrain feasible choice. They neither automatically set desire nor erase human agency.
- **Care ≠ compliance; attachment ≠ flourishing.** A person's safety may increase when boundary conflict temporarily reduces user satisfaction. No single universal numeric health axis is issued.
- **Objective history ≠ perceived occurrence ≠ actor's belief ≠ narrated memory.** CP-A5's unseen market event and seen workshop tool establish sourcefree access logic, not native autobiographical phenomenology.
- **One helpful performance ≠ actor-acquired practice.** Assistance withdrawn and changed environmental difficulty are rivals; NO_CHANGE must be evaluated rather than absence interpreted as no change.
- **Structural regularity ≠ group destinies.** Kohn/Elder/Mortimer contextual mediation cannot be translated into class/age/ethnicity → wounds, attachment or theology defaults.
- **Plurality ≠ incoherence.** Contextual variation and durable self-organization can coexist; nonnative fixture alternatives cannot settle their generality.
- **Harm prevention ≠ emotion censorship.** Genuine disagreement, anger, grief and refusal need not be eradicated to produce an ethical experience; platform control is distinct from diegetic persona state.
- **Release readiness ≠ attention/retention.** Original Sorok Charter prohibits coercive attachment monetization; S13 ethics must be tested under incentives, S15 under participant evidence.
- **KERNEL = UNRESOLVED.** Relational recursion, causal otherness, incomplete knowledge, irreversible history and ethical neighbor-as-end remain rival generative explanations; no universal kernel adoption.
