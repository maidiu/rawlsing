A newly created synthetic A is not a reconstruction of an observed human. Independent B may own, share, or withhold a scarce work instrument. An objective A inspection occurs only on separately performed A action and world feasibility; history then retains the episode. A contrary project, refusal and no-effect must remain possible.

The *current* scientific claim is physical/relational possibility and actual situated enactment. Psychological learning remains a competing downstream HYPOTHESIS, not an issued consequence. 2. Enacted situated action: actor-owned and time-ordered performed receipt, not intention, chat or narration.
3. Social participation: separately constituted B action changes A's material setting; learning/affect not inferred from mere B act.
4. Durable acquired practice: history-mediated, context-appropriate transfer to later acts; requires independently qualified causal/process source and specific CAN011 rule/grant. **NOT ISSUED; target NO_CHANGE.**
5. Memory, self-understanding or belief: independent ADP016/017 actor access evidence; **UNKNOWN** in GM01.
6. Human-equivalent C12/person experience: actual original source/grants and external A4/A5 study; **NOT RUN**.

## Source and scientific warrant
The 1982 Kohn–Schooler longitudinal article (American Journal of Sociology 87(6):1257–1286, DOI 10.1086/227593) examines reciprocal work conditions and personality, including selection versus socializing effects. It supports the general plausibility that concrete activity conditions can matter developmentally, but not the exact GM01 B tool→A behavior causal parameter or a rule that inspection becomes enduring practice. Source: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/227593. User-provided Kohn–Schooler Work and Personality (1983 edition) preface explicitly separates cross-sectional selection questions from later longitudinal evidence. Ole Dreier, Psychotherapy in Everyday Life (2007), examines people conducting lives across therapy and other social contexts; a qualitative/contextual conceptual source, not an estimated tool-sharing transition. Leontiev, Activity, Consciousness, and Personality, gives object-directed activity and goal/motive distinction, not population-validated psychological coefficients (https://lchc.ucsd.edu/mca/Paper/leontev/ch3.htm).

Even when a peer-reviewed study reports longitudinal job→orientation effects, broad observed relationships do not entitle GM01 to apply invented person-level dispositions, transfer those effects across cultures or assign deterministic B→CAN011 change. Tests are *internal* mechanistic falsification, NOT independent empirical validation.
