## Disposition

**I4 is sufficient unchanged at its declared HQ119 jurisdiction.**

Exact ruling:

`I4_DISPOSITION = SUFFICIENT_UNCHANGED_AT_DECLARED_GENERIC_COMPOSITION_JURISDICTION`

and:

`I4 != SOURCE_SEMANTIC_OWNER != JOINT_AUTHORITY_OWNER != FIELD_FORGE_RELATION_OWNER != PREV3_TARGET_USE_OWNER`

- product family;

That is the correct composition surface because the historical information classes already have current semantic owners upstream or downstream.

I4's job is to preserve those owners by exact reference. It is not to restate their fields.

## Exact anti-amputation path

### Source / wave / respondent / measure / query material

These live in the source-specific projection, I01 canonical objects, I03 query objects, P07 source relations and exact producer replay.

When M1 is used, the exact I05 graph already carries source-universe, risk, topology and measure material. M1's bundle also exposes `additional_dependency_refs`.

The current M1 runtime explicitly:

1. canonicalizes `additional_dependency_refs`;
2. places them in the disposition dependency closure;
3. preserves the exact input-bundle preimage in determinant material;
4. emits the dependency closure into `LearnedEmpiricalDisposition`.

Therefore a constitutive exact object such as a `ComponentAuthorityVectorReceipt`, specialized source-relation receipt, or other governed joint object can be retained without M1 or I4 knowing a legacy field name.

### Joint authority / non-scalar / scalarization material

The common semantic owner is `ComponentAuthorityVectorReceipt@1.0.0`.

I4 must **not**:
- reconstruct component bindings;

- turn heterogeneous/incomplete/empty vectors into scalar product fields;
- infer an old V550J/V551J/V552J equivalence.

Where a learned product depends on a component vector, the product contract's existing closure-construction rule must require that exact receipt in the upstream dependency/determinant closure.

That is an adapter validation obligation, not a new contract field.

## Why I4 must not absorb Field Forge

Field Forge remains a distinct semantic court.

Current Field Forge evidence proves:
- adopted actor-independent relation authority is broader than the one current generic-looking canary implementation;
- positive relation production is family/claim-specific;
- typed refusal is lawful without manufacturing a positive relation;
- relation currentness is distinct from PRE-V3 target-use permission;
- no neutral PRE-V3 target-use token is currently granted.

Extending I4 with structural-relation or structural-target-use fields would collapse the exact boundary HQ116/HQ118/HQ119 preserve:

`LEARNED_PRODUCT_COMPOSITION != STRUCTURAL_RELATION_TRUTH != RELATION_CURRENTNESS != PREV3_TARGET_USE_PERMISSION`

I4 therefore stays upstream of Field Forge.

## Direct-producer routes

If a direct consumer needs a component vector, source-relation authority, or exact empirical replay object, the product contract must name/preserve that object through its closure rule or direct exact route.
