# M2 owner seams and decision boundary

**ADP019/021:** only a competent event issuer may admit an occurrence and establish full content/event-ID/time lineage. M2's string `TEST_ONLY_INDEPENDENT_WORLD_FIXTURE`, `_lineage_integrity`, SHA-256 and `WorldFact` are research guards, **not** signed admission or RFC8785 custody. Same-ID/new-content must refuse before effects.

**ADP015/CAN010:** `Actor`, `History`, parent/peer/P prior-project actions are test-controlled synthetic input histories, not independent generative caregiver/peer life or longitudinal need processes. One actor cannot borrow another actor's history witness.

**ADP017:** `access` string and candidate do not prove hearing or belief. `SpeechAction` + `Exposure` require independently observed speech action and exact listened-to source, with clock. No automatic access to speaker motive/private belief, even when words are heard.

**ADP016/CAN011:** `Interpretation` and procedural residues are append-only source-free projections; test-only `fixture_grant()` is locally minted, not a third-party permission. Real durable memory/middle-state needs target owner checks of its exact prestate, rule implementation hash, actor scope and source, and atomic effect jurisdiction.

**CAN008:** M2 only enumerates noncausal possible meanings; has no qualified rule for which belief, affect, action or mediator is actually formed. A real event-to-psyche coupling would require independently qualified learning/developmental science, material and relation-specific evidence.

**ADP008/020:** no demographic-to-psyche shortcut, lifecycle phase registration, psychometric probabilities or native RNG authority.

**R04:** `NO_CHANGE` must be distinguished from a nonpsyche audit record. **R05:** custody hashes change on any byte change, but effects only on separately witnessed substantive activity. **R10:** unrelated events cannot be asserted to cause reinterpretation; filter candidates or grade as `PROPOSED_ONLY_UNEVALUATED` pending qualified relevance evidence. These are sourcefree research decisions, not issued canonical law.
