## Repairs embodied in standalone Python kernel.py
1. **Clock separation:** `eligible` refuses access to speech that occurs after the process event's clock; a separate later `PROCESS` event with exact parent-event hash may lawfully process previously admitted speech. `propose` refuses affect and public action witness records from the future. No t4/t5 leak into t3 loss.
2. **Immutable event content lineage:** `Interpretation` includes each process event SHA-256 and the exact originating event ID/content hash, and `_lineage_integrity` checks old content against current event registry. Same ID/different facts does not silently retain old interpretation.
3. **Independent test-only history evidence:** `HistoryWitness` binds actor, actual history content hash, observation clock, independent observer, scope and operation. `History.witnessed=True` alone, `prior_` prefixes and unregistered source references confer no eligibility.
4. **Typed objective grammar:** `WorldFact` restricts material facts to explicitly enumerated nonculprit categories and owner/source references, with a small legacy-atom allowlist. Attributed ideology/culpability stays in attributed speech; no string-denylist loophole.
5. **Actual speech and exposure:** separate `SpeechAction` and `Exposure` bind the exact speech hash, an independent action event, observer, actor, listener, exposure time and fixture issuer. Adding a speech ID to actor `access` cannot manufacture having heard it.
6. **Non-collapsing outcomes:** plural ACCEPT/REJECT/SUSPEND remain candidates; no selected child belief, affection or durable effect follows from LLM prose. Independent P refusal, historical habit, no-effect and deterministic replay are tested. `NO_CHANGE` returns the same durable `State`; separate immutable `NoChangeAudit` can register procedural evidence without fabricating psychological change.
