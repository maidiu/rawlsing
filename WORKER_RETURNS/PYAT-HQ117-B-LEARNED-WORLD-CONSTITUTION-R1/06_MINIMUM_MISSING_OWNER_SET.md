# PYAT HQ117-B — Minimum Missing Owner Set

## Conclusion

Rawlsing does **not** need a new serial “validation stage → release stage → lifecycle stage” machine.

It does need three currently unproved ownership capabilities before the **generic post-I05 empirical learned world** can truthfully be called complete.

They may be physically co-located. They may be implemented by one module or several. Their semantic jurisdictions must remain distinct.

## M1 — Learned Empirical Adjudication Owner

### Mission

Own the transition:

```text
exact I05 discovery receipt + exact empirical evidence
-> exact typed learned-empirical disposition
```

### It must own

- the adjudication event/identity;
- exact claim/relation family binding;
- competent validation rule binding;
- exact evidence/scope/risk/measure/uncertainty closure;
- positive acceptance or exact nonpositive/rejected/blocked disposition;
- semantic ceiling;
- replay/explanation root.

### It must not own

- source semantics already owned upstream;
- I03/I05 execution;
- source composition unless separately authorized;
- Field Forge structural meaning;
- product current-selection;
- consumer permission;
- actor state.

### Why it is missing

I05 owns discovery-grain validation.

FF02 owns a bounded Field Forge semantic court.

No current generic learned-world owner was proved for the relation/product-grain adjudication between those jurisdictions.

## M2 — Learned Product Release / Publication Owner

### Mission

Make a producer-owned learned semantic product or typed disposition immutable, referenceable and historically replayable without stealing its meaning.

### It must own

- learned-product release identity;
- publication/resolution binding;
- exact semantic producer/product ref;
- exact authority closure;
- direct dependencies;
- determinant set;
- replay/reconstruction root;
- explanation lineage;
- typed negative/uncertainty state;
- product/service kind and lawful resolution endpoint/ref.

### It must not own

- validation criteria;
- semantic meaning produced by M1 or another product owner;
- lifecycle currentness;
- target-use permission;
- physical carrier choice.

### Why it is missing

C07 requires durable learned release/service identity.

Current Route-B execution receipts are execution envelopes, not product releases.

M4 is structural publication.

No generic empirical-social learned-product release/publication owner is proved current.

## M3 — Learned Product Lifecycle / Current-Selection Owner

### Mission

Apply current P2 lifecycle law to exact learned-product releases/dispositions.

### It must own

- explicit current selection;
- typed recertification;
- correction;
- scope change;
- supersession;
- withdrawal;
- no-current state;
- lifecycle event history;
- product-level invalidation/rebuild linkage.

### It must not own

- product semantics;
- authority semantics;
- consumer permission;
- downstream Field Forge/PRE-V3 admission.

### Why it is missing

P2 lifecycle law is current.

`pre553_authority_maintenance.py` is an AuthoritySnapshot lifecycle owner only.

GT12 is a bounded relation-current-selection precedent.

B5 is a non-current lifecycle candidate.

C5R2 is a non-current prototype.

Therefore generic learned-product lifecycle execution remains provider debt.

## Cross-cutting capability — exact determinant/replay graph

This is mandatory, but it need not become a fourth semantic owner if M2/M3 bind existing exact replay/determinant mechanics correctly.

Current capital already includes:

- ExactRef/AuthorityRef identity;
- `pre553_replay_audit.py`;
- `pre553_authority_maintenance.py` determinant graph mechanics;
- C06/C07 historical replay law;
- C08 changed-determinant + transitive-dependency theorem.

The missing act is to **register learned releases into that closure** without pretending AuthoritySnapshot lifecycle already owns products.

## What does not belong in the minimum universal owner set

The following remain claim/product-specific authorities or services, not universal stages:

- statistical/uncertainty method;
- source/wave semantic interpretation;
- weights/estimands/design;
- measurement portability;
- source-universe pooling/composition/backfill;
- positioned-subject model/walkback/proxy authority;
- DDM analytical rule;
- Field Forge relation semantics;
- structural admission;
- PRE-V3 target-use permission;
- persistence/cache/index strategy.

Generic mechanics may enforce their presence.

Generic mechanics may not invent their content.

## Completion criterion

The pre-Field-Forge learned world can be called generically complete only when current authority can demonstrate:

```text
I05 exact result
-> M1 exact learned-empirical disposition
-> M2 immutable learned-product release/publication
-> M3 explicit current-selection/lifecycle
```

with:

- typed negatives conserved;
- exact history/replay conserved;
- direct producer ownership conserved;
- claim-conditional authorities required only when the requested product/use needs them;
- no structural or actor semantics introduced.

A direct Field Forge court may legally bypass M2/M3 as a **courier path** for one exact consumer operation where the consumer owns its own semantic adjudication.

That does not satisfy the generic completion criterion above.

## Recommended implementation-order consequence

Do not halt the source-free Route-B compositor merely because M1-M3 are not yet implemented: that compositor is allowed to produce exact pre-Field-Forge empirical objects and replay evidence.

But do not label its output “complete learned world” or “current learned product” until the relevant M1-M3 contracts exist.

The clean forward architecture is:

1. Route-B compositor produces exact I05/evidence packet;
2. constitute M1-M3 at the smallest current generic grain;
3. publish/select actual learned empirical products where needed;
4. present those actual products—or direct exact producer objects where lawfully sufficient—to Field Forge;
5. keep consumer-specific comparability/composition/model/target-use authority conditional.
