# PYAT HQ117-B — Validation and Learned Empirical Relation Law

## Holding

The current machine has a strong **discovery evaluator** and strong **structural/Field-Forge adjudication law**, but it does not have a proved generic owner for the semantic act between them:

```text
I05 DISCOVERY RESULT
        !=
ACCEPTED / VALIDATED LEARNED EMPIRICAL RELATION
        !=
FIELD FORGE STRUCTURAL-OBSERVATORY RELATION
```

The missing middle is real provider debt.

## 1. What I05 owns — and what it does not

Current exact I05 owns the bounded discovery court defined by an exact `DiscoveryAuthority`.

It may:

- compile the authority-declared candidate frontier;
- execute exact I03 queries;
- apply the validation kinds literally authorized inside that discovery authority;
- produce exact `DiscoveryResultReceipt` objects;
- classify candidates into their terminal discovery states;
- preserve support/uncertainty evidence;
- prove the declared frontier was completely evaluated in exact-bounded mode;
- replay the result deterministically.

That is a complete and important **discovery-grain** judgment.

It does not thereby own a universal post-discovery empirical-social truth transition.

The public schema makes the boundary visible. `DiscoveryResultReceipt@1.0.0` contains:

- discovery authority;
- candidate identity;
- winning predicate;
- empirical and validation fact refs;
- coverage state;
- evaluated frontier;
- ancestry;
- support/uncertainty;
- replay.

There is no current generic public object family named or equivalent to a universally owned `ValidatedEmpiricalRelation` or `LearnedProductRelease`.

Therefore:

`I05_VALIDATION_RULE_EXECUTION != GENERAL_LEARNED_RELATION_ADJUDICATION`.

## 2. What distinguishes a discovery candidate from an accepted learned empirical relation

The distinction is an **authority-bearing semantic adjudication at the claim/product grain**.

This is a capability description, not the name of an existing generic schema.

A lawful positive transition must bind, at minimum where relevant:

- the exact discovery candidate / discovery receipt;
- exact empirical facts and query/predicate identities;
- exact empirical universe, source release, risk set and time/scope;
- measure / weight / estimand identities when constitutive;
- exact claim/relation family and its constitutive roles/order/cardinality;
- the competent validation/adjudication rule and authority;
- support and uncertainty state;
- all applicable negative, denial, noncomparability and observability limits;
- source/semantic/operation authority closure;
- deterministic replay and explanation lineage;
- the semantic ceiling: what the accepted empirical relation does **not** establish.

The result must be an exact typed disposition. A positive accepted relation is only one possible disposition. Lawful nonpositive, rejected, blocked, unresolved or valid-empty outcomes remain first-class where the governing court requires them.

Exact provenance is necessary but never sufficient:

`PROVENANCE != VALIDATION AUTHORITY`.

## 3. Generic interface; claim-specific substance

C07's historical phrase `MISSING_AUTHORITY__EMPIRICAL_VALIDATION_LAW` should not be repaired by inventing one universal statistical threshold.

The current evidence supports a narrower constitution:

### Universal constitutional requirements

Every learned-relation adjudication must preserve:

- exact claim identity;
- exact evidence identity;
- exact authority;
- typed nonpositive states;
- scope/risk/measure semantics;
- replay;
- negative ceilings;
- separation from structural admission and actor state.

### Claim/family/source-specific requirements

The actual positive validation rule may depend on:

- relation family;
- source/wave;
- topology;
- measure and estimand;
- survey design/weight authority;
- uncertainty method;
- multiplicity;
- historical/comparability premises;
- target use.

Those should remain separately governed.

Thus the missing owner is a **generic adjudication jurisdiction/contract plus competent family-specific rules**, not a monolithic universal estimator.

- four neutral relation families;
- no relation objects instantiated;
- no relation current-selected;

This proves that exact producer-owned I05 objects can be taken directly into a **specific competent Field Forge semantic court**.

It does not prove:

`GENERAL_EMPIRICAL_VALIDATION_OWNER_EXISTS`.

It proves instead:

`A_CONSUMER_WITH_ITS_OWN_COMPETENT_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY_MAY_ADJUDICATE_EXACT_UPSTREAM_OBJECTS_DIRECTLY`.

That is lawful direct DAG reuse.

It cannot be generalized into:

`EVERY_I05_RESULT_IS_ALREADY_A_VALIDATED_LEARNED_RELATION`.

## 5. Field Forge direct consumption: exact limited consequence

Field Forge may consume exact `EmpiricalFact`, `EmpiricalQuerySpec`, `EmpiricalPredicate`, discovery receipt, authority and replay preimages directly when:

1. the requested Field Forge relation family is within current Field Forge ownership;
2. the exact family-specific rule/authority exists;
3. source/scope/risk/measure/negative prerequisites are complete;
4. no upstream empirical meaning is being invented;
5. the weakest lawful relation/refusal is emitted.

For such a use, no universal extra learned-release wrapper is required merely as a courier.

But that optimization has a strict ceiling.

It does **not** close the generic learned-world requirements for:

- reusable accepted empirical relations;
- empirical-service publication;
- historical learned releases;
- generic current selection;
- supersession/withdrawal;
- cross-consumer citation;
- validation lineage;
- DDM/empirical-inference services.

`DIRECT_CONSUMER_DEREFERENCE != LEARNED_WORLD_AMPUTATION`.

No learned-relation owner may normalize these into ordinary zero, missing, false, weak positive, or “no relation” without an exact rule authorizing that transformation.

In particular:

`VALID_EMPTY != NEGATIVE_RELATION`.

`SOURCE_UNIVERSE_COMPOSITION_MECHANICS = CURRENT`

`UNIVERSAL_POSITIVE_COMPOSITION_AUTHORITY = NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

## 9. Structural admission remains downstream

Neither an I05 supported candidate nor an accepted learned empirical relation automatically authorizes structural use.

Current P1/P3/P4 and Field Forge law preserve:

`EMPIRICAL_RELATION != STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION`.

Field Forge or later PRE-V3 admission must bind its own exact semantic/target-use authority.

## 10. Constitution

The post-I05 learned-world validation constitution is therefore:

```text
DISCOVERY RECEIPT
+ EXACT FACT / QUERY / PREDICATE / SCOPE / REPLAY
+ COMPETENT CLAIM-FAMILY VALIDATION AUTHORITY
+ NEGATIVE / UNCERTAINTY CLOSURE
        |
        v
EXACT TYPED LEARNED-EMPIRICAL ADJUDICATION
        |
        +--> accepted learned empirical relation (where positive)
        +--> exact nonpositive / blocked / rejected / unresolved disposition
```

Current Rawlsing proves the inputs and multiple bounded consumer-specific adjudications.

It does **not** yet prove one generic live producer for this transition across the complete learned world.
