## 2. What the primary theories actually constrain

The following refer to supplied primary texts examined as PDFs/TXT in this session. These are **text-based research constraints**, not validation studies or original CAN/ADP rule issuance. Page positions refer to the printed page when visible, not arbitrary PDF raster indices.

1. **A. N. Leontiev, `Actividade, Consciência e Personalidade`, Portuguese edition, theoretical introduction (paragraphs on activity, consciousness and personality).** The text explicitly situates activity as more than an input causing an internal reflection; activity's contradictions and transformations mediate psychic formation. This objects to old-V3 `update_domains` mechanically mutating a state solely because an externally labeled event occurred. The causal middle is *the actor's situated activity upon an object* and the objective activity's material relations. Consequence: Rule A identifies actual available/perceived practical relevance, Rule T identifies warranted activity/practice change, and NO_CHANGE remains an outcome. Do not reduce a humanly made motive to a biological-need vector.

2. **Lucien Sève, `Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality`, part II, “The personality as a living system of social relations between acts” (printed pp. 193–194), and preceding labor/wage analysis.** The text asks whether the labor–wage mediating social relation can disclose the structure of whole personality; it contrasts the immediate result of labor with the social mediation of personal subsistence. This is a direct objection to a self-contained “state-with-traits” engine. At minimum, link actor acts to paid/unpaid labor time, resources, social relations, and future possible actions. Its social-relations approach is not a license to infer one person's motive from class origin.

3. **Bernard Lahire, `The Plural Actor`, “Conditional dispositions,” printed pp. 50–51.** Lahire insists dispositions are not directly observed and must be reconstructed from practices, situations, and biographical history; he questions vacuous dispositional explanation. Consequence: a durable tendency needs evidence beyond one event and an independently supported activation context; invariant labels (Enneagram, childhood wound type) cannot replace behavioral and contextual histories. Multiple dispositions can coexist and conflict; do not assume plurality or any particular latent disposition for every person without a history.

4. **Ole Dreier, `Psychotherapy in Everyday Life`, section “Gathering, Adopting, and Pursuing Personal Stances,” and preceding discussion of trajectories of participation across contexts.** Persons act in trajectories spanning contexts, and the significance of a given context depends on its place among their commitments and other locations. Thus `A3` independent life and reentry must be a *cross-context activity history*, not a change counter within a single meeting scene. An unchanged stance in one context can coexist with learning in another; later integration must not demand uniform transfer.

5. **Klaus Holzkamp, `Grundlegung der Psychologie`, supplied German text introductory statement of `Handlungsfähigkeit`.** Describes practical control over living conditions and distinguishes restrictive immediate coping from generalized collective extension of possibilities. The investigated text is its introductory statement, not a full proof of all volume-specific distinctions. Architecture must represent real constraints and collective action routes, not replace structural domination with an actor's psychological deficiency. It cannot score individual “agency” independently of possible actions or social cost.

6. **Glen H. Elder, `Children of the Great Depression`, foreword (printed xvii).** Historical circumstances, structural location, experience's time and place, adaptive response and long-term consequence require linked longitudinal observation, not assumptions that childhood influence causes adult attributes. Therefore `M25` annual projection, `N11` event recursion, and objective Pyat historical/as-of time require distinct clocks and longitudinal causal proof; interstitial ordinary years do not require dramatic events.

7. **Margaret S. Archer, `Structure, Agency and the Internal Conversation`, publisher's opening synopsis.** The available opening text presents first-person deliberation about concerns and courses of action as a mediator between conditioning and action. It is *less deeply examined* here than Lahire/Dreier; use as a provisional caution: an “inner conversation” cannot be automatically generated from a structural distribution. Avoid a competing mentalist engine that treats generated self-talk as independent evidence of action.

8. **Tithi Bhattacharya et al., `Social Reproduction Theory: Remapping Class, Recentering Oppression`, supplied plain text, introduction and “How Not to Skip Class” section.** The examined passages connect paid work, the unwaged reproduction of labor power, caring activity, schools/health services, gendered labor and material subsistence. It supports making household care/time/resources and public infrastructure actual world constraints and activity relations. It does *not* provide universal formulas determining an individual's mothering, attachment or psychology from gender/class. Any claim about regional real source must still come from Pyat.

**Theoretical tension, not eclectic label-collection:** Leontiev's activity/objects, Sève's mediated act–act social relations, Lahire's conditional dispositions, Dreier's trajectory across contexts, Holzkamp's agent-relative possibility, Elder's life-course time, Archer's deliberated concerns and social-reproduction's paid/unpaid material work are not identical theories. Their overlap is a set of **negative scientific demands**: neither structure, an external event, one act, a type, one statement, nor a test fixture is sufficient to infer a psyche. Productive research must adjudicate a specific rule against each relevant counterexample; the text comparison is not itself a rule.

## 3. Minimal causal account of a historically formed person — an engineering hypothesis, not issued science

The machine's state at a time is not the person. The person is **a situated, bodily, historically mediated and relational process**, represented imperfectly by records:

- **World:** permitted objective material conditions: resources, labor markets, care time, housing, school, law, routes, collective infrastructure, temporal/currentness and limitations. Pyat maintains authority, including unknown/missing and source coverage. These are *possible* constraints—not a biography.
- **Position and dependency:** separate generated household/occupation/age/other-coactor positions, with actor-owner consent and provenance. Child needs caregivers but no default caregiver quality; absence is `UNKNOWN`, not midpoint 50. A source survey's sample person is not this person.
- **Activity episode:** independently constituted coactor circumstances, the actor's available opportunities, access and perceived conditions, actual undertaken action/inaction, time/resource costs, social feedback, and Rule A/T eligibility are different records. An insult the actor never learns of is an objective event, not a remembered humiliation.
- **Durable relation and practice:** repeated situated acts may supply evidence for a response habit, expectation, learned affordance or unresolved contradiction. Evidence must include relevant past exposure, context, intentional action, alternative explanation and owner-issued criterion. *No automatic repetition-to-disposition*, “threat-to-wound” or task success-to-general skill.
- **Contradictory/unchanged state:** the person may care for someone and resent a dependency; cooperate in one context, refuse elsewhere; endure loss with no immediate outward change; learn one local practice with no ideological transformation. `TRUE_NO_CHANGE_EVALUATED`, `HELD_MISSING_RULE`, `UNKNOWN`, `SILENCE`, `REFUSE` and `NOT_OBSERVED` must remain distinct and independently tested.
- **Memory and speech:** C9/C12 chronological event and actor-access records are not autobiographical memory. Only accessed, attended, later retained material can populate actor recollection under a relevant issued rule; a fallible/partial account is allowed. Analyst derived indices and V2 expression read state without canonizing metaphor or fabricating a new event.
- **Others:** B must have an independent material clock, resources, absence, self-relevance and power to say no. A cannot directly write B's motives, reactions or witnessed memories. A coactor can continue while A is elsewhere; A3/A4 must prove offscreen changes that affect a later real reentry without false mutual knowledge.

**Discriminating concrete test scenario (sourcefree proposal):** an economically constrained household cares for a child while one coactor works a wage shift, another bears unpaid care and a third independently changes plans. The child sometimes observes care, sometimes sees only later consequences, and has a prior stable practical stance. Across at least two contexts and two time slices, a tool is shared and then withdrawn. One locally acquired practice changes later work; another nominally identical witnessed event leaves expectations unchanged. A missed event cannot enter memory. An external wage-hour reduction changes the *feasible* care options without automatically changing any person's love, ideology, type or trauma. Run two independent B branches, fork/cold/reenter, compare actual A0–A5 same consumer. Merely selecting one psychologically satisfying narrative is not passing.

## 5. Formal design commitments to demand of future issuers (not self-issued rules)
A legitimate transition claim should identify **(i)** immutable world/actor source, **(ii)** available and actually accessed act/object/other, **(iii)** performed or genuinely unperformed response, **(iv)** material and relational consequences, **(v)** repeat/contrast evidence and alternative hypotheses, **(vi)** issued Rule A/T/CAN/ADP applicability, **(vii)** prospective later same-person consumer, **(viii)** independent adversarial severance. Unknown inputs remain held. Neither test success nor subjective coherence supplies any missing item.

**Scientific decision protocol, for each proposed mechanism:** necessity (what phenomenon requires it?), rival explanation, observed versus inferred variables, source and actor authority, counterexample that should produce NO_CHANGE, social-power/collective context, first causal downstream use, independent tests, limit of generalization. Law must be revisable without retroactively inventing historical events.

**Result:** choose composition with guarded adapters and empirically grounded personhood; stop all arbitrary psychological defaults. The strongest implementation next is not a new psychology vocabulary but an executable *continuing-person* trace that withstands changed independent coactor history, clock/anchor faults, no-change tests and cold reentry.
