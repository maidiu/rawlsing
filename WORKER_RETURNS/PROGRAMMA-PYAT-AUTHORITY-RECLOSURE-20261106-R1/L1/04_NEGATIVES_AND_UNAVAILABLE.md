## Typed distinctions retained

- `FOUND` overlay versus `MISSING_OPTIONAL`, `MISSING_REQUIRED`, `INVALID_SCHEMA` and `UNSUPPORTED_FAMILY` are different loader states. Do not collapse into a generic absence.
- `RESOLVED_DIRECTIONAL_AUTHORITY`, `RESOLVED_NEUTRAL_COVERAGE_AUTHORITY`, context-only, non-relational, administrative, explicit blocked, missing and ambiguous are distinct QRA statuses. A generic `CERTIFIED` string on a QUC row does not establish which original file row issued the exception.
- Structural classification, special/no-answer response flags, survey sampling weights, country routing, and position conditioners are not interchangeable permissions. A missing optional weight or Field Forge authority must not overblock otherwise justified raw unweighted counts.
- A Parquet or Postgres cache SHA is not the accepted manual/source authority SHA. An inferred source string or filename is not a witness to original codebook/routing meaning.
- The two recomputed response CSV hashes are **code-repository asset integrity**, not a claim that original 35-source source-response semantics and a current 18,467-operation plan have been validated.
