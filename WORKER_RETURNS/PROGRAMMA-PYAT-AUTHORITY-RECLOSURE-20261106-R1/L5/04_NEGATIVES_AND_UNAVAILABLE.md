## Negative-state conservation law

These are different types; no default collapse is licensed:

- Physical missing respondent value ≠ semantic nonapplicability or routing-unknown.
- `REJECT` is a frozen adjudication on an exact candidate; it is **not** a proven typed negative relation.
- `BLOCKED` is unresolved (with blocker/permission), not negative, false, absent or eligible.
- `NO_CURRENT_RELATION_OPPORTUNITY__NOT_ABSENCE`, zero-positive coverage, unexamined and open-world are **not** evidence of semantic absence.
- Revoked/withdrawn/superseded/historical status is lifecycle, not semantic falsity.
- Missing/unsigned response semantics is `UNRESOLVED`, not a direction, intensity or zero polarity.
- No weight for an unclaimed RAW operation is not a universal source block; a weighted estimand is conditional on positive exact design/weight purpose and estimator authority.
- No respondent linkage/equivalence/overlap/pooling permission is not a shared negative; it is distinct operation-specific `UNBOUND/HOLD`.
- A source-native higher-order or ranked configuration is not equivalent to independent scalar adjacency/co-travel.
