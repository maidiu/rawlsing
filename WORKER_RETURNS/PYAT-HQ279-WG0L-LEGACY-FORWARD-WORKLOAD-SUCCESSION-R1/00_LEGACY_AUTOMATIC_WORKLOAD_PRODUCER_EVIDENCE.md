## Verdict

**Historical automatic workload construction is real.** V550 built question lists from respondent source/wave/country scopes and a maintained `question_structural_mapping`; it automatically computed per-question marginal distributions, conditionals, outlier and recursive branches, co-signatures/clouds and checkpointed artifacts. V550J additionally assembled a **base + J-only extraction union** from source/wave-filtered, separately governed joint configurations, then generated same-respondent joint patterns. V551/J and V552/J certified and joined *produced evidence*, not original-source inventory.

**Historical workload completeness is not proved.** Its worklist was the intersection of present respondent scope and *mapped* questions, dependent on codebooks, response/use authority and a human-governed J registry. The old recursive and feature search was explicitly bounded. A selected 20-variable smoke, even across CLOSED35, is not a complete original measurement/operation frontier.

**Modern automated construction also exists, and must not be reinvented:** I05 `_compile_frontier` generates a deterministic exhaustive workload for an **already authorized declared** grammar, and source-backed Route B/I03/I05, adaptive search and BatchManifest scheduling execute real code. Their present signatures do not themselves establish original-item- and scientifically eligible operation-complete generation for all 35 waves. Whether a current factory/registry already supplies the missing observed interface is **WG0-C's independent backwards trace**, not a negative conclusion of this lane.
