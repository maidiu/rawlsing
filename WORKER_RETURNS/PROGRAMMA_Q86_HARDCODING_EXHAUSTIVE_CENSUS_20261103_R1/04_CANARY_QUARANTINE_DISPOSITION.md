## Quarantine invariant

The **unit of prohibition** is an unauthorized edge from source-specific Q86 provenance, identity, risk-set, permission, static sample counts or currentness aliases **into a claimed general producer, generic default selected-world route or user-facing sociological model**. The forbidden step is the unqualified promotion, not preservation of the primary example.

1. Mark every Q86 producer as `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_CANARY` in the functional registry, with immutable source/wave/question/risk/permission.
2. Make generic callers name a separately authorized generic relation contract. Never silently rewrite a frozen Q86 hash/ref to satisfy a general interface.
3. Distinguish `CURRENT_FOR_FROZEN_GT2_CANARY_ONLY` from general `CURRENT`, native/source-qualified active world, and user-visible actor evidence.
4. Maintain negative states (`NO_TRANSPORT`, permission refused, unavailable source, no qualified owner, absent stratum), test-only/synthetic labels and replay provenance. Historical no-action does not imply production acceptance.
5. Repair only after a real first failing consumer, eligible alternative pair and current generic-successor comparison establish the exact need. No protected rewrite and no delegated autonomous science.
