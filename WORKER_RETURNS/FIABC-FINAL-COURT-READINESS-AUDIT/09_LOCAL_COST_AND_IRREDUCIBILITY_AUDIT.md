## R35 placement and duplicate-work hazard

The R35 guard is executable but not wired into the driver. A naive operator could therefore:

1. prebuild WVS-A solely for R35;
2. delete it;
3. rebuild it during measured P2.

That would double the most expensive candidate work and contaminate cold-state comparability.

The lawful cost-minimizing interpretation of the frozen constitution is to distinguish **observation** from **admission**: S01 may generate the governed physical realization and telemetry once; R35 and semantic gates decide whether that telemetry can enter the final P2 evidence set. If HQ instead requires R35 PASS strictly before any measured stage begins, it must provide a current realization witness that does not duplicate the measured build. This is an execution-placement decision, not permission to weaken the guard.
