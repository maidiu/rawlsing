## 4. History is monotonic; interpretation is not

A useful update to the old Palimpsest principle:

> **History is monotonic; current interpretation need not be.**

Past events remain immutable. Their causal descendants, memories, accessibility, interpretation and relation to the current self can change.

Example:

```text
E7-14: Dad failed to arrive.             [immutable event]

M7-14: accessible -> inaccessible -> partially accessible

CT-18: reinforced -> context-narrowed -> redirected

DI-08:  "People don't show up because I don't matter."
DI-64:  "Dad was unreliable."
DI-103: "Dad was incapable of some kinds of responsibility."
DI-141: "Hurting someone and knowing how to stop aren't the same thing."
```

Later interpretations do not erase earlier ones from causal ancestry. A person may propositionally reject an old meaning while still procedurally enacting its residue.

## 7. The primitive causal question: what is moving?

Working ontological amendment:

> **Pressure is not the primitive substance. Pressure is a condition of living movement.**

Candidate six-part physics:

### 7.1 Desire / conation
Directional living activity:
- organismic needs;
- approach/avoidance;
- exploration;
- effectance/mastery;
- self-expression;
- sexuality;
- project pursuit;
- attachment bids;
- autonomy;
- curiosity;
- refusal;
- meaning-seeking.

### 7.2 Relation / love
Living intersubjective coupling among distinct centers.

Love is **not** currently modeled as "desire level 2" or a scalar. Working hypothesis:

> Love is a historically developing relational organization in which another subject's existence/flourishing and the relation itself become intrinsic to one's field of desire without the other's alterity being erased.

Connection + alterity are both required. Love can coexist with conflict, jealousy, dependency, fear, control, sacrifice, tenderness, rage, eroticism, care and self-protection.

### 7.3 Recognition / witness
A distinct axis:

> To be witnessed is to experience oneself / one's act / suffering / identity / achievement / claim as **socially real** to another subject.

Recognition is not approval. Hostile witnessing is still recognition. A relation may exist while systematically failing to witness the subject.

Private reality and social/intersubjective reality can diverge.

### 7.4 Power
Power belongs near the primitive layer.

Working definition:

> **Power is differential capacity to set the terms under which desire, relation, recognition, interpretation, legitimacy, dependency, risk and possibility become socially/materially consequential.**

Power includes coercion but is not reducible to coercion. Recognition power matters: some people/institutions have disproportionate capacity to make descriptions of a person socially consequential.

### 7.5 Pressure
Working definition:

> Pressure is what emerges when living desire/conation, relation, recognition and power encounter concrete constraint, incompatibility, misrecognition, scarcity, dependency, obligation, threat, domination or contradictory possibility.

### 7.6 Flow
Working definition:

> Flow is the historically conditioned routing of living activity through available organismic, psychic, relational, symbolic, practical and social channels.

Old pressure-flow therefore moves one ontological layer downstream:
living activity already exists before it becomes pressure.

- being good;
- being worthy;
- being lovable;

- being successful;
- being a man/woman;
- being a winner;

- Can I say no?
- Can I desire something incompatible with you?
- Can I disagree?
- Can I fail?

These repeated answers can generate deep personality organization.

```text

=> "I can be loved IF competent."

desire for recognition
-> competence striving
-> achievement
-> recognition
```
