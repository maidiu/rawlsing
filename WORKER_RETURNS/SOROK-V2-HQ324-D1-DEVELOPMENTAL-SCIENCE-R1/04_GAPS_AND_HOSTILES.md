### What remains genuinely unknown

Ecological transfer validity from laboratory cognitive tasks to lived action; language/culture measurement equivalence; disability/communication-specific capability assessment; immediate affect→durable memory mechanisms; independent caregiver causal decisions under material scarcity; coercion and warmth intersection; peer social-capital differences; same-person competing ideological meanings; cross-sexuality / asexual and across-cultural emergence of attraction; long-horizon memory reinterpretation without confabulation. **C07 and C12 are explicitly indirect-engineering bridges, not scientific scales.** Next science needs ethically obtained longitudinal behavioral/witness evidence, instrument adaptation in actual communities and predeclared negative tests; none is delivered or fabricated in this bounded sourcefree project.
