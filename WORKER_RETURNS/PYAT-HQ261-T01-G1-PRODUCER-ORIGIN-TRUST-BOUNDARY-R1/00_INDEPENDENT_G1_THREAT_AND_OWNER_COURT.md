## Ruling at the actual boundary

**G01 defect AFFIRMED. G01 universal impossibility argument NOT PROVED.** The missing positive is a *service-owner custody edge*, not a new digest or an inevitable cryptographic root. C1 already creates a producer-origin evidence preimage and result lineage while F8 currently accepts any well-typed caller-provided evidence. An entrypoint callable by an **external request client** may own the repository, authority registry, exact allowed query, predicate, binding witness, applicable release and C1 invocation; it can hand freshly produced C1 evidence straight to the already accepted service-applicability and F8-binding functions without permitting a caller to supply or replace that evidence. That does not protect against hostile *arbitrary Python running in the same process*. No evidence reviewed establishes that such same-process execution is part of the chosen EE14 service threat contract. Conversely, **no actually deployed, access-controlled EE14 entrypoint is verified**. Therefore this is an **implementable conditional engineering decision**, never a claim that G1 is already closed.

### Narrowest viable positive owner edge (conditional on external/data-only threat)

**Input:** only a narrowly enumerated service request/query choice and lawful externally selectable scope. A caller may not pass repository, authority_registry, empirical fact, predicate/witness construction callback, C1 result, PossibilitySliceEvidence, model-rule object, applicability authority record, source release evidence, currentness result, or arbitrary Python object.

**Owner execution:** (a) resolve exact pre-approved source-free fixture and registered q1/q2 query/predicate/condition-witness from owner-controlled immutable/frozen inputs and independently adopted authority, (b) execute C1 itself, (c) verify original analytical composition identity and the *canonical evidence preimage -> evidence_ref* relationship; verify selected producer fact, query, predicate, witness, release and modeled target correspondence using owner-held upstream objects rather than trusting presented refs, (d) construct authoritative service applicability request/receipt and matching G2 scope from **owner-held** model/use permission when separately granted, (e) pass the exact C1 result's evidence reference/object **without intervening untrusted serialization or mutation** to evaluate_bound_positioned_subject_possibility, and require parity to C1's direct F8 result, (f) return a selected-service attestation/receipt binding C1 composition identity + evidence preimage identity + G2 scope/request/receipt + F8 result hash + selection/currentness/replay determinant. The attestation is trustworthy by owner execution and verifier control; its digest alone cannot authenticate external origin. C1 FULL ENVELOPE remains reachable, not merely a scalar probability.

**Verifier:** independently controlled owner verification over immutable inputs and a clean committed runtime, including full evidence-preimage recomputation, owner route replay and C1/F8 parity. Accept only the registered owner-side selected receipt; never accept a claimant's self-declared issuer or matching hash as proof. The service entrypoint must prevent bypass: a low-level direct F8 numeric result cannot be promoted to a T1 selected-service receipt. A source eligibility receipt, G2 scope, result hash or G3 lifecycle selection does not substitute for issuer G0b.

**Important contract distinction:** standalone F8 result_hash may correctly remain unchanged for identical geometry; G1 closure is that an altered, caller-origin evidence object can **never enter the owner-selected route or obtain the owner selected-service receipt**. A bound composite receipt must change or refuse if the owned evidence provenance/authority changes; no mandate to alter the accepted F8 numeric result schema merely to simulate security.
