## Key separation

- The historical V2K digests/keys are **identity/integrity only**. They do not establish original survey meaning, source U/E, estimand authority, model use, world admission, actor/person state or scientific significance.
- Current R61 `IssuedWorkloadRecord.source_permission_ref` and `scientific_permission_refs` are issuer-bound fields; a TEST_ONLY record is not a real source/science grant.
- The current R61 negative tests prove refusal of foreign issuer, removed held row, mutated identity, wrong role, replay and severed dependency; they do not prove a native source/science positive.
- No self-issued authority; original U/E/source and science owners remain named external authorities.
