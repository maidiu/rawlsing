## 3. Exact competent EE12 source-issuer gate

A real temporal source issuer must supply, per series, before any of this becomes
source-qualified product behaviour:

1. **Observation year AND publication year, independently attested.** T9B's central
   finding is that these are different axes and conflating them silently destroys the
   as-of/retrospective distinction.
2. **Instrument, edition, cohort, country and geography identifiers** sufficient to
   decide harmonizability — T9B refuses rather than pools when these conflict.
3. **A rupture/cohort-shift declaration** where the source knows of one, so the
   `RUPTURE_BRACKET_REFUSED` path is driven by source evidence and not by a heuristic.
4. **A known-at publication index** that can answer "what was knowable in year Y?"
   without re-deriving it.
5. **Absence and non-renewal semantics** — distinct from observed zero, so the
   inherited `_build_absence_noncomparability_register` has real input.
6. **A licence stating whether a retrospective archival read is permitted at all.**

**Until items 1–6 exist, any real temporal product claim is unsupported.** T9B's
synthetic series deliberately make every one of these explicit so the missing source
shape is visible.
