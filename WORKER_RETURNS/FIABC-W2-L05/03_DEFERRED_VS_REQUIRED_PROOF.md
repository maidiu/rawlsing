## 2. Why the historical V551/V551J stage cannot simply be placed wholesale before the cut

Therefore the question is not "does V551 still run?" It is: **which meanings are required before the learned empirical terminal, and who now owns each meaning?**

## 3. F019 — applicability versus promotability

Historical V551 fused two concerns: inherited applicability and a terminal decision about whether candidate evidence could be promoted to relation evidence.

Current PRE553 separates them.

Before the terminal, I03 must know enough exact source/risk/query authority to execute lawfully, and it returns typed nonpositive facts when authority/scope/risk/measure resolution fails. I05 consumes those exact facts and preserves their typed terminal states.

After the terminal, I04 asks a different question: may this exact empirical fact become structural evidence for this exact target use? Its `StructuralAdmissionRule` binds the admission authority, target-use permission, structural identities, interpretation semantics, time/geography, measure/weight authority, uncertainty and negative authority. The admission decision is explicitly separate from empirical query execution.

**Holding:** F019 is a split succession. Empirical applicability prerequisites are pre-terminal; V551's unique structural promotability/adjudication is intentionally post-terminal under I04. Requiring each A/B/C candidate to perform structural promotion would add downstream semantic work to the physical-carrier experiment.

## 4. F020 — canonical signed projection

**Holding:** the accepted directionality/scale/coordinate authority is candidate-neutral capital and must remain preserved/reachable. The act of applying the structural signed projection is intentionally downstream of the comparison terminal. Old V551 atom materialization is not candidate work.

This holding does **not** assert that every historical V551 direction/intensity row already has a current accepted I04 rule instance. That is claim-scoped authority closure, not cut placement.

## 8. F024 — certification/review partition

Two different partitions must not be collapsed.

### Pre-terminal empirical partition

I03/I05 must preserve empirical terminal truth:

This is required for A/B/C semantic parity. Row absence is not a legal terminal state.

- `ADMISSION_DEFERRED`,

The V553 ingress contract explicitly forbids raw empirical facts and discovery results as direct structural input. Only an admitted `StructuralEmpiricalAdmission` can positively project into structural use.

**Holding:** F024 is a split succession. Typed empirical negative/coverage law is pre-terminal; structural certification/admission is intentionally post-terminal. The historical V551J certified/review/context/blocked sidecar shape is not itself an A/B/C workload obligation.
