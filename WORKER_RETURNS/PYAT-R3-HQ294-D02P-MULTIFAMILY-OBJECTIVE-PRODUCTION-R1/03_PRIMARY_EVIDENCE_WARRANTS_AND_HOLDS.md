## Time and geography scientific method

GSS 2021 PHC is a **census-period** rural household field. NCA Q4 2021 is a **quarter-end national telecom administrative count**. The observation times *overlap in historical year but are not identical*. National NCA market conditions cannot simply be transported to a rural district/household as signal coverage or individual-use probability. The two fields can be lawfully *contextually co-presented* in a selected Ghana-2021 historical world only with a signed EE13 compatible-scope decision preserving unequal time/place/units and publication as-of. Same named target population could also be lawful in other pairs; distinct source custody, not a label inequality, determines heterogeneous origin.
