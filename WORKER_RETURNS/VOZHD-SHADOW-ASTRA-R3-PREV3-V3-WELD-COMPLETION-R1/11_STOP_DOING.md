# D12 — Stop-doing / reject list

**Status:** SHADOW RECOMMENDATION.

The project should explicitly stop or reject the following unless new evidence reopens them.

1. **Do not freeze desire, love, recognition, power, pressure and flow as six independent durable psyche stores.**
   - Keep the concepts.
   - Start with event-local/relational derivation and existing owners.
   - Promote only what wins a comparative court.

2. **Do not use one hidden utility function.**
   Rawlsing requires simultaneous incompatible commitments, needs and relations.

3. **Do not generate personality from demographic/structural labels.**
   Structure supplies world/possibility, not wound/type/belief.

4. **Do not make Enneagram causal.**
   No type-at-birth, type score driving behavior or type-specific event generator.

5. **Do not store “core wound = X” as a primitive fact.**
   Organizational centrality must emerge from event/relation history.

6. **Do not treat PFF as a second psyche.**
   CAN-008 already owns shared person×event coupling at its adopted grain.

7. **Do not let lived prose self-authorize.**
   Hashes and provenance establish custody, not causal truth.

8. **Do not rehydrate unavailable memory from omniscient event history.**
   Objective history, generated memory and accessibility stay separate.

9. **Do not make every old event a trigger.**
   Recurrence requires resemblance/difference, present relation and current state.

10. **Do not make category membership behavior.**
    "wife", "man", "artist", "good daughter", "revolutionary" require mediated actor history.

11. **Do not equate relation persistence with love success or relation ending with love failure.**

12. **Do not force psychodynamic revelation.**
    Repair may occur without origin discovery; autobiographical hypotheses may be wrong.

13. **Do not make all important people full recursive universes by default.**
    Promote by causal consequence.

14. **Do not flatten important people to trait vectors when their independent action matters.**

15. **Do not full-life-prompt every turn.**
    Use exact owner state, mandatory ancestry, selective candidate retrieval.

16. **Do not use vector similarity as causal evidence.**

17. **Do not resurrect historical V558 or nine-strata physical topology as mandatory merely because downstream predecessors used it.**

18. **Do not let a thin adapter promise permanent semantic sufficiency.**
    It reduces coupling; new lawful distinctions may require consumer revision.

19. **Do not perform the physical PRE-V3→V3 weld before positive terminal service, K9/K10 and explicit competent wake/weld authority.**

20. **Do not wait for all PRE-V3 work to finish before any downstream lab work.**
    Local synthetic-fixture courts are lawful under their own explicit authority.

21. **Do not claim all35 or estate readiness from one vertical, source-free tests or repeated inherited unit totals.**

22. **Do not confuse modeled parent pairing with observed dyads.**

23. **Do not continue refusal-only Pyat work when the blocker is absence of a positive producer path.**

24. **Do not spend more renderer budget on the already-easy bounded one-turn court.**
    Wait for a harder compositional failure.

25. **Do not select SQLite/Postgres/graph stores by ideology.**
    Start simple; change when measured concurrency/traversal demands require it.

26. **Do not defend rich architecture because of sunk ideational investment.**
    K courts may simplify it.

27. **Do not defend minimal architecture if richer mechanisms win fair held-out comparisons.**

28. **Do not treat this shadow packet—or Astra's eventual advisory—as automatic adoption.**
