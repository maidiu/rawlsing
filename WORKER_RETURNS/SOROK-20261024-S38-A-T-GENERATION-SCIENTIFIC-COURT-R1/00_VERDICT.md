## Mechanism T: independently sourced episodic adaptation → possible procedural practice

**T0 — source-bound experienced episodes, not outcome labels.** Following actual attempts, append to the competent event/memory owner a `CASE` pointing to `actor_id, scene/task/goal, actor-perceived cues, current resources/constraints, method or action attempted, independent world-performer result, feedback accessible to actor, material/social costs, conflicting project context, clock/branch/source hash`. A script's predicted success, B's secret intention and A's mere exposure are not enacted feedback. If A has no feedback access, an observer may know the outcome while A cannot learn from it.

**T1 — qualify comparable experiences.** Store succeeded, failed, aborted, contrary and nonperformed episodes distinctly, with opportunity denominators when available. Retrieve only cases with explicit *task-structural* similarity: goal kind, accessible cues, relevant tool operation/constraints, role of other people and possible costs; keep context mismatches. Similar prose/embedding distance is a retrieval aid, not proof of psychological or procedural transfer. Repetition of one assigned action in one unchanged context is not automatically learned competence.

**T2 — construct conditional method proposals.** Extract from actual enacted, independently outcome-validated episodes a **guarded recipe** `IF accessible-context predicates C AND tools/operations available THEN attempt operation sequence P for goal G`. The recipe may be a context-sensitive retrieved exemplar, a constrained composed sequence, or an explicit procedural specialization. Require that all primitives are within the actor's learned/qualified capability and all goal/material preconditions match. Keep failed counterexamples and source lineage. This is the constructive mechanism for *future available action methods*, not a claim about a real person's cortex. If no usable procedure can be extracted, keep memory traces and `NO_PRACTICE_PROPOSAL`.

**T3 — independently discriminate old ability, tool substitution and real transfer.** To propose a *newly formed durable practice* rather than present execution, require an independently warranted **before-state** of what operation A could/could not perform under comparable tools and constraints (not an unknown assumed inability), independently enacted procedure with feedback, and at least one **genuinely comparable yet nonidentical later task** demonstrating autonomous application of the candidate method where assistance/role/material explanations are controlled. The number of opportunities is not a universal numerical threshold. These are **qualitative causal checks**, not “two repetitions => learned.” If prerequisites absent, `NO_CHANGE` for CAN011, while actual case memories remain stored. The later demonstration must not simply be an action scripted by the learning rule or language model; the action is evaluated against independent world/physical constraints. Explicitly mark simulator-generated observations as `INTERNAL_MODEL_EVIDENCE`, never as external human validation.

**T4 — life of a practice.** With a separately accepted exact T and grant, `CREATE V3MS_PRACTICE` records a narrowly defined `method_id, goal_scope, context_guard, actor-accessible cues, qualified enacted source refs, beforestate/comparator refs, independent performance/transfer witness, known failures/limits, effective_time`. Later `REVISE/WEAKEN/DORMANT` require their own rule/evidence; no universal forgetting, repetition-strength scalar or pro forma status threshold. A later CAN008 A may recruit this scoped practice as a *candidate method*; its appearance doesn't ensure choice, success, motivation, conscious self-description or cross-domain generalization. Practical knowledge may remain as event-linked procedural memory even if no CAN011 practice rule has been issued.

**T5 — alternatives and ceilings.** `EPISODIC_RECALL` (ADP16) is not `ACQUIRED_PRACTICE` (CAN011); `CONTEXTUAL_ACTIVATION` of already available skill is not `FORMATION`; `FAMILIAR_ROUTINE` is not a new transferable capacity; `BEHAVIORAL_REPEAT` is not improved skill; `PRIVATE_SELF_DESCRIPTION` is not independent enactment. Even after a verified simulated procedural acquisition, increased willingness, habit/automaticity, general trust, social solidarity, ideological identification and durable changed motivation each require different content and separate sources/mechanisms. T cannot create all these by renaming one trained tool operation.

## Where the exact indispensable choices remain

**Not a scientific premise:** “Someone must supply a previously observed A response” is NOT indispensable to generating an internally coherent artificial person's response. “The exact synthetic person must be studied longitudinally as a real human before we can specify any constructive learning mechanism” is NOT indispensable to constructing a disclosed computational psychology. Neither the absence of a human-signed A rule nor an empty registry logically establishes what would be scientifically impossible; they are real deployment/authority gates.

**Indispensable MODELING PREMISES:** (1) actor-specific source-grounded priorities/contextual comparators or, where these genuinely leave multiple actions open, an **explicit finite choice policy**; (2) a declared theory of how enacted experience is encoded, retrieved, generalized and verified for the intended *kind* of learning; (3) exact operational criteria separating context-specific procedural availability from a newly acquired durable practice, including a valid before-state and transfer test. Without (1) there is no unique A action in many cases. Without (2)–(3), a T `CREATE` encodes the *person we wanted to write* rather than demonstrating formation. ## Five nontrivial adversarial conclusions

1. **Same objectively feasible B share; A never sees it:** A cannot form an event-specific appraisal of B's private act without receivability; tool may objectively be usable while specific B-directed reception is UNKNOWN. Other independently sourced A projects can still motivate action, but cannot cite B's unseen kindness.
2. **Same E1, contradictory projects:** finish dangerous repair vs avoid debt; both remain. Current actor-source context can make one choice supported without erasing the other; no scalar cancellation or guaranteed “balanced liberal.”
3. **Failed attempt:** A's choice to try does not make a successful inspection. The attempt/failure is an episode, and future method proposals can reflect accessible feedback, but not automatically create practice.
4. **Repeated success in identical assignment:** source supports enacted familiarity/episodic retrieval and material history, NOT acquired independent transferable method without independently checked beforestate and changed context.
5. **Changed context:** guarded acquired procedure can become inapplicable; actor may instead ask, improvise, refuse, or have NO_ACTION even if the simulated skill remains. A later identical sentence such as “I can't do it” need not identify the underlying cause.
