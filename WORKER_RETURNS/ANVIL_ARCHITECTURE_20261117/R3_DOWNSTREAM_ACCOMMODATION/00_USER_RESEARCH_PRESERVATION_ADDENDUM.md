## 2A. Mandatory research preservation — a reusable knowledge asset, not merely a verdict

**User requirement:** Preserve the *research performed to establish the design*, including useful findings whose value is independent of which C11 architecture is ultimately selected. An accepted design is only one output; the investigation's tested distinctions, source-grounded observations, competing explanations, discarded options and continuing questions are themselves assets for PYAT, Field Forge, SEAM, SOROK, future DDM work and other research. **This obligation does not authorize new investigations beyond the bounded R3 task.**

**Maintain the record as the investigation proceeds.** Do not reconstruct a falsely clean rationale after selecting a winner. Capture each *consequential distinct* discovery, correction, decision-changing uncertainty or rejection when it arises, including specialist disagreements and any changed mind. Consolidate duplicate notes; do not dump irrelevant command chatter, speculative prose, private reasoning traces or ceremonial proof matrices. At return, preserve a readable synthesis and a machine-searchable register together.

For each research finding that materially informs the decision *or has independent foreseeable reuse*, retain:

- **Stable `R3-RES-*` identity, question and subject tags** (for example C11 physical storage; survey source semantics; DDM historical function; F1–F4; F6/F7; FF; N9/world; SEAM/SOROK; proof/authorization; economics). Note **potential applications outside ANVIL**, explicitly *possible* rather than owner-approved adoption.
- **Verifiable sources:** exact repository path and SHA, source document/issue and frozen cut, lines or function where available, original data/model assumptions, and a pointer to any applicable author or independent test. Distinguish the first-hand inspected material from inherited reports and from the researcher's interpretation. Existing source IDs and frozen records should be linked, not recopied indiscriminately.
- **Method and epistemic grade:** `INSPECTED_CODE`, `EXECUTED_MEASUREMENT`, `SOURCE_DOCUMENTED`, `HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`, `ANALYTICAL_DEDUCTION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `REFUTED_AS_PROPOSED`, `OWNER_HELD`, or `UNKNOWN`, with exact test/environment and proof ceiling where relevant. Do not report the earlier sparse sourcefree canary as a dense/native measurement or a 20/118 structural guard as scientific truth.
- **Substance:** what was found; why it follows; the strongest competing interpretation; the positive/negative evidence or counterexample; the uncertainty, sampling/permission limitations and conditions under which the finding would cease to hold.
- **Evolution and outcome:** original proposal or historical function, objections and disagreements, any revision/retraction, current `RETAIN / MODIFY / REJECT_AS_PROPOSED / CONDITIONAL / OPEN / OWNER_HOLD`, and **why**. Losing a design contest must not erase a useful capability, analytic method or caution.
- **Reusability:** whether this is a general algorithmic/epistemic insight, an exact code-cut observation, a source-/survey-specific lesson, a proposed downstream interface or an unresolved scientific hypothesis; what would need revalidation before another project relies on it.

**Preserve negative knowledge on equal terms with positives.** For example, retain why prepared-session reuse does not remove fresh-open `S` or full-population `Q` and output `O`; why a source-design stratum cannot become an individual identity; why pairwise marginals cannot imply a high-order joint; why a journal-derived Q2 would undermine independent source certification; why historical DDM gains/entropy formulas cannot simply be ported; and why structurally important findings may remain valid even if a particular proposed architecture loses. These are examples, not predetermined conclusions to copy without evidence.

**Separate the two products:**

1. A **durable research archive** records the tested research and qualified hypotheses, including ideas not adopted for ANVIL. It is *not* an authority-granting product, a new PROGRAMMA status bank, a secret alternative architecture, or an automatically promoted scientific fact.
2. The **architecture decision** cites the research IDs actually relied upon and gives the concrete C11 accommodation route. The paired CURRENT_PROGRESS may receive the scoped outcome and archive pointers through authorized receiving, **not** the entire research corpus as duplicated progress prose.

**Custody and privacy:** Commit only material permitted in the worker's isolated return root; cite restricted/original survey evidence by authorized identity and proof receipt rather than copying raw respondent data, credentials, private paths with secrets, or unapproved proprietary content. Preserve previous frozen returns; version new findings additively. If a source cannot be lawfully retained, record its metadata, scope and exact access/rights hold without manufacturing the missing content.
