# R3 RESEARCH SYNTHESIS — what the accommodation investigation found

**Status:** author synthesis of the R3 pass; not adopted science, actor or
source authority. Machine-searchable entries are in `R3_RESEARCH_LEDGER.jsonl`.

## 1. The central discovery

The R2/C inquiry established a conditional **storage/proof/query direction**
but did not separately demonstrate the **access and derivation contract** that
downstream capabilities need. Keeping the 13 canonical families is necessary
and sound; it is not sufficient by itself.

The R3 finding is therefore architectural but bounded:

> **The canonical body stays; the access architecture becomes explicit.**
> A respondent-major event view, a question-major selector/denominator view,
> and content-addressed derived-product manifests connect exact C11 facts to
> F1–F4/F6/F7/FF01/FF02/N9/N10W/SEAM/SOROK owners without a new canonical body,
> database or science engine.

This is not a new general architecture campaign. It is a minimal delta to the
accepted conditional direction.

## 2. What was actually learned

### 2.1 Storage is not access

`R3-RES-001`. The 13-family body preserves exact objects, refs, states,
occurrences and weights. But an "exact body plus generic reader" does not by
itself give an economical, semantically safe route for:

- same-person k≥3 joints;
- forward/reverse conditionals with different risk bases;
- minority/recursive/ranked patterns;
- question-major selection without a full respondent scan;
- derived F6/F7/FF/world products with owner/authority boundaries.

The access contract is now explicit. It can be implemented incrementally:
typed in-memory selection first; a persisted compact form only if measured.

### 2.2 Prepared sessions are a first step, not a destination

`R3-RES-002`. Prepared reuse removes repeated session build `S` within one
process. It does not remove fresh-open `S`, native counting `Q`, or evidence
output `O`. The 1.69–1.70× four-operation author report is not a 100-operation
measurement. This correction matters because a false "prepared-first solves
repeated queries" claim would hide the fresh-process and output problems.

### 2.3 The statistical core is intact

`R3-RES-003`/`004`. True same-person k≥3 cannot be reconstructed from pairwise
marginals (even/odd parity witness). F2 forward and reverse need distinct
eligible bases. F3 minority/recursive and occurrence/repeat semantics are
native obligations, not storage details. The existing native evaluator remains
the semantic authority; R3 does not replace it.

### 2.4 Source observability is a first-class boundary

`R3-RES-005`. A sample-design stratum is not a released respondent identity.
`OBSERVED_RESPONDENT`, `SAMPLE_DESIGN_ONLY`, `COLLECTED_BUT_NOT_RELEASED`,
`EXTERNAL_GROUP_CONTEXT`, `PROXY_OBSERVED`, `MEASUREMENT_NONCOMPARABLE` and
`RIGHTS_HELD` must remain distinct. This is both a source-rights boundary and a
scientific one. The architecture preserves the information needed to ask the
question; it does not fabricate the individual field.

### 2.5 F6/F7 are genuine owner products, not C11 fields

`R3-RES-006`/`007`. The I07/I04 structural guard is not an F6 numeric interval;
effective-N is F018, not F6. F6 partial identification and uncovered mass need
a separately owned numeric method and product. F7 cross-wave comparison needs a
qualified comparability index; same-name variables are not a measured
trajectory, and cross-sections are not an individual longitudinal self.

This is not an architectural evasion: C11 preserves separate releases,
universes, time, instruments, design and source relations. The missing piece is
the external numeric/comparability issuance, not the ability to locate the
input information.

### 2.6 FF01 and FF02 are different objects

`R3-RES-008`. FF01 is a conditional association/profile with producer
permission and typed state. FF02 requires independent institutional/legal/
economic evidence, population/time/rule. A survey frequency or group majority
does not become coercive power. The existing `ExactConditionalSupportProfile`
is a useful FF01 foundation, not an FF02 issuer.

### 2.7 Other positions and encounters are separate

`R3-RES-009`. Different distributions of other positions may change potential
Other candidates. They do not establish that actual people meet, marry,
dominate or raise one another. Encounter/access needs a separately qualified
relational source. Availability ≠ access ≠ comprehension ≠ identification ≠
action. This is the strongest SEAM boundary in the investigation.

### 2.8 N9 world is not SOROK person

`R3-RES-010`. `run_progressive_chain` and the possibility provider produce
world/possibility material; `HistoricalWorld` is a separate actor reader. A
survey vector is not a person; an objective world is not a psyche. The actual
SOROK first reader remains owner-held.

### 2.9 J-to-FF is a manifest path, not automatic science

`R3-RES-011`. `j_component_certification_validator`,
`JFamilyRoleAssignment`/`JComponentCertificationPremise`, and the M1/M2
composition path provide a scoped J-to-FF manifest route. Review-only is not
certified ALLOW; the consumer must use the true source/scope. The route is
conceptually accommodated; the selected J→FF first reader remains held.

### 2.10 Signed/neutral is partly accommodated

`R3-RES-012`. Source value/role/authority and structural admission are present;
signed/nonneutral meaning is not a standalone canonical field. It must be
carried by the source-qualified structural/FF02 owner. Neutral0 is not missing;
nonneutral is not causal power.

### 2.11 Construction/query/evidence economics

`R3-RES-013`–`016`, `R3-RES-020`/`021`. The measured/cited witness is sourcefree
and cut-specific: writer 3033.723 s; retained 310.16 MB; staging 5.26 GB;
temporary I02 4.39 GB; session index 1.30 GB; selected query 562.362 s. The
working distinction is:

- `H` cold admission; `S` session index; `Q` native counting; `O` evidence;
- one admission: `H+N(S+Q+O)`; prepared same-process: `H+S+N(Q+O)`;
- fresh opens: `N(H+S+Q+O)`; durable index: `H+I+N(Q+O)`.

Evidence output at 100k is 30–50 MB/fact before provenance and 3–5 GB/100 facts,
so exact set-ref sharing under identical identity is necessary; replay
re-executes; cached facts are never authoritative.

A durable index is the only route that removes fresh-open `S`, but the cited
SQLite index is 1.30 GB (≈4.19× the 310.16 MB estate) and invalidation/rebuild
is unproven. It is held, not rejected.

The Batch prepared path is `PROPOSED_NOT_WIRED`: exact-type guards reject the
prepared subclass, and relaxing the type alone would still open fresh sessions.
The minimal owner-approved adapter must thread one prepared context through
Batch units.

### 2.12 Research status is not scientific authority

`R3-RES-018`/`019`/`022`. The 20/118 structural guard is not science; sourcefree
native-evaluator execution is not native source proof; SEAM/26 and ANVIL/15 are
documentary reverse demands, not adopted science or actor rights.

## 3. Alternatives and reversals

### Alternative A — prepared-first only

Retained as the first query step. Insufficient as whole accommodation: no
fresh-open `S` removal, no explicit question-major selector/derived-product
contract, no answer to Q/O amplification.

### Alternative B — demand-driven typed access + derived products

Selected as the access architecture. It is the smallest extension that covers
the known capability envelopes without a new canonical body. It can start with
bounded typed views and exact native evaluators.

### Alternative C — compact durable/secondary access

Genuinely competitive and held. It can remove fresh-open `S`, but its retained
cost, compact exact encoding and invalidation/rebuild burden are unmeasured. It
must pass `#1006` H/S/Q/O and a retained-size/binding gate.

### Reversals recorded

- The initial R2 emphasis on storage/SC-ESTATE was insufficient; R3 reverses
  the emphasis to access/derivation while retaining the body.
- The initial "prepared session improves repeated queries" shorthand is
  narrowed to repeated `S` only.
- The initial generic K1 crosswalk fate is rejected as insufficient for
  K1-CAP-08/17/23/27; specific routes replace it.
- The durable index is not selected now; it remains a live conditional option.

## 6. Most consequential negative results

- Preserving bytes does not prove usable access.
- Prepared reuse does not remove fresh `S`, `Q` or `O`.
- Pairwise marginals do not determine a k≥3 joint.
- A design stratum is not an individual identity.
- I07/I04 is not F6 numeric partial identification.
- FF01 association is not FF02 power.
- Position marginals are not encounter/contact.
- N9 world is not SOROK person.
- A journal-derived Q2 is not independent source certification.
- A structural 20/118 PASS is not scientific truth.
- A sourcefree native-evaluator PASS is not native source authority.

These negatives are as important as the selected route because they prevent a
future reader from mistaking a documentary accommodation for an implemented or
authorized capability.


## 7. Post-C correction (G1–G5)

The first R3 synthesis overclaimed two things. They are corrected here rather
than silently rewritten:

1. **FF01 was not a general conditional-distribution route.** The existing
   `ExactConditionalSupportProfile` is a fixed Q86B→Q86A, two-participant
   **count** profile with no ratios. `FF01-2` is the implemented narrow object;
   `FF01-k` and reverse `P(D|Y,U,W)` are a proposed versioned derived-product
   contract, with owner-held method/eligibility/ratio/weighted estimand.
   [R3-RES-023]
2. **C11 does not itself carry every source-design field.** Inspected schema:
   `QuestionVariableBinding` has native ref/version, not option/form/ballot
   text; `EmpiricalUniverse` has respondent namespace/frame/design-regime
   labels, not household/roster or PSU/strata/variance; `WeightMeasureFact` has
   design/estimand authority refs, not variance/PSU/strata/replicate weights;
   `EmpiricalFact` has an uncertainty method/state, not a numeric interval.
   These are external source/codebook/design artifacts by exact identity or
   typed absent/RIGHTS_HELD. [R3-RES-024/026]

The typed respondent/question access views remain a `CONDITIONAL_EXTENSION`.
`R3_FIELD_AND_ADAPTER_CONTRACT.md` supplies exact per-family keys, ambiguity/
refusal cardinalities and the existing `DirectEstateI03Session.indices_for` →
`_ReleaseIndex` → `execute_empirical_query` adapter path; it does not claim the
adapter or Q reduction is implemented. [R3-RES-025]

The decision/capability/falsifier documents now cite R3-RES IDs directly;
entries 023–027 are corrections/extensions, not replacements of the original
22. [R3-RES-027]

### Retained negative knowledge

A count profile is not a conditional distribution. A design authority reference
is not variance data. A weight authority reference is not a PSU/strata table. A
source question reference is not the original-language option text. These are
now explicit so a future reader cannot mistake a reference for the referenced
material.
