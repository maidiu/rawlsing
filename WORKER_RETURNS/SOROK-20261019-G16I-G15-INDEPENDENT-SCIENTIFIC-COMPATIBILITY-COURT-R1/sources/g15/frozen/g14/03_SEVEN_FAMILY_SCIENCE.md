F1's type-specific parent substitution and F3's psyche severance test the proposed
scientific interpretation. A graph validator need not itself be a developmental
owner, and action could in principle be driven by explicit commitments rather than
psyche fields. What fails is the inference from these fixtures to independently
motivated person development. The null identity and arbitrary-consequence passes
are concrete evidence-validation defects even at the fixture boundary.

route enumerator supplies that value. Tuples are accepted/serialized Python inputs;

semantic noninterference over unrestricted text. More regexes alone are not proof;
