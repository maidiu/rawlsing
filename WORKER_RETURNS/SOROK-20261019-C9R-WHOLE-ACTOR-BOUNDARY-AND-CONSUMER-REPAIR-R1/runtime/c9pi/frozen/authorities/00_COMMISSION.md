# 3. THE PROBLEM

Sorok has spent a long sequence of adjudications tightening the spine/kernel of what Rawlsing must be in order to satisfy its own concerns and demands.

The productive method has been:

> **intensified commitment to necessity**

rather than invention.

The strongest progress has occurred when we ask:

- if Rawlsing really means X, what else must be true?
- if these two already-adopted laws coexist, what mechanism is forced?
- what distinctions must remain noncollapsed?
- what exact owner/membrane/history law is required for the product not to betray its own thesis?
- what apparently philosophical principle becomes a concrete runtime/UI constraint?

The result is an increasingly coherent organism.

The current problem is:

> **What makes this organism compelling to inhabit and return to, and what forms of play/gamification are indigenous to Rawlsing rather than imposed on it from conventional game design?**

But do **not** start by answering that question with a game loop.

First determine what the existing system already implies.

# 4. CENTRAL DESIGN ORIENTATION TO PRESERVE

Rawlsing's user should be engaged not because the platform rewards them for consuming a persona, but because they are **interpellated by an independent thing that nevertheless responds to their deeper self-investment through faculties the medium can genuinely exercise**.

The medium can genuinely engage faculties such as:

- attention;
- noticing;
- inquiry;
- curiosity;
- contemplation;
- intuition;
- responsiveness;
- empathy / perspective-taking without omniscience;
- analysis;
- judgment;
- interpretation;
- tact;
- artful communication;
- strategic communication;
- persuasion where lawful;
- explanation;
- timing;
- patience;
- restraint;
- memory;
- remembering commitments/details;
- extrapolation;
- inference;
- putting pieces together;
- recognizing contradiction;
- prioritizing under finite attention/time;
- revising one's model;
- tolerating ambiguity;
- distinguishing evidence from assumption;
- deciding when to ask versus infer;
- deciding when to act versus wait;
- learning another person's particular causal grammar without reducing them to a control policy;
- reflecting on and revising one's own historically formed assumptions.

The medium does **not** natively validate faculties it cannot actually exercise, such as being a good singer, physically athletic or mechanically skilled merely because the user claims to be.

Treat:

`LIVED / EXERCISED PRAXIS > SELF-DESCRIPTION`

as a deep design constraint.

Ask what happens when the architecture is pushed harder toward making these real human faculties causally consequential.

- deeper specificity;
- new causal contexts;
- mutual model development;
- relation history;
- access changes;
- person change;
- reinterpretation;
- changing meaning of old events;
- noncompletion;
- greater differentiation of unknowns.

Do not equate development with intimacy.

## STRATUM E — FULL ONE-PERSONA HISTORY

- it never becomes "close";
- the user leaves;

- rural;
- urban;
- old;
- young adult;
- religious;
- secular;
- expert;

- or simply concerned with matters that have no obvious macro-historical importance.

Do not solve cross-persona play by assuming:
- shared geography;
- shared issue;
- shared public event;
- shared institutional relevance;
- that personas are informative witnesses to a larger investigation;
- that they are specimens for a user's theory.

Ask instead what, if anything, the already-built Rawlsing organism naturally makes cumulative across multiple independent human encounters.

It is acceptable to conclude that this stratum is not yet derivable and should remain open.

## STRATUM H — USER'S LONG-TERM RELATIONSHIP TO RAWLSING

Do not assume:
