## 3. Commission scale: MAXIMAL COHERENT COMPLETION, not maximal unsafe scope

**Default commission = a coherent terminal-capability completion campaign or substantial bounded tranche**, not the smallest demonstrable effect. A small job is reasonable for (i) a demonstrated critical-path defect, (ii) a truly independent issuer/reviewer, (iii) a safely isolated experiment required before expensive scientific choice, or (iv) limited real qualified capacity. Do not count a review or administrative packet as replacement for substantive output. An old already-running narrow job may finish and be banked; it does not set the next whole-flight ambition.
