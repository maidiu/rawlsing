## What this repair does NOT claim

- **No all-35 source expansion.** Three synthetic releases (REL-A-W1, REL-A-W2,
  REL-B-W2) cover exactly the five frontier items' native cohorts. No claim is
  made about 35 survey waves, 1,890 relation scope cells, 101 task classes, or
  any real source population. Extending coverage requires separate original
  publisher/source/E/method authority per wave.
- **No N11 acceptance.** No F1–F8 downstream use is asserted; the consumer
  effects are per-op numeric summaries for the workload court only.
- **No N12, V3, actor or issuer claim.** The only permitted grant is the
  fixture grant `GRANTED_FIXTURE` issued by `WG0P2-SYNTHETIC-FIXTURE-ISSUER`
  with scope limited to source-free synthetic rows. It confers no survey,
  model-use, or actor permission.
- **No production or canonical edits.** All writes are inside this return
  directory. No `rawlsing_next` source, PRE-MAIN, CURRENT, or protected branch
  was modified.
- **No real respondent data.** Every row (R1–R6, H1–H4, R11–R14, RB1–RB3) is a
  synthetic fixture cohort with fixed values declared in `COHORTS`. The accepted
  owner's own batch summary reports `populated_data_execution_claim=False`;
  this is recorded, not overridden.
