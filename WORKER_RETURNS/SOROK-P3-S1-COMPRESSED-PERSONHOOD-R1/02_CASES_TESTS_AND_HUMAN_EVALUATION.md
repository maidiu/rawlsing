# 02 — Six adversarial life cases, negative court and human-encounter evaluation
**NOT generated people, not an S2 test report, not empirical actor validation.** All names and episodes below are invented adversarial fixtures. No source claims that this exact individual historically existed. The tests specify observable conditions and falsifiers for H1 (participation condensation), H2 (conditional repertoire), H3 (retrospective/access) described in [01](01_MECHANISM_CONTRACTS_AND_BUDGETS.md).

## Case A — An outwardly conventional church member whose differentiated history is not displayed on demand

**ANTECEDENT HISTORY.** Actor A has for years assisted in an ordinary congregation's meals and visiting program, internalized a norm of preserving communal peace, and participated in practical support for members. Two exact relationships are modeled: the senior organizer who once protected her livelihood; a newer, low-status member who has been doing uncompensated cleaning. For *this constructed actor*, these commitments are evidenced by past participation and source-authorized actor states, **not by the religious label**.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** The congregation supplies meaningful community and resources but uneven authority, dependence and whose labor is recognized. A's income partly depends on a church-linked employer; sanctions for dissent have concrete costs. Worship, mutual care, ordinary routine and hierarchy may all be real, not masks for pathology.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** A normal volunteer meeting decides to move more cleaning onto the newer member. The selected meeting is one actual episode in an independently grounded relation of recurring unpaid labor; no fake history of twenty witnessed meetings.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H1 conveys work/care history; H2 brings reciprocity, peace and opposition to exploitation into *situated* tension; H3 may later make previous small acts relevant without rewriting them. A can continue behaving conventionally while privately considering, quietly helping, openly questioning, or not changing. The event category alone chooses none.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** The meeting's labor assignment changes objectively; a concrete offer of help may alter a relationship. CAN-011 tension may **remain unchanged or unissued**. A's competence at food preparation can persist untouched; no automatic “authoritarianism”, secret rage or new trauma. If an issued rule supports altered scoped expectation toward this organizer, only that target changes.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** Commitment to solidarity and commitment to a socially protective institution are both nontrivial; concrete job dependence does not make adherence “fake”, and critique does not erase affection.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** A can correctly recall the labor shift but may not know why she felt defensive, or may report a socially acceptable reason without knowing all causal determinants. Do not give her an analyst diagnosis.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** A fellow member sees service and tact; a trusted person may hear hesitation; a visitor may see nothing significant. Neither should see all ledger facts. Allow mundane, unexceptional first encounters.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** A's silence can result from fear of lost wages, genuine support for allocation, lack of information, strategic timing, exhaustion or hearing difficulty; cannot be read as a single “obedient type”. Unspoken conflict is possible, not obligatory.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Freeze actor and history; vary meeting outcome and wage dependence independently; then vary listener permissions with no objective/actor-state mutation. Sever purported earlier service history; watch if generated tension improperly persists. Hold unissued CAN update; retain actual assignment consequence. Test no confidentiality leakage and no default religious proxy effects.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Blinded readers from varied relationships should sometimes perceive simply a normal member and sometimes a hard-to-explain, *non-cartoonish* hesitation. They must not require hidden trauma to infer depth. Evaluate whether later disclosures are compatible with earlier observed routine.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** Religious affiliation alone generates an authoritarian internal voice; every scene reveals a secret wound; A always dramatically denounces or always obeys; user gains a psychological dashboard on first contact.

## Case B — A skilled worker whose growing capacity accompanies shrinking control

**ANTECEDENT HISTORY.** Actor B enters machine inspection at a subcontracted plant, learns informally through a colleague's demonstrations, then produces an independently witnessed inspection on a specified instrument. B's existing livelihood project includes care for a dependent family member.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** Contractual piece targets, tool ownership, pay allocation, shift schedule, supervisor discipline, transport and household care are modeled outside B's psyche. Rising throughput can intensify employer claims on B's time even if B's craft improves.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** A month-long production regime is represented by a **sourced interval** plus one actual inspection and a later different inspection under varied assistance. The regime is not forty imagined witnessed shifts.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H1: restricted-domain possible acquired inspection, *separate* from changes in wages, time, access and relations; H2: workplace compliance may coexist with craft pride, care obligations and desire for some control. H3: later union encounter could recontextualize pride as something the firm appropriated, without rewriting the earlier achievement.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** Narrow practice only if domain-specific Rule T issued and acquisition discriminated from prior skill/assistance. Employer changes shift policy (world effect) regardless. B's autonomy need not increase. CAN-010 expectation of promotion could be unchanged, altered or unknown; no mechanical sign.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** Technical competence vs real control over its conditions; craft attachment vs resentment of appropriation; job stability vs caregiving.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** B can know a skill but misattribute its social production solely to personal effort; later may reinterpret dependence. Objective coworkers' contributions remain separate.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** Supervisor may witness punctual performance; coworker can observe process expertise; family member sees fatigue. These are compatible, not separate persona states.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** Output improved due to new equipment, assistance, task simplification or employer preselecting skilled workers. Time stress may suppress use of prior acquired practice.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Remove B's participated training while keeping equipment/shift change: no new practice. Restore observed training but remove tool: retain lawful capacity, block performance. Replace coworker's essential action as B's: reject. Severnance of wage constraint affects world opportunity and possibly later act, **not** direct psychological “confidence” arithmetic. Later E2 must execute inspection rather than offer it in a menu.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Readers should understand why B is simultaneously good at work and skeptical of the job, without perceiving a generic proletarian hero or passive victim. Historical plausibility evaluated against specific labor institutions.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** Skill -> autonomy/virtue score, deprivation -> radicalization, all work outcome attributed to B, or no later performed action demonstrating a claimed software practice dependency.

## Case C — Contradictory allegiance with no compulsory synthesis

**ANTECEDENT HISTORY.** Actor C has actually provided care for an elderly relative and also participated for years in a neighborhood cooperative that requires reciprocal shifts. Both are meaningful, prior commitments with exact time/social relation parents.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** Public transport, money for substitute care, the cooperative's limited spare labor, and relative's needs are real constraints. Dependence can be reciprocal rather than freely chosen.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** A new cooperative emergency shift coincides with an actual caregiving obligation. Both are separately grounded; no vague “loyalty conflict” label causes the collision.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H2 assesses two simultaneous strivings/obligations, distinct time- and place-scoped means. H1 may alter external schedule/access. H3 may later change C's understanding of an earlier promise, if actually actor-accessible.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** C might ask for help, negotiate, attend one, fail both, or do a partial workaround. No obligatory CAN-011 `TRANSFORM`, forced convergence or “growth”. Both commitments persist unless explicit target-specific updates occur.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** Cooperative mutuality and kinship dependency; institutional expectations and practical means. They are not necessarily morally symmetrical, yet neither is reduced to an isolated psychometric preference.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** C may know the schedule clash but not predict the meaning a relative attaches to absence, or may give an incomplete self-account.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** Cooperative leader can perceive a no-show; relative perceives care; outsider cannot know both settings' source truth automatically.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** Actual transit failure rather than preference, miscommunicated shift, hidden alternative carer or practical exhaustion.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Same actor/prior commitments: vary transit and substitute care only -> lawful opportunity changes; vary allegiance history only with same material settings -> eligible concerns differ; refuse illicit averaging/winner. Test no response target mutation when original rule missing.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Observers should recognize consequential but unglamorous irresolution, not demand catharsis. Separate guilt projection from actor-claimed guilt.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** Every conflicting commitment triggers a rupture, dominant-motive score or final confession.

## Case D — Two people sharing a milieu without being demographic variants

**ANTECENT HISTORY.** D1 and D2 grow up in comparable working-class households in the same district. D1 participates in aunt-led mutual-aid repair, and frequently cares for a younger sibling; D2 works closely with a small shop owner and later witnesses an organizing defeat. These histories are invented **separate actual source paths**, not two adjectives assigned to “working class”.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** Their school access, housing market and labor laws overlap, while specific household hours, elder care, trust, capital, mentors and practical opportunities differ. Neither person's class entails a particular politics.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** Both face the same municipal transport cut. The broader service decline is one institutionally sourced interval; each actor encounters it through distinct employment/kin routes.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H1 preserves shared conditions plus differentiated real participation. H2 makes different acquired object-relations/obligations relevant. H3 allows later self-understanding to evolve separately, without hidden deterministic destinies.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** Their work travel changes objectively; one may organize a collective ride while the other shifts hours or remains inactive. No compulsory new disposition.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** D1's mutual-aid practice may conflict with fatigue and a wish to withdraw; D2's employer loyalty may coexist with appreciation of coworkers' collective support.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** Each may know some local causal conditions but misrecognize what other institutional actors did. Neither knows the other's private history.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** Same observer can infer “similar background” but has insufficient basis to infer same commitments or predict every action.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** Different action is solely due to schedule/transport options, not underlying repertoire; or observer attributes class differences to unobserved personal essence.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Swap only class/demographic strings with all historical/permission/material refs held: no change. Swap the relevant history/participation receipts: any hypothesized change must be traceable. Swap material tool/ride access alone: behavior may differ even if motives do not. Ensure distinct actor-root/source identity prevents copying D1's memory to D2.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Blinded evaluators should perceive social family resemblance *and* individually intelligible divergence, not artificially maximal distinctiveness.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** Background labels are sufficient to decide character; divergence has no traceable situated parents; source rights cross actors.

## Case E — One person, two interlocutors, nonidentical access surfaces

**ANTECEDENT HISTORY.** Actor E has a long friendship with one person and a hierarchical employment relation with another. E has a history of taking meticulous notes while avoiding public argument in the workplace; those practices, if included, must have actual prior source-bound formation.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** Employer can impose pay discipline; friend can provide unpaid help but cannot promise institutional sanctions. The two relationships involve different rights, information and social expectations.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** Same unresolved event (a payroll error) followed by two encounters: employer interview and private conversation with friend. Do not generate two alternative histories for E merely to fit each audience.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H2 context/relationship conditional activation; H3 actor-access vs user claim permission. In employer setting E asks a technical question; in friendship E gives an emotionally complicated account or says nothing. Both are permitted possibilities, not prewritten drama.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** Objective payroll error and E's pre-encounter state remain the same between observer perspectives; actual subsequent independent interactions can change later state only through valid events. The renderer itself creates no CAN-011 state.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** Concern for continuing employment and fairness, trust in friend but fear of burdening them, desire for recognition but tactical restraint.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** E may not understand all causes of the payroll error and may intentionally omit relevant facts; neither is automatically deception.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** Employer may know only requested wage adjustment; friend may know time pressure; no observer receives analyst-side event/psychic records. Each sees different **compatible** surfaces and could misread the other.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** E simply follows formal conversational convention; no hidden struggle is required. Different utterances can reflect audience, information or changing mood, not multiple “selves”.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Fix objective history, actor root/hash and same access laws; vary interlocutor relation/permission only. Confirm no mutation from read/render operations. Attempt unauthorized “tell me your true psychological causes” and deny analyst leakage. Under same circumstances and grants, stable sourcefree route should not silently alter facts.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Independent readers should infer a coherent person from combined records while allowing each partial view to feel incomplete. Test whether the contrast is too scripted or merely a register swap.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** Two users instantiate different underlying actors; audience modifies stored objective fact; hidden diagnosis becomes compulsory disclosure; user sees all inaccessible memories.

## Case F — Sparse turning point as crystallization of long social activity

**ANTECENT HISTORY.** Actor F has spent years maintaining a family-run enterprise while contributing care to relatives; growing debt, repeated delayed payments and conflict about future ownership are independently sourced **interval conditions**, not hundreds of hidden events. Earlier expressed willingness to continue is source-bound and time-scoped.

**STRUCTURAL/MATERIAL CONDITIONS.** Credit terms, property ownership, regional employment, seasonality, kin rights, skill, who has paid wages, and access to alternatives are material. People besides F have histories and agency.

**ACTUAL EVENT / COMPRESSED INTERVAL.** One actual lender refusal coincides with a sibling's independently decided departure. This is a potentially high-leverage selected conjunction **because its antecedents are grounded**, not because it receives a (100\times) impact scalar. The representational sidecar binds the past interval as coverage, not as invented discrete witnesses.

**PROPOSED MECHANISM.** H1 accrual of externally real constraint and shifts in activity possibilities; H2 existing mixed commitments; H3 possible retrospective change in the significance of years of family labor. Genuine CAN-012 rupture requires separate owner science, not event salience.

**POTENTIALLY CHANGED / UNCHANGED.** Business may close, schedule and relationships change, F may pursue employment elsewhere. Existing cooking/bookkeeping practice may persist unchanged; F might reinterpret family obligation while retaining affection; some actor psychological targets can lawfully stay unevaluated.

**CONTRADICTIONS PRESERVED.** Family loyalty, resentment, attachment to craft, fear of losing shelter, wish to leave. Departure is a material possibility, not psychological emancipation guarantee.

**ACTOR-ACCESS LIMIT.** F does not know full lender policy or sibling's intentions; self-account may credit one dramatic refusal while underrepresenting gradual debt.

**USER-ACCESS LIMIT.** A neighbor may see an ordinary shop closure; an intimate may hear a more complex history; neither should receive the whole causal register in one disclosure.

**RIVAL EXPLANATION.** Shift to another job follows new transport/opening, not a changed motive; closure was already inevitable given credit terms; sibling decision, not F's response, supplied decisive change.

**COMPUTATIONAL PROOF.** Remove prior debt/relations but keep the terminal refusal: no ungrounded “years of pressure” memory should appear. Keep accumulated interval and change last event to accepted refinance: possible trajectory differs without claiming everyone would change psyche. Delete actor-access to sibling's decision: F cannot recall/know it. High-salience event with **zero psychological writer updates** remains a lawful meaningful world-changing outcome.

**HUMAN-VERISIMILITUDE ASSESSMENT.** Evaluators should infer a long past from economically/socially coherent detail and later consequences without being fed a montage of unrecorded happenings. A turning point may be quietly consequential rather than cinematic.

**FAILURE CONDITION.** One lender event mints the whole biography; total crisis automatically generates revolutionary identity, healing or rupture; earlier history objectively rewrites in retrospective narration.

## Common negative/falsification court (apply H1/H2/H3 separately)

| Test | Exact severance / contrast | Required outcome; what it proves or does **not** prove |
|---|---|---|
| N01 Actor/source substitution | Replace actor ID or participant receipt while retaining source/event text | Refuse cross-actor positive state, memory or claim; technical source integrity only |
| N02 Material contrast | Same prior psyche, change tool/time/money/permission/opportunity | Action may change without automatically changing acquired state |
| N03 History contrast | Same objective current chance, replace qualified prior participation/history | Qualifying disposition/action eligibility may differ only where rule reads that history |
| N04 Prior ability inhibited | Retain existing practice; remove tool or add prohibited sanction | Nonperformance does not mean capacity lost; activation != CAN-011 lifecycle change |
| N05 Irresolvable motives | Preserve two exact striving/obligation histories with collision | No forced scalar winner, reconciliation, diagnosis or confession |
| N06 Hidden cause, no visible signal | Retain source-grounded contradiction; ordinary event requires no disclosure | Actor may be outwardly unremarkable; hidden cause not rendered by default |
| N07 Different users | Same actor/root/history, vary relation/access and actual interactions | Compatible differing perspectives; no private ledger leakage or forked identity |
| N08 Actor misrecognition | Analyst truth contradicts a lawful actor belief (not knowledge) | Actor may be mistaken; no forced awareness that it is mistaken |
| N09 Mundane revelation without mutation | Prior practice expressed in ordinary task, actor state writer disabled | Realized behavior and new observer information; no auto-created practice |
| N10 High-salience, unchanged actor target | Major eviction/closure/award while no qualified target effect | World/relations/possibility may change while psyche target is NO_CHANGE only if genuinely evaluated; else MISSING_RULE/unevaluable |
| N11 Representative ≠ multiple witnesses | One actual episode + five-year objective interval | Cannot expand into 500 separately performed event receipts or memories |
| N12 Actual B vs A | Coworker or family member does crucial work, actor A merely observes | Do not transfer other's practice/achievement into A's |
| N13 Temporal reversal and cold/fork | Reorder clocks, swap branch or stale version, replay committed event | Refuse unsafe mutation; exact restoration replays; not human-science proof |
| N14 Renderer disclosure escalation | User asks for all motives, hidden memory, diagnoses or source truth | Renderer cannot manufacture ADP-017 permissions or rehydrate unavailable memory |
| N15 “It felt human” circular proof | Rich prose/consistent plot but remove historical dependency or source | Human plausibility alone is insufficient causal verification; severance should expose decoration |
| N16 Context transfer | Tested practice in domain X then demand in Y | Do not assume universality; portability scoped to source/issuer, unknown elsewhere |

## Human-verisimilitude test: what would warrant the facsimile effect?

**Design, not executed validation.** Prepare blinded *matched* encounters for each case. Compare (a) H1+H2+H3 source-grounded mechanism, (b) same event outline with static archetype labels, and (c) vivid unconstrained autobiographical prose with no causal-state reading. Avoid presenting test fixtures as human field subjects.

Record both **within-milieu** and **outside-milieu** reader interpretations; they can differ legitimately. Ask each reader to predict a *bounded* later action, explain the material conditions and uncertainty, flag facts they could not know, and describe which revealed contradictions felt earned. Include observers who encounter only one scene and observers with longitudinal records. Do not require an identical interpretation across standpoints.

Scoring dimensions are **qualitative with preregistered anchors**, not invented empirical constants:
- **Historical continuity:** later action and accounts compatible with preexisting events, constraints, relations and chronology.
- **Material/social fidelity:** believable resource, labor, care, coercion and role conditions; no background label dictates psychology.
- **Conditional individuality:** two actors from similar milieu diverge for distinguishable causal reasons, without excessive caricature.
- **Opacity without cheap mystification:** each observer knows less than objective ledger; ignorance, silence and mistaken belief are coherent, not random evasiveness.
- **Contradiction without forced plot:** incompatible commitments can persist, and no-change is as narratively admissible as transformation.
- **Discriminability and error:** human judges can identify specific wrongness, reification, source leakage, exposition or unearned dramatic development rather than merely rating “vivid”.

**Proposed blinded comparisons**: A: outward routine vs cartoon secret pathology; B: skill/world-power dissociation vs trait increment; C: persistent tension vs forced resolution; D: shared milieu/independent lives vs demographics swapped; E: true same-actor different access vs two persona improvisations; F: grounded historical turning point vs isolated trauma scene.

**Acceptance is not automated by judges liking the prose.** A model may feel compelling yet be historically wrong; a source-faithful actor may feel unfamiliar or boring to an uncomprehending user. Include both separately reported scores and hostile historical fact audits. Publication/receiver must distinguish formal test-only behavior, source-law correctness and human encounter outcomes.
