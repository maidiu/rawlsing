## Six functions and three compact mechanism families

**Six event functions remain analytically useful, not six CAN-011 kinds:** FORMATION, CONSOLIDATION, CONTEXTUAL_ACTIVATION_OR_INHIBITION, CONTRADICTION_EXPOSURE, REVELATION_TO_OBSERVER, and RETROSPECTIVE_RECONTEXTUALIZATION. Refine the proposal by recognizing (a) **material/relational modification** and (b) **unresolved/unevaluable** as cross-cutting outcomes, not compulsory psychological transitions. A single event may perform several functions. None warrants a global salience multiplier.

**Candidate H1 — Condensed participation and social reproduction:** a representative **episode/interval evidence envelope** links actual participation, recurring objective conditions, other labor, institutional authority and the narrow possible changes in practice/expectation/relations. Never multiply one witnessed act into fictional witnessed acts.

**Candidate H2 — Conditional plural repertoire and antagonistic commitments:** actor-specific historically acquired and possibly contradictory mediators, practices, expectations and strivings produce different legally available/expressible actions under different opportunities, sanctions, relations and interlocutors. Contextual activation is a **read-time effect**, not automatic CAN-011 `REACTIVATE` state mutation.

**Candidate H3 — Retrospective recontextualization with asymmetric access:** later actor-experienced developments change *current relevance/interpretation* of earlier history under an authorized internal-event or epistemic rule; exact objective history remains unedited; actor knowing/claiming/user rendering remain separate. Narration-only change remains a **proposal**.
