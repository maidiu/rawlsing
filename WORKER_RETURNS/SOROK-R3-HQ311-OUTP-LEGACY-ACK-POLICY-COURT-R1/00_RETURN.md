### Two admissible semantic definitions to distinguish

- **A1 TRANSPORT_RECEIPT_ACK:** means a bound client received/accepted a transport delivery identity, not that permitted content was rendered, displayed, visible or human-perceived. Requires admitted-event/outbox binding, addressed and authorized viewer, positive client delivery/sent receipt, stable delivery ID and revocation handling. Does **not intrinsically** require a separate `projection.offer()` action. **Cannot** create user epistemic state. The client-bound transport owner would need to explicitly issue this ACK meaning and define restoration/idempotence.
- **A2 VIEWER_OPPORTUNITY_ACK:** means acknowledgment of a viewer-specific authorized projection/delivery opportunity. Requires A1 conditions **plus** a previously lawful recorded offer/projection for the same event/viewer/delivery identity and a live permission (unless separately designed to acknowledge an already-consumed historical opportunity). Still **not** human perception. OUT4's strict viewer-scoped implementation resembles A2.

A synthetic ACK after aggregate `deliver` but without viewer `offer` is **allowed only under an expressly issued A1 transport grade**; under A2 it is refused. A1 must never be relabeled a VIEWER_PERCEIVED or knowledge receipt; if identity/viewer/source binding is absent it is not even A1. The frozen S01–S28 specification alone does not choose between these two distinct ACK meanings.

**Owner decision needed:** competent application/client transport and viewer-access/projection owner (coordinated with product/ADP019 boundary owner) must declare the exact ACK type and event/viewer/delivery/projection preconditions, initial/restore/revocation/retry transitions, and whether legacy global scalar ACK is deprecated, isolated or separately named `TEST_ONLY_AGGREGATE_ACK`.
