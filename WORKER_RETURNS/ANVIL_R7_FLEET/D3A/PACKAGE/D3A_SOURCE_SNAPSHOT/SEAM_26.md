## 0. Central correction to the SEAM requirement

SEAM's actual task is **not** to obtain `P(attitudes | demographic labels)`, hand SOROK an interesting social stereotype, or let the LLM fabricate psychological “contradictions.” Nor is the task only to preserve an individual respondent's possibly idiosyncratic attitude cloud.

**The target is a historical system of internally differentiated and mutually related social possibilities.** Empirical sources can reveal the *whole same-person joint response configurations* that circulate in differently situated populations, including ordinary and minority configurations. Other source-qualified evidence may describe **material class/resource/control and institutional-power relations** among positions and the historically changing conditions in which their occupants encounter one another. SOROK must then generate actual embodied actors who can identify with, be alienated from, oppose, reinterpret, participate in or be affected by different **strata of that larger world** through development. A particular child's identity is not a preloaded copy of a family response vector: it emerges through organism, activity, real relation, opportunities, encounters, limits, desire, love, loss and independently acquired meanings.

**Crucial revised phrase:** “Socially organized antagonism” must be limited to **differently distributed configurations across positions whose actual relations can be empirically associated with unequal power, resources or institutional authority**, plus a separately demonstrated mechanism when making a stronger antagonistic/causal claim. Neither a between-group difference nor a statistical resource association alone proves actual conflict, exploitation, hegemony or cause.

**Two directions matter:** (i) what historically social conditions may produce a rich adult response-configuration; and (ii) whether a child developing among differently situated actors can eventually acquire *equally varied and situated organization*, without reproducing or being forced into a source respondent's observed endpoint. An adult's survey response is itself produced by **how the actor understood the question in their context**, not a privileged transparent read of private psychology.

## 4. The Lebanese/GSS thought probes — illustrate *questions*, not infer people

- Lebanon three AB Wave II example: jointly reported strong religious practice, doctrinal distinctiveness, minority worship rights, differently organized Islamic personal-status positions; a woman respondent favored equal inheritance and Islamic inheritance legislation. **The data do not establish her hermeneutic.** Nor does party affinity plus location prove sect. AB II's sample stratification information and absent usable released individual sect field are **different observability layers**; audit AB I/II/V/VIII rather than assign sect.
- GSS 2010 quartet: born-again man with frequent prayer/attendance and “not religious at all.” **Evangelical anti-“religion” rhetoric is one hypothesis**, not a decoded motive; question interpretation is part of generated adult life. Source split ballot, routing and answer/unknown must survive.
- Latinobarómetro Brazilian Muslim respondent cannot be assumed Lebanese-descended when source provides religion and broad race/color but not ancestry. External group-level Joshua Project estimates are neither individual respondent joins nor a substitute for original identity. Evaluate external source bias/mission, population overlap and metadata independently.
- Observed 2010 adult responses are **adult-at-observation-time**. A child in 1995 cannot inherit the adult's 2010 questionnaire vector. Infer possible adult prehistories with explicit model grade and no fabricated longitudinal evidence.

**Statistical rule:** two or four respondent rows are **demonstrations of possibility only**. Repeated near-identical profiles require duplication/interviewer/weights checking, not treating two qids as independent sample confirmation. Real prevalence, stratified gaps and class/sect/sex associations need representative full respondent inference with actual eligible denominators and design. Claims of “contradiction” or political meaning require source meaning/context and may be normatively misleading: coexistent positions can distinguish institutional jurisdictions or authority registers without a cognitive inconsistency.

## 6. A finite falsification court, not new implementation goals

1. **Same marginals, different true 3-way joint:** fixed univariate/pairwise frequencies but rearranged k-way same-person patterns must produce different FF consumer configuration possibilities where demanded. Exact F4 native values, not a new story generator.
2. **Same person configuration, different real social surroundings:** change qualified surrounding group/position distribution while freezing sampled person's joint: eligible coactor possibilities may change **only if world/exposure owner actually consumes** surrounding field; person's existing psyche must not update because of metadata.
3. **Same opinions, different independently established power:** shift source-qualified resource/right/institution control; objective world affordances change at actual first reader when selected; no survey-to-causal upgrade.
4. **Same social field, different actor access/activity:** child exposed or not, independently acts or not. SOROK developmental outcome may differ under a separately issued rule; no automatic copying of parental response vector.
5. **Question meaning adversary:** self-reported “not religious” + born again/prayer/attendance retained; no latent motive auto-coded. Flip only codebook interpretation/source edition → exact changed/REFUSED, not silent cached categorization.
6. **Sect/ancestry blind spot:** AB respondent lacks usable sect, Latinobarómetro respondent lacks Lebanese ancestry; external group estimate cannot fabricate matched respondent joint. Distinguish available source-frame stratification from released individual field.
7. **Cross-time anti-clairvoyance:** adult wave at 2010, parent child 1995, contemporary 2026 world; same actor longitudinal facts cannot be invented from cross-sections; source/as-of mismatch refuses.
8. **Absolute Horizon noncollapse:** child's acquired identification can diverge from public membership and from C5 motif; changing group label alone does not change Horizon, but qualified activity/history may lawfully alter practical commitments. Human-validated rule remains OPEN.
9. **Noncausal descriptive survival:** valid FF01 co-travel remains useable when FF02 independent source held. Conversely no pressure, hegemony or sanction claim from majority support.
10. **Economic sufficiency:** 1/10/100 distinct authorized wide queries + controlled triple/joint/minority/branch first reader, cold proof and provenance under existing measured P0 resources. Do not require all 35 surveys or exhaustive full joint Cartesian powerset to test.

**Proof grades:** empirical/statistical P0/S1 product tests, then separately SOROK sourcefree first-reader; no single test establishes all levels of this court. #1011 retains its *frozen narrower* two-event pilot: these are **future reverse requirements**, not retroactive G1–G5 gates for that commission.
