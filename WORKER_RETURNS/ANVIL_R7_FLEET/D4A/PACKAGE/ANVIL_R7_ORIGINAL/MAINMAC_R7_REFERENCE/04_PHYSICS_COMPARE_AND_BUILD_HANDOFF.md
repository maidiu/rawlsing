# 04 — Material comparison, discovered reversers and construction inheritance

## The compact, adversarially complete physical experiment

Seven workload families: `W-S` sparse 100k with ≥600 possible source/question locations and genuinely sparse events; `W-D` dense actual respondent width, multiplicity and routed topology (not inferred from W-S); `W-R` routed/rostered/repeat/multiselect and semantic first-read correctness; `W-O` 1/10/100 distinct, both high-overlap and disjoint query predicates; `W-J` real 3+-way same-respondent joint, F2 reverse and F3 minority/recursive; `W-U` repeated source-qualified first-world and Field Forge reuse only if operative and lawful at elected scope; `W-X` cold authenticated reopen, altered CURRENT/revoke/negative restore, crash/atomic publication.

`contracts/policy.json:mandatory_floor_by_scope` is the mandatory numeric floor for a **claim of complete comparison** at the declared scope. For `C11_QUERY_DESIGN` it contains **22** case×metric combinations covering build, first query, 10/100 distinct queries, dense width, peak whole-process RSS, retention/scratch, cold open and first selected consumer; for `FF_FIRST_EFFECT_DESIGN` the cross-world reuse cell is additionally required (23). These are **a floor, not an assertion that 22 tests fully characterize every real input**. B and C must inspect likely choice reversers; one high-width lawful original survey shaped workload and a truly independent third-family fixture are desirable when original rights permit; sourcefree synthetic fixtures cannot be called original source execution. Report source scope, instrument/country/wave, density, branching/topology, query overlap, output body/witness size, CPU, wall, process-tree peak RSS, retained bytes and peak scratch, I/O and source reads, cold state, code provenance and positive/negative/restore. Do not assume all 100 operations are cheap when 100 are genuinely unrelated.

**Non-numeric court:** seven mandatory veto identities in policy, **separate candidate A and B receipts each** (14 total). Qualified source semantics and routing; true within-person k-way joint and denominators; tamper/fault/denied/restore; rights/currentness; future world/actor nonpreclusion; comparable source and runner inputs; global choice/dependency consistency. One candidate cannot borrow the other's qualifying source/rights witness. A scientific veto is not outweighed by speed; a held competency is not a fake failure or a green pass. The supplied local fixture documents are **synthetic schema tests only**.

## What counts as a measured comparison

Every predeclared material cell has immutable ID, owner decision ID, named source/first reader, A and B exact input SHA, same metric and physical unit, matched runner/host, version/commit, source grade and a recorded challenge in both directions. Candidate output has `status=MEASURED`, honest nonnegative interval, and a raw JSON receipt under a rooted evidence directory. The receipt's **actual bytes**, digest and *parsed contents* must agree on candidate, cell, metric, fixture, runner, grade, code SHA, interval, sample count, execution methodology and positive/negative/restore details. `MODELED` and `UNRUN_HELD` are permissible but cannot support complete dominance. A renamed file, missing receipt, correct hash of unrelated text, different fixture, fake metric, **wrong units** or a B-labeled measurement bearing A’s frozen design HEAD is RED. A measured RSS interval exceeding **6 GiB** is RED even if a self-described veto witness claims PASS.

Receipt authentication here is **byte and internal-consistency verification only**. An independent real C reviewer must verify the runner and qualified source provenance, actual batch/full-width first reader and causal negative; a Python JSON writer cannot manufacture original respondent/actor/science rights.

## How A/B winners are decided

Default `A_ADVANTAGE_AT_COMPLETE_SCOPE` or `B_ADVANTAGE_AT_COMPLETE_SCOPE` means across the *entire frozen declared material comparison*, all comparable nonoverlapping intervals favor that candidate in at least one dimension and never favor its rival in any; both have all nonnumeric vetoes at the stated scope. Mixed wins produce `TRADEOFF_OWNER_DECISION`, overlap `PARETO_OR_UNRESOLVED`, unmeasured matched rival `MISSING_MATCHED_DECISIVE_EVIDENCE`, unproved hard veto or exclusion a separately qualified hold. **This is numerical comparison, not automatic owner architecture adoption.** Claims of dominance are always qualified by the chosen source/workload and first-consumer scope.

**Useful real tradeoffs rather than automatic paralysis:** owner may freeze a *pre-result* `priority_rule` in plan: full priority ordering of the material cells, bounded acceptable regression ratio on **each** cell, first-consumer objective, source authority and B's explicit frame challenge. Where both candidates win some cells and no unresolved overlap remains, comparator may return `A_PRIORITY_WIN_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_C` / `B_PRIORITY_WIN_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_C` only if every sacrificed metric stays within its predeclared bound. C must separately adjudicate why that owner priority remains legitimate for the actual reader and whether B has a still material alternative. This is **not** a silent incumbent advantage, post-hoc metric weighting or an unconditional winner from a tie.

## The B-discovered discriminator law

A previously omitted materially choice-reversing dimension discovered by B after H0 is **admissible, even if that embarrasses the original H0 frame**. Controller must preserve original immutable frozen plan and either:

1. Before its first comparative results, freeze a child plan with original parent hash + B's actual frozen challenge source + at least one uniquely identified *new* cell bearing that discriminator ID; do the same exact matched A and B lawful measurement and negative; or
2. Record `EXACT_HOLD_PENDING_C` and identify the smallest qualified test/rights decision that could settle it.

A control phrase like `PROVED_IMMATERIAL_WITH_COURT`, a `decision_reverser=false` toggle, a long prose explanation or an unauthenticated C placeholder **cannot** erase an admitted live objection. When a new dimension is genuinely immaterial, C may document that independently, but no positive *author* comparison is permitted until the operative frozen scope is actually resolved. A post-result amendment to make an unfavorable measurement disappear is forbidden; a genuine later discovery requires explicit new source/result epoch, not silent moving goalposts. The code requires the actual parent plan for version >1 and tests the added-cell linkage. All side cases must preserve their source-first-consumer consequences and the old evidence.

## Material decision closure and whole architecture

Global `SPINE.json` consists of one selected candidate, named current first reader, complete real physical decision IDs, all frozen cell IDs and vetoes, global typed source→consumer edges, named future routes, source/negative/restore, qualifications and linked handoff IDs. Per-decision reuse is allowed only where a genuine dependency is explained and the source decision is exact; physically unrelated choices cannot inherit one benchmark winner by association. Engineering acceptance requires actual produced/consumed I03/F1–F4 sourcefree or source-native evidence, not merely this architecture selection. 

Only **one chosen** current-operation high-risk builder card and **one distinct future-owner-held** card need actual cold-read C challenge. Each card must identify owner, qualified source/cut, real versioned `file::symbol` producer/consumer, input/output ABI, exact first positive and negative/restore, test CLI, code snapshot, permission/currentness and means to disprove usability. Builder cannot be “READY_FOR_IMPLEMENTER” when its sole producer is “future engineer” or its consumer cannot be located at the qualified source cut. `SPECIFICATION_CONSTRUCTION_READY` denotes an independently inspected construction specification; `EXECUTED_FIRST_READER_PROOF` needs actual execution receipt. Future necessary scientific/actor functionality can remain `OWNER_HELD` with typed extension, without a false all-product blocker.

If P1.5 cannot make the selection, the result still has to advance the real product: an exact eliminated option, a qualified first-consumer interface, a reproducible sourcefree test, a newly bound native source distinction or a sharply reduced choice-reversing blocker. H1 independently compares this against the frozen H0 starting evidence; author narrative alone cannot make it a verified advance.

**Handoff-decision consistency:** every selected material PHYSICAL decision names `handoff_id`; the handoff repeats `decision_id` and `first_consumer_node_id`. A READY physical choice cannot rely on an OWNER_HELD card, and no derived comparison can refer to a fictional or self-referential parent decision. This check enforces referential consistency, not real constructor competence; C must inspect actual code and rights.
