## III. What Stage 4/Field Forge must actually be able to compute

### A. From one source-population to social positions

For source wave W and eligible target population U, choose source-authorized position descriptors D and response conjunction Y=(Y1,...,Yk), k≥3. Example: Lebanese country/subpopulation in AB II (if native source and rights qualified), or source-specific GSS 2010 sampling.

1. Compute actual joint `P(Y=y,D=d | U,W)` and valid support/routing/missingness.
2. Compute `P(Y=y | D=d,U,W)` with **own** denominator and proper weight/design, not one question's full sample.
3. Compute reverse `P(D=d | Y=y,U,W)` with its own **different** denominator, including its sample/target estimand.
4. Preserve minority responses and **alternative joint patterns** plus full coverage/tail status; availability of exhaustive true Cartesian full-domain vs actually observed cells must be honest.
5. Compare conditional distributions across **independently valid D groups** with uncertainty and benchmark; do not equate survey party preference with known sect/class without source qualification.
6. Retain exact source/question/role/release and qualified comparison; never reuse a result from incomparable wave or survey through a same-name alias.

### B. Pattern structure beyond a flat k-way table

Historical pre-FORGE V547–V550J provides relevant intended **primary/secondary selectors, minority/conform-deviate and selected recursive branch/proposition, condition/target association and ordered k-way outside-variable link**. This is already in [ANVIL/05](05_PRE_FORGE_FUNCTION_INHERITANCE.md). A satisfactory FF01 should support **discovering** which cross-domain bundles are socially characteristic, unusual, polarized or rare in the selected population *without* enumerating every possible combinatorial questionnaire subset and without claiming the old flawed MI/gain/branch metrics are good science.

Candidate analytic operations, **not new authorized issuers**: bounded adaptive contrasting groups, nested conditional branch, exact support-aware co-travel and outside-variable association, sourcegrade cross-group comparison, rejected-condition provenance and explicit unresolved mass. Need scientifically selected search criteria, multiple-comparisons/overfit safeguards, sample design, lower bound support, robust anti-duplicate/interviewer diagnostics. Ordinary/majority patterns may matter more than the “weirdest”; don't force outliers into the person generator.

### C. From own cloud to the OTHER field

If FF delivers only a coherent adult Y given D, it **does not** by itself tell SOROK which people the adult meets, which ideologies are salient to their children or what institutions can constrain them. A field-conditioned product should allow **separate selection** of relevant other positions D2 and their honest observed configurations, with separately sourced/graded **relationship/contact/role distribution** if available. Where encounter source is absent, SOROK may propose a **model-graded** specific social relation but must not claim empirical marriage/contact odds.

**This is likely the newest explicit SEAM reverse-consumer clarification.** ANVIL's PP26 external field and mediation products invite it, but the current [ANVIL/06](06_DOWNSTREAM_REVERSE_REQUIREMENTS.md) has not pinned a specific *distribution of other actors + position-to-position encounter* FF/N9/N10W recipient. Determine whether existing N9 world graph and SOROK exposure/actor owner already suffice, or document a real missing derived handoff. Do not build “RelationalCloudEngine” by default.

### D. Materials and meaning from separate authorities

An observed survey category may measure household income or party preference. Those can be *descriptively* related to other measured answers. But actual property, workplace control, legal sanction, sectarian court authority, censorship/media access and imperial regional force require independent provenance, source/population/time crosswalk and appropriate scientific rule.

Keep:
- `OBSERVED_ASSOCIATION` (what respondent data shows);
- `EXPLICIT_EXPERIMENTAL_MODEL` (fictionally bounded choice/transition/possible-world assumption);
- `STRUCTURAL_CAUSAL_AUTHORIZED` (stronger objectively evidenced, rule-issued relation).

**Do not make FF01 fail merely because FF02 is held.** Likewise, don't promote respondent co-occurrence into class conflict simply because it suits a Marxist interpretation. Power must be linked to measured/independently supported control/resources/rights/institutions and causal mechanism when claimed.

### E. Global and identity multiplicity

A 1998 Lebanese child may be affected by several concentric/overlapping, nonexclusive social affiliations: neighborhood, Lebanese state, sect/religion, gender, class, Arabic/French, diaspora, nationalist and international solidarity, global entertainment/media. The same actor may identify with one stratum's emancipatory claim while opposing its institutional practice, or use a global ideal to contest a local authority.

PYAT's role is to make historical structural/social positions, transnational power relations, *availability* of distinct practices/media and source context supportable. **Availability ≠ actor access** (device, language, family control, resources, era); access ≠ comprehension; comprehension ≠ internal identification; identification ≠ act; declared identity ≠ observed allegiance in practice.

No C11 extra “globalization” psychological column; no one-time universal cultural score. The necessary *source-side* preservation is correct multilingual identity/meaning, geography, wave, external cross-source nonpooling and provenance; the further actual participant/world graph is N9/SOROK-owned, with source-based parts where real.
