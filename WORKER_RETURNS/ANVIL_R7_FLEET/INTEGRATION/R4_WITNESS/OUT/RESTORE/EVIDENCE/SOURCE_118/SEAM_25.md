## 0. Why SEAM exists — decisive scope correction

**SEAM is not the general laboratory in which UM-PYAT and UM-SOROK repeatedly redesign Neo-V3 personality, C5 or reproductive modeling.** It is the joint place where one programme's **qualified output, limitation, permission and transformation** meets an *actually different* programme's **real named first consumer**. The charter demands **stage input → separately authorized computation → output meaning and negative → actual next reader → changed consequence and cold proof**. PYAT may not write personal psyche; SOROK may not promote generated life to original-world source evidence. The boundary is **bidirectional and asymmetrically owned**.

The mother/father/conception clarification has been retained in [SEAM/24 R5 delta](24_SEVEN_CRACKS_CONCEPT_CODE_DEVELOPMENT_BANK_R1/11_R5_CONCEPTION_MODEL_DELTA_RESOLVED_AND_OPEN.md). Its detailed generative algorithms and all seven SC-01–SC-07 developmental research cards remain **secondary to this boundary court**, owned by SOROK/V3 or the proper existing commission where internal. This agenda **does not erase** the research bank; it stops it from replacing the joint task.
