## Different evidence grades, different scientific objects

1. **ORIGINAL OBSERVATION:** exact respondent/unit/source/edition/country/wave/question/released answer, routing and own response-code meaning; no inferable latent psyche.
2. **INSTRUMENT DESIGN METADATA:** survey design, sampling frame/routing/category availability; permissible statement `Lebanese ancestry was not observed by this instrument`, never `respondents lack Lebanese ancestry`.
3. **INSTRUMENT CONSEQUENCE:** logically provable what the response structure collapses, misses or cannot distinguish. Intention of the designer is *not* inferred from an absent category.
4. **INTERPRETATION/HYPOTHESIS:** separately cited substantive analysis of epistemic tendencies, question semantics or political meaning; not an original survey observation or design intent without primary evidence.
5. **QUALIFIED DESCRIPTIVE:** lawful conditional/joint/uncertainty/statistical output; correlation is not independently sourced structural pressure.
6. **INDEPENDENT STRUCTURAL:** separately obtained field/material ownership/institution/coercion/resource evidence and rule/temporal authorizations; no source role-categorization alone implies class power.
7. **MODELED/FICTIONAL WORLD and ACTOR:** explicit distinct jurisdictions and histories, no backflow from a generated biography into respondents.

## Orthogonal observability contract

At exact instrument/release/country/wave/form/question, record `asked`, `eligibility`, `released`, `observation`, `unit`, `attribution`, `translation`, `source_status`, original code reference, and whether asserted claim is about **instrument metadata** or a **substantive respondent value**; compare target unit/attribution separately. Source design/observed consequence/scientific interpretation are different provenance grades. The validator `validate_source_claim` enforces key cross-facet exclusions; actual codebooks/source/science reviewers must still verify whether a specific category really existed or a translation is equivalent.

Forbidden promotions: `NOT_OBSERVED`→original respondent value; modeled/fictional→source fact; HOUSEHOLD→PERSON individual ownership or income; INTERVIEWER→SELF attribution; not-asked/released→zero, no-effect or missing-as-false; same English label across waves→same measurement absent issued equivalence; sample-design stratum→released individual identity; external group estimate→a respondent's ancestry; observed group difference→institutional hegemony; observed change across cross-sections→individual longitudinal psychological development. A historic record lacking an allowed category must keep the **absence of observability**, not an invented negative fact, through FF, SEAM and SOROK.

True survey response still may fail to measure an interpretation put upon it. A respondent's answer to 'religious' is not transparent evidence of theology, private conviction or conception of religion; source wording and culture matter. Require documentary original language and design when available and say UNKNOWN where not.

**Four tests required of any architectural proposal:** (a) an absent ancestry/sect distinction, including AB survey-wave-specific measurement; (b) actual household-vs-individual measure and self/interviewer distinction; (c) unreconciled translation/wave semantic equivalence; (d) true >=3 respondent-coherent minority pattern despite unchanged pairwise marginals, plus independent structural fact and actor access/no-access. At least one *new* source-specific challenge not copied from the worked examples must be considered by independent C. A fixture is sourcefree truth about fixture behavior, not native Arab Barometer science.

## The actual historically differentiated social field, not just a survey-person label

The eventual demanding reader needs more than `P(opinion | demographic)` or one adult's answer vector. Legitimate Stage4/FF producers must be *able to construct, when evidence is qualified*, within-person joint configurations including ordinary and minority/counter-patterns; distinct distributions across multiple actually source-observed social positions; qualified reverse-conditionals; historical/country/wave measurement differences; and, in separately sourced world strata, resource control, exploitation/constraint/rights, institutional power, local and transnational fields, mediation and genuine contradiction. These are conditional future *operations* and first-consumer constraints, not new respondent columns. There is no permission to infer a group actor's encounter probability, class mechanism or hegemony from a difference in survey means.

The historical-person destination must support independent Others, overlap of local/religious/class/language/global allegiances and their differential access through actual history. SOROK's mother-first possible-life construction, compressed mother pre-life, later contingent encounter and independently conditioned father, actual parents/child development and long actor-world change may legitimately depend on that social field. These are separately governed *generative science* and actual-activity obligations. **ANVIL must not invent mother/father/child algorithms, statistical partner choice, a predetermined psychological type, Absolute Horizon ordering, or children's acquired beliefs from C11.** It must preserve exactly the source/time/material and lawful extension capacity such consumers could need; a currently held SOROK scientific mechanism cannot block otherwise genuine present source→C11 engineering.

For the eventual first social-world consumer, differentiated nonfocal **other positions** must be capable of retaining their own distributions and source/model grades, not cloned or inverse traits of a focal respondent. Availability ≠ actual encounter, actual encounter ≠ understanding, understanding ≠ identification, identification ≠ action, and outward action ≠ automatic knowledge by an absent person. Model-graded fictional particularization and historically observed original survey evidence remain separate source lines.

## Five named nonpreclusion paths

`OBSERVED_TO_DESCRIPTIVE` actually connects OBSERVED source to DESCRIPTIVE_MODELED qualified output. `EXTERNAL_TO_STRUCTURAL_WORLD` includes external observation, independently issued structural relation and world opportunity. `WORLD_TO_ACTOR` must reach ACTOR_STATE and identify actor access; `ACTOR_TO_HISTORY` must reach HISTORY_EVENT; `EXOGENOUS_TO_HISTORY` must start from a fictional world and reach a separately justified exogenous committed history occurrence. `SRC→C11` cannot satisfy WORLD_TO_ACTOR. Each path names preserved payload, original/fictional clock, owner and negative. Future unbuilt paths can be `OWNER_HELD_WITH_FEASIBLE_EXTENSION` only when the ABI and non-loss information are demonstrated. Such a route does not claim SOROK or FF actually executed.

**No authority from a graph label alone.** The typed domain transition allowlist in `contracts/policy.json` and `tools/r6.py` is a *necessary* safeguard, not source-science accreditation. Exact real source, output, right, estimator, first consumer and independent scientific owner remain separately required. Heterogeneous routes are normal; do not force every capability through a survey attitude model.
