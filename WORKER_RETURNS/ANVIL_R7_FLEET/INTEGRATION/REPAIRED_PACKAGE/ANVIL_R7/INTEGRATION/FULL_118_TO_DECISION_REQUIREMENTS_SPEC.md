## 2. Stage law

**S1/FRAME** asks only whether the complete source identity universe is authenticated with original meaning, negative, owner and locator. It does not ask for a physical architecture. A legacy R5/R6 `SPINE.json` is optional historical evidence and cannot substitute for the inventory; R5 was PREPARED / LOCALLY STRUCTURALLY TESTED / NOT DISPATCHED, so a completed P2 spine was an expected output, not a historical input.

**S3/DECISION** asks for each identity to receive one candidate-specific disposition: `PRESENT_PRESERVED`, `FUTURE_NONPRECLUDED`, `EXACT_OWNER_HOLD`, `BLOCKING_LOSS`, `REFUSED_AUTHORITY`, or, only for PYAT_PC/P4R, `RETAINED_HISTORICAL_NONADOPTED`. Presence of an ID is not preservation. `PRESENT_PRESERVED` needs a verified origin hash, a non-vacuous design effect, a named first-consumer path and a positive/refusal/restore witness. The 26 cannot be adopted and the 14 cannot be closed by the decision.

**C** requires the seven T1–T7 boundary courts with positive, negative, restore, grade and exact evidence or exact hold; a typed producer/authority/time/consumer graph with no holes and no fictional/modeled backflow; actual builder handoff review; and no self-certified independence, owner grant or H1.
