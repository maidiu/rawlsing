# N1 / Scientific court: one finite conditional practice, four competing explanations

## Material phenomenon, not a label
Object: one actor A's practice of coordinated inspection of a material tool or work item while an independently constituted co-worker B may offer help, refuse, or independently perform an act. Semantic object for the existing BP2P4J receptor: c9p:coordinated_material_inspection. Proposed CAN011 target kind: V3MS_PRACTICE. These are **the existing L1 experiment's identifiers**, not an adopted current human practice taxonomy. A has separately verified occupational/material opportunity, but role and occupational class are not psychogenesis.

Separate seven observations:
1. World affordance = a tool, permitted work time and access, owned by C0/ADP010. It does not mean A acted.
2. Enacted material action = actual A operation against the object, owner-observed and dated. A plan, intent, self-report, narrator sentence or task assignment is not an enacted act.
3. Independently repeated/varied practice *evidence* = multiple genuinely distinct actual performances across source-methods, times, tasks and occasions, with enough exposure denominators and contrary cases to know what repeated action means. This may remain local task competence, compliance or necessity; NOT yet a learned disposition.
4. Socially mediated acquisition = a claim that B's separate actions and ongoing relation changed the development/reproduction of A's action possibilities. Requires B source and a counterfactual/temporal contrast; "participated with B" alone cannot establish B's causal contribution.
5. Durable CAN011 middle-state = *ruled* historically persistent, revocable/revisable V3MS_PRACTICE object supported by exact causal parents. Its storage/persistence alone is not evidence that its rule was psychologically true.
6. Contradictory commitments = multiple context-sensitive practices/tensions may coexist, including A's dissent from a proposed action or historical avoidance despite other settings of cooperation. No single winning scalar, moral-progress attractor, universal coherence or required self-awareness.
7. Self-understanding and later choice = ADP017-accessible self-report is a different observation from activity and inaccessible causal trace; E2 requires independently performed act, exact consumed target hash and timing after target commit. Later E2 change is the strongest proximal discriminatory consumer, still not enough for population-level developmental psychology.

## Required contrasts and no-effect controls
- **P+ source-qualified**: separate A/B histories, distinct real operations, independent B offer, actual encountered and ADP019-admitted E1, CAN008 applicable and CAN011 genuinely issued/granted, later owner-performed E2. Predict only a *specific* target and later effect under issuer-chosen alternatives.
- **H1 underdetermined**: two TEST_ONLY acts alone, identical source owner, an assigned role, A self-report, or old ability with no observed change → REQUEST_EVIDENCE, not universal habit and not automatically a target.
- **H2 null**: B does not actually offer or is absent/refuses; track whether A continues, no unconditional H2 effect. B's independent action may be a causal constraint even without A perceiving it.
- **H3 contradiction**: A performs a contrary act or holds a separately sourced incompatible commitment. Must preserve both histories, classify competing, do not force target denial *unless* an actual target rule decides; no "integration" scalar.

- **CAN011 DENY**: revoked/wrong-actor/wrong-rule/self-issued/missing grant or stale readset → refused mutation; committed prior event not rewritten.

## Main scientific judgment
The supported inference is a **hypothesis discrimination framework**, not an original actor-development fact. H1 needs specified cross-context persistence and actor-owned enactment; H2 needs actual separate social mediation; H3 needs observed contradictions and context, not retrospective fictional internal conflict; H0 survives all current TEST_ONLY fixture positives. The first science-worthy rule is therefore a CONDITIONAL owner-decision petition for a narrow operation- and time-specific learned practice, not an ADOPT from N1. The proposed initial threshold of two independent enacted source items in the method compiler is merely a *safety-screen requirement for owner review*, not a criterion of acquisition.
