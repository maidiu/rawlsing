# Assay validity and next acceptance conditions

## Intended inference and what the measurement can establish

A model's selection of a frozen code can measure discrimination among declared choices under a particular prompt. Forced choice is not intrinsically invalid: multiple choices permit incorrect answers, and a scorer may legitimately compare against an independently validated key. However, correctness on this menu alone does not establish history→owner formation→situated response causation, inaccessible-memory influence without recall, public standing versus private belief, or optional policing. Here the same authoring fixtures supply both context and expected outcomes, treatment machinery is absent, and identifiers/semantic menus expose the task structure. A positive answer could mean instruction compliance, recognizing labels or selecting familiar response stereotypes.

The exact all-576 label-only counterexample and first-option K3 +22.222pp result are stronger falsifiers than hypothetical bias. They show that the current assay admits a perfect score and a positive screening difference through non-architectural pathways. They do not say how any real model would perform; no model was tested.

Source-free replication is retained at F3 bounded synthetic machine grain. Future repaired forced-choice trials could provide pinned-model code-discrimination evidence. Architectural causal inference additionally needs validated lawful outcomes and interventions on implemented owner/arm machinery, with intervention and mediator receipts. Human development, durable personhood, unrestricted interactions and population science remain outside that evidence ceiling.

A deterministic scorer needs the key for equality, but that is different from blind response coding. Store keys with the independent oracle custodian. Validate unique `(block, arm, response)` identities and code universe; compare the entire dual-scoring record with a separately derived oracle. 7. Primary denominator must retain every assigned trial, separately reporting unscorable, invalid, transport-held and safety-failed outcomes. ## External methodological context (public lookup, no source export)

Research documents option-order sensitivity in tested language models; this supports treating order as an empirical nuisance factor, not assuming deterministic rotations remove it. The observation does not transfer a measured effect to an untested future model. [Pezeshkpour and Hruschka, NAACL Findings 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.130.pdf).

NIST describes exact binomial intervals and cautions against symmetric normal approximations at small sample sizes or few failures. The illustrative calculation above follows the zero-event binomial inversion; it is an independent local calculation, not a paired power certification. [NIST statistical handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm).
