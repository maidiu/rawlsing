`PositionedSubjectPossibilityResult.material()` includes query ref, source/release/target, `exact_evidence_ref`, **geometry**, fallback path, authority of the *model rule*, replay determinants and `model_rule_hash`; it **does not include the underlying PossibilitySliceEvidence.provenance_refs or authority_refs**. A binder authentication check is predicted to pass because provenance is not its job. This is a **proposed static-logic counterexample, not an executed exploit or adopted permission bypass**. It disproves any proposal that `authenticated_result.result_hash == C1.consumer_result_or_null.result_hash` *alone* authenticates C1 origin. The latter equality remains useful solely for mathematical/output parity on identical inputs. The actual service ingress must bind to the owner-produced C1 result **and** its `composed_result_identity_hash`, evidence identity/preimage, exact fact/predicate/witness, pairing/ancestor refs and trusted producer/replay entry. A caller's self-consistent C1-looking envelope and hash do not certify provenance.

This corrects a false-positive risk in S01's `RECEIPT_AND_REPLAY_CONTRACT.md`: hash equality and replay identity are necessary, **not sufficient**, for custody.
