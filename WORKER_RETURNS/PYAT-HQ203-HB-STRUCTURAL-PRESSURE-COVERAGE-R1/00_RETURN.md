## Method

I did not begin from a pressure theory. I enumerated every retained pressure-family
demand separately — deliberately **not** treating constraint, opportunity,
counterpressure, mediation, interaction, nonadditivity, topology,
occurrence/consequence, persistence, comparative pressure and
contradiction/alignment/divergence as synonyms — cited each to its exact terminal
authority, then asked which adopted form could carry it and whether its authority
actually permits that meaning.

A schema field was **not** counted as coverage unless its authority permits the
required meaning. That rule is what separates this audit from a field-presence
inventory.

## Decisive findings

**1. Directionality is committed structure, not convention.** The strongest objection
available was that V555 has no `directionality` field. It does not need one:
`m2_v555_atomic_route.py` fixes route shape
`V555-RS-001-SOURCE-EXACT-CHANNEL-EXACT-RECEIVER` and emits `ordered_structure` with
exact ordinals `1 SOURCE`, `2 MEDIATION_LOCUS`, `3 RECEIVER`, each bound by
`operator: EXACT_REF_EQ`. Direction, source/target identity and mediation locus are
therefore carried by the object's own committed structure and cannot be asserted
separately from the endpoints.

**2. Constraint and opportunity are already distinct adopted roles.** The closed V556
objective-world vocabulary includes `OBJECTIVE_CONSTRAINT` and
`OBJECTIVE_OPPORTUNITY` as separate roles. Both appear in the seam fixtures. Dva §2's
requirement that a relation be distinguishable as constraint versus opportunity is
covered without a pressure noun.

**3. Occurrence versus consequence is already owned.** SDTF R4 adopts
`K1_CONSTITUTIVE_POSITIONAL`, `K2_QUALIFIED_POSSIBILITY`, `K3_REALIZED_OCCURRENCE`,
`K4_ACTIVE_DURATIVE`. These carry Dva §2's distinction between pressure that is
structurally present but not actor-realized and pressure realized through mediation.

**4. A scalar pressure magnitude is not missing — it is forbidden.** Dva §7 freezes
that no numeric edge weight is required absent a governed formula and that
relational, ordinal, unknown and noncomparable states are *preferable* to invented
precision. OBL041 freezes `BLOCK_SIZE != WEIGHT != PREVALENCE != SPATIAL_EXTENT !=
IMPORTANCE`. Production already asserts `CONDITIONAL_SUPPORT_PROFILE !=
PRESSURE_PATHWAY`. The absence of a scalar is compliance.

**5. Comparative pressure is not a retained demand.** The phrase occurs exactly once
in the whole repository at startup: in the commission that asks the question. No
terminal authority demands it, and cross-source comparability is affirmatively gated
by E04 §9 and OBL040.

**6. No production consumer asks for pressure.** Every occurrence of
"pressure" in `rawlsing_next` production is either an adopted refusal
(`NO_FIELD_CAPITAL_HEGEMONY_HOMOLOGY_PRESSURE_MECHANISM__`,
`CONDITIONAL_SUPPORT_PROFILE != PRESSURE_PATHWAY`) or unrelated prose about
memory pressure. That absence is the adopted design: Field Forge R2 lists
`PRESSURE_FOOTPRINT` and `PRESSURE_PATHWAY` under "No current competent strong-noun
owner" with disposition `BLOCKED_EXPLICIT_AUTHORITY`, and states that neutral
precursor evidence does not self-promote.

## The counterexample attempt

I built **CANDIDATE-X**, the strongest composite demand available: one exact-identity
PRE-V3 object simultaneously carrying a directed constraint relation, a coexisting
counter-pressure relation on the same pair, an opportunity relation distinguishable
from it, a temporal interval with continuation/rupture, a cross-wave divergence
verdict relating two such objects, an explicit non-additivity claim with no scalar, a
mediation locus, and a possibility-versus-occurrence separation.

It **failed** to exceed adopted jurisdiction across four attack routes. The most
interesting failure is Route B: V555 genuinely cannot bind a relation as a source or
receiver, because its endpoint refs are F1 structural *position* refs. But that is a
**jurisdiction split resolved by V556**, not a missing meaning — the V556 result
schema is authored per rule while its vocabulary stays closed, so one rule can bind a
closed schema declaring multiple relation `exact_ref`s, coexisting-relation arrays
with `min_items >= 2`, and a string enum for the divergence verdict.

Route D is the one objection that survives, and it changes the disposition rather
than the coverage conclusion: the pressure vocabulary is absent from production
because adopted law declines to mint it. The correct reading is that coverage exists
at the form level and is unpopulated at the instance level.

Full argument, including all four routes and the two honest limits on the conclusion,
is in `04_STRONGEST_UNCOVERED_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md`.
