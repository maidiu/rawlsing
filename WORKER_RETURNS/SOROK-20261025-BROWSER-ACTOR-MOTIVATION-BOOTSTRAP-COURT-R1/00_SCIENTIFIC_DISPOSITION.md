## 1. Scientific adjudication: competing causal pathways

**A. Bodily regulation / allostatic relevance**
- Presupposes a biologically organized, developing, anticipatory regulatory organism. It is NOT an unstructured infant and not an empty psychological slate.
- A true measured nutrition/energy/thermal/health condition can constrain activity. Its perception may be nonconscious, affectively felt, consciously named, misread, or not presently available. Anticipatory regulation can occur without experienced deficit.
- Object property/affordance (edible food, water, rest, warmth), actual availability and actor-accessible encounter must be distinguished. A nutrient requirement does not itself specify a food preference, trust in offerer, acceptance or attempt.
- Strongest unique explanatory power: bounded organismic constraints, repair/maintenance demands and cross-context bodily consequences. DOES NOT explain helping, institutionally formed projects, abstract political commitment, play or refusal to eat to preserve another commitment.
- Important: CAN009 existing terminal 12-dimension registry contains ORGANISMIC_HEALTH and ORGANISMIC_VITALITY plus material capacities, but has **NO registered hunger/nourishment/satiety dimension**. Health or vitality CANNOT be relabeled hunger. A literal hunger test must use a clearly TEST_ONLY additional measurement/organismic source fixture, or await a competent domain-specific CAN009 extension and physiological input source. Nourishment must NOT be inserted by analogy into CAN010's ten need-axis references.

**B. Relational and socially co-regulated activity**
- Presupposes developing attachment/care relationships, social perception, other's independent acts, and joint bodily/environmental regulation; no premise that isolated felt deprivation temporally precedes care.
- Co-regulation may materially supply a baby's warmth, feeding, calming and access to objects before the infant has words or independent execution. Participation and comfort under support are not independently mastered operations.
- Strongest unique explanatory power: early relational regulation, supported action, contingent social signaling and later differentiated care/meaning. It does not by itself issue a child's conscious love, trust, gratitude or reciprocal motive.
- Hofer's 1994 empirical support is primarily animal-based and cannot be directly promoted to a universal human computational law; human developmental inference is explicitly qualified.

**C. Existing historically acquired practical organization / object of activity**
- Presupposes some lawful prior lived activity (including present collective participation), target/goal content or demonstrable actor-controlled act, relevant practical means and a world that admits consequences. It is NOT produced by actor's class/role label alone.
- An agent's actual ongoing object-directed activity can make an encounter relevant because it facilitates, obstructs or transforms that activity; its object is not reducible to bodily relief. Recognizing relevance may be practical, implicit, relational and nonverbal.
- Strongest unique explanatory power: continuation/protection of current work, help, play, learning, care, symbolic commitments, and the development of new needs/objective motives through activity.
- It does not license inferring a stable goal from ONE ambiguous observed behavior: actor motive/source ownership of the relevant prior activity must be established. Actor's occupational assignment, B's finishing role, material remaining, B's prior refusal or tool shortage NEVER automatically means B wants to reject A today.

**Verdict:** neither A nor B nor C is the universal ontogenetic first cause. **Current situational actor concern is produced through an object-relevance operation CONDITIONED BY whichever competent organismic, relational or activity history is actually available.** A is an excellent contrast fixture, but C may be the lower-dependency first real implementation for S49 only if its existing actor-history source actually includes a qualified directed object/purpose. Do not assume that it does.

## 4. Counterfactual scientific court: identical hunger, different meaningful worlds

Analytical fixture only, NOT B's actual life and NOT an imported CAN009 hunger dimension. Hold a separately declared hungry condition fixed in all trials (in a future native run, use exact hunger-source contract). Compare:

D. Same hunger, offer from historically coercive parent with valid actor memories: same nutritional content, different actor-significance and candidate pressures; CAN011 current mediator may affect uptake only if existing and applicable. Alternative explanations remain.

G. Same hunger and accessible food, but actor is caring for sibling in a task or has a credible overriding commitment: keep multiple concerns; actor may delay, decline, ask for help, take food and refuse apology, or become materially unable. Never hard-code winner.

**Contrasting non-deficit counterexample:** B is not hungry and has actual governed object-directed shared work; current received offer unexpectedly interrupts the ongoing task.
