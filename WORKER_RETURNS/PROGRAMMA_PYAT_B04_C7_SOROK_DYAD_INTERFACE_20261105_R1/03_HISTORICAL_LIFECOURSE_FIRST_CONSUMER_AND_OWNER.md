## 1. The actual object to generate

A person's demographic position at birth is not their psychology. In Sorok's original person-centered conception, an independently living, historically formed subject has objectively situated work, care, family, material activity, constraints, acquired expectations, opaque memories and projects; other actors have their own histories even when the user is absent. A relationship is a differentiated **generated history**, not two synthetic traits being numerically paired. [Original Real Alpha Spine](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/03_REAL_ALPHA_SPINE.md) §§2–5; [original relational ontology](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/02_PROVISIONAL_RELATIONAL_ONTOLOGY.md) §§2–3.

The time-dependent chain is *not* a compulsory linear world→couple→child story. Positions and opportunities alter one another over decades; actors form, refuse, migrate, work, care, leave, reconnect or never meet. Encounter opportunities and outcomes can be `VALID_EMPTY`/`NONOCCURRENCE`. All actual commitments, birth/adoption/care events, separations, alternative caretakers and later biographies are admitted separately, then become objective history and conditional inputs for future decisions.

## 4. The historical clock/identity law

Use these distinct timestamps and identities for any proposed generation or replay; all are **contract requirements**, not proof that their runtime exists:

- `t_survey`: when an item was administered or a household observed; `t_population`: time represented by target inference; `t_world_asof`: structural world query scope. They can differ and require an explicit transport/comparability rule.
- `t_event_effective`: when a position, contact, union, birth, separation, care move or performed act changes objective history; `t_recorded/admitted`: when independently authorized ledger captures evidence; late discovery cannot backdate actor knowledge.
- `t_actor_access`, `t_uptake`, `t_response`, `t_durable_update`, `t_later_action`: distinct causal ordering; source truth can predate actor knowledge, and a learned disposition may never form.
- `t_generation`: each child's birth/adoption/entry, `t_kinship_effective`: separate legal, social, residential and care relation transitions; a partner's later identity is not an initial historical fact before their lawful creation.
- `t_llm_batch`: user-settled two-half 3–5 event cadence; second half call N proposes batch under generated state, actual engine admits events and elapsed intervals, first half N+1 renders remembered/impressed prior actual batch. Renderers do not retroactively author outcomes.
- `world_root`, `actor_id`, `pair/relationship_id`, `genealogy_id`, `branch_id`, `source/release/rule_version`, and event indexes are different identifiers. Never replace a historical root by mutable `latest`, a household key, a display name or a survey respondent ID.

2. **Dyad/statistical and social-science owner** determines whether any local pair risk set/assortment/opportunity evidence exists, compares abstain vs limited opportunity vs estimated selection, and issues use rights separately. 3. **Sorok original ADP/C4 owners** choose a lawful actor/independent B source, named stochastic registries and causal event witnesses. Relationship/genealogy owners own append-only relation events; Pyat does not write them.
4. **C8/C9/C10/C12 owners** choose exact compatible root/schema/readset/clock and prove actual changes/NO_CHANGE with a downstream reader, source-severance RED and independent S2 review before a scoped engineering receipt.
