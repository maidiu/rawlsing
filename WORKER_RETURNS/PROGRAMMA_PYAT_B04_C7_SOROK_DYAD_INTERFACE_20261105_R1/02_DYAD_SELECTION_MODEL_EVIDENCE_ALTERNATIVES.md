# 02 — Dyad/contact/partnership/co-parent model and evidence alternatives

**Adjudication:** `UNIT_OF_ANALYSIS_MISMATCH_PROVEN__DYAD_MODEL_UNISSUED`. No statistical estimate or dyad probability is claimed. This court specifies the estimands that would have to be supported, the alternative model families and honest refusal. Valid empirical distributions can inform a risk set, but cannot identify which two people meet, reciprocate, partner, parent or continue relationships.

## 1. Six different random quantities; never substitute one for another

Use `U` for qualified survey response universe, `T` for specified local historical target, `D_i` individual demographic/position information, `A_i` survey attitudes, `W_t` material/historical world, `H_i(t)` generated actor history, `R_ij(t)` objectively eligible pair-risk-set indicator, and `i≠j` actual distinct actors. The indices are important: an F4 same-respondent multivariate row is one **i**, not a linked (i,j) pair.

| Quantity | Unit / denominator / outcome | Identification and required evidence | Nonclaim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1/F2/F4 distribution** `P_U(A_i,D_i | C_i,t)` | Survey respondent or declared household/member, exact administered item, eligible route, risk set, missing-data and weights; response configuration | Original questionnaires, codebooks, routing, ID and design, weight, administration, response/censoring, eligible population and variance/transport. Current I03/C1 code is scoped infrastructure | **Not** a position generator, psychology or partnership likelihood |
| **Local target incidence** `P_T(A_i,D_i | X_i,W_t)` | Actual individual population in precise geography, cohort and period | Survey-to-target transport, comparable categories, coverage and calibration/margins; no unlicensed extrapolation to locality, past period or institution | A national wave's unweighted row count is **not** local prevalence |
| **Opportunity / meeting hazard** `Pr(O_ij(t+Δ)=1 | R_ij(t)=1, X_i,X_j,W_t,H_i,H_j)` | **At-risk ordered/unordered pair-time**, co-presence and shared channel may be exposure but not event | Local places and mobility, neighborhood/commute/school/workplace, schedules, geography, network edges, introductions, venue participation, barriers; observed at-risk nonmeetings and time | Two demographic marginals cannot estimate pair exposures; same block ≠ actual contact |
| **Encounter/contact** `Pr(E_ij | O_ij, witnessed contact,t)` | Eligible opportunity interval, independently admitted contact occurrence | Actor-specific opportunity + exact contact witness + admission; differentiate seeing, talking, repeated contact, encounter and exposure | Contact never entails disclosure, knowledge, trust or consent |
| **Partnership / reproductive choice** `Pr(P_ij(t+Δ),B_ij(t+Δ) | E_ij,H_i,H_j,W_t, alternatives)` | At-risk pair with actual interaction histories, competing partners/options, dating/cohabitation/marriage/separation and childbearing alternatives | Longitudinal dyadic relationship, pair formation/dissolution, union and fertility histories, timing and competing risks; reciprocal decisions/refusals | Assortative mating **association among realized unions** does not identify a pair's transition probability by itself |
| **Co-parenthood and genealogy** `Pr(G_ijc | admitted birth/adoption/care/legal events)` | Specific child/person and **separately created** biological, gestational, legal, custodial, social-care and residential relations | Own reproductive, legal, care and kinship event rules, source permissions, chronology, independent child's identity and history | Parenthood is not synonymous with partnership, genetics, shared residence or a life-long dyad |

The last quantity may be recorded as an *actual generated event and relation* rather than sampled from a marginal probability. It is not inevitable that any pair yields a child; `NONE`, separation, infertility, adoption, nonresident parents, changed caretakers, unknown and lawful refusal must remain expressible.

## 2. Why pairing is not statistically identifiable from the present F2/F4 products

Given marginal distributions `p_i(D,A)` for possible persons, infinitely many joint pair distributions `q(i,j)` share those same marginals: independent mixing, complete stratification, institution-separated mixing and any number of network-constrained matchings. Their meeting, union and fertility rates can diverge arbitrarily. Even a full same-respondent F4 `P(A1,A2,D)` supplies correlations **within** one respondent; it does not observe either a second person's jointly linked characteristics or their couple selection. Inferring `P(j is coparent | D_i,A_i)` from that product is an ecological/relational leap, not merely sampling uncertainty.

Nor does the distribution **among observed couples** identify the **hazard of entering** a couple without knowing the at-risk pool, exposure mechanisms, union survival and who is missing from the observed set. Case-control or retrospective partnership samples require their own design corrections. A survey question about household spouse is evidence of a household relationship at that survey time only with qualified identity and response semantics; it does not by itself supply first meeting, earlier unions, causal preferences or a longitudinal lineage. Cross-sectional co-residence, partner reports and historical genealogies are different evidence classes.

Likewise `P(A|D)` and `P(D|A)` are mathematically related only within the **same** qualified joint universe, denominator and target. Different waves, changing response options, inconsistent geography or administered-vs-eligible route create noncomparable inverse queries. Reversing a condition is descriptive Bayes, not a causal claim that opinions produce class/ethnicity or predetermine a spouse. The [PR #696 sourcefree scientific court](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/df35c282979662bfd2d3693f1ab43f991684c2db/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/PROGRAMMA_PYAT/05_ROADMAP/TERMINAL_F2F4_GF1_SCIENTIFIC_DECISION_COURT_20261105_R1.md) and [issue #695](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/issues/695) explicitly preserve this split.

These are **candidate evidence classes**, not claims that an eligible archive/record, license, target unit, causal model or data custodian is available. Before any live use, the source owner must return `(dataset/release, licensed use, variable/unit, geotemporal coverage, sampling/selection, linkage authorization, disclosure controls, uncertainties, independent review)` or a typed denial. This C7 worker makes no source use grant.

Do **not** insert a universal `P(partner|D,A)` prior, arbitrary uniform random mate, “nearest compatible personality,” enforced family completeness, or a demographic template to fill unknown psychology. If a qualified model eventually supplies a distribution, its uncertainty/heterogeneity and optional `NONOCCURRENCE` still stand. In particular, statistical randomization is a *conditional computational selector*, not explanatory or semantic authority.

## 5. Proposed staged estimand and replay design

For each period/setting, a future investigator may *propose* the following conditional model, only after every term's owner and source eligibility are specified:

`P_t(G, histories | W, observed evidence) = P_t(eligible individual positions | W, original target) × P_t(pair opportunities | actor sets, activity/network/geography) × P_t(contact | opportunity, separate event witness/model) × P_t(reciprocal partnership | encounter, each history, alternatives) × P_t(parent/care events | separate reproductive/legal/care mechanisms, histories)`.

This is a **factorization of questions**, not a claim that the product exists or that the factors are independent, uniquely identified or probabilities of deterministic actual lives. Pair opportunity, union, fertility and care are coupled time-varying processes; update the at-risk set after migrations, relationships, births/deaths, institutional change and decisions. For genuine event generation, the Sorok owner must preserve each actor's capacity and refusal and log causal witnesses. No Pyat survey item is an admissible event witness by its prevalence alone.

Required statistical objects, if native work is authorized:

1. `target`: local geography/institution/cohort/calendar period, population coverage and pair risk-set definition; unit distinguished from sample unit.
2. `measurement`: survey source release, exact item value code and route, respondent/partner linkage provenance (if any), weights/design, missingness, dyadic nonindependence, retrospective recall and selection/censoring model.
3. `estimand`: descriptive incidence, contact hazard, union transition, fertility/care event, or structural opportunity; no claim-type ambiguity.
4. `uncertainty`: design-based interval where supported, posterior/sensitivity if separately justified, nonparametric bounds, support flags, omitted/unobserved mass and alternative explanations.
5. `transport`: original target→local historical target comparison; period/cohort and geography; explicit failure if support absent or categories changed.
6. `decision`: candidate-set selection authority, ADP020 named purpose/seed/candidate hash/draw coordinate, independent event-source/readset and versioned consumer.

## 6. Falsifiers to give an independent reviewer

- **Marginal-equivalence test:** hold all F2/F4 individual cells fixed; vary the contact network or institutional geography. A valid dyad model must change support/opportunities; an F4-only pair chooser will falsely stay unchanged.
- **Reverse conditioning test:** compute both condition directions with exact joint/counts in one universe; mutate the condition value and missing route; demand source-specific denominator change or explicit refusal. The R53 P53T original wrong-condition RED is a warning, not proof of the protected implementation failing.
- **Risk-set test:** hold realized couples fixed but add nonpaired eligible people; a partnership hazard estimate must reflect a changed denominator/selection mechanism or disclose that it cannot.
- **Zero link test:** same neighborhood, employment or household surname with no contact witness ⇒ no encounter; `AVAILABLE` may still be real.
- **Nonoccurrence and refusal:** qualifying opportunity with no contact, contact without exposure, exposure without uptake, each partner declining union, childless partnership, nonresident co-parent, adoptive caregiver and lawful separation must all remain representable.
- **Transport counterexample:** insert period with exclusionary institution/closed school/unaffordable housing; current national attitude distribution cannot override the altered contact/partnership risk set.
- **History counterfactual:** identical Pyat inputs but different actor histories must permit different lawful responses and NO_CHANGE; independent B draws and actions may not be consumed by A's stream.
- **Rights severance:** revoke source/model-use or join permission; even a numerically unchanged distribution must become `PERMISSION_DENIED` at its first protected consumer.
