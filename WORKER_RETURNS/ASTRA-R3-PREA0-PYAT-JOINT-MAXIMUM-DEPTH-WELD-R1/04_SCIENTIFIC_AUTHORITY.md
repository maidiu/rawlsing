## 1. Three warrants, none substitutable

A product needs semantic validity (the quantities/relations mean what is claimed), empirical or explicitly modeled warrant (why the inference is credible at that population/time/unit), and competent use authority (what the particular consumer may do). A valid schema proves none of the latter two. A source license does not authorize every computational operation. A reviewed fictional scenario does not estimate real-person response probabilities.

## 2. Parent/coparent model court

Represent individual configuration X, coparent configuration Y, pairing/household relation H and selected structural context W separately. An illustrative model factors `P(X,Y,H | W) = P(X | W) P(Y | X,W) P(H | X,Y,W)` only if each conditional has a declared estimand and warrant. This factorization is a bookkeeping identity for a chosen model, not source evidence for its factors. Generation of shared events E then needs a distinct developmental/household mechanism; drawing X and Y does not create E.

Candidate comparisons: independent marginals (explicit null), stratified assortative pairing with transparent parameters, hierarchical conditional model where suitable linked data exist, and a set-valued sensitivity envelope when dependence is unidentified. Independence is an assumption, never an observed dyad. Conserve native respondent/household units and selection effects. Reject impossible pairs via warranted feasibility constraints, not category stereotypes. Evaluate held-out marginals/joints and support, disclose out-of-domain settings, and preserve alternative plausible households rather than one “representative” parent pair.

No source-free fixture can calibrate these factors. It can show that different assumptions propagate transparently and that the consumer never confuses a modeled pair with an observed household. A lawful bounded generation court can therefore compare explicitly modeled family worlds without waiting for nonexistent universal dyad data, provided its separate lab/model authority and claims are narrow.

## 3. Concrete transition-rule dossier

For each proposed rule, require: named source and destination owners; exact precipitating event class; eligibility/receivability conditions; conditioning state roles; permissible candidate effects; counterexamples and no-change outcomes; quantity semantics and uncertainty if numeric; stochastic candidate/weight authority and RNG receipt if used; contradictory-stream composition; memory/access ceilings; update/supersession law; calibration or stated theory-only status; and held-out test cases prepared by someone other than the rule author.

CAN008_R2 already owns coupling at the semantic level. Its adoption explicitly creates no default uptake score, recruitment weight, effort percentage or stochastic rule. A new scientific court should fill a small concrete rule family, not reopen generic U1 ownership or invent another appraisal engine.

Deterministic admission can verify parent existence, role/type constraints, allowed target effects and access. It cannot mechanically certify that “this humiliation caused this adult attachment pattern” is scientifically true. Admit model-generated histories as generated hypotheses with a declared model, not recovered facts about real people. Historic plausibility needs external historical evidence at scope; person quality needs independent behavioral evaluation.

## 4. Test design and uncertainty

Separate three endpoints: constitutional integrity (zero forbidden state/access/authority transitions), functional sensitivity (predicted machine change under a causal intervention), and scientific adequacy (held-out performance/calibration or adjudicated plausibility with alternative explanations). A vivid scene is not any of these by itself.

Use matched worlds, fixed model versions, declared stochastic seeds/receipts, paraphrase/order controls and equal retrieval budgets. Freeze expected direction or permissible outcome set before generation. Evaluate machine state before prose. Blind judges to architecture and condition; include disagreement and negative results. Do not force every intervention to change output: a declared irrelevant change should be invariant.

For a bounded first court, preregister one primary contrast per K test, exploratory contrasts separately, and enough repeated seeds to estimate variance. A small deterministic fixture can falsify integrity but cannot prove population validity. If a quantitative claim is made later, report sampling/model uncertainty, effect size and uncertainty interval with a predeclared minimum useful effect; the threshold must not be selected after seeing results. A non-significant result is not proof of equivalence. Equivalence requires a predefined margin and sufficient power.

No numeric global readiness score is meaningful here. Unknown empirical calibration remains unknown even after all schema checks pass.

## 5. Distinct authorities and stop conditions

Source-original/instrument custody, operation-science issuance, independent prospective E_pre custody, source-use/host execution, model/use permission, generated-actor lab authority, operative N12 wake, physical seam authorization and product release are separate. The current commission grants documentary work only. A missing signature blocks its dependent action, not every independent design or productive lane.

Stop the selected mechanism if lineage is used to launder arbitrary prose into state, category/type determines behavior without generated mediation, hidden evidence becomes actor knowledge, a redundant latent state changes behavior only through its label, or an uncalibrated model is presented as measured science. If a richer model wins under fair controls, revise the baseline rather than defend architectural minimalism.
