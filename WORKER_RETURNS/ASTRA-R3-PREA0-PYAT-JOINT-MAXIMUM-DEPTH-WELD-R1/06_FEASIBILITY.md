## Dominant feasibility risk

**The strongest single risk is semantically governed but scientifically uncalibrated history→organization→situated-response transformation.** It is the bridge shared by generated parents, the focal life, memory reinterpretation, topology and live behavior. If the mechanism is arbitrary, perfect terminal data and exact storage merely make arbitrary psychology reproducible.

This is an architectural risk judgment, not a measured probability. The immediate operational bottleneck is different: lawful positive source/world production and terminal consumption remain held. Renderer/materialization and experiential viability can independently defeat the product, so “single dominant” does not mean “only important.” If the first comparative transition court succeeds, the dominant risk may move to long-horizon composition or human experience.

## Promotion ladder

F1→F2 requires an actual bounded implementation under its own grant, not more prose. F2→F3 needs independently retained inputs, predictions, executed outputs and scoped evaluation. F3→F4 needs held-out actors/settings, composition and longitudinal recovery. F4→F5 needs deployment-scale latency/cost, failure recovery, concurrency, access boundaries and product acceptance at a declared service envelope. A missing grant can hold execution while the technical design remains plausible; it must not be mislabeled scientific impossibility.
