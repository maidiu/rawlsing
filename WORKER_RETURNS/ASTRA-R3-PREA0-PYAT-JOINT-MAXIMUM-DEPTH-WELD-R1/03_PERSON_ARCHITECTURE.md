**ASTRA_NEW_RECOMMENDATION, PROVISIONAL**, bounded by GOVERNING_SPIRIT, V3_COMPLETION, ADP007/016, adopted CAN008_R2 and Sorok74/100/101/102 at their stated grains. Covers A1, P1–P10. The minimal architecture preserves maximal focal-person function through exact history and typed organization; it does not equate personhood with a small inventory of traits.

## P2–P4 — Recognition, category, power and love

Represent a recognition episode with claimant, subject, claim/category, witness audience, institution/domain, capacity to ratify or sanction, scope/time, objective occurrence and each participant's received meaning. Public recognition can change institutional standing without producing felt acceptance. Private conviction can persist against public denial. Public consensus is neither truth nor the subject's self-belief.

Category mediation uses three linked but noncollapsed layers: (1) source-warranted public category/rule/practice in a historical setting; (2) actual category assignment, claimed membership, rejection or sanction event; (3) actor uptake and history-conditioned commitments/practices. “Wife,” “artist,” “mother,” “man,” “successful,” “good person,” or “revolutionary” names no behavior by itself. A child learning that approval depends on a category is an admitted developmental hypothesis, not a lookup table. Contradictory category uses across audiences must coexist.

External discipline can follow generated history through a recruited expectation/practice plus opportunity to sanction another. A demographic label cannot directly produce punitive behavior. Compare actors exposed to similar institutions but different witnesses, counterexamples, relationships and opportunities; lack of sanction capacity should alter what can be done without dictating inner conviction.

Love is best represented through durable histories of attention, care, commitment, conflict, dependency, sacrifice, perception and response, whose interpretation can remain contested. No summed love meter establishes truth. Relational reification is useful only if it predicts a specific substitution of role/object/category for responsiveness to a particular other, supported by events and rival explanations. “Idolatry” remains downstream theological interpretation; no machine diagnosis or required repair arc.

## P5–P6 — Event, lived artifact, memory and interpretation

Maintain six distinct identities:

1. Immutable admitted event: what occurred, participants, time, world receipt, exact parent events and lawful generated consequences.
2. Lived artifact: immutable generated text tied to that event, with typed claims labeled objective occurrence, testimony, actor-accessible experience, proposed interpretation or noncausal texture.
3. Generated memory: separately admitted event-referential or causal-trace record; event existence alone does not guarantee memory formation.
4. Retention and accessibility: owner-governed states; stored objective history ≠ remembered, accessible ≠ focal, focal ≠ narratable explanation.
5. Interpretation: versioned actor or analyst hypothesis with provenance and uncertainty; later reinterpretation does not rewrite occurrence.
6. Self-model/epistemic state: separately admitted belief/uncertainty/access under ADP017, including false testimony and mistaken self-explanation.

Later recursion consumes typed admitted claims and lawful memories/traces, not arbitrary prose assertions. If a model nominates a relation from text, it must cite admitted event identities and undergo the relation rule's semantic adjudication. Hashing a prose passage does not turn it into causal evidence. Rejected/draft artifacts have zero causal self-feedback.

For age-seven residue, an adult response may recruit a lawful causal trace while no declarative memory of the event is available. ADP016's UNAVAILABLE source content must not be restored from omniscient history; relearning needs a new actor-facing event. Retained but currently inaccessible memory is a different case and may change accessibility under the competent rule. Corrective recurrence may narrow, generalize, split or revise interpretation; it need not heal, reveal the hidden origin, or force narrative closure.

## P7–P8 — Relational topology and attractor

Use a sparse temporal typed multigraph with explicit n-ary relation instances. Each causal relation binds participant roles, context, valid interval, admitted parent events, authority/rule, provenance and supersession. Pairwise edges may be indexes over a higher-order record, never its replacement. Preserve conflicting/compartmentalized organizations by context predicates rather than an arbitrary universal dominant subsystem.

A pattern such as JUSTIFIES, SUPPRESSES or COMPENSATES_FOR is a substantive mechanism hypothesis. Repeated coactivation alone is insufficient. Start with a small independently reviewed vocabulary whose relations have explicit formation/revision conditions and negative controls. Motifs are derived queries until they need their own independently justified lifecycle. “Same nodes, different topology” is an experimental claim, not proof from a graph picture.

Test graph-only against graph plus slow latent organization under held-out perturbations. A derived attractor cannot be fed back as an independent cause and thereby count the same history twice. If it is a cache, results must be equivalent to its source state. If it is a new latent mechanism, show incremental evidence and exact transition authority. Enneagram adds, at most, a versioned interpretive shadow with competing/no-conclusion outcomes; no category label or arbitrary affinity probabilities drive generation. Its causal regularizer role remains research-only.

## P9 — Bounded recursive social depth

Generate the focal person's consequential parents/caregivers and coparent relation sufficiently deeply to produce independent actions, contradictions and shared events. Parent A trait vector plus parent B trait vector is insufficient: their relationship changes exposure and interpretation. Preserve each adult's agency and asymmetric knowledge; do not generate both solely to explain the child.

Promotion rule: an initially sparse supporting person becomes fuller before a causal decision exceeds their admitted state, repeated consequential interaction requires continuity, or their independent agenda changes the focal life. Promote by consequence, not by mention frequency alone. Siblings/friends/partners use the same rule; durable partners usually require deeper histories. The user is an external agent with admitted actions and bounded actor models, not a fully simulated psyche. Incidental cast may remain role/occasion scoped.

Set a finite historical boundary: ancestor roots have explicit sparse/unknown startup under ADP007 rather than fabricated complete parents. Expand only unresolved antecedents necessary for a declared causal question, with immutable keyed completion and backward compatibility. This is not permission to manufacture a missing memory. If fidelity requires more than the allocated social width, defer the event or request a bounded expansion; never silently flatten an important relation. No recursive whole-universe ancestry requirement.

## P10 — One epochal/live transition substrate

Both schedulers call the same owner-preserving sequence: eligible world context → exact occurrence/exposure → CAN008 receivability/uptake/recruitment → multi-stream response → separately authorized target-owner effects → memory/interpretation/relationship consequences where earned. PFF retains bounded current-session residue, not a second biography.

Epochal scheduling chooses significant events and governed interval transitions; live scheduling uses seconds/minutes. An elapsed year is not itself a psyche effect. Split coarse intervals at births, moves, exposure/relationship changes, relevant world boundaries and other rule-required events. A checkpoint, unchanged tick or summary is not a pseudo-event. Coarse/fine paths need convergence tests at declared tolerance; no universal equivalence claim for nonlinear order-sensitive development.

Shared household events have one occurrence and multiple actor-specific response transactions. One actor can perceive an event differently or not receive it at all. Durable live events enter the same branch history consumed by later development. Shared root pre-contact truth and user-specific post-contact branches remain isolated.

## Renderer and phenomenal boundary

CAN008's private causal receipt can include inaccessible history. Compile only authorized expressive consequences, present constraints and actor-accessible claims for realization; do not pass SECRET CAUSE with an instruction to conceal it. The independent evaluator may retain lineage for checking. Candidate speech/body/material particulars require admission under a concrete occurrence/materialization envelope before binding outward surface; old wording-only CAN022 is not enough by assertion.

Exact utterance occurrence can be true while its proposition is a lie or mistake. User phenomenal projection consumes objective observable occurrence plus lawful attention/embodiment constraints; it may narrow perception, not rewrite the actor's act to suit the user. Preserve objective action, testimony, actor belief and user inference separately. Two model calls is a performance target pending evidence, not an authority or feasibility theorem.
