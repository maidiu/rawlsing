### Surface B — interaction/session context envelope

### Surface C — actor-local interaction-hypothesis carryover

Purpose: preserve only an actor-local hypothesis whose causal relevance genuinely survives beyond the evaluation in which CAN-008 constituted it.

9. **Same durable person state, different fast context:** Surface A/B/C/D may differ while durable V3 refs remain identical, yielding a different lawful R2 coupling/response without duplicate personality state.

No case requires a third fast-state family or new durable owner.
