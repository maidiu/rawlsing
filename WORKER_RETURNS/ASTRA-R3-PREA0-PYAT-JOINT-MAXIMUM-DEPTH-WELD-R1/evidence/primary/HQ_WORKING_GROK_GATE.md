## A. Governing end-product, at every decision point

**The highest-order practical objective of the Rawlsing project is a finished, as-good-as-achievable survey-grounded PERSONA GENERATOR.** Its historical/material world, empirical sociological distributions, mechanisms of pressure/mediation, developmental life formation, personality dynamics and eventual phenomenology/voice must work together as an actual end-to-end machine. PRE-V3 is the actor-independent world/possibility substrate feeding an independently governed V3 developmental runtime and V2 expression; respect the terminal seam rather than shortcutting raw survey variables straight into assigned actor personality.

**Science is a constraint and improvement discipline serving end-product quality, not an independent deliverable that outranks the generated person.** The aim is the best defensible practical inference, transformation and approximation available. The correct project response to an evidentiary difficulty is to develop or reuse a valid alternative method, quantify its limitations and proceed with appropriately graded output wherever lawful—not congratulate ourselves for a refusal and leave an entire persona dimension missing.

This requires considering, where fit for purpose:
- original/direct evidence and genuinely measured distributions;
- inherited cross-wave concept/driver/facet/semantic mappings and scale transformations;
- source-native respondent and population conditioning, weighting and survey-design adjustment;
- population-level synthesis across sources with **nonoverlapping respondents**, not invented respondent-level joins;
- legitimate statistical harmonization, latent-variable/proxy inference, partial pooling and model-based estimation;
- geography, cohort, historical time and temporal interpolation/smoothing/uncertainty-aware estimation;
- probabilistic priors, transparent conservative fallbacks, sensitivity checks, and propagation of uncertain or missing evidence;
- developmental causal mediation rather than automatic deterministic driver→static type assignments.

The project does **not** license fabricating observed data, treating proxy/model output as exact observed evidence, erasing contradictory or minority source patterns, suppressing provenance, or exercising permissions that do not exist. **An exact operation's HOLD is edge-local, not a universal embargo on method development or other legally/source-authorized routes.** Every approximation must carry exact input lineage, model/method version, assumptions, conditioning domain, uncertainty/sensitivity and claim grade.

### Main non-negotiable engineering question

**What does this change in the generated person's historically plausible development, social world, psychological possibilities or expressed life, and what real V3/V2/consumer path uses the result?** If the worker cannot answer, that work may be supporting infrastructure but is not itself proof of progress toward the end-product.

## C. Mandatory fresh technical adjudication — not "old is best" and not "rebuild"

For every major relevant inherited function, the HQ must enter a record in the succession-quality assessment with:

- exact inherited source/implementation/test and its **actual** function, evidence boundary and useful intent;
- exact current owner/accepted implementation/review or demonstrable lack thereof, distinguishing `CURRENT_ADOPTED`, `IMPLEMENTED_UNADOPTED`, `HISTORICAL_FUNCTIONAL_CANDIDATE`, `STRANDED`, `SUPERSEDED_PROVED`, `UNKNOWN_NEEDS_CHECK`, `REAL_FUNCTIONAL_GAP`;
- modern target persona/world consumer and whether a real production→consumer trace is observed;
- a **fresh merit evaluation**: correct science, coverage, information preservation, input/risk/weights, representational fidelity, temporal/geographic applicability, uncertainty, bias, performance/cost and actual persona-quality effect;
- the **best credible alternatives or improvements** (reuse unchanged, integrate/adapt, enhance, or replace), what each gains and loses, and comparative evidence/testing needed;
- precisely the first missing **product-to-consumer edge** and the minimal positive end-product test if not yet functioning.

**Mandatory adjudication choice:** `REUSE_AS_IS`, `INTEGRATE_OR_ADAPT`, `IMPROVE_OR_COMPLETE`, `REPLACE_AFTER_COMPARATIVE_PROOF`, or `NO_DECISION__NEEDS_EVIDENCE`. Choosing `REUSE_AS_IS` requires positive evidence that inherited implementation is suitable at the target scope; choosing replacement requires a bounded comparative case. Historical presence is neither automatic endorsement nor grounds to discard. A still-unproven succession stays `UNKNOWN_NEEDS_CHECK`, not `MISSING`. No mandatory universal choice of model class is imposed.

**F6** must be adjudicated as practical multi-survey synthesis toward world/persona conditioning, not an arbitrary pair of already identical objective fields. **EE12/temporal** must be adjudicated as real temporal usefulness toward generated historical lives, using the inherited V553 temporal/harmonization machinery and improved methods as appropriate, not compulsory Ghana questionnaire research. Preserve accepted authority to qualify *exact* comparisons and uncertainty, but do not conflate one method hold with lack of any temporal functionality.
