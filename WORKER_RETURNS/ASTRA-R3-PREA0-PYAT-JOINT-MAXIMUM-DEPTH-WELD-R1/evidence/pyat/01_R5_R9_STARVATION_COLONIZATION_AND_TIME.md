**Hostile end-to-end proof proposed:** two fixture worlds can have identical modal demographics, role labels and per-actor scalar “pressure” yet different (a) 3-way authority/access/sanction arrangement, (b) minority opportunity branch and (c) historical applicability. A lawful consumer must either distinguish the worlds in typed possibilities/context or report insufficient evidence; if it gives identical confident actor psychology, the port and/or V3 has erased a constitutive distinction. Do not count a pass by merely echoing both input ref strings.

**Positive that Pyat must eventually own:** native source facts and objective world products actually **dereferenced** by a selected query and used by a lawful conditioner under current/historical authority. Current N9 audit specifically says “all required product families” and coherent dependency closure unproved. A requirements-only Sorok port cannot by itself demonstrate this positive.

## F. Downstream-colonization attack (Sorok R9)

**Never ask PRE-V3 to compute:**
- Nadia's conative desire, subjective valuation, love, attachment, pride, shame, identity, sexual attraction, unresolved conflicts, self-trust, thoughts or ego;
- witnessed **experience** (“mother saw me”), relational felt recognition, a caregiver's lived intentionality, private social-reality struggle;
- autobiographical event occurrence, childhood encounter, trauma/wound, causal trace, repression/forgetting, recall accessibility or interpretation lineage;
- psychic graph edges such as `FEARS_ABANDONMENT`, `REIFIES_RELATION`, `MORALIZES_DAUGHTERHOOD`, “Type 3 attractor,” or a deterministic class/country-to-voice mapping;
- **actual** jointly generated parents/coparents or a particular actor's household event from survey demographics and marginals;
- a guaranteed complete social category ontology, institutional legitimacy score or ordinal class power rating without independently admitted original authority;
- material causation/effect estimates from a social-attitude joint or published rule text alone.

**Four noncollapse controls:** (1) material/institutional power distributions and constraints ≠ actor's perceived/recruited power; (2) public social role/category expectations ≠ identification/alienation; (3) population-conditioned possible care/recognition configurations ≠ an observed parent–child history; (4) official law/statute ≠ enforcement practice, legitimacy for an actor or a wound. A printed fact may be published *later* than its effective time.

**The symmetric Pyat responsibility:** refusing to supply independently available public institutional mechanisms because they are “psychological” is also wrong. If a qualified current-world producer holds a real 2021 rule defining citizenship, wage claim, marriage sanction, school exclusion, police authority, resource access or category-based sanction, its *actor-independent* position/role/applicability/exit-cost graph is PRE-V3 territory. V3 constructs how this person encounters, understands, complies, contests and changes under it.

**Sociological model alternative:** when observed objective fields cannot identify the individual situation, an appropriately authorized source-qualified *population possibility/proxy* or conservative historical prior may still support V3 uncertainty-aware generation. That prior stays marked `MODEL_INFERRED` and never claims a true individual exposure. Missing exact dyad data does **not** entail the eventual world has no households; it limits statistical certainty and enforces V3's generated-history boundary.

## G. Parent, coparent, household possibility (Sorok R5)

**Current Pyat F8/N10W means:** a model/use-governed **conditional population possibility service** that progressively updates distributions over specified target features as independently known position, prior configuration and world constraints are supplied. The conditioned respondent unit/risk, original response meaning, order, support/uncertainty and specificity walkback travel in each result. It does **not** generate persons, couples, marriages, child experiences or actor-state facts. The current `pre553_empirical_possibility_provider` computes actual slice evidence from lawful analytical owner paths; `source_backed_positioned_subject_possibility` explicitly distinguishes PROXY vs EXACT FACT vs ACTOR BELIEF. N10W full joint service remains unproved.

**Same-respondent survey evidence** can support cross-answer co-occurrence among that *respondent's* answers, conditional attitudes and native answered item risks. It cannot certify what the respondent's spouse, coparent or child said or did. Sampling units matter: respondent, household, dyad, parent-child link, administrative aggregate and independently surveyed different years are **not interchangeable**. Cross-survey products cannot be equated into fictitious same-family respondents.

**May V3 generate parents?** Eventually yes, under V3's separate actor-init/relationship/development authority, with constraints and priors legitimately drawn from qualified PRE-V3:
1. select (or remain uncertain about) geographic, cohort, household and material frame;
2. condition parent A candidate possibilities on independently warranted population/role information;
3. condition parent B only with a **competently issued** joint/dyadic source rule, or an explicit separately qualified pairing/transport/proxy model with variance/sensitivity; absent that, keep unknown and avoid presenting independence as observed relationship;
4. create actual distinct parent A and parent B V3 persons, then generate co-parent relationships, childbirth/caregiver roles, choices, power/recognition dynamics, events and child's actual exposure in V3 durable history;
5. feed generated household facts back only as **V3-owned query conditions** for subsequent permitted world/possibility resolution; these facts never become evidence of external source population statistics.

**Failure case:** “N10W sampled two sociodemographic parental profiles, therefore generated complete parents and their real relationship.” That is false. Another failure: “two independent parental marginals imply a dyad prevalence.” A third: recognizing a parent’s *reported* feeling as child's experienced recognition without separate child-event generation. No partner-pair statistics from a household-agnostic frame, and no joint child experience without generated encounter.

**Pyat-side requirement for the future port:** household structure and kin-role possibilities must preserve exact units, multiplicity, parent/coparent role identity, partner compatibility if known, temporal household membership, role asymmetry, constrained alternatives, unknown partnership state and ability to request successive lawful conditionings. A committed parent biography, living relation and reciprocal actor models are NOT terminal PRE-V3 output.

## H. Objective power, recognition and social-category seam (Sorok R4)

**PRE-V3 may warrant:**
- rule/institutional actor-independent power: who can license, exclude, sanction, punish, employ, evict, detain, allocate benefits or recognize a legal status and over whom, with governing mechanism/validity and territory;
- concrete resource/exposure opportunity: household material supply, labor/access, care infrastructure, legal eligibility, control over land/work/shelter, available exit routes and costs, documented dependency asymmetries (population/role-level, not fabricated actual family);
- lawfully documented social-category practices/rules: position classifications, official recognition credentials, prohibited memberships, enforceable role requirements, credential barriers, public role categories, sanctions and observed institutional treatment where independently measured;
- source-specific population attitudes *about* legitimacy, status, gender, parent roles or recognition **as survey-attitude distributions**, never thereby institutional enforcement or experienced approval;
- competing/contradictory methods and regional/epochal variation, including histories of colonization/classification and category boundaries, only under exact source authority and justified geographical/temporal scope.

**V3 must compute/generate:** the actor's feeling of being witnessed, care/withdrawal, trust in an institution, beliefs about legitimacy, identification, alienation, shame, category gravity, state of dependency as lived event, love, reciprocal relation, perceived/actual criticism, resistance, insult, self-justification and procedural residue. Sorok's “recognition” as a psychic primitive is not a measured institutional “recognition” variable; same English name creates neither semantic equivalence nor source authorization.

**Category hostility:** place/social-group labels can be externally consequential without being homogeneous experiences. World may report institution classifies someone as X under known rules and exact conditions, or many people report attitude Y. It may NOT state the actor self-identifies X, infers that X rigidly causes behavior, or assumes every historical time uses the same taxonomy. Preserve versioned categories, issuer, disputed classification, cross-cutting memberships, institutional authority, possible refusal/illegibility, alternative recognition sites and mobility.

**Reasoned hard boundary:** if power depends on a named institution, legal status and resource-control relation with independent original evidence, PRESERVE the n-ary graph and actor-independent mechanism. If “power” is only an interpretation of how a parent *means* a look to a child, route it entirely to V3 after the actor encounter. A “coercive category” under common social norms needs independently qualified evidence; a downstream intuitively plausible trope is not itself an objective product.

**Relevant currently unpopulated edge:** D02P/D2F located distinct GSS census household lighting and NCA subscription statistics; neither alone identifies a rural child's device, lived domination or category recognition. They may legitimately be separate historical *material-context* fields after source/field EE13 and N9 issuer grants. This illustrates how to supply concrete material conditions without sneaking psyche upstream.

## I. Life-epochal and live-time context serving (Sorok R6/R7)

**Three distinguishable times, plus actor epistemic status:**
1. **observation/effective/valid interval:** when an objectively measured resource/rule/structure applies, possible retroactive revisions expressly noted;
2. **publication/knowledge/release-as-of:** when evidence and exact authority snapshot became publicly or system-available, distinguishing as-observed from retrospective reconstruction;
3. **actor developmental/event time:** age/year or live event at which V3 generates a person-world occurrence, with an exact generated position/role/household binding and actor knowledge separate;
4. **request audit time:** when the query is made, for currentness and historical replay. (A logical fourth clock is operational even when a client exposes three domain axes.)

**Epochal service:** query historical positions and material/role opportunity at key transitions (birth, family moves, school entry, changing institution/role, crisis/rupture), with graph of exact dependency/invalidations. Request time-scoped *world possibility* and hypothetical household condition changes, not “what happened psychologically at age seven” from N9. Historical generated events and changing parent/child relationships remain V3. Migration updates actor-generated location/position binding; structural N9 then re-resolves under new applicability and retains prior snapshot at older event identity. If source historical observation does not cover a year, return UNKNOWN or separately issued MODEL_INFERRED interpolation/prior; two anchors do not authorize a measured missing year.

**Live service:** do not force raw C11/I03/I05 source rerun or N9 world recompute on each utterance. Persist selected source-world snapshot/closure at exact context epoch, with cache as nonauthoritative. Re-resolve on material source/model permission change, structural release/currentness/invalidation, declared historical-era or location/mobility change, legitimate new position binding, or novel query target requiring additional family. A new user message/actor desire may alter the *V3 scene/subject* and occasionally the query binding; that is not automatically evidence the institutions changed. Subscription/invalidation events may signal updates but do not replace the authoritative typed pull.

**Transaction boundaries:** V3 generates a candidate encounter; PRE-V3 structural read is pure and immutable at that query identity. When a live V3 event is admitted, its resulting actor state/relationship history goes to V3 ledger; world stays unchanged unless a separately competent objective producer has external changed facts. Two simulations with same preserved world snapshot may diverge in actor experience; replay must bind world snapshot root, query, actor-authored known facts, model/rule versions, temporal policy and any randomness.

**Necessary proof:** hold source world fixed, vary generated role/position → allowed world query changes; hold V3 generated binding fixed, revoke a world source or permission → current re-resolution changes/refuses, historical as-of stays separately valid if permitted; hold all actor/world parents identical → cold replay stable. No live V3/PFF actor was run for this review; these are future adversarial requirements, not executed successes.
