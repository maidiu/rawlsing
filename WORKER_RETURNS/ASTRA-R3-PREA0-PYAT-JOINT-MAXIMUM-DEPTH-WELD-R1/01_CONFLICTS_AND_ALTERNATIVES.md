## Rival architectures

1. **Rich primitive graph immediately:** expressive inventory, but duplicated owners, uncalibrated relation vocabulary and growth risk. Experimental competitor, not frozen baseline.
2. **Pure narrative long-context persona:** compelling cheap surface; weak separation of events, beliefs, inaccessible traces and interpretation. Reject as canonical state; retain as blinded rendering baseline.
3. **Learned recurrent latent state:** potentially efficient; intervention semantics, access controls and replay difficult. Research comparator after governed training/use and data. Not inherently invalid.
4. **Event-/relation-first owner state and derived views:** strong lineage, sparse startup, precise interventions; risk of underfitting complexity with brittle rules. Selected baseline; K1–K8 may kill or extend it.
5. **Hybrid plus slow residual state:** admit only for held-out improvement with matched retrieval/context/compute. Freeze residual semantics and owner effects before feedback; otherwise redundant narrative compression.

Select 4 with a falsifiable route to 5. This is not proof that a small hand-written rule set suffices for human psychology.
