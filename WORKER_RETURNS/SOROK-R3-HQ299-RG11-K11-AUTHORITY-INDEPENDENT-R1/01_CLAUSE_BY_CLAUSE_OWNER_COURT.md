- **Selection bias:** q(A)=q(B)=1/2 and completion a(A)=1, a(B)=1/4. Repeated redraw until success yields (4/5,1/5). The selected class must instead HOLD with attempt ledger.
- **Partner history fixed:** when mother anchor includes an existing other parent, reuse exact ID and forward event history or HOLD; do not choose a replacement due to prose.
- **Native unit:** same respondent observations do not imply an across-two-people dyad sampling frame.
- **Future-child embargo:** changing hidden desired child outcome cannot change proposal set, eligibility, selection, parent history, or memories.
- **Separate events:** pending situation, admitted conception, CHILD_GENERATED and birth are independent statuses/events requiring corresponding owners; no alias allowed.
