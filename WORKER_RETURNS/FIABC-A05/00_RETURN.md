## Central holding

Rawlsing's inherited governance diagnosis was substantially correct before the present FIABC court.

The project explicitly rejected carrier fossilization and instead required preservation of irreducible function, semantic authority, typed negative state, provenance, replay/current-selection meaning, and lawful downstream availability. Law 38 states that manually/adjudicatively established V550/V551/V552-era meaning is semantic capital and may not be regenerated from source bytes, labels, distributions, embeddings, convenience, or heuristic resemblance. Its mandatory future-worker checksum requires exact preservation accounting and fail-closes to:

`BLOCKED__MANUAL_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY_PRESERVATION_NOT_PROVED`

when that accounting cannot be established.

The Whole-Spine / current-ground doctrine likewise separates Git ancestry, current runtime, current authority, preservation, live promotion, and conceptual inheritance. Current governance explicitly names P10 preservation closure, Authority31 producer-owned semantic-DAG / information-conservation / physical-neutrality, and Authority39 Long Memory living succession as binding inherited law.

The historical failure evidence shows that this doctrine was not made mechanically unavoidable at every runnable integration root. Recent P2X blockers repeatedly occurred where accepted authority or implementation already existed but the execution tree failed to consume it correctly: missing fan-in, wrong exact path, local wrapper recursion instead of reuse of the accepted binder, orchestration that contradicted the verifier contract, and stopped-ledger state that converted repairable infrastructure failures into non-retryable terminal history.

This does **not** justify the master claim that every blocker is integration drift. R6Q is a direct counterexample: it proved the WVS7 current-survey-country `POSITION_COORDINATE` authority required by the frozen P2 projection was genuinely absent at that time. It rejected an otherwise mechanically binder-valid illegal promotion of `B_COUNTRY`. HQ later adopted new bounded authority; only then did R6S close the projection/backend path.

Therefore the strongest supported diagnosis is:

`PROJECT_KNOWLEDGE_PRESERVATION_WAS_STRONGER_THAN_EXECUTABLE_SUCCESSION_ENFORCEMENT`

not:

`ALL_MISSING_EXECUTION_IS_MISSING_SEMANTIC_DESIGN`

and not:

`ALL_BLOCKERS_ARE_DISCONNECTION`.

## What was supposed to prevent semantic-capital loss

1. **Law 38** — mandatory exact inventory of accepted source-semantic and Rawlsing semantic/structural authority, explicit manual/adjudicated status, provenance-preserving retirement, fail-closed UNKNOWN/BLOCKED/manual-review preservation, and exact evidence for reuse.
2. **Whole-Spine doctrine** — authority/evidence flows forward; capability must be finished through the stack rather than declared complete at one isolated layer.
3. **P10 / preservation closure** — preservation is an anti-omission theorem, not mere schema capacity or historical byte survival.
4. **Authority31** — producer ownership and physical-neutrality prevent persistence/layout from becoming semantic ownership.
5. **Authority39 / project memory** — living succession and discoverability prevent off-tree or superseded-at-one-grain capital from disappearing from project cognition.
6. **Canonical governance / anti-resurrection** — current authority, runtime, Git ancestry, preservation, historical evidence, blocked state, and superseded implementations remain typed rather than collapsed.

## Where governance remained insufficiently mechanized

The governing surfaces successfully constrained meaning, currentness, permissible inference, and archaeology. They did **not** universally force every runnable graph to carry a machine-verifiable proof of:

`inherited_function_or_authority -> exact_current_owner -> exact_runtime_binding -> actual_live_consumer`.

As a result, a worker or integration path could satisfy local tests while still assembling its runnable universe through stale paths, omitted adopted modules, wrapper defects, or state/orchestration mismatches.

This is a **composition-enforcement gap**, not evidence that the governing law was absent.

## Counterexample / falsification result

The integration-drift hypothesis is **supported but not universal**.

R6Q is a proven genuine-authority-absence case. The exact unsupported requirement was a current-survey-country position coordinate for WVS7 P2 routing. Existing FQRA semantics exposed `B_COUNTRY` as `ADMINISTRATIVE_METADATA`; no accepted authority allowed it to be promoted to `POSITION_COORDINATE`, and no accepted alternative supplied the exact current survey country. R6Q therefore blocked. R6S later consumed newly adopted HQ authority and passed.

The anti-resurrection index supplies a second class of falsifier: some historical implementations are not disconnected capital waiting to be re-fanned-in; they are explicitly superseded or forbidden as current architecture (for example D15 persistence as universal law, C06 Contract@1, PostgreSQL as canonical semantic owner). Their surviving **function/evidence** may remain relevant while the old implementation must not be revived.
