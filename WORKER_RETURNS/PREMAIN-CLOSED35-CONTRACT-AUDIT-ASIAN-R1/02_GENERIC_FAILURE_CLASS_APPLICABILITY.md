## Adjudication notes

### Decoder representation is a runtime-observation question

The generic R5 codec intentionally distinguishes `int`, `float64_finite`, strings, booleans, and other lanes. Therefore a future package may not construct governed response keys from documentary numeric codes and assume a decoder-emitted numerically equal value will intersect them.

This is most important for W1, but the law is generic across all three waves.

### Lawful representation must survive consumers

W1's reader identity, W4's ordinary `-1` state, and W5's native MR-set identity are not comments. They are governed forms. A consumer that sees the same apparent value or vector but ignores the governing form has changed the claim.

### Risk-set failure is already concrete in W4/W5

W4 and W5 both contain a documentary module-scope statement with no exact source-row membership closure. That is exactly the precondition for `RISK_SET_DECLARED__MEMBERSHIP_PREIMAGE_UNAVAILABLE`.

The correct result is not to manufacture membership. It is to keep the exact source risk set authority-blocked until a qualified documentary successor closes it.

### Shape is not truth

The present realization modules are strong precisely because they do **not** overclaim. Their successful structural shape is evidence that the laws were encoded; it is not evidence that real source execution, source certification, or substantive semantic truth has been proved.
