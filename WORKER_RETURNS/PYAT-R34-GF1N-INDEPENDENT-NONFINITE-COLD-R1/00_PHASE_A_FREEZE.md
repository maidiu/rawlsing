   fabricated result hash/support/signature, and strict JSON for the refusal itself.

   support and role counts, two distinct typed category keys/two cells per row,
   deterministic 64-hex `result_hash`, and `json.dumps(result, sort_keys=True,
   allow_nan=False)` that reparses with **no duplicate object names**.
3. **No untyped crash** at construction or consumption (a raw `TypeError`, `KeyError`
   or non-native `ValueError` is a RED, not a lawful refusal).
