# R28S / 02 — Four scientific routes to historically generated personhood

**Grade:** comparative science and buildable hypotheses, not adopted rules or data grants. Synthetic histories can be genuine simulation-state causes without being recorded human biographies.

## Route 1 — Observed real-person reconstruction

**Claim:** this particular consenting human A acted, encountered B, and changed under named conditions, subject to error and inaccessible experience.
**Required source:** source-owned dated observed acts/non-acts, observations and consent, independent B, exact clock, site/host/purpose, proper scope and ethical release. Inference needs comparison with role/task/selection H0, not just two episodes.
**Function:** authenticated observation adapter → ADP007/015 observed actor and relationship reference → ADP010/019 actual external encounter → independently signed CAN008 RULE_A and CAN011 RULE_T/grant → observed later E2 source → real C9/C10/C12 consumer.
**Output:** qualified reconstruction of actual human histories, never automatically their private psychology.
**Falsifier:** remove witnessed A act or B independent action; same named observed-practice receiving assertion must fail. **Current:** R27S preflight works test-only, no authorized observed dyad or real permission; native HOLD.

## Route 2 — Population/institution-constrained synthetic generation

**Claim:** a new simulated actor is placed within structurally credible objective opportunity, scarcity, institutional and household distributions; NOT that a known survey respondent had this history, motive or belief.
**Required source:** independently governed original survey/item/routing/weight/denominator/risk/comparability, source/host/purpose grant and approved sampling or feasible-set policy, with uncertainty and minority/unknown branches. Conditional probability is not individual destiny nor causal influence.
**Function:** licensed Pyat C0 structural-world producer → finite eligible simulated social positions and resources → ADP007 new sparse actor → independently generated ADP010 opportunities and time; no automatic psyche.
**Output:** generated WorldOpportunitySlice and actor opportunity/capability constraints consumed by C4/C9 E1, later C10. **Falsifier:** permuting a demographic label while holding actual material opportunities and actor histories fixed changes CAN011 practice/belief: illegal population→psyche shortcut. **Current:** source-native Pyat/N11/N12 pending, sourcefree feasibility F1; no full native individual.

## Route 3 — Mechanism-based situated historical generation

**Claim:** a newly created synthetic person actually *performs simulated* history and develops conditional actor-state through a scoped mechanism scientifically justified by research, not a particular original A/B biography.
**Required source:** relevant empirical process/longitudinal/experimental and historical materialist activity evidence and qualified source-use, *specific* issuer-chosen mechanism class, domain/heterogeneity, independent rivals, uncertainty, admissible action types; legal actor/world use. The simulated actor's specific acts are newly generated model events, never externally observed human acts.
**Function:** ADP007 A and separately ADP007/015 B genesis + material opportunities → independently generated simulated B offer/refusal and A performed action → event owner / ADP010 and where applicable ADP019 → CAN008 approved RULE_A → independent CAN011 RULE_T update → later E2 real in-simulation enacted action → actual C9/C10/C12/A0 reader.
**Output:** replayable simulation event ledger and actor-local practice or lawful NO_CHANGE, differentiated other action and meaningful later performance. **Falsifier:** hold E2/world/options fixed, sever actually enacted earlier history or committed target read; claimed later action difference survives → H0 or decorative ledger, NOT PROVED. **Current:** R26V/BP2P4J/R27P test-only carriers; native mechanism/rules not issued, R27P engineering REVISE.

## Route 4 — Hybrid calibrated generation plus independent validation

**Claim:** new synthetic lives under qualified world distributions + independently issued conditional developmental mechanism can exhibit historically situated, partly unpredictable and durable development at a stated statistical/behavioral fidelity; this is not proof of consciousness or of representing a real individual.
**Required source:** both route 2 and route 3 grants, plus independent *held-out* human longitudinal/process or intervention evidence suitable for calibration/validation, predeclared comparison endpoints and uncertainty, negative/nochange frequency, context and subgroup limitations, consent/ethics where prospective.
**Function:** world sampling → full governed simulated multiagent action/history → C9/C12/A0 → withheld external scientific adequacy comparator; validation never supplies synthetic person's original source identity.
**Output:** independently testable simulation trajectories and evidence-bounded fidelity grade; native permission and human validation remain separately required. **Falsifier:** matching population marginals but failing held-out practice/other agency/contradiction/no-change effects, or changes generated only from author script/role labels. **Current:** scientific F1 design, high product-grade proof NOT_RUN.

## Mechanism alternatives: H1/H2/H3/H0

**H1:** historically enacted material practice could condition later action, but two observed or simulated enacted episodes are *not* universal learning threshold; need independent rule-science evidence, matched opportunities, context transfer, contrary/nonperformance and ability/role controls. Actual simulated episodes can serve as *downstream simulated history inputs* under issued law, not as scientific validation of the law itself.
**H2:** independent B can cause or refuse the concrete opportunity, with own owner, prior projects and clock; no compelled reciprocity or new A affection. Remove B act and downstream independent mediation must fail.
**H3:** two enacted competing projects can condition action differently under scarcity; contradiction may remain unresolved, unknown or NO_CHANGE, never obligatory trauma/tension.
**H0:** apparently personalized conduct is fully explained by role, assigned task, cached adult fixture, narration or selection. Existing technical positives do not exclude it. Hold E2 options/world and surface labels fixed and selectively sever actual A history, B action, CAN011 write, later C10 read.

## Evidence-sufficiency science

- **Not enough:** demographic/class/age/religion/Enneagram→psyche mapping; matched group frequency; arbitrary RNG for agency; self-authored practice threshold; one observed A/B sample used as universal law; an actor-specific action invented by an LLM renderer; an actor seeing truth just because the engine knows it.
- **Potentially defensible narrowly:** comparative observational/activity/process evidence from real populations adequate to a *bounded mechanism*, explicit source permissions and adopted rule body with known domain/rivals/uncertainty; qualified structural sampling; separate computational simulation policy; actual internally performed, source-owned synthetic action trajectories and independent other-person histories; same named consumer edge-fault; external validation as warranted.
- **Never infer:** simulated human A is an actual research participant. A simulated performed action is genuine *inside the governed simulator*, not a report that a real person performed it. ADP019 external admission is relevant for external human interaction, while internally generated V3 events have separate causal-event owners. Source permission and scientific adjudication still apply in either case.

## Strong rival argument and limit

R27S may be right that no presently qualified research or original-source method supports a *specific claimed transfer from repeated inspection to durable practice* at required individual fidelity. Cross-sectional data cannot establish it, and two observed participants alone do not establish it either. **That yields a scientific HOLD on H1 at that grain, not a universal requirement that every generated person already exist as an observed A/B dyad.** The least-claiming synthetic first positive may use material conservation, performed project alternatives and typed unknown psyche, then seek narrower longitudinal science.
