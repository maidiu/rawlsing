## Precise scientific inference

- **Correct narrow demand:** If claiming “**these particular real people** A/B performed, met, learned, or changed,” original individual archival/prospective evidence, permission and observed/contact witness are indispensable. Sourcefree fabricated A/B packets cannot earn *that* claim.
- **Conditional synthetic demand:** If claiming “this **new simulated individual** arose under a scientifically **qualified** generative developmental mechanism,” need (a) lawful empirical **population/mechanism** source and license/host/purpose as appropriate; (b) independently accepted mechanism/transition policy with model scope/uncertainty, rival H0 and sensitivity; (c) exact enacted, independently generated **simulation events**, not copied observed acts; (d) separate CAN008/RULE_A, CAN011/RULE_T, ADP actor/event/action and custody authority; (e) actual receiving C9/C10/C12/A0 evidence and human/external validation proportionate to the claim. Neither empirically observed *identical human biographies* nor precise original dyad are logically required by generation. If no qualified general science has been issued, the result remains **TEST_ONLY/scientific HOLD**, even when software runs.
- **Indispensable original empiricism is at the level of justified population/structural and causal generalization and external validation**, not necessarily direct observation of every generated agent's personal life. A simulation trajectory can be **causally real within the represented system**, but is not a historical human life or an empirically observed source datum.

Existing surveys are often observational/cross-sectional and not causal; a simulation generator calibrated to group frequencies might counterfeit real human behavior. This is a **powerful conditional scientific HOLD**, not proof that a unique real A/B must be the simulated actors or that two people suffice to identify a general law.
