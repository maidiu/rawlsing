## Why it cannot be reused for LiTS

FF02-A's positive families prove only:
- exact support;
- exact non-directional dependence;
- exact conditioned redistribution/difference;
- exact source-local joint support.

None proves a mediation route or mediation-input affordance.

Adopted Field Forge R2 explicitly states that coexistence of source/channel/receiver refs does not self-mint a mediation route.

Therefore:

`RELATION_EXISTS + RELATION_CURRENT != MEDIATION_PERMISSION`

The generic authority kind:

`TARGET_USE_PERMISSION`

is an authority category/carrier requirement, not an already-authorized neutral use token.

This court does not invent one.

`MISSING_EXACT_CONSUMER_SPECIFIC_TARGET_USE_PERMISSION_AUTHORITY`

The future competent court must bind:
- exact consumer/contract;
- exact target-use semantic definition;
- exact relation families allowed for that use;
- family-specific admission rule(s);
- exact required source/scope/risk/measure/weight/comparability/temporal prerequisites;

## Packaging law

A single future admission authority container is allowed if all admitted families share the same exact target use and consumer, **but family-specific rule identities/validation clauses remain mandatory**.

Different target uses or consumers require separate admission authority at the changed jurisdiction.
