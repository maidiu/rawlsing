## 2. Relation publication/current-selection is consumer-independent

A Field Forge relation is an actor-independent structural-observatory semantic object owned at the Field Forge grain.

Its existence/currentness need not wait for a downstream PRE-V3 use.

Lawful sequence:

`exact current empirical object closure`
-> `family-specific Field Forge semantic authority`
-> `relation object instantiation`
-> `explicit HQ CURRENT_SELECT at exact Field Forge relation grain`
-> **stop lawfully**

At that stop point:

`PREV3_TARGET_USE_ADMISSION = NONE_CURRENTLY_GRANTED`

This is a valid terminal current state.

The relation may be discoverable/referenceable by exact identity without being usable for any stronger downstream operation.

## 3. Current-selection grants no consumer permission

A relation current-selection receipt may assert only:
- exact relation identity is current at the declared Field Forge jurisdiction/scope;
- exact family/rule authority governs it;
- exact selection purpose is Field Forge relation currentness/publication at the neutral actor-independent relation grain;
- history and predecessor selection, if any, remain immutable.

It may not assert:
- mediation eligibility;
- causal/mechanism use;
- position role;
- geometry if not constitutive;
- objective PRE-V3 producer eligibility;
- actor/V3 use.

## 4. PRE-V3 admission is a later sibling jurisdiction

A PRE-V3 admission receipt is needed only when a named downstream consumer requests an exact operation/use.

That receipt must bind separately:
- current relation ref;
- current-selection ref;
- requested downstream consumer/contract;
- exact target use;
- exact target-use permission authority;
- family-specific admission rule;
- applicable source/risk/measure/comparability/temporal prerequisites;
- applicable negative/denial refs;
- deterministic replay.

No admission receipt is minted merely because a relation is current.

## 6. One authority container versus family-specific rules

A single bounded LiTS authority document is semantically lawful **only as a container**.

Inside it, each positive relation family requires its own exact semantic rule/validation clause because P1 permits a common typed envelope only with family/rule-specific semantic validation.

Thus a lawful HQ adoption may package:
- one LiTS Field Forge relation constitution authority;
- four separately identified family clauses/rules.

It may not package:
- one family-blind `ALLOW_RELATION` rule;
- one generic relation hash whose semantics vary by candidate.

Future PRE-V3 admission has the same constraint.

One admission authority container can cover more than one family only if:
1. the requested downstream use is exactly the same;
2. the consumer/contract is exactly the same;
3. each family has an explicit family-specific rule identity and prerequisites;
4. permission ceilings are not widened.

If those conditions differ, separate admission rules/authorities are required.

## 7. Relation identity excludes lifecycle and downstream permission

At this constitution grain, a relation's semantic identity is determined by its family-specific constitutive semantics, participants/roles/order/cardinality, source/scope/risk/measure material where constitutive, and exact producer semantic/replay ancestry required by the family law.

The following are separately bound and must not be folded into relation proposition identity merely to simplify implementation:
- evidence/authority recertification identity;
- lifecycle/current-selection receipt;
- downstream consumer permission.

That separation preserves lawful recertification and later permission changes without manufacturing false new relation semantics.
