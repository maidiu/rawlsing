## I. The irreducible scientific question

What is the empirically and causally justified relation among (i) materially reproduced conditions and social positions, (ii) a specific person's history of doing, desiring, needing, interpreting and struggling, (iii) independently active other people, (iv) objective events and unequal access, (v) current action or omission, (vi) durable but potentially contradictory organization, (vii) later activity in altered conditions, and (viii) the encounter a human user actually experiences?

This is *not* the request for a universal mind-updating formula. The original Rawlsing ambition was a historically possible person rather than a generic conversational mask. However, its early inferential route from national WVS/Hofstede/colonial indicators through country-level weights to Enneagram types, health and individual persona is **not evidence that a particular person acquired a motive, wound, type or belief**. The supplied `enneagramBatch.js` computes a weighted sum of driver values and **min–max normalizes nine scores**, rather than implementing the earlier conceptual document's softmax probabilities; `(max-min)=0` has no demonstrated handling. Its `driverTypeWeights.json` read path also differs from the supplied `driverTypeWeights.js` filename. These are concrete reasons to retain early code as historical question/hypothesis, not automatically deployable psychology. ## II. What the primary authors actually dispute

These are **selected direct primary text reads** from mounted files, not full-book proofs or a claim of author consensus. PDF page locators below refer to physical PDF extraction pages; printed-page numbering differs. Text and original languages should be consulted for nuance.

**Leontiev — activity is not a scalar need-to-personality transfer.** In *Actividade, Consciência e Personalidade* (Portuguese MIA translation, PDF approximately pp. 9–16, later activity/needs discussion), needs do not simply command the individual's activity in isolation; object-directed socially formed activity, motives, actions and operations require differentiation. **Scientific challenge:** is apparent new need actually an existing need finding a different object, a new motive configuration, a changed operation, or an outside actor rearranging opportunity? S35's `REQUEST_TOOL` gives evidence of an immediate performed action, not which motive developed. A Leontiev-compatible program would preserve object/goal/operation and historical activity separately.

**Sève — personhood as the historically ordered reproduction of acts and capacities.** *Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality*, Part III and Part IV §2 (English translation, PDF pp. 184ff and 337ff), explicitly calls personality a system of social relations between acts. At PDF p. 337 the argument takes acts and capacities in a reciprocal relation: capacity enables acts, acts may produce capacities; `use-time` and biography shape what is reproducible. He expressly does **not** rank an abstract `need` as the primary concept there. **Rival to a need-first uniform engine:** reconstruct actual allocations of time, social relations and the production/consumption of capacities. A person might become *better* at an activity because they have time and support, not because a latent trait was increased.

**Lahire — a person can carry incompatible histories that different contexts recruit or inhibit.** *The Plural Actor* Act I scenes 1–3 (PDF pp. 36–39, 71–78; particularly `Conditional dispositions` and `The negative power of the context: inhibition and latency`). Past incorporation does not entail global manifestation, cross-context transfer, or a homogeneous habitus. The present actively selects, inhibits and sometimes provokes mismatched dispositions. **Test:** compare near-identical contexts with different triggers; compare the *same actor* across household/work/community contexts with matched opportunities. Missing current behavior may be inhibition, not lost competence. Rival to a single coherent long-run psyche update.

**Dreier — a person lives by participating across places and other people's ongoing practices.** *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life*, early theory chapters PDF pp. 38–40, and Chapter 8 `Conduct of Everyday Life and the Life Trajectory` approximately PDF p. 197ff. Changes that work in one room do not become effective unless integrated into everyday conduct across places; chains of action can be continued by *different* people in other contexts. **Test:** a support intervention with no later participation outside the protected context versus real practical reorganization at home/work, tracing whether the focal actor or a coactor performed the decisive step. A one-event intrapsychic learning rule cannot substitute for this.

**Archer — structural conditioning does not exhaust actors' reflexive projects.** *Structure, Agency and the Internal Conversation* (2003), introduction PDF p. 26 and concerns discussion PDF p. 133; three reflexive patterns are examined in the empirical chapters. Archer treats internal deliberation over personally important concerns as a candidate *causally efficacious mediator*, not just an after-action rationalization. **Competition:** activity/condition-first versus relatively autonomous reflexive selection of projects. Need actual actor-accessible deliberation or multiple time-separated deliberation/action traces; do not infer private conversation from rendered dialogue. Her book's interview evidence supports a theoretical mechanism, not a numerical rule for native actors.

**Holzkamp — practical control over life conditions, not conformity or happiness, is central.** *Grundlegung der Psychologie* (German supplied text), chapters 6–9 and the passages on `restriktive` versus `verallgemeinerte Handlungsfähigkeit`. A locally effective survival tactic may reproduce subordination, whereas cooperative extension of control opens possibilities; subjective reasons have to be studied from the participants' standpoint. **Competing account:** apparent 'adaptive success' in meeting a local goal can actually narrow collective effective action. Need actual material power, available alternatives and the subject's own reasons, not researcher-moralized labels. The German source was text-searched and selected arguments read; no full translation completed.

**Luria — social practices, literacy and experimental form alter the available cognitive operations.** *Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations* (1976 English), preface PDF pp. 7–9 and problem/method pp. 25–36; case studies in generalization and inference. The 1931–32 Uzbekistan/Kirghizia study compares participants facing rapid changes in schooling, work and collective economy. **Scientific strength:** the same task is not culturally innocent, and schooling changes kinds of symbolic performance. **Critical limit:** Luria's preface frames Islam and rural Central Asian life through Soviet modernization assumptions; field comparisons are entangled with coercive colonial/state transformations and schooling selection. Do not translate a change in elicited formal reasoning into deficient humanity or a universal developmental rank.

**Kohn/Schooler, Kohn/Słomczyński — reciprocal work/person effects, not one-way class predestination.** *Work and Personality* (1983) chs. 3, 5–6, PDF pp. 77ff, and *Social Structure and Self-Direction* (1990) chs. 2, 5–6, PDF pp. 40, 149ff. Job substantive complexity/autonomy/supervision correlate with self-direction and cognitive flexibility; the authors directly engage occupational selection and reciprocal causation. The 1990 text flags identification difficulty **even with longitudinal data**, and comparability of survey meaning across US and Poland. **Test:** measure job selection, prior cognitive disposition, changing tasks, and work exposure, with directional lag. Neither a survey country's class mean nor an actor's occupation suffices to issue individual personality.

**Elder and Mortimer — historical timing, cohort, family, school and work quality distinguish lives.** Elder, *Children of the Great Depression* (1974), opening chapters and family/household economic adaptations (PDF pp. 44, 54ff): macroeconomic deprivation changes household labor roles, child participation and family relations, with differences by class, cohort and family arrangements. Mortimer, *Working and Growing Up in America* (2003), Youth Development Study methods and chapters on work quality, selection and adult transitions (PDF pp. 49, 64, 139): hours of paid work alone do not mean identical development; job quality, gender constraints, parental permission and selection matter. **Test:** same exogenous shock, different age/cohort/family obligations; same hours worked, different degree of control, safety, instruction and forced labor.

**Wright — class must be modeled relationally, including contradictory locations.** *Classes* (1985/2023 supplied edition), chs. 2–3, 5–7: positions in exploitation and authority relations are not interchangeable with income rank. A manager may exercise power over labor and remain subordinate to capital. **Test:** same income, different control over others' labor and assets; model objective relations and lived participation separately. No permission to infer automatic political beliefs from a class coordinate.

**Social reproduction theory — labor and care outside waged production are constitutive.** Bhattacharya (ed.), *Social Reproduction Theory* (2017), introduction, Fraser, Ferguson and labor-power chapters: the continual making of labor power depends on households, schools, care, health and often racialized/gendered unpaid labor. *Making Death and Life in Palestine* (Bhattacharya/Ferguson eds., 2025), AbuMezied and other contributions (PDF pp. 24–25 and opening essays) applies that analysis to colonial interference with food, education, care and continuity. **Test:** identical nominal wage but different caregiving burdens, closures, checkpoints, trauma, outside institutional support and reciprocal practical care. Actors are not isolated utility optimizers. The edited books contain **multiple authors** and disagreement; do not speak of them as one self-consistent causal theory.

### Productive non-equivalences

- **Sève versus Leontiev:** the analytical primacy of acts/capacity/time versus motive/object/activity; both materialist, not reducible to one universal variable. Compare when expanded use-time without changed articulated motive predicts acquisition, and when altered motive reallocates practice without immediate competence change.
- **Lahire versus an integrated trait/person kernel:** dormant, contradictory context-conditioned repertoires versus coherent dominant dispositions. Compare cross-context switches without durable mutations.
- **Archer versus a purely situation-elicited action:** deliberated concerns may reorient conduct even without immediate material opportunity changes; rival accounts must hold actor's reasons and causally prior opportunity separately.
- **Holzkamp versus local adaptation and Sorok 24 flourishing:** successful compliance can preserve domination; reciprocally chosen dependence can expand agency. Need practical condition/control evidence and normativity explicitly declared, not 'health' inferred from cultural harmony.
- **Kohn/Schooler versus crude structural determinism:** changes in work may alter orientations, but prior orientations also affect job selection; longitudinal evidence itself has limits.
- **Luria versus ahistorical culture vectors:** observed task performance may reflect education, language, inferential norm, social transformation or an instrument's demand, not innate cognitive rank.

These are competing explanatory mechanisms. **PROGRAMMA must preserve their oppositions and falsifiers**, not settle them by rhetorical synthesis or put all under `middle_state_update`.

## IV. Scientific distinction / status requirements for ONE PROGRAMMA reference

Represent **different scientific things**, not a universal `psychological_delta`:

1. **Objective condition and power**: ownership/class/control, wage and unpaid care, time budget, bodily capability, material affordance, institutional constraint, historical and group positions, with source and applicability.
2. **Independent world/coactor**: separately constituted B actor/history/obligations/performances and their own clock; no possession of B's interiority by A or the narrator.
3. **Actor-life state**: produced needs, actual practical objects/projects, acquired capacities, prior acts/uses, multiple conditional dispositions, internal deliberations (where evidenced), contradictions, relationships; source/authority per owner.
4. **Temporal event and epistemic view**: world E occurred; actor A exposed; A *could* access; A *actually* received/understood; A acted or omitted; narration revealed some part to user; these are separately testable.
5. **Response versus durable change**: Rule A/evaluated immediate effect; possible separate Rule T and exact target owner; actual practice/expectation/memory/relation change **or** genuinely evaluated `NO_CHANGE`, independently distinguished from `MISSING_RULE`, unknown, denied/forged, mere absence and deferred.
6. **Subsequent user-facing consumer**: later *A-performed* E2 or real omission, versioned C9/C12 cold replay, user lawful observation, actor-limited V2 rendering and eventual human test.

Each conceptual claim node needs an *argument and evidence-level*, a genuine **opposes / requires / would-discriminate-by** relation, the **actual source/first consumer or MISSING**, and **owner/jurisdiction**. Time must carry when an event happened, when it became accessible, when a claim was adopted, when a rule was effective, and which C12 fork/branch observed it. Do not confuse these times. Keep statuses orthogonal: source READ/TITLE_ONLY/MISSING; conceptual PROPOSED/ADOPTED_IN_JURISDICTION/CONTESTED; engineering SOURCE_PRESENT/AUTHOR_TESTED/INDEPENDENT_SCOPED/CONSUMED; native OWNER_ISSUED/HELD; human NOT_RUN/OBSERVED. A scientific disagreement may stay OPEN while code is sourcefree-tested; a scientifically lawful `NO_CHANGE` can be completed without being 'failed'. Inverse traversal is compulsory.

**Three falsifiers of this proposed reference design:** (F1) if a later independent event with no actor access mutates A's knowledge/practice, the alleged edge distinctions are broken; (F2) if identical public `HELP_REFUSE` speech conflates B refusal with A resource exhaustion, its causal provenance is lost; (F3) if an acknowledged source change affects no actual first consumer or regrade/recompute event, this is an indexed archive rather than a living reference. Additional severe falsifier: if an actor's warm compliance is treated as improved flourishing despite documented loss of exit/material options, the system has smuggled cultural conformity into an unexamined moral score.
