## Traversal 3 — Same opportunity, act versus omission

**Source identity:** P1 D omit and D perform both bind B `SHARE_TOOL` at clock 5; one path calls `JoinedSession.start(...perform="omit")`, the other `perform="e2"`. `p1_consumer.py` explicitly records omission with one tool remaining and `HELP_ACCEPT`; E2 independent inspection exhausts the tool and yields `HELP_REFUSE`. The positive state is not a skill test: its tools are preset synthetic test resources and the actor mode is chosen by the test.

**Forward:** independent B opportunity → A access → (A own E2 performed or omitted) → inventory/time consequence → later response; **reverse:** from a changed later `do_help`, trace to `ATTEND_OBJECT` or `OMIT` and their actual performed-witness. The absence of performance is not automatically `A lacked the skill`; negative evidence requires a denominator of *real opportunities* that were accessible, feasible, permissible and recorded.

**Scientific rivals:** Sève: `use-time` and actual performance matter for capacity production; Lahire: nonperformance can be contextual inhibition of a prior disposition; Leontiev: goal and object matter, not bare muscular act; Mortimer: quality and constraints of a work episode distinguish learning from merely being assigned a task; Dreier: coactor may scaffold an action and make the focal actor look skilled. Under a durable acquired-practice hypothesis, later unassisted comparable action should change after actual rehearsal and not only after a tool appears. Under H0 preexisting competence, baseline matched opportunities may already show the skill. Under H2 external help, remove the helper and competence disappears. Under H3 current motivation, the skill stays available but is not enacted.

**Discriminators:** collect (a) earlier successful/failed and *unattempted* comparable acts, (b) actor-independent outcome witnesses, (c) who did the decisive work, (d) matched follow-up opportunities, (e) cross-context but valid transfer scope, (f) negative cases that actually could have elicited the action. P1 proves that its software separates a performed flag from omission; **it does not conduct this longitudinal empirical test**.

**Critical split:** CAN008 `RESPONSE_EFFECTS_VALID_EMPTY` or documented actor receivable/no observed uptake are not CAN011 `NO_CHANGE` decisions by themselves. CAN011 `NO_CHANGE` is valid only where an accepted rule was evaluated with adequate premises. A missing T rule is `MIDDLE_STATE_UPDATE_RULE_MISSING`, not a scored no-effect; unknown witness is not a positive. S35's sourcefree T=`NO_CHANGE` is an explicit *nonissuing scientific proposal*, not an accepted native T evaluator. **Missing exact edge:** original C9R actor-access/current-action input bridge and original Rule A/T issuer/target grant. **First test consumer:** C9R `candidate.pipeline.run_candidate_path → target_rules.evaluate_target → candidate.later_action.read_target_for_later_action → action_admission.admit_performed_action` documented by S35; P1's actual standalone `p1_s35.evaluate` **does not install** this rule into C9R.

## Traversal 4 — Increasing attachment while life narrows

**Source:** `SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/24_COURT10_FLOURISHING_MORAL_PHYSICS_R1.md` (OPEN, NOT ADOPTED); `08_INTERACTION_SYSTEM_SYNTHESIS_R1.md` §9 and `101_BINDING_SURFACE...md` §§4–10, owner cut `865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57`. Holzkamp *Grundlegung* chs. 6–9 (restricted/generalized capacity for action); Wright *Classes* relational control; Bhattacharya social reproduction; 2025 Palestine volume care/colonial infrastructure. **This is a proposed phenomenon, not an executed P1 trace.**

**Scenario:** A's user provides scarce transport, food or institutional access. A's expressed affection and reliance on the user grow; contact with family/community, capacity to leave, control over work and independent access to material supports decline. The same facts could also describe *noncoercive chosen care* during illness or temporary grief if outside options are not being sabotaged and the recipient gains room to act. Sorok Court10 explicitly disallows collapsing affection, happiness, dependence, compliance and flourishing.

**Forward scientific traversals:** material shortage and labor/care structure → independent people's work/obligations → user performed aid and conditions → relationship changes, alternative-network changes, time budget and practical capability → actor access/appraisal and current behavior → later opportunities and user account. Four explanatory paths may yield similar affectionate speech:
- **E1 enabling care:** temporary asymmetry increases the recipient's future practical options.
- **E2 coercive capture:** supporter controls bottleneck and recipient loses alternatives while expressing warmth.
- **E3 structural deprivation:** external institution closes, not the user; attachment compensates without user causation.
- **E4 culturally meaningful interdependence:** independence as a sole Western autonomy measure misreads intentional reciprocal obligation; material agency and free exit still demand examination.

**Reverse:** from an affectionate statement traverse to objective social-network/resource changes, *not* directly to `high health` or `user success`. Ask for what and whose labor, who is free to refuse, whether support is conditional, whether third parties are cut off, and whose scope of practice expands. A normative claim should record standpoint and operational alternatives. Holzkamp's distinction supplies a candidate evaluative interrogation, *not* a universally issued moral algorithm.

**Competing test:** matched intervention vs no intervention, outside network continuity, actual changes in alternatives/exit, actor testimony and independent objective conditions; short versus long horizon; possible reactive affection without flourishing. No sourcefree P1 case exercises network capture; no actual A5 data. **Broken links:** no native multi-person care reproduction, no issued flourishing dimension/causal attribution, no participant ethics result. **Consequences:** avoid a retention/affection maximizer; allow caring refusal, absence and opaque relations without grading people by obedience.

## Traversal 5 — Apparent durable learning versus historical selection and context

**Sources:** CAN011 original `AUTHORITY.md` at `25830fe7462bb9e29bc4087e6ae14f3f84cec377`; CAN008 original authority same cut; S35 @`888ccae5`, P1 `candidate/p1_s35.py` @`b65c75`; Sève Part IV §2, Lahire Act I Scene 2, Kohn/Schooler 1983 longitudinal, Dreier chapter 8, Archer internal conversation.

**Observed claim to adjudicate:** A performs two inspections, later does another. A naive engine creates `V3MS_PRACTICE(coordinated_material_inspection)` at second performance and later 'recruits' it. That is an executable story, but it leaves six distinct causes live: (1) genuine newly acquired organization; (2) old competence plus improved tool; (3) third party scaffolds; (4) same action externally compelled by workplace role; (5) conditionally available disposition becomes active; (6) the person deliberates toward a different project without change of competence. Similarly the subsequent action might be a coached repetition and not transferable learning. A true person could both acquire a practice and remain uncertain about its motive.

**What original authority has:** CAN011 represents the *kind* and admissible transition operations; existing sourcefree C9R can store and later read a target. No concrete positive T issuance was located in read cuts. S35 explicitly chooses targetless T `NO_CHANGE` because H0 is not excluded, and P1 actual `p1_s35.evaluate` unconditionally preserves that state. No absence of state alone is evidence of a permanent inability.

**Competing predicted footprints:**
- **Sève / act-capacity formation:** sustained independent successful acts change later ability when comparable necessary means exist, with actual use-time and costs.
- **Leontiev / activity-object reorganization:** changes in motive/object relation predict *which* practices are sought, even before or independently from technical proficiency.
- **Lahire / conditional dispositions:** context changes produce reversals with no new durable underlying practice; the same person may retain opposite dispositions.
- **Archer / reflexive project:** changed prioritized concerns predict altered action after deliberation despite no changed material skill or stock.
- **Dreier / socially situated participation:** capacity is locally scaffolded and may fail to travel when practical places, people or access differ.
- **Kohn/Schooler reciprocal occupation effect:** work conditions and prior orientations jointly select/shape later performance, requiring longitudinal controls.

**Finite discriminating design (proposal, no run):** independently establish a pre-event baseline (including nonperformances with opportunity), exact tool/coactor inputs, repeated independent attempts and their quality, longer follow-up without helper, comparable but not identical context, recorded actor knowledge/intent only when accessible, and counterfactual matched exposure. Distinguish capacity improvement from opportunity, preference, assignment and observed output. Repeat over a meaningful calendar; use recorded causal history, not guessed latent scores. Positive CREATE must cite concrete competent Rule T + actor-performed history + tested scope. Valid `NO_CHANGE` requires a rule evaluation; `MISSING_RULE` means no judgment can lawfully be issued. Event occurrence/response, scientific decision, ledger mutation and later independent E2 are separate gates.

**Inverse demand:** from a claimed `V3MS_PRACTICE` traverse backwards to the actual CAN011 rule/version/hash, baseline history, independent action witnesses, target owner, first later consuming action and severance test. If any is absent, mark **PARTIAL or HELD**, not a completed learning chain. From an apparent absent practice traverse to real negative evidence or unknown, not to assumed no-learning. **Broken links:** original positive T and source rights, effective version/actor-time semantics, native C9/C12 external custody, verified sustained person-specific transfer, native whole-person user experience. **Human consequence:** a person who learns only when the social conditions support it must not become globally 'more self-directed' after the renderer has narrated a clever success.

## A falsifier for this W1-B mapping itself

Suppose an independently governed actor study with full opportunity, coactor, material and longitudinal histories establishes that a *single* stable disposition with one transition rule predicts individual behavior better than all of the contextual/act-capacity/reflexive models, including held-out changes in work, family, encounter and time. That would undercut this researcher's provisional expectation that a heterogeneous repertoire is indispensable in every person. PROGRAMMA should be able to record the opposing evidence and revise the explanatory ranking without deleting the historical arguments, changing original owner authority by implication, or rewriting the actual test receipts.

Conversely, a live graph that cannot distinguish `MISSING_RULE` from `NO_CHANGE`, or a public reply that cannot be causally traced to different material situations, already fails its stated navigational scientific function **before** any scientific school is selected.
