# Field Forge input-grain disposition

## Holding

`FIELD_FORGE_INPUT_GRAIN = CLAIM_FAMILY_AND_AUTHORITY_DEPENDENT`

Neither universal answer is lawful.

### Direct exact empirical-object ingress is lawful when

A current Field Forge producer is explicitly defined over exact I03/I05 producer objects and itself owns the semantic transformation being performed, with no missing validation/comparability/composition premise. GT12 conditional support is the precedent: producer-owned exact empirical truth can become a Field Forge-owned actor-independent relation without inventing an intermediate universal learned-release wrapper.

### Validated/accepted learned-relation ingress is required when

The intended structural claim depends on a distinct validation, model-selection, comparability, composition, higher-order or accepted-relation judgment that I05 does not own. In that case, bypassing the learned relation would collapse:

`DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE != VALIDATED_RELATION != STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION`.

### Therefore

Field Forge may see:
- raw/exact I03/I05 objects for claim families whose current producer contract lawfully consumes them;
- validated/accepted learned relation objects for claim families whose authority requires that intermediate judgment.

It must never receive a discovery receipt and silently infer the missing validation/comparability authority itself.

Positioned-subject possibility remains a separate service and must not be conflated with V3 actor realization.
