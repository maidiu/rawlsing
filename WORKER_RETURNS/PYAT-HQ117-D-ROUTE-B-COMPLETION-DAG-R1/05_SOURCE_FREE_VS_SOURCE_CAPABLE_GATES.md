# Source-free vs source-capable gates

## Source-free

The following can and should be built/proved before touching real source payloads:

- thin compositor orchestration;
- exact object-graph export/dereference manifest;
- owner separation;
- deterministic ExactRef/replay closure;
- typed negative/unknown/noncomparable/partial propagation;
- discovery-frontier/completeness contract propagation;
- lifecycle/release/currentness wiring to the already-current owner;
- fail-closed absence of validation/comparability/composition authority;
- activation interfaces for adaptive discovery and positioned-subject possibility;
- Field Forge ingress dispatch by explicit claim-family authority.

Fixtures may prove mechanics, not source truth.

## Source-capable

Ultimately required for:

- physical decoder equivalence on named source formats;
- source-specific projection correctness;
- routing/applicability/risk-set/cohort/branch replay;
- source-specific weight/design meaning where used;
- real I03/I05 query/discovery truth;
- exact-negative completeness under actual failure paths;
- named-source validation/comparability claims;
- cross-source/joint composition when claimed;
- population coverage;
- end-to-end source→learned-product→Field Forge proof.

A typed authority blocker can be a correct source-capable result. “Green” does not mean forcing every specimen to positive support.
