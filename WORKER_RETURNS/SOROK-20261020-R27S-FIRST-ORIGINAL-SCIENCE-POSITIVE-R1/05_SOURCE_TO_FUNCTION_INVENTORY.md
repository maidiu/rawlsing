## Actual engineering gap versus original scientific gap

**Engineering gap.** A complete V3 historical actor/person generator is still missing. The C9R/BP2P4J chain can consume an actor-shaped source, but it cannot generate a real actor's developmental history. PA0 found no `GeneratedPersonRecord`/`DevelopmentalEventLedger` producer. R26V's sourcefree producer is a bounded technical starting point, not the missing native engine.

**Original scientific gap.** Even if the engine existed, it would have no lawful A/B history to consume. The exact missing empirical artifact is a current authorized longitudinal dyadic A/B record (or lawful equivalent) with independently performed A acts, matched nonperformance, a contrary commitment, B's independent acts, a real contact, an E1 and a later E2. A cross-sectional survey cannot supply it without demographic psychogenesis.

**Issuance gap.** Even with the source, no concrete CAN008 RULE_A and no separate CAN011 RULE_T/grant exist. The adopted R2/CAN011 texts are semantic permissions, not concrete issued rules.

**Custody gap.** C8 production trust and C12 protected custody remain held. R26E repaired a C12 verifier in an owned derivative, but raw SQLite deletion and same-user key/witness compromise remain outside a production trust boundary.
