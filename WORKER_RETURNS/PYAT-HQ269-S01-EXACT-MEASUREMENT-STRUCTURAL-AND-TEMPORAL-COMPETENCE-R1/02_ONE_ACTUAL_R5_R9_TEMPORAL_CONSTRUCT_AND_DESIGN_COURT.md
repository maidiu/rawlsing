## 2. The exact selected wording and native response codes

**R5 merged release — native `Q7`,** official merged R5 codebook PDF p. 12 (printed PDF p. index 11), extracted lines 521–528: “What about the overall direction of the country? Would you say that the country is going in the wrong direction or going in the right direction?” Values **`1=going in wrong direction; 2=going in right direction; 9=Don't know; 998=Refused to answer; -1=Missing`**. Neither zero nor 8 is a substantive response code in this R5 codebook record. This is a **merged codebook**, not yet a positive proof of country-original R5 Uganda instrument/translation/administration identity.

**R9 Uganda national original — native `Q3`,** indigenized questionnaire PDF p. 6 (zero-index 5), lines 235–240: longer balanced neutralizing prompt asks each respondent their general view of current direction, then explicitly whether “wrong direction” or “right direction”; `1=wrong; 2=right; 9=Don't know [not read]`. The Uganda-specific **R9 codebook** PDF p. 8 (zero-index 7), lines 344–353, gives actual released states **`1=wrong; 2=right; 8=Refused; 9=Don't know; -1=Missing`**. The specific R9 original questionnaire excerpt instructs DK *not read*; refusal and other missing are handled by questionnaire global routing/codebook, not an inferred substantive response. **Crosswave trap:** refusal is `998` in R5 merged, `8` in R9 Uganda; both are *non-substantive typed states*, not numerically comparable observations, zeros, or negative attitudes. R5→R9 national prompt is substantively near-identical at the two response poles but **not literally identical** in the lead-in; future comparability authority must decide whether the balanced R9 introduction changes administration and potential framing effect.

## 3. Actual shared target population and oversampling — decisive boundary

**Uganda R5**: Afrobarometer national 2012 summary at https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/uganda-round-5-summary-of-results/ and original technical form within https://www.afrobarometer.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/uga_r5_sor.pdf : fieldwork **2 December 2011–27 February 2012**, **national probability sample n=2,400 adult Ugandan citizens age ≥18, excluding institutional population**, interviewed face-to-face in selected languages. Region × urban/rural strata, 2002 census with projections for EA frame, **300 EAs selected PPPS**, eight households per EA, random-walk selection/individual household selection with gender quota. **Additional 3,800** surveys administered in USAID Uganda Mission focus districts, **6,200 total interviews** in the extended study. Official national summary **uses only the 2,400**. Unless exact local merged SAV coverage shows otherwise, a national temporal denominator **MUST NOT automatically include the 3,800 targeted extra**.

**Uganda R9**: official 2022 country summary https://www.afrobarometer.org/publication/uganda-round-9-summary-of-results/ : Hatchile Consult Ltd. fieldwork **7–25 January 2022**; a distinct **nationally representative stratified probability n=2,400 adults**, PLUS **992 special additional respondents** allocated across ten new city administrations (Arua, Gulu, Lira, Soroti, Mbale, Jinja, Entebbe, Mbarara, Fortportal, Hoima). Official released Uganda R9 codebook lists respondent identifiers `UGA0001–UGA3392`, consistent with **3,392 total**; that does **not** authorize every record for the national estimate. A national R9 estimand requires identifying the **2,400** national sample in the actual release and excluding or formally accounting for the 992 urban supplemental sample under a documented sample-inclusion/weight design.

**Proposed exact shared population (subject to R9 technical form):** independently sampled noninstitutional Ugandan adult citizens aged 18+ living in the Uganda national target frame at **the two separate field periods**. This is **repeated cross-section**, never longitudinal trajectories of actual people. The country scope is non-negotiable: R5 and R9 are changing multinational merges; a whole-merge temporal difference, or an all-Uganda 6,200-versus-3,392 difference, does not have a common probability population. Changes to regional enumeration, urbanicity definition, newly created cities, census projections, local translation/field protocols and eligibility require bounded reconciliation and limitation statements.

## 4. Proposed scientifically testable estimand, not a measured result

Let `S_w=the original nationally representative Uganda sub-sample in wave w` after exact country/baseline-vs-extra selection and validity assessment. For a **predeclared complete substantive response-risk denominator**, `p_w = Pr(response=2 | response in {1,2}, S_w)`; propose **`Δ = p_R9 - p_R5`** as the *change in the nationally sampled Uganda adult citizens' survey-reported appraisal that the country is going in the right direction*, not a change in objective performance and not a panel transition. Alternatively one may specify `Pr(response=2|all eligible sampled)` but that is a **different estimand** and cannot be swapped after observing missingness. Preserve the full observed proportions of `DK`, `refused`, `missing` by wave and country as source-native states; specify treatment of item nonresponse and whether original national weights address survey inclusion/response, including any interaction with supplemental strata.

The estimator must establish **actual exact R5/R9 national sample inclusion flag, actual comparable national population/eligibility, original R5 vs R9 design/PSU/stratum/region, source-local weights and release versions**. Do not transplant one wave's weight or naively average respondent-level rows from independent waves. Variance `Var(Δ)` must be based on each wave's genuine multi-stage stratification/clustering/design and their cross-wave sampling relationship (independent repeat samples only if verified), with appropriate design-adjusted SE/CI, unweighted sensitivity as lawful, sample sizes and **uncertainty**. Official single-wave “±2 percentage points” is **not** a CI for the difference nor a guarantee for a subgroup or this item. Nonresponse differences, survey-mode/language, preamble wording and national frame variation need explicit handling. No estimate, sign, precision or result is produced in this commission.
