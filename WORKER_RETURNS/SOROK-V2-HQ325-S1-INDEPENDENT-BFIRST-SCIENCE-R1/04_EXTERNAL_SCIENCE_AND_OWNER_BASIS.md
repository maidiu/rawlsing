# S1 scientific evidence and source-of-authority boundaries

**Review date:** 17 October 2026. External metadata and abstracts reviewed separately from private code. Evidence supports **group tendencies / plausible design constraints**, not individual conditional-effect transition parameters.

## Primary research verification

- **Doebel, S. (2020)**, *Rethinking Executive Function and Its Development*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32348707/ — theoretical critical review of EF as goal-serving skills. Supports D1's task/activity-specific approach; does not validate any C03/C07 algorithm or individual endpoint.
- **Callaghan et al. (2005)**, *Synchrony in the Onset of Mental-State Reasoning: Evidence From Five Cultures*. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.0956-7976.2005.01544.x — standardized false-belief task comparison Canada, India, Peru, Samoa, Thailand, approximately five-year population milestone. It is scientifically wrong to claim there are *no developmental regularities*; it is also wrong to impose automatic individual age-five understanding.
- **Wellman et al. (2006)**, *Scaling of Theory-of-Mind Understandings in Chinese Children*. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01830.x — Chinese and English-speaking samples, some common ordering and sociocultural variation. Not a national-stage classifier.
- **Nelson & Fivush (2004)**, *The Emergence of Autobiographical Memory*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15065919/ — multicomponent gradual development via language, self, others, narrative and memory talk. Cannot authorize an explicit childhood recollection from analyst history.
- **Fivush (2011)**, *The Development of Autobiographical Memory*. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.121208.131702 — childhood/adolescent and cultural/social variability; explicit narration is not all retained causation.
- **Morris et al. (2007)**, *The Role of the Family Context in the Development of Emotion Regulation*. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2743505/ — review of relational family associations, no particular caregiver's motive or causal deterministic response.
- **Rogoff et al. (2003)**, *Firsthand Learning Through Intent Participation*. https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.psych.54.101601.145118 — culturally conditioned practical contribution through observing and participating in shared tasks. School/literacy-age conventional achievement ≠ universal competence.
- **Gardner & Steinberg (2005)**, *Peer Influence on Risk Taking, Risk Preference, and Risky Decision Making*. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16060809/ — peer-present risk decision experiment (n=306). This is **not** evidence of universal acceptance of a particular ideological claim or racial stereotype.
- **Crone & Dahl (2012)**, *Understanding Adolescence as a Period of Social–Affective Engagement and Goal Flexibility*. https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3313 — model/review; no mandatory romance or risk.
- **Henrich, Heine & Norenzayan (2010)**, *The Weirdest People in the World?* https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X0999152X — external-validity critique; not a substitute for original data or a positive ability measure.

## Scientific limitations in source-to-rule transfer

A cognitive ability measured in a lab is neither equivalent to a named activity in a generated historical world nor an independent chance of endorsing an ideology. Parent socialization associations do not generate a caregiver motive. Peer risk-lab findings do not assign every adolescent conformity. Cultural differences need documented exposure/activity paths, never nation→trait edges. No reviewed study supplies joint transition distributions for theft plus caregiver utterance plus peer group blame plus later attraction. Even seemingly lawful conditional capabilities should be proposed as scenario constraints with observed witnesses and no occult status at birth.
