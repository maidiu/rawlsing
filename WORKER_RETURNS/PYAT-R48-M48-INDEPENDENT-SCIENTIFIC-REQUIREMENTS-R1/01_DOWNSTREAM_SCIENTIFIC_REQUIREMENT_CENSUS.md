Three terminal destinations are noncollapsible: empirical discovery/analytical service, empirical positioned-subject possibility, and structural-field production [CHETIRI]. Five lifecycles matter: discovery build, generated-person runtime, empirical inference, analysis, audit/replay. Current lack of a hot-loop reader cannot retire a required capability. Nor does a requirement prove an implementation already exists.

Exact consumer meaning must survive: E04 formal eligibility and modeled affordability are different; empirical cell balances describe a source risk set, not motivation, behavior, route selection, or an individual's propensity [E04_CODE]. N10E can supply evidence without F5 structural admission. N9 may expose an admitted objective field without authorizing F6 transport. N10W progressive conditioning must retain all alternatives and specificity loss. N11 accounting cannot promote TEST_ONLY, historical receipts, or valid nulls into unearned original measurement.

Future requirement beyond proven APIs: lawful deeper/multiresponse/repeated/ranked joints; response-to-position and response-to-response directions; joint reverse conditioning; independent uncertainty/validation at survey-design grain; source-distinct historical rebinding. Require a representability and real-consumer test per operation class before claiming these complete. Preserve unknown or unsupported classes as build obligations, not scientifically impossible classes.
