## What "successor" was intended to mean

A lawful successor preserves:

- exact producer ownership and stable object identity;
- source/universe/respondent/risk-set/topology/measure/authority distinctions;
- direct reference reachability rather than mandatory courier copying;
- typed negative and unresolved states;
- distinct empirical fact, learned relation, structural admission, structural prior, positioned-subject possibility and actor-state jurisdictions;
- immutable release/replay/provenance identity;
- selective invalidation/recompilation by dependency closure;
- PRE-V3/V3 causal separation.

A lawful successor may change:

- stage names;
- file/table shapes;
- physical co-packaging;
- cache/index strategy;
- whether an intermediate empirical representation is persistent, transient or rebuilt;
- whether logical jurisdictions are crossed by serial files or direct DAG references.

A lawful successor may **not** use physical simplification to erase authority or semantic distinctions.
