## Meaning and limit

This is a source/operation conservation oracle, not a claim that every
mathematical variable pair is scientifically meaningful. `PERMITTED` is zero:
the accepted authority has no current signed source right or E_pre for any of the
35 sources. A producer may be `PRODUCER_PENDING` without being wrong; it may not
silently upgrade HOLD to PERMITTED, drop an item, substitute same-cardinality
work, or deny an unvisited operation. Historical LiTS-I and GSS positives are
immutable evidence at their own grain, never a current all35 prospective proof.
