# FIABC-B05 — Theory Falsification Result

## Hypothesis under test

> Recent P2X blockers disproportionately reflect executable succession failure—accepted functions or authority not being consumed by the current execution graph—rather than absent semantic design.

## Verdict

**SUPPORTED AS A DISPROPORTIONATE EXPLANATION; FALSIFIED AS A UNIVERSAL EXPLANATION.**

This is deliberately two-part.

### Why the hypothesis survives

The 22-row diagnostic ledger is a causal evidence set, not a random sample. Within it, 17 episodes are not `GENUINELY_MISSING`:

- `PARTIAL_SUCCESSION`: 9
- `PRESERVED_BUT_DISCONNECTED`: 5
- `AUTHORITY_EXISTS_RUNTIME_INTEGRATION_MISSING`: 2
- `SUPERSEDED_IMPLEMENTATION_FUNCTION_PRESERVED`: 1

The strongest cases require no interpretive stretching:

- F1 proved 14 WVS semantic requirements already existed and needed only PRE553 issuance/binding.
- The AS2A blocker was an exact wrong-path reference to already-existing accepted blobs.
- P2X measured execution lacked an already-adopted R6A runtime substrate because it had not been fanned into the execution tree.
- The WVS materializer imported the right generic binder and then shadowed it with accidental self-recursion.
- R6I carried required facts but put them outside the exact `identity_material` consumed by R6A.
- S17 called a correct verifier in the wrong lifecycle state, then serialized a common-court failure as though it were a candidate cell.
- R6L’s first source-scan stop was a verifier expectation schema truncation, not source divergence.

These are executable succession failures in the strict sense: authority/function existed at levels 1–4 but level-5 live consumption failed.

### What falsifies the universal version

Five ledger episodes are `GENUINELY_MISSING` at the blocker time after active alternatives were checked:

1. LiTS Candidate-C real production determinant/atom bindings.
2. WVS Candidate-C lawful portable execution/fallback.
3. Exact mixed base/F02 successor composition authority before R4A.
4. Directional F02-scope → I02-runtime realization authority before R5A.
5. WVS `B_COUNTRY` current-survey-country position-coordinate use authority before HQD-2026-09-19-073.

The last three are especially important conceptually. Existing source/child semantics do not imply that a new **relation** or **downstream semantic role** is already authorized. Treating those as mere “packaging” would itself violate conservation law by inventing meaning at runtime.

### Therefore

“Integration drift” is not a license to presume every missing edge is mechanically recoverable. The evidence supports a stricter decision procedure:

1. prove the required function/proposition;
2. search historical accepted authority and redesign ownership;
3. search current implementation and exact alternate binding/path;
4. test supersession and deferral;
5. only then classify `GENUINELY_MISSING`;
6. if authority exists, separately prove that the production consumer actually consumes it.

That procedure explains both sides of the evidence without making either “missingness” or “integration drift” unfalsifiable.

## Structural implication, not repair proposal

The project’s failure mode is best characterized as **succession integrity not mechanically closed at consumer boundaries**. That condition coexists with narrower genuine absence of relation authority and candidate/source realizations. A future enforcement surface would need to distinguish those classes mechanically; this return does not redesign or implement that surface.
