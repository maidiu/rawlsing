# Intended mechanism — user clarification accompanying the experiment

This addendum records the user's clarified design intent from the conversation. It is specification information and a proposed research test target, not adopted CAN/ADP law or a claim that the supplied module implements it.

## Intended runtime logic

1. A person has an existing computational organization, age/developmental situation and habitus. That organization evolves under formation pressure through time, including intervals without a salient incident. An event perturbs a moving trajectory; it does not awaken an otherwise static character.
2. An initial LLM call constructs specifics of a potentially consequential event and plausible surrounding circumstances from those constraints. Circumstances belong to other actors and the world too: their work, availability, histories, projects, encounters and competing demands. Factors can influence those factors. The call can propose which principal existing schema metrics different encounters may bear on; it does not choose an ultimate persona outcome or create schema authority.
3. Probabilistic resolution determines which contingent circumstances/encounters occur. The probabilities and mechanism must be specified at the experiment's claim grade. They must not be a disguised lottery over desired future personalities.
4. Rawlsing computes immediate effects and propagation through the existing computational organization, then continues recalculation through subsequent time and pressure. Cross-coupling does not imply every metric must change on every step, and no-effect remains lawful. Independent counterpart lives continue rather than existing solely to produce a lesson.
5. A later LLM call receives the prestate, computed changes/poststate and relevant context, and produces faithful narrative realization. Some specifics may have been written before computation and others may be supplied afterward. It may add substantive compatible particulars; it is not restricted to decorative prose or to one uniquely inferable inverse explanation of the numbers.
6. Once accepted, that realization becomes canonical history whose particulars can affect later processing. It must preserve established facts and supported development. A canonized detail can later be remembered, contradicted, acted upon or reinterpreted. This continuity is part of the mechanism, not merely a presentation layer.

The extended father speech in the conversation illustrated possibilities; it was not a requirement to prewrite an entire lesson sequence. Supportive behavior does not select a moral winner or guarantee one developmental endpoint. There is no authorization to assign attachment/personality labels, arbitrary trait deltas or benevolent/romantic resolutions.

## Consequences for this commission

The current module can test a small material-event/encounter dependency with no-effect controls. It cannot decide whether the full intended mechanism works: it lacks continuing person-state evolution, schema-coupled propagation, narrative realization and canonical future consumption. Missing capabilities relative to the broader target are not retroactive failures of every limited prototype assertion.

The six old REDs must be delivered with R2's adjudication, not simply as six universal blockers. In particular, complete-trace content binding need not use Merkle propagation, and absent repair-object details do not establish that no compatible realization exists.

## Proposed bounded successor acceptance

Using actual owner-specified state/transition interfaces rather than inventing a replacement psychology engine:

- Start two or more replayable trajectories from the same pinned person/world prestate, including ordinary pressure between salient encounters. Show actual state advancement at the permitted grain; numerical progress need not imply every variable changes.
- Resolve upstream factors without a desired final-personality input. Change one designated cause in paired runs while holding shared histories, ordinary pressures and randomness for other causes consistently fixed; distinguish this intervention from separate prestates/moderation strata.
- Observe the actual resolved encounter, principal effects and propagation through the existing computational state. Preserve possible convergence, no-change, delayed effect and competing demands. Any LLM-proposed target metrics remain subject to the computational schema and its authority.
- Supply the before/after transition plus enough context/constraints for narrative realization. Permit compatible elaboration and alternative realizations; check contradictions and unsupported extra changes. Do not require every sentence to have been separately precomputed. Commit one accepted realization with source/transition references.
- Advance to a later opportunity and show the actual runtime can consume canonized details along with updated computational organization. Include a controlled history-reference probe: deleting/replacing a relevant prior detail is refused or produces an explicitly different lawful continuation. An irrelevant detail need not affect later behavior.
- Retain replay and chronology/access controls across both numerical state and canonical history. Separate an abstract numerical-state no-change from a permitted audit/history append. Do not imply language-model generation is bit-identical unless its inputs/version/output are pinned or persisted for replay.

Before implementation, HQ/owners need to pin the existing computational schema/transition API, temporal progression contract, probability/proposal authority, narrative fidelity/commit boundary and future-history read interface. These are focused interface decisions, not a demand for an exhaustive developmental narrative or a new calibrated psychology law.

No claim is made about the capacity, price or suitability of any named external model. No such model was called. Independent scientific acceptance and canonical integration remain separately commissioned owner decisions.
