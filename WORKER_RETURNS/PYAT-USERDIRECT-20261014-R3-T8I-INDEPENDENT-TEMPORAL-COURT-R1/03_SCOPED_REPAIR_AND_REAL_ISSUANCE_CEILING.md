### R2-CONSUMER — a hash of untrusted input is not a producer signature or immutable registry proof

`consume(product)` recomputes `digest(product)` from **the caller's supplied fields and caller's supplied digest**. The supplied digest is not bound to any WaveFactRegistry ref, a producer registry, source/EE12 issuer, a qualified immutable snapshot, or a trusted context.
