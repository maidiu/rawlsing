## Valid conditional bounds and power inputs

If n **independent sampled family** differences D are bounded in[-1,1], the two-sided Hoeffding bound plus a union bound for m planned contrasts gives radius

`r(n)=sqrt(2 log(2m/alpha)/n)`.

The simultaneous interval is `[max(-1,Dbar-r), min(1,Dbar+r)]`. This uses range2, not range1. Bonferroni does not require independence between the contrasts; the family-level concentration premise still requires independent sampling units. References: [Hoeffding's original paper](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/academics/courses/spring06/random/hoefding.pdf), [NIST's Bonferroni discussion](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm). These methods do not turn convenience fixtures into independent observations.

Our conservative planning derivation, not a reported empirical effect:

- Simultaneous half-width epsilon: `n >= ceil(2 log(2m/alpha)/epsilon²)`.
- To reject a superiority null at gap g between the true mean and its null boundary with power at least1−beta using that interval: it suffices that `n >= ceil(2 (sqrt(log(2m/alpha))+sqrt(log(1/beta)))²/g²)`. This follows by requiring the lower-tail error radius plus r(n) to be no larger than g.
- To place the entire interval inside ±epsilon at true difference0 with probability at least1−beta: it suffices that `n >= ceil(2 (sqrt(log(2m/alpha))+sqrt(log(2/beta)))²/epsilon²)`. This is an equivalence-power bound, distinct from the precision count above, and conditional on the true effect and sampling premises.

`outputs/r3_results.json` computes the alpha=.05, m=12, beta=.2 tables for proposed5/10/20pp effect gaps and5/10pp margins. These large worst-case bounds are a transparent fallback, not a spending recommendation or claim that this many approved independent families exist. A cheaper variance-based plan needs an independent prospective pilot, justified dependence/ICC model, target effect/margin, model draw/session design, missingness assumptions and independently checked simulation or paired analysis. Those inputs and actual provider costs are UNKNOWN here. Six original convenience families and two template-clone surface contexts cannot establish population power or equivalence. Any computed current intervals are conditional/descriptive only.
