## Actual old method behavior: critical boundary

1. **`ready_for_v553` != scientific equivalence.** Old harmonizer tests complete wave surfaces and shared semantic/direction/position overlay; it does not demonstrate original national comparability, a denominator or a valid sampling variance. `_authorize_v553_multi_wave_key` can mark a sparse temporal key authorized from harmonizer flags, resolved structural dimensions, packet IDs, and structural values. These fields are NOT an external EE12 grant unless independently authenticated.
2. **Probe contamination danger.** In `_build_v553_cross_wave_delta_index`, when no authorized structural pair is present, `delta_value` is assigned the difference of `probe_pressure_value`s, and `delta_direction` gets `up/down/flat` thresholds of ±0.05; `delta_magnitude_class` uses 0.2/0.4. Crucially the same row sets `comparability_status="probe_only"`, `delta_value_kind="probe_delta"`, `delta_interpretation_permission=false`. N8S must guard on **all three** authorization fields and authenticated source/issuer; looking only at `delta_direction` is scientifically false.
3. **Flat != no real change.** `flat` for a computed probe magnitude below 0.05 is a rule threshold, not evidence of zero population change or a CI including zero. Exact same p estimates with quantified uncertainty may support `NO_ESTIMATED_CHANGE` at a chosen precision; do not convert a non-significant test into equality.
4. **Gap remains gap.** V553's later segment and field-contract builders visibly preserve `unknown_gap`, null pressure, and disallow downstream observed use. The older interpolation policy ledger's optional linear label cannot override the actual emitted semantics. A useful prior/proxy/interpolation is a **newly authorized inferred** context channel with its own uncertainty, not a rewritten observational anchor.
5. **Country/time/structural key != stable estimator.** Census frames, oversample universe, mode, translation, questionnaire and weights can change between waves with identical mapped category labels. V553 cannot legitimately stand in for a survey statistician and original custodian.
