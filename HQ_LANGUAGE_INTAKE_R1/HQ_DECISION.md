## Narrow Sorok101 §13 compatibility clarification

The objective actor utterance retains its exact admitted words, language spans, occurrence ID and hash. SOURCE_ORIGINAL displays those exact words subject to lawful audibility/access. A separately labeled auxiliary translation may be displayed in English or DUAL mode under CAN023; it is an app presentation artifact and is never a second actor utterance or proof the actor spoke English. It does not assert the user actually understood the source language. Translation may expose only the source spans the user is entitled to access; it cannot reconstruct unheard/private speech. A change of display locale cannot rerun actor selection or alter event history.

No broad Sorok101 amendment is needed for this auxiliary-display interpretation. Its law against creative paraphrase into a different actor utterance remains intact. CAN023 already distinguishes auxiliary translation from a second semantic plan. The prototype must record both event/utterance identity and source-plan identity: neither replaces the other.

Optional translation failure preserves the admitted source event and exposes original/typed unavailable translation under display policy. Required translation failure may block translated publication, never roll back or rewrite an already admitted source occurrence. Generated translation is not automatically equivalent; unknown authority or material ambiguity remains unresolved. These are existing-owner obligations, not new psychological law.
