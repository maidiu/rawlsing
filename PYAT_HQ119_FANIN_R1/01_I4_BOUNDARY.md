# PYAT HQ119 — I4 LEARNED-WORLD COMPOSITION BOUNDARY

## Purpose

I4 is not a new semantic stage.

It is the exact adapter/compositor that binds already-owned objects across:

`M1 -> M2 -> B5`

and, where authorized, packages M01 outputs into the exact M1 input surface.

## Required explicit product contract

I4 must never infer lifecycle-facing semantic metadata from a release hash or filename.

A lawful composition call therefore requires an exact adopted product-contract binding carrying, at minimum where applicable:

- exact product family ref;
- exact producer owner ref;
- exact producer contract ref;
- exact semantic subject/product ref;
- exact semantic projection/anatomy identity;
- exact selection-scope ref;
- exact producer permission ceiling ref;
- exact release subject kind;
- exact M1 family-rule binding ref if adjudication is requested;
- exact closure-construction rules;
- exact lifecycle policy declaring whether registration/publication/current-selection is requested or forbidden;
- exact consumer/product purpose ref.

No default latest/current/positive product behavior.

## Adapter A — M1 to M2

Must construct an M2 `ProducerSubject` from one exact producer-owned semantic object/disposition without copying or reinterpreting its meaning.

For an M1 LearnedEmpiricalDisposition, use exact refs already emitted by M1 to construct:
- producer subject identity;
- authority closure;
- dependency closure;
- determinant closure;
- replay closure;
- explanation closure.

Any extra product-family contract facts must come from the exact product-contract binding.

## Adapter B — M2 to B5

Must construct the exact B5 `LearnedProductReleaseProtocol` view from:
- exact M2 ReleaseCore;
- exact registered M2 producer subject;
- exact M2 closure preimages;
- exact product-contract binding.

It must prove that:
- release ref is unchanged;
- product/subject ref is unchanged;
- authority members are unchanged;
- dependency members are unchanged;
- determinant members/hash are unchanged;
- replay root is unchanged;
- family/scope/permission fields are explicitly supplied by authority, not inferred.

## Lifecycle action law

I4 may call B5 lifecycle operations only when the exact composition request explicitly supplies the required event authority/rule/attestation and action.

No release automatically becomes:
- registered;
- published;
- current.

No publication binding automatically becomes B5 PUBLISHED.

No current product is inferred.

## Direct-consumer law

A product contract may lawfully choose direct exact producer ingress and skip M1/M2/B5 only where the downstream consumer's existing authority permits that exact producer grain.

I4 must encode that as an explicit route, never a heuristic shortcut.

## Completion meaning

A green I4 establishes the generic source-free composition path.

It does not establish:
- any particular substantive family validator;
- all source-specific projections;
- live-source correctness;
- universal comparability/composition;
- all learned-world analytical services;
- Field Forge adequacy.

Those remain claim/source/product dependent.

Terminal:

`I4_BOUNDARY_FROZEN__ADAPTER_COMPOSITION_ONLY__NO_SEMANTIC_INFERENCE_OR_AUTO_PROMOTION`
