# Codebook and authority availability

The preserved cumulative handoff contains:

- `final_codebooks_35wave.zip`;
- `authority_35wave_question_and_response_authority.zip`;
- the frozen 35-wave scope registry;
- nine source-local deep node catalogs totaling 18,276 implementations;
- source-specific row/response authority for the later audited source packages.

That is sufficient to construct the Layer-1 contract-covering variable plan.

It should not be described as a perfect archive of every original raw external codebook. The raw-codebook subdirectory in `final_codebooks_35wave.zip` is not uniform across all nine sources. The governing source-local deep catalogs, exact question text, response options, routing signals, semantic roles, directionality states, structural roles, and authority references are present for all active waves, which is what the selector uses.
