# The substantive inquiry

## 1. Why this matters to the product

Sorok aims to generate a historically constituted, independently continuing person and an encounter with that person. A replayable biography and lawful event ledger are necessary engineering ingredients; they do not by themselves establish the causal process by which a particular person develops. The question is what can warrant the transitions, while preserving ordinary life, agency, material constraints, relationships, contradiction and uncertainty.

A chosen rule can produce qualitatively different lives: an event may merely alter objective options, enter a person's current understanding, recruit an existing concern, occasion action or refusal, or contribute to a lasting change. The implementation must not quietly choose among those meanings.

## 2. The current worked mechanism

A lacks access to a tool needed for an inspection. Independently acting B shares it, withholds it, or is absent. A may encounter the change and may separately perform the inspection or pursue something else. Later A faces another opportunity, perhaps alone or in a changed context.

Keep separate:

1. World resources and actual B transfer/refusal.
2. A's objective opportunity and admitted event occurrence.
3. A's receivability, noticing and actor-local uptake/appraisal.
4. Existing needs, expectations, projects, mediators, tensions, practices, memories and relationships that condition this response through their actual owners.
5. A's own attempted/performed action, omission or refusal and its material consequences.
6. Any later durable transition and its use in a later independently performed action.
7. What A can know, recall or say about any of that.

A narrator or controller cannot perform B's or A's action by describing it. Objective occurrence is distinct from actor access. A causal trace need not be declarative memory. Actor-local interpretation of B's intentions is not truth about B.

## 3. Rule A: the immediate response question

The adopted CAN008 R2 semantics allow one bounded person×event coupling evaluation using an exact admitted precipitating event, lawful read-only person conditioning, and any separately qualified interpretive/current evidence. It distinguishes receivability, uptake/appraisal, situated recruitment and bounded current consequence.

A concrete Rule A would need to state which event/history/context evidence is admitted; what is evaluated; how different persons or histories can produce different results; what is unknown; valid-empty/no-effect/refusal behavior; and provenance/replay/currentness. It cannot create world events, B's actions, durable belief, practice, relationships or memory merely because those would be convenient outputs.

Open substantive questions include:

- When does a changed material option enter actor uptake rather than stay objective circumstance?
- What is the warranted role of enacted history, present concerns, relationship history, bodily constraints and contradictory projects?
- Which interpretation is an authorized model inference, which an actor-local possibility, and which is unsupported?
- How do partial attention, inaccessible history and uncertainty constrain a current consequence?
- Is the current proposed immediate rule actually psychological, or merely material bookkeeping mislabeled as response?

The inspected R29M proposal leaves actor uptake UNKNOWN/valid-empty when unsupported. That is a current evidence disposition, not an eternal ontology requiring all generated people to remain psychologically empty.

## 4. Rule T: lasting development

CAN011's canonical registry preserves distinct mediators, tensions and enacted practices. The worked proposed target is V3MS_PRACTICE c9p:coordinated_material_inspection. Current proposal: NO_CHANGE, grant=null.

A concrete T must explain the specific transition: acquisition, revision, persistence, dormancy or another precisely owned operation. It must bind exact prior state, enacted history and causal parents, target identity, warranted predicate/method, uncertainty and separately authorized effect. It cannot use list positions, demographic labels, universal decay, fixed repetition thresholds or an arbitrary numeric scale as scientific authority.

Questions for inquiry:

- Does repeated performance reflect learning, prior competence, task assignment, available tools, incentives or selection into action?
- What counts as a practice at this domain's grain? What would a changed context or later solo action show, and what would it fail to show?
- How can contrary projects or contradictory histories coexist without forcing one dominant disposition?
- What distinguishes a durable transition from a temporary facilitated performance or an audit record?
- Could a defensible narrower rule be issued now, or is NO_CHANGE required until a specified evidential contrast is supplied?

No rule that two inspections automatically make a practice has been established. Equally, do not assume a rule must reconstruct a particular real human or use an invented numerical learning coefficient.

## 5. Rival explanations and discriminators

| Rival | What it predicts | Discriminating evidence / limit |
|---|---|---|
| H0 role/task/script, prior competence or selection | Apparent change follows task/resources/selection with no learned transition | Comparable opportunities, prior competence, action and no-action denominators, independent source histories; a generated simulation cannot validate its own human learning law |
| H1 enacted situated history contributes to later capacity/practice | Earlier enacted activity matters under comparable later options | History contrasts, later independent enactment and context variation; association alone does not establish a universal update |
| H2 B changes material feasibility only | Tools/options change, while uptake and lasting practice may not | Hold A and world conditions fixed while varying actual B transfer; supports material causality, not psychological acquisition |
| H3 opposed projects and constraints condition action | A can decline despite feasibility; contradictory orientations persist | Opposed activity, changed constraints, negative cases and actor-specific histories; no compulsory winner or progress trajectory |

Useful matched contrasts: share versus refusal; B present versus absent; A acts versus does not; A perceives versus cannot access E1; different histories with comparable present options; same history with different counterpart; later context shift; missing target versus genuine issued target. These are candidate discriminators, not new executed findings or authorization for human research.

## 6. What belongs to Matthew

Separate normative product commitments from empirical questions. Matthew can judge what kind of person/encounter the product is intended to generate, whether a bounded synthetic mechanism is worth pursuing, and whether a particular uncertainty or abstraction is acceptable for a specified demonstration. His preference alone does not establish that a psychological causal claim is true, and a programmer's interface choice must not settle the product commitment.

Do not ask him to appoint arbitrary issuers just to satisfy a field. First determine whether existing project authority already identifies a competent accountable route. If it does not, identify that exact authority decision with its consequences. Do not presume that his accepted HQ appointment grants original source rights or all native scientific issuance.

## 7. Evidence and authority layers

Semantic adoption: exact CAN008 R2 content is adopted by HQ; CAN011 canonical registry is distinct. Scientific warrant: a concrete causal rule at the claimed grain needs an argued method, relevant evidence and rivals. Source rights: original actor/world/event material has its own host/purpose/access and custodians. Target permission: durable writes require the separate owner and exact grant. Engineering acceptance: real consumer, causal severance and cold replay. Product/human acceptance: actual continuing person and authorized human encounter. No single layer substitutes for the others.

The packet's sourcefree tests are historical reports, not reruns. No native actor/world/person generation, original empirical source access or human A5 occurred in preparing this branch.
