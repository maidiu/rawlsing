# 01 — Causal/social-language ontology and invariants

**Status:** `PROPOSED_R1_SOURCEFREE_ENGINEERING_CONTRACT`, not an adopted native scientific or actor rule. All generic personhood and authorial claims are conditioned on the existing Sorok 00E/100/101 and CAN/ADP rights.

## A. The object: one continuing person constituted in several social languages
Nadia is **one** historical person, not a collection of ideology bots or generated “personas.” Her linguistic history records overlapping participation in milieus that furnish actual genres, lexical/cultural/conceptual resources, sanctions and possibilities. Multiple cultural language-worlds can be appropriated, admired, rejected, compartmentalized, mixed, ironized and carried across settings. They may conflict without requiring a singular, logically coherent worldview. Natural recurrent speech may express the person's organization without explicitly naming the underlying ideology. Linguistic identity and alienation are **situated relations to historically formed practices and discourses**, never deterministic identities inferred from structural class/religion/geography/ethnicity.

Leontiev's social meaning versus personal sense and activity organization, Bakhtin's heteroglossia/ideological becoming, Bourdieu's field-dependent linguistic legitimacy, Lahire's plural dispositions and Holzkamp/Dreier's subjective grounds/conduct of everyday life are **inspirational analytical lenses**. They are NOT executable scientific rules, stochastic coefficients, effective demographic traits, speaker profiles, or automatic CAN011 transitions. The project is not committed to strong Sapir-Whorf determinism: acquired language can participate in thinking and activity without fixing every possible concept or requiring a lexical token for every awareness.

### B1. Not all linguistic records are psychological facts
Record kinds and permissible claims:
1. `OBSERVED_TOKEN`: X said, wrote, quoted, learned, recognized or refused term L at time T; an occurrence with source and audience. Can be logged at ordinary event/epistemic grain.
2. `OBSERVED_REPAIR`: interlocutor response, correction, laughter, sanction, embarrassment utterance, clarification, accommodation, or code-switch actually occurred; who saw/heard is constrained.
3. `ATTRIBUTED_MEANING`: someone (named) asserts X meant something. A real assertion event, not proof of content. Can be contested by different speakers.
4. `SELF_ACCOUNT`: X explicitly characterizes own motivation, concept or identity. A source-supported self-statement, not privileged objective psyche.
5. `INTERPRETIVE_HYPOTHESIS`: LLM/Rawsling analyst suggests X may attach to person state Y; `NOT_ADMITTED_AS_CAUSAL_FACT` until independently supported and competent owner.
6. `REPERTOIRE_INFERENCE`: derived description of probable expression/relevant language family with references, context scope and negations; may be invalidated by new evidence.
7. `DEVELOPMENTAL_EFFECT`: qualified permanent update to existing owner-state; **requires separately adopted concrete A/T/ADP law, signed rule and granted write; otherwise no mutation**.
8. `TRANSLATION`: rendering of actor speech into reader display language, immutable connection to exact utterance, pragmatic affordances and declared losses; neither actor speech nor psychology.

These kinds remain disjoint. A psychological category derived from `OBSERVED_TOKEN` cannot be upgraded by repeating it in successive summaries.

### B2. Social-language world vs actor relation
`SOCIAL_LANGUAGE_WORLD` is a versioned *referenced cultural/practical context* (e.g., workplace's technical shop talk, specific family habit, a religious institution's conventional exchanges, a subculture's political vocabulary, classroom method). It is not a universal culture/type description. Its source may be generated local institution with legal rights, an authentic actor encounter, or a project-approved general-language resource, each with explicit grade. Do not infer a speaker's canonical discourse affiliation from a continent or a surname.

`ACTOR_LEXICAL_RELATION(actor_id, lexical_object_id, context_scope, temporal_interval, relation_kind, evidence_refs)` can coexist in opposing forms:
- learned/exposed, recognizes, uses, quotes, parodies, avoids, contests, redefines, appropriates, strategically accommodates, habitually reproduces, misunderstands, uncertain.
- relation polarity `ATTRACTION | REPULSION | AMBIVALENCE | UNMARKED_FAMILIAR | UNKNOWN`, *interpretive labels only with evidence source and claimant*, never an ungrounded “positive/negative psychological charge.”
- **no global scalar weight**. Significance prioritization may use an engineering ranking for prompt retrieval, but not commit a psychological strength field without adopted quantitative law.
- same lexical object can link to multiple meanings, languages, speaker sources, milieus and contradictory personal relations at one time. Mutable meaning = new event and new link/version, **never retcon the old sense**.
- relation to **structural position** represented by direct, source-owned objective relation and independent actor identification/estrangement links: `OBJECTIVE_SITUATION != IDENTIFICATION != SOCIAL_LABEL != ACTOR_KNOWLEDGE`.

### B3. The concept/phrase is not its word form
A `CONCEPT_OBJECT` may have multiple languages, translations, senses, contested usage and historical versions. Tokens and conventional phrases can index distinct concepts in context. Keep:
```
concept_id   (not text alone)
sense_id     (sociohistorical meaning / situated sense or ambiguous)
lexeme_id    (language-tagged surface form(s))
discourse_id (milieu/reference)
actor_rel_id (person-specific conditional relation)
event_refs   (enacted source)
perspective  (speaker, author, recipient, analyst; evidentiary grade)
```
Do NOT equate word embeddings, lexeme co-occurrence, or translation alignments with `actor_rel_id`. “Freedom,” “independence,” and analogous translations are not automatically identical concepts with identical historical charge. Novel lexemes are allowed; actor lexical *competence* follows plausible exposure or justified extrapolation at declared grade.

## C. The multi-perspectival state must operate EVERY turn
Keep separate:
- `WORLD_NADIA`: objective person/world events and causally warranted state; hidden private parts exist only if independently permitted, not because an LLM speculates.
- `NADIA_SELF`: current actor-accessible self descriptions and uncertainty; she may misrecognize, deny, conceal or not conceptualize her own formation.
- `NADIA_MODEL_USER`: her fallible model of this particular person, updated only by evidence she can perceive plus qualified relation-owner interpretation.
- `NADIA_METAPERCEPTION`: what she thinks the user thinks of her.
- `NADIA_EXPRESSION`: objective admitted words, languages, gestures, timings and observable consequences; not identical to `NADIA_SELF`.
- `RAWLSING_MODEL_USER`: distinct uncertain, consent/scope-governed model of user understanding, linguistic competence, attentiveness and interpretation; **not** the user's hidden true psyche.
- `USER_PERCEPTUAL_SURFACE`: lawful view over objective event including hearing and language access, allowing externally visible cue but **no private motive leak**.
- `USER_OWN_ACCOUNT`: explicit user utterances and reported beliefs separately from Rawlsing's inferences and Nadia's impressions.

Every turn passes through a consistent *gate set*, but full person-development evaluation occurs only when the event meets proper trigger and permission requirements. There is no forced need for two model calls for each layer or a separate model agent per perspective. User model affects projection **after** actor objective occurrence, never the actor's private pre-utterance realization (except actual Nadia model-of-user input).

### C1. Relation between language and psychological mechanism
Conceptual appropriation may become causally operative: e.g., Nadia acquires “structural dependence” in a class, later uses it in a dispute and acts on that distinction. Three separately reportable facts: `concept exposure`, `actor accessible/use`, `developmental consequence`. None entails the next. A self-description can become a practice *only through actual rehearsed social activity and qualified transition*, not by being repeated in LLM text. Learning a word can change accessible expressive resources without an independently justified CAN011 practice update. This remains actual cognitive/social activity, not “just captions.”

### C2. Reification is a historical process, not an epistemic promotion
The phrase `I am simply the sort of person who does not depend on others` may circulate as parental maxim, Nadia's self-description, interlocutor's accusation, workplace expectation, ironic quote. Its repeated use/accommodation can alter the *social constraints* Nadia faces. Whether it constitutes an actor psychological mediator needs separate evidence. The description never gets promoted to invariant truth *just because Nadia says it sincerely*. Permit continued contradiction: she can respect other people's dependence, need help herself, condemn coercion, and privately reproduce inherited prohibitions in other contexts.

### C3. Conscious and unreflective expression
Audience accommodation is not always deliberate strategy. Selective disclosure, habitual omission, social sanction learning, self-protective concealment, sincerity, misjudgment and involuntary cue are different *candidate processes*. Expressive selection must not always optimize impression-management or force embarrassment/ideology into every output. An actor may express a contradiction the actor is not aware of, but only an admitted observable one; renderer cannot narrate the hidden explanation.

## D. Temporal continuity and provenance
- Four temporal clocks at minimum: objective event effective time, actor reception time if distinct, speaker's attributed/reported time, and ingestion/render/translation time; version and causal order explicit. Event time != reporting time.
- Immutable event and speech IDs, actor IDs, interlocutors, world branch IDs, source/owner hashes. Separate precontact root from user branch. Same person across branches does not imply the same mutable continuity.
- Each derived actor lexicon relation points to supporting source events and a known evaluation pass; contradiction is many edges, not a broken constraint.
- Materialized snapshots + lexical indexes are performance artifacts. Append-only original linguistic events/knowledge remain with their lawful owners.
- Compiler output includes its `source_state_digest`, relevant event refs, active person snapshot and interpreter/tool version. Never compile cache-of-cache without primary source verification.
- Deferred/unknown statuses valid; absence of evidence must not become default “neutral trust,” assumed native fluency or moral/political position.
- Source usage scopes include project/internal actor vs user-readable; no implicit licence from user's social identity to label, profile or publish private attributes.
