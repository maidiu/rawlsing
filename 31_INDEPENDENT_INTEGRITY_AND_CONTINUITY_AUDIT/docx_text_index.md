The missing J-family layer determines when several valid answers form an additional, bounded respondent-level proposition. V550J executes that authority; V551J proves and certifies it. The central design problem is to capture high-value semantic composition without turning every possible interaction into a family or rebuilding the accepted upstream pipeline.

Produces: Governed response states for each item.

When several governed responses form a stable respondent-level semantic configuration; membership, roles, completeness, pattern meaning and evidence ceiling.
Consumes: Frozen question and response authorities + source instrument structure + downstream requirements.
Produces: Family-concept authority, implementation authority and pattern-semantics authority.

Execution of approved family implementations against actual respondent responses.

3. What V550J is trying to preserve
Individual atoms can be perfectly governed and still lose information when separated. V550J exists where the configuration itself supports a stable proposition.
supports a civil liberty generally
+ supports it for one target
+ rejects it for another target
→ target-selective support, if and only if the source and item authorities license that composition
The joint configuration may reveal selectivity, conditionality, principle/application divergence, cost sensitivity, institutional differentiation, means/end structure, routed contingency, or narrowly defined tension. It must not automatically become a global ideological or psychological trait.

4. The combinatorial problem and the typed-collision solution
With thousands of questions and many response states, unrestricted pair and set generation is both computationally explosive and semantically permissive. The system therefore treats the corpus as a governed semantic graph.
Question nodes
canonical question identity and exact source scope
source wording, stem, module and routing
accepted question meaning
accepted response-scale authority
target, relation, domain and other semantic coordinates
respondent universe and applicability
evidence ceiling, unresolved status and provenance
Typed edges and hyperedges
A candidate edge exists only because one or more governed collision templates relate the nodes. Connected, interpretable edge patterns may form candidate hyperedges. A family authority is an approved hyperedge with explicit roles, pattern semantics and evidence limits.
The complete seed registry is machine-readable in `TYPED_COLLISION_TEMPLATE_REGISTRY_SEED.csv`. It is a starting vocabulary, not a final taxonomy.
5. Candidate generation without arbitrary combinations
Channel A — source-native structures
Common stems, batteries, grids, repeated targets or conditions, explicit contrasts, routed modules and staged questions provide a strong source prior. They are neither exhaustive nor automatically authoritative.
Channel B — governed semantic collisions
Cross-module candidates arise through accepted relations: same proposition with a different target, general norm with concrete application, commitment with cost, goal with mechanism, or a governed target hierarchy.
Channel C — downstream ambiguity search
The system searches backward from distinctions Rawlsing needs. For example, two profiles may have the same average liberty score while one applies it broadly and the other withdraws it at a particular target boundary.
Channel D — recall aids
Embeddings, lexical search and empirical association may surface missed candidates. They never create authority.
Blocking rules
shared stem/module or explicit routing
normalized proposition with varied target or condition
common target with complementary relation or mechanism
governed target hierarchy
shared downstream requirement and collision-template match
bounded semantic-neighbor search inside a source/wave or concept neighborhood

8. Admissibility and prioritization
Priority equation
incremental information × source validity × respondent coverage
× semantic portability × downstream leverage × profile discrimination
÷ complexity and uncertainty
The equation is a disciplined rubric, not false numerical precision. The accepted work queue is tiered: universal/high-leverage first, then high-leverage source-specific, then modest enrichment. Weak or redundant combinations remain atoms or diagnostics.

Rawlsing P1.5 Architecture Closure
Approval governance, coverage completion, migration/rollback rehearsal, and frozen P2 node schema
Status: PASS_P1_5_ARCHITECTURE_FROZEN   |   Build date: July 31, 2026
Executive result
P1.5 is now closed. The canonical joint-evidence architecture has a complete approval lifecycle, falsifiable corpus-completion accounting, a sandboxed migration and rollback proof against the frozen GSS registry, and a generated P2 question-node catalog schema. All closure tests pass. No live registry, database relation, respondent output, or accepted pipeline stage was modified.
1. What P1.5 now guarantees
Canonical authority is the source of truth; executable registries are deterministic projections with explicit loss reports.
Semantic meaning, evidence state, permission ceilings, portability, anti-claims, and provenance have distinct fields and stable identities.
Approval binds one exact payload hash and version. Reopening produces a new draft version; old approval remains immutable.
Coverage is not inferred from family counts. Every reviewed source structure receives a disposition, and every wave/instrument scope receives a reconciled completeness manifest.
The frozen GSS registry can be canonically backfilled, projected without semantic drift, switched by sandbox pointer, and rolled back by pointer without reconstructing authority.
P2 node population has a fixed schema and cannot improvise identity, scope, response binding, or collision features during corpus construction.
2. Approval and reopening governance
The approval model separates review evidence from authority status. A file can validate structurally and remain unapproved. Approval requires declared review roles, exact payload-hash agreement, zero blocking findings, an approval owner, and an immutable closure. Dissent and nonblocking notes remain preserved. Any semantic, membership, routing, completeness, applicability, or projection-relevant change opens a new version.
3. Coverage and completion accounting
The completion system operates at three grains: source-structure disposition, wave/instrument coverage, and corpus completion. It proves what was reviewed, what became authority, what was rejected or routed elsewhere, what remains blocked, and where a complete review produced an explicit no-family ruling. A corpus can be complete with explicit source or contract gaps; it cannot be complete with unreviewed structures.
4. GSS migration and rollback rehearsal
The rehearsal did not run respondents and did not touch the live repository. It parsed the frozen GSS registry, assembled a registry from the three canonical backfill projections in the frozen family order, compared each family payload and the complete parsed registry, switched a sandbox pointer to the projection, and switched back to the frozen pointer. All family payloads and the full registry were semantically identical.
5. P2 governed question-node catalog
P2 will create the governed node substrate for typed collision generation. One question concept may have multiple exact implementations. One implementation is scoped by source, wave, instrument version, country applicability, exact variable identity, routing universe, accepted question authority, and accepted response authority. Driver/facet/domain/axis/target fields are retained only as non-governing discovery annotations.
6. P2 population rules
Populate from original or canonical codebooks and accepted question/response authorities across all 35 active waves.
Preserve exact case-sensitive source variable identity. Lowercased keys may assist search only.
Represent country or instrument variation from source evidence, never from smoke/checkpoint bindings.
Separate source absence, respondent ineligibility, routing inapplicability, ordinary missingness, refusal, unknown, and substantive neutrality.
Attach source-native batteries/grids/stems as structure memberships without promoting them into J-family authority.
Generate bounded candidate relations later through typed collision templates and downstream requirements; do not enumerate all pairs.
