```text

A structural-position semantic object therefore has to remain reusable across qualifying occupants; an exact actor/entity identity belongs to the later occupation/fact materialization, not to the definition of the structural position itself.

## Exact qualification failure

Q03 simultaneously says its candidate is only a semantic admission for later independent qualification/materialization and that a later objective-position materializer must bind exact actor/entity identity, applicability, permission, valid-time/currentness, provenance/dependency, and replay authority.

That later gate is the lawful location for occupant identity. Making `EXACT_OBJECTIVE_ACTOR_OR_ENTITY_IDENTITY` constitutive of the semantic position and then requiring exact actor/entity identity again at fact materialization collapses two distinct layers:

```text

!=
OCCUPANT / ACTOR-INDEXED OBJECTIVE-POSITION FACT IDENTITY
```

As content-addressed, the Q03 candidate cannot be treated as a stable actor-independent structural-position semantic object suitable for later fact materialization without preserving that collapse.

This finding does **not** deny that an actor/entity fact must identify its occupant exactly. It denies only that occupant identity may be part of the reusable structural-position semantic identity.
