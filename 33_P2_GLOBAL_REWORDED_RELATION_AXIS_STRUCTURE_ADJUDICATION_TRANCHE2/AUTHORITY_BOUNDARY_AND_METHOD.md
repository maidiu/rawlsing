# Authority boundary and method

Every ruling uses two-sided exact item evidence. Inherited semantic IDs and structure labels are discovery coordinates only. Positive rulings are bounded by proposition, construct, target nesting, metadata function, or matched-member subset. Negative and unresolved rulings are first-class authority. The historical semantic master is preserved; corrections are applied by overlay. The sidecar adapter is a contract/test artifact, not a live pipeline patch.
