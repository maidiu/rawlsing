## 2. ASTRA / SORACLE-CO: affirmative, non-reductive dialectical synthesis

**Applies specifically to explicitly commissioned ASTRA constitutive theoretical synthesis or comparable deep historical-material psychological reconstruction.** Do not apply it to ordinary debugging, code editing or HQ jurisdiction merely because the repository contains psychological theory.

**Governing premise:** Assume that a **unified, internally differentiated truth-picture** of the historically constituted person is discoverable through sufficiently deep and rigorous **dialectical synthesis** of fragmented determinations. Synthesis is the affirmative objective, not an optional conclusion after a survey of competing perspectives.

**Pluralism is internal to the dialectic.** Different psychic organizations, practices, interpretations, institutions, persons, material powers, historical forms, rival explanations, nonrelations at a given scale and unresolved contradictions belong to one developing reality. The work is to discover and derive the concrete historical/material/developmental mediations that constitute their difference **within the whole**. Do not mistake naming irreducible differences for explaining their unity. Do not accept a pluralist catalogue as the final result.

**Unity is not reduction.** Do not force A and B into an invented local interaction, treat every tension as a generative contradiction, make every contradiction resolve, collapse distinct causal levels, predetermine moral liberation, or invent an all-purpose motive selector. A source-grounded negative case can invalidate a proposed *local fusion*; it must deepen the search for the adequate whole, not terminate the synthetic inquiry. A lack of demonstrated direct interaction at a given scale creates the further question of the conditions that produced that difference in the material totality. Do not fabricate an answer where evidence is absent.

**The intellectual operation:**

1. **Recover and saturate:** Before combining source determinations, recover what each actually explains, its genesis, internal differentiations, conditions, functions, contradictions, alternative histories, extinction/continuity possibilities, negative witnesses and evidence limits. Treat exceptional cases as possible corrections to the concept itself.
2. **Drive the conjunction hard:** Interrogate A with B under historically possible material relations. Determine what changes in each, what new possibilities or operative relations become possible, and which limits or contrary histories defeat a first formulation. A list of factors that “interact” is not synthesis.
3. **Derive emergent organizations:** Where actual mediation justifies a new causal organization D, explain its genesis, concrete operations, differential future effects, independent Others/world, scope, contradictions and possible dissolution. Reinterpret A and B through D rather than merely add D to a catalogue.
4. **Repeat recursively:** Confront C with the transformed A–B–D whole; revise antecedent concepts and the proposed whole when further history demands it. Follow deeper relations across scale, time, material reproduction, practical activity, acquired meaning, unconscious process and objective social relations.
5. **Discover architecture from development:** Let the explanatory centers, their reciprocal relations and internal differentiation arise from the investigation. Do not begin with R16/R17 chapter headings, famous philosophical categories, a fixed number of contradictions or a preferred implementation graph.
6. **Deliver the synthesis:** Produce an actual integrated, source-accountable theoretical result and its crucial derivations/counterhistories, not an additional investigation scheme. Expose precise unresolved evidentiary gaps as **problems within the developing whole**, not as reasons to stop seeking unity.

**Standards of intellectual evidence:** Every asserted generative relation must specify its historically possible material mediation and discriminating counterhistory. “Dialectics,” “totality,” “emergence,” “complexity” and “materialism” are never substitutes for that demonstration. Distinguish source-proven empirical claims, contestable inherited theories, original constitutive proposals, fictional historical constructions, tested code and protected adoption. The synthesist does not certify its own independence.

**Rawlsing obligations remain binding:** The object is Nadia and the independent, continuing, body-bearing human life, not an abstract soul or a Western adult template; two historical entrances are source-conditioned fictional parents and Nadia's open development through actual experience. Explain biocultural ontogenesis, simultaneous/partly inaccessible psyche, historical recruitment and novel motives, embodied need, independent Others, institutions and class power, actual activity/world consequence, differing reception, ordinary time, and later psychic reconstitution. No source statistic is a respondent's unconscious biography; no narrator sentence creates an independent worldly event. Current software must neither determine theoretical possibility nor be duplicated without source inspection when engineering is actually authorized.

**Stop rule for constitutive research:** Do not stop because plurality persists, a favorite synthesis fails, or the manuscript sounds complete. Stop when the deepest defensible unified account supported by the inspected source horizon has been substantively developed, further examination yields no warranted explanatory progress, and remaining source determinations/gaps are precisely disclosed. Never claim unexamined material was covered.
