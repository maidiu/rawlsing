## 0. User's more demanding question

We must not optimize for a couple of culturally interesting children, "radical" people, or psychologically exotic avatars. **The ambition is a large, nondegenerate reachable set of plausible historically situated persons**, including mundane majorities, divergent minorities, and exceptionally idiosyncratic outcomes—without assuming the distribution is uniform, the tails are common, that famous biographies can be accurately reproduced, or that an LLM can generate reliable population frequencies.

The earliest spine should be as **materialist** as possible: a live human infant has survival constraints and organismic capacities but limited independent control over the means of securing survival. Its developmental world is generated by caregivers' actual resources, labor, attention, reproductive arrangements, beliefs, practical histories, coactors, environments and relations of power. Caregivers **coax/scaffold** a child into some forms of activity through care, modeling, accommodation, constraints, availability or non-response. Infants also initiate and alter interactions. A culture is not a codebook given to a blank brain.

Two direct architectural traps:
1. If a child only learns on 4–10 authored showcase events, they emerge radically underformed. **NO EVENT IN OUR EXPENSIVE RECURSION != NO EXPERIENCE IN THEIR LIFE**. Conversely, never fabricate an unsupported learned content for "balance".
2. If every culture receives the same universal trait labels at birth, all persons differ only in scale. If every event emits new nodes by LLM decree, every person is untraceable soup. We need shared lawful developmental *operations* but variable learned *contents, links and scopes*, and a **cost-bounded model of ordinary ongoing life**.

## 1. Earliest beginning: biological/physical enabling, not culturally universal desires

Separate at least three levels:
- **Survival prerequisites:** material support of temperature/thermal regulation, nutrition/hydration, breathing/physiology, hygiene and medical care, injury prevention, rest/sleep and environmental viability. These are non-negotiable physical/organismic constraints, but how humans supply them varies historically. Don't equate optimal human flourishing with minimal organismic survival.
- **Initial and developing organismic capabilities:** variable sensory capacities, endogenous arousal/attention, orienting, movement, signaling/discomfort/pleasure, learning contingencies, and developing regulation. Some capacities predate birth; an imagined absolutely "pre-cultural" biological state is analytical abstraction, not an actual culturally isolated human baby.
- **Historically learned organizations:** expectations of particular coactors and cues, patterns of co-regulation, enacted language/communication, categories, routines, competencies, commitments, interpretations, practical premises, reactions to authority. NO universal at-birth honor/glory/shame/God/individualism/revolutionary/prohibition/ideology labels.

WHO's *Nurturing Care* framework recognizes good health, nutrition, security and safety, responsive caregiving and early learning as interlinked developmental requirements (https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/child-health/nurturing-care). It is a policy/evidence synthesis, **not an executable V3 developmental-law registry**. Hard edge: irreducible survival must not be confused with an exhaustive theory of historically situated personhood.

Potential "pre-cultural primitives" should be defined operationally as **minimal physiological/sensory/motor/action enablement**, not the proposition that human infants are socially unorganized at birth or possess invariant conscious motives. Genetic, prenatal and population biological variability must remain representable, with explicit limits on available evidence.

## 2. The first developmental loop is materially dependent *and* bidirectional

Conceptual sourcefree causal loop, **not an operative CAN/ADP rule**:

\`\`\`text
actual infant body/regulatory state + prior experience
     -> child active signal/orient/approach/withdraw/move (possibly no deliberate intent)
     -> signal is or is NOT accessible to given caregiver/coactor
     -> caregiver own body/time/work/other obligations/acquired mediators
        + available food/shelter/network/resources + perceived child signal
     -> caregiver independent ACTUAL response, delay, non-response, interpretation,
        feeding/carrying/touch/vocalizing/showing/soliciting/helping/refusing
     -> objective infant bodily/affordance changes AND possible experienced cue
     -> optional, qualified developmental change in what child can discriminate,
        expect, practice, anticipate and coordinate
     -> child's subsequent signaling/activity changes caregiver's lived environment
\`\`\`

An infant's cry is neither a survey ideology nor a guaranteed intentional request; maternal responsivity is not automatically 'attachment' and infant settling is not guaranteed trust. A caregiver's action may meet nutritional needs while producing different touch, language, attention, discipline and co-presence patterns. Another caregiver may fill a gap. Never infer negligence/affection automatically from a single missed cue or hours at paid work.

**Critically**, caregiver style is not purely "mom's personality". Her own material opportunity, fatigue, child temperament, available assistance, cultural practice, physical environment and institutional demands condition each response. A caregiver can love deeply while being materially constrained. A child can actively solicit, refuse, or change its own behavior, generating reciprocal effects.

Empirical guardrails:
- Nivison et al. (2026), umbrella review of 17 meta-analyses, caregiver sensitivity–attachment r≈.25, language r≈.26, cognition r≈.23; observational correlations are not individual destiny. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41499433/
- Forest et al. (2025), 103 South African infant–caregiver pairs, earlier predictability of caregiver vocalization associated with later infant auditory statistical learning EEG; this suggests potential learning-to-learn dependence, NOT a generally identified causal learning-rate rule. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39352772/
- Wass et al. (2025), infant endogenous attentional rhythms and caregiver reaction in dyadic interaction: adult didactic direction is not the only causal direction. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40434394/
- Broader cultural comparison finds caregiving attention can be mediated through touch/carrying and movement, not exclusively Western face-to-face language: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02646838.2024.2321615 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41151376/
- Adolph et al. "Ten lessons about infants' everyday experiences" (2022) warns against **a representative hour/day/infant**; variability and long-tail occurrence structure matter. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9499013/

## 3. The missing economy: a **GENERATIVE BACKGROUND-LIFE COVERAGE CONTRACT**

A simulation with a handful of expensive narrative events is not equivalent to a life with only those events. But unrecorded imaginary childhood cannot count as causal evidence either. The answer is **multi-resolution causal generation with bounded coverage and uncertainty**, not all-events narration.

Propose that the **existing world/activity scheduler and developmental owner** jointly support a conditional background-process *candidate*:
- Given the parents' actual joint/personal histories, material resources/time, shifts, locations, caregiving coactors, institutional ecology, and infant condition at t, generate **recurring opportunities and activity distributions** for the next period, in lawful actor/world scope. Not arbitrary narrator truth.
- Resolve some **routine activity** with cheap structured conditional stochastic execution (feeding, carrying, sleeping, transport, other adult work, peer contact, shared space), using existing P2 world/resource and actor-specific access where admissible. Each allocated event or sequence must maintain objective conservation and the actor-specific knowledge boundary.
- Store a period's **experience coverage capsule** summarizing actual event-family counts/rates, sequences, variability, contingent signal→response pairs, missed opportunities, caregiver/coactor identities, eligible modalities, material resource state changes, actual access, unknown/unmodeled ranges, source/scenario/model grade, branch/time/rule/RNG refs and replay hash.
- If a development rule is justified by distributions/patterns over repeated experiences, it may **consume a qualified aggregate capsule** with known semantics rather than 1,000 verbose episodes. But it must be an ACTUAL generated experience history, not simply a declared backstory: exact process version, opportunity set, denominator, episode-generating seed and observable marginals are indispensable.
- **Escalate to detailed micro-events** for unusual failures, material transformations, contradictory parental action, relationship ruptures, novel practices, rare salient encounters and actor response that requires the interaction's specific order/content.
- **Commit memory/psychological change only through competent CAN/ADP rules** with exact causal lineage. Aggregate exposure does not automatically issue practice, memory, knowledge, attachment or ideology.
- Keep **unsimulated exposure** formally unknown. It must not masquerade as observed absence or supply proven learning.

This should be a **derived/compressed read model over admitted actor/world histories**; if a coarse stochastic simulator generates aggregate transitions directly, that is a separately graded new sourcefree *model of microhistory* and **cannot pretend it logged events it did not simulate**. Do not claim exact microevent replay where only a coarse capsule exists. Use coherent checkpoint replay and document loss.

Important distinction:
- **NOT ACQUIRED** = a lawful examined/covered developmental window plus a qualified formation rule supports nonformation.
- **UNKNOWN / NOT MODELED** = no representative coverage or no rule; must not be read as absence.
- **ACQUIRED BUT DORMANT OR INACCESSIBLE** = valid state with no presently expressed/claimable role.
- **OUTSIDE APPLICABLE DOMAIN** = cannot infer this capacity from unavailable cultural input; do not default typical development.
Explicitly separate **NO RULE** from **RULE TESTED WITH ZERO UPDATE**.

## 4. Variable psychology is grounded in *functional differentiation*, not a huge adjective space

The fixed portion of V3 is the **developmental causal grammar**: organism/affordance/attention/engagement/real consequence/available coactors, and exact admissible methods for incorporating some causal consequences into actor-owned mediators/practices/tensions/memories/expectations. The variable portion is acquired: *which distinctions arise*, how organized with other acquisitions, scope/context, salience, accessibility, contradiction, embodiment. Fixed CAN-011 kinds do NOT mean fixed semantic nodes.

A category such as honor can become causally operative in one person from recurring public standing, obligations, reputational sanctions, group belonging and learned practices; but must not be prescribed from an abstract "honor culture" label. Same world allows indifference, defensive compliance, critique, misunderstanding, nonverbal skill, different modes of life. Absence of lexical word does not imply incapacity to discriminate relevant phenomena; possessing lexical word does not equal personal centrality.

**Anti-soup discipline:** a novel persistent state or link must show (1) actual source/time/actor event cause; (2) functional role in subsequent bounded situations; (3) context-specific limits; (4) plausible development rule grade; (5) coherent relation to preexisting acquired structures; (6) no forced formation to satisfy a story arc; (7) normal null/ordinary paths; (8) calibration/held-out tests for population breadth. An LLM may propose **candidate event/interpretive middle states**, never directly commit first-person truth. Preserve ordinary lives: novelty/salience budget and outcome frequency must be **source-conditional**, not LLM entertainment preference.

## 6. First test that might actually break the concept

**Common initial biological infant and world**, but independent candidate parent icebergs, labor hours/resources, alternate care network and child signals generate multiple actual **caregiving/participation traces** over an illustrative multiweek period. Compare:
- Same parent survey attitude tip; different historically plausible iceberg and material work hours → *different caregiver conduct only if actor-selection model actually reads acquired parents state*, not because of storytelling.
- Same caregivers in different material circumstances → changed care by **actual time/resource constraints**; not direct conversion of "low SES" into poor care.
- Infant changes its attention or signal pattern → caregiver responds differently where actual coactors have access; child actively co-constitutes later life.
- One child receives much experience through proximity/touch/observing adults; another through direct vocal turn-taking; not one global "good Western verbal parent" score.
- Same handful of detailed special events but different **generated background life coverage** should yield different eligible development, when actual governed update rules exist. Inverse: same qualified coverage + different LLM prose styling should yield identical actor states.
- No exposure to an eligible language/community practice: no magic culturally typical knowledge. But a handful of unmodeled routine scenes **cannot** be treated as proof the child never encountered it.
- Follow through to actual later actor choice, independent coactor response, and material consequence—not an emergent "good/bad personality" label.
- Negative controls: model-grade refusal, rare-encounter edge, empty caregiver availability, no-action, no-U, insufficient sample, source mismatch, replay/counterfactual branch changes, and no compressed-capsule hallucination.

**Three success levels to distinguish**:
1. **Representational reachability:** can the engine express a nonempty range of learned organizations including ordinary, rare, nonverbal, contradictory and context-dependent states?
2. **Causal developmental fidelity:** do actual histories generate those differences through eligible, later-consumed transitions?
3. **Population/historical calibration:** are relative outcome frequencies and within-world variation supported by external data? Even excellent (1)/(2) do not demonstrate (3). Famous-person fictional reconstructions are coverage probes, NOT observed probability estimates, and should not form a developer's psychotype taxonomy.

## 7. The thesis

**The first materialist generative principle is not "all humans desire power/security/love" but "the human infant is a developing body dependent on actual conditions and others' reproductive activity, capable of active signals and learning from consequences; historical organization of that activity governs the distribution of experiences through which further ways of acting become possible."** All later semantic/psychological contents are contingent; not all later learnings are social, language-mediated or conscious; the organism also develops internally and through nonhuman affordances.

**The missing engineering function is not more CAN-011 fields or new LLM psychotype prose. It is a scientifically bounded, material/activity-derived BACKGROUND EXPERIENCE GENERATION + COVERAGE and selective high-resolution recursion mechanism whose realized outputs can be consumed by competent developmental rules.**

Without this, limited curated events create empty people, while freeform filling creates invented persons. This is the middle path.

**Provocation to PYAT**: In the mother→conditional father→relationship→child sequence, what actual historically qualified data (work hours, household resource/care arrangements, services, caregiving practices, coactors, institutions, language practices, material ecology) can parameterize *continuing everyday life* rather than one-off survey-attitude answers? What variation and nonidentifiability must remain held? Can PYAT return admissible range/constraints for material survival and care exposures rather than input a stereotyped "parenting style"? Build one useful narrow empirical layer before promising world-human universality.
