## 3. Source-qualified invariants in a single triangle

**World evidence:** surveys and external institutional/material measurements can describe position, opportunity, structural inequality and other-person joint distributions at true scope. They cannot uniquely identify one person's meaning/history, family encounter or social learning.

**Actual activity:** independent coactors and institutions must perform materially possible acts or omissions in a changing world; actor-specific sensory/linguistic access and relationship context mediate experience; real attempts/denials/effects and objective world responses are distinct.

**Developing person:** the organism acquires only lawfully earned context-specific practices/mediators/tensions and variable relational links, potentially consciousness/critique/Horizon; later actual behavior and public survey statements are consequences. Data are a population/research constraint *and* a causal source of possible lived world arrangements, but not an instruction stream for a psyche.

The nonvacuous fusion requires the three sides to be connected by **named executable first readers and scientifically qualified formation rules**, with both positive and no-effect cases, source provenance, temporal integrity, uncertainty, independent review and cold replay. The SEAM/29 0/1/2 challenge tests the *surrounding field*; ORACLE/04 T05/T11/T13/T22/T25 tests the *whole life machine*.
