# ORACLE / 01 — Total problem: every dimension the machine must jointly carry

**Question:** What *operators, state topology, sociological inputs, world models, temporal resolution and selection/feedback laws* could actually generate a person out of historically situated data—not just make a plausible character inspired by the data?

This is a **requirements-and-research object**, not a scientific finding, validated psychology, engineering implementation or instruction to rewrite CAN/ADP/PYAT.

## A. The original ontological movement, from body to history to person

1. **Organism precedes ideology, not history.** A newborn has needs, metabolic/regulatory constraints, developing sensorimotor capacities, neural maturation and plasticity, active signaling, exploratory activity, variable organismic starting conditions, susceptibility to care and deprivation. Not a blank slate; not an adult Bayesian narrator; not a born religious liberal/conservative; not an Enneagram type; not a compulsory adult motivational taxonomy.
2. **The human organism already depends on other activity.** Food, sleep, thermal regulation, sanitation, protection, social contact, mobility, attention, language exposure, play, tools, labor of caregivers, kin and infrastructure. Reproduction is social and material; actual quantity and organization of care are historically variable.
3. **Need is not yet a culturally encoded desire.** Hunger and regulation are organismic; what counts as a desirable object, legitimate satisfaction, shameful request, proper sacrifice, competent work, worthy recognition or good love emerges in material and social activity. Do not invent identical conscious motivational needs for all neonates.
4. **Desire/conation is living directionality.** Approach, refusal, curiosity, seeking, play, mastery, distress, attachment, self-expression, pleasure, opposition, care, sexuality (developmentally appropriate), justice and meaning-seeking become differentially available in histories. Flow is activity routed through opportunities, impediments and learned organizations.
5. **Power sets terms; pressure is not the moving substance.** Unequal capacity to grant resources, require obedience, establish categories, distribute danger, make a child safe or unsafe, demand loyalty, withdraw recognition, impose sanctions, police alternatives and confer authority. Pressure arises when living activity encounters these differentiated relations; no direct 'power index → psyche' scalar.
6. **Acquisition is historical, selective and sometimes absent.** Practical distinctions, expectations, learned operations, concepts, interpretive mediators, memories and causal traces arise through actual encounter/activity and supported learning. Unobserved does not mean absent; unlearned does not mean psychologically neutral. An inaccessible trace may matter later without a conscious memory.
7. **Topology matters more than a trait inventory.** Acquired pieces inhibit, reinforce, compensate for, justify, subvert, suppress, compartmentalize, reactivate and transform others. Same pieces + different connections = different person; different pieces can generate similar public outputs; one person's internal organization need not be consistent, unified or globally accessible.
8. **Persons re-enter their world.** Actual behavior changes material arrangements and Other-person experiences (or fails); relationships have their own histories, institutions and collective conditions continue; changing world can alter perceived possibilities and future development. Agency is not exogenous randomness; material constraint is not scripted fate.
9. **Historical life makes possibility but not uniqueness.** Identical measured demographic strata admit plural trajectories, lived meanings and future responses. Some inherit dominant frameworks; some strategically comply; some reject, hybridize, misrecognize, or form novel practices. Differences are path-dependent, not pure opinion noise.

## B. Every major social and structural dimension (not a country-label lookup)

- **Nine mutually pressuring strata of original V5:** planetary/world-system (capitalism, climate, colonization, migration, resource chains); imperial block (war, occupation, aid, sanctions, patronage); nation-state (law, citizenship, policing, welfare, courts, education, regime/legitimacy); subnational region (urban/rural, border, infrastructure, deprivation and displacement); sect/ethno/class (stratification, imposed categories, caste/race/ethnicity, labor and property, religious minoritization); party/movement (unions, parties, militias, communal organizations, religious movements); neighborhood/household (kin, security, local surveillance, services, care and economy); family (power, care, discipline, gendered labor, dependency, conflict, inheritance); self (lived person/organism, attention, body, agency, aspiration and shame). **Not a single top-down ladder**: opposing upward, downward, lateral, cross-border and transgenerational forces; local and global mediation can conflict.
- **Historical time and change:** colonial and postcolonial formation, war/occupation/forced displacement, market shocks, legal change, changing classifications and practices, cohort and life-course timing, secular and religious transformations, long historical gaps and noncomparability. Year and geography alone are not mechanism.
- **Production/reproduction:** property, control of labor, authority, precarious employment, wage work, small proprietorship, household business, unpaid reproductive work, gendered division of labor, housing/tenure, land, debt, income, intergenerational dependency, service provision, employer and state sanctions, care networks, work hours, childcare availability, survival economies, education and language access.
- **Class locations are relations, not incomes.** Control, exploitation, authority, skills, autonomy, contradictory class locations, aspirational affiliation, strategically chosen obedience, material dependence, upward/downward/lateral and outside identification. Upward identification may be common in particular hegemonies; it cannot be installed as a universal probability or confused with ignorance of interests. Objectively powerless people can police others; objectively privileged people can oppose their institutions.
- **Hegemony lives through carriers.** Family, teacher, employer, mosque/church, state administration, media, school, military, community organization, party, union, neighborhood, diaspora, transnational network. What they teach, reward, prohibit, model, make obvious, silence or permit can diverge from official doctrines.
- **Norms versus force:** prevailing opinion is not institutional control; legal norm is not actual enforcement; ideology is not the totality of hegemony; a group's report is not an independently measured mechanism of domination. Actual material leverage, rule application and counterpower require separate sources and qualified models.
- **Collectivity versus individuality:** membership, imposed categorization, actual position, social identification, affective bond, inwardly naturalized lens, perceived recognition, external judgment, practical participation and conscious political affiliation are separate. Social collectives are material relations and ongoing practices; no society-wide aggregate psyche.
- **Comparative meaning:** the same conservative stance in a Moscow region, Nabatieh and a U.S. context may play very different roles depending on historical common sense, publicly available alternatives, sanctions, personal contact and organized ideological antagonism. Neither 'Russian is unexamined' nor 'American consciously rejected dissenters' follows automatically from nationality. Meaning-in-field, objective context, personal acquisition history and public response must be separate.

## C. The full inner-life ontology already demanded by SOROK

- **Pressure, flow, power and living vitality:** ordinary appetite, curiosity, joy, humor, beauty, play, sexuality, craft, aesthetic absorption, work and exhaustion alongside domination, grief, humiliation, illness, violence. No misery-only psychology, no cheerful normalization, no universal stress→trauma.
- **Need, desire and striving:** body regulation; concrete pursuits learned through activity; divergent goals, constraints, anticipations, self-expression, effectance and survival; need objects may be socially organized. Need must not be conflated with a self-report category.
- **Love and relation:** another living subject's flourishing and relation itself can enter one's field of aims without erasing alterity. Love may coexist with domination, guilt, tenderness, rage, joy, jealousy, repair, sacrifice, competition and separation. Do not implement as mere liking score.
- **Recognition / witness:** experienced reality can be recognized, ignored, misrecognized, condemned or rendered socially consequential through unequal authority. Recognition is not approval. Child's needs may be met without being understood; someone may be witnessed hostilely. Other's interpretation is not their hidden private truth.
- **Identity/identification and alienation:** when a stratum becomes a lens, its origin can be naturalized; when it becomes object, critique may become possible. Identification and alienation can coexist with partial capture, gratitude, pride, fear, dependency, shame, strategic compliance or moral dissent; alienation is not automatically liberation. A person can internalize and externally police domination, or critically love a collective while refusing its institution.
- **Awareness, solidarity, viscerality:** structurally lucid ≠ materially exposed ≠ able/willing to bear others' costs; perspectives of dominant actors, reciprocal experience and critical standpoint are acquired, not installed by class/race/gender. Double consciousness can arise under conditions of differential recognition but is not automatic epistemic superiority.
- **Absolute Horizon:** not 'stratum ten,' religiosity, a goodness score or God field. A historically mediated relation to ultimacy, finite-claim ranking, death/limit, justice, fidelity, mercy, hope, betrayal and sacrifice. An institution can colonize Horizon or be criticized in the name of a value it mediated. Horizon may not emerge as a richly conscious object in many actors; it must not be instantiated from Islamic/Christian affiliation or survey belief in God.
- **Internalized discipline and reification:** a category (good mother, good worker, true believer, patriot, marriage, revolution) can become more important than the living person/relation it purportedly serves; externally enforced norms can become self-policing and policing of Others. The model must also allow successful critical differentiation and no such reification.
- **PFF/psychic matrix:** sparse actor-variable mediators, practices, expectations, tensions, relation-specific orientations and causal traces, linked by history-supported contextual edges and motifs; varying content and topology, dormant/active/inaccessible states; multiple subsystems without forced synthesis.
- **C5 slow emergent motivational gravity:** recurrence and consequences may organize future salience, defensive habits, recognition/recovery and relation formation. No assigned Enneagram type or 'core wound' at birth. H0 history-only versus H1 history-derived slow state must be compared; if slow summary has no independent computational advantage it should not be installed as magical gravity.
- **Persons are not self-transparent:** objective history, accessible perception, bodily learned response, retrieved memory, public claim, privately articulated belief, self-misinterpretation and actual performed behavior differ. Conscious language is one possible later achievement, not the original human essence.
- **Novelty and liberation:** applying old learned distinctions to new claims; inventing practices and collective action; belonging without total capture; revaluation of one's institutions; rejection, relapse, compromise, asymmetry and no-change are equally admissible if causally supported. 'Liberated person' is not a built-in final psychotype or heroic destiny.

## D. Variation must be structural, not decorative

The machine must permit variation in **which concepts exist**, **which skills are learned**, **which practices organize life**, **which relationships are afforded**, **which contradictions become experienced**, **what each word means**, **which affiliations become deep or peripheral**, **which capacities remain nonlinguistic**, **how strongly different processes recruit one another**, and **whether any dominant slow attractor forms at all**.

Hard tests: ordinary religious and nonreligious adults; ideological hybrids; diffuse or intensely held belief with little action; active political practice + reported low political interest; same survey response through incompatible lived motives; highly practicing believer + seemingly inconsistent self-identification; conservative viewpoints without active hostility and active institutional violence without intense subjective hatred; orally socialized and literate persons; plural repertoires; multi-perspectival subjects; hypothetical severe language deprivation (sourcefree speculative stress test, not validated wolf-child science). No Western liberal adult psychological categories must exist as universal initialized values.

Protect actual distribution of ordinary persons; rare and extreme/revolutionary/predatory personalities are *reachability* tests, not what the sampler should preferentially make. Preserve intersectionality without reducing each social identity to an additive risk coefficient.

## E. The parent/family genealogy in its actual corrected order

1. PYAT draws or constrains **one mother** via a source-qualified joint demographic/material/attitude possibility and actual eligible historical world. These are conditional observed possibilities, **not her private psychology**.
2. SOROK develops multiple possible compressed biographies through the same actor ontology/rules the child will later use; selected fictional biography must causally precede all future decisions. Inverse observation consistency is at most partially identified; no unmeasured source 'grandparent reconstruction.'
3. **Mother's world does not wait for a father to be invented.** Her actual developing life and the world owner generate multiple lawful possible encounter/conception situations through **existing independent coactor attempts, refusals, past practices, contact eligibility, reciprocal access, material/social constraints and third-party effects**. She does not unilaterally choose a person who subsequently acquires a convenient past. Other actors may be represented at less expensive resolution, but any prior *causally effective* behavior must actually belong to the committed event-time world or to an explicitly uncommitted alternative branch. Do not predesign the entire childhood toward one destined father.
4. **The father may be identified computationally late, not caused late.** Select a trajectory only through a world/actor-admitted reciprocal causal encounter. A father *already present as an independent actor or event-time admitted unresolved actor token* may subsequently receive an identity binding consistent with all prior work, kin, travel, institutional action, material effects, third-party experience and temporal constraints. A reserved possibility is not a committed event; a newly inferred compatible private biography is not proof of independently executed development. If his earlier action would alter any already committed world/actor effect, **REFUSE `FATHER_LATE_ANCESTRY_CONFLICT` or rebranch before the earliest effect and regenerate all dependents**. If no legitimate event-time world/binding reader exists, `FATHER_LATE_OWNER_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`; do not promote narrated compatibility into live agency. Also distinguish partner-selection bias from adult survey frequencies.
5. Mother/father interact while retaining independent body, PFF, conscious access, models of one another, prior lives and potentially contradictory perceptions. Objective relationship, co-residence, economic household, genealogy, intimacy, parenting and caregiving are separate.
6. Birth initializes a separately developing child organism, not a blend of two adult attitudes or psychological states. Other caregivers and siblings remain independent, causal; absence of parents may matter without treating absence as a parental attitude.
7. Child develops through *ordinary, dense, materially feasible life* as well as salient events. Recurring feeding, play, sleep, work, care, speech, observations, movement, teaching, coercion, refusal, nurture and relative/neighbor roles must be covered over real periods; no 10-scene childhood or LLM-improvised missing decades.
8. Parents continue evolving in parallel after birth at lower computation resolution. Different granularity must not mean a cheaper ontology or vanishing agency. Child changes caregiver options and relationships as well as being changed by them.
9. Event semantics: available ≠ opportunity ≠ actual encounter ≠ sensory access ≠ understood significance ≠ durable learning ≠ conscious self-interpretation ≠ opinion ≠ action. Possible NON-ACQUISITION, DORMANCY, UNDETERMINED and NOT-MODELED are distinct.
10. The child may later become a full adult and the same person in interactive time; actor code cannot swap to unrelated 'chat personality' rules, nor conflate user attention with actor perception, nor allow unperformed statements to rewrite external world.

## F. The mother-with-a-tantrum test contains the whole problem

**Empirical person C from Arab Barometer Wave II (Lebanon):** 40-year-old married Muslim urban Nabatieh shop owner, spouse reported a craftsperson, comparatively higher household income/sufficiency than demographic-near male comparators; reports stronger gender egalitarian positions, higher public religious participation, support for Islamic law in some domains, some pluralism, Hezbollah affinity, low political interest despite campaign participation. She is not a reported parent of a three-year-old here. The husband and child in this thought experiment are **fully fictional additions**; her real hidden parenting history is unmeasured.

The test cannot ask what someone with C's politics 'does.' The same observational joint must support multiple alternative internally organized adult histories; a fully developing synthetic mother should execute her response to one ordinary child interruption *because* her embodied state, projects, acquired care practices, stress/availability, asymmetric household power, love, recognition, relationship with spouse, possible communal pressures, prior recurrence and specific child's actual signal jointly recruit her PFF.

The child must only acquire what they actually perceive, under a qualified learning rule, possibly nothing. Later behavior must consume that acquired state. The father and each caregiver must continue independently; co-parent effects must exist even if parent-child pairwise labels stay fixed. Holding source observables constant while changing selected fictional histories can alter outcomes; holding histories constant while changing real material conditions can also alter outcomes.

**Critical negative:** never use C's ideology to assert she 'must' mother tenderly, coercively, liberatively or with an elevated Horizon. Real source reveals no such action.

## G. The seven respondent observability probes

**GSS 2010 matched quartet:** 65-year-old white, non-Hispanic, married Southern high-school graduates, different incomes/classes and ballot/forms. M1 (1260): intense religious practice/conviction and born-again claims yet 'not religious at all' self-description; M2 (1951): fundamentalist Baptist and born again but approves all seven asked abortion circumstances and legal marijuana; F1 (1055): strongly conservative/devout Republican allowing several offensive speakers while favoring removal of corresponding library books; F2 (1401): intense private prayer/religious certainty but relatively little communal religious participation, strong Democratic identity, political moderate self-description and differentiated welfare opinions. These show distinctions in language, institution, performance, political/moral position—not proven individual internal tensions. Different questionnaire forms require exact eligible/asked/answered and do not authorize four-person coadministration on all items.

**Arab Barometer Wave II Nabatieh trio:** A (qid 492), B (1306), C (389), each age 40, married, Muslim, urban, preparatory/basic education; C is a woman who matches 17/20 non-sex selection fields with A/B, but different sex, household income, sufficiency and spouse occupation. A and B affiliate respectively with Future Movement and Amal and have differing institutional attitudes; C identifies closest party as Hezbollah and is more gender egalitarian, more publicly religious, accepts some Islamic law and religious plurality and participated in an electoral campaign while reporting low political interest. Observe category/interpretive and multiway co-occurrence, not direct causal gender effect, individual theology, actual sect, couple network or parenting. C's church/mosque non-campaign stance alongside religious law is another nontrivial conditional pattern. All three are separate respondents, not family. One 'demographic-near' comparator trio cannot estimate prevalence, association mechanism or cross-cultural invariance.

**Hemingway 'baby shoes' analogy:** a few observable details invite rich reconstruction, but narrative affordance must never become epistemic authority. These profiles discipline which kinds of person can be imagined/represented; they do not reveal unasked childhood, secrets, family interactions or conscious conflicts.

## H. Essential asymmetries and limits

- The state/collective field is **causally efficacious before actor comprehension**; people can reproduce hegemony without naming it.
- Same proposition can be routine, controversial, coerced, resistant, aspirational, ironically held, bracketed, or tied to different social identifications.
- **Numerical contradiction is not experienced contradiction**. Statistical co-occurrence, structural antagonism, practical conflict and recognized conflict are different things; none is automatically learned from another.
- "Higher SES" is relative to a qualified comparison group and indicators, not an objective universal class or moral progressive rank.
- **Choice, feeling, action, and instrument answer are different outputs**. No single 'opinion' scalar controls them all.
- Material constraints must remain real in computation: resource conservation, time, care burden, state/institutional rule, geography, capacity, risk, death and unavailable alternatives; as well as subjective perceptions that may be wrong.
- **Scientific theory is disputable.** Leontiev's activity/object/motive model, Lahire's plural dispositional context, Sève's concrete individual, Dreier's across-context participation, Archer's internal conversation, Holzkamp's subject-scientific perspective, Kohn/Schooler's work-personality reciprocity, Wright's class contradictions, Bhattacharya's social reproduction, Elder's cohort/history and Luria's sociocultural cognition are candidate resources with different units of analysis. Do not treat their names as validated parameter issuers or flatten their contradictions into 'dialectical psychology.'
- Resource limits matter: low-cost common activity, selective fine events, honest gaps and uncertainty, cold replay, stable causal ledgers and targeted operation rather than infinitely large world simulation.
