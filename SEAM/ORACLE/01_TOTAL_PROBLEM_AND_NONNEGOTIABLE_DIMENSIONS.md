## 0A. Binding parent-sampling constitution and the science of extrapolation

**Parents are synthetic people, not actual survey respondents.** The mother is given a profile-conditioned **sampled pseudo-respondent answer configuration**; the separate prospective father is given his own appropriate sampled pseudo-respondent configuration. Both are sampled/conditioned from the eligible whole population **sharing, or defensibly nearest/comparable to, their respective demographic, material, historical and social positions**. Full within-respondent joint covariance, minority configurations, conditional branches, uncertainty, missingness, question eligibility, weights/target and period matter. "Nearest" is a scientifically specified distance/support/transport/backoff operation, not an intuitive resemblance or an invitation to stereotype. The real people in §G and the uploaded archives are **observability challenges**, not persona templates, spouses, parents or identifiable historical biographies.

**PYAT provides some of what shapes the parents—neither all of it nor merely a validation fence.** Its conditional empirical distributions and objective possibilities must exert **strong generative influence on each parent's proposed life and developing personhood**, not simply on the background scene or a final population-fit report. The seam must discover how conditional sampling changes the distribution of *possible formative histories*, their relatively supported social circumstances and practices, and the adult observable configurations SOROK may genuinely produce. **This is not a license to sample a finished hidden psychological state from survey responses.** Conditional influence may require an explicit forward, inverse, hybrid or other proposed route *received by SOROK's existing machinery*, whose independent actors and developmental histories then generate meanings, identifications, wants, contradictions and contingent actions. Those further influences arise through **SOROK's already substantial developmental world, independent actors, material activity, social reproduction and psychologically consequential histories**. They are constitutive, not decoration around a prewritten PYAT-derived character or an LLM's likely story. **Astra must investigate how to achieve this high-dimensional individual-and-population conditioning; no single inferential architecture is prescribed here.**

**There is no further hidden PYAT/Rawlsing structured source dataset presumed to complete the private human lives.** The missing science is how SEAM extrapolates from qualified observations and objective-world conditions into a rich but bounded set of *SOROK-consumable formative possibilities*, under explicit generative assumptions and with scientifically honest limits. ORACLE must decide what is actually supplied, what is only bounded, what requires a hypothesis, and what SOROK itself must produce through real events. It must not claim to discover particular private histories or issue SOROK psychological law because the surveys cannot supply them.

The distinction between four different kinds of distribution is compulsory:

1. **Observed population responses.** PYAT's instrument- and design-qualified joint distribution of eligible *whole-respondent answers* conditional on demographic/material/historical position. This is not the distribution of minds, motives, childhoods, family pairings or psychological states.
2. **SEAM-conditioned possible circumstances.** A bounded or model-weighted set of historical worlds, positions, public practices, constraints and opportunities constructed from legal PYAT/N9/N10E/N10W inputs plus openly identified alternatives and assumptions. The probability of a survey answer does not determine whom an actor meets, what an institution enforces or how often a child is cared for.
3. **Differently open SOROK generation regimes.** **Parents:** a compressed, partly back-projected and conditioned subset of the same SOROK person-development operation produces rich but less openly generated adult psychological organization, subject to a **mandatory sampled pseudo-respondent survey-output target**. **Child:** a longer, substantially open-ended developmental run begins with an immature, sensing, need-bearing organism and recursively forms the person through life with those parents and society. The SOROK actor/world/recursion ontology is shared; computational depth, prior constraints and what is filled in are intentionally unequal. How to achieve this without person-state fakery is the scientific problem, not a settled algorithm.
4. **Synthetic survey observations.** When a historically appropriate questionnaire is administered to the already developed synthetic adults, their context-, meaning- and instrument-mediated responses become *outputs*. At ensemble level, a source-calibrated population should reproduce eligible whole-respondent complexity, heterogeneity, minority branches and apparent contradictions without any individual being required to copy a specific respondent. Heldout POP, developmental DEV and transport TRANS must be tested separately.

**The sampled pseudo-respondent configuration is both a required input and a binding output-correspondence target for the constructed parent**—not an optional inverse-calibration target. The parent generator can use compressed developmental recursion, backward projection from major demographic/historical elements and bounded fill-in, but **its resulting whole adult psychological computation must itself yield responses corresponding to the sampled pseudo-respondent under the appropriate reference conditions**. It may not cheat through answer lookup, permanent response locks or survey responses treated as a private psyche. The sampled target does not identify a unique inner organization or private childhood; proposal/selection choices shape which of many compatible adults is produced. After constitution, actual SOROK life can change the parent and their subsequent survey answers. The child has no analogous preselected answer target.

The statistical influence must be strong yet nonessentialist: dominant source-supported patterns matter probabilistically, minority and apparently contradictory configurations remain reachable, and the model allows persons to answer similarly through different histories or differently under changed wording and context. **There are two distinct conditioning obligations, not only an ensemble endpoint:** (a) a particular sampled parent's generated developmental trajectory and potential adult expressions must have an intelligible relationship to the support, conditional weight and uncertainty of the profile-relevant population, including justified uncommon trajectories; (b) across eligible synthetic persons, the resulting whole-response morphology must recover the relevant conditional heterogeneity without source-row copying. No single respondent's psyche or path is identified by either obligation. **Matching a population distribution is not proof of developmental validity; producing an individually persuasive persona is not proof of valid conditional sampling.**

**Mother and father:** Each parent begins with a demographic, material and historical profile and a **source-qualified pseudo-respondent configuration of survey answers**, probabilistically sampled from the appropriate whole-respondent population. The parent is still a materially and historically located person with internally interrelated needs, desires, loves, pressures, practices, memories, relationship states, identifications, alienations, contradictions and context-sensitive judgments—**not a thin questionnaire persona**.

**Different parent autonomy in different senses:** Their *initial generative construction* is intentionally less open than the child's. The sampled response target and shortcut/compression budget restrict how the initial adult state can emerge. **That does not mean the parents lack ongoing agency, internal contradictions, genuinely reciprocal relations or the capacity to change after construction.** They can act independently, perceive imperfectly, disagree and affect the child and world. Interactive surfaces require psychologically and historically credible depth even when their initialization received much less computational development than the child's. **The two pseudo-respondent targets are separate:** The father's demographics and coherent answer profile require their own eligible/defensibly near population sampling, not copying the mother or inferring a partner from a source respondent row. Plausible social sorting and relational possibility constrain which independently constituted adults can enter a shared household. Neither parent receives retroactively fabricated public history or causal acts; pre-birth world/relationship facts must be compatible with the actual actor/world owner.

**Child:** The central, computationally privileged developing person is not initialized with adult survey answers or an adult fixed-response destination. The child begins as a vulnerable **need-, discomfort- and pain-experiencing, desire-developing, care/love-receiving or care-deprived immature organism**, a 'soupbrain-meatsuit' with bodily capacities and constraints but not a ready-made adult ideology or conceptual self. Through **actual ongoing dialectical encounter and recursion** with mother, father and a wider world of class, kin, institutions, social identities, material possibilities and historical change, a developing SOROK psyche emerges. The formation is open-ended **within the causal and material dialectic**, not unbounded fantasy or demographic predestination. That person must eventually be capable of the psychological differentiation, variability, inconsistency and complexity found among actual respondent profiles while exceeding any questionnaire. **Their adult responses are emergent observations, not compliance with a preselected pseudo-respondent answer vector.**

**One machinery, two computational investments:** The same adopted or legitimately developed SOROK/CAN/ADP/PFF person-and-event concepts must underwrite both parent preparation and child formation. The asymmetry lies in **how heavily the construction is targeted and compressed**, not in granting the parents a different or less-human ontology. The parent shortcut is justified by the actual product priority: the **child is the star of the generative undertaking**, the parents are essential interactive, material and dialectical others, not equal-cost protagonist life simulations. The relationship can nevertheless transform parents after their initial constitution.

**The problem that remains unsolved:** How can one construct a rich, coherent whole adult psychological computation from a demographic/milieu/structural position plus a specific sampled pseudo-respondent target, using a compressed and partly constrained version of SOROK's dynamics, **such that correspondence to those answers emerges from the actual constructed person rather than a hidden answer key**? ## A. The original ontological movement, from body to history to person

1. **Organism precedes ideology, not history.** A newborn has needs, metabolic/regulatory constraints, developing sensorimotor capacities, neural maturation and plasticity, active signaling, exploratory activity, variable organismic starting conditions, susceptibility to care and deprivation. Not a blank slate; not an adult Bayesian narrator; not a born religious liberal/conservative; not an Enneagram type; not a compulsory adult motivational taxonomy.
2. **The human organism already depends on other activity.** Food, sleep, thermal regulation, sanitation, protection, social contact, mobility, attention, language exposure, play, tools, labor of caregivers, kin and infrastructure. Reproduction is social and material; actual quantity and organization of care are historically variable.
3. **Need is not yet a culturally encoded desire.** Hunger and regulation are organismic; what counts as a desirable object, legitimate satisfaction, shameful request, proper sacrifice, competent work, worthy recognition or good love emerges in material and social activity. Do not invent identical conscious motivational needs for all neonates.
4. **Desire/conation is living directionality.** Approach, refusal, curiosity, seeking, play, mastery, distress, attachment, self-expression, pleasure, opposition, care, sexuality (developmentally appropriate), justice and meaning-seeking become differentially available in histories. Flow is activity routed through opportunities, impediments and learned organizations.
5. **Power sets terms; pressure is not the moving substance.** Unequal capacity to grant resources, require obedience, establish categories, distribute danger, make a child safe or unsafe, demand loyalty, withdraw recognition, impose sanctions, police alternatives and confer authority. Pressure arises when living activity encounters these differentiated relations; no direct 'power index → psyche' scalar.
6. **Acquisition is historical, selective and sometimes absent.** Practical distinctions, expectations, learned operations, concepts, interpretive mediators, memories and causal traces arise through actual encounter/activity and supported learning. Unobserved does not mean absent; unlearned does not mean psychologically neutral. An inaccessible trace may matter later without a conscious memory.
7. **Topology matters more than a trait inventory.** Acquired pieces inhibit, reinforce, compensate for, justify, subvert, suppress, compartmentalize, reactivate and transform others. Same pieces + different connections = different person; different pieces can generate similar public outputs; one person's internal organization need not be consistent, unified or globally accessible.
8. **Persons re-enter their world.** Actual behavior changes material arrangements and Other-person experiences (or fails); relationships have their own histories, institutions and collective conditions continue; changing world can alter perceived possibilities and future development. Agency is not exogenous randomness; material constraint is not scripted fate.
9. **Historical life makes possibility but not uniqueness.** Identical measured demographic profiles or positions admit plural trajectories, lived meanings and future responses. Some inherit dominant frameworks; some strategically comply; some reject, hybridize, misrecognize, or form novel practices. Differences are path-dependent, not pure opinion noise.

Actual material leverage, rule application and counterpower require separate sources and qualified models.
- **Collectivity versus individuality:** membership, imposed categorization, actual position, social identification, affective bond, inwardly naturalized lens, perceived recognition, external judgment, practical participation and conscious political affiliation are separate. Social collectives are material relations and ongoing practices; no society-wide aggregate psyche.
- **Comparative meaning:** the same conservative stance in a Moscow region, Nabatieh and a U.S. context may play very different roles depending on historical common sense, publicly available alternatives, sanctions, personal contact and organized ideological antagonism. Neither 'Russian is unexamined' nor 'American consciously rejected dissenters' follows automatically from nationality. Meaning-in-field, objective context, personal acquisition history and public response must be separate.

## C. The full inner-life ontology already demanded by SOROK — receiver commitments, not ORACLE's psychology rewrite

**ORACLE must understand every commitment here as a requirement on the seam's ability to support SOROK, not an instruction to create a replacement psychological engine.** It may identify exactly necessary upstream inputs or SOROK first-reader revisions and defer unsolved developmental laws to their competent owners.

- **Pressure, flow, power and living vitality:** ordinary appetite, curiosity, joy, humor, beauty, play, sexuality, craft, aesthetic absorption, work and exhaustion alongside domination, grief, humiliation, illness, violence. No misery-only psychology, no cheerful normalization, no universal stress→trauma.
- **Need, desire and striving:** body regulation; concrete pursuits learned through activity; divergent goals, constraints, anticipations, self-expression, effectance and survival; need objects may be socially organized. Need must not be conflated with a self-report category.
- **Love and relation:** another living subject's flourishing and relation itself can enter one's field of aims without erasing alterity. Love may coexist with domination, guilt, tenderness, rage, joy, jealousy, repair, sacrifice, competition and separation. Do not implement as mere liking score.
- **Recognition / witness:** experienced reality can be recognized, ignored, misrecognized, condemned or rendered socially consequential through unequal authority. Recognition is not approval. Child's needs may be met without being understood; someone may be witnessed hostilely. Other's interpretation is not their hidden private truth.
- **Interlocking identification / alienation, not a membership vector:** family, sect, religion, class, gender, ethnicity, society, region, nation, hegemony and other historically available social fields can **overlap, compete, mutually reinforce, repel, attract, enable or inhibit** the subject's developing practical commitments. Their force is not reducible to binary belonging, survey identity or a scalar pressure. The subject can identify with a field so deeply that its historically acquired categories become the **invisible lens** through which reality is apprehended, while other affiliations remain peripheral or actively conflict. Alienation can render that prior identification **external, visible, objectifiable and criticizable**, but alienation is neither automatically freedom nor merely hostile rejection. The person may become alienated from an institution **on the basis of the very priorities and emphases they acquired through it**: the institutional expression can be negated *from within its own inherited commitments*, whose living meaning may be preserved, transformed or overcome rather than discarded. A person may thus go beyond a formative affiliation **without needing to become its reactive opposite**. Identifications and alienations may be simultaneous, layered, partially unconscious, contextually reactivated, internally opposed or unresolved. The same person can internalize and police domination, strategically comply, critically love a tradition while refusing its institution, or remain deeply identified despite contradiction. **These are SOROK's psychological-development requirements, not tasks for Astra to reconstruct as a new engine.** ORACLE must ensure the seam does not foreclose their historically specific emergence.
- **Awareness, solidarity, viscerality:** structurally lucid ≠ materially exposed ≠ able/willing to bear others' costs; perspectives of dominant actors, reciprocal experience and critical standpoint are acquired, not installed by class/race/gender. Double consciousness can arise under conditions of differential recognition but is not automatic epistemic superiority.
- **Absolute Horizon and immanent critique must not be collapsed:** not a new universal social tier, religiosity, a goodness score, a God field, a guaranteed transcendent experience or the final stage of a compulsory liberation plot. The principal's example of **immanent alienation and possible overcoming**—a subject's acquired commitments turned against the institution that taught them—illustrates one critical capacity a developed person may possess. **It does not exhaust or define Absolute Horizon**, which concerns historically mediated relationships to ultimate or limiting goods, obligations and priorities beyond any one finite institutional claim. Either possibility may occur without the other, may remain unconscious or unresolved, or may never emerge. Belonging, grief, complicity, mercy, fidelity and critique may coexist. No PYAT answer about God, religious affiliation, liberalism or institutional trust can initialize either outcome. **These are SOROK psychological possibilities whose historical conditions the seam must be capable of nourishing, never an ORACLE-installed moral destination.**
- **Internalized discipline and reification:** a category (good mother, good worker, true believer, patriot, marriage, revolution) can become more important than the living person/relation it purportedly serves; externally enforced norms can become self-policing and policing of Others. The model must also allow successful critical differentiation and no such reification.
- **PFF/psychic matrix:** sparse actor-variable mediators, practices, expectations, tensions, relation-specific orientations and causal traces, linked by history-supported contextual edges and motifs; varying content and topology, dormant/active/inaccessible states; multiple subsystems without forced synthesis.
- **C5 slow emergent motivational gravity:** recurrence and consequences may organize future salience, defensive habits, recognition/recovery and relation formation. No assigned Enneagram type or 'core wound' at birth. H0 history-only versus H1 history-derived slow state must be compared; if slow summary has no independent computational advantage it should not be installed as magical gravity.
- **Persons are not self-transparent:** objective history, accessible perception, bodily learned response, retrieved memory, public claim, privately articulated belief, self-misinterpretation and actual performed behavior differ. Conscious language is one possible later achievement, not the original human essence.
- **Novelty and liberation:** applying old learned distinctions to new claims; inventing practices and collective action; belonging without total capture; revaluation of one's institutions; rejection, relapse, compromise, asymmetry and no-change are equally admissible if causally supported. 'Liberated person' is not a built-in final psychotype or heroic destiny.

## D. Variation must be structural, not decorative

The machine must permit variation in **which concepts exist**, **which skills are learned**, **which practices organize life**, **which relationships are afforded**, **which contradictions become experienced**, **what each word means**, **which affiliations become deep or peripheral**, **which capacities remain nonlinguistic**, **how strongly different processes recruit one another**, and **whether any dominant slow attractor forms at all**.

Illustrative **expressive range, not Astra's assigned experiments**: ordinary religious and nonreligious adults; ideological hybrids; diffuse or intensely held belief with little action; active political practice + reported low political interest; same survey response through incompatible lived motives; highly practicing believer + seemingly inconsistent self-identification; conservative viewpoints without active hostility and active institutional violence without intense subjective hatred; orally socialized and literate persons; plural repertoires; multi-perspectival subjects. Severe hypothetical language deprivation is an optional speculative research question, **not a mandated test or validated wolf-child science**. No Western liberal adult psychological categories must exist as universal initialized values.

Protect actual distribution of ordinary persons; rare and extreme/revolutionary/predatory personalities are *reachability* tests, not what the sampler should preferentially make. Preserve intersectionality without reducing each social identity to an additive risk coefficient.

## E. Two parent preparations and the child's full developmental generation — shared ontology, unequal constraints

1. **Mother's sampled target:** PYAT samples a coherent pseudo-respondent answer configuration from her eligible or defensibly nearest demographic/material/historical population, with whole-respondent covariance, uncertainty and appropriate source qualification. This is **a specific target to which the resulting constructed adult must correspond under the applicable survey**, not simply a demographic prior or optional later validator.
2. **Mother's compressed generation:** Her important demographic elements, historically possible milieu and provisional backward-projected/prior circumstances condition a **shorter, partly predetermined recursion through SOROK's existing person dynamics**. Significant unobserved material may be filled in with graded assumptions and contextual specificity. At the resulting initial adult reference point, the actual whole-person computation must be capable of returning the targeted pseudo-responses **without lookup or locked-in survey traits**. Her underlying psychology is richer than the target, even if her *construction* is not fully open.
3. **Father's separate equivalent:** A separate pseudo-respondent, sampled from his own conditional demographic population, similarly conditions a compressed SOROK adult constitution with **its own mandatory survey correspondence**. The relationship/pairing must be possible under actual world, kin, class, place, institutional and temporal conditions; source similarity alone cannot manufacture the couple.
4. **Existing social history cannot be retroactively created:** These adult preparations need coherent prior organization, but no claim to a unique empirically identified childhood. Independently performed prior acts and public world consequences must obey actual actor/world owners; a late-selected father cannot acquire fictitious previous effects. Adult initialization is not a warrant to alter historical source truth.
5. **The adults as interactive Others:** Both parents have whole-human conative, affective, cognitive, relational, epistemic and practical organization. They are independently causal, internally complex, potentially contradictory, and capable of change through their subsequent histories. Their **initial generation is intentionally less open**, but their live agency and meaning are not reduced to fixed survey answer sheets.
6. **Child birth:** A separate vulnerable organism with bodily needs, discomfort, pain, developing desires and capacities, dependence on care and possible reception or deprivation of love; no preset adult pseudo-respondent or survey response destination.
7. **Child dialectical ontogenesis:** SOROK recursively generates the child's historical psychology through dense, ordinary, materially possible life with the two existing and continuing adults and other carers, institutions and social actors. The child's emerging interpretations, identifications, alienations, love, practical capacities and tensions are **open-ended within this actual dialectical field** and can transform the parents and shared world as well as being shaped by them.
8. **Adult child and observational capacity:** The child may eventually become a psychologically complex adult able to answer appropriate survey instruments with the variability and contradiction that PYAT makes empirically intelligible. Unlike parent preparation, the child's adult answers have **not** been prescribed at the beginning. One continuing SOROK ontology/actor/psychological machinery serves every actor; the parent's compressed targeted initialization and the child's deep developmental generation differ in budget, openness and conditioning.
9. **Source and semantic integrity:** Sampling, objective circumstance, possible contact, actual event, actor access, uptake, meaning, durable effect, report and action remain distinguishable. Parent survey correspondence, population-frequency accountability and child-emergence validity are three different scientific questions. The choice of actual mechanism and appropriate scientific investigation remains Astra's, not an ORACLE-authored blueprint.

## H. Essential asymmetries and limits

- The state/collective field is **causally efficacious before actor comprehension**; people can reproduce hegemony without naming it.
- Same proposition can be routine, controversial, coerced, resistant, aspirational, ironically held, bracketed, or tied to different social identifications.
- **Numerical contradiction is not experienced contradiction; nor is real psychological contradiction optional.** Statistical co-occurrence, structural antagonism, practical conflict and recognized conflict are distinct; neither a survey contradiction nor conceptual inconsistency proves its respondent experienced inner struggle. **SOROK must nevertheless be capable of genuinely generating internal contradictions** among independently acquired commitments, changing contexts, identification/alienation and material practices, including unrecognized, compartmentalized or actively experienced tensions. Calibrate observable response morphology and independently test the actor's actual internal functional relations; do not substitute one for the other.
- "Higher SES" is relative to a qualified comparison group and indicators, not an objective universal class or moral progressive rank.
- **Choice, feeling, action, and instrument answer are different outputs**. No single 'opinion' scalar controls them all.
- Material constraints must remain real in computation: resource conservation, time, care burden, state/institutional rule, geography, capacity, risk, death and unavailable alternatives; as well as subjective perceptions that may be wrong.
- **Scientific theory is disputable.** Leontiev's activity/object/motive model, Lahire's plural dispositional context, Sève's concrete individual, Dreier's across-context participation, Archer's internal conversation, Holzkamp's subject-scientific perspective, Kohn/Schooler's work-personality reciprocity, Wright's class contradictions, Bhattacharya's social reproduction, Elder's cohort/history and Luria's sociocultural cognition are candidate resources with different units of analysis. Do not treat their names as validated parameter issuers or flatten their contradictions into 'dialectical psychology.'
- Resource limits matter: low-cost common activity, selective fine events, honest gaps and uncertainty, cold replay, stable causal ledgers and targeted operation rather than infinitely large world simulation.

- **Sampling and formation are distinct but joined:** PYAT supplies heavily influential, whole-population-conditioned priors and world possibilities; SOROK produces the greater, irreducibly historical computational person. The same instrument, applied later, must be capable of observing correspondingly rich variability, not reading back a preloaded answer sheet.
- **Two-sided seam authority:** ORACLE may discover necessary changes at the end of PYAT or the beginning of SOROK; that does not transfer SOROK's psychological mission or its science/actor issuance to ORACLE.
- **Source and generated context remain distinct:** no hidden future PYAT/Rawlsing empirical supply, no unqualified LLM/web psychology, no conversion of Western professional-managerial assumptions into natural human motivation, and no treating the survey's own imposed categories as a transparent private mind. Targeted retrieval can add provenance-labeled historically and culturally particular detail **to an already emerging SOROK person**; it cannot silently determine their hidden motives or insert a generic PMC-liberal destiny. The survey itself may encode domination, selective categories and absent possibilities. Both evidentiary corruptions must remain contestable.
