Astra should read, interrogate and compare, not mechanically blend:

- **Leontiev, Activity, Consciousness and Personality:** activity/object/motive and multiplication/ordering of life relations; useful for the construction of motive from actual activity, but not a numeric ready-made rule. Primary text: https://www.lchc.ucsd.edu/MCA/Paper/leontev/ch3.htm and https://www.lchc.ucsd.edu/mca/Paper/leontev/ch5b.htm.
- **Lucien Sève, Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality:** social-historical concrete individual and critique of abstract traits; demand actual transformation.
- **Klaus Holzkamp, Grundlegung der Psychologie:** subject position, reasons for action and constrained action possibility; challenge automatic derivation from external structure.
- **Bernard Lahire, The Plural Actor:** inconsistent dispositions formed in varied socializations and recruited by context, against a globally homogeneous habitus. https://www.persee.fr/doc/rfsoc_0035-2969_1999_num_40_4_5224.
- **Ole Dreier, Psychotherapy in Everyday Life:** participation trajectories across social contexts, personal conduct and actual practices. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0959354309103539.
- **Margaret Archer, Structure, Agency and the Internal Conversation:** reflexive practical deliberation, with the caveat that reflection is not universal infant consciousness and its relationship to unformulated habits must be investigated.
- **A. R. Luria, Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations:** development within modes of activity/language/schooling, with caution about translation and presumed superiority.
- **Kohn & Schooler, Work and Personality; Kohn & Słomczyński, Social Structure and Self-Direction:** observed reciprocal longitudinal occupational structures and personality; alternative causal calibration targets. https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/227593.
- **Glen H. Elder, Children of the Great Depression; Jeylan T. Mortimer, Working and Growing Up in America:** cohort, lived period, parental material shock, life-course timing, work/adolescence.
- **Erik Olin Wright, Classes:** exploitation, domination and contradictory class locations, caution against treating income as class relation. https://newleftreview.org/issues/ii60/articles/erik-olin-wright-understanding-class.
- **Tithi Bhattacharya, Social Reproduction Theory: Remapping Class, Recentering Oppression; Making Death and Life in Palestine:** socially organized reproduction of labor, care, survival and politically organized power, not motherhood psychological default.
