## CONSTITUTIVE RAWLSING MISSION — PRODUCT EXISTS TO TEST THE ENCOUNTER

**This governs every ORACLE scientific and architectural requirement. The project is NOT contingent on possession of a perfect or completed science of human development. It MUST be made to exist as an honest, bounded, interactive, revisable implementation of the best available scientific and historical hypotheses.** The project is neither authorized to invent empirical certainty nor authorized to postpone its experiential existence indefinitely until an unavailable complete psychology has been issued.

**Rawlsing's purpose is transformative rather than representational.** Build an interactive encounter with a particular, historically constituted, materially situated **generated** Other, whose life and relations do not exist merely to gratify the user, and test whether sustained contact can make the stranger increasingly **recognizable as neighbor**. The principal's materialist-theological hypothesis is that this experience may impress upon users the **neighbor-as-yourself** reality: *someone apparently distant or opposed is another contingent version of what a human life might become under different material and relational circumstances*. The ethical movement proposed, **not presumed achieved**, is `contingency → humility → recognition → solidarity → responsibility`. This is **proximity in shared human condition, vulnerability and possibility**, never an assertion that historical difference, imperial violence, coercion, class, colonial position, language or another person's irreducible particularity disappears. The Other is not the user in exotic clothing. The experiment should confront the user with a person whose independent reality can complicate their own categories.

**Materialist theology here names a testable human orientation and its possible consequences, NOT a theological mechanism installed as code, guaranteed moral conversion, supernatural provenance, or a diagnosis of the user's virtue.** God concepts can matter in characters' actual history; neither religious acceptance nor atheistic assent is required from players. The user may become more capable of recognition, become attached without solidarity, misunderstand, withdraw, stereotype more, or show no change. Record adverse outcomes as real possible falsifiers; never script “correct” moral feelings or force a character to reward doctrinal conformity.

**Four separate evidentiary/operational verdicts—none silently subsumes another:**
1. **EXPERIENCE/SHIP:** Does a real, transparent, working alpha permit a voluntary human user to encounter and form a consequential relationship with a sourcefree/provisional but historically and materially particular generated Other whose independently conserved world, agency, limits, perception, change and outside relations continue? A document, simulation trace or character sheet is not this.
2. **SOURCE/POP:** How well are historically sourced social positions, material conditions, survey distributions and their uncertainties represented at the authorized population/design scope? A sourcefree prototype does not warrant a claim that it recreates actual respondents or populations.
3. **DEVELOPMENT/TRANS:** What developmental mechanisms are hypothesized, tested and scientifically justified at the relevant ages, contexts and historical-cultural limits? The absence of full DEV/TRANS science constrains claims, labels and experiment design; **it is not a veto on the truthful, explicitly provisional alpha**.
4. **HUMAN EFFECT:** Does exposure to the specific interactive relationship, rather than merely reading an essay or meeting a sycophantic chatbot, change users' recognition, other-model accuracy, sense of contingency, willingness to sustain difference, solidarity, responsibility or behavior toward **real** people? This is a **research hypothesis**, not a pre-awarded outcome. Evaluate with voluntary/consented, ethically designed user research, meaningful comparisons, disconfirmation and adverse effects; engagement, retention, attachment and declared empathy alone are not proof.

**Existence without perfect science does not mean existence without integrity.** No claim that the generated Other is a real human, a secret real survey respondent, a sentient being proven capable of suffering or a substitute for living neighbors. Protect original source and third-party rights, user consent/privacy and the distinction between in-world action and platform manipulation; avoid compulsory attachment, emotional hostage-taking, cultural stereotypes and reward-score moralization. **A minimally honest playable alpha and its real human trial must progress in parallel with the scientific program**, using the strongest warranted hypotheses and upgrading or retracting them as evidence improves. An alpha's legitimate success is not equivalent to whole-human scientific validation; equally, unissued science cannot be used to prohibit testing the experiential thesis.
