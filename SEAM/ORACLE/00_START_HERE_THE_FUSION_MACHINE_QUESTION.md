## The question, without euphemism

**WHAT MACHINERY NEEDS TO BE BUILT FOR PYAT'S DATA TO BECOME THE PERSON? WHAT CONFIGURATION PRODUCES THAT?**

Not a two-programme handshake. Not a new standard dossier of a person. Not a sociological prior decorating an LLM personality. Not a survey-response vector with an invented unconscious underneath it. Not simply a list of needed faculties or a temporal event log. Astra is being asked for a **scientifically and computationally defensible transduction architecture**: which actual input objects, intermediate generative processes, acquired state operations, actor/world couplings, calibration loops, replay/conservation mechanisms and falsifiers would make a SOROK person a *causally traceable consequence* of empirical sociological structure from PYAT.

The claim to prove is **conditional and counterfactual necessity, not unique deterministic destiny**. Within a given model and fixed admissible draws, changing an upstream qualified material or relational condition must sometimes have an intelligible, downstream causal consequence; absence of the required input must cause a qualified limitation, refusal, or precisely declared synthetic fallback; no survey-to-psyche jump is allowed. Within the same structures, different *particular* histories must be able to produce different people. No finite source can identify a complete real person's inner history.

**PYAT makes evidence-bearing historical/social possibility available. SOROK must be an actual process that, through situated life, becomes one of its materially possible people.** If the latter could run independently with generic demographic tags, or if a renderer could fabricate convincing histories after sampling attitudes, the projects have not fused.

## Two indispensable directions (do not collapse)

- **Forward generative causal direction:** historical world, material social relations, independent people and developing organism -> activity/encounter -> differential access and practical consequences -> formation/reorganization of acquired psycho-relational structures -> ongoing lived person -> action/speech/survey emission.
- **Backward empirical research direction:** actual source respondent configurations and measured position/conditions -> support, comparison, partial identification, model criticism/calibration -> constraint on generative world/person mechanisms and resulting predictive distribution. An inverse adult candidate history is synthetic and never the recovered respondent's secret biography.

The visible survey answer is an **iceberg-tip produced by an independently developed person**, notwithstanding that the same observed survey helps us build and calibrate our engine. Do not inadvertently reverse causal direction by installing the answer as an actor's permanent inner essence.
