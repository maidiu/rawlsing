This file's A/B/C designs are unadopted hypotheses, and no amount of mechanical ablation upgrades them to psychological science.\n\n## 0. Define 'necessary consequence' correctly

Rawlsing cannot claim that the real survey uniquely entails one true individual biography. The many-to-one observation problem and historical agency prohibit that.

It *can* demand four stronger, testable dependencies:

- **Admissibility necessity:** source-qualified world/position/period conditions determine which fictional histories, encounters, actions and observable adult configurations are allowable, disallowed or unidentifiable.
- **Distributional necessity:** changing properly qualified PYAT joint/conditional material/social evidence changes supported ensembles of possible lives/observables, with typed uncertainty and exceptions, *where the identified mechanism makes that evidence relevant*. Equal conditional inputs may admit different persons.
- **Mechanism necessity:** structural facts become actual conditions of activity and are consumed by independent actor/world developmental rules; some downstream actions and acquired states are traceable through concrete events to those source-derived facts.
- **Mechanical ablation necessity:** deleting or corrupting relevant PYAT inputs alters the reachable world/person/action or yields honest hold; retaining irrelevant inputs does not spuriously alter personhood. A prose renderer or generic actor simulator cannot replace the missing causal edge.

'Necessary' = source-to-person consequential dependence **conditional on an empirically justified mechanism and stochastic/world choices**, not deterministic derivation from demographics or guaranteed psychological effect of every source item.

## 1. Minimal dynamic causal hypothesis — name every owner

Let W(t) be a historically versioned objective/material world; D(i,t) embodied/social position; B(i,t) the organismic state; R(i,j,t) the real and perceived relationship structures kept separate; G(i,t) the acquired sparse personal relational field, practices, mediators, expectations, contextual tensions and causal traces; C(i,t) current accessible consciousness/claims; A(i,t) available/attempted/performed activity; O(Q,i,t) the answers given to a particular survey Q.

Candidate processes (operators must be actually implemented/authorized or named MISSING):

**OP-0 source compiler (PYAT):** admitted original survey event/question/risk/design and material/institutional evidence -> queried population position/world/attitude possibility and evidence ceiling, with exact negatives.

**OP-1 historically situated world constructor (PYAT/world):** independent source-qualified material and institutional relations, time and resource constraints + explicit selected synthetic scenario distributions -> conserved world/position opportunity structures. Do not confuse this with actor psychology.

**OP-2 independent actor seed/organism (SOROK/ADP):** body, natal situation, care dependency, nonuniform biological timing and capacities, material provenance, no inherited opinions or global adult traits.

**OP-3 activity and encounter scheduler (world + actor):** body needs, household labor/time/care, institutional obligations, space, partners/kin, current actor goals and coactor agency -> feasible opportunities, actual performed acts/omissions and actual event ledger. World laws refuse incompatible actions. Population social-field configurations can condition *eligible coactor possibilities* where source supports them, not actual interpersonal links by statistical fiat.

**OP-4 actor-specific uptake (SOROK):** actual event -> specific actor's accessible cues (not omniscient objective report), developmental and language capacities, attention, prior familiarities/meanings -> experienced occasion, possible uncertainty/misrecognition/no exposure.

**OP-5 **acquisition and structural change** (SOROK/CAN/ADP):** actor-owned experience and qualifying repetition/contrast, biological enablement, current practices/expectations, practical consequences and qualified formation rule -> CREATE/REINFORCE/NARROW/GENERALIZE/DIFFERENTIATE/WEAKEN/TRANSFORM/DORMANT/NO_CHANGE/UNKNOWN, with exact lineage. Need explicit formation rules, not LLM-authored interpersonal wisdom; underdetermined mechanisms sourcefree or HELD.

**OP-6 PFF typed link organizer (SOROK):** grounded acquisitions and actual activity -> conditional relations among learned elements (e.g., activation, inhibition, justification, compensation, compartmentalization, contextual priority). Structural categories as acquired semantic objects, not mandatory universal contents. Existing CAN-011 candidate ontology and PFF derived index first, not a rival authoritative graph.

**OP-7 ongoing salience/selection (SOROK):** needs/desires and organismic state + G/R + material feasibility + current information -> actor-specific candidate action, competition, choice/attempt under legitimate decision rules. Historical meaning modulates what actor considers; no world-side dictation of motive or identical action by demographic group.

**OP-8 slow recurrent organization (SOROK/C5 candidate):** supported longitudinal motifs of OP-5/6/7 -> optional compressed attractor state; measured value above history-only H0 required before it has privileged causal effect. Never direct Enneagram assignment and no unqualified 'wound'.

**OP-9 reflective self/world/other construction (SOROK):** qualified accessible language, shared practices, social recognition/misrecognition, institutional claims, self-interpreted position, identification and alienation -> fallible knowledge, interpretation, possible reflective contradiction. Absolute Horizon is a possible historically mediated ranking/relativization of claims, distinct from religion label and no automatic goodness.

**OP-10 survey emission (SOROK observation port, with PYAT Q):** historically appropriate instrument wording, options, response context/mode, asked/eligible and available actor meaning/self-interpretation -> a reported answer (or nonresponse). It cannot read true internal motive directly or force survey answer to match an initial tip forever.

**OP-11 population predictive critic (PYAT/research, outside actor life):** compare ensemble O(Q,i,t) at valid population/period/estimand against actual same-person empirical joints, conditional minority patterns, material position correlations and uncertainty; use held-out survey/wave or other external process evidence where lawful. Must not secretly mutate a chosen actor's prior history after future events to improve global fit.

**OP-12 independent world feedback (SOROK world + SDTF):** admitted attempted/performed acts, coactor responses and externally qualified effects -> changed world determinants/invalidation and further contexts. No intention-to-external-fact shortcut; immutable source stays immutable; targeted invalidate rather than full-world rewrite.

These are **conceptual operations**. Astra must identify which are already implemented by exact canonical owners and which are indispensable absent research, then select a minimal first causal path, not fabricate a monolithic new engine.

## 2. Potential mathematical shape (not yet an estimable model)

A forward joint generative process could factor conceptually as:

- Sample/choose W, institutional/material positions and initial bio-social conditions under evidence-qualified PYAT field F and explicit scenario parameters.
- For each independently acting i and time t: generate available options from W,B,R; execute actor choices conditioned on G,C,actual access; world-admit E; update actor-specific perceptions; apply qualified history-dependent learning; continue world and person.
- At selected adulthood, emit O(Q,i,t) under the appropriate questionnaire; compare population-level implied distribution to qualified empirical joint P_obs(Y_joint | D,W,Q,eligible/asked,design).

The model parameters for child learning, sociohistorical context variation, family pairing and observation likelihood are NOT all identified from cross-sectional surveys. No hidden 'real person' posterior may be declared from an elegant factorization. It is not enough to minimize survey prediction loss: a direct classifier can match survey observations and still fail as a human developmental engine.

We need **multiple independent validation targets**: longitudinal change under known occupational autonomy, historical/cohort constraints, childcare/activity observations, socialization and context shifts, action/relationship behavior, source-specific survey structures, and source-free adversarial invariants.

## 3. Temporal organization options A/B/C — NOT three independent scientific theories

### Temporal option A — constrained forward social micro-ontogenesis (world-first)

- PYAT emits a source-qualified field: objective world/position, material constraints, social and attitudinal heterogeneity, permitted coactor/household possibility pools and uncertainty.
- SOROK constructs independent mother and other actor life histories, with ordinary structured activity plus higher-resolution events, using common acquisition/PFF rules.
- Later survey answers are **predictions** of generated humans, not sampled target facts. Reweight mechanism parameters/population branching only via separate validated aggregate/holdout comparison.
- Strength: clean causal order; strong historical/provenance continuity, compatible with origin/child model, avoids psychological hindsight.
- Weakness: enormous search and compute; sparse observed fields may fail to constrain mechanisms tightly; matching empirical distributions could be difficult or nonidentifiable. Adult mother prototype may be expensive.
- Falsifier: cannot reproduce within-position rare high-order configurations even with broad admissible histories; acquires equally sophisticated adult ideology in infant or no-language environments; source ablation produces no change.

### Temporal option B — inverse constrained adult history synthesis, then prospective living

- PYAT samples an actual *synthetic observation target* as a source-qualified joint respondent tip (not necessarily any named real respondent). SOROK proposes N historically feasible prehistories using same child/parent ontology at a cheaper resolution, advances each **forward** to a possible adult.
- Compare adult emitted survey responses with target observation. Retain a *set* of compatible generated histories; select one by explicit model/proposal likelihood or honest nonprobabilistic constraint, keeping rejected candidates as alternatives.
- Commit one fictional history; **from then forward** the person lives independently and may differ from their original tip when conditions change.
- Strength: practical adult initialization and 'same tip/different icebergs' proof; puts full respondent joints to work with ordinary prototype economics.
- Weakness: danger of backwards storytelling and posterior fiction; unknown developmental prior, answer-emission likelihood, assent meaning and selection probabilities. If LLM merely invents candidate life to match response it violates the standard.
- Falsifier: chosen history has no effect on later behavior; can arbitrarily change any detail to fit all tips; silently computes numeric posterior from unvalidated terms.

### Temporal option C — calibrated hybrid / hierarchical programme of development

- PYAT-conditioned field calibrates ensemble distributions of material opportunities, institutions, language/practice repertoires, repeated activity regimes and observable answer patterns; SOROK's same primitive developmental operators yield actor-specific state under actual life events.
- A cheap source- and science-graded **mesoscopic time-window process** represents ordinary work/care/social participation with conserved opportunities and exposure counts; fine event recursion activates only where ordering, personal recognition or consequential novelty matters. Slow PFF motifs as derived history summaries.
- For preexisting adults, inverse B is used within bounded candidate prehistories and only with honest identification limits. For newborns and later events, A's forward machinery dominates. Population calibration/critic is a separate research layer.
- Strength: plausible research/economic balance, same ontology at multiple temporal resolutions, keeps actor life prospective once committed; can simulate many varied people.
- Weakness: highest danger of coarse summary inventing unsimulated episodes/learning, cached attractor becoming a hidden label, aggregate calibration laundering unsupported causal mechanisms, and coupling source population to fictional household pairs without evidence.
- Falsifier: coarsened process and fine process lead to contradictory later actors under equal qualified coverage; changed data affect narrative but no performed outcome; different histories produce identical actor behavior except prose.

**UM-SOROK R2's architectural-bias objection is accepted.** A and B are competing *adult initialization/generative direction strategies*; C combines them to satisfy both newborn-forward and adult inverse tasks. C may be operationally attractive **by definition**, which is not evidence that the underlying developmental computation is scientifically right. Do not award C the victory because it inherits A's and B's intended features. Decouple *temporal initialization* (A/B/C) from *what mechanistically constitutes acquired personhood* (D/E/F below).

### Genuine developmental-computation rival D — persistent, actor-owned acquired relational topology

- Actor state includes a dynamically earned sparse CAN/ADP/PFF network of acquired practices, expectations, inhibitions, recognition/power relations, memory parentage and potentially derived C5 motifs; no default fixed modern psychotypes.
- **Claim needing proof:** learned relational links and their context-conditional interaction have independent predictive/causal and compression value for later performed behavior, rather than merely naming what a policy could infer from history.
- **Cost:** state graph, formation-rule science, lineage, storage/identity, temporal granularity and actor independence; cannot call a schema's CREATE permission a qualified developmental law.
- **Falsifiers:** an event+context-only policy predicts the same held-out behavior and counterfactuals with lower complexity; graph edges alter prose but never later real decisions; topology is invented retrospectively.

### Genuine rival E — bounded activity-policy with event/context memory, without a persistent PFF graph

- Keep bodily need, socially organized activity, actual coactors, individual accessible events, material affordances, historical context and selective episodic memories; an event-conditioned policy produces action without independent stored psychological mediator-network identity. Learned policy parameters/limited memory can still change with history.
- **Claim needing proof:** repeated history and context-sensitive action do not need separately reified graph nodes to express ordinary dispositions, contradictions, different social identifications and practical agency at the chosen observation resolution.
- **Cost:** possible short memory, hidden monolithic policy and poor counterfactual explainability; danger of LLM-only improvisation that cannot conserve why a change occurred.
- **Falsifiers:** requires unsupported hidden memory to capture durable cross-context shifts; cannot explain contrary behavior with same recent context but different distant causal histories; fails explicit inherited relation/rupture obligations.

### Genuine rival F — restricted empirically qualified learning primitives first

- Begin with a narrow independently supported set of development mechanisms (e.g. age/context-bounded caregiver response/attention/routine acquisition) and conservative actor state, delaying broader Horizon/ideological/PFF claims until independent evidence is obtainable. This need not assume either D's graph or E's policy is sufficient.
- **Claim needing proof:** smaller identified mechanisms and honest UNMODELED domains produce more defensible causal predictions than an unvalidated whole-person dynamical grammar.
- **Cost:** limited expressivity and fewer culturally complex adults; cannot fulfill the strongest Rawlsing human representation aspiration until extended.
- **Falsifiers:** even within supported domain it cannot predict actual exposure/learning counterfactuals; results are no better than simple observation sampling; impossibly fragmented laws defeat consistent person continuity.

**Required independent comparison:** Astra must compare at least **one D/E/F architecture against a genuinely incompatible other**, under the same qualified PYAT-produced empirical fact, world/coactor first reader, actor access, later action, source/owner permissions and replay. A/B/C may be used as orthogonal initialization strategies for each. Score **POP population generalization, DEV developmental mechanism, TRANS historical transport separately**, as well as mechanical PYAT→SOROK actual-value reachability, event memory, complexity, latency, loss of psychical meaning, and source-negative behavior; no option wins by having more named features. Every chosen mechanism needs a falsifier that could cause its rejection; `NONE_ADMISSIBLE_YET` is an authorized scientific result.

**Astra need not pick A/B/C, D/E/F or a prewritten hybrid.** The dossier's candidates are *challenge targets, not recommended answers*. A composed hybrid is eligible only if its imported mechanisms have distinct, independently required and tested functions. It must propose a different architecture if these fail, including a narrower admissible project claim where necessary.

## 4. What would make the 'configuration' specifically executable?

Astra should specify a compact object/port schema of **semantics**, not generic 'more fields'. A plausible minimal set of source- and actor-owned receipts:

- **EmpiricalFieldReceipt**: exact C11/I03 F4/N10 empirical and structural product refs, universe/time/risk/denominator/weight, joint response support, uncertainty, positions, competing fields, missingness and use grade.
- **WorldSituationReceipt**: selected N9 rights/resources/institution/coactor place/time, real feasibility and source-vs-synthetic distinction, currentness, no private psyche. Distinguish reported norm from actual sanction.
- **ActorSeedReceipt**: biological/material start, genealogy, actual access to world, role, owner and named random/proposal constraints; no survey psyche default.
- **ActivityAndExposureReceipt**: objective event and material cost, actor co-presence, performed actions, actor-specific accessible cues, developmental coverage, not-modeled/unknown states.
- **FormationReceipt**: pre-state refs, experience and qualified rule, actual differentiation/expectation/mediator/practice/tension/memory changes or explicit NO_CHANGE/HOLD, causal refs and replay.
- **PersonalTopologyReceipt**: actor-specific sparse state/typed relational links, activation/dormancy, contradiction and metaperception, PFF lineages; derive from CAN-owned state before proposing a new graph truth.
- **ActionAndWorldEffectReceipt**: actual context, actor-option set, prior PFF consulted, attempted vs performed vs denied, independent Other response, objective effect and later first-reader.
- **SurveyEmissionReceipt**: exact Q and respondent/eligibility semantics, actor accessible answer, missing/no response, emission model and link to current generated state; no reverse writing into past.
- **ModelCriticismReceipt**: ensemble scope, observed target, holdout, joint rarity, material fit, failure and counterfactual data-ablation evidence; never certification of a real person's private psyche.

One or more may be views over current owners; **do not instantiate redundant stores merely because an ORACLE sketch names a receipt**.

The first proof must specify a full **input → rule → transformation → output → first effect** row for each required step, with actual machine-readable artifact and explicit absent rule where needed.

## 5. The key calibration issue: how PYAT is made generatively consequential

At least four different modes of constraint must be compared:

**Mode I, hard world constraint:** source-qualified objective rights/resources, period, geography, timing, work/household opportunity constrain feasible acts. Strongest and most readily auditable. But doesn't uniquely produce semantic life.

**Mode II, social reproduction/practice distribution:** source-supported patterns of schooling, work autonomy, household care, religious participation and intergroup contact condition which practices a fictional person can actually encounter. Requires separate distributional measures and world links; survey attitudes alone cannot establish exposure.

**Mode III, heterogeneous reported-meaning field:** source respondent-joint patterns constrain *possible* public self-descriptions and the social heterogeneity of adult alternatives. True 3+ joint matters; field supplies competing adult candidate configurations, **not** an individual child's mental contents.

**Mode IV, developmental mechanism calibration:** evidence beyond surveys links particular practiced conditions and meaningful exposure to changes in discernment, affect regulation, identity, authority relations, learning and action. This is the hardest science. It cannot be substituted with opaque scalar correlations or the LLM's belief about what a social class feels.

A strong machine uses all four where authorized, with honest holes. The empirical validity of I–III cannot launder IV. Conversely failure of IV does not render I–III meaningless, only leaves the full person-generation claim held.

## 6. Required birth-to-survey and respondent-to-parent dual tests

The ultimate vertical should execute both:

1. **Birth→adult→survey**: start an organism in a selected historical household; differentiate activity, learning and PFF over epochs; later administer the original instrument; evaluate population response configurations, not just one archetypal prose profile.
2. **Adult tip→multiple pasts→independent future**: condition on one plausible reported configuration from original source; construct *several* distinct compressed prehistories consistent with it; after commitment, change material circumstances or encounter. Different subsequent actions must be caused by prior acquired states even if initial emitted surveys are the same. If not, adult iceberg remains decorative.

Both tests must include independent partner/caregiver histories, social and institutional Others, ordinary ongoing life, specificity of coactor access and survey nonresponse. A single fully narrated case can be a demonstration but not population calibration.

## 7. Model complexity and computational design economics

The person is an evolving historical graph/process, but no automatic requirement to adopt Neo4j, xarray or Parquet as separate technologies. Reuse current typed state and immutable event ledger. If a representation is needed for efficient replay, provenance and queries, choose it only after proving:
- temporal storage and recovery of selected actual changes, no post hoc psychological causes;
- sparse actor-variable psychological topology and independently changing coactors;
- compressed ordinary life with bounded loss and explicit unsimulated spans;
- online first-reader queries and per-actor context activation, not all-history reprocessing;
- named stochastic draws, reproducible counterfactual forks, restored cold replay and predictable invalidation;
- CPU/RSS/scratch/retained growth with whole-household/person-years, not just a one-scene 1k fixture;
- independently evaluated scientific/rule versions, not retroactive silent data refitting.

No fabricated benchmark or acceptance time target. ANVIL's ≤6 GiB constraint applies to the upstream C11 pilot; future V3 budgets need separately authorized targets. Don't make a giant memory-graph a precondition for a single legitimate first experimental edge.

## 8. Strongest fork Astra must decide

**Is a new generative mechanism genuinely indispensable at the PYAT→SOROK boundary, or can existing PYAT world/possibility selectors and existing SOROK CAN/ADP/PFF operations achieve it with a small, qualified adapter and a few missing developmental rules?**

If existing owner functionality suffices, identify exact symbols, methods, first consumers and adversarial evidence. If not, specify the **minimal missing transformation** and why neither end can lawfully perform it alone.

Potential actual missing sciences: material/social practice → first-person exposure model; experience/repetition/contrast → specific acquired discrimination; personal topology links → later lawful action; empirically calibrated survey observation function; source-qualified adult partner/contact generation. Which is the highest-leverage research target may differ from the most visually compelling software component.

## 9. What not to mistake for a proof

- A source-conditioned personality description.
- Matching observed attitude marginals or even all joint distributions without developmental causality.
- PFF fields changed only by a rule that explicitly reads survey opinions.
- A field with valid hashes that never changes coactor world options or later actor choice.
- An internally coherent LLM backstory that is selected *after* a desired parenting reaction.
- Fictional child learns 'gender egalitarianism' because mother answered yes to women's inheritance.
- A learning rule that always creates something from every experience.
- A slow C5 attractor assigned from an ideology/Enneagram label instead of derived from repeated historical motifs.
- A human identity supposedly emerging from 12 salient scenes while ordinary intervening life is unmodeled.
- A model whose actor concept repertoire is essentially an English-speaking contemporary adult even without language/socialization.
- Full data retention mistaken for meaningful sociological transformation.
