## Why this archive matters

Earlier research observed genuine tensions that a scientific seam must confront: cross-sectional joint-response evidence does not identify private developmental history; whole-respondent source morphology is different from survey-based trait initialization; source-prevalence does not establish local contact or social power; valid external conditions are not automatically acquired interpretations; an independently developing actor can eventually express both source-constrained variation and unobserved richer psychology; and sourcefull mechanical wiring does not prove developmental or historical adequacy.

These tensions are **constraints on truthfulness and generative adequacy**, not a prescribed methodology for resolving them.

The prior author proposed several possibilities, for example forward or inverse history conditioning, a constrained hybrid, actor-owned sparse psychological topology versus history/context policy, and narrower scientifically supported learning processes. These were **research hypotheses written by ORACLE's previous author**. Some may inform the eventual solution; some may be misleading, overdetermined, overengineered or outside ORACLE's jurisdiction. Astra is not required to choose among them, refute a particular number, test a named 'mode', produce a receipt hierarchy, or use their variables.
