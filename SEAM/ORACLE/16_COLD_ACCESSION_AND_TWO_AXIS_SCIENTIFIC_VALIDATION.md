### Axis S — three scientifically distinct verdicts, NEVER one pooled S2

**Source-correct population fit (`POP`)**: At the actually licensed source/wave/eligible/asked/observed respondent and design scope, does the generated ensemble reproduce *held-out whole-respondent joint distributions*, rare configurations, risk bases, weights, missingness and conditional minority alternatives with valid uncertainty? The questionnaire is a measurement instrument with conditioning/response biases, not direct psychological truth. A direct conditional sampler is a legitimate baseline. A positive result says the distribution of an observation is fit at that scope, **not** that a person formed developmentally.

**Developmental causal adequacy (`DEV`)**: Does a specified organism/activity/care/need/recognition/power/exposure→acquisition/no-change rule predict *independently observed* developmental change and survive credible confounding/selection, alternative rule, intervention/natural-experiment or longitudinal/ethnographic challenge as applicable? Report exact age, language, context, subjective access, repeat exposures and formation time. Infant transition laws are not identified from a cross-sectional adult joint. A plausible child narrative, sourcefree trace or re-created mother's answers yields **no DEV causal pass**.

**Historical/cultural transport (`TRANS`)**: Under what evidence does that *particular* mechanism hold, or require alteration, across different historical periods, material production relations, languages, class/colonial/imperial positioning, family/care arrangements, institutional carriers and available psychological concepts? Preserve process regularities versus acquired content. **Successful DEV in one bounded present-day setting is not proof of nineteenth-century, nonindustrial or otherwise different cultural-historical validity.** When external data/comparability or source rights are missing, retain an explicit limited scope, do not extrapolate.

## 3. Survey calibration and inverse-history leakage prevention

Sampling target must be explicit: distribution over survey-eligible source respondents at source/wave/population/design + synthetic historical actor ensemble; observed respondent-**joint** probabilities and minority tails conditional on legitimately comparable material positions, not one aesthetically plausible response vector.

**Separation BEFORE any history fitting, architecture tuning or learning-rule selection:**
- For each authorized survey population, freeze source/version/form/wave and divide **whole respondents** into train, development/selection and untouched evaluation/holdout sets. Never split answer items from the same respondent across training and test to claim independent whole-person generalization; handle actual sample clusters, weights/design and eligibility/asked separately.
- Pre-register which statements and historical/material conditions inform candidate adult history proposal. If an actual source row/joint configuration is used to choose a particular synthetic mother, **that same row/configuration cannot constitute the independent success test** for the mother's eventual answers. A holdout group/future wave that has not influenced candidate construction must be used for population-level predictions, with source comparability explicitly qualified; where source count is small, report uncertainty rather than split until meaningless.
- Split **model fitting and calibration** from behavioral/longitudinal validation. A model might predict a held-out survey joint but still be a classifier that has no acquisition science. Conversely a bounded developmental rule may predict observed care behavior without explaining all national survey rates.
- Define predictive metrics appropriate to source: weighted joint log loss/Brier where supported, calibration on true 3+ co-occurrence and minority subgroups, risk-set and nonresponse calibration, uncertainty intervals and effective-N. Do not invent n or infer that one matched trio is a population.
- Construct an **independent developmental holdout** for the selected acquisition rule: observed child-care recurrence, learning/behavior before and after context variation, language/age bounds, or a disclosed theoretical test environment if real data unavailable. Same teacher-generated expected string is not evidence.
- If full adult inverse prior, expression likelihood, contact rates and developmental parameter families cannot be identified from existing data, report **NOT_IDENTIFIED** for those estimands. Suggest targeted new sources, do not tune an invented prior until predictions look right.

**One false-positive example:** sample observed C's Islamic-law/gender/party responses as her latent "values"; generate two styled biographies; compare the mother's survey output to C; claim fit. This only demonstrates answer reconstruction conditioned on the answers. It neither tests a separately valid person's development nor explains childrearing.
