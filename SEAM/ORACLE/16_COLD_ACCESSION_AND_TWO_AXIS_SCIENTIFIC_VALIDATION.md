### Axis S — three scientifically distinct verdicts, NEVER one pooled S2

**Source-correct population fit (`POP`)**: At the actually licensed source/wave/eligible/asked/observed respondent and design scope, does the generated ensemble reproduce *held-out whole-respondent joint distributions*, rare configurations, risk bases, weights, missingness and conditional minority alternatives with valid uncertainty? The questionnaire is a measurement instrument with conditioning/response biases, not direct psychological truth. A direct conditional sampler is a legitimate baseline. A positive result says the distribution of an observation is fit at that scope, **not** that a person formed developmentally.

**Developmental causal adequacy (`DEV`)**: Does a specified organism/activity/care/need/recognition/power/exposure→acquisition/no-change rule predict *independently observed* developmental change and survive credible confounding/selection, alternative rule, intervention/natural-experiment or longitudinal/ethnographic challenge as applicable? Report exact age, language, context, subjective access, repeat exposures and formation time. Infant transition laws are not identified from a cross-sectional adult joint. A plausible child narrative, sourcefree trace or re-created mother's answers yields **no DEV causal pass**.

**Historical/cultural transport (`TRANS`)**: Under what evidence does that *particular* mechanism hold, or require alteration, across different historical periods, material production relations, languages, class/colonial/imperial positioning, family/care arrangements, institutional carriers and available psychological concepts? Preserve process regularities versus acquired content. **Successful DEV in one bounded present-day setting is not proof of nineteenth-century, nonindustrial or otherwise different cultural-historical validity.** When external data/comparability or source rights are missing, retain an explicit limited scope, do not extrapolate.

Each of `POP`, `DEV`, `TRANS` MUST be independently reported as `UNTESTED_OR_NOT_IDENTIFIED`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `BOUNDED_INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE`, or `COMPETENT_OWNER_SCOPED_ISSUED`, with exact scope, object, method, data/provenance, rival explanations and uncertainty. Do **not** publish a single `S2` label: it would allow POP calibration to certify developmental science or DEV evidence to certify historical transport. A machine may be `POP=BOUNDED`, `DEV=UNTESTED`, `TRANS=UNTESTED`, with M2 sourcefree. That is neither a total failure nor a validated person generator.

A model without an *extra* private latent-ideology variable can still incorporate public institutional ideology and culturally organized practice; a result about representation does not prove ideology absent. Software-state sensitivity, plausible history generation, public practice continuity, independent actor chronology and human developmental validity are distinct kinds of claims. **Their specific formerly prescribed perturbation experiments, counts of candidate families and model baselines are withdrawn.**

## 3. Survey and developmental claims: evidence integrity without a prescribed evaluation design

**Sampling-target integrity:** A relevant eligible source population, survey instrument, wave and respondent-level conditional joint distribution are different from a synthetic adult actor ensemble. The survey measurement and the person's acquired psychology are also different. A claimed empirical fit must respect actual question applicability, source design, weights/missingness where relevant, and within-respondent dependence.

**Independence and leakage:** A claim of out-of-sample calibration or predictive developmental adequacy cannot be supported solely by the same respondent answers or simulated histories used to construct and tune its prediction. Retrospective fit to a sampled response vector is not evidence that the person's psyche historically generated that response. Statistical comparison does not identify an unobserved childhood prior, and absence of applicable independent historical/developmental evidence must restrict the claim.

**Methodology belongs to Astra and the competent scientific evaluators.** The previous training/development/holdout partition, specific survey metrics, pre-registration, childcare holdout and sample-splitting procedures were potential methods, not principal instructions. No particular split, scoring rule, rival model or experiment is imposed by ORACLE. If Astra claims generalization, its evidence must actually be independent in a scientifically defensible manner; if independent source information is not available, the correct grade remains limited.
