**Mechanical or documentary connectedness:** An existing source-backed output may be correctly delivered to a consumer. This establishes only the named source/value/code route at its tested scope. A sourcefree synthetic-world consumer is a research mechanism; it is not a verified PYAT-owned historical social relation.

**Substantive sociological conditioning:** A candidate PYAT-conditioned change can be materially or institutionally meaningful only if the inference from the qualified source product to the claimed operative social condition is separately warranted or openly hypothetical. Observed response prevalence, demographic similarity or higher-order covariance do not independently prove actual contacts, material sanctions, institutional enactment, religious practice or private motives.

### Important failures to avoid without preselecting a test

- **No automatic statistic→psyche transfer.** An already committed actor does not change because a survey summary elsewhere changed. The relevance and pathway of any source-conditioned formative effect must be explained, not assumed.
- **Indirect causal influence matters.** Material, institutional, social or symbolic influence can reach persons without direct contact with its originating carrier; 'no meeting' is not equivalent to 'no possible social mediation'.
- **Public institutional meaning and private acquisition differ.** An ideology may persist through real world practices and material arrangements without a separate mandatory private-ideology variable, and a private representation is not proof that public practice or development occurred.
- **Causal family chronology matters.** A father identified computationally late cannot acquire newly fabricated earlier effects on the mother, third parties or objective world. Temporal compatibility is not evidence of prior independently performed activity.
- **Inverse candidate-selection bias matters.** Candidate histories selected to match adult source observations are not automatically samples from an empirically identified historical prior. Batch or adaptive selection can change the induced distribution; a verbal claim that the sample 'came from PYAT' does not settle this.
- **Scientific owner authority matters.** PYAT production, SOROK world/actor performance, developmental science, original source rights, actual sourcefree execution and independent review each require their own applicable authority and evidence.
- **False completion claims must be refused.** The present historical source graph does not itself demonstrate full PYAT→SOROK person formation; unknown owner paths and missing science remain open until independently established.
