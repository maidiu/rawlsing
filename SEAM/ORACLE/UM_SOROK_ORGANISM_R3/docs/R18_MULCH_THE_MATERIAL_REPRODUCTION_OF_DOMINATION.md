## EXPOSE — four nonidentical historical clocks

1. **Sourcefree concession / historical permission:** an explicitly registered fictional institution may give a *particular actor* the opportunity to establish a controlled pantry. A right to establish something is NOT the thing already existing.
2. **Actor-mediated initial enclosure:** the prospective controller must actually `INSPECT` the applicable public concession, be present, possess two units of privately held food, spend them into the shared store, and finance the first enforcement service. The board exists only after this actual action.
3. **Effective ongoing reproduction:** the formal `CONTROLLED` relation can survive even when its financed enforcement interval expires. A controller can spend available funds through `MAINTAIN_PANTRY_GATE` to extend an effective restriction. Funds move into a distinct, objectively recorded institutional service account; they do not vanish into unmodeled space.
4. **Reform versus continuing counterpower:** the R17 two-independent-signature public procedure may change the rule to `SHARED_BY_COLLECTIVE_ACTS`. It does not retrospectively erase a controller's remaining funded capacity to target a person through `SANCTION_PANTRY_ACCESS`. The sanction has its own time, recipient, actual budget cost, notice and expiration. Thus a collective legal victory and contested practical access can diverge without making collective victory fake or eternal.
