## EXPOSE — the apparent triad was already too static

**Drive, practical agency, conscious intention are not separate compartments.** They distinguish causal *roles* potentially occupied within the same historical activity. Drive is not a quantity of wanting; it is a dynamic, materially embodied tendency, investment or pressure to continue, avoid, preserve, redirect or transform a relation. It may have no articulated object and no current action means. Agency is the practical performance or transformation of conditions under affordances, dependencies and opposition. Conscious intention is one *historically developable activity*, in which an actor can take some of their own prospective conduct as an object. It can become causally consequential without becoming the full causal author.

Raymond Williams, *Base and Superstructure in Marxist Cultural Theory* (1973), distinguishes historical determination as limits and pressures from predicted/prefigured results. The iceberg is not a predetermined output table; it is an ongoing production, itself partly transformed by later action. The surface tip is not freedom contrasted with a determined internal base. A person can initiate a future-regulating practice that remains conditioned by work schedules, fatigue, shame, dependency, tacit desire and the resistance of Others. **Conscious activity belongs to the iceberg's historical production, not above it.**

Leontiev's activity–action–operation distinction rescues the difference between a technical performance, a selected practical goal and a historically organizing motive. Personal sense and emotion can diverge from technical success; an action may become a new activity through changed motive. Vygotsky's *perezhivanie* rescues the developmental middle: the objective event is neither the person's lived experience nor the later psychic afterlife. Luria makes acquisition of relations among functions rather than mere accumulation of concept labels central. Sève distinguishes acquisition/exercise of powers and the allocation of time across a life. Lahire gives heterogeneous contextual activation, nonconscious analogical transfer, inhibition and failed resemblance. Dreier expands the unit to a whole conduct of life. Holzkamp refuses both environment-as-command and unrestricted individual choice: conditions are socially distributed *possibilities of action* which become individual premises in a person's own practical situation. Deleuze and Guattari resist desire-as-deficit and the isolated sovereign subject; their productive conception does not require that development improves, emancipates or becomes coherent.

Their disagreements should not be falsified into a new unified universal psychology. This is a sourcefree *generative construction grammar*. Its task is a causally intelligible fictional life, not a claim to infer any real person's private motives or clinical mind.
