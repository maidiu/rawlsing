# SORACLE R12 — POWER IS MEDIATED; FACILITY IS NOT SOVEREIGN MASTERY

**User interruption to ongoing R12 MULCH, 2026-11-20. This is a theoretical correction with actual source changes, not an assertion of protected authority, human calibration or executed tests.**

## SAVAGE — "independently exercisable power" was the wrong category

The first four R12 construction passes used "independent power" to mean "an operation currently attempted without the particular prior helper". That operational contrast is sometimes useful, but it is a conceptual category error to call it independence in the broader sociohistorical sense. No human power is independently generated ex nihilo. An individual acquires and deploys bodily potential, tools, language, collectively constituted signs, institutional recognition, resources, care and practices. A specific operation performed without Mother does not make the actor independent of the world and collective knowledge that constituted the operation.

Equally serious: the research machine's ability to identify objective coactor contributions or material permissions is not a power of the actor to recognize those sources. Most learned and practiced social capacities need not involve sophisticated reflective knowledge of their own material provenance. Reflective understanding may be partial, mistaken, rationalizing, absent or itself a historically acquired agentive power.

Three separate epistemic owners:
1. **Objective / analyst**: the world actually executes physical operations, assistance, material site rights and institutional rules. Its receipt is constrained to events that the program really models; it isn't metaphysical causal omniscience.
2. **Actor operative**: historically developed components, sensitivities, anticipation, coordination and ability to marshal means, whether conscious or tacit.
3. **Actor reflective**: contingent, fallible interpretations of the sources, boundaries and social meaning of one's power, formed only through separately qualified psychological history.

Foucault's mode of action upon others' possible action is NOT interchangeable with technical skill, and power is not identical to direct violence. A hammer can alter material things; a language user can invoke collectively maintained conventions; an official can shape others' possible conduct; a coercive actor can deploy threat or bodily force. These require different analyses. No global personal power number is valid.

## RECOVER — several dimensions, not a master scalar

Technical **component facility** can be modeled as a provisional bounded gradient in a specific domain and context. But other dimensions are *not* reducible to that gradient:

- Actual bodily susceptibility/ability is a separate organismic condition.
- Component technical facility has context, history, skill interval and uncertain transfer.
- The socially distributed activity may require Other contributions whose policy remains independent.
- Mobilization of help is a distinct acquired relational power with uncertain success; support does not prove mastery.
- Material ownership, site access and permission are independent objective circumstances, possibly coercive or unequal.
- Institutional power over others' conduct is a relation, not an individual technical achievement.
- The actor's knowledge of any of these is a further, independently formed and potentially false psychological organization.

Leontiev's activity/action/operation distinction, Vygotsky's guided development, Lave/Wenger's participation, Sève's formation of powers through temporally organized activity, and Foucault's conduct-of-conduct each illuminate a different relation; none licenses a universal stepwise climb from assisted to autonomous mastery.

## RECONSTITUTE — compressed growth without fake scenes or freak competence

The number of HIGH-RES simulation receipts is not the number of developmental occasions in the fictional life. R12's previous 'PERFORMED' record proves at most an attempted and objectively categorized operation in the model, NOT measured technical quality. Conversely, refusing all growth unless dozens of receipts exist condemns parent characters and Nadia to infantile capabilities under a finite recursion budget.

Two independent modeled quantities therefore matter:
- **Estimated operational facility** in a domain and current context.
- **Evidential resolution / interval width** supporting that estimate.

A long, source-qualified, actor-specific modeled practice period can contribute to a plausible *developmental estimate* without fabricating daily experiences or direct observation of each success. It retains wider uncertainty than independently observed detailed practice. One high-resolution joint act cannot instantly create mastery; decades of plausible routine cannot create unlimited competence, ownership of tools, other people's cooperation or knowledge of why one succeeds. No principle requires accumulated expertise to coincide with increased material command.

The model-development rates, baseline and aggregation rules are provisional, not scientific parameters extracted from any theorist. An unrecorded practice interval is not a recorded failure; it is UNKNOWN. Ordinary aging is not automatic expertise. A hypothetical parent can carry substantial fictional practice history, but it must remain marked as underidentified backfill, not a respondent's recovered personal history.

## TURN THE MACHINE AGAINST THE THEORY — new contradictions

1. **One competence estimate is only a component, not a life power.** Coordination quality, ability to mobilize social help and capacity to alter institutions require their own historically constituted operations. Do not sum them into individual mastery.
2. **Compressed process provenance can be abused.** Source-qualified modeled practice is not direct evidence. The fictional adult proposal must eventually reconcile labor-hour budgets, concurrent obligations, plausible location, life course, assistance availability and contradictory evidence. Missing time-allocation validation is an architectural gap, not evidence that fabricated practice was actually experienced.
3. **Sourcefree dose-to-facility parameters are speculative.** Their saturation prevents automatic perfect competence but cannot certify culture-, age-, activity- or embodied-constraint realism. Empirical calibration remains required.
4. **Reflective knowledge is not a 'top layer' checkbox.** To know that one marshals collective power is itself a cultural-historical development. Most actors may exercise efficacious capacities while having wholly inadequate theories of their source. Reflective insight can reduce effective power when domination blocks practical alternatives.
5. **Technical skill can remain when agency collapses.** Loss of tools, housing, money, allies, legitimacy or institutionally granted access must not be interpreted as losing the acquired component. The inverse is also possible: effective power expands with help while technical proficiency stays constant.
6. **Not all power is coercion or violence; not all coactivity is benign.** Manipulation, dependency, knowledge claims and objective force must remain separable from material participation and normative judgment.
