## MULCH PASS 1: An adult's day is not infinitely long

### Savage

R12 aggregate practice allowed a fictional parent or Nadia to gain meaningful developed component facility during compressed periods, avoiding the opposite error of requiring thousands of individually depicted scenes. But the periods could be independently nominated without a common time budget: twenty hours of practice, ten of work and additional care could coexist while a person was credited for each. Worse, another person's contribution to supported practice might remain free.

This is not simply statistical overfitting. It deletes the social reproduction that made practice possible and eliminates the material costs borne by the child's caregivers and workers.

### Build

Added `historical_social_reproduction.py` and `routine_sources.py` into the **existing** ordinary world and runtime.

- `ConstitutiveRuntime.register_historical_routine_source(spec)` creates one explicitly labeled, bounded research-fictive world source envelope. It authorizes no actual past act, source science or other person's willingness.
- A subsequently requested qualified period must match that source's **actor, site, kind, time window and daily-hours ceiling**. Unregistered string refs or unrelated periods fail before physiological and material writes.
- `qualify_period_allocation` conserves the actor's total time over PRACTICE, REPRODUCTIVE_CARE, WAGE_LABOR and OTHER_OBLIGATION. The provisional model reserves eight hours per day and caps total named activity claims at sixteen hours. These values are *explicit placeholders*, not universal anthropological data. A previously evidenced assisted practice may provisionally claim the supporter's **own** time. Unevidenced assistance downgrades to unresolved configuration rather than minting consent or independent skill.
- `ordinary_life.passage` has a first consumer: qualify the whole interval before any write, then reuse R12 `conserve_compressed_practice` with normalized qualified history. Keep old non-time `practice_access` scope rules intact.
- In `apply_period_allocation`, the objective world charges modeled care hours/rest to the caregiver, awards only provisional bodily comfort to the recipient, and retains a separate care-labor claim with unknown wages, love and substitution. Wage-labor hours record payable obligation and available payment from the *independent institutional controller*, transferring actual modeled account claims and leaving unpaid arrears. No unmodeled physical goods are invented by an aggregate work receipt.
- `world.historical_time_allocations`, `reproductive_labor_periods`, `wage_labor_periods`, and `routine_source_registry` continue across event-ledger state. One compressed period is **one period**, never a counterfeit calendar of high-resolution successes or private memories.

**First consumers with changed consequences:** sourcefree runtime registration → ordinary_life.passage → actor-specific practice facility/body, world-owned wage claims and caregiver labor → subsequent ordinary `BUY_FOOD` bodily and account conditions. Work cost and child regulation can thus compete with the conditions of later action without assuming conscious comprehension.

**Limit:** Location fixed during a historical interval, no proper wage productivity law, true cultural time-use distribution, independent daily choice or household-wide mobility model. For actual movement, split the period; do not silently teleport. Registration is scenario-qualified **fiction**, not empirically sourced time allocation.

## MULCH PASS 2: The unintended good need not have been a motive

### Savage

The older fallback could construct a possible rhythm end in a few specific singing situations. Many actual consequences remained narratively interesting but inert. Conversely, an LLM's retrospective account of why someone acted could turn an unforeseen outcome into the alleged original purpose. Astra explicitly identifies this as a critical failure: an act undertaken for one object can expose another possible object of activity that no participant previously possessed.

### Build

New `unintended_goods.py` runs **after independently settled world action and personally accessible sign**. It uses the selected practical anticipation and actual received cue to present a bounded, abstainable semantic proposal. A material object made, inspected or repaired can produce a possible later concern tied to that exact object's stable world identity. No object, no received sign, no new possibility by this shortcut.

The existing `development.admit` owner accepts a `NOTICE_POSSIBLE_END` **only after** checking the sign received by this actor, and now checks any source material object ID against that **same actually received cue**. The end does not become an established project or retroactive motive.

The R10 `prospective_horizon.project_trial_horizons` first reader was extended to detect physical continuity of **that same object** through actor-accessible cues rather than assuming the child already possesses an analyst's concept of “object.” The future trial uses the *existing* `INSPECT` or `REPAIR` operation. If a future semantic policy selects the specific trial, `runtime._actor_plan` must bind its actual object-specific practical alternative **before** independent world settlement. It refuses to substitute an anonymous site-wide operation when the particular object isn't uniquely bindable.

The actual circuit attempted is:

```text
actor's old practical object/unknown purpose
    -> actual MAKE_OBJECT or REPAIR, with Other/world independently settled
    -> unanticipated actor-received cue carrying particular object ID
    -> optional NOTICE_POSSIBLE_END via existing development owner
    -> later actual same-object cue in a possibly changed world
    -> eligible trial using already lawful body/action and material rights
    -> selected specific object alternative frozen pre-world
    -> world decides whether that attempted transformation can occur
    -> another lived consequence / future recruitment
```

The semantic fallback offers at most one qualified opportunity, with no automatic love, ownership, mastery or conscious end. A live LLM can abstain and propose historically situated rivals.

**Important negative control:** other materially similar artifacts do not become actor-recognized substitutes merely because the model used the shared label `material_object`; that would be smuggling an infant's nonexistent generalized discrimination through the analyst's category. Later functional transfer must acquire independent evidence.

**Limit:** This is one domain-specific positive circuit, not general discovery of every human good. An inherited R10 selected-purpose/afterlife status mismatch may block executable final trial afterlife (see REMONT exact-source intake). Not claimed working until independently repaired/tested.

## MULCH PASS 3: Objective settlement and psychic explanation have different clocks

### Savage

An employer or caregiver's action can settle the objective allocation today, while the actor remains uncertain about its significance. An individual's later behavior may expose only the evidence made available by their own prior choice. R7 provided expectation state and typed appraisal, but this was mostly a passive store without a historical candidate generator and live re-reader.

### Build

New `expectation_afterlife.py` and existing owner ports:

- `source_qualified_appraisal_candidates` compares a stored actor-attempt expectation with later **actually received** signs, not nonexistent counterfactual responses. It conserves whether the sign originated with this actor, another actual author or undetermined source. It neither identifies the cause of the effect nor assumes a person understands it.
- `ConstitutiveRuntime.step` supplies the bounded candidate list to the existing semantic `development` proposal, independently from R13 unintended-good nomination.
- The fallback only proposes `MEANING_UNRESOLVED` when the sign/context qualify; it does not automatically declare satisfaction, causal success, enemy intent or the worker's financial explanation.
- The **existing** `development.admit(APPRAISE_EXPECTED_DIFFERENCE)` validates receipt and stores the actor's later appraisal on the original expectation, **without rewriting its origin event**.
- `constitution.compose` now annotates **that project's specific practical alternatives** with prior actor-relative appraisals and possible responses: repeat, vary means, withhold or investigate. It does not penalize every course that shares a physical operation name, convert uncertainty to inhibition, or assume an overriding truth-seeking drive. A live LLM can select among already lawful alternatives.

**First readers:** future practical option / semantic actor policy and actor-scoped recruited histories; world remains independent. This supplies a genuine private afterlife to yesterday's objective event without granting the actor the world's privileged causal diagnosis.

**Limit:** The fallback leaves meanings unresolved and does not compute empirically calibrated human interpretation. Nor does annotation alone prove the semantic model changed conduct in an executed scenario. More differentiated language, trust, witness and meaning must be built through actual history.
