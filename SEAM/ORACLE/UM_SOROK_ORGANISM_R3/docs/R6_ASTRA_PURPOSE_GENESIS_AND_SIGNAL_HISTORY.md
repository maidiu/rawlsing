## The genuine theoretical recasting

**A bodily operation is not an undertaking. An undertaking is not yet a motive. A motive is not necessarily articulated. An event's objective success is not personal satisfaction.** Existing SOROK owners already distinguish these; R6 now requires their separation across selection, world settlement, experience, history and future initiative.

The same \`SING\`, \`WORK\`, \`CARE\` or \`REFUSE\` can be available under several continuing activities. But the fact of *eligibility* does not prove any was what this person attempted to bring about on this occasion. Nor is an actor compelled to choose only one. One operation can genuinely serve multiple ends *if the sourcefree actor policy explicitly selects that subset*. The model's chosen subset remains an **analytical fictional attribution**, not a guarantee that the actor is conscious of all motives. Missing project selection is not an error to fill automatically; it can signal an exploratory, nonproject or unresolved end.

**Leontiev's activity/object/motive–action/goal–operation/conditions distinction** is the closest inherited theoretical discipline; **Sève** requires the actual socially organized time and relations among acts; **Lahire** requires multiple dispositions and contexts rather than one personality-wide selector; **Vygotsky/Luria** allow new purposes to emerge through activity and social mediation. Existing PRE-A0/PFF/CAN/ADP remain the competent sources; the project is a sourcefree adapter.
