## SAVAGE

R18 made control over a household pantry materially acquired, maintained, contested, sometimes transferred and countered by alternative food production. It also introduced a financed institutional service, but it could still pay an abstract service without constructing the real wage-dependent people through whom institutions actually reproduce themselves.

The inherited world already has substantial `WORK`, employer-owned stock, explicit controller rights, wage payments, unpaid wage liabilities, earning/purchasing and objective finite resources. It was wrong to invent a new economy. But in its general sourcefree regime, a physically capable actor at a publicly accessible worksite can produce goods and collect a wage without entering a particular historical employment relation. That silently converts work availability into employment access and makes institutions less consequential than they claim to be.

The deeper target is how labor is voluntarily performed *within historically constrained choices*, when a controller can restrict the opportunity to earn, when earned wages can fund reproduction or collective support, and how these material experiences later become part of what the worker can do. Neither an accepted contract nor an unfinished workday is equivalent to the worker's total motives or consent to domination.

## EXPOSE

Distinguish the material existence of a workplace, enterprise title over its product, a posted offer of wage labor, a personally encountered offer, a worker's particular action accepting terms, the continuing possibility of a paid workday, an employer's ability to deny that day, independently funded support during exclusion, and the worker's historically acquired practical response.

The same `WORK` action can physically succeed and still occur under unequal output ownership and economic dependence. A controller's funds, the worker's earned claims, and an independent solidarity pool cannot be the same account. Nor is a blocked work attempt knowledge of why it failed.

## RECOVER / RECONSTITUTE / BUILD

### 1. Reuse objective employment and the original WORK owner

New `labor_dependency.py` is a precise institutional interface **on top of** the existing workshop, `workshop_steward` material-output owner, monetary accounts, real `WORK` production and `BUY_FOOD` commerce. `register_work_regime` installs an explicitly sourcefree worksite policy with worker roster, actual institutional controller, current wage, named human-delegate actors, physically recorded institution-specific enforcement service, independent worker-relief pool and posted terms.

It does **not** create contracts or call the parent a worker by fiat. `ACCEPT_WORK_TERMS` requires a real, present, individually INSPECTed terms notice plus that worker's separate performed act. The resulting contract is a recorded historical practical relationship with action-selection provenance, not proof of unqualified freedom or agreement with every future employer act.

`world.WORK` retains all inherited productive and wage-transfer logic. But where an R19 regime actually exists, objective `work_permission` now requires that specific worker's ACTIVE contract and absence of a currently effective employer suspension. No wage can be paid by presenting at a controlled workshop and ignoring its employment regime. At unrelated work sites without the new rule, the earlier engine stays available.

### 2. Employer enforcement is itself a costed relation involving separately acting people

`SUSPEND_WAGE_WORK` is **a distinct bodily/language-dependent action by a named, present delegate actor**, not an employer policy automatically overriding a person's mind. Its world owner requires the worker's actual contracted relation, a declared delegated authority and actual employer funds. A one-unit payment moves from the already existing employer institution's account to a separate fictional labor-disciplinary service account. This employer-funded act can temporarily block a future wage-work attempt, without removing the worker's capacity to act, rewriting the contract or installing a moral explanation.

The person who executes the suspension is a real R19 actor with their own body, activity and selectable policy. The original actor-policy practical-alternative seam now offers **target-specific `SUSPEND_WAGE_WORK` options for actually co-present people**. Their mere presence does not prove a valid employee contract; the independent world checks that condition, delegated authority and employer finances again at execution. No model or externally injected request can forge the target's consent. The receiving enforcement-service institution remains a provisional accounting abstraction, NOT a fully simulated human labor workforce; the external institutional grant and delegates' wage/dependency paths still need further construction.

`WITHDRAW_FROM_WAGE_WORK` provides the worker with a separate possible withdrawal operation, ending their own contract without rewriting previously performed labor, unpaid employer debts or future alternatives.

### 3. Actual earned wages can materially support other workers

`CONTRIBUTE_WAGE_RELIEF` transfers a finite real monetary unit from the contributor's account to an identified independent worker pool, but only if the contributor has a prior actual positive wage-work receipt at this exact workplace and controller. Imported money is not silently called worker earnings. A person with a current source-qualified employer suspension can attempt `DRAW_WAGE_RELIEF` from this pool. The pool cannot create money, compel any other worker to contribute, or end the suspension. It supplies a short-lived material possibility under that real relationship.

The sourcefree pool rule and contribution amount are research choices, not a scientifically issued model of unions, welfare, strike funds or charity.

**Additional construction from review:** the first relief rule checked whether the contributor had *ever* earned a wage. That allowed repeated donations to claim the same past wage as provenance indefinitely whenever other private money was available. R19 now computes the precise remaining source-attributable paid wage by summing prior actual paid `WORK` receipts and subtracting all previous relief contributions. Monetary provenance uses decimal arithmetic rather than unsafe float accumulation. A wage can fund no more relief than it actually paid; initial outside wealth does not manufacture further wage receipts. Actual food and cash flows are still fungible, so this is an upper bound on historical source-attributed contribution, not evidence of distinguishable physical currency.

### 4. The iceberg recruits from activity without the analyst explaining it

New `livelihood_history.py` conserves an individual experience only where the actor actually attempted WORK, materially failed, and received a source-qualified personally accessible `performed_work` sign. It records an experienced unfinished activity without assuming the actor knows whether the cause was bodily fatigue, an employer sanction or something else.

`runtime.step` writes that actor-specific lived record after the independent world effect. `Constitution.materialize` retains it. `constitution.compose` can nominate `DRAW_WAGE_RELIEF` when this particular person had a prior sensed failed workday, the material relief operation is currently situated and their body can perform it. `semantics.HeuristicSemanticProvider` has a separate first consumer of that contextual candidate, subject to rival projects, contradictory recruitment and `WITHHOLD`. It may abstain; nothing says the person is now politically enlightened.

The employer delegate, employee and dependent child need not acquire the same explanation of a materially common event.

### 5. Real continued life reaches household nourishment

The original world already converts paid `WORK` into wages credited to the worker and a material product controlled by the employer. R19 supplies the source-qualified *access* to that WORK.

The new joined-runtime original-repository court includes:

```
mother MOVE to workshop
  -> actually INSPECT posted wage terms
  -> independently or externally documented ACCEPT_WORK_TERMS
```

The actor no longer has an inexhaustible free productive field. Wages can fail, employers can deny the opportunity to earn, and relationships of mutual support can be materially reproduced or depleted.
