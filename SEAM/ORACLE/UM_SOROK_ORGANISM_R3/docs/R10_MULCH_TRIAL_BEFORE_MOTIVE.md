## RECONSTITUTE — Three statuses, not two

1. **Available operation**: the body, historically acquired practices and current world permit an act. It may have no defined object beyond continuing life.
2. **Historically noticed possibility**: an actually experienced sign made a potential difference salient. Its possible means are proposals, and no durable motive has yet been admitted.
3. **Situated provisional trial**: an actor policy now selects *one particular eligible means* among practical possibilities, and that exact prospective identity survives into real world resolution and actor-specific afterlife.

There is no universal amount of practice or pleasure that guarantees status 4. A life can include many status-3 acts without a stable project, or a singular event may change a person's priorities. The model must keep these possibilities separate.
