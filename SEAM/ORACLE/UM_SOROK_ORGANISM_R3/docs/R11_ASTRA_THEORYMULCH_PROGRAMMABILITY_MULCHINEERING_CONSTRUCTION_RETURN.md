## Governing theoretical reconstruction

The person is neither an inner inventory periodically read by an action selector nor a world-bound reflex circuit. The psyche conserves historically formed, differentiated and potentially contradictory *powers of participation*: abilities to discriminate, anticipate, attend, withhold, use means, depend on Others, pursue and transform possible ends. Agency is the situated exercise and possible reconstitution of those powers. Conscious intention is one acquired, fallible form of their organization, never an undetermined sovereign controller. A socially distributed capacity can be effective before the individual independently possesses its constituent operation; relational support is neither already mastered skill nor permanent obedience. Institutions and collective labor produce objective life chances; history changes the future field of possible formation.
