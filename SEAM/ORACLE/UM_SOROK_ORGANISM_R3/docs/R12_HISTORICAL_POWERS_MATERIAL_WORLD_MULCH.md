## MULCH PASS A — A PERSON DOES NOT ACQUIRE INDEPENDENT POWER BY BORROWING ANOTHER'S CAPACITY

### SAVAGE

R11 preserved legitimate co-participation and allowed future requests for help. That alone leaves an unearned inference: a joint success makes the same operation available only via a present supporter, while apparent 'solo' success is represented as routine performance without a historically developing relation to agency. The system can call for help or repeat a default action, but cannot adequately use *actual previous exercise* to change which ways of proceeding become prospectively viable.

The mistake is to encode competence as a scalar belonging wholly to one person, or to equate one shared success with an acquired independent ability. A third error is to make original support a permanent prerequisite, as if the parent retained ownership of a child's newly acquired practical means.

### EXPOSE AND RECOVER

Leontiev: a means is learned in and through activity; performed operation is not the object or motive of that activity. Sève: personally available powers develop through the distribution and temporal organization of actual acts. Dreier: possibility to exercise a power is situated and may differ across work, home and institutions. Holzkamp: competence does not dissolve property, permission, bodily restriction or the need for cooperative resources.

The real separation is between **historical source of a power**, **actor's actual contribution**, **conditions of its exercise**, **remaining dependence**, **current access rights**, **actor's possible concern**, and **consequences**. These need not co-develop.

### BUILD

New `practical_powers.py` records personally undergone, event-qualified attempted operations with local site, result receipt, witnessed co-participation, source sign refs and explicit uncertainty about independent expertise. Its read-only `situated_exercised_repertoire` alters `constitution.compose` BEFORE final psychodynamic action composition:

- Earlier personally and technically performed solo operations can become qualified *same-site repeatable attempts*, NOT a universal skill or free travel license.
- Earlier performed coactivity in the original context, with the supporter now missing, may offer a modest unassisted `PRACTICE` attempt where the operation is practiceable; it does not claim the formerly joint result can be reproduced alone.
- Blocked or partial operations retain history but cannot be promoted to competence by the read.
- Body eligibility and real current institutional gates remain separate; source-free fallback selection can attempt an available operation without declaring a new motive.

**First effective reader:** existing actor `constitution.materialize` and `compose` (additional candidates before psychic enactment); existing `runtime.step` commits personally lived exercise after independently resolved world action.

### NEW CONTRADICTION

An objectively reported PERFORMED operation is not an empirically measured quality of technical skill. We now have a richer historically acquired *attempt repertoire*, not a validated mastery model. The next biological/developmental construction must discriminate skilled performance, routine repetition, cooperation and effort without confusing social access with competence.

## MULCH PASS B — AN ACTOR CAN ACT IN ORDER TO ALTER WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS FOR THEM

### SAVAGE

R7–R11 preserve some evidence paths, yet the person's access to new evidence remains largely a byproduct of world events created elsewhere. An apparent structural refusal can be endured and remembered while never changing the actor's opportunities to investigate the apparatus that produced it. The simulation may make a human skeptical in prose without constructing an activity through which skepticism could obtain evidence.

The opposite wrong answer is a sovereign rational investigator who automatically knows that a public notice explains an employer's refusal.

### EXPOSE AND RECOVER

Holzkamp's subjective grounds require practical investigation of a world the person does not control. Williams' determination through limits and pressures does not exclude people's efforts to revise those conditions. Luria warns against giving the actor the analyst's complete category system at first perception. Astra's two-clock insight: a world decision can settle today while an actor's explanation stays unresolved indefinitely.

### BUILD

New `practical_inquiry.py` stores actor-specific receipt of actual public notices and offers `INSPECT` only when an earlier personally attempted operation was materially frustrated in that site and a **distinct actual posted public notice** is now actor-accessible.

- `actor_access` exposes that a notice is visually present without granting its text or institutional cause.
- `compose` creates a situation-specific `INSPECT` option bound to one notice.
- `runtime._actor_plan` now conserves an actor-selected **material object reference** into the actual Attempt, rather than relying on the external caller to name what was inspected.
- The independent world resolves `INSPECT`, then delivers a private-to-reader cue comprising the **actual public text** only. Hidden payroll, discrimination records, institution motive, counterfactual eligibility and the true cause of the prior refusal are not exposed.
- `runtime.step` retains receipt of this actor's own notice encounter; future recruitment need not re-ask for a sign already inspected.

**First effective reader:** actor planner and `world._resolve_untrusted_core` through actual actor-selected bound object and post-event actor evidence. Nothing is inferred about another actor's knowledge or institutional truth.

### NEW CONTRADICTION

A public explanation can be incomplete, deceptive, socially constructed or unrelated to the earlier frustration. Merely adding a notice to the actor's history is not a scientifically justified belief revision. The next constructive bridge is `received_public_explanation` → actor-grounded competing expectation hypotheses → actual future attempt, without granting official testimony automatic explanatory truth.

## MULCH PASS C — AN OBJECT BELONGS TO HISTORY BEFORE IT BELONGS TO MEMORY

### SAVAGE

The original world can transfer anonymous stock and mark generic repairs. An object exists primarily as inventory or current operation. This makes it difficult for a person to acquire a durable relation to a *particular* thing, and makes the history of production disappear behind its current owner or sentimental narrator.

The political-economic contradiction is acute: production, ownership, and recognition are different facts. The person who made an object may not own it. The owner may never have touched it. A later repairer may materially alter it without acquiring title. A child might come to associate it with a parent without knowing the labor that produced it.

### EXPOSE AND RECOVER

Marx's distinction between producing material goods and commanding their products, Sève's histories of acts and powers, and Dreier's situated participation apply to the object as well as the subject. Leontiev's object of activity should not be conflated with the world's material object: an artifact may become an end for one person and merely a means for another. Material provenance and personal significance require distinct owners.

### BUILD

New `material_objects.py` gives the same world a persistent material-object identity, location, maker, objective owner, material inputs, condition, and multi-actor production/repair history.

- Actual `MAKE_OBJECT` consumes present, controlled material and creates one durable artifact; producer and proprietor may differ without any invented wage.
- `REPAIR` can act upon a **selected existing specific artifact**, physically changing its condition while retaining maker, owner and all prior labor receipts.
- `INSPECT` can act upon an actually visible artifact and expose only a person-accessible appearance, not the complete objective provenance.
- Existing `actor_access` can perceive the object's presence and bind it as a future practical target. This is not an acquired object affection.
- Source-qualified earlier repair practice can make `MAKE_OBJECT` a *candidate attempt*, never guarantee craft skill, ownership or success.
- During ordinary-life `passage`, a generic illustrative ambient wear process changes actual condition through one aggregate objective interval receipt. It does not fabricate daily scratches, unseen workers or perceived events.

**First effective readers:** existing world resolver, existing constitution/actor planner, and existing ordinary-life process. A material object has historical continuity when no one currently speaks about it.

### NEW CONTRADICTION

This artifact physics is consciously crude, and its rate of wear is provisional. It is not enough for objects to be objectively persistent; their work histories must eventually embed real material labor time, class relations, transport, use, decay, obligations and the possibility of institutional seizure or care. The existing example is an operative sourcefree seed, not a general economic system.

## MULCH PASS D — ONE OBJECT, MULTIPLE PSYCHOLOGICAL RELATIONS, NONE THE OBJECT'S TRUTH

### SAVAGE

If a world object automatically imports its provenance into an actor's memory, the system commits an epistemic fraud. If a person privately treasures an object, that cannot turn the object into their property or convert the owner's account into an affective history. Conversely, a purely objective artifact ledger would never allow an object to become the medium of memory, resentment, attachment, repair or voluntary refusal.

### EXPOSE AND RECOVER

Lahire's plural dispositions, Sève's objectified individual acts and the Constitutive Organism's multi-stream contradictory recruitment imply that different people may carry opposed relations to the same enduring thing. A giver may regard it as proof of sacrifice; a child as an emblem of control; another person as ordinary useful furniture. Their interpretations can themselves change through material history, but remain neither the objective truth nor each other's experience.

### BUILD

New `material_investments.py` keeps actor-specific first-person material encounters and optional **typed qualified investments** `INVEST_MATERIAL_OBJECT` admitted through the existing `development.admit` owner.

- `runtime.step` retains only actual object-linked received signs and personally attempted operations.
- The semantic developmental interface receives possible rival orientations—SUSTAIN, WITHHOLD, UNRESOLVED, or NO CHANGE—grounded in that person's experienced history. A model may choose an interpretation, but it cannot grant property rights, objective object condition or an Other's meaning.
- The same actor can retain unresolved opposing investments without an enforced synthesis; distinct actors' relations never overwrite one another.
- A later encounter recruits that person's existing investment; one unopposed SUSTAIN orientation can offer a **specific, objectively world-resolved REPAIR attempt**. A contradicting WITHHOLD orientation remains material to the available policy rather than being silently suppressed.
- Nothing establishes that all makers love their products or that all people experience a conscious desire to preserve them.

**First effective readers:** actor development writer → constitution recruitment → actor-selected object-bound alternative → independent material-world operation → new actor-specific experience.

### NEW CONTRADICTION

The formation of affective meaning remains probabilistic and underidentified. Receiving an artifact is not a psychological law compelling affection. The next construction needs real semantic proposal competition, recurrent practical recognition, conflicts of memory and current object state, and stronger developmentally appropriate sensory distinctions.
