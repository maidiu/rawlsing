## 2. Binding necessities and distinctions; no authorized answer in advance

An adequate proposed fusion must preserve these necessities, without which the principal's intended product has been changed:

**Target-constrained compressed parent generation:** PYAT supplies an actual sampled pseudo-respondent target for each parent, not merely a demographic prior, descriptive decoration or final aggregate fit. The parent is generated by a thinner, bounded and partially predetermined pass of the **same underlying developmental machinery** so that the *constructed psychological whole*, not a response override, satisfies the **individual target at its initial reference point**. How to make that correspondence scientifically honest while preserving genuine personhood, contradiction, source ambiguity and resource economy is itself the unresolved question; no ORACLE-authored algorithm or experiment is adopted.

**Exceeding the data:** A developing person's psyche must contain historically generated capabilities, relations, practices, interpretations, contradictions, projects and potential outcomes not enumerated by survey questions. A full survey answer vector is not a mental state, hidden biography or generative source of motivation.

**Plurality without stereotype:** The relevant population distribution—not a single respondent—conditions the parents. Minority and apparent counterpattern combinations and within-profile differences remain possible as supported; neither demographic typicality nor mechanically maximized contradiction defines individual authenticity.

**Unequal construction openness; equal human ontology:** The child receives expansive need-first historical recursion without a required future adult survey profile. Parents have **compressed, more predetermined initialization** and then continue as independent persons with complex interaction, world consequences, access and changing state. Their genealogical relations, co-presence and household are distinct and nonautomatic. A father is not manufactured through arbitrary matching or assigned past actions retrospectively. No retrospective psychological or material event can be created by narration.

**Psychological receiving integrity:** SOROK, not ORACLE, owns the substantive psychological model. The seam must not reduce its difficult identification/alienation/recognition/Horizon relationships to canned survey attitudes or simple membership/approval variables. Nor is it forbidden to influence how SOROK's *earliest* receiving operations work when the genuine weld requires it.

**Epistemic integrity:** Observe the limits of respondent/instrument/source/wave and objective-world evidence; distinguish actual observed association from warranted structure/power and undisclosed individual causes. What cannot be inferred must not be asserted. Unobserved does not mean non-existent or interchangeable with fluent fiction.

**Cultural and political noncapture:** Neither survey categories nor pretrained/contemporary elite assumptions may silently become the universal psychology of source-conditioned people. Historically particular available meanings must be supported or declared hypothetical; there is no compulsory liberal/therapeutic emancipation or its romanticized non-Western inverse.

**Multiple independent senses of adequacy:** An executed data dependency, a defended sociological explanation, plausible individual formation, observed population fidelity, developmental validity, historical-cultural transfer, interactive product and positive effects on real users are different claims. None becomes true because another passed.

**Practical product:** A scientifically candid and bounded interactive Other must not be indefinitely vetoed by unavailable complete developmental science. Conversely a compelling encounter does not retroactively validate the underlying social/psychological model.

These are **substantive constraints**, not a specific path to a solution or a substitute for Astra's own prioritization of research.

## 3. What went wrong with earlier ORACLE direction and PR #1146

The earlier ORACLE author described a fixed menu of experiments, rival architecture families and first-vertical operations as if these were necessary. This overdetermined the scientific method before the independent researcher had determined the critical unknowns.

PR #1146 established a bounded, valuable result: a stipulated institutional charter can make an actually relevant higher-order respondent configuration matter to an opportunity/role assignment. Its reported past activities are then used as if they were actual incumbent acts and competence; its institution and coactors are partly fixture-supplied; an already formed SOROK actor receives an invitation. This is not yet a discovered full-population-conditioned **formative fusion** for independently developing parents. The inference from source reports to executed personal history remains unjustified.

Do not discard the valid institutional/practice distinctions merely to obey this criticism. Do not turn the criticism into an instruction to make that particular guild experiment larger. Whether that apparatus contributes to a genuine seam—and where—is a research question for Astra.

Likewise, neither the PYAT→world→activity→learning sequence nor forward/inverse/hybrid candidate-history generation is an adopted answer. Both may be valuable hypotheses; neither is prescribed.
