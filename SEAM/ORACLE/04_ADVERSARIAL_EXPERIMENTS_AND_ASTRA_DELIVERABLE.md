## Questions Astra must answer explicitly

**A. Population / theory:** What is the actual sampling target of whole persons, at what unit and scope? Which observed population covariance is identifiable? When is a source-conditioned model a substantive explanation versus an arbitrary plausible simulator? Does 'habitus' mean a coherent disposition system, plural cross-context repertoire, activity organization, relational graph, or some measurable combination? What can distinguish these accounts?

**B. Formation:** Which universalizable biological/learning rules, if any, can generate historically distinctive categories and PFF links without one fixed psychotype vocabulary? What experiments beyond adult surveys can qualify the rule? How are contingency, context, coactivation, recursion, generalization and nonformation distinguished?

**C. Social contradiction:** What is the mechanism from unequal *objective* power to learned perceived authority, internalized discipline, resistance, class identification, or critical fidelity? What happens when objective domination exists without any actor awareness? Can the same constitution produce different public/private declarations and practices without depicting a schizophrenically incoherent person?

**D. Multi-person world:** How much full development is necessary for parents, siblings, spouses, teachers, neighbours, employers, TV/institutional carriers? Where can a public practice be represented without a fully computed private adult? How do actor resolution and independent rights stay intact? Can one mother-first generated life and late-conditioned father coexist with real earlier father choice?

**E. Reflection and Horizon:** How do practical activity and accessible language generate meta-representation and the ability to judge a carrier by a learned criterion? How to represent Absolute Horizon without religious moral essentialism or accidental supernatural 'stratum ten'? Can an unreflective adult still have a rich PFF?

**F. Agency:** What computational mechanism allows novel responses from historical organization, not random deviance? What parts of the model are causal theory vs merely useful generative heuristics? Where does collective change enter the world independently of individual heroic intent?

**G. Evidence and distribution:** How do respondent-joint observables, minority tails, social position and separate material statistics jointly calibrate an ensemble without claiming an identified private history? How do different question semantics, periods and selection/risk sets limit the science? What count of empirical variants is enough to test a rule rather than admire examples?

**H. Software:** What is already in PYAT I03/I05/FF/N9/N10, PRE-V3/Astra R3, SOROK CAN/ADP/PFF/P2–P6 and incoming C03/C04? Which exact method is the first missing real consumer? **I. Failure/alternative:** If models A/B/C in ORACLE/03 fail, what genuinely different configuration has a stronger scientific basis? What observation would make you withdraw it? Which desiderata cannot be achieved from today's data regardless of architectural ingenuity?
