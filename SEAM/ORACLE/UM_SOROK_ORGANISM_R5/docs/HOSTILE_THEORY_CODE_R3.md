## F13 — R2 currents were merely retrieval descriptions (REPAIRED AT PROVISIONAL GRADE)

**Savage:** R2 `constitution.compose` emitted a list of ongoing projects and recruited form labels, then ranked the action menu by preset numeric scores. The supposedly contradictory life could not actually oppose an action. A list of conflicting contents was being called *pressure-flow*.

**Inherited recovery:** SEAM/30 R2 §3: "Pressure-flow is the live psychological middle—not decorative retrieval"; CAN-008 R2 multiple simultaneous streams and PRE-A0's living conation/constraint distinction; PFF conditional relation, not a scalar universal psyche. The person may remain inhibited, act despite contradiction, or be changed by a conflict without its resolution being an optimization problem.

**R3 code:** `psychodynamics.enact_currents`, `constitution.compose`, and typed per-form `tendencies` produce qualified `ACQUIRED_ACTION_CURRENT`, bodily condition, `SITUATED_ACQUIRED_INHIBITION` and `UNRESOLVED_CONTRADICTION`. `WITHHOLD` and `APPROACH` are only actor-specific, admitted-form hypotheses and never demographic/cultural default types. **No total motivational scalar is assigned.** Candidate options change: unopposed withholding suspends an action from sourcefree self-prospect; mixed currents remain auditable without forced merger.

**Executable:** `test_only_qualified_withholding_suspends_actor_generated_action`; `test_simultaneous_opposing_currents_neither_erase_nor_merge`; `test_contextual_withholding_cannot_follow_form_outside_its_scope`; baseline no-tendency control.

**Still exposed:** The current tendency is partly installed as a hypothetical property of an acquired form and identified *after* basic cue-gated recruitment. R3 does not implement preconceptual selective impression, repression that changes cue access, displaced action, affective time, or a trained developmental generator of tendencies. A privileged global form analyst must not automatically become an actor's conscious self-narrator.

## F14 — World causes and psychological inhibitor are not the same (REPAIRED IN RESEARCH READ)

**Savage:** An inhibition might be falsely represented as a physiological inability or world prohibition. The actor then becomes a hardcoded inability rather than a person struggling with materially possible conduct.

**Inherited recovery:** Holzkamp's actor premises versus objective possibility; Leontiev's motive/action distinction; Sève on material power; R2's policy separation of actor attempt from forced world effect.

**R3 code:** A withheld candidate is marked `still_bodily_possible: true` and omitted only from internally proposed choices; `step(requested_action=...)` remains a clearly disclosed **external injection** and world owner must independently resolve it. Physical eligibility still has separate body gate. Opposite learned currents are exposed, not normalized away.

**Executable:** withholding, contradiction, and unchanged physiological controls. This is not yet a fully lawful agent-native self-interruption/compromise mechanism.

## F15 — R2 possessed a hardcoded composition grammar, not acquired composition (PARTIAL REPAIR)

**Savage:** R2 `plans.propose_compositions` knew pairs like `PRACTICE + SING` before any person learned to organize such a combination. The program was showing its designer's compositional competence and calling it Nadia's. The deeper regress—the genesis of an organizer—remained.

**Inherited recovery:** SEAM/30 R2 §§5,17: finite organismic equipment → practical encounters → forming methods of attention/combination, no mature preinstalled destination. O05 Leontiev object–motive–action and Sève differentiated means under lived social time. PFF historical topology and CAN-011 acquisition only after bounded actual actor experience.

**R3 code:** `development.admit` accepts `ACQUIRE_COMPOSITION_METHOD` only after a personally performed encounter and two already acquired distinct source forms with corresponding existing actions, with a proposed `hypothesized_end`, event ancestry and scope. `plans.qualified_learned_compositions` reads only presently recruited forms and options. A new activity `CARE → SING` appears after the actor's method acquisition, not before, without introducing arbitrary new physical opcodes.

**Executable:** `test_same_acquired_means_later_become_new_learned_composition`, `test_learned_method_not_exercisable_without_its_current_means`, negative unearned/magical action cases, cold reopen of the same actor's acquired rule.

**The genuinely deeper theoretical gap:** This is **not** yet a theory of why one person invents a method and another does not. A semantic callback proposes an `hypothesized_end`; the compiler merely checks lawful antecedents. **The gate validates eligibility to acquire an operative fictional hypothesis, not its psychological genesis.** PRE-A0's conation, nonverbal contingency and affective recruitment need a causal proposal process that genuinely operates on remembered difference, anticipated encounter, failed prior attempts and irreducible social practice. This is the exact challenge bounced to SEAM/30.
