## Personhood

A materially embodied, historically individuated actor possesses acquired and partly inaccessible **functional organization**, not preinstalled universal adult traits. Its histories constitute powers, investments, ongoing object-directed activities, expectations, practices, memories and causal traces, perceived Others and their very relations. It acquires not only contents but **ways of composing contents and changing pathways of recruitment**. Rigidity and flexibility are emergent consequences of organization and actual context, not personality scalar defaults.

A **temporary situated composition** recruits the enduring iceberg under current accessible particulars, bodily/material possibilities, unfinished activities, and differentially effective social powers. The outward tip can be silence, refusal, conduct, speech or a partial disclosure; neither actor self-explanation nor public expression exhausts system-level cause.

The loop: **organism and world → actor access → historically specific recruitment → situated functional composition → possible attempt/inaction → other actors' independent participation and world settlement → each actor's own experience → proposed/qualified reconstitution of contents AND recruitment paths → future recruitment**. Actual time/history is path-dependent; interpretation is revisable and often false or absent. A real change must be consumed later, not just narrated.

## Mutually productive relational becoming, not caregiver ideology

Infant–caregiver was the principal's **proposed example** of a general, dialectical, libidinally responsive shared field, not a universal recognition-seeking development theory. There are two independently embodied and affected centers of activity. The objectively recurring unity of their coordination, pleasures, asymmetries and contradictions is a real organization, **not a fused mind**. Either can acquire new powers through the joint field; a child may later exercise a power against its formative caregiver or become the family's black sheep. Neither approval nor attachment nor liberation is an obligatory resulting state. Generalize the logic to peers, institutions, work, other caregivers, friendships and social life.

The social world is independently productive: class, labor, property, institutions, social reproduction, cultural activity and third parties constrain the conditions of intelligibility and actual exercise even when an actor does not know their source. Objective power, an actor's understanding, and an actor's activity are distinct.
