## 1. The upstream object is not 'a pile of opinions'

PYAT's proper contribution is a **source- and use-authorized historical population/structural field**, not a latent adult personality distribution. Its strengths and irreplaceable functions must survive the handoff:

1. **Original selection, source rights and meaning:** exact SAV/DTA edition, instrument, country/wave and fieldwork, variable, question/response option, source-specific wording, applicability/form/ballot/skip/risk, respondent/occurrence identity, weight/design/target universe, missingness, codebook overlays and provenance. No semantic inference from source labels alone; no false zero for not-asked.
2. **C11 complete source body:** compact durable thirteen-family exact respondent/event/question/meaning/estimator/permission/source provenance, reproducible from source; cheap cold respondent-major and question-major traversal. Historical V547–V558/J, F26 and C11 capabilities must be conserved by function, not obsolete storage carrier or report-format. ANVIL's current design process may not drop future-relevant information to save a small sourcefree benchmark.
3. **I03/F1–F4 empirical operations:** legitimate conditional/marginal estimands, F2 directional conditioning, exact same-person higher-order joints (3+ not synthesizable from pairwise), minority/counterpattern tails, ranked response occurrence, eligible/asked/answered bases, weights/design and uncertainty, repeated cohort/denominator operations and I05 conditional branching. A questionnaire respondent is one observed unit; its answers are not independent samples.
4. **Comparative structural description and inference:** FF01 descriptive same-source relations; separately source-qualified FF02 claims about material position, institutional capacity, control, exploitation, sanctions, rights, structural causality or mechanisms, where independently evidenced. Empirical survey majorities can support claims about reported norms, not automatically what a police officer or employer can force.
5. **Historical/comparative synthesis:** F6 heterogeneous-source construct/overlap, explicit shared-vs-unlinked respondent limits; F7/EE12 qualified wave/time comparison, genuine historical breaks, noncomparability and imputed time labeled. Cross-sectional respondents are not their own longitudinal future or past.
6. **SDTF and objective-world construction:** typed mechanism and source rule bound to target/scope, pressure/counterpressure and separate realized incidence, temporal material circumstances; N6/N9 objective institutions/resources/rights/positions/affordances; qualified generation of selected world snapshots. Objective structure exists even when a focal person never notices it.
7. **Empirical possibility:** N10E/C1 legitimately returns conditional configurations/alternatives and honest zeros/unknowns; selected N9 + compatible N10E can yield world-conditioned N10W. A probability of a survey answer is not a probability of a belief inside a child, or of two sampled people meeting. Preserve direct empirical route where structural world addition is not needed.
8. **Selection/version/handoff:** M1/M2 learned outputs where required, N9/N10W/PRE-V3 selection and N11/N12 rights/capability gates; generated actor position may query applicable objective conditions without changing source facts; SOROK E001/E002/C0 first consumers must use values, not merely verify hashes.
9. **Outward feedback:** SOROK performs action, world admits actual effects, authorized separate objective-world witness changes determinant, SDTF R4 may request exact invalidation/rederivation. It cannot rewrite survey source or turn private actor intent into objective world change.

**Central law:** PYAT supplies qualified *possibility, structure and historical bounds*; person-formation and an actual fictional biography are SOROK's. But 'different owners' does NOT justify two disconnected systems: an exact value/constraint must be consumed in a lawfully conditioned, later causally consequential SOROK operation.

## 3. The actual hinge is a triple construction problem

**A. Conditioned social field:** full respondent-joint distributions, material social position, competing Others in different objective positions, measured access/inequality/institutions and changing historical circumstances. Avoid a single synthetic person's questionnaire configuration being mistaken for an entire social milieu. A probability table over other views is not a social relationship.

**B. World realization and encounter:** realize particular resources, relationships, coactors, practices, authority claims, childcare/work schedules, institutions and feasible acts. Source-conditioned populations constrain opportunities but do not themselves supply actual meeting probabilities, partner links, private motives, interactions or household rosters unless a qualified source does.

**C. Formation by actual practice:** only actor-owned accessible experiences, with organismic state, developed capacities, current PFF, intersubjective relations and appropriate learning rules, can create/reconfigure internality. No crossing of A directly to C. No exhaustive micro-simulation requirement if a separately qualified coarse process can generate reliable summaries and explicit missing intervals.

Astra must find a **minimal executable transducer** for A→B→C or show exactly which existing owner implements it. The first person formed must be capable of later affecting others and of producing her own contingent survey answers.

## 4. Same-response, different-person inverse model

For synthetic adult creation we may use a joint observed tip to constrain candidate possible histories, not construct a unique posterior psyche. Candidate expression:

P(H, Z | Y, D, W, Q) proportional to P(Y | H, Z, D, W, Q) times P(H, Z | D, W).

This is an **unidentified research decomposition**, because no current empirically qualified developmental prior and observation likelihood are established. It is NOT a valid numeric estimator because its factors are aesthetically plausible. One must distinguish:
- candidate history generation under material/world permissions;
- same-ontology forward developmental recursion to produce actor state Z;
- survey observation process at time t with actual instrument Q;
- model criticism or reweighting only by source/estimator/psychological authority graded evidence;
- selection of one **fictional** causally committed timeline and keeping alternatives as counterfactuals;
- no post hoc retrofitting actual future actor action to a questionnaire tip.

**The direction must invert at use:** the observation is an *external fit constraint* on a life-generating engine; inside the fictional world it is an *output* of that engine.

## 5. The complete PYAT requirement is not merely faithful distribution replication

The empirical side should allow source-supported questions like:

- Which joint answer configurations actually occur for respondents at D (and role/period/eligible risk set), at what supported prevalence and uncertainty? What is the true 3+ morphology, and what rare alternative configurations exist?
- Which material positions have different reported exposures or positions, which resources and authority are externally verified? Where do measured association and structural causal claim separate?
- Which other positions/counterfields are empirically present in the relevant population, and which norm/practice variants are plausible? What relative prevalence versus *actual* accessible networks is known? What is unknown?
- Which differences between people in the same material niche must a person-engine be capable of explaining by heterogeneous acquired histories, rather than being smeared into a demographic average?
- Which apparently surprising joint patterns reflect genuine within-person coexistence rather than incompatible ballot forms, route differences, mistranslation, time shift, semantic homonymy or response errors?
- Which similar response codes mean different things in different worlds, and how could the actor acquire different contextual semantics for the same questionnaire token?
- When do class aspiration, religiosity, party membership, elite legitimacy, gender values, public practice, informal power, labor autonomy or critical identification move together—or not—in real qualified population data?
- Where can it only bound possibilities, because family/cohort longitudinal/counterparty/clinical/behavioral data do not exist?

Astra must resist both **statistical determinism** and **unfalsifiable psychological omnivory**.

## 7. Seven exact first-reader tests of the boundary

1. **Survey joint mutation:** identical marginals and pairwise cells but switched three-way association; qualified I03 F4 alternatives differ, and eligible adult observational candidates can differ. No fabricated psyche in C11.
2. **Institution power mutation:** same survey opinions, different actually qualified employer sanction/household time/care capacity; actual action options differ; only actors who encounter it may learn.
3. **Field-alone mutation:** change other-group response distribution without any instantiated coactor/institution/access. Actor psyche, memory and action **must remain unchanged**.
4. **Other-realization mutation:** replace a particular coactor with another separately developed qualified person; verify legal contact/action, different potential child experience, not automatic learning.
5. **Same observed mother tip, different actual adult prehistories:** if histories affect PFF formation, later caregiving can differ under invariant external encounter; no inference about actual respondent C.
6. **Same person, different care conditions:** changed shift/caretaker resources and spouse acts change real workload and interaction; psychological identity persists, though new events may subsequently change it.
7. **World feedback:** actor intention alone cannot alter historical source or objective determinant; performed and independently externally admitted act can cause targeted world invalidation and subsequent changed condition.

For each: exact product identifier and SHA, eligible source, negative/held state, selected actor/coactor, actual named first reader, output diff, restored/cold replay and proof ceiling. **A changed hash alone never proves causal use.**
