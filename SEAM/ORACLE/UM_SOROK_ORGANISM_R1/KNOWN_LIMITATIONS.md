# Hostile return: defects, holds, and scientific limitations

## L1 — Not the protected organism (major integration gap)
This implementation creates its own research `ResearchLedger` and stylized world. The real P2/P3/P4/P5/P6 pipeline is not executed by this package. Ownership remains separated in `adapters.py` and `IMPLEMENTATION_MAP.md`. Its native writer rights, protected merge, live-user validity, source-issued CAN/ADP developmental rules and independent review are **NOT GRANTED**. `HQ_CURRENTNESS_HOLD` for full HQ status: current source cut includes unmerged PRs, protected progress R39 with addendum, and unverified local worker/owner authority. The shortest eligible next step is authorized owner-side review and first-consumer connection on a branch with a verified lease.

## L2 — Psychological acquisition remains provisional
A small offline `HeuristicSemanticProvider` changes acquired forms/links/projects and can yield visible later consequences. It does **not** establish how humans actually acquire those forms. A live semantic service contract is written but **not exercised**. Inferring a rich new motive from one event without temporal or relational basis is a known risk. Developmental admissibility verifies identity, exposure, type and sourcefree grade, not scientific human-learning validity. Some inference paths are coarse, or insufficiently general; psychologically important states such as complex ideology, investment, grief, worship, alienation, political motivation and multilineage attachments are not fully modeled.

## L3 — Adult answer generation is NOT independent
The offline parent provider constructs test forms containing `asserted_answer` for individual survey questions; `parents.interview_readout` explicitly marks `DIRECT_ANSWER_LEAKAGE_NOT_INDEPENDENT`. The demo's adult questionnaires are **invented synthetic fixtures**, not real PYAT survey samples. Neither true whole-survey instrument correspondence nor a calibrated inverse model of parent life is demonstrated. Parent fiction is not source-respondent hidden truth.

## L4 — Joint action is sourcefree policy autonomy, not tamper-proof authentication
The old R0 public forced-partner-consent bug is **blocked in this R1's exposed `ConstitutiveRuntime.step` and `world.resolve` paths**, where Other conduct is derived from another actor's state and own policy; `coactor_override` and a direct forged coactor attempt are rejected. This does not constitute OS isolation, cryptographic user authorization, or protection from malicious in-process code that replaces the policy object. Objective coercive effects remain possible without consent; the model does not conflate effects with voluntary cooperation. R0 archive remains historically defective.

## L5 — The world is a compressed house/workshop/hall, not a full social world
Three locations, modeled stocks, an access-controlling hall board, simple care and material activity, and two coactors are enough for working circulation. They do not establish realistic institutions, labor, law, class, macroeconomic reproduction, peer networks or a city with independent citizens. Actor age/body variables and their rates are invented engineering placeholders, not biological measurements.

## L6 — World exposure and historical coverage remain simplified
`actor_access` filters cues by coarse location, modality and privacy; it can accumulate accessible past cues rather than modeling realistic forgetting or passage of sensory attention. Aggregate `background()` periods are not exact real episodes. Some aggregate rules may scope-generalize an existing form only under explicit `warrants_scope_change=True`, but there is no validated theory governing that warrant. A rich ordinary-life autobiographical record or distribution of meaningful event opportunities remains unwritten.

## L7 — Prospective action is still bounded by finite vocabulary and externally-injected demos
Self-proposed attempts are chosen among composed options from acquired effects, projects and organism. `perform_composed_activity` is a bounded sequence composed from existing means and is labeled `EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PLAN_INVOCATION`; it does not prove arbitrary novel purpose generation, actor-issued composition or original-source terminal child-event performer. Some demo steps are directly requested and are labeled `INJECTED_NOT_NATIVE_ACTOR_CHOICE`, not advertised as autonomy.

## L8 — No atomic cross-store production transaction
Local research world+actors changes commit atomically. Actual existing SOROK P2/P3 stores have their own owner protocols and cross-store recovery concerns. A native bridge must preserve the existing protected `PREPARED → WORLD_COMMITTED → PSYCH_COMMITTED → COMPLETE` discipline and authorized first-consumer identities. The research service cannot be copied as a universal writer.

## L9 — Ledger integrity limitations
The ledger's hash chain covers each stored event envelope, current world and actor digests; it is *not a cryptographically externally anchored attestation*. An administrator or malicious direct SQLite editor could rewrite an entire coherent chain. The separate `source_seals` table is immutably inserted through public API, but **its full contents are not included in the event chain**. Qualified production source provenance requires owner-bound, verifiable seals. Test passing confirms bounded invariants only.

## L10 — Research code quality and missing systems
No concurrency/load/security audit; no live LLM connectivity test; no human recognition or wellbeing evaluation; no independent engineering G5; no native PYAT field producer, full C5 slow organization or ORACLE complete social-field recruitment. The development engine does not guarantee contradiction is psychologically sufficient just because two form links coexist. `SUPPORTED` is not synonymous with `VALIDATED`; every output is graded `SOURCEFREE_RESEARCH_PROVISIONAL`.
