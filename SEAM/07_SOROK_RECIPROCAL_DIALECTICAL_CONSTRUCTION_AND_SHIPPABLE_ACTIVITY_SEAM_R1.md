- SOROK gives those terms their **particular history**: who, with their own past, is carrying on which activity with/against whom, what choices are materially possible, what they actually do or fail to do, what is perceived/recruited and what is changed by consequential action.

**Non-equivalence:** what a person *is obliged to do* is not what they *wish to do*, and neither is what they *actually do*. What they attempt is not necessarily what they accomplish. What objectively results is not necessarily what they experience. What they experience is not necessarily what they narrate; what they narrate is not necessarily what the user understands.

This is where the social can become personal **without the sociological system producing a motive**, and where personal activity can become social **without the individual being granted arbitrary power to rewrite institutions**.

## 2. A better minimal unit: *a living practical situation*, not a personality input and not a new packet

For **each selected concrete episode**, existing owners must be able to bind the *minimum real causal cross-section* of these ingredients. This is a **semantic assembly requirement**, not a new global data type or compulsory database:

**I. Objective practical terms — world/PYAT side, with synthetic-world status explicit**

- What is at stake in the world: a meal, shift, unpaid care, school pickup, debt, available bus, room, union vote, invitation, work task, standing service.
- Who can **control**, use, owe, demand, refuse, sanction, provide or depend on it, at what **place and effective time**.
- Which claims are institutional/structural and backed by **what actual enforcing capability**; which are personal promises, which mere expectations, and which held/unknown.
- Which alternatives are materially achievable, costly, blocked, dangerous, assisted or conditional **without assigning emotions or decisions**.
- Whether each claim is observed/source-qualified, theoretical/possible, or specifically generated-and-admitted as true **within this fictional world**.
- The world does not need the entire structure of society in RAM. It needs the *selected mechanisms that change a real practical consequence*.

**II. Actor's historically produced practical condition — SOROK, independent of PYAT science**

- What the actor has actually been doing across settings, which other people's activity theirs depends on, and what they have **already undertaken** before user arrival.
- Bounded durable actor capacities/practices/relationships only at their right current sourcefree/scientific grade; where not established, exact unknown/hypothetical rather than a seed derived from class.
- What they know, believe, expect or have access to **only where admitted**, versus machine-observed realities they cannot access.
- What ongoing commitments could compete, coexist or be coordinated; **a commitment is not a dominant motive** and may be broken, renegotiated, refused or misunderstood.
- Person↔particular counterpart historical relation; no universal same-event→same-reaction assumption.

**III. Practical intersection — a service composition, not an invented owner**

- At time `t`, establish which actual opportunities or prohibitions result from I + II under existing gate/world/C4/P2/actor owners. One action can meet several relations and many can be mutually incompatible.
- Candidate action, nonaction, abandonment, delay, continued routine, refusal or attempt should remain distinct **from the actor's reason**.
- An event can affect material feasibility *without ever being perceived*; only genuinely received aspects reach CAN-008 actor appraisal/recruitment. There is no forced event→consciousness arrow.
- If an actor lacks means, distinguish blocked capacity from refused desire; if a capacity is unobservable, do not invent inability. Inability, no opportunity, nonattempt and unwillingness are different.
- Existing 3–5 prospective proposals/CAN streams may explore contingent futures, then existing admission computes what actually happened. Not a new deterministic choice engine, and no LLM retrospective explanation may manufacture the prior choice.

**IV. Performed and nonperformed consequences — world first, later actor as appropriate**

- An **attempt** may fail. A performed act changes the *properly scoped* fictional material record if its rule and resource ownership warrant it. Unpaid rent remains due; an outlay subtracts from the payer's own stock, not the child's fictional wallet.
- Distinguish **reproduction of a relation** (the shift occurs, care is given, rent is paid, the terms persist) from **local change** (care arrangement altered, debt accrued, practice attempted) and **structural transformation** (actual organizing and institution-level changed rule, only where collective capacity is admitted).
- **Scale of consequence is set by the real mechanism and organized power, not the scene's emotional intensity.** A heated conversation cannot by itself change a law, union bargaining power or ownership. One paid bill does not abolish the landlord relation.
- Objective effects may arise even if an actor misapprehends their cause; later actor uptake and durable formation are separately qualified. New fictional state must not overwrite real source history; SDTF R4 redrive requests are not world writes.

**V. The player's encounter with the independent consequences — PFF/V2/product**

- The actor's *outward occurrence* is generated from their lawful current life; PFF mediates interaction evidence/fast carryover under CAN-008 R2, and V2 produces binding exact outward expression.
- User-facing presentation exposes a finite perspective, not the actor's owner-private condition. Player knowledge and actor knowledge may differ.
- SAY/DO/ATTEND/WAIT changes the possible *future*, not the Other's precontact history. Asking what the person did yesterday may trigger lawful materialization of an already bounded past; the user's desired answer cannot select its truth.
- Actual consequences must sometimes frustrate the player's plan. Frustration is not a selectable “difficulty setting” and not a script to increase retention; it follows the actor's commitments and world conditions.

**This is the missing crossing:** objective social power conditions *conduct*, real conduct can reorganize both objective circumstances and actor formations, and the player's learning occurs through the partial relation to that conduct.

## 3. Two paths by which social reality operates: an important correction to H3

The proposition “world relation → encounter → appraisal → action” is incomplete. It implies that objectively effective constraint must first become an actor-experienced event. **It need not.**

**Path M — materially effective without recognition.** Employer closes access, a food stock runs out, a rent obligation advances, public transport stops, a caregiver's absence changes available care. Actual material possibilities can shrink or expand without an actor observing or correctly interpreting the cause. P2/world owns this consequence, SOROK later respects changed actability, and no CAN-008 event is fabricated from omniscience.

**Path E — encounter-mediated historical reception.** A person hears an employer's demand, notices a child's need, receives an invitation or experiences a delayed payment. The specific actor's history, projects, accessible perception and relation may condition *what this becomes for them* and which practices are recruited; **CAN-008 R2** is the current semantic person×event owner. Response may or may not yield a durable effect; PFF/V2 may convey only part of it.

**Path M and Path E can interact** but must remain nonidentical. A person can be constrained without understanding; understand a constraint without accepting it; accept it without being able to act; act without intending the later consequences. In the same case a consequential objective change and actor NO_CHANGE (or scientifically UNEVALUATED psychological state) are both possible. 

**Engineering counterexample:** employer silently changes future shift. Mother cannot work under the original schedule (M), but has not learned why and does not feel any generated “humiliation” (no E); after later receipt of a phone call, same objective shift may now become an event-for-her (E), producing a situated response. One existing world writer plus actor read/epistemic gates can show both, without a newly invented psychology.

## 4. Three forms of obligation that must not be flattened

The term **commitment** is potentially misleading unless we keep three types apart:

1. **Institutional/material claim:** a rent debt, job requirement, care-time dependence, control right, sanction; may operate regardless of actor assent. Objectively committed to the fictional world under its authorized sourcefree law.
2. **Interpersonal undertaking:** an actually made promise, jointly arranged pickup, agreed reciprocal favor, obligation another actor may rightfully claim within the constructed relation. It has objective *history* (the promise occurred), not guaranteed compliance or inner loyalty.
3. **Actor's present practical project:** what they are actually trying to accomplish, possibly contradictory or unclear even to themselves. Only actor state/history/CAN and properly bound current evidence can support the relevant inference; it is not assigned by PYAT and not automatically equal to a visible promise.

Because these can diverge, a mother may owe rent, promise to collect a child and also plan to attend a meeting; her personal sense of each is not contained in the institutional graph. The scene's action space changes if the employer can sanction absence, while the person's response need not become predictable from that fact. And an actor may keep a promise while disliking it, fail while caring, refuse a claim, or misunderstand its institutional force.

This is a stronger construction of **plural, enduring practical life** than a ranked queue of emotional motivations.

## 7. Five rival hypotheses, not one dogma

UM-PYAT's H1–H5 is a valuable research program. I refine it into **competing, discriminable explanatory alternatives**:

**R1 — Material gate only (PYAT A).** World power and resources suffice to generate visible practical differences; actor reactions may be bounded without substantive history-conditioned recruitment. Good engineering control case. **Risk:** a believable NPC with no live history.

**R2 — Practical-commitment mediation (my minimal synthesis).** Material constraints plus ongoing independent practical commitments, one coactor and bounded actor-specific reception produce distinguishable actions/experiences that survive user absence. **Prediction:** no-contact and altered coactor-time cases diverge in real later conditions. Scientific native claim requires rival explanations/owner Rule T; sourcefree model can test transaction semantics, not establish a human law.

**R4 — Collective social effect.** Organized action by multiple coactors can modify a local institution/gate where actual capacity and authorization exist; a lone individual's emotional statement cannot. **Prediction:** under different organized capacities, equal discontent produces different institutional consequences.

**R5 — User epistemic-learning loop.** An observer's practical hypotheses about the Other improve through consequences, yet they never acquire universal access or control. Tested through actual play and human participants, not assuming high retention, affection or “feels real” proves meaningful neighborliness.

R1 may be sufficient for an internal mechanical demonstration but insufficient as Rawlsing's first product. R2 is my candidate minimum viable encounter. R3–R5 are essential to eventual full ambition but can be progressively tested/expanded rather than all implemented before an alpha.
