## 2. The most easily missed causal distinction: a change in the OTHER social field is not a change in a person

Let two sampled-eligible positions have their own respondent-coherent answer configurations, `F_W(Y | D1)` and `F_W(Y | D2)`. A particular adult/child is instantiated in `D1`. We change only `F_W(Y | D2)`, or the presence of a minority counterconfiguration in `D2`, holding the focal actor's previous lived history, world and permissions fixed.

**What may change:** selection of candidate potential other-person configurations or candidate message/scene possibilities, if an existing FF/N10E/world consumer actually uses that distribution and its source right.

**What MUST NOT change at that point:** focal actor's private identification, memory, current performed deed, real-world coercive right, relationship history or survey self-description. Without **actual encounter/representation/participation** and appropriate actor-specific development, the changed source metadata is outside that actor's experience.

If a particular `D2` coactor is then independently instantiated, admitted to the same objective world, actually acts (possibly without language), and the focal person *can access* the event, downstream consequences become candidates. Still: access does not compel comprehension or acquisition, and acquired conflicting commitments do not compel overt “inner conflict” or conversion.

This **two-step difference test** is more discriminating than comparing two generated character bios. It identifies the first recipient of the **other-field** product and the later distinct recipient of its **lived consequence**.

There are **three distinct unknowns**, not one “rich context” scalar:
1. **Other-pattern distribution:** what positions and whole co-patterns are empirically eligible? Native FF source/estimand or model grade.
2. **Contact/encounter mechanism:** how does an actor at `D1` actually meet an actor, text, institution, performance or mediated claim associated with `D2`? Empirical linked contact data, independently sourced social organization, or explicit fictional/synthetic particularization. **Independent survey marginals do not identify `P(contact | D1,D2)`.**
3. **Appropriation/identification process:** what particular actor has actually attended/participated, what capacity/meaning could develop, and what actual later feasible action reflects it? SOROK issuer; cannot be computed from FF alone.

A real social field must be *addressable* by a living actor; it cannot merely be numerically present in background tables. **But an addressable option is not necessarily a realized contact or an acquired motive.**

## 3. What “internal contradiction within society and person” actually demands

The principal's emphasis, sharpened across the Lebanese and GSS examples, is **not** a demand to have a person exhibit every positional disagreement, nor to detect psychological inconsistency from survey checkboxes. The proposition is:

1. Institutions, classes, religions, state and national formations, global capitalism, colonial/cultural circulation and their internal factions organize **partly conflicting material and symbolic claims** with varying authority and availability in particular times/places.
2. A person inhabits multiple, overlapping positions; **membership, being categorized, declared allegiance, actual relationships, habitual practice, previously acquired premise, practical sacrifice and ultimate Horizon** are different.
3. Multiple actual encounters and one person's *own activity* make different aspects of these claims salient, obscure, useful, lovable or costly; two siblings can acquire conflicting orientations despite common material family conditions. Some disagreements remain contextually unactivated; some are lived without felt inconsistency; some become a visible source of alienation or critical separation.
4. The *claimed good* and the *institutional carrier* can diverge. Someone may defend a value inherited from an authority **against** that same authority. They may merely oppose the old institution while still organizing their identity around it as a “negative sovereign”; or develop an independent project that no longer depends on that contest. No stage or political endpoint is guaranteed. Absolute Horizon is about developing practical orderings of what matters at what cost, not self-ascribed membership or a slow C5 basin.
5. Access to digital/global representations expands potential counterfields; **era, language, material technology, mediation, institutional gatekeeping and actual participation** constrain which ideas could become part of THIS actor's history. “Global society” is no magic input to a Nabatieh infant, 1990s school pupil or a later adult. PYAT may qualify historically available strata; SOROK must own actual access and interpretation.

**Important anti-reification:** We should not add `internal_contradiction_score` or `multiple_identification_index` to C11 or automatically to CAN. The demand is for **functionally distinct, evidentially earned relationships and later behavior**, not numerical labels that guarantee psyche. Existing CAN-011 extensible acquired mediators/tensions/practices, CAN-008 event/reception, ADP-010 exposure separation and P3 sourcefree development were designed to permit richer contents, but not established generic acquisition laws.

## 4. Source cases that make the boundary falsifiable

### Lebanon, a *source-conditioned scenario candidate*, not the story of observed respondents

Source examination of Arab Barometer II Lebanon included male respondents with combinations such as private prayer and Qur'an reading, relatively little public-service participation, minority worship rights and internal religious restrictions, positive views of some Western culture and potential emigration, combined with socially differentiated gender views. A similarly positioned woman supported equal inheritance and Islamic inheritance legislation, with different resources and party affinity. **These are recorded combinations reported in earlier project extractions, not a representative joint-distribution estimate or proof of personal sect/interpretation.** Keep multiple possible interpretive histories.

A possible fictional family has a materially constrained adult who endorses a woman's high office but opposes married employment and a differently positioned shop owner who favors married employment. A daughter or coworker receives an actual job offer. **That offer must follow an actual world opportunity,** not exist merely to stage the survey contrast. The adult's speech, choice, household work/care time, employer rights and independent spouse/daughter actions can diverge from avowed opinions. The child may observe the offer, only its practical consequences, or neither. A peer, school, global broadcast or another religious standpoint may later supply a different criterion—only if actual access is qualified.

- **Correct:** social-pattern source eligibility → separate fictional adult and employment institution → legal opportunity → independently enacted decision and consequences → child-accessed activity → possible acquired contextual expectation → later acted response under existing SOROK model.
- **Incorrect:** “conservative Muslim father opposes daughter; progressive Shia woman teaches feminism; child becomes cosmopolitan.” This invents sect, theology, causal link, child moral trajectory, class domination and actual interaction from a few survey fields.
- **Hard internal test:** a child may continue to identify with religious solidarity while rejecting one clerical interpretation, or adopt material household arrangements contrary to parental speech, or maintain both, or have no conscious view. Later actual conduct, not author narration, must be the test for the selected model grade.

### GSS question-category trap

The 2010 matched respondent “born again, frequent prayer and church attendance, `not religious at all`” records a configuration, **not** an empirically measured evangelical anti-religion hermeneutic. Different actor-created concepts of “religious” can emit the same public answer; survey-answer emission is a model-graded later test, not direct access to an acquired soul. Its birth/development timeline cannot be reverse-inferred as observed fact from 2010 cross-section. Missing/rotated survey forms and item nonadministration must remain separate.

### Sect and ancestry missingness

AB II's Lebanon **sampling design** used sect while the public respondent record examined lacks a usable individual sect. Later AB V/VIII questionnaires contain denominations but require field-level release checks; one cannot reattribute ABII rows. Latinobarómetro's broad race/color and “Muslim” do not identify a Brazilian of Lebanese descent; Joshua Project estimates, if used, are separate contextual data with their own provenance and bias. A fictional person's identity can be possible without being observed in that survey; no fictional choice becomes a newly observed source respondent fact.
