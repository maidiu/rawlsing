## 2. Correct the iceberg manager

The prior claim that A or B consciously selects an iceberg subset and then exposes a tip was misleading. A historically constituted iceberg is continuously operative, often without conscious comprehension, in attention, affect, bodily orientation, inhibition, expectation, interpretive salience, practical activity and outward conduct. The tip is not a rival power that sometimes loses to the iceberg: it is a partial manifestation or observation of the person–world process.

Reflective self-control may be a real historically ACQUIRED capacity, but it is not a universal homunculus. ## 3. What the theorists actually contribute, with conflicts preserved

**Freud:** The Ego and the Id (1923) explicitly allows unconscious ego defenses and unconscious superego moral demands, and describes ambivalence, identification, rationalization and love–hate transformation. Repression and compromise formation suggest historically variable functional inhibition, displacement, substitution, defensive maintenance. Do not instantiate universal id/ego/superego computational modules or declare a current affect proof of recovered childhood trauma. The "ego" itself is partly unconscious.

**Jung:** Shadow in Aion refers to unacknowledged, ego-incompatible personal contents, sometimes positive, and projection. A Review of the Complex Theory is especially relevant: an affectively charged association constellation can influence memory, perception, speech and activity without deliberation. The early word-association evidence supports historical investigation of interference, not universal archetypes or genuinely independent little psychic agents. Keep Shadow and complex distinct; "integration" is NOT mandatory developmental destiny.

**Klein:** Good/bad object splitting and the depressive-position model illuminate compartmentalized representations and the difficult coexistence of love and hate toward the same person. These are candidate phenomenological/computational readings, not validated obligatory infant stages or pathologies. Differentiate defensively segregated representations, genuine new evidence, affective revaluation and ambivalent whole-person regard.

**Leontiev:** Supplied Activity, Consciousness and Personality, Chapter IV, explicitly observes that a mother can be angry at her child without ceasing to love him. He differentiates affect, emotion, object-related feeling and attitude; one action can serve several motives with different relations to personal sense. Unconscious motives remain grounded in activity within an actual world. This is MUCH stronger than a universal polarity score or motive-intensity hierarchy.

**Sève:** Supplied Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality pp. 188–191/333–334: personality's real historical relationships among activities include objective labor, wages, property and use-time. These cannot be derived solely from the psychological relationship among subjective nodes; even "depth psychology" is distorted if the social relation producing the psychic conflict disappears. He explicitly challenges the fixed Freudian topology as inadequately historical.

**Lahire:** Supplied The Plural Actor on conditional dispositions, the negative power of context and practical analogy: actual settings activate AND inhibit the embodied past. A tone, smell or relational resemblance may recruit old feeling/practice without conscious analogy, sometimes mistakenly. The person is not consciously fetching dispositions.

**Dreier/Holzkamp:** Contradiction across caregiving, paid labor, intimate relationship, institutional dependency and other positions constitutes the actor's possible and restricted conduct of everyday life. Socially constrained compliance is not proof of conscious ideological agreement; history can develop contradictory and defensive strategies.

**Luria/Vygotsky:** Organismic impressions, nonverbal traces and practices precede mature language; later acquired social categories, verbal mediation and reflective practices reorganize the relationships among psychological functions. No analyst vocabulary belongs to an infant just because the model can explain infancy.

**Fromm/Fanon/Voloshinov:** Fromm's social character is passed partly through parents' historically constituted ways of relating; real economic conditions are objective, even as psychological forces develop their own historical dynamics. Fanon shows that an oppressive gaze can be externally real and materially coercive, not merely an individual's distorted projection. Voloshinov's Marxist critique warns against the socially isolated metaphysical unconscious, though his wholesale rejection of Freudian unconscious is not accepted.

## 4. Historical relational transformation is not one magnetism scalar

An admired quality can become inaccessible, painful or revalued after a relationship changes. Keep independent possibilities: actual new objective evidence; context-dependent occlusion; more intense affective charge; changed relation between admiration and obedience; reinterpretation through witnessed power; compartmentalized representations; defensive inhibition; unresolved coactivation; changed self-relation; temporary charge with no durable change.

Distinguish CHANGE TO ELEMENT from CHANGE TO RELATION from CHANGE TO RECRUITMENT CONTEXT from CHANGE TO HIGHER-ORDER MOTIF from CHANGE IN WORLD from CHANGE IN SELF-ACCESS. They may co-occur and have different time constants. Affective valence, salience, intensity, dependency, inhibition and generality are not one number. Strong attachment can survive public rupture. Earlier appreciation can remain historically true even when its current recruitment is inhibited.

A complex affect can emerge from relations AMONG existing feelings, investments, meanings and contexts: bittersweetness, relief in sadness, shame about pleasure, fear of one's own longing, eroticized consensual power exchange, delight in a ritualized transgression. These are not simple addition of basic emotion scores. Do not assign consensual BDSM or cuckolding to universal trauma, pathology or one secret sexual motive. Lacanian jouissance is a contested theory, not a universal measurable coefficient. Empirical reward research separates liking, wanting and learning and emotion dynamics show context/time dependence.

## 5. Materialist and computational discipline

Keep THREE distinct histories: (1) objective shared/world-relational activity and institution; (2) each actor's own psychic and affective relational constitution; (3) recurrently established shared practical organization, without minting a fused third mind. Family can recurse through parent A–parent B, child–A, child–B, and child's model of A–B, while institutional authority and care resources remain world-owned.

A provisional relational dynamics read service may retrieve actor-specific histories, qualified typed relations, locally relevant configurations and derived higher-order motifs. It must allow inhibition to affect other active forms subterraneously, not just remove a node. Changes can affect applicability, affective charge, sense/interpretation, remembered provenance, recruitment, or a slowly persistent configuration. Causality must be traceable and provisional; do not eagerly materialize every possible edge or declare a psychoanalytic myth true because it offers elegant coherence.

R3's exact exposed gap: its INHIBITS relation can remove an active target before psychodynamic composition, and its COMPETES disagreement does not yet modify the meaning of a third form. ## 6. Failure hazards and scientific status

- The existence of unconscious/nondeclarative causation does not validate every Freudian, Jungian or Kleinian interpretation.
- Repressed-memory explanations, especially later detailed "recovery" inferred from behavior, are disputed; no invented trauma or exact forgotten event.
- Genuine domination or betrayal must not be dismissed as the victim's projection merely because an internal relational complex is involved.
- Do not turn every contradictory act into symptom, every repression into developmental progress, every countercurrent into a totalizer, or every romantic rupture into a moral diagnosis.
- A sourcefree LLM may propose plausible fictional dynamic changes while distinguishing event fact, actor's access, candidate hypothesis, admitted functional effect and later manifest behavior. It does not issue scientific universals.
- Provisional operator names must not become adopted owner enums merely by appearing in an elegant document. Future enrichment is explicitly expected.
