## I. The irreducible living person: material history, contingency, and nonresolution

### The living movement and the products that become its conditions

A written program is not its execution. A biography and a collection of dispositions are not a living movement. RŪḤ names the inquiry into what is actually occurring in need, desire, attraction, love, pressure, flow, power, vitality and becoming. It is necessary poetry because none of these terms on its own captures the question. We do not solve it by imagining a hidden animating substance, or by dividing it into unrelated disciplinary inventories.

The provisional dialectical center is **living activity's historical production and reproduction of the conditions of its own actuality**. Living activity is bodily and energetic from the beginning; it is never just a representation of the world. It takes particular historical forms through actual relations with other independently moving beings, objects and historically made material conditions. Its products can become new powers of acting, new objects of desire, means of apprehension, dependencies, or powers governing the very movement that produced them. These relations are not successive stages. The same development can enable one activity while subordinating another, or create a power in one person and diminish the former practical importance of another.

This is the positive connection to the predecessor's center, **operative possibility**. An actually existing capacity, a materially available possibility, an apprehended possibility, a compelling object, and its actual exercise are not identical. Their relations develop through history. A child can acquire a capacity without wanting to use it. An actor can want a course that has become impossible. A desire can originate through an activity which was undertaken for quite another reason. A materially changed world can alter a life without first becoming an object of thought. No movement is required to produce a new formation, and no formation is required to be presently recruited. Maintenance, rest, stasis, ordinary enjoyment, failure, loss and extinction belong to life. The theory is not an optimization objective.

A simple encounter contains the whole problem. A caregiver assists a child. The child's activity can produce a new capacity, which may make the caregiver's former aid unnecessary. The caregiver can delight in this independence or experience the loss of an indispensable role; the child may learn the skill but not receive the intended recognition. What the relation has produced changes what the relation is. Both participants are historically developing through the occurrence, and the independently real material conditions of their life can change as well. Love and possessiveness, attraction and constraint, independent action and shared history become intelligible through this one concrete movement without being reduced to equivalent mechanisms.

The same relation can exist at different scales. A worker's activity reproduces a household and a social arrangement which consumes the time needed for household life. An artist's activity produces a publicly invested figure whose continued reproduction can subordinate the artist's expressive activity. A church can produce a language of truth and then claim control over the truthful activity it made possible. The user affirms: **the attempt to possess the conditions of life can become the negation of one's relationship to the source of life.** This is a possible internal contradiction of successful production, not an eternal law against possession. A person can acquire material security without owning another's life. Nor must we derive every contradiction from this single ancestor: independent causes, nonrelations, divergent clocks, actual accidents and chance can be constitutive. Synthesis makes the differences intelligible; it does not erase them.

### The causal grammar of one life

**Contingency belongs inside the explanation, not outside it.** The inherited correction of the novelty question is more exact than a generic acknowledgement of uncertainty. The particular conjunction of this body's development, prior activity, historically available objects, independent Others, material circumstances and partial apprehension is part of what explains why a particular course became actual. Different histories can generate similar outcomes (equifinality); comparable histories can generate unlike outcomes (multifinality). General conditional regularities do not establish an individual's complete fate. The genealogical explanation of an actual formation, the contrastive explanation of differences, and the distributional explanation of conditional regularities have different evidence requirements. None licenses an unexplained master selector. The lack of a universal arbitrary-life predictor is not itself a deficient psychological mechanism. Conversely, “the whole history explains it” is empty unless the relevant particular conditions and their actual operation can be discriminated. If several explanations remain compatible with settled history, preserve the uncertainty rather than select the best story as an established cause. Fictional construction can choose among lawful historically plausible continuations; once actually settled, that history conditions its subsequent possibilities. This is not a metaphysical decision about whether human life is deterministic. The inherited mechanism-family answer is a provisional constitutive account, not completed domain science. **Relational non-exhaustion is a positive causal requirement, not a confession of insufficient context.** World exceeds present experience; the objective relation exceeds either participant's model; each person exceeds every role, self-explanation and recorded encounter. This is why historical compression cannot be a sufficient biography written from the outside. A real relation can develop beyond what either participant could previously apprehend. A new actual consequence may introduce an independently real object or third party, not merely change the actor's evaluation of a fixed scene. History B can change how A is operative; the jointly produced consequences of AB constitute material conditions which C actually encounters. This is a noncommutative historical relation, not a universal formula for integrating everything. Answerability without possession names its ethical corollary: a more accurate understanding of another person need not finish them as an object, and a genuine relationship can make their separateness more real rather than less. The person does not wait to be triggered. Bodily regulation, ongoing work, play, care, expectation, sensory interest, remembered activity, habit and private reflection can already be underway. A present cue can matter through that continuing organization; a person can initiate, refrain or develop a new purpose without any prewritten dramatic event. The iceberg is not a dormant inventory behind an active tip. It is the historically acquired organization through which the person moves, including parts not presently operative and parts effective without awareness. Its contents can change function; one acquired relation can change another's efficacy, scope, significance or future acquisition.

The constitutive circuit is:

- Historically acquired embodied, practical and relational organization in a presently active material world.
- Situated generation, recruitment and composition of practical objects, significance, possible courses and actual constraints.
- A person's own attempted and performed activity, possibly plural, ambivalent, incomplete or absent, in the independent activity of Others.
- Real settlement of material and social effects, including consequences not intended by anyone.
- Unequal actor-local reception, experience, affect, meaning and possible expression.
- Historically qualified retention, acquisition, differentiation, revaluation, contradiction, loss or no change.
- Continuing activity through the changed world and historically reconstituted powers.

These are noninterchangeable causal relations, not a mandatory sequence of calls or a requirement that all must be materialized on every turn. Anticipation, uptake, composition and actual performance can mutually change one another within bounded time. A later articulation may itself constitute new activity. A person can undergo development without any words. A false interpretation can be historically operative without becoming true. An objective event may remain inaccessible; a private activity can be real without having occurred publicly. Do not invent an executive that owns the whole iceberg.

A useful explanation is particular, contrastive or distributional. Particular genealogy concerns which activities and relations actually made the present formation possible; contrastive inquiry varies relevant conditions and histories; distributional inquiry asks which conditional tendencies recur under specified evidence. None requires a universal metaphysical selector that decides the exact future of arbitrary human lives after all causal history has supposedly been given. The choice among historically admissible fictional possibilities is construction, not discovery of a respondent's hidden fate. But history must not be used as a tautology: the specific operative relations need actual effects. Chance may settle among lawful alternatives, never create source authority or valid access.

The irreducibility of the living person also means that no successful functional approximation licenses a claim of literal consciousness or exhaustive knowledge of the real. Materialist explanation need not invoke a mystical remainder, and does not prove a person is exhausted by the current implementation. The psychological theories in the source canon make competing claims. A synthesis can establish a more exact relation among rival explanations and their different conditions, not decree compatibility. No moral arc, compulsory inner integration, disclosure, individuation, emancipation, ideological agreement or visitor importance is a criterion of a person's being alive.

## II. Biocultural genesis: bodies acquire different kinds of psychologically possible life

### Energetic materiality and the generation of desire

The immature organism is neither blank nor a compressed adult. Gametogenesis, gestation, birth, growth, metabolism, electrochemical organization, hunger, rest, illness, physical development and death concern literal material processes. Actual care and ecological/social reproduction sustain those processes. The body is historically and materially embedded from the beginning. Death is not the disappearance of energy or a universal psychic battery reaching zero. It is the cessation of organized living processes, without thereby answering the metaphysical question of their ultimate source.

The mystery of expenditure that produces aliveness is central, not an exception to the distinction of physical and psychological energy. Dancing may tire the body while expanding experienced vitality; rest may restore the body without excitement; actual work may produce an acquired means or a new dependency; the significance of a difficult act may alter what the person can endure and attempt. The expenditure of life can generate new conditions of its further expenditure. Its return can be metabolic renewal, a material effect, an acquired power or experienced significance; these are distinguishable causal relations that may coincide or diverge. Neither material success nor subjective intensity establishes the other. We do not yet have a general law of the mediations among them.

Conation precedes pressure. A bodily movement is not necessarily a motive or a desire with an articulated object. Need concerns conditions of continued life, including historically acquired ones. Desire is a direction of movement toward historically significant objects or relations. Attraction concerns the power of such an object to orient movement, compulsion a necessity whose exercise cannot readily be suspended, power actual efficacy and capacity to set terms. Pressure arises through the movement's relations to its conditions and other movements; flow is the actual course. Recognition can make the actor socially real without being approval. Love can be a historically formed significance of another life within one's own activity. These are provisional determinations of what we investigate, not an exhaustive taxonomy or force variables. Their transformation is the object of theory.

The inherited traction/pulsion/suasion/leading vocabulary is not universal. Hebrew rûaḥ and Arabic rūḥ, prāṇa, qì, Yorùbá àṣẹ and Māori mauri/hauora introduce distinct historical questions of animation, differentiated bodily functions, efficacy, knowability and relations of life with land, food and waters. The latter is not an ecological supplement to an otherwise isolated psyche: the entire Rawlsing conception concerns the radical embedding of the individual without abolishing individuality. The traditions are not interchangeable energy theories or scientific confirmation. Their specific source qualifications remain in the nine-pass source §0.14. RŪḤ is a question-bearing name, not explanatory authority.

### Acquired organs of activity, not a variable-strength adult psychology

**Self-regulation is acquired social activity with a changed causal role.** Another's demonstrations, rhythm, reminders or checking can become an actor's own way of organizing attention, recall, withholding, comparison or a future trial. The child need not have a prior propositional conception of that method or its source. A technique can remain effective after rejection of its teacher's authority, retain tacit deference, become a means of criticism, or fail in another setting. An articulate self-account, an available internally performed operation and a practical ability to change one's conduct must not be inferred from one another. Neither linguistic fluency nor biological age grants a reflective executive. Preserve the difference between interoceptive orientation, nondeclarative anticipation, practical inhibition, private deliberate rehearsal, autobiographical self-interpretation and extended self-critical conversation. The shared biological grammar must accommodate different *kinds* of culturally acquired activity without treating unfamiliar repertoires as deficits relative to a schooled literate adult. An internally organized activity can become the means through which further ends are generated, not merely an action preference. The existing historical self-organization and reflective-regulation functions are relevant inherited capital; the old sourcefree cases do not bound their possible development. [Inherited CO, L287–319](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/57f1f537cc45c5766d214d8f053c8df908145185/SEAM/30_THREE_AGENT_MULCH_20261119_R1/00_START_HERE_THE_CONSTITUTIVE_ORGANISM.md#L287-L319); [Inherited CO, L1207–1217](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/57f1f537cc45c5766d214d8f053c8df908145185/SEAM/30_THREE_AGENT_MULCH_20261119_R1/00_START_HERE_THE_CONSTITUTIVE_ORGANISM.md#L1207-L1217).

The infant moves and another life contributes. A caregiver's actual answer can make a movement receivable, differentiable, repeatable and consequential. An offered scaffold can enable the infant's own act or substitute for it. Actual performance, witnessed achievement, personal reception, learned coordination, desire for repetition and later independent exercise are separate. The infant can develop a vocal practice whose object was not initially available, eventually singing for its own sake. The caregiver can discover a delight in the infant's accomplishment. Conversely aid may fail, be imposed, not be recognized, produce a different skill, become a condition of approval, or generate no durable formation. The new capacity creates new possible deprivation. Development is historical differentiation of stakes as well as powers.

The kind of organization is also acquired. A child can learn signed communication, bodily co-regulation, nonverbal anticipation, a culturally specific gesture, practical discrimination, tool coordination, narrative recall, literacy, privately rehearsed speech, imagination, self-interpretation or an acquired inhibition, without all other kinds being present. Signed language is not defective spoken language; literacy does not certify global cognitive superiority; skilled practice need not involve propositional explanation. The historical world and bodies determine which kinds can become available. Do not install adult symbolic understanding in an infant because the constructing LLM can explain the situation. The adult's learned means of directing another's attention or action can become the child's means of organizing itself. Reflective activity is thus historical, not an inner sovereign. It may be absent, limited, culturally different, mistaken or disabling. Some practical distinctions become fluent without ever becoming explicit. Biocultural evidence must remain situated; neither WEIRD data nor romantic countergeneralization licenses a universal developmental law.

The positive germ must have functional consequences: bodily opportunity and actual independently performed response → child-owned act → possibly received consequence and qualified formation → later exercise in a differently situated activity → possible changes in both lives. Not all steps occur and no compulsory victory or gratitude follows. The same acquisition can enlarge the child's power and threaten the adult's way of living. A developmental system should not code the adult's experience from the child's effect or the child's attachment from the adult's intention.

### Historical availability of mental functions

Recover the six non-identical achievements from the earlier preproject/prospective/reflective articulation: bodily receptivity; learned orienting anticipation; a prospectable end; an actual trial with its independently settled and personally received consequences; a practiced or correspondingly generalized means; enduring striving/project or learned reflective regulation. They may overlap, fail to arise or change direction; they are not stages or a universal developmental ladder. A stimulus may become an object of orienting activity without a stable motive. Bodily relief can occur without experienced pleasure, attribution of its cause, gratitude, or desire for the relieving Other. ## III. Material power, objective constraint, and the lived time of practical possibility

A person's activity is materially enabled or prevented through independently existing relations of production and reproduction. Need and desire do not create a tool, time, legal right, transport, wages, consent, nourishment or another person's labor. An actor's competence is real without being currently exercisable; an institution's prohibition is real without being apprehended; a changed material circumstance can produce a possibility before the person knows it. There is no inner agency scalar that substitutes for these distinctions.

### Channels, constraints and the production of efficacy

Channels are actual or historically organized mediations of activity. Their conductance depends on the available body, learned means, access, time, objects, independent participants, actual rules and conditions of exercise. A groove is a recurrent organization which can do work later; recurrence alone is not evidence of acquisition. A wall may be absent means, prohibition, other activities, bodily constraint, acquired inhibition or another's refusal. CAN-008's blocking, buffering, amplification, deflection, rerouting and transformation name useful local functional effects, not conservation of a fixed hydraulic substance. An activity can create a new channel, reproduce its obstacle, or change what an earlier channel does. Not every wall is oppressive and not every opening is experienced as freedom.

The world contains the products of human activity that endure beyond their performance: houses, tools, timetables, institutions, household routines, rules, social rights and practices. Their endurance requires materially organized maintenance, sometimes by people unrecognized by the focal actor. There is no necessary narrator-mediated reissuance of them in every encounter. The production of a condition can be historically independent of its apprehension; a real change may alter several persons' opportunities without any common experience.

One act can reproduce its material preconditions and undermine its object. Wage labor can sustain a child while consuming care time. An acquired competence can decrease the labor of doing a task while its benefit is appropriated by an employer. A person's desire for art can make an objectively alienating work arrangement personally indispensable. The joy and domination can both be real. Objectification is not in itself alienation: a product can become a real power of living or a power over its producers. Distinguish the competence, the material means, access, formal authority, enforceability, actual performance, objective result and actor's significance. An expectation of efficacy does not establish efficacy. The person who can now operate without a particular helper is still embedded in shared tools, social labor, bodily and material conditions.

### Strata and reproduction

The inherited planetary, imperial bloc, national, regional, sect/ethno/class, movement/party, neighborhood/household, family and self strata locate interpressure; they are neither nine mental substances nor an exhaustive social ontology. The same activity can reproduce several relations without its participant apprehending them. A person's spontaneous responsibility can be the acquired medium of a family or class relation. Identification and actual structural participation are different. Resistance can reproduce a different stratum; rejection need not diminish real material dependence. Collective agency is a real consequence of organized activity, not a collective private psyche.

Field Forge and PYAT can enrich independently operative material worlds with historically specific forms of access, provision, cost, control, temporal renewal, institutional consequence and empirical structural relations. A social configuration's empirical correspondence does not itself establish a fictional actor's hidden meaning or an actual causal relation among institutions. The R17 correction remains binding: an authored institutional counterhistory under held empirical evidence proved a scoped structural transport, not the empirical identification of those authored formations. No retreat from the empirical ambition follows. Rich upstream data must change actual world possibilities or qualified abduction rather than decorate a prompt. The independently real field can in turn be modified by actual actor and collective activity, not solely by upstream reassignment. Thin and full-PYAT worlds use the same psychological grammar.

## IV. The historical production of new ends, methods, motifs, and fallible analogies

### How a movement becomes a different movement

**The world can produce the material of a future desire after the action.** This is a stronger distinction than the possibility of an actor thinking of a new end. A repair undertaken only to fulfill an obligation can actually produce an unexpected sound, a usable instrument, another person's independent interest, or an unforeseen cost. Its worldly existence is not authored by the actor's later meaning. A personally received unexpected sign may become a possible object of inquiry before the actor understands its cause or desires its continuation. An unreceived consequence can alter another life without becoming the focal actor's conscious object. The subsequent actual exploration may establish a new practical end, or the person may ignore it, misrecognize it or find it threatening. The historical unintended_goods function at 6647f746 qualifies an unexpected personally available result of actual activity as a possible future object, not an original intention, a motive already acquired, or a positive outcome guaranteed. This is the concrete temporal relation through which material production, independent worldly novelty and productive desire meet. [Inherited CO, L434–455](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/57f1f537cc45c5766d214d8f053c8df908145185/SEAM/30_THREE_AGENT_MULCH_20261119_R1/00_START_HERE_THE_CONSTITUTIVE_ORGANISM.md#L434-L455).

The current activity is not reducible to a response to an incoming event. A person can privately recall, imagine, compare, try a procedure, entertain a new object, rehearse speech, question a practice, become absorbed in music or stop thinking about something. Distinguish continuing acquired organization, actual private psychological activity and external realization. An imagined departure does not settle a departure; rehearsing an apology cannot establish another person's receipt. Private activity can itself change the person's history where qualified; it is not a license to invent unperformed childhood experiences. Endogenous initiative has a historical and bodily basis, not uncaused spontaneity.

An object emerges through the movement itself: a child notices an unexpected sound; a task produces a relation between things previously experienced separately; a skill acquired for a wage becomes a source of delight; an Other's actual response makes a different end available; a contradiction makes a former premise an object of inquiry. Leontiev's differentiation of motive, goal and operation and Lahire's conditional practical analogies illuminate how a formerly subordinate means or consequence can become a new object, and how transfer can be historically qualified without being certain. Sève and Dreier connect activities through lived time and material conduct. No single universal genesis law is issued. Preserve possibility of a merely noticed object, a wished but infeasible end, trial without durable acquisition, failed or mistaken analogy and independent consequence. Actual cultural practices and reciprocal exercise are mechanisms of the production of forms, not a theme tag.

### The emergence of an object that nobody pre-enumerated: active imagination, comparison and the genesis of new purposes


**The answer to whether Nadia can bring a new object of attention, inquiry, imaginative possibility or practical purpose into being from her own acquired organization is YES: the full Constitutive Organism must support this when historically appropriate.** It need not happen on every occasion and it need not require adult words, conscious deliberation or a single intrinsically creative temperament. A person may develop rich imaginative capacities, narrow defensive counterfactuals, muted curiosity, habitual acceptance or learned creative experimentation; those are historically produced differences.

The answer to **how** is already substantially developed in [SEAM/30-03 §§1, 4–9](03_GENESIS_AND_PSYCHODYNAMICS_RECOVERED_R1.md), rather than absent from Rawlsing. Leontiev's action–activity–motive transformations show how a previously subordinate goal or consequence can become the object of a new activity. Lahire's practical analogy lets an acquired way of seeing, approaching or attempting become *tentatively relevant in an unprecedented context* without declaring transfer already successful. Sève's historical formation/exercise of capacity and Dreier/Holzkamp's continuing conduct of life show how the means and costs of one activity condition other activities. PRE-A0 contributes bodily conation and changing personal-relational fields. PFF already distinguishes accessible-but-peripheral material, partial self-awareness, current focal attention and enacted self-feedback. **These sources supply multiple interacting operations, not a deterministic universal motivation law.**

A source-grounded *generative family* follows; its elements may overlap, be absent, loop or invert rather than form a compulsory staircase.

**(A) An ongoing activity differentiates the lived situation.** The person is already trying, feeling, avoiding, remembering, expecting, caring, practicing or merely doing something; what becomes noticeable depends upon embodied and learned distinctions, actual relations, prior interests and material possibility.

**(B) An unexpected relation can become experientially consequential.** A practical breakdown, variation, surplus, contradiction between two simultaneous commitments, accidental pleasure, another person's unforeseen contribution, newly recognized resemblance, or reencounter with an old memory can make a previously unimportant difference available to attention. A new external dramatic event is not mandatory: privately comparing familiar experiences can disclose a novel relation.

**(C) A *prospectable object* emerges before a completed act or a settled motive.** The novel object may be a sound Nadia wishes to reproduce; a way to care for another without asking permission; a new possible means to an old end; an imagined refusal; a pattern she privately fears; or the value of another's independent participation. What is conceivable, desired, socially permitted, actually feasible, tried and reliably learned are not the same thing. She may imagine what she cannot do and attempt what she does not yet know how to perform.

**(D) Previously acquired means can generate still different means.** Language, bodily skill, practiced comparison, recognition of functional likeness, internal rehearsal, counterfactual play, an acquired tolerance of ambiguity, and methods first supplied by Others can become usable *on* earlier acquired organizations. The transfer can be false, compulsive, playful, defensive, generative or inhibited. A later act of imagination may be driven by love, boredom, economic constraint, desire to control, aesthetic delight or nothing narratively decisive.

**(E) Lived afterlife can transform the relation among object, activity and motive.** An incidental consequence may become an anticipated end; performing an operation may become enjoyable for its own sake; a technical success may make the larger activity feel like failure; a new method can change how old commitments recruit one another. None guarantees a new permanent project. Objective outcome, personally accessible result, affect, relation, possible end, rehearsed plan, acquired practical capacity and durable motive must remain analytically distinct.

**(F) Novelty can reorganize future novelty.** An acquired practice of comparison may widen the possible objects of attention; a defensive self-story may narrow them; a new capacity may make an old goal feasible without making it newly desirable; a learned ideal may criticize the institution through which it was acquired. These are *transformations of generative possibilities*, not addition of another finite desire-slot.

Thus the theoretical task is not to let an LLM pick an emotion and a “new goal” after a scene. **Given this actor's actually lived constellations of activity, embodied capability, relations, history, uncertainty and current material world, what new difference could become meaningful, imaginable or prospectable, by what mediation, and with what possible future afterlives?** The construction model may propose historically coherent alternatives, including misinterpretation, unacted fantasy and no new end; the future actor/world history must then establish what, if anything, actually became operative. Neither prior code action menus nor convenient psychoanalytic prose limit what the theory requires or validate an unearned historical cause.

The person may also be **stubbornly uncreative in this respect** on an occasion. A profound novel experience can be ignored; a minor routine can subtly reorganize an acquired method; a long period of reflection can fail to change conduct; a new way of wanting may arise without preceding verbal self-knowledge. The system needs the breadth of these outcomes **for specific historical reasons**, not a universal innovation bonus. The differentiable unit is the actor's changing activity and relation to practical objects, not a generic novelty scalar.



**Causal junction — creativity without predestined ends.** What emerges can be an object of attention, a privately entertained possibility, a practiced method, a new purpose or a durable motive. Treating these as synonymous would give the system a falsely miraculous power to turn every novelty into lasting psychic transformation.

The functional analogy case is especially stringent. A prior actual trial and a later personally received contrasting sign may qualify a possible similarity and a new trial, not already mastered analogy. The new similarity can be false, local or otherwise meaningful. The same teacher label does not establish the child has learned that two persons can assist in comparable ways. Cultural mediation must preserve the independently spoken word, sensory encounter, actual inquiry and learned meaning; social vocal coordination does not establish general language competence. Joint practice must include the Other's actual independent participation. A new object may be acquired without explicit conceptual understanding, and a later word can refunction an older practical experience.

### Historically charged symbols

A symbol is an objectification of earlier activity whose historical relations can become means of presently living something. The capacity to apprehend it has also been produced. A word may be meaningful before its denotation is understood; a sound can bring several historical relations into conjunction without their complete recall. The same perceptible word or object acquired through distinct routes and histories is not the same possible event. The sign's efficacy is not a scalar affect stored in it. A poem may make an unresolved contradiction available for actual expression without resolving it. It can do something its verbal explanation cannot.

Alcohol, poetry and prayer have radically different material mechanisms and effects, but one activity may take up some of another's historical functions, changing the conditions of continued life. No general addiction etiology, medical substitution or diagnosis of artists follows. The person's own articulation of what moved them may remain incomplete. Marx's historically produced musical ear, Vygotsky's social organization of emotion and Hall's differentiated reception each help illuminate this without erasing theoretical differences.

The stadium example contains a greater scale of the same problem. Michael Jackson's public figure was historically produced through Black American cultural traditions, extraordinary performance, collaborators, Motown and the music industry, and the audiences' own prior historical participation. A present gesture can bring previously distributed collective meaning into a jointly practicable expression. The audience's actual response in turn changes the performer's situation; the figure continues to acquire significance. Spirit-, energy- and symbol-mancing name the capacity of a historically formed symbolic activity to make such meanings operative in living movement, not paranormal control or one shared psyche. Synchrony alone cannot explain why this particular figure and song have acquired their power. The same genuinely vivifying form can become capital, whose reproduction confronts its producer as a demand. Its audience's joy need not diminish with the artist's alienation. The relation between the two is historical, not a static polarity of real art and false commerce.

The acquired organization does not have a fixed function once formed. A new practice can give a person a criterion of competence independent of the earlier caregiver; or the practice may become another route to approval. Both histories can coexist. A new meaning can make an old relation apprehensible as a particular contingent arrangement. This is not simply transferring intensity between objects. The historical production of practical and symbolic powers changes what another historical organization can do. The actual material relation and its significance must not be confused.

## V. The simultaneously active psyche: recruitment, affect, memory, and partial expression

### The current is an operation, not a psychological thing

**Differential integration is neither global synthesis nor a bag of local dispositions.** Leontiev's multiple meaning-forming lines and changing organization of activity do not establish inevitable internal integration; Lahire's contextual acquisition does not rule out genuine higher-order historical organization. The crossing of actual activities, incompatible acquired repertoires, felt contradiction, a persisting relation among them and a slow motif are different achievements. A child may love a caregiver and pursue a craft elsewhere without any meaningful conflict; the two activities may compete for time without generating a durable psychic contradiction; or continued care may become conditional on renouncing the craft, and actual later history may organize concealment or conflicted enjoyment. A single global coherence measure cannot distinguish these histories. Nor is a locally coherent practice necessarily a compartment maintained through defense. Nonrelation, juxtaposition, real dependency, conflict and acquired compartmentalization have different possible afterlives. Hierarchy can be real in a particular life, materially or psychologically acquired, without the psyche becoming a universal ranked pyramid. The relative authority of an activity may weaken, recur or remain domain-specific. An Enneagram-style descriptor, if used at all, does not cause such a formation. The RŪḤ synthesis requires the specificity of these connections, not a theory of automatic conflict or compulsory psychic integration. A current is the situated operation of historically produced organization through actual or apprehended objects, relations and conditions. It may contribute a direction of activity, sustain a practice, generate a practical premise, make another relation salient, alter the significance of an outcome, restrict a course, or remain indirectly effective while its direct expression is inhibited. The same act can participate in several actual activities with different objects. There may be no synthesis, no dominant motive, no conscious conflict, or one activity that temporarily constrains another's expression. The histories may remain separate. Multiple currents do not constitute an additive voting system. A choice may be performed while another ongoing activity remains actually operative and conditions the next event.

This is why recruitment and genesis are two movements of one historical life, not the same operation. An acquired organization conditions activity; activity and its consequences can alter what the organization is capable of doing. A new means of exercise can transform an old relational orientation with no change in the original Other. The exercise–parental-recognition example is a test of this actual change of function, not a score for self-worth. The practice can become a means of competence, an object of desire, a means of getting recognition, a condition of self-experience or all of these. ## VI. Independent Others and polycentric histories: the relationship neither actor wholly owns

### Caught movement produces a new condition, not a closed loop

An actor's activity can become consequential within another independently living person's activity. What returns is not the original charge but the Other's own movement, shaped by their body, purposes, acquired powers and conditions. A jointly produced practice, object or relation can become a new means of each life. The Other can also refuse, misunderstand, be absent or contribute without being witnessed. The caught current is a question of actual efficacy and historical significance, not automatic mirroring, reinforcement or reciprocity. Home can develop where one's activity can participate in a world that can receive and sustain it; that does not mean control over that world.

Independence is not isolation. A person alone can act through historically acquired obligations, anticipated authority, prior care, learned language, material provision or a remembered relationship. A present Other may be irrelevant while an absent Other remains operative. Co-presence is a useful source qualification, never a universal condition of social mediation. Conversely an actual interaction may not be personally significant or even sensed. Each person's history includes objective shared events and unevenly received personal consequences. The household has real shared practice and independent relations, not a group mind.

An ordinary meal suffices: one adult purchases the food, another cooks while exhausted, a child attempts to help, a sibling is occupied with something else. The provision, actual labor, person's reception, body, motivation, memory and acquisition differ. The child may gain a means without knowing the cost; one adult may experience relief while another takes on further work. The acquired capacity can later change how the household reproduces itself. A parent can develop a commitment through actual care while the child develops no corresponding expectation, or vice versa. Love is not one of two quantities in a relationship state; another's life can become an object of genuine concern, without guarantee of shared understanding or identical benefit. The freedom of the Other, including their actual refusal, remains a condition of the relation's independent reality.

### Reachability and relation are distinct

A route into another's activity has a controller, scope, actual opportunity, permission, temporal condition, dependencies and revocation. A spare key is not a right to inspect the person; a colleague may have legitimate access when a loved person does not. A route can change through an independent third party. Intimacy does not confer consent, affection does not prove trust, and restoration of conversation does not restore a key. A person's anticipated availability is not the user's binding commitment. Reachability changes the actual conditions of a relation and can have further consequences without changing attachment.

### A relationship is six differently owned causal realities, not a dyadic score


[SOROK Court 9 durable relation](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/23_COURT9_MINIMUM_DURABLE_RELATIONSHIP_ONTOLOGY_R1.md) and [D001 relationalization](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/dockets/D001_RELATIONAL_ONTOLOGY_V3_BOUNDARY/03_SOROK_RELATIONALIZATION_DELTA_OVER_INHERITED_PFF_R1.md) distinguish: (a) objective history of actually shared acts; (b) current enforceable commitments, debts, permissions, property and work arrangements; (c) each actor's fallible model of the other; (d) each actor's relation-conditioned practical organization, including what they allow themselves to want or request; (e) each actor's selective and differently accessible memories of shared history; and (f) actual third-party/network consequences. A convenient computed relationship summary may be used as an index, never as a new owner or a single true quantity called intimacy.

- **Trust is domain-specific prediction,** not one relationship magnitude. One person may trust Nadia with a child's care but not with money, political judgment or a secret; accuracy is not guaranteed.

## VII. Care, authority, love, hurt, and historically learned social categories

### When the activity sustaining a relation becomes a means of domination

Care is materially performed activity, not an inferred dyadic affection. The caregiver may expend time, labor and bodily resources while the child learns a useful practice, experiences humiliating discipline or simply receives nourishment. The adult may form a practiced commitment, the child a solicitation method, neither a shared emotional account. Actual material provision can come from another absent person. Care, recognition and effective authority are nonidentical. A hostile witness can confer public reality; someone ignored may have genuinely accomplished something. The differential power to make a category real for another is part of the actual world.

The same care that originally sustained growth can produce the conditions of its own apparent negation. A child exercises a new power independently; the caregiver's earlier activity becomes unnecessary. Possessiveness can originate here without an originally possessive type. The attempt to secure freely lived reciprocity through control can preserve its outward form and destroy its defining character. Its diminished return can recruit further control, a different relation or no enduring transformation. Actual security, ownership of a thing, self-disposition, mutual commitments and domination of another's movement must be distinguished.

### Identification, alienation and the self as stratum

PRE-A0's two directional tendencies—identification/internalization/naturalization/invisibility/agent-of and alienation/externalization/objectification/visibility/agency-against—are historical possibilities, not equivalent states or necessary progressions. Objectification in the sense of a durable product differs from a person's apprehension of that relation as an object. Neither is in itself Marxian alienation. Actual domination and subjective identification/disidentification can diverge. Awareness, material power, solidarity, viscerality, actual efficacy and agency against must not collapse.

These relations can organize how a body is lived.
