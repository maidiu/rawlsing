## I. The change of center: not things interacting, but a living organization that makes things what they are

An initial psychology of the iceberg might say: a person possesses bodily needs, feelings, motives, practices, attachments, memories, beliefs and defenses; the world cues some of them; relations among them determine the response; development changes those stored things.

That formulation is useful as a *data index*. As a conception of the person, it is incomplete.

**The psychologically constitutive unit is the organism's historically organized relationship to its world through concrete activity, participation, experience, recognition, material dependence and practical possibility.** What later appears in the read model as an acquired “form” may be a relatively stabilized differentiation within that history. Its meaning and function may depend on the organization in which it is presently involved.

A tenderness is not everywhere the same fixed psychological quantum. It can become a means of fostering someone else's independence, justifying coercive discipline, seeking approval, defending an endangered self-image, enduring grief, or refusing an institution. Its object may be the other person's flourishing, the continuation of the relationship, one's standing as “a good caregiver,” or several of these in contradictory configurations. **The transformation can occur without either the tenderness or its historical source vanishing.**

Nor is there a fixed independent operator standing above the person's iceberg and selecting its contents. The historically constituted psychic organization is **already operating**: conditioning notice, felt significance, anticipation, desire, inhibition, motor readiness, self-account, fantasy, defensiveness and action. The public tip is a partial manifestation of this ongoing organism–world encounter, not a rival faculty sometimes overpowered by “the unconscious.”

This is the first real recasting:

> **The individual does not merely acquire psychological contents and connections. Their life forms changing organizations of activity and relation through which a content may become a different kind of psychological possibility, and those organizations themselves can be recruited, inhibited, reproduced or transformed.**

This must not become a claim that material bodies or stable acquired capacities are illusory. Bodies have real needs; practiced operations can endure; the world has independently existing institutions; discrete memories and actions are still legitimate objects. **The relata and the relations are reciprocally constitutive without becoming interchangeable.** The explanatory question is what a durable form *does and becomes in an actual historical relation*, rather than how many points are assigned to it.

The useful smallest unit is therefore not an abstract edge or a wholly isolated event. It is a **historically positioned, actor-specific participation in material life**, with what was objectively done, what was accessible and lived, which relations were operative, what the actor attempted, what actually happened, and what remains consequential.

This center explains why the two earlier open problems—**genesis** and **psychodynamic operation**—belong to one historical process considered at different timescales. The same activity whose constituted relations make this event matter today can help produce new relations that make another event matter tomorrow.

## II. The six realities that must never be merged

The single person is a causal unity, not one sovereign data store or a flat graph. Six different realities, with different authority, must be made to meet.

| Reality | Constitutive work | False collapse to prohibit |

| **Independent world and social relations** | Actual people, objects, productive means, labor, care, property, institutions, religious/state power, time, resource flows, coercion, opportunity | The focal person's self-understanding, the LLM's invented setting or a universal structural score |
| **Durable historical psychic organization: iceberg** | Acquired expectations, investments, dispositions, practices, projects, nondeclarative traces, memories, identifications, alienations, self-models, defenses and **relations among them** | Questionnaire traits, a biography paragraph, a conscious ego, a universal type hierarchy |
| **Situated relational psychodynamics** | Bodily availability, selective salience, receivability, uptake/impression, contextual and analogical recruitment, pressures/flows, conflicts, inhibitions, partial affective experience | A neutral event completely perceived before a conscious actor retrieves motives; a scalar maximizer |
| **Conduct, lived present, and outward tip** | What is privately activated, what the actor can experience or explain, what is attempted/withheld/performed, and what each observer actually sees | Four interchangeable versions of a narrator's account; a transparent diagnostic readout |
| **Historical afterlife** | World consequence and owner-qualified retention, repetition, changed scope, relation, access, affective investment, future recruitment, rupture, or explicit no durable change | Narrative explanation as native psychology; mandatory progress; erasing actual history |

**Three especially important distinctions run across all six.**

1. **World relation ≠ psychic relation ≠ shared practical organization.** Actual household authority may form and constrain an individual's sense of obligation without being accurately understood by them; actual coordinated care is not a third fused mind.
2. **System explanation ≠ actor perception ≠ actor self-account ≠ viewer access.** The person may sincerely misread their own motivation; the model's analytical terminology does not become an infant's conceptual possession.
3. **History is monotonic; present interpretation, availability and functional organization are not.** A recorded injury, assistance, sacrifice or betrayal remains historically true even when its meaning, accessibility or practical effect changes.

These separations are not newly invented by this document. **PRE-A0 §§3–6, 13–17** already describes the canonical causal transition, retained lived artifact, psychic afterlife, changing interpretation and Personal Relational Field. **Adopted CAN-008 R2/PFF** already establishes actor receivability, uptake/appraisal, situated recruitment and multi-stream present response with separate owner effects. R3 recasts their *shared ontological meaning*, not their authority.

## III. The person×event circuit, now properly recursive

The person does not receive a psychologically neutral description, choose an iceberg item, and then ask an LLM to decide how to feel.

**Historically constituted organization and present-world conditions jointly condition what becomes salient or felt.** Actor receivability and attention can be altered by body and by acquired expectations. Inherited CAN-008's stipulated coupling order remains binding as an interface; this does not force conscious interpretation to precede preconceptual salience. Some reciprocal suboperations may occur inside the lawful current transaction.

**Source-qualified conceptual circuit:**

1. **Continuing material life and embodied actor:** actual time, needs, workplace/care/institution, independently acting Others, existing root/branch history and competing ongoing activities. Endogenous initiative and quiet routine do not depend upon an incoming user message.
2. **Present constitutive encounter:** an actual event, condition, gesture, bodily interruption, remembered cue or unfinished practical process intersects an already operating psychic organization.
3. **Situated relational recruitment:** contexts and analogical resemblances differentially activate, inhibit, redirect or leave dormant historically formed organizations, including relations **among** forms, relational motifs and previous ways of responding to contradiction.
4. **Multi-activity psychodynamics:** the same potential act participates in different projects/relations; affect and anticipation may register their differently meaningful consequences; guilt, protection, anger, longing, fear, obligation and bodily pressure can be concurrent without an internal arithmetic vote.
5. **Possible and actual activity:** what can be conceived, what bodily capability permits, what is socially authorized, what others voluntarily contribute, what can be attempted, and what world conditions actually allow must remain distinct. Quiet, delay, refusal, compromise, concealment, forced compliance and failed attempts are all possible.
6. **Independently resolved consequences:** the material world, institutions and Others do not automatically concede the actor's intent; unilateral injury does not require victim consent, but cooperative participation cannot be forged.
7. **Actor-specific lived experience and owner-qualified afterlife:** distinct received sensory/affective consequences, tacit traces, memories, changing relations, practice, eligibility, suppression, revaluation, or legitimate no durable change. Each actor may undergo a different transformation from the same objective event.
8. **The continuing person and world:** the resulting constitutional and material histories alter the conditions under which future activities become thinkable, salient, possible, wanted or resisted.

**The LLM's place is constrained by the principal's event-first rule.** The engine computes an admitted persona shift, and later actor-safe LLM realization renders something faithful to it. PRE-A0 may separately use an analytical LLM to **propose** developmental semantic changes, but they become facts *only after competent typed admission* and may not retcon performed world events. The renderer does not supply hidden causes to make a beautiful scene plausible. It is legitimate for the analytic LLM to use sophisticated concepts the actor cannot possess; no such concepts may be quietly installed as the actor's consciousness.

**The “tip” is not the psyche's opposite.** Distinguish private situated activation, first-person present, actor-authored attempted/performed conduct, and observer-specific manifestation. Some private current can be stronger than anything outwardly displayed. The engine may know a generated causal history without the person knowing it, but must still grade its fictional and scientific authority.

## IV. The missing mathematics is not a conserved emotional substance

The principal's image of a **first law of thermodynamics applied to experience** has real methodological force: a blocked or repressed historical organization must not be deleted simply to simplify current action selection. The difficulty of expressing tenderness may make anger more defensive; suppressed dependence may inform moral strictness; inhibited loyalty may produce uncertainty or fixation; unresolved desire may reorganize attention elsewhere. Negation, repression and enforced stasis can be productive of an ongoing organization.

But a literal law of psychological-energy conservation is unsupported. There is no empirically established transferable scalar of love, grief, psychic force, influence or meaning. Bodily states recover or deteriorate; expectations extinguish; skills decay; memories fade or become inaccessible; a relation can actually end; not every occurrence leaves a persistent individual mark.

**The governing invariant should be causal-historical accountability, not numerically conserved libido.**

A system must distinguish:

- **directly expressed and causally active**;
- **active in a private/indirect relationship despite outward inhibition**;
- **temporarily occluded, contextually dormant or presently unrecruited**;
- **stably suppressed by an acquired defensive organization**;
- **attenuated, extinguished, displaced or genuinely transformed**;
- **historically existent but no longer functional**;
- **not observed, not modeled or source-unknown**;
- **event with no new durable owner effect**, even though material life continues or an old pattern is reproduced.

This is not a mandatory flux event for every psychological object each minute. A latent configuration can remain historically constituted without consuming full compute on every tick. What is prohibited is **silently using an inactive/excluded-set flag as proof that the form cannot affect the current psychic configuration by any other available pathway**.

Here is the deeper dialectical understanding of “productive”:

**Production includes formation, reproduction, deformation, preservation-through-negation, inhibition, rerouting, attenuation, and the historically consequential absence of an expected response.** The reproductive continuation of domination may be more important than a freshly created trait. Sublation and sublimation are *candidate historically meaningful outcomes*, not universal three-step algorithms or moral guarantees.

## V. Relational topology: a functional grammar, not a magnetic personality meter

PRE-A0 §14 already defines the **Personal Relational Field**, including typed, directed, context-conditional relations and higher-order suppression chains, compensation loops, conflict triangles, public/private partition, identification and defensive cycles.

The prior assistant's claim to have discovered this from nowhere was wrong. The live gap is between that inherited specification and the bounded R3 implementation.

The promising computational unit is a **qualified, time-dependent functional relationship** between already constituted organizations. Its current effect depends upon real context, actor history, available body, epistemic access, social authority and the other relations in which it participates. There need not be a global affinity, salience or polarization scalar. A relation may be strong in one context, weak in another, and functionally different in a third.

| Inherited or provisional relation | What it can do in present activity | What historical change might entail |
|---|---|---|
| **REINFORCES / ACTIVATES / RECRUITS** | A form or cue makes another more relevant, available or executable | New resemblance/contrast, acquired context, recurrent association or changed source |
| **INHIBITS / SUPPRESSES / QUARANTINES_FROM** | Prevents overt expression or an eligible line of action, possibly while preserving indirect pressure and conflict | Persistent defense, recontextualization, breakdown or extinction; no automatic hidden expression |
| **SUBORDINATES / INSTRUMENTALIZES** | Makes one acquired value, end or practice function in service of another | Care becomes discipline, labor becomes proof of worth, solidarity becomes institution-preservation |
| **LICENSES / JUSTIFIES / RATIONALIZES** | Alters how an actor experiences a practice as permissible, righteous or intelligible | A powerful authority relation can legitimate harmful conduct without altering the world-owned harm |
| **COMPENSATES_FOR / REDIRECTS** | Another activity provides a learned substitute, protection or partial satisfaction when an original relation is blocked | Ongoing defensive practices, creative sublimation, substitute investment or misdirected aggression |
| **INTERPRETS_THROUGH / REVALUES** | The significance of one form changes through a second relation, actual encounter or recognized contradiction | Previously admired decisiveness becomes experienced as control; grief becomes treasured remembrance |
| **CONFLICTS_WITH / MUTUALLY_CONSTRAINS** | Coexisting organizations impose incompatible claims or costs | Compromise, compartmentalization, intensified contradiction, rupture, compliance or no change |
| **MUTUALLY_REINFORCES / relational motif** | Several historically learned relations function as a recurring configuration | A context recruits an entire defensive/attachment pattern rather than a handful of independent forms |

These are *semantic functional requirements and illustrations*, not newly issued operator enums or one-size-fits-all algorithms. Some names are existing PRE-A0 candidates; others merely describe possible effects. Competent owner semantics determine which are lawful.

**Relations among relations** matter especially. The person may come to disapprove of their own desire for recognition, fear their own longing, resent their dependence on someone they admire, or treat their affection as a threat to moral self-understanding. The new object of feeling is not just another physical person; it can be the person's **own relationship between earlier psychological forms**. This is one historically intelligible origin of reflective or defensive higher-order organization.

An acquired psychological element therefore has at least two potentially changing features: **the form of what it can do**, and **the historically constituted network through which doing it takes on different significance**. One can change without a simple increase/decrease in the other.

**Compression is indispensable.** Explicitly materialize historically consequential motific patterns where needed, retain ancestry and source qualification, and recruit bounded context-sensitive neighborhoods rather than every possible edge. A rare tone, odor, gesture or situation may recruit an old configuration through practical analogy, even mistakenly; retrieval cannot be restricted to recent frequent associations. An inhibited organization can have an indirect relation-mediated effect without being promoted to public action. No universal automatically expanding hidden trauma graph.

## VI. The demonstration that rewrites the organization: care becomes a mechanism of cruelty

The pastor example is not just a new case under “authority.” It shows **why the relation must precede a simple moral polarity in our explanation**.

**Objective history:** a religious leader humiliates or injures a congregant. This is an actual event with world-owned speaker, target, witnesses, setting, harm and consequences. Calling it “righteous rebuke” is a performed claim or audience interpretation, not a canonical judgment that the injury was just.

**Historically produced field:** the leader's institution may provide care, resources, membership, status, discipline, salvation-language, authoritative interpretation and social risk for dissent. Each follower has a different life history within that field, with varying learned meanings and material dependencies.

**Follower's existing organization, in one plausible history:** love of community, investment in care, trust in pastoral guidance, fear of exclusion, desire to belong to truth, identification with “good believer,” memories of previous rebukes, sympathy with outsiders, anxiety about personal shame. There is no single ready-made “authoritarian trait” that explains all this.

**The decisive relation:** the believer may already have acquired a linkage in which **legitimate care sometimes requires painful correction**, and the pastor is recognized as entitled to decide what counts as that correction. A performed ritual of rebuke can therefore **recruit care itself into support of the injury**. This is not necessarily compassion being switched off by aggression. The function of compassion can be **transduced** through a historically acquired relation to authority, righteous discipline and in-group protection.

Different followers can receive the *same* objectively hurtful event differently:

| Possible situated outcome | Mechanism, conditional on actual history |
|---|---|
| **Devout solidarity with the pastor** | The act is recruited as truth-protection or pastoral love; confidence and attachment may increase |
| **Zealous imitation** | “Discipline others” becomes an available practice or moral project; internalized policing becomes policing of others |
| **Protective conformity** | The follower feels unease or sympathy but performs agreement because livelihood, family or belonging are endangered |
| **Compartmentalized distress** | Tenderness or memory of being harmed remains causally significant but is inhibited in the congregation's public ritual |
| **Disillusionment** | Earlier respect and care become reorganized through witnessed hypocrisy, alternate witnesses or a new practical contrast |
| **No special afterlife** | The event is unnoticed, barely accessible or does not durably change this follower's organization |

This is the original PRE-A0 §10 movement **IDENTIFICATION → INTERNALIZATION → NATURALIZATION → INVISIBILITY → AGENT-OF**, extended to the possibility that *care or loyalty provides the means of domination*, not just a cloak over cruelty. It is also the reverse possibility of a good learned through an institution later being directed against the institution that taught it.

The leader's language and recognized form matter. “Righteous rebuke” may carry a culturally acquired genre of legitimate discipline; an unexplained “you fucking dipshit” may fail to recruit that genre. But no magic word guarantees moral authorization: some communities may themselves recognize blunt humiliation as strong or holy leadership. **The historical field makes the speech act effective or ineffective**, not piety as a property of vocabulary.

The causal chain is consequently:

**material/social authority → legitimate genre and institutional performance → actor-specific learned identification/other-model → situated relational recruitment → meaning of care/obedience/harm → actual follower response → objective reproduction/contestation of authority → altered psychic and social history.**

Gramsci's hegemony explains how rule can become commonsense leadership; Althusser directs attention to materially repeated subject-forming rituals; Bourdieu to the institution's authorized speaker; Foucault to pastoral power organized as direction of conduct and conscience; Fromm to authority experienced as one's own moral voice; Freire to the possible reproduction of an oppressor's practices; Leontiev to multiple object-directed personal senses; Sève to the objective material conditions sustaining them. **Their theoretical apparatuses are not interchangeable or universally validated psychodynamic functions.**

The victim's independent history must never be rewritten by followers' moral certainty. Outward compliance does not establish inward endorsement. This single scene already demands the whole SOROK organism, not a generic sentiment classifier.

## VII. A second demonstration: love turns painful without having been false

Suppose one actor genuinely admires another's courage, humor, protectiveness and conviction. The positive relationship is neither an illusion nor proof of complete knowledge. Different facets of the Other are visible in different circumstances; the admirer encounters only the outward tip through their own historically conditioned attention.

Over time, the admired person's “decisiveness” is encountered as deciding *for* others, restricting their autonomy, punishing dissent or concealing harm. Several different historical transformations are possible:

- **objective discovery**: the Other did something genuinely new, or previously hidden institutions/actions became known;
- **contextual occlusion**: old admiration remains accessible in some situations but is crowded out in the current relational configuration;
- **revaluation**: one recognized quality acquires new significance through another, from protection to domination;
- **split/contextual partition**: a loved representation and an aversive representation of the same person remain segregated in different activities;
- **ambivalent coactivation**: gratitude and anger are simultaneously consequential; the presence of one makes the other more painful;
- **defensive inhibition**: tenderness becomes difficult to express because it threatens another commitment or the actor's attempt to protect themselves;
- **relation-to-relation change**: the actor resents their *own dependence on recognition* as distinct from withdrawing admiration for the other person;
- **material rupture**: an actual change of access, safety, cohabitation or social status alters the relation before the actor's interpretation catches up.

These cannot be collapsed into attraction shifting from +0.7 to –0.8. **The relation between previously appreciated forms and newly recognized conduct may become more consequential than either form by itself.** Some are changes of the world, some of actor access, some of recruitment, some of affective valuation, some of the higher-order organization. They can coexist.

A later apparently small remark can recruit the whole charged configuration and produce an apparently disproportionate response. The subject may sincerely believe the response is about the immediate remark; the program may have generated a deeper historical explanation, but must not grant it to the subject or pretend it is clinical truth about an actual person. An old affection may remain the reason the rupture hurts so much.

**Do not impose an “integration” arc.** Freud's unconscious ego defenses, Jung's affectively toned complexes and Shadow, and Klein's segregated versus ambivalent object representations offer useful candidate mechanisms, but not universally proven organs or developmental destinies. A character can repress, continue conflicted, rationalize, misread, suppress, endure, partly integrate, or change the world. The same concepts must also accommodate correctly recognizing genuine wrongdoing, rather than diagnosing justified anger as projection.

## VIII. How adult emotions acquire their strange complexity

The principal's image—adult emotions sometimes resembling hot mustard or oysters to a child—points to a nontrivial developmental phenomenon. An emotion can come to have **another emotion, a relation, an anticipated loss, a symbol or one's own response as its object**.

Bittersweetness need not be a weighted average of sadness and pleasure. It may involve the present absence of something treasured, the joy remembered, the value of being able to grieve it, and a learned practice of sustaining the object's meaning through remembrance.

Similarly, someone may experience shame **about** pleasure; resentment **at** their own dependence; fear **of** longing; pleasure **in** controlled vulnerability; relief **at** permission to mourn. Such configurations involve higher-order affective, symbolic, bodily and interpersonal relations whose constitution can be historically specific.

Consensual BDSM, cuckolding or other erotic power practices illustrate how consent, trust, taboo, power, intimacy, excitement and ritual meaning can transform an experience; they **do not** imply a universal childhood wound, pathological status, humiliation origin or single theory of desire. Lacanian *jouissance* can be an interpretive provocation, not a quantitative field in the psyche. Empirical distinctions between liking, wanting and learned expectation, and evidence of mixed emotions, support taking these dimensions seriously without validating Freud, Jung or Lacan as complete developmental laws.

A simulation that simply sums two emotion labels cannot generate these meanings. A system that represents acquired relational organization **between affect, object, memory, practice, moral interpretation and personal history** has a better conceptual basis. Still, the concrete relation must be generated/earned in the particular life, not selected from a compulsory universal matrix of adult complexities.

## IX. The Other is independent, but two icebergs do not consciously manage themselves

A's deep history conditions A's salience, possible initiative, inhibition and tip. B encounters an actual portion of A's world manifestation through B's own body, history and relational organization. B's response is likewise partially opaque to A. **Neither person is an agentic manager above a hidden psychological store.** The world contains material facts beyond both. An actor may acquire limited deliberate self-direction; that acquired ability is part of the same historically constituted person.

**Three relation histories must coexist:**

1. **Objective shared and institutional history**: actions, resources, housing, care, coercion, workplace, property, kinship, law, participation and actual power.
2. **Each individual's internal relation to the Other(s)**: models, misreadings, trust, affection, erotic and recognition investments, memory, unconscious traces, anticipated authority, resentment and identification.
3. **Continuing joint practical organization** where one actually develops: recurring care, cooperative production, learned games, marital routines, congregational discipline, peer activities, collective struggles. It is not a merged consciousness.

**Family triads are irreducible to isolated dyads.** Nadia's relation to Father may change through witnessing how Father treats Mother; her understanding of Mother's accommodation may change through learning the objective conditions shaping it. The two parents independently possess lives, psychic investments and relation-specific models. The child may develop within a parental relation before possessing concepts for either “parental relationship” or “institutional power.”

**Power is constitutive, not just a modifier of feelings.** Its practical capacity to decide whose needs matter, who can work, who speaks with authority, who controls resources and which consequences are enforceable can organize recognition, shame, desire and practical means. Neither a correct idea nor intense emotion automatically changes that material structure.

PRE-A0's living-relation versus relational-reification distinction matters here. A person may sacrifice the living spouse to the ideal of THE MARRIAGE, the child to the identity of GOOD MOTHER, or actual comrades to THE REVOLUTION. This is not a command to use theology as native ontology; it is a possible relational reorganization in which a category claiming to preserve a living relationship becomes dominant over the relationship's participants. Preserve the phenomenon, allow “idolatry” or other normative readings only downstream.

Independent Other action does not imply universal consent. A person can bully, interrupt, shame, withdraw care or impose a material harm without the victim's permission. **Only voluntary co-participation must be independently authored by the participating actor.** Actual coercion and its differently experienced afterlives are not forbidden because a consent receipt is absent.

## X. How a new psychological organization begins without a preinstalled adult mind

A fixed adult list of drives cannot explain the development of Nadia. A purely empty newborn graph cannot either. The body begins with emerging sensory/motor, regulatory, attentional and practical abilities, changing through maturation and life, not a predeclared ideological destiny.

**One historical path to becoming:**

1. Actual bodily sensation and dependence arise within concrete care or neglect, with limited and developmentally changing access.
2. Repeated and varied encounters can produce a tacit sensorimotor/affective expectation or nondeclarative causal trace; an analyst's vocabulary is *not* installed into the infant.
3. Joint activity can organize a new operation; objective coactivity, a mother's help, a child's own contribution and the accomplished effect remain distinct.
4. A partial cue may recruit the trace and make a **first exploratory attempt** possible before a stable desire or independent competence has been demonstrated.
5. An originally incidental consequence may become anticipated, sought, resisted or taken as a candidate *end*. Its motivational role must be historically developed and can change.
6. Practices and signs acquired through Others can become means of coordinating one's own attention, action, recall, inhibition and self-description; a previously social operation may be independently used and later applied to a different project.
7. Contradictions, failed attempts, material exclusions, love, domination, grief and ordinary repetitions can reproduce, redirect or reorganize a learned practice. The person may acquire a new way of recruiting or bearing an old conflict.
8. Reflective self-models may eventually form and become objects of practical effort, but remain fallible and only partially govern the iceberg.

This recasts **genesis** as the historical change of relations among embodied capabilities, acquired practices, anticipations, meanings and available activities. **Psychodynamic operation** is those historically formed relations becoming consequential in current activity. They are related causal questions at different temporal scales, not two mystical missing engines. The particular developmental result remains scientifically underdetermined.

A crucial tension must remain: **new possibility can precede proven competence**. An actor may contemplate or attempt a new composition through analogy before acquiring a reproducible method. Conversely, a recognized formal combination of operations may be perfectly computable by the simulator and not yet conceivable or practicable by the actor. Distinguish *simulator combinability, actor-discoverable possibility, attempted combination, actual success, and reproducible acquired ability*.

Leontiev's action–goal–motive transformations, Vygotsky's lived-through person/environment relation, Luria's developing interfunctional relations, Sève's capacity formation/use, Lahire's contextual disposition/analogy, and Dreier/Holzkamp's whole-life practical constraint each illuminate different aspects. None identifies a unique scientific probability law over all future psychological histories.

## XII. Ordinary life is the medium, not the space between important scenes

A few dramatic events cannot constitute a whole person. **Three to five expensive high-resolution events must be embedded in represented dense everyday life**—feeding, sleeping, care, transport, chores, work, boredom, school, illness, rest, community participation, repetitive exclusion, ordinary tenderness and the use of available time.

Sève's attention to lived time, Dreier's conduct of life and life course, Elder's historical timing, and social-reproduction scholarship all clarify why a day-to-day allocation of labor and attention can gradually reorganize a person's abilities, dependencies and investments, even without an exemplary psychological revelation.

Maintain separate historical granularities: performed individual event; admitted recurring practice or coverage interval; speculative compressed parental prehistory; retained sensory/causal trace; autobiographical memory; present interpretation; actor-access restriction; unresolved historical absence. A coarse routine does not entitle the narrator to invent an exact unperformed conversation. Increasing render detail later cannot violate admitted historical parentage.

The product consequence is severe: **the focal Other exists and has a life before user contact**. The world proceeds through other people and institutions beyond user attention. ## XVI. What remains scientifically and computationally open

The recast is stronger because it reduces some apparent independent mysteries without pretending their empirical solutions exist.

**1. Specific genesis without arbitrary LLM authoring.** Which actual experiences become the basis for a particular acquired relationship, motive, practice or defensive organization? Distinguish objective occurrence, age/organism access, implicated activities, available signs, historical contrasts and candidate alternatives. The LLM can build a plausible fictional constitution from these, but no source identifies a universal unique human outcome.

**2. Nonconscious relation activation without an omniscient hidden agent.** What structures make a historical configuration contextually salient, possibly before conscious interpretation? How can a sufficiently bounded current computation recruit suppressed/distant associations and process their indirect effects without global graph traversal or a prescriptive universal psychodynamic score?

**3. Conflict as relation-to-relation dynamics.** Under what historically particular conditions does contradiction reproduce itself, become compartmentalized, acquire defensive strategies, find an alternative practice, cause rupture, or change the meaning of one constituent? A learned relation cannot merely be an unused descriptive edge.

**4. Complex affect with honestly provisional semantics.** How to make liking, wanting, shame-about-pleasure, grief-containing-joy, devotion-through-cruelty and other compound configurations operative without inventing calibrated physics or assigning psychoanalytic diagnoses?

**5. Material/ideological transmission without determining minds by labels.** What does the institution actually do; how is the performance recognized; what historic form does each follower possess; how is the event lived and reproduced? Never infer “authoritarian believer” solely from sect or congregation.

**6. Deep historical compression and temporal persistence.** Which events, recurrent practices, negative effects and relation patterns must remain available for future recruitment? What may be summarized without inventing microhistory or deleting consequential variation?

**7. Full source-conditioned parent and prospective child with one running psychology.** Establish functioning, stable initial adult correspondence without hidden answer-key override and open childhood formation without predetermined end. The current R3 research-grade implementation is a partial working organism, not the final case.

**8. Specific owner/consumer wiring and all original SOROK seams.** The exact next programming return must map existing accepted CAN/ADP/PFF/P2–P6 owners, their current heads/authority, first readers, permitted writer paths and gaps; build integrated software, not an isolated demonstration or a competing theory engine. This present document does not code those changes.

**9. Empirical and product adequacy.** A simulation can be formally rich yet shallow, stereotyped, overexplained or universally agreeable. Human and scientific evaluation of the *operating organism* remains necessary; it should not become a permanent prerequisite for implementing a provisional whole.

**10. Literal source fidelity.** This source estate is extensive but not a verified verbatim full export of every three-agent chat; the archived Astra Constitutive Person excerpt is partial. Do not describe this distillation as absolute transcript losslessness.

**SOROK's Constitutive Organism is a materially living, historically developing person whose psychological organization is constituted through relations among embodied need, concrete activity, acquired capabilities, affective investments, social categories, institutions, Other-persons, memory, interpretation, practical limitation and experience.
