## 0. The integrated proposition

**A Rawlsing person is a temporally continuous, embodied, historically acquired and relationally organized *iceberg* whose contents and connections, including inaccessible and contradictory ones, can be selectively *recruited* into a situated psychological event. What appears at the surface—the *tip*—is a contingent, incomplete, observer-relative effect of that recruitment, not a public inventory of the person. The person acts, refrains, experiences, endures and is affected in an objectively continuing world with independently existing Others; the consequences can reproduce, inhibit, differentiate, connect, sublime, sublate, rupture, recontextualize, reorganize or leave unchanged the iceberg *and the pathways through which its contents are recruited next*.**

The organism exists before its concepts; historical categories, desires, practices and self-interpretations are acquired. Production does **not** signify happiness, political virtue, autonomy, progress, utility or positive transformation. Grief, dependence, defensive repetition, stagnation, coercion and alienation are also historically productive. A particular event may legitimately cause no durable psychological update.

Two entrances lead to the **same continuing psychological machinery**: independently sampled, source-qualified, whole-survey-correspondent fictional parents constructed through LLM-assisted materialist psychological abduction and compressed backfill; and Nadia, whose own psyche emerges prospectively from a need-bearing infant through actual coactivity and a materially constrained life, with no preassigned adult destination.

**Build the interconnected runtime now.** Scientific calibration and adversarial examination remain essential but are subordinate to constructing software sufficiently integrated for them to mean anything. A broad, provisional machine is the current unit of progress, not another immaculate isolated experiment.

## 2. What the computational person must *be*, at any instant

The question “Where is Nadia?” must yield a reconstructible computational answer, not a flattering biography, list of survey responses, memory transcript or LLM self-description.

**The enduring constitution includes:** a root/branch/time/actor identity; an actual organism; needs and ongoing embodied conditions; learned practices and practical competence; desires, strivings and unfinished projects; linked mediators/tensions and inhibitions; causal-trace and event-referential memory with accessibility differences; relationships as objectively realized **and** separately actor-modeled; self-understanding and misinterpretation; cultural/linguistic distinctions; identifications, alienations and contradictory commitments; partial or nonconscious affective investments; material object meanings; and optional slow motifs. Historical source and current accessibility cannot be conflated. Missing model data is not proof of psychological absence.

**The person is also a subject of activity:** labor, care, play, work, refusal, practical exploration and other initiative can affect the world without requiring a fresh user prompt. Some ability remains unexpressed. Some desire lacks an actionable means. Some act is performed under compulsion. The person's power to conceive an alternative, capability to attempt it, permission, material feasibility, ability to recruit collective participants, and actual institutional consequence are different levels.

**The iceberg is not one scalar “personality.”** A person can have mutually inconsistent systems at family, work, religion, politics, friendship or intimacy; can reassemble familiar organization rigidly or compose it differently; can use the same practice for attachment, defense, self-recognition or care; can have similar surface reports to someone with unlike causation. Strong differences may be qualitative topology, not scores.

**Why no monolithic new BackboneState?** Legacy V3's enduring, time-indexed, multi-timescale causal object was architecturally valuable. But the contemporary CAN/ADP/PFF state owners already own separate truths. Recover *the ability to inspect and operate one person* through a time-indexed **read-only constitutional projection** and shared functional semantics. Do not create a parallel canonical psyche store or silently reintroduce fixed adult traits.

## 3. The iceberg, recruited composition, and the surface tip

### Deep iceberg

Contains both what is currently usable and what is dormant, inhibited, unavailable, nondeclarative, contradicted, inaccessible to introspection or merely not presently relevant. The narrator must not equate a character's self-explanation with its full causation. Memory trace, actor belief, actual event, public report and observer attribution belong to distinct truths.

### Recruitment

An encounter can make one or more historically formed currents consequential: a bodily need, familiar cue, practical expectation, old injury, care, obligation, learned norm, desire, institutional category or competing identification. A current can inhibit another, make a different interpretation salient, prevent action, recruit a practiced means or appear only as nonverbal tension. **Recruitment can occur before conscious appraisal**: historical sensitivities bias attention and an unfamiliar sensory difference may surprise before any available conceptual name. Do not model the person as receiving a complete neutral scene and only then “interpreting.”

Historical recruitment paths may evolve. The same singing practice acquired through Mother can later be recruited by a rhythmic cue, by a peer's need, by an institution's prohibition, or by Nadia's own new project. Its formation through Mother remains true even if Mother is absent or disapproves. Its ability to be invoked across those settings requires some historically grounded extension of practical applicability; the current exact counterpart/project lookup cannot provide it generally.

### Tip and viewer

The tip is the contingent outer consequence: speech, a smile, silence, withdrawal, action, a gesture, an interrupted intention, work, inhibition or even an ordinary “fine.” It may contain sophisticated intentional deception or no conscious explanation. The same utterance can be understood differently by employer, mother, friend, Nadia, renderer, and user. Neither actor nor user receives the system's privileged whole psychological readout. Actor-owned disclosure is a genuine practice, including partial, inaccurate and withheld disclosure.

### The cost architecture

Recruitment also answers why a large iceberg need not be LLM-fed in full on every step. The system holds an addressable historical relational store and pulls a bounded, contextually meaningful slice. It must allow occasional distant, contradictory and nonconscious recruitments, not only most recent high-frequency matches. The computational budget becomes an explicit choice of retrieval depth, scope, relation expansion and semantic model consultation, not a fixed “top five motives” universal psychology.

## 4. The person×event metabolism SOROK already specified

The adopted CAN-008 R2 person×event structure is **actor receivability → actor uptake/appraisal → situated recruitment → multi-stream immediate response**, followed where qualified by separate owner effects. The two-call design assigns semantic/recruitment to analytic Call A and situated phenomenal flesh/voice to Call B. The latter must not mint psychological formation merely to produce compelling prose. PFF specializes narrow relational interaction; V2 expresses rather than owns the psyche. This is existing doctrine, **not** a new ORACLE invention.

The joined circuit is:

```text
Actual evolving world + actor's organism and historical constitution
                 ↓
Admitted event / continuing condition / self-initiated activity
                 ↓
Actor-specific receivability, sensory reach and bodily capacity
                 ↕
Uptake, preconceptual impression, affective appraisal and attention
                 ↓
Recruit deeply constituted needs, traces, practices, relations,
competing commitments, projects, learned interpretive means
                 ↓
Concurrent psychic currents and situated possible activity
                 ↓
Action, refusal, inhibited intention, routine continuation or quiet
                 ↓
Independent Other / institution / material-world resolution
                 ↓
Actual consequences != actor-specific experience != outward tip
                 ↓
LLM-assisted and/or rule-based qualified afterlife proposals
                 ↓
CAN/ADP/PFF owner-specific durable effects, or explicit no-change
                 ↓
The same continuing iceberg with possibly changed recruitment paths
```

This is **not** a forced one-way cognitive sequence. Relevance can be organized before conscious interpretation; an infant's response may be a sound or a shift of attention; an unseen employer decision can change food security before anyone identifies its origin; several people and background processes run while the focal actor is quiet. The objective world is never made out of the character's representations of it.

**Outer social-field recruitment is distinct from inner iceberg recruitment.** The former is how conditional/source-qualified possible persons, practices and institutions enter an actual world and an actor's feasible contacts; the latter is what a resulting encounter activates *within the already constituted actor*. The existence of dissent in a source-qualified population does not mean Nadia has met or understood it.

## 5. Constituting *new kinds* of psychological possibility

The question “What did Nadia learn?” is not always a question about adjusting a numeric attribute. She may acquire a novel distinction between her own contribution and another person's; a practical ability to enter a rhythm; a nonverbal anticipation of response; a learned way to detect exclusion; a role within a group; a new project; an inaccurate model of authority; a changed relation between care and obedience; or an acquired way to regulate her own withdrawal.

A bounded runtime can implement generic transformations such as **form, connect, differentiate, inhibit, weaken/strengthen, recontextualize, extend recruitment scope, reactivate, reorganize, persist/no-change**. These are **candidate programming verbs**, not newly issued CAN-011 universal psychological-state types. The acquired contents and the links between them are variable and source/experience conditioned. No born political ideology, religiosity, abstract quest for autonomy, honor-culture wound or obligatory Enneagram basin.

The strongest opening to creativity is the **composition of previously acquired means under a new project**. A person who can organize peers, repair an object and obtain room access may conceive a workshop without a prewritten `organize_workshop` action. But the actual means, actor knowledge, social permissions, available Others and material feasibility remain separate. Typed composition is not arbitrary generated Python and not proof that the action succeeded.

An emerging self-model is itself historically acquired, fallible and possibly in conflict with nonreflective patterns. Being able to say “I withdraw when criticized” does not imply ability to stop; false self-explanations can themselves organize action. Neither auto-harmony nor compulsory alienation-to-liberation is acceptable.

**Underdetermination remains:** an experience may be compatible with several learned consequences. The LLM's proposal can be a *good fictional construction* without being a scientifically identified human developmental law. The task now is to make credible provisional results operative, not to refuse construction because identical experiences would permit other possible histories.

## 6. LLM roles: genuine psychological construction, not wallpaper

The principal's correction supersedes prior blanket bans against the LLM directly proposing content for a fictional adult iceberg. It is precisely the relevant semantic machinery for this project.

**Four distinguishable jobs:**

**Adult abduction:** Given the **whole sampled joint pseudo-respondent**, its conditional population distribution, denominators, historical location and material habitus, hypothesize a coherent-but-contradictory adult whose underlying organization might produce those survey answers. Invent plausible private psychological details **as openly fictional constitution**, with appropriate historical back-narration, not as empirical discovery of the source person's subconscious.

**Developmental semantic formation:** Given an actor's actual experience, organismic capabilities, existing iceberg and historically available meanings, propose what might be impressed, anticipated, differentiated, acquired, inhibited or reconnected. The model may reason using adult scientific concepts **without giving those concepts to the infant**.

**Situated response proposals:** Analyze recruited currents, competing needs, social conditions, feasible means and possible conduct. Proposal ≠ performed world event. Output can be uncertainty, refusal, inaction or incompatible currents.

**Phenomenal realization:** Render the legally actor/viewer-accessible tip, not a privileged full diagnostic explanation. Dialog and scenes do not become causal owners.

**Core separation:** system explanation ≠ actor's accessible experience ≠ admitted actor-specific psychological organization ≠ conscious self-explanation ≠ tip ≠ objective fact. Prompting can propose the content; typed owner effects must make it persist and influence later recruitment. Model's contextual priors can contain colonial, managerial, liberal, culturally exoticizing or reactionary assumptions. They require explicit source/fictional grades and must not overwrite empirical PYAT observation or silently dictate the moral path of the child. Still: **build first with honest provisional semantics, calibrate later.**

## 7. Parents and child: deliberately unequal ways to enter the same person

**Mother:** PYAT yields a source-qualified **whole respondent-coherent** sampled observational configuration. Give the LLM that jagged answer pattern **beside meaningful conditional variation, denominator/support and historical/material positioning**, not just a list of “personal traits.” In one country sect/SES/locality may matter; in another different variables matter. Population association doesn't uniquely determine individual psychology and nearest-cell samples can be sparse. Source wording, instrument routing, applicability, missingness and cross-cultural interpretation must be carried. A psychologically rich fictional adult is abductively constituted, with plausible material history filled in and a few compressed forward passes if useful; **do not require exhaustive forward-simulation from infancy or a Doob h-transform that finds a childhood matching target Q**. A first instrument read from functioning adult state must produce the sampled answers **without hidden cached-answer override**. The source target does not prohibit later psychological change.

**Father:** Separately whole-sampled and independently constituted. Condition plausible meeting, attraction, conflict, partnership, household, historical context and relative opportunities on actual independently existing maternal and paternal lives. Do not force complementary attitudes or invent couple prevalence unsupported by survey data. Father must possess independent work, needs, relationships and private models; mother and father can misunderstand each other. Their mutual history is an objective relation and cannot be made retroactively by an after-the-fact partner choice.

**Nadia:** Starts as biologically immature, hungry, physically dependent, sensing and feeling, capable of response and exploration but not equipped with mature adult political/moral vocabulary or destination questionnaire. Actual care, touch, response/nonresponse, bodily discomfort, play, kin, household work, social reproduction, peers, institutions, language and historical power generate an open-ended practical/psychic organization. The organism matures while culture provides ways to name and value its real experiences. A wall of indifferent or hostile objective reality survives every interpretation of it. Nadia may acquire tools from family/institution through which she later contests them; neither rebellion nor capitulation is predetermined.

**Same ongoing psychology:** the parental adult initial constitution and child-acquired forms must enter the same existing CAN/ADP/PFF current recruitment and afterlife operators with **different acquisition provenance and resource budgets**. Do not give parents Nadia's expensive full childhood or Nadia the parents' privilege to backfill adult motives. Neither parent's current survey target is a permanent answer key.

## 8. Shared fields, power, love, recognition, mystification, authority

The infant–caregiver relation is not two subjects fused into one mind. Their coordination can constitute a recurring **objective practical organization** larger than either starting capability. Both change, but differently. Keep (i) actual world-owned relationship and events; (ii) each person's psychological relation to the other; and (iii) shared practical activities or institutions separately existent. Unreciprocated love, absence, coercion and unknown power can still constitute a person without cooperative symmetry.

**Separate in the singing example:** historical source of capacity; existing power to exercise it; necessary collaborators/material venue; attachment to Mother's delight; Mother's actual disciplinary leverage; Nadia's newly formed ends; whether she can imagine or attempt a different arrangement. Independence may remove one practical dependency while leaving gratitude, rage, longing and material vulnerability intact. The history must not be erased to claim freedom; the originating mediator must not permanently gate recruitment.

**Mystification through reliable experience:** If everybody waits for Father's approval, the child may accurately predict resource access through Father's gesture while having insufficient evidence about the labor/cooperation and institutional arrangement sustaining it. Repetition can strengthen predictive accuracy and obscure causal differentiation at the same time. Later the good initially learned through Father—another person's participation—may become the ground for refusing his current act. This is a possible immanent critique, **not** an obligatory revolutionary or moral destination.

**Love and recognition:** A person can love someone they resent, desire approval yet refuse obedience, recognize someone hostilely, give material care without understanding another's experience, feel abandoned despite being cared for, or support someone's becoming while trying to control what they become. Recognition is not automatically praise, and care is not a single attachment score. Differentiated religious/class/kin/gender/national identifications may become unnoticed lenses or consciously contestable objects, often simultaneously.

**Absolute Horizon:** concerns historically situated ultimate/limiting claims, what one protects or sacrifices for what, what one can bear or impose. It is neither a fixed religion variable, nor the inevitable expression of immanent criticism, nor a universal moral-health scale. Repression, domination, ordinary adherence, contradictory fidelity and no such emergent structure are possible.

**Power in the world:** employment, property, wages, school, household authority, colonial/imperial relations, state, institutions, coercion and social reproduction are concrete material operations, not atmospheric labels on the actor's psyche. The historically superseded **mandatory nine-strata ontology** must not be reinstalled. The prior nine strata survive as functional ancestry; actual relations are historically variable and source-qualified, as later PYAT/FIELD-FORGE and ORACLE/01 §0D specify.

**Consent and transgression:** The R0 kernel was *reported* to accept caller-supplied `partner_consent=True` despite a helper refusal; this particular synthesis did not rerun that private kernel. Independently issued voluntary joint participation must be protected in any reimplementation. But everyday overstepping, pestering, interruption, bullying, institutional sanctions and coercion can be **unilaterally acted** and affect another without consent. The victim's independent action and psychic uptake cannot be forged by the initiator; objective result and lived meanings remain distinct.

## 9. Ordinary life, material objects, multiple timescales and independent product reality

**Sparse selected narrative events are not sparse life.** The settled compressed regime uses expensive high-resolution events where their actual material/psychological detail matters, and cheaper recurring care, work, feeding, sleep, play, transport, schooling, social presence, friction, illness, rest, labor and household routines between them. A history-coverage capsule may retain generated opportunities, observed sequences, denominators, body/coactor context and unknown regions; it must not fabricate exact unperformed conversations or third-party deeds. Three to five selected expensive event beats do not imply only three to five events of existence. Fast bodily/psychic response and slower recurring motifs/relational development have different timescales.

**Objects are material and relational:** an artifact has real provenance, labor history, ownership and condition; different persons may invest it with gift, memory, prestige, shame, religious association or practical use. Actual object history is neither a subject's current memory nor what a user is entitled to know. Materialization deepens independent world detail; disclosure remains actor-owned; gaze and recognition are viewer-specific.

**The focal person exists before the user:** independent pre-contact canon, work, care, projects, bodily needs, clocks, relationships and ongoing Others. Source/seed branches can be copied into independent user continuities, after which event histories diverge irreversibly; no user can rewrite a prior private life by asking the model to narrate it differently. User curiosity can make lawful details visible without authorizing involuntary disclosure. Encounters can involve intimacy, refusal, indifference, attachment, conflict or absence; no retention/engagement optimizer should turn the Other into a service of the user. The person is not a diagnostic panel, and the tip is not an iceberg export.

## 11. Software design decisions made by this synthesis (provisional, not adopted native APIs)

**Do not create a new canonical `ConstitutivePerson` database or universal psychological writer.** Use a **time-indexed `PersonConstitution` projection** over competent stores, with actor/root/branch/time/cause references, explicit known/unknown, and the dormant/competing/accessible functional relations needed for prospective activity.

**Build a general `recruit(person, accessible_event, budget)` service** with direct relevance, acquired association, contextual/practical similarity, relational and institutional connection, conflicting currents, nondeclarative trace, inhibition and bounded deep expansion. Retrieval and compression must honor *actor-specific history*, not universal moral preference scores. It should issue qualified present psychodynamic composition, not durable psychic facts simply by reading them.

**Provide an LLM semantic-construction interface** for (a) adult inverse fictional constitution, (b) developmental impressions/new forms and recruited meanings, (c) situated activity possibilities, and (d) phenomenological tip. Keep model analysis, what actor can know, the proposed form's executable effect, and its owner/provenance separate. No model-generated arbitrary Python runs, and no model commandeering world truth.

**Provide owner-directed `effects` operations**, capable provisionally of forming, relating, differentiating, inhibiting, recontextualizing and changing the recruitment scope of actor-specific functional organization. These are implementation verbs, *not new canonical state enums*. Routing must write only to the competent owner and preserve the sourcefree/fictive grade.

**Implement an organism process and an ordinary-life process** that can change current bodily capacity, recurring opportunity and developmental experience without fabricating unsimulated microhistory or a complete biological theory.

**Implement an actual activity/world bridge**: attempted actions are produced from situated actors, other voluntary contributions are independently authored, unilateral imposed effects remain possible, world materially resolves outcomes and actor-specific access projects the result. A person can fail materially even when psychologically ready.

**Join two entrances to one continuing runtime:** parents populated from PYAT targets and LLM-abductive initial iceberg, child grown through prospective organismic historical life; both consumed by the same person×event machinery. Independent survey reader verifies parent initialization but does not set lifelong behavior.

### Conceptual orchestration, deliberately not a claim of executable imported API

```python
# Adapter names are proposed. Actual receiving/owner APIs must be inspected.
def continue_person(actor_id, t, services):
    person = services.constitution.read_asof(actor_id, t)
    world = services.world.read_asof(t)
    available = services.access.project(person, world)
    situated = services.recruitment.compose(person, available)
    semantic = services.llm.propose_situated(person, available, situated)
    attempt = services.activity.form_attempt(person, situated, semantic)
    actual = services.world.resolve(attempt)  # independently acting Others
    lived = services.experience.project(actor_id, actual)
    candidate = services.llm.propose_afterlife(person, situated, actual, lived)
    services.effects.route_competent_owners(actor_id, actual, candidate)
    return services.expression.actor_view(actor_id, actual, lived)
```

This abstraction must ultimately support multiple actors, recurring processes, endogenous initiative and bodily time—not be fixed as a single adult deliberative response to each user turn.

**Implementation shape:** a substantial sourcefree integration package could contain a constitution read model, recruitment/composition interpreter, semantic LLM adapter, developmental effects compiler, organismic and recurring-life operations, material/relational bridges, parent initializer, child-ontogenesis process and continuous encounter runner. Reuse actual code where its function is competent. On protected boundaries implement integration-ready adapters and declare the receiving contract, not a second source of native truth.

## 12. The decisive building order is about joined organs, not a sequence of tiny proofs

**The principal has explicitly rejected the repeated research-first cycle** in which every new psychological operation becomes a miniature experimental court and another requirement to wait for calibrated science. Do not convert the following architectural order into ten isolated committees or demos.
