## The reciprocal theoretical construction: relation-mediated differentiation

**A. A whole act can belong to several activities.** Leontiev distinguishes activity/object/motive from goal-directed action and conditional operation; technical success may contradict personal sense (the bitter-candy example). A single singing action can succeed as technical reproduction while failing as mutual inclusion or recognition. **The relevant affective conflict is not a sum of independent preference weights; it can be a conflict among the actor's actually implicated activities and their anticipated/realized consequences.**

**B. The psychologically formative unit is lived-through participation.** Vygotsky's *perezhivanie* illuminates PRE-A0's objective occurrence ≠ actor-lived realization ≠ psychic afterlife. A material encounter does not directly write an abstract meaning. A preverbal infant may retain sensory contingency, arousal regulation, motor anticipation or a nondeclarative trace without possessing the analyst's interpretation of oppression, affection or solidarity.

**C. Formation changes conditions of recruitment.** Luria/Vygotsky's culturally mediated reorganization of functions and Lahire's conditional dispositions/practical analogy illuminate how prior experience changes attention, cue salience, cross-context prospects and inhibitory patterns. A new situation can be *tentatively comparable* before any guaranteed transfer. A distorted resemblance can recruit an old defense inappropriately. Strict historical scope must not become a ban on first-time exploratory transfer.

**D. Activity has a life-course budget and material conditions.** Sève's capacity-formation/exercise distinction, Dreier's conduct of everyday life and Holzkamp's actor-premises under restrictive action conditions explain why practice, absence of opportunity, coercion, care, labor and blocked time matter. A capacity may grow without permitted exercise; a defensive practice may repeatedly reproduce domination; a technically failed act can still change need, expectation or allegiance.

**E. The object is not simply a reward.** An effect can first be encountered unintentionally, then anticipated, then sought in a new situation, then become an activity's object, and only eventually an enduring motive. These are differentiable historical transformations, not a mandatory sequence or universal developmental timetable. Acquired ways of composing old powers can themselves become new practical resources; no infant is born with a mature reflective central organizer.

**F. Material negativity is productive without redemption.** Repression, loss, frustration, material defeat, learned submission, failed recognition, jealousy, grief, shame, coercion and unfulfilled love may generate future expectation or inhibition without morally positive development. Equally, sometimes the enduring state remains unchanged while objective history changes.

## A concrete theoretical possibility opened by R3

The first native psychological genesis operation may need **not** be \`ADD_PROJECT\`, \`ACQUIRE_COMPOSITION_METHOD\` or even \`ADD_FORM\`. It may be a **qualified actor-specific change in discriminability and expectation** grounded in *experienced difference*:

- This body experienced a particular interruption, timing, tension, contrast or contingent coactor response, under its existing capacities; the observer's adult explanation remains separate.
- A retained, still partial impression changes the chance that a later feature is noticed, that a response is anticipated, or that a familiar operation is provisionally attempted.
- Several different continuation candidates remain possible. A consequence of an activity can become an anticipated end without instantly becoming the motive for a continuing project.
- Through actual future consequences, the actor's pathways of practical analogy, attachment, inhibition, means, and self-description may differentiate or reorganize.

This is a **provisional design family to explore**, not an enacted rule, scientific necessity, new canonical owner or mandate to use a specific learning algorithm. Its significance: avoid demanding adult semantic maturity in the infant and avoid silently awarding a mature historical explanation as actor-owned concept.
