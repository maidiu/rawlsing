# Genealogy and source court for *Who gets the sleeve back?*

This is an author's judgment on a bounded transformation. Its object is the relation between becoming able to do something, learning whose answer counts, and acquiring the capacity to live with that answer. The three can develop together, apart, or at one another's expense. The manuscript is the positive account. The entries below expose the separate historical claims on which that argument could founder. A retained distinction may have its fullest treatment in the cited inheritance.

**How to read the decisions.** *Conserve* retains a distinction whose independent variation matters. *Transform* identifies the historical operation through which the earlier distinction acquires a different meaning. *Fuse through actual contradiction* requires a practice whose successful performance also produces an opposed consequence and changes subsequent activity. *Unresolved* identifies the missing discrimination and what would decide it. *Relocate* identifies where evidence or authority belongs and the positive constraint that survives the move. These judgments concern specific claims, not entire authors. The Rawlsing theoretical documents are evidence for their authors' positions. The derivations here are original proposals and the contrasting Nadia histories are stipulated fiction. Empirical and documentary exceptions are identified explicitly.

## How need, competence and an object of concern come apart

**D01 · Conserve · Hunger, relief, benefactor, recognition.** Infant hunger varies before a food is encountered. Actual provision, sensory receipt, familiar anticipation and credit to the provider each require different events. A child fed by one adult with food bought by another can anticipate the wrong hands and still flourish bodily. A delay can hurt without being remembered. A regular orientation toward a face could be familiarity alone. This is the first boundary on a theory that treats every received effect as knowledge of its cause. ## Where the same work satisfies and defeats a purpose

**D13 · Fuse through actual contradiction · Hearing that closes the case.** The object of the Friday fitting was an alterable garment. Under a rule paying for documented completion, eliciting an accurate defect report now also completes a condition for releasing that defective item. Accuracy can improve the institution's evidence of responsiveness and make another hearing harder to obtain. Nadia's prior method and the shop's legitimate hearing both acquire a new function inside the same signed act. The opposed ends are identifiable in two subsequent material facts, an order collected and a collar still pressing. An automatic signature before anyone is heard would reproduce the injury without this particular contradiction. The worker's pain and the shop's decision retain separate histories. ## How an act can be felt, misattributed, resisted and later forgotten

**D21 · Transform · Several concurrent histories specify different features of one act.** Pain limits force in Nadia's hand. The bus imposes a deadline. Anticipated blame alters her timing. Consideration for a tired wearer changes the method of offering a chair. A person may decide, delay, change method, change their mind mid-act, or do nothing. An action label such as HELP discards most of the variation. Selective removal of the bus changes the time pressure without healing the hand. One compelling current can dominate some encounters. None of this requires equal contributions, one winning motive or a permanently fragmented psyche. ## What changed in the comparison with R17

R16 already says that the same care can develop a skill, reproduce a division of labor and help constitute the generosity with which someone may later contest that division. Its discussion of operative possibility already links acquired capacities with independently controlled access. R17 explicitly describes a circuit with several independent participants and asymmetrical consequences. A general claim that care or agency is distributed would add little to either. What the night repair contributes is a **discriminating change of conditions**. Improved performance can actively sustain mistaken attribution. Vary *who may declare the repair complete* while preserving the accuracy of the report. R17's strongest revisions flow in the other direction. It compelled a distinction between the old pressure successfully suppressed and a person's new capacity to hear criticism without being obliged to answer it immediately. Its account of recuperation made it necessary to let the shop repair the actual coats and thereby give somebody *good reason* to renew loyalty. ### What would defeat the argument?

1. **Acquisition.** Establish in the fictional or observed history that Nadia could already make the relevant unfamiliar discriminations alone, and that who experienced the correction changes only her vocabulary. The Fridays then reveal competence or change attribution. They do not generate the proposed investigative method. Preserve the separate possibility that joint performance improves.
2. **A change of object.** Make the inquiry cease whenever praise, pay or the trained prompt disappears. That favors a learned operation in an existing activity. An independent interest in how other people use objects requires evidence of renewed initiation in circumstances in which other ends were available.
3. **Effective institutional settlement.** Show that signed cases remain as practically reopenable as unsigned ones for workers with the same resources and shifts. This defeats D13's particular mechanism even if the buyer saves money or workers distrust management. Show that dispatch cannot withhold an order and D41 loses its proposed collective power.
4. **Private release.** If Nadia remains calm by avoiding the wearer and cannot resume contact on reasonable terms, the stronger noncompulsion interpretation loses ground. Access, opportunity, repeated exposure, suppression, substitute employment and genuine loss of interest need independent consideration. No single behavior establishes unconscious defense or release.
5. **The scope of unity.** Demonstrate a lasting new perceptual organization arising from solitary play without anyone returning a correction. The beat supplies precisely such a possible counterhistory. It has already defeated return-to-self as a universal center. To challenge the remaining wider claim, identify a particular transformation whose onset, means and persistence cannot be explained by the actor's embodied development, independent worldly changes or histories of practice even after the relevant conditions are specified. Merely invoking “continuing material life” in such a case would convey no explanation. For example, if a change appears under matched histories of access and practice but systematically follows a change in sensory function, biological development requires its own specific account.
6. **Positive limits.** Even perfect observation of the Friday work could leave Nadia's private pleasure underdetermined. A comparison that withdraws Mother can change distress and available time at once. A stronger discrimination changes access to the correction with familiar help, comparable time and comparable material demands. None of these comparisons was conducted on human participants.
