# URGENT PROGRAMMING MANDATE — BUILD THE ORGANISM

## RAWLSING / SOROK — FROM ARCHITECTURAL CONSTITUTION TO MATERIAL SOFTWARE CONSTRUCTION

**Principal's governing correction — November 19, 2026**

The preceding architectural synthesis is retained.

Its commitments to the organism, iceberg, situated recruitment, psychological tip, independently operating world, historical events, material reproduction, relational constitution, developmental recursion, LLM semantic intelligence, and the PYAT–SOROK seam remain governing.

**But the engineering mandate is hereby corrected.**

We are no longer prioritizing isolated experiments, smallest discriminating tests, sequential scientific issuance, or narrowly scoped demonstrations of one psychological transition.

**WE ARE BUILDING THE ORGANISM.**

We have spent substantial effort recovering, reconciling and articulating the architecture. The immediate task is to turn that inheritance into a substantial, coherent, evolving software implementation.

This is an **analytical forward-construction commission**.

Scientific calibration, falsification, fidelity evaluation and empirical adequacy testing remain important. They do **not** govern the order in which we are permitted to write the organism.

## I. Stop confusing scientific uncertainty with engineering prohibition

Where an existing function has authoritative or adequately specified semantics, implement it.

Where its semantics are partly specified, reconcile the existing resources, make a defensible provisional design decision, document the assumption and implement it.

Where the developmental science remains uncertain, use a credible provisional computational approximation consistent with established Rawlsing commitments.

Where an LLM is the appropriate generative-semantic resource, use it.

Where a richer mechanism is possible but expensive, implement a simpler architecture that can develop into it without abandoning the necessary distinctions.

**Do not convert every uncertain psychological mechanism into a research HOLD that prevents building.**

A provisional mechanism is allowed to operate inside the fictional simulation while its scientific authority remains explicitly provisional.

Its fictional causal consequences can be real within Rawlsing without being represented as a validated causal explanation of actual human development.

This is not permission to fabricate survey facts, source authority or other actors' actions.

It is permission to **engineer a simulation under explicit assumptions**.

## II. The construction target is the whole SOROK organism, not a miniature demonstration

The implementation must be designed as an integrated architecture of a functioning, developing individual.

We need the machinery for:

1. **The embodied organism:** needs, physiological regulation, bodily capacities, sensory and motor development, discomfort, pain, fatigue, maturation, vulnerability and dependence.

2. **The enduring psychological iceberg:** an actor-specific, historically developed and internally related organization of needs, desires, practices, competencies, expectations, memories, nondeclarative traces, investments, conflicts, identifications, alienations, motivations and possible slow attractors.

3. **Situated recruitment:** a mechanism by which actual circumstances recruit relevant portions of the iceberg into present psychological operation, including dormant, contradictory, cross-context and incompletely conscious organization.

4. **The psychological tip:** situated experience, outward conduct, speech, silence, inhibition, disclosure, refusal, action and expression that emerge from recruited organization without revealing the entire person.

5. **Psychic reconstitution:** actual experience and activity modifying the contents, connections, applicability and future recruitment pathways of the iceberg.

6. **Developmental becoming:** progressive organismic, sensorimotor, relational, practical, linguistic, conceptual, social and reflective development without installing a universal adult psychology at birth.

7. **The independent world and Others:** material resources, class and institutional power, historically situated activities, relationships, coercion, ordinary social transgression, cooperative activity and other people's independent causal lives.

8. **Continuous ordinary life:** sparse-event hypercompression, recurring processes, routine labor and care, background-world continuity, fast/slow developmental change and a functioning clock.

9. **Historical person generation:** LLM-assisted, PYAT-conditioned, survey-correspondent parental icebergs and the distinct open-ended developmental generation of the focal child.

10. **Live situated existence:** the continuing generated person moving within a material environment, engaging other people, communicating, acting independently and remaining partly opaque to the user.

These are not ten new engines or ten separate research commissions.

They are **essential, mutually related organs of one system that must now be expressed in software**.

Do not allow the most convenient component to replace the whole construction target.

## III. Design the integrated software architecture and write it

Take the complete inherited architecture as input.

Determine the code organization, state representations, runtime operations, interfaces, ownership boundaries, dependency relationships and principal execution cycles required to make the organism function.

Then **implement them**.

Reuse existing P2/P3/P4/P5/P6, CAN/ADP, PFF, SDTF, historical V3 and SOROK machinery wherever competent.

Where existing code implements only a narrow version of the required capability, extend or supplement it lawfully.

Where existing code is unwired, connect it.

Where a competent consumer exists but no positive producer exists, write the producer.

Where the intended model requires a new computational intermediary, design and write it.

Where implementation requires a shared interface, define it and implement both sides as far as authorized.

Where the available branch lacks authority to modify protected components, build substantial integration-ready code in the authorized research branch with explicit receiving contracts. Do not represent that as production integration, but do not let the restriction become an excuse for writing another architectural essay.

**Use existing SOROK owner law to organize construction, not to indefinitely postpone it.**

The architecture should be ambitious enough to accommodate the full person, while its initial implementation may contain provisional rules, incomplete depth and imperfect performance.

We do not require a complete scientific theory of human psychological formation before implementing a functioning approximation of psychological formation.

## IV. Make the existing iceberg–recruitment–tip machinery materially real

This is the center of the implementation.

The software must possess an enduring actor-specific psychological constitution.

It must be able to recruit from that constitution in response to actual circumstances.

Recruited psychological organization must participate in generating situated activity and expression.

Events and their consequences must be capable of modifying the enduring organization.

Those modifications must become available to subsequent recruitment.

**The whole circuit must exist in code.**

Do not stop after proving that one stored expectation can affect one selected action.

Do not create a disconnected personality description that a general-purpose LLM merely consults as flavor text.

Do not replace the iceberg with a short current-state prompt.

Build the deep state, the recruitment process, the continuing activity, and the operations connecting them.

It is acceptable that early semantic recruitment uses intelligent LLM proposals and provisional heuristics. It is acceptable that some psychological subdomains initially have coarser modeling than others.

**Build the architecture that can become sophisticated through development.**

## V. Treat LLMs as actual construction machinery

The LLM is not merely a renderer or a last-resort source of dialogue.

Use its intelligence to construct plausible psychological organizations, interpret situated experiences, identify meaningful connections, hypothesize developmental effects, elaborate historical context and propose actor-specific practical possibilities.

The distinction is between **using the LLM as a generative component** and **allowing it to become an ungoverned source of canonical truth**.

Its outputs should populate and operate through the established computational architecture.

For parents, use it aggressively to construct a psychologically rich fictional iceberg from sampled pseudo-respondency and relevant population-relative information.

For Nadia, use it to reason about age-appropriate impressions, recruited historical organization, possible developmental acquisition and changing meaning.

Do not require a hand-written rule for every kind of psychological significance the LLM can intelligently propose.

Do not install its mature analytical terminology as Nadia's own conceptual understanding when she lacks the appropriate developmental organization.

**The system can think about the child in adult analytical terms without making the child think in adult analytical terms.**

Implement this distinction through the existing semantic generation, qualified state and lived-realization architecture.

## VI. The adult and child generation pathways must become software

**Mother and father:** begin from source-qualified sampled pseudo-respondent configurations, demographic/material conditioning, comparative variability and plausible relational circumstances.

Use intelligent abductive psychological construction to develop whole fictional adults whose internal organization makes their particular survey responses intelligible.

Backfill plausible material histories. Use compressed developmental recursion where appropriate.

The resulting parents must have operative psychological icebergs that are substantially richer than their questionnaires and initially capable of generating their particular sampled responses.

The father must have his own independent constitution and credible relationship with the mother.

**Nadia:** begin with the immature, materially dependent organism.

Develop her iceberg through actual forward historical recursion involving her body, caregivers, activities, relations, institutions and wider world.

No preassigned adult survey destination.

**Implement both entrances into compatible continuing SOROK person machinery.**

The full PYAT production weld does not have to be finished before this construction can advance. Defined provisional input contracts and representative source-qualified data can support software development while remaining explicitly distinguished from fully issued upstream outputs.

Do not let the absence of terminal upstream integration prevent construction of the receiving organism.

## VII. Build the developmental process, not merely a library of state mutations

A person does not develop simply because a program can append a `MEDIATOR` or change an expectation.

We need the functioning recursion.

Experience and activity must be capable of generating new psychological contents **and changing relationships among existing contents**.

A learned capacity may become recruited for a different purpose.

A relationship-specific expectation may become generalized, inhibited or contradicted.

An acquired practice may become independently exercisable.

An identification may become the basis of a later alienation.

A person may acquire reflective capacities through which aspects of their own organization become objects of practical activity.

The developmental engine must be capable of composing existing powers into new practical possibilities without restricting people to an exhaustive fixed list of actions or adult psychological properties.

Use provisional generative semantic operations to accomplish this.

Do not wait for every mechanism to be empirically calibrated.

Implement enough of the developmental process to begin generating lives, and improve the mechanisms as their limitations become apparent.

## VIII. Build broadly and coherently before optimizing narrowly

The preceding mandates repeatedly demanded the smallest possible vertical slice.

That is no longer the primary organizing instruction.

Small slices can help during programming, but the objective is a **substantial integrated organism**, not a sequence of tiny demonstrations that never join.

We need the principal structures, the runtime relationships among them, and an implementation trajectory that keeps the entire human being in view.

It is permissible for an early module to be incomplete.

It is permissible for a provisional algorithm to be crude.

It is permissible to build a general capability before it has been empirically validated.

It is permissible to stub a dependency temporarily while implementing the surrounding system, provided the stub is explicit and does not masquerade as completed causal functionality.

It is permissible to revisit interfaces when the developing implementation reveals a better organization.

**We are constructing an evolving machine, not issuing a scientific certificate for each line of code.**

The major engineering danger is no longer simply an inadequate test.

It is perpetual fragmentation: dozens of individually defensible artifacts that never become one continuing organism.

Build with integration as a continuous concern.

## IX. Testing is subordinate to construction

This is an explicit priority reversal.

Tests, smoke checks, type checks and replay checks are useful engineering tools. Use them to avoid avoidable breakage and to identify defects in newly written code.

But **do not organize the work around inventing another adversarial experiment before implementing the next major capability**.

Do not make empirical calibration a prerequisite for provisional fictional psychological generation.

Do not require complete scientific issuance for a sourcefree experimental developmental rule.

Do not spend the majority of the work proving that a tiny fragment functions while leaving the rest of the proposed organism unwritten.

Do not represent a passing toy test as a completed person.

But equally, **do not treat an untested or imperfect substantial implementation as worthless**.

For this phase, meaningful progress is measured primarily by the actual construction of coherent, substantive software capabilities and their connection to the whole architecture.

Scientific calibration and adversarial evaluation can follow as the organism becomes sufficiently complete to make such evaluation meaningful.

Integrity and permissions remain necessary. Validation maximalism is not.

## X. The required return is code

The next material return should contain a substantial body of implemented software.

It should show:

**What architecture was chosen and why**, in relation to existing SOROK authorities and actual code.

**What was implemented**, including the principal new modules, interfaces, state structures, computational operations and integration paths.

**What inherited software was reused, extended or connected.**

**What parts of the organism are now materially present**, even if some are incomplete or only provisional.

**What remains unwritten**, distinguishing absent software from immature algorithms, missing upstream input, scientific uncertainty and protected-owner integration.

**What architectural decisions were made** that permit the remaining pieces to be added without another conceptual restart.

**What defects or uncertainties are known**, without letting their mere existence negate the construction.

The written explanation should document the software, not substitute for it.

A return consisting mainly of a theoretical synthesis, another research agenda or a carefully isolated test kernel **does not fulfill this commission**.

The deliverable is the developing Rawlsing computational organism.

## XI. The still-open problem is now a construction problem

By the end of the work, the agent must be able to distinguish:

- What is now designed and implemented.
- What is implemented but not connected.
- What is connected but still provisional or incomplete.
- What is required by the established architecture but remains to be written.
- What genuine scientific uncertainty may later require calibration.
- What cannot currently be integrated because of real owner or source constraints.

Prioritize the missing software that most materially advances the integrated organism.

Do not prioritize whichever question is easiest to turn into another conclusive scientific experiment.

**What matters now is forward construction.**

The project has an extensive conception of the human organism, psyche, iceberg, recruitment, social and material world, developmental history and continuing personhood.

The task is to put that conception into running code.
