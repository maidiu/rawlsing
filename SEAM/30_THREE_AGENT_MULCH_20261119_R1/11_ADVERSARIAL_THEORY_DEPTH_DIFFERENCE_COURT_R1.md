## 0. The question posed and the methodological prohibition

The principal demands theory completion at **maximum causal and phenomenological resolution**. The danger in saying MULCH is treating an irreducible difference as a duplicate to be folded into an already preferred category, or treating theoretically inconvenient jaggedness as a defect to be compressed away. The correct task is **differential synthesis**: place distinct causal accounts into relation *without forcing identity, equivalence, convergence, commensurability or a single final mechanism*. A scientifically underdetermined relation can remain open; an actually antagonistic relation can remain antagonistic; a previously coexisting disposition can remain inhibited; an organism can remain ordinary, incoherent, uninterested, politically reactionary, loving and harmful, or psychologically opaque.

A claim of **integration** must answer four different questions, not choose the most rhetorically elegant: (a) what already exists independently, with what material history and owner of evidence? (b) what does a particular act or organization change in another? (c) what remains different, absent, unknown or untransformed? (d) what would the proposed common explanation incorrectly predict? The first three without the fourth are thematic arrangement, not reconciliation. A contradiction is not resolved because the theory announces that both poles are mediated.

**Do not mistake levels of argument:**
- **Ontic:** independent real world, body, Others, institutions, events and what this fictional actor actually lives in its admitted history.
- **Subjective:** particular felt, peripheral, preconscious, inhibited, interpreted, unrecognized or consciously reorganized psychological life.
- **Explanatory:** a theoretical reconstruction of possible mechanisms, where explanations may compete and where analyst knowledge exceeds actor knowledge.
- **Scientific:** which of these mechanisms has warrant from which kind of data and what remains a speculative model.
- **Computational:** whether a qualified run implements those relations, in sufficient detail and with independent consequences. A passing sourcefree fixture establishes only its bounded technical result.
These levels can change one another without becoming one representation or evidence grade.

## 2. Difference court I — a need does not arrive knowing its object

**Leontiev's direct argument (PDF pp. 20–21) is stronger than “a cue recruits a potential motive.”** The need as initially constituted does not yet disclose its concrete object; the object becomes psychologically efficacious through actual activity and its encounter with object-specific satisfaction. The need is itself historically altered in the process. It would be reductive to store in every infant a concealed menu of adult objects—prestige, art, parental approval, resistance, union, erotic relation, sacred belonging—then have circumstances vary weights on preinstalled drives.

Yet **Leontiev's objectification of need and the DCO's generative possibility are not identical**. An actor can imagine an object never presently encountered, desire what remains impossible, be taught a word without acquiring its existential importance, or act under false information about what an object will accomplish. Desire may be conditioned by absence, projection, anticipation and contradiction, not by empirical gratification alone. At the other extreme a bodily object may sustain life without acquiring a recognized motive beyond immediate feeding.

**Positive theory:** distinguish organismic condition; socially/materially available or imagined object; actor-developed possibility of reference; subjective anticipation; actually attempted activity; actual objective result; experienced satisfaction/disappointment; and durable transformation of the relation among need, object, means and ongoing activities. Neither preloaded innate motive taxonomy nor mandatory reward-learning is acceptable.

**Nonresolution:** the theory cannot uniquely infer which imagined object will become a particular person's durable motive. It can articulate how candidate objects can become psychologically possible, under which histories and alternatives, without inventing a deterministic law.

## 3. Difference court II — emotion is not a readout of motive or success

**Leontiev PDF pp. 29–30** differentiates motives giving an activity personal sense from those that stimulate it; emotions can color action even when the actor does not identify its motive. A technical success may contradict what mattered in another concurrently implicated activity. **Meaning-making, incentive power, bodily feeling, personal sense, conscious self-account and objective outcome must remain different.**

This matters against two simplistic reductions: `emotion = goal reward` and `emotion = unconscious motive truth`. A felt disgust can be learned social devaluation, bodily aversion, guilt, thwarted desire or an actor's fallible interpretation. A frightened person may be acting courageously while bodily afraid. Love, anger and obligation may have different temporal persistence without being forced into a resolved interpersonal “ambivalence” label.

**Positive theory:** an episode has multiple simultaneously operative activities with potentially unlike meanings, salience and emotional colors; different functions of motivation can coexist without each being a separate drivescore. Experience can be partly preconceptual; later reflection can misidentify its cause, or make a new relation possible.

**Nonresolution:** no global emotion-to-motive inverse decoder; sourcefree selection traces are not unconscious validity.

## 4. Difference court III — a plural actor is not an obligatory fractured actor

**Lahire PDF p. 13 expressly rejects a universal claim of individual plurality.** The question is the historical conditions under which a differentiated plurality of repertoires is formed. His pp. 76–77 warn that an inferred disposition becomes a fictitious self-propelled motor when torn out of the contexts in which it is formed and expressed. At p. 87, practical analogy allows partly unconscious recruitment under resemblance, including misleading transfer.

There is tension with a system that treats contradictory projects or high-dimensional cognitive structure as the minimum definition of a convincing person. Some actual histories can produce strongly coordinated repertoires; others compartmentalize; the same actor can be heterogeneous in one domain and highly continuous in another. Different social worlds do not guarantee distinct psychological dispositions, just as uniform material conditions do not guarantee one subjectivity.

**Positive theory:** repertoire **presence/absence**, context-specific accessibility, latent capacity, actually performed scope, inhibition, coexistence, fusion, heterogeneity and unobserved/unknown must not collapse into a universal “plurality index.” A practiced behavior can remain stable for years without a new conflict; a non-enacted possible disposition is not a proven acquired form. Recognition of analogy can be partly nonverbal and wrong.

**Nonresolution:** a causal explanation of the same act may involve embodied past, current context and actual ongoing activity in different proportions; the theory must retain competing interpretations and meaningful counterexamples instead of assigning a default source hierarchy.

## 5. Difference court IV — structural force without recognition versus reflexive mediation

**Archer PDF p. 145** describes a mediated structural/cultural condition through the agent's concerns, projects and practices. Her later reflexivity analysis distinguishes communicative, autonomous, meta- and fractured forms (pp. 175, 220, 269–70, 314), not a generic human self-talk operation. Her pp. 87 and 220 show what private imaginative rehearsal and self-directing deliberation may accomplish for some adults. This is a genuine rival to any account that wholly dissolves individual practical reasoning into socially inherited dispositions.

But **the stronger reading of mediation as a necessary condition of social power would be false**: a workplace shutdown, flood, loss of legal status, discriminatory exclusion or bodily coercion can remove a real opportunity regardless of whether the person has consciously formulated a relevant project. Social power has objective effects in one causal register; whether *and how* it becomes a subjective constraint, practical reason or autobiographical meaning is a different question.

This is an unresolved philosophical tension, not a reason to label one school the winner. Holzkamp's subject-side premises challenge stimulus determinism; they do not justify erasing force, scarcity, imperial borders, institutional domination or an unchosen bodily harm. Archer's reflexive modes remain contestable descriptive proposals, not compulsory four-fold actor labels.

**Positive theory:** distinguish (a) world-level material constraint/facilitation; (b) available or unavailable actual action; (c) actor's accessible practical premise; (d) enacted or unformed project; (e) internal conversation or nonverbal anticipation *when acquired*; (f) public conduct; (g) unacknowledged but material suffering. A person may be harmed without recognizing the structure causing it, or may correctly recognize the structure and remain practically unable to alter it.

**Nonresolution:** we may not resolve Archer–Lahire–Holzkamp into a homogeneous “situated agency” operator. Each explains a different possibility or imposes a different contestable necessary condition. Their distinctive falsifiers must survive.

## 6. Difference court V — adult reflexivity is historically varied; infant thinking is not adult self-talk

PFF's [original Vision Bank 00](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/WHOLE_SPINE_BUILD_CONSTITUTION_R1/RAWLSING_BUILD_WAR_PLAN_R1/CENTRAL_SYNTHESIS_R1/PSYCHIC_FIELD_FORGE_R1/00_VISION_BANK_R1.md) and [04 appendix](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/WHOLE_SPINE_BUILD_CONSTITUTION_R1/RAWLSING_BUILD_WAR_PLAN_R1/CENTRAL_SYNTHESIS_R1/PSYCHIC_FIELD_FORGE_R1/04_ZERO_LOSS_CONVERSATIONAL_VISION_APPENDIX_R1.md) already separate actual person, standing conscious self, relational activation and outward expression; later D001 genealogy expressly says these are **analytical depth views, not mandatory four tables**. PFF's dialogic interaction treatment is stringent about own-output feedback but does not prove every psychological reorganization is a social conversation or a narrated interior monologue.

Archer's adults rehearsing novel decisions illuminate one acquired kind of reflexive operation, not the developmental default. Infant organismic recognition, anticipatory sensorimotor change, socially acquired signed language, an adult's sensory imagination, silent self-regulation, deliberate internal speech and learned meditative attention can operate in unlike forms. There may be unconscious configuration without publicly narratable memory; overt fluent explanation without corresponding skill.

**Positive theory:** a person may acquire a method of mentally representing an alternative, privately practice it, reject it, or lack that particular method altogether. Internal representation need not be language; language need not be spoken; an adult may be verbally fluent in one practice and inarticulate in another. An experienced mental rehearsal can become a future practical resource without having been public; a latent acquired orientation can operate without a fresh internally generated episodic rehearsal.

**Nonresolution:** refuse both the mandatory adult inward narrator and the behaviorist ban on causally significant actual private life.

## 7. Difference court VI — the historically variable *kinds* of cognition and organization are the test

[SEAM/18 biocultural adequacy](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/4d1a6195daca54b4dd63491f55c1eff73907f534/SEAM/18_SCIENTIFIC_BIOCULTURAL_ONTOGENESIS_ADEQUACY_CRITERION_R1.md) and [SEAM/21 variable iceberg](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/4d1a6195daca54b4dd63491f55c1eff73907f534/SEAM/21_EMERGENT_ICEBERG_VARIABLE_PSYCHOLOGICAL_ONTOLOGY_R1.md) had already articulated the **most severe anti-universalism**. One common organismic starting grammar must not preinstall the schooled, literate, morally liberal, verbally introspective Western adult. Different histories may yield **different categories, methods of noticing, kinds of practical problem, social reference objects and relations among acquired capabilities**, not merely different scores on the same motivational menu.

Examples from SEAM/18 must be conserved at full seriousness: accessible signed language is language, not an incomplete spoken-language tier; learning through observing and pitching in to community activity is not failed schooling; oral practices cannot be ranked as cognitively inferior to literate ones; Vai literacy and Arabic/English institutional literacy have different practical organizations. Deafness must not be used as a proxy for cognition or language deprivation. Historical reconstruction with few sources must remain uncertain; “feral child” anecdotes or a stipulated wolf-rearing survival test are not reliable native empirical profiles. Luria's cultural-cognition interviews are important evidence of task-bound historical cognition, **not a verdict on the inherent intellectual hierarchy of populations**.

**Positive theory:** separate biological maturation and sensory access; actual communicative affordances; practices available through social organization; distinct kinds of encountered signs and relations; actor-owned discrimination, skill, imagination and self-regulation; institutional use; and changing world reach. Not every child ever develops a familiar concept of achievement, national identity, written autobiography, explicit moral rule, or bounded modern psychological “self.” Nor is absence of an endorsed adult label proof of no subjective activity.

**Nonresolution:** developmental systems theory, Vygotsky/Luria and Leontiev offer useful families of mechanisms but not a complete universal transition distribution. Apparent cultural contrast must not be turned into invented innate population difference or into romanticized alternative epistemic superiority.

## 8. Difference court VII — reproduction and appropriation are materially contradictory without requiring psychic integration

[SEAM/19 Z](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/4d1a6195daca54b4dd63491f55c1eff73907f534/SEAM/19_SOROK_Z_CONTRADICTORY_REPRODUCTION_AFFORDANCE_APPROPRIATION_KERNEL_R1.md) already challenges two reductions: an adult's survey report does not identify their enacted childcare; an objectively improved world opportunity does not automatically teach the child its value. A labor shift can provide household income, remove care time, strengthen employer dependence and change which practical world child and caregiver face. A public school can grant useful literacy and impose discipline. Protective action can reproduce domination while being genuinely protective.

The social relation's **objective harmful/beneficial effects** do not wait for the focal actor to understand them. Nor does the child necessarily learn obedience by seeing somebody obey, or learn a prohibition when they never encountered it. Learning can come from observation, coactivity, overhearing, solitary experimentation, nonhuman environments, embarrassment, care, bodily change, conflict, misrecognition or no participation. A single family project is not the whole shared field of work, property, state coercion, culture, ecology and imperial unequal power.

**Positive theory:** keep objective world production, distribution of care/resources/time, external enforcement, unequal available actions, actor-specific encounter, practical premise, motivated act, Others' different uptake, and future world effect all independently real. They can conflict; they can also be genuinely unrelated at a particular time. Social reproduction is not a household affectionate-score mechanism.

**Nonresolution:** a class relation can be causally explanatory without predicting the actor's specific belief or response; a liberationist reading cannot automatically promote a desired resistant psyche into the resulting child.

## 9. Difference court VIII — actual capacity and the *organization of available time* are reciprocally constitutive

Sève PDF pp. 319–20 distinguishes broadly usable actual capacities from the innatist implication of “aptitude,” and links growth of capability to the time and activity historically available for its exercise. His pp. 344, 363, 368 analyze the way social labor, household activity, wage dependence, personal practices and use-time constrain and reproduce capacities. His specific historical hypotheses must not be adopted as a mathematically validated human law.

**The central DCO gap:** a stored competence may remain unused when wages, care duties, inaccessible space, permission, illness or other commitments remove the time and means; repeated exercise may become necessary to retain a capability; a new opportunity may be structurally real without entering the person's current life; a person may want to change and be unable to rearrange the material dependencies that determine their day. “Freedom to choose a goal” is too coarse for this.

**Positive theory:** actual practical capacity and its opportunities for use, change and degradation must be separately related through real activity time, embodiment, institutional access and Other-person dependence. Training can steal time from care; care can be an arena of learning; routine can be a form of survival; skill can persist without exercise; a practiced act can be performed for reasons alien to its initial formation.

**Nonresolution:** neither a universal skill-decay clock nor deterministic class-to-self prediction can be derived from the source; this is a causal domain for future qualified construction.

## 10. Difference court IX — temporality is multidirectional in meaning but not in objective chronology

Dreier's multiple-context life and Sève's time-use, together with Elder's life-course emphasis as treated in the inherited DCO, yield different reasons why a person's history cannot be selected as five “formative” scenes. Events occur at particular life positions; obligations recur; some experiences are important only retrospectively; a later relationship can change the meaning of an earlier practice without rewriting what happened. A parent's compressed fictional prehistory, a child's fine-activity infancy, a friend's outside the focal scene ongoing life and a visitor's external calendar are not one evidentiary time stream.

**Positive theory:** objective event/time, bodily developmental time, practice/repetition and use-time, social/institutional rhythms, actor remembered time, actor current interpretation, future anticipation, and sparse simulation resolution must be non-identical. Historical causal effect can be delayed, nondeclarative, weak, non-linear, reversible in significance, irreversible in occurrence. Low simulated resolution is not low psychic richness in the constituted person.

**Nonresolution:** neither continuous high-frequency logging nor a universal “important episode” detector can be inferred as the correct representation of living time.

## 11. Difference court X — PFF's two-way psychic mediation is necessary but cannot monopolize the whole person

PFF original 04 §§11–20 already insists that recognition, defense and expression are nonlinear, that activation may remain outside recognition, that current self and deeper historical organization affect each other, and that the person may observe their own actually spoken action and change later. This is substantial predecessor theory, not an R13 invention. **However**, its fundamental dyadic per-turn architecture is *a specialized encounter lens*. Nadia's work, care, fatigue, art, self-initiated practical experiment, memory, general cultural world and actual solitude are not a longer conversation with the external user.

**Positive theory:** preserve PFF's four noncollapsed questions as analytical projections where relevant, not four compulsory ontological storages; use its self-feedback only when an actual enacted act has occurred. The person's causal life precedes an interlocutor and exceeds any interaction frame. A person can change under a quiet material routine, make no detectable expressive leak, and become capable of a new relational activity outside the visitor's horizon.

**Nonresolution:** neither public utterance/observed clue nor model narration is a privileged route into the unconscious. More user attention can sometimes genuinely learn more without a total opacity rule; it does not become mind-reading.

## 12. Difference court XI — contradiction is not a built-in motor of emancipation, creativity or coherence

A contradiction between objective duties, between possible activities, between forms of self-account, or between what a person seeks and what a body needs may be operative at different levels. Some conflicts become subjectively recognized and reorganized; some become durable oppositions; some are managed by habit, self-discipline, external support, submission, compartmentalization, domination of others or ordinary avoidance. An actor can live through objectively conflicting activities without identifying a contradiction. Two opposed subjective beliefs can remain causally compartmentalized while no real objective conflict is present in the focal scene.

**Strongest positive nonresolution:** preserving contradiction can be a *successful theoretical account* even where the actor has no conscious synthesis and no action that joins the poles. The “productive” significance of contradiction is an empirical and historically contingent question: it may produce an acquired method, a deeper suppression, a misdirected analogy, a reactive hatred, unchanged conduct, or sustained suffering without growth. Any model default that turns conflict into insight, relational repair, ideological liberation or novel expressive creativity falsifies the DCO.

**A×B×C is one theoretical court, NOT the natural law of every psychological change.** Historical relations may be incommensurable, noninteracting, co-operative, accidentally entangled, wrongly identified as related or unintegrated for a lifetime. Their juxtaposition need not emit a new AB node. Their afterlife can become intelligible only later. There is no “always produce more complex person” mandate.

## 13. Difference court XII — evidence and authorship are distributed; no model gets an omniscient interior

A respondent's same-person survey cell is a measured reported pattern, not a psychological mechanism, not a couple and not the survey respondent's hidden biography. An LLM can participate in **intelligent fictional psychological abduction** subject to historically and source-qualified constraints; it cannot establish that a real respondent had the imagined inner life. An objective admitted world event can change another person's bodily and material possibility without entering the focal person's self-account. The user's observation of a tell is not proof of a secret motive. A detailed computational trace is a report of program operations, not validated science of actual unconscious causation.

**Positive theory:** preserve separate truth rights for statistical source, hypothetical ecology, selected particular world, actor-received experience, durable actor-owned psyche, conscious self-account, objective performed event, observer-perceived manifestation and external analytical theory. A valid fictional person may be demonstrably *wrong about themself*; a realistic external world must be able to refuse them. LLM formation proposals should become causally operative only through qualified world/actor admission, not because a proposed motive is psychologically beautiful.

**Nonresolution:** source-scoped explanatory plurality is a scientific virtue. An unknown actor-private state is not a neutral or all-zero state and not license to backfill a convenient trauma, desire or conversion.

## 14. Eight deliberately unlike counterhistories — not staged plot beats

Each following case is a **demand on the general positive psychology**, not a screenplay or compulsory training sequence. Preserve ordinary conditions in at least half the generated histories.

1. **Same objective workday; different activities.** Two adults perform identical care-limited paid shifts, but one experiences occupational competence alongside sadness, another chiefly material survival, and a third no consciously conflicted feeling. None is more authentic by virtue of narrative drama. Objective employer/care effects stay fixed.
2. **Absent public revelation; real private work.** An individual reconsiders an old episode through an acquired comparative practice, privately imagines a new relation, then later meets an unfamiliar event. A comparable actor with equally vivid imagination may make no durable update. No invented earlier self-talk after the outcome is known.
3. **Oppressive benefit.** A school teaches a child a valuable skill through humiliating discipline. The new skill can become the child's means of resisting the school's authority, reproducing it or doing nothing socially consequential. Skill and domination remain simultaneously real.
4. **No unitary plurality.** In a strongly coordinated single-practice life, an individual has one durable orientation without compulsory contradictory drives; in another history, acquired context-specific practices conflict. Both count as full persons with possible future changes.
5. **Language and cognition uncoupled.** One child acquires a fully accessible signed-language practice; another in a predominantly oral setting learns rich practical discrimination without literacy; a third learns several written language uses with different institutions. No one requires the English-speaking adult interior monologue as criterion of cognition.
6. **Imagined object without possible deed.** An adult anticipates an unusual artistic or relational possibility while lacking resources, time or lawful access; no performed act or actual object-production occurs. The imagination may matter later, or not; the environment cannot be rewritten to vindicate the vision.
7. **Correct structure, mistaken reason.** The actor is objectively excluded by an institution but wrongly explains exclusion as an intimate person's malice. That interpretation can alter real conduct without changing the institution's independent causal role.
8. **One phenomenal tip, several psychic histories.** Identical neutral speech can follow attention, inhibition, learned public politeness, exhausted affect or an unremarkable lack of investment. There is no requirement for a narrator to add a detectable tell or for the visitor to solve it.

## 15. Strict question ledger: what remains genuinely unresolved in THEORY

**O-1 — Generative novelty without an exhaustive prior concept menu.** Strong *family of explanations* recovered (Leontiev objectification, analogy, social practice, untried possible end, reflective reorganization). **OPEN:** no scientifically established general account of which novel objects an individual will imagine/endorse under arbitrary biographies. **O-2 — Different kinds of socially formed psychic ontology.** Strong positive requirement recovered from SEAM/18/21 and user's sources. **OPEN:** what minimal general operator semantics are genuinely cross-cultural rather than an implicit projection of schooled/literate Western subjects? Counterexample families exist; no universal human psychological category list is issued.

**O-3 — Relation among nonverbal, preconscious, imagistic and reflective activity.** PFF distinguishes focal/peripheral/inaccessible; Archer provides deliberative alternatives; developmental theory requires nonadult forms. **OPEN:** scope of truly new privately originated episodes without adding a retrospective narratorial causal fiction, under owner-qualified temporal history. This is a theory of possible processes, not a requirement to record every thought.

**O-4 — Objective domination and subjective practical premises.** Material constraint remains independently effective; actor's reasons mediate their chosen attempts. **OPEN:** no universally sufficient account of when material power forms recognition, denial, adaptive acquiescence, critique or active refusal. Preserve competing models.

**O-5 — Forming a new relation among acquired organizations.** Actual history A, B and later C may yield a new method, no interaction, misapplication or suppressed possibility. **OPEN:** what semantic and experiential distinction qualifies a *new kind of acquired operation* rather than a renamed pair, and how can different histories admit unlike kinds? Theory must not assume all conjunctions create novelty.

**O-6 — Timescale and historical evidentiary granularity.** Present, routine, lifelong, phase-transition, compressed, remembered and retrospective meanings differ. **OPEN:** exactly which durably consequential nondeclarative afterlives of highly compressed lived time can be warranted without simulated minute-level autobiography or false unknown-as-no-change.

**O-7 — The relative causal weight of different rival explanatory frameworks.** Lahire, Leontiev, Archer, Sève, Dreier, Holzkamp, developmental-systems and psychoanalytic accounts often answer different questions or disagree about the necessary mediator. **OPEN:** no natural theory of globally correct reconciliation; retain competing examples with conditional domain and negative evidence.

**O-8 — Epistemic humility under source bias and fictional abduction.** The same empirical questionnaire cloud can support multiple fictional parent histories; original child life remains radically open. **OPEN:** calibrating plausible semantic hypotheses across human cultures and historical worlds without cultural stereotypes, covert moral destinations or counterfeit science.

**No theorem says O-1–O-8 must each become a standalone module.** Nor may theory declare an open problem closed because an existing code function can label it. Preserve all open items in source-grained form until a stronger causal account answers them and the single HOME integrates the positive gain.
