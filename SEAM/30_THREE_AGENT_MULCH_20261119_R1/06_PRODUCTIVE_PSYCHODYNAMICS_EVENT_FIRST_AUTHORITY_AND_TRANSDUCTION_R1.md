## 0. Three controlling corrections—and a scientific distinction

**C1. Event-first psychic operation is already intended.** The SOROK event structure activates actual persona computation from admitted world/event, the body's current conditions, prior deeply constituted state, and existing relations. **The LLM's subsequent realized expression must map to the computational shift already produced, not manufacture a pretty antecedent to justify a fictional expression.** PRE-A0's richer two-call developmental design permits a model to *propose* owner-qualified semantic afterlife effects, but never to countermand canonical event causation, other actors' choices, or grant itself retrospective proof of an unconscious origin. Distinguish (a) deterministic/sourcefree current computational dynamics; (b) bounded LLM-proposed psychological elaboration, if used and admitted by competent owners; and (c) ex post lived/narrative realization of an already admitted transition. For this stated event architecture, (c) must come *after* the relevant shift. V2/renderers never mint its causes.

**C2. Productivity is not improvement and inhibition is not deletion.** The principal's materialist principle is that every dynamic of repression, resistance, retention, misrecognition, attachment, frustration, retreat, silence, aggression and loss participates in causal production, conservation of a patterned condition, redirection, or transformation; do not silently remove a contradicted node from psychic causal possibility just because its positive expression is unavailable. A suppressed tenderness may reorganize resentment, make it defensive, produce shame, intensify longing, or simply remain part of the latent structure conditioning future recruitment. A defeated motive may shape the route of another motive without receiving current conscious representation.

**Scientific qualification:** calling this the “first law of thermodynamics applied to experience” is an evocative design analogy, **not a literal conservation law of psychological energy, affect, information, memory, moral injury or meaning**. There is no empirically justified scalar whose quantity must be conserved and transferred one-to-one when a feeling is inhibited. Biological memory can decay, expectations extinguish, ties rupture, and some events genuinely produce no durable owner update. SOROK's stronger invariant is **historical/causal accountability**: never silently equate absent expression with absence of cause, never erase an admitted lineage by editing its current interpretation, and explicitly represent retained, routed, changed, inaccessible, extinguished, unobserved or source-unknown conditions at their appropriate grade. An ongoing pattern's *reproduction* may itself be its relevant consequence; no artificial daily character-development reward is required. *Dormancy can mean nonexpression or nonrecruitment, not annihilation of the historical form.* Historical records remain monotonic even when actual capacities and present effects diminish or disappear.

**C3. Cruelty can recruit loyalty through an authorized moral performance.** A leader's harmful behavior is not guaranteed to recruit empathy or anger in followers. If the leader performs the behavior through a familiar, materially recognized genre of righteous rebuke, discipline, corrective love, purification or protection of the group, a follower may instead recruit identification, devotion, righteous indignation, compliance, zeal, shame, anxiety, defensive contempt for the victim, or an acquired practice of imitating the leader. The *genre* and its social conditions matter. Naked insult lacking the authorized signs may fail, but no fixed verbal formula determines success. The victim's harm remains world-owned and cannot be rewritten by followers' moral interpretation. Neither assent nor resistance is universal.

## 2. The strengthened productivity principle

What counts as productive is not “something good happens,” “a new lasting motive must appear,” or “the sum of emotional forces remains constant.” It is that a psychodynamic process has **causal organization and possible consequences**. Examples:

- **Inflammation:** a threat, humiliation, loss or recognition violation can make an existing complex dominate salience, intensify defensive vigilance, strengthen active suppression or generate a compensatory expression.
- **Attenuation and extinction:** a formerly powerful expectation may be repeatedly disconfirmed; its anticipatory consequence weakens while the actual historical episodes remain immutable. This is a genuine transformation or loss, not an accounting violation.
- **Suppression / repression:** public affection can be inhibited while remaining relationally invested; the resulting conflict can be lived as impatience, discomfort, overcorrection, shame, numbness, or no readily legible current effect.
- **Displacement / substitution:** a blocked organization may find expression through a more available object or practice, but this needs historical/functional justification; never posit displaced anger as the hidden truth by default.
- **Compensation and reversal:** fear of exclusion can produce identification with the excluding authority; one's learned care can be subordinated to preserving membership; apparent courage can function as reassurance-seeking.
- **Sublimation / transformation:** an impulse or affective relation may become incorporated into art, solidarity, labor, devotion or aggression through historically learned means. No universal rule guarantees a benign destination.
- **Sublation (dialectical use):** a prior organization can be negated, preserved as history or practical capacity, and reorganized at a different level. This is a possible concrete developmental pattern, not a compulsory three-stage law for every event.
- **Reproduction without new form:** a defensive pattern repeats because its material conditions and currently operative history remain; a no-change owner receipt does not mean the situation lacked social reproduction.
- **Forgetting / diminishing:** current access, strength and function may genuinely fade or end; a historical record is not a literally eternal force on present conduct.

**Implementation invariant, provisional:** For every supported transition, distinguish *historical trace*, *present availability*, *direct expression*, *indirect causal role*, *blocked/rechanneled function*, *qualified developmental change* and *unknown/no-effect*. Do not require a full dense causal graph or a nonzero flux for every element at every clock tick. A locally quiescent subsystem is compatible with a historically constituted psyche. The point is to avoid *causally arbitrary disappearance* when the scenario depends upon repression, contradiction or displacement.

## 3. The pastor's rebuke: an ideological psychodynamic worked case

**Objective event:** A pastor publicly humiliates or injures a congregant. The conduct has a world-owned description, context, audience and material consequences. “Righteous rebuke” is *the pastor's performance and possibly others' interpretation*, not an objective verdict that the injury was deserved.

**Institutional scene:** The pastor holds recognized formal/charismatic authority; congregational practices distinguish rebuke, care, doctrinal error and discipline; followers may depend on the leader and group for belonging, salvation-language, identity, pastoral care, economic opportunities or reputation. Those dependencies must be historically actual rather than activated by merely assigning a “religious follower” trait.

**Follower's prior iceberg (one possible configuration):**
- learned identification with the pastor as a trustworthy interpreter of righteousness;
- emotional investment in membership, divine approval, family loyalty and in-group continuity;
- acquired category relation “good believer protects truth”;
- capacity for sympathy, justice concern or remembered victimhood, possibly relatively weak, compartmentalized or interpreted through doctrinal norms;
- experience of earlier rebukes as corrective love, spectacle, threat or social sanction;
- self-understanding that obedience is moral courage rather than submissiveness.

**Ritualized actual cue:** The leader invokes authority, doctrinal/scriptural register, publicly recognized sanction, community witnesses, and an authorized form of corrective address. This can change the *relation among follower's available forms*, not just add a semantic label to an unchanged insult.

**Possible follower-specific psychodynamic pathways (not an inevitable type):**
1. **Righteous identification:** Leader's act recruits “truth-protection”; other person's distress becomes evidence of proper correction or necessary discipline; loyal defense feels courageous, compassionate or holy.
2. **Care through discipline:** Compassion is not absent; it is **reinterpreted or subordinated** within a learned framework in which pain is evidence of necessary care.
3. **Protective alliance:** Sympathy for the target coexists with fear of being the next target; public assent protects livelihood, family belonging or access to care, without requiring inner moral endorsement.
4. **Authoritarian imitation:** The leader's permitted mode of aggression becomes a practice available to the follower—one can *become the disciplinarian*, not only endure an authority.
5. **Ambivalent accommodation:** Genuine sympathy is inhibited or compartmentalized and remains indirectly productive in private shame, avoidance, rationalization, intrusive recall or later relational conflict.
6. **Disaffection / critique:** Hypocrisy, contrast with earlier moral teaching, material solidarity with the target or independent recognition authority can recruit an alternative organization. The follower may still comply because the world constrains defection.
7. **Nonuptake:** The act scarcely matters within that follower's available organization or is inaccessible, and no distinct durable form appears.

The seemingly trivial contrast matters: **“You are being rebuked in righteousness” and “you are a fucking dipshit” differ only if the speaker, genre, setting, prior practice and audience confer different credibility and authority.** Naked abuse can undermine the performance; it can also be normalized as “strong leadership” in a different milieu. A universal “holy language makes cruelty attractive” threshold would be nonsense.

### Theorists in one causal chain—without treating them as interchangeable

- **Gramsci**: civil-society hegemony recruits historically produced practical consent; rule by force alone differs from leadership made commonsensical and morally legitimate. Consent/coercion can coexist.
- **Althusser**: ideological interpellation works materially through recognized subject-positions, institutional practices and rituals; the follower can act “freely” in a mode that reproduces subjection. This is *not* identical to SOROK's internal psychic recruitment; outer social interpellation and inner iceberg activation must remain two realities.
- **Bourdieu**: authorized speech draws efficacy from the recognized social position and field; a ritualized rebuke is not inherently performative because its words are semantically pious. Symbolic power and misrecognition require real institutions and recognition conditions.
- **Foucault**: pastoral power individualizes, seeks to govern conscience and conduct, and often presents direction/obedience through protection, care, salvation and shepherding. His genealogical critique does not make all actual pastoral care harmful.
- **Fromm**: an internalized authoritarian conscience may be experienced as one's own moral voice; identification with authority may organize feeling beyond an explicit fear of sanctions.
- **Freire**: someone subordinated may reproduce an oppressor's practices, potentially valuing the authority's methods as the only legitimate means of influence. This is a candidate pathway, not a universal psychotype.
- **Leontiev**: a single activity carries several possible personal senses. An objectively cruel act can be experienced by a follower as loyalty, safety, achievement of moral duty or violation of care, depending on their historically formed activities.
- **Sève / social reproduction**: access to livelihood, family, status, institutional power and care materially organizes which interpretations can be enacted, repeated, challenged or safely expressed.
- **PRE-A0 §10** already specifies identification → internalization → naturalization → invisibility → **agent-of**, including explicit outward discipline of others. This is exactly the internal-to-external route the example needs; do not reinvent it.

**Critical boundary:** Objective suffering is not nullified by its moralized representation. The target's psyche is independent; the follower's admiration does not prove the pastor's righteousness or reconstruct the victim's encounter. Nor is compliant outward behavior proof of inner consent.

## 4. Multi-relational transmission: where new compound affect comes from

The causal unit is often a *configuration*: not anger plus love as two weights, but anger *at a loved person*, shame *about one's anger*, desire *for the leader's approval*, disgust *at one's dependency*, nostalgia *for a time of trust*, or pleasure *in remembered grief*.

Relations between affects, objects, permissions, self-models and others' models can themselves acquire salience, inhibition and historical parentage. A moralized humiliation may become exhilarating to a follower because their acquired authority-identification transforms another's distress into evidence of public righteousness; for a different follower, that same distress exposes the leader's domination.

An inhibited tenderness can be causally consequential even where the event causes no public tenderness: it may intensify defensive aggression, create ambivalence or make a later interruption meaningful. The computation should not assume these always occur; it should preserve their *historically supported possibility*, including latent relation/motif pressure.

A person can acquire relations *to emotions*: pleasure in sadness, fear of longing, resentment about dependence, shame about desire. Consensual erotic ritual and power exchange can acquire meaning through trust, learned symbolism, negotiated power, vulnerability and consent; no universal childhood-injury or pathological inference follows. Keep dynamic interpretive hypotheses subordinate to actual history.
