### New four-clock material reproduction and unanticipated-end courts

These are **specific extensions of the existing historical R17–R19 sourcefree tests**, not new proof or an authorization to duplicate their world/psychology owners. The source code already has named test coverage for formal grant versus installation, installed gate versus effective enforcement, post-reform residual sanction, worker entry/withdrawal, real funded relief and collective procedural learning: [R17](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6647f746f4f7d2ae3dd0d14af5cde38191044773/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/tests/test_institutional_food_power_r17.py), [R18](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6647f746f4f7d2ae3dd0d14af5cde38191044773/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/tests/test_reproduced_food_power_r18.py) and [R19](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6647f746f4f7d2ae3dd0d14af5cde38191044773/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/tests/test_labor_dependency_r19.py). **Their existence proves authored test code, not execution at this DCO HEAD or independent acceptance.**

**Court MP-1 — capacity without effective power.** Hold actor, practiced facility, bodily capacity, desired feeding and physically available stock constant. Vary only the effective financed exclusion at the site while holding formal title unchanged. The caregiver should be able to attempt both; actual provision can succeed only under the eligible world route. The child's experienced hunger/relief must follow the real result, not the caregiver's good intentions. Reversing only the caregiver's reflective explanation must not change objective provision.

**Court MP-2 — legal change, retained material force, actor-limited knowledge.** Establish the same initial controlled resource through actual installation, historic rights and material enforcement. Let distinct actors independently petition until the formal rule changes, while one previously financed selective sanction remains effective. Compare two branches with identical physical food, caregiver bodily ability and expressed care: one caregiver can actually FEED, the other is blocked despite the same formal right. Verify objective food depletion and the child's bodily benefit, **not merely a permission predicate**. Then change only the independently clocked sanction and repeat: a physical possibility may arise without a new legal act, conscious insight or promise that the caregiver will choose to retry. The bounded [R18 direct-world and joined-runtime consumer tests](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/731f71b61fad5764eb021776ae937a376a54e8a8/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/tests/test_reproduced_food_power_r18.py) now check this bodily consequence *and* a crucial actor-access nonomniscience distinction: FEED remains an actor-attemptable operation while institutional exclusion blocks its successful resolution. When the sanctioned interval lapses, the same attempted operation can succeed. The joined test's FEED steps are **explicit external research choices**, not demonstrations that the mother spontaneously knows she can retry.

**The harder MP-2 continuation** must contrast four noninterchangeable afterlives: (a) externally terminated enforcement with surviving legal title; (b) unchanged legal right with a durable physical barrier or delegated standing enforcement; (c) objective new access that the actor has not discovered and therefore does not yet attempt; and (d) an actor who attempts access from a mistaken expectation of reform and meets still-effective force. A refusal, an unperformed action, an absent occasion and an active prohibition cannot be collapsed into the same WITHHOLD value. Each variant needs an exact material history, actor-access record and later actor-selected operation, plus cold replay and valid nulls. This stronger four-variant test is **not** implemented by the new R18 unit extension and should not be certified from it.

**Court MP-3 — productive novelty without retroactive motive.** A person performs a repair for one previously admitted practical object. The world produces a separate object/sign not previously anticipated. Compare two otherwise identical continuities: in one, the actor actually receives the novel sign; in the other, the world fact exists but is not received. The historical [`unintended_goods.py`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6647f746f4f7d2ae3dd0d14af5cde38191044773/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/unintended_goods.py) candidate may propose a later bounded possible end only in the first. Neither may record this as the original motive, an already earned skill, a conscious value judgment, or permission to claim the newly produced object. A future person may act on the same object while its producer remains unaware.

**Court MP-4 — the contradictory material afterlife of one performed WORK.** Two actors possess equally qualified skills but enter separately established employment relations. Hold bodily competence and the WORK action constant; vary qualified external control, available employer cash, actual wage payment, later worker relief, and employer ability to finance an independently decided sanction. The first work event may produce *two new world resources in different hands*: additional employer-controlled output stock and earned worker wages. A second, separate wage-funded contribution creates a worker-controlled relief pool; the employer's own stock does **not** automatically become realized profit or enforcement cash. Compare output ownership, employer and worker accounts, wage claim, real fund, the colleague's later sanction and possible relief, subsequent purchasing/feeding, actor-specific interpretations and ongoing independent activity. The [R19 material double-entry test extension](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/731f71b61fad5764eb021776ae937a376a54e8a8/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/tests/test_labor_dependency_r19.py) directly probes the stock/wage/fund distinctions at sourcefree scope.

**Do not infer the missing mediations.** Goods produced are not goods sold; paid wages do not prove full labor value, realized surplus or political consent; receiving relief does not establish class consciousness; returning to work does not establish reconciliation. The same relief can stabilize labor-power reproduction, support an unanticipated cooperative alternative, or do neither. A stronger MP-4 court must later follow actually realized sales/uses, worker-controlled alternatives and genuinely actor-selected subsequent acts where existing owners and scientific permission permit them. No unearned economic totals or psychic motives may be imported to make the dialectic come out nicely.

**Cold replay and first consumer:** all four require one persisted actor/world event ledger and independently qualified actor exposure, followed by subsequent actual *action* that exercises learned possibilities. Execute normal and optimized settings at the same exact head; include malformed identity, future-cue leakage, injected consent and unsupported PYAT-native claims as negative controls. If the result changes only a philosophical annotation while runtime behavior is identical, **no additional organismic function has been demonstrated**.

## 3. Transferable principles retained in HOME

1. Material permission, practical capability, expected possibility, actor knowledge and realized action are independently variable.
2. Independent activities can have multiple contradictory objective consequences, and separate people can receive them differently.
3. An unintended result can form a new *candidate* future end without retroactively becoming a prior motive.
4. Institutional continuity and ordinary household arrangements can persist as previously produced conditions; collective action is not their universal explanatory theme.
5. Changed outer possibility does not automatically rewrite acquired subjective anticipation or produce action.
6. The evaluation must admit valid NO_CHANGE, actor error, absent receipt, lawful refusal and persistence under cold replay.
7. No result of a sourcefree test establishes a universal human psychological law.
