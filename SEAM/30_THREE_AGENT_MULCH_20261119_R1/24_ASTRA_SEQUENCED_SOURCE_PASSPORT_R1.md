## 0. What must be known before the theory is considered

### Governing ontology — epistemic sequence in service of affirmative synthesis

**This passport is the reading order for a research program that assumes the possibility and necessity of a unified, non-reductive dialectical truth-picture of the historically constituted person.** Plural determinations are not independent islands that may be declared “the irreducible plural result.” Their differing forms and sometimes nonreconciled or indirectly connected operations are **moments and relations of one historically developing totality**. Explanatory unity must account for their actual differentiation, conflict, mediation and production; it must not reduce them to identical factors or invent local causal links where none are warranted.

The staged reading order exists **to maximize the depth of synthesis**, not to substitute source inventory, negative finding, or a courteous pluralism for synthesis. The analyst should continue searching through richer material/developmental mediations when a proposed relation fails, and use singular counterexamples to transform the conception of the whole. Preserving a local absence of direct linkage is compatible with, and requires, seeking the larger dialectical constitution of that local difference.

The program does not imply a guaranteed unique psychological fate for any real individual, a prewritten adult endpoint, a reconciled harmony or a universal mathematical law. These would be reductions of the synthesized object, not the unified truth-picture being pursued.



**One project and one difficult scientific object.** Rawlsing aims to build an actual, continuing, independently living fictional Other, Nadia, in a historically and materially real-enough world populated by other independent persons. The psychology must support bodily need and maturation, partly inaccessible historical organization, desire and novel motives, simultaneously recruited and conflicting activities, actual action, material consequences, differently interpreted social experience, recursive development and ordinary time. Nadia is not a chatbot persona, a personality-factor vector, a predetermined adult, a narrative puppet or a plot about desirable moral growth. The eventual human may encounter her through a distinct NEIGHBOR UX; the human does not author her past or become the sovereign of her world. The project must eventually implement the theory, economically reusing source-qualified existing machinery, without making today's code the ceiling of theory.

**Two historical entrances, one continuing psychology:** (1) survey-conditioned *fictional* parents formed from whole-survey-correspondent empirical possibilities, contextual abduction and compressed historical backfill; (2) Nadia's actual historically open bodily, social and cultural formation through lived activities, not adult traits installed in infancy. A survey fact or aggregate association does not reveal a real respondent's unconscious, enacted caregiving, spouse or childhood. The fictional parents themselves continue to become.

**User-selected highest-level developmental question:** *What is possible? What facilitates? What inhibits or represses? By which forces? In contradiction with what other tendencies? Under which conditions might these relations change?* This applies to real material and institutional conditions **and** actor-specific interpretation and acquired life, without replacing the actor by a class label.

**Jurisdictions:** PRE-MAIN is common operating/protected integration; PYAT supplies source-qualified empirical/objective worlds; SOROK owns the developing fictional person's native actor/psychological world under proper authorization; NEIGHBOR concerns the human encounter and the asymmetry of experiences. ASTRA is a **commissioned theoretical analyst**, not an HQ, issuer, actor-data writer, scientific accreditor, independent receiving authority, programmer or new owner. ## 2. Anti-contamination rules for the handoff

- Never begin by showing ASTRA a proposed “final 6 contradictions,” a finalized chapter plan, implementation modules, or the reviewer’s alleged conclusions. ASTRA must discover centers and permit the sources to defeat a candidate center.
- Never starve it of the project's finished-person demand, source/authority limits, parent/Nadia asymmetry, culturally variable ontogenesis or independent world/Other reality.
- Treat R16 as the original **substantive** theoretical corpus at this cut; R17 and its author courts as **later interpretations** to confront after original derivation. A reviewer may discover a new fault in R16 itself.
- Allow truly negative **local** cases: no direct contradiction or new functional relation at this activity/scale, no enduring change, false proposed fusion, history that never becomes a motive, practical constraint despite freedom of imagination, care without truth, structural domination without conscious recognition, affection without reconciliation, extinction of a formerly effective pattern, ordinary ease and pleasure. **Each such difference remains internal to the historical totality and thus poses a further explanatory question for synthesis; it is not permission to stop with unrelated plural fragments.**
- Don't equate materialism with compulsory activism, revolutionary destiny, social complaint or reducing every person to a class function. Material conditions are causally irreducible; psychological specificity remains real.
- Do not impose Hegelian sublation as a required *local resolution*, Deleuzian differentiation as a universal learning mechanism, psychoanalytic repression, Archerian verbal inner conversation or a Western adult rational controller on all histories. **This is not agnosticism about dialectical unity:** distinguish the assumption of a discoverable differentiated whole from any premature formula for that whole.
- Do not infer undocumented author/reviewer status from file existence, comments or branch names. Protected science, native actor, empirical source and human/UX rights remain separately owned.
- Do not let custody workload (681 textual units) turn conceptual analysis into a stamp-and-sort exercise. Work with resegmented **causal determinations** and source exceptions; preserve source range traceability as the external check.

**Final use rule:** This passport is **just-in-time source nourishment in service of affirmative, relentless non-reductive synthesis**. The one primary ASTRA task is to derive the dialectically unified, internally differentiated constitutive whole, not to produce another source-navigation encyclopedia or take unresolved plurality as the final result. Read the next group when its epistemic role arrives; revisit primary sources only where a material contradiction needs further evidence.
