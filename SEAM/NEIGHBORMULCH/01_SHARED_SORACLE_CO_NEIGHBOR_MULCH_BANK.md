**Controlling engineering-facing translation (not a new economic formula):**
- At entrance the player-shell must be **demographically and socioeconomic-position homologous to Nadia, at exactly the same SES**, not a loose approximate class analogy.
- For an identical ordinary material purchase at the matched entry point, **the actual financial/resource burden must be equivalent for both**, not merely a common nominal price. The fictional cost of Nadia's generosity is exactly legible by the player's own cost, including its relative effect on the resources bar, while the actor's subjective meaning is not.
- A sensible concrete test is: when both could order the same takeout at entry, the visitor's percentage-cost preview and Nadia's objective counterfactual burden match. In the historic example 18% is illustrative; the exact denominator/projection algebra is not an accepted law and must be reconciled with real material ownership.
- The match can be discovered implicitly through ordinary experience; **do not require a tutorial explaining SES equality, and do not force a discovery**.
- Both material positions **can change later** through actual independent history. No permanent wallet synchronization; no retroactive equalization or compulsory similar choices.
- The same SES and same initial purchase burden do **not** assign the external human Nadia's childhood, family memories, private bodily experience, religious formation or literal past. The thin local peer shell and the actual foreign consciousness remain distinct.
- The peer shell is generated **from already constituted Nadia and her historical position**, not by changing Nadia's class to flatter or match the real external user.

## 5. Required negative courts and internal contradictions to preserve

**F1 — SES equivalence vs economic breadth.** Same socioeconomic status and equivalent ordinary purchase burden are controlling. The exact operational meaning of SES is plural (social position, effective purchasing flexibility, institutions, income, occupation, property/control). Must select a coherent *first-consumer comparison* rather than assume matching one number proves the whole. The equality is **not** merely "same price region" or similar class; the user correction explicitly requires **same cost**. Test actual ledger math against the presentation.

**F2 — Same-SES thin peer vs independent household.** Do not erase Nadia's child, family obligations, historical debts or established source-conditioned circumstances to manufacture a carbon-copy user. Instead form the visitor's local starting position from the historically prior actor's actual class and effective cost burden. Where specific SES axes cannot be matched without invented autobiography, disclose the bounded simulation artifice and choose a lawful approximation **without quietly changing the user's settled rule**.

**F3 — Encounter invitation vs person independence.** A first-session product requirement cannot become the world cause of Nadia accepting a new neighbor. Branch can open in corridor, smoke break or practical encounter, not always inside her apartment. The actor can refuse. An eligible introduction event can be selected from the person's actual life, **but no rewrite of psyche or invented grateful reaction**.

**F4 — Prelude past vs user agency.** "We briefly exchanged words" as neutral setup may be tolerable; "you promised supper, disclosed trauma, refused a date" is significant authored prior action. Make the boundary explicit, opt for starting interaction before meaningful choice and do not call the real human an autopiloted character.

**F5 — Seed root vs per-user first contact.** Root historical facts can be shared; the user's specific local position, invite, first conversation and acquired actor model belong to the user-specific continuation. No retroactive globalized pre-contact User A.

**F6 — World events vs LLM narrative bait.** First contact, day and in-scene interruptions arise from actor/world/project/institution/time; LLM proposes bounded candidates, never self-issues history or optimizes events for user compatibility, emotional revelation or retention.

**F7 — Behavioral learning vs moral scripting.** Nadia's affection, irritation, intimacy, withdrawal and indirect developmental effects follow from her actual historical organization; the system may not steer toward user intimacy or theatrical independence.

**F8 — Family and desire vs moralized good endings.** Actor enjoyment, coercive material conditions, consent, social norms, and independent network responses remain separate; no automatic trauma, attraction, condemnation or punishment.

**F9 — Compression vs false completeness.** Sparse-event hypercompression can stand in for ongoing activity but must not fabricate exhaustive day-by-day event evidence or guarantee breakthrough from each chosen event; adult psyche need not be fully self-readable.

**F10 — Currentness vs fabricated context.** A weather event or strike can shape meeting only if factually authoritative at the claimed external scope; don't invent topical current news for a hook.

**F11 — Rich user profile vs avatar invention.** System-level user-inference can operate on evidence but Nadia may receive only lawful user conduct; no completed first-person decisions on behalf of absent real user.

**F12 — Cross-agent status.** CO's assent to the conceptual demand, any CODEMULCH integration, actual protected receiving, exact G4 attribution or a passing first-consumer test have NOT occurred simply because this bank was created.
