## 1 — THE OBSERVER IS OBSERVABLE, AND CURIOSITY HAS A SOCIAL COST

**Source:** [Original Sorok H049–H062](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/a4211c3ec77e8269097601fe9a3dce2745395ac0/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/12_CHAT_EXHAUSTIVE_HARVEST_AUDIT_R5.md#L548), especially [H053–H062](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/a4211c3ec77e8269097601fe9a3dce2745395ac0/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/12_CHAT_EXHAUSTIVE_HARVEST_AUDIT_R5.md#L592); Q-OBS-001, Q-PRIV-002, Q-ATTEND-002, S8/S11/S14.

**Originally present:** a visitor asking openly, glancing, touching, secretly looking, looking while pretending to discuss Rome, asking third parties or inspecting while Nadia is absent are materially/relationally different actions. Attention costs time, but **duration and social visibility are separate**. Nadia may notice, misperceive, infer incorrectly, be indifferent, or later reinterpret. Physical access to a bill/photograph is not automatically permission.

**Targeted code comparison:** Original P6/MIII actor-relative perception and MII viewer-private attention are substantive existing sources. NEIGHBORMULCH R2 at [exact 6e05792...](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6e05792f29964dafa289b884664e9c892dc0e310/SEAM/NEIGHBORMULCH/neighbor_runtime.py) commits visitor SAY/MOVE/WAIT/LEAVE and finite actor-eligible signs; *that inspected visitor first consumer* does not yet demonstrate an attended covert glance, observer-visible gaze and independently formed suspicion or mistaken meta-model. This is a **targeted unproved join**, not a declaration that no partial owner exists.

**Reciprocal theoretical consequence:** what the user tries to learn of Nadia is itself a material act that Nadia might model. Both actor models can be wrong. This is a *field of observation of observation*, not an omniscient camera and not an automatic punishment for investigating.

**First falsifier:** same privately situated bill and user glance in worlds with Nadia physically facing the user vs occupied/absent. Different sensory possibility; optional wrong suspicion; no privileged actor-mind leak; no automatic negative relationship score.

## 2 — UNRECOGNIZED GOOD, HIDDEN HARM, AND FALSE CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION

**Source:** [Original Sorok H087–H099](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/a4211c3ec77e8269097601fe9a3dce2745395ac0/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/12_CHAT_EXHAUSTIVE_HARVEST_AUDIT_R5.md#L884), Q-CAUSAL-001, Q-MULTICAUSE-001 and original D002/D003.

**Originally present:** actual objective benefit can occur without the beneficiary knowing who helped, and therefore without gratitude or relationship advancement. A concealed theft can lead a family to blame the wrong person and really damage that relationship. Later confession can alter *interpretation of past events* without changing their objective past. Someone can believe the visitor caused a change the visitor did not cause. Social reputation is neither truth nor justice.

**Targeted code comparison:** CO R27 [world.py](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/d682db603f4c2916d68cfe30334e1ca539190bb5/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/world.py) owns several real material care, food, work, object and third-person acts. R26 can admit witnessed CARE/FEED/IMPOSE as actor-local third-person evidence. The file [unintended_goods.py](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/d682db603f4c2916d68cfe30334e1ca539190bb5/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/unintended_goods.py) is *not* an uncredited social-benefit engine: it admits **personally witnessed novel possible ends**. The inspected R27 world action path does not show a complete secret theft→false blame→later confession first consumer. Existing owners should be extended/combined.

**Reciprocal theoretical consequence:** objective causation, actor attribution and recognition must be different fields. An untrue interpretation can have objective consequences; an objectively valuable action may never become socially recognized. This is a particularly demanding anti-retention law for the game.

**First falsifier:** resource/object change is world-committed, attribution by a sibling is separately actor-limited and wrong, later testimony can reorganize the sibling field with actual downstream action. No user gratitude points, no author-forced accusation, cold replay preserves the original event and mistaken historical belief.

A thing’s deep resonance may be real without a person consciously articulating it. Childhood and adult events may share an upstream cause rather than childhood preference *destining* adulthood.

**Targeted code comparison:** R27 [material_objects.py](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/d682db603f4c2916d68cfe30334e1ca539190bb5/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/material_objects.py) supports actual created/repaired/worn object provenance. Cultural mediation and source-qualified [relational_metabolism.py](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/d682db603f4c2916d68cfe30334e1ca539190bb5/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/relational_metabolism.py) include operatively changing recruitment. **First falsifier:** same committed object's physical provenance is conserved while Nadia, brother and visitor acquire materially grounded but different meanings. ## 4 — A PERSON CAN TYRANNICALLY RETCON HER OWN PAST WITHOUT CHANGING IT

**Source:** [SOROK 72](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/72_RECURSIVE_SELF_INTERPRETATION_VISIBILITY_AND_HORIZON_R1.md), [73](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/73_FRAMEWORK_REALITY_METABOLISM_SELF_RETCON_AND_CONTRADICTION_VISIBILITY_R1.md), **correcting** [74](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/74_V3_PFF_SDTF_AUTHORITY_CORRECTION_AND_SELF_RETCON_COMPOSITION_R1.md), Q-SELFREAD-001/Q-FRAMEMETAB-001.

**Originally present:** at 23 Nadia may sincerely understand a phase as liberation; at 29 reinterpret that whole history as foolish self-deception; at 35 experience both as incomplete. Those interpretations change **which older evidence becomes accessible** and what she can attempt, without deleting either actual life. A later adopted self-myth is not just narrative content: it can alter *routing of future recruitment*. A collision with reality can yield integration, appropriation, ignoring, compartmentalization, destabilization or no durable change. Critically SOROK 74 says the historical response labels are analytic patterns, **NOT a new operator enum or new framework owner**; “reality pressure” is no new fabricated causal occurrence.

**Targeted code comparison:** R27 [historical_self_organization.py](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/d682db603f4c2916d68cfe30334e1ca539190bb5/SEAM/ORACLE/UM_SOROK_ORGANISM_R3/um_sorok_organism/historical_self_organization.py) really provides actor-owned acquisition/revision and **pre-cue recruited action pressure**, while reflective_regulation, psychodynamics and relational_metabolism provide other functional first consumers. These are *already implemented in limited sourcefree form*. The specific multi-age, counterevidence-preserving, dynamically selected and potentially tyrannical self-myth sequence is not established by these inspected sources.

**Reciprocal theoretical consequence:** Nadia's later dismissal of her adolescent Western-aesthetic rebellion may reveal genuine naivety while suppressing real joy, freedom or creative power. Brother can preserve evidence that she no longer allows herself to see. No moral-development ladder: becoming more certain or more integrated can reduce actual contact with the Other and the world.

**First falsifier:** record past event A and its effects, subsequently a new interpretive framework B that recruits differently and changes action/attention; do not erase A or other actors' memories; a later contradiction may be accepted, defended against or left unresolved. Never synthesize a “reality pressure” event from analyst opinion.

## 5 — THE PORTAL'S REALIZED SPEECH IS NOT NECESSARILY WHAT THE VISITOR TYPED

**Source:** [Court 8C SOROK 22C](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/22C_COURT8_PORTAL_TRANSLATION_CONSTITUTION_R1.md), [correcting 22E zero-profile main mode](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/22E_COURT8_ZERO_PROFILE_MAIN_MODE_R1.md), H121/H122/H133, Q-LANG-001/Q-REALIZEDSAY-001/Q-ASSIM-001/Q-PORTALTRANS-001.

**Originally present:** the player remains psychologically themselves; the world can provide a thin, historically coherent local material position without demanding autobiographical character creation. In an *explicitly selected homologous-peer mode* an authored utterance involving an out-of-world coordinate may be consistently transformed according to a **pre-governed portal binding**, and the user must see what was actually said. The portal is **not a truth filter**: “I own a car” may be a lie heard by Nadia, not magically corrected to “I do not.” Language, dialect, media, register and code-switching are real-world limitations; model translation must not rewrite political content or fabricate the user's biography.

**Targeted code comparison:** [NEIGHBOR R2 visitor first consumer](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6e05792f29964dafa289b884664e9c892dc0e310/SEAM/NEIGHBORMULCH/neighbor_runtime.py) commits SAY and actor sensory cue candidates through the original CO ResearchLedger; it does **not** demonstrate a governed local-coordinate SAY translation/feedback first consumer. P6/MIII supply important embodied access, and cultural_mediation.py offers different actor development of cultural object meanings; neither is automatically a universal bilingual portal. Do not invent a new translation source without checking legacy portal owners.

**Reciprocal theoretical consequence:** the visitor's ideals, false claims and cultural ignorance cannot be translated away for convenience. Meanwhile the visitor also should not automatically inherit all the money, physical access, linguistic authority and passport privilege of their external real self. This is a field of materially unequal encounter and perspectival translation, not roleplay cosplay.

**First falsifier:** in one selected mode, user says a hometown/credential coordinate governed by a stable preexisting portal homology; the actual realized utterance is source-recorded and shown to user; actor may independently misunderstand. A separately uttered false claim passes as testimony rather than truth-policed source. No silent transformation of ideology or user past.

## Combined MULCH

These five aren't five standalone engines. They force us to rethink a single encounter:

A visitor glances at an old photograph while trying to seem interested in something else; Nadia's brother observes this partly, perhaps misreads the visitor; an object from the picture carries different invested value for mother and daughter; a historically real anonymous benefit or wrongdoing is attributed to the wrong person, changing the siblings' actual activities; later new evidence leads Nadia to retell the old period, perhaps tyrannically erasing its remembered joy; and a source-qualified portaled visitor has spoken into the social world through a particular linguistic and class position.

**Each operation is already described somewhere in original SOROK.** No screenplay is required to make the visitor encounter a world they cannot author or completely read. What is needed is the **actual connected first consumer**, preserving source ownership and the multiple actor-relative histories, rather than an LLM narrating a perfect imitation of these effects.

**Ranking criterion:** not mathematically the five least-known ideas in an unknowable exhaustive set; these are high-impact overlooked ideas *relative to this assistant's last R27 conversational/game synthesis and the sourcefree consumers selectively inspected*. Historical source law, provisional project theory, sourcefree tested code and native human science remain separately graded. Independently competent owner must decide whether the highest-priority join lies in one of these or a still-stronger original first consumer.
