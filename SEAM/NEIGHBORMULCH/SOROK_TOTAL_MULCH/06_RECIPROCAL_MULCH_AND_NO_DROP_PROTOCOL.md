## III. Scalable dialectical social field—provisional algorithmic direction

The core consists of Nadia plus her recurring materially important Others. Every person retains an independent action permission and actual world history. Each source-qualified event can be made available to a bounded relevant actor neighborhood, not every possible psyche. A semantic model may propose a few actor-specific interpretations and higher-order relationship reconfigurations; an owner verifies actual cue source, developmental lineage, existing actor history, contradictory alternatives and whether there is ANY resulting change. A candidate can fail or leave the person unchanged. When a relationship becomes causally important to daily activities, promote peripheral actor depth; the reverse is not deletion of that person's history.

Avoid unconstrained triplets of every person (quadratic/cubic blowup) by selecting events and currently practical relational motifs, not recomputing a complete network every clock tick. This is a sourcefree engineering strategy, **not** a scientifically calibrated computational complexity bound or universal psychology.

The crucial proof is that an actor's new construction can become operative in later world activity, without reading their whole graph, forcing a moral destination, or telling the visitor what the character truly feels. Care can reproduce control; defiance can coexist with love; an affair can re-signify previously sincere admiration without deleting the past.
