## VIII. Exact initial class peerhood exposes the difference between being equal and knowing it

**SAVAGE — cost bars cannot equal class.** The user's same-SES requirement is materially exact at the entrance, but a numerical percentage for two groceries cannot prove it. Relations to employment, productive control, obligations, debt, housing, family reproduction and effective practical burden determine what exact classed peer footing can mean. The real visitor brings an externally formed mind into a deliberately thin, locally coherent social position; the system must not manufacture a false biography or grant a professional qualification where NO_LAWFUL_HOMOLOGY applies. If equivalent footing is not source-admissible, the problem is one of lawful construction—not permission to fake equality through a UI.

**RECONSTITUTE — initial equality can become materially unequal, and the actors need not notice.** Only the visitor's own YOUR RESOURCES projection may be shown at a purchase. A rent change, lost shift, inherited obligation or gift can diverge the positions. **Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “The real human is situated as an exact peer in cost, but not an omniscient resident of Nadia's history”):**

At entry the local visitor occupies **the very same SES as Nadia**—not a vague near-class neighbor, and not a secretly wealthy tourist. Nadia's own balance, available resources, obligations and sacrifice are not automatically visible. Only their own resources may appear in the UI, perhaps as a briefly foregrounded YOUR RESOURCES bar when they offer to pay for takeout. Identical initial effective burden does not erase different social, gendered, bodily or family relations. The initial equal burden does **not** require permanent synchronized wallets. Jobs, pay, gifts, unemployment, care obligations, shifts, money and debts can change one person's situation independently. A person who finds a bill easy may not notice another's pain; a formerly equal peer may become embarrassed by a newly unequal expense. The system cannot flash a special compassion lesson saying “Nadia is now poorer,” and a politeness cue is not a secret account disclosure.

SES homology is exact as a **product constraint at entry**, but SES itself is multidimensional. A defensible existing-world implementation must decide which properties, purchase burden, work, housing, class relations and resource access are actually matched without rewriting Nadia's historical household or inventing an adult user autobiography. The old 18% takeout figure is an illustrative resources-bar display, not a settled denominator. Conversely, identical purchase burden cannot prove every legal, racial, gendered, bodily or kinship experience matches.

The user must know what local utterance was realized when a meaningful translation occurred; Nadia's interpretation remains actor-local and fallible.

## IX. SAY, DO and ATTEND are constrained physical participation, not prompts that author success

**SAVAGE — a linguistic sentence is not a settled act.** “I unobtrusively inspect her bill while talking about Rome” is an attempted combination of speech, gaze and finite attention. The bill may be illegible. The visitor's body may be visible. Nadia may be busy, a child may interrupt, the clock may advance and a bus may be missed. A free-text user cannot make a repair successful by wording, narrate another's consent, unlock a room through social optimism or turn every attention request into hidden lore. **Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “SAY, DO and ATTEND are ways of acting in one world—not separate magical verbs”):**

The human must really reason, notice, explain, ask, negotiate, joke, bluff, withhold and decide. “I solve the homework” should not instantly make the child understand; “I repair the toilet” should not bypass practical access, time and skill; “I comfort her” should not legislate Nadia's reception. A broad intent can expose material affordances and return control at salient forks, while routine tasks can be compressed when no meaningful decision is being suppressed. Physical capability depends on the assigned local shell and world, while language and reasoning in the text medium can actually be performed by the external human.

ATTEND is not a library lookup that summons lore. It consumes finite attention under actual scene and temporal conditions. The world can offer ambient orientation and progressively more detail about what this visitor actually looks at, hears and historically knows how to recognize. Looking longer does not increase an object's hidden symbolism or force Nadia to reveal her psyche. A receipt can be blurry; a photograph can be familiar without meaning anything remarkable; a refrigerator magnet can remain ordinary.

The world-realized utterance must be visible to the user when translation occurs; a false ordinary statement remains a claim, not an automatically corrected fact. The visitor cannot conjure tool access, job credentials or another person's consent through prose.

SAY, DO and ATTEND are embodied, simultaneous or interrupted attempts. Looking at a bill while discussing Rome takes attention, gaze position, divided cognition and actual time. An overloaded narrative command cannot guarantee a repair or babysitting success. Source-qualified viewer exposure, actor-relative observation and the single objective world writer are separate. The whole game needs the ability to make the observer's gaze socially visible without converting ATTEND into an all-access knowledge API.

**The visitor is also an object of others' perception.** This is the earlier separated theory's challenge the earlier separate model treated too lightly. The visitor's body has a position, gaze direction, hands, timing, audible words, contact with objects and possible cover behaviors. Nadia may be cooking, occupied with a child, facing away or watching. A quiet glance may be visible; a spoken distraction may fail to conceal it. What Nadia *could* sense differs from what she actually attended to; what she attended to differs from her interpretation; what she infers about what the visitor knows differs from the visitor's real knowledge. The visitor may also misread Nadia's attention. No internal user ATTEND record automatically grants Nadia surveillance of the visitor's private screen, and no actor's suspicion is a privileged narrator fact.

Some layers can be empty. An observed glance does not imply legible document content. A received speech signal does not imply lexical comprehension. A character's expression is not necessarily proof of inner state.

This must be generated using existing P6/MII/MIII observation distinctions, original historical ATTEND, the CO actor-world ledger, and NEIGHBOR #1274's sourcefree one-writer runtime where joined and authorized. The false alternative is a side-channel “suspicion meter” that directly applies relationship penalties. The other false alternative is to call a single narrator and have it write all perceptions, beliefs and objective consequences as beautiful coherent prose.

## X. False reciprocal interpretation must become real cause without becoming truth

**SAVAGE — two beliefs are not a simulated relation if nothing changes.** Nadia may believe the visitor saw an overdue amount, even if the words were unreadable. The visitor may think her curt reply means she caught them even if she was responding to a phone call. A sibling could take the same interaction as insult or generosity. Neither a narrator's plausible reconstruction nor shared interface analytics can tell either party what the other truly knew. Their mistaken accounts may endure, be revised, conflict, generate privacy decisions, new invitations or practical refusal, or remain inert.

**The deeper mechanism:** the relation is *not* one objective fact plus two little opinion stickers. Actor-specific inferences are learned organizations that can select future activity, sometimes because social institutions enforce even a false reputation. A child's judgment about the mother's view of father may change the daughter's work and affection. The execution court must preserve separate objective cause, actor evidence, meaning, third-party view and future material settlement. Any invented universal mutual-suspicion score or cinematic confession as obligatory closure would be a regression.

**Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “The two people can be mutually wrong, and their wrongness can become history”):**

Take the dinner bill. It is an objective material artifact with a price, perhaps an unpaid claim, a time, a location and some history. The human glances quickly but cannot read it. Nadia sees the head movement from the kitchen and believes the human knows the amount. That belief might matter because of what she learned through prior experiences of financial shame, generosity or invasion of privacy. She becomes briefly curt. The human believes the curt reply means she noticed the attempted glance. In reality, perhaps she was already irritated by a phone call. They each act on evidence that is not identical and may be false.

The program must not adjudicate their errors into one accepted “true meaning” to improve the story. The objective occurrence remains: a body moved, eyes pointed, no exact amount was read, a reply occurred, a child was waiting, the meal still cost money. Nadia's belief that the human read the amount is **actor-owned subjective history**, not a world fact. The human's belief that she caught them is **user history**, not automatically a simulated hidden diagnosis. Each can become a condition of later practical conduct. If Nadia decides not to leave bills on the table, that is a new world action with objective consequences whose cause includes her possibly false belief. If the human becomes excessively apologetic, Nadia may interpret that as confirmation or have no idea why.

The central epistemic problem is not simply that Nadia may be mistaken about the visitor. She may incorrectly believe they read a bill, while the visitor incorrectly believes she noticed their attempt, while her irritation came from another independent cause. These beliefs-about-beliefs can change action, invitation, concealment, apology or silence and thus produce historical effects while neither belief is factually correct. A third participant may add another false attribution that an institution enforces.

Original H053–H062 already recognized the observer-as-observed. MII and MIII have separate rightful sensory layers; NEIGHBOR R2 has a bounded visitor/world read. The missing joined owner test is cover SAY plus physical ATTEND, partial user legibility, differential coactor sensory evidence, separately sourced false inference, later independent action or NO_CHANGE and cold-replayable provenance with no private leak or double writer. RC-1–RC-6 remain operational in 05, not a new permanent ticket file.

This is a deeper construction than adding more confidence fields to “NPC knows X.” What matters is the **provenance and practical recruitment of a belief about another's belief** under history: what evidence was physically available; who actually received it; which acquired expectation made the inference plausible to them; how that inference was or was not taken up in action; what independent Others then did; and what new actual experience followed. The system must be able to return **NO_CHANGE**, indifference, an unexpressed suspicion or a later revision without treating every scene as a psychological breakthrough.

A third actor may further transform the field. A sibling may believe the visitor was insulting, though neither principal perceived that. A mother may interpret the same dinner as generosity. Their actions can change Nadia's options without anybody discovering the original event's complete truth. Institutions may enforce a false report or a family reputation while the underlying bill remains unpaid. Historical causal effectiveness never makes false belief factually true.

This is the first **stronger operative demand** revealed by the external challenge: a joined event consumer in which the world and several situated interpretations evolve under one real chronology. If a sourcefree demo has two private character monologues but no later change in actual possible activity, it has not realized this demand.

## XI. Consequences do not owe anyone recognition, fairness or closure

**SAVAGE — the game can be visitor-centered without displaying an affinity bar.** If helping someone automatically produces thanks, intimacy or moral progress, the deeper optimization survives unseen. Actual anonymous care can benefit a person who never learns the donor. A hidden theft can deprive a household while an innocent sibling is blamed; later testimony may change attribution without changing the original cause. A successful manipulator may become closer to somebody while increasing their dependency; a respectful visitor may be refused. A person may feel genuine love while reproducing oppression.

**RECONSTITUTE — outcomes in material and historical rights.** Actor intention, physical action, cause, third-party effect, perceived event, remembered event, institutional enforcement, explicit acknowledgement and psychic reconstitution have different authorities. Recognition must not be silently backfilled because the narrative needs closure, and a new orientation may be acquired from a mistaken association without the engine asserting that association is true. This is the non-moralistic practical meaning of care: action has consequences beyond the limits of what the giver can know.

**Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “Help, injury, gratitude and blame are not interchangeable forms of consequence”):**

The most visitor-centered game would make assistance raise closeness and harm lower it. Removing the displayed affinity bar does not repair the deeper error if the hidden system still uses the other's approval as its outcome function. A real act can help somebody who never learns who helped. A kindly offered repair can damage the toilet. A visitor may steal an object without being detected and somebody else may be blamed. Later confession may change another person's model of the event but cannot change who actually took it. A child can benefit from practical tutoring while the parent dislikes the tutor; a parent may love the visitor but be harmed by them.

The separate layers are intent, admissible undertaking, world consequence, actor-local exposure, interpretation, institutional attribution, later activity, and the actual affected material position. Gratitude, guilt, perceived sincerity, law or relationship access cannot replace them. A dishonest person may enjoy temporary success. An offended person may still choose to invite somebody back. The program must not invent suffering as a lesson to the abuser, nor does it owe the abuser privileged access to another person's private consequences. A victim may love an exploitative arrangement, resist, be uncertain, seek support or experience independent conflict without a standardized moral trajectory.

Real care may benefit an Other who never learns its source. An unseen act of harm can be attributed to a person who was not there and change family labor, trust or access. Later evidence can revise the story but cannot rewrite which person committed the original act. An outwardly successful manipulator may receive affection while harming another person. No universal moral reckoning is guaranteed by a simulated reality that respects human uncertainty.

Actual event, intended outcome, materially caused change, actor exposure, actor attribution, third-party testimony, institutional judgment, outward gratitude, and later practical consequence are separate owner rights. Original H087–H099 and H140–H163 anticipate cases of anonymous help, hidden theft, false blame, repair and uncertain credit; they are preserved as exact first-consumer cases in 05. A model that translates consequences into automatic relationship increases would not be doing materialist psychology, regardless of whether it hides the score.

People may become closer through shared meaningful practice, playful ease, desire or practical support. They may become less interested because of busy lives, changed projects, irritation, incompatibility, a new relationship or nothing special. An exceptional interaction can have no durable effect, while slow work, care, humiliation and mastery change a person. **The architecture permits these outcomes; it does not optimize toward any of them.** SORACLE-CO's anti-visitor-centered challenge does not authorize an independence-reward function.

The actual game is not “earn Nadia's trust so you may finally learn her secret.” It is wanting to participate in another life without obtaining a guarantee that one's participation will be interpreted rightly or rewarded. Historical agency includes causes one fails to recognize. A robust world can surprise the narrator as well as the user because its material and actor-local constraints deny an easy interpretation.

Her present politics can be informed by verified public events only to the extent that her material conditions and channels make those events available. Real events do not automatically produce shared opinions or scripted argument.

A phone number cannot be granted just because the visitor wants to text; a message cannot arrive because the platform seeks retention; the person may refuse, delay, forget, initiate, misunderstand or remain silent. A thread may contain details the other person later recalls or misrecalls in person.

Home and messaging are **two different apertures on the same life**, not two Nadia bots. The human may knock on a household door or contact her only through a socially and technically lawful channel: an exchanged number, legitimate referral, or established coordination. A first meeting does not grant instant text availability. A message has distinct sending, network delivery, actual receipt, recognition, actor interpretation, response and later material afterlife; one of those stages can fail without a narrator inventing a new psychological reason.

Meanwhile ordinary-life clocks continue through work, caregiving, siblings, a guest arriving, a phone call, hunger or missed transport. An elastic real-world rendezvous may preserve a pending event without forcing the user to appear at a precise wall-clock minute; an optional stricter synchrony preference is not a moral advantage or source of higher affection. The app cannot send consent or commitments on behalf of an absent human; an actually missed in-world promise may still produce actor-owned effects. If the human leaves, the world does not pause. The visitor does not thereby acquire an invisible autonomous emotional psyche while offline; established material schedules and costs may continue, but significant new promises, apologies, intimacy and volitional decisions belong to the actual human.

Time elasticity and non-toxic return remain essential. A fictional seven o'clock appointment need not require the real human to log in at an exact wall-clock instant; permissible grace, held causal dependencies, optional strict synchronization and no-show meaning require explicit world versus UI distinction. Nadia should know whether the in-world meeting occurred, not the service's app-grace window. Missing a genuinely made promise can matter; not opening the app is not automatically betrayal. The human may end the relationship, leave, delete qualifying private data or return under the applicable rights without manufactured guilt.

Ordinary work and economics remain material, not a compulsory second minigame. Rent, wage periods, transport and exhaustion can condition plans. No fictional crisis should force the real human to pay money to save a simulated companion or to unlock access that her history does not permit.

## XIII. The platform is an institution whose power can contradict the theory

**Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “Platform knowledge is not Nadia's knowledge, and user data is not a free natural resource”):**

The platform can build a rich longitudinal, evidence-qualified model of how the actual human attends, speaks, reasons and acts. This is not forbidden merely because the user is human. Purpose-limited records, source-separated user analytics, permissions, accurate realization, access/export/deletion rights and refusal to target private vulnerability for retention are causal constitution, not a code-of-conduct paragraph added after the psychological story.

This condition makes **user privacy a material product right**, not an etiquette suggestion. Purpose-bound records, permissions, meaningful disclosure, export, deletion, retention limits, research separation and the ability to stop play all matter. A service cannot teach nonpossessive relations by converting the actual human's uncertainty, vulnerability and attention into a covert commercial asset. Nor can it infer the permission to reuse user data from how emotionally important a fictional person feels.

The platform itself is a social institution with power over sampling, model costs, character access, notification, time cadence, moderation, generation and wealth of source material. Its laws can contradict its professed theory. “The engine doesn't maximize intimacy” does not prove neutral event sampling; favoring an opener that appeals to a user's personality can turn Nadia's life into an engagement lure without any visible affection score. A faithful system makes its assumptions and boundaries auditable rather than letting “immersion” justify hidden capture.

A synthetic persona is a historical simulation, not a proven conscious person. The actual human's feelings are real. That asymmetry does not authorize the service to stage the fictional person's grief or injury for exploitative retention. The fictional world's causal integrity and the real person's data rights are separate forms of accountability.

**Inherited material, re-read under this stronger causal claim (formerly “A scarce Roll, a real human invitation, and a shareable historical origin are three different powers”):**

## XV. The game is what historical participation feels like, not the cause of another life

**SAVAGE — move the visitor's pleasure after the person's becoming.** The prior theory still opened with the game even while denying that the game created Nadia. This reorder deliberately begins with the material world and ends with the visitor's experience. The visitor may come for an unchosen particular Other whose life was not created to reward interest.
