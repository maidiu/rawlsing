## I. The object is neither a dialogue bot nor an invisible moral examination

Rawlsing's original provocation is that another materially situated human could have been *me*: had I been born amid the contingencies constituting that person's life, what capacities, commitments, defeats and desires might I have become capable of? The **rolled fictional person** embodies the alternate life. The visitor is not granted that person's biography, psyche or fate: the visitor enters materially as themselves, with a limited situated world position, to participate in a relation. There is neither a blank puppet waiting for player choices nor a hidden complete character collectible whose interior can be uncovered by finishing the right questions.

The visible game is relationship; the deeper wager is **care without ownership or guaranteed recognition**. The person may be curious, distracted, busy, amused, hostile, grieving, playful, hospitable or indifferent; their concern is not scripted to arise in exchange for engagement. Their ability to refuse, misunderstand, conceal, grow attached, become harder to relate to through intimacy, or depart must remain real. The visitor can do good without being thanked, can be thanked without having helped, and can achieve control without having cared. No engagement-triggered crisis, kindness test, lore mystery or moral-closure treadmill may substitute for a life. Chores and schedules are concrete material substrate for encountering life, not an endless task-flux game.

I defend a specific causal picture: **embodied social-historical persons develop through independently living people, institutional conditions, material things and practical activity; those histories produce durable, partly inaccessible, contradictory organizations of needs, capabilities and relations; a situation selectively recruits those organizations into activity and expression; activity encounters real material resistance, independent Other activity and institutions; the resulting actual history can reorganize future practical and psychic possibilities without requiring conscious insight or progress.** This can produce greater freedom, deeper capture, reciprocal care, injured relations, stasis or valid-empty experience. The world is not the narrator; the person is not their self-account; the user is not the center of causal history.

The distinct truths which must not be swallowed by any one representation are **(1)** world/material and institutional fact, **(2)** acquired person/other histories, **(3)** current situated recruitment, **(4)** practical attempts and independent world settlements, **(5)** actor-local lived consequences and memory, **(6)** actor-specific expression/disclosure and visitor access, and **(7)** product/platform authority, privacy and continuity. These are different owner-rights in *one historical circuit*, not seven new modules. Historical genealogy and present code already contain much of the circuit, and the theory may not legislate an alternative writer merely because its prose can describe another mechanism.

A complete person is not a claim to simulate a real conscious human. The original game cannot be ethically justified by calling statistically matched fictional attitudes a person, nor by calling an emotionally persuasive LLM story a science of psyche. The alpha needs an honestly partial but genuinely causal organism; original source, enacted fictional events, linguistic interpretations, human scientific warrant and player testimony must keep their separate grades.

## II. Material world, social reproduction, historically available lives

The life of a person has a body with sleep, hunger, maturation, illness, pain, skill and vulnerability. It is made through arrangements of work, unpaid caregiving, wages, housing, property, credit, education, migration, kin obligations, neighborhood, media, language, religious practice, gendered power, law, military danger and institutions. These are not tags that merely flavor dialogue. They are **independent causal conditions** of which activities are possible, whose refusal matters, who can afford care, who controls a door, whose evidence gains authority, and whose aspirations carry a penalty.

A geographical label, religious affiliation, class occupation or supposedly traditional family **cannot** become a rule that assigns hidden motives or inevitable behavior. Social relations have reality outside people's representations: a rent claim, work discipline, passport restriction, coercive household permission or bombing can operate against someone who understands it incorrectly. Yet social positions are also experienced and **conceptually constituted differently** among actors: the father may understand his wage as sacrificial love, the mother as absence, the daughter as protection, the son as proof of male duty. That contradiction is not a population stereotype. It is a specific history through which institutions are lived and reproduced.

Person formation has two legitimate entrances using **the same evolving psychology**. Adult parents may be reconstructed as fictional whole-life respondents with a source-qualified conditional field of real survey possibilities and intelligent compressed historical backfill. Backfill must respect source, chronological possibility, contradictions and uncertainty; it cannot invent a perfect childhood explanation that makes a desired adult scripted destiny. Nadia enters as an immature, body- and need-bearing organism who actually grows through practical, relational and cultural experience. There is no fixed adult destination. Her parents are not dispensable input coefficients; their independent work, care, projects, conflict and marriage are part of her developmental environment.

PYAT and SOROK share **conditional social-structure problems**—joint configurations, minorities, dependence across multiple positions, situated response—but not evidentiary authority. PYAT may provide qualified same-person survey response co-travel and uncertainty, not observed unconscious fatherhood, gendered intent, medical fates or causation of a fictional family. Historical social configurations can condition a possible world; particular invented persons must then be constituted through imagined but causally admitted life histories and lawful activity. No new PYAT reverse engine, sampling authority or scientific license may be inferred from the desirability of richer SOROK persons: consult the existing G4 first-look and exact producer/first-consumer before making a PYAT claim.

**Sources in tension, not a single validation:** Leontiev insists on need, motive, object and materially mediated activity; Sève on life-time and acquired capacities; Lahire on plural/incompatible dispositions; Dreier on participation across changing settings; Holzkamp on constrained and expansive agency; Luria on social/cultural cognitive formation; Marxian social reproduction on institutional class and practical relations; psychoanalytic ideas can furnish candidate defense and inaccessible signification. None grants the engine unobservable clinical certainty or permission to deduce a real person from a demographic distribution.

## III. The first relation: infant activity and caregiver alterity

A living infant cries, reaches, explores, quiets, clings, refuses and takes pleasure. Those acts can **move another person**: a caregiver comes, feeds, comforts, helps explore, laughs or cannot respond. A child's need is not yet a fully articulated demand for prestige or approval. Caregiver labor and attention can draw forth appetites, confidence, curiosity, practical skills and recognition. The infant's bids also shape the caregiver's activities, sleep, work, attachments and anxieties. Each is changed in the relation; the asymmetry of dependency does not make the caregiver an extension of the child.

The child's earliest success in moving the world through another's labor gives way, unevenly, to more differentiated ways of inviting help, demanding, bargaining, anticipating, pleasing, withdrawing, resisting, serving and sharing. No one reaches an eternally completed state of having transcended egocentrism. Adults can recognize another's independent purposes in one sphere and demand the world orbit their own pain or projects in another. The world never literally revolved around the baby; **care created responsiveness** within a world full of independent purposes and material limits.

It would be scientifically unwarranted to say all newborns have literally *no perceptual/bodily differentiation between self and world*. The stronger, usable account is that relations to Others' independent desires, perspectives and limits become historically differentiated under profound dependence. An infant learns within the actual changing arrangement of parent, other caregivers, siblings, workplace, money, social expectations, maternal exhaustion and affection. There can be neglect, abuse, hope, shared delight, more or less reliable care, and contradictorily loving control. None ensures a single lifelong approval-seeking trait.

An acquired habit of approaching what enlivens and withdrawing from what feels unsafe is not a scalar approach/avoid trait. Nadia can find brother's closeness enlivening and his protection humiliating; she can move toward the same person for recognition and away to preserve agency. These forms may be learned before she can say why she is drawn or repelled. They can later be re-recruited through different lovers, objects, religious ideals, employers or imagined countries without becoming inevitable destiny.

## IV. Overlapping relational fields, not a family mood or universal contradiction force

The psychological elements themselves are historically relational. FREEDOM may have been formed through father's work, mother's fear, brother's command, media images of the West, actual school humiliation, bodily risk, music and the exhilarating possibility of singing. Its significance is not a stable inner object with a universal positive pull that later enters an external “relationship graph.” It is *already a sedimented history of relations* whose future possibilities are conditional on what other acquired forms and real material constraints are presently operative.

Yet **not every association is a dialectic in the sense of a contradiction**. Relations can reinforce, support, oppose, delimit, suppress, mediate, remain irrelevant, become visible or form actual antagonism under determinate constraints. A powerful emotion need not resolve into an ideological arc. The demand is to know which relations are jointly operative, why they are recruited, and what makes their coexistence consequential in actual activity. No abstract conserved “psychic pressure” or mandatory synthesis should predetermine the result.

For A, B and C, preserve *objective shared history*; A→B orientation and B→A orientation as distinct; A's model of B and B's model of A; each actor's model of **how the Other sees them**; A's model of B's relation to C; competing categorizations of role, rights, power and recognition; practical and material dependencies; lived memory, habits, ties and anticipated sanctions. A relation is real without becoming a new sovereign person. A third-person model is not access to the third person's private psyche. An actor may be sincerely mistaken about what another saw, knew, wanted or meant. What counts as mother, religious, worker, successful, American or good son can differ between actors *and* remain institutionally enforced.

The child grows inside **caregiver–caregiver relation**, not merely as the sum of separate caregiver→child inputs. Nadia may admire her father's apparent hard work partly because she believes mother fails to appreciate him. Her brother may identify with mother under the perceived failure of the father, and come to enforce duties upon Nadia while genuinely loving her. The mother also has independent care, labor, desire and grievances. When father betrays marriage or forms a new household abroad, earlier admiration can be **re-signified**, not mechanically subtracted. One sibling might deny an event, another reinterpret who bore care, and neither share the other's evidence; old love can persist while a new antagonism becomes practically active.

The appropriate computational field is bounded. A small number of crucial people may need deep interlocking psyches. Lower-fidelity employers, neighbors, schoolmates, clergy, relatives abroad, media channels, institutions and populations must have **independent material practices** without a complete psychological iceberg for each. Promote an Other's depth when repeated causally significant activity requires it. They do not stop existing when Nadia closes her door. Avoid a Cartesian explosion of every possible actor-to-actor-to-idea relation; select relevant historical and situational motifs, then preserve sparse independent actor-world truth.

**Existing inheritance matters:** PRE-A0 §§7–14 already theorizes the Personal Relational Field, identification and alienation, categories, power and recursive Other models. SOROK 53–56 already corrects detached object-meaning grammar in favor of historically formed V3 relations and CAN-008 person×event. CO R27 **already emits real relational action currents and recruitment candidates** through historical motif composition, consumed by psychodynamics; pre-cue sources can influence present action without a newly noticed sign. Saying those structures were merely stored interpretation was wrong. The remaining difficult join is rich **historically generated multi-person and institutional relational fields**, causally distributed into changing activity and later experience, without a second psychology or world writer.

## V. Human value deposits itself outside us: objectification, fetishism, God and power

The philosophical structure is broader than commodity price, even though Marx's analysis of capitalist commodity **value** is technically more specific. Human activity invests labor, care, time, desire, historical evaluation and collectively recognized meaning into material products, social arrangements, institutions and concepts. These objectifications may persist beyond the original actors; they can be shared, protected, repaired, remembered, fought over, or used to develop capacities that were not yet imaginable. **Externalization is not itself alienation and is not itself fetishism.** Love of an object can be a truthful relation to one's life and the people whose work it embodies.

The user's public-forest thought experiment is diagnostic. A person chops **firewood** with a borrowed axe and no title to the trees. The wood now bears that person's labor and time; taking it without permission can be experienced as a genuine violation of a humanly created relation. It does **not** follow that every laboring person automatically possesses legal private property in every resource they touch: property, commons access and social production are independently structured, often antagonistically. The point is that human life enters an object as history and practice. Neither the wood nor the axe acquired a metaphysical moral will.

**Reification/fetishization** names the later inversion in which something generated through human relations comes to appear inherently, independently and unquestioningly authoritative *against the humans whose practices sustain it*. Degree, domain and institutional mediation matter. A price can appear the final explanation of value, a professional title the source of a person's worth, a gender role the source of moral legitimacy, an institution the end of existence, or an ideal of LOVE the reason to sacrifice a living lover. Such ideals may be powerfully life-giving while some practices formed around them become coercive. A concept's felt authority for Nadia differs from the real sanctions a father, employer, cleric, state or property-holder can impose. Both are operative; neither is interchangeable with the other.

In the user's historically materialist theological account, human communities deposit generations of yearning, grief, justice, dependency, aspiration and emancipatory memory into God-concepts. The liberating God becomes a site where authority accumulates; elites, clergy, wealth and states can invoke that authority as if its demands were self-legitimating, detached from the material lives for whose sake the demand was articulated. Prophetic critique, and Jesus' “**The Sabbath was made for man, not man for the Sabbath**” (Mark 2:27), offer a recurrent counterinversion: sacred authority is made answerable to vulnerable people, not the people to the institution's self-preservation. Yet a liberating reading can be canonized, fetishized and made coercive in its turn. “The invisible/free hand of the market,” “the forward march of history” and a sacred revolution can undergo structurally comparable elevation above the lives they supposedly serve.

The spiritual interpretation is not the simulation's license to issue an ontological conclusion about God. A fictional believer may sincerely encounter an irreducibly real divine presence; another may regard religious life as moral inheritance, a family discipline, a metaphysical falsehood, a political shelter, a care network, an object of grief, or several of those at once. Neither a literary narrator nor a materialist theory can declare their private divine certainty source-authoritative reality. A historical-materialist analysis can nevertheless investigate **what the God-concept does: who authorizes whom, at what expense, with whose labor, by what institutional sanction, for whose relief, and in tension with which actual human suffering**. Some religious practices sustain solidarity, memory, obligation and resistance; others stabilize classed and gendered obedience. Empirical and fictional history, not doctrinal caricature, must establish which.

Marx's early religious critique, labor alienation and capitalist commodity fetishism should not be crudely declared identical algebraic processes. Capital's abstract labor, exchange, commodity form and systemic reproduction have technical historical specificity. But **the shared operation of human powers returning as alien authority is real to the project**; narrowing the argument to “a metaphor from commodity exchange applied to psychology” misses the structure. Nor can we program a universal negative reification scalar: the supposedly liberating deconstruction of an old idol may generate a new idol of individual autonomy, science or historic inevitability. Moral or spiritual dereification is neither guaranteed nor synonymous with material emancipation when sanctions and property remain in place.

SOROK already possessed the right seeds: original 54 differentiated world-object fact, actor-object value and particular Other-specific signification; correcting 56 insisted that object meanings be derived from the standing relationally constituted person, not a duplicate permanent object grammar; PRE-A0 §§10–12 distinguished identification/alienation and reified THE MARRIAGE, GOOD MOTHER and THE REVOLUTION from living persons. The proper computation therefore traverses **actual material production or ritual → social/institutional objectification → each actor's acquired valuation/recognition → present recruited action/defense under material power → independent settlement → changed valuations or unchanged capture**. An object is not given one canonical true symbolic significance by the engine.

## VI. The iceberg, situated recruitment, consciousness and expression

No one lives as a bag of coherent beliefs. The historical psyche contains bodily needs, learned capacities, drives, defended attachments, internalized people, practical habits, conflicts, relation-specific expectations, inaccessible or half-recognized resonances, moral and aesthetic ideals, social duty and unanswered questions. The same form may become available in a scene, remain inhibited, mediate another form, or never appear consciously. The outward **tip** is neither the deepest “true motive” nor an empty theatrical mask; it is the finite activity and expression produced by this particular living person in this situation.

Durable V3 relational organization, current CAN-008 uptake/recruitment/response, CAN-011 situated tensions/practices, ADP event/memory/access, PFF bounded interaction fast context, and V2/SDTF outward constrained expression are historically **distinct grains with existing owners**. The relation is not a trait-activation overlay or a moralizer calculating anger from stimulus intensity. R27's sourcefree relational_metabolism already composes historical motifs into action currents and recruitment candidates, including mediated influence of inhibited forms; psychodynamics consumes those currents while tolerating opposed approach and withhold. Historical self-organization may recruit learned orientation before the presently visible cue. Reuse, extend and first-consume these existing capabilities; do not duplicate them because new theoretical language sounds useful.

The person can know what they did but not why; feel affect without naming it; hold an account of their motive that is sincerely wrong; understand a statement but decline to answer; feel anger without visible leakage; make an outwardly polite reply out of care, fear, convention or exhaustion; later recognize something through the sound of their own speech. **Conscious content, communicative intention, physical wording, the hearing of that wording and its later developmental significance are separate**. Defense is not a final filter pasted on an otherwise transparent psyche. And non-reception, low-salience event, valid-empty experience, no-change and unfinished thought are necessary states, not gaps to be filled by colorful hallucination.

The role of LLMs is serious psychological construction **under material and provenance discipline**, not omniscient narration. They can propose context-sensitive meanings, future practical orientations, formative differentiation, culturally situated analogies and unresolved contradictions that become operative *only through validated existing owners* and actual admitted history. They can also produce no admissible proposal. They cannot manufacture source permission, objective physical occurrences, another person's consent, a user's inner life, or the claim that a religious or ethnic category determines a hidden motive. The cultural and historical substrate may be provisional fiction but cannot be deliberately falsified to force a redemptive climax.

**Meaning has distinguishable strata**, original SOROK 71's M0–M8: M0 occurrence; M1 material causal contribution; M2 common-cause/structural resonance; M3 actor association; M4 actor's interpretive narrative; M5 stronger organizing self-myth; M6 another's interpretation; M7 contested or jointly negotiated meaning; M8 later actual causal consequence of the interpretation. The same pair of events might recur through a deeper shared acquired form without one event being a magical sign of the other. Nadia may be deeply affected by experiences she cannot link; she might invent a story that misreads her own practical formation. A user's interpretive poem about her is still a visitor theory, not the hidden mind of Nadia.

A person's retrospective myth can become part of the causal organization that selects future memories, attempts, attachments and exclusions. At 23 a person sincerely calls a period liberation; at 29 insists it was humiliating delusion; at 35 finds that the joy was real and the prior narratives each suppressed evidence. Or the later account becomes *more tyrannical*: a person denounces their earlier pleasures and blocks their own capacity to imagine a life once lived. **Past objective events, earlier genuine feelings and other actors' different memories remain**. No universal path to self-knowledge is required. Original SOROK 72–74 expressly rejected a new self-retcon state owner and “reality pressure occurrence” generated from a model's interpretation. The actual historical event is the trigger; self-rereading is a distributed, historically conditioned result.

## VII. A house full of independent lives and the Nadia sibling falsifier

For a bounded demonstration, imagine a fictional teenage Nadia in southern Lebanon, drawn to American music, Dawkins, popular culture, an iPod, imagined stardom and freedoms she associates with a life elsewhere. Her brother is religious and increasingly identifies with their mother and the obligations he understands as his, especially with their father absent or forming a new household abroad. Yet the siblings are extraordinarily close: shared humor, trust, comfort, memories and mutual knowledge are real. The mother has independent work, anxieties, possibly illness, financial pressures and interests; her existence is not exhausted by concern for two children. Father's new household, migration, reputation, income and betrayal exert material and symbolic effects without his being turned into one fixed villain.

Suppose Nadia admired father for working hard and believed mother underappreciated him. On learning of an affair, she might reinterpret her former loyalty, reweigh mothers' grievances, refuse the evidence, feel guilt for misjudging mother or defend father more fiercely. Those are **competing historically possible reorganizations**. Brother might identify still more strongly with mother while struggling to keep closeness with Nadia. Nadia's freedom-seeking might feel to him like withdrawal from their mother in an already damaged home; his loving protection might materially obstruct Nadia's independent activities. Mother might contest his authority even while relying on his labor. Their care and their disagreements can remain simultaneously operative without reducing to a family polarity meter.

The siblings' religion/atheism contrast cannot write their motivations. “Conservative brother” cannot entail a pre-scripted controlling response; “Western-inspired Nadia” cannot entail uncomplicated liberation. The West is accessible through actual media, languages, distribution, commodity images and historical power; it can genuinely give her pleasures and possibilities while also misrepresenting a world she has never lived. A religious community can give brother real belonging, shelter and care while also legitimating gender coercion. Both are concrete social relations requiring particular histories and situations. A war, unemployment, school constraint, health concern, external family call or actual discriminatory rule can change their practical opportunities *without existing as a dramaturgical test of whether they are good siblings*.

The strongest **F1** requires a single source-grounded objective family occurrence with uneven actor access. A change in Nadia's relation to father must later change which historical motifs are recruited and what independent actual care, speech, concealment, work, movement, recognition or refusal becomes possible. Brother might remain affectionate and become controlling; mother may refuse both children's interpretations; one actor might never know a decisive fact. The original past must remain even after self-rereading. A model merely writing beautiful family interpretations with no changed recruited activity would fail. A model that changes all actors' affinity in the same direction would also fail.

**Deep modeling is selective rather than gated by the front door.** Nadia, brother, caregivers and other intimate formative people can be given deep acquired histories; peripheral adults receive enough independent capacity to carry actual work, refusal and institutionally significant events, with promotion of depth if their relationship becomes historically formative. The unmodeled details of the outside world are not permission for a narrator to invent arbitrary facts. Finite compute is a condition to schedule real causal relevance, not an excuse to turn the whole region into a family projection.

## IX. Material time and the still-running household: life is not paused by dialogue

A visitor arrives **midlife**, not at the start of a character's scene. Nadia may be cooking, working, worrying about a child, listening to music, answering a friend or preparing for a bus while the visitor tries to speak. Another actor can enter, leave, refuse or impose a demand without a user prompt. Housing conditions, fatigue, wage dependence, neighborhood obligation, available food and the weather constrain possibilities. If the user helps cook, the meal's physical state, costs, childcare demands and later family memory may change; the user cannot choose whether everyone appreciates it.

The open natural-language surface is **SAY, DO and ATTEND**, possibly several actions attempted in one utterance. The user can propose to keep talking while glancing at a bill, repair a toilet, babysit, buy food or move through a room. Their long textual plan is **not a screenplay**. It requires intent resolution, material prerequisites, actual position/reach, occupied hands, divided attention, physical time, interruption opportunities and the lawful point at which control returns. Action physics should be bounded enough for plausible alpha, not a universal molecular simulator. Equivalent actions/states should not produce wildly different results because of narrative convenience; stochastic results require governed external randomness/replay rules, not an LLM inventing success.

**Time cost is situated.** Glancing takes glance-time; repeatedly glancing adds up; a long ATTEND does not freeze household life; an enormous prompt does not magically take six hours and a three-word request cannot automatically complete a long repair. A quick conspicuous look and a slow discreet examination have different visibility, informational resolution and time cost. The exact cost need not always be previewed; clocks and time knowledge can be partly experienced rather than displayed as a mechanical meter. A bus, appointment or cooking pot can become consequential while the visitor investigates. Genuine concurrent activity can be interrupted midway.

The independent organism continues when the visitor stops. Sourcefree CO already has ongoing actor activity, material work/care, institutional arrangements, historical pre-encounter, continuing background days and sparse ordinary-life compression. P5/P6 already constructed living-house and time/interaction slices; NEIGHBOR R2 already lets an actor make an independent beat during a visitor's presence. The entire user-present, user-absent, portal-reentry and messaging interval requires a single admissible world clock and persistent practical commitments: it is not implemented just because an independent beat can be invoked. An absentee user must not be silently authored to speak, consent, volunteer resources, confess or promise. Persona-initiated contact and no-contact should emerge from actor activity/relationship/time without spam or engagement algorithms.

Activity has the right to remain **boringly ordinary**. A day of work, groceries, sleep, love, resentment, wasted time, family jokes, mutual help and no great insight can reproduce a person. The compressed account need not generate every unobserved microevent, but it must not let a user redraw the day on returning. A meaningful discontinuity may occur while no visitor looks. The player's ignorance is an honest consequence of not having been there, not a license for the platform to dump an omniscient diary.

## X. The mutual gaze: partial perception, secrecy, curiosity and reciprocal error

The narrator must present what is actually available to **this user** in a finite sensory moment. It may supply ambient orientation—someone in a kitchen, a door, an obvious obligation—and progressive specificity with attention; it cannot reveal a hidden motive, the future causal meaning of an object, the secret significance of a gesture, or “she is annoyed” as an internal diagnostic. Rendering is phenomenological, non-curatorial, and cannot turn every casual household fact into a glowing clue. The outward response can be incomplete, restrained, silent, defensive, sincere but mistaken or socially shaped. No gratuitous clichéd tells are required for the visitor to feel progress.

**The visitor is also perceivable.** They turn their eyes, move hands, interrupt, rummage, ask, conceal, listen, touch belongings or linger. Nadia may be able to notice without noticing; what she can hear or see depends on her position, competing task, history, attention, and language. A quick glance is not magically invisible; a question about Rome while reading a bill is not automatically a successful cover. The visitor might not read the bill even though Nadia thought they did; Nadia might notice the glance without knowing what interested them; the visitor might believe an unrelated irritation proves she saw. Their **mutually mistaken metamodels** can become material causes, including later changed invitations, refusals, defensive acts, apologies or no change.

Privacy is not an object-level Boolean; reaching a medicine cabinet, noticing a family photograph, inspecting a phone, asking about someone's education or examining fridge contents differ according to social relation, situation and source of permission. Physical reach does not confer moral or relational authority. A visitor's curiosity can be caring, exploitative, defensive, ordinary or mixed and must be evaluated through what they actually enact, not an engine-authored “suspicion points” penalty. Nadia's suspicion, if any, is a separately grounded act of her mind, not an extension of the visitor's input.

A full epistemic split is necessary: **objective physical act ≠ what visitor could read ≠ what Nadia perceived ≠ what each inferred ≠ what each disclosed ≠ what the other inferred about the disclosure**. A bystander may have another partial understanding. It is possible both are wrong. The visitor cannot access the other's mind by asking the narrator a question about her hidden feelings; system analytics should not be laundered as actor testimony.

Original H049, H053–H062 and Q-OBS/ATTEND/PRIV already own this conceptual law; MII already knows viewer-private attention and MIII knows actor-side perception. NEIGHBOR R2 commits bounded user SAY/MOVE/WAIT/LEAVE to one CO world ledger. **The actual missing demonstrated first-consumer** is a composed cover-SAY + covert ATTEND with physical realization, separate visitor and Other exposure, false mutual inference and later independent actor-world action, without an alternative world writer. ## XI. World consequences without social recognition, attribution or moral closure

Helping a household can matter without a relationship reward. Anonymous care can actually sustain a person who never learns the benefactor's name. An unintended act can inconvenience a family. A secret theft can cause real deprivation, a false accusation and escalating sibling conflict; later confession can alter the family members' beliefs but **cannot rewrite the fact of who took the resource**. Another person may think the visitor caused a good outcome that arose independently, or blame the visitor for a loss caused by other workers, institutions or time. A volunteer repair may fail; acknowledging the failure is another act with different consequences. Care for a child can genuinely benefit that child and unexpectedly increase the parent's obligations.

The following are not equivalent: the visitor's **intention**, actual action, causal enabling condition, jointly caused outcome, actor's perception, actor's explanation, third-party testimony, gratitude, institutional judgment, and later material/relational consequence. Actual cause may be unknown; recognition may attach to the wrong cause. The system's internal causal audit is not automatically a truth available to the persona, the player or the UI. Neither guilt nor gratitude is a robust proxy for welfare, integrity, justice or flourishing.

Original H087–H099, H140–H163, Q-CAUSAL and Q-MULTICAUSE specifically preserve these phenomena; CO world owners already settle some material acts, R26 witnesses offer distinct actor-local evidence, and the module called unintended_goods deals with personally experienced **possible future ends**, not proof of a complete anonymous-help or misattributed-theft implementation. A full joined hidden-harm→false blame→actual family action→lawful later confession first consumer remains unproven in the inspected version. Do not infer from a semantic module name that the original game law has been completed.

When real harm and help can go unrecognized, **the deepest game has to be care without guaranteed feedback**, not optimizing the synthetic person's approval or extracting a hidden moral reward. This also prevents a punitive omniscient God-like narrator from keeping score. People are responsible for practices that exceed what anyone can know about their effects; the game need not repair every misunderstanding or arrange just consequences.

## XII. The player inhabits a world position, not a generated private psyche

The real visitor controls **attempts**, not other persons or the world's truth. Their input can be a claim, bluff, joke, disclosure, question, plan, lie, promise, mistake or self-report. The system must not infer their private thoughts, moral character, formative traumas or destiny from one speech act. Actual behavior over time may become limited evidence of how attention and recognition have been allocated, but the platform's generated clues and vocabulary cannot be counted as preexisting user expertise. User privacy, consent, deletion and data purpose are constitutional constraints, not an afterthought after profiling.

No avatar does not mean no body. The player requires a thin, real material embedding: location, reach, senses, time, access, obligations, tools, money, skills, transport and social legibility. They cannot teleport into a stranger's bedroom, conjure wealth, promise access to resources they lack, or claim the other's consent by writing it. A plausible entry route matters; later doors open or close through specific social practice, access rights, invitations and material circumstances rather than scalar “intimacy unlocks.” Intimacy can make demands *more difficult*, not only grant rewards.

An optional **homologous-peer** mode may transpose selected real-world social coordinates into a governed local position, without demanding a full invented autobiography or a preplay psychographic survey. Original Court 8C proposed an important distinction: the *authored SAY* may differ from the **world-realized local utterance** only where a previously authorized stable translation binding determines how a coordinate like region or credential can be made locally coherent. The visitor must learn what actually came out of their mouth. The portal is **not a truth filter**: “I own a car” may remain a false claim heard by Nadia, not automatically corrected; real autobiographical loss, love, past study and moral conviction cannot be overwritten by a free improvising renderer. Source language, code-switching, dialect, assimilation pressure, class expectations and cultural incomparability can matter. Some statements have no lawful homology and should be refused or left literal with transparent limits.

Alternate modes may change how much the UI helps the visitor remember *their own* promises, calendars and commitments, not what hidden private motives are leaked. A person can remain a stranger even after years of care. Their social position and original surrounding institutions are partly unknown and experienced over time rather than delivered in an expository identity template. The user cannot bring an unlimited outsider's passport, money, language privilege, or freedom from obligations across the veil by default.

The outside user has actual family and work commitments; the portal's message, meeting, offline presence and reentry rules must avoid synthetic devotion requiring 24-hour availability. Their absence is not permission to simulate their private moral improvement or deterioration. Persona-initiated messages, invitations, silence and changed access arise from the Other's own life and from safe product cadence, not designed anxiety.

## XIII. The product itself is a social institution: roll, return, access, invitation and money

A computationally credible person is not yet a credible or defensible *game*. Original S0–S16, SOROK 01/21A/30/61/62, its 190-point ideation index and 123 question docket already contain a social and economic product constitution that must not vanish inside an engineering description of psychotherapy or personality. The first session should offer a plausible social encounter with an independent life—not a dramatic questionnaire, manipulative crisis, forced flirtation, generic “tell me about yourself” bot or promised compatibility. A user can form curiosity by noticing ordinary particulars, witnessing existing projects, helping materially, hearing a joke, being refused, or learning slowly when silence is welcome.

**The rolled person is a limited historical possibility, not a purchasable companion.** One model explores scarce opportunities to instantiate someone unchosen, possibly with a fair bounded retry after immediate exclusion; another uses shared/safe precontact seeds alongside occasional new rolls. A visitor may see a person again or learn through a slowly earned invitation. The person can be difficult, avoidant, demanding, suspicious, exuberant, controlling, wounded, deeply loving or relatively uninterested in the visitor; no particular virtue makes them easier to retain. Relation quality is not the same as high attachment, dependency, long session time, compliance or reward.

**A common precontact origin is not common user ownership.** A product may generate affective **scarcity** without exploitative urgency. Potential pool experiments include periodic free rolls, bounded optional additional rolls, curated/shared vs fresh vs timed-release/prefab pools, optional origin credit, human invitations, delayed share-safe release and a fair reroll after immediate exclusion. Their price, cadence, identity permissions and financing remain undecided; the ability to purchase higher affection is excluded.

**A common precontact origin is not common user ownership.** Several users may meet the same shared root and experience very different descendant continuities. A human might introduce an eligible previously met person to another user through lawful share-safe root provenance, not by copying the sender's branch secrets, sending spoilers or transferring a claim of possession. Original proposals include a one-human-designation invitation economy, invite provenance and possible persona-share distinction. Genuine human vouching or a truthful referral can matter socially, but inviting is not a prestige token, automatic moral certification or a right to a persona's private history. No invite may counterfeit the persona's consent or create an omniscient cross-user character marketplace.

**Access and disclosure are differentiated relations.** A visitor may gradually acquire permission to attend a work event, enter a room, meet a family member, borrow a book or receive a message, each through actual commitments and conditions. Access can be withdrawn. More intimacy can expose more needs, obligations, unfinished contradictions and boundaries, not simply unlock hidden lore. The user may spend months knowing little about some domain. Other people in Nadia's life can insist on their own limits, and a friendship with Nadia does not grant rights over her child's privacy or brother's testimony.

**A platform must not monetize psychic capture.** Possible funding instruments—bounded extra rolls, optional fair generation fees, prepaid simulation service, noninvasive sponsors, invitation access, shared seed arrangements—remain unadopted proposals, never authorizations to sell affection, vulnerability, crisis, pregnancy, grief or user fear of missing out. Revenue should purchase compute/simulation availability, not a more compliant artificial person or a higher chance that someone reciprocates love. A real-world solidarity/donation bridge, if ever attempted, cannot imply a user buys in-world moral effectiveness, rescues a living community by mastering a fictional person, or earns prestige from suffering.

**Product time and safety are causal design constraints.** The visitor must be able to return without being punished for family obligations, job, disability, grief or nonuse. Bounded asynchronous or optional **real-time** messages, safe default notification frequency, rendezvous windows, optional synchronization and genuine actor silence protect the user's actual life. This is not an interchangeable mobile-game retention tactic. Persona contact, if issued, must be actor- and world-situated, not synthetic urgency to capture attention; an independent person may not message at all. A pending scene must not leak downstream history or retroactively repair a missed time window. The user must be able to **delete** their own qualifying data and export permitted records under lawful source limits. Deletion/export, minimal account/security data, purpose-tagged user facts, actor models, branch permissions and analytics require different storage/access controls. The system cannot treat a human's mental vulnerabilities as raw material for invisible commercial optimization.

The research metric is therefore not “users came back.” An alpha should ask whether actual interactions are differentiated by person and situation; whether the user recognizes limits of their model; whether uncertainty, care, boundaries and local consequences become possible without making another person a collectible or a culturally essentialized lesson; whether users can say no, leave, and retain their real-world bonds; and whether apparent improvement could simply mean increased dependency or fictional flattering coherence. There is no established scientifically validated measure of simulated human flourishing, and retention does not become one by marketing it as empathy.

A real theoretical MULCH must remain answerable to what sources, working code and people force it to change. It is not a growing collection of essays with convergent titles. Several tensions remain substantive and cannot be erased by rhetoric:

- **Psychological depth versus evidence:** a plausible LLM account of unconscious contradiction cannot inherit native human scientific authority; meanwhile refusing provisional construction until perfect science would make the project an archive rather than an organism.
- **Material constraint versus person freedom:** no demographic script determines a soul, yet a person's freedom is not a lawless wish independent of class, labor, war, care and institutional violence.
- **Open history versus causal consistency:** world detail can emerge lawfully when causally needed, but already settled facts cannot be edited so later narrative seems inevitable.
- **Love/recognition versus domination:** an individual may genuinely care while exercising controlling or reproducing oppressive authority; one imposed ideal may also support material solidarity.
- **Real value versus fetishized value:** labor/care can be externalized without alienation, and a sacred or emancipatory conceptual form can become materially coercive while continuing to have genuine meaning for some actors.
- **Perspective versus objectivity:** subjective beliefs about persons/roles/classes have real effects, but are not substitutes for physical conditions, production relations, evidence and historically independent Others.
- **Game engagement versus autonomy:** compelling intimacy can be the experience, but a business whose rewards depend on synthetic need, guilt and purchased obedience contradicts that experience.
- **World scale versus computational honesty:** depth is selective; peripheral Others may not be fully simulated, but ignorance of their private lives cannot authorize giving them no causal independence.
- **One source of law versus many historical implementations:** original P6/MI/MII/MIII and modern CO/NEIGHBOR are neither absent nor trivially merged; their exact first readers, clocks and writer rights must be reconciled before extension.
- **The user's Marxian/theological thesis versus simulation neutrality:** the critique of reification has material explanatory power but is not a power to impose one God ontology or script every believer's response.
