## B. Seven claims disentangled instead of made into one slogan

### B1. Every psychological element is relationally constituted—strong hypothesis

A representation of Love, West, Father, Freedom, Man, Mother, God or the Revolution is itself acquired through concrete activity, bodies, recognition, education, historical institutions and interpretation. It has a history of relations; it is not an atom and its future significance is not fixed. A second actor may give the same category different meaning and may mistake how the first actor understands it. The actor can orient not only to the second person, but to their view of that person's relation with a third person. PRE-A0 §13 already names the primitive directed models/metamodels; §14 names higher-order motifs and inherited relation-to-relation organization.

**Counterpoint:** Not every relation is a contradiction. Some are mutual support, weak association, indifference, causal dependence, failed recognition, actual institutional subordination or mutual constraint. Calling all connections dialectical only helps if the term specifies the *movement, actual constraints and possibility of transformation*. **Do not create an all-purpose “dialectic force” scalar.**

### B2. An idea/stratum can be invested, alienated, believed and materially effective—without being identical to class position

Different actors' ideas of their class, community, gender, parenthood, religion and political principles affect which experiences are salient and which activities become admissible or intolerable. Those representations may be externally produced and institutionally enforced: an identification can become a tool of oppression, including an actor enforcing a norm on loved ones. But objective class, property, state, household labor, medical cause and armed institutional relations do **not** cease to exist when no actor recognizes them. The model must keep material positions and legal/military/material constraints separate from each person's subjective category relation, other-model and spoken account.

### B3. Objectified value is not one scalar fetishization gradient

Original SOROK 54 has already distinguished **world object**, **actor-object value relation**, and **what sharing/using/withholding it means with this particular person**. Original SOROK 55–56 explicitly correct the risk of minting a new durable object-value grammar: derive present meaning from the existing relationally constituted person/world/event owner. Marx's commodity fetishism analyzes an *objective social form of labor and exchange* appearing as thing-properties, not merely private enthusiasm or psychological projection. Broader reification of love/identity/authority can be compared, but must not erase that political-economic specificity. “Fetishization” is not a universal pathology score: in one domain the actor may know the object’s labor/provenance yet act as though price/status exhausts it, while in another they may resist sale and in a third maintain mixed valuations.

### B4. The idea of love can both mediate and obstruct the living relationship

The concept of love is neither merely false nor a transparent preview of its living referent. An actor may discover **new capacities and incompatibilities** through actual loving practice. Existing conceptual identification can make the relationship possible and later render suffering invisible. A relationship can evolve into preserving the idea of THE MARRIAGE, GOOD MOTHER or pure REVOLUTION at the expense of actual people (PRE-A0 §12). Yet rupture can also become intensified idealization, not inevitable enlightenment; stasis, appropriation, confession, defense and contradiction are lawful. SOROK 71–74 already insists self-myth can change recruitment without changing the past and forbids a new special self-retcon owner.

### B5. Infant bids shape the caregiver as well as infant expectation

A living infant has bodily needs and agency before articulation or prestige/approval concepts. Bids, distress, reach, play, recognition and refusal meet **other persons who themselves have needs, exhaustion, work, care responsibilities, resources, obligations and possible refusals**. A child's successful cry may elicit the world through caregiver labor; the caregiver's world is itself being reshaped. The child progressively acquires ways to anticipate, invite, enlist, plead, negotiate, oppose and coexist with these independently living others. A repeated protective relationship can give the world a shape of safety; failure/violence can produce other differentiated coping and attraction/avoidance.

**Counterpoint:** “The child cannot originally differentiate self from world” is too absolute to assert as a universal developmental fact. Retain it as a metaphor for severe dependence and incomplete awareness of Others’ independent purposes, not as a proven absence of basic bodily/perceptual differentiation. Nor infer that maternal love automatically shapes moral freedom: caregiving includes reproduction, coercion, privilege, institutional strain, absence, contradiction and loss.

### B6. Attachment isn't an isolated pressure; becoming-other-to-the-world changes forms of recruitment

PRE-A0 §7 states *living movement precedes pressure*. Need/conation, recognition, love, power and actual blockage generate pressure; **flow** is the path-dependent routing of current living activity. An acquired historical relation can recruit an inhibited form indirectly; several mutually operative relations can produce opposed currents without forced resolution. The child's transition is not from original ego sovereignty to a final enlightened egoless state; it is an open-ended negotiation between projects and the independent world. Adults can still demand reality comply while also acknowledging its alterity in other domains.

### B7. Distinct Others and their conceptually mediated third-person fields are dynamically overlapping, but not an infinite omniscient graph

Source-qualified field representation, **analytical—not a new schema**:

- `W(t)`: owned objective bodies, material sites, practices, institutional distributions, external occurrences and clocks.
- `A_i(t)`: one actor's acquired, partly inaccessible historically constituted dispositions, practical powers and relationships.
- `R_i(j,t)`: i’s changing practical/model relation to actual j, not a copy of j’s hidden psyche.
- `R_i(R_j(k),t)`: bounded hypotheses about j’s relation to k, qualified by what i has actually witnessed/heard/lived.
- `K_i(s,t)`: i’s relation to a role/category/idea/“stratum” s, distinct from s’s objective material/institutional relations.
- `E_i(t)`: actually accessible sensory/activity event, not all of W(t).
- `C_i(t)`: situated composition/recruitment of historically formed relation motifs from the actor's finite current context.
- `P_i(t)`: actor-originated possible activity, independent of player command.
- `O(t)`: actually settled world effect; separately experienced, remembered, forgotten or interpreted by different people.

For each actual occurrence: **world qualifies→actor exposure qualifies→source-grounded historically acquired motifs recruit→actor selects activity/inaction→world settles→each affected person potentially reorganizes**. One occurrence can be relevant to several persons without each gaining omniscient access. Real world law persists even when no consciousness represents it. “Every element relational” is thus **structural**; computing a complete all-node cross-product is neither required nor desirable.

## C. One household illustration, neither scripted morality nor character-declared deterministic source

Nadia values her brother's care. She also experiences his protecting mother through restriction of her freedom. Brother is proud of helping mother, resents being forced into adult responsibility, and does not want to lose his sister. Mother has independent labor and health realities, her own possible fatigue and need for care; father abroad affects family resources, reputations and histories. School, religion, migration, media and regional war act through actual practices and constraints, **not automatic ideological traits**.

Suppose Nadia hears a true but incomplete report of father's betrayal. The historical episode must have source-qualified actuality independent of Nadia's hearsay. Her possible new interpretations depend on what she experienced of father’s work and mother's complaints. Brother may hear a different account and act to preserve a relationship or avoid pain. Mother may be unaware they know, reject their accounts, or make a practical decision independently. In one world Nadia protects brother from the knowledge; in another she tells him and their subsequent care activity changes; in neither does a universally coded TRAUMA/BETRAYAL score update all family members.

A conservative religious role need not be false to the brother as lived comfort and meaning simply because it also mediates constraint on his sister. Likewise a Western cultural artifact may be both authentic joy and an alienated colonial/commodity image. **An actor’s contradictory value does not imply the simulation endorses their harmful exercise of power.**
