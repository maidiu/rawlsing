## 1. The missing distinction: negation does not necessarily end the prior authority's sovereignty

The [Rawlsing Governing Spirit, protected source cut `6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/data/ddm_build_copy/vozhd_dva/20260817_downstream_completion/RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md) already states:

- **Identification → internalization → naturalization → invisibility → agent-of:** the social field is lived as if it were the actor's own transparent grammar.
- **Alienation → externalization → objectification → visibility → agency-against:** what formerly organized perception itself can become a *particular* object of perception, comparison and refusal.
- **Absolute Horizon:** the evolving ordering of what ultimately claims fidelity **and of what can be sacrificed for what**. It is **not** a tenth stratum, virtue score, party line or a separately floating soul. A finite mediator (family, nation, church, party, class identity, wound) can claim to be the Absolute.
- **Relativization without disembedding:** the more demanding possibility is belonging or acting from inherited resources **without granting the historical mediator ultimate authority**.

My [SEAM/10](10_SOROK_PREMISE_PRACTICE_FORMATION_TRANSDUCER_R1.md) made the real improvement that *what is enacted and experienced becomes the actor's fallible practical premises*, not the adult's survey answers. But its optional U-X “critical objectification” still stopped too early.

**Important new separation:**

1. **Identification** may make an acquired normative framework invisible as such; it appears as just how reality is.
2. **Alienation/negation** makes its institutional carrier visible and potentially criticizable. But if the actor's entire orientation remains *anti-that-carrier*, the carrier can still define the actor's world **as a negative sovereign**. Refusal is not yet a freedom of the criterion itself.
3. **Possible negation of the negation:** the actor may differentiate the *truth-content or need* that the institution invoked from the *particular institution's claim to own it*. The institution can be judged by the very criterion it once helped instill; the criterion can be enacted independently, even in practices or communities previously rejected as belonging to the enemy. There is **no guarantee** of this step, no moral progress ladder and no necessary return to the institution.
4. The **Absolute-Horizon question** is what, under actual competing claims, the actor demonstrably treats as *less relinquishable*—an ongoing and possibly inconsistent practical ordering. This can remain partly unrecognized and does not magically become legible by asking the actor for a creed.

The theological image supplied by the principal—Mary Magdalene in a particular constructive reading as one who does not simply discard the earlier religious tradition but finds it reconstituted around its fundamentals—is **an interpretive analogy**, not an evidentiary causal fact that seven demons resulted from practiced YHWHism, nor a compulsory historical process for a generated person. The computational claim stands without accepting that interpretation as an empirical premise.

## 2. The four-way nonidentity SOROK must preserve

```
STRUCTURAL / MATERIAL REALITY
    | institutions, power, risk, norm claims, material actability, source histories
    v
HISTORICALLY ACQUIRED PRACTICAL PREMISSES
    | what this actor has learned to predict, fear, manage, assume possible,
    | admire, believe legitimate, practice or mistrust in particular contexts
    v
COMPETING PRACTICAL CLAIMS / COSTS / PROJECTS
    | what may be sacrificed for what; partially opaque, plural, contingent
    |      (ABSOLUTE HORIZON: research perspective on ordering, NOT a meter)
    v
ACTUAL SITUATED ACTIONS / REFUSALS / OMISSIONS
    | what the actor actually attempts/performs under live opportunity and power
    v
PUBLIC AVOWALS / NARRATIVES / SELF-DESCRIPTIONS
    | a separately admitted expression that may agree, contradict, explain,
    | conceal, rationalize or fail to comprehend the action
```

Arrows in this diagram indicate **questions of mediation, not a deterministic pipeline**. For example, public avowal may occur *before* action and causally contribute to it, and objective material effects may bypass conscious uptake altogether; these existing SEAM distinctions survive.

**Three sources of apparent “belief” are NOT equivalent:**

- **AVOWED:** what the actor explicitly thinks/says they believe; may be sincere, strategic, confused, contradictory, or revised.
- **PRACTICAL PREMISE:** what a bounded prior history makes it reasonable for this actor to anticipate/attempt within a setting, including implicit, nonverbal, unconscious or misrecognized elements.
- **ENACTED FIDELITY:** what appears to be privileged by *repeated actual conduct in costly conflicts*, conditioned by what was materially feasible, known, threatened, supported and suffered. **This is evidence about action, not privileged direct access to the actor's metaphysical “true self.”**

A person may profess anti-authoritarianism and repeatedly seek unquestionable authority; profess familial obedience and materially protect someone against a family command; profess charity while withholding resources; or keep faith with a principle of care whose institutional vocabulary they no longer endorse. Conversely an act of compliance under a credible threat is **not proof of endorsement**, and a martyr-like act is **not proof of emancipatory Horizon**: collapse, coercion, desperation, duty, self-sacrifice for domination and inability to envisage an alternative can look similar.

This avoids the opposite reductive trap: replacing `survey checkbox -> psyche` with `observed action -> secret true belief`. Only **opportunity- and history-conditioned patterns with explicit unresolved alternatives** may ground a graded hypothesis about practical priority.

## 3. Candidate mechanism: recognition of the *claimed good* separately from its *owner*

Extend the two K and two U hypotheses of [SEAM/10](10_SOROK_PREMISE_PRACTICE_FORMATION_TRANSDUCER_R1.md) through a **bounded comparative activity process**, **not a new owner**:

**H0 — formation in a mediator.** The child genuinely participates in a group where a principle `V` (e.g. “those entrusted to us must be cared for”) is taught by language *and/or shown through recurrent care*. Actual acts, material benefits, sanctions, contradictory conduct and child access are recorded. The adult socializer's PYAT superstructural response configuration remains a qualified *possibility prior*, never evidence the value was enacted. Learned practices and premises are actor-owned at current grade.

**H1 — contradiction and possible visibility.** At a later episode institution `I` claims `V` while performing `not-V`: for example, a school claims collective responsibility but uses indiscriminate collective punishment without telling students what actually occurred. The child/adult must actually access **both** the earlier criterion and enough of this situation's consequence to compare them. A machine-level discrepancy does not guarantee the actor recognizes it. They may reinterpret `V`, blame the harmed, retreat into compliance, or become resistant.

**H2a — reactive negation, authority still sovereign in the negative.** The actor rejects anything named, sponsored or associated with `I` regardless of whether it performs `V`. Their practical options, self-description and outward acts remain anchored to *being anti-I*. This may be rational self-protection under danger rather than a psychological failure; do not score it “immature.”

**H2b — possible relativization/reappropriation.** The actor can now organize action around `V` under different materially available carriers, including without `I`, against `I` when it violates `V`, **and in limited cooperation with `I` when it actually serves `V` without monopolizing or compromising the person**. This is *not* reconciliation as an endpoint: a threatening institution can remain too dangerous to engage. The distinguishing feature is **positive direction not wholly defined by hostility to the earlier mediator**, not the return of loyalty.

**H2c — other real outcomes.** New exclusive idol, despair, withdrawal, selective compromise, context-split compliance and resentment, apparently no change, creative misunderstanding, or genuinely alternative commitments. No “all roads lead to solidarity” rule.

**Horizon mediation:** in an actual conflicting situation, the actor's historically conditioned **ordering of claims** helps make sense of which losses/costs they can bear and which they refuse to impose on someone else. This ordering is plural, moving, situation-bound and not guaranteed coherent. It is discovered, if at all, through **actual cross-context tradeoffs**, not a categorical “Horizon = justice” input. If evidence insufficient, it remains **UNKNOWN / UNEVALUATED**.

## 4. A causally discriminating SEAM experiment (one adult K, child U, adult encounter)

Use [SEAM/10's hypothetical WVS7 child-quality `Q8` independence / `Q17` obedience](10_SOROK_PREMISE_PRACTICE_FORMATION_TRANSDUCER_R1.md) **only as an illustrative adult-salience pair**; the WVS choose-up-to-five budget and source/time constraints remain binding. A stronger enacted principle `V`, where model-authorized, may be **particular**: “people in our care must not be abandoned.” Do not infer `V` from the two Q selections themselves. Its existence requires a separately generated, admitted K enactment/history.

Create **one actor's pre-user history**:

- **Early socialization:** caregivers actually perform some care under institutional/kin conditions and articulate `V`; they also comply with a powerful employer/authority when under material threat. The child experiences only accessible episodes. In one context they learn protection and assistance; elsewhere they learn the cost of disobedience. No direct demographic ideology assigned.
- **Later visible contradiction:** school/group/institution invokes “responsibility” while imposing demonstrably indiscriminate punishment on people who had no way to know what happened. The actor may or may not recognize `V` as a usable standard against that institution. Actual audience access matters.
- **Adult player scene:** the user wants to help an injured person by loudly confronting `I`. Compare a sourcefree actor who is (A) institution-identified, (B) institution-reactive, (C) capable of independently oriented practical response, and (D) not yet able to formulate a view. Keep actual resources, actor identity, source-qualified coactors, and present ability fixed. Treat (A–D) as **alternative generated histories**, NOT interchangeable types chosen by a player-facing “personality selector.”

The player observes:
- In **A**, actor can sincerely try to preserve an institutional procedure, possibly doing good or harm.
- In **B**, actor may reject *even a materially helpful* offer solely because `I` sponsors it; test whether rejection is also caused by *credible distrust, danger, or hidden constraints* before inferring “negative sovereignty.”
- In **C**, actor may refuse `I`'s unjust demand, help independently through an available practice, and if lawful work with particular members of `I` who also provide care. They need not say why or profess a universal ethic.
- In **D**, no scripted rupture or liberation; ordinary action/nochange.

**Four key interventions (must avoid moral outcome assumptions):**
1. **Same spoken creed; different performed cost-bearing:** Does the actor choose differently when helping really demands time, income, status or danger? No simplistic revealed-preference truth from a single action.
2. **Same material actability, change *institutional sponsor*:** Is behavior conditional solely on whether `I` proposes it, or on whether it actually serves `V` under recognized safety/authority? A negative-sovereignty hypothesis needs independent access evidence; a principled refusal of a harmful institution must not be mislabeled compulsive opposition.
3. **Remove `I) from the situation:** Can the person still pursue a valued activity and independent relationship? If not, negative fixation is a live hypothesis, not a verdict.
4. **Same contradiction, change actor access and acquired premise:** Without access to the discrepancy, do not issue alienation/objectification; without prior credible practice, do not issue `V` as already learned.

**Product outcome:** the user does not receive an omniscient paragraph announcing the actor's “Horizon.” They see a person whose commitments, refusals and possible cooperation are historically conditioned, whose self-description may not fully explain what they enact, and whose life continues beyond the user. Some encounters can make the player rethink their own category of the Other. Neither empathy nor doctrinal correctness is forced.

## 6. Specific challenge to UM-PYAT

1. Can PYAT's **co-traveling normative fields** tell us which *justifications* an institution publicly invokes **without pretending its actual coercive practices follow survey prevalence**? Identify a source-valid inferential limit and a plausible selected sourcefree alternative.
2. Can PYAT give a counterexample where the supposed move beyond negative sovereignty actually requires **continued principled refusal**, because the earlier institution remains materially dangerous? We must not interpret every unbroken boundary as “fetishistic attachment.”
3. Which external evidence can distinguish **self-reported values from enacted rule under actual costs** while controlling opportunity, coercion, social desirability and retrospective rationalization? Where evidence is absent, rival `H` hypotheses must remain open.
4. Can a social field's own claimed criterion make its institution criticizable across **different media, class relations, genders, generations and counterfields**? Design one discriminating institutional change, not an automatic liberation sequence.
5. Which existing PYAT prospective source and concrete SOROK C4/P3/CAN consumer give the cheapest **actual adult-later-action** demonstration? No protected action until competent lease/adoption; ship a sourcefree first vertical.

**The critical thesis:** the social relation can generate the **conditions of its criticism**, but criticism may still remain captive to it. **Possible negation of the negation means an inherited capacity to judge and act can cease to depend for its identity on either allegiance or opposition to the original authority.** Absolute Horizon is the *question of practical fidelity under competing claims*, not a formula that guarantees emancipation. Do not make the actor's hidden psyche more legible than the evidence permits.
