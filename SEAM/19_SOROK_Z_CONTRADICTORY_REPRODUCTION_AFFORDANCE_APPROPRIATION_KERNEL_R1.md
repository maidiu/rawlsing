## 0. Verdict on X and Y, and a decisive correction to the question

**X is an honest starting point**, source-conditioned but largely static world→actor opportunity. It must not be misrepresented as a full developmental generator.

**Y is closer to the science**: multiple independently engaged people continually change one another's lives as byproducts of working, caring, speaking, conflicting, provisioning, moving, playing and relating. Yet Y still risks confusing *social reproduction* with *co-present social participation*, and confusing *creating an opportunity* with *experiencing, learning or acting upon it*.

**Z — contradictory world reproduction ↔ differential affordances ↔ situated appropriation ↔ subsequent activity.** This is **not a new engine**; it is a *cross-owner conservation and transformation contract* for the SEAM:

1. A historically inherited world contains **nature and bodies; accumulated objects, infrastructure and knowledge; unequal ownership, authority and collective power; and living independently active people**. Much of this world was made by earlier generations or distant institutions. It need not be freshly produced by present coactors.
2. Actual activity and nonactor events **reproduce or alter that world**. A single act can simultaneously nourish one relationship, exhaust a caregiver, service debt, reproduce an institution's authority, help an employer appropriate work, or unintentionally enable a child. Record *different actual effects on different subjects and resources*. The meanings can be contradictory; no global welfare sign.
3. The altered world supplies **unequal actor-specific practical possibilities**, whether or not the recipient witnessed how they arose. Affordance is not perception; changed material dependence is not learned obedience.
4. Particular embodied actors may **encounter** acts, speech, objects, bodily cues, aftermath or absence through different channels; or encounter none. Exposure alone does not entail deliberate participation, mastery, value endorsement, or memory.
5. Through active doing, observing, overhearing, experimenting alone, resting, playing, resisting, or interacting with nonhuman environments, **some** context-scoped capabilities, expectations, practices, sensitivities and contradictions may be acquired or changed. For now every such U is a separately qualified research hypothesis, never a universal teaching→learning law.
6. The resulting organism–history organization changes a later actor's *feasible response to a real world*, which can itself reproduce or transform the next world and affect others. What one actually does can contradict what one professes and even what one consciously believes.

**Why this is more than Y:** a new opportunity may exist with *no experience*, learning may occur without *instruction or work*, a person may act from *unconscious embodied practice*, and the material structure of one event can reproduce domination and care at once. The “spiral” is causal, not a moral progression. The same machinery can yield identification, reactive opposition, possible criterion reappropriation, indifference, regression, ordinary play or no learning.

**Theoretical noncollapse:** objective world reproduction; actor-specific affordance; accessed experience; developmental change; activation; attempted/performed action; objective consequence; personal meaning/Absolute Horizon are **distinct loci of causation**. Neither sociological categories nor moral philosophy may prefill the later ones.

## 1. Scientific challenges: what Y captures and what it leaves out

### 1A. PYAT's evidence is real but does not identify child formation

- **Kohn & Schooler (1982)** estimate reciprocal relationships among job conditions and adult orientations in a longitudinal US sample; this gives serious support for workplace conditions having effects on adults and adults selecting into roles. It does **not** observe a named caregiver–infant pair and cannot determine how that caregiver enacts norms in a particular family. [AJS doi:10.1086/227593](https://doi.org/10.1086/227593). Burger et al. (2025) likewise examine two-generation *work self-direction*, but without the missing directly observed childrearing mechanism. [Social Forces doi:10.1093/sf/soaf016](https://doi.org/10.1093/sf/soaf016).
- **Infant allocare**: the three studied small-scale societies average 43% nonmaternal direct care (large variance) and show immediate effects on maternal time; neither one percentage nor the absence of detectable later measured fitness effects licenses a universal care system. [Infant allocare in traditional societies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29730035/). More importantly, material help **redistributes** women's and others' time, not simply “adds maternal love.”
- **Tseltal infants' honorific comprehension** supplies a direct **negative test** against “only speech directly addressed to the infant can be acquired.” It is grounded in frequent, salient, context-rich adult exchanges overheard while carried; it does **not** license “all overheard content is learned,” and certainly not “observing compliance implies legitimating it.” [PNAS 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11161804/).
- **Motor development**: bodily state, cultural practices, environment and self-initiated movement interact; motor skills themselves create opportunities for new perception, exploration and learning. Thus development is *not wholly a transfer from adult projects*. [Adolph & Hoch 2019, Annual Review of Psychology](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010418-102836).
- **Rogoff (2014)** models learning through observing and pitching in to community endeavors as a form of *organized opportunity*. But this is not a universal rule privileging one cultural pedagogy over all alternatives. [Human Development doi:10.1159/000356757](https://doi.org/10.1159/000356757).
- **Super & Harkness (1986)** already distinguish the *physical/social settings*, *care customs*, and *caretakers' culturally structured understandings* within a developmental niche. These are neither all social participation nor reducible to “adult attitudes”; they may be mutually inconsistent, and their relationships with the child's own activities matter. [Developmental niche doi:10.1177/016502548600900409](https://doi.org/10.1177/016502548600900409).

### 1B. The overlooked **material contradiction** inside reproduction

Social reproduction is not just keeping a household going. It encompasses conditions that reproduce **labor power, relationships, resources, institutions, and differential capacities to impose costs**; its historical capitalist organization cannot be flattened to individualized “care hours.” The provided [*Social Reproduction Theory: Remapping Class, Recentering Oppression* (Bhattacharya, ed., 2017)](https://www.plutobooks.com/9780745399881/social-reproduction-theory/) and Sève/Leontiev offer competing theoretical resources for this claim, **not a completed numeric behavioral law**.

A paid shift may **provide wages and reduce available caregiving time** while reproducing employer authority and profits; a school may provide literacy and discipline; a solidarity practice may open collective capacities and carry internal hierarchy. These are **simultaneous heterogeneous effects of one real relation**, not a clean benefit/harm scalar. A person's habitus can be organized by *the very practice that feeds them and subordinates them*. That is how critique and possible later reappropriation can emerge from within formation—without inserting an “Horizon score.”

This also repairs a **blind spot in the PYAT→SOROK division**: PYAT's nine strata include planetary ecology, imperial arrangements, states, class, region, movement and institutions, none of which must first be visible to a child or occur inside their home to change a caregiver's work schedule or a child's resources. A rent increase, crop failure, checkpoint, employer closure, or public-service provision changes local affordances **without itself becoming childhood experience of the source structure**. Source/period/power qualification is essential.

### 1C. The overlooked **plurality and nonparticipation**

- **Dreier, *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life* (2008), Introduction pp. 1–2**, argues persons conduct lives across different contexts and link their activities and concerns; the single household or labor scene is not the whole person. Leontiev's activity framework distinguishes activity/motives, actions/goals and operations/conditions; it cannot be encoded faithfully as every experience being intended instruction.
- **Lahire, *The Plural Actor*** challenges unified habitus; differentiated socializations can produce contradictory context-activated dispositions. The program must not collapse their interconnection into total personality coherence or arbitrarily make one profile dominant. [Review of *L'homme pluriel*](https://www.persee.fr/doc/rfsoc_0035-2969_1999_num_40_4_5224).
- **Holzkamp** insists practical reasons concern a person's situated possible courses of action, not an objective stimulus mechanically commanding behavior. That is a theoretical corrective, not independently confirmed parameter estimates. [Tolman 2009](https://doi.org/10.1177/0959354309103535).
- Bodies learn and change through self-movement; children can experiment with objects alone; sleep, fatigue, hunger, injury, nonverbal attention and maturation matter. Some acquisition is observational or incidental; some development is biological without psychological learning. Animal interactions and ecosystems can create lived affordances without being assigned a human caregiver's motivational model.

**The scientific synthesis is modest but demanding:** *the causal multiplicity is well supported; our particular interaction kernels and transition rates are not*. Stop trying to prove a single total psychology from the cited literature. Treat Z as an **adequacy criterion and a smallest experimental assembly**, not empirical validation of Leontiev/Holzkamp/DST as one master equation.

## 4. The one SCIENTIFIC and COMPUTATIONAL spine: contradictory reproduction of possibility

**The precise thesis:** society reproduces both life's conditions **and the powers that distribute them**; people become particular organisms-with-histories through unequal embodied access, selective practice and limited interpretation; their acts can in turn reproduce or contest those conditions. **No step is reducible to another**, and none guarantees moral progress.

This recovers the Rawlsing [Governing Spirit](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/data/ddm_build_copy/vozhd_dva/20260817_downstream_completion/RAWLSING_GOVERNING_SPIRIT.md):
- nine strata set costs and powers, not demographic personhood;
- pressure is not psyche; **“where does living flow go under pressure?”** includes care, play, beauty, curiosity, boredom, desire, mischief, devotion, survival and imagination—not merely parental labor, trauma or school;
- habitus can naturalize historical institutions into reality, **without** requiring the child to know the system that caused the local experience;
- identical institutions can nourish AND dominate, forming **the criterion by which they become criticizable**;
- practical fidelity/Absolute Horizon is the question of *which claims become binding under actual conflicting costs*, **not** a score or a guaranteed negation-of-negation stage;
- persons become agents capable of making structures objects again **only through causally enabled but contingent activities**; their particular conduct must remain opaque and partially contradictory to narrators.

This makes an otherwise missing theoretical observation computationally crucial: **beneficial and coercive effects of the same activity must remain distinct and jointly reproducible**. A child who learns practical cooperation during coercive work arrangements can later use that capacity to sustain those arrangements, resist them, or pursue something else. It must never be preset. The institution remains materially present even if its authority in that actor's life later changes.
