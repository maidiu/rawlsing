## 3. The proposed structural-to-Horizon mechanism (three coupled pressures)

**A. Normalization is a history of *living within* hegemony, not intensity of endorsement.** Repeated institution-mediated care, punishments, work schedules, sanctions, protected exceptions, ordinary enjoyment, learned competence and kinship constitute what the person can take for granted. The same surveyed parental salience in a different ownership/workplace structure can yield different enacted practices and consequently a different child's inference. **The child may become an agent of a hegemonic order while sincerely believing in universal humanity.**

**B. Alienation is *materially costly differentiation*, not an attitude flip.** Exposed violations of a previously lived/invoked criterion may make an authority visible as **this** authority, now contestable. Whether that produces quiet exit, obedient accommodation, rupture, violence, withdrawal, organized resistance, or mere discursive opposition depends on actual losses and alternatives: kinship and livelihood, police/employer capacity, available counterfield, earlier habits, and what the subject has actually accessed. Antagonism can be especially intense when what is negated had generated most of the subject's earlier universe. **Counter-hegemonic practice must have carriers and material support, not just superior ideas.**

**C. The possible negation of negation must be materially generative.** A person once formed by I, and later identifying in opposition to I, can find **anti-I** becoming a second prison. A further *possible* U occurs only where an actual cost of reactive reference becomes accessible and the actor has acquired independent activity/capabilities/coactors. A merely intellectual desire to be free cannot issue an ability. If successful, the actor can undertake **a new project that survives I's absence**, reusing inherited practices without restoring its authority, and rejecting I when the present costs/risks truly warrant it. This is not an obligatory emancipation arc or an ethical label assigned to the actor.

**The change in structural possibility and the change in actor meaning are distinct:** an objective counterfield may appear without anyone learning to use it; an actor may imagine an alternative that is materially blocked; a viable collective may alter sanctions without any single actor's “Horizon” changing. Precisely those separations create credible dramatic life.
