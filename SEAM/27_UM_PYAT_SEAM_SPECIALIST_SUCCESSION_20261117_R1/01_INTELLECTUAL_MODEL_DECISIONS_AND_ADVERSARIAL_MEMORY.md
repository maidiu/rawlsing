## I. What we are really trying to build

Rawlsing's original ambition is not producing a statistically plausible personality stereotype. It is a *particular embodied, historically becoming Other* who lives among independently existing and differently positioned other humans in a world of material forces, social reproduction, institutions, class power, collective and personal identification, contingent encounters, communication, desire, care, conflict, joy, and possible social transformation. The person does not become “human” by being adorned with a psychologically convincing autobiographical LLM monologue. The machine must support **real attempted/performed action with world consequences, actor-specific limited access, own developmental formation, independent others, and continuing later readers**. An unavailable science or source remains held at its precise grade without suppressing valid less-ambitious observed results.

The scientific source is partial, instrument-produced evidence about the world. The target is a temporally specific social field of **qualified possible** configurations of people/positions and relations, *not* a copy of one actual respondent or an omniscient dataset of actual real-world persons. The organism lives in a modeled but causally disciplined particular fictional world, under separate actor ownership and claim grade.

The governing strategic question, present in PYAT/SOROK PROGRAMMA and original source science: **What is possible? What is facilitated, inhibited, repressed or compelled, by which differentiated forces, in contradiction with what other tendencies, and under what conditions could those relations change?** For whom, at whose expense, and through what institutions and activity? A survey distribution is evidence about one part of this question. It never contains the entire answer.

**SEAM's specific job:** locate the true transformations where PYAT's qualified objective/empirical/social-world output becomes SOROK's **particular** encounterable world/person and, in the other direction, where SOROK's admissibly performed public/material act has a separately qualified objective-world consequence. A functional SEAM requires **actual producer → authorized transformation → changed output/negative → actual first reader → materially or behaviorally different lawful consequence → replay/cold/restore**, not only a documentary field that survives a pipeline. We are not founding a new full Neo-V3 programme.

## II. Scientific discovery: whole respondent configurations, not “inconsistent opinions”

1. **Same-person joint evidence is irreducible.** A person can sincerely hold doctrinal exclusivism, minority freedom of worship, Islamic law for personal status, disagreement with generalized Islamic state legislation; favor a woman's political office and restrict married employment; pray daily but rarely attend communal prayer; be born again and attend church while describing themselves as “not religious.” These are *not* necessarily incoherent. Different propositions govern different practical or legal jurisdictions and may use source-specific categories. Products of marginals and even arbitrary pairwise fits cannot substitute for true k-way respondent-linked joint distributions.
2. **Discover its distribution within positions**, not just a whimsical anecdote. Estimate `P(Y_1,...,Y_k | D,U,t)` and properly scoped reverse `P(D | Y,U,t)`, with actual survey design/risk, eligibility/asked/observed, eligible respondent overlaps, weights, support, uncertainty, minority tails, and selection effects. “Surprising to a Westerner” is not a statistical estimand.
3. **Surrounding-field rather than one-person-only output.** What other, countervailing configurations are distributed among *other* positions in a particular society? Which actually can come into contact; which institutions hold unequal resources, legal authority, social reproduction power, sanction and control? **Differences are not automatically antagonisms**. The corrected requirement is: positions **empirically identifiable as correlated with unequal material resources/power/institutional authority** when actually measured; explanation of *mechanism, domination, hegemony or antagonism* requires stronger independent evidence, crosswalk and source time. A simple attitudinal prevalence is not power.
4. **The field is recursively nested and overlapping**: Nabatieh within Lebanon, Lebanon within SWANA/world, different religious traditions and subgroups, class, gender, generation, language, French-colonial and global capitalist institutions, Palestinian solidarity, diaspora, digital/global media. These are not one universal taxonomy of levels or a magical normative prior; the exact group/field ontology is historically and source-qualified. National, religious, ethnic, gender and class identifications can pull toward different commitments simultaneously.
5. **Difference between observed position and personal spirit.** Don't subtract the group's modal attitudes from the respondent and call the residual “individuality.” The particular person is formed through the very social milieu, but through their own specific path of practical activity, attachment, interpretation, acquiescence and resistance. Statistical unusualness is not a measure of agency.
6. **No one-to-one survey→motive bridge.** A Lebanese woman endorsing equal inheritance and Islamic inheritance legislation may have a progressive historical-direction Qur'anic hermeneutic, or a different interpretation, or no examined reconciliation. A GSS evangelical who says “not religious at all” may contrast personal relationship with religion, or mean something else. Neither inference is observed by the questionnaire. The resulting actor has to **learn or enact meanings**, including how they interpret questionnaire categories. An answer-emission model is separate from actor's internal organization; the reverse adult questionnaire check is a *held-out distributional adequacy question* only after source-specific emission rules are justified.
7. **Chronological asymmetry:** a 40-year-old surveyed in 2010 is measured **in 2010**, not in 1995 when their fictional child might have been born. Independent cross-sectional waves do not supply that person's longitudinal life history. Do not transport their mature answer vector backward as measured fact. A bounded inverse/compressed-person historical model can propose histories compatible with an observation, at explicitly model-graded uncertainty.

**Illustrative observations, not population conclusions:** The Arab Barometer Wave II Lebanese matched trio included two age-40 urban Nabatieh Muslim male shop-owner respondents with extraordinarily similar 241 populated fields, but different party affinity and selected answers; a similarly matched woman reported notably different gender/religion configurations alongside differing income and household factors. The similarity of the two men warrants duplicate/interviewer-quality audit before treating two IDs as independent evidence. Wave II stratification by sect does not prove a usable public respondent-level sect field; party (Future/Amal/Hezbollah) and Nabatieh cannot fill it in. Arab Barometer I/V/VIII require exact instrument-versus-release checks. The GSS 2010 quartet (65-year-old married white non-Hispanic Southerners) shows born-again/fundamentalist identities with distinctive gender/abortion/religiosity responses, while different questionnaire forms produce many inapplicable items. Latinobarómetro can record Brazilian Muslim religion without Lebanese-origin respondent identity; external ethnolinguistic estimates may describe groups, not identify respondents. These cases motivate *discovery procedures*, not universal frequencies or a causal Lebanese/Brazilian/American law.

## III. The adult and child must be capable of richer internal identity than an attitude cloud

**Adult computational:** A socialized actor can have historically learned, potentially context-bound **practical repertoires, beliefs, competences, justifications, contradictory commitments and differentiated loyalties**. “Contradiction” isn't a forced psychological error or drama beat. They may recognize, disavow, coexist with, defend, conceal, reinterpret or never confront competing claims. Their actual accessible encounter, current material constraint and past acquired organization determine what is relevant; an LLM suggestion or sampled questionnaire answer is not a performed action.

**Identification / alienation:** The actor's nominal social placement, outside classification, self-professed identification, actual enacted loyalty, private avowed interpretation, and ultimate practical fidelity are distinct. Social appeals are mediated through family, work, religious practice, nation-state, party, class struggle, media, desire, pleasure, shame, affection, aspiration, coercion and dependency. The externalized institution can feel natural/invisible as one's own grammar; a previously internalized authority can become visible, criticizable and relativized through lived contradiction. This is not necessarily simple rejection: an actor might preserve the emancipatory value while challenging the institution claiming to own it.

**Absolute Horizon:** The original SOROK/Governing Spirit concept concerns the evolving ordering of ultimate practical fidelity (what is ultimately for the sake of what; what may be sacrificed), not a tenth group, morality score, politically approved endpoint, trait vector, or a slow C5 motif. SEAM/12–17 explored finite possible models; they were sourcefree/prospective and don't constitute a native psychogenetic law. In particular **C5 slow attractor** (if justified) and **Absolute Horizon** are different objects. H0 may support no additional C5 beyond acquired history; Enneagram H2 is unsafe as unearned early gravity. Identity must remain open to ordinary life, craft, humor, play, curiosity and ambivalence—not only injury and anti-power revelation.

**Child computational:** Born as an organism with biological/metabolic needs, sensory/attention/action capacity, dependence on care and a changing temporal world—not a blank mind with ten fully specified motives and not a demographic opinion vector. Development arises from active contact with **real** care, bodily regulation, language, practical learning, institutions, sibling relations, work, play, media, social approval/punishment and distinct access. The same ontology of development governs parents and child, but the focal child is run at finer resolution; parents' life before conception can be compressed, without inventing causally significant unobserved events in recalled memory. Opposing siblings must emerge through differing own activity/encounters, never be assigned polarized types to satisfy narrative needs. A mature child must become capable of new **kinds of acquired categories and meaningful relationships**, not merely varied scores on an invariant Western psychology rubric.

**Generated events:** LLM proposes a historically possible event *stack* based on the actor/world state at proposal time, with practical rather than “showcase the political belief” salience; actual event/admission and independent performer are separate. Events can be mundane and sometimes have no psychosocial update. Some are **exogenous world events** (weather, institutions, epidemics, war, closure, prices), neither authored nor performed by the focal actor. Others require actual actor-owned choice and resources. Available global media != actor access != comprehension != identification != later action. School/play/household and social reproduction can shape life without rhetoric. Valid NO_CHANGE, unknown formation and unrealized possibility must remain distinct.

**Biographical validity:** Neither LLM story quality nor resemblance of eventual adult survey answers to held-out samples proves actual causal development. Need particular timeline; correct objective event and actor-limited perception; established domain-specific learning rule; actual actor state update/no-change; independent later use, not self-confirming narration; costly choice must be materially feasible and a rule-supported inference before calling it devotion/ultimate allegiance.
