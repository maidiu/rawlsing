## 1. Boundary topology: branches and necessary conjunctions

The pathway is **not** a universal line from S1 to V3. The full law includes independent lanes and conditional joins:

- **Empirical direct lane:** qualified original source/source meaning → C11 and I03/I05/Batch → F1–F4 empirical facts → computed C1/N10E analytical possibility. No compulsory structural FF02 for an honestly empirical product.
- **Actor-independent structural lane:** qualified empirical description through FF01; independent material-position/structural evidence and competent rules through FF02/SDTF where selected → objective position/field/condition/mediation/authority and optional F6 separate-survey composition or F7 qualified time → N6/N9 objective world.
- **Learned and structural-peer lanes:** M1/M2/B5 versioned learned products and V554–V558/M4/M5 exact structural peer selection where the actual consumer requires them. These may be parallel to and conditional upon N9 or actor-context requests; they cannot silently become a universal serial prerequisite.
- **Situated possibility conjunction:** **N10W = compatible selected N9 AND N10E** under exact model/use/condition/time/source qualification. The result constrains material/empirical alternatives; it cannot decide an actor's choice.
- **PRE-V3→SOROK:** qualified N11/N12 world/use decision (if native GO is competently issued) → explicit E001 C0/world-to-C11 source/permissioned world receiver and E002 C0/world-to-C4 actor-independent situation+independent coactor input, then SOROK-owned encounter, actor access, event, response, memory, formation, time, performed later activity and bounded expression.
- **Return branch:** independently performed and **externally admitted** occurrence → independently admitted objective world response/change → exact SDTF R4 determinant invalidation/rederivation request → competent world owner application, qualified revised N9/as-of world, later SOROK encounter. Sorok private history and world source history remain separate, with no retroactive mutation.

Read those as required claims to verify **against real current selected consumers**. A diagram showing a branch does not prove it has ever executed.

## 3. Candidate interface surfaces, NOT a new runtime schema

The following are *cross-HQ semantic requirements*, to be mapped to existing typed owners and schemas. No agent may create a mandatory universal object from this draft without the owner's proof of necessity.

### A. Objective world and authorized read request

The candidate receiver must be able to specify: exact world/release identity and content root; valid-time and as-of; source and use/permission/currentness; relevant location, objectively supported position/household/institution/field roles; resources/claims/ownership/obligations and known opportunities/consequences; typed absent/inapplicable/unknown/noncomparable; relation direction and counterpressure where qualified; applicability context; replay/lineage and stale/revoked status. **It must not receive actual actor emotions, assigned type, hidden other minds or an invented self-story as world facts.**

No false uniformity: if one world service delivers material position and another delivers structural peer topology, the receiver may combine references at the qualified runtime seam; it need not consume one giant serialized field. Query-use rights and readset selection matter as much as the backing bytes.

### B. Empirical and world-conditioned possibility

A candidate possibility request/result must bind exact **conditioner / target / role / source population / unit / coadministration / risk / denominator / estimand / weight / uncertainty / multiway respondent identity where applicable / minority and counterpattern / model-use authority** and, for N10W, an exact compatible current/as-of world+position+time selection. Legitimate empty, weak, denied, unknown or out-of-scope are real outcomes. **A positive possibility result is not an individual behavior generator.**

### C. Structural-prior applicability and generated-position feedback

The actor-independent structural repository must be queryable on real contextual predicates, time and source/use; a later **actor-owned generated position** may select a different applicable structural relation without updating the repository's immutable objective content or converting the position to survey fact. Exact binding echo, distinction between available vs realized routes, typed defer/valid-empty and hard refusal are required. The modern owner must decide the smallest proper actual M4/M5/Phase-B interface; a hash-preserving receipt is not enough.

### D. Perception, activity, outward occurrence and world feedback

An objective world event must remain distinct from what an actor's sensory/access path actually makes available; independently performed public action must remain distinct from intention and rendered words. Outward effects only enter qualified world/SDTF redrive through **competently admitted externalized occurrence** and **separate competent objective world response**, with actor/scope/world/time binding, permission and content-addressed changed determinants. R4 request generation is not world-writing authority, and a current response consequence is not durable actor formation.

### E. Multiple times and independent counterparty

Every admissible actor/world conjunction must preserve (1) objective validity and comparison time, (2) actor history/causal event time, (3) source/rule/publication/current permission, (4) root/fork/as-of, and (5) counterpart who is independently acting or omitting, not merely a response parameter of the focal actor. Ordinary no-change and partial observability must be supported.

## 6. Preliminary fault line / what requires an actual scientific decision

An upstream statistical system may know which survey respondents reported employment insecurity or support for a policy. It does **not**, without external positions/institutional evidence and competent theory, know who actually controls a factory, how many families are legally dependent on a landlord, whether a union can stop production, which sanctions the state is able or willing to enforce, or which possible path a particular woman will choose. FF/SDTF must make these distinctions without either discarding valid evidence or pretending their missing objective causes are present. SOROK must make a particular history without making up an empirical survey respondent, omnisciently inserting facts into consciousness or claiming independence from the actual material world.
