## 0. Governing user-set sequence; do not relocate it

**SEAM is the generative construction of the child's personhood out of PYAT's forward–backward demographic↔opinion/attitude possibility machinery, starting with empirically qualified parents and continuing through recursively developing family life.** It is *not* just a generic boundary where an already-formed actor encounters a context.

1. **PYAT mother:** sample historically, materially and demographically coherent mother configuration; retain source-bound correlated survey answer/attitude CLOUD (not independently drawn answer sliders), design weights, minority support, structural qualifications and uncertainty.
2. **SOROK mother:** build a lightweight *but genuinely independent* parent person via few compressed developmental recursions. Must have her own iceberg, context-sensitive consciousness, projects, internal tensions, experiential and nondeclarative traces, and ability to act.
3. **Mother's real trajectory:** propose *multiple feasible encounter forms* from her actually generated place, time, work, networks, social constraints and available worlds. LLM may PROPOSE narrative alternatives but cannot admit impossible co-location, access, provenance, probability or causality. Select a lawful encounter.
4. **Conditional backward father:** given that selected intersection and the fact that a birth will occur in this branch, infer eligible father demographics/material circumstances/attitude cloud, historical availability, **independent prior life**, compatible with what the encounter requires but not designed to conform to the mother's psychology. Selection is inverse inference / conditional sampling; father prehistory is *causally earlier* and must pass prospective history validation.
5. **Together until birth:** mother and father now BOTH have own iceberg, own conscious appearance, own *partial and potentially mistaken* models of one another, and actual objective relationship/household event history. They recurse dialectically through activity, material constraints, mutual effects, independent choice, conflicts, accord, separation, absences, other persons.
6. **New child actor:** the real child-generation event then ADP-007 sparse initialization, NOT a parental trait average. Birth opens richer, child-centered developmental recursion. Mother and father continue changing (and their absence can have real effects), at lower resource cost.
7. **Playable terminal:** a particular child-person with causally held, historically generated capacities, memory, affect, practices, contradictions, actor epistemic limits and independent later conduct, not a persuasive narrative of a person.

**The parent pair are the entry route, not necessarily the enduring caregivers or even one joint household.** The genealogy event/biological lineage must remain distinct from co-residence, parenting, allocare, kin rights and actual caregivers, as demanded by ADP-015.

## 1. First serious problem: we have the tip but not the iceberg

Survey responses are **observable reported answers to measured questions**, influenced by wording, social desirability, context, period, respondent and sampling; they are **NOT direct access to consciousness**, much less an actor's entire explicit ideology. Material/demographic data are also source- and measurement-bound, not a complete real household.

Desired parent state distinction:

\`\`\`text
source-qualified P(DEMOGRAPHIC, MATERIAL MEASURES, REPORTED ANSWERS)
       ↓ one probabilistically drawn SYNTHETIC observation vector with source receipts
proposed actor-compatible historically constrained prior life histories H_i
       ↓ SAME V3 developmental semantics that later apply to the child,
         but sparse/compressed at an explicitly qualified experiment grade
distinct deep actor states Z_i (implicit habits, acquired expectations, personal
  salience, context-scoped competencies, motives/projects, bodily state,
  causal-trace memory, attachments and contradictions)
       ↓ actor-specific current situation and proposed response
conscious claim / narrated self-understanding / actual action
\`\`\`

There is no identified function \`survey_answer -> deep_motivation\`. A woman who answers "obedience is important" might endorse that answer while consistently sheltering an independent child, or treat it as duty under risk, or distinguish workplaces from kin relations. **The first graph does not specify her private historical meaning, nor a unique motive, action or mental state**.

This is a classic inverse problem. A provisional candidate model is
\`P(H,Z | A,D,W) ∝ P(A | H,Z,D,W) P(H,Z | D,W)\`.
**No empirical posterior exists unless both the historical-developmental prior and the observation/response likelihood are actually parameterized and qualified.** The expression is research architecture, not a license to attach invented numeric likelihoods or call generic LLM judgments empirical probabilities. Multiple deep histories may map to the same survey responses; preserve posterior multiplicity / partial identification, not a spurious unique "true psyche" claim.

**Proposal — *compressed forward proposal, inverse consistency check***:
1. PYAT issues a **joint observed seed** A,D,M with exact cohort/conditioner, respondent topology, denominator support, uncertainty and source/design/permissions receipts.
2. A constrained proposal stage creates *N* candidate historically possible prior lives, using actual PYAT world/position limits, possible caregiving/work/learning histories and a **common child/parent developmental rule family**; a generative LLM may suggest scenes or event types, but only as sourcefree hypotheses with explicit assumptions.
3. For each candidate, execute *compressed causal transitions* FORWARD, resulting in one parent iceberg state and testable response/behavioral surface. Use an independently specified answer-emission model as an **observation consistency check**; reject/weight only where rule/evidence grade permits. If none is graded, return UNIDENTIFIED/TEST_ONLY rather than presenting scored plausibility as calibration.
4. Pick one candidate under a separately graded generative-selection policy with **named ADP-020 randomness** and full lineage; commit its prior event capsules and derived present state as *the fiction's actual world history*. **The source population does not thereby acquire that invented biography**.
5. Future parent recursion uses that frozen actual history and *does not keep secretly adjusting the iceberg* merely to maintain the original sampled cloud. A survey-response draw is a historical initial observation, not an eternal behavioral constraint.
6. Alternative admissible histories remain available as **counterfactual branches**, not memories the selected actor magically knows.

Useful formal analogies: Bayesian inverse planning (Baker, Saxe & Tenenbaum 2009, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cognition.2009.07.005) supplies a method for inverting a model of constrained action but *not* a validated full-person inferencer. Prior/posterior predictive checks (Stan: https://mc-stan.org/docs/stan-users-guide/ppcs.html) suggest calibrating predicted observable survey response distributions and relationships against measured outcomes; neither validates the inferred iceberg without richer evidence. Household microdata/synthetic population construction can preserve joint household structure within its sampled scope (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11762717/); WVS marginal attitudes alone cannot identify real couples.

## 2. One engine at different **resolution**, not two psychologies

**Strong criterion:** Parent and child must use the **same actor causal-state schema, event admissibility, psychological transition families, expectation/memory provenance, actor access and future actor-selection protocol**. Differences:
- **Pre-contact adult:** low temporal resolution, selective historically plausible episodes, less internal detail, explicit unmodeled intervals/unknowns. Not infant-like tabula rasa and NOT a wholly separate formulaic adult psychotype.
- **Joint parental years:** finer resolution at relation-relevant activity and material consequences; other episodes can remain bounded compression.
- **Child:** selectively finer resolution in organismic, relational, sensory, practice/communication and developmental periods; parents keep independent changes at lower resolution.
- **Player-time Other:** fine resolution when an actual interaction and its consequences demand it.

**Compression is NOT equivalent to making up a few explanatory anecdotes or applying five years of psychological growth in one arbitrary jump.** The proposed compressed operator must either (a) execute actual eligible fine-grained history under a compute budget and keep a lawful stable state summary; or (b) use an explicitly TEST_ONLY *coarse-grained transition* with its own validated envelope, uncertainty, historical scope and loss-of-information statements. A coarse transition cannot claim equivalence to micro-transition unless tested. The same **ontology/authorities** need not entail identical time step counts, model calls or numeric inference algorithms.

**Checkpoint = state at t + causal predecessor refs + retained context-scoped trace and uncertainty.** The event capsule must distinguish *attempted*, *performed*, *received*, *processed*, *stored* and *forgotten*. A summary is a cache/read model of those exact results, not a newly generated cause. **Cold replay** must be able to establish the selected genealogy and prospectively used actor facts, even if every unimportant microevent was intentionally not simulated.

The *real key*: parent implicit history should be rich enough to change what they NOTICE, consider possible, say, conceal and DO with each other, and to reconfigure with subsequent events. A detailed but inert "iceberg backstory" is not enough. Test it with **same answers & demographics, different selected plausible iceberg histories** followed by one identical actor-relevant situation: later conduct may diverge only through a genuinely consumed history-derived state. Difference in narration alone fails.

## 3. Not three things: at least seven noncollapsible relational states

The principal's intuition that Nadia and the user are two iceberg-mediated consciousnesses plus a third *relationship thing* is correct **as a starting minimum, but insufficient**. A prospective parent pair requires at least:

1. Mother's own objective body/history, iceberg and currently accessible consciousness/claims.
2. Father's own objective body/history, iceberg and currently accessible consciousness/claims.
3. Mother's *perceived model of father*: attributed intentions, experienced trust, uncertainties, expectations; not father truth.
4. Father's *perceived model of mother*: independently generated, may radically disagree.
5. The objective *relationship* entity: formed/transitioned/ended only through ADP-015 lawful events; its properties are NOT a psyche.
6. Objective *household/material arrangement* if formed: members, resources, rights, time, obligations and external relations; no automatic household persona.
7. Actor-specific **joint event history and co-presence/access**; each may hear, misinterpret, miss, conceal or forget an event differently.

These are **not necessarily seven independent databases/engines**; several are views over existing event/actor owners. Additionally, at birth: child organism, child perception and its own view of each parent; other caregivers, kin and institutions are not optional in the scientific model.

The hardest relation mechanism is the reciprocal update WITHOUT fusion:
\`(Z_m, A_m, I_m(f), Z_f, A_f, I_f(m), R_objective, H_world)_t\`
\`-> independently attempted actions + admitted material/social effects -> different perceived exposures -> separate Z/I changes -> R/H consequences at t+1\`.
Even mutual agreement does not make equal knowledge, reciprocal attachment or shared motives. **No authority lives in a synthetic "couple personality score."**

This structure is not invented from zero. [ADP-015](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/data/ddm_build_copy/vozhd_dva/20260817_downstream_completion/schematics/release/W2_AUTHORITY_CONTRACT_COMPLETION/ADP_015_V3_RELATIONSHIP_GENEALOGY/AUTHORITY.md), at exact source SHA \`6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e\`, **already explicitly binds** independently generated parent histories, encounter != relationship != household != parenthood != child generation != child initialization, actor-specific perceived relationship states (lines 225–241), independent household entities (243–275), neutral parent slots (277–305), non-fused and asymmetric reciprocity (367–383), independent child-history anchors (429–441), and refuses invented partner probability (480–482). **ADP-015 is an accepted SEMANTIC CONTRACT but not evidence the complete native runtime executes.**

ADP-016 already distinguishes **EVENT_REFERENTIAL_MEMORY** from **CAUSAL_TRACE_MEMORY** (nondeclarative) and refuses fictional memory creation from omniscient histories. ADP-007 creates an actor sparsely with **no default psychology**, while legacy parent-derived default 50 values are explicitly rejected. CAN-010 preserves context-specific need/expectation/striving identities while rejecting direct demographic/caregiver-weight psyche creation. There is a serious prior constitutional frame; the missing work is the *statistical-biographical bridge and its operational consumers*, not a global relationship/metaphysical rewrite.

## 5. The hardest new research questions (not smoothed away)

1. **Non-identifiability:** different latent biographies can produce the same observed survey response. What is identifiable, partially bounded or not identifiable from F1–F4? How many candidate icebergs can be maintained before one fictional path is selected? Can subsequent actual observations distinguish them?
2. **Observation likelihood:** how can we verify a *forward* adult survey-answer emitter without inventing a latent-motive/answer mapping? Separate item-specific answer grammar, desirability, wording and historical measurement from deep personality. If not available, candidate plausibility is SOURCEFREE art, not science.
3. **Coarse-to-fine equivalence:** which event families permit compressed same-rule simulation with causally sufficient summaries? Which require replay of key encounters? What must remain unknown? A model call that writes an internally coherent biography is NOT validated developmental dynamics.
4. **Multiple persons' epistemic bubbles:** own icebergs, other-images, objective relation, actor-specific memory and shared household must not collapse even when only three actors are generated. Test mistaken beliefs surviving an honest event, mutual misunderstanding and asymmetric consent.
5. **Temporal and genealogical constraints:** maternal adult seed, earlier mother life, father prior history, intersection, joint years and birth condition must be consistent. Time-to-event and retrospective birth selection matter; same source cohort at two years does not prove a relationship or longitudinal personal transition.
6. **Computational economics:** allocate event depth according to **causal influence on child** and risk of later first-reader need. Do not silently grant a child who has only received a summary the same learning that would follow from witnessed episodes. Parents cannot become fixed backdrops after birth.
7. **Non-Western developmental plurality:** parent expectations about infancy, exposure, signed/oral learning, care distribution, authority, work and household differ historically. Do not equate mother–father–child genealogical slots with universal nuclear household formation, modern couplehood or the institution of marriage.
8. **Asymmetric world pressure:** mother and father can each be subordinate in one institution and powerful in another, hold multiple apparently contradictory reports and enact them differently under changing material conditions. A domination-protection trade-off (Genesis 3 theological provocation) is one possible historical pattern, not a universal motive or moral axis.
9. **Counterfactual control:** shared initial mother seed and initial history, fixed world, independent named RNG streams. Vary father eligibility, actual encounter, father's independently plausible history, parental material pressure or child access one dimension at a time. Reuse frozen unaltered past; no LLM post hoc rewriting.
10. **Statistical performance:** validate generated survey observables and partner/household distribution against separate eligible benchmarks; preserve design and calibration error; do not calibrate parent iceberg to the narrative's preferred child outcome. Mismatch must be inspectable.

## 6. Proposed minimal executable vertical — principled bite-sized proof

**Experimental, sourcefree only**: one small measured-or-synthetic joint demographic/answer distribution, one sampled mother with exact receipt; at least 3 alternative compressed life histories consistent with her observation cloud but with divergent *context-scoped implicit expectations*; one historically feasible encounter selected from multiple candidates; one father generated conditionally with independently valid compressed history; one objective pair encounter; one joint relationship history with **actual shared world effects**; one child-generation event; 2–3 child-eligible developmental exposures and one later performed consequence. Actual native rule and source authority are separate needed work.

**Hostile controls**:
- same mother seed and survey answers, different candidate iceberg: proposed parent action can diverge only if SOROK's transition **actually reads** a history-derived mediator; random story descriptions do not count.
- mother reports egalitarian attitude, one actual care event: child's learned belief cannot automatically become egalitarian.
- father inferred from a workplace contact despite impossible time/place: refusal, no patch-by-LLM.
- two eligible fathers with contrasting histories: preserve alternative posterior support; selecting one cannot mutate mother's pre-encounter life.
- same actual relationship but asymmetric perceived states; neither state changes merely because the other partner recognizes something.
- objective relationship without household; household absent or changed, other caregiver; no fictitious co-resident father.
- same child as-of body/history, parent absent; child can still be affected through changed care/resources if qualified, NOT through unobserved direct learning.
- sparse history where no scientific U rule: event can occur but psychology remains HELD; no miraculous growth to match a future adult personality.
- named random streams: father branch does not perturb mother's past or child's stable identity once fixed.
- cold replay exact household and child history; summary cannot invent a developmental experience.
- later child must actually choose/attempt something through real eligible world, NOT simply be described as an ideological type.

**Minimal handback to UM-PYAT**: locate existing source-producer and output of **joint mother-sampling**; show exact statistical impediment to partner/birth conditioning and provide best defensible optional inference methods; propose one bounded eligible father/encounter candidate contract. **Minimum handback to UM-SOROK**: locate developmental owner and test an adult compressed-history initialization with exact same state/transition families; preserve perceived-other vs objective relationship vs household; identify the first child developmental consumer that actually requires a parental event. Neither side may certify a full integrated person generator from sourcefree test artifacts.

## 7. Scientific status and caution

**Grounded facts / established architecture**: Source-held same-respondent joint survey products; accepted PYAT conditional composition with source-custody checks; accepted ADP-015 genealogy/relationship semantics; ADP-007 sparse activation; ADP-016 nondeclarative trace; SOROK current batch/event/perception and test-only P3 modules; empirical fact of assortative mating/nonrandom encounters and nonidentification of latent psyche by one report in general.

**Serious methodological tools, unchosen or unvalidated for this application**: conditional sampling, hierarchical measurement model, importance sampling / SMC or ABC under restricted likelihoods, constrained inverse planning, prior/posterior predictive checks, microsimulation/household matching, adaptive fidelity of developmental recursions, sourcefree LLM candidate proposals.

**NOT established**: a unique or measured statistical distribution of parent "icebergs"; a causal rule from WVS answer to innate/implicit disposition; an LLM's event plausibility as verified probability; a matched WVS mother/father observational pair; an exact biological maturation/brain outcome from broad culture labels; one family/psyche fusion; an operative general N9/N10W or full child-generating runtime; a validated compressed-to-fine psychological equivalence.

**Research and architecture advance:** PYAT provides an admissible distribution of what a prospective adult *might publicly report* and what material conditions are plausible. SOROK must construct enough of an actual life, **using the same developmental ontology at lower resolution**, that two adults can meet, misunderstand, change, and together create the concrete world in which a child develops. The child's final being is not reverse-engineered from a selected desired type; it remains the result of continuing situated ontogenesis.

**Governing checksum: adults cannot be two-dimensional ACTORS simply because their prehistories are generated at lower temporal resolution.** They may be **lower-resolution histories** of the same kind of beings.
