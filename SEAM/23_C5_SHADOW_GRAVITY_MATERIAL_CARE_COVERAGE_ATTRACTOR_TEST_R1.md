4. How a sufficiently established pattern may *recurrently bias future attention, action and learned relations*, **without a hard type**, while still allowing radical divergence, mundane life, nonformation, dormancy and change.

**New contribution:** one *two-timescale, history-conditioned developmental model* with a slow candidate attractor summarizing the changing field of recurrent acquired motifs. **Fast loop (actual ongoing living):**
\`W_t, B_{i,t}, G_{i,t}, R_{ij,t}, H_t -> eligible actions from independently active persons / infant signals -> enacted world effects -> actor-specific access -> warranted actor response, memory and local middle-state update\`.

**Slow loop (integration from an adequate historical window):**
\`M_{i,t} = derive_typed_motifs(G_{i,t}, event-recurrence, temporal order, contexts, perturbations)\`;
\`K_{i,t+1} = candidate_attractor_update(K_{i,t}, M_{i,t}, longitudinal_histories, rule)\`.
Later \`K\` may act as **computationally justified compressed state of recurrent organization**, biasing lawful consideration/salience/proposals only where a governed rule has real evidence. It cannot mint knowledge, desire, a wound, a new action, a score or an ideological outcome. **Enneagram affinity is \`interpret(K)\` only**, with no ability to mutate \`G\`, \`K\`, action or response.

**An attractor is not a second, external cause:** If full \(H_t\) and \(G_t\) already determine all behavior, \(K_t=f(H_t,G_t)\) should contain no information not in them. Calling it “downward causal organization” must mean that an actor-executable historical summary mediates how subsequent local processes are selected/limited under a bounded memory/computation budget—not supernatural/independent causation. Test whether K is *useful or necessary*, under compression and cold replay, before storing it as new durable truth.

**Biological starting conditions:** bodily survival/enabling, nutrition, breathing/temperature, sensory-motor activity, signals, early regulation and capacities to learn material contingencies. None equals a universal consciously experienced security/love/power desire. Human care and cultural life are always already situated. Neither language nor special cultural moral concepts are inborn mature state. Never make a deprivation/neglect scenario generate a deterministic later psychological wound.

**Care as production/reproduction and bidirectional activity:** caregivers’ sampled demographic/attitude observables and independently generated deep states, labor/time/care constraints, coactors/kin/institutions, infant body and signaling jointly condition actual care events. The child is a developing participant, not a passive target. This allows *same care activity* to sustain life, reproduce coercion, enable skill and generate future contradictions at once, contingent on actual event histories.

## 3. The missing implementation edge: ordinary life must be GENERATED under coverage, not guessed

A few authored recursions != all childhood. Three resolution levels:
1. **Cheap structured living:** continuous generated families of mundane activities (care, play, transport, work, observation, sleep, feeding, access to artifacts, interactions) conditioned on coactors, world conservation and source-qualified/scenario-model evidence.
2. **Qualified coverage capsule:** actor/time/world/coactors, opportunity and realized denominators, sequences of signal→response, modalities, practice counts, actual actor access, event/source grade, uncertainty and unmodeled intervals. If coarse model simulates only aggregates, do not claim individual unsimulated microepisodes or memories; any aggregate learning rule requires separate empirical license.
3. **Fine event execution:** events where sequence, misperception, distinct actor histories, material consequence, rupture or genuine new acquisition is material to later state and choice.

Distinguish:
\`NOT_ACQUIRED\` (qualified covered time/rule supports nonformation),
\`NOT_MODELED_OR_UNDETERMINED\`,
\`ACQUIRED_DORMANT\`,
\`ACQUIRED_INACCESSIBLE\`,
\`ACTIVE\`,
\`UNKNOWN_RULING\`.
Empty CAN-011 state is lawful but **cannot be promoted to exhaustive child psychological emptiness merely because CPU budget was small**.

Under compression adult history must use **the same generative actor-state semantics** as child ontology; coarse transition fidelity is an independent research proof, not guaranteed by shared field names.

## 5. The hard scientific fork: is shadow-gravity real generative improvement or category laundering?

Three rival implementations with same world, mother/father seed, eventual selected histories and actor-specific possible actions:

- **H0 — Local causal history only:** CAN-011 acquired nodes/relations + bounded memory; no separate attractor summary.
- **H1 — Type-agnostic slow attractor:** above plus recurrent motif/summary \`K\`; it may alter future scoped salience/choice via an accepted rule, not in virtue of label.
- **H2 — Enneagram-coded soft prior:** developer sets nine prototype magnets, lets them pull all future development even without lived evidence. This is an **unsafe candidate**, permissible only as an adversarial negative control, not scientific default.

**Ablation:** H1 is warranted only if it produces an inspectable increase in fidelity, explanatory economy, cold-replay causal sufficiency, stability under small perturbation and/or held-out predictive performance for later actions versus H0 under identical capacity and source constraints. **A complex attractor should not win merely by fitting its training stories more closely.** H2 cannot win by human rating of how “type-consistent” a fictional backstory seems.

Use perturbation tests (same current survey/actor tip, different genuine past; same established motif but changed real world; same history only label altered; one recurring motif under two contexts) and check representation, mechanism, frequency separately. Mixed/unstable/multiple/no basin are genuine outcomes.

The engine must not bias toward ideologically dramatic, damaged, saintly, revolutionary or freakish persons; ordinary everyday people and “unremarkable” lives are the dominant test class. Historically unusual actors are reachability/tail challenges, not a desired target distribution.
