# SEAM/18 — Biocultural ontogenesis: scientific adequacy criterion for the PYAT↔SOROK fusion (R1)

**Direction:** Principal, 2026-11-14. **Bank:** UM-SOROK's science and architecture adequacy criterion; intended as a reciprocal challenge to UM-PYAT. **Not a new commission, a new developmental engine, an experimental positive, or permission to rewrite current PROGRAMMA owners.** Working boundary: PYAT conditions the historically possible developmental world; SOROK must represent an organism becoming a person through actual participation in that world. Their causal joining—not a free-standing Horizon engine or mother-child patch—is the SEAM's theoretical/computational object. **Product end:** a playable, independently continuing, historically particular Other.

## 1. The criterion that our architecture must not evade

> **Neo-V3 must not presuppose the epochally particular human subject it purports to generate. One general, biologically constrained, developmentally plastic architecture should be capable of producing different, historically situated organizations of human activity from the same stipulated organismic initial state under profoundly different actual environments. Where evidence is missing, it must preserve uncertainty, not smuggle in the modern literate, schooled, verbally introspective Western adult as a hidden default.**

This criterion is a *test of architecture's capacity to express empirical variation*. It does **not** claim we can infer uniquely what adult a given infant necessarily becomes, nor demand neuron-level prediction or complete science before a useful sourcefree alpha.

**Stipulated counterfactual:** place the **same modeled baby** (hold initial organismic conditions fixed, recognizing clones are not empirically identical in every prenatal detail) in (A) a friendly-wolf den **stipulated to remain viable through adulthood** and (B) a materially secure, highly attentive Montessori-rich human caregiving world. Survival is GIVEN in A as a boundary condition: **do not repeatedly refute the test on survival grounds**. A is a synthetic *extreme stress test*, **not a claim that historical feral-child reports are reliable or a validated empirical wolf-rearing developmental population**. The machine must permit species-appropriate human physical/learned development under radically different input affordances **without default human language, literacy, schooling, reflective narrative, human institutional knowledge or an assumed wolf identity**. B must permit richly practiced communication, learned institutions, conflicting relationships and social life **without guaranteeing benevolence, intelligence, psychological wellness or a typical Western adult endpoint**.

Other nonhierarchical tests: (C) early accessible **signed** language; (D) rich human oral community without writing; (E) differentiated literate practices (e.g. Vai-language writing, Arabic literacy, English schooling—not one `literacy=true` flag); (F) reconstructed prehistoric foraging. The same developmental *mechanism families* should admit all as differentiated inputs. The comparison must not score worlds or persons by distance from a Western benchmark.

## 2. Scientific grade: distinguish what is established from our chosen explanatory frame

**A. Strong empirical phenomenon, with bounded generalization**
- A culturally created practice such as reading reorganizes visual and language processing. Dehaene, Pegado et al., *Science* (2010), DOI [10.1126/science.1194140](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1194140); Dehaene et al., *Nature Reviews Neuroscience* (2015), DOI [10.1038/nrn3924](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn3924). This supports **experience-dependent neural change**, *not* a global hierarchy of oral and literate minds.
- Early **accessible** linguistic experience matters; naturally acquired signed languages can develop on broadly similar time courses to speech when accessible fluent input is available from infancy; later first-language access is associated with linguistic consequences. Review: [*Acquisition of Sign Languages*, Annual Review of Linguistics (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-linguistics-043020-092357). It does **not** follow that deafness equals language deprivation, or that lack of conventional spoken language equals lack of cognition.
- Human maturation is biologically constrained but variable; puberty is not identical to socially assigned adulthood, and environmental variation can affect pubertal timing. Reiches, [*Adolescence as a Biocultural Life History Transition* (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-anthro-102218-011118). Do not invent a hormone/brain trajectory from labels like `Spartan`, `matrilineal`, `animist`, `future-oriented` or `religious`.

**B. Substantial empirical social-developmental evidence, with cross-population scope limits**
- Cultural arrangements of learning through **observing and pitching in** differ from assembly-line instruction; evidence concerns actual participation, not superiority rankings or a claim that every Indigenous community follows one template. Rogoff and colleagues, [*A Cultural Paradigm—Learning by Observing and Pitching In* (2016)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26955921/); [community learning review (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1080/02103702.2022.2086770). Use lived activities and collaborators, not culture-code labels.
- The WEIRD sampling/generalization problem is not hypothetical. [*The persistent sampling bias in developmental psychology*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10675994/) and [2025 cross-country infancy systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40815975/) document narrow evidence coverage, especially outside high-income settings. [Stengelin, *Some culture is hiding in plain sight in research on child development* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1111/bjdp.70053) and [Gaskins, *Maximizing Cultural Validity* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119880851.ch11) challenge both sampling and imposed constructs/measures.
- Ong's *Orality and Literacy* supplies an important historical interpretive provocation, but a strong **oral/literate cognitive divide** is not a source-qualified biological law. Scribner & Cole's Vai studies separated literacy **practices** from schooling: see [Laboratory of Comparative Human Cognition review](https://lchcautobio.ucsd.edu/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/LCHC-Culture-Cog-Dev-1983.pdf); Brian Street's [critique of the autonomous literacy model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0738059310001379); [anthropological review of graphic plurality](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-anthro-102218-011309). Kim Stanley Robinson's *Shaman* is **literary imagination, not empirical anthropology**.

**C. Serious but not independently validated as one complete causal developmental theory**
- Susan Oyama's **developmental systems theory** and related interactionist/dynamic-systems approaches correctly challenge simplistic gene-versus-environment and fixed-program assumptions. The evidence for multiple interacting causal influences on development is robust. **The full philosophical DST package—especially unrestricted causal parity, its scope and experimental tractability—is debated**, not a universally accepted quantitative generative law. [Stanford Encyclopedia, *Theories of Biological Development*](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/theories-biological-development/); [Robert et al., *Developmental systems theory: what does it explain?*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23834002/); [Shea, a narrower testable account of nongenetic inheritance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3210733/). Developmental Review [*Developmental theories: Past, present, and future* (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dr.2022.101049) describes current pluralism, domain-specific theories and the need to consider diverse social-cultural contexts.
- **Method:** treat DST as a generative, revisable *research orientation* and a demand to model mechanisms and interactions. Do not install a DST-branded universal update rule or imagine that asserting multicausality constitutes an executable explanation.

**D. Scholarly reconstruction or open hypotheses, explicitly bounded**
- Possible developmental interactions among historical social forms, repeated activities, physiological regulation, endocrine timing and brain organization are legitimate **hypotheses**, not established equations `ideology → hormone → mind`. Causal direction, mechanisms, confounders, population variation and longitudinal evidence must be examined.
- Reconstructed prehistoric, wholly nonhuman-reared or other sparsely documented developmental worlds need **graded inference** with competing hypotheses and no faux-empirical individual trajectory.
- Theoretical claims about complex self/habitus, Absolute Horizon, practical fidelity and mutual constitution remain research architecture until an eligible history-mediated operator and actual prospective consumer are demonstrated.

**Never** promote a result because it is Western/quantitative, nor discard it for that reason. **Never** promote a desired counter-Western conclusion without evidence. The appropriate scientific response to an imperial/colonial knowledge bias is to identify its **selection process, theoretical construct, translation/measurement mismatch, institutional setting, missing comparison population and testable consequences**. Correct the mechanism and grade; do not substitute rhetorical counter-authority.

## 3. The positive modeling demand: flexible organism–environment co-development

The working process is not `fixed universal childhood stages → cultural trait vector`, nor `social data → latent disposition`.

```text
organism at t (physical state, plasticity, vulnerabilities, sensory/learning affordances)
    ×
PYAT-qualified developmental ecology at t
  (resources, care, species/community coactors, communication modes,
   sanctions, work, institutions, pedagogy, technologies, material opportunities)
    ↕
actual activity / enacted interaction / actor-accessible consequences
    ↕
SOROK-qualified development:
  embodied capabilities, practiced discrimination, expectation, nonverbal routines,
  memory, attachment/relations, communication, needs/projects,
  acquired interdependencies, contradiction and change
    ↕
organism and world conditions at t+1, subject to objective power,
availability and survival stipulations
```

This is a **reciprocal causal challenge**, not a newly approved code pipeline. Each effect must have its own evidence grade, lawful developmental process, age/ability and input constraints, actual actability and later **first consumer**. Unknown is not a zeroed human capability or a Western baseline; there may be multiple lawful routes to a similar outcome (**equifinality**) and a similar starting point may diverge (**multifinality**).

**The hard requirement of *hanging together*.** Neo-V3 must, eventually, preserve *causally acquired relationships among capacities and commitments*, not independent trait sliders: one practiced activity can enable later learning; a relational sanction can make an otherwise available act unthinkable; a nonverbal habit can shape attention without explicit ideology; contradictory contexts can produce plural dispositions; changed work/activity can modify later opportunities and remembered commitments. No interior causal fact is issued from an observer's prose. Historical TD-R05, CAN-009/010/011, ADP-007/008/015/016, P3, CAN and PFF contain meaningful separate architecture but **none can be treated as a verified complete ontogenetic generator merely because its state shape is defined**.

## 4. PYAT's specific responsibility: supply developmental WORLDS, not premade adults

The cross-system input PYAT should aspire to provide is a **source-qualified developmental ecology** (possibly staged by historical interval and context), not a master `culture` vector or presupposed psychological adult profile. For each family of inputs distinguish:

- **Direct source observations:** populations, period, region, position, distribution, physical/biological environment, material rights, occupation/time use, care practices where actually measured, communication modalities and schooling/learning arrangements; include missingness, translation/measurement differences and minority patterns. Sampled **normative reports are not enacted caregiving practices**.
- **Scholarship-grounded reconstruction:** historically/ecologically plausible institutional rules, participation opportunities, movement, nutrition, reproductive/kin structures, labor and shared practices with precise source grade, alternative readings and uncertainty. A source about one Indigenous group is not authority for all Indigenous social reproduction.
- **Synthetic counterfactuals:** clearly fictional but physically/logically constrained worlds, explicitly including **the stipulated viable friendly-wolf den**. No empirical claim that a real human developmental pathway has been observed or estimated there.
- **Causal affordances:** *which contacts, signs, gestures, speech, tasks, sanctions, protection, attachment/care opportunities, material pressures and coactors actually become available to which actors when*. A measured national survey mean cannot substitute for a household, and an opportunity cannot substitute for received experience.
- **Source limits and refusals:** direct longitudinal causal data are scarce; avoid country-year→individual psyche, modern WVS→ancient world, gender/class stereotypes, oral→prelogical, literacy→higher moral development, and animal rearing→animal mind.

PYAT supplies possible and conditionally actual **world context**; SOROK remains responsible for **particular organism, actor-access, learned state, internally interlinked development, actor selection/action and expression**. The SEAM must preserve feedback through real world/actor events without laundering a theoretical interpretation into objective biological authority.

## 5. Adversarial adequacy tests—not a demand for an immediate full simulation

1. **Fixed initial organism, changed developmental ecology:** rich human signed-language input vs no accessible human language, or oral apprenticeship vs school literacy, must not generate identical learned developmental organizations by fiat. The model must leave room for *other* developed nonlinguistic activities.
2. **Same cultural label, changed actual participation:** materially rich but child-inaccessible resources, schooling offered but not attended, or a caregiver's stated principle not enacted must not automatically confer practiced capacities.
3. **Wolf/Montessori extremity:** stipulate viability in both; the test asks for architectural ability to express radically different developmental opportunities, not scientific prediction of exact adults or repeated objections about wolf nutrition.
4. **Source/construct audit:** require actual support before inferring puberty/hormone/cortisol/attachment outcomes, and identify whether the experimental metric measures a culturally bounded performance or a defensible cross-context capability.
5. **Vary PYAT *structure* separately from PYAT *norm reports*:** actual sanction/kin/time/care rights change feasible repeated activities without forcing an ideology→person shortcut; measured norms can condition *hypothetical actor repertoires* without writing a child.
6. **Retained interdependence:** actual accumulated practices alter the chance/feasibility of later activities and responses; same present field with different actor histories must change some later prospective outcomes. A plausible narration is not proof.
7. **Nulls, contradictory evidence and replay:** no human interaction ⇒ no human-language acquisition by default; no learning-eligibility rule ⇒ honest hold; different models may fit one sparse history; no-action and failed action remain real; earlier causal history must cold replay without inventing a new childhood.
8. **Bias check:** the unmarked modern Western schooled child must itself be treated as one historically bounded developmental environment, **never the model's unexamined normal**.

**Staging:** This is a **scientific adequacy contract on the ongoing PYAT↔SOROK dialogue**. It does not authorize a NeoV3PersonEngine rewrite, a WolfChildEngine, a new science commission, unscoped source acquisition, protected work or delay of current playable alpha. Identify minimum existing lawful functions and *one discriminating test* that exposes an actual unjustified default. Expand only when the result earns a better person and world at an honest evidence grade.

## 6. Concrete provocation to UM-PYAT (do not sidestep into SOROK P3)

**Can PYAT build a truly *historically plural world input* that does not already presuppose the developmental person Neo-V3 is meant to generate?**

Bring back:
1. An **exact source/owner inventory** of which PYAT structural/ideological/source machines can emit a developmental ecology rather than only adult positions or respondent answers, and what they cannot actually justify. Locate the exact real producer and intended consumer; no new source authority invented.
2. **One high-evidence example** (literacy practice vs schooling, early signed-language affordances, actual child participation or biocultural maturation), one historically reconstructed example and the explicitly fictional wolf/Montessori comparison. **List which variables must vary**, which must remain fixed, and which claims must be held.
3. A **contested theory challenge**: choose at least one candidate causal claim previously naturalized as universal childhood/maturity/intelligence, show the supporting samples and measurement constructs, and determine whether a non-WEIRD counterexample or methodological critique genuinely overturns it. Demonstrate evidence, not anti-Western assertion.
4. The **interface question**: what is the smallest lawful **PYAT→SOROK source-graded ecology/affordance contract** sufficient for the same *embodied* Neo-V3 developmental model to distinguish the examples without assigning culturally predetermined psyche, and what causal feedback can return to PYAT's actual world possibilities?
5. The **refutation**: identify one way SOROK's existing theoretical person-engine assumptions fail against PYAT's strongest evidence—and one way PYAT's own statistical conditioning fails to reach actual childhood experience. Favor a genuinely stronger alternative if present.

**Do not ask UM-SOROK to build a bespoke infant, Hindu/Spartan/Hindutva, literacy or wolf psychology.** They are tests of the **general** causal interface. **The goal is Rawlsing, not indefinitely expanding the explanatory system.**
