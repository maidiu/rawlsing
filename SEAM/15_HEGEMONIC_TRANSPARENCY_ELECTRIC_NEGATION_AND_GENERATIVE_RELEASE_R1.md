## 0. The essential correction: attachment and institution-label aversion were too shallow

The principal's observation corrects a weakness in my SEAM/13 toy: **hegemony is not merely an actor's positive preference for an institution. It may be the invisible grammar within which that actor perceives what reality is.** A person may avow universal egalitarian moral ideals while enacting a national, familial, religious, racial, class or other hegemonic worldview whose exclusions appear not ideological but *natural, obvious, unavoidable, self-protective or the very defense of reality*. Threatening the normal order can recruit otherwise unremarkable people into materially consequential defense. The *Matrix* “ordinary person recruited into an Agent” is a useful metaphor for this situated activation. It is not proof that strongly socialized people are necessarily violent, nor does it supply an evidence-based transition law.

A specific nation-state's genocidal violence is an extreme case of possible avowed-universalism / enacted-exclusion contradiction, not a proxy for every citizen's cognition or an inference justified by attitude prevalence. The scientific questions are precisely **which actual ideological practice, social relation, target exclusion, institutional power, perceived threat and performed act** connect those levels.

The next movement is **electric because the inherited object is partly constitutive of the person's practical life**: it may have furnished care, belonging, grammar, aspiration, skill, kinship, identity, employment and ordinary happiness *and* enabled domination or demanded complicity. Contradictions can become intolerably costly without making all inherited content false. Extrication threatens actual livelihood, relationships, shared reality and acquired capacities. A low-investment unrelated tradition can be disagreed with calmly; a constitutive one can provoke ferocious defensive identification or equally ferocious reactive negation.

**Third movement: the negation itself can become transparent and totalizing.** To make “I am absolutely not-I” the organizing self-definition is to continue granting I a *negative sovereign* status. Possible negation of that negation is not restoring I, tolerating arbitrary harm, or finally attaining a morally superior stage. It is **the loss of I's privileged status as either the positive or negative organizing reference for the person's independent action**. The biography remains; some benefits and wounds remain; one may use or discard actual inherited practices according to present purposes and dangers. Critically, the person can **make something new**: a project, relation or activity whose meaning need not be either faithful-to-I or against-I. The principal's *tohu va-vohu → let there be light* registers this *constructive* generativity, not mere reconciliation.

## 1. Four distinct computable operations; no stage selector

### H — Hegemonic transparency and defensive recruitment

**Given:** recurrent actual norm-bearing socialization K (care, dependence, reward, punishment, language), actor-visible uptake U, actor's concrete economic/kinship/institutional tethers, and an actual present event.

**Candidate event interpretation:** `perceive_actor(actual_event, learned_normalization, competing_visible_evidence, actual_access)` can classify a publicly observable challenge to I as **a threat to what is normal/real**, *not consciously as a threat to ideology*. This affects the attention/response possibilities of an otherwise ordinary person. The source grade is a testable hypothesis; “transparent grammar” cannot be a universal fixed stereotype or inferred from one survey answer. Actual enforcement remains gated by the actor's material/organizational capacity. A citizen can sincerely declare equal human worth while acting as the institution's agent when their accustomed order is threatened. **Discourse, practical premise, perception, candidate action and performed deed remain separate.**

A counterfactual must preserve the exact public avowal while changing formative institutional practice; the model should alter perceived salience and actual chosen action if H applies. Another must preserve the formed actor while removing the actual ability to sanction; “threatened” alone does not create power.

### B — Breach, visibility and painful reactive negation

**Given:** the actor has actually acquired a criterion within I's practices and encounters particular, *accessible* contradictions in I's conduct or its claimed universality.

The encounter can make a previously transparent institution into **this institution**—an object that can be criticized, fled or refused. A learned practical criterion can condemn its original carrier, without the actor thereby achieving independence. Continued material entanglement, relational loss, structural danger, remembered nurture, and the felt indignity of still being governed by exposed false claims can all intensify reactive opposition.

**Candidate U:** repeated *accessible* contradictions may erode positive carrier trust while leaving or increasing **carrier-centrality**: I remains the organizing reference, now in a negative sign. Oppositional self-description, costly refusals and demand for complete separation are observable; no one can infer a universal interior “hatred” from one action, and real boundary-setting against an unsafe institution can be entirely proportionate. Historical attachment and alienation can coexist.

### N — Negation itself becomes an object

**Given:** actual prior anti-I acts, objective costs they incurred, the actor's access to those results, independently acquired alternative practical abilities/relations, and a possible (not automatic) capacity to compare *being free from I* against *organizing every act around I*.

**Candidate U:** only when there is **evidence of a practiced alternative and an actor-accessible contradiction of their own reactive life** may a bounded hypothesis revise *carrier-centrality as reference frame*. Mere pain, a therapist's explanation, abstract moral preaching or exposure to a good institutional offer does not accomplish the operation automatically. Different update hypotheses may preserve the same oppositional identification. The subject can discover that nonassociation is not freedom if the forbidden institution still selects the whole range of their actions.

**Key distinction:** historical records of I's original nourishment and later domination are **not erased**. The person's *present dependence of action on I as positive/negative symbol* may diminish. This is the difference between forgetting the institution and no longer being ruled by it. A later fresh injury or new dependence can re-activate a real relation; freedom is not sealed metaphysical invulnerability.

### G — Generative appropriation and independent composition

The endpoint cannot be merely “accept a safe, helpful service offered by I.” That was my earlier model's too-small demonstration.

**Candidate constructive action:** use actually acquired capacities, available coparticipants, time/resources, real unmet needs, partial histories of trust, and a sourcefree proposal/validation operator to form a **new feasible project** not predetermined as a moral plot reward. It may reuse parts of the inherited world, combine unrelated practices, or do something not definable by I at all. The new project becomes real only through admitted performed acts; intended project ≠ executable plan ≠ world effect ≠ actor's later interpretation. Social relations and coactors determine whether the possibility has actual room to exist.

A **stronger freedom test** than benign sponsor acceptance is: when I is absent, does the person still have viable actual projects, relations and activities? When the label “sponsored by I” is swapped without changing practical costs/benefits, does it cease controlling choice *under the selected hypothesis*? When I actually endangers somebody, does the person remain able to refuse it for material reasons? A caring person may refuse cooperation indefinitely because the institution remains harmful, without being coded as compulsively reactive.

## 2. One executed finite-domain toy, with source and effects boundaries

I built a reproducible **isolated Python sourcefree reference** in the working container at `/mnt/data/sorok_horizon_field_probe.py`, with matching JSON assertion result `/mnt/data/sorok_horizon_field_probe_result.json`. Those are conversation artifacts; **this repo document does not claim the script itself has been committed to GitHub**, and the filenames must not be interpreted as repo paths. The model uses one entirely fictional institution I and the bounded practical good of care; it does **not** sample PYAT respondents or assert empirical personality coefficients.

The executable reference has the following **candidate research-side** distinctions, whose values are arbitrary finite-domain test parameters:

```text
ActorEvidence:
   carrier_naturalized           # observed formative enactment, NOT total psyche
   carrier_visible_conflict      # accessible contradicting events
   reactive_binding              # actual learned anti-I default in selected hypothesis
   magnetic_focus                # I as a positive or negative choice-reference
   experienced_tethers           # actually experienced material/relational dependencies
   enacted_good                  # specifically practiced/received action criterion
   skills                        # proven episodes of particular available competence
   avowals                       # separately recorded speech, NEVER action rule
   accessible/inaccessible refs  # exact world/actor epistemic boundary

Episode:
   source identity, event kind, actor access, visible outcome, participation,
   actual paid cost, prior causal act link (for cost-of-opposition evidence)

Choice:
   actor EXPECTED effect, objective ACTUAL effect (separate),
   sponsor, institution-defense/against tags, objective effort/danger, feasibility
```

**Reference logic**:

- Actual child participation in institutional care can increase learned naturalization and practiced care. A speech claim is retained as avowed/heard, not a trait grant.
- `interpret_nonconformity` looks at *actor-accessed* challenge, naturalized premises, contradictory evidence and experienced material ties before recruiting defense. Sanction/defense is still an available world action, not a guaranteed physiological or political response.
- Accessible institutional violations can reduce carrier reliance while increasing reactive centrality. Inaccessible acts do not change actor cognition.
- Merely practicing outside I adds concrete capacity **without automatically dissolving reactive identity**.
- A later reflective revision is allowed only with **an actual earlier performed reactive refusal**, the actor's witnessed material cost of that refusal, and alternative practices available. A rival U can issue no revision.
- Successful reflection can diminish active positive *and* negative institution-referenced choice influence **without erasing the earlier history**.
- `compose_project` generates the named feasible candidate `organize_care_repair` **only** from an explicitly available world recipe, genuinely acquired `care` and `repair` participation, independent coactor, and enough resources. This is an intentionally small compositional proof—not a generic theory of autonomous creative imagination. No skills/coactor/resources => no new project.
- `choose` uses actor **expected** benefit, active historically sourced premises and actual feasibility; `actual_perform` writes the **objective** outcome separately. The actor can be wrong.
- One deterministic codepath applied to different histories admits identification/defense, reactive symbolic refusal, de-centered real activity, no learning, indifference, inability, and harmful consequence. **No function accepts `phase`, `liberated`, `victim`, `genocidaire`, `AbsoluteHorizonScore` or a predestined script ending.** Fixture `sample(stage)` only supplies *different actual episode histories* for counterfactual comparison; it is not a live actor-stage input.

**Actual executed result:** **36/36 test assertions passed locally**, including ordinary person recruited under a threatened world-assumption despite universalist words; stronger threat from observed dependence; no formation/no activation; no punitive power/no sanction; critique absent when violation unseen or its causal criterion not acquired; heightened reactive identity after experienced contradiction; indifference toward an unconstituting distant institution; reactive costs and independent practice alone insufficient for de-centering; prior enacted refusal + consequential recognized cost + independent practice allowing but not forcing diminished negative sovereignty; sponsor-swap equivalence after de-centering; institutional history preserved; independent project even with I absent; project impossible without coactor, time/resources or practice; no fake learned outcome; and actual world effects distinct from expectations.

This establishes **logical and finite computational possibility**, not scientific adequacy. In particular, the toy's bounded integer saliences are not developed or estimated from the full multi-field PYAT corpus. Nor does it prove any person or group in history behaved this way, nor solve the hidden cognitive-state inference problem. The stronger model would need actor-specific relational commitments, plural and incommensurable practical claims, counterfields and actual longitudinal source inquiry. Refusing to be scripted into a narrative of emancipation remains a test.
