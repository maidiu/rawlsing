## 1. The smallest **computable** object is not a stage, ideology or virtue: it is an acquired, contested ordering of reasons under live possibilities

What the person has **experienced** is not the same as what the world **did**. What the person **avows** is not the same as what they **learn to anticipate**, and neither automatically determines which claim actually governs their **material acts under competing costs**.

To model the user-described phenomenon without an `AbsoluteHorizonScore` or “negation stage,” the smallest experimental model needs:

1. **Qualified normative claims with identifiable carriers.** A source-informed adult's reported saliences are *possible antecedents*, not a child's beliefs, deeds or justifications. In a particular synthetic world, an adult/institution may actually *claim* to care for those entrusted to it. That claim is distinct from its deeds.
2. **Admitted objective behavior and consequences.** `C4/P2/world` records when a socializer protects, neglects, punishes, materially enables, transfers resources, or responds to another actor. An actual claim alone cannot issue a learned habit, and an unsupported report cannot issue a historical action.
3. **Actor-accessed evidence and participation.** CAN-008 R2 actor-specific receivability and P3-qualified prior history determine whether this child observed a deed, perceived its consequence, received care, participated in providing care, or knows neither act nor reason. Unseen injury can still modify world conditions without becoming child cognition.
4. **Bounded practical learning hypotheses.** Distinct, amendable `U` hypotheses update **(a) association with the value-claim's institutional carrier**, and **(b) context-specific practiced commitment to the substantive activity**, differently. The hypothesis can also issue **zero uptake**. These do not exhaust a person, establish human virtue, or invent a general psyche substrate.
5. **Materially qualified future choices.** Given real time, available coactors, feasible attempts, sanction and effort, an actor's lawfully accessible understanding ranks possible acts. *Actual world effect* is written separately after the attempted/performed act. The model may mispredict the outcome.
6. **Multiple contexts and comparative evidence.** In a sourcefree model, earlier care associated with one institution, later violation by it, and subsequent practical care *without* it can change the weighting of substantive activity relative to institutional attachment, without a preselected emancipatory event flag.

**The core transformation** is that the historically acquired **object of fidelity** can gain causal priority over the institution that taught the actor its name or practice. The institution's authority is no longer treated as coterminous with the value. The actor can reject the institution's harmful demand and may accept an actually beneficial act associated with that institution, if safe and feasible. Another life can instead remain identified, turn reactively against all its acts, lack the alternative capacity, lack access to contradiction, or refuse an objectively dangerous institution for good reasons.

This is a candidate route of **identification → accessible contradiction → alienation/visibility → *possible* criterion portability**, not a deterministic path, a moral leaderboard or a guaranteed terminal state.

For the initial reference function:

```text
practiced_priority += practice_uptake   ONLY for accessible performed/received benefit
carrier_affinity  += carrier_confirmation  for witnessed beneficial enactment by I
carrier_affinity  -= contradiction_uptake  for witnessed contrary enactment by I

actor_option_score = practiced_priority × actor_expected_effect
                     + carrier_affinity[carrier] − effort − danger
```

All coefficients are **explicit arbitrary toy integers**, NOT calibrated probabilities, developmental rates, measurement of inner fidelity or validated compensatory morality. The score's additive structure is **not** an adequate general model of multiple noncommensurable claims, coercion and Absolute Horizon; its function is to prove that an **actual update-and-choice algorithm** can generate all three distinguishable phenomena *without labeling the desired one as the answer*. In the next implementation increment, multiple competing claims should be represented as **scoped, defeasible reason priorities** and actual resource costs through existing world/actor owners; no singleton `care` can represent a person's Horizon.

The model distinguishes **subjective expectation** from **actual world effect**: a helpful-looking institutional offer may objectively fail or harm, with the result admitted as world truth; it cannot reach actor learning without reception. This corrects an epistemic collapse identified while constructing the initial probe.

## 3. The three trajectories are *emergent outputs* of the same code, not three personalities chosen by the author

The **same unchanged learn/choose functions** are run on these growing, particular sourcefree example histories (only an example, not population prevalence):

| Actually accessible history | Learned local state | Distinct prospective choice |
|---|---|---|

**These do not certify institutional legitimacy, pathological aversion, or a person who has “reached the Absolute.”** They are local counterfactual results showing the program can express possible transformations. **Ablations that prevent narrative fiat:** no personally witnessed contradiction -> no corresponding psychological update; inaccessible consequence -> not known; same history but `practice_uptake=0` -> no acquired local priority; same history with `contradiction_uptake=0` -> institutional identification persists; prohibit an option by material cost -> it is not chosen; increase actual danger -> refusal may become rational; change only a carrier sponsor -> a reactive outcome may differ; same expected benefit but contrary actual outcome -> the world changes differently while the actor's earlier decision remains the same.
