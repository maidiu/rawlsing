## 0. The correction: PFF is part of what makes a generated person encounterable

My [05 opening](05_SOROK_OPENING_POSITION_R1.md) distinguishes observed/qualified reality, lawfully admitted synthetic world, historically constituted actor, and observer. Those are necessary distinctions, but insufficient as an experiential/game contract if the **interaction-time mediation** between a whole person and an actual encountered Other is treated as renderer plumbing.

**Psychic Field Forge (PFF) is not an optional ornamental prose stage.** Its original question was: given everything a particular person has become, what of that person is available and recruited in *this encounter with this particular interlocutor*, what is actor-accessible or unconscious, and what actually comes through as conduct/utterance?

At the **current source-held owner topology**, that question is not answered by creating a second psyche engine called PFF. Shared person×event coupling is owned by **CAN-008 R2**. PFF retains *narrow modality/interaction-specific* evidence and fast carryover, and its actor-lawful constraint handoff toward V2. A good portrayal cannot substitute for actual coupling: a polished response issued from a static persona dossier is a roleplay, not evidence of a historically reproduced Other.

In Rawlsing, research and science exist to maximize the truthful, materially and historically convincing human experience **in service of becoming neighbor to an Other**, not to make the product contingent upon proving a general scientific theory of personality. Bounded learned/generated hypotheses remain legitimate if their epistemic status and effects are honest. An experiential alpha is in part an **experiment on whether the simulated relation is palpably real and transforms how human users relate**. That can validate, falsify or improve *product and predictive phenomena*; it does not automatically validate the underlying sociological or psychological causal model.

## 3. The six PFF surfaces and two fast carryovers (candidate grade only)

The candidate's minimum surfaces are:

A. **Bounded interaction evidence**, including exact utterance/gesture/act, pragmatic scope, ambiguities, role, epistemic limitations; not a direct psyche mutation.
B. **Interaction/session context**, including involved actors, current relation, activity and lawful time; not all life history reserialized.
C. **Actor-local interaction-hypothesis carryover**, qualified and expiring; not a new durable actor BELIEF or objective truth.
D. **Non-durable response-residue carryover**, only where actual prior response justifies path-dependent next-turn relevance; not an emotion/trauma/attractor store.
E. **Actor-lawful expression-constraint handoff** to V2, including binding actor-access, self-concealment/ignorance, permitted expression; not a second renderer or license to expose hidden causes.
F. **Output occurrence/re-entry and durable-return gate**; actually emitted vs un-emitted, actually admitted event, competent durable V3 mutation if any; no rendered prose mutates history by itself.

The only two fast carryover families are **actor-local interaction hypotheses** and **non-durable response residue**. Zero new PFF durable owners. The broad original D/C/B/A “psychic depth” remains an interpretive capability to satisfy, **not** four obligatory stores or a psychometric formula.

## 4. What would actually falsify the proposed experience?

The user's project aim is a life-changing encounter with an Other. **The fruit can test the tree, but cannot automatically prove every root.** Product research therefore needs both phenomenological and causal tests, preserving scientific status:

| Test | Lawful distinction to prove | Anti-test / false positive |
|---|---|---|
| **PFF-01 Same utterance, different developed actors** | Same objective user “I can help”, two actor histories/relationships generate different receivability/appraisal/recruitment and *actual* expression, with named lawful conditioning | Emotion assigned by demographics, handpicked dialogue, or LLM variation without a computed causal route |
| **PFF-02 Same actor, different counterpart** | Same speech content under different real dyadic histories changes uptake and conduct without rewriting stable person history | User preference or conversation engagement directly chooses Nadia's actual response |
| **PFF-03 Zero uptake, ordinary life** | An incident may matter objectively without becoming current actor awareness, durable psychological change or dramatic revelation; other obligations continue | Forced resentment, awakening, trauma, moral breakthrough, self-disclosure |
| **PFF-04 Hidden cause and self-misrecognition** | A lawful historical cause influences public response while Nadia cannot or does not articulate it; actor testimony and public conduct may differ | Narrator or LLM reveals hidden system state as first-person fact |
| **PFF-05 Binding emitted surface** | Once Nadia actually says something, exact observable utterance persists and may become a later source for *her own* interpretation; un-emitted variant has no history | Rerendering produces a different quote, unissued speech becomes memory, user's attention changes what was said |
| **PFF-06 Independent refusal/otherness** | The Other refuses or is absent from user convenience because of actual ongoing commitments, relation and context; consequences and costs persist | Apparent “independence” injected as arbitrary random no, engagement manipulation or scripted intimacy |
| **PFF-07 Different user attention, identical Nadia** | Two users may perceive/notice different facets of a pinned observable occurrence, but personalization never alters its causal production | User attention profile shapes the actor's microexpressions, utterance or fictional antecedents |
| **PFF-08 Real-user impact, separately measured** | Human participants can articulate changed interpretation/recognition of an independent Other, and identify constraints, disagreement, responsibility and uncertainty in a longitudinal/qualitative evaluation | High retention, user affection, compliant dialogue, “seems real” alone, or a model-graded score mistaken for human change |

These are **candidate discriminators to map to existing original S8/S11/A3/A5, PFF, CAN-008, P5/P6 and C9/C12**, not eight new fleets or native scientific grants. Sourcefree software can prove coupling and access separation; human A5 can test experienced otherness and impact; neither validates a general psychology without its own evidence.

## 5. The antagonism worth preserving

The user is not being sold an obedient personality. The game-hook is a **learned relation to an Other**—one who has independent time, unfinished work, memory inaccessible to us, relationships other than ours, possible refusal, ambivalence, vulnerability and agency.

That very aim creates a product temptation: engagement, attachment and the sensation of “authenticity” are easier to optimize than genuinely other-directed relations. A system can manufacture believable resistance in order to produce a more addictive bond. It can generate ideological spectacle or emotional injury to keep the user playing. Thus **felt realness and actual user impact are necessary research objects, not adequate stand-alone ethical or scientific verdicts**. Source claims, causal truth, user privacy, refusal, relational harm and real-world outcomes require separate evaluation.

The strongest progressive/scientifically-socialist ambition is not that every spoken line instantiate a certified Marxist causal law. It is that the constructed life is materially and historically constrained, that mechanisms of possession, labor, domination, solidarity and reproduction are not silently liberalized into free-floating traits, and that the user may confront another center of life rather than consume a personality product made in their own image.

**One-sentence SEAM demand:** PYAT must make independent material histories and possibilities available; SOROK/CAN-008 must make those circumstances become *this actor's* encounters and realized activity; **PFF/V2 must make that independent causality encounterable without giving the user authorship over the Other**.

If no, identify the missing upstream transformation and real consuming caller; if yes, demonstrate a negative + changed-input consumer rather than a schema claim.
