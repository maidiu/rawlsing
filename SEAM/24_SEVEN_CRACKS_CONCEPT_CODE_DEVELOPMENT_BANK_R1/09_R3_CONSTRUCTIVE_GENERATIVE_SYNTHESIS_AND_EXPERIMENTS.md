## 1. SC-01 — Semantic openness as **acquired discrimination grounded in activity**, not a source-disposition vocabulary

**Retained science:** CAN-011 provides `V3MS_MEDIATOR / V3MS_TENSION / V3MS_PRACTICE` and optional semantic ref; older Field Forge PRF suggests PRE-V3 refs for particular disposition nodes. There is **no demonstrated conflict** unless a future implementer universalizes the older *optional/specific* provenance path. The deeper general insight is a difference between **objective referent available**, **actor senses/uses/discriminates it**, and **actor can explicitly name or articulate it**. Culturally different learning routes can produce capable nonverbal actors, linguistically articulate but practically inexperienced actors, or both.

**More economical potential operation:** reuse already admitted P2 object/activity, coactor and actor-access refs to **nominate**, not issue, a candidate actor-level distinction. Example (entirely synthetic): a child practices distinguishing two real tool tensions before learning an institutional name for that craft. The "referent" may be a traceable objective practice/context rather than a survey code or a newly invented abstract semantic dictionary. A domain-specific CAN-011 rule may transform repeated eligible practice into a scoped `PRACTICE`; a later demonstration/attempt is its first reader. Any naming/explanation is a separate possible capability.

**Computational gain:** a SOROK development owner can evolve psychological contents not enumerated in PYAT surveys **without** accepting unconstrained LLM prose as mental fact. PYAT's task shifts from "supply all possible mental categories" to "qualify actual historical conditions and available practices where sourced." Survey field support and world-object availability are different types of evidence.

**Hard negative:** identical lived tool encounters but another culturally loaded label must not create a psychological difference; a PYAT survey nomination cannot create a named mediator without actual actor update. If an object was outside the actor's accessible world, learning is not admitted. **Complication:** acquisition of imagination, inference, invention and absent-object concepts may not always require a present external referent; the domain rule must permit a historically and causally grounded transformation from prior acquired contents, rather than forcing every idea to correspond to a currently perceived P2 object.

## 2. SC-02 — Adult inverse generation as **branching forward lives with an observation boundary**

**Retained science:** an actual PYAT respondent-coherent adult answer cloud constrains the **observable tip**, not latent motives, unique past, an empirically identified latent posterior or a birth-cohort frequency. The mother's selected past becomes actual *in the fictional branch*; rejected pasts do not become her memories. Same CAN/ADP semantics as child, less temporal resolution.

**Generative improvement:** split the test into **two independent admissibility questions**:
- `SOURCE TIP`: Is the adult observation itself eligible under the specific C11/I03/I05/FF measured source, risk, respondent universe, release, design and as-of?
- `FORWARD HISTORY`: Does the synthetic *individual* with a generated state/history, passed through an explicitly MODEL_GRADED answer-emission hypothesis at the correct observation time, **permit** that surface report? The measurement model is not a population-estimated likelihood unless independently established.

Instead of ranking invented biographies by how well their prose sounds like the answer, select from **several constraint-compatible trajectories** with a separately declared candidate policy and named ADP-020 selection. Carry a small **observation-consistency explanation** (question, as-of, rule grade, what was actually checked, what remained unknown), not a pseudo-posterior over souls.

**Experiment that reaches behavior:** hold the empirical answer cloud and present material opportunities fixed; compare two admissible compressed maternal histories that preserve different CAN/ADP acquired organization. A competent later actor first-reader should be able to produce different *performed* maternal acts when justified, or same act/no-action where warranted. Stronger falsifier: changing biographical prose while registered acquired state and current world stay fixed does **nothing**. A valid initial draw without the later consumer is a **sampled observational fictional tip**, not a functioning mother.

**Computational economy:** the parent compressed operator need not recount every episode. It must preserve every retained **claim that its later first consumer actually depends on**, and mark other time/experience as unmodeled. Candidate future-reader tests can challenge whether a prelife summary is too lossy. This is not yet a validated rule for automatically choosing a summary.

## 3. SC-03 — Backward father generation as **constraint-compatible possible history**, not reverse causation or a general past-merge engine

**Governing computational order remains exact:** mother generated → her actual trajectory yields eligible encounters → select one E → **then computationally generate father backward-conditionally on E and the selected branch's eventual birth** → construct independently viable pre-encounter paternal history → forward-recurse two distinct parents and actual coactor/relationship history. The father’s in-world past precedes E; no prior mother-world history is rewritten.

**Generative operation:** carry **a partial as-of constraint witness** for E, not a full global past. It names only claims needed to justify the selected intersection and later continuity: time, physical/role co-location, eligible path/network, institutional/material eligibility, represented commitments/known conflicts, separately model-graded conditional-birth requirements. Construct different father candidate histories *inside a candidate scope*, using the same developmental ontology at parent-compressed resolution. At the join, check:

- **COMPATIBLE**: asserted candidate claims coexist with exact same-scope facts already represented in mother/shared world.
- **CONTRADICTED**: actual represented world claim and father claim cannot both hold; refuse that candidate, preserve the mother.
- **UNKNOWN/NOT REPRESENTED**: no actual fact establishes conflict; do **not** invent proof of compatibility or demand microevent replay of every earlier year. An admissibility decision under unknown facts must state its explicitly MODEL_GRADED assumptions; if required constraints are unknown, HOLD rather than assert certainty.

**This is analogous to a partial constraint-satisfaction test, not a new father posterior.** Conditional search is allowed as a generative *algorithm* without pretending that relative candidate probabilities are observed couple/fertility frequencies.

**Cheapest prospective code route:** use existing P2/ADP-015 identity, effective-time, branch and objective-relationship contracts; let a *test harness* enumerate finite candidates and reject explicitly contradictory ones before any shared-world commitment. A candidate father prehistory might be a compressed **actor-local state and sourced/material as-of claims**, not newly appended old P2 world events. Shared past events that truly matter for later causal effects require their competent owner, not an invented event-insertion bypass.

**Hard tests:** impossible place/time at E; two equally eligible independent father prehistories; father's invented earlier action contradicts a sealed public event; father's unmodeled earlier work should **not** count as a contradiction; merely changing father's private biography prose must not alter mother's frozen past. Avoid a hidden assumption that every child requires a co-resident male caregiver or a socially universal two-parent home.

## 4. SC-04 — Plural parent agency as **one public deed, different objective effects and private histories**

SEAM/20 explicitly separates mother, father, mother's perceived father, father's perceived mother, objective relationship, objective household, and actor-specific joint event/access history. Birth/lineage remains another separate obligation. One world/root must not erase independent actor identities or private experiences.

**Generative improvement:** whenever an actual actor event becomes relevant to a coactor, consider three **separately authorized operations** (not automatically simultaneous):
1. **World effect**: P2 actual event and rights/material transaction change *objective* world, including effects on others' feasible resources or time only if the competent household/structural owner admits them.
2. **Actor access**: each recipient independently may or may not encounter consequences, see the act, overhear speech, know an explanation, misattribute an intention; source/clock/perceptual channel governs receipt.
3. **Actor formation/selection**: only a valid domain/CAN/ADP rule can produce acquired updates or later proposals. The person's accessible account may conflict with public event and the other actor's account.

A maternal overnight shift may change her own hours/cash, a second caregiver's feasible availability (if actual shared-world rule), and a child's exposure to a peer (if independently admitted), while the child never learns *why* the mother was absent. Nothing about that alone issues a rejection wound. This is a particular kind of **multi-perspective public event with separate private causal continuations**. An objective act is not an omniscient narrator.

## 5. SC-05 — Child open-endedness through a **small family of lawful transformations**, not a general psych-variable factory

**Biocultural insight:** the same developmental ontology need not produce the same categories, linguistic format or motival organization. The variable is not a culture label or universal five motives. It is an organism with particular capabilities encountering historically available objects, social relations, communication channels and contingencies, sometimes actively practicing or inventing new operations. Acquired *discrimination*, *practical competence*, *expectation*, *reported explanation* and *current consciousness* are nonidentical outcomes.

**Computational idea:** test the ability of existing CAN-009/010/011, ADP-010/016/017 and P3 readers to support **more than one qualitatively distinct lawful learning path**, without inventing a universal learning function. Two narrow domain hypotheses could stress the architecture:

- **Contingency:** infant makes an eligible vocal signal; actual contingent vs noncontingent agent response and access may alter a context-specific expectation only under a separately qualified TEST_ONLY rule. The cited infant study supports bounded contingent vocal responses, not life-long trust, exact learning rate or cross-cultural transport.
- **Self-initiated practice:** child manipulates an actual object or repeatedly performs a bounded material task with feedback, perhaps no adult present. A distinct practice rule may change an actor-owned `PRACTICE` or produce NO_CHANGE. This prevents the engine from reducing all acquisition to caregiver speech/socializer instruction.

Their **common abstraction** is not a shared numeric update. It is common owner/custody: eligible embodied act or access, admitted effect/feedback, domain-specific transition authority, possible acquired state and *later first consumer*. Additional domains (sign, overheard speech, institution-specific category, craft) are further research questions, not automatically solved by these two.

**Constructive falsifier:** keep objective world and adult behavior identical, change only the child's own eligible practice history; a later actual attempt may lawfully differ **if** the domain rule reads it. Conversely keep activity constant and change the cultural label; nothing changes. Actor who never perceived a word cannot have acquired its exact meaning merely because PYAT knows the word was uttered. An unmodeled interval is not evidence of zero learning.

## 6. SC-06 — Compression as **preservation of future-relevant causal evidence**, with honest irrecoverable loss

This is the most promising computational economy linking parent and child **without conflating their regimes**. Parent **pre-contact back-generation** uses low-detail forward-compatible histories; child **continuing development** needs ordinary-life processes plus selected high-detail events; player-time interaction is finer. One shared actor-state ontology, distinct resolutions.

**Creative criterion:** compression should be tested not against “does this sound like a whole life?” but against **which later owner-qualified questions it can still answer**. A period summary is *adequate for a specific consumer* only if it preserves information needed for that consumer's allowed inference: **But optimizing only for today's expected consumers can erase future discoveries.** Preserve a small, source-graded floor of significant admitted world effects and actor-history references even when no current learning rule reads them; label all omitted domains as unknown. This is a design **hypothesis** about future-proofing, not authority to store a complete childhood.

| Future question | Possible minimum retained witness | Too-lossy substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Did this agent respond contingently to child signals? | eligible signal opportunities, response pairing/lag, agent and actor access, context controls | total care minutes, total replies |
| Could child perform this practice later? | real eligible own practice, feedback and relevant body/object context | “grew up around tools” |
| Could parent later act differently due to prelife? | actor-owned acquired state/causal trace and context where first reader can recruit it | elegant story/trait label |
| Did father's candidate past contradict mother's world? | exactly represented material place/time/ownership claims and unknowns | complete invented retrospective social history |
| Does child know why caregiver was absent? | accessible explanation/cue history, not merely objective absence | parental work event alone |
| Is a C5 relation really recurrent? | distinct qualified contexts, actual update/rupture/return witnesses | repeated motive words in generated narration |

This implies a **consumer-indexed coverage grade**, not a universal “100% childhood simulated” scalar. A capsule can be adequate for checking material/time use but **insufficient** for a lexical acquisition claim. A sourcefree aggregate can assert model-generated rate/transition assumptions, but cannot silently instantiate individual remembered microepisodes. If later detailed consumer asks for missing event order, correct answer is **UNKNOWN or a new, properly graded conditional simulation branch before admission**, never retroactive claiming an old exact memory.

**Prospective engineering pattern:** P2/period owner generates cheap admitted event families or explicitly model-aggregate transitions under a real human-time/clock mapping; coverage is a **derived read**; CAN/ADP domain update asks for its exact evidence predicates; the owner checks whether coverage contains them before issuing a new state. No second canonical ledger. Offer a paired measurement of CPU/LLM cost, memory, replay and *causal error*, not only narrative realism or token count.

**Caution from project spirit:** include ordinary play, craft, pleasure, boredom, rest and relational care as genuine processes, not merely material stress/instruction/trauma. They need not serve later "character arc" to count as living.

## 7. SC-07 — C5 as **possible higher-order organization of warranted relations**, not an inevitable basin

**Science:** original Personal Relational Field insists same learned nodes can be organized differently. It remains worth asking whether repeated, historically acquired context-dependent relationships come to bias future salience, action and repair. This is different from adding a static type label or a narrator's motive summary. However we cannot infer a functional `SUPPRESSES` edge from mere coactivation, an unconscious relation from actor testimony, or an attractor from repeated motif labels.

**Constructive two-stage route:**
1. **Stage R — relation-mode test:** under an exact sourcefree rule, compare same eligible acquired states and contextual histories with/without a **specifically evidenced** directional relation. In a controlled later choice, does the relation have an independent *admitted functional role*, or can ordinary history retrieval explain everything? A model producing an edge without a source/rule/first reader fails. Actor report, analyst hypothesis and functional behavior must remain distinct.
2. **Stage K — slow pattern candidate, conditional on R:** derive K from admitted historical states/relations and test whether a bounded actor's consideration/choice across changing contexts is better explained or computed under K than with just bounded history+relations. Equalize total information and resource budget. The hypothetical recurrence may strengthen, split, dissolve or never form. Only a separate MUST-018 authority could later make it causal; CAN-020 remains read-only V2 expression.

**Important creative extension:** try *positive organization* as well as defensive gravity: craft enjoyment recruiting attention, reciprocal care sustaining a commitment, playful curiosity persisting across projects; the Absolute Horizon remains a **different** question of ultimate practical order under costly conflict. A singular dominant wound or nine prewired attraction basins would falsify the architecture's promised generative openness.

**Honest limit:** K is derived from the same accepted history. With unconstrained perfect history reading, it contains no *new* information. Its possible merit is a bounded, stable, generatively helpful representation that changes allowed functional decisions under stated, empirical/model hypotheses—not a new causal “soul force” mysteriously added to facts. If Stage R works and K fails, **retain directed relational organization without an attractor**. If both fail, retain acquired histories without an invented graph.

## 8. A single cross-stage thought experiment (NOT a runtime claim or required first #1011 implementation)
