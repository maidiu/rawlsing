In particular **pinned or sourcefree cases**, PYAT can qualify same-respondent observed relationships via [`pre553_empirical_query.py`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_empirical_query.py), [`pre553_analytical_composition.py::compose_positioned_subject_possibility`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_analytical_composition.py) and [`pre553_empirical_possibility_provider.py::provide_empirical_possibility_slice`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_empirical_possibility_provider.py). Historical F1–F5, FFX, F6 partial identification, F7 chronology and N9/N10E/N10W supply the broader intended scientific pipeline. Their current accepted scope and original-source issuer/reader gaps must not be bypassed. In particular, **a qualified empirical possibility is not already a callable synthetic mother sampler**, and the generic #998/#1009 joined original-to-F4/Field Forge chain is separately dependency-gated. `Sample one maternal cloud` is a **prospective modeled draw**, not an executed native capability.

SEAM/20 correctly refuses `survey answer → private motive`. The goal is the opposite: propose a **source-qualified/synthetic observable maternal tip** under an explicitly named sampling rule and claimed target population, then use **the same V3 developmental semantics as the child** to generate a *few* distinct causal prehistories consistent with that reported tip. The maternal prelife is computationally compressed, not a separate categorical adult personality engine. Its iceberg has acquired practice, expectations, conflicts, partial consciousness, memory, bodily history and independent later agency.

Why worth developing: it produces genuine conditional plurality. Two mothers with *identical observed survey cells* can be different persons for historically concrete reasons, rather than the generator hiding its private motives in a demographic lookup. It also provides the actor who must later generate encounter possibilities, caregiving and parental difference.

## The exact scientific distinction: observed answer ≠ private history

Define an observation's `t_obs` and source survey question semantics, response universe/weights/missingness and respondent unit. The mother's actual fictional developmental chronology might contain childhood before `t_obs`, the encounter before/after `t_obs`, and birth before/after `t_obs`; **survey observations are not timeless immutable traits**.

The inverse expression in SEAM/20, `P(H,Z|A,D,W) ∝ P(A|H,Z,D,W)P(H,Z|D,W)`, is a useful *research target*, not an identified posterior. Neither full prior over developmental lives nor answer-emission likelihood is measured by PYAT merely because a joint frequency is. Survey question wording, social context, response style, desirability, actor knowledge/partial self-understanding and observation time matter.

**A hypothetical sourcefree response-consistency checker must be explicitly graded.** It cannot become a source-native `P(A|H,Z,D,W)` merely through an LLM ranking biographies. A fictional observation attached to a source-qualified adult *distribution* is NOT a recorded report by this generated mother. Committed candidate selection is itself a model assumption, not posterior inference. It may permit many mutually incompatible internal histories to be compatible with the same external answer. It must not force a woman who nominates obedience to practice authoritarian parenting, nor infer unique attachment, political ideology, marriage motives or permanent Enneagram gravity.

The existing [#1011](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/issues/1011) person pilot requires an empirical-to-person conditioned path with two later actual events and a cold read; it **does not require** a completed inverse-maternal-birth generator, father, or C5. ## Positive/null tests that discriminate a working mother from a biography

- **Same observed response cloud, two committed prehistories:** both pass only the allowed consistency check; later *actual performed* maternal action can differ when an actor-owned acquired state affects selection under identical present material possibilities. It may lawfully remain the same.
- **Same words, no causal prelife:** a beautiful biography with no committed prior events/CAN state cannot produce a source-qualified acquired mediator or choice.
- **Answer ambiguity:** a reported obedience nomination with materially protective conduct does not force false inconsistency.
- **Observation timing:** postbirth response cannot silently be inserted as a pre-encounter fixed prior; when `t_obs` not alignable, hold.
- **Nulls:** unsupported empirical joint, missing emission rule, zero admissible candidate, source severance, wrong design population, invalid opportunity, or no changed later act return explicit nonpositive states.
- **Compute/fidelity:** adult prelife compression must preserve *later first-consumer-relevant distinctions* while keeping unmodeled detail unknown. Identical CAN schema is not equivalence of coarse inference and fine event histories.
