## Three different computational economies must stay distinct

1. **Parents' compressed PRE-CONTACT developmental generation** from SEAM/20: construct an already formed, viable adult cheaply with the **same CAN/ADP developmental ontology** as the child, with explicit epistemic loss. Distinct function: selected candidate history supports an adult's subsequent independent acts.
2. **Continuing ORDINARY LIFE for parents, child, peers and carers:** simulate recurring periods of real work, bodily need, feeding, movement, sleep, carrying, tool use, peer play, background language, social institutions, absence and inaction. This produces the actual developmental opportunity and experience distributions in which child repertoire may arise.
3. **High-resolution consequential encounters and player-time:** detailed events when actor access, activity order, conflicting interpretations, new practiced skill, rupture or world consequence demand specificity.

**Do not conflate 1 with 2.** A compressed adult prehistory isn't a demand to compress the child's emergent psychology into five scenes. The child's acquired repertoire is open-ended; generation economy is about *how to produce enough life* for it to develop, not a restriction on content. Sparse 3–5 focal episodes are compatible with dense cheap underlying modeled ordinary activity **only if the latter actually exists at the declared grade**.

## Why this is important to the science and the product

Without ordinary life the LLM will preferentially invent a handful of dramatic events, leading to an implausibly wounded, heroic or psychologized person. With ungrounded “the child lived thousands of hours” coverage, it will invent specific skills and memories that have no actual causal basis. Both errors distort frequency, relational continuity, material reproduction, acquired skill and personality.

Adolph and colleagues' infant naturalistic experience work (discussed in SEAM/22) emphasizes temporal and distributional variability; multicarer labor and differential access mean **one representative day does not equal childhood**. And different psychological hypotheses require different retained information: a child's learning of an action–response contingency requires timing and opportunity controls; a dispute of motive may require actual words and who had access; a motor practice may require body/environment repetitions; a family relation may require distinct interpretations, not just count of meetings.

The compression rule is therefore not only an optimization trick. **Its declared loss is a scientific constraint on what later developmental inference can legitimately consume.**

## Prospective mechanism: realized low-cost causal process → coverage read

```text
As-of period t: existing P2/world actors, body states, objective material
   obligations, resources, households, time/space, institutions, prior actions
  -> period owner's eligible world/activity candidate set
  -> actor-specific cheap selected/attempted events & legitimate inaction;
     multiple carers, siblings, peers, work and nonactor events independent
  -> competent P2 writers admit real material/time/coactor effects and
     actor-specific access; keep resources conserved and views separate
  -> derived COVERAGE READ over admitted event families, or explicit
     MODEL_AGGREGATE with declared microdetail omitted/lossy
  -> CAN/ADP domain-specific readers consume ONLY information preserved
  -> sparse high-resolution microepisode if loss would invalidate a
     needed causal inference, actor perspective or later action
```

Possible **read-only** capsule semantics (not a new independent event ledger):

```yaml
actor: child/source-bound actor ID
interval: as-of start/end + effective child body/eligibility context
world_basis: prior P2 as-of, material, coactor and period refs
process: exact rule/code version, source-vs-model grade, named RNG receipts
covered_event_families: [feeding, carried, gesture, work, peer_activity]
denominators: eligible opportunities AND actual realized encounters
actor_access: channels/actors/what was actually available to perceive
temporal_information: event order or lawful sufficient lag/contingency summary
material_effects: exact admitted P2 source refs, not duplicate money
unmodeled: intervals, domains, actors, unknown exposure
omissions: whether individual microepisodes were simulated or only aggregates
replay: prior world hash, output hash, source/time/branch identity
```

### Vital economic/scientific fork

**Event-generating cheap simulator:** actual modeled events with sufficiently low-cost writer/receipts; coverage derives from them. Requires scalability, bounded snapshot frequency, actor/access conservation.

**Coarse aggregate-generating simulator:** directly draws period-level counts/transition measures without modeling each microevent. Can be much cheaper **but** no individual microevent or its detailed memory exists; any learning rule consuming aggregate requires separately qualified assumptions of sufficiency. A count of 20 responses is not a conditional response probability if signals/opportunities/order are lost.

The future engine may combine both; no “complete exact life” claim follows merely from having coverage. A high-resolution episode cannot retcon an event omitted from the aggregate without an explicit history-selection and admissible sampling rule.

## Proof and failure court

1. Same focal dramatic scenes, **different genuinely generated ordinary activity histories** may yield different later eligible acquired development if the registered domain rule consumes those histories.
2. Same qualified ordinary history, different **LLM narrative wording**: no changed psyche or world.
3. Equal child signals and caregiver replies, different **temporal contingency**: coverage retaining only raw totals must be marked insufficient for the learning experiment in SC-05.
4. Parent works, only parent ledger changes: no presumed caregiver relief, co-presence or child experience until actual shared-world/actor effect is committed.
5. Offscreen caregiver can act when legitimately eligible; their activity can affect child world without granting direct perceived-event knowledge of the remote act.
6. Unmodeled period and lost event modality: return `NOT_MODELED` / `UNKNOWN`, not `NOT_ACQUIRED`, zero, typical growth, remembered invented episode, or a predictable wound.
7. Named randomness and replay: no resampling arbitrary history on reopen, no double material action, no invented event branch due to scene generation.
8. Performance: check resident memory, CPU/model calls, world-store growth, and later reader correctness. Lower cost alone is not sufficient if causal input content is lost.
