## Why this follows, not replaces, the parent generator

SC-02–04 yield independently developed parents, an eligible historical encounter, possible relationship, world effects and separate child-generation. The child starts as a **new developing organism/actor**, not a recombination of parental motives, survey scores or Enneagram. From here, SOROK's *open-ended* psychological formation runs at selectively finer resolution than the parents' compressed pre-contact construction; same state kinds/authority and causal grammar, potentially different concrete acquired objects, memories, competencies and relations.

Scientific minimum is a body with historically qualified enabling conditions: nourishment, regulation, sensory/motor capacity, physical affordances, active signaling and variable access. There is **no** default conscious security/power/shame/love ideology, and no assumption that speech, text, formal instruction, nuclear family or verbal introspection are universal acquisition prerequisites. Biological enablement is necessary for some events but not itself a full psychological learning law. Solitary exploration, sleep, play, adults talking to one another, peers and nonhuman environments count as possible activity/access channels.

**The noncollapse at the heart of Z:**

```text
objective historical/world change
 != actor-specific material possibility
 != actor actually reaches/perceives something
 != actor attends / discriminates / performs
 != acquired expectation/practice/mediator/memory
 != current conscious access or verbal articulation
 != activation/recruitment in a novel context
 != selected attempted/performed action
 != objective eventual outcome
```

A mechanism that skips these distinctions can generate “psychology” efficiently while generating nobody.

## One economical prospective *domain-specific* learning example

Use the experimentally investigated phenomenon of infant **vocal action–response contingency** as a **bounded laboratory analogue**, not a demonstrated general caregiver-attachment, lifelong expectation or universal human developmental law. Venditti et al. (2024; DOI [10.1111/infa.12614](https://doi.org/10.1111/infa.12614)) compared contingent with yoked feedback among **64 infants aged approximately seven to eight months**, including nonhuman agent conditions. Effects were specific to their observed vocalization behavior, not measured durable personhood. The actual research (see scientific bounds in [SEAM/22 §2](../22_MATERIAL_INFANT_PRIMITIVES_CAREGIVING_BACKGROUND_LIFE_AND_DISTRIBUTIONAL_BREADTH_R1.md)) supports scoped contingency sensitivity; it cannot set a lawful numeric Rawlsing learning rate without additional work.

```text
P2 child actor/body in qualified eligible infant period
  # NOT YET a general operative infant-action issuer; owner check required
  -> child actually emits bodily/vocal/gestural signal (or does not)
  -> available carers/agents ACT if a qualified P2/coactor
     producer can actually issue their actions, under real work,
     availability, sensory/media and institutional conditions
  -> one agent may respond, not respond, or be unavailable
  -> P2 commits actual event & objective material consequence
  -> ADP-010/C12 distinguishes child-accessible timing/channel/content
  -> repeated signal-response windows under SC-06 covered ordinary life,
     WITH comparison no-signal windows, actor identity, delay/order
  -> candidate domain TEST_ONLY CAN-010 expectation of THIS action and
     THIS response/context, or NO_CHANGE/UNKNOWN/REFUSE
  -> later actor actually attempts something conditioned on acquired history
  -> P2 admits performed attempt and independent consequences
```

This is a **candidate sourcefree mechanism test**, not an authorized CAN-010 update function or a licensed age/gesture generalization. The cited trial tests a narrow vocalization channel; extending to gesture, motor exploration, all caregiver settings or long-term development requires separate evidence and body/measurement predicates. One caregiver failing a response due to a work shift cannot automatically create “rejection trauma.” Identical totals of infant signals and adult responses do not imply identical contingency; temporal pairing and access matter. No rule = unknown, not “nothing learned.” If child cannot receive the agent's actual response, don't assert acquisition. If child first learns one agent's responsiveness, do not generalize to every human.

Other domains (self-initiated motor skill, practice with tools, natural signed-language access, overhearing adult speech, acquired critical perspective) may require *different* specific evidence and admissible U rules. The **ontology remains common** but no single socialization coefficient is mandated.

### External evidence and inferred effect are different things

A study showing a short-term response under contingent experimental feedback does **not** estimate how a particular fictional infant experiences years of care or later becomes an adult. An in-simulation difference under a hand-written rule proves only its constructed semantics. Domain-specific biological applicability, cross-cultural transport, longer-term learning and human predictive validity must be separately tested; otherwise those effects remain sourcefree hypotheses.

## Required result and negative tests

1. **Actual performance as cause:** same objective world, two child own-action/practice histories; a valid test-only update may differ and eventually change a *performed* child act.
2. **Perception control:** caregiver event occurred but child had no access; no event-derived learned semantic knowledge.
3. **No reduction to adult pedagogy:** child can receive qualified overheard adult interaction without being addressed, but acquisition still needs a domain rule.
4. **Embodied eligibility:** unavailable motor/sensory channel must not pass a fictitious action.
5. **Non-forming history:** real event but no qualified formation rule → keep `MISSING_RULE/UNKNOWN_RULING`; no automatic practice/virtue/ideology.
6. **Acquired vs dormant/inaccessible:** a past formation can exist without current verbal report or current activation, per accepted actor access and CAN state semantics.
7. **Cultural permutation:** only change label `oral`/`Indigenous`/`school` while actual activity/available modality unchanged → no psyche change by stereotype.
8. **Replay:** parent/child actor roots, source, event clock, access and causal parents survive cold.
