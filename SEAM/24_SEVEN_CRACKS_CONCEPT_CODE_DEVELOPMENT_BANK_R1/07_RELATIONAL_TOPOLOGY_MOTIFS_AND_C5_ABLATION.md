## Do not reduce the third leg to a cache OR decree an independent psychic substance

The inherited [Field Forge *Personal Relational Field and Emergent Attractor*](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/data/ddm_build_copy/recovery_integration/semantic_relation_R1_through_Job3/R1_20260825/PRE_MAIN_RECONCILIATION_R1/PROJECT_INHERITANCE_R1/CAMPAIGNS/N033_ORIGINARY_PERSON_WORLD_PAX_MARXICA/CORPUS/ORIGINAL/91fd9f3214a8__26_PERSONAL_RELATIONAL_FIELD_AND_EMERGENT_ATTRACTOR_R1.md) makes the strong claim **SAME NODES ≠ SAME PERSON**. A historically acquired expectation may recruit a craft practice in one context; suppress or rationalize it in another. Recurrent chains, loops, compartmentalized subsystems, stabilization, contradiction, rupture and recovery may organize the person's future activity.

This **could** be more than memory retrieval: the proposed **relations among acquired contents** may have historically formed functional consequences **if** a rule and later consumer can discriminate them from simple context-conditioned history retrieval. Yet the original document refuses co-occurrence→directed relation, repeated relation→attractor, attractor→type, or type→causal trait imposition. SEAM/23 correctly retains **fast actual activity/learning** and **slow hypothesized recurrent integration**, with no mandatory childhood wound, nine universal basins, moral teleology or global coherent psyche.

Scientific risk: a pattern of co-activated nodes may reflect a third external cause, sampling bias, one context's pressures, or an LLM's storytelling interpretation. “Attractor” requires more than repeated edge labels; its dynamical claims of stability/return/splitting across perturbation need a formally specified evolution rule, disconfirming perturbations and **external** developmental evidence before claims about real humans. A synthetic experiment can only test the rule we wrote. A purely computational predictive summary is an important competitor, but declaring *all* relational organization merely a cache would prematurely erase the original C5 claim.

## Where the exact missing transformations lie

There are **three different** problems, to avoid inventing one global graph engine:

1. **Acquired state existence (SC-05):** content X and Y must first be lawfully formed, with distinct actor, time, contexts and event parents.
2. **Relation formation:** do lived repeated situations warrant a *directed, typed, context-scoped* claim `X SUPPRESSES Y`, `X JUSTIFIES Y`, `X RECRUITS Y`, etc., rather than mere simultaneous occurrence? Who evaluates this, against what historically meaningful causal evidence? Modes need bounded meaning and update law, not arbitrary numeric graph scores.
3. **Slow recurrent organization:** do those relations form larger motifs that recur, shift across contexts, resist perturbation, split or dissolve? Does a derived `K` earn independent *downstream predictive/selection usefulness* under a specified bounded actor-history budget? Only a separately governed MUST-018 authority can admit a durable attractor rule.

Three epistemic objects must be kept distinct: **actor's accessible claim** (“I did it because ...”); **analyst's explanatory hypothesis** about a possible directed relationship; and an **admitted functional relation** actually affecting a later action under a separately specified model rule. Neither a narrator's plausible explanation nor an actor's sincere public justification alone establishes an unconscious functional mechanism. Even a repeatedly observed pattern may be explained by shared external conditions. A mode like `JUSTIFIES` cannot be inferred merely because an LLM writes “she seeks competence to earn love.” It might be an analyst hypothesis unavailable to the person, a retrospective narrative, a coactivation without direction, or a genuine reported self-justification at one moment. Different evidentiary predicates apply. A person can hold contradictory, context-separated relations. Silence over time is not automatic reinforcement (CAN-011).

## Two separate experiments, not one test that assumes the conclusion

**Stage R — Earn relations before C5.** R0 = actor history + context-specific CAN-010/CAN-011 content with no separately admitted directed links. R1 = same history plus **only relation edges admitted under a specific candidate evidence rule**, and a later first-reader whose proposal/attempt can change because of those edges. Keep equal actual-event facts and action opportunities. Test whether the relation rule is just a relabeling of a shared external pressure, whether actor report is being mistaken for causal mechanism, and whether different relation modes predict distinct sourcefree actions. If R1 cannot pass, **do not proceed to a causal C5 test**; a graph of invented relations cannot justify an attractor.

**Stage K — Conditional on some justified relation logic**, test K0 = admitted history/states/relations without separately stored slow summary; K1 = same admitted facts plus a derived, type-agnostic candidate K, its exact update rule and documented effect on later actor consideration/selection; Kbad = developer-assigned Enneagram magnets as rejection control. Equalize information and total compute/storage budgets, fix event/actor/world histories and held-out situations, and disallow K1 access to any future event K0 did not receive. Since `K=f(history,states,relations)`, it cannot add information to an unbounded perfect reader; any advantage is **bounded computational economy, stability, interpretability or well-calibrated predictions**, not novel psychic substance. A *real-person developmental science* claim needs separate external data and model validation, not victory on fictional training histories.

## How to adjudicate H0/H1/H2 fairly

- **H0 (SOROK baseline):** same admitted actor history and CAN-011 contents/relations, no distinct slow K. A contextual retrieval/predictive summary of history may be a separate H0 comparator, not assumed equal to a full graph.
- **H1 (candidate):** same historical facts plus **derived type-agnostic slow K**, with explicit rule on what it may change in a later actor selection/attention. K cannot mint knowledge or a new desire. Evaluate whether it helps with long-range reorganization, return under perturbation, context-specific recruitment and *actual performed actions* under equal compute/storage budgets.
- **H2 (negative control only):** nine developer-assigned Enneagram-style type magnets; must not become an accepted developmental mechanism, even if narratives sound coherent.

Controls: same nodes different relations; same relation inventory different sequencing/contexts; public versus private compartments; one acquired practice revived after rupture; two rival motifs; no basin; null acquisition; label-only permutation; held-out encounter at a new institution; cold replay; source severance and inaccessible memory. **A model's own type consistency score is not scientific validation.**

If H1 shows no distinct justified action effect **and** no defensible out-of-sample predictive/information-economy benefit over H0/contextual summaries, retain C5 as a *derived research/index representation* and keep native actor mutation on hold. If H1 does help, require empirical domain evidence and MUST-018 approval before claiming human developmental law.

## Why develop this as a distinct record

SEAM/21's **variable content** alone yields a bag of skills, motives and tensions; SEAM/23 insists the realized person may possess an historically formed **organization of how those contents constrain and recruit one another**. Without examining this, mother and child might differ only by content inventory while acting implausibly in changing contexts. Conversely, an imposed attractor erases that contingency and historical openness.

The aim is a genuine independent Other whose actual later behavior depends on the *organization of lived history*, not a narrated typology. A functioning Stage R may be valuable even if no Stage K C5 basin ever earns authority. This record does not mandate a basin for everyone, a fixed wounded self, a unified habitus or an empirical Enneagram prior.
