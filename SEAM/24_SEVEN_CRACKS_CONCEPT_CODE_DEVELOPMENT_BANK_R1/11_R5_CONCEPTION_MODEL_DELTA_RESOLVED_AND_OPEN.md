## Decision: the original asymmetry survives intact

**Mother is first fully developing person; father is generated later and conditionally.** The system is constructing the history of a **particular focal child**; it is *not* an unconditioned partner-discovery simulator trying to find two random adults who eventually conceive. Independent all-adult prior sampling and fully instantiating the eventual biological father at the first encounter are **not the selected default**. The latter would risk expensively generating partners whose histories fail the chosen birth-conditioned family branch or artificially steering a relationship toward reproductive completion.

**Confirmed principal sequence (not an R5 invention):**
1. PYAT source-qualified observable maternal D/material/attitude *cloud* at its correct grade; SOROK constructs her distinct compressed historical person without survey answer→psyche.
2. Very few **compressed forward** mother recursions/LLM calls across multiple fictional years (principal speculates ~2–3, not a fixed measured/runtime count). LLM proposes qualified event stacks from the **actual mother when proposing**; **final call's last event** contains alternative possible **conception trajectories**.
3. Actual admitted intermediate events alter her. At arrival at the last event, her **then-current actor state** and objective circumstances qualify/select the conception possibility; proposal is not action/occurrence; conception need not be a voluntary intention.
4. The selected conception trajectory can entail *years-earlier* partner encounters, courtship, marriage, separation, other partners, earlier pregnancies and older children. It is **a conditioned historical trajectory**, not simply a time/place at conception.
5. **After the maternal conditioned trajectory is selected**, the focal biological father is constructed backward-conditionally given necessary observed relationship/encounter/conception constraints; independently develop his own compressed prehistory forward from an earlier starting point, preserving required encounter and performed-event facts without deriving hidden motives from maternal interpretation.
6. Where the focal child is later-born, existing siblings have their own earlier genesis, simpler but real actor histories and effects on family circumstances. Their other biological parents need not match the focal father. Parent(s), siblings, coactors keep acting and developing through focal childhood.

**Illustrative diversity is NOT the candidate distribution.** The principal's four deliberately contrasting examples (conventional multiple-child marriage; intermittent relationship; long-distance romantic contact; unexpected intercourse with a disfavored person) were **pedagogical examples**, never the intended four-slot, stratified, or stereotyped event generator. No social-class/sexual-morality type, “respectable→marriage” determinism, fixed quota of four alternatives, or implicit LLM plausibility-score-as-frequency should enter production. Alternative event types and frequency/support must emerge under actual source + explicitly model-graded scientific and fictional constraints, with viable exceptional paths and refusals.

## What R5 actually ADDS, beyond R4 (no inflated novelty)

### DELTA-1 — **Thin witnessed counterpart** before full internal back-generation (prospective technique)

During mother's forward recursion, a person with whom she has a consequential relationship can possess **stable objective identity, commitments, observable deeds, relations and causal world effects** without already having a complete, actor-owned, separately developed *internal* P3 life. The mother's actual **perception** of that person is separate again. This is not “a partner is just her imagined character”: his externally admitted actions can surprise or constrain her even if their **internal motive-history** has not yet been elaborated. We can use existing world/coactor/perception rights where licensed instead of inventing a new father engine.

Only **after** the selected conception pathway determines which person is biologically relevant do we propose full father profile and compressed independent history, conditioned by those actual objective past facts but **not by her guesses about his private motives**. If an earlier partner has already been independently instantiated for a different reason, **reuse that same actor** rather than making a duplicate “new father.” A previously existing spouse may be *not* the focal biological father.

**Constraint:** this is an **implementation option**, not proof that current C01/C04/P3 already support late internal-state admission. For any earlier act asserted as truly independently chosen, its future internally generated history must not falsely claim an incompatible source of that act. If real independently originated choice is a prerequisite to the earlier event, a thin counterpart may be insufficient and the owner must instantiate more of the other person earlier or HOLD. Deferred interiority **cannot retroactively turn an authored act into demonstrated independent volition**.

### DELTA-2 — **Preconditions on conception pathways**, not historical retcon

A candidate conception pathway is **not** just a proposed event label and biological time. It has a finite set of **necessary earlier propositions** with effective times: if it proposes a third child in a marriage, that requires an admitted earlier marriage/other legitimate relation history, two prior children, relevant body/household/care facts; if it proposes a brief encounter, it may require far less shared history. These are constraints the mother's prior actual life (or an explicitly graded, uncommitted candidate branch) must already satisfy; **a new paternal biography does not fabricate an earlier mother marriage/older children that were never part of her formation**.

**Proposed review-only witness (NOT an existing schema):**

```text
candidate_conception_path:
  proposer_mother_state_at_call: exact actor state / clock
  proposed_terminal_event_time: t_C
  required_prior_events: exact effective-time/history references
  coactor_identity_refs: when actually established; otherwise unknown role
  mother_state_at_arrival: computed at t_C after preceding events
  world/body/genealogy_eligibility: each rule and authority separately
  outcome: CONDITIONALLY_SELECTED | INELIGIBLE | HELD | REFUSED
  # Never equate selected storyline with performed conception or birth.
```

The path cannot be chosen by a model's romance-genre resemblance to the woman. The maternal selector needs historically supported or explicitly synthetic prior-support/likelihood rules and legitimate randomness, not an arbitrary maximum of author-supplied narratives.

### DELTA-3 — Make the **late binding versus independence** tradeoff an explicit design choice, not a settled “solution”

There are two distinct things the father might be “independent” of:
- **Earlier external conduct** was already committed into the objective fictional world. His later back-generated history **must match it**. This limits counterfactual freedom for those past deeds; it does not mean his motives are known.
- His **unobserved personal history, acquired organization, interpretations and *future* independent deeds** must not be determined by the mother’s understanding of him. Those can be generated later at the required resolution, with real refusal if constraints cannot be satisfied.

The open technical question is **how much of the partner's independently formed state must exist while the mother is being recursed through several years of marriage/other relation**, so that her own state can genuinely be shaped by an independently active other rather than an authorial proxy. A “thin publicly consequential actor” is a prospective compromise, not proven enough for every kind of causal formation. A proposed late candidate validation/branch redo is one research answer, **not** a principal-mandated algorithm.

## What was already in SEAM/20–23/R4, therefore NOT a new R5 discovery

- Asymmetric, **mother-led** genesis and computationally later backward-conditioned father.
- Father has independently developed personal history under fixed meeting/relationship/conception constraints; no automatic similarity of motive/psyche.
- Multiple prospective events and late selection by mother's changed state; the *final* conception-path alternatives specified in R4.
- Existing older siblings and other caregivers have separate histories; focal biological father ≠ spouse ≠ every child's father ≠ sole caregiver.
- Distinction between public event, personal perception, private psychology, actual action, memory and final expressed narrative.
- Fictional condition-on-birth branch ≠ empirically measured distribution of partners/fertility.
- Compression of parents' prelife and continuing coactor resolution ≠ loss of child's open-ended development.

## Rejected as DEFAULT (not forbidden in all other SOROK applications)

**Encounter-triggered full father instantiation:** a legitimate *optional* technique when an independently consequential nonfocal actor needs fully realized psychology **at the time they enter**. It is not a better default for selecting the focal biological father because then one either (i) privileges that partner and implicitly engineers conception or (ii) must run expensive, potentially many no-birth relationships. This was UM-PYAT's counterproposal; it is **withdrawn as the selected father-generation design**.

**Unconditioned two-full-adult matching:** rejected for the focal child branch. **Mandatory most-probable LLM story:** rejected as a substitute for historical possibility, appropriately graded conditional support, declared selection and minority contingencies. Neither a survey code nor a mother “type” supplies her reproductive destiny.
