## The actual model—DO NOT invert this inversion

The mother is computationally developed through **few long-span forward recursions**, with LLM **proposed event stacks and alternative conception pathways** based on her state *at proposal time*. After intervening events have actually changed her, **her state upon reaching the final conception-event possibility** determines which pathway is selected/realized within material and biological constraints. The choice may imply an earlier meeting, years of relationship and prior children. **Only then** is the father computationally generated backward-conditionally on the selected pathway's obligatory earlier meeting and focal conception/birth; his in-world past precedes that intersection. This corrects the old encounter-first shorthand. [R4 principal clarification](10_R4_PRINCIPAL_MATERNAL_FORWARD_CONCEPTION_FATHER_BACKFILL_AND_SIBLING_HISTORY.md).

The father must be a genuinely different person: his own demographic/material/response cloud (source-graded), his own compressed pre-encounter life under the same CAN/ADP developmental ontology, his own actor-local interpretations and projects. **He is not sampled forward as an unrelated person whom a simulator is then obliged to match to the mother**, and he is not reverse-engineered to agree with her psychology.

The stipulated branch birth is a condition of this **candidate-story construction**, not evidence that all couples reproduce, that all eligible encounters create a relationship, or that PYAT measures couple-level likelihood. ADP-015 maintains encounter ≠ relationship ≠ household ≠ parenthood ≠ child-generation ≠ child initialization. Others can be caregivers; biological father need not share household.

### Scope of the mother/father entry case

The maternal-to-paternal inverse sequence is **governing for this selected generative route**; it must not be silently generalized into an ontology of universally married, co-resident, heterosexual, two-carer or binary social-gender households. Separately distinguish survey-coded gender, biological parentage, gestation, kin-recognized parenthood and actual care. A father candidate can be biological lineage without co-residence or long-term parenting; other caregivers, children, institutions, chosen kin and households must remain independently generatable where source/model conditions permit. **Do not retrofit observed survey gender categories into biologically validated reproductive roles**.

## Why this deserves its own concept/code record

The **potential engineering integrity problem** involves two different orderings: computational construction and in-world causal chronology. **We have not proved a new general historical merge is required.** Father construction happens after a mother-world trajectory has been generated, but father's earlier actions might have world effects: moving, working, acquiring property, participating in institutions or changing someone else's possibilities. A timeline-coherent private biography is insufficient **where** it asserts effects that contradict *actually sealed, overlapping* mother-world facts. But the mother-world is **not** a complete transcript of all past activity: absence of recorded father effects is NOT evidence they never occurred.

Conversely, requiring every compressed pre-encounter father activity to have a fully performed P2 event would defeat the stated **low-cost historical construction** and impose unsupported completeness. **Differentiate** (a) established shared-world events, (b) candidate father-local performed or period-modeled effects, (c) proposed context constraints, and (d) unknown/unmodeled consequences. Only actual first-consumer-relevant overlaps require exact compatibility. The goal is a **candidate historical reconciliation** with immutable commitments and bounded uncertainty, not global retroactive rewrites or a magical historical merge operation.

PYAT's F7 temporal and F6 nonpooled discipline, positional/region/institution and opportunity qualification constrain eligibility; SOROK's actual P2/ADP-015 chronology and actor-specific states own admitted fictional effects. One sourceful mother–father couple probability **cannot be fabricated from two WVS respondents**.

### Central late-instantiation challenge

If the mother has already become who she is through a *years-long relationship* before the father was instantiated as an independently developing actor, we cannot treat those years as a complete two-person life without further proof. **Proposed** remedy: mother-dependent conception path is conditionally selected; father independently back-recurses to the obligatory first meeting, then both actors jointly develop through actual relationship years; candidate selection is finalized only if this history remains compatible with mother-state-at-arrival and admitted world constraints. Otherwise REFUSE the candidate before sealing. This is a **research hypothesis, not current implementation** and not permission to rewrite the mother's committed *pre-intersection* history. Any older siblings must have already lived and developed prior to focal conception, with independent genealogy where relevant. [R4 details](10_R4_PRINCIPAL_MATERNAL_FORWARD_CONCEPTION_FATHER_BACKFILL_AND_SIBLING_HISTORY.md).

## Proposed candidate-stage architecture (not current API)

```text
COMMITTED MOTHER: frozen ACTUALLY REPRESENTED H_m/world events,
                  partial as-of W_m(t) with UNKNOWN/unmodeled domains
  -> LLM proposes long-span maternal events and conception alternatives
     from then-current mother M(t_i), NOT omniscient future mother
  -> actual intermediate events change M; at later t_C maternal state
     selects eligible conception PATHWAY with prior meeting constraints
  -> locate obligatory earlier first intersection E (t_E <= t_C), and
     any earlier births/siblings; keep selected trajectory conditional

FATHER INVERSE CANDIDATE (not yet shared-world committed):
  -> PYAT candidate F observable/cloud conditioned on earlier E,
     selected conception pathway C and stipulated focal birth B
     [SOURCEFUL support only if actually identified; else TEST_ONLY SCENARIO]
  -> SOROK construct independent H_f over t < t_E in isolated candidate scope
  -> validate father's work/place/role/care/resources/actor state as-of t_E
  -> compare father claims with FROZEN, SHARED, actually represented
     mother/world facts (NOT a fabricated complete world past):
       time/order, ownership/currency, institutional events, overlap,
       any encounter effects on mother, named RNG, causal ancestry

  -> ACCEPT candidate only at its stated evidence scope if it coexists
     with all *represented* relevant facts under world/branch contract
     OR REFUSE / produce another independent candidate, never rewrite H_m
  -> join distinct actor IDs/actor-local histories under one admitted
     shared-world root/branch and objective E event (or exact HOLD)
  -> father independently recurses from spawn to E, then both actors
     develop through objective relation years with distinct perceptions
  -> prior sibling actor genealogies/births/development before focal C
  -> only then conception/focal birth/child initialization separately
     admitted, and all family actors continue afterward
```

**Possible reconciliation strategies to compare, not decree:**
- **Cheapest first:** father prehistory is an actor-local, model-graded compressed candidate with as-of constraints and explicit unknown material effects; at encounter, verify its *represented* resource/role/location/history consistency against the existing world, rather than demanding all missing microevents be retroactively written into P2.
- If a past father event must become an **actual shared-world fact**, require the competent existing owner to reconcile its effects against already sealed overlapping facts; if this cannot be done, HOLD rather than silently assign a root/clock or invent a new temporal event store.
- Candidate father history can reference shared earlier world facts without re-enacting them.
- A father candidate may have actor-local earlier activity with bounded effects on world facts that do not conflict with the frozen mother-world history; the competent owner must prove admissibility, not merely string equality.
- If both histories would require mutually incompatible changes to already committed world history, refuse this candidate, or branch to an **uncommitted** alternative mother-world construction with its own provenance. Never silently mutate the accepted mother.
- Do not materialize father-specific earlier events by appending them at t_E and lying about their performed time.

This is a proposed **world/history admissibility** obligation under existing owners, not permission to install a general retroactive event store.

### Important non-equivalences

A father may be constructed later in computation without any *backwards causal influence*: the birth condition is a candidate-selection constraint, not an event that caused his past. Nor does an unrecorded pre-encounter event equal a false event. World-source silence is generally **UNKNOWN**, not a negative witness. A conflict is real only if two asserted, same-scope, temporally overlapping consequences cannot coexist. Historical candidate snapshots may carry partial, model-graded constraints without falsely presenting whole-life performed P2 events. Keep actor-local private recollections distinct from objective-world events.

## Hostile counterfactuals and acceptance evidence

1. **Impossible co-location:** mother intersects father at workplace at time t_E but father candidate has incompatible location/work constraint. REFUSE, no LLM relocation.
2. **Independent alternatives:** two plausible fathers satisfy t_E/B, retain different possible prehistories. Selecting one leaves unchanged mother H_m and other candidate fates.
3. **Shared-history contradiction:** father past requires a world effect that contradicts a sealed mother-world fact. Refuse or reconstruct a distinct uncommitted candidate branch; never graft silently.
4. **In-world vs computation order:** father can be created later in software while having chronologically valid earlier event parents; no clock rollback or double event.
5. **Separated relation:** encountered father never co-resides; household/carer structure independently governed. No forced marriage or nuclear family.
6. **Birth condition epistemic:** branch selected for eventual birth, but no empirical couple/birth likelihood exists. Retain `SOURCEFREE_CONDITIONAL`; do not claim population frequencies.
7. **Cold root and named RNG:** restore and replay do not change maternal earlier state due solely to father search.
