## 1. The exact correction to our R2/R3 description

Our earlier “mother's actual history → select a feasible encounter E → infer father conditional on E and a future birth B” **compresses away the principal's decisive mechanism**. The mother is not matching an already-defined father or even simply picking a meeting from a set of external co-location opportunities. A **very small number of compressed forward mother-development recursions** (the principal envisages perhaps **2–3 LLM/recursion calls**, provisional engineering budget, *not* a fixed rule) move her through *multiple fictional years*. In each step, **the LLM integrates the currently grounded computational mother-persona**, her existing past/identity/material-social habitus and actual world to **propose** an event stack and conditional alternatives.

The proposer **does not know exactly who the mother will have become at the future final event**, because intermediate qualified recursions have not occurred. The mother undergoes those events; her **later computed state at the point of selecting/encountering the conception possibility** determines **which conception trajectory is realized**, and *how its circumstances are incorporated into her actual life* subject to objective biological/material/temporal admissibility. LLM suggestion is neither event performance nor authoritative actor intention.

The **conception event is intended as the terminal event in this pre-focal maternal forward proposal/recursion segment** (the principal's provisional design); it is **not** the end of mother's development and does not mean no later pregnancy, birth or parenting events. The programme is conditional on creating a **particular eventual focal child**. That macro goal is visible to the construction procedure but **must not be used to inject the mother's exact future person-state into earlier LLM calls**, or force a specific spouse, attitude, desired pregnancy or preordained relationship dynamic.

**Core noncollapse:**
- **LLM proposal time:** what is conditionally offered based on current mother's *then-current* state and observed world; proposals may cover future fictional years.
- **Fictional event time:** when the mother reaches each proposed opportunity after being altered by the intervening actually admitted events; *that* state grounds choice/occurrence.
- **Computational father-construction time:** after the relevant maternal conception trajectory has been selected, construct a father back-conditionally, including a causally earlier history.
- **Commitment/admission time:** a candidate can remain pending until obligatory prior relations, actor histories, body/genealogy and objective world consistency are validated. Do not retroactively declare an unproved candidate a fully performed past.

**Conception is not necessarily a consciously chosen goal:** voluntary planning, consensual unplanned pregnancy, unequal agency, coercion, reproductive failure and other conditions have distinct lawful event structures. Given the programme's selected eventual birth branch, no unconditional real-world fertility or parenthood law can be invented; actor intention and biological occurrence are separate. This document does not impose moralized or universal marriage conventions.

## 2. The selected option may contain a RELATIONSHIP CHRONOLOGY, not merely a meeting coordinate

The principal's illustrative mother is straight-laced and conservative *because of her actual generated biography and habitus*, not because the survey label magically determines morality. Her eligible proposed conception trajectory might entail:

1. Earlier acquaintance/meeting in a historically possible social network or setting;
2. subsequent mutual interaction and a possibly socially conventional marriage, where actually chosen/admitted;
3. separately realized pregnancies and births of any existing children;
4. the eventual focal conception as first/second/third/fourth/etc child under the actual generated trajectory.

Other mothers may generate radically different options and genealogical/care arrangements. **“E” must not be treated as necessarily the time of conception**. The earliest consequential parental intersection may occur years before it. A selected terminal conception option can carry **constraints on earlier event/relationship history** that must be realized and validated at their actual effective times. Those constraints need not fully disclose or dictate the father's interiority.

The father is then constructed from a PYAT-qualified or clearly model-graded conditional **observable/material profile** inferred from the intersection and selected conception trajectory. Historically plausible similarities in location, language, resources, social milieu, institutions, networks and observable attitudes are **conditional candidate constraints**, not a validated “people like her marry men like X” probability law and not a requirement that their private psychologies match. He has his own source-grade demographic/attitude cloud (where qualified), otherwise hypothesis; the unexplained remainder of his person is **not forced to resemble her**.

Then **his own compressed developmental recursion proceeds forward from an earlier spawn point to the historically stipulated first encounter**. These **intersection-required constraints are fixed**; other elements of his development remain genuinely variable. Once he meets the mother, their ongoing material/relational activity—and *each one's distinct acquired organization, interpretations, projects and effects on the other*—must be represented across the years leading up to the selected conception event.

**Why this matters:** “find a plausible man at t_conception” is a huge under-specification. A father who was a spouse for five years cannot first computationally acquire a past the moment of conception and leave the couple's five years as already psychologically completed mother-only narration. At minimum, the shared years must be reconciled as a real **two-actor development** with objective relationship and distinct perceived-other states.

## 3. The hard computational crack: forward maternal state vs later instantiated paternal independence

There is a genuine design tension (not a refutation): the mother **selects a conception trajectory after several forward recursions**, but when it contains prior marriage/shared years, the father—who helped shape *who the mother would become* by the selected conception—has **not yet been fully instantiated**. An incorrect implementation can make the father an after-the-fact puppet, silently rewrite the mother's acquired state, or use omniscient future knowledge to backfill perfect compatibility.

**Promising resolution for SOROK to scrutinize: stage candidate trajectory vs fully admitted joint event history.** Distinguish:

- **Mother-owned pre-intersection development:** actually completed and immutable, under her known world/actor history.
- **Mother-proposed relational/conception option:** event-stack/trajectory hypothesis generated from then-current mother state. The hypothetical spouse/partner has bounded **role/encounter constraints**, *not* a fake finished independent psyche. Until second-actor admission, any partner-dependent maternal updates must be **provisional or supported by admitted actual encounters**, not asserted as completed independent-person interaction.
- **Conception-option selection at arrival:** grounded in her **then-developed** mother-person state and the admissible current world. The candidate is *selected for conditional realization*, not granted unconditional world truth while required father history/relationship remains unconstructed.
- **Father backward construction and bounded independent forward life:** keep his exact necessary meeting and focal-conception intersection constraints; generate a genuinely other person and his non-locked prior development, without retro-changing sealed mother pre-intersection history.
- **Shared-year reconciliation:** realize real objective interaction/relationship episodes and separately qualified mother/father actor-state consequences from the first meeting to conception, without double-counting those years or treating the already authored maternal relationship depiction as the father's true psychology.
- **Final joint admission:** re-check whether the mother's **jointly realized state**, the father's actual independence, objective events, conception eligibility, existing children's histories and world state still permit the selected maternal trajectory. If impossible, **REFUSE this candidate and return to an uncommitted branch/checkpoint** rather than force a father or re-author the frozen mother's psyche.

This **provisional/final distinction is a proposed engineering solution**, NOT a principal-specified algorithm or current function. SOROK may have a better one in the existing P2/P3/ADP branch model. The scientific/computational obligation is to avoid *both* predetermined mother destiny and retrofitted father puppetry.

A useful **two-phase admission analogy** (NOT a requirement for a new database/transaction framework):
1. **Selection/constraint phase:** mother-driven conceived possibility and necessary earlier relation anchors.
2. **Reconciliation/admission phase:** independently generated father and, where relevant, existing siblings plus executed joint-development history satisfy those constraints, preserving all actually committed causal past.

At no point does selecting the birth branch establish a measured partner distribution, biological efficacy, or a unique latent psyche.

## 4. Older siblings: full causal family members before focal child's genesis

**New explicitly principal-grounded requirement:** if the focal child is the **second, third, fourth, etc**, any older siblings must be generated as **real simpler-resolution actors** *before* the focal child's birth—indeed their own conceptions, births and development are chronological predecessors that help make the mother's/father's and household's present state what it is. Their child-specific actor state, body/age, activity, access, relationships, prior experiences and material effects must participate in the co-developing family. **“Simpler” is compute resolution, not absence of independent agency, separate access or ability to continue developing.**

At focal birth, do not inject fake “older sister is jealous” / “older brother protects” as a personality variable because of their age/role. Their responses are generated from their own prior activity and current position; rivalry, affection, indifference, care, conflict, absent contact, and third-person involvement are all possible, not predetermined.

Important genealogical complication: existing siblings may have a different biological parent from the focal child. The maternal and conditional paternal generators must not automatically assign the newly generated father as the father of every existing child. An earlier sibling's other parent, separated caregiver or coactor may need to exist (at compressed resolution) to the extent their actions and relationships materially affect this actual family. **One focal father's backfill does not replace all prior genealogy.**

## 5. The forward recursion after focal birth remains genuinely plural

Once the focal child is born and sparse actor initialization is admitted, **the focal child develops at greater detail**, but parents, siblings, coactors and institutions **do not freeze as scenery**. Existing family actors continue to change through independently qualified opportunities, actual activity, new access and historically formed structures at a computationally appropriate lower resolution. They can initiate consequences for each other and for the focal child, including deeds the focal child does not witness.

The intended output is not only “the child experienced caring mother and sibling.” It is a world in which **the sibling had already become somebody**, a parent can surprise or change, and their independently structured activity transforms what the focal person can attempt. This satisfies SEAM/19 Z, SEAM/21 variable psychology, SEAM/22 ordinary-life economy and SEAM/23 optional recurrent motivational organization **inside** the specified genesis, not instead of it.

**Computational tradeoffs to compare:**
- Avoid one giant omniscient LLM call with exact mother’s future self/partner. Instead run short calls conditioned on current committed checkpoints, with actual event-time selection.
- Branch/role late-binding may preserve proposal openness without constructing a full paternal actor too early. Yet leaving important spouse years merely provisional can make mother's last-state selector scientifically incoherent; *where is the earliest point father must become computationally real*?
- A final-only birth target helps choose a focal child branch but risks teleology if it biases every mother's initial event; record conditioning grade and resist hidden causal force.
- Older sibling prelife may be represented in fewer events **only if** their actor causal state supports later independent behavior; a role label is not a real sibling.

## 7. Negative controls and minimal proofs for the UMs

1. **No clairvoyant proposal:** generate a candidate conception list from mother M(ti). Alter legitimately admitted intermediate events, leading to different mother M(t_last); test different selected option or legitimate refusal. The LLM cannot pre-read M(t_last) at candidate-generation time.
2. **No default marriage:** same source cloud but changed material and acquired history may yield different relationship/conception configurations; a straight-laced conservative persona does **not** compel marriage by schema.
3. **No last-timepoint meeting substitution:** selected fourth-child conception in existing marriage implies an actual earlier meeting and family history; inserting a newly instantiated father only at t_conception with no earlier causal lineage must fail.
4. **No paternal puppet:** keep mandatory historical meeting/conception constraints; permit other paternal prior experiences to differ. Where legitimate, his later independently performed deed/perception differs while maternal frozen pre-intersection facts remain the same.
5. **No precognition through retrospective family narration:** if mother's selected last state depends on partner's past interaction, verify that the actual father and relationship generated after conditional selection *satisfy the same actor-state formation constraints*; inconsistent branch fails, not overwritten.
6. **Older sibling must matter:** fix focal infant initial body; compare a real older sibling in a family at t_birth with a branch where sibling's own earlier acts and material effects differ, changing a later focal opportunity **only if** competent coactor/world owner admits it. Same “older brother” label alone changes nothing.
7. **No automatic shared paternity:** earlier sibling child-generation may reference another biological parent; do not assign the focal father retrospectively to all children.
8. **Continued co-constitution:** postbirth mother, father, older siblings each act or lawfully remain quiet through distinct owner-access-state paths, and one independently created event changes another's possibilities without magically imparting knowledge.
9. **Cold chronology:** conception event, first encounter, prior sibling births, focal birth, names/actor IDs, as-of times, candidate refs and source grades survive cold reopen; rejected paternal candidates do not become family memories.
