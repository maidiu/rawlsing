## Governing object, before any of the seven records

**DO NOT replace SEAM/20's generative computational order with forward-only random coupling or with a generic PYAT→P6 affordance adapter.**

1. PYAT can currently qualify **bounded observed respondent/distributional relationships** in particular sourcefree/source-governed cuts; a **correlated synthetic maternal demographic/material/survey-response draw** is the *intended, separately gated consumer*, not an already issued capability. Preserve respondent design, source, support, historical as-of, uncertainty; an adult answer is not psyche.
2. SOROK generates **several compressed, forward-developed maternal candidate histories using the SAME developmental ontology as the eventual child**, checks compatibility with her observed surface at the proper evidence grade, and commits one actual fictional iceberg, with underdetermination visible.
3. The mother recurses **forward through a small number of compressed calls over years**. LLMs propose event stacks and conditional **conception trajectories** from her *then-current* computational state; actual intervening events change her before she reaches the proposed final conception. **Her state at that later event-time** helps determine which option is selected/realized under objective constraints. A relationship/first meeting may have begun years earlier; event proposal is not performance.
4. **Only after** the mother has selected a conception trajectory, generate the father **backward-conditionally** on its **required earlier first encounter/relationship history and focal conception/birth constraints**. Recurse his independently formed history forward from an earlier spawn to that encounter; thereafter independently develop **both** through the selected relationship years. Computational order and in-world causal order differ. The exact staged joint admission remains a research question, not an existing engine.
5. Independently developing mother and father undergo actual joint/independent activity, changing material conditions, relationship, subjective perceived-other images, and perhaps household. **If the focal child is not firstborn, earlier siblings must already exist and have separately developed at appropriate lighter resolution**; prior sibling genealogy can involve other parents. Encounter ≠ relationship ≠ household ≠ parenthood ≠ child-generation.
6. Distinguish **selected conception event, later gestation and focal birth/actor initialization** under the relevant biological/ADP constraints. The focal child develops an **open-ended acquired repertoire and organization** through its own ongoing material/relational life. **Both parents, any older siblings, other caregivers and coactors keep computationally developing** at appropriate resolutions rather than becoming family scenery.
7. Ordinary life is economically generated with qualified coverage and some finer events; eventual acquired organization (possibly, not necessarily, C5) is recruited in **actual independent later action and a playable continuing Other**.

**Hard distinction:** compressed **parental back-generation** is *not* the same operation as open-ended **child psychological formation**. SEAM/22's multiresolution ordinary-life process is a third, separately governed economy; same state *semantics* do not imply same temporal resolution, omniscient childhood detail, or identical compute budget.

## Source / authority discipline

- **Empirical observed:** licensed, correct source/risk/same-respondent/joint/weights/temporal validity. Observed conditional frequency is not parent action, couple formation or child psyche.
- **Accepted semantic authority:** what existing CAN/ADP and PYAT governance permit as vocabulary and refusal; not proof a current runtime executes it.
- **Observed source code:** pinned callable/guard with measured or inspected behavior at a named source HEAD, not proof of joint/native implementation.
- **Synthetic SOURCEFREE_TEST_ONLY hypothesis:** an openly proposed generative rule/scenario. Fictional committed events may be real *inside the generated world* without becoming real-world empirical facts.
- **UNKNOWN/HELD/NOT_MODELED:** a legitimate result, not zero, typical development, negative evidence, or an invitation to fill narrative gaps.
- **Not allowed:** raw survey→private motive; partner marginal→measured couple; co-presence→learning; narrated past→admitted event; compression→remembered ungenerated episodes; typed motif→attractor→Enneagram type as automatic cause.
