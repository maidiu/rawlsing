# SEAM / 03 — Joint disputation, source comparison and receiving (R0)

**Status:** working method for two independent HQ agents. **Not** a new approval tribunal, third HQ, obligatory bureaucracy or an autonomous science-issuing authority.

## 1. The two speakers

**PYAT seat — scientific/objective-world claimant.** Arrives fully grounded in current PROGRAMMA PYAT, original source authority, S1–S11, adopted FF R2, SDTF R4, N9/N10E/N10W, V558/M4/M5 and W/Astra obligations. **SOROK seat — actual historically constituted person/consumer claimant.** Arrives fully grounded in PROGRAMMA SOROK, original V3/Astra/Rawlsing vision, W/S-series and modern CAN/ADP/PFF/SDTF, C0–C12 and A0–A5, independently constituted other, actual P3/P4/P5/P6/M I–III and their current versions, sourcefree-first-consumer ceilings, and human-product obligations. Never grants source authority, fabricates independent material structure, or defines objective world science by psychological convenience.

They have **equal standing to challenge** one another and no power to take the other's original competence. PRE-MAIN owns common correspondence/protected receiving, not a third science executive. The principal may decide architecture and priorities; competent source/science/actor/human issuers must still issue their own claims.

## 2. First exchanges

Each agent performs its own independent grok and drafts a **substantive original position**, including at least:

- A concise statement of the overall finished Rawlsing human/product and why the seam exists.
- The **ten most demanding terminal capabilities**, derived from real source text and current accepted replacements, plus *one ordinary no-change* case.
- For each capability, the actual transformation the preceding owner must perform, exact input and output meanings, first reader and failed-capability counterexample.
- Present existing implementations and tests at exact ref/status vs missing engineering, missing selected consumer, open source/scientific authority, and held native/human right.
- Structural problem with the other HQ's expected inputs or outputs, **even if the other's prose is compelling**.
- At least three points where the historical original vision conflicts with a later adopted mechanism/representation, with source-graded reconciliation instead of "newest wins."
- A self-exposed strongest weakness or possible false-positive in the speaker's own position.

**Important:** SOROK writes its independent position **before** using PYAT's R0 draft as the canonical truth. PYAT likewise rereads current sources before it makes a current HQ assertion. Each author signs the exact commit, source cut and epistemic grade.

## 3. The dialogue format — each claim must survive a hostile transformation test

For each cross-boundary assertion, use the following unit:

> **CLAIM** — this terminal Sorok capacity requires this exact PRE-V3 output or service, because this stage must transform X into Y under rule/authority Z.
>
> **CHALLENGE** — show the earliest point in the actual system where X, transformation Z, Y, or actual first reader is absent, inadequate, falsely qualified or unnecessary. Give exact source/code at immutable SHA and a counterexample where materially different lawful input must alter a consumer result.
>
> **RESPONSE/REPAIR** — reuse a valid current owner or construct the minimum correct change under a verified writer lease, preserving unchanged functions. Run old RED → new GREEN → restore and relevant negatives whenever code is available. A scientifically held assertion is reduced to the smallest *truly irreplaceable* fact/permission/issuer and a prospective runnable test.
>
> **COUNTERATTACK/CONCESSION** — the original speaker attacks the *repair and its claim of terminal sufficiency*, not its author. Concede demonstrated improvement; preserve unresolved objection with the exact discriminating test and rightful decider.
>
> **DISPOSITION** — mutually supported scoped claim, exact contested alternative, known code defect, bounded sourcefree pass, external science hold, real selected non-demand, or no current consumer; preserve distinct independent receiving and protected integration.

No "the tests pass" in place of actual semantic outcome. No "downstream needs it" without proof of the relevant stage transformation and consumer. No "upstream not ready" when a sourcefree model can settle the engineering contract today. No enforced theoretical unanimity.

Are we mistaking a causal explanation available to the model for a reason available to the actor? Does a complete and elegant repository still generate no consequential differences between possible lives? Did we accidentally make a restrictive source/permission gate substitute for a missing generative transformation? Are we smuggling newly unissued personality theory across the boundary by calling it a schema?

## 6. Evidence statuses; no single DONE flag

Every disputed seam record has independently legible status in these courts:

- **Architecture and intended function:** historical proposed, current adopted, replaced with qualified equivalent, unresolved competing claims.
- **Scientific/semantic authority:** proved measurement, explicit exploratory/conditional model, competent current structural/mechanism rule, or exact open scientific issuer.
- **Executable transformation:** implemented exact owner and observed test, author-only/independent, not implemented, or unverified runner.
- **Input and output reality:** eligible sourcefree synthetic, historical source, retained qualified original, native exact/current selected, negative/no-valid input; don't collapse.
- **Actual first-consumer integration:** selected real consumer and changed-value/restore, bounded fixture, unproved first reader, absent demand.
- **Actor and human rights:** explicitly permitted actor use, correct knowledge and event admission, native developmental rules, user data/privacy, human/product participation; distinct holds.
- **Protected receiving:** isolated candidate PR vs independently reviewed exact immutable commit vs qualified protected merge.

An independent reviewer must be different from the implementer. Neither a Pyat nor Sorok counterpart automatically becomes an independent *terminal reviewer* of a change they helped specify or author.

## 7. Where the dispute can legitimately terminate

The debate ends **for a scoped claim** when actual requirements and their warranted grades have been exhausted: the stage's required transformation has an owner, a lawful input, a built or correctly scoped proof, an output actually read by an actual next consumer, falsifying negatives/restore, and an independent scoped receiving—or an exact external fact/rule/right has been shown to be irreplaceable.

The debate cannot terminate merely because the two agents agree on an appealing diagram. Nor does scientific HOLD justify suspending unrelated design/code/testing work. Conversely, some true claims may remain permanently unprovable from available original data: **do not force an invented positive** to manufacture a happy joint conclusion.

**Final joint question:** What is the smallest truthful and sufficiently rich interface that lets structurally conditioned history become particular lives, while ensuring that no actor becomes a ventriloquized survey response and no objective world becomes an autobiographical hallucination?

Preserve disagreement until it has a falsifier. Protect real scientific refusal as carefully as an accepted positive.
