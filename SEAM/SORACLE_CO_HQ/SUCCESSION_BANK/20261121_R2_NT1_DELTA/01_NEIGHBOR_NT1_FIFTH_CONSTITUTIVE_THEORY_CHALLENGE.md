## N-T1's new positive thesis

**Her earlier fictional world made Nadia; the later actual user encounter can become part of the world that goes on making her.** This is not an informational relationship with a finished simulated biography, nor an unconstrained player-controlled character editor. The user already has a history elsewhere; a small bounded portal/social position puts their real authorship in touch with Nadia's longer independent history. They may have effects, or none. Their participation can be materially consequential while their understanding of her motives remains incomplete. The two participants have nonidentical ontological/epistemic relationships to the encounter.

**Leitmotif:** the game of getting close does not mean maximization of a friendship scalar. What develops is **a differentiated field of what the pair and their wider relations have become able, willing and entitled to do together**. Proximity, access, disclosure, erotic contact, affection, dependency, trust, freedom, domination, benefit, moral good and accurate understanding can all diverge; any monotonic summary will erase the causal human world.

## The seventeen-section reconstruction's CO-facing demands

1. **History before scene (``I–IV, IX–X).** Actor's embodied activity, preattentive recruitment, projects, prior family/work/institutions, self-opacity and current operations must exist before user input. The player does not initialize Nadia by asking her a question; an "NPC response" is not the unit of computation. Interaction must become a qualified new occasion inside an existing developmental process.
2. **Participation more than revelation (``IV–VI).** User knowing means acquiring situated practices, shared routines, competences and mistaken expectations through actual events; not unlocking a scripted catalog of Nadia's private traits. A user's joke, practical offer or absence can alter material routines and future recruitment, without guaranteeing a motive, favor or social reward.
3. **One action, many actor-specific lived histories (`V).** Helping with a child, for example, can objectively enable an appointment while generating relief in one person, indebtedness/shame in another, distrust in a third and delight in the child. Do not turn these distinct experienced realities into one "event meaning", "relationship gain", or LLM narrator consensus.
4. **Relation of relations (`VII).** Nadia's family, friends and institutions must have sufficiently independent participant histories when causal stakes require them. Third parties aren't moral police or props generated by the user's importance. A parent/caregiver/child relation can be mediated by the co-parent relation itself. **The user is never the sole social cause of Nadia's life.**
5. **Differentiate pleasure, consent, coercion and material power (`VIII).** A fictional adult may voluntarily enjoy an unconventional or socially condemned activity, including under unequal material conditions; enjoyment is not proof of material freedom. Coercive acts and material harms can occur without another's consent; a voluntary co-act requires each actor's actual eligible authorship. Outward agreement, fear, coercive structural dependence and an actor's subjective pleasure are not interchangeable. Do not mechanically assign trauma, pathology, empowerment or compliance because of a social category.
6. **Iceberg causally operative and sometimes silent (`IX).** Learned, contradictory pre-cue orientation shapes what she notices, anticipates, wants, avoids, attempts and says; neither user nor actor has unlimited reflective access. There need not be a dramatic gesture or secret inner exposition. A self-explanation may itself become an operative historically formed practice without becoming infallibly true.
7. **Three asymmetric entrances (`X).** Fictional parents: source-qualified PYAT whole-respondent situated adult construction, abductively backfilled without forged receipts. Nadia: bodily infant with open development. Visitor: **real, already formed human**, afforded only a bounded local practical/social position, not an independently generated second fictive psyche. Role translation must preserve what they really authored and disclose material changes.
8. **Two genuinely separate user models (`XI).** Platform may infer a source-qualified fallible longitudinal external user's attention/participation; Nadia models only an actual encountered person from lawful actor evidence. Private screen/gaze/profile data must never become Nadia's knowledge or be fed back as tailored personality-reward/penalty. Exposure and offered opportunities condition what user conduct proves.
9. **World fidelity and human artifice (``II–III, XII).** An openly artificial portal can make human historical contingency strange, not pretend the user inherited Nadia's childhood. Translation can be useful, but silently assigning the user fabricated personal history defeats human authorship. Materialized changes require honest feedback where meaning actually changed.
10. **Time without ownership (`XIII).** Continuing actor worlds can have consequences for promises, absence and other relations without treating user app attendance as compulsory caregiver labor. No offline user impersonation, coercive reminders or moral punishment for being away. User may leave, encounter a new source-qualified life, or delete real account data; that does not make prior fictional acts into undoable non-events within a retained continuity.
11. **Platform/fictional rights separation (`XIV).** Human data rights, unwanted psychological inference and platform incentives are actual governance issues. Fictional conflict, injury, manipulation and ethically disturbing relationships must remain causally representable without the engine engineering a punitive moral lesson, a tailored abuse reward route, exploiter instructional content, or secretly installing suffering as retention.
12. **Direct future consumer challenge (`XV).** Real external human contributions may change **multiple independent persons' historical activity**, not just Nadia's response. Need actor-qualified sensory/temporal/world reception, distinct actor-world activity participation, plural third-party differential afterlife, relation/practice changes under contradiction and no compulsory affected-person interpretation.
13. **Adversarial falsifiers (`XVI).** A sympathetic user can be rejected; a cruel/biased user may find an affectionate companion; more intimacy may accompany worse material domination; same precontact person under distinct visitors acquires divergent post-contact histories; unrealized subjective psychology must remain hidden; family doesn't intervene just to deliver the designer's moral lesson. If a beautiful LLM family scene lacks lawful independently qualified causes, it's a false success.
14. **Full vision vs economical alpha (``II, XIII, XVII).** Original population-dependent unchosen draw ("someone you could have been born as") remains a long-run source-qualified aim, not currently delivered PYAT capability; an honest handful of richly constructed text-first persons can test person-specific independent contact now, without swapping full-depth construction for surface-friendly fictional profiles or building a political-quest game.

## Direct immanent problems CO must press *back* against N-T1 (not already resolved by its rhetoric)

- Can access be a genuine material/practical field without reducing relationships to a graph of affordances? How do contradictory motifs, self-myths, active/inhibited history and plurally mediated practice acquire *operative* sourcegrade change?
- Does the "game getting close" privilege user/desirable interpersonal access over actor projects where user presence is structurally insignificant or disruptive? What about non-dyadic social activity and offscreen relations that never become user-readable?
- Can one material event produce different *qualified lived signs* without an omniscient developmental model inferring every participant's interiority? How much fidelity is necessary for third parties when their actions first become causal?
- What actually distinguishes a person's voluntary activity, acquiescence, material dependence, coercion, failed attempt, unperformed intention and unilateral injury in the **world authority**, rather than merely in literary account?
- How can the fictional "possible person" be drawn from population constraints while leaving that person's developmental history non-deterministic and not asserting scientific unconscious causes from questionnaires?
- How are ordinary life hypercompression, absent user time and participant autonomy reconciled with actual event provenance, cold replay and one writer?
- What does the platform actually have the right to infer about a real user from gamified choices, given it controls the choice set/exposures? How is representation separated from inference?
- How do future psychologically richer than today's R25 processes become genuine first consumers while preserving original MI/MII/MIII sensory and privacy distinctions? Never flatten one to match the other's fields.

## C-T1 must be a new fully positive theory and code demand, not a reaction memo

The Constitutive Organism already made important advances: acting persons with acquired/inaccessible recruitment, independent work, relation revaluation, revisable forms and world consequences. N-T1's profound contribution is to make **actual human participation** a class of world and social activity that can contribute to lifelong reconstitution of actor-world organization—without becoming the sole cause, moral judge or plot owner. C-T1 should recast its own deepest scheme where this reveals structural limits, not simply note that a user-facing adapter is missing. **Keep the current material construction and tests moving.**
