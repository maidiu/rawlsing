## A fundamental revision in the conception of the person

The psychologically constitutive unit is **the organism's historically organized relationship to its world through concrete activity, participation, experience, recognition, material dependence and practical possibility**. A form, project, affect, need, moral category or tendency is a relatively stabilized differentiation of that organization. It is not an autonomous psychological object whose meaning is constant across history or relationships.

The "iceberg" metaphor points to deep, partly inaccessible, contradictory, historically acquired organization, not a static inventory of latent traits. A particular care relation can become protection of another's freedom, a means of violent discipline, a defense of status, a culturally legitimized domination, an occasion for sorrow, or solidarity. The same outward behavior can have different historical causes; internal tension can be unacknowledged; actors do not consciously run a spreadsheet of their own motives. No "sovereign iceberg manager" chooses everything. No winner-by-sum motivational scalar is scientifically warranted.

## Distinctions to conserve, not fuse into one event object

- Objective material events, bodies, institutions, practical possibilities and Others are real *within the fictional world* independently of the actor's beliefs.
- An actor's actual sensor-/time-/privacy-qualified experienced signs are a proper subset of world effects.
- Their lived psychic meaning, antecedent acquired forms and attention/recruitment are historically constituted, not a direct world label or narrator reading.
- An actor's speech, outward expression, attempted action and objective outcome may differ, and may contradict their intention.
- Their self-model can be mistaken, defensive, mythologizing and revalued without retroactively changing original events.
- A human user's encounter and finite phenomenal surface is another distinct epistemic center; neither protagonist nor player owns omniscient story truth.

## Psyche × society: relation-of-relations

Relational constitution without relational exhaustibility: a person exceeds one dyad, a dyad exceeds either member's experience of it, the world exceeds all viewers. Every current function of a relation depends on other forms/relations and institutions. A source previously inhibited can exert indirect historical influence without "reactivating" its own operation. A target inhibited operation cannot be revived by mere narrative re-description. Acquired relations can change under lived contradiction without the earlier relation being erased. Opposing currents and genuine ambiguity remain consequential instead of being collapsed into a coherent personality.

Power: all "authority", religion, work, employer, family, culture and class dynamics must be *located in actual material dependencies, practices, consequences and actor-specific histories*. Do not hardcode that religious people obey or that hardship produces liberation. Objective unilateral imposition and another's nonconsent cannot become ethically justified because the performer recruited care or believes their act loving.

## Reality of ordinary time

One life includes quotidian compressed offscreen care, meals, labor, institutions, weather, dependency, social visits, failed action, intermittent attention and private thought. Days/weeks/years must change practical and relational history without user-written scenes. A perfect dramatic encounter model is a false organism if it cannot autonomously sustain mundane life when no user watches.
