# SEAM / 01 — PYAT's opening position on the PRE-V3 ↔ V3 boundary

**Speaker:** initial PYAT-side reconstruction, **not** the appointed PYAT HQ's certified submission. **Status:** SOURCE-GROUNDED PRELIMINARY PROPOSAL, 2026-11-12. **Counterparty:** an independent modern SOROK HQ that has not yet replied.

## I. A proposition about the seam

**PRE-V3 must deliver a historically and materially meaningful, independently qualified world in which a particular actor can have a life. SOROK must turn that available world into the actual histories of particular persons—through their own embodied needs, relations, knowledge, activity, encounters, attempts, consequences and development—without treating objective structure as their already-written psyche.**

Equally: PRE-V3 cannot stop at supplying descriptive survey objects, a demographic field table, an unlabeled probability distribution or a sophisticated immutable repository. It must create the *objective, source- and theory-qualified possibilities, constraints, relations, powers and temporal situations* needed by the person-generating runtime. When actual persons act outwardly and their independently admitted acts have real objective consequences, qualified world owners must be able to incorporate those changes without reclassifying private actor experience as objective evidence.

Thus SEAM is **bidirectional, temporally ordered, asymmetric in authority, and conditional in its routes**. It is not one magical handoff, one globally mandatory serialized object, one nine-strata pressure vector, a source→person inference map, or a shared psychology/world state database.

A useful causal distinction is:

- **Evidence** constrains what can be said about the world. Survey proportions, same-person joint configurations, sampling limits and documentary/structural material cannot simply become an individual's disposition.
- **World constitution** establishes actual independent positions, institutional capacities and restrictions, resources, obligations, channels, changing conditions and source-limited opportunities. It also preserves what is not known, not comparable, not causally established or not currently authorized.
- **Possibility** is what can happen under these qualified conditions; it is not a promised or uniquely determined event. N10E and N10W must remain different: actual empirical candidate distributions and world-conditioned possibilities are not identical operations.
- **Mediation** is the concrete channel through which possible objective forces can become a particular person's circumstances, rather than a direct class→emotion arrow. Structural mediation routes and their prerequisites may be upstream; *which route is actually realized through an actor's life* belongs downstream.
- **Actor history** is constituted only through the SOROK/neo-V3 lawful machinery: actor/coactor genesis, encounters, access, present response, independent action or omission, durable formation or evaluated no-change, memory and later activity. It is not an attractive posterior explanation written by the LLM.
- **Public expression and observer experience** are again separate. Rendered text, private player attention, unreported world events and actor knowledge cannot substitute for one another.

The terminal Rawlsing test is substantive: **a person's history should be intelligible when followed backward, without a unique future being deducible from their starting conditions**. The same structural world can support different actual lives; the same survey conditional cannot certify one person's interiority.

## II. What SOROK needs in order to generate a life

The world has to be substantial enough for all of these questions to receive *qualified, discriminating answers* where a real source and rule permit them:

**1. Situated material reproduction.** Who owns, holds, controls, owes, uses, needs, pays, cares, works, feeds, shelters, excludes and protects? The world must distinguish legal title, access, tenancy, dependent use, care, work, time allocation, institutional eligibility, money and nonmarket provisioning. A world that knows only age, sex, country, income and average social attitudes cannot generate an independently continuing mother, workplace, landlord or household. It can at most stereotype one.

**2. Social power and pressure, not mere prevalence.** For whom is an option materially possible, easy, expensive, dangerous, forbidden, rewarded or degraded? What organizations, classes, offices, families and institutions can enforce their terms; where are buffers, counterpowers, rival solidarities, contradictory imperatives and opportunities for resistance? A large survey majority need not govern an institution; prevalence is not hegemony. Power and mediation need their own competent evidence and scientific rule. Opposed fields must not be arithmetically canceled into a tidy scalar.

**3. Historical change and location.** What was actually true of this world at the relevant place and time? Which constraints persist, expire, rupture, recur, vary by household or remain unknown? An empirical measured shift across comparable waves is not automatically an objective mechanism changing a person's life. The world must retain multiple timescales and preserve real valid-time, as-of/currentness and source population.

**4. Multiple conditional futures.** The world can genuinely restrict an opportunity set while still supporting different materially conditioned possible experiences and courses of action. Same-person F4 joints, minority and counterpattern distributions, honest uncertainty, population/conditioner roles, true risk sets and independent F6 cross-survey nonpooling must be available to the appropriate consumer where demanded. Survey marginals may constrain possibilities; they do not provide an independently sampled cast of personality traits or a finished parent/child genealogy.

**5. Carriers and encounters.** The actor does not meet "imperialism" or "class" in the abstract: they meet working hours, an employer, a social service, an eviction, a school, a family demand, a union, an invitation, a religious institution, a checkpoint, a promise. PRE-V3 owes the actual independent objective position, route, institution, channel and event-eligibility context when qualified; SOROK owes particular actor/coactor histories, which circumstances are encountered or noticed, the causal response and later consequences.

**6. Ordinary life and genuine opacity.** Care, humor, play, work, practical competence, attachment, pleasure, refusal, indecision and uneventful continuing activity must remain available; the dominated cannot be pre-programmed as symptoms of their oppression. A structurally powerful circumstance may never be known to the actor, and an actor may misrecognize or resist it. Actor knowledge cannot be populated from the world owner's omniscience.

**7. Non-deterministic development.** Repeated mediation can recruit, inhibit, reinforce, interrupt or transform historically formed patterns. If attractor/basin concepts or Absolute Horizon remain requirements at the competent modern source grade, they cannot be assigned as type labels at survey admission. They are downstream developmental histories and evolving commitments. Historical nine finite strata express an older terminal *relational capability requirement*; the adopted FF R2 minimal grammar forbids assuming that every current stage must store a universal nine-column schema. This is an explicit version-reconciliation question, not a reason to erase multi-level power.

The joint question is not whether SOROK needs *all possible social facts*. It is whether it receives **enough accurate, differentiated, revisitable material and possibility** for its selected causal mechanisms to work without inventing missing structure.

Mere intentions, internal orientation, a predicted outcome, the LLM's retrospective words or an unadmitted event must not update the objective world. ## IV. SDTF R4 changes the seam—without filling it by magic

At the protected source cut there are four substantial SDTF R4 implementation modules. I inspected their exact code definitions; **that is not equivalent to independently rerunning their tests, authenticating all original theoretical decisions or proving live V3 integration**.

**SDTF's evidence modes matter.** The witness-currentness interface distinguishes **K1 constitutive positional**, **K2 qualified possibility**, **K3 realized occurrence** and **K4 active/durative** claims, each evaluated with separate **positive/negative polarity, current/historical status and actual/projected-counterfactual modality**. Its typed owner adapters distinguish witness-currentness from asserted truth. Unknown, stale or revoked witnesses cannot be turned into a positive assertion or fabricated negative. For continuing facts K4, interval proof or a carefully closed continuing-state/termination history matters—not a timeless active flag.

**Its mechanism vocabulary is not a universal sociology theorem.** The R4 rule-authority binding names five distinct classes: **P1 material access/dependency; P2 directed authority/obligation/sanction; P3 organized activity/time; P4 institutional gate/treatment; P5 counterpart/channel/relation**. This vocabulary makes better questions possible: *what institution or owner could act on whom, by which rule, under which temporal condition, and at what consequence?* But a class label or binding alone does not issue a concrete causal mechanism, numeric coefficient or permission. Rule-owner, measurement, estimand, identification, causal-use and calibration authorities remain separately bound or explicitly not applicable; a constitution's CURRENT status cannot make an unissued mechanism rule CURRENT.

**D1' hosting is dependency/replay plumbing, not a legislator of causality.** The SDTF hosting adapter records exact existing fact/context coordinates, rule applications and determinants; when a determinant changes it can invalidate/re-drive affected lineage and retain old immutable revisions. It hosts supplied facts and current-rule admission evidence. It does **not** write a world, select what a causal rule should be, decide rule authority, or replace V558.

**The externalized world-response seam is intentionally asymmetric.** SDTF R4 accepts only correctly bound *already admitted* externalized occurrences and independently admitted objective world responses, then produces an invalidation/rederivation **request**, not the world event itself. It explicitly refuses private V3 state, generic simulation or interpretative striving as an objective response source; current response consequences in this adapter are non-durable. This is an existing engineering foundation for lawful world feedback, not proof of an integrated continuous life loop.

Consequently SDTF cannot be positioned either as a generalized transformer of attitudes into material power or as a shared hidden-person-state engine. It is crucial precisely because **what is position, what is possible, what occurred, what continues, whose rule applies, and when a changed determinant requires recomputation are different scientific operations**.

## V. The seam has at least three clocks and two epistemic bubbles

**World effective/as-of time** belongs to qualified objective facts, structural applicability, SDTF witness and historical world selection. **Actor event/developmental time** belongs to particular encounters, responses, durative practice/mediator change, later performed action and recalled history. **Publication/permission/currentness time** belongs to immutable source/semantic rule, released world/product use, invalidation and revocation. Collapsing them lets stale rules govern a changed present, a later child become a cause of an earlier parent, or a retrospective rendering rewrite the world.

The **world/analyst readset** can know admitted independent facts the actor does not. The **actor's own permitted readset** includes only eligible perceived, remembered or historically constituted information under its own rule; even physically co-present people do not necessarily perceive one another. The user's phenomenal/observer readset is a third, different surface. At no point may the most omniscient available store be dumped into the actor or viewer for convenience.

## VI. The dangerous counterfactuals we should insist on

1. **Same survey percentages, different actual property/control:** the resulting world and affordance set may diverge; the actor's personality is not selected by either percentage.
2. **Same physical world, different legitimate histories of mother and other:** available structural conditions remain; their situated choices, attention and later effects may diverge through SOROK-owned causal mechanisms.
3. **Two opposing pressures at once:** neither vanishes because the other is arithmetically larger; actual actor response may be contradictory, ordinary or absent.
4. **Same objective event, inaccessible to actor:** no actor knowledge or emotional update follows without a qualified encounter and causal rule.
5. **Same available structural repository, two generated positions:** applicable M4/M5 structural selections must differ when CAN-001/006 requires it; mere query-hash difference is no substitute.
6. **Same marginals, different true respondent-level triple joint:** N10E cannot return the same claimed joint possibility when F4 distinguishes the distributions.
7. **Actor privately decides to quit vs actually resigns:** the first can alter actor-private state under lawful SOROK rules; only an admitted outward resignation and qualified objective response can change work status/world determinants.
8. **Observed objective change with no durable psychogenesis:** the world may change without acquiring a new lifelong actor disposition; current response and Rule T/NO_CHANGE have separate owners.
9. **Past world and future fork:** an interaction changes post-contact contingent world/actor facts; it cannot retroactively rewrite the precontact parent, institutional facts or original source evidence.
10. **A revoked or stale source/rule:** a cached world or actor read may not manufacture a current positive claim or a false observed negative.

These are not comprehensive tests of humanity. They are examples of where a superficially plausible seam can be materially false.

Its value is measured by the lives it can lawfully make possible, and by the false inferences it refuses to make.
