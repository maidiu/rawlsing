# How societies form people and people remake societies

## What crosses the seam

A society can give a woman the right to speak at a meeting and train her, through years of experience, to understand exactly when speaking is pointless. It can also teach her what promises bind a community so thoroughly that she later uses those promises against the officials who taught them. The difference matters on the next occasion even if both women give identical answers about authority, faith and free speech. The architecture has to generate the difference, carry it forward in the person, and account for the distribution of answers at interview.

**I propose conditioning the distribution of relations in which each person can learn what their activity means and does.** These include relations to objects, people, public signs and institutions. The operative quantities include who can initiate something, who can make it effective, what difference an actor's own attempt can make, which consequences return to that actor, and which regularities or exceptions the actor has the means to distinguish. One institution may invite everyone to speak and repeatedly act only on a proprietor's proposals. A child may enjoy a song for years before knowing the words distinguish two communities. A woman may acquire a way of tending someone that she continues after the organization that taught it dissolves. Powers of action, semantic distinctions and attachments acquire different histories.

What enters SOROK is a *prospective distribution over these formative relations in shared historical time*, with constraints on their frequency, arrangement and carriers. Each realized relation supplies something particular for its participants to do, perceive, possibly acquire and later use. The unit that persists in the person is whatever SOROK actually forms. It can be a tacit operation, a distinction with a name, an expectation about one particular person, a temporally extended project, an inhibition or an unresolved collision. The input can change whether a distinction exists for her at all. A different lived relation can change what an already acquired word calls forth. Merely changing the topic of the next scene does neither.

This requires two separable capabilities in one system. The researcher must infer which distributions over uncommitted circumstances can produce the source-qualified observations when independent actors live through them. The world must then *actually yield* circumstances drawn under such a distribution, and SOROK must compute the person through them. An external inverse target is one possible research device. An individual adult needs no designated answer sheet. A policy fitted over comparable populations can condition an individual directly through the opportunities, regularities, absences and independent people that constitute her life. Save the source dependence of those draws outside her actor state. Preserve her ability to do something whose frequency or very description the source never measured.

The relevant directed dependencies are as follows. Subscripts denote event time. The second line can only consume particulars available to that actor.

```text
world(t), dated obligations, bodies, coactors' independent attempts → resolved occurrence(t)
person_i(t), project underway, access_i(occurrence), bodily change → person_i(t+1)
person_i(t+1), new situation as she apprehends it → her attempted act
acts, other people, stocks, authorities and enforcement → world(t+1), others' access
person_i(T), interview and language context, item sequence → situated response sequence

qualified source facts + independently admitted historical relations
  → inference over laws of uncommitted circumstances
  → sampled formative conditions → forward world/person process above
generated interviews + actual instrument/design → criticism of the generative law
```

A change to a PYAT release changes the researcher's selectable laws or their weights. A change of shift at a factory can change the life of a woman already employed there. Only the second can make her miss her child's meal today. This distinction prevents both retroactive biography and the opposite mistake of leaving statistics outside the computation that forms a person.

## Conditioning each parent without inventing a joint life

Let D describe a target *at a specified age and date*. It may include attained conditions such as occupation or having had children. Let P be the family of PYAT facts for that profile and the other positions available in its world. Each fact carries its own eligible unit, risk set, observation mask, ordered categories, weights, uncertainty and permission. Let S be the separately admitted objective relations and their effective periods. Define a candidate law g over **uncommitted opportunities and shared histories**. SOROK supplies the transition law L for perception, formation, action and world response where available. Q is an instrument-specific, separately criticized observation law. The researcher can estimate the resulting law K(g,L,Q) of (position attained, eligible, response configuration) by running whole coupled worlds. The denominator matters since a change in g can change who becomes a parent, gets a job, survives or is interviewed.

1. **Construct the empirical domain.** Start at the exact position/period and legal coadministration. Query the whole eligible population there. For sparse support, first retain and report the exact result. Consider coarsening one *source-meaningful* material or demographic distinction at a time. A candidate backoff retains geography, period, language or other categorical boundaries whose comparability requires independent evidence. Evaluate the pooled estimate on neighboring supported domains by held-out joint predictive loss and interval coverage. Keep the exact and pooled results with their different estimands and uncertainties. When admissible choices materially disagree, propagate both. If a target is outside supported scope, retain a scenario with transport unresolved. A language model has no say in who counts as close. Employment and parenthood at T condition proposals for prehistories retrospectively and never operate as causes at birth.
2. **Represent the unknowns where they occur.** For example, a report of taking part in decisions anchors possible recurring work relations. A schedule, a supervisor's effective discretion, a worker's suggestions, the material result and the worker's access to it are distinct unknowns. Generate rosters and calendars jointly under resource, time, language, care and jurisdiction constraints. Sample independent Others from their own positioned possibilities. Draw candidate public practices and privately initiated projects through their competent owners. Let independently performed acts determine encounters. The same recurring participation can be realized through formal delegation, kin brokerage, strategic compliance or a collective that changes its rules. A dated document might establish a practice was available. Its uptake and its meaning still require readers and actors.
3. **Infer a set of compatible laws.** For each independent source fact j and cell c, accumulate expected weighted numerator a_jc(g) and eligible denominator b_j(g) from forward runs. For a mixture w over whole candidate regimes, constrain Σw a_jc relative to Σw b_j by the source estimator and its warranted *joint* uncertainty. Include constraints on real structural relations at their own units. Account for reused respondents and correlated products. Incompatible masks remain separate likelihoods or moment restrictions. Preserve within-form higher-order joints. Where forms never overlap, keep the set of compatible couplings. Compare independently supported formative observations as well as outcomes. A good fit obtained by inventing extra interchangeable meanings is weak evidence. Report infeasibility, effective support, and bounds on consequential predictions across compatible choices of g, L and Q.
4. **Make a prospective draw for a particular life.** Choose a particular admissible branch law under declared evidence or a disclosed scenario policy. Draw a shared setting once, then addresses, time intervals, role vacancies, reachable relationships and formative particulars conditional on that setting and D. For a researcher sampling a possible adult tip y, use P(y | D) and an explicit distribution g(h | D,y,S) over possible *inputs*, including rare alternatives. The executable case below does this. In an adult reconstruction one may also simulate candidate pasts forward and bind an uncommitted branch conditional on its attained position. Audit proposal, joint inclusion, stopping and selection. Never redraw a person's contemporaries or later choices to fix her interview. Costs or poor coverage can make inverse reconstruction infeasible. A set of unresolved regimes is a scientific result. A playable root can realize one disclosed fictional regime without turning its draw weight into a historical estimate.
5. **Test what reached the individual.** With the profile and random inputs coupled where meaningful, change the admitted evidence and trace which *distributions of actor-accessible histories*, acquired organization and independent future acts change. Inspect draws where the individual does and does not change. Also compare the unconditioned, marginal-only and full source laws at ensemble level. The magnitude must be estimated, including uncertainty. A negligible formative effect despite excellent endpoint fit is evidence against the proposed coupling. A model can have strong formative effects and produce the wrong population.

There is a useful implementability constraint. For fixed public prefix b, let e range over lawful **opportunity** policies and let K_e be the joint law of subsequent accessible events and observations with independent actor transitions held fixed. Ex ante randomizing e can produce only mixtures of these laws. In a finite sequential version, choose e before the next actor or chance outcome z, average the attainable continuations with their actual probabilities p(z | b,e), then take the convex hull over allowed e. A continuation may depend only on information actually available to its scheduler. Enforce one policy for observationally indistinguishable prefixes. If an observation target is outside this set even allowing its uncertainty, routing alone cannot achieve it. Inspect missing material regimes, access, formation or instrument interpretation. Conditioning on favorable future actor draws would enlarge the set illicitly. The four-shift calculation below enumerates the opportunities exactly. Scaled up, bound decisions over dated intervals and retain only distinctions whose removal can change a subsequent read.

**A finite implementation of that inference.** Let j index qualified estimates and [l_j,u_j] be simultaneous uncertainty bounds when the design warrants them. For each candidate regime r cache whole-world counts A_jr and B_jr at the same unit, route and weight as j. Solve for w ≥ 0, Σw = 1, subject to l_j Σw B_jr ≤ Σw A_jr ≤ u_j Σw B_jr (sums run over r), positive eligible mass, and independently admitted structural restrictions. Alternatively use the authorized joint covariance to define the acceptance region. Report Monte Carlo error in A and B separately from sampling error. Minimize and maximize substantive predictions over feasible w. For example, bound the fraction of workers whose *accessible history* contains a witnessed disagreement with an enforceable resolution, then bound the fraction of those workers whose later choice changes. The first can be constrained even when the second spans its full range. For design weights on persons rather than worlds, draw a focal eligible person from regime r in proportion to w_r times its eligible mass, then draw within that regime. Once selected, all subsequent parties belong to the same committed world. Independent regime draws for every member of a household destroy exactly the dependence one is trying to model.

If an operational draw requires one w, use a declared scenario prior over feasible regimes. For a warranted base measure one implementable choice is the feasible distribution closest to it in relative entropy. Vary the base measure, candidate support and resolution and publish the resulting range. A fitted multiplier remains an *inference parameter*; it does not become a social force or a preference in an actor. If all feasible laws predict the same thing for interviews and different outcomes for actual decisions, the latter remains unidentified until independent evidence can discriminate. This is also how to keep the science productive with no further hidden source available. The simulator can explore those different futures honestly today.

I used NORC's public 2010 US GSS cross-section as an independent research reference. The exact archive SHA-256 is in the accompanying program. This is a new calculation on 2,044 records, with only aggregate outputs retained. It neither uses the dossier's four chosen respondents as a population nor claims production PYAT has ingested this release. The two sources of mechanical evidence in this PR are complementary. **An exact failure of ensemble fit as a criterion for how a person was formed.** Write Y for the four adult reports and X for the two retrospective reports. Draw a target y, sample x from the empirical P(X | y,D), then independently emit a fresh Y′ from the empirical P(Y | x,D). This deliberately thin inverse–forward model reproduces the *entire* empirical adult joint exactly since summing over y gives P(x), and summing over x gives P(Y′). Yet after conditioning on all four features, the probabilities of obtaining those features again are only 9.07% and 10.55%. Across all tips the mean total variation of the fresh answer law from its profile baseline is only 0.049 and 0.102. Repeating an assigned tip is **not** the goal. The calculation shows how readily an apparently rich fit can be obtained from a channel carrying little person-specific information and no learned meaning whatsoever.

Making the inverse model more detailed also incurred a cost. Five whole-variance-stratum folds, with the stated eight-pseudocount shrinkage, gave held-out joint log losses of 2.434 and 2.356 nats using only the adult-profile joint. Adding the two reported past conditions yielded 2.635 and 2.495. Smaller is better. This challenges this particular estimator and sample support. It supplies no warrant for concluding that parental employment or religious upbringing lack developmental consequences. Treating those retrospective categories as the formative substrate would give the architecture authority it has not earned. ### From the same source to the meaning of an invitation

A different query uses the four adult items together with PARTTEAM and WKDECIDE among people currently working full- or part-time. These items ask about teamwork and participation in decisions affecting the respondent. The risk set differs from the retrospective query. In the employed female profile 138 answered all six, including eight in the all-four adult cell. Their weighted proportions reporting both team work and frequent participation are 30.4% and 53.3%, respectively. In the employed male profile the corresponding values are 30.0% of the weighted domain (136 observations) and 23.3% of the all-four cell (ten observations). The two profiles are separate. These sparse subgroup estimates justify a sensitivity demonstration, not a claim about sex, religious practice or managerial authority. Before binary coarsening the four adult items alone had 90 and 91 distinct configurations in the retrospective domains. Inference at full resolution needs uncertainty and partial pooling.

Which observations can support this particular formative input? I compared three readers of those same employed populations against the simple profile rate. A work reader used employment status, self-employment versus working for someone, and reported ability to change work times. An expression reader used the four *original-scale* items and then the exact eleven-item combination adding religious preference, strength of affiliation, party identification, political self-description, views on a racist speaker and book, and subjective class. A third combined the two. Each reader used training strata only, backed off to its shorter prefix on missing or unmatched combinations, and applied the same eight-count shrinkage. There were **zero held-out matches at the exact eleven-item resolution** in either profile. Female-profile held-out binary log losses for profile, work, expression, and combined readers were 0.638, 0.643, 0.657 and 0.648. Male-profile losses were 0.617, 0.592, 0.635 and 0.602. These are exploratory point comparisons. At this sample size I would retain the rich reported joint as an observation constraint, carry the work reader as a candidate, and withhold historical frequency authority from the expression-to-work extrapolation. A meaningful synthesis needs a different relevance and support calculation for *each* formative relation. Requiring every survey dimension to determine every aspect of every life would discard what this comparison teaches.

What should *participation* do inside this system? The survey already gives a reason to refuse one easy answer. Of 38 female and 41 male respondents in the respective employed domains reporting teamwork and frequent participation, four in each domain also rated having much freedom to decide how they do their job as only weakly true or untrue (WKFREEDM codes 3–4). Group consultation and individual discretion have different objects. Neither report establishes who controlled a particular machine. A program that gives a worker control whenever she reports participation has lost a distinction people actually make. [NORC Quality of Worklife module](https://gss.norc.org/quality-of-worklife.html)

The executable translation gives a worker and a dispatcher one machine slot per shift and two destinations for its output. The worker has a pickup to meet. The dispatcher has a posted allocation and the power to retain it or accept revisions. When a comparison is possible the worker proposes a destination. Across four shifts the posted destination and the needed one sometimes agree and sometimes differ. The dispatcher's policy is fixed independently of the worker's source condition. The worker can see her own suggestions, the posted allocations and, if accessible, the results. She cannot inspect the dispatcher's policy. From what she can distinguish, the proposed receiving rule keeps the explanations consistent with her experience. They include following her proposal, following the roster and always using one destination. Agreement on every observed occasion need not resolve which applies. An imitation or coincidence learner credits agreement even where there was no opportunity to tell. A third process could track an unseen delivery signal that usually agrees with the worker, making even apparent recognition misleading.

A concrete string such as `recognized_plan` here abbreviates a narrow practical expectation about *this continuing relation and project*. The worker can use it before formulating a theory of authority. After an admitted formation event, the existing SOROK reader computes a different later approach to the same request. In the branch where trying something different repeatedly mattered, P4 issued an invitation to inspect together. Where the roster repeatedly prevailed, P4 issued a request to inspect privately. When the worker never saw the results, the incumbent used its default solitary check. The ambiguity branch and the inaccessible branch give the same default, although their input evidence differs. The alternative coincidence learner instead issued a joint invitation after observing only agreements. This last disagreement can be tested on a *new* conflict between a worker's proposed change and a posted allocation.

There is a further observational test inside the example. ### What a semantic change in this particular person consists of

The central operation is a change in the actor's practical relation to a situation. Consider the mother who keeps a register for a religious association distributing food. In her early life a caregiver may take her along on distributions because shift times and transport make this necessary. She hears names in one language and sees goods move. She can help carry a parcel long before she can read an eligibility entry. An elder may show her whom the list misses. In a different lawful history, she helps reconcile accounts and sees unrecorded gifts treated as theft. Another person may welcome the shared meal while avoiding registration altogether. The architecture must allow PYAT to change the relative support for those histories through work arrangements, material position, religious participation and the distribution of surrounding Others. The specific meeting, elder's actions, injury, joy or capacity to read comes from the lived branch. A recorded religious preference cannot issue a schedule, a doctrine or a semantic category.

The person's computational changes have distinct future readers:

| Developmental input actually accessible to this actor | Possible change in SOROK-owned organization | Operation that would reveal whether it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Repeatedly carrying a parcel on hearing a name; seeing who receives it; sometimes finding nobody home | A practical distinction between listing, delivering and reaching a person. It can remain nonlinguistic. | At a new address, does she seek the person, return the parcel, or mark the delivery complete? A changed route can defeat a spatial habit without defeating recipient recognition. |
| Learning the written entry from someone who uses it; encountering another worker for whom the same entry creates a debt | Links between sign, anticipated material consequence, beneficiary, audience and power. Different contexts may recruit different links. | Does a request for a name mean finding a beneficiary or exposing a debtor *to her*? Holding the visible form fixed while changing the authorized reader can alter which action she even considers. |
| Repeatedly seeing that registering people gets them fed, with competent workers and the association affirming the practice | An acquired commitment to feeding people, fidelity to the institution, a reputation-preserving routine, or several of these. Success alone cannot decide among them. | When faithful recordkeeping and feeding someone first come apart, what does she sustain, abandon, conceal or ask others to change? | The employer uses a record to penalize a recipient. At a receiving port, record an actor-accessible difference as a tuple of *occasion, particular object or beneficiary, own ongoing activity, possible means known to this actor, independent other's act, encountered consequence, channel, clock*. A proposed SOROK rule can change the **scope of a distinction**, the **means it recruits**, the **end it serves**, the **conditions that inhibit it**, or the **priority of independently acquired commitments**. Those are different edits with different readers. A new word can attach to a practiced distinction, or a familiar word can acquire a new practical use. A bodily habit can remain unchanged while an explicit avowal changes. Store contrary episodes and context, including learning that did not occur. An association is psychologically invisible as a lens when its distinctions organize ordinary perception and action without the person needing to represent their institutional origin. Making that origin available for reflection is another acquisition. Losing trust, objectifying the institution, turning a good against its carrier, and acquiring an ultimate ordering of goods are four possible developments with different conditions. Any can be absent.

This is where love, class, recognition and power can change the *same computation*. A recipient's well-being can become one of her ends. Her employer can control whether her next shift is paid. Her fellow distributor can recognize a gift as solidarity and her creditor can treat it as evidence of unreliability. Choosing to conceal a name can jeopardize a relationship she needs and cherishes. In one history these commitments occupy separate contexts and never collide. In another she must act under both on the same afternoon. The presence, recruitment and possible conflict of those commitments is an actor-history claim. A questionnaire cell cannot settle it. ## Material reproduction and succession

Implement social reproduction as the joint production of **an immediate result and the conditions under which someone else can act next time**. When a workshop delivers a repair, it can also teach a technique, use up an apprentice's afternoon, confer standing to supervise, exclude a rival, or leave nobody able to take over. Each consequence needs its own cause. The same distinction applies to raising a child, conducting a service, collecting rent, speaking a language, and organizing a strike. Some practices end with their participants. Others survive a change of personnel precisely because somebody keeps doing the work of transmission, often unpaid and unrecognized.

The unit of simulation is a dated undertaking with participants, an object of activity, competing claims on bodies and resources, and a history of who can take which part. The unit of psychological explanation is what a particular participant comes to **notice, attempt, sustain, relinquish, or make possible for someone else** across subsequent undertakings. They connect at performed events. The world records one allocation of material responsibility. Its participants can live through quite different events.

This answers a concrete architecture question. Represent the right to *use*, the ability to *perform*, the disposition to *undertake*, and the power to *admit another* as separately caused relations. The last can belong to a board, a landlord, a kin group, an employer, or nobody. A skilled woman can train every new worker while the proprietor alone decides who gets a bench. Her expertise and the proprietor's power remain quite distinct even if everyone describes her as “in charge.” In turn she might organize an association capable of taking the lease. That requires a feasible alternative, other people's actions, and an actual change of control. Renaming her psychological state cannot accomplish it.

**Design-changing counterexample.** Two workshops can have the same output, headcount, survey approval, and even the same census of which people have worked, taught and inducted members. Under a personal-license charter a single authorized worker must perform all three functions to create a successor. Under a divided charter an artisan, instructor and council may cooperate. In a third world people have the legal rights but will not expose apprentices to retaliation. The next generation of practitioners can differ across all three. Neither an individual personality vector nor a correct whole-respondent table selects the charter or the future. Conversely, a structural simulator that treats everybody who can participate as willing and able to do so also fails.

The following practice and succession operations supply the world side of the formative relation. The workshop experiment tests this part of the system. Its institution, learning assumptions and interview are fictional.

## 2. One transition for production, reproduction and change

A **practice specification** is a versioned rule for undertaking something in a setting. Store its object and possible beneficiaries, role requirements and alternatives, acts with their durations and material inputs/outputs, who controls admission and enforcement, how overt acts become publicly legible, and how a rule can be renewed, abandoned or contested. Ritual, play and companionship can have participation or another person's well-being as their object. They need no commodity output. Coercion needs the means to impose a consequence. Even an unjust institution depends on people or machinery still able to enact it.

A rule resembles a timed production with jointly bound roles. For each event it specifies which roles must be distinct, which one person may combine, which can substitute, and which can block the result. Distinguish a person *having* a right, *invoking* it, another person *recognizing* it and the party capable of *enforcing* it. Model a court ruling, an employer's retaliation, and a community's refusal to comply as different events. This preserves political conflict inside the world instead of hiding it in a single “permission” bit.

```text
advance(selected_world, actor_heads, clock, rule_versions, named_draws):
    mature bodies, service due obligations, apply dated shocks and continuations
    intersect place, time, rights, resources and role eligibility to form candidate occasions
    deliver perceptible openings or demands through their actual material or symbolic carriers
    each addressed or initiating actor recruits projects and proposes an act, refusal or alternative
    resolve competing claims under this jurisdiction's rule and actors' actual power
    commit performed acts, attempts, expenditures, unpaid costs, recipients and changed rights
    project each occurrence through each affected actor's particular access at that time
    apply qualified formation rules to those histories and recruit them only after the event
    enqueue due institutional renewals, embodied consequences and affected opportunities
    at an instrument or player encounter, use the continuing actors and the same event machinery
```

Negotiation occupies time. A counteroffer is a later action that can alter the other party's response. Concurrent proposals read the same starting cut until something is performed or publicly disclosed. Resolution may follow queue priority, bargaining, a lease, a vote or force, depending on that practice. No omniscient social welfare optimizer chooses everybody's acts. Material balances use `stock_after = stock_before + produced + received − consumed − transferred − loss` in the declared units, with a paired transfer entry, exclusive time interval for each occupied body/tool, and explicit arrears or expropriation. Food delivered, money paid, debt accrued, goods sold, bodily recovery, care received, and the distribution of surplus are different outputs. A refusal can incur a real loss. Waiting can allow an independent person to finish, leave, or intervene.

**Ideological mediation changes the act before resolution.** A shopkeeper can understand giving someone credit as a duty to a neighbor, a threat to kin subsistence, or a public obligation owed to members. Those distinctions are recruited by actual cues and an acquired organization of activity. Two readings can favor the same sale today. They separate when the buyer cannot reciprocate, belongs to another community, or is unseen by the shopkeeper's peers. A believer can support an institution because it housed a relative, oppose its exclusionary rule because of a conception of mercy learned there, and still depend on its credit. The decision policy must be able to preserve all three relations while only one loan is made. The loan changes stock, risk, household options and what a witness sees. The recipient can decline it.

**Knowledge, power and the production of truth.** An examination, debt register, census or clerical judgment is an action by someone competent in an institution. Implement `classify(procedure_version, admissible_observations, examiner, subject, time) → public_record`. The record has an issuer, criterion, jurisdiction, audience, validity interval and contest route. A different reader decides whether to honor it. The system keeps the underlying performance, what the examiner could observe, the official finding, what each actor learns of it, and the later deployment of the finding. A failed examination can exclude a genuinely competent person and eventually erode their skill by denying practice. Later statistics can then appear to confirm the original classification. Conversely a credential can create a competent practitioner by opening an initially unavailable apprenticeship. Test these paths by crossing the criterion used to issue records with actual access to subsequent practice, and by testing uncredentialed people on the same independent task. ## 4. How people can produce a different society

Carry forward three distinct kinds of effects. **Allocation** changes who has time, food, money, shelter, company or access today. **Reproduction** changes whether bodies, relationships, infrastructure and practices can operate tomorrow. **Transformation** changes the rules or distribution of control by which subsequent allocations are made. An exceptional favor can feed someone without redistributing authority. A strike can change a bargaining rule even if today's pay is unchanged. Repeated exceptions can also become an informal rule, but only through continued enactment, recognition and the capacity to sustain it.

A collective is represented by membership/representation relations, communication routes, material capacity, organizers and decision procedures. It can own a fund or a lease. It has no unified private consciousness. Make proposal, circulation, commitment, ratification, enforcement and implementation separable events. An employer can concede while a supplier refuses credit. A coalition can win a vote without gaining possession of the premises. A social movement can also build a parallel institution, speak a new name for an old harm, arrange meals, or stop supplying labor. Those acts change the world even if the incumbent never ratifies them. The mechanism for counterpower is the actual replacement, coordination or withdrawal of the work and resources on which another actor's power depends.

For a practice version r define its **reachable successors** as the people or coalitions who could reproduce its operation after specified carriers exit, subject to current means, competences, dependencies and assent. Compute this as a constrained role assignment. One role may require a single individual with several qualifications. Another may permit several people to divide that work. Remove a controller or an institution's continued recognition and recompute. This yields a counterfactual measure of dependence. Keep feasible replacements separate from replacements who independently choose to act. Count beneficiaries and those excluded as well as carriers. The disappearance of a coercive practice can be a legitimate outcome.

When an external effect is admitted, write a new world version with its effective time. Cross the boundary to SDTF only with its required independently bound occurrence and world-response evidence. Recompute affected *future* determinants and opportunities. For an actor unaware of a rule change, an old expectation can persist and produce a failed attempt. Someone who never meets the organizers may nevertheless benefit from a new bus route or suffer a price increase. A later survey samples this changed social landscape at a new historical date. Preserving the earlier observation is essential to learning what changed.

* **Birth and ordinary time.** The child begins hungry, sensing, moving, signaling and dependent. The depot's schedule changes which adult is present for repeated feeding; the child need never know what a depot is. The father's language is heard on some mornings, the neighbor's on others. Caregiver fatigue, sibling play, illness, affection and the child's own initiative change what is mutually possible. When the child cries at the shop, the mother may want to console, preserve the child's privacy and complete a paid order. Her history can make those aims compete. The father can take the child, decline, or be objectively unavailable. Maternal intention, paternal action, shop income and the child's accessible reassurance are four different results. A temporary inability to soothe has no automatic consequence of trauma or ideology.
* **Learning and conflict.** The child can acquire a practical distinction between whom to approach privately and whom to ask in a group long before they can explain why. Another child can learn the same linguistic formula while expecting a different response. Later, the register can become a consciously named object of conflict through literacy, testimony and public argument. A parent can protect a child while imposing the very silence that hides an injury. Joy in repairing an instrument, friendship and pleasure in performance can attract the child to a workshop independently of deprivation or discipline. These activities consume time and alter relations just as paid work does.
* **Collective return.** Years later the child, now an adult, may translate a petition, organize anonymous meal access, preserve the inherited register, or leave. If enough people actually fund and staff an alternative, people who never met this family gain a different means of eating. The incumbent committee may retaliate, adapt or dissolve. Stock, labor, public records, institutional authority, personal obligations and testimony update separately. The next cohort experiences another distribution of occasions. A survey can show the same level of religious observance before and after this transformation, even as the practice has changed meaning and control. It can also show a changed distribution if the new arrangements alter lived activity and its expression.
* **A visitor arrives.** Interactive time reads this same adult, the committee's records, the repair bench, available time, unpaid obligations and actor-specific memories. The player may help carry a meal, insist on recording a name, misread a refusal, or leave. The adult can insist on privacy while continuing a religious practice the player regards as oppressive. The player sees the refusal and its practical consequences; a permitted conversation might reveal the reason, or might make the misunderstanding worse. Other people's lives continue after the player leaves. No renderer supplies retrospective motives to make the exchange moving.

This example also explains the generality condition. A language persists through speakers, audience and occasions to use it; a border status through officials, documents and enforcement; a song through performers, listeners, embodied memory and instruments. They share a causal grammar while differing in material constraints, learning and powers of succession. Some relationships have no formal issuer. Some commitments are solitary and persist after a carrier disappears. An architectural rule that requires a guild examination for music, love or faith would fail this design.

## 5. The inverse problem and the survey as an event

Let W represent material and institutional history, Hᵢ the embodied and accessible history of person i, Zᵢ the acquired organization a candidate representation retains, and Q the instrument and administration. The historical generator factors each dated step through feasible occasions, actor attempts, world resolution, access and formation. Feedback enters the next step. The observation law is separately conditional on Q, interview setting, linguistic resources, eligibility, current position, what the person can retrieve, privately avow, publicly say or decline. A respondent has one continuing history across items. Do not sample answers independently; do allow order, ambiguity, privacy and strategic expression to matter. A form that never asked a question creates a routing outcome, not a substantive “no.”

Use two samples and two clocks. For the empirical sample, reconstruct the exact C11 query, denominator, joint, uncertainty and target. For simulated households, advance people forward and administer the same qualified instrument to eligible fictional respondents, with the same observation and selection design where known. Map the fictional interview events back through the same response topology in a separate synthetic release. For cell c at position d, compare the ratio of weighted eligible respondents emitting c to the weighted common-risk population. Design-based variance, clustering, rare support, translation, form and period require their own authority. An unsupported translation is a failed comparison. A sample zero need not be a population impossibility.

Let K(w) be the vector of expected eligible counts and joint event counts emitted by a whole generated world under fixed prospective actor and instrument laws. Compare mixtures of **whole worlds** using ratios of summed counts at the person-level target. Independently evidenced constraints on rights, case mix and resource totals restrict the world library. Infer sets of parameters or of compatible histories wherever the data cannot distinguish them. If no feasible library fits the measured joint and its warranted error, expose the discrepancy; change a proposed model after investigation. Do not solve it by splitting one observed family into statistically convenient independent people. Use held-out households, institutions, periods or source domains appropriate to the claim, held out **before** history fitting. Test independent actions, material balances and access separately from survey fit.

For adult initialization, run candidate pasts forward. Compare the emitted observations to the admissible source configurations. Record the proposal law or its opacity, whole candidate sets, stopping, selection, unmatched cells and unproposed support. Choose a *fictional* history before interaction. The frequencies of selected latent lives are generator-dependent. A best-of-ten preference for one of two equally proposed classes can select it 1023 times in 1024 without any new empirical evidence. Never search future random draws for ones that give the desired adult action. For children, keep developing them and their contemporaries prospectively. A change to the measured PYAT estimate affects future inference over worlds; an actual embargo or act of care enters the lived world's forward history.

The GSS and Nabatieh examples in the dossier constrain expressive adequacy at their reported scope. A devout person may reject an identity label. A participant may attend a campaign and report low political interest. Two demographically similar respondents can use the same phrase differently. Reproduce coherent mixtures, form eligibility and contrary cases before comparing distributions. The seven selected people do not estimate a population frequency or identify any one marriage, private faith, action toward a child, or latent class. For real respondents, even the map from a practice to an instrument response needs validation. The reference experiment uses a disclosed, unusually strong truthful-recall instrument; its assumptions are deliberately easier to break than a real interview's.

For a finite first learner, let each actor-induced cue pattern and candidate operation index counts of **accessible consequences at eligible opportunities**. An example provisional implementation estimates the next categorical consequence with a Dirichlet predictive distribution. Score a proposed perceptual split s against pooling by the sum over subsequent events of `log p_split(observed | previous) − log p_pool(observed | previous)`, less a description cost for s. Create or strengthen s only under the applicable rule; keep exact contrary episodes. A new cue can be formed by joining two previously distinguishable cues, by detecting a repeated temporal relation, or by learning that a public word marks a contrast in situated use. No word or contrast can enter via an inaccessible survey dictionary. A transfer rule binds a variable to a recurring role only after discriminating experience with changes of participant. A context-bound exception, missing support and a newly productive abstraction should have different future readers. Dirichlet smoothing belongs to this proposed actor predictor; it has no permission to smooth empirical source zeros or to impute a developmental rate.

For projects, generate candidate **ends** by projecting what changed for the actor, the activity and its beneficiary in previously sustained or resumed acts. A narrowly implementable learner retains alternative completion predicates S. For each later accessible choice where two ends predict different feasible actions, compare the consequences of preserving each end under a changed means, beneficiary, audience or cost. Retain the observationally equivalent set when action is constrained to one possibility. Recurrent voluntary resumption under changing means supplies evidence for making that end independently recruitable. Mere success or externally compelled repetition cannot do so by itself. A proposed *formation* mechanism rehearses a successfully sustained joint activity, recruits its completion condition when a related activity falters, and retains that new link only if future situated activity repeatedly uses it. Distinguish this forward hypothesis about acquisition from the researcher's backward inference about possible motives. Evidence might instead favor episodic imitation, compliance, bodily habit or an earlier unobserved commitment. This is a competition of laws, with no empirically issued utility or universal reinforcement rate.

Before choosing an action, recruit projects from bodily demands, unfinished undertakings, perceived claims, accessible relations and earlier activity. Expand candidate means through known substitutions and feasible requests to other people. Eliminate subjectively unthinkable or unavailable candidates *in the actor's view*, keeping uncertainty and perceived sanctions. Select among remaining projects with a versioned, context-dependent priority relation and identified tie rule. Learned inhibitions can suppress a once-valued act in public while it remains available in private. Cycles or unresolved comparisons may yield hesitation, seeking advice, refusal or the prior activity continuing. Use a declared fictional tie policy if evidence does not discriminate. Recheck the attempted act against *objective* permissions and actual coactors. This preserves material coercion, self-discipline, creativity, ambivalence and the possibility of misrecognition in a single transition.

On the public side, the same occurrence can reproduce, weaken or introduce a category. A clerk recording “uncredentialed” reproduces a category when other readers treat it as consequential. Workers can invent and circulate “community-trained,” organize an alternative assessment, and persuade a buyer to accept it. Implement those as symbol creation, transmission, collective enactment, procedural adoption and material acceptance with separate parents. A novel label alone cannot open a workshop. A new practice may open one before anybody has agreed on a name. This is also a route for bottom-up history to change a later instrument's categories and comparability.

## 7. What the evidence decides

**Production and formation can move in different directions.** In a Ghanaian field experiment, offering firms an apprentice increased firm size and profits. A related randomized trainer-incentive experiment found differences in later apprentice skills and earnings. These studies give reasons to measure the shop's production, trainers' incentives, participation, learner outcomes and later economic opportunity separately. The actual Ghanaian institutions and study designs govern those findings. The toy charters below are invented counterfactuals. [Hardy & McCasland, firm experiment](https://doi.org/10.1257/app.20200503) · [Brown et al., training incentives](https://doi.org/10.1257/aeri.20220696)

**Practical meaning can alter material activity even while the institution remains in place.** In a Saudi experiment with 500 married men, information about peers' reported support for women working outside the home increased selection of a costly job-matching service from 23.48% to 32.02%. The authors did not remeasure respondents' own opinion immediately after treatment. Both changed expectations of others and changed personal endorsement could explain part of the response. A separate recruitment experiment recorded women's actual attendance. The audience, action owner, stakes and outcome differed. These are reasons to implement circulation of information, separate people's decisions and measure costly action. They give no reason to assign a particular motive to a Lebanese respondent. [Bursztyn, González & Yanagizawa-Drott, figure 3, table 2 and recruitment experiment](https://home.uchicago.edu/bursztyn/Misperceived_Norms.pdf)

There is a quantitative constraint here. Taking the two reported arm rates as illustrative population moments, let h be the share who would sign up only when informed and d the share who would sign up only when uninformed. Then h − d = 0.0854. The marginals permit h from 0.0854 to 0.3202 and d from 0 to 0.2348. One compatible mechanism moves 8.54% in one direction. Another moves 32.02% in one direction and 23.48% in the other. Both produce exactly the same arm means. The extremes follow from the possible overlap of two binary potential outcomes; random assignment estimates their separate marginals, not their joint distribution in individuals. Sampling uncertainty widens the empirical question further. Even a real intervention with an observed behavior cannot issue the private historical law that formed the people who responded. The architectural consequence is to retain multiple compatible mechanisms and look for differences under a *new* intervention.

**Political authority, household work and imagined futures can change together.** In an Indian policy experiment, exposure to councils assigned female leadership for two electoral cycles changed adolescent aspirations and educational outcomes and narrowed the gender gap in time spent on chores. This challenges a design where public office can affect only direct recipients of an official's orders. It also challenges a model that inserts a universal empowerment trait on exposure. Changes in household allocation, visible precedents, parental expectations and access to education are competing or interacting routes. The study supports a total effect in its setting; Rawlsing needs additional evidence to select those internal pathways. [Beaman, Duflo, Pande & Topalova](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22245740/)

**Learning from accessible life is itself variable.** Correa-Chávez and Rogoff studied children aged 5–11 who could observe a demonstration directed to a sibling. Attention and learning differed across groups with different everyday traditions of participation. This challenges an access rule requiring direct address, as well as one that awards learning for mere physical presence. It does not qualify infant learning, a national learning style, or a universal skill-transfer probability. Paluck's Rwanda radio experiment also found changes in perceived norms and behavior with little change in personal beliefs. That is positive evidence for keeping a strong public-practice and situated-memory rival when considering an additional private ideological state. [Correa-Chávez & Rogoff](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19413421/) · [Paluck](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19254104/)

### What would falsify the architecture or simplify it

Prioritize **N**, the incremental explanatory value of an acquired relation, under a design capable of finding no increment. In a consenting apprenticeship or analogous local practice, identify a procedure people already use, who exercises each power, and which performances participants can witness. Enroll before an opportunity, including nonparticipants and refusals. Record prior encounters, production, private and public commitments, the issuer's decision, downstream access, and actual subsequent performance. Hold out entire sites or cohorts before fitting. Distinguish withdrawal of a person's personal support from loss of a public grant and from loss of the actual tools. Document who can see an intervention and allow interference within the group.

| Decision-changing contrast | Expected discrimination and failure condition |
|---|---|
| Change the underlying joint at fixed lower marginals; independently vary whether duties can be distributed | The field should matter when joint membership is needed, and may become irrelevant when cooperating actors can divide the roles. An invariant direct statistic-to-person effect across both regimes fails. |
| Vary a person's actual performance and the issuer's criterion independently in a voluntary simulation; independently test work under a masked assessment | Official standing and demonstrated ability can dissociate. If future access never depends on institutional uptake of the record, remove that proposed classificatory path for this setting. |
| Cross beneficiaries, institutional carriers and audiences. Compare the same individual's later costly choices when the historical good and its carrier come apart | Compare R0, R1 and R2 on held-out decisions, with available information and compute matched. Use log loss or Brier score for acts, report calibration and performance by eligible stratum, coverage of abstentions, paired site-cluster uncertainty and costs. If a history-only policy is equally predictive and affordable, prefer it. |
| Change what another person does while holding the focal person's direct view fixed; separately block every indirect path | A caregiver's changed resources can affect the child's day in the first condition. In the second there is no downstream change attributable to revised field prevalence. An inaccessible event producing private knowledge fails. |
| Replace all incumbents; independently remove reproduction labor, means, legitimacy or transmission | Distinguish continued practice, altered control, a surviving public name with changed operation, and dissolution. If a practice continues with no sustaining processes, the world implementation fails. |
| Vary history proposals and selectors at fixed evidence; challenge genealogy at the earliest shared-world effect | P remains open for latent frequencies; F remains open for independent paternal history and real institutional continuity. Equal survey fit across sharply different latent mixtures preserves nonidentification. A late identity that alters a committed earlier fact fails. |
| Change the interviewer's language, item applicability or privacy; keep the lived history fixed | Some expressions can change and some items become inapplicable. If changing the questionnaire changes the already committed actor, reject the model. |
| Let a player make an unwelcome request, leave and return; compare an interactive encounter with a matched account of the same life | Test whether the actor's independent projects and relationships continue. In a consented study measure unprompted prediction of the Other's conduct under changed circumstances, recognition of mistaken assumptions, later behavior toward real people, adverse effects and withdrawal. Attachment and engagement alone cannot decide the human thesis. |

For a human formation claim, use repeated observations before and after a credibly varied experience and a novel transfer task with a relevant comparison group. Randomize transparent, harmless simulation conditions where appropriate. For a historical natural experiment check assignment, prior trends where applicable, changing composition, exposure and alternative pathways. A short-run change of action under a newly accessible opportunity is evidence for situation dependence. Persistence after the opportunity disappears, accompanied by prospective differences in a new setting, tests acquisition. Even this may reflect changed networks, fatigue or resources; measure them and compare the alternatives. Choose a minimum meaningful difference and sample size from local base rates, clustering and attrition before collecting outcomes. No universal sample size or transition rate follows from the dossier.

## 8. Building the system at human and historical timescales

**Integration within a selected domain.** Extend the selected-world reader with role, controller, effective interval and evidence bindings; make P2 admit an actual multi-party act with a consumed resource and a separately issued public finding. Carry that event to distinct actor projections and the P3 rule registry. Have a later P2/P6 operation read the result and have the world admit or deny a consequential action involving another person. Administer a small, versioned instrument after this action and requery the synthetic population separately. Completion means that withdrawing any indispensable authority changes the corresponding outcome or yields a typed hold, that restored inputs reproduce semantic outcomes, and that none of the actors sees hidden calibration data. A single domain needs all these boundaries; richer domains can contribute additional rules without another generic engine.

**Keep the alpha on the same path.** The current P6 encounter is runnable. The research harness can retain its roots. To expose the proposed society to a player, replace the fixture visitor with voluntary player actions and have the new domain's actual P2 events drive the existing perceptions, actor rules and public P6 surface. A player can ask, witness, be refused, help, misunderstand or leave. A later meeting reads the same person, altered relations and obligations. Use generated prose only from actor-accessible evidence and grounded action options. Never let the renderer award an acceptance, change a budget, invent a memory, or diagnose the user. Label the world fictional and the developmental rules provisional. A matched voluntary user study is a separate evidentiary task and can run alongside scientific improvement.

**Allocate compute by causal consequence.** Index active roles by jurisdiction, interval, place and resource; intersect these candidate lists before attempting a joint assignment. The general role-binding problem includes constrained matching and can be combinatorial. Put a declared search budget on large coalitions and report unresolved feasibility on exhaustion. Do not scan a national population every time a child cries. Use an event queue for price changes, debts, work schedules, births, aging, institutional succession and project deadlines. Advance background people to their next material or relational dependency, preserving the same rights and ontology. Escalate resolution for a contested allocation, first language distinction, relocation, institutional rupture, new relation or a decision that changes somebody else's future. Keep an exact event for a first grant and its effects even when it would be cheap to summarize it.

Coarsen repeated intervals only against a named downstream question. Preserve time spent, stock and debts, participants and accessible exposure opportunities, ordering when it affects learning, capacities, unresolved projects, exclusions and earned traces. Two histories with identical hours of care can differ if all distress signals were answered by one particular person. A summary adequate for food balance might be inadequate for attachment or language. Compare coarse and fine forward runs against the next independent decisions; split the interval or mark it unknown where parity fails. P3's existing singular episode, representative episode, accumulated interval and retrospective crystallization envelopes can carry coverage evidence.
