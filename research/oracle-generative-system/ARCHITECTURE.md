# Constituting the parents and growing the child

**At the mother's first adult instant, two things must already be true of the same running person.** Administer the qualified instrument and she gives the particular configuration PYAT sampled for her. Leave the instrument out and she can still go to work, love someone who refuses her, keep a promise, misread what an institution will let her do, and acquire a reason to oppose it. Both paths read the same acquired organization. The father must meet the same condition under his own target and additional constraints from an actual common history. The focal child inherits a world transformed by those people and gets no adult answer target. These are three uses of one person-and-world transition.

Build **one computational person with two ways to enter a life**. For a parent, PYAT supplies a coherent fictional pseudo-respondent and the variation around its position. A construction LLM reads the whole, proposes a materially and psychologically intelligible adult, populates SOROK's operative relations, and back-narrates a credible becoming. A few compressed forward recursions reconcile it with history and independent people before play. For Nadia the developmental investment is reversed: sparse embodied beginnings, recurring model-assisted encounters and actual actor-owned acquisitions which progressively change how she experiences the next encounter. After entry, all three live through the same person/world machinery.

The previous version made conditioned search over parents' possible lives the main construction algorithm. That solved a harder historical reconstruction problem while leaving the requested model-assisted adult initialization unspecified. This revision puts the parent compiler, the growing child, and SOROK's differentiated model calls at the center. The source, public world, person formation, independent action, reference interview and intergenerational return still have distinct causal roles. ## 3. Construct the adult from the whole configuration

Give the construction model a fictional pseudo-respondent, the comparative distributions **around** them, a dated possible world and SOROK's writable opening-state vocabulary. Ask what sort of person could live all these particulars together. Let it propose the computational organization of that person directly. Then have it reason backward about how someone could have become organized this way and forward about what was possible over those years. Revise the construction while it is still private. Some relations may begin as stipulated adult dispositions; only a few consequential developments need enactment before the opening. This is a different product from discovering the actual formative causes of a respondent. The quality of the fictional mother lies in what her organization lets her perceive, care about, do and become with others.

Its proposed interface is `propose_opening(target, comparisons, dated_world, shared_anchors, permitted_operations) → opening, antecedents, prospective_events, unresolved`. The input includes the *whole* target. The output is a structured proposal to the existing adult owners. A field with fluent text but no eligible reader is still narrative material. Never put the four survey answer numbers into the person's ordinary memory to make the interview pass. Give the initialization its own provenance; after admission, use the ordinary owners and update laws for everything that happens to this person.

**What materialist back-psychoanalysis asks.** Who has to do the work here, who can live from the proceeds, whose need has become this person's concern, and who can deny the means? Where does she recognize herself, and whose recognition can she live without? What is immediately intelligible to her but hard to name, defend or give up? In whose language can a wrong be expressed and still count as fidelity? These prompts constrain attention, without supplying an ideology for the fictional person. One possible small proprietor extends credit as a religious obligation, fears losing the business that sustains relatives, and resents the patron who can honor the debts. She can welcome religious counsel over distribution, refuse a cleric's claim to a client's vote, believe herself apolitical and still attend a meeting for somebody. Another can make similar reports while emphasizing privacy, maintaining the social standing of the shop or sincerely valuing religious legislation. The first construct enlists a person to whom a debt is owed; the second may require none. These are fictional alternatives. They make no claim about C's private history. Love can make another's good an end in itself; power can give that same loving person coercive leverage. Both need to survive the call.

**Pre-retcon.** The next pass lays proposed antecedents along a calendar and runs a few forward intervals through the actual world: where could she live, who could have recognized her, which institutions existed, who held the resource, who else could occupy that role, which consequences would already be public? The model may revise the uncommitted opening or replace an anecdote. An owner refuses incompatible dates, prior deeds, access or resource claims. A material contradiction can force a different origin for a perfectly serviceable disposition. If no one needs an exact origin, retain the disposition as constructed and leave the past coarse. This is cheap because the system can author most of an adult at once. A simple implementation budgets one opening proposal and one joint antecedent/forward repair per parent, with escalation limited to a rejected or unreadable part. Those are engineering starting estimates, not a scientific call count.

**A concrete parent-preparation request.** For each proposed relation, return `{actor, operation_kind, object_or_beneficiary, recruitment_cues, anticipated_change, conflicting_relation_refs, access_to_self, scope, provenance}`. Backfill is atomic at the opening head: an interrupted call cannot leave a half-installed person, and an ordinary developmental call cannot reopen this inlet after events have been committed. Retain narrative for texture and review; the acting code uses the installed relations even when that narrative is unavailable. For a hypothetical commitment outside the current vocabulary, an LLM can suggest a new *instance* or composition; a new *operation* needs an implemented reader. Do not discard an unresolved contradiction to get a cleaner opening.

## 4. Constitute two independent parents in one chronology

1. **Construct the mother first.** Sample her Dₘ and yₘ, resolve her relevant comparison populations and pass them together to the opening proposal. Admit the fictive iceberg, reconcile its plausible antecedents with the actual historical and material opening, and carry out the bounded forward and reference reads. Keep the target outside her subsequent actor state. Her later choices can change what she would say.
2. **Advance the mother in compressed forward time.** Propose stacks of possible events from her state at proposal time. At the last proposed interval present feasible *conception trajectories*. At arrival, reread her changed state, anyone else's binding acts, world head and material possibilities. An invitation may have expired, a job or illness may have changed her movements, an existing child may need care, a relationship she desired may have ended. A selected trajectory can involve a first meeting years earlier, a marriage, other relationships, separations, earlier pregnancies or older siblings. Conception is an occurrence under bodily and world conditions. Desire for it, consent to a particular interaction, ability to prevent pregnancy and the occurrence itself are distinct. Generating a world conditional on the focal child's birth provides no estimate of how often such a world occurs.
3. **Construct the father conditionally after trajectory selection.** Distinguish what the selected intersection already requires (dated presence, a prior deed, a shared language when necessary, an existing sibling) from hypothesized affinities or conflicts. An encounter in a particular workplace can establish overlapping access. A history of contest over sending money to kin can require a real disagreement on that occasion. Neither implies equal occupations, sects, political labels or enduring personalities. Record why those two people could meet, what each could have wanted or refused, and what created the encounter independent of either wish. Where a reportable trait is relevant and supported, bind it at its source grain. Let his remaining D and his own y vary jointly. Sequential sampling is legitimate via P(yⱼ | D, previously drawn components, instrument/risk); choosing independent marginal answers would erase the very contradictions sought. Insufficient joint support leaves an explicit modeled completion or a partial empirical target. A mother's own attachments and experience can alter the opportunity for endogamy or exogamy, but her preference cannot make the partner reciprocate. Contact and pairing laws need distinct evidence or a declared fictional scenario. Do not require a quota of agreement or opposition. His separate back-projection and forward repair can produce meanings, loyalties or fears she never sees.
4. **Join events, never biographies.** Computational selection order can differ from event time. Maintain the objective acts of an already active counterpart under a stable identity and preserve what the mother could know of them. Reuse that actor if it is the father. Where a thin counterpart lawfully accounts for an effect, a proposed later internal history must be compatible with both effect and prior author, with any conjectural motive graded as such. If that act required an independently motivated choice absent from the thin owner, branch *before* the earliest affected act, run both candidates through it and all affected successors, and retain only a lawful continuation. Before publishing that prefix, recheck both persons' reference points, proposals and consequences in the same chronology. If an already committed common prefix lacks the necessary earlier person or cannot be reproduced, hold the join. An initial generation can keep intermediate genealogies private until this resolution. A player-visible past cannot be silently forked away. [Father and historical join](../../SEAM/24_SEVEN_CRACKS_CONCEPT_CODE_DEVELOPMENT_BANK_R1/03_BACKWARD_CONDITIONED_FATHER_CHRONOLOGICAL_WORLD_JOIN.md)
5. **Live the relation in both directions.** From the appropriate clocks keep at least the mother, father, her model of him, his model of her, the objective relationship, the household's material organization and the common world/event ledger distinct. Each has its own reader and predecessor. He can construe her silence as assent while she treats his repeated offers as unreliable. Whose childcare shift was actually performed is a third fact. Each may have lovers, dependents, co-workers and subsisting obligations outside the household. Earlier children get actual origins, ages, carers and causal effects. A breakup can change where siblings live without changing biological genealogy. Once the joint prehistory is committed, subsequent acts arise forward from the continuing persons. A later change of faith, resentment, absence or reconciliation need never preserve an old answer.

**The exact conditional distribution is usually unknown.** The father’s own answer target must respect the actual encounter constraints where those constrain reportable facts. Estimating how frequent such combinations are requires independently warranted pairing evidence or a declared scenario, and the male population denominator differs from the denominator of eligible fathers on that branch. Sorting candidates by how easy they are to complete is an additional selection mechanism. Report its selection probabilities when known or refrain from latent frequency claims. In a playable scenario one compatible family is enough to commit a possible history with its assumptions disclosed. It cannot establish a typical family. A single cross-section cannot determine where in this genealogy an interview should fall.

When estimating frequencies across families, keep the empirical law for targets separate from the unknown law of the LLM's fictional completions. A prolific model can offer thousands of variants without yielding a population distribution for motives. If a quantified forward law and sampling probabilities are independently available for a bounded question, use those to evaluate that question only.

A separate feasibility calculation exposes a failure specific to the father join. In a finite *birth-conditioned* scenario with warranted maternal-path masses and proposed paternal-type capacities, put an edge only where a candidate pair could have the required earlier events and can jointly pass both reference reads. To realize both marginals, every subset of path mass must fit within the capacity of the union of its eligible partners. This is the bipartite flow condition; a deficient cut quantifies the minimum unmatched probability. ### How a growing repertoire changes the next computation

**State at time t.** Maintain body and maturational capacities; executable sensorimotor operations; actor-specific distinctions, anticipations and traces; acquired sign/operation links; commitments to particular others and goods; and the actor's accessible models of people and institutions. These grow at different rates. Track relations with historical predecessors, cues and scope, activation conditions, incompatible recruitments, expiry/revision and the actual subsequent read. Parent initialization can directly fill some of these relations. The child begins with only those warranted for a dependent organism. The media used to fill an adult should not determine the schema for what a child can later become.

**One transition.** Advance the body independently of what the actor calls its condition. From the currently reachable world produce sensory particulars under actual posture, distance, modality and social access. Recruit existing distinctions and active projects to select what the actor notices among those particulars. Let existing motor or communicative means produce attempts, including exploratory movement and inaction. Other people act from their own heads. Resolve the objective outcome, including the failure of an attempt or continuation of pain. Deliver what the actor could experience of that difference. A provisional law can retain a scoped action–outcome expectation, differentiate a previously pooled cue, link an available sign to a practiced relation, reorganize a project, or yield no interpretable change. **The output of one acquisition becomes an input to later *perception and action selection*.** This is where a culturally acquired category can govern a person's paradigm. It can determine whether she even notices a choice or thinks a request is possible. It cannot produce unstocked food, reverse bereavement, compel love or make an absence perceptible as someone else's secret intention.

The cases produce two different 40-hour weeks, both readable as 40; the parent with 96 units of opening debt purchases a 40-unit instrument, while the parent with the identical work history and 210 units of debt declines.
