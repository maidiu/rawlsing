# Constituting the parents and growing the child

**At the mother's first adult instant, two things must already be true of the same running person.** Administer the qualified instrument and she gives the particular configuration PYAT sampled for her. Leave the instrument out and she can still go to work, love someone who refuses her, keep a promise, misread what an institution will let her do, and acquire a reason to oppose it. Both paths read the same acquired organization. The father must meet the same condition under his own target and additional constraints from an actual common history. The focal child inherits a world transformed by those people and gets no adult answer target. These are three uses of one person-and-world transition.

I would build SEAM as a **conditional history generator with a forward-only acting world**. Work backward from the *operations* a situated interview and an ongoing life require. Find historically admissible formative histories in which SOROK can acquire the capacities, attachments and meanings that make those operations possible. Execute the selected past forward in the owners that will run the person tomorrow. Match the entire sampled interview at the reference point. Preserve variation among matching histories precisely where it can matter to other people. After initialization detach the target from the run. What a parent later learns or refuses can undo the very commitments by which the system first recognized a match.

This changes the earlier #1146 architecture. Conditioning invitations, exposures and formative relations is necessary inside the forward model. Population fit through those relations cannot constitute a particular parent. ## 3. Solve backward for a parent who can go on living

Give SEAM three things in addition to the mandatory target. **G** is a candidate forward law over feasible, uncommitted histories using the shared transition above. It includes a qualified law of coactors, contexts and periods, and grades for conjectural choices. **Q** is an independently specified interview operation over an actual actor head under the reference instrument. **C** is the set of admissible continuing responsibilities and operations for this position, such as relating to an independent person, working or caring with competing claims, and acting after the interview. These keep the constructor accountable to a life far exceeding what the survey tests.

**Start with the work of answering.** An item about hours last week needs a reference interval, episodes of paid work across eligible jobs, the ability to distinguish paid work, recall and counting. A question about prayer and one about service attendance have distinct temporal frames, forms of participation and audiences. A frequent private practice may be possible during shifts that prevent communal attendance. It can be a relation to God, an obligation to kin, a source of comfort or several at once. None follows from the frequency code. A question about continuing to work after acquiring enough money requires imagining an altered material constraint while carrying forward actual projects, felt obligations, ties, ways of spending time and the person's interpretation of “work.” It can elicit a yes from someone devoted to a craft or from someone who cannot imagine a place among others outside her workplace. Their response to a real paid leave can diverge. An invitation to remove a book demands an understanding of the speaker, institution, proposed act and who is asking. These are distinct uses of ordinary retrieval, imagination, judgment, communication and refusal by one actor. ## 5. The child has no backward boundary

At an admitted birth under ADP-015, create a fresh actor through the sparse ADP-007/008/020 beginning. Bind genealogy, actual carers, date, bodily condition and concrete setting. Begin with a vulnerable, changing sensorimotor organism with needs, discomfort, movements and dependency. It has no adult religion, class consciousness, survey obligations, mature understanding of love or lexical self. A parent being able and willing to respond, actually hearing the cry, recognizing what it calls for and possessing the necessary food or time can all differ. The child's hunger can interrupt the mother's project, give the father a new occasion to offer help, and change what each learns about the other. Feeding, warmth, injury, comfort, sensory exploration, play and periods of apparently uneventful life require time and can carry developmental effects. Keep ordinary childhood running between conflicts and highlights. For example, a child can come to expect food from one caregiver on hearing a particular sound through repeated sequences of discomfort, approach, contact and relief. Recruitment of the person or sound in anticipation is a candidate developmental law with culturally and developmentally bounded evidence. Later a food or song can become desired because it brings the family together, even without the original bodily deficit. A need becoming the desire for *this food with this person* changes what the child seeks and whose action it recruits. An older sibling may make the same sound in play. The same sound can then have several acquired uses. Later, school or worship may supply a shared name, a prohibition or a different occasion for the same practice. An acquired identification can become an unnoticed way of perceiving until a friend is excluded. Alienation from an established practice may prompt avoidance, effort to regain recognition, or a more general project of inclusion. Recognition and solidarity remain historical possibilities. The state transition must permit an acquisition to fail, an expectation to persist after disconfirmation, or a loving act to be misread.

Advance this new person with ordinary G and independently acting contemporaries, at finer temporal resolution where bodily regulation, perception, practice or conversation requires it. Preserve both parents' continuing histories through their own lower-frequency updates and on-demand shared acts. The child receives only the effects that reach them. No loss on a future adult interview may select a childhood or replace a person already born. In research, interview independent eligible cohorts at the appropriate age and historical date to see what response patterns actually emerge. A divergent emerging joint can indict a scenario, contact law, developmental rule, observation model or assumed historical transport. Attribute discrepancies by independent observations appropriate to each mechanism. The eventual interactive Other has this same identity, debts, repertoire, private life and limited access. A player can fail to win their trust. P6 should project what an encounter makes available and admit the player's attempts through the same world. A human effect requires separate voluntary human evidence.

### Reproduce the two new results

With Python, pandas, NumPy, the original NORC archive linked above and a clean SOROK candidate checkout at `ee82397a979e52eab83f2bd4ea48ed9c28ea7ab9`, run the following from this research directory. The first command verifies the archive digest and reports weighted results. The second draws the whole target again, checks its reference operations against the available emission and exercises the incumbent parent. Neither command emits a respondent record.

```sh
python3 reference_target.py /path/to/2010_stata.zip
python3 parent_week.py /path/to/pinned/sorok /path/to/2010_stata.zip
```

The cases produce two different 40-hour weeks, both readable as 40; the parent with 96 units of opening debt purchases a 40-unit instrument, while the parent with the identical work history and 210 units of debt declines. Changing the target to 32 while keeping opening debt at 96 also produces a decline. The sampled interview and the surrounding material condition have distinguishable consequences. I set the lower opening debt four units below the post-purchase balance for the 40-hour case so that the additional eight hours could be decisive. This tests the existence of a causal path. It supplies no empirical estimate of the frequency of such decisions, how much debt a historical woman actually had, or whether she loved music. The target bound and the refusal can coexist with either answer to that latter question.

**Implementation judgment.** Build the conditional history constructor and independent survey reader over the existing person/world owners. The hour case establishes one complete causal circuit and the venue result rules out a plausible economy in representing the rest. A whole survey-correspondent independent parent, a joined pair of them, and open development from their child's birth have not been demonstrated. The scientific dependency is specific: shared, historically grounded operations by which what happened to a person can become what she recalls, takes something to mean, values enough to act for, or declines to disclose. Those operations must survive into her life with others. Without them exact answer matching can be bought with almost any biography. With them the same conditional construction can constrain a particular parent tightly at one instant and leave the next generation genuinely open.
