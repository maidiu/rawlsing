# Constituting the parents and growing the child

**At the mother's first adult instant, two things must already be true of the same running person.** Administer the qualified instrument and she gives the particular configuration PYAT sampled for her. Leave the instrument out and she can still go to work, love someone who refuses her, keep a promise, misread what an institution will let her do, and acquire a reason to oppose it. Both paths read the same acquired organization. The father must meet the same condition under his own target and additional constraints from an actual common history. The focal child inherits a world transformed by those people and gets no adult answer target. These are three uses of one person-and-world transition.

Build **one computational person with two ways to enter a life**. For a parent, PYAT supplies a coherent fictional pseudo-respondent and the variation around its position. A construction LLM reads the whole, proposes a materially and psychologically intelligible adult, populates SOROK's operative relations, and back-narrates a credible becoming. A few compressed forward recursions reconcile it with history and independent people before play. For Nadia the developmental investment is reversed: sparse embodied beginnings, recurring model-assisted encounters and actual actor-owned acquisitions which progressively change how she experiences the next encounter. After entry, all three live through the same person/world machinery.

## How a shared power becomes available to a person

**The person who made a power possible can remain its historical source long after they cease to be necessary to its exercise. They can also retain the power to punish its exercise.** These dependencies can change at different times. A mother starts a song slowly enough for Nadia to join. Later Nadia can find the entrance herself. Still later she can bring another singer in. Her mother can remain the person whose pleasure she most wants, the person who can bar her from the hall, or someone she loves whose judgment of this particular song she rejects. Erasing the mother's formative contribution to represent independence falsifies the history. Requiring the mother's presence forever to retrieve the practice forecloses a possible future.

The proposed developmental operation is **differentiation within a shared activity**. What began as one practically indivisible experience can become several things the person can enact, seek, anticipate, fear or judge separately. Whether any such differentiation occurs is a scientific question about that history. This puts SEAM earlier than the interpretation of a present situation. It must help constitute what the person has means to notice and what can become worth doing in the first place. The Governing Spirit's question about where vitality goes under pressure applies as much to the desire to hear the rest of a song as to the fear of losing a home. Its design tie-break favors the least new machinery that preserves both possibilities. ### What can change in an encounter

Imagine that the mother pauses in a funny place in a familiar song. The child makes a sound, the mother answers it, and they laugh. On another occasion the mother holds the note too briefly and the child cannot enter. Sometimes the child starts the game and the mother is absorbed in other work. Sometimes a sibling joins with a sound neither had intended. The mother's timing can make a joint achievement possible before the child has acquired any independent musical operation. Her attention can disclose something that had no prior standing as an object for the child: *the gap into which someone else's sound can go*. Taking pleasure together can change which event the child tries to bring about next. An account of that change has to track what Nadia herself does with an opening and what she can hear or feel of its consequences. Her mother's delight is an act with effects of its own, including ways it changes the mother's willingness to make time again.

For implementation, propose separately qualified changes over the existing actor state. A scoped sensorimotor practice could make an entry possible with less external timing support. An expectation could lead Nadia to wait for a particular person's answer. A newly acquired striving could lead her to *make room for an answer* by someone who has never sung with her. She might equally come to seek the sound itself, the joke, or another hour alone with her mother. These alternatives make different demands on what gets acquired. Give a child every musical operation and she may never initiate the song. Give her a reason to seek it and she can still lack the timing or the breath. At infancy, feeding, settling, carrying, sleep, pain and changes in posture have bodily effects even in the absence of any represented project. Background coverage has to preserve whose adjustments made what possible, who experienced them and over what intervals. A summary of ten “successes” has already discarded the distinction between Nadia finding the entrance and her mother doing all the timing. [SEAM/22 on embodied infancy and ordinary life](../../SEAM/22_MATERIAL_INFANT_PRIMITIVES_CAREGIVING_BACKGROUND_LIFE_AND_DISTRIBUTIONAL_BREADTH_R1.md)

**One way to locate an emerging end is to ask what difference the actor tries to restore when a familiar activity stops working.** When an adult substitutes praise for a missing partner's reply, does Nadia vary the rhythm until the partner can join? When the partner sings but the adult looks away, does she turn to recover the adult's attention? Does she transform the game so that they can laugh together again? Each is an intelligible end. An attempt to restore any one of them remains ambiguous in isolation. In the generator the stronger claim requires a previously unavailable candidate for action, a history-bound reason for its recruitment, and an independent resolution that may disappoint it. An outside observer additionally needs evidence against prior interest, cue imitation, chance, maturation, compliance and simple opportunity. A silent or exhausted child may possess the power. That observation alone cannot adjudicate its formation.

There is a specific identification limit. If every audible reply has always coincided with approval, then a history of continuing after both and stopping in the absence of both offers no contrast between seeking the reply and seeking approval. More repetitions of that arrangement preserve the ambiguity. A social order can *produce* precisely this lack of contrast by monopolizing the space, time or permission for an activity. A child who correctly anticipates that nothing happens here without Father's word can grow remarkably competent within that order while coming to experience his authority as a property of the man himself. Other people's continuing labor of hearing, complying, provisioning and recognizing his position makes the appearance come true again tomorrow. This is an architectural consequence of Marx's account of appearances generated through social practice. It does not require a defect in the child's inference machinery. [Marx, *Capital*, vol. I, chapter 1, §4](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch01.htm#S4)

The history that confers goodness on a mediator can contain real goods. A father's attentive care can both relieve distress and teach his daughter to notice distress in others. If he later humiliates a younger child, that same acquired sensitivity can make the humiliation visible. She can defend him despite what she noticed because a public accusation would cost someone their livelihood, because she wants to preserve his authority, or because his approval still organizes what feels bearable. She might oppose him and still control when others can speak. She might use his own patient way of listening with the younger child, refuse to obey him in this matter and continue to love him. Distinguish these possibilities by the specific encounters she had access to, whose perspective she can now use, what she attributes to whom, what costs are real, whom she protects when the costs shift, and what her subsequent acts do to others. Her silence alone identifies none of this. Her clear recognition of exploitation can also help her administer it more effectively if she benefits.

**Objectification has two senses that matter here.** People confer force on an office by repeatedly acting on its words, issuing pay, allocating rooms and recognizing credentials. That social accomplishment can confront the next generation as an objective power. Separately, Nadia can come to experience *this office's way of deciding* as one possible practice that could itself be judged and altered. A discrepant experience might make the official's role newly visible. Another participant may supply language or a practical alternative. Only action by people who hold or can change the relevant means alters the arrangement. A parish can provide refuge, music and dignity and exclude a family from its hall. A demand for mercy learned in the parish can expose that exclusion more sharply for someone who loves it. Rent, access to paid time, migration restrictions, language, standing in the congregation and the willingness of particular people to share the risk determine whether she can secure another room. A successful collective booking can change which children ever encounter a credible alternative. A transfer of keys and hours has different evidence from a change of mind. ### Where this changes the engineering

Origin identifies the actual antecedents or the parent's bounded fictional initialization. Present exercise refers to current bodily capacities, acquired ways of acting and genuinely needed contributions. An anticipated consequence belongs to a particular actor and may be wrong. Authority over use belongs to people and institutions who can enable, sanction or appropriate the act. None requires four universal fields on every child. The design consequence is that the lookup key for past participation cannot, by itself, decide where its effects may ever operate.

**How a new end could first enter candidate generation.** Waiting for evidence that Nadia already seeks the effect would be circular. One candidate law starts from an unsolicited, bodily possible variation during play. Her sound happens to draw another singer in. Nadia can hear the reply, stay engaged and vary her timing. The infant version needs only an executable movement, perceptual discrimination and ongoing bodily regulation. There is no requirement for a proposition about another mind. Whether a particular trace can reinstate this activity at all is a provisional rule, and unavailable evidence must leave its effect unresolved.

Only subsequently could repeated recruitment reorganize the activity around bringing about a consequence the child previously just encountered. For an implementer, the proposed distinction is between selecting a means **given an already active project** and letting an accessible or anticipated consequence **nominate a project that was absent from the current choice set**. ### Rival designs and the smallest discriminating encounter

Start with **retrieval from experience**. Use eligible ADP-016 episodes or traces, existing practices and current activation to propose what to try now. This accounts economically for transfer when an accessible similarity recruits a prior way of acting. Retain the exact supporting sources. If it predicts prospective conduct, including mistakes, as well as a durable abstraction at comparable cost, there is no case for an additional persistent object. A rival uses a scoped CAN-011 practice with a newly differentiated applicability condition, such as sensing *when another participant can still enter*. It predicts selective transfer across surface changes and can operate without explicit episodic recollection. Dormant nondeclarative traces can do that too. Withholding declarative recall alone cannot distinguish them. A third rival keeps capability constant and changes what is worth undertaking through CAN-010. It predicts a difference in spontaneous initiation or repair even where prompted performance is equal. The accounts can combine. A working physical skill, imagining a new possibility, fearing disapproval and making an ultimate commitment demand different evidence for persistence.

Three empirical results put pressure on the choice. Gweon and Schulz found that 16-month-olds' responses to failed toy activation differed with evidence about the agent and the object. This supports separating access to causal contrasts from the external cause of failure. Butler and Walton found that cues of collaboration could increase preschoolers' persistence and liking even without actual joint work. That makes current social framing a serious rival to durable motive formation. Bonawitz and colleagues found that pedagogical demonstration can focus preschoolers on the demonstrated function while narrowing further exploration. Successful teaching can generate a selective horizon through perfectly informative conduct. None of these studies establishes a law for love, mystification, lasting vocation or religious judgment. [Gweon and Schulz](https://sll.stanford.edu/docs/2011_Gweon_Schulz_Science.pdf) · [Butler and Walton](https://bingschool.stanford.edu/sites/bingschool/files/butlerwalton2013_0.pdf) · [Bonawitz et al.](https://cocolab.stanford.edu/papers/BonawitzEtAl2011-Cognition.pdf)

**Proposed test, still unrun.** Use a short, actor-accessible history of turn-taking in a shared sound game. Give the caregiver an independent activity and schedule. The caregiver can wait, join, interrupt, be absent or support a different activity. Retain positive shared delight when it occurs. The child may discover the pause, attempt an entry or remain occupied elsewhere. Measure assistance and timing from the performed acts. In a matched history the child hears the same sequence of sounds and praise, but the next sound follows an independent timetable. Preserve opportunities for the child's timing to vary, otherwise the two histories supply indistinguishable evidence. Treat developmental age, already usable language and prior ability to vary timing as eligibility conditions. Avoid transporting this design to preverbal infancy without a different perception and response law.

At a later gathering let a new participant actually try to join. Cross two independently enacted conditions: whether the participant can respond to Nadia's change of timing and whether the original caregiver's approval accompanies it. Compare also a protected opportunity to act privately with a costly public one. A missed entrance creates a choice of repair. Does Nadia alter the timing, call her mother's attention back, try something else, ask the newcomer what they want, or leave? Record what she could hear, where she oriented, the candidate source and its readset, the attempted act, actual partner response, and effect on each person's subsequent opportunity. Keep the partner free to decline. A third occasion outside music can test whether she notices a pause in deliberation that a quieter person could use. Musical competence alone supplies no warrant for moral or political transfer. Holding the floor open can empower the newcomer, stage apparent inclusion, or expose them to harm. What happens must depend on their independent response and on who can still change the decision.

The smallest causal comparison uses the *same later world prefix* twice. First let the ordinary consumer use its eligible acquisition. Then withhold only that recruitment, preserving the performed history, physically available cues, earlier sensory access, other actors' starting states and decision randomness. Allow the worlds to diverge after the first changed act. Log where the first difference occurs. For the retrieval rival, separately withhold the earlier trace read while retaining any independently admitted practice. For the motive rival, compare free initiation with the ability to perform upon request. Comparing models requires the same evidence and opportunity before the decision. If consent, familiarity, predictability or audience still covary, keep the result unidentified. If every candidate can emulate the others on those contrasts, choose the cheaper read and retain scientific uncertainty.

A positive recursive result needs an actual change in Nadia's eligible or attempted activity, receipt by a separately acting participant, and an effective world consequence consumed later. ### What the good can come to demand

The hardest case for the design is fidelity. Nadia may learn justice from her father in encounters in which his care for another person is real. Much later, preserving the effect she learned to care about can require *refusing the person who disclosed it*. This is possible if her subsequent activity can recruit the younger child's distress, a practiced way of intervening and a commitment to the other child's good even while her attachment to her father remains active. The particular contradiction is which fidelity can be maintained at whose expense. Fear can inhibit the act without erasing the distinction. Love can make her more attentive to the betrayal. A father can also respond to the intervention and change the terms of their relationship. No law here makes refusal virtuous or a parent dispensable.

Absolute Horizon enters when conflicts disclose **which claim can judge another and whose loss can be borne or imposed**. Preserve the actual alternatives the person could entertain, whose future remained imaginable, actual beneficiaries and cost-bearers, and which competing claims survived the choice. The ordering may be incomplete, context-bound, unarticulated or changing. In the project's theological reading, Jesus models kenotic fidelity capable of relinquishing kinship's final claim while preserving love. Luke 14:26 has force because of what kinship first made possible. Adoring a prototype can itself replace participation in the fidelity the prototype discloses. In suicidal collapse the accessible future may have contracted painfully, a possibility with heterogeneous personal and clinical histories. A willingness to bear loss in hope of another's future has a different causal organization. In the Epstein/Moloch limit, lucid understanding, insulation and command over means permit someone to pass costs onto others and preserve their own projects. Moloch here names organized social sacrifice. No individual's hidden motives follow from wealth or an outward act. These theological, historical and limiting interpretations constrain what a generator must leave possible. They supply no psychological types, diagnoses, scores or prescribed trajectories. ## What holds the system together

**Follow what a person comes to care about far enough to find whose activity makes it possible.** Then let that other person act. SOROK already takes historically unfolding, object-directed activity as its unit. The engineering addition I propose is to preserve the particular dependency within that activity across world resolution, person formation and observation. A worker can replace a lost wage and still lose the apprentices she teaches. Another can leave that very job and keep faith with the very same apprentices because they secured a room and arranged to come back. “She values teaching” describes both women. It cannot tell the engine what either has lost, whom she will seek out or what she will do with a free hour.

There are three different facts here. Someone controls the room and can revoke its use. The worker has acquired an expectation about whether these people will turn up elsewhere. The association recognizes some people as authorized to call a rehearsal. These facts can come apart. If the association changes its recognition rule tonight, the door can still be locked tomorrow. If the worker learns that somebody has a key, she may think to approach them for the first time. If a pupil persuades the keyholder to open the room, the pupil changes the world in which the worker makes her next decision. No global “access” flag can do all three jobs. An actor can act on a mistaken relation, suffer a real consequence and still retain the mistake.

### The operation shared by the six parts of the commission

At a dated encounter the **world owner** answers which concrete means, rights and contributions could bring about a particular effect. Give the construction model a fictional pseudo-respondent, the comparative distributions **around** them, a dated possible world and SOROK's writable opening-state vocabulary. Ask what sort of person could live all these particulars together. Let it propose the computational organization of that person directly. Then have it reason backward about how someone could have become organized this way and forward about what was possible over those years. Revise the construction while it is still private. Some relations may begin as stipulated adult dispositions; only a few consequential developments need enactment before the opening. This is a different product from discovering the actual formative causes of a respondent. The quality of the fictional mother lies in what her organization lets her perceive, care about, do and become with others.

### The model writes an opening, in a vocabulary with consequences

The new receiving operation is **parent-only initialization against an empty, uncommitted actor head**. Its proposed interface is `propose_opening(target, comparisons, dated_world, shared_anchors, permitted_operations) → opening, antecedents, prospective_events, unresolved`. The input includes the *whole* target. The output is a structured proposal to the existing adult owners. Whoever handles the interview or later plays the person sees the installed state through their own access rules.

A construction call can propose an otherwise unrepresented distinction in local terms. To admit it, bind it to concrete referents, a discernible contrast, eligible contexts, a prospective computation that would consume it, and its provenance. A field with fluent text but no eligible reader is still narrative material. Never put the four survey answer numbers into the person's ordinary memory to make the interview pass. Conversely, requiring every installed expectation to exhibit an independently simulated childhood receipt would defeat direct parent construction. Give the initialization its own provenance; after admission, use the ordinary owners and update laws for everything that happens to this person.

**What materialist back-psychoanalysis asks.** Who has to do the work here, who can live from the proceeds, whose need has become this person's concern, and who can deny the means? Where does she recognize herself, and whose recognition can she live without? What is immediately intelligible to her but hard to name, defend or give up? In whose language can a wrong be expressed and still count as fidelity? These prompts constrain attention, without supplying an ideology for the fictional person. One possible small proprietor extends credit as a religious obligation, fears losing the business that sustains relatives, and resents the patron who can honor the debts. She can welcome religious counsel over distribution, refuse a cleric's claim to a client's vote, believe herself apolitical and still attend a meeting for somebody. Another can make similar reports while emphasizing privacy, maintaining the social standing of the shop or sincerely valuing religious legislation. The first construct enlists a person to whom a debt is owed; the second may require none. These are fictional alternatives. They make no claim about C's private history. Love can make another's good an end in itself; power can give that same loving person coercive leverage. Both need to survive the call.

**Pre-retcon.** The next pass lays proposed antecedents along a calendar and runs a few forward intervals through the actual world: where could she live, who could have recognized her, which institutions existed, who held the resource, who else could occupy that role, which consequences would already be public? The model may revise the uncommitted opening or replace an anecdote. An owner refuses incompatible dates, prior deeds, access or resource claims. A material contradiction can force a different origin for a perfectly serviceable disposition. If no one needs an exact origin, retain the disposition as constructed and leave the past coarse. This is cheap because the system can author most of an adult at once. A simple implementation budgets one opening proposal and one joint antecedent/forward repair per parent, with escalation limited to a rejected or unreadable part. Those are engineering starting estimates, not a scientific call count.

**A concrete parent-preparation request.** For each proposed relation, return `{actor, operation_kind, object_or_beneficiary, recruitment_cues, anticipated_change, conflicting_relation_refs, access_to_self, scope, provenance}`. For a proposed formative episode return `{earliest, latest, participants_or_unfilled_roles, required_means, required_authority, private_or_public_consequences, relation_refs_it_would_explain}`. The opening reader resolves references, checks temporal and material compatibility, accepts only supported operation kinds, binds scenario-only assumptions to the fictional root and reports which proposed relations have no reader. Backfill is atomic at the opening head: an interrupted call cannot leave a half-installed person, and an ordinary developmental call cannot reopen this inlet after events have been committed. Retain narrative for texture and review; the acting code uses the installed relations even when that narrative is unavailable. For a hypothetical commitment outside the current vocabulary, an LLM can suggest a new *instance* or composition; a new *operation* needs an implemented reader. Do not discard an unresolved contradiction to get a cleaner opening.

**Reference correspondence.** Use an isolated copy of the same adult to take the actual instrument, with source language, wording, order, time, audience and nonresponse. The interpreter may use an LLM to construe a sentence or render an answer from accessible grounds. Neither this call nor the acting adult sees the construction target, the comparative table or the origin-story author's explanation. Preserve first whole-read results, discrepancies and missing operations. If local repair is needed, change the meaning-bearing state, an interpretation or a proposed antecedent at the proper owner; reassess the whole configuration and affected nonsurvey functions. Repairs that amount to adding an exception keyed to an item reveal a missing human operation. A lucky stochastic answer is insufficient; define the reference expression at the declared context or return unresolved correspondence. No lifelong stability across contexts is implied.

The former backward continuation calculation has a narrower use. It supplies no reason to search whole childhoods for ordinary parent initialization. ## 4. Constitute two independent parents in one chronology

1. **Construct the mother first.** Sample her Dₘ and yₘ, resolve her relevant comparison populations and pass them together to the opening proposal. Admit the fictive iceberg, reconcile its plausible antecedents with the actual historical and material opening, and carry out the bounded forward and reference reads. Keep the target outside her subsequent actor state. Her later choices can change what she would say.
2. **Advance the mother in compressed forward time.** Propose stacks of possible events from her state at proposal time. At the last proposed interval present feasible *conception trajectories*. At arrival, reread her changed state, anyone else's binding acts, world head and material possibilities. An invitation may have expired, a job or illness may have changed her movements, an existing child may need care, a relationship she desired may have ended. A selected trajectory can involve a first meeting years earlier, a marriage, other relationships, separations, earlier pregnancies or older siblings. Conception is an occurrence under bodily and world conditions. Desire for it, consent to a particular interaction, ability to prevent pregnancy and the occurrence itself are distinct. Generating a world conditional on the focal child's birth provides no estimate of how often such a world occurs.
3. **Construct the father conditionally after trajectory selection.** Distinguish what the selected intersection already requires (dated presence, a prior deed, a shared language when necessary, an existing sibling) from hypothesized affinities or conflicts. An encounter in a particular workplace can establish overlapping access. A history of contest over sending money to kin can require a real disagreement on that occasion. Neither implies equal occupations, sects, political labels or enduring personalities. Record why those two people could meet, what each could have wanted or refused, and what created the encounter independent of either wish. Where a reportable trait is relevant and supported, bind it at its source grain. Let his remaining D and his own y vary jointly. Sequential sampling is legitimate via P(yⱼ | D, previously drawn components, instrument/risk); choosing independent marginal answers would erase the very contradictions sought. Insufficient joint support leaves an explicit modeled completion or a partial empirical target. A mother's own attachments and experience can alter the opportunity for endogamy or exogamy, but her preference cannot make the partner reciprocate. Contact and pairing laws need distinct evidence or a declared fictional scenario. Do not require a quota of agreement or opposition. His separate back-projection and forward repair can produce meanings, loyalties or fears she never sees.
4. **Join events, never biographies.** Computational selection order can differ from event time. Maintain the objective acts of an already active counterpart under a stable identity and preserve what the mother could know of them. Reuse that actor if it is the father. Where a thin counterpart lawfully accounts for an effect, a proposed later internal history must be compatible with both effect and prior author, with any conjectural motive graded as such. If that act required an independently motivated choice absent from the thin owner, branch *before* the earliest affected act, run both candidates through it and all affected successors, and retain only a lawful continuation. Before publishing that prefix, recheck both persons' reference points, proposals and consequences in the same chronology. If an already committed common prefix lacks the necessary earlier person or cannot be reproduced, hold the join. An initial generation can keep intermediate genealogies private until this resolution. A player-visible past cannot be silently forked away. [Father and historical join](../../SEAM/24_SEVEN_CRACKS_CONCEPT_CODE_DEVELOPMENT_BANK_R1/03_BACKWARD_CONDITIONED_FATHER_CHRONOLOGICAL_WORLD_JOIN.md)
5. **Live the relation in both directions.** From the appropriate clocks keep at least the mother, father, her model of him, his model of her, the objective relationship, the household's material organization and the common world/event ledger distinct. Each has its own reader and predecessor. He can construe her silence as assent while she treats his repeated offers as unreliable. Whose childcare shift was actually performed is a third fact. Each may have lovers, dependents, co-workers and subsisting obligations outside the household. Earlier children get actual origins, ages, carers and causal effects. A breakup can change where siblings live without changing biological genealogy. Once the joint prehistory is committed, subsequent acts arise forward from the continuing persons. A later change of faith, resentment, absence or reconciliation need never preserve an old answer.

**The exact conditional distribution is usually unknown.** The father’s own answer target must respect the actual encounter constraints where those constrain reportable facts. Estimating how frequent such combinations are requires independently warranted pairing evidence or a declared scenario, and the male population denominator differs from the denominator of eligible fathers on that branch. Sorting candidates by how easy they are to complete is an additional selection mechanism. Report its selection probabilities when known or refrain from latent frequency claims. In a playable scenario one compatible family is enough to commit a possible history with its assumptions disclosed. It cannot establish a typical family. A single cross-section cannot determine where in this genealogy an interview should fall.

When estimating frequencies across families, keep the empirical law for targets separate from the unknown law of the LLM's fictional completions. A prolific model can offer thousands of variants without yielding a population distribution for motives. If a quantified forward law and sampling probabilities are independently available for a bounded question, use those to evaluate that question only.

A separate feasibility calculation exposes a failure specific to the father join. In a finite *birth-conditioned* scenario with warranted maternal-path masses and proposed paternal-type capacities, put an edge only where a candidate pair could have the required earlier events and can jointly pass both reference reads. To realize both marginals, every subset of path mass must fit within the capacity of the union of its eligible partners. This is the bipartite flow condition; a deficient cut quantifies the minimum unmatched probability. For example, if .60 of candidate trajectories require already-witnessed presence before a relocation, and all eligible father histories jointly carry only .35 under the proposed conditional law, at least .25 cannot be joined. Generating more copies of easy fathers would conceal a disagreement about history or pooling. Absence of an edge can mean an unimplemented internal reader, an exhausted search or a physically impossible chronology; mark the reason before interpreting the cut. Male population prevalence alone supplies no warranted capacity for a sample of fathers on those trajectories. With only marginal evidence, report a set of couplings and let genuinely independent shared action narrow it. This is a research feasibility calculation, never a matchmaking service that orders actors to cooperate.

## 5. The child has no backward boundary

At an admitted birth under ADP-015, create a fresh actor through the sparse ADP-007/008/020 beginning. Bind genealogy, actual carers, date, bodily condition and concrete setting. Begin with a vulnerable, changing sensorimotor organism with needs, discomfort, movements and dependency. It has no adult religion, class consciousness, survey obligations, mature understanding of love or lexical self. A parent being able and willing to respond, actually hearing the cry, recognizing what it calls for and possessing the necessary food or time can all differ. The child's hunger can interrupt the mother's project, give the father a new occasion to offer help, and change what each learns about the other. Feeding, warmth, injury, comfort, sensory exploration, play and periods of apparently uneventful life require time and can carry developmental effects. Keep ordinary childhood running between conflicts and highlights. [Infant primitives, care and ordinary life](../../SEAM/22_MATERIAL_INFANT_PRIMITIVES_CAREGIVING_BACKGROUND_LIFE_AND_DISTRIBUTIONAL_BREADTH_R1.md)

For example, a child can come to expect food from one caregiver on hearing a particular sound through repeated sequences of discomfort, approach, contact and relief. Recruitment of the person or sound in anticipation is a candidate developmental law with culturally and developmentally bounded evidence. Later a food or song can become desired because it brings the family together, even without the original bodily deficit. A need becoming the desire for *this food with this person* changes what the child seeks and whose action it recruits. An older sibling may make the same sound in play. The same sound can then have several acquired uses. Later, school or worship may supply a shared name, a prohibition or a different occasion for the same practice. An acquired identification can become an unnoticed way of perceiving until a friend is excluded. Alienation from an established practice may prompt avoidance, effort to regain recognition, or a more general project of inclusion. Recognition and solidarity remain historical possibilities. The state transition must permit an acquisition to fail, an expectation to persist after disconfirmation, or a loving act to be misread.

Advance this new person through the ordinary forward owners alongside independently acting contemporaries, at finer temporal resolution where bodily regulation, perception, practice or conversation requires it. Preserve both parents' continuing histories through their own lower-frequency updates and on-demand shared acts. The child receives only the effects that reach them. No loss on a future adult interview may select a childhood or replace a person already born. In research, interview independent eligible cohorts at the appropriate age and historical date to see what response patterns actually emerge. A divergent emerging joint can indict a scenario, contact law, developmental rule, observation model or assumed historical transport. Attribute discrepancies by independent observations appropriate to each mechanism. The eventual interactive Other has this same identity, debts, repertoire, private life and limited access. A player can fail to win their trust. P6 should project what an encounter makes available and admit the player's attempts through the same world. A human effect requires separate voluntary human evidence.

**Sparse development is a settled product constraint for Nadia as well as for her parents.** SOROK's 3–5 selected events per batch can stand for sustained participation, a recurring practice, a long deprivation or the occasion on which an accumulated conflict becomes expressible. Preserve which interval a selection represents and what the actor could experience. A representative meal can expose an established division of care; counting that one receipt as twenty independent experiences would fabricate evidence. An uneventful interval can sustain dependence, consume another person's time or leave a capacity dormant. Event selection needs consequences for continuing life; a durable psychological write on every selected event would distort it. Nadia's greater developmental depth comes from what successive intervals *actually constitute*, under the same scarcity constraint. ### How a growing repertoire changes the next computation

**State at time t.** Maintain body and maturational capacities; executable sensorimotor operations; actor-specific distinctions, anticipations and traces; acquired sign/operation links; commitments to particular others and goods; and the actor's accessible models of people and institutions. These grow at different rates. Track relations with historical predecessors, cues and scope, activation conditions, incompatible recruitments, expiry/revision and the actual subsequent read. Parent initialization can directly fill some of these relations. The child begins with only those warranted for a dependent organism. The media used to fill an adult should not determine the schema for what a child can later become.

**One transition.** Advance the body independently of what the actor calls its condition. From the currently reachable world produce sensory particulars under actual posture, distance, modality and social access. Recruit existing distinctions and active projects to select what the actor notices among those particulars. Let existing motor or communicative means produce attempts, including exploratory movement and inaction. Other people act from their own heads. Resolve the objective outcome, including the failure of an attempt or continuation of pain. Deliver what the actor could experience of that difference. A provisional law can retain a scoped action–outcome expectation, differentiate a previously pooled cue, link an available sign to a practiced relation, reorganize a project, or yield no interpretable change. **The output of one acquisition becomes an input to later *perception and action selection*.** This is where a culturally acquired category can govern a person's paradigm. It can determine whether she even notices a choice or thinks a request is possible. It cannot produce unstocked food, reverse bereavement, compel love or make an absence perceptible as someone else's secret intention.

Consider hunger in a household that has learned to call waiting for food “patience.” An infant can feel hunger and undergo delayed relief before understanding a word. **How a practice can become a paradigm.** Suppose the child repeatedly sees an adult wait for a person to speak before making a shared decision, later learns the local words for “having a say,” and begins to seek out the person whose voice is missing. These are three different changes. A cue/expectation changes what the child anticipates; an acquired way of addressing someone changes the acts available to them; recruiting that operation in a different institution changes *what the situation looks like before they choose anything*. A public meeting can now appear unfinished even after the chair announces a decision. Test the last change by holding the meeting fixed and withholding the acquired recruitment link in a research fork. Does the missing participant cease to attract attention while the words remain understood? If it only changes the sentence a renderer emits afterward, the proposed paradigm has no psychological effect. Another route to the very same public phrase is learning that the chair rewards people who say it. That route predicts different attention when the chair is absent.
