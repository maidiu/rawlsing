# 0. Problem

Recent feasibility discussion exposed a conflation in the phrase **"Claude creates flesh."**

There are at least two fundamentally different realization problems:

1. **actor/world realization** — computational person/world state becomes an exact observable occurrence;
2. **user phenomenal realization** — an already-existing observable occurrence becomes the finite, perspectival surface available to this user.

These MUST NOT be collapsed.

The user-facing system must preserve all of the following simultaneously:

- continuity with the full generated Nadia;
- noncontradiction with already admitted Nadia/world/history;
- reasonable extrapolability from Nadia rather than mere logical permissibility;
- relation-specificity with this user;
- actor epistemic limitation;
- lawful lying, misrecognition, concealment and self-misunderstanding;
- user-specific attention / recognition / phenomenal filtering;
- user epistemic limitation;
- binding persistence of whatever observable surface is actually emitted;
- bounded latency / cost, preferably without iterative LLM appellate loops.

This artifact banks the current strongest architecture for doing so.

# 1. Primary noncollapse

Preserve:

`TOTAL CAUSAL NADIA`
!=
`CURRENT PERSON×EVENT NADIA`
!=
`NADIA'S CURRENT CONSCIOUS / ACCESSIBLE STATE`
!=
`NADIA'S COMMUNICATIVE / ACTION FORMATION`
!=
`OBJECTIVE OBSERVABLE NADIA OCCURRENCE`
!=
`USER PHENOMENAL ACCESS TO THAT OCCURRENCE`
!=
`USER INTERPRETATION OF NADIA`.

Compact:

> **CAUSAL NADIA != OBSERVABLE NADIA != USER-EXPERIENCED NADIA != USER-INTERPRETED NADIA.**

All four may be causally connected.

None may silently substitute for another.

# 2. Actor/world realization must occur before user-phenomenal personalization

The strongest current direction is:

```text
USER ACT / UTTERANCE
        ↓
semantic transduction
        ↓
RAWLSING CAUSAL MACHINE
  durable V3 Nadia
        ↓
  CAN-008 Nadia × event
        ↓
  narrowed PFF / interaction mediation
        ↓
  V2 actor expression formation
        ↓
OBJECTIVE OBSERVABLE OCCURRENCE
        ↓
══════════ EPISTEMIC MEMBRANE ══════════
        ↓
USER PHENOMENAL PROJECTION
        ↓
USER-FACING SURFACE
```

The user's learned attention/recognition profile MUST NOT influence the causal generation of Nadia's outward occurrence.

Hard law:

> **USER PERSONALIZATION MAY SELECT, RESOLVE, LEXICALIZE, OR WEIGHT OBSERVABLE REALITY. IT MAY NOT ENTER THE CAUSAL PRODUCTION OF THE OBSERVABLE REALITY IT WILL LATER FILTER.**

This is the anti-mirror wall.

Forbidden loop:

`USER MODEL SAYS USER LIKES MICROEXPRESSIONS`
→ `NADIA IS GENERATED WITH INTERESTING MICROEXPRESSIONS FOR USER`
→ `NARRATOR SURFACES THEM`
→ `SYSTEM "LEARNS" USER LIKES THEM`.

Preferred:

`NADIA DOES WHATEVER NADIA LAWFULLY DOES`
→ `USER MODEL AFFECTS WHICH PARTS OF THAT INDEPENDENT OCCURRENCE ENTER THIS USER'S PHENOMENAL SURFACE`.

# 3. Relation state appears on both sides of the membrane in different forms

The objective/durable relationship and each participant's model of it are distinct.

## Nadia-production side may consume

- objective Nadia↔user event/history state;
- Nadia's model of the user;
- Nadia's metaperception / model of what the user thinks of her;
- relation-specific expectations;
- commitments;
- injuries / repair history;
- current relation-specific recruitment;
- actor-local uncertainty.

These can causally affect what Nadia does.

## User-phenomenal side may consume

- objective outward occurrence;
- user's actually experienced relation history;
- user's remembered/known facts;
- Rawlsing's bounded model of the user's attention / recognition;
- user's current position/access/task;
- user's own warranted model/hypotheses where relevant to lexicalization or assisted reflection.

It MUST NOT receive as ordinary phenomenal inputs:

- Nadia's private trust state;
- Nadia's hidden suspicion;
- Nadia's inaccessible motives;
- Nadia's private model of the user;
- Nadia's suppressed thought;
- actor-private relation state that has not become observable.

Hard law:

> **RELATION-SPECIFIC CAUSATION MAY SHAPE NADIA'S OUTWARD ACT. USER PHENOMENOLOGY MAY OBSERVE ONLY THE OUTWARD ACT AND OTHER USER-LAWFUL EVIDENCE.**

# 4. Whole-person continuity requirement

Rawlsing requires more than factual noncontradiction.

A rendered occurrence may contain no explicit falsehood and still fail to be Nadia if it makes her systematically:

- too articulate;
- too psychologically transparent;
- too warm;
- too agreeable;
- too patient;
- too narratively neat;
- too witty;
- too consistently responsive to the user;
- too direct;
- too generically "human";
- too much like the renderer model's default social distribution.

Therefore the required standard is:

`NONCONTRADICTORY WITH NADIA`
+
`REASONABLY EXTRAPOLABLE FROM NADIA`.

The second condition is distributional/person-specific, not merely logical.

Hard law:

> **A LAWFUL SURFACE MUST COME FROM THE NADIA-SHAPED REGION OF THE LAWFUL POSSIBILITY SPACE.**

This requires a sufficiently rich **whole-person expressive substrate** to reach actor realization.

# 6. Hidden causal state must shape outward expression without becoming renderer-accessible user knowledge

The actor realization stage must preserve inherited PFF/CAN-008 noncollapse:

`CAUSAL ACTIVITY != ACTOR ACCESSIBILITY`

`ACTOR ACCESSIBILITY != FOCALITY`

`FELT != NAMED`

`ACTOR SELF-EXPLANATION != MACHINE CAUSAL ACCOUNT`

`CURRENT RESPONSE != DURABLE CHANGE`.

Example:

Machine causal Nadia may include:
- user touched a deeply recruited developmental organization;
- Nadia is affected;
- Nadia does not know the deeper cause;
- Nadia consciously experiences irritation;
- Nadia partly suspects the user's observation could be right;
- she resists easy concession;
- she wants evidence;
- she withholds further self-disclosure.

The actor-expression envelope should expose downstream expressive consequences, not a narratable hidden-cause explanation.

Preferred:

```text
actor accessible:
  irritation
  uncertainty

actor inaccessible:
  deeper causal parent X

communicative tendency:
  resist easy concession

inhibition:

  silence
  brief challenge
  humor
  gaze shift
  no mandatory leakage
```

Not:

`SECRET TRUE CAUSE = X; PLEASE DON'T TELL USER`.

Hard law:

> **HIDDEN CAUSAL TRUTH SHOULD BE TRANSDUCED INTO AUTHORIZED EXPRESSIVE CONSEQUENCES BEFORE USER-FACING PHENOMENOLOGY, NOT HANDED TO THE PHENOMENAL RENDERER WITH A REQUEST FOR RESTRAINT.**

Candidate fields include:

actor speech:

actor body:
  position

This layer answers:

> **What did Nadia/world actually externally do?**

It does NOT answer:

> **What did the user notice or infer?**

# 8. Exact emitted surface becomes binding

The phrase "Claude renders" must not imply decorative noncausal prose in a live causal scene.

Once the user is shown an observable particular, Rawlsing is thereafter constrained by it.

Example emitted surface:

> She catches the mug before it tips, sets it farther from your elbow, and glances toward the hallway before answering.

If published as the live occurrence, this commits at least:

- mug existed there;
- mug was near user's elbow;
- mug tipped enough to motivate catching;
- Nadia perceived the event;
- Nadia caught it;
- Nadia moved it;
- Nadia glanced hallway-ward;
- time elapsed;
- later speech occurred after those actions.

Therefore:

> **A USER-VISIBLE LIVE SURFACE IS BOTH A PROJECTION OF PRIOR CAUSAL STATE AND A CAUSAL PARENT OF FUTURE STATE.**

Compact:

`BACKWARD CONTINUITY + FORWARD BINDING = LIVE SURFACE LAW`.

# 9. Claude / renderer authority correction

Earlier shorthand "Claude has almost no freedom over ontology" is too strong.

A rich natural-language realization necessarily proposes concrete commitments.

Correct distinction:

> **THE RENDERER MAY HAVE BROAD CANDIDATE-REALIZATION FREEDOM BUT ZERO UNILATERAL CANONICALIZATION AUTHORITY.**

In a live turn, renderer-generated specifics may include:
- exact gesture;
- object handling;
- precise timing;
- physical beat;
- concrete sensory detail;
- exact language;
- a previously unresolved ordinary world particular.

Where those become user-visible occurrences, they must be either:
1. already authorized by the render envelope; or
2. generated inside explicitly permitted unresolved/materialization slots whose commitments can be accepted deterministically/by existing Rawlsing law.

Do not allow open-ended renderer invention of:
- biography;
- relationship history;
- inaccessible motives;
- unsupported memories;
- arbitrary supporting actors;
- hidden world truth;
- historical incidents.

# 10. Latent completion is not historical rewriting

Preserve:

`NOT YET MATERIALIZED != DID NOT EXIST`.

A later concrete fact may be a lawful completion of previously unresolved pre-existing reality.

It must satisfy reverse compatibility with:
- current Nadia;
- already admitted history;
- structural conditions;
- family/network facts;
- prior statements;
- existing memories;
- material constraints;
- root seed / governed randomness;
- branch-local versus shared-root ownership.

Preferred:

```text
CURRENT GENERATED NADIA
+ ADMITTED HISTORY
+ STRUCTURAL / FAMILY / WORLD CONSTRAINTS
+ PRIOR CLAIMS / MEMORIES
+ CANONICAL TARGET KEY
+ GOVERNED RANDOMNESS
        ↓
LAWFUL LATENT COMPLETION
```

This is constrained historical completion, not retcon-as-overwrite.

A newly materialized pre-contact fact may become shared-root canon only under the cross-branch compatibility law.

Post-contact branch-local facts remain branch-local unless an explicit exogenous shared-world owner governs otherwise.

# 11. Lying / error / self-misrecognition are lawful and do not violate noncontradiction

Noncontradiction does NOT mean every proposition spoken by Nadia is objectively true.

Maintain distinct:

`OBJECTIVE WORLD TRUTH`
!=
`NADIA BELIEF`
!=
`NADIA MEMORY`
!=
`NADIA TESTIMONY`
!=
`USER BELIEF`.

Example:

Objective truth:
`Nadia was fired.`

Nadia knows/believes:
`I was fired.`

Current generated state makes concealment reasonably extrapolable.

Nadia says:
`"I quit."`

The binding event is:
`NADIA UTTERED "I quit."`

The proposition inside the utterance is false.

That is lawful.

Likewise:
- misremembering;
- rationalization;
- bluffing;
- exaggeration;
- joking;
- sincere but causally wrong self-explanation;
- partial truth;
- uncertainty.

Hard law:

> **ALL OBSERVABLE REALIZATION MUST BE LAWFUL. OBSERVABLE SPEECH NEED NOT BE OBJECTIVELY TRUE.**

# 12. User phenomenal projection must operate only on observable reality

Once an objective occurrence exists, user phenomenal projection may use:

- user's physical position;
- sensory access;
- proximity;
- current gaze / ATTEND;
- current task / load;
- learned attention prior;
- recognition repertoire;
- prior user knowledge;
- language competence;
- physically dominant salience;
- exploration mass;
- already user-accessible relation history.

It may:
- select;
- omit;
- rank;
- lexicalize;
- describe at lawful resolution.

It may NOT:
- change what Nadia did;
- change what Nadia said;
- inject Nadia-private motive;
- expose hidden world truth;
- convert system interpretation into observed fact;
- make user attention causally produce interesting actor behavior.

Example objective occurrence:

```text
silence ~1.4 s
gaze → carton
thumb flattens crease
Nadia: "Do I?"
low volume
no laugh
gaze → user
```

User A may lawfully receive:
> There's a beat before she answers. She looks down at the carton in her hands and presses its fold flat with her thumb. "Do I?" No laugh this time. She looks back at you.

User B may lawfully receive:
> She looks down at what she's folding. "Do I?" she says quietly, then looks back at you.

Same objective Nadia occurrence.

Different finite phenomenal surfaces.

# 15. Normal-turn call-budget requirement

A routine live turn SHOULD NOT require an LLM appellate loop such as:

`DeepSeek → Rawlsing → Claude → DeepSeek critique → Rawlsing → Claude repair → ...`.

Reasons:
- latency;
- cost;
- possible nontermination/oscillation;
- compounded model variance;
- difficult provenance;
- user experience degradation.

Current target:

> **NORMAL LIVE TURN = DEEPSEEK-CLASS TRANSDUCTION CALL ×1 + CLAUDE-CLASS ACTOR REALIZATION CALL ×1.**

The model brands are experimental placeholders; the functional separation is what matters.

Any additional model call should be exceptional rather than constitutive.

If ordinary turns routinely require rerender/review loops, treat that as evidence against the architecture rather than normal operating cost.

It must not itself overwrite Rawlsing truth.

# 16A. Minimum competent temporal grain / no compulsory full-V3 turn

A live user turn does NOT automatically justify rerunning the full durable developmental machine.

The semantic-transduction layer should route the admitted event to the minimum competent owner/grain.

Candidate outcomes include:

- no durable update;
- objective world/material delta only;
- session/interaction-fast update only;
- Nadia-model-of-user evidence/update under competent authority;
- relationship-state effect under competent authority;
- memory/accessibility effect under competent authority;
- CAN-008 current person×event coupling;
- one or more exact durable V3 owner effects;
- deeper developmental recomputation only when the event actually warrants it.

Preserve inherited law:

`CURRENT RESPONSE != DURABLE PERSON CHANGE`.

And:

> **DO NOT RUN THE WHOLE PERSON'S DEVELOPMENTAL HISTORY AS A RITUAL CONSEQUENCE OF EVERY TURN.**

A trivial acknowledgment, ordinary object move, or low-significance exchange may lawfully produce little or no durable psychic update.

This is both an authority rule and a cost-control rule.

# 19. Whole-person context criterion

Current operational shorthand:

> **KEEP THE CURRENT PERSON WHOLE; RETRIEVE THE HISTORICAL TAIL SELECTIVELY.**

The actor-realization model should normally receive a sufficiently complete current whole-person expressive substrate rather than reconstructing Nadia from a few retrieved facts each turn.

By contrast, the potentially unbounded historical event/provenance tail should be retrieved selectively according to present causal/expression dependency.

Preserve:

`CURRENT PERSON SUBSTRATE != COMPLETE EVENT LEDGER IN PROMPT`.

`SELECTIVE HISTORY RETRIEVAL != SELECTIVE PERSONHOOD`.

The durable Rawlsing state remains authoritative even where the model working context is a bounded projection.

Do not assume generic RAG suffices.

For any Nadia information omitted from actor-realization context, one of the following must hold:

1. it is genuinely irrelevant to the present realization; or
2. its present causal/expressive effects have been preserved in a sufficient derived projection; or
3. it can be retrieved through an explicit dependency before realization.

Hard law:

> **OMISSION IS LAWFUL ONLY WHEN PRESENT EXPRESSION CANNOT BE DISTORTED BY THE OMISSION.**

This creates an empirical problem:
how compressed can the renderer Nadia substrate become before Nadia drift appears?

Do not decide by intuition.

# 21. Bindingness and same-turn admission

The accepted V2 architecture already requires causal-mode actor output to remain:

`CANDIDATE_NONCAUSAL_PENDING_V3_ADMISSION`

until the exact artifact/hash is admitted.

This artifact preserves that law while adding the new live-surface requirement:

- actor realization must not be published before it is known to be lawful under its supplied render contract;
- exact published actor speech/action becomes binding observable history;
- emitted material particulars become future constraints according to their owner/status;
- user phenomenal projection occurs over that admitted observable layer.

The preferred alpha design should minimize open-ended post-generation adjudication by making the actor-realization contract sufficiently constrained that ordinary output is directly admissible through deterministic/local checks.

Exceptional hard violation may:
- fail closed;
- trigger one stricter rerender;
- require a fresh causal/materialization decision.

An iterative semantic verifier loop is NOT the normal architecture.

# 22. Same-call commitment declaration is optional optimization, not sole truth authority

The actor renderer may return structured metadata alongside the exact observable surface, for example:

```json
{
  "utterances": [...],
  "body_actions": [...],
  "object_actions": [...],
  "new_material_particulars": [...],
  "timings": [...]
}
```

This can improve deterministic admission and storage.

However:

`MODEL SELF-DECLARED COMMITMENT LIST != INFALLIBLE SEMANTIC ACCOUNT OF ITS PROSE`.

Therefore early testing must measure whether the structured occurrence representation and natural-language surface remain extensionally equivalent.

Where possible, prefer generating the structured observable occurrence first inside the same model call and rendering the visible prose from that declared occurrence, rather than prose-first free generation plus self-audit.

# 23. Important architecture option — structured occurrence as primary, prose as co-realization

Strong candidate within one Claude-class call:

```text
INPUT:
  complete actor/world render contract

MODEL OUTPUT:
  A. typed objective observable occurrence
  B. exact actor utterance(s)
  C. actor/world surface realization derived from A/B
```

Application treats A/B as the principal occurrence payload and visible prose as its rendered form.

This can reduce the chance that prose introduces undeclared ontology.

It does not prove equivalence; hostile testing remains required.

# 24. What remains genuinely difficult

Status: `F1/F2__NEEDS_EMPIRICAL_CHARACTERIZATION`.

## B. Lossless-for-expression Nadia projection
Can a bounded current actor packet preserve enough of full Nadia to avoid generic-person drift?

Status: `F1/F2__UNKNOWN REQUIRED SIZE/STRUCTURE`.

## C. One-shot actor realization
Can a Claude-class model simultaneously preserve:
- whole-person continuity;
- relation specificity;
- current event recruitment;
- epistemic limits;
- material/world constraints;
- exact actor access;
- lawful lying/misrecognition;
- binding occurrence discipline;

with a low exceptional rerender rate?

Status: `F2 CANDIDATE__NOT DEMONSTRATED`.

## D. Distributional Nadia continuity
Even without contradictions, does the renderer systematically select overly warm, coherent, witty, user-responsive or dramatically satisfying realizations?

Status: `FU/F2__MAJOR TEST TARGET`.

Can mostly local deterministic/stochastic selection and lexicalization produce a compelling user surface without a second high-capability model call?

Status: `F1/F2__BOUNDEDLY TESTABLE`.

## F. Same-call structured occurrence / prose equivalence
Can the renderer reliably make its typed occurrence record and visible prose describe exactly the same event?

Status: `F2 CANDIDATE__TEST REQUIRED`.
