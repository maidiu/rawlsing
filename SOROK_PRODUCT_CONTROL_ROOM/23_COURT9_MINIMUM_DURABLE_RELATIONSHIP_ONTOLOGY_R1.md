# Sorok Court 9 — Minimum Durable Relationship Ontology R1

Status: OPEN_COURT__D001_CORE_DEPENDENCY__NOT_ADOPTED

Date: 2026-09-29

## 0. Core question

What must actually be durable and canonical for a relationship to remain causally real across years without collapsing into a hidden friendship vector?

Strong current suspicion:

> the relationship may be less a single psyche object than a structured view over shared history, current objective ties, and each person's own relation-specific organization.

This court must determine whether that suspicion survives hard cases.

---

## 1. Candidate durable grains

### A. Canonical shared event history

Objective occurrences:
- meetings;
- conversations that actually occurred;
- help/refusal;
- promises;
- invitations;
- absences;
- injuries;
- apologies;
- repairs;
- shared projects;
- resource transfers;
- introductions;
- conflicts;
- access grants/revocations;
- explicit relationship declarations.

Occurrence is not interpretation.

### B. Current objective relational facts

Potential examples:
- active commitment;
- agreed routine;
- access permission;
- debt/obligation;
- shared project;
- practical dependency;
- custody/care arrangement;
- possession/key/shared resource;
- explicit role or agreement;
- current communication channel availability.

These are objective because actions/agreements/world facts established them.

### C. Actor-owned model of counterpart

For each person separately:
- expected reliability by domain;
- anticipated reactions;
- model of counterpart's values/preferences;
- belief about what counterpart understands;
- perceived safety in conflict;
- inferred motives;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- pattern hypotheses.

These may be wrong.

### D. Actor-owned relation-conditioned organization

Potentially:
- what I permit myself to ask from this person;
- what I expect this person owes me;
- what kinds of vulnerability are possible here;
- attachment/importance;
- grievance;
- fear of loss;
- relation-specific defense or anticipation;
- role-conditioned need.

Exact owner status must be crosswalked into durable V3 rather than presumed as a new dyad subsystem.

### E. Actor memory of shared history

Distinct from canonical event history.

Each actor may remember, forget, reinterpret or never have perceived the same event.

### F. Network-position consequences

Examples:
- introduced to family;
- recognized by household as a trusted visitor;
- included/excluded from group;
- relation changes another relationship;
- third-party expectation now exists.

These may belong to network/world relation owners rather than the focal dyad.

---

## 2. What probably should NOT be one objective scalar

Do not presume canonical pair-level numbers for:
- friendship;
- closeness;
- trust;
- intimacy;
- attachment;
- health;
- affection;
- dependency;
- understanding;
- compatibility.

These may require:
- domain-specific person-owned predictions;
- multiple objective facts;
- behavior histories;
- derived summaries;
- uncertainty.

A convenient UI/internal cache must not silently become causal authority.

---

## 3. Relationship view versus relationship owner

Candidate engineering distinction:

### AUTHORITATIVE STATE
Owned by exact systems:
- event ledger;
- commitments/access/roles;
- person-owned memory/model/expectation states;
- network facts;
- world resources.

### MATERIALIZED RELATIONSHIP VIEW
A derived/indexed object for efficient querying:
- recent shared history;
- active commitments;
- permissions;
- actor-local relation summaries;
- unresolved injuries;
- routine patterns;
- network ties.

The view may be cached.

But:

`MATERIALIZED VIEW != NEW SEMANTIC OWNER`.

Recomputability/provenance must remain possible.

---

## 4. Trust as domain-specific prediction

Test decomposition:

Nadia may expect the user to:
- keep a confidence;
- show up when promised;
- safely watch a child;
- return borrowed money;
- tolerate disagreement;
- remember practical instructions;
- avoid humiliating her publicly;
- respect physical boundaries;
- tell the truth about mistakes.

These predictions can diverge.

Question:
which are generated person-owned models versus durable learned expectations versus objective history summaries?

Do not solve by averaging them into TRUST=.72.

---

## 5. Intimacy as transformed affordance

Candidate representation target:

Intimacy becomes real when history changes:
- what can be asked;
- what can be refused;
- what access exists;
- which needs become expectable;
- which emotions/actions become safe enough;
- what absence means;
- how much explanation is assumed;
- who is included in household/network life.

This may be represented through a combination of:
- objective permissions/roles/history;
- person-owned expectations;
- current mediation;

rather than an objective intimacy score.

---

## 6. Mutual recognition is itself an event/history

Examples:
- both explicitly say they are friends;
- both agree to exclusivity;
- both establish a weekly dinner;
- both agree the user may enter without knocking;
- both acknowledge a rupture.

These are objective relational facts because reciprocal acts occurred.

But:

> both call each other friends

does not prove equal attachment, equal expectations, equal benefit or equal models of what friendship means.

---

## 7. Injury and repair

Need to separate:
- event that caused injury;
- each actor's perception;
- each actor's attribution;
- actor memory;
- unresolved practical consequence;
- grievance/expectation changes;
- repair attempt;
- restitution;
- observed changed conduct;
- later reclassification.

Repair should not delete history.

Question:
what durable residue is necessary so later safe conflict or persistent distrust emerges lawfully?

---

## 8. Pattern formation

Hard case:
one missed instruction.

Possible states:
- objective event exists;
- persona remembers it weakly;
- no generalized pattern yet;
- later similar events reactivate it;
- a person-owned hypothesis forms;
- counterexamples weaken it;
- years later it is reinterpreted.

Need a model in which:
`EVENT != PATTERN`
`PATTERN != TRAIT`
`PATTERN HYPOTHESIS != OBJECTIVE TRUTH`.

---

## 9. Relationship-specific durable change versus general person change

If repeated history with one person changes the actor, ask:

### Relation-specific
Does this remain scoped to this person/role?

### Relation-class generalization
Does it change expectations of similar people/roles?

### Cross-relational durable change
Does it modify the actor's broader organization?

Promotion/generalization must be causally earned.

This is a key D001/V3 owner question.

---

## 10. Shared routine as emergent object

Some relational facts may be neither static trait nor single event.

Examples:
- Tuesday dinner;
- morning school pickup;
- Sunday phone call;
- always knocking before entering;
- shared joke ritual;
- regular borrowing of a tool.

Question:
does routine require a durable dyad-owned representation, or can it be represented as repeated commitments/expectations across person/world owners?

This is an important hostile case for the 'relationship is only a view' hypothesis.

---

## 11. Joint projects / genuinely pair-level organization

Potential cases where a true shared object may be necessary:
- co-running a garden;
- shared childcare arrangement;
- joint savings goal;
- collaborative campaign/project;
- caring for a third person;
- co-owned object;
- explicit plan with tasks assigned to both.

These may justify **joint project objects**.

But a joint project object is not the same as a general 'relationship psyche'.

---

## 12. Relationship termination

After estrangement/death/moving away:
- canonical history remains;
- active permissions/commitments may close;
- memories remain actor-owned;
- generalized effects may persist;
- network consequences may persist.

Therefore a relationship need not be 'active' to remain causally important.

---

## 13. Hard test matrix

1. Nadia trusts user with child but not money.
2. Nadia likes user but expects them to be unreliable.
3. User and Nadia disagree on whether they are close.
4. They share an important obligation while disliking each other.
5. Repair improves conflict safety but not practical trust.
6. One actor forgets an event the other never forgets.
7. A third party treats the user as family before Nadia does.
8. A shared routine survives after warmth declines.
9. A relation ends but permanently changes both people.
10. One dyad changes how Nadia approaches later relationships.

Any ontology that cannot represent these without scalar collapse fails.

---

## 14. Candidate minimal architecture hypothesis

Current candidate:

`CANONICAL EVENT HISTORY`
+ `CURRENT OBJECTIVE COMMITMENTS / ACCESS / ROLES / JOINT PROJECTS`
+ `PERSON-OWNED MEMORY / MODELS / EXPECTATIONS / RELATION-CONDITIONED ORGANIZATION`
+ `NETWORK/WORLD FACTS`
+ `DERIVED MATERIALIZED RELATIONSHIP VIEW`.

Open question:
is any additional irreducibly dyad-owned psychological state actually required?

Do not answer yes merely because ordinary language names a relationship property.
