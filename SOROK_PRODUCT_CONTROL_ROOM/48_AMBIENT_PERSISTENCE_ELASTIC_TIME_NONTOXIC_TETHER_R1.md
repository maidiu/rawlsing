# Sorok — Ambient Persistence, Elastic Time, and Non-Toxic Tether R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_ALPHA_PRODUCT_LAW

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Core target

Rawlsing should be intrusive enough that the persona remains a persistent relational presence in the user's consciousness, but not intrusive enough that the product itself becomes coercive, socially displacing, or unhealthy.

Candidate theorem:

> **Optimize for cognitive persistence, not calendar capture.**

The user should sometimes think:
- `I wonder how Nadia's doing.`
- `I should answer her.`
- `I forgot about that thing she told me.`
- `I want to see her again.`

without the product requiring:
- daily check-ins;
- streaks;
- exact synthetic appointments by default;
- constant push notifications;
- synthetic-person obligations that compete directly with real human relationships.

---

## 1. Default temporal mode — elastic present

Default mode keeps:
- real historical calendar coupling;
- persona/world life continuing;
- real missed opportunities;
- actor-owned messages and invitations;
- relationship-specific consequences of absence;

while mediating scheduled co-presence through elastic rendezvous windows.

Thus:

> **Nadia's Friday at seven is real to Nadia without automatically claiming the external user's literal Friday at seven.**

---

## 2. Cancellation/rescheduling resolves pending events normally

If a scheduled scene is causally pending, the user can message:
- cancel;
- decline;
- ask to reschedule;
- counteroffer;
- explain uncertainty.

Example:

> `I can't make Friday. Saturday afternoon?`

Nadia then receives that message as an ordinary relational event and may:
- agree;
- counter;
- be disappointed;
- be annoyed;
- refuse;
- say it doesn't matter;
- change plans.

Once the event is canceled/rescheduled/rejected, the original pending branch is resolved and downstream history may proceed.

`CAUSALLY PENDING != TEMPORALLY FROZEN UNTIL USER PLAYS`.

---

## 3. Default rendezvous window

Current candidate:
- one diegetic appointment time;
- bounded user play window, perhaps up to ~24 hours for planned low-emergency events;
- user may play the scene anywhere within that product window;
- from actor/world perspective, the scene occurs at the scheduled diegetic time;
- if user cancels before resolution, normal relationship history replaces the meeting;
- if window expires without cancellation/attendance, no-show/nonresponse history is admitted.

Exact window length requires prototype testing.

---

## 4. Event classes should differ

Not all time is equally elastic.

### PLANNED
`Dinner Friday at seven.`
Broad elastic window by default.

### SOFT OPPORTUNITY
`We're going to the fair later if you want to come.`
Shorter window; opportunity may expire.

### IMMEDIATE
`I'm downstairs.`
`Can you help me right now?`
Very short or near-real-time window.

This preserves life flow without converting every appointment into a real-calendar demand.

---

## 5. Product persistence budget

Default product-side intrusiveness should be intentionally bounded.

Potential constitution:
- actor-originated messages may generate ordinary notifications subject to user settings;
- product does not add engagement reminders merely because user has been absent;
- exact appointment reminders are neutral and finite;
- missed messages may accumulate silently;
- no streaks, badges or `Nadia misses you` platform copy;
- user can mute, quiet-hours, or disable push without pausing world time.

The person's life creates salience. The product should not manufacture it.

---

## 6. Optional stronger synchronization

A user may opt into tighter real-time coupling.

Possible modes:

### ELASTIC (default)
Broad rendezvous windows; low platform intrusiveness.

### CLOSE SYNC (optional)
Shorter rendezvous windows and more literal message/event timing.

### REAL-TIME / MAXIMAL VERISIMILITUDE (optional)
Near wall-clock mapping; scheduled scenes have narrow windows; missed timing becomes more literal.

Exact naming remains open.

---

## 7. Do not market strict synchronization as the superior experience

Do not call stricter mode:
- `true mode`;
- `real mode`;
- `hardcore`;
- `full Rawlsing`;
- `the authentic experience`.

Those names create prestige pressure toward a potentially unhealthy level of synthetic obligation.

Prefer neutral descriptive wording such as:
- `Elastic time`;
- `Closer time sync`;
- `Real-time scheduling`.

If 'maximal verisimilitude' remains an internal term, user-facing copy should explain practical consequences rather than glamorize them.

---

## 8. Stronger modes require explicit informed opt-in

Before enabling real-time scheduling, tell the user plainly:
- synthetic meetings may map closely to real clock times;
- missed commitments may become relationship history;
- persona messages/life continue independently;
- notification controls remain available;
- the mode can be turned back down prospectively.

Do not enable based on inferred engagement.

---

## 9. Stronger synchronization never affects relational warrant

A user must not receive more:
- relational warrant;
- persona affection;
- moral credit;
- invitations;
- access;
- prestige;

for choosing a more intrusive temporal mode.

`TEMPORAL VERISIMILITUDE != VIRTUE`.

---

## 10. Persona toxicity remains independent

A manipulative/demanding persona may exert strong relational pressure even in elastic mode.

Product mediation should not:
- hide lawful actor behavior;
- amplify it;
- use strict scheduling to intensify it for engagement.

Thus the user can experience a demanding person without the platform itself becoming a demanding product.

---

## 11. Social-health design target

Rawlsing should not require the synthetic social graph to displace the user's real social graph.

Design signals to avoid:
- users feel obligated to check before sleeping/waking;
- users routinely cancel real plans for synthetic appointments;
- notification anxiety becomes a core retention mechanism;
- strict synchronization becomes status-coded;
- relationship consequences correlate with app engagement rather than diegetic conduct.

These are alpha/early-beta health failures even if retention rises.

---

## 12. Deep theorem

> **The persona should persist in consciousness because the relationship matters, not because the app refuses to leave the user alone.**

And:

> **Rawlsing may make time matter without making itself master of the user's time.**
