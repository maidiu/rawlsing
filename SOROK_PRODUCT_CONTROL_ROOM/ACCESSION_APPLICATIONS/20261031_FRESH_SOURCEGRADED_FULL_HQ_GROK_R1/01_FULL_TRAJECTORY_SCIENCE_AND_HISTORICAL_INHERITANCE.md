## B. Source-rooted developmental alternatives, not generic trait scores

**Need, object, motive, action (N01/O01/E03).** Leontiev: activity is organized through historically accessible objects, not merely internal appetite; distinguish bodily/socially reproduced need, desire, objectified motive, local goal, available means, opportunity and performed action. A parent can give a tool without the child acquiring the technique; a goal can be inherited/obligatory without a stable desire; a religious formula can be repeated without internal consent. Strong falsifier: removing means changes success but not acquired capacity; moving context changes activation of an existing capacity but not actor's long-horizon formation.

**Relationally constituted but non-dyad-owned person (N02,N03,N05,N07; O02/O04/O07/O08).** Repeated ordinary care forms scoped anticipations and practical dispositions; absence, danger, withdrawal and repair may or may not alter them. A parent has a history *before the child exists*. A dyad is neither two fixed trait vectors nor one shared trust meter; coactors retain independent labor, love, conflicting commitments and social relations. Historical household labor, childcare, repair and mundane stability can produce developmental specificity without a scripted trauma. Falsifier: holding a single exceptional event fixed while varying long-term ordinary care alters meaning/response even if event intensity is identical. Conversely a strong routine baseline may absorb a contradiction and remain genuinely stable.

**Plural dispositions and context (N04,N05,N08; O03/O06/O07).** Lahire's plural actor is a direct challenge to single globally consistent personality variables: repeated differentiated socializations can produce coexisting repertoires which specific roles, institutions and interlocutors differently recruit. A person can be deferential in a workplace, protective of a sibling and resistant to clergy without a single global "submission" value. Archer's internal conversation/concerns is useful as a competing reflexivity model, but cannot be simply appended as a compulsory fully articulate inner narrator. A single event may change the context of recruitment without causing durable acquisition. Falsifier: exact same task under different relational objects activates previously existing distinct routines, while no new learning is justified.

**Historical class/activity and life course (N06,N07,N10; O08/O09/O13).** Sève's social distribution of lived time and historically specific individuality; Elder's Depression cohort demonstrates how economic disruption, household/child work, gendered family responsibilities, different starting class conditions and linked lives matter through stages and temporal order; Kohn/Schooler link work self-direction and personality, but causal attribution remains empirical and cannot be inferred from one occupation code. Mortimer's adolescent work lives and Luria's culturally mediated cognitive tasks warn against universal stage assumptions. Holzkamp's subject standpoint and Dreier's participation across social practices argue against treating laboratory behavior or a game turn as the entire person's ecology. Wright locates contradictory structural class positions; social-reproduction accounts insist caregiving, unpaid labor, households, and the production of labor power are not noise outside "work." This is a theoretical/empirical comparison, NOT a ready numeric generator. The inspected attached texts were limited-page extracts; detailed chapter claims require separate precise page verification.

**Alienation, meaning and ultimate claims (N09,N10; O10–O13; M06/M11/M19/M22).** Structural domination is often internalized as normality or duty; "agent-of" and "agency-against" may coexist at different social scales. Ideology, theology and cosmology must arise from accessible historical institutions, lived speech, practices, interpretation and the actor's mediation—not from parental category, nation, hidden simulation tags or the model's moral preferences. The Absolute Horizon is what an actor may actually experience as ultimately binding, and which sacrifices they will justify; it does not imply correct belief, liberation, God score or a predetermined trajectory. A child before possessing such language cannot be given a sophisticated theology as an unmediated state. Loss of an institution may precipitate nihilism, repair, new submission, liberation, or stability. **Contradiction is a legitimate durable result, not an invariant violation.**

**Memory/phenomenal differentiation (N08/N09; O10/O11/O12; E08).** An objective B event may change A's resource situation while remaining unobserved and unremembered. An actor can have a false belief with a real source (misinformation, inference, error), not a fake positive sensory episode. A historically induced current tendency, an episodic memory, a trace/groove and a retrospective self-account are different objects with different clocks, sources and read rights. SCI05 after-call reflection cannot retroactively justify earlier unissued action. V2 can show cues, speech, silence and fallible first-person interpretation; user sees only lawful phenomenal evidence. A correct dramatic summary does not prove a person remembers.

## G. Three scientific/product discriminations the next HQ must be able to adjudicate

**Case 1: Routine care without trauma.** Two independently formed caregivers maintain ordinary meals, transit, repair, and shared play for years. Child A forms stable scoped trust with one caregiver, confidence in a learned practical skill, and cautious respect for a second; an outsider's isolated hostile act occurs later. Why does the same event not necessarily yield a wound? If the system cannot form durable organization from non-dramatic repetition and actor-accessible activity, N02/N06 are absent whatever C9 logs. A full sourcefree scaffold can prove time/order and carried history; only valid original science proves the psychological transition.

**Case 2: Material independent B refusal.** B has scarce hours, work, own obligation and a possible SHARE/REFUSE/SILENCE action. B chooses without reading A's future demand. REFUSE is an actual decision that can change resources/opportunities; SILENCE may leave no authored performed act; neither is the same as missing evaluator. A may see changed tool stock without reading B's mind. Holding B's original prehistory fixed, test distinct physical outcomes, actor access, A interpretation, later performed choice and no retrocausal mutation.

**Case 3: Contradictory political/theological formation.** A person learns solidarity through a church, nation or movement but also absorbs exclusionary hierarchies, performs work in contradictory institutional relations, later meets a credible dissenting actor and undergoes loss. A can distance from church and still use inherited Christian idioms for justice; can resist nation and reproduce familial power, or double down. No program can predeclare emancipation as the outcome. Source-world produces institutions, not individual conviction; historical speech/activities, received experience and reflection must be distinct qualified links. The user should infer contradiction through lawful visible behavior, not be told the character's secret ideological vector.
