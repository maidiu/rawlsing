## III. Why continuity may matter even where explicit events are not remarkable

**Critical distinction:** what is predictable, habitual or unremarkable to the subject can be *formative* without being encoded as a distinct autobiographical episode. It can strengthen the accessibility, perceived reliability, context-specificity and practicability of an expectation. It may teach what need not be thought about or narratively remembered.

A **baseline is not a one-dimensional start value**. Its constitution requires distinguishable dimensions, potentially stored or derivable through established mechanisms:

1. **Reliability / history:** actual repeated conditions under which expectation was upheld; evidence provenance, duration, consistency and counterinstances.
2. **Scope:** relevant people, locations, activities, bodily state, institutional setting, supports and exceptions.
3. **Need-/object-/activity-stakes:** what caring, safety, recognition, continuity, opportunity or action depends on the expectation.
4. **Practical availability:** rehearsed ways of responding, seeking help, withdrawing, challenging, comforting or sustaining activity; with explicit support constraints.
5. **Relational embedding:** who can confirm, violate or repair it; independently differing relations; power, dependence, authority and intimacy.
6. **Conflict and alternative models:** coexistence of competing expectations; what contexts may recruit or inhibit them.
7. **Memory and accessibility:** bodily anticipation, procedural fluency, emotionally charged episode, later episodic reconstruction and articulated self-meaning are different phenomena.

These are not seven mandatory scalar dimensions. `thick` should not be equated with rigid, healthy, high-confidence, universal, correct, consciously held or permanently enduring. Thick caring reliability may make novel surprises *easier* to contextualize. Thick unstable history may yield sensitive, contradictory anticipations. No finite count of 'confirmations' is a universal empirically warranted update formula.

**Baseline acquisition is not restricted to repeatedly LLM-generated highlighted events.** Everyday recurrent participation and interstitial time must have licensed ways to reproduce and consolidate conditions—without arbitrary clock-driven psyche drift or invented invisible episodes.

## IV. Event consequences: proposed type distinctions

Do not equate *event salience*, *episode memory*, *instant affect*, *predictive mismatch*, *persistent expectation*, *developmental reorganization* and *overt action*. An encounter may produce:

- `ASSIMILATION/CONSOLIDATION`: expected event with no noteworthy episode but greater stable practicability/reliability through actual routine;
- `SENSITIZATION`: heightened future susceptibility to a specified configuration without novel explicit doctrine;
- `DIFFERENTIATION`: a previously undistinguished class is separated by situation, relation, object, support or perceived condition;
- `CONTEXTUAL_EXCEPTION`: counterexample registered within or alongside prior stable history; no automatic general inversion;
- `REORGANIZATION`: established source/target relation changes, including which needs, motives, operations, memory, practices, desires and regulators recruit/inhibit one another;
- `DURABLE_CONTRADICTION`: incompatible expectations/commitments co-exist; context switches or simultaneous recruitment may occur without resolution;
- `TRANSIENT/NO_DURABLE_CHANGE`: an intense experience alters current behavior but has no warranted persistent target change;
- `LOSS/NARROWING`: practice or operation becomes less available, including lost social conditions, without claiming it was erased.

These are analytical labels for differentiated causal phenomena, **not new issued CAN011 event enums**. A single encounter can involve several. Nor is a single additive +/− pressure delta an adequate general update law.

**Salience/difference is potentially conditioned by:** mismatch to the relevant expectational domain; novelty of object, context or relation; intensity and duration; need and care stakes; perceived agent responsibility and relationship authority; degrees of agency/control; recurrence; ability of existing regulation to metabolize the interruption; simultaneous bodily/social state; subsequent response and repair. Do not collapse these to one universal salience score with guessed coefficients. 'Disruption' or 'confirmation' must be defined relative to *the child's/current actor's accessible expectation*, not omniscient truth. An event's significance might be reappraised later even if its objective record stays unchanged.

**One-off example:** the child has reliable history of being retrieved and soothed. Being briefly lost, mistaking another person's leg for a parent, being frightened, and subsequently being found and comforted need not reset global trust. It may yield a context-specific fear/recognition, newly articulated 'not everybody is Daddy', stronger reliance on particular caregiving responses, or no future accessible episode. The actual handler's independent conduct and timing matter. No algorithm may always prescribe one of those results from the event tag.

## V. Distinguish needs, desires, pressure, flow, power and love

**Need:** embodied or socially produced dependency/requirement that acquires significance in organized activity; hunger and fatigue coexist with needs for attachment, recognition, purpose and shared activity, but should not be flattened into an incommensurable yet magically conserved pool. **Leontiev** is relevant because activities and their motive-object relations can reorganize needs and generate new ones.

**Desire / motive / striving:** an orientation to an object or possible life, formed and revised through situated practice and relations, not merely a biological appetite or population-assigned type. Separate a meaningful motive from a momentary stimulus and what is institutionally achievable.

**Pressure:** typed objective demands, mismatches, affordances, constraints, competing projects and subjectively experienced urgency **must stay distinguishable**; 'pressure' alone is not a conserved fluid. Multiple pressures may recruit one another, inhibit, amplify, redirect or transform particular action possibilities.

**Flow:** provisional name for current effective engagement, recruitment, coordination or obstruction among activity-related elements, and the existing V3 pressure-routing reservoir. Do not treat historic channel algorithm parameters or 'energy conservation' as proven psychology. Verify original code and meanings function-by-function.

**Power:** the actor's materially situated effective capacity or constraint in relations with others (resources, social rights, dependence, recognitional authority, coercion, opportunities) and perceived power; not a fixed trait or scalar dominance. Other agents possess independent interests and real capacities.

**Love:** lived caring/attachment/commitment and reciprocal or asymmetrical activity with specific other persons; not a one-number positivity multiplier. Caregiving, domination, dependency, repair and conflict can coexist. Present generosity can be sincere yet received as manipulation, with both realities separately causally operative.

## VI. Couple events to the person, not just annotate outcomes

For an encounter involving Nadia and her mother, an ordinary friend interaction changes Nadia's current condition before her mother offers food as apology. Her reception may become wary or seeking, depending on formed history and current context. The mother's intention can be conciliatory independently of Nadia's suspicion; her next conduct must be resolved through her own actor/world constraints. Defensive injury and retaliation could deepen mutual expectations of misrecognition; patient acknowledgment plus actual engagement with Nadia's issue may de-escalate without falsely erasing the dispute. The mother is not scripted to 'teach' Nadia the developer's favored lesson.

The prospective LLM can offer compact **conditional circumstances, feasible independently resolved possibilities and initial element entry points**. Rawlsing must compute actor-limited reception, potentially cyclic recruitment across the whole relevant organization, actual action within social feasibility, and changed/not-changed person/world history. Later LLM narration is conditioned by **the ordered trajectory and admitted memory/meaning**, not final-state free invention. Retrospective meaning that is itself a new causal internal act must be admitted through legitimate computation before it is allowed to influence the following batch.

Do not make random `mood` a precomputed essence. Stochastic daily occurrence/context can be sampled at its permitted grain; the person's current felt response to it must be generated from their history and organizational contingencies.

## VII. Time and stage without arbitrary age ladders

**Event time:** bodily change, reception/anticipation, interacting streams, action and other-agent feedback. **Between-event life:** ongoing rest, work/care, repeated routines, institutional situations, independent others, rehearsal, missed opportunities and physical recovery. **Developmental interval:** formation, context-dependent retention and support changes in operations, expectations, relations and accessibility; contradiction and forgetting. **Life history:** linked lives, material structural changes, accumulated action possibilities and reinterpretations. The user-settled 3–5 events are highlighted anchors, not the entire person's lived time.

Developmental age can change **available participation** and access (care dependence, school institutions, peer organization, literacy, bodily and relational circumstances, wage work, legal constraint), but no universal chronological `age × learning` multiplier or inevitable emotional stage. Newly acquired concept/operation is not an automatic mood readjustment. Likewise an early preverbal history is not a fully articulated ideology; subsequent ideology can recruit, name and reinterpret pre-existing histories without presupposing one-to-one determination.

## IX. What a future warranted computation must distinguish (non-numeric conceptual contract)

For each actor at time t, a source/authority-limited historical organization `P_t`, world/other actors `W_t`, and current activity/received event `E_t` produce:

`uptake(P_t, W_t, E_t)` → simultaneous typed recruitment and regulation → accessible action alternatives and actual conduct jointly resolved with others → objective consequences and actor-specific `impression` → `P_(t+1)` and `W_(t+1)` via authorized transient, persistent and context-specific effects → time/continued activity → next event.

`impression` is neither neutral event data nor necessarily conscious memory. `P_t` is not a bag of unrelated scalars; it contains historically consequential relations. The computation must preserve causal source, mechanism, actor access, scope, support, uncertainty, contradiction and NO_CHANGE. A function's input/output nomenclature alone does not settle the mathematical law.

**First counterfactual question for the future engine** (conceptual, not a claim of executed test): with the same later objective encounter and relevant world conditions, how does earlier *history-specific* conditioning change which expectations, regulators or operations can be recruited, and hence which outcomes are possible? Make sure any difference is caused by a real consumed mediated state or link, not the LLM adding `remembered trauma`, `acquired insight`, or a convenient outcome flag.

- **Luria**, *Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations*, foreword on socially/historically mediated higher functions and conclusion on formation of new psychological systems; cultural-historical changes cannot simply be read as universal child stage law.
- **Sève**, *Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality*, section 'The personality as a living system of social relations between acts', attention to actual activity/time/social relations; no claim all actors socially reduce to identical schema.
- **Dreier**, *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life*, personal trajectories of participation across contexts and ongoing conduct of everyday life; an encounter's significance changes with other sites of participation.
- **Lahire**, *The Plural Actor*, conditional dispositions, inhibition/latency in present contexts and heterogeneous even opposing acquired habits; cautions against reifying a situated act as a trait.
- **Elder**, *Children of the Great Depression*, crisis as person–environment interface and the ways material/resource disruption challenges established routines; no event category determines universal impression.
These are **interpretive source-informed implications**, not an exhaustive forensic scientific review, and no quantitative coefficients have been established from them.
