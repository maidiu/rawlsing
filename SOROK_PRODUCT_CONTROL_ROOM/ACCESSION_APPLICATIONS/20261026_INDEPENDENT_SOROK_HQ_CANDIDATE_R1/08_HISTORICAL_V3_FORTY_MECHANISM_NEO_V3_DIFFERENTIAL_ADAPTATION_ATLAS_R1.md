## I. Challenge, complication, threat: relational diagnoses, not event tags or scalar arousal levels

| Relational process | Causal question | Possible result without foregone developmental moral |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge** | Does the actor face a valued attempt or demand whose fulfillment depends on limited, assisted, untested or newly developing capacity? | Supported achievement, failure, further effort, abandonment, growth in practical availability, no change |
| **Complication** | Does actuality no longer fit a sufficiently differentiated current expectation/operation, introducing an exception, ambiguity or conflicting possibility? | Contextual differentiation, coexisting interpretations, practical confusion, renewed old explanation, deferred contradiction |
| **Threat** | Is a valued dependency, relation, bodily safety, capability, object, position or anticipated future objectively/subjectively endangered? **Do not conflate objective threat with actor-accessible danger.** | Protective/avoidant/appeasing/support-seeking/defiant response, later stabilization, changed vigilance, continued contradiction, no change |
| **Confirmation** | Does the received episode match a relevant expectation, perhaps repeatedly or intensely? | Routine non-salient consolidation, reassurance, increased rigidity, selective reinforcement, little or no durable effect |
| **Repair/containment** | Do independently acting others and actual resources alter the conditions or interpretation of a rupture/threat? | Recontextualized event, new distinction, changed trust, continued injury alongside reliable care, no guaranteed reset |
| **Novelty/disorientation** | Is the existing organization unable to make a difference available, or is an experience unlike the familiar without yet being evaluated positively/negatively? | Curiosity, fear, attention, exploratory play, indifference, confusion or new conceptual capacity |

All can coexist or transition *within* an encounter. An objective challenge can be subjectively threat; a loving act may be received as manipulative; a compelling novelty may be unremembered. Its effect must be relative to actor history, needs, material situation, relationship, accessibility, regulation, present bodily/social condition and action of others. There is no independent psychological law in the labels.

## II. What "thick baseline" entails

An expectation is not simply `entitled/hopeful/forbidden/hopeless`, a confidence or a drive scalar. Distinguish **actual historical reliability**, **scope/contexts/people**, **need and relation stakes**, **means of practical enactment**, **social supports**, **contradictory exceptions**, **felt/bodily/procedural or verbal form**, and **history of repair and renewed refusal**. Distinguish observable routine formation from distinct narrated episodic memory. Reliable caring even amid materially driven absence can establish palpable interpersonal love simultaneously with a durable expectation of worldly insufficiency. An apparently supportive baseline may be more capable of accommodating exceptional disruption; strength is not the same as inability to change.

**Critical anti-fluidity:** unmet longing, deprivation and emotional demand do not have to transfer to a literally conserved quantity or obligatory ideological destination. They may persist as uneasy anticipation, bodily vigilance, a seeking practice, ambivalent care, or an unrealized object. Only later participation, institutions, language, interpretive practices and possible life projects could mediate them into theology/ideology/ontology/cosmology. Later concepts can reinterpret earlier impressions without objective old memories becoming newly accessible. A materially absent yet palpable loving parent must not be coded as `bad attachment` or predetermined theology.

## V. Differential causal example: loving care, material absence, future cosmology

- **World**: caregiver genuinely loves child, does the available care, but work hours and limited resources produce real patterned absence and thwart some care/attention/desires.
- **Child's current experience**: palpable warmth in some contacts and repeated bodily/social insufficiency in others; may learn that love and effective availability do not coincide. Child does not need to have abstract words for employment, class or love for both histories to be formative.
- **Thickened histories**: reliable `mother cares` under particular conditions alongside `wanting does not make help available` or `the world does not give enough`. Not necessarily stable universal beliefs; may become felt orientations, competing needs and practices, context-limited anticipation or latent tensions. Nothing forces one globally negative parent expectation.
- **Later challenge**: a new opportunity tests possibility of asking, insisting, trusting or seeking an outside source. **Complication**: person discovers deprivation arose from unjust institutions while care was real. **Threat**: a particular experience endangers the previously reliable caring relation. Distinct source, stakes and control -> distinct reception.
- **Later conceptualization**: a social institution, practice, text, peer discourse or religious community makes divine provision, abandonment, injustice, liberation, grace, fate or cosmic inadequacy available as interpretive operations. Actor may recruit or contest them with pre-existing felt histories. **No predetermined ideology/theology**, no pressure thermodynamics, no retroactive true infant proposition.
- **Memory/meaning**: adult can narrate a re-interpretation of childhood; objective care patterns, original felt traces, memories and retrospective belief remain distinct. New later self-interpretation can itself become a causal event through the person engine.
