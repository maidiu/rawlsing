**Date:** 2026-10-12

## 0. Mission

Construct the smallest Rawlsing product that preserves the defining interactional proposition:

> **BECOME A NEIGHBOR TO SOMEONE YOU COULD HAVE BEEN.**

while deliberately bounding, fixing, precomputing or deferring every feature whose maximal form could become a white-whale dependency.

This is not permission to fake Rawlsing by removing its constitutive machinery.

The governing question is:

> **What is the cheapest bounded product in which the user still encounters an unchosen, independently caused person through partial perception, actual user-owned action, irreversible history and non-player-centric consequence?**

Then:

> **Which fidelity dimensions can be increased one at a time without making alpha depend on solving the maximal version first?**

Hard law:

`CORE INVARIANT MAY NOT BE BRACKETED`

`FIDELITY MAY BE BRACKETED`

`A BOUNDED ALPHA MUST SAY EXACTLY WHICH HARD PROBLEMS IT FIXES OR EXCLUDES`

## 1. Architectural correction — persistent state need not be destructively summarized

The current Sorok/V3 direction already supports:

- append-only admitted event/history provenance;
- materialized current causal state;
- actor memory/accessibility distinct from objective history;
- relation-specific history/state;
- user-model state;
- actor-model-of-user state;
- thin active runtime projections;
- exact-history retrieval when needed.

Therefore alpha should not treat an LLM-generated summary as the canonical memory substrate.

Preferred pattern:

`APPEND-ONLY CANONICAL EVENT LEDGER`
+ `MATERIALIZED CURRENT STATE`
+ `INDEXES / REFS / CHECKPOINTS`
+ `DERIVED SUMMARIES AS NONAUTHORITATIVE CACHES`.

A summary may optimize retrieval or prompting.

It may not erase or replace the underlying event/provenance record.

This lowers the white-whale risk of longitudinal history at the storage/identity layer.

What remains unproved is efficient retrieval, significance/promotion logic and long-horizon behavioral coherence.

## 2. App-local per-user continuity package

For each user×seed-person continuity, alpha may maintain a local/private package conceptually containing:

1. user-independent root-person/root-world identity refs;
2. branch-local Nadia current causal state;
3. branch-local objective event ledger;
4. branch-local user↔Nadia relation history/state;
5. Nadia's model of this user;
6. Rawlsing's bounded model of the user / attention / praxis where authorized;
7. actor memory/accessibility indices;
8. current material/world deltas;
9. retrieval/index structures;
10. derived prompt/render caches.

The user need not have ordinary gameplay access to hidden state.

The application/orchestrator may provide the model with the exact relevant slices required for the current operation.

Do not architecturally require the remote LLM to possess direct filesystem access.

Hard law:

> **LOCAL PERSISTENCE != WHOLE-STATE REPROMPT.**

The app owns storage and retrieval.

The model consumes bounded projections.

## 3. Shared root versus branch-local truth

Existing shared-root/copy-on-write architecture remains controlling.

### Shared pre-contact canon

May include:
- biography/history;
- pre-contact relationships;
- pre-contact memories;
- material ecology;
- home/world state;
- object provenance;
- routines;
- projects/obligations;
- pre-contact supporting actors.

A user query may trigger unresolved pre-contact completion.

The substantive completion must be conditioned by:
- root-person computational state;
- canonical target key;
- historical/world constraints;
- governed randomness;

not by the querying user's preferences or desired story.

Once admitted and cross-branch-compatible, a pre-contact fact may become shared root canon and therefore discoverable by other user forks through lawful routes.

### Branch-local post-contact truth

After meaningful contact, copy-on-write applies.

A fact caused by or entangled with User A's continuity does not become truth in User B's continuity.

Preserve:

`SHARED ROOT NADIA`
!=
`NADIA × USER-A DESCENDANT`
!=
`NADIA × USER-B DESCENDANT`.

If a truly exogenous fact after T0 is intended to propagate across branches, it requires an explicit shared-exogenous-world owner and compatibility law.

Do not smuggle branch-local events into global canon merely because they are "hard facts."

## 4. Dual-track rule for every dangerous feature

Every major feature should be represented in two columns:

### ALPHA BASELINE — facile but functional

The cheapest implementation that preserves the relevant Rawlsing invariant.

### FIDELITY FRONTIER — maximal / potentially white-whale form

The richer form that may be investigated later without blocking alpha.

No maximal form becomes an alpha prerequisite merely because it is spiritually or aesthetically preferable.

## 5. Minimal-white-whale alpha fidelity matrix

### A. Person generation

**Alpha baseline**
- 3–5 deeply generated and heavily audited seed persons;
- dense pre-contact history/world;
- manually inspectable and repairable root canon before testing;
- fixed starting date/state;
- no requirement that arbitrary new focal persons be generated on demand.

Preserves:
- unchosen person;
- historically produced specificity;
- independent antecedent reality;
- comparison across identical-root forks.

**Fidelity frontier**
- fully automatic arbitrary-person generation at production scale;
- open-ended country/context coverage;
- automatic generation of equivalent depth without manual audit.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### B. World/material particularity

**Alpha baseline**
- bounded neighborhood / household / work-route world;
- deep ahead-of-attention closure around likely interaction surfaces;
- deterministic keyed backfill for unresolved pre-contact particulars;
- actions outside the represented causal envelope may be lawfully abstracted, delayed, clarified or refused rather than spuriously invented.

Preserves:
- independent world;
- discovery;
- causal materialization;
- user curiosity cannot author truth.

**Fidelity frontier**
- arbitrary open-world action across unlimited geography/institutions/objects with seamless lazy expansion.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### C. Natural-language action control

**Alpha baseline**
- SAY / DO / ATTEND;
- open natural language inside a declared causal envelope;
- parser returns explicit clarification when semantic ownership is uncertain;
- compound actions may be decomposed conservatively;
- unsupported large-scope actions are not silently accepted.

Preserves:
- real user semantic contribution;
- no menu-only authorship;
- attempt != outcome;
- parser uncertainty != diegetic resistance.

**Fidelity frontier**
- near-unrestricted natural-language plans, nested contingencies and arbitrarily complex procedural acts with low clarification rate.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### D. History

**Alpha baseline**
- full append-only event ledger for the continuity;
- typed causal provenance;
- materialized Nadia/user/relation state;
- no destructive canonical summarization;
- bounded session/history horizon during early trials;
- exact retrieval available for relevant events.

Preserves:
- irreversibility;
- genuine historical consequence;
- memory != history;
- later interaction can depend on actual prior events.

**Fidelity frontier**
- years of dense interaction with extremely efficient retrieval, compression and reinterpretation.

Alpha does not depend on proving years-long scaling.

### E. Offscreen life

**Alpha baseline**
- bounded discrete agenda scheduler;
- pre-existing obligations/projects/routines;
- small event vocabulary;
- rule/state-driven transitions;
- sparse bounded LLM flesh only at materialization/render points;
- no crisis-generation requirement;
- life advances only across represented domains.

Preserves:
- Nadia does things for reasons not originating in user engagement;
- user absence does not pause her;
- return conditions can change independently.

**Fidelity frontier**
- high-fidelity autonomous synthetic life across arbitrary institutions, relationships and world events.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### F. Supporting actors

**Alpha baseline**
- Tier 0 aggregates;
- Tier 1 incidental actors;
- a very small fixed set of Tier 2/3 supporting actors for each seed person;
- promote only when causal burden actually requires it;
- supporting actors may be pre-generated for alpha rather than lazily solved from scratch.

Preserves:
- Nadia has other people;
- other actors can constrain/cause events;
- user is not sole social cause.

**Fidelity frontier**
- unlimited dynamic social graph with automatic deep-person promotion.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### G. Present-world coupling

**Alpha baseline**
- fixed or periodically updated bounded external context;
- no requirement for continuous real-world event ingestion;
- only a small set of manually/governedly admitted external events where needed.

Preserves:
- historical situatedness enough for alpha.

**Fidelity frontier**
- live broad current-event relevance routing across locations/domains.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### H. Player capability / embodiment

**Alpha baseline**
- medium-native faculties primary;
- simple portaled embodiment envelope;
- bracket or user-select only what is causally necessary;
- no claim of measuring external human skill.

**Fidelity frontier**
- rich homologized capability transposition and optional device/audio/performance modalities.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### I. Adaptive phenomenal lens

**Alpha baseline**
- mostly opinion-resistant narrator;
- simple explicit ATTEND;
- minimal slowly learned recognition/attention priors if needed;
- no requirement for sophisticated personalization.

**Fidelity frontier**
- mature user-specific phenomenal lens, reflexive perturbation, longitudinal attention modeling.

Alpha does not depend on this.

### J. Cross-persona/social/game structure

**Alpha baseline**
- none required.

**Fidelity frontier**
- shared roots, invitations, comparison, repertoire/practice, social ecology.

Alpha viability must stand without it.

## 6. What alpha may not bracket

The following are constitutive and therefore cannot be removed merely to make the prototype easy:

1. an unchosen other;
2. independent antecedent person/world state;
3. partial/phenomenal rather than omniscient access;
4. actual user-owned semantic/attentional contribution;
5. attempt distinct from result;
6. independent actor reception/resistance/nonresponse;
7. irreversible admitted occurrence/history;
8. some later consequence of history;
9. some independent life/agenda not generated solely for the user;
10. user cannot edit Nadia's truth by wording;
11. LLM prose does not directly own canonical truth;
12. no hidden relationship score as the main objective;
13. the person remains non-exhaustible by the user's model.

If alpha removes one of these, it is no longer a valid test of Rawlsing's core proposition.

## 7. Minimal-white-whale product candidate

A lawful early product can therefore be:

- 3–5 deeply pre-generated seed persons;
- opaque/unselected assignment;
- each user receives a copy-on-write continuity;
- one bounded neighborhood/home/work ecology per seed;
- deeply materialized high-likelihood surfaces;
- deterministic keyed pre-contact backfill;
- local/private per-user event ledger + current person/relation/user-model state;
- SAY / DO / ATTEND within a bounded causal envelope;
- conservative parser with clarification;
- a small number of persistent supporting actors;
- sparse agenda/routine/event scheduler for offscreen life;
- natural scene endings;
- messaging only when lawfully established;
- no cross-persona metagame;
- no continuous world-wide simulation;
- no requirement for arbitrary geography;
- no requirement for automatic unlimited person generation;
- no requirement for years-long scaling before alpha.

This is not "fake Rawlsing."

It is a deliberately bounded Rawlsing whose core causal/relational commitments remain present while fidelity-frontier problems are bracketed.

## 8. Maximal-incision evidence program

Do not build the whole bounded alpha before testing the most dangerous seams.

### Gate 0 — executable state kernel

Build an inspectable local state package for one seed Nadia:
- root canon;
- branch state;
- event ledger;
- relation state;
- Nadia model of user;
- user model/attention state;
- exact retrieval refs.

Pass only if no model prose can mutate canonical truth without admission.

### Gate 1 — P1 causal cell

Use one bounded ordinary scene.

Test:
- SAY/DO/ATTEND;
- compound parsing;
- broad-intent causal-neighborhood entry;
- hinge/control return;
- actor-specific perception;
- time;
- materialization;
- no invented user judgment.

Kill/repair if lawful controls still produce interface wrestling or simulated agency.

### Gate 2 — P2 historical return

Use repeated encounters over enough events to require real retrieval.

Test:
- objective history versus memory;
- expectation/convention/access effects;
- wrong interpretations;
- forgetting;
- event ablation;
- no callback theater.

Kill/repair if later difference cannot be traced to admitted prior causes.

### Gate 3 — P3 facile independent life

Use the bounded agenda scheduler, not maximal autonomous simulation.

Test:
- absence;
- independent schedule changes;
- actor-originated contact or noncontact;
- canceled/rescheduled plans;
- ordinary life developments;
- no retention dramaturgy.

Kill/repair if life feels random/content-generated unless high-fidelity simulation is added.

This gate specifically determines whether sparse offscreen life is sufficient or whether the maximal version is becoming a white whale.

### Gate 4 — generated-person substitution

Replace the highly audited fixture/root with an actual current Rawlsing-generated seed product while holding interface/runtime constant.

This isolates:

`INTERACTION ARCHITECTURE QUALITY`
from
`GENERATED PERSON QUALITY`.

Kill/repair if the experience collapses when hand-curation is removed.

### Gate 5 — constrained human alpha

Only after Gates 0–4 produce technical receipts.

Test the proximal theorem:
- does the person cease to feel interchangeable?
- does persistent history matter?
- do users voluntarily return without artificial retention mechanics?
- does independent agenda feel like personhood rather than obstruction?
- are ordinary scenes enough?

A technically valid but experientially dead result is a negative result.

## 9. White-whale escalation protocol

For every fidelity frontier:

1. alpha begins with the bounded form;
2. record what the bounded form cannot represent;
3. gather actual failures attributable to that bound;
4. escalate fidelity only when evidence says the bound is harming the core experience;
5. increase one dimension at a time;
6. rerun previous gates;
7. do not assume maximal fidelity is necessary until bounded fidelity fails for a constitutive reason.

Hard law:

> **DO NOT SOLVE A WHITE WHALE IN ADVANCE OF EVIDENCE THAT RAWLSING NEEDS IT.**

And conversely:

> **DO NOT CALL A BOUND GOOD ENOUGH IF IT REMOVES A CONSTITUTIVE RAWLSING EXPERIENCE.**

## 10. Current revised white-whale map

### Storage/history identity
Risk reduced.

The architecture already permits exact event provenance + materialized state + derived caches.

Remaining risk:
retrieval/significance/long-horizon coherence, not basic persistence.

### Lazy pre-contact truth
Risk reduced.

Shared-root deterministic keyed completion provides a bounded route.

Remaining risk:
constraint compatibility and exact admission behavior.

### Arbitrary open-world expansion
Still a fidelity-frontier risk.

Not alpha-critical if the causal envelope is honestly bounded.

### Natural-language causal parsing
Still material.

Alpha can bound scope and clarify ambiguity.

Need empirical error characterization.

### Independent offscreen life
Risk can be split.

Facile functional version:
bounded agenda/event scheduler.

High-fidelity version:
rich autonomous world/person simulation.

P3 should determine whether alpha needs more than the facile version.

### Generated-person quality
Still material.

Can be isolated by seed-person alpha and later substitution testing.

### Experiential viability
Still existential and cannot be engineered away.
