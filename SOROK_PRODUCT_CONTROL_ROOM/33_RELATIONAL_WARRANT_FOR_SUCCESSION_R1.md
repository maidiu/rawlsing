# Sorok — Relational Warrant for New-Person Succession R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_SUCCESSION_THEOREM__NORMATIVE_CORE_OPEN

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Correction

Historical substance alone is not sufficient to justify introduction of another Rawlsing person.

A relationship may be long, intense and highly specific while still being exploitative, dominating, purely extractive, or otherwise contrary to Rawlsing's purpose.

Therefore separate:

`RELATIONAL SUBSTANCE` — this relation has become historically real/specific;

from

`RELATIONAL WARRANT` — the user's demonstrated praxis in this relation provides sufficient basis for Rawlsing to open another relation within the same continuity.

---

## 1. Warrant is not persona improvement

Do not gate succession on:
- Nadia being 'fixed';
- Nadia being happier;
- affection level;
- gratitude;
- dependency;
- compliance;
- closeness;
- user approval;
- absence of conflict;
- persona flourishing as a single scalar.

Rawlsing is not rewarding successful management of Nadia.

---

## 2. Candidate target

The likely target is something like **relational generativity / capacity to sustain another as a separate center of life**.

Evidence might include:
- the user attends to the actual person rather than only projecting a type;
- the user updates beliefs when evidence resists their model;
- the user can tolerate refusal/separateness without treating it as a broken game;
- the user remembers and responds to specific history;
- the relationship becomes reciprocal rather than purely extractive;
- the user can be affected/corrected by the relation;
- breaches can sometimes produce acknowledgment/repair rather than immediate replacement;
- the user's presence can contribute to possibilities in the persona's life without requiring control over them;
- the user remains capable of disagreement/resistance rather than mere conformity.

These are candidate evidentiary dimensions, not a score.

---

## 3. Warrant must be based on realized praxis

Rawlsing cannot know the user's hidden motive.

Therefore warrant can rely only on:
- realized actions;
- attention allocation;
- inquiry/speech;
- remembered commitments;
- response to correction/refusal;
- repair behavior;
- relationship-specific consequences;
- actor-owned voluntary reciprocity/invitation;
- causal effects where sufficiently supported.

Not:
- claimed empathy;
- stated values;
- self-described goodness;
- inferred secret intention.

---

## 4. No single required relationship shape

Warrant must not require:
- friendship;
- intimacy;
- constant contact;
- romance;
- high attachment;
- agreement;
- the persona liking the user.

A bounded acquaintance or difficult but mutually recognizing relationship could conceivably provide warrant.

The key question is whether the user has demonstrated the sort of **other-person recognition and consequential relational participation** Rawlsing is built to cultivate.

---

## 5. Possible product interpretation

New-person succession can mean:

> the user's current Rawlsing social world has shown enough capacity for another irreducible person to enter without the first becoming a disposable tutorial object.

This is not:
> you passed Nadia.

It is closer to:
> **you have begun to demonstrate that you can carry a relationship as history rather than consume a persona as content.**

---

## 6. Gate should remain non-visible and non-optimizable

Do not display:
- readiness percentage;
- empathy level;
- relational warrant progress;
- checklist of required behaviors.

If the criterion is exposed mechanically, users will optimize to it.

Instead, the invitation simply becomes possible when the constitutional evidence is sufficient.

---

## 7. Persona-owned endorsement may be one contributor, not the whole gate

Nadia voluntarily introducing the user to someone, speaking well of them, or choosing to include them socially is meaningful actor-local evidence.

But it cannot be the sole criterion because:
- persona approval may reflect dependence or domination;
- some healthy/meaningful relations remain low-affection;
- some personas may never make introductions;
- actor judgment is not objective moral truth.

Thus actor endorsement can contribute to relational warrant without defining it.

---

## 8. Relation to flourishing

Court 10 remains necessary.

Relational warrant likely needs some negative constraints against patterns where the user's presence systematically narrows the persona's agency/world while being rewarded as attachment.

But it should not require 'improving' the persona or maximizing any flourishing dimension.

Possible formulation:

`RELATIONAL WARRANT = REALIZED RELATIONAL CAPACITY + SUFFICIENT HISTORY + NO DISQUALIFYING DOMINATION/DESTRUCTION PATTERN`

Exact constitution remains open.

---

## 9. Deep succession theorem

> **A new person should enter not because the previous person has been completed, but because the user's way of relating has become capable of bearing another unresolved human life.**

This is the current best interpretation of the user's `[whatever]`.
