## 0. Problem

Rawlsing needs to distinguish:
- capacities the real user can actually exercise through the medium;
- capacities the medium can partially measure but would distort the game if made central;
- embodied/procedural capabilities that must be simulated or translated;
- structural power that homologous-peer mode must bound;
- claims about inner character that Rawlsing must never pretend to certify.

This cannot be one generic skill-stat system.

## 1. Five authority classes

### A. Medium-native exercised faculties

The user can genuinely perform these through play:
- attention allocation;
- observation targeting;
- inquiry;
- explanation;
- argument;
- joke construction;
- refusal;
- persuasion attempts;
- memory use;
- inference;
- pattern recognition;
- prioritization;
- interpretation;
- tact;
- timing at conversational/social scale;
- restraint;
- deciding when to act or wait;
- model revision;
- social implication management.

These should normally be exercised directly rather than replaced by stats.

### B. Technically measurable external performance

Possible examples:
- reaction time;
- simple motor timing;
- voice/singing audio;
- some pronunciation/acoustic performance;
- perhaps bounded visual/spatial tasks.

These may be measurable, but measurement can be confounded by:
- hardware latency;
- device quality;
- disability;
- age;
- input modality;
- environment/noise;
- accessibility needs.

They may enrich some moments, but should not become Rawlsing's primary skill economy without separate authority.

### C. Embodied/procedural capability

Examples:
- cooking technique;
- strength;
- running;
- driving;
- musical performance;
- repair skill;
- hand-eye coordination;
- profession-specific procedure.

The text medium generally cannot authenticate these from ordinary play.

They require a transposition/setup/simulation rule.

### D. Structural position / resource power

Examples:
- purchasing power;
- housing security;
- transport access;
- institutional leverage;
- occupational rank;
- legal privilege;
- network reach.

These are not free self-reported buffs in main homologous-peer mode.

They are structurally homologized or otherwise governed by the local-field transposition constitution.

### E. Forbidden pseudo-capabilities / essence claims

Rawlsing should not create or certify generic stats such as:
- Empathy 8;
- Good Person;
- Trustworthiness;
- Charisma as universal effect;
- Cultural Competence score;
- Sincerity stat;
- moral worth.

Those are either actor-relative outcomes, longitudinal behavior patterns or inaccessible inner realities.

## 2. Central law

> THE MORE CENTRAL A CAPACITY IS TO RAWLSING'S RELATIONAL PURPOSE, THE MORE IT SHOULD BE EXERCISED DIRECTLY THROUGH PLAY RATHER THAN REPRESENTED AS A CHARACTER STAT.

This extends the existing medium-native agency theorem.

## 3. Capability transposition is dimension-specific

For each non-native capability, use the Court 8 operations:
- CARRY;
- HOMOLOGIZE;
- BRACKET;
- USER-SELECT.

Do not assume one default for all capabilities.

Examples:

### Cooking
Some conceptual/practical knowledge may carry because it is actually part of the user's present mind.

But fine motor execution, timing and technique are not directly established by text input.

### U.S. corporate law
Some reasoning habits may carry.
Jurisdiction-specific authority, credential power and procedural competence may require heavy homologization or bracketing.

### Driving
Some skill may carry, but local road rules, vehicle familiarity and local legal/institutional meaning may differ.

### Strength
Depends on embodiment translation; cannot simply be imported as a verbal claim without deciding what body crosses the portal.

### Language
Needs separate treatment across comprehension, vocabulary/grammar, accent, pragmatic fluency, cultural reference and translation assistance.

## 4. "Portaled capability" is not yet one system

Earlier shorthand such as "portaled cooking ability" is too coarse.

A better formulation:

> when the user delegates embodied/procedural execution that the medium cannot directly test, Rawlsing needs some governed execution prior representing what the transposed player-body can plausibly do.

But the source of that prior is unresolved.

Possible sources include:
- carried actual-user capability where the design can reasonably preserve it;
- homologized local equivalent;
- bounded user-selection;
- simulation default;
- later demonstrated in-world history.

Do not yet adopt:
USER SAYS "I AM GOOD AT X" -> HIGH X STAT.

## 5. Self-report is representation authority, not verification

If a future mode permits the user to declare a capability, the system must preserve:

USER SELF-REPORT / RUN DECLARATION
!= VERIFIED EXTERNAL-HUMAN CAPABILITY.

A declaration may lawfully define part of the continuity's represented embodiment if the relevant court authorizes it.

It may not be reflected back as proof about the external person.

## 6. Structural power is already constrained

Homologous-peer mode prevents self-report from minting unlimited structural advantage.

The user cannot simply declare:
- billionaire resources;
- diplomatic immunity;
- limitless transport;
- private security;
- unlimited housing/medical/legal leverage.

Main-mode structural position remains locally comparable/homologous to the generated field position.

Compact:

> SELF-AUTHORED PERSONAL CAPABILITY, WHERE ALLOWED, DOES NOT OVERRIDE HOMOLOGOUS STRUCTURAL POSITION.

## 7. Optional embodied micro-input

Some externally measurable performances may be worth admitting because real bodily contingency affects relationships.

Example:
Nadia tosses the user keys.

Possible implementation modes:
- simulated embodied catch;
- assisted/default resolution;
- optional real reaction-time input.

If the real user misses:
- that is a lawful event;
- Nadia may form an impression;
- the user may protest, joke, confess clumsiness or dislike the gesture;
- later relational history may incorporate the event.

However:
- the product should not convert occasional embodied tests into a reflex-game meta;
- accessibility/device differences must remain governed;
- actor perception of performance remains distinct from system truth about the external user.

Candidate future mode principle:

> EMBODIED PERFORMANCE INPUT MAY ENRICH THE CAUSAL FIELD; IT MUST NOT COLONIZE THE CORE SKILL ECONOMY.

## 8. Audio / singing as a model case

Microphone input could, in principle, provide measurable acoustic evidence such as:
- pitch/timing stability;
- volume;
- breathiness;
- vocal range;
- some timbral descriptors.

But:
- aesthetic "goodness" is not purely objective;
- device/noise conditions matter;
- Nadia's valuation remains actor-local.

Therefore a lawful chain would be:

ACTUAL AUDIO PERFORMANCE
→ BOUNDED ACOUSTIC FEATURES / EVIDENCE
→ NADIA'S OWN MUSICAL EXPERIENCE + TASTE + CONTEXT
→ NADIA'S REACTION.

A technically competent user may still be disliked, mocked, misunderstood or ignored.

## 9. Cooking as mixed authority

A user can genuinely supply:
- meal choice;
- ingredients;
- seasoning;
- heat level;
- timing intention;
- doneness criterion;
- tool choice;
- prioritization;
- memory of restrictions;
- decision to ask/not ask;
- social handling of mistakes.

The world/system may still need to resolve:
- fine motor execution;
- burner response;
- heat transfer;
- actual physical result;
- accidental spill/burn;
- cookware/table effects;
- interruption.

Example:

User:
> I add paprika and lemon to the broccoli, cook it on medium-high until it just starts to soften, then pull the cast iron to the table.

This is rich user-authored causal content.

If the pan is dangerously hot for the wood, Nadia may intervene.

The next user response is genuine social play.

No cooking minigame is required for the moment to be meaningful.

## 10. Capability should fill delegated procedure, not replace judgment

Where a capability prior exists, its best role is usually:

> governing the quality/reliability of procedural execution the user has explicitly or implicitly delegated,

not:

> awarding a general success bonus.

Thus a high cooking prior may help determine routine knife/heat execution when unspecified.

It does not choose:
- what to cook;
- whether to respect a restriction;
- whether to use expensive ingredients;
- whether to ask;
- whether to place a hot pan on wood.

Those remain user/world decisions where causally live.

## 11. Capability authority matrix — current direction

### Directly exercised
Use real user input.
Do not replace with stats.

### Partially measurable but peripheral
Optional/modal.
Do not make load-bearing without strong evidence.

### Embodied/procedural
Resolve via explicit transposition/simulation authority.
Do not pretend text proves the external skill.

### Structural power
Homologize/bound under main-mode field position.

### Inner moral/psychological essence
Do not score or certify.

## 12. Open questions

Still unresolved:
- whether self-reported skills are default, optional-mode only or disallowed in main mode;
- whether self-report increases identification or cheapens realism;
- which skills carry versus homologize;
- whether any real-time motor/audio tests belong in alpha;
- how capability priors are represented without RPG statification;
- how accessibility mode preserves equivalent causal dignity;
- how much capability variation materially affects actor models.

## 13. Strongest current theorem

> Rawlsing should maximize direct exercise of faculties the medium can authentically carry, govern simulated embodiment separately for capacities the medium cannot verify, keep structural power under homologous-field authority, and refuse pseudo-stats that would replace the very relational cognition the game exists to exercise.
