# Sorok — ATTEND Materialization, Disclosure, and Actor Testimony R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_DIRECTION__Q_LAZY_Q_FLESH_RELEVANT`

Date: 2026-10-03

## 0. Problem

Rawlsing needs ATTEND to be genuinely rewarding.

Closer attention should reveal more detail.

But the following must not collapse:

`RAWLSING GENERATES MORE TRUTH`

`USER CAN PERCEIVE MORE`

`USER NOW KNOWS THE HIDDEN HISTORY`

`NADIA KNOWS THE HIDDEN HISTORY`

`NADIA TELLS THE USER THE HIDDEN HISTORY`.

The intended design is:

> **Attention should pull the simulation's materialization frontier forward faster than it pulls the user's epistemic frontier forward.**

The world becomes deeper because the user approaches it.

The user receives only what the current epistemic route actually makes available.

---

# 1. Core noncollapse

Preserve:

`CANONICAL WORLD / OBJECT TRUTH`
!= `USER PERCEPTUAL ACCESS`
!= `USER ACQUIRED KNOWLEDGE`
!= `USER INFERENCE`
!= `ACTOR KNOWLEDGE / MEMORY / BELIEF`
!= `ACTOR TESTIMONY / EXPRESSION`.

This means a fact may be:
- true and generated;
- inaccessible to the user;
- inaccessible to Nadia;
- known to Nadia but undisclosed;
- falsely remembered by Nadia;
- known by Nadia's child instead;
- lied about by Nadia;
- inferred correctly by the user from evidence;
- inferred incorrectly by the user despite accurate visible detail.

---

# 2. Two different frontiers

## A. Canonical materialization frontier

How much world/person/object truth Rawlsing has actually resolved.

This may include:
- physical detail;
- hidden physical structure;
- provenance;
- acquisition history;
- repair history;
- appearance in prior events/photos;
- actor knowledge/memory relation;
- objective causal contribution;
- relevant third-party knowledge.

This frontier should normally remain a bounded distance **ahead** of what the user currently knows.

## B. User epistemic/disclosure frontier

What the user can presently acquire through:
- current sensory access;
- physically lawful inspection/manipulation;
- remembered prior observation;
- actor testimony;
- third-party testimony;
- records/photos/documents they actually encounter;
- inference.

The two frontiers move together but do not coincide.

Compact:

> **MATERIALIZATION DEPTH MAY ADVANCE BEYOND DISCLOSURE DEPTH.**

---

# 3. ATTEND semantics

ATTEND has two simultaneous effects.

## 3.1 User-facing acquisition

ATTEND deepens lawful perception of the target.

Example:

ambient room frame:
> sandals by the front door.

Closer ATTEND:
> old brown Birkenstocks.

Closer lawful inspection:
> uneven heel wear, darkened cork, one strap repaired.

These are properties available through inspection.

ATTEND should be expected to reveal this kind of increasing specificity.

## 3.2 Hidden closure/materialization

The same ATTEND may cause Rawlsing to materialize still-deeper truth **without disclosing it**.

For the Birkenstocks, Rawlsing might now resolve:
- when/how Nadia acquired them;
- whether somebody gave them to her;
- who repaired the strap;
- whether they appear in a canonical Guatemala photograph;
- whether she owns newer shoes;
- what Nadia remembers about them;
- what she misremembers;
- whether her child knows a relevant story;
- whether the shoes have any substantial developmental significance at all.

The user gets none of this merely because the engine generated it.

Compact:

> **ATTEND TRIGGERS DEEPER CLOSURE; ACCESS GOVERNS DISCLOSURE.**

---

# 4. Bounded causal overhang

Rawlsing should not eagerly precompute every detail of every object.

Instead maintain a rolling **causal/material overhang**.

Conceptually:

`USER FRONTIER = N`

then resolve enough canonical truth at roughly:

`N + bounded overhang`

plus any deeper dependencies required to keep the newly materialized facts causally coherent.

The overhang need not be a literal fixed number of levels.

It may depend on:
- object significance already established by world/person history;
- causal dependencies;
- likely near-term inspectability;
- cost;
- replay needs;
- cross-object consistency;
- active actor knowledge/memory.

Strong rule:

> **The engine should know somewhat more than the user currently can, but not infinitely more than the current causal neighborhood requires.**

---

# 5. Truth depth is not an access ladder

Earlier L0–L5 language can be misread as:

`ATTEND HARDER → L1 → L2 → L3 → L4 → L5`.

Reject that interpretation.

Physical inspection may expose L1/L2 material.

But:
- acquisition history may require testimony or documentary evidence;
- Nadia's memory requires Nadia's actor state and voluntary/causal expression;
- objective causal contribution may be unavailable to every actor;
- relational significance may emerge only over later interaction;
- a third party may know what Nadia does not.

Therefore the truth strata classify **what exists / has been resolved**, not what the player is entitled to receive.

# 6. The Birkenstock sequence

## Scene entry

User is in kitchen with line of sight into living/front-door area.

Narrator may surface:

> sandals by the front door.

Behind the surface Rawlsing may already know:
- brown Birkenstocks;
- old;
- worn unevenly;
- strap repair;
- exact placement/orientation.

It may also already know or now lazily resolve some hidden provenance.

## User approaches / ATTENDs

Now perceptual conditions permit:

> old brown Birkenstocks, cork darkened with wear, one heel more worn than the other, one strap repaired.

This is not a "lore reward."

It is the lawful payoff of looking closely.

At this moment the hidden closure frontier may deepen further.

## User sees an old photo later

If the canonical photo really contains the Birkenstocks and the feet are perceptually available:

> Nadia is wearing the same pair.

That is now lawful user knowledge because the user encountered the evidence.

The photo does not narrate:
> these shoes have followed Nadia through eleven years of reinvention.

That is an interpretation.

## User asks Nadia

User:
> "How long have you had those?"

The system does not query canonical object truth and hand it to Nadia.

Instead:

`QUESTION EVENT`
+ `NADIA ACTOR-LOCAL MEMORY / BELIEF / ACCESS`
+ `CURRENT RELATION / MOTIVE / CONTEXT`
→ CAN-008 R2
→ response.

She may say:
- "Forever. I don't remember."
- "Since Guatemala, I think."
- "My friend gave them to me."
- "Why are you interrogating my shoes?"
- an incomplete version;
- a mistaken version;
- a deliberately false version.

The fact that canonical truth exists does not make Nadia omniscient or honest.

# 7. Actor testimony is an event, not a truth API

Strong law:

> **ASKING AN ACTOR DOES NOT TURN THE ACTOR INTO THE NARRATOR'S DATABASE INTERFACE.**

Actor speech should be downstream of:
- actor perception;
- actor memory/access;
- actor belief/model;
- current affect/recruitment;
- social motive;
- privacy;
- playfulness;
- deception;
- confusion;
- effort;
- relationship state.

Thus Nadia can:
- know and reveal;
- know and conceal;
- know and lie;
- partly know;
- misremember;
- infer;
- make something up;
- refuse.

Third parties behave identically under their own epistemic state.

A child may know something Nadia forgot.

A friend may confidently tell a wrong version.

Another actor's statement establishes:
`THEY SAID X`
not automatically
`X IS WORLD TRUTH`.

---

# 8. Actor visibility of ATTEND

ATTEND is embodied.

If the user spends noticeable time inspecting Nadia's shoes, Nadia or another actor may perceive that.

Therefore:

`USER ATTEND`
may itself become
`ACTOR-PERCEIVABLE EVENT`.

This can produce:
- Nadia volunteering information;
- joking;
- discomfort;
- irritation;
- curiosity;
- concealment;
- no reaction because she never noticed.

Crucially, actor volunteering is caused by the actor's perception/appraisal of the user's attention, not by the engine deciding:
> the player earned the next lore packet.

---

# 9. Why this avoids both failure modes

## Failure A — dead inspection

`ATTEND → no extra specificity`.

This makes the world feel thin and attention pointless.

Reject.

## Failure B — lore vending

`ATTEND → significant backstory automatically disclosed`.

This makes every object a planted clue.

Reject.

Desired loop:

`ATTEND`
→ more perceptual specificity
→ deeper hidden closure
→ more evidence now and later
→ possible actor reaction
→ possible question/testimony
→ user inference
→ later cross-context pattern recognition.

This means:

> **The reward for attention is a denser reality, not an authorial explanation.**

And:

> **The player puts pieces together because the pieces become increasingly concrete, not because the narrator marks them as meaningful.**

# 10. Relationship to significance

Meaning can emerge from accumulated specificity.

Example:

- old Birkenstocks;
- same shoes in Guatemala photo;
- still present after grad school;
- newer pair mostly untouched;
- Nadia gives conflicting explanations;
- her daughter remembers her always wearing them;
- Nadia laughs at the old travel phase;
- some user-observed continuity cuts across several self-retcons.

Nothing requires Rawlsing to state:
> the shoes symbolize autobiographical continuity.

But the user may develop that interpretation.

Nadia may reject it.

Years later she may partly adopt it.

That later interpretive event can itself become causal.

Thus:

`DETAIL → EVIDENCE → PATTERN POSSIBILITY → INTERPRETATION`

not:

`ATTEND → PREAUTHORED MEANING`.

# 11. Engineering consequence

This architecture likely needs distinct receipts/state for:

1. **canonical materialization**
   - fact ID;
   - effective time;
   - provenance;
   - resolution cause/trigger;
   - user-query semantic independence;
   - root/branch scope;

2. **perceptual availability**
   - what could currently be sensed;

3. **user acquisition**
   - what was actually surfaced/observed;

4. **actor epistemic state**
   - knowledge/memory/belief/accessibility;

5. **actor expression**
   - what was actually said/shown/concealed;

6. **user inference**
   - if represented, keep distinct from truth.

Do not infer that this requires six new monolithic owners.

Apply SPD-0232:
derive/compose through existing authoritative owners before reifying new state.
