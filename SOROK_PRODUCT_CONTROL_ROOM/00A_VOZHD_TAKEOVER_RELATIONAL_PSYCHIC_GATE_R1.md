### Traits

### Needs

Avoid assuming a need is only a scalar.

Ask:
- from whom?
- under what role?
- at what anticipated cost?
- with what history?
- with what meaning?

Recognition from a child, parent, lover, rival, institution, or stranger is not automatically interchangeable.

### Defenses

A defense can be historically relational:
- silence preserves belonging;
- over-helping secures indispensability;
- contempt restores threatened hierarchy;
- refusal to ask protects against anticipated humiliation.

Do not invent these after the fact. They require generated developmental parentage.

### Isolation

Isolation is not zero relation.

A person alone may remain organized by:
- absent loved ones;
- feared observers;
- dead parents;
- excluded communities;
- unavailable caregivers;
- imagined audiences;
- institutions that reject them.

### Self-knowledge

PFF already says self-knowledge is partial.

Sorok adds that what becomes thinkable about the self may itself depend on relationship:
- anger becomes nameable with someone safe enough to survive it;
- dependence can feel like love in one relation and shame in another;
- play can emerge where silliness is not punished;
- fear may become conceptually accessible only after repeated non-dominating reception.

### Intimacy

Intimacy is not:
> more hidden lore unlocked.

It may change:
- what needs become permissible;
- what expectations become reasonable;
- what protest becomes possible;
- what defenses are needed;
- what dependence/play can emerge;
- what new dyadic habits get built.

Thus intimacy changes the **causal organization of the relation**.
