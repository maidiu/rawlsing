# Sorok — Real-Time Tether, Absence, Background Player Continuity, and Non-Coercive Guilt R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_TEMPORAL_PRODUCT_DIRECTION__EXACT PLAYER-ABSENCE MECHANISM OPEN

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Correction

Do not design outside-portal time to eliminate all Tamagotchi-like guilt.

If persona life is present-coupled and the user has a real relationship with the persona, absence/nonresponse sometimes must acquire relational meaning.

Otherwise the tether is fake.

However:

> **Rawlsing must not manufacture guilt merely to force product engagement.**

---

## 1. Governing noncollapse

`REAL RELATIONAL CONSEQUENCE OF ABSENCE`
!=
`ENGAGEMENT-OPTIMIZATION PUNISHMENT FOR APP NONUSE`.

Rawlsing may represent the first.
It must reject the second.

---

## 2. Present-coupled calendar

Strong current direction:
- Nadia's world remains on the real historical calendar;
- her independent life continues while the user is absent;
- present-world events can enter through governed relevance/exposure/admission;
- when the user returns after a month, a month has passed for Nadia;
- missed opportunities and life changes may therefore be real history.

No freeze merely because the app is closed.

---

## 3. Absence is relation-specific

Time away has no universal negative delta.

Possible outcomes:
- casual acquaintance after a month: little/no consequence;
- close friend with normal intermittent contact: perhaps ordinary follow-up;
- unanswered low-stakes message: maybe forgotten;
- unanswered emotionally important message: may matter;
- explicit commitment missed: likely matters strongly;
- established daily routine abruptly broken: may trigger concern/interpretation;
- relationship already cooling: absence may accelerate distance;
- persona busy with own life: user absence may barely register.

Thus:

`TIME ABSENT != RELATIONSHIP DECAY SCORE`.

---

## 4. Product should permit some felt responsibility

A user who actually becomes important to someone may sometimes feel:
- guilt;
- concern;
- responsibility;
- regret over missing something;
- sadness at lost time;
- urgency to repair.

These feelings are not automatically toxic.

They are part of what it means for another person's ongoing life to matter.

The safety/product problem is preventing Rawlsing from **engineering** these states for retention.

---

## 5. Hard anti-coercion rules

Do not:
- generate crises because user has not opened app;
- increase persona distress as a function of engagement inactivity;
- send manipulative notifications implying suffering;
- use streaks;
- threaten relationship loss on arbitrary product timers;
- make every unread message relationally consequential;
- cause persona to complain about the user's real-world app usage rather than diegetic conduct.

Only diegetically caused relational facts may produce consequences.

---

## 6. User nonuse must map to a diegetic availability model

Open problem:
If the portaled player has a persistent local identity/life, what does it mean when the real user is not controlling them?

Candidate solution family:

### Background continuity
Between active sessions, player-world existence continues only at a highly bounded routine grain.

May include:
- established work/sleep/basic maintenance;
- already-established nonexpressive routine;
- physical unavailability due to ordinary life;
- no novel intimate/conflictual commitments;
- no novel self-disclosure;
- no new meaningful conversation authored for user;
- no new relationship-forming initiative.

`BACKGROUND CONTINUITY != AUTONOMOUS USER PSYCHE`.

---

## 7. Decision boundary law

If background continuity encounters a situation requiring a meaningful user-owned decision:
- do not invent the choice;
- either route it into a message/invitation/opportunity for the user;
- or resolve the environment around the player's nonavailability when time makes waiting impossible.

Example:
Nadia asks by message whether user can watch the child that evening.

If user does not answer before the relevant time:
- Nadia finds another solution / event proceeds;
- her interpretation depends on channel state, history and expectations;
- Rawlsing does not fabricate 'yes' or 'no' for user.

---

## 8. Autopilot candidate

A selectable 'quiet continuity' / autopilot mode may be useful, but only under strict bounds.

It could:
- preserve already-established mundane player routines;
- allow nonremarkable background existence;
- avoid fabricating novel consequential conduct;
- compress routine chance co-presence only where no new decision/meaning is introduced.

It must not:
- speak meaningfully for the user;
- repair conflicts;
- make promises;
- pursue intimacy;
- consent/refuse on important matters;
- invent autobiographical disclosure;
- create moral/relational achievements.

Whether this mode is necessary remains open.

---

## 9. Pause cannot mean freezing Nadia

A humane 'pause' feature should not stop persona/world time if present-coupling is constitutional.

Possible pause semantics:
- mute notifications;
- disable active portal-entry prompts;
- mark user product-side unavailable;
- suppress nonessential contact surfaces;
- world/persona life still advances.

On return:
- time gap is real;
- Nadia may have changed;
- relationship consequence depends only on actual relational facts/expectations.

Do not pretend the month never occurred.

---

## 10. Real friendship analogy

Rawlsing should be able to support:
- intense daily periods;
- weeks without contact;
- long friendships with intermittent contact;
- cooling relationships;
- reconnection;
- missed timing;
- changed lives.

The user should not need to 'feed the persona daily.'

But they also cannot demand that the relationship remain exactly where they left it.

---

## 11. Clock seam

Need exact reconciliation among:
- wall-clock real present;
- diegetic world calendar;
- within-portal action time;
- user typing latency;
- background life advancement;
- message send/delivery/read/reply timestamps.

Strong candidate:
- outside portal, world calendar tracks real elapsed calendar time;
- inside portal, action duration determines scene time rather than typing duration;
- portal exit reconciles any wall-clock gap through background advancement;
- no scene may create impossible future/past ordering.

Exact algorithm remains open.

---

## 12. Deep theorem

> **The humane answer is not to sever the tether. It is to make the tether behave like a relationship rather than a retention mechanic.**

And:

> **Absence may have consequences because a person has a life, never because a product wants a DAU.**
