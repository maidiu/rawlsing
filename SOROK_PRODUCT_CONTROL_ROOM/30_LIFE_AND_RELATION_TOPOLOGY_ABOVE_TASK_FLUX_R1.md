# Sorok — From Task Flux to Life-and-Relation Topology R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_SCOPE_SYNTHESIS__NEXT_PRODUCT_LAYER

Date: 2026-09-29

## 0. Correction

The recent D002 task-entry / affordance-reveal work governs **how the user navigates being thrown into the flux of another person's world**.

It is not the complete game.

Laundry, groceries, dinner, homework, errands and practical help are accoutrement and causal substrate for the deeper product:

> **getting to know a particular generated person through life and relation over time.**

---

## 1. Highest-level product loop

Candidate long-horizon loop:

`ENCOUNTER`
→ `ATTEND`
→ `INQUIRE / TALK / ACT`
→ `FORM HYPOTHESES`
→ `PERSONA DISCLOSES / WITHHOLDS / MISDIRECTS / INVITES / REFUSES`
→ `SHARED ACTIVITY / EVENT / CONFLICT / ORDINARY LIFE`
→ `REMEMBER / REINTERPRET`
→ `CHANGED ACCESS / EXPECTATION / POSSIBILITY`
→ `NEW PART OF LIFE BECOMES REACHABLE OR REMAINS CLOSED`
→ repeat over months/years.

The key object is not task completion.

It is **historically earned relational access to an independent life**.

---

## 2. Nadia has a life, not content buckets

The generated person may have:
- job / work relationships;
- child / household obligations;
- religion / ritual / community;
- friends / relatives / neighbors;
- interests / hobbies;
- opinions / politics / moral commitments;
- habits / preferences / irritations;
- health / energy constraints;
- aspirations / projects;
- private history;
- places they frequent;
- practical competencies;
- pleasures;
- unresolved conflicts;
- things they avoid;
- things they care about but rarely discuss.

These are not quest categories.

They are causal domains of an independent life.

---

## 3. Access is relationally mediated

The user should not automatically receive all domains at game start.

Different life domains become accessible through different routes:
- incidental observation;
- direct question;
- repeated ordinary contact;
- practical cooperation;
- conflict;
- accidental disclosure;
- invitation;
- third-party introduction;
- shared interest;
- ritual/community participation;
- trust in a specific domain;
- necessity/crisis;
- persona choosing to disclose.

Thus:

`PERSONA LIFE EXISTS != USER HAS ACCESS`.

---

## 4. Invitation is a major relational event type

If a relationship develops, Nadia may begin to invite the user into parts of life that were previously unavailable.

Examples:
- come to a family meal;
- attend a religious/community event;
- accompany her to a market/appointment;
- join a hobby/activity;
- meet a friend/relative;
- help with a practical task;
- come along to a child's event;
- participate in a neighborhood/community obligation;
- stay for tea instead of leaving;
- ask for advice;
- ask for presence rather than help.

Invitation is not a reward token.

It must arise from:
- current purpose;
- access/permission;
- relationship history;
- actor preference;
- practical relevance;
- network context;
- timing.

NO INVITATION remains lawful.

---

## 5. Conversation is not exposition delivery

The user learns Nadia primarily through ordinary dialogic processes:
- asking;
- listening;
- following up;
- remembering prior details;
- noticing contradiction;
- asking at the wrong time;
- changing subject;
- joking;
- disagreeing;
- pressing too hard;
- backing off;
- waiting;
- returning later;
- sharing something of themselves;
- misunderstanding;
- being corrected.

Nadia should not answer every good question fully.

Possible outcomes:
- detailed answer;
- partial answer;
- practical answer;
- joke;
- evasion;
- refusal;
- change of subject;
- answer that raises more questions;
- sincere but causally inaccurate self-explanation;
- different answer years later after reinterpretation.

---

## 6. Disclosure is actor action, not unlock loot

Disclosure depends on:
- what Nadia currently knows/accesses about herself;
- what she thinks the user can understand;
- privacy norms;
- relevance;
- emotional cost;
- role expectations;
- current time/attention;
- relationship-specific expectations;
- whether she wants to be known in that way.

Therefore:

`CLOSENESS != MORE LORE`.

Greater intimacy may produce:
- more candor;
- more conflict;
- more ordinary unguarded presence;
- greater permission to ask;
- stronger refusal;
- more practical dependence;
- more silence that does not need filling.

---

## 7. Shared activity is a carrier of relation

Tasks matter because they create situations where:
- attention is divided;
- conversation happens sideways;
- habits become visible;
- competence/incompetence matters;
- preferences surface;
- third parties enter;
- time pressure exists;
- help can be accepted/refused;
- ordinary knowledge accumulates;
- the user sees the persona outside direct self-presentation.

Thus chores/work/help are **relational carriers**, not the destination.

---

## 8. Life domains should have independent continuity

If Nadia has a job, that job should generate:
- schedule;
- coworkers/supervisors;
- demands;
- stories;
- conflict;
- fatigue;
- opportunities;
- changes;

whether or not the user asks about it.

If religion matters, it may generate:
- practice;
- calendar;
- community;
- obligations;
- language;
- disagreement;
- comfort;
- identity;

without becoming a religion tutorial.

If she has interests, she may pursue them even if the user does not share them.

---

## 9. User interest can change the relationship without summoning content

If the user asks repeatedly about one part of Nadia's life:
- Nadia may appreciate it;
- find it intrusive;
- notice the interest;
- invite the user further in;
- decide they misunderstand it;
- get bored;
- begin asking the user analogous questions;
- route future conversation toward that domain.

But the world should not manufacture dramatic material merely because the user clicked the domain repeatedly.

---

## 10. Knowing the person is not completion

There is no exhaustive Nadia completion state.

Even after years:
- unknown history remains;
- inaccessible memory remains;
- self-understanding changes;
- new life events create new person-state;
- old interpretations are revised;
- private relationships remain partly opaque;
- the user can still be surprised.

Therefore:

`KNOWING SOMEONE != COMPLETING THEIR DATA`.

---

## 11. Relationship progression as topology change

Progress can be modeled partly as changing **reachable parts of life** rather than scalar closeness.

Examples:
- user goes from hallway contact → kitchen presence;
- kitchen presence → practical help;
- practical help → child interaction;
- child interaction → school-event invitation;
- casual talk → discussion of work conflict;
- work stories → being introduced to coworker;
- religious reference → invitation to attend something;
- shared joke → recurring ritual;
- conflict → boundary becomes explicit;
- repair → permission to re-enter a previously closed domain.

These are graph/topology changes in access, expectation and history.

---

## 12. Persona chooses relation too

Critical law:

> **The user does not unlock Nadia; Nadia also chooses what relation becomes possible.**

She may:
- like the user;
- dislike them;
- use them instrumentally;
- trust them in one domain only;
- invite them into one sphere and exclude them from another;
- become dependent;
- remain bounded;
- cool off;
- reconnect;
- choose someone else;
- prioritize family/work/community.

---

## 13. Broader-scope product question

After entry-layer interaction mechanics are sufficient, the next design problem is:

> **What long-horizon life topology, invitation/disclosure logic, domain continuity and relational access mechanics make the user feel they are actually coming to know an independent person over years?**

This should become the next product layer above D002 task navigation.

---

## 14. Candidate macro architecture

Layer 1 — **ENTRY / FLUX NAVIGATION**
- SAY / DO / ATTEND;
- task-entry reveal;
- physical/social feasibility;
- time;
- interruptions;
- immediate actor response.

Layer 2 — **RELATIONAL ACCESS / CONVERSATION**
- inquiry;
- disclosure;
- refusal;
- invitation;
- shared activity;
- remembered details;
- domain-specific expectation;
- changing permissions.

Layer 3 — **INDEPENDENT LIFE DOMAINS**
- work;
- family;
- religion/community;
- interests;
- projects;
- health/material constraints;
- other relationships.

Layer 4 — **LONGITUDINAL HISTORY**
- routines;
- commitments;
- conflicts;
- repair;
- changing access;
- network embedding;
- absence;
- life transitions;
- relationship phases.

Layer 5 — **REFLECTION**
- what the user learned;
- what they repeatedly did;
- where their model changed;
- what remained unknowable.

---

## 15. Current deepest product theorem

> **Rawlsing does not give the user a persona to interrogate. It gives them a life beside a person, and the person becomes knowable only insofar as life and relation make them knowable.**

And:

> **Tasks are where relation happens; relation is what the game is about.**
