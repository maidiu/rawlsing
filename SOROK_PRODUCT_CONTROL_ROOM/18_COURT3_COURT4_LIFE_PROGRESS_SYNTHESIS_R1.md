# Sorok Courts 3–4 — Life Outside the User + Success Without a Score R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_IDEATION__NOT_ADOPTED__CROSS_CUTTING_S4_S6_S10_S11_S15

Date: 2026-09-29

## 0. Holding

A live Rawlsing person must have a life that causally advances without the user.

But 'independent life' does not require continuous expensive LLM consciousness simulation.

Likewise, a relationship game needs readable progression without reducing relationship to a score or equating closeness with benefit.

These two problems are coupled: the relationship becomes meaningful partly because it occupies only one place inside a larger life.

---

## 1. Hybrid event-driven life advancement

Strong current hypothesis:

Persona life should advance through a hybrid event-sourced scheduler rather than continuous turn-by-turn LLM simulation.

Candidate ingredients:
- projects/goals;
- obligations;
- routines;
- household responsibilities;
- work/institution schedules;
- other relationships;
- material constraints;
- current health/energy where lawfully modeled;
- unresolved prior events;
- timed commitments;
- external/local world events;
- lawful stochasticity.

The engine advances only the causal state that matters.

No need to generate a prose interior monologue for every hour Nadia spends at work.

---

## 2. Time advancement has multiple grains

Live scene time:
- seconds/minutes;
- continuous SAY/DO/ATTEND;
- interruption and immediate causality.

Near-term life time:
- hours/days;
- errands, work shifts, school, calls, appointments, practical consequences.

Longitudinal life time:
- weeks/months/years;
- relationship drift;
- job/family changes;
- developmental changes;
- accumulated patterns;
- rupture/repair;
- major transitions.

The engine may use different transition mechanisms at different grains as long as exact causal lineage and authority are preserved.

---

## 3. Advance-on-demand is not fake persistence

The simulation does not need to compute every absent hour at wall-clock time.

At return/contact/trigger time it may lawfully advance the interval from last admitted state to current simulated time using:
- scheduled events;
- deterministic obligations;
- candidate events constrained by current world/person state;
- prior consequence chains;
- bounded randomness;
- exact admission and replay records.

From the diegetic perspective, life continued.

Implementation laziness must not become ontological popping-in.

---

## 4. Events are caused by the world, not the product's pedagogical need

Event generation should draw from:
- Nadia's projects;
- child/family projects;
- work/institution decisions;
- schedules;
- money/material constraints;
- local network actions;
- environmental conditions;
- prior admitted consequences.

Not:
- 'the player needs a kindness test';
- 'engagement is dropping';
- 'the relationship needs drama';
- 'we need a poignant scene now'.

Playability should come from choosing a causally rich life, not from secretly rewriting causation around the user.

---

## 5. Contact with the user is one actor decision

A life event does not automatically generate a message.

Conceptual path:

EVENT / CURRENT CONDITION
→ Nadia perception/access;
→ CAN-008/PFF/V3 lawful current mediation;
→ does the user become relevant to Nadia's current purpose/need/thought?;
→ does she want or decide to contact them?;
→ does she currently have access/time/permission/channel?;
→ actual message/call/invitation/silence.

Possible reasons not to contact:
- user is not close enough;
- Nadia does not want help;
- shame/pride/privacy;
- she is busy;
- someone else is more relevant;
- she assumes user is unavailable;
- prior user behavior makes contact unattractive;
- cultural/role expectation;
- she simply does not think of them;
- no meaningful reason exists.

NO MESSAGE is a valid output.

---

## 6. No retention-guilt substitution

The app must not convert persona independence into:
- 'Nadia misses you';
- escalating distress notifications;
- invented crises;
- punishment for not logging in;
- artificial dependence.

If absence matters, it matters because the actual relationship/history makes it matter.

If it does not matter much, the product must tolerate that.

---

## 7. User absence changes opportunity, not only emotion

While the user is gone:
- events happen without them;
- other people may fill practical/social roles;
- invitations expire;
- promises become missed;
- routines adapt;
- opportunities disappear;
- a problem may resolve without the user;
- Nadia may become less available;
- new obligations may form.

Thus absence can change a relation without requiring melodramatic hurt.

---

## 8. Third parties require proportional independence

Do not fully simulate every named person at maximum fidelity.

But if a third party can:
- remember;
- form plans;
- be harmed/helped;
- alter Nadia's life;
- carry consequences forward;

they need enough independent causal state to support those claims.

Fidelity should scale with causal relevance, not user interest.

---

## 9. Live current-events ingestion is probably not an alpha requirement

Current hypothesis:

The alpha can test the relationship theorem inside a generated/local simulated world without live-news ingestion.

Reasons:
- reduces truth/admission burden;
- avoids turning news retrieval into the product;
- keeps causal provenance tractable;
- allows replay;
- tests whether the relationship itself is compelling.

Later modes can admit current/historical external events through explicit authority.

---

## 10. What is progress if there is no score?

Candidate answer:

Progress is **increasing relational specificity, consequence and mutual historical dependence**, not monotonically increasing warmth.

The world changes because these two people now have a history.

Evidence of progression may include:
- commitments that did not exist before;
- changed access;
- routines;
- inside references;
- expectations;
- requests;
- obligations;
- network introductions;
- trust in particular domains;
- grievances;
- repair history;
- more consequential absence;
- greater ability to disagree;
- greater risk of disappointment;
- practical interdependence;
- changed future choices.

Some of these are positive. Some are difficult. All can make the relation more real.

---

## 11. Progress != health

A relationship may become:
- more intimate and less healthy;
- more attached and more controlling;
- more emotionally intense and less trustworthy;
- less warm but more honest;
- less dependent but healthier;
- practically important without deep affection.

Therefore:

RELATIONAL DEVELOPMENT != FLOURISHING.

Both must be separately represented.

---

## 12. The game's reward should be consequence and recognition, not points

Potential intrinsic rewards:
- being remembered specifically;
- seeing prior acts matter later;
- being trusted in a new domain;
- being asked for something meaningful;
- being disagreed with honestly;
- being introduced to another person;
- recognizing one's earlier misreading;
- a joke/history becoming shared culture;
- being absent and finding the world changed;
- repairing something that could not be erased;
- helping with no recognition;
- learning that one did harm despite good intent.

These rewards are narrative/relational facts.

They need not be scored.

---

## 13. Difficulty can naturally increase with significance

As the user becomes more important:
- expectations increase;
- promises matter more;
- conflicts have more history;
- third parties become involved;
- tradeoffs appear;
- the user has more opportunities to disappoint;
- the persona may safely protest more;
- helping one need may conflict with another;
- the user may need to choose between care and control.

Thus 'later game difficulty' can emerge from the causal density of relation rather than artificial enemy scaling.

---

## 14. Valid-empty remains necessary

A persistent life cannot be nonstop drama.

Some scenes/messages should be:
- ordinary;
- brief;
- practical;
- funny;
- boring;
- uneventful;
- emotionally low-significance.

The product-level challenge is cadence, not forcing every interaction to reveal depth.

---

## 15. Retrospective as history, not score screen

A later retrospective can show:
- what actually happened;
- commitments kept/missed;
- turning points;
- long patterns;
- changed routines;
- major misunderstandings;
- relationship phases;
- what the user repeatedly did;
- observable consequences where causally supported.

It should not automatically reveal every hidden actor state.

Nor should it convert the life into:
- kindness score;
- friendship percentage;
- moral grade.

Candidate principle:

the retrospective tells a **human history** and separately offers uncertainty-aware behavioral reflection.

---

## 16. Hard tests for Court 3

1. Can Nadia have a consequential week in which the user is irrelevant?
2. Can an event occur and never be told to the user?
3. Can she contact someone else instead?
4. Can no-contact be lawful without feeling like a broken app?
5. Can a missed promise matter without a guilt notification?
6. Can life advance cheaply without becoming random plot generation?
7. Can replay reconstruct why an event happened?

---

## 17. Hard tests for Court 4

1. Can the relationship clearly advance while Nadia is angrier with the user than before?
2. Can the user be more important but less beneficial?
3. Can a respectful bounded acquaintance count as a meaningful history?
4. Can a user recognize progress without any friendship meter?
5. Can ordinary scenes remain compelling because of accumulated specificity?
6. Can a retrospective make the user care without grading them?

---

## 18. Current provisional lean

**Rawlsing should simulate life as a replayable event-driven causal process, not an always-on LLM stream. The user occupies only one causal position inside that life. Relationship progression is the accumulation of specific shared history, expectations, permissions, obligations and consequences; it may increase stakes without increasing warmth or flourishing.**

This remains hypothesis-level.
