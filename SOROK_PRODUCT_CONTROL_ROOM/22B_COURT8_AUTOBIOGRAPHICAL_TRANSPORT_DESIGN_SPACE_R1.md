# Sorok Court 8B — Autobiographical Transport Design Space R1

Status: EXPLORATORY_OPTION_SPACE__HOMOLOGOUS_PEER_MAIN_ROUTE

Date: 2026-09-29

## Core problem

How can the player remain the actual moral/psychological subject while their local social/material coordinates are transformed enough for homologous-peer play?

Videogames commonly solve player/world mismatch through preset protagonists, blank/custom avatars, roleplay, outsider/amnesiac frames, or portal fantasies. Rawlsing needs a narrower fifth problem: transform coordinates without replacing the chooser.

## Option A — literal-self portal

Carry actual biography literally. Homologize only present local material power/embodiment as needed. User may truthfully refer to actual places, family and history.

Strength: maximal autobiographical honesty.
Weakness: foreignness rapidly becomes explicit and may dominate interpretation.

## Option B — diaspora / returnee homologous peer

Player is locally socially legible as belonging or partially belonging, but was formed elsewhere. Actual foreign-formed habits therefore have a diegetic explanation without making the user a pure visitor.

Strength: produces inside/outside tension, partial belonging, assimilatory friction, and a plausible reason for nonlocal habitus.
Weakness: may restore an outsider explanatory shield and may require invented ancestry/migration facts.

## Option C — structural autobiographical homology

Map coordinate-bound biography into locally analogous facts while preserving relational/formative structure.

Example classes: region type, school/status level, occupation prestige, household class position.

Strength: maximal local coherence and unmarkedness.
Weakness: can become fake biography/roleplay and may erase unique cultural meaning.

## Option D — semantic autobiography with coordinate translation

Preserve experiential/relational truth but translate only references whose literal coordinates break the veil.

Potentially preserve: bereavement, family relations, formative conflicts, learned skills, beliefs.
Potentially translate: location labels, institutional prestige, monetary scale, credential social meaning.

Strength: keeps actual formative content.
Weakness: speech mediation can feel like the system is putting words/history in the user's mouth.

## Option E — selective autobiographical anchors

Before play, user explicitly chooses a small set of real autobiographical facts that remain canonical/portable. Everything else is bracketed unless later admitted.

Strength: consent, privacy, clear truth boundary.
Weakness: setup burden and user may not know in advance what will matter.

## Option F — T0 insertion / prior-life reserve

Only the player's current local position and post-T0 history are canonical in the simulated world. The user's prior real life remains extra-diegetic/off-stage and can be discussed only as user testimony unless a separate transport rule admits it.

Strength: extremely bounded; no fake past.
Weakness: awkward when local actors naturally ask autobiographical questions; can feel metaphysically hollow.

## Option G — pre-existing local social slot / doppelganger shell

User inhabits an already coherent local social position with job/family/status but does not inherit the prior person's psyche.

Strength: full local embeddedness.
Weakness: impostor/Quantum-Leap problem; obligations/history the user did not author; likely becomes roleplay rather than Rawlsing main mode.

## Option H — explicit metaphysical veil translation

The game openly states that autobiographical coordinates are transformed by the veil. The user remembers their actual history, but when it must become diegetic, a governed homology maps it into local form.

Strength: philosophically honest about the impossible-subject premise.
Weakness: less naturalistic; translation authority must be extremely disciplined.

## Stronger formal decomposition

Treat autobiographical facts as different transport classes:

### INVARIANTS
Facts whose identity should survive field change whenever user chooses to carry them: relationships, remembered events, learned knowledge, commitments, important losses.

### FIELD-COVARIANT FACTS
Facts whose social meaning depends on the field and may need homologous mapping: class rank, educational prestige, occupational authority, purchasing power, institutional leverage.

### COORDINATE-BOUND FACTS
Literal places, employers, institutions, currencies and similar identifiers that may be bracketed, carried as foreign, or transformed depending on mode.

### NON-TRANSLATABLE RESIDUES
Facts for which no defensible local homologue exists without falsifying the user. These must remain marked/foreign, bracketed, or trigger mode-specific handling; never invent an equivalence.

## Current promising composite

Possible main-mode synthesis:

1. homologous local peer material/power position;
2. local/social embodiment governed separately;
3. real subjectivity and memories retained by the human;
4. small explicit autobiographical invariants may be carried;
5. field-covariant facts may be homologized;
6. coordinate-bound facts default to bracketed/off-stage;
7. non-translatable residues remain honestly unresolved/marked;
8. diaspora/returnee framing remains a candidate diegetic wrapper where lawful and not identity-fabricating.

## Hard principle

`TRANSPORTING A FACT != TRANSPORTING ITS SOCIAL MEANING`.

A fact may be literally true of the user while its local significance changes, and a structural analogue may preserve social function while not being the same autobiographical fact.

Rawlsing must track this distinction explicitly.
