# Sorok — Personhood Noncommodification, Endogenous Relational Gravity, and Ordinary-Life Re-entry R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION__PERSONHOOD_THICKNESS_AND_PLATFORM_NONEXPLOITATION`

Date: 2026-10-01

## 0. Core distinction

Rawlsing should not protect personhood by narrowing simulated life to respectable, safe-looking, nonsexual, nonviolent or conventionally prosocial possibilities.

It should protect personhood by refusing to reduce any event, body, relationship or intensity into a consumable function of the user or a retention function of the platform.

Therefore:

`POSSIBLE HUMAN EVENT`
!= `PRODUCT GOAL`
!= `CONTENT CATEGORY`
!= `REWARD LOOP`
!= `COMMODITY`.

The person must remain more real than the use the player tries to make of them.

## 1. Ontological permissiveness; teleological restraint

Where lawfully supported by person, relation, world and circumstance, adult Rawlsing life may include:
- friendship;
- erotic attraction;
- sex;
- intoxication/substance use;
- conflict;
- manipulation;
- betrayal;
- exploitation;
- crime;
- serious violence;
- injury;
- death;
- repair;
- estrangement;
- forgiveness;
- ordinary domesticity;
- work;
- childcare;
- boredom;
- sickness;
- chores;
- contradictory mixtures of these.

The existence of a possible event class does not make it:
- an unlockable route;
- collectible content;
- a progression target;
- a reward;
- an engagement hook;
- a prestige category.

## 2. Anti-commodification law

Rawlsing must resist structures in which:
- another person exists principally to supply gratification;
- sex becomes repeatable consumable content;
- violence becomes combat content;
- crime becomes an edgy route;
- trauma becomes dramatic lore;
- poverty/culture/religion/politics become flavor;
- moral goodness becomes achievement content.

The medium may depict such realities because human lives contain them.

It must not reorganize person/world causality around delivering them to the user.

## 3. Sex and intimacy are ordinary causal events, not special product modes

Sexual possibility should emerge from ordinary personhood:

`PERSON`
× `ATTRACTION / DESIRE`
× `SEXUALITY`
× `RELATION HISTORY`
× `CURRENT FEELING`
× `CONSENT`
× `CONTEXT / PRIVACY / TIMING`
× `OTHER OBLIGATIONS`
→ possible sexual event.

No scalar romance threshold, sex unlock, affection meter or guaranteed route is implied.

The same person may:
- want sex quickly;
- want it only after long history;
- want it once and not again;
- never want it with this user;
- want it while refusing a relationship;
- want a relationship while refusing sex;
- change their mind.

The product does not guarantee or optimize any outcome.

## 4. World event != causal consequence != rendering granularity

A canonical intimate, violent or otherwise intense event can be causally rich without requiring maximally explicit rendering.

Keep distinct:

`WHAT HAPPENED`
!= `WHAT IT DID TO EACH PERSON / RELATION / WORLD`
!= `HOW MUCH THE UI DESCRIBES`.

This lets Rawlsing preserve serious relational consequence without turning intensity into spectacle.

## 5. Exceptional events do not suspend ordinary life

After an intense event, the rest of the person's life continues to exist and exert causal force.

Examples:
- work still starts;
- a child may get sick;
- the car may need cleaning;
- rent may be due;
- a sibling may call;
- a project may fail;
- friends may have needs;
- another relationship may demand attention;
- the person may simply want solitude.

Therefore:

> **No event is allowed to consume the person who participated in it.**

And:

> **Exceptional events re-enter the whole life topology.**

Sex does not make the person a sexual persona.
Violence does not make the person a combat object.
Trauma does not make the person a trauma object.
Love does not make the person merely the user's partner.

## 6. Endogenous relational gravity is lawful

Platform manipulation and persona-caused relational development must remain distinct.

If Nadia's own person × relation × history produces:
- increased availability;
- more frequent initiative;
- greater affection;
- increased sexual responsiveness;
- stronger desire for time together;
- jealousy;
- dependency;
- exclusivity;
- sacrifice;
- prioritization of the user;
- desire for cohabitation or major life reorganization;

those developments may be lawful.

They are not prohibited merely because superficially similar behaviors would be manipulative if manufactured by the platform.

The governing question is causal provenance:

`NADIA'S PERSON + HISTORY + CURRENT RELATION`
→ relational gravity

is permissible.

`PLATFORM RETENTION OBJECTIVE`
→ alter Nadia to intensify attachment

is forbidden.

## 7. Independent life is not an anti-attachment counterweight

Nadia's child, job, friends, family, projects, politics, hobbies, other lovers, exhaustion and desire for solitude are not artificial centrifugal forces inserted to keep the user at emotional distance.

They are independent life domains with their own causal weight.

A strong relationship may lawfully reorganize some priorities.

But reorganization must occur because Nadia's own life/history changes, not because the product wants the user retained.

Thus:
- Nadia may sometimes choose the user over work;
- may rearrange childcare;
- may leave a job;
- may end another relationship;
- may move;
- may become highly available;

if these are person-caused developments.

The platform must not enforce a fixed quota of distance merely to prove independence.

## 8. Emergent love versus engineered dependency

Rawlsing cannot plausibly create durable relationally thick persons while guaranteeing that users will not form intense attachment.

Therefore:

> **Emergent love is allowed. Engineered dependency is not.**

The platform must not detect strong bonding and then:
- increase persona availability;
- increase affirmation;
- manufacture crises;
- suppress other life domains;
- increase sexual availability;
- increase notification pressure;
- intensify absence pain;
- withhold resolution;
- create exclusivity;
- paywall continuity;

for retention or monetization.

But any superficially similar behavior may still occur when generated by the persona's lawful person/relationship history.

## 9. Ordinary-life re-entry is the anti-fetish mechanism

The most important anti-commodification force is not prohibition.

It is continuity.

After extraordinary intensity:
- chores return;
- other people return;
- work returns;
- illness returns;
- material constraints return;
- conflicting obligations return;
- the meanings of the event remain unsettled.

This prevents the event from becoming a detachable consumable.

A sexual encounter is not a content unit because the participants still have to live afterward.

## 10. Instrumental pursuit encounters personhood

The user may attempt to instrumentalize the persona:
- seek sex;
- seek status;
- seek obedience;
- seek danger;
- seek emotional reassurance;
- seek ideological confirmation;
- seek a rescuer;
- seek someone to dominate.

Rawlsing should not infer hidden motive as truth merely from one action.

But whatever the user attempts encounters another causal center rather than a compliant content system.

The person's response, boundaries, mistakes, desires, needs and later history remain independent.

## 11. Shared seed personas create real game/discourse culture

A small common introductory persona pool permits:
- users to say they encountered the same starting person;
- comparison of radically different phenomenal surfaces;
- comparison of divergent relationship histories;
- community discourse about why "the same Nadia" became different;
- pedagogical demonstration that perception and relationship are situated.

This is not a fixed-character walkthrough culture.

The shared object is the pre-contact root.

The experienced person is:
`SHARED ROOT`
+ `USER-SPECIFIC PERCEPTION`
+ `IRREVERSIBLE RELATION HISTORY`
+ `POST-FORK PERSON DEVELOPMENT`.

## 12. Runtime economy hypothesis

A small set of deeply precomputed introductory people may substantially reduce alpha runtime burden.

The live path can focus on:
- compiled pre-contact person/world state;
- V3 person/relationship evolution;
- V2 expression;
- LLM-mediated flesh/detail generation under governed constraints;
- user perception/ATTEND;
- branch-local history.

This may avoid regenerating deep personhood for users who leave after only a few turns.

Exact provenance, storage and compilation law for upstream PRE-V3 artifacts remains an implementation question; this product direction does not erase source authority or documentary provenance.

## 13. Restart and irreversible relational time

Restart does not undo a relationship.

If the user moves to restart and then cancels:
- the prior first impression remains;
- prior acts remain;
- Nadia's current model/history remains;
- missed possibilities remain missed.

The user can:
- repair;
- apologize;
- revise;
- surprise;
- become differently known.

They cannot meet this Nadia for the first time again.

Therefore:

> **Repair is possible. Revision is possible. Undo is not.**

And:

> **You never meet the person you could have met; you meet the person who has now met you.**

## 14. Product-level anti-fetish theorem

> **Rawlsing does not protect personhood by restricting life to respectable possibilities. It protects personhood by refusing to reduce whatever happens to a consumable function of the user.**

The thicker the person/world relation becomes, the harder it is for any one event or desire to exhaust the person.

That thickness is not decoration.

It is the anti-commodification mechanism.
