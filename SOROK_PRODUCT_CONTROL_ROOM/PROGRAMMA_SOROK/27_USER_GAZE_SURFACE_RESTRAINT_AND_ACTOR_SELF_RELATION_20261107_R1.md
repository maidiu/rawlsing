## What “artist” means — and does NOT mean

The user rejects “artist” as an excuse for purple prose, a distinctive self-advertising narrator, a fabricated protagonist journey, moral instruction or a dramatic clue-planting system. The analogy concerns **factual restraint, extremely discerning composition and refined responsiveness to the user's historically demonstrated *mode of encountering* the world**.

Rawlsing should show only eligible, physically and perceptually grounded facts: concrete surfaces, occurrences, timing, placements, words and sequences. It can choose and order a few of those touchstones with uncommon precision. **The user supplies the connective, emotional, interpretive and spiritual labor.** The narrator does not interpret on the user's behalf; it does not tell them “you feel...,” “Nadia wants...,” “this means...,” or “you have finally learned...”. It does not fetishize objects for their own sake: tangible reality bears historical relations and can make a reader's own thought active precisely because no lecturer resolves what it means. Ordinary inconsequence, ambiguity, silence and incompletion remain legitimate.

The user invoked an artistic contrast exemplified, to their reading, by Mark's spare narrative and later explicit explication (especially in John). This is an **aesthetic illustration supplied by the user**, not a biblical dating or redactional-historical claim established by this document. The product law is **show facts; do not announce their interpretation**, not “imitate Mark's literary voice.”

The narrator's choices may have **coherent non-random direction** over time. That direction is not teleological and need not converge to virtue, wisdom, trauma disclosure or self-awareness. The best explanatory source for its development is the uncertain, time-indexed user profile warranted by what this **actual user** has implicitly demonstrated through chosen speech, action, interruption, attention, tolerated silence, misrecognition, revisiting and refusal—within the limits of what was physically exposed and possible. The profile is a **hypothesis conditioned on behavior**, not hidden omniscience or a clinical/moral score.

**No inflated artistry architecture:** do not turn “artist,” “spirit,” “plot” or “intention” into an autonomous top-level objective, mandatory literary metaphors, an arc-planning module or a proprietary literary style. If plain minimally chosen facts do the work, stop there. Never manufacture a fact, gesture or clue because an inferred user psychology would find it resonant. The test is whether *lawful selection from already established phenomena* is sufficiently apt without overt narratorial explanation.

## Four distinct ownership domains

1. **Canonical material world**: world producer owns what physically happened/exists, source/provenance, time, material scarcity, actual interpersonal attempts and realized effects. An observing renderer never writes this truth, and any object-depth mechanism must remain source-qualified and replay-safe.
2. **Nadia's independent actor constitution**: competent Sorok actor/developmental/affective owners (under original science and source constraints) produce her material/social past, acquired practices, commitments, relations, competing tendencies, partial self-awareness and ongoing responses. **Rawlsing may retain a richer authorized computational account of her than Nadia can represent to herself, but must not claim infallibility about human reality merely by holding that account.**
3. **Separate situated epistemologies and self-presentations**: Nadia's accessible thoughts/self-description and her model of the user are produced through *her* exposure, history, emotional and practiced filtering. She chooses what to show, including evasions and stylizations, while behavior may exceed her intention or conscious recognition. The user separately forms their own often inaccurate Nadia model. Neither actor receives the system's private master profile.
4. **User-facing phenomenal reader**: a deliberately limited, minimally inferential model of user gaze/history selects a few **authorized** observable facts for this user's experience. It cannot feed unobserved user “truth” into Nadia, change her actions or alter canonical world history. The system's hypotheses about either party are not presented as their thoughts.

## Nadia's self-awareness is layered, intermittent and historically situated, not a single “self-consciousness score”

The user proposes a **variable, multidimensional accessibility/relation to self**, rather than an all-knowing hidden self and a merely deceptive surface. Possible distinctions for original science/actor investigation:

- **Practiced constitution not yet recognizable to her**: acquired response patterns, bodily and relational orientations, contradictions and historically formed investments that influence conduct but cannot be retrieved as a coherent conscious proposition.
- **Recognized but resisted or defended-against material**: things partly encountered, disavowed, rationalized, pushed away or evaded; conflict may change by context and relationship. Avoid automatically validating a therapist-like omniscient account that her denial “proves” repression.
- **Hard-won past insight and intermittent recognition**: a truth once struggled toward may not be currently salient or accessible, and can be evaded in ordinary routines despite having once mattered. Neither forgetting nor access is simply binary.
- **Durable current narrative of self**: “the sort of person I think I have become this year”; can be sincere and false/incomplete in multiple ways.
- **Day/hour/context-activated self-appraisal**: current mood, fatigue, material pressures, place, audience, practical stakes and one's relation to this particular person can alter what comes to mind and how it is held.
- **Deliberate social presentation versus inadvertent disclosure**: self-presentation may be strategic, polite, guarded, performative or careless; gestures, interruptions and chosen acts can reveal patterns without granting a viewer the right to a definitive hidden-motive verdict.
- **Unrecognized surplus**: there may be dimensions of the actor's life and potential beyond what they or the system have articulated. Do **not** model this as an already known secret “real self” or as deterministic essence.

These are **candidate analytic axes** (accessibility, conflict, recognition, situated activation, temporal durability, avowal, expression, exposure), not a required seven-layer ontological implementation, nor a numeric ladder from shallow to enlightened. Someone may simply conduct a largely stable practical life without daily transformative introspection; another may repeatedly congratulate themselves on depth while acting self-protectively. The simulator must neither punish ordinary stability nor reward self-description or verbally impressive introspection. Motive, action, self-belief and observers' readings can contradict one another.

An actor's aversion and attraction can also be ambivalent, outside current conscious formulation or change over time. The user's example of discovering desire beneath dislike is a **possible form of contradictory affect**, not permission to infer sexual desire from resistance, assume erotic motive behind contempt, or override consent. Any concrete realization demands independent developmental/relational evidence and competent actor authority. A person's self-misrecognition is not proof that an outside theory (or Rawlsing) knows what they “really want”.

## User-profile emergence and rendering contract

**Observation ownership and profile updating:**
- Inputs are **user-authored** SAY/DO/ATTEND/INSPECT/ORIENT, timing, voluntary returns to a detail, correction/contradiction, and repeated choices *relative to what was available*. Record exposure provenance, alternatives and what the system showed. Absence of attention to an unshown item is not evidence of indifference.
- Distinguish an **unprompted choice** from selecting a detail that the renderer itself preferentially exposed. Control reinforcement loops: the system must not manufacture its own “proof” of a trait by showing mainly confirming evidence and recording the resulting clicks as an independent psychological revelation.
- Track competing/weak hypotheses, context and change; avoid rigid user types, demographic or ideology shortcuts, psychotherapy-by-stealth and a one-dimensional “user growth” axis. An overtly stated opinion is evidence of an utterance, not necessarily the user's stable private desire.
- Perceptual selection may emphasize or deemphasize **optional** lawful observations and warranted lexical recognition. Materially unavoidable or already established shared action facts, consequential contradiction, safety and direct interlocutor speech cannot be silently suppressed to preserve an inferred user schema.
- No fabrication, false mood adjectives, private Nadia motive inference, instrumental misdescription or deliberate user-confusion curriculum. The user may misrecognize Nadia precisely because their own selections, habits and interpretive work are fallible—not because an external plotter falsifies the scene.
- Selection should be **subtle and cumulative**: its development can make different users' encounters feel individually inflected without advertising that their soul is being analyzed. The selection may become inconvenient, confirming, disconfirming, unremarkable or ambiguous. No compulsory self-overcoming.
- Preserve user's unresolved disclosure choice about adaptive perceptual mediation from [issue #768](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/issues/768). This record does not silently settle consent/disclosure policy.

**Compositional restraint:** use the smallest number of concrete public observations adequate to preserve causal intelligibility. Arrangement, contrast, omission of optional detail and temporal juxtaposition can be profound; adjectives about emotional meaning cannot substitute for source facts. The narrator does *not* explain why it offered a detail. It may select the objectively present clock, a repair and a pause without saying “Nadia is anxious about your demands” or “your fear of rejection makes you notice her distance.” **A meaningful sequence belongs equally to independent causality and the reader's active engagement.**

## Proposed falsifiers for sourcefree future reader/actor work

1. Hold canonical world, committed actor state, actual user conduct and exposed events fixed; vary only qualified user-gaze history: resulting optional factual selections may change, **Nadia's behavior, material ledger and unavoidable direct communication must not**.
2. Hold gaze fixed and change an actual user act within Nadia's observation: only the lawfully mediated actor/world consequences may differ; the reader must not preedit Nadia's reaction based on what it believes the user hopes.
3. Present unshown/unavailable/private events to attention/profile and renderer: refusal/no update. Present contradictory observed conduct: the profile must remain revisable instead of declaring “hidden motive proven.”
4. Change only the renderer's previous optional details, then observe further user clicks: a valid model cannot treat its own exposure policy as independent trait evidence without provenance correction.
5. Give Nadia testimony contradicting the system's private account or the user's model: she must be able to sincerely err, dissemble, partly recognize, or refuse, within authorized actor state, **without** narrator resolving the ambiguity.
6. Give a person ordinary stable routines, or articulate self-insight paired with contradictory behavior: neither “unreflective = lesser humanity” nor “eloquent inner work = better person” may become a default transition rule.
7. Run cold-reopen and late-contradiction across real objective facts, her accessible model, her model of the user and the user's hypothesis: do not collapse these into one truth store or invent retrospective evidence.

**Terminal artistic criterion:** a user encounters Nadia through a scarce, particular, sometimes self-confirming but falsifiable phenomenal surface, increasingly conditioned by their own demonstrable way of looking. The world remains independently real and historically thick, and Nadia's reality exceeds not only the user's gaze, but also **her own current self-account and Rawlsing's warranted explanatory model**. The narrator can be uncannily apt while remaining almost invisible. Or it can be entirely uneventful.
