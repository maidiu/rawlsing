The legal test for *any* proposed resurrection is: (1) controlling modern requirement and owner, (2) no satisfactory accepted successor at the same scope, (3) exact old symbol/code compatibility, source right and actual first consumer, (4) independent same-cut proof and original science/actor/product authority, and (5) demonstrable comparative gain over adapting selected current code.
