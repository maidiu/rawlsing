## 1. The first conceptual correction: formation, activation and opportunity are different causal operations

The question is not 'what response should a personality have to this event?' That implies a preexisting type and turns the event into a narrative stimulus. Nor is it merely 'was one behavior performed?'. A scientifically meaningful *acquired* disposition/practice is a **historically evidenced change in an actor's future action possibilities under specified triggering conditions**.

Separate five processes:
1. **Opportunity or constraint change** in the objective environment (time, money, institution, equipment, other people, danger). No psychological acquisition follows automatically.
2. **Activation or inhibition** of an already acquired, setting-sensitive mode of action (a prior practice that is recruited, unavailable or suppressed). This is neither formation nor forgetting.
3. **Situated immediate response** to an admitted, accessible encounter, conditioned by actor history and competing relations/motives. This is CAN-008 territory, not automatically CAN-011 or ADP-016.
4. **Formation/transformation of durable organization** (a new/revised capacity, mediator, tension or practice, if its mechanism is proved and exact Rule-T target authority exists).
5. **Formation or change of accessible/nondeclarative memory** (distinct from objective occurrence, immediate response, later narration and ADP-017 permissible claim).

The executable P3 transaction should distinguish these rather than issue one universal `experience → personality_delta` rule. **This distinction is an analytical hypothesis to test against actual owner law, not a new registry of state kinds**.

## 2. Actual primary theoretical reading used here

- **Bernard Lahire, *The Plural Actor*, Act I, Scene 2, printed pp. 49–55:** Past dispositions are only reconstructed from practices, situations and trajectories; the current situation can activate or inhibit them. A single aggressive action in one relation does not prove a general aggressive disposition. **Mechanism implication:** do not let one observed behavior mint a portable CAN-011 mediator; record conditions of use/inhibition and test transfer rather than infer invariance.
- **Ole Dreier, *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life*, ch. 8.1, printed pp. 181–184:** A person's conduct of everyday life is organized across diverse settings, activities, social arrangements, times and coparticipants. A shift in one place may not transport unaltered into another; changes must be traced through practical participation. **Mechanism implication:** later E2 must preserve which activity and which opportunities were present, not treat a prior self-description as proof of context-independent learning.
- **A. N. Leontiev, *Activity, Consciousness and Personality* (Portuguese user copy, passage on multi-motivated activity / sense-forming versus stimulating motives):** A single social act can realize several motives, which can differ in what they do for the person; acknowledged reasons need not exhaust motivating activity. **Mechanism implication:** CAN-008 streams should not collapse into a one-scalar winner, and an actor's retrospective narrative may omit causally relevant motives. The precise translated page locator has **not** been independently normalized; retain the passage as a bounded textual reading only.
- **Melvin Kohn and Carmi Schooler, *Work and Personality*, Preface pp. xi–xii:** They explicitly distinguish selection into work from being changed by work and motivate longitudinal reciprocal-effect inquiry. **Empirical implication:** observed association of employment, practice and response cannot establish acquisition without controlling prior state, selection and evolving work conditions.

These texts clarify the *scientific problem and rival explanations*. None provides a source-valid numerical update function for a generated actor, and none is original CAN/ADP issuance. Broader Sève, Holzkamp, Luria, Elder, Mortimer, Archer and social-reproduction materials remain research targets in [07](07_SECONDARY_THEORY_READING_MAP.md), not purportedly fully read here.

## 3. Three falsifiable test situations, and what each would actually establish

### Case A — A new practical operation or changed equipment?

Actor A repeatedly works with a particular instrument, aided by B. Later, A performs the operation without B under independently documented material access. Candidate positive O05 is **not established merely because the later act succeeds**.

Competing explanations: prior undocumented competence; A had the capacity but previously lacked permission or the instrument; B secretly performed essential steps; the later task differs; a temporary cue remains; the observed response is a one-off imitation. Necessary observations include prior opportunity/ability probes, actual actor rather than proxy performance, withdrawn/varied assistance, comparable affordances, stable provenance and later E2 *performed* action rather than simply menu eligibility.

**Preliminary conclusion:** Repeated witnessed, conditional performance can strengthen a *narrow* acquisition hypothesis; it cannot by itself issue a universal Rule T. Record residual uncertainty and exact domain of portability. If original authority requires an intervention/control not present, status remains UNEVALUABLE, not NO_CHANGE.

### Case B — Same encounter, different historically produced responses

An institution imposes the same external demand on two actors. One complies in public, privately organizes a workaround; another refuses immediately; an individual may do both at different moments. Their differing practical projects, dependency relations, sanctions, resources and prior activity must be consulted by the real rule.

**Required discriminators:** admitted external event ≠ personally accessible exposure; actor's prior projects/relationships are actual sources, not author-supplied hidden labels; multiple response streams can coexist; later reader sees the committed response/middle-state history rather than being handed a narrative 'rebellious personality'. If changing only the demographic label changes response with all mediation held fixed, **reject**.

**Preliminary conclusion:** A bounded CAN-008 response rule may be easier to test before O05 acquired practice, but the existence of a test-only Rule-A engine (HQ300) **must be examined first**. A fresh hypothetical response rule without inspecting it wastes capital.

### Case C — Event record, memory, and claimed recollection

A witnessed event is archived. A has some qualifying access to it. At a later encounter A reports an altered, incomplete or contradictory account; the record persists.

**Three distinct empirical issues:** did a governed memory trace form at all; is the trace currently accessible/retained or transformed; what can A responsibly claim? An LLM's fluent 'I recall' demonstrates none of these by itself. Nondeclarative causal trace might condition later activity without warranting any first-person recollection. Conversely a claim may be mistaken or strategic.

**Preliminary conclusion:** ADP-016 can provide typed memory custody infrastructure now, but positive formation/reconsolidation requires exact owner-issued effect semantics, admissible parent, state and reader. R3 M01 already tests some effects under authored fixture rules; its specific negative/conditional controls are starting capital.

## 4. The sharpest science decision: what does an O05 positive actually mean?

There is an underexamined risk of **tautology**. If 'practice' is defined as 'whatever state changes later behavior', a newly added flag plus a later conditional read will always pass; this proves a program, not that the actor acquired a practical organization. Likewise, defining a memory as a row that changes later behavior is insufficient scientific differentiation between memory, expectation, state cache, cue and narrative.

A defensible candidate should specify an independent acquired operation, the *conditions of its execution*, and the causal production story not circularly defined by the later output. An experiment needs to distinguish **formation** from preexisting competency, **activation** from novelty, and **material enablement** from internal organization.

I propose we make the *first positive scientific decision* very narrow: **one evidenced object-directed practical operation in one bounded material domain, with source-known assistance/conditions and an independently observed later use**. Study as O05 candidate, not automatic adoption. Its restricted scope creates a real falsifier and avoids making one school skill a total theory of personhood.

Before we issue that Rule T, separately decide whether the necessary **current CAN-008 event response** has an original Rule-A source. A positive O05 implementation cannot use an unrelated synthetic A's response to justify a native acquired T.

## 5. O07 positive nonchange cannot be equated with absence of change data

The original four-way proposed split in [04] is defensible as *analysis*: (1) rule unissued; (2) qualified evaluator but unable to evaluate present record; (3) positive evaluated unchanged under a rule-sensitive test; (4) positive change committed. The **target field and time interval must be specified**. No behavioral change observed despite no occasion to express a practice is **not** evaluated NO_CHANGE. A no-change rule must be sufficiently sensitive to a relevant possible effect under the correct admissible conditions.

A scientific `NO_CHANGE` cannot be the default branch of a failed attempt to prove acquisition. It needs its own evidence and owner.
