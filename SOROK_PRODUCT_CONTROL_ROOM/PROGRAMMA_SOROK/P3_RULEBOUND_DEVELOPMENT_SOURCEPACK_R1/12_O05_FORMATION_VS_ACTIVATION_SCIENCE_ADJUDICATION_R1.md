## I. Principal judgment: O05 must NOT be mistaken for the science of personality as a whole

A recurrent practical operation is a comparatively inspectable **test case** for developmental transformation, not the most fundamental unit of personality. The governing psychological unit for this project is closer to an **actor's historically unfolding, object-directed activity within definite material and social relations**. In that trajectory, actions, motives, operations, relationships and objective results can change in distinct, contradictory ways.

This difference is not merely philosophical vocabulary.

- **Leontiev, Activity, Consciousness and Personality**, attached Portuguese text, PDF pp. 18–19 (hierarchies of related activities and the personally transformed significance of a child's successful-but-conflicted act), pp. 20–25 (needs receive concrete objects/motives through activity), pp. 29–31 (one act can participate in different motive relations). The point is not a private faculty acquiring a label, but the development of relations among activities and motives. This is a *theoretical interpretation*, not an issued update formula.
- **Sève, Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality**, printed pp. 193–205 (PDF pp. 199–211, chapter III, living system of social relations between acts): what productive activity means to the person cannot be inferred from the technical outcome because the worker's labor product, wage access, time and capitalist appropriation are socially mediated. A new technical capacity can coexist with diminished control over life's conditions. No automatic skill -> freedom/virtue/motive increment.
- **Lahire, The Plural Actor**, Act I, Scene 2 ('conditional dispositions', 'negative power of context: inhibition and latency', printed pp. 50–65; PDF around pp. 69–86), and Scene 3 ('analogy and transfer', printed pp. 66–77; PDF around pp. 87–98): the present context activates or inhibits heterogeneous historically acquired repertoires; past experience is not one homogeneous score constantly expressing itself.
- **Dreier, Psychotherapy in Everyday Life**, chapter 8 'The Conduct of Everyday Life and the Life Trajectory', printed pp. 182–191 (PDF around pp. 198–207): participation is arranged across settings and coparticipants; continuity, change, and learning must be traced across those actual contexts. Individual conduct is not reducible to a setting-free self-description.
- **Kohn and Schooler, Work and Personality**, ch. 7, printed p. 155 and ensuing longitudinal models (PDF p. 177 onward): investigate reciprocal relations between occupational conditions and psychological functioning, rather than treating current occupational position as a one-way personality cause. Study-specific results do not warrant universal generated-actor transition coefficients.

**Materialist caution:** 'practice' is not an internal skill-object detachable from instrument access, labor process, social cooperation, social power and institutional continuity. Nevertheless CAN-011 owns only **actor-specific acquired organization**, not the independent world or another person's labor. Preserve linked C4/C8/ADP15 material/social sources rather than copying them into a person's psyche.

## II. The causal-identification problem

Given an observed later performance, the claim 'A acquired an operation' is underdetermined.

Let **B** = actor's prior capacity, **H** = actual activity history, **W** = contemporaneous material/role affordances, **R** = other actors' support/contribution, **C** = contextual activation or inhibition, **M** = projects/motives, and **Y** = later actual action. This is only an explanatory notation, not a proposed Rawlsing ABI or a quantitative law.

A difference in Y after a series of episodes cannot, by itself, identify a change in B: W, R, C and M may have changed. Conversely no new observed Y may reflect inhibition or absence of opportunity rather than absence of acquired capacity. An author-programmed change in a synthetic Y after flipping a stored state proves **software causal dependency**; it does not independently establish that real people learn that way.

We must distinguish:

1. **Objective change in opportunity:** tools, time, money, authority, institutional access or another actor changed.
2. **Recruitment/inhibition of an existing ability:** present circumstances make something already acquired actionable or unavailable.
3. **One episode's immediate response:** separately CAN-008, not necessarily enduring organization.
4. **Actual acquisition/transformation of actor-specific organization:** a Rule-T claim requiring history, target kind and exclusion or acknowledged limitation of serious alternatives.
5. **Formed nondeclarative/accessible memory:** separately ADP-016, not automatically practice, belief, narrative or response.
6. **Change in personal motive/meaning/relations:** distinct CAN-010/MEDIATOR/TENSION/ADP-015 questions; may occur without new technical skill, and technical skill may occur without them.

**No hard-coded number-of-exposures threshold.** Some operations may be learned through a rare transformative event, others through long participation; universal 'two acts -> mastery' is a test-fixture convention, not scientific Rule T.

## III. Finite source-grounded test case: A learns to carry out one object-directed inspection

Use the actual S35/R31J material tool and object-directed action frontier, *not* a new abstract classroom skill.

- **T0 baseline:** establish actor A, object, instrument, accepted role/permission and a real task opportunity. Record whether A *actually attempted* inspection, outcome, help and motive/action sources. A failure while tool denied is **NOT baseline incompetence**; nonperformance without demand is **NOT incapacity**.
- **T1 actual participated activity:** independent B shares/refuses/assists; C8/ADP-019 admits what actually happened; A's access is separately witnessed; independently record A's enacted movements/choices and B's contribution, timing and constraints. A retrospective report is not an independent witness.
- **T2 later matched probe:** under a *comparably specified* inspection opportunity, A attempts a distinct actual operation. The later E2 must **perform**, not merely be offered an option, and the source must show whether B/other tools/cues still supply essential action.
- **T3 context contrast:** with relevant affordance absent, A may fail to perform despite retaining a capacity; with contradictory projects or sanction, A may deliberately abstain. Do not automatically weaken/end a practice.
- **T4 limited transfer probe (only if claimed):** a related but materially different object/institution tests the scope of learned organization. Failure bounds transfer; it does not prove original practice was never acquired. Successful portability must also have rule-based warrant.

**Rival hypotheses:** H0 preexisting ability or earlier undocumented practice; H1 newly acquired narrow operation; H2 temporary assisted performance/imitation; H3 action materially accomplished by B or another institution; H4 changed opportunity/permission explains the observable difference; H5 context activation/inhibition or contradictory motive explains performance variation; H6 observer/model/test-writer imposed an apparent state rather than independently generated actor activity.

**Required causal severances** (different claims!):
- Remove witnessed A participation but preserve B's world action: Rule-T positive must not survive on B's action alone.
- Restore tool, task, actor and other assistance but remove the candidate newly formed state: an actual later E2 difference tests **computational use of state**, not validity of the formation rule.
- Remove only material affordance while preserving lawful learned state: nonperformance must not automatically delete capacity.
- Swap performer/source actor, forge receipt or cross branch: deny mutation/replay.
- Restore exact evidence and rule version after a severance: recover prior software result.
- Replace practiced object/activity with one outside claimed scope: deny unwarranted universal transfer; unknown is not negative capability.
- Reassign a claim as prose: no direct state issuance or actor knowing.

This distinction avoids two symmetrical falsifications: passing software tests does not prove human learning, but the lack of native human proof does not erase genuine already-executed code.
