The P3 question is not “which thinker has the best theory of personality?” It is: **which falsifiable historical-material mechanisms could justify a bounded actor-state transition under actual social activity, and which familiar theoretical slogans remain underdetermined?**

### Four evidence levels to keep distinct

1. **Philosophical/metatheoretical account**: clarifies what historical activity, agency and mediation mean. Does not directly supply a validated executable transition.
2. **Mechanism hypothesis**: actor/state/time/relationship sequence with rival outcomes that could be tested.
3. **Empirical finding**: study-specific population, measures, counterfactual strategy, effect heterogeneity, biases and temporal scope.
4. **Executable scientific issuance**: original owner/competent evaluator has approved an exact rule, current source identity and state/consumer mapping after independent testing.

Reading books is meaningful *only* if it can clarify the contested mechanism and improve discrimination between rival causal accounts. Use the user-supplied PDFs/texts when lawfully accessible; otherwise record `SOURCE_NOT_ACTUALLY_AVAILABLE` rather than paraphrasing from titles.
