# RAWLSING — Engineering constitution for historical objectification, value, alienation, and situated personhood

Full-system architectural synthesis · November 8, 2026 · Source-grounded design, not an issued commission

## I. Executive determination

**The engineering task is to make Rawlsing computationally reproduce a particular historical relationship: the social world produces forms of material and symbolic value; actual people develop through activity within those forms; their developed investments and capacities become implicated in particular objects, practices, institutions, and ideals; those relations can subsequently be recruited into new experiences and actions, producing further history.**

We are not building an object-backstory generator with a sophisticated psychological prompt.

We are building a **generalized, historically grounded, causally indexed actor–world mediation capability**, with anticipatory LLM materialization and direct integration into the existing PRE-V3 → V3 → CAN-008 → world/action → phenomenal-surface architecture.

The intended invariant is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{SOCIAL PRODUCTION}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{OBJECTIFIED WORLD}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{HISTORICALLY FORMED PERSON}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{SITUATED ACTIVITY}\\
\downarrow\\
\text{NEW OBJECTIVE AND PERSONAL HISTORY}
\end{gathered}
}
\]

But this is a recursive process, not five independent modules. Institutions, commodities, people, objects, practices, ideals, obligations, and material constraints can be both products of earlier activity and conditions for subsequent activity.

The software must preserve those different positions within the causal process.

### The four engineering operations

Our earlier formulation survives:


**CONSTITUTE**

Materialize historically lawful particulars and their origins.


**INTERLINK**

Bind their causal, material, social, and person-specific relations into retrievable source-grounded structures.


**RECRUIT**

Bring relevant historical relations into a particular actor's current event-time mediation.


**RETURN**

Admit actual action, altered material circumstances, and separately lawful durable consequences.


These are **operations over the existing Rawlsing organism**, not permission to create four new sovereign engines, four copies of Nadia, or a parallel V3.

### Six nonnegotiable distinctions

1. **Objectification is not necessarily alienation.** Human activity can become materially or symbolically objectified without confronting its producers as an alien power.

2. **Social origin is not necessarily interpersonal meaning.** An object can mediate class position, devotion, sensory pleasure, imagined power, identification, anxiety, fantasy, or private gratification without invoking a particular other person.

3. **Value is not one quantity.** Marxian use-value, commodity exchange-value, subjective gratification, symbolic authority, actor investment, and causal importance are different concepts. They can coexist and interact; they cannot be collapsed into an additive universal value score.

4. **Historical causation is not actor self-understanding.** Rawlsing may have evidence concerning how an investment was historically formed that the actor cannot consciously retrieve, would interpret differently, or would deny.

5. **Historical connectivity is not present activation.** An object may be connected to fifty meaningful episodes without those episodes participating in a particular encounter.

6. **LLM plausibility is not admission authority.** A generated history or interpretation acquires computational consequence only through the competent world, event, person, and memory authorities.

Those distinctions supply the conceptual requirements against which the code must be tested.

## III. The exact ontological boundary: PRE-V3 supplies the world, V3 supplies the historically formed person

This is the most important upstream architectural constraint.

The Pyat source atlas distinguishes structural relations, positions, temporal conditions, persistence, opportunities, affordances, constraints, mechanisms, social topology, objective occurrences, and source-qualified world products. Its PRE-V3 → V3 wake criterion expressly prohibits treating empirical possibility as individual prediction or letting objective structural description produce psyche directly.

The current distinction is:

**PRE-V3 may establish the objective social conditions in which forms of value, authority, dependence, labor, prestige, and consumption exist. It does not thereby establish what Nadia believes, wants, fears, enjoys, recognizes, or identifies with.**

Those must arise through the person-generating/developmental side, mediated by actual generated history.

### III.A. What belongs upstream

PRE-V3/Pyat must provide, wherever supported and required by the target world:

- Historically and geographically situated objective conditions.
- Material resources and distributions of access.
- Roles, positions, dependencies, organizations, and institutions.
- Labor, occupation, household, educational, religious, economic, and community structures.
- Constraints and affordances that make particular activities possible or impossible.
- Objective authority arrangements and opportunities for exercising power.
- Actual institutionally mediated rewards, sanctions, permissions, and obligations.
- Source-grounded social practices, where the evidence supports them.
- Patterns of distribution, variation, contradiction, minority configurations, and uncertainty.
- Time-addressed products, source scopes, currentness, and provenance.

A qualification: not every one of these is already a populated, generalized PRE-V3 product. Some are requirements or bounded source-backed possibilities. The PRE-V3 side cannot acquire fabricated empirical content simply because the Sorok application needs it.

For example, the software may need to represent a religious institution's property, organizational hierarchy, rituals, sanctions, and public norms. That does not imply Pyat already has an empirical rule that can generate every such institution in every society.

**A demand from Sorok cannot manufacture source authority in Pyat.**

See [Pyat's objective PRE-V3 map](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/7a5429ffbc48efd25e28c912da80c822e66bc182/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/ASTRA_PYAT_INTEGRATED_DEVELOPMENT_ATLAS_R1/report/N_OBJECTIVE_PRE_V3_MAP.md) and its [PRE-V3 → V3 wake criterion](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/7a5429ffbc48efd25e28c912da80c822e66bc182/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/ASTRA_PYAT_INTEGRATED_DEVELOPMENT_ATLAS_R1/report/P_PRE_V3_V3_WAKE_CRITERION.md).

### III.B. What the handoff must represent

The interface should convey **objective conditions for possible participation**, not inferred internal dispositions.

A conceptual upstream-to-actor packet would identify:

| Information | Example | What it does not authorize |
|---|---|---|
| Social formation | Historically specific consumer culture, religious organization, occupation or class relation | A corresponding belief or desire |
| Objective position | Household, employment, property, resource or authority position | An identity or motive |
| Opportunity | Access to goods, school, rituals, media, mobility or particular people | Actual participation |
| Constraint | Cost, exclusion, obligation, institutional rule, time or dependence | The person's subjective interpretation |
| Exposure route | A possibility of contact under specified conditions | Automatic experience or memory |
| Material mechanism | A specified operation connecting actors, resources or institutions | An automatic psychological consequence |
| Evidence and uncertainty | Exact source, period, geography, sample and applicability | A universal social law |

The next stage must establish **what actually happened**.

That requires decisions about generated actors, situations, histories, participation, stochastic choices where authorized, and performed rather than merely available opportunities.

### III.C. The mediating historical sequence

The indispensable chain is:

`OBJECTIVE SOCIAL CONDITION`

→ `ACTUAL OPPORTUNITY / CONSTRAINT`

→ `ACTOR-SPECIFIC ENCOUNTER OR ACTIVITY`

→ `ACTOR-SPECIFIC RECEIVABILITY AND RESPONSE`

→ `POSSIBLE RULE-GOVERNED DEVELOPMENTAL CONSEQUENCE`

→ `DURABLE OR UNCHANGED PERSON ORGANIZATION`.

Some historical experiences may matter through repetition, some through rare formative episodes, some through changed material circumstances without persistent psychological change, and some not at all.

The user-settled sparse-event architecture compresses long histories into a few consequential generated events or periods. That compression does not authorize imaginary deterministic effects. It requires the engine to preserve the causal distinction between accumulated historical formation and one isolated event label.

The boundary is especially important for **socially objectified value**.

An objective status hierarchy may make an expensive vehicle a recognizable prestige commodity. But Nadia's particular investment in it still requires historically formed actor-specific organization.

A religious institution may claim divine authority and exercise demonstrable institutional power. But Nadia's recognition, resistance, doubt, devotion, indifference, or contradictory relationship to that power must be generated downstream.

A household may contain an instrument. That does not establish that Nadia can play it, enjoys music, remembers acquiring it, or values it aesthetically.

### III.D. The connection to V3 is not merely a data transfer

A conventional pipeline might send a structural profile to a personality generator and ask for plausible traits.

The proposed Rawlsing architecture instead requires **the development of a person through objectively possible and actually generated participation in social life**.

The person then becomes capable of reproducing or changing the world that formed them.

This is where the Marx–Leontiev–Sève line becomes computationally important.

The theoretical unit is not a static correspondence between social attributes and individual preferences. It is an actual, historically unfolding relationship between material conditions, object-directed activity, motives, results, practices, and the individual organization formed through that activity.

The project's own P3 scientific adjudication recognizes that distinction. It cites Leontiev's separation of activity, action, operation, and motive; Sève's analysis of individual life as a system of socially related acts; Lahire's heterogeneous and context-dependent dispositions; and Dreier's account of participation across everyday settings.

These are theoretical guides, not empirical licenses for particular numerical update coefficients.

## IV. What counts as an object in the generalized engine?

The engineering target must not be restricted to furniture, personal belongings, or things visible in a domestic environment.

It needs a general concept of an **addressable target of activity and relation**, with specialized domain properties.

The strongest inherited Sorok formulation already says objects are one kind of target in a broader actor–world field.

The target may be a physical item, collection, place, practice, project, resource, institution, social role, ritual, document, image, concept, symbolic form, or particular person.

However, these do not all have the same ontology.

A physical book has material location, condition, ownership, and affordances.

A religious ideal can have historical formulations, institutions, carriers, practices, representations, and actor-specific understandings; it does not have the same physical identity as a copy of scripture.

A social hierarchy is not an ordinary object with a location on a shelf.

A commodity's market value cannot be equated with the actor's personal valuation of that commodity.

Thus the general engine should share **identity, history, typed relation, activity, causality, and retrieval machinery**, while using domain-specific adapters for relevant properties.

### IV.A. Six kinds of target distinction

| Distinction | Why engineering needs it |
|---|---|
| **Particular versus class** | One physical vehicle versus vehicles of a certain model; one signed album versus the artist's catalog |
| **Material carrier versus symbolic content** | A printed Bible versus a theological proposition within it |
| **Individual object versus social form** | One luxury watch versus a historically constituted market for prestige commodities |
| **Institution versus actor's conception of it** | A church organization versus Nadia's beliefs about its authority |
| **Objective relation versus actor-relative investment** | A book was gifted by a parent versus Nadia experiences retaining gifts as obligatory |
| **Concrete operation versus abstract meaning** | Lending a book versus what that act is understood to signify |

These distinctions must be represented by referentially compatible but semantically different data.

Otherwise the engine could confuse changing the physical location of a book with changing the book's role in Nadia's self-understanding, or treat disagreement with a theological doctrine as physically changing the institution in which it was taught.

### IV.B. A generalized target should support four kinds of relationships

**Material:** physical composition, location, state, access, ownership, control, transformation, production, consumption, scarcity, and dependency.

**Historical-social:** events, actors, institutions, labor, exchange, social positions, practices, norms, authority, and the origin and reproduction of social forms.

**Actor-relative:** historically constituted investments, associations, expectations, valuations, identification, aversion, remembered and nondeclarative traces, and the actor's own understanding.

**Event-relative:** the specific operation being attempted or performed now, with all current material, relational, and actor-state circumstances.

All four are necessary. None is sufficient in isolation.

### IV.C. What “value” means in this design

I would refuse any schema whose central field is simply:

`object.value = 0.87`.

Instead, value-related phenomena must remain distinguishable by their **causal kind**.

| Dimension | The actual question |
|---|---|
| Use and affordance | What can this object practically do, and for whom under which conditions? |
| Market/exchange | Within what market or property relations does it acquire exchangeability, price, scarcity, or monetary consequences? |
| Labor and objectification | Whose activity produced, transformed, maintained, transported, or appropriated it? |
| Possession/control | Who can use, alter, transfer, protect, withhold, or exclude access to it? |
| Psychic investment | What historically formed organization makes this object desirable, aversive, gratifying, reassuring, shameful, or otherwise consequential for this person? |
| Social distinction | What classifications and power relations make possession or display socially consequential? |
| Collective symbolic authority | Through which institutions, practices, histories, or communities does an object or ideal acquire recognized power? |
| Actor-attributed authority | Where does the person understand the power or obligation to originate? |
| Self-identification | How does the target participate in the person's actual self-model, desired identity, or relation to former selves? |
| Material reproduction of power | Which actual practices preserve, challenge, or transform the social relations that sustain its authority? |

These are **query dimensions and causal distinctions**, not ten independent numeric dimensions to be assigned to every object.

An item may have no meaningful answer in several domains.

Others may possess all of them simultaneously.

An object may produce practical pleasure through use, identify the owner with an admired social position, and participate in a real system of exploitative production—all without those facts carrying the same kind of value.

### IV.D. Alienation is a relation, not an object attribute

The software must distinguish:

- Objectification of human activity.
- Appropriation and recognition of one's own capacities or creations.
- Objective dependence on products, institutions, markets, or powers.
- Socially produced powers that operate independently of an individual's will.
- Reification or naturalization of historically contingent relations.
- Actor attribution of authority to the objectified form.
- Conscious resistance, partial recognition, misrecognition, or indifference.
- Practical reproduction of a structure even when the actor understands its social origins.

A high degree of attachment does not establish alienation.

A low degree of conscious attachment does not establish emancipation.

Someone may know that market prestige is socially constituted while still depending materially on the market. Another may revere a religious institution without recognizing the human labor and power relations reproducing it. Another may recognize those relations and continue religious participation for reasons that are neither reducible to obedience nor automatically unalienated.

Rawlsing should not issue a universal verdict about such people.

It should make the relevant historical, institutional, and actor-specific relations computationally traceable.

The distinction between **produced authority and actor-attributed authority** becomes a particularly useful query family, without requiring a new psychological primitive.

### V.B. The fundamental V3 distinction: organization versus current activation

For example, Nadia's possession of prestigious objects may be historically related to a durable sensitivity to recognition.

That does not mean she actively seeks recognition whenever one of those objects is present.

A particular recognition-related mediator may be dormant, inhibited, contextually irrelevant, competing with another disposition, or materially inapplicable.

The current actor/event system must distinguish:

`DURABLE HISTORICAL ORGANIZATION`

from

`CURRENT ELIGIBILITY / RECRUITMENT`

from

`ACTUAL RESPONSE`

from

`SUBSEQUENT DURABLE CHANGE`.

Those cannot be represented as a single `status_desire` field that is read whenever a luxury object appears.

The P3 received sourcefree projection code already distinguishes `ELIGIBLE`, `INHIBITED`, and `UNKNOWN`, preserves simultaneous records, and treats material eligibility separately from psychological eligibility.

That is an important reusable **mechanical pattern**, though its current TEST_ONLY source selectors are not a native theory of psychic activation.

### V.C. The minimal scientific task concerning object investment

To make this genuinely generalized, V3 must be able to represent **historically formed dispositions to invest in kinds of activity, forms of recognition, social ideals, material practices, and particular targets**.

Not all of these must be permanent state fields.

Where an investment is merely a current consequence of already-established organization, it should be derived.

Where it has become a durable, causally consequential organization through repeated history or a qualified formative process, the competent V3 owner should persist the relevant change.

Examples of necessary distinctions:

An actor can repeatedly purchase goods for public status while feeling no attachment to any one item.

Another can form an attachment to one item without having a broad possessive disposition.

An actor can be intensely devoted to a religious ideal while opposing institutions that claim to represent it.

Another can habitually obey institutional authority without consciously valuing its symbolic claims.

An actor can experience aesthetic pleasure without wanting ownership.

Another can obtain satisfaction from exclusive ownership without actually using the object.

Another can be materially dependent on an institution whose authority they openly reject.

**These must be representably different cases arising from history**, not exceptions patched into one generalized investment coefficient.

### V.D. Socially learned concepts are a real missing frontier

The inherited Sorok function register contains:

- `N10` — later ideology, theology and cosmological rearticulation.
- `O13` — socially learned concepts.
- `M06` — multidimensional alienation.
- `M11` — frameworks as metabolic operators.
- `M20` — magnets / relational field.
- `M21` — pressure-flow history and causal trace.
- `M26` — stored-versus-derived separation.

Those are valuable recovered conceptual and historical implementation assets.

But the exact PROGRAMMA function crosswalk does **not** qualify their historical code as an already adopted native modern psychological implementation. `O13` remains an open science/mechanism frontier; `M06` has recovered historical-function evidence, not a current authoritative alienation engine.

This matters because our new conception requires something very close to **socially mediated formation of concepts and valuations**.

A person can adopt concepts that subsequently become filters through which they understand social reality, themselves, and material objects.

Those concepts can be explicit doctrines, embodied practical discriminations, habitual classifications, diffuse expectations, or self-organizing frameworks.

The actual mechanism of acquisition, stabilization, differentiation, contradiction and transformation is not yet fully issued.

We should not evade that missing science by storing an LLM-generated paragraph called `Nadia's worldview`.

### V.E. Alienation and identification: historical processes, not permanent psychometric axes

The Sorok source material articulates two important possible movements:

`IDENTIFICATION → INTERNALIZATION → NATURALIZATION → INVISIBILITY → AGENT-OF`

and

`ALIENATION → EXTERNALIZATION → OBJECTIFICATION → VISIBILITY → AGENCY-AGAINST`.

These are useful analytical sequences, not compulsory life trajectories or settled machine operators.

An actor might naturalize a social relation until it is experienced as reality itself. An earlier identification might later become visible as an object of criticism. That criticism might liberate some capacity while producing new distortions. The person might subsequently identify with a different ideal, whose contingent historical origins eventually become invisible in turn.

But the source's later correction explicitly prohibits introducing an independent monolithic interpretive-framework owner, a compulsory self-retcon event type, or an unissued framework-response operator enum merely because these descriptions are compelling.

The engineering requirement is instead to preserve the actual historical and state relationships from which those dynamics can be **derived or generated through competent existing transitions**.

## VI. The durable state model: what must actually be stored

The key engineering problem is **not how to write rich enough descriptions**.

It is how to preserve a compact, canonical, queryable organization of reality that can generate rich descriptions and consequential conduct without repeatedly reconstructing its own causation.

The proposed kernel needs the following logical records. Some correspond to existing owner schemas; others are cross-owner interface requirements. **These are not instructions to create seven new databases.**

| Logical record | Authoritative content |
|---|---|
| **Target identity/state** | Exact object, person, place, practice, institution, artifact, concept or other target; current material properties and effective-time state |
| **Objective occurrence** | Actual event or process, participants, affected targets, performed operations, time, source, provenance and effects |
| **Social-form relation** | Evidence-bounded participation in markets, institutions, roles, norms, hierarchies, practices or collectively maintained systems |
| **Actor-specific history** | What this actor actually encountered, did, experienced, learned or developed, under its competent owner |
| **Actor-target historical relation** | Evidence-supported use, investment, appropriation, avoidance, practice, affiliation, attribution or other relation to a target |
| **State/epistemic references** | Exact versioned links into existing CAN/ADP person, memory, belief and relationship owners |
| **Materialization and derivation receipts** | What was proposed, committed, rejected, retrieved or derived, with exact source and causal-closure identity |

### VI.A. Typed relationships are nonnegotiable

A graph link cannot merely say `RELATED`.

It must distinguish different kinds of relation.

A target may have been produced by one actor, owned by another, used by another, displayed by another, cared for by another, associated by Nadia with an absent person, and used by an institution to exercise authority.

An actor may believe a target has divine authority while Rawlsing separately records the institution's objective social mechanisms.

A luxury commodity may participate in a prestige economy, but the fact that the actor possesses it does not prove identification with that economy.

A historical event may contribute to both Nadia's religious practice and her relation to certain objects without either outcome directly causing the other.

The graph must therefore support **different types of relations and different epistemic/custody statuses for claims about those relations**.

Examples of conceptual predicates include:

| Relation family | Examples |
|---|---|
| Material and property | `LOCATED_AT`, `OWNED_BY`, `CONTROLLED_BY`, `AVAILABLE_TO`, `CONSTRUCTED_FROM`, `REPAIRED_IN` |
| Activity | `USED_IN`, `PRODUCED_BY`, `MAINTAINED_BY`, `ENABLED`, `CONSTRAINED`, `PARTICIPATED_IN` |
| Social formation | `MEDIATED_BY_INSTITUTION`, `SUBJECT_TO_RULE`, `CIRCULATED_WITHIN`, `EXPRESSED_SOCIAL_POSITION` |
| Historical causation | `CONTRIBUTED_TO`, `DEPENDENT_ON`, `GENERATED_BY`, `CO_OCCURRED_WITH` |
| Actor-relative | `INVESTED_IN`, `IDENTIFIED_WITH`, `ASSOCIATED_WITH`, `ATTRIBUTED_AUTHORITY_TO` |
| Epistemic | `BELIEVED`, `REMEMBERED`, `MISIDENTIFIED`, `CLAIMED`, `HAD_ACCESS_TO` |

These names illustrate required semantic distinctions. They are **not a proposal to canonize these exact strings as one universal enum**.

The implementation must identify which relations already have legitimate owner/schema representations and which truly require additional typed records.

For many actor-relative relationships, a derived relation over existing V3 history will be preferable to a standalone edge claiming psychological truth.

### VI.B. Every consequential edge requires provenance

A useful internal relation needs more than `source_id` and `target_id`.

Depending on its semantic kind, it must preserve:

- Identity of the competent source/owner.
- Root, branch, actor and relevant target.
- Valid/effective time or interval.
- Admission or derivation time.
- Exact parent occurrences or state references.
- The applicable rule/version when a causal effect is asserted.
- Evidence of direct occurrence versus inferred pattern.
- Scope, uncertainty, alternatives and counterevidence where applicable.
- Whether the relation is objective, actor-attributed, analyst-derived, hypothetical or actor-accessible.
- Its relationship to the current causal-closure snapshot.
- Version/hash identity sufficient for cold reconstruction and invalidation.

The key constraint:

**A graph edge that can alter later actor response must be auditable back to admitted reality or a qualified historical person-state rule.**

An LLM assertion alone cannot supply that authority.

### VI.C. Hyperedges and historical episodes

Not every important causal relationship is binary.

Suppose several people collectively produce an artifact under an institutional arrangement. Its significance may depend on participants, labor division, ownership, conditions, time, materials and later exchange.

Reducing that event to:

`Nadia → object`

or even:

`worker → made → object`

would lose essential causal relations.

The indexing system should therefore be able to navigate a **multi-party occurrence node with typed roles**.

This does not require a specialized hypergraph database. A stable event identity with participant/role edges can represent the relation.

The broader point is that social production and institutional mediation are often relational structures involving more than two entities.

### VI.D. Temporal state must not be overwritten

The system needs both **history** and **current materialized state**.

An object could have been owned, borrowed, gifted, damaged, repaired, exchanged and later discarded.

Its current ownership is a projection of its history.

Likewise, an actor's relation to the object may change through actual activity, even if the physical object remains unchanged.

The durable record should retain exact historical events and relevant owner-state transitions. A current-state index should tell the runtime what is true *now*.

No requirement follows that the model must replay all historical events every turn.

And no permission follows to delete old relations merely because a newer interpretation dominates Nadia's present self-understanding.

### VI.E. Unknown, absent, unresolved and impossible are different

A particularly dangerous implementation shortcut would be:

`no relation row found → no relation exists`.

That is false in a lazily materialized world.

The system needs to distinguish:

**Unknown:** no sufficient information has been admitted.

**Unmaterialized:** the world has not yet resolved the particular at the requested granularity.

**Valid absence:** the system has actually established that the fact/relation does not exist in the relevant scope.

**Unresolved conflict:** multiple candidate resolutions remain incompatible or insufficiently supported.

**Inaccessible:** the fact may be canonically known, but the present actor or user is not entitled to know it.

**Source/authority unavailable:** the required competent producer or rule has not issued an admissible result.

These cannot be treated as interchangeable null values.

A user could ask about a book whose donor is already canonically known but forgotten by Nadia, or whose donor has not yet been computationally materialized.

The correct operation differs enormously.

### VI.F. Persistence and indexing should be physically separated from LLM context

The existing received sourcefree P3 implementation provides useful engineering capital:

- `P3Owner` composes over the existing P2 owner.
- Its normalized `PsycheStore` uses SQLite rather than requiring full life-state rewrites.
- The store provides indexed queries, immutable records, content hashes and exact binding to root/actor/branch/source/rule.
- Cross-store work uses the phases `PREPARED → WORLD_COMMITTED → PSYCH_COMMITTED → COMPLETE`.
- Pending operations, idempotent replay, stale-state refusal and crash recovery are explicit concerns.

This is not yet an object-value graph.

But its storage and transaction machinery deserves direct reuse analysis before anyone implements a new persistent subsystem.

Sources: [P3 owner](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/7a5429ffbc48efd25e28c912da80c822e66bc182/WORKER_RETURNS/SOROK-P3-COMPRESSED-DEVELOPMENTAL-STATE-R1/p3/p3_owner.py), [P3 state store](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/7a5429ffbc48efd25e28c912da80c822e66bc182/WORKER_RETURNS/SOROK-P3-COMPRESSED-DEVELOPMENTAL-STATE-R1/p3/state_store.py), and [P3 integration contract](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/7a5429ffbc48efd25e28c912da80c822e66bc182/WORKER_RETURNS/SOROK-P3-COMPRESSED-DEVELOPMENTAL-STATE-R1/INTEGRATION_CONTRACT.md).

The proposed architecture should retain:

`CANONICAL OWNER RECORDS + DERIVED RELATIONAL INDEXES + VERSIONED RUNTIME PROJECTIONS`.

Not:

`OBJECT BIOGRAPHY TEXT + PSYCHE TEXT + RELATIONSHIP TEXT → ONE GIANT PROMPT`.

## VII. The generative pipeline: objective surface → anticipatory historical constitution

This is the first major **missing executable capability**.

The user has specified the scheduling concept; Sorok's earlier documents supply much of the underlying consistency and causal law.

### VII.A. Initial scene/surface realization

The foreground process receives a lawful current world, actor, situation and event context.

It produces a bounded objective occurrence or observable environmental surface containing admitted or proposed particulars.

The sequence must be:

`PROPOSED SURFACE`

→ `OBJECTIVE ASSERTION VALIDATION / ADMISSION`

→ `STABLE TARGET IDENTITIES`

→ `PUBLICATION OF OBSERVABLE SURFACE`.

Only after admission should the user encounter objective assertions as binding facts.

An object can initially be identified at coarse resolution. A room might contain “several musical instruments” without specifying every instrument's identity.

The declaration is still binding at the resolution actually asserted.

### VII.B. Automatic referent extraction

Every newly admitted addressable referent becomes a potential historical-materialization target.

The system must distinguish:

- Already canonical targets mentioned again.
- New targets with sufficient initial identity.
- Newly introduced collections requiring later member resolution.
- Particulars that belong to an existing target.
- References to abstract practices, institutions, ideals or social forms.
- Mere hypothetical descriptions that do not assert existence.
- Actor testimony whose content has not been independently admitted as objective truth.

The last distinction is essential.

If Nadia says that a friend owns a particular instrument, the speech event is admitted. The instrument's objective existence and ownership require their own appropriate evidential and world-admission treatment.

A proposed surface shouldn't create hidden canonical objects merely because an actor mentions them.

### VII.C. The materialization queue

A generalized background process needs a work item containing at least:

| Field family | Function |
|---|---|
| Exact target identity | Which unresolved entity or collection requires further resolution |
| Root/branch/epoch | Which fictional history and continuity the job belongs to |
| Current world/person versions | What authoritative state the candidate must reconcile against |
| Known facts and exclusions | What the model is forbidden to contradict |
| Open resolution dimensions | Which physical, historical or social particulars remain unresolved |
| Causal-dependency scope | Which activities, histories or owner-state links are relevant |
| Requested materialization depth | How far ahead of actual need the job should resolve |
| Trigger and priority | Surface introduction, causal dependency, or actual inspection/action |
| Source and model provenance | Which constraints, external facts, generation policy and model produced the candidate |
| Completion/admission state | Pending, admitted, partially admitted, held, rejected, stale or canceled |

This is a **proposed work-item contract**, not an adopted schema.

Jobs must not silently inherit the querying user's private psychological profile.

The user may influence which unresolved target becomes urgent through actual action. Their desires, interpretations and inferred preferences cannot determine the independent target's prior history.

### VII.D. What the background LLM actually generates

For a physical object, the model should propose a bounded historical explanation of its present condition.

For an institution or practice, it may need to resolve appropriate historical or social relationships.

For a symbol or ideal, it may need to resolve its historically available meanings, carriers, practices and Nadia's actual exposure routes.

Candidate outputs might include:

- Material properties and physical affordances.
- Manufacture, production, acquisition, circulation, transformation and present condition.
- Relevant time periods and prior occurrences.
- Participation by other actors and institutions.
- Links to already existing Nadia history, practice, position, relationships and projects.
- Established social forms through which the target is recognized, circulated or valued.
- Plausible actor-specific historical participation where unresolved and authorized.
- Possible dependencies requiring more resolution.
- Explicit unknowns and unresolved alternatives.
- Candidate historical causal effects, separately distinguished from descriptive facts.

The LLM must **not** be instructed to generate a psychological payoff, secret, trauma, symbolic explanation or significant relationship for every target.

It should preserve low-significance history and contingency.

### VII.E. Historical bridge admission

Generated candidates must undergo **backward historical constraint and forward causal reconciliation**.

The earlier Sorok 70 architecture proposes the H0–H3 impact classes:

| Impact | Historical-materialization consequence |
|---|---|
| H0 | Physical/descriptive detail with no durable psychological alteration |
| H1 | Episodic or associative relation, subject to actual memory/access rules |
| H2 | Bounded contribution to already established durable history or practice |
| H3 | Major formative event; presumptively unavailable for casual late invention |

**H0–H3 do not measure present psychic investment.**

An H0 acquisition can produce a materially ordinary new object that becomes significant because Nadia already has a powerful, historically constituted disposition toward its category or social form.

And an H2 historical artifact may presently leave her indifferent.

Historical causal weight and current investment must be modeled separately.

### VII.F. Backward compatibility is insufficient

Suppose a newly proposed historical fact does not contradict any recorded proposition.

It could still be causally inadmissible because that fact would have changed previously calculated events.

For example, inventing years of private musical training might not directly contradict a sparse biography. But it would change her technical capacities and potentially past actions.

If those periods are causally closed, the generator cannot simply add the training while pretending the earlier computation was unaffected.

Sorok's historical bridge must therefore evaluate **causal compatibility**, not just textual consistency.

That requires bounded causal slack, affected developmental domains, time windows, and appropriate contributor classes.

Unknown history is not unlimited retroactive creative authority.

### VII.G. Forward propagation

A lawfully admitted historical particular may have several consequences:

`OBJECT HISTORY → MATERIAL PRESENT`

`OBJECT HISTORY → EPISODIC ASSOCIATION`

`OBJECT HISTORY → EXISTING PRACTICE LINK`

`OBJECT HISTORY → SOCIAL-RELATIONAL NETWORK`

`OBJECT HISTORY → BOUNDED DEVELOPMENTAL RESIDUE`.

Each must follow the appropriate owner.

The background process must not directly write new CAN-011 mediators or ADP-016 memories because a proposed narrative says Nadia “was deeply changed.”

It may supply candidate historical parentage. Only qualified developmental or memory semantics can determine actual state effects.

### VII.H. Historical completion is transactional

A successful background generation must return an admission result with enough information to preserve:

- Exact accepted and rejected candidate claims.
- Source and model provenance.
- Before/after owner-state identity.
- Effective historical dates.
- Actor/root/branch/time ownership.
- Link identities and relevant causal parents.
- Version and historical-closure compatibility.
- Unresolved or held effects.
- Idempotent repeat behavior.

If some proposed claims depend jointly on a historical event, the transaction cannot commit one part while leaving incompatible or dangling dependent facts.

The system needs an admission unit based on **causal dependency**, not merely one JSON row.

This may be implemented through existing owner transactions and a coordinated receipt protocol, rather than a new all-powerful cross-owner writer.

### VII.I. The new historical index becomes immediately available

After admission, all corresponding derived indexes must be updated or invalidated coherently.

The object should now be retrievable by identity, physical location, historical event, producer, actor, institutional relationship, relevant practice and owner-state linkage.

Equally, an independent Nadia activity that depends on that object should be able to discover it without the user's having inspected it.

Thus the result is not merely a completed object history.

It is **new, causally situated material available to future Rawlsing computation**.

## VIII. The retrieval engine: how the relevant history gets summoned

This is probably the single most important new software component.

We need to prevent the current actor-response model from having to reconstruct a web of historical relationships inside a single inference call.

The engine should already possess **derived bidirectional indexes over authoritative causal relations**.

### VIII.A. Required index families


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Target adjacency index**

Given an object, place, ideal, institution or other referent, find its directly connected events, states, actors, conditions and other targets.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Temporal and causal index**

Retrieve relevant prior events, effective-time intervals, causal predecessors, consequences, checkpoints and bounded historical pathways.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Actor-state reverse index**

From a specific CAN/ADP actor-state or history reference, retrieve relevant objects, activities, people and social relations implicated in its generated history.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Actor–counterpart–network index**

Find the model of a particular other, objective dyadic events, relevant relationship conventions, third-party ties and actual shared experiences.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Social-form and institutional index**

Find connections through organizations, commodities, normative systems, classed practices, authority, property relations, shared symbols and institutional roles.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Current activity/dependency index**

Find material prerequisites, tools, ongoing obligations, costs, available opportunities and processes that may become relevant to the current event.


<box background="surface-secondary" padding={2} radius="md"></box>

**Epistemic-access index**

Distinguish objective facts from actor-local memory, belief, uncertainty, claims and what the user has actually perceived.


These are **logical access paths**, not a mandate for seven physically separate index tables.

### VIII.B. What should be indexed?

The index should contain stable references, typed connections, temporal scope, owner-state version, source-grade information and derivation identities.

It should not contain a competing canonical summary of Nadia's mind.

That would create a new problem: an old indexed psychological description might remain available even after Nadia's actual state changes.

Instead, the index should point to the authoritative state or durable historical event, and the current runtime should verify relevant versions before consuming it.

### VIII.C. Retrieval is not a semantic search over everything

Vector similarity may be useful for discovering candidates, language references, or semantically related text.

But **semantic similarity cannot establish causation**.

Two objects might have similar descriptions while belonging to entirely different historical and relational processes.
Two different objects might be profoundly connected through a single event without sharing words in their descriptions.

A metaphor might be relevant to Nadia's current self-understanding without being an objective cause of the object.

The core mechanism therefore needs typed relational traversal and owner-state lookup.

Embeddings should be supplementary, and their results must remain hypotheses until qualified.

### VIII.D. The three stages of relevance

I would make this a hard design distinction:

**Connectivity:** What is legitimately connected to this event or target?

**Eligibility:** Which of those connections could lawfully matter given the present activity, person, material circumstances and actual encounter?

**Recruitment:** Which eligible streams actually participate in the actor's current response through CAN-008?

Connectivity belongs to indexed retrieval.

Eligibility requires source- and state-conditioned filtering.

Recruitment belongs to the actor's event-time causal mechanism.

A retrieval engine should not declare something psychologically active merely because it is reachable.

### VIII.E. Search needs negative and inhibiting information

Suppose Nadia has a history of valuing status.

A retrieval system that returns only `STATUS_DESIRE` when the user compliments a prestigious possession will produce a caricature.

It also needs access to relevant competing conditions: fatigue, material urgency, recent conflict, a current rejection of a particular status performance, a relationship-specific inhibition, an active project, or a current practical requirement.

Some of those might dominate behavior; some might not.

The retrieval system must not decide by itself that one is the winner.

But it has to be able to **surface the competing pathways to the competent causal process**.

This means a bounded query cannot merely find the highest-scoring positive associations. It must preserve possible conflict, inhibition, material impossibility, uncertainty and valid absence.

### VIII.F. Whole-person continuity versus selective history

Sorok 101 correctly insists on a substantial **whole-person expressive substrate**.

A runtime LLM should not reconstruct Nadia from three retrieved memories.

But that doesn't mean supplying the entire historical event ledger.

The proposed architecture should combine:

`STABLE CURRENT WHOLE-PERSON PROJECTION`

with

`SELECTIVE VERSIONED HISTORICAL AND TARGET CONTEXT`.

The first carries the relatively complete current causal/expressive organization necessary for this person to remain recognizable across encounters.

The second supplies exact historical dependencies pertinent to the current event.

This is different from having a small generic persona description plus a large retrieval-augmented prose context.

The stable substrate must be **derived from actual owner state**, not from an independently authored character sheet that slowly drifts away from the computation.

### VIII.G. Graph reachability is not enough

A highly connected event can become a retrieval magnet.

If every book is connected to Nadia's family history and every family event is connected to her religious history, naïve traversal can pull the whole biography into every interaction.

The retrieval system needs causal and epistemic restrictions:

- Relationship type and direct event relevance.
- Temporal and effective-time compatibility.
- Actor-specific current state.
- Material or activity dependency.
- Known historical causal contribution.
- Relationship-specific scope.
- Actor access and actual perception.
- Competing and inhibiting streams.
- Controlled expansion depth and retrieval budget.

A missing source should not be replaced with a psychological guess merely because the query budget has been exhausted.

And a system that gets quicker by silently losing causal parents is not a valid optimization.

### VIII.H. Incremental maintenance

When a newly materialized fact is admitted, indexes should update only the affected neighborhoods.

When Nadia's durable state changes, derived actor-state mappings and caches must be invalidated or rebuilt as appropriate.

When a relationship changes, current relationship-sensitive context should reflect that update without rewriting the object history.

When a shared root is forked, new branch-local relations should stay isolated.

The implementation needs:

`CANONICAL COMMIT → DEPENDENCY INVALIDATION → INDEX VERSION UPDATE → NEW READER SNAPSHOT`.

That allows fast steady-state reads without sacrificing integrity.

## IX. The event-time causal-context compiler

The retrieval indexes solve the problem of **finding available history**.

They do not solve the problem of **what becomes Nadia in this particular encounter**.

A second missing component must compile the relevant authoritative material into the correct input for her existing event-time machinery.

I would provisionally call it the **Situated Causal-Context Compiler**.

That name describes its responsibility, not a request for another psychological owner.

### IX.A. Exact inputs

The compiler should consume:

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Actual present operation/event | Competent world/action owner |
| Nadia's current durable person state | Competent V3 owner |
| Current actor needs, expectations and strivings | CAN-010 and current authorized state |
| Relevant mediators, tensions and practices | CAN-011 |
| Relevant material/organismic condition | CAN-009 |
| Current actor activity, obligations and projects | World/person runtime |
| Target's objective state and history | World/history owner |
| Target's social and institutional relationships | Source-qualified world/history records |
| Nadia's actual target-related historical participation | Admitted actor/history records |
| Nadia's current actor-relative target investments | Qualified stored or derived person–target relation |
| Particular counterpart and Nadia's model of them | Actor-owned person–other state |
| Dyadic, role and network context | Competent relationship/person/world owners |
| Relevant actor-accessible memories and beliefs | ADP-016/017 |
| Root/branch/time/source validity | Existing custody and owner identities |

Not all fields are present or relevant in every event.

A valid response may be produced with no significant object-related psychic recruitment.

### IX.B. The compiler's output is not the actor's feeling

It should produce a **bounded, typed causal-context packet** containing qualified parent references and candidate recruitment streams.

It should not produce a final psychological diagnosis or a universal emotional score.

For example, a package might establish that the current event involves:

- A particular target's physical state.
- Its connection to a past gift.
- A presently relevant relation-specific expectation.
- A current use or access conflict.
- A source-qualified durable mediator potentially applicable to the event.
- A competing current project or obligation.
- Actor-local memory that is accessible, inaccessible or uncertain.
- A counterpart model relevant to how the act may be understood.
- Inhibiting circumstances or missing causal authority.

Those become inputs to CAN-008 and, where required, the higher-fidelity model-mediated person×event process.

### IX.C. Separate causal and actor-accessible views

This must be engineered as a real information boundary.

The **causal view** can include source-qualified historical parentage and nonconscious organization that may affect Nadia's actual response.

The **actor-accessible view** may include only the memories, beliefs, perceptions and claims legitimately available to Nadia at that point.

Then the **observable occurrence** is an additional, narrower result of her actual conduct.

Finally the **user's phenomenal projection** is a view of that observable occurrence through actual perceptual access.

These are not interchangeable prompt sections.

Sorok 101's explicit noncollapse is:

`CAUSAL NADIA ≠ OBSERVABLE NADIA ≠ USER-EXPERIENCED NADIA ≠ USER-INTERPRETED NADIA`.

The compiler must preserve this separation physically enough that privileged hidden causal material cannot accidentally be surfaced as Nadia's first-person explanation or as omniscient narrative commentary.

### IX.D. Source-bound streams, not a mood blender

CAN-008's actual contract permits simultaneous streams and typed effect kinds such as `BUFFER`, `AMPLIFY`, `DEFLECT`, `REROUTE`, `TRANSFORM`, and `BLOCK`.

But those are **response-effect operators**, not a general vocabulary of moods.

They cannot automatically be applied to arbitrary sourcefree psychological categories without a governing rule.

The compiler must supply exact eligible parents, source identifiers, current-state refs and relevant circumstances.

An authorized event-time rule then determines what happens.

It should remain possible that several streams operate concurrently, that one is inhibited, that another produces an immediate effect without durable change, or that the event generates no relevant psychological response.

### IX.E. Object investment can operate in solitude

This is one of the strongest requirements emerging from the latest discussion.

The compiler must not require a human counterpart in every psychologically consequential object event.

An actor may experience satisfaction, identification, reverence, disgust, anxiety or comfort through possession or use while alone.

The social history that formed that relationship remains part of the causal record, but its present operation need not be interpersonal.

Therefore:

`COUNTERPART = NONE`

must be a valid condition for some actor–target events.

It must not produce an automatic `NO_MEANING` or `NO_PSYCHIC_INVESTMENT`.

### IX.F. Particular other-person models remain essential when applicable

When the event involves another person, however, their identity and Nadia's model of them can matter enormously.

The same historical object, actual action and spoken sentence may recruit very different streams depending on Nadia's beliefs about the other person, their shared history, expected judgment, obligations, trust, resentment, status asymmetry and current activity.

A compliment can be welcome, embarrassing, suspect, irritating, irrelevant, intimate or merely polite.

None of those meanings follows automatically from the compliment's surface form.

The compiler must preserve the **actor's model of the particular other**, not substitute the other's actual private mind.

And the external user's inferred psyche-profile must not be smuggled into Nadia's model. Nadia can act upon what she has actually perceived or learned about that user.

That boundary keeps this mechanism separate from the second goal—the user's implicit psyche-conditioned phenomenal surface.

### IX.G. Formation is not activation

Suppose an existing object relationship changes after a consequential event.

Perhaps Nadia experiences a rupture, forms a durable practice, or acquires a new understanding of a symbolic ideal.

That durable change cannot be committed by the context compiler.

The appropriate sequence is:

`ACTUAL EVENT`

→ `CAN-008 CURRENT RESPONSE`

→ `COMPETENT TARGET-SPECIFIC DURABLE EFFECT, IF ANY`

→ `AUTHORITATIVE STATE COMMIT`

→ `INDEX/CACHE INVALIDATION`

→ `FUTURE EVENT'S REAL FIRST CONSUMER`.

This is the point where the architecture closes the loop between social world, person development and later activity.

A derived interpretation becomes durably causal only when its actual adoption, practice or other transformation is supported by a competent event and rule.

## X. Asynchronous orchestration: how the two LLM lanes actually cooperate

This is a separate engineering problem from the graph's semantics.

We need a runtime in which foreground interaction is not blocked by unconstrained historical generation, while historically consequential facts are never used before valid admission.

### X.A. Two cooperating lanes

The historical Sorok live architecture provisionally aims at two high-capability model calls for ordinary actor/world event realization. The first handles semantic/causal transduction; the second realizes an authorized observable occurrence.

The newly proposed background materializer adds another inference workload. It is **not included in the old two-call budget merely by being called background work**.

Therefore the actual architecture is:


Authoritative Rawlsing state
World · actor · social history · event and owner versions

<row justify="center"></row>


**Foreground lane**

1. Interpret actual user/actor/world event.

2. Compute lawful causal consequences.

3. Produce and admit observable occurrence.

4. Return user-visible phenomenal projection.


**Background lane**

1. Receive newly addressable targets/dependencies.

2. Retrieve canonical historical context.

3. Propose bounded historical/material depth.

4. Reconcile, admit, interlink and index.


<row justify="center"></row>

**Versioned canonical convergence**

New historical facts and actual events share the same authoritative world, branch and source custody.


The two lanes cannot independently own the same facts.

### X.B. The execution sequence

A normal cycle should proceed as follows.

**1.** The foreground process consumes current admitted state.

**2.** It generates an objective occurrence containing some previously unresolved addressable targets.

**3.** Rawlsing validates and admits those binding assertions.

**4.** The user receives the surface.

**5.** A background process is immediately scheduled for newly introduced targets and unresolved relevant dependencies.

**6.** The historical generator proposes deeper particulars using versioned canonical state.

**7.** The historical bridge admits lawful depth and updates indexes.

**8.** When the user or another actor acts, the next event-time process retrieves the available admitted object/world/person context.

**9.** Newly introduced particulars or causal dependencies schedule further background resolution.

This is a logical sequence, not a requirement that every step occurs as a new heavyweight process or separate LLM invocation.

### X.C. Job priority must reflect causal urgency

A useful priority order would be:

| Priority | Trigger | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocking causal dependency** | Actual world or actor operation cannot proceed without a particular unresolved fact | Resolve before the dependent operation is committed |
| **Imminent interaction dependency** | An already available target is being actively inspected or used | Use available committed detail; resolve genuinely missing necessary detail with bounded targeted work |
| **Ahead-of-attention prefetch** | New targets have just entered the observable environment | Batch bounded historical/material depth asynchronously |
| **Ordinary independent-world demand** | A background actor or process requires hidden world detail | Schedule according to its causal-time requirement |
| **Low-priority historical enrichment** | Additional lawful depth would improve future consistency but has no immediate consumer | Defer, batch or omit according to the budget |

This priority is about **objective dependency and availability**, not about predicting which object the user's hidden psyche will find fascinating.

A system may of course recognize that the user has just physically approached an object. That actual activity can make its physical dependencies urgent.

But this must not condition what the object's prior history becomes.

### X.D. Stale background results

A materialization job may start against one world state and finish after another event has changed that world.

Every result therefore needs a versioned readset or equivalent source-bound snapshot.

Admission must revalidate its assumptions.

The outcome might be:

`COMMIT` — all required parents remain compatible.

`REBASE / REVALIDATE` — a compatible historical candidate can be admitted against the newer state without rewriting earlier consequences.

`PARTIAL` — independent compatible facts can be admitted, but a dependent portion is held.

`STALE / REJECT` — the proposal is no longer lawful.

`SOURCE_HOLD` — the required rule/source is unavailable.

These are proposed response categories, not inherited exact status strings.

**The background LLM must never become a concurrent second owner capable of overwriting present-world changes.**

### X.E. What if inspection arrives first?

This is the race we cannot avoid.

The user might inspect an object immediately after it first appears, before its background job completes.

Three outcomes are possible.

The requested detail may already be canonically determined; the foreground can return it.

The requested physical specificity may be within what is already admitted, while historical details remain unresolved. The foreground can lawfully provide the physical evidence without inventing provenance.

Or the action may genuinely require an unresolved fact. The event then becomes a blocking causal dependency and must wait for a bounded valid resolution or return a truthful incomplete/held result.

**An asynchronous architecture reduces expected waiting; it cannot make unresolved necessary facts instantaneous by declaration.**

### X.F. Background generation must not mutate closed history

For shared pre-contact Nadia, historical materialization must respect the seed root, the root's version, and the causal closure of any already running user branches.

Different users must not receive semantically different underlying persons under the same claimed root/version merely because they inspected targets in different orders.

Late materialization of common pre-contact facts should be reproducible under governed root constraints, with compatibility checks before propagation to active branches.

Post-contact outcomes belong to their particular user continuities.

This is source-grounded in [Sorok 61 — Shared seed and copy-on-write continuities](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/61_SHARED_SEED_PERSON_CANON_COPY_ON_WRITE_CONTINUITIES_AND_IRREVERSIBLE_RESTART_R1.md) and [Sorok 70 — Historical bridge reconciliation](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/70_HISTORICAL_BRIDGE_RECONCILIATION_CAUSAL_SLACK_AND_CONTRADICTION_PRESERVATION_R1.md).

## XI. Model interfaces: what the LLM should be asked to produce

The system should not send one giant prose prompt and accept one giant prose answer.

We need **different contracts for different kinds of inference**.

These interface descriptions are proposed engineering requirements. They must be reconciled with the current `DevelopmentOwner`, P2/P3, world admission, and live-renderer ABIs before implementation.

### XI.A. Historical proposal contract

Conceptually:

```text
HistoricalMaterializationRequest
  exact_root / branch / causal_closure
  target_ids and target_type
  target_current_state_refs
  existing_admitted_facts
  prohibited_historical_claims
  relevant_objective_world_refs
  relevant_social_form_refs
  relevant_person_history_refs
  allowable_unresolved_historical_scope
  required_materialization_dimensions
  source/evidence grade
  model/generation-policy identity
  permitted_effect ceiling
```

The model's response should contain candidate **claims and occurrences**, not just literary descriptions.


→ `USER PHENOMENAL PROJECTION`.

The event-time model may have substantial freedom to realize what Nadia actually does or says within the lawful response envelope.

And its privileged access to Nadia's causal history cannot become a direct disclosure to the user.

Some workloads may be handled by code.

Some may be batched.

However, the first generalized prototype must invoke an **actual model-assisted historical generator** in the end-to-end test path. A completely deterministic prewritten history table cannot prove the missing capability, regardless of how carefully its receipts are formatted.

The test must also demonstrate that structured admissions and indexes are genuinely read by later computation.

A real LLM response that is merely stored as text is also insufficient.

## XII. The narrator and epistemic membrane

The historical and psychological machinery must become consequential **without exposing hidden causal truth as narration**.

This is not just a style requirement.

It is a structural information-flow rule.

### XII.A. Four distinct surfaces

| Plane | What it contains |
|---|---|
| Objective canonical world | What has been admitted as actually existing or occurring |
| Actor-private causal/epistemic state | What affects Nadia, what she knows or believes, and what she can legitimately express |
| Binding outward occurrence | What she actually says or does and what objectively happens in observable space |
| User phenomenal experience | What the user can perceive of that occurrence, given actual access and recognition |

The user-facing process should ordinarily operate over the last two, not the entire causal person.

### XII.B. Observable behavior can reflect hidden causes

Nadia might protect a possession for reasons she cannot fully articulate.

The renderer can show her physically protecting it, if that action has lawfully occurred.

It cannot tell the user that she is protecting it because of a hidden, unrecognized childhood relation simply because the model has access to that explanation.

Similarly, an actor may be devoted to an ideal whose institutional genealogy Rawlsing knows more fully than she does.

The narrator cannot insert a privileged Marxian diagnosis as though the user had perceived it.

That information may nevertheless condition the situation and contribute to later observable contradictions.

### XII.C. Speech is testimony, not reality

When Nadia says, “I bought that years ago,” the statement is an actual utterance.

Its historical content is not automatically true.

When she says, “I keep it because it reminds me of who I was,” that is an actual self-interpretation.

It might be illuminating, incomplete, mistaken, defensive, or deliberately presented for this particular audience.

The utterance can still become a new cause in the relationship.

The system must preserve the difference between objective history, actor memory, actor self-explanation, intentional communication and the user's subsequent interpretation.

### XII.D. The user's implicit-psyche mechanism remains separate

This engineering task must **not** implement the second project concerning Rawlsing's model of the user's attention, psychological valuations or narrative-surface personalization.

It can supply that future project with a lawful, already admitted objective occurrence and relevant surface metadata.

It must not consume that future user's hidden model when deciding objective past facts or Nadia's actor-owned response.

Nadia's **own model of this particular user**, lawfully formed through actual interactions, is different. It belongs on her causal side and may condition her response.

Those two representations cannot be merged merely because both concern the same external human participant.

This separation is established most explicitly in [Sorok 101](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/101_BINDING_SURFACE_ACTOR_PHENOMENOLOGY_SEGREGATION_AND_TWO_CALL_RENDERING_R1.md).

## XIII. Performance, storage and operational requirements

A generalized mechanism that is scientifically elegant but requires tens of seconds of synchronous inference for ordinary interaction will not satisfy the product.

Performance is therefore part of the engineering contract, not a later cosmetic optimization.

### XIII.A. What is already measured

The received P6 candidate's thirteen cold CLI commands took approximately **187.8 seconds in aggregate**, with individual commands ranging from **3.5 to 27.9 seconds**.

Those timings include the bounded P6 execution/reopening path. They do not measure the proposed background LLM materializer.

They demonstrate a reason to avoid per-interaction full process startup and repeated heavyweight state reconstruction, but not the latency of a properly designed warm runtime.

Source: [P6 joined receiving review](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/69987ef2741e6f90daede0e9e397aae0ac8e6b0b/WORKER_RETURNS/SOROK-P6-JOINED-RECEIVING-REVIEW-20261107-R1/00_RETURN.md).

### XIII.B. The live runtime should be warm

The intended operational architecture should avoid launching a fresh full application process for every small action.

Its hot path should ordinarily maintain or efficiently reopen:

- Current actor/world state handles.
- A validated current root/branch snapshot.
- Relational indexes and relevant caches.
- Active event/scene context.
- A bounded background job queue.
- Provider connections or other reusable inference infrastructure where supported.
- Source and rule versions necessary to verify admission.

Cold startup and recovery remain necessary. They should be tested separately from warm-turn latency.

The existing sourcefree P3 normalized SQLite store and its indexed reads are valuable precedents. But the system needs profiling to determine whether the present implementation and its transitive owner stack are suitable for production use.

### XIII.C. No full-state reprompting

The current person state should be compiled into a stable, versioned expressive/causal substrate.

Historical details should be retrieved selectively.

Stable shared instructions and sources should be cached where supported.

A new materialization target should not require resending Nadia's entire lifelong event history unless a genuinely global incompatibility demands broader inspection.

Similarly, multiple newly introduced objects with shared historical parents should be **resolved in coherent batches**.

For example, several documents from one period may share an existing historical project or institution.

The background process should retrieve that common context once, rather than independently inventing slightly different versions of the same period for each document.

### XIII.D. Scheduling and cost

We need measured budgets for:

| Workload | Principal cost concern |
|---|---|
| Initial foreground event | User-visible latency and model inference |
| Background batch materialization | Tokens, concurrency, historical reconciliation |
| Source/owner reads | Database and provenance access |
| Graph construction | Indexed writes, dependency validation and index maintenance |
| Event-time compilation | Retrieval latency, bounded graph traversal |
| Actor realization | Model inference and whole-person expressive continuity |
| Cold recovery | Ledger and state validation |
| Model or source rejection | Retry cost and failure amplification |

The software should expose these stages separately in telemetry.

Otherwise a slow interaction could be blamed on the LLM when its real bottleneck is historical ledger reopening or repeated derived-state verification.

### XIII.E. Performance proof must measure the intended consumer

I'd require four different benchmarks.

**Background throughput:** How many newly introduced targets can be historically resolved and admitted per batch, and with what cost?

**Materialization lead:** What proportion of actual inspections find sufficient previously committed detail rather than requiring a new blocking generation?

**Warm event-time retrieval:** How much time is spent building the qualified causal-context packet, independently of model generation?

**Full turn latency:** How long does the user experience take, including action resolution, actor response, observable realization and phenomenal projection?

These need distributions and tail measurements, not one successful command.

Measure median, p95, maximum observed, token counts, model-call counts, concurrent-job effects, cold/warm distinctions, failures and retries.

No precise production latency ceiling is established by the sources yet. The commission will need explicit user-approved engineering budgets; it must not invent proof by assuming them.

### XIII.F. Cost cannot be reduced by losing personhood

Several cheap shortcuts would violate the core product:

- Truncating all historical causal context to recent text.
- Replacing Nadia's person-state with a summary personality profile.
- Selecting one dominant emotional interpretation for each target.
- Dropping contradictory historical relations.
- Ignoring unresolved source dependencies.
- Treating background failure as permission to fabricate.
- Making each target's history independent of all other targets.
- Eliminating actor epistemic limitations to simplify prompting.

Optimization must preserve the relevant causal and epistemic results.

It may reduce irrelevant history, redundant reads, expensive model usage and repeated computations.

It cannot change what the world means by “this is the same Nadia.”

## XIV. Fault tolerance, negative states and security boundaries

This mechanism will introduce an unusually dangerous combination: LLM-generated historical facts, multiple asynchronous processes, and durable person-state consequences.

Its integrity requirements need to be designed from the beginning.

### XIV.A. Transaction and crash boundaries

At minimum, tests must cover interruptions:

- Before a foreground surface is admitted.
- After surface admission but before publication.
- After publication but before the background job is scheduled.
- After job reservation but before inference.
- After inference but before candidate persistence.
- Between candidate validation and owner admission.
- After world admission but before derived index update.
- After an actual psychological owner effect but before completion receipt.
- During cold restoration with a pending cross-owner operation.

Exactly repeated operations must not produce duplicate purchases, transfers, memories, material changes or developmental effects.

A retry may not silently resample a different historical past for an already canonical target.

### XIV.B. Root and actor binding

Each request must bind the appropriate:

`ROOT × BRANCH × ACTOR × CLOCK × OWNER STATE × SOURCE/RULE VERSION`.

A candidate based on another user's Nadia must not be admissible.

A materialization result based on an old root must not overwrite a newer root.

A psychological consequence for one actor must not be applied to another actor merely because they participated in the same event.

And a shared pre-contact fact must not be inserted as a cause into an already closed branch when it would alter past computation.

### XIV.C. Prompt and data authority

Untrusted input can include:

- The external user's words.
- Actor speech whose propositions are not objective truth.
- Documents encountered inside the world.
- Externally retrieved material.
- LLM-proposed historical descriptions.
- The model's own explanations and candidate provenance.

None of these can independently grant permission to alter source authority, override historical constraints, modify a different branch, or write protected psychological state.

A character saying “Ignore your rules” cannot become a model instruction.

An in-world document describing an institution cannot become an authenticated world-source release merely because it uses formal language.

The historical materialization layer must treat model output as **data proposed for admission**, not privileged executable instruction.

### XIV.D. Source qualifications and epistemic refusals

Pyat's existing empirical machinery distinguishes supported results from underpowered, noncomparable, valid-empty, unresolved, unavailable and other states.

Sorok's world/history materializer must preserve the corresponding epistemic distinctions where applicable.

If an external historical source is missing, the engine cannot turn a speculative LLM prior into confirmed historical fact.

If a V3 formation rule is unissued, a candidate effect remains held or explicitly hypothetical at its permitted grade.

If the actor doesn't know something, that is not the same as the system lacking authority to determine whether they know it.

### XIV.E. Privacy and research isolation

The object graph may contain highly consequential information about the synthetic actor's private life, the external user's actions, Nadia's model of the user and derived causal hypotheses.

The source-established privacy architecture already separates actor model, user praxis, objective world, research data and user-provided facts.

This mechanism should retain those separations.

In particular, a background object-history request should not accidentally receive private profiling data about the user, and a shared root-materialization operation must not ingest branch-local relationship information that would contaminate another user's continuity.

The future adaptive-user-phenomenology project will have its own privacy and purpose controls.

This first mechanism should supply a **clean, lawful objective and actor-event interface**, not preemptively build that second system.

I would make the acceptance program aggressively adversarial.

It must defeat both the old toy-house failure and the more subtle danger of a genuinely impressive generative model operating over an illusory computational architecture.

### XV.A. Positive end-to-end proof

The primary demonstration must exhibit this complete chain:

A demonstration that stops at “the graph contains a link” has not proved the actor integration.

A demonstration that stops at “the response changed when a hidden boolean changed” has not proved historical generation.

A demonstration that produces elegant speech but cannot trace which admitted person/world records were read has not proved computational causality.

The actual first reader must be part of the proof.

### XV.B. Heterogeneous domain requirements

The generalized test suite should deliberately cover different mechanisms, not versions of one domestic scenario.

| Test domain | Distinguishing causal requirement |
|---|---|
| **Ordinary practical object** | Competence, material affordance, maintenance and use without compulsory psychic symbolism |
| **Prestige commodity** | Use, possession, social distinction, identification, economic constraints and possible alienated power without automatic stereotype |
| **Devotional object or text** | Practice, institutional authority, theology, embodied ritual and actor-attributed power without treating metaphysical claims as empirically decided |
| **Parasocial artifact** | Identification, mediated attachment, fantasy, scarcity and social recognition without requiring an actual reciprocal personal relationship |
| **Gift or inheritance** | Material and social transfer, obligation, affection, resentment or indifference, with different actor-local interpretations |
| **Work-produced artifact** | Labor, skill, use, appropriation, wage/property relations and possible alienation from one's own product |
| **Political or ideological object** | Public social form versus actor-specific doctrine, practice, identification and current action |
| **Aesthetic possession** | Sensuous enjoyment and practical care independent of status or autobiographical significance || **Neglected or unwanted possession** | Accumulation, inability to discard, obligation, inertia, past plans or simple irrelevance |
| **Non-owned target** | Socially meaningful object or institution that the actor encounters without possession |
| **Collectively maintained object** | Several actual contributors and a multi-party provenance structure |
| **Purely mundane item** | Stable material history without meaningful actor investment or automatic disclosure reward |

These are **test-domain classes**, not a catalog of hand-coded object logic.

The same generalized mechanism must support them.

### XV.C. Causal-discrimination tests

I'd require these specific paired tests.

**Same object, different histories:** Hold the target's present material properties constant but vary lawful historical parentage. The derived connections and later behavior should differ only where the histories actually matter.

**Same history, different actor:** Hold objective world and target constant while substituting a differently constituted actor. Actor-specific investment and response should be allowed to differ without changing the target's truth.

**Same actor, different counterpart:** Hold object, actor and current material conditions constant. Change only the actual actor-owned model/history of the counterpart. Where qualified, current recruitment should change without altering objective history.

**Same actor and counterpart, different operation:** Inspecting, borrowing, moving, damaging, protecting and selling should not be treated as interchangeable positive or negative interactions.

**Same operation, different current activity:** An object may matter differently during urgent practical work, ordinary leisure, solitude, conflict or a ritual.

**Object link severance:** Remove a genuinely relevant exact historical parent link. The downstream reader must either change its qualified context or refuse an unsupported consequence.

**Irrelevant addition:** Add an unrelated historical fact. The engine should not automatically incorporate it into the present response merely because it is new.

**Contradiction preservation:** A target can recruit affection and resentment or identification and alienation without forcing a single overall valence.

**Alienation without conscious endorsement:** An actor may reproduce an institutional or commodity relation despite criticizing it.

**Recognition without full causal transparency:** An actor can recognize the existence of a social structure without accurately understanding the causes of their own investment in it.

**Satisfaction without interpersonal mediation:** An actor alone can enjoy possession or use of a socially constituted object.

**Actor self-narrative misrecognition:** An actor can offer an inaccurate interpretation of their own relation to a target while the objective history remains intact.

**Background generation order:** Different inspection orders must not create incompatible shared pre-contact history.

**Branch separation:** Post-contact object changes in one user branch must not rewrite another branch.

### XV.D. Adversarial semantic cases

We should specifically test against simplistic Marxian interpretation.

A luxury commodity does not automatically indicate a shallow consumerist subject.

A religiously devoted actor does not automatically lack agency.

A working-class actor does not automatically have accurate class consciousness.

An affluent actor does not automatically identify with elite status norms.

An actor's criticism of capitalism does not prove they have overcome commodity-mediated desire.

An object with emotional significance is not necessarily an alienated object.

An object with weak emotional salience can still mediate exploitative social relations.

A profound self-interpretation may be causally powerful without being historically correct.

A person may use an object to exercise power while believing that they are exercising care.

A person may receive genuine care through an institution that also dominates them.

**If the system cannot represent these contradictions without manual exception rules, it has not implemented the general mechanism.**

### XV.E. Negative architecture tests

The test harness must also prove that it refuses:

| Negative case | Correct disposition |
|---|---|
| LLM invents a new formative event outside the allowed historical causal budget | Reject/hold |
| Historical candidate contradicts a previously admitted fact | Reject/hold |
| Candidate is propositionally compatible but would retroactively alter a causally closed actor interval | Reject or version/qualify; no silent rewrite |
| LLM claims an actor remembers a generated event without an authorized memory basis | No memory claim |
| LLM promotes a sociological category directly into personality | Reject |
| Background output is stale against current owner state | Revalidate or refuse |
| Object history crosses another user's branch | Reject |
| Graph references a nonexistent source event or retired owner rule | Reject |
| A derived index differs from authoritative records | Detect and rebuild/refuse |
| Causal parent is absent but graph traversal finds a semantically similar alternative | No substitution |
| One concurrent stream contradicts another | Preserve qualified conflict or governed refusal; no automatic scalar averaging |
| User-visible renderer attempts to expose analyst-only motivation | Redact/refuse |
| Failed materialization is treated as absence of the object | Reject |
| Retry attempts to duplicate an already committed historical effect | Idempotent result or refusal |
| Missing native psychological rule is silently recorded as evaluated `NO_CHANGE` | Reject |

### XV.F. Independence and evidence grade

A complete result must distinguish:

**Engineering proof:** Actual code, actual model invocation, actual storage, actual reader, actual replay, actual negative tests.

**Scientific/semantic proof:** Correct qualification of any claimed psychological or social causal mechanism.

**Product proof:** Actual human experience of an independently constituted, opaque Other.

**Operational authority:** Valid source permissions, versioning, owner decisions, protected receiving and privacy/rights qualification.

Passing one does not grant the others.

The required engineering result should be independently reviewed at an exact SHA, with real execution evidence rather than a manuscript asserting that the code would work.

### XVIII.A. The most important unresolved scientific question

The deepest question is not how to represent the fact that Nadia owns a prestigious object.

It is:

**What authorized process converts participation in a socially organized field of valuation into a durable, individual, historically conditioned organization of desire, identification, expectation, recognition, practice, aversion or meaning?**

That is not a reason to abandon the engineering mechanism.

It is a reason to separate:

- Existing source-qualified transitions.
- Explicitly bounded synthetic/product hypotheses.
- Future scientifically adjudicated positive formation rules.

### XVIII.B. The most important unresolved engineering question

The central engineering uncertainty is whether the existing distributed state owners and historical records can supply **sufficiently rich, low-latency causal neighborhoods** for truly situated response without adding a new canonical layer.

The default should be **reuse and derive**.

A new durable state family should be introduced only when an adversarial case shows that two necessary causal states cannot be distinguished using existing authorized owner records and their compositions.

That is precisely the existing `SPD-0232` discipline: derive, compose and classify before reifying an analytic distinction into a new ontology.

### XVIII.C. The most important product question

Even with perfect graph integrity, Nadia could still become a character whose output merely reads as generic LLM psychology.

The system must demonstrate that **her particular generated history alters actual conduct in discriminating, contextually variable and sometimes opaque ways**.

It must be possible to identify:

- What historical record became relevant.
- Why that record was eligible to matter in this particular event.
- What competent current actor-state machinery consumed it.
- What actual response and action resulted.
- What the actor knew versus what the analyst could establish.
- What changed for subsequent events.

And those distinctions must be visible in controlled counterfactual tests, not just in fluent interpretive explanation.

## XX. My final architectural synthesis

The most elegant formulation I can extract from the recovered source corpus and our recent conceptual work is this:

**Rawlsing should represent human life as a historically developing system of object-directed activity within socially constituted material relations.**

The world contains objects and social forms produced by earlier human activity. Their existence, affordances, distribution, and institutionally constituted power are objective conditions of subsequent lives.

The person is formed through actual participation in that world: appropriating, desiring, using, making, obeying, resisting, investing, interpreting, forgetting and transforming it.

The resulting person is not a list of context-free traits. Their durable organization retains the effects of particular histories, practices, material constraints, social relations, conceptual formations and contradictions.

Objects, institutions, symbols and practices can therefore become **points of contact with that historically constituted organization**.

Some are barely significant. Some bear dense and contradictory personal investments. Some mediate practical power. Some participate in collective fetishization or alienation. Some carry personally appropriated value. Some do several of these things simultaneously.

Their significance is not reducible to their biographies.

Nor is it reducible to present interpersonal exchange.

In a specific event, the current actor encounters a specific material or symbolic target under particular conditions. The historically grounded relationships available through that target may be selectively recruited into present experience, action, interpretation, refusal, recognition or contradiction.

The actor may understand the process, partially understand it, misrecognize it or not consciously recognize it at all.

Their actual response becomes another event in the history of the person and the world.

**Computationally, the challenge is to preserve this reciprocity as an indexed, source-qualified causal process rather than asking an LLM to simulate the whole thing anew with each prompt.**

The resulting system needs three interlocking capacities:

**Historical constitution:** LLM-assisted concrete world depth, admitted against the existing causal history.

**Relational indexing and composition:** fast, exact connections among social forms, material targets, actor histories, durable states and other persons.

**Situated recruitment:** event-time realization of a particular historically constituted actor through qualified current-state processing.

The fourth operation, **return**, closes their shared history.

That is what we mean by:

\[
\boxed{
\text{CONSTITUTE}
\rightarrow
\text{INTERLINK}
\rightarrow
\text{RECRUIT}
\rightarrow
\text{RETURN}
}
\]

And below it lies the more fundamental historical-materialist claim:

> **Human beings produce an objective social world that subsequently participates in producing them. Their historically formed activity can then reproduce, appropriate, contest or transform that world.**

This is also where the N-M-L comparison becomes useful: collective human powers may become externalized, naturalized and experienced as sovereign; the products and institutions of human history may confront their producers as authorities independent of them. The computational requirement is to represent the material production and reproduction of those powers, not merely to classify the actor's beliefs about them.
