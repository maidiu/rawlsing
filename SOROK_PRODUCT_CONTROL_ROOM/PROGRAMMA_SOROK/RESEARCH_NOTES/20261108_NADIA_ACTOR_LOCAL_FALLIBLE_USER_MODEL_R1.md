# NADIA'S FALLIBLE ACTOR-LOCAL MODEL OF THE PARTICULAR USER
## Sorok scientific research note, 2026-11-08 — NOT AN ISSUED CAN/ADP RULE

**Question:** How can this historically constituted person form an expectation, suspicion, appraisal, mistake, confidence or uncertainty about this specific external participant through actual interaction, without the platform's private hypothesis about that participant becoming Nadia's knowledge?

## Distinct epistemic domains

1. External human: has more private experience than Rawlsing can observe. The system lacks access to a complete inward psychology.
2. Observable user praxis: actual performed action, speech, omission within known alternatives, refusals and repeated activity. The utterance is real, but its content is not automatically verified.
3. Nadia's perceivable user behavior: what her situated activity and admitted observer access allow, which may be narrower than (2).
4. Nadia's actor-local account of this person: possibly contradictory and context-dependent expectations, classifications, misreadings, anticipated judgments, practices and partial confidence, under competent CAN/ADP authorship.
5. What Nadia tells the user she believes: speech and chosen presentation, not necessarily transparent export of (4).
6. System user gaze hypothesis: separately derived from exposed user choices for Mechanism II presentation, NOT the source of (4) and never an actor input.

## Six independently discriminating cases

A. Same objectively performed user event; only Nadia's lawful access differs. Her belief may differ but the event remains identical.
B. Same user words; different earlier Nadia experiences and current commitments. Different response requires qualified actor history, not a personality keyword or narration.
C. User claims an intention and later acts contrary to it. Her expectation may update, remain uncertain or remain unchanged if the responsible scientific rule permits; no automatic contradiction-resolution operator.
D. Nadia believes user judges her incompetent, though no such judgment was actually expressed. Her metaperception may alter Nadia's conduct without establishing the user's hidden thought.
E. Change only system gaze profile with world, user action and Nadia actor state constant. Nadia's behavior and belief must not change. The gaze engine is not her psychological authority.
F. Genuine user redirection of attention becomes a physically legible action she can perceive. Only that actual action may lawfully change her next response, through existing actor/world consumers.
