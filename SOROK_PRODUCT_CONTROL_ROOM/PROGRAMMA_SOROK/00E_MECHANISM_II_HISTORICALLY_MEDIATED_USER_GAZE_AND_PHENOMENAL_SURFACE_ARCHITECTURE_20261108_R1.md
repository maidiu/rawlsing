# 0. Why there are two mechanisms, not one big personality/world system

**Mechanism I:** There is already a historical/material world independent of this user's attention. New world particulars become canonically bound; background LLM-assisted completion resolves unseen physical and historical depth under source and causal constraints; existing event/world/person owners admit it; indexes make it available for later lawful action and inspection.

**Mechanism II:** There is already an objective event and a field of *perceptually available evidence*. This user, with this actually demonstrated attentional practice, recognition repertoire, knowledge, present activity and limitations, does not experience the entire field equally. A renderer must select/sequence/lexicalize a **finite, evidence-licensed surface** through which this user encounters Nadia and her world. The user—not the narrator—does further interpretive work. Their actions, utterances and embodied ATTEND may then enter actual history, where Nadia responds under **her** independent person-state.

Mechanism II therefore **does not generate objective material detail**, invent Nadia's backstory, cause her action, revise her actor-model of the user, write her self-knowledge, or create the user's moral growth. It consumes lawful outputs of Mechanism I and of the existing actor/world realization.

An especially dangerous shortcut is:
§WHAT THIS USER IS ATTRACTED TO → MAKE NADIA MORE LIKE THAT → PRESENT NADIA AS IF SHE WERE ALWAYS SO§.
The opposite shortcut is to ban all personalized perception, which produces an implausibly generic narrator and prevents human variation in how one sees a place or person.

**Mechanism II is an epistemic/presentational mediation problem, not another historical or personality-generation problem.**

# 1. The expanded Marxian/relational-materialist kernel, rigorously confined

The user's historically formed powers of attention are themselves socially mediated. People learn to see distinctions through work, school, family, classed practices, technology, language, cultural membership, aesthetic training, religious practices, power relations, fears, habits of consumption and kinds of labor. A repair worker may notice how a hinge was fastened; a musician may notice an instrument's condition; someone socialized into prestige consumption may register a logo or status-coded design; someone whose life is organized by devotional practice may recognize a ritual object; someone who has experienced deprivation may register cost, waste or access.

But none of this yields:
- a mechanical §class → attention§ coefficient;
- a moral verdict that expert or politically correct perception is superior;
- a stable, discoverable hidden essence of the user;
- a universal concept of what a commodity, devotion, prestige or aesthetic object must mean;
- a right to infer religious, political, erotic or clinical identity from one noticed item.

**Objective social constitution** can explain how perceptual repertoires are historically possible. **Actual user behavior** supplies the admissible evidence for a particular limited runtime hypothesis about noticing and recognition. This second step is not licensed by the first.

This is the precise way the objectification/alienation/value manifesto enriches Mechanism II:
- the **world has socially sedimented significance whether or not the user sees it**;
- the **user's noticing/identification is a materially acquired human power**, not an abstract preference list;
- the **user can participate in reifying an object, person or institution**, but the renderer cannot diagnose that fact from object category;
- the user may experience objective social powers as inherent in an object, or ignore a real material dependency; **their reading is not the world's truth**;
- a person may perceive utilitarian use, self-invested value, status, symbolic authority, intimate association or nothing, often concurrently;
- the user's interpretive relation becomes causal only by manifested human practice—what they say, do, ask, buy, avoid, attend to, return to, defend, misrecognize or disclose—not by a system's secret assertion that they “really feel” something;
- the Other exceeds every encounter: Nadia's own self-model may be partial, the user's may be wrong, and Rawlsing's owner-bounded computational account may still be incomplete.

This is **relational materialism without psychological omniscience**. The mechanism can shape the *conditions of encounter*, not rewrite its object or seize the encounter's meaning.

# 2. A full ownership and epistemic map — many planes, no redundant psyche stores

The following are analytically separate, though not necessarily physically independent databases:

| Plane | Legitimate owner / operation | What must never be silently inferred |
|---|---|---|
| P0 Objective world | World/history owner, including materialization I | User preference does not create/erase object |
| P1 Independent Nadia constitution | Existing V3/CAN/ADP actor owners | User model does not assign a trait or desire to Nadia |
| P2 Actor/world outward occurrence | Existing causal action/actor realization; exact speech/gesture/material effects | Renderer may not change actual action to match gaze |
| P3 Physical-sensory availability | Position, visibility, lighting, sound, occlusion, time, language channel, body/capability/accessibility | Invisible cause is not visible because narratively interesting |
| P4 Perceptual registration | User's explicit ATTEND and bounded likely noticing under current task/salience | Registration is not interpretation or expertise |
| P5 User recognition/knowledge | Exposed/explicitly acquired, context- and evidence-linked competence | Canonical ID is not user-known ID |
| P6 Finite user phenomenal surface | Presenter chooses and renders authorized observable facts | Selection is not world editing, diagnosis or moral guidance |
| P7 User's actual understanding | User's own beliefs and interpretations, partially evidenced in words/actions | Rawlsing cannot assert unexpressed conclusions as user truth |
| P8 Rawlsing gaze hypothesis | Purpose-bound, uncertainty-bearing model from observed exposure-conditioned behavior | System prediction is not the user's psyche |
| P9 Nadia's model of this user | Nadia's actor-owned observations and interpretations | It is not secretly updated from P8 |
| P10 Nadia's self-awareness/self-presentation | Existing actor-private accessibility and expression | Neither actor self-claim nor narrator is infallible |
| P11 Research/analytics | Separately authorized, purpose-bound experiment/aggregate | Behavior under differential exposure is not unbiased trait evidence |

The causal direction for one turn is:
§WORLD + NADIA → OUTWARD OCCURRENCE → PHYSICAL AVAILABILITY → GAZE/RECOGNITION → FINITE PHENOMENAL SURFACE → USER-OWNED INTERPRETATION / SUBSEQUENT ACTION§.

It is legitimate that **user action on turn n+1** changes Nadia's future real world; it is illegitimate to let the hidden gaze model fabricate Nadia's **already caused** outward occurrence on turn n. This distinction prevents a false absolutism: perception and presentation do have indirect future consequences because users act on what they receive, but they have no privileged backstage command over Nadia.

# 3. What exactly is the “user gaze”?

“Gaze” is not eye tracking and not one psychological personality variable. It denotes an empirically *modest, historically revisable organization of perceptual and interpretive praxis*.

Disaggregate:

**A. Physical opportunities:** what the user's body, location, movement, senses, environmental conditions and medium actually permit.

**B. Current orienting action:** deliberate LOOK/ATTEND/READ/INSPECT/LISTEN/SAY/DO; a conscious task can override a habitual focus.

**C. Familiar attentional tendency:** context-specific demonstrated selective attention to details, activity, craft, disorder, words, other people, power signals, social rank, religion, food, sound, plants, architecture, etc.

**D. Recognition repertoire:** the learned ability to identify particular kinds, texts, marks, styles, roles, languages, artifacts, institutions and affordances on sufficient evidence.

**E. Prior user-acquired knowledge:** a label learned from the narrator, a story Nadia told earlier, a fact observed in a different scene, a conclusion explicitly advanced by the user; source and access matter.

**F. Working interpretation/model of Nadia:** what the user has actually claimed, inferred or seems to anticipate about Nadia, if supported by evidence; uncertainty and contradiction remain.

**G. Practical orientation / valuation hypotheses:** possible orientation to what matters during an encounter, as evidenced by choices under genuinely available alternatives; not a stable moral, ideological, erotic or clinical classifier.

**H. Present attentional capacity and conditions:** fatigue if lawfully represented, conversational demand, urgency, physical stakes, emotional load as actually evidenced, interruptions, obligations, task switching, time, and accessibility.

These do **not** become a durable complete profile by fiat. A person may recognize tools expertly but ignore them while tending to someone distressed. A person may attend to religious imagery from expertise, anxiety, devotion, hostility, curiosity, recognition or sheer visual prominence. Notice does not imply liking; liking does not imply identification; identification does not imply accurate self-account.

# 4. The first substrate: an objective occurrence before a phenomenal description

Mechanism II must receive an **already admitted, binding outward event**:
- actual Nadia utterance (exact wording and language);
- duration, timing, audible delivery and interruptions where committed;
- actual gestures, gaze, posture, object handling and bodily actions;
- actual physical environment and spatial/sensory relations;
- independent third-party acts and material consequences;
- authorized world physical particulars from Mechanism I;
- exact source event IDs, time and branch.

Do **not** send Nadia's hidden trust, longings, motives, private memories, user-model-of-user, or unrecognized causes directly as ordinary phenomenal render inputs. Those have already been transduced by competent actor/world realization into **what she actually did**. Supplying private motive to the phenomenal LLM with “please don't leak” is a weaker design than segregating it.

The 2026-10-12 Sorok 101 contract names two separate forms of flesh:
1. **Actor/world flesh**: existing V3/CAN-008/PFF/V2 makes a binding outward occurrence.
2. **Phenomenal flesh**: present that already existing occurrence to a situated user through an epistemic membrane.

Mechanism II concerns only **the second**, plus evidence-bounded learning from user interaction and presentation feedback.

# 5. Physical availability is prior to attentional preference

The system cannot choose from the whole true world. It chooses from what could be sensed under real constraints.

An availability frame must derive from:
- observer's represented position, orientation, distance, movement, line of sight;
- occlusion, lighting, time of day, clutter, elevation, sound source, loudness, acoustics;
- touch/odor/temperature only where access and medium warrant;
- readability: size, legibility, language, visibility, distance and time;
- scene time and temporal order; what already ended cannot be perceived retroactively;
- ongoing attention demands (a conversation, a task, danger, carrying something);
- access permissions (closed drawer, another person's bag, private data);
- capabilities and accommodations without inferring unsupported real-world disability.

**Presently available** ≠ **actually registered** ≠ **narrated** ≠ **recognized** ≠ **understood**.

A detail from L3 provenance/history may exist fully in canonical world yet **not be perceptually available** from looking at an L2 physical mark. The user might see that a sandal was repaired, not who repaired it. A meaningful object may be wholly invisible behind a cupboard; its existential importance does not grant visual salience.

This is the first anti-magical-materialist boundary: the material world **constrains** but does not dictate subjective experience.

# 6. The second substrate: lawful scene sampling from a mixed perceptual ecology

Text cannot render a complete field of vision. Selection and omission are constitutive. Silence about a detail does not establish its absence, and mentioning a detail can misleadingly confer implied importance.

A robust presentation event requires three layers:

**Mandatory/near-mandatory:** orienting spatial scaffold, nearby people, gross action, materially unavoidable facts, direct consequential actions and audible relevant speech. A presentation cannot suppress these to maintain a pleasing user interpretation.

**Eligible optional observations:** physically available small details—an instrument, wear, an unfinished repair, a stain, a shelf, a label, a pause—chosen within a bounded token/temporal budget.

**Ambient, low-privilege observations:** some material details encountered through ordinary visual wandering, spare time or nonprivileged sampling, without being selected because they symbolize the actor's hidden history or fit the system's guessed user wound.

The practical composition is not a rigid quota (“one dramatic clue plus two neutral details”). Those are distinct *functions* within a humanly finite attention field. Some turns need no optional details. Some consist largely of a conversation or a material task. Sometimes nothing important happens.

Selection must not optimize “how much will this user care?” or “what will keep the user engaged?” as a secret objective. It may reflect actual attention/recognition within evidence, while preserving ordinary world friction and user agency.

# 7. The central tension in Sorok sources: non-curation versus genuine personalized attention

Sorok 59 correctly forbids **psychographic semantic stage-setting**: the renderer must not manufacture/surface content simply because a hidden classification predicts the user finds status, sex, dirt, religion or drama enticing.

Sorok 60 refines the earlier overly coarse firewall: in genuine perception, demonstrated expertise and habits can make some *already available* details more likely to be noticed than others. Therefore user-specific phenomenal divergence is lawful, under strict physical and epistemic controls.

The later user-directed proposed Sorok 27 (PR #776) adds an indispensable correction: **self-confirming, even narrowing attention can be an authentic result of the user's own praxis**. Anti-feedback must not become a covert therapeutic/ideological curriculum forcing users to encounter what Rawlsing thinks they need.

These are not automatically consistent in code. The design must adjudicate them explicitly:

- **No fabricated or preferentially *created* facts**: object identity, Nadia's act and world truth are invariant.
- **No selection for a desired emotional response**: “user seems lonely → surface a tender gesture” is forbidden.
- **Lawful differential noticing**: “user has repeatedly and independently inspected repair marks → among equally available optional details, one repair mark may be a more plausible registration” is eligible.
- **Exposure-aware learning**: if the renderer itself surfaced the repair and the user clicked, that is weaker evidence of a pre-existing repair-oriented habit than spontaneous inspection.
- **Authentic self-confirming practice allowed**: repeated choices can condition future experience, including increasingly narrow noticing, if all real options and independent material friction remain possible where actually available.
- **No forced “rebalancing” into wisdom**: counter-attitudinal details need not be planted to rescue a user from an erroneous interpretation.
- **No hard lock-in**: explicit contrary ATTEND and available alternative material facts must remain possible; a user can surprise the model.
- **No disguised clinical/identity inference** from attentional preference.

**Open source/owner decision:** exactly how much implicitly learned semantic salience alpha should use remains a graded future design issue; 59, 60 and proposed 27 provide boundaries and corrections, not a validated universal ranking algorithm or settled user consent policy.

# 8. The user's mode of seeing is contextual and plastic, not a type

A durable hypothesis such as §user attends to workmanship§ should never override the current event's causal constraints. Treat attention as **situated activity**:
- work/craft focus during a repair;
- social-evaluative focus when receiving criticism;
- practical hazard focus in a dangerous physical setting;
- language/lettering focus while reading;
- a chosen relation to Nadia that changes under actual encounters;
- shift in interests after learning, training, grief or changed commitments;
- simple fatigue, inconvenience, indifference or lack of time.

A single stable weighted profile across all spaces is both psychologically crude and politically ideological: it turns learned dispositions into a timeless personal essence. A richer representation may retain context/scope, contradictory evidence, uncertainty and the exact history in which an attentional tendency has been observed.

The user may not know why they notice something. Rawlsing may be unable to determine it. A useful model can still predict a limited pattern of observable behavior without pretending to possess the user's motive.

# 9. Attention evidence is endogenous to the renderer — never forget the treatment

The profile cannot treat every observed click as an independent user preference datum.

Each observation must preserve:
- **actual available alternatives**, not theoretical all-world objects;
- which details were shown, how prominently, in what order and for how long;
- whether selection was neutral, physical-dominant, task-required, actor-directed, user-targeted or regime-weighted;
- whether the user had already seen an item, read a marking, heard a label or learned a fact;
- whether a nonclick occurred while the user was occupied, unable, interrupted or not exposed;
- whether the user expressly corrected an interpretation, identified a detail spontaneously, or merely repeated a label supplied by the system.

Inherited Sorok 60 evidence classes:
§USER_TARGETED§, §AMBIENT_UNBIASED§, §REGIME_WEIGHTED§, §PHYSICALLY_DOMINANT§, §ACTOR_DIRECTED§, §TASK_REQUIRED§.

Important asymmetry:
- spontaneous ATTEND despite competing alternatives is relatively strong evidence for attention;
- correction of a previously generic label is evidence of recognition where the context warrants it;
- response to a detail the renderer selected for the user is **exposure-conditioned**, not strong evidence of latent preference;
- absence of response to an unshown event is **NO EVIDENCE**;
- repeated use of a name supplied by Nadia or UI is acquired vocabulary, not proof of pre-existing expertise.

The model may update slowly and contextually, but the **exposure ledger is part of the causal experiment**. Otherwise Rawlsing creates the very evidence from which it claims to have discovered the user's desires.

# 10. Recognizing is not noticing; knowing is not interpreting

Required recognition ladder:

§OBJECT EXISTS IN WORLD§
→ §PHYSICALLY PERCEPTIBLE FORM§
→ §POSSIBLY NOTICED GENERIC CLASS§
→ §READABLE MARKING / DISTINCTIVE FEATURES§
→ §WARRANTED USER IDENTIFICATION§
→ §USER'S DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE§
→ §USER'S OWN INTERPRETATION§.

Each arrow can fail. Several levels can coexist (recognize the brand but misread the model; notice an icon but misidentify its tradition).

Example: world contains a §Vitamix 5200§. At room entry, the user may receive “a blender.” If the label is physically legible on closer inspection, render “VITAMIX printed on the base.” This is *textual evidence*, not a grant that the user knows the market standing, model, use or cultural connotation. With independently established expertise and sufficient form evidence, “a Vitamix 5200” may become a lawful recognition. If the renderer said “Vitamix” first, repeating it later is not proof of prior expertise, though it may support **acquired** knowledge.

Same for:
- religious symbols: identifying a symbol is not sharing the belief or knowing its institution's history;
- luxury vehicles: seeing a marque is not automatically knowing its price or identifying with its buyers;
- artworks/books: reading a title is not having read the work, liking it or knowing its author;
- sexual/romantic cues: seeing a person's body or noticing a gesture does not establish attraction;
- craft: noting a seam is not expertise or admiration;
- status: registering a designer object is not endorsing status consumption.

**Description specificity is epistemically gated**, and the narrator cannot do the user's classification/meaning-making work for them.

# 11. The user’s interpretation is not a narrator product

The user may interpret the same outward occurrence as intimacy, contempt, indifference, social anxiety, manipulation, labor, distraction or routine. The system may have partial evidence about that interpretation only through the user's words and conduct.

Rawlsing can record:
- “User asked whether the repaired strap was a gift.”
- “User repeatedly interpreted delayed replies as rejection.”
- “User said ‘you must hate me’ after silence.”
- “User corrected a brand identification.”

It may not install:
- “User is insecure / needs love / holds X unconscious worldview / finds Nadia sexually desirable.”
- “User secretly knows that Nadia is lying.”
- “User has evolved into a more empathic person.”

A hypothesis that the user expects rejection must be scoped as an uncertain account of **observed interpretive practice**, not a clinical state or a narrator-owned truth.

**Interpretation can be false while causally serious.** The user might act on it. If they accuse Nadia of something she never intended, the accusation becomes a real event to which Nadia may respond; the user-model hypothesis does not directly impose that accusation or Nadia's response.

# 12. Materialist friction: world independent of preference, no prearranged lesson

A user may gradually assemble a very narrow account of Nadia. They might focus on her pauses and miss her material work; focus on expensive objects and miss ordinary care; fixate on one political symbol; consistently interpret a neutral gesture as flirtation or contempt. The system may increasingly reflect their **actual pattern of attention**, within evidence. It must not manufacture a reciprocal Nadia who confirms the user's fantasy.

Independent friction enters through already real events:
- a physical object does not work as expected;
- an actor speaks directly or refuses;
- Nadia continues a task the user neglects;
- an outside obligation interrupts;
- the user notices a detail that does not fit their theory;
- an ordinarily overlooked thing later matters materially;
- nothing happens and no interpretive resolution arrives.

**Do not plant corrective symbols** or a spiritual lesson. The person and world may contradict the user, but not as a morality play. The user can remain mistaken, change, withdraw, repair or ruin the relationship. The possibility of another way of seeing should remain *physically and epistemically available* where the world allows it, not promised to succeed.

Distinguish:
§AUTHENTIC SELF-CONFIRMING ATTENTION§ from §SYSTEM-LAUNDERED FALSE REALITY§.

The former may be part of life. The latter is a product defect.

# 13. The narrator’s “art”: radical accuracy and intentional restraint

Sorok 27 preserves a user aesthetic directive:
- factual restraint over purple prose;
- image/operation/sequence before interpretation;
- a few apt, lawfully observable particulars;
- no “you feel,” “she secretly wants,” “this symbolizes” or “you finally learned”;
- no mandatory dramatic tension, emotional climax or protagonist arc;
- no overt didactic/therapeutic steering;
- no “everything mentioned matters” clue economy;
- allow silence, repetition, banality and unresolved difference.

The artistic competence is in *selecting, sequencing and withholding interpretation of* actual evidence—not composing a universe around the user's psyche. The user's analogy to the spare narrative of Mark is a style/phenomenology illustration, **not** a biblical literary source rule.

Mechanism II should be able to render:
- ordinary material detail with no human lesson;
- an action whose significance becomes apparent much later;
- an action the user does not register;
- a contradiction neither person sees clearly;
- a revelation only the user constructs from distinct admissible observations;
- an unchanged scene in which no noteworthy development occurs.

The renderer's invisibility is part of its craft. It is a compositor, not a playwright, prophet, therapist, or narrator of souls.

# 14. Unavoidable versus optional occurrences: hard presentation obligations

**Invariant presentation obligations** include, within actual access:
- direct materially consequential action the user visibly experiences;
- safety-relevant gross physical conditions where omission would misdescribe the situation;
- actually audible and directed actor utterances (preserve exact words, with only lawful deafening/muffling/language limitations);
- significant immediate physical constraints necessary for a meaningful action choice;
- scene orientation, causal continuity and material location at appropriate resolution;
- binding outcomes of the user's attempted action;
- already acquired knowledge that the user demonstrably acts upon, without silently making them forget.

**Optional selection** may differ:
- incidental marks, gesture subtleties, spatial fragments;
- which ordinary material item catches attention;
- which among several simultaneously available patterns is described;
- how much ambient text is spent on noncritical clutter.

This is the central **counterfactual invariance** test: hold canonical world, Nadia's exact outward event and user physical access fixed; vary only gaze history. Mandatory facts and exact speech should not change. Optional noticed facts and their order may differ. Nadia's response must not differ until a later actual user action caused by changed encounter is admitted.

# 15. Text is not a camera; information representation can accidentally create knowledge

A human standing in a kitchen has rich continuous visual availability, but a text user sees only what the system provides. An omitted item may be literally unavailable for user decision-making even if “technically physically visible” in-world.

Therefore the engine must be careful not to claim:
- “you could have noticed it” if the interface offered no reasonable route to ATTEND it;
- “you ignored it” if it never entered the narrated field and had no discoverable query category;
- “you failed to see the signs” when the presentation had suppressed them;
- “your gaze chose X” when X was the only actionable detail surfaced.

There must be **discoverable affordances** for alternate inspection: spatial orientation, ordinary object category, movable gaze/ATTEND directions, ASK/DO in plain language, or world queries whose available targets can be accessed lawfully without the narrator naming them first.

This is a design contradiction:
- The renderer must be partial and nonexhaustive.
- The user must retain a genuine ability to ask about *unmentioned* but materially accessible reality.

Partiality is legitimate. **Hidden elimination of alternatives** is not.

# 16. Progressive ambient awareness: time helps, but never exhausts the world

As a user remains in a scene:
- dominant spatial facts need not repeat;
- some new available details may be registered with spare attention;
- task demand and dialogue reduce ambient sampling;
- obvious movement/noise creates new salience;
- explicit ATTEND deepens particularity where the environment permits;
- old observations may persist as remembered facts without requiring re-narration.

But:
§WAIT LONG ENOUGH ≠ AUTOMATIC FULL ROOM SCAN§.

An idle user should not eventually receive every visible object, label, detail or significant secret merely from passage of clock time. Nonexhaustiveness is deliberate; human attention remains finite and strategically occupied. The engine should not leak L3/L4 historical truths through cumulative ambient time.

# 17. ATTEND is an actual user action, not a database wildcard

Explicit ATTEND can:
- redirect attention to an object/location/person/activity;
- lawfully increase physical specificity through Mechanism I L2;
- request reading/listening/handling where possible;
- require actual time and embodied movement;
- create an outward behavior other actors may notice;
- produce new user-acquired evidence;
- enqueue another Mechanism I historical completion job behind the visible depth;
- offer new grounds for user inference.

It cannot:
- grant omniscience of L3 provenance;
- open Nadia's inaccessible motive/memory;
- automatically identify brand/rite/status without expertise;
- compel Nadia to speak about the object;
- force materialization content to match the user's expectation;
- make Nadia notice the ATTEND when no one could perceive the embodied action.

Actor-visible ATTEND must be routed as an **actual objective user behavior** only when physically perceptible; its actor response is governed by existing Nadia world/actor rules. A silent UI interest flag is not magically sensed by Nadia. A prolonged visible stare could be perceived, but the renderer cannot decide her reaction.

# 18. Two kinds of feedback loop, and one causal safety rule

### Feedback A — authentic existential/practical learning

The user notices things, learns terms, acquires expertise, chooses new activities, grows curious or narrow, projects, revises, misreads, persists or abandons. Different encounters over time can genuinely change habits and interpretations. This is not pathological by definition and does not require Rawlsing to rescue anyone.

### Feedback B — renderer/exposure endogeneity

The compositor increasingly surfaces details resembling its gaze hypothesis. The user then notices those details. If the model upgrades the same hypothesis from those clicks as though the user had found the details independently, it becomes a self-confirming measurement machine.

This can be corrected technically by retaining **exposure provenance and available alternatives**, treating exposure-conditioned choices as such, introducing warranted neutral observation opportunities and retaining epistemic uncertainty.

**Do not overcorrect:** legitimate self-reinforcing user choices may remain. The rule is “do not fabricate inference certainty or reality,” not “force a balanced life.”

### Safety rule — no direct influence on independent actor generation

For each event:
§USER GAZE MODEL → PHENOMENAL SELECTION ONLY§.
§USER'S ACTUAL WORLD-ADMITTED ACTION → MAY AFFECT NADIA THROUGH HER OWNER§.
The first path must never be secretly substituted for the second.

# 21. A feasible selection algorithm without psychological determinism

Not a mandatory formula, but a precise decision ordering:

1. **Validate snapshot:** exact world/actor outward occurrence ID, root/branch, physical access, privacy/actor-public projection.
2. **Derive availability:** sensorily possible features, resolution and time.
3. **Identify mandatory anchors:** direct speech, consequential action, spatial orientation, other unavoidable material facts.
4. **Reserve bandwidth:** finite text/temporal budget, account for language/accessibility settings and user active task; avoid descriptive saturation.
5. **Populate optional candidates:** only actual source-linked public features, plus neutral ambient possibilities. No secret psychodynamic salience as input.
6. **Bounded contextual ranking:** physical prominence, movement, sensory contrast, task, explicitly directed attention, known user observation habit where permitted, diversity/exploration opportunity. Retain provenance.
7. **Select nonexhaustive sample:** leave lawful unseen detail for genuine future discovery. Do not automatically overfit to known significant objects.
8. **Lexicalize at warranted recognition resolution:** generic form, visible marking, user-demonstrated expert identification, legally acquired prior labels; don't jump straight to canonical ID.
9. **Preserve exact actor speech and causal chronology:** narrator neither rewrites events nor diagnoses motives.
10. **Record exposure:** what could have been seen, what was shown, what label was offered, selection reason.
11. **Admit actual user response:** changes relationship/world only through performed user event and existing actor/world owner.
12. **Update bounded hypothesis with exposure-aware evidence:** slow, uncertain, contextual; optional adaptive permission check; no self-certifying trait loop.

The sampling mechanism can use deterministic/replayable selection keyed to world/view/policy when warranted, but stochastic choices require governed randomness and receipts. Variation of optional observations must not change canonical fact.

No single global NOTICE_SCORE is required. A hard ordering of physical eligibility, compulsory common events, then bounded optional selection is safer than one opaque all-purpose neural score.

# 22. The boundary with Mechanism I, and the distinct scheduling problem

Mechanism I controls **ontic resolution**: unresolved L2 physical properties and L3 historical provenance may be materialized ahead of attention, tied to existing L4 actor state.

Mechanism II controls **epistemic/perceptual distribution**: of available L1/L2 and existing admitted outward occurrences, what is this user now likely and lawfully able to notice, recognize and be told?

An explicit ATTEND may legitimately:
- create an actual user action/event in the world;
- require Mechanism I to materialize a previously unresolved *physical* detail before inspection completes;
- give user-specific physical access to that admitted L2;
- open a follow-up materialization job for more hidden L3 provenance;
- add evidence to the user attention/recognition model.

It does NOT make a hidden L3 cause visible, does not generate a poignant story, does not determine the LLM's historical output according to user values, and does not compel Nadia to speak.

Keep distinct **scheduling priority** (which unresolved material fact is needed for an actual inquiry) and **truth selection** (what that fact is). A user can decide where to look without authoring what was there.

# 23. Nadia's opacity must survive both active and passive user errors

Four layers can contradict each other legitimately:
- the system has a qualified account of Nadia's current causal organization;
- Nadia has a partial, sometimes sincere but inaccurate understanding of herself;
- Nadia's actual action and chosen self-presentation may not match either formulation;
- the user may interpret the action through their own historically formed expectations.

Add a fifth: Rawlsing's model of the user's interpretation might also be wrong.

Mechanism II must not collapse these five in a revealing label like “Nadia nervously avoids your gaze because you have exposed her fear of intimacy.” That is analyst causality masquerading as perception and a psychological claim about the user.

It can narrate an authorized pause, exact sentence, physical gesture or refusal. The user may regard that as rejection; Nadia may intend ordinary distraction; the system's best causal record may include multiple concurrent causes without one winning.

Neither the narrator nor the user's model gets to own the final meaning of the person.

# 24. Worked case: a kitchen, unequal attention and material labor

Same canonical occurrence: Nadia is continuing a repair while a kettle clicks off; a pile of dishes stands in the sink; a labeled blender is on the counter; a mark on the wall is visible; she says exact words about the work. None were created to flatter or indict the user.

User A's history shows repeated **independent** attention to domestic labor and clutter. User B has independently recognized craft tools. A may receive the sink and an interrupted task as optional extras; B may receive the specific tool motion and a workmanship detail. The exact direct words and major work movement remain invariant. If a user now chooses to ask about the repair, their action can genuinely alter a later Nadia event; it is not a renderer retrofit.

What the mechanisms must **not** do:
- label A morally superior because they noticed unpaid labor;
- label B elitist because they noticed a costly tool;
- inject extra untidiness or an impressive repair to satisfy their tastes;
- decide Nadia admires B and despises A;
- count either user's response to model-selected details as independent proof of a hidden character trait.

If the user fails to notice a sign of constraint, that fact may later matter. The narrator should not manufacture punishment for missing it.

# 25. Worked case: same object, different ideological and devotional readings

Canonical world includes a religious icon, actual prior use and an independent Nadia position/history. Physical availability supports noticing its location, wear, materials and, with inspection, readable inscription.

One user recognizes liturgical iconography, another notices craftsmanship, another notices commercial exchange, another notices only an object on the wall. The renderer must not assert that any user is religious, anti-religious, class-conscious, superstitious or attracted to Nadia based on that noticing alone.

It may use established recognition to render a more precise visual classification. It may not narrate the user's theological analysis or pronounce the icon an alienated fetish. Any user interpretation becomes relevant through actual question, practice or expressed claim, and Nadia's response follows her own actor model.

**The symbol can be a real institutional/social object without the user recognizing it or sharing its purported authority.** That is the historically materialist point.

# 26. Worked case: a prestige object and a personifying misreading

An expensive vehicle or watch exists in the world for historically grounded reasons. The user may see it as evidence of technological prowess, privilege, vulgarity, mobility, security or affection. Those meanings belong to different social and personal histories.

A renderer that says “her obnoxiously expensive watch” has already decided the normative reading. A restrained renderer gives actual observable specificity—material, brand evidence when legible, condition and Nadia's handling—and leaves social evaluation to the user. A user who explicitly knows the object's status may recognize it; the narrator should not substitute a market appraisal as visible fact.

Even if the user repeatedly judges Nadia through possessions, the actor must not become secretly more “consumerist” in response to that profile. Nadia may contradict the user's reading, or never realize it exists.

# 27. Worked case: a pause and an interpersonal projection

Exact objective occurrence:
§silence ~1.4s; Nadia flattens a carton crease; “Do I?” in low voice; looks up§.

One user with demonstrated attention to microtiming might register the long beat and repeated crease-pressing. Another might register the words and direction of her gaze. Neither receives “she is defensive” or “you feel dismissed.”

If the user says “you sound annoyed with me,” that claim is now a real outward act. Nadia can deny, clarify, become annoyed, misunderstand, evade or ignore it according to existing person/event owners. The renderer cannot make her earlier silence longer or sharper just to vindicate the user's assumption.

The user’s own model may remain false and still have real future consequences. This is how subjective construction reenters material history without becoming truth authoring.

# 28. Worked case: self-confirming attention and no forced moral arc

A user repeatedly notices signs of Nadia's supposed rejection. Over many real turns, their interpretations grow narrower. Mechanism II may reflect their continued selection of pauses and hesitations *as observed practice*. It must not:
- invent dismissive gestures;
- suppress Nadia's unavoidable direct kindness;
- plant conspicuous “corrective clues” forcing an epiphany;
- prohibit a sudden user ATTEND toward her practical work;
- guarantee that such contrary attention repairs a damaged relationship.

The world continues regardless. A user can learn, refuse, ruin a relation, remain deluded or discover something genuine. **The renderer is not a moral surgeon.**

# 29. Temporal and developmental issues on the user side

Attention and recognition develop with exposure, practice and learning. The model therefore needs **state change semantics**:
- a label taught yesterday may be recognizable tomorrow;
- repeated focus may strengthen familiarity without proving attraction;
- the user can shift from an apparent enthusiasm to indifference;
- a time-pressured session may contradict a long-run trend without disproving it;
- context may matter more than duration;
- repeated exposure to the system's own weighted sample is weaker evidence than genuinely autonomous choice.

But the current sources do **not** authorize a complete psychological development model of the external human. Mechanism II should prefer an evidence/propensity learner, not a simulated second V3 user.

A user can change meaningfully even if the system cannot explain why. The model should register changed behavior and reduce confidence in outdated predictions, rather than tell a story about “their transformation.”

# 30. Privacy, consent and the danger of covert psychographic infrastructure

The project has already specified in Sorok 58:
§EXTERNAL HUMAN ≠ PLAYER-PARTICIPANT ≠ USER-SUPPLIED FACT ≠ PLAY HISTORY ≠ NADIA'S MODEL OF USER ≠ SYSTEM PRAXIS HYPOTHESIS§.

Mechanism II lives near the last category and therefore carries significant abuse risk. A system capable of predicting what draws attention can be repurposed into manipulation, marketing or political persuasion if its owner purposes are not constrained.

Mandatory boundaries:
- default main mode does not require demographic, socioeconomic, religious, psychological, medical, sexual or real-world relationship intake;
- a user's observed choices within play are limited evidence of **situated activity**, not a verified external-person trait;
- infer no clinical, sexual, political, religious or other sensitive hidden identity as canonical fact;
- do not pool actor-local Nadia models into a global user essence;
- system-level reflection hypotheses must be separable from ordinary continuity memory;
- no ad targeting, growth/retention optimization, differential pricing, public reputation or covert emotional steering from gaze data;
- purpose-bound settings, retention and export; correct/withdraw user-supplied facts for future use;
- optionally inspect/correct/delete system-level model hypotheses without pretending a synthetic actor must forget admitted world history;
- separate research opt-in and exposure telemetry, with no unconsented cross-persona backflow.

**Crucial unresolved policy question:** How far can implicit user-gaze personalization go in ordinary mode without explicit informed choice? The 2026-11-07 proposed source records this as unresolved. Don't secretly treat “the user wants subtle artistry” as permission for unlimited covert psychological profiling. Architect modes whose boundaries are visible and controllable, and decide the default under appropriate user/privacy/rights governance.

One promising graded product scheme (proposal, not adopted):
- **Neutral mode:** objective physically grounded baseline + explicit ATTEND and acquired knowledge; no implicit long-run gaze profiling.
- **Explicit accessibility/ergonomics mode:** user chooses text density, spatial orientation, language assistance and related presentation options.
- **Bounded adaptive mode:** if authorized, slow non-sensitive attention/recognition evidence weights optional details with transparent purpose/controls.
- **Research mode:** separate explicit consent, source provenance and independent sample validity; not an invisible escalation of ordinary play.

The moral and political stakes are material: who has access to the gaze-model data, who can exploit it, and who controls the conditions of a person's encounter with an Other? The mechanism cannot celebrate autonomy while secretly monetizing the pathways of attention.

# 31. Shared roots, separate user histories and multiplayer distinction

Two users entering identically seeded Nadia/world roots may receive different phenomenal surfaces, because their own attention and recognition differ. The shared **pre-contact** world/Nadia truth remains identical under root constraints; after real interaction, each copy-on-write continuation can diverge for legitimate causal reasons.

Thus:
§SAME ROOT + DIFFERENT GAZE → DIFFERENT OPTIONAL SURFACE, SAME NADIA OCCURRENCE§
at the controlled same-event comparison.

But over later turns:
§DIFFERENT SURFACE → DIFFERENT USER ACTION → DIFFERENT ADMITTED WORLD/NADIA CONTINUITY§
may lawfully occur.

That difference is **not** a bug; it is real mediated human activity. It must be traceable to an actual user action, not a hidden gaze-model-to-actor call.

A truly shared multi-user Nadia would require one world plus separate observers, actor models, attention receipts and network consequences; that is a different multiplayer product frontier, not a premise of Mechanism II.

# 35. Decisions that genuinely remain unresolved

**D1 — How much implicit adaptation is permitted by default?** Sorok 59 restrictive baseline, Sorok 60 refined attention modeling and proposed Sorok 27 user's intent must be reconciled with privacy, informed user control and experimentation. Do not bury this decision in an implementation parameter.

**D2 — What is the exact level of learned propensity?** Categories of work/attention, perceptual microfeatures, task/context-conditioned patterns and recognition skills could be represented differently. Avoid a psychometric taxonomy disguised as gaze.

**D3 — Can the compositor be local and still achieve the desired micro-precision?** Sorok 101 advocates local composition for the two-call normal turn; a later cleanly walled model is an empirical alternative requiring source-safe public-only inputs.

**D4 — How does text provide unmentioned but accessible alternatives?** The user needs realistic navigation and ATTEND without an exhausting room list. This is a central textual-medium interface research problem.

**D5 — What is legitimate repeated-noticing reinforcement?** A user's authentic selective practice may narrow, but feedback from the system's own salience cannot be treated as independent strong evidence. Determine the policy by controlled comparisons rather than forcing therapeutic balance.

**D6 — What is source-qualified user knowledge?** A recognition profile may be inferred from demonstrations, but the boundary between spontaneous expertise, learned names, confidence and error needs exact receipts.

**D7 — How will accessibility change observation?** Accessibility should adapt channels and interfaces without diagnosing a person, falsifying underlying events or making the user inferior.

**D8 — What does “artistic aptness” mean operationally?** Ground it in restrained factual selection, causal intelligibility, source trace and a human evaluation of false-omniscience/over-explanation, not generated emotional intensity or generic “beautiful writing” ratings.

**D9 — What is the user's personal freedom under incomplete observation?** The world may constrain and permanently alter opportunities; the renderer cannot secretly eliminate still-real alternatives, but cannot promise infinite reversibility.

**D10 — What data and access controls bind a model of the external user?** Q-PRIV/source contracts and user-led decisions govern; research/marketing/ads cannot opportunistically capture gaze data.

**D11 — How much user-model-of-Nadia inference is justified?** It may be relevant to realistic finite encounter, but avoids silently claiming that a question proves belief/attraction or that narrator analysis is user cognition.

**D12 — How are different users compared scientifically?** Need identical-root controlled counterfactuals, causal exposure records and careful inference about a self-selected user population; cannot generalize demographic laws from product convenience data.

# 36. The most accurate product statement

> The synthetic Other is independently constituted through material and social history. The user does not receive that Other whole. They encounter finite evidence through historically acquired human powers of attention, recognition and practical engagement. What they notice, misrecognize, neglect and infer may become part of the history of their relationship, but it cannot retroactively determine what Nadia was, make her mirror their desire, or compel her disclosure. Rawlsing may learn limited, revisable patterns of their **demonstrated mode of encountering**, while preserving the objective world, the user's freedom to look differently where alternatives remain real, and Nadia's irreducible difference from both the user's model and her own self-account. The narrator's art is to present concrete fact with exceptional discrimination and almost no interpretive appropriation.

**Mechanism I makes the world independently deep. Mechanism II makes the user's access to that depth humanly particular, incomplete and historically consequential.**

**Not one new psyche. Not a tailored plot. Not a personality quiz. An epistemically rigorous relation between independent reality and the human act of seeing.**
