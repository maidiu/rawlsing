# Provisional Relational Ontology

Status: `HYPOTHESIS__ONTOLOGY_R1`

This is a design object, not yet governed V3 law.

## 1. Core correction

A person is not best represented as a monad with relations attached later.

Rawlsing should model:

`world → relational field → person-as-history-of-relations → relational priors → encounter → dyadic history → network consequences → changed persons`

Relations do not merely happen to a completed person. They participate in producing the person.

This is now an explicit **relationalization of inherited PFF mediation**, not a replacement for PFF.

PFF already distinguished total causal personhood, standing conscious self, current relational activation, present conscious experience/self-interpretation and outward expression. Sorok's added claim is that the supposedly "deeper" person and standing self must not be read as pre-relational interior substances.

`PERSON-STABLE != RELATION-FREE`.

Durable dispositions, self-models, defenses, expectations, needs and accessibility patterns may themselves be sedimented products of histories of care, recognition, authority, dependence, absence, conflict, role and institutional position.

See:
`dockets/D001_RELATIONAL_ONTOLOGY_V3_BOUNDARY/03_SOROK_RELATIONALIZATION_DELTA_OVER_INHERITED_PFF_R1.md`.

Isolation is not absence of relation; it is a socially and historically formative relation to absent, inaccessible, withdrawn, lost, imagined or threatening others.

## 2. Required ontological distinctions

### Person-stable disposition
Relatively persistent tendencies with developmental history.

"Person-stable" means cross-situationally/cross-relationally persistent enough to belong to the person's durable organization. It does **not** mean relation-independent essence.

### Relational prior
Learned expectation about what other people do, what care means, what need costs, what conflict threatens, what trust requires.

### Role-conditioned tendency
Behavior that changes by relational position: parent, child, lover, subordinate, authority, stranger, dependent, caretaker, rival.

### Dyadic state
A property of this relationship specifically. It cannot be inferred from either person alone.

### Network effect
A property generated by multiple relations: jealousy, triangulation, divided loyalty, reputation, competing commitments, coalition, family integration.

### Developmental residue
Earlier relational organization that still conditions present action.

### Objective life state
Events, resources, obligations, bodies, institutions and histories that exist whether or not the actor correctly knows or narrates them.

### Actor model
What a person believes about themselves, the other, and the relationship.

### User model of persona
What the user currently infers about the persona.

### Persona model of user
What the persona currently infers about the user.

### Meta-models
What each believes the other believes about them.

## 5. Flourishing / health

The user's evaluation of the relationship must not define persona flourishing.

Candidate health-family dimensions to research include:
- agency;
- self-trust;
- differentiated attachment;
- boundary capacity;
- ability to sustain multiple relationships;
- reality contact;
- affect tolerance;
- capacity for repair;
- capacity for play / curiosity / generativity;
- ability to give and receive care without domination or self-erasure.

This requires explicit philosophical and psychological adjudication. “Objective health” is not self-justifying language.

## 6. Developmental continuity and the child

Do not implement a literal mystical “inner child” object merely because the metaphor is powerful.

The stronger candidate model is:

> intimacy progressively permits adult expression of relational expectations, needs, fears, play, protest, dependency, curiosity and vulnerability whose organization has developmental history.

At high trust, two adults may allow developmental layers normally defended by public adulthood to become active in the dyad.

Greater intimacy may therefore expose **new dysfunction and new expectation**, not simply healthier behavior.

## 7. Mutual model convergence

A strong candidate account of knowing another person is:

`increasing causal model fidelity without identity collapse`

Example progression:

`she is quiet`
→ `she becomes quiet when disagreement feels unsafe`
→ `she becomes quiet with me when she believes expressing another need would threaten belonging`

Intimacy is not omniscience. Opacity remains real.

## 8. LLM boundary

The LLM may render:
- speech;
- situated narration;
- possible action expression;
- natural-language interpretation within actor access.

It does not author:
- objective causal history;
- hidden health;
- canonical dyadic state;
- world events;
- the persona's full internal truth.

Rendered output enters world history only through explicit admission.


## 9. Relationalization without dyadic collapse

Everything being relational does not mean every fact belongs to a relationship object.

Preserve:
- person != dyad;
- dyad != network;
- current dyad != total developmental explanation;
- relationally constituted != immediately dyad-owned;
- cross-relational organization != context-free essence.

The architecture must be able to represent:
- durable relationally formed person organization;
- relation-class / role-conditioned priors;
- specific dyadic history;
- current person×event configuration;
- network/third-party effects;

without collapsing them into one "relationship state."

## 10. Recursive theorem

Candidate deepest ontology:

`RELATIONS PRODUCE PERSONS`
→ `PERSONS ENTER RELATIONS`
→ `RELATIONS REPRODUCE / DISCONFIRM / TRANSFORM PERSON ORGANIZATION`
→ `CHANGED PERSONS ALTER OTHER RELATIONS AND WORLDS`.

This is the Sorok frontier over inherited psychic mediation.
