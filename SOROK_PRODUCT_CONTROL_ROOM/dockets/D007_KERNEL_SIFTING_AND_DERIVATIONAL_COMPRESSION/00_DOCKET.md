## Current hottest compression region — provisional only

The strongest present candidate region is:

> **irreducible first-personhood without self-sufficient individuality**

Expanded:

> **Every person is an irreducible first-person center of valuation whose present organization is radically constituted through material, historical and interpersonal relation, while neither any single relation, the total enumerable history of relations, nor any participant's representation of those relations finally exhausts the person generated. Real relations nevertheless enter causally and irreversibly into what persons become.**

A closely related phenomenological / ethical compression is:

> **Every person is an irreducible center from which reality is valued, never finally possessable by another representation, yet constituted and transformed through relation. Because no human center encompasses the whole, encounter requires kenotic attention: relinquishing the sovereignty of one's own phenomenal horizon sufficiently to become answerable to reality disclosed from another center. Love of neighbor is the indefinitely deepening practice of such answerability without appropriation.**

These are **candidate compressions**, not the adjudicated Rawlsing kernel.

## Key noncollapse

Do not read relational constitution as either:

`SELF-SUFFICIENT MONAD + OPTIONAL RELATIONS`

or:

`PERSON = ENUMERABLE SUM OF RELATION EDGES`.

Current candidate:

`RELATIONAL / MATERIAL / HISTORICAL FORMATION`
→ `SINGULAR ORGANIZATION / TENSION-SITE`
→ `NEW CAUSAL CAPACITIES`
→ `NEW RELATIONS`
→ `FURTHER TRANSFORMATION`.

The person's excess need not be explained by a hidden relation-free essence.

It may arise because relational formation produces an organized first-person causal center capable of:
- synthesis;
- refusal;
- reinterpretation;
- novelty;
- self-misrecognition;
- creation;
- transformation of inherited relations;
- transformation of other people and worlds.

## Nested non-exhaustion hypothesis

Current conversation-derived clue:

> **The person exceeds every relation.  
> The relation exceeds either person's experience of it.  
> The world exceeds every person's phenomenal access to it.  
> Yet person, relation and world genuinely constitute one another historically.**

This nested structure is one of the strongest current clues to Q-KERNEL-001.
