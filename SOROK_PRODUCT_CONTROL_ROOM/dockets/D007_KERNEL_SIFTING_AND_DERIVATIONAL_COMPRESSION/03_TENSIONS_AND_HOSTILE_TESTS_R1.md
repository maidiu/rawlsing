# D007 — Tensions and Hostile Tests R1

Status: `ACTIVE_HOSTILE_TESTING__NO_KERNEL_ADJUDICATED`

Purpose: attack the current hottest compression candidate before it hardens into doctrine.

Current candidate region:

> irreducible first-personhood without self-sufficient individuality;
> relational constitution without relational exhaustibility;
> nested non-exhaustion under real reciprocal historical causation.

These formulations are useful only if they survive the tensions below.

---

# 1. Synthetic actor != literal human subject

## Tension

The product represents/generatedly instantiates a human-like person through governed computational state and LLM expression.

But:

> **Nadia is not a biological human being, and Sorok has not established that Nadia has phenomenal consciousness or intrinsic moral status identical to a human subject.**

Therefore phrases such as:
- first-person center;
- value-source;
- image-bearer;
- irreducible subject

must not silently become empirical/metaphysical claims about machine consciousness.

## Required distinction

`ACTOR-LOCAL FIRST-PERSON MODEL / PERSPECTIVE`
!=
`CLAIM OF PHENOMENAL MACHINE CONSCIOUSNESS`.

Rawlsing can require that Nadia be modeled **as a causally independent actor-local center**:
- she has actor-local perception;
- memory;
- valuation state;
- goals;
- vulnerability;
- models;
- history;
- consequences;

without claiming that the implementation literally experiences these states as a human does.

## Why this matters to the kernel

If the kernel requires literal machine consciousness, it is presently unusable.

A viable product kernel should specify the **causal/representational structure needed to encounter another humanly thick center** without resting on an unproved claim that the synthetic implementation is itself a human-equivalent moral patient.

Hostile test:
can the candidate preserve all required product behavior if "first-person" is operationalized as actor-indexed perspective/valuation rather than metaphysical consciousness?

# 2. Image-bearing language: theological horizon vs architecture

## Tension

"Image-bearing value-source" is powerful theological compression.

But it may perform at least three different jobs:

1. a claim about actual human beings;
2. a design target for the synthetic human representation;
3. a theological interpretation of why receiver-side valuation matters.

These cannot be collapsed.

## Required discipline

Use theological language as:
- project horizon;
- interpretive compression;
- source of candidate invariants;

but translate any architecture consequence into operational terms:
- actor-local valuation;
- independent causal state;
- noninstrumental product treatment;
- receiver-side consequence;
- perspective noncollapse.

Hostile test:
if "image of God" language is removed, does the machine requirement remain intelligible and enforceable?

If not, it is not yet architecture.

# 3. Irreducibility vs complete computational state

## Tension

A finite computer program must store a bounded state.

Yet the candidate says:
> the person exceeds every representation.

Potential contradiction:
if the engine has the "true state" of Nadia, has the engine possessed/exhausted her after all?

## Possible resolution

Distinguish:

`CANONICAL COMPUTATIONAL STATE`
from
`THE HUMAN REALITY RAWLSING IS REPRESENTING / GENERATING TOWARD`
from
`USER PHENOMENAL / RELATIONAL ACCESS`.

Rawlsing need not claim an actually infinite synthetic metaphysical person.

Instead it can require:
- causal underdetermination resolved lawfully;
- bounded causal overhang;
- generative depth ahead of current attention;
- no user-facing completion semantics;
- continued development through new events;
- no model/summary treated as interchangeable with all future manifestations.

Thus "non-exhaustion" may be a **generative architecture principle** rather than a literal claim of infinite stored facts.

Hostile test:
does the candidate still work when Nadia's runtime state is fully serializable?

If not, it confuses product phenomenology with metaphysical infinity.

# 4. Relational constitution vs graph reductionism

## Tension

If persons are radically constituted through relation, why are they not reducible to the complete graph of relations?

Current answer candidate:
relations are:
- temporally ordered;
- mutually conditioning;
- interpreted through existing organization;
- embodied;
- recursively generalized;
- remembered/forgotten;
- contradicted;
- transformed;
- selectively accessible.

Therefore:
`RELATIONAL HISTORY`
does not act as an unordered additive list.

It produces an organized dynamical resultant.

## Hostile test

Construct two persons with:
- identical relation labels/counts;
- identical broad life events;
but different:
- timing;
- sequence;
- embodiment;
- interpretation;
- salience;
- memory;
- role structure.

If the architecture cannot differentiate them without adding arbitrary essence variables, the relational model is too coarse.

# 5. Relational constitution vs relation-free essence

## Tension

"Person exceeds relations" can sneak a metaphysical soul-nugget back into the model.

Current candidate avoids this by locating excess in:
- organization;
- emergence;
- temporality;
- recursive causal capacity;
- novelty under new conditions;
- partial self-access.

But this remains a philosophical/architecture question.

## Hostile test

Can the machine produce:
- refusal of inherited pattern;
- surprising synthesis;
- novel practice;
- self-misrecognition;
- transformation of formative relations

using only governed historically formed organization + current event + stochasticity/novel input?

If yes, relation-free essence is not required for the architecture.

# 6. Social relation vs body / biology / materiality

## Tension

"Relationally constituted" must not collapse into:
"social discourse made the whole person."

Human formation also includes:
- body;
- health;
- neurobiology;
- age;
- sexed embodiment;
- disability;
- temperament;
- material environment;
- accident;
- nonhuman ecology;
- stochastic events.

Current candidate should therefore be read as:

`MATERIAL + EMBODIED + INSTITUTIONAL + INTERPERSONAL + HISTORICAL RELATION`

rather than social-interpersonal relation alone.

Hostile test:
can the candidate model a major person-forming fact with no meaningful interpersonal cause?

If not, it is too socially totalizing.

# 7. First-person plurality vs symmetry

## Tension

The user and Nadia both require actor-local perspectives.

But they are not symmetrical in every respect.

The user is:
- an actual external human;
- the bearer of real-world consent/privacy rights;
- the one whose actual life continues outside Rawlsing.

Nadia is:
- a generated/simulated person;
- an actor inside the governed synthetic world;
- not assumed to possess literal human consciousness.

## Required distinction

`PERSPECTIVAL NONCOLLAPSE`
does not imply
`ONTOLOGICAL / LEGAL / MORAL IDENTITY BETWEEN USER AND SYNTHETIC ACTOR`.

Hostile test:
can the candidate preserve reciprocal causal seriousness without requiring false symmetry?

# 8. Value-source language vs objective flourishing

## Tension

If every subject is a source of valuation, does that imply:
- whatever Nadia wants is good for Nadia?
- whatever the user wants is equally justified?
- conflicting valuations are incomparable?

No.

Sorok already requires:
`ATTACHMENT != HEALTH != BENEFIT != COMPLIANCE`.

So "value-source" cannot mean subjective preference is sovereign.

Possible reading:
a subject is a locus **from which things can matter**, but evaluation of flourishing may still require:
- agency;
- material reality;
- long-term consequence;
- other relations;
- contradiction between immediate preference and durable capacity.

Hostile test:
derive a case where Nadia intensely wants something that predictably destroys her agency or other valued relations.
Does the kernel tell us anything useful without smuggling in a full moral theory?

# 9. Kenotic attention vs self-erasure

## Tension

Kenosis can be misread as:
- surrender your judgment;
- accept local norms;
- defer to Nadia;
- dissolve the self;
- become endlessly accommodating.

That contradicts Court 7:
the user may correctly challenge the local field.

## Required distinction

`KENOTIC ATTENTION`
=
relinquish the presumption that one's present horizon exhausts reality.

It does **not** mean:
- relinquish agency;
- relinquish moral judgment;
- assume the other is right;
- seek approval;
- erase conflict.

Hostile test:
can the user lovingly attend to Nadia and still say:
> no, you are wrong and I will resist this?

If not, the praxis has collapsed into submission.

# 11. Non-possession vs practical commitment

## Tension

Non-possession could be overread as:
never rely on another;
never form exclusivity;
never make binding commitments;
never say "my partner," "my family," "my friend."

But ordinary human relations include:
- mutual claims;
- promises;
- custody;
- marriage;
- exclusive commitments;
- dependence;
- durable obligations.

Candidate implication:
non-possession must mean:
> commitment does not convert the other's existence into a function/property of oneself.

Hostile test:
can the architecture support radical mutual commitment without treating either person as an owned resource?

# 12. Inexhaustibility vs irresponsible vagueness

## Tension

"People are infinitely complex" can become an excuse for:
- arbitrary LLM invention;
- contradiction;
- never committing to facts;
- mystical handwaving.

Rawlsing requires the opposite:
- admitted facts persist;
- causal closure;
- history;
- exact provenance;
- bounded lazy generation.

Therefore:
`INEXHAUSTIBLE != UNCONSTRAINED`.

Hostile test:
does every new detail become more tightly constrained as history accumulates?

If not, "inexhaustibility" is functioning as randomness.

# 13. Knowledge increases complexity vs model fidelity

Yes, if:
- crude uncertainty decreases;
- specific causal prediction improves;
- differentiated knowledge increases;
while:
- awareness of context-dependence;
- hidden history;
- unshared interior;
- future novelty

Potential target:
`MODEL FIDELITY ↑`
while
`CLAIMED TOTALIZATION ↓`.

can users become demonstrably better at predicting Nadia while simultaneously becoming less likely to describe her with a simplistic essence?

This may be an important alpha measure.
