## 0. Candidate insight

One of Rawlsing's deepest purposes may be stated as:

> challenge perception so the user becomes capable of confronting the invisible-to-them, identified-with, structurally pressured, ideologically filtered perceptual field through which reality ordinarily appears self-evident.

This is not merely a reflection feature.

It may compose directly with:
- homologous-peer transposition;
- attention personalization;
- partial knowledge;
- independent otherness;
- actor-local modeling;
- irreversible history.

## 1. The playable contradiction

The adaptive phenomenal system can become increasingly good at rendering the world in a way consonant with the user's demonstrated attention, vocabulary, valuation and recognition.

That creates:
- comfort;
- fluency;
- continuity;
- subjective legibility;
- a sense that the interface has learned the user's eye.

But the game's deeper purpose pushes in the opposite direction:

> becoming a neighbor to Nadia requires exceeding, revising or at least becoming conscious of the perceptual field through which Nadia is being encountered.

Therefore the personalization mechanism contains its own contradiction:

ADAPTIVE PHENOMENAL FIT
vs.
NEIGHBORLY ADEQUACY TO AN INDEPENDENT OTHER.

A more personalized rendering can make the user know Nadia less adequately by recursively strengthening one partial mode of seeing.

## 2. Elephant theorem

A user may learn the tail or trunk of the elephant extremely well.

Greater:
- detail;
- recognition;
- local prediction;
- lexical sophistication;
- repeated evidence;

need not yield greater adequacy to the whole person.

Candidate compact law:

> MORE DIFFERENTIATED KNOWLEDGE OF A PART != GREATER ADEQUACY TO THE PERSON AS A WHOLE.

This is stronger than generic noncompletion.

It identifies a failure mode in which knowledge itself becomes a vehicle of enclosure.

## 3. Why Nadia is necessary

Without an independent other, adaptive phenomenal reflection could collapse into refined self-study.

Nadia supplies the resistant remainder:

- the user-specific narrator may learn the user's eye;
- Nadia's own personhood does not become fitted to that eye;
- Nadia reacts only through her own causal organization;
- the user's increasingly coherent interpretation may therefore repeatedly fail.

This makes the gap between:
USER MODEL
and
OTHER PERSON
experiential rather than merely philosophical.

## 4. Ideology at the phenomenal level

The system may expose that categories experienced as properties of objects can partly reflect the history of the perceiver:

- messy;
- homey;
- pretentious;
- expensive;
- tacky;
- religious;
- sad;
- normal;
- authentic;
- evidence-worthy;
- ignorable.

The project does not need to decide that any such characterization is false.

The point is to make visible that:
- the characterization has a history;
- another perceiver may organize the same evidence differently;
- the other person's own relation to the object may differ from both.

Candidate theorem:

> EVEN WHAT PRESENTS ITSELF AS OBVIOUS REALITY MAY BE PARTLY AN ACHIEVEMENT OF A HISTORICALLY PRODUCED PERCEIVER.

## 5. Reflexivity without view-from-nowhere

The game should not promise:
> remove your bias and see objectively.

Instead:
- disclose the adaptive lens;
- show concrete behavioral evidence of what has shaped it;
- allow deliberate excursions outside its habitual sampling;
- preserve the fact that any new view remains situated.

Thus the veil task is not neutrality.

It is the development of a relation to one's own partiality.

## 6. Relation to homologous peer

Court 7 strips or translates much of the user's original structural position while carrying their present historically formed consciousness.

The adaptive lens can make that carried formation visible in action.

Composition:

CARRIED HABITUS / PRACTICAL DISPOSITIONS
+
HOMOLOGIZED FIELD POSITION
+
USER-SPECIFIC PHENOMENAL LENS
+
INDEPENDENT LOCAL OTHER
→
HABITUS FRICTION + REFLEXIVE PERCEPTUAL FRICTION.

This may operationalize the veil more strongly than material transposition alone.

## 7. Kernel-relevant symmetry

Candidate only:

> NO PARTICIPANT IS EXHAUSTED BY ANOTHER PARTICIPANT'S MODEL OF THEM—INCLUDING THE PLAYER.

Possible nested structure:
- Rawlsing has a bounded model of the user;
- user has a bounded model of Nadia;
- Nadia has a bounded model of user;
- each model can be causally useful;
- none becomes the person.

This symmetry may connect:
- non-exhaustive personhood;
- partial knowledge;
- adaptive narration;
- actor opacity;
- user reflection;
- relation.

It should be hostile-tested for derivational power rather than promoted by elegance.

## 8. Hostile tests

1. Can the adaptive lens produce useful phenomenological specificity without becoming algorithmic confirmation enclosure?
2. Can a user become more locally accurate while globally more mistaken about Nadia?
3. Can Nadia falsify the user's model through ordinary causal life rather than authored corrective lessons?
4. Can reflexive disclosure reveal the lens without making users optimize for a new "open-mindedness" score?
5. Can two users with the same shared-root world receive meaningfully different phenomenal renderings while all canonical facts remain compatible?
6. Can a user's lens change substantially over years without Rawlsing declaring an identity transformation?
7. Can Rawlsing preserve the user's own non-exhaustion under its behavioral model?

This candidate appears unusually compatible with:
- homologous-peer Rawlsing;
- neighbor-love/nonprojection;
- attention architecture;

Required next work:
