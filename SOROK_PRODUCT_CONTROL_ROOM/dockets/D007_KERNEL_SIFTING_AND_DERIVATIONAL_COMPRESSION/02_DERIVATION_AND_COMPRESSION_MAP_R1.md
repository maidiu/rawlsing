# D007 — Derivation and Compression Map R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_DERIVATION_MAP__KERNEL_OPEN`

Purpose: test whether the current composite candidate actually **derives** Rawlsing's architecture, rather than merely providing attractive language for it.

## 0. Candidate components under test

For derivational purposes, decompose the current hottest region into five components:

### P1 — plural first-person/value centers
Each person is represented as a distinct center of:
- perception;
- memory;
- valuation;
- intention;
- vulnerability;
- action;
- consequence.

No human center is the privileged circumference of all others.

### P2 — relational/material/historical constitution
Persons are not self-originating monads.

Durable organization is formed through:
- material conditions;
- institutions;
- roles;
- care;
- authority;
- conflict;
- absence;
- dependence;
- recognition;
- loss;
- prior relations.

### P3 — non-exhaustion
No:
- relation;
- event;
- role;
- model;
- expression;
- perceptual surface;
- biography;
- current self-understanding

is identical to the total person/world/relation it presents.

### P4 — reciprocal causal permeability and historical becoming
Relations are not merely interpretations laid over fixed persons.

Actual encounter can:
- change expectations;
- alter models;
- create obligations;
- form memory;
- transform durable organization;
- reorganize practical life;
- alter future possibility.

These changes enter irreversible history.

### P5 — situated partial disclosure
Access to reality is conditioned by:
- position;
- attention;
- recognition;
- relation;
- time;
- action;
- lawful information routes.

Disclosure changes what can be known without granting authorship of what is true.

# 4. Kernel versus constitution versus implementation

One major tightening from this pass is a three-level distinction.

## Kernel candidate
Should explain **why the organism has its basic causal form**.

Examples:
- relational constitution;
- non-exhaustion;
- first-person plurality;
- situated disclosure;
- irreversible reciprocal causation.

## Product constitution
Normative/product commitments governing what Rawlsing should do with that organism.

Examples:
- neighbor as end;
- no retention manipulation;
- no commodification;
- no moralizing purity system;
- privacy;
- research boundaries.

These may be strongly motivated by the kernel without being logically identical to it.

## Implementation strategy
One current way to realize the organism.

Examples:
- five-ish shared seed people;
- copy-on-write storage;
- compiled root package;
- exact LLM pipeline;
- renderer choice;
- exact database schema.

These should be judged **in relation to the kernel**, not mistaken for parts of it.

# 5. Strongest present derivation chain

The most compressed current chain is:

`RELATIONAL / MATERIAL HISTORY`
→ `SINGULAR FIRST-PERSON ORGANIZATION`
→ `PARTIAL SITUATED ACCESS BETWEEN CENTERS`
→ `ACTION UNDER INCOMPLETE MODELS`
→ `REAL CONSEQUENCE`
→ `IRREVERSIBLE SHARED HISTORY`
→ `CHANGED PERSON / RELATION / WORLD`
→ `NEW PARTIAL ENCOUNTER`.

With a standing non-exhaustion condition:

> no manifestation at any stage is identical to the generative totality from which it arose.

Possible ethical/praxis consequence:

> because the other center cannot be possessed by one's model, competent care requires ongoing attention, revision and answerability rather than projection.

This chain is presently the strongest candidate **machine skeleton**.

It is not yet the adjudicated kernel.

# 6. Current diagnostic question

The central test for the next pass is:

> Can `P1–P5` be compressed further without losing derivational power?

Especially:

- Is P3 non-exhaustion a consequence of P1 + P2, or genuinely independent?
- Is P5 partial disclosure a necessary consequence of plural first-person centers, or an implementation/product commitment?
- Is P4 irreversibility already contained in real constitutive causation?
- Is "value center" necessary, or can the normative layer be derived later from first-person agency/receivability?
- Does "first-person" belong in the machine kernel given that synthetic actors are not being claimed to be biological/conscious humans?
- Is the deepest form actually not "person" at all, but a more general ontology of **relationally constituted, causally irreducible centers under partial access**?

These questions belong to hostile testing, not rhetorical resolution.

# 7. New derivation probes from artifact 69

### Agency without sovereignty
Source: P1 + P3 + P5.
Strength: `D3 STRONG`.

If plural centers are real and no user's model exhausts another center, then the player may possess action authority without possessing truth authority over the other.

### Persona introduction / same-root comparative branches
Source: P2 + P3 + P4 + P5.
Strength: `D2 SUBSTANTIAL`.

The kernel strongly predicts the value of comparing:
- common origin;
- different situated access;
- different relation history;
- different becoming.

The private introduction feature itself remains a product/social implementation choice.

### Shared pre-contact expansion vs branch-local constitution
Source: P2 + P4 + P5.
Strength: `D3 STRONG`.

Newly resolved pre-contact facts can be common because they belong to common prior history.
Post-contact effects cannot become common because they belong to relation-specific histories that did not occur in the other branch.

This is one of the cleanest current derivations of the root/branch membrane.
