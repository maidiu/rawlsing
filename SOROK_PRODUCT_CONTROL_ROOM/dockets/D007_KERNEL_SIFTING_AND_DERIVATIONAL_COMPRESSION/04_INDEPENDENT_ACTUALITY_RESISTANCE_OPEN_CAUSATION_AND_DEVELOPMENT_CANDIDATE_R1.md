## 0. Core candidate

A recurrent source of native Rawlsing tension may be:

PLAYER POSSIBILITY <-> INDEPENDENT ACTUALITY.

The user can genuinely attend, inquire, infer, speak, refuse, persuade, joke, help, interfere, withdraw, remember, misremember, repair, wait and act.

What those acts meet is not generated as a function of player need. The world/person brings prior history, current agendas, material constraints, relationships, boundaries, memories, blind spots, valuations, access conditions and causal continuity independent of the player.

Candidate compact formulation:

> THE PLAYER PUSHES INTO POSSIBILITY; THE WORLD/PERSON PUSHES BACK WITH ACTUALITY.

“Pushes back” means causal independence, not hostility or a difficulty dial.

## 1. Resistance is reality, not anti-player balancing

Reject:
- making Nadia harder to read because the user is succeeding;
- injecting contradiction because a scene feels too easy;
- randomizing actor behavior to preserve mystery;
- withholding lawful affordances to manufacture challenge;
- treating parser uncertainty as diegetic resistance.

Allow:
- a learned approach to work repeatedly where relevant determinants remain stable;
- a user to understand one domain of Nadia accurately;
- Nadia to be straightforward;
- nothing dramatic to happen;
- the user to persuade successfully;
- the user to fail because their model/action met independent conditions they did not control.

Therefore:

> OTHERNESS IS PRESERVED BY CAUSAL INDEPENDENCE, NOT BY SABOTAGING LEARNING.

D002/12 already states this law. The present candidate asks whether its composition with user possibility is a major source of play.

## 2. Accountability

Because admitted events persist, the user is answerable to what was actually there, not only to what they thought was there.

A mistaken model can become a misjudgment, awkward act, boundary crossing, missed opportunity, harmless misunderstanding, an event another actor remembers differently, or a fact with no durable consequence.

Later understanding does not rewrite the earlier occurrence.

Candidate:

> MODEL ERROR MAY BECOME HISTORY BEFORE IT BECOMES KNOWLEDGE.

This is not moral punishment. It is the consequence of acting under partial access in a world with its own state.

## 3. Hard substrate does not imply fixed trajectory

Preserve:

HARD PREEXISTING CONDITIONS != FIXED FUTURE.

More exact:

REAL CONDITIONS + OPEN CAUSATION -> CONTINGENT BECOMING.

The user cannot author the substrate merely by wanting a result. But the substrate is not a prewritten story: new relations can form, old interpretations can change, closed possibilities can reopen where causally warranted, apparently promising possibilities can disappear, and both participants can be changed by actual history.

## 4. Development is not upward relationship progression

Development may consist in increasing differentiation:
- more specific knowledge;
- more specific uncertainty;
- more exact expectations;
- more exact mistakes;
- more history;
- altered conventions;
- opened possibilities;
- closed possibilities;
- stronger disagreement;
- estrangement;
- practical familiarity;
- actor models of one another becoming more articulated.

Therefore:

> DEVELOPMENT != RELATIONSHIP IMPROVEMENT.

And:

> DEEPENING != INTIMACY.

Candidate meaning of deepening: greater causal and epistemic thickness.

A relation can deepen in this sense while becoming worse, more distant, more conflictual or more bounded.

## 5. Discovery and becoming must remain distinct

Some later-accessed reality was already true before the user knew it. Other reality exists only because history occurred.

Preserve:

Discovery: ANTECEDENT REALITY -> LATER LAWFUL ACCESS.

Becoming: ACTUAL EVENT/HISTORY -> NEW REALITY THAT DID NOT PREVIOUSLY EXIST.

Do not turn all deepening into “unveiling hidden Nadia.” Do not turn all change into retroactive truth.

## 6. Failure and fallout can enlarge the future without being rewards

In an irreversible but open world, failure does not need to mean restart, point loss or game over.

A failed attempt may create a new misunderstanding, clarified boundary, obligation, embarrassment, changed expectation, damage, no durable effect, or a different later decision surface.

The failure is not valuable because the product rewards it. It is playable because the world after it is now genuinely different where causality warrants.

Candidate:

> FAILURE MAY PRODUCE FUTURE PLAY BY CHANGING ACTUAL CONDITIONS, WITHOUT BEING CONVERTED INTO A REWARD LOOP.

REAL USER POSSIBILITY
