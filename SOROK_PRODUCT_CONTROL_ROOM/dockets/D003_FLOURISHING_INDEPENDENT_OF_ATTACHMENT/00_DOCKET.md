# D003 — Flourishing Independent of Attachment

Status: `OPEN__P0`

## Question

What does it mean for the user's presence to be objectively or intersubjectively **good for the persona** when the persona may like, need, defend, fear or depend on the user?

## Why this is foundational

Without this distinction Rawlsing can accidentally reward domination.

A user can:
- become indispensable;
- induce disclosure;
- secure forgiveness;
- monopolize attention;
- increase attachment;
- receive declarations of love or gratitude;

while making the other person less agentic, less connected, less reality-based or less capable of boundaries.

The game must be able to represent that as relational failure even if the user “gets one over” on the persona.

## Normative warning

Do not call the measure objective merely because it is hidden from the player.

Rawlsing will encode a substantive account of human flourishing. That account must be:
- explicit;
- plural;
- research-informed;
- culturally non-naive;
- contestable;
- uncertainty-bearing;
- resistant to one-number collapse.

## Candidate dimensions for investigation

- agency / efficacy;
- self-trust;
- differentiated attachment;
- boundary capacity;
- capacity to sustain multiple relationships;
- reality contact / reduced self-deception where measurable;
- affect tolerance;
- repair capacity;
- reciprocity without ledger domination;
- ability to give and receive care;
- play / curiosity / generativity;
- ability to tolerate another's separateness;
- reduced compulsive enactment of the person's own maladaptive attractors.

## Hard cases

The docket must answer how the model treats:
- culturally normative interdependence versus dependency;
- sacrifice;
- obedience;
- caregiving;
- religious self-surrender;
- family obligation;
- conflict avoidance;
- consensual hierarchy;
- material survival choices;
- temporary regression under grief/trauma;
- the persona rejecting a relationship that nevertheless helped them grow;
- the persona retaining a relationship that harms them.

## Deliverable

A candidate multidimensional flourishing constitution, epistemic limits, anti-colonial/cultural checks, relation to Enneagram health, and explicit statement of what cannot be inferred from the persona's self-report.
