# D009 — Human Faculty and Capability Authority Constitution R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW__NEEDS_EXACT_TRANSPOSITION_AND_UI_ADJUDICATION
Date: 2026-10-06

## 0. Core problem

Rawlsing must preserve the difference between:
- what the actual user genuinely performs through the medium;
- what the system can measure only indirectly or noisily;
- what the portaled body may be represented as capable of doing;
- what the homologous world assigns as structural position;
- what another actor merely believes about the user.

The product must not counterfeit user competence.

Nor should it throw away potentially rich embodied/social consequences merely because the text interface cannot directly measure every skill.

## 1. Five authority classes

### A. Medium-native faculty

The user actually performs the meaningful operation through Rawlsing.

Examples:
- attention allocation;
- choosing what to inspect;
- recognition when not supplied by system;
- inquiry;
- explanation;
- argument;
- humor content;
- objection;
- refusal;
- interpretation;
- memory expressed in action;
- model revision;
- prioritization;
- tact/strategic wording;
- timing at the interaction grain;
- restraint;
- choosing whether to intervene.

These should be exercised rather than replaced by stats wherever the medium can carry them.

Hard law:

> IF RAWLSING CAN MAKE THE USER DO THE MEANINGFUL PART, DO NOT REPLACE THAT PART WITH A CHARACTER STAT.

### B. Externally measurable but confounded performance

The interface may technically measure aspects of:
- reaction time;
- microphone audio / vocal performance;
- some spatial quickness;
- perhaps pronunciation or timing.

But measurements may be confounded by:
- hardware;
- latency;
- disability;
- age;
- environment/noise;
- accessibility aids;
- device modality.

These may be optional input modalities, never presumed universal proof of the user's general worth or capability.

### C. Portaled embodied / procedural capability

Some capabilities cannot be honestly tested through the central text medium:
- physical strength;
- running;
- manual dexterity;
- cooking hand technique;
- driving execution;
- mechanical technique;
- musical instrument technique;
- professional procedural skill.

If Rawlsing represents these, it must do so as a property of the portaled participant under an explicit carry/homologize/bracket/user-select rule.

It is not external-human certification.

### D. Structural position / power

Examples:
- wealth;
- purchasing power;
- labor position;
- housing security;
- institutional leverage;
- transport;
- network reach;
- legal/citizenship power;
- service access.

These are not personal skill buffs.

Under homologous-peer mode they are generated/translated into a structurally comparable local position.

A user cannot override the field by simply declaring:
> I am a billionaire.

Court 7 remains controlling:

STRUCTURALLY LOCAL / DEVELOPMENTALLY FOREIGN.

### E. Forbidden pseudo-capabilities

Do not create global stats such as:
- empathy;
- goodness;
- trustworthiness;
- humility;
- cultural competence;
- charisma as an objective universal property;
- insight;
- care.

These are either:
- actor-relative;
- behaviorally demonstrated;
- normatively contested;
- context dependent;
- or simply not measurable as a single capability.

## 2. Carry / homologize / bracket / user-select remains dimension-specific

Court 8 already provides the right operation vocabulary.

For each capability dimension ask:
- CARRY: does this meaningfully remain the actual present user's skill?
- HOMOLOGIZE: does the competence require local translation?
- BRACKET: should it remain unspecified unless causally needed?
- USER-SELECT: can bounded run setup lawfully choose it?

Examples remain open:
- cooking may carry partly as actual knowledge while manual execution is simulated;
- U.S. corporate legal expertise may require major homologization;
- driving may partly carry but depend on local road/vehicle familiarity;
- strength depends on embodiment translation;
- language competence may require a special translation constitution.

There is no single PORTALED_CAPABILITY switch.

## 3. Self-report is not a buff claim about reality

If user self-report is admitted for a capability, preserve:

USER SAYS “I'M A GOOD COOK”
!= RAWLSING PROVES EXTERNAL HUMAN COOKING ABILITY.

At most, under an adopted run rule, it may establish:
- a portaled embodiment capability prior;
- a user-authored fictional/translated capability;
- or evidence to be tested through future medium-native behavior where applicable.

Self-report should not automatically:
- grant structural privilege;
- prove expertise to Nadia;
- become player moral credit.

Nadia only knows what she can lawfully learn or infer.

## 4. Execution-prior interpretation

A useful candidate handling for simulated procedural competence:

> Portaled capability primarily governs the reliability/quality of procedural work the user delegates rather than specifies.

Example:
user says:
> I make scrambled eggs.

If the relevant cooking capability is represented as high, routine omitted technique may execute competently.

If user instead specifies:
> low heat, butter, keep them moving, pull them while glossy,

the user has supplied more of the procedure themselves.

Thus capability fills in **missing execution**, not missing judgment.

Candidate:

USER-SPECIFIED PROCEDURE
+ PORTALED EXECUTION PRIOR
+ WORLD / TOOLS / INGREDIENTS
+ TIME / INTERRUPTION / STOCHASTICITY
→ REALIZED RESULT.

The less the user delegates, the less work the prior owns.

This is promising but remains an implementation hypothesis, not a universal final rule.

## 5. Cooking worked boundary

User:
> I add paprika and lemon to the broccoli, cook it on medium-high until it just starts to soften, and pull the cast iron over to the table.

User genuinely supplies:
- seasoning;
- lemon;
- heat;
- doneness target;
- timing;
- cookware;
- decision to move it.

World/embodiment resolves:
- burner response;
- heat transfer;
- exact manual execution;
- whether the intended doneness is achieved;
- pan temperature;
- material compatibility with table.

Nadia may intervene:
> Hey, not right on the wood.

User:
> Oh shit, what am I thinking.

The user's inability to recall the word “trivet” is irrelevant to whether they understand the practical concept.

Rawlsing should not over-reward vocabulary where practical understanding is what matters.

## 6. Social performance of competence remains separate

See D008/02.

A user may sound competent without being competent.

Another actor may believe them.

Therefore:
- actor impression is actor state;
- system evidence of demonstrated user performance is separate;
- portaled capability is separate again.

Do not promote social signaling into capability merely because it successfully persuaded Nadia.

## 7. Homologous structural envelope

The user's external wealth/power cannot simply cross the portal.

Homologous-peer mode deliberately removes the original structural guarantee from which many habits became easy.

Thus capability transposition must not become an escape hatch restoring:
- wealth;
- elite institutional access;
- imported social networks;
- external professional authority

unless the homologous mapping explicitly licenses an analogue.

## 8. Native-skill priority

Rawlsing's deepest gameplay investment should remain in capacities the medium can authentically carry.

This does not mean embodied skill is irrelevant.

It means embodied skill should not crowd out:
- attention;
- inquiry;
- inference;
- judgment;
- communication;
- memory;
- contextual learning;
- model revision.

Hard design principle:

> EMBODIED CAPABILITY MAY SHAPE EVENTS; IT MUST NOT BECOME THE PRIMARY SUBSTITUTE FOR THE HUMAN FACULTIES RAWLSING EXISTS TO EXERCISE.
