# D009 — Physical Chance, Optional Modalities and Actor Impression R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_DIRECTION__NEEDS_ACCESSIBILITY_AND_MODALITY_PROTOTYPES
Date: 2026-10-06

## 0. Problem

Real relationships are affected by chance, reflex, grace, clumsiness, singing, embodied limitation, device-mediated performance and other events outside the text-native cognitive core.

Rawlsing should not erase this merely because the central interface is linguistic.

But neither should it become a reflex/minigame product.

## 1. Lucky physical outcomes can still have social meaning

Example:
Nadia tosses the user keys.

Outcome may depend on:
- portaled coordination;
- current orientation;
- occupied hands;
- toss quality;
- distance;
- lawful randomness;
- optional actual external-user reaction input if mode permits.

A catch may be partly luck.

Nadia may still lawfully infer:
> nice catch / they're quick.

That does not make the inference objectively true.

Preserve:

OBJECTIVE OUTCOME
!= CAUSAL CONTRIBUTION OF SKILL/CHANCE
!= NADIA'S ATTRIBUTION
!= USER'S ACTUAL EXTERNAL ABILITY.

Actor misattribution is lawful social life.

## 2. Physical events can become linguistic/social material

A missed catch can produce:
- embarrassment;
- teasing;
- irritation;
- apology;
- a request not to toss things;
- disclosure of clumsiness;
- a changed routine;
- a revised actor model.

Example:
> Hey! I wasn't ready for that.

or:
> Can you hand them to me like a normal person next time?

or:
> Honestly I'm a klutz; save us the embarrassment and hand them to me.

The embodied event matters because the user then lives socially with what happened.

## 3. Optional external-performance modalities

Potentially measurable modalities include:
- reaction timing;
- microphone audio;
- limited spatial/selection tasks;
- perhaps pronunciation/rhythm.

These should be evaluated under:
- accessibility;
- device variance;
- privacy;
- latency;
- measurement reliability;
- relevance to Rawlsing's purpose;
- risk of optimization/minigame drift.

Candidate mode family:
- external performance enabled;
- assisted;
- simulated.

Exact UX is open.

## 4. Singing example

If audio is admitted, the system may potentially derive bounded acoustic evidence such as:
- approximate pitch;
- rhythm;
- stability;
- range;
- loudness;
- timbral descriptors;
- breathiness/vibrato where robust.

It should not declare:
> GOOD SINGER = 82.

Actor response remains local.

A technically decent performance can still be disliked by Nadia.

Nadia may:
- love it;
- hate the song;
- mock it;
- be musically ignorant;
- be impressed;
- be a bitch.

The artistic valuation belongs to actors/context, not a universal score.

## 5. No embodied-skill meta by default

Avoid making the product reward:
- faster reaction times;
- better hardware;
- able-bodied motor performance;
- repeated quick-time-event optimization

as a central route to relationship success.

An occasional embodied micro-event may enrich reality.

A recurring stat economy would distort the project.

## 6. Disability and material difference are real

Age, disability, motor skill and device conditions do affect ordinary life and relation.

Rawlsing should not erase them under a false universal avatar.

But their treatment belongs under embodiment/accessibility/transposition law, not a simplistic difficulty penalty.

The social meaning of limitation is contextual and actor-relative.
