# D009 — Human Faculty, Capability Authority and Homologous Transposition

Status: PROVISIONAL_CONSTITUTION_BANKED__OPEN_P0

## Question

Which aspects of the external human can Rawlsing actually make the user perform, which can it measure only imperfectly, which may lawfully be carried or self-reported into the portaled embodiment, which must be homologized by the world, and which must never be converted into a game stat or claim of knowledge about the user?

## Why this docket exists

Rawlsing's medium can directly carry some human faculties—attention, language, inference, timing, memory-in-play, interpretation—while it cannot directly verify others such as strength, knife technique, running speed or singing quality with comparable certainty.

Homologous-peer mode also prevents structural power from becoming a freely chosen buff.

This docket preserves those boundaries.
