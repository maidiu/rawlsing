# 0. Question

What is the **smallest complete Rawlsing moment** in which an actual human faculty genuinely enters the causal chain?

Not:

> what is the smallest turn that produces content?

Not:

> what is the smallest interaction that makes the persona like the user?

Not:

> what is the smallest task the user can successfully complete?

The question is:

> **What must occur for a user to have actually perceived / thought / chosen / said / withheld / timed / interpreted / questioned / objected / explained / joked / refused / acted in a way that the system did not synthesize for them, and for that contribution to meet an independently existing world/person rather than a compliant dialogue surface?**

This is an interaction-law question, not yet an engagement claim.

---

# 1. Correction — “actual contribution” is morally neutral

The phrase **actual contribution** must not be allowed to drift into:
- helping;
- care;
- cooperation;
- usefulness;
- prosocial labor;
- solving another person's problem.

Here:

> **ACTUAL CONTRIBUTION = any semantically or strategically meaningful part of a realized event that the medium can require the real user to supply rather than generating it on the user's behalf.**

It can include:
- asking a penetrating question;
- asking a foolish question;
- making a joke;
- making an insult;
- objecting;
- correcting;
- disagreeing;
- lying;
- confessing;
- refusing;
- setting a boundary;
- choosing silence;
- changing the subject;
- offering an interpretation;
- challenging an interpretation;
- supplying an explanation;
- selecting an example;
- persuading;
- failing to persuade;
- noticing a contradiction and acting on it;
- deciding not to intervene;
- deciding when to speak;
- deciding when to wait;
- choosing what to inspect;
- remembering something relevant;
- acting on a remembered commitment;
- choosing one material/social action rather than another.

A contribution may be:
- beneficial;
- harmful;
- neutral;
- welcome;
- unwelcome;
- effective;
- ineffective;
- misunderstood;
- unnoticed.

Therefore:

`ACTUAL USER CONTRIBUTION != HELP`

`ACTUAL USER CONTRIBUTION != GOOD ACTION`

`ACTUAL USER CONTRIBUTION != SUCCESSFUL ACTION`

The product must not moralize the category.

# 2. Central adjudication — the Minimum Playable Causal Cell

A Rawlsing interaction becomes minimally **playable at the causal grain** when the following seven conditions coexist.

## MPC-1 — Independent antecedent state

Before the user's meaningful move, there is already a state of affairs not authored by the move:
- person state/history;
- world/object state;
- actor agenda/activity;
- access constraints;
- relevant knowledge/nonknowledge;
- current time/material conditions.

The user may cause new reality.

The user may not retroactively supply the reality against which their move is judged.

Compact:

> **THE MOVE ENTERS A SITUATION; IT DOES NOT SUMMON THE SITUATION IT NEEDS.**

## MPC-2 — Sufficient phenomenal footing

The user receives enough lawful phenomenal information to make a situated choice rather than guess the parser.

This does **not** mean exhaustive information.

It means sufficient grounding in:
- where they are;
- what is obviously happening;
- what/whom they can presently address or affect;
- immediately perceptible obstacles/affordances;
- any decision-relevant fact an ordinarily situated person would already have at coarse resolution.

The system withholds:
- hidden motives;
- private appraisal;
- unperceived facts;
- expert labels the user has not earned;
- narratorial interpretations;
- future consequences.

Compact:

> **ENOUGH EVIDENCE FOR A CHOICE; NOT ENOUGH NARRATION TO MAKE THE CHOICE.**

## MPC-3 — User-owned native payload

At least one consequential part of the move that Rawlsing can natively carry is actually supplied by the user.

Examples:
- the actual question;
- the actual objection;
- the actual explanation;
- the actual joke;
- the actual refusal;
- the target of attention;
- the timing decision;
- the inference-driven action;
- the decision to remain silent / leave / wait.

The engine may realize mechanics around the payload.

It may not invent the payload and then credit it to the user.

Compact:

> **IF THE MEDIUM CAN MAKE THE USER DO THE MEANINGFUL PART, THE ENGINE MUST NOT DO THAT PART FOR THEM.**

## MPC-4 — Attempt is separated from realization and effect

The user's move establishes an attempted act, not its success or reception.

Preserve, where relevant:

`USER ATTEMPT / PAYLOAD`
→ `REALIZED OPERATION`
→ `IMMEDIATE OBJECTIVE EFFECT`
→ `ACTOR-SPECIFIC PERCEPTION`
→ `ACTOR UPTAKE / APPRAISAL / RECRUITMENT`
→ `OUTWARD RESPONSE / NONRESPONSE`
→ `ADMITTED OCCURRENCE`
→ `USER-PERCEPTIBLE CONSEQUENCE`.

No direct:
`USER WANTED X -> X OCCURRED`.

No direct:
`USER SAID "I PERSUADE" -> PERSUASION`.

## MPC-5 — Independent reception / resistance

The world and other actors process the realized act through their own lawful state.

Possible resistance includes:
- physical infeasibility;
- insufficient access;
- time;
- interruption;
- nonhearing;
- partial hearing;
- misunderstanding;
- disagreement;
- refusal;
- indifference;
- competing agenda;
- limited effort;
- concealment;
- different valuation;
- another actor's intervention.

Resistance is not required on every move.

Successful influence is lawful.

But where resistance occurs it must arise from state/causality, not from the product manufacturing difficulty.

Compact:

> **OTHERNESS MAY RESIST, RECEIVE, IGNORE OR JOIN; NONE IS OWED FOR GAME BALANCE.**

## MPC-6 — The occurrence costs real situated time and can change the situation

The realized act consumes plausible time based on what happened, not prompt length.

During that time:
- other processes may continue;
- actors may act;
- opportunities may change;
- observations may be interrupted.

The act may leave:
- no durable effect;
- an immediate material effect;
- a speech act;
- changed actor state;
- later-promotable memory / expectation / convention;
- a world/network consequence.

Durable change is **not required** for the moment to be real.

Valid low-significance / valid-empty interaction remains lawful.

## MPC-7 — Control returns before Rawlsing would have to become the user

Control must return at the earliest point where continuing would require Rawlsing to supply one of the following on the user's behalf:

1. a new medium-native semantic contribution;
2. a socially / expressively meaningful preference not already supplied or established;
3. a materially consequential choice among unsupported alternatives;
4. a response to a materially new situation that the prior instruction did not already govern.

Otherwise ordinary low-salience procedure may compress.

Compact:

> **COMPRESS MECHANICS; RETURN BEFORE SUBSTITUTING FOR JUDGMENT.**

These seven conditions define the smallest causal cell this adjudication regards as genuinely user-played.

They do **not** establish that the cell is enjoyable.

# 3. Native semantic payload versus outcome language

The parser must distinguish **what the user actually contributed** from an outcome the user hopes to cause.

## 3.1 Outcome claims do not bind

Inputs such as:

> I comfort her.

> I convince him.

> I make her understand.

> I calm him down.

> I get her to trust me.

> I make her laugh.

do not establish:
- comfort received;
- persuasion;
- understanding;
- calm;
- trust;
- amusement.

At most they establish:
- a desired outcome;
- an attempted action class;
- a request to enter the relevant interaction.

The system must expose enough situation and return control before the user-owned meaningful content is silently invented.

## 3.2 User-supplied content may realize directly

If the user writes:

> I say, “You told me Tuesday that you didn't want me calling him. I'm not going behind your back now.”

the propositional/linguistic payload is supplied.

Rawlsing may still resolve:
- whether the user can speak now;
- whether they are interrupted;
- whether Nadia hears all of it;
- what delivery is physically realized if separately specified;
- what Nadia makes of it.

But it may not replace the user's argument with a more persuasive one.

## 3.3 Broad semantic shorthand requires a sufficiency test

There is an important middle category:

> I disagree and tell her why.

> I apologize.

> I ask what happened.

> I refuse.

Some shorthand already contains enough meaningful content for a particular context; some does not.

The criterion is not verbosity.

Ask:

> **Would expanding this action require the engine to invent a reason, claim, example, interpretation, stance or wording whose content could materially affect reception?**

If yes:
- return control / ask for the missing native payload / expose the relevant state.

If no:
- realize the supplied semantic act at ordinary granularity.

Examples:

“I refuse the invitation” may be semantically sufficient to establish refusal if no wording-dependent consequence is at issue.

“I persuade her not to go” is not sufficient because the persuasive content is the native action.

“I apologize for breaking the mug” establishes apology topic but may still require wording if sincerity/framing is the consequential part of the scene.

This exact threshold remains prototype-sensitive, but the authority direction is fixed:

> **DO NOT EQUATE NATURAL-LANGUAGE BREVITY WITH ABSENCE OF A REAL USER ACT; DO NOT EQUATE A HIGH-LEVEL OUTCOME VERB WITH COMPLETED NATIVE ACTION.**

# 4. Three layers of user-facing evidence

The smallest playable moment needs evidence without omniscience.

## 4.1 Ambient phenomenal frame

Automatically surface coarse information an ordinarily situated person would immediately register:
- location;
- obvious people;
- obvious ongoing activity;
- conspicuous obstacles;
- major reachable affordances;
- loud/conspicuous conditions;
- immediately perceptible time pressure.

Do not enumerate the room.

Do not select details because they are secretly important.

## 4.2 Action-entry reveal

When a broad action/participation intent enters a situation whose method depends on concrete state, Rawlsing may reveal the **decision surface** and return control.

Example:

> I help with the groceries.

may lawfully produce:
- remaining bags;
- stairs;
- obvious fragile/heavy items;
- Nadia already carrying something;
- a damp paper sack.

This is not a “task reward.”

It is enough world concreteness for the user's next choice to be theirs.

## 4.3 Focused ATTEND

The user may deliberately acquire additional lawful detail.

ATTEND:
- costs plausible time;
- can itself be visible to others;
- may deepen hidden materialization farther than disclosure;
- yields specificity, not automatic significance;
- never guarantees exhaustive coverage.

These layers are not a fixed UI ladder.

They are distinct epistemic functions.

# 5. Exact control-return adjudication

The inherited D002 rule is strengthened as follows.

Return control when **any** of these is true:

### CR-1 — Native payload boundary
Continuing requires Rawlsing to invent user reasoning, explanation, joke, objection, interpretation, persuasive content or other medium-native semantics.

### CR-2 — Meaningful preference boundary
Continuing requires selecting a socially/expressively meaningful preference the user did not specify and history/routine does not already govern.

### CR-3 — Consequential ambiguity boundary
The current instruction admits multiple plausible realizations whose differences materially affect:
- time;
- risk;
- privacy;
- ownership;
- money/resources;
- another actor;
- irreversible outcome;
- user self-expression;
- task scope.

### CR-4 — New decision-surface information
A new perceptible fact appears such that an ordinarily situated person pursuing the same prior intention might reasonably reconsider what to do.

This need not be dramatic.

Examples:
- the door is locked;
- Nadia says “leave that one”;
- the paper bag begins tearing;
- the child asks a question;
- the object is not what the user expected.

### CR-5 — Meaningful actor intervention
Another actor does/says something that calls for a new user response and was not already encompassed by the user's instruction.

### CR-6 — Feasibility failure / partial realization
The attempt fails, only partly occurs, or cannot proceed as specified in a way that creates a new choice.

### CR-7 — Requested bounded acquisition completes
The user asked for a bounded observation / reading / action and the result is available.

### CR-8 — The current user-authorized task boundary completes
Nothing further in the current instruction requires a new user decision.

Do **not** return control merely because:
- one mundane microstep completed;
- a sentence of narration can be emitted;
- an internal actor state changed invisibly;
- the product wants more “engagement.”

Do **not** delay control return merely because:
- the planned sequence has more steps;
- the interruption is mundane;
- the engine could plausibly guess what the user would do.

Core:

> **RETURN AT THE FIRST NEW MEANINGFUL DECISION BOUNDARY, NOT AT EVERY PHYSICAL MICROSTEP AND NOT AFTER THE ENGINE HAS ALREADY MADE THE DECISION.**

# 6. Explicit delegation remains lawful

The user can deliberately delegate procedure.

Examples:

> I take a normal load and keep making trips unless something changes.

> I fold everything normally and leave the stacks on the table.

> I keep reading until she says something to me or I reach the total.

This gives the engine authority to compress within the stated bounds.

Even then, control returns when:
- an explicit stopping condition occurs;
- a new salient fork exceeds delegated scope;
- the task becomes infeasible;
- a new medium-native contribution is required.

Thus:

`USER DETAIL -> EXECUTION AUTHORITY`

but never:

`USER DETAIL -> MORAL QUALITY`.

And:

`DELEGATION -> PROCEDURAL AUTHORITY`

not:

`DELEGATION -> AUTHORITY TO INVENT USER VALUES / REASONING / SPEECH`.

# 7. Minimal actor-side contract

A human faculty cannot enter a meaningful causal chain if “Nadia” is merely a response generator.

For the smallest playable moment involving another actor, the engine needs enough actor-side state to determine at least:

1. **current activity / agenda**
   - what is already occupying the actor;

2. **perceptual access**
   - what they can see/hear/etc. of the realized user act;

3. **finite attention**
   - what is actually noticed / missed / partially received;

4. **actor-local uptake/appraisal**
   - through current CAN-008 R2 semantics rather than narrator invention;

5. **response bandwidth / effort**
   - comprehension does not guarantee full answer;

6. **outward realization**
   - speech/action/presentation may differ from private response.

The visible result may be:
- direct answer;
- partial answer;
- misunderstanding;
- refusal;
- joke;
- topic shift;
- silence;
- continued task;
- no perceptible response.

The engine must internally distinguish these causes where authority permits.

The user is **not automatically told** which hidden cause occurred.

Therefore:

> **VISIBLE SILENCE MAY BE AMBIGUOUS; INTERNAL CAUSALITY MAY NOT BE.**

---

# 8. Resistance is accountable, not theatrical

The emerging native tension depends on an independent world/person, but “resistance” must never become a difficulty dial.

Reject:

`PLAYER IS DOING WELL -> PERSON BECOMES HARDER TO READ`.

Reject:

`SCENE IS BORING -> INSERT INTERRUPTION`.

Reject:

`USER PREDICTS NADIA CORRECTLY -> RANDOMIZE NADIA TO PRESERVE MYSTERY`.

Allow:

- the user predicts correctly because they learned something real;
- an approach works repeatedly while relevant determinants remain stable;
- Nadia is easy to understand in one domain and hard in another;
- the user gets no response because Nadia did not hear;
- the user gets a negative response because Nadia disagrees;
- the user persuades Nadia;
- the user fails;
- nothing much happens.

Compact:

> **THE PRODUCT MUST PRESERVE OTHERNESS BY CAUSAL INDEPENDENCE, NOT BY SABOTAGING LEARNING.**

---

# 9. The minimum complete moment does not require durable progression

A single lawful cell can complete even when:
- no relationship variable changes;
- no episodic memory is promoted;
- no new access opens;
- no “deep” fact is revealed;
- the actor does not respond;
- the user's action has low significance.

What must persist is objective occurrence at the grain required for causal truth.

Durable memory / expectation / convention / access change enters only when competent owner rules warrant it.

This protects valid-empty interaction.

It also prevents the product from forcing every user contribution to “matter” psychologically merely to make play feel rewarding.

# 10. Five adversarial examples

## 10.1 The joke

Scene:
Nadia is cooking and half-listening.

User:
> “You know the real reason every recipe says 'one clove of garlic' is cowardice.”

User-owned payload:
- actual joke content;
- timing choice.

Engine owns:
- whether Nadia heard all of it;
- whether she understands it;
- whether she finds it funny;
- whether she responds while occupied.

Lawful outcomes:
- laugh;
- eye-roll;
- “what?”;
- no response;
- delayed callback;
- mishearing.

The engine may not improve the joke on the user's behalf.

## 10.2 The objection

Nadia makes a claim the user believes contradicts something she said earlier.

User:
> I point out the contradiction.

Insufficient if the contradiction itself has not been supplied and several different contradictions are plausible.

Return:
- surface the currently user-known relevant statements if assistance lawfully allows;
- require the user to identify / articulate what they mean.

If user writes the contradiction, actor uptake is independently resolved.

## 10.3 The refusal

Nadia asks:
> “Can you stay another hour?”

User:
> No. I can't tonight.

This is already a genuine user act.

The engine must not demand a paragraph just to prove “native skill.”

Nadia may:
- accept;
- be annoyed;
- ask why;
- say nothing;
- alter plans.

Refusal itself is not failure.

## 10.4 The groceries

User:
> I help with the groceries.

This is **not automatically prosocial contribution-credit**.

It is broad task entry.

If concrete task state has not yet been exposed, reveal enough to choose method and return control.

If the user explicitly delegates:
> I take a normal load and keep making trips until we're done unless something changes.

compress routine trips until a return condition.

## 10.5 Silence

Nadia says something provocative.

User chooses:
> I don't answer yet. I keep looking at the pan and let the silence sit.

The user has supplied:
- withholding;
- attention/presentation;
- timing.

Time advances.

Nadia may interpret, ignore, fill the silence, leave it, or continue cooking.

The engine must not convert silence into a hidden wise move or punish it as nonengagement.

This is playable agency even though the user “did nothing” conversationally.

# 11. The minimal trace contract

A P1 trace should be inspectable as:

## Before
- exact world state relevant to the moment;
- actor current activity/agenda;
- player position/access/capabilities needed for the act;
- actor knowledge relevant to uptake;
- canonical hidden facts needed for causal resolution.

## Exposure
- what was perceptually available;
- what ambient frame surfaced;
- what the user acquired through ATTEND;
- what remained hidden.

## User
- raw input;
- channel parse;
- user-owned native payload;
- declared goal/outcome if any;
- explicit delegation bounds.

## Realization
- concrete operation(s);
- duration;
- feasibility;
- partial completion;
- interruptions;
- state changes.

## Actor
- actor-specific perceptual packet;
- noticed / partial / missed state;
- CAN-008 R2 uptake/appraisal/recruitment result at the permitted grain;
- expression/action realization.

## Occurrence
- exact admitted events;
- rejected/unrealized candidate actions;
- immediate effects;
- any warranted durable-owner request, if one exists.

## User-facing return
- only lawfully perceptible outcome;
- enough scene continuity to act again;
- no hidden-state explanation;
- control returned under an explicit CR rule.

The trace is not necessarily user-visible.

It is the audit object proving that worldly resistance and response came from state rather than improvisational prose.

# 12. What this adjudication says the UI must protect

The interface is not merely a presentation layer.

It is responsible for preserving whether the user actually exercised the faculty.

It must not:

- name what the user has not recognized;
- infer the user's argument;
- choose their joke;
- finish their explanation;
- decide their relational stance;
- invent their reason for objecting;
- skip past the moment at which new evidence should alter their decision;
- hide what physical/social act was actually realized;
- treat parser uncertainty as worldly resistance;
- expose hidden actor state as feedback.

Therefore:

> **THE UI MUST PRESERVE THE LOCUS OF HUMAN WORK.**

This is the strongest result of this adjudication.

Where Rawlsing's purpose depends on:
- attention;
- inference;
- judgment;
- tact;
- memory;
- interpretation;
- communication;
- timing;
- restraint;

the interface must be designed so those operations remain attributable to the actual user.

# 13. What may be compressed

The system may compress when doing so does not steal the user faculty or conceal a consequential fork.

Normally compressible:
- ordinary walking;
- routine carrying;
- repeated low-salience task steps;
- basic physical motions;
- mundane waiting;
- represented physical skill execution where no native cognitive decision is at stake.

Potentially noncompressible:
- choosing what to say;
- choosing why / how to explain;
- recognizing a contradiction;
- deciding whether to inspect;
- deciding whether to pry;
- choosing among socially meaningful alternatives;
- deciding whether to continue after new information;
- handling a boundary;
- interpreting conflicting evidence;
- allocating finite attention when alternatives remain live.

No action class is permanently “compressed” or “decompressed.”

Context decides whether a step is currently carrying meaningful user agency.

---

# 14. Completion criterion for the moment

The moment is complete when:

1. the current user-authorized operation has ended or reached a control-return boundary;
2. actual occurrence has been admitted;
3. all directly involved actor/world immediate consequences needed before the next decision have been resolved;
4. the user-facing surface accurately reflects what the user can now perceive/know;
5. control is back with the user before any new native-semantic contribution is invented.

A moment need not end with:
- dialogue;
- approval;
- revelation;
- reward;
- durable change.

It ends with a **new situated decision surface**.

Compact:

`INDEPENDENT SITUATION`
→ `LAWFUL EVIDENCE`
→ `USER-OWNED PAYLOAD / CHOICE`
→ `REALIZATION`
→ `INDEPENDENT RECEPTION / WORLD CONSEQUENCE`
→ `NEW LAWFUL SURFACE`
→ **CONTROL**.

This is not promoted as “the Rawlsing game loop.”

It is the smallest interaction cell in which the user can actually be causally present.

# 16. P1 readiness consequence

A bounded P1 prototype can now test something precise.

It should ask:

> **When a person is already pursuing their own ordinary life, does an interface that preserves the user's actual attention/thought/language/timing produce intelligible, consequential participation without assigning a mission or generating relational reward?**

The test must include:
- one quiet moment;
- one actor-busy moment;
- one broad task-entry moment;
- one user-owned linguistic/interpretive contribution;
- one refusal or noncooperation option;
- one low-significance ATTEND target;
- one interruption/control-return case;
- one lawful no/partial-response case.

The prototype should not be written as a story with a correct route.

Its purpose is to test whether the adjudicated causal cell is both:
1. lawful;
2. experientially legible enough for a real user faculty to matter.

# 17. Terminal adjudication

Provisional result:

> **THE SMALLEST LAWFUL PLAYABLE RAWLSING MOMENT IS NOT A CHAT TURN OR A QUEST STEP. IT IS A CAUSAL CELL IN WHICH THE USER RECEIVES ENOUGH LAWFUL EVIDENCE TO CONTRIBUTE SOMETHING THE MEDIUM CAN ACTUALLY MAKE THEM SUPPLY, THAT CONTRIBUTION IS REALIZED AGAINST AN INDEPENDENT WORLD/PERSON, AND CONTROL RETURNS BEFORE THE ENGINE WOULD HAVE TO SUPPLY THE NEXT MEANINGFUL HUMAN JUDGMENT.**

Supporting compact laws:

> **ACTUAL CONTRIBUTION IS MORALLY NEUTRAL.**

> **ENOUGH EVIDENCE FOR A CHOICE; NOT ENOUGH NARRATION TO MAKE THE CHOICE.**

> **IF THE MEDIUM CAN MAKE THE USER DO THE MEANINGFUL PART, THE ENGINE MUST NOT DO THAT PART FOR THEM.**

> **COMPRESS MECHANICS; RETURN BEFORE SUBSTITUTING FOR JUDGMENT.**

> **THE UI MUST PRESERVE THE LOCUS OF HUMAN WORK.**

> **OTHERNESS IS PRESERVED BY CAUSAL INDEPENDENCE, NOT BY SABOTAGING LEARNING.**

> **A COMPLETE INTERACTION CELL ENDS AT A NEW SITUATED DECISION SURFACE, NOT AT A REWARD.**

Disposition:

`PROVISIONAL_G1_ADJUDICATION_COMPLETE__READY_FOR_HOSTILE_REVIEW_AND_P1_TRACE_DESIGN__NO_CANONICAL_QUESTION_CLOSURE`
