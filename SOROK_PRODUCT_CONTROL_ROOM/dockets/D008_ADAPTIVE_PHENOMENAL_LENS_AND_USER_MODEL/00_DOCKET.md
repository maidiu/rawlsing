# D008 — Adaptive Phenomenal Lens and User-Model Noncollapse

Status: PROVISIONAL_LAWS_BANKED__OPEN_P0

## Question

How may Rawlsing learn from the user's demonstrated attention, recognition, valuation, vocabulary and recurring interpretive practice in order to render a finite phenomenal surface that increasingly bears the user's own way of seeing—without altering canonical reality, manufacturing confirmation, diagnosing an essential user identity, or contaminating Nadia's independent model of the user?

## Why this docket exists

Earlier Sorok already established:
- phenomenal-surface narration rather than omniscient narration;
- finite, non-curatorial scene sampling;
- attention history as valid behavioral evidence under provenance constraints;
- user-specific attention regimes;
- recognition-gated lexicalization;
- ATTENTION HISTORY MAY BE DESCRIBED; ATTENTION IDENTITY MUST NOT BE DECLARED.

The current design conversation forces a stronger composition:

> The narrator may become increasingly user-specific at the level of finite sampling, lexicalization and supportable characterization, while world truth and actor truth remain independent.

This docket owns that refinement and its reflexive/anti-enclosure consequences.
