# D008 — Adaptive Phenomenal Lens Constitution R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW__NEEDS_PROTOTYPE_AND_PRIVACY_ADJUDICATION
Date: 2026-10-06

## 0. Thesis

Rawlsing begins from a relatively spare, opinion-resistant phenomenal narrator.

Over longitudinal play, Rawlsing may use demonstrated behavioral evidence about:
- what the user attends to;
- what they repeatedly expand;
- what they spontaneously recognize;
- what distinctions they make easily;
- what vocabulary they themselves deploy;
- what kinds of surfaces they pursue or leave alone;
- what characterizations they repeatedly choose or contest;

to condition the finite phenomenal surface presented to that user.

This is not world generation from preference.
It is not Nadia adapting to the user's expectations.
It is not an essential personality diagnosis.

It is a bounded, revisable user-lens model that may shape:
1. which roughly comparable low-salience perceptible particulars enter finite narration;
2. how already-perceptible particulars are lexicalized;
3. which supportable characterizations/framings the narrator can increasingly use.

Compact:

CANONICAL WORLD
→ PERCEPTUAL AVAILABILITY
→ FINITE PHENOMENAL SELECTION
→ USER-LENS CONDITIONING
→ USER-SPECIFIC PHENOMENAL SURFACE.

## 1. Reality does not bend; rendering does

Hard law:

> USER MODEL MAY CONDITION PHENOMENAL SAMPLING AND LEXICALIZATION; IT MAY NOT CONDITION CANONICAL REALITY.

If a KitchenAid stand mixer exists, it exists independently of the user's history.

The user's history may affect whether narration lawfully progresses from:
- “a large mixer”;
- to “a KitchenAid stand mixer” after recognition is established;
- to some more user-consonant but still evidence-supported description.

The user-lens may not:
- create the mixer;
- choose its color because the user likes KitchenAid;
- make it emotionally important to Nadia;
- cause a hidden fact to exist because it fits the user's theory.

## 2. Beyond recognition: supportable phenomenal characterization

Earlier Sorok established recognition-gated lexicalization:
CANONICAL OBJECT IDENTITY != NARRATOR LABEL.

The current extension is that some descriptions contain valuation or construal, not merely classification.

Examples:
- “cluttered and messy”;
- “homey, with signs of life”;
- “a literary Xanadu”;
- “storied and well-loved”;
- “on its last legs”.

These cannot be promoted as objective world truth merely because the narrator can generate them.

They may become lawful user-facing renderings only where:
1. underlying visible evidence supports the characterization;
2. the user's prior behavior provides sufficient provenance for that mode of construal;
3. the wording does not smuggle in inaccessible motive/history;
4. the system retains the characterization as a user-lens rendering rather than canonical fact.

VISIBLE EVIDENCE
+ DEMONSTRATED USER CONSTRUAL HISTORY
→ perhaps USER-SPECIFIC CHARACTERIZATION.

But:

USER-SPECIFIC CHARACTERIZATION != OBJECTIVE PROPERTY.

## 3. Low-salience sampling may be user-conditioned

A finite narrator cannot mention every available detail.

Existing hard selectors remain primary:
- proximity;
- movement;
- contrast;
- loudness;
- obstruction;
- ordinary scene organization;
- explicit ATTEND target/depth;
- current load.

Within the remaining field of roughly comparable, lawfully perceptible low-salience particulars, the user-lens may alter sampling probabilities.

Example:
the same refrigerator may bear:
- a bill;
- a child's drawing;
- a Rome magnet;
- a grocery list;
- a religious image;
- a repair mark;
- a restaurant coupon.

Different users may initially receive different finite subsets where all are comparably available.

This does not make any subset more real.

Hard law:

> ADAPTIVE NARRATION MAY PROJECT WITH THE USER; IT MAY NOT CONFIRM THE USER.

## 4. No hidden-salience laundering

The user-lens may never become a route for leaking what secretly matters to Nadia or the plot.

Forbidden:
- surface an ordinary object because it secretly matters to Nadia;
- preferentially surface confirming poverty facts because the user suspects poverty;
- surface a religious object because it will later unlock a confession.

Selection must remain based on lawful perceptual availability plus user-lens prediction, not hidden narrative importance.

## 5. The lens can deepen faster than understanding

Critical law:

> MORE PERSONALIZED PERCEPTION != GREATER ADEQUACY TO THE OTHER.

And:

> THE LENS CAN DEEPEN FASTER THAN UNDERSTANDING.

A user may become increasingly sophisticated in one partial reading of Nadia.

They may know the “tail” or “trunk” of the elephant in ever finer detail.

Greater experience can produce:
- greater knowledge;
- more refined error;
- better local prediction;
- stronger but partial projection;
- an insight that becomes a new blind spot.

Rawlsing must not automatically correct this by arranging pedagogical counterevidence.

Independent reality and Nadia provide the counterpressure when causally warranted.

## 6. The productive contradiction

The adaptive narrator is deliberately in tension with Rawlsing's neighbor-making purpose.

The same mechanism that makes the world feel increasingly legible to the user can enclose the user more deeply inside their own historically produced field of attention.

Therefore the product should not hide the mechanism forever.

Strong requirement:

> THE USER SHOULD EVENTUALLY BECOME AWARE THAT THE PHENOMENAL SURFACE IS PARTLY THEIR OWN HISTORY RETURNED TO THEM.

This is not only an anti-manipulation disclosure.
It is part of Rawlsing's spiritual/pedagogical purpose.

The user should be confronted with the fact that:
- what feels obvious;
- what counts as “messy”;
- what looks expensive;
- what seems religious;
- what looks sad;
- what appears pretentious;
- what registers as evidence;
- what never initially registers

has a history.

## 7. Reflexive disclosure

Rawlsing may eventually tell the user, in non-diagnostic behavioral language, that its descriptions have been conditioned by observed play.

Example:

> Rawlsing's descriptions are not a neutral inventory. Over time the system may use patterns in what you attend to, recognize, ask about and repeatedly engage with to influence which lawfully perceptible details it surfaces and how it describes them. This does not change what exists, and it does not represent what Nadia thinks.

Longitudinal reflection may cite concrete behavior:

> Across recent scenes, you repeatedly expanded into books, signs of repair and religious objects before family photographs.

It may not conclude:

> You are an anxious status-sensitive intellectual.

Existing law remains:

ATTENTION HISTORY MAY BE DESCRIBED; ATTENTION IDENTITY MUST NOT BE DECLARED.

## 8. Lens perturbation / de-personalization operation

A promising product direction is a user-invoked operation such as:

> What might my usual attention be filtering out here?

The system may temporarily weaken/suppress the learned lens and resample from the same currently available phenomenal field.

It must not:
- inject counterevidence because it is “good for” the user;
- reveal hidden truth;
- highlight objectively important details;
- expose Nadia's private state.

It merely changes the finite sample/lexicalization policy.

This allows the user to experience the lens as a lens.

## 9. Nadia remains independent

Absolute membrane:

> RAWLSING MAY LEARN THE USER'S WAY OF SEEING. IT MUST NEVER MAKE THE OTHER PERSON BECOME WHAT THAT WAY OF SEEING EXPECTS TO FIND.

Nadia's perception and model of the user arise from:
- what she can perceive;
- her own history;
- her own attention;
- her own interpretations;
- actual shared events.

The system user-lens is not injected into Nadia.

The user's phenomenal rendering of Nadia's apartment may become increasingly “theirs.”

Nadia's relation to the apartment remains hers.

## 10. Three separate models

At minimum preserve:

SYSTEM MODEL OF USER-LENS / PRAXIS
!= USER'S MODEL OF NADIA
!= NADIA'S MODEL OF USER.

The system model may influence non-diegetic phenomenal rendering.
The user's model influences their actions only through what they actually do/say/attend.
Nadia's model influences Nadia through lawful actor-local causality.

No model is automatically true of its target.

## 11. Anti-enclosure requirements

Because adaptive narration can create a reinforcement loop, require:
- weak rather than sovereign influence on finite sampling;
- context-specific rather than global hypotheses;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- decay/revision/counterevidence;
- provenance of the behavior from which the lens was inferred;
- explicit ATTEND overrides;
- conspicuous reality overrides lens preference;
- no hidden salience;
- periodic/user-invoked lens inspection or perturbation;
- privacy/deletion boundaries.

Do not design a feed-ranking system disguised as perception.

## 12. Kernel implication

Rawlsing's purpose is not to grant neutral perception.

It is to challenge the user's invisible-to-them, identified-with, structurally pressured, ideologically filtered perceptual field by placing it in sustained encounter with an independently real other.

Candidate kernel-level formulation:

> There is no view from nowhere. Rawlsing makes the user's situated view increasingly perceptible to the user by confronting it with another person who is never exhausted by it.
