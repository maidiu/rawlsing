# D001 — Relational Ontology / V3 Boundary

Status: `OPEN__P0`

## Question

What is the minimum ontology necessary to represent a historically produced person in relation, how should each semantic be placed across PRE-V3, durable V3, CAN-008, PFF and V2, and what if anything remains genuinely ownerless?

## Why this is first

Storage, time, messaging, game mechanics and player modeling will all ossify the wrong design if the canonical objects are wrong.

## Current hypothesis

The actual Sorok delta is now sharper:

**inherit PFF-mediated personhood, but relationalize personhood all the way down.**

The durable person is not a context-free monad that relations merely activate. Their relatively stable organization is itself historically produced through relations, roles, absences, dependencies, recognitions, conflicts, institutions and material interdependence. Current relations then instantiate, reproduce, disconfirm or transform that organization.

At the same time, do **not** collapse the person into the dyad or assume a new monolithic “later dyadic runtime.”

The prior architecture already distinguishes:
- durable/from-birth and persistent V3 developmental ownership;
- CAN-008 person×event receivability / uptake / appraisal / recruitment / immediate response;
- PFF interaction-fast / session / user-opacity state;
- V2 expression.

D001 must place each relational semantic into that existing temporal-grain and ownership structure first.

Only semantics that remain genuinely ownerless after that crosswalk may justify a new runtime owner.

This boundary is provisional, but the prior split is inherited architecture, not a green-field suggestion.

## Required distinctions to adjudicate

- relationally constituted cross-relational disposition;
- relational prior;
- role-conditioned tendency;
- developmental residue;
- self-model;
- generalized other-model capacity;
- specific model-of-user;
- dyadic state;
- network state;
- health potential versus realized health trajectory;
- objective event;
- actor memory;
- actor interpretation;
- expression.

## Key tests

1. Can two identical V3 persons develop materially different relationships with different users without V3 mutation becoming arbitrary?
2. Can one person behave differently by relational role without contradiction being treated as drift?
3. Can dyadic history alter the person over time without erasing the distinction between person state and relationship state?
4. Can the ontology represent isolation as formative relation?
5. Can the LLM remain expressive rather than ontological?
6. Can durable V3 represent a person's relationally formed organization without turning every property into specific-dyad state?
7. Can the same durable person instantiate materially different organizations across role/counterpart without treating them as inconsistency?
8. Can relationship history recursively change the person without making the dyad the sole owner of personhood?

The N-M-L child-development work should be reviewed for developmental continuity, adult contraction, attachment/dependence and non-alienated relational development. It is not automatically product law.
