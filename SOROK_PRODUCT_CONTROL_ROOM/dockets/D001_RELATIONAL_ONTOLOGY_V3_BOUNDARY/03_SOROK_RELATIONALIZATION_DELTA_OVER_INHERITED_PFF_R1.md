## 0. Holding

PFF already established **mediated personhood**:

TOTAL CAUSAL PERSON
!= STANDING CONSCIOUS SELF
!= CURRENT RELATIONAL ACTIVATION
!= PRESENT CONSCIOUS FIELD
!= SELF-INTERPRETATION
!= INTENTION / DEFENSE / PRESENTATION
!= EXPRESSION.

Sorok's new proposal is not to replace that architecture.

Sorok proposes to **relationalize the ontology of the person across those layers**.

The new question is:

> What if the "person" whose psyche is being mediated is not fundamentally a monadic interior that later enters relationships, but a relatively persistent organization produced through histories of relation and continually re-instantiated, reproduced or transformed through new relations?

Short form:

PFF = HOW A PERSON BECOMES PRESENT / CONSCIOUS / EXPRESSIVE IN AN ENCOUNTER.

SOROK DELTA = WHAT THE PERSON IS WAS ALREADY RELATIONALLY PRODUCED, AND THE ENCOUNTER CONTINUES THAT PRODUCTION.

## 1. Core relationalization

Old-monadic reading to reject:

WORLD
→ PERSON WITH INTERNAL PSYCHE
→ RELATION ACTIVATES SOME PART OF PERSON
→ EXPRESSION.

Sorok candidate:

WORLD / RELATIONAL FIELD
→ HISTORIES OF DEPENDENCE, RECOGNITION, AUTHORITY, CARE, ABSENCE, CONFLICT, INSTITUTIONAL POSITION
→ PERSON-AS-SEDIMENTED-RELATIONAL-ORGANIZATION
→ RELATION-INDEXED PRIORS / EXPECTATIONS / DEFENSES / SELF-OTHER MODELS
→ SPECIFIC DYAD / ROLE / NETWORK CONTEXT
→ CURRENT PERSON×OTHER CONFIGURATION
→ PFF/CAN-008 MEDIATION
→ EXPRESSION / ACTION
→ RELATIONAL CONSEQUENCE
→ CHANGED DYAD / NETWORK / PERSON.

Relations therefore do not merely happen **to** a completed person.

They are among the processes through which the person became the person.

## 2. What changes in the inherited PFF four-band picture

### D — total causal personhood

Inherited PFF:
the total generated causal person, including material outside actor awareness.

Sorok relationalization:
D must not be interpreted as a pre-relational inner substance.

D includes the sedimented organization produced by:
- caregiver relations;
- sibling/kin relations;
- authority/subordination;
- friendship;
- dependence/care;
- classed and institutional relations;
- gendered/social-role relations;
- inclusion/exclusion;
- violence/protection;
- recognition/misrecognition;
- migration/displacement;
- community/religious relations;
- histories of absence, abandonment, isolation and inaccessible others;
- prior dyadic and network histories;
- repeated position within material/social fields.

The person's durable organization may be stable across situations, but its stability is historically relationally produced.

PERSON-STABLE != RELATION-FREE.

### C — standing conscious self

Inherited PFF:
the actor's relatively stable ordinary self-model / consciously endorsed identity / recognized worldview.

Sorok relationalization:
the standing self is also relationally constituted.

It may include:
- who I am in relation to family;
- who I believe others take me to be;
- what kinds of dependence I regard as legitimate;
- what I think I owe;
- what I expect care to look like;
- whose judgment matters;
- what roles feel natural, shameful, impossible or sacred;
- what forms of recognition have become necessary to self-coherence.

Therefore:

SELF-MODEL != PRIVATE DESCRIPTION OF A SELF THAT PRECEDES RELATION.

It is partly a historically stabilized position in a field of real and internalized others.

### B — current relational activation

Inherited PFF already made this explicitly relational:
actor × interlocutor × relationship history × event × situation × prior live state.

Sorok strengthens the interpretation.

B is not merely:
"which hidden trait gets activated by this other person?"

It may be:
"which historically formed organization of self-with-other becomes instantiated in this relation, under this role and this history?"

The same person's:
- need;
- courage;
- tenderness;
- hostility;
- play;
- dependency;
- directness;
- shame;
- authority;
- submission;
- curiosity;

may be differently organized across relations without any one relation exposing the one "true" personality.

### A — expression

Expression is already audience- and relation-bound in PFF/V2.

Sorok makes this continuous with the upstream relational ontology:

the outward act is the present edge of a relation-shaped person addressing a particular other under a particular relationship history and network position.

## 3. Relational priors are not ordinary traits

Candidate distinction:

TRAIT-LIKE SUMMARY:
"Nadia is conflict avoidant."

RELATIONAL PRIOR:
"Nadia expects direct disagreement with a person whose withdrawal matters to endanger belonging."

ROLE-CONDITIONED REALIZATION:
- may appease a desired/intimate other;
- may become direct with a secure sibling;
- may be contemptuous toward a subordinate where no abandonment is feared;
- may openly fight an institution she expects to be hostile anyway.

Thus:

CONFLICT AVOIDANCE != ONE GLOBAL RESPONSE PARAMETER.

A durable V3 person may carry relationally generalized priors, but their meaning is defined by expected relations.

---

## 4. Defenses become relationally intelligible

Inherited PFF correctly treated defense as connective causation rather than a late filter.

Sorok adds:

a defense should not be understood only as an intrapsychic mechanism protecting an isolated self from its own hidden content.

A defense may be a historically learned way of preserving a relation, position or anticipated relation.

Examples:
- silence preserves belonging;
- humor avoids indebtedness;
- contempt restores threatened hierarchy;
- over-helping secures indispensability;
- intellectualization preserves nondependence;
- refusal to ask protects against anticipated humiliation;
- magnanimity preserves a self-position as the one who does not need.

This does not mean every defense must be given a social explanation ad hoc.

It means the developmental origin and current function of defenses should be representable in relational terms where generated history warrants it.

---

## 5. Needs are relationally organized

Sorok should avoid treating all needs as free-floating scalar pressures.

Examples:
- reassurance is reassurance **from whom**?
- recognition by a child differs from recognition by a parent, peer, lover, rival or institution;
- autonomy may mean distance from one person and secure dependence on another;
- dependency may be degrading in one relation and life-giving in another;
- disclosure may feel safe with one counterpart and dangerous with another.

Therefore:

NEED DOMAIN
!= CURRENT NEED INTENSITY
!= TARGET / RELATIONAL OBJECT
!= ROLE
!= EXPECTED COST OF SEEKING
!= CURRENT EXPRESSION.

This complements, rather than replaces, existing CAN-010/PFF noncollapse.

---

## 6. Isolation is relational, not zero-relation

A person alone is not outside relations.

Isolation may encode relation to:
- absent loved ones;
- unavailable caregivers;
- expelled communities;
- feared observers;
- imagined audiences;
- dead people;
- institutions that exclude;
- persons one avoids;
- persons whose recognition is desired;
- roles one cannot occupy.

Thus:

ISOLATION != ABSENCE OF RELATIONAL FORMATION.

This matters both developmentally and in live product simulation.

A persona can remain relationally organized by another person who is not physically present and may never appear on-screen.

---

## 7. Internalized other / anticipated other

Sorok's relational ontology should be able to represent that current action may be shaped by relations whose counterpart is absent.

Examples:
- "What would my mother think?"
- behaving as though a former abusive partner's reaction still governs risk;
- anticipating community judgment;
- refusing dependence because an earlier caregiver made need humiliating;
- pursuing status for recognition from a dead parent.

This must not become an unconstrained psychoanalytic story generator.

The relevant internalized/anticipated other must have generated developmental parentage.

---

## 8. Self-knowledge is relational too

PFF established that actor self-knowledge is partial.

Sorok adds that what becomes knowable or thinkable about oneself may itself vary relationally.

A person may:
- recognize anger only with someone safe enough to survive it;
- experience dependence as love in one role and shame in another;
- discover playfulness with someone who does not punish silliness;
- become able to name fear only after another person repeatedly receives it without domination;
- misrecognize need because their inherited relational grammar makes need illegible.

Thus the wall:

WHAT PERSON IS
!= WHAT PERSON KNOWS ABOUT THEMSELF
!= WHAT PERSON EXPRESSES

remains.

But each side of the wall may have relational history and relational conditions of accessibility.

## 9. The dyad is real without consuming the persons

"Everything is relational" does NOT mean:

PERSON DOES NOT EXIST.

It means:
the person is not ontologically prior to all relation.

Preserve:

PERSON != DYAD

DYAD != NETWORK

CURRENT DYAD != TOTAL PERSON

RELATIONALLY CONSTITUTED PERSON != RELATIONSHIP DATABASE

Two people bring different histories into a new relation.

The new dyad develops properties not reducible to either person alone.

The dyad can then durably alter each person.

Network/third-party relations can alter the dyad.

This is recursive, not eliminativist.

PERSONAL CONTINUITY
= historically persistent organization across changing relations,
not

Some tendencies may generalize widely because similar relational expectations recur across many contexts.

Others remain highly role- or counterpart-specific.

The architecture therefore needs to distinguish:

CROSS-RELATIONAL INVARIANT / STRONG GENERALIZATION
from
RELATION-CLASS PRIOR
from
ROLE-CONDITIONED ORGANIZATION
from
SPECIFIC-DYAD HISTORY
from
CURRENT EVENT-TIME CONFIGURATION.

## 11. Mutual modeling becomes constitutive, not decorative

If persons are relationally organized, models of others are not optional chat flavor.

Relevant distinctions may include:
- model of this other;
- model of what this other expects from me;
- model of what this other thinks I am;
- model of the relationship;
- uncertainty in all of the above.

These can become causally important without becoming objectively true.

PFF perspective law remains binding.

MODEL OF OTHER != OTHER.

METAPERCEPTION != OTHER'S ACTUAL VIEW.

## 12. Network context enters person×event processing

A dyad does not float outside other relations.

The same user act may be mediated differently because:
- Nadia's child is present;
- her mother would disapprove;
- a spouse knows about the user;
- a coworker is watching;
- the user is being integrated into family life;
- resources/attention given to user compete with obligations elsewhere;
- intimacy with user threatens or supports another relationship.

Therefore candidate current relation:

RELATIONAL_RESPONSE =
f(
  relationally_constituted_person,
  counterpart_model,
  role,
  dyadic_history,
  relevant_network_context,
  current_event,
  current_condition,
  recruited_developmental_relations
).

This is a conceptual function, not adopted formula.

## 17. Product consequence

This relationalization changes what it means for the game to "learn Nadia."

The user is not uncovering a fixed hidden essence.

They are learning:
- histories that formed her;
- recurring relational expectations;
- how she becomes different with different people;
- what their own relationship brings into being;
- what the user is becoming to her;
- what she is becoming with the user;
- how that dyad alters other relations and is altered by them.

Therefore:

KNOWING THE PERSON
!= REVEALING THE STATIC CHARACTER SHEET.

A relationship can produce new truths without those truths having been prewritten as hidden lore.

## 18. Product consequence for intimacy

Earlier Sorok insight:
greater trust may expose new developmental material and make the relationship harder.

Relationalized version:

intimacy is not simply access to deeper hidden layers.

It is a changing relational condition under which:
- different expectations become reasonable to the actor;
- different needs become permitted;
- different defenses become necessary or unnecessary;
- different forms of play/protest/dependence become possible;
- new dyadic habits are created;
- old relational priors may be reproduced, disconfirmed, transformed or newly intensified.

Thus intimacy changes the **causal organization of the relation**, not merely how much hidden content the user can see.

## 20. Exact Sorok delta statement

The inherited Rawlsing/PFF insight was:

> The generated person has more causal depth than they consciously know, and what is active, conscious, intended and expressed must remain distinct.

The Sorok relationalization adds:

> The causal depth itself is historically composed through relations; self-knowledge is a relation-shaped achievement; current interaction recruits and reorganizes relation-formed personhood; and durable dyadic/network history can recursively transform the persons who enter it.

Compact:

MEDIATED PERSONHOOD
→ RELATIONALLY CONSTITUTED MEDIATED PERSONHOOD.

This is the actual novel D001 frontier.
