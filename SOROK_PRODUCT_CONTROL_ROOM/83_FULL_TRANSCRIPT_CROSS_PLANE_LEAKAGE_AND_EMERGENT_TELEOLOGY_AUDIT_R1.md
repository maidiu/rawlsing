## 1. Control-plane action != diegetic event

Transcript source:
the restart discussion around the shared-seed/irreversibility sequence.

The conversation explicitly observes that if the user opens Restart, receives the warning, and backs out, Nadia does not thereby know that the external human hovered over a software control.

The stronger general law is:

`EXTERNAL USER CONTROL-PLANE ACTION`
!=
`DIEGETIC PLAYER ACTION`
!=
`ACTOR-PERCEIVED EVENT`.

Examples of non-diegetic control-plane operations include:
- opening/canceling restart UI;
- settings;
- privacy/export controls;
- research consent;
- presentation/verbosity configuration;
- out-of-world help/recap surfaces.

They may have explicit product effects.

They do not become Nadia's perception, memory, relational evidence or world history merely because the system observed them.

Some interface verbs are intentionally diegetic:
- SAY;
- DO;
- embodied ATTEND.

Therefore the boundary is not "UI action = non-diegetic."

It is:
only explicitly governed diegetic realization crosses the membrane.

Actual abandonment/restart may terminate access to a continuity as a product operation; it still does not imply that the synthetic actor experienced the software gesture or knows the user's private control-plane deliberation.

Compact:

> **CONTROL-PLANE INTENT IS NOT ACTOR-LOCAL EVIDENCE.**

## 2. User contribution may transform the persona without making the user the missing key

Transcript source:
the Buddhist-rug / later book / self-rereading sequence, read together with agency-without-sovereignty and anti-explanatory-saturation.

The conversation rightly preserves a powerful possibility:

- the user notices a pattern;
- offers an interpretation;
- recommends a text or practice;
- Nadia encounters it;
- Nadia's own uptake may reorganize later self-understanding.

That is genuine user causal agency.

But combining:
- user interpretation as a present cause;
- late unresolved historical detail;
- resonance;
- developmental openness;

creates a new failure mode if the generator starts arranging Nadia's past so that the user's insight is retrospectively the thing she was always waiting for.

Reject:

`USER INSIGHT`
→ retro-resolve past so user was secretly correct/necessary
→ persona development completes around player.

Preserve:

`USER CONTRIBUTION EVENT`
→ Nadia-specific uptake under current state
→ possible durable consequence.

The user can become important.
The user can genuinely change Nadia.
The user can even articulate something Nadia later experiences as life-changing.

But:

> **REAL USER CAUSAL CONTRIBUTION != PREWRITTEN SAVIOR / MISSING-KEY TELEOLOGY.**

The engine should not optimize unresolved history, contradiction or causal slack to make the player uniquely therapeutic, revelatory or developmentally necessary.

This is the user-centered analogue of anti-explanatory saturation.

## 3. Behavioral research requires exposure provenance

Transcript source:
population-learning / scene-sampling discussion.

Artifact 59 already states the substance, but it was not promoted into live decision/risk/current-state control.

If Rawlsing studies user behavior, the research record must distinguish at minimum:
- what objectively existed;
- what was perceptually available;
- what was actually narrated/surfaced;
- what the user explicitly ATTENDed to;
- relevant presentation settings or experimental conditions;
- what the user then inferred/did.

Otherwise:

`USER DID NOT RESPOND TO X`

cannot be distinguished from:

`USER WAS NEVER EXPOSED TO X`.

This is especially important when the product itself has:
- user-specific attention weighting;
- progressive phenomenal coverage;
- adaptive presentation ergonomics.

Compact:

> **BEHAVIOR WITHOUT EXPOSURE PROVENANCE IS NOT INTERPRETABLE EVIDENCE.**

This applies to:
- optional gameplay research;
- alpha evaluation;
- attention-model validation;
- sociological analysis of interaction behavior.

## 4. Rawlsing users are not automatically a population sample

Transcript source:
the optional sociology/research discussion.

The archived conversation explicitly accepts:

> Rawlsing users will be a highly self-selected sample.

Therefore product research may validly support:
- within-user-population associations;
- model failure discovery;
- heterogeneity discovery;
- prior refinement;
- hypothesis generation;
- external study design.

It does not automatically support:
- population prevalence;
- universal cultural laws;
- broad demographic generalization.

Compact:

> **RAWLSING USER SAMPLE != GENERAL POPULATION.**

Internal generator improvement can still use carefully audited evidence without pretending convenience/product sampling is representative social science.
