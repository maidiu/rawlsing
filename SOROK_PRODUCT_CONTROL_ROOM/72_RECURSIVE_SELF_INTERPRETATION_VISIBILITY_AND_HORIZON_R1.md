## 0. Problem

A person does not merely accumulate facts and then possess one increasingly accurate autobiography.

Across time they:
- live through events without fully noticing their formative role;
- absorb impressions whose source later becomes inaccessible;
- form self-stories under one affective/social organization;
- later become alienated from those stories and see them as objects;
- replace, revise or partially preserve them;
- discover new language that articulates something they had already been living toward;
- adopt interpretations that may be only partly true yet become causally powerful;
- forget how contingent a once-new framework was after it becomes naturalized.

Rawlsing must model this process without:
- giving the engine one omniscient psychological explanation;
- making every persona a compulsive autobiographical analyst;
- deleting earlier self-myths when later ones appear;
- treating new interpretation as retroactive truth.

## 1. Structural-pressure connection

Recovered governing spirit:

`IDENTIFICATION`
→ `INTERNALIZATION`
→ `NATURALIZATION`
→ `INVISIBILITY`
→ `AGENT-OF`.

And:

`ALIENATION`
→ `EXTERNALIZATION`
→ `OBJECTIFICATION`
→ `VISIBILITY`
→ `AGENCY-AGAINST`.

These are dynamic relations, not truth states.

Therefore:
- what is invisible may be deeply causal;
- what becomes visible is not automatically correctly understood;
- alienation can free, distort, embitter or reroute;
- a new identification can itself later become naturalized/invisible.

This structure applies not only to institutions/ideologies but to **self-interpretive frameworks**.

## 2. Interpretive framework / self-myth as routing structure

Do not represent a durable self-story only as text.

Candidate V3 representation:

`INTERPRETIVE FRAMEWORK F`

with properties such as:
- origin / adoption period;
- domain scope;
- confidence;
- affective charge;
- identity investment;
- accessibility;
- salience-routing effects;
- memory-retrieval bias;
- evidence-selection bias;
- action affordances;
- contradiction tolerance;
- social/relational carriers;
- linked symbols/phrases/texts;
- relation to Absolute Horizon;
- visibility state;
- destabilizers / cracks;
- predecessor/successor framework refs.

Once sufficiently internalized, F may alter:
- what Nadia notices about herself;
- which memories are recruited;
- which events feel diagnostic;
- which acts feel coherent/incoherent with self;
- how she narrates the same past;
- what she seeks next.

Compact:

> **SELF-MYTH IS NOT ONLY CONTENT. ONCE ADOPTED, IT CAN BECOME ROUTING.**

## 3. Temporal self-interpretation stack — do not overwrite

Example:

### Age 23 — elated self-myth A
Nadia experiences a period as:
- breakthrough;
- liberation;
- becoming her real self;
- signs everywhere confirming a newly discovered identity.

A may be:
- partly accurate;
- grandiose;
- creatively coherence-building;
- selective.

### Age 29 — depressive/alienated counter-myth B
Nadia re-reads 23 as:
- naive;
- embarrassing;
- self-deceived;
- evidence of instability.

B may expose genuine contradictions A hid.

B may also:
- overcorrect;
- flatten joy;
- reinterpret neutral evidence negatively;
- produce its own invisibilities.

### Age 35 — softened synthesis C
Nadia becomes kinder toward 23 and less identified with 29's cuttingness.

She may now hold:
- some of A's sense that something real opened;
- some of B's critique of excess/grandiosity;
- compassion for both selves;
- unresolved uncertainty about what "really" happened.

Do not compute:

`A → overwritten by B → overwritten by C`.

Preserve:

`A`
→ later becomes object of `B`
→ both become objects/material for `C`.

Past self-myths remain historical causes even when no longer endorsed.

## 4. Recursive rereading is itself causal history

At each time T:

`CURRENT PERSON STATE`
+
`ACCESSIBLE PAST`
+
`CURRENT FRAMEWORKS`
+
`CURRENT RELATIONS / HORIZON / AFFECT`

→ `CURRENT READING OF PAST`.

That reading may change:
- memory accessibility;
- shame/pride;
- identity;
- future attention;
- relationship choices;
- practices;
- later framework adoption.

Therefore:

> **The past does not change, but the person's relation to the past becomes new history.**

## 5. Latent influence before explicit recognition

An event can matter without becoming an explicit self-story.

Example:
- Nadia is already seeking relief from control/anxiety;
- therefore she is willing to attend a meditation class;
- she does not become Buddhist;
- the teacher's affect, pacing, silence or atmosphere leaves low-level residues;
- she later gravitates toward environments/practices producing a similar felt organization;
- she cannot name the class as a formative cause and may barely remember it.

Years later:
another person recommends Buddhadasa Bhikkhu.

Reading may produce:
> "This is saying something I've been looking for my whole life."

Do not infer:
> Buddhism secretly caused Nadia's life.

Possible structure:

`PREEXISTING NEED / OPENNESS`
→ makes meditation-class exposure probable;

`EXPOSURE`
→ leaves bounded residues;

`OTHER EXPERIENCES`
→ independently reinforce / elaborate compatible organization;

`LATER TEXT / RELATION`
→ provides symbolic/conceptual articulation;

`RECOGNITION EVENT`
→ reorganizes memory and future practice.

The later book may be powerful partly because Nadia already has an **unarticulated receptivity / lived shape** capable of receiving it.

## 6. Recognition is neither pure discovery nor pure invention

A recognition event may combine:

### Discovery
The new framework captures real recurrent structure already present.

### Selection
It privileges some facts over others.

### Construction
It creates a new continuity story that did not previously exist.

### Omission
It leaves counter-evidence or unrelated material outside the frame.

### Transformation
Once adopted, it makes the person's future more like the story.

Therefore:

> **RECOGNITION CAN BE TRUE-ISH, CREATIVE, SELECTIVE, AND SELF-FULFILLING AT ONCE.**

Rawlsing should not force one classification.

## 7. Absolute Horizon connection

Recovered governing law:

Absolute Horizon is not a tenth stratum or religiosity score.

It is the evolving relation to what is experienced as ultimately binding:
- truth;
- justice;
- God;
- mercy;
- death;
- human worth;
- vocation;
- solidarity;
- nation;
- family;
- self-preservation;
- power;
- nihilism;
- etc.

It governs especially:
> **what may be sacrificed for what.**

A self-myth / interpretive framework may have several relations to Horizon:

### Finite framework
"I am a serious academic."

Important but relativizable.

### Horizon carrier
"Truth matters more than being liked."

A framework through which an ultimate claim is lived.

### Horizon colonization
"My wound / nation / career / family role IS the final measure of value."

Finite identity becomes ultimate.

### Horizon judgment
A criterion learned through one mediator later judges that mediator:
- justice learned through family judges family;
- God learned through church judges church;
- emancipation learned through movement judges movement.

### Horizon reordering
A new text/person/event can reorganize what Nadia treats as ultimately binding.

This is deeper than changing an opinion.

## 8. Visibility / invisibility of self-organization

A framework can move through:

`NOVEL / VISIBLE`
→ `IDENTIFIED-WITH`
→ `INTERNALIZED`
→ `NATURALIZED`
→ `INVISIBLE / "JUST REALITY"`.

Then contradiction/pressure/other relation can produce:

`CRACK`
→ `ALIENATION`
→ `EXTERNALIZATION`
→ `FRAMEWORK BECOMES VISIBLE`
→ possible:
  - critique;
  - grief;
  - resentment;
  - rejection;
  - transformation;
  - new identification.

This applies to:
- ideology;
- family scripts;
- gender expectations;
- class aspiration;
- religious belonging;
- self-myth;
- therapeutic language;
- political identity;
- "the kind of person I am."

## 9. Why users can genuinely help Nadia discover herself

A user may possess:
- different symbolic repertoire;
- different historical knowledge;
- a text Nadia has never encountered;
- a comparison Nadia has never made;
- an interpretation made possible by being outside one of Nadia's naturalized frameworks.

The user proposes a new frame.

Then Nadia's existing organization governs:
- receivability;
- resistance;
- recognition;
- affect;
- later reconsideration.

If the frame takes hold, it may make previously invisible patterns visible.

This does not mean:
> user discovered Nadia's secret canonical essence.

It means:
> relation introduced a new interpretive possibility that Nadia could metabolize because of who she had already become.

This is **constitutive relation** in a particularly clear form.

---

## 10. The user's interpretation may be wrong and still matter

A user's frame can:
- misread causes;
- overstate continuity;
- impose the user's favorite theory;
- nevertheless direct Nadia toward a text/practice/person that becomes genuinely transformative.

Therefore causal chains can include:

`BAD / PARTIAL INTERPRETATION`
→ `GOOD ENCOUNTER`
→ `REAL TRANSFORMATION`.

Rawlsing must not retroactively vindicate the original interpretation just because the outcome was beneficial.

## 11. Meaning-bearing encounters as articulation events

Some encounters do not originate a need/tension.

They **give language/form to something already partly organized**.

Candidate type:
`ARTICULATION EVENT`.

Examples:
- book;
- sermon;
- friend;
- therapist;
- political organizer;
- poem;
- religious practice;
- concept;
- lover's remark.

An articulation event may:
- increase visibility;
- bind dispersed impressions;
- make a latent orientation nameable;
- generate new practice;
- create retrospective coherence;
- reorganize Horizon.

This is not predestination.

The same encounter may do little for another person.

## 12. Conversion / deep adoption

"Conversion" need not be religious.

A framework becomes deep when it shifts from proposition to routing structure.

Possible indicators:
- repeatedly recruited across contexts;
- organizes memory;
- changes salience;
- changes sacrifice/tradeoff decisions;
- becomes identity-bearing;
- generates practice;
- survives challenge;
- supplies criteria for judging other frameworks.

But deep adoption still:
- does not prove the framework true;
- does not erase contradiction;
- can later become invisible/naturalized;
- can later become object of alienation.

Compact:

> **WE BECOME PARTLY CONSTITUTED BY THE INTERPRETATIONS THROUGH WHICH WE LEARN TO ORGANIZE OUR OWN HISTORY.**

> **Visibility is not truth, and invisibility is not absence of causal power.**

> **Interpretive frameworks can become routing structures.**

> **Recognition is neither pure discovery nor pure invention.**
