# Sorok Court 8C — Portal Translation Constitution R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_MAIN_ROUTE_MECHANIC__NOT_GOVERNED_ARCHITECTURE

Date: 2026-09-29

## 0. Core proposal

Collapse much of the homologous-peer autobiography/identity problem into a single explicit portal constitution:

> **The user may type or attempt anything. The portal does not grant authorship over reality. It translates or constrains the user's authored attempt through the canonical reality of the world, then shows the user what was actually said, done, perceived, or achieved.**

This extends an already-established Sorok law:

`USER AUTHORS ATTEMPT != USER AUTHORS REALIZED WORLD EVENT`.

to diegetic speech and identity translation:

`USER AUTHORS INTENDED UTTERANCE != USER GUARANTEES EXACT DIEGETIC UTTERANCE`.

---

## 1. Why this matters

The homologous-peer user is structurally local / developmentally foreign.

Therefore some literal self-references made by the external user may contradict the canonical portaled identity/world-position through which local actors encounter them.

Rather than forcing the user to roleplay a fake biography or solving every autobiographical mapping before play, the portal may mediate these conflicts at realization time.

The user remains psychologically themself.

The local world remains internally coherent.

---

## 2. Speech is an attempted action

Ordinary Sorok already distinguishes:

`ATTEMPTED DO`
→ feasibility / interruption / realization
→ `ACTUAL EVENT`.

Portal speech should use the same constitutional pattern:

`USER AUTHORED SAY`
→ language mediation
→ portal-identity / reality compatibility
→ optional translation
→ actual utterance event
→ actor perception.

Therefore exact typed wording is not automatically the objective utterance event.

---

## 3. Critical limit — the portal is NOT a truth filter

Users must remain able to:
- lie;
- bluff;
- exaggerate;
- conceal;
- misremember;
- make false claims;
- manipulate;
- joke;
- speak hypothetically.

Example:

If the canonical local player does not own a car and the user says:
> I own a car.

the portal should ordinarily allow the false statement to be heard as a statement.

It must NOT auto-correct it to:
> I don't own a car.

The listener may believe or disbelieve it through ordinary actor-local processing.

Therefore portal translation applies only to **constitutively translated coordinates / identity facts governed by the portal**, not to every proposition that conflicts with world truth.

`PORTAL TRANSLATION != TRUTH ENFORCEMENT`.

---

## 4. Portal-governed coordinate classes

Potential classes that may be translated under homologous-peer mode:
- hometown / region;
- nationality / local belonging where the mode establishes a homologue;
- institution names/status coordinates;
- currency / purchasing-power references;
- credential/status references;
- occupation-status references;
- local legal-status coordinates;
- selected embodiment/social-legibility coordinates;
- externally impossible geographic references when ordinary local identity would otherwise break.

Each coordinate class requires explicit portal authority.

Do not permit generic LLM paraphrase to decide what gets translated.

---

## 5. Autobiographical invariants

Some user claims should ordinarily pass through without coordinate substitution when the user chooses to state them:
- a person mattered to me;
- my parent died;
- I was afraid of X;
- I learned a particular skill;
- I used to believe Y;
- I remember a conflict;
- I love this kind of music;
- I studied a subject;
- I experienced a loss.

These are not automatically objective world truth merely because the user says them; they remain user testimony unless separately admitted.

But the portal need not rewrite their substantive meaning simply to make the local shell coherent.

---

## 6. Realization feedback

If the portal materially changes an authored utterance/action, the user should be shown the realization.

Candidate surface:

User authors:
> I grew up in South Carolina.

Portal realizes a governed homologous coordinate, for example:
> You hear yourself give the local hometown/region bound to your portal identity.

or, once the mapping is established:
> You hear yourself say, 'I grew up in [canonical homologous place].'

The exact UI remains open.

Core law:

`MATERIAL TRANSLATION MUST NOT BE SILENT TO THE USER`.

The player should be able to learn the parameters of their own portaled existence.

---

## 7. Persistent translation bindings

Portal translation cannot improvise a new analogue every time.

If a coordinate is translated, the result must become or reference a stable canonical binding.

Candidate concept:

`PORTAL_TRANSLATION_BINDING {`
- external/reference semantic class;
- canonical diegetic ref;
- translation class;
- provenance;
- confidence / noncomparability state;
- effective time;
- version;
`}`.

Example:
a hometown mapping established once must remain consistent with later school/family/geography references unless explicitly revised by governed law.

Do not let the renderer invent these bindings turn-by-turn.

---

## 8. Lazy mapping without lazy ontology

Not every autobiographical coordinate needs to be generated before play.

A binding may be resolved only when a fact first becomes causally relevant.

But:

`LAZY RESOLUTION != NARRATIVE IMPROVISATION`.

Resolution must use the same homologous field/portal authority and become canonical once admitted.

---

## 9. Non-translatable facts

Some user facts may have no defensible local homologue.

The portal must support:

`NO_LAWFUL_HOMOLOGY`.

Possible outcomes by class:
- preserve the foreign fact;
- bracket it from ordinary diegetic speech;
- communicate only its relevant semantic content;
- require explicit user choice;
- expose a portal mismatch to the user.

Never invent an equivalence merely to keep conversation smooth.

---

## 10. Pre-entry briefing is constitution, not tutorial lore

The player should be explicitly told the operating law before entry.

Essential content:

1. **You remain you.** Your judgments, memories and choices are yours.
2. **You do not control the world by describing it.** You control attempts.
3. **The portal gives you a locally coherent identity, embodiment and material position.** Some external coordinates are translated.
4. **You may say or try anything.** Lies, resistance, mistakes and rule-breaking remain possible.
5. **If the portal or world changes what can actually happen or be said, you will be shown the realized result.**
6. **You will do better by learning the reality you have entered.** The system does not guarantee that your inherited assumptions will work here.

This briefing should invite experimentation rather than demand conformity.

---

## 11. Primary gameplay theorem

Candidate user-facing constitutional idea:

> **You can attempt anything. You cannot dictate what reality makes of the attempt.**

And specifically for homologous peer:

> **You are still yourself, but this world determines what that self can materially and socially be here.**

---

## 12. Learnable portal identity

The user need not receive a 40-field character sheet.

They should know enough to avoid absurd confusion:
- immediate dwelling;
- broad livelihood/time constraints;
- obvious local relations/status;
- core resource limits;
- how others generally perceive their social position.

Other translated facts may be discovered when relevant.

This preserves mystery without making the player unable to answer ordinary questions.

---

## 13. Portal-law violations become informative

If the user attempts something inconsistent with the portal, the failure/translation itself teaches them the field.

Examples:
- attempting to spend beyond local means;
- invoking credentials that do not exist here;
- assuming imported institutional access;
- using a social identity coordinate that the portal homologized differently;
- attempting a physically impossible act;
- speaking through a pragmatic register the interface cannot make locally natural without changing meaning.

Thus the system can be permissive at the input layer while strict at the realization layer.

---

## 14. No arbitrary punishment

The rule is not:
> break portal norms and get punished.

It is:
> reality remains causal.

Some apparent violations may work.
Some local norms may be wrong.
Some imported habits may be useful.
Some attempts may change the local field.

Better outcomes come from more accurate models of reality, not from obedience to hidden developer rules.

---

## 15. Relationship to existing Sorok action law

Portal translation should reuse the same conceptual stack as SAY / DO / ATTEND:

`AUTHORED ATTEMPT`
→ `PORTAL / WORLD CONSTRAINT`
→ `REALIZED EVENT`
→ `ACTOR PERCEPTION`
→ `RESPONSE / CONSEQUENCE`.

This unifies:
- physical feasibility;
- resource constraints;
- language mediation;
- autobiographical coordinate translation;
- social identity translation;

under one general constitutional principle.

---

## 16. Hard cases

1. 'I grew up in South Carolina.'
2. 'I went to the University of South Carolina.'
3. 'My mother died when I was twenty.'
4. 'I make $120,000 a year.'
5. 'I'm a U.S. citizen.'
6. 'I'm a plumber.'
7. 'I own a car.'
8. 'My sister lives in Atlanta.'
9. 'Back during the Iraq War...' while living in a place directly affected by it.
10. An intentional lie about any of the above.

The constitution must distinguish portal-coordinate translation from ordinary deception and testimony.

---

## 17. Current Court 8C verdict

This mechanism appears capable of resolving much of the autobiography problem **without generating a fake user biography**.

The player may remain maximally free at the input layer while the portal preserves causal coherence at the realization layer.

Main unresolved problems:
- exact coordinate classes;
- exact translation-binding authority;
- non-translatable facts;
- what local actors initially know;
- UI for realized-utterance feedback;
- ensuring translation does not become hidden moral/cultural correction.
