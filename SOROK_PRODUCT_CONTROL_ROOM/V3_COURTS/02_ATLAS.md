## Meaning of indicators
- **Historic original mechanism**: underlying source inspected by TD-R05, but no fresh local code execution claimed by the foundation author.
- **Coverage**: not proven by a same-named old field, historical passed test, isolated S50 fixture or generic CAN008 body. Requires precise operator, inputs, outputs and effect on actual consumer.
- **Needed target**: needs-first scientific and product functionality, not necessarily implemented as one class or new store.
- **Dependency**: refer to [sequencer](03_DEPENDENCY_SEQUENCER.md) and machine graph; adoption versus concurrent investigation.
- **Progression**: only increment an axis on immutable evidence plus independent review, preserve REDs, source/permissions and unknowns.

Original code audit is a reservoir. Do not infer this list alone covers the full scientific ontology. Future courts may split/merge N items with evidence and reciprocal versioning, without silently rewriting historical M facts.
