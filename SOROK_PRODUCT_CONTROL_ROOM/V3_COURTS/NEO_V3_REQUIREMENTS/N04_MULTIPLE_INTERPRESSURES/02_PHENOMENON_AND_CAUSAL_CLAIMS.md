# T03 / N04 — Phenomenon, theory and causal alternatives

## What interpressure must mean

One person's response to one event is *jointly conditioned* by historically acquired and presently accessible operations, strivings, relationships, bodily state, alternative routes and another's independently acting material position. Joint conditioning is **not addition**: one operation can alter the meaning of the event that recruits a second; one can make another executable by supplying a skill/tool/help; inhibit outward expression while leaving its presence intact; or redirect a concrete act into a different activity. Reciprocal recruitment can end in sustained contradiction, underdetermination or valid-empty, not necessarily resolution.

**Leontiev**, *Actividade, Consciência e Personalidade* (provided Portuguese PDF pp.18,20,25,27–28,32): socially formed relationships between activities, objects of need and hierarchies of motives shape personal meaning; the *same* action can realize different activities and have contradictory emotional valences. The page-27 discussion explicitly warns that succeeding in an action may be a defeat for a salient motive. Scientific warrant: motives/activity and meaning cannot be inferred from surface action alone. **Provisional extrapolation**: typed multi-activity relation projections for CAN008, not Leontiev's own computer algorithm.

**Lahire**, *The Plural Actor* (provided English PDF pp.12–16,76–79,101): the dispositional actor is conditioned by plural socializations; context selects, triggers, suspends or inhibits acquired schemes, including ones that resurface after apparent abandonment. **Critically, Lahire p.13 expressly rejects universal mandatory plurality.** A coherent/low-contradiction historical actor must remain permitted. Warrant: contextual recruitment and inhibited persistence; **not** a scalar context × trait scoring law.

**Dreier**, *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life* (provided PDF pp.49–56,198): abilities are powers developed in social practice; available participation, positions, locations and a person's movement among contexts must be represented together; capacity cannot be presumed a fully internal trait. Warrant: an actor can retain an ability but become unable to exercise it because the social route vanished. Proposed implication: distinguish CAN011 operation from world/relationship enablement and actor-facing access, plus capture material and social support for performance.

**Luria**, *Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations* (provided PDF pp.29,35,67–68): formalized language and socialized activity can reorganize classification and self-regulation; the empirical fieldwork explicitly changed tasks to ones participants found intelligible and meaningful (p.35). Warrant: content and form of cognitive operation can change under participation/instruction; **not** a fixed hierarchy in which abstract answers make people universally more intelligent. His historical Soviet Central Asian setting and research categories require contextual critique; do not port the study's test types as normative actor ranks.

**Sève**, *Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality* (provided PDF pp.319–321,364–365): acquisition and reproduction of capacities consume biographical time, in tension with socially patterned use-time and relations. This is an explanatory emphasis, not a measurement-ready universal temporal coefficient. **Archer**, *Structure, Agency and the Internal Conversation* (provided PDF pp.84,112): reflexive deliberation among plural concerns/projects may reorganize prioritization; neither internal conversation nor articulate awareness can be made a compulsory first-person prerequisite for operative regulation.

## Competing scientific accounts / internal contradictions

- **A — Conditional repertoire (Lahire/Dreier)**: several context-scoped acquired operations coexist; eligible situation/support activates a subset; no weight averaging. Strong for plural selves and activity across domains; risks treating interrelations as mere lookup tables.
- **B — Activity/motive system (Leontiev/Sève)**: the meaning of a single action depends on its place within *several activities* and concrete object-relations; goal achievement can oppose higher-level motive. Strong for interpressure/cost, but requires independently evidenced activity meanings.
- **C — Functional reorganization (Luria)**: acquisition of signs/operations alters how other operations coordinate; not just adding a new response option. Strong for true wiring change; risks schooling/cognition reductionism or unjustified general transfer.
- **D — Reflective revaluation (Archer)**: inner deliberation actively revises concerns and projects; useful for older persons and narratable choices, but false as universal explanation of infant or pre-reflective recruitment.
- **E — Legacy hydraulic model (historical M21)**: charge/provenance/conductance gives replayable transaction structure and inertia. Mechanically robust but treating conserved pressure as literally conserved psychological substance is unwarranted and can coerce conflict into one path.

**Adversarial alternatives:** The apparent inhibition might instead be poverty/no transport; a reported 'new motive' might be social-desirability narration; a confidence boost might be improved opportunity not acquired practice; mixed-affect after a successful action might be exhaustion not incompatible motives. Every positive mechanism must preserve those rival explanations as separate typed hypotheses and causal tests.

**Negative case:** a child sees an adult repeatedly repair an object, without receiving instruction, participating in any repair or acquiring means; the child need not acquire the technique or spontaneously recruit it. **Neutral case:** a quiet, ordinary routine produces no newly distinguishable psychological operation; it may maintain established habit under a separately warranted process, but the observation must not force change.
