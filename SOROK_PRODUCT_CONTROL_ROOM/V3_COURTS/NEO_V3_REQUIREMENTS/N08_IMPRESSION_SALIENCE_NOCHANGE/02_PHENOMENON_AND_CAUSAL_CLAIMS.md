# T02 / N08 — Degrees of experience and lawful nondevelopment

**Status: PROPOSED causal taxonomy; no salience formula, scalar, "trauma tag", age coefficient or native mutation authority.**

## Four different kinds of information
1. **Encounter descriptors** (about world): ordinary continuity, repeated instance, novelty, expectation-congruent/incongruent, duration, actual intensity/danger, people/actions, available supports, material consequences. These alone have no privileged mental meaning.
2. **Actor reception** (history/context dependent): accessible sensory cue, noticed mismatch, affective activation, bodily safety response, practical goal interruption, recognized or unrecognized dependency, trust/caution/effort, degree of ambiguity, appraisal when developmentally supported. A child can have intense fear without remembering autobiographically; an adult can remember an event without durable belief change.
3. **Transient consequences**: crying, startle, inhibition/approach, help-seeking, attentional shift, physiological activation, short-term avoidance, exploratory action, felt relief, exhaustion. Preserve even when later persistent NO_CHANGE.
4. **Admissible enduring consequences** (only with rule): context-specific expectation qualification, reinforced or weakened enacted anticipation, new rival anticipation, new/modified practice, protective mediator, unresolved tension, altered dependency or support relation, possible major reorganization. No transition is an evidentially meaningful output. Episodic memory/retelling is a **separate** and fallible process.

Never merge any of the four into an event "salience" number. A single event may have several concurrent outcomes with distinct time courses. Salience can mean attention, emotional energy, retrieval accessibility, normative significance or practical reorganization: these are not interchangeable. Epistemic rupture is a minority possibility, not the default after contradiction.

## Causal categories, without automatic effects
| Phenomenon | What qualifies descriptively | Reception candidates | Durable inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary continuity | Routine proceeds as available | Little surprise, practical fluency | Often lawful NO_CHANGE; aggregate pattern may later warrant scoped baseline |
| Repeated confirmation | Multiple qualifying experiences match a scoped anticipation | Predictability/security/ease, sometimes irritation | May support conditional consolidation; no auto increment |
| Salient confirmation | Sought support reliably arrives at unusually consequential moment | Relief, increased reliance, new differentiation | Could become referential exemplar without universalized security |
| Novelty | Unexpected feature/person/condition | Curiosity, caution, surprise, none | Usually no automatic change; future tests possible |
| Challenge | Valuable aim demands new action/skill | Effort, excitement, discouragement | Acquisition only with enacted practice and warrant |
| Complication | Exception or ambiguity in current understanding | Confusion, attention, tension | Qualification/rival hypothesis, sometimes no change |
| Threat | Valued dependency/capacity objectively or perceived endangered | Fear/vigilance/urgent action, possibly absent | Specific sensitivity or adaptive reorganization possible, never scene-imputed |
| Disorientation | Usual practical means/source/context inaccessible | Search, hesitation, confusion | May lead to remapping, else recover/no change |
| Intensity | High affect/physiological activation or serious external stakes | Strong momentary response | Neither sufficient nor necessary for permanence |
| Repair | Independent coactor's later concrete restorative act | Relief/trust/anger/disbelief coexist | Partial restoration, qualification or no durable effect |
| Negative response | Actor resists, rejects, flees, misunderstands, or support fails | Bodily/practical negative outcome | Can remain transient, accumulate or persist with rule |
| Lawful no-change | No qualified state transition despite an admitted real encounter | May still have strong reaction | Stable records preserved; explanatory receipt recorded |

## Why intensity need not become life-long change
- It may be a context-bounded exception with intact differentiable baseline.
- An established relation plus actual support may transform immediate fear into an anticipable repair sequence.
- Later evidence may countercondition/fail to reproduce the expected danger without erasing the prior episode.
- Experience may not be re-encountered and its cue/context may not generalize.
- Attention and memory accessibility are not identical with changed habit/action.
- Different rival expectations may remain simultaneously in context, with no winner-take-all replacement.
- An actor may have actual options, resources or collective support to absorb adverse demands.
Each is a hypothesis to be tested against specific case evidence, not an automatic protective constant.

## Why a single event nevertheless can reorient
One material catastrophe, consequential betrayal, serious injury, rescue, or radical opportunity can remove/institute an indispensable condition for living and action. Baseline persistence may yield to actual dependency disruption, forced change in activity, new sustained objective constraints, blocked alternatives or newly reliable support. A major change can occur at once or after extended practical consequences/retrospective mediation. There is no universal "need N repetitions" law and no one-time "shatter" law.

## Additional timing distinctions
**Clock time** is not automatically developmental time. An interval of repeated practice and social reproduction can matter even if the scheduler emits no salient scene. The user's 3–5-event compact batch must transmit exact intervening activity/other-actor/world conditions: the state at encounter B depends on what happened at A and between. First-half of next LLM call may organize *retrospective fallible account*; this output cannot supply missing lived actions or rewrite earlier causal facts.

**Memory distinctions**: reactivation and generalization can be context dependent (Hayne & Rovee-Collier 1995; Amabile & Rovee-Collier 1991), and early memory trajectories differ from later autobiographical recall (Bauer 2015 doi:10.1037/a0038939; Bauer/Larkina doi:10.1080/09658211.2013.854806). Lack of later narrative retrieval does not establish absence of early learning; neither does a powerful retrospective narrative prove original autobiographical storage.

## Scientific competitors and limitations
Association/context-dependent retrieval, attachment/co-regulation, social referencing, dynamic systems, activity theory, plural habitus, appraisal/stress and autobiographical construction explain different grains. None supplies a universal computational salience law; their evidence (often laboratory, cultural-period or dyad-specific) must not be back-projected to every synthetic life. Positive modeling studies (PMID 23795591, PMID 28569540), Tronick (doi:10.1002/anzf.1274), Rutter (doi:10.1111/j.1939-0025.1987.tb03541.x), Lahire, Dreier and Leontiev constrain alternatives but do not mandate simulated coefficients. Neurological discussion likewise supplies no determinate actor-level mutation.
