## 1. The phenomenon that must exist before psychic updating
The person lives through a temporally distributed organization of food, rest, paid/unpaid work, transit, care, public services, school, play, discretionary attention, friends, conflict, collective activity and institutional obligation. Events are not replacements for this organization. The same headline event can have radically different meaning depending on the preceding ordinary interval and what was practically possible.

**Activity** = situated, temporally extended doing-in-a-practice, with practical object, motive hypotheses, means, others, division of labor, costs and actual outcomes. **Act** = event within activity; **operation** = condition-sensitive way of achieving an act; **practice** = socially reproduced set of participant relations/resources; **participation** = one's partial and located contribution in that practice. **Life-course period** = a change in materially available roles, capacities and commitments, not a universal psychological coefficient multiplying by age. **Interstitial life** = events, continuities and concrete time between selected narrative highlights. **Routine** = repeated, grounded practical sequence that can confirm or trouble expectations without dramatic salience.

**Social reproduction** = labor and institutional arrangements sustaining life daily and across generations. In a capitalism-specific account this includes the contradiction that households must provide care while wage/time discipline and unequal public provision may withdraw caregivers' availability. The historically variable unit is *who performs what care for whom and with which resources, constraints and collective options*, not an abstract "work-life balance score."

## 2. Source court and its limits
- **Sève, _Man in Marxist Theory_, IV.I, pp. 331–35:** developed personality is not inferable from ideal schedule or autobiographical declaration; "use-time" is a real system of temporal relations between *objective categories of activity*. Read as requirement to distinguish actual time-occupancy and aspiration. Not a claim that clock time alone determines thought.
- **Leontiev, _Activity, Consciousness and Personality_, activity/personality section:** identical operations can be subordinate to different motives; an activity system, not function or trait inventory, is central. His treatment of multiple motive relations warns against single-activity single-personality lookup.
- **Dreier, _Psychotherapy in Everyday Life_, ch. 2, pp. 28–38 and ch. 5:** persons have partial, situated participation across multiple places. The meanings and practicability of proposed changes can differ by place, coparticipant and available support.
- **Elder, _Children of the Great Depression_, ch. 2 and chapters "Children in the Household Economy", "Family Relations":** deprivation reorganizes labor allocations, family participation and power through mediated adaptations; historical timing/cohort and family-level adaptation matter. The Oakland cohort is not an everywhere-general causal coefficient.
- **Bhattacharya (ed.), _Social Reproduction Theory_ (2017), introduction and Nancy Fraser's "Crisis of Care?" plus Susan Ferguson's "Children, Childhood and Capitalism":** distinguishes producing capital's commodities from the undervalued labor sustaining people. Read 2017 text directly from attached local file, not as an empirically calibrated person-transition function.
- **Lahire, _The Plural Actor_, "Wellsprings of Action", pp. 50–62:** activation/inhibition is context-dependent and non-transfer is lawful; situated contexts draw differently on retained repertoires. A change in household, school or job can release or suppress practice without rewriting a universal disposition.

These are theoretical and empirical traditions with differences: Leontiev foregrounds object-mediated activity; Sève socially consequential acts/use-time; Dreier participation across sites; Elder cohort/family mediation; Fraser reproduction crisis; Lahire contextual multiplicity. Their overlap licenses a requirement for temporally and relationally situated operations, **not** an agreed universal law for psyche change.

## 3. Required causal layers, no invented time multiplier
1. **World/institutional reality:** wages, shifts, transport, household composition, school calendars, housing, mobility constraints, service availability, legal/custody boundaries and collective supports come from permitted qualified sources or authored actor-specific facts; distinguish known/unknown.
2. **Opportunity/occupancy:** derive who could be where, who is responsible and actually present, which time is spent on care, sleep, work, waiting, practice and transit; support overlaps and simultaneous care/work, not time as a single undifferentiated bucket.
3. **Actual participation:** resolve acts of child and coparticipants, including refusals, interruptions, substitutions and ordinary no-event repetition. Impossible acts must not be replayed because narratively attractive.
4. **Exposure/reception:** present reachable cues to that actor given sensory, language, location, attention, age-conditional *acquired abilities* and involvement; direct psychological effects require downstream N03/N04/N08 authority. Never assume awareness of employer or class structure at infancy.
5. **Persistence/later reworking:** repeat practices, capacities, relation-specific expectations, institutional access, changed time uses and discontinuities are potential durable objects; unregistered intervals may yield NO_CHANGE. Later reflection uses N09/N10 only when social concepts have become available.

**Multiple clocks:** instant encounter, repeated daily interval, long institutional season, maturation with acquired practices, historical cohort/life course. Clock order is a constraint on causation, not a predetermined age-to-belief mapping. The user's settled 3–5 conditional-event batch requires sequential grounding of intervening activity without intermediate LLM calls; it does **not** authorize silently synthesizing thousands of unseen memory-imprinting microevents.
