# T02 / N02 — Phenomenon and competing causal accounts

**Status: SCIENTIFIC_CONTRACT_PROPOSAL, not issued native law.** Historical reference: pinned V3 Courts charter, N02 card, TD-R05; exact inherited CAN011 schema verified as documentary text. Source material: Leontiev, Activity, Consciousness, Personality (1978, project Portuguese translation, Introduction); Lahire, The Plural Actor (English preface xi–xvi, scenes on conditional dispositions); Dreier, Psychotherapy in Everyday Life (Introduction 1–2 and chapters 2, 5–11); empirical sources below. The conceptual synthesis is T02's interpretation, NOT their formal scientific endorsement of Rawlsing.

## Constitutive object
A **thick baseline** is not one unitary belief, internal confidence number, emotional mood or summary of memorable incidents. It is a historically formed *set of condition-sensitive practical anticipations* about what can be done, who and what can be depended upon, how needs tend to be met, what costs or delays are incurred, and what acts may be effective in particular relationships, places and times. Some anticipation is bodily/procedural, some sensory-affective, some learned practice, some represented linguistically and some reflected upon later. The temporal record includes patterns of actual care, missed care, agency, resource constraints and coactor response — not merely actor recollection.

A representative record would separate: subject actor; need/motive/object; expected other/action/resource; scope (people, place, activity, need, time/condition); practical expectation; evidence trace (ordinary intervals, repeated episodes, exceptions, repair); support/dependency; rival expectations; learnability/accessible interpretation; and provenance. These are **candidate semantic slots**, not canonically registered fields or quantitative parameters.

An act of care can be reliably recurrent without being consciously enumerated or later recalled as a scene. The child's practical organization (settling when footsteps approach, looking to a caregiver before exploring, anticipating feeding/rest, expecting embrace after distress) may be conditioned by many ordinary exchanges. One extraordinary episode can remain retrievable without becoming a generally applicable rule. Conversely, one intervention may reorganize action possibilities when it closes a genuinely vital dependency, reveals a previously unseen danger, or institutes a new relation; no rule of "many small vs one large" is settled in advance.

## Historical thickness is a relation, not merely frequency
At least seven non-equivalent elements can give a scoped anticipation continuity: (1) recurrent world regularity; (2) responsive contingent care, where another actor reliably answers need; (3) cross-time and cross-context confirmation; (4) practical investments dependent on the anticipation; (5) alternative supports and competing expectation records; (6) learned repair after mismatch; (7) structurally constrained routines whose necessity persists independently of explicit belief. Relative stability is not psychological invulnerability. An adult can be loving but repeatedly absent because labor extraction/migration/shifts prevent availability; the child may simultaneously expect affection on return and experience distress or practical insufficiency during absence. Neither a single "loving parent" label nor a single "neglect" label reconstructs this relation.

**Objective:** affection, income/housing/work hours, time present, actual nourishment, response delay, who answers distress, safe conditions and institutional support. **Reception:** whether the child senses safety, affection, abandonment, uncertainty, adequate relief, bodily arousal, or expects particular people to return. **Transition:** whether any of those relations actually alter future enacted behavior/expectation conditional on admissible evidence. Lack of verbal report never licenses imputing a belief. Objective care and felt love can diverge; security for one need need not imply sufficiency for every need.

## Mechanistic competitors — none independently universal
A. **Association/expectancy learning:** repeated contingencies provide conditional predictive structure. Strong for nonverbal specificity, not adequate by itself for motive, institutional conditions or conflict of social meanings.
B. **Attachment and co-regulation:** repeated responsiveness/reunion can make a particular caregiver an effective security resource; the relationship is dyadic and mismatch/repair is normal. It is not a deterministic binary "secure person" identity. A 2024 meta-analysis finds modest pooled sensitivity–security association (r=.25), with marked method heterogeneity, not 1:1 prediction (doi:10.1037/bul0000433).
C. **Activity theory:** needs are transformed through historically developing activities and their means/objects (Leontiev). Primary significance is what actual living, acting, caring and learning reorganize, not a stimulus deposited as a trait.
D. **Plural dispositional sociology:** heterogeneous socialization permits simultaneous dispositions with different scopes; contexts activate, inhibit and qualify them (Lahire). No forced global average.
E. **Situated participation:** persons traverse family, labor, school and care sites; meaning and change are made across these sites (Dreier). Source-free family "warmth" cannot substitute for this actual web.
F. **Dynamical stabilization/hysteresis:** recurrent coupling can support resilient trajectories and occasionally nonlinear reorganization, but a "basin" is a model metaphor until operational measures and source warrant exist.
G. **Explicit cognitive prediction/updating:** applicable when representational resources warrant it; infants' anticipations must not automatically be called metacognitive Bayesian credences.

These accounts can cooperate or conflict. Attachment classifications, learned contingency, habitus, internal speech and neurobiological arousal are different measurements and should not be collapsed into one invisible latent scalar.

## Hostile cases and expected distinctions
1. **Ordinary repeated affectionate care:** durable context-specific reliance may develop with no remembered discrete gift. It may remain entirely undramatic; require demonstrated pattern, not invented "happy scene".
2. **Love plus unmet need due to wage work:** preserve two records: reliable tenderness when together; uncertain relief at critical absent hours. Material employment and childcare infrastructure causally matter. Neither cancels the other.
3. **One child and two adults:** parent/step-parent/grandparent responses can diverge; do not propagate a quality to all adults or households.
4. **Repeated delay + excellent repair:** may support "comfort comes eventually" while maintaining a real sensitivity to waiting. Repair can be developmentally positive without retroactively meeting the original need.
5. **Stable baseline + exceptional disaster:** safety in normal home context persists while threat orientation may be introduced for a dark alley/dog/weather cue. A generalization is a separate transition requiring evidence.
6. **Real discontinuity:** abandonment, forced displacement, prolonged deprivation or a previously trusted adult's sustained dangerous conduct can warrant significant reorganization even if encounters are few. Demand actual dependency, access, support, behavior and change evidence; no arbitrary minimum count.
7. **No durable change:** an isolated inconvenience with prompt remedy, ambiguous relevance, robust differentiated anticipation or absence of access leaves persistent state intact, even if momentarily upsetting.

## Scientific falsifiers
- If changing the caregiver's actual contingent response pattern while holding scene label fixed cannot change candidate reception/outcome, the mechanism is scene-label determinism.
- If loving provision and objectively inadequate care cannot coexist, it is falsely one-dimensional.
- If an unfamiliar person's cruelty globally rewrites trust in one's existing caregiver absent bridging evidence, it overgeneralizes.
- If a routine with no narrated incident cannot be learned, the proposed baseline mistakes episodic memory for activity.
- If NO_CHANGE or contradictory active expectations are illegal, the ontology is invalid.
- If persistence is merely a fixed "five years"/age multiplier, the rule is unwarranted.
