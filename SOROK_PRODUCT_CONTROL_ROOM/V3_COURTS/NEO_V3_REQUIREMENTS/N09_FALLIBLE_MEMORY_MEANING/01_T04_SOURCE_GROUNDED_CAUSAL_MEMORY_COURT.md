## 1. The irreducible distinctions

A successful N09 is not a better biography. It must permit, simultaneously, at least the following **different truth bearers**, none substituting for another:

1. **Objective occurrence**: world/actor actions, opportunity, material and care facts, independently acting people, precise source and causal time. An adult caregiver's affection and actual labor/time/care availability can conflict without either being fictional. Stored in immutable event/history/actor/world receipts under the actual admitting owner.
2. **Actor exposure/receivability**: what that person could sense, participate in or be told, conditioned by bodily, social, linguistic and attentional situation. A parent's underlying motive and actual work shift are not automatically accessible to an infant. CAN008 R2's event-side receivability/uptake is not yet a concrete Rule A.
3. **Situated impression**: what the encountered arrangement did in that historical activity; possible coexisting relief, frustration, warmth, helplessness, curiosity, no noticeable effect. It is not an event's objective valence, an LLM-supplied label or proof of a persistent trace.
4. **Retained causal trace or practical orientation**: history-conditioned possibility of a later response/practice without recollectable episode. The trace must be earned under a qualified CAN011/ADP016-compatible transition; inference cannot posit an invisible latent disposition merely to explain a later outcome.
5. **Event-referential memory**: source-anchored retained, possibly uncertain, incomplete or inaccessible content, with stable identity and a lawful formation/forgetting/recall history under ADP016; does not imply articulate recollection.
6. **Actor epistemic stance**: an actually generated knowledge, belief, uncertainty or positive nonknowledge concerning a governed canonical claim under ADP017. Accessible memory != automatic knowledge, and absent analyst knowledge != actor's conscious 'I do not know.'
7. **Autobiographical retrieval/reconstruction**: an occasion-specific act of remembering, using accessible cues, prior claims, present projects and linguistic repertoire; can be partial, selective, misleading or uncertain. Its *statement* is not automatically a newly acquired memory fact or objective source correction.
8. **Narrated meaning / public, private, inner expression**: a V2-safe rendition within actual actor-access/register permissions. Rendering cannot issue retroactive V3 memory, true history, internal experience or durable mediator.
9. **New reflective/internal activity**: a later person may interpret, doubt, revise, discuss or commit, but its influence on subsequent practice requires a separately authorized actor-owned V3 event/effect and CAN008/CAN011/ADP017/SCI05 admission; it cannot be produced by narrative fluency alone.

The six operational cut-lines are **objective != reception != trace != recollection != belief != narration**; an additional seventh line separates an actual new interpretive act from its mere verbal depiction. We must preserve causal relations among these objects without collapsing their identity.

## 2. Scientific question and rival explanatory families

**Leontiev** (_Actividade, Consciência e Personalidade_, uploaded Portuguese PDF pp.18,20,25,27): relational activity and concrete objects mediate formation of motives, and a successful act can carry contradictory affect in relation to different motives. It licenses the question whether the child repeatedly meets unavailable practical care *and* experiences caregiver warmth; it **does not** license automatic 'scarcity -> abandonment schema', a remembered preverbal sentence or a specific update magnitude.

**Luria** (_Cognitive Development, Its Cultural and Social Foundations_, PDF pp.29,67–68): language and culturally organized operations transform available conceptual distinctions; group-context interviews are not longitudinal within-person proof of every child's private memory. A later word or category can make past experience thinkable in new terms but is not evidence the child once thought those terms.

**Sève** (_Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality_, PDF pp.337,364–365): real acquired capacities and use-time, educational restrictions and participation challenge both 'fixed temperament' and 'all motives arise from biological shortage'. A person can develop interpretive competence while an objectively deprived circumstance remains materially unresolved.

**Lahire** (_The Plural Actor_, PDF pp.12–14,76,79): embodied past and present situation interact; heterogeneity is historical and possible, **not universal**. A never manifested disposition cannot be conjured from a gap. A stable coherent actor is a genuine rival to the contradictory-trace hypothesis.

**Dreier** (_Psychotherapy in Everyday Life_, PDF pp.49–56): first-person reasons, abilities and action possibilities are situated in particular everyday participations; reflective disclosure in therapy need not transfer to another relationship or change actual conduct. Context/practical support can be a causal condition rather than an inner trait.

**Elder** (_Children of the Great Depression_, PDF pp.94,110,115): material household change, family roles, timing and social participation matter; his cohort/historical findings cannot establish an invariant family-income -> remembered affection function.

**Archer** (_Structure, Agency and the Internal Conversation_, PDF pp.18–20): structurally given constraints and enablements acquire practical force through particular projects and reflexive mediation. The book's reflective adult/project language is **not** a warrant to attribute developed internal conversations to an infant.

**Additional independent memory science:** Nelson & Fivush, *Psychological Review* 111(2), 2004, pp.486–511, DOI 10.1037/0033-295X.111.2.486 (PubMed PMID 15065919; abstract directly checked): preschool autobiographical-memory emergence is multicomponent—basic memory, language/narrative, parent talk, time and understanding of self/other. Conway & Pleydell-Pearce, *Psychological Review* 107(2), 2000, pp.261–288, DOI 10.1037/0033-295X.107.2.261 (PubMed abstract directly checked): specific autobiographical memories are constructed through cue/goal-dependent access to autobiographical knowledge. These are theoretical/empirical model summaries, not a proven implementable rule for individual generated agents. Bartlett's 1932 *Remembering* is relevant reconstructive tradition but original book **not directly inspected here**; do not attribute page-specific claims.

**Unresolved rival accounts:** (a) contextual learning and practice without episodic recall; (b) a remembered episode without durable practice change; (c) repeated social co-narration changing autobiographical account without altering the objective event; (d) current ideology making a prior experience salient, rather than prior experience determining ideology; (e) a present support/opportunity change, rather than acquired disposition, explaining later behavior; (f) stable coherence rather than plurality. None can be excluded by a narrative alone. Distinguish evidence for formation from convenient story-fitting.
