# T03 / N05 — Development is change in causal possibilities, not larger numbers

## Seven mutually distinguishable transition families

1. **Temporary activation** of an already-acquired operation: no durable internal change; triggered by a counterpart, cue, task, embodied condition or context. A later nontriggering case remains unaffected.
2. **Strength/scope/access change** within an existing operation: same identity, changed applicability, confidence/availability or support requirement *if* source authority defines the semantics. Could restrict, expand, split or suspend; avoid invented 'trait strength' arithmetic.
3. **New practical operation**: earlier available acts cannot perform distinction/action; actual activity with necessary instruction/help, constructed relation and discriminating feedback justifies a scoped new operation. Need evidence distinguishing guided from independent use. This is **candidate SCI02/SCI04**, not a standing rule.
4. **Changed relation between existing operations**: two capacities preexist, but their conditional enablement/inhibition/composition changes. Must store a changed relation with causal parents, not silently adjust both components.
5. **Persisting contradiction**: two context-local organizations survive side by side and can be separately recruited; no forced synthesis or healing trajectory.
6. **Lost opportunity or social support**: the operation may remain while material capacity, location, sanction/permission, counterpart assistance or institution changes; later different observable performance is **world change**, not unlearning.
7. **Durable reorganization of later response**: authorized CAN010/011 or another owner mutation persists and is **read semantically** in a later event, altering typed possibilities/recruitment/response under independent world control. A checkpoint hash or narrative description alone is insufficient.

## Historical-materialist explanatory anchors, with explicit epistemic limits

**Leontiev** (provided Portuguese *Actividade, Consciência e Personalidade*, pp.18,20,25,27,32): personality organization emerges through social relations between activities and motives; the same surface act may be contradicted by its relation to other motives; internal work and changing motives are meaningful. This supports distinguishing transformed **activity relations** from updated numerical states, not a specific deterministic learning threshold.

**Luria** (provided *Cognitive Development: Its Cultural and Social Foundations*, pp.29,35,67–68): mediated speech, roles and forms of participation can reorganize practical classification/goal-setting; what task means to participant conditions its result; 'cognitive reorganization' must not be construed as universal schooling ladder, fixed superiority of abstract classification or coercive universal transfer. Need observe the concrete operation before/after, supports and changed use conditions. Historical research context must be criticized rather than taken as neutral developmental essence.

**Lahire** (provided *The Plural Actor*, pp.13,76–79): plural dispositions are socially conditional, not necessary for every human; submerged/suspended dispositions can reappear when social micro-context changes. The decisive diagnostic is not a zero score but reappearance under conditions resembling their historical constitution. Contradiction need not disappear, and outside support can switch use independently of dispositional change.

**Sève** (provided *Man in Marxist Theory and the Psychology of Personality*, pp.319–321,364–365): actual capacities have socially conditioned modes of production, investment and use-time; socially imposed time use can block self-development despite retained productive ability. A timeless internal vector misses this contradiction. His sector/time analysis is interpretive source for causal design, **not a universal numerical timetable**.

**Dreier** (provided *Psychotherapy in Everyday Life*, pp.49–56,198): persons form abilities by participating across contexts with unequal situated possibilities; assistance, position, trajectory and activity meaning cannot be removed from the ability claim. Merely placing a `skill=true` internal marker contradicts this theory. **Archer** (provided *Structure, Agency, and the Internal Conversation*, pp.84,112): adult internal deliberation can prioritize and recompose concerns; do not require articulate deliberation, particularly for preverbal child or implicit/habitual action. Her account is a rival/partial lens, not mandatory mechanism for all human change.

## Rival explanations and stringent cases

**Skill-looking behavior but NO learning:** B learns nothing new between E1 and E2; E1 lacked required tool/instructor, E2 brings tool and assistance. B completes task at E2 using already-held operation. Correct state diff: CAN011 operation unchanged; ADP/world support changed, newly executable alternative, no CREATE. If the simulation writes `skill_acquired` due to E2 success, science RED.

**Genuine new discrimination:** after repeated attempts at resolving a material-help/relational-obligation conflict, the actor constructs and uses a distinction with qualified feedback and support. Later, within the same warranted scope, an identical help offer yields `accept assistance without conceding authority` as a newly *thinkable* alternative; previous person could only consider unconditional acceptance, refusal or ambiguity. This is provisional; a single event is not universally sufficient, and transfer to unrelated institutional settings is separately unproved.

**Dormant contradiction:** person behaves obediently at workplace under a manager's power but resists equivalent demands in mutual-aid collective where role/sanction history differs. Multiple acquired dispositions exist; context governs practical expressibility, not hypocrisy score. Later reaction may be held under fear, not erased.

**Hostile alternative:** apparent change from E1 to E2 is memory leak, newly provided prompt, unauthored added option, partner's changing behavior, adult language acquisition without practical mastery, or an original event not actually received. Freeze/sever one edge at a time.

## Source/claim scale

This dossier proposes a **computationally falsifiable architecture**, not a psychology proven by examples. Empirical work must test real developmental observations with ethically/epistemically qualified historical sources, feedback and alternatives; simulation adequacy must compare same-person semantically typed outcomes, not fluent retrospective storytelling or apparent narrative consistency.
