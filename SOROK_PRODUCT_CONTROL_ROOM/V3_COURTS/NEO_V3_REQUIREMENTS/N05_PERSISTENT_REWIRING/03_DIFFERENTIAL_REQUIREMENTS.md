## Distinct causal changes, not a single `learning_delta`

**N04 interpressure** (event-local evaluation) reads prior CAN011 records, witnesses their relations and outputs immediate CAN008 evaluation/effects; it does **not** write persistent training. **N05 development** (later admitted owner lifecycle) operates on history-bearing state under genuine activity/feedback and may emit CREATE, TRANSFORM, SUSPEND, RETIRE or NO_CHANGE. A type-label, mere age advancement, raw event presence, LLM self-narration, scalar pressure magnitude or amount of suffering cannot satisfy this function.

For each state change record independently: `actual_activity_occurrences`, actor partial reception/interpretation under CAN008, practical operations attempted, assistance/tool/counterpart conditions, feedback that distinguishes success from chance, old and new availability/scope, disconfirming events, target owner, causal parents, uncertainty. If no source grants retention, output a proposed admission with `SCIENCE_RULE_MISSING` not a durable mutation.

## Power / reproduction / family differentiation

An exploited employee's learned compliance, actual ability to negotiate, and employer's power to dismiss are different kinds of facts. The same operative disposition can appear across two contexts because sanctions differ without inner transformation. A child whose caregiver supplies reliable love but little food can retain love expectation **and** an insufficiency orientation; do not average these. Repeated repair of a relationship may strengthen contextual trust while failing to alter material scarcity; an apparent contradiction may be objectively real rather than psychological inconsistency.

**No global graph-store necessity.** Each meaningful relation can initially be represented as a canonical scoped owner record with n-ary parent refs and time-scope; compile a derived event-local graph only as a view for N04 joint evaluation. Later topology/storage is engineering evidence question.

## Falsifiers

- Same actor pre/post with acquired distinction removed: if later choice remains attributable to newly formed distinction despite no CAN011 read, original causal claim fails.
- Same actor same CAN011 state, help/tool added: observable success may change, but admission of learned operation is **RED**.
- New assistant prose adds a favorable self-account but no admitted actor activity: any CAN011 CREATE is **RED**.
- A deliberate contradictory orientation persists after a contrary encounter: forced unification is **RED**.
- Known not-received event creates a new aversion or belief: **RED**.
- No qualified evidence, honest uncertainty: `NO_CHANGE` when rule says exact neutrality, otherwise `HOLD`; unknown is not known zero.
