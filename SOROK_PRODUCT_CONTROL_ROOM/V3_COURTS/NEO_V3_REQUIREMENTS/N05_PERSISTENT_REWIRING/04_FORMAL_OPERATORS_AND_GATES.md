A single episode may lawfully cause several distinct target effects (e.g. new scoped practice AND separate memory access, or stable operation but materially worsened ability), but **each effect requires its own target-owner rule and qualified causal parents**, not an unrestricted `psychology_patch`. Certain effects may co-occur or conflict and demand atomic rollback/no-change. Event occurrence never proves any single state transition.

## Exact changed-person → later response witness

`T0`: Actor has ordinary ability to receive assistance but **no proved practical distinction** between accepting material help and conceding interpersonal authority; record owner state exactly, not 'incapable human'. `T1`: qualified, actually received event + actor action/learning attempt, supported social instruction and feedback; actor proposes or demonstrates the distinction. `T2`: **competent** CAN011 owner decides CREATE/NO_CHANGE and separately ADP016/017 if authorized; records scope `relative_work_help`, required support and epoch. `T3`: later independently generated help offer E2 with identical material affordances across branches; CAN008 receives versioned CAN011 operation as conditioning state, *without requiring autobiographical recall*. Its typed output may newly admit `ACCEPT_MATERIAL_WITHOUT_CONCESSION` as thinkable/practical alternative, or may maintain the conflict under support restriction. `T4`: actor selects/defers among available alternatives through lawful response; independent other/world consequence is admitted separately.

**Strong discriminant**: counterfactual same T0 actor/world/offer with T1 **SIGN_ONLY** (words heard, no constructive practice) leaves operation uncreated; later new alternative absent **if** formation was truly causal. Repeat keeping same owner state but withdrawing T3 tool or ally: new *internal* operation remains but performance blocked. Repeat with irrelevant later offer: new operation may not recruit. Compare actor-owned semantic value reads, not status labels or prose.
