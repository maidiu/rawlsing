## Source, ordering and irreversible world semantics
Each candidate i has proposal identity private to server, but its realized event receives *new source-owner event identity*, independent B action and real effective clock. A coactor may choose an unproposed act or no act; no forcing story beat merely to satisfy 3–5. Realistic intervals include productive and reproductive labor and mundane continuity; no mandatory traumatic increment. Owner world response cannot be rolled back by psychological transaction after B has actually acted. Three competing C6/C9/C12 policies: (1) PREFIX_CONTIGUOUS: commit each complete source-qualified step and hold suffix on first failure; (2) WHOLE_BATCH_ATOMIC: permissible only for fully controllable staged virtual world with zero externally committed actions; (3) INDEPENDENT_SUFFIX: only when owner proves a later event independent of failed slot and nonleaking gap. **NO_POLICY_ASSUMED**; batch shell can record each option but not select one.

Example: call N proposes E1 tutoring, E2 caregiver repair, E3 new school task, E4 changed workplace availability. E1 world source permits and learner forms/doesn't form qualified practical procedure via original rule. E2 source refused: world/actor state after E1 remains genuinely committed; E2 not realized, E3/E4 cannot be auto-realized or remembered. A printed E4 prospective sentence is a proposal, never event. If E2 happened objectively but child could not observe it, world ledger persists and actor-safe memory excludes it; another event's natural cause may still incorporate objective consequences under real source law.

## Actor access, memory and SCI05
ADP016 nondeclarative trace is not necessarily recall; ADP017 actor propositions and misremembered explanation require a real later source/time and claim grade, without synthetic positive sensory childhood details. Retrospective derives from accessible features of *realized* events and its own accessible chronology; remove an ADP016 feature and any direct or indirect reference, including opaque count/order/prefix ID, must disappear. Objective ledger and event IDs are never placed in model-visible prompt; even gap labels or “3 of 5 events” may reveal inaccessible event existence. Memory projection is a deterministic no-leak read-only step. Actor may be sincerely wrong; do not correct from omniscient narrator unless actual actor source.

Because half1 and half2 run in **one uninterrupted ordinary model inference**, the model can linguistically premise later conditional prospects on a persuasive half1 self-account, but that does not mean the act was admitted. Postcall SCI05 validator applies original rule to source/access evidence. On denial it changes neither actor state nor history, then traverses typed dependency graph: dependent prospects INVALIDATED_POSTCALL_SCI05 or HELD_REQUALIFY, independent proposals individually source-checked if user/owner policy allows. No injected mid-call validation or uncounted extra LLM.
