## Typed candidate semantic entities

- OperationRef: typed actor/owner/semantic version, lawful inputs, outputs, preconditions, task object, required social/material means.
- CompositeProcedure: n-ary components with roles/order/cooperation, task scope, provenance of acquisition, external prerequisites. Do not explode into pairwise fictional edges.
- RelationCandidate: source operation, target operation, mode (RECRUITS, INHIBITS, ORDERS_BEFORE, REDIRECTS, COOPERATES), context predicate, actor and branch, originating activities/Rule A/T, competitor explanations and first subsequent semantic consumer; candidate-only before issue.
- ActivationTrace: event-local consulted operands, qualified eligibility, active supports, actual Rule A result, no durable status rewrite.
- ExternalActivityConfiguration: actual coactor actions, institution, tools and feasible activity; may change outcomes without changing internal state.
- Qualified NO_CHANGE: Rule T application, owner identity, evidence, accepted readset, effective time; cannot stand for NO_EVENT, SOURCE_DENIED, NOT_YET_OBSERVED, UNKNOWN or HELD_MISSING_RULE.

Existing operation ineligible now, independently lost tool, activity not encountered, or temporarily inhibited current response **does not cause** durable CAN011 DORMANT or ENDED. Those only via owner-qualified durable transition. Persistent trace unsupported by subsequent observation may remain UNKNOWN or context-unevaluated; do not imagine a latent disposition to fill absence.

## Sufficient and efficient storage

Store only actually admitted actor-owned operations and issued relations as sparse keyed entries. For k operations and e qualified relations, target O(k+e) persistence; context/type index roughly O(log k+log e), relevant local evaluation O(active candidates+incident eligible edges), no O(k²) dense graph. Bound composite arity, predicate vocabulary, depth, per-event operations and source lookup. A graph cycle is never legal simply because abstract nodes exist; demand owner-issued iteration/termination policy or HOLD_CYCLE. No automatic coactivation count, Hebbian weight, arbitrary decay, fixed age/type prior or universal numerical learning rate. Original source defines capabilities and empirical comparators.

## Identification protocol

For a proposed acquired relation, compare same world/coactor/task/actor access with: (1) only preexisting value changed, (2) fixed context eligibility changed, (3) learned composition with no novel relation, (4) changed external assistance, (5) no durable change. Isolate and sever only specific committed operation/relation semantic field in E2; if output is unchanged, **reject unique edge claim**. A difference in state hash, prose or actor activity alone is not evidence. Report ambiguity and HOLD if data cannot discriminate. Qualify coercive contexts and power differentials; observed compliance does not by itself prove internalized ideology.
