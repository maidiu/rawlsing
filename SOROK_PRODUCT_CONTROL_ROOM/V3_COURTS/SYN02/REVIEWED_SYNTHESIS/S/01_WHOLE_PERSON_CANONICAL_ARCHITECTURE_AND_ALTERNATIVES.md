## Developmental life

Start from source-qualified organism, kin, location, infrastructure, material history and social roles; UNKNOWN is not “normal”, safe, loving or zero. Infancy involves actual dependency and reception, not adult-language ideology. As the actor traverses childhood and work, opportunities and constraints develop historically. Each sampled event is connected by *actual* lived intervals of work, care, noncontact, schooling, illness, play, household labor, social cooperation and state power. Other persons act on their own worlds and conflicting projects. Adult concepts, memory, ideology and reflexivity may form through actual situated participation and admitted internal activity; inaccurate later beliefs about childhood do not rewrite old events. Some people remain comparatively coherent, stable, apparently unchanged, or externally aided. No mandatory rupture, type or plurality.

At each event: source-owner world/B fact → actor access/eligibility → original CAN008 immediate multi-stream response → actual action/world consequences → separate CAN011/010/012 governed durable decision (change, evaluated nochange or hold) → lived interval → subsequent event reads the state **by semantic payload**. An event can be objective but unperceived; no internal effect inferred. World and actor clock advance independently subject to causal ordering. V2 never writes back.

## Scientific warrant and limits

Leontiev distinguishes need, motive, object and action; Sève distinguishes capability-development time and exercise/reproduction; Dreier locates learned competence in multiple social sites; Elder and Kohn show differentiated material experience; Archer permits reflective response under projects. Lahire explicitly warns against universal plural dispositions. Luria, *Cognitive Development*, PDF pp29–30 allows examination of interfunctional organization but calls radical new systems evidentially unresolved. These primary materials are referenced from C/RA selected-page review; none prescribes CAN011 edge formation, quantitative rates, trauma labels or an individual update operator.

## Countermodels and costs

1. **Old BackboneState and age/type scalar updates:** easily serializable but coercively seeds psyche and conflates structural position, experienced meaning and narrator.
2. **Dense Hebbian network:** representationally expressive but O(k²), nonidentified by sparse biography, automatically invented learning law.
3. **Only fixed gates:** adequate for some events; insufficient for source-qualified newly acquired composed operations.
4. **Only distributed activity, never actor durable ability:** captures support/tool environment but misses genuine skill acquisition carried into future tasks.
5. **Sparse optional typed hybrid [SELECTED]:** fixed context/value as default, acquired practice if qualified, new relation only for cases not explained by those or external aid; portable across material worlds and open to scientific refusal.
