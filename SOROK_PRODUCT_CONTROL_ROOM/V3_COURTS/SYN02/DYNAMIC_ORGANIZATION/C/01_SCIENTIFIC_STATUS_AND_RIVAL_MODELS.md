## A. Exact scientific problem and grades

The question is whether a particular person's lived activity may alter not just the magnitude of existing states but the *available and actual organization* of operations: which perception/anticipation/practice/motive/interpretation/recall/volitional control can be brought to bear on which others, in what contexts, by whose material means, with what persistence or inhibition. Development can mean learning to accomplish an activity that was previously impossible, learning to use a previously available capability for a different object or situation, recruiting one operation to regulate another, or reorganizing a relation among several operations. Each differs from a temporarily different choice under unchanged competence.

**Three predicates must never collapse:**

1. **Phenomenon claim**: people can reorganize complex activity, language-mediated thinking, practical capabilities and personal participation through historically specific life. This is supportable by the cited theory, with distinctive empirical limits.
2. **Representational hypothesis**: representing certain such differences with a versioned, sparse actor-owned relation between operation references might be economical and causally transparent. No primary author specified our schema.
3. **Rule claim**: this exact event/reference creates, removes, activates, inhibits or strengthens a durable individual relation according to fixed conditions. No such domain-specific Rule T/A is established here. Status ORIGINAL_ISSUER_REQUIRED.

A functional relationship is not a trait correlation, metaphorical network, unobserved unconscious psychodynamic postulate, narrative association, child-assigned ideology, or evidence that two variables happened to be high. It has a source-qualified input-output role in an actual or properly justified counterfactual person-world activity.

## D. Causal identification and competing models

**The critical identification problem:** for finite observed action histories, a fixed conditional policy with hidden long-term contextual variables can be observationally indistinguishable from a newly created relation. A dynamic-edge representation is therefore not an empirically observed “synapse”; it is one candidate *minimal explanatory mechanism* after alternatives have been tested and constraints held constant. Avoid circularly proving topology because the chosen schema stores an edge.

Discriminating controls:
- **External-support explanation:** better tool, real independent peer's help, safer institution or caregiver available now; remove means while keeping person state and compare. Support may build later personal capacity but immediate success is not proof of it.
- **Preexisting-gate explanation:** existing fixed procedure activated by context or changed value. Inspect exact actor read-set, prior context, operator identity and scope. Compare against candidate new procedure/edge on held inputs.
- **Self-report explanation:** actor narrates a learned relation/ideology or performs public agreement without qualifying observed practical competence; an ADP017 proposition is not an ADP016 event memory, CAN011 new learned relation or C9 performance.
- **Single-sign learning:** an instructional sign can enable a genuinely new inference/action under suitable prior competence; do **not** impose repetitive coactivation as necessary. Conversely hearing words is not sufficient by itself.
- **Real multi-motive alternative:** same successful action can have negative felt meaning relative to another activity/motive (Leontiev p27) without requiring a new relation or pathology. A person can be coherent rather than universally split.
- **Actor-world distributed explanation:** work rhythms, aunt's actual availability, peer/institution affordances and relational power alter effective possibilities without private operations changing.

A new relation must admit a **counterfactual where only earned relation availability or typed semantic content changes and a *later actual qualified consumer's* considered/realized action differs**, or legitimate NO_CHANGE with rule and witness. Behavioral change alone is insufficient: other actors, opportunity, material conditions, age, narration, inference thresholds and source rights must be causally separated.

## E. Scientific conclusion and unresolved disagreement

1. **AGREE_SCOPED:** Development can involve qualitatively reorganized functional activity and interfunctional relationships; Leontiev/Luria/Dreier/Sève provide substantial reason not to limit the person to fixed trait magnitudes. Luria p29 is especially direct.
2. **AGREE_SCOPED:** The person/world boundary is explanatory, not one container. A “functional connection” can be acquired internal integration, joint activity, acquired use of an external cultural resource, or temporary support.
3. **REVISE_PRECISE:** Luria's functional-systems vocabulary is not proof of a literal learned graph edge in every individual or of a universal neural Hebbian law; Sève activity-time is not a learning multiplier; Lahire explicitly forbids universal plurality.
4. **HOLD:** which domain-specific events create/destroy an actor-owned relation, what evidence suffices and whether learned “edge” and composed procedure are distinct at the native grain. Require scientific owner, actual source and later semantic consumer; do not choose ad hoc coefficients.
