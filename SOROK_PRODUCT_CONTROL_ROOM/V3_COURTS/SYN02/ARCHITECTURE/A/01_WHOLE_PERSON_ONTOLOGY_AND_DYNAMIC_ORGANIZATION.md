## Executive architectural thesis: the person is a history-conditioned organization of activities, not a personality label

A person is one causally continuous embodied agent acting within a historically changing material/social world, with needs and vulnerabilities, attainable objects of activity, situated capacities, patterns and expectations formed in particular relations, contradictory or coherent mediating dispositions, temporally limited knowledge, event-referential and nondeclarative memory, and potential reflective agency. **Not every capacity is psychological or internal**: bodily energy, available money, literacy acquired through instruction, transport, employer control, care by an aunt, friendships, workplace rules, organization and personal commitments have different owners. Some enable a previously unperformable action without changing internal ability. Conversely internal activity may change even when objective circumstances remain.

The minimum complete architecture must represent *whether an operation or relationship exists*, *what it can do under actual circumstances*, *which source qualified its acquisition*, *where and when it is eligible*, *what else might be concurrently recruited*, and *which later actual decision consumes it*. It cannot be exhausted by \(S_{t+1}=S_t+\Delta\) for a scalar vector, an Enneagram type, a language-model backstory, an untyped graph, or a single event-local responder.

**Selected design hypothesis (not original psychology law):** an **event-sourced, single-person, typed and sparse operation/relationship state**, consuming externally owned activity/world facts, with owner-issued interpreters for event-local recruitment and historical formation. This gives a *representational capability* for contingent new functional connections, but neither requires every history to add edges nor presumes a Hebbian or biological update rule. The full comparison is in document 02; typed interfaces in 04.

## How new functional connectivity can arise — and when it must not

Let an actor at time t have *registered operations* (e.g. asking for help, interpreting a promise, applying a learned technical procedure, using a religious concept in a social dispute) and source-qualified **contextual recruitment relations**. A relation is not a synapse or universal correlation; it is a typed, actor-specific *conditional applicability or influence*, with provenance, eligible semantic conditions, prior evidence and destination semantics. Permitted **representational** outcomes of an owner-evaluated historical transition:

- **PERSIST**: encounter is real yet no durable operation/relation changes; distinct from missing rule/unknown.
- **CHANGE_VALUE**: existing sensitivity/state value changes while operation topology stays fixed. Historical V3 route-driven wound/practice values can later change `_pick_channel`; that is value-mediated change, not evidence a relation was acquired.
- **CHANGE_ELIGIBILITY**: a previously acquired operation is recruited in an additional proven context, or its prior scope is narrowed/denied; this differs from mere intensity change.
- **ACQUIRE_OPERATION**: a specific historically demonstrated practical or conceptual operation becomes available to this person (through instruction, trial, practice, reflection or other owner-qualified mechanism); not every case requires repetition—one sign may sometimes suffice.
- **ACQUIRE_RELATION / RECOMPOSE**: a source-verified new typed conditioning/inhibition/facilitation relation among already existing operations makes a novel *composed* possibility. Must show independent evidence that neither unchanged values nor available environmental scaffolding explains it.
- **SUSPEND / TRANSFORM / END**: under qualified later evidence a practice or relation can be temporarily ineligible, reconfigured or ended; silence is persistence, not automatic forgetting/healing.
- **UNRESOLVED / HELD**: competing possible interpretations survive, missing owner rule/access/source prevents a lawful transition, or currently available evidence cannot distinguish alternatives.

None of these verbs is scientifically self-executing. Existing CAN011 `V3MS_MEDIATOR/TENSION/PRACTICE` has an explicit generic proposal/transition grammar but **not** a blanket license to add dynamic relations. An independent qualified CAN011/other owner must decide whether and how the proposed edge/operation is representable in existing registries, or issue a *narrow* extension. The existing CAN008 R2 generic coupling is semantically adopted without current executable domain Rule A; it accepts multiple streams/recruitment but does not imply a fully implemented learned-edge evaluator.

### Strong discrimination: acquired connection vs alternatives

*Acquired connection claim* requires (a) originally absent functional availability at a controlled later context, (b) history with actual qualifying participation/receipt, (c) emitted owner-qualified transition with before/after semantic identities, (d) later same-person **read of the content** of that new relation by CAN008/C9, (e) contrasting later action under equal world/coactor conditions when the link is omitted, and (f) controls for fixed-value change, new tool, support, increased resources, social imitation and narrator choice. **A new edge hash is not a changed person.** If the link was always there but context changed, prefer *context recruitment* explanation. If help from a competent friend now makes the job possible while the person's technique did not change, prefer *distributed scaffolding*. If age tick or S39 prompt changed choice flags, classify non-evidence.

### A developed person may be coherent, plural or uncertain

Lahire's *The Plural Actor* (project-source direct examination reported by independent RS and RT04) does **not** require plural dispositions in every actor; disposition persistence and activation require actual evidence. A person can simultaneously retain a trusting expectation in one relationship and scarcity anticipation in a different setting; equally, an integrated stable actor across both contexts is a legitimate possible result. Internal tension requires explicit historically grounded opposing relevance, not a default dramatic profile. Nor should a person be compelled to have a dominant ideological framework, wound, Enneagram, or 'trauma'. Valid-empty states are substantive.

## One whole-life construction, not a predetermined script

**Dependency:** infant needs and access to care precede abstract cognition, but socially organized relationships exist from the first interaction. Source-owned caregivers have actual time/obligations; the child senses only certain features. (1) Early receiving and bodily/practical response might yield context-qualified expectations *under a competent formation rule*, or no durable learning; (2) ordinary recurring activity and opportunities/support can teach/maintain abilities and regulate attention without a dramatic event; (3) school, peers, language and civic/religious institutions present new acts, concepts, and possible relations between practices and reasons; (4) work, household formation, loss/repair, economic power and joint production create new objects/motives and constraints; (5) adult reflective projects may reinterpret old experiences with later culturally available concepts; (6) later activity changes history and possibly meaning again. Every phase includes other actors, practical resources and no-effect alternatives; stages here are **descriptive opportunities**, not automatic age-gated development or ideology destination.

**Worked non-deterministic trajectory:** loving night-shift caregiver A, child X, independently acting aunt B. A's work produces real care gaps but A offers genuine warm attention when present. B sometimes covers. Actor X may retain partly distinct affiliative and scarcity expectations, a nuanced co-care belief, stable ordinary trust, or no qualified inference. Teacher C later demonstrates a repair/skill in real activity; X may acquire a new operation or merely borrow C's support. As an adult X may learn a religious/political explanatory concept and use it to organize mutual care, hold an objectively mistaken claim about A's motives, reject the account, or never adopt an ideological explanation. **No branch is chosen from child deprivation or a type.** Event-by-event admissions, actual opportunities, actor exposure and rules decide what is justified; LLM narrative illustrates only accessible outcomes.
