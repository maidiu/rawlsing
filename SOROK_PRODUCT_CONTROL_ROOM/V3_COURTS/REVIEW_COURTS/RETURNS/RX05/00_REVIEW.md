## 3. Scientific assessment and rival explanations

X05's central scientific distinction is correct: the source contains **constraint scalars, relationship labels and diagnostic scaffolding**, not a demonstrated actor-world with actual care transactions, independent co-actors, or lawfully no-change intervals. But the source evidence supports only a **differential**, not a developmental law:

1. **Objective material circumstances vs received care.** `capacity.time`/`money`/`social_support` and `caregiving_load` are scalar constraints. No work/care schedule, household roster, presence/absence, care transaction or child receipt exists. A child difference can be produced by parent warmth, event selection, LLM narration or external co-care. M02/M04 cannot decide among them.
2. **Parent intention/love vs practical availability.** Parent behaviour is averaged into need-axis labels; parent type is inferred from sampled behaviour. A warm parent who works long hours, with an aunt providing daily care, is not representable as one arrangement. Conversely, high material provision plus coercion is not a distinct independent action.
3. **Independent other actor vs relational label.** Magnets have no `who`/entity ID and no relation history; the seeded `mother`/`father` labels are not proof of actual parents. The relational field is a nine-strata summary with a small conductance bonus, not a graph of obligations/power. The M20 parent-micrograph counterexample must be corrected, but the label-only defect remains.
