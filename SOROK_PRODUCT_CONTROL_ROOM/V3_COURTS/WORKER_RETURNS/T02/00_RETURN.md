## 1. Main scientific court — categorical holding
**N02: Thick historical baselines** are sets of multiple, context-specific, need/relationship/practice-conditioned anticipations. Their support comes from actual recurrent activity and coactor response, not merely memorable episodes; they can be bodily/practical and nonverbal. Retain event and routine historical trace and supporting/opposing examples separately. One relation may be affectionate yet unable to provide sufficient care because actual labor and care infrastructures constrain availability. Ordinary repetition may matter even where nothing is remembered as a dramatic event. Opposed scoped anticipations can coexist.

**N03: Challenge ≠ complication ≠ threat.** Challenge demands new effort/capacity/adaptation for valued action. Complication introduces exception, ambiguity or insufficiency of prior understanding. Threat endangers valued dependency/relation/action possibility/bodily condition. They can co-occur, can be absent, and can transform across time. An objectively dangerous situation and an actor's perceived threat are independent; either can exist without the other. Other actors have their own decisions and actual responses. Repair is an actual later interaction restoring some conditions or abilities, not assumed from caregiver intent or apology; repair need not erase original failure or satisfy every need.

**N08: Scene description ≠ actor reception ≠ transient effect ≠ persistent change ≠ remembered narrative.** Differentiated categories include ordinary continuity, repeated confirmation, salient confirmation, novelty, challenge, complication, threat, disorientation, intense event, repair, negative response and qualified no-change. Intensity and novelty neither guarantee durable change nor exclude it. An established reliable relationship can survive isolated disorientation; one major consequential incident can still reorganize practical relationships or possibilities. A transient upset with stable persistent state is legitimate; a narrated memory without current developmental change is also possible. No global confidence/salience scalar, age multiplier, or trauma from scene label.

## 3. Alternative mechanisms assessed
Associative/context-specific learning explains procedural expectancy but not the full material organization of care. Attachment sensitivity/co-regulation explains differentiated dyadic reliance but average associations are not deterministic classifications. Activity theory locates need transformation in actual motive/object/means and life activity, while Lahire accounts for plural sometimes contradictory dispositions. Dreier locates adaptive change across places and coparticipants. Stress physiology captures arousal/protection but does not manufacture beliefs or life narratives. Dynamic-systems/hysteresis supplies mathematical analogies, not ready-to-use universal psychological constants. Rutter's protective mechanism account emphasizes changed risk, cascading consequences, efficacy and opportunities; no automatic resilience label. Empirical sources mainly come from particular populations/tasks: heterogeneity and cultural-generalizability cautions remain.
