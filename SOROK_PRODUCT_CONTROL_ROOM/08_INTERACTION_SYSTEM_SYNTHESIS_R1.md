# Sorok Interaction-System Synthesis R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_SYNTHESIS__D001_D002`

This file is the navigation/synthesis layer for the interaction mechanics developed during the first Sorok product-ideation pass.

It does not replace the detailed D001/D002 constitutions.

## 1. Product interaction thesis

Rawlsing interaction is **situated action inside a causally live, partially observed social world**.

The player is dropped into the middle of another person's ongoing life rather than entering an empty chat surface.

That life already contains:
- material constraints;
- household/institutional obligations;
- other people;
- time pressure;
- history;
- current needs;
- incomplete knowledge;
- privacy;
- consequences already in motion.

The user may intervene, observe, speak, help, harm, wait, ignore, intrude, repair or leave.

The world and persona remain independently causal.

## 2. Primary interaction grammar

Three high-level user verbs are sufficient as the current hypothesis:

- `SAY`
- `DO`
- `ATTEND`

Natural language remains open-ended.

The user controls attempted action and presentation, not:
- feasibility;
- interruption;
- success;
- detection;
- reception;
- meaning;
- consequence.

## 3. Epistemic architecture

The engine may know the full admitted world/history.
Each actor knows only their lawful perception/memory/model.
The user knows only their lawful perception/history.
The narrator exposes only the user's phenomenal surface.

No direct mind access.

No emotional labels merely because the engine knows the emotion.

No narrator-parent explaining what the person “really means.”

The player must infer.

## 4. Temporal architecture

Turns are interface boundaries, not world-time quanta.

Actions consume variable continuous simulated time.

Observation consumes time.

Different observational depths consume different time.

The world continues during observation.

Exact duration is ordinarily not pre-announced.

Temporal knowledge itself is access-controlled:
- visible clock;
- watch/phone;
- another actor's speech;
- rough felt passage;
- otherwise uncertainty.

## 5. Attention as gameplay

ATTEND has candidate dimensions:
- target;
- depth;
- presentation;
- divided attention;
- persistence.

Higher perceptual resolution may require:
- more time;
- closer position;
- more obvious gaze;
- touching/moving an object;
- abandoning another activity.

Thus information acquisition has a situated price.

The core game becomes:

`FINITE ATTENTION + PARTIAL KNOWLEDGE + INDEPENDENT OTHER + CONTINUOUS CONSEQUENCE`

The player repeatedly decides what is worth knowing before action.

## 6. Reciprocal observation

The user's attention is embodied and potentially visible.

Other actors may:
- notice;
- fail to notice;
- misidentify the target of attention;
- falsely suspect;
- correctly infer;
- interpret the same behavior differently.

The user may attempt cover, sincerity, politeness, deception or casualness.

They cannot dictate that the performance succeeds.

## 7. Non-curatorial environment

Environmental details exist because the world caused them to exist, not because the narrator planted clues.

Bills, menus, magnets, school schedules, food, furniture, wear, handwriting and object placement can all become evidence.

The narrator does not preferentially surface what hidden system state knows is psychologically important.

The world may be meaningful before the user understands it.

## 8. Binding reality

User mention does not instantiate fact.

Narrated environmental fact must already have been lawfully retrieved/completed and canonically admitted.

`USER SUGGESTION ≠ WORLD FACT`

`ADMITTED NARRATED FACT = PERSISTENT WORLD FACT`

Leading questions must not summon reality.

## 9. Intention / impact / attribution

Rawlsing preserves separately:
- user intention;
- attempted action;
- realized event;
- immediate objective effect;
- actor-specific perception;
- actor-specific attribution;
- immediate response;
- relationship effect;
- flourishing/health effect;
- delayed network/world consequence;
- later user knowledge.

Consequences can exist without feedback.

Good intent can have bad impact.
Bad reception can coexist with beneficial impact.
Attachment can rise while flourishing falls.
Benefit can occur without recognition.

## 10. Pattern and memory

One event need not become a durable judgment.

Events may remain:
- episodic;
- weakly salient;
- provisionally interpreted;
- unresolved.

Later events can reactivate and reconsolidate meaning.

The past occurrence stays fixed while its significance changes.

D001 must determine exact ownership and promotion through:
- durable V3;
- CAN-008;
- PFF;
- V2.

## 11. Inherited temporal-grain law

Do not invent generic “live V3.”

Existing architecture already distinguishes:
- SDTF/upstream structural-developmental transduction;
- durable/formative from-birth V3;
- CAN-008 person×event immediate response;
- PFF interaction-fast/session/user-opacity state;
- V2 expression.

Sorok must place new relational semantics through those owners before proposing anything new.

## 12. User-praxis implications

Potential longitudinal evidence includes not just what the user says/does but what they attend to:
- self-evaluative cues;
- practical burdens;
- status markers;
- ideological symbols;
- children/third parties;
- privacy boundaries;
- direct versus covert information acquisition;
- tolerance of uncertainty.

Any inference requires sufficient history and must remain behavioral rather than diagnostic.

## 13. Difficulty / accessibility hypothesis

Potential future modes may preserve identical world/person causality while changing epistemic scaffolding.

`RAWlsing` pole:
- minimal assistance;
- no consequence preview;
- no psychological labels;
- no exact time-cost UI;
- no clue highlighting.

`Rawlsing` assisted pole:
- humane memory/commitment scaffolding;
- approximate action-depth cues;
- accessible clock where lawful;
- resurfacing only already-learned facts.

Assistance never supplies hidden mind-state or future consequences.

## 14. Anti-completion law

There is no True Nadia clue set.

No 100-percent observation state.

A user may know:
- some domains deeply;
- others badly;
- some facts never;
- some meanings only years later.

Successful intimacy increases model fidelity without eliminating otherness.
