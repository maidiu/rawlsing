## 2. Core principle — overwrite in routing, never overwrite history

A later self-retcon may become experientially dominant.

But it may not erase:
- objective past;
- prior framework history;
- prior framework causal effects;
- contradictory middle-state records;
- counterevidence;
- other actors' memories;
- embodied/material residues.

Therefore:

> **SELF-RETCON MAY OVERWRITE ACCESS / ROUTING / NARRATION WITHOUT OVERWRITING CAUSAL HISTORY.**

Example:

At 23:
`FRAME A = "I have finally become my real liberated self."`

At 29:
`FRAME B = "That whole phase was embarrassing self-deception."`

If B becomes strongly internalized, Nadia may:
- retrieve mainly evidence that confirms B;
- reinterpret A-compatible memories under B;
- feel shame when A-linked artifacts appear;
- speak as though B were obviously the truth;
- lose spontaneous access to what A genuinely enabled.

But Rawlsing preserves:
- A as a historical mediator;
- A-caused actions;
- pleasures and relationships formed through A;
- counterevidence to B;
- unresolved tensions between A and B.

Thus the user may observe contradiction that Nadia cannot currently see.

## 3. Reality pressure

Do not define "reality" as the analyst's preferred interpretation.

Define local **reality pressure** as exact causal/evidentiary friction between an active framework and generated/admitted world/person history.

Candidate sources:
- failed prediction;
- repeated outcome inconsistent with framework expectations;
- another actor's resistant testimony;
- behavior inconsistent with self-description;
- material consequence;
- memory/canonical event that does not fit;
- tension between two active mediators;
- practice results;
- Horizon conflict;
- bodily limit.

Reality pressure does not itself dictate one correct interpretation.

It only means:
> something in admitted reality is not being cleanly accounted for by the current frame.

## 4. Framework response operators

When framework F encounters reality pressure P, do not force one integration path.

A governed update may produce one or more operations.

### R0 — HOLD_UNRESOLVED
Actor can tolerate the discrepancy without immediately resolving it.

Effects may include:
- active tension;
- uncertainty;
- later revisitation.

This is not automatically superior.

### R1 — ACCOMMODATE / INTEGRATE
Framework changes to admit previously excluded material.

Possible effects:
- increased complexity;
- narrower claims;
- preservation of counterevidence;
- successor framework containing residues of predecessor.

This can be generous/capacious but still incomplete.

### R2 — ASSIMILATE / APPROPRIATE
New material is reinterpreted so the existing framework remains substantially unchanged.

Examples:
- counterevidence becomes "proof" of the theory;
- contradictory behavior is absorbed as a special case;
- another person's objection is recoded as evidence of their blindness.

This can strengthen coherence while reducing reality contact.

### R3 — PARTITION / SEAL
Contradictory material remains causally present but is kept domain-isolated.

Examples:
- egalitarian politics + authoritarian family behavior;
- spiritual compassion + ruthless professional conduct.

Actor may sincerely experience no contradiction across partitions.

### R4 — DISCOUNT / IGNORE
Material fails to receive enough salience/credibility to alter the frame.

It may still:
- leave causal trace;
- accumulate tension;
- later become visible.

### R5 — EXTERNALIZE
Contradiction is located primarily in:
- enemy;
- institution;
- other person;
- corrupt world;
rather than self/frame.

This may sometimes be accurate.

It is not definitionally defensive or false.

### R6 — DESTABILIZE / CRACK
Framework loses binding/coherence without a successor being ready.

Possible outcomes:
- confusion;
- grief;
- openness;
- panic;
- nihilism;
- searching;
- reversion.

### R7 — TRANSFORM / REFRAME
A successor interpretive mediator reorganizes several prior frames/tensions into a new structure.

This may be:
- more capacious;
- more tyrannical;
- simply different.

Transformation itself carries no moral sign.

## 5. No single integration score

Do not create:
`ENLIGHTENMENT = 0..100`.

Do not create:
`INTEGRATION = GOOD`.

Instead represent several separable properties where governed evidence supports them.

Candidate dimensions/relations:

- **scope** — how many domains the framework claims;
- **binding** — how strongly actor organization recruits it;
- **visibility** — whether actor recognizes it as a frame;
- **aperture** — how readily contradictory material can enter evaluation;
- **appropriation pressure** — tendency to assimilate material into frame;
- **partitioning** — degree contradictory domains remain isolated;
- **counterevidence accessibility** — whether disconfirming material remains actor-retrievable;
- **revision permeability** — whether frame can change under pressure;
- **Horizon load** — whether frame bears/colonizes ultimate claims;
- **identity investment** — cost of frame revision to actor self-continuity;
- **relational dependence** — which people/institutions sustain or threaten the frame.

These should not necessarily be universal numeric scalars; they may be typed states/relations/predicates.

## 6. Capacious self-retcon

A more generous self-retcon is not "the true one."

Example C at 35 may:
- recover truths A saw;
- retain criticisms B saw;
- reduce shame;
- differentiate domains;
- restore previously inaccessible memories;
- tolerate ambiguity;
- stop requiring one total verdict on 23-year-old Nadia.

This may increase:
- aperture;
- counterevidence accessibility;
- framework plurality;
- compassion;
- revision permeability.

But C can still:
- omit facts;
- have blind spots;
- preserve flattering interpretations;
- understate harm;
- fail in unrelated domains.

Therefore:

> **MORE CAPACIOUS != COMPLETE.**

And:
> **GENEROSITY TOWARD PRIOR SELVES != FINAL TRUTH ABOUT PRIOR SELVES.**

## 7. Tyrannical self-retcon

A self-retcon can become more coherent while becoming more alienated from reality.

Candidate characteristics:
- broad scope;
- high binding;
- high identity investment;
- low aperture;
- high appropriation pressure;
- active reinterpretation of counterevidence;
- partitioning or externalization;
- Horizon colonization;
- inaccessible contradictory memories/meanings;
- suppression of predecessor-framework residues.

Externally visible contradictions may persist because:
- objective event history remains;
- other relationships encode different Nadia;
- practices diverge from declared frame;
- repeated outcomes resist prediction;
- dormant/active middle-state records coexist;
- tensions remain even when actor lacks access to them.

This is not necessarily pathology.

A powerful framework may also be necessary to break a prior domination.

Example:
a newly adopted feminist, religious, revolutionary, therapeutic or anti-abuse framework may correctly expose massive prior falsification while also:
- overextending into domains it explains poorly;
- brutalizing ambiguous memories;
- recoding every prior relationship through one lens;
- becoming temporarily necessary precisely because nuance would reopen intolerable capture.

Rawlsing must be capable of representing both:
- liberating causal power;
- new exclusions/alienations.

## 8. Reality contact is continuous pressure, not guaranteed correction

Every actor continues encountering:
- world;
- body;
- other persons;
- consequences;
- material constraints;
- memory;
- contradiction.

Therefore no interpretive frame can literally stop reality from acting.

But it can alter:
- what is noticed;
- how it is categorized;
- what gets remembered;
- whether contradiction is admitted;
- where blame/cause is assigned;
- which responses are available.

So:

> **REALITY IS INESCAPABLE AS CAUSAL PRESSURE; REALITY IS NOT INESCAPABLE AS CORRECT INTERPRETATION.**

This is the core non-enlightenment law.

A person can repeatedly butt against reality and become:
- more integrative;
- more defensive;
- more partitioned;
- more rigid;
- more confused;
- more transformed;
depending on history/capacity/framework/social support/Horizon and event sequence.

## 9. Intensification through repeated metabolism

The user's intuition that the person's mode of integrating/appropriating/ignoring reality can intensify is plausible and fits V3.

But do not encode a fixed "metabolic type."

Instead repeated framework-response history can create:
- practices;
- grooves;
- mediators;
- tensions;
- changed accessibility;
- changed expectation;
- changed aperture/binding/revision conditions.

Example:
repeated successful ASSIMILATE operations may:
- strengthen framework binding;
- make confirmatory routes easier;
- reduce access to counterevidence;
- make later cracks more costly.

Repeated HOLD_UNRESOLVED / ACCOMMODATE experiences may:
- establish practices of uncertainty tolerance;
- preserve plural frames;
- make revision less identity-threatening.

Repeated SEAL/PARTITION may:
- make domain switching stable;
- keep contradictions externally visible but internally disconnected.

These are historical products, not personality destiny.

## 14. Horizon and tyranny/generosity

Absolute Horizon may help distinguish why some frames become unusually powerful.

A finite self-frame can:
- remain one identity among others;
- carry a Horizon claim;
- colonize Horizon;
- be judged by Horizon.

### Tyrannical possibility
Framework F becomes equivalent to Truth/Good/Safety itself.

Then contradictory material threatens not one belief but the actor's ultimate ordering.

This can increase:
- appropriation;
- externalization;
- partition;
- sacrifice of other goods/relations to preserve F.

### Generative possibility
A Horizon criterion exceeds F.

Example:
"truth matters more than my self-image."

Then F can be judged by the value it once claimed to serve.

This can make self-critique possible without guaranteeing it.

Thus Horizon is not enlightenment.

It is the level at which sacrifice/order become intelligible.
