USER-SPECIFIC RENDERING != WORLD TRUTH

USER MODEL MAY CONDITION PHENOMENAL SAMPLING AND LEXICALIZATION; IT MAY NOT CONDITION CANONICAL REALITY.

ADAPTIVE NARRATION MAY PROJECT WITH THE USER; IT MAY NOT CONFIRM THE USER.

IMPORTED/CARRIED HABITUS

## USER

The user then added that the system should eventually alert the user to the fact that the reality they experience bends to observed patterns of attention.

The user challenged the competence problem in detail:
social performance that sounds knowledgeable is not identical to actual competence.

A person can:
- perform feints, gestures and implications that create an impression of breadth;
- deliberately scale those signals back if they expect to be tested or if they fear intimidating someone;
- invoke Italian Renaissance poets as a breadth-of-field marker without actually being able to discuss Renaissance poetry competently;
- invoke Aston Martins, truffle oil, Seychelles, astrology or quantum uncertainty because those references have been absorbed lexically/socially without implying actual domain mastery or lived milieu.

The user emphasized that such invocations can suggest proximity to a milieu that may not actually exist.

The user also affirmed that the adaptive narrator should encourage awareness of its own personalization because the user's encounter with an independent Nadia is strongly shaped by that perceptual lens.

## ASSISTANT

The user-model architecture was sharpened around a new distinction:

Rawlsing must model not only what the user seems to know, but what they are doing with signs of knowing.

Core noncollapse:
LEXICAL FAMILIARITY
!= RECOGNITION
!= SOCIAL-SIGNALING COMPETENCE
!= SUSTAINED DISCURSIVE COMPETENCE
!= PROCEDURAL / DOMAIN COMPETENCE
!= ORIGIN / MILIEU.

A user may correctly deploy a cultural reference for pragmatic/social effect without demonstrating deep knowledge.

What may be demonstrated is:
> this person knows what social-semantic work these references can do here.

That itself is a real capacity.

The system and Nadia must remain separate:
USER SOCIAL PERFORMANCE
→ NADIA PERCEIVES PERFORMANCE
→ NADIA FORMS HYPOTHESIS OF COMPETENCE.

Nadia's hypothesis may be wrong.

The system may retain more cautious provenance: correct reference deployed, sustained expertise unproven.

This opens lawful social consequences:
- Nadia overestimates the user;
- user knowingly allows the impression;
- later Nadia requests actual expertise;
- the gap is revealed;
- disillusionment, awkwardness, accusation or repair follows.

The event is not a failed skill check; it is history produced by social implication and misinference.

The adaptive narrator was also reframed as something that should eventually disclose:
> the phenomenal surface is partly the user's own history returned to them.

The mechanism is therefore not merely personalization but part of the veil/pedagogical project.

A symmetry principle was proposed:
NO PARTICIPANT IS EXHAUSTED BY ANOTHER PARTICIPANT'S MODEL OF THEM—INCLUDING THE PLAYER.

Thus:
USER != RAWLSING'S BEST MODEL OF USER
just as
NADIA != USER'S BEST MODEL OF NADIA.

## ASSISTANT

This was treated as potentially kernel-level.

The personalization mechanism contains its own contradiction:
the better Rawlsing becomes at rendering the world through the user's historically demonstrated perceptual field, the greater the danger that the user becomes enclosed inside that field.

The task is not to develop an exquisitely personalized interpretation of Nadia.

It is to become capable of encountering Nadia as someone whose reality continually exceeds the perceptual machinery through which the user encounters her.

Strong laws proposed:
- MORE PERSONALIZED PERCEPTION != GREATER ADEQUACY TO THE OTHER.
- THE LENS CAN DEEPEN FASTER THAN UNDERSTANDING.

Greater experience may create:
- greater knowledge;
- more refined error;
- a more coherent projection;
- better local prediction coupled with a bad global model;
- an insight that becomes a new blind spot.

Rawlsing should not guarantee correction by pedagogically arranging the right counterevidence.

The adaptive lens itself should become an object of attention.

Possible reflection:
> These descriptions have not been neutral. Rawlsing has been learning from what you repeatedly notice, name, pursue and leave alone.

Concrete behavior may be reflected without interpreting what it means.

A user-invoked lens perturbation may temporarily suppress personalization and resample the same lawful perceptual field:
> What might my usual attention be filtering out here?

This should not inject important counterevidence or hidden truth.

Nadia functions as the independent remainder:
the user's narrator may increasingly speak partly in their language while Nadia remains not-the-user.

A strong formulation was proposed:

> RAWLSING MAY LEARN THE USER'S WAY OF SEEING. IT MUST NEVER MAKE THE OTHER PERSON BECOME WHAT THAT WAY OF SEEING EXPECTS TO FIND.

And:

> THE USER SHOULD EVENTUALLY BECOME AWARE THAT THE PHENOMENAL SURFACE IS PARTLY THEIR OWN HISTORY RETURNED TO THEM.

The deeper formulation:
even what becomes available as “obvious reality” has a history.

Rawlsing operationalizes this not by granting a view from nowhere, but by teaching the user that there is no view from nowhere and confronting their view with another person who refuses to be exhausted by it.
