## 1. Objective

Process the full supplied conversation as evidence, sequentially, until no material contribution remains unadjudicated.

This is deliberately stricter than summarization.

A colorful example may encode a missing mechanism.

## 2. Unit of work

The mechanical coverage ledger divides the normalized transcript into contiguous line ranges.

For each range:

1. read the range in context;
2. identify every material contribution;
3. inspect the relevant current Sorok owners;
4. classify each contribution;
5. bank/correct/crosslink as required;
6. update that range's ledger record with exact dispositions and repository refs.

If a thought crosses a range boundary, read enough adjacent context to adjudicate it as one thought.

## 3. Required disposition vocabulary

Every material contribution must receive one primary disposition:

- `ALREADY_OWNED` — exact substance is already controlled by current repo authority;
- `BANKED_NEW` — materially useful and absent; banked during this harvest;
- `CORRECTION_REQUIRED` — transcript/user correction reveals current repo content is wrong or overclaimed;
- `SUPERSEDED` — once-useful idea was explicitly replaced by a later formulation;
- `OPEN_FRONTIER` — important but deliberately unresolved; ensure a question/frontier owns it;
- `NO_RETAINED_VALUE` — conversational scaffolding, joke, repetition, or abandoned speculation with no residual architectural/product/research value.

Do not use `NO_RETAINED_VALUE` merely because something is hard to classify.

## 4. Organism-level classification

Where useful, also classify the architectural relation:

- `DERIVE` — follows from a deeper already-owned necessity;
- `SEPARATE` — prevents an illicit collapse of distinct owners/concepts;
- `CONNECT` — establishes a causal/semantic bridge between existing organs;
- `TUNE` — exposes a balance/parameter rather than a new law;
- `FALSIFY` — hostile case that can break a candidate architecture/kernel;
- `PROMOTE` — evidence that a local rule belongs higher in the organism/spine.

Standing method:

> **MORE RULES != MORE ARCHITECTURE.**

If several findings are consequences of one deeper structural necessity, prefer compression/derivation over rule accretion.
