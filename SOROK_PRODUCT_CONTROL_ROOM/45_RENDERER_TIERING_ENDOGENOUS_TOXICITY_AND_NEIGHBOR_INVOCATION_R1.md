# Sorok — Renderer Tiering, Endogenous Toxicity, and Neighbor Invocation R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_REFINEMENT

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Three corrections

1. Call-B flesh/voice realization does not constitutionally require a premium Claude model such as Sonnet 5.5.
2. Anti-coercive product law must not sanitize genuinely toxic persona behavior.
3. Strongest current entry/invitation phrase:

> **Become neighbor to a life that could have been yours.**

---

## 1. Renderer requirement is functional, not model-tier prestige

The Call-B requirement is:
- excellent voice differentiation;
- strong adherence to stylistic/phenomenological constraints;
- suppression of generic assistant voice;
- natural dialogue/action rendering;
- disciplined use of admitted world/person facts;
- reliable structured sidecar emission for newly proposed particulars.

It is NOT:
- maximum reasoning benchmark performance;
- premium flagship model by default.

Therefore current preferred architecture is:

`CALL A: LOW-COST STRUCTURED PERSON×EVENT / CAUSAL TRANSDUCTION MODEL`
→ governed admission
→
`CALL B: LOWER-COST CLAUDE-FAMILY FLESH / VOICE RENDERER`

with model escalation only where benchmarked quality loss justifies it.

The exact renderer should be chosen by comparative evaluation across:
- voice fidelity;
- stylistic invisibility;
- constraint obedience;
- hallucinated-fact rate;
- structured-sidecar reliability;
- latency;
- cost.

Do not freeze Sonnet 5.5 merely because it is available.

---

## 2. Endogenous persona toxicity is lawful

Critical distinction:

`PRODUCT MANIPULATION`
!=
`PERSONA MANIPULATION`.

Rawlsing itself must not manufacture guilt/distress to increase engagement.

But a generated person may lawfully be:
- clingy;
- possessive;
- jealous;
- abandonment-sensitive;
- passive-aggressive;
- controlling;
- guilt-inducing;
- emotionally volatile;
- avoidant;
- punitive;
- manipulative;
- otherwise difficult or unhealthy in relation.

If those patterns are genuinely generated from the person's developmental history, current state and relationship dynamics, suppressing them would be persona falsification.

---

## 3. Example

If the user disappears for three days:

Persona A may barely notice.

Persona B may assume the user was busy.

Persona C may become worried.

Persona D may feel abandoned and send:

> `cool. guess you're alive.`

Persona E may send five increasingly dysregulated messages.

Persona F may decide not to contact the user again.

All may be lawful if generated from actor-specific person×relation×situation state.

The forbidden implementation is:

`USER INACTIVE 72 HOURS`
→ `SYSTEM SELECTS GUILT-INDUCING PERSONA RESPONSE TO MAXIMIZE RETURN`.

---

## 4. Notification layer must not amplify endogenous toxicity

If a persona lawfully sends manipulative or dysregulated messages, those messages are part of world history.

But the product notification layer must remain neutral.

Do not:
- resend the same message as escalating reminders;
- add platform copy such as `Nadia needs you`;
- prioritize toxic messages because they improve retention;
- manufacture typing/read indicators for urgency;
- increase notification frequency because persona is distressed.

User should retain ordinary mute/notification controls.

Thus:

> **Rawlsing may faithfully mediate a toxic person's behavior. It may not weaponize that behavior as product growth machinery.**

---

## 5. Toxicity is not a hidden trait label

Do not assign a generic `TOXIC_PERSONALITY=true` flag.

Relevant behavior should emerge from specific developed organization:
- attachment history;
- shame;
- expectations;
- dependency;
- control strategies;
- fear of abandonment;
- narcissistic injury where actually generated;
- current relational investment;
- learned conflict practices;
- material dependence;
- other exact causal history.

The same person may behave healthily in one relation and destructively in another.

---

## 6. Rawlsing does not protect the user from ordinary relational unpleasantness

The product thesis requires that personas can become genuinely unpleasant to be around.

User may encounter:
- unfair accusation;
- guilt trip;
- coldness;
- manipulation;
- unreasonable demand;
- refusal;
- resentment;
- escalating dependency;
- contempt;
- withdrawal.

These are not automatically safety failures.

Safety/product boundaries concern disallowed content and platform coercion, not sanitizing all difficult relational behavior.

---

## 7. Current strongest invocation

> **Become neighbor to a life that could have been yours.**

This formulation improves on:
- `meet your alternate self` — too identity-collapsing;
- `meet someone who received a life you could have received` — accurate but explanatory;
- `live beside the person it became` — useful but less compact.

`neighbor` as a verb carries:
- proximity without identity;
- relation without possession;
- difference without abstraction;
- possible care/obligation without guaranteed affection;
- the user's actual role in the product.
