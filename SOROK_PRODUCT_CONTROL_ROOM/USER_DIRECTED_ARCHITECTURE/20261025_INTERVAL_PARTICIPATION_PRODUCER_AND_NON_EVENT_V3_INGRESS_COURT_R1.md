The missing distinction has now been physically modeled as a deterministic two-part interface, in explicitly synthetic form:

1. A **coarse-grain activity-production evaluator** consumes independently typed actor conduct and world-performance receipts for one or more periods spanning the same life interval. It emits a **positive, bounded, actor-specific participation-state receipt** only where both actual actor activity and world performance are affirmed. A schedule, household title, obligation, actor intention or narrated recurrence is insufficient.
2. A **non-event conditioning interface** validates that receipt, its source/actor/interval identity and its contents, then delivers a source-addressable world/participation condition to a putative V3 consumer. It does not create discrete daily events, actor beliefs, memories, emotions, CAN008 outputs or CAN011 updates. A separate deliberately synthetic candidate evaluator can inspect it alongside exactly four fixed episodes and explicit actor uptake, but only issue a **candidate**, never mutate V3.

## 2. Physical sourcefree model — exact contracts and distinctive safeguards

Files in this package: `sorok_interval_witness_lab.py`, `test_sorok_interval_witness_lab.py` (inherited), `sorok_interval_participation_producer_r1.py`, `test_sorok_interval_participation_producer_r1.py` (new), and machine-readable receipts.

- `WorldRegime`: actor, household, activity scope, interval, obligation flag, schedule/ref and source rule. Establishes context/permission only, not actual performance.
- `EpochOutcome`: coarse-time actor participation receipt, independent world-performance receipt, source owner, actor, household, scope, effective interval, currentness and supersession. The synthetic prototype models two periods inside ages 8–10. This is NOT two years of detailed individual actions; it asserts the aggregate-period participation as an ACTUAL generated state only upon separate evidence of performance.
- `qualify_epoch`: positive realized recurrent participation requires actual `ACTED` or `RESISTED_BUT_ACTED` conduct, `CONFIRMED` external-world performance, both source receipts, valid source owner/time/scope and non-expired evidence. Lack of actual action or independent confirmation yields UNKNOWN. An explicit both-refused/not-performed outcome can yield SOURCE-OWNED NEGATIVE. Mismatched conduct/performance is refused, not resolved by LLM prose.
- `realize_interval`: merges only the independently qualified positive periods into bounded objective participation `coverage`; preserves each period's evidence and exact receipt hash. It fabricates **zero** daily events and emits no psyche state. Partially attested intervals retain gaps.
- `non_event_v3_conditioning`: checks exact producer identity, actor/household/scope/time, report/epoch content consistency and deterministic content hash; emits a CONDITION receipt referencing the independently produced interval. Actor receivability or first-person experience is **not inferred**, and neither CAN008 response nor CAN011 change is applied.
- `diagnostic_candidate`: only for comparison with old 24-test witness court, emits a target-specific **synthetic** `SCOPED_INTERPRETIVE_ORIENTING` candidate when it has complete positive interval evidence and two stipulated received/uptaken episode refs; all native persistence fields remain false. This is a purposefully artificial local discriminator, not an endorsed theory of how childcare develops an ethic, skill, or orientation.

**Important further limitation:** even a hashed receipt is not a competent signature by itself. Authority comes from the actual source-owning machine and valid rule, not from the SHA-256 digest. The local prototype trusts that its input source actor/world conduct receipts were produced by test-only owners. A native consumer must validate their authentic issuer/registry/capabilities and cannot accept caller-forged fields just because rehashing succeeds.

## 4. Causal firewalls / negative law

- Objective *role* is not *participation*. Actual participation is not an automatically acquired V3 practice or belief.
- World-generated two-year activity fact need not consist of hundreds of exposed daily events. The machine must nevertheless represent a competent aggregate-period realization process, not a narrator writing "often".
- Exogenous imposed participation is not identified with willing adoption; the actor's different uptake matters.
- School conflicts and time allocation remain actual worldly constraints even if no learned practice forms.
- A person may retain contradictory love and resentment toward caretaking; no scalar loyal/resentful winner is necessary.
- An actual event may produce a singular transformation without two-year recurrence if a separate legitimate rule warrants it; a two-year role may produce no CAN011 transition.
- The non-event input MAY trigger a later governed slow or actor-local processing rule; it does not itself claim subjective meaning or update persistent state.
- The user-endorsed ONE LLM call per ordinary event still outputs retrospective self-interpretation proposal and prospective possible next reality; any qualified interpretive influence is computed BEFORE next-event realization. Interval-scale slow process must respect ADP021 order and not create mandatory filler calls.

Emits a source-owned aggregate actual participation receipt, with current valid-time, continuation/termination and actual actor performance proof, not just a certificate accepted from arbitrary input.
