## 1. The actual object: scientifically guided artistic/computational facsimile

Rawlsing is meant to create a **hyper-compressed facsimile of a socially constituted, historically developing, internally contradictory human Other**. It does not attempt a perfect naturalistic reconstruction of a real human lifetime, nor can it do so under present compute, latency, storage, monetary and scientific/LLM capability limits. The science **guides and constrains** generative hypotheses; the constructive/representational model necessarily uses compression, selection, extrapolation and artistic design. Production should never claim that compression is empirically exact causal inference.

The objective is an encounter that feels palpably *other*: situated, opaque, historically formed, contradictory, socially and materially embedded, capable of individuality and surprises not reducible to milieu tags, and internally far richer than the immediately visible exchanges.

Maintain representative fidelity to **habitus and milieu without absolute social determinism**, and meaningful human variation in the same conditions. Source structure constrains possibilities, but does not mechanically select every motive, action or interpretation.

## 2. Developmental event scarcity is an OPERATING constraint, not an accident

The model cannot process hundreds or thousands of lived episodes per year. The settled 3–5-event N/N+1 batching is a sparse sample of a much denser lived developmental process. Therefore selected episodes must carry **concentrated historical and formative work**. An individual Rawlsing event may stand in for a recurring relation, prolonged institutional exposure, a gradually accumulated contradiction, the consequences of practical activity, or a high-leverage inflection that would in reality be mediated by many repetitions and intervening experiences.

Do **not** require that one selected event have the same small developmental impact as a single randomly sampled natural event. That would make sparse simulation psychologically inert. Equally, do **not** multiply all changes by a universal “100x event weight” or assume trauma/rupture at every turn; compression must remain situated, mechanism-sensitive, socially plausible and heterogeneous.

**Scarcity rule:** almost every selected event should be *meaningfully relevant to the actor's continuing life*—formative, revealing, consolidating, constraining, complicating, preparing, reinterpreting or actualizing some historically situated causal/relational possibility. “Formative” is the broad artistic/developmental criterion, NOT synonymous with an obligatory durable CAN-011 mutation.

A result that leaves a target psychological field unchanged can still be consequential through another actor's action, altered material opportunity, reinforced dependence, an unresolved conflict, changed relational exposure, new practical knowledge, or the dramatic demonstration of the actor's previously established organization. A lawful no-change evaluation remains distinct from MISSING_RULE, just as before.

## 3. Human opacity in at least two registers

1. **The simulated actor is not transparent to itself.** Its objective history, causal organization, active/inhibited dispositions, motives, tensions and acquired practices need not be fully known, verbalizable or accurately interpreted by the actor. ADP-016/017 and actor access continue to govern memory and claims. The LLM must not receive privileged psychic explanations as the person's own first-person knowledge merely because an underlying engine stores them.
2. **The human user is not transparent to the actor.** The user receives a situated expression/interaction surface, not a diagnostic dashboard or personality type listing. The actor has its own constrained perceptions, limited expectations of the user and independent practical concerns.
3. **The actor is not transparent to the human user.** Sparse expressed conduct should emerge as an asymmetric, often misleading projection of a deeper historical organization. The user may perceive coherence, boredom, contradiction, or strangeness depending upon their own standpoint and shared milieu, not only a person-intrinsic “interestingness” setting.

The externally ordinary church member, diligent worker or agreeable neighbor may have intense contradictions, repressed conflicts, authoritarian internal rules, eschatological explanations and fragile organizing commitments beneath routine conformity. None of this should be imposed as universal hidden pathology. Conversely a highly integrated actor may appear verbally chaotic or unintelligible from an outsider's categories. **Hegemonic familiarity changes how legible a person appears**, and a changed interlocutor or angle can expose a rich underlying differentiation.

Test against flattening ordinary conformity into “boring/NPC,” and against making every actor conspicuously eccentric or theatrically traumatized.

## 4. Engineering implications

- Prioritize **sparse-event representative selection** and **background-period condensation**: identify what larger trajectory an admitted event samples or crystallizes and retain enough causal provenance not to treat a montage as 100 literal independent lived acts.
- Represent **low-visibility accumulation and latent or conflicting consequences** without demanding overt new exposition after every event. A selection can advance world/relationship/projects, reveal standing organization, or change later relevance without forcing a psychological state update.
- Keep distinct **objective world occurrence**, **actual lived participation**, **actor psychological state**, **actor's self-understanding**, and **user-visible expression**. Compression cannot be permission to collapse them.
- Allow **disproportionately formative events** under bounded, historically meaningful conditions. The scientific question is whether the hypothesized mechanism is reasonably guided and internally coherent at the selected scale—not whether each single event has a separately proven naturalistic effect size.
- Avoid **narrative forced drama**: no universal adversity→wound, exposure→habit, conflict→rupture, confession→self-knowledge, elapsed time→healing, or class label→character mapping.
- Preserve **path dependence, discontinuity, contradictions, social relation, and variable context sensitivity**: similar environments can yield differentiable actors; the same actor may present differently across friends, employers, family, institutions, and differing user milieus.
- Account for **compute/storage/latency/cost** as first-class limits: do not propose hidden exhaustive microevent simulation or research perfection as a prerequisite for viable software. Prefer credible compressed dynamics, evidence-graded hypotheses, reusable algorithms and measured resource use.
- Renderer success is not source-truth narration or maximal exposition. Its objective is **believable situated opacity**, selective revelation, internally and historically grounded tension, and the possibility of encountering someone who is not simply a mirror of user expectations.

## 5. Science is a guide, not an impossible universal acceptance burden

CAN-008, CAN-010/011, ADP-016/017 and the original O05/O07/O15 distinctions still matter for code custody and for preventing implausible invented laws. But **do not convert original-source native acceptance requirements into a claim that no bounded synthetic/personality construction can proceed until a field-science experiment justifies every compressed event effect**. Distinguish:
- Original/native scientifically qualified model and exact permission.
- Plausible **explicitly hypothetical, bounded, test-only** generative transitions and their artistic/calibration assumptions.
- Product-level judged human verisimilitude, historical/milieu fidelity and observed interpersonal opacity.
These require different labels, evidence and review; none automatically grants another.

Retain all original native permission holds and negative evidence. Do not alter CAN/ADP acceptance semantics just to make an expressive simulation run. Equally, do not define “scientifically guided” as “only effects that could be asserted from naturalistic causal identification of a single episode.”
