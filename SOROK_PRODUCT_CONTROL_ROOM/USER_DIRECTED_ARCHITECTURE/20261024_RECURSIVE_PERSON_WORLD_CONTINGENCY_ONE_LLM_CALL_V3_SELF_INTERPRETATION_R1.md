## 1. Governing conceptual judgment: historically conditioned, contingent, recursively self-producing life

Rawlsing's intended product is not a static personality profile generating stylized replies, a deterministic disposition score machine, a reinforcement learner with a general positive/negative valence, or an omniscient LLM novelist making up character causes after selecting desirable outcomes.

An actor's prior life conditions present perception, practical capacities, relations, commitments, projects, embodied state, contradictions and available means, but does not necessarily exhaust the possible ways present activity can develop. A situated action realizes one possibility; other independent agents and the material world act without being reducible to that actor; the resulting event reconditions future activity. Actor interpretation and self-interpretation are themselves activities and potentially consequential parts of further history.

The user describes **recursive self-reification**: a person is partly produced and reproduced through the activity they carry out and through the material/social consequences of that activity. An act may reproduce an established orientation, contradict it, reorganize it, develop a new possibility, or produce no qualified durable CAN011 change. Recursive reproduction is neither automatic repetition-strengthening nor obligatory moral/psychological improvement.

**Key test of historical intelligibility:** A later action may surprise the observer while remaining intelligible through actual prior history and present contingencies. Cause must be generated forward and recorded, not invented backward to justify a preselected literary outcome. Continuity is NOT global consistency; development is NOT convergence on coherence, virtue, competence, a single dominant worldview, or a net psychological score.

## 2. The situation/meaning boundary comes BEFORE an abstract choice ranking

"Someone offers hungry Nadia food: accept or refuse?" is not yet a sufficiently constituted human encounter. The offer's social significance depends on who offers it, relationship history, current conflict, expected obligations, actor-accessible cues, embodied need, practical purposes, and what Nadia makes of the interaction. Her mother offering food immediately after a quarrel differs from an uninterested stranger or a romantically interested man. An actor may accept the food while refusing the apparent relational obligation; may reject what she considers manipulation while recognizing material need; may not notice or may misread intent. Do not collapse material acceptance into emotional reconciliation, consent, gratitude, debt or learned skill.

Likewise, a choice between a loyal old friend's tame birthday party and a novel exciting club night may involve incompatible but coexisting projects. Nadia may attend one or both, avoid the framing, hesitate, create a new compromise, refuse the forced choice, or discover a new purpose through reflexivity. The actor's **available action grammar and conceptual framing are themselves situated, historically constituted and possibly generative**. Do not start with a fixed universal menu and merely rank it by default numeric traits. Explicit conscious Sartrean deliberation is a special case, not a requirement of everyday activity. Immediate habit and practical conduct, nonconscious causal recruitment, partial later self-explanation and self-misrecognition all remain possible.

An unresolved action often signals insufficiently generated relational/event detail or insufficient specification of actual actor capacities and concept formation. First inspect this causal underconstitution; do NOT reach immediately for uniform random tie-break, forced persona silence, or default refusal. This does not logically require metaphysical hard determinism: with a richly constituted encounter, more than one genuine contingent possibility may remain. A transparent, authorized synthetic selection policy may realize one; it is a model-choice, not a validated human response frequency.

## 3. Generate candidate REALITIES for the world, not pre-scripted psychological outcomes

A pre-event/prospective LLM can generate multiple concrete possible realities for a birthday gathering: particular arrivals, interactions, interruptions, remarks, games, social developments, accidents, and independently situated activities. It SHOULD NOT merely label branches "Nadia has a good night", "Nadia feels grateful", "friendship strengthened", or "she learned wholesome fun". Such labels precompute the outcome in the renderer and evade V3.

Example possibilities after Nadia chooses to attend:
- A: An old acquaintance arrives, remembers something important about Nadia, and an unexpectedly absorbing conversation occurs. Potential recognition, belonging, novelty, intimacy or ambivalence are NOT yet her final response.
- B: The evening is dull to Nadia, but the birthday friend directly expresses gratitude and past anxiety that she might not attend. Potential loyalty, irritation, obligation, reciprocal care, guilt or pride are NOT pre-resolved.
- C: The friend makes a cutting remark about Nadia's recent lifestyle; other guests laugh, perhaps without malice. Potential social exclusion, defiance, shame, anger, humor or indifference depend on the historically formed actor and actual reception.

One eligible reality is realized under a declared causal/world/agent mechanism, not according to which story makes for the neatest character arc. The realized facts and their independent actor ownership, time, access and source identity become immutable objective history. Other candidates remain unrealized alternatives/counterfactual models, not experiences falsely inserted into Nadia's memory. Nadia cannot regret a rejected club invitation she did not know existed.

Probabilities belong to exact **candidate events/conditional world distributions** only when a lawful model actually supplies their interpretation and parameters. A machine-observed stimulus is not necessarily a Nadia-received stimulus. Another person's motive is not her knowledge of that motive.

These potential loadings are NOT an automatic +10 loyalty / -5 adventure / +7 self-respect psychological update. They cannot be smuggled in as already-effective CAN011 state. A prospective LLM must not declare an acquired skill, a newly durable practice, an internal psychological response, a relationship reconciliation, a motive or a new belief by attaching a tag to an external event.

## 8. EXACT ONE-CALL-PER-TURN temporal architecture — user-endorsed requirement

For every ordinary event/decision turn after bootstrapping, **ONE LLM invocation does TWO distinct operations**:
(A) RETROSPECTIVE: give narrative flesh to the preceding already-realized world event and V3's first-pass organismic response, and propose Nadia's accessible self-interpretation of that experience, which may itself become causally consequential;
(B) PROSPECTIVE: generate several feasible concrete next-world candidate realities, with possible event-side pressures/attractions, explicit conditional guards, source/actor ownership, without predetermining Nadia's psychological response.

**These are two separately typed products of ONE call, not two calls.** The former must be processed computationally BEFORE the latter may be realized and processed. The user expressly corrected a previous proposed two-LLM-call conceptualization.

Mandatory temporal sequence:

0. Bootstrap: an original lawful root/world and first admissible event plan exist; initial proposal requires separately acknowledged bootstrap conditions.
1. World/event T has been selected, independently admitted/performed and recorded (or a typed unresolved/refusal, with no forged event).
2. Live V3 FIRST PASS consumes actual event T and its admitted actor-access/event loadings, computes coupled current response and any appropriately justified changes. This is not an LLM story.
3. ONE LLM call, using a tightly scoped causal and actor-access readset, returns two separate products:
   - R_T retrospective outward rendering and **proposed actor self-interpretation** of T plus its computed experienced effects; may be absent/ambiguous;
   - P_T+1 prospective **possible world realities** for the next episode, with actual candidate event structure and potential loadings, source/owner/feasibility/conditional guards; NO determined person-outcome.
4. Interpretive owner checks R_T. If actor self-interpretation is admitted, **V3 SECOND PASS computes its pressure/attraction/recruitment/developmental effects against the latest state**. No new event T+1 has yet been computed. Separate conditional durability from transient response and memory.
5. Freeze Nadia/world state after interpretation as S_T+ (the next event's actual predecessor). Validate P_T+1 **against this post-interpretation state**, not the pre-self-interpretation state; evaluate conditional guards, source authorization, material constraints and independently acting other-agent state.
6. Explicit event/world/actor policy selects/realizes one eligible P_T+1 (or typed refusal/defer). Commit independent performance, actor access and occurrence history; no LLM secretly fixes a failed candidate.
7. Live V3 FIRST PASS processes event T+1 using S_T+ (and the actual event/other-world changes). Then the next single LLM call is invoked, completing the rolling loop.

**No temporal leak:** because the LLM produced R_T and P_T+1 in the same invocation, prospective candidates cannot falsely presuppose that R_T caused a particular V3 transition. P_T+1 must be valid over possible authorized interpretations, explicitly guard its applicability, or fail closed and await the next ordinary opportunity. A later revalidation does not require another LLM call. Do NOT secretly rewrite P_T+1 or retrospectively edit R_T to force coherence. Correct strict order: RETROSPECTIVE ADMISSION → SECOND V3 COMPUTATION → NEXT EVENT QUALIFICATION/REALIZATION → FIRST V3 COMPUTATION OF NEXT EVENT. No assumption that every loop requires a long conscious reflective monologue.

**Call budget caveat:** one call is a design target for normal turns, not a claim that the currently implemented S44 makes LLM calls (it makes zero) or that every malformed/denied candidate can be rescued synchronously. Preserve typed invalid/no-event cases, validity and continuity over forced activity.
