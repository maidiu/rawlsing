## 0. Answer / verdict

**A sparse childhood can be represented causally by the same V3 person experiencing (a) persistent, externally validated arrangements and organized activity and (b) a handful of directly realized episodes. Neither the event count nor an LLM's descriptive labels determines psychological formation.**

The minimal missing bridge is **not a new psyche or a stochastic "practice strength"**. It is a testable and owner-valid chain:

1. A competent world/household/work/institution/actor-activity owner establishes what ongoing obligations, affordances, and participation actually exist over a bounded time interval, distinct from merely scheduled or possible activity.
2. SDTF-like P1–P5 **objective** actor-relative consequences can be derived under valid governed mechanism rules and CURRENT, type-compatible realization witnesses; SDTF D1′ is still a candidate rather than native law.
3. A *non-event conditioning interface* allows the continuing activity/resource/relation state to remain causally available to V3 across the interval **without** minting fake daily events or treating background social position as an actor's psyche. This is the existing OBL062 open seam, not a new SDTF primitive.
4. Realized spotlight events and actor-specific CAN008 uptake interact with that continuing ecology. Independent performance and other-agent responses are recorded through their own owners.
5. A concrete CAN011 target-specific rule may qualify a later persistent mediator/practice/tension, at an actual commit time, from appropriately witnessed participation and response. If no such rule exists, the lawful result is `NO_CHANGE`/not-issued, not a fake character update.
6. ADP021 may schedule an authorized slow-process invocation or interval integration; it supplies **when**, not learning semantics, process authority, or an automatic two-year cadence.

**Vital separation:** a recurring **assignment** is a P2 obligation; a recurring **schedule/task organization** is a P3 objective condition; **actual recurring participation** additionally requires a competent participation/performance witness. None individually proves a V3 practice or a feeling of obligation.

## 2. Expert comparison: life course is more than noteworthy events

- **Glen Elder** distinguishes extended *trajectories* from shorter *transitions* and locates development in historical time/place, interdependent lives, timing and agency. A school/care conflict matters differently because of where it occurs in an ongoing life, not because of a universal event weight. (Elder, “Perspectives on the Life Course”: https://faculty.washington.edu/matsueda/courses/401D/Readings/Elder.pdf ; Elder 1998: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8624.1998.tb06128.x.)
- **Jeylan Mortimer's Youth Development Study** links characteristics of experienced adolescent work to longer work trajectories, distinguishing work *quality*, opportunities, hours and aspirations rather than employment as a generic dose. This supports treating time/activity ecology as causally important, **not** the fiction that its measured correlations supply transition coefficients for Nadia. (Mortimer 2012: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3404601/ ; Mortimer et al 2024: https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12915.)
- **Leontiev / Sève** via existing SDTF theory: organized activity and actually enacted operations reproduce and alter capacities, while motive, goal, operation and socially organized activity conditions must be distinguished. Time allocated to one activity can condition further capacities/opportunities.
- **Dreier**: participation across locations and roles is not a sequence of isolated laboratory tasks. Different places organize possibilities, constraints and agency, and capacities may be socially distributed.
- **Lahire**: dispositions are heterogeneous and contextually triggered/inhibited; sustained care responsibility may generate resentment, skill, conformity, resistance or simultaneous alternatives. A role never means automatically "responsible personality".
- **Elder's linked lives and SDTF's actor/world firewall:** one person's care role is partly constituted by what other people do and by historically specific institutions, but their objective acts never authorize Nadia's private thoughts directly.

**Theoretical synthesis:** simulated *trajectories of participation* (ongoing time-bound practices/conditions), selected *events and transitions*, and V3 actor-specific activity/development are three different causal grains. No standalone “habitus score,” “role dose,” or “scene importance coefficient” is justified.

## 3. Minimal time-span participation witness: three different proofs

We need **three** separately typed things, not one `habitual = true` flag:

**W1. Objective durative regime:** exact world/time/owner, actor/counterpart/institution refs, social relation or legal obligation, task content, effective span, exclusions/gaps, possible sanctions/material supports, supersession/termination references, and source/rule identity. A role can be assigned without action.

**W2. Performed-participation evidence:** owner-qualified positive attestations that an actor *actually participated* in a recurrent activity over a bound span (or segments), with temporal coverage rule, support/counterpart performance, and any explicit limits/interruptions. This can be a legitimate **aggregate state/interval witness**: it need not enumerate every afternoon. But somebody must have causal authority to issue it. A schedule, a demographic label, a sourcebook assertion, or a single scene cannot issue this witness automatically. The aggregate may not invent her unrecorded words, choices, private emotion, or exact count of repetitions.

**W3. Actor-side experience and transformation:** specific source-eligible actor reception/uptake, demonstrated means of handling the task, contradictions, interpretations, or activities. The first two world/social receipts do NOT automatically issue W3. They can condition its rule inputs, alongside spotlighted episode responses and prior state.

The state distinction most worth defending is:

    ACTIVE_ROLE / PARTICIPATION_EXPECTATION

An interval witness's *coverage* may be interval-level: the external owner explicitly attests a recurring pattern over that period. Coverage need not mean uninterrupted minute-by-minute performance. In actual implementation the registered recurrence rule must declare the meaning of coverage and tolerable gaps. The TEST_ONLY lab below uses synthetic full-interval certification solely to isolate the boundary; it is **not a native standard for recurrency**.

### Who can issue W2 in a compressed simulation?

Propose an **interval-scale world/actor activity realization owner** (reuse existing C4/C8/C9/world activity owner if available), which can admit a bounded *aggregate activity participation fact* as a single causal artifact, conditioned by: prior world regime, actor's lawful means/response, actual counterpart demands, material resources, time competition, interruptions, and explicit test-only or issued activity-transition policy. It must NOT simply extrapolate the LLM's claim that Nadia "often did care work" into objective performed history.

## 4. Non-event ingress: the exact outstanding OBL062 seam

A recurring regime is not itself a CAN008 discrete shock; inventing a daily `care duty occurred` event would defeat the purpose. We need a target-bound **conditioning view** that includes a current objective activity-time or obligation fact with exact owner/time/provenance/actor scope, offered to CAN008 or another competent V3 response/slow-process rule as *context* rather than as a fictional encountered action.

A clean interface would distinguish:

- `WORLD_CONDITION_CURRENT`: externally established obligation/resource/access, permitted to constrain feasibility and opportunity during the interval;
- `ACTOR_PARTICIPATION_TO_DATE`: source-qualified actual performed recurrent activity, with time-limited coverage and gaps;
- `ACTOR_UPTAKE_NOW`: separately established reception and current meaning (CAN008/other rule), perhaps entirely absent;
- `SLOW_INTEGRATION_CANDIDATE`: an explicit, rule-bound interval evaluation consuming the first three as required and creating a candidate owner-target effect.

When a recurring regime changes, only future conditioning/requery behavior changes; historical source and already performed event identities remain immutable. Asynchronous scheduling and chronology must ensure a T+1 event uses the current state after admitted T retrospective interpretation. An interval boundary does not automatically generate an LLM call or a character milestone.

**ADP021 does not solve this by scheduling alone**: its slow-process binding requires one process semantic authority, separate cadence authority, prestate and rule refs. If missing, the correct status is `SLOW_PROCESS_UNGOVERNED`.

## 5. Nadia ages 8–10 — same four events, different ongoing lives

Set the same actual spotlight episodes in each trial:

1. Nadia performs sibling care one afternoon.
2. Care responsibility conflicts with a school opportunity.
3. Nadia encounters a distinction or instruction about negotiating care arrangements.
4. Nadia attempts a different arrangement, with outcomes owned by the independent participants.

**Life A:** separately generated and current owner-valid evidence of an ongoing sibling-care activity across the interval, with actual participation independently attested at aggregate life-interval grain. Objective P2 obligation and P3 performed participation are distinguishable. Nadia's actor uptake of conflict and attempted negotiation is separately witnessed. A test-only formation rule can emit *a candidate scoped interpretive mediator* around care-negotiability, NOT an automatic native learning claim.

**Life B:** exactly the same four episodes but no competent evidence of recurrent care participation beyond the sampled occurrence(s). Her caregiving might have been exceptional. The specifically **durative** formation rule cannot issue the same interval-participation warrant. A separate, independently justified **singular-event** formation rule could still support a formation candidate if that different mechanism is specified and proven. Not having a recurrence witness is UNKNOWN, not objective nonparticipation.

**Life C (negative control):** a parent assigned recurring care, and household schedule records say Nadia was supposed to provide it, but there is no admissible evidence that she actually did. This may constrain her possibilities and shape school conflicts, but it cannot automatically qualify practiced recurrent caregiving. She may have refused, delegated, been prevented or complied; without warrant those remain unresolved.

**Life D (partial):** participation is validly attested for only part of two years, with a documented interruption/unknown period. The model must not stretch a one-year participation witness across two years. The target rule may still legally use the narrower span if its own requirements allow it; no global all-or-nothing learning threshold is inferred.

**Life E (singular):** despite no recurrent care, a qualified exceptional encounter reorganizes Nadia's interpretation. Its potential formative effect must remain representable under a different, specific singular mechanism. Sparse-life compression cannot be reduced to durative repetition either.

### Expected distinctions

- All scenarios may have the same explicit four event identities and count. Naive `number_of_events >= k` returns the same answer, therefore cannot identify history-of-care effects.
- Multiplying an event's effect by months/years can manufacture any desired intensity without proving actual participation or a lawful CAN011 transition.
- A/B can differ in the *available evidence and eligibility for a test-only specific formation rule* even with identical episode lists.
- A valid continuing objective role may constrain actual time/resources in B/C as well; those consequences should not be mistaken for identical learned psychological practices.
- An unwitnessed private feeling across the intervening months remains unwitnessed. Causal effects may arise through owner-valid mechanisms, never fabricated inner monologues.
