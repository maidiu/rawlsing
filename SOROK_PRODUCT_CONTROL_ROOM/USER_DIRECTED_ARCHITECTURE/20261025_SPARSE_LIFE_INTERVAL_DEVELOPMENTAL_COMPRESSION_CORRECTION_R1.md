## 1. Direct user correction

Rawlsing does **not** simulate thirty years of continuous lived events. It develops the preadult person through several recursions at successive life stages, separated roughly by a couple of years and generating around **3–5 concrete LLM-proposed, Rawlsing-processed events per interval**, all within an ongoing historically established habitus.

**One represented event may legitimately be formative where real life might require many repetitions.** Otherwise real character formation would be impossible at the intended sparse resolution. The simulation must distinguish **actual modeled historical duration and causal organization** from the number of explicitly narrated/witnessed events. This is not permission to make any scene's self-description into a psychological change.

## 2. Two resolutions over one canonical person and history

**Continuing life-interval ecology:** independently sourced and time-scoped household and kinship roles, material dependence, recurring caregiving, work and school schedules, institutions, peers, tools and linguistic practices; the actor's actual participation and counterparty effects must have appropriate owners and valid-time bounds. These do not directly produce learned psychological traits.

**Sparse spotlight episodes:** three to five specific independently realized events that may be ordinary, representative of independently established recurrent activity, a culmination of prior contradictions, or a genuinely singular formative occurrence. These characterizations are *candidate evidentiary roles*, not four new canonical event kinds and not learning commands. One spotlight occurrence does not prove an entire pattern of activity by itself.

**Interval-level integration:** where an independently qualified slow process or target-specific CAN011 rule exists, it may consume the actual span of activity, the actor's historically formed state, and genuine representative/exceptional events. Its effects belong to existing owner state and causal ledgers, not to a second biography or psyche. `NO_CHANGE`, partial and unresolved outcomes remain legitimate.

## 3. Existing architecture to preserve

- **ADP021:** hybrid event-driven scheduler already coordinates discrete events, phase boundaries and separately governed slow-process invocations. It does not install a universal yearly timestep, compel filler biography, or grant a slow psychological learning law.
- **CAN008:** actor-specific uptake and response to actual encounters; event-side conditions are not psychological changes and contradictory pressures do not disappear through summation.
- **CAN011:** durable mediators, tensions and practices remain individually rule-qualified; no universal number-of-repetitions, reinforcement multiplier or automatic practice formation.
- **CAN010, ADP016/017 and relationship/material owners:** need and striving, memory, epistemic accessibility, social roles and objective actions retain distinct provenance.
- **SDTF/non-event ingress:** ongoing social obligations and organized activity may matter between spotlight events without inventing daily event transcripts.
- **One-call rolling design:** each ordinary completed event may generate retrospective self-account plus prospective next-event candidates in one LLM call, but admitted interpretive effects must be computed in V3 before the next event is realized and processed. Slow interval integration does not grant the renderer mind-writing.

## 4. Revised formation-warrant evidence patterns

A rule-specific developmental warrant may rest on:

1. **Singular qualified transformation:** one event is causal enough under an explicitly supplied mechanism.
2. **Documented durative participation:** an actual role/activity with exact source, actors, span, participation and changing conditions; a spotlight scene may exemplify but cannot create the recurrent regime.
3. **Accumulated contradiction and culmination:** actual earlier state/history and conditions are reorganized in a subsequent event or actor-owned interpretive act.
4. **Underdetermined case:** event may leave a current response, memory or social consequence, but formation is unsupported and durable middle state remains unchanged.

These can coexist. **Do not** use arbitrary multipliers (years × loyalty, five scenes × practice, age × maturity). Do not invent a number of daily occurrences, infer unshown subjective experiences, double-count an event and its representative role, or backdate a state formation inferred from a later event.

## 5. Nadia example — ages 8–10

A valid household/relationship owner establishes recurring sibling-care obligations during part of the interval, with independent family participants, material conditions and time boundaries. This is objective social practice, not proof of Nadia's virtue, resentment, knowledge or parenting competence.

Four actual spotlight events may then occur: (1) a particular day of sibling care; (2) a clash between care responsibility and a school opportunity; (3) an encounter with a means of negotiating the obligation; (4) a performed attempt to rearrange duties, possibly with another actor's support. The actor's reactions and other people's actions are separately computed/admitted.

A qualified interval-level transition could recognize local practical competence, a persisting contradiction, an interpretive mediator or no durable change. It must use both the continuing activity and actual actor reception/participation under a **specific target-owned rule**, not a threshold requiring fifty simulated episodes. A comparable case with only one isolated day of care cannot inherit the same duration evidence merely because the prose says it was “typical.”

## 6. Essential falsification tests

- Same spotlight event, **with versus without** a separately evidenced recurring activity regime, must not produce the same durative warrant automatically.
- Same episode budget under differing social/household participation can create different admissible *candidate* development without changing sample count.
- A singular event can be formative if a specific qualified rule warrants it; a long role need not automatically change personality.
- A representative scene cannot mint hidden observed episodes, precise counts, actor feelings or unperformed actions.
- Quiet intervals may carry material, participation and slow-process consequences without obligatory new scenes or positive CAN011 updates.
- No unsupported numerical amplification and no repeated counting of one causal parent.
- Later reflection cannot rewrite earlier objective history or create retroactive childhood knowledge.
- Cold replay reproduces exact interval conditions, event/slow process receipts and person state, including valid `NO_CHANGE`.
