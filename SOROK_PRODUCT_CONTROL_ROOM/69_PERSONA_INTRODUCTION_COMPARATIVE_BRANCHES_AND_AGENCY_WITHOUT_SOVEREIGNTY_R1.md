# Sorok — Persona Introduction, Comparative Branches, and Player Agency Without Sovereignty R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_DIRECTION__KERNEL_ALIGNED__SOCIAL_MECHANIC_OPEN`

Date: 2026-10-02

## 0. Two linked clarifications

This artifact banks two related discoveries:

1. Rawlsing gives the human user **agency without sovereignty** over the focal persona/world.
2. A user may privately introduce a real friend to a seed-person lineage they have themselves encountered, creating independent branches around a common pre-contact person rather than sharing the sender's branch.

Neither proposition makes the persona morally authoritative.
Neither creates a shared multiplayer Nadia.
Neither permits one user's post-contact history to alter another user's Nadia.

---

# Part I — Player agency without sovereignty

## 1. Ontological polycentricity, product-level decentering

Preserve three levels:

### Phenomenologically / actor-locally

Every represented person remains a center of:
- perception;
- memory;
- valuation;
- intention;
- action;
- consequence.

No actor-local perspective is the circumference containing all others.

### Structurally / game-design-wise

Rawlsing deliberately refuses to make **the user's gratification or interpretation** the sovereign center of the played world.

The user remains the subject of play.

But:

> **The user has agency without sovereignty.**

They may:
- love;
- refuse;
- argue;
- help;
- persuade;
- manipulate;
- have sex;
- misunderstand;
- repair;
- harm;
- leave.

They do not own:
- who Nadia is;
- what Nadia experienced;
- what their action meant to Nadia;
- what objectively happened;
- what earlier acts caused;
- what Nadia remembers or infers;
- whether their diagnosis/pathologization of Nadia is true;
- what consequences continue after the user leaves.

### Dramaturgically

The focal synthetic life is the persona's continuing life.

The user enters an already existing life and becomes one of its causes.

This is not:
> Nadia is always right.

It is:
> Nadia is not reducible to the user's verdict about Nadia.

---

## 2. Structural pressure against player sovereignty

Rawlsing should not depend on a tutorial asking the user to "be humble."

The ordinary affordances of the product should instead make player sovereignty inadequate.

Current mechanisms include:

### Unchosen encounter
The user does not begin by selecting the person most fitted to desire or compatibility.

### Human-carried invitation
A real human used a scarce relational act to bring the user to Rawlsing.

### Independent persona/world causality
Nadia has causes, obligations, projects, relations and valuations that are not the user.

### User-independent truth
The user may inquire into reality but cannot author it by asking.

### Model != person
Calling Nadia:
- narcissistic;
- avoidant;
- manipulative;
- wonderful;
- broken;
- whatever else

does not mutate Nadia into that ontology.

The user's interpretation may change the user's next action.
It does not become canonical person truth.

### Irreversible relational history
The user's acts become part of the conditions under which Nadia later encounters them.

### Restart as abandonment
The user may leave the dynamic they helped create.
They do not receive an alternate T0 version of the same person in order to optimize the encounter.

### Repair rather than undo
The future remains open without making the past unhappen.

### Independent life continuation
Nadia's other relations/domains continue to exert causal pressure.

### Partial disclosure / ATTEND
The user can become more answerable to what exceeds their initial horizon, but cannot obtain sovereign completion.

## 3. Kenotic attention — corrected game-design reading

Current candidate:

> **Kenotic attention is epistemic submission to the other's excess, not moral submission to the other's judgment.**

Operationally:

`PLAYER-CENTERED EXPECTATION`
→ `OTHER RESISTS REDUCTION`
→ `PLAYER ACTION HAS CONSEQUENCE`
→ `NO RESET / NO ONTOLOGICAL VETO`
→ `FRICTION`
→ `ATTEND`
→ `MORE SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE`
→ `REVISED ACTION`
→ `MORE SPECIFIC RELATION`
→ `NEW CONSEQUENCE`.

The user may still conclude:
- Nadia is wrong;
- Nadia is treating them badly;
- a local norm is unjust;
- distance is necessary;
- the relation should end.

Kenosis requires relinquishing the claim that one's current representation exhausts reality.

It does not require surrender of judgment, agency or resistance.

---

## 4. Structural devices as pedagogy rather than moral lecture

Current deep product form:

> Rawlsing does not primarily tell the user to decenter themselves. It creates a world in which centering oneself is epistemically and practically insufficient.

Each mechanism says something structurally:

- event persistence: **this happened**;
- memory: **this is now part of what she knows of you**;
- independent life: **you are not the only thing occurring here**;
- unexpected response: **your model is not her**;
- repair: **history can change the future without becoming unhappened**;
- restart: **you may leave; you do not get to rewrite the relation you created**.

This is a core contribution to Q-KERNEL-001.

# Part II — Person introduction / share-a-person mechanic

## 5. Core proposal

A user who has encountered a particular seed-person lineage may be able to **privately introduce that person to a real friend**.

Example:

> "I randomly encountered Nadia. I want to know what Tom will make of her."

Tom then receives his **own independent continuity fork** from Nadia's lawful pre-contact root/lineage.

Tom does **not** receive:
- the sender's Nadia branch;
- the sender's relationship history;
- the sender's Nadia-model-of-user state;
- the sender's disclosures/intimacy/conflicts;
- the sender's branch-local world changes.

Compactly:

`SHARE PERSON LINEAGE`
!=
`SHARE PERSON BRANCH`.

---

## 6. Two distinct social permissions

Do not collapse:

### A. HUMAN ACCESS INVITATION

Purpose:
- grant a human who does not yet have Rawlsing access the opportunity to enter.

Current scarcity law:
- one deliberate human designation;
- invitation spent at credential commitment;
- no conversion refund;
- no referral optimization.

### B. PERSONA INTRODUCTION

Purpose:
- direct an already eligible Rawlsing user toward a particular seed-person lineage.

This is not access creation.

Therefore a persona introduction to an **existing Rawlsing user** need not consume a scarce human invitation merely because it creates a new synthetic-person encounter.

Exact entitlement/frequency remains open.

Possible rule:
> existing-player person introduction is a separate private social operation, not part of the human-acquisition invite economy.

---

## 7. New-user invite may optionally carry a persona introduction

Candidate extension:

When a user spends their scarce real-human invitation, they may optionally choose among lawful seed-person lineages they have themselves encountered and say, in effect:

> **I want this person to meet Nadia.**

The invitation still means:
> I chose this human for Rawlsing.

The optional persona selection additionally means:
> I am introducing them to this particular synthetic person lineage.

This must remain distinct from:
- transferring the inviter's Nadia;
- endorsing Nadia as "good";
- claiming compatibility;
- psychological matchmaking;
- content recommendation optimization.

Exact alpha inclusion and whether this ability requires later unlock/warrant remain open.

---

## 8. Existing-player introduction

For two people who already have Rawlsing access:

`PLAYER A`
→ private person-introduction reference
→ `PLAYER B`
→ independent fork of the named seed-person lineage.

No access invite is spent because no new human access is being granted.

Candidate product value:

- "I wonder what Tom will think of Nadia."
- "Did Nadia ever tell you about X?"
- "Did you notice the thing in her apartment?"
- "Did you ever learn why she does Y?"
- "Mine became incredibly guarded with me."
- "That's wild—mine was the opposite."

This creates real-world conversation around:
- differential attention;
- model revision;
- situated relation;
- divergent history;
- the same person's pre-contact formation.

---

## 9. Shared pre-contact truth, branch-local becoming

Hard law:

`PRE-CONTACT PERSON / WORLD ROOT`
may be common.

`POST-CONTACT USER RELATION HISTORY`
must remain branch-local.

Therefore:

> Nadia's interactions with User A must never alter the shared Nadia root in ways that later become available as Nadia's pre-contact personality/history to User B.

No:
- aggregate relational learning from users into Nadia's root;
- "Nadia learns from all her players";
- statistical personality drift based on branch outcomes;
- sexual/attachment preference learning from users;
- cross-branch memory;
- cross-branch user models.

The shared person is a **common origin**, not a federated collective product of the players.

---

## 10. ATTEND-triggered root expansion — exact correction

The user's intended phenomenon is valuable but the word "retcon" needs a precise technical meaning.

A user's ATTEND or inquiry may cause a previously unresolved **pre-contact** locus to be materialized.

But:

`ATTEND CHOOSES RESOLUTION LOCUS`
!=
`ATTEND CHOOSES RESOLUTION CONTENT`.

The new fact must be generated from:
- existing root person/world state;
- pre-contact historical constraints;
- canonical target key;
- governed randomness where lawful;

and independently of:
- the querying user's preferences;
- branch relationship history;
- desired answer;
- branch-local outcome.

Once lawfully admitted, it may become part of the **shared pre-contact fact-base** for compatible/new forks.

This can look phenomenologically like:
> "Tom discovered something about Nadia's childhood that had always been true, even though I never found it."

But preserve causal closure:

> a late-resolved pre-contact fact may not silently become an antecedent cause inside an already-running branch if it would have materially changed that branch's prior behavior/history.

Therefore prefer the term:

> **retro-resolved pre-contact canon**

rather than unconstrained "retcon."

Exact inheritance of newly resolved but causally inert facts into already-running branches remains an implementation question.

## 11. Why this social mechanic is kernel-aligned

This mechanism creates an unusually strong lived demonstration of the current D007 candidate.

### Same root does not exhaust the person
Two people can encounter the same pre-contact Nadia and emerge with radically different knowledge.

### Perception does not exhaust world
One notices what the other never saw.

### Knowledge does not exhaust person
One may know a true formative fact the other never discovers.

### Relation really changes person
Post-contact Nadias can legitimately diverge because actual relation histories become causes.

### Relation does not transfer across persons
User A's formative effect on Nadia-A does not become part of Nadia-B's history.

### Human discussion creates meta-knowledge
Tom can tell Matthew something true about the shared root that Matthew's own branch never lawfully discovered.

That changes Matthew-the-human's knowledge without rewriting Matthew's branch provenance.

Thus the social mechanic becomes a **demonstration of nested non-exhaustion + constitutive relation**.

## 12. Product/community form

Prefer:
- private person-to-person introduction;
- no public persona marketplace;
- no trending-person list;
- no share-count prestige;
- no "Nadia route" optimization;
- no visible percent-discovered;
- no branch comparison dashboard exposing hidden truth.

The meaningful object is:
> **I want to know what happens when my real friend encounters this person.**

Not:
> "This character is great content; unlock her."

---

## 13. Spoilers and meta-knowledge

Once two real users talk, spoilers are socially inevitable.

Do not pretend otherwise.

Preserve:
- external-human knowledge;
- branch-local acquired knowledge;
- engine canonical truth

as distinct.

A user may act on something a friend told them.

The system must not silently rewrite that as:
- direct observation;
- Nadia testimony;
- ATTEND discovery;
- branch-local memory.

Research using shared personas must record such prior exposure where relevant.

## 14. Open questions

### Q1 — entitlement
Should persona introduction:
- exist immediately between existing users;
- require the sender to have spent meaningful time with that persona;
- require some non-score relational warrant;
- unlock only after succession;
- be bounded in frequency?

Do not answer by engagement optimization.

### Q2 — new-user starting person
Should a human inviter be able to select:
- the invitee's first persona;
- one option among an opaque small candidate set;
- a later guaranteed encounter rather than first persona?

### Q3 — consent
Should recipient:
- know which friend introduced this persona;
- choose whether to accept the specific introduction;
- receive a neutral "Matthew thought you might want to meet Nadia" presentation?

Avoid compatibility claims.

### Q4 — root version / closure
Which exact root/version/causal-closure state is used when a person is introduced?

Comparability requires exact lineage.

### Q5 — anti-optimization
How do we preserve:
> "I wonder what Tom will make of Nadia"

without creating:
> "Everyone should run Nadia because she has the best content"?

### Q6 — persona availability
Can any randomly encountered/succession persona be introduced, or only precompiled/share-safe seed lineages?

This has direct compute and reproducibility implications.

## 15. Compact laws

> **Human invitation grants access; persona introduction chooses a person-lineage to encounter.**

> **Share the person, never the branch.**

> **A shared persona is a common origin, not a collective product of her players.**

> **ATTEND may expand shared pre-contact canon by selecting where unresolved reality is lawfully resolved; user branch history may never select what that pre-contact truth becomes.**

> **Different users should be able to compare what they discovered about the same starting person while retaining genuinely different post-contact persons.**

> **The social value is curiosity about another real human's encounter with the same irreducible synthetic other—not character recommendation as content consumption.**
