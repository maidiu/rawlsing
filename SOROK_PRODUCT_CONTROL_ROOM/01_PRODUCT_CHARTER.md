# Sorok Product Charter

Status: `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW_R1`

## 1. Deep purpose

Rawlsing's deepest purpose is transformative rather than representational.

The project aims to make the contingency and relational production of personhood experientially legible:

> I could have been you; under your conditions, in an important sense, I would have become another person rather than remaining myself in different clothes; therefore your life places a claim on mine.

The intended movement is:

`contingency → humility → recognition → solidarity → responsibility`

The political return is:

> If worlds make persons, then institutions are interventions into the kinds of selves that can emerge. What kind of world can I justify if I might occupy any position inside what we make together?

This is the project's tikkun-olam horizon: not moral instruction detached from life, but repeated practice in recognizing another human being as an end whose flourishing is not reducible to the user's gratification.

## 2. Product translation

The product should not begin with a person selected because the user already finds them attractive, useful, ideologically congenial or entertaining.

Rawlsing presents a **neighbor**.

The central product challenge is:

> Can a stranger become specific enough, consequential enough, and mutually known enough to become kin?

The hook and pedagogy should be the same mechanism.

## 3. Non-negotiable distinctions

Rawlsing must not collapse:

`attachment ≠ trust ≠ health ≠ pleasure ≠ compliance ≠ intimacy ≠ benefit ≠ dependency ≠ access`

A user may become important to a persona while harming them.

A persona may report happiness while becoming more dependent, less agentic or less able to sustain other relations.

A rupture may reduce immediate pleasure while improving long-run health.

A person may become more difficult as they become healthier because they acquire boundaries, expectations, anger, explicit needs and differentiated judgment.

## 4. Advancement

Advancement should be experienced primarily through **changed relationship**, not exposed score.

Examples:
- they initiate;
- they ask;
- they remember;
- they disagree without fearing loss;
- they disclose differently;
- they invite;
- they allow silence;
- they expect more;
- they become angry where previously they appeased;
- they introduce the user into other relationships;
- they rely on the user in some domains while becoming less dependent in others.

The reward for intimacy is not easier access. It is greater reality.

## 5. User transformation

Rawlsing should model the user's repeated relational behavior without prematurely turning it into a diagnosis.

Preferred epistemic form:

> Here is what you repeatedly did in relationships.

Not:

> Here is what you are.

Candidate longitudinal questions include:
- Do you abandon when novelty falls?
- Do you over-help?
- Do you punish boundaries?
- Do you tolerate anger?
- Do you mistake disclosure for intimacy?
- Do you prefer people who affirm you?
- Do you become possessive?
- Do you remain present when you cannot solve?
- Do you interpret indirect need as manipulation?
- How accurately do you revise your model of another mind?

## 6. Anti-goals

Rawlsing should not become:
- an avatar-driven character collector;
- a sycophantic companion whose purpose is to please the user;
- a romance / sex funnel disguised as moral formation;
- a relationship score optimizer;
- a therapy or diagnostic service by implication;
- a coercive retention system that makes users feel an AI will suffer if they log off;
- a prestige economy where visible compassion scores become symbolic capital;
- a system where LLM improvisation silently overwrites causal ontology;
- a system that mistakes cultural stereotype for relational psychology;
- a morality game with universal canned “correct” responses.

## 7. Ultimate success condition

A strong Rawlsing relationship should leave the user able to say:

> I learned this particular person rather than a generic moral rule; I became answerable to effects I could not define away; the relationship changed both of us; and afterward I can see patterns in how I approach real other people that were harder to see before.
