# Temporal and social strata

Pass III, following the [composition audit](DERIVATION_MATRIX.md). These are scales of possible experience, not stages through which a person should progress. “Can stand” means no higher layer is conceptually necessary; it is not a claim of demonstrated engagement.

## A — Turn / seconds

**Native faculties:** noticing, recognition, inquiry, inference, tact, precise expression, deciding whether to act, prioritizing attention. The user performs these through actual selection and language; saying “I am perceptive” does not instantiate them.

**Independent resistance:** sightlines, embodied reach, object state, incompatible actions, another actor's attention and interpretation. **Consequence:** an observation acquired, an utterance actually heard or missed, an attempted act partly realized, time consumed. Neither every turn nor every acquired fact must cause durable person change.

**Uncertainty:** what a visible detail supports; whether someone noticed; what a pause means. Unknown parser behavior is an interface defect, not desirable mystery. **Development:** a local hypothesis becomes more precise or is abandoned. **Failure:** misunderstanding, infeasibility, saying something ineffective, missing an opportunity; parser failure must be distinguished from these.

**Deepening/unveiling:** additional warranted detail, recognition of an object or incongruity; no automatic historical explanation. **Reason to continue:** a concrete unanswered possibility in the present exchange, interest in the material itself, or the next contribution one wants to make. This reason can be absent.

**Lower dependencies:** lawful state and an actionable phenomenal frame exist beneath the turn; no new timescale is needed. **Without higher support:** yes, as local play; must not require a promise of later secrets or social comparison. **Audit parents:** M01–M05, M12; [E03–E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08).

## B — Scene / minutes

**Native faculties:** sustained attention, timing, explanation, perspective-taking, responsiveness, memory of the ongoing exchange, balancing participation against observation.

**Independent resistance:** concurrent agendas, a material process already underway, fatigue, a third party's own intention, finite co-presence. A quiet scene need not be packed with competing demands. **Consequence:** something gets done, interrupted, refused, postponed or misunderstood; a new possibility may emerge from actual interaction.

**Uncertainty:** which demands will become relevant, how a contribution lands, how long an opportunity remains. **Development:** better coordination or contextual understanding; an emergent shared method; revised expectations. **Failure:** confusing an explanation, talking over a concern, doing a practical task incorrectly, persisting after refusal. Continuation can involve a different activity, clarification, silence or departure.

**Deepening/unveiling:** context makes earlier words legible; observation can reveal a different side of an already generated person without disclosure. **Reason to continue:** participation in what is actually happening, including enjoyment, ordinary conversation or completion of a self-chosen contribution. No mandatory bounded undertaking is needed.

**Lower dependencies:** A must expose enough evidence, actual uptake and timely control return for choices to be real. **Without higher support:** yes; an ordinary scene must have possible value without later attachment. **Audit parents:** M01–M06, M11–M12; [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), [E05](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e05).

## C — Encounter / day

**Native faculties:** remembering context across exchanges, following through, revising plans, judgment about asking or leaving, recognizing when one has misunderstood the day.

**Independent resistance:** schedules, obligations, other relationships, access controllers, limited resources, a visit ending for its own reasons. **Consequence:** admitted practical aftermath, a kept or missed commitment, a channel established or not, a memory or expectation changed where warranted. A scene can matter without a climax or psychological breakthrough.

**Uncertainty:** which effects will persist, whether apparent understanding was shared, whether future contact will be possible. **Development:** an actual local convention can begin; knowledge becomes situated in a lived encounter rather than collected facts. **Failure:** a missed arrangement, embarrassment, wasted effort, losing an opportunity. Repair is a possible further cause, not undo.

**Deepening/unveiling:** the day's different contexts reveal partial patterns; practical consequences can correct one's earlier account. **Reason to continue:** an actor's plan, an unfinished exchange, a voluntarily undertaken commitment, or simple desire to meet again. No guaranteed contact is owed.

**Lower dependencies:** A–B plus exact occurrence, memory/knowledge provenance and a comprehensible transition out of co-presence. **Without higher support:** yes as an encounter; whether it motivates another day is precisely a validation question. **Audit parents:** M06–M08, M10, M13; [E06–E07](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06).

## D — Longitudinal single-persona continuity

**Native faculties:** person-specific memory, contextual discrimination, patience, tact, extrapolation, model revision, keeping and renegotiating commitments.

**Independent resistance:** the person changes through many causes; their availability and interests need not track the user; histories and interpretations differ. **Consequence:** events affect future expectations, routines, access, material possibilities and actor models through competent owners. Old events may later be interpreted differently without being rewritten.

**Uncertainty:** how much a pattern generalizes; what changed offscreen; whether familiar practices still have familiar meanings. **Development:** more differentiated knowing, newly constituted conventions, altered responsibilities, changed ways of participating. This may involve less intimacy or access. **Failure:** overgeneralizing from past success, taking permission for granted, misremembering, harming or losing contact. A narrowing relation is not automatically evidence of bad play.

**Deepening/unveiling:** discovery of antecedent reality and creation of subsequent history coexist; recurring ordinary contexts can acquire specificity. **Reason to continue:** this particular ongoing life, a shared practice, lawful invitation, a question occasioned by actual history, or curiosity about a changed present. Repeated visits need not each yield novelty.

**Lower dependencies:** A–C plus causally effective history, independent advancement and humane absence semantics. **Without higher support:** yes; this is the decisive solo test, with no other user, referral, persona succession or meta-objective. **Audit parents:** M05, M07–M10, M13; [E07–E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e07).

## E — Full one-persona history, including departure

**Native faculties:** long-horizon memory, reinterpretation, acknowledgment of limits, decisions about continued participation or leaving. **Independent resistance:** another life has trajectories beyond this relation; opportunities can end; the user's account never contains the whole person.

**Consequence:** a history remains particular even if peripheral, intermittent, difficult, deteriorated or abandoned. Loss of access does not authorize rewriting it. **Uncertainty:** causal attribution, whether anything would have gone differently, effects the user never learns. **Development:** changes in one's way of relating, a new boundary, a more accurate but less total account, or recognition that the relation should end. None is guaranteed.

**Failure:** harm, estrangement, boredom, a relation that never becomes mutually rewarding. These must be distinguishable from technical incoherence. **Deepening/unveiling:** hindsight can reorganize the interpretation of a history while preserving contrary evidence and inaccessible aspects. A meaningful ending is not a completed persona.

**Reason to continue:** continued interest or relation-specific purpose, when present; **there may instead be a good reason to stop**. Treating every departure as a product failure would invite coercion; treating every departure as profound success would conceal a weak product.

**Lower dependencies:** coherent D and provenance-preserving records; no omniscient epilogue required. **Without higher support:** yes as a potentially meaningful history; no proof of decades-long engagement exists. **Audit parents:** M06–M10, M16; SPD-0172, SPD-0201, SPD-0205, SPD-0234. No universal completion mechanic is justified.

## F — Across globally random personas, well over 100 countries

**Native faculties:** carrying a repertoire of inquiry, discriminating context, noticing one's imported assumptions, resisting premature generalization. **Independent resistance:** people share no necessary geography, institution, concern, education, expertise or desire to explain themselves.

**Consequence:** new independent histories may form; changes in the user's actual practice may carry over. A previous person's story grants no knowledge of this person. **Uncertainty:** which lessons transfer; whether an apparent cross-person pattern is real or projection. **Development:** possible breadth of repertoire and differentiation, not countries completed or universal social mastery.

**Failure:** stereotyping, importing an effective local approach into a different setting, searching for a better “drop,” treating an obscure or uninformed person as useless. **Deepening/unveiling:** contrasts may illuminate the limits of the user's earlier concepts; no assigned macro-investigation follows. **Reason to continue:** interest in encountering another independent life, if present. This is neither guaranteed nor an allocation rule for new people.

**Lower dependencies:** D–E must already work; lawful succession remains unresolved under Q-SUCCESSION-001. **Without higher support:** potentially, without G–H. **No strong native cross-persona gamification is yet derived.** The possibility of cumulative practice does not justify an externally assigned question, persuasion campaign or specimen collection. **Audit parents:** M11, M14, M17; [E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), [E09–E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09).

## G — Shared seeds, invitations and real-human social ecology

**Native faculties:** comparing accounts critically, respecting provenance, revising overconfident interpretations, choosing a human invitation thoughtfully. **Independent resistance:** other humans' choices, divergent exposure and history, branch privacy, limited lawful sharing. A synthetic actor cannot know external discussions without a diegetic information route.

**Consequence:** a real invitation is an act in a real human relationship; shared roots can lead to different post-contact histories. **Uncertainty:** why experiences differ, what was actually shared, whether a newly resolved root fact can lawfully propagate. **Development:** possible richer conversation and critical comparison. **Failure:** spoilers, route optimization, prestige capture, coercive referral, mistaken attribution of branch difference to skill.

**Deepening/unveiling:** another account may unsettle the user's theory, but it is not automatically first-hand branch knowledge. **Reason to continue:** optional discussion or a genuine invitation; not a platform-generated duty to grow the network.

**Lower dependencies:** solo D; immutable root identity, causal closure, branch quarantine, privacy and exact invitation lifecycle. **Without higher support:** yes without H, but this layer cannot certify or rescue A–E. **Audit parents:** M15; [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10). Human comparison is compatible; no necessary social game or leaderboard is derived.

The existing one-for-one invitation rule can give a real human designation consequence before entry: designation consumes the opportunity even if the recipient declines or never uses it. Same-identity technical repair does not create a fresh choice. This may give an invitation weight, but its meaning belongs to the actual human relationship; it is not platform-certified praise, persona access earned by virtue, or a reason the synthetic actor knows anything about the invitation. SPD-0147–0152 and SPD-0240 support the distinction, not a viral-growth game.

## H — The user's relationship to Rawlsing across years

**Native faculties:** sustained practice of attention, explanation, reflection, contextual judgment, memory, restraint and uncertainty calibration. **Independent resistance:** one's habits and limits, varied situations, others who remain resistant to full understanding; ordinary life also competes for the user's time.

**Consequence:** the user may acquire repertoire, memories and historical self-knowledge. The system can support only evidence-bounded claims about observed play. **Uncertainty:** whether apparent improvement transfers; which effects come from the interface, selection, outside assistance or life elsewhere. **Development:** possible changed ways of noticing and acting; no inevitable moral or spiritual ascent.

**Failure:** compulsive attachment, intellectualizing everyone, optimizing for a flattering praxis reflection, repetitive misjudgment, or years of use without useful development. **Deepening/unveiling:** revisiting one's own recorded decisions can reveal assumptions one did not recognize at the time. **Reason to continue:** valued practice, curiosity, relation, contemplation or pleasure as the user actually experiences them; stopping remains legitimate.

**Lower dependencies:** evidence of meaningful A–D experience and lawful exposure/provenance; F–G are optional. **Without higher support:** this is the highest listed stratum and must add no external moral tribunal. **Audit parents:** M14, M16; SPD-0234, SPD-0244, SPD-0245. Durable benefit remains an empirical question; no level or achievement system is derived.

## Relations between strata

A–B concern **how to respond now**. C–D concern **what this occurrence becomes in a continuing history**. E concerns **what a finite relation can have meant without an ideal ending**. F–H concern possible transfer, comparison and practice, not a larger objective that assigns the earlier strata their purpose.

The dependencies are asymmetric. A good shared-seed discussion cannot repair a scene in which nothing the player says matters. A moving retrospective cannot repair history that only produces cosmetic callbacks. Conversely, a lucid, worthwhile encounter need not establish a multi-person metagame. This is why the next work should close A–D's constitutive mechanics before designing F–H's superstructure.
