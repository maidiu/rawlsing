# Necessity and composition audit

Pass II. This matrix derives affordances and tensions, not a guarantee of pleasure. Source groups link to exact immutable paths in [EVIDENCE_RECEIPT](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md). Decision/question/risk IDs refer exclusively to the baseline registers there. Every claim of gameplay potential in the return resolves to a row here; proposed tests are not additional authority.

Classifications: `A_ANCHORED` = explicit baseline commitment; `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED` = consequence of composing stated commitments; `C_GROUND_MECHANIC_REQUIRED` = experiential claim awaits a specified seam; `D_GENUINE_DESIGN_SPACE` = compatible choice, not forced; `REJECT` = incompatible or unjustified promotion. A B derivation can be sound while its engagement hypothesis fails.

## M01 — The player's contribution must actually occur

**Sources:** [E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), artifacts 26–29; SPD-0010, SPD-0076–0080.

**Composition:** a user controls attempts, not reception; native cognitive and linguistic contributions must be supplied; mundane steps can compress. **Experience:** “I help explain this” brings the user to evidence and a meaningful next contribution, not to automatic successful teaching. **Why it may engage:** an actual explanation or diagnostic question can make a difference attributable to its content. **Corruption:** turning every task into micromanagement, or generating a flattering contribution for the user. **Class:** `A_ANCHORED`.

## M02 — Learning can alter the conditions of further learning

**Sources:** [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), D002/02, /03, /08, /10; [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), artifact 80 §§7–8; SPD-0025, SPD-0033, SPD-0237.

**Derivation:** (1) acquisition consumes situated attention/time; (2) some embodied attention and questions are perceivable events; (3) perceived events enter independent actor uptake; (4) resulting action can alter availability, testimony or the scene. Therefore, where those conditions obtain, an inquiry changes the future evidence situation rather than merely reading it. **Experience:** the player weighs looking, asking, participating, waiting and leaving; a useful question can also redirect a conversation or expose an assumption. **Why it may engage:** inquiry requires judgment about timing and method as well as content. **Corruption:** making every glance detected or every question costly, treating privacy as a trap, or providing fixed “optimal inquiry” routes. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`.

## M03 — Finite attention creates opportunity costs without a points economy

**Sources:** [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), D002/02, /08, /09; [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), SPD-0247; Q-TIME-002, Q-TIME-003, Q-CONTROL-001.

**Derivation:** (1) perception/action have duration and incompatible bodily demands; (2) other processes may continue independently; (3) the player cannot acquire everything by passively waiting. Thus, in a scene containing concurrent relevant possibilities, attending to one can leave another unacquired or an opportunity elapsed. **Experience:** prioritization under finite access; sometimes finishing a thought is worth missing something. **Why it may engage:** choices matter without an artificial energy bar. **Corruption:** universal countdowns, typing-speed penalties, arbitrary attention prices, guaranteed consequential detail in every scene. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`. Actual frequency and intelligibility of these costs remain C-level questions.

## M04 — Better evidence need not provide the answer

**Sources:** [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), artifacts 70, 78, 80, 87; SPD-0236, SPD-0237, SPD-0248; RISK-193, RISK-194, RISK-204.

**Composition:** lawful inspection increases physical specificity; hidden materialization, acquired knowledge, recognition and significance stay separate. **Experience:** wear, markings or a repair can become visible while provenance and significance remain unknown. **Why it may engage:** recognition, inference and later connection belong to the player. **Corruption:** a brand label solving recognition; every inspected object yielding biography; repetition revealing an exhaustive inventory. **Class:** `A_ANCHORED`. No inference that every object is worth prolonged investigation follows.

## M05 — Competence is local and revisable, not necessarily powerless

**Sources:** [E05](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e05), artifact 41; [E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09), D007; [E12](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e12); SPD-0205, SPD-0234.

**Derivation:** (1) responses depend on this person's organization, current condition, counterpart and history; (2) the player encounters only partial evidence; (3) these determinants can change; (4) repeated experience may nevertheless reveal stable local regularities. Therefore learning can improve contextual prediction and action while no context-free response policy is warranted. **Experience:** a familiar approach can remain useful, need adjustment, or become inappropriate for an intelligible reason. **Why it may engage:** mastery of discrimination and revision, with genuine local successes. **Corruption:** randomizing responses to defeat learning, equating uncertainty with wisdom, or rewarding obedience to one “true” person model. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`. Improvement by real users remains untested; SPD-0234 is a hypothesis.

## M06 — Success has several independent predicates

**Sources:** [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), D002/09–11; [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), artifact 52; [E11](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e11); SPD-0010, SPD-0130, SPD-0131.

**Derivation:** (1) attempt, realized act, uptake and effect differ; (2) practical effect, actor approval, access and flourishing can diverge; (3) causal attribution and user knowledge also differ. Therefore no single success signal faithfully summarizes an interaction. **Experience:** help may work but embarrass; a justified refusal may anger; coordination may improve without affection. **Why it may engage:** judgment must accommodate consequences beyond immediate approval. **Corruption:** replacing an affection meter with a hidden “ethical complexity” score, or teaching that disapproval proves virtue. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`. Flourishing remains an open normative question, not a solved simulation output.

## M07 — History changes the meaning of repetition

**Sources:** [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), artifact 53; [E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09), D007; [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10), artifact 83; SPD-0172, SPD-0238.

**Derivation:** (1) actual events persist; (2) memory, expectations and local conventions may lawfully change; (3) later uptake recruits such history; (4) shared convention does not guarantee shared interpretation. Therefore repeating an externally similar act may now enact a routine, breach an expectation, carry a joke, or expose a mismatch that did not previously exist. **Experience:** “same visit” becomes a different situated choice; competence includes knowing what can now be assumed and what still must be asked. **Why it may engage:** development consists partly in creating a history whose implications are not fully planned. **Corruption:** automatic promotion to relationship stages, secretly counting visits, or interpreting every repetition as intimacy. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`.

## M08 — Failure may leave a playable situation, not a reset demand

**Sources:** [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), D002/09–11; [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), access constitution; [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10), irreversible continuity; SPD-0172, SPD-0201, SPD-0205.

**Derivation:** (1) a failed attempt does not abolish actor/world state; (2) admitted effects remain history; (3) subsequent lawful actions can still occur wherever access and circumstances permit. Therefore many failures create changed situations in which clarification, repair, acceptance, withdrawal or unrelated activity remain possible. **Experience:** fallout is material to subsequent judgment. **Why it may engage:** a mistake can make what follows specific rather than merely subtracting points. **Corruption:** guaranteeing forgiveness or continued access; manufacturing trauma as a reward for failure. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`. Some relations or opportunities end; continuation is permitted, never guaranteed.

## M09 — Deepening has both discovery and creation

**Sources:** [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), artifact 70; [E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09), artifact 71; SPD-0212, SPD-0235, SPD-0239, SPD-0243.

**Derivation:** (1) lawful unresolved past is constrained by existing history; (2) evidence/testimony can disclose portions of that past; (3) present interpretation is an event with actor-specific uptake, not retrospective truth authority; (4) new associations and conventions can form. Thus deepening can unveil an antecedent, create a new meaning, or do both without conflating them. **Experience:** an ordinary object may matter later because something happened with it, not because it always concealed the player's destined revelation. **Why it may engage:** discovery and participation coexist. **Corruption:** harmonizing all contradictions, making user interpretations correct by retcon, or creating a universal symbolic decoder. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`.

## M10 — Independent return does not require escalating content

**Sources:** [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), artifacts 30, 32, 52; [E07](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e07), artifacts 46–48; SPD-0176–0178.

**Derivation:** (1) independent projects and other relationships continue; (2) this relation can retain memories, channels or commitments; (3) contexts and opportunities can change without the user. Therefore a later lawful encounter can have new relevance without deeper disclosure, greater attachment or a platform-authored crisis. **Experience:** returning to see, participate, follow through or simply spend time can encounter a changed present. **Why it may engage:** the person has an ongoing life, and the existing relation has particular history. **Corruption:** generating contact to recover churn, faking background meaningful player agency, or manufacturing change every visit. **Class:** `B_NECESSITY_DERIVED`. Independence entails neither contact nor user desire to return.

## M11 — Friction can revise the player's own assumptions

**Sources:** [E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), artifact 21; SPD-0078, SPD-0205; Q-HABITUS-001, Q-EMBODYTRANS-001.

**Composition:** locally peer-bounded material position coexists with the external user's differently formed judgments; actor judgments remain local and other locals can disagree. **Experience:** a familiar moral or practical prescription encounters unfamiliar costs and alternatives. **Why it may engage:** perspective-taking becomes action under constraints rather than recital of cultural facts. **Corruption:** assimilation as victory, culture as one mind, hidden etiquette exams, or declaring that the user has literally become their alternate self. **Class:** `A_ANCHORED` as a provisional Sorok direction; educational effectiveness is unproven.

## M12 — The interface can preserve or consume the very faculty being exercised

**Sources:** M01–M04; [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), artifact 87; Q-SCENE-001, Q-SALIENCE-001, Q-PARSER-002, Q-CONTROL-001.

**Composition:** exposure selection determines evidence; action decomposition determines the user's actual contribution; time/control return determines whether revision is possible. **Experience sought:** sufficiently concrete and intelligible choices without supplied interpretations. **Why it may engage:** the user can think and act rather than guess the parser or accept narrator judgments. **Corruption:** beautifully written output doing the player's cognition; opaque input realization masquerading as worldly resistance. **Class:** `C_GROUND_MECHANIC_REQUIRED`. The laws alone do not choose a usable cadence or evidence density.

## M13 — Meaningful history must survive beyond transcript recall

**Sources:** M07–M10; [E05](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e05), [E12](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e12); Q-MEM-001, Q-MEM-002, Q-PROMOTE-001, Q-RELFACT-001, Q-RELSIGN-001.

**Composition:** occurrence, actor memory/access, durable effects and present interpretation have distinct owners. **Experience sought:** a prior act changes later situated behavior when causally relevant, including effects without explicit recollection. **Why it may engage:** history is usable reality, not decorative callbacks. **Corruption:** mentioning an old detail as proof of causality, updating relationship state for every utterance, or silently identifying forgetting with objective erasure. **Class:** `C_GROUND_MECHANIC_REQUIRED`.

## M14 — Contrast between independent lives may accumulate in the user

**Sources:** M05, M11; [E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09), D007; [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10), artifact 63; SPD-0234.

**Composition:** different persons can defeat overgeneralization while actual faculties recur. **Experience sought:** a broader repertoire and more discriminating attention carried between encounters. **Why it may engage:** seeing differently can itself become valuable. **Corruption:** treating globally random people as curriculum units, national specimens or tests of one universal thesis. **Class:** `D_GENUINE_DESIGN_SPACE` for deliberate cross-persona reflection. Transfer is possible, not forced; no global mission follows.

## M15 — Shared roots permit comparison, not a conquest ladder

**Sources:** [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10), artifacts 57, 61; SPD-0191, SPD-0193, SPD-0201; Q-PERSONSHARE-001.

**Composition:** shared pre-contact lineage plus branch-local history can make different encounters discussable. **Experience sought:** surprise at how different contact can be without either user possessing the whole person. **Why it may engage:** optional human conversation can sharpen questions and attachment to one's own history. **Corruption:** spoilers as earned perception, persona wikis, best-route rankings, or treating all divergence as user skill rather than exposure and world differences. **Class:** `D_GENUINE_DESIGN_SPACE`. Sharing policy and social uptake are unproven and not solo dependencies.

Invitation is a distinct subcase: artifact 57's one-for-one human designation is consumed at deliberate designation, before credential issuance; refusal, nonuse or an existing account does not refund it, while technical repair is limited to the same designated identity. That constraint is anchored (SPD-0147–0152), but the claim that it gives entry felt human weight is only compatible/empirical (SPD-0240). It creates neither synthetic-actor knowledge nor proof that the recipient is morally worthy. Scarcity cannot be promoted into referral competition or made a prerequisite for the solo experience to matter.

## M16 — Retrospective reflection can be useful without a verdict on the self

**Sources:** [E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), artifact 25; [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10), artifacts 58, 63, 83; SPD-0244, SPD-0245; Q-ALPHA-001.

**Composition:** actual recorded choices and exposure can support bounded, revisable observations about play. **Experience sought:** noticing a recurrent assumption, omission or successful adjustment across time. **Why it may engage:** a user may develop historical self-understanding. **Corruption:** attention-style diagnosis, moral rank, spiritual-improvement certification, or unaided-skill claims when external assistance is unknown. **Class:** `D_GENUINE_DESIGN_SPACE` for a designed reflective practice; bounded praxis reflection itself is anchored. No empirical growth claim is made.

## M17 — Universal relational progress and external metagames

**Proposed composition:** history → closeness → disclosure → new person; or arbitrary lives → assigned investigation → completion. **Experience promised:** an unambiguous objective and content supply. **Apparent appeal:** familiar progression and legible success. **Why it fails:** access is nonmonotonic; testimony is actor-owned; non-exhaustion excludes completion; global random persons need not serve a common inquiry; the solo core cannot borrow its purpose from a metagame. **Parents violated:** SPD-0127–0133, SPD-0205, SPD-0236–0238, SPD-0243; [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), [E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09), [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10). **Class:** `REJECT`.

## Limits of the derivation

The strongest composition is M01 + M02 + M05 + M07 + M08: **actual contributions enter an independently caused situation and may change the history against which later contributions are understood.** This supports accountable possibility without ownership. It does not by itself entail fun, ethical regard, intimacy, a correct relational role, economic design, or a complete Rawlsing kernel. Those would require additional premises or evidence.
