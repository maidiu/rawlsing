# Ground-mechanics frontier

Pass IV. The critical unanswered question is often not “what activity should we add?” but “what must the existing activity actually expose, consume, remember and change?” These are questions about the source of play, not just engineering debt.

All question stages below are from the exact baseline [register](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e01). This report proposes priorities; it does not alter closure states or promote post-alpha work into official alpha blockers.

## Ranked adjudication value

The four columns are qualitative judgments, not measured scores. Ranking concerns what next local investigation will teach us, not implementation order. In particular, G4 supplies truth/occurrence prerequisites for G1–G3 and cannot be omitted because it ranks fourth.

| Rank / frontier | Conceptual leverage | Dependency centrality | Ability to reveal indigenous play | Premature-superstructure risk if skipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / G1 Perception, action, time and control return | Decides whether a native faculty is actually exercised | Every in-scene choice | Direct: can users make informed, consequential moves? | Very high: invented quests compensate for an inert interface |
| 2 / G2 Actor agenda, uptake, effort and initiative | Decides whether there is an independent responsive other | Every interpersonal consequence | Direct: does the same contribution meet a particular person? | Very high: dialogue content and drama substitute for person causality |
| 3 / G3 History, conventions and changed future possibilities | Decides whether recurrence develops rather than repeats | C–E and any claimed long-term core | Direct: does a return mean something different? | Very high: trust bars and disclosure ladders substitute for history |
| 4 / G4 Materialization, causal closure and epistemic provenance | Secures independent actuality beneath specificity | Foundation for all three above | Indirect but essential: can discovery survive adversarial querying? | Very high: curated mysteries and retcon manufacture depth |
| 5 / G5 Absence, messaging and humane return | Separates continuing life from calendar coercion | D and later scales | High: can return remain person-caused and usable? | High: notifications and crises manufacture urgency |
| 6 / G6 Local embodiment, resources and language | Secures globally situated resistance | Entry and cross-context validity | High outside an easy shared-language fixture | High: generic English chatbot plus country decoration |

Q-ALPHA-001 and its validation questions are a **cross-cutting evidence gate**, not a seventh feature. They determine whether lawful operation is worth inhabiting.

## G1 — What is available, what did I do, and when can I respond?

**Exact questions:** Q-SCENE-001, Q-NARR-001, Q-SALIENCE-001, Q-TIME-002, Q-TIME-003, Q-CHANNEL-001, Q-PARSER-002, Q-CONTROL-001, Q-REALIZEDSAY-001 are ALPHA_VALIDATION. Q-PLAYER-003 and Q-PHYS-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION. Q-CONCUR-001 is POST_ALPHA; use its constitutional incompatibilities in a bounded test without claiming its full architecture is now required. Q-ATTEND-001 is provisionally answered, not an open semantic blank.

**Why it matters:** M02–M04 only become experienced if a player can distinguish what they saw, inferred, said and physically attempted. Return too late and the system performs their judgment; return too often and play becomes clerical. Surface too little and uncertainty becomes guessing; surface too much and the narrator performs recognition and interpretation.

**Already constrained:** coarse orientation plus noncuratorial phenomenal sampling; recognition-gated specificity; embodied ATTEND; no exhaustive waiting; time follows realized operation rather than text length or typing duration; return before inventing meaningful user contribution; compress mundane steps. Task-entry refinement in artifact 29 must qualify artifact 28: an ordinary “I help” can enter and expose the task before a method decision. [E03–E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), [E08](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08); SPD-0079, SPD-0080, SPD-0247, SPD-0248; RISK-202–204.

**Underdetermined:** default evidence density; recognition confidence/repair; duration granularity; interruption thresholds; partial completion reporting; ambiguous mixed SAY/DO/ATTEND parsing; what the player sees when an action is infeasible or only partly occurs.

**Next local work:** compare three render/control traces of the **same fixed causal scene**. Hold facts and actor state constant, vary only exposure and control-return policy. Require a user to know enough to choose and to identify what actually happened afterward, without being told why the actor acted. Include one compound instruction that must return control, one routine that should compress, and a prompt-packing attempt.

**Defer:** attention currencies, activity minigames, procedural task ladders and claims about moment-to-moment hookiness until this is legible.

## G2 — What does this person take up, pursue and initiate?

**Exact questions:** Q-AGENDA-001, Q-PERCEPT-001, Q-ATTNACTOR-001, Q-EFFORT-001, Q-EXPR-002, Q-LIVE2CALL-001, Q-WORLDEV-001, Q-LIFETOPO-001, Q-DISCLOSE-001 and Q-ACTORFID-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION. Q-RECIPROCITY-001, Q-INVITE-001 and Q-SYC-001 are ALPHA_VALIDATION. Q-THIRDPARTY-001 remains POST_ALPHA; a consequential child/coworker in a fixture still needs enough lawful actor state to avoid being a prop.

**Why it matters:** resistance alone is insufficient. If the system ignores everything, the player cannot exercise consequential skill; if it reacts helpfully to everything, the other is user-owned. The load-bearing seam is selective, causally intelligible responsiveness, including voluntary initiative and valid nonresponse.

**Already constrained:** actor agendas survive user arrival; perception differs from uptake, conscious access and expression; limited effort may yield partial response despite comprehension; full inhibition without a tell is lawful; an actor can disclose, refuse, evade, deceive or misremember. CAN-008 R2 is the adopted person-event semantic route, not a license for an LLM to invent plausible state. [E05](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e05), [E12](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e12); SPD-0025, SPD-0033; RISK-016, RISK-031.

**Underdetermined:** concrete agenda arbitration and attention rules; what counts as heard; how current condition limits response coverage; exact invitation/initiative derivation; fidelity promotion before consequential third-party interaction; renderer validation and latency budgets.

**Next local work:** hold a user contribution fixed while changing only an authorized determinant: actor load, knowledge, counterpart history or active purpose. Also hold determinants fixed and paraphrase semantically equivalent input. Demonstrate distinct lawful outcomes in the first case and stable causal interpretation in the second. Include attentive uptake, nonhearing, understood-but-unanswered, explicit refusal and actor-initiated contact. Hidden receipts must distinguish these even when the visible silence is identical.

**Defer:** authored “difficult character” challenges, crisis schedules, reciprocity guarantees, psychic meters and conclusions that independent life is engaging. Do not label missing rules VALID_EMPTY.

## G3 — What does an occurred interaction change for later life?

**Exact questions:** Q-MEM-001, Q-MEM-002, Q-PROMOTE-001, Q-MODEL-001, Q-RELFACT-001, Q-RELSIGN-001, Q-ACCESSIMPL-001 and Q-PLAYERPART-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION. Q-ACCESS-001 is provisionally answered. Q-HEALTH-001 and Q-CAUSAL-001 remain POST_ALPHA; basic event attribution must remain truthful without pretending to close their full courts.

**Why it matters:** a transcript callback can mimic remembrance while nothing causally changes. Conversely, a durable effect can be real even when no one recollects its cause. Longitudinal play requires the second encounter to consume history at the relevant owner grain, not just quote the first.

**Already constrained:** occurred events, objective convention, actor memory, mutual models and actor-local meaning are distinct. Access is sparse, conditional, revocable and controller-specific; it need not monotonically expand. Permission does not entail expectation or a user promise. Shared convention does not entail shared meaning. [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10); SPD-0127–0133, SPD-0172, SPD-0238; RISK-195.

**Underdetermined:** thresholds and rules for durable admission; memory accessibility/forgetting; derivation of expectations from actual history; representation of mutually different interpretations; exact evidence for commitments; what changed access can be lawfully made apparent to the user.

**Next local work:** carry a mundane convention across three encounters. Keep objective agreement fixed while actor interpretations differ. Show one reliable expectation, one overreading, one renegotiation/refusal and a later context where the earlier event matters without being explicitly recalled. An ablated trace without that admitted event should identify which later differences are warranted; it must not invent a general relationship score to make the difference.

**Defer:** relationship stages, “become indispensable,” universal repair arcs, new-person warrant implementation and full self-retcon/Horizon theory. Specificity emerging is not an objective to impose.

## G4 — How does unresolved reality become concrete without becoming user-authored?

**Exact questions:** Q-WCOMP-001, Q-LAZY-001, Q-FLESH-001, Q-HISTBRIDGE-001, Q-KNOW-001, Q-ASSERT-001, Q-WORLDPRIOR-001 and Q-CURRENTINGRESS-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION. Q-MEANINGSTRATA-001, Q-SELFREAD-001 and Q-FRAMEMETAB-001 are also implementation frontiers, but their full richness need not precede a bounded ordinary-life demonstration. Q-RAND-001 is POST_ALPHA.

**Why it matters:** the player must be accountable to what is there. If searching for betrayal creates betrayal, or a good interpretation rewrites the past, apparent skill becomes prompt authorship. If hidden dependencies never resolve until the player looks, independence is equally fictitious.

**Already constrained:** query determines locus, not truth; canonical truth can precede disclosure; actor/world causal demand can trigger materialization; historical completion has bounded causal capacity and preserves contradiction; testimony records the speech act, not objective truth. Current named public facts require authority within the declared anchor; model priors are insufficient. [E02](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e02), [E06](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), [E08–E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08); SPD-0232, SPD-0235–0237, SPD-0239, SPD-0243; RISK-032, RISK-192–194, RISK-196, RISK-199.

**Underdetermined:** admission/replay contracts, bounded hidden overhang, history reconciliation, recognition and knowledge provenance, currentness-collision behavior, late root-fact propagation into existing branches.

**Next local work:** use a small immutable synthetic truth fixture for the first prototypes; explicitly mark it as fixture authority, not a generated or empirically representative person. Separately compare adversarially phrased queries and actor-only causal demand against a bounded lazy resolver. Require query-invariant historical truth, lawful hidden consequences, stable prior history and no forbidden disclosure. This avoids making a whole generator a prerequisite for testing G1–G3 while still testing the foundation it will need.

**Defer:** universal symbolic meaning graphs, limitless generated backstory, curated object mysteries, broad world-generation architecture changes. A new analytical name does not earn a new owner.

## G5 — How can a continuing life remain approachable?

**Exact questions:** Q-MSGTIME-001, Q-ABSENCE-001 and Q-PENDINGSCENE-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION; Q-DUALUI-001, Q-TETHERHEALTH-001, Q-TIME-001, Q-MSG-001 and Q-UX-001 are ALPHA_VALIDATION. Q-REALTIME-001 is closed for alpha.

**Why it matters:** independent time can make an encounter consequential, but an unusable scheduling system can make lawful play practically unavailable. Messaging is a different causal channel, not a full-access substitute for co-presence.

**Already constrained:** no meaningful player autopilot; no punishment inferred from app inactivity; actor purposes cause invitations; default elastic rendezvous separates diegetic timing from a bounded real user window; pending dependency closure prevents future leakage. Settings and restart deliberation do not become actor evidence. [E07](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e07), [E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10); SPD-0176–0178, SPD-0242; RISK-198.

**Underdetermined:** exact window lengths, cancellation/expiry handling, message timestamps and visibility, safe world catch-up, notification ergonomics.

**Next local work:** replay the same arrangement through attended, canceled, expired and long-absence paths. Demonstrate no invented consent/apology, no future exposure, no guilt notification and no disappearance of independently caused commitments. Test usable return with notifications disabled.

**Defer:** appointment streaks, real-time prestige, contact cadence optimization and claims about healthy long-term attachment.

## G6 — Which constraints and capacities actually belong to this player here?

**Exact questions:** Q-EMBODYTRANS-001, Q-RESOURCE-001, Q-PLAYERRES-001 and Q-PORTALBIND-001 are ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION; Q-ENTRY-001 and Q-BRIEFING-001 are ALPHA_VALIDATION. Q-LANG-001 and Q-ASSIM-001 remain POST_ALPHA. The homologous-peer direction itself is provisionally answered under Q-PEER-001 and Q-HABITUS-001.

**Why it matters:** finite attention has little force if the user can import arbitrary money, access or embodied power. Globally random people also cannot be reduced to a universal fluent-English conversational substrate without altering what faculties are being exercised.

**Already constrained:** locally coherent peer affordances, actual user judgment, no generated alternate consciousness, no cultural conformity score; simulated physical ability is not verified external-human skill. [E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03); SPD-0076–0078, SPD-0205.

**Underdetermined:** minimum participant facts, capability admission, resource bounds and explanation; translation's preservation of register/pragmatic uncertainty; accessibility scaffolding that does not supply the substantive inference.

**Next local work:** bind one explicit player-presence envelope, then test “I pay,” “I drive,” “I explain” and an impossible access claim. Use a second contrasting context before claiming global transfer. Broader language closure requires its own court; the first prototype must state its language limitation.

**Defer:** global difficulty curves, national archetypes, cultural mastery and universal claims from one convenient fixture.

## The evidence gate

Q-GAME-001 being `CLOSED_FOR_ALPHA` means the action skeleton is specified sufficiently at that grain. Q-ALPHA-001 remains the sole ALPHA_SPEC blocker; Q-FIRSTHOOK-001, Q-EARLYREWARD-001, Q-RECIPROCITY-001, Q-SYC-001, Q-TOXICREL-001 and Q-UX-001 still need prototype evidence. Lawful operation and compelling experience are separate findings.

Use exposure receipts under SPD-0244 and the self-selection limit under SPD-0245. A user cannot demonstrate inattention to what was never surfaced. Liking an eloquent transcript does not establish causal agency. A lawful refusal does not establish enjoyable resistance. No new high-level gameplay court should bypass these distinctions.
