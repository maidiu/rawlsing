# Astra Sorok — Native Gameplay Necessity Synthesis R1

**Disposition: `PARTIALLY_MATERIALIZED__TARGETED_GROUND_WORK_REQUIRED`.**

Rawlsing already has substantial sources of play in its constitution. The strongest are **judgment while inquiry changes the situation, actual contribution to independently caused activity, and history that changes the meaning and possibilities of later participation**. What remains insufficiently materialized is how those properties become intelligible and consequential in an ordinary solo encounter. More high-level gamification would currently obscure that frontier.

The recommendation is to adjudicate three local seams and test them in three linked prototypes: the available evidence and control cadence; actor agenda and selective uptake; and the causal return of history through renewed contact. These should work with an ordinary, peripheral or difficult person, without guaranteed intimacy, dramatic events, assigned missions or social amplification.

This is a substantive return for HQ review, **not an adopted kernel, implementation specification, runtime validation or playtest result**.

## Pass I — The organism being interrogated

Rawlsing is a governed source → structural world → generated life/person → situated event/response → expression → admitted history system. It is not a demographic persona sheet followed by fluent dialogue.

The actor-independent world supplies lawful possibilities and constraints. V3 realizes a particular developmental history through encounters, relationships and responses; structural availability does not establish an actor's belief, experience or identity. The present person is relationally formed without being owned or exhausted by the current dyad. They retain a wider material and social life, other relations, contradictory organizations and limited self-knowledge. Sorok's relationalization changes the sufficiency questions asked of the existing machinery; it does not authorize a second psyche. [E02–E03](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e02).

At encounter time, actual events meet this particular person's receivability, uptake/appraisal, recruited organization and current condition through CAN-008 R2. The result may involve multiple simultaneous causal streams, partial response, inhibition or lawful emptiness. PFF has a narrowed interaction role and bounded fast carryover; durable memory, relationship, belief and developmental effects remain with competent V3 owners. V2 expresses actor-accessible material and cannot restore hidden knowledge to make a response more satisfying. A proposed utterance is not an occurred event; durable consequence requires exact admission and an authorized transition. [E05](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e05), [E12](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e12).

The user participates through a bounded diegetic presence while retaining their actual judgment. Their attempted speech, action and attention are theirs; feasibility, reception and effect are not. The system's model of user praxis, the actor's belief about the player and the external human's actual inner state remain distinct. A declared physical ability can be represented under player rules without proving that the human possesses it. Native explanation, inquiry, timing and judgment must instead enter through actual contributions. [E03–E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), SPD-0010, SPD-0076–0080.

What the user sees is neither the entire world nor an author-curated sequence of important clues. Finite phenomenal sampling, embodied ATTEND and recognition-gated language expose warranted evidence. Canonical materialization can extend beyond disclosure, including when a hidden actor/world process needs a fact before the user looks. Actor testimony remains testimony. History can later disclose something antecedently true or acquire newly created meaning; these are different causal operations. [E08–E09](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), SPD-0235–0239, SPD-0248.

The current constitution therefore contains independent actuality, open future possibility and mediated causal participation. It does **not** yet contain evidence that these reliably produce compelling play. Q-GAME-001 is closed for alpha at action-skeleton grain, while Q-ALPHA-001 remains the sole ALPHA_SPEC blocker. The exact register has 47 implementation, 25 validation, 19 post-alpha and 31 NONE-stage questions besides that one specification blocker; Q-KERNEL-001 is still researching within NONE. Artifact 37's earlier accounting cannot be used as the current count. [E01](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e01).

## Finding 1 — The deepest immediate tension is acting while finding out

The player cannot simply collect a complete model and then act on it. Attention has duration and bodily conditions; questions and some inspections are events another actor may perceive; the actor's uptake can change the next situation. Where these conditions apply, gathering evidence and changing the world are coupled. A question can clarify, redirect, embarrass, amuse, be ignored or close a conversational route. This is derived from D002's temporal/reciprocal observation laws and artifact 80's actor-testimony and observable-attention rules, not from the later “game loops” discussion. [M02–M04](DERIVATION_MATRIX.md#m02--learning-can-alter-the-conditions-of-further-learning).

That makes several real faculties consequential together: deciding what matters, choosing whether to inspect or ask, formulating a question, recognizing ambiguity, acting before certainty and revising afterward. It also makes restraint potentially competent. But restraint is not a universal correct response; the person may welcome inquiry, and a timely direct question may be excellent.

The hidden product requirement is **enough evidence for judgment without supplying the judgment**. Recognition-gated narration is a game mechanic because naming the object can consume the very expertise the interface should let the user exercise. Control-return timing is equally constitutive: if the engine completes the user's explanation or waits through the meaningful interruption, the user's skill never enters the causal chain. Conversely, too little orientation or too many microsteps make the experience a parser problem. The exact ground seam is G1; its consequence is not resolved by simply writing richer prose.

## Finding 2 — Development includes creating history, not only learning more about someone

An objective convention, remembered encounter or practical dependency can come into existence through interaction. Later action is then received against conditions that did not previously exist. Shared practice may converge before shared interpretation does: the two participants can agree on a routine while disagreeing about what it means. Thus repeating the same outward act can now honor an expectation, overstep, continue a joke or require renegotiation. None requires a relationship level. [M07–M09](DERIVATION_MATRIX.md#m07--history-changes-the-meaning-of-repetition), artifact 53, SPD-0238; RISK-195.

This distinguishes **unveiling** from **development**. Looking at a repaired object may uncover evidence of a prior event. Later doing something with that object may make it significant for a new reason. Offering an interpretation may change the actor's future self-understanding without becoming the truth about their past. A user's contribution can be substantial without retroactively making them the person this life was waiting for. Artifact 70's bounded historical bridge, artifact 71's meaning distinctions and artifact 83's anti-teleology correction jointly require this separation. [E08–E10](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e08), SPD-0212, SPD-0235, SPD-0243; RISK-192, RISK-199.

The practical frontier is G3: occurrence must alter later possibilities through memory, expectation, convention, access and actor-local meaning where warranted. Merely mentioning an earlier phrase is not evidence of this machinery. Nor must every event leave a durable trace. A peripheral relation can develop genuine specificity; a close relation can become more complicated; a healthy boundary can narrow access.

## Finding 3 — Skill can become more useful while the person becomes less reducible

Independent causality is not arbitrary resistance. An actor's learned habits, commitments and patterns can be stable enough for the player to act more effectively. The same actor can also respond differently when context, counterpart, current load or history changes. Competence consists partly in knowing the limits of a local model, rather than acquiring an input policy that guarantees desired outputs. The game must not sabotage correct predictions simply to preserve mystery. [M05–M06](DERIVATION_MATRIX.md#m05--competence-is-local-and-revisable-not-necessarily-powerless), CAN-008 R2, SPD-0205, SPD-0234.

This is why consequence cannot be compressed into feedback. A practical action may succeed while its reception is mixed; a refusal may anger someone without being harmful; an important effect may remain unknown to the user. The interface should clarify what the player actually did and what evidence is available, while preserving uncertainty about inaccessible meanings and downstream effects. Flourishing and user causal credit remain distinct, open questions. [E04](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04), [E11](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e11).

The native form of mastery is consequently **better situated participation**, not moral rank or person-control. SPD-0234's “model fidelity up, totalization down” is a valuable discriminating hypothesis, but no improvement in real users has been demonstrated here. The medium can exercise these faculties; it cannot certify empathy, wisdom, spiritual growth or unaided skill from text alone.

## The strongest synthesis, and its exact limit

SPD-0010 grants attempted agency while withholding ownership of effect. SPD-0025 preserves independent agendas. SPD-0076–0080 require actual native contribution. CAN-008 R2 mediates its uptake through a particular person. SPD-0172 preserves occurred history, and SPD-0238 allows that history to carry divergent meanings. Composed, these laws yield:

**The player must make a real contribution under partial knowledge; an independently caused world/person receives it; what occurs may change the history against which the player next understands and acts.**

That is a warranted refinement of the diagnostic possibility/otherness intuition. It is stronger than a thematic resemblance because every causal step has pre-checkpoint parents. It is narrower than a universal kernel: independent agency does not logically entail ethical regard, product pleasure, sustainable costs or an invitation economy. Some normative and product commitments still require independent grounds. D007 should remain open.

This synthesis also has an adverse case: a perfectly independent, opaque and historically coherent person can still be boring to encounter. An interface can meet every negative prohibition while offering too little evidence or reciprocal possibility to engage a human faculty. That is why the disposition is partial materialization, and why ordinary solo prototypes—not more elegant causal diagrams—are now decisive.

## What is sufficient, and what is not

| Already sufficient at constitutional grain | Still required before claiming an experiential core |
|---|---|
| SAY/DO/ATTEND, attempted agency, native-contribution rule | A usable evidence/control cadence with lawful time and interruption |
| Independent life, person-event mediation and actor opacity | Concrete selective uptake, effort and initiative rules that produce particular responses |
| Occurrence, irreversible history, nonmonotonic access | A demonstrated path from an admitted event to later convention/meaning/possibility |
| Materialization/disclosure separation and anti-retcon law | Replayable bounded resolution and exposure/knowledge provenance |
| Absence, message/co-presence distinction and elastic rendezvous direction | Usable cancellation, expiry, catch-up and return without calendar coercion |
| Rejection of person-completion and external metagame obligations | Evidence that ordinary solo encounters are worthwhile under those constraints |

There is enough to identify native candidates N1–N4: finding out while acting, actual co-activity, living with history, and returning to a changed present. There is not enough to claim their engagement, broad generality or production sufficiency. N5 comparison and N6 reflective practice are merely compatible additions.

Across the strata, A–B chiefly involve present judgment and participation; C–D add history and continuing life; E permits meaningful finite or peripheral histories without completion. F supplies no derived universal mission across random persons. G can amplify an already functioning solo core. H may support accumulated faculty and self-understanding, but no inevitable progression. ## What remains genuinely open

The most consequential unresolved choice is how much reality and control must be available at each moment for the user to act intelligently without the interface performing the cognition. The next is how an independent person selectively takes up and initiates interaction. The third is how history creates later differences without becoming a scalar relation model. These are G1–G3; truth materialization/provenance in G4 is their common prerequisite.

Other open matters should not be hidden: cross-language pragmatics, resource/embodiment binding, the normative account of flourishing, long-term benefit, production cost and the kernel. The existing relational-warrant proposal also has a real tension: a gate to another person can function as a reward even without visible points. SPD-0092 excludes simple approval and closeness, but does not by itself prove that an implementation avoids instrumentalization. Q-SUCCESSION-001 needs its own adjudication; this return does not operationalize it.

No additional extrinsic gamification layer is derived. Practical constraints, local goals and meaningful consequences may already give play structure, but an imposed universal task, progression ladder or global thesis is not needed by the current laws. The negative evidence's objection to arbitrary undertakings must not erase the baseline's lawful practical co-activity; the distinction is whether the situation follows from the life or is imposed to supply a game-shaped purpose. [Hostile review](NEGATIVE_CONTROLS.md).
