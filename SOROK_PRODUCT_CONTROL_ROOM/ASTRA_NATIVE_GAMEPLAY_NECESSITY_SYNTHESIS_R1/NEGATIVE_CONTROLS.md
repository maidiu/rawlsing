## What the later failed search does and does not establish

1. **“Action → history → changed future”** was criticized for being prematurely promoted to the master loop. M07 independently derives path dependence from exact event, memory, convention and uptake laws. The causal claim survives; the conclusion “therefore fun” does not.
2. **Bounded undertakings** felt imposed. This does not invalidate the baseline's homework, planning and task-entry cases in artifacts 27–29. Their lawful status depends on actual world/actor purposes and actual user contribution, not on assigning every life a useful task. N2 preserves the former and rejects the universal shell.
3. **Becoming noninterchangeable / finding one's role** turns an emergent outcome into a target. N3 therefore concerns acting within a particular history, including peripheral contact and departure. It does not prescribe becoming important.
4. **Cross-persona investigations and existential theses** do not follow from globally random independent lives. N6 permits bounded reflection by the user, but does not supply a question every persona exists to answer.
5. **Possibility meeting independent actuality** survives only in a refined form. SPD-0010 and SPD-0025 establish attempted agency and independent life; SPD-0076–0080 require actual contribution; CAN-008 R2 supplies mediated response; SPD-0172 supplies irreversible occurrence; SPD-0205 denies sovereignty. Together these justify accountable participation. They do not alone derive Rawlsing's ethics, kernel, pleasure or long-term viability.

The user's reported dissatisfaction is a reason to interrogate our method. It is not empirical validation that one phrase or mechanic is correct.

## Revisions forced by hostile review

The report does **not** claim that every random persona will compel every user, that every uncertainty is productive, that every failure continues, that deeper knowing necessarily improves conduct, or that a lawful engine automatically produces a viable product.

N1–N4 survive as **falsifiable forms of play**, not promises. N5–N6 remain optional and deferred. Full cross-persona gamification is not derived. The report's terminal disposition is consequently partial materialization, not sufficiency for an engagement or kernel claim.

The strongest remaining threat is not lack of an extrinsic goal. It is that the interface may supply too little usable evidence, actor response may be too generic, or history may be too weakly causal for the user's faculties to matter. Those failures would make even excellent philosophical commitments experientially thin. The finite plan tests them directly.
