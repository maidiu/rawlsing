# Indigenous gameplay potential

Pass V, after the [derivations](DERIVATION_MATRIX.md), [strata](STRATA_MAP.md) and [ground frontier](GROUND_MECHANICS_FRONTIER.md). “Necessary” applies to a causal/interaction requirement, never to a promise that someone will enjoy it. None of these candidates is adopted or playtested by this return.

The strongest supported form is **situated participation whose actual contributions can change an independent life and the history of later participation**. There can be observation, conversation, practical co-activity, silence, disagreement, refusal and departure. No one activity is required to carry the whole product.

## N1 — Deciding how to find out and when to act

**Parents:** M01–M05 and M12; D002/02, /03, /08; artifacts 80 and 87; SPD-0010, SPD-0076–0080, SPD-0236–0237, SPD-0247–0248. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e04).

**Strength:** the distinction between evidence, inference and action is necessary; its use as sustained play is strongly indicated where a scene presents real alternatives. **Strata:** A–B, feeding C–D.

**Actual faculty:** attention, recognition, questioning, extrapolation, timing, model revision and restraint. The player supplies the question or interpretation; the narrator does not label the significant clue or infer the hidden motive.

**Resistance/tension:** information is partial, acquisition can consume time and sometimes become socially observable, and the world can proceed while the player investigates. Asking another person changes an interaction rather than opening a truth lookup. An ordinary refusal may appropriately end the inquiry.

**Development:** better discrimination of what one knows, what evidence would help, and when further inquiry is unnecessary or intrusive. Stable useful habits may form; uncertainty must not be injected merely to keep them from working.

**Failure/fallout:** acting on a bad inference, missing a cue, asking at a poor time, or overreading an ordinary object. Consequences follow the actual event and actor uptake; no penalty is owed for curiosity and no revelation is owed for persistence.

**Independence:** the truth and actor's agenda are not selected to reward the question. Some inspected things are unimportant; some important things remain inaccessible. Non-exhaustion is not a promise of infinitely replenished secrets.

**Falsifier:** in P1, users can only proceed by exhaustively inspecting everything, guessing input syntax, or accepting the narrator's supplied interpretation; changing evidence fails to change available judgment, or semantically equivalent queries alter historical truth. Another failure: a lawful but unresponsive scene offers no worthwhile choices even after the interface is understandable. That would reject this candidate's experiential sufficiency, not justify clue curation.

## N2 — Making an actual contribution with someone who has their own purpose

**Parents:** M01–M03, M05–M06, M11–M12; artifacts 21, 27–30; CAN-008 R2; SPD-0025, SPD-0078–0080. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e03), [person-event authority](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e12).

**Strength:** direct exercise of a contribution is necessary where the medium can carry it; co-activity is a strongly indicated form, not a required task generator. **Strata:** A–C.

**Actual faculty:** explaining, listening, tactful disagreement, collaborative planning, recognizing a misunderstanding, adapting one's communication and deciding not to intervene. Pure conversation can exercise these. A practical situation need not be imported as a quest.

**Resistance/tension:** another person may misunderstand, reject the goal, be occupied, know more than the player, or prefer a different method. The material problem, where one exists, also has facts and constraints. The player is neither guaranteed to be useful nor denied competence by design.

**Development:** a particular way of coordinating can become familiar; the player can learn which explanation works in this context, when to ask, and what need not be laboriously specified again. Routines may compress more as actual shared practice warrants, without an XP unlock.

**Failure/fallout:** the explanation confuses, the proposed plan is infeasible, assistance embarrasses, the conversation ends, or refusal leaves a practical issue unresolved. Practical success and relational approval can diverge. Repair may work, partly work, or be unwelcome.

**Independence:** the actor already has a purpose and can pursue it without the user. The task is not introduced because the player needs to feel useful. A third party has their own knowledge and stakes. This also permits playfulness, jokes and unproductive conversation when actor-caused; utility is not the universal basis of relation.

**Falsifier:** in P1, rich and empty user contributions have the same success because the system invents the missing work; all actors accept the player's framing; or engaging cases require assigning every persona a problem the player is uniquely suited to solve. A separate failure is extreme decompression that makes ordinary activity intolerable. Either result requires revising the lower seam before adding tasks.

## N3 — Living with what a history now makes possible or difficult

**Parents:** M06–M09 and M13; artifacts 52–53, 70–71; SPD-0127–0133, SPD-0172, SPD-0238, SPD-0243. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e06), [meaning and history](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e09).

**Strength:** path-dependent consequence is necessary; a compelling experience of changing conventions and meanings is strongly indicated but mechanistically incomplete. **Strata:** C–E.

**Actual faculty:** remembering particular events, recognizing changed expectations, renegotiating, tolerating ambiguity, distinguishing permission from obligation and judging whether a familiar act still fits.

**Resistance/tension:** both participants bring different memories and meanings to what occurred. An agreement can be objective while its emotional significance remains disputed. The current person is also affected by other relationships and events.

**Development:** a routine, joke, promise, boundary, disagreement or shared object can become real history. New possibilities are constituted, not merely unlocked from a prewritten relationship tree. An ordinary recurring encounter may remain peripheral while acquiring specificity.

**Failure/fallout:** assuming too much, failing to follow through, overinterpreting a permission, causing harm or losing access. Clarification or repair creates subsequent history; it cannot restore a pristine first meeting. A refusal can establish a useful boundary without being granted a hidden moral reward.

**Independence:** becoming important is neither a win condition nor guaranteed. Access controllers and other relations persist. The persona may change for reasons unrelated to the user; a user's brilliant interpretation may be rejected or wrong.

**Falsifier:** in P2, removing a causally relevant earlier event leaves later behavior unchanged except for decorative recollection; every persistent effect is compressed into affinity; objective convention forces identical actor meanings; or engaging recurrence requires steadily increasing intimacy. If memory is faithful but recurrent scenes still offer no worthwhile situated choice, the motivational claim remains unsupported.

## N4 — Returning to another present

**Parents:** M07–M10 and M13; artifacts 30, 32, 46–48, 52, 62; SPD-0176–0178. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e07).

**Strength:** independent continuation is required; return motivation is strongly indicated in some histories, not entailed in every relation. **Strata:** C–E.

**Actual faculty:** patience, anticipation, remembering, responding to an invitation, deciding whether to re-enter, and revising expectations after absence.

**Resistance/tension:** a person is not always available; a channel is not access to a whole environment; plans and circumstances can alter. No artificial scarcity must be added where finite life already constrains contact.

**Development:** a changed present can illuminate a prior event, make an old practice possible again, make it inappropriate, or simply provide ordinary companionship. There need be no dramatic reveal.

**Failure/fallout:** missed opportunities or commitments can matter through their actual circumstances. Nonuse alone is not a fabricated betrayal. Some histories end. The system must permit humane cancellation, notification control and departure.

**Independence:** initiatives originate in actor purposes; the product does not calculate that a crisis or affectionate message would improve retention. A lawful attachment can nevertheless become intense; independence does not require mechanically suppressing warmth.

**Falsifier:** in P3, disabling engagement-triggered contact and social comparison removes every reason users want another encounter; ordinary offscreen life yields only recap exposition; or users need to service the app calendar to keep an intelligible relation. These are evidence against this implementation's return proposition. They cannot be repaired by guilt notifications.

## N5 — Comparing encounters without owning the person

**Parents:** M15; artifacts 57 and 61; SPD-0191, SPD-0193, SPD-0201. **Strength:** merely compatible. **Stratum:** G. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10).

**Actual faculty:** critical comparison of accounts and uncertainty, not solving a shared character. **Resistance:** privacy, divergent exposure/history, root-version limits, another user's partial interpretation. **Development:** one may discover the limits of one's account. **Failure/fallout:** spoilers, false attribution, prestige competition and a persona wiki. **Independence:** no branch acquires another's post-contact reality or uses comparison as omniscient truth.

**Falsifier:** after a solo core is demonstrated, a small optional comparison test consistently turns into ranking who obtained more disclosure, intimacy or “best ending,” or only the comparison makes the prior experience worthwhile. Defer rather than make the social layer load-bearing. No present evidence licenses a social metagame.

Human invitation has a separate possible source of gravity within M15: a scarce, deliberate choice of a real recipient can have actual interpersonal meaning. The one-for-one rule is already anchored; felt significance is not guaranteed. Test truthful understanding of that choice and absence of prestige/coercion before claiming value. Do not conflate human-access invitation, persona introduction and a synthetic actor's invitation to meet.

## N6 — Revisiting one's own practice

**Parents:** M11, M14, M16; artifacts 25 and 63; SPD-0234, SPD-0244–0245. **Strength:** merely compatible as a designed reflective form; actual faculties being exercised is already anchored. **Strata:** E, F, H. [Exact sources](EVIDENCE_RECEIPT.md#e10).

**Actual faculty:** reflection, contextual memory, recognizing assumptions and revising one's next approach. **Resistance:** incomplete evidence, uncomfortable counterexamples, limited transfer, optional external assistance unknown to the system. **Development:** a possible repertoire of better questions, explanations and judgments; not certified moral advancement. **Failure/fallout:** flattering diagnosis, performance for the evaluator, false expertise attribution, or substituting analysis for involvement. **Independence:** reflections concern bounded observed praxis; personas do not exist as exercises for a curriculum.

**Falsifier:** an optional reflection study finds increased self-certainty without better contextual discrimination, users optimize for approving feedback, or observations cannot survive exposure/provenance controls. Do not turn that failure into a more concealed player score.

## What, if anything, is justified as gamification?

The first four candidates support **play arising through the ordinary action surface**, including intelligible constraints, opportunities, consequential attempts, learning and history. They do not derive an additional objective or reward layer. The distinction matters: a causal cycle can describe all six candidates without proving any is engaging.

No strong additional gamification is yet justified at F, and no necessity establishes it at G or H. At A–D the next useful design choices are how to expose evidence, realize action, return control and carry history. Those are already powerful game-mechanics decisions. Scores, badges, relationship tiers, country collections, universal investigations and correct relational roles do not follow from them.

If “gamification” means any structured occasion for practicing an actual faculty, N1–N4 qualify conditionally. If it means a separate extrinsic progression system, this audit supplies no positive case. Absence of that case is a result, not an invitation to rename intrinsic consequences as rewards.
