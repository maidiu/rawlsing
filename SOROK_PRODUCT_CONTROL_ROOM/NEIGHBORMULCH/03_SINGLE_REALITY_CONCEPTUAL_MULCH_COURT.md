## I. What does "one reality" mean?

**Not** one codebase, one class, one file, one database file or one theory.
**Not** one consciousness representing all knowable facts.
**Not** a user-facing transcript called canonical by convention.

**Yes:** a single causally authorized history of one world, in which each world event, actor attempt, physical outcome, actor-accessible sign, actor experience, personal reconstitution and finite user-visible presentation has its own differentiated relation to that history. Multiple computational representations are acceptable **when only one legitimately causes each canonical transition**, all others are derived or separately authorized, and their mappings survive replay, fork, crash and revocation.

Six distinct realities may need multiple stores or indexes but not rival truths:

1. **Objective material/world history** — who physically acted, moved, owned, held rights, occupied a room, changed an object, emitted sound, set a procedure, underwent a sanction, with the world's independent eligibility and time.
2. **Potentially accessible event affordances** — sound propagation, visual geometry, facing, peripheral motion, obstruction, lighting, body capacity, attention load, distance, latency, privacy. "Person in the room" is not "person saw the user."
3. **Actor-specific lived experience** — exactly what this person actually received and enacted in this interval, and what could remain unreceived despite a true world event.
4. **Actor-constituted psychological organization** — previous source-grounded form/relation/project/inhibition, attention/anticipation, recruited possibilities and post-event reconstitution. Usually not readable by actor, user or narrator as an omniscient self-account.
5. **User-specific finite phenomenal surface** — the user's actual available/perceived/recognized/exposed evidence, mediated by MII attention, but not controlling Nadia's life. A private gaze choice stays private unless outward conduct changes.
6. **Historical materialization/knowledge** — what is true and source-qualified about a world's earlier material past, what is still unresolved, what was **actually experienced** by a person, and what is only a plausible, admissible historical completion. Mechanism I may enrich independent object history without retroactively fabricating Nadia's childhood memory.

**Cardinal invariant:** Different epistemic access must be allowed to produce different beliefs **about one world**, not alternate worlds manufactured for their convenience.

## II. Event-time and causal order (proposed conceptual topology)

An ordinary encounter need not be dialogue-first. Begin with a world and independently preoccupied actors. Then:

A. **Pre-existing life**: ongoing work, projects, bodily needs, objects, relationships, institutional/physical boundaries, source-qualified history, separate Others and living future prospects. The world does not wait for the user.

B. **User's human contribution**: an utterance, query, inspect, physical move, attempt, refusal, delay or silence. A free-text input can convey intention or proposed content; it does NOT pre-authorize success, the other's response, objective truth or a hidden interpretation.

C. **Admission/realization**: an authorized action/world owner validates and commits actual occurrence(s), distinguishing intent, effort, possibility, obstruction, performed behavior and externally settled effect. Not every move succeeds. Waiting costs time and can matter even if nothing dramatic occurs.

D. **Actor-relative sensory/attentional access**: the material event yields potentially accessible modality/geometry/time evidence. Actual actor bodily and situational attention qualify what is received. MIII's footsteps-behind and visible outward-facing distinctions must survive. The organism may historically influence the actor's attention and anticipation, **not** manufacture sensory input or direct access to the user's private mind.

E. **Independent actor recruitment**: the *actual continuing organism* receives only qualified access and its own historical state; projects/needs/relations/contradictions/psyche may influence attention and selection. Model proposals are **hypotheses admitted to source owners**; the model may not arbitrarily reconstruct a new Nadia from MIII's small scene fixture.

F. **Activity and world settlement**: actor independently speaks, looks, leaves, continues a task, declines, risks, errs, imposes, suffers, ignores or does nothing. A physical/world owner decides what actually happens, including objective harm, nonconsent, resource limits and actions of independent Others. An actor's good intention is not proof of good effect.

G. **Differentiated afterlife**: effects on world, actor's actual experience, provisional psychological changes and historical knowledge are committed only under their appropriate owners, without retroactively turning one into the other. Offscreen other-actor activity continues independently.

H. **Finite presentation and return**: MII/P5/P6 present only user-licensed perceptible consequences, without disclosing secrets or supplying user's conclusions. D002 returns control at a real decision boundary, not after the engine has silently authored the user's next act.

I. **Next contact**: cold restart, fork or elapsed ordinary time cannot erase actual consequences; two otherwise similar people or two different user histories may react differently **for causal reasons**. No forced intimacy or revelation.

This ordering is a conceptual relationship, **not a mandatory sequence of nine software modules**. Real systems can schedule concurrent events, asynchronous Mechanism I work and autonomous activity, but any single committed transition must preserve causal precedence, objective time and source authority.

## IV. The writer-adoption options — investigate; do not preselect

**Option A — P4/P6 authoritative, organism becomes recruited causal actor/afterlife consumer.**
Potential merit: preserves existing protected human-action/outward/MII/MIII event identities and historical owner; may require substantial organism transition/SQLite reengineering to write within P4 source/actor rights. Hazard: shrinking the organism to a narrator or losing committed development.

**Option B — Organism event store authoritative, MIII/MII/MI become faithful readers/eligible producers.**
Potential merit: preserves continuous person/project/psychic development and living world as an integrated runtime. Hazard: losing rich protected sensory/gaze/MI source-time gates, historical event/branch semantics and existing user/p4 consumer behavior. **Untested latest R25 cannot outrank protected solely by being newest.**

**Option C — Refactor one existing owner into a generalized shared transaction/projection kernel, transplanting genuinely better functions from the other.**
Potential merit: both historical lines survive in one owner without two separately committed events. Hazard: most complex migration and potential silent loss of previously accepted guarantees; requires exact old-to-new replay, source/rights and independent receiving. Do not create a fourth engine just to avoid reconciling old ones.

**Option D — Two physical stores, ONE authoritative world writer + derived actor/use projections.**
Potential merit: separate canonical world and actor-private history may be physically desirable; recognized material vs lived relation is not identical data. Hazard: a derived store that can independently decide/commit the same world event becomes a second authority. Require explicit write-domain separation, idempotent event identity, transaction/outbox replay and cold reconciliation.

**Immediately REJECT:** "Call both runtimes with the same action and record both world events, then assert parity." It is a dual-sovereign system even when tests agree by accident.

**Adoption evidence:** frozen exact source/head; preserved every necessary historical first reader; single event identity; correct source/actor/time permissions; no unearned actor memory; private gaze independence; world-settled constraints; negative and crash/replay tests; no regression of current joined green; independent reviewer after author execution; qualified protected receiving only by competent owner.

## V. What would actually change the theory?

Run material counterexamples:
- The user privately shifts attention to Nadia but makes no outward change: MII presentation history may change, but Nadia's evidence and response **must not** change.
- The user physically turns or approaches; Nadia hears steps behind while facing away: new audible evidence may arrive, but the private object of the gaze and visual face detail remain unavailable.
- Two adults share a current event but differ in acquired projects/relational histories: actual qualified action recruitment may differ; no deterministic "same stimulus → same NPC".
- A historical object's past is newly source-admitted by MI: inspection may reveal facts, but no actress receives retrospective false memory.
- Nadia never meets the user while growing up: years of world/actor history and offscreen caregiver/child becoming persist before any user-access root.
- The actual world blocks the user's or actor's attempt: narrative cannot grant permissions by reading a psychodynamic motive.
- The actor believes the user insulted them, wrongly: belief may become historically operative without changing the objective user utterance.
- The user goes away and returns after time; the actor did not freeze in place waiting for a prompt.

If a counterexample fails because "access" has been reduced to an inventory of visible things, reconceive access. If it fails because "continuation" means only reloading a frozen person, reconceive offscreen life. If "display" requires leaked private psychological truth, reconceive phenomenal selection. **Change theory as well as code.**
