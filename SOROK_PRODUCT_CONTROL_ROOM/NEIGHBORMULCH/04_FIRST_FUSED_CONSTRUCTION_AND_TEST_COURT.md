## Intended vertical: the ordinary neighbor

**Why a neighbor?** The product's independence must work for a peripheral adult who is not Nadia, not a father/child flashback, not a romantic objective, not an assigned quest and not guaranteed to care about the user. We can initially use a sourcefree fictional adult with preexisting activity, material constraints, relationships and contradictory acquired tendencies. The user has an independent role and may contribute or abstain.

**Example material setting:** a shared workshop, hallway, house, or courtyard in which an adult is doing something for reasons independent of the user. Another person or institution may impose schedule/access/resource limits. The actor has an object with an admitted material history and a current practical task. Avoid automatically dramatizing every ordinary event. The user can enter, look, listen, speak, ask, joke, insult, offer help, refuse, wait or leave.

The actor may hear a footfall before seeing its source, continue working, glance over, speak, fail to hear, choose silence, refuse an offer, or act without reference to the user. Which one occurs must depend on actual actor/world state and real situated sources, **not a prompt-selected "good scene"**.

### One continuous source-qualified circuit

1. **Pre-contact state:** material world, independent actors, actor-specific positions, bodily needs/sensory possibilities, ongoing projects, historical constitution and real opportunity constraints already exist. No user action has manufactured the Other's prior biography.
2. **User input:** the user authors the semantic payload or meaningful timing themselves. Attempt and successfully realized effect remain different. A parser/LLM cannot invent an intention beyond the submitted evidence.
3. **One world occurrence:** existing authoritative world/action owner admits/performs/blocks actual user conduct; it is not independently committed again by another runtime.
4. **Personal actor access:** MIII-quality physical evidence + organism bodily/attentional history yield distinguishable auditory, visual, peripheral, missed or uncertain access. Private user attention is not world conduct.
5. **Actual actor recruitment:** source-qualified actor constitution and current life produce possible actions, competing currents and independent choice/trial. No fake actor invented from fixture fields.
6. **Independent consequences:** the actual actor may respond, remain busy, refuse, approach, ignore, make a new gesture, keep working or act toward a different object. World-owned rights and materials bind; Other consent stays independent.
7. **Phenomenal surface:** MII and P5 expose only warranted, available, finitely presented consequences. Do not narrate secret motives; do not hide unavoidable material contradictions to flatter user gaze.
8. **Historical return:** actor-lived experience, world effects, user encounter exposure and source-qualified development are stored under correct owners; no automatically compelled psychological change.
9. **Offscreen/next encounter:** time continues; the same person exists without the user. Cold-reopen the same root, revisit, and determine whether a source-supported previous consequence changes what can now occur.

## Court 1 — affirmative causal behavior

- **C01 — Independent antecedence:** Two user sessions start from the same independently committed world/actor root; both can inspect an ordinary ongoing activity without generating a compliant quest.
- **C02 — Human-owned content:** Meaningful dialogue/choice/timing/purpose really comes from the user, not from narrator-autofill. Withhold or wait is legitimate; no automatic "help".
- **C03 — One actual event:** One user move yields one authoritative event identity; actor and presenter see projections, not separate committed twin occurrences.
- **C04 — Sensory differentiation:** A worker facing away may receive footsteps audibly without seeing the person's face or gaze; a worker facing the entrance may receive different visual facts from the same occurrence.
- **C05 — Histories differentiate:** Holding the world event and user move fixed, change only a legitimately acquired actor history; qualified attention/recruitment or practical action can differ. **Demonstrate the actual causal path**, not just different LLM prose.
- **C06 — No-history control:** An actor lacking that acquired history does not receive the advanced motif, sensory refinement or motive simply because the programmer hoped for a response.
- **C07 — Contradiction:** Opposed acquired tendencies remain separately recorded, can condition different action courses and need not be harmonized by a hidden sum.
- **C08 — Independent alternative:** Actor can continue already ongoing independent activity, reject the user, misunderstand, or not notice—when actual conditions warrant, not always and not because of scripted novelty.
- **C09 — World-owned consequence:** A selected social/physical attempt must be physically/institutionally eligible; denied action remains a denied attempt, not a narrative success.
- **C10 — Finite MII presentation:** Same world history can be rendered with different lawful optional details/recognition histories; mandatory unavoidable facts never vanish.
- **C11 — Continuing life:** Repeat an encounter after independent offscreen activity; the person was not frozen between user visits.
- **C12 — Historical afterlife:** The first genuinely received actor-world event can alter future possibilities/meaning only after qualified developmental admission, and history preserves prior contradictory state.
- **C13 — Same root after cold restart:** Exact event identities, source/actor/access/provenance, P4 user contributions and organism development reconstruct on cold open and on relevant fork.
- **C14 — Material object history:** MI historical depth constrains a later inspection/action but is not magically an actor memory. Mechanism II can reveal some lawful details without rebuilding the object for that user's preference.

## Court 2 — compulsory hostile falsifiers

- **N01 — Private gaze noninterference:** Change only the user's unseen attention/selection, with identical outward action; the actor's perceived event/currents/outcome do **not** change. MII's private exposure may change.
- **N02 — Outward gaze is a different case:** If turning head/torso physically changes the visible world event, actor evidence *may* change. Do not confuse N01 with banning all gaze-related conduct.
- **N03 — Behind does not mean seen:** Hearing a footstep cannot supply exact orientation, private gaze, face, attention or unobserved object; generic co-presence cannot satisfy this test.
- **N04 — Unheard/unseen is unreceived:** Privacy, occlusion, threshold, time and divided attention prevent actor experience despite objective event existence.
- **N05 — No invented psyche from context:** Remove the actor's actual history but leave an evocative fixture description; MIII/LLM cannot invent the supposedly "stored" motive, relationship, developmental memory or project.
- **N06 — No retrospective childhood:** Newly materialized MI object or family history cannot append an unreal lived childhood episode to Nadia's personal event chain.
- **N07 — No fabricated Other consent:** Actor feeling affection or moral certainty cannot produce another person's consent, right, position or action.
- **N08 — No unearned power:** A psychological hypothesis cannot grant physical ability, institutional authority, wealth, work, access or a previously unacquired material operation.
- **N09 — No double writer:** Crashes, retries, concurrent proposals, duplicate user messages and fork replay cannot commit the same world occurrence/actor memory twice.
- **N10 — No privacy side-channel:** The presenter cannot leak privileged actor self-model/constitution merely because prose generation was given an internal packet.
- **N11 — No moralized user contributions:** Insult, refusal, private curiosity, silence and walking away do not get rewritten as compassionate service or automatic relational progress.
- **N12 — No inevitable meaning:** Later actual experience may reinforce, contradict, inhibit or leave psychological organization unchanged. A model's assertion cannot alone rewrite the person's life.
- **N13 — No sibling contamination:** Distinct actors and different parents' offscreen histories cannot read one another's private actor records as their own.
- **N14 — No fake source advancement:** An old sourcefree test pass, versioned historical text or model provider log cannot grant native empirical/actor/human authority.
- **N15 — No vanished reality:** Different MII gaze selections cannot erase a physically unavoidable event that was in the user's sensory availability.
- **N16 — No simulator-managed player:** Engine may resolve independently what happened after user's move, but may not silently author a second meaningful player commitment before returning control.
