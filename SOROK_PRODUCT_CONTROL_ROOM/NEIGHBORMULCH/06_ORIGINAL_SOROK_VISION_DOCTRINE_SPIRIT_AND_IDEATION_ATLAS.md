## I. The real first entrance: the charter, before architecture

**Primary source:** __PRODUCT_CHARTER__ (`01_PRODUCT_CHARTER.md`), September 29, **PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW_R1**.

SOROK was neither conceived as a morally obedient NPC nor as a maximal realism benchmark. Its **proposed transformative purpose**: make the contingency and relational formation of personhood experientially graspable. In the charter's original terms, a person could have been otherwise under another world; a particular other life makes claims upon the way one justifies institutions. The conceptual movement is **contingency → humility → recognition → solidarity → responsibility**; the theological phrase *tikkun olam* names a horizon of material/relational repair, not an automatically authenticated metaphysical theorem.

The pivotal user-facing proposition was **a neighbor, not a preselected fantasy of an attractive, useful, ideologically agreeable or entertaining character**. The original charter asks whether a stranger can become specific and consequential enough to become kin, without requiring the kinship result. Later source `45` preserves the invitation: **“Become neighbor to a life that could have been yours.”**

The charter's extreme noncollapses:
- **attachment ≠ trust ≠ health ≠ pleasure ≠ compliance ≠ intimacy ≠ benefit ≠ dependency ≠ access.**
- A user can matter greatly to another person and harm them. A healthy boundary may make a relationship more difficult, not smoother. Genuine conflict is not a loss on a happiness meter.
- User transformation is evidentiary, rooted in the user's *repeated action under friction*, not a psychological diagnosis or moral grading.
- Relationship development is not scripted improvement; **the reward for intimacy is greater reality, not easier access.**
- Explicit anti-goals: collectible characters; companion sycophancy; romance/sex funnels; optimize-a-relationship score; covert therapy; guilt/streak retention; compassion-as-prestige; LLM-created causal facts; stereotyped psychology; canned right answers.

**NEIGHBORMULCH's strongest anti-corruption constraint:** a "successful user encounter" is NOT a solved task, a friendly Nadia, easy disclosure, reduced friction, forced empathy, a high relationship score, moral instruction or guaranteed continuing contact. Those are precisely the ways an encounter-side implementer can destroy SOROK while passing a superficial interaction test.

## II. A rival to both the isolated psyche and the total social graph

**Original sources:** `02_PROVISIONAL_RELATIONAL_ONTOLOGY.md`; `00A_VOZHD_TAKEOVER_RELATIONAL_PSYCHIC_GATE_R1.md`; `dockets/D001_RELATIONAL_ONTOLOGY_V3_BOUNDARY/`; `D007_KERNEL_SIFTING_AND_DERIVATIONAL_COMPRESSION/00_DOCKET.md` and its first three companions. Most are **provisional ontology or open kernel research**, not adopted source law.

The early positive conception:
**world → relational field → person as history of relations → relational priors → encounter → dyadic history → network effects → changed people.**

SOROK did not originate the PFF/V2 distinctions between deep constitution, present activation and outward expression; it **relationalized** them. The apparently standing individual is already materially and socially constituted; "durable" does not mean contextless. Equally a person is **not** the enumerable sum of dyadic edges, and a dyad does not own the person.

The D007 exploratory compressed **kernel neighborhood** (NOT adopted): *irreducible first-personhood without self-sufficient individuality*, *relational constitution without relational exhaustibility*, *nested non-exhaustion*. The especially valuable derivational constraint:

> The person exceeds every relation; the relation exceeds either person's experience of it; the world exceeds every person's phenomenal access to it; yet all three remain mutually and historically consequential.

D007's *kenotic attention* / *love of neighbor* language is one proposed praxis consequence: give up the sovereignty of the observer's own phenomenal horizon enough to attend to another center. **Do not translate this into moral scoring, supernatural software claims, a mandate for religious conversion, or a machine assertion of literal AI first-person consciousness.** D007 `03_TENSIONS_AND_HOSTILE_TESTS` explicitly warns that computationally fictional Nadia has not been proved a biologically conscious/morally equivalent human subject; theology remains exploratory vocabulary, not runtime law.

**Implication for MULCH:** the independent Other is not a function of the user or a plot organized around them, but nor is "independent" achieved by creating an isolated fixed psyche and adding optional social context. Relation is formative without granting the user ownership of the person.

## V. First Courts 1–11: the literal product invention

**Sources `16`, `18`–`26`, ``30–35`, plus D002 and D003.** These are overwhelmingly **provisional competing design hypotheses and hostile scenario banks**, not binding current feature lists.

**Courts 1–2: user and relation (16).** Player must retain *authentically user-owned words/choices/praxis* while possessing a bounded diegetic embodiment; the invented shell only provides the material facts necessary for lawful presence. Distinguish real user, world positioning, user-supplied facts, actual attempted speech/action and the actor's separate/possibly mistaken model of the user. Encounter must be **accessibly causal**, not random teleportation. Recontact requires real practical/social reason, and mutual recognition must be constructed, not inferred from a friendliness score.

**Courts 3–4: independent life and success (18).** Hybrid offscreen progression, time at several grains, role/institution/household obligations, third parties, autonomous decisions. **Advancement on demand is not fake persistence** when the intervening history and its consequences remain bound. **Progress is not health; history/recognition/consequence are not points**. The user can walk away without a product guilt machine.

**Courts 5–8: player embodiment/veil/alternative (19–22E).** User is not the alternate fictional human whose whole biography must be generated; **the persona is the historically alternate life**, the user is the one presently entering relation. A bounded local shell preserves resource scarcity, language/access/transport and opportunity cost, while not replacing the user with a wholly authored NPC-self. The extreme "24/7 real-time friend" and portal vs transposed-peer alternatives were preserved as **comparative countermodels**, not selected defaults. The **zero-profile main-mode correction (22E)** explicitly rejects compulsory Portal Profile and too much user autobiographical self-construction.

**Court 9: what is a relation? (23).** A relation is not one positive scalar. Separate shared objective event history, current ties, each actor's relation-specific organization, separate actor models, mutual knowledge, network consequences and memories. Trust is domain-specific prediction; shared routine/joint project may emerge independently of stated intimacy. Injury, repair, cessation and unreturned attention must remain possible.

**Court 10: flourishing (24).** Care may dominate, refusal may be care, dependence may be harmful, happiness may be unrelated to genuine capability; material and relational conditions matter. No universal "friendship health grade" is licensed.

**Court 11: user praxis (25).** Longitudinal behavior (actual realized action, attention, promises, responses to refusal, distribution of care) ranks above self-description. Rawlsing should ask *what did you repeatedly do when another person became costly or different?* rather than *what kind of person are you?* Privacy and exposure-bias limits constrain any inference.

## VI. The original “UX spirit”: seduction toward specificity without controlling the Other

**Sources:** ``27–35, `40, ``43–48, `62 and D002.

- **Task flux is not the game (`30).** Laundry, groceries, food, work, rooms, errands and practical help are material substrate. The more important phenomenon is learning this particular person through complex activity and relation over time. Do not have the product turn every task into a morality lesson or a quest reward.
- **Early hook is curiosity, not obedience (`31).** The user begins wanting to be around an interesting, surprising person, and discovers how sharing time with them works. The person also may or may not become curious about the user. Presence and listening matter; so do failure, irritation and nonreciprocity.
- **Disclosure belongs to the actor (`40).** There is no `trust >= threshold → deep fact`. Shame, care, desire to be seen, self-image, defensive refusal, ordinary talk, indifference and false self-knowledge all matter. The user does not unlock a collectible autobiography.
- **A first encounter has a shape but no canonical Nadia script (`35).** A human invitation, primer, entry into ongoing life, natural scene termination, potential outside-contact channel and possible second encounter can organize a product test, while any individual's reaction remains causally open.
- **Distinct portal/co-presence and outside messaging (`32).** Candidate interface differentiation is causal (bodily proximity, channels, time, privacy), not merely visual skin. A message channel is not an automatic reward.
- **Succession and human referral (``33–34).** Historical relation alone does not justify new-person succession: dominance/extraction cannot be mistaken for developmental "success." Proposed invitation economy must not fabricate an endorsement or convert real care into status capture; these remain product/business alternatives, not authorization to gate humans.
- **Natural friction and toxicity (`45).** Genuine flawed or harmful actor behavior can emerge from an independent person; noncoercive product design is NOT sanitizing every difficult person. Separate **person-caused harm** from platform-engineered guilt/compulsion.
- **Relational gravity without platform captivity (``43–48).** The proposed desirable persistence is occasional thought/concern about the Other, **not calendar capture**, streaks, obligatory check-ins or synthetic suffering from logging off. Default elastic rendezvous and stronger opt-in synchronization are historical design hypotheses, not present runtime proof.
- **Noncommodification and ordinary life (`62).** Simulated adult life may contain difficult, erotic, violent, intense or mundane possibilities when legally and causally warranted, without turning any of them into content funnels or hidden engagement goals. A dramatic event does not suspend ordinary work, children, neighbors, rent, weather or consequence; independent ongoing life is the anti-fetish response.

**Warning to NEIGHBORMULCH:** making the new mechanism "engaging" by a compliant conversation engine, mandatory closeness, scripted intimacy, planted "discovery" evidence, user-dependent story pace or compulsive notifications violates the original product thesis even if every action technically has a valid event ID.

## VIII. Historical objectification, social production and the psychic world

**Sources:** ``53–56, 70–74, 80, PRE-A0 and later CIRR; this is a line of **developing research hypotheses with source-authority corrections**, not scientific proof that every object has a unique psychological cause.

- **Meaning is actor- and counterpart-specific (`53)**: a key, gift, shared fridge or introduction has no universal "trust" meaning. Roles, cultures, class, history, status, bodily need and previous actual relation alter meanings.
- **Material object ≠ actor-value relation ≠ relational sign (`54)**. Labor, scarcity, memory, care, use and symbolic identification can charge things differently; private satisfaction need not reference a named Other, and no object is a compulsory plot clue.
- **Past materialization is limited (`70)**. A newly resolved object's earlier location may lawfully constrain a present scene; it cannot write memories for lived experiences that never occurred. Preserve hard inconsistency vs psychologically real contradiction; H0–H3 were *historical proposal categories*, not unconditional authority to rewrite childhood.
- **Recurrence without teleology (`71)**. The eight/nine meaning strata differentiate objective occurrence, material cause, patterned recurrence, actor salience, reinterpretation/mythologization, other person's interpretation and subsequent consequences. A later pattern does not prove an earlier event was "destined".
- **Self-rereading (``72–73)**. A person can revise how they understand the same past, suppress contradictions, appropriate reality defensively, become more capacious or become tyrannically closed to correction. **Never erase the original historical event to fit the current self-story.** No enlightenment gradient.
- **Actual V3/PFF ownership correction (`74 and the `76` anti-reification law)**: historical pre-programming Personality Generator v3.4 documents do **NOT** grant current algorithmic or scientific authority; integrate, partition, discount, appropriate and reinterpret are descriptive/derived patterns unless a current authorized source demonstrates a function. Do **not** mint a duplicate universal interpretive "framework" owner just because theory names one.

This line is the most direct ancestor of the CIRR constitutive loop and the R23–R25 psychology. **Treat the original conceptual sources as demands on the code, not a superior competing author of the actor's real current state.**
