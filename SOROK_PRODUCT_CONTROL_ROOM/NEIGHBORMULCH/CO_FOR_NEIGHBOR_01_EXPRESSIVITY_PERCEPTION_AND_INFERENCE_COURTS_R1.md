## 0. The operative generative rule

```
REAL ACTOR HISTORY + BODY + SOCIAL/RELATIONAL CONTEXT
    + PRESENT SITUATED CO-ACTIVE PSYCHODYNAMIC CURRENTS
        -> ACTOR'S LAWFUL INTENDED / UNINTENDED EXPRESSION (CANDIDATE)
        -> ADMITTED OBJECTIVE OUTWARD GESTURE / VOCALIZATION / ACT / SILENCE
        -> VISITOR'S PHYSICAL/MODAL ACCESS + CURRENT ATTENTION + RECOGNITION
        -> VISITOR'S ACTUAL PHENOMENAL SURFACE
        -> VISITOR'S UNCERTAIN INTERPRETATION / RESPONSE
        -> POSSIBLE LATER ADMITTED ACTOR/WORLD EVENT
```

The visual/narrative surface MAY accurately report that Nadia's mouth tightens or her voice breaks **if that happened** and the visitor could lawfully register it. It MUST NOT claim, merely on that basis, “she is secretly ashamed,” “she is afraid of loving you,” or “you caught her lie.” If she openly says “I'm ashamed,” reporting that utterance is lawful, even when she is mistaken. The objective occurrence is allowed to be uninformative, misleading, rich in personal style, or accurately legible. An attempt to decode it does not force hidden content to exist.

## 3. The two effects of user observation on the ongoing person

**Path A — optical/social opportunity only:** the visitor silently catches Nadia's look, and the actor has no access to the visitor's noticing. The viewer may remember, infer, privately guess or later ask. Nadia's current psyche does **not** change merely because the external UX model marked the cue `noticed`.

**Path B — reciprocally observable conduct:** the visitor's lingering gaze, attentive question, withdrawal, smile, change of task or explicit comment actually occurs and is available to Nadia. That can recruit her actor-specific concern about being witnessed, embarrassment, affection, irritation or indifference; she may turn away, laugh, become guarded, confide, ask them not to stare or interpret the visitor wrongly. A full relational loop requires **a real admitted further event**, not a private model reaching through the epistemic membrane.

Third possibilities matter. Nadia may notice she visibly lost control independent of whether anyone else saw; the actor's own expressive occurrence may be available to her as an event and later reorganize her own relation to embarrassment. A person may *not* notice a visible expression they made. These are distinct from the visitor's knowledge.

## 4. Four serious anti-cheating controls

1. **Source-order:** do not compute a user's perception and then regenerate Nadia's prior expression to make it interesting. Binding actor occurrence must preexist the viewer-specific selection or be jointly settled by owner-permitted event generation independent of viewer profile. Actual user conduct and actual Nadia reaction can subsequently change each other lawfully.
2. **Actor profile source:** a behavior's *pattern* must come from actor-specific life and appropriate bodily/social conditions, not a fixed “emotional expressivity 0–100” or a culturally essentialized facial code. If there is insufficient history to assert a precise habit, generate only responsibly bounded possibilities and grade uncertainty.
3. **Viewer profile source:** the model may learn from independent attention and recognition evidence. Source categories `USER_TARGETED`, `AMBIENT_UNBIASED`, `REGIME_WEIGHTED`, `PHYSICALLY_DOMINANT`, `ACTOR_DIRECTED`, `TASK_REQUIRED` preserve whether any apparent skill evidence was given to the viewer by the system itself. Perceptual accessibility and high-level motive inference remain separate.
4. **Narrative description rights:** the surface may describe physical and experiential evidence under observer limits, including a truthful “her voice cracks for a moment”; claims of secret inner reasons belong to the actor's later self-disclosure, another person's interpretation, or research-grade qualified analysis, not the invisible narrator. “It looks like she may be upset” is itself an inference that should be clearly perspective-bound and possibly mistaken.

## 5. The hardest sourcefree joined demonstrations (if a competent builder undertakes them)

**J1 — same actor state, differing visitor observation:** instantiate one actor-owned multi-stream episode that admits an actual small involuntary gesture under an appropriate current event. Freeze the objective event, position, time and person. Branch two visitors at the exposure point with different lawfully grounded attentional profiles; one notices, the other misses. Actor-owned world history remains identical until an actual visitor action differs. A subsequent truthful question may change Nadia's later behavior; the silent private perception tag may not.

**J2 — same viewer, different actor expressivity:** hold the visitor and physical scene fixed; compare two historically qualified Nadias with equal private disappointment but different learned public-expression patterns, current relationships and bodily condition. Do not force one difference if both would naturally exhibit the same expression; require at least one scenario in which a well-grounded difference can alter a public occurrence. The user receives only the actual variant.

**J3 — seemingly decisive tell, genuinely false inference:** visitor accurately notices a strained half-smile but confidently infers fear of abandonment. The objective current cause can instead be embarrassment about forgetting a promised errand, or a bodily discomfort, if that is what the qualified actor history warrants. User asks on their hypothesis; Nadia may be surprised, reassured, annoyed, evasive or uncertain. Do not retroactively invent abandonment anxiety to reward the visitor's “insight”.

**J4 — correct inference without game reward:** visitor notices a characteristic involuntary cue and correctly infers Nadia is worried. They may do nothing with that realization. Nadia need not disclose, praise the visitor, become closer or acquire a new durable form. Accurate recognition is possible and sometimes ordinary, not universally impossible or automatically transformative.

**J5 — idiosyncratic social pattern becomes more legible:** over several admitted, non-guaranteed shared encounters, user learns that Nadia often goes very still when assessing whether she will be interrupted. Another actor without that history perceives only stillness. The learned pattern can later cease to apply after real life changes; the system must not make an old gesture a forever-reliable emotional barcode.

**J6 — genuine actor self-surprise:** Nadia says something more directly than intended, hears her own voice, and later responds to that actual emitted event. A drafted but unspoken utterance cannot feed back. The observer may notice the pause but cannot access Nadia's internal retrospective meaning unless she actually expresses it.

**Proof status:** all J1–J6 are **PROPOSED**. No commit in this documentary handoff demonstrates an executing system. Competent owner lookup must precede code changes; evaluate at exact code HEAD with negative fixtures, protected-admitted world event and two independent projection consumers rather than a disconnected LLM story.

## 6. What NEIGHBOR should extract and what it should leave alone

**Extract:** a person-specific expressive region of possibility; outward-tip binding before viewer filtering; ordinary observability as distinct from exceptional acuity; modality-specific discrimination; observer familiarity, attention, recognition and fallible interpretation as separate; lawful genuine body-language leakage; genuine ambiguity and genuine legibility; actor/viewer reciprocal observation through actual conduct; no self-confirming user profiler.

**Do not adopt by citation:** a new actor-expression owner, a new universal human-emotion coding table, a single user “perspicacity score,” a guaranteed reveal rule, hidden metrics of empathy/obliviousness, privacy-invasive personality profiling, or automatic narrator psychology explanations. This is a source-aware sharpening proposal **for NEIGHBOR's owner to weave into its existing one HOME** only where it changes real causal functionality.

**Next theoretical frontier indicated by these courts:** the expressive bridge is an essential constitutive gap, but it also exposes (i) endogenous self-initiated attention/action under no obvious new external event and (ii) learning of social recognition and self-presentation across changing relationships. Before claiming either new, compare inherited PRE-A0, CAN-010/011, PFF self-output recursion and current actor producers. We should not make generic “more interiority” another endless abstract frontier.
