## 0. The principal's direct correction, governing this handoff

The previous CO explanation was too strong when it said the product must not construct a little facial clue to disclose Nadia's cause. **The correct restriction is against clue manufacture for gameplay, not against an independently becoming person actually betraying some of her feeling through a historically characteristic expression.** Nadia may have a slight facial alteration, involuntary flush, vocal shift, involuntary laugh, tear, unguarded glance, delayed response, repeated gesture, changed breathing, unusual stillness or unintentionally revealing movement. Whether it occurs must originate in **her actual embodied, acquired and presently situated expressive functioning**. What reaches the real visitor depends independently upon actual visibility/audibility, task and access, plus the visitor's warranted attention, recognition and interpretive ability.

The visitor may correctly understand something, partly recognize it, completely miss it, perceive a signal but misread it, or overinterpret a neutral behavior. Partial opacity is a **causal consequence of persons and perception**, not a deliberate platform mandate that every feeling remain hidden or every expression be misleading. Genuine successful insight is allowed. So are transparent, emotionally direct people. A person whose emotions often show on their face is not automatically a thinner person than a private person.

## I. CO's distinct responsibility: an acquired, context-sensitive *expressive repertoire*

An organism with a deep psyche but an arbitrary output palette is not yet a convincingly particular person. A source-graded fictional actor may develop recognizable, revisable patterns in:

- **Baseline expressive morphology and habits:** ordinary facial mobility/immobility, posture, gesture, movement, typical manner of looking or looking away, vocal prosody, pace and hesitations, habitual laughter or silence, social and bodily forms of engagement. These are *not* universal diagnostic labels, racialized or gendered stereotypes, innate Enneagram types or fixed mappings between emotion and face.
- **Historical formation:** actual family mirroring and correction, changing caregiving and school norms, language and linguistic pragmatics, acquired self-presentation strategies, relational security and danger, learned playfulness, bodily development, illness/fatigue, habits of expressive control and loss of control; uncertain parent backfill is not evidence for a hidden exact microgesture.
- **Relation-specific expression:** freer with a sibling, careful around an authority, quiet around an estranged friend, unusually awkward when expecting criticism, warmly direct with another person, or ordinarily candid with strangers. What is stable across settings, what is deliberately cultivated, what changes involuntarily and what has no stable pattern are all allowable.
- **Current modulation:** actual emotion and its object, bodily arousal/condition, competing activity, attention, fatigue, social stakes, learned inhibition, desired presentation, awareness of being watched, real sensory context, habit and the actor's model of the listener.
- **Nonvoluntary expression and imperfect control:** affect may shape the face or voice despite a different intended communicative act; an actor can also have an automatized expressive habit they do not experience as conscious management. Suppression can succeed, fail, partly succeed or alter the shape of a gesture in another way. Some actors are exceptionally difficult to read without any hidden “coldness” diagnosis.
- **Improvisation/novelty:** a particular event can generate a new expressive move that was not a prelearned canned template, but it must fall in Nadia's *reasonable actor-shaped possibility space*, as [SOROK 101](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/865b5b01e1a27e99020cf2bb389af8cd4c874b57/SOROK_PRODUCT_CONTROL_ROOM/101_BINDING_SURFACE_ACTOR_PHENOMENOLOGY_SEGREGATION_AND_TWO_CALL_RENDERING_R1.md) demands; later real reactions can transform the repertoire.

**The decisive underincorporated CO function:** historically formed psychological organization must condition how a private current *becomes or does not become bodily or communicatively manifest*, including involuntary, habitual and strategic channels. R10 specifies multi-stream current psychology but does not yet establish an empirically grounded whole-person expressive generator, its owner read contract or demonstrated first consumer. That is an actual open bridge, not proof no historical producer exists.

## II. Separate expressive formation from epistemic revelation

The original SOROK 101 noncollapse is stronger than a generic “privacy wall”:

```text
ACTOR'S EMBODIED/PSYCHIC HISTORY AND CURRENT MULTISTREAM LIFE
    != actor's present awareness and self-account
    != intended speech / willed presentation / nonwilled expression
    != EXACT LAWFULLY ADMITTED OUTWARD GESTURE, SOUND, OR MOVEMENT
    != outward signal's sensory availability to a particular observer
    != observer's actually attended and distinguished cue
    != observer's recognition (what kind of cue?)
    != observer's inference about its cause/meaning
    != observer's subsequent belief and consequential conduct
```

These are **interacting realities**, not compulsory separate database owners or sequential omniscient model calls. There is a difference between an actor suppressing a spontaneous laugh, failing to suppress it, *choosing* an ironic smile, involuntarily smiling while denying pleasure, and being misdescribed as smiling by a narrator seeking drama. Some belong to actor current formation; some are objectively observable events; some are only visitor hypotheses.

Only an actual outward occurrence can become observer-perceptible. Its actual cause may remain opaque **even when the visitor confidently and correctly recognizes a change**. A lip tremor is an occurrence; “she is ashamed” is a psychological inference unless additional lawful evidence licenses her own statement or explicit observer attribution. Different causes can produce similar outward forms; the same cause need not produce the same outward form under different bodily, social and acquired conditions.

**A clue is not forbidden; a conveniently authored clue is.** If Nadia really exhibits a tell and the visitor could actually catch it, the telling detail is part of the relationship. It may eventually prove important, unimportant, misinterpreted or forgotten. The game must not reward cue-searching with guaranteed secret revelations, or require the performer to manufacture an expressive anomaly in each interaction.

## III. Observer modeling: distinguish acute perception from ordinary non-obliviousness

The principal specifically identifies two possible mechanisms that are **not safely reducible to one scalar**:

1. **Positive acuity/sensitivity:** the visitor can discriminate subtle auditory, gestural, temporal or contextual differences others plausibly miss, sometimes with learned expertise or relationship-specific familiarity.
2. **Non-oblivious baseline attention:** the visitor is simply sufficiently present and oriented not to miss a salient or ordinary expression; they need not possess expert signal detection.

Separate these candidate dimensions, and also consider:

- **Opportunity and sensory conditions:** actual position, distance, line of sight, hearing, lighting, occlusion, medium, accessibility, time, co-present third parties and movement. No profile can confer eyesight into another room or a word never spoken.
- **Attentional allocation:** where the visitor actually directs attention; divided attention, competing tasks, active searching, habit, anticipated target, social anxiety and fatigue; current salience can help or distract.
- **Detection/registration:** whether an *existing* cue was noticed at all, without automatically recognizing its social/psychological relevance.
- **Discrimination/resolution:** how well subtle intensity, timing, modality or deviation from this particular actor's baseline can be differentiated. Detecting a change differs from knowing what changed.
- **Recognition/familiarity:** whether one knows Nadia's recurring gestures or has contextual knowledge of typical human expression; actor-specific familiarity should emerge through actual exposure and may itself be false.
- **Interpretation and calibration:** which hypotheses the visitor forms, what evidence supports them, overconfidence, false positives, inattentive false negatives, and capacity to revise after other evidence. Reading a facial tell correctly in one instance does not bestow objective access to a secret motive.
- **Communicative self-report:** the actual human's words and inquiries are evidence of what they expressed, not a blanket truth API for hidden real-world perceptual traits or external autobiographical competence.

These dimensions may correlate, vary with modality or current circumstance, and later be empirically modeled parsimoniously; **do not pre-decide they lie on a single “observant” spectrum**. A person may notice every voice change but be weak at recognizing brand labels; be ordinarily alert but unable to read subtle eyebrow movement; be expert in body-language *hypotheses* yet overconfident and often wrong; be sharply familiar with this one friend despite generally low attention to faces; or see a clear expression without attributing any hidden motive.

**S60 is controlling conceptual inheritance:** source evidence for attentional regimes is stronger when the human initiates an ATTEND action, spontaneously recognizes something, corrects a mislabel or repeatedly allocates limited attention, weaker where the narrator previously surfaced the cue. Distinguish `USER_TARGETED`, `AMBIENT_UNBIASED`, `REGIME_WEIGHTED`, `PHYSICALLY_DOMINANT`, `ACTOR_DIRECTED`, and `TASK_REQUIRED`. Narrator-selected cues cannot be used as circular proof of prior user acuity. Ordinary scene details must remain accessible to a normally attentive visitor even if they lack a special perceptual profile; a hypothetical excellence score cannot become the condition of all basic noticing.

The **user-modeling mechanism and settings belong to NEIGHBOR**, not CO. It may make lawful, bounded and revisable hypotheses from encounter evidence; it must not claim to have measured the actual human's hidden personality or psychological diagnosis. If preference, privacy, explicit user consent or accessibility settings matter, that is NEIGHBOR's separate product administration question and must not be silently imported into the in-world plot.

## IV. Three legally distinct interfaces

**CO / actor generation:** historically supported expressive dispositions, current embodied condition, situational participation, acquired regulation, nonwilled current affect and existing relationship history may condition **the actor's actual outward occurrence**. The simulated user is not a psychologically scripted puppet; Nadia may react only to the visitor's *actual admitted actions and to her actor-owned perception or beliefs about them*, not a platform-private guess that the real user enjoys spotting eye twitches.

**World / binding occurrence:** which gesture, posture, voice shift, silence, gaze, body movement, tool manipulation, timing or expressed sentence objectively occurred? Was it audible/visible? Who was there? Did an interruption change what happened? What exact source qualified admission and causality? An LLM's plausible alternate or private analytic note does not author the canonical event. Speech, gesture and physical consequences must persist consistently.

**NEIGHBOR / phenomenal projection:** which already-settled observable cues can this observer lawfully encounter under current physical access, attention and warranted recognition repertoire? How specifically may the narrator describe them? Which interpretations must remain the visitor's rather than being printed as fact? An attention regime may influence **selection and lexicalization of actual observation**, not the actor's expressive behavior or the world's historical cause.

**Important nuance:** an actual user **action** influenced by the user's own observed emotion or intention can genuinely affect Nadia. This does not violate the anti-mirror wall: the actor reacts to world-admitted user conduct and her own limited interpretation, not to the platform's hidden profiler directly. Likewise, Nadia may legitimately become more guarded *because she sees that particular visitor scrutinizing her face*, provided the scrutiny is actually performed and available to her. A private user-model prediction must not be smuggled into Nadia's psyche as a substitute occurrence.

## V. A closed-loop relational consequence, not a facial-decoding mini-game

Nadia may notice that a normally subtle expression of hers has been seen. That realization can itself become a *new actor-owned event*: delight in being understood, irritation at being scrutinized, worry about having betrayed a confidence, surprise that the visitor knows her habits, or genuinely no consequence. The visitor may likewise learn through lawful repeated exposure that her ordinary silence differs from her strained silence. Both can adapt their communication—often tacitly—through real histories of contact. **The loop is relationally reciprocal but neither self-knowledge nor interpersonal understanding is guaranteed.**

This is why observer inference has to be causally downstream of the genuine manifestation. If the visitor says “I noticed you looked upset” and Nadia was not upset, her response can be shaped by the misunderstanding, not by a retroactive requirement to have been upset. If Nadia *was* upset, she may deny, deflect, reveal, laugh at the accuracy, or not know herself what was happening. These are choices/performances within her actor-owned history, not UX-mandated intimacy rewards.

**The genuinely hard CO × NEIGHBOR condition:** a gesture can be both (i) real evidence of a materially caused affective state and (ii) underdetermined as to its inner cause for a finite observer. Legitimate human understanding is possible without the model converting every legible gesture into objective psychological fact. Conversely sometimes Nadia is perfectly open, and it would be artificial to impose an opacity filter on an unambiguous self-given, truthful statement.

## VI. Safeguards against replacing life with legibility optimization

- No universal one-to-one mapping “facial sign X means lying/anger/desire.” Context, individual history, bodily conditions and uncertainty matter. No scientifically issued expression decoder is claimed.
- No deliberate insertion of stereotyped nervous tics or purposeful “breadcrumbs” merely so the user can infer hidden secrets.
- No anti-expression purity rule: unguarded warmth, visibly shaking anger, blushing, clumsy concealment, laughter, tears and obvious affection remain possible when historically coherent.
- No total body-language transparency: actor may honestly express a feeling without knowing its source, intentionally stage an expression, or present ambiguously.
- No demand that every event leave a special clue, lesson, development or user reward.
- No viewer-profile-driven mutation of Nadia's private psyche, external biography, choices or actually performed body movements.
- No captive psychological character “profile” of the external human from platform activity. Models of perception are bounded evidence-sensitive UX hypotheses; never automatic proof of human motive.
- No invisible microcues authoring inaccessible information: physical opportunity, modality and actual emitted event must come first.
- No forced mirror: even when the visitor is highly attentive, the world should continue showing unexpected, ordinary, uninteresting and contradictory things.
