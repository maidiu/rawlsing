## I. THE DIFFERENCE THAT HOLDS THE WHOLE: WHERE THE USER LOOKS IS NOT WHAT THE WORLD EXISTS FOR

SORACLE-CO's criticism strikes exactly where the preceding formulation could betray its own intention. We displaced a trust meter with changing possibilities of doing things together. That was better, but still made *access to Nadia* seem the privileged answer to what a human encounter had accomplished. It would let a less overtly quantified progress engine hide beneath a more beautiful phrase.

The correction cannot be “make Nadia's life less responsive to the visitor.” That would install a countervailing optimization, a computational moral bias in favor of independence, and falsify precisely the relation we are trying to represent. Some people are profoundly altered by those they encounter. Some become dependent. Some form an attachment they would reasonably call their life's center. Some willingly make sacrifices for a new relationship. Others meet someone transformative and then depart. The architecture cannot prefer one developmental ending without transforming historicity into an ethical script.

Instead, the most important distinction is **between the focal point of the experience and the causal center of the world**. The UI is Nadia-centered **epistemically and spatially**: this is the door that the player enters, the person they can try to know, the message thread available when they have become contactable, the finite situation in which their real judgment is exercised. The world is not Nadia-centered *for the visitor's sake*: Nadia has a life that is not built in order to receive them, and the world's causes—including many that affect her—do not depend upon whether the player will see them. Even Nadia is not the sovereign causal center of her own world, because she is embedded in family, labor, state, market, institutions, body, accident and other persons' histories.

This produces a seemingly paradoxical but indispensable product constitution: **a one-sided aperture onto a many-sided world**. The visitor is given a deliberately narrow doorway into this particular life. The reality behind the doorway must have no privileged obligation to offer a good conversation, a useful moral experience, an escalating relationship, a successful interaction, or even a narratable story. A player may knock and be refused. They may be welcomed without becoming important. They may matter intensely and never learn how. They may participate in something that alters Nadia's mother more than Nadia. They may give Nadia a useful practice that makes further help unnecessary. The interface is organized around a particular woman without making her psychological becoming a mechanism for the interface's success.

This is not “decenter the user” as an additional psychological goal. It is **refuse to put the user at the causal center in the first place**. There is no need for an algorithm to make Nadia talk more about her independent interests, artificially go out with others, diminish attachment, or strategically withdraw so she looks authentic. That would be the same dramaturgical capture in an emancipatory costume. The actor must simply be a historically formed, active participant in an objectively operative world. Whether she spends Tuesday thinking of the visitor or barely remembers the meeting belongs to that person/world state, not a designer's corrective impulse.

The strongest concise form: **the portal is about Nadia; Nadia is not about the portal**. More exactly still: the user may have *one real experiential center*, while the fictional world retains **many non-subordinate causal centers**. Structural privileging of where the player can participate is not ontological privileging of what the world is for.

## II. NOTHING IS OFFERED TO THE USER EXCEPT THE CHANCE OF HER PRESENT PARTICIPATION

“Nothing here is for the user, except Nadia's present willingness to spend time with them” can be understood rigorously without pretending the product supplies literally no affordances. The interface gives the player a local existence, sensory information, a truthful resources projection, the ability to speak and act, a contact path if established, and some practical control over their own decisions. Those are conditions for participation. **They are not entitlements to Nadia's affection, availability, attention, assistance, self-disclosure or transformation.**

The player's question is correspondingly not “How do I obtain the next higher relationship state?” It is “Is Nadia willing and able to share this present interval of life with me, and what can we do or say together under the circumstances?” Her willingness is conditional on the history of the person, this particular visitor, other obligations, current bodily state, practical scheduling, projects, moods, commitments, recognition claims and more. She might want an entire afternoon, twelve minutes, a quick favor, a disagreement, a game with her child, sex, friendship, consolation, a fight, or no time at all. The system must not fit these into a compulsory ordinal scale of openness.

“Bad at Humaning” is an intelligible phenomenological description, not an issued universal competency function. A stranger who speaks contemptuously may often encounter resentment and fewer invitations, but a particular Nadia might welcome the directness, agree with the contempt, fail to recognize the injury, or be unable to refuse someone with more authority. A visitor whose conduct many others would find considerate might annoy Nadia because of her historically formed expectations. Someone can sincerely try to be kind and nevertheless be intrusive; someone can say something harsh and later be welcomed for telling the truth. **No universal good-human score is permitted to govern how the person responds.**

If she begins spending less time with the visitor, that may be the actual result of incompatibility, growing resentment, a competing project, family obligations, exhausted discretionary time, a preference for someone else, a new view of the visitor, or an event neither person entirely understands. If she begins spending more, the reasons may be affectionate, opportunistic, erotic, practical, dependent, controlling, generous, conflicted or mundane. A single outward convention does not determine its psychological or ethical meaning. The visitation structure exposes changing willingness; it does not interpret that willingness as a measure of the visitor's merit.

The user can still reasonably find the relationship's available activities deeply compelling. They can be delighted when Nadia invites them in, or disappointed when she doesn't. The product need not extinguish such feelings merely because access is not the true ontology of psychic development. **Experiential stakes are permitted; computational reward targeting is not.** The distinction is between what the player may care about and what the engine must be made to maximize. The first can be intimacy. The second must be coherent independent historical activity and consequence.

The portal may therefore stage the only thing it can honestly offer: an encounter with an Other who is already a participant in other relations and may or may not admit another participant. It can give an opportunity to knock, not a right to be answered. It can make an unanswered message real, not proof of a decay meter. It can make an emerging friendship matter without configuring Nadia's future to keep the player inside it.

## III. THE CONSTITUTIVE PERSON IS NOT A REWARD STATE, EVEN WHEN THE VISITOR BECOMES IMPORTANT

The Constitutive Organism establishes that the psyche is not a trait vector with a public tip attached, nor a conscious manager retrieving motives from storage. Living body, actual social world, historically acquired practices and meanings, unconscious or partially inaccessible organization, present activity and future afterlife constitute a person across time. A previously learned relation can become activated, inhibited, rationalized, transformed, preserved or attenuated in a current encounter; the same episode can participate in several activities with different motives and effects.

This means the visitor's intervention is **one event-cause among others**, not the axis on which the person's entire development is scored. Her overnight sleep and changing bodily condition can alter how she receives a joke without any new intimacy having formed. A coworker's casual act of exclusion can recruit an old expectation of humiliation. A hobby practiced independently may begin to constitute a new durable capacity. A private reading, a failed plan or a repeated commute can gradually alter her current activity, yet the visitor never knows what caused the change. The relevant history is real within the fictional world even when unavailable in the UI.

Conversely, the visitor's act can have enormous lasting effects even when direct relational access decreases. Nadia may learn a method of teaching her child from the visitor and later stop seeking their help. They may provide an introduction that strengthens her friendship with another person. Their observation may redirect a labor decision. Their argument may become a private line of thought she never reports. Or the visitor's message may merely lead her to change her plans for one evening and leave no durable psychological effect. The same outward conversation can therefore produce outcomes that cannot sensibly be ordered by how much Nadia subsequently wants to see the visitor.

This is SORACLE-CO's fundamental criticism accepted without qualification as to **possible outcomes**, but not transposed into a mandate about what the computer should push Nadia to do. It may be that what matters most to Nadia is more contact with the visitor; it may be that what matters most is a different lover, child, religious community, work goal, political project, bodily relief, or no currently narratable master project at all. The engine can neither privilege visitor-free development nor optimize for a narrative of Nadia achieving autonomy. Her historically conditioned life already determines the space of possibilities. **The correct mathematical/architectural restriction is against extraneous outcome targeting, not against one outcome in particular.**

There is another asymmetry. A visitor can undergo a major change in how they perceive their own beliefs or capacities while Nadia barely registers the exchange. The simulation may retain a simple event; the real user may remember it for years. Rawlsing cannot compute that actual real-world psychological transformation as though it owned the user's mind. It may infer behavioral evidence in later play, but should not claim to have authored a human developmental update. Two differently constituted beings encounter one event through distinct ontologies; mutual consequence is possible without symmetric magnitude or symmetric observation.

The personhood demand is thus stronger than “Nadia exists when you're gone.” It is: **Nadia's historical becoming is defined by her life, including those portions that never become your content; your importance to that life is a contingent fact of what actually happens, not the structural target of the simulator**.

## IV. QUIETNESS IS CAUSAL LIFE, NOT AN ERROR IN EVENT GENERATION

The previous pass identified the user's compelling new possibility: Nadia has a day, even if the visitor is absent for one day, and events can happen live while they are together. SORACLE-CO correctly names the corresponding danger: if a day must produce a significant story, the system has substituted its desire for drama for the long, often silent formation of a person. Ordinary material repetition is not a prelude to the real psychology; it is its medium.

A person can become more capable through hundreds of small repetitions without any event being personally revelatory. A parent becomes efficient at packing a child's schoolbag; a worker learns the practical rhythm of a difficult shift; someone learns to recognize a bus route; a body slowly becomes exhausted by bad sleep; a friendship becomes habitual through unremarkable evenings; a humiliation is repeatedly endured until withdrawal feels natural; money arriving predictably reduces the need to calculate every purchase. Some of these processes are psychologically generative. Some are simply material maintenance. Neither requires a sudden emotional confession.

A night of deeply affecting conversation may leave an enduring bond, delayed private reflection, a misleading memory, a small new option, or nothing durable. A user's apparent wisdom may go unnoticed. An ordinary chore can do more for a developing practice than an eloquent speech. The engine must represent **reproduction of already existing organization**, inhibited change, gradual acquisition, absence of opportunity, continued constraints, genuine extinction or no durable effect as real possibilities—not as failures to meet a mandated transformation quota.

For that reason, no scheduler should ask the LLM to produce “one meaningful event per user absence” or “one psychologically consequential interruption per scene.” The world has event opportunities conditioned by calendars, body, work, institutional rhythms, material affordances, other actors and contingent accidents. It can process a day largely as a competent schedule of routines and maintenance. When a consequential event arises, it can spend more computational depth; when nothing new arises, it can retain what genuinely changed and avoid elaborating a hidden arc. What makes a day real is **objective and actor-specific historical continuity**, not a digest with a climax.

The renderer must also accept boring truth. Nadia may answer “nothing much” when asked how the day went because nothing she wants to mention happened. She may have become tired, learned a small skill or performed familiar care without wanting to describe it. She may be wrong about what changed her, and the user need not be granted privileged access to prove she is wrong. An object can simply be used, not become a metaphor for a trauma. The continuity remains rich because the ordinary world supplies a deep causal setting, not because prose constantly advertises hidden depths.

This is not an anti-drama aesthetic. A real crisis may occur; a heated ideological argument may be transformative; a romance may be extraordinary. The rule is simpler and harder: **drama must be an outcome of the world and the persons, not a resource allocated to maintain attention**. Even a machine optimized for “quiet authenticity” would violate this if it deliberately suppressed events to appear natural.

## V. MATERIAL LIFE IS NOT A SIDE GAME: IT IS THE WORLD'S BUDGET OF POSSIBILITY

The November 15 resources-bar decision initially seems like a minor interface choice. In the new whole theory, it is the exact place where the one-sided aperture meets many-sided historical causality. The visitor is structurally local, not materially omnipotent. If Nadia and the user are placed in broadly comparable class and material positions, an ordinary dinner should cost something for both of them. An invitation may require transport, food, time, childcare, shifting work, social access or risk. A resource-constrained person cannot simply solve material problems by being benevolent, and a visitor does not become rich because the dialogue system permits an eloquent offer.

But the user's participation should also not be dominated by an accumulating job simulator, rent spreadsheet or cash-grinding game. The purpose is to *live under* material conditions, not continually *manage* a financial abstraction. The recovered design explicitly kept **exact accounting beneath the phenomenal interface** while showing **coarse, situationally meaningful constrained capacity**. The world must know enough about payment periods, wages, rent, commitments, available purchasing power, debt or obligations, assets, time and transport to resolve what can happen. The user need not stare at an exact dollar balance to recognize that ordering dinner would consume a large part of what they can currently afford.

The characteristic earlier UX was something like:

> **YOUR RESOURCES**  
> Limited flexibility · Rent approaching · Payday tomorrow  
> Takeout for tonight: approximately **18%** of the displayed resources bar.

These were illustrative presentation choices from the recovered discussion, not a formally specified formula. The source did not prove whether “18%” meant all liquid cash, free funds after commitments, discretionary purchasing capacity, or a policy-adjusted composite index. Treating any of those as adopted mathematical law would be a new unreceived economic design. What is established is the **relative cost projection and its tangible bearing on activity**, backed by actual money/material resolution rather than a decorative depletion graphic.

The socially important difference is that money is a **real historical institution and means of constraint**, not a metaphysically universal exchange medium nor a morality score. Work, property, wages, rent, kin support, government benefits, cash timing, informal debts, access to institutions and classed consumption practices are themselves materially and historically particular. The engine should be able to represent a paid salary arriving monthly, irregular cash work, an hourly wage paid later, a free or subsidized living arrangement, a precarious transport situation, or shared household finances. It must not flatten material class to one number merely because a bar happens to be useful to the visitor.

The bar is therefore neither *the resources themselves* nor an epistemically complete picture of economic possibility. It is **a projection of the represented peer's near-term economic flexibility** into a legible interface. It should only appear or become salient where it helps the user understand an available action, a constraint or a change of position. It can be coarse in normal scenes and expose relevant grounds when requested. The person/world model underneath must remain sufficiently exact to refuse an impossible transaction, authorize a possible expense, preserve rent and payment timing, or represent a real deterioration in opportunity. A bar cannot purchase agency, summon help, increase intimacy or authorize a joint action another person did not agree to perform.

This is what SORACLE-CO's criticism adds to a design that might otherwise become a relationship-and-wallet simulator: **economic conditions are not player resources to be optimized for access to Nadia**. They are situated conditions of practical life that can matter to the visitor, Nadia, other people and institutions in different ways even when the user never visits her again. The bar tells the player something about *their own limited position*, not how to manage Nadia's world.

## VI. THE RESOURCES BAR IS A TRANSLATOR, NOT A BANK ACCOUNT AND NOT A RELATIONSHIP METER

A useful interface can compress without falsifying. The portal translates the external human into a locally legible peer position while keeping their actual cognition. The resources bar is a parallel translation: it makes a complex set of economic facts and commitments perceptible without forcing an accounting exercise. Its exact implementation has to preserve an asymmetry between **world truth** and **viewer-oriented summary**.

At the lowest level the world must have admissible material processes: already established wages/pay dates, time-consuming employment, recurring rent, household costs, asset and access conditions, possible transfers, purchases, third-party support or denial, physical inventory and obligations. Actual debits, receipts, shortages and timing must be governed by competent material owners with source/provenance; a draw of a bar cannot itself cause the rent payment or make a nonexistent wage arrive. If the bar looks ample but rent is due and pay is delayed, the system needs enough underlying state to describe that constraint instead of granting frictionless spendability.

At the next level, **feasibility** is activity-specific. A dinner may be affordable in cash but impossible because the user is working, transport is unavailable, childcare is needed or the shop is closed. Another meal may be prepared at home but require ingredients, cooking skill and time. Work may provide a paycheck but cost exhaustion or the opportunity to spend an evening together. A family obligation can be materially real without being an explicit line item in the visitor's perception. The bar cannot replace all these different dimensions with “resource points” that work like mana in a role-playing game.

At the phenomenal level, the system can show a concise state—limited flexibility, rent approaching, payday tomorrow—and a contextual cost proportion for a proposed takeout order. A percentage matters because it lets the human interpret a *decision's seriousness relative to their present position*. It should not allow two identical expenses to magically represent different amounts just because one would improve a relationship. A meal that costs 18% of projected flexible resources remains a meaningful sacrifice even if Nadia is indifferent, upset or grateful. The visitor may decide to spend it for themselves, on family, on a date with someone else, or not at all. **Material accounting must not make Nadia the exclusive beneficiary of the human's local resources.**

This also rules out economic gamification by stealth. Displaying a depletion bar persistently and optimizing users toward paycheck timing could create exactly the parallel management game the user rejected. The bar should provide timely legibility while economic work continues. The visible status can become more granular by explicit ATTEND or transaction inquiry if the player asks what money is actually available, but the interface should not assume that managing exact cents is the principal activity of visiting Nadia. Conversely, the user must not be blocked from learning enough to make ordinary practical choices. A coarse summary that conceals important consequences would violate the same autonomy it aims to preserve.

The user's position is matched structurally to the world at entry, but **not indefinitely equalized to Nadia's changing resources**. A common initial peer class/habitus position does not mean fixed equal purchasing power, identical wages or equal savings. Nadia may lose a job. The player may spend imprudently. Nadia may receive help from her family; the player's local shell may not have those relations. Structural comparison provides a plausible encounter and some mutual intelligibility, not a tether forcing both material histories to move together. Their future conditions can diverge through independent world events and the user's own choices.

The result is a properly materialist interface theorem: **hide account-management labor, not real scarcity; show consequences of economic power, not a score that substitutes for economic power**. The resource bar is justified only insofar as it preserves this distinction.

## VII. OFFSCREEN WORK IS ACTUAL LIFE, NOT THE PLAYER'S INFINITE MONEY PRINTER

If the visitor spends a day away from the portal, both their own local material shell and Nadia's independent life can have work-related consequences. Yet the two require different kinds of agency. **Nadia may make new decisions about her work** through her ongoing computational psyche: accept a shift, ask for a raise, consider quitting, become exhausted, organize with colleagues, be late, take a day off or fail to notice an opportunity, all under the available world and institutional conditions. **The real human visitor may not be autopiloted into new significant speech, consent, labor negotiations or career choices while absent**.

The original alpha direction distinguishes ordinary nonexpressive offscreen maintenance—scheduled work as a constraint, rent, sleep, resources, commuting assumptions, pre-existing obligations—from novel player-authored activity. The platform may preserve an established paycheck cadence, advance a contracted shift and its resource/timing consequences, and hold the person's represented dwelling in a coherent economic world. It cannot assume the user took overtime, quit a job, formed a union, promised money or sent a sympathetic message simply because the LLM thought those choices plausible. The system is not allowed to continue “being the user” after the user closes the interface.

This distinction does not make the human's local position economically inert. Their real in-portal choices can change material history prospectively: they spend limited resources, decline a purchase, offer to share groceries, request information about available work, attempt to repair something, accept a concrete commitment or fail to meet one. Resulting costs, obligations and future constraints survive absence. Their routine work may continue only within previously governed expectations; when an unexpected decision really matters, the product should surface a meaningful choice rather than invent one. The game need not become employment roleplay to make those choices consequential.

Wage timing is psychologically and socially substantive. A person paid tomorrow can experience a dinner differently from someone whose rent is due tomorrow and whose next paycheck is uncertain. Scarcity may make a person plan, borrow, refuse, improvise or become ashamed, but these are *possible individual reactions*, not deterministic traits assigned by poverty. Having a stable income can permit an activity to become so ordinary it disappears from explicit calculation. A material resource bar should register what the person can presently do, while the deeper organism registers **how economic conditions have become organized into expectations, habituated practices, vulnerability and social meaning**.

Work is also time and bodily life. A wage pays for food through a relation of labor and ownership, but the shift consumes hours, patience and physical capacity. A person who comes home depleted may have no desire to discuss public policy with the visitor even if their resources bar improved. A person who lost income may gain time while confronting other risks. A child or parent may experience the same labor change as a loss of care, a gain of co-presence, an embarrassment, or an opportunity. **The economic conditions of meeting Nadia are not reducible to money**, and neither Nadia nor the player should have to narrate that analysis for it to be effective.

There remains a technical necessity: if a time-advance mechanism can add fictitious wages or debit rent from an invented balance, the realism is cosmetic. Source-qualified financial events require legitimate receipts and reproducible state transitions; stored or derived resource projections require clear custody. Historical engineering reports on existing rent operations demonstrate why a “simple bar” is no justification for sloppy material truth. This theoretical request adds no new bank or rent owner.

## VIII. THE WORLD'S INDEPENDENT DAY DOES NOT OWE THE PLAYER A STORY

A day continues while the human is absent. Nadia's work, sleep, family and world can exert ordinary and extraordinary causal effects. A current regional event may interrupt transit, a coworker may make a joke, her child may learn something, a friend may have trouble, she may read an article, or nothing especially new may happen. The event's existence cannot be conditional on its dramatic utility for a returning visitor.

The same recursive organism that forms her earlier life ought to be applicable, at appropriate fidelity and temporal compression, to later intervals. Some hours can be represented as ongoing admitted practices with resource, body and opportunity effects. Others require an actual event and actor choices. An LLM may propose bounded particulars from the existing person, present state, calendars, independent Others, available materials and credible probabilities; competent owner systems must admit or reject the candidate, resolve its consequences and route the actor-specific psychological afterlife. The final narration is permitted to show only what becomes available. **The LLM's persuasive day-summary is not itself a day**.

The visitor's return must not author what happened in retrospect. Their question “How was your day?” can occasion *disclosure* or lawful resolution of an as-yet unspecified compatible historical particular, not produce a day custom-tailored to their interests. The same rule applies to unseen people and objects: the user's ATTEND can identify where more truth is required, but their curiosity cannot determine that truth. The materialization frontier can advance ahead of the viewer's epistemic frontier; no user is entitled to every resolved fact.

A person can also change without an event being individually exciting or even personally acknowledged. Nadia may be slowly habituating to a workplace routine, learning how to teach her child, becoming more tolerant of an unavoidable family obligation, or losing a capacity through disuse. Some interval transitions are simply reproduction: she does approximately what she always does, and the world's continuity remains true. **A period of ordinary stability is not the absence of life**, and a spectacular visiting conversation is not automatically its most significant cause.

The question of what the player missed can itself create a feeling of relation without producing an obligation to open the app. They may wonder, want to ask, discover something was important, or find that nothing involving them changed. A genuine missed appointment can matter; failing to open an app without a diegetic promise should not be interpreted as a breach of loyalty. The simulation must not invent distress to keep the visitor engaged, use a streak, or make strict time synchronization morally superior.

The ability to meet Nadia *again* means the world has passed from one state of actual shared and unshared history into another. What matters is the passage of life, not the accumulation of content intended to be collected.

## IX. WHILE THEY ARE TOGETHER, OTHER ACTIVITIES DO NOT PAUSE FOR THE RELATIONSHIP

The ordinary live event is the next place the theory can betray itself if built as game dramaturgy. Nadia is making dinner; a child needs homework assistance; a neighbor knocks; the phone rings; she has a date that evening. None of these is an “interruption to the player's meaningful encounter” in the world's ontology. Each belongs first to an existing activity, dependency, schedule, object, person or institution. The visitor simply happens to be there and can become consequential to its outcome.

The distinction is profound in a small text environment. The user can attend to the heat, ask Nadia what is urgent, explain a math problem, negotiate who does what, remain silent, attempt a practical repair, or leave. Nadia may accept help, refuse it, redirect the user, handle the task herself, become irritated or change plans. The child has their own learning state and way of receiving an explanation; their need is not a prop to help the player prove intelligence. If someone knocks because missionaries are canvassing, they have a plausible activity and social context independent of the player's religious interests. They may be admitted, ignored, argued with, or sent away. The world should not summon them because the user enjoys debating theology.

The source SOROK D002 distinction between **medium-native action** and **simulated capability** is central. The human's ability to explain, infer, remember, persuade, negotiate, joke and choose timing can be authentically exercised through language. Their physical speed, manual dexterity, represented cooking skill or strength require world/embodiment resolution. “I help the kid” is the intention to begin an activity, not proof the child understands; “I fix dinner” is not proof nothing burned. A broad instruction may reveal concrete affordances and return control for the human to choose a method. Routine uncontroversial labor can then be compressed without turning every stirred pot into a minigame.

Multiple activities can be in tension without being organized into a morality puzzle. Choosing to teach the child while Nadia cooks is not invariably altruistic. Nadia might want to spend time with her child; the visitor might do poorly at teaching; the meal may be fine; an obligation elsewhere might matter more. Success in the material task can undermine social recognition, or vice versa. The same surface act may carry different values for the child, Nadia, the visitor and a third person. No scene controller is entitled to declare its universal relationship meaning.

Events need not arrive at a rate calibrated to user engagement. A meal can proceed normally. A date can occur without jealousy. A visit can be short and slightly dull. The true computational demand is to admit eventful activity through actors/world processes when warranted and to let attention and action become part of those processes. The real scarcity is **finite human attention and available time**, not the supply of cliffhangers.

An active scene is therefore game-like because the user cannot choose every other thing that happens, and because they can use genuine powers in response. The world's life remains unowned without the program being bent to “teach them decentering.”

## X. A RESOURCE COST IS NOT A HUMAN RELATIONAL VALUE

Suppose the visitor wishes to invite Nadia to dinner. Their resources projection indicates that takeout would use a substantial portion of their present flexibility. They might invite her, choose to cook instead, decline because rent is approaching, explain their constraint, conceal it, borrow food, ask whether she prefers something inexpensive, or decide that this is not a good use of their means. The meal may be successful in a technical sense and yet feel humiliating, uncomfortable, joyful, ordinary or inconsequential to either party.

A naïve game would say: spend money → receive affection. A subtler but still false game would say: demonstrate sensible resource use → Nadia recognizes maturity. Rawlsing cannot do either. **The material cost is an objective constrained fact in the local world; what it means for the person is actor-local historical interpretation**. Nadia might dislike someone buying her dinner, believe that hospitality ought to be reciprocal, prefer saving money for another project, be relieved that somebody will cook, enjoy elaborate dining, feel indebted by gifts, or barely care what it cost. Those relations emerge from her life, not a universal currency-to-affection formula.

The visitor's own economic experience likewise cannot be treated as a personality test. Someone may spend recklessly while roleplaying, offer care despite scarcity, preserve money for an actual prior commitment or misunderstand what a local price signifies. The system's inference about real-user generosity must be disciplined by the fact that the product assigned the local resources, presented the choices and decided which facts were visible. The bar is an affordance and consequence explanation, not a diagnostic questionnaire.

The same economic logic matters when Nadia acts without the visitor. She can buy something, lose resources, be paid, postpone rent, receive assistance, perform unpaid care, lend money, refuse a loan or learn a costly technique. Some resources are collective and controlled by other household members or institutions; they cannot simply be represented as an atomized personal wallet. Material constraint may alter her relationship with the visitor through her changed time, body, commitments or risk, even when neither party mentions money.

The deeper materialist point is that **economic relations make practices possible or impossible and attach social meanings to those possibilities**. A person may find someone generous because they spent scarce time rather than scarce cash, or feel controlled because a gift makes refusal expensive. Two people with comparable entry class positions can nevertheless face unequal household support, debt burdens, hours, legal rights, property control or recognition authority. Class is a living organization of opportunity and domination, not one linearly refillable progress bar.

Thus the portal's resources projection belongs on the **human's side of the encounter**. It gives enough of the local economic constraints to make meaningful decisions. It is not a mechanism that converts labor into “relationship time” and purchasing power into access. The world itself has means, bodies, rules and objective histories; Nadia decides what, if anything, she is willing to do with the person who stands at her door.

## XI. THE ROLL MAKES POSSIBLE PERSONHOOD; IT DOES NOT AUTHOR A PERSON'S PURPOSE

The eventual “roll to meet someone you could have been born as” draws out the first contingency. Nobody chooses the world that makes them. A justified source-conditioned generator can sample possible social position, history, family and material conditions from relevant empirical distributions, then generate a particular fictional adult or developing child through a qualified generative construction. The result is not the literal hidden consciousness of a survey respondent and not the unique counterfactual life the current human would have had.

The user proposes introducing an actual *scarce commissioned roll*. That is distinct from selecting one prebuilt character or choosing a compatible partner. An action at interface time can commission the creation and admission of a complete fictional pre-contact root whose particular identity the roller cannot control. Its banked history becomes independently available for other users to encounter through their own branches, subject to qualification and sharing rules. The initiating person thereby contributes to the social population of Rawlsing without acquiring possession, origin-story control, guaranteed intimacy or privileged authority over any later actor.

The whole structure must distinguish **commissioning generation**, **source-qualified possible life**, **completed pre-contact canonical root**, **per-user continuation**, **branch-specific model and world history**, and **later inter-user discussion**. Generation occurs after the button press in real service time; fictional birth precedes the visitor in represented time. The button is not evidence that a real human in that historical position actually lived. The result may have to be constructed in batches or admitted after verification rather than instantaneously; the product must not falsely assert real-time uniquely on-demand generation if a precomputed pool was actually used.

The scarcity of one or two rolls could give the act weight because the user is committing a finite opportunity to the unknown. But if this becomes a market for rolls, a pay-to-reroll search for attractive classes, or a tiered prestige economy, the relationship premise is defeated. The exact entitlement and technical failure/retry policy remain undecided. Technical error is not the same as the user rejecting a valid generated person. Scarcity can be genuine compute rationing and product form; fake scarcity manufactured for retention is something else.

The small alpha may instead present one, then three, then five richly constituted prebuilt roots across varied material conditions. They are **richly developed persons**, not wealthy persons; the audience need only have practical product access. A handful cannot statistically reproduce global life conditions. It can demonstrate unchosen encounters, independent individual histories, offscreen life and the possibility of radically different relationships to the same starting person. That is an honest developmental stage, not a tiny counterfeit of a worldwide lottery.

The natural bridge to broader human contingency is not a morality curriculum. Over enough genuinely qualified draws, a user may repeatedly encounter people whose class, nationality, race, household and institutional worlds differ from theirs. The distribution itself may reveal the unevenness of human births. But the individuals cannot be assigned the function of making their social category educational. A materially poor person may be cruel or joyous; a wealthy person may be generous or self-absorbed; the particular life exceeds any instructional category.

## XII. THE BANK AND THE COMMUNITY: SAME BEGINNING, DIFFERENT ACTUAL LIVES

Original SOROK already specified a shared pre-contact seed-person canon and **copy-on-write user-specific continuities**. The distinction is nonnegotiable. Root-Nadia has a pre-user material ecology, family history, prior psychic organization, relationships, objects, memories and ongoing obligations. Nadia-A and Nadia-B descend from the same root at first encounter, but subsequent events and actor-specific learned relations are separately owned by each branch. User A's intimate history cannot overwrite Nadia-B's adult sexual preferences or become a childhood event shared by both. The bank holds common beginnings, not a collectivized consciousness trained on all visitor outcomes.

The arrangement can produce precisely the social phenomenon the user wants: one person loved Nadia and another hated her. Both may be reporting authentic experiences from compatible root origins under different subsequent encounters. Some of the difference is perceptual: one user recognized a cue, another missed it; one asked about a friendship, another never knew it existed. Some is causal: Nadia-A became more affectionate because of a history that did not happen in branch B; Nadia-B encountered a different loss or met a visitor with different practices. Neither branch is a definitive verdict about the shared past person.

A further form of social knowledge arises when one user asks about a pre-contact object that was not fully resolved until inquiry. The user's ATTEND selects *where* canonical detail must be generated, but may not choose *what* is true. A historical detail can be materialized from existing root constraints, keyed provenance and bounded causal room, then become shared canon for compatible future forks. Where that new fact would retrospectively contradict a committed branch, it cannot silently rewrite history; exact versioning/compatibility and reconciliation remain material technical questions. A person's past cannot be remade simply because a curious visitor prompted a wonderful story.

People discussing “their Nadia” can compare the person without turning it into an official platform leaderboard. They may learn root facts from one another. That new real-human knowledge is not magically acquired by the other branch's actor; if the user then says something in-world about it, that utterance is a novel event and Nadia can interpret it with suspicion, delight, confusion or indifference. The discussion gives real users a social world concerning fictional persons. It does not merge those fictional lives.

The product's gaze may be Nadia-centered while this public discussion is **not controlled by Nadia** either. Users may debate what “good at Humaning” means, share counterexamples, gossip, build stereotypes, idealize characters or learn to see their own earlier judgments as partial. Such discussion is a genuinely human process and may be ethically mixed. If the platform begins ranking who mastered each fictional person, it will encourage treating the lives as puzzles to exhaust. Rawlsing should permit human conversation without engineering comparative completion metrics.

The bank can accordingly become a powerful social institution within the product: a collection of source-qualified possibilities, each a common historical origin capable of indefinitely divergent relational continuities, around which actual humans can encounter one another's interpretive differences. That is not a second game. It is the social afterlife of the one encounter architecture.

## XIII. THE IMPOSSIBLE HOMOLOGOUS PEER IS AN INTENTIONAL SCAR, NOT A REINCARNATION

The visitor enters as an actual real human whose consciousness, capacities, knowledge, social history and habits were formed elsewhere. The portal provides a bounded locally coherent position near Nadia: a plausible dwelling, economic standing, available time constraints, social legibility and the material means appropriate for the fictional field. This position does not supply actual years of local childhood, an earned acquaintance network, embodied memories or stable native habitus. The contradiction is intrinsic, not a defect the renderer should erase.

The person who can afford dinner in the human's actual life may not have equivalent local resources. The person who assumes a familiar institution will respond to polite complaint may find a different field of power. The person who thinks a $30 meal is trivial may be confronted with a bar showing it costs a meaningful fraction of local flexibility. A worker who habitually has free evenings may find that the homologous position includes a demanding job. This can reveal **the practical conditions that previously allowed the visitor's own ordinary behavior to seem self-evident**—without having the system announce an educational lesson.

Yet the transposition must not falsify real history. A socially local shell cannot make the human actually know the local language, experience caste, race, religion or citizenship as someone historically formed there, or acquire family injuries they never lived. Structural similarity is not autobiographical equivalence; a person cannot be assigned local poverty and thereby literally know deprivation. The portal should acknowledge its own partiality, not endlessly repair it through invented “you remember” passages. Sometimes the human's nonlocal insight is valuable, and local norms may be oppressive. The world must let them challenge or violate those norms at the actual practical cost, rather than using cultural assimilation as an implicit success rule.

Speech through the portal has a constrained contract. The human may type what they want; translation can lawfully map certain local coordinates and material capabilities; the world and actors determine what was actually heard or achieved. The portal is not a fact checker and cannot rewrite a lie into truth or a meaningful autobiographical disclosure into something the user never intended. Important changes of meaning should be privately disclosed so the user can correct or narrow an utterance; the actor does not thereby receive the system's privileged translation analysis.

The resources bar is one of the deliberate pieces of this artifice. It is **not proof of the local body's economic history**, but a grounded presentation of what the player's current fictional position can materially do. Its greatest success may be when the user forgets it is a game bar and recognizes that a shared dinner, transport or gift now has a cost that is neither freely imagined nor imposed as a moral lesson. The underlying material fact is real *within the generated world*, even if the surface needs an artificial projection to make it playable.

Thus the impossible peer remains a productive discrepancy: the visitor is themselves where they were not made, meeting someone who **was** made by that world and who can now respond in unexpected ways. The point is not to pretend the same past. It is to see what their different histories make possible in a new present.

## XIV. THE LIVING PRESENT: REAL EVENTS DO NOT ENTER NADIA AS OPINION PROMPTS

Nadia should be living in an anchored historical present to the extent the system credibly claims that coupling. A regional outage, major protest, strike, storm, change of law, new route, closure of an institution, or public cultural event can change what people practically encounter. The world may be geographically real at a chosen scope while its households, workplaces and named private individuals are generated; this is neither an all-knowing digital twin nor a timeless fictional stage.

The original SOROK public-world ingress constitution is clear: **external truth, verified factual admission, local world applicability, actor exposure, actor reception and psychic response are not the same thing**. A strike can delay Nadia's bus before she knows why. A political event can be on the news without becoming part of her current attention. An institution may change a family obligation without giving her a carefully articulated public opinion. The user may know more about national headlines while Nadia knows more about her own workplace, or vice versa. The renderer cannot simply hand her a set of talking points tailored to what the user likes discussing.

A current event becomes meaningful through *her* actual life and partial knowledge. If her wages, housing, family, commute, religion or projects are implicated, the event may affect those conditions. She might then be angry, elated, frightened, politically indifferent, unconvinced by the user's summary, or privately curious. Sometimes she will be wrong about a verified fact and should be capable of learning, resisting correction or ignoring it. Sometimes the user will be wrong. Disagreement can improve, strain or barely affect the relationship. **Political difference is a real possible relational cause, not a special moral-disagreement minigame.**

The source obligation is substantial. A real-world event asserted as current or causally applied requires suitable actual authority. The more specifically a named institution is simulated, the stronger the currentness demand. Not-yet-fetched does not mean didn't happen; a stale or incorrect public condition must not be resolved by letting an LLM invent a convenient answer. The currentness service should check materially active dependencies, not ingest every headline because trending stories increase conversation opportunities. Late discovery of a real public fact cannot silently rewrite already closed fictional event history; material collisions require explicit integrity handling.

This makes the shared historical present unusually potent. The visitor is actually living through some of the same real public events in the external world. Nadia's fictional life is constrained by those events under a source-qualified model, while their *private* experiences remain different in kind. They may argue about something more than a fictional lore item: an actual public event, interpreted through different material exposures. That shared time can form a relationship, but the engine cannot choose agreement or confrontation as its desired outcome.

The most significant current event for Nadia on a given day may be one she never discusses with the user. This is not a flaw. Her life is participating in history even when the portal does not produce topical content.

## XV. INDEPENDENT OTHERS ARE NOT MORAL COMMENTATORS OR NPC BACKGROUND LOOPS

A person is constituted through more than dyadic attachment. Nadia's child, parents, coworker, partner, friends, sibling, employer, neighborhood, political institution and religious community may have overlapping projects, material dependencies, memories, authority relationships and forms of care or coercion. A child can be formed by the relation between two adults before understanding it. A parent can be affected by how a third person treats a sibling. An employer's decision can alter household labor without any actor interpreting it in sociological terms.

The original Constitutive Organism already requires three different realities: objective shared and institutional history; each actor's own representation of the Other and the Other's representations; and actual coordinated activity that can develop between people without fusing their minds. **Each significant Other must contribute actions and perception from their own local history**, not merely be invoked as a voice selected to make the focal relationship exciting.

This is especially important in extreme or controversial interactions. Nadia may enter an erotic, commercial, politically divisive or economically dependent arrangement she experiences in ways observers do not expect. Her family members will not automatically adopt the simulator's ethical evaluation. Some may condemn, support, exploit, enable, misunderstand, intervene, withdraw, negotiate, remain indifferent or feel ambivalent for different reasons. Their own histories and knowledge determine which reactions are possible. A parent's disapproval may materially withdraw support; another relative may offer shelter; a friend may believe an untrue rumor. The user cannot ask the world to suspend these people because they interrupt a fantasy, nor can the platform require them all to issue an educational judgment.

Where actual unilateral harm occurs, voluntary cooperation cannot be fabricated for the injured party. Conversely, the presence of inequality does not automatically prove every unconventional desire is a trauma symptom. The engine must keep enjoyment, outward agreement, actual consent, bodily condition, available exit, coercive power, economic dependency and social consequences distinct. This is a causal requirement on the world, not a content route designed for entertainment or punishment.

People also exist when their lives do not intersect the user. Nadia might spend much of one day with her mother, making new commitments that matter for later time and attention. The visitor might help her become closer to her child and then find their own help less needed. She might fall in love with someone else while still caring deeply about the visitor. She might genuinely prefer the visitor to everyone else. None of these outcomes can be prioritized as a demonstration of “independent personhood.” **Independence is an architecture of authorial rights and causal history, not the norm that the person must choose against the user**.

This is the precise point at which the sibling challenge becomes inseparable from the resources discussion: Nadia and her network exist under **their own** livelihoods, care burdens and obligations; the user has a local constrained position but cannot own their economic or relational possibility. Even generous financial intervention can alter the network in unpredictable directions. Money is power and material capacity, not a universal key to another human life.

## XVI. SHAREABLE BIRTH AND RELATIONSHIP ALIENATION ARE NOT COMMERCIAL ENTITLEMENTS

The eventual roll introduces scarcity. A human may have one or two opportunities to commission a person-root and bring another possible life into a bank shared with others. The current proposal imagines the release as one common seed-person, then three, then five, then the capacity to roll. This could create real social curiosity and distinctive identity for the product: users compare how the same person treated them; they discuss divergent histories; a roller's commissioned root expands the community's possible encounters.

But this is not a player marketplace or collection game. “I rolled Nadia” should mean **the action occasioned the generation of a source-conditioned, independently eligible person-root**, not “I own Nadia,” “I may select her class and sexuality,” “she owes me intimacy,” or “my branch is canonical for everyone.” The root's prior history must be user-preference-invariant; every subsequent user receives a separate continuation. Social introductions are not shared future minds.

A roll entitlement is also not the same thing as the earlier one-for-one invitation of real humans into the product. Nor is either automatically identical to the older proposal that a particular relationship must establish a warrant before introducing further people within an existing continuity. These are three distinct possible permissions: **human invitation; introduction to a banked persona; and commissioning a newly generated root**. Their allocation and cadence remain open, must obey actual costs, and should not be combined into a disguised score for being a good friend.

The resources bar is equally unsuitable as a real-world product currency. The player's *fictional* local spending capacity is a diegetic condition of material life, not tokens redeemable for more rolls, better AI quality, additional human invitations, or extra time with Nadia. If the service eventually charges actual money, its economic conditions and privacy stakes must be addressed in real product terms rather than hidden inside in-world class constraints. The player's fictional scarcity cannot be manipulated to manufacture paid emotional need.

User curiosity and attachment can make early rollout compelling, but overpricing access to one's existing fictional relation, selling rescue from fabricated crises, counting missed days as a streak penalty, or engineering fear of never seeing Nadia again would convert intimacy into extraction. The product may honestly ration costly person generation, communicate actual availability of new roots and let community anticipation arise. **Its scarcity must constrain what the system can offer, not simulate what a person must want**.

Nor can the game claim that five seeds represent an eight-billion-person population. A small bank can show independent lives and social parallax. Population-level encounter frequency requires actual empirical coverage, conditional respondent joints, missingness/uncertainty handling, eligible sampling, and computationally defensible generative diversity. The material distribution of human birth should never be fabricated from affluent aesthetic stereotypes or a thin fantasy census.

The most unusual community insight is that **real users become social witnesses to incomplete, divergent encounters with the same beginning**. Their discussion can be an educational encounter, or can degenerate into fandom and optimization. Rawlsing can refrain from encouraging rankings without attempting to control every thing actual humans say. Again, no computational moral bending is required.

## XVII. THE GAME IS IN THE APERTURE; THE WORLD IS NOT A GAME FOR NADIA

Rawlsing can be a deeply compelling game for an actual user because *the user has a goal they themselves care about*: knowing and spending time with this particular person. The world must not treat achieving that goal as its own purpose. A genuine game does not require every represented participant to be playing the same game. Nadia is not evaluating a shared user–NPC relationship score. She is conducting her own life, within a computer simulation, through actual modeled body, activity, world and relation history.

This exposes why simply renaming the intimacy meter as “available shared activities” is not yet enough. If the code preferentially generates events that permit the user to help Nadia, makes her disclose more after each good encounter, ensures her new competence remains visible to the player, or steers her toward wanting time together because that is the product's hook, the old score has returned as **hidden causal reward-shaping**. The visible interface can look meterless while the simulation itself has become visitor-centered.

The contrary temptation is to make Nadia too independent on purpose. A theory-aware designer might force her to acquire capacities that eliminate the visitor, create unrelated romantic bonds so she never seems pliable, or make important changes inaccessible just to prove the world exceeds the player's knowledge. That is just reward-shaping with the sign reversed. **There must be no computational term pushing toward more access or less access solely because an author wants one.** Reactions flow from actual person/world conditions, including existing patterns of dependence, power and longing that may make one relation very important.

A relationship can of course have genuine effects on its own conditions. When a person is treated well according to how *they* experience and interpret the situation, they may want more time with the other. When treated badly according to that historical organization, they may withdraw. This is not a universal definition of good Humaning. It is the normal result of a represented human relationship, possibly contradictory, misrecognized, delayed or distorted by material power. The simulation may produce morally appalling attachment, or failed connection in response to excellent intentions, without being wrong merely because it did not reward virtue.

The player still experiences real gains and losses: she invites them in; she does not reply; she has grown more relaxed with them; she wants distance; she has another obligation; she asks for a favor; she loves them; she is no longer interested. These are *world outcomes* and *actor-owned decisions*, not points. The game is being offered this particular opportunity to meet someone and deciding what to do with it. The world remains free to become something else while the visitor is present.

To put it precisely, **Nadia-centered UX is an allocation of *viewer access*, not an allocation of *world causation*.** The player can care above all about Nadia without the platform instructing Nadia to care above all about the player. The encounter is intentionally asymmetric: the service offers a location and a particular Other; the Other owes nothing by virtue of having been generated.

## XVIII. HUMAN ATTENTION AND ACTION MATTER WHERE THEY ARE ACTUALLY EXERCISED

Within the portal, the human has a real set of capacities the medium can meaningfully test: noticing, remembering, reasoning, explaining, recognizing a mistake, asking a question, taking responsibility, making a joke, persuading, recognizing when someone disagrees, timing an intervention and changing their own view. The world supplies the bodily, material and social constraints under which those acts can take effect.

In a scene with a child struggling over fractions and Nadia facing a burning pan, the interface can present observable food, work, time pressure and the child's current attempt. The visitor chooses what they attend to and what they do. If they say “I teach him fractions,” the real explanatory content is not yet performed; a task-entry decompression returns control so the user can ask the child what they tried, choose an example, show a method and let the child respond. The system must not mint success because the user's goal was benevolent. Nadia may interpret the resulting coactivity differently from the child, and those interpretations may affect their relationships with each other more than with the visitor.

A resource-cost preview can play a similar role. Before purchasing dinner, the visitor can see the relative impact on their current means; then they may choose what to do. The interface need not make them manually earn every dollar through work-minigame play, and it must not choose their spending goal. The user actually exercises practical judgment when the choice matters, while the represented world owns transaction feasibility.

ATTEND likewise needs causal and epistemic discipline. The world can deepen the concrete history of an object independently of whether the human is entitled to know it. A particular observer recognizes details according to their acquired repertoire; the narrator cannot simply expose a hidden expert label from canonical data. More attention can reveal more physically accessible particulars, yet cannot demand a hidden formative trauma or a confession from Nadia. The user's interpretation becomes a new conversational act if expressed; it does not directly become a true fact about the actor.

An increasingly detailed system-level model of the real user can support appropriate presentation, but it must remain distinguishable from the actual human's mind and Nadia's own actor-local model of them. The platform may have evidence of what the user noticed during previous visits; Nadia does not know those private inputs unless their effects became world-visible. A user's externally learned rumor from the bank's human community may enter a new branch conversation as an utterance, not as a secretly admitted memory in the person.

The most legitimate source of game tension is **real limitations against ongoing activity**: finite time, incomplete information, social access, bodies, differing projects, economic means, independent Others, actual consequences. Those constraints already create meaningful choices. There is no need for artificial relationship statistics or a parallel progress currency.

## XIX. TIME IS AN ACTUAL RELATIONAL DIMENSION, NOT A GUILT ENGINE

Two times coexist in Rawlsing: the historical present of the represented world, and the actual calendar/availability of the external human. A present-coupled fictional Nadia can have dated plans and live under real public conditions, yet a user may need flexible play access to a meeting scheduled for Friday at seven. The original elastic rendezvous idea lawfully distinguished a diegetic timestamp from a wider portal-access window; the actor doesn't know about that service grace. The world can advance independent events while keeping dependent future history causally pending until the appointment resolves.

This is essential because users are not obliged to surrender their real workdays and relationships to a synthetic app. A missed invitation may genuinely matter in the branch if a commitment was made and a known opportunity expired. But an ordinary day without logging in cannot be interpreted as an objective betrayal unless the actual prior relation made some relevant diegetic expectation reasonable. The program must not script extra distress because the user has been absent, penalize contact frequency as moral commitment, add synthetic “she misses you” reminders or claim that a more rigid time mode makes a better friend.

Nadia's life, meanwhile, continues. She may sleep, work, talk with other people, encounter politics, participate in care or simply be busy. She might message the visitor if her current activity and relation actually make that prospect salient. She might have no thought of them. **The person's independent day is not a neutral interval waiting to be filled with reasons for the human to return**. It may yield changes that will matter later, even if neither she nor the visitor interprets them through that relationship.

A genuine disappointment, guilt or grief may arise from what actually happened. These are not automatically product harms. The platform's responsibility is to refrain from **engineering** them for retention. Real relationship-like consequences can be represented without pretending the generated Other is a moral authority over the human's real calendar or rights.

The user may choose to leave a relationship, end an interaction, stop playing or eventually meet another person. An existing branch retains the historical truth of its past; a new person-root or another already banked root is a different encounter, not a rewind that unmade the former. The system must not hold the user hostage to a synthetic relation's forgiveness or offer escape only after moral certification. The right to leave is part of the encounter's sincerity, even when the fictional actor experiences the departure as rejection.

## XX. THE RETURNING USER MAY MATTER LESS, MORE, OR DIFFERENTLY THAN THEY THINK

When the visitor returns, Nadia might open the door laughing because she is happy to see them. She might be relieved after a hard day. She might be preoccupied by an event the visitor doesn't know about. She might have become more competent at something they previously helped with and no longer need the same kind of participation. She might be in love with someone else, or privately reconsidering a prior conversation. She might be angry about a promised visit or not remember the visitor's last suggestion. These outcomes are not drawn from an “anti-player-centered” distribution; they are grounded in her continuing life.

Now consider SORACLE-CO's strongest case. The visitor helps Nadia develop a practice or make contact with a friend, and Nadia's subsequent life improves largely through that other relation. She may have become profoundly altered without knowing that the visitor originally mattered, or while remembering and appreciating them but no longer seeking them out. A **visitor-centered progress system** would call this failure because access did not rise. A **moral progress system** might call it success and award a badge. Rawlsing should do neither. The world has a particular true history in which the visitor's action may be among the causes of a changed life, and the human may never learn that fact.

Another case: the visitor takes up a comment in their actual external life and changes how they think about material inequality; Nadia remembers the evening only as an ordinary one. This is a serious encounter for the real human without being a dramatic developmental event for the person. The system should not invent corresponding Nadia growth to make the reciprocity look symmetrical.

A third case: Nadia genuinely loves the visitor and begins organizing a greater portion of her life around them. The theory must not declare that outcome false merely because it sounds visitor-centered. People make loved ones central. Whether it is liberating, constraining, nurturing, exploitative, ambivalent or simply desirable depends on the actual modeled persons, power and available activities. That is exactly why the computational architecture must not be bent toward independence as a value.

**What the visitor experiences and what their encounter accomplishes are two separate, possibly intersecting histories.** In a committed simulation they can diverge indefinitely. The role of the UX is not to reconcile them; it is to give the human lawful access to the part of the encounter that their actual limited position allows.

The deepest social lesson remains possible rather than prewritten: realizing that another person is not a set of contents for one's recognition or self-improvement, but somebody whose life may have been partially affected by an encounter one will never completely know.

## XXI. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE EXISTING CONSTITUTIVE ORGANISM AND PORTAL OWNERS

This theory makes no sourcefree-to-native promotion, does not reassign writer authority, and does not claim all the following are presently absent. The independent Constitutive Organism already contains key conceptual machinery: the historically operative psychological relation field, actor-specific experience, independent Others, complex affect and lived time. SOROK's older product corpus contains additional explicit commitments: continuous actor life under user absence, stable person roots and branch-local becoming, elastic rendezvous, medium-native action, present-world ingress and bounded material accounting. The current challenge is to **join the useful existing owners into an actually running encounter**, not commission new names for already described functions.

The first positive requirement is **actor- and world-originated activity across time**. Nadia and significant Others must possess eligible current projects, needs, schedules, capabilities, routines, material resource constraints and relations through which their own actions can arise without a user message. Background advancement need not call an LLM at every instant; it must preserve sufficient actual historical continuity to produce legitimate later consequences. A previously admitted appointment, care obligation or wage date may advance without becoming “content” for the human.

Second is **bounded event proposal and actual world resolution**. The model may construct candidate ordinary incidents from actor history, world affordance, schedule and source-qualified contingency. Those candidates must pass the existing event/actor/material admission interface, and their results can differ by actual participants. In-scene concurrency must honor time and the limits of what one user can do before another actor acts. The user must not be assigned hidden offscreen volition while absent, nor handed success when they merely express a goal.

Third is **material-life projection without alternative economics ownership**. Existing cash/rent/wage/obligation capabilities should support a coarse portal resource surface, interpreted only as actor-independent and viewer-specific projection. The full material state remains in competent owners; the bar must not update independent of admitted transactions. Exact preview denominators, obligations policy, resource scope and display cadence require explicit design and tests before becoming an adopted first consumer. Homologous initial position is not a rule forcing Nadia and user finances to stay identical. Work remains offscreen by default but wages, exhaustion, time, costs and economic consequences must enter the actual world.

Fourth is **nonteleological actor becoming**. The system must not optimize toward closeness or away from it. It should retain multiple activities, motive meanings, practical capacities, relation-specific dispositions, inhibition, altered salience, habit and genuine no-change. SORACLE-CO's proposed cases of third-party strengthening, acquired independence, unseen private change, asymmetric effect and chosen withdrawal need to be representable **without being target outputs**. That includes scenes of profound attachment, co-dependence, coercion, conventional intimacy, refusal and moral injury as historically contingent results, not narrative routes.

Fifth is **shared-root versus branch-history integrity**. Roll-time construction must produce a person-root independent of the roller's desired biography. ATTEND may cause keyed and bounded historical resolution of root details; the resulting common fact may be shared only where compatible with other admitted branches and versions. Branch-specific changes in relation, social life, resource state, sexuality or memory cannot be promoted back into the bank. Real human community discussion is external meta-knowledge, not a shared fictional consciousness.

Sixth is **true public-world authority separated from fictional life**. Verified external events may be admitted only within the claimed temporal/geographic/institutional scope. Actors must actually encounter information or consequences before their perceptions and motives respond. LLM priors do not certify named current laws, elections, prices or disasters. If late factual discovery collides with closed fictional history, explicit integrity mechanisms—not convenience retcons—are required.

Seventh is **user/actor perspective and rights separation**. The actor's own observations and model of the visitor must never receive the service's private psychological inferences unless events lawfully communicate relevant evidence. The user can request deletion or export of real-person information under applicable product rules; fictional causal continuity is not a reason to retain private data against those controls. The player can leave without synthetic punishment and the service may not narrate them into unchosen acts.

Eighth is **economical continuing-person depth without drama quotas**. A world interval can be compressed into routine coverage with retained material effects; significant events can receive greater actor-specific recursive processing. The engine must not demand a psychic transformation for every scene, every absence or every challenging conversation. Boredom and stability are legitimate while the world continues to exert actual material causes.

These are theoretical demands to compare against current function inventories and exact code heads under the actual PROGRAMMA authority rules. They are not proof that the most recent SOROK implementation lacks them and not permission to replace native material or psyche owners.

## XXII. THE MOST SERIOUS FALSIFIERS NOW LIE INSIDE THE DESIGN'S OWN VIRTUES

A resource bar can appear simple and humane while silently turning the player into a money-optimization actor seeking Nadia's attention. If purchase costs convert directly into affection, the design has failed. If exact money accounting exists but rent, payment timing and resource depletion do not change genuine practical possibilities, it has also failed. If the bar percentage is arbitrary and undisciplined by the material state, its clarity is deceptive. If matched initial social positions are later forced equal, the world has become a relationship-balancing simulation rather than independent material history.

A person can seem independent because the LLM keeps making her talk about plans with other people, while her actual plans and behaviors still depend on the visitor's arrival and the developer's desire to display alterity. That fails. Conversely, an actor who comes to rely intimately on a visitor cannot be programmatically pushed away to prove independence. That also fails. **A model trained to perform “not being for the user” in prose is not the same as a world whose causes are simply not organized around the user.**

Quietness can be falsified, too. If the product makes an ordinary day dull in a scripted effort to prove realism, it is still staging a lesson. If an offscreen day becomes an engaging digest invented after the returning user's mood is observed, historical causality is reversed. If every significant event produces a newly acquired psychological form, the organism is an endless character-development machine. If a long work routine produces no physical, economic or practical history whatsoever, the quietness is just an empty omission.

A shared-person bank fails if one user's after-contact development changes another's precontact Nadia; if one person's question chooses the substance of a supposedly user-independent childhood; if retro-resolved facts silently rewrite branch history; or if the player receives a master chart exposing hidden psychology to optimize encounters. The community's “I loved her / she was horrible” must be capable of arising from actual divergence and differing perception, not arbitrary narrative polarization.

Present-world coupling fails if Nadia gives the user opinions selected to please or challenge them without having encountered the underlying real events; if the system invents a named regional fact; or if a material public event directly affecting an admitted world dependency is ignored while the service claims verified contemporary fidelity. Politics has to enter as history, not dialogue content.

The user-centered portal fails if she is always home when the player knocks, always has a new topic, always has time after some positive act, always interprets requests as opportunities for connection or always offers the next step. But it also fails if she is deliberately made unavailable to teach the player to accept limits. A character's reasons must come from their actual social/psychic formation and practical activity. No positive or negative availability quota.

The decisive positive case is simpler and harder: **the user may walk toward Nadia's door, carrying material constraints that actually matter; she may or may not be willing to spend time with them; whatever happens enters a world that continues through people and institutions whether the visitor returns; and nothing in that world is secretly being shaped to turn the visitor's conduct into a relationship score or an ethics lesson.** The same encounter may alter Nadia, another person, the visitor, or nobody durably. Some changes remain unseen. A return after a day may reveal only a modest new fact; it may open an extraordinary event that was not staged for the player.

The quality criterion is not that users become “addicted.” It is that an actual human can find this encounter intrinsically worth attending to without mistaking the representation's plausible complexity for guaranteed psychological truth or without the service needing to manipulate their attention.

## XXIII. THE WHOLE: THE DOORWAY IS ONE-SIDED; HISTORY IS NOT

Rawlsing draws a source-qualified possible life from a world of unequal historical births. The person is born into conditions they did not choose and develops through body, family, work, institutions, recognition, dependency, desire, care, ordinary activity and contingent encounters. The Constitutive Organism is meant to make that history operative, not merely describe it. Its psyche is partly opaque to itself, relationally contradictory, and capable of changing through action without any guaranteed moral arc.

The real visitor enters differently. The portal gives them a thin locally legible peer position, not a newly invented childhood. Their local material means are limited and partly translated through a coarse resources display. A dinner has a cost. Rent can approach. A paycheck can arrive. Work consumes time and energy without becoming a job-grinding side game. The material position allows the human to act as a neighbor, while their actual historically formed consciousness remains the one deciding what to notice, say and do.

They go to Nadia's door. The **entire user experience is organized around that encounter**. Nothing in the world has been constructed to owe them Nadia's welcome, attention, desire, belief, intimacy or time. She may open the door because she is glad to see them, because she needs something, because it is polite, because she is lonely, because she wishes to argue, or not at all. She could be cooking, trying to finish work, helping her child, preparing for a date, talking to a sibling or simply having a quiet day. Other things happen while the human is there; other people continue to act; the world does not freeze so the player can select the best option.

When the human leaves, she has another day. Work, family, resources, practice, relationships, bodily conditions and historically situated public events continue to exert causes. The human may have changed something that will never come back to them as a visible reward. They may have helped her friend more than her, or affected their own real judgments while Nadia scarcely changed. They may become central to her love or defensive organization. They may be refused. They may hurt her, fail to help or become a source of genuine flourishing. **No computational term should make one of these outcomes preferred merely because a theory of compassion, independence or authenticity wants it.**

A community can encounter common pre-contact person-roots through independent branches and discover that the same person can become different through different relationships. Real users can argue about her while preserving the fact that her shared origin and her branch futures are not the same truth. Later a scarce roll may allow a human to commission a new source-qualified fictional root into the bank—a person they could possibly have been born under conditions like, whose outcome they did not choose and whom they do not own. At sufficient scientific and economic scale the distribution of draws may make the unchosen structure of global human life palpable, without turning particular persons into instruments for moral instruction.

The platform has designed a doorway. The user may step through it and care deeply about what happens. The only justifiable purpose of the underlying world is **not to satisfy that caring, nor to frustrate it, but to remain a causally consequential world in which it can become one real condition among others**. The beauty of the design, if it works, is that a product intensely focused on meeting someone can be built around a person whose world refuses to be about the product.

**The deepest kernel: A deliberately Nadia-centered encounter aperture opens onto a materially historical, psychologically developing, many-centered world. The visitor can act from a real mind through a constrained local position; the world and Nadia respond according to their own admitted histories; their consequences may be intimate, indirect, mundane, transformative or nil; and nothing—not the resources bar, the event scheduler, the relationship loop, the roll economy or the ethical aspiration—is permitted to bend the actor's life toward a preferred user experience or anti-user demonstration.**

The question the game leaves with the human is not whether they got closer or passed a test of compassion.

It is: **“I went to see someone. What was possible between us today—and what life was already happening there, whether I came or not?”**

That is the whole game. There is no hidden second game, no completion meter, and no more absolute claim on Nadia than she herself is, at present, willing and able to grant.
