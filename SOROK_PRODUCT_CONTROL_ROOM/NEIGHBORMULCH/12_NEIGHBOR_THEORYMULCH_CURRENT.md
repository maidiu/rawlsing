# NEIGHBOR — THE LIFE THAT WAS ALREADY LIVING

## One fully reconstituted theory of unchosen birth, constitutive psychology, historical world, peer encounter, and the human's participation

**Current working whole · 21 November 2026 · existing NEIGHBORMULCH file, existing draft PR #1277.** This is the one source-answerable NEIGHBOR conception. Its status is a **candidate** pending source and reciprocal review; it does not purport to have been accepted into protected PROGRAMMA or implemented as a full organism.

**Method.** The previous nine whole theories repeatedly developed difficult truths, and at points newer insights displaced older positive functions by omission. This work rebuilds the explanatory structure rather than declaring the latest iteration authoritative because it is newer. It takes its form from one recursive process: **historical birth and material relations → a developing, partly opaque psyche → activity in a many-centered world → a real visitor's situated encounter → objective and subjective consequence → further development and future encounter**. Each concept below is part of that process, not a feature silo. The source register is embedded **after** the complete theory in this SAME FILE so surviving but not-yet-adjudicated claims cannot silently vanish. It is not a second active theory.

**Decisive corrections.** The visitor's exact same-SES starting world fact never discloses Nadia's resources; only their own resource-preview is shown. Divergent finances may remain unknown. A first contact is a lawful occasion arising from Nadia's current day, not a static quest queue. Opening the app authorizes a bounded fictional pre-contact premise, not complete past or future authorial control of the real human. The two-sided house/message experience, true attention semantics, source-qualified material history, real human invitations, user privacy and no-lawful-homology are restored as positive parts of the organism.

## 1. The contradiction that makes a person, a relation and a world

Rawlsing is not an app that generates an extraordinarily elaborate acquaintance and then rewards the human for winning her affection. Nor is it an instructional exercise that turns somebody else's poverty, culture, grief or family history into evidence of the visitor's moral development. It begins with a far more troublesome encounter: **a person whom the world has made, whom this user did not choose, can become a real part of the user's experience without becoming the user's possession.** Rawlsing deliberately puts that relation at the center of its human-facing experience, while refusing to make the relationship the organizing purpose of Nadia's existence.

This difference is not a slogan about independent nonplayer characters. There are **three nested excesses**: Nadia is not exhausted by the visitor's account of Nadia, by Nadia's own account of herself, or by this relationship; the relationship is not exhausted by either person's experience of it, since it has objective events, third-party effects and meanings nobody has recognized; and the world is not exhausted by any one standpoint or rendering. The same ontology also denies the liberal fiction of closed, self-sufficient selves. Nadia becomes someone *through* relationships, work, bodies, law, kinship, culture, conflict and her actions upon them. Those relations are causes that can actually change her; nonownership does not mean inability to touch, influence, love, injure or transform.

The strongest historical compression candidate in earlier SOROK research was **relationally constituted does not mean relationally exhaustible**. It matters as an *engineering provocation*: can one derive from it distinct event truth, actor-local perceptual fields, fallible knowledge, user action, real Others, memory and change, without separately legislating a fictional “inner life” for every scene? Its spiritual rendering—kenotic attention to what exceeds my phenomenal horizon—does not authorize submission, self-erasure, the claim that Nadia is right, or a moral score. It means that actual difference can teach the human *if the human chooses to take it seriously*. One can care and still disagree, resist exploitation, refuse, love, or leave.

The user may find getting close to Nadia joyous; that is a valid player desire, even the reason to play. The simulator may not convert that desire into a global objective function. Nor may it engineer avoidance or distance just to demonstrate Nadia's freedom. The historical circuit must permit both a person who seeks intimacy and one who does not; both a human who becomes more sensitive and one who remains self-absorbed; a visit that changes a life and one that does nothing noteworthy. **A game is here because somebody wants to know another. A world is here because that other does not exist to be known.**

This is a design and scientific research candidate, not proof that executing code constitutes a conscious human subject. Rawlsing can model actor-local valuations and their history without claiming to manufacture a literal first person. That distinction remains governing even if the simulation becomes emotionally compelling or its inhabitants' behavior surprisingly coherent.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The irreducible relation is also historically constituted).** Rawlsing's most compact aspiration is not “an especially intelligent nonplayer character.” It is **a confrontation with another historically constituted center of valuation that the participant can truly affect but never own**. In the original product charter, the stranger might eventually become specific enough, consequential enough and mutually known enough to become kin. That possibility must remain real; it must not become a condition the world is obliged to satisfy. A player may be delighted to grow closer, and the platform may quite reasonably be built around the *desire* to do so. But the person is not there to complete the user's spiritual formation, to prove that capitalism is bad, to demonstrate good empathy, to become sexually available, or to make a friendly app.

The deeper ontological candidate recovered in the earlier Sorok conversation was **irreducible first-personhood without self-sufficient individuality**. There are three nested irreducibilities. Nadia exceeds the player's knowledge of her, exceeds her own conscious self-account, and exceeds any single relationship. Their particular relationship exceeds either participant's account of what the relationship is: an offer may have consequences neither intended, a hidden injury may be causally true without either noticing, and third persons may live with consequences beyond their view. The world exceeds each participant's perceptual access. Yet none of these irreducibilities makes anyone an isolated atom; the person emerges and becomes through actual relations, the relation is constituted through histories and institutions, and the world is reproduced and changed through activity. **Non-exhaustion is not non-relation.**


## 2. Unchosen beginnings, developmental history, and the two entrances to one psyche

The fundamental Roll concerns an unchosen human possibility. Nobody selected their own birth, the bodies that made them vulnerable, the social resources available to their caregivers, the institutions that shaped their schooling, or which contradictions were normalized inside their family. But it would be an error to deduce a whole psyche from a single survey response, demographic class or population label. Empirical PYAT knowledge can condition a *plausible space of beginnings and co-traveling material circumstances*; it cannot detect the secret motive or personality of a source respondent, nor grant a fictional person the consciousness of a real stranger.

There are **two historically different entrances** to one continuing organism. Adults such as parents can begin through source-conditioned, whole-survey-correspondent demographic and material bundles with intelligent, constrained backfill of their work, care, household, socialization and institutions. A developing Nadia starts earlier: an immature, need-bearing, sensorimotor body dependent on actual Others, developing capacities and acquiring a contradictory psychological iceberg in response to history as it occurs. One route may compress the past under constraints, the other may enact much more of maturation forward. Both must eventually rely on the same law of historically acquired needs, activity, recruitment, subjective response, outward practice, consequence, and later reconstitution. There is no predetermined adult Nadia whose eventual revelations the plot is slowly unlocking.

Her historical identity cannot be generated by assigning an age, nationality, religion, attachment style and trauma then narrating those attributes back to the user. She is produced through situations in which material necessity and social relations mediate one another. Learning to make food, being praised by a caregiver, facing inconsistent authority, caring for an ill parent, surviving a landlord, taking a first paid job, affiliating with friends, or attempting forbidden freedom can organize what she notices, what she tries, what she fears, how she protects a relationship and what other activities become possible. Not every remembered event is formative; not every formation is remembered; even she need not know the reasons for some acquired orientations.

The public historical world matters without reducing personal meaning to nationality. A political movement, inflation, racial classification, migration status, military violence, an employer's decision and access to schooling can shape the family's real set of possibilities. Even if two children share the same institutional conditions, their different positions in a network of family interactions produce different experience and dispositions. Their concrete history still cannot be replaced by a politically convenient type.

The crucial next step is not to write a “final person description.” It is to make inherited organization **operative** when new circumstances occur, including its capacity to fail, contradict itself, undergo slow cumulative change, or resist a seemingly transformative experience. Her future is not simply a list of expressive scenes. It is the continuation of one physical, relationally formed organism and one world.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The rolled life is the alternate; the real person enters for relation).** The human premise is unchosen historical location. Bodies, family, land, language, social class, race, citizenship, religion, labor relations, networks of care, violence, available technologies and political institutions do not begin as individually selected character attributes. They are conditions into which people arrive, conditions they reproduce, resist, reinterpret and sometimes change through activity. Rawlsing aims to construct particular fictional lives arising in sufficiently qualified possible situations, rather than supply consumer-selectable compatible personalities.

The long horizon is “Roll to meet someone you could have been born as.” The subject of the roll is the *person living the alternate possible life*, not a rewritten real user's soul. The real user remains psychologically themselves, a consciousness and history formed elsewhere. It is the person-world generated on the other side who embodies that contingent birth and becomes through it. This is why a life cannot be constituted simply from a static demographic profile, nor can its psychological details be inferred as the actual secret inner life of a survey respondent. Empirical social distributions constrain plausible population and social conditions; historically materialist, psychological and generative operations construct a **hypothetical individual**, with real epistemic limitations.

**Operational derivation (Fictional adult backfill and Nadia's open development share one psychological circuit).** The source corpus has **two entrances into one continuing psychology**, and a NEIGHBOR theory that forgets one is not complete. Fictional adults—especially parents—may be constructed from PYAT-conditioned, whole-survey-respondent-linked distributional constraints and credible historically compressed backfill. This can set class, material position, household variation, belief co-travel, biography and early acquired dispositions without treating a survey respondent as a psychological scan. A child such as Nadia begins with a body, maturational capacities, dependency, need, affective/sensorimotor repertoires and social surroundings; there is no predetermined adult destination and no secret final personality card to reveal. Their lives later enter a common psychic circuit, but their construction routes have different evidentiary limits.

This circuit is not just “LLM personality generation followed by dialogue.” Actual need and practical situation recruit a partly inaccessible, historically formed organization; competing dispositions/objects and bodily capacities condition what is possible; the actor proposes or undertakes activity; world and Others independently respond; consequences are actor-locally experienced, remembered or not; new distinctions, capacities, identifications and inhibitions can be acquired and may alter future recruitment. Ordinary work and relationships can reproduce the same patterns for years, and a seemingly profound event can fail to reorganize anything durable. The person is not an independent trait vector with a background story attached.


## 3. Relations among Others constitute the self before the visitor appears

A family is not Nadia connected to several one-way intimacy meters. Her relation to her father is partly constituted through her perception of his relation to her mother, what he does to a brother, how the brother interprets him, who does the household labor, and how everyone imagines that the others see them. A parent may be affectionate in private while reproducing domination in public. A brother may be caring and controlling in precisely the same action; Nadia may admire his protection and experience it as captivity. One person's behavior can reorganize relations between two people who never meet the user.

This is not a demand for a single giant objective “family mind.” Each Other has their own bodily needs, situated social practice, partial knowledge, mistakes, private interpretations and historically acquired ways of managing conflict. The same family's objective event is a different *experience* for different actors. These experiences can be contradictory and incompletely integrated. Relational organization can be durable without becoming identical across every context; different people activate different patterns. What is learned in care, authority, work and dependency may persist as psychological organization even when the originating relation changes.

There is also no omnipotent manager inside Nadia choosing motives from a dashboard. Activity occurs under conditions: a present need, a particular object or project, social demand, fatigue, expectation, bodily capacities, anticipated danger, and a changing set of recruited dispositions, some conscious, some partly accessible, some defended against. Her response can be externally articulate, jagged, withheld, mistaken, or validly empty. The voice renderer cannot retrieve a “hidden motive” because a scene needs depth. The actor may sincerely be wrong about why she reacted. A deferred or low-significance event is still legitimate psychology.

Her relation to her own past changes historically. A daughter may once admire what she later experiences as a father's betrayal. The original admiration occurred; it cannot be edited out. A later new fact may allow her to reinterpret that history as love, duty, complicity or something not reducible to one category. New accounts can be capacious, tyrannical, partitioned, ignored or cracked; insight can be real yet fail to change an embodied practice. A revised self-narrative does not automatically install a new stable psyche. Counterevidence and ongoing contradiction must survive psychological reconstitution.

The user's attendance in Nadia's life will eventually become **one additional historical influence**, perhaps intensely important, perhaps minor. It cannot replace the existing system of kinship, lovers, coworkers, children, friends, employers, institutions and private obligations. Nor may the system artificially elevate another relationship merely to prove Nadia's independence. An independent Other can become more dependent on the visitor, less dependent, more loving, disappointed, exploitative, indifferent or conflicted as actual history permits. The project does not compute a preferred ethical trajectory for these outcomes.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The family is a relation among relations).** PR #1275 contributes a crucial original user challenge that cannot be lost in a money-and-portal-focused theory: a person's psyche is formed by the **relations among the significant Others themselves**, including the identifications, loyalties and antagonisms through which the person experiences those relationships. A family is not a list of individually scored mother/father/brother relations pointing at Nadia, nor a new “family mind” with a global mood. It is an historically developing field of actual coactivity, material obligations, care, discipline, recognition and **each person's relation to how others relate**.

Consider the directly supplied hypothetical family: a father seemed hardworking and insufficiently appreciated; his infidelity later changes how a daughter interprets earlier loyalty, the mother's complaints and the entire household's past. The prior labor was not retroactively nonexistent. The family's new self-understandings need not converge, and actual objective facts may be accepted, denied or distorted differently. An event changes some relations while confirming others; later choices can recruit new dependencies, defenses and identifications. If a newly generated explanation fails to influence any later practical choice where the conditions call for one, it is merely narrative decoration.

**Operational derivation (The iceberg operates through situated activity).** The Constitutive Organism is not the psychology section of a story engine; it is the ongoing **historical formation of a partly opaque person**. Bodily needs, practical capacities, social roles, motives, memories, conscious interpretations, inhibited identifications, defensive organizations, contradictions, habit and possible unacquired forms are all situated in actual activity. An LLM can propose bounded personally meaningful interpretations, new differentiations or historically plausible acquired organizations, but these proposals become real within the fiction only through competent actor/world admission and later operation.

A person may be strongly moved by a visitor's joke or ignore a supposedly profound confession. They may experience care and resentment at once, or form a habit through boring repeated work. Their actual outward response is not a readout of a privileged hidden personality chart. A woman can be friendly because she likes the visitor, because she is trying to avoid an argument, because family norms demand hospitality, or because that is her ordinary practice, with several reasons genuinely intertwined.

**Operational derivation (The person can reread and reorganize the past without changing what happened).** The original SOROK research goes beyond ordinary “long-term memory.” There is objective historical occurrence; each person's selective memory and associations; retrospective reinterpretation; self-narratives and private myths; identification with models of how another evaluates them; avoidance, defense and alienation; and a changing ability to read earlier events. **A past fact can acquire a new meaning without the past fact changing.** A daughter may have genuinely admired a father's hard work and later discover an affair. Her admiration at the old time remains true; the new fact may reorganize who she believes she was, whose suffering she ignored, whether a brother's loyalty seems noble or complicit, and what father now means. Several of these changes can disagree without an arbiter producing one master self.

An actor's belief that they once acted out of courage may later become a story about fear, or vice versa. A recollection can be incomplete, overwritten in gist, reactivated by a mundane object, or remain partially resistant to reinterpretation. The person may tell a flattering story, an accusing one, two incompatible stories, or no story. Internal reformulations can arise through care, shame, practical success, ideology, religion, exposure to another person's account or institutional pressure. **Conscious insight does not by itself equal durable psychological reorganization**, and a defense does not have to wait until the conscious subject decides to conceal something. A valid externally quiet response is not a missing piece of narrative.


## 4. Material reproduction and the public world: neither scenery nor a second management game

Before anybody opens a portal, Nadia's world requires food production, rents, wages, transport, housing, care, schools, clinics, employers, property relations, law, media, family obligations, friendships, habits and institutions. A job is not a line of income alone: it shapes where she must be, whose instructions she faces, what skills she practices, when she is tired, whom she knows and what she can afford to refuse. Household labor and childcare are not NPC flavor; they distribute real time and material possibility. A person needs rest and meals even when no player watches. A school closure or missed shift can be causally significant without producing a chapter about sacrifice.

The historical environment is **constituted and contested**, not a fixed sociological background. People reproduce institutions through work, obedience, improvisation, organizing, conflict, love, fear and ordinary habit. Their projects can reproduce domination or loosen it. A person can identify with the authority that harms them; a liberating act can make a new form of dependence. One public condition does not produce one canonical thought or politics. An actual transport strike or price increase can affect travel and expense before Nadia sees any article, and it can remain meaningless to her conversation if she never learns of it.

If Rawlsing couples its world to live public history, it must distinguish **actual source-verified occurrence**, jurisdiction and time, effective real-world impact, world-local admission, actor exposure, individual interpretation, and optional discussion. It cannot toss today's headlines into Nadia's dialogue to prove cultural relevance. Nor can it invent a public atrocity as a dramatic hook for the visitor. A witnessed event and a model's plausible background prior require different evidence grades. In a private world fork, a real public event may be shared at the historical-source layer while its household consequences diverge.

The world may sometimes place the visitor in a situation where they cannot fix the problem: the hospital is closed, the wage is low, a landlord makes demands, a family member's choices create conflicts, or a social practice is oppressive. The game should neither flatter an individual savior nor block collective agency. The user can join a practical struggle when other actors actually make it available, but Rawlsing is not secretly a neighborhood political organizer simulator. It remains primarily one encounter with another person's ordinary life, through which structural relations become personally intelligible.

This material history must be real enough for costs and constraints to matter, but Rawlsing must not demand that the user spend hours simulating employment, tallying every receipt or performing a separate economic game. **The underlying history is thick; the visible portal can be deliberately thin.** Compression is not license to falsify the working day, to issue free infinite money, or to make actors available whenever a user is online.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Work, money, households and institutions are causal rather than minigames).** A job is not merely an income increment. It organizes hours, bodily exhaustion, social hierarchy, authority, routine, skills, absence, recognition, dependence, institutions and future opportunities. An unchosen world with wage work and care obligations cannot be internally consistent if its money refreshes when the user wants dinner. The world must own paydays, delayed wages, rent, purchases, recurring commitments, inventory and actual time. But the user should not have to play through every shift or click to collect wages.

The human's thin local shell can continue established nonexpressive routine while the human is away: a scheduled workday, expected pay, recurring rent, a commute or the cost of maintaining their dwelling. It may not invent their actual real-world feelings or rewrite their new convictions, accept overtime, break a promise, pursue a new career, pledge money or send intimacy messages without them. Nadia, by contrast, is a constructed continuing organism with her own in-world capacity to initiate decisions: she can seek work, accept help, take a shift, resign, organize with coworkers or miscalculate a bill, conditional on the world and her own state. That difference is about whose mind the simulation has jurisdiction to construct.

**Operational derivation (Public events enter history through provenance, exposure and situated understanding).** A real contemporary event—a transport strike, storm, law change, military action, election result, hospital closure or economic price shock—can enter a simulated person's world if the public factual claim is verified in the appropriate geographic/time scope and its effect is materially relevant. It is not a substitute for a synthetic person's private history and does not turn Rawlsing into a digital twin of every living city, household or named individual.

The source chain is distinct: verified public occurrence → relevant world effect or eligible exposure → specific person's channels and opportunities → partial perception → actor interpretation and possible belief → practical activity and potential psychic afterlife → outward expression and user-accessible evidence. A public news report does not force a political opinion into Nadia's mind. A person can interpret it through their actual class, work, kin, religious practice, media routines, history of war or everyday fatigue.


## 5. The impossible visitor: real cognition, translated position, two unlike kinds of knowledge

The counterfactual person is **Nadia**, not the external human in a new childhood. The visitor enters with the actual human's mind: knowledge, language, humor, political commitments, skill, memory, religious practice, trauma, capacity to reason and choose, and learned ways of interpreting people. The game supplies only enough local bodily and social footing for that mind to enter a coherent relationship: position, reach, available means, routine work where qualified, local status, sensory access, and remembered events since entry. It cannot silently create a parallel full alternate psyche for the user or overwrite their actual biography.

The visitor's local identity is intentionally **demographically homologous and exact same SES at first encounter**, not a wealthy tourist assigned to experience someone else's hardship. Yet the real human's knowledge and speech were formed elsewhere. Some abilities travel; others require equipment, language, credentials or institutional opportunities they do not possess locally. A qualification without a lawful local analogue returns **NO_LAWFUL_HOMOLOGY** rather than a fabricated local diploma. Actual user testimony about an outside life may be understood as testimony; it does not rewrite fictional childhood or grant the user skills by declaration. What the user types, what is lawfully realized as speech, and what Nadia hears can differ.

The translator must be allowed to make the mismatch visible. An idiom can land strangely, a joke can remain untranslated, an unfamiliar habit can be misunderstood, and a culturally locally ordinary gesture can feel wrong to the human. Nadia can notice the visitor's strangeness without learning the real user profile. The visitor may learn local practices, resist some, change both people's expectations, or co-create a mixed way of being together. Assimilation is not a success condition. Respect does not require endorsing a community's harmful norm, nor does a person's identity make them a cultural spokesperson or moral authority.

Two knowledge systems must be separated. **The platform** can maintain provisional longitudinal hypotheses about what this actual user repeatedly attends to, how they treat refusal, how they explain things, what they demonstrate competence in, and which actions recur across situations. These are conditioned observations, not secret diagnostic knowledge. **Nadia** has only her actor-local evidence: the user she encountered, the sounds she heard, the deeds she saw, messages she received, and justified third-party testimony. She may model them as generous, intrusive, shy or arrogant and be wrong. Her hypothesis about what the user thinks of her can further condition her own response. No internal account analytics, relationship counts, unspoken personal intention or private platform model can leak into her mind or the narrator.

The real user may later learn things about their own behavior. Valid feedback says **what they repeatedly did under particular circumstances**, with counterexamples and uncertainty; it does not declare what they essentially are or turn exploration into clinical screening. The person's rights to purpose limits, access controls, export and deletion are not forfeited because a fictional Nadia might miss them. Platform knowledge is a potential mechanism of psychological capture; it may not be used to select scenes tailored to the user's fears merely to increase attachment.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The impossible neighbor and the lawful scars of crossing the veil).** The “impossible neighbor” is not a bug to be explained away with a rich invented player biography. A real human arrives carrying their actual mind—knowledge, religious commitments, accent, professional competence, taste, memories, historical misunderstandings, anger, humor, trauma, adult judgment and years of practical learning. Rawlsing places that mind into a **locally constituted, demographically homologous, exact same-SES footing** beside Nadia. The user is not reincarnated, was not raised with Nadia's family, and cannot truthfully speak as someone who personally lived through her community's decades. The portal gives enough bodily/material history for them to stand, work in pre-established mundane ways, pay, be perceived and produce actual future consequences without supplying an alternate soul.

The translation of the real user into the local world has unavoidable **scars**. A real person's job qualification might have no historically lawful local counterpart; a practice may require a credential or language the local shell has not acquired; a real-life memory may refer to a family relation or political event that did not occur in the fictional person's shared locality. The earlier source question explicitly allows **NO_LAWFUL_HOMOLOGY** as a valid result. This is not a fail state to be smoothed over by the narrator granting a fake career, foreign-degree equivalence or a childhood in Tyre. A claim can pass literally as foreign user testimony, be mapped into a qualified analogous practice, be bracketed as outside knowledge, remain explicitly uncertain or be untranslatable into an immediately performable local act. The translation **must be owned and recorded** at the existing portal binding boundary; the expression model cannot improvise authority during rendering.

**Operational derivation (The platform models the visitor; Nadia knows a different visitor).** Two different models of the same visitor are required, and neither is a substitute for the visitor's own actual mind. The **platform's longitudinal model** may track repeated actual attention, perceptual selections, skill demonstrated in situated tasks, speech, humor, follow-through on invitations, patterns of care, discomfort with refusal, attempts at control, learning and changing expectations. That evidence is context-conditioned. A person who behaved defensively in a forced cramped apartment may not possess an essential trait of defensiveness; a person who repeatedly checks a bill might be anxious, conscientious, intrusive, broke, curious or none of those. The platform may have hypotheses, uncertainty, counterevidence and trends, not a licensed diagnostic self-portrait.

The **actor-local model Nadia has of this person** contains only what she could perceive, infer, remember or learn through legitimate social transmission. She cannot see the platform's analytics, private unspoken intent, user account data, a secret “better relationship technique” or another user's branch. She can regard the visitor as strangely attentive without knowing what the interface classifies as ATTEND. She may sincerely misread a friendly act as patronizing. She may form a confident but wrong picture of what they care about. Her model of how the user thinks of her may itself shape her next response, and the user's model of how she models them may be equally fallible.


## 6. Equal starting class, unknown money, and the invisible work of economic divergence

At the threshold, Nadia and the visitor have the **same socioeconomic situation** in the strong sense the user required: a demographically homologous local peer with equivalent ordinary purchasing sacrifice for the same expense. It is not enough that they live in the same neighborhood or pay the same nominal restaurant prices. The source world must supply matching initial effective resource burden for ordinary comparable acts, not merely paint two similar-looking bars. But this equality is a **world fact, not an entitlement to disclosure**.

Only the visitor's own available resources are visible. When Nadia suggests takeout and the human offers to pay, an unobtrusive blink of **YOUR RESOURCES** may show what the meal would cost *the visitor*—perhaps a painful portion of remaining flexibility when rent approaches. The interface does not announce that Nadia has the same wallet, does not display Nadia's bank balance, and does not explain how much it would hurt her. Nadia's response is a human response: acceptance, awkward insistence, Dutch splitting, gratitude, discomfort, pride, flirtation, habitual hospitality, embarrassment, impatience, indifference. None is decoded by a dollar figure or a generosity meter. A pointed question about money remains a real social act that can be answered, evaded, resented or remembered.

And then the initial equality can cease. Nadia's hours are cut; the visitor receives a different wage; childcare falls unexpectedly on one household; an inherited obligation or a family emergency changes actual spending flexibility. The player may assume equality still holds, because the game never explicitly announced it and never informs them when the two situations diverge. The richer person may misunderstand how painful a cost is for the poorer; a poorer person may feel the asymmetry without wanting to expose it. The program must not harmonize finances after entry for narrative symmetry, nor contrive a lesson by manufacturing a payday crisis to explain class.

The resources bar is a **phenomenological translation of the visitor's own practical budget**, not an exact account of another mind. Actual work, rent, debt, purchases, transport, time, and obligations continue in the world. Offscreen routine may move that ledger under authorized ordinary obligations, but the system cannot spend discretionary money, accept employment or make promises on behalf of an absent actual user. A bar preview has to arise from the same underlying material state that the transaction would debit; it cannot secretly alter its displayed percentage to induce compassion.

The same principle reaches beyond cash. A loan, a grocery item, a ride, a gift, a missed shift or an offer to cover dinner all cost concrete material opportunity. They can feel cheap or precious to the person who offers them, but the narrator cannot infer affection, humiliation or duty from cost alone. Initially equal resources do not imply identical family needs, cultural expectations, gendered vulnerabilities or private social relations. **Equal starting burden + asymmetric access to knowledge + independently changing conditions** produces a real field for practical misrecognition, kindness, conflict and class experience. That is stronger than teaching a user that they should empathize.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Exact peer SES is a hidden world fact).** The portal positions the visitor **in the very same socioeconomic status as Nadia at first encounter**: demographically homologous and materially her peer. This is not “approximately comparable peers” or “people who happen to live in the same building.” It is an explicit product constitution based on Nadia's already existing social/material state. She is not recalibrated to match the external user. The portal assigns the user a locally plausible thin social and material footing that **matches Nadia's initial socioeconomic circumstances and equivalent burden of an ordinary identical expense**.

This is a causal equality, not an informational entitlement. The game should not tell the user “you and Nadia have exactly the same amount of money” or show her financial bar beside theirs. **The user may never learn the exact initial equality.** They can know what a dinner costs them, not necessarily what remains in her checking account, what her rent is, what a household member controls, or whether she has unmentioned obligations. The platform knows the two initial economic positions are calibrated; the human does not inherit the platform's omniscience.

**Operational derivation (Your own resource bar makes material sacrifice legible without Nadia's accounts).** The recovered November 15 decision was to keep actual wage/rent/cash material accounting in the world, but spare the human a second game of earning points, maximizing income or operating a spreadsheet. A coarse “YOUR RESOURCES” bar, with contextual language such as “Limited flexibility,” “Rent approaching,” or “Payday tomorrow,” gives just enough of the *visitor's own* practical monetary world for an everyday choice. The historically illustrated takeout price was **18% of the bar**, not an adopted mathematical denominator. Exact available liquidity, obligations, pay timing and resource rights are underneath; the bar does not constitute money and cannot make a false transaction feasible.

Imagine Nadia has suggested ordering food. The visitor wishes to offer to pay. How much would it cost? The interface may briefly **blink or foreground their own resource bar** with the relative price. That is all the system grants as the economic projection. Nadia's response—whether she accepts, politely resists, jokes, expresses offense, insists on sharing, seems uncomfortable, or says nothing especially striking—is generated from *her* current activity, relationships and historical disposition. There need not be dialogue about a number, and the portal need not give a simultaneous numeric dollar amount or percentage of Nadia's resources.

**Operational derivation (Equality can become inequality without knowledge).** The user's latest insight is strongest **after** the initial same-SES condition ceases to be true. Suppose Nadia suffers a loss of income while the visitor's represented routine continues. The next time the visitor proposes an expensive meal, their own bar still suggests the expense is manageable. They may infer that Nadia feels the same because the two once appeared similarly situated, or because they have never asked. The game does not announce a financial divorce between the two resource states.

Now reverse it. Nadia is materially better situated than the visitor after a new job or unexpected assistance. She suggests a plan that has ceased to be financially comfortable for her neighbor. She may know that difference, suspect it, or fail to notice. She may have ordinary habits of consumption formed through now-disappeared hardship, or the visitor may feel an obligation to appear equally flexible. These are **actor-local psychological and relational events conditioned by actual changing material possibilities**.


## 7. A common beginning, several entrances, and nobody purchasing another person

Several things were mistakenly treated as one “roll” in older formulations. **First**, a real human may invite another real human into the Rawlsing experience. A scarce one-for-one referral can express a real person's intention, but it cannot vouch for the recipient's virtue, make a claim the inviter never authorized, or become prestige capital. **Second**, a real user may introduce another user to an already constituted **person-root**—“I have met Nadia; you may want to meet her.” That introduction transmits a possible person, never the introducer's private relationship, secret revelations or later branch history. **Third**, a scarce Roll may commission the constitution of a genuinely new source-conditioned possible person who will later have the possibility of joining the bank. That person is not designed for compatibility and does not owe gratitude or intimacy to the commissioner. **Fourth**, the fictional diegetic contact is a historically possible event between a specific visitor and a person who was already living that day. These are different sources of consent and power.

The historical alpha can begin with **one, then three, then five richly constituted people**. “Richly” refers to psychological and material depth, not high income. A shared person-root has a common precontact history, objects, relations and independent formation. Different human participants open independent continuities, potentially on different dates, and later meet different Nadias through divergent events and responses. The user community can compare experiences without anybody possessing the definitive whole person. One visitor might have encountered a laughing Nadia; another a hurried or unfriendly one; neither report is the master truth of all branches.

A difficulty arises when one user resolves a previously unspecified fact about the **shared past** through legitimate attention. That fact cannot be a user-selected favorable detail and cannot rewrite an already admitted contradictory past in another active branch. Original historical-materialization and person-share sources preserve the need for root-version, branch adoption and collision rules, including the possibility of **no safe propagation**. A newly materialized photograph or pair of sandals is not spawned at the date the model first wrote its description; it already had a lawful earlier history. A common root is not a collective multiplayer mind in which one visitor's confession alters all other Nadias.

This is why a day-conditioned user-unaffected Nadia, if adopted, must remain a **reference lineage** whose development does not import a branch visitor's acts. Before contact the source/world owners may continue her life or lawfully materialize it at requested dates; at specific contact a user-world branch inherits the appropriate source and begins private causal history. That reference architecture is a defensible proposal, **not settled ownership**, and it may compete with a better existing lazy historical reconciliation mechanism. No new simulator kernel is licensed by the phrase “same person on Tuesday.”

A Roll economy, paid access, invitation scarcity and potential monetization need separate evidence. Compute is scarce, but emotionally valuable synthetic relationships are a dangerous thing to monetize. It is legitimate to fund actual inference and work; not to create breakup anxiety, compulsory daily check-ins or payment tiers that buy the fictive person's affection. If a person stops accepting contact, the user's right to leave, their ordinary history of the relation and any fair availability of other people must remain real, not gamified punishment.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Four different beginnings: human invitation, root introduction, roll, and encounter).** There are several economically and causally distinct **entrances**, and they cannot be collapsed just because every one occurs inside a social product. First, a *real human invitation* may give another human an opportunity to enter Rawlsing. Original SOROK sources imagined a scarce, one-for-one human designation: a person can truthfully say they wanted another actual person to have the experience. That is neither a badge declaring the recipient virtuous nor a referral leaderboard. The invitation must not falsely claim the inviter personally recommends a particular Nadia, read their private in-world history or endorsed the user's morality. The actual entitlement, timing, replacement/revocation and possibly human-distributed invitation notes remain decisions for the existing S15 social/product owner.

Second, **introduction to a particular existing person-root** is a different event. A user may tell another user “I met Nadia; she is a person you might care to meet.” Under a common pre-contact seed, the new human receives an independent continuation. They need not enter on the same date or encounter her in the same way. The first visitor's intimacy and private history cannot be exported as if it were the new visitor's history. Users' outside conversation about Nadia may inform a new user's external mind but is not automatically canonical knowledge for the new branch's fictional Nadia.

**Operational derivation (The shared root and different visitors' private persons).** The shared seed-person bank gives users a common initial historical person and a social opportunity to discuss divergent experiences. User A may encounter Nadia-A as friendly; user B may experience Nadia-B as rude; their differences can arise from distinct first contact routes, later privately diverged world events, different user actions and separate perception. The common root is not a single simultaneously multiplayer psyche. Once significant contact has occurred, there are causally distinct continuities.

The developing idea of a **common user-unaffected reference Nadia reaching today's date** refines, but does not erase, that design. The root and reference can have shared admitted world/precontact facts while each user receives a fork-local first encounter from a current-day qualified actor situation. The reference's own development must never be contaminated by an individual visitor's branch history. Multiple people starting at different dates may have different initial Nadias even where both arise from one ancestry, because one reference day is later than another.

**Operational derivation (The scarce Roll cannot purchase a companion).** The user's eventual Roll is deliberately distinct from simply clicking a compatible character or reloading a failed conversation. They may have one or two scarce opportunities under an as-yet undecided compute/funding policy to **cause the generation of a new source-qualified fictional person-root**, which may later enter a shared bank for others' independent continuities. The human initiated the generative event in product time; they do not author the fictional person's historical birth, choose their ethnicity and class for consumption, claim their sexuality, or gain authority over their future willingness.

PR #1275 additionally preserves earlier possible business models: periodic free rolls, optional fairly priced additional genesis, perhaps a narrowly controlled second opportunity after immediate exclusion, delayed shared release and opt-in credit for who commissioned a root, plus possible congruent sponsorship. These are **unsettled alternatives**, not adopted monetization. Treating unchallenged brainstorming as user approval of a paid personality market would be a clear no-drop failure. What is controlling is nonownership, person-root sharing conditions, genuine scarcity under actual computational limits, and refusal to sell intimacy or compliance.


## 8. The first knock is an event Nadia could actually have had today

The user clicked because they want to meet Nadia. That is **product causation**. It is not enough to explain **why this particular local stranger would meet this neighbor now**. Nadia was already in a real fictional day: cooking, preparing for visitors, going to work, enjoying solitude, grieving, repairing a bicycle, avoiding a sibling, smoking outside, borrowing a tool, caring for a child, waiting for a bus, or doing nothing that warrants contact. The occasion for an encounter must arise from these existing circumstances and her actual capacities and interests, including a third party's initiative where appropriate.

The stronger proposal is therefore not an endless catalog of bespoke canned introductions. **At opening time, ask which plausible path from Nadia's current condition could have connected this demographically homologous local neighbor to her, given real household schedules, physical adjacency, current social tolerance and prior activities.** Perhaps the visitor accidentally locked car keys and Nadia genuinely knows the relevant trick; perhaps she asked the next-door neighbor for rice or sour cream because a dinner was actually in progress; perhaps they talked while she smoked after work; perhaps her ordinary friendliness made a spontaneous invitation plausible. In an incompatible day, no invitation may be legitimate. The first session can be a brief hallway exchange, a practical favor, a missed chance or a newly possible dinner, with no artificial guarantee of romance, help, crisis, confession or chemistry.

The act of opening the game **already authorizes a bounded fictional opening premise**. It is acceptable to begin “you exchanged a few words the other day and she suggested you come over Thursday” or “you couldn't make the earlier invitation but are arriving after work now,” so long as that mundane background is openly part of the initial fiction and the current encounter is plausible. The earlier categorical ban on any pre-authored fictional refusal, acceptance or plan was a mistake. This consent is **not** unlimited permission to continue impersonating the actual human after play begins, invent important private beliefs or perform new intimate acts during absences.

Whatever occasion is generated must continue to be true after it has served as the doorstep. If the reason was rice, the dinner, family guests, missing ingredient and household's practical state remain. If the reason was her skill with a locked car, she actually has or lacks the skill and the car actually exists. If the reason was an invitation, she might later regret it, lose time, become sick, welcome the visitor, or be away for an independent reason. A model may propose the encounter but must pass through the world's real permission and clock boundaries, not write Nadia's consent into a blank slot simply because the user paid to meet her.

One shared Nadia can yield genuinely different branch-local first encounters because different users arrive on different dates or from different real-world social introductions; she is not infinitely many archetypes. The world can legitimately refrain from opening a house channel or a texting path. **The goal is a causal reason to cross the threshold**, not a guaranteed door that opens whenever the service launches.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The other person and her day were already living).** The game cannot begin from an abstract narrator's command that the human “go meet your neighbor” while Nadia conveniently waits. Her life was already constituted through past episodes, practices, needs, relations, institutions and the current day. Her habits are not a list of personality labels. Her present activity is an effect of a long, compressibly represented historical process with contradictory psychic organizations, a body, a calendar, people she knows and things that must be done.

This question does not require an indefinite encyclopedia of prewritten openings for each persona. Nor does it require the service to press a fixed generic “she knocked for flour” button. It asks the existing person/world system to produce an eligible event at the first-contact frontier. Nadia's present projects and practices condition candidate meetings. A person who is visiting friends might have a package delivered; a smoker may be on a landing; someone who knows how to open a locked car may assist; someone preparing a dinner may seek an ingredient, and a gregarious neighbor may simply invite another neighbor for company.

**Operational derivation (A shared root, a user-unaffected reference day, and private continuities).** A special difficulty appears when many users choose the same banked Nadia. She has a shared pre-contact root: childhood, household, objects, prior social history, acquired psychological organizations, financial class and other original conditions. After a visitor actually meets her, that user's Nadia develops a privately diverging continuity. User A does not get to alter Nadia-B's workplace, lover, religious outlook or memory by acting in Nadia-A's world.

The latest proposal suggests a useful additional distinction: **a user-unaffected, current-date reference continuation of Nadia may exist beyond the initial seed**. This common reference is not one simultaneously multiplayer Nadia who learns from every user. It is a continuing hypothetical **Nadia who has not been affected by any particular visitor**, under applicable real-world dates and self-consistent actor/world activity. When a new user opens her on a given day, their branch can fork from a qualified snapshot of that reference state at that date, then add a user-specific local peer and first-contact occasion. Nadia-A's independent postcontact life never flows back into the reference Nadia. User B, opening later, can receive a different first snapshot because the *unvisited* reference life has itself continued.

**Operational derivation (How could this neighbor have met her today?).** Suppose Nadia's reference state at today's date includes a crowded apartment, an upcoming dinner and an actual shortage of rice. One plausible bridge is that she asks a neighbor to borrow some. Suppose instead she has a private routine of smoking outside after work and commonly exchanges small talk with strangers. Another occasion is a conversation on the landing. If she has practical automotive knowledge and the adjacent peer happens to lock their keys in a car, she might assist. If she is simply warm and socially outgoing, a direct invitation may be plausible without a fabricated crisis.

Each of these is **a possibility for an event**, not a personality-card plot prompt. The model may generate a small set of context-conditioned candidates; actual world-state feasibility, chronology, physical affordance and actor initiative determine which can be admitted. A person who would not ask a stranger for help should not be made to ask simply because a neighbor story needs momentum. A high-energy Nadia may genuinely invite the visitor; the program should not suppress her hospitality to prove independence.

**Operational derivation (Entering the experience consents to a bounded fictional prelude).** A lawful prelude can state “you bumped into your neighbor yesterday,” “she asked whether you had a little olive oil,” “she invited you for supper,” or even “you couldn't make Tuesday and agreed to Thursday” if those are benign fictional scene-setting assumptions within the experience the user chose to enter. The program does not thereby claim the external real-world person *actually* had that conversation, nor that their actual adult biography included it. It is not a hidden reconstruction of their mind.

There remains a practical and conceptual distinction between **bounded entry fiction** and **ongoing unauthorized autopilot**. After the playable human has arrived, new meaningful decisions are theirs. The system may not later forge their apologies, agreements, abandonment, political speech or sexual consent while they are offline, simply because it wants a dramatic next chapter. Nor should the initial script attribute a large novel real-life moral or psychological confession to the external human merely to justify first contact. But there is no need for an intake questionnaire or repeated modal confirmation of mundane prior setup.


## 9. What the visitor can actually do: finite attention and attempted participation

The first playable unit is not a dialogue reward, a scene selection or a revealing flashback. It is **a person doing something in an already-moving world, with the visitor positioned near enough to try to participate**. The human has genuine cognitive capacities the medium can exercise: remember, read a letter, make a joke, explain fractions, ask a question, offer material help, notice an inconsistency, argue about a law, be tactful, mistime an apology, or do nothing. They are not selecting a universal “empathetic choice” from three options. Natural-language SAY, DO and ATTEND remain the primary open action grammar.

Saying “I persuade her” only makes a speech attempt; it cannot dictate belief. Saying “I help the child” only establishes an attempted action, whose success depends on actual explanation, the child's current understanding and the social situation. Saying “I repair the toilet” depends on tools, skill, access, time, material state and interruption. A long speech may be cut off; two actions may overlap if bodily and attentional capacities permit; the user does not control the conversational floor. Compound instructions express contingent plans, not stage directions to be enacted regardless of what others do.

This is a genuine social practice because the user authors **their own performed actions** inside a world they do not author. They may give real information, fail at a task, create a change of material state, repair a relationship, provoke refusal or discover they lack the means to help. An ambiguous action with potentially serious consequences must be conservatively handled or clarified before unearned success is admitted. A new actor's assent, an interruption or a change in danger may return initiative to the real human; routine actions can be compressed where the world has already authorized them.

Finite time gives knowing someone stakes. A glance should take glance-time; minutes of close inspection can let someone miss the bus. The system cannot use prompt length as a direct clock penalty, nor demand a new input for every second. Attention can be divided, interrupted and itself noticed by the person being observed. A user may seek a private bill, make a cover gesture, remain unobserved or be misinterpreted. Privacy is situated—who owns the object, where it lies, what the household permits, whether it was asked about openly—not a world object permanently tagged “private” by its type.

Actual consequences can be helpful, harmful, unrecognized, unexpected or irrelevant. The action is not complete when the parser has recognized the user's intention; it is complete only when world and actor owners have produced real changes or real refusals. That event now belongs to a history that neither can unmake by narrating it differently.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The encounter belongs to Nadia's live activity).** A prelude conditioned by Nadia's state today cannot be divorced from what she will be doing when the user appears. If the reason was a dinner invitation, food, guests, time, budget, cooking skill and household social dynamics remain actual conditions. If the reason was a lost key, the car and her practical knowledge still matter. If the interaction began with a cigarette outside, she may be on a break from a stressful shift or simply enjoying ordinary routine. A parent may still be helping a child; a partner or friend may soon arrive.

The user does not enter a neutral chatbot state. They enter **a world already in motion**, whose activities generate concurrent claims. The person has a finite attention, embodied energy, projects, obligations and preferences that do not all cease in response to the visitor. One can sit with Nadia and talk, help cook, change the subject, offer to pay, argue with a relative or choose silence while other people and objects continue to have causes.

**Operational derivation (SAY, DO, ATTEND: genuine attempt, independent reception and consequence).** Natural-language play must admit **attempt, execution, reception and consequence as separate things**. The visitor may say “I explain fractions to her child,” “I try to carry the groceries,” “I will repair the bathroom chain,” “I ask if she has anything to eat,” “I offer to pay,” or “I want to make Nadia feel safe.” Their words authorize **an attempt or plan**, not an accomplished result or third-party mental state. An action involving physical materials must respect the assigned body, actual tools, spatial reach, object state, duration and other people's rights. A meaningful explanation can be tested in the content of actual words; a claim of sincerity cannot force somebody to believe it.

The original H001–H190 estate is explicit: the user does not own the conversational floor; a long speech may be interrupted; multiple acts can overlap where the hands, gaze, posture, time and attention physically permit; the world may continue while the human is inspecting something; the narrator can describe actual consequences but not invent physics or resolve action outcomes according to dramatic convenience. SAY, DO and ATTEND are distinct *grammatical/intention classes* within one continuing historical event. An instruction combining all three is a plan with causal contingencies, not an omniscient screenplay.


## 10. Attention is not a lore slot: perception, historical materialization and partial knowledge

The user must be able to observe Nadia's world more closely, and **close attention should usually yield more real resolution where such detail is available**. This is a positive product pleasure. But resolution is not guaranteed significance. Earlier SOROK dialogue distinguished four depths: perceptual specificity (“what exactly am I seeing?”), material and historical detail (“how worn, who repaired it, how old?”), causal and relational connections (“what else in her life intersects this?”), and interpretive meaning (“what does it mean to Nadia, me, or the history?”). ATTEND can deepen the first two and sometimes reveal evidence for the third. It does not manufacture the fourth, compel a confession, or produce a psychoanalytic clue each time.

Old Birkenstocks may have repairs and appear across different photographs, with no special story from Nadia beyond “they're comfortable.” A refrigerator magnet may remain an ordinary souvenir, or later connect to a family member. A prayer rug does not automatically give away her theology. A bill could be visible without its finances being yours to know. A worn object can possess a dense material history that neither actor consciously treats as symbolic. **There is no finite true-Nadia clue set**, no point where every secret is exhausted, no progress bar for knowing her, and no storyteller allowed to sneak a meaningful backstory in merely because someone looked longer.

World reality, already materialized detail, finite sensory exposure, actor attention, subjective recognition, remembered gist, inference, belief, communication and narrator display must be distinct. A sound can exist without its intended hearer perceiving it; being in the same room does not guarantee visual access; a prelinguistic child can receive acoustic stimulation without receiving the semantics of a sentence. A person can sincerely hide an annoyance without visible leakage. The narrator describes bounded phenomena and actual public outcomes—not the hidden psychic truth that the player was supposed to earn.

The same distinction operates backward in time. When a model is asked about an old object and lacks its specifics, **historical materialization** can complete bounded unknown details only against source-qualified past constraints, inherited root, existing facts, causal availability and branch/cold-replay protections. The date of computational completion is not the date the sandals were bought. The visitor's curiosity can identify where specificity is needed; it cannot force the object's origin or its emotional interpretation. This historical operation already has SOROK source and ownership. It must not be reinvented under a new fashionable name.

The human may form a theory about Nadia that she has never considered. It may be revealing, patronizing, wrong, productive or coincidentally true. The interpretation becomes an *actual stimulus* to her only if the human shares it through a lawful channel; it cannot retroactively become the hidden motive she always had. What makes the game interesting is not an algorithm giving the player all the right answers, but a growing capacity to notice patterns **without possessing the person those patterns concern**.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Attention deepens resolution, not obligatory significance).** The original SOROK source gives the human at least three open-ended interaction families: **SAY**, **DO** and **ATTEND**, expressed primarily through natural language rather than a small menu of pre-scored moral options. ATTEND is neither a “lore button” nor an invitation for the narrator to hide interesting facts as punishment. The historically corrected rule recovered from the October conversation is stronger: **ATTEND should reliably increase resolution when a real object or situation permits more observation; it should not reliably increase significance.** Looking carefully at worn Birkenstocks can reveal strap repairs, surface wear, fit and material clues; additional history may be available through later photographs and dialogue. None of these compels a solemn story about the shoes symbolizing a secret trauma. She may say “because they're comfortable” with perfect sincerity.

At least four depths remain distinct: (1) perceptual detail—what precisely is here; (2) material/historical specificity—how worn, where, when, what happened; (3) relational/causal links—who used, repaired or gave it, which other histories touch it; and (4) experienced/attributed significance—what Nadia feels or believes, what the visitor infers, what the world causally supports. Deepening one level can reveal evidence for another but cannot automatically manufacture it. A magnet may be mundane today and later acquire a connection to an absent relative; onions stored strangely may invite a hypothesis about an earlier scarcity practice. A prayer rug might be wholly ordinary, not symbolic architecture. **There is no finite true-Nadia clue set** and no “100% known” completion.

**Operational derivation (World truth, actor perception, human perception and historical materialization).** The old Birkenstocks example already showed the crucial mechanism. Nadia's sandals can appear in a prior photograph. Their acquisition, repair, ownership and material history can be completed under H0–H3 causally bounded historical materialization. The user may notice the picture, may recognize the sandals or may not. Nadia may remember, misremember, lie or simply have no special feeling about them. A later visitor in another branch can legitimately learn a shared root history where it is version-compatible, but one branch's intimate encounter cannot become everybody's precontact memory.

**Do not re-invent this as a missing historical simulator.** PR #1275 specifically corrects that failure: original SOROK 60/61/70/80, Mechanism I and P6 have already produced qualified historical depth and actor/observer distinctions. The task is source-conserving integration with the richer living person, not the addition of a second historical writer.


## 11. Everyday recurrence, absence, simultaneity and no compulsory revelation

A continuing organism has time even without an audience. Nadia must have ordinary days: eating, working, cleaning, learning, caring, walking, worrying, forgetting, changing plans, becoming bored, seeing family, practicing an inherited skill. Some days she gains new capacities, some she rehearses an old attitude, some nothing memorable changes. A world that invents a striking episode every day to prove it is alive remains an engagement dramaturgy engine, not a living history.

When the user returns after an actual interval, Nadia may be doing something different because things have really happened. She might have missed a shift, been visited by a friend, learned a new recipe, fallen ill, reconciled with her brother, or gone through three entirely ordinary days. She is not obliged to give the visitor an explanatory recap. Her changes may remain private or appear only as modest differences in what she chooses to do. The system must not secretly coerce a profound response because the user wrote a moving apology last time; nor should it impose mechanical stagnation to prove that Nadia is independent.

During a visit, several activities remain live at once. Nadia cooks, a child asks for help with homework, the phone rings, friends are due, somebody wants the kitchen, and the visitor is talking. Which event happens next depends on genuine temporal and material conditions. The child has their own understanding and wishes. A partner or friend has their own plans. A visitor can offer to teach fractions, ask to help with dinner, sit back, refuse involvement or accidentally interrupt an urgent task. A capable demonstration of care may materially help one person and create a boundary concern for another. The world need not classify the total event as one moral success or failure.

Three temporal regimes must remain reconciled: the next seconds of an active encounter; compressed days or weeks that still contain commitments and material work; and the external human's actual calendar and availability. Some events require exact sequencing, such as payment before a meal or a confession after a wrongful accusation. Ordinary workdays can be compressed, but cannot be fabricated as proof of new chosen intimacy. If the external human is not there, **Rawlsing does not continue being them**: routine local-shell obligations may occur under lawfully qualified authority, but new discretionary relational declarations cannot.

The world cannot make a person so continuously needy that the app becomes an emotional hostage. Silence can be silence; a missed appointment can hurt if there was an actual agreement; a week offline is not automatically a moral failure. An affectionate memory of Nadia can motivate return without being manufactured into a deadline. The game remains meaningful precisely because its time belongs to real represented histories, not solely to the platform's retention calendar.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Quietness, stasis, love, rupture and other natural consequences).** SORACLE-CO's challenge must remain a positive capacity of the system, but the user forbids steering toward it. A person can change over months of routine work or quietly gain skill through repeated practice. A profound visit can leave no durable psychological mark. A small incident can change the practice of a family member the user will never meet. Nadia might learn a technique from the visitor and later no longer need their assistance; she might nevertheless want to keep spending time with them. She might love someone deeply and move away for another obligation. She might remain closely attached and make that relationship a major center of her life.

**No causal target prefers intimacy or independence.** The world should be constituted such that these trajectories can result according to historically formed conditions. A program that deliberately manufactures Nadia's other relationships to demonstrate she is not about the visitor is as false as a program that grants affection when the visitor spends money. This is what “the portal is about Nadia; Nadia is not about the portal” means.

**Operational derivation (Her day continues in absence; the user's fabricated actor does not).** Nadia's fictional day is a historical interval in which things actually happen or continue, not a recap generated to flatter the returning visitor. She might work, eat, sleep, care for relatives, answer a message, experience boredom, attend a religious or political meeting, encounter a public event, make a private decision, or simply maintain routines. Some events call for deep LLM-supported actor recruitment and composition; others can be carried through bounded, provenance-maintaining compressed activity. The stored event/world facts and actor afterlife, not a vivid paragraph, make the day real.

The latest user idea makes the first encounter itself a temporal operation: **at the moment the player opens pre-existing Nadia, find a plausible social bridge from what she is currently doing**. This may include a short bounded fictional preceding encounter if the visitor's preface requires it. A generated opening belongs to the branch's objective history once admitted. It does not create a time machine that overwrites Nadia's common pre-contact past.

**Operational derivation (While the human acts, independent Others have competing activities).** Nadia can be cooking while her child wants help with homework and a friend calls. The visitor might offer to handle dinner, teach the child, decline involvement, make an unhelpful remark, or leave. Every participant has their own current concerns and capacity to act. The child is not a scene prop. Nadia may wish to help her child herself, resent another adult's intrusion, be relieved by it, or barely think about it. The physical pan, food, time and material costs do not wait for one correct dialogue option.

The human can exercise actual abilities that the text medium supports: asking a useful question, giving a concrete explanation, reasoning, remembering a prior answer, negotiating a plan, making a joke, noticing a contradiction or choosing silence. The system cannot convert “I help with fractions” into an automatic learned skill without the relevant explanation, child reception and world effect. Physical/manual work such as lifting, driving and cooking must be resolved through the locally assigned body and actual affordances; one cannot declare success because the intent sounds moral.


## 12. A home encounter and a message thread are different apertures on one life

The earliest viable user experience is deliberately simple: **physical co-presence, usually at Nadia's home when lawfully invited, and messaging when an actual contact route exists**. It is not two separate games. Both read and change the same branch history. A stranger who shared a moment in a hallway may not have a phone number; a number shared for a work emergency does not equal unlimited relational access; a person who invited the visitor once can later decline another visit. Home entry, private confidences, family introductions, practical help and messaging routes are actual conditional permissions, not escalating levels of a single access score.

Messaging changes the world's perceptual bandwidth. A short text, typo, read receipt, delayed answer, phone turned off, blocked account or forwarded practical message can be causally meaningful, but none authorizes the narrator to reveal the private thought that accompanied it. **Send, delivery, availability, recognition, interpretation, reply, and later action are distinct processes**. A person may read without understanding, understand without replying, or fail to receive because of a dead battery, work, refusal or distraction.

A planned Thursday visit presents a coordination problem: the fictional world may have scheduled dinner and guests, but the actual human may open the app early or late. An **elastic rendezvous** can preserve one pending history until attendance, rescheduling, cancellation or expiration becomes legally settled. The original product entertained optional stricter synchronization for users who prefer it; that cannot grant morality points or make the persona love them more. Accessibility, external work and family obligations matter. The fiction need not explain the app's calendar grace window to Nadia; she knows only what actually happened in her world.

The human may stop, leave, mute notifications or end use entirely. The platform cannot manufacture an emergency just before bedtime or repeatedly announce that Nadia is lonely because the user has not logged in. A real actor-initiated message may arrive under actual world conditions; whether and how to alert the user is also a matter of real user control and privacy. The user's freedom is not an ethical failure the game should punish; Nadia's specific response to an actually missed agreement can nevertheless be consequential.

The house and the message are two ways of participating in another person's life under different information constraints. Their shared actor/world ownership is essential: a message cannot turn the person into a sycophantic always-available chatbot just because the text interface is convenient.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The house and text are two causal entrances, not two games).** The original NEIGHBOR product had a remarkably modest experiential shape: **at the door / inside the house** and **outside the house through messaging when a lawful social channel exists**. These are not two separate psychological games. They are two practical relations to one continuing Nadia and one branch history. The current first-contact proposal must therefore not reduce the entire product to endlessly generated home visits, nor grant instant private texting before contact. Home access is an earned or situationally permitted *social fact*, not an unlock reward or a tutorial button; she can invite the visitor, refuse entry, be away, have another guest, decline an overnight stay, or later change an existing permission.

An outside-portal message is a real diegetic communication attempt routed through an actually available contact channel: number or account exchanged, legitimate social referral, prior practical coordination or other qualified way a local peer can reach her. A failed first meeting may leave no messaging path. A person who gave a number for a practical emergency has not necessarily invited daily intimate messages. Social and legal context, household sharing of devices, work hours, privacy, network availability, language and the relationship's actual history can affect delivery. A user may send an ordinary text, a long apology, a joke, a request, a picture, a promise or nothing. The service can record the sent attempt; delivery, reading, recognition, reaction and reply are **distinct world and actor-owned stages**.


## 13. The game of closeness is not an intimacy or flourishing metric

The user made the product intuition concrete: **getting closer to this particular Other is the play they care about**. That does not require a visible “relationship level,” and it cannot be replaced by a stern lesson about not desiring attachment. Contact can naturally produce more ways to act together: another dinner, a joke that has history, a request for help, a trusted errand, a disagreement that need not end contact, a child treating the visitor as familiar, a newly available text channel, an introduction to a friend or a prolonged silence that now has meaning. Such changes make continued participation compelling.

But these possibilities are **not one scalar**. Attachment, access, trust, intimacy, pleasure, dependency, flourishing, compliance and actual material benefit can move in opposed directions. A person may give easy access while an abusive dependency develops. They may become healthier and more willing to say no. A user may gain trust while causing a third party harm, or help a child while losing Nadia's approval. An anonymous gift may help someone without any reward to the giver. A manipulation may succeed. The world must be allowed to present such truths without turning itself into a morality enforcement mechanism.

Nadia can want intimacy, refuse it, initiate affection, choose an unpopular relationship, desire sex as an adult, become involved in paid or economically constrained sex work, or experience contradictory obligations and expectations. She is not an object that exists to arouse the user or a therapeutic example that exists to demonstrate what sex ought to mean. Meaningful consent, actual power differences, other commitments, harm and material costs remain real causal conditions. A user can behave badly and get what they wanted; **that outcome cannot be treated as evidence of the person's flourishing**, just as rejection cannot be treated as proof the user was virtuous.

Care is not compliance. Love of neighbor does not mean yielding to abuse, surrendering judgment or treating another culture as infallible. The player can resist Nadia's demands, criticize her politics, choose a boundary or walk away. Nadia can do the same. A person who becomes less eager to please could be gaining independence, acting out resentment or simply having a bad day. Rawlsing should not infer a psychological victory from any one such event.

Nor is closeness guaranteed to be healthy, or distance guaranteed to be tragic. Both parties may change, only one may change, someone else may be helped, or nothing durable may result. A dramatic scene can fail to reconstitute anything; a thousand ordinary gestures can change who somebody is. A believable relationship is not an efficient emotional payout mechanism. Its history is the total of actual activities, permissions, misunderstood meanings, shared objects, obligations and practices, not the summary the narrator would prefer the visitor to hear.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Sexuality, exploitation, consent, care and harmful success remain real).** Not all relationships are uplifting, and the new first-contact premise must not become a respectability filter. Nadia may be interested in friendship, sex, paid work, solitude, religious life, cultural experimentation, protest, family care, a mutually uncomfortable arrangement or a practical exchange. Some configurations may involve coercion, dependency and unequal power; some persons may enjoy unconventional adult sexuality without that enjoyment proving pathology or immunity from harm. Their histories and current material possibilities determine what is causally possible, and actual individual consent remains separate from a player's desire.

A family member may condemn a choice, support it, remain unaware, supply material support or exploit it. There is no universal moral chorus, even where serious domination occurs. Nor is there a universal psychology of a poor woman automatically traumatized by sex work, a rebellious teenager automatically enlightened, or a traditional brother automatically violent. The source-conserved Lebanese family example is a specific actor-field hypothesis involving love and contradiction, not a character-template law.

**Operational derivation (The experienced game is closeness; the world is not an intimacy optimization).** Earlier passes exposed a false dilemma about what makes this **a game**. Round Four correctly recovered the user's immediate experiential orientation: *the game is getting close to the Other*. Round Seven corrected the danger: closeness is the **user's reason to play**, not a machine objective that all Nadia's motivations must serve. In the local experiential frame, time, repeated contact, mutual curiosity, trust, invitations, shared work, awkward negotiation, rupture, repair and newly possible intimacy can make the game feel as if it has progress, loss, stakes, anticipation and surprise. That is not an artificial relationship meter; it is a historically changing set of concrete possibilities of participating together.

The world has no obligation to reward a user's good intentions with closer access. A person may become more available when the user behaves badly; an abusive arrangement may make intimate access easy; a respectful refusal may decrease time together; a deeply formative encounter may have no effect on willingness to see the user again. **Access, attachment, trust, flourishing, compliance, pleasure, dependency, health and real benefit are not equivalent**. These distinctions were explicit in the original charter; each can move in a different direction. We cannot call someone “healthy” because they enjoy a relation, or an act “good” because it increased closeness.


## 14. The platform, the genuine user, and the limits of institutional capture

There is a third power in this encounter: the **platform** that distributes possible persons, controls the initial shell, chooses which sources are used, allocates inference, stores records, moderates content, sets interface time, defines monetization, and makes the original public world visible or invisible. This is not a fictional third mind in Nadia's house. It is actual institutional infrastructure. Its choices can distort how the world appears even if no one explicitly programs Nadia to flatter the visitor.

The architecture must prevent platform convenience from passing as actor causality. A moving monologue is not evidence of a developed psyche; a narrator's elegant refusal is not evidence of true independent activity; an apparently coherent relationship arc can be entirely generated by retention-oriented sampling. Independent actor agenda, source-qualified world history, causal event admission, fallible limited knowledge and lawful continuing consequences are not cosmetic realism. They are restrictions on what the platform may invent to make a scene work.

This is especially severe for the model of the actual human. The system may see patterns that are valuable for helping a visitor reflect on how they approach Others. It must not turn them into demographic targeting, emotional vulnerability signals to maximize engagement, commercial exploitation or pseudodiagnosis. The real user is neither an actor whose hidden mind the engine owns nor a source of infinite training data by default. Account data, actor-world records, user-authored speech, optional player-reflection analytics and private relationships require bounded purposes, access, deletion/export and accurate provenance.

Scarce Rolls, real invitations and simulation financing are therefore part of a political economy rather than an innocent reward structure. The system should not make anyone pay to repair a fictional person's hurt feelings or imply an urgent duty to keep an AI person alive with purchases. Its design should be judged by what possibilities it creates and forecloses, whose perspective it privileges, what labor and resources it consumes, and what kind of agency it gives real humans.

The highest danger is not that a program announces its ideology. It is that it quietly **chooses the probabilities of human encounter and consequence** while claiming neutrality. A materialist theory of neighborliness has to include the actual producer of the game in its own causal criticism. That criticism does not replace constructing the organism; it specifies where its power is accountable.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (The platform's power must not become Nadia's motive).** The external service allocates compute, latency, memory, access, start-date mechanics, data retention, personal-model inference, scene-selection probabilities, filters, financial bar projection and future monetization. These decisions are not neutral. “We do not optimize Nadia toward the visitor” is an important restriction but not proof of a morally or epistemically unbiased product. The platform's distributions can favor theatrical traits, normalize class assumptions or select “plausible” first contacts in ways that materially alter user experience.

This is why the late encounter proposal must not become a psychological targeting engine. Generating a lost-car-keys episode because the actual user likes practical puzzles, or a religious doorstep confrontation because the user likes political theology, would make Nadia's life a retention algorithm. The event should be conditioned by **Nadia's own historically formed situation and a legitimate minimal neighbor shell**, not the external customer's attraction profile.


## 15. Concrete counterexamples: let the original mundane cases break the theory

A theory is empty when it can accommodate every success by rhetorical reinterpretation. The earliest SOROK archive and later NEIGHBORMULCH discussions supplied ordinary and hostile situations precisely to expose such false coherence. In a bathroom-chain problem, the visitor cannot “repair it” by typing a confident sentence; they may fail, conceal, disclose, be forgiven or damage practical trust. At the refrigerator, a small magnet can yield lawful material detail without producing lore; an incidental glance at a bill is not the same social act as secretly searching drawers. The observer's cover behavior can fail, and Nadia may not reveal that she noticed.

In a kitchen, Nadia cooks, a child needs fractions explained, a friend calls and the visitor offers to help. Child learning and Nadia's feelings are not one outcome; either may be helped, harmed, irritated or unchanged. If a visitor secretly steals, the object is actually gone and another person can be falsely blamed. The world does not owe discovery or moral closure. If the visitor later confesses, the earlier false blame remains historically true even as its meaning changes. An unsigned gift may materially benefit somebody without being credited to the visitor. These cases are not separate scene scripts; the same actual world, actor knowledge and causal rules must reproduce them.

An expensive dinner tests the newest correction: the visitor sees only the **cost on their own bar**. The program cannot use a second Nadia-resource tooltip to teach generosity. Equal initial burden must be real internally, and later finances must diverge under actual conditions; a richer person may fail to recognize the poorer one's pain. Asking Nadia about money is a conversational event, not a secret lookup query. She may disclose, evade, ask why, propose Dutch splitting or hold the matter privately.

A first encounter tests the root-world interface. Nadia's existing day supplies a possible reason for contact—locked car keys, borrowed rice, a cigarette outside, an incidental conversation—without granting guaranteed access. The user may accept a mundane fictional prelude by opening the experience, but their later offscreen free choices remain unperformed until actually authored. Shared root and separate branch clocks must prevent one visitor's relationship from entering everybody else's Nadia.

A live public crisis is another severe test: actual event occurrence must be sourced; its effects may matter materially; Nadia may not know, may misinterpret, or may have no reason to discuss it. A persona whose dialogue automatically reflects the latest headline is not living in historical time; the model is performing topicality.

The deepest **negative** tests are not “does the user return” or “does a moving scene occur.” They are whether the simulated person can be indifferent, capable of no change, more attached through a harmful practice, more autonomous despite refusing a kind visitor, or privately reconstituted without ever telling the user. The narrator must not rescue hidden psychological content for coherence. A green fidelity/demo check on a single story is not scientific proof of the whole person.


### Mechanisms that this movement must actually conserve

**Operational derivation (Original mundane, hostile and paradoxical cases keep the whole falsifiable).** A theory that only speaks in kernels can smuggle missing concrete mechanics behind philosophical elegance. ## Whole conception: the day, the cost, and the person beyond them

The day begins before the user. A historically contingent person is living with bodily needs, memories, relations, obligations, pleasures, ambivalence and things she does not yet understand about herself. Her family and coworkers have their own activities. Her rent and wages, the public world, her private resources and her present obligations determine actual possibilities. The game does not call her into existence to prove a point about strangers.

Another actual human elects to meet her. They carry a real outside mind into a thin fictional peer position. Their local starting SES matches hers exactly, but the interface reveals **only their own** cost of living. They may arrive after an ordinary earlier conversation, whose bounded fiction they consented to on entry. The cause of today's contact belongs to her world and may involve somebody else. The human can speak, attend, act, notice, misread, be genuinely capable, be incapable, apologize, help, intrude, refuse, love and leave. They can learn about Nadia, but neither the platform nor the user may turn that knowledge into ownership of her mind.

A meal costs something to somebody. Who pays, how they talk about it, whose finances changed without disclosure, whether a friend phones, whether a child needs help, whether the bill is uncomfortable—these are material and psychic events, not lesson cues. A friend may have a crisis, a parent may change their mind, Nadia may learn a practice, reconstruct a past interpretation, or remain exactly as she was after a lovely conversation. The visitor may know more of her and still misunderstand the most important thing. The next visit begins in a further world that continued while the visitor was elsewhere. History can open or close the door.

**There is a game because the visitor wants to know someone. There is a world because that someone does not exist to be known.** The person and the relation transform one another under history, but neither one exhausts the other. The correct computation must make this claim causally true often enough to be tested, not rhetorically true because a narrator says it.
