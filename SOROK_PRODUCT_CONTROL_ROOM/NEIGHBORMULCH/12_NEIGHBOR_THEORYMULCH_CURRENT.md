## 1. The irreducible relation is also historically constituted

Rawlsing's most compact aspiration is not “an especially intelligent nonplayer character.” It is **a confrontation with another historically constituted center of valuation that the participant can truly affect but never own**. In the original product charter, the stranger might eventually become specific enough, consequential enough and mutually known enough to become kin. That possibility must remain real; it must not become a condition the world is obliged to satisfy. A player may be delighted to grow closer, and the platform may quite reasonably be built around the *desire* to do so. But the person is not there to complete the user's spiritual formation, to prove that capitalism is bad, to demonstrate good empathy, to become sexually available, or to make a friendly app.

The deeper ontological candidate recovered in the earlier Sorok conversation was **irreducible first-personhood without self-sufficient individuality**. There are three nested irreducibilities. Nadia exceeds the player's knowledge of her, exceeds her own conscious self-account, and exceeds any single relationship. Their particular relationship exceeds either participant's account of what the relationship is: an offer may have consequences neither intended, a hidden injury may be causally true without either noticing, and third persons may live with consequences beyond their view. The world exceeds each participant's perceptual access. Yet none of these irreducibilities makes anyone an isolated atom; the person emerges and becomes through actual relations, the relation is constituted through histories and institutions, and the world is reproduced and changed through activity. **Non-exhaustion is not non-relation.**

This is a conditional scientific *design hypothesis*, not a proven declaration that code produces conscious human subjects. An actor-local model of lived perspective and valuation is useful without being proof of machine consciousness. The theological image of a neighbor bearing worth beyond my grasp, the materialist account of historical social constitution, the phenomenological fact of finite perspective, and the Rawlsian question of contingent birth can be productive *translations* of the same tension without treating them as one validated metaphysics. The model can legitimately be built and adversarially studied under that demanding candidate; it cannot claim a scientific human subject simply by passing its own rules.

The notion of **kenotic attention**, in the historical Sorok research, is especially valuable when stripped of moral flattery: relinquish the presumption that what appears from *my* center exhausts what is. Look again, discover unfamiliar facts, revise mistaken models, be answerable to another's reasons and actual conditions. It emphatically does **not** mean “Nadia is right,” “her culture is authoritative,” “submit to her,” “erase oneself,” “all conflict is a failure,” or “receive a moral grade for listening.” One can care and argue, help and refuse, recognize oppression and resist it, love somebody and leave. It is a practice whose reality the human exercises rather than a meter the software awards.

The original charter formulated a moral horizon: contingency can produce humility, recognition, solidarity and responsibility; worlds that make people should be accountable for which human possibilities they support and suppress. This remains **a horizon for product research and institutional criticism**, not an optimizer for Nadia's choices or a hidden student-evaluation curriculum. Some users will become more attentive; some will become more controlling; some will be uninterested. The system must not coerce the world to make the aspiration come true.

If a purported kernel is useful it should let us **derive**, not endlessly legislate: the other person's independent life, limited perception, history, incompatible views, reciprocal effects, fallible self-knowledge, consent, actual material constraints, time and the impossibility of completion. A kernel fails when it cannot explain playability, when it requires every incident to be important, or when it quietly converts nonownership into a command for the user to suffer a rejection. DERIVE / SEPARATE / CONNECT / TUNE / FALSIFY / PROMOTE are different intellectual operations. A more concise source law is better only when its omitted distinctions remain reproducible under hard cases.

Thus the unifying contradiction is precise: the **product is deliberately organized around a real human wanting to know Nadia**, but Nadia is organized by her own historically produced life. The world may make an encounter possible. Neither its movement nor Nadia's psychic constitution is permitted to maximize continued app engagement. The service can place the human near her, and then must relinquish ownership of what follows.


## 2. The rolled life is the alternate; the real person enters for relation

The human premise is unchosen historical location. Bodies, family, land, language, social class, race, citizenship, religion, labor relations, networks of care, violence, available technologies and political institutions do not begin as individually selected character attributes. They are conditions into which people arrive, conditions they reproduce, resist, reinterpret and sometimes change through activity. Rawlsing aims to construct particular fictional lives arising in sufficiently qualified possible situations, rather than supply consumer-selectable compatible personalities.

The long horizon is “Roll to meet someone you could have been born as.” The subject of the roll is the *person living the alternate possible life*, not a rewritten real user's soul. The real user remains psychologically themselves, a consciousness and history formed elsewhere. It is the person-world generated on the other side who embodies that contingent birth and becomes through it. This is why a life cannot be constituted simply from a static demographic profile, nor can its psychological details be inferred as the actual secret inner life of a survey respondent. Empirical social distributions constrain plausible population and social conditions; historically materialist, psychological and generative operations construct a **hypothetical individual**, with real epistemic limitations.

The narrow alpha can start from one, then three, then five *richly constituted* fictional persons with shareable beginnings. Richly constituted does not mean rich-income people or affluent users. The alpha cannot claim to sample world birth probabilities merely because it introduces several disparate characters. It can establish a smaller but essential proposition: a stranger whose life was not selected by the player can become particular, surprising and consequential.

The deeper distributive structure needs PYAT-qualified actual joints, minority configurations, missingness rules, social class, cultural and institutional variation, and proper uncertainty. It does not authorize universal ethnic, religious, gendered or psychological stereotypes. SOROK's fictional psychic dynamics own what *this* hypothetical person later learns, identifies with, opposes and may do; PYAT's conditional co-travel cannot become a hidden motive detector.


## 3. Four different beginnings: human invitation, root introduction, roll, and encounter

There are several economically and causally distinct **entrances**, and they cannot be collapsed just because every one occurs inside a social product. First, a *real human invitation* may give another human an opportunity to enter Rawlsing. Original SOROK sources imagined a scarce, one-for-one human designation: a person can truthfully say they wanted another actual person to have the experience. That is neither a badge declaring the recipient virtuous nor a referral leaderboard. The invitation must not falsely claim the inviter personally recommends a particular Nadia, read their private in-world history or endorsed the user's morality. The actual entitlement, timing, replacement/revocation and possibly human-distributed invitation notes remain decisions for the existing S15 social/product owner.

Second, **introduction to a particular existing person-root** is a different event. A user may tell another user “I met Nadia; she is a person you might care to meet.” Under a common pre-contact seed, the new human receives an independent continuation. They need not enter on the same date or encounter her in the same way. The first visitor's intimacy and private history cannot be exported as if it were the new visitor's history. Users' outside conversation about Nadia may inform a new user's external mind but is not automatically canonical knowledge for the new branch's fictional Nadia.

Third, **commissioning a new root by Roll** is a third event. The user does not choose its demographic/personality bundle for compatibility, cannot buy access to a woman's future affection, cannot guarantee sexual chemistry or instruct the new fictional person to be grateful for existence. Paid and free opportunities, compute cost, discoverable pools, fair second chance after complete exclusion, delayed sharing and attribution are actual design hypotheses, not settled policies. A person who refuses the commissioner is not defective merchandise.

Fourth, there is the **diegetic first contact itself**: a fictional neighbor's historical opportunity to meet Nadia, generated at the branch's present moment from Nadia's actual life and a homologous local peer. Unlike a real onboarding invite, this encounter can properly include a bounded fictional previous exchange, a missed Tuesday invitation and a subsequent Thursday dinner as its opening premise. Opening the experience is an act of consent to that thin diegetic prelude, not authority over Nadia's choices or the user's future offline conduct.

These separate the wrong equation “rolling a person = selecting a character = earning a relationship = onboarding a friend.” They are related by a historically generated *possible person*, a source-qualified *root*, *private forks*, finite *ways of meeting* and humans who may discuss how one same beginning produced diverging histories. A small 1→3→5 alpha emphasizes person-depth before population breadth. Global birth-probability claims require much more than a few richly developed examples. Scaling breadth without psychic depth would negate the ethical/experiential premise; scaling psychological depth without real world/social causality would equally negate it.

The special issue of a user-unaffected reference Nadia updated to the opening day is still an engineering/research proposal: it creates no independent entitlement to root-adjacent facts. If two users meet an unvisited-reference Nadia on different days, the user's separately forked encounters cannot rewrite the reference's history. If the original seed contains a fact whose specificity was lazily completed by another branch's lawful attention, compatible current forks may inherit that root fact only under existing H0–H3 and conflict/version law. It is not an unconditional all-worlds lore merge.

Real-person privacy rights, the right to stop, the right to reject an introduction, and the inability to buy the next person's emotional response constrain the whole distribution system. A platform may use modest funding to finance actual simulation; it may not turn longing for a synthetic person into a mechanically induced duty to pay.


## 4. Exact peer SES is a hidden world fact

The portal positions the visitor **in the very same socioeconomic status as Nadia at first encounter**: demographically homologous and materially her peer. This is not “approximately comparable peers” or “people who happen to live in the same building.” It is an explicit product constitution based on Nadia's already existing social/material state. She is not recalibrated to match the external user. The portal assigns the user a locally plausible thin social and material footing that **matches Nadia's initial socioeconomic circumstances and equivalent burden of an ordinary identical expense**.

This is a causal equality, not an informational entitlement. The game should not tell the user “you and Nadia have exactly the same amount of money” or show her financial bar beside theirs. **The user may never learn the exact initial equality.** They can know what a dinner costs them, not necessarily what remains in her checking account, what her rent is, what a household member controls, or whether she has unmentioned obligations. The platform knows the two initial economic positions are calibrated; the human does not inherit the platform's omniscience.

For the same dinner at the initial matched position, the *objective* ordinary economic sacrifice can be equal, even though its psychological significance is not. But the user's belief that it costs Nadia the same is at most an inference from social appearances or actual disclosure, not a fact the interface forces into awareness. The earlier R8 claim that seeing one's own cost automatically gives the user precise knowledge of Nadia's generosity was wrong. What the same starting SES establishes is **the possibility of materially grounded understanding without guaranteed recognition**.

The effective definition of SES is multidimensional. Income, job status, property and access, financial flexibility, consumption, household ownership, kin obligations and institutional power cannot be reduced to an arbitrary scalar. The user insists on **real same status and matched ordinary purchase burden at entry**, so it is not sufficient to use a decorative matching bar with incoherent accounts underneath. The exact cost-projection denominator was not recovered from the November 15 money discussion and remains a receiving/design problem for the existing material owner. Theoretical precision is required on that unresolved question, not invention of a new finance simulation.

An initial equality is not an everlasting equality. After the two lives have begun interacting, they may spend, save, earn, lose work, borrow, lend, inherit, take on care, receive support, become ill or experience a change of rent. Their financial positions can and should diverge under the actual represented events. **No periodic resynchronization** is permitted merely to preserve the product's initially symmetric form. Precisely because the portal did not disclose equality at entrance, the later divergence may be even less legible to the visitor.


## 5. Your own resource bar makes material sacrifice legible without Nadia's accounts

The recovered November 15 decision was to keep actual wage/rent/cash material accounting in the world, but spare the human a second game of earning points, maximizing income or operating a spreadsheet. A coarse “YOUR RESOURCES” bar, with contextual language such as “Limited flexibility,” “Rent approaching,” or “Payday tomorrow,” gives just enough of the *visitor's own* practical monetary world for an everyday choice. The historically illustrated takeout price was **18% of the bar**, not an adopted mathematical denominator. Exact available liquidity, obligations, pay timing and resource rights are underneath; the bar does not constitute money and cannot make a false transaction feasible.

Imagine Nadia has suggested ordering food. The visitor wishes to offer to pay. How much would it cost? The interface may briefly **blink or foreground their own resource bar** with the relative price. That is all the system grants as the economic projection. Nadia's response—whether she accepts, politely resists, jokes, expresses offense, insists on sharing, seems uncomfortable, or says nothing especially striking—is generated from *her* current activity, relationships and historical disposition. There need not be dialogue about a number, and the portal need not give a simultaneous numeric dollar amount or percentage of Nadia's resources.

The user can offer to pay even when the bar indicates significant cost. They can ask to split the bill Dutch, ask whether she minds an inexpensive dinner, discuss what either earns, reveal personal financial discomfort, or ask a painful question about whether she can afford something. These are **actual social negotiations**. Nadia may give an answer, hesitate, be direct, conceal, miscalculate, minimize, exaggerate, resent the question, or find it refreshing. The precise world-state accounts and the person's own knowledge of their finances are different from the words she says. A character's dialogue must not be compelled to serve as an accounting statement for the user's UI.

The player's bar has a simple privacy law: it displays **the player's own materially relevant projected cost**. It does not proxy Nadia's finances by analogy, and should never update as if her expenses are transactions in the user's wallet. If Nadia pays, the transaction belongs to her money. A user can still see or remember what it *would have cost them* without possessing her balance. Their knowledge is situational, partial, and open to later correction.

This may produce discomfort of a rare and proper kind. The player has to decide whether offering to pay was generous, condescending, necessary, ordinary, embarrassing or economically irresponsible while knowing only their own side and observing Nadia's actual response. The interface cannot install the moral interpretation. **The bar can expose a material cost without supplying a moral or interpersonal explanation.**


## 6. Equality can become inequality without knowledge

The user's latest insight is strongest **after** the initial same-SES condition ceases to be true. Suppose Nadia suffers a loss of income while the visitor's represented routine continues. The next time the visitor proposes an expensive meal, their own bar still suggests the expense is manageable. They may infer that Nadia feels the same because the two once appeared similarly situated, or because they have never asked. The game does not announce a financial divorce between the two resource states.

Now reverse it. Nadia is materially better situated than the visitor after a new job or unexpected assistance. She suggests a plan that has ceased to be financially comfortable for her neighbor. She may know that difference, suspect it, or fail to notice. She may have ordinary habits of consumption formed through now-disappeared hardship, or the visitor may feel an obligation to appear equally flexible. These are **actor-local psychological and relational events conditioned by actual changing material possibilities**.

Someone for whom the bill is easy may have less immediate felt knowledge of what it costs someone who cannot easily pay it. That asymmetry is not a universal moral rule about wealthy persons; it is a plausible practical effect of differing exposure to material necessity. The burden can reverse by situation. People with similar wages may have very different obligations and temporary available liquidity. A person can notice deprivation and still behave thoughtlessly, or fail to notice and nevertheless be helpful. A materialist system should allow each combination as warranted by actual history, not force a tutorial moment of enlightenment.

The surface must retain *epistemic asymmetry*. The user can learn through a declined invitation, a joke that lands badly, a conversation about pay, an uncharacteristic choice, a changed routine or an explicit disclosure. They can also remain ignorant. The platform should not flash “Nadia is now poorer than you” or present a sympathy bar; neither should it deliberately conceal materially necessary facts from the actual user when their own purchase or contract is at stake. **Opaque is not deceptive about the visitor's money; it is privacy and nonomniscience about the Other's.**

The psychological consequence of economic divergence is only one layer. Nadia may experience tiredness, shame, pride, necessity, anger, generosity, practical relief or no marked feeling. Other members of her household may control income, supply childcare or create debts. The visitor cannot read all of these through a glance at a receipt. This is a living class relation, not an economy minigame.


## 7. Work, money, households and institutions are causal rather than minigames

A job is not merely an income increment. It organizes hours, bodily exhaustion, social hierarchy, authority, routine, skills, absence, recognition, dependence, institutions and future opportunities. An unchosen world with wage work and care obligations cannot be internally consistent if its money refreshes when the user wants dinner. The world must own paydays, delayed wages, rent, purchases, recurring commitments, inventory and actual time. But the user should not have to play through every shift or click to collect wages.

The human's thin local shell can continue established nonexpressive routine while the human is away: a scheduled workday, expected pay, recurring rent, a commute or the cost of maintaining their dwelling. It may not invent their actual real-world feelings or rewrite their new convictions, accept overtime, break a promise, pursue a new career, pledge money or send intimacy messages without them. Nadia, by contrast, is a constructed continuing organism with her own in-world capacity to initiate decisions: she can seek work, accept help, take a shift, resign, organize with coworkers or miscalculate a bill, conditional on the world and her own state. That difference is about whose mind the simulation has jurisdiction to construct.

A person who has more money but less time may be less able to host a visitor; someone who lost work may have hours but little security. Care work and kinship may shape availability even with no cash exchange. A bar does not replace transport, sleep, body, law, housing tenure, household dependency, education or workplace power. A dinner's financial feasibility is one condition among several, and even financial success cannot cause another person to consent.

At first contact the same-SES rule conditions the visitor's entry position. Afterward the wages, obligations, fatigue and practical capacities evolve through separately owned histories. The user may feel a change in their own bar while failing to perceive what happened in Nadia's work. That is not a flaw in the product; it is what living with another partially knowable person costs.


## 8. The impossible neighbor and the lawful scars of crossing the veil

The “impossible neighbor” is not a bug to be explained away with a rich invented player biography. A real human arrives carrying their actual mind—knowledge, religious commitments, accent, professional competence, taste, memories, historical misunderstandings, anger, humor, trauma, adult judgment and years of practical learning. Rawlsing places that mind into a **locally constituted, demographically homologous, exact same-SES footing** beside Nadia. The user is not reincarnated, was not raised with Nadia's family, and cannot truthfully speak as someone who personally lived through her community's decades. The portal gives enough bodily/material history for them to stand, work in pre-established mundane ways, pay, be perceived and produce actual future consequences without supplying an alternate soul.

The translation of the real user into the local world has unavoidable **scars**. A real person's job qualification might have no historically lawful local counterpart; a practice may require a credential or language the local shell has not acquired; a real-life memory may refer to a family relation or political event that did not occur in the fictional person's shared locality. The earlier source question explicitly allows **NO_LAWFUL_HOMOLOGY** as a valid result. This is not a fail state to be smoothed over by the narrator granting a fake career, foreign-degree equivalence or a childhood in Tyre. A claim can pass literally as foreign user testimony, be mapped into a qualified analogous practice, be bracketed as outside knowledge, remain explicitly uncertain or be untranslatable into an immediately performable local act. The translation **must be owned and recorded** at the existing portal binding boundary; the expression model cannot improvise authority during rendering.

The player may be fluent in a real language but not the local language, or the reverse: the fictional social footing may provide minimal practical language where appropriate without making the human suddenly understand idiom, religious allusion or code-switching as a native. Translation preserves **what was actually realized as spoken or understood**, not always the exact surface string the user typed. An ambiguous joke, political epithet, awkward formality or technical explanation can become a consequential mismatch. The user should receive enough indication of a significant diegetic translation to know what their character actually said, while immersion is preserved. A miscommunication may be funny, painful, repaired, ignored or instrumentalized. Nadia is not an anthropological interpreter obliged to explain all her cultural context.

Equal initial SES is **economic conditioning**, not equality of every institutional advantage. Legality of housing, gendered risk, nationality, labor rights, mobility, social network, relationship to kin and physical skill may remain different even where the initial ordinary expense has identical material weight. The user is a demographically homologous *peer*, not a copy of Nadia and not an outside wealthy rescuer. Their local material resources and continuing cost projection should be coherent and nonmiraculous. Their actual cognition remains foreign and can sometimes clash with assumptions local actors take for granted. Nadia is allowed to notice that foreignness, misunderstand it, be amused, resent it or find it compelling.

Different approaches to the same conversation can involve assimilation, code-switching, refusal, changed local habits or collaborative invention of a hybrid way of interacting. **Conformity to local norms is not the hidden criterion of success**. The visitor can learn without surrendering critique; Nadia can take a view the visitor finds harmful. They can each help change the other's dispositions while neither is declared the cultural judge. This is what makes the transposition a lived relational problem rather than a simulator that asks the player to parrot a local persona.

The local shell's continuation is grounded in actual world permissions. If the human has an established shift, basic commuting and a recurring rent payment can be maintained offscreen under finite routine. No system may write their new political confession, marriage proposal, theft, act of rescue, abuse, religious conversion or apology while they are away. The user's bounded consent to a **first-meeting fictional prelude** does not silently create an all-purpose player actor with autonomous drives. The portal's very partiality is its epistemic virtue: the human can enter another world without the software claiming to have recreated them.


## 9. The platform models the visitor; Nadia knows a different visitor

Two different models of the same visitor are required, and neither is a substitute for the visitor's own actual mind. The **platform's longitudinal model** may track repeated actual attention, perceptual selections, skill demonstrated in situated tasks, speech, humor, follow-through on invitations, patterns of care, discomfort with refusal, attempts at control, learning and changing expectations. That evidence is context-conditioned. A person who behaved defensively in a forced cramped apartment may not possess an essential trait of defensiveness; a person who repeatedly checks a bill might be anxious, conscientious, intrusive, broke, curious or none of those. The platform may have hypotheses, uncertainty, counterevidence and trends, not a licensed diagnostic self-portrait.

The **actor-local model Nadia has of this person** contains only what she could perceive, infer, remember or learn through legitimate social transmission. She cannot see the platform's analytics, private unspoken intent, user account data, a secret “better relationship technique” or another user's branch. She can regard the visitor as strangely attentive without knowing what the interface classifies as ATTEND. She may sincerely misread a friendly act as patronizing. She may form a confident but wrong picture of what they care about. Her model of how the user thinks of her may itself shape her next response, and the user's model of how she models them may be equally fallible.

Reflection on the real user's patterns may become a meaningful optional product use, but it should say **“here are the actions you repeatedly took in these represented situations,” not “this is what you essentially are.”** A structural pattern can still be worth confronting when it is statistically or observationally supported. The system must preserve source contexts, opportunities, evidence gaps, ambiguity and the possibility that its encounter generator itself induced those actions. Human actual consent, purpose limits, deletion/export, appropriate separation of account/payment metadata, and meaningful control over private records are not an optional afterthought to a believable Nadia. The previous Round Eight explicitly said deletion/export rights survive any fictional relation. Round Nine's near-omission of those rights was a substantive preservation failure.

The real human can bring outside meta-knowledge about a shared root from real community conversation or a memory of another simulation session. That knowledge becomes Nadia's potential causal stimulus only when the visitor actually communicates it within a lawful channel. A character's ability to recognize the fact depends on her own knowledge. The engine must not preload the user's externally obtained research into a fictional character's memory, nor treat the user's professed certainty as objective history.

The system also must not respond to an inferred player weakness by selecting a Nadia occasion likely to hook that user. A source-qualified encounter can be intrinsically compelling without tuning first-contact events to the person's abandonment fears, sexuality, political appetite or spending history. Platform knowledge is a site of possible exploitation. The strongest privacy protection is not only which data are stored but **what causal authority those data may exercise in the constructed world**.

The user can learn to attend differently because attention can become practically consequential. Their activity can change actual other lives. It is neither necessary nor legitimate for the app to tell them they have become virtuous. The participant's external human history remains larger than every provisional model the platform builds.


**The strongest anti-leak rule on player analytics.** Earlier C05 work specifically raised the risk that the platform's relation indexes and summaries could reveal relationships, their number, or sensitive participation patterns to an actor or user who has never lawfully encountered them. World graph membership, private models, relationship counts, hidden actor labels and internal negative-control evidence are **not public domain facts**. Neither a narrator's “relationship summary” nor Nadia's psychologically fluent reply may reveal private connections merely because an internal readset has them. A truthful source of private information needs lawful perception, testimony, communication or other world admission, with independent data-purpose and actual-user protection.

## 10. The other person and her day were already living

The game cannot begin from an abstract narrator's command that the human “go meet your neighbor” while Nadia conveniently waits. Her life was already constituted through past episodes, practices, needs, relations, institutions and the current day. Her habits are not a list of personality labels. Her present activity is an effect of a long, compressibly represented historical process with contradictory psychic organizations, a body, a calendar, people she knows and things that must be done.

The earlier N-T1 Round Eight proposal made the first contact a **late person-derived prelude**, rather than a randomly selected example from a stock genre of neighbor meetings. That remains the correct demand, but the user now makes the temporal conditioning sharper: **when the visitor opens the existing Nadia, at that time and in the world conditions of that day, how could they actually have met?**

This question does not require an indefinite encyclopedia of prewritten openings for each persona. Nor does it require the service to press a fixed generic “she knocked for flour” button. It asks the existing person/world system to produce an eligible event at the first-contact frontier. Nadia's present projects and practices condition candidate meetings. A person who is visiting friends might have a package delivered; a smoker may be on a landing; someone who knows how to open a locked car may assist; someone preparing a dinner may seek an ingredient, and a gregarious neighbor may simply invite another neighbor for company.

**The criterion is causal possibility from this actor today**, not attractiveness of the premise to the visiting customer. The human has chosen to meet someone. The world must be able to supply a social route for that attempt without making the actor interested, grateful or available beyond what her condition permits. The doorway is structured by the service; the other person's invitation is not guaranteed.

The oldest SOROK encounter court already knew that randomly choosing an Other should not mean teleporting them into a room. Its sample grocery scene is a good original example. The new advance is to give the *mechanism of first contact* the same actual person/world causal grounding as the events that follow, and to let today rather than a permanently preselected “intro chapter” determine what is plausible.


## 11. A shared root, a user-unaffected reference day, and private continuities

A special difficulty appears when many users choose the same banked Nadia. She has a shared pre-contact root: childhood, household, objects, prior social history, acquired psychological organizations, financial class and other original conditions. After a visitor actually meets her, that user's Nadia develops a privately diverging continuity. User A does not get to alter Nadia-B's workplace, lover, religious outlook or memory by acting in Nadia-A's world.

The latest proposal suggests a useful additional distinction: **a user-unaffected, current-date reference continuation of Nadia may exist beyond the initial seed**. This common reference is not one simultaneously multiplayer Nadia who learns from every user. It is a continuing hypothetical **Nadia who has not been affected by any particular visitor**, under applicable real-world dates and self-consistent actor/world activity. When a new user opens her on a given day, their branch can fork from a qualified snapshot of that reference state at that date, then add a user-specific local peer and first-contact occasion. Nadia-A's independent postcontact life never flows back into the reference Nadia. User B, opening later, can receive a different first snapshot because the *unvisited* reference life has itself continued.

This is **a proposed causal model, not a proven existing implementation** and not a declaration that PR #1275's existing seed/copy-on-write Mechanism I lacks necessary operations. It has nontrivial consistency and cost consequences. A person already forked months ago may be at a completely different job or residence from the reference Nadia today. A later new entrant can have a different intro because the source world evolved, not merely because a new random hook was drawn. Public events can apply to all appropriate worlds; private user effects cannot.

An alternative is **lazy, keyed, compatible historical completion of a reference period up to the requested day**, rather than continuous high-depth shared runtime. A small bank may use bounded pre-generated eligible openings at some future development stage. The user's new idea does not definitively prohibit pre-generation; it rejects assuming we must precompute endless introductions and invites a better question: what is lawful for this Nadia *at this moment*? Which physical place, ongoing activity, material need, social route or ordinary accidental contact could bring her near a new peer?

The time-domain contracts need care: one public date can be shared across different postcontact worlds, but actor relevance and knowledge differ. A public transit problem might affect origin Nadia today while a user-specific Nadia who moved elsewhere is not affected. An inviting reference Nadia and a postcontact Nadia who learned to mistrust a stranger can be the same seed ancestor, not the same present relational person. A common reference event is not automatically a universal postcontact feeling.

A truly shared historical root plus contemporary reference continuation can therefore coexist with infinitely many private histories, without one player's success or offense becoming a global training update. The common reference's own advancement must be authorized by competent historical/world owners and use source-qualified causality, not arbitrary recap prose.


## 12. How could this neighbor have met her today?

Suppose Nadia's reference state at today's date includes a crowded apartment, an upcoming dinner and an actual shortage of rice. One plausible bridge is that she asks a neighbor to borrow some. Suppose instead she has a private routine of smoking outside after work and commonly exchanges small talk with strangers. Another occasion is a conversation on the landing. If she has practical automotive knowledge and the adjacent peer happens to lock their keys in a car, she might assist. If she is simply warm and socially outgoing, a direct invitation may be plausible without a fabricated crisis.

Each of these is **a possibility for an event**, not a personality-card plot prompt. The model may generate a small set of context-conditioned candidates; actual world-state feasibility, chronology, physical affordance and actor initiative determine which can be admitted. A person who would not ask a stranger for help should not be made to ask simply because a neighbor story needs momentum. A high-energy Nadia may genuinely invite the visitor; the program should not suppress her hospitality to prove independence.

The user asks why, after having lived nearby for an unspecified period without really talking, the two people would be on their way to a visit *now*. A thin premise can compress that prior adjacency. An opening might say a conversation happened yesterday, or that she mentioned Thursday supper, or that an emergency car key mishap introduced them. It is a locally accepted starting fiction that makes the first playable scene socially legible. It need not pretend the human had a secret childhood in the world.

At the instant of first access, the prelude can be generated from **the present Nadias's plausibility**, the peer's assigned local position and a qualified short prior interval. It need not be regenerated repeatedly in pursuit of a perfect entertainment hook; there must be a stable admitted event once the branch begins. If no home invitation is plausible, the first lawful encounter may occur in a hallway, outside a shop, on a street or through a shared neighbor. The user's intention to meet the person is not a warranty that the actor will host them.

The encounter's final form—an already granted invitation, a current interruption, an errand, a meeting scheduled for a particular evening—is a small part of her life. Her day continues through it. The dinner that caused a rice request still has guests. The locked car must actually exist and be resolvable. The cigarette break has bodily and social context. The event does not evaporate once it has successfully delivered a player to the doorstep.


## 13. Entering the experience consents to a bounded fictional prelude

Round Eight imposed an unnecessarily strict no-authorship law: the engine could not state that the visitor had previously accepted or declined an invitation because the external human had not literally typed that decision. The user correctly rejects that categorical ban. **Opening Rawlsing in order to meet Nadia itself authorizes a limited diegetic premise that gets the encounter started.** This is basic fictional participation: a novel can start midway into a social circumstance without forcing the reader to roleplay all prior hours.

A lawful prelude can state “you bumped into your neighbor yesterday,” “she asked whether you had a little olive oil,” “she invited you for supper,” or even “you couldn't make Tuesday and agreed to Thursday” if those are benign fictional scene-setting assumptions within the experience the user chose to enter. The program does not thereby claim the external real-world person *actually* had that conversation, nor that their actual adult biography included it. It is not a hidden reconstruction of their mind.

There remains a practical and conceptual distinction between **bounded entry fiction** and **ongoing unauthorized autopilot**. After the playable human has arrived, new meaningful decisions are theirs. The system may not later forge their apologies, agreements, abandonment, political speech or sexual consent while they are offline, simply because it wants a dramatic next chapter. Nor should the initial script attribute a large novel real-life moral or psychological confession to the external human merely to justify first contact. But there is no need for an intake questionnaire or repeated modal confirmation of mundane prior setup.

The user consents to **meet Nadia**, not to command Nadia's will. Their structural consent can authorize the fiction of their own local social route; it cannot authorize the engine to make a private person receive them willingly. Thus the event must have a plausible actor source, and Nadia may still find the neighbor strange, be too busy or retract a casual invitation when circumstances change.

This distinction is stronger than both extremes. It refuses to puppet the *actual human* throughout the game, while also refusing the absurd requirement that the human hand-author every minor prehistory of a fictional neighbor encounter. Their first real act can be responsive to the already plausible situation: knock, arrive, say hello, ask, leave, postpone, laugh or offer help. The encounter has begun because the world made it possible and the human chose to be there.


## 14. The house and text are two causal entrances, not two games

The original NEIGHBOR product had a remarkably modest experiential shape: **at the door / inside the house** and **outside the house through messaging when a lawful social channel exists**. These are not two separate psychological games. They are two practical relations to one continuing Nadia and one branch history. The current first-contact proposal must therefore not reduce the entire product to endlessly generated home visits, nor grant instant private texting before contact. Home access is an earned or situationally permitted *social fact*, not an unlock reward or a tutorial button; she can invite the visitor, refuse entry, be away, have another guest, decline an overnight stay, or later change an existing permission.

An outside-portal message is a real diegetic communication attempt routed through an actually available contact channel: number or account exchanged, legitimate social referral, prior practical coordination or other qualified way a local peer can reach her. A failed first meeting may leave no messaging path. A person who gave a number for a practical emergency has not necessarily invited daily intimate messages. Social and legal context, household sharing of devices, work hours, privacy, network availability, language and the relationship's actual history can affect delivery. A user may send an ordinary text, a long apology, a joke, a request, a picture, a promise or nothing. The service can record the sent attempt; delivery, reading, recognition, reaction and reply are **distinct world and actor-owned stages**.

No default user right to a same-day reply follows from the messaging interface. Nadia might be working, asleep, bored, distracted, without reception, avoiding the visitor, regretting disclosure, celebrating with friends, trying to write a careful answer, or simply not prioritize this conversation. Silence is not necessarily anger or indifference; a read receipt is not proof of understanding; a response is not proof of pleasure. The user may text when Nadia cannot receive, cancel a message under actual medium affordances, or discover a changed number. Messages can intersect her independent activities and third-party obligations without becoming a retention-nag scheduler.

The clock needs at least three related scales: **diegetic event-time**, **simulated elapsed-world intervals** and **the real external human's availability**. A dinner agreed for Thursday may be played through a reasonable elastic rendezvous window, with dependent downstream world facts pending until arrival/cancellation/expiry is settled. A user can choose a stricter synchronization mode if they want, but the product must not reward it with affection, moral standing or prestige; accessibility and ordinary work schedules matter. Nadia knows whether the meeting happened, was postponed or missed in her world. She does not know the app's grace period. If the actual human goes offline for a week, the platform cannot call that abandonment by default. A specific unfulfilled in-world commitment can carry real consequences only when it was lawfully committed and the time contract is coherent.

Notifications must be consequences of genuine actor-initiated communication or other lawfully user-relevant events, not fake anxiety “she needs you” triggers manufactured for retention. User settings can limit alerts without making Nadia metaphysically interpret notification silence. A person's real pain, illness or need must not become an addictive alarm product. The human retains the right to end contact or leave the platform without a manufactured moral hostage.

Co-presence and messaging have different **phenomenal bandwidths**. One may witness the interrupted cooking, bodily posture, sound of rain, a child's cry or another person arriving. The other may see only a short line of text, delay, typo, selected emoji or no reply. Both can be full causal actions with durable consequences. A text conversation may produce a real invitation; a doorstep argument may lead to a blocked contact route. The narrator cannot compensate for reduced bandwidth by telling the user exactly what Nadia privately felt while typing.

This is the positive architecture of two channels Round Eight carried and Round Nine had compressed. The tenth whole explicitly restores it without turning communication into a constant availability funnel.


**Assisted and more exacting modes.** Historical SOROK explicitly left open an optional distinction between stronger and weaker epistemic scaffolding: the player may prefer reminders of their *own* prior commitments, ordinary timing assistance and accessible summaries, or a more demanding mode with fewer explicit cues. Neither mode may disclose Nadia's hidden state, the future consequences of a choice, the exact meaning of a gesture, or privileged knowledge of her money. A stricter schedule cannot confer prestige or affection; an assisted schedule cannot counterfeit a promise fulfilled or erase a person's refusal. These are different presentations and attention aids within the **same causally owned world**, not an easy/moral and hard/authentic psychology. The exact modality, defaults, data controls and access affordances remain open S11/UX owner decisions.

## 15. The encounter belongs to Nadia's live activity

A prelude conditioned by Nadia's state today cannot be divorced from what she will be doing when the user appears. If the reason was a dinner invitation, food, guests, time, budget, cooking skill and household social dynamics remain actual conditions. If the reason was a lost key, the car and her practical knowledge still matter. If the interaction began with a cigarette outside, she may be on a break from a stressful shift or simply enjoying ordinary routine. A parent may still be helping a child; a partner or friend may soon arrive.

The user does not enter a neutral chatbot state. They enter **a world already in motion**, whose activities generate concurrent claims. The person has a finite attention, embodied energy, projects, obligations and preferences that do not all cease in response to the visitor. One can sit with Nadia and talk, help cook, change the subject, offer to pay, argue with a relative or choose silence while other people and objects continue to have causes.

The game-like property lies in this irreducibility: real human thought and activity can change what follows, but the human cannot select all the facts or have all the time. There is no need for a generic mission system. The available task is just being a particular neighbor in this particular world. The quality of an encounter is not measured by the number of quest tokens or newly unlocked rooms.

A short first interaction can genuinely end without an invitation or a messaging channel. A person may remember and later reconsider, or never care. The product must not conjure a new crisis for the user because it fears that honest ordinary life is insufficiently engaging. Equally, where her present actual life naturally creates a funny or consequential event, it need not be suppressed in the name of austere realism.

The first visit is **one event in a longer life**, not an opening level where every social result exists to demonstrate the rules.


## 16. Attention deepens resolution, not obligatory significance

The original SOROK source gives the human at least three open-ended interaction families: **SAY**, **DO** and **ATTEND**, expressed primarily through natural language rather than a small menu of pre-scored moral options. ATTEND is neither a “lore button” nor an invitation for the narrator to hide interesting facts as punishment. The historically corrected rule recovered from the October conversation is stronger: **ATTEND should reliably increase resolution when a real object or situation permits more observation; it should not reliably increase significance.** Looking carefully at worn Birkenstocks can reveal strap repairs, surface wear, fit and material clues; additional history may be available through later photographs and dialogue. None of these compels a solemn story about the shoes symbolizing a secret trauma. She may say “because they're comfortable” with perfect sincerity.

At least four depths remain distinct: (1) perceptual detail—what precisely is here; (2) material/historical specificity—how worn, where, when, what happened; (3) relational/causal links—who used, repaired or gave it, which other histories touch it; and (4) experienced/attributed significance—what Nadia feels or believes, what the visitor infers, what the world causally supports. Deepening one level can reveal evidence for another but cannot automatically manufacture it. A magnet may be mundane today and later acquire a connection to an absent relative; onions stored strangely may invite a hypothesis about an earlier scarcity practice. A prayer rug might be wholly ordinary, not symbolic architecture. **There is no finite true-Nadia clue set** and no “100% known” completion.

The world needs a finite, useful ambient scene for orientation; an ordinary glance should be cheap; an elaborated inspection can take time. A verbose prompt should not itself consume 20 minutes of diegetic time. Repeat micro-glances can accumulate actual duration, divided attention can reduce detail, the observer can be observed, and a person being watched may become uncomfortable or not. A private letter, bank card, magnet, bill or medicine bottle has no magic PRIVATE/SAFE tag independent of actual placement, visibility, social expectation and acquisition route. Obtaining a detail by asking, innocently glancing, searching drawers or hearing a rumor changes its social meaning even when the factual content is the same. An attempt to inspect another person's resources does not make the information lawfully public.

ATTEND can be a psychologically revealing activity for the visitor, but only through actual observed patterns and genuine actor reception. Cover behavior (“I'm looking for something else”) is an attempted presentation, not successful concealment by declaration. A glance at a refrigerator magnet may be seen and misinterpreted; a person can half-notice or know without reacting. Their outward politeness need not leak a coded clue to annoyance. The player may learn as much from the pattern of their own curiosity as from the object, but the service must not make a pseudo-diagnosis based on one LOOK.

Material historical completion already belongs to original SOROK 60/61/70/80 and Mechanism I H0–H3, which protect backwards causality, root/branch consistency, causal slack and independently admitted detail. User attention can select where the world must become more specific; it cannot author the wanted outcome. The result may be boring, funny, painful or unexpectedly significant decades later. The user may interpret something about Nadia that she herself would not; that interpretation may later enter the world if shared with her, but cannot retroactively make it the fact she always believed.

This is a game of **judging what is worth noticing and when enough is known to act**, while the world and other people continue. It must be worth looking carefully without the app bribing every look with confession or mystery.


## 17. SAY, DO, ATTEND: genuine attempt, independent reception and consequence

Natural-language play must admit **attempt, execution, reception and consequence as separate things**. The visitor may say “I explain fractions to her child,” “I try to carry the groceries,” “I will repair the bathroom chain,” “I ask if she has anything to eat,” “I offer to pay,” or “I want to make Nadia feel safe.” Their words authorize **an attempt or plan**, not an accomplished result or third-party mental state. An action involving physical materials must respect the assigned body, actual tools, spatial reach, object state, duration and other people's rights. A meaningful explanation can be tested in the content of actual words; a claim of sincerity cannot force somebody to believe it.

The original H001–H190 estate is explicit: the user does not own the conversational floor; a long speech may be interrupted; multiple acts can overlap where the hands, gaze, posture, time and attention physically permit; the world may continue while the human is inspecting something; the narrator can describe actual consequences but not invent physics or resolve action outcomes according to dramatic convenience. SAY, DO and ATTEND are distinct *grammatical/intention classes* within one continuing historical event. An instruction combining all three is a plan with causal contingencies, not an omniscient screenplay.

For example: the visitor starts inspecting a troublesome toilet chain while Nadia warns that the bathroom is in use. They may fail the repair and choose to disclose or hide the result. They might offer to help a child with fractions while Nadia is cooking. The child might learn, remain confused, refuse the stranger's instruction or feel embarrassed. Nadia might be grateful for relief yet concerned about the stranger's intrusion. Another adult might hear the offer and react differently. An intended benefit is not proof of flourishing, and **an actual benefit need not be recognized or rewarded**. A theft may never be discovered; a false blame may damage a sibling relation; a later confession may transform the meaning of old events without undoing the fact.

Ambiguous materially consequential user prose must not be realized as a confident arbitrary deed. The existing action owner can clarify at the minimum decisive fork, conservatively limit execution, preserve alternatives or refuse without writing false history. In an extended plan the human should regain control at a real interruption, before an unanticipated risk, or where a new person's consent is required. The service should compress routine steps when legitimate, not demand ten clicks to pour a glass of water or treat every intention as success.

Narration is **phenomenal testimony about the presented world**, not a privileged psychologist explaining the actor's hidden motives. Speech volume, awkward gesture and tiredness may be observable; “she is furious because she was abandoned as a child” is not. The renderer must not add random hesitations to make someone seem human. A sincerely hidden state can leave no visible clue. A half-heard sentence can produce no response without proving no uptake or no care.

Outcomes must be causally stable enough to survive cold replay and the natural action's source cut; bounded randomness can be real but cannot let identical situations give opposite results because a different prose style was fashionable. The system has no mandate to implement universal physics, but cannot grant expensive services, shared property, transport, expertise or time because a sentence sounded helpful. An independently capable Other has first-person causal authority over their own response and refusal. The narrator records what happened after competent owners decided it; it cannot decide what happened simply by choosing a satisfying sentence.

This is where the simulated action becomes a genuine piece of the visitor's own history. The human may have an intention; the world gives a partly independent result; the human must now live with what happened. The point is not to be maximally penalized for every slip but to participate in irreversible, learnable and sometimes reparable historical activity.


## 18. World truth, actor perception, human perception and historical materialization

The old Birkenstocks example already showed the crucial mechanism. Nadia's sandals can appear in a prior photograph. Their acquisition, repair, ownership and material history can be completed under H0–H3 causally bounded historical materialization. The user may notice the picture, may recognize the sandals or may not. Nadia may remember, misremember, lie or simply have no special feeling about them. A later visitor in another branch can legitimately learn a shared root history where it is version-compatible, but one branch's intimate encounter cannot become everybody's precontact memory.

**Do not re-invent this as a missing historical simulator.** PR #1275 specifically corrects that failure: original SOROK 60/61/70/80, Mechanism I and P6 have already produced qualified historical depth and actor/observer distinctions. The task is source-conserving integration with the richer living person, not the addition of a second historical writer.

The same distinction applies to money. The world has objective resources. The visitor knows their own bar, not Nadia's actual balance. Nadia has her own fallible financial knowledge and her own reaction to the offer. If the visitor asks for a number, Nadia's response is a human statement subject to privacy, misunderstanding and social motive, not necessarily an exact account balance.

The same applies to current events. A public event can objectively occur without the actor encountering the news. A work closure can affect her wage without her yet knowing why. The user may know a different portion of the information and interpret it through an unrelated history. The narrator cannot give either an omniscient topical explanation just because the subject is interesting.

The same applies to the system's model of the external human. The service may build an evidence-graded profile of actual observed attention, speech and conduct, but Nadia's actor-local model can only respond to what she could legitimately perceive and infer. System knowledge does not automatically become character knowledge, and a human-user profile is neither a diagnosis nor a license to invent a user's hidden mind.


## 19. The family is a relation among relations

PR #1275 contributes a crucial original user challenge that cannot be lost in a money-and-portal-focused theory: a person's psyche is formed by the **relations among the significant Others themselves**, including the identifications, loyalties and antagonisms through which the person experiences those relationships. A family is not a list of individually scored mother/father/brother relations pointing at Nadia, nor a new “family mind” with a global mood. It is an historically developing field of actual coactivity, material obligations, care, discipline, recognition and **each person's relation to how others relate**.

Consider the directly supplied hypothetical family: a father seemed hardworking and insufficiently appreciated; his infidelity later changes how a daughter interprets earlier loyalty, the mother's complaints and the entire household's past. The prior labor was not retroactively nonexistent. The family's new self-understandings need not converge, and actual objective facts may be accepted, denied or distorted differently. An event changes some relations while confirming others; later choices can recruit new dependencies, defenses and identifications. If a newly generated explanation fails to influence any later practical choice where the conditions call for one, it is merely narrative decoration.

The user's southern Lebanese sibling thought experiment is even stronger, and should be preserved **as an individual hypothetical case, never as a population stereotype**. Nadia may identify with certain Western art, music, freedom and imagined futures, while her brother identifies with responsibility for their mother and a male role made salient by a largely absent father. They can love each other enormously, share intimate ordinary life and nevertheless be in active conflict because his sincere protective obligation reproduces restrictions that she experiences as domination. Their mother can love both, have independent interests and be affected by material stress without becoming the medical consequence of Nadia's defiance. The original user sentence in PR #1275 ends unfinished; its ending must not be fabricated. The adolescent details in that prompt belong to a bounded historical formation thought experiment, not to a requirement for explicit depiction of minors' sexual activity.

This makes the first prelude more than a dyadic event selector. It may be caused by Nadia's relation to a sibling's expectation, the mother's need, a child's homework, a friend due for dinner or a former partner's behavior. A person is already inside a social field when the human arrives. The other people have their own actor-local histories and cannot be puppeted to provide the visitor with a moral demonstration. The same-SES visitor is economically homologous but does not inherit the family's internal life, and can easily misunderstand it.

The family's effect on Nadia is not only what she consciously tells the visitor. People can acquire a pattern of feeling and acting through care, scarcity, authority and repeated conflict without articulating any theory of it. The Constitutive Organism must make these acquired relations **operative in future activity and new situations**, not merely store all the intersubjective edges and narrate them beautifully.


## 20. The iceberg operates through situated activity

The Constitutive Organism is not the psychology section of a story engine; it is the ongoing **historical formation of a partly opaque person**. Bodily needs, practical capacities, social roles, motives, memories, conscious interpretations, inhibited identifications, defensive organizations, contradictions, habit and possible unacquired forms are all situated in actual activity. An LLM can propose bounded personally meaningful interpretations, new differentiations or historically plausible acquired organizations, but these proposals become real within the fiction only through competent actor/world admission and later operation.

A person may be strongly moved by a visitor's joke or ignore a supposedly profound confession. They may experience care and resentment at once, or form a habit through boring repeated work. Their actual outward response is not a readout of a privileged hidden personality chart. A woman can be friendly because she likes the visitor, because she is trying to avoid an argument, because family norms demand hospitality, or because that is her ordinary practice, with several reasons genuinely intertwined.

The first contact must be sourced from that operative organization, not from a trait label assigned after a perfect scene is written. A private person can have a pressing material reason to ask an unfamiliar neighbor for food. A highly sociable person can be temporarily exhausted or angry. The model must permit each without constantly coding someone as introvert or extrovert. Particular events recruit particular acquired organization under present constraints; present behavior cannot be reverse-engineered into one correct conscious motive.

The narrator can report what is visible and audible. Nadia may tell a sincere but false account of why she did something. Her mind may not be able to say what is actually operative. The service's private understanding is not her personal knowledge, and the human's model of her is necessarily fallible. If the user asks why she asked for rice, a response is her **testimony**, not the engine divulging its complete causal graph.

Psychological richness therefore arises from real differentiated causal persistence, not an obligation to display deep interiority each time the human enters.


## 21. Fictional adult backfill and Nadia's open development share one psychological circuit

The source corpus has **two entrances into one continuing psychology**, and a NEIGHBOR theory that forgets one is not complete. Fictional adults—especially parents—may be constructed from PYAT-conditioned, whole-survey-respondent-linked distributional constraints and credible historically compressed backfill. This can set class, material position, household variation, belief co-travel, biography and early acquired dispositions without treating a survey respondent as a psychological scan. A child such as Nadia begins with a body, maturational capacities, dependency, need, affective/sensorimotor repertoires and social surroundings; there is no predetermined adult destination and no secret final personality card to reveal. Their lives later enter a common psychic circuit, but their construction routes have different evidentiary limits.

This circuit is not just “LLM personality generation followed by dialogue.” Actual need and practical situation recruit a partly inaccessible, historically formed organization; competing dispositions/objects and bodily capacities condition what is possible; the actor proposes or undertakes activity; world and Others independently respond; consequences are actor-locally experienced, remembered or not; new distinctions, capacities, identifications and inhibitions can be acquired and may alter future recruitment. Ordinary work and relationships can reproduce the same patterns for years, and a seemingly profound event can fail to reorganize anything durable. The person is not an independent trait vector with a background story attached.

Theoretical sources from Leontiev (activity/motive/object), Sève (socially formed capacities), Lahire (plural sometimes inconsistent dispositions), Dreier (participation in everyday contexts), Holzkamp (constraints and expansion of agency), Luria (development in sociocultural practice) and traditions of psychoanalytic/unconscious organization can challenge one another here. No single theory supplies a scientifically calibrated numerical psyche. Their actual overlap is the requirement that historical social relations and mediated activity become **operative** in current behavior and future formation. Models can propose new psychological meanings; world owners must reject illegal causal assertions; actor owners must evaluate genuine recruitment/construction and allow NO_CHANGE without the expression model forcing depth.

The family field is decisive. Nadia's attachment to father is partly mediated by what father does for mother, by the brother's relation to mother, by kinship's distribution of care and power, by institutional expectations, and by how Nadia believes those relations work. Each family member has their own actual history and model of the others. A father's affair, a mother's illness, an absent father forming another household in Sweden, a brother's sincere care that reproduces restrictions on his sister, and an adolescent's Western identification can all become contradictory reality inside one network. No family global “harmony” scalar, population identity stereotype or one interpretive flip can substitute. The user-supplied Lebanese thought experiment remains one particular unfinished *scenario*, not a completed biography of a real population.

Independent Others need **graded depth without graded person-rights**. Nadia and her formative, recurrent Others deserve deeper, causally renewable constitution; incidental actors can have bounded but real activities, material capacities and limited epistemic viewpoints. Promotion to deeper representation should follow actual repeated causal significance, not how much the visitor wants a fascinating side character. A peripheral worker who can refuse or lose a shift is not a puppet even if the system cannot afford a full multi-year subjective biography for them. Their histories should be enough to support genuine constrained action and later lawful promotion when they become constitutively important.

A real public event can change the material world without anyone reading the news. A caregiver relation can become different because another household member sees a betrayal. An actor's private meaning can change without dialogue. A person may form a competence through repeated labor, then bring that competence to the next encounter. The system has succeeded in psychological construction only if such history changes future possibilities and responses under qualified causes—and has not simply stored a lovely interior explanation.


## 22. The person can reread and reorganize the past without changing what happened

The original SOROK research goes beyond ordinary “long-term memory.” There is objective historical occurrence; each person's selective memory and associations; retrospective reinterpretation; self-narratives and private myths; identification with models of how another evaluates them; avoidance, defense and alienation; and a changing ability to read earlier events. **A past fact can acquire a new meaning without the past fact changing.** A daughter may have genuinely admired a father's hard work and later discover an affair. Her admiration at the old time remains true; the new fact may reorganize who she believes she was, whose suffering she ignored, whether a brother's loyalty seems noble or complicit, and what father now means. Several of these changes can disagree without an arbiter producing one master self.

An actor's belief that they once acted out of courage may later become a story about fear, or vice versa. A recollection can be incomplete, overwritten in gist, reactivated by a mundane object, or remain partially resistant to reinterpretation. The person may tell a flattering story, an accusing one, two incompatible stories, or no story. Internal reformulations can arise through care, shame, practical success, ideology, religion, exposure to another person's account or institutional pressure. **Conscious insight does not by itself equal durable psychological reorganization**, and a defense does not have to wait until the conscious subject decides to conceal something. A valid externally quiet response is not a missing piece of narrative.

In relational terms, these are not merely changes in beliefs “about events.” They are changes in who the person can be while inhabiting a role: a daughter, a coworker, a friend, a woman negotiating freedom, a brother whose protection has become control, a user who now approaches Nadia's silence differently. An adopted judgment can create new restrictions; an apparent liberation can form a new dependence; a newly articulate criticism can coexist with unchanged habit. The historical SOROK questions distinguish **capacious revision**, **tyrannical appropriation**, **partition**, **ignoring**, **cracking**, visible contradiction, preservation of counterevidence and incomplete synthesis. These are source hypotheses to be tested, not a license for a universal algorithm of healing.

The person's new articulation has no privileged power to rewrite the whole psyche. A model proposal must specify which events, motives, embodied practices, external relationships or accumulated contradictions make a new organization plausible; how strongly and in what setting it can become available; what competing explanations remain; what later behavior would distinguish it; and whether nothing changed. A later account can be mistaken while still becoming causally real for subsequent action. The object of modeling is not the perfect true autobiography; it is an operative, fallible, revisable relation between memory, experience, social history and practice.

The external human has analogous interpretive capacity without being simulated as a complete generated psyche. They may rethink an old interaction with Nadia after seeing a photograph, hearing another version, encountering a financial disparity or learning about a sibling. That interpretation is an actual human event, not automatically a fact in Nadia's mind. If communicated, it can be a new stimulus; she can find it illuminating, offensive, ridiculous, self-serving or indifferent. The relationship itself may change what each can become while remaining **more than the sum of either person's account**.

That is why “Nadia has an iceberg” is insufficient. The iceberg must acquire and lose structure through life, and its internal representations must remain partial even to Nadia. A richly narrated memory with no future causal consequences is not necessarily evidence of psychological formation. A mundane habit that later changes the person's work, tenderness or fear may be stronger evidence of the organism.


## 23. Quietness, stasis, love, rupture and other natural consequences

SORACLE-CO's challenge must remain a positive capacity of the system, but the user forbids steering toward it. A person can change over months of routine work or quietly gain skill through repeated practice. A profound visit can leave no durable psychological mark. A small incident can change the practice of a family member the user will never meet. Nadia might learn a technique from the visitor and later no longer need their assistance; she might nevertheless want to keep spending time with them. She might love someone deeply and move away for another obligation. She might remain closely attached and make that relationship a major center of her life.

**No causal target prefers intimacy or independence.** The world should be constituted such that these trajectories can result according to historically formed conditions. A program that deliberately manufactures Nadia's other relationships to demonstrate she is not about the visitor is as false as a program that grants affection when the visitor spends money. This is what “the portal is about Nadia; Nadia is not about the portal” means.

The user's game can be intensely organized around wanting more of a particular person's time. The Other can respond positively, negatively, ambiguously or not at all. Their willingness is a world outcome owned by them. “Good at Humaning” is an ordinary human desire to recognize, participate, respond and sometimes improve at relations—not a secret score that the service awards. A caring act may provoke resentment; a manipulative act may succeed socially; a kind but thoughtless person may not notice what mattered; a stranger may develop genuine companionship without ever having gone through a moral improvement arc.

The LLM's goal must not be to make every day dramatically consequential. Some periods reproduce existing organization, sustain material conditions and reveal nothing narratively novel. The correct organism can nonetheless carry a rich body, relations and institutions into the next day.


## 24. Her day continues in absence; the user's fabricated actor does not

Nadia's fictional day is a historical interval in which things actually happen or continue, not a recap generated to flatter the returning visitor. She might work, eat, sleep, care for relatives, answer a message, experience boredom, attend a religious or political meeting, encounter a public event, make a private decision, or simply maintain routines. Some events call for deep LLM-supported actor recruitment and composition; others can be carried through bounded, provenance-maintaining compressed activity. The stored event/world facts and actor afterlife, not a vivid paragraph, make the day real.

The latest user idea makes the first encounter itself a temporal operation: **at the moment the player opens pre-existing Nadia, find a plausible social bridge from what she is currently doing**. This may include a short bounded fictional preceding encounter if the visitor's preface requires it. A generated opening belongs to the branch's objective history once admitted. It does not create a time machine that overwrites Nadia's common pre-contact past.

Nadia's world time and the human's real calendar are not simple synchronized clocks. Elastic rendezvous can allow the user to enter a diegetic appointment through a humane availability interval while holding dependent events causally pending; the fictional person does not know the platform's grace window. Genuine missed commitments can matter, but app inactivity alone cannot become an accusation of betrayal. The service may not manufacture “she misses you” pressure merely because a retention alarm fired.

Offscreen work on the human's local shell is limited to established nonvolitional maintenance; the system cannot impersonate actual external human speech, love, apologies or career choice while they are away. The entry-prelude license is a **one-time bounded initialization**, not a loophole for perpetual user autopilot.

A person can have a day whose most important cause never enters the visitor's awareness, and a visitor can have an experience that changes their actual real-world thought while Nadia hardly changes. The simulation must not symmetrize those afterlives for narrative satisfaction.


## 25. While the human acts, independent Others have competing activities

Nadia can be cooking while her child wants help with homework and a friend calls. The visitor might offer to handle dinner, teach the child, decline involvement, make an unhelpful remark, or leave. Every participant has their own current concerns and capacity to act. The child is not a scene prop. Nadia may wish to help her child herself, resent another adult's intrusion, be relieved by it, or barely think about it. The physical pan, food, time and material costs do not wait for one correct dialogue option.

The human can exercise actual abilities that the text medium supports: asking a useful question, giving a concrete explanation, reasoning, remembering a prior answer, negotiating a plan, making a joke, noticing a contradiction or choosing silence. The system cannot convert “I help with fractions” into an automatic learned skill without the relevant explanation, child reception and world effect. Physical/manual work such as lifting, driving and cooking must be resolved through the locally assigned body and actual affordances; one cannot declare success because the intent sounds moral.

The source D002 demand for task-entry decompression matters here. A general desire to help can open the practical situation and return control to the user before committing a demanding result. Routine labor may be compressed once decisions are genuinely understood; that avoids making the person's home into a tedious click-based simulation. Meanwhile other people and the world retain their own initiative.

A missionary can knock because of their actual neighborhood canvassing, not because the user likes theology. A friend can arrive because she was already invited. A phone can ring because a workplace has an actual problem, not because someone must interrupt the player every five minutes. No universal drama cadence is required.

The game is meaningful because **finite human attention and action enter a situation already containing other activities**, not because every task has a positive or negative relationship-point result.


## 26. Public events enter history through provenance, exposure and situated understanding

A real contemporary event—a transport strike, storm, law change, military action, election result, hospital closure or economic price shock—can enter a simulated person's world if the public factual claim is verified in the appropriate geographic/time scope and its effect is materially relevant. It is not a substitute for a synthetic person's private history and does not turn Rawlsing into a digital twin of every living city, household or named individual.

The source chain is distinct: verified public occurrence → relevant world effect or eligible exposure → specific person's channels and opportunities → partial perception → actor interpretation and possible belief → practical activity and potential psychic afterlife → outward expression and user-accessible evidence. A public news report does not force a political opinion into Nadia's mind. A person can interpret it through their actual class, work, kin, religious practice, media routines, history of war or everyday fatigue.

In the source-conserved example, a visitor's casual comment about a famous entertainment event might be received through Nadia's historically personal knowledge of violence and cultural alignment, or not. She can be angry without explaining, outwardly polite while privately hurt, or genuinely uninterested. She may reject the visitor, withdraw subtly, argue, laugh or redirect the conversation. The service cannot script Palestinian, Lebanese, Muslim, atheist or other identities into compulsory responses. Material conditions and actual acquired ideology matter; national or religious labels are insufficient.

The same source-qualified public event can be offered to differently developed branch worlds, but its actual relevance depends on each branch's location, routines and history. A person who moved no longer lives next to the incident in the old neighborhood. A family illness may have diverged under independent fictional paths; one cannot determine medical outcomes from a player's ideology. No background ingestion of “latest news” authorizes arbitrary dated world facts.

The first-contact proposal can be conditioned by real public circumstances if actually relevant: a blackout may cause neighbors to gather, a weather event may change travel, a local closure may redirect schedules. But it cannot manufacture a news crisis as a dramatic meeting device. Most of the time, a meal or brief greeting is sufficient.


## 27. Sexuality, exploitation, consent, care and harmful success remain real

Not all relationships are uplifting, and the new first-contact premise must not become a respectability filter. Nadia may be interested in friendship, sex, paid work, solitude, religious life, cultural experimentation, protest, family care, a mutually uncomfortable arrangement or a practical exchange. Some configurations may involve coercion, dependency and unequal power; some persons may enjoy unconventional adult sexuality without that enjoyment proving pathology or immunity from harm. Their histories and current material possibilities determine what is causally possible, and actual individual consent remains separate from a player's desire.

A family member may condemn a choice, support it, remain unaware, supply material support or exploit it. There is no universal moral chorus, even where serious domination occurs. Nor is there a universal psychology of a poor woman automatically traumatized by sex work, a rebellious teenager automatically enlightened, or a traditional brother automatically violent. The source-conserved Lebanese family example is a specific actor-field hypothesis involving love and contradiction, not a character-template law.

The world can preserve harmful consequences even where the perpetrator experiences success or the victim describes mixed pleasure and injury. An abusive visitor cannot simply author the other person's consent through dialogue. But the simulation must not mechanically punish every moral fault by making the person withdraw; actual harmful dependency can occur. This is another form of rejecting a hidden morality score.

The same-SES start reduces an easy outsider-wealth shortcut to domination or rescue, but does not erase gender, law, networks, debt, institutional power or future material divergence. The resources bar can only show the user's local burden; it never proves the ethical status of a sexual, economic or caring arrangement.

The portal's fundamental object is a person living under historical conditions, not a safe therapeutic exhibit arranged to teach an ethical doctrine.


## 28. The shared root and different visitors' private persons

The shared seed-person bank gives users a common initial historical person and a social opportunity to discuss divergent experiences. User A may encounter Nadia-A as friendly; user B may experience Nadia-B as rude; their differences can arise from distinct first contact routes, later privately diverged world events, different user actions and separate perception. The common root is not a single simultaneously multiplayer psyche. Once significant contact has occurred, there are causally distinct continuities.

The developing idea of a **common user-unaffected reference Nadia reaching today's date** refines, but does not erase, that design. The root and reference can have shared admitted world/precontact facts while each user receives a fork-local first encounter from a current-day qualified actor situation. The reference's own development must never be contaminated by an individual visitor's branch history. Multiple people starting at different dates may have different initial Nadias even where both arise from one ancestry, because one reference day is later than another.

The bank also raises a true historical-completion problem. A user's ATTEND can select the locus where unresolved but causally eligible precontact detail becomes specified; their wishes cannot choose its content. The new detail may become root-shared to compatible forks, but cannot retroactively rewrite the lives that were already committed under conflicting facts. Version, source, admission and causal slack constrain the result; no omniscient model simply declares the past was always as the last viewer preferred.

Outside the game, real human users may discuss their respective Nadias. That conversation changes human knowledge, not automatically Nadia-B's actor-local awareness. If one user introduces an externally learned fact to their own Nadia, that is a fresh user-authored world event and can have its own social meaning.

The community should not be organized as a leaderboard ranking the most successful conqueror of a person. Its extraordinary possibility is comparative testimony: many different true fictional branch encounters with a common historical possibility, none exhausting who that person could become.


**Undo, retry and irreversible history.** A visitor's first misunderstanding is not a reason to make the service unusably punitive. But “try ten different confessions until Nadia's trust bar fills” would turn her into a controlled branch puzzle. Original Q-RETRY-001 and shared-seed sources preserve a genuine open design question: what can be restarted, when may the visitor enter a new independent continuation, what history is retained for themselves, what counts as a technical correction, and which actions cannot be undone inside a committed branch? A branch snapshot is not moral permission to cancel another person's admitted refusal or pretend the actual user never said something. An irreversible lived event can nevertheless be forgiven, repaired, revisited and differently interpreted. Exact save, restart and fair reroll policy is **not settled**; it must preserve both user exit and actor historical nonownership.

## 29. The scarce Roll cannot purchase a companion

The user's eventual Roll is deliberately distinct from simply clicking a compatible character or reloading a failed conversation. They may have one or two scarce opportunities under an as-yet undecided compute/funding policy to **cause the generation of a new source-qualified fictional person-root**, which may later enter a shared bank for others' independent continuities. The human initiated the generative event in product time; they do not author the fictional person's historical birth, choose their ethnicity and class for consumption, claim their sexuality, or gain authority over their future willingness.

PR #1275 additionally preserves earlier possible business models: periodic free rolls, optional fairly priced additional genesis, perhaps a narrowly controlled second opportunity after immediate exclusion, delayed shared release and opt-in credit for who commissioned a root, plus possible congruent sponsorship. These are **unsettled alternatives**, not adopted monetization. Treating unchallenged brainstorming as user approval of a paid personality market would be a clear no-drop failure. What is controlling is nonownership, person-root sharing conditions, genuine scarcity under actual computational limits, and refusal to sell intimacy or compliance.

There are three distinct permissions: introducing a real human into the service, introducing a person-root to another user, and commissioning a new root. None is automatically interchangeable. A limited initial pool of one, three or five deeply constituted persons can seed discussion without pretending to be a population-level statistical lottery. Moving toward broad distributional sampling must meet actual empirical coverage and conditioning standards.

Scarcity must not become an addiction loop of pay-to-reroll, simulated abandonment, manufactured emotional rescue or paywalled contact with an existing fictional relation. A person can reject the person who commissioned their generation, and that rejection is not product failure automatically warranting a friendlier replacement. Equally, the user can leave without a synthetic person becoming moral blackmail.

The roll is a deep part of the encounter conception because it makes the unchosen life a **human-triggered existence without human sovereignty over its outcome**. It remains a design requiring actual service economics and normative accountability, not a magic random-person vending machine.


## 30. The platform's power must not become Nadia's motive

The external service allocates compute, latency, memory, access, start-date mechanics, data retention, personal-model inference, scene-selection probabilities, filters, financial bar projection and future monetization. These decisions are not neutral. “We do not optimize Nadia toward the visitor” is an important restriction but not proof of a morally or epistemically unbiased product. The platform's distributions can favor theatrical traits, normalize class assumptions or select “plausible” first contacts in ways that materially alter user experience.

This is why the late encounter proposal must not become a psychological targeting engine. Generating a lost-car-keys episode because the actual user likes practical puzzles, or a religious doorstep confrontation because the user likes political theology, would make Nadia's life a retention algorithm. The event should be conditioned by **Nadia's own historically formed situation and a legitimate minimal neighbor shell**, not the external customer's attraction profile.

The platform must also not suppress actor initiative to prove that she is independent of player desires. If she happens to be affectionate, curious or hospitable, those are possible. If she is closed off or coercive, those can also be possible, provided the world keeps proper actor and third-party consequences. The simulation cannot treat every alternative as an ethical or commercial prize.

The model represents fictional persons, not verified conscious entities or measured social science. The actual human's emotional engagement may be profound, while the character's pain is a computationally represented event rather than an assertion of real suffering. That asymmetry does not excuse designing the service to exploit the user's attachment, nor does it require fictional persons to behave like compliance assistants.

The most honest product goal is an intrinsically compelling, materially plausible experience of another particular life, without making the user's attention itself a thing to manipulate for retention.


## 31. The experienced game is closeness; the world is not an intimacy optimization

Earlier passes exposed a false dilemma about what makes this **a game**. Round Four correctly recovered the user's immediate experiential orientation: *the game is getting close to the Other*. Round Seven corrected the danger: closeness is the **user's reason to play**, not a machine objective that all Nadia's motivations must serve. In the local experiential frame, time, repeated contact, mutual curiosity, trust, invitations, shared work, awkward negotiation, rupture, repair and newly possible intimacy can make the game feel as if it has progress, loss, stakes, anticipation and surprise. That is not an artificial relationship meter; it is a historically changing set of concrete possibilities of participating together.

The world has no obligation to reward a user's good intentions with closer access. A person may become more available when the user behaves badly; an abusive arrangement may make intimate access easy; a respectful refusal may decrease time together; a deeply formative encounter may have no effect on willingness to see the user again. **Access, attachment, trust, flourishing, compliance, pleasure, dependency, health and real benefit are not equivalent**. These distinctions were explicit in the original charter; each can move in a different direction. We cannot call someone “healthy” because they enjoy a relation, or an act “good” because it increased closeness.

The strongest aspect of gameplay is that **the human's actual ability to notice, remember, understand, explain, negotiate and decide matters**. The interface should not decide whether the user “really helped” by checking a social skill number. If they cannot explain fractions coherently, the child may not learn. If they misinterpret a financial offer, they may create tension. If they act courageously, another person may or may not recognize it. A user who listens for five years may really become better at knowing this person; they can also use acquired knowledge to manipulate. Nothing scores the person as won.

The user can genuinely desire Nadia's willingness and can feel rejection, irritation, joy, affection, loss or grief. The design should not deliberately minimize access to prove an anti-attachment thesis. It must allow delighted affection, loving companionship, cohabitation, sexual desire between adults, real mutual trust and durable shared projects when the actor/world and both parties support them. But a game that simply maximizes these returns sells a responsive doll. A product that always contrives distance is the same manipulation with opposite sign.

The game's deeper question remains political without becoming a political board game: a human born into a different material world meets someone who could have been themselves but isn't, discovers the limits of their received categories, encounters real social needs and may become responsive to them. The world must support harmful actions, powerful people, institutions and consequences without guaranteeing edification. Sometimes a user will do a lousy job of knowing Nadia and she will still be kind; sometimes they will do well and she will remain unavailable.

**Failure is not a universal punitive condition.** It can be a refused invitation, a joke that falls badly, inability to help, a misunderstanding or simply an unremarkable evening. Success can be a beautiful conversation, a useful act, an ordinary meal, a friendship, an uncomfortable but truthful recognition or continued access to a life that does not stand still. Neither is a terminal state. The only durable “score” is the actual history that occurred; any user meaning attached to it remains their own.

The system cannot become more truly humane by adding an invisible ethical grade. Its strongest playable tension is **finite situated agency in a many-centered history**, for a real human who sincerely wants to know this particular person.


## 32. Original mundane, hostile and paradoxical cases keep the whole falsifiable

A theory that only speaks in kernels can smuggle missing concrete mechanics behind philosophical elegance. The original source corpus's mundane and hostile cases are not optional “examples”; each is an attack on a possible shortcut.

**The old toilet-chain incident.** Nadia mentions a sensitive bathroom chain. The visitor enters while the room has its own access limits, attempts a repair, may fail and may decide whether to disclose or obscure the failure. Her reaction is neither the narrator's moral commentary nor a guaranteed reward for help. Her annoyance could be fleeting, masked, cumulative or absent; the event can later matter if repeated behavior makes the visitor's unreliability a practical concern. A perfectly kind act can cause an objective material inconvenience.

**The refrigerator magnet and a bill.** The visitor notices a foreign-city magnet, then looks toward a bill. One can study the magnet openly, glance at the bill incidentally or fabricate an excuse. Attention takes time and is itself visible. Nadia may observe, be suspicious, be mistaken, or miss it. A magnet's origin may be ordinary, socially important later or completely insignificant. A private bill cannot be converted into an omniscient list of another person's financial constraints merely because the user typed ATTEND. The same factual detail acquired through different routes may have different effects on trust.

**The child and dinner.** Nadia is cooking while her child struggles with fractions. The visitor may offer a mathematically sound explanation, fail to help, take over dinner, refuse the request or remain involved in a different conversation. The child has limited understanding, desire, embarrassment and their own path; Nadia's gratitude might coexist with irritation that an unfamiliar adult assumed authority. Other obligations can interrupt the user's explanation; one event may benefit the child and stress the mother.

**Covert theft and false blame.** An object genuinely disappears. No character knows the true culprit. One person accuses another, which changes a family relationship. Later the visitor may confess, or never do so. The world's causal truth, each person's belief, and later reinterpretation remain distinct. A secret harm does not require a “bad player” banner or its moral closure; a confession can change relationships without restoring the lost time.

**An unsigned or anonymous gift.** Somebody materially benefits, with no knowledge that the visitor caused it; no intimacy reward can follow automatically. Conversely an expensive public gift may win immediate affection while creating later dependency or shame. Benefits and harms can be politically and psychologically real without being recognized or credited.

**Observation that costs the bus.** A human spends too long examining details while Nadia has to leave for work. She misses transport, loses wages or becomes annoyed only if the timeline and conditions genuinely support that outcome. Attention shouldn't be punished for being intellectually rich, but it cannot pause others' worlds for free. A glance should remain glance-time. A long autobiographical speech can be interrupted by a kettle, phone call or somebody who refuses to listen.

**A surprising political encounter.** The visitor remarks on a music award. Nadia may hear it through a history of displacement, family loss, religious culture or pop fandom, or not care. A private wound may become later estrangement without an immediate outraged monologue. Neither geography nor identity label is enough to program an opinion, and no verified current event automatically enters her consciousness.

**Financial negotiation over dinner.** Nadia suggests takeout. The visitor offers to pay and sees only a momentary preview of their own resources. She may accept, offer Dutch splitting, resist or reveal something about her finances. They originally entered at equal SES, but the program never announced that equivalence and did not maintain it after independent changes. A later richer person may genuinely fail to feel how much an expense hurts the poorer one; neither narrator nor economy UI lectures them about it.

**First-contact by her actual life.** An unfamiliar neighbor may have helped when she lacked rice, met while she was outside smoking, received skilled car-key help, or simply been warmly invited. The present-day reference Nadia could have been doing something else and no invitation may be possible. Opening the game authorizes a bounded prior fictional encounter but cannot force her to welcome this particular visitor after her conditions change.

Each case requires the **same** material world, actor-local history, event admissibility, observer surface, time and continuing reconstitution that governs all the others. The strong theory is one that produces different plausible outcomes across these cases without a separate bespoke rule that transforms each into a hidden test of virtue.


**No hidden ethical output score.** The old S0/health workstream explicitly distinguishes approval, health, attachment, dependence, enjoyment, flourishing and actual material benefit. None can be authoritatively compressed into a single scalar nor inferred from the player's retention. A game may benefit from qualitative user research into the feeling of meeting a stranger; a developmental psychological claim requires appropriate independent epistemic qualification. Genuine human love of neighbor may be an ethically productive horizon, but must not be used to train actors toward good outcomes or label a human “mature” because the model likes their diction. The source-negative case where an exploitative visitor gets the access they wanted must remain possible in relevant consenting-adult/harm-bounded fictional circumstances, with actual institutions and downstream effects rather than intervention by a moral narrator.

## 35. A set of negative courts, not a moral grading rubric

**Equal but unreadable:** if the visitor at entry sees a sidebar identifying Nadia's balance or a caption saying “same economic status,” the product has betrayed the epistemic design even if its numbers are accurate. If the expense burdens are actually different at entry despite identical rendered bars, it has betrayed the material design. Both the invisible equality and the real equality are required.

**Cost without psychic oracle:** offer to pay when takeout consumes a significant fraction of the visitor's flexibility. The own-resource bar briefly displays a contextual cost. Nadia may be grateful, annoyed, evasive, insistent on Dutch splitting or preoccupied. The cost projection cannot choose her reaction, and her reply need not specify exact money. An actor's actual financial condition is not inferred directly from her demeanor.

**Divergence without revelation:** after a branch-specific job loss or material gain, the same dinner costs different fractions of two now-divergent finances. The service neither rebalances nor publishes a “class change” banner. A human can misread the situation. Later money talk can reveal or complicate it, subject to Nadia's actual knowledge and willingness.

**Today's Nadia, today's contact:** open the same source-root on two different days. The eligible intro should be governed by distinct current activities, location and obligations; the engine must not merely choose two arbitrary hooks. When no visit is plausible, the system must not rewrite her to make it plausible. An actor able to help with keys can do so only under actual material/skill conditions.

**Shared reference without shared visitor:** user A opens today and acquires an independently diverging Nadia. User B opens tomorrow against a qualified unvisited reference Nadia. User B's history must not include A's conversation or gift, though both may inherit independently admitted precontact root facts. A conditional public event can affect each according to actual local applicability. Do not require infinite precomputed openings as an implementation theorem.

**Bounded fictional consent:** an opening may say the neighbor had briefly talked yesterday and accepted a mundane Thursday invitation. That is a valid fictional premise of the voluntarily entered encounter, not a recorded external human confession. But once play begins, the system cannot invent major new user choices while absent. Distinguish initial accepted fiction from continuing unauthorized ventriloquism.

**Family relation-of-relations:** if a father betrayal makes every household member instantly and identically hostile, or if a sibling's identification with mother produces no later different practical behavior, the claimed historical family field is shallow. Likewise, deep family consequences cannot be inferred from one label such as “southern Lebanese” or “religious.”

**Silent change and no change:** a routine year may consolidate a practical skill without a speech about personal growth, while a transformative-seeming visitor conversation may leave no lasting organization. The engine cannot enforce one insight per day or one interruption per visit.

**Historical world truth:** an old sandal's photographed provenance must remain root-consistent under H0–H3 rather than be reinvented by viewer desire. External current events must be verified and actor-exposed before claims of knowledge. A private emotion cannot be narrated as fact simply because the system has an actor-recruitment record.

**Platform power:** test whether different first-introduction circumstances are actually selected for psychological user engagement even when the person/world would make another event more plausible. If so, the user's “Nadia is not about the portal” principle is false, notwithstanding a perfect invisible intimacy score.

**No-drop:** future authors who preserve a 757-row inventory and a 104-row conversation register but omit a direct controlling correction from the next whole have still failed. Whole-theory word count, beautiful rhetoric, green integrity tests and archival Git ancestry are not substitute proof of live conceptual preservation.

## 37. The unchosen person, living day, uncertain cost and first knock

A source-qualified possible person was born into material and historical conditions that no one selected for her. Her family has relationships with one another, not only with her. Her psyche formed through bodies, obligations, institutions, love, exclusion, care, labor, contradiction, imaginative possibilities and practical repetition. She does not have to be conscious of all that has made her. She is already living through a day, with a position in her world, when the external human chooses to open the portal.

The human is situated as **her exact socioeconomic and demographically homologous neighbor** at the moment of entry. They begin from the same ordinary financial burden and local material conditions. But the visitor does not see Nadia's economic account. They see their own resources when they need to decide whether a meal, ride, gift or other act is affordable. Her material life is not a second meter on their screen. Their foreign real-world mind remains theirs, while the thin neighbor position makes shared practical life possible.

A socially plausible event occurs from Nadia's actual world at this moment. Perhaps she has guests arriving and needs rice; perhaps she is smoking outside, knows a practical skill that helps a neighbor, or is genuinely disposed to invite somebody for supper. Perhaps the nearest lawful contact is a brief hello and nothing more. The user accepts the small fictional prehistory necessary to reach the scene by choosing to meet her. They have not thereby chosen how Nadia feels, why she acts or whether she will make time.

They come to her house or meet her outside. The day hasn't stopped. Cooking, children, family, another visitor, work, an appointment or the quietness of ordinary life continue. The human can help, ask, joke, argue, listen, neglect, leave or offer something. Their actual language and attention matter, but so do the independent Others and physical world. No score declares a good act or a moral achievement.

Nadia suggests takeout. The human offers to pay. They ask what it will cost, and the only special economic information may be a brief blink of **their own** resources bar. What it means for Nadia to accept, decline or insist on splitting is not encoded in the bar. It arises from her own condition and how she receives the offer. Both began materially equal, but neither was obliged to know it, and they may no longer be equal tomorrow.

If her income changes, the visitor might not know. If the visitor's circumstances improve, Nadia might misunderstand how easily they now spend. One person can miss another's pain precisely because the material expense has ceased to hurt them in the same way. It may become the source of a joke, a silence, a painful discussion about money, an unrecognized injury, or nothing durable. The system may not disclose the disparity simply to educate them.

When the visitor leaves, Nadia's life continues, and an independent unvisited reference Nadia can remain available as the source of new users' branch-local first encounters under a future, qualified temporal architecture. Shared historical roots and public conditions can remain common without merging different visitors' private histories. Later a scarce roll may commission yet another unknown possible life to the bank, without ownership of her outcomes. Actual humans may compare their experiences of the same origin, and those experiences need never converge.

The interface remains **deliberately about meeting Nadia**. Nadia's life, reactions, resources, family and future are not computationally optimized to welcome the visitor, punish them, raise intimacy or demonstrate independence. Her private life is neither a prize nor an ethics lesson. The only thing the service can legitimately provide is **an opportunity to participate in a world not authored for the visitor's gratification**.

**The ninth kernel: An unchosen person already lives in a materially and relationally moving historical world. The real human enters as her exact same-SES neighbor without privileged knowledge of her money, meets her through an occasion generated from her plausible present day and accepted bounded fictional prelude, and acts with their actual mind through a constrained local position. Her own world, body, Others and contradictory psyche determine the response. Both lives continue; equality may diverge without disclosure; effects may be private, indirect, intimate or nil. Shared reference, branch and root are separate. No relation metric, resource bar, scarcity economy, event scheduler or platform doctrine may bend her toward a desired outcome.**

There is a game because the visitor wants to know someone.

There is a world because that someone does not exist to be known.

**And the first knock, the price of dinner, and the things neither one of them knows have their places in that world.**
