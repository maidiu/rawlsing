## 1. The problem is not that Nadia might refuse us; it is that Nadia and we can change each other without knowing what occurred

The visitor enters because they want to meet a particular unchosen person. A growing acquaintance is a perfectly legitimate reason to play. But neither the visitor's desire nor the service's choice to organize experience around Nadia may dictate what she becomes, what she wants, or whether the relationship improves. A player can be capable, thoughtful, manipulative, careless or generous; Nadia can be affectionate, bored, drawn, repelled, dependent, annoyed or contradictory. There is no computationally preferred direction toward closeness and no inverse direction toward independence. Her actual condition and relationships produce what she can do.

Our earlier formula—“There is a game because the visitor wants to know someone; there is a world because someone does not exist to be known”—was true but too static. A world independent of perception can still be animated through a falsely sovereign narrator who decides what every action *means*. Even a pair of actor-local perception records can remain theatrical if they never affect activity. The stronger phenomenon is that living beings interpret one another with incomplete evidence, act on those interpretations, and thereby produce a world that later confronts them as objective history. An incorrect interpretation can be causally effective without becoming true. A correct observation may do nothing. One person may care without any other person recognizing it; another may feel accused by somebody who did not even notice them.

In a kitchen, the visitor glances at an overdue bill. Nadia thinks they read it and suspect she is struggling. Perhaps the visitor could not read a single word. They think her silence means she caught them snooping. Perhaps she is thinking about her child and never noticed. Both then choose how to speak, withdraw, offer help, change the subject, keep quiet or leave. Nothing requires a climactic explanation. Yet the encounter can acquire an afterlife through their different beliefs, ordinary schedules, financial relations and third-party interpretations. The event need not be important; its significance cannot be established by authorial insistence. The existing person and world must decide whether anything changes.

**The new kernel:** an independently becoming life has a body, material relations, values and history that no one standpoint exhausts. The encounter produces not one meaning but **several materially conditioned experiences, some false, which become new possible causes**. The game offers participation in this circuit, not ownership of the explanation.

## 2. Birth is the first condition of possibility, not a personality-picker's answer

“Roll to meet someone you could have been born as” remains the long human horizon. The rolled life is the unchosen alternate person, not the actual user rewritten into a local infancy. Class, land, household, languages, care institutions, migration regimes, religion, gendered and racialized hierarchies, war, bodily needs and accessible work are conditions of becoming rather than personality tags. PYAT may qualify joint distributions and conditional co-travel from actual respondent-level evidence and uncertainty. It does not reveal an original person's secret psychic iceberg, deduce every minority life from a median type or grant an LLM the power to convert survey correlations into causation.

The two construction entrances belong to one later psychology. Parents and other whole-survey-conditioned fictional adults can receive historically constrained compressed backfill; Nadia can begin as an immature, need-bearing organism developing through actual activity, caregiver labor, changing body and material-social surroundings. Neither route licenses a predetermined adult destiny. A fictional adult's backfill is not a fully observed documentary life; a child's developmental simulation is not a claim to have reproduced actual human ontogenesis. Both must feed the same historically mediated recruitment, undertaking, experience, social response and future constitution.

This recasts the roll's apparent randomness. It is not a vending machine that chooses ethnicity and income as aesthetic genre. It introduces one historically possible world-position whose actual relations continue to become. A small alpha of one, three or five **richly developed** people does not demonstrate the global distribution of birth; neither the users nor these persons are presumed wealthy. When statistically and economically qualified scale arrives, the user may confront repeated birth inequalities by meeting particular Others who are not demographic morality exhibits.

The visitor's historical alternative is not that they get to *be* Nadia. They are another mind entering a materially homologous position next to her; the difference between their external formation and her internal formation is the tension that creates the encounter. The strongest future version lets this difference appear through real practice, not a sign saying which civilization or class to identify with.

## 3. The first dependency gives a person an experience of responsiveness without making other people extensions of their needs

The independent challenge begins earlier than the first dinner. An infant acts upon a caregiver and something happens: food, warmth, delay, irritation, comfort, laughter, a changed work schedule. Care is actual labor performed by an Other with their own body, other bonds, limited time and institutional constraints. A child's cry can reorganize a household's night without the whole world becoming the child's possession. That first experienced responsiveness can help form later anticipation, desire, frustration, initiative, trust, vigilance or defense, but no single response automatically installs an attachment-style trait.

The child develops within **caregiver–caregiver** relations as well as caregiver–child relations. A mother's practical capacity may depend on wages, a partner, grandparents, transportation, social assistance, bodily exhaustion and who controls the household. A father's praise may be experienced differently depending on how he treats the mother. A sibling may become the practical interpreter of a family rule. The child's attempts themselves change what caregivers do and how the Others relate to one another. This is a many-sided historical field, not an infant or parent psychological dashboard.

The machine must therefore be able to represent a *performed* bid, an independent caregiver's constrained actual response, actor-specific reception, and the possibility that repeated relations acquire a new orientation in future contexts. There is no universal “need satisfaction → affection gain” law. A caretaker can lovingly fail; a dangerous pattern can also be experienced as care; an infant's capacity for exploration may form through recurrent ordinary availability rather than one dramatic foundational memory.

The conceptual attack falls on a common coding shortcut: admit a psychologically plausible APPROACH or WITHHOLD current, then describe its acquisition by an exogenous LLM explanation. **Eligibility is not genesis.** To earn an acquired disposition, a system must carry enough of lived attempts, bodies, relations, outcomes and later uptake into the construction proposal that the proposal could have come from this historical activity, and could have failed to form under another activity. Existing CO historical self-organization and relational metabolism are capital to extend, not nominal placeholders to replace.

## 4. Relations are not edges that point to Nadia; they are histories that change how every participant reads the others

A family is not Nadia plus mother, father and brother intimacy values, nor a single family mood. It is a bounded network of independently operating relationships and relation-about-relation judgments. Father's actual work and care can sustain the household. Nadia may admire him and judge that her mother fails to appreciate him. Her brother may be protecting mother against a danger Nadia does not see. Mother may be struggling with a burden neither child understands. A later revelation of infidelity does not cancel the earlier labor or retroactively make the original admiration fictitious. It can make old memories acquire new practical force while other memories remain true.

A person might reinterpret earlier loyalty as love, blindness, survival, conformity or betrayal, and can become rigidly attached to the new interpretation. Her brother might believe that fulfilling the “good son” role means enforcing restrictions on his sister precisely because he loves mother and Nadia. Nadia can love her brother and resist what he demands. The mother can value both children and fear a conflict without becoming a mere repository of family angst. Third-party conditions—income, father absence, community authority, school or work discipline—can intensify or diminish which relations become practically antagonistic.

The Lebanese sibling thought experiment in the source lineage is a *particular possible life*, not a statistical stereotype or script for a Muslim or Arab family. Nadia's love of a song or a Western cultural possibility may genuinely coexist with love of family; her brother may be neither one-note tyrant nor secretly liberal. The content of identifications is materially and historically acquired, cannot be inferred from nationality, and must operate through present conditions.

**A missing operation revealed by this court:** a later actor's model of *what an Other believes about a third party* can change their actual next undertaking, even when that model is false. The objective relation, each person's perspective, each model of the other's model and the actual sanctioning institution are distinct. The system need not simulate every possible nested belief in a full town; it must support bounded relevant motifs with exact actor-local origins and allow not knowing. Peripheral Others still have world practices, while their psychological depth can be promoted when their actual repeated involvement requires it.

## 5. Human beings put their powers into things, and the things can come to rule them

The external PR #1278's strongest conceptual challenge is to the HOME's implicit idea that objects are facts and actors merely attach meanings to them. A bill, an axe, a worn shoe, a religious symbol, an employment contract, a family home or a photograph is already **historically social**. It condenses labor, claims, care, class, institutional authority, histories of acquisition and competing valuations that can operate before anyone in the current room comments on it. “What does this object mean?” is not a cosmetic semantics pass over an otherwise completed world.

Consider borrowed-axe firewood gathered from a common forest. A person's time, bodily labor and use enter the wood. That investment can be a genuine basis for attachment and for feeling violated by another's appropriation; it does **not** automatically create legal private title to the forest or erase communal rights. The wood's history, property conditions, actor-specific valuations and outside sanctions must remain separate. Or consider Nadia's shoes in an old photo. They may have ordinary provenance, repair and accumulated family meaning without containing an encoded secret destined for the visitor to decode.

Here is the stronger dialectic: valuable human relationships can become **objectified** in material things, roles, social institutions and concepts. Objectification is not automatically alienation. A family meal, a wedding ritual, a child's toy or a shared song can carry real collective life. Alienation or reification emerges when the produced form confronts the people who made and sustain it as if it had independent unquestionable authority: THE MARRIAGE must survive though living partners suffer; the GOOD SON must obey though the practical act harms a sister; the MARKET knows best though people cannot eat; a sacred symbol is invoked to legitimate coercion. Institutions may enforce those forms through wages, marriage law, reputation, property, work and violence; the subjective feeling of authority and the actual material sanction are not interchangeable.

This is not permission to collapse Marx's specifically capitalist commodity-value form into every human attachment or call all symbolic significance fetishism. Capital's abstract labor, exchange and reproduction have historical specificity. Nor may the engine impose a general “de-reification” reward. A liberating interpretation can later become the new tyrannical abstraction; a person can refuse a sacred claim and remain materially dependent upon the institution that sustains it. The narrative is not licensed to explain all religion away or decree that God is or is not real. A fictional religious person's actual belief and the social work that belief performs are distinct things.

**The new functional demand is historical valuation-in-practice**, not a new second object grammar or ideology engine: recover material production and institutional rights; trace which actor has lived, learned or inherited valuation through what activity; determine whether a present object/ideal/institution cues an available practice or inhibition; settle what people actually do, with sanctions; and allow the outcome to reinforce, displace, split or leave unchanged their relation to that value. Existing original 53–56, PRE-A0 and V3 relational meaning are the producers to inspect before building.

## 6. A real psyche is a historical way of taking up the world, not a list of hidden propositions

Nadia's iceberg contains needs, bodily limits, skills, recurrent social activities, expectations, cares, ideological commitments, sensations, pleasure, defended identifications, practical habits, desired objects, fears, contradictions and things she cannot easily tell about herself. These are not finished traits waiting for a story cue. They are historically acquired organizations whose recruitment depends on a **specific encounter** with bodies, things and Others. The visible tip—SAY, DO, silence, refusal, work, generosity—is the finite activity of the person under those conditions, not a transparent expression of the innermost truth.

A strong LLM proposal about “the meaning Nadia now acquired” is still just a proposal. It cannot declare a motive because a generated scene would be compelling. Source-qualified activity has to make a new form plausible; actor and world owners decide what occurred, what she could receive, what she undertook and whether any reconstitution was warranted. Acquiring a possible end can differ from actually taking it up later. An unexpected practice can fail to change psyche. A recurrent shift can change practical expectation without producing an eloquent moment of self-discovery.

Multiple ends may motivate the same bodily action. Nadia bringing rice to the table can nourish a child, sustain hospitality, avoid shame, please a friend, satisfy her own hunger and defend an ideal of good motherhood. These ends may complement, delimit or contradict one another without an omniscient little executive choosing one “real motive.” A present event may recruit a historical current into actual action; it must not infer the historical formation backwards from that action alone.

Original Leontiev, Sève, Holzkamp, Luria, Dreier, Lahire, social reproduction and class research enter here as critical constraints on the model's explanatory structure, not a license to claim one settled science of all persons. Scientific claims about real people, especially conditional distributions and causal interpretations, need separate evidence grades. ## 9. The real human is situated as an exact peer in cost, but not an omniscient resident of Nadia's history

At entry the local visitor occupies **the very same SES as Nadia**—not a vague near-class neighbor, and not a secretly wealthy tourist. The socioeconomic and demographic homology conditions the assigned local position. An identical ordinary purchase carries an equivalent initial resource burden. That is a world truth, however, **not a disclosure to the actual human** and not a claim that their real-life family and formative experience match Nadia's. The thin position places an actual foreign mind into lawful physical/social coordinates.

Only **YOUR RESOURCES** can appear in the visitor's UI, and even that can be a brief contextual blink rather than a permanent HUD. Actual wages, work, obligations, rent, cash and costs are owner-maintained beneath the coarse presentation. If takeout costs a substantial fraction of the visitor's practical flexibility, a purchase preview may show it. Nadia's own balance, available resources, obligations and sacrifice are not automatically visible. The visitor can ask, offer, split Dutch, misjudge, bluff, decline or feel embarrassed; Nadia can answer, resist, lie, misunderstand or ask in return. No narrator guarantees that what would cost the visitor also costs Nadia at the present time, especially after their finances diverge.

The initial equal burden does **not** require permanent synchronized wallets. Jobs, pay, gifts, unemployment, care obligations, shifts, money and debts can change one person's situation independently. A person who finds a bill easy may not notice another's pain; a formerly equal peer may become embarrassed by a newly unequal expense. Their world positions affect what they can do; their individual historical valuations affect how they experience it. The system cannot flash a special compassion lesson saying “Nadia is now poorer,” and a politeness cue is not a secret account disclosure.

SES homology is exact as a **product constraint at entry**, but SES itself is multidimensional. A defensible existing-world implementation must decide which properties, purchase burden, work, housing, class relations and resource access are actually matched without rewriting Nadia's historical household or inventing an adult user autobiography. The old 18% takeout figure is an illustrative resources-bar display, not a settled denominator. It is unacceptable to claim matched SES because two decorative percentages look equal while real underlying purchase ability differs. Conversely, identical purchase burden cannot prove every legal, racial, gendered, bodily or kinship experience matches.

The unresolved portal translation is therefore an active source of productive tension. The player can try to say anything, including a false claim or true private autobiography. A stable authorized local-coordinate translator may render certain contexts intelligibly, but it may not quietly rewrite testimony, make lies true, or invent the actual person's convictions. Some expressions are genuinely untranslatable. Cultural habitus can come into conflict with local material position. The user must know what local utterance was realized when a meaningful translation occurred; Nadia's interpretation remains actor-local and fallible.

## 10. SAY, DO and ATTEND are ways of acting in one world—not separate magical verbs

The human must really reason, notice, explain, ask, negotiate, joke, bluff, withhold and decide. “I solve the homework” should not instantly make the child understand; “I repair the toilet” should not bypass practical access, time and skill; “I comfort her” should not legislate Nadia's reception. A broad intent can expose material affordances and return control at salient forks, while routine tasks can be compressed when no meaningful decision is being suppressed. Physical capability depends on the assigned local shell and world, while language and reasoning in the text medium can actually be performed by the external human.

ATTEND is not a library lookup that summons lore. It consumes finite attention under actual scene and temporal conditions. The world can offer ambient orientation and progressively more detail about what this visitor actually looks at, hears and historically knows how to recognize. Looking longer does not increase an object's hidden symbolism or force Nadia to reveal her psyche. A receipt can be blurry; a photograph can be familiar without meaning anything remarkable; a refrigerator magnet can remain ordinary.

**The visitor is also an object of others' perception.** This is the external #1278 challenge our former HOME treated too lightly. The visitor's body has a position, gaze direction, hands, timing, audible words, contact with objects and possible cover behaviors. Nadia may be cooking, occupied with a child, facing away or watching. A quiet glance may be visible; a spoken distraction may fail to conceal it. What Nadia *could* sense differs from what she actually attended to; what she attended to differs from her interpretation; what she infers about what the visitor knows differs from the visitor's real knowledge. The visitor may also misread Nadia's attention. No internal user ATTEND record automatically grants Nadia surveillance of the visitor's private screen, and no actor's suspicion is a privileged narrator fact.

The core event therefore requires distinct proofable layers: **the user's attempted utterance/action/attention → one world-owned physical occurrence → visitor-eligible sensory evidence → Nadia-eligible sensory evidence → independently acquired or missed perception → actor-local interpretations and models of the other's understanding → actor-owned future actions → actual world and psychic afterlife**. Some layers can be empty. An observed glance does not imply legible document content. A received speech signal does not imply lexical comprehension. A character's expression is not necessarily proof of inner state.

## 11. The two people can be mutually wrong, and their wrongness can become history

Take the dinner bill. It is an objective material artifact with a price, perhaps an unpaid claim, a time, a location and some history. The human glances quickly but cannot read it. Nadia sees the head movement from the kitchen and believes the human knows the amount. That belief might matter because of what she learned through prior experiences of financial shame, generosity or invasion of privacy. She becomes briefly curt. The human believes the curt reply means she noticed the attempted glance. In reality, perhaps she was already irritated by a phone call. They each act on evidence that is not identical and may be false.

The program must not adjudicate their errors into one accepted “true meaning” to improve the story. The objective occurrence remains: a body moved, eyes pointed, no exact amount was read, a reply occurred, a child was waiting, the meal still cost money. Nadia's belief that the human read the amount is **actor-owned subjective history**, not a world fact. The human's belief that she caught them is **user history**, not automatically a simulated hidden diagnosis. Each can become a condition of later practical conduct. If Nadia decides not to leave bills on the table, that is a new world action with objective consequences whose cause includes her possibly false belief. If the human becomes excessively apologetic, Nadia may interpret that as confirmation or have no idea why.

This is a deeper construction than adding more confidence fields to “NPC knows X.” What matters is the **provenance and practical recruitment of a belief about another's belief** under history: what evidence was physically available; who actually received it; which acquired expectation made the inference plausible to them; how that inference was or was not taken up in action; what independent Others then did; and what new actual experience followed. The system must be able to return **NO_CHANGE**, indifference, an unexpressed suspicion or a later revision without treating every scene as a psychological breakthrough.

A third actor may further transform the field. A sibling may believe the visitor was insulting, though neither principal perceived that. A mother may interpret the same dinner as generosity. Their actions can change Nadia's options without anybody discovering the original event's complete truth. Institutions may enforce a false report or a family reputation while the underlying bill remains unpaid. Historical causal effectiveness never makes false belief factually true.

This is the first **stronger operative demand** revealed by the external challenge: a joined event consumer in which the world and several situated interpretations evolve under one real chronology. If a sourcefree demo has two private character monologues but no later change in actual possible activity, it has not realized this demand.

## 12. Help, injury, gratitude and blame are not interchangeable forms of consequence

The most visitor-centered game would make assistance raise closeness and harm lower it. Removing the displayed affinity bar does not repair the deeper error if the hidden system still uses the other's approval as its outcome function. A real act can help somebody who never learns who helped. A kindly offered repair can damage the toilet. A visitor may steal an object without being detected and somebody else may be blamed. Later confession may change another person's model of the event but cannot change who actually took it. A child can benefit from practical tutoring while the parent dislikes the tutor; a parent may love the visitor but be harmed by them.

The separate layers are intent, admissible undertaking, world consequence, actor-local exposure, interpretation, institutional attribution, later activity, and the actual affected material position. Gratitude, guilt, perceived sincerity, law or relationship access cannot replace them. A dishonest person may enjoy temporary success. An offended person may still choose to invite somebody back. The program must not invent suffering as a lesson to the abuser, nor does it owe the abuser privileged access to another person's private consequences. A victim may love an exploitative arrangement, resist, be uncertain, seek support or experience independent conflict without a standardized moral trajectory.

People may become closer through shared meaningful practice, playful ease, desire or practical support. They may become less interested because of busy lives, changed projects, irritation, incompatibility, a new relationship or nothing special. An exceptional interaction can have no durable effect, while slow work, care, humiliation and mastery change a person. **The architecture permits these outcomes; it does not optimize toward any of them.** SORACLE-CO's anti-visitor-centered challenge does not authorize an independence-reward function.

The actual game is not “earn Nadia's trust so you may finally learn her secret.” It is wanting to participate in another life without obtaining a guarantee that one's participation will be interpreted rightly or rewarded. Historical agency includes causes one fails to recognize. A robust world can surprise the narrator as well as the user because its material and actor-local constraints deny an easy interpretation.

## 13. Life goes on at home, through messages, and through the institutions nobody sees

Nadia has a day even when the external human is absent. She works, sleeps, eats, cares for others, perhaps goes to school or organizes, receives money, uses resources, visits family, practices religion, enjoys an ordinary hobby or changes her mind. Some intervals call for a source-admitted deep actor event; others are legitimately compressed routines. Her present politics can be informed by verified public events only to the extent that her material conditions and channels make those events available. Real events do not automatically produce shared opinions or scripted argument.

The human encounter has two ordinary windows: **in her home or local physical space**, and **through a relationship-eligible messaging channel**. These are not different nonplayer chatbots; they are two modes of access to the same actor, possessions, obligations, finite attention and historical clock. A phone number cannot be granted just because the visitor wants to text; a message cannot arrive because the platform seeks retention; the person may refuse, delay, forget, initiate, misunderstand or remain silent. A thread may contain details the other person later recalls or misrecalls in person.

The house is never a static social arena. Food can burn; guests can arrive; a child may need homework help; a friend can call; a date can begin. These are not dramaturgical interruptions selected to ensure session engagement. They arise from independent schedules, actual events and people who continue their own practices. If the human leaves, the world does not pause. The visitor does not thereby acquire an invisible autonomous emotional psyche while offline; established material schedules and costs may continue, but significant new promises, apologies, intimacy and volitional decisions belong to the actual human.

Time elasticity and non-toxic return remain essential. A fictional seven o'clock appointment need not require the real human to log in at an exact wall-clock instant; permissible grace, held causal dependencies, optional strict synchronization and no-show meaning require explicit world versus UI distinction. Nadia should know whether the in-world meeting occurred, not the service's app-grace window. Missing a genuinely made promise can matter; not opening the app is not automatically betrayal. The human may end the relationship, leave, delete qualifying private data or return under the applicable rights without manufactured guilt.

Ordinary work and economics remain material, not a compulsory second minigame. Rent, wage periods, transport and exhaustion can condition plans. No fictional crisis should force the real human to pay money to save a simulated companion or to unlock access that her history does not permit.

## 14. Platform knowledge is not Nadia's knowledge, and user data is not a free natural resource

The platform can build a rich longitudinal, evidence-qualified model of how the actual human attends, speaks, reasons and acts. This is not forbidden merely because the user is human. But the model must distinguish recorded user-authored acts, what was actually shown, the service's own selection of possible scenes, uncertain learned tendencies and explicitly shared real-world facts. The service co-produces the evidence it learns from by choosing what the person was permitted to see. It cannot treat in-game reactions as proof of real-life diagnosis, political essence, trauma, sexual preference or hidden autobiography.

Nadia's model of the visitor is entirely separate. She sees only actor-legible conduct: speech, appearance, offers, movement, timing and actual world effects. She may acquire expectations through repeated encounters and be mistaken. The hidden system analytics and the human's private ATTEND do not leak into Nadia's psyche. Neither narrator nor actor may act as an all-seeing interface because a model has sufficient tokens to invent an explanation.

This condition makes **user privacy a material product right**, not an etiquette suggestion. Purpose-bound records, permissions, meaningful disclosure, export, deletion, retention limits, research separation and the ability to stop play all matter. A service cannot teach nonpossessive relations by converting the actual human's uncertainty, vulnerability and attention into a covert commercial asset. Nor can it infer the permission to reuse user data from how emotionally important a fictional person feels.

The platform itself is a social institution with power over sampling, model costs, character access, notification, time cadence, moderation, generation and wealth of source material. Its laws can contradict its professed theory. “The engine doesn't maximize intimacy” does not prove neutral event sampling; favoring an opener that appeals to a user's personality can turn Nadia's life into an engagement lure without any visible affection score. A faithful system makes its assumptions and boundaries auditable rather than letting “immersion” justify hidden capture.

A synthetic persona is a historical simulation, not a proven conscious person. The actual human's feelings are real. That asymmetry does not authorize the service to stage the fictional person's grief or injury for exploitative retention. The fictional world's causal integrity and the real person's data rights are separate forms of accountability.

## 15. A scarce Roll, a real human invitation, and a shareable historical origin are three different powers

The long-run Roll may give a human one or two scarce, qualified chances to initiate a new fictional person-root under actual compute economics and PYAT source permissions. The human causes the commissioning event in the product but does not choose the resulting person's family, class, psyche or future desire. A small alpha could introduce one, three, then five deeply developed persons before such a roll is economically feasible; that is a rich-person-depth claim, not that the persons or users are wealthy. A possible shared bank lets users compare independent continuations, sometimes disputing whether “Nadia was lovely” or “Nadia was awful” because they met her under different histories.

An **invitation of a real human** into the service, an **introduction to an existing source-root person**, and a **commission to roll a new root** are distinct entitlements. A friend or community may share knowledge of a person's bank root without granting possession of that person's private branch. A user's postcontact history must not leak through an invitation. A person-root can be shareable while a specific Nadia's current affection belongs only to that branch; this is not a universal character-conquest competition. No invite carries prestige rights over Nadia's house, sexuality or relatives.

Ideas for fair bounded rerolls, optional generation fees, sponsorship or periodic free rolls remain **alternatives**, not settled monetization. Revenue can pay for compute; it must never buy a more compliant psyche, emergency “rescue,” privileged disclosure of someone else's hidden interior, or a higher chance of romantic success. Scarcity may create the meaningful involvement of commissioning a particular possible life but cannot be manufactured into dependency traps.

The social roll also needs a non-fetishist reading of human value. Creating a bank root by clicking a button does not mean creating a possession. The platform and its users produced a computational object in which a historically plausible person's life can be encountered; that object should not return as an unquestionable economic authority demanding sacrifice to maintain its own availability. The user may care deeply for that fictional person while retaining their own living relationships, time and rights.

## 16. What would actually make the computation stronger than today's theory?

The productive intervention is **not** another master event/psyche system. Original P2–P6, Mechanism I source-qualified history, Mechanism II user-private finite gaze, Mechanism III actor-local perception and independent initiative, V3 relational formation, CAN/ADP, PFF/SDTF, PRE-A0, existing PYAT G4 and SOROKUDA source/consumer inventories and the Constitutive Organism R21–R27 already own significant parts of the circuit. NEIGHBOR R1/R2 has a candidate one-world ledger actor/visitor join on its own branch. The #1278 external theory specifically identifies a joined observer-to-observer gap with first-consumer tests. The governing question is **which existing producer/consumer has not yet carried the causal distinction into the next operation**, not which clever name deserves a new class.

A strong next joined first consumer is bounded enough to execute. The user meets a historically constituted Nadia at her home. She has a bill with material and historical provenance, a situated current activity and finite availability. The user utters a cover sentence and quickly looks toward the bill. One world writer admits only the physically realizable SAY+ATTEND occurrence and actual clock. MII separately determines what the visitor actually read. MIII separately determines what Nadia could see or hear, depending on pose, task and sensory opportunity. Neither receives privileged knowledge of the other's private attention. Nadia's historically acquired response may form an inference that the visitor read the amount even when no text was legible. The user may infer she saw them even when she was looking away. Then **one or both inferences may recruit subsequent actual actions**, or neither may matter.

This demands a sequence, not an invented “mutual suspicion score”:

1. **Objective prior conditions:** bill/source, position, readable text or unreadable distance, current finances, clock, Nadia's activity, prior relations and legitimate access. No user question authors these.
2. **Human-authored attempt:** SAY plus covert ATTEND with particular timing, possible motion and cover. The engine cannot instantly resolve “I successfully conceal it.”
3. **Single world occurrence:** authorized real utterance, gaze, hand/body positions and physical effects settled through the existing writer and source permissions.
4. **Different sensory opportunities:** MII visitor read access, MIII Nadia-side visible/heard signs, non-equivalence of notice and understanding, including zero exposure.
5. **Different lived interpretations:** historically grounded, actor-private inference, uncertainty, possible metamodels (“she thinks I read it” / “he thinks I caught him”) without converting them into world truth or secretly displaying them to the visitor.
6. **Operative afterlife:** later invitation, private bill placement, offer to pay, awkward silence, practical action, independent child interruption or NO_CHANGE admitted through existing actor/world/development owners.
7. **Adversarial replay:** cold store/reopen, duplicate request idempotence, no actor-private leaks, no cross-user branch contamination, and distinct results under counterfactual pose/exposure. Claims limited to actual executed code, not fictional proof of source-native psychological science.

An **additional value/history case** should be tied to the same consumer rather than added as a separate fetish module: the bill records objective resource debt and maybe historical work, but for Nadia it may signify an earlier humiliating experience, duty to family, a landlord's authority or merely an ordinary payment. These valuations must be traced to her historical activity; the user seeing the bill must not choose which interpretation is true. A later action should differ where a source-qualified valuation is genuinely recruited, not because the LLM names THE RENT an oppressive symbol.

A **hidden cause case** is the next attack: unnoticed theft affects resources, another family member mistakenly attributes it to someone else, the false accusation changes actual work and care, and a confession later alters attribution without editing the original event. This demands world/cause/knowledge separation already uncovered by original H087–H099/H140–H163, not an externally imposed lesson about honesty.

**Current owner boundary:** PR #1277's code tree does not contain the NEIGHBOR runtime or current Constitutive Organism owner files; they live on distinct draft branches. Inserting a second world writer or copying them without their dependencies, source checks and owner authority would be a backwards step. This whole reconstitution changes the exact *testable integration demand* but is not a claim of delivered integrated code. The next authorized material transition must reconcile actual branch HEADs/owners and adapt a single eligible consumer, then execute independent tests and record resulting new contradictions. Claiming sourcefree completed organism because a theory-home file was updated would violate the work's purpose.

## 17. The game becomes serious where nobody can know what their action meant

The first theory of NEIGHBOR wanted a person who had a life before the user and continued afterward. The stronger theory can now explain why this requires more than autonomous events. Human value and expectations have been deposited through lives into things, roles, law, family and ideology. Those deposits make some possibilities easy, others shameful or impossible. A historically formed psyche approaches them under actual material needs. Another actual human enters through a constrained, initially same-SES position but with a foreign mind and no special access to the Other's interior. Their attempted action has limited physical and conversational reality. Several situated people notice different things or fail to notice them, misunderstand one another, act on the misunderstandings, and live with effects that do not ask anyone's permission to become facts.

The world does not owe the user an explanation of which meaning was most important. Nadia does not owe the user truthful self-diagnosis. A family may act on false blame; a helpful act may not receive credit; a lover may be cherished and still left; a psychologically intense meeting may have no durable consequence. The platform must neither force enlightenment nor install absence of enlightenment as a more virtuous outcome. Its own economic and informational powers must be as subject to criticism as the fictional institutions it renders.

In its best historical form, **the game is the desire to know and be with a particular Other in a reality that you can help make but cannot comprehensively read**. That includes joy, jokes, food, desire, music, work, privacy, political struggle, family contradiction, inconvenient time, ordinary care and the possibility of harm or estrangement. It also includes a fundamentally reciprocal question: *What do you think I saw? What do I think you think I meant? Which of those mistaken ideas has already begun changing what we can do together?* The actual answer may be unknown to both.

The deepest developmental circuit is now:

**Unchosen material birth → dependent activity with independent Others → historical externalization of human powers into objects, roles and institutions → acquired, partly opaque valuations and capacities → situated overlapping activity → actual limited human participation → differentiable world event and several fallible receptions → independently owned action and material/psychic consequence → new historical organization and future recruitment.**

This is a stronger HOME only if the next first consumer makes the mutual error and material consequence operative. Until then it is a source-accountable **candidate**, not a working completed person.

**There is a game because the visitor wants to know someone. There is a world because that someone does not exist to be known.** And between them there is a historical relation neither can fully know, yet both can help change.
