# Sorok — Remaining Alpha-Spec Blockers Explained / Candidate Alpha Falsification R1

Status: EXPLANATORY_SYNTHESIS__CANDIDATE_TEST_CONSTITUTION

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Current product-spec blockers

After present-life, no-save, life-topology and disclosure adjudication, six ALPHA_SPEC questions remain:

1. current-world/present coupling;
2. outside-portal message time;
3. changing relational access;
4. person-to-person invite economy;
5. privacy/data constitution;
6. alpha evidence/falsification.

Life topology and disclosure now move primarily to implementation/architecture.

---

## 1. Invite economy means distribution rules, not friendship scoring

The open question is not whether person-to-person invitation is attractive; that is already provisionally adopted.

It is the concrete economy:
- who seeds the first cohort;
- how many invitations a user may ever have;
- whether outbound invite ability depends on relational warrant, time, or both;
- whether synthetic-person succession and real-human invitation use the same warrant or separate rules;
- whether inviter identity is shown;
- whether optional notes are allowed;
- what exact claim the invite page may truthfully make;
- whether an ignored/declined invite returns anything to inviter;
- anti-harassment/cooldown rules;
- what happens when press creates demand from people with no inviter.

Do not conflate:
`NEW SYNTHETIC PERSON ENTERS MY RAWLSING WORLD`
with
`I MAY INVITE A REAL HUMAN INTO RAWLSING`.

They may share a normative logic while remaining separate product events.

---

## 2. Privacy/data constitution is architectural, not checkbox privacy

Rawlsing may accumulate unusually intimate longitudinal material:
- every user utterance/action;
- disclosed autobiography;
- attention choices;
- relationship history;
- inferred praxis hypotheses;
- messages;
- mistakes/conflicts;
- possibly sensitive beliefs/identity facts the user voluntarily introduces.

The alpha needs a constitutional answer to:
- what is actually stored;
- what is merely transient;
- what user facts are never proactively solicited;
- whether off-platform data is ever imported;
- whether the user can inspect/export raw interaction history;
- whether they can inspect system praxis hypotheses;
- what deletion means for persona/world continuity;
- whether invite/referral metadata survives deletion;
- whether current-location/current-event coupling requires real user location (preferred answer: no unless separately needed);
- data retention after restart.

Zero-profile design strongly supports data minimization:

> collect what emerges through play; do not construct a pre-game dossier.

## 3. What 'falsify the alpha' means

An alpha test should articulate a claim that could be wrong.

Candidate proximal alpha theorem:

> **An unchosen, persistent, independently living generated person encountered through situated co-presence and later mediated contact can become intrinsically worth knowing, such that users voluntarily return because accumulated relational specificity and consequence matter—not because of scores, scripted cliffhangers, endless availability, or novelty rerolls.**

This is testable enough for alpha.

The larger spiritual claim is not.

---

## 4. Evidence that would seriously weaken/falsify the alpha theorem

Possible kill signals:
- users experience the persona primarily as a generic chatbot after repeated encounters;
- users do not care whether prior history persists;
- users routinely restart/reroll because losing the relationship feels inconsequential;
- ordinary scenes cannot generate curiosity without dramatic authorial bait;
- users prefer declarative fantasy actions to actual attention/inference/speech gameplay;
- users do not voluntarily re-engage absent notifications/rewards;
- the persona's independent agenda mostly feels like unresponsiveness rather than personhood;
- reciprocal inquiry feels like canned chatbot questioning;
- accumulated history fails to make later interactions noticeably more specific;
- users consistently optimize for hidden approval rather than trying to understand the person despite no visible score;
- task/world simulation overwhelms the relationship;
- latency/cost required for believable causality is economically impractical.

No single metric automatically decides the verdict; alpha should use convergent behavioral + qualitative evidence.

---

## 5. Evidence supporting the alpha theorem

Candidate signals:
- users spontaneously remember/person-reference details across sessions;
- they voluntarily return wanting to know what happened / see the person again;
- they describe the persona in specific, non-generic terms;
- they report changing an interpretation after later evidence;
- they care about what the persona thinks without a score telling them to;
- they experience invitations/refusals/silence as person-specific consequences rather than game gating;
- later callbacks/history feel earned rather than authored;
- users prefer preserving continuity over rerolling even when relationship becomes difficult;
- users use ATTEND/inquiry/speech in increasingly person-specific ways;
- users notice that the persona is also learning/modeling them.

These establish the experiential preconditions for the deeper Rawlsing thesis; they do not prove moral/spiritual transformation.

---

## 6. Present-coupling and message-time should probably be adjudicated together

Current strong direction:

- persona/world are anchored to the historical present;
- portal scene time follows simulated action duration, not typing speed;
- between portal scenes, life advancement should remain coupled enough to the real calendar for contemporary events to matter;
- exact minute-for-minute coupling and user-response obligation remain open;
- current external events enter only through relevance/exposure/admission;
- app absence must not become engagement-pressure machinery.

The remaining design problem is the mapping between:
`REAL ELAPSED TIME`
`DIEGETIC CALENDAR TIME`
`MESSAGE SEND/DELIVERY/READ/REPLY TIME`
`PORTAL SCENE TIME`.

---

## 7. Current recommendation for alpha falsification scope

Do NOT attempt to prove:
- increased empathy;
- spiritual growth;
- reduced prejudice;
- better real-world friendship;
- moral improvement.

Those require longer and more rigorous research.

Alpha should test whether the **relational engine is experientially real enough to deserve further investment**.

That is the right kill gate.
