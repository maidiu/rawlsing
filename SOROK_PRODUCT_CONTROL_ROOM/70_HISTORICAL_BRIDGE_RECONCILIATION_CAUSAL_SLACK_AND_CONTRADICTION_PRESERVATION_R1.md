## 0. Problem

Late ATTEND/inquiry may force previously unresolved pre-contact detail to become concrete.

If the resolved fact lies years in Nadia's past, it may have:
- persisted materially;
- entered memories;
- affected habits;
- acquired symbolic valence;
- interacted with later developmental transitions;
- appeared in photographs or routines;
- influenced later person-state.

Therefore late historical completion cannot be:

`GENERATE PAST FACT → WRITE DATABASE ROW`.

But the opposite extreme is also wrong:

`GENERATE PAST FACT → TOTAL-LIFE PSYCHOLOGICAL HARMONIZATION`.

Rawlsing must preserve both:
- causal/history integrity;
- human contradiction, contingency, partial self-understanding and under-explained attachment.

---

## 1. Core answer — backward constraint + bounded forward reconciliation

For a previously unresolved historical detail:

`KNOWN PAST CONTEXT`
+
`KNOWN PRESENT FACT`
+
`KNOWN V3 DEVELOPMENTAL CHECKPOINTS`
+
`KNOWN INTERVENING CANON`
+
`UNRESOLVED HISTORICAL SLOT`

→ generate candidate historical instantiation

→ assign typed causal residues

→ propagate only through affected later checkpoints/domains

→ compare against already-canonical later state

→ `ACCEPT / DOWNGRADE / MODIFY / REJECT / VERSION`.

Compact law:

> **Late historical materialization is a constrained historical bridge, not free backstory generation.**

The present constrains what past may still be lawfully resolved.

But:

> **constraint compatibility does not require total explanatory harmony.**

---

## 2. Causal slack: preserve unknown causal allocation

Distinguish:

`UNRESOLVED FACT`

from

`UNREPRESENTED CAUSE`.

A deep V3 life may already establish:

- a developmental transition occurred;
- its approximate period;
- its affected domains;
- several known causes;
- some causal contribution or contextual exposure remains unresolved.

Example:

`AGE 25–28`
- close friendship ruptures under Nadia's control rigidity;
- anxiety becomes increasingly costly;
- she becomes more open to practices/ideas outside her prior repertoire;
- control rigidity softens;
- self-observation increases;
- exact concrete practices/exposures partly unresolved.

This is **causal slack**.

A later-resolved meditation/Buddhist episode may lawfully instantiate part of that already-accounted-for developmental channel.

It does not newly decree:

> Nadia became less controlling because Buddhism.

Instead it may resolve:

> one of several experiences through which the already-established transition was lived happened to include meditation/Buddhist contact.

This lets late flesh become real without making user-triggered detail a new author of present Nadia.

### 2.1 Causal slack is a bounded budget, not a universal escape hatch

The transcript's stronger engineering warning is:

> **UNSPECIFIED != AVAILABLE FOR RETCON.**

Where late materialization relies on unresolved causal allocation, the remaining allocation must be constrained enough to reject overpowered insertions.

The constraint may be represented explicitly or derived from current authoritative state; do **not** infer a mandatory new slot object.

Relevant bounds can include:
- the already-established transition/change;
- affected domains;
- temporal window;
- approximate remaining contribution capacity;
- lawful contributor/source classes;
- exclusions on major new causes.

Illustrative shape:

`TRANSITION: reduced control rigidity`
- known contributors: friendship rupture, anxiety cost;
- unresolved contribution capacity: `LOW–MODERATE`;
- admissible late contributors: bounded practices/exposures/relationships;
- inadmissible late contributor: unrepresented conversion-scale ideological transformation.

Compact:

`UNKNOWN CAUSE != UNLIMITED CAUSAL BUDGET`.

See:
- SPD-0235;
- RISK-192;
- Q-HISTBRIDGE-001.

## 3. Contradiction preservation law

Rawlsing must not optimize historical completion for maximum psychological neatness.

People may contain:
- beliefs that conflict with habits;
- political commitments that conflict with intimate behavior;
- obsolete tastes they still love;
- objects whose emotional significance outlives the ideology around them;
- practices learned in a phase they later critique;
- sincere self-explanations that are incomplete or wrong;
- motives that remain mixed;
- unresolved tensions that never become explicit;
- attachments whose "why" is ordinary, contingent, or partly inaccessible.

Therefore:

> **HISTORICAL COMPATIBILITY != TOTAL PSYCHOLOGICAL HARMONIZATION.**

And:

> **CONTRADICTION IS OFTEN GENERATIVE STATE, NOT DATA ERROR.**

The engine should preserve contradiction when:
- no hard fact is violated;
- the contradiction is plausible under Nadia's history;
- different domains or time periods can lawfully retain different logics;
- the contradiction itself may become future relational material.

---

## 4. Hard inconsistency vs human contradiction

### Hard inconsistency — reject or repair

Examples:
- Nadia canonically lived in Beirut in June 2018, but new history places her in Peru continuously that month;
- the rug was allegedly inherited from a grandmother after a death date that has not yet occurred;
- a new event requires Nadia to have been Buddhist clergy despite already-canonical incompatible life history;
- a late fact would have materially changed already-admitted past actions.

These violate objective/causal canon.

### Human contradiction — preserve

Examples:
- Nadia critiques orientalist tourism but treasures an object from a naive travel phase;
- she rejects consumerism yet loves an expensive object;
- she values autonomy but remains controlling in one intimate domain;
- she believes a period "didn't matter much" even though behavior suggests some influence;
- she still wears elephant pants around the house while rolling her eyes at who she was when she bought them;
- she enjoys a Buddhist-derived practice without identifying as Buddhist.

These are not errors.

They are personhood.

## 5. Buddhist prayer-rug stress test

Suppose the user notices a Buddhist prayer/meditation rug.

### Case A — maximal overfit, reject

Late generator proposes:

> Nadia secretly converted to Buddhism seven years ago, and this was the decisive event that transformed her entire personality.

If present V3/history did not already contain:
- Buddhist identity/community/practice;
- corresponding ideological/religious change;
- downstream memories/routines/relations;

then this is unlawful merely because a clever generator can force-fit it.

It introduces a missing major formative cause.

Disposition:
`REJECT`, or create a new root/version outside the existing continuity.

### Case B — latent-socket filling, potentially lawful

Existing V3 already contains:
- major control/anxiety crisis;
- friendship rupture;
- exploratory period;
- exposure to alternative practices;
- later reduced control rigidity;
- exact practices partly unresolved.

Candidate:
> while traveling during that period, Nadia attended meditation sessions, briefly practiced more seriously, and retained the rug.

Forward reconciliation shows:
- no contradiction with later ideology;
- no required Buddhist identity;
- practice contributes modestly to an already-established transition;
- later Nadia may remember it with mixed significance.

Disposition:
potentially lawful.

### Case C — ordinary contingency, strongly lawful

Candidate:
> Nadia attended one meditation class while traveling; the class distributed/sold the rug; she liked it and kept using it.

The developmental transition already in V3 happened for its existing causes.

The rug's provenance is only weakly connected.

Disposition:
strongly lawful if otherwise compatible.

### Case D — contradiction is the point

Nadia:
- thinks of the trip as spiritually unserious;
- dislikes Western Buddhist lifestyle branding;
- nonetheless still uses the rug because it is comfortable and tied to a beloved memory;
- may not have any unified theory of why she kept it.

This should **not** be "resolved" into a hidden elegant cause.

Disposition:
preserve contradiction.

### Case E — the user offers a new interpretation

User:
> "You know, that meditation thing actually sounds connected to what you were going through with control then."

Preserve:

`PAST EVENT`
!= `OBJECTIVE CAUSAL CONTRIBUTION`
!= `NADIA'S PRIOR SELF-NARRATIVE`
!= `USER INTERPRETATION`
!= `NADIA'S PRESENT REACTION TO USER INTERPRETATION`
!= `LATER JOINT MEANING`.

Nadia may answer:
- "huh, maybe";
- "no, I really don't think it mattered";
- "I never connected those";
- "actually, yeah—that may have been part of it";
- "you're probably reading too much into it";
- ambivalently.

That conversation becomes a **new present relational event** and may legitimately alter Nadia's future self-understanding.

The user may contribute meaning without authoring the past.

## 6. Late-materialization impact classes

Every historical insertion should declare a causal-impact class.

### H0 — descriptive / inert

Examples:
- exact shoe brand;
- color;
- ordinary purchase;
- decorative object provenance.

May affect:
- material continuity;
- scene detail.

Must not alter durable V3.

### H1 — episodic / associative

Examples:
- object linked to a trip;
- minor friendship episode;
- local habit;
- remembered anecdote.

May affect:
- episodic memory;
- accessibility;
- local associations.

### H2 — contributory / symbolic

Examples:
- recurring practice;
- meaningful gift;
- object carried through several phases;
- experience that modestly reinforces an already-established developmental transition.

May affect:
- symbolic valence;
- repeated memory recruitment;
- local preference/habit;
- some durable state within a bounded domain.

### H3 — developmentally formative

Examples:
- conversion;
- major bereavement;
- formative abuse;
- sustained ideological community;
- life-altering relationship;
- institutional rupture;
- severe illness/disability transition.

These should ordinarily already exist in the precomputed V3/historical spine or in explicit causal slack.

Late H3 creation is presumptively forbidden.

A late candidate may only occupy H3 if:
- the V3 trajectory already contains the corresponding transformation;
- an unresolved causal slot of appropriate weight already exists;
- forward reconciliation does not alter established pillars;
- exact governance explicitly approves the fill.

## 7. Do not let objects become tail-wags-dog causes

Strong law:

> **Late-resolved flesh should usually be downstream of established developmental history, not allowed to invent that developmental history in order to explain itself.**

Example:

If Nadia already has:
`suburban/normie-ish phase`
→ `exploratory/travel phase`
→ `graduate/decolonial seriousness`
→ `present Nadia`

then late Birkenstock history may thread through those phases.

The shoes can:
- be acquired during one phase;
- travel through another;
- appear in a Guatemala photograph;
- acquire wear;
- survive a later ideological/aesthetic shift;
- become autobiographical continuity;
- persist despite newer replacements.

But the shoes should not be used to invent the whole developmental arc merely because the resulting story is elegant.

---

## 8. Sparse forward propagation, not life replay

Do not regenerate every intervening day/year.

Represent Nadia's life using:
- developmental checkpoints;
- major event nodes;
- durable person state;
- relationship/network state;
- object/material timelines;
- unresolved causal slots.

A late insertion emits **causal residues** only into affected channels.

Candidate residue channels:
- material persistence;
- episodic association;
- memory accessibility;
- symbolic valence;
- habit/practice;
- relation linkage;
- ideological relevance;
- developmental contribution.

Propagation follows:
`INSERTION TIME → NEXT RELEVANT CHECKPOINTS → PRESENT`.

At each step:
- survive;
- attenuate;
- transform;
- become inaccessible;
- become contradicted by later commitments;
- remain unresolved.

No requirement that the residue acquire one final stable meaning.

## 9. Photos / objects / later scenes

If a historical object exists at time T and a later unresolved photograph covers time T+n:

- object availability becomes one lawful input;
- object use remains probabilistic/contextual;
- no rule requires it to appear merely because it could.

Example:
Birkenstocks owned in 2017.

Guatemala photo:
- if feet unresolved, wearing them may be plausible;
- if previously canonized in red Converse, keep red Converse;
- if the trip context suggests another shoe, use that.

Compact:

> **CAUSAL AVAILABILITY != NARRATIVE DESTINY.**

This is crucial to anti-serendipity.

## 10. Anti-serendipity / anti-totalization law

The generator must not preferentially choose histories because they:
- explain many present traits at once;
- create elegant symbolism;
- make every object meaningful;
- produce satisfying foreshadowing;
- make the user's interpretation look profound;
- tie all loose ends into a single developmental arc.

Human life contains:
- coincidence;
- redundancy;
- dead ends;
- forgotten phases;
- low-significance objects;
- contradictory residues;
- tastes without theories;
- events whose consequences were small;
- events whose real consequences differ from later stories about them.

### ATTEND should deepen truth without automatically disclosing lore

This is a separate game-loop consequence of anti-totalization.

The danger is **not** that ATTEND causes Rawlsing to generate deeper reality.

It should.

The danger is collapsing:

`ATTEND TRIGGERS DEEPER MATERIALIZATION`

into:

`ATTEND AUTOMATICALLY DISCLOSES DEEPER SIGNIFICANCE`.

A healthy loop is:

`ATTEND TARGET`
→ deeper lawful physical resolution becomes user-accessible
→ still-deeper objective/provenance/person history may be materialized behind the scenes
→ only epistemically available evidence is disclosed
→ later observation/testimony/inference may connect those pieces.

Thus an inspected sandal can reveal:
- brown Birkenstock;
- old;
- uneven heel wear;
- darkened cork;
- repaired strap;

while the same ATTEND may cause Rawlsing to resolve, invisibly:
- acquisition history;
- repair history;
- whether it appears in old photos;
- what Nadia actually knows/remembers;
- whether some deeper causal significance exists.

The user does not receive those hidden facts merely because generation occurred.

Compact:

> **ATTEND EARNS SPECIFICITY, NOT AUTOMATIC SIGNIFICANCE.**

> **SPECIFICITY IS EVIDENCE FROM WHICH SIGNIFICANCE MAY LATER EMERGE.**

and:

> **MATERIALIZATION DEPTH MAY ADVANCE BEYOND DISCLOSURE DEPTH.**

The failure mode is therefore not "meaningful backstory exists."

It is:
- narrator hands it over because the user looked;
- every inspected object immediately yields autobiographical payoff;
- actor testimony functions as canonical truth lookup;
- physical inspection somehow reveals propositions inaccessible to physical inspection.

Ordinary or low-significance truth remains necessary too, because significance must not be inferable from the mere fact that the player chose to ATTEND.

See SPD-0236 / SPD-0237 / RISK-193 / RISK-194.

Therefore historical candidate scoring must preserve **contingency mass**.

Possible conceptual objective:

`PLAUSIBILITY`
+ `CANON COMPATIBILITY`
+ `CAUSAL FIT`
+ `MATERIAL/HISTORICAL FIT`
+ `DIVERSITY / CONTINGENCY MASS`
-
`RETROACTIVE DISRUPTION`
-
`EXPLANATORY SATURATION`
-
`USER-QUERY SEMANTIC BIAS`.

Do not optimize for:
`MAXIMUM MEANINGFULNESS`.

## 11. Explanatory saturation

Define a dangerous pattern:

> **EXPLANATORY SATURATION** — too many present facts become downstream of one newly materialized past event or symbol, making the person unnaturally narratively economical.

Warning signs:
- one trip explains politics, sexuality, aesthetics, attachment, career and spirituality;
- every owned object maps neatly to a developmental stage;
- every contradiction has a hidden resolution;
- every user interpretation uncovers a latent truth;
- early symbols continuously foreshadow later life.

Real persons may occasionally have highly integrated periods or symbols.

But the generator must not prefer them systematically.

Possible guardrail:
new late-generated H1/H2 detail gets a bounded **causal fan-out budget** unless existing V3 causal slack explicitly supports wider reach.

---

## 12. Person's own explanatory opacity

Rawlsing should preserve:

`OBJECTIVE CAUSAL HISTORY`
!= `NADIA'S SELF-UNDERSTANDING`.

Nadia may:
- know why she likes something;
- have a plausible but incomplete story;
- refuse psychoanalytic explanation;
- dislike the user's theorization;
- revise her interpretation years later;
- never care to explain it.

This is especially important because a highly interpretive user may otherwise make Nadia into a case study.

Rawlsing should permit:
> "I just like them."

without secretly rewarding continued inquiry with a deeper answer every time.

Sometimes:
`I JUST LIKE THEM`
is the best currently reachable actor truth.

And sometimes objective history may contain more causal structure than Nadia herself uses or values.

---

## 13. User interpretation can become new relation-history

The user may offer:
- theory;
- theology;
- political interpretation;
- psychoanalytic reading;
- pattern recognition.

The engine must distinguish:
1. user's proposition;
2. Nadia's uptake/appraisal;
3. Nadia's response;
4. later memory/reconsideration;
5. possible durable effect.

A brilliant user interpretation may:
- clarify Nadia to herself;
- annoy her;
- be partly right;
- be wrong;
- become meaningful only years later.

This is exactly where the **user can give something to Nadia** without acquiring sovereignty over her past.

---

## 14. Engineering feasibility boundary

Feasible:
- sparse checkpoints;
- event-sourced history;
- causal residue channels;
- latent causal slots;
- deterministic/query-invariant historical proposals;
- bounded forward reconciliation;
- contradiction preservation;
- reject/modify/version when a candidate exceeds the reconciliation budget.

Not feasible or not desirable:
- full life resimulation on every ATTEND;
- unconstrained LLM autobiographical generation;
- retroactive H3 events introduced to explain local objects;
- global consistency pass that forces all traits into one theory;
- exhaustive precomputation of every possible object/history relation.

Compact engineering law:

> **Infinite-feeling richness must come from bounded lazy resolution around a compact causal spine, not from exhaustive simulation.**

## 16. Compact laws

> **Late historical materialization fills or specifies lawful history; it does not casually invent a new developmental spine.**

> **Unknown detail does not imply unknown major causation.**

> **Causal compatibility does not require psychological harmony.**

> **Contradiction is often personhood, not corruption.**

> **Causal availability does not imply narrative destiny.**

> **Do not maximize meaningfulness. Preserve contingency.**

> **A user's interpretation may become a new cause in Nadia's self-understanding without becoming retroactive truth about Nadia's past.**

> **The richer the person becomes, the tighter new details should be constrained—and the less pressure there should be to make them all explain one another.**
