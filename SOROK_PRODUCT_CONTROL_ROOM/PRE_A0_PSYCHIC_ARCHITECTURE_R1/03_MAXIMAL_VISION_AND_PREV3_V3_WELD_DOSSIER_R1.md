Rawlsing's downstream target is not a chatbot persona and not a trait-conditioned roleplay system.

- a sociologically constrained but non-deterministic world;
- recursively generated parents, caregivers and causally important others;

> **VERTICAL DEPTH, BOUNDED HORIZONTAL SURFACE.**

We are willing to bound:
- number of focal persons;
- geographic/world breadth;
- institution count;
- incidental cast receiving full recursion;
- open-world action breadth;

We are **not** willing to achieve tractability by flattening:

- focal relations;
- parent/co-parent generation;
- historical psychic residue;
- unconscious / inaccessible causation;
- contradiction;
- rich actor-model-of-user;
- rich user-facing phenomenal mediation;
- recursive relation formation;
- generated memory and actor self-misrecognition;
- the person's relation to social categories and power.

This is the intended "lean Rawlsing": small world, deep person.
