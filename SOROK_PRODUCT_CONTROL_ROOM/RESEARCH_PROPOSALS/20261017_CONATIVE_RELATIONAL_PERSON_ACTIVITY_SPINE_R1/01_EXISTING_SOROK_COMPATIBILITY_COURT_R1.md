## Where the October 17 synthesis adds indispensable stress

1. **Agency authorship contrast:** physical act unchanged but another's command replaces own initiating project; test whether implementation distinguishes *doing* from *being made to do* without a hardwired “defiance” reaction.
2. **Power and love as capacity transformation:** a caregiver may enlarge a child's acquired practical power, potentially increasing their ability to refuse the same caregiver. No assumed love→compliance or support→automatic competence gradient.
3. **Two independent conative centers:** shared work and shared delight are co-produced, but actor experiences need not match; relational outcome cannot be owned by one psyche.
4. **Persistent pre-conscious organization of practical possibility:** beyond latent pressure/trace magnitude, history affects whether offers are read as care, intrusion, demand, humiliation, opportunity or ordinary cooperation.
5. **Activity before discrete event:** developmental formation occurs through repeated organized activity, temporal conditions, care work, routine, labor and bodily changes; cannot force all causal input into discrete event counterfeits. The existing SDTF non-event V3 ingress frontier OBL062 is not silently closed.
6. **Generative counterfactual validation:** hold apparent physical action or utterance fixed while perturbing historical person, author, counterpart, permission, audience, activity situation, recognition and power. The expected mechanism difference must follow from lawful causes; no scripted emotion.
7. **No universal pathology or ethical gradient:** conflict, separation and refusal do not automatically prove health; intimacy, support and togetherness do not automatically prove benefit. Enneagram is optional downstream uncertain interpretation, not psycho-causal input.
8. **Specificity of agentic life:** not every moment is an adverse pressure transaction or a user-triggered response; the actor pursues other projects, with valid no-effect or no-response outcomes. Must preserve independently continuing world and life.
