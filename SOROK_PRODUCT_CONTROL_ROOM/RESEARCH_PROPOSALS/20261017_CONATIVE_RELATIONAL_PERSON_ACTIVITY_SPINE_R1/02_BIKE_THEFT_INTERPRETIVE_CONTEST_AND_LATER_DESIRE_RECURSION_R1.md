## A. The exact constitutive claim

A particular child's bike is stolen from a garage. The material theft is one objective occurrence. It does NOT contain the child's later interpretation or the thief's identity, motivation, social group, or morality, nor does it decide a durable response. The actual child's immediate loss, anger, fear, embarrassment, outrage or indifference, if any, cannot be inferred directly from the event label. Response/interpretation evolves through prior child organization, developmental capacities, attachment and estrangement histories, practical ownership and effort, actual household material consequences, parent and sibling agency, peer speech, school milieu, ideology and affective attunement, and future experience. Different causal sources act through different routes; socially circulating stereotypes are **attributed utterances and possible biases**, never objectively validated facts.

Potential meaning trajectories include:
- heightened watchfulness about property/access or instrumental care;
- self-blame for not locking or otherwise securing property;
- generalized suspicion/vulnerability, potentially narrowed to a context;
- angry devaluation of particular people or groups based on prejudice circulated by peers;
- compassionate interpretation involving deprivation, consequences and mutual harm;
- an ethic of refusing to inflict similar loss;
- moralistic or religious attribution, or disbelief/rejection of such attributions;
- neither durable shift nor lasting autobiographical importance;
- several contradictory interpretations and habits that do not sum to one 'lesson'.

**Not:** a finite mutually exhaustive menu; a single theft automatically establishes any of these meanings; a child is a transparent self-interpreting homunculus; one event automatically updates a globally typed 'trust', 'hardness', 'racism', 'morality' or 'attachment' scalar. Narrative descriptions above are **examples of hypotheses generated by an LLM**, not adopter-licensed outcomes.

## C. The event is a sequence of interpersonal causal opportunities, not a one-line psychological shock

Possible episodes:
- bike actually acquired/used/valued; its cost and significance generated;
- theft discovered; objective identity/evidence not inflated;
- child has first bodily/affective reaction, potentially pre-verbal or at odds with later articulate views;
- caregiver arrives with own emotions/constraints/ideological account; child may seek care, ignore account, feel unwitnessed, be materially comforted, or reject interpretation;
- parent can accurately describe material risks while failing to recognize affect; can be generous yet ineffectual in this particular conflict; can be controlling but protect against genuine risks; and each combination can recur/change over time;
- peers offer socially resonant interpretations (including scapegoating/racialized prejudice) that may recruit shame, anger, belonging or desire for recognition, without being factually true or inevitable;
- other adults, material events and child's own practical reasoning can resist or undermine these accounts;
- child may privately internalize one interpretation, publicly perform another, enact a habit without believing its story, or leave the incident unresolved and later forgotten but residually active.

Each offered interpretation has a source, actor-relative accessibility, legitimacy/credibility under particular history, relational recognition/power conditions, practical and emotional fit, other competing models, possible material cost and explicit uncertainty. Do not infer child attitude from class/religion/ethnicity or peer speech by direct edge.

## D. Recursion under new, meaningfully different experience

Several years later, the child may develop a crush on an independently constituted peer whose actual warmth, compassion, resilience or emotional intelligence is valued, perhaps overlapping with traits in a parent the child had formerly discounted. It is a **possible contingent scenario**, not a Freudian invariant, prescribed heterosexual trajectory, therapeutic reward or compulsory mother's-trait return. The admired person has their own values, refusals, desires and trajectory: they are not created to 'teach' the focal actor a lesson.

The child's previous defensive hardness or worldview could now:
- block desired recognition/approach while still supporting perceived safety elsewhere;
- split public peer reputation from private aspiration;
- recruit envy, shame, disgust at vulnerability, fascination, gratitude, denial or curiosity;
- become consciously revised without procedural change;
- partially soften within one relationship while persisting in another;
- intensify defensively because the contradiction feels intolerable;
- yield no relevant change, or be displaced by different practical concerns.

The later person does not time-travel to change the original theft or the original private emotional episode. **History immutable; interpretation, accessibility, relationships, procedural dispositions and actor models revisable with causal parentage.** The later meaning must be due to *actual similarity + difference + relations + current projects + acquired capabilities + event outcome*, not automatic 'healed by crush' genre tropes.

## F. Hostile counterfactual court — minimum proving cases

1. **Fixed theft, varied parent histories:** well-resourced sensitive attunement; authoritarian control; careful protection from materially realistic danger; overworked caring scarcity. Responses need not sort on a goodness axis; prove real independently formed parent choices and child's response conditioned rather than label-scripted.
2. **Fixed theft + fixed parent statement, varied child history:** different previous reliable recognition/alienation histories alter whether the statement feels validating, controlling, implausible or merely irrelevant; not a universal function of caregiver label.
3. **Fixed parent and incident, varied peer environments:** prejudicial scapegoating available versus challenged versus absent. It must be possible for child to reject peers or parents, not simply copy most emotionally evocative narrative. No claim that ethnic identity objectively caused theft.
4. **Factual explanation vs affective reception:** parent can be objectively correct but emotionally missing; child can recognize correctness and still develop resentment, or accept relationship care while rejecting propositional claim.
5. **Identification without simplistic moral valence:** cooperative generous parent produces high identification; a fearful controlling parent also does, by different historical routes; controlling parent yields rebellion in another actual context; generous parent yields autonomy or estrangement too. No direct SES/sect/race to allegiance edge.
6. **Developmental capacity counterexample:** do not give a young child capacities acquired later, but show different historically formed abilities in similarly aged actors; research-backed developmental constraints must be named, not invented numeric thresholds or 'foundational' stages.
7. **Multiplicity across timelines:** parent says one thing, child believes another, acts through a third acquired practical habit, remembers selectively, narrates fourth; later interpretation does not rewrite objective event.
8. **No immediate fixed personality conversion:** one theft can have small, temporary, contradictory or no lasting effect; repeated experiences can progressively alter tendencies by accepted mechanism.
9. **Later desire tests, not moral reward:** independent desired counterpart shares parental trait but is not scripted rescuer; earlier hardness may impede relationship or be irrelevant; later counterexample may deepen, soften, differentiate, reverse or leave unchanged prior organization.
10. **Repeatable causal contrast:** same seed/rule/source/event prestate reproducibly replays; changing a genuine historical input changes *some* permitted downstream distributions, not guaranteed exact morally preferred behavior; no hidden post-hoc LLM backfill.
11. **Otherness in live product:** focal person can want something the user does not, misunderstand, decline, withhold, change slowly or prioritize independent practical engagements, for real reasons in actual world. Random annoyance/persona prompt defiance does not count.
12. **Negative-authority cases:** missing actor permissions, absent admissible event, unowned developmental stage, invented cross-actor knowledge, unregistered effect rule, fake per-scenario probability, false peer attribution and invalid producer facts fail closed with typed receipt.
