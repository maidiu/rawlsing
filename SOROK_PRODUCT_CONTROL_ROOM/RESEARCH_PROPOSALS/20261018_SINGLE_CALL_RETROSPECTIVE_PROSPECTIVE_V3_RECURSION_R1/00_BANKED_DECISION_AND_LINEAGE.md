## Decision being banked

**Use one combined LLM invocation per developmental interval by default, with two independently typed outputs: (A) retrospective, strictly noncausal narrative realization of an already-computed interval, and (B) prospective, strictly noncausal candidate event/possibility packets for the next interval.** The V3/Sorok causal engine adjudicates a potentially large, time-ordered sequence of events *between* such calls. The one-call schedule is a performance and expressivity **design preference**, not a scientific inference or mandatory call count for all future cases.

**Default evaluation cadence:** annual; **multiple events may occur during each year**, including mundane, positive, adverse and mixed developments. Shorter 6- or 3-month horizons and extra proposal calls triggered by a large realized context change are explicit alternatives to test. Annual cadence must not replace event/context/phase-driven temporal scheduling. A `no-event` interval, valid-empty psyche update, unchosen candidate, failed event or no LLM call are permitted; no fallback may manufacture an event.

At the first call, there is no previous interval to render; initialize from an independently authorized actor/world and emit candidate events only (plus any explicitly noncausal presentation). At the final call, prospective candidates can be omitted and the last completed interval rendered. Every other call joins the two logical functions without combining their authority.

## Exact intended recurrence

Let `S[n]` be a V3 owner-committed actor/world causal checkpoint, `H[n]` its immutable event/admission/history refs, and `R[n]` a scoped immutable actor-safe render/structural proposal input derived from them.

1. `LLM(R[n]) -> { retrospective_render[n-1 -> n], prospective_candidates[n -> n+1] }`. These are separately typed, independently validated *noncausal* outputs; the proposal is grounded in machine-owned refs, not in its own invented retrospective prose.
2. V3 admits/selects none, some, or a lawful sequence of proposals under exact proposal/event/actor/world issuer rules. Each candidate carries event identity, participants, prerequisites, purported relevance dimensions, uncertainty and prompt/model/version provenance. The LLM cannot authorize occurrence or psychic outcome.
3. Iterate chronological triggers `t1 < t2 < ...` within the interval: refresh permitted context, revalidate outstanding candidates, admit actual external occurrence only from its competent event owner, expose separately to each actor, compute situated CAN008 responses and any separately authorized CAN011/ADP016/relationship/world effects, and advance slow processes between events. Consequences of `t1` alter eligibility/relevance/outcome at `t2`. Other actors have independently formed projects; nonaction/no-change remains lawful.
4. Commit actor/world history and state only via their separately governed owners and append-only receipts; next `R[n+1]` derives from that committed history. The next single LLM call retrospectively realizes that period and prospectively proposes the following one.
5. Pure retrospective rendering **never** changes `S[n]`, `H[n]`, a prior utterance, an existing relation or the candidate admission outcome. Render-specific invented names/episodes cannot become future candidate prerequisites unless separately admitted as genuine world/person events.

**Essential difference from a movie script:** proposed future events and their possible `angles of relevance` are provisional hypotheses, *not* a predestined year. Relocation may invalidate a friendship or competition in the original proposal packet. Recheck at each event, reject/retarget only through permitted rules, or make a fresh bounded proposal call if candidate depletion and material change warrant it. Refusal/unknown are productive results, not reasons to fabricate a substitute.

## Scientific purpose of the proposed 'angles'

An LLM may suggest that a football team, friend, move, family job loss, school project, creative success, illness, pleasant habit, conflict or unexpected support could recruit skill, initiative, belonging, recognition, rivalry, autonomy, practiced procedures, ambivalence, embodied need, practical competence, desire, fear, etc. These are `CANDIDATE_RELEVANCE_HYPOTHESES`, not generated affection/belief/trauma, nor numeric weights on a person's putative universal psychology. Rawlsing must evaluate only authorized cause/effect rules against *that particular actor's* accumulated actual activities, position, relations, capability and competing dispositions. The meaning and degree of change can be zero, plural, contradictory or deferred.

The intended product is a distinctive historically constituted person emerging from independent, contingent encounters and recurrent practice, not a psyche generated from demographics, psychotype labels, an LLM's preferred plot, or a hand-assigned ending. Longitudinal actor developmental evidence is not supplied by current survey marginals.
