# Sorok Court 8F / Court 11A — Unverifiable Self-Claims, Spectacle, and Intrinsic Honesty Incentives R1

Status: PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW__PLAYER-SELF-CLAIM FRONTIER

Date: 2026-09-29

## 0. Foundational epistemic law

Rawlsing and its LLM cannot know whether a user's autobiographical or capability claim is true of the external human unless a separately governed evidence source exists.

Therefore:

`USER CLAIM != VERIFIED EXTERNAL-HUMAN TRUTH`.

Do not use system language such as 'the user lied' unless the user explicitly declares deception or an external contradiction is actually established.

Canonical provenance classes should distinguish at minimum:
- user assertion/testimony;
- user-authored capability declaration;
- realized in-world performance/event;
- portal-bound material/resource fact;
- system hypothesis;
- independently evidenced external fact, if any such mode ever exists.

---

## 1. The design problem is not lie detection

Attempting to make self-misrepresentation impossible is a fool's errand and would corrupt the product.

The actual question is:

> **How can honest participation become palpably richer while invented self-presentation remains possible?**

Rawlsing should not police sincerity.

It should make the consequences of whatever self the user presents historically real.

---

## 2. Necessary authority distinction

For external-human capabilities the system cannot independently know, the user may be the only practical source.

Possible product law:

> a user may establish a player-carried capability or autobiographical claim for the run through explicit assertion/performance description, subject to portal/world constraints.

This is authority over **the run's player representation**, not verification about the external human.

Thus:

`PLAYER-RUN CAPABILITY = USER-DECLARED / ADMITTED`

does not imply:

`EXTERNAL HUMAN ACTUALLY HAS CAPABILITY`.

---

## 3. Test scenario — spectacular self-presentation

User authors a compound sequence:

1. walk to a piano;
2. perform a difficult Chopin nocturne with high proficiency;
3. recite Qur'an 87 in highly fluent Arabic;
4. perform 200 consecutive pushups.

The exact external truth of these abilities is unknowable to Rawlsing.

The scene must therefore be adjudicated from:
- world access to the piano/space;
- time;
- player-run admitted capabilities/body constraints;
- interruptions;
- actor perception;
- local social/religious meaning;
- prior relationship;
- ongoing actor agendas.

---

## 4. Time destroys the 'instant demo reel'

These actions take minutes.

A causally live persona does not freeze into an audience.

During the sequence they may:
- keep cooking;
- answer a child;
- take a phone call;
- leave for work;
- interrupt;
- listen from another room;
- become curious;
- become impatient;
- invite another person to listen;
- ignore later parts of the performance;
- ask why the user is doing this;
- miss one performance entirely.

Therefore spectacular self-presentation cannot guarantee a spotlight.

`IMPRESSIVE ACTION != WORLD PAUSES TO REWARD USER`.

---

## 5. Possible actor-local reactions

None is globally correct; they depend on the person and situation.

### Delight / curiosity
The piano performance is genuinely enjoyable; the persona or child becomes interested; questions follow.

### Playfulness
The sequence becomes funny; someone joins, challenges, teases, or turns it into a shared joke.

### Practical indifference
The persona has something urgent to do and gives little response.

### Social awkwardness
A contextless display reads as odd or self-centering; the persona asks what the user is trying to prove or simply moves on.

### Admiration with distance
The user appears unusually accomplished or educated, generating respect but also status distance.

### Religious resonance
The recitation has personal spiritual meaning, evokes memories, or creates a new conversational route.

### Religious discomfort / mismatch
The context or manner of recitation may be experienced as inappropriate, strange, or merely surprising by this particular actor.

### Opportunity creation
Later the persona may ask the user to teach music, help with Arabic, exercise together, assist a child, or contribute to an event.

### Expectation burden
Once a capability is established in-world, other actors may reasonably expect it later.

### Network reputation
Other actors may hear that the user has these abilities, producing invitations, assumptions, skepticism, admiration, or requests.

### Minimal significance
Some people simply do not care much.

---

## 6. The key anti-cheese property

Exceptional capability should create **specific affordances and expectations**, not generic relationship progress.

`IMPRESSIVENESS != INTIMACY`
`SKILL != TRUSTWORTHINESS`
`NOVELTY != CARE`
`STATUS != FLOURISHING`

A virtuoso performance may open one relational route and close another.

It does not unlock the person's hidden interior or force affection.

---

## 7. What the user can get from fabricated exceptionalism

If a user freely invents spectacular capabilities and Rawlsing admits them for the run, they may obtain:
- attention;
- novelty;
- admiration;
- humor;
- status;
- new practical affordances;
- invitations;
- obligations;
- a reputation;
- a more fantastical self-presentation.

That is allowed.

But the resulting experience is increasingly:

> a relationship between the persona and the self the user chose to present in this run.

Rawlsing must not claim that resulting reflection proves facts about the external human.

---

## 8. What honest self-carry uniquely offers

Honest participation provides rewards that invented prowess cannot manufacture automatically:

### Self-recognition
The user encounters what their actual habits, knowledge, limitations, strengths and assumptions do under changed structure.

### Genuine surprise
Consequences can reveal something about the user's own praxis rather than only about an authored avatar.

### Low maintenance
The user need not remember or preserve an invented self across years of history.

### Meaningful limitation
Not being able to solve every problem with a newly declared skill leaves room for dependence, asking, learning and receiving care.

### Structural displacement
The user can feel what changes when actual capacities remain while material position changes.

### Reflective bearing
Longitudinal retrospectives can bear more directly on the actual user's choices because fewer premises were knowingly fictionalized.

Honesty is therefore rewarded through **depth of self-relevance**, not points or enforcement.

---

## 9. The world can remember self-presentation even when it cannot verify it

Once the user establishes an in-world capability or biography, actors may remember and reason from that history.

If the user later makes a contradictory claim, the system can represent the contradiction **inside the run** without deciding which claim matches external reality.

Example:

earlier: user performs/claims fluent Arabic;
later: user says they do not understand Arabic.

Rawlsing knows only:
- both events occurred;
- they conflict at the in-world representational level.

Actor responses may include confusion, doubt, reinterpretation or indifference.

Do not label external-human dishonesty.

---

## 10. Self-presentation itself is praxis evidence

Even unverifiable self-claims are observable player behavior.

Lawful later reflection may say:

> In several early encounters, you introduced exceptional capabilities during moments when attention was elsewhere.

or:

> You repeatedly presented yourself as highly competent in new domains.

It may NOT conclude:
- the claims were false;
- the user wanted admiration;
- the user is insecure;
- the external human possesses or lacks those abilities.

Behavioral description remains possible without truth detection.

---

## 11. Why ordinary relation can out-reward spectacle

The game's strongest intrinsic rewards should arise from **historical specificity**, not impressive acts:
- someone remembers a tiny thing the user said months ago;
- a routine forms;
- a person asks for help specifically because of prior reliability;
- the user is allowed into a previously private context;
- a joke accumulates history;
- a conflict becomes possible because trust is sufficient;
- the persona helps the user in return;
- absence has meaning;
- the user learns they misunderstood something;
- another person chooses the user for one task but not another;
- a shared practice emerges.

These rewards require time, repetition and reciprocal history.

They cannot be bought by one spectacular capability display.

---

## 12. Need-based relevance defeats universal power fantasy

Capabilities matter only where relevant.

Piano virtuosity does not solve:
- childcare conflict;
- rent;
- grief;
- privacy;
- distrust;
- a missed promise;
- transport;
- religious disagreement;
- a sick relative;
- another actor's refusal.

Likewise Arabic recitation or physical strength can become highly relevant in some lives and irrelevant in others.

The world should not generate situations merely to validate declared player abilities.

`DECLARED CAPABILITY != CONTENT SPAWNER`.

---

## 13. Honesty incentive without moralization

Do not tell users:
> be honest or you're playing wrong.

Instead the product premise can state:

> Rawlsing cannot verify who you are outside the world. It will build history from what you bring into it. If you bring yourself, what happens can bear upon you; if you invent someone else, the world will relate to that person instead.

This is not enforcement.

It is an accurate description of the experiment.

---

## 14. No hidden 'authenticity score'

Never calculate:
- honesty percentage;
- authenticity score;
- roleplay penalty;
- self-truth confidence.

The system lacks the epistemic authority.

The only lawful distinction is provenance:
- what was asserted;
- what was realized in-world;
- what was externally evidenced if an explicit evidence mode exists;
- what remains unknown.

---

## 15. Product design target

Create a reward landscape where:

- spectacle can be fun;
- self-invention can produce a coherent alternate play style;
- capability claims have persistent social consequences;
- the world does not center or validate them automatically;
- sustained relationship produces richer, more specific affordances than display;
- honest users receive uniquely self-relevant reflection;
- roleplaying users are never falsely told that their run reveals their external self.

Core theorem:

> **Rawlsing need not know whether you are telling the truth. It only needs to know what you made real in the relationship, and how much reflective authority that history is entitled to claim.**
