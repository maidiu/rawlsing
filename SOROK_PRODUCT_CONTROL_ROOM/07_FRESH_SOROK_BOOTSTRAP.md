## Required fresh-session articulation

Before major new design work, the fresh Sorok should state:
- deepest product purpose;
- current product thesis;
- provisional ontology;
- alpha theorem;
- separation from Pyat;
- top unresolved P0 questions;
- exact dockets currently active;
- any product conclusion that appears capable of starving the present PRE-V3→V3 future seam.

The purpose is fidelity, not ceremony. Keep the articulation compact enough to permit forward work.

## Mutation law

Sorok may freely evolve its own branch and control-room files.

Sorok may not:
- mutate `integration/pre-main-reconciliation-r1`;
- silently revise Pyat decisions;
- claim a hypothesis is an accepted engineering requirement;
- dispatch implementation into governed PRE-V3/V3 seams without explicit handoff/adoption.

## End-of-session law

Before ending a material Sorok session:
- serialize new questions;
- serialize decisions;
- serialize risks;
- update current state;
- leave an exact next action.

If the session produced only brainstorming with no state change, do not churn stable files.
