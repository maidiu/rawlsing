## 0. Correction

Artifacts 53 and 54 surfaced real product phenomena but risked implying new durable "relational grammar" and "object-value grammar" subsystems.

That is not the preferred Sorok architecture.

The controlling Sorok relational remodel already supplies the intended central spine:

`RELATIONAL FIELD / MATERIAL CONDITIONS`
→ `RELATIONAL HISTORY`
→ `PERSON-AS-SEDIMENTED-RELATIONAL-ORGANIZATION`
→ `RELATION-CLASS / ROLE-CONDITIONED PRIORS`
→ `MODEL OF PARTICULAR OTHER`
→ `SPECIFIC DYADIC HISTORY / CONVENTIONS`
→ `NETWORK CONTEXT`
→ `CURRENT PERSON×OTHER×EVENT RECRUITMENT`
→ `ACTOR-LOCAL RESPONSE / MEANING`
→ `NEW OBJECTIVE HISTORY`
→ possible governed durable change to dyad / person / network.

This follows D001 and Court 9.

## 1. Central design law

Prefer deriving many game-domain meanings from the relationally remodeled person/history spine.

Do **not** create a new durable subsystem merely because the queried target is:
- an object;
- food;
- money;
- a room;
- a home;
- a ritual;
- a practice;
- a project;
- a disclosure;
- a gift;
- time/availability;
- a favor;
- a boundary.

A new owner or stored mechanism requires proof that the existing relational spine plus objective world/history facts underdetermines behavior that the product needs to distinguish.

## 2. Durable V3 contribution

The relation-geared V3 rewrite should carry the durable causal organization from which such meanings can be recruited, including where lawfully grounded:

- cross-relational durable organization;
- relation-class priors;
- role-conditioned organization;
- relation-indexed needs / expectations / defenses / recognition patterns;
- actor models of particular others;
- models of relationship and metaperception where warranted;
- specific dyadic history and learned conventions;
- durable memory/significance/accessibility;
- self-model changes;
- histories of care, dependence, authority, recognition, conflict, absence and loss;
- projects, strivings, commitments and investments;
- network-conditioned expectations;
- internalized / anticipated other effects;
- generalizations learned from prior relations.

These are not separate "meaning grammars." They are the relational constitution of the person.

## 5. Objects are proving cases, not a separate psyche

Question:

> What does Nadia feel about lending this book to this user?

Should normally be answerable from:

### Objective/world history
- what book is this?
- where did it come from?
- who gave/made/bought/repaired it?
- what has happened to it?
- how scarce/replaceable/useful is it?
- what objective events connect it to Nadia or others?

### Nadia's existing durable person state
- memories/significance connected to those events;
- strivings/projects in which the book participates;
- relations and internalized others linked to it;
- patterns of care, recognition, dependence, ownership, scarcity, exchange, privacy, etc. already generated through her history;
- current self-model and accessibility.

### This counterpart / dyad
- who is this user to Nadia?
- what does she expect from this person?
- what has this person done with entrusted things?
- what conventions exist?
- how does she model their understanding of her?

### Current event
- show, lend, give, refuse, reclaim, damage, sell, annotate, borrow, etc.;
- current context and alternatives.

The current meaning is then a derived/recruited configuration.

Do not store a separate canonical `BOOK_MEANING_WITH_USER` unless later history makes some durable relation-specific fact worth storing under an existing owner.

## 6. Marxian/material-value insight as derivation principle, not subsystem

The Marxian insight remains useful as a **cross-domain interpretive constraint**:

objects and resources may bear sedimented human relations through labor, care, exchange, dependence, recognition, scarcity, inheritance, memory and use.

But those ingredients should be recovered from:
- world/object provenance;
- relationship/event history;
- material position;
- actor memory/significance;
- strivings/projects;
- role and network relations;
- current person×event recruitment.

Do not instantiate a parallel "object-value grammar" merely to encode this insight.

The object is a material node through which already-generated human relations may become causally present.

## 7. Generalized derivation approach

The same central pipeline should be tested across multiple game dimensions.

### Material/object
What does lending/giving/using/damaging this thing mean with this other?

### Hospitality/space
What does entering, feeding, staying, helping oneself, privacy, or giving a key mean here?

### Money/resource
What does paying, lending, owing, gifting, splitting, refusing or depending mean here?

### Care/help
What does giving, receiving, asking, refusing or failing care mean with this counterpart?

### Disclosure/information
What does telling, withholding, asking, discovering or snooping mean here?

### Time/availability
What does waiting, lateness, spontaneous contact, making time, absence or expected presence mean here?

### Ritual/practice
What does inclusion, exclusion, teaching, participation, refusal or desecration mean here?

### Work/project
What does delegation, collaboration, criticism, credit, abandonment or responsibility mean here?

Each domain supplies factual event/target structure into the same relational person×event spine.

## 8. Thin add-on rule

Add only what is not already recoverable.

A domain may need thin factual adapters such as:
- object provenance / ownership / material condition;
- place access/control;
- money/resource accounting;
- ritual role / recurrence;
- project commitments.

These belong primarily to world/object/project authorities, not to a parallel psyche.

Durable psychic add-ons require a hostile-case demonstration that existing V3 relational state cannot support a necessary distinction.

## 9. Stored-versus-derived discipline

A queried meaning is not automatically stored.

Preserve:

`CANONICAL CAUSAL STATE != DERIVED CURRENT INTERPRETATION`.

Querying:
> what does this mean to Nadia?

must not create meaning.

Durable update requires:
- an actual admitted event;
- actor-specific processing;
- sufficient significance/repetition/developmental weight;
- exact promotion authority.

## 10. Central Sorok theorem

> **The goal of the relation-geared V3 rewrite is to make a wide range of concrete human meanings lawful extrapolations from one historically generated relational person, rather than a patchwork of bespoke meaning systems.**

And:

> **Domain-specific mechanics should mostly supply facts to the central relational spine; they should not each reinvent personhood.**

Artifacts 53 and 54 remain useful as proving-domain inventories, but their "grammar" language must be read as descriptions of derivable organization, not presumptive new stored subsystems.
