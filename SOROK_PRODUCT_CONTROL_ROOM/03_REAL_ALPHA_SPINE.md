This file is the **current system-level shape of Rawlsing**, not a claim that Sorok has found its final kernel.

Therefore preserve this distinction:

`CURRENTLY VISIBLE ORGANISM`

`FINAL GENERATIVE KERNEL`.

## 7. Current tunable balances

This is where "organism" becomes more useful than "rulebook."

Future design work should often be framed as changing a balance while preserving the surrounding system.

Examples:

### Pre-generation ↔ lazy completion
More pre-generation:
- higher cost before use;
- stronger causal closure;
- better cross-user reproducibility.

More lazy completion:
- lower initial cost;
- more flexibility;
- higher contamination/retrocausality risk.

### Shared phenomenal frame ↔ user-specific noticing
More shared frame:
- common reality;
- easier orientation;
- less experiential differentiation.

More attention weighting:
- stronger situated perception;
- stronger reflexive pedagogy;
- higher echo-loop risk.

### Independent life ↔ relational gravity
More independent obligations:
- stronger personhood/non-user-centrism;
- less availability.

More endogenous user-prioritization:
- stronger emergent intimacy;
- risk of apparent companion optimization unless causal provenance is preserved.

No fixed "correct" scalar is assumed.

### World depth ↔ runtime cost
Greater causal depth:
- stronger discovery;
- stronger person thickness;
- more storage/generation burden.

### Opacity ↔ legibility
Greater opacity:
- stronger otherness;
- harder inference;
- possible frustration.

Greater legibility:
- easier relationship learning;
- risk of narrator-parent / solvable-NPC collapse.

### Irreversibility ↔ accessibility
Greater irreversibility:
- stronger stakes and relational time;
- greater user loss/friction.

More recoverability:
- lower friction;
- save-scumming / content optimization risk.

### Community comparability ↔ spoiler contamination
A common seed pool creates discourse and experimental power but also meta-knowledge and optimization culture.

### Realism ↔ platform safety / product framing
Human possibility should remain broad while product teleology and rendering avoid commodification.

These are **balance problems in one machine**, not independent policy questions.

## 9. Kernel falsification discipline

For each candidate, perform hostile derivation.

Do not ask only:
> can I redescribe the system using this idea?

Ask:

> If this were the only deep principle I were given, what parts of Rawlsing would I be forced to reconstruct, and what parts would remain arbitrary?

Score candidates qualitatively by:

- **derivational power** — how much existing architecture follows;
- **compression** — how many independent rules disappear into consequences;
- **nontriviality** — whether it constrains choices rather than merely restating them;
- **cross-domain reach** — person, world, perception, relation, time, platform;
- **failure prediction** — whether it predicts known bad architectures;
- **implementation guidance** — whether it helps decide new cases;
- **pedagogical identity** — whether it preserves what Rawlsing is actually for;
- **anti-overreach** — whether it avoids claiming ownership of things better left separate.

No candidate is "the kernel" until it survives this test across the whole machine.

## 16. Historical bridge reconciliation and contradiction preservation

Late-resolved pre-contact detail must not be handled as free backstory generation.

Current candidate loop:

`KNOWN PAST CONTEXT`
+
`KNOWN PRESENT FACT`
+
`KNOWN V3 CHECKPOINTS`
+
`KNOWN INTERVENING CANON`
+
`UNRESOLVED HISTORICAL SLOT`
→ candidate historical insertion
→ typed causal residues
→ bounded forward propagation
→ reconciliation against later checkpoints / present V3
→ `ACCEPT / DOWNGRADE / MODIFY / REJECT / VERSION`.

Strong law:

> **Late historical materialization may explain, specify and locally enrich established developmental state; it may not casually manufacture a new major developmental cause whose absence would have made current V3 materially different.**

### Causal slack

V3 should be allowed to contain **abstractly known developmental change with partially unresolved causal allocation**.

Example:
- friendship rupture;
- anxiety cost;
- increased openness;
- reduced control rigidity;
- some concrete exposures/practices still unresolved.

A later meditation episode may fill part of that open channel without inventing the transformation itself.

But causal slack is not "anything not yet explained."

> **UNSPECIFIED != AVAILABLE FOR RETCON.**

Any unresolved causal allocation used by late materialization must be bounded or derivably constrained by the already-established transition/domain, temporal window, remaining contribution capacity and lawful contributor classes.

This is an admission budget, not necessarily a new slot object.

See SPD-0235 / RISK-192.

### Contradiction preservation

Do not confuse reconciliation with harmonization.

Preserve:

`HARD FACTUAL / CAUSAL INCONSISTENCY`
→ reject/repair.

`LAWFUL HUMAN CONTRADICTION`
→ preserve.

Examples:
- old objects surviving ideological change;
- beliefs conflicting with habits;
- mixed motives;
- self-narratives that are incomplete;
- preferences with no articulated deep reason;
- one relational domain remaining control-heavy while another loosens.

Compact:

> **PERSON != BEST AVAILABLE THEORY OF PERSON.**

And:

> **Contradiction is often generative person-state, not corruption.**

### Anti-serendipity

Do not maximize:
- elegant causal connection;
- symbolic meaningfulness;
- foreshadowing;
- explanatory compression.

Preserve:
- contingency;
- dead ends;
- ordinary liking;
- weak associations;
- coincidence;
- unresolved significance.

Compact:

> **CAUSAL AVAILABILITY != NARRATIVE DESTINY.**

and:

> **Do not maximize meaningfulness. Preserve contingency.**

### ATTEND, materialization and disclosure

Focused ATTEND should reliably deepen lawful perceptual specificity.

But the user-facing payoff and the engine's hidden materialization are distinct.

`ATTEND`
→ more inspectable detail becomes available
→ Rawlsing may also resolve still-deeper hidden provenance/history/actor-epistemic state
→ only evidence reachable through the current sensory/action/social route is disclosed.

Thus:

> **ATTEND EARNS SPECIFICITY, NOT AUTOMATIC SIGNIFICANCE.**

> **SPECIFICITY IS THE RAW MATERIAL FROM WHICH SIGNIFICANCE MAY EMERGE.**

and:

> **MATERIALIZATION DEPTH MAY ADVANCE BEYOND DISCLOSURE DEPTH.**

A user inspecting old Birkenstocks may lawfully see wear, repair and brand while remaining ignorant of acquisition history. Asking Nadia does not query canonical truth: her reply is downstream of her own memory/belief/access/motive and may be partial, mistaken, evasive, playful or false.

See artifact 80, SPD-0236/0237, RISK-193/194.

ATTEND is not the only reason hidden truth becomes concrete. Any lawful actor/world process that actually depends on an unresolved fact may trigger bounded canonical materialization without user disclosure.

> **THE WORLD DEEPENS WHERE CAUSALITY REQUIRES, NOT ONLY WHERE THE PLAYER LOOKS.**

See SPD-0239 / RISK-196.
