## 3. The pre-event boundary at time t

Immediately before exact event `E_t` is coupled to Nadia, the **machine** must possess or be able to obtain the following distinct resources, each with current time, branch and provenance validity:

**(a) Canonical already-developed causal person.** A stable Nadia identity and lineage; current actor causal materialization/checkpoint; all live distributed V3-owned state with exact rule/version/parentage; accumulated actor-specific history including actual encounters and responses; current embodied and material constraints; outstanding projects, positions, obligations and network ties where actually governed. The ledger is not a prompt. It is the authority against which a projection can be audited.

**(b) Distinct objective world and relational reality.** Current objective relationships and independent other persons, households, institutions, objects, resources and constraints. A thin relationship entity carries objective roles and transitions, not a mutual psychological state. Shared pre-contact Nadia and world are distinguishable from the branch-local post-contact Nadia; a prior user cannot rewrite another user's descendant. A missing potential incident is not permission for post-hoc biography fabrication.

**(c) Actor-owned perspective.** Nadia's actual generated event-referential memories and causal traces, accessible or inaccessible; her actor-specific epistemic stances and current claim permissions; her fallible views of relations and other people; standing self-understanding where supportably derived. Her objective history may cause her without being recallable or claimable by her.

**(d) Person-wide and relation-scoped organization.** Durable but not context-free needs/expectations/strivings; interpreters/regulators/sensitivities/practices/tensions; their contradictions and histories; where generated, role/relation-class generalizations and counterpart-specific organization. They must not be flattened to trait scores or inferred immediately from objective social categories.

**(e) Selective exact historical dependencies and unresolved-status markers.** History retrieval/indexing permits exact admitted past occurrences, scope, causal parents, effective time, source and branch checks when a current action, expectation, claim, concealment, obligation, or physical behavior depends on them. `UNKNOWN`, `VALID_EMPTY`, `DEFERRED`, `UNAUTHORIZED`, and corruption/integrity failures must remain distinguishable; missing material is not a blank for the renderer to fill.

This is the pre-event **target envelope in semantic terms**, not a proposal to serialize all of (a)–(e) into one database record or supply them verbatim to Claude.

After `E_t` is admitted and its sensory/receivability conditions established, CAN-008 can carry out uptake/appraisal, situated recruitment, immediate simultaneous response streams and valid-empty outcomes; narrowed PFF may carry only its specialized interaction context/evidence and minimal authorized fast hypothesis/residue; V2 may make the outward occurrence concrete. Exact later durable effects return to competent owners. This sequence is a **consumption path**, not proof that each t-turn demands a full developmental rerun. See D001/01 §§2,5–6 and 101 §16A.

## 4. What must remain whole, what can be retrieved

`PERSON-STABLE != RELATION-FREE`  
`SELECTIVE HISTORY RETRIEVAL != SELECTIVE PERSONHOOD`  
`WHOLE PERSON != PROMPT`

**Whole current person** means a causally sufficient present distribution of available orientations/constraints, not a list of the last five remembered scenes. At minimum, the person-wide expressive substrate must preserve the presently action-relevant consequences of biography, contrasting relational roles, persistent needs/strivings, tensions and practices, material limits, acquired competence/register, independent agenda, standing self-understanding and contradictions. It cannot simply retrieve whatever is semantically similar to the latest user utterance and call that Nadia.

**Selectively retrievable** means exact older episodes and supporting detail may remain out of the active rendering context when their current effects are already faithfully represented or explicitly recoverable through causal dependency. Memory recall for Nadia must still pass ADP-016/017; analyst-side access to the ledger is not a prompt-level recall warrant.

**Compression is conditional and non-destructive.** Objective history survives append-only; current canonical state survives at exact owner grain; a summary is only a derived/indexed cache. A field may be absent from the actor-realization packet iff (1) it cannot change lawful present expression; (2) its present effect is in an adequate downstream projection; or (3) retrieval resolves the dependency **before** expression. This restates Sorok 101 §19 without claiming a currently verified sufficiency test. Qualitative, relational and contradictory structure is the material most likely to be destroyed by benign-looking prose compression.

**Direct vs precompiled is owner-relative.** For example, CAN-011 mediator records need not be revealed as narrator labels; their behavioral effects may constrain current realization. ADP-016 causal trace need not be explained as a memory; its effect may remain. A forbidden/private causal parent may condition action through lawfully compiled consequences but may not become an actor claim or a user-visible motive. Conversely, exact known prior promise or concrete accessible memory required for literal dialogue cannot be summarized so coarsely that Nadia makes an incompatible assertion. Sorok 101 §§5–6,18–19 establishes this distinction as a product requirement, not a solved compiler.

## 7. Laboratory surrogate: honest minimum

A later laboratory Nadia should have a **provenance-labeled hand-built fixture** rather than be described as a rebuilt V3 output. For each fixture:

1. Fix a pre-contact generated-person *surrogate* identity/lineage, objective biography, event chronology and bounded material ecology; distinguish shared-root facts from branch-local post-contact development. State openly which pieces are hand-authored.
2. Populate **separate** concrete examples of scope-specific expectations/identified strivings, overlapping mediators/tensions/practices, objective/perceived relations, two different relation-class/role patterns, independent projects/constraints and nondeclarative traces. Avoid universal scalar psychology and synthetic motivational defaults.
3. Carry a three-way knowledge court: objective canon; Nadia's actual generated-*surrogate* memories/beliefs/access; user-lawful observable information. Include a true fact Nadia cannot claim and a belief Nadia sincerely holds that is objectively false. These are annotated fixture facts, not an ADP-017 engine.
4. Specify at least one unresolved or genuinely absent authority field explicitly. A renderer must not repair it with biography or a convenient goal. Keep `VALID_EMPTY` distinct from `UNKNOWN`.
5. Provide a stable whole-person expressive reference with multiple *non-user* settings and relationships, then a current exact dyad slice and current material/world slice. Mark any compiled behavioral consequence as **hand-authored approximation** with precise causal parent refs.
6. Use paired scenes in which the **same** durable Nadia meets different counterpart/role contexts, and scenes where similar current cues recruit conflicting durable streams. Include physically grounded mundane/silent turns, no current durable effect, and hidden-cause nonleakage.
7. Compare full fixture against progressively thinner derived projections; test both single-turn noncontradiction and **distributional continuity**, independent agenda, actor-access nonleakage, competence/action bounds, relation differentiation and unsupported biography invention.
8. Independently inspect exact realized utterances, gestures/actions, object effects, material commitments and admission identity before the user phenomenal compositor; do not use iterative model semantic review as the normal live architecture.

**Surrogate validity classes:** `GOVERNED_OWNER_CONTRACT_EMULATION` for faithfully copied distinctions; `SOROK_PROVISIONAL_REQUIREMENT_FIXTURE` for role-prior/relational sufficiency; `OPEN_OWNER_ASSUMPTION` for user participation, joint project, competence/register and attractor semantics. None is `IMPLEMENTED_AND_PROVED`.

### Discriminating scenario battery

- Same Nadia with a secure sibling versus an authority figure versus this user; preserve both cross-relation continuity and difference. A dyad summary must not overwrite the other two.
- User names a real causal pattern correctly that Nadia cannot access; Nadia may resist without being secretly omniscient.
- Nadia's coworker needs a promised intervention during an intimate user conversation: competition from outside commitments should be causally real.
- Contradictory expectation postures remain scope-indexed; actor truth, objective truth, actor testimony and user inference remain separate.
- One scene reveals nothing significant; one old trace affects timing without recall; one unrecalled incident is not lazily quoted as dialogue.
- A physical task reveals bodily competence/limit and exact object history; no convenient instant skill or material magic.
- Duplicate the same pre-contact Nadia into two user branches, induce divergent relationship histories, then test that no branch contaminates the other.
- Withhold one expected semantic owner and demand the simulator mark the dependency unresolved rather than produce a pretty completion.

**Fail conditions:** generic sociable Nadia in every scene; apparent contradiction-free prose achieved by erasing tensions; actor claims analyst-only causes; a new user interaction manufactures old childhood memory; actor behavior tailored to user gaze-preferences; absent past episode filled from demographic priors; a user dyad displaces independent agenda; no attributable parent refs for new persistent effects. A passing staged demonstration would still not prove the full product or generalizable lossless projection.
