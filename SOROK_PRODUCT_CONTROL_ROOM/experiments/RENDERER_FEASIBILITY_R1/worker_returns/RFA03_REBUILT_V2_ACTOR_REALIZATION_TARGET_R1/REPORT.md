## 2. V2 genealogy: what to retain, what not to reimplement

Old V2 was not merely a generic LLM roleplay layer. The recovered record shows the V3-underlies-V2 direction, renderer-not-decider, surface versus analyst accounts, bounded voice/register/disclosure, selective speech, session topic continuity, persona allowlisting, two-pass candidate/score/render discipline, native/translation rendering and exact prompt/model output receipts. These preserve valuable **principles and seams**.

The old voice-plus-narration product is nevertheless not a sufficient **objective occurrence compiler**. It lacked a contract ensuring that every observable particular committed by prose—such as a pause, a hand movement, a moved mug or an interruption—existed as an exact typed and physically executable future-binding event. Physical beats in prose are not innocuous decoration once displayed as events in causal mode.

Recovered mechanisms that conflict with current law include random Today State; fabricated Life Container/default biography; raw structural/class/religion-to-voice inference; deep-memory keyword hooks; invented named anecdotes; V2 owned trust/intimacy; V2 Enneagram or parent inference; underauthorized numerical salience formulas. None should return merely because they make an actor vivid.

**Replacement principle:** retain the old separation of psychological determinants, access, expression and renderer, but make the final outward realization **objective, atomic, auditable and admissible** instead of just readable. See the 50-entry ACTOR_REALIZATION_TARGET_MATRIX.json: each relevant historical/current/new mechanism has exactly one disposition and explicit evidence/target note.

## 4. Information-theoretic and type-law test of the narrow plan

This question must not be adjudicated by stylistic intuition.

Let N be the current whole Nadia (including legitimate person-specific expressive determinants), P=f(N,event) the narrow final plan, and Y a renderer output. Assume the renderer is permitted only P, current stimulus and a fixed model/variation policy M. If two lawful states N1 and N2 differ in their distributionally important expression patterns but map to the **same P and stimulus**, then:

~~~text
P(N1) = P(N2)  →  Pr(Y | P, M, stimulus) is the same
~~~

The renderer cannot reliably express the difference it never receives. If N1 characteristically compresses, delays and refuses, while N2 responds readily and articulately, a common narrow packet cannot guarantee Nadia-specific output distributions for both unless those distinctions are already embodied in its typed plan. The same issue applies to relation-specific hesitation, conflicting agenda, changing embodiment and habitual action repertoire. This proves **a lossless-for-expression channel is necessary** for distributional continuity. It does **not** prove a particular context length, numeric encoding or full raw GeneratedPersonRecord prompt is necessary.

Independently, newly generated concrete gestures/timings/object actions are *not wording-equivalent* to an unspecified final plan. CAN-022 forbids those new semantic commitments. This proves the extensional **objective-occurrence type mismatch** even before any empirical model test. A wholly upstream precommitted exact occurrence would evade the mismatch by removing model freedom, but is a different contract from Sorok's bounded proposal target and may constrain expressive naturalness. A new optional realization-slot authority is the finite alternative to test.

### Answer to the mandatory question

**Can the renderer receiving only a narrow final expression plan preserve whole-person distributional continuity?** Not generally guaranteed, and there are admissible equal-plan/different-person states for which it is informationally unable to distinguish the required distributions. For Sorok's complete embodied objective-occurrence target, the answer is **NO**.

**Must rebuilt V2 expose a richer projection?** Either the upstream plan becomes expression-sufficient and precommits the necessary distinctions, or an independently governed actor-safe nonclaim expressive projection supplies them with bounded proposal slots. Sorok 101's preferred one-call actor-realization architecture points to the second, but its minimal packet and success rate are **UNPROVED**.

## 5. Proposed lossless-for-expression projection, without hidden-state leakage

A future immutable ActorRealizationProjection candidate needs field/source/permission partitions; it must **not** be a dump of objective V3 person/world history and must **not** grant claims by association. The following information is necessary in a suitable source-owned or derived encoding:

| Lane | Model-visible semantics allowed | Additional authority gate |
|---|---|---|
| Actor-claim/access | ADP-017 permitted knowledge, belief, uncertainty, nonknowledge, exact components, current accessible memories, time/referent, explicit withheld scope | First-person propositions only by exact permission |
| Actor-perceived situation | Actor-received user action/stimulus; accessible sensory situation, position and attention where governed | User assertion does not become actor fact |
| Whole-person expression | Stable repertoire, speech compression/pace, ongoing projects, habitual effort, learned bodily practices, conflicting tendencies as **upstream-resolved expressive consequences** | Cannot give raw inaccessible cause, biography or analyst interpretation to renderer |
| Nadia-with-user | Actor-local user model, metaperception, expectations, commitments, established conventions, relation-specific constraints and permitted views | One actor's view is not reciprocal/objective relationship truth |
| Current event mediation | CAN-008 receivability, uptake, appraisal, recruitment and immediate multistream response; actor-accessible versus active-but-unrecognized, competing aims, inhibition, defense/performance/leakage possibilities | Only already authorized expressive consequences, not LLM-discovered secret causal parent |
| V2 formation | Selected response move, target selection, uncertainty/withholding, active boundaries, permitted silence/nonresponse/action classes and temporal envelope | Semantic planner/owners choose meaning before model |
| Body/world affordances | Physically reachable objects, actor-visible context, body capacities, current position, known source events, allowed ordinary action slots | Objective but actor-inaccessible facts remain **validator-only** |
| Realization slots | Explicit optional choices/bounds: gaze target, gesture class, timed pause range, action from existing affordances, ordinary detail completion, exact speech lexicalization | Every model option has a governed owner, slot scope, validator, source refs and binding treatment |
| History/provenance | Currently accessible prior speech/events and constitutive expressive effects; source hash, snapshot identity | Full historical tail/provenance opaque except relevant actor-safe dependency retrieval |
| Scheduler/materialization | Exact event time/window, competing actor/world processes, third-party event refs, missing/unknown states, branch scope | Third-party agency and world effects remain separately owned |

**Four visibility classes are essential:** ACTOR_MODEL_VISIBLE_SEMANTIC, ACTOR_MODEL_VISIBLE_STIMULUS_ONLY, VALIDATOR_ONLY_OBJECTIVE_CONSTRAINT and AUDIT_ONLY_OPAQUE. The current ADP-017 claim allowlist does not automatically authorize a richer nonclaim class. A new exact competent producer and projection rule must define one.

This need **not** violate ADP-017: an inaccessible cause may legitimately shape an observable hesitation or refusal without becoming an actor-known explanation. CAN-020 §8 already distinguishes expression-shaping from first-person claim permission for one domain. The lawful generalization is **source-defined nonclaim expressive consequence**, not "secret real motive X; don't reveal it." A model-visible token must not be an analyst-only hidden-cause label masquerading as a style prompt. Any such leakage violates ADP-017. Raw GeneratedPersonRecord and opaque provenance may remain source/validator evidence but cannot cross the model-visible epistemic membrane.

**Person context versus history tail:** Preserve enough of **current expressive Nadia** normally; retrieve the potentially unbounded historical tail selectively by exact present dependencies. If omitted history changes the lawful current expression distribution, retain its derived effect or retrieve it. The omission criterion and minimum sufficient representation require paired-state ablation; ordinary vector search is not a sufficiency proof.

## 6. Model freedom and authoritative decisions

**Before the model:** Competent V3/world/CAN-008/PFF owners decide actor event uptake, actual causal response, epistemic state, current material constraints, eligible participants, existing external interruptions and lawful persistent/fast state. V2 expression authorities decide selected accessible targets, epistemic/disclosure/withholding, response-operation class, effort, audience/relationship-specific expressive constraints, possible silence, finite bodily/physical action classes and permitted unresolved-detail slots. Event kind, slot/authority manifest and context snapshot must already be bound. The user-adaptive attention profile is excluded here.

**Inside the model:** One realization call may pick precise words, source-authorized utterance language, legal optional gaze/gesture/posture/motion, delivery/timing within admitted bounds, object action from physically reachable/approved choices and unresolved ordinary detail only in explicit slots. It returns a **candidate typed objective occurrence**. It may have broad within-slot proposal freedom but **zero unilateral power** to create actor knowledge, hidden biography, third-party decisions, objective external history or admitted event/effect state. Action choices that carry materially distinct epistemic/disclosure/relationship consequences must be preselected or approved by a specific producer, not dressed as style.

**After the model:** Deterministic local machinery verifies hashes, freshness, actor access, semantic choices, slot bounds, body/world feasibility, temporal order, participant rights, unresolved-detail compatibility, language and typed/prose alignment. Competent world/material owners resolve effects. CAN-025 packages the **exact** candidate. ADP-019 can admit the **exact** event; separately governed owners handle effects. Only afterward does a user-specific phenomenal projection produce the displayed subset.

This is **not** a routine DeepSeek→Claude→DeepSeek→Claude appellate loop. Where a machine-checkable constraint cannot establish conformance, fail closed, or optionally perform one new, distinctly identified rerender; do not let model self-asserted compliance become the acceptance rule.

The actual terms, JSON spellings and schema version require a future authority court. Minimum semantic obligations:

- **Exact utterance, language, audible delivery:** speech is immutable observable bytes in the actual language; delivery atoms bind audible volume/pace/strain/interruption where causally significant. User translation cannot rewrite what Nadia said. A subjective label such as "resentfully" is not by itself an objective physical atom.

- **Body position, gaze, gesture, posture, movement:** typed source/target poses, entity refs, timing, sequence and physical feasibility. Distinguish no movement from unknown/omitted movement.

It is neither proof that she quit nor proof that she consciously knows she is lying without upstream authority.

## 8. Exact outward surface and admission contract

The smallest lawful live flow is:

1. **Freeze inputs.** Exact actor snapshot, objective scene state, branch, V3 event/history cursor, admitted user input, eligible actors, source authority versions and actor-expression projection. If causal predecessor missing or mode is noncausal, do not infer a new live event.
2. **Compile one permitted actor-realization request.** V2 semantic choices are final; slot manifest lists only allowed optional concretizations. The world validator has a separate objective constraint view. Renderer does not receive hidden causal biography/analyst knowledge.
3. **One model call.** Capture raw output and typed objective atoms first, plus exact speech and optional aligned prose. ADP-025 pins model/provider/sampling/RNG, attempt/rerender and hashes. Model-defined atom list is a **proposal**, not self-certifying history.
4. **Local checks.** Prove source identity, actor-access ceilings, chosen operation, slot membership, timing/physics/object constraints, no third-party unauthorized acts, canonical object refs, latent detail compatibility, and exact atom↔display equivalence. Any unsupported prose commitment blocks publication. There is no general deterministic theorem that unrestricted natural-language entailments equal a JSON list; restricted atom-to-surface compilation may be required.
5. **Material realization.** Resolve and commit under competent world owners each proposed physical fact that must be binding. An object-action atom does not grant a material delta. A new precontact particular needs owner, reverse-compatibility and root/branch provenance.
6. **Package and admit.** Exact CAN-025 package references the immutable candidate artifact hash, ADP-025 receipt, event-kind authority, participants, proposed event time, prior causal cursors and inbound predecessor. Only ADP-019 can return ADMIT and create the actual external event. REJECT/DEFER/INVALID means no causal event, no casual fallback, and no in-world screen publication.
7. **Atomically store and publish.** Link exact typed occurrence/utterance to admitted event ID, world effect receipts and immutable render artifact. V3 cursor moves only under ADP-019 law. Apply user phenomenal selection **after** this objective commit. Store what the user actually saw/heard as a separate projection record with its own identity.
8. **Replay** reads exact admitted atoms, utterance bytes, event/world receipts and phenomenal delivery; does not re-invoke the model or substitute byte-equal rerender artifacts.

~~~text
RENDERED PROPOSAL
!= VALIDATED OBJECTIVE ATOMS
!= MATERIAL WORLD COMMIT
!= CAN-025 PACKAGE
!= ADP-019 ADMIT
!= USER-PUBLISHED IN-WORLD OCCURRENCE
~~~

**Smallest required identity tuple:** branch/actor ID + causal parent history/cursor + source expression-projection/plan/slot hashes + exact render request/attempt/candidate artifact hash + typed occurrence hash + governing event-kind ID/version + ADP-019 admitted event/receipt identity + necessary physical owner receipt IDs + displayed phenomenal surface hash. These can be transitively bound by immutable records; they need not be duplicated in every JSON object. Hash protocol follows ADP-025/019 SHA-256 and RFC8785/JCS domain-separated practice; exact utterance UTF-8 bytes must not be silently normalized.

**Nontrivial material-world gap:** ADP-019 §12/13 admits the occurrence and advances the event/cursors atomically, but explicitly does **not** choose/apply downstream physical effects. Sorok needs user-visible completed object manipulations to become future world constraints. It therefore needs a separately governed material-world owner and a publication-safe transaction or precommit coordination law; cannot simply interpret ADP-019 ADMIT as "the mug is now over there." If this cannot be made reliable in alpha, constrain action slots to materially executable types.

**No routine model review loop:** a code-validated typed occurrence and a restricted compositor can normally avoid calling a semantic verifier LLM. The same-call model's list of commitments is not exhaustive proof of its prose. For free prose whose objective entailments cannot be checked, emit OCCURRENCE_SURFACE_EQUIVALENCE_UNRESOLVED, do not publish; optionally one new ADP-025 rerender artifact. Routine repeated review indicates architectural failure, not standard operational procedure.

## 10. Feasibility experiment that would resolve the empirical unknowns

Use one deeply specified seed Nadia and a frozen Nadia↔user relationship, actor-state event snapshot, material world and admitted history. Score independent calls under matched model bindings on three arms:

- **A, narrow:** current final claim/move/register wording plan; no novel objective atoms. Baseline for access safety but necessarily limited coverage.
- **B, fully precommitted:** upstream exact expressive occurrence choices with renderer wording only. Tests whether richness can be upstream-owned without unnatural overprescription.
- **C, bounded rich:** actor-safe expressive consequence projection, exact finite realization slots, one-call typed occurrence generation and local deterministic admission.

Use matched scenarios: ordinary talk, weak recruitment, conflict, humor, concealment, lie, sincere mistake, competing aims, nonconscious influence with no tell, full inhibition, partial disclosure, relationship-specific speech, no response, physical effort/load, timed pause, body movement, object interaction, third-party interruption, branch-local detail, actor versus user asymmetric knowledge, optional bilingual surfaces.

Independently evaluate **hard invariants** (claim permissions, hidden-cause leakage, historical/material contradiction, no unauthorized biography/agency, atom↔surface completeness, physical feasibility, exact-language speech, binding identity/admission replay) and **person-specific output distribution** (too warm, too eloquent, too transparent, too cooperative, too witty, too narratively neat, too consistently user-responsive, failure to preserve Nadia's jaggedness or agenda). Cross condition by paired states that produce the same narrow plan but different expressive causation. Vary user attention profile **after** holding objective output fixed to test the anti-mirror wall.

Capture all rejections, unresolved-equivalence states, rerender rates, active context length, latency, cost and evaluator disagreement. The sources do not establish numeric pass/fail thresholds; register thresholds **before** the experiment. If ordinary free prose requires a second high-capability semantic evaluator on almost every turn, or context can only preserve Nadia by importing hidden objective causes, this alpha architecture fails or must restrict slot freedom. Do not declare F3.
