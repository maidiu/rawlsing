## 3. Minimum semantically complete pre-realization target

The target is a composition of seven distinct planes. These are analytical planes, not seven required storage objects.

### A. Exact current occurrence basis

Before actor realization there must be an exact generated/admitted current event basis, with identity, hash/provenance, effective causal time, actor/counterpart scope, and occurrence authority.

For user/external interaction, machine interpretation may accompany the admitted event as bounded modality evidence, but it cannot replace the occurrence.

The target also needs the objective sensory/perceptual opportunity relevant to the actor: what was physically or modality-wise available to Nadia, not what she actually noticed.

### B. Read-only current person/world conditioning basis

The current event must be evaluated against exact source-owner bindings, not a copied mini-person:

- current relationship/history state and actor-perceived relationship material where lawful;
- existing memory and causal-trace refs;
- current actor epistemic/access state;
- current needs/expectations/strivings;
- current mediators/tensions/practices;
- current material/organismic state;
- exact current world/material conditions;
- exact prior CAN-008 response history where a rule uses it;
- prior lawful PFF fast carryover only where needed.

These remain owned by their competent durable authorities.

### C. CAN-008 R2 person×event coupling

Before realization, the actor side must have a typed result for:

- actor receivability;
- actor uptake;
- actor appraisal;
- situated recruitment;
- multiplicity/contradiction;
- any lawful current effort/reachable-response-bandwidth constraint;
- immediate multi-stream response/metabolism;
- valid-empty, ambiguous, unresolved, or blocked outcomes.

Uptake and recruitment may be mutually conditioning. They need not be forced into a simplistic serial pipeline, but they must remain separately inspectable/reconstructible.

### D. Actor-perspectival current state

The actor-realization boundary must distinguish:

- content currently actor-accessible;
- content causally active but actor-inaccessible;
- uncertainty/nonknowledge;
- present focal/peripheral organization where the case actually depends on it;
- current self-explanation where one exists;
- no self-explanation where none exists.

Hidden cause must not be turned into actor insight merely because the machine can explain it.

### E. Narrowed PFF interaction specialization

PFF may contribute only the narrowed interaction functions preserved by the current reconciliation:

A renderer output that makes these cases impossible is not preserving Rawlsing's person.

## 7. Required scenario stress tests

### S1 — user correctly identifies a pattern Nadia does not understand

User says, for example, "You go quiet whenever you think I might leave."

The utterance may be exactly admitted. Machine interpretation may correctly identify a canonical pattern. Nadia may receive the literal words but not the deeper causal relation. A relevant mediator/trace may still be recruited. Her current accessible content may contain discomfort, disagreement, uncertainty, or selected memories without containing the machine's causal account. She may ask for evidence, reject the interpretation, change subject, or give a partial response.

Illicit result: machine correctness becomes Nadia self-knowledge or a durable belief merely because the user happened to be right.

### S2 — user incorrectly attributes a motive

User says, "You're jealous."

The assertion is a real external event, not world truth. CAN-008 may constitute an actor-local appraisal of the accusation. Nadia may reject it, entertain it, misread why the user said it, or be uncertain. The engine must not install the user's attribution as Nadia's motive.

Illicit result: user speech becomes objective psychic fact.

### S3 — Nadia deliberately lies

Objective truth and actor knowledge remain intact while communicative intention licenses a false utterance, for example "I quit" when Nadia knows she was fired.

The outward occurrence is the exact false utterance. Its propositional content does not rewrite objective truth.

Illicit result: noncontradiction validator "corrects" the lie or renderer treats truthful speech as mandatory.

### S4 — Nadia sincerely says something false

Nadia may have a false belief, misremembered event, incomplete model, or causally wrong self-explanation and report it sincerely.

Illicit result: machine objective truth is injected into her answer to make it accurate.

### S5 — hidden cause, no behavioral tell

A hidden causal trace or regulatory mediator may be recruited while current response and inhibition produce no detectable surface evidence.

Illicit result: the system manufactures gaze shifts, delays, humor, or other "tells" so the user can solve the actor.

### S6 — hidden cause with lawful indirect consequences

The hidden source remains inaccessible, but its downstream effect may alter response coverage, topic return, directness, bodily action, or another already-authorized expression consequence.

Illicit result: the indirect consequence carries the hidden causal explanation along with it.

### S7 — event received but omitted from response

Nadia receives a thread and understands it, but current effort, competing agenda, inhibition, or communicative choice leaves it unaddressed.

Illicit result: omission is rewritten as nonreception, ignorance, defense, or indifference.

### S8 — Nadia fails to receive part of the event

The semantic transducer notices a nuance in the user's wording. Actor receivability excludes it or leaves it unresolved. Uptake therefore lacks that nuance.

Illicit result: V2/renderer rescues the machine nuance because it would make the reply more coherent.

### S9 — trivial exchange, valid-empty durable consequence

A routine "thanks" may be receivable and understood yet produce low-significance or valid-empty recruitment/response consequences and zero durable owner effect.

Illicit result: filler intimacy, trust, insight, or biography is synthesized to make every turn "matter."

### S10 — relation-specific history changes uptake without globally changing Nadia

The same words from this user and from a stranger may couple differently because current relationship/history refs differ. The durable global person need not change.

Illicit result: a relation-specific current configuration is generalized into a global personality mutation.

### S11 — contradictory response streams coexist

The same event may simultaneously be appraised as cooperative in one respect and costly/disruptive in another. Different exact expectations, strivings, mediators, or practical constraints may remain active. No winner is required.

Illicit result: collapse to one net valence, one dominant motive, one emotion label, or one "response style."

## 9. Genuine runtime-semantic gaps

These are not merely absent code.

### G1 — concrete CAN-008 R2 coupling/response rule inventory

R2 defines the authority grammar and exact obligations, but it does not supply the concrete rule inventory for arbitrary interaction events. A real runtime still needs event-kind/scope-specific rules that lawfully determine receivability, uptake/appraisal, recruitment, current-condition use, and response composition.

A laboratory fixture may hand-author expected R2 outputs, but that does not close G1.

### G2 — event-time focal/peripheral actor-conscious organization

PFF strongly requires accessibility != focality and represents present-conscious-field phenomena conceptually. CAN-008 R2 explicitly does not own focality. The formal PFF freeze rejects a persistent conscious-field store and allows focality/current-condition facts only where materially required.

What is still missing is an exact current runtime semantic authority/composition for forming the event-time focal/peripheral actor-perspectival view when realization depends on it.

This is a missing operation, not a missing database table.

### G3 — event-time actor self-explanation formation

Current authority clearly says actor self-explanation != machine causal account and may be absent, partial, or wrong. But there is no single accepted current contract inspected here that constitutes a new event-time self-explanation from accessible material while preserving those limits.

If a response depends on what Nadia thinks is causing her own state, the surrogate must either supply an explicit fixture or mark the case unresolved.

### G4 — communicative intention / inhibition / presentation composition for the full scenario range

The conceptual distinctions are strong, and V2 has accepted boundaries for actor access, register/withholding, response moves, relationship expression, and render admission. But the current V2 response-move authority intentionally registers only the ordinary ENGAGE_SELECTIVE base operation and a valid-empty special-operation registry. Recovered labels such as silence, challenge, deflect, exit, etc. are not automatically canonical.

Therefore the full event-time semantics needed for deliberate lying, strategic concealment, some forms of silence/challenge, and mixed automatic/conscious presentation are not completely supplied by the inspected current contracts.

Do not solve this by creating a persistent "defensive style."

### G5 — relation-indexed durable source sufficiency

D001/03 identifies a real product sufficiency question: whether current durable owners provide enough lawful relation-class, role-conditioned, specific-dyad, internalized-other, and network-context material for CAN-008 to recruit what the product requires.

The relationalization delta is still hypothesis-level. If a stress test requires a relation-indexed prior that no current owner can actually represent, that is a V3/source-owner semantic gap, not permission for PFF to invent it.

### G6 — objective actor/world realization authority beyond wording-only V2

Artifact 101 provisionally requires one actor/world realization step that may produce exact speech, gesture, body action, timing, object action, and world effects before user phenomenal projection.

The inspected CAN-022 current authority is stricter: renderer wording only over an already-final semantic plan; unsupported physical details are forbidden.

The product target is coherent, but the authoritative seam that licenses and admits model-proposed observable particulars/body/world actions is not yet fully reconciled with the older wording-only render boundary.

### G7 — exact durable-owner update rules for arbitrary current interaction consequences

Durable owner sovereignty is clear. What is not universally supplied is the concrete owner-specific rule that says when this exact user/world event creates, revises, or leaves unchanged a CAN-010/011, ADP-015/016/017, CAN-012, or MUST-018 state.

Missing target-owner authority must remain a typed block/defer, not a fabricated no-change.
