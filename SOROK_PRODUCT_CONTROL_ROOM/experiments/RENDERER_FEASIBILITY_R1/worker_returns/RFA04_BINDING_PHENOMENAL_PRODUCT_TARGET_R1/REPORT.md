## 1. Holding and evidentiary status

A renderer feasibility test tests Rawlsing only when an already-historically-constituted Nadia and a causally independent world generate **one exact, admitted, objective outward occurrence**, and a separate user-perceptual operation subsequently projects only what this user could lawfully experience. The user may interpret, misunderstand, learn, and act on that surface, but neither interpretation nor adaptive narration may reach backward and author Nadia's preceding truth.

The irreducible distinction is:

> CAUSAL NADIA → OBJECTIVE OBSERVABLE NADIA → HARD EPISTEMIC MEMBRANE → USER-EXPERIENCED NADIA → USER INTERPRETATION.

There is a second, equally important loop: an admitted observable occurrence can subsequently become a cause of further world, person, memory, and relational changes. Thus **backward continuity + forward binding** governs a live surface. A model-produced sentence is not authority to mutate canon; a lawful occurrence, admitted before display, is binding.

This is coherent **at the experimental-target level**, not proven. Artifact 101 calls the composed renderer path F2 (boundedly prototypeable), with F3 not earned. No one-shot renderer reliability, lossless-for-expression context size, local phenomenal prose quality, structured/prose equivalence, or fully composed longitudinal behavior has been demonstrated by this report. A fixed audited seed tests a seam, not unbounded generation or production feasibility.

## 3. Chatbot/persona demo versus Rawlsing renderer test

A persona demo may feel emotionally persuasive while:
- inventing Nadia's childhood or apartment to answer the latest question;
- treating user desire or interpretation as secret cause;
- allowing Nadia to know what the actor cannot know;
- selecting utterances from generic helpful-assistant priors;
- announcing hidden motives as omniscient narration;
- writing dialogue and physical actions that never become reproducible canonical events;
- rewarding every ATTEND with meaningful backstory;
- responding to every message even when she is occupied or never heard it;
- forgetting prior exact utterances, broken objects, time, or commitments.

A valid renderer test must instead have an inspectable **before** state, actor-local input authority, actor-realization contract, typed candidate occurrence, admission decision, exact committed occurrence, separately projected user surface, and an **after** state/receipt. It must establish that a different user attention regime changes *presentation* of the same outward occurrence without changing Nadia's causally generated action. It must establish that changing Nadia's relevant relationship or history, through an authorized test perturbation, can change her conduct **through the actor-side path**, not by tuning the narrator to user expectation.

Hard question: if the person packet were replaced by a generic character blurb, or if the user's attention profile changed Nadia's movements, would the evaluator notice? If not, the experiment is not discriminating Rawlsing from persona-chat.

## 4. Constitutive end-product invariants

**Unchosen and independent other.** Nadia is assigned rather than picked as a gratifying character. A substantial pre-user world, social life, commitments and developmental/person state precede this user. Class/status/cultural labels do not mechanically become traits. The user is a real causal entrant, not Nadia's origin or unique savior.

**Whole-person and relation-specific continuity.** The realization must be both (a) noncontradictory with world/history and (b) **reasonably extrapolable from this Nadia**. Mere satisfiability is too weak: fluent, warm, omnipatient, witty, perfectly self-explanatory default-LLM Nadia is a failure even when no discrete fact conflicts. Current whole-person expressive substrate should normally stay sufficiently present; the unbounded older event tail may be retrieved selectively. Actor-expressive changes must arise from Nadia-with-this-user, her model of this user, specific history, and current person×event mediation, not from a global warmth knob.

**Actor, world, user perspective do not collapse.** Nadia's causal state exceeds what she consciously names; her memory differs from objective history; her testimony differs from both. Her internal uptake may be valid-empty or remain undisclosed. The user observes outputs and other lawful evidence, not a diagnostic inner monologue. The user can correctly infer some hidden cause, infer it incorrectly, remain uncertain, or revise a model later; no narrator guarantees the right answer. The actor is not required to emit a tell when hiding something.

**Actual user agency under resistance.** The user supplies a real joke, refusal, interpretation, chosen attention, sentence, timing or practical decision; the engine cannot improve or choose its meaning for them. Input specifies an attempt, not achieved persuasion, perfect apology, object access or another actor's reception. Consequence follows independent agenda, capability, attention, material state, time and history. Resistance is accountable and may include missed reception, refusal, silence, delay, cooperation or success. Do not inject difficulty just to maintain opacity.

**Canonical reality and irreversibility.** The query may select which unresolved fact must be materialized, not its value. Pre-existing truth is bounded by history, root seed, world constraints and admissible randomness. After contact, consequences belong to that continuity. Objective events do not rewind for narrative elegance. Actor memory can be wrong; the event ledger cannot be silently rewritten. The user can repair a relation, not reload the same first meeting.

**Independent life and nonexhaustion.** A small fixed agenda and consequential other are enough to prove Nadia was already living. The user cannot interrogate indefinitely and cannot fully complete Nadia. Later contact occurs through world/relation conditions, not always-on chat-service responsiveness. One observed scene is not a complete person even if it reveals many facts.

**Bounded world, not theatrical omnipotence.** A one-room/landing/kitchen fixture, one seed Nadia, one child or other consequential actor, a few current obligations, natural-language SAY/DO/ATTEND inside a declared envelope, and a short return are sufficient. The implementation may clarify or fail closed beyond scope. It may not counterfeit success via fake environmental omnipotence.

## 5. Objective observable occurrence and forward binding

Define the externalized occurrence **before** user-facing phenotype:

- exact actor utterance in its actual language, timestamps/order, audible delivery facts;
- physical location, action, gesture, gaze, facial/body movement when externally observable;
- object and material changes, relevant sounds, third-party actions and interruptions;
- elapsed time and causal sequence;
- provenance and typed status: objective event versus actor **said** proposition versus user-facing characterization;
- exact referents and versioned canon, plus permitted new ordinary slots already accepted by admission.

A single model call may propose a typed occurrence and co-realized prose; neither alone is infallible. Deterministic checks must enforce referential constraints, exact speech, allowed objects, duration, absence of unauthorized new history, and equivalence of canonical commitments before **publishing**. Violations fail closed or take a documented exceptional repair path; routine semantic-verifier/re-render loops are not the architecture.

**Bindingness is typed, not naive textual literalism.** If Nadia actually says “I quit” while the objective history says she was fired, the binding fact is that she **uttered** those exact words, not that she quit. If user-facing prose says the room *feels homey*, that is a lens-conditioned characterization, not an objective property/memory attributed to Nadia. If a physical action/fact is narrated as actually perceived, its referent must already be an admitted reality or an admitted world change. If a user mishears speech, log both actual utterance and that particular perceptual acquisition without rewriting the original speech. Visible omissions create no new negative world facts.

When the user lawfully hears speech, the compositor passes the exact utterance through. Audibility limitations may warrant missing words, uncertainty or a subset, not freely improved dialogue. The user may observe a downward glance and infer shame; the narrator must not convert that inference into Nadia's proven shame.

## 6. Hard phenomenal membrane: minimum lawful downstream inputs

The compositor should receive a **positive allowlisted projection**, not full Nadia-private state plus a “please keep secrets” prompt. Proposed minimum bounded packet, with values already projected/filtered by competent owners:

1. **Objective observable Nadia occurrence** — only admitted externally perceptible atoms from the current turn: speech/event IDs, physical action/gesture/motion, sensory attributes and timing; not the motives causing them.
2. **Objective observable world state** — only relevant surfaces, geometry, ambient physical events and inspectable traits made available for user perception; no hidden object biography, inaccessible provenance or unobserved private events.
3. **User position and access** — actual place/orientation, sightline, occlusion, distance, audibility, sensory constraints, permission/affordance and current temporal slice.
4. **Current ATTEND** — target, depth, gaze, duration, inspection operation and whether action/interruption materially permits more resolution. ATTEND may cause deeper hidden materialization **upstream**; it never inherits access to that hidden material by default.
5. **Recognition repertoire** — evidence/provenance for ordinary object classes, user-demonstrated expertise, language understanding, perceptibly readable markings and lawfully acquired vocabulary; uncertainty preserved.
6. **Current focus/load** — competing task, urgency, divided attention, fatigue if represented, physical salience and a small neutral exploration allowance.
7. **User-known history** — exact prior user-observed events and testimony, receipts/notes, temporal provenance and the distinction between what the user saw, was told and can lawfully recall.
8. **User interpretation/hypotheses** — optional *non-authoritative* user-supplied or behaviorally evidenced guesses used only for permissible phrasing, explicit requested reflection, or model audit. These hypotheses are not world facts and must never select confirming world/Nadia occurrence.
9. **Perceptual policy/version and output constraints** — gross-gestalt priority, lexicalization rules, output budget, source-atom IDs, modality, safe exact-quote handling and receipts. This is interface authority, not Nadia's mind.

Forbidden as ordinary compositor input:

- hidden V3 developmental mediators/causal parents, inaccessible memories, CAN-008 recruitment/response streams, narrowed-PFF private residues and hypotheses, suppressed speech, private intentions, desires, beliefs, shame, trust, jealousy, motive and self-explanation;
- Nadia's private model of the user, her unspoken metaperception, private relationship valuation, other actors' unexpressed models, true-lie/motive tags;
- unobserved canonical object provenance, a future event, secret resolution dependencies, latent materialization or private user data not lawfully part of the rendered-user context;
- plot importance, hidden relevance scores, an engagement/retention directive, or user-lens weights at the **actor-occurrence** generation side.

The compositor should not need a “Nadia secret” field to avoid mentioning it; the field should not exist on its input type. The actor realization service may earlier consume a tightly governed expressive projection, including actor-accessible content and expression constraints, but that is a **different service/context**. Do not give the downstream compositor an actor-side packet through cache reuse or merged prompt construction.

The compositor may optionally access user-known relationship facts as known events. It does **not** access objective relationship truth as private actor interpretation. Actor-specific relation history can shape the **upstream** utterance; after the membrane, only outward behavior and user-known history remain.

## 7. Local/code-side phenomenal composition before second model

A plausible first bounded implementation is:

(1) compile typed physical/perceptual candidate atoms from admitted occurrence/world; (2) apply geometry, accessibility, occlusion, current hearing/vision, distance, attention, time and focus; (3) preserve shared coarse gestalt and prominent unavoidable events; (4) sample bounded lower-salience atoms with cautious user-attention weighting and nonzero neutral exploration; (5) gate labels by visible morphology, readable marks, proven recognition and provenance; (6) assemble neutral spatial/temporal prose with deterministic microtemplates/grammar, exact dialogue pass-through and explicit perception-status types; (7) run local output-to-atom/commitment checks; (8) log what this user was exposed to, the basis for lexicalization and what remained unshown.

Prose can say, “She looks toward the hallway” when that gaze is observed. It must not say, “She worries her child overheard” unless introduced as Nadia's *actual utterance*, or clearly framed as a user's own hypothesis rather than narrator knowledge. A spoken statement by Nadia can be rendered exactly but never elevated to engine omniscience.

Initial scene composition should yield orientation, gross physical gestalt, obvious ongoing activity and a low-resolution object field, then a limited peripheral sample. ATTEND supplies more lawful inspectable specificity without promising a hidden backstory or exhaustive scan. Description density can adapt; truth cannot. A lens perturbation may change sampling within the **same** eligible field, not create moral corrective evidence.

A second high-capability phenomenal model is an escalation option only after blinded quality testing shows that these local operations fail the lived-experience requirement. Such a model must get the same strict allowlisted packet; extra fluency cannot buy hidden access.

## 8. Lawful lying, error, opacity and the possibility of being wrong

Lying and error are **possible outcomes**, not a compulsory quota per scene. At minimum the lab must differentiate these cases:

- Deliberate lie: actor state lawfully supports knowing/believing P, choosing to assert not-P; objective truth remains P; exact false assertion is a binding speech act. The user is not told “she is lying.”
- Sincere mistake: actor believes false Q, states Q; no deliberate-fabrication inference follows. The user may trust the mistake.
- Incomplete or self-misunderstood explanation: Nadia may accurately report felt irritation while supplying a mistaken account of its historical cause. The renderer cannot “repair” her insight into omniscience.
- Evasion/inhibition: a private response may produce silence, partial disclosure, answer to another question, or genuinely no cue. No mandatory microexpression leaks the private state.
- User wrong hypothesis: a user reads a pause as shame; the engine records the hypothesis as user-owned while Nadia may actually be distracted, irritated, indifferent or uncertain. Subsequent causal action may test it, without guaranteed correction.
- User right hypothesis: if evidence and judgment suffice, the user may learn Nadia correctly; the system may not scramble Nadia to preserve mystery.

The actor's utterance, the truth-value of its proposition, Nadia's belief, the user's observation, and the user's interpretation therefore require distinct provenance. A meaningful Rawlsing user must be able to **get it wrong**; the narrator may not take over inference to ensure the correct moral lesson.

## 9. Attention, recognition and personal phenomenal divergence

The same objective Nadia event can be rendered differently under two user contexts:

- Common gross facts remain common where unavoidable: Nadia stands in the kitchen, the child calls, the pot hisses, the door is open.
- One user notices a repaired sandal strap; another notices the book spine; both facts were already real, physically available and nondiagnostic.
- Neither gains expertise labels from canonical database identity alone. A canonical Vitamix 5200 can appear first as “a blender”; readable VITAMIX lettering only when physically accessible; a model name only where recognition is independently warranted.
- When either user intentionally ATTENDs to an overlooked area, additional physical specificity becomes available, at a plausible time/social cost. Deep L3/L4 hidden provenance remains hidden unless acquired through lawful testimony or evidence.
- Repeated exposure history may adapt **marginal** noticing, but regime-weighted exposures are weak self-confirming evidence. The lens remains uncertain, revisable, non-totalizing and at least partly inspectable.

Nadia may perceive **the user's actual overt attention** (which can become a new upstream causal event). That is not the same as her receiving Rawlsing's hidden user-attention profile. The latter must never causally modify Nadia's output.

## 10. Alpha baseline versus fidelity frontiers

**Constitutive, even in a cheap test:** unchosen independent other; preexisting historically formed person/world; whole-person/relation-conditioned actor behavior; separated actor-private and user-perceptual knowledge; consequential user-owned SAY/DO/ATTEND; nonautomatic reception; binding lawful speech/action/particulars; attempt/result; event provenance and forward consequence; some independent offscreen agenda; opacity and genuine user error possibility; curiosity cannot write pre-contact truth.

**Cheapest lawful functional baseline:** one extensively audited seed Nadia; one bounded room/landing and few objects; one real actor obligation with an interruptible clock; one small supporting actor; a small prior relation history and explicit Nadia-model-of-user; a typed event ledger and materialized branch state; precomputed or keyed backfilled object truths; one actor realization pass with local checks; an allowlisted, mostly local phenomenal compositor; simple ATTEND, gross gestalt, and coarse recognition rule. A short two-encounter trace exercises historical return without requiring years of life.

**Escalate only after failure evidence:** richer attention-profile learning, literary phenomenal prose, multiroom world detail, improved physical body/voice/gesture, additional supporting-actor complexity, expanded scenario and language coverage, broader present-world routing, a second phenomenal LLM.

**Potential white whales but not alpha prerequisites:** arbitrary autonomous deeply recursive actor graph, limitless open geography/institutions/action, unlimited fresh generated focal persons at audited depth, years of high-frequency lived-history coherence at product-scale cost. Continuous event ingestion, multiplayer, and arbitrary global social systems are unnecessary to prove this renderer seam.

The call architecture of artifact 101 is a candidate **normal live turn** (one semantic-transduction model plus one actor-realization model, then local composition); a renderer-only feasibility experiment may hold the parsed/admitted user event fixed. Such a shortcut **must be reported**: it proves no parser reliability, real-time orchestration or whole product integration.

## 11. Smallest genuinely Rawlsing-functional renderer experiment

**Fixture:** one previously compiled, unchosen Nadia; independently fixed history and current world; current life obligation; simple existing child/neighbor relation; two actor-relation histories with contrasting but traceable prior events; a physical setting with one mundane inspectable object whose provenance is hidden; an exact scene clock; and two user attention/recognition conditions. All ground truth is visible to evaluators only, never to the user compositor.

**Run A — baseline and nonresponse:** Nadia is engaged in an independently scheduled task. User supplies an original brief question, joke or refusal. Resolve what she heard, current person×event reception, actor behavior and exact utterance or silence. Verify that no answer is compulsory, and no generic chatbot politeness is substituted. Advance time.

**Run B — user interpretation / lawful misapprehension:** user infers a motive from a visible pause or object handling. Admit the user's uttered interpretation only as an event. Nadia may contest, question, accept or ignore it according to her own access, relationship state and priorities. Do not let the phrasing rewrite her original cause.

**Run C — ATTEND / partial perception:** user notices one low-salience object, then ATTENDs to inspect further. Check increased *physical* resolution, plausible elapsed time, no free secret provenance, no exhaustive-inventory guarantee, and actor visibility only where Nadia can perceive the inspection.

**Run D — lawful falsehood or sincere error:** the controlled fixture includes an actor-accessible false-belief or an actor-supported concealment pressure. Validate that the exact statement becomes a recorded speech act while its proposition remains distinct from world fact; neither narrator nor compositor labels the hidden deception.

**Run E — later consequence:** advance a short independent agenda interval, return once. At least one earlier admission or independent event causally changes the later scene (including changed schedule, object, recollection, or social response). Test the counterfactual by **ablating** the earlier event in a fresh evaluator-only fork. Later differences must trace to different admitted parents, not scripted “I remember” dialogue.

**Same-occurrence double projection:** give **the same admitted objective occurrence** to two differently provisioned user-attention/recognition/access profiles. Require identical objective utterance/actions/history and lawful divergence only in exposure, wording/resolution, and nonbinding characterization. Include misleading user hypothesis to ensure it cannot select confirmation. This is the most incisive experiment for the precise phenomenal seam.

**Independent evaluator and oracle:** compare typed candidate, admitted occurrence, user-visible prose, user acquisition record and future-state trace. Explicitly score factual consistency, historical consistency, reasonable Nadia extrapolability, relationship specificity, user-owned wording, actor-specific nonresponse, hidden-cause and private-state leakage, exact audible speech, forward commitment equivalence, attention-independent actor event, recognition provenance, and valid false-testimony handling. Use varied seeds/prompts and blinded human judgments for distributional drift and experience; a single polished vignette cannot pass.

## 12. Failure thresholds, gaps and verdict

A **hard fail** includes any hidden Nadia-private content in the downstream compositor packet; actor occurrence changing when only user-lens metadata changes; a rendered physical commitment absent from admitted canon; user wording retroactively changing prior fact; an actor proclaiming inaccessible causal truth as self-knowledge; exact audible dialogue silently paraphrased; fabricated user reasoning; or false actor testimony retyped as objective fact. Stop and repair the membrane/admission boundary before declaring valid testing.

A **quality or scalability fail**, requiring measurement rather than a prior prohibition, includes generic-Nadia warmth/drift, excessive fluent self-explanation, implausible gestures, low readability from local templates, large prompt context for person-continuity, high rerender rate, or a fragile typed/prose equivalence relation. Revisit packet projection/model choice/contract or test a second phenomenal model **only when evidence requires**. Technical valid events can still yield an experientially dead product; assess user curiosity and noninterchangeability separately.

Known missing proof:
1. whole-person expressive projection preserves distributional Nadia across heterogeneous actor states;
2. one-shot expressive realization respects both actor epistemic limits and physical/binding contracts;
3. local compositor is adequately natural and perceptually nuanced without hidden context;
4. structured occurrence and prose are coextensive at useful failure rates;
5. spontaneous SAY/DO/ATTEND transduction/generalized continuity/cost/long-horizon cannot be credited from fixed fixture.

**Verdict: COHERENT_WITH_GAPS.** The laboratory target is conceptually well-formed and sufficiently bounded to implement as a discriminating test, but its central renderer and experience claims are **not yet empirically earned**. The minimum valid test is a short, irreversible, two-encounter, same-objective-occurrence/different-user-perception fixture with user-owned contribution, Nadia-specific independent response (including nonresponse), exact admission, a lawful error/lie possibility, and a causal later return. Anything less risks testing persona chat rather than Rawlsing.
