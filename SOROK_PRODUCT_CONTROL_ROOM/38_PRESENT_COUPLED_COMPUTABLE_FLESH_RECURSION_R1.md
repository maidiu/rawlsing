# Sorok — Present-Coupled Life, Computational/Flesh Recursion, and Curiosity Firewall R1

## 0. Grounded inheritance

Accepted V3 already establishes:
- V3 as code-owned actor developmental causal spine;
- hybrid event-sourced causal history + materialized actor state;
- optional bounded LLM candidate proposal;
- code/rule-side admission and delta application;
- render/phenomenology downstream of admitted causal state;
- recursive relationship/household/genealogy where independently generated actors become one another's causal environment;
- rendered prose never mutates causal truth directly.

Sorok's live-interactional requirement should extend this architecture rather than replace it.

---

## 1. Present-coupled life

Main-mode persona life should be anchored to the historical present.

Events materially affecting the persona's actual place, institutions, prices, laws, weather, conflict, infrastructure, culture, work sector, school environment, community, etc. may become part of the causal fabric when relevant and lawfully sourced/admitted.

This does NOT require every news event to enter the simulation.

Need a governed relevance/admission seam:

`CURRENT EXTERNAL EVENT`
→ `PLACE/TIME/DOMAIN RELEVANCE`
→ `ACTOR/WORLD EXPOSURE OPPORTUNITY`
→ `LAWFUL ADMISSION`
→ `GENERATED CONSEQUENCE`.

External occurrence does not equal actor knowledge or impact.

---

## 2. Persona truth has at least four distinct stores

Maintain noncollapse among:

1. **canonical generated history / world truth** — admitted events/facts that actually occurred in the synthetic world;
2. **current computable causal state** — provenance-maintained V3 state resulting from that history;
3. **actor memory / accessibility** — what Nadia remembers, can retrieve, notices, believes she knows;
4. **self-narrative / testimony** — what Nadia says happened or says about herself.

These may diverge lawfully.

`HISTORY != MEMORY != SELF-NARRATIVE != UTTERANCE`.

A state change may persist causally without being actively remembered.
A sincerely held self-explanation may conflict with the deeper causal record.

---

## 3. Computational → flesh → computational loop

Candidate live-world loop:

`CURRENT COMPUTABLE PERSON + RELATION + WORLD STATE`
→ `UNRESOLVED PARTICULAR SLOT / FUTURE EVENT FRONTIER`
→ `LLM PROPOSES BOUNDED CONCRETE PARTICULARS`
→ `CODE/RULE/PROBABILITY ADMISSION FROM CURRENT STATE`
→ `ADMITTED PARTICULAR BECOMES IMMUTABLE WORLD/HISTORY FACT`
→ `PARTICULAR'S CAUSAL EFFECTS TRANSLATED INTO TYPED EVENTS/DELTAS`
→ `V3 / RELATION / WORLD RECOMPUTE`
→ `UPDATED COMPUTABLE STATE`
→ `LLM RENDERS EXPERIENCED WORLD FLESH`
→ later particulars feed back again.

The LLM supplies concrete flesh; it does not own the probability law or causal truth.

---

## 4. Curiosity firewall

Critical law:

> **User curiosity may trigger completion of an unresolved particular; it may not influence the substantive distribution from which that pre-existing particular is generated.**

Example:
The user asks where Nadia went to school.

The fact may not have been concretely materialized yet.

The query may trigger materialization of `school_id / school_history` from Nadia's historical position, family state, geography, class/resources, prior generated constraints and lawful randomness.

The user's:
- interests;
- rapport strategy;
- desired theme;
- expectations;
- conversational framing;
- apparent values;

must not bias which school becomes true.

`QUERY TRIGGERS MATERIALIZATION != QUERY CONDITIONS TRUTH`.

This is an absolute anti-player-centricity wall for latent/background truth.

---

## 5. Critical distinction: latent completion vs prospective causation

The curiosity firewall applies to facts that are logically/historically prior to the user's influence or otherwise independent of it.

It does NOT erase legitimate future causality.

### Latent/background completion
`USER ASKS` may trigger generation but cannot affect substance.

### Prospective world development
`USER ACTS` may legitimately become one causal input among others affecting what happens next.

Example:
- user's question cannot make Nadia's old employer prestigious because the user values prestige;
- user's actual recommendation might later influence whether Nadia applies for a new job if she receives it, considers it and acts.

---

## 6. Particular generation must be profile/history-conditioned

Concrete details should arise from the computable state and exact generated history.

Candidate conditioning inputs:
- structural/world context;
- generated geography and positions;
- material resources;
- relationship/household history;
- actor capacities;
- ongoing projects/strivings;
- current developmental state;
- actual other actors;
- prior events;
- lawful local distributions;
- current external-world conditions;
- explicit random stream.

The LLM may produce candidate concrete particulars inside this envelope.

Admission must be code/rule governed.

Do not let narrative plausibility become probability authority.

---

## 7. Particulars create overflow

An admitted particular is not inert flavor.

Example:
`Nadia takes a new job`.

That concrete event may introduce:
- coworkers;
- commute;
- schedule changes;
- new status/power relations;
- material resources;
- new exposure routes;
- fatigue;
- new possible relationships;
- conflicts;
- skills/practices;
- future opportunities.

Those particulars now constrain later causal development.

Thus:

> **the causes that generated a particular need not exhaust the consequences introduced by that particular.**

This is the core reason flesh must feed back into computable state.

## 8. Relationship alchemy

If Nadia enters a relationship with another generated actor, that other actor must not remain a static tag.

The relationship can become an emergent causal field because:
- the other actor has their own history/state;
- Nadia has her own history/state;
- repeated encounters generate relationship history;
- each changes the other's opportunity, appraisal, expectation, material state and development;
- rupture, love, betrayal, boredom, support, domination, repair, departure or other trajectories may emerge lawfully.

A later V3 recursion must therefore consume **the developed relationship history**, not merely the factors that made the initial relationship likely.

---

## 9. Recursive supporting persons

Close causal actors may require their own generated V3 histories.

Strong candidates:
- parent/co-parent;
- long-term partner;
- child;
- household members;
- very close friends;
- key coworkers where repeated interaction materially changes Nadia.

For a child, the already-accepted genealogy architecture is directly relevant:
generated parents + relationship/household history become actual developmental environment for the child, while the child remains a separately generated person.

Exact supporting-actor fidelity remains a cost/architecture question.

---

## 10. Lying

Nadia must be able to lie.

When she lies:

`UNDERLYING WORLD/HISTORY TRUTH` remains unchanged.

Store separately:
- the utterance;
- proposition/content claimed;
- audience;
- current believed truth if modeled/accessed;
- intentional fabrication status only if actor causal state lawfully supports it;
- reasons/pressures/motives at actor-accessible and analyst levels without collapse;
- relation context;
- later memory of having lied where encoded;
- downstream beliefs/consequences.

Example:
Nadia carries substantial shame around underachievement. User repeatedly presents prestige camaraderie and Nadia wants affiliation/recognition. A lie that inflates an achievement may become lawful if the actual event-time mediation produces it.

The user's relational behavior may causally influence **whether/how she lies**.

It may not rewrite the underlying history to make the lie true.

---

## 11. Self-narrative can be sincerely false

Not every discrepancy is a lie.

Nadia may:
- misremember;
- rationalize;
- simplify;
- sincerely believe an inaccurate causal story;
- have incomplete access to her own motives/history;
- adopt a later narrative that differs from earlier ones.

Therefore:

`FALSE TESTIMONY != INTENTIONAL LIE`.

Rawlsing must preserve historical truth, epistemic state, memory and communicative presentation separately.

---

## 12. Live-thin interaction implication

Live interaction should operate over a **thin active slice** of the richer V3 causal person:
- current relevant state;
- current relation-state refs;
- current life/world context;
- actor-accessible memory/knowledge;
- event-time receivability/appraisal/recruitment;
- minimal active nearby actors;
- exact history refs for retrieval.

The thin slice is not a thinner person.

It is a bounded runtime projection of a deep provenance-maintained person.

After consequential interaction:

`LIVE EVENT`
→ `typed external-event proposal`
→ `V3 admission`
→ `history/state recompute`
→ `future thin slice changes`.

---

## 13. Non-player-centric completion theorem

> **The user may determine what part of reality needs to become concrete enough to answer or act upon. The user may not determine what that reality turns out to be.**

And:

> **Concrete world flesh is not decoration around the computational person. Once admitted, it becomes causal material from which future personhood is made.**
