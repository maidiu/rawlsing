## Strong current product conclusions

1. The product is better framed as an **AI neighbor / relational practice environment** than a character chatbot.
2. Random encounter is spiritually constitutive: the user should not begin by selecting a person for preexisting desirability.
3. Advancement should appear primarily through changed relationship, not an exposed friendship meter.
4. Persona flourishing must remain independent of user satisfaction, attachment, compliance and intensity.
5. Greater intimacy may increase difficulty by activating expectation, need, conflict, dependency risk, developmental residues and social-network consequences.
6. Rawlsing should longitudinally model the user's **relational praxis** without pretending to diagnose the user.
7. Persona life should persist beyond a chat session; asynchronous contact should be causally generated from life events + persona + relationship, not engagement optimization.
8. V3 must be reconsidered as a relational-developmental person generator rather than a monadic personality generator.
9. The actual user↔persona relationship likely needs a distinct dyadic runtime owner downstream of V3; exact ownership is unresolved.
10. No-avatar remains the preferred product direction.
