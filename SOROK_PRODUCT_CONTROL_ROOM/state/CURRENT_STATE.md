## Strong current product conclusions

1. Rawlsing is better framed as an **AI neighbor / relational practice environment** than a character chatbot.
2. Random/unselected encounter is spiritually constitutive: the user should not begin by selecting a person for preexisting desirability.
3. Advancement should appear primarily through changed relationship, not an exposed friendship meter.
4. Persona flourishing must remain independent of user satisfaction, attachment, compliance and intensity.
5. Greater intimacy may increase difficulty by activating expectation, need, conflict, dependency risk, developmental residues and social-network consequences.
6. Rawlsing should longitudinally model the user's **relational praxis** without pretending to diagnose the user.
7. Persona life should persist beyond a chat session; asynchronous contact should be causally generated from life events + persona + relationship rather than engagement optimization.
8. V3 must be reconsidered as relational-developmental rather than monadic, but Sorok must preserve the already-developed temporal-grain split: SDTF/upstream transduction → durable/formative V3 → CAN-008 person×event response → PFF interaction-fast/session/user-opacity → V2 expression.
9. A new dyadic runtime owner is **not presumed**. New relational semantics must first be placed through the existing owner structure; only a genuine remainder may justify a new owner.
10. No-avatar remains the preferred product direction.
11. The core interaction is **situated action inside a causally live scene**, not open-ended chat.
12. The minimum high-level interaction grammar is currently `SAY / DO / ATTEND`.
13. The user controls attempts and presentations, not feasibility, success, detection, reception, interpretation or consequence.
14. The narrator is hard show-not-tell: user-phenomenal surface only, no hidden psychology, motives or relationship metrics.
15. The narrator is **non-curatorial**: it must not expose details merely because hidden system state knows they are psychologically important.
16. User wording does not instantiate world facts. Any narrated world fact must already be lawfully completed/retrieved and canonically admitted; narrated facts persist.
17. Consequence is not feedback. Effects may propagate through actors, networks and world history without the user ever learning them.
18. Intention, realized action, objective impact, attribution, immediate response, relationship effect, flourishing effect and later knowledge must remain separable.
19. One event need not become a trait judgment. Episodic traces can remain weak/provisional and later be reactivated, consolidated, weakened or reinterpreted.
20. **ATTEND consumes variable continuous simulated time.** Observation does not pause the world.
21. Exact action duration should ordinarily remain prospectively uncertain; user temporal knowledge is itself access-limited.
22. The observer is observable. Where/how the user looks, searches, handles and conceals attention may itself become social evidence.
23. Information acquisition has temporal, perceptual, privacy and social-visibility costs.
24. Divided attention may reduce information resolution.
25. There is no 100-percent clue state and no exhaustive True-Person completion condition.
26. Ordinary objects are socially produced evidence, not necessarily author-planted clues; the world may be meaningful before the user learns the meaning.
27. Attention allocation is itself candidate player-praxis evidence distinct from attention capacity.
28. A future `RAWlsing / Rawlsing` mode distinction may vary epistemic/agency scaffolding while preserving identical underlying moral physics and hidden psychology.
29. The world/persona are non-player-centric: actors retain independent agendas and may ignore, delay, interrupt, leave or prioritize other tasks rather than “respond” to every user act.
30. Interaction reasoning uses four operational views—objective world, actor-private state, relational/distributed-knowledge state and user phenomenal surface—without replacing inherited architecture owners.
31. Narrator-directed ATTEND, diegetic SAY, embodied DO and player-private intention must remain distinct channels even when mixed inside one natural-language turn.
32. Player-private intention is distinct from later self-description; exact anti-retroactive-rewrite semantics remain open.
33. Finite narrator detail should follow perceptual salience and explicit attention, never hidden psychological importance.
34. Variable duration must remain physically/socially plausible rather than punitive; cheap micro-observations may accumulate consequential time.
35. Prompt packing/verbosity must not compress or inflate world-time; duration follows realized operations.
36. No arbitrary per-scene observation quota is preferred; scarcity should arise from the world itself.
