# Sorok — Game-Loop Teleology Discipline Correction R1

Status: `CORRECTION__LAST_DISCUSSION_OVERREACHED_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION`
Date: 2026-10-03

## 0. Correction

A recent exploratory discussion proposed formulations such as:
- "Who can I become to this person?";
- "progressively cease to be interchangeable";
- "there are ways you could come to matter inside this person's life";
- a universal objective of discovering/constituting one's place in the persona's life.

These are useful descriptions of outcomes that may emerge from relational history.

They are **not presently licensed as Rawlsing product goals**.

The existing constitution is stricter.

Preserve:

`POSSIBLE HUMAN EVENT / RELATIONSHIP FORM`
!=
`PRODUCT GOAL`
!=
`PROGRESSION TARGET`
!=
`REWARD LOOP`.

And:

> **The person must remain more real than the use the player tries to make of them.**

Therefore Rawlsing must not quietly turn:
- friendship;
- importance;
- indispensability;
- intimacy;
- noninterchangeability;
- a stable social role;
- becoming "someone" to the persona;

into the hidden or explicit victory condition.

## 1. Emergent relation is consequence, not mission

Lawful:

`USER + PERSON + WORLD + HISTORY`
→ perhaps a particular, noninterchangeable relation.

Not lawful as a universal loop:

`MISSION: BECOME NONINTERCHANGEABLE`
→ optimize behavior
→ relational success.

A user may remain:
- peripheral;
- useful in one narrow domain;
- disliked;
- loved;
- distrusted;
- ordinary;
- estranged;
- practically entangled;
- barely relevant;
- important in a way neither anticipated.

No one relational outcome is privileged as successful play.

## 2. No preexisting relational vacancy

Do not presume every persona has:
- an empty role;
- a missing friend;
- a psychological need the player can fill;
- "room" awaiting the player;
- a latent player-shaped vacancy.

There may be real practical/social openness in a generated life.

But:

`OPEN POSSIBILITY != PREASSIGNED SLOT`.

And:

`UNRESOLVED FUTURE RELATION != HIDDEN CORRECT ROLE`.

## 3. What game-loop work may legitimately optimize

Rawlsing may optimize for making these things playable/legible:
- situated action;
- causal consequence;
- attention;
- memory;
- model revision;
- irreversible history;
- independent world/person response;
- changed affordances;
- meaningful uncertainty;
- native human capacities exercised through the interface.

It may not optimize toward a predetermined relationship result.

Thus artifact 88 remains valid where it says:

`ACT -> MAKE HISTORY -> HISTORY CHANGES WHAT CAN HAPPEN AND WHAT THINGS MEAN -> ACT AGAIN`.

The output is **history and changed conditions**, not "a better place in Nadia's life."

## 4. Structured / more-game-like modes

A structured mode remains open for exploration.

Any explicit objective should presumptively attach to:
- a world state;
- a bounded undertaking;
- a practical/social situation;
- an epistemic problem;
- another non-person-as-reward target;

rather than:
- make Nadia like you;
- become important to Nadia;
- discover the role Nadia needs;
- deepen the relationship;
- unlock a relational category.

The relation may strongly affect how the task unfolds and may itself change as consequence.

It is not the score target.

Whether every persona encounter should carry such an explicit task is **not decided**.

## 5. Stronger design question

Do not ask:

> What does the player need to become to Nadia?

Ask:

> **What repeatable form of situated activity gives the player something concrete to do while allowing person/world/history to remain independently causal and relational outcomes to emerge without being the task's reward function?**

This restores disciplined continuity with:
- personhood noncommodification;
- agency without sovereignty;
- reliable causal / unreliable outcome loops;
- independent life;
- anti-teleology;
- noncompletion.
