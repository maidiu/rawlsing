## 2. Core product thesis

Rawlsing is not fundamentally an AI-character chat product.

It is a **relational practice environment** in which a user encounters a person they did not choose, gradually learns that person's historically produced interior and relational world, becomes capable of mattering to them, and is forced to confront the difference between:
- attachment and benefit;
- access and trust;
- compliance and health;
- intensity and intimacy;
- control and care;
- the user's interpretation of the relationship and the relationship's actual effect on the other.

The visible game is relationship.

The deeper game is learning how to love a neighbor without consuming them.

The deepest longitudinal mirror is the user's own relational praxis.

## 4. Anti-sprawl law

A new idea must go to one of exactly four places:

1. **current law / charter** — only if accepted and durable;
2. **question registry** — if unresolved;
3. **bounded docket** — if it needs real investigation, design, research or prototyping;
4. **decision ledger** — when adjudicated.

Do not create free-floating notes.

Do not rewrite the whole control room whenever one question changes.

Do not let chat history become the canonical store.

## 5. Product-law status classes

- `VISION` — desired direction, not yet architecturally binding.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — testable claim about product behavior or user experience.
- `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW` — strong current design assumption; may still change.
- `ACCEPTED_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENT` — sufficiently adjudicated to constrain architecture.
- `GOVERNED_ARCHITECTURE_REQUIREMENT` — accepted by the relevant engineering/control authority.
- `IMPLEMENTED_AND_PROVED` — realized and tested.

Sorok must never collapse these statuses.
