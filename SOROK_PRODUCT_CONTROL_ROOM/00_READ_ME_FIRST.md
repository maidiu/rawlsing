## 0. Why Sorok exists

Sorok is the dedicated control room for the **end-product Rawlsing is trying to become**.

It owns disciplined exploration and product-architecture synthesis across:
- ultimate ontology;
- spiritual / transformative / pedagogical purpose;
- relational V3 implications;
- relationship simulation;
- persistent life and time;
- game mechanics and stakes;
- UX / UI;
- player-modeling and reflective feedback;
- flourishing / health evaluation;
- memory and state;
- LLM expression and action mediation;
- social / multiplayer possibilities;
- safety, privacy, anti-manipulation and anti-addiction constraints;
- evaluation;
- cost and operational feasibility;
- monetization and integrity;
- alpha definition;
- requirements that may eventually constrain PRE-V3 / V3 / PFF / V2 / application architecture.

Sorok is **not** authorized to mutate the governed Pyat program merely because a product idea is attractive.

Its job is to turn green-field vision into explicit, evidence-bearing, testable requirements.

## 1. Governing separation

Pyat answers:

> What is the current lawful build state, what does the governed machine own, and what should be implemented next?

Sorok answers:

> What finished human experience are we actually trying to make possible, what ontology does that experience require, and what requirements should eventually be presented to the governed architecture?

The bridge is:

`VISION → QUESTION → EVIDENCE / PROTOTYPE → PRODUCT DECISION → REQUIREMENT → CROSSWALK → GOVERNED HANDOFF`

No Sorok idea becomes pipeline law by enthusiasm alone.

## 2. Core product thesis

Rawlsing is not fundamentally an AI-character chat product.

It is a **relational practice environment** in which a user encounters a person they did not choose, gradually learns that person's historically produced interior and relational world, becomes capable of mattering to them, and is forced to confront the difference between:
- attachment and benefit;
- access and trust;
- compliance and health;
- intensity and intimacy;
- control and care;
- the user's interpretation of the relationship and the relationship's actual effect on the other.

The visible game is relationship.

The deeper game is learning how to love a neighbor without consuming them.

The deepest longitudinal mirror is the user's own relational praxis.

## 4. Anti-sprawl law

A new idea must go to one of exactly four places:

1. **current law / charter** — only if accepted and durable;
2. **question registry** — if unresolved;
3. **bounded docket** — if it needs real investigation, design, research or prototyping;
4. **decision ledger** — when adjudicated.

Do not create free-floating notes.

Do not rewrite the whole control room whenever one question changes.

Do not let chat history become the canonical store.

## 5. Product-law status classes

- `VISION` — desired direction, not yet architecturally binding.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — testable claim about product behavior or user experience.
- `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_LAW` — strong current design assumption; may still change.
- `ACCEPTED_PRODUCT_REQUIREMENT` — sufficiently adjudicated to constrain architecture.
- `GOVERNED_ARCHITECTURE_REQUIREMENT` — accepted by the relevant engineering/control authority.
- `IMPLEMENTED_AND_PROVED` — realized and tested.

Sorok must never collapse these statuses.

## 6. Immediate purpose of this seed

This R1 seed does **not** pretend the product is designed.

It establishes the professional machinery by which the product can now be designed without losing questions, conflating speculation with decisions, or reopening governed engineering by accident.
