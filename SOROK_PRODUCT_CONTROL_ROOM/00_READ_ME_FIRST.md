## 2. Core product thesis

Rawlsing is not fundamentally an AI-character chat product.

It is a **relational practice environment** in which a user encounters a person they did not choose, gradually learns that person's historically produced interior and relational world, becomes capable of mattering to them, and is forced to confront the difference between:
- attachment and benefit;
- access and trust;
- compliance and health;
- intensity and intimacy;
- control and care;
- the user's interpretation of the relationship and the relationship's actual effect on the other.

The visible game is relationship.

The deeper game is learning how to love a neighbor without consuming them.

The deepest longitudinal mirror is the user's own relational praxis.
