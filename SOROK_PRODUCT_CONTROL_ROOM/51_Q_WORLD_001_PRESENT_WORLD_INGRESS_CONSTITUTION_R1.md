# Sorok — Q-WORLD-001 Present-World Ingress Constitution R1

Status: `PROVISIONAL_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION__Q_WORLD_001_FIRST_ADJUDICATION__NON_DISPOSITIVE_TO_PYAT_PREMAIN`

Date: 2026-09-30

## 0. Question

`Q-WORLD-001`:

> How much real-world current-event ingestion is needed for alpha, and how are relevance, truth, temporality and causal admission governed?

This artifact adjudicates the product requirement. It does not choose retrieval vendors, source APIs, storage schemas, refresh intervals, PRE-V3 owners, or production implementation.

---

## 1. Holding: present-coupled does not mean digital twin

Rawlsing should not attempt to mirror the whole actual world.

The alpha needs a bounded **historical-present constraint layer** sufficient to prevent the focal person's represented life from drifting free of materially relevant public reality.

Therefore:

`PRESENT_COUPLED != COMPLETE_REAL_WORLD_REPLICA`

and:

`CURRENTNESS_OBLIGATION_SCALES_WITH_THE_EXTERNAL_REALITY_RAWLSING_CLAIMS`.

The product should claim only the real-world resolution it can lawfully keep current.

A persona may inhabit a real country/city and historical date while many private/local particulars remain synthetic.

Rawlsing is not obliged to reproduce every actual shop, neighbor, building, headline or street-level event merely because the city is real.

---

## 2. Three world-truth strata

### A. External-anchor reality

Publicly factual reality that Rawlsing explicitly binds into the synthetic world at a declared resolution.

Examples may include, where relevant:
- historical date;
- country/region/city;
- public law/regulation;
- weather/disaster;
- major outage/infrastructure condition;
- transport disruption;
- conflict/security condition;
- public institutional closure/change;
- named public-entity facts;
- other current public facts whose truth materially constrains a represented life domain.

These require external factual authority when asserted or causally used.

### B. Synthetic lived world

Generated private/local particulars that belong to the Rawlsing world without pretending to be exact copies of real private reality.

Examples may include:
- generated households;
- generated private workplaces;
- generated coworkers;
- generated neighbors;
- generated private businesses/institutions where the product does not claim a real named entity;
- generated schedules, objects, local events and private histories.

These particulars may be conditioned by the external-anchor layer and learned-world priors, then admitted through Rawlsing's ordinary world/history authority.

### C. Learned-world prior

Model-learned ordinary-world generalization used as proposal authority for generic texture:
- common occupational pressures;
- ordinary routines;
- plausible institutional role ecology;
- common household practices;
- likely forms of friction/satisfaction/boredom;
- context-appropriate social texture.

This is not current-fact authority.

`LEARNED_WORLD_PRIOR != NAMED_CURRENT_FACT`.

---

## 3. Declared external-anchor envelope

Each continuity/persona needs a bounded external-anchor envelope that states what dimensions of actual public reality the product is promising to track.

Candidate alpha grain:

- current historical calendar;
- focal persona's country/region/city or equivalent locality;
- broad public legal/institutional conditions that directly govern represented life;
- materially significant locality-level weather/disaster/infrastructure/transport conditions;
- explicitly adopted named public institutions/entities when they enter the world;
- demand-driven current facts beyond that envelope when a later action/claim makes them necessary.

The alpha should not maintain a global news feed for every continuity.

It should maintain enough currentness around the focal life to prevent material contradiction at the resolution it claims.

---

## 4. Ontic law: not ingested yet does not mean did not happen

For facts inside the declared external-anchor envelope:

> **External public reality is not created when Rawlsing fetches it.**

Therefore:

`NOT_YET_INGESTED_EXTERNAL_FACT != ABSENT_EXTERNAL_EVENT`.

This extends the existing lazy-world law:

`NOT_MATERIALIZED_YET != ABSENT`.

If a citywide transit strike actually occurred within a continuity's declared real-present envelope, Rawlsing's failure to retrieve it does not make the strike false inside the claimed external substrate.

It means Rawlsing has a currentness/coverage gap.

This is necessary to avoid converting retrieval timing into ontology.

---

## 5. Ingress is not exposure

The public-world chain must preserve at least:

`EXTERNAL OCCURRENCE`
→ `EXTERNAL FACTUAL VERIFICATION / AUTHORITY`
→ `SYNTHETIC-WORLD APPLICABILITY / ADMISSION`
→ `OBJECTIVE LOCAL CONSEQUENCE OR EXPOSURE OPPORTUNITY`
→ `ACTOR-SPECIFIC INFORMATION / SENSORY ACCESS`
→ `CAN-008 RECEIVABILITY / UPTAKE-APPRAISAL / RECRUITMENT`
→ `IMMEDIATE RESPONSE`
→ possible durable effects through exact owners.

Hard noncollapses:

`EXTERNAL EVENT != ACTOR EXPOSURE`

`WORLD ADMISSION != ACTOR KNOWLEDGE`

`ACTOR EXPOSURE != UPTAKE`

`UPTAKE != DURABLE BELIEF`

`OBJECTIVE MATERIAL EFFECT != AWARENESS OF ITS CAUSE`.

A bus may fail to arrive because of an admitted strike before Nadia knows there is a strike.

A national event may be objectively true while never entering her attention at all.

---

## 6. World relevance is dependency intersection, not newsworthiness

Do not define relevance as:
- what is trending;
- what the user cares about;
- what would create drama;
- what resembles the user's values;
- what would teach a lesson;
- what the persona would find psychologically resonant.

Candidate product law:

> **An external fact is world-relevant when its effective scope intersects a represented dependency of the synthetic world at the resolution currently being simulated.**

Relevant dependency classes may include:
- geography;
- institution;
- legal regime;
- infrastructure;
- transport route;
- resource/price environment;
- public schedule;
- active project;
- household obligation;
- current location;
- named public entity.

This is objective routing.

Actor salience and psychological meaning occur only downstream.

---

## 7. User curiosity may trigger checking, never truth

The curiosity firewall applies equally to external present facts.

A user may ask:
> Is the transit system on strike today?

That question may trigger retrieval/admission work.

It may not increase the probability that a strike is true.

Likewise a user asking about a named current institution may create a factual-resolution demand.

`QUERY TRIGGERS EXTERNAL RESOLUTION != QUERY CONDITIONS EXTERNAL TRUTH`.

The user's future action can legitimately create new future causal relevance.

The user's interest cannot rewrite already-existing public reality.

---

## 8. Currentness is causal, not topical

Rawlsing does not need to make Nadia talk about the news.

The point of present coupling is that public reality can constrain life.

Current facts may enter through:
- changed transport;
- prices/resources;
- work/school rules;
- closures;
- weather;
- safety conditions;
- schedules;
- family/community effects;
- media/conversation exposure;
- institutional demands.

A current event may have:
- material effect without actor knowledge;
- actor knowledge without immediate material effect;
- both;
- neither.

No news-conversation compulsion follows from ingestion.

---

## 9. Alpha ingress budget

Alpha should use a **bounded focal-life currentness budget**, not continuous general-news ingestion.

### Proactive / cadence-bounded checking is justified when

an event class is both:
1. inside the declared external-anchor envelope; and
2. capable of materially changing an active represented dependency during the interval being advanced.

Strong alpha candidates:
- severe/local weather or disaster;
- major infrastructure/outage conditions;
- materially relevant transport disruption;
- law/regulation/public-institution changes directly governing an active life domain;
- major local conflict/security disruption;
- explicit named-public-entity state required by a represented scene or project.

### Demand-driven retrieval is justified when

a scene, actor action, user inquiry, message, or world transition requires a current/named factual claim not already admitted.

### Fresh retrieval is normally unnecessary when

the system only needs generic ordinary-world texture that can lawfully come from a context-bound learned-world prior.

Thus:

`CURRENTNESS SERVICE != FIREHOSE`.

---

## 10. Namedness / resolution law

The more specifically Rawlsing claims the real world, the stronger its factual burden becomes.

Examples:

`REAL COUNTRY / CITY`
→ country/city-scale public currentness burden.

`NAMED REAL HOSPITAL`
→ current facts about that hospital become evidence-bound where material.

`EXACT REAL ADDRESS / ROUTE`
→ much finer currentness and geospatial burden.

Therefore alpha should prefer synthetic private particulars unless a real named entity materially improves the experience.

Do not casually name real institutions/addresses and then treat the model prior as sufficient authority for their current state.

---

## 11. Synthetic private layer may be constrained by real public reality

A generated private clinic can exist in a real city.

Its staff, private history and internal schedule may be synthetic.

But it remains constrained by admitted public conditions such as:
- applicable law;
- citywide outage;
- severe weather;
- public transport disruption;
- conflict/security restrictions;
- relevant public-health/institutional rules where governed.

This is the intended hybrid:

`REAL HISTORICAL-PRESENT CONSTRAINTS`
+
`SYNTHETIC PRIVATE LIFE`
→ `PRESENT-COUPLED GENERATED WORLD`.

---

## 12. Late discovery / causal-closure law

Current facts have effective time.

Rawlsing must distinguish:
- when the external fact was true;
- when Rawlsing discovered it;
- when it was admitted into the synthetic world.

If a fact is retrieved before the affected synthetic interval is causally closed, it may participate normally in that interval.

If Rawlsing discovers an earlier external fact only after downstream synthetic history has already been materially resolved:

### Compatible late discovery
If the fact does not contradict or materially alter admitted history, it may be backfilled with its true effective time and provenance.

### Material collision
If the fact would invalidate or materially rewrite already-admitted user-visible/persona history, Rawlsing must not silently retcon the past.

Record a typed currentness/integrity defect.

Possible downstream architecture may:
- fail closed before contradictory claims;
- preserve the already-occurred synthetic continuity while marking divergence;
- repair/replay only where no admitted occurrence would be rewritten.

Exact repair mechanics are implementation work.

Product law:

`LATE_EXTERNAL_DISCOVERY != RETROACTIVE_NARRATIVE_REWRITE`.

---

## 13. Causal-closure frontier

To reduce late-currentness collisions, the system should not advance an actively represented focal-life interval across a dependency whose current external state is required but stale/unknown.

Conceptually:

`ACTIVE REPRESENTED DEPENDENCY`
+ `CURRENTNESS-SENSITIVE INTERVAL`
→ `REQUIRED EXTERNAL CHECK OR VALID CURRENT CACHE`
→ world advancement.

This is a currentness gate, not a requirement to research every possible event.

Exact refresh cadence/cache/failure behavior remains architecture work.

---

## 14. Persona knowledge remains channel-bound

Ingesting a public fact does not put it into Nadia's mind.

She may learn through:
- direct physical effect;
- news/media she actually encounters;
- messages;
- coworkers/family/community;
- public signs/announcements;
- institutional communication;
- later inference.

She may:
- know early;
- know late;
- misunderstand;
- know only part;
- never learn.

Actor knowledge must retain provenance.

No global-news omniscience.

---

## 15. Non-teleological admission wall

External ingress may not be selected because:
- user engagement is low;
- the relationship needs drama;
- a moral dilemma would be useful;
- the product wants a reason for the user to return;
- the user has a known ideological interest;
- a persona's hidden wound makes an event narratively convenient.

External discovery/admission is governed by factual truth + declared anchor + objective dependency relevance.

Synthetic endogenous events remain governed by their own actor/institution/material causality.

---

## 16. Worked example — transit strike

Assume Nadia's continuity is anchored to a real city and current date, and her commute depends on public transit.

1. A real transit strike occurs.
2. External evidence establishes its scope/effective time.
3. The event falls inside the declared city/currentness envelope.
4. Nadia's represented commute is a dependency intersecting that scope.
5. The local world admits transit consequences.
6. Her bus/service may be unavailable whether or not she knows why.
7. Whether she knew in advance depends on actual information exposure.
8. Her event-time response follows through CAN-008 only from what she receives plus lawful person state.
9. The user learns only through their own lawful contact/perception.

Nothing in this chain exists because the product wants a dramatic scene.

---

## 17. Worked example — generic clinic texture

Nadia works reception at a generated private clinic.

The system needs to realize:
- interruptions;
- scheduling pressure;
- coworker role ecology;
- repetitive administrative work;
- emotional labor.

No named/current public fact is being asserted.

Use the learned-world prior, conditioned by:
- locality;
- historical period;
- institution type;
- generated resources/role;
- admitted current public constraints.

Fresh web retrieval is not required merely to make the scene concrete.

## 18. Alpha closure criterion for Q-WORLD-001

At product-spec grain, Q-WORLD-001 can close when Sorok accepts all of the following:

1. present-coupled means bounded historical-present constraint layer, not digital twin;
2. alpha has a declared external-anchor envelope around the focal life;
3. current/named external fact and learned-world prior remain separate authorities;
4. synthetic private/local particulars may coexist with real public constraints;
5. world relevance is objective dependency intersection, not user interest/newsworthiness;
6. occurrence/admission/exposure/knowledge/response remain noncollapsed;
7. curiosity may trigger checking but never determine truth;
8. late factual discovery may not silently rewrite causally closed history;
9. actively represented currentness-sensitive dependencies require freshness before advancement at their claimed resolution;
10. no engagement/pedagogical relevance may drive external-event admission.

Exact schemas, source resolvers, refresh cadence, caches, failure handling, and owner placement belong to ALPHA_IMPLEMENTATION.

## 19. Product theorem

> **Rawlsing should live in the historical present without pretending to contain the whole historical present.**

And:

> **The real world constrains the generated life where Rawlsing has claimed the real world; retrieval discovers that constraint, it does not create it.**

And:

> **Currentness belongs first to the world. Whether it reaches the person is a later causal question.**
