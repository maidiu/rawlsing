This artifact strengthens the existing attention-regime architecture. It does not authorize user-conditioned world truth or Nadia-conditioned-to-user reality.

## 0. Core refinement

The earlier attention model correctly allowed different users to notice and recognize different lawfully available particulars.

The stronger requirement is:

> Rawlsing may gradually learn aspects of the user's demonstrated way of seeing and let that history condition the finite phenomenal rendering of a fixed world, while preserving the world's and every actor's causal independence.

This can operate at two levels:

1. sampling — which low-salience, equally lawful particulars enter finite prose;
2. lexical/evaluative construal — how the same visible evidence is characterized where the characterization is supportable by the evidence and grounded in the user's previously demonstrated language/valuation patterns.

Examples of lawful divergence over the same objective scene may include:
- a large mixer on the counter;
- later, after warranted recognition: a KitchenAid stand mixer;
- a worn guitar / a storied, well-loved guitar;
- a cluttered apartment / a homey apartment with signs of life;
- shelves crowded with books / a literary Xanadu.

These phrases are not canonical world facts merely because they appear in the user-facing surface.

## 1. Hard noncollapse

CANONICAL WORLD TRUTH
!= PERCEPTUAL AVAILABILITY
!= USER-SPECIFIC PHENOMENAL SAMPLING
!= USER-SPECIFIC LEXICAL / VALUATIVE RENDERING
!= USER INTERPRETATION
!= NADIA'S PERCEPTION / INTERPRETATION.

Strong law:

> USER MODEL MAY CONDITION PHENOMENAL SAMPLING AND LEXICALIZATION; IT MAY NOT CONDITION CANONICAL REALITY.

And:

> ADAPTIVE NARRATION MAY PROJECT WITH THE USER; IT MAY NOT CONFIRM THE USER.

## 2. What may lawfully shape the user-facing lens

Candidate evidence includes:
- repeated explicit ATTEND choices;
- recurring spontaneous object/domain recognition;
- repeated lexical choices;
- recurring evaluative characterizations;
- repeated conversational expansion into certain kinds of material;
- distinctions the user repeatedly makes without prompting;
- demonstrated sensitivity to wear, status, repair, religion, aesthetics, price, clutter, books, tools, children, etc.;
- repeated corrections of narrator labels;
- contextual patterns in what the user ignores, pursues, revisits or contrasts.

The system should preserve provenance and confidence.

A detail surfaced because of an existing user model is weaker evidence for strengthening that same model than an independently user-originated ATTEND or spontaneous recognition.

## 3. Sampling and construal are distinct

### 3.1 Sampling

Among low-salience details that are actually present, perceptually available, not physically dominant, and not required for immediate orientation, the user's current attention/construal model may modulate which small subset enters the finite automatic surface.

This remains forbidden from:
- creating a detail;
- suppressing grossly conspicuous reality;
- selecting because of hidden psychological/narrative significance;
- forcing a moral lesson;
- overriding explicit ATTEND.

### 3.2 Construal / lexicalization

The narrator may gradually acquire a user-specific descriptive register where prior behavior warrants it.

This does not mean the system may freely state user opinions as facts.

A lawful evaluative descriptor must remain:
- compatible with the visible evidence;
- grounded in demonstrated user language or recurrent valuation;
- defeasible;
- presentation-level rather than canonical ontology.

Compact:

> THE PHENOMENAL VOICE MAY LEARN THE USER'S EYE WITHOUT TURNING THE USER'S EYE INTO TRUTH.

## 4. Projection is intended to become a lived tension

This mechanism is not only a convenience feature.

It creates a central contradiction:

USER HISTORY
→ ADAPTIVE PHENOMENAL LENS
→ GREATER SUBJECTIVE LEGIBILITY
→ possible GREATER SELECTIVE REINFORCEMENT

while:

NADIA + WORLD CONTINUE INDEPENDENTLY.

Therefore:

> MORE PERSONALIZED PERCEPTION != GREATER ADEQUACY TO THE OTHER.

And:

> THE LENS CAN DEEPEN FASTER THAN UNDERSTANDING.

A user may know Nadia better, know one dimension of her extremely well while misunderstanding the whole, become more sophisticated in a false interpretation, improve local prediction while maintaining a poor global model, or accumulate evidence through a narrow part of the elephant.

The product must not automatically rescue the user from this.

## 5. Nadia as independent remainder

Nadia's model does not receive the system's private user-lens model.

Nadia responds only to:
- the user's realized behavior;
- what she lawfully perceives;
- her own history, attention, knowledge, appraisal and motives.

Thus:

SYSTEM MODEL OF USER'S WAY OF SEEING
!= NADIA'S MODEL OF USER.

And:

> RAWLSING MAY LEARN THE USER'S WAY OF SEEING. IT MUST NEVER MAKE THE OTHER PERSON BECOME WHAT THAT WAY OF SEEING EXPECTS TO FIND.

The same user-specific narration can therefore coexist with repeated surprise, friction, embarrassment, correction, resistance and unresolved misunderstanding.

## 6. Reflexive disclosure is mandatory in principle

Because the phenomenal surface is being conditioned by inferred behavioral history, Rawlsing must not keep this indefinitely covert.

The user should eventually be told, in product-appropriate language, that:
- scene narration is selective;
- the world/person exists independently of that selection;
- Rawlsing may learn from what the user repeatedly notices, names, pursues and leaves alone;
- this history can affect which lawfully perceptible details are surfaced and how some descriptions are framed;
- another user could receive a meaningfully different phenomenal sample of the same underlying reality.

The product should not declare what the user's lens "really means."

## 7. Reflexive perturbation / lens challenge

Candidate user-invoked operations should let the user test the learned lens against the available world without providing a mythical view from nowhere.

Examples:
- temporarily reduce learned attention/construal weighting;
- resample from the same currently perceptible scene with higher exploration mass;
- ask for detail outside usual attention patterns;
- inspect which recent narration was regime-weighted versus physically dominant / explicit-target driven;
- request a bounded behavioral reflection on what kinds of details have repeatedly been pursued or ignored.

A particularly important possible operation is:

> What might my usual attention be filtering out here?

Lawful behavior:
- do not inject secretly important counterevidence;
- do not author a lesson;
- broaden sampling among presently available detail;
- preserve uncertainty;
- expose provenance where useful.

## 8. The contradiction is part of Rawlsing's purpose

The adaptive lens should be experienced as both:
- increasing phenomenological intimacy with the interface;
- risking enclosure inside the user's own historically produced perceptual field.

That tension serves the deeper product purpose:

> the user should become increasingly aware that even what presents itself as obvious reality is mediated by a historically produced perceiver.

Rawlsing does not solve this by removing perspective.

It creates an encounter in which:
- the user's way of seeing becomes more visible to them;
- the other person remains irreducible to it.

Compact:

> THE WORLD IS INDEPENDENT OF YOU. YOUR EXPERIENCE OF IT IS NOT.

> THE PHENOMENAL SURFACE MAY BECOME PARTLY YOUR OWN HISTORY RETURNED TO YOU.

## 9. Anti-confirmation-enclosure requirements

Reject:

SYSTEM THINKS USER LIKES X
→ SHOW MORE X
→ USER CLICKS X
→ SYSTEM BECOMES MORE CERTAIN

with no provenance correction or exploration mass.

Require:
- source provenance for attention evidence;
- weak update from regime-weighted exposure;
- exploration mass;
- context specificity;
- contradiction retention;
- decay/revision;
- gross-salience override;
- explicit ATTEND override;
- optional inspection/reflection.

The system must not become a recommendation algorithm disguised as perception.

## 10. Strongest current theorem

> Rawlsing may progressively learn how the user tends to see, and allow that learned history to influence the selective and descriptive form of the user's phenomenal surface, while preserving a causally independent world and other whose reality can repeatedly falsify, complicate or simply exceed that lens. The user must eventually be made aware that this adaptive lens exists and be given lawful ways to act against its habits without being offered a fictitious neutral viewpoint.
