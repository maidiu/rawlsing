# Where population evidence can enter the generation of a life

**Implementation and scientific development, 18 November 2026.** There are two different inverse problems here. Given a qualified population of observable answers, which distributions of whole generated lives can produce it? Given one such life, through what actual relations did the actor acquire the capacity, expectation or commitment that produced a particular act? The first has a computable solution on a specified finite generator. The second requires an executable forward account of people acting on one another. Solving the first makes the missing inputs to the second discoverable. Trying to infer the second directly from the survey conceals them.

## The forward weld the inverse calculation presupposes

An enforceable social relation and a socially available interpretation of that relation have to meet in the *same operation*. Suppose the allocation of a woman's time is disputed. Give the world owner the paid hours, care still to be performed, who controls wages, who can provide cover and who can impose a loss. Give the historically situated participants the publicly available ways of characterizing the claim: an obligation of a wife, a worker's right, a debt between kin. Each characterization has carriers, audiences and conditions of intelligibility. Only a competent participant can invoke it. A listener with authority can take it up, refuse it or enforce it for reasons of their own. If an invocation succeeds, the rota, pay or labor allocation changes. That material result feeds the next situation. A customary entitlement can persist across a change of personnel if successors actually acquire and exercise it; a written rule can survive even when nobody can enforce it. Those require different updates.

What develops in a person is a historically acquired way of *distinguishing situations and finding certain responses possible, necessary, natural, shameful or worth fighting for*. Bind a disposition to the object, relationship and practical stake that recruits it. A woman can acquire authority through running a shop and acquire responsibility for absorbing a household's care shortfalls. The same person can come to recognize women's authority in public and marital duty in a dispute over employment. She can endorse both, contest either, act against what she endorses, or experience their conjunction as a conflict when a particular situation brings both claims to bear. The generator must let both organizations make competing bids on a single act. A tie or collision has practical consequences, including delay, compromise, concealment, refusal, recruitment of allies or reorganization of a commitment. A survey analyst's coding of two items cannot choose which of these happened.

Administration of an instrument is itself a social encounter. Preserve which organization asked what, which topics and answer categories it made available, the wording, audience, historical meanings and applicable universe. Run the same generated actor's practical and reflective organization through all coadministered items. The resulting joint answers are an outward act of that particular person in that situation. Unasked questions generate no observations. Requiring historically positioned populations of generated people to be able to make those same configurations constrains the historical generator. It does not turn the questionnaire into the architecture of a mind. The quantitative implementation below solves one part of that inverse constraint. It does not yet implement this forward production of social meanings and dispositions.

## Implemented. Compile a population constraint into construction probabilities

Let G be a forward generator over **complete shared-world histories**, with explicit proposal law q. Its permissible edges are defined by world/actor owners. At a specified historical time a separately graded instrument reads a public, situated outcome y. For the single-position case let b(y) be the forward mass reaching each observed cell, and p(y) the source target. At a completed candidate set h=p(y)/b(y). Propagate h backward through each uncommitted prefix s by summing q(s′|s)h(s′). At construction time draw the next continuation with probability

    q*(s′|s) = q(s′|s) h(s′) / h(s).

The ratios telescope. The new generator emits cell y with mass p(y). Within any one cell it preserves the base generator's relative odds among histories. This is a finite change of measure. Its distinctive use here is that it supplies an **actual construction-time call** between empirical compilation and historical generation while respecting the actor owner's forward decisions. For a particular preselected maternal response replace the target by a point mass on that cell. For population generation retain all positive cells, including minority configurations. Unsupported source use, an unmodeled response and a positive source cell unreachable in the supplied history library cannot be repaired by normalization.

The root law is fictional. The backward calculation neither estimates hidden childhood probabilities nor gives an actor information about their future. Calibration rehearsals use isolated roots. The selected initial life is generated afresh and can respond to a different user. After commitment, freeze the past. An intervention on a live actor changes only prospectively reachable events. Reapplying the backwards weights after observing the user's preferred reply would select the person for compliance.

**Actual call path.** raw_joint calls replay_empirical_query with repository, exact query, predicate, current registry, release and expected fact reference. It reads the RAW ordered complete-cell projection, verifies partition mass and rejects unsupported inputs. ConditionedHistories computes the continuation masses with rational arithmetic in two traversals. The selected history enters run_p4.new → build_case → P3Owner.realize. The ordinary say operation uses admit_speech → read_response → projections.activate → express. The available inspection requires the outward expression and the user's actual perception. A cold view and inspect-edge use the persisted state. No source frequency, desired reply or calibration transcript enters read_response. **Whole populations and hard historical constraints.** For each candidate world j count eligible units dᵢⱼ and joint positive units nᵢⱼ for each qualified target i. Solve for *one weight per whole world* s.t. Σwⱼ(nᵢⱼ−pᵢdᵢⱼ)=0, Σwⱼ=1, wⱼ≥0 and each target has positive denominator. Separately known historical margins supply additional rows. No counterparty can be independently resampled out of a performed event. For one positive eligible person in world A and zero among three eligible in B, a target proportion of one half requires world weights 3/4 and 1/4. Averaging household-level response rates equally would silently give the wrong person-level population. The bounded implementation computes exact attainable extrema and returns witnessing distributions. If one independently requires half of histories to be imposed, the first actual target remains feasible and the second is impossible on the supplied support. The engine reports the incompatibility. It does not quietly move the historical margin to improve the survey fit.

For real surveys, replace exact RAW matching with the legitimate observed-sample estimand or design-qualified interval inequalities, retain positive risk and keep unlinked populations separate. An exact sample zero imposes zero mass only in an exact sample-reproduction mode. A generated fictional future may differ. A stochastic instrument can be expanded into outcome leaves of the uncommitted rehearsal distribution; conditioning on a lucky reply must never manufacture a permanently corresponding actor disposition. The research implementation currently accepts one single-release RAW law for prefix sampling; the separate finite constraint solver takes integer count columns and exact rational constraints. It does not implement weighted survey uncertainty, a household sampler coupled to that general solver, or a free-language response model.

### A necessary distinction between generating a compatible past and rigging a future

There is a trap in applying the backward sampler to a stochastic actor. Suppose a historically possible mother with initial state x invites joint inspection with probability 1/2 under a fixed, qualified forward law. A second state never invites. Every mixture of *freely acting* mothers then has an invitation rate between zero and one half. A path conditioner given a target of three quarters could still produce that rate by oversampling lucky future choices from the first mother. It would have silently changed the conditional action law. This matters even for a small fictional model. With an open future, server-side selection of random seeds for the desired forthcoming action is a different product from meeting an independent Other.

For open interactive time use the second implemented inversion, free_action_range. First compute the complete instrument emission column K[y,x] for each **initial** shared world x, with its subsequent actor/world policies fixed. Solve K·w=p over w≥0, Σw=1. Only w may change. Equivalently p must lie in the convex hull of the independently forward-generated columns. A feasible witness is a law over initial conditions. Sampling a root from this law and then releasing it to the unchanged owners preserves their ability to act otherwise. The example above requires exactly half of initial worlds in each state for target 1/4 and **refuses** target 3/4. These are rational-kernel regression results, separate from the deterministic P4 run. With several eligible people per world, use expected eligible and positive *counts* per world instead of unit columns. Protect a known conditional historical rule in the same way, with a linear constraint on the mass reaching its antecedent and the mass taking its consequence. No empirical fit licenses changing an independently qualified conditional law. Unknown instrument likelihoods stay alternatives, not decimals estimated from a single fluent reply.

This yields a precise design decision. **Use backward conditioning for already-uncommitted historical candidates, and invert over initial-world mixtures for claims about how free-running people will act.** In the joined P4 run the same instrument on a fresh selected root reproduces the operative cell under deterministic fixture rules. In the general case fitting possible complete histories and fitting a deployable generator are distinct feasibility problems. ### Proposed replacement. Compute whose contribution had practical force

Represent a joint task at time t by its feasible allocations X(t). Each allocation binds actual participants, scarce resource intervals, an intended beneficiary and a completion criterion. Each participant can propose an alteration, consent, exercise a right, substitute a means, withdraw or do nothing, limited by their own current capacities and access. A shop manager can change a rota. A household member can volunteer time. Neither can perform the other's labor. Each operation has three distinct results: the actor's performed bid, its effect on the feasible allocations, and the committed implementation after the necessary counterpart acts. Persist the right's issuer, competent party and interval separately from the bid. The accounting invariant is that the same unit of labor, money or time is never spent twice.

To measure **practical authorship**, take the world immediately before a bid. Resolve the admitted joint action, then evaluate the same situation under withdrawal or a feasible alternative bid, holding fixed the other actors' pre-bid states, rights and external randomness. Keep any *later* counterparty responses endogenous. Record the change in implementable allocations and in the realized beneficiary, burden and means. This is a role-, object- and situation-specific effect, not a personality score. It distinguishes at least four cases collapsed by the word recognition:

| Actual operation | Counterfactual or evidence | What can enter the participant's history |
|---|---|---|
| The superior praises the worker's proposal. The rota was already fixed. | Replacing the proposal leaves the rota and task path unchanged. | Public acknowledgment can have an effect on dignity, standing or future speech. No evidence of influence on that rota. |
| A low-status worker's amendment reroutes actual relief; someone else independently covers the time. | An otherwise admissible alternative amendment yields a different allocation and the required counterpart can still refuse. | The world records influence. The worker can learn about it only from what they could access. |
| An authority mandates the same arrangement the worker wanted. | Withdrawal still produces the arrangement. Refusal carries a separately established cost. | Preference alignment, coercive capacity and perceived safety can coexist. Agreement alone distinguishes none of them. |
| The worker does not speak because earlier objections carried costs. | An actually available counterfactual bid might have worked today. Its absence has several possible causes. | No fictitious refusal or perceived punishment today. Earlier accessible episodes may recruit anticipation. |

Counterfactuals are available to the research evaluator and to the fictional-world generator where its laws warrant them. They are not observations available to the character. A child's own action can be followed by an adult's response without the child knowing what would otherwise have happened. This yields **two distinct learnable relations**. One links my attempt to an accessible consequence under a particular counterpart and setting. The other links the consequence to a good or shared project I have come to pursue. An actor can become very competent at obtaining an outcome they privately oppose. A learning model that reinforces a single utility on every successful action would encode that opposition as preference.

**First candidate acquisition rule.** At an actor-local project and counterpart, retain a sparse table over accessible cues, performed operation, and accessible consequence, with opportunity and coverage counts. Include comparable opportunities in which the bid was withheld or the counterpart was unavailable. Only encode a contrast over states the actor had the capacity to discriminate. Split two contexts into different practical expectations when a prequential predictor using that perceptible difference beats the pooled predictor on later episodes after paying the cost of the extra distinction. With insufficient discriminating history retain a local trace or uncertainty. Support can justify anticipating an effect. It cannot by itself justify endorsing the end, understanding the authority, or generalizing to a new counterpart. This is a candidate bounded learning rule, not an assertion that children compute model description lengths. Contingency versus mere exposure is an empirically consequential distinction in at least one narrow developmental domain. [Goldstein, King & West, infant vocal development](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC164707/)

**How ends can develop.** Let an actor carry unfinished activities whose continuation has mattered in its own prior conduct, including play and care. Represent the *object* of an activity by what would count as its continuation or success under materially different means. A child initially returns a dropped implement while playing with a caregiver. Later the child might keep a game going, seek acknowledgment, practice a skill alone, or enable the caregiver to finish something. They coincide in the original episode. They come apart when the game ends, the caregiver cannot see, an equivalent implement is available, or helping now costs time in another activity. Keep these as competing models of recruitment until independent later conduct or a declared fictional choice separates them. A new end has a behavioral meaning when it reorganizes which previously available projects are interrupted, resumed or repaired. This gives motives a functional consequence beyond adding a label to PFF. It also permits baseline social engagement or spontaneous helping before repeated reinforcement. A controlled study of 20-month-olds found materially rewarded helping had lower subsequent incidence than praise or no reward in that task. A universal positive-reward update would predict the wrong direction there. [Warneken & Tomasello, 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18999339/)

**The ideological and theological consequence has an implementation.** Distinguish the practice's carrier, the good it historically mediated, the subject for whom that good matters, and the actions that sustain the carrier. One institution may have made a family both materially dependent and capable of caring for a neighbor. If it later withholds relief, at least four different projects formerly realized by the same public action can now diverge: protect the institution, protect the neighbor, avoid exclusion, or sustain an ideal of fidelity learned through the institution. Give the actor access only to actual signs of the breach, a language for recognizing it if learned, a feasible alternative if one exists, and others who may participate or refuse. Now an actor can preserve an inherited good by opposing its carrier, or retain the carrier while sacrificing that good. Someone else can need the institution to survive and comply despite understanding the breach. That distinction requires changing the beneficiary, means, audience or sanction independently while tracking what the actor actually does. No scalar for progress, faith, authentic love or solidarity can implement it. Absolute Horizon becomes a hypothesis about which acquired commitments reorganize other ends when they conflict at a limit. It can remain absent, inaccessible or empirically indistinguishable from ordinary loyalty. The user's changing recognition of a generated neighbor is a separate human outcome.

### Which social others actually reach the person

In a historical period distinguish the number of people holding a response configuration z in position D2 from the number of **eligible participations** those people have in the focal actor's practices. If a random occasion rather than a random person is the unit, its distribution is proportional to the number of eligible participants in each configuration multiplied by their mean number of eligible occasions. Conditioning on D1, calendar, ties, travel and rights is indispensable. A numerically small group controlling many allocations can dominate a child's experience while a common orientation remains largely absent. Holding population responses fixed and altering who controls schedules produces a different world. Holding public power fixed and altering private orientations need not change any enacted arrangement.

Use incumbent material and relationship readers to generate *occasion slots* with role, time, capacity and rights. A world generator proposes concrete holders and feasible counterparts under separately evidenced or explicitly fictional mixing. Before committing, attach each whole candidate world's respondent-level instrument counts and occasion-level counts to different rows of the constraint matrix. Compare alternative worlds by their reachable histories. Where no linkage evidence exists, both concentration and dispersion may remain feasible. A model that fills every slot by drawing an independent respondent from D2 assumes equal exposure and discards power by construction. An actor can still be affected indirectly through a caregiver's lost hours without meeting the originator. A revision of D2 alone cannot alter an already committed child.

### Construction order and compute

Generate maternal prehistory with the forward event grammar, then use observation-conditioned construction only across uncommitted alternatives. A prospective father's earlier public work, effects on third parties and refusal options must exist at event time. Keep two bindings: the independently acting event-time participant and any later identity resolution. A future binding that changes a committed allocation forces a fork before that allocation. After birth, keep accruing parental activity and costs concurrently. A separately developing infant enters through bodily regulation, signaling, dependency and perceptual capacity. A political or religious answer has no infant-state initializer. In adult interactive time keep the same actor and project/state stores. A user can change this person's opportunities and relations, including through absence and misunderstanding, and can fail to change them.

The prefix algorithm takes O(V+E) rational operations and O(V+E) storage for a finite acyclic library, excluding integer bit growth and the cost of running the owner for a continuation. The exact multi-constraint reader is for small support libraries. It enumerates candidate bases up to a caller budget and returns a distinct computation limit. Whole-life enumeration is infeasible. For longer historical periods merge prefixes only when the relevant world rights, stocks, unfinished projects, reproductive labor, actor-accessible histories and future observation/decision distributions agree for the intended query. A permissible optimization for a shop-cost question may be destructive for a later authority or language question. At large scale estimate continuation masses by branching *before* collisions over scarce resources or discriminating interactions, carrying importance weights and effective support per minority cell. Retain exact rare branches when approximation would erase them. A single rare success from a vanishing proposal population cannot justify stable prevalence. This extension has not been benchmarked or implemented here.

**The next production incision is specific.** Extend the existing joint task/event owner to commit who offered which change, who could refuse, which alternative actually occurred, and who bore the material cost. Bind that event to each participant's signed access and prior project at the previous clock. Supply those facts where history_rows presently supplies the verdict; have a versioned formation rule derive or withhold an actor-relative record. Reuse P3 activation and P4's normal response unmodified. The engineering discriminator is an outwardly identical allocation achieved once through effective amendment and once through compulsory compliance: public material output can match, objective practical influence differs, and private acquisition may still match if the actor could not distinguish the histories. The scientific discriminator is whether the inferred distinction predicts later uncoached conduct across changed carriers and costs better than a counterpart-specific memory or no acquisition. This is the work that remains between a functioning distribution-conditioned generator and historically formed persons.
