# When can a neighbor actually cover a shift?

**Decision.** Give the world owner the opportunity to record a *specific offer of relief, delivered in time to be used*. A mother who stays at work after her child needs care could be enforcing an obligation to finish her shift, or she could have received no usable offer. The same observed work, care and public answers fit both accounts. Even measuring exactly how many potential helpers qualify cannot resolve that ambiguity. The first measurement to fund is when she could have handed off responsibility, who knew this, and what she did next.

Consider a finite fictional roster. Three conditions on the same helper matter at the same time: free during the interval, entitled to cover the task, able to reach the workplace before handoff. Let I03 return the number K satisfying all three among n people. Let m be the number whom this mother can actually reach through a separately established network. With no linkage information the number in both sets is between max(0,K+m−n) and min(K,m). These bounds are sharp by placing the K people outside or inside the m slots first. They bound candidates. Each can still refuse, and an accepted offer can arrive too late. Treating the population fraction K/n as this mother's chance of relief assumes a mixing law. Across unrelated survey and workplace populations even the count bounds require a population bridge.

**Architectural consequence.** In a set of admissible worlds, PYAT can establish impossibility, or widen what is feasible, without changing any particular person's disposition. Preserve that asymmetry. Select a fictional roster under an explicit scenario policy before the shift. An independently acting coworker controls the offer. The employer controls release and its wage or disciplinary cost. The mother controls whether she attempts the handoff. Record delivery, acknowledgment and the actual transfer of the task in event order. A rule someone professes about a “good worker” can be materially reproduced by routinely covering for management or by organizing collective relief. The same words allow either practice. A child may experience the time and manner of the caregiver's return without hearing the workplace exchange.

**First research priority N.** Rival one uses bounded memory of comparable shifts, previous refusals and actual consequences to choose whether to ask for relief. Rival two learns a relation between the locally acquired role, the person entitled to release it and what makes an obligation transferable. Acquire that relation provisionally only after the actor has encountered a contrast between accepted and refused transfers with accessible acknowledgment. Retain uncertainty when the contrast is unavailable. Both use the same event history, bodily/time constraints and public institution. A third rival predicts wholly from current wage cost, notice and opportunity. An apparent private disposition in a person who has never been offered relief is weak evidence against any of them.

**Discriminator.** Freeze the models and prospectively observe first offers, including refusals, from a familiar peer, an unfamiliar peer occupying an equivalent role, and a supervisor. Compare role transfer across people with person-specific memory, with off-duty and unreachable-offer controls. Keep pay, actual authority, time remaining and task coverage comparable. Record independently the *attempt* and *release* as well as later care. Both models can predict staying when no substitute exists. The informative disagreement is conditional on a delivered feasible offer in a novel role/person combination. If a memory model with equal evidence and budget predicts transfer equally well, the extra private relation has earned no architectural priority. If the role and the person never vary independently, preserve both accounts. P history sampling and F ancestry/reproduction remain open for their own claims.

**Product.** A returning player might discover why yesterday's promise fell through only if the coworker or mother chooses to tell them. The player can offer help. Others can turn it down or already have arranged relief. That makes the appointment and its consequences playable while preserving the mother's outside relationships. The private motive cannot be revealed to the player by a survey lookup.

**Evidence boundary.** This is an original identification argument and a provisional historical mechanism. Seattle's scheduling study supplies evidence that institutional scheduling rules can alter actual schedule stability and worker outcomes in its studied setting. It supplies no dyadic relief roster, child acquisition law or cross-cultural estimate. [Harknett, Schneider & Irwin, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2107828118). The experiment alongside this memo measures what the existing sourcefree producer contributes. The missing empirical unit is the dated, linked offer opportunity. PYAT source and structural owners must qualify its denominator and rights, SOROK's world and actor owners must admit the exchange, and its developmental issuer must separately qualify any learning.
