# How PYAT can generate the social substance of a SOROK life

**Proposed scientific and computational architecture, 18 November 2026.** The object to generate is a history that several people actually make together. Nadia is the person who develops at one position in that history. PYAT enters twice. Its empirical sociology constrains which populations of people and circumstances a historical generator can credibly produce. Its independently warranted structural sociology enters the rules by which particular people can secure, withhold and change the conditions of one another's activity. SOROK supplies the development through which living under such conditions can become a person's own practical organization. Both entries matter. A generator can reproduce a survey perfectly while making its households materially impossible. A materially coherent generator can build whole towns in which nobody resembling an observed minority could ever develop.

## What crosses the seam

Keep each historical population in the units in which PYAT knows it. From the complete C11 source body, I03/I05 can supply respondent-linked configurations and conditional alternatives, including a contrary case that disappears in every lower-order projection. Retain form, occurrence, wording, routing, universe, estimator, permissible use and time. A direction of conditioning chooses a denominator. It confers no causal direction. F6 and F7 can extend comparison only across warranted construct and time overlaps. Distinct respondents in successive waves give evidence about historical populations. The time between waves still needs its own historical account.

Use these products in two distinct draws. First constrain the observable position and possible response configuration of a focal synthetic mother. Then construct the distribution of *other positions around her*, including positions quite unlike hers. Opportunities to encounter any of them depend on geography, division of labor, institutional membership, household arrangements and histories of actual contacts. The population proportion of a position supplies neither the size of her network nor the probability of contact. If the eligible proportions of two positions are known but their mixing is unknown, segregated and mixed settings can both satisfy the source. Retain those alternatives wherever they change a conclusion.

The selected PRE-V3 cut should carry a qualified historical setting, a position, its admissible observable configurations and the structural claims applicable to that position. N10E can supply an empirical possibility directly. A compatible selected N9 world and N10E can supply N10W when world conditioning is warranted. FF01's reported pattern and FF02's separately grounded claim about who can confer a right or impose a cost have different jobs. SDTF supplies the scope and circumstances of a mechanism, including counterpressure. V3's first substantive read is to construct who can do what *with or to whom*, at what time and cost, under those actual circumstances. If the source supplies a prevalence but no enforcement rule, the prevalence can constrain the population while enforcement remains open. If a law is established but local realization varies, eligibility can be determinate while realization remains open.

## A generative unit with two sides

Take a recurring practice such as arranging relief from a shop to collect a child. The smallest useful representation contains a time and place, a demand on someone's activity, the people in positions to respond, the resources or rights each controls, and what happens if nobody responds. On its objective side, collect only compatible performances into one event. Someone actually had to take the counter, the journey had to be possible, and the same quarter hour cannot be spent in two places. The ability to demand relief, the ability to refuse without losing future work, and the ability to make a refusal stick can belong to three different people. The very same mechanism applies to invitations, play, worship, teaching and collective action, including actions participants seek for their own sake.

On its subjective side, each participant meets a different event. One meant to help a sister. One understood the offer as a debt. A child heard an argument and saw a familiar adult arrive, and had no access to the earlier agreement. A fourth person stayed at work and learned nothing of the argument. The shared history needs only one actual transfer of labor. It can support four different histories of formation. In particular, inequality can organize a child's day long before the child has a concept for it.

The computational join is **the performed practice together with each participant's situated access to it**. It has two properties missing from direct person sampling. The available acts of one participant depend on what others independently attempt. And one participant's action can change the future opportunities of people who never perceive that action. For example, a refusal can keep the mother at work and change who feeds the child, even if the child never hears the refusal. This indirect route is essential to representing institutions and power through a life.

At each historical interval the existing world owner advances institutions, material stocks, obligations, places and feasible use of time. A world-system price change can alter a shop's hours through a supply chain, policy and local labor arrangement. It need never become a conscious geopolitical opinion. Keep the actual intervening relations across the original strata, down to intimate life and the embodied person. A country label cannot tell us which intermediary exists. Each actor owner reads its body's changing needs and capacities, unfinished projects, accessible situation, learned operations, relations and conflicting commitments. It may make a bid, persist, improvise, decline or do nothing. The material and institutional owners resolve compatible bids and their consequences. The actor's access owner then delivers what that participant could sense or otherwise encounter. Development consumes that participant's history. The world keeps the actual result, including a failed bid and any cost it incurred. A conflict between an employer's power and a worker's interest can endure for years without becoming a consciously recognized conflict.

## How a social relation can become an individual organization

Here is a candidate formation rule specific enough to distinguish rival explanations. Start with an actor attempting something that already matters to them. Record what operation they tried, what they could distinguish in that situation, what followed within their reach, and which other activity the outcome enabled or interrupted. Repetition can make the operation available for reuse. When similar attempts repeatedly have distinguishable consequences, a perceptible feature can become a condition of recruitment. When the actor cannot perceive what governs the difference, the outcomes can remain unpredictable to them, or they can learn the wrong distinction. If a skill carries to a new place, identify what the actor could recognize in both places. A relation repeatedly enacted can eventually organize an unprompted attempt, anticipation, hesitation or refusal, even in the absence of the person who first enforced it. That is a concrete way in which past social organization can remain active inside a person.

Anticipation alone cannot explain what comes to matter. A child can learn exactly who will come and still be indifferent, frightened, delighted or absorbed in another activity. Keep the history of *participation in ends* alongside the history of learned operations. A caregiver initially enables regulation or play. Later the child may take up part of a shared task, seek to continue it, discover a pleasure particular to it, or care what happens to the caregiver when the task fails. The step that changes an end is an actor-owned reorganization in which continuing a practice, another person's flourishing, or being seen a certain way begins to recruit activity in a different situation. Distinguish that from repeating a rewarded movement. Love can make another's project a reason to act even when they will never know. Institutional discipline can make appearing to be a good carer more important to the actor than the living person. A public performance can fit either account. Later unobserved action, costs voluntarily borne, transfer, rupture and repair could differentiate the accounts. No general developmental frequency or threshold for these changes is supplied by the survey.

Represent the result in the existing PFF as actor-specific connections among perceived situations, operations, projects, bodily responses, relationships and, when acquired, signs and concepts. Connections can recruit, inhibit, substitute, or remain inaccessible. Habitual action, felt expectation, verbal account, public compliance and the capacity to criticize can therefore diverge within one person. Language enters through historically particular use with others. An actor can acquire two usages compartmentally, lose access to one, or turn an inherited term against the institution that taught it. Where a history supports an Absolute Horizon, what becomes consequential is how a person orders finite claims at a limit or judges an institution by a fidelity learned through it. No religious answer supplies that organization. Pleasure, curiosity, play and aesthetic absorption also initiate activity. An account that only learns from sanctions would systematically exclude much ordinary life.

There is a precise economy question about C5. A slow motif computed solely from a complete history contains no independent evidence beyond that history. Its possible scientific and engineering value is to explain which past organization remains effective under bounded retrieval, and why it transfers to new situations. A context-indexed practice memory is the simpler rival. Install a slower organization only where it explains recurrence or transfer the simpler account misses under comparable information and cost. The formation laws here are hypotheses. The architecture works with rival laws and preserves consequences on which they disagree.

## How PYAT constrains the generator without dictating an individual

Imagine administering a particular instrument to the synthetic population in its applicable historical setting. An actor encounters a question with its wording, available answers, social audience and consequences of disclosure. Recruit what the actor can understand and express there. Allow refusal, strategic compliance and the absence of an applicable concept. Route questions as the instrument did and distinguish unasked, missing and valid zero. Several incompatible lived organizations can issue the same answer. The same living actor can act differently at home and still give that answer in good faith.

This gives an inverse constraint on forward generation. For a qualified target and one particular denominator D, let Y be the joint response to the administered items for the *same person*. With positive denominator, the ratio of expected weighted counts of (D and Y=y) to expected weighted counts of D must be compatible with PYAT's corresponding estimand and uncertainty. Use design weights only in the population and sampling representation for which they apply. An observed-sample constraint has its own target. A suppressed or unsupported estimate supplies no numerical equality. Fit applicable joints simultaneously. Multiplying overlapping pairwise and higher-order summaries as independent likelihoods would count the same evidence repeatedly. An observed zero constrains the sample result. A structural impossibility requires separate grounds.

In practical terms, generate candidate shared histories forward, compute their response implications, and retain or calibrate *ensembles* consistent with the qualified constraints. Preserve households, counterpart actions, costs and common institutions when selecting a history. Independently reweighting each family member into a preferred biography after they have acted destroys precisely the dependencies we need. For a mother with a selected response configuration, condition among compatible histories before committing the fictional past. Never force her answers at the observation end to disguise an incompatible generator. Fitting the same configuration used to select her proves only compatibility. Separate held-out population configurations can test empirical generalization. Evidence about actual encounters, transfer or relationships is needed to distinguish developmental accounts that are observationally equivalent on every survey item.

There are thus two ways a PYAT change can legitimately matter. A revised estimate can change which *new worlds* are credible or how often particular adult configurations are generated. A historical change in an actual right can change a particular actor's feasible activity inside a world, with consequences downstream if that right is exercised or anticipated. Rerunning an estimator tomorrow cannot change yesterday in Nadia's committed life. Conversely, an actual collective act can alter a local arrangement inside the fiction. Only an authorized objective witness can initiate SDTF rederivation for the affected world and time. The survey archive does not learn fictional facts.

## A mother's life, a child's life, and the common world

Take the empirical Nabatieh shop owner who reports campaign participation alongside low political interest. Those answers must remain jointly possible. In one fictional history a recurring obligation to a relative draws her into a campaign. In another she cares deeply about a particular local dispute and uses the instrument's term for politics narrowly. A third explanation could concern the circumstances of disclosure. Nothing in the source adjudicates these biographies. It does not establish that this respondent even has the child in the following illustration.

Generate the fictional mother's prior life and relevant coactors under the same forward grammar that will develop the child. Keep multiple eligible pasts until one is committed. Other lives progress before a father is identified. Later binding to an earlier actor is lawful only if that actor's prior effects on employment, kin, travel and third parties already belong to the dated history. If binding would change a past effect, refuse or branch before it. Contact, reciprocity, conception, co-residence, caregiving and authority are distinct relations. None follows from two survey respondents being demographically compatible.

Now let the fictional child interrupt her at closing time. In one history the possibility of relief lets her stop and attend. In another, relief is physically present but accepting it would entail a claim she has previously resisted. In a third, nobody can relieve her and she finds a game the child can join while she works. These outcomes require different conditions or histories to reach the decision. None follows from her party, sex or a tender-mother coefficient. The child could acquire an expectation that bids for attention have to wait, learn this particular game, or acquire nothing durable from the episode. The game might later become something the mother seeks with this child. That reversal matters. The child is already a participant in the mother's development.

Birth starts a dependent organism with variable bodily conditions, developing capacities and active signaling. No parent's attitude or PFF copies into it. Developmental time must contain ordinary feeding, movement, shared work, waiting, speech, care, absence and play. Sparse narrated turning points cannot stand in for this substrate. Compress recurring periods by retaining exposure, participation, resource consequences, the actors involved, and developmental changes the selected rule actually needs. A compressed period is defensible only for questions for which the omitted order or differences cannot change the answer within the admitted uncertainty. Refine before committing when a refusal, missed encounter, new capacity or scarce resource makes order matter. Keep unknown intervals visible. Coactors can run at lower resolution while retaining independent histories, claims and effects. This bounds computation without making the parents disappear at birth.

The adult who meets the user continues this same life. The user can provide relief, make an unwelcome demand, be misheard or be of little consequence. What changes depends on the performed act, what Nadia can sense, and what it means in her particular history. Her projects and those of people outside the room continue. The product question is whether encountering someone in this way changes how the user recognizes real others. Neither a sociologically credible generated population nor an engaging conversation by itself answers that question.

## The engineering choice

Use the protected PYAT empirical provider and its real source predicates for population constraints. Use separately qualified structural products for world constraints. At E001/E002/C0 resolve those values into actual position-specific resources, counterpart opportunities and refusals before a life is selected. In SOROK use the incumbent historical-world, P2/P3 activity and formation, and perception/public-experience owners. The missing work is to express the **same recurring practice** across those owners with executable reads in both directions. The world must consume actors' compatible performances and debit time or resources. Each actor must consume their own accessible part of the resulting event. Later, an ordinary decision must read what that actor acquired. The generator must also be able to administer a source-specific observation to a synthetic adult without revealing the target answer. These are design responsibilities, not claims of qualified implementations.

For an engineering return, retain the selected source and release, population denominator, structural warrant or explicit fictional assumption, event-time resource and coactor bindings, actor access, and the later operation that used the result. Existing provenance and replay owners should carry these relationships. For a scientific return, report separately what the population constraint identifies, what the structural evidence warrants, and which outcomes vary across admissible formation or contact laws. A playable fictional life can commit one such history. A claim about how actual people develop has to survive the alternatives. The previous opportunity calculation and separate P6 run establish only a narrow boundary property. They neither implement the join above nor establish a developmental law.

A concrete first reader would resolve a dated request for relief. It reads the worker's release rights, the shop's coverage requirement, each potential substitute's independent availability, and each party's already committed activity. It returns to each actor only the options that actor can access. A substitute's offer reserves nothing until performed and admitted. Admission transfers coverage for the actual interval and releases the first worker's time. The mother's next ordinary decision consumes that interval. The child's later decision can consume only an acquired consequence of what followed. This gives a precise severance criterion. If removing the only qualified source of the release right leaves the same unqualified release in force, the world generator has supplied its own sociology. If removing the child's access to the relevant cue still produces learning *from that cue*, the formation model has supplied its own experience. Either defect could coexist with perfect survey fit.

The proposed division of labor also yields a prediction about underdetermination. Suppose relief requires three facts at once: an available substitute, permission to delegate, and a child reachable before closing. If differently organized populations agree on every one- and two-way frequency, their frequency of viable episodes can still differ. Where PYAT actually measures a corresponding same-respondent joint, use that joint for the respondent-level facts it covers. Where a fact belongs to another household member, an institution, or another hour, the remaining linkage requires its own evidence or an explicitly varied fictional assumption. It is possible for a distinction in the source to be decisive for one design question and silent about the very next link. Keeping that boundary lets sociology do more work, because its real conclusions no longer have to answer for fictional ones.

**Design conclusion.** PYAT determines something irreducible about the *worlds of mutual dependence in which persons can form*. SOROK generates how a particular organism, situated in one of those worlds, lives those dependencies, makes some of them its own, and acts back upon them. An individual's freedom to turn out otherwise is part of the computational design. The costly error would be to buy that freedom by making PYAT dispensable.
