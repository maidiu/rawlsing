# Canonical PRE–Field Forge Artifact Boundary — R2B Holding

## Required logical boundary

The canonical boundary is not one physical file. It is a governed learned empirical release/service identity satisfying EE09/EE10.

Minimum information:

### Empirical determinants
- source/release/wave/universe identity;
- exact risk set / routing / applicability;
- topology/multiplicity/order where relevant;
- measure/estimand/weight/design authority where relevant;
- exact query/predicate/candidate identities;
- exact I03 facts and I05/discovery receipts;
- complete frontier/coverage accounting.

### Validation/adjudication
- candidate identity;
- exact validator/rule identity;
- validation evidence/fact refs;
- support/uncertainty state;
- typed disposition;
- exact negative/noncomparability/missing-authority state;
- explicit current-selection/adoption status.

### Release/replay
- learned-release identity/manifest;
- source and authority roots;
- dependency fingerprint;
- explanation lineage;
- replay receipt;
- invalidation vector;
- supersession/currentness state;
- exact reconstruction path or retained authoritative body as required by lifecycle law.

### Field Forge ingress
- exact empirical claim family;
- explicit allowed structural-use purpose or typed no-admission;
- required geometry/role/weight/transport premises identified without inference;
- negative ceiling forbidding causal/mechanism/actor/V3 promotion unless separately authorized.

## Three distinct closure levels

`PREFF-L0__CANONICAL_EMPIRICAL_CLOSURE`
I03/I05 objects + exact preimages + replay/coverage.

`PREFF-L1__GOVERNED_LEARNED_EMPIRICAL_RELEASE`
L0 + EE09 validation/adjudication + EE10 release/currentness/invalidation.

`PREFF-L2__FIELD_FORGE_INGRESS_READY`
L1 + exact structural-use docket/admission premises or typed refusal.

LiTS final canonical checkpoint proves L0 strongly and supplies all preimages for L1/L2 work. It does not by itself prove a universal generic L1 owner.

## Architectural prohibition

Field Forge must not become the generic empirical validator that PRE-V3 failed to implement.

Field Forge consumes governed learned empirical releases and decides structural relation formation/admission at its own jurisdiction.
