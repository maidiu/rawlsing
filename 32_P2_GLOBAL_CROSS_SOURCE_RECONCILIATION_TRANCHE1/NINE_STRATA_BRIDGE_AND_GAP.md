# Nine-strata bridge and gap

The requested nine-strata bridge cannot honestly be represented as a complete all-nine authority surface from the current artifacts.

The recovered field-lineage evidence shows that strata, horizon and position-conditioning fields exist authoritatively in V552 `question_semantic_hierarchy` for 998 rows across three GSS waves. They are dropped at the V552→V552J boundary because the V552J input index is derived from `structural_fact_index`, which does not carry them. Thin identifiers such as `relation_axis_id`, `target_entity_id`, `driver_facet_id`, `structural_channel_id`, `horizon_constraint_id` and `semantic_signature` survive; the richer strata/horizon payload does not.

This tranche therefore:

- preserves every available lineage-level semantic, driver, position, conditional-strata and horizon field without promoting it;
- records missing or dropped fields as explicit gap authority;
- copies the source lineage ledger unchanged;
- emits a normalized row-governed summary because the source ledger’s own summary metadata is stale: it declares 30 traced field families and status totals summing to 28, while the actual ledger contains 33 field-family rows—16 dropped at V552J, 8 preserved, 8 present-but-empty and 1 missing generated artifact;
- quarantines three inherited semantic mappings whose content visibly contradicts the exact Asian Barometer item, and preserves 27 items with no semantic-reference match.

No strata labels, weights, horizons or driver assignments were invented. Full bridge closure requires a governed doctrine definition plus repair of the V552→V552J handoff contract or an explicit sidecar carrying the authoritative V552 hierarchy fields.
