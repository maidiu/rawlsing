# HUMAN STORY — two things called “temporal pressure,” and the reader that chose one

V553 began with a strict identity step: one survey family, one country, one real calendar anchor per wave. It refused mixed families, mixed countries, duplicate years and wave ordinals pretending to be years. That is temporal identity, not measurement.

It then harmonized at least two certified waves: do they share semantic, directionality and position signal structure? Are the V552J surfaces complete and valid? That produced a harmonization authority report and evidence table. That is comparability structure, not a pressure value.

Only after those steps did V553 build two different temporal products. The first was a **keyed sparse authorized field**: for each exact temporal pressure key, observed authorized anchors carry signed/structural pressure, direction, volatility, confidence and authority; unobserved years remain explicit unknown gaps with no interpolation; probe-only keys stay probe-only; only a key with two or more authorized anchors gets a positive handoff index. The second was a **country/year aggregate** built from certified overlay row counts — coverage ratios, contradiction/absence/uncertainty penalties and a heuristic pressure intensity by calendar year. It is useful, but it is not the signed source-keyed evidence.

Then came V554. It read V553's readiness report, the harmonization report and the country/year aggregate. It did **not** read the keyed authorized field or the keyed handoff index. Its regional seed strength came from the country/year point estimate and its confidence from the country/year penalty. Its national seed strength came from readiness and harmonization scores. The keyed handoff could make V553 “ready,” but it did not supply a pressure value.

That is the precise handoff: V553 produced both authorized sparse evidence and a heuristic aggregate; V554 consumed the heuristic aggregate and the readiness gate, not the authorized evidence. A status-vocabulary mismatch makes the positive regional branch unreachable in this pinned producer→consumer pair: V553 anchors say `ready`/`ready_with_gaps`, while V554 only treats `anchor_derived` as positive. The later V555 lead does not turn these seeds into individual parent or household history; its mediation slots remain unresolved. This is a source-level information severance, not a global claim that V553's temporal capability vanished.
