## R3 denominator and receiving correction — source-level, independently reviewed

**Concrete illustrative population split (not an observed native run):** A is answered by 100 respondents, B by 80, and both by 60. The paired crosstab and NMI/Cramers V use the 60 shared respondents. If the modal anchor category is answered by 70 of A's 100 responders and the selected pair occurs in 40 jointly answered respondents, `p_cotravelling_given_anchor=40/70`. If the candidate modal category appears for 48 of B's 80 responders, `p_cotravelling_baseline=48/80`, and lift is `(40/70)/(48/80)`, not `40/60` divided by a paired-only baseline. Independently, `support_status` and `confidence_tier` receive `n_overlap=40` and `paired_total=min(anchor_meta.n_respondents,candidate_meta.n_respondents)` where those positive metadata fields exist; that total need not equal 60. This is a four-population contract, not a general claim that the estimator is calibrated correctly. Evidence `ZAK02:EV:0081`–`0084`.
