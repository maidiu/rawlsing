### T0026: position-indicator response-information diagnostic, NOT T0018 signature ranking
**Source:** [`ddm_v550_branching.indicator_summary_rows`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_branching.py#L143-L200), immutable blob `8f6d40e0d016d66e5a3b334b31747eaef752873f`. Inputs per-question respondent `response_value_normalized`, any of 12 `INDICATOR_FIELDS`, optional validated source weight series from compiler and qmeta. For each nonblank position indicator, compute original whole-question response entropy, entropy conditional on observed-indicator respondents, clipped entropy difference labelled `mutual_information`, independently calculated normalized MI and Cramer's V, dominant response, support and confidence. Outputs **one `position_indicator_summary` row per indicator**, with indicator_name, a chosen indicator_value, n_unweighted/n_weighted, baseline/conditional entropy, association and uplift, `weight_status`. It is **not** the separately-ranked top-five `branch_position_signature` at T0018, not a category marginal T0020, and not conditional answer cell matrix T0021.

**Distinct falsifiers from source:** `baseline_h=H(Y)` on *all* question rows while `cond_h=H(Y|X)` is calculated on only `X != ""` rows. Thus their subtraction is *not necessarily mutual information on one common sample*. An entirely constant indicator X on an answer-selected subset can report positive entropy_reduction against whole-question entropy, although on the common indicator-observed sample the true MI is zero. This is an **algebraic source counterexample, not observed output**. Normalized MI and Cramer's V are independently computed on filtered sample, so may contradict the clipped source label. When weights are supplied, `indicator_value` is chosen as category with the largest **within-category weighted dominant-response share**, whereas unweighted branch uses most frequent indicator category. Those are different selection rules. `dominant_response_value` and both `dominant_response_pct_unweighted/weighted` are populated from baseline dominant response/pct (even if baseline_pct was weighted); do not mistake them for category-specific weighted vs unweighted facts. Source helper L143–200; metrics helper `ddm_v550_metrics.py` L32–96 blob `e7675f4080bb30e6526027a34db747b7be7f85c9`.

**Producer and first reader:** `build_determinicity_wave_shard_v550.py` L1626/1634/1984, blob `82ffc18df29a8e5ebd78cde97c89d463b3842df6`; `build_determinicity_repository_v550.py` L2004/2012/2358, blob `04f3a3646d8b468660385087d92541f03f860cb0`. `ddm_v550_bridge.py` L99–124 blob `29edd5e24f095334683035f2ce1ec95674fe274b` maps **`position_indicator_summary` to `position_context`**, while T0018's `branch_position_signature` also enters context through a **different separately gated producer**.
