# R7 human story — who sees the actual observed structural history?

Imagine two surveys for one country, years apart. A V552J packet for each wave has an exact-scope structural measurement. V552J itself can say only “this particular row is safe for a V553 **probe**.” V553 can then compare the two waves, with real pressure values and identity matched, decide that an exact semantic key is comparable, record the measured change, and leave intervening years empty. That is an actual analytic operation within the historical code, subject to being run on real sources.

But the V554 seed builder does not pick up that key-based measured timeline. It picks up a different country-year series based on how many certified overlay rows exist and how much penalty to assign. It consults a global readiness flag. The source-qualified field with per-key use decisions is present **elsewhere** but not a verified input to that builder.

Worse, the country-year generator calls its anchors “ready” or “anchor,” while V554 is waiting for “anchor_derived” to issue a positive regional seed. Consequently a legitimate measured V553 anchor can leave the regional seed blocked in the inspected code pair. National seed may still become positive based on global readiness; neither result reconstructs an individual household, parent, carrier, biography, or actor.

The point is not to demand a grand campaign. Preserve the old accurate source observation, its uncertainty and forbiddance, identify the real reader, and test exactly this missing join before declaring the temporal machinery carried into N9 and the actor.
