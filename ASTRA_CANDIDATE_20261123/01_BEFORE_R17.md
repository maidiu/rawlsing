A child gets a sleeve through a sewing machine. Someone else unpicks and resets the seam every evening. The wearer receives a good sleeve. Completion has become reliable across the household. The child can have learned to guide fabric and to expect success without having learned to distinguish a seam that holds when worn. The adult also learns something, possibly a quick way to correct the child's particular pull on the cloth. Under a deadline the easiest way to keep producing good sleeves is to continue a division of work that withholds this distinction from the child. A productive relation can reproduce the conditions under which its own division of competence goes undetected. The mother's affection and the purchaser's deadline operate at different sites and hours. Their unity here is demonstrable in who stays up to unpick the seam.

Now vary who actually gets the sleeve back, while keeping assistance, finished quality, demonstrations and affection similar. In one history Nadia gets to watch it pull when the wearer bends an arm, undo her own row, vary the allowance, and see what the wearer can do after that. She begins leaving some seams provisional, even on unfamiliar shapes. A new practical object emerges. The first stitch can now be a way of asking a question answerable by a moving body. The wearer's objection can change when Nadia takes a task to be finished. Making room for another's answer becomes exercisable through an altered material procedure. It can also become a motive in its own right, evidenced if she spends scarce discretionary time arranging a return even when she could collect payment without one. A pre-existing tailoring trick, imitation, pleasure in the tool, or fear of complaints could produce the same loose stitch. Distinguish by changing size, audience, who pays, whose answer is adverse and whether the wearer wants to participate at all. Silence by a wearer who never had a chance to return cannot settle fit.

An institution can pay for a signed fitting yet refuse time to alter the garment. It can count an answered question while making the answer powerless. The skill of eliciting a complaint may now secure the signature that closes it. Nadia may dislike this and still produce the closure, take the extra work home, become an adept supervisor of others, or return to making garments for pleasure. A new arrangement reaches back into the acquired gesture, turning a provisional finish into final settlement without changing the worker's hands. The cost of keeping a consequence revisable has moved. The change is ascertainable even for a wearer who never notices the paperwork.

Initial favored center: an actor develops by coming to receive the consequences of her acts. The night worker defeats it. Another's unreceived corrections can sustain her material existence and teach her a false but locally effective expectation. The wearer who is injured belongs to this history even if no report ever returns. Replace the self-closing circuit with the continuing production of the conditions of further activity, distributed among bodies, tools, Others and institutions. Within this whole the unequal practical power to return, withhold, test, answer, or settle a consequence is a particular generator of psychological differences. It does not account for every desire. Nadia can love the sound of the machine when it is unthreaded, and both children can learn by watching without ever damaging a real garment. Noninteraction between that pleasure and a wage dispute may itself be produced by separate rooms, work times and responsibilities. In a different history their coincidence may simply be accidental.

Rival explanations to carry forward: selective attention and familiar cue; pre-existing dexterity or self-direction; a changed tool alone; obedience to a powerful witness; genuine learning organized by a person privately sensing a practical difference; practical knowledge embodied in the pair and unavailable to either alone. Unexpected loss of a helper separates some of these, but risks changing distress and time simultaneously. Mere withdrawal or persistent performance cannot identify an unconscious cause. An independently formed collective convention that changes what each new participant can distinguish may require a different history from both personal improvement and transfer to another task.
