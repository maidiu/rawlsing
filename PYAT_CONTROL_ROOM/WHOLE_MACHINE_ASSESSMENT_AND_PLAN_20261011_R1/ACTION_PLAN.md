# Forward action plan through PRE-V3 acceptance

**Status: proposed, dependency-bound program for operating-HQ adoption and scoped issuance.** The user requested assessment and publication; no implementation, raw-source execution, new authority, actor work or worker launch occurs through this document. Package IDs below are planning identifiers, not new HQ decision numbers. See TASKS.json for machine-readable dependencies and outputs.

## 1. Intended result and scheduling principle

Deliver one generalized machine that takes the complete governed 35-wave source estate and its competent original scientific authorities, automatically constructs and executes the applicable finite workload, preserves the resulting empirical and learned information, populates the warranted objective/multi-field/temporal world, and exposes permitted progressive possibility distributions to actual downstream consumers.

The plan has five productive programs plus a small shared control/evidence function:

| Program | Principal result | Starts alongside |
|---|---|---|
| A: source science and workload | Independent administered-item/operation expectation and machine bindings | B runtime preparation; C release boundary; D original comparability; E service contract |
| B: empirical execution | Governed real products, batch/shard/resume and all-source reconciliation | Each authorized A slice; do not wait for unrelated sources |
| C: learned/release and rich products | Authenticated, lossless C1/M2/B5 or lawful direct routes | A and B; source-free boundary work can start independently |
| D: objective and temporal world | Warranted N5/N6/N7/N8E/N8S artifacts with real consumers | Already-qualified retained products and original comparability work |
| E: selected service | Competent model/use plus coherent N9 and progressive N10W | D and C contract work; operative serving waits on its actual inputs |
| R/Q: evidence and acceptance | Exact state, independent oracles, integration and full-estate court | Continuous; must not consume all productive capacity |

One integrating HQ resolves authority and common interfaces. Source owners retain science; producer owners retain semantics; carrier/lifecycle owners retain transport/currentness. Use independent acceptance of frozen candidates. Concurrency follows dependency, write ownership, reviewer independence and measured host capacity; no fixed project-wide lane count is inferred from older packets.

## 2. Milestones and critical paths

**M0 — current evidence made actionable:** F8F/WVQ correctly intaken; staged/canonical distinctions and primary-evidence gaps recorded; task duplication avoided; existing adopted work credited.

**M1 — one reusable issued-work bridge:** a complete, independently sourced item/operation slice is expressed in the general schema, authorized at its exact scope, compiled into existing owner inputs, and tested with deliberate silent-omission attacks. This is a mechanism milestone, not all-estate acceptance.

**M2 — productive real slice with downstream consumption:** actual authorized source products, rich analytical outputs, appropriate release/direct route, consumer and restart/replay receipts. A meaningful measured null qualifies; an unexpected authority refusal does not.

**M3 — minimum integrated PRE-V3 witness:** at least two distinct families contribute authorized source-indexed fields to the same objective consumer; one competently comparable within-family temporal path reaches its declared world consumer; actual N9 and N10E/N10W progressive possibility operate with competent model/use and lifecycle boundaries. This is proof of integration at a small declared scope; it leaves the full all-35 requirement intact.

**M4 — full-estate productive occupancy:** every independently expected, scientifically applicable operation and demanded product across all 35 is accounted for, produced and consumed or remains an explicit failure. All source-intrinsic exclusions are independently justified; no held/deferred/missing item is counted as success.

**M5 — independent PRE-V3 acceptance and seam decision:** accepted exact integrated build, all applicable F1–F8/AT01–AT35 evidence, coherent current/as-of snapshots, replay and invalidation; then a separate N12 wake/defer decision. Passing PRE-V3 does not itself instantiate an actor.

Three paths can independently delay completion:

1. **Science/production:** A01→A02→A03→A04/A05→B02→B03→Q02.
2. **Trusted serving:** C01/C02→C03→E03/E04, with E01 model/use and E02 world inputs.
3. **World:** D01→D02/E02; D03→D04/E02; E02+E03→Q01→Q02.

These arrows describe actual data/acceptance dependencies. Documentary design, source-free probes and independent science review may proceed earlier. A task may consume an already accepted retained input instead of rerunning its predecessor.
