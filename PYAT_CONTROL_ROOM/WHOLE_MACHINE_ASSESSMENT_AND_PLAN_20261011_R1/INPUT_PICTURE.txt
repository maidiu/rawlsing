Rawlsing PRE-V3 — The whole-machine progress picture
The central conclusion from challenging the HQ276–278 completion matrix with LR-A, LR-B and LR-C is this:
Rawlsing is considerably further along in implemented machinery, inherited scientific documentation and real empirical production than the original matrix made visible. But it is not yet close to satisfying the full PRE-V3 acceptance standard.
Those two things are not contradictory.
The problem is no longer that we cannot find the machinery that produces distributions, conditionals, discoveries, learned products and structural inputs. Much of it exists, and significant portions have actually executed.
The principal unfinished work is turning the recovered source-scientific estate into a complete, independently authorized workload, executing that workload through the existing machinery, and producing the demanded objective sociological world and possibility service.

3. The most important unfinished edge: WG0–WG4
The WGA→WGE series was productive because it progressively narrowed the central problem identified by HQ277.
We now possess:
A conserved historical 18,276-implementation inventory.
A corrected permission-shaped ledger preserving 78,037 original operation annotations.
Separation of 28,316 proposed job-candidate annotations from constraints, prerequisites, topology duties and participant flags.
3,142 separately represented held, denied or inapplicable obligations.
Stable and replayable documentary keys, including WGE's correction.
Real no-drop demonstrations at bounded source-free and retained empirical frontier scopes.
What we do not yet have is the machine-readable, independently source-qualified set of all scientifically applicable executable operations across all original measurements.
That difference is the critical path.
The scientific-authority frontier must determine that an operation is legitimate before observing its result. Otherwise Rawlsing could silently omit questions, treat unresolved routing as an empty denominator, turn a shared-topic pair into an illegitimate joint operation, or announce completion merely because its own manifest contains everything it decided to run.
WGC/WGD/WGE repaired much of the accounting problem. They did not confer the missing original-source scientific permissions.

The last four gates are especially important.
We should not let the successful Afrobarometer CONTEXT route stand in for a populated objective social world. A conditional association is genuine empirical evidence, but it cannot automatically represent a material institution, causal mechanism or durable social condition.
Likewise, having multiple survey families indexed in a repository is not F6. We need their genuinely distinct source-indexed structural products to enter the same authorized world composition without pooling respondents or inventing common sampling units.
And having two WVS or Afrobarometer waves available is not F7. Their original measurements, national frames, response meanings, routing, weights and estimands must be comparable before the temporal engine can make a governed claim about historical change.

Reconcile original measured items, permitted operations, expected results, completed jobs and actual consumed artifacts; preserve scientific nulls and unresolved holds; execute AT01–AT35 and required F1–F8 positive/hostile tests. Every source-supported mandatory product must either exist and be consumed or remain an explicit, unresolved failure—not silently disappear.

The central recommendation
The next major HQ decision should be to reissue the completion matrix as a genuinely evidence-backed live progress register, populated directly from LR-A/B/C and all the accepted subsequent receipts.
That should happen alongside—not instead of—productive dispatch. The register must show which exact original-item/operation/frontier/consumer edges are satisfied, held on scientific issuance, missing a real product, or missing an implemented connector.
Then make WG1/WG2 → WG3 the dominant engineering/science line, while F5/F6/F7 world production and N9/N10W selected-service work continue in parallel.
The finish line is no longer obscure. The machine's parts and much of its scientific material are there. The remaining challenge is governing and joining them into a complete, populated, independently verifiable source-to-world system without losing original meaning or manufacturing authority.
