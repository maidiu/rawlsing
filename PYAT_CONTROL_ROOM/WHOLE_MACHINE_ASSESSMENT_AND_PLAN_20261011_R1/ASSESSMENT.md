## 1. Answer to the supplied assessment

The central diagnosis is sound: the project is well beyond inventing its empirical engines, and substantial source documentation and actual bounded production already exist. The decisive work is to establish the complete scientifically warranted workload, connect it to existing operators, populate the required objective world and serve it under competent authority. Full PRE-V3 completion is not established.

Two qualifications matter. First, “not yet close” is an intuition, not a measured engineering estimate. The unreconciled estate and unknown issuer/consumer dependencies prevent a defensible percentage or date. Second, the remaining work cannot all be called integration: source interpretation, actual authority issuance, production authentication, demanded objective producers and independently tested deployment may each require substantive work. A real owner failure can justify a narrow repair; unoccupied scope alone does not justify redesign.

The stronger corrected conclusion is: **substantial scoped capability and empirical capital; incomplete source-to-operation authorization; incomplete authenticated source-to-world realization; full-estate readiness unproved.** This preserves HQD124 while applying HQD125/HQD216's stronger productive standard. ## 4. Important omissions and stronger distinctions

### 4.1 Publication, review, adoption and current operation

At the read cut, canonical control remained `6a8c91d99dca1ae2fd3559ba3c11ee26649e3cd9`; LATEST named the sealed HQ280 checkpoint at `2936ccaca35d8a0414869efdd2870f1ea244f1c2`, and designated semantic PRE-MAIN `1f47063c3205a45888b3c55e81e7485943ed1390`. Consequently, the picture blends canonical authority, staged adjudications, worker candidates and externally held evidence. This is not automatically a loss of scientific work, but it is a real handoff/integration risk. A future HQ must reconcile each event against exact authority and code identity. This packet adds a current documentary map without changing LATEST or claiming a new HQ decision.

### 4.2 B5's authenticated publication boundary is a separate critical path

F8F's K4/K5 probes create modified lifecycle subjects and call actual B5 owners. `stage_release` accepts a subject object; `publish_product` checks a replay-state string and nonempty authority/determinant references. The inspected code does not establish the origin of those claims. Fake replay-complete state, fake production-looking ceiling, and fake receipt/root references can reach PUBLISHED in the demonstrated source-free invocation. [E20–E21]

This is a specific trust-boundary result, not proof of an external deployment compromise or a reason to reject all earlier B5 local contracts. The next task is to identify the trusted producer-to-B5 boundary, keep unrestricted subject construction outside the serving surface, and authenticate product identity, replay evidence, permission ceiling and current authority before operational selection. A producer credential is one candidate solution; an already competent owner-controlled ingress may be another. Require an exact threat model and proof, rather than presupposing a new crypto infrastructure.

**280 passed does not mean 280 security refusals.** Several tests pass because they reproduce the gap. Also, F8F's prose calls K6 an equal-arity/equal-denominator alien-condition attack; the test source constructs the alternative via q2 while targeting q1. ### 4.3 Four separate authority acts must not collapse

Keep separate: original measurement/routing/design authority; allowed operation/finite discovery grammar; producer/release/lifecycle and downstream structural-use authority; operative model/use/serving authority. A technical registrar is not a source scientist. An AuthorityRef is not a signer. A hash proves content identity, not competence. A prospective workload seal does not predetermine empirical truth. G0b is not solved by E2B retained-CONTEXT permission, and the latter is not solved by source-free EE14 development competence.

### 4.4 Source science is richer than labels and item counts

WG1 must recover administered item instances, country/language/form/occurrence, response polarity and special missing states, respondent/household/other-person unit, routing risk, native MR/rank/repeat structure, design and estimand. WG2 must issue operation-specific scope and uncertainty/support rules. Physical same-row availability, same target entity, the existence of a codebook, and a field-name match are insufficient substitutes.

WVQ illustrates a general failure class: a parent battery label, its 18 children, the retained file version, and two anonymous five-state query targets are different identities. Do not let a source-specific investigation turn into a manual-analysis program; extract reusable identity and eligibility rules. [E12]

The actual I05 compiler enumerates the authority-declared finite frontier before evaluation. Its signature and loop are directly inspected. Route B takes supplied projection/catalog/I03 requests/discovery authority. Neither claims to discover every scientifically legitimate input on its own. [E18–E19]

Declare exhaustive bounded scopes, candidate meaningfulness, arity/depth/support rules and any adaptive extension mechanism before running. ### 4.6 Objective world, temporal world and actual service are distinct deliverables

F5 needs exact structural claims and demanded objective functions: conditions, positions, opportunities/exposure, relations, rules/mechanisms or consequences only at warranted scopes. A CONTEXT relation or reported attitude is not automatically a material institution or causal law. Do not manufacture mechanisms to fill a table; do not omit demanded source-supported producers either.

F6 needs separately sourced fields in one real world consumer. The global graph contains nine families, 36 syntactic family-pair candidates and generalized higher-order subsets. It does not demand 36 positive pair products. F7 similarly contains 63 syntactic within-family wave pairs and higher-order scopes, not 63 authorized trends. The 564 historical temporal audit units are a different population. Sampling-frame and measurement comparability must be established at the claim/estimand grain. N8E empirical change or no-change and N8S structural interpretation are different outputs. [E22]

F8 needs actual N9 plus N10E→N10W service, preserving progressively conditioned alternatives, uncertainty, current/as-of identity, permission and the actor firewall. G1/G3 source-free successes and a hardcoded XOR release candidate establish bounded capabilities; they do not constitute that service. Route-specific dependencies remain conditional: no universal forced M1/I4 or LiTS D2 detour.

### 4.7 Operational completion also needs custody and resource evidence

Reproducibility requires exact owner trees, dependencies, environment, frozen expectation bytes, durable outputs and independently retrievable receipts. Recorded host absence is not global data absence. Budgets, sharding, deterministic resume, cancellation, cache neutrality, atomic publication and invalidation are first-class production concerns. Test the composed path under interruption and mixed-success runs; never declare all-estate success from a completed shard.
