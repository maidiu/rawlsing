## Scope

This is an independent regrade of the immutable S5 `FUNCTION_COURT.jsonl` at `1aa0407869620cd8ba2e8ca1f1533b99fd097162`, under the corrected evidence law `f50651a2bb0758e9fe6d6a2852441dcc782344be`. It does not rewrite the old return, run branch code, or claim current production acceptance.

The six axes are kept separate for every row: `HISTORICAL_MERIT`, `CURRENT_FUNCTIONAL_PRESENCE`, `SCIENCE_AND_THEORY`, `COMPATIBILITY`, `PERMISSION`, `ADOPTION`.

## 1. The 119 `UNIQUE_USEFUL_READY` promotions

**S5 claim:** 119 functions are ready.

**Counterexample:** S5 itself records `tests_run_by_this_lane: NONE` and `first_consumer.kind = BRANCH_TESTS_ONLY__NO_CURRENT_CONSUMER` for 119 rows. Historical `APPROVE_AT_DECLARED_GRAIN`, an old 55-test claim, or an author court is not a current ABI/first-consumer/fault-negative execution.

**Correction:** all 119 are `HISTORICAL_ACCEPTED_SCOPED_CURRENT_UNTESTED`. 114 have no current function path; 5 have a current same-name/different implementation and no current test. `ADOPTION=HISTORICAL_ACCEPTANCE_ONLY`; `COMPATIBILITY` is `NOT_TESTED`; no `READY`. Promotion condition: exact current path/ABI, independent current positive and fault-negative tests, exact current consumer, and the named sourcefree/current permissions.

## 2. The 6 `SUPERSEDED_BETTER` claims

**S5 claim:** 6 historical variants are superseded by better current code.

**Counterexample:** S5 compared blobs/AST and the existence of a current final blob, not a reproduced positive/fault-negative assertion on the current receiver. AST difference is not superiority. The two analytical C1 and one EE14 rows point at a current exact final blob, but no current behavioral comparison was run; the three temporal rows remain under F7 named defects and are not adopted.

**Correction:** all 6 are `SUPERSEDED_UNPROVEN__COMPARISON_REQUIRED`, not `SUPERSEDED_BETTER`. Historical merit remains `PREVIOUSLY_ACCEPTED_SCOPED` for the analytical/EE14 old variants and `REVIEW_REVISE` for the temporal variants. Promotion condition: same function, reproduced original positive and fault-negative on the current receiver, and a demonstrated correctness/safety/custody improvement or removal of defective reliance.

**Correction:** all 20 are `SOURCEFREE_TEST_APPLICABLE__SOURCE_AUTHORITY_HELD`; `ADOPTION=SOURCEFREE_INTEGRATION_CANDIDATE`, `HISTORICAL_MERIT=REVIEW_REVISE`, and `SOURCE_NATIVE_READ`/`SCIENTIFIC_RULE_ISSUE`/`MODEL_USE`/`WORLD_STRUCTURAL_ADMISSION` are `REQUIRED_UNGRANTED` only for actual source/rule use, with the named issuer/operation. ## 10. Missing path is not missing function; AST equality is not current acceptance

- `IDENTICAL_BLOB`/`EXACT_AST_SAME_PATH` (86 rows) is code identity only.
