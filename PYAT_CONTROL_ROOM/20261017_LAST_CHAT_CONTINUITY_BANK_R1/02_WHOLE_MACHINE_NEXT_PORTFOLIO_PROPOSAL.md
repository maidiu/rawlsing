The terminal consumer must eventually distinguish:
- World/structural fact and actor independent possibility versus a person's actual exposure and experience.
- Nine finite strata as *relations and pressures*, not a nine-feature demographic spreadsheet; Absolute Horizon is a separate actor-level orientational domain.
- Institutions, sanctions, rents, household labor, care, protections, exclusions, occupations, movement and counterpressure as concrete encounterable conditions, not generalized "social context".
- Person needs/vitality/desire and changing capacities; mediations via specific people and organizations rather than direct country/survey→psyche.
- Simultaneous contradiction: agency-of one inherited formation and agency-against another; no ideological coherence requirement.
- Contingency and path dependence: changed material conditions and changed events can lead to different plausible histories, not one predetermined type.
- Longitudinal development/attractors rather than fixed Enneagram assignment. Voice and self-narration cannot create hidden causal facts; historical occurrence ≠ private knowledge/memory ≠ expression.
