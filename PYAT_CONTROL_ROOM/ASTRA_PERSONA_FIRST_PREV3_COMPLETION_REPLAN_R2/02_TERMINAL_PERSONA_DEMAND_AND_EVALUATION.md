# Terminal contract and falsifiable evaluator

A usable deliverable is a generated person with a coherent life history, material and cultural situation, relationships, motives, capacities, contradictions and distinctive expression, plus an inspectable explanation of which inputs and model decisions shaped them. It must support a changed-context comparison. A source table, fixture receipt or vivid biography alone does not satisfy this contract.

## Proposed minimum conditioning envelope

Bind an immutable release/as-of snapshot, source and population identities, geography/period/cohort, household and institutional material constraints, source-conditioned possibilities with joint/conditional roles and denominator/risk context, structural pressure histories, uncertainty and contradictory alternatives, method/policy versions, eligibility/conditioning roles and replay dependencies. No mandatory universal container or single-owner topology is introduced: adapt the existing typed owners at the consuming seam.

Each value has an epistemic mode: `OBSERVED`, `TRANSFORMED`, `MODELED`, `PRIOR` or `GENERATED`. These are proposed annotations mapped to existing authority types, not new issuers. Include applicability, units, provenance, support/extrapolation bounds and what uncertainty means. A confidence heuristic must not be labeled a confidence interval. A prior may be deliberately simple and uncalibrated; it must say so.

Actor-independent conditions constrain opportunities. Generated parents, attachment episodes, rupture, learning, coping and attractor development belong to actor history. Do not derive a measured Enneagram type from survey answers. Do not require identical persons under identical demographics; require replay under identical complete inputs and seed.

Finite household fallback: generate a limited parental/household context using explicit priors, rather than recursively simulating ancestors indefinitely. Record the boundary and test its influence. Expression consumes the resulting state; it cannot silently rewrite it.

## Eight required counterfactual classes

| Case | Controlled change | Expected useful effect | Falsifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | Same demographics/seed, changed historical material constraint | Opportunities and mediated trajectory can change in the declared direction; trace identifies mechanism | Only prose changes, or a type is assigned directly from country |
| C02 | Same context, changed qualified empirical conditional | Relevant possibility distribution changes; downstream use is logged | Context label is read but conditional payload is ignored |
| C03 | Same evidence, alternative method policy | Difference is attributable and uncertainty/cost visible | Model choice is presented as new observation |
| C04 | Different birth cohorts in same location | Exposure windows and developmental timing differ; no automatic causal certainty | Survey year is copied as birth year or age-period-cohort effects falsely identified |
| C05 | Minority and contradictory joint configurations | Low-frequency alternatives remain possible with exact support retained | Majority averaging removes contradictions or sampled minorities |
| C06 | Different parent/household priors with other inputs fixed | Plausible relational variation; boundaries and sensitivity visible | Parents are country averages or generated family history called measured |
| C07 | Strong versus weak evidence | Weak evidence yields broader/explicitly less justified possibilities, not arbitrary confidence | Missing evidence maps to a precise neutral personality |
| C08 | Same marginals, different same-respondent joints | Joint-aware paths distinguish configurations | Independent marginal multiplication produces identical answers |

Run matched seeds and compare distributions/trajectories, not a requirement that every individual changes in every intervention. Prototype suite: at least one paired witness for each class, 16 seeded cases total; expand when a test exposes instability. Freeze expectations independently before inspecting candidate outputs. These are proposed counts, not executed results.

Deliver biography/state, snapshot/seed/policy hashes, per-case causal-use trace, uncertainty annotations, reviewer findings and a comparison page. Prototype success requires no fabricated observation, replayable state, meaningful input sensitivity where specified, preserved alternatives and expression fidelity. Human plausibility review is evidence of usefulness, not proof of psychological causal truth.

## Evaluation jurisdiction

Before V3 authorization, C01–C08 can be specified and exercised only at permitted source-free PRE-V3/interface grain: compare context payloads, conditional distributions, support/uncertainty, parent-prior envelopes and replay receipts. Actor trajectories, actual parent generation, biography quality and expressive confidence require the separately authorized developmental runtime. The 16-case person suite is a future acceptance target, not a run performed or presently authorized by this research commission.
