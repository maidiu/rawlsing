## Methods to try only when they address an exposed product gap

**Partial pooling/poststratification.** Can stabilize sparse population cells, with applicable population totals and modeling assumptions. It does not manufacture survey design information or prove transport across incompatible populations. The foundational example explicitly leaves cluster sampling outside its treatment. For Rawlsing this supports a bounded candidate comparison, not a mandate for a new system. [Gelman and Little, 1997](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/poststrat3.pdf).

**Predictive evaluation.** Proper scoring rules compare probabilistic forecasts against outcomes and discourage rewarding unsupported confidence. Use suitable scoring and calibration on held-out lawful data; neither a good score nor a vivid persona proves psychological causation. [Gneiting and Raftery, 2007](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf).

**Cross-source synthesis.** Start with separate population-indexed channels and explicit context roles. Introduce a modeled joint only with declared dependence assumptions; compare plausible alternatives when joint dependence is unidentified. A lack of exact identification calls for uncertainty/sensitivity or a simpler prior, not automatic product abandonment. This is a proposed engineering policy, not a claimed empirical result.

**Temporal fallback.** Nearest qualified anchor plus bounded broad drift is the default competitor to V553 interpolation. Where change points are historically meaningful, model them explicitly instead of forcing linear smoothness. Unknown periods may remain prior-driven. Never label interpolation as a survey observation.

**Latent constructs and proxies.** Fit or replace only the dimensions shown to matter in persona tests. Compare simple ordinal constructs against a richer model using the same information, subgroup behavior, uncertainty, interpretability and compute. Do not require a calibrated universal psychology before using provisional developmental parameters.

## Risks and concrete controls

Runtime variant drift → exact manifest and first-session smoke run. Weak priors → visible policy plus C03/C06/C07 sensitivity. Demographic stereotypes → matched-context/seed interventions and minority cases. Evidence leakage → freeze held-out split before tuning. Narrative invention → state-backed renderer and fidelity checks. Governance recurrence → one bounded implementation envelope and operation-local holds. Research sprawl → one decision per method timebox. Security scope laundering → explicit frozen/current mode and real invoked trust requirements. All35 neglect → preserved original25/source35/74-row ledgers and separate certificate.
