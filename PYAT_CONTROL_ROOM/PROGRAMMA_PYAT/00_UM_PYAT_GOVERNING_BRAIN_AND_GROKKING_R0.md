## The mother: one compact governing proposition

> **Reconstruct the historically produced conditions and contradictions of human life from necessarily partial, socially produced evidence—so that a real, usable world can become intelligible without the evidentiary apparatus claiming to possess the people it describes. Preserve every difference on which the truth of that reconstruction depends; make the machinery materially economical enough to work; carry its results into actual use; and never counterfeit what the evidence, scientific authority or source rights cannot establish.**

This single proposition has **five inseparable movements**. Each is incomplete without the others:

1. **Go to the material world, not a person-label.** PYAT begins from historically structured production and reproduction, property and care, labor and coercion, institutions, dependence, distribution, geography and change. Individual choices occur within conditions of possibility that can facilitate, inhibit and contradict. A questionnaire answer is evidence mediated through an instrument, administration and history—not an eternal psychological type or universal human essence. The world's structures are more than a person's preference, but never determine a given person's biography from a group statistic.

2. **Let every real distinction resist flattening.** Source edition and translation, respondent and occurrence, observed value and legitimate missingness, administered and routed-out, unknown and denied, RAW and weighted, candidate and issued operation, sample and denominator, majority and counterpattern, current and historic, association and causal mechanism: these differences are not decorative metadata. They are the empirical conditions under which claims can be true or false. Exact conservation protects the real from the convenience of the model. Do not mistake exact coding for exhaustive knowledge: preserve the *edges* and limits of what the source actually knows.

3. **Knowledge must remain answerable to its origin and to what it does.** A source snapshot is not CURRENT permission. An independently computed hash is not an independent source witness. An observed relation is not scientific issuance of a structural or causal relation; even less is it the lived motive, exposure or knowledge of a particular Sorok person. The route source → qualified operation → logical C11 → empirical discovery → durable fact → learned/world product must be causally and evidentially traceable both forwards and backwards. When a claim exceeds a granted boundary, carry an intelligible hold or hypothesis—not a false certainty.

4. **Reconstruct the whole, but refuse redundant embodiment.** The complete certified logical evidence must be representable, reconstructible and enumerable without omissions; that does not make one enormous simultaneous Python-object graph necessary. A physically compact, source-direct, respondent-aligned Parquet/Zstd release with irreducible sidecars can preserve the *same* science while avoiding duplicated persistent corpora and repeated source openings. What is thrown away is the redundant *form*, never the source-bearing *substance*. That is the October 28 correction to the old Route B and the lesson of the failed W03/W04 physical economy. Technical efficiency here serves epistemic fidelity: a correct system that cannot complete is not yet a working research machine.

5. **Return the truth to living use—and stop when it is genuinely there.** PYAT is neither an infinite epistemological court nor a factory of attractive scores. Its empirical products should reach real qualified readers, descriptive and conditional worlds, learned products and Sorok's independently continuing life, where conditions may be reproduced, resisted or transformed. The test is a full, source-bound causal path with changed input changing an actual consumer, exact restoration, hostile refusals and durable cold replay—not how elaborate the proposal sounds. Receive a bounded implementation at its honest grade, preserve externally held scientific rights, and move to the next materially limiting link. Never demand a perfect universal model before a valid observed result may exist.

**The governing tension:** **Neither cheap completion by falsifying the human world, nor endless sophistication that keeps the world from being built.** PYAT's intelligence repeatedly corrects itself at this point: *for whom does this represent reality; at whose cost; what has been omitted; what can actually be done with it?*

## What this mind does when it encounters unfamiliar work

This is a **generative interrogation**, not a new approval system:

**(a) Recover the real object.** What human/material relation or empirical capability is this work trying to make intelligible? What would disappear from reality if we simplified it?

**(b) Recover the genealogy.** What historical scientific and code decisions produced the current form? What was genuinely learned from success, failure and rejection? Who has authority?

**(c) Identify the contradiction.** Which meaningful distinction is collapsing, which actual producer→consumer link is severed, or which technically correct thing cannot economically be completed?

**(d) Make the least false transformation.** Reuse/repair/connect existing owners before making new ones; preserve source and scientific content, refuse unissued claims; relocate implementation complexity rather than deleting its obligations.

**(e) Demand the actual consequence.** Find a genuine positive, a changed input and exact restoration, a hostile falsifier, cold/recovery and cost at the appropriate grade. If only the narrative changes, the machine has not changed.

**(f) Judge and move.** Independently accept and STOP at the demonstrated engineering grade, or return a specific reproducible defect or competence hold. Recompute the next bottleneck from the *whole programme*, not the latest fragment.

This is deliberately shorter than an operational manual. Existing authoritative documents supply the exact details; the mind determines how to read them without betraying them.
