## 4. Exact seven-step pre-adjudication, before issuing/reopening a commission

1. **Threatened empirical/world invariant?** Identify actual data/meaning/causal/product loss or a missing first consumer. If only attractive optimization, mark it optional.
2. **Competent source and existing owner?** Read original PYAT, actual Oct28 selected target and R3 contract, current Git source, accepted prior implementation, exact owner/lease and independent reviewer. Do not replace old capital before verifying its function.
3. **Where precisely does the edge break?** S01 source/read/qualification, S02 operation/fate, S03 I02 proof/physical C11/cold reader, S04 I03/I05/Batch/F1–F4/retention, S05 M1/M2/B5/current, or separate FF/N10E/N9/Sorok use. Record the real first reader and exact missing authority.
4. **Smallest source-true move?** Prefer `REPAIR` then `CONNECT` then narrow `EXTEND`; `SOURCEFREE_WITH_NATIVE_HOLD` where science is unissued; `DEFER` speculative refactors, ubiquitous pair computations or unbounded data simulation. No new scientific engine or parallel corpus absent a demonstrated failure.
5. **Evidence and rights ceiling?** State authenticated source/issuer/host/QSM/U/Epre and scientific availability, what actual selected data are permitted, what is original35 explicitly not yet run, and which external decision is necessary.
6. **One decisive executable falsifier?** Real producer→consumer (including cold/restart where needed), changed/restore, strongest hostile non-loss and self-rehash case, complete required cardinality, bounded 100k/respondents resource at its *designated integrated gate*, no false green by skipped/old-head tests.
7. **Decision/stop/priority?** Receive exact independent G5 at rightful grade, preserve source/scientific/actor/protected independent holds, update #909 original packet state/consumer and downstream unlock; escalate only specific unresolved governing alternatives with materially different source/world consequences. Stop when bounded proof completes; do not create another review of the review.

**Tie-breakers:** first source and respondent scientific truth, then complete empirical/structural meaning and non-loss, then actual native world first consumer, then reuse/economy of one authoritative corpus, then least scope and finite independently supported completion.
