## I. The generative center

> **Human beings emerge historically through their participation in material, social, symbolic and contradictory relations they did not choose, but which their own activity helps reproduce or transform. The empirical world bears partial, power-mediated traces of those relations. PYAT must make the conditions, constraints, distributions, possibilities and contradictions they disclose as intelligible and source-traceable as possible—without converting a social average into an individual's essence, a constraint into a motive, an association into a cause, or an economical simulation into a counterfeit person. The entire machine must be materially complete and usable: preserve the real distinctions, refuse unwarranted claims, deliver to actual world and human-facing consumers, and stop when its rightful proof is achieved.**

This is the **mother**, not any single technology. Everything else follows from the attempt to make *this* true under actual empirical, scientific and resource constraints.

Its two irreducible negations are:

**Against reductive completion:** to make a person/culture/type seem intelligible by flattening actual property relations, labor, care, colonial history, plural activities, uncertainty and contradiction into convenient national means, type probabilities or narratives. A good story does not justify an invalid causal inference.

**Against endless incomplete correctness:** to preserve so many theoretical qualifications, source audits, intermediate objects and tests that a scientifically valid lower-grade observed result never reaches a real consumer. A perfectly described unbuildable system has not yet made its objects usable.

The governing intelligence moves *through* these tensions, rather than choosing one side.

## II. Nine consequences an unfamiliar agent must be able to derive

### 1. The social is relational, not a bundle of individual attributes

Class is not simply income; it can involve property, control of labor, exploitation and contradictory positions. Life is reproduced by paid and unpaid work, care, housing, migration, institutional power and access to collective resources. Colonialism is not just a historical duration with a trauma coefficient; it reorganizes the material conditions under which generations live, interpret and act. Gender relations cannot be reliably reduced to a survey 'equality score.' Different groups encounter the *same* institution unequally.

**Derived inference:** PYAT's legitimate context is a historically organized **field of relations and constraints**, not a national-cultural psychic average. WVS, WID, source codebooks and other data may measure *aspects* of the field, but a codebook's categories do not construct its ontology by themselves. This has strong affinities with Sève, Erik Olin Wright and social-reproduction theory as provided; **their specific substantive theories remain scientifically contestable and require exact issuance before implementation**.

### 2. A person's psychology is mediated by practical living, not directly caused by population indicators

The person is constituted through specific ways of participating: work, care, schooling, law, institutions, family, friendship, religion, conflict, desire, abandonment, protection, collective struggle and inaction. A socially imposed condition may create incentives and penalties; it does not determine how each individual interprets, resists, evades or reproduces them. One structure can produce multiple historically intelligible responses, including contradicting responses within the same person's life.

**Derived inference:** between *social structure* and *subjective disposition* lie **situated histories of activity and relations**. Neither materialist determination nor personal agency warrants deleting these mediations. Leontiev's activity/motive, Dreier's cross-context participation, Lahire's conditional plural dispositions, Kohn's proximate conditions and historical life-course research supply resources for this conclusion; **PYAT is not thereby authorized to install one hybrid personality algorithm**.

The decisive forbidden shortcut is:
`country/wave/group average → person's type, desire, obedience, trauma, psychic health, or utterance`.

### 3. The real social world is a structured space of conflicting possibilities

The question inherited from PROGRAMMA is not simply *what do people believe?* It is: **What can people do? What is available, facilitated, obstructed, compelled or forbidden? By which institutions, resources, relations and countervailing powers? Whose possibilities shrink when another's expand? What may become possible through changed collective action?**

The same social relation can be experienced as security by one position, constraint by another, and contradictory by an individual who occupies multiple positions. Contradictions and minority counterpatterns are not data-cleaning errors. Domination may reproduce itself in everyday activity while creating tensions, alternative practices and possibilities of resistance.

**Extrapolated modal grammar (NOT an adopted scientific state machine):**
`structurally possible ≠ practically accessible ≠ socially incentivized/compelled ≠ perceived ≠ desired ≠ attempted ≠ occurred ≠ learned ≠ narrated`.

A probability of a reported answer is not automatically the probability that a course of life is feasible or that someone will take it.

### 4. History is uneven, recursive and collective, not a static 'cultural climate'

What a person experiences depends on when an institution or historical rupture enters their life, who depends on whom, what opportunities were previously available, which practices were acquired and which later contexts activate or inhibit them. The same generation shares history without sharing one destiny; different cohorts, classes, regions and relationships live heterogeneous temporalities. People also change their conditions through action, together and in conflict.

**Derived inference:** a credible generator must distinguish historical world formation, actual exposure/participation, acquired organization, later activation, retrospective testimony and future consequences. Reusing a static vector with an 'adult recursion' prompt does not prove these mechanisms. F6 nonpooling, F7 wave comparability, genuine historical-source qualification and Sorok's actual actor-development work become scientifically consequential *where demanded by the specific claim*, never an excuse to ban truthful cross-sectional observations.

### 5. The record is produced within the world it purports to describe

Surveys, state archives and classifications are made by institutions, in particular languages and power arrangements, for specific interests, from selected populations. Translation, trust, censorship, sampling, questionnaire branching, nonresponse, missing instruments and fieldwork timing shape what was measurable. The unmeasured is not therefore nonexistent. A state's record of 'obedience,' 'religion' or 'ethnicity' need not coincide with lived meaning or subaltern categories.

**Derived inference:** preserve source provenance, edition, population, instrument, respondent/occurrence and typed nonanswer because they constitute what can be known—not merely database hygiene. Every interpretation must remain answerable to what the instrument *actually captured* and to those it excluded. One cannot resolve colonial classification problems by attaching a generic 'colonial trauma' score to the output.

### 6. Evidence, explanation, personhood and linguistic expression are different levels

Exact observed covariation is not identified causation; an explanatory hypothesis is not a fact; an objective material condition is not personal knowledge; an immediate response is not durable formation; acquired disposition is not conscious memory; a sincere first-person retrospective report is not an infallible record of the mechanisms that produced an action.

**Therefore:** separate `OBSERVED_EMPIRICAL`, `EXPLICIT_EXPERIMENTAL_MODEL`, `STRONG_CAUSAL_STRUCTURAL`, objective-world eligibility, actor-local perception/choice and later narration. A source-graded lower claim should be allowed to work while an unsupported upper claim is refused. PYAT must never counterfeit original35 U/E_pre, sampling, QSM or scientific authority; Sorok cannot infer a generated actor's motive merely from PYAT's group average. Equally, no scientist's hold on causal issuance should delete an independently valid RAW observation.

### 7. The project's earliest psychometric architecture is itself an object of critique

The original documents declare psychic types 'not fixed essences,' but then assign Enneagram probability via country-level cultural and colonial driver weights. The cross-cultural model says 'health is not morality' while proposing `HealthScore = -SSI + Harmony + Development`. A person aligned with oppressive institutions could score consonant; a resisting person facing retaliation could score inconsonant. Neither mapping is an independent determination of human flourishing.

Rawlsing v3.4 proposes useful *questions* about childhood symbols, contradictory selves, permeability and life change, but its numerical DRTS/TT/RMT rules risk treating defiance/trauma/isolation as psychopathy or 'madness' without demonstrated calibration. The supplied driver schema contains **40** keys but the provided Enneagram weight table weights **22**; even intended coverage and validity require clarification. These are not mere stylistic blemishes. They expose how a machine built to resist psychological essentialism can **reintroduce essentialism through its coefficients**.

**Derived imperative:** conserve these earlier design attempts as historical candidates, not as irrevocable laws or silently repudiated research. Either validate a specified use against appropriately sampled individual-level evidence and mediating processes, relabel it explicitly as speculative creative conditioning, or keep it scientifically unissued. Novel successful code does not settle that theory.

### 9. The world must remain usable without becoming owned by its model

PYAT should provide a scientifically qualified material world of constraints, dependencies, inequalities and empirically observed relations; Sorok separately represents an actor's historically contingent living, practical interpretations and relations. A socially conditioned generated person is not a statistically observed individual, and a moving LLM narrative is not proof of an autonomous internal psychological history. No person can be reduced to the model's grasp of their social conditions; no source permits a model to claim more than it can have witnessed.

The undertaking succeeds not by explaining away the Other, but by making genuinely consequential **situated encounters** possible without erasing the distinction between the world, a social position, a particular life and a user's interpretation. ## III. A proposed analytic compression: historical reality, mediation, evidence, use

**This is a conceptual extrapolation, not code or a native causal graph.**

`historically formed relations (property, labor, care, institutions, coercion, ecology)`
→ `unequally distributed conditions and capacities of possible action`
↔ `particular participation, practical activity, confrontation and care`
↔ `plural learned tendencies, interpretations, loyalties and contradictions`
→ `contextually attempted/realized activity, including refusal or none`
→ `outcomes that reproduce, damage, or transform those relations`.

**Observed survey or historical data are partial measurements produced under a separate instrument/selection/power process, not transparent access to every arrow.** This mediating chain is a set of distinctions an agent must preserve; it is **not** a mandate to build an additional PYAT behavior engine or to announce settled psychological science. That difference is the safeguard against both structural determinism and individualist voluntarism.

## IV. How the mother derives decisions without becoming a checklist

- **A high cultural 'harmony' score is used to call resistance unhealthy.** Reject the inferential step: norm-consonance and human flourishing differ. Preserve the observed sociological measure if valid, but relabel the psychological health inference `UNISSUED` until genuinely warranted.
- **A national poverty/culture coefficient is used to decide a fictional person's childhood trauma.** Require actual or explicitly simulated mediating household, institutional and event history. Without it, this is narrative invention, not source-based psychology.
- **A minority subgroup contradicts the country average.** Preserve the actual denominator, routing and subpopulation; do not call it noise. An unconditional national personality conclusion is thereby falsified, not merely made less confident.
- **An all-green I02 selector certificate has no event bodies for saved cold reconstruction.** Keep the complete logical proof, admit the physical gap, connect one qualified body-preserving source stream to one complete persistent estate. Never re-open source to build a redundant second authority corpus.
- **A valid F4 triple is replaced by the product of F2 pairs.** Refuse it: the joint distribution is a different empirical object; external mathematical plausibility cannot replace same-respondent evidence.
- **A sourcefree scientific product passes real G1–G5 while original35 rights are held.** Accept its honest sourcefree functionality and stop author cycling; hold exactly the unissued claim. Only an actual first consumer justifies further engineering, not desire for a grander theory.
- **A new theoretical model is proposed because the present simulation is not 'rich enough.'** Identify a real violated constitutive relation/consumer and source justification. If only additional expressive fidelity, bank it without preventing the actual S01–S05 machine from working.
