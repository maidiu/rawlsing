Statistically distinct tasks and estimands:
- Descriptive survey conditional `n(Y=y,C=c)/n(C=c)`, exactly from one eligible same-respondent risk set, *sample* probability if RAW.
- Design-weighted population conditional with denominator `Σ_{r∈eligible,C} w_r`, numerator `Σw_r 1[Y=y]`, plus design-based uncertainty and target/routing weights. - Predictive/modelled distribution for *unseen* or sparse configurations: hierarchical smoothing or partial pooling is a separate model intervention with calibrated uncertainty and exclusions; may never be presented as observed fact.
- Reverse `P(D|A)` is another **statistical conditional**. Can be computed from the same valid joint with a changed denominator if authorized, or using Bayes only with compatible measured priors and risk universe. It does NOT imply attitudes *cause* demography.
- `P(A,B,C|D)` requires actual higher-order respondent joint incidence or a separately disclosed model; all the `P(A_i|D)` marginals cannot determine its true joint.
- `P(coparent demographics, attitudes | mother, encounter)` requires **dyadic selection/contact/partner model and actor causal life**. Same-person response co-travel is not partnership probability. What is known about sex/reproduction, parenthood, class and meeting networks changes by place/time and needs source/use owners.
