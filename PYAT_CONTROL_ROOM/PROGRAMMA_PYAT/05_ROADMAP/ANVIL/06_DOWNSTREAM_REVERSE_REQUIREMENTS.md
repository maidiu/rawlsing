## 0. Start from the *terminal* thing, not the database

The ultimate enterprise is a credible, historically situated **objective/social world and developing person**. The source-to-person pipeline must answer the protected PROGRAMMA's product questions: **what is possible, facilitated, inhibited or repressed, by which forces; in contradiction with what other tendencies; and under what historical conditions might these relations change?**

A respondent distribution is only partial input into that question. It **does not** disclose automatically what any person wants, who owns land/capital, which institutions compel conduct, how two people meet, who forms a relationship, what causal influence operates, or how an actor develops over subsequent real events. Those require independent materially grounded positions, world constraints, interaction/activity and relevant science/permissions. Data-conditioned possibility is not demographic determinism.

**Full reverse chain (source of requirements, not accepted execution status):**
`source custody+science+rights → U/E_pre→ complete source/13-family C11 → I03/I05/Batch + F1–F4 → S04 inferential/conditional/multi-source/time → Field Forge FF01 + separate FF02/N6 → N9 current/as-of objective world + N10E empirical possibility → N10W situated possibility → PRE-V3 objective social/material position → separately authorized SOROK V3 person/actor → multiple genuine activity/world events and continuing-person read → N11/N12 acceptance and handoff`.

This chain must preserve two **nonidentical** independent success criteria: (a) true statistical/material evidence and (b) honest living-person/actor development over time. Neither success can be manufactured by the other.

## 2. Why “all later consumer requirements” cannot be inferred from current code alone

The current `pre553_analytical_composition.py` already has direct scalar, higher-order/joint/recursive paths; `pre553_empirical_possibility_provider.py` delegates geometry to accepted composition and carries external determinants; `pre553_universal_empirical_extension.py` extends I05/recursive consumption; `pre553_learned_world_composition.py` supports native learned-graph consumption. **Those existing objects are capital, not evidence every intended downstream producer and recipient actually runs.**

If we design the substrate solely around existing `_eval_predicate` and `EmpiricalFact`, we risk excluding:
- information-theoretic indicators, selected/secondary gain and recursive candidate search;
- source-qualified signed and neutral material, coverage vs score, F03/F08 specificity;
- separate-sample partial bounds and source-level uncertainties;
- cross-wave sparse time and population reweighting;
- downstream counter-possibilities and conditional contradictions;
- actual graph ancestry, revision and replay when observed evidence or world state changes;
- learning/lifecycle products that must remain current/as-of across reopens.

**Conversely**, we must not “solve” missing source/structure science by making an unrestricted general-purpose inference engine. A computational substrate can represent an operation without authorizing its scientific conclusion.

## 3. Full statistical detail a typical query engine loses

**Population distinctions:** admitted universe; intended frame; respondent selected; eligible; asked; answered; observed substantive; unknown/missing/refused; explicit inapplicable; outcome cell; subgroup/branch; composite/same-person overlap; external-survey counterpopulation. Unweighted/weighted denominators and design bases are separate. For V550J a pattern-member basis, answered-variable basis, pattern×variable overlap and eligible baseline are **different** quantities.

**Directional inference:** exact `P(attitude|position)` and `P(position|attitude)`, possibly asymmetric and differently selected; subset/condition/reversal in a genuinely compatible universe only.

**High-order structures:** 3+ respondent-coherent joint patterns, repeated/ordered/ranked/multiselect response topology, covariance/co-travel that cannot be inferred from marginals, sparse minority and contradictions, recursive branch path, full vs truncated candidate sets.

**Time and space:** multiple countries and waves, independently sampled people, temporary household/survey designs, geopolitical and institutional context, period comparability, true as-of point, changes in instruments, valid sources/permissions, no fictitious longitudinal person when only repeated cross-sections exist.

**Meaning and power:** a response's political/psychological meaning is conditioned by question wording, cultural/linguistic context, source classifications and separate scientific rules; a correlation cannot itself give causal class/hegemony force. Objectively situated persons need labor, ownership, dependence, institutions and material contradictions **from legitimate evidence**, not personality labels or statistical conjuring.

**Lifecycle:** persisted derived products and their exact dependency graphs must be replayable/restorable/revocable; an old historical observation can still be cited as history while CURRENT selection of operative product changes.

## 4. What is conditional, not automatically mandatory

- F6 multi-survey bounds are conditional on actual selected downstream demand and independent source/estimand qualification.
- F7 measured change is conditional on comparable original waves and selected consumer.
- Signed structural interpretation needs role/position/power scientific issuance; descriptive FF01 must still work if absent.

- A person/couple/parent project requires independent encounter/activity/partner-selection evidence and SOROK owner authorization, not automatic generation from joint attitude data.
