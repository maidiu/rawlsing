## F. Expected final evaluation form (NOT populated yet)

The concluding analysis must assess **all** options at whole-machine scope on:
- scientific completeness and no-drop function envelope;
- source/right/authority fidelity and independent proof;
- analytic expressiveness, compositionality, conditional/joint/recursive/temporal capability;
- cold and repeated amortized CPU/wall/IO/scratch/RSS under realistic width;
- output/evidence cost and restart/replay/invalidation;
- compatibility with accepted current engine and owners, implementation risk/time;
- future extension without a new canonical corpus or unauthorized science;
- first-consumer trace from source to meaningful FF/N9/N10W/SOROK products.

Do not mechanically assign unjustified numeric scores. Predict likely outcome from code/dataflow complexity and consumer requirements; explicitly mark cases where only one finite test would change a decision. **Select ONE coherent system and retire the weak architectural alternatives** when the evidence suffices. A design may combine sound elements from alternatives but must not produce multiple indefinite production implementations.

## G. Questions to put to each alternative

1. What crucial downstream information could this layout never recover after source projection?
2. Where does source-native identity actually become a canonical event, and where may duplication occur?
3. Which 13-family component must be reread/reconstituted for a genuine query and can that cost be amortized?
4. How does one authorized operation create reusable work for a *different* operation with different denominator?
5. How does a recursive/3-way/weighted/temporal first reader obtain needed exact evidence efficiently?
6. Where exactly does observed description stop and owner-issued structural authority begin?
7. Can this design provide timely correct live positives and negatives after source/permission change?
8. What lower-bound output obligations remain even with perfect computation?
9. What is the memory/disk/CPU risk in the worst realistic width and complexity?
10. Which already accepted code can be reused, and which proof/owner interface actually must change?
11. What is the smallest test capable of *falsifying* the whole design rather than affirming one small happy path?
12. Have we accounted for work that hasn't yet been built but is already demanded by historical or future consumers?

**Default action now:** interrogate these factors and downstream source/consumer graphs with the user. **No candidate is yet an adopted solution**; don't issue a build team based on this ledger alone.
