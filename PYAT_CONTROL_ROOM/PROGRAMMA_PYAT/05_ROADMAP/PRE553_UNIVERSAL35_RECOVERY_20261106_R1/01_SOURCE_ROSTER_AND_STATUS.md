## Exactly what a source pack must provide to ONE generic engine

Required where applicable: `source_id` and edition identity, expected physical SHA/format, reader/encoding configuration, exact field names and ordinals, original item IDs/wording and value/option meanings, administered occurrence and multiresponse/ranked topology, respondent vs household key/row binding, country/time coordinates, missing/DK/refusal/not asked/skipped/routing eligibility and their evidence grades, question-role/semantic relationships, actual source-specific restrictions when operative, and **optional** weights/design/estimands. The engine must not infer unknown administration from physical missingness, or replace missing weights with fake population weighting. RAW observed-sample calculations can proceed on known supported evidence.

External original source files are supplied at runtime through the already-established source-read/physical-decoder owners. Original files do not belong in Git. A local runner missing a physical file emits `SOURCE_NOT_PRESENT_HERE`/typed hold for that exact source; it must not transform all35 into “unavailable” or assume every source is inaccessible. A held mapping blocks only its dependent operations, not all unrelated same-survey items.

**Prohibited acceptance shortcut:** Running `fixture-call SRC04`, a two-item demo or a “one survey first” experiment does **not** fulfill the 35-source production architecture. Tests may isolate failures, but the user-selected deliverable requires a **35/35 per-source registry walk and automatic operation accounting**, including nondefault topologies, observed original-source execution wherever available, exact held/unknown and source-not-present rows, and generically produced outputs.
