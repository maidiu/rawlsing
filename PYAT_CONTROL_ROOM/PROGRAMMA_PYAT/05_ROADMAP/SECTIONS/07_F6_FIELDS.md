## 1. What F6 must actually do

A served objective world may require facts from multiple independently sampled survey universes. F6 must compose **source-indexed structural field contributions** for a named target population/use, not pretend the same respondent answered both surveys. The scientifically admissible outcomes include: (a) a warranted nonpooled, uncertainty-qualified composition; (b) a **partially identified** target, retaining excluded/unknown mass and intervals; or (c) a typed incompatibility/authority refusal that leaves separately valid source products intact. Only an authorized joint design/operation could authorize an actual respondent-level joint; parallel marginals never do so by default.

**Source/plan scope:** [N7 C6 declared product and owner](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9809203fd915a84bae056a67210e9213879840db/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/ASTRA_PYAT_INTEGRATED_DEVELOPMENT_ATLAS_R1/nodes/N7.md) (blob `75ada5f2f187a76f45d3913a2dc927df96eaa578`), [global F6/F7/F8 scope matrix](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9809203fd915a84bae056a67210e9213879840db/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/WHOLE_MACHINE_COMPLETION_R1/GLOBAL_F6_F7_F8_RELATION_SCOPE_MATRIX.json) (blob `8870545724d2b3353e4cb4125bc06c041d463954`), [G2 plan](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a6f10443811c5f1a5c8ffed3f2dab16e0f8054d/PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/PROGRAMMA_PYAT/05_ROADMAP/SECTIONS/07_F6_FIELDS.md). **36 cross-family pairings are candidate applicability surfaces, not 36 owner approvals, runnable joins, or an obligation to synthesize all pairs.** Plan membership: N7/F6/EE13, Whole101 F07–F09 and Whole151 F-class; these are separate identity vocabularies, not aliases or code proof.

### Selected source-to-product contract (for an eventual implementation)

| Layer | Minimal exact material; refusal rule |
|---|---|
| Two or more source inputs | Immutable source-product IDs, source family/wave, source root and derivative lineage, original respondent unit/sample design, original weighting/variance design and measured construct/category/route definitions; **do not join respondent IDs across independent source families**. |
| Target/use and bridge | Target population, frame/geography/boundary, period, supported strata, target-share denominators, construct crosswalk, permitted transport/standardization, explicit authority snapshots. Source label coincidence is not a bridge. |
| Applicability and exclusion | For every source × target stratum preserve applicability, nonduplication/unknown overlap, source-specific refusals, excluded source weights, target coverage and missing mass. Unknown overlap does not imply disjointness. |
| Computation | Retain original per-source local distributions; a candidate derived field must declare whether it is a source-specific bundle, nonpooled synthesis, partial bounds or other owner-admitted estimand, plus assumptions and uncertainty. No covered-only renormalization to total population. |
| Output to N9, if demanded | Versioned `composed_field_product` with covered subprobability, **uncovered_target_mass**, any valid lower/upper bounds, method limitations, input/authority refs and as-of scope; or a typed refusal. An N9 reader must actually import and use it, never merely appear in a plan. |

**Negative and evidence discipline:** If a stratum covers fraction `c < 1`, retain at least `1-c` unknown target mass; a covered subprobability is **not** a total-population distribution. F6M's historical `distribution: null` when `uncovered_target_mass > 0` is useful non-loss behavior. Its sensitivity envelopes expressly are not confidence intervals; its variance handling can lack covariance and conditional uncertainty. Do not copy that experimental statistical choice into source-native law without the original science owner. Historic S03FO A is **rejected at that original variant scope**; later code is not automatically RED.
