## 3. What “manageable demographic resolution” needs to mean in code

We do **not** declare a magic maximum of 3 conditions, top 2 answers, 30 subjects, 100 queries or a universal “best predictor” percentage. Those are historically useful implementation heuristics or agent proposals, none equivalent to a calibrated scientific+resource decision.

**Eligible configuration:** source/wave/source-defined target population, selected demographic/source-measured predictors, selected answer target, exact eligible/asked/observed overlap, respondent/source scope, permitted weighting, plus optional independently qualified world condition at the appropriate service. Its validity is **not** a list of individual fictional personas.

**Minimal useful analytical step:** given already prepared population D and question Y, compare eligible observed splitters X by valid within-D association or predictive discrimination, retaining baseline distribution, eligible n/weighted support, uncertainty, missing/unknown and respondent-coherent multivariate evidence if the actual question demands it. It must also show whether stronger conditions expose alternatives, make a category sparse, or force a walkback.

**Search without combinatorial bankruptcy:** reuse prepared membership/risk sets and a narrow set of genuinely repeated source facts; rank *candidate inquiries* by plausible empirical value and cost without allowing ranking to mutate observed truth; issue an honest finite exact I05 frontier where required or explicitly graded adaptive/budgeted discovery otherwise. Retain **unsearched** versus **searched-and-weak/zero**. Do not materialize Cartesian combinations, all pairings, all survey waves × all answer options × all demographic vectors or individual templates.

**Open-ended conditional depth is not unlimited computation:** preserve the ability to ask a *further* measured question later, but permit the engine to answer “insufficient support at this grain” and offer the next useful broader context. No SOROK-induced request may manufacture an empirically observed joint across different surveys. Support thresholds, maximum active arity, retention policy and scientific estimate quality must follow actual source distributions and task cost, not a frozen number pulled out of a historic prototype.

**Practical success shape:** a real meaningful country/wave source, one question with multiple real answer options, at least two eligible candidate predictors, a conditional ranking, a narrower supported/minority contrast, an authentic same-respondent co-occurrence where source provides it, and an honest underpowered/unknown negative. That is a proposed **scientifically discriminating first witness**, not an arbitrarily multiplying test matrix.

## 6. Deliberate adversarial challenges to the positive mulch

**Challenge A: determinicity is not predictive fate.** High within-D information gain might be unstable, survey-administered, social-position-correlated or dominated by a rare answer. It is an empirical splitting property, not the cause of a belief. Also, a globally strong splitter may provide almost no incremental value inside the already conditioned D; conversely a globally weak splitter can matter in a well-defined subgroup. Rank *incremental within-D* gains and retain uncertainty; do not turn a historical label into doctrine.

**Challenge A2: the legacy determinicity score can be mathematically wrong under missing predictor values.** In historical V550 `indicator_summary_rows` and `branch_nodes_rows`, baseline entropy can be calculated over all target-response rows while conditional entropy is calculated only where candidate X is observed. If X-observation is correlated with Y, the resulting claimed information gain compares two different cohorts. A finite, simple falsifier: keep Y fixed and X **constant among all records where X exists**, alter only Y among X-missing records; the within-X-predictor discrimination of Y in the observed-X cohort must remain zero, rather than rising due to the changed missing-X group. Under a separately justified policy, missingness itself can be analyzed as its own predictive state, but must be labeled as such. **First test the current I03/modern scoring implementation against this case before resurrecting or porting V550 code.** Weighted and unweighted diagnostics must likewise keep their estimand and sample definitions aligned.

**Challenge B: the objective is not “calculate everything.”** Recovery of a historically lost *ability to ask a question* does not require precomputing all combinations. Never conflate source-preserving capacity, conditional compute-on-demand and eager output multiplication. The original35 estate and respondent-native C11 are the reusable source, not a preordained Cartesian analysis bank.

**Challenge C: semantics before pretty rankings.** A statistically “best” branch is false if it compares respondents who were not all asked the target question, ignores multiple-selection topology, collapses missing into no, weights a nonrepresentative design as a population, or treats changing wording across waves as identical. These are *correctness faults*, not costly scientific luxuries.

**Challenge D: neither under- nor over-validate.** Typed negative states are valuable only if eligible positive computations also work. Conversely two green synthetic outputs, an unissued original query, and 26 metadata admissions cannot certify original-world scientific adequacy. The shortest *honest positive witness* outranks a larger new refusal taxonomy.

**Challenge E: stop at the appropriate jurisdiction.** PYAT makes demographic configurations and their observed variability available at useful resolution. The source-qualified N9/N10W interface and qualified conditional data may support SOROK's later selection. Do **not** use a “sampling capability compiler” as a pretext for constructing a new personal psychology, synthetic couple database or additional mandatory ABI.

**Challenge F: some supposedly binding numerical targets were never user-selected.** Unless a quantitative rule is truly constrained by the physical machine, independent science or explicit user adoption, **the number is a proposed measurement**, not a command. Reopen it when it would delay building without changing a defensible decision.
