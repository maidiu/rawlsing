## 0. THE DISASSEMBLY: WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO BE ONE MACHINE?

The inherited fragments initially appear to disagree about what the primary thing is: an answer distribution; a recursive social branch; same-person attitude co-travel; a source-valid proposition; a material position; a pressure-bearing social field; an institutional carrier; a hypothetical adult; an embodied child; a layered psyche; a historical life; a world changing in response to actions; or a model able to inquire and learn. Taking any one as the primitive and translating the rest into its vocabulary mutilates the system.

The old vector/Enneagram route starts with a statistical or cultural environment, pushes estimated weights into pre-named strategies, and asks an LLM to speak through a resulting personality. It offers a useful historical insistence on **context, cultural discordance, and dynamic developmental change**, but its causal shortcuts do not establish that a demographic coordinate *produces* an inner type. The older nine-strata doctrine grasped different pressures and mediations, but its fixed number of strata could not be allowed to decide in advance what social formations exist. The V550 branch and cloud designs recovered empirical differentiation, but their sociology remains incomplete if relations are reduced to predictor strength. Field Forge brought actor-independent relational truth, but cannot replace the upstream discovery of respondents' actual configurations. The psychological materials reject the adult vector as a person and show that conduct, consciousness, motive and meaning are historically formed in activity with Others. The conservation campaigns demonstrate that an apparently working pipeline can lose meaning, authority roles, minority branches or downstream access while retaining the same bytes. The more recent identification work shows that a lawful unknown can itself contain mathematically necessary information.

These are not detachable features. Their collision requires a distinct account of **what exists**, **what can be observed about it**, **what operations evidence permits**, **how material worlds generate and constrain options**, **how a particular life takes up or changes them**, and **how consequences alter the next conditions of life**. The exact place where the machine must refuse a transformation is as important as the place where it must make one.

The deeper **spine emerging from the recovered material** is **historically organized, object-directed activity as the mediating substance of both social reproduction and personal becoming**, with irreducible evidentiary and ontological owners for each level. Societies reproduce and change their material arrangements through labor, care, institutions and collective practice. Surveys are radically partial records of what situated people say, judge or report doing in historical observation encounters. An individual's psyche is neither a direct reflection of those conditions nor an uncaused interior: it is acquired and reorganized through an embodied person's participation in, interpretation of and lived consequences from plural activities. A later encounter recruits that acquired past under changed conditions. The process that makes psychology *operative* is therefore also among the processes through which psychology *becomes different*.

This does not appoint activity theory as a universally demonstrated psychological law or collapse every real thing into an all-purpose 'activity' variable. Source truth, institutional truth, actor experience and fictional world history have different warrants. But the source families converge on a decisive construction problem: **what happens to the several historically constituted activities a person is trying to sustain when they encounter this particular world, and how does the lived consequence alter what can be recruited next?** That question integrates plural dispositions, motives, material possibilities, Others and developmental change without making demographic position into personality.

What crosses owner boundaries is not a deterministic identity. It is qualified possibilities for situated activities, concrete affordances and inhibitions, independent worldly events, and histories of what participants actually do and live through. What returns is a changed world, changed organization of ongoing activities, reconstituted psychic iceberg and new scientifically qualified questions—never a fictional result promoted into source observation.

No one of these can be dismissed as a downstream decoration. Their differences must be represented and enforced, not affirmed in prose and then erased in code.

## I. MATERIAL LIFE BEFORE A PERSONALITY: WHAT IS BEING MODELED?

### I.1 Persons are neither demographic exemplars nor self-originating interiors

An actual person's conditions begin before their concepts of them: birth into relations of dependence, access to food and shelter, a household and caregivers, the distributions of labor and property, geographic position, schooling and work, language and law, violence and protection, social classifications imposed by Others, and the existing histories of those institutions. These conditions do not produce a one-to-one mental reflection. They structure exposure, needs, conflict, the value of resources, enforceable choices, and the historical materials from which the person can organize a life.

The **material relation** is primary: employee/employer, tenant/landlord, debtor/creditor, carer/dependent, citizen/state, prisoner/jailer, pupil/teacher, kin/kin, colonial authority/colonized subject, migrant/border apparatus, worker/household, institution/community. A demographic label may proxy a relation but cannot define it. Class is not income quintile. Access to education is not control over one's labor. Prestige is not political power. Religion-as-observed is not a ready-made motive. Position is multi-scale, field-relative and internally contradictory: a person can have authority at home, precarity at work, subordination before a state, and resources enabling resistance in another field.

**Social reproduction is causal infrastructure, not background scenery.** Food, housing, caregiving, maintenance, rest, education, health, intimacy, and the organization of labor power require activity, time, resources and institutions. Who performs this work and who is relieved of it affects which trajectories are sustainable. Reproduction includes its own conflicts, appropriations, exclusions, uneven burdens and possibilities of collective reorganization. It is never only a set of consumption values attached to an income percentile.

The historical Permeability Index and Defiance/Revolutionary Tendencies System likewise named genuine problems: differential exposure to outside symbolic systems, defensive or creative uptake, dissent, repression, accommodation and reorganization. Their historical hand-set scales (like the legacy outlier/health coefficients) are **not** scientific inference rules. The reconstituted answer is relational: who actually encountered which carriers and alternative practices, what material conditions enabled or prevented their use, and how those lived encounters altered available activity, recruitment and future struggle.

The legacy cultural-vector model had a valid question—how is an individual differently situated within historically patterned meanings?—but an inadequate answer when it averaged national cultural dimensions and converted them to preselected types. Preserve the *question about historically situated meanings and contradiction*, not the authority of unvalidated weights or personality-type coefficients.

### I.2 Social structure, actual power, pressure and flow are irreducible

**Structure** is the historically sustained organization of positions, rights, dependencies, material production/reproduction, and relations. **Power** is differential capacity to determine the feasible actions and consequences of oneself and Others: to exclude, make costly, coerce, protect, enable, resource, authorize, sanction or redirect. **Pressure** is a specific field-conditioned tendency that alters relative attraction, risk or cost among alternatives, not a moralized scalar force. **Flow** is the activity and transmission through which social conditions are reproduced, resisted, translated or transformed.

Do not call a majority view hegemony without explaining institutions and power. Do not turn a conditional association into coercion. Do not infer that a person with a contradictory occupation automatically recognizes the contradiction. Do not infer that an opportunity is realized merely because it was formally available. Distinguish the steps:

**structural position → exposure/opportunity → encounter → recognition or non-recognition → interpretation → acquired orientation/motive → situational recruitment → activity/expression → consequence**.

The arrows are conditional and revisable; none is a compulsory psychological law. A person may be constrained by a relation they cannot name, describe an institution they do not accept, or use the values transmitted by a dominant institution to criticize that institution.

### I.3 Reproduction does not imply social stasis

Institutions actively **transform** the meanings they transmit. A school does not merely pass on an ideology; it grades, allocates credentials, regulates bodies, disciplines time, offers solidarities, teaches dissent and closes alternatives. Work reproduces skills and wages but also fatigue, conflict, technical knowledge, collaboration and resistance. Care reproduces life but can redistribute power, loyalty and vulnerability. Religious communities can transmit obedience, solidarity, sanction, ethical rupture and incompatible frameworks. States and borders arrange entitlements and risks, while the people governed can evade, contest and reorganize these arrangements.

Thus carriers must be modeled as relations among institutions, groups, practices, material resources, sanctions, interpretive frameworks and active participants; **not parcels containing one ideological message**. The old 'pressure vector' is insufficient if it never knows which activity, which bearer, which institution and which alternative produced the effect.

### I.4 Others and institutions are independent realities

Each significant Other has their own needs, position, situated constraints, memory, priorities and power to refuse. A parent is not a psychological stimulus delivered to Nadia on demand; a sibling, manager, landlord, teacher, friend or state official is not an obedient extension of the focal actor. Relationships have histories and changing asymmetries. Institutions operate through rules, calendars, material stocks, personnel, enforcement practices and uncertainty. Even an apparently ordinary day requires multiple simultaneous processes outside the actor's awareness.

The simulator need not compute the entire population at equal detail. It must maintain **enough independent world state and other-agent agency** that the focal actor can be surprised, disappointed, blocked, supported, injured, mistaken, helped and transformed by events not manufactured merely to confirm a predetermined adult destiny.

### I.5 Historical beings do not start with finished consciousness

Infancy has embodied needs, capacities and dependence; it does not contain a latent adult ideological scale merely waiting for its birthday. What the organism can recognize, attend to, remember, feel, desire, interpret or perform emerges through maturation, language, repeated social practice, relationships, work and conflict. Psychological constructs differ in age of availability; learning is not fixed by age gates alone. The user-supplied Leontiev, Luria, Sève, Lahire, Holzkamp, Dreier, Archer, Elder, Mortimer and social-reproduction works are theoretical resources for this problem, not an excuse to treat their distinct approaches as harmonized scientific proof.

**Leontiev's distinction** among activity, goal-directed action and condition-bound operations prevents collapsing a motive into an isolated surface act; the same apparent gesture can organize different activities. **Lahire's plural actor** counters the unified disposition inferred from a single situation: heterogeneous contexts may recruit acquired, partially incompatible schemes. **Dreier's conduct of everyday life** demands a person across multiple places and practices rather than a psychotherapy- or scene-local self. **Elder and Mortimer** press on historical timing, social pathways, contingent turning points and institutional transitions. **Social-reproduction theory** asks who makes this daily and generational psychological life materially possible, through which unpaid and paid labor. **Critical psychology** raises the person's reasons for acting under concrete conditions rather than making social constraint a direct psychic input. **Archer's internal conversation** is one theoretical resource for reflexive deliberation, not a universal verbal mind. **Sève's historicized personality** asks for the social division of activity, time and real life rather than an isolated inventory of traits.

These approaches disagree in important respects. The system need not prematurely reconcile their total philosophies. It must recover the **non-optional modeling questions** each exposes, use competing architectures where live evidence can distinguish them, and make unproved psychological hypotheses explicitly provisional.

## II. THE WORLD'S EVIDENCE DOES NOT REPLACE THE WORLD

### II.1 A survey is a historically situated social encounter

The first PYAT object is not an answer cell but a **Survey Observation Event**: source/release, respondent unit, sampling frame, question implementation, actual wording and language, category and component affordances, routing, eligibility, wave/place/time, observation state, authorized use, and original provenance. A response may be a stated belief, reported conduct, hypothetical intention, judgment about another, perception of social prevalence, experienced event, institutional fact or refusal. It is not unmediated consciousness.

An eligible respondent who leaves a question unanswered, an inapplicable respondent, a person never sampled, a person who was not offered that wording, and a respondent who answered a legitimate 'none' are five different objects. Preserve them. A coded zero may be observed absence, a negative answer, or a mechanical placeholder; no universal default can decide.

**Privacy and respondent non-extraction are substantive constraints.** Preserve eligible respondent identity internally where authorized for valid joints and replay, but do not expose real survey individuals as reusable fictional personalities. Small or identifiable intersections require source-specific disclosure controls, aggregation or refusal; 'interesting rare minority' is not permission to reidentify survey respondents or reveal sensitive response combinations. Use exact source permission and privacy rules rather than assuming anonymity from a locally stripped identifier. A fictional adult is a historically conditioned construction, never a real respondent reproduced with a new name.

The estate's finite source budget makes the semantics particularly important. You cannot repair a missing source question by turning an LLM's plausible sociological interpretation into data. The right reaction is to ask **what is positively recoverable, what else is constrained, what remains unidentified, and which available measurement could distinguish alternatives**.

### II.2 Identity, meaning and permission travel together but never collapse

Retain distinct:
- source-native question/option/component identity and respondent/risk-set identity;
- source-to-concept mapping and any *separately adjudicated* semantic equivalence across source versions;
- valid measurement direction and response topology, including multiple selections and non-scalar ordered component vectors;
- source admissibility and original35 native use permission;
- estimand, design, weight, variance and nonresponse method authority;
- derived proposition/interpretation status, structural-use license and actor-use boundary;
- immutable authority history, current selection, invalidation and rollback.

The same phrase in two questionnaires does not create a portable measure. P3C semantic equivalence cannot confer respondent-join, cross-population transport, weight or causal rights. The valid denominator must be an executable or exact-source warranted eligibility predicate. A logical V550J composition without positive executable scope is not enough. Do not let a missing special S14 semantic-relation permission erase otherwise valid empirical co-travel; interpretation and occurrence have different rights.

Non-scalar component values may become a single projected value **only** under complete exact component matching and unanimous nonempty projected value. If any required component is absent, contradictory, empty or role-incompatible, preserve the component vector and its status. V551 canonical signed projection has one governed owner. V552-style pressure is a separately qualified structural transformation, never a synonym for count, support, correlation, confidence, packet score or respondent frequency.

### II.3 The observation field has positive science even when answers are missing

For a known eligible finite record set N with observed yes Y and eligible unresolved M on a defined binary completed-response outcome, the share over all compatible completions lies in:

\[
\frac{Y}{N} \leq p \leq \frac{Y+M}{N}.
\]

For N=100 with 36 yes, 24 no, 40 genuinely eligible unresolved, the observed-answer yes fraction is 36/60=.60; the eligible-record possibility interval is [.36,.76]. A majority of all eligible cases **is unidentified**, not negative. With 81 yes, 9 no and 10 unresolved, [.81,.91] makes the finite-eligible-record majority robust. Neither interval establishes the unanswered respondents' private beliefs, a cause of silence or a population probability without additional sample and inference warrants.

Change the 40 unresolved cases into valid skip/inapplicable cases and the risk set changes: the original bound is no longer legal. Change the eligible universe without authority and even N becomes unknown. Generalize to multinomial/vector outcomes by treating the compatible completions as an admissible set rather than arbitrarily imputing a preferred completion. Keep the distinction between exact finite-sample mathematical bounds, sampling uncertainty, source coverage error, and sensitivity to nonignorable nonresponse.

The observation field is itself socially informative when reach, administrative constraints, disclosure risk or differential missingness is independently evidenced. But an apparent group difference in nonresponse does not by itself prove suppression, resistance, apathy, secrecy or trauma.

### II.4 The necessary evidence object

A useful record should carry a full multidimensional warrant rather than a global confidence number:

```text
EvidenceClaim {
  proposition, target_event, observed_unit, source_identity, exact_root,
  source_release, historical_place_time, risk_set, population_scope,
  response_grammar, component_roles, source_permission, estimand_design,
  evidence_kind, mathematical_or_statistical_method, uncertainty,
  empirical_support_and_countercases, relation_to_other_claims,
  comparison_and_transport_scope, semantic_authority, structural_warrant,
  actor_permission, search_coverage, derivation_dependencies, invalidation,
  first_consumer, next_discriminating_question
}
```

**Evidence kinds remain distinguishable:** directly observed; respondent-co-observed; estimated population relation; mathematically partially identified; model-dependent extrapolation; semantic conjecture; structurally admitted mechanism; valid empirical null; underpowered; unasked; noncomparable; blocked; integrity-failed; source-intrinsically inapplicable; producer/consumer unfinished. They may coexist on different axes of a single claim. A proposal made by an LLM is not equal to an issued semantic decision. A whole-questionnaire decode is not proof of approved original-source survey inference.

The great epistemic temptation is to make each later representation simpler by erasing qualifications. The correct architecture lets a consumer get a narrow usable object **without** having to reconstruct the qualifications from chat history or guess which upstream source it came from.

## III. DISCOVERY IS A RECURSIVE OPERATION, NOT A PREDICTOR REPORT

### III.1 One inquiry changes the next inquiry

The finite survey estate is not a finite list of possible analyses. Inference is a graph of lawful questions and propositions. A source-supported answer can become a new dependent variable, a conditioning context, a discovered exception, a warning about meaning, or a request to the social-world owner. The machine should not require the designer to pre-enumerate all combinations or nominate every sociological concept in advance.

One **Discovery Move** has eight inseparable parts:

1. **Question:** which possibility, relation, absence, contradiction, historical change or model claim would be consequential to know?
2. **Eligibility:** what exact source, population, risk set, component/semantic authority, observation and permitted inference could answer it?
3. **Operation:** which deterministic or statistically issued transformation and actual evidence does it invoke?
4. **Product:** what distribution, counterconfiguration, bound, negative, graph, difference or new social object did it find?
5. **Challenge:** which alternative explanation, conditioning factor, held eligibility, selection effect or model assumption could overturn it?
6. **Concept change:** which candidate grouping, branch, measured distinction or research category should split, merge, disappear or become more precise?
7. **New question:** which *available and lawful* discriminating observation best reduces the uncertainty that matters?
8. **Replay:** what input identities, methods, random seeds where relevant, search frontier, root/authority versions, evidence and consumer paths make the operation reproducible and invalidatable?

It can start from any point. A novel minority might generate a new response-response inquiry; a source-translation conflict can undo a previously assembled motif; a structural hypothesis can require rechecking the distribution within exposed versus unexposed eligible populations; a simulated historical change can request an as-of empirical comparison **without turning the simulation into evidence**.

The output is not just the latest probability; it is a **difference in the map of possibility**: what became known, what stopped being tenable, what remains compatible with the evidence, and why the next question is now different.

### III.2 First recover the complete shape of a response

For every lawful question and subgroup, compute the full response morphology: category options and counts, conditional and absolute frequencies, dispersion, rankings, ordinal structure, missingness/refusal/inapplicability, multi-label overlap, true zeros, sample/estimator uncertainty and any source-native component structure. A multi-answer battery can hold several true selections simultaneously. An item with no measured option is not equivalent to an observed 'none'. A diffuse distribution is positive information, not 'failed determinicity'.

The output must retain the *whole denominator and full alternative space*, even when the user asks about one selected target. A category's individual support must not erase parent distributions, or quietly redefine all alternatives as a binary yes/no.

Then ask what each response act refers to. Comparing reported institutional mistrust with actual experience of sanctions is potentially productive; calling both 'distrust' can be an error. Where an empirical concept requires combining multiple items, expose exact component identity, inclusion, order, missingness, direction and legitimacy of aggregation. The tool may output both the exact responses and an explicitly modeled new score; it must not substitute the latter for the former.

### III.3 Conditionally map, reverse and fracture the field

The original Arab Barometer recursive-discrimination experiment exposed a crucial *two-track* operation that must remain native to the generic engine. A multi-label answer set needs both **option-presence branches** (selected SES yes/no; selected religion yes/no, etc.) and **whole-response-pattern branches** (SES only; SES plus religion; religion without SES; none of those; other overlaps). These are different partitions of the same respondent evidence. The presence of SES never means exclusive SES. The system must preserve both, and avoid presenting overlap counts as a partition of all respondents.

For the first split, and then independently inside the majority, each exit, each distinct alternative and each residual, calculate branch-local conditional determinants. The node's record includes **exact predicate, eligible respondent N, weighted denominator only if issued, observed answer configuration, overlap and missing states, competing splitter hypotheses, uncertainty, shape, stability and stop reason**. A tiny Lebanese no-formal-education residual in an early exploratory chat was explicitly unstable; numbers from that historical chat are *hypothesis provenance*, not qualified new survey facts. Fixed cutoff rules proposed in that discussion are likewise provisional—use validated thresholds and decision-sensitive uncertainty rather than pretending 'N=50' is a universal sociological truth.

**A 'none of these' category is particularly vulnerable to theft of meaning.** It may represent genuine denial of the listed bases, unmeasured local categories, a failure of the question grammar, an answer strategy, withholding or a routing/method artifact. The code records the chosen option faithfully; the source does not thereby tell us which of these social interpretations is correct. A concentration of 'none' may generate a strong research question, not automatic evidence of false consciousness, depoliticization or fear of speaking. Neither 'none' nor multiple selections may be treated as disposable residual noise.

For a target Y, and eligible contextual conditions X:

- Compare \(P(Y)\) with \(P(Y\mid X=x)\) across all meaningful X and the permitted joint conditions, reporting absolute support, magnitude, uncertainty, multiplicity and coverage.
- Ask the inverse \(P(X=x\mid Y)\) on the **same actual joint and appropriate base rates**, not by inverting a predictor ranking. A large likelihood of an attitude within a rare position does not make that position the dominant social location of the attitude.
- Distinguish a broadly widespread tendency, a near-deterministic local relation, a highly concentrated but tiny minority, and a practically weak effect. Do not collapse prevalence, predictability, specificity, causality and political significance.
- Search for conditional reversals, higher-order synergies, redundancies and suppression. Some structures (including XOR-like dependence) are absent from every one-variable comparison and appear only in genuine combinations.
- **Fracture the majority** into retention, departure, distinct alternatives, ambiguous/residual and refusal; give *each* branch a fresh opportunity to find its own conditioning variables. Do not repeat the root splitter mechanically inside every subgroup.
- Follow a coherent minority all the way into its own distinct world. Discover where it is materially concentrated, what beliefs/acts co-travel with it, what its internal divisions are, and how its contextual affordances differ. Rarity is neither proof of noise nor automatic proof of importance.

A branch tree is a useful *view* of the discovered relations, not a personality tree or a mandatory physical data layout. Branches can overlap, recross, remerge, refer to several responses, and be revised after a better question or source correction. Unvisited or budget-pruned branches remain explicitly marked; their absence from the output is not a measured null.

**Determinicity is a family of questions**, not one strength slider: conditional concentration, uncertainty reduction, effect stability, context dependence, breadth of coverage, predictive performance out of sample, minority survival, and the degree of identification. A social relation can be stable in its sign but poor at individual prediction; it can be predictive and weakly interpretable; it can reverse within a different historically constituted risk set.

### III.4 Discover relations inside actual respondents

A survey's respondent-level records allow questions a table of separate marginals cannot answer. Recover genuine *co-signatures* of beliefs, stated commitments, normative attitudes, actual reported conduct, perceptions, costs and social conditions recorded within the **same respondent and applicable period**.

An **Attitude Cloud** is a qualified pattern of within-respondent co-travel and conditional exclusion. It may contain mutually reinforcing judgments, nominally opposed commitments, principle/application disjunction, differentiated treatment of target groups, aspirations that conflict with reported action, or overlapping alternatives that split by material circumstances. A cloud's dimension and role information must remain available even if a downstream interface requests a short summary.

A branch's **shape** can be a graded drift, sharp polarization, stable local majority with minority, fragmented multimodality, or collapse toward a residual response option. These are empirical descriptions of eligible responses, not a license to declare which strata the person 'sees'. A sociological account of category legibility requires interpretation, possible missing categories and world context, keeping the alternative readings alive.

An **Empirical Repertoire** is a family of supported configurations and context-conditioned variants, not proof that any respondent's deep psyche has a unified schema. A respondent can perform different acts in different fields. Repertoire coherence is not individual psychological integration; observed co-travel is not causal recruitment; surface contradiction is not automatically hypocrisy.

The engine must query across component vectors, multi-label batteries and non-scalar joints. Preserve whole-support consistency, component-specific eligibility, source-native answer order, roles, missing/blocked values and exact respondent identity. Downstream pressure calculations cannot change simply because extra joint *context* has been attached; any actual structural pressure transformation requires its own rule.

### III.5 A missing joint may have mathematical content without becoming a fictional respondent

Suppose two binary events A and B are measured in separate sources. They do not share respondent IDs. If, and only if, an independently defensible **common target population, time, semantic meaning, exposure and relevant risk set** is established, then exact marginals \(p_A\) and \(p_B\) constrain—but do not identify—their intersection:

\[
\max(0,p_A+p_B-1) \le P(A\cap B) \le \min(p_A,p_B).
\]

For .70 and .60, the sharp compatible overlap range is [.30,.60]; overlap is necessary, but its exact rate, direction of association and individual bearers are unidentified. For .45 and .40, zero overlap is compatible with the evidence. If the samples differ in eligible population or period without a qualified bridge, neither cross-source interval is lawful, regardless of how plausible the matching sounds.

A **Multi-Universe Identification Court** constructs the feasible family of joint laws under qualified marginals, legitimate partial joints, structural zeros and any *explicitly issued* further restrictions. It optimizes claims and contrasts over that set rather than picking an arbitrary synthetic coupling. With more events, the result is a constrained finite-distribution feasibility problem rather than a casual pairwise multiplication. Sampling, design, meaning, comparability and transport uncertainty form separate outer layers around any exact mathematical identified set.

This produces a **Partially Identified Repertoire Relation**, not a Respondent-Coherent Repertoire. It may show a necessary overlap, a robust lower bound, an excluded hypothesis, or a direction of association that remains ambiguous. A statistical matching or generative model can propose a *modeled* joint if its owner issues assumptions and checks; no amount of decimal precision upgrades it to observed co-travel. Field Forge may receive bounded constraints; a parent generator cannot use a chosen compatible coupling as though a historical source had observed it.

The best next query often follows from **which admissible observation would narrow the identified set most**. If no legal existing question can distinguish the couplings, the rival scenarios must remain live possibilities rather than being quietly settled by the model.

### III.6 The machine must be allowed to invent concepts, not facts

The old sociological categories are starting hypotheses: occupation, class, religion, national identity, ideology, household type, community, trust, liberalism, conservatism and so on. The survey could contain recurring forms that the questionnaire designers never named. A combination of material circumstances and response acts may appear unexpectedly, cross familiar social labels, split an allegedly homogeneous category, or expose that one named concept conflates two phenomena.

**Concept formation** takes a *source-qualified motif* and generates candidate definitions, exemplars, negatives, countercases, context restrictions, boundary uncertainties and new tests. A concept is allowed to become more abstract when genuine recurrence demands it, or more specific when a second observation splits it. It is never 'confirmed' merely because a cluster algorithm produced a label or an LLM gave an evocative interpretation.

Keep two concept identity layers: the immutable empirical object and the revisable interpretive construct. A candidate can have aliases, genealogy and competitors. If a framework is rejected, its original observed cases do not disappear. A model's rhetorical confidence is not a source warrant. Some source categories may prove structurally misleading; others may remain useful even without a new grand theory.

### III.7 Scientific learning must attack its own attractiveness

Adaptive search can discover a beautiful false relation by repeatedly testing enough possibilities. The engine must therefore preserve the searched and unsearched frontier; compare to null/permutation and negative-control constructions where suitable; assess sampling variability, design effects, sparse cell support, multiple comparisons and out-of-sample performance where relevant. A narrow interval cannot repair a wrong population or unlicensed semantic interpretation. A large association cannot authorize an institutional mechanism. A 'statistically insignificant' result cannot be conflated with no effect or absence of variation.

**Scientific success includes both identification and honest nonidentification.** An independently authorized result can be weak, absent under a declared threshold, multimodal, inconclusive, sensitive to missingness, transport-limited, or contradictory to a prior concept. These are usable products. If a source supports a positive conditional but the current pipeline always refuses because its producer was never built or an owner failed to bind it, that is not a scientific null: it is **unfinished machine work**.

### III.8 Autonomous inquiry must operate within a finite estate

The question frontier can be large, but source evidence cannot magically grow. The investigator ranks queries by expected relevance, surprise, uncertainty reduction, minority preservation, potential falsification and computational cost, with nonzero exploration budget for unfashionable and low-prevalence branches. Its policy should be replayable and auditable, not a cascade of unrecoverable language-model hunches.

Separate **finite high-stakes semantic adjudication** from **open-ended abductive research**. Strong model assistance may help adjudicators analyze wording and map bounded concepts before execution, subject to human/competent authority. During discovery, LLMs can propose competing motifs, target combinations, explanatory hypotheses and falsifiers; exact approved computation supplies the evidence. A failed conjecture should teach the inquiry controller which kind of hypothesis failed, not disappear from memory.

The engine remembers *computational work*, not just conceptual answers: exact respondent masks, qualified joint intersections, prepared cohorts, approved semantically normalized queries, canonical source and authority versions, cached node dependencies and replay roots. Build a **shared query DAG** rather than paying full cold-source scans for every follow-up. Cache equivalence does not grant authority: a stale or corrupted authority root must invalidate a plausible cached answer, and deleting all caches must leave exact reconstructible truth.

## IV. SOCIAL FORMATIONS EMERGE FROM RELATIONS; THEY DO NOT GET ASSIGNED BY A CLASSIFIER

### IV.1 Position and disposition are overlapping but different spaces

The empirical machine needs two distinguishable yet related geometries: **material/institutional position** and **measured response/repertoire**. Within declared legal comparison metrics, it can study their homologies, mismatches, concentrations, gradients, bridges, exclusions, local boundaries and cross-field analogies. No default Euclidean map of all humans is warranted. The distance metric, comparison population and meaning of each axis are part of the claim.

A material position can contain income, property, control over productive assets, supervision, credential, contractual insecurity, dependence, access to collective power, care load, legal status, geography, and institutional recognition—not as identical variables, but as empirically or structurally linked parts of relations. A response/disposition space contains actual recorded judgments and practices, not an invisible personality field.

**Homology** is a declared, testable correspondence between relational structures in different spaces or fields, not numerical similarity by magic. A position–repertoire homology requires common eligible observations or a separately authorized comparison; cross-field homology requires explicit translation of roles, scope, institutional context and historically altered meanings. Two groups sharing an attitude are not thereby the same social formation, and two statistical curves with similar shapes do not prove one underlying mechanism.

The **Motif Atlas** takes intersections across these spaces and suggests candidate social formations without naming classes based solely on cluster geometry. A pattern crossing two positions may reflect shared institutional exposure; a pattern appearing only in an occupation may reflect an organization or a questionnaire artifact; a pattern with no observed positional concentration is still a real empirical pattern. The atlas must keep these explanations open.

### IV.2 Hegemony is not frequency

Assess separately a repertoire's prevalence, reach across groups, prestige and salience, field-specific recognition, alignment with resource holders, institutional reinforcement, political coercion, organized counterforces and temporal persistence. A numerically small privileged repertoire can dominate institutional language; a large subordinated population can share a practice yet lack coercive power; a locally authoritative subhegemony can lose convertibility across class or geographic boundaries.

**Hegemony, counterhegemony and subhegemony** are therefore multidimensional structural-empirical hypotheses. They are not a ranking from a popularity score. The engine can identify empirical distribution and independently grounded power relations; strong interpretations require explicit mechanism and source rights. It must also find absent anticipated correlations: if a proposed institutional hegemony predicts a certain source-measurable relation and the adequately powered source contradicts it, that countercase must return to the structural owner.

### IV.3 Capital has value only within a relation

Money, land, credentials, networks, language, physical safety, recognized legitimacy, citizenship and time are not substitutable units on one universal 'capital' meter. Their *convertibility* depends on institutional gates and collective relationships: a qualification valued in one country may be rejected across a border; home ownership confers power under one tenancy regime and debt vulnerability under another; intimate networks may protect a household or expose it to coercion.

The field must model **what a resource allows its holder to do, to whom, under which rules and costs**. Two formally equal salaries may support very different feasible lives when one worker bears unpaid dependent care and the other owns housing or commands another's labor. Transformations of capital and institutional standing create new trajectories without rewriting the original survey observations.

### IV.4 Contradictions are concrete relations, not a universal minus sign

Keep distinct:
- contradictory responses recorded in one person;
- different reported practices under different contexts;
- conflict between formally professed ideals and material cost;
- material incompatibility between labor obligations and care needs;
- institutional conflict between law, enforcement and informal practice;
- competing collective powers and reproduction strategies;
- actor-internal ambivalence or inaccessible conflict.

A survey may support the first two. A situated-world record may support the middle three. The last requires a psychologically developing actor. A high-level social contradiction can affect people who do not consciously perceive it, while a psychologically salient dilemma may have no large-scale structural significance. The machinery must allow both.

**A contradiction is productive when it changes the search or the possible action**, not when it merely appears as a red flag. Ask what positions bear it, which alternatives it suppresses, how different groups negotiate it, who pays the material cost, whether a minority holds a workable counterpractice, and which reorganization of resources or institutions would make the conflict different.

### IV.5 Historical materialism must distinguish proof from explanation

An observed covariance between occupation and support for redistribution is not yet a demonstration that wage exploitation caused the support. A field theory may hypothesize pathways through bargaining power, socialization, workplace organization, class encounter or experienced precarity. Each pathway has distinct observable footprints and competing accounts. The system must preserve them as candidates until qualified independently.

Likewise a moral claim can be both a response act and an institutionally produced discourse; neither permits the machine to deny the respondent's agency or infer the precise path by which it was acquired. The purpose of material explanation is to locate dependence, control and changeable relations, not to replace people with a mechanistic stereotype.

The world builder should be able to ask: **what would have to change materially for this observed pattern to become easier, harder or unavailable?** Then it distinguishes a sourced counterfactual, an explicitly modeled intervention, a mere possible alternative, and an unsupported fantasy. An empirical probability is not a causal transport coefficient.

## V. FIELD FORGE IS THE WORLD'S STRUCTURAL COURT, NOT A SECOND EMPIRICAL ENGINE

### V.1 What Field Forge receives and what it constitutes

The old DDM-to-strata path correctly wanted empirical dispositions to meet the structured society, but its required nine-strata mold is historical ancestry, not controlling ontology. The adopted Field Forge R2 architecture permits **actor-independent structural relation objects** derived from qualified input evidence, with exact roles, direction, time, scope, negative states and allowed uses. It explicitly does **not** demand one gigantic universal wrapper or the automatic promotion of every observed correlation into a social-power relation.

Field Forge must see the actual DDM products the relevant lawful producer already owns: source-qualified distributions, conditional minority branches, same-respondent co-travel and joints, typed response concepts, positional contrasts, partial identification, historical comparisons, and empirical counterexamples. It may request original facts for canary inspection, but must not take over all upstream analytical producers simply because they can be accessed.

It separately receives world evidence: institutions, rules, labor and care arrangements, ownership/control, housing, sanctions, historical policy, geography, collective capacities, statuses, resources, cultural practices and the pathways by which different actors encounter these. Source-specific legal/semantic/structural authority determines which statements can be admitted. The absence of a special semantic relation owner does not delete valid empirical co-travel; it prevents only an unsupported special interpretation.

The fundamental output is **a typed, time- and field-scoped relational claim** or a precise typed refusal. It can be served directly from legitimate producers, referenced from exact roots or materialized when necessary. Its physical location is an implementation decision, not a reason to invent a second generic Field Forge compiler.

### V.2 Four different statements with four different evidentiary burdens

A useful field might contain:

1. **Empirical footprint**: among eligible employed respondents in a specified source, a response pattern differs sharply by reported job security. This is an observed or estimated source relation, not a proof of cause.
2. **Structural relation**: a particular employment regime gives one party the authority to terminate a contract, provides the other weak bargaining protection, and ties income to access to basic needs. This depends on independently issued institutional or material evidence.
3. **Pressure mechanism**: given this relation, participation in a collective action has a modeled or evidenced cost, sanction risk, buffer, or opportunity pathway. The mechanism must state actors, conditions, direction, time, scope and any assumptions; empirical footprint alone does not create it.
4. **Situated encounter**: this specific worker becomes aware of, misperceives, ignores, resists or uses the pressure during an actual encounter. The actor's history, psychology, access and present circumstances determine this; it is not Field Forge's jurisdiction.

Do not call the fourth 'structural pressure' and infer it from the first. A field can contain a real pressure without the subject understanding it; a subject can strongly feel a pressure that is misperceived or exaggerated. Both matter in a simulation that takes persons seriously.

### V.3 Construct the relational world at multiple scales

The constitutive field includes spatial and temporal grains: household, workplace, village, city, border region, state, transnational market, institutions and social networks. The scales overlap and can conflict. A local subhegemony may be subordinate nationally; an institution may have legal authority but little effective reach in one district; migration may alter the convertibility of a credential or relationship; a household may buffer coercion while reproducing another form of domination.

Represent **positions, relations, capacities, resource stocks, institutional rules, relevant schedules, costs, benefits, exclusions, recognized and informal norms, potential sanctions, transmission paths, and uncertainty**. Different claims have different scope; a region-level statistic cannot be transplanted into a specific family's exact bank balance or personal history.

Power should be a relation with **controller, controlled resource or decision, targets, feasible interventions, enforcement, potential resistance, and consequences**. Hegemony should be a pattern of power and symbolic recognition within fields; pressure should refer to concrete alternative-sensitive pathways; reproduction should include the labor that keeps those pathways operative.

### V.4 Carriers make culture materially operative

A cultural message is not a weight emitted continuously by 'society.' It is heard, practiced, enforced, interrupted, embodied and reinterpreted through **carrier events**: a classroom lesson, a prayer meeting, a workplace evaluation, a strike, a family meal, a border interview, a neighbor's assistance, a media encounter, a police checkpoint, a wage payment, a school expulsion.

The carrier has material conditions and an actor-relative path of access. It may confer a norm while also furnishing its critique. A school can punish defiance and teach a language of justice; a religious institution can endorse hierarchy and expose an ethical demand to defend the oppressed; a workplace can exploit and generate collective competence; a family can care and constrain simultaneously.

The engine must store at least: origin institution/actor, medium and practice, purpose, possible audience, access conditions, credibility cues, costs/sanctions, public and private meanings available, temporal exposure, and conflicting carrier alternatives. It must not assume a carrier is accepted simply because it arrives. **Institutional transmission is an interaction, not a transfer of a ready-made internal belief**.

### V.5 The opportunity–inhibition landscape is the world-facing social product

For an actor situated at a given time in this material world, Field Forge and the objective-world producers should support a query:

**What courses of activity are physically, legally, economically, socially and institutionally reachable? Which have low friction, high cost, sanctions, support, dependence, collective alternatives or uncertain access? What contradictory demands coexist? Which changes in resources, law, relations or historical circumstances would expand or contract the field?**

Return alternatives with evidence and mechanism status, not one best action. The world may allow a formally legal course that is practically unaffordable; prohibit a course that people nevertheless attempt; enable an action collectively but not individually; alter what costs mean depending on buffers, care work, documentation, language and allies. Compare **formal availability, practical reachability, anticipated consequences, actual enforcement, and actor-perceived possibility** without merging them.

A pressure claim must remain conditional. A demographic group average is not a pressure mechanism, and a structurally situated risk is not a prediction that an individual will comply. No source-supported empirical result is sacrificed just because the causal interpretation is held.

### V.6 Cross-source composition is not synthetic census-making

Multiple surveys can illuminate different aspects of one historical world without sharing respondents. Each source retains its own sample frame, release, time, respondent units, questionnaires, estimands and special meanings. Field Forge may compose **qualified relational constraints, complementary observations and externally sourced mechanisms**, preserving their scopes and conflicts.

Three distinct possibilities:

- Direct same-respondent conjunction: legitimate within one source (or independently certified linkage) at the actually shared unit, time and risk set.
- Same-target-population inference without linkage: partial identification or an explicitly assumption-bearing statistical model, never silently a witnessed joint.
- Analogical relation across different worlds: an explicitly compared pattern that may suggest a mechanism but grants neither shared respondents nor common population nor historical transport.

No operation may stitch people from different waves into a synthetic person solely to produce a cleaner social world. A graph of separately true facts is not proof that they all describe the same individual or that one institution caused them.

### V.7 Relations have real negative and unknown states

Field Forge's lawful outputs include a warranted positive relation, warranted valid empty, independently tested absence, insufficient evidence, noncomparable, missing authority, no transport, coverage limited, blocked and integrity failure. Each state keeps the original empirical evidence available at its own legitimate scope. A *correct typed refusal* is not an implementation victory if the needed positive structural producer or consumer has not been built.

The world owner must maintain valid structural rules over time, including genealogy, issuance, revision, invalidation and rollback. Old V552 pressure seed tables do not grant universal causal authority. An actor-facing pressure may be modeled where explicitly issued as an assumption-bound simulation; it cannot be advertised as an observed social law.

### V.8 Field Forge must remain different from SOROK

Field Forge can specify that an employer may fire a worker, that medical care requires travel, that a household has exhausted a resource, or that an organization offers mutual aid. It can specify a possible sermon, demonstration, shift schedule, rejection letter or neighborhood encounter. It cannot specify that the worker *therefore* fears the employer, that the mother *therefore* identifies with a social role, that the child *therefore* becomes compliant, or that a norm has been internalized. Those are historically situated actor processes.

The distinction is reciprocal. SOROK may develop a psychologically convincing fear or resentment, but it does not make a falsified real-world labor relation true. An actor's narration of an institution is evidence of that actor's current perspective within the simulation, not an independent proof that the institution operates as described. If the world's implementation is underdetermined, the actor may reasonably be uncertain too.

## VI. HISTORICAL BECOMING: PEOPLE AND STRUCTURES ARE MOVING OBJECTS

### VI.1 A source snapshot is a historically located window, not an eternal cultural constant

Different waves may differ because of population composition, within-position attitude change, questionnaire wording, translation, sampling design, legal classification, selection, cohort replacement, migration or an actual institutional rupture. Historical comparison must separate these rather than subtracting national averages and narrating the difference as modernization.

The comparison court asks whether the **same target proposition** can be meaningfully measured across periods, whether eligible populations and estimands are comparable, whether source protocols changed, and which analytical decompositions are identifiable. Class, occupation and religious identity can themselves change meaning, not merely prevalence. Even valid repeated cross-sections do not automatically distinguish age, period and cohort effects. A cohort resemblance is not longitudinal same-person change.

When supported, decompose observed change into **population-composition changes, within-position changes, changing position relations, changes in meaning or observation, and externally sourced institutional events**; retain the residual unidentified component. A named historical event can be relevant as an independent context fact without being proven the cause of a survey difference.

### VI.2 Transmission, maintenance and rupture are processes

A social field persists only if people and institutions keep reproducing it: making contracts, raising children, paying wages, organizing households, teaching norms, maintaining coercive capacity, allocating goods, contesting decisions. Its reproduction may simultaneously cultivate the abilities and solidarities through which people challenge it.

The world model must therefore support **eventful transformations**:
- changes in resource quantities, access, prices and time obligations;
- law, policy, enforcement and institution governance;
- collective action, coalition formation, breakdown and repression;
- migration, displacement, demographic shifts and changing networks;
- ecological, health and material shocks;
- learning, technological change, educational and occupational pathways;
- care, dependency, mortality, birth and changing household composition;
- altered legitimacy, competing collective interpretations and new carriers.

Some events arise through simulated actions; others are exogenous, historically scripted, stochastically modeled or supplied from external authoritative records. Their origins must be separately tagged. A fictional action cannot retrospectively alter an original 2010 survey response. Historical reconstruction cannot be treated as experimental identification simply because the simulation can run a counterfactual.

### VI.3 History has multiple clocks

Calendar time, age, developmental time, institutional cycles, labor shifts, relational duration and cultural generations do not advance in lockstep. The same world event can meet one actor at infancy, another during parenthood, another after retirement. Childhood, adolescence and adulthood are not merely stages where different fixed coefficients switch on; they are qualitatively distinct arrangements of dependence, feasible activity, language, recognition, institutions and responsibility.

A person can carry an earlier organization into a new condition where it is newly recruitable, unable to function, or transformed. Old experiences can be reinterpreted by later events. A historical society can change sharply while a household carries older forms of sense-making; a person's internal organization can change while the surrounding institution remains materially stable.

### VI.4 Counterfactuals belong to their own epistemic lane

Ask: under what changed conditions might this opportunity, relation, prevalence or actor trajectory differ? There are at least three grades:
- a **source-supported historical comparison** of actually observed different conditions;
- a **qualified structural scenario** whose interventions and transition rules have explicit warrant and sensitivity;
- an **exploratory fictional counterfactual** whose purpose is to reveal possible consequences and contradictory pathways, not report empirical frequencies.

Do not flatten them into 'the simulation proves X would have happened.' A compelling counterfactual narrative may be a useful design witness or source of test questions. It is not a recovered historical event or independent evidence of causal necessity.

### VI.5 History feeds the discovery engine backward as a question

The temporal circuit is recursive. A changed institutional rule can expose a historical shift worth querying within eligible sources. A failure of an apparently stable attitude cloud across a wave can expose translation drift, changing cohort composition, newly created social positions, or a true alteration in repertoires. A persistent minority under immense structural pressure may challenge a simplistic field model. A reported rise in an attitude that disappears after correcting a changed question is a positive finding about the history of observation, not a failed sociology.

The historical engine therefore revises its *question* as well as its modeled world. It must preserve competing interpretations when the evidence cannot distinguish them.

## VII. THE PSYCHOLOGICAL ORGANISM IS CONSTRUCTED IN HISTORY, NOT SAMPLED FROM A GROUP

### VII.1 What exactly enters SOROK from PYAT and the world

Two **different** entrances lead into one continuing psychological architecture.

**Parents and other fictional adults** begin from a situated, historically plausible *whole-configuration proposal*: not a trait draw, not the nearest real survey respondent, not a made-up joint of independently sampled questionnaire items. PYAT supplies conditional empirical possibilities and the alternatives within them; Field Forge supplies independent material/institutional worlds; historical backfill constructs a fictional person's life in those conditions by intelligent and explicit **abduction**, including relational history, maturation and contradictory experiences. An adult who appears at the beginning of Nadia's life is not a statically completed label; the backfilled history is a revisable fictional explanatory construction whose events and inferential status must be retained. Once realized in the simulated world, that adult continues to learn and change rather than periodically resampling an entire personality from population percentages.

**Nadia and other developing children** begin as immature, embodied, need-bearing organisms with capacities, physical vulnerability, emergent attention and dependency—not adult ideology, secret predetermined type or a target biography baked into an initial seed. Their particular psychological iceberg is **acquired** through actual world exposures, activity, relationships, bodily experience, interpreted events, cultural meanings, care, conflict and uneven maturation. No adult endpoint has authority over which infancy experiences are 'needed' to construct it.

The two entrances can use different initialization methods, but thereafter use **one psychological physics**: need, encounter, recruitment, action, consequence, memory, reorganization and future recruitment. An adult's reconstructed history and a child's simulated history may have different warrant (fictional inference versus actual prior simulated events); the runtime must not erase that difference.

### VII.2 The psyche is stratified, contradictory, nontransparent and historically acquired

A person's operative organization needs more than a flat character summary. Represent at least:

- **Embodied organism:** needs, energy, fatigue, pain, illness, arousal, hunger, temperature, comfort, growth, aging and developmental capacity; not reducible to ideological vectors.
- **Active concerns and obligations:** what this being is trying to sustain or avoid now, including dependents, work, material obligations, commitments, immediate tasks and conflicting goals.
- **Acquired response organizations:** learned expectancies, routines, skills, protective strategies, relational templates, practices, habitual interpretations, symbol systems, emotional associations and context-dependent affordances.
- **Motives and values:** partial, developing hierarchies of meaningful activity, including explicit commitments and contradictory nonverbal attractions. Do not identify stated ideological views with dominant operative motives.
- **Representations of Others and the world:** incomplete, revisable, socially learned models of what other people will do and what institutions permit; these can be biased, out of date, or wrong.
- **Reflexive and narrative resources:** the ability, when developmentally and situationally available, to interpret oneself, deliberate, rationalize, pray, analyze, narrate, repress, reconsider or refuse a social role.
- **A partly inaccessible layer:** organizations whose origins, triggers, or coexistence are not fully available to consciousness or available narrative. 'Hidden' does not license a universal Freudian ontology or invisible numerical driver with no behavioral impact. Every proposed hidden organization must earn its place by a functional constraint, developmental origin, observable consequence or deliberately marked uncertainty.
- **History and relational bonds:** episodic and schematic memories, skill acquisitions, role commitments, contested identities, ruptures, habits of care, betrayals, reconciliations and prior changes. History is not one concatenated story string.
- **Current standpoint and expression:** what this actor is attending to, permitted to say, willing to reveal, able to articulate, and trying to achieve in this precise encounter. Public speech is a situated action, not a transparent dump of the psyche.

These levels should be represented as **interacting, independently updatable organizations**, not mandatory modules with fixed coefficients. Some experiences produce pervasive transformations; others alter a single habit or a relationship-specific expectancy. Two organizations can be genuinely inconsistent, one conscious and one not, or both conscious but recruited under different conditions. They need not be averaged into an internally consistent personality vector.

### VII.3 Lived activity mediates both today's recruitment and tomorrow's acquisition

An event does not reach the psyche as a ready-made semantic input. The organism encounters it through bodily state, present activity, existing relational investments, language, practical capacity, attention, historical habits, material stakes and the limited means available to it. Two children can live through the same objectively recorded event differently. Vygotsky's **perezhivanie** exposes the irreducible middle: **objective occurrence ≠ lived-through realization ≠ later psychic afterlife**. This does not require introducing an arbitrary new Perezhivanie class; it requires the existing event, PRE-A0 experience and developmental traces to conserve those differences. A preverbal infant can experience bodily coordination, frustration or rupture without holding a proposition about it.

The same action can participate in several *continuing activities* with different objects and motives. Nadia's technically successful singing can sustain coordination with her mother, provide sensory pleasure, seek recognition, allow another child to participate and protect a valued role. If her mother praises Nadia while humiliating the other child, the notes may be correct while several implicated activities receive incompatible consequences. Affect can register those differences before Nadia can name them. An action's technical success is **not** identical to satisfaction of the activity's motives; one positive/negative reward is insufficient.

A minimal but causally concrete recruitment and reconstitution circuit:

1. **Independent world occurrence**: an Other acts, need emerges, institution intervenes, material circumstance changes or a practical opportunity appears.
2. **Situated access and lived-through realization**: the actor can perceive only what their embodied, developmental, relational and practical situation makes available; perception and expectation can be mistaken.
3. **Contextual recruitment**: existing acquired organizations, skills, memories, concerns and projects become differentially available, activated or inhibited by material, social and symbolic cues.
4. **Analogical possibility**: a resemblance to prior activity can make an old organization *provisionally* recruitable in an unfamiliar context without declaring the transfer already acquired. A tentative attempt can succeed, fail or reveal the analogy to be false.
5. **Composition across activities**: one response may serve multiple ongoing, partly conflicting motives and relationships; incompatible organizations can coexist and influence the action without a master personality score choosing a winner.
6. **Situated action, operation or inaction**: the actor uses a learned method toward a present aim, speaks, withholds speech, waits, helps, resists, attempts a new practice or sustains routine activity.
7. **Independent arbitration**: material conditions, institutions and Others determine what physically and socially occurred, distinct from the actor's intention and interpretation.
8. **Differentiated consequences and affect**: ask what happened *separately* to the activities, means, objectives and relationships implicated, what the actor expected and what they could actually register. The affective result need not be univocal or conscious.
9. **Potential reconstitution**: new practical distinctions, memories, expectations, altered relationships, capabilities, projects and future applicability of organizations can be proposed and committed only where grounded in lived experience and developmental availability. A change may be narrow, dormant, contradictory or absent.
10. **Changed future recruitment**: the now-altered psychic and social worlds make different activities, cues, inhibitions and analogical uses available.

The activity between recruitment and reconstitution is **not a disposable output stage**. It is where the acquired organization encounters its own possibility and limit, including conditions of success unavailable to the actor's prior imagination. Some activity serves only maintenance; some develops a skill, shifts a motive, creates an opposition, or changes the relation among earlier investments. The system cannot demand conscious introspection at every step or collapse this long process into a Markov trait update.

### VII.4 Activity, action and operation are different levels of an actor's life

The same behavior may belong to materially and psychologically different activities. A parent bringing food can act from care, fear of sanction, obligation, habit, guilt, calculation or mixed motives; the act's physical description alone does not disclose them. A worker performing a task may be maintaining wages, protecting a colleague, seeking competence, or avoiding humiliation. A child practicing a phrase may be experimenting with sound, earning praise, imitating a parent, asserting affiliation or trying to avoid exclusion.

Model the distinction:
- **Activity:** relatively enduring organization around some motive and social object, always embedded in practices and material conditions.
- **Action:** a goal-directed or situated contribution within activity; goals can be conscious or only partially articulated.
- **Operation:** the condition-dependent method by which a goal is carried out, often routinized and not the subject of active reflection.

An operation can become an action when conditions break a routine; an activity can change motive without every surface action changing; conflicting activities can recruit the same skill. This solves a frequent mechanical failure in personality simulation: a model can update 'trust' yet never change which concrete practices the person is capable of undertaking or why they undertake them.

### VII.5 A new anticipated end is not yet an enduring motive

An LLM serves as **genuine psychological construction machinery**, but its question is not 'what trait should event E install?' It is: **given this actor's historically constituted activities and relations, how could this being live through these consequences, and what acquired organization, distinction or future recruitment pathway might emerge?** It proposes within the limits of known experience, existing psyche, maturational capacities and social conditions, then passes through an explicit SOROK adjudication and state-commitment contract.

**Separate three states** that old generator heuristics were tempted to merge:
- A **newly available possible end**: Nadia remembers a pleasurable sound and can anticipate trying to make it again.
- A **provisional project or attempt**: an available opportunity and analogy make repetition imaginable and actionable without a settled activity.
- An **established motive-bearing activity**: repeated or decisive lived practice has made the object significant within continuing life, in relation to other concerns, means and social practices.

One episode does not mechanically produce a stable music-oriented project. Equally, requiring a fully established project before any novel attempt prevents the very beginning of transfer. The acquisition path can be rapid or gradual, fail entirely, change its object, or be redirected through an independent Other. The model must specify what makes a proposed transition defensible, which alternative interpretation remains, and what future encounter would reveal a difference.

**Analogical recruitment** is not silent permanent scope expansion. A cue resembling earlier practice can provisionally recruit it; the subsequent activity reveals whether the organization transfers, must narrow, becomes a new competence, or remains ineffective. This preserves both the lesson of Lahire's context-dependent past and the rule against unauthorized learned-association overrides of genuine eligibility.

**Capacity acquisition and capacity exercise** are also different. As Sève emphasizes, an acquired operation can be used in new activities, and exercising a power can further develop it; but a person can also acquire an ability they materially cannot exercise. The organization of time, care work, class access and institutional rules mediates whether a possibility becomes practiced, central, inhibited or lost.

A committed psychological proposal must therefore state:
- the experienced event and the actor-accessible portion of its real consequences;
- which ongoing activities/motives and relationships were implicated, and how their consequences differed;
- the proposed new or revised orientation, its developmental availability, and its scope;
- recruitment triggers, provisional analogies, inhibitions and relationships to contradictory organizations;
- what behavior, practice, attention, anticipated end or possible activity it can actually change;
- alternative hypotheses, uncertainty, refusal or NO_CHANGE;
- the future encounter that could test or transform it.

A moving paragraph that alters no subsequent activity is not psychological genesis. A list of reward-weight updates with no account of which lived activities changed is not psychodynamics. The actor can register an affective contradiction before understanding it, understand a conflict without changing it, or change behavior without any corresponding articulate theory of the self.

### VII.6 Contextual plurality is the norm, not an exception

Lahire's challenge to unified habitus is particularly operative here. A person may learn distinct organizations in family, school, work, worship, collective action, migration and intimate relationships. Not every acquired scheme is always active. Some contexts recruit incompatible schemes; some combine them into new patterns. A person can sincerely hold rival commitments, switch behavior without consciously planning the shift, and revise old motives after seeing the consequences.

**Recruitment must depend on context and development**, not on a universal highest-weight trait. At a minimum track activation cues, accessible memory, material affordance, current motives, perceived Others, fatigue, role expectations, symbolic objects, recent events, antagonistic inhibition, and capacity to improvise. These influences must be represented sufficiently for events to change recruitment rules over time.

Not all contradictions require resolution. They may remain dormant, be rationalized, erupt under new pressure, or produce genuine synthesis. The model must allow identity to become less unified, more unified, or organized differently without assuming psychological coherence is always the good.

### VII.7 Development as open-ended formation, not a prescribed age curve

Biological maturation changes bodily needs and the available operations of attention, memory, language, emotion regulation, social inference, reflection and practical skill. Cultural institutions organize developmental opportunities unequally. Childhood labor, schooling, play, migration, war, care, exclusion, class and collective life alter what is encountered, learned and possible.

Implement **capability availability as a stateful developmental condition**: age and maturation matter but are not enough. Learned symbols, practice, support, nutrition, historical setting, disability, stress and institutions affect the real available repertoire. There should be no universal birthday switch that turns on 'political ideology', 'romantic attachment' or a final personality class. A new capacity can make an earlier experience newly interpretable.

Luria's cultural-developmental inquiry asks how thinking is organized by historically available practices and cognitive tools; Elder asks how life timing, historical shocks and social pathways matter; Mortimer asks how work, adolescence and transition shape reciprocal trajectories. Those questions constrain the architecture, but do not supply universal deterministic update weights.

### VII.8 The parents' backfill must explain, not predestine, Nadia

The adult seeding procedure should operate like **constrained, whole-survey-informed historical abduction**:
1. Choose a legitimate material/world position and time, not a stereotyped nation-level profile.
2. Query PYAT for conditional *whole-configuration alternatives* and uncertainty, keeping minority routes visible.
3. Bring in the world: household history, institutions, livelihoods, resource access, care patterns, cultural carriers, events and possible critical encounters.
4. Generate several plausible, causally distinct biographies compatible with the evidence and world. Prefer configurations whose relational history could produce their current organizations; document what is fictional, not empirically observed.
5. Select/commit one fictional backfill with explicit temporal events and reconstruct enough operative psyche for future interaction. Do **not** copy any real respondent or invent a secret survey profile.
6. Put the adult into the actual continuing world as an independent Other whose actions may surprise Nadia and whose own state may change.

Two adults who share every measured survey answer can have different needs, motivations, memories and responses to their child. Conversely two adults with different survey patterns can converge in a particular act because of a shared practical obligation. Whole-survey conditioning constrains plausible starting configurations but does not manufacture psychology or destiny.

### VII.9 Nadia's upbringing must be actual interaction, not retroactive biography

Nadia encounters adults, objects, illness, hunger, affection, disagreement, limits, care, danger, language, stories, religious practice, school, neighborhood and institutional power as actual world events. Her responses have consequences for others; their responses in turn constrain or extend what she can do. Care is a material process performed by independently burdened people, not a timeless trait called 'parental warmth'. Her psychological organizations develop from these interactions, not from a script describing the adult she is supposed to become.

At each age, the system must be capable of explaining:
- **What she needed or was engaged in**, not an abstract adult motive.
- **What she actually encountered**, and what she had the capacity to notice or interpret.
- **Which learned organizations were available and recruited**, and which were not yet acquired or inhibited.
- **What she did or failed to do**, with independently determined world consequences.
- **What changed in her capacity, relationship, memory or motive organization**, including unresolved contradictions.
- **What new possibility or vulnerability is now present** that was not present before.

A developmental trajectory that can be predicted in detail from the parent's national culture and a child seed violates this constitution. A trajectory in which each scene is entertaining but the same psychic organizations never change also violates it.

### VII.10 Psychic opacity, alienation and the ultimate horizon

The earlier A/S/V frameworks insisted on distinguishing **material position, awareness, solidarity, viscerality, belief and expression**. Keep them separate. A person may be in a class relation without identifying with that class, recognize a contradiction without acting, act in solidarity without a coherent ideology, or express values they do not consistently practice. **Buffers and cracks** matter: material security, relational protection, ideological availability and lived crisis mediate what structures become conscious or actionable.

Alienation, identification and solidarity must have *traceable relations* to activity, world, and subjective organization; they are not decorative numeric dials. The old Absolute Horizon / ultimate meaning language can function as an **open question about how finite commitments are relativized, absolutized, criticized or transcended**. It is not a fixed psychological 'spiritual depth' score, a doctrinal verdict, or a hidden totalizing variable. A person may locate ultimate concern through religion, justice, care, art, labor, grief or wordless attention, or never articulate it. No metaphysical theology is installed as an empirical fact.

The actor can be uncertain about their own motives. The runtime needs provisional, private and inaccessible organizations without inventing an omniscient narrator who explains them all. A meaningful response may be an action before it can be spoken; language can both reveal and alter what becomes conscious.

### VII.11 Expression is a consequential act, not the final cosmetic layer

An utterance has audience, timing, risk, desired effect, linguistic repertoire, possible concealment, emotion, habits and social conditions. The language model constructing speech must consult actual actor/world state and the selected activity; it should also allow silence, hesitation, misstatement and refusal. A polished monologue inconsistent with a tired, frightened person in a workplace encounter is not good expression just because it matches the personality description.

After speech, the world responds. Words can change relationships, reputations, resource access, shared interpretations and internal organization. Expression may be retrospectively reinterpreted by the speaker. Therefore **the loop continues after language**; there is no terminal 'GPT output' stage in a living psychological organism.

## VIII. ORDINARY LIFE IS THE UNIT OF CONTINUITY, NOT THE HEROIC SCENE

### VIII.1 Everyday conduct, life-time allocation and slow formation

Continuous becoming requires routines: waking, eating, cleaning, commuting, working, waiting, caring, resting, encountering bureaucracy, attending school, reading, praying, talking, being bored and keeping relationships alive. These are not fillers between cinematic formative scenes. They maintain needs, reproduce household and institutional relations, provide practice, allocate time, inhibit other activities, and form expectations.

Dreier's **conduct of everyday life** demands a whole organization across places, not merely a sequence of scene-local decisions. A parent may remain at work while concerned for a child. A worker can sacrifice practice of one capacity to preserve the income that makes another possible. Continuing a seemingly unchanged life requires continuing activity. Holzkamp's perspective likewise makes the person's practical reasons and available means irreducible to either coercive structure or freely chosen preference.

**Ordinary-life hypercompression** may skip uninformative seconds but must conserve causally consequential duration. Implement multiple clocks: bodily needs, work and care schedules, learning/practice opportunities, relationship maintenance, institutional cycles and meaningful interruption triggers. A compression interval should retain:
- which activities were undertaken or prevented, and for how long;
- who performed reproduction labor, at what cost, and what material stocks changed;
- which skills were acquired, exercised, frustrated or allowed to deteriorate;
- which organizations had repeated cue exposure or were kept dormant;
- what relationships and institutional conditions persisted, strengthened or weakened;
- what surprise, changed need or conflict requires expansion into an explicit event.

The distinction **capacity possessed ≠ opportunity to exercise it** is a hard constraint on compression. A child who learns a skill but loses access to its tools must not be represented as continuously practicing it; a caregiver constrained by a work shift must not be narrated as constantly present; a repetitive occupation can develop practical competence while exhausting the time available for another project. Do not turn months of structured activity into an ungrounded 'personality +0.1' update.

### VIII.2 A persistent relationship is a two-sided, historically evolving system

A relationship is more than the focal actor's tag for another person. Track concrete encounters and mutual dependencies; commitments and asymmetries; interpreted histories and their divergences; boundary and consent; help, neglect, coercion and resistance; shared memories and mismatched recollections; altered trust, attachment and expectations; changed power or material dependence. Each participant retains their own psychic state and version of the history.

A parent-child relationship changes through the child's maturation and the adult's independent activities. A friendship can persist across places with changing frequency and meaning. Institutions can enter relationships as creditors, employers, schools or courts. A relationship can fracture while both participants believe they acted understandably. The world need not contrive a single morally tidy narrative.

### VIII.3 Independence is tested by surprises and disagreement

An independent Other can refuse a request, pursue a goal elsewhere, become unavailable, misread the focal person's intent, change in an unanticipated direction, or suffer institutional consequences the focal actor did not cause. The world can make a plan impossible despite the actor's determination; it can also supply an unanticipated ally or opening.

A simulation with many 'NPCs' who exist only to trigger scripted psychological updates is not a social organism. The test is not raw number of other-agent tokens but whether their persistent aims and independent conditions constrain the focal actor. Even a low-fidelity background process is better than a deterministic accessory if it preserves real otherness.

### VIII.4 The full recursive circuit

The runtime must repeatedly implement a causally interpretable trace of:

**material world and ongoing reproductive activity → event/encounter/exposure → bodily and psychic recruitability → composition of motives and acquired organizations → situated intention/action/inaction/expression → independent other/world arbitration → material, relational and institutional consequence → memory and acquired-organization revision → changed future possibility and recruitment.**

The process is *historical* in both directions: the actor changes, and the world—including Others—changes. On a future turn, an earlier institution can be absent, a relationship may have been damaged, a once-scarce skill may now be practiced, a previously invisible pressure may be recognized. The life history must be sufficient to explain these changes without repeatedly inventing causes from scratch.

### IX.4 One historical Nadia, actor-local perspectives, and a rolling world

**There is one Nadia.** The historical P3 organism cannot generate a biography that is handed to a freshly invented P4–P6 personality. Its actual developing-person/world history must compile to the **same versioned world/person root** used by the live interaction machinery. Preserve root, actor roster, branching identity, clocks, objective world history, relationships and acquired psychological organization. The root is a *causal state* with eligible unresolved detail, not a prose sheet that licenses new backstory whenever asked.

**Use existing owner jurisdictions, not a second life engine.** P3 orchestrates chronology and records; P2/ADP-019/S47 own objective occurrences, objects, material changes and world effects; ADP-015 owns relationship states; CAN-010/011 own durable acquired psyche; CAN-008 owns immediate recruitment and response; ADP-016/017 own memories, availability of knowledge and claims; PFF/V2 handles actor-limited expression; Mechanism I fills permissible historical-object detail, Mechanism II governs user perception, and Mechanism III handles independent present activity. P4–P6/S46 handle live encounters. This is a historical owner map to recheck at current code HEADs, not an authority issuance or instruction to build duplicate modules.

**An objective world fact, a parent's private interpretation, the parent's actual utterance and Nadia's perception are four records.** The owner can know the full material constraint and admissible options; a parent acts from their own accessible circumstances; their actual speech may reveal only part of their motivation; the child acquires only the episode they could hear, observe or infer. Provide a read-only actor-relative situated view from existing world/actor/relation state: actual local affordances, actor-controlled resources, work/care obligations, current activity, existing acquired organizations, accessible memories, perceived Others and causal readsets. The owner/adjudicator may inspect objective information unavailable to that actor, but may not present it as the actor's private knowledge. Do not install a redundant authoritative 'Nadia profile' beside the person's durable organizations.

**Four clocks cannot be collapsed:** historical/as-of calendar time (age, source wave, employment, tenancy, entitlements); P3 owner logical order (proposals, admissions, retrospect); S46 present activity intervals; and actual body, relation and developmental duration. A historical change *inside* a compressed interval must split it at its effective date. A job lost in January and restored in June cannot cause twelve months of unemployment expense; rent begins and ends with actual tenure. Workshop S46 ticks are not automatically months or years. The same source survey wave is not a longitudinal panel following these fictional people.

The **duration pass precedes each selected event**: split interval at actual changes, maintain stocks and flows, care and work, schedules, repeated practice and thwarted activities, update independent parents and Others, and apply only qualified actor-durational rules (including evaluated NO_CHANGE). A lasting material pressure without an actual learning rule remains a world pressure, not a compulsory trait increment. Sparse P3 episodes may occur in batches, with later retrospective processing, but duration continues between them.

The positive event circuit is: qualified prospective candidate → owner-chosen event and actor action → separately admitted outward speech → independent world and relational consequences → actor-local perceptual receipt → CAN-008 immediate recruitment → possible later CAN/ADP psychological proposal and competent commit → a different future reader consuming the actual persisted effect. **Generate the conduct before explaining it**: the actor can be impulsive, self-defeating, mistaken or opaque to themselves, not a reward-maximizer equipped with a tidy post-hoc explanation.

A hosted LLM's new output need not match byte-for-byte on repeated invocation. Exact causal replay uses its **previously recorded selected proposal**, source/world root, readset, actor/clock, contingency and admitted owner receipts rather than calling the model again. Joint effects must not present a half-completed purchase or move as completed world history. Preserve causal and authority roles even if multiple owner deltas are atomically committed.

### IX.5 Psychological proposals must be executable transformations

A model-generated developmental proposal must specify its *mechanism of future effect*. A suggested new relational expectation 'care is conditional on performance' is incomplete until it provides:
- a source in actually experienced interactions or fictional backfill under the correct lane;
- an acquisition or reinterpretation pathway;
- developmental availability;
- contexts, cues and relationship targets where it may become recruitable;
- competing organizations it inhibits or is inhibited by;
- possible behavioral, attentional or expressive changes;
- conditions under which future events could challenge or transform it.

A proposal that only adds purple prose to a character's narrative, or one that overgeneralizes one frightening event into a universal worldview without a modeled learning pathway, should not be committed as psychological fact. The model may propose several interpretations and their differential predictions; SOROK's owner sets which are legal to become operative. Do not merely store a 'psychological suggestion' in side metadata while the runtime ignores it.

### IX.6 Memory, persistence, cache and recomputation are part of psychology and science

There are two independent histories:
- **epistemic history**: releases, source rights, method decisions, queries, accepted/rejected concepts, corrections and replay roots;
- **fictional life and world history**: events, actions, relationships, developmental changes, persistent resource changes and others' decisions.

Both need versioned persistence, but they obey different truth rules. A revised semantic mapping can invalidate a PYAT answer and N10E possibility without erasing that Nadia previously experienced a fictional event generated under an earlier scenario. A world correction may require a governed simulation fork, not a silent retroactive rewrite of her memories. A world rollback needs to resolve resulting actor-state dependencies at the same version boundary.

A physically optimized DAG can:
- reuse exact eligible masks, canonical joints, query subexpressions and derived concepts;
- use Parquet/Zstd or suitable persistent carriers where existing owner contracts demand them, and bounded caches for warm query efficiency;
- maintain exact dependency roots and **delete-and-rebuild** behavior for nonauthoritative caches;
- invalidate on changed source, semantic rights, structural mechanisms, comparison assumptions, time context, scenario branch or actor-history version as applicable;
- demonstrate identical meaning across cold and warm paths and across harmless physical layout changes;
- require atomic stage/promote and exact recovery after interruption.

Do not confuse a hash-matching payload with a scientifically complete dataset or an authorized interpretation. A stale authority root is not repaired by a cached answer that happens to have the right numerical value. A consumer-access failure is information loss even if the underlying bytes still exist.

### IX.7 A powerful LLM has two positive roles and four prohibitions

**Role 1: pre-adjudication assistant** for finite, expensive, high-stakes source semantics and ontological interpretation. It can reason deeply over exact evidence and propose decision alternatives with source quotations and negative tests. Only a competent issued authority makes the binding semantic or method selection.

**Role 2: live abductive construction** in both open-ended PYAT research and SOROK psychic development. In PYAT, it nominates surprising empirical questions, motifs, rival mechanisms and falsifiers whose claims must be tested by admissible computation. In SOROK, it proposes context-sensitive meanings, organizations, relationships and developmental transformations that become operative only through explicit actor-state commitments.

**Prohibitions:** it cannot fabricate a survey observation, manufacture an unmeasured respondent join, issue a structural/causal law from confident prose, or overwrite independent world history with a retrospective story. Its creative strength should be directed toward *candidate formation and realistic psychological construction*, where new possibilities genuinely matter, not toward laundering uncertain claims into facts.

The model's role is not merely 'speaker of the personality.' Speech itself is a situation-dependent action; the model is permitted to generate it from actual world and actor states. It must also be permitted to revise its theory of a fictional actor when the life falsifies a previous interpretation.

### IX.8 Reuse the existing organism; do not build a decorative replacement

SOROK already possesses P2–P6, CAN/ADP, PFF, SDTF, legacy V3, the Mechanisms, PRE-A0, SEAM and ORACLE; PYAT already possesses C11, conditional/branch/joint primitives, semantic and structural owners, partial first consumers, N10E/N10W and Field Forge R2 architectural law. A construction agent should establish *which functions are actually implemented, tested and consumed* at exact heads, and then extend or replace only the missing functions while preserving valuable accepted capital.

The architectural decision rule is **functional conservation under lawful replacement**, not loyalty to a historical acronym. If an existing engine is already a legitimate recruitment mechanism, use it rather than inventing a second; if it only narrates after decisions are made, that is not the missing psychological-construction function. If an old Enneagram coefficient was always speculative, do not reinstate it to achieve superficial backward compatibility. The old *question it was trying to answer* must find a stronger mechanism or an explicitly held open problem.

## X. FOUR WHOLE-MACHINE INQUIRIES AND THEIR DISCRIMINATING FAILURES

These are **constructed acceptance scenarios**, not assertions of findings in the real 35-wave estate. The system must discover its own empirically warranted answers, including answers that contradict these sketches.

### X.1 A multi-label questionnaire becomes a new social question

An eligible survey asks which kinds of discrimination respondents perceive or experience, allowing multiple selections. A conventional pipeline computes the most commonly selected category and perhaps compares it to a country average. The intended machine obtains complete multi-answer morphology, respondent denominators, option overlap, refusal, eligibility and valid risk sets. It may discover that the dominant option is not the dominant **whole response pattern**: people who choose it disagree sharply about what accompanies it.

The investigator follows majority retention and departure, distinct minority combinations and residual response sets. It searches for conditional forks by work, schooling, household, institutional access and place only where source identity and semantics permit. A second splitter inside one unusual minority could expose a configuration invisible to global one-variable rankings. It now asks what else travels together **within these same people**—normative judgment, reports of actual institution encounters, willingness to act and conflicting responses—while the response-act grammar prevents treating perception and action as synonyms.

A newly found motif generates **rival interpretations and a testable question**. An empirical footprint can reach Field Forge as a question about institutions, status and coercion, not a magically proven causal story. The conditioned possibility service retains the minority branch with real uncertainty or performs an explicit specificity walkback.

**Hard counterexample:** if the motif vanishes under a proper independent assessment, the machine must revise or withdraw the candidate social concept without erasing original observed configurations. If semantic authority for a special relation is absent, the empirical co-observation still survives while the prohibited interpretation is withheld. **Complete product:** a newly discovered, challenged, reusable question reaching an actual consumer, not a fashionable cluster name.

### X.2 Work, care, class and the material cost of acting otherwise

Imagine a fictional adult caregiver employed under an insecure labor regime, responsible for dependent care and possessing little financial buffer. This is a **hypothetical social position**, not an actual matched survey respondent. PYAT may recover source-supported patterns of labor precarity, collective-action judgments, household conditions or institutional trust—but may not fabricate any unavailable same-person joint.

Field Forge independently constructs schedules, wages, childcare hours, transit, employer powers, household dependencies, contractual protection, sanctions, collective organization and available supports. A workplace action becomes a differently costly alternative depending on solidarity networks, care obligations and material buffers. The *frequency* of survey respondents saying they fear dismissal is not itself the power relation.

Two fictional adults with the same measured survey answer pattern may have distinct practical motives because their actual histories differ. One has repeatedly experienced coworker solidarity; another has experienced a failed collective attempt; a third acts out of care-driven caution while sincerely endorsing redistribution. Those psychic organizations require actual or explicitly abducted life histories.

Now change one material relation: a childcare cooperative becomes operational, or a protective legal rule is amended but left unenforced. The system must say which **practical alternatives change**, for whom, and why; it must not automatically raise the actor's 'defiance score'. The actor encounters the new situation, acts or refrains, receives independent replies and materially consequential outcomes, and learns from them. The cooperative itself requires labor, resources and governance.

**Hard counterexamples:** the cooperative changes nothing despite care having been asserted as the main impediment; group political averages dictate the individual's decision; the legal protection is treated as effective with no enforcement; an adult's acquired psychology never changes after a meaningful outcome. **Complete product:** a witnessed divergence among survey-conditioned possibility, actual material opportunity, perceived opportunity, chosen activity and realized consequence.

### X.3 The drum kit: material necessity, independent parents, Nadia's accessible event

The recovered SOROK November 11 material provides a better whole-machine **hostile case** than a vague 'formative scene'. Nadia, seven, asks for a drum kit. The family rents a small apartment with thin walls, little storage, noise-sensitive neighbors and possible landlord/superintendent penalties. Money is a distinct constraint: the family might *afford the kit but not safely use it*. The mother has an independently formed life, fatigue, care labor, values and habits of managing risks and children's requests; the father may disagree or control different resources. The mother is not a programmed psychological stimulus delivered to Nadia.

The objectively available alternatives include buy, defer, refuse, negotiate, buy a quieter substitute, find external practice space or seek a neighbor accommodation. The mother chooses from her actual acquired organizations and feasible world, potentially impulsively or self-contradictorily. Suppose she refuses with: “You have your recorder from school, and I never hear you play it; you're not ready yet.” Her private reason may involve rent sanctions, noise, cost, an educational conviction, frustration or all of them. **The private reason is not the utterance. The utterance is not Nadia's knowledge of its origin.** Nadia hears the claim about readiness and previous practice; she might see some contextual cues but cannot access her mother's mind.

Nadia's immediate lived response depends on embodied state, her relationship histories and active projects. She may be angry, ashamed, disappointed, encouraged to practice, eager to negotiate, indifferent or conflicted. A durable new expectation like “I must prove full use before deserving more” is a *possible acquired organization*, not the automatic consequence of one event. It might only form through repetition, be limited to this relationship, be contradicted by the father, appear much later, or never form at all. A qualified NO_CHANGE is a real outcome; MISSING_RULE is an implementation/authority hold.

**Mutation court:** change the mother's hidden financial/noise reason while holding her actual utterance fixed; Nadia must not gain private omniscience. Change the mother's own history while preserving the objective apartment: her action/utterance may differ. Hold the utterance fixed and vary Nadia's prior experience: reception/recruitment may differ. Increase future household affluence: the old learned organization must not instantly disappear, though practical options expand. Let the purchase succeed: actual money, space, sound and neighbor consequences must occur, or explicit mitigation must explain their absence. Apply a newly committed durable orientation to a **different later event**; if severing the exact earlier learned target has no effect when it should, the update was cosmetic. Change employment midway through a long interval and verify proper segmentation of costs and exposure.

**Whole witness:** independently existing world/parent → parent's selected action and actual outward utterance → child-accessible occurrence → immediate CAN-008 processing → qualified durable acquisition, alternative interpretation or no-change → persisted psychological history → later situated activity with a different result, even as the mother and material world continue changing. This is a mechanical/causal proof design, not empirical proof of a universal childhood-development theory.

### X.4 Independent survey marginals cannot become an imagined joined population

Separate sources estimate binary marginals .70 and .60. First test whether they genuinely support a common target population, time, meanings, eligibility and inference authority. Otherwise retain the valid source-local findings and refuse the cross-source bridge. If fully warranted, their sharp possible overlap is [.30,.60]; that establishes necessary overlap but **not** the exact association or which respondent holds both. For .45 and .40, zero overlap remains compatible.

A synthetic statistical matching algorithm may pick a coupling, but does not establish it as observed. A new legitimate partial joint may narrow the identified set. A more confident language model cannot. Alter the population eligibility and the original bound ceases to be legal. Separately, with 36 yes, 24 no, 40 eligible unanswered among 100, the 60% yes rate among responders cannot certify a majority of all eligible cases; the finite eligible-record bound is [.36,.76]. Change the unanswered to valid skips and the calculation must change.

**Complete product:** precise positive claims about what all admissible worlds share, alongside explicit refusal to invent the rest. This is an active scientific capability, not a footnote about uncertainty.

## XI. ACCEPTANCE IS WHOLE-FUNCTION, ADVERSARIAL AND SOURCE-EXACT

### XI.1 Five questions determine maturity

For each required capability, distinguish:
1. **Local operation:** on competent input, does it produce an actual positive or valid empirical null result?
2. **Authority conservation:** does meaning, provenance, risk set, component identity, negative status and use permission survive?
3. **Composed integration:** is the positive product reachable by the **real** next owner/consumer without invented data or unauthorized interpretation?
4. **Estate/world occupancy:** can governed real source input or genuinely independently configured actor/world state reach the code at its declared scope?
5. **End-use adequacy:** does the result actually alter the empirical inquiry, material opportunity or developing actor process for which the capability exists?

Local sourcefree capability is not original35 scientific accreditation. A valid empty or well-measured null is a positive result; a refusal because nobody has implemented the necessary producer is unfinished development. A missing upstream permission is not automatically a defect in the downstream component, but it remains an unresolved integration/estate obligation.
