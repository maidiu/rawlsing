# DHIKRYMULCH
## THE EMPIRICAL-SOCIAL POSSIBILITY ENGINE
### The constructive vision of what Rawlsing will now become

**PROGRAMMA PYAT · living theory · positive functional synthesis · 20 November 2026**

**This is the machine we intend to build, described in the order in which its intelligence comes into existence.** DHIKR is the resurrection effort by which recovered and presently missing capacities are made available. It does not define the endpoint. The endpoint is the most powerful truthful, generative, historically situated empirical-social possibility engine we can construct: a system that can discover, explain, navigate, compare and serve the structured multiplicity of human possibilities recorded in survey evidence, without mistaking those possibilities for finished psychological persons.

This is a **conceptual architecture**, not an insistence upon one giant runtime, one physical file schema, one sequence of physically materialized stages or one scalar called *determinicity*. Its order is **logical dependency, not a compulsory once-through serial conveyor**. Empirical discovery must reenter earlier questions when later results reveal a hidden branch, contradiction, contradictory interpretation, absent population or new measurement problem. The structure is a continuously revisable inquiry graph and a separate historically material field-construction process, not one analytics waterfall. The objects named here are conceptual capabilities and service results. An existing, more general lawful implementation may realize several at once. The theory tells us **what must be possible**, why each operation exists, what it needs, what it produces, and what new question that output makes answerable. A later independent code confrontation will determine which capabilities are already in fact delivered and which must be built or repaired. The separate historical source/conservation register remains available, but it does not govern the order of this affirmative account.

## I. THE INTENDED RESULT: A MACHINE THAT KNOWS THE SHAPE OF POSSIBLE SOCIAL HUMANITY

Imagine asking Rawlsing:

*Among people in a specific society, historical period, material position and relational environment, what configurations of perception, commitment, conflict and action are actually evidenced as possible? Which are prevalent? Which are rare but coherent? What conditions make each more or less common? Where does the prevailing configuration fracture? What alternatives appear behind the fracture? What else tends to accompany each alternative? How much does the answer change if we reverse the question, move one level down into a social subgroup, add a contradictory condition, move through time, or enter a different institutional field? Which of those relationships are only observed correlations, which admit stronger structural interpretations, and what remains completely unknown?*

And then imagine asking:

*Construct a hypothetical adult occupying this specified historical position. Do not independently sample a caricatured collection of traits. Select a coherent possibility from the discovered, respondent-supported space. Show what other configurations remained possible, what conditions brought this one into focus, how specific the source evidence was, which parts were inferred or weakened through walkback, and exactly what you do not know about that person's actual biography, motivations or psychology.*

That is our target. It is neither a survey dashboard nor a trait generator. It is a **navigable field of socially conditioned possibilities** that makes actual historical variation and contradiction operative in downstream construction.

The machine must be capable of saying "most," "some," "nearly none," "this minority," "this alternative," "these patterns travel together," "the ranking changes here," "we have evidence but the meaning is disputed," "this specific combination is unsupported," "the question cannot be transported," and "we lack the scientific authority to make that stronger claim." All of those answers carry information.

The ultimate achievement is not predictability at any price. It is the ability to produce **specificity without stereotype, contingency without randomness, and coherence without predestination**.

The machine has four simultaneously active circuits rather than one long assembly line: an **empirical discovery circuit** that finds admissible patterns and directs new questions back into governed evidence; an **actor-independent social-world constitution circuit** where Field Forge interprets and composes social structures without taking over upstream empirical producers; a **post-Field-Forge historical-condition circuit** that resolves structural pressure, temporal change and circumstance into usable PRE-V3 contexts; and a **generative query circuit** in which downstream hypothetical-person construction requests more conditional evidence without rewriting the observed world. A fifth, orthogonal **proof/conservation circuit** follows every produced claim across those four. These circuits reinforce one another but never exchange permissions by proximity.

## II. FIRST: MAKE THE OBSERVED SOCIAL WORLD AVAILABLE

**Input:** Real survey releases and their question/option structures, response observations, respondent units, applicable populations, material and demographic variables, wave/place/time, routing, sampling context and permitted analytical uses.

**Operation:** Constitute the source as an addressable *empirical world*. Establish what each dimension measures, what each value actually signifies, who could have answered the question, what was not asked, what was refused or unavailable, what belongs to the same respondent, and which comparisons or inferences are legitimate. Keep the original social and historical coordinates.

**Product:** The **Observed World**: source-coherent, unit-coherent, versioned, question-meaning-bearing populations and measurements with exact, reusable identities and truthful unknowns.

**Why:** No downstream intelligence can be more real than its observational basis. An observed negative is different from nonresponse; not applicable is different from no; a source concept is not its English label; a country in one cross-national wave is not a universal country type; a sampled respondent is not a generated parent.

### Before calculation: distinguish a questionnaire's visible categories from the social concepts we hope to understand

The empirical-world constructor must recognize a profound separation between a **source item**, the **response options actually administered**, a **reconstructed substantive content identity**, a **candidate explanatory construct**, a **structural/material position**, and a **person's possible interpretation**. An old Rawlsing implementation already tried to organize source questions under content keys and drivers. Its fragile mappings were evidence of a real conceptual need, not warrant for promoting a false proxy: a poll item about religious confidence is not automatically a complete measure of religious identification; party preference is not collective political agency; household income does not exhaust class position; a response concerning meaning or purpose does not measure the person's ultimate organizing moral horizon.

The source question is an **observation instrument**, not society's ontology. The machinery should therefore expose the measurable content and its limitations *before* deciding what deserves statistical discovery. A proposed construct should be able to point to several different measures, disclose how those measures disagree, and refuse forced agreement when their wording, scale, opportunity or target population changes. Conversely, different source items may be candidates for the same conceptual inquiry while remaining distinct observations with no automatic value portability.

This creates an important additional kind of discovery: **measurement criticism generated by empirical contradictions**. If an alleged class-awareness scale breaks apart in particular labor positions, if a presumed equivalent pair of questions moves in contrary historical directions, or if a supposedly uniform demographic variable contains a locally organized counterpattern, the engine should be able to ask whether its *construct itself* was inadequate. It must be able to learn "the variable does not adequately measure the phenomenon," not only "the variable's effect is weak." Improved question/construct interpretation still requires its competent meaning authority; the autonomous engine may nominate the problem and preserve the evidence without privately correcting the source.

A strong social science must know the difference between a negative finding, an imperfect measure, a wrong theory of the measure and an unavailable historical phenomenon. It must also know that unobserved patterns are not missing because the people failed to produce them: the questionnaire may not have offered language through which respondents could express the relevant distinction. This is why the next stage must recover entire response morphologies rather than immediately fit a preferred trait.
**Next:** Once the world can be addressed truthfully, the machine can ask what the entire observed response landscape looks like.

This is not a demand to keep materializing whole original surveys. A compact, exact, reusable source and measure interface may be enough. Physical storage is subordinate to what questions can be answered without erasing meaning or requiring source reconstruction.


### The organizing research object: two empirical spaces and a homology between them

The empirical machine must be able to discover two different spaces from the *same source-governed evidence*. The first is a **Position and Resources Space**: material, occupational, educational, geographic, legal, household, communal and other measured or lawfully constructed social positions, their resources, dependencies and contrasts. A position is not merely an income score; it is a historically situated configuration of observable and independently interpreted relations.

The second is the **Attitude and Disposition Space**: respondent-coherent ways of judging, trusting, endorsing, refusing, reporting practices, articulating values and imagining futures as actually measured by source questionnaires. Its structure includes joint repertoires, minorities, response-pattern overlaps, discordance and contradictions, not just independent percentages. It is not anybody's acquired psychological iceberg.

The central bridge is a **Position–Repertoire Homology and Conditional Redistribution Field**. It asks: how does location in the measured social-position space redistribute the likelihood of entire response repertoires? Where do these relationships intensify, dissolve, invert, intersect or cross boundaries that the researcher assumed would hold? What similarity is observed, what is a local effect, and what remains unknown? The output is a map of *possible response configurations conditional on positions*, not a statement that class or religion created someone's psychology.

Local relational geometry, multiple correspondence analysis where its measurement assumptions fit, categorical similarity, conditional-dependence methods, network/community analysis, full observed joints and interaction search can help discover these two spaces. None has the authority to invent a social class, validate an institutional relation or decide a semantic question. The methods discover structure in the admitted evidence; they do not grant structure an explanation.

**Discover locally before generalizing globally.** Different survey families measure different worlds. A source-specific position geometry may be legitimate even where there is no comparable universal embedding across 35 waves. Cross-source similarity is a later question that requires the appropriate comparison basis. We therefore begin with the strongest empirical object each available source can support, then ask what can be juxtaposed, compared or composed without inventing a universal synthetic respondent population.

This distinction organizes the rest of the machine: complete response morphologies occupy one evidentiary space, social and material positions the other, and conditional inference plus higher-order respondent co-travel make the relationship between them navigable.

### The observation field: a survey is itself a historically structured social encounter

Before asking what a population believes, the engine must know **which population can appear as an answerer under these conditions**, and what act of answering has actually been observed. A questionnaire is not a transparent aperture through which society automatically describes itself. It is an institutional encounter: somebody designed the categories and language, a sampling apparatus determined reachability, an interview mode made some answers easier or more costly, and respondents brought their own practical relations to disclosure, refusal and interpretation.

This observation structure is a real part of the data's meaning, not a later optional caveat. A worker employed informally, an undocumented migrant, a displaced household, a child excluded by age, a woman whose interview is overheard, or a person living under police intimidation may inhabit very different **conditions of observability**. Sometimes available evidence actually permits comparisons of response rates, item nonresponse, mode, language, coverage or subgroup eligibility. Sometimes it does not. The latter is an **observational shadow**, not evidence that excluded or silent people possess a particular belief.

**Input:** Source-defined population and coverage, sampling frame, interview mode and time, questionnaire language, administration and skip rules, response and participation opportunity, known nonresponse, survey weights and independently admitted external population information. **Operation:** Build a typed map of who was reachable, eligible, asked, observed, able to decline, actually recorded and represented by each statistical estimator. Compare observed participation and missingness patterns when the design permits. Identify where the questionnaire's own categories obscure distinctions or lack relevant responses. **Product:** An **Observation and Silence Field** attached to every empirical question: source-covered positive and negative claims, known excluded domains, differential nonresponse where actually measurable, conjectured but unverified silencing mechanisms, and the precise additional observation that would discriminate among explanations.

The engine should *not* claim that silence equals fear, that "none of these" necessarily means alienation, or that a powerless group is intrinsically voiceless. Nor should it convert every unobserved population into missing data requiring fabricated imputation. A strong sociological machine makes itself aware of its **selection mechanisms** and produces explicit proposals for new questions without confusing those proposals with answers. It can say: "The observed group has this repertoire; the survey does not allow us to characterize the group excluded from its frame. The observed refusal pattern is consistent with several explanations, none identified." This is knowledge about the reach of its knowledge.

The new power is reciprocal: when a discovered motif clusters around an unexpectedly high refusal or a strange residual category, the observation field becomes an **active object of inquiry**. The machine can challenge its apparent social-formation discovery by asking whether the pattern tracks administration, translation, applicability, response-option incompleteness or survey selection instead. If the challenge survives, the formation becomes better supported; if not, it corrects the ontology rather than propagating a flattering but false social type.

## III. GIVE EVERY QUESTION ITS COMPLETE RESPONSE MORPHOLOGY

A conventional analysis begins by choosing a dependent variable and one favored outcome. Ours first asks what the question permits people to say and what the observed population actually did say.

**Operation:** For each valid question and risk set, recover its full response distribution, the spread or concentration of answers, its observed zeros, unknowns, refusals and missingness, and the meaning of its categories. Recognize distinct kinds of measurement: a single categorical selection, ordinal response, numerical scale, multiple overlapping selections, rank ordering, list of behaviors or composite battery.

For a multi-select question, do not pretend the options are exclusive. Recover both the incidence of each option and the combinations respondents actually selected. For an ordinal item, preserve its authorized order and direction rather than using arbitrary code numbers. For a question whose categories may not exhaust people's lived grammars, keep the residual responses available without assigning them invented motives.

**Product:** A **Response Morphology** for each exact question-in-world: the answer space, observed frequencies, complete pattern structure when supported, concentration/fragmentation, meaningful category relations and the limits of the measure.

**Why:** We cannot discover minorities or contradictions by first deleting every outcome except the mode. We cannot discover overlapping interpretive frames if the data model requires exactly one answer. A source response can be a selection of multiple frames, an ambivalent rating, or a refusal to accept the offered choices.

**Next:** Ask which social and material positions differentiate the entire morphology—not simply which feature best predicts one selected answer.

### Responses are acts, not unmediated interior states

The observable object is often a **reported position under a particular instrument**, rather than a transparent belief stored inside a person. Agreement with a principle, recognition of an injustice, support for an institution, recollection of a practice, an intended vote and an observed action are different evidentiary kinds. Even "what is the main basis of discrimination?" measures a respondent's selection among offered ways of naming discrimination at that moment; it does not directly measure the objective causal composition of discrimination, nor the whole psychological reality through which that person interprets it.

A response morphology should therefore organize **expressive possibilities** as well as answer values: selection, omission, multi-frame composition, ranked priority, degree, hesitation if observed, contestation of the question's frame, report of one's own conduct, report about others and missing/no-access states. Do not manufacture hesitation or lived ambiguity when the instrument did not record it. But do not flatten an actual multi-response or norm/conduct differentiation into a single trait. The engine's downstream value partly depends on making such distinctions available before a hypothetical-person constructor mistakes a survey response for an acquired, all-context disposition.

**Product added to the response layer:** a **Response-Act Grammar** that states the target of each observation (self-report, judgment of society, experienced event, hypothetical intention, enacted practice, factual condition), its available categorical/ordinal/multi-answer topology, and what is *not* observed. This typed grammar is what permits meaningful cross-question relational discovery later.

## IV. DISCOVER THE CONDITIONAL TOPOGRAPHY OF EVERY MEASURED POSSIBILITY

For every meaningful response or observed configuration, search across the governed social and material dimensions that might condition it: income, labor location, education, gender, religion as measured, location, age, citizenship, institutional affiliation, household condition, political relation and other authorized variables. These are not all equally objective or equally meaningful; their roles remain typed.

**Operation:** Compare conditional response distributions across positions. Recover the differences between \(P(Y)\) and \(P(Y\mid X=x)\), across values of X, and across jointly observed conditions. Record what changes, how much, for whom, under what risk set, and by which admitted statistical lens. Preserve absolute prevalences as well as contrasts: a large proportional increase in a tiny category is different from a small change affecting half the population.

Seek multiple kinds of structure: gradients, thresholds, sharp divisions, nonlinear patterns, concentration, diffusion, polarization, near-universality, almost-disappearance and reversals. A conditional effect may be small globally but decisive in one local region of the population.

**Product:** A **Conditional Topography**: a collection of evidence-bearing maps of how whole response distributions shift across actual observed positions and contexts, with exact counts, legitimate measures, sample and design uncertainty, and no false statement of causal force.

**Why:** Two populations can have the same overall percentage and radically different positional structure. A response can be common because it occurs across many social worlds, or because it is nearly universal in one large subgroup while rare elsewhere. These are different social facts.

### Determinicity as a family of useful questions, not one strength metric

The conditional topography must be able to return several **noninterchangeable descriptions of effect**. It may calculate change in the chance of each outcome, reduction in uncertainty of the whole distribution, distributional separation, odds or risk contrasts where meaningful, ranked response shifts, and altered minority support. In a multinomial or multi-label outcome, a predictor might substantially reorganize the alternatives while leaving the majority's prevalence nearly unchanged. A different predictor might move the majority a little while radically affecting only one small, source-supported minority.

It must therefore distinguish *explanatory reach* from *predictive utility*, *distributional difference* from *causal force*, *global average* from *local interaction*, and *prevalence change* from *the shape of a person's possible answer combinations*. Statistical choices depend on the measured variable, admissible population and intended scientific claim; there should be no universal "strongest predictor" whose score erases all the competing dimensions.

A particularly important result is the **conditional stability or fragility of a relationship**. Does the position effect persist under reasonable additional conditions? Does it reverse? Does it cease to distinguish alternatives in held-out cases? Is the apparent signal generated by high-cardinality splitting or a survey design artifact? Does a minority remain patterned after the main contrast is accounted for? The engine must not treat the same data-mined discovery and its training-set accuracy as independent corroboration. It should provide a disciplined claim about what was learned *at the grain actually measured*, not claim future-person predictability or population-causal truth from a seductive fitted number.
**Next:** Turn the question around.

## V. REVERSE THE INFERENCE: FROM A POSITION TO AN ATTITUDE, AND FROM AN ATTITUDE BACK TO POSSIBLE POSITIONS

Rawlsing must never be trapped in the direction chosen by the original question writer. We must be able to ask both *what tends to be thought from here?* and *where in the measured social space does this way of thinking tend to appear?*

**Operation:** Permit lawful directional queries from observed position to response, response to position, response A to response B, multiple responses to candidate positions, and any admissible observed configuration to further measurements. Preserve the actual joint distribution and priors. A reverse conditional is not a careless inversion of a score: \(P(Y\mid X)\) and \(P(X\mid Y)\) answer different questions and must be computed over their proper denominators.

**Product:** The **Bidirectional Possibility Service**: position-conditioned, response-conditioned and configuration-conditioned distributions, each explicit about the population it describes and what can and cannot be inferred about a hypothetical individual.

**Why:** A particular attitude may be widespread among one group but mostly expressed by another because of different group sizes. Inference without base rates manufactures stereotypes. Reverse inquiry lets the data challenge our assumptions about what kind of social position "should" hold a particular conviction.

**Next:** Ask not merely where the general pattern is strongest, but where and how it **breaks**.

## VI. FIND THE FRACTURES: MAJORITY, EXIT, ALTERNATIVES AND THEIR OWN INTERNAL WORLDS

The majority is a first clue, not an ontological center. At any conditioned context we want to know how the prevailing pattern holds together, who departs from it, what the departing people actually choose, and whether those alternative configurations have their own social bases.

**Operation:** At each source-supported conditional context, discover candidate splitters. Examine the **majority-retention branch**, the **majority-exit branch**, each empirically distinct **alternative branch**, and **residual or category-refusal branches**. Reconsider predictors afresh within each branch. The best splitter at the root may cease to matter within a subgroup; an apparently weak predictor may become decisive after a second condition. A minority exit may itself fracture into several contrasting alternatives.

Do not constrain the search to two highest-frequency states and call all others noise. The machine may rationally allocate expensive follow-up unevenly, but it must preserve all observed alternatives and distinguish *not searched*, *searched without usable evidence*, and *empirically absent from the observed joint*. In multi-label data, an exit from one option can coexist with selection of another; presence and whole-response-pattern branches must coexist without false exclusive partitioning.

A branch must know what defines it, what respondents it describes, which responses remain available, how confident the contrasts are, what was withheld for low support, and what question would most usefully be asked next.

**Product:** The **Recursive Positional Fracture Field**: not one attractive tree diagram, but a searchable, reproducible structure of majorities, minority deviations, alternatives, changing splitters, nested subcultures, convergence, reversals and honest unknown frontiers.

**Why:** This is how the system discovers that "the working class thinks X" may conceal several historically distinct working-class worlds; that wealthy respondents are not homogeneous; that one minority response is socially patterned rather than random; and that a new material condition changes what the original predictor seemed to mean.

### A fracture is an opportunity to discover an unfamiliar social formation

The most powerful branch operation should not ask only which already-known demographic column explains an already-known answer. It should be able to nominate a **new empirical configuration worthy of study**: a bounded cluster of respondents who share a response pattern but whose apparent social unity has not yet been tested. Such a configuration is not immediately a social group, movement, class fraction or ideology. It is a *candidate formation*: a repeated difference from an existing distribution, possibly organized by several material positions and several attitudes.

The investigator can then test whether the candidate formation has a coherent response repertoire, stable conditional location, meaningful internal alternatives, cross-position boundaries, source-specific exclusion patterns or a comparable expression elsewhere. It may turn out to be a valid local social-pattern object, a statistical accident, an artifact of questionnaire routing or a false semantic grouping. Those are different discoveries. The machinery should retain the rejected candidate and what defeated it, not merely drop the node and rediscover it later.

In this way the machine need not assume that the culturally important divisions are precisely the categories the programmer placed in a driver taxonomy. It can discover **unexpected social cleavages, hybrid positions, cross-cutting blocs, internally contradictory repertoires and locally distinctive interpretations** as testable observational structures, while leaving causal sociological naming to the evidence capable of supporting it. A genuine new formation should be able to become the context of fresh bidirectional, minority, interaction and cloud queries without a custom human-written analysis script.
**Next:** Search for patterns that no single question or branch reveals.

The fracture-map must also be able to produce **a next-question** that is itself shaped by the branch: if the dominant response in a provincial labor stratum fragments by occupation, the system can nominate an occupation-conditioned multi-label pattern and retrieve it on the same governed risk set. A subsequent answer may require the system to revise its original branch priority, merge two proposed subcultures, or investigate a small retained exception. This is already the empirical discovery loop beginning to feed back into itself, not merely a final autonomous-explorer chapter waiting at the end.

## VII. DISCOVER HIGHER-ORDER INTERACTIONS AND HIDDEN CONFIGURATIONS

Some social possibilities do not announce themselves in any individual predictor. Two variables can each look uninformative alone and jointly become decisive; three conditions can expose a stable minority concealed by every pairwise table. In other contexts, a strong apparent interaction is an artifact of sparse, high-cardinality memorization.

**Operation:** Search beyond one-axis contrasts for conditional interactions, suppression, synergistic and redundant evidence, local reversals and configuration-specific effects. Reuse exact same-respondent joints, not independently reconstructed marginals. Challenge proposed findings with alternative partitions and models, including out-of-fold prediction where appropriate and licensed uncertainty methods where available. Track the opportunity set not searched due to budget.

**Product:** An **Interaction and Conditional Surprise Map**, identifying where the joint changes the prediction, where additive summaries lose real evidence, and where the apparent novelty disappears under validation.

**Why:** The person we want to generate lives among combinations of conditions, not in isolated column-wise demographic bins. A system that sees only main effects cannot recover complex social possibility even if every individual association is correctly calculated.

**Next:** Stop thinking only in the form of predictors and targets. Examine the configurations **inside respondents**.

## VIII. RECOVER ATTITUDE CO-TRAVEL, CLOUDS, REPERTOIRES AND COHERENT EXCEPTIONS

The **Attitude Cloud** is one of the central original inventions worth taking all the way. It is not a cluster of demographic traits disguised as beliefs, and not an LLM's intuition that two attitudes sound related. It is an empirically observed network or family of response combinations co-traveling **within the same respondents**.

**Operation:** For a response or disposition-like measurement, discover which other eligible attitudes, judgments, reported practices and commitments occur with it, which do not, and in which social contexts their co-occurrence changes. Search pairwise and higher-order patterns. Preserve complete and partially observed joints; preserve overlaps, joint minorities and opposing configurations. Compare clouds under different position conditions. Notice when two nominally opposing judgments coexist, when a small coalition of attitudes has a coherent social location, or when one apparently homogeneous cloud divides into different internal repertoires.

Demographic and material positions condition these clouds; they are not automatically members of the *attitude* cloud itself. Nor does repeated co-selection prove that one attitude causes, justifies or psychologically suppresses another.

**Product:** A set of **Respondent-Coherent Repertoire Fields**: whole-case configurations, conditional co-signatures, clouds, competing subclouds, rare coherent combinations, and observed contradictions at measured grain.

**Why:** An adult with a specific material situation need not be a random sample of separate attitudes. Actual respondents combine commitments in surprising ways; those combinations, with their prevalence and alternatives, are vital to generating plausible variation without ideological stereotypes.

**Next:** Ask where these repertoires live in the wider social space and how their relations vary.

### Co-occurrence is only the beginning: discover typed relationships among principles, applications, conduct, costs and targets

A respondent-coherent attitude cloud says that measurements co-travel. But there is a far more powerful question: **what kind of relationship do the measurements themselves express?** Two jointly selected opinions may be mere companions, one may be the concrete application of a principle the other expresses, one may be a proposed means to a stated end, or they may describe the same rule as applied to different social targets. Their *pairing* can carry information that neither standalone answer nor numerical correlation captures.

The older O10/S14/S15 structural-grammar aspiration made this explicit. The forward design should recover, with governed semantic roles, at least these *possible analytic questions*: **principle ↔ application; norm ↔ reported conduct; goal ↔ means; public commitment ↔ personal cost; universal declaration ↔ target selectivity; rule ↔ exception; claimed duty ↔ reciprocal obligation; prescribed loyalty ↔ competing commitment**. Some require two responses by the same person; some compare source-observed distributions across people or settings; some would require observations that a survey simply lacks. The analytic operation must know which it is doing.

**Operation:** A qualified semantic authority identifies which measured propositions have a legitimate typed relation. The empirical engine then performs the actual same-unit co-administration and joint checks, conditions on observed positions, and discovers patterns of consistency, asymmetry, reversals, conditional selectivity, diffuse connection and apparent contradiction. It separately tests whether a relation depends on a particular target or practical cost. The result is not an arithmetic "hypocrisy" or "moral worth" score: a person can consistently hold two responses because the questions have different objects, or a social contradiction may be real without every respondent experiencing it as psychological conflict.

**Product:** A **Typed Repertoire-Relation Field**: which patterns coexist, which logical or practical relation an authorized meaning analysis proposes, which social positions carry each configuration, which limits belong to sampling/semantics, and which new contrast could falsify the interpretation. A compelling finding would be that an apparently universal norm is expressed differently across specifically named targets under a fixed position condition, *if the survey actually administered all those questions*. The engine can discover such selective application without inferring an unseen motive.

**Why this precedes structural interpretation:** A field's ideological contradictions are not recoverable from bare marginal distributions. Yet assigning them unilaterally from moral theory would also be false. The typed joint grammar gives Field Forge precise candidates to evaluate against independent institutions, conflicts and material relations.

## IX. CONSTRUCT THE POSITION–DISPOSITION LANDSCAPE

A cloud tells us what travels together. A conditional topography tells us how responses vary by position. Now we need to understand the *geometry* of both: which positions are empirically near or distant under a declared comparison, where multiple repertoires overlap, where their boundaries are sharp, and which subcultures are simultaneously similar on one axis and antagonistic on another.

**Operation:** Construct admissible position and response geometries from exact measured dimensions and declared distance, similarity or neighborhood rules. Treat geography, class, migration, education, communal membership and institutional role as distinct, potentially overlapping dimensions. Identify **concentrations, boundaries, bridges, cross-position reach, isolated clusters, sharp transitions, internal heterogeneity and minority penetrations**.

A proximity score never automatically establishes a social relation, shared fate, political solidarity, historical homology or latent causal pathway. Geometry is a means of discovering comparative structure and proposing further questions, not a metaphysical map of society.

**Product:** A **Position–Disposition Geometry** with multiple lawful views: actual empirical neighborhoods, repertoire concentrations, relational contrasts and uncertainty. No compulsory universal embedding or compulsory nine-stratum schema.

**Why:** We need to know not only that one configuration is common among a group, but whether it is confined, widespread, situated at a boundary, common across opposed material positions, or locally unique.



### The material scale of a field is not a fixed list of demographic variables

A class relation can be locally mediated by an employer, landlord, union, family dependency, migration status, school, military institution or communal organization. A national law can change a person's local opportunities without being the best predictor of their expressed attitudes in a particular survey. A religious institution may be powerful at one geographic grain and marginal at another. The old planetary → imperial block → nation → region → sect/class → party → neighborhood → family → self conception was trying to express **nested and adjacent social pressure**, not an injunction to force every social fact into nine immutable bins.

The better machine should preserve the **scales and sites at which an empirically observed condition exists**, and later the scales and channels of its independently constituted structural meaning. A respondent-based association has a measured question universe. A geographic institutional relation has a different kind of existence and coverage. A social network position may cross territorial divisions. A class relation is neither reducible to household income nor automatically recoverable from an occupational label. These objects should be able to meet in one actor-independent field only through a lawful relation, never because they share a misleadingly familiar category name.

The goal is a **multi-grain possibility topology**. One disposition may be diffuse nationally but concentrated in a bounded locality; one source-defined grouping may show a high response rate but cross-cut several material classes; two geographically close neighborhoods can occupy different institutional opportunity spaces; a historical border can reorganize the plausible conditions of everyday life. Geometric similarity, social connection, territorial overlap and structurally comparable position must therefore be different questions. As the discoveries advance, even the relevant boundaries of the field may need to be revised.

### Material positions are relations, not merely coordinates on a demographic scatterplot

The old Rawlsing ambition cannot be realized by converting census-like variables into an ontology of class. Two people with the same income may occupy opposite relations to ownership, wage dependence, supervision, property, debt, household care, state employment or migration. The same nominal education level can have a different social meaning under different regional labor markets, social hierarchies and historical ruptures. A measured demographic category is one *possible indicator* of a materially constituted position, and sometimes a bad one.

A more ambitious field must allow a position to be constituted from **separately observed or independently warranted relations**: who controls productive resources, who must sell labor power, who supervises whose work, who appropriates or provides care, who is exposed to state coercion, who bears the costs of social reproduction, and who controls entry into institutions, employment, land or housing. Some of these will be wholly absent from a given survey. The absence should generate a *question about the missing relation*, not an invented class label attached to a coarse income bin.

The machine should be able to compare a response *by* such positions when they are legitimately constructed, and also expose where an ordinary demographic proxy causes spurious apparent uniformity. Its product is not a universally privileged class fraction taxonomy, but the capacity to represent **relational power and dependency as distinct from proximity in the survey feature space**. Where the material relation cannot be established, the output remains an observed demographic conditional with a visible limitation rather than taking on a stronger name.

This makes the question "for whom, at what cost, to whom?" a set of possible empirical and structural inquiries rather than a rhetorical flourish. It asks who benefits from an institutional arrangement, who supplies the necessary labor and resources, who absorbs its externalized costs, and where the survey can or cannot measure those divisions. These may be discovered at household, workplace, regional, national and transnational scales and may be linked by one material relation rather than located in one fixed spatial stratum.

### The empirical motif atlas: a discovery object before sociological naming

The machine should identify **empirical motifs**: reproducible, bounded configurations of positions, respondent-joint repertoires, conditional fractures, co-signatures and interactions that deserve further examination. A motif is not a class fraction, ideology, movement, personality archetype or pressure mechanism until independent interpretation warrants that stronger status. It is a *measured pattern in a particular source world*, capable of having its own history of discovery.

One motif might be an unexpected minority cloud concentrated among people whose education and labor positions differ. Another might connect respondents across class fractions on one belief but sharply divide them on another. A third might be a three-variable interaction missed by every pairwise association. A fourth could reveal that a supposedly uniform demographic grouping contains several irreducibly different configurations. None should be deleted merely because it does not resemble a familiar social category.

The atlas must retain each motif's exact measured components, contextual population, whole-case evidence where available, internal alternatives, material-position distribution, prevalence, surprise, underpowered states, rivals, falsification attempts and the part of the search opportunity actually examined. A motif may split when a new condition is supplied, merge when a misleading category is corrected, persist as a minority, or be rejected as an artifact. Its transition history is information.

This is where **branches, clouds, conditional geometry and higher-order interactions become one research instrument** rather than four unrelated products. The autonomous explorer can investigate whether a motif is robust, whether it persists across legitimate context shifts, and whether its interpretation requires a new concept. The key operation is the discovery of **a social pattern the designer did not already know how to name**. A justified sociological name may follow; the empirical discovery does not require one to exist.

### Field-relative capital: resources only acquire value inside actual relations

Material and symbolic resources do not possess one permanent amount of social power. The practical value of a credential, wealth, occupation, language, style, organizational membership, reputation or kin connection depends on the *field* and *historical moment* in which it can be recognized or converted. A resource can command prestige in one religious or professional milieu and little in another, facilitate work but not citizenship, support local security while preventing national advancement, or lose its value after displacement or institutional collapse.

The system therefore needs a **Field-Specific Capital Value and Conversion** concept: which resources are at issue; where they are recognized; what access, status or capacity they can be converted into; which institutions and gatekeepers govern conversion; which histories alter it; and where its empirical basis is merely a proxy. Capital should also be described by **volume, composition and trajectory**, rather than a flat income-based rank. This allows differently constituted middle-class positions, localized prestige, and opposing forms of social advantage to remain distinguishable.

An observed education–attitude association does not itself prove that education converts into power. Surveys may measure income and schooling while leaving actual property control, managerial authority, care dependencies, family capital and institutional recognition partly unobserved. We must use the fixed available evidence as far as it legitimately reaches, then show which resource relations need independent already-existing structural authority. We do not create a new-source procurement project to satisfy theoretical elegance, and we do not invent missing dimensions.

This changes the meaning of **subhegemony**. A repertoire can be widely respected within a bounded community but unable to convert its local symbolic authority into national, professional or political power. Another can be narrow in prevalence yet highly convertible into institutional access. The first may be locally dominant and externally subordinate; the second may be nationally marginal but resource-aligned. The field must represent both without treating prevalence, prestige and power as the same number.

Position–repertoire homology, empirical motifs, field-relative resources and conversion together offer a much more capable alternative to a compulsory nine-strata lookup table. The old strata remain one optional view of the underlying, context-responsive relational topology.

**Next:** Bring the purely empirical geometry to the separately authorized social-world constitution process, where its possible relation to power can be examined.

### Concept birth: discover candidate social kinds rather than endlessly sort people into inherited categories

The hardest original ambition was not to describe an already named subgroup. It was for the machine to **discover formations its designers had not anticipated**. This cannot be fulfilled by choosing a top splitter, assigning a label like "sectarian subculture," and repeating. Nor does it require the software to pronounce a new objective class or community whenever some statistical cluster appears.

The creative empirical move begins with **an unnamed respondent-supported motif**: a combination of observed answers, response-act relations, a conditional position geometry, unusual minorities, differentiated membership and an actual support/stability profile. An explorative model or search algorithm may detect a motif because a three-way interaction is not reducible to pairwise relations; because two supposedly antagonistic principles coexist; because a global minority becomes a local majority; or because the same pattern appears across social positions which the initial ontology treated as separate.

The next move is **concept formation under challenge**. The machine proposes a candidate description of the motif and its differentiating features. Then it tries to defeat the candidate: does it survive alternative cuts, a change of target response, leave-one-context-out tests, valid design sensitivity, observed countercases, or an entirely different authorized measure of the proposed concept? Is it a stable collective configuration, an artifact of routing, a dense intersection of already known conditions, a questionnaire response style, a mixture of two distinct groups, or a temporary statistical accident? Where no test is possible, it remains a bounded descriptive candidate rather than a recognized social kind.

The machine must preserve **overlapping, nested, cross-cutting and discontinuous membership**. A person may occupy several institutional positions and express several repertoires. A social formation may have porous membership, a core and periphery, and an exception field; it is not automatically a mutually exclusive latent class. A recursive tree can **discover** a context, but the resulting possibility space may be better represented as an overlapping family of intersections and relations. Branches are exploratory paths, not metaphysical partitions.

**Product:** An **Empirical Concept Candidate** consisting of a reproducible observed signature, respondent universe, admissible description, positive and counterexamples, social-position distribution, validated or unresolved differentiation, open tests and explicit claims it cannot support. An LLM can propose an illuminating tentative name or competing interpretations, but cannot turn its interpretation into source fact. A human/source-adjudicated sociological concept can be associated with the candidate later; the statistical motif itself continues to exist even if its proposed name is rejected.

Now new concepts can drive new work. A concept candidate can become a conditioning object for further multi-label branch searches, a target of reverse position inference, a comparator across waves, a question to Field Forge, and a fresh alternative in adult possibility exploration. **The epistemic ontology is allowed to grow, be corrected, split or merge**, while the immutable observed evidence and original rejected interpretations remain distinguishable. That is genuine self-expanding empirical sociology: not endless automatic tabulation, but an engine that can create and contest the *terms* of its next inquiry.

## X. BUILD PROFILES OF HEGEMONY, COUNTERHEGEMONY, SUBHEGEMONY AND SOCIAL COMMON SENSE

A view can be widespread without being hegemonic. A locally dominant repertoire can be nationally subordinate; a prestige-laden conception may be numerically rare; a politically empowered group may hold an internal diversity of views. The theory needs these distinctions because Rawlsing is about historically structured people, not a political opinion leaderboard.

**Operation:** For an observed complex or repertoire, ask separately about population prevalence, reach across position space, concentration in high-resource measured positions, independently grounded institutional or political-power alignment, geographic reach, temporal persistence where comparable, and stable countercomplexes. Where evidence supports it, examine how repertories gain prestige, travel across institutional boundaries, lose convertibility, remain localized, or coexist with antagonistic common senses.

Do **not** infer ruling-class status from high income alone or treat every national minority as subaltern. Do **not** infer a theory of domination from category frequency. These are evidence profiles supporting later governed interpretations.

**Product:** **Plural Hegemony and Social-Repertoire Evidence Profiles**: locally dominant, broadly diffuse, power-aligned, regionally bounded, contested, counterposed and unresolved complexes, often coexisting in the same society. Before Field Forge admission, these are **candidate empirical indicators and open questions**, not already-established relations of institutional hegemony. It is the presence of additional governed social-world evidence—not the rhetorical intensity of the name—that permits a stronger claim.

**Why:** This gives Field Forge the distinction between what an average respondent says and what a society's institutions may normalize, reward, stigmatize or impose—without pretending surveys already measure all those institutional relations.

**Next:** Introduce independently evidenced institutions, material constraints and historical power.

## XI. FIELD FORGE: FROM EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY TO THE SOCIALLY CONSTITUTED FIELD

The strongest next question is no longer "what predicts Y?" It is **what becomes possible, facilitated, inhibited, costly, rewarded, stigmatized or foreclosed for differently positioned people—and through which material relations?**

**Architectural location:** This is the **replacement for the old nine-strata/inter-strata-pressure representation**, not a new name for DDM and not a direct source-data analysis stage. The upstream DDM must already have produced the governed response structures, co-signatures, recursive branches, joints, minority configurations and learned empirical objects available to Field Forge. Field Forge may descend to source facts for evidentiary canaries, but **an epistemic ability to derive a relation from respondents is not proof that source → Field Forge is the desirable production insertion point**. The proper production seam is the current legitimate output of the pre-strata empirical machine; the older respondent sources remain the ultimate proof floor.

**Operation:** Field Forge receives the authorized empirical possibility objects and, where independently governed evidence permits, composes them with material positions, institutions, resources, geographical arrangements, collective organization, political and economic power, cultural legitimacy and historical events. It distinguishes a **measured statistical contrast** from an **interpreted structural pressure**, a **specific institutional channel**, an **asserted causal mechanism**, and a **downstream actor's experienced response**.

A source-observed contrast does not make its own structural rule. The higher-order interpretation must say what independent fact or rule licenses claims about access, coercion, bargaining power, social reproduction, gatekeeping, resource conversion, institutional sanction or historical contradiction. Some currently known empirical results may enter this process only as exploratory evidence, and must be allowed to remain useful at that weaker grade.

**Product:** A **Material–Relational Field of Situated Possibilities** whose elements can preserve separate dimensions of opportunity, constraint, resource pressure, legitimacy, domination, competing normative orders, structural mediation and unresolved questions. Components need not collapse into one signed pressure scalar.

**Why:** Social position and belief can correlate without any particular mechanism; mechanisms can act differently across contexts; institutions have consequences even where a survey did not ask about them. This is where empirical regularities can be related to a historical world rather than left as a numerical social taxonomy.



### Several measured universes, one historical world—never one invented respondent population

The field to be constituted may be informed simultaneously by distinct survey families, source periods, regional populations, institutional studies and independently observed material conditions. That does not make their samples commensurable. Two questionnaires may ask about a related political value in different words; one source may measure household circumstances while another asks about political participation; a cross-national project may be sampled and weighted differently from a regional series. Even where the same proposition can be related, the actual respondent identity, answer choices, eligible universe, comparability and estimand do not move automatically from one source to the other.

A maximally capable architecture should preserve **source-local empirical worlds as plural contributors**. It should be able to ask whether two admitted source fields agree at a legitimately comparable grain, disagree for substantive historical reasons, expose a measurement mismatch or simply remain noncomparable. Each can inform a distinct aspect of the actor-independent world without ever manufacturing a survey person who answered questions in both. A spatially specific labor relation and a national political-attitude distribution might both be relevant to a fictional locality; their combination is a *world-composition problem*, not a larger respondent joint.

The product should be a **multi-universe empirical contribution field**: separate evidentiary supports, explicit scope of allowed comparison, contrasting or mutually reinforcing claims, residual gaps and the exact roles each source is permitted to play. Field Forge can then admit actor-independent structural relations at its own grain, with the originating surveys still available as distinct derivation floors. This is stronger than trying to find one super-survey whose synthetic respondents contain all of society. It also preserves the ability to learn that two sources offer conflicting pictures rather than smoothing their difference out of existence.

### Structure, power, pressure and living flow must remain four different things

The historical pressure-flow vision distinguished **structure** (reproduced relations that organize a world), **power** (a relation's capacity to set terms, allocate resources, command legitimacy and make alternatives costly), **pressure** (a historically and positionally specific demand, constraint, sanction, inducement or opportunity) and **flow** (the living organism's embodied need, attention, desire and activity as those conditions are actually encountered). A survey estimate is none of the last three by itself. The aim of the empirical–structural machine is to build the first three *to the extent that independent facts and authority permit* and provide the objective conditions through which the fourth can later occur. Sorok, not PYAT, owns the realized organism and what its flow becomes.

**Power must be able to appear as provision as well as prohibition.** A landlord can withhold housing, a state can fund medical care, an employer can command time, a school can recognize qualifications, a church or union can distribute belonging, prestige, discipline and material assistance. A resource may enable survival while requiring compliance. A socially valued norm may be reinforced through ridicule, advancement, debt, shame or exclusion without the direct threat of police. A weaker agent may exercise counterpower through refusal, evasion, mutual aid, collective organization or institutional leverage. These are distinctive *relations and possible operations*, not interchangeable intensities on a single domination scale.

Pressure is **conditional and directed**. It may operate only on a specific category of people, through one institution, upon one target of life (employment, housing, citizenship, care, sexuality, bodily safety, association, political speech), under a particular historical rule. For each admitted pressure the field should be able to ask what establishes the relation, where it applies, whom it empowers, what alternatives it forecloses or opens, what material costs it imposes, what channels can carry it and which other pressures can weaken or amplify it. A bare country average cannot perform this function.

The resulting **objective affordance/pressure footprint** describes what the world offers or makes costly to someone in a legitimate position *before a particular actor thinks anything about it*. A second, separate **encounter eligibility** question asks which institutions, media, caregivers, work arrangements, languages and events could actually become available along a life route. Only a third and downstream **realized actor exposure and interpretation** can answer what this particular person encountered, noticed, misunderstood, accepted, refused or internalized. This three-way split is essential: an institution's objective availability is not an actor's exposure, and exposure is not internal psychic organization.

A capable Field Forge and post-Forge context producer must also allow pressures to interact **downward, upward and laterally**, rather than just falling from "planetary" to "individual." National economic policy can squeeze local work; household labor and migration can reproduce or reorganize regional labor markets; a movement can alter state terms; a religious carrier can amplify one class norm and contest another; a local employer can mediate international commodity dependence. It is the *conditional relation across scales*, not a prefabricated nine-slot structure, that carries the relevant explanation. The original strata remain useful as prompts to look for otherwise forgotten scales; they do not control the ontology.

### Carriers are transmission relations, not packages of ideological content

A structural field becomes part of everyday life through **actual carriers**: families, schools, workplaces, housing markets, local political organizations, religious institutions, media systems, migrant networks, bureaucracies, militias, unions and social services. A carrier has its own resources, governing practices, speech opportunities, discipline, contradictions, geographic reach and target populations. It may oppose one institution while reproducing another's norms; it may give a child safety while transmitting obedience; it may teach universal values while restricting participation through class. A school is not simply a "liberal" node. A family is not simply a "conservative" node. The same carrier can bear conflicting possibilities.

For our empirical-social machine, the positive work is to constitute where those carriers are materially present and what **opportunities, demands and limits** they objectively mediate, when independently warranted, and to estimate the socially patterned repertoires available around them when actual surveys measure those distributions. An attitudinal answer may be evidence about someone's reported relationship to a carrier; it is not evidence that the carrier actually indoctrinated them. We should distinguish *carrier existence*, *access/eligibility*, *encounter opportunity*, *institutional practice*, *survey-observed repertoire*, and *actor-acquired framework*.

The payoff is enormous for a historically situated generative world. Sorok need not receive an unexplained tag saying that a child is "religious" or "working-class conscious." It can receive a structured, actor-independent world in which particular carriers, power relations, opportunities and socially available interpretive repertoires exist. Whether the child encounters those carriers, how they satisfy or frustrate needs, and what psychological organization emerges remain matters for the actual continuing organism. Objective richness increases potential psychological diversity precisely because it **does not prescribe the actor's destination**.

### Pressure footprint versus pressure mechanism: the required two-stage translation

For the purpose of constructing historically situated hypothetical persons, Rawlsing does not need a complete theory explaining society before it can represent socially patterned possibilities. It needs an **Empirical Positional Pressure Footprint**: the source-supported redistribution of probability over a measured response/repertoire space conditional on legitimate material and social position C and historical context K. Its positive product is how the available empirical outcomes move, including minority alternatives and reversals. It is a description of conditioned possibility, not a material cause or an actor's felt pressure.

A **Structural Pressure Mechanism** is something stronger and different: an independently grounded account of how ownership, citizenship, work, schooling, coercion, provision, resource conversion, institutions or other actual relations change opportunities, risks, rewards, sanctions, protections or legitimate standing. A respondent association may suggest several such mechanisms but cannot select its own explanation merely by being statistically strong. Likewise, a real institutional rule can exist without a survey asking respondents whether they approve of it.

The machine must be able to keep both objects available, compose them only where the relation is warranted, and treat mismatches as further research questions. A plausible mechanism that seems inconsistent with source-backed respondents can generate a new conditional inquiry into mediating conditions or measurement; the mechanism may not correct the survey answers, and the survey contrast cannot automatically falsify an explanation of a different causal target. This is the essential middle object connecting computational sociology with independent material-world construction.

### Semantic intelligence: pre-adjudicate difficult meaning, automate the operations it licenses

A major engineering discovery was that certain finite meaning decisions were better made once by high-quality, bounded agentic adjudication and recorded as reusable authority than continually guessed by runtime heuristics. The direction of a response scale, source item meaning, relation-role legitimacy, construct mapping, measurement portability and lawful structural promotion are not routine numerical operations. Yet once governed, they can support extraordinarily broad automatic computation.

Thus divide the work: **mechanical operations** can be deterministic; **descriptive empirical computation** can explore already-admitted meanings; **finite consequential semantic judgments** can be adjudicated into versioned reusable authority banks; **open-ended interpretation** can be nominated by reasoning models but not automatically promoted to facts. Precomputing semantics does not mean precomputing every possible empirical motif: the discovery of new motifs and questions remains open-ended within the legitimate source and measurement surface.

This avoids twin failures: a sophisticated yet false world generated by automatic meaning guesses, and an inert encyclopedia in which every imaginable sociological pattern must have been written down before the machine can discover anything. The best system conserves expensive reasoning as dependable data, then uses cheap, wide computation to discover patterns no human had predeclared. A later verified source correction can revise the authority and invalidate the affected downstream knowledge without destroying the other accepted facts.


**Next:** Produce the actor-independent **structural world and relation field** in whatever smallest competent object forms suffice, then submit it to the **distinct downstream post-Field-Forge layer** before PRE-V3. Not all empirical possibilities require structural promotion: legitimate source-observed co-travel can remain consumable without a special structural relation, and a rejected causal interpretation must not erase the descriptive observation.

### The indispensable downstream after Field Forge

The Field Forge output itself is **not yet resolved historical pressure in a fictional person's circumstances**. The original design interposed structural/pressure/temporal/linking machinery between the new structural world and the PRE-V3 context. This additional machinery must be able to apply authored structural claims at specified times, places, institutional scales, relevant material relations and target position-grains; distinguish baseline pressure from a contingent event; combine competing heterogeneous components without illegitimate scalarization; represent channels by which resources and sanctions operate; and carry temporal change, exposure relevance and uncertainty to the final actor-independent context. No preexisting concrete implementation is assumed complete merely because it shares these names. But the *functions* cannot be silently skipped.

What Field Forge constitutes is an actor-independent **social world**. What the post-Forge machinery resolves is the **situational pressure/constraint/opportunity field for a specified historical setting**. What PRE-V3 hands off is a **historically conditioned possibility and exposure context**, never an actor's acquired belief, remembered event or chosen action. All three boundaries have separate meanings and potentially separate source/owner permissions; they can be implemented through direct typed objects rather than a mandatory mega-packet.


### Plural, contradictory social repertoires are not yet an actor's organization

A person can live among several partially incompatible repertoires—professional, familial, religious, communal, national, political, artistic and personal. The empirical machine should never assume one culturally or materially indexed "habitus" must explain every response. It must permit **plural frameworks and context-specific applicability**, including circumstances in which a respondent expresses a dominant view in one domain and a counterpattern in another. A repertoire may circulate across opposed material positions; a single position may make several competing repertoires accessible. It is the empirical breadth and conditional coexistence of these possibilities that our machine should discover.

Here the conceptual contribution of plural-disposition sociology is essential, though PYAT's measurement ceiling remains strict. A respondent's inconsistent answers are not automatically enduring mental compartments, and different answers in different waves are not the same person's developmental change. What can be measured is **co-travel, conditional divergence, within-instrument response tension, group-specific availability and cross-context recurrence**. Those are useful inferences about socially available interpretive materials. They should be served as distinct competing alternatives for actor-independent contexts, not installed as a fictional individual's inner architecture.

This gives the larger project a different kind of richness: Sorok may encounter a local institution whose available moral vocabulary contradicts what another institution makes normal, while the surrounding survey field demonstrates that both repertoires are socially represented. A particular developing actor can then selectively attend, resist, compartmentalize, synthesize, or ignore them through lived events. The actual organization belongs to the continuing psychology; the **plural opportunities for organizing meaning** belong to the empirical-social world.

### The limit-case of ultimate meaning: an evidence horizon, not a moral-depth score

Historical Rawlsing distinguished the multiple finite claims of family, class, nation, institution and community from a possible **Absolute Horizon**: the evolving relation to what a person treats as finally binding, through which finite loyalties may be absolutized, questioned or relativized. The important implication for the empirical and structural world is **not** that PYAT should model an actor's spirituality. It is that the questions of meaning, justice, sacrifice, ultimate allegiance and normativity cannot be reduced to religious affiliation, church attendance, abstract moral depth or the average value of one survey item.

Some source questions may measure parts of an available moral vocabulary, religious identification, norm/authority judgments, the relative importance of work and family, reported conceptions of meaning or willingness to bear social costs. The source authority may warrant such **measured normative and interpretive opportunities**. It cannot thereby establish how an individual ultimately orders their loyalties or whether they have achieved moral lucidity, alienation, self-transcendence, resentment or devotion. Those are actor-specific historical formations.

The stronger field should consequently preserve the **plurality of socially available ultimate claims** and the actual institutions that carry them, including their points of contention. It should be able to represent a movement whose normative commitments compete with those of a state, a family whose reproductive demands conflict with a profession, or a religious carrier whose principle of solidarity can criticize its own institutional power. It must not equate social prevalence with truth or declare the actor's response in advance.

The most provocative generative possibility lies here: **the social forms through which somebody encounters a norm may later become subject to that norm's own criticism**. An actor may learn justice through an institution and eventually judge the institution by justice. The material world can make such a conflict possible and costly; the empirical field can describe the distribution of the relevant commitments; Sorok must construct whether, how, and at what cost that particular life develops the capacity to make the judgment. The boundary protects the very freedom from stereotype that the original project sought.


## XII. MAKE CONTRADICTION, COMPETING PRESSURES AND ALTERNATIVE FUTURES FIRST-CLASS

Contradiction is not one universal negative number. It can mean the coexistence of responses that a simple ideology model expects to be exclusive, conflicting material incentives, divergent institutional demands, competing ways of interpreting an event, or pressures that pull a social position toward mutually incompatible actions.

**Operation:** Distinguish **observed response co-presence**, **semantic tension**, **material/structural antagonism**, **institutional friction**, **competing field pressures** and **actual actor-internal organization**. PYAT and the actor-independent Field Forge can discover and qualify the earlier layers; Sorok owns how those pressures become psychologically organized in a particular life.

For each relevant contrast, preserve the opposing possibilities and the conditions in which they operate. A person-like configuration may be socially possible without being statistically modal; a repertoire may be locally coherent despite being nationally unusual; a material position may make several mutually antagonistic responses simultaneously plausible. What appears contradictory in a formal typology may be ordinary coherence within a specific historical situation.

**Product:** A **Plural Contradiction and Pressure Landscape**: distinguishable, evidentially qualified tensions, coexistences and alternative trajectories, not a program for assigning everyone false consciousness.

**Why:** This is the basis of a nonreified historical subject. A developing person may later organize, repress, justify, sustain or transform these contradictions—but that psychological outcome cannot be hard-coded from their survey prevalence.

**Next:** Move the field through time without faking comparability or continuity.

## XIII. LEARN SOCIAL HISTORIES, NOT JUST CROSS-SECTIONS

Historical possibility changes. Positions, institutions, cultural meanings, prevalence, branch boundaries and repertoires move. But comparing waves is not simply subtracting two percentages.

**Operation:** Maintain source-local historical fields and, where exact measurement and population comparability exist, discover changes in distributions, positional relationships, cloud membership, minority bases, hegemony dimensions, structural pressures and conditional reversals. Distinguish actual trend, changing question wording, changing sample coverage, category drift, demographic reweighting and unobserved mechanisms. Do not transport across waves merely because two labels sound similar.

Where sources cannot be harmonized, preserve several distinct temporal witnesses and their disagreement rather than invent one universal chronological curve. A time- and place-bounded repertoire cannot be silently used as the default for a person born into an earlier period.

**Rupture is not interpolation noise.** War, occupation, genocide, sanctions, coups, mass displacement, economic collapse and institutional reorganization can alter what a position is, which populations are reachable, what speech is safe and which choices even exist. A temporal layer must therefore retain source-wave distance, what change is merely interpolated, the confidence penalty of that interpolation, independently authorized event overrides and any discontinuity that defeats smooth modeling. Where the rupture's effect is not measured, the event belongs to historically grounded context with uncertainty—not to a magically reconstructed time series of survey answers. A person passing through a rupture is a problem for Sorok's lived history; the empirical system can supply the changing historical field but cannot manufacture that person's experience.

**Product:** **Historically Indexed Possibility Fields** and explicit **Change/Noncomparability Profiles**: what is observed at each period; what changes are legitimately inferable; what remains ambiguous or unmeasured.



Historical meaning also changes without a measurable prevalence trend. An identical response option can name a different social relation after a revolution, violent partition, displacement, or reorganization of employment. A new legal classification may make the same survey term unavailable or dangerous. Conversely, a material pressure may persist while the words available to describe it change. Our temporal intelligence therefore needs **separate tracks for changed measurement, changed empirical prevalence, changed institutional relations and changed historical interpretability**. It is precisely this separation that prevents the apparent movement of questionnaire percentages from being mistaken for the movement of social reality itself.

**Why:** A hypothetical person must come from a historical world rather than a timeless demographic calculator. But a social trend is not yet that person's acquisition history.

### Living history changes the routes of reproduction, not only the average opinion

A historical field must be able to represent **reproduction and crisis together**. Housing, food provision, work schedules, schooling, health care, gendered care labor, migration, child-rearing, property transmission and political obedience reproduce social arrangements across generations, often through ordinary life rather than spectacular events. The machine should not imagine history as a sequence of wars punctuating an otherwise static survey distribution. Its base conditions must include what kinds of everyday activity are materially available, how lives can be sustained and how the burden of sustaining them is divided.

A rupture can then reconfigure these **reproductive channels**: war destroys a school and pushes families into displacement; austerity changes access to medicine; a labor transformation changes who can make a household; segregation or citizenship law changes who can move, marry, vote, work or receive institutional protection. Temporal change is therefore a changed *field of constraints and opportunities*, not merely a new wave-average predictor. Where evidence permits, the machine can articulate distinct temporal processes: slowly reproducing material relations, gradual repertoire movement, sudden coercive ruptures, adaptive institutional transformations and divergent local trajectories.

No statistical field can assert that one particular adult experienced any such event just because it occurred in their country. The field can mark the event's geographic/time scope and possible exposure channels; the post-Forge layer can make the objective event relevant to a historical setting; Sorok supplies the actual encounter, activity and consequences for the particular life. Between these gates lies the genuine possibility of **historically credible developmental difference without a pre-scripted character arc**.
**Next:** Pass only legitimate, actor-independent structural/temporal conditions through the post-Field-Forge layer, then make it possible to ask highly specific questions of the resulting field, even when the precise conditioning slice is sparse.

## XIV. BUILD THE POSITIONED-SUBJECT QUERY ENGINE AND HONEST SPECIFICITY WALKBACK

The empirical intelligence must become *usable*. A downstream caller will ask for possibilities conditioned on age, place, class location, religion-as-observed, household environment, educational path, existing jointly selected dispositions, or a combination of other allowed historical facts.

**Operation:** Treat each requested condition as an exact empirical proposition or governed derived input. Find support at that specificity. Where the exact joint exists and has adequate measured support, offer its actual whole-case distribution. Where the conditioning slice is too sparse, do not manufacture a highly specific answer: broaden along explicitly allowed dimensions, with a clear **walkback path** showing what specificity was relinquished and what distribution changed as a result. Where a proxy field is allowed, mark it as proxy and distinguish its observed origin from the requested world. Where no lawful candidate remains, abstain.

Crucially, conditioning does not imply independent feature sampling. The engine should either select from supported whole cases, use separately justified models of joint dependence, or expose that the requested configuration has not been observed. The caller should be able to ask which possibilities are still open, not only which one was selected.

**Product:** A **Positioned Possibility Service** returning the appropriate distribution of complete and partially supported configurations, exact context roots, candidate alternatives, local minority support, uncertainty, walkback, specificity loss and negative evidence. It must be capable of **counter-conditional interrogation**: retain fixed legitimate conditions, change one additional condition, expose how the entire remaining joint possibility set moves, and show when a supposedly decisive predictor was an artifact of conditioning or sample opportunity. This is not a causal counterfactual unless separate interventions/mechanisms authorize that stronger meaning. We are navigating conditional evidentiary possibilities, not rewriting history.

**Why:** This is the bridge from general population intelligence to historically situated hypothetical adults—and the protection against reducing uncommon people to average demographic expectations.

### A possibility inquiry should return its *differences*, not merely its latest probability table

As a developing request adds conditions, the output should retain **what became more likely, what became less likely, what new configurations emerged, what possibilities disappeared only because of sample sparsity, what became genuinely excluded by a legitimate condition, and where a different walkback route would give a different picture**. This makes the service actively navigable and permits a consumer to hold competing hypothetical configurations before deciding which fictional constraints to add.

An important use is to ask why a proposal would have changed: "Had this fictional adult remained in a rural district rather than moving to the city, which empirical alternatives would still be defensible?" That is **conditional contrast in the empirical field**, not an assertion that we have observed the same person in both lives. If the request is counterfactual in a stronger historical-causal sense, the required source and mechanism authority must be separately established. The machine's honesty about that distinction makes its possibilities more useful, not less.

At a sufficiently developed stage, a query can also request **maximal informative alternatives**: not only the most probable complete configuration, but a few differently supported possibilities illustrating the major internally coherent branches. These alternatives should be anchored to actual respondent co-travel, modeled dependence with an explicit inferential grade, or an honest abstention. They should not be selected as an ideologically balanced theater of equal probabilities. If the empirical majority is overwhelming, the machine can say so while still conserving the rare source-supported alternative.

The most generative form of the result is therefore a **conditional possibility neighborhood**: an organized account of which adjacent configurations remain possible under fixed material conditions, which empirical changes move us between them, and what is unknown about their eventual lived formation. It offers much more than a sampled vector and less than a fictional biography—a fertile and disciplined input for the next owner.
**Next:** Let the generative consumer construct a hypothetical configuration that remains accountable to this space.

### Neither copy actual respondents nor fabricate every unobserved combination

The historically useful generation problem is richer than "select one whole survey respondent" versus "sample every question marginally." Whole-respondent support is the strongest direct demonstration that a configuration of *measured answers* co-occurred. It is not an ontological claim that configurations never observed in a finite sample could not occur, nor is it permission to recombine columns arbitrarily.

A powerful Positioned Possibility Service should distinguish **four constructive forms of possibility**. An *observed configuration* is supported at its exact source/respondent grain, including required nonresponse semantics. An *inferred but unobserved configuration* may be offered only by a separately admitted, dependence-preserving generative model or bounded conditional inference, with its validation and extrapolation limits. An *unresolved configuration* is one whose absence could reflect finite support, incomplete measurement or unsearched conditions and cannot be called impossible. An *excluded configuration* is forbidden by an actual logical, physical, source-defined or authoritative incompatibility—not merely because its empirical count is zero.

**Operation:** Condition on the situated request, find the strongest respondent-backed joint neighborhoods and observed alternatives; where full exact cases are sparse, apply admissible specificity walkback. If broader generative breadth is necessary, consider only a model with an explicitly preserved joint-dependence structure, an evaluated ability to reproduce known joint and minority configurations, and a limit on unobserved extrapolation. Never claim that a synthetic draw is a newly found real survey respondent. Distinguish "this sampled adult is coherent under an admitted model" from "this exact whole configuration occurred in source data." Protect source privacy: a generative request must not cause a restricted row to be exposed as a fictional biography.

**Product:** A **Qualified Possibility Envelope** with observed whole-case anchors, unresolved neighborhoods, conditionally inferred alternatives, explicitly impossible combinations, probability ranges where justified, and the fidelity/authority of its construction mode. It allows some genuinely new synthetic combinations without claiming that the survey has measured their real-world prevalence. Especially when many necessary dimensions were never co-administered, honest partial description can be a better offer than a superficially complete person.

The principle is **creative breadth under known dependence**. Respondent fidelity must not become a regime of copying sampled individuals, and fictional creativity must not become a regime of independent marginals. Both mistakes would make social life narrower or more stereotyped than the evidence warrants.

## XV. PRODUCE PARENT/ADULT CONFIGURATIONS WITHOUT INVENTING PSYCHOLOGICAL PERSONS

The parents' empirical entrance is the point where the knowledge becomes generative, but not yet the point where PYAT constructs a person.

**Operation:** Given a historical-fictional specification and any legitimate material conditions, use the Positioned Possibility Service to propose a **whole, respondent-coherent hypothetical adult configuration**. Draw or select from the actual supported conditional alternatives without illegitimate demographic independence or personality-type shortcuts. Preserve minority options even when the modal profile dominates. Allow context to be revised and remaining alternatives queried. Keep exact source and method limitations visible so another system can refuse an unsupported psychological inference.

**The second parent's entrance must be conditionable on the first parent's situated world.** The original parent/coparent ambition was not to independently sample two national adults and simply declare them a household. The richer proposal is a *two-entrance possibility problem*: first select a source-coherent, historically situated Parent A candidate; then evaluate admissible **Parent B possibilities conditioned on Parent A's position, local social field, class proximity, educational and religious/ethnic boundaries, plausible meeting opportunity and other independently established structural constraints**. The output should distinguish whether each restriction is survey-observed, structurally inferred, an explicitly fictional/marriage-field heuristic, or missing. It must leave open whether the desired pair can be supported at the requested specificity and must permit abstention, backoff and a plurality of candidate B configurations.

None of that makes the relationship a survey fact. **A conditional proposal for B is not empirical proof of partner selection.** Survey respondents are generally not linked to their actual partners; a source-faithful conditional model may constrain possible pairings without discovering a particular household. Independent empirical parent candidates may be used as a minimal sourcefree seed, but cannot be confused with the full coparent/encounter vision. Sorok or the competent world/relationship owner supplies and realizes the actual fictional association, encounter, mutual response, parenting, life history and psychological change. PYAT supplies only what a legitimate conditional possibility model knows or refuses to know.

**Product:** **Historical Adult Empirical Entrance Offers**: distributions, sampled whole-case proposals, alternatives, contextual constraints, specificity history and qualified unknowns. They can be revisited or resampled as downstream construction chooses, but are never a bank of prewritten fixed personalities.

**Why:** A person who has some situated answer configuration still does not have an acquired psyche, a biography, a relational motive or a set of justified memories. PYAT is strongest precisely when it makes the empirical possibilities rich enough that Sorok need not invent a social worldview from generic prose.

**Next:** Make the empirically conditioned adult and coparent *possibility fields* usable by the downstream structural and Sorok consumers as revisitable questions, not fixed biographies or a one-off export. Changes to the social world may call for new possibilities but do not retroactively rewrite an already realized psychological person.

## XVI. SERVE A LIVING FIELD, NOT A STATIC CATALOGUE

A single huge final JSON packet is not the system. The right output is an **evidence service** able to answer a downstream question, return an exact conditional object or typed refusal, and identify what new conditioning or authorized empirical observation would change it.

**Operation:** Compose reusable field capabilities through exact references. A consumer can ask for a modal profile, minority/exception alternatives, branch structure, whole-joint co-travel, reverse posterior, a source-qualified hypothesis, comparative field geometry, evidence for structural admission, historical difference, or a hypothetical adult possibility. The system need not instantiate one universal Field Forge carrier; the appropriate producers can serve their exact objects directly under existing owner contracts.

Make the field **progressively explorable**: each new condition updates the possibilities; each changed source root invalidates descendants that actually depended on it; an authorized reanalysis can produce a new lineage without destroying the previous historical record. The service can explain the precise empirical basis for a claim without exposing restricted respondent identities.

**Product:** A **Queryable Empirical-Social Possibility Intelligence** with reproducible answers, negative states, explanations, partial and complete discovery frontiers, cached-but-nonauthoritative derived views, and exact consumer boundaries. Engineering requires a *knowledge graph of exact operations* rather than an ever-growing collection of static tables: each answer retains which eligible evidence universe and authorized semantic roles support it, which conditions it answered, which other questions it makes possible, and which downstream objects depend on it. The graph must support reentry, selective invalidation, repeatable view construction and deferred recomputation. The runtime need not build every potential condition/product eagerly; it must know what is legally constructible and produce or refuse it on demand.

**Why:** Theoretical ambition is wasted if the downstream system cannot obtain the relevant part at the moment it needs it, or if it must reread all source files to answer every new question.

**Next:** Let this intelligence itself help determine what should be investigated next.

## XVII. BUILD AN AUTONOMOUS EPISTEMIC EXPLORER

An empirical discovery engine must move beyond predeclared one-variable questions. It should be able to propose its **next empirical question** in response to what it has just discovered.

**Operation:** Maintain the active space of legal questions, measured opportunities, prior findings, untouched branches, surprising interactions, minority concentrations, unresolved interpretations, cost estimates and test status. Explore where an additional variable, a higher-order joint, a reversed conditional, a different position cut or a temporal comparison could change the current picture. Use learned novelty and expected discrimination as provisional priorities, while deliberately retaining budget for rare coherent minorities and contradiction-bearing branches.

The machine must also know when not to continue: insufficient source support, forbidden transport, excessive complexity, unstable evidence, missing question semantics or exhausted authorized search opportunities. Every inquiry should produce not only an interesting result but a **state of knowledge**: what was asked, what was learned, what failed, what remains open, and which new operation that result supports.

**Product:** The **Autonomous Social Discovery Frontier**: a replayable empirical inquiry graph that can discover previously unknown conditional subcultures and relation patterns, distinguish genuinely examined absence from unexplored territory, and generate a bounded next worklist. It must account for the **independently declared opportunity space**: a controller cannot claim its inquiry complete merely because it has visited every candidate it chose to generate. A discovered branch can reopen the response morphology or condition-selection policy; a new attitude cloud can change which configurations deserve search; structural/world contradictions can nominate further empirical hypotheses but may not automatically grant those hypotheses truth. Thus every frontier advance can reenter earlier evidence functions. Discoveries, rejected hypotheses, holds, source-qualified next questions, partial-search coverage and resource costs are all outputs of one continuing inquiry.

**Why:** Without this, Rawlsing is still dependent on a human analyst to imagine every important subgroup and hypothesis. With it, the machine becomes capable of finding what its designer did not know to look for—without declaring itself a source-authority or sociology oracle.

**Next:** Reopen the empirical possibility service with the frontier's newly discovered question, and preserve what was learned during the preceding search rather than restarting the world from raw data. The machine thereby becomes a *continuing circle of empirical inquiry*, not a list of reports.

### The constructive grammar of a single discovery move

The autonomous explorer must be able to carry a meaningful **question**, not merely a list of columns to correlate. A question identifies the measured response or configuration of interest; the material, social or prior-response conditions held fixed; the legitimate universe of eligible people; the candidate dimensions or alternatives under consideration; the empirical contrast worth resolving; and what sort of answer will change its understanding. "Does education predict this response?" is one permissible opening. "Within the minority that does not express the dominant response, what alternative configuration co-travels with its refusal, and does it retain a comparable position structure across another lawful context?" is a more revealing *compound question*. It needs several computations; its purpose is one coherent inquiry.

Every candidate question should be able to nominate its **epistemic prize**: discovery of a new coherent minority, overturning an apparent main effect, identifying the conditions of an exception, finding that a supposed cloud dissolves under a better source measure, establishing an honest low-N boundary, or locating a historical/structural question for an independent owner. It must also nominate its **cost and risk**: the additional eligible cases required, degree of multiplicity/search, categorical sparsity, the scope of any respondent join, the sensitivity of the result to missingness and the possibility that the question is already answered by an exact cached joint. The machine should not spend the same budget on every question merely because each has been given a numerical ID.

A successful inquiry yields more than the best predictor. It yields: the *entire distribution of competing observations*; an account of what changed compared with the previous context; the best-supported positive interpretations at the admitted evidence level; explicit alternative hypotheses and negative results; the limits of generalization; the next question that the discovery made meaningful; and a durable way to reproduce the inference without inventing inaccessible source rows. The strongest discovered relation may also be: "**the apparent pattern is not robust to this necessary conditioning**." That result improves the field.

The machine's search policy should carry **three overlapping obligations**. It should exploit high-information opportunities, deliberately conserve low-prevalence but coherent alternatives, and spend some attention on attempts to falsify currently favored structures. It should track how much of the relevant eligible possibility frontier was ever examined. A system that only searches where its own predictor agrees with itself can never achieve autonomous discovery; a system that never stops cannot serve any consumer. Its intelligence is partly the art of allocating **finite, transparent epistemic effort**.

The inquiry frontier must distinguish a **source question that cannot be asked** from an **interesting question whose answer is unknown**, and both from a **proposed structural interpretation awaiting evidence**. It should be possible for a later Field Forge hypothesis to send a query back upstream—"is this material position actually associated with the proposed repertoire under comparable contexts?"—without Field Forge reaching into source records or declaring its own corroboration. Likewise a downstream parent construction can ask for a better-constrained minority alternative without commanding a new population fact. In this way the architecture has feedback without having an authority hierarchy collapse.

### Productive intelligence: a lawfully empty finding is a product, an unfinished machine is not

A mature empirical-social discovery engine must satisfy a stronger standard than lawful termination. If the source and its qualified semantics support a meaningful distribution, minority fracture, complete joint, conditional contrast, source-based null or temporal nonmovement, the machine should be able **to produce it** and make it consumable. Merely reporting that some library has an owner or that the request ended without violating permissions does not constitute that intelligence.

A null association is not a program refusal. A diffuse distribution is not absence of result. A fully measured category with zero observed members is not the same thing as missing source or unsupported query. The opposite failure is equally serious: a module that invents a usable-looking pressure or history because a legitimate producer is unavailable. Both are failures to realize the intended social world.

Every product class therefore needs an explicit **positive output criterion** and a **negative taxonomy** that distinguishes intrinsic source inapplicability, real empirical null/weak/empty findings, and implementation/authority/mapping/composition defects. Where an external prerequisite is missing, the competent downstream stage should still prove its conditional positive behavior on a legitimate fixture; it cannot claim full real-world occupancy on that basis alone. Where the full source can support the product but no consumer can use it, functionality is unfinished even when the data were stored correctly.

This is not a demand to exhaustively materialize all possible combinations of the 35-wave estate. It is a demand for a **governed, bounded discovery grammar** that genuinely reaches the products it says it can produce. It should be able to compute positive and null results, preserve unsupported and uninterpretable conditions, replay the inquiry and make its outputs available to Field Forge or the qualified downstream consumer. In the limit, a functioning science is not a warehouse of potential intelligence, but the capacity to answer real questions under real conditions, to learn what it does not yet know, and to help choose the next evidence-bearing inquiry.



### A finite estate, an open-ended empirical question grammar

We do not acquire new surveys merely because a favorite theoretical model wants variables that are absent. The governing task is to make the existing 35-wave estate and already available authorized structural assets **more intellectually productive**. An available question might support a novel three-way joint, a useful minority context, an incompatible attitude cloud, a change in field-relative resources or a valid historical contrast. The machine must generate more such inquiries without pretending it has observed every sociological mechanism named in the theory.

An unanswerable empirical request can yield a valuable answer about its missing measurement or invalid conditioning, together with the strongest weaker available observation. But it must not become a convenient substitute for every positive supported product. If the source genuinely permits a bounded conditional pattern, the engine should produce it, including measured nulls and diffuse outcomes. It is not scientifically complete because a generic rule says "HOLD" in every interesting case.

In practical terms, our architecture must optimize the **finite empirical input plus a rich, reusable and adaptive query grammar**, not unlimited source acquisition or exhaustive materialization of every combination. The program's creative power comes from making the best use of exactly what has been observed, recognizing where the source vocabulary is inadequate, and refusing to fabricate the rest.


## XVIII. ATTACK THE MACHINE WHERE IT WOULD BE MOST SEDUCTIVELY WRONG

The machine must be able to fail intelligently. Construct hostile worlds in which ordinary shortcuts produce persuasive false knowledge: high-cardinality memorization, rare coherent minorities pruned away, XOR-like interactions invisible to all pairs, overlapping multi-select responses treated as exclusive, unasked mistaken for "no," contextual reversals lost by global pooling, false cross-wave equivalence, apparent hegemony inferred only from a majority, structural pressure inferred from raw correlation, or fictional couple selection inferred from independent survey draws.

For each challenge the system should provide its own appropriately scoped answer, not a blanket “passed.” It must preserve exact risk/denominator conservation, complete alternative support, method uncertainty, partial search and external source/actor authority. Predictions should be evaluated on information they were not fitted to when such evaluation is relevant; a score that collapses out of sample must be allowed to collapse. Survey design or weighting can be applied only where legitimately issued. A genuine negative is a finding, not an embarrassment; a held result is neither a negative nor a success.

The acceptance standard is not that every capability be computed on every source wave from the outset. It is that the architecture can deliver its declared conceptual function **at an actual authorized source grain**, that we can say exactly which grains remain unproved, and that no final consumer is tricked into thinking a partial or exploratory demonstration provides broad authority.

## XIX. THE COMPLETE INTELLIGENCE CIRCUIT

### A worked conceptual journey: how one inquiry changes its own questions

Begin with the actual kind of survey question that animated the older discovery project: **which bases of discrimination do respondents select?** This example illustrates how the *machine is supposed to inquire*, not a new empirical finding or a present-day verified set of Arab Barometer percentages.

An ordinary dashboard counts responses to "socioeconomic class," "religion/sect," "nationality," "region" and the remaining offered options. Our first engine preserves that the question can be multi-select. It knows that **class + sect** is a distinct observed response pattern from **class alone**, and that "none of these" is a survey response requiring interpretation rather than a missing value or a declaration of false consciousness.

The conditional topography discovers that the prevalence of naming class differs by legitimate country and position contexts. Now the *questions themselves change*. It is no longer enough to know whether class is the most frequently named category overall. In one place, the machine asks **who remains in the class-naming branch despite a different education or religious position**. In another, it asks **who departs from class and what competing explanations they actually select**. Where the deviation splits again by denomination, locality, income or education, the next candidates are generated **within that minority context**, not inherited blindly from the top global ranking.

Suppose a small but sufficiently source-supported branch selects **sect without class**. The system asks whether this is an isolated answer to one questionnaire item or belongs to a broader *respondent-coherent repertoire*. It searches admissible same-person measurements of institutional trust, redistribution, communal affiliation, employment insecurity or reported action, where such measurements truly exist. The result might be a cloud, several distinct clouds, a contradiction, or no reproducible co-travel. The machine carries all these possible dispositions. It **does not** interpret sect-selection as an individual's psychological defense against class recognition, because the survey has not observed that internal mechanism.

Now reverse the inquiry. Among people who exhibit a discovered multi-item repertoire, what measured material/position fields remain plausible? How often is the repertoire seen in positions other than those where it was initially discovered? Does one apparently positional association collapse when an independently measured condition is included? Does a minority remain an exception or become a local majority inside a newly discovered region? This is the **bidirectional and recursive** circuit operating upon its own earlier findings.

Bring the resulting source-qualified field toward structural interpretation. If the society's independently established institutions organize work, marriage, resource access, school entry, migration or political opportunity through documented boundaries, Field Forge may constitute relevant actor-independent relations. If no such independent evidence exists, a sociologically interesting correlation remains just that. **Field Forge is not allowed to translate "respondents in this group selected sect" into "sectarian institutions caused this person's perception."** It can instead retain an explanatory hypothesis and request a more discriminating empirical question.

The post-Forge machinery can then ask what a documented institution or event means for an *actual historical situation*. Different years, neighborhoods, social positions or periods of rupture can entail different opportunities or pressures. It must not infer a smooth personal history merely because two comparable survey waves differ. The resolved context is an objective history-conditioned **possibility and exposure field**, not a life already experienced.

Now a parent-construction request arrives: a fictional adult of a given formative period, locality and social situation. The empirical service returns the exact supported whole-case alternatives or a declared, progressively broadened possibility set when evidence is sparse. If the adult is associated with a second potential parent, the engine does **not** simply draw another independent respondent and assert a relationship. It asks a new conditional question under whatever is legitimately established about encounter opportunity, class proximity and social boundaries. The result may support several plausible B configurations, a much weaker one, or none. Sorok or the appropriate relationship/world owner decides what actually happens between the two fictional individuals.

And the circuit **does not end** when an entrance is provided. A rare configuration might prompt a question about which wider social contexts support it. An actor-independent structural hypothesis might nominate another measured conditional contrast. An independently revised source mapping might invalidate an apparent sect/class branch. A new wave might show a historical discontinuity requiring the prior empirical field to be contextualized differently. This is not a prediction engine hallucinating a person. It is **a persistent research intelligence preserving multiple possible histories and accountable limits**.

### The simultaneous circuit, rather than a false serial assembly line

Now the whole sequence can be stated as one **self-revising constructive operation**, whose different products can be called directly rather than being physically manufactured in one fixed order:

**Real survey and historical observations** become truthful, versioned measured social worlds. Their questions are opened into complete **response morphologies**, including multiple selections, residuals and negative states. Those morphologies become **conditional topographies** over observed positions. Every lawful relation becomes **bidirectional possibility inference**. The engine discovers **where each apparent consensus fractures**, then follows majority, minority, alternative and residual routes recursively. It detects **hidden interactions**, and examines **actual same-respondent attitude clouds** and higher-order repertoires. Those configurations become an **empirical motif atlas** and two distinct but interacting empirical spaces: **positions/resources** and **attitudes/dispositions**. Their **homology and conditional redistribution** explain where repertoires become more or less empirically possible, while their field-relative resource position, local capital conversion, reach, prestige, contestation and institutionally qualified alignments become **plural hegemony and material-world evidence**. The discovered motif remains an empirical object until independent adjudication legitimately names a stronger social formation. A separately governed semantic and structural judgment process admits demanding **meaning and relation claims** that statistical discovery cannot lawfully create; once such decisions are made, cheap computation can reuse them without reinventing meanings. Field Forge can then constitute independently warranted **actor-independent structural relations and resource worlds** from lawful pre-Forge empirical inputs without consuming or replacing DDM. Observed **pressure footprints** (how response probabilities redistribute by position) remain different from proven **pressure mechanisms** (how institutional power changes opportunity, cost or legitimacy). The **separately necessary post-Field-Forge structural/pressure/temporal and linking machinery** resolves their conditional manifestations for actual historical circumstances without writing actor state. The competing tendencies remain visible as **contradiction and alternative possibility fields**. Comparable sources and historical moments become **changing fields** that also preserve ruptures and noncomparability. A positioned-subject request moves through this field with **honest specificity and walkback**, yielding **coherent hypothetical adult empirical entrances and conditional coparent alternatives** and a continuing **queryable evidence service**. At every stage the system itself keeps searching through an **autonomous epistemic frontier** whose discoveries can reopen conditional, branch, joint, source-meaning and structural questions at the appropriate owner, learning where the social field still contains unexamined structure.

The multi-universe contribution process takes place *between empirical worlds and actor-independent field constitution*, and does not erase the original separate sources. The post-Forge context is a distinct historical product. The discovery controller can reopen its own earlier questions whenever later findings expose a new comparison, contradiction or deficiency. Thus the machine's conceptual direction is a **repeatable, potentially cyclic inquiry**, not a compulsory serial physical implementation.

The existing governed source estate is finite, but the admitted empirical questions and relational interpretations can be multiplied, recombined and revised without fabricating new respondents or demanding an endless source-acquisition programme. Its objective world contains carriers, constraints, opportunities, histories and competing repertoires, never an actor's guaranteed reception of them. Scientific null findings must be produced as real results rather than hidden inside generic refusals; unavailable measurements and missing mechanisms must not be conjured into existence. The machine's ambition is to remain **productively intelligent under limitation**, not merely technically safe.

The terminal product is not a person and not a raw probability table. It is the **operative, historically conditioned landscape of possibilities** in which a genuinely constructed person might develop. PYAT supplies that landscape and its empirical qualification. Field Forge constitutes the legitimate actor-independent structural meaning. Sorok builds the actual organism, consciousness, contradictory psychic organization, lived activity, memory, relationships and developmental history. No one of these owners can quietly substitute for the others.

## XX. HOW HARDER CAN WE GO?

Even this vision is not an endpoint. Its full ambition is to make social possibility **dynamically interrogable**, not merely measured once. An observed minority can become a question about a material position; a surprising positional reversal can become a question about an attitude cloud; an attitude cloud can challenge the assumed boundaries of social subcultures; a temporal reversal can challenge the alleged persistence of a hegemonic repertoire; a structural mismatch can call for source-meaning review rather than an invented mechanism. The system should eventually be able to explain **why its own prior hypothesis was insufficient**, not simply emit a fresh number.

We can go further in three complementary directions. **Greater empirical depth:** recover interactions, multi-label grammars, weak signals, rare but supported configurations, and ever more complex conditional contexts without pretending tiny samples prove general laws. **Greater historical-material depth:** articulate the differences between prevalence, legitimacy, resource position, institutional power, actual opportunity and contradiction, always demanding the evidence that transforms one into another. **Greater generative fidelity:** provide Sorok with richly varied, source-qualified starting possibilities and changing social worlds without reducing the realized person to survey values.

This is the strongest form of determinicity: **not assigning everything a cause and a score, but discovering a vast, constrained and contradictory space of actual social possibilities, together with the conditions under which those possibilities become more or less likely and the limits of our ability to know why.**

If the completed machine can answer the question *“What could be true of somebody situated here, what else could be true, how would the answer differ under changed conditions, which of those alternatives is empirically supported, and what cannot legitimately be inferred?”*—across social positions, conflicting repertoires, historical transformations and structural fields—then the resurrected vision has become a live intelligence.

If it can answer only “the top predictor is education,” then no amount of new nomenclature will mean the resurrection is finished.
