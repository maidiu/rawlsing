## 0. THE DISASSEMBLY: WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO BE ONE MACHINE?

The inherited fragments initially appear to disagree about what the primary thing is: an answer distribution; a recursive social branch; same-person attitude co-travel; a source-valid proposition; a material position; a pressure-bearing social field; an institutional carrier; a hypothetical adult; an embodied child; a layered psyche; a historical life; a world changing in response to actions; or a model able to inquire and learn. Taking any one as the primitive and translating the rest into its vocabulary mutilates the system.

The old vector/Enneagram route starts with a statistical or cultural environment, pushes estimated weights into pre-named strategies, and asks an LLM to speak through a resulting personality. It offers a useful historical insistence on **context, cultural discordance, and dynamic developmental change**, but its causal shortcuts do not establish that a demographic coordinate *produces* an inner type. The older nine-strata doctrine grasped different pressures and mediations, but its fixed number of strata could not be allowed to decide in advance what social formations exist. The V550 branch and cloud designs recovered empirical differentiation, but their sociology remains incomplete if relations are reduced to predictor strength. Field Forge brought actor-independent relational truth, but cannot replace the upstream discovery of respondents' actual configurations. The psychological materials reject the adult vector as a person and show that conduct, consciousness, motive and meaning are historically formed in activity with Others. The conservation campaigns demonstrate that an apparently working pipeline can lose meaning, authority roles, minority branches or downstream access while retaining the same bytes. The more recent identification work shows that a lawful unknown can itself contain mathematically necessary information.

These are not detachable features. Their collision requires a distinct account of **what exists**, **what can be observed about it**, **what operations evidence permits**, **how material worlds generate and constrain options**, **how a particular life takes up or changes them**, and **how consequences alter the next conditions of life**. The exact place where the machine must refuse a transformation is as important as the place where it must make one.

The **spine emerging from this disassembly** is therefore not a chosen metaphor. It is a sequence of **mediated transformations that preserve the differences between historical reality, evidence, social possibility, psychic organization and actual becoming while allowing each to change the questions posed of the others**. What flows forward is not a deterministic identity. It is an increasingly situated **space of admissible alternatives**, their pressures and pathways, until a genuinely independent actor experiences a world and makes something happen. What flows back is the changed world, the new lived history, and epistemically qualified new questions—not fabricated empirical validation.

**The completed organism must be simultaneously:**
- a governed, historically situated empirical investigator, able to recover actual whole-respondent structures from a finite multi-survey estate and discover unforeseen concepts;

## I. MATERIAL LIFE BEFORE A PERSONALITY: WHAT IS BEING MODELED?

### I.1 Persons are neither demographic exemplars nor self-originating interiors

An actual person's conditions begin before their concepts of them: birth into relations of dependence, access to food and shelter, a household and caregivers, the distributions of labor and property, geographic position, schooling and work, language and law, violence and protection, social classifications imposed by Others, and the existing histories of those institutions. These conditions do not produce a one-to-one mental reflection. They structure exposure, needs, conflict, the value of resources, enforceable choices, and the historical materials from which the person can organize a life.

The **material relation** is primary: employee/employer, tenant/landlord, debtor/creditor, carer/dependent, citizen/state, prisoner/jailer, pupil/teacher, kin/kin, colonial authority/colonized subject, migrant/border apparatus, worker/household, institution/community. A demographic label may proxy a relation but cannot define it. Class is not income quintile. Access to education is not control over one's labor. Prestige is not political power. Religion-as-observed is not a ready-made motive. Position is multi-scale, field-relative and internally contradictory: a person can have authority at home, precarity at work, subordination before a state, and resources enabling resistance in another field.

**Social reproduction is causal infrastructure, not background scenery.** Food, housing, caregiving, maintenance, rest, education, health, intimacy, and the organization of labor power require activity, time, resources and institutions. Who performs this work and who is relieved of it affects which trajectories are sustainable. Reproduction includes its own conflicts, appropriations, exclusions, uneven burdens and possibilities of collective reorganization. It is never only a set of consumption values attached to an income percentile.

The legacy cultural-vector model had a valid question—how is an individual differently situated within historically patterned meanings?—but an inadequate answer when it averaged national cultural dimensions and converted them to preselected types. Preserve the *question about historically situated meanings and contradiction*, not the authority of unvalidated weights or personality-type coefficients.

### I.2 Social structure, actual power, pressure and flow are irreducible

**Structure** is the historically sustained organization of positions, rights, dependencies, material production/reproduction, and relations. **Power** is differential capacity to determine the feasible actions and consequences of oneself and Others: to exclude, make costly, coerce, protect, enable, resource, authorize, sanction or redirect. **Pressure** is a specific field-conditioned tendency that alters relative attraction, risk or cost among alternatives, not a moralized scalar force. **Flow** is the activity and transmission through which social conditions are reproduced, resisted, translated or transformed.

Do not call a majority view hegemony without explaining institutions and power. Do not turn a conditional association into coercion. Do not infer that a person with a contradictory occupation automatically recognizes the contradiction. Do not infer that an opportunity is realized merely because it was formally available. Distinguish the steps:

**structural position → exposure/opportunity → encounter → recognition or non-recognition → interpretation → acquired orientation/motive → situational recruitment → activity/expression → consequence**.

The arrows are conditional and revisable; none is a compulsory psychological law. A person may be constrained by a relation they cannot name, describe an institution they do not accept, or use the values transmitted by a dominant institution to criticize that institution.

### I.3 Reproduction does not imply social stasis

Institutions actively **transform** the meanings they transmit. A school does not merely pass on an ideology; it grades, allocates credentials, regulates bodies, disciplines time, offers solidarities, teaches dissent and closes alternatives. Work reproduces skills and wages but also fatigue, conflict, technical knowledge, collaboration and resistance. Care reproduces life but can redistribute power, loyalty and vulnerability. Religious communities can transmit obedience, solidarity, sanction, ethical rupture and incompatible frameworks. States and borders arrange entitlements and risks, while the people governed can evade, contest and reorganize these arrangements.

Thus carriers must be modeled as relations among institutions, groups, practices, material resources, sanctions, interpretive frameworks and active participants; **not parcels containing one ideological message**. The old 'pressure vector' is insufficient if it never knows which activity, which bearer, which institution and which alternative produced the effect.

### I.4 Others and institutions are independent realities

Each significant Other has their own needs, position, situated constraints, memory, priorities and power to refuse. A parent is not a psychological stimulus delivered to Nadia on demand; a sibling, manager, landlord, teacher, friend or state official is not an obedient extension of the focal actor. Relationships have histories and changing asymmetries. Institutions operate through rules, calendars, material stocks, personnel, enforcement practices and uncertainty. Even an apparently ordinary day requires multiple simultaneous processes outside the actor's awareness.

The simulator need not compute the entire population at equal detail. It must maintain **enough independent world state and other-agent agency** that the focal actor can be surprised, disappointed, blocked, supported, injured, mistaken, helped and transformed by events not manufactured merely to confirm a predetermined adult destiny.

### I.5 Historical beings do not start with finished consciousness

Infancy has embodied needs, capacities and dependence; it does not contain a latent adult ideological scale merely waiting for its birthday. What the organism can recognize, attend to, remember, feel, desire, interpret or perform emerges through maturation, language, repeated social practice, relationships, work and conflict. Psychological constructs differ in age of availability; learning is not fixed by age gates alone. The user-supplied Leontiev, Luria, Sève, Lahire, Holzkamp, Dreier, Archer, Elder, Mortimer and social-reproduction works are theoretical resources for this problem, not an excuse to treat their distinct approaches as harmonized scientific proof.

**Leontiev's distinction** among activity, goal-directed action and condition-bound operations prevents collapsing a motive into an isolated surface act; the same apparent gesture can organize different activities. **Lahire's plural actor** counters the unified disposition inferred from a single situation: heterogeneous contexts may recruit acquired, partially incompatible schemes. **Dreier's conduct of everyday life** demands a person across multiple places and practices rather than a psychotherapy- or scene-local self. **Elder and Mortimer** press on historical timing, social pathways, contingent turning points and institutional transitions. **Social-reproduction theory** asks who makes this daily and generational psychological life materially possible, through which unpaid and paid labor. **Critical psychology** raises the person's reasons for acting under concrete conditions rather than making social constraint a direct psychic input. **Archer's internal conversation** is one theoretical resource for reflexive deliberation, not a universal verbal mind. **Sève's historicized personality** asks for the social division of activity, time and real life rather than an isolated inventory of traits.

These approaches disagree in important respects. The system need not prematurely reconcile their total philosophies. It must recover the **non-optional modeling questions** each exposes, use competing architectures where live evidence can distinguish them, and make unproved psychological hypotheses explicitly provisional.

## II. THE WORLD'S EVIDENCE DOES NOT REPLACE THE WORLD

### II.1 A survey is a historically situated social encounter

The first PYAT object is not an answer cell but a **Survey Observation Event**: source/release, respondent unit, sampling frame, question implementation, actual wording and language, category and component affordances, routing, eligibility, wave/place/time, observation state, authorized use, and original provenance. A response may be a stated belief, reported conduct, hypothetical intention, judgment about another, perception of social prevalence, experienced event, institutional fact or refusal. It is not unmediated consciousness.

An eligible respondent who leaves a question unanswered, an inapplicable respondent, a person never sampled, a person who was not offered that wording, and a respondent who answered a legitimate 'none' are five different objects. Preserve them. A coded zero may be observed absence, a negative answer, or a mechanical placeholder; no universal default can decide.

The estate's finite source budget makes the semantics particularly important. You cannot repair a missing source question by turning an LLM's plausible sociological interpretation into data. The right reaction is to ask **what is positively recoverable, what else is constrained, what remains unidentified, and which available measurement could distinguish alternatives**.

### II.2 Identity, meaning and permission travel together but never collapse

Retain distinct:
- source-native question/option/component identity and respondent/risk-set identity;
- source-to-concept mapping and any *separately adjudicated* semantic equivalence across source versions;
- valid measurement direction and response topology, including multiple selections and non-scalar ordered component vectors;
- source admissibility and original35 native use permission;
- estimand, design, weight, variance and nonresponse method authority;
- derived proposition/interpretation status, structural-use license and actor-use boundary;
- immutable authority history, current selection, invalidation and rollback.

The same phrase in two questionnaires does not create a portable measure. P3C semantic equivalence cannot confer respondent-join, cross-population transport, weight or causal rights. The valid denominator must be an executable or exact-source warranted eligibility predicate. A logical V550J composition without positive executable scope is not enough. Do not let a missing special S14 semantic-relation permission erase otherwise valid empirical co-travel; interpretation and occurrence have different rights.

Non-scalar component values may become a single projected value **only** under complete exact component matching and unanimous nonempty projected value. If any required component is absent, contradictory, empty or role-incompatible, preserve the component vector and its status. V551 canonical signed projection has one governed owner. V552-style pressure is a separately qualified structural transformation, never a synonym for count, support, correlation, confidence, packet score or respondent frequency.

### II.3 The observation field has positive science even when answers are missing

For a known eligible finite record set N with observed yes Y and eligible unresolved M on a defined binary completed-response outcome, the share over all compatible completions lies in:

\[
\frac{Y}{N} \leq p \leq \frac{Y+M}{N}.
\]

For N=100 with 36 yes, 24 no, 40 genuinely eligible unresolved, the observed-answer yes fraction is 36/60=.60; the eligible-record possibility interval is [.36,.76]. A majority of all eligible cases **is unidentified**, not negative. With 81 yes, 9 no and 10 unresolved, [.81,.91] makes the finite-eligible-record majority robust. Neither interval establishes the unanswered respondents' private beliefs, a cause of silence or a population probability without additional sample and inference warrants.

Change the 40 unresolved cases into valid skip/inapplicable cases and the risk set changes: the original bound is no longer legal. Change the eligible universe without authority and even N becomes unknown. Generalize to multinomial/vector outcomes by treating the compatible completions as an admissible set rather than arbitrarily imputing a preferred completion. Keep the distinction between exact finite-sample mathematical bounds, sampling uncertainty, source coverage error, and sensitivity to nonignorable nonresponse.

The observation field is itself socially informative when reach, administrative constraints, disclosure risk or differential missingness is independently evidenced. But an apparent group difference in nonresponse does not by itself prove suppression, resistance, apathy, secrecy or trauma.
