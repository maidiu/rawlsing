# DHIKRYMULCH
## THE EMPIRICAL-SOCIAL POSSIBILITY ENGINE
### The constructive vision of what Rawlsing will now become

**PROGRAMMA PYAT · living theory · positive functional synthesis · 20 November 2026**

**This is the machine we intend to build, described in the order in which its intelligence comes into existence.** DHIKR is the resurrection effort by which recovered and presently missing capacities are made available. It does not define the endpoint. The endpoint is the most powerful truthful, generative, historically situated empirical-social possibility engine we can construct: a system that can discover, explain, navigate, compare and serve the structured multiplicity of human possibilities recorded in survey evidence, without mistaking those possibilities for finished psychological persons.

This is a **conceptual architecture**, not an insistence upon one giant runtime, one physical file schema, one sequence of physically materialized stages or one scalar called *determinicity*. The objects named here are conceptual capabilities and service results. An existing, more general lawful implementation may realize several at once. The theory tells us **what must be possible**, why each operation exists, what it needs, what it produces, and what new question that output makes answerable. A later independent code confrontation will determine which capabilities are already in fact delivered and which must be built or repaired. The separate historical source/conservation register remains available, but it does not govern the order of this affirmative account.

## I. THE INTENDED RESULT: A MACHINE THAT KNOWS THE SHAPE OF POSSIBLE SOCIAL HUMANITY

Imagine asking Rawlsing:

*Among people in a specific society, historical period, material position and relational environment, what configurations of perception, commitment, conflict and action are actually evidenced as possible? Which are prevalent? Which are rare but coherent? What conditions make each more or less common? Where does the prevailing configuration fracture? What alternatives appear behind the fracture? What else tends to accompany each alternative? How much does the answer change if we reverse the question, move one level down into a social subgroup, add a contradictory condition, move through time, or enter a different institutional field? Which of those relationships are only observed correlations, which admit stronger structural interpretations, and what remains completely unknown?*

And then imagine asking:

*Construct a hypothetical adult occupying this specified historical position. Do not independently sample a caricatured collection of traits. Select a coherent possibility from the discovered, respondent-supported space. Show what other configurations remained possible, what conditions brought this one into focus, how specific the source evidence was, which parts were inferred or weakened through walkback, and exactly what you do not know about that person's actual biography, motivations or psychology.*

That is our target. It is neither a survey dashboard nor a trait generator. It is a **navigable field of socially conditioned possibilities** that makes actual historical variation and contradiction operative in downstream construction.

The machine must be capable of saying "most," "some," "nearly none," "this minority," "this alternative," "these patterns travel together," "the ranking changes here," "we have evidence but the meaning is disputed," "this specific combination is unsupported," "the question cannot be transported," and "we lack the scientific authority to make that stronger claim." All of those answers carry information.

The ultimate achievement is not predictability at any price. It is the ability to produce **specificity without stereotype, contingency without randomness, and coherence without predestination**.

## II. FIRST: MAKE THE OBSERVED SOCIAL WORLD AVAILABLE

**Input:** Real survey releases and their question/option structures, response observations, respondent units, applicable populations, material and demographic variables, wave/place/time, routing, sampling context and permitted analytical uses.

**Operation:** Constitute the source as an addressable *empirical world*. Establish what each dimension measures, what each value actually signifies, who could have answered the question, what was not asked, what was refused or unavailable, what belongs to the same respondent, and which comparisons or inferences are legitimate. Keep the original social and historical coordinates.

**Product:** The **Observed World**: source-coherent, unit-coherent, versioned, question-meaning-bearing populations and measurements with exact, reusable identities and truthful unknowns.

**Why:** No downstream intelligence can be more real than its observational basis. An observed negative is different from nonresponse; not applicable is different from no; a source concept is not its English label; a country in one cross-national wave is not a universal country type; a sampled respondent is not a generated parent.

**Next:** Once the world can be addressed truthfully, the machine can ask what the entire observed response landscape looks like.

This is not a demand to keep materializing whole original surveys. A compact, exact, reusable source and measure interface may be enough. Physical storage is subordinate to what questions can be answered without erasing meaning or requiring source reconstruction.

## III. GIVE EVERY QUESTION ITS COMPLETE RESPONSE MORPHOLOGY

A conventional analysis begins by choosing a dependent variable and one favored outcome. Ours first asks what the question permits people to say and what the observed population actually did say.

**Operation:** For each valid question and risk set, recover its full response distribution, the spread or concentration of answers, its observed zeros, unknowns, refusals and missingness, and the meaning of its categories. Recognize distinct kinds of measurement: a single categorical selection, ordinal response, numerical scale, multiple overlapping selections, rank ordering, list of behaviors or composite battery.

For a multi-select question, do not pretend the options are exclusive. Recover both the incidence of each option and the combinations respondents actually selected. For an ordinal item, preserve its authorized order and direction rather than using arbitrary code numbers. For a question whose categories may not exhaust people's lived grammars, keep the residual responses available without assigning them invented motives.

**Product:** A **Response Morphology** for each exact question-in-world: the answer space, observed frequencies, complete pattern structure when supported, concentration/fragmentation, meaningful category relations and the limits of the measure.

**Why:** We cannot discover minorities or contradictions by first deleting every outcome except the mode. We cannot discover overlapping interpretive frames if the data model requires exactly one answer. A source response can be a selection of multiple frames, an ambivalent rating, or a refusal to accept the offered choices.

**Next:** Ask which social and material positions differentiate the entire morphology—not simply which feature best predicts one selected answer.

## IV. DISCOVER THE CONDITIONAL TOPOGRAPHY OF EVERY MEASURED POSSIBILITY

For every meaningful response or observed configuration, search across the governed social and material dimensions that might condition it: income, labor location, education, gender, religion as measured, location, age, citizenship, institutional affiliation, household condition, political relation and other authorized variables. These are not all equally objective or equally meaningful; their roles remain typed.

**Operation:** Compare conditional response distributions across positions. Recover the differences between \(P(Y)\) and \(P(Y\mid X=x)\), across values of X, and across jointly observed conditions. Record what changes, how much, for whom, under what risk set, and by which admitted statistical lens. Preserve absolute prevalences as well as contrasts: a large proportional increase in a tiny category is different from a small change affecting half the population.

Seek multiple kinds of structure: gradients, thresholds, sharp divisions, nonlinear patterns, concentration, diffusion, polarization, near-universality, almost-disappearance and reversals. A conditional effect may be small globally but decisive in one local region of the population.

**Product:** A **Conditional Topography**: a collection of evidence-bearing maps of how whole response distributions shift across actual observed positions and contexts, with exact counts, legitimate measures, sample and design uncertainty, and no false statement of causal force.

**Why:** Two populations can have the same overall percentage and radically different positional structure. A response can be common because it occurs across many social worlds, or because it is nearly universal in one large subgroup while rare elsewhere. These are different social facts.

**Next:** Turn the question around.

## V. REVERSE THE INFERENCE: FROM A POSITION TO AN ATTITUDE, AND FROM AN ATTITUDE BACK TO POSSIBLE POSITIONS

Rawlsing must never be trapped in the direction chosen by the original question writer. We must be able to ask both *what tends to be thought from here?* and *where in the measured social space does this way of thinking tend to appear?*

**Operation:** Permit lawful directional queries from observed position to response, response to position, response A to response B, multiple responses to candidate positions, and any admissible observed configuration to further measurements. Preserve the actual joint distribution and priors. A reverse conditional is not a careless inversion of a score: \(P(Y\mid X)\) and \(P(X\mid Y)\) answer different questions and must be computed over their proper denominators.

**Product:** The **Bidirectional Possibility Service**: position-conditioned, response-conditioned and configuration-conditioned distributions, each explicit about the population it describes and what can and cannot be inferred about a hypothetical individual.

**Why:** A particular attitude may be widespread among one group but mostly expressed by another because of different group sizes. Inference without base rates manufactures stereotypes. Reverse inquiry lets the data challenge our assumptions about what kind of social position "should" hold a particular conviction.

**Next:** Ask not merely where the general pattern is strongest, but where and how it **breaks**.

## VI. FIND THE FRACTURES: MAJORITY, EXIT, ALTERNATIVES AND THEIR OWN INTERNAL WORLDS

The majority is a first clue, not an ontological center. At any conditioned context we want to know how the prevailing pattern holds together, who departs from it, what the departing people actually choose, and whether those alternative configurations have their own social bases.

**Operation:** At each source-supported conditional context, discover candidate splitters. Examine the **majority-retention branch**, the **majority-exit branch**, each empirically distinct **alternative branch**, and **residual or category-refusal branches**. Reconsider predictors afresh within each branch. The best splitter at the root may cease to matter within a subgroup; an apparently weak predictor may become decisive after a second condition. A minority exit may itself fracture into several contrasting alternatives.

Do not constrain the search to two highest-frequency states and call all others noise. The machine may rationally allocate expensive follow-up unevenly, but it must preserve all observed alternatives and distinguish *not searched*, *searched without usable evidence*, and *empirically absent from the observed joint*. In multi-label data, an exit from one option can coexist with selection of another; presence and whole-response-pattern branches must coexist without false exclusive partitioning.

A branch must know what defines it, what respondents it describes, which responses remain available, how confident the contrasts are, what was withheld for low support, and what question would most usefully be asked next.

**Product:** The **Recursive Positional Fracture Field**: not one attractive tree diagram, but a searchable, reproducible structure of majorities, minority deviations, alternatives, changing splitters, nested subcultures, convergence, reversals and honest unknown frontiers.

**Why:** This is how the system discovers that "the working class thinks X" may conceal several historically distinct working-class worlds; that wealthy respondents are not homogeneous; that one minority response is socially patterned rather than random; and that a new material condition changes what the original predictor seemed to mean.

**Next:** Search for patterns that no single question or branch reveals.

## VII. DISCOVER HIGHER-ORDER INTERACTIONS AND HIDDEN CONFIGURATIONS

Some social possibilities do not announce themselves in any individual predictor. Two variables can each look uninformative alone and jointly become decisive; three conditions can expose a stable minority concealed by every pairwise table. In other contexts, a strong apparent interaction is an artifact of sparse, high-cardinality memorization.

**Operation:** Search beyond one-axis contrasts for conditional interactions, suppression, synergistic and redundant evidence, local reversals and configuration-specific effects. Reuse exact same-respondent joints, not independently reconstructed marginals. Challenge proposed findings with alternative partitions and models, including out-of-fold prediction where appropriate and licensed uncertainty methods where available. Track the opportunity set not searched due to budget.

**Product:** An **Interaction and Conditional Surprise Map**, identifying where the joint changes the prediction, where additive summaries lose real evidence, and where the apparent novelty disappears under validation.

**Why:** The person we want to generate lives among combinations of conditions, not in isolated column-wise demographic bins. A system that sees only main effects cannot recover complex social possibility even if every individual association is correctly calculated.

**Next:** Stop thinking only in the form of predictors and targets. Examine the configurations **inside respondents**.

## VIII. RECOVER ATTITUDE CO-TRAVEL, CLOUDS, REPERTOIRES AND COHERENT EXCEPTIONS

The **Attitude Cloud** is one of the central original inventions worth taking all the way. It is not a cluster of demographic traits disguised as beliefs, and not an LLM's intuition that two attitudes sound related. It is an empirically observed network or family of response combinations co-traveling **within the same respondents**.

**Operation:** For a response or disposition-like measurement, discover which other eligible attitudes, judgments, reported practices and commitments occur with it, which do not, and in which social contexts their co-occurrence changes. Search pairwise and higher-order patterns. Preserve complete and partially observed joints; preserve overlaps, joint minorities and opposing configurations. Compare clouds under different position conditions. Notice when two nominally opposing judgments coexist, when a small coalition of attitudes has a coherent social location, or when one apparently homogeneous cloud divides into different internal repertoires.

Demographic and material positions condition these clouds; they are not automatically members of the *attitude* cloud itself. Nor does repeated co-selection prove that one attitude causes, justifies or psychologically suppresses another.

**Product:** A set of **Respondent-Coherent Repertoire Fields**: whole-case configurations, conditional co-signatures, clouds, competing subclouds, rare coherent combinations, and observed contradictions at measured grain.

**Why:** An adult with a specific material situation need not be a random sample of separate attitudes. Actual respondents combine commitments in surprising ways; those combinations, with their prevalence and alternatives, are vital to generating plausible variation without ideological stereotypes.

**Next:** Ask where these repertoires live in the wider social space and how their relations vary.

## IX. CONSTRUCT THE POSITION–DISPOSITION LANDSCAPE

A cloud tells us what travels together. A conditional topography tells us how responses vary by position. Now we need to understand the *geometry* of both: which positions are empirically near or distant under a declared comparison, where multiple repertoires overlap, where their boundaries are sharp, and which subcultures are simultaneously similar on one axis and antagonistic on another.

**Operation:** Construct admissible position and response geometries from exact measured dimensions and declared distance, similarity or neighborhood rules. Treat geography, class, migration, education, communal membership and institutional role as distinct, potentially overlapping dimensions. Identify **concentrations, boundaries, bridges, cross-position reach, isolated clusters, sharp transitions, internal heterogeneity and minority penetrations**.

A proximity score never automatically establishes a social relation, shared fate, political solidarity, historical homology or latent causal pathway. Geometry is a means of discovering comparative structure and proposing further questions, not a metaphysical map of society.

**Product:** A **Position–Disposition Geometry** with multiple lawful views: actual empirical neighborhoods, repertoire concentrations, relational contrasts and uncertainty. No compulsory universal embedding or compulsory nine-stratum schema.

**Why:** We need to know not only that one configuration is common among a group, but whether it is confined, widespread, situated at a boundary, common across opposed material positions, or locally unique.

**Next:** Ask which of these configurations is simply frequent, and which is entangled with power.

## X. BUILD PROFILES OF HEGEMONY, COUNTERHEGEMONY, SUBHEGEMONY AND SOCIAL COMMON SENSE

A view can be widespread without being hegemonic. A locally dominant repertoire can be nationally subordinate; a prestige-laden conception may be numerically rare; a politically empowered group may hold an internal diversity of views. The theory needs these distinctions because Rawlsing is about historically structured people, not a political opinion leaderboard.

**Operation:** For an observed complex or repertoire, ask separately about population prevalence, reach across position space, concentration in high-resource measured positions, independently grounded institutional or political-power alignment, geographic reach, temporal persistence where comparable, and stable countercomplexes. Where evidence supports it, examine how repertories gain prestige, travel across institutional boundaries, lose convertibility, remain localized, or coexist with antagonistic common senses.

Do **not** infer ruling-class status from high income alone or treat every national minority as subaltern. Do **not** infer a theory of domination from category frequency. These are evidence profiles supporting later governed interpretations.

**Product:** **Plural Hegemony and Social-Repertoire Evidence Profiles**: locally dominant, broadly diffuse, power-aligned, regionally bounded, contested, counterposed and unresolved complexes, often coexisting in the same society.

**Why:** This gives Field Forge the distinction between what an average respondent says and what a society's institutions may normalize, reward, stigmatize or impose—without pretending surveys already measure all those institutional relations.

**Next:** Introduce independently evidenced institutions, material constraints and historical power.

## XI. FIELD FORGE: FROM EMPIRICAL POSSIBILITY TO THE SOCIALLY CONSTITUTED FIELD

The strongest next question is no longer "what predicts Y?" It is **what becomes possible, facilitated, inhibited, costly, rewarded, stigmatized or foreclosed for differently positioned people—and through which material relations?**

**Operation:** Field Forge receives the authorized empirical possibility objects and, where independently governed evidence permits, composes them with material positions, institutions, resources, geographical arrangements, collective organization, political and economic power, cultural legitimacy and historical events. It distinguishes a **measured statistical contrast** from an **interpreted structural pressure**, a **specific institutional channel**, an **asserted causal mechanism**, and a **downstream actor's experienced response**.

A source-observed contrast does not make its own structural rule. The higher-order interpretation must say what independent fact or rule licenses claims about access, coercion, bargaining power, social reproduction, gatekeeping, resource conversion, institutional sanction or historical contradiction. Some currently known empirical results may enter this process only as exploratory evidence, and must be allowed to remain useful at that weaker grade.

**Product:** A **Material–Relational Field of Situated Possibilities** whose elements can preserve separate dimensions of opportunity, constraint, resource pressure, legitimacy, domination, competing normative orders, structural mediation and unresolved questions. Components need not collapse into one signed pressure scalar.

**Why:** Social position and belief can correlate without any particular mechanism; mechanisms can act differently across contexts; institutions have consequences even where a survey did not ask about them. This is where empirical regularities can be related to a historical world rather than left as a numerical social taxonomy.

**Next:** Examine contradiction inside and between these fields, while keeping empirical observation separate from psychological conflict.

## XII. MAKE CONTRADICTION, COMPETING PRESSURES AND ALTERNATIVE FUTURES FIRST-CLASS

Contradiction is not one universal negative number. It can mean the coexistence of responses that a simple ideology model expects to be exclusive, conflicting material incentives, divergent institutional demands, competing ways of interpreting an event, or pressures that pull a social position toward mutually incompatible actions.

**Operation:** Distinguish **observed response co-presence**, **semantic tension**, **material/structural antagonism**, **institutional friction**, **competing field pressures** and **actual actor-internal organization**. PYAT and the actor-independent Field Forge can discover and qualify the earlier layers; Sorok owns how those pressures become psychologically organized in a particular life.

For each relevant contrast, preserve the opposing possibilities and the conditions in which they operate. A person-like configuration may be socially possible without being statistically modal; a repertoire may be locally coherent despite being nationally unusual; a material position may make several mutually antagonistic responses simultaneously plausible. What appears contradictory in a formal typology may be ordinary coherence within a specific historical situation.

**Product:** A **Plural Contradiction and Pressure Landscape**: distinguishable, evidentially qualified tensions, coexistences and alternative trajectories, not a program for assigning everyone false consciousness.

**Why:** This is the basis of a nonreified historical subject. A developing person may later organize, repress, justify, sustain or transform these contradictions—but that psychological outcome cannot be hard-coded from their survey prevalence.

**Next:** Move the field through time without faking comparability or continuity.

## XIII. LEARN SOCIAL HISTORIES, NOT JUST CROSS-SECTIONS

Historical possibility changes. Positions, institutions, cultural meanings, prevalence, branch boundaries and repertoires move. But comparing waves is not simply subtracting two percentages.

**Operation:** Maintain source-local historical fields and, where exact measurement and population comparability exist, discover changes in distributions, positional relationships, cloud membership, minority bases, hegemony dimensions, structural pressures and conditional reversals. Distinguish actual trend, changing question wording, changing sample coverage, category drift, demographic reweighting and unobserved mechanisms. Do not transport across waves merely because two labels sound similar.

Where sources cannot be harmonized, preserve several distinct temporal witnesses and their disagreement rather than invent one universal chronological curve. A time- and place-bounded repertoire cannot be silently used as the default for a person born into an earlier period.

**Product:** **Historically Indexed Possibility Fields** and explicit **Change/Noncomparability Profiles**: what is observed at each period; what changes are legitimately inferable; what remains ambiguous or unmeasured.

**Why:** A hypothetical person must come from a historical world rather than a timeless demographic calculator. But a social trend is not yet that person's acquisition history.

**Next:** Make it possible to ask highly specific questions of the resulting field, even when the precise conditioning slice is sparse.

## XIV. BUILD THE POSITIONED-SUBJECT QUERY ENGINE AND HONEST SPECIFICITY WALKBACK

The empirical intelligence must become *usable*. A downstream caller will ask for possibilities conditioned on age, place, class location, religion-as-observed, household environment, educational path, existing jointly selected dispositions, or a combination of other allowed historical facts.

**Operation:** Treat each requested condition as an exact empirical proposition or governed derived input. Find support at that specificity. Where the exact joint exists and has adequate measured support, offer its actual whole-case distribution. Where the conditioning slice is too sparse, do not manufacture a highly specific answer: broaden along explicitly allowed dimensions, with a clear **walkback path** showing what specificity was relinquished and what distribution changed as a result. Where a proxy field is allowed, mark it as proxy and distinguish its observed origin from the requested world. Where no lawful candidate remains, abstain.

Crucially, conditioning does not imply independent feature sampling. The engine should either select from supported whole cases, use separately justified models of joint dependence, or expose that the requested configuration has not been observed. The caller should be able to ask which possibilities are still open, not only which one was selected.

**Product:** A **Positioned Possibility Service** returning the appropriate distribution of complete and partially supported configurations, exact context roots, candidate alternatives, local minority support, uncertainty, walkback, specificity loss and negative evidence.

**Why:** This is the bridge from general population intelligence to historically situated hypothetical adults—and the protection against reducing uncommon people to average demographic expectations.

**Next:** Let the generative consumer construct a hypothetical configuration that remains accountable to this space.

## XV. PRODUCE PARENT/ADULT CONFIGURATIONS WITHOUT INVENTING PSYCHOLOGICAL PERSONS

The parents' empirical entrance is the point where the knowledge becomes generative, but not yet the point where PYAT constructs a person.

**Operation:** Given a historical-fictional specification and any legitimate material conditions, use the Positioned Possibility Service to propose a **whole, respondent-coherent hypothetical adult configuration**. Draw or select from the actual supported conditional alternatives without illegitimate demographic independence or personality-type shortcuts. Preserve minority options even when the modal profile dominates. Allow context to be revised and remaining alternatives queried. Keep exact source and method limitations visible so another system can refuse an unsupported psychological inference.

A pair of independently plausible adults is not a survey-evidenced marriage. Empirical respondent relations do not establish these two fictional people's kinship, mutual attraction, role negotiation, life story or parenting. Those belong to separately supplied fictional-world conditions and Sorok's developmental and relational construction.

**Product:** **Historical Adult Empirical Entrance Offers**: distributions, sampled whole-case proposals, alternatives, contextual constraints, specificity history and qualified unknowns. They can be revisited or resampled as downstream construction chooses, but are never a bank of prewritten fixed personalities.

**Why:** A person who has some situated answer configuration still does not have an acquired psyche, a biography, a relational motive or a set of justified memories. PYAT is strongest precisely when it makes the empirical possibilities rich enough that Sorok need not invent a social worldview from generic prose.

**Next:** Make the empirical outputs usable by Field Forge and Sorok as live, queryable intelligence rather than as a one-off export.

## XVI. SERVE A LIVING FIELD, NOT A STATIC CATALOGUE

A single huge final JSON packet is not the system. The right output is an **evidence service** able to answer a downstream question, return an exact conditional object or typed refusal, and identify what new conditioning or authorized empirical observation would change it.

**Operation:** Compose reusable field capabilities through exact references. A consumer can ask for a modal profile, minority/exception alternatives, branch structure, whole-joint co-travel, reverse posterior, a source-qualified hypothesis, comparative field geometry, evidence for structural admission, historical difference, or a hypothetical adult possibility. The system need not instantiate one universal Field Forge carrier; the appropriate producers can serve their exact objects directly under existing owner contracts.

Make the field **progressively explorable**: each new condition updates the possibilities; each changed source root invalidates descendants that actually depended on it; an authorized reanalysis can produce a new lineage without destroying the previous historical record. The service can explain the precise empirical basis for a claim without exposing restricted respondent identities.

**Product:** A **Queryable Empirical-Social Possibility Intelligence** with reproducible answers, negative states, explanations, partial and complete discovery frontiers, cached-but-nonauthoritative derived views, and exact consumer boundaries.

**Why:** Theoretical ambition is wasted if the downstream system cannot obtain the relevant part at the moment it needs it, or if it must reread all source files to answer every new question.

**Next:** Let this intelligence itself help determine what should be investigated next.

## XVII. BUILD AN AUTONOMOUS EPISTEMIC EXPLORER

An empirical discovery engine must move beyond predeclared one-variable questions. It should be able to propose its **next empirical question** in response to what it has just discovered.

**Operation:** Maintain the active space of legal questions, measured opportunities, prior findings, untouched branches, surprising interactions, minority concentrations, unresolved interpretations, cost estimates and test status. Explore where an additional variable, a higher-order joint, a reversed conditional, a different position cut or a temporal comparison could change the current picture. Use learned novelty and expected discrimination as provisional priorities, while deliberately retaining budget for rare coherent minorities and contradiction-bearing branches.

The machine must also know when not to continue: insufficient source support, forbidden transport, excessive complexity, unstable evidence, missing question semantics or exhausted authorized search opportunities. Every inquiry should produce not only an interesting result but a **state of knowledge**: what was asked, what was learned, what failed, what remains open, and which new operation that result supports.

**Product:** The **Autonomous Social Discovery Frontier**: a replayable empirical inquiry graph that can discover previously unknown conditional subcultures and relation patterns, distinguish genuinely examined absence from unexplored territory, and generate a bounded next worklist.

**Why:** Without this, Rawlsing is still dependent on a human analyst to imagine every important subgroup and hypothesis. With it, the machine becomes capable of finding what its designer did not know to look for—without declaring itself a source-authority or sociology oracle.

**Next:** Subject the entire system to the hardest attacks before mistaking it for an achieved science.

## XVIII. ATTACK THE MACHINE WHERE IT WOULD BE MOST SEDUCTIVELY WRONG

The machine must be able to fail intelligently. Construct hostile worlds in which ordinary shortcuts produce persuasive false knowledge: high-cardinality memorization, rare coherent minorities pruned away, XOR-like interactions invisible to all pairs, overlapping multi-select responses treated as exclusive, unasked mistaken for "no," contextual reversals lost by global pooling, false cross-wave equivalence, apparent hegemony inferred only from a majority, structural pressure inferred from raw correlation, or fictional couple selection inferred from independent survey draws.

For each challenge the system should provide its own appropriately scoped answer, not a blanket “passed.” It must preserve exact risk/denominator conservation, complete alternative support, method uncertainty, partial search and external source/actor authority. Predictions should be evaluated on information they were not fitted to when such evaluation is relevant; a score that collapses out of sample must be allowed to collapse. Survey design or weighting can be applied only where legitimately issued. A genuine negative is a finding, not an embarrassment; a held result is neither a negative nor a success.

The acceptance standard is not that every capability be computed on every source wave from the outset. It is that the architecture can deliver its declared conceptual function **at an actual authorized source grain**, that we can say exactly which grains remain unproved, and that no final consumer is tricked into thinking a partial or exploratory demonstration provides broad authority.

## XIX. THE COMPLETE INTELLIGENCE CIRCUIT

Now the whole sequence can be stated as one continuous constructive operation:

**Real survey and historical observations** become truthful, versioned measured social worlds. Their questions are opened into complete **response morphologies**, including multiple selections, residuals and negative states. Those morphologies become **conditional topographies** over observed positions. Every lawful relation becomes **bidirectional possibility inference**. The engine discovers **where each apparent consensus fractures**, then follows majority, minority, alternative and residual routes recursively. It detects **hidden interactions**, and examines **actual same-respondent attitude clouds** and higher-order repertoires. Those configurations become a **position–disposition landscape**. Their reach, prestige, contestation and institutionally qualified alignments become **plural hegemony evidence**. Field Forge can add independently warranted **structural relations, resource constraints and pressures**. The competing tendencies remain visible as **contradiction and alternative possibility fields**. Comparable sources and historical moments become **changing, nonfalsified fields**. A positioned-subject request moves through this field with **honest specificity and walkback**, yielding **coherent hypothetical adult empirical entrances** and a continuing **queryable evidence service**. The system itself keeps searching through an **autonomous epistemic frontier**, learning where the social field still contains unexamined structure.

The terminal product is not a person and not a raw probability table. It is the **operative, historically conditioned landscape of possibilities** in which a genuinely constructed person might develop. PYAT supplies that landscape and its empirical qualification. Field Forge constitutes the legitimate actor-independent structural meaning. Sorok builds the actual organism, consciousness, contradictory psychic organization, lived activity, memory, relationships and developmental history. No one of these owners can quietly substitute for the others.

## XX. HOW HARDER CAN WE GO?

Even this vision is not an endpoint. Its full ambition is to make social possibility **dynamically interrogable**, not merely measured once. An observed minority can become a question about a material position; a surprising positional reversal can become a question about an attitude cloud; an attitude cloud can challenge the assumed boundaries of social subcultures; a temporal reversal can challenge the alleged persistence of a hegemonic repertoire; a structural mismatch can call for source-meaning review rather than an invented mechanism. The system should eventually be able to explain **why its own prior hypothesis was insufficient**, not simply emit a fresh number.

We can go further in three complementary directions. **Greater empirical depth:** recover interactions, multi-label grammars, weak signals, rare but supported configurations, and ever more complex conditional contexts without pretending tiny samples prove general laws. **Greater historical-material depth:** articulate the differences between prevalence, legitimacy, resource position, institutional power, actual opportunity and contradiction, always demanding the evidence that transforms one into another. **Greater generative fidelity:** provide Sorok with richly varied, source-qualified starting possibilities and changing social worlds without reducing the realized person to survey values.

This is the strongest form of determinicity: **not assigning everything a cause and a score, but discovering a vast, constrained and contradictory space of actual social possibilities, together with the conditions under which those possibilities become more or less likely and the limits of our ability to know why.**

If the completed machine can answer the question *“What could be true of somebody situated here, what else could be true, how would the answer differ under changed conditions, which of those alternatives is empirically supported, and what cannot legitimately be inferred?”*—across social positions, conflicting repertoires, historical transformations and structural fields—then the resurrected vision has become a live intelligence.

If it can answer only “the top predictor is education,” then no amount of new nomenclature will mean the resurrection is finished.
