**Determinicity** in the historic V550 sense is empirical answer-split informativeness: entropy/conditional entropy reduction, MI/NMI, Cramér's V, response uplift and recursive best conditional split. It is not an omnipotent demographic cause and is never one universal ranking independent of question, risk set, country, wave or subgroup. ## D. Historic scientific function already recovered: determinicity

Protected historical `data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_branching.py` blob `8f6d40e0d016d66e5a3b334b31747eaef752873f` has `indicator_summary_rows`, `secondary_indicator_summary_rows`, `branch_nodes_rows` and `outlier_cohort_rows`. V551's `ddm_v551_conditional.py` derives a conditional determinicity index after explicit source/support/role gates. **Critical historical statistical defect-risk, verified in source:** V550 `indicator_summary_rows` calculates baseline `distribution_entropy(y, weights)` over *all* response rows before restricting to records with an observed X, then evaluates `conditional_entropy(y[mask], x[mask], weights[mask])` only over the X-observed subset. The subtraction can therefore compare **different risk populations** and overstate the informativeness of X. Likewise `branch_nodes_rows` computes node entropy on all Y then candidate conditional entropy only on nonmissing X. With 100 respondents, 50 with observed X all giving Y=A and 50 missing X splitting 25/25 A/B, overall Y entropy is positive; the X-observed subset has a *constant* X and constant Y, so there is **no predictive discrimination within that subset**—yet the naive all-row baseline minus observed-subset conditional entropy reports an apparently strong gain. This is a **historical code-level error-risk**, not a proven defect in the current I03/I05 engine. Test the same eligible population on both sides of every candidate split, handle missing X as its own governed category when justified, or explicitly distinguish missingness prediction from answer discrimination. Also note that the historical mutual-information calculation can be weight-aware while the simultaneously reported NMI/Cramér's V calls are unweighted: never compare unlike estimands as one ranking.

V550 historically defaulted to branching depth **3**, support capped at **30** under its support formula, and expanded the **two most populous** eligible values per selected splitter. Those were *historical implementation heuristics*, NOT a current scientific claim that only two branches can be meaningful or that exactly 30 observations establish adequate certainty. Preserve the ideas and check whether current I03/I05/DHIKR already implements their estimands before any port.

Modern exact I05 (protected `pre553_discovery_exact.py`) insists on finite owner-issued candidate grammar and exact frontier evaluation; optional MI/NMI/Cramér's V/entropy diagnostics cannot *silently* reduce its declared complete search. Adaptive discovery (`pre553_discovery_adaptive.py`) may order exploration under authorized budget and replay, with ranking no semantic truth. **A practical optimizer should issue meaningful finite conditioned inquiries rather than enumerate every demographic-power-set intersection.** Exploration scores may prioritize work; support/uncertainty and issued source rights determine what can be claimed.
