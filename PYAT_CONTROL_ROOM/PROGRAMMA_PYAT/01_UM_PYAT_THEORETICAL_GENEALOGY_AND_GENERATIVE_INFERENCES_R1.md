## B. What kind of reality must the model take as primary?

### B1. Relation and activity, not a covariate bundle

Sève's discussion of the concrete historical individual distinguishes general socially produced *forms of individuality* from the detailed psychic history of a particular person. Wright makes class a relational concept of exploitation/control in production rather than just an income quintile. Social reproduction literature reveals that reproducing a life and the capacity to work includes unpaid care, domestic labor, housing, schooling, migration and coercion. Kohn's social-structure research argues for **proximate conditions of life** as an intervening route from social position to psychological process. These sources collectively expose a limitation of Rawlsing's earlier macro-persona shortcut.

**Extrapolation T1:** the basic causal subject of PYAT is not 'person with 40 drivers' but a **temporally located nexus of relations**: access to means of subsistence, work and care, control over time/space, institutional rights and coercion, socially mediated categories, relations to other people, political action, symbolic resources. National indicators can condition a *population context* only; they cannot instantiate the particular relations or histories of a given person.

**Consequence:** treat class, gendered reproduction, migration, empire and household not as independent sliders that simply add psychic weights but as entangled, sometimes antagonistic relations. A wage-earner who supervises labor, owns a small property, provides unpaid care and lives under police control occupies multiple relations that a single 'economic tier' does not describe. The exact current relational-class operationalization would require its own scientific court, not a retrofit of Wright into existing WVS columns.

**Falsifier:** if shuffling the label of country, income tier or class proxy changes the person's generated inner motive without any independently specified change in the situations, relationships, activities or mediating experiences through which that motive supposedly forms, the system is performing ecological typology, not historical generation.

### B2. Personality as formed through ongoing activities, not a latent essence inserted before them

Leontiev distinguishes activity, consciousness and personality and attends to how relations among motives/activities are organized through life. His example of a child's act and later painful meaning illustrates that personal meaning emerges in historically situated relations of activity, not simply from attaching an emotion label to an event. Dreier studies the conduct of everyday life as participation across places and relationships, rather than 'treatment' in a detached psychological container. Lahire shows that heterogeneous socialization can produce a plural repertoire of **conditional, contextually activated or inhibited** dispositions.

**Extrapolation T2:** motive, value, capacity, expressed behavior, habit, self-interpretation and durable disposition must not be one immutable driver. They are distinct relations in a sequence of lived practice; sometimes contradictory experiences coexist and different contexts call forth different repertoires. The unit of development is **changed participation in material, relational and symbolic activities**, not a static trait's change score. The fact a sourcefree generator can attach ten remembered childhood scenes does not prove that a psychological disposition was caused or consolidated by them.

**Consequence:** the history of actual obligations, refusals, care, leisure, work, institutional encounters, learned capacities and social conflicts is needed to justify any specific invented biography. Existing Sorok native CAN/ADP/V3/P3/M1/P6 owners are the proper place for a qualified actor formation rule; PYAT can supply source-graded conditions and alternative hypotheses, not insert its own psychogenesis engine. An externally observed custom is not evidence of its internal meaning to the person enacting it.

**Falsifier:** the same situated actor unconditionally outputs one preferred response because it scores high on an Enneagram or cultural driver, regardless of changing actual interlocutor, permission, personal commitments, contradictory learned history and material situation.

### B3. Structure does not act instead of people

Archer's structure/agency account emphasizes mediation by situated deliberation, while critical psychology (Holzkamp) places human capacities to act within socially given and transformable life conditions. Leontiev/Dreier and the other supplied sources vary in their philosophical premises; there is **no source warrant to declare one single settled synthesis**. Nevertheless, all undermine the one-step mapping 'structural hardship → mental content'. A rule can produce conformity, compromise, refusal, opportunism, solidarity or no relevant effect depending on conditions.

**Extrapolation T3:** structural necessity, available course of action, practical opportunity, pressure, subjective concern, selection, execution and outcome form a **modal sequence of nonidentical states**:
`structurally possible ≠ practically accessible ≠ incentivized/coerced ≠ meaningfully perceived ≠ desired ≠ attempted ≠ objectively realized ≠ learned from ≠ retrospectively narrated`.
This is not an already implemented state-machine schema; it is a generative conceptual test of future models.

**Consequence:** a dominant class's worldview can constrain institutions and reward deference while an individual's lived practice challenges or subverts it. The model must accommodate active counterpressure, incoherence and nonparticipation, not require a single 'most predictive' cultural pattern to win. Probability measures empirical prevalence; it does not turn that prevalence into social necessity.

**Falsifier:** suppressing a minority pattern as low-frequency 'noise' changes a theoretical claim from conditional coexistence to deterministic national essence; or a sampled observed choice is interpreted automatically as autonomous preference rather than as a bounded feasible choice under coercion.

## C. Historical time is not decoration

Elder follows the changing consequences of economic crisis across family and age trajectories; Mortimer attends to concrete adolescent work conditions, commitments and opportunities; Kohn relates self-direction to experienced conditions of work; Lahire and Dreier show the same individual's participation across heterogeneous settings. A 'society at time t' is not a uniform psychological climate: cohorts, regions, households, labor positions, schooling regimes and political experiences coexist.

**Extrapolation T4:** personality and practical possibility develop through **uneven historical time**—a person's age at a structural rupture, dependent relatives' circumstances, earlier acquisition, changed work relations and countervailing loyalties can change what an identical public event means. Social relations reproduce themselves through recurring action and can also be transformed by that action. An adult's response today cannot be justified by merely appending a 'historical trauma' scalar to a national mean.

**Consequence:** WVS wave comparability (F7), genuinely independent cross-survey nonpooling (F6), age/cohort/vintage source semantics, missing historical evidence and explicit no-change branches matter scientifically. Different waves may ask unlike questions to different populations. Without comparable longitudinal or event-history observations one can test hypothetical trajectories but **cannot claim that a particular person's psyche was observed evolving**.

**Falsifier:** two incompatible survey waves are pooled as though their respondents, wording and administered populations were identical, or a fictional childhood flashback is retroactively edited merely to make present behavior narratively consistent.

## D. The instrument and the source are themselves social relations

Surveys are not direct readouts of 'society.' Someone funds, selects, translates, administers, codes and interprets them; state categories may encode colonial, racial, class or gender regimes, and response availability depends on literacy, citizenship, censorship, safety, refusal, trust and social position. Luria's historically situated research into culturally mediated cognition itself occurred under Soviet social transformation; neither his findings nor the state's classifications escape contextual interpretation.

**Extrapolation T5:** the data collection process is **inside** the historical object under study. A source's categorical partition of society may itself be an effect of power. A translated item about 'respect for authority' is not guaranteed to refer to an invariant psychological quantity in two languages, and 'colonial trauma' cannot be established from colonial duration and contemporary trust proxies alone. What is absent from a survey is not necessarily absent from the community.

**Consequence:** the original35 source ledger, instrument edition, question/universe, sampling, translation, administration, respondent occurrence, source-use rights and typed nonanswer are epistemic structures, not mere SQL quality controls. QSM/semantic mapping cannot silently turn weak source comparability into strong psychological equivalence. Where no source witnessed something, preserve `UNKNOWN` / `NOT_MEASURED` / `INAPPLICABLE` / `HELD` as required, **rather than fabricate an observed zero**.

**Falsifier:** deleting a questionnaire branch, translation flag or source permission leaves native facts and their declared evidentiary authority unchanged, or a missing optional survey weight invalidates a perfectly legitimate RAW result while a fake imputed weight would admit it.

## E. The scientific asymmetry: measurable pattern is not causal mechanism, and neither is an inner person

The project explicitly permits `OBSERVED_EMPIRICAL`, `EXPLICIT_EXPERIMENTAL_MODEL` and separately qualified `STRONG_CAUSAL_STRUCTURAL` uses. Its real S04 F1–F4 and conditional-discovery programme is an architecture for exact observed relations and counterpatterns; it cannot on its own identify causal effects, person-specific interpretation or persistent psychological formation.

**Extrapolation T6:** precision and validity occupy **different axes**. A source-exact F4 same-respondent triple joint is better evidence of that joint than a fabricated product of pairwise marginals. It is **not** therefore evidence that one component causes the others. A learned model may explore the consequences of a declared hypothetical relation without the hypothesis becoming a source observation or native world law.

**Consequence:** each meaningful claim needs a clear provenance graph: raw source → semantics/question applicability → qualified operation/fate → exact respondent risk and denominator → descriptive discovery/negative → externally held issuer and claim grade → derived world/actor-facing use. Scientific grades should prevent *upward laundering* while allowing lawful observations to remain usable at lower grades. No metaphysical certainty from signed hashes: code and cryptographic proofs establish *which source-qualified computation occurred*, not whether the original category fully captured human reality.

**Falsifier:** a structural pressure model trained on national aggregates is asserted as the motive of an individual Sorok actor, or an independently validated C11 exact root is treated as proof of representativeness, causal identification or scientific authorization.

## F. The original Rawlsing model's internal contradictions are research objects

1. **Anti-essentialist philosophical claim vs national Enneagram weighting.** The document disavows fixed psychic types, then deterministically assigns country-conditioned softmax type distributions. It needs actual calibration/within-country heterogeneity/counterevidence and an account of mediating activities. Without these, type weights must remain **UNVALIDATED_PROPOSAL**, not a native psychological fact.
2. **'Health is not morality' vs high structural consonance as health.** The formula rewards harmony between cultural norms and reported outcomes and penalizes systemic stress. Such measures may track subjective comfort, exposure or institutional alignment, but cannot identify flourishing independently of domination. The dissident under oppressive institutions may suffer while acting with greater practical freedom. **Conformity is not health; suffering is not pathological inferiority.**
3. **'Not a character' vs LLM narration as constructor.** v3.4 insists on a historically plausible interior life, yet stages and symbols are prompt-assigned and LLM-completed. A psychologically coherent story is not equivalent to an independently unfolding causal biography. The later Sorok distinction between world event, actual actor activity, experienced access, acquired disposition and fallible retrospective narration is the necessary methodological correction; current implementation must still demonstrate it at its own grade.
4. **Resistance as self-making vs thresholds that pathologize defiance.** DRTS/RMT/TT formulae risk turning dissent plus trauma/isolation into 'psychopathic' or 'mad' subject templates. **No supplied empirical calibration licenses that assertion.** Specific contextual stress/behavioral outcomes may be modeled only under independently justified research design and appropriate constraints.
5. **'Colonial legacy' as structural history vs 'colonial trauma' as psychic score.** A colonizer's institutions and extraction transform land, labor, states, kin, law and life options. Duration/war counts cannot by themselves measure a particular person's subjective trauma or memory. Colonial history is a *source-graded structural relation* before it can become a claim about transmitted psychological meaning.
6. **Human agency vs national scalar 'culture'.** A society comprises conflicting institutions, dominant/subaltern projects, mixed practices and persons with different histories. A national scalar can be useful background only when its uncertainty, segmentation and explanatory limits are explicit.
7. **Generative richness vs evidence scarcity.** A fictional generated person may be richly plausible even when actual data cannot warrant the particular details. The correct honesty is `FICTIONAL_SCENARIO_CONDITIONED_BY_SOURCE`, not `STATISTICALLY_PROVEN_INDIVIDUAL`; where a native rule is absent, use explicit hypothesis or HOLD.

These contradictions should **not** be 'solved' by deleting the earlier documents or pretending the project already empirically rejected each old weight. They should be historically preserved as scientific hypothesis/architecture decisions requiring an authorized owner if revived. UM is a mechanism of **critical continuity**, not retrospective wishful editing.

## G. A compact generative ontology to use as a *test*, not a new adopted statistical model

A useful conceptual abstraction is:

`H_t` historically produced social relations (property/labor/care/empire/institutions/ecology)
→ `C_{i,t}` the person's actual constraints, access, obligations and relations
↔ `P_{i,t}` their situated activities and participation with others
↔ `D_{i,t}` acquired, plural, conditional practical dispositions and interpretations
→ `A_{i,t}` contemplated/attempted/committed activity in a concrete situation
→ `Y_{i,t}` objective consequences, counteractions, interruptions and losses
→ changed `H/C/P/D` over historical time.

**Crucial qualification:** the arrows express *proposed levels of mediation*, not proved causal coefficients or the sequence of currently implemented Sorok code. Structures and subjectivity interact; no one arrow is a shortcut around source science. The empirical data are partial `O_j = M_j(H,C,P,A,Y; instrument_j, population_j, time_j, authority_j)` observations. **There is no licensed inverse `O_j → individual's D` without additional assumptions and observations.** This is precisely why survey-cell accuracy and historically plausible generative fiction are different claims.

The often-missing middle terms are **participation, access, activity, power, relational dependence, contradictory socialization and actual temporal sequence**. If UM can teach an agent to ask for these before inferring a psyche, it has begun doing its job.
