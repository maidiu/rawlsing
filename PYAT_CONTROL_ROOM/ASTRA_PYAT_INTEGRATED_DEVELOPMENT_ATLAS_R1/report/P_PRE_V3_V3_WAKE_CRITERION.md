# P. PRE-V3→V3 wake criterion

The seam may wake only after a competent explicit decision verifies all applicable conditions:

1. **Declared scope:** the 35-wave objective and required product families are accounted for; difficult sources or functions are not silently removed.
2. **F1–F4 composition:** current consumer-visible distributions, bidirectional conditionals, recursive alternatives and same-respondent configurations retain their semantics, support and uncertainty.
3. **F5 world content:** actual competent structural products exist, accompanied by correct refusals. An empty world of correctly blocked cells is not sufficient.
4. **F6/F7 witnesses:** positive multi-field and temporal cases exist wherever claimed, under exact composition/comparability authority.
5. **Encounterable objective ontology:** required position, context, opportunities and constraints are served. Mechanism/consequence paths are required when the downstream interface actually demands them.
6. **F8 usability:** progressive conditioning updates plausible configurations while retaining minority/counterpattern possibilities and uncertainty.
7. **Operational integrity:** exact releases, current/historical selection, permissions, negatives, dependency closure and replay/reconstruction grades work across the served scope.
8. **Actor firewall:** possibility remains distinct from individual prediction, generated psyche, actor uptake and durable person change.

Same-respondent survey configurations do not establish household or coparent dyads. A dyadic claim needs its own sampling-unit/linkage authority; that is not a universal prerequisite for individual possibility service.

No test-count threshold, isolated canary, historical stage parity or owner inventory can replace this wake criterion.
