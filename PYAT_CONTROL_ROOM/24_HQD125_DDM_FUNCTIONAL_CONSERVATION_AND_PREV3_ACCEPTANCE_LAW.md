## 3. DDM is an empirical-social possibility engine

```text
DEMOGRAPHIC / MATERIAL POSITION → ATTITUDE POSSIBILITY
ATTITUDE / BEHAVIOR → DEMOGRAPHIC / MATERIAL POSSIBILITY
ATTITUDE / BEHAVIOR → OTHER ATTITUDE / BEHAVIOR POSSIBILITY
JOINT CONFIGURATION → CONDITIONAL SOCIAL / ATTITUDINAL POSSIBILITY
```

These are population-conditioned possibility relations.

They are **not** essentialist identity assignments.

Freeze:

`DEMOGRAPHIC_PROBABILITY != PERSONHOOD`

`POSITION_CONDITIONING != PERSONALITY_ESSENCE`

`POPULATION_FACT != POSITIONED_SUBJECT_POSSIBILITY != INDIVIDUAL_PREDICTION != V3_REALIZED_ACTOR_STATE`

## 4. Determinicity and forks are first-class products

A DDM predictor surface is incomplete if it preserves only a modal answer or one marginal association where the source supports richer branch structure.

The governed machinery must be capable of representing:

- modal / majority configurations;
- deviation from the modal configuration;
- minority convergence;
- supported exceptions;
- first splitters;
- secondary splitters;
- recursive splitters;
- conditional subgroup structure;
- multimodal and contradictory configurations;
- typed underpowered / unresolved / inapplicable branches.

Outliers are not noise by default.

Recursive fork information must not be silently flattened into a stereotype or a single country/group average.

- response-response co-signatures;

## 7. Parent / coparent possibility priors

The DDM substrate exists partly to support parent/coparent creation and later positioned-subject generation without independent-trait sampling or free invention.

The intended service is not:

`DEMOGRAPHIC_PROFILE → INDEPENDENT_RANDOM_ATTITUDES`

It is a progressively conditioned possibility service capable of asking:

- given this material/social position, what attitude/configuration branches are plausible?
- given already-realized attitudes or behaviors, what additional social positions or attitudes become more or less plausible?
- which minority or counterpattern configurations remain supported?
- what uncertainty remains after conditioning?

Parent/coparent priors remain **possibility distributions**.

They are not persons, biographies, personalities, or V3 actor states.
