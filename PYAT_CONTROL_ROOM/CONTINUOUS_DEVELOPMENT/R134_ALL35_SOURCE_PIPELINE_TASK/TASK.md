### Important ordering detail

The source has to be inspected under an applicable permission **before** response processing can be authorized and executed. Instrument/codebook metadata supplies the prospective item/role/workload specification. If bounded response inspection is indispensable for representation, that read needs its own exact permitted purpose; it cannot be hidden inside preimage construction. An admission check performed after responses were already decoded is not proof that all source access was gated.

For adaptive/recursive work, freeze the admissible generation law and bounds prospectively; record each generated child, ancestry, eligible/unvisited state and stopping reason. Do not pretend the final outcome-dependent search count was known in advance. Do not reduce required coverage to the earlier50/77 example.

## What differs across waves

- Country, split/form/module, household/person/event and occurrence boundaries must be respected. Repeated observations need actual respondent/occasion mappings; no adjacency/modulo reconstruction.
- Ranked/multiple-response answers retain component order/cardinality and joint topology. Parent stems are not guessed to be observed child items. Open text is not automatically numeric or automatically a scored opinion.
- Raw sample support and governed weighted population estimates are distinct. No unit-weight, comparability, smoothing or causal default is invented.
- Question/response semantics determine lawful conditioner and target roles. A well-formed numeric field is not automatically a social-position variable. Observed associations can support bounded structural priors without causal proof, while structural mechanisms require their own warrant.
- Cross-wave links require source/item/response/population/time/design comparability. Cross-family fields retain source identities; they do not create person-level joint observations across unrelated surveys.
