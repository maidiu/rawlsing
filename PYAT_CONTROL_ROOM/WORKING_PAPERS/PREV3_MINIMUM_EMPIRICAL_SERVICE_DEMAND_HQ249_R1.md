## 1. The actual question a service must be able to answer

At a declared **source population, time, release/as-of snapshot, supported use and sampling grain**:

1. What empirically supported possibilities for a target response/configuration `Y` are associated with a lawful material, social, or already-attitudinal condition `A`? Represent the distribution and its branches as `q(Y | A)`, **provided** the selected operation authorizes that interpretation.
2. After one further *compatible, jointly supported* condition `B`, what changes in `q(Y | A,B)`? A second accepted condition must actually alter the governed query/denominator/identity, **whether or not the numerical distribution changes**. Keep all supported alternatives, including minority/counterpatterns, uncertainty, exact joint cells, denominators and ancestry. Never infer same-respondent support from cross-survey aggregation.
3. What actor-independent structural context is legitimately relevant? Show at least two **distinct source-indexed survey-family contributions** to a common objective/world-facing service, preserving their separate universes, measurement and permitted composition operations. **Coexisting source-indexed contributions may satisfy the world contract where adopted; do not invent a pooled synthetic respondent table or a numerical structural-pressure multiplier.**
4. For a supported **same-survey** comparable-wave pair, what exact governed temporal product is available (increase, decrease, no change, changed branch/co-signature/structural relation, or a genuinely produced typed noncomparability)? Temporal labels and subtracted marginals alone do not confer comparability. The temporal product must be received by the selected structural/world/serving consumer, not merely exist in an isolated test.
5. Can the service return the correct current/as-of interpretation and **reproduce or invalidate the answer** after a governed input/release change? An affected dependency must become noncurrent or be lawfully recomputed; unaffected products must survive. The output must be consumable by the PRE-V3 positioned-subject/parent-prior **possibility** seam, without entering V3 or instantiating an actor.

If the actual use requires different public names, narrower declared claims or multiple complementary response types, D1/HQ should bind that exact existing architecture; do not impose one invented universal response object.

## 2. Three demanded output families — one coherent product program

### S1 — Progressively conditioned possibility (F1/F2/F3/F8)

**Necessary witnesses:** an admitted, reproducible `q(Y|A)` followed by `q(Y|A,B)`; different query/predicate/witness IDs, and demonstrably jointly supported cells or configurations. One conditioner alone must not impersonate two. Show position→attitude and, at the exact supported grain, attitude→position or attitude→attitude reversibility without swapping unsupported denominators or sampling units. Preserve modal, minority, counterpattern/outlier, secondary and recursive branches where the selected source and claim support them.

**Consumer:** the actual selected EE14/PRE-V3 possibility consumer and its promised currentness/replay contract—not a test-only precomputed scalar and not a realized individual.

**Source-supported null:** a faithfully measured weak/no-effect/diffuse distribution or governed empty/inapplicable state may be the truth. Merely failing to construct the query, lacking an owner, or returning `UNRESOLVED` for missing authority is not a positive product.

### S2 — Empirical co-travel, rich joint and selective conservation (F3/F4)

**Necessary witnesses:** source-native same-respondent co-signature / attitude-cloud and ordered joint configuration, including exact risk set, per-component meanings, routing, denominator/weight, uncertainty, minority support and recursive ancestry; release/currentness evidence carries the same information through the actual consumer. If special semantic relation authority is absent, lawful **empirical co-travel still exists** and must not be deleted. A special J-family semantic role is demanded only when its exact declared operation requires it.

**Consumer:** existing accepted rich C1 / I03 / discovery / recursive and selected downstream service. The accepted one-binding scalar N10E wrapper is *not* presumed to encode an arbitrary multi-binding response; use the already-lawful explicit multi-binding route unless a different fully lossless contract is independently demonstrated.

**Negative/contrast:** flattening the joint to marginals, truncating minority branches, substituting fake same-respondent identities or omitting recursive lineage must fail the selected acceptance court even if the modal result still looks reasonable.

### S3 — Objective structural, multisource and temporal world (F5/F6/F7)

**Necessary witnesses:** two independently admissible source-family structural/field products, each with its own universe/respondents, scope, operation and evidence identities, actually entering the same declared objective-world view as **distinct contributions**; and one exact source-family comparable-wave product of authentic measured movement/no-change/noncomparability, passed through a lawful temporal→world consumer. Supported source-indexed coexistence is not a license for pooling; same-survey wave population snapshots are not individual longitudinal histories.

**Consumer:** identified N9 world/current-as-of selection and N10W or other actually adopted world-conditioned service path with visible response, provenance and effective-use closure. A Field Forge relation or existing GT2 local `CONTEXT` alone does not prove world service completion or that context changed probabilities.

**Negative/contrast:** raw time labels, unexplained signs, unqualified wave pairing, averaged cross-family respondents, invented causal pressure or a disconnected source report must not pass.

**Illustrative query sequence only (not executable schema, not numerical prediction):**
