# Why Rawlsing has this architecture — history as an anti-regression constraint

## Originary person/world aim
The original Structural Persona / Pax Marxica / developmental-person project aimed to generate socially produced yet non-determined persons living in historically specific, material/social worlds. Personhood is formed through needs, care, power, institutions, relationships, contingencies, contradictions, agency and time. An abstract trait generator or demographic-to-personality lookup is invalid. The later Governing Spirit and Terminal Demand Envelope amplify this rather than replace it.
