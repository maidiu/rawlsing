## 0. HAQQMULCH's first law: BUILD PYAT, BUILD PYAT, BUILD PYAT

MULCH asks us not to worship yesterday's architecture. **HAQQMULCH adds a stronger, sharper warning: not even an intellectually more beautiful scientific architecture is a success if it makes the working PYAT impossible, unaffordable, indefinitely deferred, or useless to the actual downstream inquiry.**

The user's controlling first, second and third commitments converge on **getting PYAT built**. Those are not three new invented numerical acceptance gates; they are the repeated priority that must govern practical choice. Never translate a conceptual aspiration into compulsory storage, more exhaustive all-combinations analysis, immense precomputation, repeated work, large-model spending, or new engine design **unless** its additional downstream truthfulness and usability outweigh its *measured* costs.

**The sharp distinction: sacrifice optional methodological sophistication, exhaustive coverage, maximal resolution and preferred ontology when they prevent viable construction; do NOT sacrifice the truth-status of an empirical claim.** A cheaper estimate, smaller admissible slice, sourcefree approximation, explicitly modeled alternative or legitimate unknown can serve the machine. A knowingly false denominator, fictitious respondent joint, invented survey permission or causal claim manufactured from an association cannot be promoted to science by invoking the three commitments. If a scientifically admissible product cannot be computed affordably, report that limit, find a coarser or cheaper *honest* product, or alter the downstream question. **Honest inability is preferable to fake precision; functioning approximation is preferable to endless unimplemented perfection.**

**The unit to build:** source-qualified **demographic/social configurations** and their **within-configuration distributions, variation, associations, joint co-travel, minorities, conditional changes and uncertainty**, at manageable resolution. PYAT is not tasked with eagerly constructing fictional people, all possible respondent-shaped tuples, a national personality vector, or a synthetic household database. Retaining exact respondent identity and joint occurrences inside the empirical substrate is nevertheless nonnegotiable because without it a *configuration-level* claim about same-respondent co-occurrence may be false. SOROK's later particularizing sample is its operation, not a completed PYAT row.

## 1. The inviolables: firm ends and truth boundaries, flexible means

1. **Build-first, reuse-first.** The decision target is the smallest genuinely functioning current source→C11→I03/I05/F1–F4→conditioned-configuration/first-consumer improvement. Do not rebuild an accepted writer, accepted reader, source grammar or query engine merely to enact a new philosophical vocabulary. Historical V547/V550 are witnesses and possible algorithms, not automatic implementation authorities.
2. **Observed meaning cannot be reverse-engineered from the desired answer.** Original source/wave, actual administration/eligible/asked/answered populations, source-native missing and multi-response topology, respondent identity, permitted weighting and use, exact negative and versioned provenance remain distinguishable. A computational shortcut that conflates them is not a permissible MULCH.
3. **Real country conditioning, not the word “within-country.”** In the ordinary single-country question, country is part of the *admitted population scope*, not a rival splitter and not a mere variable excluded from the ranking. Country variation, language/edition differences, geography and historical comparability remain indispensable for other products, but must not masquerade as within-country predictor informativeness.
4. **Demographic differentiation without determinism.** “Determinicity” is the **degree and reliability of an observed measured predictor's contribution to answer splits under specified conditions**; it is not an actor's fate, the causal force of a category, a unique essence of a population or a substitute for the distribution's dissenting tails.
5. **Retain minority paths and legitimate unsearched space.** Modal answer ≠ all answers; top two branches ≠ all branches; low prevalence ≠ impossible; no observed instance ≠ established exclusion. Do not spend unlimited cycles preserving every tiny leaf in memory, but do preserve its ability to be queried in the original substrate, its known supported alternatives and its unsearched/underpowered/unsupported status.
6. **The real machine's capacity is an epistemic constraint.** The actual host's available RAM, scratch/retained disk, source width, CPU/wall, cold reopen and repeated-query work determine which algorithms, widths and resolutions are viable. An unexplained inherited benchmark is not hard law. The earlier `1/10/100 distinct-query` gate is explicitly **NOT BINDING**; an older 1/10/100 **selected-column** measurement is not the same experiment. Even the historical 6 GiB RSS/100k distinct-respondent performance courts must be interpreted against current host/user authorization, not treated as sacred machine-independent limits.
7. **A changed downstream operation may be the right MULCH.** If a truthful, useful PYAT service can expose a smaller conditional query, bounded partial joint, narrower confidence statement, on-demand branch, more useful field/period restriction, or differently staged evidence to SOROK, prefer that to materializing a high-dimensional universal object. The downstream consumer may change its protocol **by its actual owner**, preserving its real needed function. SOROK must not inherit fabricated source probabilities or a prechosen psyche.
8. **Qualified outcomes are not uniform “green.”** Distinguish observed, estimated under an issued survey design, exploratory diagnostic, modeled, partially identified, descriptive, structurally qualified, sourcefree, held and unknown. A fail-closed engine with no useful positive products remains unfinished; an approximate positive product must never borrow a stronger authorization grade.
9. **Every real upgrade has a first consumer and economic witness.** Demand a changed positive fact or changed legal refusal, a downstream use and observed cost; otherwise stop abstracting and go back to the existing machine. Do not mint new mandatory work packages merely because a scientific question can be written down.
10. **Inherit actual owner authority.** The general primer's oath, canonical PROGRAMMA, verified protected pair, source/science issuers, existing #997/#998/#1006/#1097 owners, independent reviews and protected receiving survive. HAQQMULCH cannot appoint itself a coding or scientific owner, and must not present a pinned sourcefree prototype as a completed original35/SEAM/actor system.

**What may be mulched:** predictor score formula, branching rule, search order, materialization strategy, precision/width, selected target estimand, cache policy, request granularity, cross-stage responsibility assignment, historical nomenclature, and even the boundary-facing PYAT product shape when a real first consumer benefits.

**What may not be silently mulched away:** original-source truth, response meaning and risk-set identity; honest evidence/model/use grades; real same-respondent dependence; minority/uncertainty/negative distinctions; independent-world versus survey-attitude distinctions; current protected/owner boundaries; and the obligation to actually deliver a functioning machine.

## 2. Recover the *real* historical country decision—and its implementation contradiction

**Intended historical decision.** V547 `build_question_determinicity_summary_v547.py`, protected blob `3fc894501ce77c6519a2b32213f3f017465553e5`, explicitly records `analysis_mode="within_country"` and `country_code_policy="baseline_zero_predictor"`. It computes country mutual information for comparison, excludes `country_code` from the ordinary top-predictor competition, and was evidently trying to prevent country from trivially winning the ranking of social position predictors.

**What the same code actually does:** `compute_rows` (approximately lines 502–558 on that source cut) joins all respondent/source observations for each question, obtains `country_code_score = mi_score(merged["country_code"], target)` and scores the other predictors across the same **pooled multi-country `merged` frame**. It does not partition the observed target population by country before calculating those rankings. Labeling that result “within_country” **does not implement within-country conditioning**. Excluding a country variable from the finalist list cannot eliminate between-country distribution differences embedded in education, religion, income or other candidate predictors. A confounded candidate can still win.

**Why country can dominate the pooled contest:** it indexes many distinct sampling, source-language, measurement, social, institutional, regional and historical differences at once, and for some waves is a strong **dataset/administration proxy**. That is neither arbitrary statistical magic nor evidence that nationality uniquely causes opinions. Its aggregate predictive strength may conceal exactly the variation PYAT should learn inside a historically situated national sample.

**Correct default:** treat `(source identity, instrument/wave, country, target question, eligible/asked universe, intended estimand)` as the scope for the first *country-specific* determinicity inquiry. In a legitimate fixed-country run, country is constant and cannot compete as a within-country splitter. Produce separate country-specific distributions and ranks where the original design supports them. Keep cross-country contrasts in a **separate, properly qualified cross-population question**, using genuine common instruments/estimands/response translations and F6/F7 as appropriate; neither erase country from history nor pool unlike respondent populations to increase N automatically.

**Important limit:** within-country is a **default scientific question**, not a dogmatic rule for every possible original source. A single-country source, a region-specific survey, a national minority sample, a comparable cross-national estimand or a small country's low support may require a different explicitly declared population or model. If the fixed-country grain is too sparse, **walk back or return uncertainty**; do not silently treat another country's respondents as the same country. A qualified pooled/partial-pooling model is a modeled operation with transport assumptions, not ordinary native same-population truth.

## 3. Construct the determinicity mechanism: conditional, costed, reusable

The present work is **not** to adopt a single “most deterministic variable” coefficient. It is to build a **callable, responsive, country-bracketed, population-consistent predictor-split inquiry** out of existing empirical machinery.

### 3.1 An issued query, not a precomputed demography lattice

Specify the input:

- `S`: exact source/edition, wave, permitted use, respondent-root and authority;
- `C`: source-qualified country/geography or another explicitly admitted geographic population;
- `D`: already established demographic/source-measured conditions *within S,C*, including valid original response conditions where needed;
- `Y`: exact question/response target, ordered or multi-response topology, denominator/risk set, treatment of unanswered/inapplicable states and selected raw versus design-weighted estimand;
- `X_1...X_k`: a **finite, source-observed candidate predictor catalog** whose semantic roles and joint co-administration make the comparison meaningful;
- `B`: current supported economic envelope and requested answer depth: available resources, empirical support, permitted search kind, acceptable approximation/uncertainty, cost of a fresh build versus reuse, and real downstream consumer.

Use the extant `C11` source-preserving substrate, `I03` exact conditional/risk computation, `I05` bounded exact discovery or legally separate adaptive exploration, and `S04/DHIKR` analytics/diagnostics where competent. No second respondent database, new compulsory all-cubes index, or up-front fictional-profile warehouse.

### 3.2 Two indispensable checks before ranking: actual population and comparison eligibility

For a candidate `X`, let `R(D,Y,X)` be its source-qualified same-respondent **eligible/asked/target and observed-predictor population** under the chosen missingness policy. Calculate both the baseline response entropy **and** conditional entropy over **that same R**:

`gain(X;Y | D,R) = H(Y | D,R) − H(Y | D,X,R).`

A result computed with `H(Y)` over all rows but `H(Y|X)` over only X-observed rows is *not* a valid answer-split gain. Missing X may be its **own explicitly source-defined candidate state** when appropriate; then it is a meaningful measurement/availability predictor, not magically the value “no.” Missing Y, not asked, refusal, nonresponse, repeated answer, multiple choice and ordinal codes cannot all be assigned the same numeric null.

**Across different X candidates**, even individually valid pairwise complete-case gains may refer to different subpopulations. A numeric ranking is warranted only when the comparison populations/estimands are genuinely comparable. Use a common qualified comparable cohort when it leaves adequate information, or a single source-legitimate modeled missingness policy with a clear coverage disclosure. Otherwise retain **candidate-specific gains without a false global ordinal rank**, mark coverage and compare only in a compatible subfamily. Do not solve missingness by quietly discarding most of the population and calling the result general.

The same rule governs interactions: a pair's joint gain must be compared with each single predictor **over the pair's exact common respondent population**. V547 `pair_mi_score` can compute pair MI after three-column complete-case filtering while its single MI scores use separate pairwise complete-case populations; subtracting one from the other need not estimate an actual extra interaction gain. Test and repair the estimand, not just the implementation syntax.

### 3.3 What to estimate and how to choose

For each admissible `X` calculate *source/estimand-compatible* within-configuration evidence:

- **Required minimal content:** response distribution before/after X split; raw eligible/asked/observed N and valid design weights, status/coverage, value-specific branches including minority responses, original morphology/positive zeros and support/uncertainty;
- **Candidate discrimination:** empirical conditional mutual information or entropy reduction on the same population, normalized improvement when meaningful, plus answer-specific contrasts. A strong *raw* MI may be biased upward by high-cardinality splitters and small cells: penalize or qualify complexity and compare against a source-appropriate baseline;
- **Reliability:** compare to a simple supported no-split estimator; use a small, justified design-aware held-out or resampling check **only where feasible and scientific design permits**. Do not impose heavy universal cross-validation on every tiny cell. Report instability instead of fabricating precision;
- **Additional predictors:** after conditioning on the legitimately observed branch, recompute *incremental* gain on that new risk set. A predictor strong across the country can add almost nothing inside an already specified class/education/age configuration; another can become important there;
- **Scientific meaning:** a ranking is a *question-specific diagnostic*, not a timeless hierarchy of social causes. A structurally conditioned power mechanism requires separate material evidence and competent Field Forge rules.

Do **not** concatenate MI, NMI, Cramér's V, coverage, uncertainty, causal “importance” and resource cost into one unexamined magical score. Return **a small transparent comparison vector and an evidence-qualified ordering** when comparability holds. If the best candidate is unstable, tied or underpowered, state that. “Most deterministic” is conditional and may have no justified single winner at the desired resolution.

### 3.4 Which branch becomes the next question?

A useful branch is neither “always the maximum information gain” nor “all possible intersections.” Issue a bounded next inquiry using:

1. eligibility and correct same-source meaning;
2. demonstrated or promising *incremental* discrimination relative to the current D, with uncertainty, subgroup coverage and comparison validity;
3. ability to expose meaningful minority/counterpattern variation rather than merely produce a purer national mode;
4. cost in actual prepared-cohort reuse, additional I03 reads, new index construction, scratch/RSS, output retention and first-consumer value;
5. an explicit finite **exploration budget** for counter-hypotheses/rare configurations and an **unsearched register** for what was not evaluated.

An inexpensive preliminary ranking may **order the next eligible questions**, but cannot turn an exploratory search into an exhaustive source-authorization claim. Modern `pre553_discovery_exact.py` explicitly rejects diagnostic-score-driven pruning under an exact `NONE_EXACT_EVALUATE_ALL` contract. If an exhaustive frontier is genuinely required, **declare a small affordable issued frontier and evaluate it completely**. If its full product is not required, use the separately authorized adaptive route with honest partial coverage. **Do not optimize by lying about completeness.**

**Resolution is a controller, not an ideology.** It may reduce simultaneous conditioner arity, postpone a costly interaction, return a broader coarser distribution, use a sparse rather than dense joint, offer a source-qualified underpowered interval or request another condition *later*. It may not rewrite the original source, silently drop a supported minority, or claim an unsearched cell is zero. Previous V550 `max_depth=3`, `DEFAULT_SUPPORT_CAP=30`, top-two branch expansion, or an agent's `1/10/100` query-count trial remain **historical heuristics/benchmarks**, not inherited compulsory truths.

### 3.5 The retained product and downstream use

Return an **economical configuration-level conditional topology**, not an individual:

- exact root `S/C/D/Y`, source/codebook/estimand/use and as-of scope;
- baseline response morphology and distribution, all relevant denominator/missingness/weight distinctions;
- a bounded set of eligible splitter candidates with matched-population informativeness, coverage, support/uncertainty, alternatives, observed zeros and source-derived nonanswers;
- the first selected or candidate next refinements and how the answer split changes; justified conditional/walkback levels;
- genuinely observed same-respondent 2+ / k-way relationships when demanded, without inventing cross-survey persons;
- economical re-query/replay handles, which result costs what, and what remains unsearched/model-only/unresolved;
- a named actual `I03/I05 → S04/DHIKR → FF01/N10E` first reader and, where independently qualified, later `N9 AND N10E → N10W`; **no sampled person and no psyche**.

An architecture that yields a sophisticated rank but cannot be consumed by the existing conditioned-configuration service **fails HAQQMULCH**. An architecture that returns fewer but correct, re-queryable, graded results at materially tolerable cost can be the superior machine. If SOROK's interface needs an overlarge object, **challenge that consumer's representation** through its rightful owner; prefer on-demand field/context questions with typed uncertainty over coercing PYAT to precompute billions of profiles.

## 5. HAQQMULCH decision court: when to savage, retain, adapt or stop

For any proposed method/engine change, demand a concise **before → after** result with these questions answered in order:

| Court | Binding question | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| **Reality** | Is the needed source/predictor/response joint actually measured, interpretable and authorized? | Return typed missing/held/partial or redesign the question; no invented statistics |
| **Functional contribution** | What *new real conditional fact/contrast/branch/minority/first-consumer use* does it produce? | Reject conceptual refactor; reuse existing output |
| **Physical feasibility** | What is measured cost on the actual current machine and relevant workload? | Reduce grain/retention/search; bounded optimization; do not rebuild speculatively |
| **Total-machine conservation** | Do F1–F8, same-person coherence, field/time/unknown rights and downstream possibility remain attainable through existing or changed interfaces? | Reconstitute the interface without silently losing the old function |
| **Ownership** | Which existing code owner can lawfully change it and who independently checks the result? | Stage read-only falsifier; no protected or science self-grant |
| **Stop** | Does the working bounded operation now deliver the needed result at acceptable cost? | If yes, **stop theorizing and build the next working consumer**, rather than restart the entire mulch |

An ambitious approach can be **rejected because it is not implementable within current limits** even if it is more statistically refined. A historically simple method can be retained if it produces honest, useful answers. **In neither case may cost or downstream convenience authorize fabricated observations.**

## 6. Seven focused falsifiers, no arbitrary benchmark religion

**H-COUNTRY:** Make two countries with different Y baselines and a demographic X that is correlated with country but not with Y *within either country*. The pooled X–Y association may be strong; **each correctly country-fixed split must be null/near-null**. Excluding country from predictor labels without conditioning must fail.

**H-MISSING-X:** Keep observed X constant among its observed cases, change only the Y distribution of X-missing respondents. An observed-X conditional gain must remain zero; if missing-X is explicitly modeled as an eligible state, report the separate missingness contrast, never disguise it as discrimination by observed X values.

**H-PAIR-COMMON:** Give X1, X2 and Y different observed overlap sets. Score individual and pair interactions on the identical qualified common sample for any direct pair-gain comparison; otherwise mark the gain not comparable. Catch the V547 pairwise-complete versus triple-complete mismatch.

**H-RESPONSES:** A source-administered multiselect/ranked target, routing-derived nonanswer, true valid empty, and absent response must not collapse into the same Y cell. A false gain driven by coding collapse is RED.

**H-MINORITY:** Under the same source scope, create a rare but real co-occurring minority configuration omitted by the top-two modal branch expansion. It must remain queryable/evidence-graded, even if its expensive recursive expansion is deferred.

**H-BUDGET:** Fix the original source and estimator, reduce the available runtime/memory/search budget. The service must return a coarser but valid output or honest partial frontier; it must not turn unexamined alternatives into observed negatives or secretly alter its scientific claim.

**H-CONSUMER:** A real I03/F1–F4 fact changes under an eligible source value/condition; the S04/DHIKR/FF01 or N10E first reader must actually change or explicitly refuse at the correct independent-law boundary. A beautiful self-contained ranking with no recipient is NOT completion.

These cases are **falsification proposals within existing S02/S04/REMONT owners**, not seven new commissions, seven new mandatory whole-estate gates or an instruction to hold software until full science perfection. Perform the smallest feasible tests that discriminate current code from the required behavior; record actual execution and separate test/source grades.
