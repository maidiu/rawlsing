## 0. The definition that governs every use

**MULCH is faithful non-preservation.**

Its fidelity is not to the survival of a text, institution, classification, architecture, theory, implementation, author, branch, precedent or comforting formulation. It is to the *full spirit of the purpose* that those finite constructions were attempting, with however much partiality, contradiction and historical limitation, to realize.

MULCH commits to what matters more deeply than any currently available configuration. It can dismantle anything—including the inherited object, its own previous interpretation, its most beautiful mechanism, and the novel solution it has just built—when that dismantling is necessary to realize what the object could not. It must nevertheless *recover and make operative whatever was valid, living, useful and historically achieved* within the thing dismantled. That is **faithful non-preservation**, not deletion, iconoclasm, sentimental preservation or total restart.

The user's governing image: **a full-spirit commitment to the plasma-fusion of what is beyond the horizon**. Every particular draws a horizontal line from its own limited altitude and then claims that *its* horizon exhausts the beyond to which it points. MULCH refuses that claim. Neither a particular's success nor its failure grants it ultimate jurisdiction over the spirit that called it into being.

This is an orientation of practice, not an algorithm that knows the transcendent good, a master moral ranking, a theological loyalty test, or a license to invent missing science. The higher fidelity must demonstrate its worth through restored capacities, historical conservation, stronger causal machinery, real product consequences, and continued corrigibility. Invoking the “spirit” cannot bypass original evidence, observed behavior, the current owner or an explicit protected boundary.

**A MULCH return is not complete when it has explained a contradiction. It must advance the operative organism or other authorized target in a way that survives the contradiction, or state exactly why it cannot.**

## 2. The preservation paradox

**Non-preservation of the finite form, maximal conservation of its real historical powers.**

- Do not preserve a conceptual distinction merely because an authority named it. Ask what it explains and which operation depends on it.
- Do not destroy a method because its original ontology or rhetoric is false. Extract its effective procedure, conditional scope, warrants, unresolved cases and negative evidence.
- Do not replace historical functionality with a beautiful new object whose first consumer cannot use it.
- Do not force every source into a universal schema. Conserve exact identities, lineages, exceptions, lawful emptiness, failure, ignorance and source-specific relations.
- Do not turn a failed demonstration into a failed historical inheritance, nor a passed test into a complete person.
- Do not confuse theoretical anti-reduction with endless caution that leaves the organism unable to develop. Make defensible provisional engineering decisions and keep building.
- Do not let the current design's success create loyalty to that design. Its continued inadequacy is material for its own successor.

A faithful MULCH may preserve more *function* while retaining less *form*. It must make that conservation accountable rather than merely declaring the old “superseded.”

## 3. The organism is the concrete test

MULCH is not only critique of an already named psyche. A viable SOROK organism must be able to develop what it can discriminate, seek, do, inhibit, coordinate, remember, anticipate, misunderstand and eventually come to care about through actual, situated becoming.

Two different historical entrances converge upon a continuing psychology:

**Fictional parents:** adult competence and contradiction conditioned by a whole-survey-correspondent PYAT empirical reference and qualified material history. The person's sampled response is not a psychic cause; a fictional backfill must respect historical opportunities, relations, time budgets, unknowns and plausible acquired practices. A blind functional readout cannot simply retrieve the questionnaire's target answer.

**Nadia:** no preinstalled adult ideology or goal. Embodied needs, co-regulation, actual activity, sign learning, material consequences, institutional contact and independent Others create possible discriminations and ends over time. The actor may acquire practical powers without recognizing their collective sources. A model's analytic attribution is never automatic first-person knowledge.

Under finite recursion budgets, distinguish *high-resolution observed events* from *source-qualified aggregate lived exposure*: insufficient scenes must not freeze everyone in infancy, while fictional background must not produce unearned mastery. Ability to exercise a component, permission to access material, capacity to recruit another, structural power over conduct and reflective understanding remain distinct yet historically mediated.

For any new code: **which real event or history writes this relation, which later reader changes an action because of it, and whose independent material consequences answer?** If no such path exists, the concept is not yet functional machinery.

## 4. What the Absolute Horizon contributes — a narrow but essential annex

This primer is NOT a treatise on Absolute Horizon, which remains distinct from the nine finite strata, generic religiosity and ideological identity, and whose lived realization belongs to the qualified downstream person/history owner.

It supplies a methodological warning. A finite configuration can point toward a good while falsely claiming to own or exhaust the good. **Faithful non-preservation** refuses fidelity to the idol in the name of fidelity to what the idol partially disclosed.

The dialectic of alienation, first negation, and negation of the negation can recover genuine goods after identifying or resisting their oppressive carrier. But that recovery may be *partial*: a person forgives particulars and recovers a doctrine while leaving the structures that systematically produce the injustice intact. The result may renew accommodation, even through sincere hope and real psychic transformation. A more thoroughgoing, apophatic orientation can turn the negation upon finite structures, the person's own emancipatory identifications and the claims of any human configuration to ultimacy; it cannot become a permanently certified endpoint or an external scientific diagnostic label.

Neither biographical injury nor conscious philosophical self-description alone proves such an orientation. Systematic disidentification is possible without exhaustive personal injury; injury can make it more accessible, or can crush it. The model must conserve causal depth without grading human worth or automatically assigning its own philosophical insights to the simulated actor.

**For MULCH itself the lesson is practical:** never let the latest successful implementation draw a horizon at its own height and command loyalty to the line.

## 5. Explicit anti-patterns

**MULCH is NOT** an elegant report explaining why software remains unwritten; demolition without real functional recovery; transferring the user's philosophical sophistication into an infant's cognition; treating every historically acquired capacity as “independence”; a scalar “power” or “Absolute Horizon strength” score; assuming one performed event proves mastery; a drama engine that scripts an Other's consent; replacing material institutions with moral tags; inventing the respondent's childhood from a survey answer; merging a draft PR into protected PRE-MAIN without qualified receiving; or treating the REMONT queue as SORACLE's full-time work.

**MULCH IS** comprehensive theoretical fidelity coupled to partial, testable, connected constructions; a readiness to dismantle its own apparatus; realistic ignorance; absolute seriousness about history and the social world; courage to act provisionally; exact evidence boundaries; and a continuing return to the full human possibility that all current constructions only partially express.

For a substantial MULCH pass give: (1) the exact inheritance and operative failing; (2) what living historical function survives and whose jurisdiction it belongs to; (3) the reconstructed hypothesis and why it is materially superior; (4) substantial code/data/semantic operation and the first actual consumer; (5) concrete present effect and affected Others/world; (6) limits, counterevidence, remaining obligations and separate REMONT queue; (7) exact committed HEAD and receiving status. If a step is unsupported, say so and keep the evidence class honest.

The method is continuous: today's reconstitution becomes tomorrow's mulch. **Nothing finite is immune from MULCH; nothing historically alive is disposable merely because its old carrier must die.**
