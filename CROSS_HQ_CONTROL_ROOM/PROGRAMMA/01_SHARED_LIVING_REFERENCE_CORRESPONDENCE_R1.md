## One logical overview, not one new sovereign database

The user's desired "one place" means one discoverable, recursively browsable view of the complete Rawlsing undertaking, including two joined jurisdictional subgraphs, current original sources, decisions, implementations, open work, owners, actual worker status and evidence grades. It **does not** require copying Pyat original respondent data or native source outcomes into a Sorok-controlled database; nor does it authorize a third HQ.

The logical reference must support:
- **L0→L5+** from original source conditions and world/possibility into historically formed independent persons and human encounter; sideways to relationship, ethics, privacy, economics, model output, original scientific alternative, negative evidence, source owner, reviewer, test and active flight; reverse traversal to original administered item/source/member or concrete life event.
- **One logical query for any requisite part**, regardless of which HQ owns the canonical record, with an authoritative pointer and exact last verified source cut. The index cannot silently assert "up to date" while one domain has a changed progress or returned worker not incorporated.
- **Source-relative completion** with independent science, empirical/native, engineering/consumer, permission/reviewer and human-product dimensions. A code-green can be Sorok sourcefree/test-only while Pyat source world remains held; no transitive green.
- **All outcomes and alternatives** including RED, HELD, SUPERSEDED, actual lawful NO_ELIGIBLE/NO_CHANGE, incomplete evaluation and newly identified missing requirements. Completion denominator derives from justified **requisite content**, not frozen known IDs or invented uniform source-wave work.
- **Actual orchestration**: owner/issue/branch/lease, proposed versus user-dispatched versus verified-running versus returned, independent review/adoption, qualified costly parallel work, priority justification and actual product/source consumer.
- **Continuity**: every returned code, scientific ruling, original-source recovery, failed test or user requirement is linked into current nodes/edges, and effects on consumer readiness and next priorities are reconciled with exact reviewed receipts. A worker inbox or chat summary by itself is not incorporation.

1. Verify incoming immutable source/return and competent owner.
2. Resolve original source, first real consumer and affected typed nodes/edges.
3. Classify event as source discovery, science decision, engineering result, authority, RED, changed active worker or user/product requirement.
4. Update only warranted grade dimensions; propagate claimed positive/negative and relevant cross-HQ world→actor interface evidence without automatic downstream green.
5. Recalculate missing obligations, terminal readiness, critical path, alternatives, dependent cross-HQ slots and maximal lawful independent work.
6. Submit to existing responsible owner review/CAS. If not adopted, show candidate evidence without mutating canonical status.
7. Publish readback at a single **composed view version** with independent original Pyat and Sorok authoritative cuts (a version vector), claim-relative staleness warnings and changed/unchanged/blocked/next snapshots.

No narrative statement “updated Programma” qualifies unless the logical view, relevant local source-linked graph and progress/handoff reflect the new evidence at exact version and the change is actually accepted at the proper scope.
