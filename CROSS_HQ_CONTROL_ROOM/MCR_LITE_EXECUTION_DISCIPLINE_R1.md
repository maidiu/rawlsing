## PYAT REMONT — canonical first-fault, repair and builder-intake discipline

**USER-DIRECTED PROSPECTIVE REMONT RULE · 2026-11-20.** This section is an **extension of the one existing PRE-MAIN MCR-lite operational discipline**, not a fourth HQ, a substitute PROGRAMMA, another queue, or new authority to issue science, source rights, actor policy, review accreditation or protected writes. The existing [PYAT G4 development guide](../PYAT_CONTROL_ROOM/PROGRAMMA_PYAT/05_ROADMAP/README.md), canonical PROGRAMMA/OATH, PYAT paired CURRENT_PROGRESS, controlling #960/#1097, and original source/owner rights remain authoritative at their actual protected/current grade. The living REMONT first-fault queue and the standing OpenCode executor commission remain in their **existing** WORKER_RETURNS/WORKER_COMMISSIONS paths on their authorized branches; their current HEADs and QIDs must be resolved live, not read from a frozen example here.

### REMONT's purpose and its limit

**REMONT is PYAT's independent engineering first-fault, regression, and repair custodian.** Its product is *a more reliably working existing computational function and its actual receiving edge*, not another architecture, review bureaucracy, scientific issuer or replacement PYAT HQ. It receives material failures and contested evidence, identifies the **earliest consequential false behavior**, repairs or routes the smallest competent boundary, preserves working/historical functionality, proves both the restored positive and an adversarial negative, and returns the operation to its real first consumer.

**Builders and MULCH agents keep building.** A provisional feature, intellectual ambition, ordinary unfinished implementation, legitimate source/science question or honest limitation does not require REMONT permission. An in-scope builder can fix ordinary self-discovered defects before finalizing a candidate. Distinct OpenCode or independent first-fault findings, serious cross-owner breaches, false-green claims and consequential regressions go to REMONT with exact evidence. Once a separate receiver freezes a source HEAD, do not silently replace code underneath its review; an author correction is a new, separately unexecuted version until tested.

### Intake — what belongs here, and what does not

| Incoming kind | SEND to REMONT when | REMONT disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **P: Production/semantic/integrity bug** | An actual implementation violates an established source/ABI/meaning contract, drops qualified respondents, destroys known historical behavior, mishandles missing/unknown, accepts a forged replay, or fails the actual first consumer | Reproduce/locate earliest causal break; narrowly repair under competent owner, independently test positive + negative, conserve earlier behavior |
| **T: Test/runner/fixture fault** | A test asserts behavior contrary to the real issued contract, a checkout lacks an already committed fixture, or CI fails before any executed test step | Repair/route test infrastructure rather than distorting correct production; record test execution, runner failure and false expectation separately |
| **E: Evidence/integration/currentness/authority-boundary fault** | Exact source identity, manifest/hash, lease/currentness, branch/QID namespace, code/proof pairing, protected receipt or external-interface verification is wrong or misleading | Authenticate independent pins and first reader, resolve real cross-branch/owner conflict, preserve exact head and actual test grade; block only the unsafe edge |
| **R: Actual missing first-consumer input, producer or issuance requirement** | After consulting G4 and existing historical/live producers, a named real consumer still lacks a specific callable producer, lawful query, original-source administrative fact or qualified decision | Record a separate **requirement**, identify accountable owner and specific input→transformation→output, route/build allowed machinery; **do not manufacture a PF code defect or counterfeit science** |
| **Reuse/novelty conflict** | A new candidate duplicates an accepted/historical numeric producer, adaptive scheduler, cross-source composition, person/world service or authority ABI; or retires a known function without justification | Targeted function-specific G4/source/first-consumer parity; reuse, extend, reject or hold precise overlap without a fresh repository-wide audit |

**DO NOT send as a REMONT repair:** a feature idea or forward MULCH mandate; an honest incomplete developmental model; generic “not yet built” with no real consumer or G4 check; source codebook interpretation, eligibility/administration, estimand, inferential validity, legal access, original-data permission, SOROK actor choice, human consent, owner lease or protected accreditation **as a decision to be made by REMONT**; routine author green, editorial update, speculative philosophy, or code whose only alleged defect is that later scientific/native/actor functions remain unfinished. Return such inputs with `NOT_REMONT_RETURN_TO_BUILDER` or `ROUTE_R_TO_OWNER`, naming the real owner and the lawful parallel work, rather than inventing a review.

**Immediate suspected-harm exception:** A credible false permission, privacy/source-byte loss, forged source/science/actor authority, materially contaminated protected receipt, or silent historical-function destruction may be reported before full reproduction. Mark **SUSPECTED vs OBSERVED**, hold only the affected unsafe operation, preserve evidence and notify the qualified owner. REMONT cannot itself issue substitute rights.

### Minimum useful first-fault handoff (prose, not a mandatory form)

An accepted report identifies **(1)** repository/PR/exact 40-hex code HEAD, actual producer → **first consumer**, existing competent owner/lease, and the relevant G4 function or targeted stale-row discrepancy; **(2)** smallest observed versus expected failure with sourcefree/native/test provenance and downstream consequences; **(3)** actual command, clean tree, exit, JUnit/log, independently held source/proof identifiers or honest `NOT_EXECUTED/SOURCE_STATIC_SUSPECTED`; **(4)** a real positive and a discriminating adversarial negative, including conserved history and source/risk/missingness/unknown/identity cases; **(5)** a tentative P/T/E/R/reuse classification and requested bounded disposition. Do not counterfeit a local MainMac result from GitHub metadata or make the user act as a permanent test executor.

The one existing numbered REMONT test queue retains **stable, never-recycled QID meanings**. New testable builder output enters it only when the relevant source/tests and first-consumer falsifiers are identified, not as another automatic urgent invocation. Different branches may NOT each reserve the same QID for a different court; reconcile their code lineages into one selected exact HEAD before running combined QIDs. A queued job is not a running job. The existing OpenCode worker is the test executor/first-fault diagnostician; REMONT controls consequential P/T/E repairs and receiving; PYAT HQ owns forward construction and the lawful producer/issuer commissions. Source/actor/science owners retain their distinct decisions. No redundant manual rerun merely to re-document an already preserved immutable test receipt.

### REMONT cold-grok inheritance of already-queued tests

**Mandatory on first contact, succession, resumption and after a new test is sent:** Before a PYAT HQ, REMONT worker, OpenCode executor or repair reviewer commissions another test, allocates a QID, requests another user-host run or reports repair closure, it must inspect the **existing** `WORKER_RETURNS/PYAT_PREFF_ONE_MACHINE_C0_20261119/REMONT_EXECUTION_QUEUE.md`, the matching `rawlsing_next/tests/run_preff_remont_batch.sh`, the standing `WORKER_COMMISSIONS/PYAT_REMONT_GROWING_SOURCEFREE_EXECUTION_R1/00_OPENCODE_COMMISSION.md`, and exact attached execution/first-fault receipts **at the live selected branch HEAD**. These may reside only on unmerged author/REMONT branches; if absent from PRE-MAIN, find their actual repository ref rather than assume the queue is empty or invent a replacement. Keep this a narrow queue/QID/HEAD check, not another whole-repository audit.

**State each relevant QID honestly:** `TEST_AUTHORED_NOT_QUEUED` (test exists only in author code), `QUEUED_NOT_LAUNCHED` (QID, tests and executor registered on a named branch), `HEAD_MISMATCH_HELD` (queue/runner/tests cannot execute together at one authorized immutable SHA), `LAUNCHED/RUNNING` (only with verified active worker/session evidence), `EXECUTED_AT_SHA` (actual command, runtime, exit/JUnit/log and positive/negative cases), `FIRST_FAULT_RETURNED` (specific P/T/E/R evidence handed to REMONT), and `INDEPENDENTLY_RECEIVED` (distinct competent receipt and protected/currentness grade). Queued, dispatched, green, reviewed and protected are **not interchangeable**. Old green at an ancestor SHA does not test a later code HEAD. A closed/unmerged queue PR does not install that queue into PRE-MAIN.

**When a builder deposits a new test:** Update the **one existing** queue with its stable QID, named actual producer/first consumer, code/test HEAD, sourcefree/native restrictions, existing executable/selector and falsifying negatives; update the standing executor instructions only if their current routing is genuinely incompatible. The developer's handoff should say **“sent to REMONT queue” only if this registration actually exists and is recoverable at an exact ref**. Otherwise say `TEST_AUTHORED_QUEUE_PENDING`. Do not duplicate QIDs or create new master trackers. At next separate authorized execution, the standing OpenCode executor reconciles source+queue+runner into one clean exact-head worktree, ingests already-valid prior receipts rather than rerunning them, runs only eligible pending cases, and reports failures directly to REMONT with immutable evidence. **Adding a test to the queue neither launches it nor obliges the user to manually relay results.**

**On return/handoff:** State the already-queued QIDs and source locators, execution status and any exact-head mismatch in the normal REMONT/PROGRAMMA report; maintain accepted fates in existing PF/REQ/G4 and paired CURRENT_PROGRESS **only through authorized receiving**. Where an independent current executor or writer is unavailable, preserve `HQ_CURRENTNESS_HOLD` on that exact edge without stopping unrelated forward construction. No new REMONT role, queue, ceremony or permission is created.

### REMONT's evidentiary and scientific discipline

1. **Five statuses never collapse:** user priority, implemented exact source, *actually executed tests and independent reviewer grade*, current protected/rights receiving, and remaining functionality/consumer. Synthetic/sourcefree green cannot establish original survey science. Cold read cannot establish an issued query. Metadata validation cannot establish physical row semantics. An unmerged correction cannot inherit tests at its parent SHA.
2. **Inspect real payloads and inherited producers before writing adapters.** The saved ANVIL estates showed `roots`, not a speculative `i02` field. An unknown format is not proof of missing decoding machinery. An absent implementation in one working branch is not proof of historical nonexistence. Use G4 and a **targeted** exact-source comparison; never build duplicate MI, adaptive frontier, Fréchet overlap or possibility engine merely to obtain a new module.
3. **Independent identity must be independently held.** An internally rehashed object can carry a forged assertion. Verify external source, manifest/member, release, replay, universe, mapping-edition, currentness and actual first-consumer semantics at the warranted edge. Preserve typed refusal rather than silently relabeling incompatible schema or authorizing oneself.
4. **Do not turn ignorance into findings.** Unknown administration is not NOT_ASKED; no emitted answer is not recorded physical MISSING; refused/DK responses remain eligible when source-proven; numeric response codes require exact codebooks; sample MI is not causal, design-weighted or a certified population hierarchy. Small/rare groups remain visible even when automatic recursion is statistically unqualified. Unsearched alternatives remain unsearched, not false or zero.
5. **Distinguish a working feature from a useful feature.** An isolated green module must demonstrate its actual producer→consumer edge. Preserve historical function, semantics, original negative courts and no-drop F6/FF/SOROK commitments; show the *new* first-consumer behavior rather than a detached alternate engine.
6. **Adversarially test REMONT's own repairs.** A clever compatibility fix, a green fixture, a documentation update and a direct GitHub source readback are distinct proofs. Verify first-fault reversal, hostile counterexample, no regression and exact version. If a later worker reveals that REMONT's fix was mistaken, preserve its evidence and openly correct it.
7. **Do not bureaucratize ordinary building.** One proportionate independent review where warranted. Return `REPAIR_P`, `REPAIR_T`, `PROVE_E`, `ROUTE_R_TO_OWNER`, `REUSE_OR_NARROW_EXTENSION`, or `NOT_REMONT_RETURN_TO_BUILDER`, with reason and smallest next step. A legitimate HOLD on one source/actor/permission edge does not halt unrelated code construction.

**Terminal receipt:** Each consequential intake gets an exact-source fate in the **existing** PF/REQ ledger, test queue, and—through **authorized** PROGRAMMA receiving—affected G4 rows and paired PYAT CURRENT_PROGRESS. Distinguish author findings from independent G5, original-source scientific authority and protected CAS. Where writer authority, independent review, current head reconciliation, or source permission is missing, declare `HQ_CURRENTNESS_HOLD` with the exact gap; do not claim a rule is protected merely because it lives on a draft branch. No additional permanent REMONT charter, inventory, sign-off layer or duplicate test system is created.

**Measure success by the consequential repairs and newly reliable first-consumer operations returned to PYAT and SEAM—not defect volume, green test count, or the length of REMONT's paperwork.**

## Working MCR-lite standard

1. **Preserve the whole mission.** Know what must actually be accomplished. Don't quietly reduce a comprehensive assignment to one demonstrable example.

2. **Inspect before inventing.** Identify the existing code, authority, accepted work, and real missing functionality. Don't commission archaeology that merely repeats established knowledge.

3. **Specify success concretely.** Know what working result must exist, who consumes it, and what would demonstrate failure. Use exact source pins where consequential.

4. **Build the damn thing.** Once the problem is sufficiently understood and execution is authorized, implement. Don't commission another investigation merely to establish permission to plan an implementation.

5. **Test causally, not ceremonially.** Actual positive behavior, a meaningful negative or severance, and restoration. Green tests must prove the claimed dependency.

6. **Preserve competent boundaries.** Engineering success doesn't manufacture scientific truth, source rights, actor authority, or human consent. But those restrictions block only the operations they genuinely govern.

7. **One substantive independent receiving.** Independent review where warranted, with precise defects and finite repairs. No endless chain of reviews of reviews.

8. **Close at the honest grade and move forward.** An accepted sourcefree result is accepted sourcefree engineering. Don't reopen it because native science remains unfinished. Record the remaining problem and pursue the next substantial product capability.

## What is explicitly rejected

**BUREAUCRATIC RECURSION**

Investigation authorization → scoping investigation → feasibility commission → independent feasibility review → review correction → permission assessment → implementation planning → planning review → pilot specification → pilot review → actual work, eventually.

**MCR-LITE**

**Understand → Specify → Execute → Falsify → Independently receive → Integrate**

These aren't always six separately serialized phases. Specification, implementation, testing, and lawful parallel investigation can overlap. An isolated pilot is an instrument, not the product.

## Governing distinction

**Rigor is not proportional to administrative complexity.**

An engineer who identifies the actual consumer, preserves inherited code, fixes the source defect, proves positive/negative behavior, and submits an independently reviewable implementation has exercised more rigor than an agent who produces five perfectly formatted compliance documents without fixing anything.

Equally, passing 94 tests is not enough if none of them exercises the claimed behavior.

**The objective is maximum substantive rigor per unit of friction.**

## Operational interpretation: lightweight by default, full where explicitly requested

- **For a real agent launch:** keep one complete copy-paste instruction, a named implementer/reviewer role, executable scope, actual branch/source cut where material, first consumer, observable positive and negative, qualified runner, three honest engineering/scientific/authority grades, and bounded post-return independent receiving *where warranted*. The `verify_copy_paste_commission_r1.py` `MCR: LITE` path checks this structural minimum. It is NOT permission or substantive acceptance.
- **For an explicitly FULL MCR-C task:** `MCR: REQUIRED` keeps the complete `MCR_COMPLETION_CONTRACT_BEGIN` frozen universe, per-obligation evidence, exact source tuples, return verifier, independent court and all repairs, unchanged. Do not quietly downgrade an existing binding full MCR-C assignment to lite.
- **For an implementation or material repair:** preserve its complete assigned functional tranche, prove the actual first-consumer causal edge, perform a discriminating negative and restoration, and preserve independent judgment where the claim warrants it. A REAL scientific/source/legal/actor grant is never simulated.
- **For reviews, investigations, documentation and small bounded decisions:** select the proportional proof scope. No artificial full source-enumerator, one-register-per-sentence, independent-review-of-a-review or permission-to-commission-a-permission packet without a concrete material reason.
- **For HOLD:** name the missing function or genuinely competent permission and what concrete work can proceed now. A HOLD on an unissued edge cannot become a blanket halt on other lawful code or a claim that an otherwise accepted scoped result is incomplete.
- **For integration:** source-pin the accepted implementation when consequential, inspect the combined first consumer, run relevant regression/fault checks and preserve branch CAS/review rules. Do not re-review the unchanged historical universe.
- **No additional courts, boards, universal registers, schedules or mandatory external agents.** Where a law specifically mandates a source rights decision, qualified scientific review, protected merge check or human consent, that substantive boundary remains.
