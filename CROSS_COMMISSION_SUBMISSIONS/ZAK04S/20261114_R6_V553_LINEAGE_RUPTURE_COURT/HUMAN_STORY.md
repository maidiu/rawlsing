# Why the missing delta ID matters
A measured structural-pressure observation can have an exact identity and source lineage. V553 can compare two qualified observations and compute a signed temporal delta. But if the result drops its own relation ID, the downstream temporal-field record cannot say *which precise change* explains its reported state.
That is a narrower and more actionable gap than the entire temporal pipeline being absent. Observations exist; numeric change computation exists; unknown gaps are typed. The loss is in the relationship identifier and the qualified next reader, not necessarily the numerical values.
The rupture signal is an additional trap: a single large delta is not reversal, and a literal-status typo currently blocks even the positive interpretation path. Retaining that accidental denial is not a substitute for the governing scientific rule; versioned rules and exact endpoint provenance must be separate.
A source file that lists forbidden outputs and writes `violation_detected=False` for each is documentation, not an audit of what was emitted. Protect the legitimate functionality, record the missing proof, and leave execution/repair to a separately authorized commission.
