# What the pressure file actually becomes
The old V553 source investigator could authorize a sparse observed structural pressure change between matching, measured survey waves. A gap between observations stayed unknown. That is a real protective distinction: a plausible-looking coverage statistic cannot manufacture an observed social transformation.
The V5 thin executor can carry the authorized parquet into its next layer, but transport is not interpretation. Its V554 contract builder instead reads year-indexed coverage pressure on the normal thin route; a standalone V554 run prefers country-year pressure. Both yield a numeric seed from overlay readiness/coverage, not the source-keyed signed observed measure. The status labels then do not match the regional positive seed branch. A national seed can still be produced from separate global readiness; no blanket failure is proved.
In another branch, F6M actually computes numeric partial-identification bounds across independent sources; F6P verifies them. Modern I07 only permits or denies cross-source operations. It does not reproduce F6M numbers. Meanwhile L01 can really read signed empirical support values and make selected test-only world routes, but this says nothing about F6M or the old V553 values being its inputs. Each real piece of functionality should survive, and none should borrow authority from a neighboring label.
