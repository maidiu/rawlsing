## R10 finding 1 — distinguish three historical outputs and the old reduced bridge

**T0023:** V550 took one question's respondent response and multiple position indicators; ranked the primary by MI/Cramér V/support; compared conditional entropy on primary-observed respondents against the conditional entropy on the **intersection** with a secondary indicator (minimum 50). Output included secondary indicator names, a conditional entropy gain, support, and confidence. The two entropy operands can have different risk sets; the gap is **not purely proven incremental information**. [source](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_branching.py#L203-L328).

**T0024:** V550 recursively selected a positive entropy-gain split indicator, emitted node_id, parent_node_id, depth, entropy, gain, stop reason and dominant answer; it expands *only the two most frequent split values*, not all categories. This is an adaptive, bounded, non-exhaustive tree, not I05's fixed complete candidate frontier. [source](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_branching.py#L372-L432).

**T0025:** V550 compared each subgroup's **unweighted** dominant answer/share to the global answer and classified conforms/deviates/minority_converges. The last means a *different* dominant answer with share >= baseline, **not temporal convergence or social minority science**. Weight affects n_weighted, not the classifier predicate. [source](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_branching.py#L435-L466).

The original [V550 bridge](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_bridge.py#L737-L860) reduces rows into `source_row_hash` and `empirical_object_id` using a payload which includes source/wave/country/question/var, position_field/value, response and source_table, but **not** indicator_name/value, secondary_gain or node/parent/branch IDs. These historical source functions use distinct indicator/category fields, not position_field/value. Two original rows with the same cited hash inputs but (sex,female) versus (education_level,college) **can logically map to the same bridge hash** despite different conditional denominator and meaning; this is a source-code counterexample, **NOT an executed collision**. The [V551 required bridge schema](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_schema.py#L1714-L1780) and [derived audit projection](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/9253064bc9423a0f0ccda4fbdea43c2f54f800c6/data/ddm_build_copy/ddm_v550_bridge.py#L1560-L1595) do not automatically recover those keys. The earlier full V550 output tables were separately written and must **not** be declared lost simply because the reduced interface is noninjective. The reader's actual selected invocation and resulting drop remain unproved.

## R10 finding 2 — current C11 is stronger than this specific R56 adapter

- **General C11 producer capability:** [`GovernedC11Projection`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_backed_c11_facade.py#L268-L345) has `coordinate_slots: tuple[CoordinateSlotRule,...]` alongside distinct response slots, per-row respondent binding, routing and weight owner authorities, slot identity and replay controls. It is **capable in structure** of receiving multiple independently issued coordinate slots. This is **SCHEMA_CARRIER_POSSIBLE**, not empirical science or same-grain F03 execution.
- **R56 native sourcefree adapter:** [`NativeSourcePackage`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L145-L256) chooses one `coordinate_variable`, one `coordinate_unit`, one `coordinate_effective_time`; [materializer](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L837-L906) constructs exactly `coordinate_slots=(coordinate_slot,)` and `weight_slots=()`. A single such adapter product is **not** a demonstrated T0023 simultaneous two-indicator conditional-entropy input, nor a T0024 multi-indicator branching substrate. This is a **bounded adapter** limitation, not an architecture-wide C11 constraint.
- **Original QSM identity remains outside R56:** `NativeSourceIdentity` contains source_id/family/wave/canonical filename/declared source SHA/format, while `NativeSourceRule` contains implementation ID/source variable/unit/administered/semantic direction/aliases. There is no claimed selected historical F03 `authority_rule_id/version`, `row_payload_hash`, `country_code_scope`, `question_id` to native var binding or separate modern TARGET_USE_PERMISSION in the package. [`QuestionVariableBinding`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L639-L685) explicitly sets `canonical_question_ref_or_typed_absence=NOT_AUTHORIZED`. An actual issued mapping is needed; source identity alone cannot grant relation directionality, pressure or structural role.
- **Sourcefree routing is not original questionnaire routing:** For every selected administered source question, the [materializer](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L785-L815) constructs a `RoutingEligibilityFact` for **every** respondent with `typed_state=ELIGIBLE_APPLICABLE`, `applicability_state=ELIGIBLE_APPLICABLE` and `routing_state=ELIGIBLE_APPLICABLE`. This does not independently derive original asked/not-asked, skip or conditional same-person risk sets. Modern I03 may properly classify missing response as UNRESOLVED rather than silently reinterpreting as old IAM blank category. The general [source-local admission](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_local_universe_admission.py#L270-L354) still imposes SOURCE_LOCAL_ONLY/RAW_ONLY without separate positive science.
- **Working strength:** [governed occurrence plan](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L520-L573) refuses wrong source edition/row count/format and requires an explicit row-occurrence plan for repeated-cell sources. Exact respondent and occurrence identities are present in the adapter. We **preserve** that capability, rather than call the entire preimage code a fake interface.

## R10 finding 3 — exactly what the modern current readers do differently

**I03:** Its [coordinate predicate](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/_pre553_query_predicate.py#L165-L222) selects a specifically authorized semantic coordinate with EQ/IN/STATE_EQ, verifies current source authority on matching C11 facts, requires exact effective time when coordinate values are ambiguous, and can select the correct subgroup **if** all original indicator names/values and issuer mapping were independently preserved. Its [evaluation core](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/_pre553_query_eval.py#L404-L542) constructs routing risk members first, then condition-selected denominators, then exact response-domain configurations. If a positive denominator contains an unresolved target, it returns `UNRESOLVED`, not a convenience old blank response category. The valid route can preserve same respondents with a properly specified conjunction of two coordinate predicates, but that depends on an **actually issued multi-coordinate source release**; the R56 single-coordinate fixture does not establish one.

**I05:** [`execute_exact_bounded_discovery`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_discovery_exact.py#L1025-L1160) enumerates the **entire already-authorized** candidate frontier before querying. Its evaluated validations are `DISTRIBUTION_DIFFERS_FROM_BASELINE`, `JOINT_NOT_PRODUCT_OF_MARGINALS`, `CONDITIONAL_REVERSAL` and `TARGET_CELL_SUPPORTED`. Its actual outputs are typed candidate receipts and fact refs. That can be a legitimate *related* empirical operation, but it does **not** demonstrate V550 T0023's entropy difference on two differently filtered respondent frames, T0024's recursive top-two tree, or T0025's unweighted dominant-answer label. The existence of I05 does not settle historical same-grain succession.

**Potential additional package-hash limitation—qualified rather than sensationalized:** [`NativeSourcePackage.package_hash`](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L145-L256) directly hashes coordinate_variable/unit and other source identifiers **but omits coordinate_effective_time**. The [materializer](https://github.com/maidiu/rawlsing/blob/6a5c12f04323d74001f9632063ec907f4fc4468e/rawlsing_next/pre553_source_native_preimage_adapter.py#L837-L906) includes that effective time in its actual `CoordinateSlotRule`. Consequently the *package hash alone* is **not a complete fingerprint of the coordinate-time configuration**. The result also builds a validated C11 release and a `bundle_hash` referencing the release; those **may** distinguish changes. No source-level evidence here proves an identical final release, successful tampering, incorrect actual I03 execution or use of package_hash as a sole trusted credential. A future owner should inspect any actual package-hash-only consumer **before** opening an engineering defect.
